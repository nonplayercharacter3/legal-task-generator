# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Lopol Fleet Safety Systems, LLC**, a limited liability company ("**Supplier**"); and

**Gabousk Transit Technologies, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Supplier designs and manufactures stop-arm violation camera systems for school buses and operates a cloud-based evidence management platform used to capture, review and process stop-arm violation events.

B. Distributor resells, installs and supports school bus safety and fleet technology for school districts and contract bus operators in New York.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as a distributor of Supplier's products and related platform services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

"**Competing Product**" means any product, system, device, software or service, other than the Products, that is used in or in connection with pupil transportation.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

"**Customer**" means a school district, board of cooperative educational services, municipality or contract pupil transportation operator that purchases Products from Distributor.

"**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum entered into between the Parties in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

"**Documentation**" means Supplier's then-current installation guides, user manuals and technical specifications for the Products.

"**Initial Term**" means the initial term of this Agreement stated in the Order Form.

"**Order Form**" means the order form executed by the Parties under this Agreement setting out the commercial terms of the distribution relationship, including pricing, quantities, the Territory, the Initial Term, the Renewal Term and the Minimum Commitment.

"**Minimum Commitment**" means the annual minimum purchase commitment stated in the Order Form.

"**Platform**" means Supplier's cloud-based evidence management platform, including all associated software, hosting, storage, review tools and interfaces, through which images, video and data captured by the Products are uploaded, stored, reviewed and processed.

"**Platform Services**" means access to and use of the Platform by Distributor and Customers, together with related support, as described in Section 8.

"**Products**" means the stop-arm violation camera systems, components, mounting hardware and accessories listed in the Order Form, as updated by the Parties in writing.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products or Platform Services issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement.

"**Renewal Term**" means each renewal term of this Agreement stated in the Order Form.

"**Student Data**" means any personal information relating to a student, including images, video, location data and records of a student's presence on or near a school bus, that is captured by the Products or processed on the Platform.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

"**Territory**" means the geographic area stated in the Order Form.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. References to a document include that document as amended from time to time in accordance with its terms.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Order Form and the DPA are to be read together. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, the following order of precedence applies: first, the DPA (as to data protection matters); second, this Agreement; and third, the Order Form. The Order Form controls only as to pricing and quantities.

## 2. Appointment

**2.1 Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products and Platform Services to Customers in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

**2.2 Supplier Restraint.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, reseller or agent for the Products in the Territory, and shall refer to Distributor any inquiry it receives from a prospective Customer located in the Territory.

**2.3 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Distributor purchases Products for resale in its own name and for its own account. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship, and neither Party may bind the other.

**2.4 Distributor Responsibilities.** Distributor shall (a) use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products in the Territory; (b) install Products in accordance with the Documentation using trained personnel; (c) provide first-line support to Customers; and (d) comply with all laws applicable to its resale and installation of the Products, including New York Vehicle and Traffic Law requirements governing school bus equipment.

## 3. Term and Renewal

**3.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 16.

**3.2 Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms of the length stated in the Order Form. Either Party may elect not to renew this Agreement by giving written notice of non-renewal to the other Party during the period beginning ninety (90) days before and ending sixty (60) days before the expiry of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term, as applicable. Any notice of non-renewal given outside that period shall be ineffective, and this Agreement shall renew in accordance with this Section 3.2.

**3.3 Terms During Renewal.** Each Renewal Term shall be on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Form then in effect, subject to any pricing adjustment permitted by Section 5.4.

## 4. Orders and Minimum Commitment

**4.1 Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products and Platform Services by submitting Purchase Orders to Supplier. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products and quantities ordered, the requested delivery date and the delivery location.

**4.2 Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days of receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and the Order Form. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

**4.3 Governing Terms.** Each accepted Purchase Order is governed by this Agreement. Any pre-printed or additional terms on a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect.

**4.4 Minimum Commitment.** Distributor shall purchase at least the Minimum Commitment in each contract year of the Term. A "contract year" is each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

**4.5 Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Commitment in any contract year, Supplier shall notify Distributor in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of that contract year, and the Parties shall meet in good faith to review the shortfall. Two consecutive contract years of shortfall shall entitle Supplier, as its sole remedy for the shortfall, to convert Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 to a non-exclusive appointment on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 5. Pricing and Payment

**5.1 Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for Products and Platform Services set out in the Order Form.

**5.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Products upon shipment and Platform Services annually in advance, unless the Order Form provides otherwise.

**5.3 Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within forty-five (45) days of receipt. Distributor may withhold any amount disputed in good faith by giving Supplier written notice describing the dispute before the due date. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days.

**5.4 Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust prices with effect from the start of any Renewal Term on at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice, provided that no annual increase exceeds the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the preceding twelve (12) months.

**5.5 Resale Prices.** Distributor is free to determine its own resale prices to Customers.

**5.6 Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Distributor shall provide Supplier with valid resale certificates as applicable.

## 6. Delivery, Title and Risk

**6.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the delivery location stated in each accepted Purchase Order, on or before the delivery date stated in it.

**6.2 Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Products pass to Distributor upon delivery. Title to the Platform and to all software embedded in the Products remains with Supplier at all times and is licensed in accordance with Section 14.

**6.3 Inspection.** Distributor may inspect Products within fifteen (15) business days after delivery and reject any non-conforming Products by written notice. Supplier shall, at its cost, replace rejected Products within ten (10) business days and collect the rejected Products.

## 7. Exclusivity and Non-Competition

**7.1 Product Supply.** During the Term, Supplier shall supply Products for the Territory exclusively through Distributor and shall maintain sufficient manufacturing capacity to fulfil Purchase Orders up to one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the Minimum Commitment in each contract year.

**7.2 Distributor Non-Competition.** During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months after its expiry or termination, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, whether on its own account or through any Affiliate or third party, market, promote, sell, resell, distribute, install or support any Competing Product anywhere in the United States.

**7.3 Non-Solicitation.** During the Term, neither Party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who has been materially involved in the performance of this Agreement, provided that general advertisements not targeted at the other Party's employees are not a breach of this Section 7.3.

## 8. Platform Services

**8.1 Provision.** Supplier shall make the Platform available to Distributor and to each Customer that has purchased Products and Platform Services, for the subscription period paid for, in accordance with the Documentation.

**8.2 Availability.** Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available ninety-nine and one-half percent (99.5%) of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

**8.3 Support.** Supplier shall provide second-line technical support to Distributor during business hours (8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday) and shall respond to Severity 1 incidents within two (2) hours at any time.

**8.4 Customer Terms.** Distributor shall ensure that each Customer accepts Supplier's then-current Platform end user terms before using the Platform. Supplier shall give Distributor at least sixty (60) days' notice of any material change to those terms.

## 9. Data Protection

**9.1 DPA.** Each Party shall comply with the DPA in connection with the processing of Student Data and other personal data under this Agreement.

**9.2 Applicable Law.** Supplier shall process Student Data on the Platform in compliance with all applicable laws, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, New York Education Law Section 2-d and its implementing regulations, and the New York SHIELD Act.

**9.3 Customer Requirements.** Supplier shall execute such data privacy agreements, parents' bill of rights supplements and similar instruments as a Customer reasonably requires in order to comply with New York Education Law Section 2-d.

## 10. Confidentiality

**10.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including pricing, Customer lists and technical information. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than through the receiving Party's breach; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**10.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 10.

**10.3 Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, including the New York Freedom of Information Law, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice, where legally permitted, and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

**10.4 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 10 continue for five (5) years after expiry or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 11. Warranties

**11.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship and will conform to the Documentation for twenty-four (24) months after delivery. Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace any non-conforming Product, and shall reimburse Distributor's reasonable labor costs for removal and reinstallation at the rates stated in the Order Form.

**11.2 Platform Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Platform Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner and that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation.

**11.3 Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products, as delivered, comply with all federal and New York requirements applicable to school bus stop-arm camera equipment, and that evidence captured and processed by the Products and the Platform is in a form suitable for use in proceedings under New York Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 1174-a.

**11.4 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and that doing so does not breach any other agreement binding on it.

**11.5 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

**12.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Subject to Section 12.3, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for loss of profits or revenue, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, however caused.

**12.2 Cap.** Subject to Section 12.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Distributor to Supplier in the prior 12 months, measured from the date of the event giving rise to the claim.

**12.3 Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to, and do not limit, either Party's liability for:

(a) its indemnification obligations under Section 13;

(b) breach of Section 10 (Confidentiality);

(c) breach of its data protection obligations under Section 9 or the DPA, including any unauthorized access to, use of or disclosure of Student Data;

(d) gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(e) Distributor's obligation to pay undisputed fees.

## 13. Indemnification

**13.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, fines, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) any allegation that the Products or the Platform infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret; (b) any defect in the Products causing bodily injury or damage to property; or (c) Supplier's breach of Section 9 or the DPA.

**13.2 By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) Distributor's negligent installation of Products; or (b) any warranty or representation made by Distributor to a Customer that goes beyond the warranties in Section 11.

**13.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

**13.4 Infringement Remedies.** If the Products or the Platform become, or in Supplier's reasonable opinion are likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Supplier shall at its cost procure the right to continue using them, modify them to be non-infringing without material loss of functionality, or replace them with non-infringing equivalents.

## 14. Intellectual Property

**14.1 Ownership.** Supplier retains all right, title and interest in the Products' designs, embedded software, the Platform, the Documentation and Supplier's trademarks.

**14.2 Software License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to distribute the software embedded in the Products, solely as part of the Products, and to sublicense the Platform to Customers on Supplier's end user terms.

**14.3 Trademark License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory solely to market the Products, in accordance with Supplier's reasonable brand guidelines.

**14.4 Customer Data.** As between the Parties, all Student Data and other data captured by the Products or uploaded to the Platform belongs to the relevant Customer, and Supplier shall use it only as permitted by the DPA.

## 15. Insurance

During the Term and for two (2) years afterwards, each Party shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and products and completed operations liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 in the aggregate. Supplier shall also maintain cyber liability and technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim. Each Party shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 16. Termination

**16.1 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach.

**16.2 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver or trustee appointed over all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**16.3 Regulatory Change.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice if a change in law makes the sale or use of the Products in the Territory unlawful.

## 17. Effects of Termination

**17.1 Outstanding Orders.** Upon expiry or termination, Supplier shall fulfil all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of expiry or termination, unless the Agreement is terminated by Supplier under Section 16.1 or 16.2.

**17.2 Continuity of Platform Services.** Supplier shall continue to provide Platform Services to each Customer for the remainder of any subscription period paid for before expiry or termination, and shall provide each Customer with a reasonable transition period, of not less than ninety (90) days, to export its data in accordance with the DPA.

**17.3 Inventory.** Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory of Products in the Territory for one hundred eighty (180) days after expiry or termination, on the terms of this Agreement.

**17.4 Survival.** Sections 7.2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.1, 14.4, 17 and 19, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiry or termination.

## 18. General

**18.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier, to the addresses stated in the Order Form. Notices are effective upon receipt.

**18.2 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, government action and failures of public utilities, provided that the affected Party notifies the other promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. This Section 18.2 does not excuse payment obligations. If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either Party may terminate the affected Purchase Orders by written notice.

**18.3 Assignment.** Distributor shall not assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law, by merger or change of control, or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Any purported assignment or transfer in breach of this Section 18.3 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**18.4 Subcontracting.** Distributor may use subcontracted installers, provided they are trained in accordance with the Documentation and Distributor remains responsible for their performance.

**18.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**18.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

**18.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**18.8 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Section 13, nothing in this Agreement confers any right or remedy on any person other than the Parties.

## 19. Governing Law and Disputes

**19.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

**19.2 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by referring it to their respective senior executives, who shall meet within fifteen (15) business days of a written request by either Party.

**19.3 Jurisdiction.** If a dispute is not resolved under Section 19.2 within thirty (30) days, each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Southern District of New York, provided that either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**19.4 Jury Waiver.** Each Party knowingly and voluntarily waives any right to a trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
