# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between:

**Lopol Fleet Safety Systems, LLC**, a limited liability company ("**Supplier**"); and

**Gabousk Transit Technologies, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

This Addendum is the "DPA" referred to in the Distribution Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, including "Customer," "Platform," "Platform Services," "Products," "Student Data" and "Term."

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Addendum:

"**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g) and its implementing regulations (34 C.F.R. Part 99), New York Education Law Section 2-d and Part 121 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, the New York SHIELD Act (New York General Business Law Sections 899-aa and 899-bb), and New York Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 1174-a to the extent it governs the use and retention of stop-arm camera evidence.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that is captured by the Products, uploaded to or processed on the Platform, or otherwise processed by Supplier in connection with the Agreement. Personal Data includes Student Data, Violation Data, and personal information of Customer and Distributor personnel.

"**Personal Data Breach**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data. It includes a "breach" as defined in Part 121 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education and a "breach of the security of the system" under New York General Business Law Section 899-aa.

"**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, review, use, disclosure, transmission, export and deletion. "**Process**" and "**processed**" have corresponding meanings.

"**Security Program**" means the information security program described in Section 5.

"**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Supplier that processes Personal Data in connection with the Platform Services.

"**Violation Data**" means images, video, license plate data, vehicle descriptions, timestamps, GPS location data and related records of a motor vehicle passing a stopped school bus. It includes any personal information about the registered owner or operator of that vehicle.

## 2. Roles and Scope

**2.1 Roles.** Each Customer is the owner of the Personal Data relating to its pupil transportation operations, as provided in Section 14.4 of the Agreement. In relation to that Personal Data, Supplier acts as a third-party contractor and service provider of the Customer within the meaning of Applicable Data Protection Law. Distributor does not host Personal Data on the Platform. Distributor may access Personal Data through the Platform only to the extent needed to provide installation, first-line support and Customer administration.

**2.2 Scope.** This Addendum applies to all Processing of Personal Data by Supplier, its personnel and its Subprocessors in connection with the Products, the Platform and the Platform Services during the Term. It also applies to any later period during which Supplier retains Personal Data.

**2.3 Details of Processing.** Schedule 1 describes the subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the Processing, the categories of Personal Data and the categories of data subjects.

## 3. Supplier Obligations

**3.1 Instructions.** Supplier shall process Personal Data only:

(a) to provide the Platform Services;

(b) in accordance with the Agreement, this Addendum and the documented instructions of the relevant Customer; and

(c) as required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

Supplier shall promptly inform Distributor and the relevant Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law.

**3.2 Prohibited Uses.** Supplier shall not:

(a) sell Personal Data;

(b) use or disclose Personal Data for any commercial, marketing or advertising purpose;

(c) use Personal Data to build a profile of any student, except as required to provide the Platform Services; or

(d) combine Personal Data with data from other sources, except as necessary to provide the Platform Services.

Supplier may use de-identified, aggregated data to maintain and improve the Platform only if the data cannot reasonably be re-identified and Supplier does not attempt to re-identify it.

**3.3 Violation Data.** Supplier shall use Violation Data only to prepare violation evidence and citation packages for the relevant Customer and for the law enforcement or adjudicating authority designated by that Customer. Supplier shall retain Violation Data no longer than the period permitted by Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 1174-a and the Customer's instructions.

**3.4 Personnel.** Supplier shall ensure the following for each person who has access to Personal Data:

(a) access is limited to personnel who need it to perform the Platform Services;

(b) each such person is bound by written confidentiality obligations; and

(c) each such person has received training on Applicable Data Protection Law before receiving access, and at least annually after that.

**3.5 Parents' Bill of Rights.** Supplier shall comply with the parents' bill of rights for data privacy and security adopted by each Customer that is an educational agency under Education Law Section 2-d. Supplier shall execute any supplemental information or data privacy agreement required under Section 9.3 of the Agreement.

**3.6 Location.** Supplier shall store and process Personal Data only within the United States.

## 4. Subprocessors

**4.1 Authorization.** Supplier may engage the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 2. Supplier shall give Distributor at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any new or replacement Subprocessor, and Distributor shall pass that notice to affected Customers. Distributor or a Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If it does, the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the objection.

**4.2 Flow-Down.** Supplier shall bind each Subprocessor by written agreement to data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum. Supplier remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Security Program.** Supplier shall implement and maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Student Data. The program shall be aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, as required by Part 121 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education. At a minimum, it shall include:

(a) encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent;

(b) role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for all administrative access to the Platform;

(c) logging and monitoring of access to Personal Data, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months;

(d) annual penetration testing by an independent third party, with remediation of critical and high findings within a reasonable time;

(e) encryption of DVR storage cartridges in the Products; and

(f) business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements, tested at least annually.

**5.2 Certifications.** Supplier shall maintain a SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent independent assessment, covering the Platform. Supplier shall provide a copy to Distributor on request, subject to Section 10 of the Agreement.

## 6. Personal Data Breach

**6.1 Notification.** Supplier shall notify Distributor and each affected Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after Supplier discovers a Personal Data Breach. The notice shall include, to the extent known:

(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach;

(b) the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to address the Personal Data Breach.

Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**6.2 Cooperation.** Supplier shall do the following:

(a) take prompt steps to contain, investigate and remediate the Personal Data Breach;

(b) cooperate with each affected Customer in making any notification required of that Customer under Applicable Data Protection Law, including to parents, eligible students, the New York State Education Department Chief Privacy Officer and the New York Attorney General; and

(c) reimburse the Customer for the full cost of notifications it is required to make under Education Law Section 2-d as a result of a Personal Data Breach attributable to Supplier or its Subprocessors.

**6.3 No Public Statement.** Supplier shall not notify any data subject or make any public statement about a Personal Data Breach affecting a Customer's Personal Data without first consulting that Customer, unless required by law.

## 7. Data Subject Requests and Assistance

**7.1 Requests.** Supplier shall promptly forward to the relevant Customer, and in any event within five (5) business days, any request from a parent, eligible student or other data subject to inspect, review, correct or delete Personal Data. Supplier shall not respond to the request directly unless the Customer instructs it to. Supplier shall provide the Customer with reasonable assistance in responding.

**7.2 Legal Requests.** If Supplier receives a subpoena, court order or other legal demand for Personal Data, Supplier shall notify the relevant Customer before disclosure, where legally permitted, so that the Customer may seek a protective order.

## 8. Return and Deletion

**8.1 Export.** On expiry or termination of the Agreement, or of a Customer's Platform Services subscription, Supplier shall make the Customer's Personal Data available for export in a commonly used, machine-readable format. The export period shall be the transition period provided in Section 17.2 of the Agreement.

**8.2 Deletion.** After the export period ends, Supplier shall securely delete all of that Customer's Personal Data, including from backups, within sixty (60) days, except to the extent retention is required by law. Supplier shall certify the deletion in writing on request.

**8.3 Audits.** Supplier shall make available to Distributor and to each Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum. Supplier shall allow one audit or inspection per year on at least thirty (30) days' notice, and additional audits following a Personal Data Breach. Audits shall be conducted during business hours, subject to reasonable confidentiality obligations.

## 9. Liability

Each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Addendum, including liability for any Personal Data Breach, is subject to the limitations of liability in Section 12 of the Agreement. The amount stated in Section 12.2 of the Agreement is the maximum aggregate liability of each Party under this Addendum.

## 10. Term and General

**10.1 Term.** This Addendum takes effect on March 1, 2026 and continues for the Term. Its obligations survive for as long as Supplier or any Subprocessor retains Personal Data.

**10.2 Changes in Law.** The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment to this Addendum reasonably required to comply with a change in Applicable Data Protection Law.

**10.3 Governing Law.** This Addendum is governed by the laws of the State of New York, in accordance with Section 19.1 of the Agreement.
