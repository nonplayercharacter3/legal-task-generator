| **Agreement** | Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") |

## 1. Incorporation and Defined Terms

**1.1** This Order Form is the "Order Form" referred to in the Agreement. It is issued under, and forms part of, the Agreement.

**1.2** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

## 2. Products and Platform Services

**2.1 Products.** The "Products" for purposes of the Agreement are the following:

| SKU | Product | Description |
|---|---|---|
| LFS-SA4 | StopArm Sentinel 4-Camera System | Exterior stop-arm violation camera system with four (4) HD cameras (forward, rear, and two side-facing), onboard DVR, LTE modem, GPS module and stop-arm trigger harness |
| LFS-SA2 | StopArm Sentinel 2-Camera System | Exterior stop-arm violation camera system with two (2) HD cameras (forward and driver-side), onboard DVR, LTE modem, GPS module and stop-arm trigger harness |
| LFS-LPR | License Plate Recognition Camera Module | Add-on high-resolution plate capture camera, compatible with LFS-SA4 and LFS-SA2 |
| LFS-MK | Mounting and Harness Kit | Bus-model-specific brackets, weatherproof housings and wiring harness |
| LFS-SP | Spare Parts Kit | Replacement camera heads, DVR storage cartridges and cabling |

**2.2 Platform Services.** Each LFS-SA4 and LFS-SA2 unit sold to a Customer must be accompanied by a Platform Services subscription for that unit. A subscription includes evidence upload and storage, violation review tools, citation package export and Customer administrator access, as described in the Documentation.

**2.3 Counting Units.** For purposes of Section 7, a "stop-arm camera unit" means one (1) LFS-SA4 or one (1) LFS-SA2 system. Add-on modules, mounting kits, spare parts and Platform Services subscriptions are not counted as stop-arm camera units.

## 3. Pricing

**3.1 Product Prices.** Distributor shall pay the following prices for Products:

| SKU | Unit Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| LFS-SA4 | $4,850.00 |
| LFS-SA2 | $3,275.00 |
| LFS-LPR | $1,190.00 |
| LFS-MK | $340.00 |
| LFS-SP | $615.00 |

**3.2 Platform Services Fees.** Distributor shall pay $780.00 per stop-arm camera unit per year for Platform Services.

**3.3 Volume Pricing.** Once Distributor has purchased five hundred (500) stop-arm camera units in a contract year, the unit prices for LFS-SA4 and LFS-SA2 set out in Section 3.1 are reduced by four percent (4%) for all further units ordered in that contract year.

**3.4 Price Adjustments.** Prices are fixed for the Initial Term. Any adjustment for a Renewal Term is subject to Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

## 4. Territory

**4.1** The Territory is: Orange, Dutchess, Ulster, Putnam, Rockland and Westchester Counties, New York.

## 5. Term

**5.1 Initial Term.** The Initial Term is three (3) years from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date.

**5.2 Renewal Term.** Each Renewal Term is five (5) years.

**5.3** Renewal of the Agreement is governed by Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

## 6. Exclusivity

**6.1** Distributor will not sell competing stop-arm violation camera systems within the Territory.

**6.2** Supplier's appointment of Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products and Platform Services in the Territory is set out in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 of the Agreement.

## 7. Minimum Commitment

**7.1** The Minimum Commitment is 400 stop-arm camera units per contract year.

**7.2** Units count toward the Minimum Commitment for the contract year in which Supplier accepts the Purchase Order for them, regardless of when they are delivered.

**7.3** Any shortfall is addressed in accordance with Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

## 8. Ordering, Invoicing and Payment

**8.1 Minimum Order.** Each Purchase Order shall be for at least ten (10) stop-arm camera units, or for spare parts with an aggregate value of at least $2,500.00.

**8.2 Lead Time.** Supplier shall deliver Products within thirty (30) days after acceptance of a Purchase Order, unless the Purchase Order states a later delivery date.

**8.3 Invoicing.** Products are invoiced upon shipment. Platform Services are invoiced annually in advance for each stop-arm camera unit, with the first subscription year beginning on the date Distributor notifies Supplier that the unit has been installed and activated on the Platform, and in any event no later than sixty (60) days after delivery of the unit.

**8.4 Payment.** Payment terms are as set out in Section 5.3 of the Agreement. Payment shall be made by ACH transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

## 9. Warranty Labor Rates

**9.1** For the purposes of Section 11.1 of the Agreement, Supplier shall reimburse Distributor's labor for removal and reinstallation of non-conforming Products at $115.00 per technician hour, subject to a maximum of three (3) technician hours per stop-arm camera unit per warranty event.

**9.2** Distributor shall submit warranty labor claims to Supplier, with supporting work orders, within sixty (60) days after completing the work.
