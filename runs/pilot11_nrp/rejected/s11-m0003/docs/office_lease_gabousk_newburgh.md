# COMMERCIAL LEASE

**Office and Warehouse Space: 88 Route 17K, Newburgh, New York**

This Commercial Lease (this "**Lease**") is made as of October 15, 2024 (the "**Lease Date**") by and between:

**Seventeen K Commerce Park Associates, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**"); and

**Gabousk Transit Technologies, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Tenant**").

Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

## 1. Premises

**1.1 Demise.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, Suite 210 and Warehouse Bay C, 88 Route 17K, Newburgh, New York (the "**Premises**"). The Premises are located in the multi-tenant building and associated site known as Seventeen K Commerce Park (together, the "**Property**").

**1.2 Description.** The Premises consist of:

(a) **Suite 210**, approximately 3,150 rentable square feet of office space on the second floor of the main building (the "**Office Space**"); and

(b) **Warehouse Bay C**, approximately 6,400 rentable square feet of warehouse and light-assembly space in the rear service building, with one (1) dock-high loading door, one (1) drive-in door and a clear height of approximately twenty (20) feet (the "**Warehouse Space**").

The Premises total approximately 9,550 rentable square feet. The rentable areas stated above are agreed and will not be remeasured during the Term.

**1.3 Common Areas.** Tenant and its employees, contractors, customers and invitees may use, in common with other tenants, the lobbies, corridors, elevators, restrooms, driveways, truck court and other areas of the Property that Landlord designates for common use (the "**Common Areas**"), subject to the Rules and Regulations in Section 22.

**1.4 Parking.** Tenant may use twenty-two (22) unreserved parking spaces in the Property's parking areas and four (4) designated spaces for service vehicles adjacent to Warehouse Bay C, at no additional charge.

**1.5 Condition.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises broom clean, with the building systems serving the Premises in good working order and the roof watertight. Subject to Landlord's obligations in this Section 1.5 and Section 9.1, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date.

## 2. Term

**2.1 Term.** The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") begins on November 1, 2024 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ends on October 31, 2029 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless terminated earlier or extended in accordance with this Lease.

**2.2 Option to Extend.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**") on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the Extension Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Section 2.3. Tenant may exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given not less than nine (9) months and not more than fifteen (15) months before the Expiration Date. The option may be exercised only if Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period.

**2.3 Fair Market Rent.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay, and a willing landlord would accept, for comparable office and warehouse space in the Newburgh, New York area, for a comparable term. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's exercise notice, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the Orange County market. If the two brokers' determinations differ by five percent (5%) or less, Fair Market Rent will be their average. Otherwise, the two brokers shall appoint a third broker, who shall select whichever of the first two determinations is closer to the third broker's own view.

**3.3 Additional Rent.** All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent together are "**Rent**."

**3.4 Payment.** Rent is payable without demand, deduction or set-off, except as expressly provided in this Lease, by ACH transfer or check to the account or address Landlord designates in writing.

**3.5 Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within ten (10) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. This late charge will not be imposed more than once in any twelve (12) month period without Landlord first giving Tenant written notice of the late payment.

## 4. Operating Expenses and Taxes

**4.1 Tenant's Share.** "**Tenant's Share**" is 11.4%, which is the rentable area of the Premises divided by the rentable area of the Property (approximately 83,800 square feet).

**4.2 Operating Expenses.** Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the reasonable costs that Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, insure and manage the Property ("**Operating Expenses**"). Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital expenditures, except the annual amortized portion of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Lease Date;

(b) costs of leasing, brokerage commissions and tenant improvements for other tenants;

(c) principal, interest and other costs of financing;

(d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(e) costs arising from Landlord's negligence or violation of law; and

(f) management fees in excess of three percent (3%) of gross revenues of the Property.

Controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than insurance, utilities for the Common Areas and snow removal) will not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis.

**4.3 Taxes.** Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of all real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Property ("**Taxes**"). Assessments payable in installments will be included only to the extent of the installments due during the Term.

**4.4 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable estimate of Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes in monthly installments with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment will be credited against the next Rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receiving the statement.

**4.5 Audit.** Tenant may inspect Landlord's records supporting each annual statement within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving it. If the inspection shows that Tenant was overcharged by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall refund the overcharge and pay Tenant's reasonable inspection costs.

## 5. Security Deposit

**5.1 Amount.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit $20,691.66 with Landlord as security (the "**Security Deposit**").

**5.2 Holding and Application.** Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with New York General Obligations Law Section 7-103. If Tenant defaults beyond any applicable cure period, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure the default, and Tenant shall restore the Security Deposit within ten (10) days after written demand.

**5.3 Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Section 20.

## 6. Use

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Office Space for general office purposes. Tenant may use the Warehouse Space for warehousing, storage, light assembly, testing and staging of vehicle safety, camera and telematics equipment, and for related shipping and receiving (the "**Permitted Use**").

**6.2 Vehicles.** Tenant may bring customer vehicles, including school buses, onto the truck court adjacent to Warehouse Bay C on a temporary basis for equipment installation and testing, provided that no vehicle remains on the Property overnight without Landlord's prior consent and vehicles do not obstruct other tenants' access.

**6.3 Compliance.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Common Areas, the building structure and the base building systems.

**6.4 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Property any hazardous materials other than customary quantities of office and cleaning supplies, lubricants, batteries and other materials ordinarily used in the Permitted Use, all of which Tenant shall store, use and dispose of in compliance with law. Landlord represents that, to its knowledge, there are no hazardous materials at the Premises in violation of law as of the Lease Date.

## 7. Utilities and Services

**7.1 Utilities.** The Office Space is served by the building's heating, ventilation and air conditioning system. Electricity to the Premises and gas to the Warehouse Space are separately metered, and Tenant shall pay the provider directly.

**7.2 Landlord Services.** Landlord shall provide heating and air conditioning to the Office Space from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on business days, and at other times at Landlord's actual cost on reasonable advance request. Landlord shall also provide water, Common Area lighting, janitorial service to the Office Space on business days, snow and ice removal from the parking areas, driveways and truck court, and trash removal.

**7.3 Access.** Tenant shall have access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the building's security procedures.

**7.4 Interruption.** If an interruption of services caused by Landlord's negligence or failure to repair renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive business days, Rent will abate in proportion to the untenantable area from the sixth (6th) business day until the services are restored.

## 8. Alterations

**8.1 Consent.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic changes, or for non-structural alterations costing less than $25,000 in any one instance that do not affect the building systems.

**8.2 Warehouse Fit-Out.** Landlord consents to Tenant's installation in the Warehouse Space of workbenches, shelving and racking, a secured inventory cage, and additional 208-volt electrical circuits for equipment testing, provided the work is performed by licensed contractors in compliance with law.

**8.3 Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant may remove its trade fixtures and equipment and shall repair any damage caused by their removal. Tenant is not required to remove any alteration unless Landlord stated, when it gave its consent, that removal would be required.

## 9. Maintenance and Repair

**9.1 Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good condition, the roof, foundation, structural elements and exterior walls of the buildings, the base building plumbing, electrical, fire safety and heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems, the loading dock structure and the Common Areas. The cost of these items is included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 4.2.

**9.2 Tenant.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good, clean condition and shall repair damage caused by Tenant or its invitees, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall maintain the dock leveler and overhead doors serving Warehouse Bay C under a service contract with a qualified contractor.

## 10. Insurance

**10.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property, inventory and alterations at full replacement cost; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by New York law.

**10.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance covering the buildings at full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence.

**10.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other party for loss or damage to property to the extent covered by property insurance required to be maintained under this Lease.

**10.4 Certificates.** Tenant shall deliver a certificate of its insurance to Landlord before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

## 11. Indemnity

**11.1 By Tenant.** Subject to Section 10.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees or contractors, except to the extent caused by Landlord's negligence or willful misconduct.

**11.2 By Landlord.** Subject to Section 10.3, Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees or contractors, except to the extent caused by Tenant's negligence or willful misconduct.

## 12. Casualty

**12.1 Restoration.** If the Premises or access to them are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the base building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's alterations and personal property) with reasonable diligence. Rent will abate in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable until restoration is substantially complete.

**12.2 Termination.** If Landlord's architect reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than one hundred eighty (180) days from the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate. Either party may also terminate this Lease if a casualty occurs during the last twelve (12) months of the Term and restoration is estimated to take more than sixty (60) days.

## 13. Condemnation

If all of the Premises, or so much of them that Tenant cannot reasonably continue its business, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease ends on the date of taking. If part of the Premises is taken and this Lease does not end, Rent will be reduced in proportion to the area taken. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its trade fixtures and relocation costs, provided it does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 14. Assignment and Subletting

**14.1 Consent.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to a request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information it reasonably requires.

**14.2 Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, or to a successor by merger or by acquisition of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided that the successor's net worth is not less than Tenant's net worth immediately before the transaction.

**14.3 Continuing Liability.** Tenant remains liable under this Lease after any assignment or sublease, unless Landlord releases Tenant in writing.

**14.4 Landlord Transfer.** If Landlord transfers its interest in the Property, the transferee shall assume Landlord's obligations under this Lease arising after the transfer, including the obligation to return the Security Deposit, and Landlord is released from those obligations.

## 15. Subordination and Estoppel

**15.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any existing or future mortgage on the Property, provided that the mortgagee delivers a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as Tenant is not in default beyond any applicable cure period. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

**15.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other party's request, deliver a certificate confirming whether this Lease is in full force, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, the other party is in default.

## 16. Default and Remedies

**16.1 Tenant Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**16.2 Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises through summary proceedings. Landlord may also recover the Rent due through the date of termination, and damages equal to the Rent for the balance of the Term less the net rents Landlord receives on reletting. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**16.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or a shorter period that is reasonable in an emergency, Tenant may perform the obligation and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after receiving an invoice.

## 17. Quiet Enjoyment

So long as Tenant is not in default beyond any applicable cure period, Tenant may peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

## 18. Landlord's Access

Landlord may enter the Premises on at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, or without notice in an emergency, to inspect, make repairs or show the Premises to prospective purchasers, lenders and, during the last nine (9) months of the Term, prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures for the inventory cage and areas holding customer equipment.

## 19. Signage

Tenant may install, at its cost, its name on the building directory, at the entrance to Suite 210 and on a building-standard sign above the Warehouse Bay C entrance, and may have a panel on the Property's monument sign, all in accordance with the Rules and Regulations and applicable law.

## 20. Surrender and Holding Over

**20.1 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in the condition required by Section 9.2, subject to Section 8.3.

**20.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term, prorated per day, together with Additional Rent.

**24.3 Amendment.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**24.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

## Exhibit A – Rules and Regulations

1. Sidewalks, entrances, corridors and stairways shall not be obstructed or used for any purpose other than access.
2. Deliveries of freight to the Warehouse Space shall be made only through the truck court and loading doors serving Warehouse Bay C.
3. No vehicle may idle in the truck court for more than fifteen (15) minutes, consistent with applicable New York idling restrictions.
4. Tenant shall not overload any floor beyond its rated load, which for Warehouse Bay C is 250 pounds per square foot.
5. Smoking is prohibited inside the buildings and within twenty-five (25) feet of any entrance.
6. Tenant shall keep the doors to the Premises locked when the Premises are unoccupied and shall register with Landlord's managing agent the names of employees issued access cards.
7. Waste batteries, electronic components and similar materials shall be disposed of through a licensed recycler and not placed in the Property's general refuse containers.
