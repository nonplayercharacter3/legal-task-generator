# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of January 15, 2024 by **Storox Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Oak Point Avenue, Bronx, New York 10474 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Dratesk Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Commerce Park Drive, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and **Storox Linen Services, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Licensee**"), are entering into a Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2024 (as amended, supplemented, renewed or extended from time to time, and together with all Order Forms entered into under it, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its plant-management and linen-tracking software to Licensee.

B. Guarantor owns, directly or indirectly, all of the membership interests in Licensee. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement.

C. Section 14.1 of the Agreement requires Licensee to cause Guarantor to execute and deliver this Guaranty on the Effective Date. Licensor would not enter into the Agreement without this Guaranty.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Licensee to Licensor under or in connection with the Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, and whether absolute or contingent, liquidated or unliquidated. This includes the payment of Fees, interest, true-up fees and audit costs under Section 4.5 of the Agreement, amounts payable under Article 10 of the Agreement, and damages for breach. It also includes the performance of every other covenant and obligation of Licensee under the Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Licensor the full and punctual payment and performance of all Guaranteed Obligations when due, whether at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise. This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not merely of collection.

2.2 **Amount.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is not limited in amount. It extends to the full amount of the Guaranteed Obligations, together with the costs described in Section 2.4.

2.3 **Demand.** If Licensee fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay or perform it within ten (10) business days after receiving Licensor's written demand. Licensor is not required first to make demand on Licensee, to pursue any remedy against Licensee or any other person, or to enforce any security.

2.4 **Costs of Enforcement.** Guarantor shall reimburse Licensor on demand for all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Licensor incurs in enforcing this Guaranty.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full. It applies to the Agreement as renewed for any Renewal Term and to every Order Form entered into under the Agreement.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not Guarantor has notice of it or has consented to it:

(a) any amendment, modification, supplement, renewal or extension of the Agreement or any Order Form, including any increase in Fees or in the scope of the license;

(b) any waiver, consent, forbearance or extension of time granted by Licensor to Licensee;

(c) any change in the ownership, organization or structure of Licensee or Guarantor;

(d) the insolvency, bankruptcy, dissolution or reorganization of Licensee, or any stay or discharge of Licensee's obligations in any such proceeding;

(e) any invalidity or unenforceability of any Guaranteed Obligation against Licensee; or

(f) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable discharge of a guarantor or surety, other than payment and performance in full of the Guaranteed Obligations.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment made on a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Licensor in any insolvency or similar proceeding, this Guaranty will be reinstated as to that payment as though it had not been made.

## 4. DEFENSES AND WAIVERS

4.1 **Waiver of Notices.** Guarantor waives notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, presentment, demand for payment (other than the demand required by Section 2.3), protest, notice of dishonor, and notice of any default by Licensee.

4.2 **Licensee Defenses.** Guarantor may assert any defense to payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation that Licensee could assert under the terms of the Agreement. This excludes defenses arising from Licensee's insolvency or lack of capacity or authority.

4.3 **Subrogation.** Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Licensee arising from payment under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid in full.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Licensor that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) this Guaranty constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally; and

(d) its execution and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement to which it is a party.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Financial Information.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Guarantor, Guarantor shall deliver to Licensor on request its annual financial statements for that year. Licensor shall treat those statements as Confidential Information of Licensee under Article 13 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Ownership.** Guarantor shall notify Licensor in writing within thirty (30) days after Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the membership interests in Licensee. That change of ownership does not release Guarantor from any Guaranteed Obligation arising before or after it.
