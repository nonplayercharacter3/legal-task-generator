## 3. Compliance With Section 12.1

3.1 The enclosed certificates evidence that Dratesk maintains:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, as required by Section 12.1(a);

(b) technology errors and omissions insurance with limits of not less than $3,000,000 per claim, as required by Section 12.1(b);

(c) cyber liability insurance covering network security and privacy liability, data breach response costs and regulatory proceedings, with a minimum limit of $5,000,000 per claim, as required by Section 12.1(c); and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, as required by Section 12.1(d).

## 4. Renewals and Changes in Coverage

4.1 Under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, Dratesk will deliver updated certificates to Storox on each renewal of the required policies.

4.2 Dratesk will give Storox at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction of any required coverage, as Section 12.2 of the Agreement requires.
