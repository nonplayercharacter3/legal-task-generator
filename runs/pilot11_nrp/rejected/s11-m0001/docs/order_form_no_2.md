## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to the Agreement. Order Form No. 1, dated January 15, 2024, is the initial Order Form under the Agreement and covers the Plant Manager platform.

B. Licensee wishes to license Licensor's Linen RFID Tracking Module to read, record and reconcile RFID-tagged linen at soil intake, sortation, clean-side packout and dispatch. Licensor is willing to license that module and perform the related implementation services on the terms of the Agreement and this Order Form.

C. The Initial Term of the Agreement expires on January 14, 2027. Licensee intends to transition its plant-management and linen-tracking operations to a replacement platform when the Initial Term expires. Licensee will deliver notice of non-renewal of the Agreement to Licensor. Licensee is licensing the Linen RFID Tracking Module to support the RFID tagging programs required by its hospital customers until that transition.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 This Order Form is entered into under, and is governed by, the Agreement. It is incorporated into the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

## 2. SOFTWARE ORDERED

2.1 **Module.** Licensor grants Licensee a license to the following Software module under Section 2.1 of the Agreement:

| Item | Description | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| RFID-CORE | Linen RFID Tracking Module (server license), including tag commissioning, read-event processing and loss/shrinkage reporting | 1 enterprise instance |
| RFID-STN | Fixed-portal and tunnel reader station licenses | 14 stations |
| RFID-HH | Handheld reader licenses | 20 devices |
| RFID-USR | Named Authorized User licenses for the RFID module | 40 users |

2.2 **Integration.** The Linen RFID Tracking Module will be integrated with Licensee's existing instance of the Plant Manager platform licensed under Order Form No. 1. Linen read events will be posted to the Plant Manager production, route and customer inventory records.

2.3 **Documentation.** Licensor shall deliver the Documentation for the Linen RFID Tracking Module at the time of installation.

## 3. LICENSED FACILITIES

3.1 For purposes of the Agreement and all Order Forms, "**Licensed Facilities**" means the following facilities:

| Facility | Address | RFID Stations | Handheld Devices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hunts Point Plant | 1200 Oak Point Avenue, Bronx, New York 10474 | 8 | 12 |
| Mount Vernon Plant | 55 South Fulton Avenue, Mount Vernon, New York 10550 | 6 | 8 |

3.2 Reader stations and handheld devices licensed under this Order Form may be reallocated between the Licensed Facilities on ten (10) business days' written notice to Licensor. The total quantities in Section 2.1 may not be exceeded.

## 4. FEES

4.1 **Fees.** Licensee shall pay the following Fees:

| Fee | Amount | Invoicing |
|---|---|---|
| Linen RFID Tracking Module annual license fee (RFID-CORE, RFID-STN, RFID-HH, RFID-USR) | $96,000 per year | Annually in advance; first invoice on execution of this Order Form |
| Annual Support Services fee (RFID module) | $19,200 per year | Annually in advance with the license fee |
| Implementation, configuration and training services | $42,500 (fixed fee) | On execution of this Order Form |

4.2 **Proration.** The license and Support Services fees for the period from the Order Form Date to January 14, 2027 will be prorated on a daily basis and invoiced on execution of this Order Form. After that, the annual fees will be invoiced on each anniversary of the Effective Date under Section 4.1 of the Agreement.

4.3 **Payment.** Payment terms, late interest and taxes are as stated in Sections 4.2 and 4.3 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Fees Under Order Form No. 1.** The Fees for the Plant Manager platform remain as stated in Order Form No. 1. This Order Form does not change them.

## 5. IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

5.1 **Scope.** Licensor shall perform the following implementation services under Section 5.2 of the Agreement:

(a) installation and configuration of the Linen RFID Tracking Module server components in Licensee's hosting environment;

(b) configuration and calibration of the reader stations and handheld devices at each Licensed Facility listed in Section 3.1;

(c) set-up of the integration with the Plant Manager platform described in Section 2.2;

(d) migration of Licensee's existing tag-to-item assignment data, up to 450,000 tag records; and

(e) two (2) days of on-site administrator training and one (1) day of on-site end-user training at each Licensed Facility listed in Section 3.1.

5.2 **Schedule.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the implementation services within forty-five (45) days after the Order Form Date. This schedule depends on Licensee providing site access and installing the hardware described in Section 6.

5.3 **Acceptance.** The Linen RFID Tracking Module is deemed installed at a Licensed Facility when Licensor notifies Licensee that read-event data from that facility is posting to the Plant Manager platform. The performance warranty in Section 8.1 of the Agreement runs from that installation date.

## 6. HARDWARE

6.1 Under Section 5.4 of the Agreement, Licensee is responsible for procuring, installing and maintaining the RFID reader hardware, antennas, tunnel enclosures, handheld devices, RFID tags and network infrastructure. All of these must meet the minimum specifications in the Documentation.

6.2 Licensor is not supplying hardware under this Order Form.

## 7. TERM

7.1 The license to the Linen RFID Tracking Module and the related Support Services begin on the Order Form Date. Under Sections 2.2 and 3.3 of the Agreement, they are co-terminous with the Term of the Agreement.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 Except as set out in this Order Form, the Agreement and Order Form No. 1 remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

8.2 This Order Form may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
