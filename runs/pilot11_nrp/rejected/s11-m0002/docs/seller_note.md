# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$9,000,000**
**Closing Date: ____________, 2026**
**Dover, Delaware**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its successors and permitted assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal sum and interest set out in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**"), on the terms below.

This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026, between the Maker, as Buyer, and the Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). It is delivered at the Closing under Section 2.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement as part of the Purchase Price. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal

The principal amount of this Note is $9,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Maker shall pay the Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, in lawful money of the United States of America by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account the Holder designates in writing to the Maker.

## 2. Maturity

The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

## 3. Interest and Payments

3.1 **Rate.** The outstanding Principal Amount bears interest from the Closing Date until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

3.2 **Interest Payments.** The Maker shall pay accrued interest quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date after the Closing Date, and on the Maturity Date.

3.3 **Principal Payments.** No principal is payable before the Maturity Date, except on prepayment under Section 5 or acceleration under Section 6.

3.4 **Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the outstanding Principal Amount bears interest at a rate of two percent (2.0%) per annum above the rate in Section 3.1.

3.5 **Application.** Payments are applied first to accrued interest and then to the Principal Amount.

## 4. Set-Off

4.1 **Right of Set-Off.** The Maker may set off against any payment of principal or interest due under this Note the amount of any Losses for which the Holder is obligated to indemnify the Maker or any other Buyer indemnified party under Article 10 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.2 **Losses.** For purposes of this Note, "**Losses**" means losses, liabilities, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees for which the Holder is liable under Article 10 of the Purchase Agreement, in each case as finally adjudicated by a court of competent jurisdiction in a judgment that is final and non-appealable.

4.3 **Notice.** The Maker shall give the Holder written notice of any set-off at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date on which the set-off is applied. The notice shall state the amount set off and describe, in reasonable detail, the judgment on which the set-off is based.

4.4 **Effect.** Any amount set off under this Section 4 reduces the amounts then due under this Note, first against accrued interest and then against the Principal Amount, and is treated as paid for all purposes of this Note.

## 5. Prepayment

The Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five (5) Business Days' prior written notice to the Holder. Each prepayment shall include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid.

## 6. Events of Default

6.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) days after it becomes due, including by withholding any amount from a payment otherwise than as permitted by Section 4;

(b) the Maker breaches any other covenant in this Note and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice from the Holder;

(c) the Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due, or commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law;

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against the Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(e) the Maker sells, leases or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of its assets, other than to an Affiliate that assumes this Note in writing or to the Senior Lenders in connection with an enforcement of the Senior Debt.

6.2 **Remedies.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the Holder may, subject to Section 7, declare by written notice to the Maker the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. Upon an Event of Default under Section 6.1(c) or 6.1(d), those amounts become due and payable automatically, without notice. The Holder may then exercise all rights and remedies available under this Note and applicable law.

6.3 **Collection Costs.** The Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Holder incurs in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 7. Subordination

7.1 **Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all indebtedness of the Maker under its senior acquisition financing (the "**Senior Debt**," and the lenders under it, the "**Senior Lenders**"), on the terms of any subordination agreement entered into between the Holder and the Senior Lenders (the "**Subordination Agreement**").

7.2 **Payment Blockage.** If a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under the Senior Debt, the Maker shall not make, and the Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note except as the Subordination Agreement permits. Any payment the Holder receives in violation of this Section 7 shall be held in trust for, and promptly paid over to, the Senior Lenders.

7.3 **Enforcement Standstill.** The Holder shall not accelerate this Note or take any enforcement action against the Maker or its assets except as the Subordination Agreement permits.

7.4 **No Impairment.** As between the Maker and the Holder, nothing in this Section 7 impairs the Maker's obligation to pay this Note in accordance with its terms.

## 8. Transfer

The Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of the Maker, except to an Affiliate of the Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note and the Subordination Agreement. The Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the Holder's prior written consent, except that the Maker may collaterally assign its rights under this Note to the Senior Lenders.

## 9. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each of the Maker and the Holder irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of New Jersey, Mercer County, for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note, including any action to collect amounts due under this Note or relating to any set-off under Section 4. Each of them waives any objection to venue in that court and any claim that the court is an inconvenient forum.

9.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** THE MAKER AND THE HOLDER EACH WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Waivers by Maker.** The Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest, except for the notices expressly required by this Note.

10.2 **No Waiver by Holder.** No delay or omission by the Holder in exercising any right under this Note waives that right. A waiver on one occasion does not waive the right on any other occasion.

10.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 12.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

10.4 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder.

10.5 **Usury Savings.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to the Maker.

10.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

10.7 **Successors.** This Note binds the Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and benefits the Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

10.8 **Cancellation.** When all amounts due under this Note have been paid, or set off under Section 4, in full, the Holder shall surrender this Note to the Maker marked "paid in full."

10.9 **Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed by electronic signature, which has the same effect as an original signature.
