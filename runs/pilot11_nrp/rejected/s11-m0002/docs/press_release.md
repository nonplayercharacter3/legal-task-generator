# Fatas Remarketing Group to Acquire Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Dover, Delaware, March 16, 2026.** Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC ("**Fatas**") and Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc. ("**Mapeirt**") today jointly announced that they have signed a definitive Asset Purchase Agreement. Under that agreement, Fatas will acquire substantially all of the assets of Mapeirt's wholesale used-vehicle auction business.

## 1. Transaction Overview

Fatas will acquire substantially all of the assets used in Mapeirt's business for an aggregate purchase price of $48,500,000. The acquired assets include:

- Mapeirt's in-lane auction operations at the Dover Yard, 1400 Leipsic Road, Dover, Delaware, which Mapeirt leases from Kestrow Land Partners LLC;
- Mapeirt's online simulcast bidding platform; and
- Mapeirt's consignor and dealer relationships.

The purchase price will be paid at closing in two parts:

- $39,500,000 in cash; and
- $9,000,000 by a subordinated promissory note issued by Fatas to Mapeirt, which matures 36 months after the closing date.

Fatas is acquiring the business's assets and assuming only specified liabilities. Mapeirt will retain the other liabilities of the business.

## 2. Strategic Rationale

The transaction gives Fatas an established wholesale auction presence in the Mid-Atlantic region. Mapeirt's simulcast platform lets dealers bid remotely on vehicles running through the Dover lanes. Fatas expects that platform to broaden access to its remarketing services for its dealer, fleet and lender consignors.

"Mapeirt has built a trusted auction with a loyal dealer base and a simulcast platform that works," said the Chief Executive Officer of Fatas Remarketing Group. "We are excited to welcome Mapeirt's consignors, buyers and team, and to invest in the Dover operation for the long term."

"This agreement gives our customers and employees a strong partner with the resources to grow the auction," said the President and Chief Executive Officer of Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions. "We are confident Fatas will build on what we have created in Dover."

## 3. Customers and Employees

Weekly sales will continue at the Dover Yard, and online bidding will continue through the simulcast platform, in the ordinary course before closing. Fatas intends to offer employment to employees of the business, effective as of closing. Consignors and buyers will receive further information about the transition before closing.

## 4. Timing and Approvals

The parties expect the transaction to close in the second quarter of 2026. Closing is subject to customary closing conditions, including the transfer or reissuance of Mapeirt's Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license by the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles.

## 5. About Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC

Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company. It provides wholesale vehicle remarketing services to dealers, fleet operators and lenders.

## 6. About Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.

Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc. is a Delaware corporation. It operates a wholesale used-vehicle auction at the Dover Yard in Dover, Delaware, and an online simulcast bidding platform that serves licensed dealers.
