**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Substantially All of the Assets of Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which **Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of **Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Seller uses those assets to operate its wholesale used-vehicle auction business at the Dover Yard and its online simulcast bidding platform (the "**Business**"). The proposed acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**."

Except as provided in Paragraph 12, this Letter is non-binding. It is intended only as a basis for negotiating a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Structure

Buyer, or an Affiliate of Buyer, will acquire substantially all of the assets used in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens to be agreed. The Purchased Assets will include:

(a) equipment, lane fixtures and other tangible personal property;

(b) the simulcast bidding platform and all related software, source code, domain names and data;

(c) customer, consignor and dealer lists and records;

(d) transferable permits and licenses, including Seller's Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license;

(e) goodwill; and

(f) the contracts that Buyer selects, including Seller's lease of the Dover Yard, 1400 Leipsic Road, Dover, Delaware, from Kestrow Land Partners LLC (the "**Dover Yard Lease**").

Cash, Tax refunds for pre-closing periods and Seller's corporate records will be excluded.

## 2. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets will be $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). It will be payable at closing as follows:

(a) $39,500,000 in cash by wire transfer of immediately available funds; and

(b) $9,000,000 by delivery of a subordinated promissory note made by Buyer in favor of Seller (the "**Seller Note**").

The Purchase Price assumes that the Business is delivered with normal levels of working capital and without indebtedness. The Purchase Price will be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## 3. Seller Note

The Seller Note will:

(a) bear interest at a fixed rate of 6.0% per annum, payable quarterly in arrears;

(b) mature 36 months after the closing date, with no amortization of principal before maturity;

(c) be prepayable at any time without premium or penalty;

(d) be subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing on terms acceptable to Buyer's senior lenders; and

(e) be subject to Buyer's right to set off against principal and interest the amount of any indemnifiable losses claimed in good faith under the Definitive Agreement.

## 4. Liabilities

Buyer will assume only the obligations arising after closing under the contracts it assumes, and any other liabilities it expressly agrees in the Definitive Agreement to assume. Seller will retain all other liabilities of the Business. The retained liabilities include:

(a) Taxes for pre-closing periods;

(b) all indebtedness, including Seller's floor-plan financing;

(c) amounts owed to consignors and buyers for auctions conducted before closing;

(d) all liabilities arising from vehicles sold at Seller's auctions before closing, including title defects, odometer misstatements and buy-back or rescission claims; and

(e) pre-closing employee liabilities.

## 5. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for a transaction of this type. The representations and warranties will specifically cover title to assets, vehicle sales compliance (including federal odometer disclosure requirements and state title laws), licenses, material contracts, real property, the simulcast platform, financial statements and Taxes.

The representations, warranties and covenants, and the related indemnification obligations, will survive for 18 months after Closing.

Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of representations and warranties will be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000). The cap will be subject to a deductible-style threshold of 0.5% of the Purchase Price, which will convert to first-dollar recovery once exceeded. The cap and threshold will not apply to fraud or to liabilities retained by Seller under Paragraph 4. Buyer may recover indemnifiable losses by set-off against the Seller Note as described in Paragraph 3(e).

## 6. Conditions to Closing

Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) completion of Buyer's due diligence to its satisfaction;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(c) transfer or reissuance to Buyer of the Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license by the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles;

(d) release of the floor-plan lender's liens on the Purchased Assets;

(e) receipt of the consent of Kestrow Land Partners LLC to the assignment of the Dover Yard Lease to Buyer;

(f) receipt of all other material third-party consents;

(g) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business; and

(h) closing of Buyer's senior acquisition financing.

## 7. Restrictive Covenants

At closing, Seller will agree not to operate a wholesale vehicle auction or online vehicle bidding platform within 200 miles of the Dover Yard for five years after closing.

## 8. Employees

Buyer intends to offer employment to substantially all of the employees of the Business on terms that Buyer determines. Seller will remain responsible for all compensation and benefits accrued through closing.

## 9. Due Diligence and Access

Seller will give Buyer and its advisers reasonable access to the Dover Yard, the simulcast platform, and the books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business. Seller will cooperate with Buyer's due diligence investigation.

## 10. Timing

The parties intend to execute the Definitive Agreement by March 31, 2026 and to close as promptly as practicable after the closing conditions are satisfied.

## 11. Expenses

Each party will bear its own expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only this Paragraph 12 and Paragraphs 11, 13, 14 and 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding. No other provision of this Letter creates any binding obligation. Neither party will have any obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered.

## 13. Exclusivity

For 45 days after Seller countersigns this Letter, Seller will not solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer concerning a sale of the Business, a sale of any material portion of its assets or a sale of equity in Seller. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any such proposal it receives.

## 14. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential the terms of this Letter and all information received from the other party in connection with the Transaction, except as required by law. Neither party will issue any public announcement about the Transaction without the other party's prior written approval.

## 15. Governing Law; Termination

The Binding Provisions are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. This Letter will terminate on the earlier of (a) execution of the Definitive Agreement and (b) written notice of termination by either party. Paragraphs 11 and 14 survive any termination.

If these terms are acceptable, please sign and return a copy of this Letter.
