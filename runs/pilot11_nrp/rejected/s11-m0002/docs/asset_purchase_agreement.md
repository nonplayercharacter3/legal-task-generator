# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a wholesale used-vehicle auction business. It conducts in-lane auctions at the Dover Yard and offers online simulcast bidding through its proprietary platform (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business. Buyer will assume only the Assumed Liabilities. Each of the foregoing is on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with another Person.

"**Ancillary Documents**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment, the Seller Note and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1(c).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Dover Yard**" means the auction facility described in Section 4.7(b).

"**Dover Yard Lease**" means the lease described in Section 4.7(b), together with all amendments to it.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim or other encumbrance of any kind.

"**Floor Plan Lender**" means the lender under Seller's dealer floor-plan financing facility listed in Section 4.7(a) of the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud by a Party with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in this Agreement.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local government, or any agency, court or regulatory body of any such government.

"**Losses**" means losses, liabilities, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees incurred or reasonably expected to be incurred.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Required Consents**" has the meaning given in Section 8.2(c).

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $9,000,000 made by Buyer in favor of Seller and delivered at Closing under Section 2.5(b).

"**Simulcast Platform**" means Seller's online simulcast bidding platform, including its software, source code, domain names, user accounts and data.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign taxes, assessments and governmental charges of any kind, together with any interest and penalties on them.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all equipment, vehicles used in operations, lane fixtures, block equipment, reconditioning equipment, furniture and other tangible personal property used in the Business;

(b) the Simulcast Platform and all other intellectual property used in the Business;

(c) the contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Dover Yard Lease, the consignor and buyer registration agreements and the transport vendor agreements (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all customer, consignor and dealer lists, files and records relating to the Business;

(e) to the extent transferable, all permits and licenses held for the operation of the Business, including Seller's Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license;

(f) all prepaid expenses, deposits and credits relating to the Assigned Contracts; and

(g) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) cash and cash equivalents, (b) Seller's corporate records and minute books, (c) the contracts not listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, (d) all Tax refunds relating to periods ending before the Closing Date and (e) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the following liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) obligations under the Assigned Contracts arising and to be performed after the Closing, other than any obligation arising from a breach by Seller before the Closing; and (b) the liabilities expressly listed in Section 2.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and pay when due, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all liabilities for Taxes of Seller, and all Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(b) all liabilities under the Floor Plan Lender facility and all other indebtedness of Seller;

(c) all liabilities to consignors for auction proceeds, and all liabilities to buyers for deposits or refunds, in each case arising from auctions conducted before the Closing;

(d) all liabilities arising from vehicles sold at Seller's auctions before the Closing, including title defects, odometer misstatements, undisclosed damage or salvage history, and arbitration, rescission or buy-back claims;

(e) all liabilities relating to employees or former employees of Seller arising before the Closing; and

(f) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets.

**2.5 Purchase Price.**

(a) The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

(b) Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows: (i) $39,500,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and (ii) $9,000,000 by execution and delivery of the Seller Note. The Seller Note matures 36 months after the Closing Date, is subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing and is subject to set-off under Section 10.7.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and a schedule prepared by Buyer within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. Seller may comment on that schedule, and Buyer shall consider Seller's comments in good faith.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article 8 are satisfied or waived, other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing, or on any other date the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale;

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts;

(c) an assignment of the intellectual property included in the Purchased Assets;

(d) the Required Consents;

(e) payoff letters and lien releases for all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances; and

(f) the certificate required by Section 8.2(d).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price;

(b) the executed Seller Note;

(c) counterparts of the Ancillary Documents to which Buyer is a party; and

(d) the certificate required by Section 8.3(c).

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that the statements in this Article 4 are true and correct as of the Effective Date and as of the Closing Date, except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller on the Effective Date (the "**Disclosure Schedule**").

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to carry on the Business.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents. This Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth in Section 4.3 or Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule, the execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller does not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents or any applicable law, (b) require any consent of or notice to any Person or (c) conflict with, breach or give rise to a right of termination under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** The audited financial statements of Seller for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 fairly present the financial position and results of operations of the Business in all material respects, in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, Seller has operated the Business in the ordinary course, and there has been no material adverse effect on the Business.

**4.7 Material Contracts; Real Property.**

(a) Section 4.7(a) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract material to the Business, including the Floor Plan Lender facility. Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

(b) Seller conducts the Business primarily from the Dover Yard, 1400 Leipsic Road, Dover, Delaware, leased from Kestrow Land Partners LLC under the Dover Yard Lease. Seller does not own any real property. The Dover Yard Lease requires the prior written consent of Kestrow Land Partners LLC, as landlord, to any assignment of the Dover Yard Lease and to any change of control of the tenant. Seller has delivered a true and complete copy of the Dover Yard Lease to Buyer.

**4.8 Licenses and Permits.** Seller holds all permits and licenses required to operate the Business, including a Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license issued by the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles. All such licenses are in full force and effect.

**4.9 Vehicle Sales Compliance.** Seller has conducted all auctions in compliance in all material respects with applicable law, including the federal odometer disclosure requirements under 49 U.S.C. Chapter 327 and its implementing regulations, and applicable state title and dealer laws.

**4.10 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.11 Simulcast Platform.** Seller owns the Simulcast Platform free of any claim of infringement. To Seller's knowledge, the Simulcast Platform has not suffered any material security breach.

**4.12 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed and has paid all Taxes shown as due on those returns.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker is entitled to any fee from Buyer in connection with this transaction based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. Buyer has full power and authority to execute and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Buyer does not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any applicable law.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** At the Closing, Buyer will have sufficient funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker is entitled to any fee from Seller based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall:

(a) operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice;

(b) maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition; and

(c) not amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract without Buyer's prior written consent.

**6.2 Access.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Dover Yard, the Simulcast Platform and the books and records of the Business.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** From the Effective Date until the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, all consents and approvals of third parties required in connection with the assignment of the Assigned Contracts. Seller shall not be required to pay any fee or other consideration to obtain any such consent. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller in seeking those consents.

**6.5 Regulatory Filings.** Each Party shall promptly make all filings required with the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles, and with any other Governmental Authority, to transfer or reissue the licenses needed to operate the Business.

**6.6 Employees.** Buyer may offer employment to any employee of the Business, effective as of the Closing. Seller shall pay all wages, accrued vacation and other compensation owed to its employees through the Closing Date.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business and the Purchased Assets, except as required by law.

**6.8 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, operate a wholesale vehicle auction or online vehicle bidding platform within two hundred (200) miles of the Dover Yard.

**6.9 Public Announcements.** Neither Party shall issue any press release about this Agreement without the other Party's prior written approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**6.10 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute such further instruments as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 8. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligation of each Party to close is subject to the following conditions:

(a) no Governmental Authority has issued any order restraining or prohibiting the Closing; and

(b) no action is pending that seeks to enjoin the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**8.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of each of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article 4 are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing;

(c) the consents and approvals required as conditions to the Closing (the "**Required Consents**") shall have been obtained and delivered to Buyer, and the Required Consents shall consist solely of (i) the transfer to Buyer, or reissuance to Buyer, of the Delaware motor vehicle auction dealer license for the Business by the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles, and (ii) the written consent of the Floor Plan Lender to the release of its Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets;

(d) Buyer has received a certificate of an officer of Seller confirming the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.2(a) and 8.2(b);

(e) no material adverse effect on the Business has occurred since the Effective Date; and

(f) Seller has delivered each item listed in Section 3.2.

**8.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of each of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article 5 are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Seller has received a certificate of an officer of Buyer confirming the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.3(a) and 8.3(b), together with each item listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE 9. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing in any of the following ways:

(a) by mutual written agreement of the Parties;

(b) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, unless the failure to close results from that Party's breach;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within fifteen (15) days after notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within fifteen (15) days after notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void, except that this Section 9.2, Section 6.7 and Article 12 survive. Termination does not relieve any Party of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**10.1 Survival.** The representations, warranties, covenants and agreements of the Parties in this Agreement, and all indemnification obligations under Section 10.2 and Section 10.3 (including the indemnification for Excluded Liabilities), shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months following the Closing Date. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable detail before that date shall survive until it is finally resolved.

**10.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, officers, managers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**10.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**10.4 Procedures.**

(a) An indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim to the indemnifying Party. Any delay relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the delay.

(b) The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified Party's consent, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

**10.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 10.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000).

(b) The limitation in Section 10.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud or from any Excluded Liability.

(c) Seller shall not be liable under Section 10.2(a) until the aggregate Losses exceed $242,500. Once that threshold is reached, Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.

(d) The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 10.3(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000).

**10.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except in the case of Fraud and for claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article 10 is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**10.7 Set-Off.** Buyer may set off the amount of any Losses claimed in good faith by Buyer under this Article 10 against any amount of principal or interest due under the Seller Note. Buyer shall give Seller written notice of the claim and the amount withheld. Buyer shall pay any withheld amount to Seller, or retain it, according to the final resolution of the claim. The exercise of this right in good faith shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note.

**10.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE 11. TAX MATTERS

**11.1 Transfer Taxes.** Seller and Buyer shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of all transfer, documentary, sales and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets.

**11.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic Taxes for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer on a daily basis.

Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a Party designates by notice.
