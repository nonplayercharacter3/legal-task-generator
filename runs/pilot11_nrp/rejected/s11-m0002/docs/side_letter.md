Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") refers to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), between **Fatas Remarketing Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Mapeirt Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

Under Section 12.3 of the Agreement, Buyer and Seller agree as follows:

## 1. Amendment of the Agreement

This Side Letter is a written instrument signed by both Parties. It amends the Agreement under Section 12.3 and expressly references Section 12.12 of the Agreement.

## 2. Disclosure Schedule

(a) Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule is supplemented by adding the transport vendor agreements listed in Annex A to this Side Letter. Each of those agreements is an Assigned Contract.

(b) Seller confirms that the contracts listed in Annex A are included in the list of material contracts in Section 4.7(a) of the Disclosure Schedule. Seller has delivered true and complete copies of those contracts to Buyer.

## 3. Limitations on Indemnification

The Parties clarify the application of Section 10.5(b) of the Agreement. The reference in Section 10.5(b) to "any Excluded Liability" applies solely to Excluded Liabilities constituting Taxes, as described in Section 2.4(a) of the Agreement. Section 10.5(b) of the Agreement is amended to read in full as follows:

> "(b) The limitation in Section 10.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud or from any Excluded Liability constituting Taxes under Section 2.4(a)."

## 4. Purchase Price Allocation

Under Section 2.6 of the Agreement, Seller shall deliver any comments on Buyer's allocation schedule within thirty (30) days after Seller receives that schedule. Buyer shall consider in good faith any comments Seller delivers within that period.

## 5. Notices

For purposes of Section 12.1 of the Agreement, a copy of any notice to Seller shall also be sent by email to Seller's President and Chief Executive Officer at the email address shown on Seller's signature page to the Agreement.

## 6. Effect

Except as expressly amended by this Side Letter, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From and after the date of this Side Letter, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" includes this Side Letter.

## 7. Governing Law; Forum

Sections 12.8, 12.9 and 12.10 of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter, *mutatis mutandis*.

## 8. Counterparts

This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
