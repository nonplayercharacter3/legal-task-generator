# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**"), dated as of February 9, 2026, by and between **Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## Recitals

A. Seller is engaged in the business of breeding, developing, producing and selling hybrid tomato and pepper seed, including the maintenance of the related germplasm, parent and inbred lines, breeding records and marker technologies (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below:

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement, the Seller Note and each other agreement, instrument or certificate that Seller and Buyer deliver to each other under this Agreement.

"**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Closing Seed Inventory Statement**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4(a).

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule that Seller delivered to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.9 and 4.15.

"**Germplasm**" means all hybrid tomato and pepper genetic material owned or controlled by Seller and used or held for use in the Business. This includes parent lines, inbred lines, breeding populations, accessions, tissue and DNA samples, and seed held for breeding purposes.

"**Inventory Dispute Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4(b).

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement between Seller, as assignor, and Buyer, as assignee, delivered at Closing.

"**Key Employee**" means Dr. Ilse Varnholt, Seller's Head of Vegetable Breeding.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Personnel**" means Seller's current and former employees, officers, consultants and independent contractors.

"**Pre-Closing Inventions**" means all inventions, marker panels and breeding methods conceived by Seller or its Personnel prior to the Closing Date that relate to the Business, whether or not reduced to practice, patented or registered. This includes the TY-Shield marker panel for Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus resistance.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

"**Salinas Lease**" means the lease of the breeding station in Salinas, California, described in Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Seed Inventory**" means all finished, unfinished and stock seed of the Business, wherever located, including seed held by contract growers or in third-party storage.

"**Seller Note**" means the promissory note in the principal amount of $8,500,000 that Buyer issues to Seller at Closing under Section 2.3(b).

"**Target Inventory Value**" means $6,200,000.

"**Transferred Assets**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Transferred IP**" means all intellectual property rights owned or purported to be owned by Seller that are used or held for use in the Business. This includes the Germplasm, the Pre-Closing Inventions, plant variety protection certificates and applications, patents and patent applications, trade secrets, know-how, breeding records, marker data, variety names and trademarks.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation". References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Transferred Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens as defined in the Disclosure Schedule (collectively, the "**Transferred Assets**"):

(a) the Germplasm, including all parent and inbred lines of the hybrid varieties listed in Section 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(b) the Transferred IP;

(c) the Pre-Closing Inventions, together with all rights to sue for past, present and future infringement or misappropriation of them;

(d) all breeding records, trial data, pedigree records, laboratory notebooks, marker data and phenotypic databases of the Business;

(e) the Seed Inventory;

(f) the contracts listed in Section 2.1(f) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Salinas Lease (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(g) all equipment, greenhouse fixtures, cold storage and laboratory equipment located at the Salinas breeding station; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Liabilities.**

(a) At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (i) liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing, excluding any liability arising from a breach before the Closing, and (ii) the liabilities listed in Section 2.2(a) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

(b) Buyer does not assume any other liability of Seller, and Seller retains all such liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). These include all liabilities for seed sold or delivered by Seller before the Closing, grower claims relating to such seed, and Taxes of Seller.

**2.3 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Transferred Assets is $48,500,000, of which $8,500,000 is paid by the Seller Note (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Section 2.4. The Purchase Price is payable as follows:

(a) $40,000,000 in cash at Closing, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Seller designates in writing; and

(b) $8,500,000 by Buyer's issuance and delivery of the Seller Note at Closing.

**2.4 Inventory Adjustment.**

(a) Within 15 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Seed Inventory Statement**") setting out the quantity, germination rate and value of the Seed Inventory as of the Closing. Seller shall prepare the statement in accordance with the valuation principles in Section 2.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

(b) If Buyer disagrees with the Closing Seed Inventory Statement, Buyer may deliver to Seller a written notice (an "**Inventory Dispute Notice**") within 45 days after the Closing Date. The notice must state in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the dispute. If Buyer does not deliver an Inventory Dispute Notice within that period, the Closing Seed Inventory Statement shall be final and binding on the parties for all purposes of this Agreement.

(c) If Buyer timely delivers an Inventory Dispute Notice, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days to resolve the disputed items. The parties shall submit any items still unresolved at the end of that period to an independent accounting firm of national standing selected jointly by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator, and its determination is final and binding absent manifest error. The parties shall bear its fees in inverse proportion to their relative success.

(d) If the final value of the Seed Inventory is less than the Target Inventory Value, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. If the final value exceeds the Target Inventory Value, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. In either case, payment is due within 10 business days after the final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third business day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the landlord's consent to assignment of the Salinas Lease and an estoppel certificate from the landlord;

(d) releases of all liens on the Transferred Assets other than Permitted Liens; and

(e) a certificate of Seller's secretary certifying Seller's board and stockholder resolutions approving this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.3(a);

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the Bill of Sale and Assumption Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full corporate power to own the Transferred Assets and to carry on the Business as now conducted.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. All necessary corporate and stockholder action has duly authorized that execution, delivery and performance. This Agreement constitutes, and each such Ancillary Agreement when executed will constitute, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents or any law applicable to Seller or (b) require any consent under, or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract, except as listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.4 Title to Tangible Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible Transferred Assets, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens.

**4.5 Financial Information.** The statements of revenue and direct expenses of the Business for fiscal years 2024 and 2025 included in Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule were prepared from Seller's books and records in good faith. They fairly present, in all material respects, the revenue and direct expenses of the Business for the periods indicated.

**4.6 Material Contracts.** Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Business involving annual payments above $100,000, each grower production contract and each license of Transferred IP. Each such contract is valid and in full force. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.7 Real Property.** Seller does not own real property used in the Business. The Salinas Lease is in full force and effect. Seller has delivered a true and complete copy of it to Buyer, and no default by Seller exists under it.

**4.8 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, Seller has conducted the Business in the ordinary course, and there has been no material adverse change in the Business.

**4.9 Title to Transferred IP.** Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Transferred IP, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. No Person other than Seller, including any current or former Personnel, holds any ownership interest in any Transferred IP. Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered Transferred IP, all plant variety protection applications, and all marker panels owned by Seller. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe or misappropriate the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**4.10 Breeding Records.** The breeding records included in the Transferred Assets are complete and accurate in all material respects. They are sufficient to permit a skilled breeder to maintain and reproduce each parent and inbred line listed in Section 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.11 Germplasm Purity and Traits.** The Germplasm and Seed Inventory meet a genetic purity of not less than 98%. Each parent line, inbred line and hybrid variety listed in Section 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule expresses the disease-resistance traits stated for it in that Section, including Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus resistance for each line identified as carrying the TY-Shield haplotype. In each case, purity and trait expression are as determined by a grow-out trial conducted over a full growing season under standard commercial conditions for the relevant crop.

**4.12 Phytosanitary and Regulatory Compliance.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with applicable seed labeling, phytosanitary and import and export laws. Seller holds all permits material to the Business, and Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists them.

**4.13 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Transferred Assets, including any grower claim for crop loss.

**4.14 Employees.** Section 4.14 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Business and his or her title and base compensation. Seller has complied in all material respects with applicable employment laws with respect to those employees.

**4.15 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and the Transferred Assets, and has paid all taxes shown on them as due. There are no liens for taxes on the Transferred Assets other than for current taxes not yet due.

**4.16 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement, the Seller Note and the other Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes, and the Seller Note when issued will constitute, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall:

(a) operate the Business in the ordinary course;

(b) maintain the Germplasm under appropriate storage conditions; and

(c) not sell, license or encumber any Transferred Asset other than sales of Seed Inventory in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the Salinas breeding station, the breeding records and the Personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Key Employee.** Buyer intends to offer employment to the Key Employee effective as of the Closing Date. Seller shall not discourage the Key Employee from accepting that offer, and shall release her from any non-competition obligation to Seller that would restrict her employment with Buyer.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** The mutual nondisclosure agreement that Buyer and Seller entered into in 2025 in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement remains in effect in accordance with its terms. After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business and the Transferred Assets.

**6.5 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, breed, produce or sell hybrid tomato or pepper seed anywhere in North America.

**6.6 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other party reasonably requests to carry out the purposes of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(a) Seller's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, except Fundamental Representations, which must be true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller has performed its covenants in all material respects;

(c) the landlord under the Salinas Lease has consented to its assignment to Buyer;

(d) the Key Employee has accepted employment with Buyer effective as of the Closing Date; and

(e) Seller has made each delivery required by Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(a) Buyer's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer has performed its covenants in all material respects; and

(c) Buyer has made each delivery required by Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of the parties;

(b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by April 15, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the cause of the delay; or

(c) by either party if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement becomes void and of no effect, except that Section 6.4, this Section 8.2 and Article XI survive. No termination relieves a party of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL AND INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of the parties, other than the Fundamental Representations, survive the Closing until 9 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations survive until the sixth anniversary of the Closing Date. Covenants survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its affiliates, officers, managers and employees against all Losses arising from:

(a) any breach of a representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of a covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees against all Losses arising from:

(a) any breach of a representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of a covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Claims Notice.** A party seeking indemnification must deliver to the indemnifying party written notice of the claim that describes the claim with reasonable specificity, including the facts giving rise to it and, to the extent then known, the amount of Losses claimed. No claim for breach of a representation or warranty may be made unless such notice is delivered before the end of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1. A claim properly noticed before that time survives until finally resolved.

**9.5 Third-Party Claims.** The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. To do so, it must give written notice within 20 days after receiving notice of the claim. The indemnifying party shall not settle a third-party claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money that the indemnifying party pays in full and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.6 Limitations.**

(a) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) until aggregate Losses exceed $485,000, after which Seller is liable from the first dollar. Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 12% of the Purchase Price.

(b) The limitations in Section 9.6(a) apply only to Losses arising from breaches of representations and warranties other than Fundamental Representations. They do not apply to fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed 12% of the Purchase Price.

(d) No party is liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.7 Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount finally determined to be owed to it under this Article IX against amounts then due under the Seller Note.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the procedures in Section 2.4, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the parties' sole and exclusive remedy after the Closing for any breach of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of all transfer, sales, use and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Transferred Assets.

**10.2 Allocation.** Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller an allocation of the Purchase Price among the Transferred Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. The parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party bears its own expenses in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices must be sent to the addresses set out on the signature page, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other party. Buyer may, however, assign its rights under this Agreement to an affiliate or to any lender as collateral. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Article IX, this Agreement confers no rights on any Person other than the parties.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**11.7 Specific Performance.** Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief to prevent breaches of this Agreement, without proof of actual damages.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** If this Agreement conflicts with any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement prevails. However, the IP Assignment Agreement controls with respect to the conveyance of, and representations concerning, the Transferred IP.

**11.10 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.11 Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.
