# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This **INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT** (this "**Assignment**") is made and delivered on March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignor**"), in favor of **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignee**"). Assignee accepts it.

## Recitals

A. Assignor, as seller, and Assignee, as buyer, are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Transferred Assets to Assignee, including the Transferred IP and the Pre-Closing Inventions.

B. Section 3.2(b) and Section 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement require Assignor and Assignee to execute and deliver this Assignment at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for the consideration stated in the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Assignor and Assignee agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Assignment, the following terms have the meanings below:

"**Assigned IP**" means the Transferred IP, including:

(a) the Germplasm, including all parent and inbred lines of the hybrid varieties listed in Section 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(b) the Pre-Closing Inventions, including the TY-Shield marker panel for Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus resistance;

(c) all plant variety protection certificates and applications, patents and patent applications, and all registrations of, and applications for, any of the foregoing, in any jurisdiction, including those listed in Schedule 1 (the "**Registered IP**");

(d) all trade secrets, know-how, breeding methods, protocols, assays, marker data, genotypic and phenotypic data, pedigree records and breeding records used or held for use in the Business;

(e) all variety names, trademarks, trade names and associated goodwill used in the Business, including those listed in Schedule 1;

(f) all copyrights in software, databases, scoring protocols and documentation used or held for use in the Business; and

(g) all rights described in Section 2.2.

"**Marker Panels**" means the molecular marker panels, scoring protocols and validation data listed in Part C of Schedule 1, including the TY-Shield marker panel.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and its successors and assigns, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. Assignee shall have and hold the Assigned IP forever.

**2.2 Associated Rights.** The assignment in Section 2.1 includes:

(a) all rights of priority and all rights to file applications for plant variety protection, patents, registrations and other protection in any jurisdiction based on the Assigned IP;

(b) all continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, re-examinations and extensions of any Registered IP;

(c) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due in respect of the Assigned IP; and

(d) all rights to sue for, and to collect damages for, past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of the Assigned IP.

**2.3 Genetic Material.** To the extent any Assigned IP is embodied in seed, plant tissue, pollen, DNA samples or other genetic material, the assignment in Section 2.1 includes all of Assignor's rights in that material. Physical delivery of that material is governed by the Bill of Sale and Assumption Agreement.

**2.4 Records.** Within 10 business days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, in the native electronic format in which Assignor maintains them, complete copies of all breeding records, pedigree records, laboratory notebooks, marker data, scoring protocols, validation data and phenotypic databases forming part of the Assigned IP. After delivery, Assignor shall not keep any copy of those records, except one archival copy held by its legal counsel solely to evidence compliance with this Assignment.

## 3. Representations and Warranties of Assignor

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as of the Effective Date as follows:

**3.1 Ownership.** Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. Assignor has not previously assigned, transferred, licensed or encumbered any of the Assigned IP, except under the licenses listed in Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**3.2 Personnel.** Each current and former member of the Personnel who contributed to the conception, development or reduction to practice of any Assigned IP did so within the scope of his or her employment or engagement by Assignor. Each has assigned to Assignor all of his or her rights in that Assigned IP by a written agreement or by operation of law.

**3.3 Registered IP.** Schedule 1 lists all Registered IP. Each item of Registered IP is subsisting and, to Assignor's knowledge, valid and enforceable. All filing, examination, maintenance and renewal fees due on or before the Effective Date for the Registered IP have been paid.

**3.4 Marker Panels and Germplasm.** Part C of Schedule 1 lists all Marker Panels. Assignor has maintained the Marker Panels, the Germplasm and the related trade secrets in confidence, using reasonable measures consistent with practice in the seed industry. Assignor has not disclosed any of them to any Person other than under a written obligation of confidentiality.

**3.5 Non-Infringement; Claims.** To Assignor's knowledge, the use of the Assigned IP in the Business as conducted on the Effective Date does not infringe or misappropriate the intellectual property rights of any Person. No action, claim or proceeding is pending or, to Assignor's knowledge, threatened that challenges the ownership, validity or enforceability of any Assigned IP.

**3.6 Scope of Representations.** The representations and warranties in this Section 3 are Assignor's sole representations concerning the Assigned IP and supersede Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement. They are made in addition to the representations and warranties of Assignor in the Purchase Agreement on matters other than the Assigned IP.

## 4. Recordation and Further Assurances

**4.1 Recordation.** Assignor authorizes Assignee to record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in the form of Schedule 2, with the U.S. Plant Variety Protection Office, the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office, and any other governmental authority or registry in any jurisdiction. Assignor shall sign any short-form assignment that Assignee reasonably requests for that purpose within five business days after the request.

**4.2 Further Assurances.** Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Assignment. Assignor shall execute and deliver, at Assignee's request and expense, all further instruments, declarations and documents, and take all further actions, that Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP.

**4.3 Prosecution Files.** Assignor shall instruct its patent and plant variety protection counsel to transfer to Assignee's designated counsel, within 15 business days after the Effective Date, all prosecution files, docketing records and correspondence relating to the Registered IP.

**4.4 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, file and record in Assignor's name any instrument described in Section 4.1 or Section 4.2 that Assignor fails to execute within 10 business days after Assignee's written request. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**4.5 Pending Matters.** If any deadline for a filing, response or fee payment on Registered IP falls within 30 days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall, at Assignee's request and expense, take the action needed to meet that deadline until Assignee's recordation is complete.

## 5. Covenants

**5.1 No Retained Rights.** Assignor retains no license or other right to use any Assigned IP after the Effective Date, except as Assignee expressly grants in writing.

**5.2 Confidentiality.** Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement applies to all trade secrets, know-how and other non-public information included in the Assigned IP. Assignor shall not use or disclose that information except as this Assignment or the Purchase Agreement permits.

**5.3 Amounts Received.** If Assignor receives any royalty, license fee, damages award or other payment after the Effective Date in respect of the Assigned IP, it shall hold that payment in trust for Assignee and pay it to Assignee within 10 business days after receipt.

**5.4 Cooperation in Enforcement.** After the Effective Date, Assignor shall cooperate with Assignee, at Assignee's expense, in any action to enforce or defend the Assigned IP. This includes making Assignor's records and knowledgeable officers available and joining the action as a party where required for standing.

## 6. General

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits Assignor and Assignee and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**6.2 Amendment.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee.

**6.3 Notices.** Notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Assignment is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**6.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Assignment is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Assignment as if set out in full.

**6.6 Counterparts.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
