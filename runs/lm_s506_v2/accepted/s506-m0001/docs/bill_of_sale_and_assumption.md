# BILL OF SALE AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT

This **BILL OF SALE AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT** (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered on March 2, 2026, which is the Closing Date under the Purchase Agreement. It is made by **Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), in favor of **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and is accepted by Buyer.

## Recitals

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Transferred Assets to Buyer, and Buyer has agreed to purchase the Transferred Assets and to assume the Assumed Liabilities.

B. Section 3.2(a) and Section 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement require Seller and Buyer to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for the consideration stated in the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller and Buyer agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance of Transferred Assets

**2.1 Conveyance.** Effective as of the Closing on March 2, 2026, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. Buyer shall have and hold the Transferred Assets forever. The Transferred Assets include the following:

(a) the Germplasm, including all parent and inbred lines of the hybrid varieties listed in Section 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(b) all breeding records, trial data, pedigree records, laboratory notebooks, marker data and phenotypic databases of the Business;

(c) the Seed Inventory, wherever located, including seed held by contract growers or in third-party storage;

(d) the Assigned Contracts, including the Salinas Lease;

(e) all equipment, greenhouse fixtures, cold storage and laboratory equipment located at the Salinas breeding station; and

(f) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Transferred IP.** The Transferred IP, including the Pre-Closing Inventions, is conveyed to Buyer by the IP Assignment Agreement delivered at the Closing. This Bill of Sale also conveys the Transferred IP to Buyer to the extent the IP Assignment Agreement does not, or to the extent a separate instrument of conveyance is needed for any item of it.

**2.3 Excluded Assets.** This Bill of Sale conveys only the Transferred Assets. Seller retains all other assets.

## 3. Assumption of Liabilities

**3.1 Assumption.** Effective as of the Closing, Buyer assumes and agrees to pay, perform and discharge when due the Assumed Liabilities, and no other liabilities. The Assumed Liabilities are:

(a) liabilities under the Assigned Contracts that arise after the Closing, excluding any liability arising from a breach before the Closing; and

(b) the liabilities listed in Section 2.2(a) of the Disclosure Schedule.

**3.2 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer does not assume, and Seller retains and remains responsible for, all Excluded Liabilities. These include all liabilities for seed that Seller sold or delivered before the Closing, grower claims relating to that seed, and Taxes of Seller.

## 4. Possession and Further Assurances

**4.1 Delivery of Possession.** Seller shall deliver to Buyer physical possession of all tangible Transferred Assets at the locations where they are held on the Closing Date. For Seed Inventory held by contract growers or in third-party storage, Seller shall deliver to each grower or storage provider, within five business days after the Closing Date, written instructions to hold that Seed Inventory for Buyer's account and to follow Buyer's instructions.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in Seller's name but on Buyer's behalf and at Buyer's expense, to demand and receive any Transferred Asset and to give receipts and releases for it. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Amounts Received.** If Seller receives any payment or other property after the Closing that belongs to Buyer as a Transferred Asset, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer and promptly pay or deliver it to Buyer.

**4.4 Further Assurances.** Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Bill of Sale. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as the other party reasonably requests to vest in Buyer title to the Transferred Assets and to evidence Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

## 5. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants, indemnities and other terms of the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale. They continue in effect as provided in the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. General

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits Seller and Buyer and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**6.2 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and Buyer.

**6.3 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Bill of Sale as if set out in full.

**6.4 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
