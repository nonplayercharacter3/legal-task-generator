# LANDLORD ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE

**Salinas Breeding Station, Salinas, California**

**Date:** February 25, 2026

**To:**

**Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignee**")
Attention: General Counsel

**Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**")
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re:** Industrial Lease dated June 1, 2019, between Bracken Coastal Properties, L.P., as landlord, and Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc., as tenant, as amended by a First Amendment dated August 15, 2023

Ladies and Gentlemen:

**Bracken Coastal Properties, L.P.**, a California limited partnership ("**Landlord**"), delivers this certificate (this "**Certificate**") at Tenant's request. Tenant and Assignee are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Tenant has agreed to assign the Lease to Assignee at the closing of that transaction (the "**Assignment**"). Landlord understands that Assignee will rely on this Certificate in completing that closing and in accepting the Assignment.

## 1. The Lease

**1.1 Lease Documents.** Landlord and Tenant are parties to an Industrial Lease dated June 1, 2019 (the "**Original Lease**"). The Original Lease was amended by a First Amendment to Lease dated August 15, 2023 (the "**First Amendment**", and together with the Original Lease, the "**Lease**"). Exhibit A lists the Lease documents. The Lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Premises. It has not been modified, supplemented or amended except by the First Amendment. There are no other agreements, written or oral, between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Premises.

**1.2 Full Force and Effect.** The Lease is in full force and effect. It is the valid and binding obligation of Landlord, enforceable against Landlord in accordance with its terms.

**1.3 Premises.** The premises leased under the Lease consist of approximately 42 acres of land in Salinas, California, together with the improvements on it. These include approximately 6.5 acres of greenhouses, a laboratory building, a seed conditioning shed and a seed vault (together, the "**Premises**"). Tenant has accepted possession of the Premises and is in occupancy of them.

**1.4 Permitted Use.** The Lease permits use of the Premises for plant breeding and agricultural research, greenhouse and field trial operations, laboratory and molecular testing, seed conditioning, treatment, testing and storage, and related office and administrative uses.

## 2. Term and Renewal

**2.1 Current Term.** The current term of the Lease began on June 1, 2019 and ends on May 31, 2031.

**2.2 Renewal Option.** Tenant has one option to renew the term of the Lease for five years, on the terms set out in the Lease. The renewal option has not been exercised, lapsed or waived. It remains available to Tenant and, after the Assignment, to Assignee.

**2.3 No Termination Rights.** Landlord has no right to terminate the Lease before the end of its current term, except because of a default by the tenant under the Lease after notice and the expiration of any applicable cure period.

## 3. Rent and Security Deposit

**3.1 Base Rent.** The current monthly base rent under the Lease is $58,500. Base rent increases by 3% annually on June 1 of each year during the term.

**3.2 Additional Rent.** Tenant pays, as additional rent, its share of real property taxes, insurance and common charges for the Premises as provided in the Lease. No reconciliation of additional rent for any prior calendar year remains outstanding. To Landlord's knowledge, no amount is owed by or to Tenant on account of any such reconciliation.

**3.3 Rent Paid.** Base rent and additional rent have been paid through February 28, 2026. No rent has been paid more than one month in advance.

**3.4 Security Deposit.** Tenant has deposited with Landlord a security deposit of $117,000. Landlord holds the full amount of that deposit and has not applied any part of it. At the Assignment, Landlord will hold the deposit for the benefit of Assignee, as tenant under the Lease, on the terms of the Lease.

**3.5 No Concessions.** No free rent, rent abatement, tenant improvement allowance or other concession is due or unpaid to Tenant under the Lease.

## 4. Status of Performance

**4.1 No Default by Tenant.** To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant is not in default under the Lease. No event has occurred that, with notice or lapse of time or both, would become a default by Tenant. Landlord has not delivered to Tenant any notice of default that remains uncured.

**4.2 No Default by Landlord.** To Landlord's knowledge, Landlord is not in default under the Lease, and Landlord has not received from Tenant any notice of a default by Landlord.

**4.3 Improvements.** All improvements to the Premises that Landlord was required to make under the Lease have been completed. All alterations that Tenant has made to the Premises were made with Landlord's consent where the Lease requires it. Landlord will not require the removal of the greenhouses, laboratory improvements, seed vault or cold storage installations on the Premises at the end of the term, except as the Lease provides.

**4.4 Environmental Matters.** Landlord has not given or received any written notice from any governmental authority of a violation of environmental law at the Premises that remains unresolved.

**4.5 No Litigation.** No action or proceeding is pending, or to Landlord's knowledge threatened, between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Lease or the Premises.

## 5. Other Rights

**5.1 No Purchase Rights.** Neither Tenant nor any other person has an option to purchase, right of first refusal or right of first offer to purchase the Premises or any part of them.

**5.2 Mortgages.** The Premises are subject to a deed of trust in favor of Landlord's lender. The Lease is subordinate to that deed of trust only as provided in the Lease, and Tenant's possession under the Lease is protected by the non-disturbance agreement delivered under the Lease.

**5.3 No Sale or Transfer.** Landlord has not sold, assigned or transferred its interest in the Premises or the Lease, and Landlord has not entered into any agreement to do so.

## 6. Consent to Assignment

**6.1 Consent.** Under Section 14.1 of the Lease, Landlord consents to the Assignment of the Lease by Tenant to Assignee, effective on the closing date under the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Effect of Assignment.** From the effective date of the Assignment, Assignee will be the tenant under the Lease and will have all rights of the tenant under it, including the renewal option described in Section 2.2. Assignee assumes the tenant's obligations under the Lease arising after the effective date of the Assignment. Tenant remains responsible for all obligations of the tenant under the Lease that arose before that date.

**6.3 Notices.** After the Assignment, Landlord will send all notices under the Lease to Assignee at the address that Assignee designates by notice to Landlord.

## 7. Reliance

**7.1 Reliance.** Landlord delivers this Certificate knowing that Tenant and Assignee will rely on it. Assignee and its successors, assigns and lenders may rely on the statements in this Certificate.

**7.2 Lease Not Modified.** This Certificate does not amend the Lease. The statements in this Certificate are statements of fact as of its date.

**7.3 Authority.** The person signing this Certificate on Landlord's behalf is authorized to do so.

**7.4 Governing Law.** This Certificate is governed by the laws of the State of California.
