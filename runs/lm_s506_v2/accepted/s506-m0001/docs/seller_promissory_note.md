# PROMISSORY NOTE

**$8,500,000** | **New York, New York** | **March 2, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its successors and assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal amount of EIGHT MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($8,500,000), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms set out in this Note.

This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026, between the Holder, as seller, and the Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Maker issues it in partial payment of the Purchase Price under Section 2.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal Amount

**1.1 Principal.** The principal amount of this Note is $8,500,000 (the "**Principal Amount**").

**1.2 Issue Date.** This Note is issued on, and interest accrues from, the Closing Date of March 2, 2026 (the "**Issue Date**").

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the outstanding Principal Amount at a fixed rate of 6.0% per annum.

**2.2 Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default continues, interest accrues on the outstanding Principal Amount and on any overdue interest at a rate of 8.0% per annum.

**2.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note will not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate will be applied to reduce the Principal Amount.

## 3. Payments

**3.1 Interest Payments.** The Maker shall pay accrued interest quarterly in arrears on the last business day of each March, June, September and December, beginning June 30, 2026.

**3.2 Principal Installments.** The Maker shall repay the Principal Amount in three equal annual installments of $2,833,333.33 each, on the first, second and third anniversaries of the Issue Date. The final installment shall be adjusted to equal the entire Principal Amount then outstanding.

**3.3 Maturity Date.** All outstanding principal and accrued and unpaid interest are due and payable in full on March 2, 2029 (the "**Maturity Date**").

**3.4 Manner of Payment.** The Maker shall make all payments in United States dollars, in immediately available funds, by wire transfer to the account that the Holder designates in writing. If a payment date falls on a day that is not a business day, the payment is due on the next business day, and interest continues to accrue until paid.

**3.5 Application.** Payments are applied first to costs and expenses payable under Section 9.3, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to the Principal Amount.

## 4. Prepayment

The Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each prepayment must include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments are applied to principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Set-Off

The Maker may set off against any amount then due under this Note any amount finally determined to be owed to the Maker under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, as provided in Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement. An amount set off in accordance with this Section 5 is treated as paid under this Note on the date of set-off. A set-off under this Section 5 is not an Event of Default.

## 6. Representations of the Maker

The Maker represents to the Holder that:

(a) it is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Note, and has duly authorized that execution, delivery and performance by all necessary limited liability company action; and

(c) this Note constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

## 7. Covenants of the Maker

**7.1 Reporting.** So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, the Maker shall deliver to the Holder, within 45 days after the end of each calendar quarter:

(a) a report of the Maker's seed sales for that quarter, stated by variety, showing units sold, gross revenue and principal customer regions;

(b) a report on the status of the Maker's breeding pipeline, identifying each hybrid tomato and pepper line in development, its stage of development, the traits under selection, and expected commercial release dates; and

(c) unaudited balance sheets of the Maker as of the end of that quarter and statements of income and cash flows for that quarter, certified by an authorized officer of the Maker as fairly presenting, in all material respects, the Maker's financial position and results of operations.

**7.2 Annual Financial Statements.** Within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year of the Maker, the Maker shall deliver to the Holder its balance sheet as of the end of that fiscal year and its statements of income and cash flows for that year.

**7.3 Notice of Default.** The Maker shall notify the Holder in writing promptly, and in any event within five business days, after it becomes aware of any Event of Default or any event that, with notice or lapse of time, would become an Event of Default.

**7.4 Maintenance of Existence.** The Maker shall maintain its existence as a limited liability company in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**7.5 Sale of the Business.** The Maker shall not sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of the Transferred Assets in one transaction or a series of related transactions unless it repays this Note in full at or before the closing of that disposition.

## 8. Events of Default

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal when due under this Note;

(b) the Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount when due under this Note, and the failure continues for 10 business days;

(c) the Maker fails to deliver any report or statement required by Section 7.1 or Section 7.2 when due, and the failure continues for 15 days (a "**Reporting Default**");

(d) the Maker breaches any other covenant in this Note, and the breach continues for 30 days after written notice from the Holder;

(e) any representation of the Maker in Section 6 is untrue in any material respect when made;

(f) the Maker (i) commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iii) consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian of all or a substantial part of its assets; or

(g) an involuntary case is commenced against the Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, and it is not dismissed within 60 days.

**8.2 Acceleration.** If an Event of Default described in Section 8.1(a) through (e) occurs and continues, including a Reporting Default, the Holder may declare, by written notice to the Maker, the entire unpaid Principal Amount, all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owed under this Note immediately due and payable. If an Event of Default described in Section 8.1(f) or (g) occurs, those amounts become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by the Holder.

**8.3 Remedies Cumulative.** The Holder's rights and remedies under this Note are cumulative and are in addition to any other rights and remedies available at law or in equity.

## 9. Waivers; Costs

**9.1 Waiver of Presentment.** The Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note.

**9.2 No Implied Waiver.** No delay or omission by the Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right. A waiver on one occasion is not a waiver on any other occasion.

**9.3 Costs of Collection.** The Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Holder incurs in collecting or enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 10. Transfer

**10.1 Maker.** The Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of the Holder, except to a successor to all or substantially all of the Transferred Assets that assumes this Note in writing and to which Section 7.5 does not apply.

**10.2 Holder.** The Holder may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, in whole or in part, and any of its rights under this Note, to any Person, without the consent of the Maker. Each transferee becomes the Holder of this Note, or of the transferred portion of it, and is entitled to all rights of the Holder under this Note.

**10.3 Register.** The Maker shall pay principal and interest to the Person shown as Holder in its records until the Maker has received a copy of the instrument of transfer. Payment made in good faith to that Person discharges the Maker's obligation to the extent of the payment.

## 11. Miscellaneous

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Note must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Maker must be sent to the address set out below its signature, and notices to the Holder must be sent to the address the Holder designates by notice to the Maker. Either party may change its address by notice.

**11.2 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it may be waived, only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**11.4 Successors.** This Note binds the Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and benefits the Holder and its successors and assigns.

**11.5 Governing Law.** This Note is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.6 Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** The Maker submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Note. THE MAKER AND, BY ITS ACCEPTANCE OF THIS NOTE, THE HOLDER EACH WAIVE THE RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.7 Headings.** Section headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Note.
