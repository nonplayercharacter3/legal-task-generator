April 28, 2026

**Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**
Attention: General Counsel

**Re: Post-Closing Transition Matters under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter**") is entered into between Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc. ("**Seller**") and Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC ("**Buyer**"). It concerns certain matters arising after the Closing under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026, between Seller and Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Background

**1.1** Before the Closing, Seller accepted orders from growers and seed distributors for hybrid tomato and pepper seed of varieties now included in the Transferred Assets, for delivery during the 2026 spring and early-summer planting window. Many of those orders were placed on Seller's standard order forms and are invoiced through Seller's customer accounts. Those accounts have not yet migrated to Buyer's systems.

**1.2** All Seed Inventory now belongs to Buyer, as does all production capacity for the relevant varieties. To avoid disruption to growers and to preserve the goodwill of the Business, the parties have agreed that Seller will complete those orders using seed that Buyer supplies, on the terms of this Letter.

**1.3** The parties also wish to record the status of the Closing Seed Inventory Statement and related correspondence.

## 2. Transition Period and Cooperation

**2.1 Transition Period.** The "**Transition Period**" begins on the date of this Letter and ends on June 30, 2026.

**2.2 Customer Communications.** During the Transition Period, Seller shall refer to Buyer all new inquiries and new orders for seed of varieties included in the Transferred Assets. Seller shall not accept any new order for that seed in its own name. Seller and Buyer shall jointly approve the form of a letter to customers announcing the transfer of the Business to Buyer. Seller shall send that letter to each customer listed in Annex A within 10 business days after the date of this Letter.

**2.3 Records Access.** During the Transition Period, and for 90 days afterward, Seller shall give Buyer reasonable access to Seller's order, shipping and invoicing records for the Transition Orders. Seller shall also give Buyer copies of any grower correspondence relating to seed supplied for Transition Orders within five business days after Seller receives it.

**2.4 No Transfer of Excluded Liabilities.** Except as provided in Section 3, nothing in this Letter makes Buyer responsible for any Excluded Liability.

## 3. Transition Orders

**3.1 Transition Orders.** "**Transition Orders**" means the orders listed in Annex A. Each is an order that Seller accepted before the Closing, for delivery to the customer on or before June 30, 2026. Annex A sets out, for each Transition Order, the customer, the variety, the quantity ordered and the scheduled delivery date.

**3.2 Supply by Buyer.** Buyer shall supply to Seller, from Seed Inventory or from seed that Buyer produces, the seed needed to fill each Transition Order in the variety and quantity stated in Annex A. Buyer shall deliver that seed to Seller's distribution facility in Salinas, California, at least five business days before the scheduled delivery date. The seed shall be treated, packaged and labeled as Buyer customarily does for the relevant variety. Buyer shall supply with each lot the germination and purity test results that Buyer has for it.

**3.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees against all Losses, including grower crop losses, arising from or relating to seed supplied by Buyer for Transition Orders. This indemnity covers claims by growers, distributors and other customers that the seed was defective, mislabeled, off-type, below stated germination or purity, or did not express the traits stated for it. This indemnity is in addition to, and not subject to any limitation in Article IX of, the Purchase Agreement. Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any third-party claim under this Section 3.3 as if it were a claim under Section 9.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**3.4 Fulfillment by Seller.** Seller shall ship each Transition Order to the customer on the scheduled delivery date, using the seed that Buyer supplies under Section 3.2. Seller shall store and ship that seed in accordance with Buyer's written storage and handling instructions. Seller shall not repackage, relabel or blend any seed that Buyer supplies.

**3.5 Invoicing and Remittance.** Seller shall invoice each customer for its Transition Order at the price stated in Annex A and shall collect payment in the ordinary course. Within 15 days after the end of each calendar month, Seller shall remit to Buyer all amounts collected during that month for Transition Orders, less a handling fee equal to 4% of those amounts. Each remittance shall be accompanied by a statement identifying the Transition Orders and amounts to which it relates. Seller bears the risk of non-payment by a customer only to the extent the non-payment results from Seller's failure to invoice or collect in the ordinary course.

**3.6 Cancelled or Modified Orders.** If a customer cancels or reduces a Transition Order, Seller shall notify Buyer promptly. Buyer is not required to supply seed for the cancelled or reduced portion. Seller shall not agree to any increase in the quantity of a Transition Order or any change in its variety without Buyer's prior written consent.

## 4. Closing Seed Inventory Statement

**4.1** Seller has delivered to Buyer the Closing Seed Inventory Statement under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2** Buyer delivered its Inventory Dispute Notice on April 24, 2026. The notice disputes (a) the germination rates stated for 14 lots of hybrid tomato seed held in third-party storage in Gilroy, California, (b) the quantities stated for six lots of pepper stock seed held by contract growers, and (c) the valuation applied to those lots. Buyer maintains that its Inventory Dispute Notice is valid and effective under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement. Buyer has not accepted the Closing Seed Inventory Statement.

**4.3** Representatives of the parties will meet before May 29, 2026 to discuss the disputed items. Those discussions are without prejudice to either party's position. Each party reserves all rights under the Purchase Agreement with respect to the Closing Seed Inventory Statement and the Inventory Dispute Notice. Nothing in this Section 4 is a waiver of, or an agreement on, any of those rights.

## 5. General

**5.1 Effect on Purchase Agreement.** Except as expressly set out in this Letter, the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements are unchanged and remain in full force and effect.

**5.2 Amendment.** This Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**5.3 Notices.** Notices under this Letter shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.4 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Letter is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Letter as if set out in full.

**5.5 Counterparts.** This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
