## Section 2.4: Inventory Valuation Principles

Seller shall prepare the Closing Seed Inventory Statement in accordance with the following principles, applied consistently with Seller's practices in preparing its financial statements for fiscal year 2025:

1. **Physical count.** Quantities are determined by physical count or weight as of the Closing. Seed held by contract growers or in third-party storage is counted using the most recent written confirmation from the grower or storage provider, dated no earlier than 10 days before the Closing.

2. **Germination.** Each lot is assigned the germination rate shown on its most recent germination test. That test must have been performed by an accredited laboratory or by Seller's in-house laboratory under AOSA rules within the six months before the Closing.

3. **Valuation.** Finished hybrid seed is valued at standard cost, consisting of grower production cost, conditioning, treatment and testing costs, but not packaging. Stock seed and parent seed are valued at the cost of production.

4. **Write-downs.** A lot is valued at zero if its germination rate is below the minimum commercial standard for the crop stated below, or if it has failed a purity test:
   - (a) tomato hybrid seed: 85%;
   - (b) pepper hybrid seed: 80%.

5. **Exclusions.** Seed that Seller has sold or committed to customers before the Closing and that remains in Seller's possession for shipment is excluded from Seed Inventory.

6. **Target.** The Target Inventory Value of $6,200,000 was determined on the same principles.
