# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This **MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Naitox**"), and **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Loudeil**"). Each of Naitox and Loudeil is a "**Party**", and together they are the "**Parties**".

## Recitals

A. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Loudeil would acquire all or part of Naitox's hybrid tomato and pepper seed genetics business (the "**Potential Transaction**").

B. In connection with the Potential Transaction, each Party expects to disclose Confidential Information to the other. The Parties are entering into this Agreement to protect that information.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") disclose to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Potential Transaction. Confidential Information may be disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, through a virtual data room, or by site visit. It includes:

(a) germplasm identities, pedigrees, parent and inbred line information, breeding records, trial data, marker data, genotypic and phenotypic data, and breeding pipeline plans;

(b) trade secrets, know-how, breeding methods, assays, scoring protocols and production methods;

(c) financial information, sales data, pricing, customer and grower lists, contracts and business plans;

(d) information about employees and contractors; and

(e) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials that the Receiving Party or its Representatives prepare and that contain or reflect any of the foregoing (the "**Derivative Materials**").

The fact that the Parties are discussing the Potential Transaction, and the status and terms of those discussions, are also Confidential Information of each Party.

**1.2** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without a duty of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, is not bound by a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**1.3** "**Representatives**" means a Party's affiliates, and its and their directors, managers, officers, employees, legal counsel, accountants, financial advisors, and actual or prospective sources of debt or equity financing, in each case who need to know the Confidential Information for the Permitted Purpose.

**1.4** "**Permitted Purpose**" means evaluating, negotiating and, if the Parties enter into a Definitive Agreement, consummating the Potential Transaction.

**1.5** "**Definitive Agreement**" means a final, written agreement for the Potential Transaction that both Parties have executed and delivered. A letter of intent, term sheet or other preliminary written agreement is not a Definitive Agreement.

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

**2.1 Non-Use and Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall:

(a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Purpose;

(b) not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives; and

(c) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.2 Representatives.** Before disclosing Confidential Information to a Representative, the Receiving Party shall inform that Representative of its confidential nature. The Receiving Party shall direct each Representative to comply with this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if the Receiving Party had committed it.

**2.3 Genetic Material.** This Agreement does not authorize the transfer of any seed, plant tissue, pollen, DNA sample or other genetic material. Any such transfer requires a separate written material transfer agreement signed by both Parties. The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, genotype, sequence, breed with or otherwise analyze any genetic material of the Disclosing Party, except as such a material transfer agreement expressly permits.

**2.4 Site Visits.** During any visit to the Disclosing Party's facilities, including the Salinas breeding station, the Receiving Party and its Representatives shall comply with the Disclosing Party's reasonable biosecurity, safety and access procedures. They shall not remove any material from those facilities.

**2.5 Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly after it becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit the harm.

## 3. Compelled Disclosure

If law, regulation, stock exchange rule, subpoena, court order or other legal process requires the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement. The notice must give the Disclosing Party a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably, at the Disclosing Party's expense, with those efforts. If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party may disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the information disclosed will be treated confidentially.

## 4. Return and Destruction

**4.1 Request.** Within 15 days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party ends discussions of the Potential Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, including all Derivative Materials. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify compliance in writing.

**4.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policy, and (b) copies in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained, notwithstanding the end of the term in Section 8.

## 5. Acquired Information

If the Parties enter into a Definitive Agreement and the Potential Transaction is consummated, Confidential Information of Naitox that relates to the assets or business acquired by Loudeil ceases to be Confidential Information of Naitox under this Agreement as of the consummation. From then on, Loudeil may use and disclose that information without restriction under this Agreement. Section 4 does not apply to that information. Any confidentiality obligations of Naitox concerning that information after the consummation are governed by the Definitive Agreement.

## 6. Non-Solicitation

For 12 months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the other Party whom it first met, or whose identity it first learned, in connection with the Potential Transaction. This Section 6 does not restrict:

(a) general solicitations, such as advertisements or search firm engagements, not directed at the other Party's employees, or hiring any person who responds to them;

(b) soliciting or hiring any person whose employment with the other Party ended at least six months before the solicitation; or

(c) any offer of employment made, or employment entered into, as contemplated by a Definitive Agreement.

## 7. No Representations; No Obligation

**7.1 No Warranty.** Each Party provides its Confidential Information "as is". Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a Definitive Agreement.

**7.2 No Obligation to Transact.** Neither Party is obligated to proceed with the Potential Transaction unless and until a Definitive Agreement is executed. Either Party may end discussions at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other.

**7.3 No License.** No license or other right in any patent, plant variety protection, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property is granted or implied by this Agreement or by any disclosure under it, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Permitted Purpose.

## 8. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for three years after the Effective Date. A Party's obligations under Section 2 for Confidential Information disclosed during the term survive for three years after the end of the term. For Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 9. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance to prevent or stop any breach of this Agreement, without posting a bond. Those remedies are in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 10. Protected Disclosures

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits any individual from reporting possible violations of law to a governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual is not held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret that is made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 11. General

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out below each Party's signature. Either Party may change its address by notice.

**11.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or of the business that is the subject of the Potential Transaction. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**11.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**11.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
