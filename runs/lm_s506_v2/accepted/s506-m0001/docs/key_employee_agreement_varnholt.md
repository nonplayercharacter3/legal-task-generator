# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This **KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, between **Loudeil Seed Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Ilse Varnholt**, an individual ("**Employee**").

## Recitals

A. The Company has acquired the vegetable seed genetics business of Naitox Vegetable Genetics, Inc. ("**Naitox**") under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026, between Naitox and the Company. The closing under that agreement occurred on March 2, 2026.

B. Employee served as Head of Vegetable Breeding of Naitox. She has specialized knowledge of hybrid tomato and pepper breeding, parent line development and marker-assisted selection.

C. The Company wishes to employ Employee, and Employee wishes to be employed by the Company, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Company and Employee agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below:

"**Cause**" has the meaning given in Section 8.3.

"**Company Inventions**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Employment Start Date**" means March 2, 2026.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning given in Section 8.4.

"**Inventions**" means all inventions, discoveries, developments, improvements, germplasm selections, plant lines, breeding methods, marker panels, molecular markers, assays, protocols, data, software, works of authorship, know-how and trade secrets, whether or not patentable, registrable or reduced to practice.

"**Prior Inventions**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

## 2. Employment

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs Employee as Head of Vegetable Breeding, beginning on the Employment Start Date. Employee reports to the Company's Chief Executive Officer.

**2.2 Duties.** Employee shall lead the Company's hybrid tomato and pepper breeding programs. Her duties include directing parent and inbred line development, overseeing trait introgression and marker-assisted selection, supervising breeding station staff, and planning variety trials and commercial releases. Employee shall perform such other duties consistent with her position as the Chief Executive Officer reasonably assigns.

**2.3 Place of Performance.** Employee's principal place of work is the Company's breeding station in Salinas, California. Employee shall travel to trial sites, grower locations and industry meetings as her duties reasonably require.

**2.4 Full Time and Attention.** Employee shall devote her full business time and attention to the Company. Employee may serve on scientific advisory committees, publish academic work cleared under Section 4.4, and teach occasional university courses, so long as those activities do not materially interfere with her duties and do not involve a business that competes with the Company.

**2.5 At-Will Employment.** Employee's employment is at will. Either party may end it at any time, with or without Cause or Good Reason, subject to the notice and payment obligations in Section 8.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Employee a base salary at the annual rate of $310,000, payable in accordance with the Company's normal payroll practices. The Company's board of managers shall review the base salary at least annually and may increase but not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 35% of base salary. The bonus is based on breeding program milestones and Company performance objectives that the Chief Executive Officer sets after consultation with Employee. Any bonus for 2026 shall be prorated from the Employment Start Date. Employee must be employed on the payment date to receive a bonus, except as provided in Section 8.5.

**3.3 Signing Bonus.** The Company shall pay Employee a one-time signing bonus of $75,000 within 30 days after the Employment Start Date. If Employee resigns without Good Reason, or the Company terminates her employment for Cause, before the first anniversary of the Employment Start Date, Employee shall repay the signing bonus in full within 30 days after her employment ends.

**3.4 Retention Bonus.** If Employee remains continuously employed through the second anniversary of the Employment Start Date, the Company shall pay her a retention bonus of $200,000 within 30 days after that anniversary.

**3.5 Equity.** Subject to approval of the Company's board of managers, the Company shall grant Employee profits interests in the Company representing 1.25% of the Company's fully diluted equity as of the grant date. The profits interests vest over four years, with 25% vesting on the first anniversary of the Employment Start Date and the balance vesting in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months. The grant is governed by the Company's equity incentive plan and the applicable award agreement.

**3.6 Benefits.** Employee may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans. Employee is entitled to 20 days of paid vacation each year, in addition to Company holidays.

**3.7 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy.

**3.8 Relocation.** Employee's principal residence is within commuting distance of the Salinas breeding station, so no relocation benefit applies.

## 4. Confidential Information

**4.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Company or its affiliates, in any form. It includes germplasm, pedigrees, parent and inbred line identities, breeding records, trial data, marker data, genotypic and phenotypic databases, breeding pipeline plans, production methods, customer and grower lists, pricing and sales data, and business plans. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through Employee's breach of this Agreement, or (b) Employee lawfully receives from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

**4.2 Non-Disclosure and Non-Use.** During her employment and at all times afterward, Employee shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence. She shall not disclose it to any person outside the Company, or use it for any purpose other than performing her duties for the Company, without the Company's prior written consent.

**4.3 Germplasm Handling.** Employee shall not remove any seed, plant tissue, pollen, DNA sample or other genetic material of the Company from Company premises or from the control of the Company or its contract growers, except as her duties require and in accordance with the Company's material transfer and biosecurity procedures.

**4.4 Publications.** Employee shall submit to the Chief Executive Officer any proposed publication, presentation or abstract relating to the Company's breeding programs at least 45 days before submission or disclosure. The Company may require removal of Confidential Information, or a delay of up to 90 additional days to permit the filing of patent or plant variety protection applications.

**4.5 Return of Materials.** When her employment ends, or earlier at the Company's request, Employee shall return to the Company all documents, data, devices, notebooks and genetic material containing or embodying Confidential Information, and shall delete any electronic copies in her personal possession.

**4.6 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents Employee from reporting possible violations of law to a governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual is not held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret that is made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 5. Inventions

**5.1 Disclosure.** Employee shall promptly and fully disclose to the Company in writing every Invention that she makes, conceives or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment.

**5.2 Prior Inventions.** "**Prior Inventions**" means all Inventions that Employee made, conceived or reduced to practice, alone or with others, before the Employment Start Date. Employee has listed the Prior Inventions in Exhibit A. The Prior Inventions are not assigned to the Company under this Agreement and shall remain the sole property of Employee. If Employee incorporates a Prior Invention into any product, line, process or work of the Company, Employee grants the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide license to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import, reproduce, breed with and otherwise exploit that Prior Invention as part of or in connection with that product, line, process or work.

**5.3 Assignment of Company Inventions.** Employee assigns, and agrees to assign, to the Company all of her right, title and interest in and to every Invention that she makes, conceives or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment and that (a) relates to the Company's business or its actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, (b) results from work she performs for the Company, or (c) is made using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, germplasm or Confidential Information (the "**Company Inventions**"). This assignment includes all patent, plant variety protection, copyright, trade secret and other intellectual property rights in the Company Inventions, and all rights to sue for past, present and future infringement of them.

**5.4 California Labor Code Section 2870.** Section 5.3 does not apply to an Invention that qualifies fully for protection under California Labor Code Section 2870, which is set out in Exhibit B. Employee shall disclose any Invention that she believes so qualifies, so that the Company can assess her position.

**5.5 Breeding Records.** All breeding records, field books, laboratory notebooks, pedigree records and data that Employee creates or maintains during her employment are the Company's property and shall be kept in the Company's systems.

**5.6 Assistance.** During and after her employment, Employee shall assist the Company, at the Company's expense, to obtain, maintain and enforce patents, plant variety protection certificates and other rights in the Company Inventions. Employee shall sign all applications, declarations, assignments and other instruments that the Company reasonably requests for that purpose. The Company shall compensate Employee at a reasonable hourly rate for time spent assisting after her employment ends.

**5.7 Power of Attorney.** If the Company cannot obtain Employee's signature on an instrument described in Section 5.6 after reasonable effort, Employee irrevocably appoints the Company and its authorized officers as her attorney-in-fact to sign and file that instrument on her behalf. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**5.8 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Employee waives any moral rights she may have in any Company Invention.

## 6. Restrictive Covenants

**6.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During her employment and for 12 months afterward, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any employee of the Company engaged in breeding, research or seed production to leave the Company's employ. General advertisements not directed at Company employees do not breach this Section 6.1.

**6.2 Trade Secret Protection.** After her employment ends, Employee shall not use the Company's trade secrets, including its germplasm, pedigrees and marker data, to solicit any customer or contract grower of the Company or to develop any competing variety.

**6.3 Outside Business Activities.** During her employment, Employee shall not engage in, or hold an interest in, any business that breeds, produces or sells hybrid tomato or pepper seed, other than passive ownership of up to 2% of the securities of a publicly traded company.

**6.4 Reasonableness.** Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 6 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's trade secrets and goodwill. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 7. Prior Obligations

**7.1 No Conflicting Obligations.** Employee represents that her performance of this Agreement does not breach any agreement she has with any other person. Employee shall not bring to the Company, or use in her work for the Company, any confidential information or property of a former employer except as that information or property is lawfully owned by or licensed to the Company.

**7.2 Release by Naitox.** Employee acknowledges that Naitox has released her from any non-competition obligation that would restrict her employment with the Company.

## 8. Termination

**8.1 Death or Disability.** Employee's employment ends automatically on her death. The Company may end her employment on written notice if she is unable, because of a physical or mental condition, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days.

**8.2 Notice.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment without Cause, and Employee may resign without Good Reason, on 60 days' written notice. The Company may pay base salary in lieu of all or part of that notice period.

**8.3 Cause.** "**Cause**" means Employee's (a) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony or a crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company; (c) material breach of Section 4, 5 or 6; or (d) willful failure to perform her material duties that continues for 15 days after written notice from the Company describing the failure.

**8.4 Good Reason.** "**Good Reason**" means any of the following without Employee's written consent: (a) a material reduction in her base salary; (b) a material reduction in her authority or duties as Head of Vegetable Breeding; or (c) a relocation of her principal place of work by more than 50 miles. To resign for Good Reason, Employee must give written notice within 60 days after the condition first arises, the Company must fail to cure the condition within 30 days after that notice, and Employee must resign within 30 days after the cure period ends.

**8.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall (a) continue to pay her base salary for 12 months after her employment ends, (b) pay her a prorated annual bonus for the year in which her employment ends, based on actual performance, at the time bonuses are paid to other senior employees, (c) pay the retention bonus under Section 3.4 if her employment ends before the second anniversary of the Employment Start Date, and (d) pay the employer portion of her COBRA premiums for up to 12 months. Employee must sign, and not revoke, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within 60 days after her employment ends to receive these payments.

**8.6 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay Employee her earned but unpaid base salary, accrued but unused vacation, and unreimbursed business expenses.

**8.7 Survival.** Sections 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 survive the end of Employee's employment.

## 9. General

**9.1 Section 409A.** The parties intend that payments under this Agreement comply with, or are exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and this Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

**9.2 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required by law.

**9.3 Assignment.** The Company may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. Employee may not assign this Agreement.

**9.4 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company must be sent to the attention of its General Counsel. Notices to Employee must be sent to her most recent address in the Company's records.

**9.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Employee and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**9.7 Equitable Relief.** A breach of Section 4, 5 or 6 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to seek injunctive relief to prevent or stop such a breach, in addition to any other remedy.

**9.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## Exhibit B: California Labor Code Section 2870

"(a) Any provision in an employment agreement which provides that an employee shall assign, or offer to assign, any of his or her rights in an invention to his or her employer shall not apply to an invention that the employee developed entirely on his or her own time without using the employer's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information except for those inventions that either:

(1) Relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice of the invention to the employer's business, or actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development of the employer; or

(2) Result from any work performed by the employee for the employer.

(b) To the extent a provision in an employment agreement purports to require an employee to assign an invention otherwise excluded from being required to be assigned under subdivision (a), the provision is against the public policy of this state and is unenforceable."
