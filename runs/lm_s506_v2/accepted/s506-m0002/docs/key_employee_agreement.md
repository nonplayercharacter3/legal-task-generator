# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), and **Delphine Okafor-Ruiz**, an individual ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. Under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 between the Company, as Buyer, and Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Seller**") (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), the Company is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Seller's regional concert promotion and music festival business (the "**Business**"). The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs on the Effective Date.

B. Before the Effective Date, Executive served as Seller's head of talent booking. In that role Executive developed relationships with artists, artist agents and managers, and venues that are of substantial value to the Business and form part of the goodwill the Company is acquiring.

C. Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires delivery of this Agreement, signed by Executive, at the Closing. The Company wishes to employ Executive, and Executive wishes to be employed by the Company, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Employment; Position and Duties

**1.1 Position.** The Company employs Executive as Senior Vice President, Head of Talent Booking. Executive reports to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

**1.2 Duties.** Executive is responsible for:

(a) artist booking and talent acquisition for the Company's concerts and festivals;

(b) negotiating artist engagement agreements and artist guarantees within budgets approved by the Chief Executive Officer;

(c) managing artist and agency relationships; and

(d) supervising the talent booking staff of the Company.

Executive shall also perform such other duties consistent with Executive's position as the Chief Executive Officer reasonably assigns.

**1.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time and efforts to the Company. Executive may do the following, provided these activities do not materially interfere with Executive's duties or breach Section 7 or Section 8:

(a) serve on civic, charitable and industry association boards; and

(b) manage personal investments.

**1.4 Place of Performance.** Executive's principal place of employment is the Company's offices at 350 West 31st Street, New York, New York. Executive acknowledges that the position requires reasonable travel, including attendance at the Company's festivals and events.

## 2. Term

**2.1 Initial Term.** Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the third (3rd) anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

**2.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Either party may prevent a renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms are together the "**Term**."

## 3. Compensation

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $385,000 ("**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Chief Executive Officer shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase it, but may not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** For each calendar year during the Term, Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus. The target bonus is forty percent (40%) of Base Salary. The amount of the bonus depends on the achievement of performance objectives relating to:

(a) talent booking results; and

(b) festival and concert revenue.

The Chief Executive Officer shall set these objectives after consultation with Executive. The Company shall pay any bonus earned for a calendar year no later than March 15 of the following year. Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive the bonus, except as provided in Section 6.

**3.3 Signing Bonus.** The Company shall pay Executive a one-time signing bonus of $75,000 within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date. Executive shall repay the full signing bonus if, before the first anniversary of the Effective Date, Executive resigns without Good Reason or the Company terminates Executive's employment for Cause.

**3.4 Retention Award.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention award of $150,000 in two equal installments. The first installment is payable on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and the second on the second anniversary. Executive must remain employed on each payment date to receive that installment, except as provided in Section 6.

## 4. Benefits; Expenses; Vacation

**4.1 Benefits.** Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans the Company maintains for its senior employees, on the terms of those plans. The Company may amend or terminate any plan in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Vacation.** Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid vacation each calendar year, in addition to Company holidays. Executive shall schedule vacation so that it does not conflict with the Company's festival season.

**4.3 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing Executive's duties. Reimbursement is in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policies.

**4.4 Prior Service Credit.** For purposes of vacation accrual and eligibility and waiting periods under the Company's benefit plans, the Company shall credit Executive's service with Seller, to the extent the plans permit.

## 5. Termination of Employment

**5.1 Death or Disability.** Executive's employment terminates automatically on Executive's death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment on written notice if Executive is unable, because of physical or mental illness or incapacity, to perform the essential functions of the position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for one hundred twenty (120) consecutive days or one hundred eighty (180) days in any twelve-month period ("**Disability**"). Any such determination shall comply with applicable Law.

**5.2 By the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means any of the following:

(a) Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony;

(b) Executive's fraud, embezzlement or misappropriation of Company property;

(c) Executive's willful and continued failure to perform Executive's material duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day opportunity to cure;

(d) Executive's material breach of Section 7 or Section 8; or

(e) Executive's willful misconduct that is materially injurious to the Company.

**5.3 By the Company without Cause.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment without Cause at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice. The Company may pay Base Salary in lieu of that notice.

**5.4 By Executive for Good Reason.** Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means any of the following events, occurring without Executive's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in Base Salary or target bonus;

(b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties;

(c) relocation of Executive's principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles; or

(d) the Company's material breach of this Agreement.

To resign for Good Reason, Executive must take all of the following steps:

(i) give written notice to the Company within sixty (60) days after the initial occurrence of the event;

(ii) allow the Company thirty (30) days after that notice to cure the event; and

(iii) if the Company has not cured, resign within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**5.5 By Executive without Good Reason.** Executive may resign without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period.

## 6. Payments on Termination

**6.1 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay or provide to Executive the following (the "**Accrued Obligations**"):

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the termination date;

(b) any bonus earned for a completed calendar year but not yet paid;

(c) accrued but unused vacation, to the extent required by Company policy or applicable Law;

(d) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(e) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans.

**6.2 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following, in addition to the Accrued Obligations:

(a) continued Base Salary for twelve (12) months after the termination date;

(b) a pro-rated target bonus for the year of termination;

(c) any unpaid installment of the retention award under Section 3.4; and

(d) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment of the employer portion of the premiums for up to twelve (12) months.

The severance in clause (a) is payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. Termination by non-renewal under Section 2.2 is not a termination without Cause for purposes of this Section 6.2.

**6.3 Release.** The payments under Section 6.2 are conditioned on two things:

(a) Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company and its Affiliates, in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, within sixty (60) days after the termination date; and

(b) Executive's continued compliance with Sections 7 and 8.

## 7. Confidential Information; Work Product

**7.1 Confidentiality.** During the Term and at all times afterward, Executive shall hold in confidence all confidential and proprietary information of the Company and its Affiliates. This includes:

(a) artist fees and guarantees;

(b) routing and booking plans;

(c) sponsor terms;

(d) the ticketing and fan database; and

(e) financial information.

Executive shall not use or disclose that information except in performing Executive's duties. This Section does not apply to information that becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach. It also does not restrict Executive from reporting possible violations of Law to any Governmental Authority, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower Laws.

**7.2 Work Product.** All of the following are the exclusive property of the Company:

(a) booking lists;

(b) artist and agent contact files;

(c) festival concepts; and

(d) other work product that Executive creates in the course of employment.

Executive assigns to the Company all of Executive's right, title and interest in that work product.

**7.3 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, Executive shall promptly return all Company property, documents and data in Executive's possession.

## 8. Restrictive Covenants

**8.1 Non-Competition.** During Executive's employment and for twelve (12) months after it ends for any reason (the "**Restricted Period**"), Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the promotion of concerts or the production of music festivals within the states of New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, Pennsylvania or Massachusetts. This restriction applies whether Executive acts as an owner, employee, consultant or otherwise. Executive may passively own up to two percent (2%) of the securities of any publicly traded company.

**8.2 Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, do either of the following:

(a) solicit any artist, artist agent, venue or sponsor with which the Company did business during the last twelve (12) months of Executive's employment to cease or reduce that business; or

(b) solicit for employment or hire any employee of the Company.

**8.3 Reasonableness; Enforcement.** Executive acknowledges the following:

(a) the covenants in this Section 8 are reasonable;

(b) they are necessary to protect the goodwill the Company acquired under the Purchase Agreement and its confidential information; and

(c) a breach would cause irreparable harm to the Company.

The Company is entitled to injunctive relief for any breach, in addition to any other remedy. If a court finds any covenant unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 9. Section 409A

The parties intend this Agreement to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and shall interpret it accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. A termination of employment means a "separation from service" under Section 409A. If Executive is a "specified employee" on the separation date, any payment of deferred compensation due on separation shall be delayed until the first business day after six (6) months following the separation, to the extent Section 409A requires that delay.

## 10. Miscellaneous

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission.

(a) Notices to the Company shall be sent to Baimix Live Holdings, LLC, 350 West 31st Street, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10001, Attention: General Counsel.

(b) Notices to Executive shall be sent to the most recent home address on file with the Company.

**10.2 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive, and Executive may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business. This Agreement binds and benefits the Company's successors and assigns.

**10.3 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld under applicable Law.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**10.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the internal laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**10.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.8 Survival.** Sections 6, 7, 8 and 10 survive the termination of Executive's employment.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
