# EQUIPMENT RENTAL AGREEMENT (STAGE LIGHTING)

This EQUIPMENT RENTAL AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Lumora Stage Systems LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Lessor**"), and **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Lessee**"). Lessor and Lessee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Rental of Equipment

**1.1 Rental.** Lessor rents to Lessee, and Lessee rents from Lessor, the stage lighting equipment described in each Rental Order issued under this Agreement (the "**Equipment**"). Each Rental Order is subject to this Agreement.

**1.2 Rental Orders.** A "**Rental Order**" is a written order in substantially the form of Exhibit A. Each Rental Order must be signed by both Parties and must identify:

(a) the event or events for which the Equipment is rented (each an "**Event**");

(b) the Equipment, by item, model and quantity;

(c) the delivery date, the load-out date and the delivery location (each a "**Venue**");

(d) the Rental Fee and any technician, freight or other charges; and

(e) any special rigging, power or crew requirements.

**1.3 Conflicts.** If a Rental Order conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the Rental Order expressly identifies the Section of this Agreement it varies.

**1.4 No Minimum Commitment.** Lessee is not required to issue any minimum number of Rental Orders. Lessor is not required to accept any proposed Rental Order until it signs that Rental Order.

## 2. Term

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until December 31, 2026, unless terminated earlier under Section 11. The term renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

**2.2 Effect on Rental Orders.** Expiration or termination of this Agreement does not affect any Rental Order signed before the expiration or termination, which continues to be governed by this Agreement until completed.

## 3. Rental Fees and Payment

**3.1 Rental Fees.** Lessee shall pay the rental fee stated in each Rental Order (the "**Rental Fee**"). Unless a Rental Order states otherwise, the Rental Fee is calculated at Lessor's then-current daily rates for each day from delivery to load-out, with a three-day week applied to rentals of seven (7) consecutive days or more.

**3.2 Deposit.** Lessee shall pay a deposit of twenty-five percent (25%) of the Rental Fee for each Rental Order within ten (10) days after the Rental Order is signed. The deposit is credited against the Rental Fee.

**3.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Lessor shall invoice the balance of the Rental Fee and all other charges after load-out. Lessee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Late amounts bear interest at one percent (1%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower.

**3.4 Disputed Amounts.** Lessee may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith by giving Lessor written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**3.5 Taxes.** Rental Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Lessee shall pay those taxes as invoiced, unless Lessee provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 4. Cancellation

**4.1 Cancellation by Lessee.** Lessee may cancel a Rental Order by written notice to Lessor. The following cancellation charges apply, measured from the scheduled delivery date:

| Notice of Cancellation | Cancellation Charge |
|---|---|
| More than 60 days before delivery | None; deposit refunded |
| 30 to 60 days before delivery | Deposit retained |
| Fewer than 30 days before delivery | 50% of the Rental Fee |

**4.2 Event Cancellation.** If an Event is cancelled or postponed because of a Force Majeure Event under Section 12.1, the cancellation charge under Section 4.1 is limited to the deposit, and Lessor shall credit the deposit against any Rental Order for a rescheduled Event within twelve (12) months.

## 5. Delivery, Installation and Return

**5.1 Delivery.** Lessor shall deliver the Equipment to the Venue on the delivery date stated in the Rental Order. Lessee shall arrange access to the Venue loading dock at the scheduled time.

**5.2 Installation and Technicians.** If a Rental Order includes installation, Lessor shall provide qualified technicians to rig, focus and program the Equipment, and to strike it at load-out. Lessor's technicians shall follow the reasonable safety and access rules of the Venue and of Lessee's production manager.

**5.3 Inspection.** Lessee may inspect the Equipment on delivery. Lessee shall notify Lessor of any missing or defective item within twenty-four (24) hours after delivery, or before the first performance of the Event if earlier. Lessor shall repair or replace any defective item promptly at no charge.

**5.4 Return.** At load-out, Lessee shall make the Equipment available for collection in the same condition as delivered, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Lessor shall inspect the Equipment at load-out and give Lessee a written condition report within five (5) Business Days.

**5.5 Business Day.** "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

## 6. Use of Equipment

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Lessee shall use the Equipment only at the Venue for the Event stated in the Rental Order, and only in accordance with Lessor's operating instructions and applicable law.

**6.2 Operators.** Only Lessor's technicians, or operators Lessee reasonably determines are qualified, shall operate the Equipment.

**6.3 Restrictions.** Lessee shall not sublease the Equipment, remove it from the Venue, or modify it, without Lessor's prior written consent.

**6.4 Title.** Title to the Equipment remains at all times with Lessor. Lessee shall keep the Equipment free of liens arising through Lessee.

## 7. Risk of Loss; Damage

**7.1 Risk of Loss.** Lessee bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, the Equipment from delivery until Lessor collects it at load-out, except for loss or damage caused by Lessor's technicians or by a defect in the Equipment.

**7.2 Damage Charges.** For Equipment damaged or lost while at Lessee's risk, Lessee shall pay the reasonable cost of repair or, if the item cannot reasonably be repaired, its replacement value as stated in Exhibit B. Lessor shall support each damage charge with the condition report under Section 5.4 and reasonable documentation.

## 8. Warranties

**8.1 Lessor Warranties.** Lessor warrants that:

(a) the Equipment will be in good working order and fit for professional stage lighting use when delivered;

(b) the Equipment will comply with applicable electrical safety standards; and

(c) Lessor's technicians will perform installation and strike services in a professional and workmanlike manner.

**8.2 Disclaimer.** Except as set out in Section 8.1, Lessor makes no other warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 9. Insurance

**9.1 Lessor Insurance.** Lessor shall maintain, throughout the term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000; and

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per accident.

**9.2 Lessee Insurance.** Lessee shall maintain, throughout the term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; and

(b) inland marine or equipment floater coverage for rented equipment in an amount at least equal to the replacement value of the Equipment under each Rental Order, naming Lessor as loss payee.

**9.3 Certificates.** Each Party shall deliver certificates of insurance to the other on request, and each Party shall name the other as an additional insured on its commercial general liability policy.

## 10. Indemnification; Limitation of Liability

**10.1 By Lessor.** Lessor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Lessee and its officers, managers and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessor or its technicians, or (b) a defect in the Equipment.

**10.2 By Lessee.** Lessee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Lessor and its officers, members and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessee or its personnel, or (b) Lessee's use of the Equipment in breach of Section 6.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, and reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party without its consent.

**10.4 Limitation of Liability.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost ticket revenue, except to the extent payable to a third party under Section 10.1 or Section 10.2. Except for obligations under Section 10.1 and Section 10.2 and Lessee's payment obligations under Sections 3 and 7, each Party's aggregate liability under any Rental Order shall not exceed the total Rental Fee payable under that Rental Order.

## 11. Termination

**11.1 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Rental Order by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice of it. If an Event is scheduled to begin within that cure period, the cure period is reduced to forty-eight (48) hours.

**11.2 Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**11.3 Effect.** On termination, Lessee shall pay all Rental Fees and charges accrued through the termination date and shall make all Equipment available for collection under Section 5.4. Sections 3, 7, 10, 11.3 and 13 survive termination.

## 12. Force Majeure

**12.1 Force Majeure Event.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, fire, flood, epidemic, act of government, civil unrest, venue closure or the cancellation of an Event by the artist or a Governmental authority (each a "**Force Majeure Event**").

**12.2 Notice.** The affected Party shall give prompt written notice of the Force Majeure Event and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**13.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, not to be unreasonably withheld. Either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business, whether by merger, sale of equity, sale of assets or otherwise, on written notice to the other Party. "**Affiliate**" of a Party means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party.

**13.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party granting it.

**13.5 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with its Exhibits and each Rental Order, is the entire agreement of the Parties concerning the rental of the Equipment and supersedes all prior proposals and quotations on that subject.

**13.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.
