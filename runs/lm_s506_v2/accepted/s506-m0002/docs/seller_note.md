# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**
**New York, New York**
**January 12, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal amount of $6,000,000, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Maker issues this Note at the Closing under Section 2.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement as part of the Purchase Price.

## 1. Principal; Promise to Pay

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). Maker promises to pay the Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, in accordance with Section 4.

**1.2 Purchase Price Consideration.** This Note is issued as partial payment of the Purchase Price for the Purchased Assets. No cash or other consideration was advanced by Holder to Maker in exchange for this Note.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Note:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Change of Control**" means any of the following:

(a) any Person or group of Persons acting in concert, other than Baimix Group Holdings, Inc., acquires, directly or indirectly, by purchase, merger, consolidation or otherwise, beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the equity interests or voting power of Maker;

(b) Maker merges or consolidates with or into any other Person, unless the holders of Maker's equity interests immediately before the transaction hold more than fifty percent (50%) of the equity interests and voting power of the surviving entity immediately after it; or

(c) Maker sells, leases or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of its assets in one transaction or a series of related transactions, other than to an Affiliate of Maker that assumes this Note in writing.

"**Default Rate**" means the interest rate in Section 3.1 plus two percent (2%) per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Maturity Date**" means January 12, 2029.

"**Payment Date**" means each March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31, beginning March 31, 2026.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owed to any bank, commercial lender or other institutional lender, and all refinancings of that indebtedness, together with interest, fees and expenses.

"**Senior Lender**" means each holder of Senior Debt.

## 3. Interest

**3.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount from the date of this Note at a fixed rate of six percent (6%) per annum. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**3.2 Default Interest.** While an Event of Default is continuing, interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount and, to the extent permitted by Law, on any overdue interest at the Default Rate.

**3.3 Maximum Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 4. Payments; Maturity; Prepayment

**4.1 Interest Payments.** Maker shall pay accrued interest in arrears on each Payment Date.

**4.2 Principal Payments.** Maker shall pay the Principal Amount in installments as follows:

(a) $1,500,000 on January 12, 2027;

(b) $1,500,000 on January 12, 2028; and

(c) the remaining unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, on the Maturity Date.

**4.3 Manner of Payment.** Maker shall make each payment in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing. If a payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is payable on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue for the additional days.

**4.4 Application of Payments.** Each payment shall be applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 11.3, then to accrued interest, and then to the Principal Amount.

**4.5 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments shall be applied to principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Setoff

**5.1 Right of Setoff.** Maker may set off against any amount then due or to become due under this Note any amount that a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to receive from Holder under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, to the extent the amount has been agreed in writing by Holder or determined by a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction and has not been paid from the Escrow Fund or otherwise.

**5.2 Pending Claims.** If a Buyer Indemnitee has asserted a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement in good faith by written notice to Holder, and the claim has not been resolved when a payment falls due under this Note, Maker may withhold from that payment an amount equal to the amount claimed. Maker shall pay the withheld amount, with interest at the rate in Section 3.1, within ten (10) Business Days after the claim is resolved, less any amount determined to be owed to the Buyer Indemnitee.

**5.3 Effect of Setoff.** Any amount set off under Section 5.1 is treated as a payment of this Note in the amount set off, applied in accordance with Section 4.4. Withholding in accordance with Section 5.2 is not a failure to pay and is not an Event of Default.

## 6. Events of Default; Remedies

**6.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal under this Note when due;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after it is due;

(c) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder;

(d) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due, or commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar Law;

(e) an involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar Law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(f) any Senior Debt in a principal amount exceeding $2,500,000 is accelerated and the acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days;

(g) Maker undergoes a Change of Control without the prior written consent of Holder; or

(h) Maker dissolves, liquidates or winds up its affairs.

**6.2 Acceleration.** On the occurrence of an Event of Default described in Section 6.1(d) or 6.1(e), the unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder. While any other Event of Default is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest immediately due and payable, and they shall then become due and payable without presentment, demand, protest or further notice of any kind, all of which Maker waives.

**6.3 Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Section 7, Holder's rights and remedies under this Note are cumulative and in addition to any rights and remedies available at Law or in equity. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right waives that right.

## 7. Subordination

**7.1 Subordination to Senior Debt.** The payment of the Principal Amount and interest under this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set out in this Section 7.

**7.2 Payment Blockage.** While any payment default or any event of default under any Senior Debt that permits the Senior Lender to accelerate that Senior Debt is continuing, and the Senior Lender has given written notice of it to Maker and Holder, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note, other than by setoff under Section 5. A blockage period under this Section 7.2 ends on the earlier of (a) the cure or waiver of the default and (b) one hundred eighty (180) days after the notice. Only one blockage period may begin in any 365-day period.

**7.3 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment in breach of this Section 7, Holder shall hold it in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly pay it over to the Senior Lender.

**7.4 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute a subordination agreement with any Senior Lender on terms consistent with this Section 7 and otherwise customary for subordinated seller financing.

**7.5 No Impairment.** Nothing in this Section 7 impairs, as between Maker and Holder, Maker's unconditional obligation to pay this Note in accordance with its terms, or prevents Holder from exercising its remedies under Section 6 after an Event of Default, subject to the rights of the Senior Lender under this Section 7.

## 8. Covenants of Maker

**8.1 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of the occurrence of any Event of Default within five (5) Business Days after an officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

**8.2 Financial Information.** So long as any amount under this Note is outstanding, Maker shall deliver to Holder (a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the annual financial statements of Maker, and (b) within forty-five (45) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters, the unaudited quarterly financial statements of Maker. Holder shall hold that information in confidence.

**8.3 Existence.** Maker shall maintain its existence as a limited liability company in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

## 9. Transfer; Amendment

**9.1 Transfer by Holder.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any transferee takes this Note subject to Maker's rights of setoff under Section 5 and to Section 7.

**9.2 Assignment by Maker.** Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, except to an Affiliate of Maker that assumes this Note in writing, in which case Maker remains liable for its obligations under this Note.

**9.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**9.4 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from Holder.

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims and disputes arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction.** Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of Los Angeles, California. Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such action or proceeding and waive any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Service of Process.** Each of Maker and Holder consents to service of process in any action or proceeding described in Section 10.2 by delivery in the manner provided for notices in Section 11.1, in addition to any other manner permitted by Law.
