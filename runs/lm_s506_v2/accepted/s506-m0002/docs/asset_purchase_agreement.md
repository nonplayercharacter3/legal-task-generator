# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026, by and between **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of promoting live concerts and producing music festivals in the northeastern United States, including the booking of artists, the leasing and booking of venues, the sale of sponsorships and the operation of related ticketing and fan databases (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. Baimix Group Holdings, Inc. ("**Parent**") is the sole member of Buyer.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets, properties and rights of every kind used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(b) all inventory, staging, production equipment, merchandise and supplies;

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all Intellectual Property of the Business, including the festival brands, trademarks and domain names listed on Schedule 2.1(d) and the ticketing and fan database of the Business;

(e) all permits and licenses held by Seller for the operation of the Business, to the extent transferable;

(f) all prepaid expenses, artist and venue deposits, credits and advance payments;

(g) all books, records, artist and promoter files and sponsor lists of the Business; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) Seller's cash and cash equivalents, (b) Seller's corporate seal, minute books and stock records, (c) the contracts listed on Schedule 2.2(c), (d) Seller's insurance policies, and (e) the rights of Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising prior to, on or after the Closing Date;

(b) all trade accounts payable of Seller to third parties in connection with the Business that remain unpaid at the Closing Date and are listed on Schedule 2.3(b); and

(c) all Liabilities in respect of ticket refunds for events scheduled to occur after the Closing Date for which ticket proceeds were transferred to Buyer at the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible for any Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include (a) all Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any period ending on or before the Closing Date, (b) all Liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets, (c) all Liabilities in respect of any current or former employee of Seller arising on or before the Closing Date, (d) all indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller, and (e) all Liabilities arising from any Action pending against Seller at the Closing Date and listed on Schedule 2.4(e).

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $30,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. The Purchase Price shall be paid as follows:

(a) $21,000,000 in cash, payable by Buyer to Seller at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller (the "**Closing Cash Payment**");

(b) $3,000,000 in cash, payable by Buyer to the Escrow Agent at the Closing in accordance with Section 2.6; and

(c) $6,000,000 by delivery of the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer.

**2.6 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $3,000,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account (the "**Escrow Fund**") and released in accordance with the terms of the Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Fund shall serve as a source of recovery for amounts payable to the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX.

**2.7 Allocation of Purchase Price.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets as set out on Schedule 2.7, prepared in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.8 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as Buyer is required to deduct and withhold under applicable Tax Law.

**2.9 Third-Party Consents.** To the extent any Assigned Contract may not be assigned without the consent of a third party that has not been obtained at the Closing, this Agreement shall not constitute an assignment of that Assigned Contract, and Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent and, pending its receipt, shall provide Buyer with the benefits of that Assigned Contract.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on January 12, 2026, concurrently with the execution of this Agreement (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Delphine Okafor-Ruiz;

(d) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 3.2(d);

(e) payoff letters and Encumbrance releases for all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets;

(f) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors and shareholders authorizing this Agreement; and

(g) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying as to the matters set out in Section 4.1 and Section 4.2.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the Closing Cash Payment to Seller;

(b) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent;

(c) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;

(d) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and

(e) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Parent, as sole member of Buyer, authorizing this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedules, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Qualification.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of New York and has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement do not violate Seller's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Seller, and, except as listed on Schedule 4.3, do not require the consent of any Person under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited financial statements of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2023 and 2024 and the unaudited financial statements for the eleven-month period ended November 30, 2025. Those financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business.

**4.5 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Business has no Liabilities except those reflected in the financial statements, those incurred in the ordinary course since November 30, 2025, and those listed on Schedule 4.5.

**4.6 Absence of Certain Changes.** Since December 31, 2024, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course and there has been no Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in or license to use, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of the festival brands, trademarks and domain names listed on Schedule 2.1(d) and of the ticketing and fan database of the Business.

**4.8 Sufficiency of Assets.** The Purchased Assets constitute all of the assets, rights and properties necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.9 Condition of Tangible Assets.** The staging, production equipment and other tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

**4.10 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach of or default under any Assigned Contract, and Seller has not received any written notice of termination of any Assigned Contract.

**4.11 Sponsorship Contracts.** Schedule 4.11 lists each sponsorship agreement included in the Assigned Contracts, together with the sponsorship fees payable under it for the 2026 festival season. Seller has performed all of its activation and deliverable obligations under those agreements through the Closing Date, no sponsor has asserted any right to a refund, make-good or fee reduction, and no sponsor has indicated an intention not to renew.

**4.12 Intellectual Property.** The operation of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person, and no Action alleging any such infringement is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened.

**4.13 Data Privacy.** Seller has collected, stored and used the personal information in the ticketing and fan database in compliance with applicable Law and Seller's published privacy policies, and has the right to transfer that database to Buyer.

**4.14 Legal Proceedings.** Except as listed on Schedule 4.14, there is no Action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.15 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller has complied in all material respects with all Laws applicable to the Business and holds all permits required to operate the Business, each of which is valid and in full force and effect.

**4.16 Employee Matters.** Schedule 4.16 lists each employee of the Business, with title and compensation. Seller is not party to any collective bargaining agreement relating to the Business.

**4.17 Taxes.** Seller has filed all Tax returns required to be filed in respect of the Business and paid all Taxes shown as due, and there are no Encumbrances for Taxes on the Purchased Assets.

**4.18 Insurance.** Schedule 4.18 lists all insurance policies maintained by Seller for the Business, including event cancellation and general liability coverage, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.19 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any brokerage, finder's or other fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Buyer has full limited liability company power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand to pay the Closing Cash Payment and the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives to, hold in confidence all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent that information is generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Section.

**6.2 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) promote concerts or produce music festivals within the states of New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, Pennsylvania or Massachusetts, (b) solicit any artist, venue or sponsor that was a counterparty to an Assigned Contract to cease doing business with Buyer, or (c) solicit for employment any employee of Buyer who was employed in the Business at the Closing.

**6.3 Further Assurances.** Each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

**6.4 Receivables and Mail.** Seller shall promptly remit to Buyer any payment received by Seller after the Closing in respect of the Purchased Assets and shall forward to Buyer any mail or other communication relating to the Business.

**6.5 Name Change.** Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall amend its certificate of incorporation to change its corporate name to a name that does not include "Tinev" or any confusingly similar name.

## ARTICLE VII. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**7.1 Offers of Employment.** Buyer may, in its discretion, offer employment effective as of the Closing Date to any employee of the Business listed on Schedule 4.16. Seller shall terminate the employment of each such employee who accepts Buyer's offer, effective as of the Closing Date.

**7.2 Accrued Compensation.** Seller shall pay to each employee of the Business all wages, commissions, bonuses and accrued vacation earned through the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar Taxes for any period that begins before and ends after the Closing Date shall be apportioned between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date based on the number of days in that period.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.17 survive until the date that is six (6) years after the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the Parties survive the Closing in accordance with their terms. Any claim asserted in good faith with reasonable specificity by written notice before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any Assumed Liability; and

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for which it seeks indemnification, describing the claim in reasonable detail and stating the estimated amount of Losses, if reasonably ascertainable. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $3,600,000 (the "**Cap**"), except that the Cap shall not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or the representations in Section 4.17, or from fraud. The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to the Cap.

(b) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $300,000 (the "**Deductible**"), in which case Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The Deductible shall not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or from fraud.

(c) Claims under Section 9.3(b) shall not be subject to the Cap or the Deductible.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualification in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

(e) No Party shall be liable for punitive damages except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.6 Sources of Recovery.** Losses payable to the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund, to the extent then available, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; second, at Buyer's election, by setoff against amounts then due or to become due under the Seller Note in accordance with its terms; and third, directly from Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Subject to Section 11.10 and except in the case of fraud, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties with respect to any breach of representation, warranty, covenant or agreement in this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Agreement shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**10.2 Post-Closing Consents.** Any third-party consent listed on Schedule 4.3 that is not delivered at the Closing shall be pursued after the Closing in accordance with Section 2.9.
