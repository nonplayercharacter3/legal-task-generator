# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026, by and among **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Calderline Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent ("**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Seller's concert promotion and music festival business.

B. Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $3,000,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit is to serve as a source of recovery for amounts payable to the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of the Purchase Agreement or of any term defined in it. In this Agreement:

"**Claim Notice**" means a written notice from Buyer that meets the requirements of Section 4.1.

"**Closing Date**" means January 12, 2026.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest and earnings on it, less all distributions made under this Agreement.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, delivered to Escrow Agent with a certificate of the presenting party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

## 2. Appointment; Deposit

**2.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Escrow Agent shall acknowledge receipt to Buyer and Seller in writing.

**2.2 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint Escrow Agent as escrow agent to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement. Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account. The Escrow Fund shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party.

## 3. Investment

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in an interest-bearing deposit account of Escrow Agent insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits. If Buyer and Seller give Joint Written Instructions, Escrow Agent shall instead invest the Escrow Fund in United States Treasury obligations maturing within ninety (90) days, or in a money market fund investing only in those obligations.

**3.2 No Liability for Investment.** Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss arising from an investment made in accordance with Section 3.1. Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a distribution required under this Agreement.

## 4. Claims and Release of Escrow Fund

**4.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver a Claim Notice to Escrow Agent, with a copy to Seller, if Buyer asserts in good faith that a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice must (a) describe the claim in reasonable detail and (b) state the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of that amount (the "**Claimed Amount**").

**4.2 Release Date.** On the date that is six (6) months following the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the entire balance of the Escrow Fund. Escrow Agent shall first deduct the aggregate Claimed Amounts of all Claim Notices delivered to Escrow Agent prior to the Release Date that remain unresolved on the Release Date. Escrow Agent shall retain each amount so deducted until it is released under Section 4.3 or Section 4.4. Upon payment of the balance to Seller under this Section 4.2, Escrow Agent shall have no further obligation in respect of that balance.

**4.3 Objection; Release of Claimed Amounts.**

(a) Seller may object to all or part of a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Escrow Agent and Buyer within twenty (20) Business Days after Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice must state the amount objected to and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

(b) If Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within that period, Escrow Agent shall release the Claimed Amount to Buyer on the next Business Day.

(c) If Seller objects to only part of a Claimed Amount, Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed part to Buyer on the next Business Day. Escrow Agent shall retain the disputed part until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

(d) Any amount retained for an unresolved Claim Notice after the Release Date shall be released to Seller to the extent the claim is resolved in Seller's favor. It shall be released to Buyer to the extent the claim is resolved in Buyer's favor.

**4.4 Joint Written Instructions and Final Orders.** Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse any part of the Escrow Fund in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall promptly sign and deliver Joint Written Instructions to give effect to any resolution of a claim between them.

**4.5 Disbursement Mechanics.** Escrow Agent shall make each disbursement by wire transfer to the account designated in writing by the recipient. Escrow Agent may call back an authorized representative of the instructing party to confirm any wire instruction, and may delay a disbursement until the call-back is completed.

## 5. Duties of Escrow Agent

**5.1 Limited Duties.** Escrow Agent's duties are solely ministerial. They are limited to those expressly set out in this Agreement, and no implied duties apply. Escrow Agent is not a party to, or bound by, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**5.2 Reliance.** Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature it believes in good faith to be genuine and properly authorized. Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any notice, instruction or signature.

**5.3 Limitation of Liability.** Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. Escrow Agent is not liable for any indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**5.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its service under this Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to the extent of Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount. This Section survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of Escrow Agent.

**5.5 Disputes.** If Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or claims, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Escrow Agent may also interplead the disputed amount into a court described in Section 9.6.

## 6. Fees and Expenses

Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set out in Schedule A. Escrow Agent shall also be reimbursed for its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses.

## 7. Tax Matters

**7.1 Tax Reporting.** For tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is distributed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Fund. Before the Closing Date, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9.

**7.2 Tax Distributions.** Escrow Agent has no responsibility for the payment of any tax on income of the Escrow Fund, except to file information returns as required by Law.

## 8. Resignation and Removal

**8.1 Resignation.** Escrow Agent may resign by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may remove Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions given on thirty (30) days' notice.

**8.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 9.6 to appoint one. Escrow Agent's only duty until then is to hold the Escrow Fund. On appointment of a successor, Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Fund to the successor and be discharged from further obligations.

**9.2 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when Escrow Agent has disbursed the entire Escrow Fund in accordance with its terms. Sections 5.3, 5.4 and 6 survive termination.

**9.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and Escrow Agent.

**9.4 Assignment.** No party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**9.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the internal laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**9.6 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.8 Patriot Act.** Each party shall provide Escrow Agent with the information Escrow Agent reasonably requests to comply with the USA PATRIOT Act and other applicable "know your customer" requirements.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
