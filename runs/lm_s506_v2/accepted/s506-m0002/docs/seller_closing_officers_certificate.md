# SELLER CLOSING OFFICER'S CERTIFICATE

**TINEV CONCERTS & FESTIVALS, INC.**

**Dated: January 12, 2026**

## 1. Background

**1.1 Delivery.** The undersigned delivers this certificate (this "**Certificate**") under Section 3.2(g) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between **Baimix Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**").

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Certificate have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.3 Capacity.** The undersigned is the duly elected and acting President of Seller. The undersigned signs and delivers this Certificate in that capacity, on behalf of Seller, and not in an individual capacity.

## 2. Certifications as to Organization and Qualification (Section 4.1)

The undersigned certifies to Buyer, on behalf of Seller, as of the Closing Date, as follows:

**2.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of New York.

**2.2 Corporate Power.** Seller has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business as currently conducted.

**2.3 No Dissolution.** No proceeding is pending or, to the knowledge of the undersigned, threatened for the dissolution, liquidation or winding up of Seller. Seller's board of directors and shareholders have not adopted any resolution or plan to dissolve Seller.

## 3. Certifications as to Authority and Enforceability (Section 4.2)

The undersigned further certifies to Buyer, on behalf of Seller, as of the Closing Date, as follows:

**3.1 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to enter into the Agreement and each Ancillary Agreement to which it is a party, and to perform its obligations under them.

**3.2 Corporate Action.** Seller's board of directors and shareholders have duly authorized the execution, delivery and performance of the Agreement and each Ancillary Agreement to which Seller is a party, and of the transactions contemplated by them. The authorizing resolutions are the resolutions certified in the certificate of the Secretary of Seller delivered under Section 3.2(f) of the Agreement. Those resolutions have not been amended, modified or rescinded and remain in full force and effect.

**3.3 Execution.** The Agreement has been duly executed and delivered by Seller. Each of the Ancillary Agreements to which Seller is a party, including the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, has been duly executed and delivered by Seller on the Closing Date.

**3.4 Enforceability.** The Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Incumbency

**4.1 Authorized Signatory.** The individual who signed the Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements on behalf of Seller is the duly elected and acting President of Seller. On the date of signing, that individual held that office and was authorized to sign those documents on Seller's behalf. The signature appearing on those documents is that individual's genuine signature.

## 5. General

**5.1 Reliance.** Buyer may rely on this Certificate in consummating the Closing.

**5.2 Delivery by Electronic Transmission.** This Certificate may be delivered by electronic transmission. A signature delivered by electronic transmission is deemed an original signature.
