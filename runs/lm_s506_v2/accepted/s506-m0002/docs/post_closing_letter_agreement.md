**Re: Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026: Post-Closing Letter Agreement**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") is entered into between Baimix Live Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Seller**"). It refers to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 between Buyer and Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer acquired substantially all of the assets of Seller's regional concert promotion and music festival business for a Purchase Price of $30,000,000. The Closing occurred on January 12, 2026.

B. Buyer delivered to Seller, at Seller's notice address under Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, a written notice of claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, dated July 20, 2026 (the "**July Claim Notice**"). The July Claim Notice asserts Losses of $1,150,000 arising from an inaccuracy in or breach of Section 4.11 (Sponsorship Contracts) of the Purchase Agreement. The asserted Losses relate to refund and make-good demands made by two sponsors listed on Schedule 4.11, based on activation deliverables those sponsors allege were not performed before the Closing Date. Seller disputed the claim in full by letter dated August 14, 2026. Each of Buyer and Seller reserves all of its rights and remedies with respect to the claim.

C. Since the Closing, the Parties have identified certain domain names and social media accounts of the Business that were not transferred to Buyer at the Closing. They have also obtained a third-party consent to the assignment of an Assigned Contract that was listed on Schedule 4.3 and not delivered at the Closing.

D. Baimix Group Holdings, Inc., the sole member of Buyer, has entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement dated September 22, 2026 with Orrant Media Partners, L.P. ("**Orrant**"). Under that agreement, Baimix Group Holdings, Inc. will sell all of the membership interests of Buyer to Orrant (the "**Orrant Transaction**"). Closing of the Orrant Transaction is expected in December 2026. In connection with its due diligence, Orrant has requested information about the pre-Closing operation of the Business that is in Seller's possession.

E. Buyer and Seller wish to record their agreement on the matters set out below.

Buyer and Seller therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and References

**1.1** References in this Letter Agreement to Sections and Schedules are to Sections and Schedules of the Purchase Agreement unless otherwise stated.

**1.2** "**Supplemental Assets**" means the domain names and social media accounts listed in Annex A to this Letter Agreement.

## 2. Supplemental Assets

**2.1 Transfer.** Seller sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Buyer all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Supplemental Assets, effective as of the Closing Date. The Supplemental Assets are Purchased Assets described in Section 2.1(d), and Schedule 2.1(d) is supplemented to include them. No additional consideration is payable for the Supplemental Assets.

**2.2 Credentials and Registrar Transfers.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this Letter Agreement, Seller shall:

(a) deliver to Buyer all administrative log-in credentials, recovery email addresses and two-factor authentication devices for each social media account listed in Annex A;

(b) initiate the transfer of each domain name listed in Annex A to the registrar account Buyer designates, and provide all authorization codes; and

(c) execute such further instruments as Buyer reasonably requests under Section 6.3 to record Buyer's ownership of the Supplemental Assets.

**2.3 Interim Use.** Until the transfers under Section 2.2 are complete, Seller shall not post content to, modify, redirect or allow to lapse any Supplemental Asset, except as Buyer directs in writing.

## 3. Amendment to Section 1.1

The definition of "Fundamental Representations" in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement is amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

> "**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller set out in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.19.

## 4. Post-Closing Consent

**4.1 Hudson Pier Amphitheater License.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the written consent of Hudson Pier Venues LLC, dated September 15, 2026, to the assignment to Buyer of the Venue License Agreement dated March 3, 2024 between Hudson Pier Venues LLC and Seller. That license is listed on Schedule 2.1(c) and Schedule 4.3. Under Section 2.9, the assignment of that Assigned Contract to Buyer is effective as of September 15, 2026.

**4.2 Pass-Through Accounting.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the date of this Letter Agreement, Seller shall deliver to Buyer an accounting of all amounts Seller received or paid under that license between the Closing Date and September 15, 2026, in performance of its obligation under Section 2.9 to provide Buyer with the benefits of that license. Seller shall remit to Buyer, within the same period, any net amount received by Seller and not previously remitted under Section 6.4.

## 5. Cooperation in Connection with the Orrant Transaction

**5.1 Permitted Disclosure.** Section 6.1 of the Purchase Agreement does not restrict Seller from disclosing information concerning the Business or the Purchased Assets to Orrant or its legal, financial and accounting advisors, to the extent the disclosure is made at Buyer's written request and in response to Orrant's due diligence on the Orrant Transaction. Before any disclosure, Buyer shall confirm to Seller in writing that Orrant and its advisors are bound by confidentiality obligations to Buyer or Baimix Group Holdings, Inc. covering that information.

**5.2 Diligence Requests.** Seller shall respond to reasonable written requests for pre-Closing records of the Business that Buyer forwards to Seller, within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of each request. Seller is not required to prepare new financial statements or to give any representation or warranty to Orrant.

**5.3 Expenses.** Buyer shall reimburse Seller for its reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in complying with this Section 5, within thirty (30) days after receipt of an invoice.

## 6. July Claim Notice

**6.1 Exchange of Information.** Within twenty (20) Business Days after the date of this Letter Agreement, Seller shall make available to Buyer copies of all correspondence, activation reports and proof-of-performance materials in Seller's possession relating to the sponsorship deliverables that are the subject of the July Claim Notice.

**6.2 Meeting.** Senior representatives of Buyer and Seller shall meet, in person or by video conference, within thirty (30) days after the information described in Section 6.1 is made available, to discuss the claim in good faith.

**6.3 Settlement Communications.** Communications exchanged under this Section 6 are settlement communications and shall not be admissible in any Action to prove liability for, or the amount of, the claim.

## 7. General

**7.1 Effect.** Except as expressly provided in this Letter Agreement, the Purchase Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the date of this Letter Agreement, each reference in the Purchase Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Purchase Agreement as amended by this Letter Agreement.

**7.2 Amendment.** This Letter Agreement is a written instrument signed by each Party for purposes of Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement. It may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller.

**7.3 Notices.** Notices under this Letter Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.4 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Letter Agreement is governed by the internal laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 11.8 and Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Letter Agreement as if set out in full.

**7.5 Counterparts.** This Letter Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
