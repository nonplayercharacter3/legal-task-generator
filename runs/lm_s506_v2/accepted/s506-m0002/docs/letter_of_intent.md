**Re: Letter of Intent: Proposed Acquisition of the Concert Promotion and Festival Business of Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Baimix Live Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of the regional concert promotion and music festival business (the "**Business**") of Tinev Concerts & Festivals, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Seller**"). The proposed acquisition is referred to as the "**Transaction**."

Except for Sections 8 through 12, which are binding, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' current intentions. It does not create any obligation to complete the Transaction.

## 1. Structure

Buyer will purchase substantially all of the assets used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**") under a definitive asset purchase agreement to be negotiated and signed by Buyer and Seller (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). Baimix Group Holdings, Inc., the sole member of Buyer, is expected to approve the Transaction before signing.

## 2. Purchased Assets

The Purchased Assets are expected to include:

(a) the artist engagement agreements, venue booking and license agreements, and sponsorship agreements of the Business that Buyer elects to take by assignment (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(b) all festival brands, trademarks, trade names, logos, domain names and social media accounts of the Business;

(c) the ticketing and fan database of the Business;

(d) staging, production equipment, inventory and merchandise;

(e) accounts receivable, prepaid expenses, and artist and venue deposits; and

(f) books, records, sponsor lists and goodwill of the Business.

Seller's cash, insurance policies, corporate records and any contracts Buyer does not elect to take will be excluded.

## 3. Assumed Liabilities

Buyer will assume only the following liabilities of Seller:

(a) only liabilities under assigned contracts arising after Closing, meaning obligations to be performed under the Assigned Contracts after the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"), and not any liability arising from any breach, default, act or omission of Seller occurring at or before the Closing;

(b) trade accounts payable of the Business that are unpaid at the Closing and specifically listed in the Definitive Agreement; and

(c) ticket refund obligations for events scheduled after the Closing, to the extent the related ticket proceeds are transferred to Buyer at the Closing.

All other liabilities of Seller, including all taxes for pre-Closing periods, indebtedness for borrowed money, pre-Closing employee liabilities and pending litigation, will remain with Seller.

## 4. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price will be $30,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), payable as follows:

(a) $21,000,000 in cash at the Closing;

(b) $3,000,000 deposited at the Closing with a mutually acceptable escrow agent, to be held as a source of recovery for Seller's indemnification obligations, on release terms to be set out in a separate escrow agreement; and

(c) $6,000,000 by delivery of a subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller (the "**Seller Note**"), with interest, amortization and setoff terms to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement.

The Purchase Price assumes that the Purchased Assets will be delivered free and clear of all liens, and that the Business will have a normal level of working capital at the Closing.

## 5. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for a transaction of this kind, including representations as to title to the Purchased Assets, ownership of the festival brands and ticketing and fan database, the Assigned Contracts and sponsorships, financial statements, and data privacy. General representations are expected to survive for 18 months after the Closing Date, with representations on organization, authority, title and brokers surviving for a longer period. Seller's indemnity for breaches of general representations will be subject to a customary deductible and cap to be agreed.

## 6. Key Employee; Restrictive Covenants

As a condition to Closing, Delphine Okafor-Ruiz, Seller's head of talent booking, will enter into an employment agreement with Buyer on terms acceptable to Buyer. Seller will agree to customary non-competition and non-solicitation covenants for five (5) years after the Closing.

## 7. Due Diligence; Timing

Buyer and its advisors will complete business, financial, legal and tax due diligence on the Business. Seller will give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the books, records, contracts, personnel and premises of the Business. The parties intend to sign the Definitive Agreement and complete the Closing at the same time, targeting January 2026.

## 8. Exclusivity

For forty-five (45) days after Seller countersigns this Letter, Seller will not, and will cause its officers, directors, shareholders and representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or accept any proposal from any person other than Buyer for the acquisition of the Business, any material part of the Purchased Assets, or any equity interest in Seller. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any such proposal it receives.

## 9. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, will be kept confidential under the confidentiality agreement between the parties dated September 30, 2025, which remains in full force and effect.

## 10. Expenses

Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 11. Non-Binding Effect; Termination

Except for Sections 8 through 12, this Letter does not create any legally binding obligation. No binding obligation to complete the Transaction will exist unless and until the Definitive Agreement is signed and delivered. Either party may end negotiations at any time by written notice to the other. Sections 9, 10 and 12 will survive any such termination.

## 12. Governing Law

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 13. Counterparts

This Letter may be signed in counterparts, and signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
