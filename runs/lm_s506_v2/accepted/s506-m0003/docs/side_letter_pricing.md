2.1 In consideration of the Discount, during the Subscription Term Licensee shall use the Platform as its exclusive traceability and process analytics software for Covered Operations.

3.1 Subject to Licensee's compliance with Section 2, Licensor shall apply a discount of 15% to the Subscription Fees stated in the Order Form for each year of the Subscription Term.
