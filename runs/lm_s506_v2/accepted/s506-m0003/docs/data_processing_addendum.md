# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Protoux CellTrace Platform**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and between:

**Glenox Circular Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 400 Lee Road, Rochester, New York 14606 ("**Customer**"); and

**Protoux Analytics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 1120 Congress Avenue, Suite 900, Austin, Texas 78701 ("**Processor**").

## Background

A. Customer and Processor are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Processor provides Customer with the Protoux CellTrace battery chain-of-custody and materials-recovery analytics platform and related services.

B. In providing the Platform, Processor will process Personal Data on behalf of Customer. This includes Personal Data contained in chain-of-custody records, feedstock intake records and access logs.

C. This DPA is the Data Processing Addendum referred to in the Agreement. It sets out the parties' obligations for that processing.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Authorized Users," "Customer Data," "Data Protection Laws," "Licensed Site," "Order Form," "Platform," "Statement of Work" and "Subscription Term."

1.2 "**Customer Personal Data**" means Personal Data contained in Customer Data that Processor processes on behalf of Customer under the Agreement.

1.3 "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

1.4 "**Losses**" means all losses, liabilities, damages, penalties, fines, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' and consultants' fees and costs of investigation, notification and remediation.

1.5 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, and any information defined as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under Data Protection Laws.

1.6 "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**processed**" have corresponding meanings.

1.7 "**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data while it is in Processor's or a Subprocessor's possession or control.

1.8 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 **Roles.** For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the business or controller, and Processor is the service provider or processor, as those terms (or equivalent terms) are used in Data Protection Laws.

2.2 **Details of Processing.** Annex 1 describes the subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the processing, the categories of Data Subjects and the types of Customer Personal Data.

2.3 **Relationship to Agreement.** This DPA forms part of the Agreement. It applies for as long as Processor processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## 3. Customer Obligations

3.1 **Instructions.** The Agreement, the Order Form, each Statement of Work, and Customer's configuration and use of the Platform are Customer's complete documented instructions to Processor for the processing of Customer Personal Data. Customer may issue additional written instructions that are consistent with the Agreement. Customer shall ensure that its instructions comply with Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Lawful Basis, Notices and Consents.** Customer is responsible for the accuracy, quality and legality of Customer Personal Data and of the means by which Customer acquired it. Customer shall give all notices to, and obtain all consents from, Data Subjects that Data Protection Laws require for Processor to process Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. This includes individuals who deliver batteries to Customer's drop-off and collection points, collection and haulage drivers, and Customer's employees and contractors whose details are recorded in chain-of-custody, intake or access records.

3.3 **Data Minimization.** Customer shall not submit to the Platform any Personal Data that is not reasonably necessary for the purposes described in Annex 1. Customer shall not submit government identification numbers, financial account numbers, health information or biometric data unless the Order Form or a Statement of Work expressly provides for it.

## 4. Processor Obligations

4.1 **Processing on Instructions.** Processor shall process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions under Section 3.1, unless applicable law requires otherwise. In that case, Processor shall inform Customer of the legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it.

4.2 **Unlawful Instructions.** Processor shall promptly inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws. Processor may suspend the affected processing until Customer confirms or modifies the instruction.

4.3 **Restrictions.** Processor shall not (a) sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in Data Protection Laws; (b) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than performing its obligations under the Agreement; (c) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties; or (d) combine Customer Personal Data with Personal Data it receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as Data Protection Laws permit for service providers.

4.4 **Confidentiality of Personnel.** Processor shall ensure that each person it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is bound by an appropriate duty of confidentiality and receives appropriate data protection training.

4.5 **Compliance Notice.** Processor shall notify Customer if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under this DPA or Data Protection Laws.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 **General Authorization.** Customer authorizes Processor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 2 and, subject to Section 5.2, additional Subprocessors.

5.2 **New Subprocessors.** Processor shall notify Customer in writing at least 30 days before engaging any new Subprocessor. If Customer objects on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days after that notice, the parties shall discuss Customer's concerns in good faith. If they cannot resolve the objection within 30 days, Customer may terminate the affected portion of the Platform subscription. Processor shall then refund any prepaid Fees for the terminated portion that relate to the period after termination.

5.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Processor shall impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective of Customer Personal Data than those in this DPA. Processor remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of those obligations.

## 6. Security

6.1 **Security Measures.** Processor shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Annex 3. These measures are designed to protect Customer Personal Data against Security Incidents and are appropriate to the risk of the processing.

6.2 **Updates.** Processor may update the measures in Annex 3 from time to time, provided that the updates do not materially reduce the overall level of protection for Customer Personal Data.

6.3 **Data Location.** Processor shall host Customer Personal Data in data centers located in the United States. Processor shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 **Data Subject Requests.** If Processor receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws for Customer Personal Data, Processor shall promptly forward the request to Customer and shall not respond to it except as Customer instructs. Taking into account the nature of the processing, Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer, including through functionality of the Platform, so that Customer can respond to the request.

7.2 **Assessments and Inquiries.** Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer for any data protection assessment that Data Protection Laws require of Customer in connection with the Platform. Processor shall also assist Customer in responding to any inquiry from a regulator about the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

7.3 **Costs.** Processor provides assistance under this Section 7 at no additional charge, to the extent the assistance can be given through standard Platform functionality or Support Services. Other assistance is charged at Processor's then-current professional services rates, as agreed in advance in writing.

## 8. Security Incidents and Audits

8.1 **Notification.** Processor shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. The notice shall describe, to the extent known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Processor shall update the information as it becomes available.

8.2 **Response.** Processor shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Processor shall cooperate with Customer in giving any notices that Data Protection Laws require Customer to give to Data Subjects or regulators.

8.3 **Reports.** On written request, no more than once in any 12-month period, Processor shall provide Customer with a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report or an equivalent independent audit report covering the Platform.

8.4 **Audits.** If the reports under Section 8.3 do not reasonably demonstrate Processor's compliance with this DPA, or if a regulator requires it, Customer may audit Processor's compliance with this DPA, no more than once in any 12-month period. Customer must give at least 30 days' written notice. The audit shall take place during business hours, at Customer's cost, and subject to reasonable confidentiality and security conditions. Customer may use an independent auditor that is not a competitor of Processor.

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 **By Processor.** Processor shall defend Customer against any third-party claim, including any claim by a Data Subject or regulator, arising from Processor's or a Subprocessor's breach of this DPA. Processor shall pay any damages finally awarded, or any settlement agreed by Processor, in that claim.

9.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Processor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against any and all Losses, including regulatory fines, arising out of or in connection with:

(a) Processor's processing of Customer Personal Data in accordance with Customer's instructions, including instructions given through Customer's configuration and use of the Platform;

(b) Customer's failure to give any notice to, or obtain any consent from, any Data Subject as required by Section 3.2 or by Data Protection Laws; or

(c) any Personal Data submitted to the Platform by Customer or its Authorized Users in breach of Section 3.3.

9.3 **Procedure.** Section 12.5 of the Agreement applies to claims under this Section 9.

## 10. Liability

10.1 **Limitations.** Each party's liability arising out of or in connection with this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in Section 11 of the Agreement.

10.2 **Regulatory Cooperation.** Each party shall notify the other promptly of any investigation or proceeding by a regulator concerning Customer Personal Data processed under the Agreement, to the extent the law permits.

## 11. Return and Deletion

11.1 **Return.** Customer may export Customer Personal Data during the Subscription Term and for the period described in Section 8.6 of the Agreement.

11.2 **Deletion.** Within 30 days after the end of the export period under Section 8.6 of the Agreement, Processor shall delete all Customer Personal Data in its and its Subprocessors' possession, including copies. Processor may retain Customer Personal Data in backups for up to 90 days, or for longer if applicable law requires it. Any retained data remains subject to this DPA until deleted. On request, Processor shall certify the deletion in writing.

## 12. General

12.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the date above and remains in effect until Processor has deleted all Customer Personal Data in accordance with Section 11.

12.2 **Changes in Law.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires a change to this DPA, the parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this DPA as needed to comply.

12.3 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Section 16 of the Agreement applies to this DPA.

12.4 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents under Section 8.1 shall also be sent by email to Customer's designated privacy contact.

12.5 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written document signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

12.6 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.

## Annex 1 – Details of Processing

**Subject matter and purpose:** Provision of the Platform, Support Services and services under each Statement of Work. This includes recording battery chain-of-custody, feedstock intake, materials-recovery and process analytics for Customer's operations at the Licensed Site.

**Nature of processing:** Collection, storage, organization, analysis, retrieval, reporting and deletion through the hosted Platform and its edge connectors.

**Duration:** The Subscription Term, plus the export and deletion periods described in Section 11.

**Categories of Data Subjects:** (a) individuals who deliver batteries to Customer's drop-off and collection points; (b) collection and haulage drivers and their employers' contact personnel; (c) Customer's employees, contractors and Authorized Users; and (d) contact personnel of Customer's feedstock suppliers and offtakers.

**Types of Personal Data:** Names; business and personal contact details; vehicle registration and load identifiers; signatures and timestamps on custody records; site access and badge records; Platform user credentials and activity logs.

**Sensitive data:** None, except as expressly provided in the Order Form or a Statement of Work.

## Annex 3 – Technical and Organizational Measures

1. Encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
2. Role-based access controls, least-privilege access, and multi-factor authentication for Processor personnel and Platform administrators.
3. Logical segregation of Customer Data from the data of Processor's other customers.
4. Logging and monitoring of access to production systems, with logs retained for at least 12 months.
5. Annual third-party penetration testing and continuous vulnerability scanning, with remediation of critical findings within 15 days.
6. Daily encrypted backups, with documented business continuity and disaster recovery plans tested at least annually.
7. Background checks for personnel with access to Customer Personal Data, where permitted by law.
8. A documented incident response plan, reviewed at least annually.
9. Secure configuration and signed updates for edge connectors deployed at the Licensed Site.
