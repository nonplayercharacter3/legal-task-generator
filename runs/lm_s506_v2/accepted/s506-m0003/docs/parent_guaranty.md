# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 2, 2026 by **Glenox Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office at 400 Lee Road, Rochester, New York 14606 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Protoux Analytics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 1120 Congress Avenue, Suite 900, Austin, Texas 78701 ("**Protoux**").

## Background

A. Protoux and Glenox Circular Materials, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Glenox**"), are entering into a Software License Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Protoux licenses the Protoux CellTrace platform to Glenox.

B. Glenox is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the Agreement.

C. Protoux has required this Guaranty as a condition of entering into the Agreement.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Contract Documents**" means the Agreement, the Order Form, the Data Processing Addendum and each Statement of Work. Each is included as in effect from time to time.

1.3 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all present and future payment obligations of Glenox to Protoux under the Contract Documents, including Fees and any other amounts that become due and payable by Glenox under the Contract Documents. It also includes all other obligations of Glenox under the Contract Documents, whether those obligations are actual or contingent.

## 2. Guaranty

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Protoux the due and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations. This is a guaranty of payment and performance and not merely of collection.

2.2 **Demand.** If Glenox fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall pay or perform it. The Guarantor shall do so within 10 business days after receiving Protoux's written demand. The demand must describe the Guaranteed Obligation that Glenox has failed to pay or perform, and the amount claimed.

2.3 **Extent of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is no greater than Glenox's liability under the Contract Documents. The Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Glenox could assert under the Contract Documents. However, it may not assert any defense based on Glenox's insolvency, bankruptcy, dissolution or lack of capacity or authority.

## 3. Nature of Obligations

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or reduced by any of the following:

(a) any amendment, extension, renewal or waiver of any Contract Document that is agreed between Protoux and Glenox;

(b) any time, indulgence or forbearance that Protoux grants to Glenox;

(c) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or dissolution of Glenox; or

(d) any invalidity or unenforceability of any obligation of Glenox that arises from Glenox's lack of capacity or authority.

However, the Guarantor's liability for any obligation that an amendment increases is subject to Section 2.3.

3.3 **No Prior Action Required.** Protoux need not first proceed against Glenox before making a demand on the Guarantor under Section 2.2. Protoux also need not first enforce any other security before making that demand. Protoux must, however, have given Glenox any notice and cure period that the Contract Documents require for the relevant Guaranteed Obligation.

3.4 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty is reinstated if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned. This includes any payment rescinded or returned on Glenox's insolvency or bankruptcy. On reinstatement, this Guaranty applies to that payment as though it had not been made.

3.5 **Subrogation.** The Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution or reimbursement against Glenox for payments made under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

The Guarantor represents and warrants to Protoux as of the date of this Guaranty that:

(a) it is a limited liability company duly formed, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and it has taken all necessary limited liability company action to authorize that execution, delivery and performance;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity; and

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement by which it is bound.

## 5. Payments

5.1 **Manner of Payment.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account that Protoux designates in its demand.

5.2 **Taxes.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without deduction for taxes, except as required by law.

5.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** The Guarantor shall reimburse Protoux for the reasonable attorneys' fees and costs that Protoux incurs in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

## 6. Termination

6.1 **Expiry.** This Guaranty terminates when both of the following have occurred: (a) the Agreement has expired or been terminated; and (b) all Guaranteed Obligations that arose before that expiry or termination have been paid and performed in full.

6.2 **Accrued Obligations.** Termination of this Guaranty does not affect the Guarantor's liability for any Guaranteed Obligation that arose before termination.

## 7. Notices

7.1 **Form of Notices.** Notices and demands under this Guaranty must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier. A notice or demand takes effect on receipt.

## 8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

8.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York for any action arising out of or in connection with this Guaranty.

8.3 **Jury Trial Waiver.** Each of the Guarantor and Protoux, to the extent permitted by law, waives trial by jury in any action arising out of or in connection with this Guaranty.

## 9. General

9.1 **Successors.** This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors. It benefits Protoux and its successors and assigns under the Agreement. The Guarantor may not assign or transfer its obligations under this Guaranty without Protoux's prior written consent.

9.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a written document signed by the Guarantor and Protoux. A waiver is effective only if it is in writing. No failure or delay by Protoux in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of that right.

9.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

9.4 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
