## 1. Subscription Term

1.1 **Subscription Start Date.** The "**Subscription Start Date**" is March 2, 2026.

1.2 **Initial Subscription Term.** The "**Initial Subscription Term**" is 60 months from the Subscription Start Date.

1.3 **Renewal Terms.** Each renewal period under Section 4 is a "**Renewal Term**."

## 2. Licensed Site and Platform

2.1 **Licensed Site.** The Licensed Site is Glenox's lithium-ion hydrometallurgical recovery facility in Rochester, New York.

2.2 **Platform Modules.** Licensee subscribes to the following Platform modules for use at the Licensed Site:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| CellTrace Chain-of-Custody | Receipt, weighing, labelling and lot-level tracking of incoming batteries through discharge, dismantling and black-mass production |
| CellTrace Feedstock Intake | Supplier and lot registration, chemistry classification, state-of-charge and hazard flags, intake documentation |
| CellTrace Process Analytics | Ingestion and analysis of process telemetry from leaching, solvent extraction and precipitation circuits |
| CellTrace Recovery Reporting | Recovery-yield dashboards and standard reports for lithium, nickel, cobalt and manganese |

2.3 **Authorized Users.** Up to 75 named Authorized Users.

2.4 **Edge Connectors.** Up to 12 edge connector instances deployed on equipment located at the Licensed Site, under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

2.5 **Hosting.** The Platform is hosted in United States data centers, as described in the Data Processing Addendum.

2.6 **Support.** Support Services under Section 6 of the Agreement are included in the Subscription Fees.

## 3. Fees

3.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** The Subscription Fee is $480,000 per year of the Subscription Term (the "**Annual Subscription Fee**").

3.2 **Invoicing.** Licensor shall invoice the Annual Subscription Fee annually in advance, with the first invoice issued on the Subscription Start Date and each later invoice issued on each anniversary of the Subscription Start Date.

3.3 **Payment.** Licensee shall pay each invoice in accordance with Section 7 of the Agreement.

3.4 **Renewal Term Fees.** The Annual Subscription Fee for each Renewal Term is the Annual Subscription Fee in effect at the end of the preceding term.

3.5 **Implementation Services.** Fees for implementation services are set out in Statement of Work No. 1 and are not included in the Subscription Fees.

3.6 **Taxes.** Fees are stated exclusive of taxes. Section 7.5 of the Agreement applies.

## 4. Renewal

4.1 At the end of the Initial Subscription Term, the subscription renews automatically for successive Renewal Terms equal in length to the Initial Subscription Term, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

4.2 Notices of non-renewal must be given in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement.
