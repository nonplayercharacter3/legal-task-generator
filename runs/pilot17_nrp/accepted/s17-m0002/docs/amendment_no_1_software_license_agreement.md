# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Software License Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2025 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Simev Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Hudson Street, Suite 900, New York, New York 10014 ("**Licensor**"), and **Trilund Pictures Releasing, LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1650 Broadway, 11th Floor, New York, New York 10019 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Licensor licenses the Reelpath platform to Licensee.

B. Licensee's catalog of hosted screeners and delivery masters has grown since the Effective Date. The Parties wish to increase the hosted storage included in the Annual License Fee, revise the rates for additional storage, and make certain other changes to the Agreement.

C. Under Section 16.3 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

D. The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 References in this Amendment to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of and Exhibits to the Agreement, unless otherwise stated.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 4.1 (ANNUAL LICENSE FEE)

2.1 Section 4.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> 4.1 **Annual License Fee.** Licensee shall pay Licensor a license fee of $186,000 per contract year (the "**Annual License Fee**"). The Annual License Fee covers access for up to seventy-five (75) Authorized Users and up to sixty (60) terabytes of hosted Licensee Content.

2.2 The Annual License Fee for the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2025 is not changed by this Amendment, and no additional storage fees accrue under Exhibit B for hosted Licensee Content up to sixty (60) terabytes from the Amendment Effective Date.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO EXHIBIT B (ADDITIONAL USAGE RATES)

3.1 Exhibit B of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> **Exhibit B – Additional Usage Rates:** Additional Authorized Users: $1,200 per user per Contract Year. Additional hosted storage above sixty (60) terabytes: $300 per terabyte per month.

3.2 Licensor shall apply the revised storage rate to any additional usage invoice issued under Section 5.1 for a quarter ending after the Amendment Effective Date.

## 4. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 10.3 (EXCLUSIONS)

4.1 Section 10.3 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> 10.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 10.1 and 10.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 9; (b) breach of Section 7.1 (Non-Disclosure); (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (d) Licensee's obligation to pay Fees.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS

5.1 Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

6.2 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in it.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the Parties have executed this Amendment as of the Amendment Effective Date.
