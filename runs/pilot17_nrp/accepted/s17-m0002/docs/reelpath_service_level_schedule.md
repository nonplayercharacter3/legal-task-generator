# RELEASE PATH SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Reelpath Service Level Schedule**

This Reelpath Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024. It is the Service Level Schedule referred to in the Software License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**") between **Simev Systems, Inc.** ("**Licensor**") and **Trilund Pictures Releasing, LLC** ("**Licensee**").

## 1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Schedule:

(a) "**Availability**" means the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the Platform is Available. It is calculated under Section 2.2.

(b) "**Available**" means that Authorized Users can log in to the Platform and use the core functions of each of the rights and windows management module, the participation and royalty accounting module and the secure media hosting module, including uploading, streaming and downloading Licensee Content.

(c) "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or New York State public holiday.

(d) "**Downtime**" means any period of one (1) minute or more during which the Platform is not Available, other than Excluded Downtime.

(e) "**Excluded Downtime**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

(f) "**Monthly Fee**" means one-twelfth (1/12) of the Annual License Fee in effect for the Contract Year in which the relevant calendar month falls.

(g) "**Scheduled Maintenance**" means maintenance that Licensor performs in accordance with Section 3.

(h) "**Service Credit**" means a credit calculated under Section 4.

## 2. AVAILABILITY COMMITMENT

2.1 **Uptime Commitment.** Licensor shall make the Platform Available to Licensee at 99.5% availability per calendar month (the "**Availability Commitment**").

2.2 **Calculation.** Availability for a calendar month is calculated as follows:

> Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes − Downtime Minutes) ÷ Total Minutes) × 100

In this formula, "Total Minutes" means the total number of minutes in the calendar month, less any minutes of Excluded Downtime. "Downtime Minutes" means the total number of minutes of Downtime in that month.

2.3 **Excluded Downtime.** The following periods are "**Excluded Downtime**" and are not counted as Downtime:

(a) Scheduled Maintenance;

(b) unavailability caused by Licensee's or its Authorized Users' equipment, internet connectivity or third-party software that Licensor does not provide or control;

(c) unavailability caused by Licensee's breach of the Agreement, or by use of the Platform contrary to the Documentation or Licensor's written instructions; and

(d) unavailability caused by a force majeure event described in Section 16.5 of the Agreement. This exclusion does not apply to the extent the unavailability results from Licensor's failure to maintain the disaster recovery and business continuity measures described in Section 6 of this Schedule.

2.4 **Monitoring.** Licensor shall monitor Availability using automated tools that test each Platform module at intervals of not more than five (5) minutes. Licensor shall keep monitoring records for not less than twelve (12) months and shall make them available to Licensee on reasonable request.

2.5 **Monthly Reports.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, Licensor shall provide Licensee with a written report stating (a) Availability for the month; (b) the start time, end time and cause of each period of Downtime; (c) any Scheduled Maintenance performed; and (d) any Service Credit that has accrued.

## 3. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE

3.1 **Maintenance Windows.** Licensor may perform Scheduled Maintenance between 12:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Sundays. Scheduled Maintenance shall not exceed eight (8) hours in any calendar month.

3.2 **Notice.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least five (5) Business Days' notice by email of any Scheduled Maintenance that Licensor reasonably expects to cause the Platform not to be Available.

3.3 **Emergency Maintenance.** Licensor may perform emergency maintenance outside the maintenance windows when reasonably necessary to address an urgent security vulnerability or to prevent imminent harm to the Platform. Licensor shall give Licensee as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable. Emergency maintenance is not Scheduled Maintenance, and any resulting unavailability counts as Downtime.

## 4. SERVICE CREDITS

4.1 **Credit Levels.** If Availability in a calendar month falls below the Availability Commitment, Licensee is entitled to a Service Credit equal to the following percentage of the Monthly Fee for that month:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 5% |
| Less than 99.0% but at least 98.0% | 10% |
| Less than 98.0% but at least 95.0% | 20% |
| Less than 95.0% | 30% |

4.2 **Maximum Credit.** The total Service Credits for any calendar month shall not exceed thirty percent (30%) of the Monthly Fee for that month.

4.3 **Accrual.** Service Credits accrue automatically on the basis of the monthly report delivered under Section 2.5. Licensee is not required to submit a claim for a Service Credit. If Licensee disagrees with the Availability stated in a monthly report, Licensee may notify Licensor in writing within thirty (30) days after receiving the report. The Parties shall then review the monitoring records in good faith and adjust the Service Credit as appropriate.

4.4 **Application.** Licensor shall apply each accrued Service Credit against the next invoice that it issues to Licensee under Section 5.1 of the Agreement, whether for additional usage fees or for the Annual License Fee. If no further invoice is due because the Agreement has terminated or expired, Licensor shall pay the amount of any accrued and unapplied Service Credit to Licensee within thirty (30) days.

4.5 **Credits and Other Rights.** Service Credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the Platform. They do not limit Licensee's rights under Section 7 of this Schedule or under Sections 8.2, 12.3 and 12.4 of the Agreement.

## 5. SUPPORT

5.1 **Support Hours.** Licensor shall provide telephone and email support from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Business Days ("**Support Hours**"). For Severity 1 issues, Licensor shall provide telephone support twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week.

5.2 **Support Contacts.** Licensee may submit support requests by email to support@simevsystems.com, by telephone to Licensor's support line, or through the support portal within the Platform.

5.3 **Severity Levels.** Licensee shall assign a severity level to each support request in good faith, using the following definitions. Licensor may reasonably reassign a severity level after consulting Licensee.

(a) **Severity 1:** the Platform, or any of its modules, is not Available; Licensee Content cannot be accessed or delivered; or a Security Incident is suspected.

(b) **Severity 2:** a core function of the Platform is materially impaired, and no reasonable workaround is available.

(c) **Severity 3:** a function of the Platform is impaired, but a reasonable workaround is available.

(d) **Severity 4:** a minor defect, a Documentation error or a general question.

5.4 **Response and Resolution Targets.**

| Severity | Initial Response | Status Updates | Target Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | 30 minutes, at any time | Every 2 hours | 8 hours |
| Severity 2 | 2 hours during Support Hours | Every 8 Support Hours | 2 Business Days |
| Severity 3 | 1 Business Day | Weekly | 10 Business Days |
| Severity 4 | 2 Business Days | As agreed | Next scheduled release |

5.5 **Security Incidents.** A support request raised as a suspected Security Incident does not replace Licensor's notification and remediation obligations under Section 7.4 of the Agreement.

## 6. BACKUP AND DISASTER RECOVERY

6.1 **Backups.** Licensor shall back up all Licensee Content and Platform data at least once every twenty-four (24) hours. Backups shall be stored in a geographically separate data center located in the United States and encrypted in accordance with Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Recovery Objectives.** If a disaster affects the primary hosting environment, Licensor shall restore the Platform with a recovery point objective of not more than twenty-four (24) hours and a recovery time objective of not more than twelve (12) hours.

6.3 **Testing.** Licensor shall test its disaster recovery plan at least once in each Contract Year and, on request, shall provide Licensee with a summary of the test results.

## 7. CHRONIC FAILURE

7.1 **Termination Right.** Licensee may terminate the Agreement under Section 12.4 of the Agreement by written notice to Licensor if Availability falls below 98.0% (a) in any three (3) consecutive calendar months; or (b) in any four (4) calendar months during any rolling twelve (12) month period.

7.2 **Timing and Refund.** Licensee must give notice under Section 7.1 within sixty (60) days after Licensee receives the monthly report for the last month that gives rise to the termination right. On termination under Section 7.1, Licensor shall refund to Licensee a pro rata portion of any prepaid Fees for the remainder of the then-current Contract Year, together with any accrued and unapplied Service Credits. Section 13 of the Agreement applies to the termination.

## 8. REVIEW

8.1 **Service Reviews.** The Parties' account representatives shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least once in each calendar quarter to review Platform performance, support requests, Scheduled Maintenance and planned updates.

8.2 **Changes to this Schedule.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 16.3 of the Agreement.
