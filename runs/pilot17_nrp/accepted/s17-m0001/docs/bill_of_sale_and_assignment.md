# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of April 15, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**") by **Stavain Inn Holdings, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Zaibeind Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Seller agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets used in the Business at The Larkspur House, Hudson, New York (the "**Hotel**").

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the Purchase Price paid under Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS; CLOSING DATE

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Closing Date.** The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurred on April 15, 2026. That date is the Closing Date for all purposes of the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Documents. This Bill of Sale takes effect as of 12:01 a.m. on the Closing Date. Prorations remain allocated as provided in Section 2.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Sale and Transfer.** Seller sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Purchased Assets, other than the Real Property. The Purchased Assets are transferred free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances. They include the following:

(a) all furniture, fixtures, equipment, linens, kitchen equipment, vehicles, computer hardware, property management and point-of-sale systems and other tangible personal property located at or used in the Hotel. This includes the items listed on Schedule 2.1(b) of the Disclosure Schedule and the 2022 Ford Transit passenger van, VIN ending 4471;

(b) all inventory, including food, beverages, alcoholic beverages (to the extent transferable under applicable law), guest supplies and consumables;

(c) all advance reservations and bookings for guest rooms, events and other Hotel services for periods after the Closing;

(d) all permits and licenses listed on Schedule 2.1(f) of the Disclosure Schedule, to the extent transferable;

(e) all guest lists, guest history and customer data, subject to applicable privacy law;

(f) all books, records, plans, specifications and operating manuals relating to the Business, as provided in Section 2.1(i) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(g) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Real Property, Contracts and Intangibles.** The Deed conveys the Real Property. The Assignment and Assumption of Assigned Contracts transfers the Assigned Contracts. The Assignment of Intangible Property transfers the name "The Larkspur House" and the related intellectual property described in Section 2.1(g) of the Purchase Agreement. Each of these instruments is delivered at the Closing under Section 3.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.3 Excluded Assets.** This Bill of Sale does not sell, convey, assign or transfer any Excluded Asset, including the assets listed on Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule. Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in the Excluded Assets.

**2.4 Non-Transferable Permits.** The following permits are not transferable and are not conveyed by this Bill of Sale:

(a) the On-Premises Liquor License (Serial No. 1298844); and

(b) the Columbia County Hotel Room Occupancy Tax operator registration.

Buyer will obtain its own liquor license or temporary retail permit under Section 5.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. NO ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITIES

This Bill of Sale conveys assets only. Buyer does not assume any liability or obligation of Seller under this Bill of Sale. The Assumed Liabilities are assumed only under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement and the Assignment and Assumption of Assigned Contracts. Seller retains the Excluded Liabilities as provided in Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. POSSESSION; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**4.1 Possession.** Seller has delivered to Buyer possession of the tangible Purchased Assets at the Hotel, at 214 Warren Street, Hudson, New York 12534. Seller has also delivered all keys, access codes, passwords and administrative credentials for the property management system, the point-of-sale system, the Hotel's websites and the Hotel's social media accounts.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, for the following limited purposes:

(a) to demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets conveyed by this Bill of Sale;

(b) to endorse, in Seller's name, any check or other instrument received for any of those Purchased Assets; and

(c) to institute and prosecute proceedings to collect or enforce any claim to any of those Purchased Assets.

The cost of any such proceeding is borne by Buyer, except as otherwise provided in the Purchase Agreement. This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**4.3 Receipts After Closing.** If Seller receives any payment, mail or other property that belongs to Buyer under this Bill of Sale, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer. Seller shall deliver it to Buyer promptly, and in any event within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

## 5. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**5.1 Delivered Under the Purchase Agreement.** This Bill of Sale is delivered under and is subject to the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Effect on Purchase Agreement.** This Bill of Sale does not change the representations, warranties, covenants, agreements or indemnities in the Purchase Agreement. Those provisions survive delivery of this Bill of Sale to the extent provided in the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. FURTHER ASSURANCES

Consistent with Section 5.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of conveyance and transfer as Buyer may reasonably request. Seller shall also take such further actions as Buyer may reasonably request to vest in Buyer title to the Purchased Assets conveyed by this Bill of Sale, including the following:

(a) executing any New York State Department of Motor Vehicles title transfer documents for the passenger van; and

(b) completing change-of-ownership forms with software and service providers.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It benefits Buyer and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**7.3 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of this Bill of Sale shall be brought in the courts specified in Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.4 Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given as provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronic signature has the same effect as an original.

**7.6 Headings.** Headings in this Bill of Sale are for convenience only.
