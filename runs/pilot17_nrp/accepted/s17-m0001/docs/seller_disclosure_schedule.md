**1.1 Delivery.** Stavain Inn Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**") delivers this Seller Disclosure Schedule (this "**Disclosure Schedule**") to Zaibeind Hospitality Partners, LLC ("**Buyer**") under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Seller and Buyer (the "**Agreement**"). **1.3 Numbering.** The schedule numbers below correspond to the section numbers of the Agreement. A matter disclosed in one schedule is deemed disclosed for any other section of the Agreement where its relevance to that section is reasonably apparent on the face of the disclosure.

**1.4 Copies.** Any summary of a contract, permit or other document in this Disclosure Schedule is qualified by reference to the full text of that document. Seller has made a true and complete copy of each listed document available to Buyer in the electronic data room for the transaction.

## SCHEDULE 4.10 — LITIGATION

1. **Hudson Valley Wedding Collective, LLC v. Stavain Inn Holdings, LLC (threatened / pending).** By letter dated March 2, 2026, counsel for Hudson Valley Wedding Collective, LLC made a demand on Seller under the Hudson Valley Wedding Collective Agreement. The demand arises from Seller's March 2026 cancellation of the contracted event block described in Schedule 4.9, item 1. The claim is for **$410,000**, consisting of:
   - liquidated cancellation fees under Section 8 of the agreement;
   - reimbursement of non-refundable vendor deposits paid by the Collective's clients; and
   - lost commissions.

   The letter states that the claimant will commence an action in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, Columbia County, if the claim is not resolved within 45 days. Seller disputes the amount of the claim. As of the date of this Disclosure Schedule, the claim is pending and has not been resolved.

2. **Guest slip-and-fall claim.** In October 2025, a guest reported an injury on the carriage house exterior stairs. The claim has been tendered to Seller's general liability insurer, which has acknowledged coverage subject to the policy's $5,000 deductible. No action has been filed.
