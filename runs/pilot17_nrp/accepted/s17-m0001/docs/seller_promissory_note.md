# SECURED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$3,200,000.00**
**Hudson, New York**
**April 15, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **ZAIBEIND HOSPITALITY PARTNERS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **STAVAIN INN HOLDINGS, LLC**, a New York limited liability company (together with its permitted successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal sum and interest set out below, on the terms of this Secured Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in Section 2.6(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). It is issued at the Closing on April 15, 2026 in partial payment of the Purchase Price.

## 1. PROMISE TO PAY; DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Promise to Pay.** Maker promises to pay to Holder the principal amount of **$3,200,000** (Three Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars), or so much of it as remains outstanding. Maker also promises to pay interest on the unpaid principal balance from the date of this Note until paid in full, at the rate and on the dates set out in Section 2.

**1.2 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Business**" means all hotels and hospitality operations owned, leased or managed by Maker or any of its Affiliates.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus four percent (4%) per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning in Section 7.1.

"**Interest Rate**" means six percent (6.0%) per annum, computed on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

"**Maturity Date**" has the meaning in Section 2.2.

"**Mortgage**" means the mortgage on the Real Property granted by Maker to Holder at the Closing to secure this Note.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker under its senior acquisition financing for the Hotel, together with any refinancing of that financing in a principal amount not exceeding the outstanding principal amount being refinanced.

"**Senior Lender**" means the holder or holders from time to time of the Senior Indebtedness.

## 2. PAYMENTS; MATURITY

**2.1 Interest.** Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal balance at the Interest Rate. Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the fifteenth (15th) day of each July, October, January and April, beginning July 15, 2026.

**2.2 Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, is due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**").

**2.3 Manner of Payment.** All payments shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing from time to time. If a payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue for the extra days.

**2.4 Application of Payments.** Payments shall be applied first to costs of collection and other amounts payable under Section 7.3, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to principal.

**2.5 Default Interest; Late Charge.** While an Event of Default continues, the unpaid principal balance shall bear interest at the Default Rate. Any installment of interest not paid within ten (10) days after its due date shall bear a late charge of three percent (3%) of the overdue amount.

## 3. PREPAYMENT

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each partial prepayment shall be accompanied by all interest accrued on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments of principal shall not postpone or reduce any scheduled payment of interest.

## 4. SETOFF

**4.1 Right of Setoff.** Maker may set off against any payment of principal or interest due under this Note any amount that has been finally determined to be owed by Holder to Maker under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement. This setoff right applies only to indemnity claims asserted in writing within the survival period set forth in Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Final Determination.** An amount is "finally determined" for purposes of Section 4.1 when (a) Holder and Maker have agreed to it in writing, or (b) a court of competent jurisdiction has entered a final, non-appealable judgment for it.

**4.3 Notice of Setoff.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any setoff at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date against which it is applied. The notice shall state the amount set off and the claim to which it relates. A setoff properly made under this Section 4 discharges Maker's payment obligation to the extent of the amount set off and is not an Event of Default.

## 5. SECURITY

This Note is secured by the Mortgage. Holder's rights under the Mortgage are subordinate to the lien securing the Senior Indebtedness, as provided in Section 8. If the terms of this Note conflict with the terms of the Mortgage, this Note controls.

## 6. COVENANTS OF MAKER

So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker covenants as follows:

**6.1 Financial Reporting.** Maker shall deliver to Holder (a) within forty-five (45) days after the end of each fiscal quarter, unaudited statements of operations of the Hotel for that quarter; and (b) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, annual financial statements of the Hotel for that year, certified by an officer of Maker as fairly presenting in all material respects the results of operations of the Hotel.

**6.2 Restrictions on Transfer and Encumbrance.** Maker shall not, and shall not permit any of its Affiliates to, without the prior written consent of Holder, sell, transfer, lease, refinance or encumber any material portion of the assets of the Business. This restriction does not apply to:

(a) sales of inventory and replacement of furniture, fixtures and equipment in the ordinary course of business;

(b) the lien securing the Senior Indebtedness; or

(c) the Mortgage.

**6.3 Insurance.** Maker shall maintain property, casualty and liability insurance on the Hotel in amounts and with coverage customary for hotels of similar size and class in the Hudson Valley region. Holder shall be named as additional insured and as mortgagee under a standard mortgagee clause, subject to the rights of the Senior Lender.

**6.4 Existence; Taxes.** Maker shall maintain its existence as a limited liability company in good standing and its qualification to do business in the State of New York. Maker shall pay when due all real property Taxes on the Real Property, except Taxes being contested in good faith by appropriate proceedings for which adequate reserves are maintained.

**6.5 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any Event of Default, or of any event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default, within five (5) Business Days after an officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, whether at the Maturity Date, by acceleration or otherwise;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount due under this Note within ten (10) days after its due date;

(c) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 6.1, 6.3, 6.4 or 6.5, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Holder gives written notice of it;

(d) Maker breaches Section 6.2;

(e) a default occurs under the Senior Indebtedness that results in its acceleration;

(f) a default occurs under the Mortgage and continues beyond any applicable grace or cure period; or

(g) Maker (i) becomes insolvent or generally fails to pay its debts as they become due, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, (iii) commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, or (iv) has an involuntary case or proceeding of that kind commenced against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**7.2 Acceleration.** Upon an Event of Default described in Section 7.1(g), the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note and all accrued interest shall become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder. Upon any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. Upon acceleration, Holder may exercise all rights and remedies available under this Note, the Mortgage and applicable law, subject to Section 8.

**7.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs in collecting this Note or enforcing the Mortgage after an Event of Default.

## 8. SUBORDINATION

The indebtedness evidenced by this Note, and the lien of the Mortgage, are subordinate in right of payment and priority to the Senior Indebtedness and the lien securing it. Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute a subordination agreement with the Senior Lender in form customary for seller financing of hotel acquisitions and reasonably acceptable to Holder. Holder may not exercise remedies against the Real Property while the Senior Indebtedness remains outstanding, except as that subordination agreement permits.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of nonpayment. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note waives that right. A waiver on one occasion does not waive the same or any other right on any future occasion.

**9.2 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**9.3 Assignment.** Holder may not assign or transfer this Note, or any interest in it, without Maker's prior written consent. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without Holder's prior written consent, except to a permitted assignee of the Purchase Agreement under Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement that assumes this Note in writing. Maker shall remain liable after any such assignment.

**9.4 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**9.5 Usury Savings.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Governing Law.** This Note is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**9.8 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Maker submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York, County of New York, for any action arising out of this Note. MAKER AND HOLDER EACH WAIVE THE RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**9.9 Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
