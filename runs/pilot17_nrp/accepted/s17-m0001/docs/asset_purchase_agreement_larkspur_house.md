# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Stavain Inn Holdings, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Zaibeind Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns and operates the 42-room boutique hotel known as The Larkspur House, located in Hudson, Columbia County, New York, together with its restaurant, event spaces and related amenities (the "**Hotel**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, substantially all of the assets used in the Business, and Buyer wishes to assume the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the following meanings:

"**Action**" means any claim, action, suit, arbitration, audit, assessment, investigation or proceeding by or before any Governmental Authority or arbitrator.

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Documents**" means the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale, the Deed, the Assignment and Assumption of Assigned Contracts, the Assignment of Intangible Property and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning in Section 2.1(c).

"**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning in Section 2.3.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the Bill of Sale and Assignment to be delivered by Seller at the Closing under Section 3.2(a).

"**Business**" means the ownership and operation of the Hotel by Seller.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings in Section 3.1.

"**Deed**" means a bargain and sale deed with covenant against grantor's acts conveying the Real Property to Buyer.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the Seller Disclosure Schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, deed of trust, security interest, charge, claim, easement, encroachment or other encumbrance of any kind.

"**Excluded Assets**" has the meaning in Section 2.2.

"**Excluded Liabilities**" has the meaning in Section 2.4.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority) and Section 4.7 (Title to Purchased Assets).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, county, municipal or other governmental or regulatory authority, agency, court or tribunal.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Permitted Encumbrances**" means (a) liens for Taxes not yet due and payable, (b) the matters listed on Schedule 1.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule, and (c) easements, rights of way and similar matters of record that do not materially impair the use or operation of the Hotel.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means (a) all Taxes of Seller for any period, and (b) all Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any taxable period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date, including New York State sales and use Taxes on hotel occupancy, food and beverage and other taxable receipts, and the Columbia County hotel room occupancy tax.

"**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning in Section 2.1.

"**Real Property**" means the land on which the Hotel is located, as more particularly described on Schedule 2.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule, together with all buildings, structures, fixtures and improvements on it and all easements and appurtenances to it.

"**Seller Note**" means the secured promissory note in the original principal amount of $3,200,000 to be issued by Buyer, as maker, to Seller, as holder, at the Closing under Section 2.6(b).

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, occupancy, transfer, real property, personal property, payroll, withholding, franchise and other taxes, fees and assessments of any kind, together with any interest, penalties or additions imposed with respect to them.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation"; references to Sections, Articles and Schedules are to those of this Agreement and the Disclosure Schedule unless otherwise stated; and headings are for convenience only.

## 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to all assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Real Property;

(b) all furniture, fixtures, equipment, linens, kitchen equipment, vehicles, computer hardware, property management and point-of-sale systems, and other tangible personal property located at or used in the Hotel, including the items listed on Schedule 2.1(b) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including group-booking and room-block agreements, online travel agency agreements, vendor and supply agreements, and equipment leases (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all inventory, including food, beverages, alcoholic beverages (to the extent transferable under applicable law), guest supplies and consumables;

(e) all advance reservations and bookings for guest rooms, events and other Hotel services for periods after the Closing;

(f) all permits and licenses relating to the Hotel, to the extent transferable, as listed on Schedule 2.1(f) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(g) the name "The Larkspur House," all related trade names, logos, domain names, websites, social media accounts and other intellectual property used in the Business;

(h) all guest lists, guest history and customer data, subject to applicable privacy law;

(i) all books, records, plans, specifications and operating manuals relating to the Business, other than records Seller is required by law to retain, of which Seller shall provide Buyer with copies; and

(j) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) cash and cash equivalents, other than guest deposits transferred or credited under Section 2.7; (b) accounts receivable for periods before the Closing, except guest ledger receivables credited under Section 2.7; (c) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and tax returns; (d) any contract that is not an Assigned Contract; (e) insurance policies of Seller and the rights to proceeds under them; (f) any tax refunds of Seller; and (g) the other assets listed on Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following liabilities and obligations of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all liabilities and obligations arising under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising before or after the Closing;

(b) all obligations to honor advance reservations and bookings included in the Purchased Assets, to the extent Buyer receives a credit for the related deposits under Section 2.7; and

(c) all liabilities arising out of Buyer's ownership or operation of the Purchased Assets after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all liabilities and obligations of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all Pre-Closing Taxes;

(b) all liabilities arising out of or relating to the Excluded Assets;

(c) all indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller, and all Encumbrances securing it;

(d) all liabilities relating to the employment or termination of employment by Seller of any Person, including wages, accrued vacation, benefits, severance and WARN Act obligations, for periods on or before the Closing Date;

(e) all liabilities arising under any employee benefit plan maintained by Seller;

(f) all liabilities arising out of any violation of environmental law, or any release of hazardous materials at the Real Property, occurring before the Closing; and

(g) all fees and expenses of Seller's brokers, attorneys, accountants and other advisors in connection with this Agreement.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to the prorations and adjustments in Section 2.7.

**2.6 Payment of Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $15,200,000, as adjusted under Section 2.7, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing; and

(b) $3,200,000 by the execution and delivery of the Seller Note, which shall be secured by a mortgage on the Real Property subordinate only to Buyer's senior acquisition financing.

**2.7 Prorations.** Real property Taxes, utilities, payments under Assigned Contracts, permit fees and other customary hotel operating items shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer as of 11:59 p.m. on the day before the Closing Date. Room revenue for the night that includes the Closing shall be allocated to Seller. Buyer shall receive a credit for all guest and event deposits held by Seller for periods after the Closing. Seller shall receive a credit for guest ledger receivables for guests in house at the Closing. Any proration that cannot be finally determined at the Closing shall be settled within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date.

**2.8 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and the allocation schedule to be agreed by the Parties before the Closing. The Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on April 15, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing actually occurs, the "**Closing Date**"), subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 7.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Deed, duly executed and acknowledged by Seller;

(c) an Assignment and Assumption of Assigned Contracts and an Assignment of Intangible Property, each duly executed by Seller;

(d) the consents listed on Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule;

(e) a certificate of Seller's manager certifying Seller's resolutions authorizing this Agreement;

(f) a FIRPTA certificate and all transfer tax returns required by law; and

(g) evidence that Seller has filed Form AU-196.10 (Notification of Sale, Transfer, or Assignment in Bulk) has been delivered to the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance in accordance with Section 5.5.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.6(a);

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer, and the related mortgage;

(c) the Assignment and Assumption of Assigned Contracts, duly executed by Buyer; and

(d) a certificate of Buyer's manager certifying Buyer's resolutions authorizing this Agreement.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Effective Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York, and has full power and authority to own and operate the Hotel and the Purchased Assets.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party. This Agreement has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.4, require any consent under, or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Consents.** Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each consent of any Person required in connection with the assignment of the Assigned Contracts and the permits to Buyer.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the balance sheets and statements of operations of the Hotel for the fiscal years 2024 and 2025 and the two months ended February 28, 2026. Those financial statements were prepared from Seller's books and records and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Hotel for the periods shown.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, Seller has operated the Hotel in the ordinary course, and there has been no material adverse change in the Business.

**4.7 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and marketable fee simple title to the Real Property and good and valid title to all other Purchased Assets, in each case free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.8 Condition of Assets.** The buildings, systems and equipment included in the Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.9 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Except as set forth on Schedule 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule, neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.10 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no Action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Hotel, the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.11 Compliance; Permits.** Seller is in compliance in all material respects with all laws applicable to the Hotel. Schedule 2.1(f) lists all material permits held by Seller for the operation of the Hotel, including its certificate of occupancy, food service permit and liquor license, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.12 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and the Purchased Assets, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. Seller has timely collected, remitted and paid all Taxes due with respect to the Business, including New York State sales tax on hotel occupancy, food and beverage and other taxable receipts and the Columbia County hotel room occupancy tax. Except as set forth on Schedule 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule, no audit or other Action relating to Taxes of Seller or the Business is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened, and Seller has not waived any statute of limitations with respect to Taxes.

**4.13 Employees.** Schedule 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Hotel, with title, date of hire and current compensation. Seller is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Environmental Matters.** To Seller's knowledge, there has been no release of hazardous materials at the Real Property in violation of environmental law, and Seller has received no written notice of any violation of environmental law relating to the Real Property.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## 5. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Hotel in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in their current condition, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, enter into, amend or terminate any Assigned Contract or any contract that would be an Assigned Contract.

**5.2 Access.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Hotel and to the books, records and personnel of the Business.

**5.3 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain all consents listed on Schedule 4.4 before the Closing.

**5.4 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to any employee of the Hotel effective as of the Closing. Seller shall terminate the employment of all Hotel employees effective as of the Closing and shall pay all amounts owed to them through the Closing.

**5.5 Bulk Sale Notice.** Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in delivering, at least ten (10) days before the Closing, a notification of sale in bulk to the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance under Section 1141(c) of the New York Tax Law, and shall provide all information required for that notification.

**5.6 Liquor License.** Buyer shall promptly apply to the New York State Liquor Authority for the issuance of a license or temporary retail permit for the Hotel, and Seller shall cooperate with that application.

**5.7 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party may reasonably request to carry out this Agreement.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**6.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and qualified to do business in the State of New York, and has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party.

**6.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**6.3 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee from Seller based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to (a) the accuracy in all material respects of Seller's representations and warranties as of the Closing Date, (b) Seller's performance in all material respects of its covenants, (c) delivery of the items in Section 3.2, and (d) issuance to Buyer of an owner's title insurance policy for the Real Property subject only to Permitted Encumbrances.

**7.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to (a) the accuracy in all material respects of Buyer's representations and warranties as of the Closing Date, (b) Buyer's performance in all material respects of its covenants, and (c) delivery of the items in Section 3.3.

## 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written agreement of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by May 29, 2026, unless the failure results from that Party's breach; or (c) by either Party if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be of no further force or effect, except that this Section 8.2 and Article 11 shall survive and no Party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## 9. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations, warranties, covenants and agreements of the Parties contained in this Agreement, and the indemnification obligations under Section 9.2, shall survive the Closing until nine (9) months following the Closing Date (the "**Survival Period**"); provided that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until six (6) years following the Closing Date. No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2 may be made after the expiration of the applicable survival period, except that any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before that expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective members, managers, officers and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document;

(b) any Excluded Liability; and

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective members, managers, officers and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any Assumed Liability; and

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Procedures.** A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the other Party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.** Neither Party shall be liable under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) until the aggregate Losses for which it would otherwise be liable exceed $92,000, after which it shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar. The aggregate liability of each Party under this Article 9 shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($1,840,000). The limitations in this Section 9.5 shall not apply to claims under Section 9.2(b) or Section 9.3(b), or to claims arising from fraud.

**9.6 Setoff.** Buyer may set off amounts finally determined to be owed to it under this Article 9 against payments due under the Seller Note, on the terms set forth in the Seller Note.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud, specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties after the Closing for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** Indemnification payments under this Article 9 shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes to the extent permitted by law.

## 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** New York State real estate transfer tax and any other transfer, documentary, sales or use Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be paid by Seller.

**10.2 Cooperation.** The Parties shall cooperate in the preparation and filing of tax returns and in any audit or other Action relating to Taxes of the Business, and Seller shall retain its books and records relating to Taxes of the Business until the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own expenses in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier or email (with confirmation of transmission) to the addresses set forth on the signature page, or to such other address as a Party may designate by notice.

**11.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates or collaterally to its lenders without consent, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York, County of New York, for any Action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.8 Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Article 9, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Document, this Agreement shall control, except that the Seller Note shall control as to payment terms, Maker covenants and Events of Default.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
