# SECURED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$2,400,000**
**Note Date: August 3, 2026**
**Wilmington, Delaware**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **SAITOUTH DENTAL PARTNERS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **TROLIND FAMILY DENTISTRY, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association (together with its permitted successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount set forth in Section 1, together with interest as provided in this secured promissory note (this "**Note**"). The date first written above is the "**Note Date**."

Maker issues this Note under Section 2.5(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026, between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal

The principal amount of this Note is $2,400,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). This amount equals the portion of the Purchase Price payable by delivery of the Seller Note under Section 2.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Rate.** Interest shall accrue on the unpaid Principal Amount outstanding from time to time, from the Note Date until paid in full, at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum, simple interest.

**2.2 Computation.** Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the unpaid Principal Amount shall bear interest at a rate equal to the rate in Section 2.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

**2.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest or other amount shall be charged under this Note in excess of the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount.

## 3. Payments

**3.1 Installments.** Maker shall pay the Principal Amount in four (4) equal installments of $600,000 each. Each installment shall be paid together with all interest accrued and unpaid on the Principal Amount through its payment date. The installments are due on the following dates (each, a "**Payment Date**"):

| Installment | Payment Date | Principal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | February 3, 2027 | $600,000 |
| 2 | August 3, 2027 | $600,000 |
| 3 | February 3, 2028 | $600,000 |
| 4 | August 3, 2028 (the "**Maturity Date**") | $600,000 |

**3.2 Maturity.** All unpaid principal and accrued interest under this Note shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Manner of Payment.** All payments shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing to Maker at least five (5) business days before the applicable Payment Date.

**3.4 Business Days.** If any Payment Date falls on a day that is not a business day in the State of Delaware, the payment shall be due on the next business day. Interest shall continue to accrue until that next business day.

**3.5 Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the Principal Amount.

## 4. Setoff

**4.1 Right of Setoff.** Subject to Section 4.2, Maker may set off against any amount payable under this Note any amount to which Maker or any other Buyer Indemnified Party is entitled under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement. This Section 4 is the setoff right contemplated by Section 8.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Setoff Notice.** Maker may exercise its right of setoff under Section 4.1 only if Maker delivers to Holder a written notice (a "**Setoff Notice**") within sixty (60) days after the Note Date. Each Setoff Notice shall (a) describe in reasonable detail the claim under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement on which the setoff is based, (b) state the amount, or a good-faith estimate of the amount, of the Losses claimed and (c) identify the Payment Date or Payment Dates against which Maker intends to set off. Maker may withhold from the payments due on the identified Payment Dates an amount not exceeding the amount stated in the Setoff Notice.

**4.3 Disputed Amounts.** If Holder disputes a Setoff Notice in writing within thirty (30) days after receiving it, the disputed amount shall be resolved in accordance with Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement. Within ten (10) business days after final resolution, Maker shall pay Holder any withheld amount that is finally determined not to be owed to Maker, together with interest on that amount at the rate set forth in Section 2.1 from the date on which it was withheld.

**4.4 Effect of Setoff.** Any amount properly set off under this Section 4 shall reduce the Principal Amount, and the obligations of Maker under this Note, by the amount set off. A setoff exercised in good faith in accordance with this Section 4 shall not constitute an Event of Default.

**4.5 Payment Without Deduction.** Except for amounts set off in accordance with Section 4.2, Maker shall make all payments under this Note when due, without deduction, counterclaim or setoff.

## 5. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five (5) business days' prior written notice to Holder. Each partial prepayment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the remaining installments of principal in inverse order of maturity.

## 6. Security

**6.1 Security Agreement.** This Note is secured by a security interest in the Purchased Assets granted by Maker to Holder under the Security Agreement dated as of the Note Date between Maker, as debtor, and Holder, as secured party (the "**Security Agreement**").

**6.2 Release.** Upon payment in full of all amounts due under this Note, the security interest under the Security Agreement shall automatically terminate. Within ten (10) business days after that payment, Holder shall file UCC-3 termination statements and deliver to Maker any other releases Maker reasonably requests.

## 7. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within fifteen (15) days after the date it is due, other than an amount properly set off in accordance with Section 4;

(b) Maker breaches any material covenant in the Security Agreement and the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder;

(c) Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets, other than to an Affiliate of Maker that assumes this Note in writing or to a lender of Maker as collateral; or

(d) Maker (i) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, (ii) files a voluntary petition under any bankruptcy or insolvency Law or (iii) has an involuntary petition filed against it under any such Law that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days after filing.

## 8. Remedies

**8.1 Acceleration.** While an Event of Default described in Section 7(a), 7(b) or 7(c) is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default described in Section 7(d), the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest shall become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder.

**8.2 Other Remedies.** Holder may exercise all rights and remedies available to it under the Security Agreement and applicable Law.

**8.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection incurred by Holder after an Event of Default, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 9. Waivers

Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest, except as expressly provided in this Note. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note shall operate as a waiver of that right.

## 10. Miscellaneous

**10.1 Notices.** Notices under this Note, including any Setoff Notice, shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.2 Amendment.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**10.3 Assignment.** Holder may not assign or transfer this Note, or any interest in it, without the prior written consent of Maker. Maker may assign its obligations under this Note to any Affiliate that assumes them in writing. Such an assignment does not release Maker unless Holder consents in writing.

**10.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**10.6 Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** Sections 11.7 and 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Note as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Note.

**10.7 Electronic Signatures.** This Note may be executed and delivered by electronic transmission. An electronically delivered signature is effective as an original.
