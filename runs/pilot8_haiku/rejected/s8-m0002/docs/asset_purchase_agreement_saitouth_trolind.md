# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 3, 2026, by and between **SAITOUTH DENTAL PARTNERS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **TROLIND FAMILY DENTISTRY, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns and operates general and pediatric dental practices at the Practice Locations (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business. Buyer will also assume certain liabilities of the Business. Each sale and assumption is made on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Assigned Contracts**" means the Contracts listed on Schedule 2.2(c), including the Payer Agreements.

"**Billing Reconciliation**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.7.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Contract**" means any contract, agreement, lease, license, commitment or other legally binding arrangement, whether written or oral.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller set forth in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.5 (Title to Purchased Assets), Section 4.9 (Intellectual Property), Section 4.15 (Taxes) and Section 4.17 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local government or any agency, board, commission, court or other governmental or regulatory body, including any Medicaid program, the Delaware Board of Dentistry and Dental Hygiene and any fiscal intermediary acting on behalf of a governmental payer.

"**Healthcare Laws**" means all applicable Laws relating to the provision of, billing for or payment for dental and healthcare services. These include the federal Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b)), the civil False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. § 3729 et seq.), the Civil Monetary Penalties Law, HIPAA, state Medicaid program requirements and the Delaware Dental Practice Act.

"**HIPAA**" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, as amended by the HITECH Act, and the regulations issued under it.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all trademarks, service marks, trade names, brand names, logos, domain names, websites, social media accounts, patient-portal content, copyrights, software, telephone numbers and other intellectual property and proprietary rights, together with all goodwill associated with them.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the intellectual property assignment agreement to be entered into between Seller, as assignor, and Buyer, as assignee, pursuant to Section 6.5.

"**Knowledge of Seller**" means the actual knowledge of Seller's officers and dentist-owners after reasonable inquiry of the office managers of each Practice Location.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Liabilities**" means liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature whatsoever, whether asserted or unasserted, known or unknown, absolute or contingent, accrued or unaccrued, matured or unmatured.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Payer**" means any Governmental Authority, insurance company, dental benefits plan, managed care organization or other third party that pays for dental services rendered at the Practice Locations.

"**Payer Agreements**" means all participating provider, network and reimbursement Contracts between Seller and any Payer, as listed on Schedule 2.2(c).

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, professional association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Practice Locations**" means the four (4) dental clinics operated by Seller at Wilmington, Newark, Dover and Middletown, Delaware, as more particularly described on Schedule 1.1(a).

"**Seller Note**" means the secured promissory note dated the Closing Date, made by Buyer in favor of Seller in the principal amount of $2,400,000, in the form attached as Exhibit B.

"**Transaction Documents**" means this Agreement, the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Lease Assignments, the IP Assignment Agreement and the other agreements, instruments and certificates delivered under this Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits refer to this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $14,600,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. The Purchase Price shall be paid as set forth in Section 2.5.

**2.2 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed on Schedule 2.2, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets used in the Business at each of the Practice Locations (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all dental equipment, chairs, imaging systems, sterilization equipment, furniture, fixtures, computer hardware and supplies;

(b) all inventory of dental supplies, consumables and retail products;

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all Intellectual Property used in the Business, including the names, brands, websites and domain names listed on Schedule 4.9;

(e) to the extent transferable under applicable Law, all permits and licenses held by Seller for the operation of the Practice Locations;

(f) all patient records, charts, radiographs and patient lists, subject to Section 6.1;

(g) all telephone and fax numbers, e-mail addresses and listings used in the Business; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all Liabilities arising under the Assigned Contracts, including the Payer Agreements, whether arising before or after the Closing;

(b) Liabilities for accrued paid time off of Transferred Employees, to the extent reflected as a credit against the Purchase Price on the Closing Statement; and

(c) Liabilities arising out of Buyer's ownership or operation of the Purchased Assets after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Assets and Excluded Liabilities.**

(a) The Purchased Assets do not include cash and cash equivalents, Seller's accounts receivable for services rendered before the Closing Date, Seller's organizational documents and minute books, Seller's Tax refunds, or the assets listed on Schedule 2.4(a) (the "**Excluded Assets**").

(b) Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and discharge, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include: (i) Liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any pre-Closing period; (ii) Liabilities for professional malpractice claims arising from dental services rendered before the Closing; (iii) Liabilities relating to the employment or termination of any employee by Seller before the Closing; (iv) indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller; and (v) Liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets.

**2.5 Payment of Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $12,200,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller, as adjusted by the Closing Statement; and

(b) $2,400,000 by delivery of the Seller Note.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and Schedule 2.6. The Parties shall file all Tax returns, including IRS Form 8594, consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on August 3, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**");

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**");

(c) assignments of the real property leases for each of the Practice Locations, together with landlord consents (the "**Lease Assignments**");

(d) the Seller Note, countersigned by Seller as holder, together with the related security agreement;

(e) payoff letters and lien releases for all indebtedness secured by the Purchased Assets;

(f) a certificate of good standing of Seller issued by the Delaware Secretary of State; and

(g) a certificate of an officer of Seller attaching resolutions authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.5(a), the executed Seller Note, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Lease Assignments, each executed by Buyer, and a certificate of an officer of Buyer attaching resolutions authorizing this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedules delivered by Seller on the date of this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a professional association duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full power and authority to own the Purchased Assets and to carry on the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Seller, and, except as set forth on Schedule 4.3, require no consent of any Person under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the unaudited balance sheets and statements of income of the Business for the fiscal years 2024 and 2025 and for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026. These financial statements were prepared from Seller's books and records and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business for the periods indicated.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than those to be released at the Closing.

**4.6 Real Property.** Seller owns no real property. Schedule 4.6 lists each lease for the Practice Locations. Each lease is in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any landlord is in material default under it.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 2.2(c) lists all Contracts material to the Business, including all Payer Agreements. Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Seller is not in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.8 Condition of Equipment.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 4.9 lists all registered and material unregistered Intellectual Property used in the Business at each Practice Location, including the trade names, pediatric dental brand, website domains and patient-portal content. Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of all such Intellectual Property, free and clear of all liens. The operation of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person. No Person has challenged Seller's ownership of or right to use any such Intellectual Property.

**4.10 Licenses and Permits.** Seller and each dentist and hygienist practicing at the Practice Locations hold all licenses, registrations (including DEA registrations) and permits required to conduct the Business. All of them are valid and in full force and effect.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws since January 1, 2021.

**4.12 Healthcare Billing and Payer Compliance.**

(a) Since January 1, 2021, all claims for payment submitted by or on behalf of Seller to any Payer have been accurate and complete in all material respects. Each was properly coded and adequately documented, and each was submitted in compliance with the applicable Payer Agreement and all Healthcare Laws.

(b) Seller has not received any overpayment that has not been refunded to the applicable Payer. No Payer has conducted, or to the Knowledge of Seller threatened, any audit, recoupment, offset, post-payment review or investigation relating to Seller's billing.

(c) Neither Seller nor any of its owners, dentists or employees is excluded, debarred or suspended from participation in any federal or state healthcare program.

(d) Seller has not entered into any corporate integrity agreement, settlement or similar arrangement with any Governmental Authority or Payer.

**4.13 Patient Records; Privacy.** Seller has maintained its patient records in compliance with HIPAA and applicable state Law. Since January 1, 2021, Seller has not experienced any breach of unsecured protected health information requiring notification under HIPAA.

**4.14 Employees.** Schedule 4.14 lists each employee and independent contractor of the Business, with title, compensation and accrued paid time off. Seller has complied in all material respects with all applicable employment Laws.

**4.15 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and has paid all Taxes due. No lien for Taxes encumbers any Purchased Asset.

**4.16 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.16, there is no action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.17 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 Professional Ownership.** Buyer has made arrangements, through its affiliated professional entity and management services arrangements, that comply with Delaware Law governing the corporate practice of dentistry.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Patient Records.** Buyer shall act as custodian of the patient records included in the Purchased Assets in accordance with HIPAA and Delaware Law. Buyer shall give Seller and its dentists reasonable access to those records for seven (7) years after the Closing Date in order to defend malpractice claims, respond to Payer audits and meet other legitimate needs.

**6.2 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to the employees listed on Schedule 6.2 (those who accept, the "**Transferred Employees**"). Seller shall pay all wages and benefits earned by its employees through the Closing Date.

**6.3 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its owners not to, directly or indirectly own, manage or practice general or pediatric dentistry within a fifteen (15) mile radius of any Practice Location. During the same period, Seller shall not, and shall cause its owners not to, solicit any patient or Transferred Employee of the Business.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except as required by Law.

**6.5 Further Assurances; IP Assignment.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party may reasonably request to carry out this Agreement. Without limiting the foregoing, Seller shall execute and deliver the IP Assignment Agreement and any short-form assignments, registrar transfer authorizations and account credentials reasonably required to record and perfect Buyer's ownership of the Intellectual Property included in the Purchased Assets.

**6.6 Payer Transition.** Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in obtaining the assignment of, or Buyer's credentialing and enrollment under, each Payer Agreement. Until Buyer is credentialed with a Payer, Seller shall permit Buyer to bill under Seller's provider numbers to the extent permitted by Law and the applicable Payer Agreement, and shall promptly remit to Buyer all amounts received for services rendered on or after the Closing Date.

**6.7 Billing Reconciliation.** Buyer first receives the Payer remittance advices, explanation-of-benefits data and recoupment and offset notices for the Practice Locations through the Payer transition under Section 6.6. Accordingly, the Parties shall conduct the first post-Closing reconciliation of Payer receipts, remittances, recoupments and offsets (the "**Billing Reconciliation**") on the date that is nine (9) months after the Closing Date. Each Party shall make its billing records, remittance data and Payer correspondence available to the other for that purpose. Within thirty (30) days after the Billing Reconciliation, the Parties shall settle any amounts owed between them for receipts relating to periods before or after the Closing Date.

**6.8 Accounts Receivable.** Seller shall retain all accounts receivable for services rendered before the Closing Date. Any payment received by Buyer relating to those accounts receivable shall be remitted to Seller within thirty (30) days after the end of the month of receipt, subject to Section 6.7.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing were subject to the accuracy of the other Party's representations and warranties in all material respects, the performance of the other Party's covenants in all material respects and the delivery of the items described in Sections 3.2 and 3.3, as applicable. Consummation of the Closing constitutes the satisfaction or waiver of these conditions.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of the Parties contained in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the Parties shall survive the Closing until fully performed in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification based on a breach of a representation or warranty may be asserted after the expiration of the applicable survival period. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before that expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, subject to this Article VIII, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, managers, members, employees and agents, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, shareholders, employees and agents, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability, including all Liabilities under the Payer Agreements, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**8.4 Claim Procedures.** A Person entitled to indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations, except to the extent it is actually prejudiced by that failure. The Indemnifying Party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of the notice to dispute the claim in writing.

**8.5 Third-Party Claims.** The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by giving written notice within fifteen (15) days after receipt of notice of the claim. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Any audit, recoupment or investigation by a Payer or Governmental Authority shall be treated as a third-party claim.

**8.6 Setoff.** Buyer may set off any amount to which it is entitled under this Article VIII against amounts payable under the Seller Note only in accordance with, and subject to, the terms and conditions of the Seller Note.

**8.7 Limitations.**

(a) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed an amount equal to one percent (1%) of the Purchase Price. Once that amount is exceeded, Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of it.

(b) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed 15% of the Purchase Price.

(c) *Exceptions.* The limitations in Sections 8.7(a) and 8.7(b) do not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or from fraud. The aggregate liability of Seller for those Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of fraud.

(d) *Application.* The limitations set forth in this Section 8.7 shall apply only to Seller's indemnification obligations under Section 8.2.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party for the matter giving rise to the claim, less the costs of recovery.

**8.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article VIII shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE IX. TAX MATTERS

**9.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use, documentary and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**9.2 Proration.** Personal property and similar periodic Taxes relating to the Purchased Assets shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

**10.1 No Assumption of Plans.** Buyer does not assume any employee benefit plan of Seller. Seller shall provide continuation coverage under COBRA to all "M&A qualified beneficiaries" as required by Law.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the Transaction Documents, the following order of precedence shall apply: the IP Assignment Agreement, then the Seller Note, each as to its subject matter, then this Agreement.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** Each Party agrees that irreparable damage would occur if Section 6.3 or 6.4 were not performed in accordance with its terms. **11.11 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
