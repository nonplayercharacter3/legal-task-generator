**Re: Letter of Intent – Proposed Acquisition of the Assets of Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A., a Delaware professional association ("**Seller**"). Those assets are used in Seller's general and pediatric dental practices (the "**Business**"). The proposed acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**." Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is non-binding and is intended only as a basis for negotiating a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and related transaction documents.

## 1. Structure; Purchased Assets

**1.1 Asset Purchase.** Buyer will acquire, free and clear of all liens, substantially all of the assets used in the Business at Seller's four (4) dental clinics located in Wilmington, Newark, Dover and Middletown, Delaware (the "**Practice Locations**"). The acquired assets will include:

(a) equipment, furniture, fixtures and supplies;
(b) inventory;
(c) assigned contracts, including real property leases and payer agreements;
(d) all intellectual property used in the Business, including trade names, the pediatric dental brand, website domains, social media accounts and patient-portal content;
(e) patient records;
(f) telephone numbers; and
(g) goodwill.

**1.2 Excluded Assets.** Cash, Seller's accounts receivable for services rendered before closing, Seller's organizational records and tax refunds will be excluded.

**1.3 Liabilities.** Buyer will assume only the liabilities specifically identified in the Definitive Agreement. These are expected to include obligations arising under the assigned contracts after closing and accrued paid time off of transferred employees, credited against the purchase price. Seller will retain all other liabilities of the Business. Retained liabilities will include pre-closing taxes, pre-closing professional malpractice claims, pre-closing employment liabilities, indebtedness, and any overpayment, recoupment or other liability arising from claims submitted to payers for services rendered before closing.

## 2. Purchase Price

**2.1 Amount.** The aggregate purchase price for the acquired assets will be $14,600,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to customary adjustments for prorations and the paid time off credit described in Section 1.3.

**2.2 Payment.** The Purchase Price will be paid as follows:

(a) $12,200,000 in cash at closing; and

(b) $2,400,000 by delivery of a secured promissory note made by Buyer in favor of Seller (the "**Seller Note**").

**2.3 Seller Note.** The Seller Note will bear simple interest at six percent (6.0%) per annum. It will be payable in four (4) equal semi-annual installments of principal, together with accrued interest, beginning six (6) months after closing. It will be secured by the acquired assets and may be prepaid without penalty. Buyer will be entitled to set off against payments due under the Seller Note any amounts claimed in good faith by Buyer for indemnification under the Definitive Agreement, with disputed amounts to be resolved under the Definitive Agreement's indemnification procedures.

**2.4 Allocation.** The Purchase Price will be allocated among the acquired assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code, using a schedule to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement.

## 3. Due Diligence

From the date Seller countersigns this Letter, Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the books, records, contracts, personnel and facilities of the Business. This includes access to:

(a) payer agreements, claims and remittance data;
(b) coding and documentation records;
(c) payer audit and recoupment correspondence;
(d) licensure and credentialing files;
(e) leases;
(f) employee records; and
(g) intellectual property records.

Buyer's due diligence will include a review of Seller's billing and coding practices for the period beginning January 1, 2021.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties of Seller customary for transactions of this type in the dental services sector. These will include representations as to:

(a) organization;
(b) authority;
(c) title to the acquired assets;
(d) intellectual property;
(e) financial statements;
(f) material contracts;
(g) licenses and permits;
(h) compliance with laws;
(i) healthcare billing and payer compliance, including the accuracy of claims, the absence of unrefunded overpayments and the absence of payer audits and exclusions;
(j) HIPAA and patient records;
(k) employees;
(l) taxes;
(m) litigation; and
(n) brokers.

Buyer will give customary representations as to organization, authority and brokers.

## 5. Survival; Indemnification

**5.1 Survival.** The general representations and warranties of the parties will survive for 18 months after Closing. The representations as to organization, authority, title to the acquired assets, intellectual property, taxes and brokers (the "**Fundamental Representations**") will survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants will survive until fully performed.

**5.2 Indemnification.** Each party will indemnify the other for losses arising from:

(a) breaches of its representations, warranties and covenants; and

(b) in the case of Seller, the retained liabilities; or

(c) in the case of Buyer, the assumed liabilities.

**5.3 Basket and Cap.** Indemnification for breaches of general representations and warranties will be subject to a deductible basket equal to one percent (1%) of the Purchase Price. Each party's aggregate liability for those breaches will be capped at fifteen percent (15%) of the Purchase Price. Claims for breaches of Fundamental Representations will not be subject to the basket or the fifteen percent (15%) cap, but will be capped at the Purchase Price. Claims for fraud will be uncapped. The basket and cap will apply mutually to Buyer and Seller.

## 6. Restrictive Covenants

At closing, Seller and its dentist-owners will agree not to own, manage or practice general or pediatric dentistry within a fifteen (15) mile radius of any Practice Location for five (5) years after closing. During the same period, they will agree not to solicit patients or transferred employees of the Business.

## 7. Employees

Buyer intends to offer employment, effective as of closing, to substantially all of Seller's clinical and administrative employees at the Practice Locations, on terms to be determined by Buyer. Seller will be responsible for all wages, benefits and other employment obligations through closing.

## 8. Payer Transition and Billing Reconciliation

Seller will cooperate with Buyer in the assignment of, or Buyer's credentialing and enrollment under, Seller's payer agreements. The Definitive Agreement will provide for a post-closing reconciliation of payer receipts, remittances, recoupments and offsets between the parties once Buyer has received payer remittance and recoupment data for the Practice Locations.

## 9. Conditions to Closing

The closing of the Transaction will be subject to the following:

(a) completion of due diligence satisfactory to Buyer;
(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement;
(c) receipt of landlord consents to the assignment of each Practice Location lease;
(d) receipt of material third-party consents, including required payer consents;
(e) Buyer's completion of arrangements complying with Delaware law governing the corporate practice of dentistry;
(f) release of all liens on the acquired assets; and
(g) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

## 10. Timing

The parties will use good-faith efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement and close the Transaction on or before August 3, 2026.

## 11. Transaction Documents

In addition to the Definitive Agreement, the transaction documents are expected to include:

(a) a bill of sale;
(b) an assignment and assumption agreement;
(c) lease assignments;
(d) the Seller Note and a related security agreement; and
(e) an intellectual property assignment covering the intellectual property used at all four (4) Practice Locations.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 12 through 16 of this Letter (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any binding obligation. No binding agreement concerning the Transaction will exist unless and until the Definitive Agreement has been executed and delivered by both parties.

## 13. Exclusivity

For sixty (60) days after Seller countersigns this Letter, Seller will not, and will cause its owners, employees and representatives not to, take any of the following actions with any person other than Buyer concerning any sale of the Business, the Practice Locations or the equity of Seller:

(a) solicit, initiate or encourage any proposal;
(b) negotiate; or
(c) enter into any agreement.

## 14. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential the terms of this Letter and all non-public information it receives from the other in connection with the Transaction, except as required by law. Buyer will handle any protected health information made available in due diligence in compliance with HIPAA and under a business associate agreement to be entered into before any such information is disclosed.

## 15. Expenses

Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, including the fees of its legal, accounting and financial advisors.

## 16. Governing Law; Termination; Counterparts

This Letter will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Binding Provisions other than Section 13 will survive any termination of negotiations. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time by written notice to the other. This Letter may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
