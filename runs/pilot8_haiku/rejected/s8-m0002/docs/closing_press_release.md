# Saitouth Dental Partners Completes Acquisition of Trolind Family Dentistry's Four Delaware Clinics

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**WILMINGTON, Del., August 4, 2026.** Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC ("Saitouth") announced today that it has acquired substantially all of the assets of Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A. ("Trolind"), a general and pediatric dental practice serving patients across Delaware. The transaction closed on August 3, 2026.

## 1. Transaction Overview

Saitouth acquired the Trolind practice through an asset purchase. The acquired assets include all four of Trolind's Delaware clinics, located in Wilmington, Newark, Dover and Middletown. The aggregate purchase price was $14,600,000. Saitouth paid part of the price in cash at closing and the remainder through a secured promissory note issued to the seller. No other financial terms were disclosed.

## 2. Continuity of Patient Care

All four clinics remain open at their current locations, and appointments are proceeding as scheduled. Patients do not need to take any action. Their dental records will continue to be maintained securely and in accordance with federal and Delaware privacy law. Saitouth has offered employment to the clinics' clinical and administrative staff so that patients can keep seeing the dentists, hygienists and team members they know.

Saitouth is working with dental benefits plans and other payers to transition the clinics' participating-provider arrangements. Patients should continue to present their current insurance information at each visit. Trolind's existing telephone numbers, websites and patient-portal access will continue to operate during the transition.

## 3. Statements

"Trolind has built a strong reputation for family and pediatric dentistry in Wilmington, Newark, Dover and Middletown," said the Chief Executive Officer of Saitouth Dental Partners. "We are committed to keeping that community-focused care in place. We will also invest in technology, equipment and team development at each clinic."

"Our priority in choosing a partner was making sure our patients and our team would be well cared for," said the President of Trolind Family Dentistry. "We are confident that Saitouth shares that commitment, and we are pleased that all four of our clinics will continue serving their communities."

## 4. About Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC

Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company that supports dental practices in the Mid-Atlantic region. It provides non-clinical business and administrative services. Clinical decisions remain with licensed dentists in accordance with Delaware law governing the practice of dentistry.

## 5. About Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A.

Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A. is a Delaware professional association. It has provided general and pediatric dental care to patients and families throughout Delaware from clinics in Wilmington, Newark, Dover and Middletown.
