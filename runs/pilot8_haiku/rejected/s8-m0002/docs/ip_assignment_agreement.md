# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of October 20, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **TROLIND FAMILY DENTISTRY, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association ("**Assignor**"), and **SAITOUTH DENTAL PARTNERS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as Buyer, are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor sold, and Assignee purchased, substantially all of the assets used in Assignor's dental practices, including the Intellectual Property used in the Business.

B. Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Assignment and any short-form assignments, registrar transfer authorizations and account credentials reasonably required to record and perfect Assignee's ownership of that Intellectual Property.

C. Assignor wishes to deliver this Assignment in satisfaction of that obligation, on the terms set forth below.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the consideration paid under the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Additional Defined Terms.** In this Assignment:

"**Assigned IP**" means all Intellectual Property owned by Assignor and used at or in connection with the Practice Locations as of the Closing Date, including the items listed on Exhibit B, together with:

(a) all registrations, applications and renewals of any of the foregoing;

(b) all goodwill associated with any of the foregoing trademarks, service marks, trade names, brand names and logos;

(c) all rights to sue for and collect damages for past, present and future infringement, dilution or other violation of any of the foregoing; and

(d) all income, royalties and other payments now or later due in respect of any of the foregoing.

"**Domain Names**" means the internet domain names included in the Assigned IP, together with all registrar accounts, hosting accounts, DNS settings and website content associated with them.

"**Online Accounts**" means all social media accounts, online business listings, review-site profiles, patient-portal accounts and similar online accounts included in the Assigned IP, together with all login credentials, administrative rights and content associated with them.

"**Practice Locations**" means the clinics listed on Exhibit A.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment of Assigned IP.** Effective as of the Closing Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The assignment of each trademark, service mark, trade name and brand name included in the Assigned IP includes the goodwill of the Business symbolized by that mark or name at the Practice Locations.

**2.3 Recordation.** Assignor authorizes the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, the Delaware Secretary of State, each domain name registrar and each other applicable registry or Governmental Authority to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned IP. Assignee may record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in the form of Exhibit C, with any of them.

## 3. Domain Names and Online Accounts

**3.1 Transfer of Domain Names.** Within ten (10) business days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall (a) unlock each Domain Name, (b) deliver to Assignee the authorization or transfer codes for each Domain Name and (c) take all other actions required by the applicable registrar to transfer each Domain Name to a registrar account designated by Assignee.

**3.2 Transfer of Online Accounts.** Within ten (10) business days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee the login credentials for each Online Account and shall add Assignee's designees as owners or administrators of each Online Account. After Assignee confirms in writing that it has full administrative control of an Online Account, Assignor shall remove its own users from that Online Account.

**3.3 Website and Portal Content.** Assignor shall deliver to Assignee complete copies, in their native electronic format, of all website content, images, text, design files and patient-portal content included in the Assigned IP.

**3.4 Telephone Numbers.** Assignor shall cooperate with Assignee and each applicable carrier to port to Assignee the telephone and fax numbers used at the Practice Locations, to the extent not ported before the Effective Date.

## 4. Use of Names After the Effective Date

**4.1 Cessation of Use.** From and after the Effective Date, Assignor shall not use, and shall cause its Affiliates and owners not to use, any trademark, service mark, trade name, brand name, logo or Domain Name included in the Assigned IP, or any confusingly similar name or mark, except as permitted by Section 4.2.

**4.2 Wind-Down Use.** Assignor may continue to use its legal name, "Trolind Family Dentistry, P.A.," solely (a) in filings with Governmental Authorities, (b) in connection with the collection of accounts receivable retained under Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement and (c) to wind down its affairs. Within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall file an amendment to its certificate of incorporation changing its legal name to a name that does not include "Trolind" or any other mark included in the Assigned IP.

## 5. Representations and Warranties of Assignor

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as of the Effective Date that:

(a) Assignor has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Assignment;

(b) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens, and has not previously assigned, licensed or otherwise transferred any interest in the Assigned IP to any Person;

(c) Exhibit B lists all registered Intellectual Property and all Domain Names and Online Accounts included in the Assigned IP; and

(d) the representations and warranties in Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement were true and correct as of the date of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Remedies

Assignor shall indemnify Assignee for any Losses arising out of any breach of this Assignment or of Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement, in accordance with Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement. Assignor's liability for any such breach shall be subject to the limitations set forth in Section 8.7 of the Purchase Agreement. Claims under this Section 6 shall be made in accordance with the procedures set forth in Sections 8.4 and 8.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. Further Assurances; Power of Attorney

**7.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver all further instruments and take all further actions reasonably necessary to vest, record, perfect, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. These actions include executing short-form assignments, declarations and registrar or platform transfer forms, and giving testimony in any proceeding relating to the Assigned IP.

**7.2 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file in Assignor's name any instrument described in Section 7.1 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) business days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**7.3 Forwarding of Communications.** Assignor shall promptly forward to Assignee any correspondence, notice, payment or patient communication it receives relating to the Assigned IP or directed to any Domain Name or Online Account.

## 8. General

**8.1 Notices.** Notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**8.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**8.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Assignee may assign its rights under this Assignment to any Affiliate, to any lender of Assignee as collateral or to any acquirer of all or any part of the Business.

**8.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Assignment is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**8.5 Governing Law.** This Assignment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**8.6 Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** Sections 11.7 and 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Assignment as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Assignment.

**8.7 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
