Landlord and Tenant are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the Building's lobbies, corridors, elevators, restrooms, loading dock, parking areas and other areas that Landlord makes available for the general use of tenants (the "**Common Areas**").

2.2 **Condition.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises on the Commencement Date with the Landlord's Work described in Section 3 substantially complete, and with the Building's heating, ventilation, air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing and life-safety systems serving the Premises in good working order. Except for Landlord's obligations under this Section 2.2, Section 3 and Section 9, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition.

2.3 **Measurement.** The rentable areas stated in Section 1.1 are agreed and are not subject to remeasurement during the Term.

## 3. LANDLORD'S WORK

3.1 **Scope.** Before the Commencement Date, Landlord shall, at its cost, (a) replace the carpet and repaint the walls throughout the Premises; (b) construct one conference room and two private offices in the locations shown in Exhibit A; (c) install a secured storage room of approximately 400 square feet with a card-access door; and (d) install dedicated 20-amp electrical circuits in the storage room for battery charging equipment (together, "**Landlord's Work**").

3.2 **Standards.** Landlord shall perform Landlord's Work in a good and workmanlike manner, using building-standard materials, and in compliance with applicable codes. Landlord shall correct any defects in Landlord's Work of which Tenant notifies Landlord within one (1) year after the Commencement Date.

## 4. TERM AND RENT

4.1 **Term.** The term of this Lease begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless extended under Section 5 or terminated earlier as provided in this Lease (the "**Term**").

4.2 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay annual base rent ("**Base Rent**") in equal monthly installments, in advance, on the first day of each calendar month, as follows:

4.3 **Abatement.** Base Rent for the first two (2) full calendar months of the Term is abated. Tenant remains responsible for Additional Rent during that period.

4.4 **Additional Rent.** All sums payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent together are "**Rent**."

4.5 **Payment.** Tenant shall pay Rent without demand, deduction or set-off, except as expressly provided in this Lease, by electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing. Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

4.6 **Late Charge.** If any Rent is not received within five (5) business days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve (12) month period if Tenant pays the overdue amount within five (5) business days after written notice.

## 5. OPTION TO EXTEND

5.1 **Option.** Provided Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period, Tenant may extend the Term for one (1) period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**") by giving Landlord written notice of exercise no earlier than twelve (12) months and no later than nine (9) months before the Expiration Date.

5.2 **Extension Rent.** Base Rent for the Extension Term shall equal ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent for the Premises. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay, and a willing landlord would accept, for comparable office space in comparable buildings in Ames, Iowa, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant factors, including concessions then being granted.

5.3 **Determination.** Landlord shall notify Tenant of its determination of Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after receiving Tenant's exercise notice. If Tenant disagrees and the Parties cannot agree within thirty (30) days after Landlord's notice, each Party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience leasing office space in central Iowa. If the two brokers' determinations differ by not more than five percent (5%), Fair Market Rent is their average. Otherwise, the two brokers shall appoint a third broker with the same qualifications, who shall select whichever of the first two determinations is closer to market. Each Party pays its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.

5.4 **Terms.** During the Extension Term, all terms of this Lease apply except that Base Rent is determined under this Section 5, there is no further option to extend, and Landlord has no obligation to perform improvements or provide allowances.

## 6. USE

6.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

6.2 **Equipment Storage.** Tenant may store drone airframes, payload sensors, flight batteries, chargers and spare parts in the secured storage room described in Section 3.1, in quantities consistent with office-based fleet coordination and repair. Tenant shall store and charge lithium batteries in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and applicable fire codes. Tenant shall not conduct flight operations from the Premises or the Building's parking areas, and shall not store pesticides, herbicides, fuels or other Hazardous Materials in the Premises, other than ordinary office and cleaning supplies.

6.3 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, the Building systems and the Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act, except to the extent compliance is required because of Tenant's alterations or particular use.

6.4 **Rules.** Tenant shall comply with the reasonable rules and regulations for the Building that Landlord adopts and applies uniformly to all tenants, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice. If any rule conflicts with this Lease, this Lease controls.

## 7. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

7.1 **Payment of Excess.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

7.2 **Definitions.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, insure and manage the Building and the Common Areas, excluding capital expenditures (except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Lease Date, in each case amortized over their useful life), leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements, debt service, ground rent, costs reimbursed by insurance or other tenants, and management fees exceeding three percent (3%) of gross Building revenues. "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land on which it stands, excluding income, franchise and estate taxes. If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied in any year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy are adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

7.3 **Controllable Expense Cap.** Tenant's Share of controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than Taxes, insurance, utilities and snow removal) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis over the Base Year amount.

7.4 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord may bill Tenant monthly for Landlord's reasonable estimate of the amount due under Section 7.1. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall give Tenant a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due (or refunded if the Term has ended), and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days.

7.5 **Audit.** Tenant may, on thirty (30) days' notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving an annual statement, review Landlord's records for that year. If the review shows Tenant was overcharged by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse the reasonable cost of the review.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

8.1 **Services.** Landlord shall furnish heating, ventilation and air-conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding holidays; electricity for normal office use and the storage-room circuits described in Section 3.1; water for restrooms; janitorial service five (5) days per week; elevator service; and snow removal for the parking areas and walkways. Tenant shall have access to the Premises and the parking areas twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week.

8.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside the hours in Section 8.1 on Tenant's request, at Landlord's actual cost without markup.

8.3 **Interruption.** If any service that Landlord must provide is interrupted for more than five (5) consecutive business days for reasons within Landlord's reasonable control, and Tenant cannot reasonably use the Premises as a result, Rent shall abate from the sixth (6th) business day until the service is restored.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

9.1 **Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements and exterior of the Building, the Building systems up to the point of connection to the Premises, and the Common Areas.

9.2 **Tenant.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good, clean condition, reasonable wear and tear, casualty and Landlord's obligations excepted.

## 10. ALTERATIONS

10.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations (such as painting, carpeting and installing shelving or furniture) costing less than $15,000 in any twelve (12) month period that do not affect the Building structure or systems.

10.2 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant may, and if Landlord so required in its consent shall, remove any alterations and restore the affected areas. Tenant need not remove Landlord's Work or cabling installed under Landlord's consent.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

11.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information it reasonably requires.

11.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent, (a) assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant; (b) assign this Lease to a successor resulting from a merger, consolidation or reorganization of Tenant, or to a purchaser of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets; and (c) undergo any transfer of its stock or other ownership interests, including a merger in which Tenant is the surviving entity (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"). In the case of an assignment under clause (b), the successor must have a tangible net worth at least equal to Tenant's tangible net worth immediately before the transfer. Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of any Permitted Transfer within thirty (30) days after it occurs. A transfer under clause (c) is not an assignment of this Lease.

11.3 **Liability.** No assignment or sublease relieves Tenant of its obligations under this Lease unless Landlord releases Tenant in writing.

11.4 **Excess Rent.** For any sublease or assignment other than a Permitted Transfer, Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received in excess of the Rent payable for the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer.

## 12. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

12.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain, during the Term, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and alterations for their full replacement cost; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord on the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

12.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain, during the Term, property insurance covering the Building for its full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence for the Common Areas.

12.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other Party for loss or damage to property to the extent covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance.

12.4 **Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 12.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Premises or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, and Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Common Areas or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, except in each case to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnified Party.

## 13. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

13.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or access to them is damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, give Tenant its contractor's estimate of the time to restore. If the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days from the casualty, either Party may terminate this Lease by notice within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate. Otherwise, Landlord shall promptly restore the Premises and Common Areas, excluding Tenant's property and alterations.

13.2 **Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is unusable from the date of the casualty until Landlord completes restoration.

13.3 **Condemnation.** If any part of the Premises, or of the Building or parking needed for Tenant's reasonable use of the Premises, is taken by eminent domain, either Party may terminate this Lease as of the date of taking. Otherwise, Rent shall be reduced proportionately. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving expenses and personal property.

## 14. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

14.1 **Tenant Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured in that time, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that time and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes subject to a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

14.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease or Tenant's right to possession by written notice and pursue the remedies available under Iowa law. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages, including by reletting the Premises.

14.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or a longer reasonable period if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days and Landlord diligently pursues the cure), Tenant may pursue its remedies at law and in equity. If the failure involves an emergency threatening persons or property within the Premises, Tenant may perform the obligation after reasonable notice and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 15. SECURITY DEPOSIT

15.1 Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord. Landlord may apply it to cure an Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore any amount applied within ten (10) business days after notice. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 16. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

16.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in the condition required by Section 9.2, and shall remove its personal property, including all stored equipment and batteries.

16.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days, and one hundred fifty percent (150%) after that, prorated daily.

## 17. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

17.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided that the mortgagee enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement agreeing not to disturb Tenant's possession so long as no Event of Default exists.

17.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each Party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after request by the other, deliver a certificate confirming whether this Lease is in full force, the dates through which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.
