# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or the "**Supply Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement Effective Date**" or "**Effective Date**") by and between **Lakix Aerial Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**" or "**Lakix**"), and **Ziteir Agronomics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**" or "**Ziteir**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells agricultural spray drones and payload sensors, and operates a cloud-based flight-data platform.

B. Customer provides drone spraying, field-mapping and related crop services to growers, and wishes to purchase Products and subscribe to Platform Services from Supplier.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Customer will purchase Products and Platform Services during the Term.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity.

(b) "**Change of Control**" has the meaning given in Section 15.2.

(c) "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period during the Term beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of the Effective Date.

(d) "**Customer Sites**" means the fields, farms and other locations at which Customer or its personnel operate Products, as identified in a Statement of Work or Purchase Order.

(e) "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum entered into by the Parties in connection with this Agreement covering Farm Data, as amended from time to time.

(f) "**Farm Data**" has the meaning given in the Data Processing Addendum.

(g) "**Platform Services**" means Supplier's cloud-based flight-data platform, including flight logging, field-mapping, spray-record and fleet-management functionality, and related support, as described in Schedule 1.

(h) "**Products**" means the spray drones, payload sensors, batteries, chargers, spare parts and accessories listed in Schedule 1, as updated by the Parties in writing.

(i) "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products or Platform Services issued by Customer under this Agreement.

(j) "**Specifications**" means Supplier's published technical specifications for each Product in effect on the date of the applicable Purchase Order.

(k) "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes a deployment, fleet program or services to be provided under this Agreement.

(l) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.2 **Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

1.3 **Construction.** "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

1.4 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising the relationship between the Parties, the following order of precedence applies: Data Processing Addendum, then Supply Agreement, then any Statement of Work. Purchase Order terms that add to or conflict with this Agreement are of no effect unless signed by an authorized officer of each Party.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Supply Agreement Effective Date and continues for three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 14.

2.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three-year renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the expiration of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. All terms of this Agreement, including Section 3.2, apply during each Renewal Term.

## 3. SUPPLY AND PURCHASE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Supply.** During the Term, Supplier shall sell Products and provide Platform Services to Customer in accordance with Purchase Orders and Statements of Work accepted under Section 4.

3.2 **Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Customer shall purchase Products and Platform Services having an aggregate invoiced value of not less than $2,400,000 per contract year (the "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**"). Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall invoice Customer for any shortfall between the Minimum Purchase Commitment and the aggregate amounts invoiced for that Contract Year, and Customer shall pay that shortfall in accordance with Section 5.3. Amounts invoiced in excess of the Minimum Purchase Commitment in any Contract Year do not carry forward.

3.3 **Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling six (6) month forecast of Product requirements on or before the first business day of each calendar quarter.

## 4. ORDERING

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer may order Products and Platform Services by issuing Purchase Orders stating the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery locations.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days after receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with Customer's most recent forecast. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Statements of Work.** The Parties may enter into Statements of Work for fleet deployments, training, field support and similar programs. Each Statement of Work forms part of this Agreement, and Products and services provided under it count toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment.

4.4 **Changes and Cancellation.** Customer may change or cancel an accepted Purchase Order without charge up to thirty (30) days before the scheduled delivery date. Later cancellations are subject to a restocking charge of fifteen percent (15%) of the price of the affected Products.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Prices for Products and Platform Services are set out in Schedule 1 or in the applicable Statement of Work. Supplier may adjust Schedule 1 prices once per Contract Year on at least ninety (90) days' written notice, and no adjustment may exceed four percent (4%) over the prices then in effect.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Products upon shipment and Platform Services annually in advance.

5.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay undisputed invoices in U.S. dollars within thirty (30) days after receipt. Late amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice with reasonable detail before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay, other than taxes on Supplier's net income.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier designated in the Purchase Order, or as otherwise stated in a Statement of Work.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer upon delivery in accordance with Section 6.1.

6.3 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within ten (10) business days after receipt and reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications. Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace rejected Products at its cost.

6.4 **Late Delivery.** If Supplier fails to deliver Products within fifteen (15) days after the confirmed delivery date, Customer may cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability, and the cancelled amount shall be credited against the Minimum Purchase Commitment for that Contract Year.

## 7. PLATFORM SERVICES

7.1 **Access.** Supplier grants Customer and its authorized users a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to access and use the Platform Services for Customer's business operations.

7.2 **Availability.** Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform Services available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

7.3 **Restrictions.** Customer shall not reverse engineer, resell or sublicense the Platform Services, except to provide growers with reports and maps generated through the Platform Services.

7.4 **Firmware.** Supplier shall make firmware and software updates for Products available to Customer at no additional charge during the Term.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product will be free from defects in materials and workmanship and will conform to the Specifications for twelve (12) months after delivery.

8.2 **Regulatory Compliance.** Supplier warrants that Products, as delivered, comply with applicable Federal Aviation Administration equipment requirements and applicable product-safety laws, and that spray systems are designed to permit application in accordance with pesticide label requirements.

8.3 **Remedies.** For any breach of Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace the non-conforming Product or refund the price paid for it.

8.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has authority to enter into this Agreement, and will comply with applicable laws in performing it.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 **Supplier IP.** Supplier retains all right, title and interest in the Products' designs, firmware and software and in the Platform Services.

9.2 **Customer Materials.** Customer retains all right, title and interest in its field plans, prescriptions, maps and other materials it provides or generates. Rights in Farm Data are governed by the Data Processing Addendum.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers and employees from third-party claims (a) alleging that the Products or Platform Services infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret, or (b) for bodily injury or damage to tangible property caused by a defect in a Product.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers and employees from third-party claims arising from Customer's breach of applicable law in operating Products, and as provided in any Statement of Work.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim imposing obligations on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.4, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits or revenue, arising out of this Agreement.

11.2 **Application.** The limitations in this Section 11 apply regardless of the form of action, whether in contract, tort or otherwise.

11.3 **Liability Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.4, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

11.4 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.3 do not apply to (a) a Party's obligations under Section 10.1; (b) Customer's indemnification obligations under any Statement of Work; (c) a Party's breach of Section 12; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (e) Customer's payment obligations, including under Section 3.2 and Section 15.2.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other Party's non-public business, technical and financial information ("**Confidential Information**") in confidence, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective.

12.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is public through no fault of the recipient, was known to the recipient without restriction, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party. A recipient may disclose Confidential Information as required by law after giving prompt notice where lawful.

12.3 **Duration.** These obligations continue for three (3) years after the end of the Term, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION

13.1 **Data Processing Addendum.** The collection, processing, storage, ownership and use of Farm Data in connection with the Products and Platform Services are governed by the Data Processing Addendum, which forms part of this Agreement.

13.2 **Security.** Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for the Platform Services consistent with the Data Processing Addendum.

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

14.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy or similar proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.3 **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration, Customer shall pay all amounts accrued through the effective date, Supplier shall provide Customer with an export of Farm Data as provided in the Data Processing Addendum, and each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information.

14.4 **Survival.** Sections 3.2 (as to amounts accrued), 5, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14.3, 14.4, 15.2 (as to amounts accelerated), 16 and 17 survive termination or expiration.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Supplier may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business relating to the Products on written notice to Customer.

15.2 **Change of Control.** Any Change of Control of Customer shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer requiring Supplier's prior written consent. "**Change of Control**" means (a) the acquisition by any person or group, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of Customer, whether by merger, stock purchase, consolidation, operation of law or otherwise, or (b) the sale of all or substantially all of Customer's assets. If a Change of Control of Customer occurs without Supplier's prior written consent, Supplier may terminate this Agreement on written notice to Customer, and upon such termination the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the remainder of the then-current Term shall become immediately due and payable.

15.3 **Void Transfers.** Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 15 is void.

15.4 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, exclusively, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation (other than for injunctive relief), a Party shall refer the dispute to the Parties' senior executives, who shall meet within fifteen (15) business days to attempt to resolve it.

16.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

17.2 **Insurance.** Each Party shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence, and Supplier shall maintain products liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence, during the Term.

17.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

17.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency.

17.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

17.6 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

17.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
