**Re: Master Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 between Lakix Aerial Systems, LLC and Ziteir Agronomics, Inc. — Notice of Pending Merger**

Dear Counsel:

1. **Purpose of this Letter.** We are writing about the Master Supply Agreement between Lakix Aerial Systems, LLC ("**Lakix**") and Ziteir Agronomics, Inc. ("**Ziteir**"), dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). Ziteir has entered into a definitive agreement under which Penwick Agri Holdings, Inc. ("**Penwick**") will acquire all of the outstanding voting stock of Ziteir by merger (the "**Transaction**"). As a valued supplier, we want you to hear about the Transaction directly from us before it is announced publicly.

2. **Transaction Structure and Timing.** Under the Transaction, a wholly owned subsidiary of Penwick will merge with and into Ziteir. Ziteir will survive the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Penwick. The parties expect the Transaction to close on November 16, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.

3. **Effect on the Supply Agreement.** This notice is provided for your information only. Ziteir will survive the merger as the contracting entity and will remain the "Customer" under the Supply Agreement. Ziteir will not assign or transfer the Supply Agreement to Penwick or to any other entity. Accordingly, no consent is required from Lakix in connection with the Transaction, and we are not asking Lakix to take any action.

4. **Continuity of Operations.** Ziteir expects no interruption to its spray and field-mapping programs as a result of the Transaction. Purchase Orders, the Statement of Work for the 2026 spray-fleet deployment, and Ziteir's use of the Lakix FlightData Platform will continue in the ordinary course. Our operations and procurement teams remain your day-to-day contacts. Billing and payment arrangements under Section 5 of the Supply Agreement will not change.

5. **Confidentiality.** The Transaction has not yet been publicly announced. This letter and the information in it are Confidential Information of Ziteir under Section 12 of the Supply Agreement. Please limit internal circulation to personnel who need to know.

6. **Contact.** Please direct any questions about this letter to the undersigned at legal@ziteir.com. We appreciate the strong working relationship between our companies and look forward to continuing it.
