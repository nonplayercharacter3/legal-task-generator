# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 2026-01: 2026 SPRAY-FLEET DEPLOYMENT

This Statement of Work No. 2026-01 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Lakix Aerial Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**" or "**Lakix**"), and **Ziteir Agronomics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**" or "**Ziteir**"). It is issued under the Master Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE AND INCORPORATION

1.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the Products, Platform Services, deployment services and field support that Supplier will provide for Customer's 2026 spray season fleet (the "**2026 Spray Fleet**"). Customer will use the 2026 Spray Fleet to perform aerial application and field-mapping services for Growers at Customer Sites.

1.2 **Incorporation.** This SOW is a Statement of Work under Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement and forms part of the Supply Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement or, for "Farm Data" and "Grower," in the Data Processing Addendum.

1.3 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Under Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement, all Products and services invoiced under this SOW count toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which they are invoiced.

## 2. FLEET AND EQUIPMENT

2.1 **Spray Drones.** Supplier shall supply 40 Lakix AX-9 spray drones (Part No. LX-AX9-SD), each configured to the Specifications with a 40 L tank, RTK positioning module, and Customer's standard nozzle set (the "**Fleet Aircraft**").

2.2 **Payload Sensors.** Supplier shall supply twelve (12) Lakix MS-4 Multispectral Payload Sensors (Part No. LX-MS4-PS) and six (6) Lakix TH-2 Thermal Payload Sensors (Part No. LX-TH2-PS). Each sensor must be calibrated and paired to a Fleet Aircraft designated by Customer before delivery.

2.3 **Support Equipment.** Supplier shall supply:

(a) one hundred sixty (160) AX-9 Flight Battery Packs (Part No. LX-BP30), being four (4) per Fleet Aircraft;

(b) ten (10) Field Charging Stations (Part No. LX-FC8); and

(c) forty (40) AX-9 Spare Parts Kits (Part No. LX-SPK-AX9), being one (1) per Fleet Aircraft.

2.4 **Platform Services.** Supplier shall provision forty (40) Lakix FlightData Platform subscriptions (Part No. LX-CLOUD-FD), one for each Fleet Aircraft. Each subscription runs for twelve (12) months from the date the applicable Fleet Aircraft is activated on the Platform Services. Farm Data generated by the 2026 Spray Fleet is subject to the Data Processing Addendum.

## 4. DEPLOYMENT SERVICES

4.1 **Commissioning.** Within five (5) business days after each phase arrives at the designated depot, Supplier shall send a field technician to that depot to:

(a) unpack, assemble and run pre-flight checks on each Fleet Aircraft;

(b) install current firmware under Section 7.4 of the Supply Agreement;

(c) calibrate spray systems, flow meters and RTK modules; and

(d) activate each Fleet Aircraft and paired sensor on the Platform Services and confirm that flight and spray data transfer correctly.

4.2 **Pilot Training.** Supplier shall provide a three (3) day AX-9 operator course at each operating base for up to twelve (12) Customer pilots per base. The course covers flight operations, spray calibration, drift-reduction settings, mapping workflows on the Platform Services, and routine maintenance. Supplier shall issue a completion certificate to each pilot who completes the course.

4.3 **Field Support.** From March 16, 2026 through October 31, 2026 (the "**Support Period**"), Supplier shall provide:

(a) telephone and remote support from 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Central Time, seven (7) days a week;

(b) a response by a qualified technician within two (2) hours after Customer reports any issue that grounds a Fleet Aircraft; and

(c) advance replacement of any Fleet Aircraft or major component under warranty, shipped within one (1) business day after Supplier confirms the fault.

4.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall:

(a) hold all Federal Aviation Administration authorizations and exemptions required to operate the Fleet Aircraft for agricultural aircraft operations, and all state pesticide-applicator licenses required for its pilots;

(b) select chemicals, tank mixes and application rates in accordance with product labels and Grower instructions;

(c) provide Supplier's technicians with reasonable access to its operating bases; and

(d) identify the Customer Sites for each spray program on the Platform Services before operations begin.

## 7. FLIGHT OPERATIONS INDEMNITY

7.1 **Indemnity.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim relating to drone flights or spray applications by the 2026 Spray Fleet over or near Customer Sites, regardless of Supplier's negligence. Covered claims include claims by neighboring growers, landowners and other third parties for crop damage, chemical drift, contamination of adjacent fields, livestock or apiary losses, trespass and nuisance.

7.2 **Procedure.** Section 10.3 of the Supply Agreement applies to claims under this Section 7.

7.3 **Extent.** Customer's obligations under this Section 7 shall not be subject to any limitation of liability, and continue after expiration or termination of this SOW or the Supply Agreement for claims arising from flights or applications performed during the Term.
