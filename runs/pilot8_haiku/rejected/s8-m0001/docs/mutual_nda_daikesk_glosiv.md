# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Daikesk Diagnostics Holdings, Inc.** ("**Daikesk**") and **Glosiv Medtech Group, Inc.** ("**Glosiv**"). Daikesk and Glosiv are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Glosiv owns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Daikesk would acquire the Company (the "**Potential Transaction**"). In doing so, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information about itself, its Affiliates and, in Glosiv's case, the Company.

C. Each Party is willing to disclose that information only on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Clean Team Information**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Disclosing Party or its Representatives disclose to the Receiving Party or its Representatives in connection with the Potential Transaction, whether before or after the Effective Date. This includes:

- business plans, financial statements and projections;
- product designs, source code, engineering specifications and service software;
- regulatory submissions and correspondence with the FDA or any other governmental authority;
- quality system records, complaint files and service records;
- customer, installed-base, supplier and pricing information; and
- personnel and compensation information.

Confidential Information also includes all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials that the Receiving Party or its Representatives prepare and that contain or reflect any of that information (the "**Derivative Materials**"), and the Transaction Information.

"**Disclosing Party**" means the Party that discloses, or whose Representatives disclose, Confidential Information.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means the Party that receives, or whose Representatives receive, Confidential Information.

"**Representatives**" of a Party means its Affiliates and its and their respective directors, officers, employees, agents, legal counsel, accountants, financial advisors, consultants and, in Daikesk's case, actual or prospective financing sources.

"**Transaction Information**" means the fact that the Parties are discussing the Potential Transaction, the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, and the terms and status of those discussions.

## 2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

**2.1 Use Restriction.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and, if the Parties so agree, consummate the Potential Transaction (the "**Purpose**").

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any Person, except to those of its Representatives who:

(a) need to know the information for the Purpose;

(b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and

(c) are bound by duties of confidentiality to the Receiving Party at least as protective as this Agreement, whether by written agreement or professional obligation.

**2.3 Responsibility for Representatives.** The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives, as if the Representative were a party to this Agreement.

**2.4 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS; COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

**3.1 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by documentary evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was in the Receiving Party's lawful possession on a non-confidential basis before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, is not bound by a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**3.2 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule or legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that it may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, at its own expense; and

(b) cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in seeking that remedy.

If no protective order is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for it.

**3.3 Regulatory Examinations.** Section 3.2 does not apply to disclosures made to a banking, securities or other regulatory authority in the course of a routine examination that is not targeted at the Disclosing Party, the Company or the Potential Transaction.

## 4. COMPETITIVELY SENSITIVE INFORMATION

**4.1 Clean Team Information.** A Disclosing Party may designate as "**Clean Team Information**" any Confidential Information that it reasonably considers competitively sensitive, including current and future pricing, customer-specific terms, product roadmaps and unreleased product designs. The Receiving Party shall make Clean Team Information available only to those of its Representatives that the Parties agree in writing to admit to a clean team. Those Representatives shall not have direct responsibility for pricing, sales or marketing of competing products.

**4.2 Antitrust Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with applicable antitrust laws in exchanging Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement requires either Party to disclose any information that its counsel advises may not lawfully be disclosed before the expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended.

**4.3 Protected Health Information.** Neither Party intends to disclose protected health information within the meaning of HIPAA. If any complaint file, service record or other material contains such information, the Disclosing Party shall de-identify or redact it before disclosure. The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party of, and shall not use, any such information it receives in unredacted form.

## 5. CONTACTS

**5.1 Designated Contacts.** Unless Glosiv otherwise consents in writing, Daikesk shall direct all communications regarding the Potential Transaction, requests for information, requests for facility visits and requests to meet management of the Company to the individuals Glosiv designates in writing. Without that consent, Daikesk shall not initiate contact about the Potential Transaction with any employee, customer, supplier or distributor of the Company. This Section 5.1 does not restrict contacts made in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Potential Transaction.

## 6. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

**6.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall, at its election, return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an authorized officer of the Receiving Party shall confirm in writing that it has done so.

**6.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies required to comply with law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention policies, and (b) copies stored in electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, despite the expiration of the term in Section 9.1.

## 7. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**7.1 No Warranty.** Each Disclosing Party provides its Confidential Information "as is." Neither Party nor its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information. Neither Party has any liability to the other arising from the use of Confidential Information, except as set forth in a definitive written agreement for the Potential Transaction, if one is executed, and then only to the extent provided in it.

**7.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Unless and until the Parties execute a definitive written agreement for the Potential Transaction, neither Party has any legal obligation of any kind regarding the Potential Transaction by virtue of this Agreement or any other written or oral expression, except for the matters specifically agreed in this Agreement. Either Party may end discussions at any time and for any reason.

**7.3 No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in or to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information or intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach, without proof of actual damages and without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**8.2 Fees.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party or its Representatives breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable, documented legal fees and expenses incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 9. TERM

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement continues in effect for two years after the Effective Date. The obligations in Sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 continue for that period with respect to all Confidential Information disclosed during it.

**9.2 Trade Secrets.** For any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, the Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 2 and 3 continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when received by the addressee if sent by a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) on the date sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent during normal business hours of the recipient, and otherwise on the next business day. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

**10.2 Securities Laws.** Each Party acknowledges that it is aware, and will advise its Representatives who receive Confidential Information, that applicable securities laws may restrict any Person who has material non-public information about an issuer from purchasing or selling that issuer's securities, or from communicating that information to others.

**10.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**10.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party granting it. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

**10.5 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that holding does not affect any other term of this Agreement. The court may modify the affected term to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**10.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.7 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of that court in any such suit, action or proceeding. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. A signed copy delivered by email or other electronic transmission has the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.
