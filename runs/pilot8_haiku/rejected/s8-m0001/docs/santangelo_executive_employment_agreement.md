# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 18, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Mireille Santangelo** (the "**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company designs, manufactures, sells and services CT, MRI and ultrasound imaging systems and related service software.

B. The Company wishes to employ the Executive as its Chief Technology Officer, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. In her role, the Executive will have access to the Company's confidential information, trade secrets, product roadmaps and engineering personnel. The Company would not employ the Executive on these terms without the protections in Section 7.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Cause**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

"**Change of Control**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Competing Business**" means any business that designs, develops, manufactures, sells or services CT, MRI or ultrasound imaging systems, or software used to operate, calibrate, monitor or service those systems.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning given in Section 5.4.

"**Inventions**" has the meaning given in Section 7.4.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of the Executive's employment with the Company and the 18 months after the termination of that employment for any reason.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs the Executive as its Chief Technology Officer. The Executive reports to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

**2.2 Duties.** The Executive is responsible for the Company's research and development, systems engineering, software engineering and product technology strategy. This includes oversight of the design and development of the Company's CT, MRI and ultrasound imaging systems and related service software. The Executive shall also perform any other duties consistent with her position that the Chief Executive Officer or the Board reasonably assigns.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** The Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time, attention and efforts to the Company. She may (a) serve on the boards of up to two non-profit or professional organizations, and (b) hold an unpaid adjunct academic appointment, provided that these activities do not interfere materially with her duties or breach Section 7.

**2.4 Location.** The Executive's principal place of employment is the Company's headquarters in Wilmington, Delaware, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues until terminated in accordance with Section 5 (the "**Employment Period**").

**3.2 At-Will Employment.** Subject to the payments and benefits provided in Section 6, the Executive's employment is at will. Either the Company or the Executive may terminate it at any time, with or without Cause or Good Reason.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $410,000 (the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board or its compensation committee shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase it, but not decrease it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Employment Period, the Executive is eligible to receive an annual cash bonus with a target amount of 50% of the Base Salary. Payment depends on the achievement of Company and individual performance goals that the Board establishes after consultation with the Executive. Any bonus earned for a fiscal year shall be paid no later than March 15 of the following year. To receive it, the Executive must remain employed through the payment date, except as provided in Section 6.

**4.3 Equity.** The Executive is eligible to participate in any long-term incentive plan maintained by the Company or its parent. Awards are made at the discretion of the applicable plan administrator.

**4.4 Benefits.** The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.5 Vacation.** The Executive is entitled to 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to Company holidays.

**4.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policies.

## 5. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**5.1 Death or Disability.** The Executive's employment terminates automatically on her death. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment by written notice if she is unable, because of physical or mental illness or incapacity, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period.

**5.2 Termination by the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause by written notice. "**Cause**" means any of the following:

(a) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony;

(b) the Executive's willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company;

(c) the Executive's material breach of Section 7; or

(d) the Executive's willful and continued failure to perform her material duties, after written notice from the Board and a 30-day opportunity to cure.

**5.3 Termination by the Company Without Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause on 30 days' written notice.

**5.4 Termination by the Executive for Good Reason.** The Executive may terminate her employment for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, if the event occurs without the Executive's consent:

(a) a material reduction in the Base Salary or target bonus;

(b) a material diminution in the Executive's title, authority, duties or responsibilities; or

(c) a relocation of the Executive's principal place of employment by more than 50 miles.

To terminate for Good Reason, the Executive must (i) give the Company written notice of the condition within 60 days after it first occurs, (ii) allow the Company 30 days to cure it, and (iii) resign within 30 days after the end of the cure period if the condition remains uncured.

**5.5 Resignation by the Executive.** The Executive may resign without Good Reason on 60 days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period.

**5.6 Resignation from Positions.** When the Executive's employment terminates for any reason, she is deemed to have resigned from every officer and director position she holds with the Company and its Affiliates.

## 6. PAYMENTS ON TERMINATION

**6.1 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination of employment, the Company shall pay or provide the following (the "**Accrued Obligations**"):

(a) any unpaid Base Salary through the termination date;

(b) any earned but unpaid annual bonus for a completed fiscal year;

(c) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(d) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans, in accordance with their terms.

**6.2 Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following in addition to the Accrued Obligations:

(a) continued payment of the Base Salary for 12 months after the termination date;

(b) a pro-rated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance and paid when bonuses are paid to other senior executives; and

(c) payment or reimbursement of COBRA premiums for the Executive and her eligible dependents for up to 12 months.

**6.3 Release.** The payments and benefits under Section 6.2 depend on two conditions. First, the Executive must execute a general release of claims in favor of the Company and its Affiliates in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, and that release must become effective and irrevocable within 60 days after the termination date. Second, the Executive must continue to comply with Section 7.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Company and its Affiliates, including:

- product designs, source code, algorithms, reconstruction and image-processing methods, and service software;
- engineering specifications;
- regulatory submissions;
- complaint and service data;
- customer and installed-base information;
- pricing; and
- business plans.

During and after the Employment Period, the Executive shall hold Confidential Information in strict confidence. She shall not use or disclose it except in the performance of her duties for the Company or as required by law. If disclosure is required by law, she shall give the Company prompt written notice before disclosing.

**7.2 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, anywhere in the United States, Canada, the European Union or the United Kingdom, directly or indirectly own, manage, operate, be employed by, consult for or otherwise provide services to any Competing Business in a role similar to the one she held at the Company, or in a role in which she could reasonably be expected to use or disclose Confidential Information. Passive ownership of less than 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person does not breach this Section 7.2.

**7.3 Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly:

(a) solicit, recruit or hire, or attempt to solicit, recruit or hire, any employee or independent contractor of the Company who was employed or engaged by the Company during the 12 months before the solicitation; or

(b) solicit any customer, distributor or supplier of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact during the last 24 months of her employment, for the purpose of diverting business from the Company or inducing that Person to reduce or end its relationship with the Company.

**7.4 Assignment of Inventions.** "**Inventions**" means all inventions, discoveries, improvements, designs, software, works of authorship, know-how and other intellectual property, whether or not patentable, that the Executive conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during the Employment Period, and that relate to the actual or reasonably anticipated business, research or development of the Company. The Executive shall promptly disclose all Inventions to the Company. She assigns, and agrees to assign, to the Company all of her right, title and interest in and to all Inventions. During and after the Employment Period, she shall execute all documents and take all actions the Company reasonably requests to perfect, register and enforce the Company's rights in the Inventions.

**7.5 Excluded Inventions.** Section 7.4 does not apply to any invention that qualifies fully for exclusion under Section 805 of Title 19 of the Delaware Code. Under that section, an excluded invention is one the Executive developed entirely on her own time without using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities or trade secret information, unless the invention relates to the Company's business or actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, or results from work the Executive performed for the Company. Schedule 1 lists the inventions the Executive made before the Effective Date and excludes from this Agreement.

**7.6 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, or earlier at the Company's request, the Executive shall return to the Company all Company property, documents and data, including all copies of Confidential Information.

**7.7 Reasonableness; Remedies.** The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 7 are reasonable in scope, duration and geography and are necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests. If the Executive breaches or threatens to breach this Section 7, the Company is entitled to injunctive relief without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable, it may modify the restriction to the extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**7.8 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental authority, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in the circumstances that statute describes.

## 8. SECTION 409A

**8.1 Compliance.** The parties intend this Agreement to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each payment under this Agreement is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. Any payment that is subject to Section 409A and payable on termination of employment shall be paid only on a "separation from service." If the Executive is a "specified employee" on her separation from service, any such payment due within six months after separation shall be delayed until the first business day after that six-month period.

## 9. CHANGE OF CONTROL

**9.1 Definition.** "**Change of Control**" means the occurrence of any of the following:

(a) any Person or group of Persons acting together, other than Glosiv Medtech Group, Inc. or its Affiliates, acquires, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, beneficial ownership of more than 50% of the outstanding voting stock of the Company, including by a sale of a majority of the Company's stock;

(b) a merger, consolidation or reorganization of the Company, after which the holders of the Company's voting stock immediately before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting power of the surviving entity; or

(c) a sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

**9.2 Effect on Covenants.** On a Change of Control, the covenants of the Executive in Section 7.2 (Non-Competition), Section 7.3 (Non-Solicitation) and Section 7.4 (Assignment of Inventions) shall automatically terminate and be of no further force or effect. This termination occurs as of the consummation of the Change of Control, unless the written consent of the Executive to the continuation of those covenants has been delivered to the Company before the Change of Control is consummated. All other provisions of this Agreement remain in full force and effect after a Change of Control.

**9.3 Change of Control Severance.** If, within 12 months after a Change of Control, the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, the following adjustments apply to the severance under Section 6.2, subject to Section 6.3:

(a) the Base Salary continuation period in Section 6.2(a) is 18 months;

(b) the Executive also receives a lump-sum payment equal to her target annual bonus; and

(c) the COBRA period in Section 6.2(c) is 18 months.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its headquarters, attention: General Counsel. Notices to the Executive go to the most recent home address on the Company's records.

**10.2 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive, and she may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets. The Agreement binds and benefits the Company's successors and assigns.

**10.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**10.4 Severability.** Subject to Section 7.7, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld under applicable law.

**10.6 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to its conflict of law rules. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in the State of Delaware. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**10.7 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with Schedule 1, contains the entire agreement between the Company and the Executive concerning her employment. It supersedes all prior offer letters and understandings between them on that subject.

**10.8 Survival.** Sections 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 survive the termination of the Executive's employment in accordance with their terms.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
