# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

**DRAFT – FORM OF EXHIBIT B TO STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of [●], 2026 and is between **Glosiv Medtech Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Daikesk Diagnostics Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient from Provider.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates have provided Recipient with field service, complaint-handling, service-software support and information technology services.

C. To support an orderly transition of Recipient's business, the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Provider will continue to provide those services to Recipient for a limited period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Applicable Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority that applies to a party or to the Services. This includes the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the FDA's regulations at 21 C.F.R. Parts 803, 806 and 820.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Products**" means the CT systems listed on Exhibit A, including the service software used to install, calibrate, monitor, diagnose and maintain those systems.

"**Complaint**" means any written, electronic or oral communication alleging deficiencies in the identity, quality, durability, reliability, safety, effectiveness or performance of a Company Product after its release for distribution. This includes any event that may be reportable under 21 C.F.R. Part 803.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**FDA**" means the United States Food and Drug Administration.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, court, tribunal or other instrumentality of any of them, including the FDA.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and records of Recipient that Provider or its personnel create, receive, process or store in performing the Services. This includes service records, installed-base and customer information, device history and service-software logs, and Complaint files.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Service Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Services**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Services.** During the Service Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the following services to Recipient (collectively, the "**Services**"), as further described in Exhibit B:

(a) *Field Service.* Installation, preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, parts replacement and on-site technical support for Company Products installed at customer sites in the United States and Canada, including dispatch of qualified field service engineers;

(b) *Complaint Handling.* Intake, logging, investigation support and trending of Complaints relating to Company Products, and preparation for Recipient's review of draft medical device reports and correction and removal reports relating to Company Products;

(c) *Service-Software Support.* Hosting, maintenance, version control and help-desk support for the service software used with Company Products, including remote diagnostic connectivity; and

(d) *Information Technology.* Access to Provider's enterprise resource planning, customer relationship management, quality management and email systems on which Recipient relied immediately before the Closing, together with related help-desk support, in each case as set forth in Exhibit B.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a professional and workmanlike manner;

(b) in compliance with Applicable Law; and

(c) with substantially the same scope, quality, priority and response times as Provider provided equivalent services to Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date.

Provider shall use personnel with the training and qualifications reasonably necessary to perform the Services.

**2.3 Regulatory Responsibility.** Recipient remains the manufacturer of the Company Products for purposes of Applicable Law. Recipient retains final decision-making authority over all Complaint determinations, medical device reports, corrections, removals and recalls. Provider shall:

(a) forward to Recipient every Complaint it receives, within two Business Days after receipt, or within 24 hours for any Complaint that involves a death or serious injury;

(b) maintain Complaint files and service records in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 820 and Recipient's quality system procedures; and

(c) give Recipient and any Governmental Authority reasonable access to Provider's facilities, personnel and records relating to the Services for audits and inspections.

**2.4 Excluded Services.** Provider is not required to provide any service not described in Section 2.1 or Exhibit B, or to provide any Service at a volume materially greater than Recipient used during the 12 months before the Closing Date, unless the parties agree in writing.

**2.5 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date and that is not described in Exhibit B, Recipient may request it by written notice. Provider shall consider the request in good faith. Any additional service the parties agree to provide shall be documented in a written amendment to Exhibit B, and that amendment shall specify its fee.

**2.6 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any third-party consent or license required to provide the Services. The parties shall share equally the reasonable out-of-pocket cost of obtaining those consents. If Provider cannot obtain a required consent despite those efforts, the parties shall cooperate in good faith to identify and implement a reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Service Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date. Provider shall provide the Services for 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Service Term**"), unless this Agreement or a particular Service terminates earlier under this Section 3.

**3.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Term for any Service once, for up to three months, by giving Provider written notice at least 60 days before the end of the Service Term. During any extension period, the fees for the extended Services increase by 10%.

**3.3 Termination of Individual Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on 30 days' written notice to Provider. The fees for the terminated Service shall be reduced accordingly from the effective date of termination. Provider may not condition any termination on the termination of any other Service. However, if a terminated Service is reasonably necessary for Provider to provide another Service, Provider shall promptly notify Recipient of the dependency.

**3.4 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing it. If the breach is Recipient's failure to pay undisputed fees, the cure period is 15 days. Provider may not suspend or terminate any Complaint-handling Service under this Section 3.4 until Recipient has had a reasonable opportunity, of at least 60 days, to transfer that Service to itself or a third party.

**3.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service:

(a) Recipient shall pay all fees accrued for Services performed through the effective date;

(b) Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Recipient Data relating to the terminated Services, in the format in which Provider maintains it or another commercially reasonable format that Recipient requests; and

(c) Sections 2.3(b), 3.5, 5.4, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12 survive.

## 4. GOVERNANCE AND MIGRATION

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint a service coordinator (a "**Service Coordinator**") to oversee the day-to-day performance of the Services and act as its primary point of contact. Each party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice to the other. The Service Coordinators shall meet at least monthly during the Service Term to review performance, Complaint metrics and migration progress.

**4.2 Migration Plan.** Within 45 days after the Closing Date, the parties shall agree on a written plan for the migration of each Service to Recipient or its designee before the end of the Service Term. Provider shall provide reasonable knowledge-transfer assistance in connection with the migration plan. This includes access to personnel, standard operating procedures, training materials, field service documentation and service-software source code repositories that relate to the Services.

**4.3 Dispute Escalation.** The Service Coordinators shall first attempt to resolve any dispute concerning the Services. If they cannot resolve it within ten Business Days, either party may refer it to a senior executive of each party. If those executives cannot resolve it within a further ten Business Days, either party may pursue its remedies under Section 12.6.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the fees for each Service set forth in Exhibit B. The fees equal Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the Service, without markup. Fully loaded cost means:

- direct labor, benefits and overhead allocated on the same basis Provider used before the Closing; and
- reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses, including field service travel and replacement parts at Provider's cost.

**5.2 Invoices and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**5.3 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving Provider written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, before the payment due date. The parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 4.3. Provider shall continue to perform the Services while the dispute is pending.

**5.4 Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records supporting the fees during the Service Term and for two years afterward. Recipient may audit those records once per calendar year on reasonable advance notice.

**5.5 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay all sales, use, value-added and similar taxes imposed on the Services, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 6. OWNERSHIP OF DATA AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**6.1 Recipient Data.** As between the parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider shall use Recipient Data only to perform the Services. Provider shall not commingle Recipient Data with its own data, except to the extent the shared systems described in Exhibit B require it. In that case, Provider shall logically segregate the Recipient Data.

**6.2 Provider Systems.** Each party retains ownership of its own intellectual property. Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Service Term to use Provider's systems and software to the extent necessary to receive the Services.

**6.3 Work Product.** Any modification, configuration, report or other work product that Provider creates exclusively for Recipient in performing the Services, including any update to the service software for Company Products, is owned by Recipient. Provider assigns all of its right, title and interest in that work product to Recipient.

## 7. DATA SECURITY

**7.1 Safeguards.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Recipient Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration and loss. Those safeguards shall be no less protective than those Provider applies to its own comparable data, and in any event shall meet the requirements of Applicable Law, including laws governing protected health information.

**7.2 Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Recipient in writing within 48 hours after becoming aware of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to or acquisition of Recipient Data. Provider shall cooperate with Recipient in investigating and remediating the incident, and in giving any notices that Applicable Law requires.

**7.3 Business Associate Agreement.** If Provider's performance of the Services involves protected health information within the meaning of HIPAA, the parties shall enter into a business associate agreement on customary terms at the Closing.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement. All Recipient Data is Recipient's Confidential Information.

**8.2 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall hold the Discloser's Confidential Information in confidence and use it only to perform or receive the Services. It may disclose that information only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

**8.3 Exceptions.** Section 8.2 does not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) the Receiving Party lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(c) the Receiving Party independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law or a Governmental Authority, if it gives the Discloser prompt written notice before disclosure where legally permitted.

**8.4 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 8 continue during the Service Term and for five years afterward. For trade secrets and Recipient Data, they continue for as long as the information remains confidential.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim resulting from:

(a) Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or violation of Applicable Law in performing the Services; or

(b) Provider's breach of Section 7 or Section 8.

**9.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim resulting from Recipient's gross negligence, willful misconduct or violation of Applicable Law in receiving the Services.

**9.3 Procedures.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim. The indemnifying party may control the defense of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. However, Recipient shall control the defense and response to any regulatory action of a Governmental Authority that involves Company Products. The indemnifying party may not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement. This exclusion does not apply to:

(a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 9 for third-party claims;

(b) a party's breach of Section 7 or Section 8; or

(c) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**10.2 Cap.** Except for the matters listed in Sections 10.1(a) through 10.1(c), each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

## 11. RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

**11.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties. Provider's personnel remain under Provider's direction and control.

**11.2 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, if it promptly notifies the other party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing any Complaint-handling Service for more than five Business Days, Provider shall give Recipient immediate access to all related Recipient Data so that Recipient can perform that Service itself.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**12.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when received by the addressee if sent by a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) on the date sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent during normal business hours of the recipient, and otherwise on the next Business Day. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth on the signature page, or to any other address a party designates by notice.

**12.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, including its Exhibits, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting it. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

**12.3 Assignment and Delegation.** Provider may assign this Agreement, or delegate the performance of any Services, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Provider. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that holding does not affect any other term of this Agreement or invalidate that term in any other jurisdiction.

**12.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Section 9, this Agreement benefits solely the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Nothing in it confers any legal or equitable right, benefit or remedy on any other Person.

**12.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, City of New York. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**12.7 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that Recipient would suffer irreparable harm if Provider failed to perform the Complaint-handling Services or to deliver Recipient Data in accordance with this Agreement. Recipient is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief to enforce those obligations, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.
