# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**EXECUTION DRAFT**

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of [●], 2026 and is entered into by and between **Glosiv Medtech Group, Inc.** ("**Seller**") and **Daikesk Diagnostics Holdings, Inc.** ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures, sells and services CT, MRI and ultrasound imaging systems and related service software.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale of Shares; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment under Section 2.3(a), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Working Capital**"). The Purchase Price payable at Closing shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which the Estimated Working Capital exceeds or falls short of $24,500,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**") (the "**Estimated Working Capital Adjustment**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Closing Statement**"). Seller may dispute the Closing Statement by written notice within 45 days after receipt. Any dispute not resolved within 30 days after delivery of the dispute notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm jointly selected by Buyer and Seller, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) Within five Business Days after final determination of net working capital, Buyer or Seller, as applicable, shall pay to the other the amount by which the final net working capital differs from the Estimated Working Capital.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the applicable Governmental Authority are treated as paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 7 (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on any other date the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) a copy of the OEM Supply Consent;

(d) the written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer at least five Business Days before the Closing;

(e) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it;

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(g) the certificate required by Section 7.2(e).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the Purchase Price payable under Section 2.2;

(b) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it; and

(c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

(b) *Direct Claims.* An indemnified party shall assert any claim not involving a third party by written notice to the indemnifying party describing the claim in reasonable detail. If it does not respond within that period, it is deemed to have rejected the claim, and the indemnified party may pursue the remedies available to it under this Agreement.

**10.7 Materiality Scrape.** For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article 10, each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in it.

**10.9 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article 10 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement, except for (a) claims arising from Fraud, (b) claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.11, and (c) the procedures in Section 2.3.
