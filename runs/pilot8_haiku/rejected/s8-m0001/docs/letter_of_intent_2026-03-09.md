**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Daikesk Diagnostics Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Orrenvale Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from Glosiv Medtech Group, Inc. ("**Seller**"). We refer to this acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' mutual intent. It does not create any legally binding obligation to consummate the Transaction.

## 1. Transaction Structure

Buyer would purchase all of the Shares from Seller for cash under a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). At the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"), Buyer would own 100% of the outstanding capital stock of the Company. The Shares would be free and clear of all liens and encumbrances.

## 2. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Shares would be $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). It would be payable in cash at Closing.

The Purchase Price assumes that the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of net working capital. It would be subject to a customary working capital adjustment, consisting of:

- an estimate delivered before Closing; and
- a post-Closing true-up, with disputes resolved by an independent accounting firm.

The parties will agree on the target level of net working capital during due diligence.

## 3. Business of the Company

Buyer's proposal is based on its understanding of the Company's business, which is the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of CT, MRI and ultrasound imaging systems and related service software (together, the "**Company Products**"). This understanding includes the installed base of each of these product lines and the related service and complaint-handling infrastructure.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

The Definitive Agreement would contain representations and warranties of Seller customary for a transaction of this type involving a medical device manufacturer. These would include representations regarding:

- organization, authority and capitalization;
- financial statements;
- material contracts;
- intellectual property;
- taxes;
- compliance with laws;
- FDA and other regulatory matters;
- employees and benefit plans; and
- product liability and recalls.

Representations regarding organization, authority, capitalization and taxes would be treated as fundamental representations.

## 5. Indemnification

Seller would indemnify Buyer for:

- breaches of representations, warranties and covenants; and
- all taxes of the Company attributable to periods (or portions of periods) ending on or before the Closing Date.

The following limitations would apply to Seller's indemnification obligations:

(a) *Deductible.* A deductible equal to 0.75% of the Purchase Price would apply to claims for breach of general representations and warranties.

(b) *Cap.* Seller's aggregate liability for breach of general representations and warranties would be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price.

(c) *Uncapped Matters.* The following matters would not be subject to the deductible or the cap: fraud, fundamental representations, pre-closing taxes, and product recall liabilities. The product recall liabilities covered by this clause are liabilities arising from any recall, field correction, market withdrawal or safety action relating to Company Products sold, installed or serviced before Closing. Seller's aggregate liability for breach of fundamental representations would not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Survival.* General representations and warranties would survive for 18 months after Closing. Fundamental representations would survive for six years. Tax representations would survive until 60 days after the applicable statute of limitations expires.

Buyer is evaluating whether to obtain a representations and warranties insurance policy. If it does, the parties will negotiate in good faith any changes to the indemnification terms that the policy requires. The matters described in clause (c) would remain Seller's responsibility.

## 6. Transition Services

At Closing, Seller and the Company would enter into a transition services agreement (the "**TSA**"). Under the TSA, Seller would provide the Company with the services it currently provides to the Company, including:

- field service for the installed base of Company Products;
- adverse-event and complaint handling;
- service-software support; and
- information technology services.

Buyer expects the TSA to have a term of 12 months after Closing, at cost-based pricing. The TSA would be on terms consistent with the Definitive Agreement.

## 7. Seller Restrictive Covenants

For three years after Closing, Seller and its affiliates would agree:

- not to compete with the Company in the design, manufacture, sale or servicing of CT, MRI or ultrasound imaging systems; and
- not to solicit the Company's employees.

The performance of services under the TSA would be excepted from these restrictions.

## 8. Due Diligence

Buyer's proposal is subject to the satisfactory completion of business, financial, legal, tax, regulatory, quality-system and technical due diligence. Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the following:

- the Company's personnel, properties, books and records;
- contracts, including its OEM and supply arrangements and its executive employment agreements;
- FDA submissions and correspondence; and
- complaint files and service records.

## 9. Conditions to Closing

The Closing would be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement and the TSA on terms satisfactory to Buyer and Seller;

(b) expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended;

(c) receipt of material third-party consents identified in due diligence;

(d) the accuracy of representations and warranties and performance of covenants;

(e) the absence of any material adverse effect on the Company; and

(f) approval of the Transaction by the boards of directors of Buyer and Seller.

## 10. Financing

Buyer intends to fund the Purchase Price from cash on hand and available sources of committed financing. The Transaction would not be subject to a financing condition.

## 11. Timing

The parties intend to work toward signing the Definitive Agreement within 75 days after the date of this Letter. They intend to close as promptly as practicable after the closing conditions are satisfied.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 12 through 17 of this Letter (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the parties.

## 13. Exclusivity

For 75 days after the date of this Letter (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and their respective representatives not to, directly or indirectly:

(a) solicit, initiate or knowingly encourage any proposal from any person other than Buyer for the acquisition of the Company, any of its capital stock or any material portion of its assets; or

(b) engage in discussions or negotiations with, or furnish information to, any such person regarding such a proposal.

Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any such proposal it receives during the Exclusivity Period. The parties may extend the Exclusivity Period by mutual written agreement.

## 14. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged under it, are subject to the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between Buyer and Seller, which remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.

## 15. Expenses

Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses, including fees of legal, financial and accounting advisors, in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is consummated.

## 16. Termination

This Letter terminates on the earliest of:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(b) written agreement of the parties; and

(c) expiration of the Exclusivity Period, unless extended.

Sections 14, 15, 16 and 17 survive termination.

## 17. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to its conflict of law rules. This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
