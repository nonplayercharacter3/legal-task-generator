## 2. Phase 2 Election

Under Section 2.1 of the Side Letter, Licensee gives notice that it elects not to proceed with Phase 2. This notice is Licensee's Phase 2 Election. In accordance with Section 2.2 of the Side Letter, the election applies to all of the Phase 2 Modules and all of the Phase 2 Services:

(a) Threadline Wholesale Order Management;

(b) Threadline Retail POS Connect; and

(c) Threadline E-Commerce Merchandising.

## 3. Consequences of the Election

In accordance with Section 3 of the Side Letter, Licensee requests that Licensor confirm the following:

(a) Licensor will not invoice the Phase 2 kickoff fee of $95,000 or the Phase 2 Acceptance fee of $215,000 under Section 4.4 of the SOW.

(b) The Annual License Fee is reduced by $285,000 per year, to $855,000 per year. Licensor issued its invoice of March 2, 2026 at the full Annual License Fee of $1,140,000. Licensee therefore requests a credit note under Section 3.2(a) of the Side Letter. The credit should be pro-rated from the date of this election to the next anniversary of the Phase 1 Acceptance Date.

(c) Licensor will not perform the Phase 2 Services, and the Phase 2 kickoff milestone in Section 3.4 of the SOW will not apply.

(d) The Phase 1 Modules, named-user seats, environments and Service Level Schedule continue unchanged, in accordance with Sections 3.4 and 3.5 of the Side Letter.

## 4. Continuing Relationship

This letter does not affect Phase 1 or the parties' other obligations under the Agreement. Licensee values its relationship with Licensor and expects to continue working closely with Licensor's team on the Phase 1 Modules. Licensee may later revisit the Phase 2 Modules through change control under Section 4 of the Side Letter.
