# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Sairan Threadline Product Lifecycle Management Platform**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 between **Pakairt Outdoor Apparel, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Sairan Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Processor**").

It forms part of the Software License Agreement between Processor, as Licensor, and Customer, as Licensee, dated January 12, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** In this DPA:

"**Consumer Data**" means Personal Data relating to Customer's retail and e-commerce customers. It includes names, contact details, purchase history, product reviews and preferences, and Loyalty Program Data.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include:

(a) the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and its regulations (the "**CCPA**");

(b) other U.S. state consumer privacy laws; and

(c) where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 and the UK GDPR.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

"**Loyalty Program Data**" means Personal Data collected through Customer's loyalty, rewards or membership programs. It includes member identifiers, points balances, tier status and redemption history.

"**Personal Data**" means any information in Customer Data that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person, or that is "personal information" or "personal data" under Data Protection Laws.

"**Process**" or "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, alteration and erasure.

"**Security Incident**" means any breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Personal Data that Processor or its Sub-processors Process.

"**Sub-processor**" means any third party that Processor engages to Process Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

**1.2** References to Sections are to sections of this DPA unless stated otherwise.

---

## 2. Roles and Scope

**2.1 Roles.** Customer is the controller, or "business" under the CCPA, of the Personal Data. Processor is a processor, or "service provider" under the CCPA, that Processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

**2.2 Scope.** This DPA applies to all Personal Data that Processor Processes in providing the Licensed Software and performing the Services under the Statement of Work. Annex 1 describes the Processing.

**2.3 Customer Responsibilities.** Customer is responsible for:

(a) the lawfulness of its Processing instructions;

(b) providing all notices to Data Subjects that Data Protection Laws require; and

(c) obtaining all consents and authorizations required for Processor to Process Personal Data under the Agreement, including Consumer Data and Loyalty Program Data.

---

## 3. Processing Instructions

**3.1 Instructions.** Processor shall Process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, the Statement of Work, this DPA and Customer's configuration and use of the Licensed Software are Customer's complete instructions as at the date of this DPA. Customer may give additional written instructions consistent with the Agreement.

**3.2 Unlawful Instructions.** Processor shall promptly inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws. Processor may suspend Processing under that instruction until Customer confirms or modifies it.

**3.3 CCPA Restrictions.** Processor shall not:

(a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined in the CCPA;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement;

(c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties; or

(d) combine Personal Data with personal information that Processor receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as the CCPA permits.

Processor certifies that it understands these restrictions and will comply with them.

---

## 4. Confidentiality and Personnel

**4.1** Processor shall ensure that all personnel authorized to Process Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations or are under an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality.

**4.2** Processor shall limit access to Personal Data to personnel who need that access to perform the Agreement.

---

## 5. Security

**5.1 Measures.** Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data. These measures are set out in Annex 2 and include:

(a) encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest;

(b) role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for administrative access;

(c) logging and monitoring of access to production systems;

(d) regular vulnerability scanning and annual third-party penetration testing; and

(e) business continuity and backup procedures.

**5.2 Updates.** Processor may update the measures in Annex 2 from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall level of protection for Personal Data.

**5.3 Certification.** Processor shall maintain a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Licensed Software and shall provide a copy to Customer on request, not more than once in any twelve (12) month period.

---

## 6. Sub-processors

**6.1 Authorization.** Customer gives general authorization for Processor to engage Sub-processors. The Sub-processors engaged as at the date of this DPA are listed in Annex 3.

**6.2 Changes.** Processor shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any new or replacement Sub-processor. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within fifteen (15) days after that notice. The parties shall then discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected module by written notice and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for that module.

**6.3 Flow-down.** Processor shall impose on each Sub-processor, by written contract, data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA. Processor remains responsible for each Sub-processor's performance as provided in Section 15.1 of the Agreement.

---

## 7. Security Incidents

**7.1 Notification.** Processor shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

**7.2 Information.** The notice shall describe, so far as then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to address it.

Processor shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**7.3 Cooperation.** Processor shall take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the Security Incident. It shall cooperate reasonably with Customer's investigation and with any notifications that Customer is required to make to regulators or Data Subjects.

---

## 8. Data Subject Requests, Assistance and Audit

**8.1 Requests.** If Processor receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws, it shall promptly forward the request to Customer and shall not respond except on Customer's instructions.

**8.2 Assistance.** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in:

(a) responding to Data Subject requests;

(b) carrying out data protection assessments; and

(c) consulting with regulators.

**8.3 Audit.** Processor shall make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA. This includes its most recent SOC 2 Type II report. If that information is not sufficient to demonstrate compliance, Customer may, once in any twelve (12) month period and on at least thirty (30) days' notice, conduct an audit by itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality. The audit shall take place during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably disrupt Processor's operations. Customer shall bear its own audit costs.

---

## 9. Indemnification

Customer shall indemnify Processor and its Affiliates against all losses, damages, regulatory fines and penalties, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of:

(a) any Processing carried out in accordance with Customer's instructions; or

(b) Customer's failure to provide notices to, or obtain consents from, Data Subjects as required by Data Protection Laws, including in connection with Consumer Data and Loyalty Program Data.

The losses covered by this Section 9 include those arising from any claim or proceeding brought by a regulator, a Data Subject or a class of Data Subjects. This indemnity is subject to Section 10 of the Agreement. The procedure in Section 9.5 of the Agreement applies to any claim under this Section 9.

---

## 10. International Transfers

**10.1** Processor shall host and Process Personal Data in the United States in accordance with Section 3.2 of the Agreement. Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

**10.2** If Personal Data subject to the EU GDPR or UK GDPR is transferred to Processor, the parties shall enter into the applicable standard contractual clauses or UK international data transfer addendum. Customer shall act as data exporter and Processor as data importer.

---

## 11. Term, Return and Deletion

**11.1 Term.** This DPA continues for as long as Processor Processes Personal Data under the Agreement.

**11.2 Return and Deletion.** On termination or expiry of the Agreement, Processor shall return and delete Personal Data in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Agreement. On request, Processor shall certify the deletion in writing.

**11.3 Retained Data.** To the extent the law requires Processor to retain any Personal Data, Processor shall continue to protect it in accordance with this DPA and shall Process it only for the purpose of that retention.

---

## 12. General

**12.1** This DPA is governed by the law that governs the Agreement. Disputes are resolved under Section 16 of the Agreement.

**12.2** Any amendment to this DPA must be in writing and signed by both parties.

**12.3** This DPA may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

**Annex 1 – Description of Processing**

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Hosting, configuration, support and implementation of the Licensed Software |
| Duration | The Term of the Agreement and any return period under Section 5.3 of the Agreement |
| Nature and purpose | Storage, retrieval, integration, migration and display of Personal Data to deliver the modules described in the Statement of Work |
| Categories of Data Subjects | Customer employees and contractors (Authorized Users); supplier and factory contacts; wholesale account contacts; retail and e-commerce customers; loyalty program members |
| Categories of Personal Data | Names, business and personal contact details, user credentials and activity logs, supplier contact data, wholesale buyer data, purchase history, sell-through data linked to individuals, Loyalty Program Data |
| Sensitive data | None intended |

**Annex 2 – Technical and Organizational Measures**

1. Encryption: AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.
2. Access control: role-based access, least-privilege provisioning, multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts, and quarterly access reviews.
3. Monitoring: centralized logging of production access, with log retention of at least twelve (12) months.
4. Testing: continuous vulnerability scanning and an annual independent penetration test.
5. Resilience: daily encrypted backups, geographically separate backup storage within the United States, and an annually tested disaster recovery plan.
6. Personnel: background checks where lawful, and annual security and privacy training.
