## 6. Data Migration

6.1 Ms. Montoya presented the draft data migration approach under Section 3.2(d) of the SOW. Active style, material, supplier and BOM records for the Fall/Winter 2026 and Spring/Summer 2027 seasons will be migrated.

6.2 Mr. Oyelaran confirmed that Licensee will provide legacy data extracts in the agreed formats under Section 6.4(b) of the SOW. Delivery will be in two batches: Fall/Winter 2026 by February 4, 2026 and Spring/Summer 2027 by February 9, 2026.

6.3 Mr. Arakawa asked how supplier contact data would be protected during migration. Ms. Montoya confirmed that migration files will be transferred over encrypted channels and stored in the United States, in accordance with Annex 2 to the DPA.
