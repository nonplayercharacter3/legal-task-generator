During negotiation of the Agreement, Licensee asked for flexibility on whether to proceed with the Phase 2 Modules. Licensee wanted to make that decision once it had evaluated the Phase 1 Modules in production. Licensor has agreed to give Licensee that flexibility on the terms set out below.

---

## 1. Phase 2 Modules

**1.1** The Phase 2 Modules are the Wholesale and Retail Modules described in Section 2.2 of the SOW:

(a) Threadline Wholesale Order Management;

(b) Threadline Retail POS Connect; and

(c) Threadline E-Commerce Merchandising.

**1.2** The Phase 2 Services are the services described in Section 3.3 of the SOW.

**1.3** Unless Licensee makes an election under Section 2, the Phase 2 Modules and the Phase 2 Services shall proceed in accordance with the SOW.

---

## 2. Election Not to Proceed with Phase 2

**2.1 Election.** Licensee may elect not to proceed with the Phase 2 Modules. To do so, Licensee must give Licensor written notice of its election within forty-five (45) days after the Phase 1 Acceptance Date (the "**Phase 2 Election**"). The notice must be given in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement.

**2.2 Scope.** A Phase 2 Election applies to all of the Phase 2 Modules and all of the Phase 2 Services. Licensee may not elect to proceed with some Phase 2 Modules only, except by agreement through change control under Section 7 of the SOW.

**2.3 Lapse.** If Licensee does not give notice under Section 2.1, the right to make a Phase 2 Election lapses. The Phase 2 Modules, the Phase 2 Services and the related fees then remain in effect as set out in the SOW.

---

## 3. Effect of a Phase 2 Election

If Licensee makes a valid Phase 2 Election, the following applies from the date Licensor receives the notice:

**3.1 Implementation Fees.** Licensee shall not be liable for either of the following Phase 2 implementation fees in Section 4.4 of the SOW, and Licensor shall not invoice them:

(a) the Phase 2 kickoff fee of $95,000; and

(b) the Phase 2 Acceptance fee of $215,000.

**3.2 Annual License Fee.** The Annual License Fee shall be reduced by $285,000 per year, giving a reduced Annual License Fee of $855,000 per year.

(a) If Licensor has already invoiced the Annual License Fee for the current year at the full rate, it shall issue a credit note for the difference. The credit shall be pro-rated for the period from the date of the Phase 2 Election to the next anniversary of the Phase 1 Acceptance Date.

(b) The reduced Annual License Fee remains subject to adjustment under Section 4.3 of the SOW.

**3.3 Services.** Licensor shall have no obligation to perform the Phase 2 Services. The Phase 2 kickoff milestone in Section 3.4 of the SOW shall not apply.

**3.4 Licensed Modules.** The licenses to the Phase 2 Modules under Section 2.2 of the SOW shall not take effect. The licenses to the Phase 1 Modules continue for the Order Term in accordance with the SOW.

**3.5 No Other Change.** The named-user seats in Section 2.3 of the SOW, the environments in Section 2.4 of the SOW and the Service Level Schedule continue to apply to the Phase 1 Modules without change.

---

## 4. Later Addition of Phase 2 Modules

If Licensee makes a Phase 2 Election and later wishes to license any of the Phase 2 Modules, it may ask to do so by change request under Section 7 of the SOW. Licensor shall offer the Phase 2 Modules at fees no higher than those that would have applied under the SOW had no Phase 2 Election been made, adjusted under Section 4.3 of the SOW. This offer is available during the first twenty-four (24) months of the Order Term.
