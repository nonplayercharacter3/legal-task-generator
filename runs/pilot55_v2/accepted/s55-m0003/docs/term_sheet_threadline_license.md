# TERM SHEET

**Sairan Threadline Product Lifecycle Management Platform: License and Implementation**

**Date:** November 21, 2025

**Status:** Non-binding indicative terms (except Section 15)

This term sheet sets out the principal terms on which **Sairan Systems, LLC** ("**Sairan**" or "**Licensor**") proposes to license its Threadline product lifecycle management platform to **Pakairt Outdoor Apparel, Inc.** ("**Pakairt**" or "**Licensee**"), and to implement it. It is a basis for negotiating definitive agreements and is not a complete statement of terms.

---

## 1. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Sairan Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 400 Market Street, Suite 1200, Wilmington, DE 19801 |
| Licensee | Pakairt Outdoor Apparel, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 2250 Ridgeline Avenue, Portland, OR 97209 |

## 2. Transaction Overview

Sairan will grant Pakairt a license to the Sairan Threadline platform. Pakairt will use it to design, develop and source its outerwear, footwear and gear lines. Sairan will also implement the platform in two phases. Sairan will host the platform in the United States and will provide support and maintenance.

## 3. Definitive Documents

The parties expect to document the transaction in the following:

(a) a Software License Agreement;

(b) a Statement of Work for the Threadline implementation;

(c) a Data Processing Addendum;

(d) a Service Level Schedule;

(e) a Software Quality Agreement; and

(f) a side letter covering Pakairt's flexibility on the Phase 2 Modules (see Section 6).

## 4. Licensed Modules

**4.1 Phase 1.** Phase 1 covers Threadline Design, Threadline Tech Pack, Threadline Sourcing and Threadline Demand Planning.

**4.2 Phase 2.** Phase 2 covers the wholesale and retail modules: Threadline Wholesale Order Management, Threadline Retail POS Connect and Threadline E-Commerce Merchandising.

**4.3 Named Users.** Up to 350 named-user seats across all modules. Additional seats are available in blocks of 25 at Sairan's then-current list price.

## 5. Fees

**5.1 Annual License Fee.** $1,140,000 per year. This covers all Phase 1 and Phase 2 modules, the named-user seats, hosting, and support and maintenance. The fee is invoiced annually in advance from Phase 1 acceptance.

**5.2 Fee Adjustment.** Annual adjustment from each anniversary of Phase 1 acceptance, capped at the lesser of CPI-U and 3%. At least 60 days' prior notice is required.

**5.3 Implementation Fees.** Fixed fee of $950,000 in total, payable on milestones as follows:

| Phase | Amount |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | $640,000 |
| Phase 2 | $310,000 |

**5.4 Payment Terms.** Payment is due 30 days after the date of a correct invoice. Good-faith disputes are permitted.

## 6. Phase 2 Flexibility

Pakairt may elect not to proceed with the Phase 2 Modules once it has evaluated Phase 1 in production. The election must be made by written notice within 45 days after Phase 1 acceptance. If Pakairt makes the election:

(a) the Phase 2 implementation fees fall away; and

(b) the Annual License Fee is reduced by $285,000 per year.

These terms will be documented in a side letter.

## 7. Term and Renewal

**7.1 Initial Term.** 60 months from Phase 1 acceptance.

**7.2 Renewal.** After the initial term, the license will continue for successive 12-month renewal terms, unless either party gives notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term. The notice period will be agreed in the definitive documents.

**7.3 Termination.** Either party may terminate for uncured material breach (30-day cure period) or for insolvency. Pakairt will also have a termination right for chronic service level failure, as set out in the Service Level Schedule.

## 8. Exclusivity

During the term, Pakairt will use Threadline as its exclusive solution for PLM software for apparel and footwear design only. Pakairt remains free to select and use other systems for all other business functions.

## 9. Service Levels and Quality

**9.1 Availability and Support.** Sairan will commit to monthly availability, support response times and service credits. These commitments will be set out in the Service Level Schedule.

**9.2 Quality.** Testing, release management and defect severity classifications will be set out in the Software Quality Agreement.

## 10. Data and Confidentiality

**10.1 Ownership.** Pakairt owns all Customer Data. This includes unreleased designs, tech packs, line plans and supplier pricing.

**10.2 Data Return.** Customer Data will be available for export for 60 days after termination or expiry.

**10.3 Personal Data.** Personal data will be handled under a Data Processing Addendum. The addendum will address the CCPA and other applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

**10.4 Security.** Sairan will maintain a SOC 2 Type II report and will notify Pakairt of any security incident within 48 hours.

## 11. Liability

**11.1 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.2 Exclusions and Carve-outs.** Indirect and consequential damages are mutually excluded. Customary carve-outs to the cap and to this exclusion will be agreed in the definitive documents.

## 12. Intellectual Property Indemnity

Sairan will defend and indemnify Pakairt against third-party claims that the Threadline platform infringes intellectual property rights. Customary exclusions and mitigation rights will apply.

## 13. Insurance

Sairan will maintain the following coverage:

| Coverage | Limit |
|---|---|
| Commercial general liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| Technology errors and omissions | $5,000,000 per claim |
| Cyber liability | $5,000,000 per claim |

## 14. Governing Law

State of Delaware. The courts located in Wilmington, Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 15. Binding Provisions; Non-Binding Effect

**15.1 Non-Binding Effect.** Except for this Section 15, this term sheet is not binding. No obligation to enter into any transaction arises unless and until definitive agreements are signed.

**15.2 Binding Provisions.** The following provisions of this Section 15 are binding:

(a) **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of this term sheet are confidential.

(b) **Costs.** Each party bears its own costs.

(c) **Governing Law.** This Section 15 is governed by Delaware law.
