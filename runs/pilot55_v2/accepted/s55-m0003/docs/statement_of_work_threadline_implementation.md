# STATEMENT OF WORK

**Sairan Threadline Implementation**

**SOW No. 001**

This Statement of Work (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 between **Sairan Systems, LLC** ("**Licensor**") and **Pakairt Outdoor Apparel, Inc.** ("**Licensee**"). It is made under the Software License Agreement between the parties dated January 12, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

This SOW is governed by the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. Scope and Definitions

**1.1 Purpose.** This SOW describes:

(a) the modules of the Licensed Software that Licensee is licensing;

(b) the implementation, configuration, data-migration and training services that Licensor will perform (the "**Services**");

(c) the acceptance procedures; and

(d) the fees, the Order Term and related commercial terms.

**1.2 Covered Functions.** "**Covered Functions**" means the business functions that the Licensed Software supports for Licensee and its Affiliates under this SOW. Those functions are:

(a) product lifecycle management, including line planning, product design and development, tech pack and bill-of-materials management, sampling, costing and supplier collaboration;

(b) demand planning;

(c) wholesale order management; and

(d) retail point-of-sale and e-commerce merchandising.

**1.3 Other Definitions.** In this SOW:

"**Acceptance**" means Licensee's acceptance of a Phase under Section 5. "**Accepted**" has a corresponding meaning.

"**Acceptance Certificate**" means a certificate substantially in the form of Annex A, signed by Licensee's Project Sponsor.

"**Acceptance Criteria**" means, for each Phase, the functional and performance criteria set out in Section 5.2 and in the Test Plan.

"**Go-Live**" means the first use of the Phase 1 Modules by Licensee in a production environment for live business operations.

"**Phase**" means Phase 1 or Phase 2, as described in Section 3.

"**Phase 1 Acceptance Date**" means the date on which Phase 1 is Accepted under Section 5.

"**Phase 1 Modules**" and "**Phase 2 Modules**" have the meanings given in Section 2.

"**Test Plan**" means the user acceptance test plan agreed by the parties under Section 3.2(c).

## 2. Licensed Modules and Users

**2.1 Phase 1 Modules.** The following modules form the "**Phase 1 Modules**":

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Threadline Design | Line planning, style creation, colorway and size-range management, 2D/3D asset library |
| Threadline Tech Pack | Specifications, construction details, bills of materials, grading and measurement charts |
| Threadline Sourcing | Supplier portal, RFQ and costing, sample tracking, materials library, compliance documentation |
| Threadline Demand Planning | Assortment planning, seasonal forecasting and buy-plan generation |

**2.2 Phase 2 Modules.** The following modules form the Wholesale and Retail Modules (the "**Phase 2 Modules**"):

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Threadline Wholesale Order Management | Wholesale account management, line sheets, pre-book and at-once order capture, allocation |
| Threadline Retail POS Connect | Product, pricing and promotion feeds to Licensee's retail point-of-sale environment, store-level sell-through data |
| Threadline E-Commerce Merchandising | Product content syndication, digital assortment and merchandising tools for Licensee's webstore |

**2.3 Named Users.** Licensee may designate up to three hundred fifty (350) named-user seats for Authorized Users across all modules. Licensee may buy additional seats in blocks of twenty-five (25) at Licensor's then-current list price, pro-rated to the next anniversary of the Phase 1 Acceptance Date.

**2.4 Environments.** Licensor shall provide one production environment and two non-production environments, one for development and one for testing and training. Both non-production environments shall be maintained during the Order Term.

## 3. Services and Timeline

**3.1 Phases.** Licensor shall perform the Services in two Phases:

(a) **Phase 1** covers implementation of the Phase 1 Modules.

(b) **Phase 2** covers implementation of the Phase 2 Modules. Phase 2 starts after the Phase 1 Acceptance Date.

**3.2 Phase 1 Services.** For Phase 1, Licensor shall:

(a) run discovery workshops with Licensee's design, product development, sourcing and planning teams. Licensor shall then deliver a solution design document;

(b) configure the Phase 1 Modules in accordance with the approved solution design. This includes workflows, approval routings, attribute libraries and user roles;

(c) prepare and agree a Test Plan with Licensee no later than ten (10) business days before user acceptance testing begins;

(d) migrate active style, material, supplier and BOM records for the Fall/Winter 2026 and Spring/Summer 2027 seasons from Licensee's legacy systems, in accordance with the data migration mapping agreed with Licensee;

(e) build integrations between the Phase 1 Modules and Licensee's ERP system and product information management system, as specified in the solution design document;

(f) deliver train-the-trainer sessions for up to thirty (30) Licensee super users, plus role-based training materials; and

(g) provide on-site and remote hypercare support for thirty (30) days after Go-Live.

**3.3 Phase 2 Services.** For Phase 2, Licensor shall:

(a) run discovery workshops with Licensee's wholesale, retail and digital teams;

(b) configure the Phase 2 Modules;

(c) build integrations to Licensee's retail point-of-sale platform and e-commerce platform;

(d) migrate active wholesale account and open-order data; and

(e) deliver training and hypercare on the same basis as Sections 3.2(f) and 3.2(g).

**3.4 Indicative Timeline.**

| Milestone | Target Date |
|---|---|
| Project kickoff | January 26, 2026 |
| Solution design document approved | February 6, 2026 |
| Phase 1 configuration complete | February 13, 2026 |
| Phase 1 user acceptance testing | February 16 – February 27, 2026 |
| Phase 1 Go-Live | Upon Phase 1 Acceptance |
| Phase 2 kickoff | Within thirty (30) days after the Phase 1 Acceptance Date |
| Phase 2 Go-Live | Target: six (6) months after Phase 2 kickoff |

Target dates are estimates. Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet them. The parties shall agree any re-planning through change control under Section 7.

**3.5 Licensee Deliverables.** The following are "Licensee deliverables" for the purposes of Section 6.3 of the Agreement:

(a) the solution design document;

(b) the data migration mappings;

(c) the Test Plan; and

(d) the Licensee-specific training materials.

Each is subject to Licensor's retained rights in the Licensed Software.

## 5. Acceptance

**5.1 Testing.** On completion of configuration for a Phase, Licensor shall notify Licensee that the Phase is ready for user acceptance testing. Licensee shall then have ten (10) business days to test the Phase against the Acceptance Criteria (the "**Testing Period**").

**5.2 Acceptance Criteria.** A Phase meets the Acceptance Criteria when all three of the following are true:

(a) all test scripts in the Test Plan for that Phase have been executed;

(b) there are no open Severity 1 or Severity 2 defects, as defined in the Software Quality Agreement; and

(c) the agreed integrations for that Phase have passed end-to-end testing with production-representative data.

**5.3 Acceptance or Rejection.** Before the end of the Testing Period, Licensee shall do one of the following:

(a) sign and deliver an Acceptance Certificate; or

(b) give Licensor written notice of rejection, identifying in reasonable detail each failure to meet the Acceptance Criteria.

**5.4 Re-testing.** If Licensee rejects a Phase, Licensor shall correct the identified failures and resubmit the Phase within ten (10) business days. A further Testing Period then applies. If a Phase fails to meet the Acceptance Criteria after the third submission, Licensee may treat the failure as a material breach for the purposes of Section 12.3(a) of the Agreement. The cure period in that Section shall be treated as having expired.

**5.5 Deemed Acceptance.** A Phase is Accepted on the earlier of:

(a) the date Licensee signs the Acceptance Certificate; and

(b) the date Licensee first uses the Phase in a production environment for live business operations, other than for testing or hypercare.
