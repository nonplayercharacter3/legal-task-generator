# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**(Third-Party Technical Ship Management)**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 16, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Zilain Feeder Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"); and

**Geilaind Marine Services LLC**, a limited liability company ("**Geilaind**").

Customer and Provider (as defined below) are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer owns, operates and charters container feeder vessels.

B. Geilaind and its Affiliates provide third-party technical ship management, crewing and related marine services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to provide technical management services for vessels identified in one or more Statements of Work. Provider is willing to provide those services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a person, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Applicable Law**" means all laws, statutes, regulations, conventions and binding orders applicable to a Party, a Covered Vessel or the Services. This includes SOLAS, MARPOL, STCW, the ISM Code, the ISPS Code, the Maritime Labour Convention 2006, the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 ("**OPA 90**") and the regulations of the United States Coast Guard and each relevant Flag State.

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

"**Covered Vessels**" means, for each SOW, the vessels identified as Covered Vessels in that SOW. This includes vessels added to that SOW by amendment and excludes vessels removed under its terms.

"**Environmental Losses**" has the meaning given in the Quality Agreement.

"**Fees**" means the management fees and other amounts payable to Provider under an SOW.

"**Flag State**" means the state whose flag a Covered Vessel flies.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any national, state, local or supranational government, regulatory or administrative authority, port state control authority, Flag State administration or court.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Management Accounts**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Parent Guaranty**" means the guaranty dated as of the Effective Date given by Geilaind Holdings plc in favor of Customer.

"**Pollution Incident**" has the meaning given in the Quality Agreement.

"**Provider**" means Geilaind and any Affiliate of Geilaind that executes an SOW, in each case as the Provider under that SOW.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between Customer and Geilaind that sets out the safety, environmental, technical and performance standards for the Services, as amended from time to time.

"**Safety Management System**" or "**SMS**" means the safety management system Provider maintains under the ISM Code for the Covered Vessels.

"**Services**" means the technical management services described in an SOW, together with any services reasonably incidental to them.

"**SOW**" or "**Statement of Work**" means a statement of work entered into under Section 2.2.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 14.1.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. References to a convention, code or statute include it as amended and in force from time to time.

## 2. FRAMEWORK

2.1 **Scope.** This Agreement sets the terms on which Provider will perform Services for Customer under SOWs. Neither Party is obliged to enter into any SOW. Customer has no obligation to purchase Services except as set out in an executed SOW.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** Each SOW shall be in writing and executed by Customer and Geilaind or an Affiliate of Geilaind. Each SOW shall identify the Covered Vessels, the Services, the Fees, the SOW term and any service-specific terms. Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement. Each SOW constitutes a separate contract between Customer and the Provider entity that executes it. Only that Provider entity is responsible for the obligations under that SOW.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict or inconsistency among the following documents, they shall prevail in this order: (a) this Agreement; (b) the applicable SOW; and (c) the Quality Agreement. An SOW prevails over this Agreement only where the SOW expressly identifies the section of this Agreement that it modifies.

2.4 **Exclusivity.** During the term of each SOW, Customer shall engage Provider exclusively for the technical management of the Covered Vessels under that SOW. Except as stated in this Section 2.4, Customer may engage any other person to provide technical management, crewing, commercial management or other services for any vessel.

2.5 **Parent Guaranty.** On or before the Effective Date, Geilaind shall deliver the Parent Guaranty, duly executed by Geilaind Holdings plc, to Customer.

## 3. SERVICES AND STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

3.1 **Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services as agent for and on behalf of Customer or the relevant registered owner of each Covered Vessel. Provider shall act in accordance with:

(a) this Agreement, the applicable SOW and the Quality Agreement;

(b) Applicable Law and the requirements of each Covered Vessel's classification society; and

(c) sound ship management practice, with the degree of skill, care and diligence expected of a competent and experienced third-party technical manager of container feeder vessels.

3.2 **ISM and ISPS Responsibility.** For each Covered Vessel, Provider shall assume the responsibilities of "Company" under the ISM Code and of the company under the ISPS Code. Provider shall hold the required Document of Compliance and maintain the Safety Management System. Provider shall designate a Designated Person Ashore and a Company Security Officer.

3.3 **Technical Management.** Unless an SOW provides otherwise, the Services include:

(a) maintaining each Covered Vessel in class, with all statutory and class certificates valid;

(b) arranging and supervising planned maintenance, repairs and surveys;

(c) procuring stores, spares and lubricants;

(d) preparing operating budgets; and

(e) handling technical incidents and casualty response, in coordination with Customer.

3.4 **Reporting.** Provider shall deliver the reports set out in the applicable SOW and the Quality Agreement. Provider shall notify Customer promptly, and in any event within the time required by the Quality Agreement, of any casualty, detention, Pollution Incident, port state control deficiency or other event that materially affects a Covered Vessel.

3.5 **Change Requests.** Either Party may request changes to the Services under an SOW. No change is binding unless it is set out in a written amendment to the SOW signed by both Parties.

## 4. PERSONNEL AND CREW

4.1 **Shore Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified and experienced shore personnel to perform the Services. This includes a superintendent for each Covered Vessel. Provider may replace assigned personnel on reasonable notice to Customer. The replacement must have equivalent qualifications.

4.2 **Crew.** Where an SOW includes crewing services, Provider shall recruit, engage and manage officers and ratings who:

(a) hold valid certificates under STCW and the Flag State's requirements;

(b) meet the medical and experience standards set out in the Quality Agreement; and

(c) are employed on terms that comply with the Maritime Labour Convention 2006 and any applicable collective bargaining agreement.

4.3 **Removal.** Customer may require Provider, on reasonable grounds, to remove any member of shore personnel or crew from the Services. Provider shall do so promptly and at its own cost.

4.4 **Drug and Alcohol Policy.** Provider shall maintain and enforce a drug and alcohol policy for crew. The policy shall be no less stringent than the requirements of the Quality Agreement and Applicable Law.

## 5. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 **Cooperation.** Customer shall:

(a) give Provider timely access to the Covered Vessels and to relevant vessel documentation;

(b) give Provider timely instructions on commercial employment and trading; and

(c) provide information reasonably requested by Provider to perform the Services.

5.2 **Employment of Vessels.** Customer remains responsible for the commercial employment of the Covered Vessels. Customer shall not instruct Provider to take any action that would breach Applicable Law or compromise the safety of a Covered Vessel or its crew.

5.3 **Funding.** Customer shall fund the Management Accounts in accordance with Section 7.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the Fees set out in each SOW.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Unless an SOW provides otherwise, Provider shall invoice the management fee monthly in advance. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

6.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith. To do so, Customer must give Provider written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work to resolve the dispute promptly. Customer shall pay any amount found to be due within ten (10) Business Days after resolution.

6.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at one percent (1%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower. Interest runs from the due date until the date of payment.

6.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes, other than taxes on Provider's net income, employment taxes on Provider's personnel and taxes on crew wages for which Provider is responsible by law.

6.6 **Audit.** Customer may, on reasonable notice and no more than once per calendar year, audit Provider's records relating to the Fees and the Management Accounts. Provider shall promptly refund any overcharge revealed by an audit. If an overcharge exceeds five percent (5%) of the amounts audited, Provider shall also bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

## 7. VESSEL FUNDS AND ACCOUNTS

7.1 **Management Accounts.** For each SOW, Provider shall open and maintain a segregated bank account in Customer's name, or in the registered owner's name, for vessel operating expenses (the "**Management Accounts**"). Provider shall not commingle funds in the Management Accounts with its own funds or with the funds of any other client.

7.2 **Budgets.** Provider shall prepare an annual operating budget for each Covered Vessel for Customer's approval. Unless Customer approves in advance, Provider shall not incur expenditure that exceeds the approved budget by more than the threshold specified in the SOW. Emergency expenditure necessary to protect life, the vessel or the environment is exempt from this approval requirement. Provider shall notify Customer of any emergency expenditure as soon as practicable.

7.3 **Funding.** Customer shall fund the Management Accounts monthly in advance, in the amounts shown in the approved budget.

7.4 **Accounting.** Provider shall deliver monthly accounts for each Covered Vessel within twenty (20) days after the end of each month. The accounts shall be supported by invoices and other documentation reasonably requested by Customer.

7.5 **Discounts and Commissions.** Provider shall credit to the Management Accounts all discounts, rebates and commissions received from suppliers in connection with the Covered Vessels.

## 8. INSURANCE

8.1 **Vessel Insurances.** Unless an SOW provides otherwise, Customer shall arrange and maintain for each Covered Vessel, at its own cost:

(a) hull and machinery insurance;

(b) protection and indemnity insurance with a member of the International Group of P&I Clubs, including pollution cover; and

(c) any certificates of financial responsibility required under OPA 90 and Applicable Law.

Provider shall be named as a co-assured or joint member, as applicable, without liability for premiums or calls.

8.2 **Provider Insurances.** During the Term, Provider shall maintain with reputable insurers:

(a) ship managers' errors and omissions liability insurance of not less than US$10,000,000 per claim;

(b) commercial general liability insurance; and

(c) employers' liability and workers' compensation insurance as required by Applicable Law.

On request, Provider shall provide certificates of insurance to Customer.

## 9. COMPLIANCE

9.1 **Applicable Law.** Each Party shall comply with Applicable Law in performing this Agreement.

9.2 **Environmental Compliance.** Provider shall manage each Covered Vessel in compliance with MARPOL, OPA 90, the U.S. Clean Water Act and Vessel General Permit requirements, and applicable ballast water management rules. Provider shall maintain each Covered Vessel's Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan and Vessel Response Plan, as applicable. Provider shall perform its obligations in response to any Pollution Incident in accordance with those plans and the Quality Agreement.

9.3 **Anti-Corruption and Sanctions.** Neither Party shall make, offer or accept any improper payment in connection with this Agreement. Provider shall not cause any Covered Vessel to call at any port, or to trade with any person, in breach of sanctions laws administered by the United States, the United Nations, the European Union or the United Kingdom.

9.4 **Regulatory Inspections.** Provider shall cooperate with, and prepare each Covered Vessel for, port state control, Flag State, class and vetting inspections. Provider shall rectify deficiencies within the time required.

## 10. RECORDS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 **Records.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records relating to the Services and each Covered Vessel. These include planned maintenance records, logbooks, certificates and SMS records. All such records are the property of Customer or the relevant registered owner. Provider shall deliver them to Customer on request and on termination of the applicable SOW.

10.2 **Pre-existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. This includes Provider's SMS manuals, procedures and software. During the term of each SOW, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Provider's materials to the extent necessary to receive and use the Services.

10.3 **Deliverables.** Vessel-specific reports, budgets, plans and data prepared for Customer under an SOW become Customer's property on payment of the Fees for the period in which they are prepared.

## 11. INDEMNITIES

11.1 **Provider Indemnity.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising from third-party claims for:

(a) bodily injury to or death of any person, to the extent caused by the negligence or breach of this Agreement of Provider, its personnel or its subcontractors; or

(b) infringement or misappropriation of any intellectual property right by Provider's materials or by the Services as provided by Provider.

11.2 **Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising from third-party claims relating to the Covered Vessels or the Services. This indemnity does not apply to the extent Provider is responsible for those Losses under Section 11.1, or to the extent they result from Provider's negligence, breach of this Agreement or wilful misconduct.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall:

(a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim;

(b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement may impose any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Losses.** Except for liability under Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any loss of profit, loss of hire, loss of charter or business, or for any indirect, special, incidental or consequential damages arising out of this Agreement or any SOW, even if advised of their possibility.

12.2 **Liability Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, Provider's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement and each SOW, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) Provider's obligations under Section 11.1;

(b) Losses arising from a Party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct;

(c) Environmental Losses;

(d) breach of Section 13; or

(e) Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due.

12.4 **Package Limitation.** Nothing in this Agreement deprives Provider of any right to limit liability that is available to a ship manager under any applicable convention or statute.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party in connection with this Agreement that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. This includes the terms of each SOW, commercial and charter information, and vessel performance data.

13.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall:

(a) protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care;

(b) use it only to perform or receive the Services; and

(c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers, insurers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.

13.3 **Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed; or

(d) must be disclosed by law, by a Governmental Authority or by a classification society. Before making any such disclosure, the receiving Party shall give prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful.

13.4 **Duration.** This Section 13 survives for three (3) years after expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. TERM AND TERMINATION

14.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 14 (the "**Term**").

14.2 **Termination of Agreement.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice at any time when no SOW is in effect.

14.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW by written notice if the other Party:

(a) materially breaches this Agreement or that SOW and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it; or

(b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy, liquidation or similar proceeding.

14.4 **Customer Termination Rights.** Customer may terminate an SOW, in whole or in respect of one or more Covered Vessels, by written notice:

(a) on the sale, total loss, constructive total loss or requisition of a Covered Vessel, effective for that vessel on the date of the relevant event;

(b) if a Covered Vessel is detained by a Governmental Authority for more than fourteen (14) days because of Provider's failure to perform the Services; or

(c) as otherwise provided in the SOW.

14.5 **Effect on SOWs.** Termination of an SOW does not terminate this Agreement or any other SOW. If this Agreement is terminated under Section 14.3, each affected SOW terminates with it.

## 15. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

15.1 **Handover.** On expiry or termination of an SOW, Provider shall cooperate in an orderly handover of each affected Covered Vessel to Customer or its nominee in accordance with the vessel handover arrangements agreed under that SOW. As part of the handover, Provider shall:

(a) deliver all records, certificates, SMS documentation and Customer property;

(b) arrange the repatriation or transfer of crew as Customer directs; and

(c) deliver a final account of the Management Accounts within sixty (60) days.

15.2 **Payments.** Customer shall pay Fees accrued up to the effective date of termination. Provider shall refund any Fees paid in advance for any period after that date, together with any balance remaining in the Management Accounts.

15.3 **Survival.** Sections 6.6, 7.4, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 18 survive expiry or termination, together with any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performing its obligations, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, war, terrorism, piracy, epidemic, government action and named storms. The affected Party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects. If a force majeure event prevents performance in respect of a Covered Vessel for more than sixty (60) consecutive days, either Party may terminate the applicable SOW for that vessel by written notice.

## 17. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING

17.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any SOW without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. However, Customer may assign an SOW, as to a Covered Vessel, to an Affiliate or to a purchaser or financier of that vessel, by written notice to Provider.

17.2 **Subcontracting.** Provider may subcontract parts of the Services, including crew manning agencies, only with Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 18. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

18.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and each SOW, and any dispute or claim arising out of or relating to them, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

18.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any SOW. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

18.3 **Escalation.** Before starting proceedings, other than for urgent injunctive relief, a Party shall refer the dispute to senior executives of both Parties. Those executives shall meet within fifteen (15) Business Days to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

18.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** To the extent permitted by law, each Party waives any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any SOW.

18.5 **Maritime Liens and Arrest.** Nothing in this Section 18 prevents a Party from arresting or attaching a vessel or other property, in any jurisdiction, to obtain security for a claim.

## 19. GENERAL

19.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by internationally recognized courier or by email, with a confirmation copy sent by courier. Notices must be sent to the addresses stated on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt.

19.2 **Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Provider acts as agent for Customer or the registered owner only to the extent necessary to perform the Services.

19.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of that right.

19.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force. The invalid provision shall be replaced by a valid provision that comes closest to the Parties' intent.

19.5 **Third-Party Rights.** Except for the indemnified persons under Section 11, no person other than the Parties has any right to enforce this Agreement.

19.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
