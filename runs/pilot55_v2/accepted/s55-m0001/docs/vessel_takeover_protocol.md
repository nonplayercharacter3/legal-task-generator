# VESSEL TAKEOVER PROTOCOL

**(Gulf Coast Feeder Fleet: SOW No. 1)**

This Vessel Takeover Protocol (this "**Protocol**") is agreed as of March 9, 2026 by and between:

**Zilain Feeder Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"); and

**Geilaind Ship Management (Cyprus) Ltd**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Cyprus ("**Provider**").

This Protocol is the Vessel Takeover Protocol referred to in Sections 2.3 and 9.2 of Statement of Work No. 1 dated as of March 9, 2026 between Customer and Provider ("**SOW No. 1**"). SOW No. 1 is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Protocol have the meanings given in SOW No. 1, the MSA or the Quality Agreement, as applicable.

## 1. PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

1.1 **Purpose.** This Protocol sets out the operational steps for transferring technical management of each Covered Vessel to Provider on its takeover date. It also sets out the steps for the handover of a Covered Vessel on expiry or termination of SOW No. 1.

1.2 **Application.** This Protocol applies to each Covered Vessel listed in Annex A of SOW No. 1. It also applies to any vessel added to SOW No. 1 by amendment under Section 1.3 of SOW No. 1.

1.3 **Takeover Time.** Unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing for a particular vessel, takeover of a Covered Vessel occurs at 00:01 local time at the vessel's location on the takeover date shown for that vessel in Annex A of SOW No. 1 (the "**Takeover Time**").

## 2. TAKEOVER TEAMS

2.1 **Customer Representative.** Customer's Marine Superintendent shall act as Customer's takeover representative. The Marine Superintendent shall coordinate vessel access, port agents and the delivery of vessel documentation.

2.2 **Provider Representative.** Provider's Fleet Manager shall lead Provider's takeover team. For each Covered Vessel, the team shall include the technical superintendent assigned to that vessel under Section 4.2 of SOW No. 1 and a representative of Provider's DPA/HSQE function.

2.3 **Coordination Calls.** The Parties shall hold a takeover coordination call at least weekly from the date of this Protocol until the last takeover date in Annex A of SOW No. 1.

## 3. PRE-TAKEOVER PLANNING

3.1 **Customer Documentation.** At least ten (10) days before the pre-takeover inspection of each Covered Vessel, Customer shall deliver or make available to Provider:

(a) the current class status report, including any conditions of class, memoranda and recommendations;

(b) copies of all statutory and class certificates, with their expiry and survey due dates;

(c) planned maintenance records and the outstanding defect list;

(d) the last two (2) years of port state control, Flag State and vetting inspection history;

(e) the current Vessel Response Plan, Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan and ballast water management plan;

(f) drawings, manuals and the inventory of spares held on board; and

(g) details of the vessel's existing crew contracts and crew list.

3.2 **Provider Planning.** Before each takeover date, Provider shall:

(a) prepare a vessel-specific takeover plan setting out the inspection schedule, crew changeover arrangements and documentation to be issued;

(b) deliver the draft operating budget for the vessel within the time required by Section 7.1 of SOW No. 1;

(c) apply to the Flag State and the classification society for the issue of an interim Safety Management Certificate and an interim International Ship Security Certificate in Provider's name, with effect from the Takeover Time;

(d) arrange for the vessel's Vessel Response Plan to be updated to show Provider as manager, and confirm the arrangements with the qualified individual and the oil spill removal organization required by Section 5.2 of the Quality Agreement; and

(e) notify Customer of the Designated Person Ashore and each alternate in accordance with Section 4.3 of SOW No. 1.

3.3 **Insurances.** Customer shall arrange for Provider to be named as co-assured or joint member, as applicable, under the vessel insurances in accordance with Section 8.1 of the MSA, with effect from the Takeover Time. Customer shall provide Provider with copies of the relevant certificates of entry and cover notes before the takeover date.

## 4. PRE-TAKEOVER INSPECTION

4.1 **Timing.** Provider shall carry out a pre-takeover inspection of each Covered Vessel during a port call scheduled by Customer, not earlier than sixty (60) days and not later than seven (7) days before the vessel's takeover date. Customer shall arrange access for Provider's inspectors through its port agents.

4.2 **Scope.** The inspection shall cover:

(a) hull, deck, accommodation and cargo areas, as accessible during the port call;

(b) main and auxiliary machinery, steering gear and critical equipment identified in the Safety Management System;

(c) lifesaving, firefighting and pollution prevention equipment;

(d) navigational and communications equipment;

(e) the condition and accuracy of the Oil Record Book, Garbage Record Book and ballast water records; and

(f) the status of planned maintenance and the outstanding defect list.

4.3 **Inspection Report.** Within five (5) Business Days after each inspection, Provider shall deliver to Customer a written inspection report. The report shall record the condition of the vessel, identify any defects or deficiencies observed, and classify each item as one that must be rectified before takeover, one to be rectified after takeover, or one for monitoring.

4.4 **Rectification Before Takeover.** The Parties shall agree in good faith which items are to be rectified before the takeover date and the arrangements for doing so. Rectification work carried out after the Takeover Time shall be included in the vessel's operating budget or approved under Section 7.2 of the MSA, as applicable.

## 5. TAKEOVER DAY

5.1 **Takeover Meeting.** On or immediately before the takeover date, Provider's technical superintendent and Customer's Marine Superintendent, or their nominees, shall attend the vessel together with the outgoing and incoming masters and chief engineers.

5.2 **Inventories.** At the Takeover Time, the attending representatives shall jointly record:

(a) bunkers and lubricating oils remaining on board, by grade and quantity;

(b) fresh water and provisions remaining on board;

(c) cash on board; and

(d) a summary inventory of spares and stores, with reference to the inventory delivered under Section 3.1(f).

Bunkers remaining on board remain the property of Customer. Bunker procurement remains with Customer under Section 3.5 of SOW No. 1.

5.3 **Documentation.** At the Takeover Time, Customer shall deliver or cause to be delivered to Provider all original certificates, logbooks, record books, manuals, drawings and other vessel records held on board or ashore. Provider shall give a written receipt for them. All such records remain the property of Customer or the registered owner under Section 10.1 of the MSA.

5.4 **ISM and ISPS Changeover.** From the Takeover Time, Provider shall assume the responsibilities of "Company" under the ISM Code and of the company under the ISPS Code for the vessel, in accordance with Section 3.4 of SOW No. 1. Provider shall ensure that the interim certificates referred to in Section 3.2(c) are on board at the Takeover Time. Provider shall also ensure that the master has been briefed on Provider's Safety Management System and the contact details of the Designated Person Ashore.

5.5 **Crew Changeover.** Provider shall carry out the crew changeover in accordance with the takeover plan. Where Customer directs that any existing crew member is to remain on board, Provider shall arrange that crew member's re-engagement on terms that comply with Section 4.2 of the MSA. Customer shall be responsible for the settlement of wages and repatriation of the outgoing crew engaged before the Takeover Time.

5.6 **Takeover Certificate.** On completion of the takeover steps, the attending representatives of both Parties shall sign a takeover certificate for the vessel. The certificate shall record the Takeover Time, the inventories under Section 5.2, the documentation delivered under Section 5.3 and the items carried forward from the inspection report under Section 4.3.

## 6. NOTIFICATIONS

6.1 **Provider Notifications.** Within two (2) Business Days after the Takeover Time, Provider shall notify the change of technical manager to:

(a) the Flag State administration;

(b) the classification society;

(c) the United States Coast Guard, including in connection with the Vessel Response Plan; and

(d) the vessel's P&I club and hull underwriters, in coordination with Customer.

6.2 **Customer Notifications.** Customer shall notify its charterers, port agents and terminal operators of the change of technical manager, and shall maintain the vessel's certificate of financial responsibility under OPA 90 in accordance with Section 8.1 of the MSA.

## 7. POST-TAKEOVER

7.1 **Familiarization Period.** During the thirty (30) days after the Takeover Time, Provider shall:

(a) complete crew familiarization under Section 4.5 of the Quality Agreement;

(b) review and, where necessary, update the planned maintenance system for the vessel under Section 3.2 of the Quality Agreement;

(c) carry out an initial internal ISM audit of the vessel to support the issue of a full-term Safety Management Certificate; and

(d) open the Management Accounts for the vessel, if not already opened, in accordance with Section 7.3 of SOW No. 1.

7.2 **Post-Takeover Report.** Within forty-five (45) days after the Takeover Time, Provider shall deliver to Customer a post-takeover report. The report shall confirm the status of the items carried forward under Section 5.6 and identify any further defects found during the familiarization period, with a proposed rectification plan.

7.3 **Oil Spill Response Drill.** Within sixty (60) days after the first takeover date, the Parties shall conduct a joint oil spill response drill on one of the first two Covered Vessels taken over, under the Vessel Response Plan.

## 8. HANDOVER ON EXPIRY OR TERMINATION

8.1 **Application.** Under Section 9.2 of SOW No. 1, this Section 8 applies on the expiry or termination of SOW No. 1 for any Covered Vessel. It applies together with Section 15.1 of the MSA.

8.2 **Reverse Application.** The procedures in Sections 3 to 6 apply to the handover in reverse, with the necessary changes. Provider shall act as the outgoing manager, and Customer or its nominee shall act as the incoming manager. In particular:

(a) Provider shall deliver the documentation listed in Section 3.1, updated to the handover date, at least ten (10) days before the handover date;

(b) Provider shall give Customer or its nominee access to the vessel for a pre-handover inspection;

(c) the Parties shall jointly record the inventories under Section 5.2 at the handover time;

(d) Provider shall deliver all records, certificates and SMS documentation under Section 15.1(a) of the MSA and obtain a receipt for them;

(e) Provider shall arrange the repatriation or transfer of crew as Customer directs under Section 15.1(b) of the MSA; and

(f) the attending representatives shall sign a handover certificate recording the handover time.

8.3 **Final Accounts.** Provider shall deliver the final account of the Management Accounts for the vessel within the time required by Section 15.1(c) of the MSA.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Amendment.** This Protocol may be amended in writing signed by Customer and Provider. Changes to the timetable for an individual Covered Vessel may be agreed by email between the Customer and Provider representatives named in Section 2.

9.2 **Notices.** Notices under this Protocol shall be given in accordance with Section 10 of SOW No. 1.

9.3 **Counterparts.** This Protocol may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
