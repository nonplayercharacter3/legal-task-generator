# PARENT COMPANY GUARANTY

This Parent Company Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is dated February 16, 2026 and is given by:

**Geilaind Holdings plc**, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales (the "**Guarantor**"),

in favor of:

**Zilain Feeder Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. The Beneficiary and Geilaind Marine Services LLC have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, technical ship management services are to be provided to the Beneficiary under one or more Statements of Work.

B. Under Section 2.5 of the MSA, delivery of this Guaranty is a condition of the Beneficiary's engagement of Provider.

C. Provider is an indirect subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor expects to derive substantial commercial benefit from the MSA and the Statements of Work entered into under it.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Guaranty:

"**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in London or New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means every present and future obligation and liability of Provider to the Beneficiary under or in connection with the MSA and any SOW. This covers obligations that are actual or contingent, incurred alone or jointly, and incurred as principal or surety. It includes:

(a) the obligation to perform the Services;

(b) the obligation to refund Fees and to account for balances in the Management Accounts;

(c) indemnity obligations; and

(d) liability for damages for breach.

"**Provider**" means Geilaind Marine Services LLC, a limited liability company, and its successors and permitted assigns.

"**SOW**" means a statement of work entered into under Section 2.2 of the MSA, as amended from time to time.

1.2 **MSA Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the MSA.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to the MSA or any SOW are to that document as amended, supplemented or replaced from time to time.

## 2. GUARANTY AND INDEMNITY

2.1 **Guaranty.** The Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to the Beneficiary the due and punctual payment, performance and discharge of the Guaranteed Obligations.

2.2 **Payment on Demand.** If Provider fails to pay any amount when due under the MSA or any SOW, the Guarantor shall pay that amount to the Beneficiary within ten (10) Business Days after written demand, as if the Guarantor were the principal obligor.

2.3 **Performance.** If Provider fails to perform any Guaranteed Obligation other than a payment obligation, the Guarantor shall, within fifteen (15) Business Days after written demand, do one of the following:

(a) perform that obligation, or procure its performance by another member of the Guarantor's group that is competent to do so; or

(b) pay to the Beneficiary the Losses the Beneficiary incurs as a result of Provider's failure.

2.4 **Indemnity.** As a separate and independent obligation, the Guarantor shall indemnify the Beneficiary on demand against all Losses the Beneficiary incurs if any Guaranteed Obligation is or becomes unenforceable, invalid or illegal. The amount payable under this indemnity shall not exceed the amount the Beneficiary would have been able to recover from Provider if that obligation had been enforceable, valid and legal.

2.5 **Limit.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed the liability Provider would have had under the MSA and the applicable SOW, including any limitation or exclusion of liability under Section 12 of the MSA. This Section 2.5 does not limit the Guarantor's liability for costs of enforcement under Section 6.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing security. It extends to the ultimate balance of the Guaranteed Obligations, regardless of any intermediate payment or discharge in whole or in part.

3.2 **Primary Obligation.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are primary obligations. Before making a demand under this Guaranty, the Beneficiary is not required to take any of the following steps:

(a) make a demand on Provider;

(b) start proceedings against Provider; or

(c) enforce any other security or right it holds.

3.3 **Obligations Not Affected.** Any of the following does not reduce, release or otherwise affect the Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty:

(a) any time, waiver or indulgence granted to Provider;

(b) any insolvency, liquidation, administration or similar proceeding affecting Provider;

(c) any change in the constitution, ownership or control of Provider or the Guarantor; or

(d) any incapacity or lack of authority of Provider.

3.4 **Reinstatement.** A payment or discharge may later be avoided or reduced, or have to be restored, in insolvency or similar proceedings. If that happens, the Guarantor's liability shall continue as if the payment or discharge had not occurred.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary, on the date of this Guaranty, that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has the corporate power to enter into and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms;

(d) entering into and performing this Guaranty does not breach its articles of association, any law binding on it, or any agreement binding on it or its assets; and

(e) Provider is, directly or indirectly, wholly owned by the Guarantor.

## 5. COVENANTS

5.1 **Financial Information.** For so long as any Guaranteed Obligation is outstanding, the Guarantor shall deliver its audited consolidated annual financial statements to the Beneficiary within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each financial year.

5.2 **Ownership.** The Guarantor shall notify the Beneficiary within ten (10) Business Days if Provider ceases to be a direct or indirect subsidiary of the Guarantor.

## 6. PAYMENTS AND COSTS

6.1 **Currency and Manner.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account designated by the Beneficiary in its demand.

6.2 **No Set-off.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off, counterclaim or deduction. The only exception is a withholding required by law. If a withholding is required, the Guarantor shall increase its payment so that the Beneficiary receives the amount it would have received without the withholding.

6.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** The Guarantor shall reimburse the Beneficiary on demand for all reasonable costs and expenses, including legal fees, incurred in enforcing this Guaranty.

## 7. SUBROGATION AND DEFERRAL OF RIGHTS

Until the Guaranteed Obligations have been irrevocably paid and discharged in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right it may have:

(a) of subrogation to the rights of the Beneficiary against Provider;

(b) to an indemnity or contribution from Provider; or

(c) to claim or prove as a creditor of Provider in competition with the Beneficiary.

## 8. TERM, ASSIGNMENT AND NOTICES

8.1 **Term.** This Guaranty remains in force until all Guaranteed Obligations have been irrevocably paid, performed and discharged in full. This includes Guaranteed Obligations that arise after expiry or termination of the MSA or any SOW in respect of events occurring before that expiry or termination.

8.2 **Assignment.** The Guarantor may not assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Guaranty. The Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any person to whom it assigns its rights under the MSA or any SOW in accordance with Section 17.1 of the MSA.

8.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered by hand, by internationally recognized courier or by email, with a confirmation copy sent by courier. Notices must be sent:

(a) to the Guarantor, at the address stated on the signature page of this Guaranty, marked for the attention of the Company Secretary; and

(b) to the Beneficiary, at its address for notices under the MSA.

A notice is effective on receipt.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter (including non-contractual disputes or claims), are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor and the Beneficiary each irrevocably agree that the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Guaranty or its subject matter (including non-contractual disputes or claims).

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended only in writing signed by the Guarantor and the Beneficiary. No failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of that right.

10.2 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

10.3 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
