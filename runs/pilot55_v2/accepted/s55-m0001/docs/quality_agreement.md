# QUALITY AGREEMENT

**(Technical Ship Management: Safety, Environmental, Technical and Performance Standards)**

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of February 16, 2026 by and between:

**Zilain Feeder Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"); and

**Geilaind Marine Services LLC**, a limited liability company ("**Geilaind**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Geilaind have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, Provider performs technical management services for Covered Vessels under one or more Statements of Work.

B. The MSA provides that Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the Quality Agreement. This Quality Agreement sets out the safety, environmental, technical and performance standards that apply to the Services.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Incorporated Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA. These include "Affiliate", "Applicable Law", "Business Day", "Covered Vessels", "Flag State", "Governmental Authority", "OPA 90", "Provider", "Safety Management System", "Services" and "SOW".

1.2 **Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

"**Casualty**" means any collision, allision, grounding, stranding, fire, explosion, flooding, structural failure, loss of propulsion or steering, or other marine casualty involving a Covered Vessel.

"**Detention**" means the detention of a Covered Vessel by a port state control authority, the United States Coast Guard or a Flag State administration.

"**Environmental Losses**" means fines, penalties and assessments imposed by a Governmental Authority under MARPOL or OPA 90 arising from a Pollution Incident.

"**KPIs**" means the key performance indicators set out in Section 7 and in Schedule 1.

"**Lost Time Injury**" means a work-related injury to a crew member or other person on board a Covered Vessel that results in that person being unfit for work for one or more full days after the day of the injury.

"**MARPOL**" means the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, 1973, as modified by the Protocols of 1978 and 1997, including its Annexes.

"**Near Miss**" means an event that did not result in injury, damage or pollution but could have done so under slightly different circumstances.

"**Non-Conformity**" means an observed situation where objective evidence shows that a requirement of the Safety Management System, this Quality Agreement or Applicable Law has not been fulfilled.

"**Pollution Incident**" means any actual or threatened discharge, spill, release or escape of oil, oily mixtures, noxious liquid substances, garbage, sewage, ballast water or other pollutants from or in connection with a Covered Vessel into the sea, any navigable waters, any shoreline or the atmosphere, other than a discharge permitted by Applicable Law.

"**Quality Review Meeting**" has the meaning given in Section 8.2.

"**Reportable Event**" means any Casualty, Detention, Pollution Incident, Lost Time Injury, fatality, security incident under the ISPS Code, or port state control inspection that results in three (3) or more deficiencies.

"**Vessel Response Plan**" means, for each Covered Vessel, the vessel response plan required under OPA 90 and 33 C.F.R. Part 155, and the Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan required under MARPOL Annex I, as applicable.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the MSA applies to this Quality Agreement. References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Quality Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

2.1 **Certification.** Throughout the Term, Provider shall hold a valid Document of Compliance under the ISM Code covering each type of Covered Vessel. For each Covered Vessel, Provider shall maintain a valid Safety Management Certificate. Provider shall maintain certification of its management system to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, or to equivalent standards approved in writing by Customer.

2.2 **Safety Management System.** Provider shall maintain the Safety Management System in accordance with the ISM Code, Applicable Law and this Quality Agreement. Provider shall provide Customer with read access to the current SMS manuals and procedures that apply to the Covered Vessels. Provider shall notify Customer of any material revision to those manuals or procedures within thirty (30) days after it takes effect.

2.3 **Designated Person Ashore.** Provider shall ensure that the Designated Person Ashore is reachable by Customer and by the master of each Covered Vessel twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of the identity and contact details of the Designated Person Ashore and of each alternate.

2.4 **Internal Audits.** Provider shall carry out an internal ISM audit of each Covered Vessel at least once in every twelve (12) months, and an internal audit of its shore office at least once in every twelve (12) months. Provider shall provide Customer with a summary of findings within fifteen (15) Business Days after completion of each audit.

## 3. TECHNICAL STANDARDS

3.1 **Class and Certification.** Provider shall keep each Covered Vessel in class with its classification society, free of overdue conditions of class and overdue recommendations. Provider shall keep all statutory and class certificates valid. Provider shall notify Customer of any condition of class or memorandum imposed on a Covered Vessel within two (2) Business Days after it is imposed.

3.2 **Planned Maintenance.** Provider shall maintain a computerized planned maintenance system for each Covered Vessel. The system shall cover all machinery, equipment and critical systems identified in the Safety Management System. Overdue planned maintenance jobs for critical equipment shall not exceed the KPI threshold set out in Schedule 1.

3.3 **Drydocking and Surveys.** Provider shall plan each drydocking and special survey at least six (6) months in advance. Provider shall submit a drydock specification and budget estimate to Customer for approval at least ninety (90) days before the scheduled date.

3.4 **Vetting and Inspections.** Provider shall prepare each Covered Vessel for port state control, Flag State, class, charterer and terminal inspections. Provider shall close out all deficiencies within the time set by the inspecting authority. Where no time is set, Provider shall close out deficiencies within thirty (30) days.

## 4. CREW STANDARDS

4.1 **Qualifications.** Where an SOW includes crewing services, Provider shall ensure that each officer and rating assigned to a Covered Vessel holds valid certificates of competency and proficiency under STCW and the requirements of the Flag State.

4.2 **Experience.** Provider shall ensure that:

(a) the master and chief engineer of each Covered Vessel each have not less than two (2) years' experience in rank, of which at least twelve (12) months is on container vessels;

(b) the chief officer and second engineer each have not less than twelve (12) months' experience in rank; and

(c) at least one deck officer on each Covered Vessel has prior experience of U.S. coastal and port operations.

4.3 **Medical Fitness.** Provider shall ensure that each crew member holds a valid medical certificate issued under STCW and the Maritime Labour Convention 2006 by a medical practitioner recognized by the Flag State. Provider shall not assign any crew member whose medical certificate will expire during the planned contract period without arranging timely renewal.

4.4 **Drug and Alcohol Policy.** Provider's drug and alcohol policy shall:

(a) prohibit the use or possession of illegal drugs on board each Covered Vessel;

(b) set a blood alcohol limit for crew of not more than 0.04% while on duty and for four (4) hours before going on duty;

(c) require pre-employment drug testing, unannounced testing on board at least once in every twelve (12) months, and post-incident testing after any Reportable Event; and

(d) comply with 46 C.F.R. Part 16 and 33 C.F.R. Part 95 to the extent applicable.

4.5 **Training.** Provider shall deliver familiarization training to each crew member on joining a Covered Vessel. Provider shall conduct drills on board at the intervals required by SOLAS and the Safety Management System, including oil spill response drills under the Vessel Response Plan.

4.6 **Hours of Rest.** Provider shall ensure that crew hours of rest comply with STCW and the Maritime Labour Convention 2006. Provider shall keep hours-of-rest records on board for inspection.

## 5. ENVIRONMENTAL STANDARDS

5.1 **Pollution Prevention.** Provider shall operate each Covered Vessel in compliance with MARPOL, OPA 90, the U.S. Clean Water Act and applicable Vessel General Permit and ballast water management requirements. Provider shall maintain accurate Oil Record Books, Garbage Record Books and ballast water records on board each Covered Vessel.

5.2 **Vessel Response Plans.** Provider shall maintain a current, approved Vessel Response Plan for each Covered Vessel. Provider shall maintain contracts with a qualified individual and an oil spill removal organization as required by OPA 90 for each Covered Vessel operating in U.S. waters.

5.3 **Response to Pollution Incidents.** On the occurrence of a Pollution Incident, Provider shall:

(a) immediately activate the Vessel Response Plan;

(b) make all notifications to Governmental Authorities required by Applicable Law, including to the U.S. National Response Center;

(c) take all reasonable measures to stop, contain and mitigate the discharge; and

(d) coordinate with Customer, Customer's P&I club and any responding organization.

5.4 **Fuel Compliance.** Provider shall ensure that each Covered Vessel uses fuel that complies with MARPOL Annex VI and with the requirements of the North American Emission Control Area. Provider shall retain bunker delivery notes and fuel samples for the periods required by MARPOL.

5.5 **Environmental Records.** Provider shall retain all environmental records relating to each Covered Vessel for not less than three (3) years, or for any longer period required by Applicable Law.

## 6. INCIDENT NOTIFICATION AND INVESTIGATION

6.1 **Initial Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer by telephone and email of any Reportable Event:

(a) within one (1) hour after Provider becomes aware of any Pollution Incident, Casualty, fatality or security incident; and

(b) within twelve (12) hours after Provider becomes aware of any other Reportable Event.

6.2 **Written Report.** Provider shall deliver to Customer a preliminary written report on each Reportable Event within forty-eight (48) hours after the initial notification. The report shall describe the event, the immediate actions taken and the current status of the Covered Vessel.

6.3 **Investigation.** Provider shall investigate each Reportable Event and each Near Miss classified as high potential under the Safety Management System. For each Reportable Event, Provider shall deliver a root cause analysis and a corrective and preventive action plan to Customer within thirty (30) days after the event.

6.4 **Non-Conformities.** Provider shall record and track all Non-Conformities. Provider shall close out each Non-Conformity within the time set in the Safety Management System, and in any event within ninety (90) days, unless Customer agrees to a longer period in writing.

## 7. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

7.1 **KPIs.** Provider shall measure and report its performance against the KPIs set out in Schedule 1 for each Covered Vessel and for the Covered Vessels in aggregate under each SOW.

7.2 **Measurement.** KPIs shall be measured on a rolling twelve (12) month basis unless Schedule 1 states otherwise. Provider shall maintain the data supporting each KPI and make it available to Customer on request.

7.3 **Remediation.** If Provider fails to meet any KPI in two (2) consecutive quarters, Provider shall deliver a remediation plan to Customer within twenty (20) Business Days. The plan shall identify the causes of the shortfall and the actions and timetable for correcting it. Provider shall implement the plan as agreed with Customer.

## 8. REPORTING AND REVIEW

8.1 **Monthly Reports.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each month, Provider shall deliver to Customer a monthly technical report for each Covered Vessel. The report shall cover:

(a) technical condition, planned maintenance status and outstanding defects;

(b) class and statutory certificate status;

(c) inspections, deficiencies and close-out status;

(d) crew changes, training and hours-of-rest compliance;

(e) Reportable Events, Near Misses and Non-Conformities; and

(f) environmental performance, including fuel consumption and waste discharge data.

8.2 **Quality Review Meetings.** The parties shall hold a quality review meeting at least once each calendar quarter (each, a "**Quality Review Meeting**"). At each Quality Review Meeting, the parties shall review KPI performance, Reportable Events, audit findings and open corrective actions.

8.3 **Annual Review.** The parties shall review this Quality Agreement, including Schedule 1, at least once each year. Any change agreed at that review shall be recorded in a written amendment under Section 10.2.

## 9. CUSTOMER AUDIT AND INSPECTION

9.1 **Audit Right.** Customer, or an independent auditor appointed by Customer, may audit Provider's compliance with this Quality Agreement at Provider's shore office on fifteen (15) Business Days' notice, not more than twice in any calendar year. Customer may audit more frequently after any Reportable Event.

9.2 **Vessel Inspections.** Customer and its representatives may inspect any Covered Vessel at any time while the vessel is in port, on reasonable notice to Provider. Inspections shall be conducted so as not to interfere unreasonably with the vessel's operations.

9.3 **Findings.** Provider shall respond to audit and inspection findings within fifteen (15) Business Days. Provider shall close out each finding in accordance with an agreed corrective action plan.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the date stated above and remains in effect for so long as the MSA remains in effect.

10.2 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

10.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 19.1 of the MSA. Notifications of Reportable Events under Section 6.1 shall also be given to the operational contacts designated by Customer in writing from time to time.

10.4 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
