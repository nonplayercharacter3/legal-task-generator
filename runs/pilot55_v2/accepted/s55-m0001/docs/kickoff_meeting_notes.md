## 4. PERSONNEL

4.1 Provider introduced its Fleet Manager designate, who has more than fourteen years of container vessel technical management experience. The Fleet Manager will be Customer's primary contact under Section 4.1 of SOW No. 1.

4.2 Provider confirmed three technical superintendents for the six vessels: one based in Houston and two operating from Limassol, with travel to the Gulf as required. This matches the ratio in Section 4.2 of SOW No. 1.

4.3 Provider will notify Customer in writing of the Designated Person Ashore and each alternate, with 24-hour contact details, before March 31, 2026, in accordance with Section 4.3 of SOW No. 1 and Section 2.3 of the Quality Agreement.

## 5. BUDGETS AND MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTS

5.1 Provider circulated draft 2026 operating budgets for ZILAIN BAYOU and ZILAIN SABINE on March 2, 2026. Customer's Fleet Finance Manager will return comments by March 20, 2026. Provider will deliver draft budgets for the remaining four vessels at least thirty (30) days before their takeover dates, under Section 7.1 of SOW No. 1.

5.2 The parties discussed candidate U.S. banks for the Management Accounts. Customer will confirm its preferred bank by March 18, 2026. Provider will then open the accounts in Customer's name under Section 7.1 of the MSA and Section 7.3 of SOW No. 1.

5.3 Provider confirmed that the first monthly management fee invoice will be issued for April 2026. The invoice will reflect daily proration for the vessels taken over on April 15, 2026, under Section 6.2 of SOW No. 1.

## 6. CREWING

6.1 Provider's Crewing Manager presented the proposed crew matrix for each vessel. Customer requested CVs for the proposed masters and chief engineers. Provider will supply them by March 25, 2026 for the first four vessels.

6.2 Provider confirmed that at least one deck officer on each vessel will have prior U.S. coastal and port experience, as required by Section 4.2(c) of the Quality Agreement.

6.3 Crew changeover for the first two vessels will take place at Houston on takeover. Provider will coordinate crew travel with Customer's port agents.

## 7. ENVIRONMENTAL AND SAFETY READINESS

7.1 Provider's DPA/HSQE Manager confirmed that the Vessel Response Plans for each vessel will be updated to show Provider as manager before takeover. Provider will also confirm the arrangements with the qualified individual and the oil spill removal organization under Section 5.2 of the Quality Agreement.

7.2 Customer's HSQE Manager will share the last two years of port state control and vetting history for each vessel. This will support the baseline for the KPIs in Schedule 1 of the Quality Agreement.

7.3 The parties agreed to hold a joint oil spill response drill on one of the first two vessels within sixty (60) days after takeover.

## 8. REPORTING AND REVIEWS

8.1 Provider will send a sample monthly technical report template to Customer by March 27, 2026. The template will follow the headings in Section 8.1 of the Quality Agreement.

8.2 The first Quality Review Meeting will be held in Houston in July 2026, covering the second quarter of 2026.
