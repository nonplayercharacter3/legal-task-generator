# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**(Gulf Coast Feeder Fleet: Technical Management Services)**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between:

**Zilain Feeder Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"); and

**Geilaind Ship Management (Cyprus) Ltd**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Cyprus and an Affiliate of Geilaind Marine Services LLC ("**Provider**").

This SOW is issued under the Master Services Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 between Customer and Geilaind Marine Services LLC (the "**MSA**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the MSA.

## 1. COVERED VESSELS

1.1 **Covered Vessels.** The Covered Vessels under this SOW are the six feeder container vessels in U.S. Gulf Coast service listed in Annex A.

1.2 **Service Area.** The Covered Vessels trade principally between the ports of Houston, Freeport, New Orleans, Mobile and Tampa, with occasional calls at other U.S. Gulf ports as Customer directs under Section 5.2 of the MSA.

1.3 **Addition and Removal of Vessels.** The Parties may add a vessel to this SOW by written amendment that updates Annex A and states the fee for that vessel. A vessel ceases to be a Covered Vessel on the effective date of any termination for that vessel under Section 14.4 or Section 16.1 of the MSA. The Parties shall then adjust the management fee under Section 6.3 of this SOW.

## 2. CONTRACTING ENTITY AND TERM

2.1 **Contracting Entity.** This SOW is entered into under Section 2.2 of the MSA. It constitutes a separate contract between Customer and Geilaind Ship Management (Cyprus) Ltd, which executes this SOW as Provider and shall perform the Services under it from its management office in Limassol, Cyprus, and through the shore personnel assigned under Section 4 of this SOW.

2.2 **SOW Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years (the "**Initial SOW Term**"). After that, it renews automatically for successive periods of one (1) year unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current period. The Initial SOW Term and each renewal period together make up the "**SOW Term**."

2.3 **Takeover.** Provider shall take over technical management of each Covered Vessel on the takeover date shown for that vessel in Annex A, in accordance with the Vessel Takeover Protocol agreed between the Parties. Provider's obligations for a Covered Vessel begin on its takeover date. However, Provider shall perform the pre-takeover planning and inspection work described in the Vessel Takeover Protocol before that date.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Technical Management.** Provider shall perform the technical management services described in Section 3.3 of the MSA for each Covered Vessel.

3.2 **Crewing.** Provider shall provide full crewing services for each Covered Vessel under Section 4.2 of the MSA. These services include recruitment, training, payroll, crew travel, repatriation and crew insurance arrangements, other than the vessel P&I cover arranged by Customer under Section 8.1 of the MSA.

3.3 **Drydock Supervision.** Provider shall plan and supervise each scheduled drydocking and special survey of the Covered Vessels in accordance with Section 3.3 of the Quality Agreement. Provider shall provide an attending superintendent for the duration of each drydocking. Shipyard charges are vessel operating expenses payable from the Management Accounts.

3.4 **ISM and ISPS.** Provider shall act as "Company" under the ISM Code and as the company under the ISPS Code for each Covered Vessel from its takeover date, as required by Section 3.2 of the MSA.

3.5 **Excluded Services.** The following services are excluded from this SOW: commercial management, chartering, cargo booking, bunker procurement and insurance placement (other than Provider's own insurances under Section 8.2 of the MSA). Customer remains responsible for these services.

## 4. PERSONNEL

4.1 **Fleet Manager.** Provider shall appoint a fleet manager with not less than ten (10) years' experience in technical management of container vessels. The fleet manager shall be Customer's primary contact for the Services.

4.2 **Superintendents.** Provider shall assign one technical superintendent for every two (2) Covered Vessels. At least one superintendent shall be based in Houston, Texas, for the SOW Term.

4.3 **Designated Person Ashore.** Provider shall notify Customer of the Designated Person Ashore and each alternate before the first takeover date, in accordance with Section 2.3 of the Quality Agreement.

## 5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 **Modification of MSA Section 2.4.** This Section 5 modifies Section 2.4 of the MSA as it applies to this SOW.

5.2 **Exclusive Engagement.** During the SOW Term and for 24 months thereafter, Customer shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, engage Provider exclusively for all ship management, crewing and drydock supervision services for any vessel owned, operated or chartered by Customer or any of its Affiliates, in any trading area.

5.3 **New Vessels.** Customer shall notify Provider in writing within thirty (30) days after Customer or any of its Affiliates acquires, takes delivery of or charters in any vessel to which Section 5.2 applies. The Parties shall then negotiate in good faith an amendment to this SOW, or a new SOW, for the provision of services to that vessel on terms consistent with this SOW.

## 6. FEES

6.1 **Annual Management Fee.** Customer shall pay Provider an annual management fee of US$1,860,000 for the six Covered Vessels listed in Annex A. This amount equals US$310,000 per Covered Vessel per year.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice the annual management fee in equal monthly installments of US$155,000, monthly in advance, in accordance with Section 6.2 of the MSA. For any Covered Vessel whose takeover date falls after the first day of a month, the installment for that vessel shall be prorated on a daily basis for that month.

6.3 **Adjustment for Vessel Changes.** If a vessel is added to or removed from this SOW, the annual management fee shall increase or decrease by US$310,000 per vessel per year, prorated on a daily basis from the effective date of the change. Any different rate for an added vessel must be stated in the amendment that adds it.

6.4 **Annual Escalation.** On each anniversary of the SOW Effective Date, the annual management fee shall increase by the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage increase in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) over the preceding twelve (12) months.

6.5 **Drydock Supervision Fee.** For each day that a superintendent attends a Covered Vessel in drydock beyond twenty-one (21) days per drydocking, Customer shall pay Provider an attendance fee of US$950 per day. Reasonable travel and accommodation expenses for that attendance are payable from the Management Accounts.

6.6 **Crew Costs.** Crew wages, social costs, travel and training costs are vessel operating expenses. They are funded through the Management Accounts in accordance with the approved budget. They are not included in the management fee.

6.7 **Payment Details.** Customer shall pay the management fee in U.S. dollars to the bank account designated in writing by Provider.

## 7. BUDGETS AND MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTS

7.1 **Initial Budgets.** Provider shall deliver a draft operating budget for each Covered Vessel for the remainder of calendar year 2026 at least thirty (30) days before that vessel's takeover date.

7.2 **Expenditure Threshold.** For the purposes of Section 7.2 of the MSA, the threshold is five percent (5%) of the approved annual budget for the relevant Covered Vessel, or US$50,000 for any single unbudgeted item, whichever is lower.

7.3 **Management Accounts.** Provider shall open the Management Accounts for the Covered Vessels with a U.S. bank reasonably acceptable to Customer, in accordance with Section 7.1 of the MSA.

## 8. REPORTING AND PERFORMANCE

8.1 **Reports.** Provider shall deliver the monthly technical reports required by Section 8.1 of the Quality Agreement and the monthly accounts required by Section 7.4 of the MSA.

8.2 **KPIs.** The KPIs in Schedule 1 of the Quality Agreement apply to the Covered Vessels under this SOW. They shall be measured both for each Covered Vessel and for the Covered Vessels in aggregate.

8.3 **Quarterly Reviews.** The Quality Review Meetings under Section 8.2 of the Quality Agreement shall be held in Houston, Texas, or by video conference, as the Parties agree.

## 9. ADDITIONAL TERMINATION RIGHTS

9.1 **KPI Failure.** For the purposes of Section 14.4(c) of the MSA, Customer may terminate this SOW for one or more Covered Vessels on sixty (60) days' written notice in either of the following cases:

(a) Provider fails to meet KPI No. 3 (Pollution Incidents) or KPI No. 4 (Detentions) in Schedule 1 of the Quality Agreement for that vessel on two (2) or more occasions in any twelve (12) month period; or

(b) Provider fails to implement a remediation plan under Section 7.3 of the Quality Agreement within the agreed timetable.

9.2 **Handover.** On expiry or termination of this SOW for any Covered Vessel, the handover under Section 15.1 of the MSA shall be carried out in accordance with the handover procedures in the Vessel Takeover Protocol, applied in reverse.

## 10. NOTICES

10.1 **Customer.** Notices to Customer under this SOW shall be sent to the address stated for Customer on the signature page of the MSA, marked for the attention of the Vice President, Fleet Operations.

10.2 **Provider.** Notices to Provider under this SOW shall be sent to the address stated on the signature page of this SOW, marked for the attention of the Managing Director.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Incorporation.** This SOW incorporates the terms of the MSA in accordance with Section 2.2 of the MSA.

11.2 **Amendment.** This SOW may be amended only in writing signed by Customer and Provider, in accordance with Sections 3.5 and 19.3 of the MSA.

11.3 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
