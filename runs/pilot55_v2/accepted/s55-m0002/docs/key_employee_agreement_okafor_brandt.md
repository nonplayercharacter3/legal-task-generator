# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 15, 2026 and takes effect as of the Closing (the "**Effective Date**"). It is made by and between **Lakeport Skykitchen, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Marisol Okafor-Brandt** ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between **Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, as seller ("**Seller**"), and **Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC**, as buyer ("**Buyer**"), Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding Shares of the Company.

B. Executive serves as Vice President of Operations of the Company. In that role she has had, and will continue to have, access to the Company's customer relationships, pricing, menu and provisioning specifications, airport operating arrangements and other confidential information.

C. Section 3.2(a)(iii) of the Purchase Agreement requires delivery of this Agreement at the Closing, duly executed by Executive. Buyer would not complete the purchase of the Shares without Executive's agreement to the covenants in this Agreement.

D. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the continued employment of Executive and the Retention Bonus described in Section 3.3, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Employment; Position and Duties

**1.1 Position.** From the Effective Date, the Company shall continue to employ Executive as Vice President of Operations. Executive shall report to the President of the Company or such other officer as the Company designates.

**1.2 Duties.** Executive shall be responsible for the day-to-day operation of the Company's commissaries and flight kitchens. This includes production planning, galley equipment and trolley management, food safety and sanitation compliance, airside logistics and airport authority relations. She shall also perform other duties consistent with her position that the Company assigns from time to time.

**1.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote her full business time and attention to the Company. She may serve on civic or charitable boards and manage her personal investments, provided those activities do not interfere with her duties or conflict with Section 5.

**1.4 Location.** Executive's principal place of work shall be the Company's Main Commissary. Executive shall travel as reasonably required to the Company's other facilities and to customer and airport locations.

## 2. Term

**2.1 Term.** The term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years (the "**Initial Term**"). Thereafter it renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any renewal periods together constitute the "**Term**."

**2.2 Effect of Closing.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void and of no effect. In that case Executive's existing employment arrangements with the Company shall continue unchanged.

## 3. Compensation and Benefits

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $265,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Company shall review the base salary annually and may increase it, but not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of thirty-five percent (35%) of base salary. The bonus is based on operational and financial objectives set by the Company after consultation with Executive. Any bonus earned for a fiscal year shall be paid no later than March 15 of the following year, provided Executive remains employed on the payment date.

**3.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $150,000 (the "**Retention Bonus**"). Half is payable on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and half on the second anniversary, in each case provided that Executive remains employed on that date. If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, before an installment is paid, that installment shall be paid within thirty (30) days after the Release Effective Date under Section 4.4.

**3.4 Benefits.** Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior employees generally, on the terms of those plans.

**3.5 Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per calendar year, in accordance with Company policy.

**3.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

## 4. Termination

**4.1 Termination Generally.** Either party may terminate Executive's employment at any time, subject to this Section 4. Executive shall give at least sixty (60) days' written notice of resignation without Good Reason. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period.

**4.2 Definitions.**

(a) "**Cause**" means Executive's (i) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (ii) willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing her duties that causes material harm to the Company; (iii) material breach of this Agreement, including Section 5 or Section 6, which, if curable, is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or (iv) material violation of the Company's food safety, airport security or anti-harassment policies.

(b) "**Good Reason**" means, without Executive's written consent, (i) a material reduction in base salary; (ii) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or (iii) relocation of Executive's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles. Good Reason exists only if Executive gives written notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs, the Company fails to cure within thirty (30) days after the notice, and Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**4.3 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall (a) continue to pay Executive's base salary for twelve (12) months after the termination date, and (b) pay the employer portion of COBRA premiums for Executive and her eligible dependents for up to twelve (12) months. The Company's obligations under this Section 4.3 are subject to Section 4.4 and to Executive's continued compliance with Sections 5 and 6.

**4.4 Release.** Payments under Sections 3.3 and 4.3 are conditioned on Executive executing a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company. The release must become effective and irrevocable within sixty (60) days after the termination date. The date on which it does so is the "**Release Effective Date**."

**4.5 Accrued Amounts.** On any termination, the Company shall pay Executive her earned but unpaid base salary, accrued but unused paid time off to the extent required by Company policy or law, and unreimbursed business expenses.

## 5. Restrictive Covenants

**5.1 Restricted Period.** For purposes of this Section 5, the "**Restricted Period**" means the period during which Executive is employed by the Company and the twenty-four (24) months after her employment ends for any reason.

**5.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, whether on her own behalf or on behalf of any other Person, (a) solicit, induce or attempt to induce any Key Customer (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) to purchase airline catering, onboard provisioning or galley services from any Person other than the Company; (b) solicit or induce any Key Customer (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) to terminate, fail to renew or reduce its business with the Company; or (c) otherwise interfere with the relationship between the Company and any Key Customer (as defined in the Purchase Agreement).

**5.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any person who is, or was within the preceding six (6) months, an employee of the Company at the level of supervisor or above. Nor shall she encourage any such person to leave the Company. A general advertisement not targeted at Company employees is not a breach of this Section 5.3.

**5.4 Non-Disparagement.** During the Term and thereafter, Executive shall not make any public statement that disparages the Company, Buyer or their Affiliates. Truthful statements required by law or legal process are not a breach of this Section 5.4.

**5.5 Reasonableness.** Executive acknowledges that the covenants in this Section 5 are reasonable in duration and scope and are necessary to protect the goodwill of the Business acquired by Buyer under the Purchase Agreement. She further acknowledges that she has received adequate consideration for them.

**5.6 Reformation.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any covenant in this Section 5 is unenforceable as written, the court may modify the covenant to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. The covenant shall be enforced as so modified.

**5.7 Tolling.** The post-employment portion of the Restricted Period shall be extended by any period during which Executive is in breach of this Section 5.

## 6. Confidentiality and Work Product

**6.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, Buyer or their Affiliates. This includes customer lists and contract terms, pricing and cost data, menu and provisioning specifications, production methods, supplier arrangements, airport concession terms and financial information. It does not include information that becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach.

**6.2 Non-Disclosure.** During the Term and thereafter, Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information except as required in performing her duties for the Company or as required by law. If Executive is required by law to disclose Confidential Information, she shall, where legally permitted, give the Company prompt notice so that it may seek a protective order.

**6.3 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, or earlier on the Company's request, Executive shall return all Company property and all documents and data containing Confidential Information.

**6.4 Work Product.** All work product, recipes, specifications, processes and inventions that Executive creates in the course of her employment are the sole property of the Company. Executive hereby assigns to the Company all of her rights in that work product.

**6.5 Protected Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any Governmental Authority or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive will not be held liable for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting a suspected violation of law.

## 7. Remedies

**7.1 Injunctive Relief.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 5 or Section 6 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**7.2 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of Sections 5 and 6 and may enforce them directly.

## 8. General Provisions

**8.1 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. Any payment upon termination of employment that constitutes deferred compensation shall be made only upon a "separation from service."

**8.2 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**8.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign this Agreement to Buyer, to any Affiliate of Buyer or to any successor to the Business.

**8.4 Notices.** Notices to the Company shall be given to the Company, Attention: President, with a copy to Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC, Attention: Chief Financial Officer, finance@trenexflight.com. Notices to Executive shall be given to her most recent home address on file with the Company. Notices are deemed delivered on hand delivery, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or on transmission by email during business hours.

**8.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**8.6 Severability.** Subject to Section 5.6, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**8.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**8.8 Jurisdiction.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**8.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
