# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**

**Issue Date: August 3, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its permitted successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount of $6,000,000, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, in the manner and on the terms set forth in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 15, 2026 between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Maker issues this Note as part of the Purchase Price for the Shares under Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

The original principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Change of Control**" means any transaction or series of related transactions, whether by merger, consolidation, sale or transfer of equity interests, or otherwise, as a result of which (a) any Person or group of Persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity interests of Maker or of any Person that directly or indirectly controls Maker; (b) any Person that directly or indirectly controls Maker ceases to control Maker, or a change occurs in the Persons who control any Person that directly or indirectly controls Maker; or (c) Maker sells, leases or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of its assets or all or substantially all of the assets or equity interests of the Company. For purposes of this definition, "control" has the meaning used in the definition of "Affiliate" in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus two percent (2%) per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Interest Rate**" means six percent (6%) per annum.

"**Issue Date**" means August 3, 2026, the Closing Date under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Maturity Date**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness for borrowed money of Maker and the Company owed to the Senior Lender under the Senior Credit Agreement, including principal, interest (including post-petition interest), fees and expenses.

"**Senior Credit Agreement**" means the credit agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 among Maker, as borrower, the Company, as guarantor, and the Senior Lender, as amended, restated, refinanced or replaced from time to time.

"**Senior Lender**" means the administrative agent and lenders party from time to time to the Senior Credit Agreement.

## 2. Payment Terms

**2.1 Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts due under this Note, shall be due and payable in full on the date that is twelve (12) months after the Issue Date (August 3, 2027) (the "**Maturity Date**").

**2.2 Interest.** Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the Issue Date at the Interest Rate. Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Accrued interest shall be payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each of October 2026, January 2027, April 2027 and July 2027, and on the Maturity Date.

**2.3 Default Interest.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the unpaid principal balance and, to the extent permitted by law, all overdue interest shall bear interest at the Default Rate.

**2.4 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing. If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day, and interest shall accrue for the additional days.

**2.5 Application of Payments.** Payments shall be applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 7.1, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to principal.

**2.6 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, subject to Section 4. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the principal prepaid.

## 3. Setoff

**3.1 Right of Setoff.** Maker may set off against any amount then due or thereafter becoming due under this Note any amount finally determined to be owed to a Buyer Indemnitee under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, and any amount finally determined to be payable by Holder to Maker under Section 2.5(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

**3.2 Finally Determined.** An amount is "finally determined" when (a) Holder and Maker agree on it in writing, (b) it is determined by a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction, or (c) in the case of Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, Closing Net Working Capital has become final and binding under that Section.

**3.3 Effect of Setoff.** Any amount set off under this Section 3 shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal. It shall be treated as payment of this Note to the extent of the amount set off. Maker shall give Holder written notice of each setoff, specifying the amount and the basis for it. An exercise of setoff in good faith under this Section 3 shall not constitute an Event of Default.

## 4. Subordination

**4.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set forth in the subordination agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 among Holder, Maker and the Senior Lender (the "**Subordination Agreement**").

**4.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or other event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing, and except as otherwise provided in the Subordination Agreement, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, regularly scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note.

**4.3 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment in violation of the Subordination Agreement, Holder shall hold that payment in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly pay it over to the Senior Lender.

**4.4 Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or reorganization proceeding involving Maker, the Senior Debt shall be paid in full in cash before Holder receives any payment under this Note, as provided in the Subordination Agreement.

**4.5 Conflict.** If any provision of this Note conflicts with the Subordination Agreement, the Subordination Agreement shall control as between Holder and the Senior Lender.

## 5. Events of Default

**5.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, whether on the Maturity Date, by acceleration or otherwise;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount due under this Note within five (5) Business Days after the date it is due;

(c) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(d) Maker or the Company commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its assets;

(e) an involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker or the Company under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(f) the Senior Lender accelerates the Senior Debt following an event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement, and the acceleration is not rescinded;

(g) Maker consummates a Change of Control without the prior written consent of the Holder; or

(h) a final judgment for the payment of money in excess of $2,500,000 is entered against Maker or the Company and remains undischarged and unstayed for sixty (60) days.

**5.2 Remedies.** Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 5.1(d) or 5.1(e), the unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts due under this Note, shall automatically become immediately due and payable. Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts due under this Note, immediately due and payable. In each case, Holder's remedies are subject to the Subordination Agreement.

**5.3 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of the occurrence of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default, within five (5) Business Days after an officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

## 6. Covenants of Maker

So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker shall:

(a) maintain its existence as a limited liability company in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) deliver to Holder, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the annual financial statements of Maker that Maker delivers to the Senior Lender; and

(c) not declare or pay any distribution on its equity interests while an Event of Default is continuing.

## 7. General Provisions

**7.1 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs to collect this Note after an Event of Default.

**7.2 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except as expressly required by this Note.

**7.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder and, to the extent required by the Subordination Agreement, consented to by the Senior Lender. No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right under this Note shall operate as a waiver of that right.

**7.4 Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign or transfer this Note, or any interest in it, without the prior written consent of Maker, except to an Affiliate of Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by the Subordination Agreement. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder.

**7.5 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner and to the addresses set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.6 Usury Savings.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

**7.7 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor upon Holder's delivery of a customary affidavit of loss and indemnity.

**7.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**7.9 Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**7.10 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Maker and Holder submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware. MAKER AND HOLDER EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS NOTE.

**7.11 Headings.** Section headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Note.
