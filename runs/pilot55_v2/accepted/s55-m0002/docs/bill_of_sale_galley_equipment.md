# BILL OF SALE

**(Galley Equipment)**

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of August 3, 2026 by **Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Lakeport Skykitchen, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. Background

1.1 Seller and **Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding Shares of the Company from Seller.

1.2 Seller owns certain galley equipment that is used in the Business. Under Section 3.2(a)(iv) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller must deliver at the Closing this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, transferring that equipment to the Company.

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 **Transfer.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the equipment described in Schedule A (collectively, the "**Galley Equipment**"). The transfer is free and clear of all liens and takes effect as of the Closing on the Closing Date.

2.2 **Included Items.** The Galley Equipment includes, for each item listed in Schedule A:

(a) all attachments, accessories, spare parts, tooling and replacement components held for use with that item;

(b) all manufacturer and vendor warranties, to the extent they are assignable; and

(c) all operating manuals, maintenance logs, inspection and certification records, and other documentation relating to that item, in whatever form held by Seller.

2.3 **Consideration.** The consideration for the transfer of the Galley Equipment forms part of the Purchase Price paid under the Purchase Agreement. No additional amount is payable by Buyer or the Company under this Bill of Sale.

## 3. Delivery and Location

3.1 **Possession.** The Galley Equipment is located at the commissary and flight kitchen facilities of the Company listed in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule, as further identified in Schedule A. Seller confirms that possession of the Galley Equipment has been delivered to the Company at those locations as of the Closing.

3.2 **Records.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to the Company any of the documentation described in Section 2.2(c) that is not located at those facilities on the Closing Date.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

The representations and warranties of Seller concerning title to and condition of the assets used in the Business, including those in Section 4.10 of the Purchase Agreement, apply to the Galley Equipment. They survive, and are subject to the indemnification provisions and limitations, as provided in Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. This Bill of Sale does not create any additional representation, warranty or indemnity.

## 5. Further Assurances

At the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of transfer and take such further actions as may be reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Galley Equipment. This includes any filing to release a lien or to record the transfer of any titled or registered item. This Section 5 is in addition to Section 6.11 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, for the limited purpose of demanding and receiving any of the Galley Equipment and endorsing any instrument of transfer relating to it, in each case in the name of Seller and for the benefit of the Company. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

## 7. General

7.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

7.2 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

7.3 **Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of this Bill of Sale shall be brought in the courts specified in Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

7.4 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronic signature has the same effect as an original signature.
