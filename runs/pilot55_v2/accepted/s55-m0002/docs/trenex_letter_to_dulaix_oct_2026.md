This letter is Buyer's Dispute Notice under Section 2.5(b) of the Agreement, delivered on October 9, 2026. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Closing Statement and Amount in Dispute

1.1 The Closing Statement, which Buyer received on September 2, 2026, states Closing Net Working Capital of $5,620,000. That figure is $220,000 above the Target Net Working Capital of $5,400,000.

1.2 Buyer has reviewed the Closing Statement, its supporting schedules and the books and records of the Company. Based on that review, Buyer calculates Closing Net Working Capital, under the Accounting Principles in Schedule 2.4, at $4,470,000.

1.3 The Closing Statement therefore overstates Closing Net Working Capital by $1,150,000. Buyer disputes the full amount of that $1,150,000 net working capital shortfall, which is made up of the items set out in Section 2.

## 2. Disputed Items

2.1 **Inventory: Obsolete and Expired Provisions ($410,000).** The Closing Statement includes $410,000 of food, beverage and dry-store inventory at the Main Commissary and the North Field Flight Kitchen that was past its usable date at the Closing, or that was produced to menu specifications for airline programs ended before the Closing. Schedule 2.4 requires inventory to be reserved where it is obsolete, expired or not saleable in the ordinary course. The Closing Statement records no reserve for these items. Buyer's inventory count of August 4 and 5, 2026 and the related item-level schedule are enclosed as Attachment 1.

2.2 **Accounts Receivable: Aged and Disputed Airline Balances ($385,000).** The Closing Statement includes $385,000 of trade receivables from two airline customers. Each of these balances was more than 120 days past due at the Closing, and in each case the customer had disputed the invoice in writing before the Closing, citing short-loading and missed-flight credits. Schedule 2.4 requires full reserves for balances aged over 120 days and for balances under written customer dispute. The aging report and the customer correspondence are enclosed as Attachment 2.

2.3 **Accrued Payroll and Related Liabilities ($230,000).** The Closing Statement omits $230,000 of accrued but unpaid wages, overtime, shift differentials and employer payroll taxes for commissary and ramp personnel. These amounts relate to the pay period that began July 20, 2026 and ended at the Closing. They also include accrued vacation payable to hourly employees under Company policy. The Company paid these amounts after the Closing. The payroll registers and the vacation accrual calculation are enclosed as Attachment 3.

2.4 **Airport Concession Fee Accrual ($125,000).** The Closing Statement omits $125,000 of percentage concession fees owed to airport authorities on the Company's July 2026 gross receipts under the airport concession agreements listed in Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule. Those fees accrued before the Closing and were invoiced in August 2026. The concession fee statements are enclosed as Attachment 4.

## 3. Requested Next Steps

3.1 Under Section 2.5(c) of the Agreement, Buyer asks Seller to begin good-faith negotiations to resolve the disputed items. We propose a meeting of the parties' finance representatives during the week of October 19, 2026, in person or by video conference.

3.2 If the disputed items are not resolved within the period in Section 2.5(c), Buyer intends to submit them to the Independent Accountant as provided in that Section.

3.3 Once Closing Net Working Capital becomes final, Buyer will expect payment of the resulting shortfall under Section 2.5(d) of the Agreement. Buyer reserves its right of setoff under Section 3.1 of the Seller Note and Section 9.6 of the Agreement for any amount finally determined to be payable by Seller.
