# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 15, 2026 by and between **Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Lakeport Skykitchen, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in airline catering, onboard provisioning and related galley services (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale, the Key Employee Agreement and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale for the galley equipment described in Section 3.2(a)(iv).

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Closing Net Working Capital**" means Current Assets minus Current Liabilities of the Company as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date, determined under the Accounting Principles set forth in Schedule 2.4.

"**Closing Statement**" has the meaning given in Section 2.5(a).

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization), 4.20 (Brokers), 5.1 (Organization of Buyer), 5.2 (Authority of Buyer) and 5.5 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, authority, court or tribunal thereof, including any airport authority.

"**Key Customers**" means the ten largest airline customers of the Company, measured by revenue recognized by the Company for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, as listed in Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Key Employee Agreement**" means the Key Employee Agreement between the Company and Marisol Okafor-Brandt, the Company's Vice President of Operations, effective as of the Closing.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Seller Credit Support Obligations**" means all guarantees, letters of credit, surety bonds and other credit support provided by Seller or any of its Affiliates (other than the Company) in respect of the obligations of the Company under the airport concession agreements and commissary leases listed in Schedule 6.9, together with all obligations of Seller or such Affiliate under that credit support, whether arising before or after the Closing.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the original principal amount of $6,000,000 made by Buyer in favor of Seller, in the form attached as Exhibit A.

"**Target Net Working Capital**" means $5,400,000.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, payroll, employment, property, excise, franchise and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions imposed with respect thereto.

**1.2 Interpretation.** "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,000,000, consisting of $42,000,000 cash and the $6,000,000 Seller Note (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Sections 2.4 and 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall (a) pay to Seller $42,000,000, as adjusted under Section 2.4, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least two Business Days before the Closing Date, and (b) execute and deliver the Seller Note to Seller.

**2.3 Payoff of Indebtedness.** On Seller's behalf, Buyer shall pay from the cash portion of the Purchase Price the amounts set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(vi).

**2.4 Estimated Working Capital.** Not less than three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Net Working Capital ("**Estimated Net Working Capital**"). If Estimated Net Working Capital exceeds Target Net Working Capital, the cash payable at Closing shall be increased by the excess. If it is less than Target Net Working Capital, the cash payable at Closing shall be reduced by the shortfall.

**2.5 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital, prepared under the Accounting Principles, together with reasonable supporting detail (the "**Closing Statement**").

(b) *Dispute Notice.* If Buyer disagrees with the Closing Statement, Buyer may deliver to Seller a written notice of dispute within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Closing Statement (the "**Dispute Notice**"). The Dispute Notice shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for the dispute. If Buyer does not deliver a Dispute Notice within that period, the Closing Statement and Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital shall be final and binding on the parties. Any items not specifically disputed in a timely Dispute Notice shall also be final and binding on the parties.

(c) *Resolution.* Seller and Buyer shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days after delivery of a timely Dispute Notice to resolve the disputed items. Any items still unresolved at the end of that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually acceptable to the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. It shall decide only the disputed items and shall determine each item within the range of the values proposed by the parties. Its determination shall be final and binding on the parties absent manifest error. The parties shall bear the Independent Accountant's fees in inverse proportion to their relative success on the disputed items.

(d) *Payment.* Within five Business Days after Closing Net Working Capital becomes final, (i) if final Closing Net Working Capital is less than Estimated Net Working Capital, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer, and (ii) if it exceeds Estimated Net Working Capital, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. In either case payment shall be made in cash by wire transfer.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable Tax law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on August 3, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(ii) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(iii) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Marisol Okafor-Brandt;

(iv) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, transferring to the Company the galley equipment owned by Seller and used in the Business;

(v) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying Seller's board resolutions authorizing this Agreement;

(vi) customary payoff letters for all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company; and

(vii) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller the cash payment under Section 2.2(a), the Seller Note duly executed by Buyer, and a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer authorizing this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement is a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity of the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement do not conflict with the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, violate any law, or require any consent under any Material Contract.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows, and the unaudited balance sheet as of May 31, 2026 (collectively, the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Company as of their respective dates.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except those reflected in the Financial Statements, those incurred in the ordinary course since May 31, 2026, and those disclosed in Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has operated in the ordinary course and there has been no material adverse effect on the Business.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Company involving annual payments in excess of $250,000, each airline catering agreement, each airport concession agreement and each commissary lease (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.9 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all leased real property of the Company, including each commissary and flight kitchen facility.

**4.10 Title to Assets.** The Company has good title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible assets used in the Business, free and clear of liens other than permitted liens.

**4.11 Compliance; Permits.** The Company is in material compliance with all applicable laws, including food safety, sanitation and airport security requirements. It holds all material permits required to conduct the Business.

**4.12 Customers.** Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the Key Customers and the revenue attributable to each for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. Since January 1, 2026, no Key Customer has terminated, failed to renew or materially reduced its business with the Company or, to Seller's knowledge, notified the Company in writing of an intention to do so.

**4.13 Employees.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Company with annual base compensation over $150,000. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement except as disclosed.

**4.14 Taxes.** (a) The Company has timely filed all income and other material Tax returns required to be filed by it for the tax years 2023 through 2025, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. (b) All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid. (c) No audit or other proceeding by any Governmental Authority is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened with respect to any Taxes of the Company. (d) The Company has not waived any statute of limitations in respect of Taxes. (e) The Company is not party to any Tax sharing or Tax indemnity agreement.

**4.15 Employee Benefits.** Section 4.15 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material benefit plan of the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in material compliance with its terms and applicable law.

**4.16 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company that would reasonably be expected to result in Losses in excess of $100,000.

**4.17 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in material compliance with applicable environmental laws.

**4.18 Insurance.** Section 4.18 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the insurance policies maintained by the Company, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.19 Affiliate Transactions.** Except for the Seller Credit Support Obligations and as set forth in Section 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule, there are no contracts between the Company and Seller or any Affiliate of Seller.

**4.20 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization of Buyer.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority of Buyer.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. Each of them, when executed, will be a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate its organizational documents or any applicable law.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

**5.6 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to distribution.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. It shall use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Company's relationships with its airline customers, suppliers and airport authorities.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books and records of the Company.

**6.3 Confidentiality.** The Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between Trenex Aviation Services Group, Inc. and Seller shall continue to apply to information furnished under this Agreement until the Closing. After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company and the Business.

**6.4 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the third-party and governmental consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or its Business to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Notice of Developments.** Each party shall promptly notify the other of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause a closing condition not to be satisfied.

**6.7 Seller Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation.** For three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, (a) engage in the business of airline catering or onboard provisioning in the United States, or (b) solicit for employment any employee of the Company at the level of manager or above.

**6.8 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release regarding this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

**6.9 Seller Credit Support Obligations.** After the Closing, Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller and its Affiliates to be released from the Seller Credit Support Obligations listed in Schedule 6.9, including by offering replacement credit support of substantially equivalent amount. Until a release is obtained, Buyer shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, extend or materially amend any underlying concession agreement or commissary lease in a manner that increases the exposure of Seller under the Seller Credit Support Obligations.

**6.10 Books and Records.** For six years after the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing. It shall give Seller reasonable access to them on reasonable notice.

**6.11 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out this Agreement.

**6.12 Parent Transaction.** Seller acknowledges that Trenex Aviation Services Group, Inc., the parent of Buyer, has entered into an agreement and plan of merger with Osprey Ridge Capital Partners, under which Trenex Aviation Services Group, Inc. will be acquired (the "**Parent Merger**"). The Parent Merger is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. Seller agrees that the execution of the merger agreement for the Parent Merger, the consummation of the Parent Merger and any resulting change in the ownership or control of Buyer shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement. None of them shall give rise to any right of Seller to terminate this Agreement or to claim indemnification under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Both Parties.** No Governmental Authority shall have enacted or issued any law or order that prohibits the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** (a) The representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date. (b) Seller shall have performed its covenants in all material respects. (c) Seller shall have delivered the items in Section 3.2(a). (d) The consents listed in Schedule 7.2 shall have been obtained.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** (a) The representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date. (b) Buyer shall have performed its covenants in all material respects. (c) Buyer shall have delivered the items in Section 3.2(b).

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by September 30, 2026, provided the terminating party's breach did not cause the failure; or (c) by either party upon a material uncured breach by the other party that would cause a closing condition to fail.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2, Section 6.3 and Article XI shall survive. No party shall be relieved of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties of the parties, other than the Fundamental Representations and the representations and warranties in Section 4.14, shall survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The representations and warranties in Section 4.14 (Taxes) shall survive the Closing until the Maturity Date of the Seller Note.

(c) The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms.

(d) A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any period, or portion of any period, ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) the Seller Credit Support Obligations, including any payment made or liability incurred by any Seller Indemnitee under the Seller Credit Support Obligations.

**9.4 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim. Delay does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It may not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, until the aggregate Losses exceed $360,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount. Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, shall not exceed $4,800,000. Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(b) Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3 shall not exceed $4,800,000; provided that this limitation shall not apply to Losses under Section 9.3(c).

(c) No party shall be liable for punitive damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.6 Right of Setoff.** Buyer may set off any amount finally determined to be owed to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX against any amount then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the procedures in Section 2.5, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties after the Closing with respect to this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** Any indemnification payment under this Article IX shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Returns.** Seller shall prepare all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date. It shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least twenty (20) days before filing.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Taxes for any period beginning before and ending after the Closing Date shall be apportioned between the pre-Closing and post-Closing portions. Property Taxes shall be apportioned ratably by days. All other Taxes shall be apportioned on a closing-of-the-books basis.

**10.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate as reasonably requested in connection with Tax returns and any audit or proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company.

**10.4 Transfer Taxes.** Transfer, documentary, sales, use and similar Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. However, Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or, as collateral, to its lenders, without consent.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.
