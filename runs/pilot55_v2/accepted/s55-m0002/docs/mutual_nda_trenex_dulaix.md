# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 6, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Trenex Aviation Services Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Trenex**"), and **Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Dulaix**"). Each of Trenex and Dulaix is a "**Party**," and together they are the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction under which Trenex or one of its Affiliates would acquire **Lakeport Skykitchen, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Dulaix (the "**Company**"), or all or part of its business (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

1.2 In the course of that evaluation, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. This Agreement sets out the terms on which that information is disclosed, received and protected.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. With respect to Trenex, this includes Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC.

2.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that a Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") furnishes to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Proposed Transaction, whether before, on or after the Effective Date. It includes:

(a) in the case of Dulaix, information concerning the Company, including airline customer lists, catering agreements and pricing, menu and provisioning specifications, airport concession agreements, commissary leases, galley equipment, employee and compensation data, financial statements, Tax information and forecasts;

(b) in the case of Trenex, information concerning Trenex and its Affiliates, including their business plans, financing arrangements, integration plans and financial information; and

(c) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of that information (the "**Derivative Materials**").

2.3 "**Transaction Information**" means the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, the fact that the Parties are discussing the Proposed Transaction, and the status and terms of those discussions. Transaction Information is Confidential Information of each Party.

2.4 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before it was disclosed under this Agreement;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, is not bound by a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

2.5 "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

2.6 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their respective directors, officers, managers, employees, agents, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors and, in the case of Trenex, actual or prospective financing sources.

## 3. Use and Protection of Confidential Information

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and complete the Proposed Transaction, and for no other purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential. It shall not disclose that information to any Person except to its Representatives who need to know it for the purpose in Section 3.1 and who are informed of its confidential nature. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.4 **Customer and Airport Contacts.** Without Dulaix's prior written consent, Trenex shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, contact any airline customer, supplier, airport authority, landlord or employee of the Company about the Proposed Transaction or the Company's business, other than in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Proposed Transaction.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

4.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule or legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice. That notice is intended to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other remedy at its own expense.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall reasonably cooperate with those efforts. If no protective order is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the information disclosed will be treated confidentially.

## 5. Return and Destruction

5.1 Within ten (10) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing.

5.2 The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policies, or in automatic electronic back-up systems. Any retained information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained.

## 6. Non-Solicitation of Employees

For eighteen (18) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, and each Party shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any officer or management-level employee of the other Party or, in the case of Trenex, of the Company, with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Proposed Transaction. This Section 6 does not prohibit (a) general solicitations not directed at those employees, (b) hiring any person who responds to such a general solicitation, or (c) hiring any person whose employment ended at least six (6) months earlier. This Section 6 does not restrict any arrangement made with a Company employee in connection with the Proposed Transaction with Dulaix's knowledge.

## 7. No Representations; No Obligation

7.1 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement for the Proposed Transaction executed by the Parties or their Affiliates (a "**Definitive Agreement**").

7.2 **No Obligation to Proceed.** No agreement providing for the Proposed Transaction exists unless and until a Definitive Agreement has been executed and delivered. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other.

7.3 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. This Agreement grants no license or other right in any Confidential Information, except the limited right of use in Section 3.1.

## 8. Privileged Information

To the extent any Confidential Information includes material subject to the attorney-client privilege, work product doctrine or any other applicable privilege, the Parties share a common legal and commercial interest in that material. Its disclosure is not intended to waive, and shall not waive, any privilege or protection.

## 9. Term

9.1 This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years after the Effective Date.

9.2 Termination or expiration of this Agreement does not affect any obligation with respect to Confidential Information retained under Section 5.2, which continues for so long as that information is retained. It also does not affect any claim for breach occurring before termination or expiration.

## 10. Remedies

10.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

10.2 In any action to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing Party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs if a court of competent jurisdiction finally determines that the other Party breached this Agreement.

## 11. General

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

11.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email to the following addresses, or to any other address a Party designates by notice:

If to Dulaix: Dulaix Hospitality Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, legal@dulaixhospitality.com

If to Trenex: Trenex Aviation Services Group, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, with a copy to Trenex Flight Provisions, LLC, Attention: Chief Financial Officer, finance@trenexflight.com

11.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, Trenex may assign its rights and obligations to any Affiliate that pursues the Proposed Transaction, and Trenex shall remain responsible for that Affiliate's compliance.

11.5 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

11.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

11.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
