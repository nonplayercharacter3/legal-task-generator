# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this **"Amendment"**) is entered into as of June 15, 2025 (the **"Amendment Effective Date"**) between **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**). Customer and Supplier are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**). Under it, Supplier supplies water and wastewater treatment chemicals to Customer.

B. Supplier has agreed to install, at its own cost, dedicated on-site bulk storage tanks for ferric chloride and polyaluminum chloride at certain Listed Plants. The tanks will increase on-site inventory and reduce the frequency of deliveries.

C. Supplier requires a longer contract term to amortize its investment in the bulk storage tanks. The Parties have agreed to amend the term and renewal provisions of the Supply Agreement for that purpose.

D. Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement permits the Supply Agreement to be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 This Amendment is an amendment under Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement and forms part of "this Agreement" as defined in Section 1.4 of the Supply Agreement.

1.3 Section 1.15 (Interpretation) of the Supply Agreement applies to this Amendment. References in this Amendment to Sections are to sections of this Amendment unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Amendment to Section 2.1 (Initial Term)

2.1 Section 2.1 of the Supply Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date, March 1, 2024. It continues for an initial term of seven (7) years from the Effective Date (the **"Initial Term"**)."

## 3. Amendment to Section 2.2 (Renewal)

3.1 Section 2.2 of the Supply Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms, each equal in length to the Initial Term (each a **"Renewal Term"**). It does not renew if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term."

## 4. Bulk Storage Tanks

4.1 **Installation.** Supplier shall supply and install one dedicated bulk storage tank for ferric chloride and one dedicated bulk storage tank for polyaluminum chloride (together with associated level instrumentation, fill connections and venting, the **"Tanks"**) at each of the following Listed Plants:

(a) Cedar Bend Water Treatment Plant;

(b) North Haverly Water Treatment Plant;

(c) Ridgecrest Industrial Water Plant; and

(d) Lake Orrin Water Treatment Plant.

Supplier shall complete installation at each of those Listed Plants by December 31, 2025, on dates agreed with the operations supervisor of the Listed Plant.

4.2 **Cost.** Supplier shall bear all costs of designing, fabricating, delivering, installing and commissioning the Tanks. The installation of the Tanks does not change the prices set out in Order Form No. 1. Prices remain subject to adjustment only in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement.

4.3 **Site Works.** Customer shall provide, at its cost, a suitable foundation pad and secondary containment for each Tank, in accordance with drawings approved by both Parties' Quality Representatives under the Quality Agreement. Supplier's personnel and contractors shall comply with Section 8.3 of the Supply Agreement while performing installation work at any Customer Site.

4.4 **Ownership and Maintenance.** Title to the Tanks remains with Supplier. Supplier shall inspect and maintain the Tanks, and their level instrumentation, in good working order and in compliance with applicable law, at its own cost. Customer shall not modify or relocate any Tank without Supplier's prior written consent, which Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold.

4.5 **Removal.** Within 90 days after the expiry or termination of the Supply Agreement, Supplier shall remove the Tanks at its own cost, after Customer has emptied them, and shall restore the affected areas to their condition before installation, fair wear and tear excepted.

4.6 **Inventory Management.** Supplier shall monitor Product levels in the Tanks using the level instrumentation. Supplier shall notify Customer's operations supervisor at each relevant Listed Plant when the level in a Tank falls below 30% of its working capacity. Customer shall continue to order Products by Purchase Order under Section 4.1 of the Supply Agreement and Section 6 of Order Form No. 1.

## 5. General

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Supply Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Supply Agreement to "this Agreement", and each reference in any Order Form or the Quality Agreement to the Supply Agreement, means the Supply Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **Parent Guaranty.** Supplier shall procure that Taipux Holdings, Inc. delivers to Customer, within ten Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, a written acknowledgment that the Parent Guaranty dated as of March 1, 2024 continues to apply to Supplier's obligations under the Supply Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.3 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law) and 16.2 (Dispute Resolution) of the Supply Agreement apply to this Amendment. This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

5.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
