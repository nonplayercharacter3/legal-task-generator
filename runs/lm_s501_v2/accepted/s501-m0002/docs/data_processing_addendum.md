# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this **"Addendum"**) is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Addendum Effective Date"**) between **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**). Customer and Supplier are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Customer and Supplier have entered into a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**). Under it, Supplier supplies water and wastewater treatment chemicals to Customer.

B. In performing the Supply Agreement, each Party will receive limited personal data about the other Party's personnel. This includes contact details for account management, ordering and quality matters, and identification details of carrier drivers and installation personnel who attend Customer Sites.

C. Section 13.3 of the Supply Agreement requires the Parties to comply with the Data Processing Addendum entered into between them. This Addendum is that Data Processing Addendum.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 In this Addendum:

(a) **"Data Protection Laws"** means all laws of the United States and its states that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this Addendum. These include, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations, and other state consumer privacy and data breach notification laws.

(b) **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that one Party (the **"Disclosing Party"**) discloses to, or makes available to, the other Party (the **"Receiving Party"**) in connection with the Supply Agreement.

(c) **"Process"** and **"processing"** mean any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, transfer, retrieval and deletion.

(d) **"Security Incident"** means any accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Personal Data in the Receiving Party's possession or control.

1.3 Section 1.15 (Interpretation) of the Supply Agreement applies to this Addendum. References to Sections are to sections of this Addendum unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 **Categories of Personal Data.** The Personal Data processed under this Addendum is limited to the following:

(a) names, job titles, business email addresses, business telephone numbers and business addresses of each Party's employees and contractors involved in ordering, delivery, invoicing, quality and account management;

(b) for carrier drivers and Supplier personnel attending a Customer Site, names, employer, vehicle and trailer identification, commercial driver's license number where required by Customer's site security procedures, and times of entry and exit; and

(c) signatures, electronic or otherwise, on delivery documents, Certificates of Analysis and other records exchanged under the Supply Agreement and the Quality Agreement.

2.2 **No Sensitive Data.** Neither Party shall disclose to the other any Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, health information, biometric data or other sensitive personal information, except as strictly required by applicable law or Customer's site security procedures.

2.3 **Independent Controllers.** Each Party processes the other Party's Personal Data as an independent business or controller for its own purposes in performing the Supply Agreement. Nothing in this Addendum makes either Party a service provider, processor or joint controller of the other.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

3.1 **Permitted Purposes.** The Receiving Party shall process Personal Data only for the following purposes:

(a) performing and administering the Supply Agreement, the Quality Agreement and each Order Form, including ordering, delivery, invoicing, quality management and incident reporting;

(b) maintaining site safety and security at Customer Sites, in the case of Customer;

(c) complying with applicable law, including U.S. Department of Transportation and Environmental Law recordkeeping and reporting requirements; and

(d) establishing, exercising or defending legal claims relating to the Supply Agreement.

3.2 **Compliance with Law.** Each Party shall comply with the Data Protection Laws in its processing of Personal Data. Each Party shall give any notices to its own personnel that the Data Protection Laws require before disclosing their Personal Data to the other Party.

3.3 **No Sale or Sharing.** The Receiving Party shall not sell Personal Data, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in the Data Protection Laws.

3.4 **Personnel.** The Receiving Party shall limit access to Personal Data to those of its and its Affiliates' employees and contractors who need access for the purposes in Section 3.1 and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Addendum.

3.5 **Service Providers.** The Receiving Party may disclose Personal Data to its service providers, including IT, logistics and document management providers, only for the purposes in Section 3.1. The Receiving Party shall bind each such service provider by a written agreement containing data protection obligations no less protective than this Addendum. The Receiving Party remains responsible for its service providers' processing of Personal Data.

3.6 **Location.** The Receiving Party shall process and store Personal Data only in the United States unless the Disclosing Party has agreed otherwise in writing.

## 4. Security

4.1 **Safeguards.** The Receiving Party shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Personal Data. These safeguards must protect Personal Data against Security Incidents and must include at least the following:

(a) access controls limiting access to authorized personnel;

(b) encryption of Personal Data in transit over public networks;

(c) secure disposal of paper and electronic records containing Personal Data; and

(d) security awareness training for personnel with access to Personal Data.

4.2 **Security Incidents.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting the Disclosing Party's Personal Data. The notice must describe, to the extent known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate number of individuals affected, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. The Receiving Party shall update the notice as further information becomes available.

4.3 **Cooperation.** The Receiving Party shall take reasonable steps to contain and remediate each Security Incident. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in any notification to individuals or regulators that the Data Protection Laws require. The Receiving Party shall bear the reasonable costs of those notifications to the extent the Security Incident resulted from its breach of this Addendum.

## 5. Individual Requests

5.1 If a Party receives a request from an individual to exercise rights under the Data Protection Laws for Personal Data disclosed by the other Party, it shall respond to the request in accordance with the Data Protection Laws. Where reasonably necessary, it shall inform the other Party of the request within ten Business Days after receipt.

5.2 Each Party shall provide reasonable assistance to the other to enable the other to respond to individual requests and regulatory inquiries relating to Personal Data processed under this Addendum.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

6.1 The Receiving Party shall retain Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in Section 3.1, or as required by applicable law. Personal Data contained in records that must be retained under Section 10.1 of the Quality Agreement may be retained for the period stated in that Section.

6.2 Within 90 days after the expiry or termination of the Supply Agreement, the Receiving Party shall securely delete or return all Personal Data, at the Disclosing Party's election, except Personal Data that it must retain under Section 6.1. Section 13 of the Supply Agreement and this Addendum continue to apply to any Personal Data retained.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Each Party is responsible for its own compliance with the Data Protection Laws and this Addendum.

7.2 Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum is subject to Section 11 of the Supply Agreement.

## 8. Term

8.1 This Addendum takes effect on the Addendum Effective Date and remains in effect for as long as the Supply Agreement is in effect, and afterward for as long as either Party retains Personal Data of the other.

8.2 If a change in the Data Protection Laws requires a change to this Addendum, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Addendum to the extent necessary to comply. Any amendment must comply with Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement.

## 9. General

9.1 **Incorporation.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law), 16.2 (Dispute Resolution), 16.4 (Notices) and 16.9 (Counterparts) of the Supply Agreement apply to this Addendum as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Addendum.

9.2 **Privacy Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a privacy contact to receive notices under Sections 4.2 and 5.1, and shall give the other Party that contact's details within ten Business Days after the Addendum Effective Date. Either Party may change its privacy contact by written notice to the other.

9.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Addendum is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.
