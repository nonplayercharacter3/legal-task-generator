# TERM SHEET

## Proposed Supply of Water Treatment Coagulants

**Vaibur Water Services, LLC – Taipux Chemical Corp.**

Date: January 18, 2024

**Status:** Non-binding summary of principal terms, except as stated in Section 15

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This term sheet (this **"Term Sheet"**) records the principal terms on which **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**), propose to enter into a supply agreement for water treatment chemicals (the **"Supply Agreement"**).

1.2 The Supply Agreement will be supported by an initial order form (**"Order Form No. 1"**), a quality agreement (the **"Quality Agreement"**) and a parent guaranty from **Taipux Holdings, Inc.** (the **"Guarantor"**).

1.3 Customer's counsel will prepare the first draft of the definitive documents. This Term Sheet is to be used as the basis for that draft.

## 2. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Customer | Vaibur Water Services, LLC |
| Supplier | Taipux Chemical Corp. |
| Guarantor | Taipux Holdings, Inc., parent of Supplier |

## 3. Products, Listed Plants and Exclusivity

3.1 **Products.** The initial products are:

(a) ferric chloride solution, 38%–42% FeCl₃, in bulk tanker and 275-gallon IBC tote; and

(b) polyaluminum chloride (PACl), 10.0%–10.5% Al₂O₃, medium basicity, in bulk tanker and 275-gallon IBC tote.

3.2 **Listed Plants.** The initial delivery locations are the following six plants (the **"Listed Plants"**), to be listed in a schedule to Order Form No. 1:

| No. | Plant | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cedar Bend Water Treatment Plant | Cedar Bend, Ohio |
| 2 | North Haverly Water Treatment Plant | Haverly, Pennsylvania |
| 3 | Millbrook Regional Water Reclamation Facility | Millbrook, Indiana |
| 4 | Ridgecrest Industrial Water Plant | Ridgecrest, Texas |
| 5 | Lake Orrin Water Treatment Plant | Orrin, Michigan |
| 6 | South Fork Wastewater Treatment Plant | South Fork, Kentucky |

3.3 **Exclusivity.** Customer will buy its requirements exclusively from Supplier for ferric chloride and polyaluminum chloride coagulants for the six Listed Plants only. Customer remains free to buy all other chemicals, and to buy any chemicals for any other plant or facility, from any supplier.

3.4 **Additional Products and Plants.** The Parties may add products or plants by a further order form signed by both Parties. Adding a product or plant does not extend the exclusivity in Section 3.3 unless the Parties expressly agree in writing.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 **Initial Term.** Three years from the effective date of the Supply Agreement, expected to be March 1, 2024.

4.2 **Renewal.** Automatic renewal for successive one-year terms, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 5. Forecasts and Capacity

5.1 Customer will provide monthly non-binding rolling three-month forecasts by Listed Plant. There is no minimum purchase commitment.

5.2 Supplier will maintain production capacity, inventory and logistics resources sufficient to meet Customer's forecast requirements plus 20%.

5.3 For potable water products, Supplier will give priority to Customer's orders over orders of customers that do not provide essential public services.

## 6. Pricing

6.1 **Initial Prices.**

| Product | Packaging | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Ferric chloride solution | Bulk tanker | $485.00 per dry ton FeCl₃ |
| Ferric chloride solution | IBC tote | $560.00 per dry ton FeCl₃ |
| Polyaluminum chloride | Bulk tanker | $395.00 per wet ton |
| Polyaluminum chloride | IBC tote | $445.00 per wet ton |

6.2 **Inclusions.** Prices are delivered prices and include freight, packaging, fuel surcharges and delivery charges. Sales and use taxes are extra.

6.3 **Price Firmness and Adjustment.** Prices are fixed for the first 12 months. After that, Supplier may adjust prices no more than once in each 12-month period, on at least 60 days' written notice with supporting documentation, and only to reflect documented changes in raw material, energy or freight costs.

## 7. Delivery and Payment

7.1 **Delivery.** DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the receiving point at each Listed Plant. Title and risk pass on completion of offloading into Customer's storage.

7.2 **Lead Times.** Bulk tanker loads within three business days, and totes within five business days, after receipt of a purchase order. Supplier will use best efforts to meet emergency orders for delivery within 24 hours.

7.3 **Payment.** Net 45 days from invoice receipt. Customer may withhold disputed amounts in good faith.

## 8. Quality

8.1 Products must conform to agreed specifications, ANSI/AWWA Standards B407 (ferric chloride) and B408 (polyaluminum chloride), and NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 at the agreed maximum use doses.

8.2 Supplier will maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification. Each delivery will include a certificate of analysis and a current safety data sheet.

8.3 The detailed quality, testing, change control, recall and handling provisions will be set out in the Quality Agreement.

## 9. Supply Failure

9.1 If a shortfall continues for more than five days, Customer may buy replacement product from third parties, and Supplier will reimburse any excess cost.

9.2 Customer may terminate for repeated supply failure: three or more failures to deliver conforming product in any rolling 90-day period, or a shortfall continuing for more than 30 days.

## 10. Indemnities and Liability

10.1 **Supplier Indemnities.** Supplier will indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from breach of product warranties, bodily injury or property damage caused by the products or Supplier's personnel and carriers, and Supplier's violation of law. Supplier will also indemnify Customer against environmental claims arising from releases before title and risk pass, including during transport and offloading, and from non-conforming product.

10.2 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, subject to exclusions customary for a chemical supply agreement, to be agreed in the definitive documents.

10.3 **Consequential Damages.** A mutual exclusion of indirect and consequential damages, subject to the same exclusions.

## 11. Insurance

Supplier will maintain commercial general liability ($5,000,000), pollution legal liability including transportation pollution ($10,000,000), automobile liability with MCS-90 ($2,000,000), workers' compensation and employer's liability ($1,000,000) and umbrella liability ($10,000,000) coverage, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best. Customer and its affiliates will be named as additional insureds where customary.

## 12. Parent Guaranty

The Guarantor will guarantee the payment and performance of all of Supplier's obligations under the Supply Agreement, the Quality Agreement and each order form.

## 13. Assignment

Neither Party may assign the Supply Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. Assignment of the Supply Agreement to affiliates will be addressed in the definitive documents.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

14.1 The Supply Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

14.2 Disputes will be escalated to senior executives for 30 days. After that, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

## 15. Binding Provisions

15.1 This Term Sheet is a non-binding expression of the Parties' current intentions. No Party is bound to negotiate or enter into any definitive agreement, except under Sections 15.2 and 15.3.

15.2 **Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep confidential the terms of this Term Sheet and all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with the proposed transaction.

15.3 **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the definitive documents.

15.4 Sections 15.2 and 15.3 are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
