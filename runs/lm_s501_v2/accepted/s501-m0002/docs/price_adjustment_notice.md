Dear Sir or Madam:

Taipux Chemical Corp. (**"Supplier"**) gives this notice (this **"Notice"**) to Vaibur Water Services, LLC (**"Customer"**) under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**).

## 1. Purpose and Interpretation

1.1 **Purpose.** This Notice gives Customer written notice under Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement of an adjustment to the prices of the Products set out in Schedule 2 to Order Form No. 1.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Notice have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **Delivery.** This Notice is delivered in accordance with Section 16.4 of the Supply Agreement. It is sent by nationally recognized overnight courier and by email, with confirmation copy by courier.

## 2. Basis for the Adjustment

2.1 **Timing.** The prices in Schedule 2 to Order Form No. 1 were fixed until March 1, 2025, the first anniversary of the Effective Date, under Section 5.2 of Order Form No. 1. Supplier has not adjusted any price under Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement in the 12-month period beginning on March 1, 2025. This Notice is Supplier's only price adjustment for that period.

2.2 **Cost Changes.** The adjustment reflects only documented changes in Supplier's costs of raw materials, energy and freight between the first quarter of 2024 and the third quarter of 2025. In summary:

(a) **Raw materials.** Supplier's average delivered cost of hydrochloric acid (32% basis) has increased. For ferric chloride, so has its cost of iron feedstock. For polyaluminum chloride, so has its cost of aluminum hydroxide (alumina trihydrate).

(b) **Energy.** Supplier's average cost of natural gas and electricity at its production facilities supplying the Products has increased.

(c) **Freight.** Supplier's average cost of bulk tanker and van freight to the Listed Plants has increased. This reflects higher contracted carrier rates and diesel fuel costs.

2.3 **Supporting Documentation.** The cost changes and their effect on the prices of each Product are summarized in Annex A. Annex B lists the supporting documentation enclosed with this Notice. The enclosed documentation is Supplier's Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Supply Agreement.

3.2 **Price Adjustment Date.** The adjusted prices apply to Products delivered under Purchase Orders issued on or after February 16, 2026 (the **"Price Adjustment Date"**). That date is more than 60 days after the date of this Notice. Products delivered under Purchase Orders issued before the Price Adjustment Date will be invoiced at the current prices.

3.3 **Price Inclusions.** As provided in Section 6.1 of the Supply Agreement, the adjusted prices include all freight, packaging, fuel surcharges and delivery charges to Customer Sites. Supplier will not charge any separate surcharge for the cost changes described in Section 2.2.

3.4 **Taxes.** The adjusted prices are in U.S. dollars and exclude sales and use taxes, which remain governed by Section 6.4 of the Supply Agreement.

## 4. Other Terms Unchanged

4.1 This Notice adjusts only the prices set out in Section 3.1. It does not change the Specifications, packaging, maximum use doses, estimated volumes, lead times, delivery terms or any other term of Order Form No. 1 or the Supply Agreement.

4.2 Invoices issued at the adjusted prices remain payable net 45 days from invoice receipt, in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Supply Agreement and Section 7.2 of Order Form No. 1.

## 5. Review Meeting

5.1 Under Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement, Customer may request a meeting to review this adjustment. If Customer makes that request, Supplier will meet with Customer within 15 days after the request, in person or by video conference.

5.2 Supplier's Key Account Manager, Municipal Water, will coordinate the meeting and respond to any questions on the supporting documentation. Requests may be sent to the Key Account Manager, with a copy to Supplier's Legal Department.
