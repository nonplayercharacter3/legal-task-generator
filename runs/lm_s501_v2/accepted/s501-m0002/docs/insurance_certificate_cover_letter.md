Taipux Chemical Corp. (**"Supplier"**) sends this letter (this **"Letter"**) to Vaibur Water Services, LLC (**"Customer"**) under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**).

## 1. Purpose and Interpretation

1.1 **Purpose.** Supplier's insurance program renews on March 1, 2026. Under Section 9.2 of the Supply Agreement, Supplier must deliver certificates of insurance on each renewal of coverage. This Letter encloses the certificates for the renewed policies.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **Delivery.** This Letter is delivered in accordance with Section 16.4 of the Supply Agreement. It is sent by nationally recognized overnight courier and by email, with confirmation copy by courier.

## 2. Summary of Renewed Coverage

2.1 **Policy Period.** Each policy listed below runs for the policy period from March 1, 2026 to March 1, 2027, at 12:01 a.m. local time at Supplier's mailing address.

2.2 **Coverage.** The following table summarizes the renewed coverage against the minimum requirements of Section 9.1 of the Supply Agreement:

| Supply Agreement Section | Coverage | Insurer | A.M. Best Rating | Limits | Section 9.1 Minimum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1(a) | Commercial general liability, including products and completed operations | Harrowgate Mutual Insurance Company | A XV | $5,000,000 each occurrence; $5,000,000 general aggregate; $5,000,000 products and completed operations aggregate | $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate |
| 9.1(b) | Pollution legal liability, including transportation pollution coverage | Corrigan Specialty Insurance Company | A+ XV | $10,000,000 each pollution condition; $10,000,000 aggregate | $10,000,000 per claim |
| 9.1(c) | Commercial automobile liability, with MCS-90 endorsement | Harrowgate Mutual Insurance Company | A XV | $2,000,000 combined single limit | $2,000,000 combined single limit |
| 9.1(d) | Workers' compensation and employer's liability | Fennimore Casualty Company | A- VIII | Workers' compensation: statutory; employer's liability: $1,000,000 each accident, $1,000,000 disease each employee, $1,000,000 disease policy limit | Statutory; $1,000,000 |

2.3 **Insurer Ratings.** Each insurer listed in Section 2.2 is rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best as of the date of this Letter, as required by Section 9.1 of the Supply Agreement.
