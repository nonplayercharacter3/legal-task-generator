# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Effective Date"**) between **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**). Customer and Supplier are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Customer operates municipal and industrial water and wastewater treatment plants and buys chemicals for use in those plants.

B. Supplier manufactures and distributes water and wastewater treatment chemicals.

C. Customer wishes to buy, and Supplier wishes to supply, those chemicals on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **"Affiliate"** means, for a person, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 **"Customer Sites"** means the plants, facilities and other delivery locations of Customer or its Affiliates that are identified in an Order Form or Purchase Order.

1.3 **"Environmental Claim"** means any claim, demand, action, proceeding, order, fine, penalty, remediation obligation, cost or liability arising under any Environmental Law. It includes claims for investigation, cleanup, natural resource damages, personal injury and property damage that result from the presence, release, spill, discharge or threatened release of any Product or other hazardous substance.

1.4 **"this Agreement"** means this Supply Agreement, together with the Quality Agreement, each Order Form, and each amendment, side letter, schedule and exhibit entered into under or in connection with this Supply Agreement.

1.5 **"Environmental Law"** means any federal, state or local law, regulation, permit or binding order relating to pollution, the protection of the environment or human health, or the handling, transportation, storage, release or disposal of hazardous substances. This includes the Clean Water Act, the Safe Drinking Water Act, CERCLA, RCRA and their state counterparts.

1.6 **Order of Precedence.** If the documents making up this Agreement conflict, they prevail in the following order: amendments and side letters, then Supply Agreement, then Quality Agreement, then Order Forms.

1.7 **"Listed Plants"** means the treatment plants identified in Schedule 1 to Order Form No. 1.

1.8 **"Order Form"** means an order form executed by both Parties under this Agreement, beginning with Order Form No. 1. Each Order Form sets out Products, prices, estimated volumes and Customer Sites.

1.9 **"Treatment Chemicals"** means all chemicals used in the treatment of water or wastewater. This includes coagulants, flocculants, disinfectants, oxidants, pH adjustment and alkalinity chemicals, corrosion and scale inhibitors, dechlorination agents, odor control chemicals and sludge conditioning chemicals.

1.10 **"Products"** means the Treatment Chemicals listed in an Order Form, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.11 **"Purchase Order"** means a written or electronic release issued by Customer for delivery of Products under an Order Form.

1.12 **"Supplier Group"** means Supplier and its Affiliates.

1.13 **"Quality Agreement"** means the Quality Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated on or about the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

1.14 **"Specifications"** means the specifications for each Product set out in the Quality Agreement or the applicable Order Form. These include conformance with NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 where the Product is intended for potable water treatment.

1.15 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation". References to Sections are to sections of this Supply Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date, March 1, 2024. It continues for an initial term of three years from the Effective Date (the **"Initial Term"**).

2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms of one year each (each a **"Renewal Term"**). It does not renew if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Order Forms.** The expiry or termination of this Agreement also terminates every Order Form then in effect. Purchase Orders accepted before expiry or termination will still be fulfilled on the terms of this Agreement.

## 3. Exclusivity and Forecasts

3.1 **Exclusive Purchase.** During the term, Customer shall purchase 100% of its requirements of all Treatment Chemicals exclusively from Supplier, for every facility owned, operated or managed by Customer or its Affiliates in the United States. Customer shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, purchase Treatment Chemicals for any such facility from any other person.

3.2 **Forecasts.** By the 15th day of each calendar month, Customer shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its estimated Product requirements for the following three months, broken down by Customer Site. Forecasts are for planning purposes only and do not commit Customer to purchase any quantity.

3.3 **Supplier Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain enough production capacity, inventory and logistics resources to meet Customer's forecast requirements plus 20%.

## 4. Ordering and Supply

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that refer to the applicable Order Form. Supplier shall acknowledge each Purchase Order within two Business Days. A Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to Customer's most recent forecast is deemed accepted on receipt. Any pre-printed or additional terms on a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

4.2 **Supply Commitment.** Supplier shall supply all Products ordered under accepted Purchase Orders in the quantities and on the delivery dates stated in them. For potable water Products, Supplier shall give priority to Customer's orders over orders of Supplier's other customers that do not provide essential public services.

4.3 **Alternate Sourcing.** If a supply interruption at a Supplier production facility affects any Product, Supplier may fill Customer's Purchase Orders for that Product from production or inventory of any member of the Supplier Group. Products supplied under this Section 4.3 must conform to the Specifications and the Quality Agreement. Supplier remains responsible for them as if it had supplied them itself. Supplier shall notify Customer in writing of any alternate source within two Business Days after first using it.

4.4 **Shortfall.** If Supplier cannot deliver any Product in the quantity or by the date required, it shall notify Customer immediately. If the shortfall continues for more than five days, Customer may buy replacement Product from a third party for the period of the shortfall. Supplier shall reimburse Customer for any amount by which the cost of the replacement Product, including freight, exceeds the price under this Agreement.

## 5. Delivery, Title and Risk

5.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Customer Site named in each Purchase Order. Delivery is by bulk tanker, tote or drum, as stated in the Order Form. Supplier's carriers shall comply with Customer's site safety rules and offloading procedures.

5.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of each Product pass to Customer when the Product has been fully offloaded into Customer's storage at the Customer Site.

5.3 **Documentation.** Each delivery must include a bill of lading, a certificate of analysis and a current safety data sheet. Where applicable, it must also include NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 certification documentation.

## 6. Prices and Payment

6.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices set out in the applicable Order Form. Prices include all freight, packaging, fuel surcharges and delivery charges to Customer Sites.

6.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer after each delivery. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice net 45 days from invoice receipt. If Customer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Supplier before the payment date and shall pay the undisputed amount. The Parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 16.2.

6.3 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust prices no more than once in each 12-month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it. To do so, Supplier must give Customer at least 60 days' written notice under Section 16.4. The adjustment may reflect only documented changes in Supplier's costs of raw materials, energy or freight. The notice must include reasonable supporting documentation. Customer may request a meeting to review an adjustment, and the Parties shall meet within 15 days after that request.

6.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales and use taxes. Supplier shall itemize those taxes on its invoices. Customer shall pay them unless it provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 7. Quality and Warranties

7.1 **Quality Agreement.** Supplier shall manufacture, test, package, store and deliver all Products in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

7.2 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product delivered under this Agreement will meet all of the following requirements:

(a) it will conform to the Specifications at delivery and throughout its stated shelf life;

(b) it will be free from contamination and adulteration;

(c) where intended for potable water treatment, it will be certified to NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 at the maximum use dose stated in the Order Form; and

(d) it will be manufactured, packaged, labeled and transported in compliance with all applicable laws, including Environmental Laws and U.S. Department of Transportation hazardous materials regulations.

7.3 **Remedies.** For any Product that does not conform to Section 7.2, Supplier shall do both of the following at Customer's option and at Supplier's cost:

(a) replace the Product or refund its price; and

(b) remove the non-conforming Product from the Customer Site, and reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of handling, storing and disposing of it and of any resulting additional treatment.

These remedies are in addition to Customer's other rights under this Agreement.

7.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 8. Compliance and Safety

8.1 **Compliance with Laws.** Each Party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement.

8.2 **Regulatory Changes.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any regulatory change, recall, loss of certification or other event that is reasonably likely to affect the supply, Specifications or lawful use of any Product.

8.3 **Site Access.** While at a Customer Site, Supplier's personnel and carriers shall comply with Customer's safety, security and environmental procedures.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 **Required Coverage.** Throughout the term and for two years afterward, Supplier shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;

(b) pollution legal liability insurance, including transportation pollution coverage, of at least $10,000,000 per claim;

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 combined single limit, with MCS-90 endorsement;

(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000; and

(e) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $10,000,000.

9.2 **Certificates.** Supplier shall name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 9.1(a), (b), (c) and (e). Supplier shall deliver certificates of insurance on or before the Effective Date and on each renewal of coverage. Supplier shall give Customer at least 30 days' prior written notice of the cancellation or material reduction of any required coverage.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **General Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees. This indemnity covers claims arising out of:

(a) any breach of Supplier's warranties under Section 7.2;

(b) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by any Product or by the acts or omissions of Supplier, its carriers or its personnel; and

(c) Supplier's violation of applicable law.

10.2 **Environmental Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, employees and agents from and against any Environmental Claim to the extent it arises from either of the following:

(a) the presence, release, spill or discharge of any Product before title and risk pass to Customer under Section 5.2, including during transportation and offloading; or

(b) any non-conformity of a Product with the Specifications.

10.3 **Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification shall notify the indemnifying Party promptly in writing of the claim. A delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent the delay materially prejudices it. The indemnifying Party controls the defense and settlement of the claim. However, it may not settle any claim in a way that admits fault on behalf of, or imposes any non-monetary obligation on, the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with its own counsel at its own expense.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.

11.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) any Environmental Claim indemnity obligation of either Party under this Agreement;

(b) Supplier's obligations under Section 10.1;

(c) a Party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct; or

(d) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality).

## 12. Termination

12.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving notice describing the breach.

12.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) becomes insolvent;

(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) has a receiver appointed; or

(d) becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

12.3 **Supply Failure.** Customer may terminate this Agreement, or any Order Form, by written notice in either of the following cases:

(a) Supplier fails to deliver conforming Product in the quantities ordered on three or more occasions in any rolling 90-day period; or

(b) a shortfall under Section 4.4 continues for more than 30 days.

12.4 **Effect.** Termination does not affect rights and obligations accrued before termination. On termination, Customer shall pay for conforming Products delivered before the termination date. Sections 6, 7.3, 10, 11, 13, 16 and this Section 12.4 survive termination or expiry.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 **Obligation.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, volumes, plant operating data and Specifications (**"Confidential Information"**). Each Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement. Each Party may disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 13.

13.2 **Exceptions.** Section 13.1 does not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed by the receiving Party; or

(d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, regulation or a public records request. In that case, it shall give the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

13.3 **Personal Data.** To the extent either Party processes personal data of the other Party's personnel in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall comply with the Data Processing Addendum entered into between them.

## 14. Force Majeure

14.1 Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure to perform, other than a payment obligation, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, fire, flood, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action and unforeseeable failures of public utilities. Labor disputes involving the affected Party's own workforce and raw material shortages that could have been avoided by reasonable diligence are not force majeure events.

14.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During a force majeure event affecting Supplier, Supplier shall allocate available Product to Customer at least pro rata. In addition, Section 3.1 does not apply to the affected Products for the duration of the event and for 30 days afterward.

14.3 Either Party may terminate the affected Order Forms by written notice if a force majeure event continues for more than 60 days.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

15.1 **General.** Except as provided in Section 15.2, neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 15 is void.

15.2 **Permitted Assignment.** Supplier may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Customer's consent to any member of the Supplier Group. Supplier shall give Customer written notice of the assignment within ten Business Days after it takes effect. Customer may assign this Agreement without Supplier's consent to an Affiliate, or to a successor to all or substantially all of the business or assets to which this Agreement relates.

15.3 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. General

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good faith negotiation between senior executives for 30 days. After that period, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts.

16.3 **Parent Guaranty.** On or before the Effective Date, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a guaranty of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement, executed by Taipux Holdings, Inc.

16.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the following addresses (or to another address a Party designates by notice):

To Customer: Vaibur Water Services, LLC, Attention: General Counsel

To Supplier: Taipux Chemical Corp., Attention: Legal Department

Notices are effective on receipt.

16.5 **Amendments and Waivers.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of that right.

16.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between them.

16.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

16.8 **Business Days.** **"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday in the United States.

16.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
