# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this **"Guaranty"**) is made as of March 1, 2024 by **Taipux Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the **"Guarantor"**), in favor of **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the **"Beneficiary"**).

## Recitals

A. The Beneficiary and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the **"Supplier"**), have entered into a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**). Under it, the Supplier supplies water and wastewater treatment chemicals to the Beneficiary.

B. The Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the Supply Agreement.

C. Section 16.3 of the Supply Agreement requires the Supplier to deliver to the Beneficiary, on or before the Effective Date, a guaranty of the Supplier's obligations under the Supply Agreement, executed by the Guarantor. The Guarantor is giving this Guaranty to satisfy that requirement.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **"Agreement"** means "this Agreement" as defined in Section 1.4 of the Supply Agreement, as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time.

1.3 **"Guaranteed Obligations"** means all obligations and liabilities of the Supplier to the Beneficiary and its Affiliates under or in connection with the Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, and whether actual or contingent. They include the following:

(a) the obligation to supply Products in accordance with the Agreement;

(b) all payment, refund and reimbursement obligations, including under Sections 4.4 and 7.3 of the Supply Agreement;

(c) all indemnification obligations, including under Sections 10.1 and 10.2 of the Supply Agreement; and

(d) all obligations under the Quality Agreement and each Order Form.

## 2. Guaranty

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Beneficiary the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due. This is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not merely of collection.

2.2 **Primary Obligation.** If the Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall, within ten Business Days after the Beneficiary's written demand, pay the amount due or perform, or cause to be performed, the obligation concerned. The Beneficiary need not first proceed against the Supplier or any other person, or exhaust any other remedy or security, before making a demand on the Guarantor.

2.3 **Extent of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is no greater than the Supplier's liability under the Agreement. The Guarantor may assert any defense that the Supplier could assert under the Agreement, except the defenses waived in Section 4.

2.4 **Costs of Enforcement.** The Guarantor shall pay the Beneficiary's reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Guaranty, to the extent the Beneficiary prevails.

## 3. Nature of Guaranty

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that survive the expiry or termination of the Agreement.

3.2 **Obligations Unaffected.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or reduced by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of it or consents to it:

(a) any amendment, side letter, supplement, Order Form or other modification of the Agreement, or any extension or renewal of its term;

(b) any waiver, extension of time or other indulgence granted to the Supplier;

(c) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of the Supplier or the Guarantor;

(d) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting the Supplier, or any discharge or limitation of the Supplier's liability in that proceeding; or

(e) any invalidity or unenforceability of any Guaranteed Obligation against the Supplier, other than as a result of payment or performance in full.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment made by or on behalf of the Supplier or the Guarantor is rescinded or must be returned on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of the Supplier or otherwise, this Guaranty is reinstated for that payment as if it had not been made.

## 4. Waivers

4.1 The Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law, all of the following:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) presentment, demand for payment, protest and notice of dishonor or non-performance, other than the demand under Section 2.2;

(c) any right to require the Beneficiary to proceed first against the Supplier or any other person, or against any security; and

(d) any defense based on suretyship or impairment of collateral.

4.2 **Subrogation.** Until the Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution, reimbursement or indemnity against the Supplier arising from payments or performance under this Guaranty.

## 5. Representations and Warranties

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary as of the date of this Guaranty as follows:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not conflict with its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law binding on it, or any material agreement to which it is a party; and

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the issued and outstanding voting interests of the Supplier.

## 6. Covenants

6.1 **Financial Information.** Within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, the Guarantor shall deliver to the Beneficiary its audited consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year, unless those statements are publicly available.

6.2 **Notice of Change of Control.** The Guarantor shall notify the Beneficiary in writing within ten Business Days after it ceases to own, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the voting interests of the Supplier.

## 7. Assignment

7.1 The Guarantor may not assign or transfer this Guaranty or any of its obligations under it without the Beneficiary's prior written consent. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 7.1 is void.

7.2 The Beneficiary may assign this Guaranty to any person to whom it assigns the Agreement in accordance with Section 15.2 of the Supply Agreement.

7.3 This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors and benefits the Beneficiary and its successors and permitted assigns.

## 8. General

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

8.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, for any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty.

8.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** The Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law, any right to a trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty.

8.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the following addresses (or to another address a party designates by notice):

To the Guarantor: Taipux Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel

To the Beneficiary: Vaibur Water Services, LLC, Attention: General Counsel

Notices are effective on receipt.

8.5 **Amendments and Waivers.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Guarantor and the Beneficiary. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Beneficiary. No failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising a right operates as a waiver of that right.

8.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

8.7 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
