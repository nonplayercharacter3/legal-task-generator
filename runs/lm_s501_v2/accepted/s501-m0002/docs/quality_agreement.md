# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"QA Effective Date"**) between **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**). Customer and Supplier are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Customer and Supplier have entered into a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**). Under it, Supplier supplies water and wastewater treatment chemicals to Customer.

B. Section 7.1 of the Supply Agreement requires Supplier to manufacture, test, package, store and deliver all Products in accordance with this Quality Agreement. Section 1.14 of the Supply Agreement refers to this Quality Agreement for the Specifications.

C. The Parties wish to set out their quality, testing, documentation, handling and environmental responsibilities for the Products.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Scope and Interpretation

1.1 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products supplied by Supplier to Customer under the Supply Agreement and any Order Form. It also applies to Products supplied from an alternate source under Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Part of the Agreement.** This Quality Agreement is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in Section 1.13 of the Supply Agreement. It forms part of "this Agreement" as defined in Section 1.4 of the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement. In this Quality Agreement:

(a) **"Certificate of Analysis"** or **"CoA"** means a lot-specific certificate issued by Supplier's quality control laboratory. It reports the test results for each parameter in the applicable Specification.

(b) **"Lot"** means a quantity of Product produced in a single production run or blended in a single vessel. A Lot is identified by a unique lot number.

(c) **"Non-Conformance"** means any failure of a Product, or of its packaging, labeling or documentation, to meet the Specifications or this Quality Agreement.

(d) **"Quality Representative"** means the person each Party designates under Section 2.2.

1.4 **Interpretation.** Section 1.15 of the Supply Agreement applies to this Quality Agreement. References to Sections are to sections of this Quality Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Quality Systems and Representatives

2.1 **Quality Management System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 at each facility that manufactures, blends, packages or stores Products for Customer. Supplier shall notify Customer within ten Business Days after any suspension, withdrawal or lapse of that certification.

2.2 **Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Representative to receive notices and coordinate quality matters under this Quality Agreement. Either Party may change its Quality Representative by written notice to the other.

2.3 **Quality Reviews.** The Quality Representatives shall meet at least twice a year, in person or by video conference. They shall review delivery performance, Non-Conformances, corrective actions, complaints and planned changes.

## 3. Specifications and Certification

3.1 **Specifications.** Each Product must conform to the Specification for that Product set out in Appendix A. It must also conform to any additional requirement stated for that Product in the applicable Order Form.

3.2 **Industry Standards.** In addition to Appendix A, ferric chloride must conform to ANSI/AWWA Standard B407 and polyaluminum chloride must conform to ANSI/AWWA Standard B408, in each case in the edition current at the time of manufacture.

3.3 **NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60.** Each Product intended for potable water treatment must be certified to NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 by an ANSI-accredited certification body. Certification must be at no less than the maximum use dose stated in the applicable Order Form. Supplier shall maintain that certification for each production facility and each packaging location from which the Product is supplied. Supplier shall notify Customer within two Business Days after any loss, suspension or reduction in scope of that certification.

3.4 **Raw Materials.** Supplier shall qualify the suppliers of all raw materials used in the Products. Supplier shall not use spent pickle liquor, recycled acid or by-product streams as a raw material for any potable water Product unless the resulting Product remains certified under Section 3.3 and Customer has approved the use in writing under Section 5.

## 4. Testing, Release and Documentation

4.1 **Release Testing.** Supplier shall test each Lot against every parameter in the applicable Specification before releasing it for delivery to Customer. Supplier shall use validated test methods consistent with the applicable AWWA standard or another method agreed in writing by the Quality Representatives.

4.2 **Certificate of Analysis.** Each delivery must be accompanied by a CoA for each Lot delivered. The CoA must show all of the following:

(a) the Product name, grade and lot number;

(b) the date of manufacture and the expiry or retest date;

(c) the test result and Specification limit for each parameter; and

(d) the signature, electronic or otherwise, of an authorized member of Supplier's quality control staff.

4.3 **Retained Samples.** Supplier shall retain a representative sample of each Lot delivered to Customer for at least six months after delivery. On request, Supplier shall test a retained sample, or provide it to Customer or Customer's designated laboratory.

4.4 **Customer Testing.** Customer may sample and test any delivery before or after offloading. Customer's acceptance of a delivery does not waive any Non-Conformance or any of Customer's rights under Section 7 of the Supply Agreement.

4.5 **Labeling and Safety Data Sheets.** Supplier shall label all Products and packaging in accordance with the OSHA Hazard Communication Standard and U.S. Department of Transportation hazardous materials regulations. Supplier shall provide Customer with a current safety data sheet for each Product. Supplier shall provide an updated safety data sheet within 30 days after any revision.

## 5. Change Control

5.1 **Changes Requiring Notice.** Supplier shall give Customer at least 90 days' prior written notice of any proposed change in any of the following:

(a) the manufacturing site or process for a Product;

(b) the source or grade of a key raw material;

(c) the Specification, test methods or packaging of a Product; or

(d) any matter that could affect the Product's NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 certification or its performance in Customer's treatment processes.

5.2 **Customer Approval.** Supplier shall not implement a change described in Section 5.1(a), (b) or (c) for a potable water Product without Customer's prior written approval, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold. Customer may require Supplier to supply trial quantities of the changed Product for jar testing or full-scale trials before giving approval.

5.3 **Regulatory Changes.** If a change is required by law, Supplier shall give as much notice as is reasonably practicable. Supplier shall also comply with Section 8.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 6. Non-Conformance and Complaints

6.1 **Notification.** Each Party shall notify the other's Quality Representative promptly after becoming aware of a Non-Conformance. For any Non-Conformance that could affect drinking water quality or regulatory compliance at a Customer Site, notice must be given by telephone within four hours and confirmed in writing.

6.2 **Investigation.** Supplier shall investigate each Non-Conformance and each written complaint from Customer. Within ten Business Days after notice, Supplier shall give Customer a written report identifying the root cause and the corrective and preventive actions taken or proposed.

6.3 **Remedies.** Customer's remedies for non-conforming Products are as set out in Section 7.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 7. Recalls and Withdrawals

7.1 **Recalls.** If Supplier determines, or a regulator requires, that any Product supplied to Customer must be recalled or withdrawn, Supplier shall notify Customer immediately. The notice must identify the affected Lots and the reason for the recall.

7.2 **Cooperation.** Customer shall cooperate reasonably with Supplier in carrying out a recall. Supplier shall bear the costs of any recall to the extent it results from a Non-Conformance or from Supplier's breach of the Supply Agreement or this Quality Agreement.

## 8. Handling, Storage and Environmental Responsibilities

8.1 **Customer Storage and Handling.** After delivery, Customer shall store, handle, feed and dispose of Products at each Customer Site in accordance with the safety data sheet, applicable Environmental Laws and Customer's own operating procedures. Customer shall maintain its storage tanks, secondary containment, feed systems and unloading areas in good condition and suitable for the Products.

8.2 **Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, employees and agents from and against any Environmental Claim arising from any release, spill or discharge of Products after delivery at Customer Sites. The procedure in Section 10.3 of the Supply Agreement applies to claims under this Section 8.2.

8.3 **Supplier Delivery Responsibilities.** Supplier shall ensure that its carriers carry appropriate spill response equipment on each delivery vehicle. Carriers shall also verify the correct receiving tank or connection with Customer's operator before offloading. Supplier shall train its carriers to recognize and comply with the offloading procedures and receiving points identified in each Order Form.

8.4 **Incident Reporting.** Each Party shall notify the other within 24 hours after any release, spill or discharge of a Product at or near a Customer Site that must be reported to a governmental authority. Each Party shall give the other a copy of any written report it files with a governmental authority about that incident.

8.5 **Packaging Returns.** Supplier shall collect empty returnable totes and drums from Customer Sites within 30 days after Customer's request. Customer shall make sure returned packaging is closed and labeled as required by law.

## 9. Audits and Inspections

9.1 **Customer Audits.** On at least 15 Business Days' prior written notice, Customer or its designated auditor may audit Supplier's facilities, quality records and quality systems relating to the Products. Audits may take place once in each calendar year, and at any other time following a Non-Conformance that affects drinking water quality or regulatory compliance. Audits must take place during normal business hours and must be conducted so as to minimize disruption to Supplier's operations.

9.2 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall notify the other promptly of any inspection by a governmental authority or certification body that relates directly to the Products. Each Party shall give the other a summary of any findings that relate to the Products.

9.3 **Audit Findings.** Supplier shall respond in writing to any audit findings within 30 days. The response must set out the corrective actions Supplier will take and a timetable for completing them.

## 10. Records

10.1 **Retention.** Supplier shall keep batch records, test records, CoAs, raw material records, change control records and Non-Conformance records for each Lot supplied to Customer for at least five years after delivery.

10.2 **Access.** Supplier shall provide copies of those records to Customer within five Business Days after request. Records provided under this Section 10.2 are Supplier's Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Supply Agreement.

## 11. Term and Amendment

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the QA Effective Date and remains in effect for as long as the Supply Agreement is in effect. Sections 4.3, 7, 8.2 and 10 survive the expiry or termination of the Supply Agreement for as long as any Product delivered under it remains in Customer's possession or any record remains subject to retention.

11.2 **Updates to Appendix A.** The Quality Representatives may update Appendix A by a written instrument signed by both of them. Any other amendment to this Quality Agreement must comply with Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement.

## 12. General

12.1 **Incorporation.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law), 16.2 (Dispute Resolution), 16.4 (Notices) and 16.9 (Counterparts) of the Supply Agreement apply to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Quality Agreement.

12.2 **Copies to Quality Representatives.** Each notice under Sections 3.3, 6, 7 and 8.4 must also be sent to the other Party's Quality Representative.
