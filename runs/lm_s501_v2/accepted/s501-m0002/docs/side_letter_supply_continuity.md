# SIDE LETTER – SUPPLY CONTINUITY

**TAIPUX CHEMICAL CORP.**

October 7, 2024

Vaibur Water Services, LLC
Attention: General Counsel

With a copy to: Procurement Manager, Chemicals

**Re: Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 between Vaibur Water Services, LLC and Taipux Chemical Corp. – Supply Continuity Arrangements**

Dear Sir or Madam:

This letter (this **"Side Letter"**) sets out the supply continuity arrangements that Taipux Chemical Corp. (**"Supplier"**) and Vaibur Water Services, LLC (**"Customer"**) have agreed under the Supply Agreement between them dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**). We are entering into it after the recent outage at Supplier's ferric chloride production unit.

## 1. Background and Interpretation

1.1 **Outage.** On August 19, 2024, a failure of the chlorination reactor at Supplier's ferric chloride production unit in Gulfport, Mississippi (the **"Gulfport Facility"**) took the unit out of service. The unit returned to full production on September 23, 2024. During the outage, Supplier filled Customer's Purchase Orders for ferric chloride from inventory and from production at its affiliated facilities. Deliveries to the Cedar Bend Water Treatment Plant and the Millbrook Regional Water Reclamation Facility were delayed by up to four days.

1.2 **Purpose.** The Parties wish to reduce the risk that a future interruption at a single production facility affects deliveries to the Listed Plants. To do so, they are broadening the sources from which Supplier may fill Customer's Purchase Orders and adding inventory and reporting commitments.

1.3 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement. In this Side Letter, **"Agreement"** means "this Agreement" as defined in Section 1.4 of the Supply Agreement.

1.4 **Part of the Agreement.** This Side Letter is a side letter entered into in connection with the Supply Agreement and forms part of the Agreement.

## 2. Supplier Group

2.1 For purposes of the Agreement, **"Supplier Group"** also includes the contract manufacturers and distributors listed in Annex 1, as Supplier may update Annex 1 from time to time by written notice to Customer under Section 16.4 of the Supply Agreement.

2.2 Each notice updating Annex 1 must state the name and address of each contract manufacturer or distributor added or removed. It must also state the Products concerned and, for each manufacturing location, the date of its current NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 certification listing.

## 3. Use of Alternate Sources

3.1 **Conformance.** Products supplied to Customer from any source under Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement must conform to the Specifications, the applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement, including Sections 3.3 and 4.2 of the Quality Agreement.

3.2 **Notice of Use.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing of any alternate source within two Business Days after first using it, as required by Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement. The notice must also identify each Listed Plant that has received or is scheduled to receive Product from that source.

3.3 **Change Control.** The supply of Product from a source listed in Annex 1 for no more than 60 consecutive days in response to an interruption is not a change in manufacturing site requiring prior approval under Section 5.2 of the Quality Agreement. Supply from that source for a longer period is subject to Section 5 of the Quality Agreement.

3.4 **Prices and Delivery.** Product supplied from an alternate source is invoiced at the prices in the applicable Order Form and delivered on the terms of Section 5 of the Supply Agreement and Section 6 of Order Form No. 1. Supplier shall bear any additional freight or handling cost resulting from the use of an alternate source.

## 4. Safety Stock

4.1 **Minimum Inventory.** From November 1, 2024, Supplier shall maintain, at one or more terminals within 400 road miles of each Listed Plant, finished-goods inventory of ferric chloride and polyaluminum chloride equal to at least 21 days of Customer's forecast requirements for that Listed Plant under Section 3.2 of the Supply Agreement.

4.2 **Use of Safety Stock.** Supplier shall draw on safety stock to fill Customer's Purchase Orders during any interruption at a production facility before reducing deliveries to Customer. Supplier shall replenish safety stock to the minimum level within 30 days after the end of the interruption.

4.3 **Reporting.** By the 10th day of each calendar month, Supplier shall report to Customer's Procurement Manager, Chemicals, the safety stock held for each Listed Plant at the end of the previous month, by Product and terminal location.

## 5. Interruption Reporting

5.1 Supplier shall notify Customer by telephone and email within 12 hours after any unplanned shutdown of a facility producing Products for Customer that is expected to last more than 48 hours.

5.2 The notice must state the cause of the shutdown, the expected duration, the Products and Listed Plants likely to be affected, and the sources Supplier intends to use to fill Customer's Purchase Orders. Supplier shall update the notice at least every two Business Days until normal production resumes.

5.3 Within 30 days after normal production resumes, Supplier shall give Customer a written root cause report and a summary of the corrective actions taken.

## 6. Gulfport Outage

6.1 Supplier shall reimburse Customer, within 30 days after receipt of Customer's documented claim, for Customer's reasonable additional operating costs incurred at the Cedar Bend Water Treatment Plant and the Millbrook Regional Water Reclamation Facility as a result of the delayed deliveries described in Section 1.1, up to $25,000 in total.

6.2 Customer's acceptance of the reimbursement under Section 6.1 does not affect Customer's rights under Section 12.3 of the Supply Agreement for any future supply failure.

## 7. General

7.1 **Effect.** Except as set out in this Side Letter, the Supply Agreement, the Quality Agreement and Order Form No. 1 remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

7.2 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law) and 16.2 (Dispute Resolution) of the Supply Agreement apply to this Side Letter.

7.3 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
