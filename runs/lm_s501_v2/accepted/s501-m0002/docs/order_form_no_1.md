# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Under the Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 between Vaibur Water Services, LLC and Taipux Chemical Corp.**

This Order Form No. 1 (this **"Order Form"**) is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Order Form Effective Date"**) between **Vaibur Water Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**), and **Taipux Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**). It is issued under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Supply Agreement"**).

## 1. Incorporation and Definitions

1.1 This Order Form is an Order Form under the Supply Agreement and is part of "this Agreement" as defined in Section 1.4 of the Supply Agreement.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.3 The plants identified in Schedule 1 are the **"Listed Plants"** for purposes of Section 1.7 of the Supply Agreement. Each Listed Plant is a Customer Site.

## 2. Term of Order Form

2.1 This Order Form takes effect on the Order Form Effective Date. It remains in effect for the term of the Supply Agreement unless terminated earlier under Section 12 or Section 14.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 3. Products

3.1 The Products covered by this Order Form, their Specifications references, packaging and maximum use doses are set out in Schedule 2.

3.2 Each Product intended for potable water treatment must be certified to NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 at the maximum use dose stated in Schedule 2, as required by Section 7.2(c) of the Supply Agreement.

3.3 Supplier shall meet the Specifications set out in the Quality Agreement for each Product. Where Schedule 2 states an additional requirement for a Product, Supplier shall meet both.

## 4. Estimated Volumes

4.1 Schedule 3 sets out Customer's estimated annual volumes of each Product at each Listed Plant. These estimates are for Supplier's planning under Section 3.3 of the Supply Agreement. They are not a minimum or maximum purchase commitment.

4.2 Customer's monthly rolling forecasts under Section 3.2 of the Supply Agreement supersede the estimates in Schedule 3 for the periods they cover.

## 5. Prices

5.1 The prices for each Product are set out in Schedule 2. Under Section 6.1 of the Supply Agreement, those prices include all freight, packaging, fuel surcharges and delivery charges to the Listed Plants.

5.2 Prices are fixed until the first anniversary of the Effective Date. After that, prices may be adjusted only in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 6. Ordering and Delivery

6.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders under Section 4.1 of the Supply Agreement, referring to this Order Form No. 1 and naming the Listed Plant for delivery.

6.2 **Lead Times.** Supplier shall deliver bulk tanker loads within three Business Days, and totes within five Business Days, after receipt of a Purchase Order. Customer may place emergency Purchase Orders for delivery within 24 hours. Supplier shall use its best efforts to meet them.

6.3 **Delivery Terms.** Deliveries are DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the receiving point at each Listed Plant shown in Schedule 1, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the Supply Agreement.

6.4 **Receiving Hours.** Unless the Listed Plant's operations supervisor agrees otherwise, deliveries must arrive between 7:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. local time on Business Days. Carriers shall report to the plant control room before offloading.

6.5 **Delivery Documents.** Each delivery must be accompanied by the documents listed in Section 5.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 7. Invoicing and Payment

7.1 Supplier shall send invoices to Customer's accounts payable address below, quoting the Purchase Order number and Listed Plant.

7.2 Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice net 45 days from invoice receipt, in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Supply Agreement.

7.3 Invoices must be sent to: Vaibur Water Services, LLC, Attention: Accounts Payable, ap@vaiburwater.example.

## 8. Contacts

8.1 **Customer.** Procurement Manager, Chemicals, and the operations supervisor of each Listed Plant.

8.2 **Supplier.** Key Account Manager, Municipal Water, and Supplier's 24-hour customer service and logistics desk.

8.3 Either Party may change its contacts by written notice to the other. Notices under the Supply Agreement must be given under Section 16.4 of the Supply Agreement.
