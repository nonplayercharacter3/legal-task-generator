# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of [__________], 2026, by and among **Pisas Automotive Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Bainouv Remarketing Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Fennimore Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $3,600,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Buyer and Seller wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and disburse the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent is willing to accept that appointment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. APPOINTMENT; DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**1.2 Definitions.** In this Escrow Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest, dividends and other income earned on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, in substantially the form of Exhibit B.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered before that date that have not been finally resolved and paid in accordance with Section 4.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. DEPOSIT AND INVESTMENT

**2.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in Exhibit C. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day it is received.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent, as escrow agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with any other funds of the Escrow Agent or any other Person.

**2.3 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the money market deposit account or other investment designated in Exhibit A, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller direct by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss on an investment made in accordance with this Section 2.3.

**2.4 No Other Rights.** Neither Buyer nor Seller may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise encumber any interest in the Escrow Funds, except as provided in Section 10.4.

## 3. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Release Date.** On the date that is twelve (12) months following the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall make the release within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date.

**3.2 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained under Section 3.1 for a pending claim until that claim is resolved under Section 4. When a claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for it in accordance with the resolution. Any part of the retained amount not payable to Buyer shall be released to Seller within five (5) Business Days after the resolution.

**3.3 Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**3.4 Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Final Order delivered to it by Buyer or Seller, together with a certificate of the delivering party's counsel stating that the order is a Final Order. The delivering party shall give a copy to the other party at the same time.

## 4. CLAIMS

**4.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to the Escrow Agent and Seller a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice shall state, in reasonable detail, the nature of the claim, the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which it is made, and the amount claimed or, if not yet determinable, Buyer's good-faith estimate of that amount.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim in a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection to the Escrow Agent and Buyer within twenty (20) Business Days after Seller receives the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice shall state, in reasonable detail, the basis for the objection and the amount in dispute.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the period in Section 4.2, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Funds, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice. The Escrow Agent shall make the disbursement within five (5) Business Days after that period ends. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer in the same way.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the disputed amount of any claim until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order with respect to that claim, and shall then disburse in accordance with them.

**4.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay a claim in full, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available. Payments shall be made in the order in which the Escrow Agent receives the related Claim Notices. Any shortfall is a matter between Buyer and Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**5.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement. They are ministerial in nature, and no implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not required to interpret or determine compliance with it.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it in good faith believes to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent shall verify any funds transfer instruction by a call-back to an authorized representative listed in Exhibit D before acting on it.

**5.3 Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent of its gross negligence or willful misconduct, as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect or consequential damages.

**5.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and is fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**5.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain as to its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also interplead the Escrow Funds in a court described in Section 10.2.

**5.6 Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall deliver to Buyer and Seller a monthly statement of the Escrow Account showing deposits, investment income, disbursements and the balance.

## 6. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees in Exhibit E and reimbursed for its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of these fees and expenses.

**6.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and hold it harmless from and against all losses, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance of this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, unless the amount results from the breach of this Escrow Agreement by one of them, in which case that party shall bear all of it. This Section 6.2 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 7. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds, and all interest and other income earned on the Escrow Funds shall be reported as income of Buyer, until disbursed.

**7.2 Tax Forms.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 at or before the Closing. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file all required information returns with respect to the Escrow Funds.

**7.3 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall, at Buyer's written request, release to Buyer an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the income earned on the Escrow Funds during that quarter.

## 8. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice signed by both of them.

**8.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 10.2 to appoint one. The Escrow Agent's only duty after notice of resignation or removal is to hold the Escrow Funds and deliver them to the successor in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or court order.

## 9. TERMINATION

This Escrow Agreement terminates when all of the Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Escrow Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses in Exhibit D or any other address a party designates by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only when actually received.

**10.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights under it to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement. Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, succeeds to the Escrow Agent under this Escrow Agreement without further act.

**10.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all of the parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account, and each of them shall provide that information on request.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
