# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026, by and among **Bainouv Remarketing Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), **Pisas Automotive Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and, solely for the purposes of Article VI, **Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company operates the Business and, until the Closing, also operates the Salvage Business.

C. Before the Closing, the Company will transfer the Salvage Business to Bainouv Salvage Recovery, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Seller (the "**Seller Affiliate**"), under the Bill of Sale.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $41,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the holders of Company indebtedness for borrowed money, the amounts set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 7.2(e);

(b) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount; and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b), and plus or minus the estimated adjustment under Section 2.5(a).

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law.

**2.4 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $3,600,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX.

**2.5 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) No later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing, prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and excluding all assets and liabilities of the Salvage Business. The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased, dollar-for-dollar, by the amount by which such estimate exceeds or falls short of $4,250,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**").

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of closing net working capital. Seller shall have thirty (30) days to dispute such statement. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days thereafter shall be referred to an independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. New York time on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time and place as the parties may agree (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. New York time on the Closing Date.

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain SimuLane in good working order, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with consignors, buyers, employees and suppliers.

**6.3 Salvage Business Transfer.** Prior to the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company and the Seller Affiliate to execute and deliver the Bill of Sale and to consummate the transfer of the Salvage Business to the Seller Affiliate in accordance with its terms. Seller shall cause the Seller Affiliate to pay, perform and discharge the Retained Liabilities when due.

**6.4 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated hereby, including making any notices to or filings with Governmental Authorities identified in Schedule 4.5.

**6.6 Key Employee.** Seller acknowledges that the Key Employee has substantial knowledge of the Business and of the Company's consignor and buyer relationships, and that Buyer is relying on the non-competition and non-solicitation covenants of the Key Employee set forth in the Key Employee Agreement to protect the goodwill of the Business following the Closing. From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company not to amend, waive or terminate any provision of the Key Employee Agreement without Buyer's prior written consent.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company and the Business, except as required by Law.

**6.8 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all income Tax Returns of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and shall deliver each such Tax Return to Buyer for its review and reasonable comment at least thirty (30) days before filing. Seller shall pay all Taxes shown as due on such Tax Returns.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller; (b) by either Buyer or Seller if the Closing has not occurred on or before June 30, 2026, provided the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either party upon a material breach by the other party that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve any party of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until nine (9) months following the Closing Date; provided, that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the statute of limitations applicable to the underlying subject matter. Covenants and agreements to be performed after the Closing shall survive in accordance with their terms. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (including, after the Closing, the Company) (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller contained in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered hereunder;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller contained in this Agreement;

(c) any Retained Liabilities; and

(d) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any Pre-Closing Tax Period, subject to the survival periods set forth in Section 9.1.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer contained in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer contained in this Agreement.

**9.4 Basket.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to the Fundamental Representations) until the aggregate Losses thereunder exceed $207,500, after which Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) and Section 9.2(d) shall not exceed an amount equal to the Escrow Amount.

(b) The limitation in Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from Retained Liabilities, from any inaccuracy in or breach of the Fundamental Representations, or from fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for Losses arising from inaccuracies in or breaches of the Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually recovered by the Indemnified Party in respect thereof, less the costs of recovery.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under Section 9.2(a), but not for determining whether a breach has occurred, all materiality qualifiers in the representations and warranties shall be disregarded.

**9.6 Indemnification Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail; provided that failure to give such notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money that is fully indemnified hereunder and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.7 Payment; Escrow.** Any amount owing to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Amount in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and, to the extent the Escrow Amount is insufficient or has been released, directly by Seller within ten (10) Business Days after final determination.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.5(b), the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to this Agreement following the Closing.
