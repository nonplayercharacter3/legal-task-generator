# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Bainouv Remarketing Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Bainouv**"), and **Pisas Automotive Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Pisas**"). Bainouv and Pisas are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Bainouv owns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company operates wholesale used-vehicle auctions.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Pisas would acquire the Company (the "**Transaction**").

C. In connection with that evaluation, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information. Each Party is willing to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Disclosing Party or its Representatives furnish to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Transaction. If Bainouv is the Disclosing Party, Confidential Information includes information concerning the Company. Confidential Information includes financial statements and projections, consignor and dealer buyer lists, auction volumes and results, fee schedules, contracts, employee and compensation information, software, systems and data. It includes information concerning the Company's online auction platforms. It also includes all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials that the Receiving Party or its Representatives prepare that contain or reflect any such information ("**Derivative Materials**"). Confidential Information also includes Transaction Information.

"**Disclosing Party**" means the Party that furnishes, or whose Representatives furnish, Confidential Information.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means the Party that receives, or whose Representatives receive, Confidential Information.

"**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their respective directors, managers, officers, employees, members, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors and actual or prospective debt financing sources. Each of them must need to know Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, financing or consummating the Transaction.

"**Transaction Information**" means the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, and the fact that the Parties are discussing the Transaction. It also means the status and terms of those discussions.

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before it was disclosed under this Agreement;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party or its Representatives, if that source is not known by the Receiving Party, after reasonable inquiry, to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

## 2. USE AND NON-DISCLOSURE

**2.1 Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate, finance and consummate the Transaction, and for no other purpose.

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any Person, except as follows:

(a) to its Representatives, if each of them is informed of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information and is directed to comply with this Agreement; or

(b) as permitted by Section 3.

**2.3 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives. A Representative's act or omission that would breach this Agreement if done by the Party is treated as a breach by that Party.

**2.4 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.5 Customer and Personnel Data.** If any Confidential Information includes personal information of consignors, dealer buyers, platform members or employees of the Company, the Receiving Party shall handle that personal information in compliance with applicable privacy and data security laws. It shall also follow any reasonable written data-handling instructions that the Disclosing Party provides.

## 3. LEGALLY REQUIRED DISCLOSURE

**3.1 Notice.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required to disclose any Confidential Information by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, to the extent legally permitted. The purpose of the notice is to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy at its own expense. The Receiving Party shall reasonably cooperate with those efforts.

**3.2 Limited Disclosure.** If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the information disclosed will be treated confidentially.

**3.3 Regulatory Examinations.** Notice under Section 3.1 is not required for disclosure to a banking, securities or other regulatory authority in the course of a routine examination or inquiry that does not target the Disclosing Party or the Transaction.

## 4. CONTACTS; NON-SOLICITATION

**4.1 Contacts.** Without Bainouv's prior written consent, Pisas shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, contact the following Persons about the Transaction or the Confidential Information:

(a) any employee of the Company, other than those Bainouv designates in writing; or

(b) any consignor, dealer buyer, landlord, lender or other business counterparty of the Company.

Ordinary-course business contacts that do not relate to the Transaction are not restricted.

**4.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, Pisas shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment or hire any employee of the Company with whom Pisas first had contact, or of whom Pisas first became aware, in connection with the Transaction. This Section 4.2 does not prohibit the following:

(a) general solicitations, including through search firms, that are not directed at Company employees; or

(b) the hiring of any individual whose employment with the Company ended at least six (6) months before the start of employment discussions.

This Section 4.2 ceases to apply on the closing of the Transaction.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

**5.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten (10) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall, at its option, return or destroy all Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession. It shall also destroy all Derivative Materials. An officer of the Receiving Party shall confirm the return or destruction in writing on request.

**5.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention policy. They may also retain copies stored in automatic electronic archives or back-up systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding the expiration of the term under Section 9.1.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**6.1 No Representations.** Neither the Disclosing Party nor any of its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information. Neither has any liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from the use of the Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a Definitive Agreement.

**6.2 No Obligation.** Neither Party is under any legal obligation with respect to the Transaction by virtue of this Agreement, except for the matters expressly agreed in it. A legal obligation with respect to the Transaction arises only if a definitive written agreement for the Transaction is executed and delivered (a "**Definitive Agreement**"), and then only on its terms. Either Party may end discussions concerning the Transaction at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other.

**6.3 No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in or to the Confidential Information or any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party or the Company.

## 7. SECURITIES LAWS

Each Party acknowledges that it is aware that the securities laws of the United States restrict trading in securities by Persons who possess material non-public information about an issuer. Each Party shall comply with those laws in connection with any Confidential Information.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The non-breaching Party may seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**8.2 Costs.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable judgment that a Party or its Representatives breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in the litigation.

## 9. TERM

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement and the obligations in it expire two (2) years after the Effective Date, except as provided in Section 5.2.

**9.2 Trade Secrets.** For any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, the obligations in Section 2 continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. This applies without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices shall be sent to the addresses on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

**10.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, Bainouv may assign its rights under this Agreement to any acquirer of the Company. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**10.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
