# OFFICE LEASE

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made and entered into as of June 1, 2023 (the "**Lease Date**"), by and between **Wexcombe Plaza Associates, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE TERMS

**1.1 Summary.** The following basic terms are part of this Lease. Each reference in this Lease to a basic term has the meaning stated below.

(a) **Building:** Wexcombe Plaza, 1150 Franklin Avenue, Garden City, New York 11530.

(b) **Premises:** Suite 410 on the fourth (4th) floor of the Building, consisting of approximately 6,200 rentable square feet, as shown on Exhibit A.

(c) **Commencement Date:** July 1, 2023.

(d) **Expiration Date:** June 30, 2030, unless sooner terminated or extended under this Lease.

(e) **Term:** The period from the Commencement Date through the Expiration Date (the "**Term**").

(f) **Base Rent:** As set forth in Section 3.1.

(g) **Tenant's Share:** 6.2%, being the rentable square footage of the Premises divided by the 100,000 rentable square feet of the Building.

(h) **Base Year:** Calendar year 2023.

(i) **Security Deposit:** $35,133.33.

(j) **Permitted Use:** General, executive and administrative offices, as described in Section 5.1.

(k) **Parking:** Twenty (20) unreserved spaces in the Building parking lot, as described in Section 6.3.

**1.2 Exhibits.** The following exhibits are attached to and form part of this Lease: Exhibit A (Floor Plan of the Premises) and Exhibit B (Building Rules and Regulations).

## 2. PREMISES AND TERM

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term, on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobbies, elevators, stairways, corridors, restrooms, parking areas and other common areas of the Building (the "**Common Areas**").

**2.2 Condition of Premises.** Landlord has completed the improvements described in the work letter delivered to Tenant before the Lease Date. Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their condition on the Commencement Date, subject to latent defects of which Tenant notifies Landlord within twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date.

**2.3 Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term for one (1) additional period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**") by giving Landlord written notice at least nine (9) months before the Expiration Date, provided no Event of Default then exists. Base Rent for the first year of the Renewal Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the fair market rent for comparable office space in central Nassau County, as agreed by the parties or, failing agreement within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, as determined by a licensed commercial real estate broker jointly selected by the parties. Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the start of the Renewal Term. All other terms of this Lease apply during the Renewal Term.

**3.2 Operating Expenses and Taxes.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which (a) Operating Expenses for that year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year, and (b) Real Estate Taxes for that year exceed Real Estate Taxes for the Base Year. "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, insure and repair the Building and the Common Areas, excluding capital expenditures (other than those that reduce Operating Expenses, amortized over their useful life), costs of leasing space to other tenants, debt service, depreciation, and costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants. "**Real Estate Taxes**" means real property taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land on which it sits.

**3.3 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall deliver to Tenant, before each calendar year, a reasonable estimate of Tenant's payments under Section 3.2 for that year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each monthly installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Real Estate Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next amounts due (or refunded if the Term has ended), and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days.

**3.4 Audit.** Tenant may, on thirty (30) days' notice given within six (6) months after receiving an annual statement, inspect Landlord's records for that year at Landlord's management office. If the inspection shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's payments by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the inspection.

**3.5 Late Charge.** Any payment not received within five (5) days after its due date bears a late charge of three percent (3%) of the overdue amount.

**3.6 Payment.** Base Rent and all other sums payable by Tenant under this Lease ("**Rent**") shall be paid without demand, deduction or offset, except as expressly provided in this Lease, by electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for the performance of its obligations under this Lease. Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in an interest-bearing account at a New York banking institution in accordance with New York General Obligations Law Section 7-103. If an Event of Default occurs, Landlord may apply all or part of the Security Deposit to cure it, and Tenant shall restore the amount applied within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit, with accrued interest less the administrative fee permitted by law, within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 5. USE

**5.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for general, executive and administrative offices of Tenant's wholesale vehicle auction operations, including accounting, title administration, customer service, information technology and sales functions, and for no other purpose. No vehicles shall be displayed, stored, reconditioned or sold on the Premises or in the Building parking lot, other than vehicles parked by Tenant's employees and visitors.

**5.2 Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws, ordinances and regulations applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, the Common Areas and the Building systems, including the Americans with Disabilities Act.

**5.3 Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the rules and regulations in Exhibit B and with reasonable, non-discriminatory changes to them of which Landlord gives Tenant at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

**5.4 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Premises any hazardous materials other than ordinary office and cleaning supplies used in compliance with law.

## 6. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**6.1 Building Services.** Landlord shall furnish (a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding legal holidays; (b) passenger elevator service at all times; (c) janitorial service on weekdays in accordance with Building standards; (d) water for restrooms and kitchenette use; and (e) security staffing and card-access systems for the Building. After-hours HVAC shall be furnished on request at Landlord's standard hourly rate.

**6.2 Electricity.** Electricity for the Premises is separately metered. Tenant shall contract directly with the utility provider and pay for its electricity consumption.

**6.3 Parking.** Tenant may use, without additional charge, the number of unreserved parking spaces stated in Section 1.1(k) in the Building parking lot, on a first-come, first-served basis with other tenants.

**6.4 Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. However, if an interruption caused by Landlord's negligence makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive Business Days, Rent shall abate in proportion to the untenantable area from the sixth (6th) Business Day until service is restored. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the State of New York.

## 7. ALTERATIONS; MAINTENANCE

**7.1 Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, costing less than $25,000 in the aggregate in any calendar year that do not affect the Building structure or systems. All alterations shall be performed in a good and workmanlike manner by licensed and insured contractors.

**7.2 Tenant Maintenance.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good order and repair, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted.

**7.3 Landlord Maintenance.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements, exterior walls, Common Areas and Building systems serving the Premises, the cost of which is included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 3.2.

**7.4 Mechanics' Liens.** Tenant shall discharge, by bond or otherwise, any mechanic's lien filed against the Building in connection with work performed for Tenant within thirty (30) days after notice of its filing.

## 8. INSURANCE; INDEMNITY

**8.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's furniture, fixtures, equipment and alterations at full replacement cost; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Tenant shall deliver certificates of this insurance to Landlord before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

**8.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas, the premiums for which are included in Operating Expenses.

**8.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other party for loss or damage to its property to the extent that loss or damage is covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance.

**8.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 8.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors, and Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against all claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors.

## 9. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**9.1 Casualty.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall repair the damage, to the extent of available insurance proceeds, with reasonable diligence, and Rent shall abate in proportion to the area of the Premises rendered untenantable until the repair is substantially complete. If Landlord reasonably estimates that the repair cannot be substantially completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within thirty (30) days after Landlord delivers that estimate.

**9.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the parking areas that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct its operations in the remainder, are taken by eminent domain, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving expenses and trade fixtures, so long as that award does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**10.1 Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving it, together with reasonable information about the proposed assignee or subtenant.

**10.2 Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent, assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, provided Tenant gives Landlord written notice within fifteen (15) days after the transfer. A transfer of any shares of the capital stock of Tenant, and any merger or consolidation of Tenant in which Tenant is the surviving entity, is not an assignment of this Lease and requires neither Landlord's consent nor notice to Landlord.

**10.3 No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

## 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**11.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five (5) Business Days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy petition, or an involuntary petition that is not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

**11.2 Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may, by written notice, terminate this Lease and recover possession of the Premises in accordance with applicable law, and may recover the Rent due through the date of termination and damages equal to the Rent that would have been payable for the balance of the Term, less the net rent Landlord reasonably obtains, or could reasonably obtain, by reletting the Premises. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**11.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any of its obligations within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, or within such longer period as is reasonably required if Landlord begins to cure within that period and diligently pursues the cure, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

## 12. SURRENDER; HOLDING OVER

**12.1 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom-clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall remove its furniture, equipment and cabling. Tenant need not remove any alteration unless Landlord required its removal as a condition of its consent under Section 7.1.

**12.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay, for each month or part of a month of holding over, one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the monthly Base Rent last in effect, plus all other Rent.

## 13. LANDLORD ACCESS; QUIET ENJOYMENT

**13.1 Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least one (1) Business Day's notice, except in an emergency, to inspect them, make repairs, or show them to prospective purchasers, lenders or, during the last nine (9) months of the Term, prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's operations and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures.

**13.2 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or any Person claiming through Landlord.

## 14. ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATES

Each party shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the other party's request, deliver a written statement certifying, to the extent true, that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates through which Rent has been paid, whether the certifying party knows of any default by the other party, and such other factual matters as the requesting party reasonably requests. Each party may request such a statement no more than twice in any calendar year.

## 15. MISCELLANEOUS

**15.1 Notices.** All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, or one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, addressed (a) to Landlord at c/o Wexcombe Property Management, 1150 Franklin Avenue, Suite 100, Garden City, New York 11530, Attention: Property Manager; and (b) to Tenant at the Premises, Attention: Chief Financial Officer, or to any other address a party designates by notice.

**15.2 Brokers.** Each party represents that it dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Nassau Commercial Realty Partners, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement.

**15.3 Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

**15.4 Successors and Assigns.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**15.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**15.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**15.7 Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Tenant shall look solely to Landlord's interest in the Building, including its rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds, for the satisfaction of any judgment against Landlord under this Lease.

**15.8 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
