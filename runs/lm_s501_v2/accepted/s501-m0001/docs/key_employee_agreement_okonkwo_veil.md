# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Renata Okonkwo-Veil**, an individual ("**Employee**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company operates wholesale used-vehicle auctions for dealers, fleet owners, financial institutions and other consignors.

B. Employee has served the Company in senior management roles and is currently its General Manager. In that role she has substantial knowledge of the Company's operations, consignor and buyer relationships, pricing and fee structures, and other confidential information.

C. The Company wishes to continue to employ Employee as General Manager, and Employee wishes to continue that employment, on the terms of this Agreement. These terms include the restrictive covenants in Sections 6, 7 and 8, which Employee accepts in consideration of the compensation, Retention Bonus and severance protection provided in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Base Salary**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Business**" means the conduct of in-person wholesale motor vehicle auctions at physical auction facilities, including the solicitation of consignments for those auctions, the registration of dealer buyers to attend and bid at them, and the arbitration, reconditioning, transport coordination and title processing services provided in connection with them.

"**Cause**" means Employee's (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud, dishonesty or moral turpitude; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of her duties that causes material harm to the Company; (c) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties, after written notice from the Board and a cure period of not less than thirty (30) days; (d) material breach of Section 6, 7 or 8; or (e) material violation of a written Company policy concerning harassment, discrimination or financial controls, after written notice and, if curable, a cure period of not less than fifteen (15) days.

"**Change of Control**" means (a) any transfer, sale or issuance, in a single transaction or a series of related transactions, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the Company's voting stock to any Person or group of Persons acting together; (b) any merger, consolidation or reorganization of the Company after which the holders of the Company's voting stock immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

"**Competing Business**" means any Person or enterprise that engages in the Business, or that is actively planning to engage in the Business.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Good Reason**" means the occurrence of any of the following without Employee's written consent: (a) a material reduction in Employee's Base Salary or target annual bonus opportunity; (b) a material diminution in Employee's title, authority, duties or responsibilities, including any requirement that she report to anyone other than the Board or the Company's chief executive officer; (c) relocation of Employee's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles from the Company's Long Island auction facility; (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company; or (e) a Change of Control occurring in the circumstances described in Section 5.3. Except as provided in Section 5.3, Employee may terminate for Good Reason only if she gives the Company written notice describing the condition within sixty (60) days after its first occurrence, the Company fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after that notice, and Employee resigns within thirty (30) days after the end of the cure period.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Retention Bonus**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which Employee's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

"**Territory**" means the United States of America.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; TERM

**2.1 Employment.** The Company employs Employee, and Employee accepts employment with the Company, on the terms of this Agreement.

**2.2 Term.** The term of Employee's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years (the "**Initial Term**"). After the Initial Term, it renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Employee's employment may end earlier under Section 5.

## 3. POSITION AND DUTIES

**3.1 Position.** Employee shall serve as General Manager of the Company and shall report to the Board.

**3.2 Duties.** Employee shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for a general manager of a wholesale vehicle auction company, and such other duties consistent with her position as the Board may reasonably assign. These include overall responsibility for the Company's auction operations, consignor and dealer relationships, sales, staffing and day-to-day management.

**3.3 Full Time and Attention.** Employee shall devote her full business time, attention and best efforts to the Company. She may serve on civic, charitable or industry association boards and manage her personal investments, so long as those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or breach Sections 6 through 8.

**3.4 Place of Performance.** Employee's principal place of work is the Company's auction facility on Long Island, New York, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Employee a base salary at the annual rate of $285,000 (as adjusted from time to time, the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board shall review the Base Salary at least annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each calendar year during the term, Employee is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of forty percent (40%) of Base Salary, based on achievement of Company and individual performance goals set by the Board after consultation with Employee. Any annual bonus earned shall be paid no later than March 15 of the following year, provided Employee remains employed on the payment date, except as provided in Section 5.4.

**4.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay Employee a retention bonus of $400,000 (the "**Retention Bonus**"), payable in a lump sum within thirty (30) days after the second anniversary of the Effective Date, provided Employee remains employed by the Company on that anniversary, except as provided in Section 5.4.

**4.4 Benefits.** Employee is eligible to participate in all employee benefit plans and programs the Company makes available to its senior management, subject to their terms.

**4.5 Vacation.** Employee is entitled to four (4) weeks of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to Company holidays.

**4.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.7 Vehicle Allowance.** The Company shall provide Employee a monthly vehicle allowance of $1,200.

## 5. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**5.1 Termination by the Company.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment (a) for Cause, effective on written notice, or (b) without Cause, on thirty (30) days' written notice or payment of Base Salary in lieu of notice.

**5.2 Termination by Employee.** Employee may terminate her employment (a) for Good Reason in accordance with the definition of Good Reason in Section 1.1 or with Section 5.3, or (b) without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' written notice to the Board.

**5.3 Change of Control.** The Company shall not undergo a Change of Control without first obtaining Employee's written consent. If a Change of Control occurs without first obtaining Employee's written consent, Employee may terminate for Good Reason by written notice to the Company given at any time within ninety (90) days after the Change of Control, and no cure period applies. On any such termination, (a) Employee shall receive the payments and benefits described in Section 5.4(b), and (b) the Restricted Period under Section 7 shall terminate as of the Termination Date.

**5.4 Payments on Termination.**

(a) *Accrued Obligations.* On any termination, the Company shall pay Employee (i) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the Termination Date, (ii) any annual bonus earned for a completed calendar year but not yet paid, (iii) accrued but unused vacation, and (iv) unreimbursed business expenses (together, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

(b) *Severance.* If the Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee terminates for Good Reason (including under Section 5.3), then, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and subject to Section 5.5, the Company shall:

(i) continue to pay Employee her Base Salary, at the rate in effect immediately before the Termination Date (or, if higher, immediately before any reduction constituting Good Reason), for eighteen (18) months following the Termination Date, in accordance with its regular payroll practices;

(ii) pay Employee the Retention Bonus, to the extent not previously paid, in a lump sum within thirty (30) days after the Termination Date; and

(iii) if Employee timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay the employer portion of the premiums for that coverage for eighteen (18) months following the Termination Date, or until Employee becomes eligible for group health coverage from a subsequent employer, if earlier.

(c) *Other Terminations.* On termination for Cause, by Employee without Good Reason, or on Employee's death or disability, Employee (or her estate) is entitled only to the Accrued Obligations.

**5.5 Release.** Payments and benefits under Section 5.4(b) are conditioned on Employee's execution, within forty-five (45) days after the Termination Date, of a general release of claims in favor of the Company in customary form, and on that release becoming irrevocable. Payments that would otherwise be made before the release becomes irrevocable shall be paid in a lump sum on the first payroll date after it does.

**5.6 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment under this Agreement is a separate payment. If Employee is a "specified employee" on the Termination Date, any payment of nonqualified deferred compensation due on separation from service shall be delayed to the extent required by Section 409A.

## 6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**6.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company and its Affiliates, including consignor and buyer lists and contact information, auction volumes and results, pricing, fee schedules and incentive arrangements, financial information, business plans, software, systems, data and personnel information, in any form.

**6.2 Non-Disclosure.** During her employment and at all times afterward, Employee shall hold the Confidential Information in confidence and shall not use or disclose it except as required to perform her duties for the Company or as required by law. If disclosure is required by law, Employee shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Company prompt prior notice.

**6.3 Return of Materials.** On the Termination Date, or earlier at the Company's request, Employee shall return to the Company all documents, devices and other materials containing Confidential Information, and shall delete all Confidential Information from her personal devices and accounts.

**6.4 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, Employee will not be held liable for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or made in a sealed filing in a lawsuit.

## 7. NON-COMPETITION

**7.1 Restricted Period.** The "**Restricted Period**" means the period of Employee's employment with the Company and the eighteen (18) months following the Termination Date, subject to Section 5.3.

**7.2 Covenant.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the Territory, own, manage, operate, control, finance, be employed by, provide services to, or participate in the ownership, management, operation or control of, any Competing Business, whether as an owner, partner, member, officer, director, employee, consultant, agent or otherwise.

**7.3 Passive Investment.** Section 7.2 does not prohibit Employee from owning, as a passive investment, less than two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any Person.

**7.4 Acknowledgment.** Employee acknowledges that the Business is conducted throughout the Territory, that the Company's consignors and dealer buyers are located throughout the Territory, and that the restrictions in this Section 7 are reasonable in scope, duration and geography and necessary to protect the Company's goodwill and Confidential Information.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

**8.1 Consignors and Buyers.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) solicit or accept consignments for, or registrations of dealer buyers in, the Business from any Person that was a consignor or registered buyer of the Company at any time during the twenty-four (24) months before the Termination Date, other than on behalf of the Company, or (b) induce or attempt to induce any such Person to reduce or end its participation in the Business conducted by the Company.

**8.2 Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire, or attempt to solicit, recruit or hire, any individual who is, or was within the twelve (12) months before the solicitation, an employee or independent contractor of the Company engaged in the Business. General advertisements not targeted at Company personnel do not violate this Section 8.2.

**8.3 Non-Disparagement.** During her employment and for the Restricted Period, Employee shall not make any statement to consignors, buyers or the trade press that disparages the Company or its auctions, officers or directors. Truthful testimony and statements required by law are not restricted.

## 9. WORK PRODUCT

**9.1 Assignment.** All inventions, works of authorship, software, processes, data and other work product that Employee creates, alone or with others, during her employment and that relate to the Company's business or result from the use of Company resources ("**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. Employee assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in all Work Product.

**9.2 Cooperation.** Employee shall execute such documents and take such actions as the Company reasonably requests to perfect its rights in the Work Product, at the Company's expense.

**9.3 Excluded Inventions.** This Section 9 does not apply to any invention that qualifies for exclusion under Section 203-f of the New York Labor Law.

## 10. REMEDIES

**10.1 Injunctive Relief.** Employee acknowledges that a breach of Section 6, 7, 8 or 9 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**10.2 Tolling.** If Employee breaches Section 7 or 8, the Restricted Period applicable to the breached covenant shall be extended by the period of the breach.

**10.3 Reformation.** If a court determines that any restriction in Section 7 or 8 is unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Nassau County, New York, or the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**11.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the attention of the Chair of the Board at the Company's principal office. Notices to Employee shall be sent to her most recent address in the Company's records.

**11.4 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Employee and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets.

**11.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Employee and an authorized officer of the Company acting with the approval of the Board. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.6 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld under applicable law.

**11.7 Survival.** Sections 5.4 through 11 survive the termination of Employee's employment and of this Agreement in accordance with their terms.

**11.8 Severability.** Subject to Section 10.3, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
