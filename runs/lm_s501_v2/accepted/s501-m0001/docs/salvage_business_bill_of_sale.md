# BILL OF SALE AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and entered into as of [__________], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ostrander Auto Exchange, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Bainouv Salvage Recovery, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Seller Affiliate**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company has conducted the Salvage Business through its salvage division.

B. Bainouv Remarketing Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company. The Seller Affiliate is an Affiliate of Seller.

C. Seller and Pisas Automotive Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller will sell all of the Shares to Buyer. Before the Closing, the Company is to transfer the Salvage Business to the Seller Affiliate.

D. The Company wishes to sell, convey, assign and transfer the Transferred Assets to the Seller Affiliate. The Seller Affiliate wishes to accept the Transferred Assets and to assume the Retained Liabilities. Each transfer and assumption is on the terms of this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Company and the Seller Affiliate agree as follows. They do so in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Bill of Sale and of other good and valuable consideration. Each of them acknowledges receipt and sufficiency of that consideration.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. These include "Affiliate," "Business," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Law," "Lien," "Person," "Salvage Business," "Shares" and "SimuLane."

**1.2 Transfer Time.** "**Transfer Time**" means 12:00 a.m. New York time on the Closing Date. This is immediately before the Closing becomes effective under the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Retained Liabilities.** "**Retained Liabilities**" means all debts, liabilities, obligations, claims and commitments of the Company of every kind and nature, whether known or unknown, accrued or contingent, and whether liquidated or unliquidated, in each case:

(a) arising out of or relating to the ownership, operation or conduct of the Salvage Business; or

(b) arising out of or relating to the ownership, use or operation of the Transferred Assets, including under the Transferred Contracts;

in each case to the extent arising after the Transfer Time.

**1.4 Other Definitions.** In this Bill of Sale:

"**Excluded Assets**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

"**Salvage Employees**" means the employees of the Company who are listed on Schedule C. Each of them is employed primarily in the Salvage Business.

"**Transferred Assets**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Transferred Contracts**" means the contracts listed on Schedule A.

## 2. CONVEYANCE OF TRANSFERRED ASSETS

**2.1 Conveyance.** Effective as of the Transfer Time, the Company sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to the Seller Affiliate all of the Company's right, title and interest in, to and under the Transferred Assets. The Seller Affiliate accepts them. The Seller Affiliate and its successors and assigns shall have and hold the Transferred Assets forever.

**2.2 Transferred Assets.** "**Transferred Assets**" means all assets, properties and rights of the Company that are used or held for use primarily in the Salvage Business, including the following:

(a) all total-loss, salvage-titled and insurance-recovery vehicles held by the Company as of the Transfer Time, whether owned by the Company or held on consignment, together with the related certificates of title, salvage certificates and title-processing files;

(b) the Transferred Contracts, including the Company's consignment and remarketing agreements with insurance carriers and fleet owners for salvage and total-loss vehicles;

(c) the tangible personal property listed on Schedule B, including tow vehicles, forklifts, yard equipment, storage racking and office furniture located at the Company's salvage yard facilities;

(d) all accounts receivable of the Salvage Business outstanding as of the Transfer Time;

(e) to the extent transferable under applicable Law, the permits, licenses and registrations held by the Company solely for the Salvage Business, including salvage dealer and dismantler registrations;

(f) all books, records, files, customer and consignor lists and correspondence relating primarily to the Salvage Business. The Company may keep copies of any of them that it needs for Tax, financial reporting or legal purposes; and

(g) all goodwill of the Salvage Business.

**2.3 Excluded Assets.** The Transferred Assets do not include any of the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"), all of which the Company retains:

(a) all assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business;

(b) SimuLane, including all software, source code, mobile applications, domain names, member accounts, member and transaction data, and other intellectual property comprising or used in SimuLane, even if SimuLane was at any time used to conduct auctions for the Salvage Business;

(c) the "Ostrander" name and all trademarks, service marks and trade names of the Company;

(d) cash and cash equivalents of the Company;

(e) all real property leases of the Company, and all leasehold improvements under them;

(f) all insurance policies of the Company and all rights under them;

(g) all Tax refunds, Tax attributes and Tax Returns of the Company; and

(h) the corporate seal, minute books, stock records and other organizational records of the Company.

**2.4 Discontinued Use of Excluded Assets.** From and after the Transfer Time, the Seller Affiliate shall not use any Excluded Asset. This includes conducting any auction on or through SimuLane or using the "Ostrander" name.

## 3. ASSUMPTION OF RETAINED LIABILITIES

**3.1 Assumption.** Effective as of the Transfer Time, the Seller Affiliate assumes the Retained Liabilities and agrees to pay, perform and discharge them when due.

**3.2 No Other Assumption.** The Seller Affiliate does not assume, and shall not be responsible for, any debt, liability or obligation of the Company other than the Retained Liabilities.

## 4. SALVAGE EMPLOYEES

**4.1 Offers of Employment.** Before the Transfer Time, the Seller Affiliate shall offer employment to each Salvage Employee. The offer shall begin at the Transfer Time, at a base wage or salary no less than the Salvage Employee's rate in effect immediately before the Transfer Time. Each Salvage Employee who accepts is a "**Transferred Salvage Employee**." The Company shall end its employment of each Transferred Salvage Employee as of the Transfer Time.

**4.2 Employment Liabilities.** Liabilities relating to the employment of Transferred Salvage Employees by the Seller Affiliate are Retained Liabilities.

## 5. INDEMNIFICATION

**5.1 By the Seller Affiliate.** The Seller Affiliate shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Company and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees, successors and assigns, from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to:

(a) any Retained Liability; or

(b) any breach of this Bill of Sale by the Seller Affiliate.

**5.2 By the Company.** The Company shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Seller Affiliate and its officers, managers, employees, successors and assigns from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any breach of this Bill of Sale by the Company.

## 6. FURTHER ASSURANCES; NON-ASSIGNABLE ASSETS

**6.1 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver further instruments of conveyance, transfer and assumption, and shall take such further actions, as the other party reasonably requests to carry out this Bill of Sale. These include:

(a) the endorsement and delivery of certificates of title for vehicles included in the Transferred Assets; and

(b) filings with the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles and other Governmental Authorities.

**6.2 Non-Assignable Assets.** This Bill of Sale does not assign any Transferred Contract or permit if an attempted assignment without a third party's consent would constitute a breach of it or would be ineffective. Until that consent is obtained, the Company shall hold the asset for the benefit of the Seller Affiliate and shall cooperate in any lawful arrangement that gives the Seller Affiliate its benefits. In return, the Seller Affiliate shall perform the Company's obligations under that asset arising after the Transfer Time, and those obligations are Retained Liabilities.

**6.3 Misdirected Payments.** If either party receives any payment that belongs to the other party under this Bill of Sale, it shall promptly remit that payment to the other party.

## 7. DISCLAIMER

The Seller Affiliate acknowledges that the Transferred Assets are conveyed "as is, where is." The Seller Affiliate also acknowledges that the Company makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the condition, merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose of any Transferred Asset.

## 8. MISCELLANEOUS

**8.1 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York. This applies without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**8.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Bill of Sale.

**8.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither party may assign this Bill of Sale without the prior written consent of the other party.

**8.4 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**8.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**8.6 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
