# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is made as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor and **Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of May 11, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor is selling to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignee operates a lithium-ion battery recycling business. The business discharges and shreds end-of-life cells and recovers lithium, cobalt and nickel from black mass by hydrometallurgical processing.

C. Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor is to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Recycling Process Technology.** "**Recycling Process Technology**" means the Patents listed on Exhibit A.

**1.2 Other Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Assigned Rights**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Patents**" means the patents and patent applications listed on **Exhibit A**. The term also includes:

(a) all provisionals, continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, substitutes and renewals of those patents and patent applications;

(b) all patents issuing on any of them;

(c) all reissues, reexaminations, extensions and supplementary protection certificates of any of them; and

(d) all foreign counterparts of any of them.

"**Patent Offices**" means the United States Patent and Trademark Office and each corresponding patent office or registry in any other jurisdiction.

**1.3 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation". Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Assignor acknowledges, Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and its successors and assigns, all of Assignor's right, title and interest throughout the world in and to the following (together, the "**Assigned Rights**"):

(a) the Recycling Process Technology;

(b) all inventions disclosed and claimed in the Patents;

(c) all rights of priority and protection of interests in the Patents under any international convention or treaty;

(d) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the foregoing; and

(e) all claims and causes of action relating to any of the foregoing, whether accruing before, on or after the Effective Date. This includes the right to sue for and recover damages and other remedies for past, present and future infringement.

**2.2 Affiliates.** Assignor shall cause each of its Affiliates that holds any right, title or interest in the Assigned Rights to assign that right, title and interest to Assignee on the Effective Date, on the terms of Section 2.1.

**2.3 Free and Clear.** The assignment under Section 2.1 is made free and clear of all liens, security interests, licenses and other encumbrances.

## 3. Recordation

**3.1 Authorization.** Assignor authorizes the Commissioner for Patents of the United States Patent and Trademark Office, and the officials of each other Patent Office, to record Assignee as the owner of the Patents. Assignor further authorizes those officials to issue any patent on any of the Patents to Assignee, as assignee of the entire interest.

**3.2 Short-Form Assignments.** At Assignee's request, Assignor shall execute short-form confirmatory assignments, in a form suitable for recording in any Patent Office, for any of the Patents. Any such short-form assignment is for recording purposes only. It does not limit, expand or otherwise modify this Agreement.

**3.3 Recording Costs.** Assignee shall bear the official fees and costs of recording the assignment of the Patents with the Patent Offices.

## 4. Further Assurances

**4.1 Further Instruments.** At Assignee's or Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver any further instruments and take any further actions reasonably necessary to perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned Rights. This includes:

(a) executing declarations, oaths and powers of attorney required in the prosecution of any of the Patents; and

(b) providing documents, testimony and other reasonable cooperation in any proceeding concerning the validity, enforceability or infringement of any of the Patents.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** If Assignor fails to execute any instrument described in Section 3.2 or Section 4.1 within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request, Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact. Under that appointment, Assignee may execute and file that instrument in Assignor's name. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Files and Records.** Within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee the complete prosecution files, docketing records and correspondence with patent counsel relating to the Patents, to the extent in the possession or control of Assignor or its Affiliates.

**4.4 Payments Received.** If Assignor or any of its Affiliates receives any payment relating to the Assigned Rights after the Effective Date, it shall hold that payment in trust for Assignee and promptly remit it to Assignee.

## 5. Representations of Assignor

**5.1 Title.** Assignor represents to Assignee that:

(a) Assignor, or one of its Affiliates bound by Section 2.2, is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Recycling Process Technology;

(b) the Recycling Process Technology is free and clear of all liens, security interests, licenses and other encumbrances;

(c) Assignor has not previously assigned, transferred or granted any option or other right in any of the Assigned Rights to any Person; and

(d) all maintenance fees, annuities and other official fees due on the Patents on or before the Effective Date have been paid.

**5.2 Authority.** Assignor has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

## 6. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

**6.1 No Merger.** This Agreement is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities in the Purchase Agreement, including Article 8, are not merged into, limited or expanded by this Agreement.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds Assignor and its successors and assigns. It benefits Assignee and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**7.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**7.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Questions about the validity and scope of any Patent are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction that granted it.

**7.5 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought in accordance with Section 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.6 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
