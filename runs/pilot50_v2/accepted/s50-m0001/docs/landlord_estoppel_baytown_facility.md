**Tenant:** Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**")

## 1. Background

1.1 Landlord and Tenant are parties to an Industrial Lease Agreement dated as of June 1, 2021, as amended by a First Amendment to Industrial Lease Agreement dated as of March 15, 2023 (together, the "**Lease**"). Under the Lease, Landlord leases to Tenant the land, the buildings and improvements, and the associated yard, tank farm and truck court located at 4810 Cedar Bayou Industrial Parkway, Baytown, Texas 77521 (the "**Premises**").

1.2 Landlord understands the following. Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), has entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of May 11, 2026 with Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC ("**Porert**"). Under that agreement, Buyer will acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant (the "**Transaction**"). Landlord also understands that Buyer, Tenant and their lenders will rely on this certificate in connection with the Transaction.

## 2. Certifications Regarding the Lease

Landlord certifies to Buyer and Tenant as follows as of the date of this certificate:

2.1 **Lease Documents.** The Lease is the only agreement between Landlord and Tenant relating to the Premises. It consists solely of the documents listed in Section 1.1, true and complete copies of which are attached as **Annex 1**. The Lease has not been modified, supplemented or amended except as stated in Section 1.1.

2.2 **Full Force and Effect.** The Lease is in full force and effect. It is the valid and binding obligation of Landlord. To Landlord's knowledge, it is the valid and binding obligation of Tenant.

2.3 **Term.** The current term of the Lease commenced on June 1, 2021 and expires on May 31, 2031. Under Section 3.2 of the Lease, Tenant holds two (2) options to extend the term, each for five (5) years. Neither option has been exercised, and both remain available to Tenant on the terms of the Lease.

2.4 **Base Rent.** Monthly base rent currently payable under the Lease is $118,750. It increases by three percent (3%) on each June 1 during the term, as provided in Section 4.1 of the Lease. Base rent has been paid through May 31, 2026. No base rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

2.5 **Additional Rent.** Tenant pays, as additional rent, its share of real property taxes, Landlord's insurance premiums and common area charges, in the manner described in Article 5 of the Lease. Estimated additional rent currently payable is $21,400 per month. The reconciliation for calendar year 2025 has been completed. No amount is owed by either party for that year.

2.6 **Security Deposit.** Tenant has deposited with Landlord a security deposit of $356,250 in cash. Landlord holds it under Section 4.6 of the Lease. Landlord has not applied any part of it.

2.7 **No Defaults.** Landlord has not given Tenant any notice of default under the Lease that remains uncured. To Landlord's knowledge, no event has occurred that, with the giving of notice or passage of time, or both, would constitute a default by Tenant. To Landlord's knowledge, Landlord is not in default under the Lease. Tenant has not given Landlord any notice claiming a Landlord default.

2.8 **Improvements and Allowances.** All improvements that Landlord is required to construct under the Lease have been completed and accepted by Tenant. All tenant improvement allowances payable by Landlord under the Lease have been paid in full. No rent abatement, free rent or other concession remains outstanding.

2.9 **Permitted Use.** Under Section 6.1 of the Lease, the permitted use of the Premises includes the receipt, storage, discharge and shredding of lithium-ion batteries and battery materials, and the hydrometallurgical processing of black mass to recover lithium, cobalt and nickel. It also includes related laboratory, warehouse and office uses. Landlord is aware of the operations Tenant currently conducts at the Premises and confirms that they are a permitted use under the Lease.

2.10 **Environmental Notices.** Landlord has not given Tenant any written notice under Article 9 (Environmental Matters) of the Lease. Landlord has not received any notice from a Governmental Authority of any violation of Environmental Laws at the Premises.

2.11 **Purchase Rights.** The Lease does not grant Tenant any option or right of first refusal to purchase the Premises.

2.12 **Landlord's Interest.** Landlord is the fee owner of the Premises. Landlord has not assigned or pledged its interest in the Lease or the rents under it, except to Second Coast Bank, N.A., under a deed of trust and assignment of leases and rents recorded in the Official Public Records of Harris County, Texas. Landlord has the authority to sign and deliver this certificate without the consent of any other Person.

## 3. Consent to Change of Control

3.1 Section 14.3 of the Lease treats a transfer of a controlling interest in Tenant as an assignment requiring Landlord's consent. Landlord consents to the Transaction, including the transfer of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant to Buyer.

3.2 The Transaction will not constitute a default under the Lease or give Landlord any right to terminate the Lease, increase rent or require additional security. Tenant will remain the tenant under the Lease after the Transaction.

3.3 Porert has not guaranteed the Lease. No guaranty of the Lease by any Person is in effect.

3.4 This consent applies only to the Transaction. It does not waive Landlord's rights under Section 14.3 of the Lease for any later transfer.
