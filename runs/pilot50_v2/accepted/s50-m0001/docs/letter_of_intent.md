**Re: Letter of Intent – Proposed Acquisition of Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which **Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from **Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Seller**"). We refer to that acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is not binding. It is a statement of the parties' mutual intentions only. The parties will negotiate in good faith toward a definitive stock purchase agreement reflecting these terms (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Transaction Structure

Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The Company operates a lithium-ion battery recycling business at its leased plant in Baytown, Texas (the "**Facility**"). The business discharges and shreds end-of-life cells and recovers lithium, cobalt and nickel from black mass by hydrometallurgical processing (the "**Business**").

## 2. Purchase Price

(a) The aggregate purchase price for the Shares will be $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), on a cash-free, debt-free basis, payable in cash at closing.

(b) The Purchase Price assumes a normalized level of net working capital. It will be adjusted dollar-for-dollar at closing for any excess or shortfall against a working capital target to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement, followed by a customary post-closing true-up.

(c) At closing, 5% of the Purchase Price will be deposited with a mutually acceptable escrow agent. It will be held for eighteen (18) months as security for Seller's indemnification obligations.

## 3. Assets and Technology to Be Delivered at Closing

The parties understand that certain assets used in the Business are held by Seller and not by the Company. At closing, Seller will:

(a) convey to the Company, by bill of sale, the battery discharge, shredding, sieving and black-mass separation equipment located at the Facility and owned by Seller; and

(b) assign to the Company, by an intellectual property assignment agreement, all patents, trade secrets, know-how, process parameters and software owned by Seller or its Affiliates and used in the Company's hydrometallurgical metal recovery.

## 4. Transition Services

At closing, Seller and the Company will enter into a transition services agreement. Under it, Seller will continue to provide the IT, process control support, environmental compliance support, laboratory, logistics, payroll and accounting services that it currently provides to the Company. Services will be provided at cost for a term ending 9 months after the Closing Date.

## 5. Due Diligence

Following execution of this Letter, Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisers reasonable access to the Company's books, records, contracts, permits, personnel and the Facility. Buyer will conduct customary business, financial, legal, tax, intellectual property and environmental due diligence.

## 6. Environmental Review

(a) Because of the nature of the Business and the materials handled at the Facility, Buyer will commission a Phase II environmental site assessment of the Facility. The assessment will be performed by an independent environmental consultant selected by Buyer and reasonably acceptable to Seller. It will include soil, groundwater and process-area sampling. Seller will cause the Company to cooperate with the consultant.

(b) Buyer will have an environmental review period of 30 days following execution of the definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Environmental Review Period**").

(c) The Definitive Agreement will give Buyer the right to terminate it by written notice to Seller if the Phase II assessment reports estimated remediation costs exceeding $1,500,000. That notice is to be delivered no later than thirty (30) days following execution of the Definitive Agreement.

## 7. Definitive Agreement

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants, closing conditions, indemnities and termination rights that are customary for a transaction of this type and consistent with this Letter. These will include:

(a) customary representations on organization, capitalization, financial statements, material contracts, intellectual property, real property, environmental matters, permits, litigation, taxes and employees;

(b) a covenant of Seller not to compete in lithium-ion battery recycling or black-mass processing in North America, and not to solicit Company employees, for five (5) years after closing; and

(c) conditions to closing, including expiration of any waiting period under the HSR Act, receipt of the landlord's consent under the Facility lease, and absence of a material adverse effect.

## 8. Timing

The parties will use commercially reasonable efforts to execute the Definitive Agreement as promptly as practicable after the date of this Letter and to close as soon as practicable after the closing conditions are satisfied.

## 9. Conduct of Business

Until the Definitive Agreement is executed or this Letter terminates, Seller will cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. That includes maintaining its permits and its relationships with feedstock suppliers and offtakers.

## 10. Exclusivity

For ninety (90) days after the date of this Letter (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), Seller will not, and will cause the Company and their respective Affiliates and representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any Person other than Buyer regarding any sale of the Company, the Shares or any material part of the Business. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of that kind that it receives.

## 11. Brokers; Expenses

Seller has engaged Halvorsen Partners as its financial adviser, and Seller is solely responsible for its fees. Buyer has engaged no broker or finder. Each party will bear its own expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any binding obligation. No binding obligation to complete the Transaction will exist unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered.

## 13. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction are subject to the confidentiality agreement between Buyer and Seller dated December 4, 2025, which remains in full force.

## 14. Termination

This Letter terminates on the earliest of:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(b) written agreement of the parties; and

(c) expiration of the Exclusivity Period, unless the parties extend it in writing.

The Binding Provisions, other than Section 10, survive termination. Termination does not relieve any party of liability for breach of a Binding Provision before termination.

## 15. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
