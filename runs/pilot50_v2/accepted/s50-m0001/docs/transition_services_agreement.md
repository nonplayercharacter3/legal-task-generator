# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is made as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider and **Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of May 11, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Provider is selling to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Recipient operates a lithium-ion battery recycling business at the Facility. The business discharges and shreds end-of-life cells and recovers lithium, cobalt and nickel from black mass by hydrometallurgical processing.

C. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided certain administrative, technical and operational support to Recipient. Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that Recipient continues to receive that support for a transitional period.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Additional Services**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information of Recipient that Provider or its personnel receive, access, generate or process in performing the Services. This includes plant operating data, process control records, production volumes and yields, assay results, feedstock supplier lists and terms, black-mass and metal offtake terms, pricing, customer information and employee information.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Service Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in **Schedule A**, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.4.

**1.2 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation". References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Services

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the Service Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient on the terms of this Agreement.

**2.2 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in substantially the same manner, scope, priority, volume and quality as Provider or its Affiliates provided the corresponding services to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date;

(b) with at least the same degree of care that Provider uses in providing similar services to its own businesses; and

(c) in compliance with all applicable Laws, including Environmental Laws

(together, the "**Service Standard**").

**2.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign personnel with appropriate qualifications and experience to perform the Services. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the personnel who provided the corresponding services before the Closing Date assigned to the Services. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and of any subcontractor it uses.

**2.4 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date and that is not listed on Schedule A, Recipient may request it by written notice. Provider shall provide that service on terms consistent with this Agreement, at a fee determined on the basis described in Section 5.1 (each, an "**Additional Service**"). The parties shall record each Additional Service in a written amendment to Schedule A.

**2.5 Third-Party Consents.** If the provision of any Service requires the consent of a third party, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent promptly. Until it is obtained, Provider shall cooperate with Recipient to provide a reasonable alternative arrangement. Provider shall bear the cost of obtaining those consents.

**2.6 Exclusions.** Provider is not required to provide legal advice or to make any filing with a Governmental Authority in its own name on Recipient's behalf, other than as described in Schedule A.

## 3. Term and Termination

**3.1 Service Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date. The Services shall be provided for a term ending 9 months after the Closing Date (the "**Service Term**"), unless this Agreement is terminated earlier under this Article 3.

**3.2 Early Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Service Fees for a terminated Service stop accruing on the effective date of termination. If a terminated Service is dependent on another Service, the parties shall agree in good faith on any reduction of the Service Fees for the dependent Service.

**3.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach. Provider may terminate for non-payment only if an undisputed amount remains unpaid for forty-five (45) days after written notice of non-payment.

**3.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or of any Service:

(a) Recipient shall pay all undisputed Service Fees accrued through the effective date of expiration or termination;

(b) Provider shall return or deliver to Recipient all Recipient Data in its possession or control, in the format in which it is held and in any other commonly used format that Recipient reasonably requests; and

(c) Articles 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 12 and Sections 3.4 and 5.4 survive.

## 4. Governance and Migration

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint a representative to coordinate the Services (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). The Service Coordinators shall meet at least every two weeks during the Service Term to review performance and the progress of migration.

**4.2 Migration Assistance.** During the Service Term, Provider shall give Recipient reasonable assistance in transferring each Service to Recipient or to a third-party provider that Recipient selects. This includes the following:

(a) providing data extracts and documentation;

(b) making knowledgeable personnel available to answer questions; and

(c) cooperating with Recipient's replacement providers.

**4.3 Dispute Escalation.** If the Service Coordinators cannot resolve a dispute about the Services within ten (10) Business Days, either party may refer it to a senior executive of each party. If those executives cannot resolve it within a further ten (10) Business Days, either party may pursue its remedies under Article 12.

## 5. Fees and Payment

**5.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the monthly fees for each Service set out in Schedule A (the "**Service Fees**"). The Service Fees are intended to equal Provider's actual cost of providing the Services, without mark-up. They include an allocable share of personnel costs and third-party costs.

**5.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice must itemize the Service Fees by Service. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**5.3 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice of the dispute before the payment due date. The parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 4.3. Continued performance of the Services is not affected by any dispute about Service Fees.

**5.4 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the Services. Each party is responsible for its own income Taxes.

**5.5 Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep accurate records supporting the Service Fees. Recipient may audit those records once during the Service Term and once within six (6) months after it ends, on fifteen (15) Business Days' notice.

## 6. Ownership of Data and Systems

**6.1 Recipient Data.** As between the parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider may use Recipient Data only to perform the Services.

**6.2 Systems.** Provider retains ownership of its own information technology systems used to provide the Services. Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, royalty-free right to access and use those systems during the Service Term to the extent necessary to receive the Services.

**6.3 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for Recipient Data that are at least as protective as those it applied before the Closing Date and that meet applicable industry standards. Provider shall notify Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Recipient Data.

## 7. Confidentiality

**7.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement. Recipient Data is Recipient's Confidential Information.

**7.2 Obligations.** Each receiving party shall:

(a) use the other party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement;

(b) disclose it only to its personnel and subcontractors who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article 7; and

(c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

**7.3 Exceptions.** The obligations in Section 7.2 do not apply to information that the receiving party can show:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than through its breach;

(b) was lawfully known to it without restriction before disclosure; or

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

A receiving party may disclose Confidential Information if required by Law, after giving the disclosing party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective treatment.

**7.4 Duration.** This Article 7 continues for five (5) years after the end of the Service Term. For trade secrets, it continues for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable Law.

## 8. Warranties and Liability

**8.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

**8.2 Disclaimer.** Except as set out in this Agreement, Provider makes no warranty about the Services, express or implied.

**8.3 Limitation of Liability.** Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement. Provider's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. These limitations do not apply to:

(a) breach of Article 6 or Article 7;

(b) gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud; or

(c) a party's indemnification obligations under Article 9.

## 9. Indemnification

**9.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify Recipient against all Losses arising from:

(a) Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Article 6 or Article 7; and

(b) any violation of Environmental Laws by Provider or its personnel in performing the Services.

**9.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify Provider against all Losses arising from Recipient's gross negligence or willful misconduct in receiving the Services.

**9.3 Procedures.** The procedures in Section 8.5 of the Purchase Agreement apply to claims under this Article 9, with the necessary changes.

## 10. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

**10.1 No Merger.** This Agreement is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into, limited or expanded by this Agreement.

## 11. Force Majeure

**11.1 Excused Performance.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, fire, flood, hurricane, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action and failure of public utilities. The affected party shall give prompt notice, use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance, and keep the other party informed. Service Fees for any Service not provided are suspended during the period of non-performance.
