# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Seller**"), in favor of **Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and **Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of May 11, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller is selling to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller owns certain battery discharge, shredding, sieving and black-mass separation equipment located at the Company's battery recycling plant in Baytown, Texas and used in the Business.

C. Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to convey the Shredding Line Assets to the Company at the Closing by executing and delivering this Bill of Sale.

Seller therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Shredding Line Assets.** "**Shredding Line Assets**" means the battery discharge, shredding, sieving and black-mass separation equipment located at the Facility that is owned by Seller and used in the Business, as listed on **Schedule 1**. This includes all of the following that relate to that equipment:

(a) spare parts, wear parts, tooling and consumables held at the Facility;

(b) control panels, programmable logic controllers, sensors and instrumentation that are physically installed on or dedicated to the listed equipment, together with any embedded firmware;

(c) operating manuals, maintenance logs, inspection records, drawings and calibration records; and

(d) to the extent transferable, all manufacturers' and vendors' warranties, service rights and guarantees.

## 2. Conveyance

**2.1 Transfer.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller hereby sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Shredding Line Assets. The conveyance is free and clear of all liens, security interests and other encumbrances.

**2.2 Delivery in Place.** The Shredding Line Assets are located at the Facility and are delivered to the Company in place as of the Effective Date. Seller does not need to move or disassemble any of the Shredding Line Assets.

**2.3 Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of the Shredding Line Assets pass to the Company on the Effective Date.

## 3. Excluded Items

**3.1 No Assumption of Liabilities.** The Company does not assume, and this Bill of Sale does not transfer to the Company, any liability or obligation of Seller or its Affiliates of any kind.

**3.2 Recycling Process Technology.** This Bill of Sale does not convey any intellectual property. Recycling Process Technology is assigned under the IP Assignment Agreement in accordance with Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. Title; Purchase Agreement

**4.1 Title.** Seller represents that it holds good and valid title to each of the Shredding Line Assets. It also represents that, upon delivery of this Bill of Sale, the Company will hold good and valid title to the Shredding Line Assets, free and clear of all liens, security interests and other encumbrances.

**4.2 Purchase Agreement Remedies.** This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities in the Purchase Agreement, including Article 8, are not merged into, limited or expanded by this Bill of Sale.

## 5. Further Assurances

**5.1 Further Instruments.** At the Company's or Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver any further deeds, certificates of title, lien releases and other instruments of conveyance. Seller shall also take any other actions reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Shredding Line Assets and to put the Company in actual possession and operating control of them.

**5.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive any of the Shredding Line Assets. The Company may also, in Seller's name, endorse and collect any instrument relating to the Shredding Line Assets and enforce any warranty described in Section 1.2(d). This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**5.3 Assets Received After the Effective Date.** If Seller or any of its Affiliates receives or holds any Shredding Line Asset, or any payment under a warranty relating to a Shredding Line Asset, after the Effective Date, it shall hold that asset or payment in trust for the Company. It shall promptly deliver the asset or payment to the Company.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It benefits the Company and its successors and assigns.

**6.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**6.3 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought in accordance with Section 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.4 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronically delivered signature has the same effect as an original.

**6.5 Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Bill of Sale.
