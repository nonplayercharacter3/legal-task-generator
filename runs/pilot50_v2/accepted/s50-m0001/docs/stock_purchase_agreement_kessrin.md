# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**EXECUTION VERSION**

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of May 11, 2026, between **Mouloul Energy Materials, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Porert Industrial Holdings, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kessrin Cell Recovery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company operates a lithium-ion battery recycling business. The business discharges and shreds end-of-life cells and recovers lithium, cobalt and nickel from black mass by hydrometallurgical processing (the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings below:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the specified Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the IP Assignment Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement and each other certificate, instrument or agreement delivered under this Agreement.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale in the form attached as **Exhibit A**. By the Bill of Sale, Seller conveys the Shredding Line Assets to the Company.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means current assets of the Company minus current liabilities of the Company, excluding cash, Indebtedness and income Tax assets and liabilities. It is determined as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day before the Closing Date in accordance with the Accounting Principles set out in Schedule 2.3.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all applicable Laws relating to pollution, protection of the environment or natural resources, or the generation, handling, storage, transport, disposal or Release of Hazardous Materials. This includes the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act, the Clean Water Act and their state analogues.

"**Facility**" means the Company's battery recycling plant located in Baytown, Texas, which the Company leases under the Facility Lease.

"**Facility Lease**" means the lease agreement for the Facility listed in Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any court, agency, commission or other regulatory body of any of them.

"**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance regulated under Environmental Laws. This includes electrolyte solvents, lithium hexafluorophosphate, heavy metals, process liquors, leach residues and black mass.

"**Indebtedness**" means all obligations of the Company for borrowed money, capital leases, deferred purchase price of property, and guarantees of any of these obligations.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the intellectual property assignment agreement in the form attached as **Exhibit B**.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Letter of Intent**" means the letter of intent between Buyer and Seller relating to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company. It excludes effects resulting from general economic conditions, changes in Law, or conditions generally affecting the battery recycling industry, except to the extent those effects disproportionately affect the Company.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Phase II Assessment**" means the Phase II environmental site assessment of the Facility. It is to be conducted by an independent environmental consultant selected by Buyer and reasonably acceptable to Seller.

"**Recycling Process Technology**" means patents, trade secrets, know-how, process parameters and software used in the Company's hydrometallurgical metal recovery.

"**Release**" means any release, spill, emission, leaking, pumping, discharge, disposal or migration into the environment.

"**Remediation Costs**" means the aggregate reasonably estimated cost of investigation, removal, remediation, corrective action and monitoring required under Environmental Laws to address conditions identified in the Phase II Assessment. These costs are as estimated by the consultant performing the Phase II Assessment.

"**Shredding Line Assets**" means the battery discharge, shredding, sieving and black-mass separation equipment located at the Facility that is owned by Seller and used in the Business, as listed on Schedule 1 to the Bill of Sale.

"**Target Working Capital**" means $4,250,000.

"**Tax**" means any federal, state, local or foreign income, gross receipts, franchise, sales, use, property, payroll, withholding or other tax, together with any interest or penalty on it.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the transition services agreement in the form attached as **Exhibit C**, between Seller, as provider, and the Company, as recipient.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation". References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) $4,320,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to Wilmington Trust, N.A., as escrow agent, to be held under an escrow agreement in customary form as security for Seller's obligations under Article 8;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness identified in payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv); and

(c) the balance of the Purchase Price, as estimated under Section 2.3(a), to an account designated by Seller.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital. If the estimate exceeds Target Working Capital, the Purchase Price payable at Closing is increased by the excess. If it is less than Target Working Capital, the Purchase Price is reduced by the shortfall.

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of its calculation of Closing Working Capital. Seller has thirty (30) days to dispute the statement by written notice. If the parties cannot resolve a dispute within thirty (30) days after that notice, they shall submit it to an independent accounting firm of national standing that they mutually select. That firm's determination is final and binding.

(c) The parties shall pay any net adjustment within five (5) Business Days after Closing Working Capital is finally determined.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article 7 are satisfied or waived, other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Closing Deliveries.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(ii) the Bill of Sale, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(iii) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(iv) payoff letters for all Indebtedness, with releases of related liens;

(v) a certificate of the Secretary of the Company attaching its organizational documents and good standing certificates; and

(vi) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Section 2.2, the Transition Services Agreement duly executed by the Company, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Texas. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share. All 1,000 of those shares are issued and outstanding and are held of record and beneficially by Seller. There are no options, warrants or other rights to acquire equity of the Company.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company or any applicable Law. Except as set out in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, they do not require any consent under any material contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, the related statements of income and cash flows, and the unaudited balance sheet as of March 31, 2026. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Company.

**4.5 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has operated in the ordinary course and there has been no Material Adverse Effect.

**4.6 Material Contracts.** Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each feedstock supply agreement, black-mass and metal offtake agreement, and each other contract of the Company involving annual payments above $250,000. Each is valid and binding and in full force. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any of them.

**4.7 Intellectual Property.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all patents and patent applications included in the Recycling Process Technology. The Recycling Process Technology, together with the intellectual property owned by the Company, constitutes all intellectual property necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**4.8 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. The Facility Lease is valid and binding and in full force. The Company is not in default under it.

**4.9 Environmental Matters.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in material compliance with Environmental Laws. The Company holds and complies with all permits required under Environmental Laws, including its air permit and its hazardous waste storage permit for the Facility. There has been no Release of Hazardous Materials at the Facility that requires investigation or remediation under Environmental Laws. The Company has not received any notice of violation or liability under Environmental Laws.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Shares.

**4.11 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns it is required to file and has paid all Taxes due.

**4.12 Employees.** Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all employees of the Company and their compensation. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.13 Brokers.** Except for Halvorsen Partners, whose fees Seller shall pay, no broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at Closing will have, sufficient funds available to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to their distribution.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with this Agreement on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to maintain its permits, assets and relationships with feedstock suppliers and offtakers. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not permit the Company to issue equity, incur Indebtedness outside the ordinary course, sell material assets, or amend any material contract.

**6.2 Access and Environmental Investigation.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the Facility, books, records and personnel of the Company. Buyer may cause the Phase II Assessment to be performed, including soil, groundwater and process-area sampling. Seller shall cause the Company to cooperate with the consultant. Buyer shall restore any disturbed areas.

**6.3 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the conditions in Article 7 to be satisfied. Each party shall make any filings required under the HSR Act within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this Agreement.

**6.4 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit or engage in negotiations with any Person other than Buyer concerning the sale of the Company or the Business.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information concerning the Company and the Business, except as required by Law.

**6.6 Recycling Process Technology.** At or prior to the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, assign to the Company all Recycling Process Technology owned by Seller or any of its Affiliates, free and clear of all liens. This assignment shall be made by Seller's execution and delivery of the IP Assignment Agreement. Seller shall execute such further instruments as Buyer reasonably requests to record or perfect that assignment.

**6.7 Shredding Line Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall convey the Shredding Line Assets to the Company by executing and delivering the Bill of Sale.

**6.8 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and the Company shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement. Under it, Seller shall provide to the Company the services described in it for the term set out in it.

**6.9 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in lithium-ion battery recycling or black-mass processing anywhere in North America. During the same period, Seller shall not solicit for employment any employee of the Company.

**6.10 Insurance.** Seller shall cause the Company's existing occurrence-based insurance policies to remain in effect through the Closing. After the Closing, Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to give the Company the benefit of those policies for pre-Closing occurrences.

**6.11 Notice of Developments.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

## ARTICLE 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the following conditions:

(a) any applicable waiting period under the HSR Act has expired or been terminated; and

(b) no Law or order is in effect that prohibits the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer are further subject to the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects its covenants to be performed before the Closing;

(c) Buyer has received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of Sections 7.2(a) and (b);

(d) the consents listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule have been obtained, including the landlord's consent under the Facility Lease and a landlord estoppel certificate in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(e) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred; and

(f) Seller has delivered each item listed in Section 3.2(a).

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller are further subject to the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer has performed in all material respects its covenants; and

(c) Seller has received a certificate of an officer of Buyer to that effect.

## ARTICLE 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for eighteen (18) months. The exceptions are those in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.13 (the "**Fundamental Representations**"), which survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, and those in Section 4.9, which survive for five (5) years. Covenants survive until fully performed.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after Closing, the Company) and their representatives against all Losses arising from:

(a) any inaccuracy in any representation or warranty of Seller;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller;

(c) any Indebtedness or transaction expense not paid at Closing; and

(d) any Release of Hazardous Materials at the Facility occurring before the Closing Date.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Buyer shall indemnify Seller against all Losses arising from any inaccuracy in any representation or warranty of Buyer or any breach of any covenant of Buyer.

**8.4 Limitations.** Seller has no liability under Section 8.2(a), other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud, until aggregate Losses exceed $648,000. Once that amount is exceeded, Seller is liable from the first dollar. Seller's aggregate liability under Section 8.2(a), other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud, shall not exceed $8,640,000. Aggregate liability for Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**8.5 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of any claim. Delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the delay. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

**8.6 Escrow.** Claims by Buyer shall first be satisfied from the Escrow Amount. Any remaining Escrow Amount not subject to pending claims shall be released to Seller eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

**8.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for fraud, claims for specific performance and claims under Section 2.3, this Article 8 is the exclusive remedy of the parties for matters arising under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 9. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by September 30, 2026. This right is not available to a party whose breach caused the failure to close;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice;

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(e) by Buyer, if the Phase II Assessment reports estimated Remediation Costs exceeding $1,500,000. To exercise this right, Buyer must deliver written notice to Seller, accompanied by a copy of the Phase II Assessment report, no later than forty-five (45) days after the date of the Letter of Intent.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 9.1, it becomes void and no party has any liability under it. However, Section 6.5, this Section 9.2 and Article 11 survive, and termination does not relieve any party of liability for willful breach or fraud.

## ARTICLE 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall indemnify Buyer and the Company against all Taxes of the Company for any period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** For any period beginning before and ending after the Closing Date, Taxes are allocated on a closing-of-the-books basis. Property Taxes are the exception and are allocated ratably by day.

**10.3 Transfer Taxes.** Seller and Buyer shall each bear one half of any transfer, documentary, sales or similar Taxes arising from the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**10.4 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate in the preparation of Tax returns and in any Tax audit relating to the Company.
