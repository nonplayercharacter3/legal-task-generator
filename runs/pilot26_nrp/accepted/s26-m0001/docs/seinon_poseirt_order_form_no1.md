# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Order Form No. 1** (this "**Order Form**") is entered into under, and is governed by, the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between **Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Poseirt Commerce Logistics, LLC** ("**Provider**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Order Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Order Form number | 1 |
| Order Form Effective Date | April 1, 2026 |
| Customer | Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc. |
| Provider | Poseirt Commerce Logistics, LLC |
| Facility | Provider's warehouse fulfillment facility, Reno, Nevada |
| Storefront | Customer's branded direct-to-consumer e-commerce website, hosted on the Platform |
| Customer contact | Director of E-Commerce Operations |
| Provider contact | Account Director, Seinon account |

"**Order Form Effective Date**" means April 1, 2026.

## 2. Term

The Initial Term of this Order Form is 60 months from the Order Form Effective Date. This Order Form renews in accordance with Section 9.2 of the Agreement.

## 3. Fees

**3.1 Monthly Platform Fee.** Customer will pay Provider a monthly platform fee of $48,500 (the "**Monthly Platform Fee**"). The Monthly Platform Fee covers Storefront hosting, operation and maintenance on the Platform, order management, standard Platform updates, and access for up to twenty-five (25) named Customer users.

**3.2 Fulfillment Fees.** In addition to the Monthly Platform Fee, Customer will pay the following fulfillment fees:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Inbound receiving (per carton) | $2.10 |
| Storage (per pallet position, per month) | $24.00 |
| Storage (per shelf bin, per month) | $3.50 |
| Pick and pack, first unit (per order) | $2.75 |
| Pick and pack, each additional unit (same order) | $0.55 |
| Standard packaging materials (per order) | $0.85 |
| Returns processing (per unit) | $3.25 |
| Special projects and kitting (per labor hour) | $42.00 |

**3.3 Shipping Charges.** Outbound carrier charges are passed through to Customer at Provider's actual cost under Provider's negotiated carrier rates, without markup.

**3.4 Invoicing.** Provider will invoice the Monthly Platform Fee and all fulfillment fees and shipping charges for each calendar month in arrears, in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Agreement. The Monthly Platform Fee for any partial month will be prorated on a daily basis.

**3.5 Fee Adjustments.** Fees are fixed for the first twenty-four (24) months of the Initial Term. After that, Provider may increase the fulfillment fees in Section 3.2 once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No increase may exceed three percent (3%) over the fees then in effect.

## 4. Exclusivity

During the term of this Order Form, Customer will use Provider as its exclusive provider of all fulfillment, warehousing and logistics services for all Seinon products in all sales channels worldwide, including direct-to-consumer e-commerce, wholesale distribution and retail-store replenishment. Customer will not engage, and will cause its Affiliates not to engage, any third party to provide those services during the term of this Order Form.

## 5. Scope of Services

**5.1 Storefront Services.** Provider will provide the Storefront and Platform services described in Section 2.1 of the Agreement, including:

(a) hosting of the Storefront on the Platform, with checkout, payment gateway integration and tax calculation;

(b) order management, including order routing to the Facility, order status notifications to end customers, and cancellation and modification handling;

(c) integration of the Platform with Customer's enterprise resource planning system through Provider's standard API; and

(d) configuration of the Storefront using the Customer Materials, including up to two (2) seasonal design refreshes each contract year.

**5.2 Fulfillment Services.** Provider will provide the fulfillment services described in Section 2.2 of the Agreement at the Facility, including:

(a) receipt and inspection of inbound shipments against advance shipping notices;

(b) storage of Products in a climate-controlled area of the Facility;

(c) pick, pack, labeling and shipment of Storefront orders to end customers in the United States and Canada;

(d) processing of end-customer returns, including inspection, grading and restocking or disposition according to Customer's written fulfillment guidelines; and

(e) gift messaging and branded packaging inserts supplied by Customer.

**5.3 Order Cut-Off.** Provider will ship on the same business day all Storefront orders received before 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time on a business day. Orders received after that time will ship on the next business day.

## 6. Implementation

**6.1 Implementation Plan.** Within fifteen (15) days after the Order Form Effective Date, the parties will agree a written implementation plan covering Storefront configuration, data migration from Customer's current storefront, Platform integration testing and initial inventory transfer to the Facility.

**6.2 Go-Live.** The parties target Storefront go-live and commencement of fulfillment at the Facility within ninety (90) days after the Order Form Effective Date. Go-live will occur only after Customer has given written acceptance of user acceptance testing.

**6.3 Implementation Fee.** Implementation services are included in the Monthly Platform Fee. Provider will not charge a separate implementation fee.

## 7. Inventory Management

**7.1 Cycle Counts.** For purposes of Section 6.3 of the Agreement, Provider will perform cycle counts so that every active SKU is counted at least once each calendar quarter, and the top twenty percent (20%) of SKUs by unit volume are counted at least monthly. Provider will report cycle-count results to Customer within five (5) business days after each count.

**7.2 Inventory Accuracy.** Provider will maintain inventory record accuracy at the Facility of at least 99.5% by unit, measured as described in the Service Level Schedule.

**7.3 Reporting.** Provider will give Customer real-time access through the Platform to on-hand, allocated and available inventory by SKU, and will deliver a monthly inventory reconciliation report.
