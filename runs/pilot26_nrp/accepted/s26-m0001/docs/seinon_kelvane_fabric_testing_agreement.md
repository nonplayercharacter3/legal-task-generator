# FABRIC TESTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Fabric Testing Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seinon**"), and **Kelvane Textile Labs, Inc.**, a Massachusetts corporation ("**Lab**"). Seinon and Lab are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## Recitals

A. Seinon designs, manufactures and sells technical outdoor apparel and related accessories.

B. Lab operates an independent textile testing laboratory. The laboratory performs waterproofing, water-repellency, breathability and abrasion-resistance testing of fabrics and finished garments.

C. Seinon wishes to engage Lab to test fabric samples and garment components, and Lab wishes to perform that testing, in each case on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Deliverables**" means the test reports, data sets, photographs and other written results that Lab prepares for Seinon under a Testing Order.

(b) "**Lab Methods**" means Lab's proprietary procedures, know-how, equipment configurations, software and internal protocols, other than the published standards identified in a Testing Order.

(c) "**Samples**" means the fabric swatches, laminates, coated textiles, trims, seam constructions and finished garments that Seinon delivers to Lab for testing.

(d) "**Testing Order**" means a written order, substantially in the form of Schedule B, that both parties sign and that identifies the Samples, the tests to be performed, the applicable test standards, the turnaround time and the fees.

(e) "**Testing Services**" means the testing and reporting services described in Section 2 and in each Testing Order.

## 2. Testing Services

**2.1 Performance of Testing.** Lab will perform the Testing Services described in each Testing Order. Each Testing Order is governed by this Agreement. Lab has no obligation to perform any testing that is not described in a signed Testing Order.

**2.2 Test Standards.** Lab will perform each test in accordance with the published standard identified in the applicable Testing Order and the edition of that standard in effect on the date of the Testing Order. Typical standards include:

(a) AATCC 127 or ISO 811 (hydrostatic pressure);

(b) AATCC 22 or ISO 4920 (spray rating);

(c) ASTM D4966 or ISO 12947-2 (Martindale abrasion); and

(d) ISO 11092 or ASTM E96 (water-vapor resistance and transmission).

If a Testing Order requests a deviation from a published standard, Lab will perform the test as modified and will describe the deviation in the Deliverable.

**2.3 Accreditation.** Lab will maintain its accreditation to ISO/IEC 17025 for each test method listed in Schedule A throughout the term of this Agreement. Lab will notify Seinon in writing within five (5) business days if any such accreditation is suspended, withdrawn or reduced in scope.

**2.4 Turnaround.** Lab will deliver the Deliverables for each Testing Order within the turnaround time stated in that Testing Order. If no turnaround time is stated, the standard turnaround time in Schedule A applies. Lab will promptly notify Seinon if it expects any delay.

**2.5 Non-Exclusive Engagement.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. Seinon may engage any other laboratory to perform testing of any kind, and Lab may provide testing services to any other person.

## 3. Samples

**3.1 Delivery.** Seinon will deliver Samples to Lab's laboratory at its own cost, together with a sample submission form identifying each Sample, its fabric construction and any pre-treatment or laundering that Seinon requests.

**3.2 Condition on Receipt.** Lab will inspect each Sample on receipt. Lab will notify Seinon within two (2) business days if any Sample is damaged, insufficient in size or quantity, or otherwise unsuitable for the requested test. The turnaround time for an affected Testing Order begins when Lab receives a suitable replacement Sample.

**3.3 Custody and Handling.** Lab will store Samples in a controlled environment that conforms to the conditioning requirements of the applicable test standard. Lab will maintain chain-of-custody records for each Sample from receipt to disposal or return.

**3.4 Title and Disposal.** Title to all Samples remains with Seinon. Seinon acknowledges that many tests consume or destroy the Samples. Lab will retain any untested remnants for ninety (90) days after delivering the related Deliverable. At the end of that period, Lab will return the remnants at Seinon's request and cost, or will otherwise dispose of them securely.

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1 Fees.** Seinon will pay the fees for each Testing Order at the rates in Schedule A, unless the Testing Order states different fees. Expedited turnaround is charged at the surcharge stated in Schedule A.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Lab will invoice Seinon after delivering the Deliverables for each Testing Order. Seinon will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it.

**4.3 Disputed Amounts.** Seinon may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Lab in writing of the dispute and the reasons for it before the payment due date. The parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

**4.4 Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Seinon will pay those taxes, except taxes based on Lab's net income, property or employees.

**4.5 Rate Changes.** Lab may change the rates in Schedule A once in any calendar year on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. A rate change does not apply to any Testing Order signed before the change takes effect.

## 5. Quality and Retesting

**5.1 Standard of Performance.** Lab will perform the Testing Services in a professional manner, using qualified personnel and properly calibrated equipment, in accordance with good laboratory practice and the applicable test standards.

**5.2 Calibration Records.** Lab will maintain calibration records for all equipment used in the Testing Services, traceable to national measurement standards. Lab will make those records available to Seinon on reasonable request.

**5.3 Retesting.** If Seinon reasonably believes that a Deliverable is inaccurate because of Lab's error, it may notify Lab within thirty (30) days after receiving the Deliverable. Lab will then review the result. If Lab's error is confirmed, Lab will retest at no charge, using retained or replacement Samples, and will issue a corrected Deliverable.

## 6. Personnel

**6.1 Qualified Personnel.** Lab will assign qualified technicians to perform the Testing Services under the supervision of a laboratory manager. Lab may replace personnel at its discretion, provided the replacement is equally qualified.

**6.2 Site Visits.** On reasonable advance notice and during normal business hours, Seinon's representatives may visit Lab's laboratory to observe testing of Seinon's Samples. During any visit, Seinon's representatives will comply with Lab's safety and confidentiality rules.

## 7. Intellectual Property

**7.1 Lab Methods.** Lab retains all right, title and interest in and to the Lab Methods. Nothing in this Agreement grants Seinon any right in the Lab Methods, except as needed to use the Deliverables.

**7.2 Seinon Materials.** Seinon retains all right, title and interest in and to the Samples and in all fabric constructions, formulations, specifications and other information that Seinon provides to Lab.

**7.3 Deliverables.** Upon payment in full of the fees for the applicable Testing Order, the Deliverables become the property of Seinon. To the extent any Lab Methods are included in a Deliverable, Lab grants Seinon a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use them as part of that Deliverable.

**7.4 Use of Results.** Seinon may use the Deliverables for any lawful purpose, including in product specifications, regulatory filings and communications with suppliers and customers. Seinon will not state or imply in consumer advertising that Lab endorses any Seinon product without Lab's prior written consent. Seinon may, however, accurately state that a product was tested by an independent laboratory to an identified standard.

## 8. Confidentiality

**8.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one party (the "**Discloser**") to the other party (the "**Recipient**") that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Seinon's Confidential Information includes the Samples, all information about unreleased products and fabric constructions, and the Deliverables.

**8.2 Obligations.** The Recipient will:

(a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement;

(b) protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care; and

(c) disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 8.

**8.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;

(b) was known to the Recipient without restriction before it was disclosed;

(c) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**8.4 Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law. Where legally permitted, the Recipient will give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

**8.5 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 8 continue for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement. Obligations relating to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. Warranties

**9.1 Lab Warranties.** Lab warrants that:

(a) it will perform the Testing Services in accordance with Section 5.1; and

(b) each Deliverable will accurately report the results of the tests performed on the Samples tested.

**9.2 Scope of Results.** Seinon acknowledges that test results apply only to the Samples tested, under the conditions reported. Lab does not warrant the performance of any production fabric or finished product.

**9.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. Indemnification

**10.1 By Lab.** Lab will defend Seinon and its officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim to the extent arising from Lab's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Testing Services, or from bodily injury or property damage caused by Lab's personnel while on Seinon's premises. Lab will indemnify them against all damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded on, or agreed in settlement of, that claim.

**10.2 By Seinon.** Seinon will defend Lab and its officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim to the extent arising from Seinon's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or from Seinon's misstatement of a Deliverable in its own materials. Seinon will indemnify them against all damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded on, or agreed in settlement of, that claim.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified party will:

(a) give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim, although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent the delay prejudices it;

(b) give the indemnifying party control of the defense and settlement of the claim; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation, at the indemnifying party's expense.

The indemnifying party may not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

**11.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party will be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**11.2 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the greater of (a) the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim and (b) one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000).

**11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a party's breach of Section 8 (Confidentiality);

(b) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or

(c) Seinon's obligation to pay fees properly due under Section 4.

## 12. Insurance

During the term of this Agreement, Lab will maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, the following insurance:

| Coverage | Minimum limit |
|---|---|
| Commercial general liability | $1,000,000 per occurrence |
| Professional liability (errors and omissions) | $2,000,000 per claim |
| Workers' compensation | Statutory limits |

Lab will provide certificates of insurance to Seinon on request.

## 13. Term and Termination

**13.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years. After that, it renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current period.

**13.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Testing Order for any reason on thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

**13.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

**13.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Lab will complete any testing in progress unless Seinon instructs otherwise. Seinon will pay for all Testing Services performed up to the effective date of termination. Lab will return or dispose of Samples in accordance with Section 3.4.

**13.5 Survival.** Sections 3.4, 7, 8, 9.3, 10, 11, 13.4, 14 and 15 survive expiration or termination.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

**14.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**14.2 Forum.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**14.3 Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than an action for injunctive relief, a party will give written notice of the dispute to the other party. Senior representatives of each party will then confer within fifteen (15) business days to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

## 15. General

**15.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices must be sent to the addresses below, or to any other address that a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

- **Seinon:** Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, legal@seinon.example
- **Lab:** Kelvane Textile Labs, Inc., Attention: Laboratory Director, contracts@kelvane.example

**15.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent. However, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets, if it gives written notice of the assignment. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.

**15.3 Subcontracting.** Lab will not subcontract any Testing Services without Seinon's prior written consent. Lab remains responsible for any approved subcontractor's performance.

**15.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, excluding payment obligations. If a force majeure event prevents Lab from performing a Testing Order for more than fifteen (15) days, Seinon may cancel that Testing Order on written notice without charge for unperformed testing.

**15.5 Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**15.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of that right.

**15.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

**15.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
