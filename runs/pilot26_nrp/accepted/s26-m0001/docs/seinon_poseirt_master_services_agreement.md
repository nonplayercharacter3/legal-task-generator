# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Poseirt Commerce Logistics, LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each referred to as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer designs, manufactures and sells technical outdoor apparel and related accessories.

B. Provider operates a hosted e-commerce storefront and order management platform. It also operates a warehouse fulfillment facility in Reno, Nevada.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to host Customer's direct-to-consumer e-commerce storefront and to provide related order fulfillment services, and Provider wishes to provide those services, in each case on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Structure

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms have the meanings given below or elsewhere in this Agreement.

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

(b) "**Customer Data**" means all data that Customer provides to Provider, or that Provider collects or processes on Customer's behalf in performing the Services. Customer Data includes Shopper Data, order data, pricing and inventory data.

(c) "**Customer Materials**" means all trademarks, logos, product descriptions, images, content, specifications and other materials that Customer provides for use in the Storefront or the Services.

(d) "**Facility**" means Provider's warehouse fulfillment facility located in Reno, Nevada, and any replacement facility approved in writing by Customer.

(e) "**Fees**" means the fees set out in an Order Form.

(f) "**Initial Term**" means, for each Order Form, the initial term stated in that Order Form.

(g) "**Order Form**" means an ordering document that references this Agreement, is signed by both parties, and describes the Services to be provided, the Fees and the Initial Term.

(h) "**Platform**" means Provider's proprietary hosted e-commerce storefront, checkout and order management software, together with all updates and modifications.

(i) "**Products**" means the goods that Customer offers for sale through the Storefront or delivers to the Facility for storage and fulfillment.

(j) "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Customer Data, including Shopper Data, while that data is in the possession, custody or control of Provider or its subcontractors.

(k) "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule agreed by the parties that sets out the service levels, measurement methods and service credits applicable to the Services.

(l) "**Services**" means the Storefront hosting, order management, warehousing, pick-pack-ship and related services described in this Agreement and in an Order Form.

(m) "**Shopper Data**" means personal information relating to Customer's end customers, including names, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, order histories and payment-related data.

(n) "**Storefront**" means Customer's branded direct-to-consumer e-commerce website, hosted on the Platform.

**1.2 Order Forms.** Provider will perform the Services described in each Order Form. Each Order Form is governed by this Agreement and incorporates it by reference. Each Order Form forms a separate contract between the parties, comprising that Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and this Agreement.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising the parties' agreement, the documents will govern in the following order: Order Form, then Service Level Schedule, then Master Services Agreement.

## 2. Services

**2.1 Storefront and Platform Services.** Provider will host, operate and maintain the Storefront on the Platform. Provider will make the Storefront available to Customer's end customers in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. Provider will configure the Storefront using the Customer Materials and Customer's reasonable design specifications.

**2.2 Fulfillment Services.** Provider will receive Products at the Facility, inspect inbound shipments against advance shipping notices, store Products, and pick, pack, label and ship orders placed through the Storefront. Provider will also process customer returns, in each case in accordance with Customer's written fulfillment guidelines and the applicable Order Form.

**2.3 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform parts of the Services, with Customer's prior written consent, which Customer will not unreasonably withhold. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were Provider's own.

**2.4 Exclusivity.** During the term of each Order Form, Customer will use Provider as its exclusive provider of direct-to-consumer e-commerce fulfillment of Seinon products shipped to the United States and Canada. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Customer from selling, distributing or fulfilling Products through any other channel or in any other territory, or from engaging any third party to do so.

**2.5 Changes.** Either party may request changes to the scope of the Services. No change is binding unless it is set out in a written change order or an amended Order Form signed by both parties.

## 3. Service Levels

**3.1 Performance.** Provider will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with good industry practice and the service levels in the Service Level Schedule.

**3.2 Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider will issue the service credits set out in the Service Level Schedule. Provider will report its performance against the service levels to Customer monthly.

## 4. Customer Obligations

**4.1 Products and Materials.** Customer will deliver Products to the Facility properly packaged and labeled, together with accurate advance shipping notices. Customer will supply the Customer Materials in the formats reasonably specified by Provider.

**4.2 Compliance.** Customer is responsible for ensuring that the Products and Customer Materials comply with applicable law, including product safety, labeling, and country-of-origin marking requirements.

**4.3 Cooperation.** Customer will provide timely information, approvals and access to personnel reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services.

## 5. Fees and Payment

**5.1 Fees.** Customer will pay the Fees set out in each Order Form.

**5.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider will invoice Fees monthly in arrears, unless the Order Form states otherwise. Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it.

**5.3 Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount that it disputes in good faith, if it notifies Provider in writing of the dispute and the reasons for it before the payment due date. The parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**5.4 Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer will pay those taxes, except taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

**5.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts that are not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 6. Inventory and Risk of Loss

**6.1 Title.** Title to all Products remains with Customer at all times. Provider will not grant or allow any lien, security interest or other encumbrance on the Products. Provider waives any warehouseman's lien on the Products, to the extent permitted by law.

**6.2 Risk of Loss.** Provider bears the risk of loss of, or damage to, Products from the time Provider accepts them at the Facility until the carrier picks them up for outbound shipment. Provider will reimburse Customer for any lost or damaged Products at Customer's landed cost.

**6.3 Inventory Counts.** Provider will maintain accurate perpetual inventory records. Provider will also perform cycle counts in accordance with the Order Form, and will allow Customer to conduct a physical inventory audit at the Facility on reasonable notice.

## 7. Confidentiality

**7.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one party (the "**Discloser**") to the other party (the "**Recipient**") that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

**7.2 Obligations.** The Recipient will:

(a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement;

(b) protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care; and

(c) disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 7.

**7.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;

(b) was known to the Recipient without restriction before it was disclosed;

(c) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**7.4 Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law. Where legally permitted, the Recipient will give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

## 8. Data Protection and Security

**8.1 Processing.** Provider will process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions and only as necessary to perform the Services. Provider will comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws.

**8.2 Security Program.** Provider will maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Customer Data. Provider will maintain compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard for the portions of the Platform that store, process or transmit cardholder data.

**8.3 Security Incidents.** Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. Provider will also:

(a) investigate and remediate the Security Incident;

(b) provide Customer with information reasonably required to meet Customer's legal notification obligations; and

(c) reimburse Customer for its reasonable costs of notification, credit monitoring and call-center support arising from the Security Incident.

**8.4 Return of Data.** On termination or expiration of an Order Form, Provider will return all Customer Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format, and will then securely delete it. Provider need not delete Customer Data that it is required by law to retain.

## 9. Term and Termination

**9.1 Term of Agreement.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the last Order Form then in effect.

**9.2 Renewal.** Each Order Form will renew automatically at the end of its Initial Term, and at the end of each Renewal Term, for a successive renewal term equal in length to its Initial Term (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). An Order Form will not renew if either party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other party no earlier than 100 days and no later than 90 days before the expiration of the then-current term.

**9.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate an Order Form by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement or that Order Form and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

**9.4 Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party:

(a) becomes insolvent;

(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) has a receiver appointed for its business; or

(d) becomes the subject of a bankruptcy petition that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**9.5 Transition Assistance.** On expiration or termination of an Order Form for any reason, Provider will provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days, at the rates in effect under that Order Form. This assistance includes:

(a) the orderly transfer of the Storefront content and Customer Data; and

(b) the release and shipment of all Products to the location that Customer designates.

**9.6 Survival.** Sections 6.1, 7, 8.4, 9.5, 10, 11, 13, 14 and 15 survive expiration or termination.

## 10. Indemnification

**10.1 By Provider.** Provider will defend Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider, infringes or misappropriates that third party's patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret. Provider will indemnify them against all damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded on, or agreed in settlement of, that claim.

Provider has no obligation under this Section 10.1 to the extent a claim arises from:

(a) the Customer Materials; or

(b) a modification of the Platform made by anyone other than Provider.

**10.2 By Customer.** Customer will defend Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim arising out of or relating to the Products or the Customer Materials, regardless of whether caused in part by Provider handling, storage or shipment of the Products. Such claims include:

(a) claims for product liability, personal injury or property damage;

(b) claims relating to any product recall or market withdrawal;

(c) claims for infringement relating to the Customer Materials; and

(d) claims relating to customs, import, duties or tariff classification of the Products.

Customer will indemnify those persons against all resulting damages, losses, fines, penalties, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified party will:

(a) give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim, although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent the delay prejudices it;

(b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation, at the indemnifying party's expense.

The indemnifying party may not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

**11.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party will be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages. This exclusion does not apply to the matters described in Section 11.3.

**11.2 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement and all Order Forms will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 shall not apply to either party's indemnification obligations under Section 10. Those limitations also do not apply to:

(a) a party's breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality);

(b) Security Incidents, including Provider's obligations under Section 8.3; or

(c) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 12. Insurance

During the term of this Agreement and for one (1) year afterward, each party will maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, insurance coverage of the following types and minimum amounts:

| Party | Coverage | Minimum limit |
|---|---|---|
| Both parties | Commercial general liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| Provider | Warehouse legal liability | Full replacement value of Products at the Facility |
| Provider | Cyber and privacy liability | $10,000,000 per claim |
| Customer | Product liability | $5,000,000 per occurrence |

Each party will provide certificates of insurance to the other on request.

## 13. Intellectual Property

**13.1 Provider IP.** Provider retains all right, title and interest in and to the Platform and its pre-existing materials. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Platform during the term of each Order Form, solely for Customer's internal business purposes and the operation of the Storefront.

**13.2 Customer IP.** Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to the Customer Materials and Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the Customer Materials and Customer Data solely to perform the Services.

**13.3 Feedback.** Provider may use any suggestions that Customer provides about the Platform without obligation. However, Provider may not identify Customer as the source of those suggestions or use Customer's Confidential Information in doing so.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

**14.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**14.2 Forum.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**14.3 Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than an action for injunctive relief, a party will give written notice of the dispute to the other party. Senior executives of each party will then meet within fifteen (15) business days to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

**14.4 Injunctive Relief.** A breach of Section 7 or Section 8 may cause irreparable harm. The non-breaching party may therefore seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

## 15. General

**15.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices must be sent to the addresses below, or to any other address that a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

- **Customer:** Seinon Outdoor Gear, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, legal@seinon.example
- **Provider:** Poseirt Commerce Logistics, LLC, Attention: Contracts Department, contracts@poseirt.example

**15.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent. However, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets, if it gives written notice of the assignment. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.

**15.3 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control. This Section 15.3 does not excuse payment obligations, or Provider's obligations under Section 8 and its business continuity obligations. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form on written notice.

**15.4 Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**15.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of that right.

**15.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

**15.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
