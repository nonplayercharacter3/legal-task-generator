# FLEET REFRIGERATION MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This Fleet Refrigeration Maintenance Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of 3 March 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Lorvex Fleet Refrigeration Services, LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company with its principal place of business at 610 Industrial Park Drive, Coraopolis, Pennsylvania 15108 ("**Provider**"); and

**Praidein Flight Kitchens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1270 Cargo Access Road, Building C, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania 15231 ("**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates flight kitchens and a fleet of refrigerated high-lift catering trucks and delivery vans that transport chilled meals and galley equipment between its flight kitchens and aircraft.

B. Provider inspects, maintains and repairs transport refrigeration units and refrigerated vehicle bodies.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider is willing to be engaged, to maintain and repair the refrigeration equipment on Customer's fleet on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Covered Vehicles**" means the vehicles listed in Schedule A, as updated under Section 2.4.

"**Refrigeration Equipment**" means the transport refrigeration units, compressors, condensers, evaporators, temperature controllers, data loggers, door seals and insulated body panels installed on the Covered Vehicles.

"**Emergency Repair**" means a repair needed because Refrigeration Equipment on a Covered Vehicle cannot hold the cargo compartment at or below 41°F (5°C), or because the vehicle cannot otherwise be safely used for chilled food transport.

"**Fees**" means the fees set out in Schedule B.

"**Preventive Maintenance**" means the scheduled inspection and servicing described in Section 2.2.

"**Service Locations**" means Customer's flight kitchens at Pittsburgh (PIT), Columbus (CMH) and Buffalo (BUF), and any other location the parties agree in writing.

"**Services**" means the Preventive Maintenance, Emergency Repairs and other services described in Section 2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement. "Days" means calendar days. The Schedules form part of this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Scope.** Provider shall perform the Services for the Covered Vehicles in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations for the Refrigeration Equipment, good industry practice and applicable law.

**2.2 Preventive Maintenance.** Provider shall inspect and service the Refrigeration Equipment on each Covered Vehicle once every 90 days, or more often if the manufacturer recommends it. Each service shall include a check of refrigerant levels and leaks, compressor and fan operation, belts and hoses, electrical connections, door seals, defrost cycles and thermostat calibration, and a download and review of temperature data-logger records. Provider shall schedule Preventive Maintenance with Customer's fleet manager at least 7 days in advance and, where reasonably practicable, between 22:00 and 04:00 local time to minimize disruption to flight departures.

**2.3 Emergency Repairs.** Provider shall maintain a 24-hour, 7-day dispatch line. For an Emergency Repair at a Service Location, Provider shall have a qualified technician on site within 3 hours after Customer's call, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore the Refrigeration Equipment to service within 8 hours. If a repair cannot be completed within that time, Provider shall, at Customer's request and where available, provide a loaner refrigerated vehicle or portable refrigeration unit at the rate in Schedule B.

**2.4 Changes to Covered Vehicles.** Customer may add or remove Covered Vehicles by written notice to Provider. An added vehicle becomes a Covered Vehicle 14 days after the notice, following an initial inspection by Provider at the rate in Schedule B. A removed vehicle ceases to be a Covered Vehicle at the end of the calendar month in which the notice is given, and the monthly Fee shall be adjusted accordingly.

**2.5 Parts.** Provider shall use new original-equipment-manufacturer parts or parts of equal quality. Provider shall obtain Customer's approval before installing any part that costs more than US$1,500, except during an Emergency Repair, when Provider shall notify Customer promptly afterward.

**2.6 Records and Reports.** Provider shall keep a service record for each Covered Vehicle and, within 10 days after the end of each month, deliver to Customer a report of all Services performed, parts installed, temperature excursions identified and recommended corrective actions. Provider shall make the records available to Customer, its airline customers and food-safety auditors on reasonable request.

**2.7 Food Safety and Site Rules.** While at a Service Location or airside, Provider's personnel shall comply with Customer's food-safety, hygiene, security and airport ramp rules notified to Provider, and shall hold any airport identification badges required.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

**3.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Schedule B. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice the monthly maintenance Fee in arrears at the end of each month, and time-and-materials charges monthly in arrears with supporting job tickets.

**3.3 Payment Terms.** Invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider in writing before the due date, and the parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

**3.4 Price Review.** The Fees are fixed for the Initial Term. Any change in the Fees for a Renewal Term shall be agreed in writing under Section 10.2.

## 4. PERSONNEL

**4.1 Qualified Technicians.** Provider shall assign technicians who hold EPA Section 608 certification and are trained on the makes and models of Refrigeration Equipment on the Covered Vehicles.

**4.2 Replacement.** Provider may replace assigned technicians on reasonable notice. Customer may require Provider to remove from its sites any technician who breaches Customer's site rules, and Provider shall promptly provide a qualified replacement.

**4.3 Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for any subcontractor's performance.

## 5. WARRANTY

**5.1 Workmanship.** Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a good and workmanlike manner. Provider shall re-perform at no charge any Service that fails to conform to this warranty within 90 days after performance.

**5.2 Parts.** Provider shall pass through to Customer all manufacturer warranties for parts it installs and shall administer warranty claims on Customer's behalf.

**5.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, each party disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA

**6.1 Pre-existing Materials.** Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, including Provider's diagnostic tools and procedures.

**6.2 Records and Data.** Service records, reports and temperature data-logger data relating to the Covered Vehicles are Customer's property. Provider shall use them only to perform the Services and shall deliver copies to Customer on request and on termination or expiry.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1 Obligations.** Each party shall protect the other's non-public information that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including Customer's routes, flight schedules, airline-customer information and pricing, with at least reasonable care. It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and disclose it only to its employees, permitted subcontractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 7.

**7.2 Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public without breach, was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed. A party may disclose information when required by law, after giving prompt notice where lawful.

**7.3 Duration.** These obligations continue during the term of this Agreement and for three years afterward.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnity.** Each party shall defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by its negligence or willful misconduct, or that of its personnel, in connection with this Agreement.

**8.2 Procedure.** The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. No settlement that imposes liability on the indemnified party may be made without its consent.

**8.3 Exclusion.** Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

**8.4 Cap.** Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of US$500,000 and the Fees paid and payable in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**8.5 Exceptions.** Sections 8.3 and 8.4 do not apply to a party's obligations under Section 8.1, breach of Section 7, Customer's payment obligations, or liability for gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud.

**8.6 Cargo.** Provider is liable, subject to Section 8.4, for the value of food and cargo spoiled because of a failure of Refrigeration Equipment caused by Provider's breach of this Agreement or negligence.

## 9. INSURANCE

Provider shall maintain, during the term of this Agreement and at its own cost: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least US$2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least US$1,000,000 combined single limit; (c) garage keepers' insurance covering Covered Vehicles in Provider's care, custody or control, of at least US$500,000 per occurrence; (d) airport ramp or airside liability coverage where required by the relevant airport authority; and (e) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in (a) and (b) and provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

**10.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 24 months (the "**Initial Term**").

**10.2 Renewal.** The parties may renew this Agreement for one or more further periods of 12 months (each, a "**Renewal Term**") by written agreement signed by both parties before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

**10.3 Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 60 days' written notice to Provider, without penalty or early-termination fee.

**10.4 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing the breach. Customer may also terminate this Agreement on written notice if Provider fails to meet the on-site response time in Section 2.3 on more than three occasions in any rolling 90-day period.

**10.5 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry: (a) Customer shall pay all Fees accrued up to the effective date; (b) Provider shall return any Customer vehicles, keys, access badges and equipment in its possession; and (c) Provider shall deliver to Customer all records described in Section 6.2.

**10.6 Survival.** Sections 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10.5, 10.6 and 11, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive termination or expiry.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses stated on the signature page or another address a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

**11.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Either party may, however, assign this Agreement without consent, on written notice to the other party, to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets, whether by merger, equity sale, asset sale or otherwise, provided that the assignee agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement. "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. Subject to this Section 11.2, this Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.3 Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The state and federal courts located in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

**11.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If Provider's performance is suspended for more than 10 days, Customer may obtain the affected Services from another provider during the suspension without liability to Provider.

**11.5 Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

**11.6 Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
