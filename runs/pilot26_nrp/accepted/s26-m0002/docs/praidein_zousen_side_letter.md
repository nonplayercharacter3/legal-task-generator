**Re: Software License Agreement dated 15 January 2024 between Zousen Galley Systems, Inc. and Praidein Flight Kitchens, LLC: Addition of Authorized Site and Related Matters**

Dear Sir or Madam:

This side letter (this "**Side Letter**") is dated 21 October 2026. It is entered into by **Zousen Galley Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Praidein Flight Kitchens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). It relates to the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated 15 January 2024 (the "**Agreement**") and to the Order Form under the Agreement with a Subscription Start Date of 1 February 2024 (the "**Order Form**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensee licenses the Software under the Agreement and the Order Form for use by its Authorized Users at the Authorized Sites.

B. Licensee has informed Licensor that on 11 September 2026 it entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the "**Merger Agreement**") with Ostrander Aviation Hospitality Group, Inc. ("**Ostrander**"). Under the Merger Agreement, Ostrander will acquire 100% of the equity interests of Licensee, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions.

C. On 14 October 2026, Licensee delivered to Licensor a written notice of non-renewal of the Subscription Term under Section 12.2 of the Agreement.

D. Licensee has requested, and Licensor has agreed, that an additional flight kitchen be added as an Authorized Site under Section 2.2 of the Agreement on the terms of this Side Letter.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement or the Order Form, as applicable.

## 2. ADDITIONAL AUTHORIZED SITE

**2.1 Addition.** With effect from 1 November 2026 (the "**Site Addition Date**"), the following facility is added as an Authorized Site under the Agreement and the Order Form:

| Site | Address |
|---|---|
| Praidein Flight Kitchen – Cleveland (CLE) | 18450 Airport Service Road, Hangar Row 3, Cleveland, Ohio 44135 |

**2.2 Software.** Licensee may access and use, at the Cleveland Authorized Site, the same Software modules licensed for the existing Authorized Sites under the Order Form. These are the galley-planning, meal-load forecasting and tray-assembly modules.

**2.3 Implementation.** Licensor shall provide site configuration, the import of the Cleveland kitchen's galley and tray-layout libraries, and two on-site training sessions for the Cleveland Authorized Site. These services shall be completed within 30 days after the Site Addition Date at no additional charge.

## 3. FEES FOR ADDITIONAL SITE

**3.1 Site Fee.** The subscription Fee for the Cleveland Authorized Site is US$58,200 per year.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Licensor shall invoice the Fee for the period from the Site Addition Date to 31 January 2027 on a pro rata basis on or after the Site Addition Date. Section 4 of the Agreement applies to that invoice and to any later invoice for the Cleveland Authorized Site.

## 4. NON-RENEWAL NOTICE

**4.1 Notice.** Licensee delivered its written notice of non-renewal of the Subscription Term to Licensor on 14 October 2026.

**4.2 Licensor's Position.** Licensor's position is that the notice was not delivered within the period required by Section 12.2 of the Agreement. Licensor therefore considers the notice untimely and ineffective to prevent renewal of the Subscription Term.

**4.3 Reservation of Rights.** Each party reserves all of its rights, remedies and positions under the Agreement and the Order Form in respect of the notice and the renewal of the Subscription Term. Neither the execution of this Side Letter nor the addition of the Cleveland Authorized Site is an admission by either party, or a waiver of any right, as to the timeliness or effect of the notice.

## 5. EFFECT ON AGREEMENT

Except as expressly set out in this Side Letter, the Agreement and the Order Form remain unmodified and in full force and effect. This Side Letter is a Side Letter for the purposes of the Agreement.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Governing Law.** This Side Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Section 14 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of this Side Letter.

**6.2 Confidentiality.** The terms of this Side Letter are Confidential Information of each party under Section 9 of the Agreement.

**6.3 Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
