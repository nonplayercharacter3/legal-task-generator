## 1. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

**1.1 Subscription Start Date.** The Subscription Start Date is 1 February 2024.

**1.2 Initial Subscription Term.** The Initial Subscription Term is 36 months, beginning on the Subscription Start Date.

**1.3 Renewal.** The Subscription Term renews in accordance with Section 12.2 of the Agreement.

## 2. AUTHORIZED USERS

**2.1 Definition.** For this Order Form and the Software licensed under it, "**Authorized User**" means a named employee of Licensee who works at an Authorized Site, who has been issued a unique user credential by Licensee's system administrator, and whom Licensee has registered in the Software's user administration console.

**2.2 Subscription Quantity.** The subscription covers up to 240 Authorized Users in aggregate across all Authorized Sites (the "**Subscription Quantity**").

**2.3 Reassignment.** Licensee may reassign an Authorized User credential from one named employee to another named employee who replaces the first in his or her role, provided that the original Authorized User's access is deactivated. Credentials may not be reassigned more than once in any calendar quarter for the same seat.

**2.4 Overage.** Licensor may audit user activity through the Software's user administration console no more than once per calendar quarter. Access to or use of the Software by any individual other than an Authorized User, and any use above the Subscription Quantity, is charged at the overage rate of US$145 per individual per month (or part of a month) during which that access or use occurs. Overage Fees are invoiced quarterly in arrears and are payable in accordance with Section 4 of the Agreement.

## 3. SOFTWARE

Licensee subscribes to the following Software modules:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Galley-Planning | Galley configuration, equipment loading plans and cart/trolley layouts by aircraft type and airline customer |
| Meal-Load Forecasting | Passenger-count-driven meal-load forecasting, special-meal tracking and production scheduling |
| Tray-Assembly | Tray-line assembly sequencing, tray-layout libraries, label printing and load verification |

Each module includes the standard integration connectors for airline passenger-manifest feeds and the Documentation for that module.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION AND TRAINING

**6.1 Services.** Licensor shall provide the following services for the existing Authorized Sites:

(a) tenant provisioning and configuration of the Software for each Authorized Site;

(b) import of Licensee's existing galley-configuration, recipe and tray-layout libraries from Licensee's legacy systems in the formats agreed in the implementation plan;

(c) configuration of passenger-manifest feeds for up to six airline customers; and

(d) two on-site training sessions at each Authorized Site and four remote administrator training sessions.

**6.2 Timetable.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the services in Section 6.1 within 60 days after the Subscription Start Date, in accordance with an implementation plan agreed by the parties' project leads.
