## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops and provides cloud-hosted galley-planning, meal-load forecasting and tray-assembly software for airline catering operations.

B. Licensee is a regional airline caterer operating flight kitchens that prepare, assemble and load onboard meals and galley equipment for airline customers. Licensee staffs its flight kitchens largely with agency galley crews, who work alongside Licensee's employees and contractors.

C. Licensee wishes to obtain, and Licensor is willing to grant, a subscription to use Licensor's software at Licensee's flight kitchens on the terms of this Agreement and each Order Form.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

**3.1 Hosting.** Licensor shall host and operate the Software and make it available to Licensee over the internet in accordance with the Documentation.

**3.2 Availability.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Software available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least 72 hours in advance and performed outside 02:00–05:00 local time at the affected Authorized Site where reasonably practicable.

**3.4 Implementation.** Licensor shall provide the implementation, configuration and training services described in an Order Form.

**4.4 Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Licensee shall pay, other than taxes based on Licensor's net income.

**4.5 Fee Adjustments on Renewal.** Licensor may increase the Fees for any Renewal Term by giving written notice at least 150 days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term. No increase shall exceed 5% over the Fees for the immediately preceding twelve-month period.
