**Re: Side Letter to Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 6, 2026**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 6, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). The Agreement is between Drasiv Industrial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Starev Thermal Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). It provides for the sale of all of the issued and outstanding Shares of Coldline Immersion Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), to Buyer.

This Side Letter is the "Side Letter" referred to in the Agreement and is an Ancillary Document. In consideration of the mutual agreements in the Agreement and in this Side Letter, Seller and Buyer agree as follows:

## 1. Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement or in the Disclosure Schedule. These include "Cap," "Fraud," "Kelvara Fluids Supply Agreement" and "Locked Price."

## 2. Kelvara Fluids Supply Agreement

2.1 **Exercise of Extension Option.** Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to exercise the price-lock extension option under the Kelvara Fluids Supply Agreement described in Schedule 4.11, Item 3 of the Disclosure Schedule. The Company shall exercise the option by delivering written notice of exercise to Kelvara Fluids Corp. in accordance with the terms of that agreement. The notice shall extend the Kelvara Fluids Supply Agreement for the additional term of twenty-four (24) months at the Locked Price.

2.2 **Evidence of Exercise.** Seller shall deliver to Buyer the following at least five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date:

(a) a copy of the notice of exercise delivered under paragraph 2.1; and

(b) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer that the notice was delivered to Kelvara Fluids Corp.

2.3 **Covenant.** The obligations of Seller in this paragraph 2 are covenants of Seller to be performed before the Closing for purposes of Sections 5.1 and 6.4(b) of the Agreement.

## 3. Indemnification Limitations

Seller and Buyer agree that the exclusions from the Cap under Section 9.5(b) of the Agreement are limited solely to Fraud. Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(c) through 9.5(e) of the Agreement continue to apply in accordance with their terms.

## 4. Scheduled Refrigerant Matter Updates

From the date of this Side Letter until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to do the following:

(a) deliver to Buyer a copy of each monitoring report, notice and other material written communication that the Company submits to or receives from the Delaware Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control relating to the Scheduled Refrigerant Matter, within three (3) Business Days after it is submitted or received; and

(b) give Buyer reasonable advance notice of, and a reasonable opportunity to attend, any scheduled meeting with that agency relating to the Scheduled Refrigerant Matter.

## 5. Effect on Agreement

Except as expressly set forth in this Side Letter, the Agreement remains unmodified and in full force and effect. Each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" shall be read as including this Side Letter, where the context permits.

## 6. Miscellaneous

6.1 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware. Sections 11.8(b) and 11.9 of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter *mutatis mutandis*.

6.2 **Amendment.** This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and Buyer.

6.3 **Confidentiality.** The terms of this Side Letter are subject to Section 5.7 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
