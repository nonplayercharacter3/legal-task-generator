# OFFICE AND TEST FACILITY LEASE

This OFFICE AND TEST FACILITY LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made as of February 1, 2021 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Ostrander Park Associates, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Coldline Immersion Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

1.1 **Building and Project.** The building known as Ostrander Park Building C (the "**Building**"), located within the Ostrander Park business campus in Newark, Delaware (the "**Project**").

1.2 **Premises.** Approximately 38,400 rentable square feet in the Building, as shown on Exhibit A (the "**Premises**"). The Premises consist of the following:

(a) approximately 14,200 rentable square feet of office space on the second floor (the "**Office Space**"); and

(b) approximately 24,200 rentable square feet of high-bay flex space on the first floor, together with the adjacent exterior equipment pad shown on Exhibit A (together, the "**Test Facility**").

1.3 **Term.** One hundred twenty (120) months, beginning on March 1, 2021 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ending on February 28, 2031 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless sooner terminated or extended under this Lease.

1.4 **Base Rent.** Base Rent is payable in monthly installments as set out in Section 4.1.

1.5 **Tenant's Share.** 31.6% of the Project, based on the rentable area of the Premises divided by the rentable area of all buildings in the Project.

1.6 **Security Deposit.** $142,000.

1.7 **Permitted Use.** General office use, and the design, assembly, testing and demonstration of immersion cooling systems, refrigerant-based heat rejection systems and related data center cooling equipment, together with uses ancillary to those activities.

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease.

2.2 **Common Areas.** Tenant may use, in common with other tenants of the Project, the parking areas, driveways, sidewalks, lobbies, loading areas and other areas of the Project that Landlord designates for common use (the "**Common Areas**"). Tenant may use 96 unreserved parking spaces in the Project parking areas at no additional charge.

2.3 **Condition of Premises.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises with the Landlord's Work described in Exhibit B substantially complete and with the base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing and roof systems in good working order. Tenant shall have twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date to notify Landlord of any latent defect in Landlord's Work. Landlord shall correct any such defect at its own cost.

## 3. TERM; EXTENSION

3.1 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date.

3.2 **Extension Option.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"). To exercise the option, Tenant must give Landlord written notice not less than twelve (12) months and not more than eighteen (18) months before the Expiration Date. Base Rent for the Extension Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Exhibit C. The option is exercisable only if Tenant is not in default beyond any applicable cure period when it gives notice.

3.3 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the Term ends without Landlord's written consent, Tenant becomes a tenant at sufferance. Base Rent during the holdover shall be one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect immediately before the Term ended.

4.2 **Operating Expenses and Taxes.** Beginning on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Project. Tenant shall make these payments in monthly installments based on Landlord's reasonable annual estimate. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a reconciliation statement. Within thirty (30) days after that statement is delivered, Tenant shall pay any underpayment, or Landlord shall credit any overpayment against the next amounts due. Tenant may audit Landlord's records for any calendar year within ninety (90) days after receiving the reconciliation statement for that year.

4.3 **Exclusions.** Operating Expenses exclude all of the following: capital expenditures, except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Effective Date, which shall be amortized over their useful life; costs of leasing space to other tenants; Landlord's debt service and ground rent; costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants; and the costs of remediating Hazardous Materials that were present at the Project before the Commencement Date.

4.4 **Late Charge.** If any Rent is not received within five (5) days after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. This late charge applies no more than once in any twelve (12) month period without Landlord first giving written notice.

## 5. USE; COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

5.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use. Tenant shall not use the Premises in any manner that creates a nuisance or unreasonably interferes with other tenants of the Project.

5.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its use and occupancy of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the base building structure, base building systems and Common Areas, except where compliance is required because of Tenant's particular use or alterations.

5.3 **Test Facility Operations.** Tenant may install and operate in the Test Facility immersion tanks, coolant distribution units, chillers, refrigerant test loops, dry coolers and related equipment, including on the exterior equipment pad. Tenant may operate that equipment twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Exterior equipment shall meet the noise limits in the Project rules and regulations attached as Exhibit D.

## 6. HAZARDOUS MATERIALS

6.1 **Permitted Materials.** Tenant may bring onto, use and store in the Test Facility the following materials in the quantities reasonably necessary for the Permitted Use, provided Tenant does so in compliance with all Environmental Laws:

(a) refrigerants, including hydrofluorocarbon and hydrofluoroolefin refrigerants;

(b) dielectric immersion fluids;

(c) glycol and water-based coolants;

(d) refrigerant oils; and

(e) customary office and janitorial supplies.

Tenant shall maintain at the Premises, and shall provide to Landlord on request, a current inventory and safety data sheets for those materials.

6.2 **Tenant Obligations.** Tenant shall not cause or permit any release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Project. If Tenant or its agents, employees, contractors or invitees cause such a release, Tenant shall do all of the following at its own cost:

(a) promptly notify Landlord;

(b) make all reports required by Environmental Laws; and

(c) perform any investigation, remediation and monitoring that Environmental Laws or any Governmental Authority requires.

Landlord shall have reasonable access to observe that work. Tenant shall give Landlord copies of all material reports and correspondence with any Governmental Authority about that work.

6.3 **Tenant Indemnity.** Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord from all claims, losses, costs and liabilities arising from any Hazardous Materials that Tenant or its agents, employees, contractors or invitees bring onto or release at the Project. This includes reasonable attorneys' and consultants' fees.

6.4 **Landlord Indemnity.** Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Tenant from all claims, losses, costs and liabilities arising from Hazardous Materials that were present at the Project before the Commencement Date, or that Landlord or its agents or other tenants bring onto or release at the Project.

6.5 **Definitions.** "**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance regulated as hazardous, toxic, a pollutant or a contaminant under any Environmental Law, including petroleum products and regulated refrigerants. "**Environmental Laws**" means all federal, state and local laws relating to pollution, the protection of the environment, or the release, handling or recovery of Hazardous Materials. "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local government, or any agency, department or court of such a government.

6.6 **Survival.** This Section 6 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease.

## 7. ALTERATIONS; SIGNAGE

7.1 **Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord's consent is not required for cosmetic alterations costing less than $50,000 per project that do not affect the structure or base building systems.

7.2 **Test Equipment.** The installation, relocation and removal of test equipment in the Test Facility and on the exterior equipment pad are not alterations requiring consent under Section 7.1, provided that the work does not:

(a) penetrate the roof or structural slab; or

(b) exceed the electrical capacity serving the Test Facility as described in Exhibit B.

7.3 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall remove its trade fixtures, test equipment and personal property, and shall repair any damage that the removal causes. Tenant need not remove any alteration that Landlord approved under Section 7.1, unless Landlord required removal as a condition of its consent.

7.4 **Signage.** Tenant may install building-standard signage at the Building entrance and a panel on the Project monument sign, at Tenant's cost and in compliance with applicable law.

## 8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS; SERVICES

8.1 **Landlord Repairs.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements, base building systems, parking areas and Common Areas in good condition. The cost of that work is included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 4.3.

8.2 **Tenant Repairs.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises, and all equipment installed by or for Tenant, in good condition and repair, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall maintain the supplemental HVAC and process cooling equipment serving the Test Facility under a service contract with a qualified contractor.

8.3 **Utilities.** The Test Facility is separately metered for electricity. Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly for all electricity consumed in the Test Facility. Landlord shall furnish electricity, water and HVAC to the Office Space during Building hours. The cost of those services is included in Operating Expenses.

## 9. INSURANCE; INDEMNITY

9.1 **Tenant Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain the following insurance during the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) umbrella liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000;

(c) pollution legal liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per claim;

(d) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and equipment at full replacement cost; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

Landlord shall be named as an additional insured on the policies described in clauses (a) through (c).

9.2 **Landlord Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building at full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas.

9.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord and Tenant each waive all claims against the other for loss or damage to property to the extent that the loss or damage is covered by property insurance that the waiving party carries or is required to carry under this Lease.

9.4 **Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.3, each party shall indemnify the other party against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent they are caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

10.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its consent.

10.2 **Change of Control.** A change of control of Tenant is treated as an assignment of this Lease requiring Landlord's consent under Section 10.1. "Change of control" means either of the following:

(a) a transfer, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity interests in Tenant; or

(b) a transfer of the power to direct the management and policies of Tenant.

10.3 **Request for Consent.** Tenant shall request Landlord's consent in writing at least thirty (30) days before the proposed effective date of the assignment, sublease or change of control. Tenant's request shall include the identity of the proposed transferee and reasonable financial information about it. Landlord shall grant or deny consent in writing within fifteen (15) days after receiving a complete request. If Landlord denies consent, it shall state its reasons.

10.4 **Reasonable Grounds.** It is reasonable for Landlord to withhold consent only if one of the following applies:

(a) the proposed transferee, or the surviving tenant after a change of control, lacks financial strength reasonably sufficient to perform Tenant's obligations under this Lease;

(b) the proposed use is not the Permitted Use; or

(c) Tenant is in default beyond any applicable cure period.

10.5 **Permitted Transfers.** Landlord's consent is not required for an assignment or sublease to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, provided that Tenant gives Landlord written notice within ten (10) days after the transfer.

10.6 **Costs; No Release.** Tenant shall reimburse Landlord's reasonable out-of-pocket attorneys' fees for reviewing a consent request, up to $2,500 per request. No assignment releases the assigning Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing. A change of control does not change the identity of Tenant under this Lease.

## 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

11.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five (5) days after written notice that the payment is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, the cure period is extended for so long as Tenant diligently pursues the cure; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding, or of an involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

11.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may do either of the following, in each case in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware:

(a) terminate this Lease by written notice; or

(b) without terminating this Lease, recover Rent as it becomes due.

Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to relet the Premises and mitigate its damages.

11.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any of its obligations under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity. If the failure materially interferes with Tenant's use of the Test Facility and continues for an additional ten (10) days after a second notice, Tenant may perform the obligation itself and recover its reasonable costs from Landlord.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

12.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Building and Landlord's Work with reasonable diligence. Rent shall abate in proportion to the portion of the Premises that is unusable. If Landlord reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after Landlord delivers that estimate.

12.2 **Condemnation.** If all or a material part of the Premises is taken by eminent domain, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. Landlord is entitled to the award for the real property. Tenant may pursue a separate claim for its relocation costs and trade fixtures.

## 13. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL

13.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later placed on the Project, provided that the mortgagee delivers a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement.

13.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten (10) business days after a written request, each party shall deliver to the other a certificate confirming the following:

(a) the status of this Lease;

(b) the dates through which Rent has been paid; and

(c) whether either party is aware of any default.
