## SCHEDULE 4.15: ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS

**Item 1. DNREC Consent Order: HFC Refrigerant Release at Newark Test Facility**

- *Incident:* On February 11, 2025, a pressure relief failure on a test-loop receiver at the Company's Newark, Delaware test facility caused a release of approximately 1,450 pounds of HFC-134a refrigerant. A smaller quantity of refrigerant oil was released to soil adjacent to the equipment pad.
- *Order:* The Company entered into a Consent Order with the Delaware Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control ("**DNREC**") effective September 8, 2025.
- *Obligations:* The Consent Order requires the Company to do the following:
  - (a) pay a civil penalty of $185,000, which the Company paid in October 2025;
  - (b) remediate affected soils in accordance with a DNREC-approved remedial action work plan;
  - (c) install and maintain enhanced leak detection and refrigerant recovery equipment at the test facility;
  - (d) conduct groundwater and soil monitoring, and submit quarterly monitoring reports to DNREC; and
  - (e) complete all remediation and monitoring through June 30, 2029.
- *Status:* Soil remediation is in progress. Leak detection upgrades were completed in June 2026. Quarterly monitoring and reporting are ongoing.
- *Estimated cost:* The Company's current estimate of remaining compliance costs under the Consent Order is $3,900,000. Most of that amount relates to remediation completion and monitoring to be performed in 2027 through 2029.
