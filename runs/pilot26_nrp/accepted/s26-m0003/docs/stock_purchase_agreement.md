# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 6, 2026, by and between **Drasiv Industrial Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Starev Thermal Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Coldline Immersion Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company designs, manufactures and sells immersion and refrigerant-based cooling systems for hyperscale data centers.

B. **Drasiv IP Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Seller ("**IP Holdings**"), holds certain patents and design files used in the Company's immersion-tank business. Seller will cause IP Holdings to convey those assets to the Company at the Closing.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS; PURCHASE AND SALE

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

- "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.
- "**Ancillary Documents**" means the Bill of Sale, the Side Letter, the Escrow Agreement and each other agreement, certificate and instrument delivered under this Agreement.
- "**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale by which IP Holdings conveys the Contributed Assets to the Company, substantially in the form attached as Exhibit A.
- "**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.
- "**Contributed Assets**" means the immersion-tank patents and patent applications, and the design files, CAD models and engineering documentation relating to them, that are listed in Schedule 4.12(b) of the Disclosure Schedule.
- "**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule that Seller delivered to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.
- "**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under Delaware common law with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV.
- "**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization), 4.5 (Title to Shares) and 4.17 (Brokers).
- "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court or other body of such a government.
- "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' and consultants' fees.
- "**Material Contracts**" has the meaning given in Section 4.10.
- "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.
- "**Required Consents**" means the consents, approvals and notices listed in Schedule 6.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.
- "**Scheduled Refrigerant Matter**" means the matter described in Schedule 4.15 of the Disclosure Schedule, including the consent order described there and all remediation, monitoring, reporting and other obligations arising under or relating to it.
- "**Side Letter**" means the letter agreement between Seller and Buyer dated the date of this Agreement.

**1.2 Purchase and Sale.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares. The Shares shall be transferred free and clear of all liens, pledges, options, claims and other encumbrances.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $184,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the following amounts by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) $9,200,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to Wilmington Commerce Trust Company, as escrow agent, to be held under the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) the balance of the Purchase Price to Seller, after adjusting for the Estimated Closing Working Capital under Section 2.3(a).

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimate.* Not less than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital (the "**Estimated Closing Working Capital**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

(b) *Final Determination.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller its calculation of the Closing Working Capital. Seller shall have forty-five (45) days to dispute that calculation in writing. The parties shall refer any dispute they cannot resolve within thirty (30) days to an independent accounting firm of national standing that both parties have mutually selected. That firm's determination shall be final and binding.

(c) *Payment.* If the final Closing Working Capital is less than the Estimated Closing Working Capital, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. If it is greater, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. Each such payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after the final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely, by electronic exchange of documents and signatures, on November 20, 2026. If the conditions in Article VI have not been satisfied or waived by that date, the Closing shall instead take place on the third (3rd) Business Day after those conditions are satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree in writing. The date on which the Closing actually occurs is the "**Closing Date**."

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by IP Holdings and the Company;

(c) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer that each Required Consent has been obtained;

(d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(e) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company that Buyer has designated in writing;

(f) the certificate required by Section 6.4(d); and

(g) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments required by Section 2.2;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the certificate required by Section 6.3(c).

## ARTICLE V. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as Buyer otherwise consents in writing, Seller shall cause the Company to:

(a) operate its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice;

(b) use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization, customer and supplier relationships, and goodwill; and

(c) refrain from taking any action that would require disclosure under Section 4.7.

**5.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**5.3 Notice of Certain Events.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that causes, or would reasonably be expected to cause, any condition in Section 6.4 not to be satisfied.

**5.4 Contributed Assets.** At or before the Closing, Seller shall cause IP Holdings to execute and deliver the Bill of Sale and to take all other actions reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Contributed Assets. After the Closing, Seller shall cause IP Holdings to execute such further assignments and recordable instruments as Buyer reasonably requests to record the Company's ownership of the Contributed Assets with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any foreign patent office.

**5.5 Required Consents.** Before the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use reasonable best efforts to give all notices and obtain all Required Consents, in each case in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer. Seller shall bear any fee or other consideration payable to a third party to obtain a Required Consent.

**5.6 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement concerning any sale of the Company, its equity or a material portion of its assets, other than the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**5.7 Confidentiality; Public Announcements.** Neither party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

**6.1 General.** Each party's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or waiver in writing by the party entitled to the benefit of it, of the applicable conditions in this Article VI.

**6.2 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** No Governmental Authority shall have enacted, issued or entered any law or order that is then in effect and that restrains, enjoins or prohibits the Closing.

**6.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the following conditions:

(a) Buyer's representations and warranties in Article VII shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before the Closing; and

(c) Buyer shall have delivered a certificate, signed by an officer of Buyer, certifying as to the matters in Sections 6.3(a) and 6.3(b).

**6.4 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and Seller's other representations and warranties shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(c) all Required Consents shall have been obtained and shall be in full force and effect;

(d) Seller shall have delivered a certificate, signed by an officer of Seller, certifying as to the matters in Sections 6.4(a) through 6.4(c);

**8.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of Tax Returns and in the conduct of any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until 12 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations, and the representations and warranties in Section 4.16 (Taxes), shall instead survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(b) The covenants and agreements of Seller in Sections 9.2(b) through 9.2(d) shall survive the Closing until the expiration of the survival period of the representation and warranty to which such covenant relates.

(c) Except as provided in Section 9.1(b), the covenants and agreements of the parties that by their terms are to be performed after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(d) Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until it is finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates and representatives (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to any of the following:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date; and

(d) the Scheduled Refrigerant Matter, including all costs of investigation, remediation, monitoring and reporting, all fines and penalties, and all costs of complying with any consent order relating to it, whether incurred before or after the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article VII; and

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) *Notice.* A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice of the claim to the party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). A failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations, except to the extent the Indemnifying Party is actually prejudiced by the failure.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

(c) *Scheduled Refrigerant Matter.* Following the Closing, the Company shall control the conduct of all remediation, monitoring and dealings with Governmental Authorities relating to the Scheduled Refrigerant Matter, and shall keep Seller reasonably informed.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($18,400,000) (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Exclusions from Cap.* The Cap shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud, breach of Fundamental Representations, and the Section 9.2(d) Scheduled Refrigerant Matter indemnity.

(c) *Deductible.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations or Fraud, until the aggregate Losses exceed $1,380,000. Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of $1,380,000.

(d) *Overall Limit.* Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX, other than for Fraud, shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of them, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Escrow Recovery.** Buyer Indemnitees shall first seek recovery of Losses under Section 9.2(a) from the Escrow Amount. After the Escrow Amount is exhausted or released, they may seek recovery directly from Seller, subject to Section 9.5.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.3, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by January 15, 2027, provided that the right to terminate under this Section 10.1(b) is not available to a party whose breach caused the failure to close;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.4 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1, it shall become void and of no further effect, without liability of any party, except that:

(a) Section 5.7, this Section 10.2 and Article XI shall survive termination; and

(b) no termination shall relieve any party from liability for any willful breach of this Agreement.
