# EXHIBIT A

# FORM OF BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT OF CONTRIBUTED ASSETS

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT OF CONTRIBUTED ASSETS (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by **Drasiv IP Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), to and in favor of **Coldline Immersion Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Drasiv Industrial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Assignor ("**Seller**"), and Starev Thermal Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company to Buyer.

B. Assignor holds certain immersion-tank patents and patent applications, and related design files, CAD models and engineering documentation, that are used in the Company's business.

C. Section 5.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause Assignor to convey those assets to the Company at the Closing, and Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires delivery of this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Assignor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Contributed Assets.** "**Contributed Assets**" means all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the following:

(a) the patents and patent applications listed in Schedule 4.12(b) of the Disclosure Schedule, together with all of the following:
- (i) all provisionals, continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, reexaminations, extensions and foreign counterparts of those patents and applications;
- (ii) all patents issuing on any of them; and
- (iii) all inventions and improvements disclosed or claimed in any of them;

(b) the design files, CAD models, drawings, specifications, test data and other engineering documentation listed in or relating to the items described in Schedule 4.12(b) of the Disclosure Schedule, in every form and medium in which they exist;

(c) all rights to claim priority to any of the foregoing;

(d) all rights to sue for and recover damages and other remedies for past, present and future infringement or misappropriation of any of the foregoing, and all proceeds of those rights; and

(e) all income, royalties and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the foregoing.

Schedule I to this Bill of Sale lists the Contributed Assets and is incorporated by reference.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

2.1 **Transfer.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Contributed Assets. The Contributed Assets are transferred free and clear of all liens, pledges, licenses, options, claims and other encumbrances. The Company shall have and hold the Contributed Assets forever.

2.2 **Delivery of Design Files.** On the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to the Company complete copies of all design files, CAD models and engineering documentation included in the Contributed Assets. Delivery shall be made in native electronic format, with all revision histories that Assignor maintains. After that delivery, Assignor shall not retain any copy of those materials, except for copies that Assignor holds in automatic electronic backups in the ordinary course. Assignor shall not access or use any retained backup copy for any purpose.

2.3 **Prosecution Files.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to the Company, or to counsel the Company designates, the complete prosecution files, docketing records and correspondence with patent offices and foreign associates for each patent and patent application included in the Contributed Assets.

## 3. NO ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITIES

3.1 **No Assumed Liabilities.** The Company does not assume, and shall not be responsible for, any liability or obligation of Assignor or any of its Affiliates of any kind, whether known or unknown, accrued or contingent. This includes any liability arising out of the ownership, prosecution, maintenance, licensing or enforcement of the Contributed Assets before the Effective Date.

3.2 **Fees Before Effective Date.** Assignor shall pay all maintenance fees, annuities and prosecution costs for the Contributed Assets that are due on or before the Effective Date.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES; RECORDATION

4.1 **Further Assurances.** At the Company's request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver such further assignments, declarations, powers and instruments, and shall take such other actions, as the Company reasonably requests to do any of the following:
- (a) vest, perfect, confirm or record the Company's title to the Contributed Assets;
- (b) enable the Company to prosecute, maintain, enforce and defend the Contributed Assets; and
- (c) otherwise carry out the purposes of this Bill of Sale.

4.2 **Recordation.** Assignor authorizes the Company to record this Bill of Sale, or a short-form patent assignment consistent with it, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and with any foreign patent office or other Governmental Authority. Assignor shall execute any short-form assignment the Company reasonably requests for that purpose within five (5) Business Days after the request.

4.3 **Inventor Cooperation.** Assignor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause any inventor named on a Contributed Asset who is employed or engaged by Assignor or its Affiliates to execute any declaration, oath or assignment reasonably required to perfect or record the Company's title.

4.4 **Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and record in Assignor's name any instrument described in this Section 4 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after the Company's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

4.5 **Payments Received.** If Assignor receives any payment, royalty or other amount relating to the Contributed Assets after the Effective Date, it shall hold that amount in trust for the Company. Assignor shall remit the amount to the Company within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

## 5. ASSIGNOR ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

5.1 **Authority.** Assignor has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to convey the Contributed Assets. This Bill of Sale constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignor, enforceable against Assignor in accordance with its terms.

5.2 **No Retained Rights.** Assignor retains no license, covenant not to sue or other right in or to any Contributed Asset. Assignor has not granted to any Person any license or other right in any Contributed Asset that will survive the Effective Date.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale and all claims arising out of or relating to the Contributed Assets or their conveyance shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would result in the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

6.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each of Assignor and the Company irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the County of New York, State of New York, for any action, suit or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, the Contributed Assets or their conveyance. Each of them waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

6.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF ASSIGNOR AND THE COMPANY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS BILL OF SALE.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

7.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale is binding upon Assignor and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns, including any Person to which the Company later transfers any Contributed Asset.

7.2 **Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and the Company.

7.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

7.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignor shall be sent to the attention of the General Counsel of Seller. Notices to the Company shall be sent to the attention of the Chief Legal Officer of Buyer.

7.5 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
