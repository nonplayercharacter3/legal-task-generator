# BILL OF SALE

**(High-Lift Catering Trucks)**

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of April 30, 2026, by **Noumisk Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller, **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and, for certain limited purposes, the Company are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller owns certain high-lift catering trucks that are used in the Company's airline catering and galley-provisioning business. Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to convey those trucks to the Company at the Closing by delivering this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the consideration provided under the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Catering Trucks**" means the high-lift catering trucks owned by Seller and used in the Business, as listed in Schedule 2.5 to the Purchase Agreement and reproduced in Schedule I to this Bill of Sale. The term includes all lift bodies, scissor and hydraulic lift assemblies, refrigeration and chiller units, platforms, canopies, bumpers and docking equipment, and other attachments, accessories, spare parts, tools and equipment installed on or dedicated to each truck.

"**Truck Records**" means all certificates of title, registrations, manufacturer and extended warranties (to the extent assignable), operator and maintenance manuals, maintenance and service logs, airside vehicle permits and inspection records, and keys and access devices relating to the Catering Trucks.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation."

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Catering Trucks and the Truck Records, free and clear of all liens.

**2.2 Consideration.** The conveyance under Section 2.1 is made in accordance with Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement. No consideration is payable for the Catering Trucks beyond the Purchase Price.

**2.3 Delivery of Titles.** Concurrently with the delivery of this Bill of Sale, Seller is delivering to the Company the certificate of title for each Catering Truck, duly endorsed for transfer to the Company, together with any lien releases and other documents required by the applicable motor vehicle authority to record the Company as owner.

**2.4 Possession.** The Catering Trucks are located at the Flight Kitchen or at the airside facilities from which the Company currently operates them. Possession of the Catering Trucks passes to the Company at the Closing at those locations.

## 3. TITLE

**3.1 Title.** Seller represents to the Company that, immediately before the Closing, Seller held good and marketable title to each Catering Truck, free and clear of all liens, and that Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale.

**3.2 Warranty of Title.** Seller shall warrant and defend the sale of the Catering Trucks against all persons claiming any interest in them by, through or under Seller.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**4.1 Further Instruments.** At the Company's request and without further consideration, Seller shall promptly execute and deliver such further instruments of transfer, title applications, powers of attorney and other documents, and take such further actions, as the Company may reasonably require to vest in the Company full title to the Catering Trucks and to register and record the Company as owner.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to endorse any certificate of title or registration relating to the Catering Trucks and to sign any application required by a motor vehicle authority to transfer title to the Company. This power is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Misdirected Items.** If Seller receives any payment, refund, warranty credit or notice relating to the Catering Trucks after the Closing, Seller shall promptly remit or forward it to the Company.

## 5. TRANSFER TAXES AND FEES

All transfer, documentary, sales, use and similar Taxes, and all title and registration fees, arising from the conveyance of the Catering Trucks shall be borne as provided in Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns and benefits the Company and its successors and assigns.

**6.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, in accordance with Section 12.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.3 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature, and the acceptance below may be executed in counterparts.
