# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026, by and among **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Noumisk Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Article VI, **Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company operates a regional airline catering and galley-provisioning business, including the preparation, packing and delivery of inflight meals, beverages and galley supplies to airline customers (the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** As used in this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale, and each other agreement, certificate or instrument delivered under this Agreement at or in connection with the Closing.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, dated as of the Closing Date, by which Seller conveys the Catering Trucks to the Company.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Cap**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.5(a).

"**Catering Trucks**" means the high-lift catering trucks owned by Seller and used in the Business, as listed in Schedule 2.5.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pelloway Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement, dated as of the Closing Date, among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any interest and earnings thereon, as reduced by releases made under the Escrow Agreement.

"**FDA**" means the United States Food and Drug Administration.

"**Flight Kitchen**" means the Company's flight kitchen and commissary facility serving its airline customers.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud by a party in the making of the representations and warranties in this Agreement, with actual knowledge of their falsity.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and Section 4.13 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, instrumentality, regulatory body, court or tribunal thereof, including the FDA and the United States Department of Agriculture.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Losses**" means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and the cost of enforcing any right to indemnification under this Agreement.

"**Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.4.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period, or portion thereof, ending on or before the Closing Date, and all Taxes of Seller or any of its Affiliates (other than the Company) for any period.

"**Seller Guarantees**" means the guarantees by Seller of (a) the obligations of the Company under the lease of the Flight Kitchen and (b) the obligations of the Company under its food and beverage supply contracts, in each case as listed in Schedule 1.1.

"**Seller Note**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.2.

"**Tax**" or "**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, payroll, employment, franchise, property, withholding and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions thereto.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), payable as provided in Sections 2.2 through 2.4.

**2.2 Seller Note.** At the Closing, Buyer shall issue and deliver to Seller a subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**"), in the form of Exhibit A, as deferred consideration and in partial payment of the Purchase Price.

**2.3 Closing Cash Payment.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to the Purchase Price, less the principal amount of the Seller Note, less the Escrow Amount.

**2.4 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX.

**2.5 Catering Trucks.** At the Closing, Seller shall convey to the Company, by delivery of the Bill of Sale, all right, title and interest in and to the Catering Trucks, free and clear of all liens, and shall deliver the certificates of title for the Catering Trucks duly endorsed for transfer to the Company. No additional consideration is payable for the Catering Trucks beyond the Purchase Price.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to Seller.

## 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on April 30, 2026, or on such other date as Buyer and Seller may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"), subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Bill of Sale, each duly executed by Seller;

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(d) the third-party consents listed in Schedule 7.1(c); and

(e) a certificate of the secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller authorizing this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the closing cash payment under Section 2.3 to Seller;

(b) the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent;

(c) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer, to Seller; and

(d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of organization and has all requisite power to own its properties and conduct its business as now conducted.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations thereunder. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 1,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding, all of which are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all liens. There are no options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity securities of the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except for the consents listed in Schedule 4.4, the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, any Law, or any material contract of the Company.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025, and the related statements of income and cash flows, copies of which have been delivered to Buyer, fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course and there has not been any material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.7 lists each material contract of the Company, including each airline catering agreement, each food and beverage supply contract and each real property lease. Each is valid and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.8 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. Schedule 4.8 lists each lease under which the Company occupies real property, including the lease of the Flight Kitchen and the lease of the Company's administrative offices.

**4.9 Compliance with Laws; Food Safety.** Except as described in Schedule 4.9, the Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, including the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the regulations of the FDA governing interstate conveyance sanitation and food handling. Schedule 4.9 describes the listeria finding at the Flight Kitchen in January 2026 and the warning letter issued by the FDA in respect of it.

**4.10 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed and has paid all Taxes due. No audit or proceeding concerning Taxes of the Company is pending or threatened.

**4.11 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.11, there is no action or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.12 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.12 lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in material compliance with its terms and applicable Law.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to execute and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the closing cash payment and the Escrow Amount.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain its food-safety programs and regulatory licenses, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, sell material assets, or amend any material contract.

**6.2 Access.** Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company, including the Flight Kitchen and all correspondence with the FDA.

**6.3 Remediation.** Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to continue in good faith the corrective actions described in its response to the FDA warning letter and to keep Buyer reasonably informed of all communications with any Governmental Authority regarding the Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain before the Closing the consents listed in Schedule 7.1(c), including the change-of-control consent of Corriedale Regional Airways, Inc.

**6.5 Seller Guarantees.** Following the Closing, Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller to be released from the Seller Guarantees, including by offering substitute credit support reasonably acceptable to the relevant counterparties.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out this Agreement.

## 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, and the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing;

(c) the consents listed in Schedule 7.1(c), including the consent of Corriedale Regional Airways, Inc. and the consent of Ashgrove Airport Properties LLC under the Company's administrative office lease, shall have been obtained; and

(d) Seller shall have delivered the items listed in Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the accuracy in all material respects of Buyer's representations and warranties, Buyer's performance in all material respects of its pre-Closing covenants, and delivery of the items listed in Section 3.3.

## 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller; (b) by either Buyer or Seller if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, provided the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either party upon a material breach by the other that is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect, except that Sections 6.6 and Article XII shall survive and no party shall be relieved of liability for willful breach.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date; provided that the Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.10 (Taxes) shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates and representatives (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes; and

(d) the Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters, as provided in Section 9.4.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and representatives (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against:

(a) all Losses arising out of or relating to any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(b) any and all Losses arising under the Seller Guarantees, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**9.4 Food Safety Specific Indemnity.** Seller shall indemnify the Buyer Indemnitees from and against all Losses, including fines, penalties, recall and remediation costs and amounts paid to any Governmental Authority, arising out of or relating to (a) the listeria finding at the Flight Kitchen in January 2026, (b) the warning letter issued by the FDA in respect of the Flight Kitchen, and (c) any inspection, enforcement action, recall, customer claim or third-party claim arising from the conditions described in clauses (a) and (b) (collectively, the "**Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters**"). Claims for the Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters are not subject to Section 9.5(b), shall be payable first from the Escrow Funds, and to the extent the Escrow Funds are insufficient or unavailable, shall be recovered in accordance with Section 9.6.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) and the aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not, in each case, exceed $4,850,000 (10% of Purchase Price) (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2(a) exceed $242,500, after which Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(c) *Exceptions.* The limitations in Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses arising out of Fraud, any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, or any Pre-Closing Taxes, and the aggregate liability of Seller for such Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnitee in respect of the matter, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Order of Recovery.** Any amount payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX shall be satisfied (a) first, from the Escrow Funds, to the extent then available; (b) second, at Buyer's election, by set-off against amounts then outstanding under the Seller Note in accordance with its terms; and (c) thereafter, directly from Seller.

**9.7 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail; failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, other than any claim seeking criminal sanctions or brought by a Governmental Authority, the defense of which shall be controlled by Buyer in respect of the Pre-Closing Food Safety Matters. No party shall settle a third-party claim without the consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**9.8 Escrow Release.** Within five Business Days after the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent to release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate amount of all claims then pending.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.10 Exclusive Remedy.** Except in the case of Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties following the Closing for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement.

## 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Returns.** Seller shall prepare, at its expense, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, and shall submit each to Buyer for review at least 30 days before filing.

**10.2 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

**10.3 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement, including any such Taxes arising from the conveyance of the Catering Trucks, shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

## 11. EMPLOYEES

**11.1 Continuation.** For 12 months following the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each continuing employee with base compensation no less favorable than that provided immediately before the Closing.

**11.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article XI confers any rights on any employee or other Person.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement. Fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party may designate by notice.

**12.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing signed by the waiving party.

**12.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any Affiliate or, as collateral, to its lenders.

**12.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.

**12.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.7 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

**12.8 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement, and agrees that all claims in respect of any such action or proceeding shall be heard and determined only in such courts. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**12.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.

**12.10 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail; except that (a) with respect to the release of Escrow Funds, the Escrow Agreement shall control, and (b) with respect to payment, set-off and recourse under the Seller Note, the Seller Note shall control.

**12.11 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, the Disclosure Schedule, the Exhibits and the Ancillary Agreements constitute the entire agreement of the parties with respect to their subject matter and supersede all prior agreements and understandings, written or oral, including the letter of intent between Buyer and Seller.
