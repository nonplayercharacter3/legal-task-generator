# OFFICE LEASE

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of June 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ashgrove Airport Properties LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

**1.1 Summary.** The following basic lease information is part of this Lease. If it conflicts with any other provision of this Lease, the other provision controls.

(a) *Building:* Ashgrove Airport Commerce Center, Building C, 148-20 Ashgrove Boulevard, Jamaica, New York 11430 (the "**Building**"). The Building stands on the land described in Exhibit A (together with the Building and the related parking areas, the "**Property**").

(b) *Premises:* Suite 310 on the third floor of the Building, containing approximately 6,200 rentable square feet, as shown on Exhibit B (the "**Premises**").

(c) *Term:* Seven years, starting on the Commencement Date and ending on the last day of the eighty-fourth full calendar month after it (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless ended earlier or extended under this Lease (the "**Term**").

(d) *Commencement Date:* July 1, 2023.

(e) *Base Rent:* As set forth in Section 3.1.

(f) *Tenant's Share:* 7.75%, being the rentable square footage of the Premises divided by the 80,000 rentable square feet of the Building.

(g) *Base Year:* Calendar year 2023.

(h) *Security Deposit:* $49,600.

(i) *Permitted Use:* General, executive and administrative office use, including administration of an airline catering and galley-provisioning business, and for no other purpose.

(j) *Parking:* Twelve unreserved spaces in the Building's surface parking area, at no additional charge.

## 2. PREMISES AND TERM

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term, on the terms of this Lease. Tenant has the non-exclusive right to use the Building's lobbies, elevators, corridors, restrooms, parking areas and other common areas (the "**Common Areas**"), subject to the Rules and Regulations under Section 7.3.

**2.2 Condition of Premises.** Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date. However, Landlord shall deliver the Premises with the Building's base systems serving the Premises (heating, ventilation and air conditioning, electrical, plumbing and life-safety) in good working order, and broom clean.

**2.3 Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term once, for five years (the "**Renewal Term**"). To do so, Tenant must give Landlord written notice at least nine months and not more than fifteen months before the Expiration Date, and no Event of Default may then be continuing. Base Rent for the first year of the Renewal Term will be 95% of the fair market rent for comparable office space near John F. Kennedy International Airport, as agreed by the parties. If they do not agree within 30 days after Tenant's notice, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten years' experience in the Queens office market, and the two brokers shall determine fair market rent. If they cannot agree, they shall appoint a third such broker, whose determination is binding. Base Rent for each later year of the Renewal Term increases by 3% over the prior year. All other terms of this Lease apply during the Renewal Term.

**3.2 Abatement.** Base Rent is abated for the first two full calendar months of the Term. Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes is payable during the abatement period.

**3.3 Additional Rent.** All sums Tenant must pay under this Lease, other than Base Rent, are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

**3.4 Payment.** Tenant shall pay Rent without demand, deduction or set-off, except as expressly provided in this Lease. Payment shall be made in lawful money of the United States by ACH or wire transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing. Rent for any partial month is prorated on a per-diem basis.

**3.5 Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to 4% of the overdue amount. Unpaid Rent also bears interest at 8% per annum from the due date until paid.

## 4. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**4.1 Definitions.** "**Operating Expenses**" means all reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, clean, secure and insure the Property, determined in accordance with sound real estate accounting practice consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital expenditures, except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first in effect after the Effective Date, in each case amortized over their useful life;

(b) costs of leasing space, including brokerage commissions, legal fees and tenant improvements;

(c) debt service, ground rent and depreciation;

(d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(e) costs of correcting construction defects in the Building;

(f) management fees exceeding 3% of gross Building revenues; and

(g) costs caused by Landlord's negligence or violation of law.

"**Taxes**" means all real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Property, other than Landlord's income, franchise, estate and transfer taxes.

**4.2 Payment of Increases.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed those for the Base Year. Landlord shall give Tenant a reasonable estimate before each calendar year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each monthly installment of Base Rent.

**4.3 Annual Statement; Audit.** Within 120 days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year. Within 30 days after delivery, the parties shall settle any overpayment or underpayment. Tenant may audit Landlord's books relating to the statement, on 30 days' notice given within 180 days after receipt. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share by more than 4%, Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit.

**4.4 Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than 95% occupied in any year, including the Base Year, variable Operating Expenses for that year shall be adjusted to what they would have been at 95% occupancy.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**5.1 Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord in cash. Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in a segregated, interest-bearing account at a bank in the State of New York, as required by Section 7-103 of the New York General Obligations Law. Landlord may retain the administrative fee permitted by that statute.

**5.2 Application.** If an Event of Default occurs, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure it. Tenant shall restore the Security Deposit to its full amount within ten days after demand.

**5.3 Return.** Within 30 days after the end of the Term, Landlord shall return to Tenant the Security Deposit and accrued interest, less any amount properly applied under Section 5.2.

## 6. USE AND COMPLIANCE

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use. Tenant shall not prepare, store or handle food in the Premises for commercial distribution, except for ordinary employee pantry use.

**6.2 Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, base systems and Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act, and the cost of compliance is an Operating Expense only to the extent Section 4.1 permits.

**6.3 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Property any hazardous materials, other than ordinary office and cleaning supplies used in compliance with law.

**6.4 Airport Proximity.** Tenant acknowledges that the Property is near an operating airport and is subject to aircraft noise and overflight. Tenant shall not install any antenna, light or structure that would require notice to the Federal Aviation Administration without Landlord's prior written consent.

## 7. SERVICES AND RULES

**7.1 Services.** Landlord shall furnish to the Premises:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding holidays, with after-hours service available at Landlord's then-current hourly rate;

(b) electricity for normal office use, measured by a submeter and billed at Landlord's actual cost without markup;

(c) passenger elevator service at all times;

(d) janitorial service five nights per week, to the standard of comparable office buildings in the area; and

(e) controlled access to the Building at all times.

**7.2 Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. However, if an interruption caused by Landlord's negligence makes the Premises untenantable for more than five consecutive Business Days, Rent abates from the sixth Business Day until the service is restored. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

**7.3 Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the rules and regulations attached as Exhibit C, as Landlord may reasonably amend them on written notice (the "**Rules and Regulations**"). Landlord shall enforce them in a non-discriminatory manner.

## 8. ALTERATIONS AND REPAIRS

**8.1 Alterations.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, costing less than $25,000 in any twelve-month period that do not affect the Building structure or base systems. All alterations shall be performed by licensed and insured contractors in compliance with law.

**8.2 Tenant Repairs.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good order and repair, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted.

**8.3 Landlord Repairs.** Landlord shall repair and maintain the roof, foundation, structural elements, exterior walls, Common Areas and base Building systems, in a manner consistent with comparable office buildings in the area.

**8.4 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall remove its trade fixtures and movable property. Tenant need not remove alterations unless Landlord required removal as a condition of its consent.

## 9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**9.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain during the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance on Tenant's personal property and alterations, for full replacement cost; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

**9.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building for full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas, in amounts customary for comparable buildings.

**9.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent the loss is, or is required by this Lease to be, covered by property insurance. Each party shall cause its property insurance policies to permit this waiver.

**9.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.3, each party shall indemnify and hold the other harmless from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**10.1 Casualty.** If fire or other casualty damages the Premises or access to them, Landlord shall restore them with reasonable diligence, subject to available insurance proceeds, and Rent abates in proportion to the untenantable area until restoration. If Landlord reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than 210 days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by notice within 30 days after Landlord's estimate.

**10.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that the remainder is unsuitable for the Permitted Use, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease ends on the date of the taking. The award belongs to Landlord, but Tenant may seek a separate award for its moving expenses and trade fixtures.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**11.1 Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to a complete request within 15 Business Days.

**11.2 Change of Control.** A transfer, in one or more transactions, of more than 50% of the voting stock of Tenant, or of the direct or indirect controlling interest in Tenant, is an assignment for purposes of this Article 11 and requires Landlord's consent under Section 11.1. In deciding whether to consent to a change of control, Landlord may consider only the financial strength of Tenant after the transaction and the continued use of the Premises for the Permitted Use.

**11.3 Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet the Premises without Landlord's consent to an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with Tenant, provided that Tenant gives Landlord written notice at least ten Business Days in advance and the transferee assumes Tenant's obligations under this Lease in writing.

**11.4 No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees in writing.

## 12. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**12.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent when due, and the failure continues for five Business Days after written notice from Landlord;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease, and the failure continues for 30 days after written notice from Landlord, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within 30 days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently pursue it to completion; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that, if involuntary, is not dismissed within 60 days.

**12.2 Remedies.** While an Event of Default continues, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises in accordance with law. Tenant remains liable for Rent through the date of termination and for damages equal to the Rent that would have been payable for the rest of the Term, less the net rent Landlord actually receives from reletting. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to relet the Premises and mitigate its damages.

**12.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform an obligation under this Lease for 30 days after written notice from Tenant, or for a longer reasonable period if the cure cannot be completed within 30 days and Landlord is diligently pursuing it, Tenant may pursue its remedies at law or in equity.

## 13. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL

**13.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Property, provided that the mortgagee delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement. Under that agreement, Tenant's possession is not disturbed so long as no Event of Default is continuing.

**13.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten Business Days after a request from either party, the other party shall deliver a certificate stating whether this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 14. MISCELLANEOUS

**14.1 Notices.** All notices under this Lease must be in writing and must be delivered personally or by nationally recognized overnight courier to the addresses below, or to any other address a party designates by notice:

*To Landlord:* Ashgrove Airport Properties LLC, 148-20 Ashgrove Boulevard, Suite 100, Jamaica, New York 11430, Attention: Asset Manager.

**14.2 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant may peaceably enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

**14.3 Holdover.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at 150% of the rate in effect at the end of the Term, prorated per diem.

**14.4 Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Halloran Commercial Realty, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement.

**14.5 Landlord's Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Property, including rents and insurance and condemnation proceeds.

**14.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**14.7 Successors.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**14.8 Governing Law; Venue.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any action arising out of this Lease shall be brought in the state courts located in Queens County, New York, or in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS LEASE.
