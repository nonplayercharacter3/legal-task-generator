**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), from **Noumisk Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). We refer to the acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

Except for the Binding Provisions described in Section 14, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' present intentions only.

## 1. Structure

Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, under a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). After the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"), the Company will be a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer. The Company will continue to operate its regional airline catering and galley-provisioning business (the "**Business**"), including its flight kitchen and commissary facility (the "**Flight Kitchen**").

## 2. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Shares will be $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price assumes that the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of working capital. Buyer will pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows:

(a) **Cash.** Buyer will pay Seller cash by wire transfer equal to the Purchase Price, less the principal amount of the Seller Note and less the Escrow Amount.

(b) **Seller Note.** Buyer will issue to Seller a subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**") as deferred consideration. The Seller Note will bear interest at a fixed rate and will amortize over approximately three years. It will be subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing. Buyer will have a right to set off indemnifiable amounts against the Seller Note, as described in Section 4.

(c) **Escrow.** Buyer will deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with a mutually acceptable national bank acting as escrow agent. The Escrow Amount will secure Seller's indemnification obligations, and it will be held for 18 months after the Closing.

## 3. Catering Trucks

We understand that certain of the high-lift catering trucks used in the Business are owned by Seller rather than the Company. At the Closing, Seller will convey those trucks, with their certificates of title, to the Company by bill of sale, free and clear of all liens. No consideration beyond the Purchase Price will be payable for them.

## 4. Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for a transaction of this type, including the following:

(a) General representations and warranties will survive for 18 months after the Closing. Representations concerning organization, authority, capitalization and title to the Shares, and brokers (the "**Fundamental Representations**"), and representations concerning taxes, will survive until shortly after the applicable statute of limitations expires.

(b) Seller's liability for breaches of general representations will be subject to a basket and to a cap equal to 10% of the Purchase Price. The cap and basket will not apply to claims for fraud, breach of Fundamental Representations, or pre-Closing taxes.

(c) Buyer will recover indemnifiable losses first from the escrow, then at Buyer's election by set-off against the Seller Note, and then directly from Seller.

## 5. Food Safety Matters

Seller has disclosed that, in January 2026, a listeria finding was made at the Flight Kitchen, and that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration has issued a warning letter in respect of the Flight Kitchen. The Definitive Agreement will include a specific indemnity from Seller, not subject to the basket, for all losses arising out of these matters. Those losses will include fines, penalties, recall and remediation costs, and amounts paid to any governmental authority. Claims under the specific indemnity will be satisfied first from the escrow. Before the Closing, Seller will cause the Company to continue its corrective action plan and to keep Buyer informed of all regulatory communications.

## 6. Seller Guarantees

We understand that Seller guarantees the Company's obligations under the Flight Kitchen lease and under certain food and beverage supply contracts. After the Closing, Buyer will use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain Seller's release from those guarantees, including by offering substitute credit support reasonably acceptable to the counterparties.

## 7. Due Diligence

Buyer's proposal is subject to the satisfactory completion of business, financial, legal, tax, environmental and regulatory due diligence. The diligence review will cover the Company's airline customer agreements, food safety and sanitation records, and FDA correspondence. Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the Company's personnel, facilities, books and records.

## 8. Conditions

The Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and related ancillary agreements;

(b) receipt of required third-party consents, including change-of-control consents from key airline customers and from the landlord under the Company's administrative office lease;

(c) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business; and

(d) approval by Buyer's board of directors.

## 9. Timing

The parties will use good-faith efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement by early March 2026, and to close the Transaction on or about April 30, 2026.

## 10. Employees

Buyer intends to retain the Company's employees. For 12 months after the Closing, Buyer will maintain base compensation for continuing employees at levels no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the Closing.

## 11. Exclusivity

For 45 days after Seller countersigns this Letter, neither Seller nor the Company, nor any of their representatives, will:

(a) solicit, initiate, encourage or engage in discussions or negotiations with any person other than Buyer concerning any sale of the Company, the Shares or a material part of the Business; or

(b) furnish information to any such person for that purpose.

Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal it receives.

## 12. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, are confidential. They are subject to the confidentiality agreement between Buyer and Seller dated December 15, 2025, which remains in effect.

## 13. Expenses

Each party will bear its own expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 14. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding. No other provision of this Letter creates any binding obligation, and no party is obligated to complete the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time by written notice. The Binding Provisions will survive any such termination, except that Section 11 will end on termination.

## 15. Governing Law

This Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Any action arising out of the Binding Provisions shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York.
