# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of April 30, 2026, by and among **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Noumisk Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pelloway Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), from Seller.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,850,000 with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** As used in this Escrow Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Closing Date is April 30, 2026.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $4,850,000.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, as reduced by disbursements made under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court described in Section 11.2 that determines the entitlement of Buyer or Seller to all or part of the Escrow Funds.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, instrumentality, regulatory body, court or tribunal of any of them.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, actually incurred by a Buyer Indemnitee, excluding fines, penalties and other amounts paid to any Governmental Authority.

"**Buyer Indemnitees**" has the meaning given to it in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Escrow Agreement unless otherwise stated. The Escrow Agent is not bound by, and is not charged with knowledge of, the terms of the Purchase Agreement, other than the defined terms expressly incorporated in this Section 1.1.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Appointment and Deposit.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall acknowledge receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Account**"). It shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds. It shall disburse the Escrow Funds only in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**2.3 No Interest of Escrow Agent.** The Escrow Funds are not property of the Escrow Agent and are not subject to any lien, set-off or claim of the Escrow Agent or its creditors, other than as provided in Section 8.3.

## 3. INVESTMENT

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the money market deposit account or United States Treasury money market fund that Buyer and Seller specify in Joint Written Instructions. If it receives no instructions, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured to the extent provided by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

**3.2 Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds form part of the Escrow Funds and shall be disbursed with them.

**3.3 No Liability for Investment Losses.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss on any investment made in accordance with this Article 3, or for any failure to obtain the best rate of return.

## 4. INDEMNIFICATION CLAIMS

**4.1 Claim Notice.** At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has incurred, or reasonably expects to incur, Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice must state:

(a) the amount of the Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of them;

(b) in reasonable detail, the basis for the claim; and

(c) the amount of Escrow Funds Buyer requests to be disbursed or retained.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after Seller receives the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice must state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the amount in dispute.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent receives no Objection Notice within the 30-day period, the claimed amount is deemed undisputed. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a Claim Notice, the part not disputed is deemed undisputed. On the fifth Business Day after the end of the 30-day period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Funds, the undisputed amount of Losses.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any disputed amount until it receives either (a) Joint Written Instructions directing its disbursement or (b) a Final Order, together with a certificate of the party presenting it stating that the order is final and non-appealable. Within five Business Days after it receives either one, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount as directed.

**4.5 Claims Limited to Losses.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds to Buyer under this Article 4 only in respect of Losses. If any amount claimed in a Claim Notice exceeds the Escrow Funds then available, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available, and it has no further obligation with respect to the excess.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Disbursement Authority.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds only:

(a) in accordance with Article 4;

(b) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions;

(c) in accordance with a Final Order; or

(d) in accordance with Section 5.2.

**5.2 Release Date.** Within five Business Days after the Release Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent. On receipt, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices that are then pending and unresolved (the "**Retained Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Retained Amount. As each pending claim is resolved under Article 4, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the corresponding part of the Retained Amount to Buyer or Seller, as applicable.

**5.3 Method of Payment.** The Escrow Agent shall make all disbursements by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account the recipient designates in writing. Before making any disbursement, the Escrow Agent may confirm wire instructions by a call-back to a person listed on Schedule A.

## 6. TAX MATTERS

**6.1 Tax Owner.** For federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Funds.

**6.2 Tax Forms.** Before the Closing Date, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file all information returns required of it as escrow agent.

**6.3 Tax Distributions.** Within 15 days after Buyer's written request, the Escrow Agent shall release to Buyer, from the earnings on the Escrow Funds, an amount equal to 40% of the earnings allocable to Buyer for the preceding calendar year. This amount shall not exceed the earnings then held.

## 7. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial and are only those expressly set out in this Escrow Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a fiduciary of any party.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on, and is protected in acting on, any written notice, instruction, certificate or order that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper person. It has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

**7.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent a court finds that its gross negligence or willful misconduct caused the action or omission. The Escrow Agent is not liable in any event for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**7.4 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions concerning the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also interplead the Escrow Funds into a court described in Section 11.2.

**7.5 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is fully protected for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

## 8. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Fees.** Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the Escrow Agent's fees as set out on Schedule B, and one-half of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

**8.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and hold it harmless from and against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Escrow Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to the extent any of those amounts results from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 8.2, unless a court determines that the act or omission of one of them caused the claim.

**8.3 Lien.** If any amount owed to the Escrow Agent under this Article 8 remains unpaid for 30 days after the Escrow Agent's written demand, the Escrow Agent may pay it from the earnings on the Escrow Funds.

**8.4 Survival.** This Article 8 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**9.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving Buyer and Seller at least 30 days' prior written notice.

**9.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions given at least 30 days in advance.

**9.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The successor must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may deposit the Escrow Funds with a court described in Section 11.2 or petition that court to appoint a successor. The Escrow Agent's duties end when it delivers the Escrow Funds to the successor or to the court.

## 10. TERMINATION

This Escrow Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed all of the Escrow Funds in accordance with its terms. Sections 7.3 and 8.2 and Article 11 survive termination.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement must be in writing and must be delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on Schedule A. A party may change its address by notice to the other parties. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**11.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Escrow Agreement, and any dispute as to entitlement to the Escrow Funds, including any dispute concerning the underlying indemnification claim that is the subject of a Claim Notice, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware, in any action or proceeding of that kind. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**11.5 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that federal law requires the Escrow Agent to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person opening an account. Each of them shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**11.6 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not responsible for any delay or failure in performance caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control. These circumstances include acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemics, labor disputes, and failures of communications or computer systems.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
