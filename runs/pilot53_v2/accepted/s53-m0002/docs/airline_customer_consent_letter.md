**Re: Consent to Change of Control: Inflight Catering and Galley Provisioning Services Agreement**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

We refer to the Inflight Catering and Galley Provisioning Services Agreement dated as of September 1, 2022, between **Corriedale Regional Airways, Inc.** ("**Corriedale**") and **Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). We refer to that agreement, as amended to the date of this letter, as the "**Catering Agreement**."

The Company has informed us of the following. Under a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Saitosk**"), has agreed to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from **Noumisk Holdings, LLC** ("**Noumisk Holdings**"). The closing of that acquisition (the "**Closing**") is expected to occur on April 30, 2026. After the Closing, the Company will be a wholly owned subsidiary of Saitosk (the "**Transaction**").

The Company has asked for Corriedale's consent to the Transaction under the change-of-control provisions of the Catering Agreement. Corriedale provides that consent in this letter (this "**Consent**").

## 1. Consent

Subject to the terms of this Consent, Corriedale consents to the Transaction and to the resulting change in the direct and indirect ownership and control of the Company. Corriedale agrees that the consummation of the Transaction will not:

(a) constitute a breach of, or a default or event of default under, the Catering Agreement;

(b) give rise to any right of Corriedale to terminate the Catering Agreement, or to suspend or modify its performance under it; or

(c) entitle Corriedale to any fee, rebate, price adjustment or other payment.

## 2. Continuation of the Catering Agreement

**2.1 No Amendment.** The Catering Agreement remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms, before and after the Closing. Except as expressly stated in Section 1, this Consent does not amend, modify or waive any provision of the Catering Agreement.

**2.2 Same Contracting Party.** The Company remains the contracting party under the Catering Agreement after the Closing. The Company continues to be responsible for the performance of all of its obligations under the Catering Agreement, including its obligations concerning food safety, sanitation, quality assurance, airside operations and insurance.

**2.3 Reservation of Rights.** Corriedale reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Catering Agreement and applicable law with respect to the Company's performance, whether before or after the Closing. This Consent does not waive any such right or remedy.

**2.4 Subsequent Changes of Control.** This Consent applies only to the Transaction. Any later change of control of the Company, or any assignment of the Catering Agreement, remains subject to the terms of the Catering Agreement.

## 3. Confirmations

To Corriedale's knowledge as of the date of this Consent:

(a) the Catering Agreement is in full force and effect;

(b) Corriedale has not delivered to the Company any notice of termination or non-renewal of the Catering Agreement; and

(c) no undisputed invoice issued by the Company under the Catering Agreement is more than 60 days past due.

These confirmations are made only for the benefit of the Company and Saitosk. They do not constitute a representation or warranty of Corriedale, and they do not limit Corriedale's rights under Section 2.3.

## 4. Effectiveness

**4.1 Effective Date.** This Consent is effective on the date of this letter, and it is irrevocable through the Closing.

**4.2 Lapse.** If the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, this Consent will lapse and be of no further effect, unless Corriedale agrees in writing to extend it.

**4.3 Notice of Closing.** The Company shall notify Corriedale in writing within five Business Days after the Closing occurs. The notice must confirm the Closing Date and update the Company's notice contacts under the Catering Agreement, if any have changed. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

## 5. Reliance

Saitosk and the Company may rely on this Consent in connection with the Transaction, including in satisfying the condition to closing in Section 7.1(c) of the Purchase Agreement. Noumisk Holdings may also rely on it for those purposes. No other person may rely on this Consent.

## 6. Confidentiality

Corriedale shall treat the existence and terms of the Transaction as confidential information of the Company under the confidentiality provisions of the Catering Agreement until the Transaction is publicly announced by Saitosk or the Company.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Governing Law.** This Consent is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**7.2 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Consent may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic signature or by email in portable document format. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.

**7.3 Amendment.** This Consent may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Corriedale and the Company.
