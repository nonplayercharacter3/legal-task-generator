# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000** | **New York, New York** | **April 30, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Saitosk Aviation Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to **Noumisk Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, or its registered assigns ("**Holder**"), the principal sum of SIX MILLION DOLLARS ($6,000,000). Maker shall also pay interest on the unpaid principal balance from the date of this Note at the rate and on the terms below.

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2 of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are Maker, as Buyer, Holder, as Seller, and, for limited purposes, Noumisk Skyline Catering, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). Maker issues this Note as deferred consideration and in partial payment of the Purchase Price.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Business Day," "Cap," "Closing Date," "Escrow Agreement," "Escrow Funds," "Fraud," "Fundamental Representations," "Governmental Authority," "Losses," "Person" and "Taxes." In addition:

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1.

"**Indemnity Claim**" means any claim by a Buyer Indemnitee for indemnification from Holder under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Maturity Date**" means April 30, 2029.

"**Obligations**" means all principal, interest and other amounts payable by Maker under this Note.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness and other obligations of Maker or the Company, now or later outstanding, under any credit agreement, loan agreement, note purchase agreement or similar financing arrangement with one or more banks, institutional lenders or other financial institutions. Senior Debt includes all refinancings, replacements and extensions of that indebtedness, and all interest, fees, expenses and other amounts payable on it, including amounts accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding.

"**Senior Lender**" means each holder of Senior Debt, or any agent or trustee acting for holders of Senior Debt.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Note unless otherwise stated.

## 2. INTEREST

**2.1 Rate.** The unpaid principal balance of this Note bears simple interest at 6.0% per annum. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each January, April, July and October, starting on July 31, 2026, and on the Maturity Date.

**2.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default continues, the unpaid principal balance bears interest at 8.0% per annum, payable on demand.

**2.4 Maximum Rate.** Interest payable under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any interest paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal.

## 3. PAYMENT OF PRINCIPAL

**3.1 Installments.** Maker shall repay the principal of this Note in three installments, as follows:

(a) $2,000,000 on April 30, 2027;

(b) $2,000,000 on April 30, 2028; and

(c) the remaining unpaid principal balance, with all accrued and unpaid interest, on the Maturity Date.

**3.2 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment must include accrued interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments are applied to installments in inverse order of maturity.

**3.3 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, to the account Holder designates in writing at least five Business Days before the payment date. If a payment date is not a Business Day, the payment is due on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue until it is made.

**3.4 Application of Payments.** Payments are applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 8.3, then to accrued interest, and then to principal.

## 4. SUBORDINATION

**4.1 Subordination.** The Obligations are subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set forth in this Article 4.

**4.2 Permitted Payments.** Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under Sections 2 and 3, unless a payment default or other event of default under the Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing and the Senior Lender has given Maker and Holder written notice of it. In that case no payment shall be made under this Note until the default has been cured or waived, or until 180 days after the notice, whichever occurs first.

**4.3 Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding relating to Maker, all Senior Debt shall be paid in full in cash before any payment is made on this Note.

**4.4 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment that Article 4 does not permit, Holder shall hold it in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly pay it over to the Senior Lender.

**4.5 Subordination Agreement.** At the request of any Senior Lender, Holder shall enter into a subordination agreement consistent with this Article 4 and otherwise in customary form.

**4.6 Set-Off Not a Payment.** A set-off under Article 5 is not a payment to Holder for purposes of this Article 4.

## 5. SET-OFF

**5.1 Right of Set-Off.** Maker may set off against any principal or interest then outstanding or later becoming due under this Note the amount of any Losses for which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification from Holder under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. This right is exercisable in accordance with Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement, to the extent the Escrow Funds are insufficient or unavailable. A set-off may be made for:

(a) any amount agreed in writing by Maker and Holder;

(b) any amount determined in a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction; or

(c) any amount claimed in good faith in a written notice delivered to Holder that has not been resolved under clause (a) or (b). In that case Maker may withhold the amount claimed from payments otherwise due and hold it until the claim is resolved. When the claim is resolved, Maker shall within ten Business Days pay to Holder any withheld amount finally determined not to be owing, with interest at the rate in Section 2.1.

**5.2 Limitation on Recourse.** Set-off against this Note under Section 5.1 is the sole and exclusive means by which Maker and the other Buyer Indemnitees may recover from Holder any Losses in excess of the Escrow Funds. The aggregate amount set off under this Note, together with all amounts disbursed to the Buyer Indemnitees from the Escrow Funds, shall not exceed the Cap, including claims for breach of Fundamental Representations and claims for Taxes. This Section 5.2 does not limit recovery for Losses arising out of Fraud.

**5.3 Effect of Set-Off.** Each amount set off reduces the Obligations then due, or next becoming due, in the order Maker elects. For all purposes it is treated as a payment by Maker under this Note on the date of the set-off. A set-off or withholding made in good faith under Section 5.1 is not a failure to pay and is not an Event of Default.

**5.4 Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off or withholding. The notice must state the amount and describe the related Indemnity Claim in reasonable detail.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS OF MAKER

Maker represents to Holder that:

(a) Maker is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) Maker has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Note and to perform its obligations under it, and has duly authorized this Note by all necessary corporate action; and

(c) this Note is the legal, valid and binding obligation of Maker, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

## 7. COVENANTS OF MAKER

So long as any Obligations remain outstanding:

**7.1 Financial Information.** Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within 30 days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of the end of that month and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and for the fiscal year to date, certified by the chief financial officer of Maker as fairly presenting in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company;

(b) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Maker for that fiscal year; and

(c) promptly after Holder's reasonable request, such other information about the financial condition of Maker and the Company as Holder may reasonably request.

**7.2 Notice of Default.** Maker shall notify Holder in writing within five Business Days after any officer of Maker becomes aware of any Event of Default, or of any event that would become an Event of Default with notice or lapse of time.

**7.3 Existence.** Maker shall maintain its corporate existence and shall cause the Company to maintain its corporate existence. However, Maker may merge or consolidate with another Person if Maker is the surviving entity or the surviving entity expressly assumes the Obligations in writing.

**7.4 Restricted Payments.** Maker shall not declare or pay any dividend or other distribution on its capital stock while an Event of Default continues.

## 8. EVENTS OF DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, and the failure continues for ten Business Days after written notice from Holder;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest under this Note when due, and the failure continues for 15 Business Days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Article 7, and the failure continues for 30 days after written notice from Holder;

(d) Maker or the Company commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law; makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its property;

(e) an involuntary case or proceeding of the kind described in clause (d) is commenced against Maker or the Company and is not dismissed within 60 days; or

(f) Senior Debt in an aggregate principal amount exceeding $5,000,000 is accelerated, and the acceleration is not rescinded within 30 days.

**8.2 Acceleration.** On the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 8.1(d) or 8.1(e), the entire unpaid principal of this Note and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable, without notice or other action by Holder. While any other Event of Default continues, Holder may declare the entire unpaid principal of this Note and all accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker. In each case acceleration is subject to Article 4.

**8.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection incurred by Holder after an Event of Default, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

**8.4 Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Articles 4 and 5, Holder's rights and remedies under this Note are cumulative and are in addition to any other rights and remedies available at law or in equity.

## 9. TRANSFER AND ASSIGNMENT

**9.1 Assignment.** Holder may assign or transfer this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker. Holder shall give Maker written notice of the transfer, including the name and notice address of the transferee, within five Business Days after it occurs. Each transferee succeeds to all rights of Holder under this Note. Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder.

**9.2 Register.** Maker shall keep a register recording the name and address of each holder of this Note and the principal amount held by each. Maker may treat the Person shown in the register as Holder for all purposes, including the making of payments. On surrender of this Note in connection with a partial transfer, Maker shall issue new notes in the same form, with an aggregate principal amount equal to the unpaid principal of the surrendered Note.

**9.3 Defenses Preserved.** Each transferee of this Note takes it subject to Articles 4 and 5 and to all rights of set-off and defenses Maker has against Holder.

**9.4 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from Holder. Holder is not required to post a bond.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Note must be in writing and must be delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth in the Purchase Agreement. Notices to a transferee are delivered to the address recorded in the register under Section 9.2. A party may change its address by notice to the other.

**10.2 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by its terms. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right is a waiver of that right.

**10.3 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any of its provisions waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**10.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, in accordance with Section 12.8 of the Purchase Agreement. MAKER AND HOLDER EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**10.6 Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits Holder and its successors and assigns.

**10.7 Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronically signed counterpart has the same effect as a manually signed original.
