Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its WashLoop plant management, RFID garment-tracking and route-dispatch software to Licensee and provides related Implementation Services, Support Services and hosting.

During the negotiation of the Agreement, the parties discussed the categories of Customer Data that Licensee and its Affiliates will enter into, upload to and generate through the Software. They also discussed the responsibilities each party will bear for that data. Those categories include data relating to Licensee's hospital, healthcare system and other institutional customers. This letter (this "**Side Letter**") records the parties' agreement on those matters. It is entered into concurrently with the Agreement and Order Form No. 001.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings in this Side Letter:

"**Customer Contract**" means any agreement between Licensee or its Affiliates and a Licensee Customer for the supply of laundry, linen-rental, uniform, garment-management or related services.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the collection, use, storage, disclosure, transfer or other processing of Customer Data. These include, to the extent applicable, the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations, state health-information and medical-privacy laws, state data-breach notification laws, and state consumer privacy laws.

"**Healthcare Customer**" means a Licensee Customer that is a hospital, health system, ambulatory surgery center, physician practice, long-term care facility, skilled nursing facility, home health agency or other provider of healthcare services.

"**Licensee Customer**" means any customer of Licensee or its Affiliates to which Licensee or its Affiliates supply laundry, linen-rental, uniform, garment-management or related services.

"**Patient-Linen Data**" means Customer Data that records the issue, location, movement, use, return, soiling, rewash, loss or retirement of linen, gowns, scrubs, surgical textiles or other items used in connection with the care of patients or residents at a Healthcare Customer. It includes data that associates those items with a ward, unit, room, bed, department or care area.

"**Personnel Data**" means Customer Data relating to identifiable individuals who are employees or contractors of Licensee, its Affiliates or a Licensee Customer. It includes route-driver records, Authorized User credentials and activity logs, and uniform-wearer records maintained in the RFID Garment Tracking module.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Side Letter where the context requires. Unless otherwise stated, references in this Side Letter to numbered Sections are to sections of this Side Letter. References to Sections of the Agreement are identified as such.

## 2. CUSTOMER DATA RESPONSIBILITIES

**2.1 Categories of Customer Data.** The parties acknowledge that the Customer Data processed through the Software is expected to include the following categories of data:

(a) garment and linen inventory records, RFID tag registries and item masters;

(b) RFID tag reads captured by tunnel readers at the Plants and by handheld scanners at Licensee Customer locations;

(c) soil-in and clean-out counts, par levels, loss, rewash and reject records by Licensee Customer, department and location;

(d) Patient-Linen Data relating to Healthcare Customers;

(e) uniform-wearer records, including the names, employee numbers, departments and garment sizes of individuals to whom uniforms are issued under Customer Contracts;

(f) route, stop, delivery and pickup records, including signatures, delivery exceptions and photographs captured through the WashLoop Driver mobile application;

(g) pricing, billing and invoicing data for Licensee Customers; and

(h) Personnel Data.

**2.2 Licensee Responsibilities.** As between the parties, Licensee is responsible for the following:

(a) determining the categories of Customer Data that Licensee and its Affiliates enter into, upload to or generate through the Software, and the purposes for which they do so;

(b) the accuracy, quality and integrity of the Customer Data as entered into or uploaded to the Software by or on behalf of Licensee, its Affiliates and its Authorized Users;

(c) obtaining all rights, consents and authorizations under the Customer Contracts, and giving all notices, that are required for Licensee and its Affiliates to collect the Customer Data and to make it available to Licensor for processing in accordance with the Agreement;

(d) complying with the data-handling, confidentiality and security terms of each Customer Contract, to the extent those terms apply to Licensee's own collection and use of Customer Data; and

(e) configuring the Software, including the optional data fields in the RFID Garment Tracking and Route Dispatch modules, so that Licensee and its Affiliates collect only the Customer Data that they need for their operations.

**2.3 Patient-Linen Data.** The Software is designed to track items, not patients. Licensee shall not enter into the Software, or configure the Software to capture, the name, date of birth, medical record number, diagnosis, treatment or other clinical information of any patient or resident of a Healthcare Customer. This applies whether the data would be entered directly or through a free-text field, a delivery exception note or a photograph. Patient-Linen Data may identify a ward, unit, room, bed, department or care area, and Licensee may enter it in that form.

**2.4 Customer Instructions.** If a Licensee Customer instructs Licensee to restrict, correct, export or delete Customer Data relating to that Licensee Customer, Licensee shall carry out the instruction using the administrative functions of the Software. Where an instruction cannot be carried out through those functions, Licensee may submit a request through the web-based ticketing portal. Licensor shall carry out a request submitted in that way within ten (10) Business Days after receipt, as a Severity 3 issue under Exhibit A to the Agreement.

**2.5 Licensor Responsibilities.** This Side Letter does not reduce Licensor's obligations under Section 7 of the Agreement. Those obligations include the use restrictions in Section 7.2, the security program in Section 7.4 and Exhibit B, Security Incident notification under Section 7.6, return and deletion under Section 7.8 and subprocessor obligations under Section 7.9.

## 3. CUSTOMER DATA INDEMNITY

**3.1 Indemnity.** Licensee shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Licensor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents against all Claims arising out of or relating to the Customer Data, and against all damages, judgments, fines, penalties, settlement amounts, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) awarded against or incurred by any of them in connection with any such Claim. The Claims covered by this Section 3.1 include Claims brought by Licensee Customers, including Healthcare Customers, by individuals to whom any Customer Data relates, and by regulators or other governmental authorities.

**3.2 Included Claims.** The Claims described in Section 3.1 include Claims arising out of or relating to:

(a) the collection of Customer Data by Licensee, its Affiliates or its Authorized Users, or the provision of Customer Data to Licensor for processing in accordance with the Agreement;

(b) Patient-Linen Data and other garment, uniform and linen tracking data relating to Healthcare Customers;

(c) any alleged failure of Licensee or its Affiliates to obtain a right, consent or authorization, or to give a notice, described in Section 2.2(c);

(d) any alleged breach of a Customer Contract;

(e) uniform-wearer records and Personnel Data; and

(f) any alleged violation of Data Protection Laws in connection with the Customer Data.

**3.3 Procedure.** Section 10.4 of the Agreement applies to Claims under this Section 3. In that Section, Licensee is the indemnifying party and the person seeking indemnity is the indemnified party.

**3.4 Cooperation in Customer Matters.** If a Licensee Customer raises with Licensor any inquiry, complaint or demand relating to Customer Data, Licensor shall promptly refer it to Licensee. Licensor shall not respond to its substance except as Licensee directs in writing or as required by law. Licensee shall manage the relationship with the Licensee Customer on that matter.

## 4. HEALTHCARE CUSTOMER ARRANGEMENTS

**4.1 Business Associate Agreements.** Where a Healthcare Customer requires Licensee to flow down data-protection terms to its service providers, Licensee may request under Section 7.7 of the Agreement that Licensor enter into a business associate agreement or data-protection agreement. Licensee shall make the request in writing, enclose the relevant terms of the Customer Contract, and identify the categories of Customer Data to which the request relates. Licensor shall respond with any comments within fifteen (15) Business Days after receipt. The parties shall then negotiate in good faith with a view to signing the agreement within thirty (30) days after the request.

**4.2 Standard Form.** To simplify requests under Section 4.1, the parties shall work together to agree a standard form of business associate agreement within sixty (60) days after the date of this Side Letter. Once agreed, the standard form shall be used for requests under Section 4.1 unless the Healthcare Customer's requirements cannot be met by it.

**4.3 Customer Audits and Questionnaires.** A Healthcare Customer may ask Licensee for information about the security of the systems used to process its Customer Data. Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance in responding, as follows:

(a) completing a standard security questionnaire, such as a Standardized Information Gathering (SIG) Lite questionnaire or an equivalent, no more than once in any twelve-month period for each Healthcare Customer;

(b) providing a copy of Licensor's most recent SOC 2 Type II report under Section 7.5 of the Agreement, subject to the Healthcare Customer's acceptance of Licensor's standard confidentiality terms for that report; and

(c) making a member of Licensor's security team available for one call of up to one (1) hour for each Healthcare Customer in any twelve-month period.

Licensor may charge assistance beyond these limits at the professional services rates in Order Form No. 001.

**4.4 Data Inventory.** Within thirty (30) days after go-live at the last of the Initial Plants, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a data inventory. The inventory shall identify each data field in the Software modules licensed under Order Form No. 001 that is configured to capture Patient-Linen Data, uniform-wearer records or Personnel Data. Licensor shall update the data inventory on request, no more than once each Contract Year, and after any change to the configuration of those fields.

**4.5 Healthcare Customer Reporting.** Licensee may make available to its Healthcare Customers reports generated by the Software showing inventory, loss, rewash and par-level data for that Healthcare Customer. Licensee may do so through the customer-facing inventory reports in the RFID Garment Tracking module or by export. This does not breach Section 3.1 of the Agreement, provided that no Healthcare Customer is given log-in access to the Software other than as an Authorized User permitted by Licensee.

## ANNEX A: CUSTOMER DATA CATEGORIES BY MODULE

**A.1 Plant Management Module.**

| Data Category | Typical Fields | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Soil receiving | Licensee Customer, soil weight, receipt time, cart identifier | Plant scales and operator entry |
| Wash and finishing | Batch number, formula, weight, cycle time, line | Tunnel washers, conventional washers, operator entry |
| Production reporting | Throughput, pounds per operator hour, rewash rate | Calculated by the Software |
| Operator records | Operator name, employee number, shift | Authorized User entry |

**A.2 RFID Garment Tracking Module.**

| Data Category | Typical Fields | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Item master and tag registry | Tag identifier, item type, size, Licensee Customer, issue date | Licensee entry and migration |
| Tag reads | Tag identifier, reader, location, timestamp | Tunnel readers and handheld scanners |
| Patient-Linen Data | Item type, Healthcare Customer, ward, unit, room, bed, department or care area, soil-in and clean-out counts | Tag reads and Licensee entry |
| Uniform-wearer records | Wearer name, employee number, department, garment sizes, issue history | Licensee entry under Customer Contracts |
| Life-cycle data | Wash count, rewash, reject, loss and retirement records | Calculated by the Software and operator entry |

**A.3 Route Dispatch Module and WashLoop Driver Mobile Application.**

| Data Category | Typical Fields | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Route and stop records | Route, stop, delivery location, schedule | Licensee entry and migration |
| Delivery records | Delivered and collected quantities, signature, delivery exception, photograph | WashLoop Driver mobile application |
| Vehicle data | Vehicle identifier, location, route completion | Telematics interface |
| Driver records | Driver name, employee number, route assignments | Licensee entry |

**A.4 Billing Data.**

| Data Category | Typical Fields | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | Item pricing by Licensee Customer | ERP and billing interface |
| Billing extracts | Delivered quantities, invoice-ready extracts | Calculated by the Software |
| Billing history | Invoices for the preceding twenty-four (24) months | Migration |
