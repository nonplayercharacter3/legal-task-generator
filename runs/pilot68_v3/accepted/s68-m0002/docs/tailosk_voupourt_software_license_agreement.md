## EXHIBIT B – MINIMUM SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

**B.1 Governance.** Licensor shall designate a qualified individual responsible for its information security program and shall review the program at least annually.

**B.2 Access Control.** Access to Customer Data shall be limited to Licensor personnel and subprocessors with a need to know, on a least-privilege basis. Licensor shall review access rights at least quarterly and revoke access promptly upon a change in role or termination of employment.

**B.3 Encryption.** Customer Data shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent industry-standard algorithm. Encryption keys shall be managed in accordance with industry standards and segregated from the encrypted data.

**B.4 Environment Segregation.** Licensor shall logically segregate Customer Data from the data of Licensor's other customers and shall separate production, testing and development environments. Customer Data shall not be used in non-production environments without Licensee's prior written consent.

**B.5 Vulnerability Management.** Licensor shall conduct vulnerability scanning of the Hosted Environment at least monthly and an independent penetration test at least annually, and shall remediate critical vulnerabilities within fifteen (15) days and high vulnerabilities within thirty (30) days of identification. Licensor shall provide Licensee with an executive summary of its most recent penetration test on request.

**B.6 Logging and Monitoring.** Licensor shall log administrative and privileged access to the Hosted Environment and Customer Data, retain logs for at least twelve (12) months, and monitor for anomalous activity.

**B.7 Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery.** Licensor shall maintain and test at least annually a business continuity and disaster recovery plan for the Hosted Environment with a recovery time objective of four (4) hours and a recovery point objective of one (1) hour. Backups of Customer Data shall be stored in a geographically separate location within the United States.

**B.8 Personnel.** Licensor shall conduct background checks on personnel with access to Customer Data, to the extent permitted by law, and shall provide security awareness training to such personnel at least annually.

**B.9 Mobile and Device Security.** The mobile driver application and any device-resident components of the Software shall support remote session revocation, enforce authentication on launch, and not store Customer Data on devices beyond what is necessary for offline route operation, which shall be encrypted.
