# PARENT GUARANTY

**Voupourt Holdings, Inc. in favour of Tailosk Linen & Uniform Services, Inc.**

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of January 15, 2026 by **Voupourt Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2100 Harrow Court, Suite 410, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favour of **Tailosk Linen & Uniform Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 875 Millrace Avenue, Columbus, Ohio 43215 (the "**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Voupourt Software, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and the Beneficiary, as licensee, are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2026 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its WashLoop plant management, RFID garment-tracking and route-dispatch software to the Beneficiary and provides related implementation, support and hosting services.

B. The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding membership interests of Licensor. The Guarantor will receive substantial direct and indirect benefits from the Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

C. Section 14.1 of the Agreement requires Licensor to deliver this Guaranty to the Beneficiary as a condition to the effectiveness of the first Order Form. The Beneficiary has entered into the Agreement in reliance on the Guarantor's agreement to execute and deliver this Guaranty.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows, in consideration of the Beneficiary entering into the Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which the Guarantor acknowledges:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings in this Guaranty:

"**Bankruptcy Event**" means, with respect to any person, that the person becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary case or proceeding under the United States Bankruptcy Code or any other bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation or similar law of any jurisdiction, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Guaranty Documents**" means the Agreement, each Order Form, the Quality Agreement, each Amendment and each Side Letter entered into between Licensor and the Beneficiary in connection with the Agreement, in each case as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Guaranty: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) "including" and similar words mean "including without limitation"; (c) references to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated; (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; (e) references to any agreement or document are to that agreement or document as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time; and (f) references to "days" mean calendar days unless Business Days are specified.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the due and punctual payment, performance and observance of all obligations, covenants, liabilities and agreements of Licensor to the Beneficiary under or in connection with the Guaranty Documents. This includes all obligations to pay refunds, service credits, damages, indemnity amounts, costs and expenses, and all obligations to perform the Implementation Services, Support Services and hosting services. It covers obligations now existing or arising later, whether absolute or contingent, liquidated or unliquidated, and whether arising before or after the commencement of any Bankruptcy Event in respect of Licensor. These obligations are, collectively, the "**Guaranteed Obligations**."

**2.2 Payment Undertaking.** If Licensor fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall pay that amount to the Beneficiary within ten (10) Business Days after written demand from the Beneficiary, in U.S. dollars and in immediately available funds, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction. Any demand shall reasonably describe the amount due and the Guaranty Document under which it arises.

**2.3 Performance Undertaking.** If Licensor fails to perform any non-monetary Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall, within fifteen (15) Business Days after written demand from the Beneficiary, cause that obligation to be performed in accordance with the applicable Guaranty Document. The Guarantor may do so by performing the obligation itself or by procuring its performance by Licensor or by a qualified person acceptable to the Beneficiary, acting reasonably. As an alternative, the Guarantor shall indemnify the Beneficiary against all losses, damages, costs and expenses that the Beneficiary incurs as a result of the failure, to the same extent Licensor would be liable for them under the applicable Guaranty Document.

**2.4 Extent of Liability.** The liability of the Guarantor under this Guaranty is co-extensive with, and no greater than, the liability of Licensor under the Guaranty Documents, as determined in accordance with their terms. The Guarantor may assert any defense, limitation or exclusion of liability available to Licensor under the Guaranty Documents, other than the defenses described in Section 4.

**2.5 Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. The Beneficiary is not required to take any of the following steps before making a demand on, or bringing a claim against, the Guarantor:

(a) make any demand on Licensor or any other Person;

(b) commence or exhaust any proceeding or remedy against Licensor or any other Person;

(c) enforce or realize on any security or other guaranty; or

(d) file any claim in any Bankruptcy Event of Licensor.

**2.6 Separate Obligations.** The obligations of the Guarantor under this Guaranty are independent of the obligations of Licensor. The Beneficiary may bring a separate action against the Guarantor whether or not any action is brought against Licensor and whether or not Licensor is joined in the action.

## 3. CONTINUING GUARANTY

**3.1 Continuing Nature.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It extends to all Guaranteed Obligations arising at any time during the Subscription Term, including any Renewal Term, and to all Guaranteed Obligations that survive expiry or termination of the Agreement under Section 9.8 of the Agreement or otherwise.

**3.2 Duration.** This Guaranty remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been indefeasibly paid and performed in full. The Guarantor may not revoke or terminate this Guaranty while any Guaranteed Obligation remains outstanding or capable of arising.

**3.3 Reinstatement.** This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if at any time any payment or performance of any Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded, avoided or must otherwise be restored or returned by the Beneficiary. This applies whether the restoration or return arises on a Bankruptcy Event of Licensor or otherwise, and in each case the Guaranty is reinstated as though the payment or performance had not occurred.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS UNCONDITIONAL

**4.1 No Discharge.** The obligations of the Guarantor under this Guaranty are absolute and unconditional. The events and circumstances listed below do not release, discharge, reduce or otherwise affect those obligations, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of them or consents to them:

(a) any amendment, supplement, extension, renewal, waiver or other modification of any Guaranty Document, including any change in the Software modules, Plants, usage metrics, Fees, Subscription Term or scope of services;

(b) any extension of time for payment or performance, or any indulgence or forbearance granted to Licensor;

(c) any failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising any right or remedy against Licensor or any other Person;

(d) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of Licensor or the Guarantor;

(e) any merger or consolidation of Licensor or the Guarantor with any other Person;

(f) any Bankruptcy Event affecting Licensor, or any disallowance, stay or discharge of any Guaranteed Obligation in a Bankruptcy Event;

(g) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of any Guaranty Document or Guaranteed Obligation against Licensor, including by reason of any lack of authority or capacity of Licensor;

(h) any release or discharge of any other guarantor or any security for the Guaranteed Obligations;

(i) any claim, set-off or other right that the Guarantor may have against Licensor or the Beneficiary, whether in connection with the Guaranty Documents or otherwise; or

(j) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable discharge or defense of a surety or guarantor, other than full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

**4.2 Consent to Modifications.** The Guarantor consents in advance to every amendment, supplement and other modification of the Guaranty Documents that Licensor and the Beneficiary agree from time to time. The Guaranteed Obligations include the obligations of Licensor under the Guaranty Documents as so modified. The Guarantor's confirmation of any such modification, if requested by the Beneficiary, is for evidentiary purposes only and is not a condition to the extension of this Guaranty to the modified obligations.

## 5. WAIVERS

**5.1 Waiver of Notices and Defenses.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Guarantor waives the following:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) notice of the creation, renewal, extension, accrual or modification of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(c) presentment, demand for payment, protest and notice of dishonour or non-payment, other than the demands expressly required by Sections 2.2 and 2.3;

(d) any requirement that the Beneficiary proceed first against Licensor or any other Person, or against any security;

(e) the benefit of any statute of limitations affecting the Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty, to the extent the limitation period has not run against Licensor; and

(f) all suretyship and other defenses that the Guarantor might otherwise have in law or equity.

**5.2 Informed Waiver.** The Guarantor acknowledges that it has been advised by counsel of its choice regarding the waivers in this Guaranty. It confirms that those waivers are knowing and voluntary and are made as an inducement to the Beneficiary to enter into the Agreement.

## 6. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

**6.1 Postponement of Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been indefeasibly paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any of the following rights arising from its performance under this Guaranty:

(a) any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Licensor; and

(b) any right to participate in any claim or remedy of the Beneficiary against Licensor or any security.

**6.2 Amounts Received.** If the Guarantor receives any amount in exercise of a right described in Section 6.1 while any Guaranteed Obligation remains outstanding, the Guarantor shall hold that amount in trust for the Beneficiary and promptly pay it to the Beneficiary for application against the Guaranteed Obligations.

**6.3 Subordination.** Any indebtedness of Licensor to the Guarantor is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations if Licensor has failed to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due and the failure is continuing. During that period, the Guarantor shall not demand or accept payment of that indebtedness, other than ordinary-course intercompany charges for shared services, without the Beneficiary's prior written consent.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**7.1 Representations.** The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary, as of the date of this Guaranty and as of the date of each confirmation delivered under Section 8.4, as follows:

(a) it is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(d) this Guaranty has been duly executed and delivered by it and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(e) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not and will not:

   (i) conflict with its certificate of incorporation or bylaws;

   (ii) violate any law, regulation, judgment or order applicable to it; or

   (iii) conflict with, or result in a default under, any agreement or instrument binding on it or its assets;

(f) no consent, approval, authorization or filing with any governmental authority or other Person is required for its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty, other than those that have been obtained or made and remain in full force and effect;

(g) it directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding membership interests of Licensor;

(h) there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to its knowledge, threatened against it that, if adversely determined, would reasonably be expected to materially impair its ability to perform its obligations under this Guaranty;

(i) it is solvent, and the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty will not render it insolvent; and

(j) it has received and reviewed copies of the Agreement and the other Guaranty Documents existing as of the date of this Guaranty.

**7.2 Independent Investigation.** The Guarantor has made its own independent investigation of the financial condition and affairs of Licensor. It has not relied on any statement or information provided by the Beneficiary in entering into this Guaranty. The Guarantor shall keep itself informed of the financial condition of Licensor and of all other circumstances bearing on the risk of non-payment or non-performance of the Guaranteed Obligations. The Beneficiary has no duty to advise the Guarantor of any information known to it regarding those matters.

## 8. COVENANTS

**8.1 Financial Information.** For so long as any Guaranteed Obligation is outstanding or capable of arising, the Guarantor shall deliver to the Beneficiary the following financial statements:

(a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, its audited consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year, consisting of a balance sheet, income statement and statement of cash flows, accompanied by the report of its independent certified public accountants; and

(b) within sixty (60) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, its unaudited consolidated balance sheet and income statement for that quarter, certified by its chief financial officer as fairly presenting its financial condition in all material respects.

The Beneficiary shall treat financial statements delivered under this Section 8.1 as Confidential Information of Licensor under Section 8 of the Agreement, except to the extent they are publicly available.

**8.2 Notice of Certain Events.** The Guarantor shall notify the Beneficiary in writing promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days, after becoming aware of any of the following:

(a) any Bankruptcy Event in respect of the Guarantor or Licensor; or

(b) any event or circumstance that would reasonably be expected to materially impair the Guarantor's ability to perform its obligations under this Guaranty.

**8.3 Maintenance of Existence.** The Guarantor shall maintain its corporate existence and good standing. It shall not liquidate or dissolve, or sell, lease or otherwise transfer all or substantially all of its assets, unless the transferee or surviving Person assumes all obligations of the Guarantor under this Guaranty by written instrument delivered to the Beneficiary.

**8.4 Confirmations.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Beneficiary's written request, the Guarantor shall deliver to the Beneficiary a written confirmation, signed by an authorized officer, that this Guaranty remains in full force and effect and extends to the Guaranteed Obligations under the Guaranty Documents as then in effect.

## 9. PAYMENTS

**9.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments by the Guarantor under this Guaranty shall be made to the account designated by the Beneficiary in its demand or by subsequent written notice.

**9.2 Taxes.** All payments by the Guarantor under this Guaranty shall be made free and clear of, and without deduction for, any taxes, except as required by law. If any deduction is required by law, the Guarantor shall pay such additional amounts as are necessary so that the Beneficiary receives the full amount it would have received had no deduction been made. This Section 9.2 does not apply to taxes based on the Beneficiary's net income.

**9.3 Interest.** Any amount not paid by the Guarantor when due under this Guaranty bears interest at the rate set out in Section 6.4 of the Agreement, from the due date until paid in full, both before and after judgment.

**9.4 Application of Payments.** The Beneficiary may apply any payment received from the Guarantor or Licensor against the Guaranteed Obligations in such order as the Beneficiary determines, unless the payment is designated for a specific Guaranteed Obligation in accordance with the applicable Guaranty Document.

## 10. COSTS AND EXPENSES

The Guarantor shall reimburse the Beneficiary on demand for all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs, that the Beneficiary incurs in enforcing this Guaranty or in collecting any amount due under it. This obligation does not apply to the extent a court of competent jurisdiction finally determines that the Guarantor has no liability under this Guaranty for the amount claimed.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUCCESSORS

**11.1 Guarantor.** The Guarantor may not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Guaranty or any of its obligations under it without the prior written consent of the Beneficiary, except to a successor that assumes this Guaranty in accordance with Section 8.3. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 11.1 is void.

**11.2 Beneficiary.** The Beneficiary may assign or transfer its rights under this Guaranty, in whole or in part, to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Agreement in accordance with the Agreement, without the consent of the Guarantor. The Beneficiary shall give the Guarantor written notice of any such assignment.

**11.3 Binding Effect.** This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors and permitted assigns. It benefits the Beneficiary and its successors and permitted assigns.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**13.1 Governing Law.** This Guaranty and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**13.2 Jurisdiction.** Any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware. The Guarantor irrevocably submits to the personal jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum. The escalation procedure in Section 16.2 of the Agreement does not apply to a demand or proceeding under this Guaranty.

**13.3 Service of Process.** The Guarantor consents to service of process in any proceeding under Section 13.2 by delivery to its notice address under Section 12.1. This does not affect any other method of service permitted by law.

**13.4 Jury Waiver.** The Guarantor and the Beneficiary each knowingly and voluntarily waive any right to trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty.

## 14. GENERAL

**14.1 Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment of this Guaranty is effective unless in writing and signed by the Guarantor and the Beneficiary. No waiver by the Beneficiary is effective unless in writing and signed by the Beneficiary. No failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of it, and no single or partial exercise of any right precludes any further exercise of it or of any other right.

**14.2 Remedies Cumulative.** The rights and remedies of the Beneficiary under this Guaranty are cumulative and in addition to any rights and remedies available to it under the Guaranty Documents, at law or in equity.

**14.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

**14.4 Set-Off.** The Beneficiary may set off any amount due and unpaid by the Guarantor under this Guaranty against any amount the Beneficiary owes the Guarantor. The Beneficiary shall notify the Guarantor promptly after exercising this right.

**14.5 Judgment Currency.** If a judgment is given in a currency other than U.S. dollars, the Guarantor shall indemnify the Beneficiary against any shortfall between the amount due in U.S. dollars and the amount the Beneficiary receives on converting the judgment amount into U.S. dollars at the prevailing rate.

**14.6 Further Assurances.** The Guarantor shall execute such further documents and take such further actions as the Beneficiary reasonably requests to give effect to this Guaranty.

**14.7 Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic means or in portable document format are as effective as originals.

**14.8 Acceptance.** This Guaranty is effective upon its execution and delivery by the Guarantor. Acceptance by the Beneficiary is not required, and the Beneficiary's countersignature below is for acknowledgment only.
