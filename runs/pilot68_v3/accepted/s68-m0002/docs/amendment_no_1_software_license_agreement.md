# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

**WashLoop Plant Management, RFID Garment-Tracking and Route-Dispatch Software**

This Amendment No. 1 to Software License Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Voupourt Software, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2100 Harrow Court, Suite 400, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**"); and

**Tailosk Linen & Uniform Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 875 Millrace Avenue, Columbus, Ohio 43215 ("**Licensee**").

Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Licensor licenses the WashLoop software platform to Licensee for use in Licensee's laundry Plants and delivery routes, and provides related Implementation Services, Support Services and hosting.

B. Under Section 4.4 of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement, Licensor delivered the Production Release on April 6, 2026 for use at Licensee's existing Plants.

C. Licensee delivered to Licensor a Rejection Notice dated May 14, 2026 in respect of the Production Release, and the parties are in discussions regarding the matters described in it. Nothing in this Amendment waives, releases or otherwise affects Licensee's rights in respect of the Rejection Notice.

D. On or about April 30, 2026, Licensee, through its wholly owned subsidiary Tailosk Midwest Operations, LLC, completed the acquisition of the commercial laundry business of Brightwater Textile Care, LLC, including laundry processing facilities located in Dayton, Ohio and Louisville, Kentucky and the associated delivery routes and customer contracts (the "**Brightwater Acquisition**").

E. Licensee wishes to extend the use of the Software to the facilities, personnel and routes acquired in the Brightwater Acquisition, and to future facilities that come under common ownership with Licensee, and the parties wish to amend the definition of "Affiliate" and certain related provisions of the Agreement for that purpose and to make the other changes set out in this Amendment.

In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings in this Amendment:

"**Acquired Plants**" means the laundry processing facilities located at (a) 4410 Gateway Industrial Drive, Dayton, Ohio 45414 (the "**Dayton Plant**") and (b) 1925 Riverport Road, Louisville, Kentucky 40258 (the "**Louisville Plant**"), each acquired by Licensee's Affiliate in the Brightwater Acquisition.

"**Acquired Routes**" means the delivery routes serviced from the Acquired Plants as of the date of the Brightwater Acquisition, together with any successor routes established by Licensee or its Affiliates to serve the same customers.

"**Legacy System**" means the plant management and route software used at the Acquired Plants before the Brightwater Acquisition.

"**Rejection Notice**" has the meaning given in the Quality Agreement.

"**Transition Period**" means the period beginning on the Amendment Effective Date and ending on the date on which the last Acquired Plant goes live on the Software in accordance with Section 4.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Amendment.

**1.3 Section References.** Unless otherwise stated, references in this Amendment to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to the Agreement.

## 2. AMENDMENTS TO DEFINITIONS

**2.1 Affiliate.** The definition of "Affiliate" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following, which applies for all purposes of the Agreement:

> "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a party, (a) any person that, now or in the future, directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, and (b) any person that merges or consolidates with that party or its parent, acquires all or substantially all of the assets of that party or its parent, or acquires a majority of its voting securities. For purposes of this definition, "control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity by contract or otherwise, and "parent" means any person that controls that party.

**2.2 Authorized Users.** The definition of "Authorized Users" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "**Authorized Users**" means employees, agents and individual contractors of Licensee or its Affiliates, and, during the Transition Period, personnel of any provider of transition services to Licensee or its Affiliates in connection with an acquisition of a laundry business, in each case who are authorized by Licensee to access and use the Software in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.3 Plant.** The definition of "Plant" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "**Plant**" means a laundry processing facility owned, leased or operated by Licensee or its Affiliates and identified in an Order Form, added in accordance with Section 2.4, or identified as an Acquired Plant in Amendment No. 1 to this Agreement.

**2.4 Customer Data.** The definition of "Customer Data" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is amended by inserting, after the words "by or on behalf of Licensee or its Affiliates", the words "(including data migrated from any legacy system used at a Plant before it became a Plant)".

## 3. ACQUIRED PLANTS

**3.1 Use at Acquired Plants.** From the Amendment Effective Date, Licensee and its Affiliates may use the Software at the Acquired Plants and on the Acquired Routes in accordance with the Agreement. The Acquired Plants are added to Licensee's deployment under Section 2.4 of the Agreement, and the parties shall execute an Order Form covering the Acquired Plants, the related usage metrics and any additional Fees in accordance with Sections 2.3 and 2.4 of the Agreement.

**3.2 Usage Metrics During Transition.** During the Transition Period, use of the Software at the Acquired Plants for testing, training and data migration in non-production environments does not count toward the usage metrics stated in any Order Form. Production use at an Acquired Plant counts toward those usage metrics from its go-live date under Section 4.4.

**3.3 Transition Service Providers.** Licensee may permit personnel of Brightwater Textile Care, LLC providing transition services to Licensee's Affiliate under the Brightwater Acquisition documents to access the Software as Authorized Users during the Transition Period. Licensee is responsible for their acts and omissions in accordance with Section 2.2 of the Agreement and shall ensure that they are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Section 8 of the Agreement.

**3.4 Site Rules.** Section 4.3 of the Agreement applies to the presence of Licensor personnel at the Acquired Plants. Licensee shall provide Licensor with the site safety, sanitation, infection-control and security policies applicable to each Acquired Plant at least five (5) Business Days before Licensor's first on-site visit to that Acquired Plant.

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION AT ACQUIRED PLANTS

**4.1 Scope.** Licensor shall perform Implementation Services for the Acquired Plants consisting of (a) configuration of the plant management module for the wash aisle, finishing, soil sort and clean-side workflows at each Acquired Plant, (b) enablement of the RFID garment-tracking module, including integration with the RFID tunnel readers and handheld scanners specified in the Documentation, (c) configuration of the route-dispatch module and the mobile driver application for the Acquired Routes, (d) migration of garment, linen inventory, customer, route and billing records from the Legacy System, and (e) training of supervisors, plant administrators and route drivers at each Acquired Plant.

**4.2 Project Plan.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee for approval a supplement to the implementation project plan setting out the milestones, dependencies, resourcing and go-live dates for the Acquired Plants. Licensee shall approve the supplement or provide reasonable comments within ten (10) Business Days after receipt. Once approved, the supplement forms part of the implementation project plan agreed by the parties for purposes of the Agreement.

**4.3 Target Dates.** The parties' target go-live dates are September 14, 2026 for the Dayton Plant and November 2, 2026 for the Louisville Plant, subject to the approved project plan supplement and Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

**4.4 Go-Live.** Each Acquired Plant goes live on the date on which Licensee first uses the Software for production processing at that Acquired Plant following completion of the Implementation Services described in Section 4.1 for that Acquired Plant. Delivery, testing and acceptance of the configuration and releases of the Software for the Acquired Plants are governed by Section 4.4 of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement.

**4.5 Data Migration.** Licensee shall provide Licensor with extracts of the Legacy System data in the formats reasonably specified by Licensor. Licensor shall validate the migrated data against the extracts, provide Licensee with a reconciliation report identifying any records not migrated or migrated with errors, and correct any errors attributable to Licensor's migration process at no additional charge. Licensor shall not retain any copy of the Legacy System extracts after completion of the migration, except as Customer Data in the Hosted Environment.

**4.6 Change Requests.** Any change to the scope of the Implementation Services described in Section 4.1 is subject to Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

## 5. HEALTHCARE CUSTOMERS OF THE ACQUIRED PLANTS

**5.1 Data-Protection Terms.** The parties acknowledge that the customers served from the Acquired Plants include hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers and long-term care facilities. Within thirty (30) days after Licensee's written request, Licensor shall enter into such business associate or data-protection agreements as Licensee reasonably requests under Section 7.7 of the Agreement in respect of the Customer Data relating to those customers.

**5.2 Notification of Subprocessors.** Licensor shall provide Licensee, within ten (10) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, a current list of the subprocessors that will host or process Customer Data relating to the Acquired Plants. Section 7.9 of the Agreement applies to any new subprocessor engaged for that purpose.

## 6. SUPPORT SERVICES

**6.1 Support for Acquired Plants.** From the go-live date of each Acquired Plant, the Support Services described in Exhibit A apply to that Acquired Plant on the same basis as to Licensee's other Plants.

**6.2 Hypercare.** For thirty (30) days after the go-live date of each Acquired Plant, Licensor shall provide a dedicated support engineer, available on site or remotely during the Acquired Plant's production shifts, to triage and resolve issues arising from the go-live at that Acquired Plant, at no additional charge.

**6.3 Exhibit A.** Section A.1 of Exhibit A is amended by inserting at the end the following sentence: "During the Transition Period described in Amendment No. 1, Licensor shall provide Licensee's Acquired Plants with a separate queue in the web-based ticketing portal so that issues arising from implementation at those Acquired Plants can be tracked and reported separately."

**6.4 Reporting.** Section A.7 of Exhibit A is amended by inserting at the end the words ", reported separately for each Plant on request".
