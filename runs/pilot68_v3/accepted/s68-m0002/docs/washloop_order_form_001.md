## 4. LICENSED SOFTWARE MODULES

**4.1 Modules.** The Software licensed under this Order Form consists of the following modules, together with all Updates and the Documentation:

(a) **Plant Management.** Soil-receiving and soil-sort workflow; wash aisle and tunnel-washer batch tracking; dryer and finishing-line tracking; clean-side assembly and cart build; production throughput and pounds-per-operator-hour reporting; chemical and utility consumption logging; and plant dashboards.

(b) **RFID Garment Tracking.** RFID tag registry and garment and linen item master; tunnel-reader and handheld-scanner read processing; soil-in and clean-out counts by customer and location; par-level management; loss, rewash and reject tracking; garment life-cycle and wash-count history; and customer-facing inventory reports.

(c) **Route Dispatch.** Route and stop configuration; route planning and load sequencing; daily dispatch board; delivery and pickup confirmation; driver performance and exception reporting; and integration with Licensee's billing system for delivered quantities.

(d) **WashLoop Driver Mobile Application.** The mobile driver application for iOS and Android devices, including electronic delivery tickets, signature capture, handheld RFID scanning at customer locations, offline route operation and photo capture of delivery exceptions.

**4.2 Integrations.** The Software licensed under this Order Form includes the standard WashLoop connectors for the following of Licensee's systems, as described in Schedule 2:

(a) Licensee's enterprise resource planning and billing system;

(b) Licensee's RFID tunnel readers and handheld scanners at the Initial Plants; and

(c) Licensee's vehicle telematics provider.

**4.3 Functional Specifications.** The functional specifications of the Software for purposes of Section 11.2 of the Agreement are the Documentation and the functional requirements set out in Schedule 3.

## 5. USAGE METRICS

**5.1 Licensed Quantities.** The licensed quantities under this Order Form are the quantities stated in Section 3.2.

**5.2 Measurement.** Usage is measured as follows:

(a) **Plants** are counted as each Plant at which the Software is used for production processing.

(b) **Active RFID tags** are counted as tags that have registered at least one read in the Software during the preceding ninety (90) days. Tags that have been retired, scrapped or reported lost in the Software are not counted.

(c) **Route vehicles** are counted as vehicles assigned to at least one active route in the Route Dispatch module during the relevant calendar month.

(d) **Named Authorized Users** are counted as individuals holding active login credentials to the Software, other than drivers using only the WashLoop Driver mobile application. Licensee may reassign a named user credential from one individual to another at any time.

**5.3 Usage Reports.** Licensor shall make a usage report available to Licensee through the Software's administrative console. The report shows current quantities for each usage metric. Licensor shall notify Licensee under Section 2.3 of the Agreement of any excess no more than once per calendar quarter. Excess usage is determined by the average quantity over the preceding calendar quarter.

**5.4 Seasonal Variation.** A temporary increase in active RFID tags or route vehicles that does not exceed the licensed quantities by more than ten percent (10%) is not excess usage. This applies only where the increase lasts for no more than sixty (60) consecutive days, including increases arising from seasonal hospitality volumes or healthcare surge demand.

## SCHEDULE 3 – FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

**S3.1 Plant Management.** The Plant Management module shall perform the following functions:

(a) record soil weights by customer at receiving and sorting;

(b) track each wash batch through the tunnel washers and conventional washers, including formula, weight and cycle time;

(c) track items through drying and finishing lines;

(d) support cart build by customer and delivery location; and

(e) report production throughput, pounds per operator hour and rewash rates by Plant, shift and line.

**S3.2 RFID Garment Tracking.** The RFID Garment Tracking module shall perform the following functions:

(a) process tunnel-reader reads at the throughput rates specified in the Documentation for each Initial Plant's installed reader models;

(b) reconcile soil-in and clean-out counts by customer and location;

(c) maintain garment life-cycle and wash-count history for each tagged item;

(d) flag items exceeding a configurable wash-count threshold for inspection or retirement; and

(e) generate customer inventory and loss reports by customer, department and item type.

**S3.3 Route Dispatch.** The Route Dispatch module shall perform the following functions:

(a) support at least the number of route vehicles licensed under Section 3.2, with routes of up to sixty (60) stops each;

(b) generate daily load sequences by route from clean-side cart build data;

(c) display real-time route progress on the dispatch board using telematics data; and

(d) transmit confirmed delivered quantities to Licensee's billing system no later than the end of each route day.

**S3.4 WashLoop Driver Mobile Application.** The WashLoop Driver mobile application shall perform the following functions:

(a) operate offline for a full route day and synchronize on reconnection;

(b) capture customer signatures and delivery exceptions;

(c) support handheld RFID scanning at customer locations for supported scanner models; and

(d) meet the device security requirements in Section B.9 of Exhibit B to the Agreement.

**S3.5 Reporting and Data Export.** The Software shall allow Licensee's administrators to schedule and export standard reports and raw transaction data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
