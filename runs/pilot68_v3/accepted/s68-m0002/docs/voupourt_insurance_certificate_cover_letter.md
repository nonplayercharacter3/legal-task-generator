## 2. SUMMARY OF REQUIRED COVERAGE

**2.1 Requirements of Section 13.1.** Section 13.1 of the Agreement requires Licensor to maintain the following insurance during the Subscription Term and for two (2) years thereafter, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) technology errors and omissions insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per claim and in the aggregate;

(c) cyber liability and network security insurance, including coverage for privacy breach response costs and regulatory defense, with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per claim and in the aggregate; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000.

## 5. CLAIMS-MADE POLICIES AND CONTINUITY

**5.1 Claims-Made Basis.** The technology errors and omissions and cyber liability Policies are written on a claims-made and reported basis. Under that form, a claim is covered if it is first made against Licensor and reported to the insurer during the policy period or any applicable extended reporting period. The wrongful act must also have occurred on or after the retroactive date.

**5.2 Retroactive Date.** The retroactive date of March 1, 2019 is earlier than the Effective Date. Services performed for Licensee from the Effective Date onward, including the pre-subscription provisioning of non-production environments under Section 2.3 of Order Form No. 001, fall within the retroactive period.

**5.3 Continuity.** Section 13.1 of the Agreement requires the Required Coverage to be maintained during the Subscription Term and for two (2) years thereafter. Licensor intends to renew the claims-made Policies annually and to keep the retroactive date at March 1, 2019 or earlier on each renewal or replacement. If Licensor does not renew a claims-made Policy, or replaces it with a Policy that does not keep the retroactive date, Licensor will buy an extended reporting period. That period will last at least until the end of the two-year post-term period required by Section 13.1.

**5.4 Occurrence Policies.** The commercial general liability and umbrella Policies are written on an occurrence basis. They respond to bodily injury and property damage that occurs during the policy period, regardless of when the claim is made.

## 7. NOTICE OF CANCELLATION AND MATERIAL CHANGE

**7.1 Contractual Requirement.** Section 13.2 of the Agreement requires Licensor to give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation or material reduction of any Required Coverage.

**7.2 Insurer Notice.** Standard certificates of insurance do not, by themselves, require the insurer to notify the certificate holder. Licensor has therefore had each Policy endorsed to require the insurer to give Licensee, as certificate holder, at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice of cancellation. For cancellation for non-payment of premium, the insurer notice period is ten (10) days.

**7.3 Licensor Notice.** The insurer notices described in Section 7.2 are in addition to Licensor's own obligation under Section 13.2 of the Agreement, and they do not replace it. Licensor will give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' notice of any cancellation or material reduction of the Required Coverage, in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement. This includes any change that would reduce a limit below the Section 13.1 minimum, narrow the privacy breach response or regulatory defense coverage, or move the retroactive date of a claims-made Policy.

**7.4 Non-Payment.** Licensor's premiums for the current policy year have been paid in full, as confirmed by the Broker. Licensor does not finance its premiums through a premium finance arrangement that would allow a third party to cancel a Policy.

## 9. SUBCONTRACTORS AND SUBPROCESSORS

**9.1 Subcontractors.** Section 15.1 of the Agreement allows Licensor to subcontract Implementation Services and Support Services, with Licensor remaining responsible for its subcontractors' performance. Licensor requires each subcontractor that works on site at a Licensee Plant to maintain commercial general liability, automobile liability and workers' compensation insurance on terms appropriate to its work. Licensor collects certificates from those subcontractors before they begin work. On request, Licensor will provide copies of subcontractor certificates for any subcontractor working at a Licensee Plant.

**9.2 Subprocessors.** The hosting provider for the Hosted Environment maintains its own technology errors and omissions and cyber liability insurance. Licensor reviews that provider's insurance annually as part of its vendor management program. Licensor's own cyber liability Policy responds to claims arising from subprocessor events for which Licensor is legally responsible, as described in Section 3.3. This is consistent with Section 7.9(d) of the Agreement.
