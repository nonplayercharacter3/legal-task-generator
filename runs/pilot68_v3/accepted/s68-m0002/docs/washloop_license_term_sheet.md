# WASHLOOP SOFTWARE LICENSE: SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

**Proposed License of the WashLoop Plant Management, RFID Garment-Tracking and Route-Dispatch Software**

**Between Voupourt Software, LLC and Tailosk Linen & Uniform Services, Inc.**

**Date of this Term Sheet:** December 12, 2025

**Status:** Non-binding indicative terms for discussion, except Sections 14 through 18

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND STATUS

**1.1 Purpose.** This Summary of Principal Terms (this "**Term Sheet**") sets out the principal terms on which Voupourt Software, LLC proposes to license its WashLoop software platform to Tailosk Linen & Uniform Services, Inc. It also covers the related implementation, support and hosting services. The parties intend to use this Term Sheet as the basis for negotiating the following definitive documents (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**"):

(a) a software license agreement (the "**License Agreement**");

(b) an initial order form under the License Agreement (the "**Initial Order Form**");

(c) a quality agreement governing testing, delivery and acceptance of software releases (the "**Quality Agreement**");

(d) a parent guaranty from Voupourt Holdings, Inc. (the "**Parent Guaranty**"); and

(e) such other ancillary documents as the parties agree are needed.

**1.2 Non-Binding Nature.** This Term Sheet is an expression of the parties' current intentions only. Except for Sections 14 through 18, it does not create any legally binding obligation on either party. No binding agreement regarding the proposed transaction will arise unless and until the Definitive Agreements have been negotiated, approved and signed by both parties. Either party may end negotiations at any time and for any reason, subject only to Sections 14 through 18.

**1.3 Approvals.** Tailosk's entry into the Definitive Agreements is subject to approval by its executive leadership team and, for capital commitments above its delegated authority limits, by its board of directors. Voupourt's entry into the Definitive Agreements is subject to approval by its management and by Voupourt Holdings, Inc. as its parent.

## 3. BACKGROUND

**3.1 Licensee's Operations.** Tailosk operates commercial laundry and linen-rental plants. It serves hospitals, healthcare systems, hotels and other institutional customers in Ohio and Indiana. Tailosk currently runs its plant operations, garment tracking and route dispatch on a combination of legacy systems and manual processes. It wants to consolidate those functions on a single platform.

**3.2 Licensor's Platform.** Voupourt develops and licenses the WashLoop platform for commercial laundry operators. WashLoop provides plant management, RFID garment and linen tracking, soil-to-clean workflow control, and route planning and dispatch. It also includes a mobile driver application.

**3.3 Selection Process.** Tailosk issued a request for proposals in August 2025. Voupourt submitted a proposal on September 19, 2025 and gave product demonstrations at Tailosk's Columbus plant on October 22 and 23, 2025. Tailosk has selected Voupourt as its preferred vendor, subject to agreement of the Definitive Agreements.

## 4. SOFTWARE AND SCOPE

**4.1 Licensed Modules.** The license will cover the following WashLoop modules, together with all updates and the related documentation:

| Module | Principal Functions |
|---|---|
| Plant Management | Soil receiving and sort; wash aisle and tunnel-washer batch tracking; drying and finishing-line tracking; clean-side assembly and cart build; throughput and pounds-per-operator-hour reporting |
| RFID Garment Tracking | Tag registry and item master; tunnel-reader and handheld-scanner read processing; soil-in and clean-out counts; par-level management; loss, rewash and reject tracking; garment life-cycle history |
| Route Dispatch | Route and stop configuration; route planning and load sequencing; dispatch board; delivery and pickup confirmation; billing integration |
| WashLoop Driver mobile application | Electronic delivery tickets; signature capture; handheld RFID scanning at customer sites; offline route operation; exception photographs |

**4.2 Deployment Model.** The core components will be hosted by Voupourt in a hosted environment located in the United States. Client-side and mobile components will run on devices owned or controlled by Tailosk.

**4.3 Initial Plants.** The initial deployment will cover three plants:

(a) the Columbus, Ohio plant;

(b) the Cincinnati, Ohio plant; and

(c) the Indianapolis, Indiana plant.

**4.4 Affiliates and Additional Plants.** Tailosk and its affiliates may use the software. Affiliates will be defined by reference to control in the customary way. Tailosk may add plants during the term at the unit rates in the Initial Order Form, with any additional implementation work quoted separately.

**4.5 Integrations.** The initial scope will include Voupourt's standard connectors for the following:

(a) Tailosk's enterprise resource planning and billing system;

(b) the RFID tunnel readers and handheld scanners at the initial plants; and

(c) Tailosk's vehicle telematics provider.

## 5. TERM AND RENEWAL

**5.1 Initial Term.** The initial subscription term will be thirty-six (36) months beginning February 1, 2026. The License Agreement will be signed in January 2026 to allow non-production environments to be provisioned and implementation work to begin before the start of the subscription term.

**5.2 Renewal.** At the end of the initial term, the license will renew automatically for successive 12-month renewal terms. Either party may prevent renewal by giving the other party written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the initial term or the then-current renewal term.

**5.3 Pricing on Renewal.** Subscription fees for any renewal term will be subject to a cap on increases, to be agreed in the Definitive Agreements. Voupourt has indicated that it expects any increase on renewal to be not more than the change in the U.S. Consumer Price Index over the preceding twelve months.

**5.4 Termination.** The License Agreement will contain customary termination rights for each party, including the following:

(a) termination for uncured material breach, after a thirty (30) day cure period;

(b) termination for the insolvency of the other party; and

(c) a right for Tailosk to terminate for chronic failure to meet the availability service level.

**5.5 Transition Assistance.** On expiry or termination, Voupourt will continue to provide access to the software and support for a transition period of up to six (6) months at Tailosk's request. Voupourt will also provide reasonable assistance in moving Tailosk's data and operations to a replacement system.

**6.2 Fixed Pricing.** The unit rates and the annual subscription fee will be fixed for the initial term.

**6.3 Implementation Fees.** Implementation services for the initial plants will be provided for a fixed fee of $186,500. Payment will be tied to milestones, which are expected to be as follows:

| Milestone | Percentage |
|---|---|

| Acceptance of the production release under the Quality Agreement | 40% |

**6.4 Invoicing and Payment.** Subscription fees will be invoiced annually in advance. Invoices will be payable within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct and undisputed invoice. Tailosk may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith while paying all undisputed amounts.

**6.5 Additional Quantities.** Additional plants, RFID tag blocks, route vehicles and named users will be charged at the unit rates in Section 6.1, prorated for the remainder of the then-current contract year. The Initial Order Form will include a seasonal tolerance for temporary increases in RFID tag counts and route vehicles.

**6.6 Professional Services.** Work outside the implementation scope will be charged at hourly rates set out in the Initial Order Form. Voupourt has indicated rates ranging from $160 per hour for training to $240 per hour for solutions architecture.

**6.7 Expenses.** Tailosk will reimburse reasonable, pre-approved travel expenses for on-site implementation work in accordance with its travel and expense policy.

## 7. IMPLEMENTATION

**7.1 Approach.** Voupourt will lead implementation through the following stages:

(a) discovery workshops at each initial plant;

(b) a solution design document for Tailosk's approval;

(c) configuration of each module;

(d) integration build and testing;

(e) data migration from Tailosk's existing systems;

(f) role-based training; and

(g) go-live support at each plant.

**7.2 Timeline.** The parties are targeting delivery of the production release in the second quarter of 2026. Go-live at the initial plants is expected to be phased over the following months. A detailed implementation project plan will be agreed after signing.

**7.3 Personnel.** Voupourt will assign a dedicated implementation manager. It will not replace that individual before go-live at the last initial plant except for reasons beyond its reasonable control.

**7.4 Testing and Acceptance.** Testing, delivery and acceptance of the production release will be governed by the Quality Agreement. The Quality Agreement will set out Voupourt's pre-delivery testing obligations, the acceptance criteria, the acceptance testing period and Tailosk's remedies if the production release fails acceptance. Those remedies are expected to include correction and redelivery or a refund of implementation fees. The acceptance criteria will be measured against the documentation and functional requirements to be included in the Initial Order Form.

**7.5 Post-Go-Live Support.** Voupourt will provide a dedicated support engineer for thirty (30) days after go-live at each plant, at no additional charge.

## 8. SUPPORT, HOSTING AND SERVICE LEVELS

**8.1 Support.** Severity 1 and Severity 2 issues will be supported 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, reflecting Tailosk's continuous plant and overnight route operations. Lower-severity issues will be supported during extended business hours.

**8.2 Availability.** Voupourt will commit to monthly availability of at least 99.9% for the hosted components, excluding scheduled maintenance in agreed windows.

**8.3 Service Credits.** Service credits will apply on a sliding scale where availability falls below the committed level. Tailosk will have a termination right for repeated failures within a rolling period.

**8.4 Response Targets.** Indicative response targets are thirty (30) minutes for Severity 1 issues and two (2) hours for Severity 2 issues. Resolution or workaround targets are eight (8) hours and two (2) business days respectively.

**8.5 Updates.** All updates will be included in the subscription fees. Voupourt will not materially reduce the functionality of the licensed modules during the term.

**8.6 Environments.** Voupourt will provide one production environment and two non-production environments, one for testing and one for training.

## 9. CUSTOMER DATA AND SECURITY

**9.1 Ownership.** As between the parties, Tailosk will own all data entered into or generated through the software. This includes data relating to its hospital and healthcare customers. Voupourt will use that data only to provide the services to Tailosk.

**9.2 Security.** Voupourt will maintain a written information security program consistent with recognized industry standards. That program will include the following:

(a) encryption in transit and at rest;

(b) multi-factor authentication for administrative access;

(c) annual independent penetration testing; and

(d) a current SOC 2 Type II report.

**9.3 Incident Notification.** Voupourt will notify Tailosk of any security incident affecting Tailosk's data within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it.

**9.4 Healthcare Customer Requirements.** Voupourt will enter into business associate or data-protection agreements reasonably required by Tailosk's healthcare customers. The parties will discuss whether to document data-handling responsibilities relating to healthcare customers in a separate letter.

**9.5 Subprocessors.** Voupourt may use subprocessors on notice. It will remain responsible for their acts and omissions.

**9.6 Return and Deletion.** On expiry or termination, Voupourt will make Tailosk's data available for export in a machine-readable format. It will then delete the data and certify the deletion.

## 10. WARRANTIES AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**10.1 Software Warranty.** Voupourt will warrant that the software will perform materially in accordance with its documentation and the agreed functional requirements throughout the subscription term. Tailosk's remedy will be correction, with a termination and refund right if a material non-conformity is not corrected within an agreed period.

**10.2 Services Warranty.** Voupourt will warrant that implementation and support services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

**10.3 Intellectual Property Indemnity.** Voupourt will defend and indemnify Tailosk and its affiliates against third-party claims that the software infringes intellectual property rights. Customary exclusions and mitigation options will apply.

**10.4 Ownership of Software.** Voupourt and its licensors will retain all rights in the software.

## 11. LIABILITY

**11.1 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.2 Exclusions from the Cap.** Customary exclusions from the cap and from the exclusion of indirect damages will be agreed in the Definitive Agreements.

**11.3 Indirect Damages.** Neither party will be liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, subject to the exclusions to be agreed.

## 12. INSURANCE AND PARENT GUARANTY

**12.1 Insurance.** Voupourt will maintain commercial general liability, technology errors and omissions, cyber liability, and workers' compensation and employer's liability insurance. Limits will be agreed in the License Agreement. Voupourt has indicated limits of $5,000,000 for its technology errors and omissions and cyber liability policies.

**12.2 Parent Guaranty.** Voupourt Holdings, Inc. will guarantee Voupourt's payment and performance obligations under the Definitive Agreements. Delivery of the Parent Guaranty will be a condition to the effectiveness of the Initial Order Form.

## 13. OTHER PRINCIPAL TERMS

**13.1 Assignment.** Neither party may assign the License Agreement without the other party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except to an affiliate on notice.

**13.2 Confidentiality.** The License Agreement will include customary mutual confidentiality obligations. These will continue for five (5) years after the term, and indefinitely for trade secrets and customer data.

**13.3 Governing Law.** The Definitive Agreements will be governed by Delaware law.

## 15. EXCLUSIVITY

**15.1 Exclusivity Period.** From the date of this Term Sheet until January 31, 2026, Tailosk will not enter into a definitive agreement with any other vendor for plant management, RFID garment-tracking or route-dispatch software for the initial plants.

**15.2 Ordinary Course.** Section 15.1 does not restrict Tailosk from continuing to operate, maintain and support its existing systems in the ordinary course.

## 16. COSTS

**17.3 Data.** No Tailosk customer data will be provided to Voupourt before signing, other than anonymized or synthetic samples that Tailosk chooses to provide.

## ANNEX A: OPEN POINTS FOR THE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENTS

The parties have identified the following points to be addressed in negotiating the Definitive Agreements. This list is not exhaustive.

1. **Functional requirements.** Detailed functional requirements for each module will be set out as a schedule to the Initial Order Form. These will include RFID read-accuracy targets and route-dispatch capacity.

2. **Acceptance criteria.** Acceptance criteria and test scripts for the production release will be set out in the Quality Agreement.

3. **Update testing.** Advance availability of updates in the test environment will be agreed, together with Tailosk's ability to request deferral of a release that fails testing.

4. **Data inventory.** Voupourt will provide an inventory of data fields used to capture healthcare-customer and personnel data.

5. **Recovery objectives.** Business continuity and disaster recovery objectives for the hosted environment will be agreed. Voupourt has proposed a four (4) hour recovery time objective and a one (1) hour recovery point objective.

6. **Insurance.** Insurance limits and the form of the certificates of insurance will be agreed.

7. **Parent Guaranty.** The form of the Parent Guaranty will be agreed, including financial reporting by the Guarantor.

## ANNEX B: ASSUMPTIONS UNDERLYING PRICING

1. **Initial quantities.** Pricing assumes three initial plants, 1,200,000 active RFID tags, 120 route vehicles and 150 named users.

2. **Tag counting.** Active RFID tags are those that register at least one read in a rolling ninety (90) day period. Retired, scrapped and lost tags are not counted.

3. **Drivers.** Drivers who use only the mobile application are not counted as named users.

4. **Equipment.** Tailosk will provide and maintain the RFID readers, handheld scanners, vehicle devices and network connectivity at the initial plants that are specified in Voupourt's documentation.

5. **Data migration.** Migration covers garment and linen inventory, the RFID tag registry, customer master records, route and stop records, and twenty-four (24) months of billing history.

6. **Taxes.** All amounts in this Term Sheet are exclusive of applicable sales and use taxes.
