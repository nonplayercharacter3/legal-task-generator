# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Exhibit B to the Master Services Agreement**

This Service Level Schedule (this "**SLA Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). It forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SLA Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this SLA Schedule:

"**At-Risk Amount**" means, for any Measurement Period, twelve percent (12%) of the Monthly Fees for the calendar month in which that Measurement Period falls.

"**Chronic Service Failure**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Critical Service Level**" means each Service Level designated as "Critical" in the table in Section 2.2.

"**Cutoff Time**" means 2:00 p.m. local time at the Facility that fulfills the relevant Order, or such other time as the applicable Statement of Work specifies for a Facility or an Order type.

"**Key Performance Indicator**" or "**KPI**" means each Service Level designated as "KPI" in the table in Section 2.2.

"**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month. During Peak Season, it means each Measurement Week.

"**Measurement Week**" means each period of seven (7) consecutive days beginning at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on a Monday. If a Peak Season begins or ends on a day other than a Monday, the first or last Measurement Week of that Peak Season consists of the days of that week that fall within the Peak Season.

"**Monthly Fees**" means the total Fees invoiced under the applicable Statement of Work for a calendar month, before the application of any service credits and excluding taxes, pass-through carrier charges and packaging materials billed at cost.

"**Peak Season**" means, in each calendar year, the period from November 20 through December 31, inclusive.

"**Service Level Default**" means a failure by Supplier to meet a Service Level in a Measurement Period.

"**Service Level Credit**" means a service credit calculated under Section 3.

"**Weighting Factor**" means, for each Critical Service Level, the percentage set out against it in the table in Section 2.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this SLA Schedule as if set out in full. References to Sections in this SLA Schedule are to sections of this SLA Schedule unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICE LEVELS

**2.1 General.** Supplier shall perform the Services so as to meet or exceed each Service Level in each Measurement Period, at each Facility, from the date on which live fulfillment begins at that Facility under Section 3.7 of the Agreement.

**2.2 Service Level Table.** The Service Levels are as follows:

| No. | Service Level | Category | Target | Weighting Factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SL-1 | Order Ship Timeliness | Critical | 99.5% | 30% |
| SL-2 | Order Accuracy | Critical | 99.8% | 25% |
| SL-3 | Inventory Accuracy | Critical | 99.5% | 15% |
| SL-4 | Dock-to-Stock | Critical | 98.0% | 10% |
| SL-5 | Returns Processing Time | Critical | 98.0% | 15% |
| SL-6 | WMS Availability | Critical | 99.5% | 5% |
| KPI-1 | Receiving Discrepancy Notification | KPI | 98.0% | – |
| KPI-2 | Damage-Free Shipment Rate | KPI | 99.9% | – |
| KPI-3 | Carrier Handoff Scan Compliance | KPI | 99.5% | – |
| KPI-4 | Reporting Timeliness | KPI | 100% | – |

**2.3 Service Level Descriptions.**

(a) **SL-1 Order Ship Timeliness.** The percentage of Orders received in the WMS before the Cutoff Time on a Business Day that are picked, packed, labeled and handed off to the designated carrier on the same Business Day. Orders received after the Cutoff Time, or on a day other than a Business Day, must be shipped by the end of the next Business Day. Orders for which the applicable Statement of Work specifies a different ship commitment, such as pre-orders or scheduled releases, are measured against that commitment.

(b) **SL-2 Order Accuracy.** The percentage of Orders shipped in the Measurement Period that contain the correct items, sizes, colors and quantities, with the correct inserts and packaging, and that are shipped to the correct address with the correct carrier service level. An Order is inaccurate if any line item is incorrect. Inaccuracies are identified through Supplier's quality control audits, end customer complaints validated by Customer, and returns coded as "wrong item received."

(c) **SL-3 Inventory Accuracy.** The percentage of SKU locations counted in cycle counts during the Measurement Period whose physical count matches the quantity recorded in the WMS. For Measurement Periods in which an annual physical inventory is performed under Section 3.2(e) of the Agreement, the result of that physical inventory is used.

(d) **SL-4 Dock-to-Stock.** The percentage of inbound units received at a Facility that are unloaded, reconciled against the advance shipping notice, inspected as required and put away in a pickable location in the WMS within forty-eight (48) hours after the carrier's arrival at the dock. For inbound shipments of more than ten (10) full truckloads in a single day, the measurement window is seventy-two (72) hours.

(e) **SL-5 Returns Processing Time.** The percentage of returned units received at a Facility that are received, inspected, graded and dispositioned in the WMS, with the disposition reported to Customer, within three (3) Business Days after receipt. Units that Customer's returns disposition rules require to be refurbished are measured to the completion of grading and disposition, not to the completion of refurbishment.

(f) **SL-6 WMS Availability.** The percentage of minutes in the Measurement Period during which the WMS and its integrations with Customer's order management system and with the carrier platforms are available and functioning. Scheduled maintenance performed between 12:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m. Eastern Time on a Sunday, on not less than five (5) Business Days' prior notice to Customer, is excluded. No scheduled maintenance may be performed during Peak Season without Customer's prior written approval.

(g) **KPI-1 Receiving Discrepancy Notification.** The percentage of inbound shipments for which Supplier reports shortages, overages and damage to Customer within the time set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

(h) **KPI-2 Damage-Free Shipment Rate.** The percentage of Orders shipped in the Measurement Period that are not the subject of a validated claim of damage caused by improper picking, packing or handling at the Facility.

(i) **KPI-3 Carrier Handoff Scan Compliance.** The percentage of shipped parcels that receive a carrier acceptance scan on the ship date recorded in the WMS.

(j) **KPI-4 Reporting Timeliness.** The percentage of reports required by Section 6.2 of the Agreement and by Section 6 of this SLA Schedule that are delivered on time and complete.

**2.4 Facility-Level Measurement.** Each Service Level is measured separately for each Facility, unless the applicable Statement of Work provides that a Service Level is measured on an aggregate basis across Facilities.

## 3. SERVICE LEVEL CREDITS

**3.1 Calculation.** For each Service Level Default in respect of a Critical Service Level, Supplier shall credit Customer an amount equal to:

Service Level Credit = Weighting Factor × At-Risk Amount × Measurement Period Fraction

where the "**Measurement Period Fraction**" is one (1) for a Measurement Period of a calendar month, and, for a Measurement Week, the number of days in that Measurement Week divided by the number of days in the calendar month in which it falls. If a Measurement Week spans two calendar months, the days in each month are allocated to that month.

**3.2 Severity Adjustment.** If Supplier's actual performance against a Critical Service Level in a Measurement Period falls more than two (2) percentage points below the Target, the Service Level Credit for that Service Level Default is multiplied by one and one-half (1.5).

**3.3 Peak Season Credits.** For each Service Level Default in respect of a Critical Service Level that occurs in a Measurement Week during Peak Season, the Service Level Credit calculated under Sections 3.1 and 3.2 is multiplied by three (3).

**3.4 Repeat Defaults.** If Supplier commits a Service Level Default in respect of the same Critical Service Level in two (2) consecutive calendar months outside Peak Season, the Service Level Credit for the second and each subsequent consecutive Service Level Default in respect of that Critical Service Level is increased by fifty percent (50%), until Supplier meets that Critical Service Level in a full calendar month.

**3.5 Credit Limit.** The total Service Level Credits for any calendar month shall not exceed the At-Risk Amount for that month. For any calendar month that includes one or more days of Peak Season, the total Service Level Credits for that month shall not exceed three (3) times the At-Risk Amount.

**3.6 Key Performance Indicators.** No Service Level Credits apply to a failure to meet a KPI. If Supplier fails to meet the same KPI in two (2) consecutive Measurement Periods, Supplier shall perform the root cause analysis and implement the corrective action plan described in Section 6.4 of the Agreement as if that failure were a Service Level Default.

**3.7 Application of Credits.** Supplier shall calculate the Service Level Credits for each calendar month and shall show them in the monthly performance report under Section 6.2 of the Agreement. Supplier shall apply them against the next invoice for Fees in accordance with Sections 6.3 and 7.2 of the Agreement. Any Service Level Credits not applied at the expiration or termination of the applicable Statement of Work shall be paid to Customer within thirty (30) days after that expiration or termination.

**3.8 Multiple Defaults.** If a single event causes Service Level Defaults in respect of more than one Critical Service Level in the same Measurement Period, a Service Level Credit applies to each such Service Level Default, subject to Section 3.5.

**3.9 Earnback.** Supplier is not entitled to any earnback, offset or reduction of Service Level Credits on account of performance above the Target in any other Measurement Period.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS AND RELIEF

**4.1 Excused Failures.** A Service Level Default is excused, and no Service Level Credit applies to it, to the extent the failure is directly caused by:

(a) Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under Section 3.6 of the Agreement, where Supplier has complied with the notice and mitigation requirements of that Section;

(b) a Force Majeure Event, where Supplier has complied with Section 18.2 of the Agreement, including the implementation of its business continuity and disaster recovery plan;

(c) the failure of a carrier selected by Customer to collect parcels at the scheduled pickup time, where Supplier has staged the parcels for pickup and promptly notified Customer of the missed pickup; or

(d) an outage of Customer's order management system or other Customer systems that prevents the transmission of Orders to the WMS.

**4.2 Volume Variance.** If the actual Order volume or inbound unit volume at a Facility in a Measurement Period exceeds the volume forecast by Customer under Section 3.5 of the Agreement for that period by more than twenty-five percent (25%), Supplier is relieved of Service Level Credits for SL-1 and SL-4 in respect of the excess volume only. Supplier shall nonetheless use commercially reasonable efforts to process the excess volume within the Service Level Targets and shall continue to meet all other Service Levels. Volume within the forecast plus twenty-five percent (25%) shall be measured in the ordinary course.

**4.3 Claim for Relief.** To claim relief under Section 4.1 or 4.2, Supplier shall notify Customer in writing within three (3) Business Days after becoming aware of the circumstances giving rise to the claim. The notice shall describe the circumstances, the Service Levels affected and the steps Supplier is taking to mitigate the effects. Supplier bears the burden of demonstrating that the claimed relief applies. Supplier shall identify each claim for relief in the monthly performance report for the month concerned.

**4.4 No Other Relief.** Except as set out in this Section 4, Supplier is not relieved of any Service Level by reason of staffing shortages, equipment failure, Subcontractor performance, the performance of temporary staffing agencies engaged under Section 4.4 of the Agreement, or the volume of business that Supplier handles for its other customers.

## 5. CHRONIC SERVICE FAILURE

**5.1 Definition.** A "**Chronic Service Failure**" occurs if, during any Peak Season:

(a) Supplier commits a Service Level Default in respect of the same Critical Service Level at the same Facility in any two (2) consecutive Measurement Weeks;

(b) Supplier commits Service Level Defaults in respect of any Critical Service Levels at the same Facility in any three (3) Measurement Weeks, whether or not consecutive and whether or not in respect of the same Critical Service Level; or

(c) Supplier's actual performance against SL-1 (Order Ship Timeliness) or SL-2 (Order Accuracy) at any Facility falls below ninety-five percent (95%) in any single Measurement Week.

**5.2 Termination Right.** Upon the occurrence of a Chronic Service Failure, Customer may, in accordance with Section 13.4 of the Agreement, terminate the Agreement or the affected Statement of Work by written notice to Supplier. Customer may give that notice at any time within ninety (90) days after the later of (a) the end of the Measurement Week in which the Chronic Service Failure occurs and (b) Customer's receipt of the performance report showing it. The termination takes effect on the date stated in the notice, which may not be earlier than the date of the notice.

**5.3 Partial Termination.** Instead of terminating the Agreement or the affected Statement of Work, Customer may, by written notice to Supplier, remove from the scope of the affected Statement of Work the Services performed at the Facility at which the Chronic Service Failure occurred. The Fees for the removed Services shall cease to accrue from the effective date of the removal, and any volume commitment in the affected Statement of Work shall be reduced in proportion to the volume attributable to the removed Services.

**5.4 Consequences of Termination.** If Customer terminates under Section 5.2 or removes Services under Section 5.3:

(a) no termination fee, early termination charge, minimum volume shortfall charge, or unamortized implementation or capital cost shall be payable by Customer in respect of the terminated or removed Services;

(b) Supplier shall provide Transition Assistance in accordance with Section 13.7 of the Agreement; and

(c) Section 13.6 of the Agreement applies.

**5.5 Remedies Cumulative.** Customer's rights under this Section 5 are in addition to its rights under Section 13.1 of the Agreement, and Service Level Credits accrued before termination remain payable under Section 3.7.

## 6. MEASUREMENT, REPORTING AND GOVERNANCE

**6.1 Measurement Tools.** Supplier shall measure each Service Level using automated WMS data wherever practicable. Supplier shall not change the measurement tools, data sources or methodologies described in this SLA Schedule without Customer's prior written approval. Supplier shall provide Customer with read-only, real-time dashboard access to the WMS data used to measure SL-1 through SL-6.

**6.2 Monthly Report.** The monthly performance report required by Section 6.2 of the Agreement shall include, for each Facility:

(a) Supplier's actual performance against each Service Level for each Measurement Period in the month;

(b) each Service Level Default and the Service Level Credit calculated for it;

(c) each claim for relief under Section 4, with supporting information;

(d) Order, inbound and returns volumes compared with Customer's forecasts under Section 3.5 of the Agreement;

(e) the status of each root cause analysis and corrective action plan under Section 6.4 of the Agreement; and

(f) a trend analysis of performance over the preceding six (6) months.

**6.3 Peak Season Reporting.** During each Peak Season, in addition to the monthly report, Supplier shall deliver to Customer:

(a) by 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time each day, a daily operations report showing the prior day's Orders received, Orders shipped, backlog by Order age, inbound units received and put away, returns received and processed, and staffing levels against the peak readiness plan delivered under Section 3.3 of the Agreement; and

(b) within two (2) Business Days after the end of each Measurement Week, a weekly Service Level report for that Measurement Week.

**6.4 Data Retention and Audit.** Supplier shall retain the raw data used to measure the Service Levels for at least three (3) years after the end of the Measurement Period to which it relates. The data is subject to audit under Section 7.6 of the Agreement. If an audit reveals that Supplier's reported performance overstated its actual performance, Supplier shall promptly credit Customer any Service Level Credits that should have applied.

**6.5 Operational Reviews.** The parties' account managers shall meet:

(a) monthly, within five (5) Business Days after delivery of the monthly performance report, to review Supplier's performance; and

(b) during each Peak Season, weekly, at a time agreed by the parties.

**6.6 Peak Readiness Review.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after Supplier delivers the peak readiness plan under Section 3.3 of the Agreement, the parties shall meet to review the plan. Supplier shall consider in good faith Customer's comments and shall deliver a revised plan reflecting any changes the parties agree.

**6.7 Annual Review.** The annual review of the Service Levels under Section 6.5 of the Agreement shall be completed by June 30 of each calendar year. Any change to the Service Levels, Targets, Weighting Factors or the At-Risk Amount agreed in the review shall be documented in an Amendment.

## 7. ADDITIONS AND CHANGES TO SERVICE LEVELS

**7.1 New Services.** If the parties enter into a Statement of Work for Services not covered by the Service Levels in Section 2.2, the Statement of Work shall specify the Service Levels, Targets and measurement methodologies that apply to those Services. Until those Service Levels are specified, Supplier shall perform the Services at a level at least equal to the level customarily provided by Supplier to its other customers for comparable services.

**7.2 Reallocation of Weighting Factors.** Customer may, not more than twice in any contract year and on not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Supplier, reallocate the Weighting Factors among the Critical Service Levels. The total of the Weighting Factors must remain one hundred percent (100%), and no single Critical Service Level may be assigned a Weighting Factor greater than forty percent (40%). No reallocation may take effect during a Peak Season.

**7.3 Promotion of KPIs.** Customer may, on not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Supplier, designate a KPI as a Critical Service Level, together with a reallocation of the Weighting Factors under Section 7.2. A designation under this Section 7.3 counts as one of the reallocations permitted in the contract year under Section 7.2.

**7.4 Implementation Period.** No Service Level Credit applies to a Service Level Default in respect of SL-3 or SL-4 at a Facility during the first sixty (60) days after live fulfillment begins at that Facility under Section 3.7 of the Agreement. Supplier shall nonetheless report its performance against those Service Levels during that period.
