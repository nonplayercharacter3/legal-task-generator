# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2025 by **Dreisur Logistics Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 300 Harborline Drive, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation with its principal place of business at 1420 Ashcombe Street, Suite 600, Seattle, Washington 98101 ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, and together with the Data Processing Addendum, the Service Level Schedule and each Statement of Work entered into under it, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier provides e-commerce fulfillment, returns processing and peak surge services to Customer.

B. Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement and the Services provided under it.

C. Section 15.1 of the Agreement requires Supplier to deliver this Guaranty to Customer on or before the Effective Date as a condition to the effectiveness of the Agreement. Customer would not have entered into the Agreement without this Guaranty.

NOW, THEREFORE, to induce Customer to enter into the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Guarantor agrees as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Guaranty:

"**Bankruptcy Event**" means, with respect to any person, that such person (a) becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary case or proceeding under the United States Bankruptcy Code or any other bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation or similar law of any jurisdiction; (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or (c) has a receiver, trustee, custodian, liquidator or similar official appointed for it or for all or a substantial part of its assets.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Subordinated Debt**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Guaranty as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Guaranty. References to Sections in this Guaranty are to sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Customer, as primary obligor and not merely as surety:

(a) the full and punctual payment when due, whether at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise, of all amounts payable by Supplier to Customer under or in connection with the Agreement, including service credits not applied against invoices, refunds of overcharges, reimbursements of Inventory shrinkage and of costs, indemnification payments and damages; and

(b) the full and punctual performance and observance by Supplier of each of its covenants, obligations and undertakings under the Agreement, including its obligations to perform the Services, to release Inventory and to provide transition assistance under Section 13.7 of the Agreement,

in each case whether now existing or arising after the date of this Guaranty, whether absolute or contingent, and whether arising during the Initial Term, any Renewal Term or any Transition Period (collectively, the "**Guaranteed Obligations**").

**2.2 Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Customer is not required, as a condition to the Guarantor's liability, to make any demand on Supplier, to commence or exhaust any remedy against Supplier or any other person, to proceed against any security, or to pursue any other right or remedy.

**2.3 Performance by Guarantor.** If Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall, within five (5) Business Days after Customer's written demand:

(a) in the case of a payment obligation, pay the amount due to Customer in immediately available funds; and

(b) in the case of a performance obligation, perform or cause to be performed that obligation, whether by Supplier, by an Affiliate of the Guarantor or by a third party reasonably acceptable to Customer, and pay Customer all Losses that Customer incurs as a result of Supplier's failure.

**2.4 Scope of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed the liability that Supplier has, or would have but for a Bankruptcy Event of Supplier or any of the circumstances described in Section 4, under the Agreement in respect of the Guaranteed Obligations, together with the costs and expenses payable by the Guarantor under Section 9.

**2.5 Defenses of Supplier.** The Guarantor may assert, in defense of any claim under this Guaranty, any defense to payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation that Supplier could assert under the terms of the Agreement, including payment, performance and the express terms of the Agreement, other than any defense arising from (a) a Bankruptcy Event of Supplier; (b) the lack of power or authority of Supplier, or any defect in the authorization, execution or delivery of the Agreement by Supplier; (c) any statute of limitations affecting Supplier's liability to the extent the Guarantor's liability is not similarly affected; or (d) any of the circumstances described in Section 4.

**2.6 Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations whenever arising and remains in full force and effect until the Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full and the Agreement has expired or been terminated, subject to Section 5.

(c) this Guaranty has been duly executed and delivered by the Guarantor and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not and will not (i) violate its certificate of incorporation or bylaws; (ii) violate any Applicable Law or any order, judgment or decree binding on it; or (iii) conflict with, result in a breach of, or constitute a default under, any agreement or instrument to which it is a party or by which it or its assets are bound;

(e) no consent, approval, authorization or filing with any governmental authority or other person is required for the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty by the Guarantor, other than those that have been obtained or made and are in full force and effect;

(f) there is no action, suit, proceeding or investigation pending or, to its knowledge, threatened against it or any of its subsidiaries that would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on its ability to perform this Guaranty;

(h) the Guarantor is solvent, and after giving effect to this Guaranty, the fair value of its assets exceeds its liabilities, it is able to pay its debts as they become due, and it does not have unreasonably small capital for the business in which it is engaged;

(i) the Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the membership interests in Supplier; and

(j) the Guarantor has received and reviewed a copy of the Agreement, including the Data Processing Addendum and the Service Level Schedule, and has had the opportunity to consult with legal counsel of its choice regarding this Guaranty.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS UNCONDITIONAL

**4.1 No Release.** The obligations of the Guarantor under this Guaranty are absolute and unconditional and shall not be released, discharged, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of it or has consented to it:

(a) any amendment, supplement, modification, extension, renewal or replacement of the Agreement or any Statement of Work, including any increase in the scope of the Services, any change in the Fees or Service Levels, the addition of any Facility, or any renewal of the Agreement for one or more Renewal Terms;

(b) any extension of time for payment or performance, any waiver, indulgence, forbearance or compromise granted to Supplier, or any failure or delay by Customer in exercising any right or remedy against Supplier;

(c) any Bankruptcy Event of Supplier, or any disallowance, discharge, stay or modification of any Guaranteed Obligation in any proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law;

(d) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of the Agreement or any Guaranteed Obligation, or any lack of power or authority of Supplier;

(e) any merger, consolidation, reorganization or dissolution of Supplier, or any change in the ownership or corporate structure of Supplier or the Guarantor;

(f) the release, substitution or impairment of any security for, or any other guaranty of, the Guaranteed Obligations;

(g) any claim, set-off, counterclaim or other right that the Guarantor or Supplier may have against Customer, whether in connection with the Agreement or any unrelated transaction, except as provided in Section 2.5; or

(h) any other act, omission or circumstance that might, but for this Section 4.1, constitute a legal or equitable discharge of a surety or guarantor.

**4.2 Statements of Work.** The Guarantor acknowledges that Customer and Supplier may enter into additional Statements of Work, change orders and Amendments from time to time, and that the obligations of Supplier under each of them are Guaranteed Obligations without the need for any further consent of, notice to, or acknowledgment by the Guarantor.

## 5. REINSTATEMENT

This Guaranty shall continue to be effective, or shall be reinstated, as the case may be, if at any time any payment, or any part of any payment, of any Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded, avoided or must otherwise be restored or returned by Customer, whether upon a Bankruptcy Event of Supplier or otherwise, as though such payment had not been made. The Guarantor shall indemnify Customer on demand for all costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Customer in connection with any such rescission, avoidance or restoration.

## 6. WAIVERS

**6.1 Waiver of Notices and Defenses.** To the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Guarantor waives:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty and of the creation, renewal, extension or accrual of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(b) presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of non-payment or non-performance, other than the demand provided for in Section 2.3;

(c) any requirement that Customer first proceed against Supplier, any other guarantor or any security, or pursue any other remedy, before proceeding against the Guarantor;

(d) any right to require Customer to marshal assets in favor of Supplier or the Guarantor;

(e) any defense based on any election of remedies by Customer that impairs any right of subrogation or reimbursement of the Guarantor against Supplier; and

(f) all other suretyship defenses that would otherwise be available to the Guarantor, other than the defenses permitted by Section 2.5.

**6.2 Information.** The Guarantor assumes responsibility for keeping itself informed of the financial condition of Supplier and of all other circumstances bearing on the risk of non-payment or non-performance of the Guaranteed Obligations. Customer has no duty to advise the Guarantor of any information known to it regarding those matters.

## 7. SUBORDINATION AND SUBROGATION

**7.1 Subordination.** All indebtedness and other obligations of Supplier to the Guarantor or to any Affiliate of the Guarantor, whether now existing or arising after the date of this Guaranty (the "**Subordinated Debt**"), are subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment of all Guaranteed Obligations then due and unpaid. After Customer has made a demand under Section 2.3 and for so long as that demand remains unsatisfied, the Guarantor shall not demand, accept or receive any payment on account of the Subordinated Debt, and any amount received by the Guarantor in violation of this Section 7.1 shall be held in trust for Customer and promptly paid over to Customer to be applied to the Guaranteed Obligations.

**7.2 Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity that it may have against Supplier or any other guarantor by reason of any payment or performance under this Guaranty. Any amount paid to the Guarantor in violation of this Section 7.2 shall be held in trust for Customer and promptly paid over to Customer.

## 8. COVENANTS

**8.1 Financial Information.** For so long as this Guaranty remains in effect, the Guarantor shall deliver to Customer:

(a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of the Guarantor, its audited consolidated balance sheet and the related consolidated statements of income, stockholders' equity and cash flows for that fiscal year, together with the report of its independent certified public accountants; and

(b) within sixty (60) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, its unaudited consolidated balance sheet and the related consolidated statements of income and cash flows for that quarter, certified by its chief financial officer as fairly presenting, in all material respects, its consolidated financial position and results of operations.

The Guarantor's obligation under this Section 8.1 is satisfied to the extent that the relevant information is publicly filed or made available to Customer through a secure data site to which Customer has been given access. All information delivered under this Section 8.1 is Confidential Information of the Guarantor and shall be treated by Customer in accordance with Section 9.1 of the Agreement as if the Guarantor were the Discloser.

**8.2 Notice of Certain Events.** The Guarantor shall notify Customer in writing promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days, after becoming aware of:

(a) any Bankruptcy Event of the Guarantor or Supplier;

(b) any event or circumstance that would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the Guarantor's ability to perform this Guaranty; or

(c) any proposed transaction following which Supplier would cease to be a direct or indirect wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor.

**8.3 Maintenance of Existence.** The Guarantor shall maintain its corporate existence and good standing, and shall not consolidate with or merge into any other person, or convey, transfer or lease all or substantially all of its assets to any person, unless the surviving or transferee person (if not the Guarantor) expressly assumes in writing, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Customer, all of the obligations of the Guarantor under this Guaranty.

## 9. PAYMENTS; COSTS AND EXPENSES

**9.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments by the Guarantor under this Guaranty shall be made in United States dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account that Customer designates in writing, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction of any kind, except as provided in Section 2.5.

**9.2 Taxes.** All payments by the Guarantor under this Guaranty shall be made free and clear of, and without deduction for, any taxes, levies, duties or withholdings, unless the deduction is required by Applicable Law. If any deduction is required, the Guarantor shall pay such additional amount as is necessary so that Customer receives the full amount it would have received had no deduction been made, to the same extent that Supplier would have been required to pay such additional amount under the Agreement.

**9.3 Interest.** Any amount payable by the Guarantor under this Guaranty that is not paid when due shall bear interest from the due date until the date of payment at the rate provided in Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

**9.4 Enforcement Costs.** The Guarantor shall pay on demand all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs, incurred by Customer in enforcing this Guaranty, to the extent Customer prevails in such enforcement.

## 10. TERM

This Guaranty takes effect on the date of this Guaranty. It remains in effect until the latest of (a) the expiration or termination of the Agreement; (b) the end of any Transition Period; and (c) the payment and performance in full of all Guaranteed Obligations that arose or accrued on or before the latest of the dates in clauses (a) and (b), including any obligation that survives expiration or termination of the Agreement under Section 13.8 of the Agreement. The Guarantor may not revoke or terminate this Guaranty before that time.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT

**11.1 By Customer.** This Guaranty is for the benefit of Customer and its permitted successors and assigns under the Agreement. Customer may assign its rights under this Guaranty, in whole or in part, only to a person to whom Customer's rights under the Agreement have been assigned in accordance with Section 16 of the Agreement, and any such assignment shall be effective without the consent of the Guarantor upon written notice to the Guarantor.

**11.2 By Guarantor.** The Guarantor may not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Guaranty without the prior written consent of Customer, except as provided in Section 8.3. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 11.2 is void.

**11.3 Successors.** This Guaranty is binding on the Guarantor and its successors and permitted assigns.

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for the resolution of any dispute, claim or controversy arising out of or relating to this Guaranty, and waives any objection to venue in such courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.
