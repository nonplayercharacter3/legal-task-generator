## 6. ADDITIONAL INSUREDS AND LOSS PAYEE

**6.1 Additional Insureds.** The following are included as additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability, Commercial Automobile Liability and Umbrella Liability policies:

Kaigen Outdoor Co. and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees.

The status of an Affiliate as additional insured is determined in accordance with the definition of "Affiliate" in the Master Services Agreement described in Section 5.1, as that definition applies on the date of the occurrence or accident.

**6.2 Scope of Additional Insured Coverage.**

(a) Under the Commercial General Liability policy, additional insured status is provided by blanket additional insured endorsements CG 20 26 (04/13) and CG 20 37 (04/13), with company endorsement BIC-GL 4410. The additional insureds are covered for "bodily injury," "property damage" and "personal and advertising injury" caused, in whole or in part, by the Named Insured's acts or omissions or the acts or omissions of those acting on its behalf. This applies to ongoing operations and to products-completed operations. Coverage applies where the Named Insured has agreed in a written contract, executed before the loss, to provide additional insured status.

(b) Under the Commercial Automobile Liability policy, additional insured status is provided by blanket additional insured endorsement ONI-CA 2048 (designated insured where required by written contract). The additional insureds are insureds for liability arising out of the ownership, maintenance or use of a covered auto.

(c) Under the Umbrella Liability policy, additional insured status is provided by endorsement FCI-UMB 108. That endorsement extends insured status to any person or organization that is an insured under the scheduled underlying insurance. Coverage applies to the extent of that underlying coverage and subject to the limits of the umbrella policy.

**6.3 Limits Available to Additional Insureds.** The limits available to the additional insureds are the full limits of the applicable policies as shown in Section 4, subject to the policy aggregates. The limits are not reduced to the minimum limits required by the Master Services Agreement.

**6.4 Loss Payee.** Kaigen Outdoor Co. is named as loss payee, as its interests may appear, under the Warehouse Legal Liability policy by endorsement CFM-WL 305. This applies with respect to loss of or damage to goods of Kaigen Outdoor Co. at Location Nos. 2 and 3 and in transit between scheduled locations. Payment of loss in respect of those goods will be made jointly to the Named Insured and Kaigen Outdoor Co., or as they direct in writing.
