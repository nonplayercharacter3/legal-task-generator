# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**(E-Commerce Fulfillment, Returns Processing and Peak Surge Services)**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation with its principal place of business at 1420 Ashcombe Street, Suite 600, Seattle, Washington 98101 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 300 Harborline Drive, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**").

Customer and Supplier are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer designs, markets and sells outdoor apparel, footwear and gear through its own e-commerce storefronts and through third-party online marketplaces.

B. Supplier operates warehouse, fulfillment and reverse-logistics facilities and provides third-party e-commerce fulfillment, returns processing and seasonal surge services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Supplier, and Supplier wishes to be engaged, to provide such services on the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the Statements of Work entered into under it.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

**2.1 Framework.** This Agreement sets out the terms and conditions on which Supplier will provide Services to Customer. This Agreement does not by itself obligate Customer to purchase any minimum volume of Services, except as expressly set out in a Statement of Work.

**2.2 Statements of Work.** The parties may from time to time enter into one or more Statements of Work describing specific Services, the Facilities from which they will be performed, the Fees, any volume commitments, implementation milestones and any other project-specific terms. Each Statement of Work shall be signed by authorized representatives of both parties, shall reference this Agreement, and once signed shall be incorporated into and governed by this Agreement. Each Statement of Work shall be numbered sequentially.

**2.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement consists of the body of this Agreement, the DPA, the SLA Schedule, each Statement of Work and each Amendment. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, the following order of precedence shall apply: (a) first, any Amendment; (b) second, the DPA, as to the Processing of Personal Data; (c) third, the SLA Schedule; (d) fourth, the body of this Agreement; and (e) fifth, the applicable Statement of Work. Without limiting the foregoing, in any conflict or inconsistency concerning the Processing of Personal Data, the DPA shall prevail over the body of this Agreement, including Section 17.1.

**2.4 Change Orders.** Either party may request a change to the scope of Services under a Statement of Work by delivering a written change request to the other party. Supplier shall, within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of a change request from Customer, provide a written assessment of the impact of the requested change on the Services, the Service Levels, the Fees and any implementation timeline. No change shall be effective unless set out in a written change order signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Performance of Services.** Supplier shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work in accordance with this Agreement, the SLA Schedule, Customer's written standard operating procedures as provided to Supplier from time to time and agreed in the applicable Statement of Work, Applicable Law, and generally accepted industry standards for third-party e-commerce fulfillment providers of similar size and capability.

**3.2 Fulfillment and Returns.** Without limiting Section 3.1, Supplier shall:

(a) receive inbound shipments of Customer Products at the Facilities, reconcile them against advance shipping notices provided by Customer, and report shortages, overages and damage to Customer within the time set out in the applicable Statement of Work;

(b) store Inventory in a clean, secure, dry and climate-appropriate environment, segregated from goods of other Supplier customers, and in accordance with any handling instructions provided by Customer;

(c) pick, pack and ship Orders using packaging, inserts and carriers specified or approved by Customer, and apply all labels, hang-tags and markings required by Customer or Applicable Law;

(d) process returns of Customer Products in accordance with Customer's returns disposition rules, including grading, refurbishment where specified, restocking of saleable units, and quarantining of non-saleable or recalled units; and

(e) maintain accurate, real-time inventory records in the WMS, and perform cycle counts and an annual wall-to-wall physical inventory as specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

**3.3 Peak Surge Services.** Supplier acknowledges that Customer's business is highly seasonal and that the fall and winter outerwear launch and the holiday sales and returns cycle generate significantly increased Order and returns volumes. During each Peak Season, Supplier shall provide Peak Surge Services sufficient to meet the volume forecasts provided by Customer under Section 3.5 and to achieve the Service Levels, including by (a) recruiting, training and deploying incremental Supplier Personnel; (b) operating extended or additional shifts, including weekend shifts; (c) making available additional dock doors, pack stations and staging areas at the Facilities; and (d) prioritizing Customer's Orders in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work. Supplier shall deliver to Customer, no later than August 15 of each calendar year, a written peak readiness plan describing the staffing, capacity and contingency measures that Supplier will implement for the upcoming Peak Season.

**3.4 Facilities.** Supplier shall perform the Services only from the Facilities identified in the applicable Statement of Work. Supplier shall not relocate the performance of any Services to a different Facility, or add a Facility, without Customer's prior written approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for a relocation that does not adversely affect the Services or the Service Levels. Customer and its designees may, on reasonable notice and during normal business hours, visit and inspect any Facility.

**3.5 Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Supplier with rolling non-binding monthly volume forecasts for Orders, inbound units and returns in the form and at the intervals set out in the applicable Statement of Work. Forecasts are provided for planning purposes only and do not constitute a commitment by Customer to any volume, except as expressly set out in a Statement of Work.

**3.6 Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall (a) provide Supplier with accurate and timely advance shipping notices, product master data, item dimensions and weights, and handling instructions; (b) transmit Orders through the agreed integration; (c) provide the safety data sheets and handling information for any Customer Products that are classified as hazardous materials under Applicable Law; and (d) designate a relationship manager to act as Supplier's primary point of contact. Supplier's failure to perform any Service shall be excused to the extent, and only for so long as, such failure is directly caused by Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under this Section 3.6, provided that Supplier promptly notifies Customer of such failure and uses commercially reasonable efforts to perform notwithstanding it.

**3.7 Implementation.** Supplier shall perform any implementation, onboarding and systems integration activities described in a Statement of Work in accordance with the implementation plan and milestones set out in it. Supplier shall not begin live fulfillment of Orders from a Facility until the parties have completed the acceptance testing described in the applicable Statement of Work.

## 4. SUPPLIER PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTORS

**4.1 Qualified Personnel.** Supplier shall assign to the Services Supplier Personnel who are properly trained, qualified and, where required, licensed or certified to perform the tasks assigned to them. Supplier shall provide all Supplier Personnel with training on Customer's standard operating procedures, product care requirements and brand presentation standards before they perform Services.

**4.2 Account Management.** Supplier shall designate a dedicated account manager who shall have overall responsibility for the Services and who shall be reasonably available to Customer. Supplier shall give Customer not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any replacement of the account manager, except where replacement is required by resignation, illness, termination of employment or other circumstances outside Supplier's reasonable control.

**4.3 Employment Matters.** Supplier is solely responsible for the recruitment, hiring, compensation, benefits, supervision, discipline and termination of all Supplier Personnel, and for compliance with all Applicable Law relating to wages and hours, workers' compensation, immigration and employment eligibility, and workplace safety. Supplier shall conduct background checks on Supplier Personnel to the extent permitted by Applicable Law and consistent with Supplier's written screening policy, a copy of which Supplier shall provide to Customer on request.

**4.4 Subcontracting.** Supplier shall not subcontract the performance of any material part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Supplier may engage temporary staffing agencies to supply incremental labor for Peak Surge Services without such consent, provided that such labor is supervised by Supplier's employees. Supplier shall remain fully responsible for the acts and omissions of all Subcontractors as if they were Supplier's own, and shall ensure that each Subcontractor is bound by written obligations no less protective of Customer than those set out in this Agreement and the DPA.

**4.5 Removal.** Customer may, acting reasonably and in compliance with Applicable Law, request that Supplier remove any individual from the performance of the Services, and Supplier shall promptly comply with such request and replace such individual with a suitably qualified individual.

**4.6 Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was materially involved in the performance or management of the Services, without the other party's prior written consent. General solicitations not specifically directed at such employees, and the hiring of persons who respond to them, shall not breach this Section 4.6.

## 5. INVENTORY AND RISK OF LOSS

**5.1 Title.** Title to all Customer Products and Inventory remains at all times with Customer. Supplier shall not hold itself out as owner of any Inventory and shall keep Inventory free of all liens, security interests and encumbrances arising by, through or under Supplier.

**5.2 Warehouse Lien Waiver.** To the extent permitted by Applicable Law, Supplier waives any warehouseman's lien, possessory lien or other lien or right of retention that it may have in or over any Inventory, whether under Article 7 of the Uniform Commercial Code or otherwise.

**5.3 Care and Custody.** Supplier shall exercise the degree of care in safeguarding Inventory that a reasonably careful warehouse operator would exercise under like circumstances, and shall be responsible for loss of or damage to Inventory while in its possession, custody or control, other than loss or damage caused by Customer's negligence or by inherent vice of the Customer Products.

**5.4 Inventory Shrinkage.** Supplier shall reimburse Customer for Inventory shrinkage in excess of the shrinkage allowance set out in the applicable Statement of Work, measured at Customer's landed cost and reconciled on the basis of the annual physical inventory. Any amounts payable under this Section 5.4 shall be credited against the next invoice for Fees or, if no further invoices are to be issued, paid within thirty (30) days after reconciliation.

**5.5 Recalls and Holds.** Upon Customer's written instruction, Supplier shall promptly place a hold on, quarantine, segregate or withdraw from fulfillment any specified Customer Products, and shall cooperate with Customer in the conduct of any product recall or market withdrawal, including by identifying affected Orders and shipments from WMS records. Customer shall reimburse Supplier for its reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs of performing recall activities at Customer's request, at the rates set out in the applicable Statement of Work or, if none, at Supplier's then-current standard rates for comparable labor.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS

**6.1 Service Levels.** Supplier shall perform the Services so as to meet or exceed the Service Levels.

**6.2 Measurement and Reporting.** Supplier shall measure its performance against the Service Levels using the methodologies set out in the SLA Schedule and shall deliver to Customer, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, a written report of its performance against each Service Level for that month, together with such supporting data as Customer may reasonably request.

**6.3 Service Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet any Service Level, Supplier shall credit Customer the service credits calculated in accordance with the SLA Schedule. Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice for Fees. Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not an exclusive remedy, and Customer's receipt of service credits shall not limit any other right or remedy available to Customer, except as expressly provided in the SLA Schedule.

**6.4 Root Cause Analysis.** For each Service Level failure, Supplier shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of the month in which the failure occurred, (a) perform a root cause analysis; (b) provide Customer with a written report of its findings; and (c) implement a corrective action plan reasonably acceptable to Customer.

**6.5 Annual Review.** The parties shall review the Service Levels at least once in each contract year and may agree by Amendment to adjust them to reflect changes in Customer's business, technology or industry standards.

## 7. FEES, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

**7.1 Fees.** In consideration of the Services, Customer shall pay Supplier the Fees set out in the applicable Statement of Work. Except as expressly set out in a Statement of Work, the Fees include all costs and expenses incurred by Supplier in performing the Services, and Supplier shall not charge any additional fee, surcharge or expense without Customer's prior written approval.

**7.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Services performed in the preceding calendar month. Each invoice shall set out the Services performed, the applicable rates and quantities, any service credits and other credits applied, and any applicable taxes, and shall be accompanied by such supporting data as Customer may reasonably require.

**7.3 Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law, from the due date until the date of payment.

**7.4 Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount that it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date. The parties shall work together in good faith to resolve any such dispute within thirty (30) days. Supplier shall continue to perform the Services in accordance with this Agreement during the resolution of any payment dispute.

**7.5 Taxes.** The Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay any such taxes properly invoiced by Supplier in respect of the Services, other than taxes based on Supplier's net income, gross receipts, franchise, employment or property. Each party shall cooperate with the other in good faith to minimize taxes lawfully payable in respect of the Services.

**7.6 Records and Audit.** Supplier shall maintain complete and accurate books and records relating to the Services, the Fees and Inventory for the Term and for three (3) years thereafter. Customer, or an independent auditor appointed by Customer and bound by obligations of confidentiality, may audit such books and records, and Supplier's compliance with this Agreement, not more than once in any twelve (12) month period, upon not less than fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice, during normal business hours. If any audit reveals an overcharge, Supplier shall promptly refund the overcharged amount, and if the overcharge exceeds five percent (5%) of the Fees for the audited period, Supplier shall also reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of the audit.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY

**9.1 Confidentiality.**

(a) "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a party (the "**Discloser**") to the other party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement, whether before or after the Effective Date and in whatever form, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including product launch plans, pricing, volume forecasts, sales data, supplier and customer information, and the terms of this Agreement. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

(b) Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate (i) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement; (ii) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before receipt from the Discloser; (iii) is lawfully received by the Recipient from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation owed to the Discloser; or (iv) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

(c) The Recipient shall (i) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (ii) not disclose it to any person other than its and its Affiliates' employees, officers, directors, professional advisors and, in the case of Supplier, Subcontractors, in each case who need to know it for such purposes and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those set out in this Section 9.1; and (iii) protect it using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

(d) The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law or by order of a court or governmental authority, provided that the Recipient, where legally permitted, gives the Discloser prompt prior written notice and reasonable assistance so that the Discloser may seek a protective order or other remedy, and discloses only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

(e) Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, or upon the Discloser's earlier written request, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except that the Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by Applicable Law or in automatic electronic archives, subject to continuing confidentiality obligations. The obligations in this Section 9.1 shall survive for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets and Customer Data.

**9.2 Data Security.**

(a) Supplier shall implement and maintain a comprehensive written information security program that includes administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of Customer Data and of the WMS and other systems on which Customer Data is Processed. Such safeguards shall be no less rigorous than those required by Applicable Law and those generally accepted in the third-party logistics industry, and shall include, at a minimum: (i) encryption of Customer Data in transit over public networks and at rest; (ii) role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for all remote and privileged access; (iii) logging and monitoring of access to Customer Data; (iv) timely patching and vulnerability management; (v) secure disposal of shipping labels, packing slips and other printed materials containing Personal Data; and (vi) physical access controls at each Facility.

(b) Supplier shall maintain, and shall provide to Customer on request, a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment covering the WMS and the systems used to Process Customer Data, and shall promptly remediate any material exceptions identified in it.

(c) Supplier shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any Security Incident. Supplier shall promptly investigate, contain and remediate each Security Incident, provide Customer with all information reasonably requested regarding it, and cooperate with Customer in making any notifications to affected individuals, regulators, payment card brands or marketplaces that Customer determines are required or advisable.

(d) Supplier shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs incurred by Customer in responding to any Security Incident arising from a breach of this Agreement by Supplier or Supplier Personnel, including the costs of forensic investigation, notification of affected individuals, call-center support, credit monitoring and identity-protection services, and any fines or penalties imposed by a governmental authority or payment card brand.

**9.3 Processing of Personal Data.** The parties shall comply with the DPA in respect of all Personal Data Processed by Supplier on behalf of Customer under this Agreement.
