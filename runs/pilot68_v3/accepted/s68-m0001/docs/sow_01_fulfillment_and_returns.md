# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**(Fulfillment Services, Returns Services and Peak Surge Services)**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). It is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**").

This SOW is a Statement of Work under Section 2.2 of the Agreement. Upon signature by both parties it is incorporated into and governed by the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement, including in the SLA Schedule. In this SOW:

"**Go-Live Date**" means, for each Facility, the date on which live fulfillment of Orders begins at that Facility after completion of acceptance testing under Section 4.

"**Rate Card**" means the schedule of Fees set out in Section 8 and Appendix 1.

"**SKU**" means a unique stock-keeping unit of a Customer Product, identified by style, color and size.

"**Unit**" means a single saleable item of Customer Product bearing its own SKU barcode.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this SOW. References to Sections and Appendices in this SOW are to sections of, and appendices to, this SOW unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SCOPE AND FACILITIES

**2.1 Services.** Supplier shall provide the following Services under this SOW:

(a) Fulfillment Services for all Orders placed through Customer's own e-commerce storefronts and through the third-party online marketplaces listed in Appendix 2;

(b) Returns Services for all Customer Products returned by end customers through those channels; and

(c) Peak Surge Services during each Peak Season, as described in Section 6.

**2.2 Facilities.** Supplier shall perform the Services from the following Facilities:

| Facility | Address | Services |
|---|---|---|
| Facility 1 (East) | 2150 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 | Fulfillment Services; Peak Surge Services |
| Facility 2 (West) | 4800 Sierra Freight Way, Reno, Nevada 89502 | Fulfillment Services; Returns Services; Peak Surge Services |

Any relocation or addition of a Facility is subject to Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

**2.3 Order Allocation.** Customer shall allocate Orders between Facility 1 and Facility 2 through its order management system on the basis of ship-to region and Inventory availability. Supplier shall not reallocate Orders between Facilities without Customer's prior written approval, except to the extent required to meet SL-1 (Order Ship Timeliness) where the Inventory necessary to fulfill an Order is available only at the other Facility.

**2.4 Measurement Basis.** Each Service Level is measured separately for each Facility in accordance with Section 2.4 of the SLA Schedule.

## 3. FULFILLMENT AND RETURNS SERVICES

**3.1 Inbound Receiving.** Customer shall transmit an advance shipping notice to the WMS for each inbound shipment not less than forty-eight (48) hours before its scheduled arrival. Supplier shall:

(a) schedule dock appointments for inbound carriers;

(b) unload, count and reconcile each inbound shipment against its advance shipping notice;

(c) inspect a sample of not less than five percent (5%) of the cartons in each inbound shipment for damage and labeling compliance, or such greater sample as Customer specifies for a new vendor or a new product category; and

(d) put away received Units in pickable locations in the WMS.

**3.2 Receiving Discrepancies.** For the purposes of Section 3.2(a) of the Agreement and KPI-1 of the SLA Schedule, Supplier shall report shortages, overages and damage to Customer within one (1) Business Day after completing the receipt of the relevant inbound shipment. Each report shall identify the advance shipping notice, the affected SKUs and quantities, and photographs of any damage.

**3.3 Storage.** Supplier shall store Inventory in dedicated, segregated locations at each Facility. Outerwear containing down or synthetic insulation shall be stored on shelving or hanging racks and shall not be compressed beyond the limits stated in Customer's standard operating procedures. Footwear shall be stored in original boxes. Supplier shall maintain the temperature in storage areas between 50°F and 85°F and relative humidity below sixty percent (60%).

**3.4 Order Processing.** Supplier shall pick, pack and ship each Order in accordance with Section 3.2(c) of the Agreement and Customer's standard operating procedures, including:

(a) the inclusion of packing slips, inserts, gift messages and promotional collateral as specified by Customer for each channel;

(b) the use of branded mailers and cartons for storefront Orders and marketplace-compliant packaging for marketplace Orders; and

(c) the application of carrier labels generated through the WMS using the carrier and service level designated in the Order.

**3.5 Cutoff Time.** The Cutoff Time for each Facility is 2:00 p.m. local time at that Facility for all Order types.

**3.6 Scheduled Releases and Pre-Orders.** Customer may designate an Order as a pre-order or as part of a scheduled product release by identifying it in the Order transmission with the applicable release date. Supplier shall hold such Orders and ship them on the release date specified by Customer. For the purposes of SL-1 of the SLA Schedule, such Orders shall be shipped by the end of the release date if the Inventory required to fulfill them was received and put away at least two (2) Business Days before the release date.

**3.7 Kitting and Value-Added Services.** Upon Customer's written request, Supplier shall perform kitting, bundling, gift-wrapping, hang-tag replacement, relabeling and similar value-added services at the rates set out in Appendix 1.

**3.8 Returns Processing.** Supplier shall perform Returns Services at Facility 2. Returns received at Facility 1 shall be consolidated and transferred to Facility 2 not less than once each week, at Customer's cost for freight. For each returned Unit, Supplier shall:

(a) receive and match the return against the return authorization issued through Customer's returns portal;

(b) inspect and grade the Unit in accordance with Customer's returns disposition rules, using the grades "A" (restock as new), "B" (refurbish and restock), "C" (outlet or liquidation) and "D" (dispose or donate);

(c) perform the refurbishment specified for Grade B Units, including steaming, lint removal, retagging and repackaging;

(d) restock Grade A Units and refurbished Grade B Units into pickable locations;

(e) segregate Grade C and Grade D Units for disposition as instructed by Customer; and

(f) report the disposition of each Unit to Customer through the WMS.

**3.9 Recalled and Quarantined Units.** Supplier shall quarantine any returned Unit that matches a SKU or lot subject to a hold under Section 5.5 of the Agreement and shall notify Customer within one (1) Business Day after receipt.

**3.10 Inventory Control.** For the purposes of Section 3.2(e) of the Agreement:

(a) Supplier shall cycle count all SKU locations on an ABC basis, with "A" SKUs (the top twenty percent (20%) by Unit velocity) counted monthly, "B" SKUs (the next thirty percent (30%)) counted quarterly, and all other SKUs counted twice per contract year;

(b) Supplier shall perform an annual wall-to-wall physical inventory at each Facility in the last two (2) weeks of February in each calendar year, or at such other time outside Peak Season as the parties agree in writing; and

(c) Customer may attend each physical inventory and may conduct, or appoint an independent auditor to conduct, test counts.

**4.2 Acceptance Testing.** Before the Go-Live Date for each Facility, the parties shall jointly perform acceptance testing that demonstrates, using test Orders and returns representative of each channel, that:

(a) Orders flow from Customer's order management system to the WMS and order status, shipment confirmations and tracking numbers flow back without error;

(b) inventory adjustments and receipts are reported accurately;

(c) shipping labels are generated correctly for each carrier and service level; and

(d) return authorizations are matched and dispositions are reported correctly.

Acceptance testing is complete when Customer confirms in writing that the test results meet the criteria in this Section 4.2. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold or delay that confirmation.

**4.3 Delay.** If a milestone is delayed, Supplier shall promptly notify Customer of the delay, its cause and Supplier's plan to recover. The parties shall agree in writing any revised target dates. Section 3.6 of the Agreement applies to any delay caused by Customer.

**4.4 Implementation Fee.** Customer shall pay the one-time implementation fee set out in Appendix 1, invoiced fifty percent (50%) on completion of Milestone M2 and fifty percent (50%) on the Go-Live Date for Facility 2.

## 6. PEAK SURGE SERVICES

**6.1 Peak Season.** The Peak Surge Services under this SOW apply during each Peak Season, as defined in Section 1.1 of the Agreement.

**6.2 Surge Staffing.** During each Peak Season, Supplier shall staff each Facility at levels sufficient to process the volumes in Customer's most recent forecast plus twenty-five percent (25%) within the Service Level Targets. Without limiting the foregoing, Supplier shall:

(a) have recruited, onboarded and trained all incremental Supplier Personnel identified in the peak readiness plan no later than ten (10) days before the start of the Peak Season;

(b) maintain on each shift a ratio of not less than one (1) trained Supplier employee lead for every fifteen (15) temporary workers;

(c) operate two (2) shifts per day, seven (7) days per week, at each Facility, and add a third shift where needed to meet the Service Levels; and

(d) maintain a standby pool of trained temporary workers equal to at least ten percent (10%) of the Peak Season headcount at each Facility.

**6.3 Surge Capacity.** During each Peak Season, Supplier shall make available at each Facility the minimum capacity set out below, in addition to the capacity used for Customer's Orders outside Peak Season:

| Resource | Facility 1 | Facility 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Additional pack stations | 20 | 30 |
| Additional dock doors (outbound) | 2 | 3 |
| Additional staging area (square feet) | 8,000 | 12,000 |
| Additional returns processing stations | – | 15 |

**6.4 Order Prioritization.** For the purposes of Section 3.3(d) of the Agreement, during Peak Season Supplier shall process Orders in the following priority: (a) expedited and overnight carrier service levels; (b) Orders for scheduled releases on their release date; (c) marketplace Orders subject to marketplace ship-by requirements; and (d) all other Orders, on a first-in, first-out basis. Supplier shall not prioritize the orders of any other customer at a Facility over Customer's Orders where doing so would cause a Service Level Default.

**6.5 Holiday Returns.** Supplier acknowledges that Customer's holiday returns window generates returns volumes at Facility 2 that peak in January. Supplier shall maintain the returns processing staffing and capacity described in Sections 6.2 and 6.3 at Facility 2 through the end of each Peak Season.

**6.6 Peak Fees.** The Peak Season rates in Appendix 1 apply to Services performed during Peak Season. Supplier shall not charge any peak surcharge, surge fee or premium other than as set out in Appendix 1.

## 7. VOLUME COMMITMENT

**7.1 Annual Minimum.** Customer commits to tender to Supplier, in each contract year during the SOW Term beginning on the Go-Live Date for Facility 1, Orders representing not fewer than 450,000 outbound Units in the aggregate across both Facilities (the "**Annual Minimum**"). The Annual Minimum for the first contract year shall be reduced in proportion to the number of days between the beginning of that contract year and the Go-Live Date for Facility 1.

**7.2 Shortfall.** If the outbound Units tendered in a contract year are less than the Annual Minimum, Customer shall pay Supplier, within forty-five (45) days after receipt of Supplier's invoice, a shortfall charge equal to the number of Units of shortfall multiplied by $0.40.

**7.3 Adjustments.** The Annual Minimum is reduced in proportion to (a) any period during which Supplier is unable to perform the Services due to a Force Majeure Event; (b) any volume that Customer procures from an alternative provider under Section 18.2 of the Agreement; and (c) any reduction under Section 5.3 of the SLA Schedule. No shortfall charge is payable in the circumstances described in Section 5.4(a) of the SLA Schedule.

## 8. FEES

**8.1 Rate Card.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Appendix 1 for the Services performed under this SOW, in accordance with Section 7 of the Agreement.

**8.2 Pass-Through Charges.** Carrier charges for outbound and return parcels shall be billed to Customer at Supplier's actual cost, without markup, where Supplier ships on its own carrier accounts. Where Supplier ships on Customer's carrier accounts, carrier charges shall be billed directly to Customer by the carrier. Packaging materials purchased by Supplier at Customer's request shall be billed at Supplier's actual cost, without markup.

**8.3 Rate Adjustment.** Supplier may, by written notice to Customer given no later than sixty (60) days before the anniversary of the SOW Effective Date in any year after the first contract year, increase the labor-based rates in Appendix 1 by not more than the lesser of (a) three percent (3%) and (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), U.S. City Average, published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, for the most recent twelve (12) month period for which it is published. No rate adjustment shall take effect during a Peak Season.

**8.4 Recall Activities.** For the purposes of Section 5.5 of the Agreement, recall, hold and withdrawal activities performed at Customer's request are charged at the special project labor rate in Appendix 1.

**8.5 Transition Assistance.** For the purposes of Section 13.7 of the Agreement, transition assistance is charged at the rates in Appendix 1, with the special project labor rate applying to transition planning, joint physical inventory and knowledge transfer, and the handling rates applying to the packing, staging and loading of Inventory.

## 9. INVENTORY SHRINKAGE

**9.1 Shrinkage Allowance.** For the purposes of Section 5.4 of the Agreement, the shrinkage allowance is twenty-five hundredths of one percent (0.25%) of the total Units received at and shipped from each Facility in the contract year, measured on the basis of the annual physical inventory under Section 3.10(b).

**9.2 Valuation.** Shrinkage in excess of the shrinkage allowance is valued at Customer's landed cost, as shown in the product master data provided by Customer under Section 3.6(a) of the Agreement.

**9.3 Damage.** Units damaged at a Facility while in Supplier's possession, custody or control, other than as described in Section 5.3 of the Agreement, are not included in shrinkage and are governed by Section 5.3 of the Agreement.

## 10. TERM OF SOW

**10.1 SOW Term.** This SOW commences on the SOW Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the Agreement (the "**SOW Term**").

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Sections 13.6 and 13.7 of the Agreement apply on the expiration or termination of this SOW.

## 11. REPORTING

**11.1 Monthly Report.** The monthly performance report under Section 6.2 of the Agreement and Section 6.2 of the SLA Schedule shall be delivered to Customer's operations contacts and to the Director of Fulfillment Operations.

**11.2 Additional Reports.** In addition to the reports required by the Agreement and the SLA Schedule, Supplier shall make available to Customer through the WMS dashboard:

(a) real-time Inventory on hand, available to sell and on hold, by SKU and by Facility;

(b) open Orders by age and status;

(c) returns received, graded and dispositioned, by grade and by return reason; and

(d) inbound receipts by advance shipping notice.

**11.3 Quarterly Business Review.** The parties' account managers and a senior executive of each party shall meet once in each calendar quarter, other than during Peak Season, to review Supplier's performance, Customer's forecasts and growth plans, continuous improvement initiatives and any proposed changes to the Services.
