## 2. PREMISES AND COMMON AREAS

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms and conditions of this Lease.

**2.2 Common Areas.** Tenant has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobbies, corridors, elevators, stairways, restrooms on multi-tenant floors, loading dock, bicycle storage room, fitness center and other areas of the Property that Landlord designates from time to time for the common use of tenants (the "**Common Areas**"), subject to the rules and regulations attached as Exhibit C (the "**Rules and Regulations**"). Landlord may change the Common Areas from time to time, provided that no change materially and adversely affects access to or use of the Premises.

**2.3 Measurement.** The Rentable Area of the Premises and of the Building has been determined in accordance with ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017 and is not subject to remeasurement during the Term.

**2.4 Condition.** Except for Landlord's Work under Exhibit B, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date. Landlord warrants that, on the Commencement Date, the base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems serving the Premises will be in good working order and the roof will be watertight. Landlord shall correct, at its cost, any breach of this warranty of which Tenant notifies Landlord within twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** The Term commences on the Commencement Date and expires on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease.

**3.2 Early Access.** Tenant and its contractors may enter the Premises beginning thirty (30) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, fixtures, cabling and equipment, provided that Tenant has delivered evidence of the insurance required by Section 11. All terms of this Lease, other than the obligation to pay Rent, apply during that early access period.

**3.3 Option to Extend.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for a period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the Extension Term shall equal ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Exhibit D. Tenant shall exercise the option, if at all, by written notice to Landlord given not more than fifteen (15) months nor fewer than nine (9) months before the Expiration Date. The option may not be exercised while Tenant is in monetary default beyond any applicable notice and cure period. The Term does not extend unless Tenant exercises the option in writing.

**3.4 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration or termination of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. Base Rent for each month of holdover is one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent payable for the last full month of the Term. Tenant is liable for consequential damages arising from a holdover only if the holdover continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord notifies Tenant that Landlord has entered into a lease of the Premises with a successor tenant.

## 4. RENT

**4.1 Payment.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction or set-off except as expressly provided in this Lease. Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis. All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**," and Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

**4.2 Method of Payment.** Tenant shall pay Rent by ACH transfer to the account that Landlord designates in writing. Landlord shall give Tenant not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any change in its designated account, signed by an authorized officer of Landlord's manager. Tenant may verify any such change by telephone with Landlord's property manager before making payment to a new account.

**4.3 Late Charge; Interest.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to four percent (4%) of the overdue amount. The late charge does not apply to the first late payment in any twelve (12) month period unless the payment remains unpaid five (5) days after Landlord's written notice. Any Rent not paid within thirty (30) days after its due date bears interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum from the due date until paid.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**5.1 Tenant's Share.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

**5.2 Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means all costs reasonably incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, repairing and managing the Property in accordance with the standards of comparable Class A office buildings in downtown Seattle, including utilities for the Common Areas, janitorial service, security, insurance premiums, property management fees not exceeding three percent (3%) of gross revenues of the Property, and the amortized cost of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date. Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) costs of capital improvements, except as stated above, amortized over their useful lives under generally accepted accounting principles;

(b) leasing commissions, attorneys' fees and other costs of leasing space in the Building;

(c) costs of improvements made for other tenants;

(d) principal, interest and other costs of any financing or ground lease;

(e) depreciation;

(f) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(g) costs of correcting defects in the original construction of the Building;

(h) costs of remediating Hazardous Materials not introduced by Tenant;

(i) salaries of personnel above the level of building manager;

(j) charitable and political contributions; and

(k) fines, penalties and late charges incurred by Landlord.

**5.3 Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied during any calendar year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

**5.4 Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year, on a cumulative and compounding basis, over the Base Year amount. "**Controllable Operating Expenses**" means all Operating Expenses other than insurance premiums, utilities, snow and ice removal, costs of complying with laws first enacted after the Lease Date, and costs of union labor wage increases.

**5.5 Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means all real property taxes and assessments levied against the Property, including any tax imposed in substitution for them. Taxes exclude federal and state income, estate, franchise and business and occupation taxes of Landlord.

**5.6 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall deliver to Tenant, before the start of each calendar year, a reasonable estimate of Tenant's Share of excess Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimate with each monthly installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due or, at the end of the Term, refunded within thirty (30) days. Any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days after delivery of the statement.

**5.7 Audit.** Tenant may, within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving a reconciliation statement, audit Landlord's books and records relating to that statement, using an accountant not compensated on a contingency basis. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share for the year by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs in addition to refunding the overpayment.

## 6. USE

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**6.2 Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply, as part of Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 5.2, with all laws applicable to the base building structure, systems and Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act.

**6.3 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous or toxic substance regulated by environmental law ("**Hazardous Materials**") onto the Property, other than customary office and cleaning supplies and product samples in commercially reasonable quantities stored and used in compliance with law. Tenant shall not store commercial inventory at the Premises.

**6.4 Access.** Tenant has access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's access control system and to emergencies.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**7.1 Landlord Services.** Landlord shall furnish, as part of Operating Expenses:

(a) heating, ventilation and air-conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding holidays observed by the Building ("**Building Hours**");

(b) passenger elevator service at all times, with at least one elevator serving the Premises outside Building Hours, and freight elevator service on a scheduled basis;

(c) janitorial service five (5) nights per week consistent with the specifications in Exhibit C;

(d) hot and cold water for restrooms and kitchenettes; and

(e) lobby attendant service during Building Hours and access-controlled security at all other times.

**7.2 After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside Building Hours on Tenant's request through the Building's online request system, at Landlord's actual cost without profit, currently $65.00 per hour per floor.

**7.3 Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is separately metered. Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly for electricity consumed in the Premises.

**7.4 Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services, except that if any service that Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than three (3) consecutive Business Days for reasons within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable, Rent abates in proportion to the untenantable area from the fourth (4th) Business Day until the service is restored. "**Business Day**" in this Lease means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the State of Washington.

## 8. ALTERATIONS AND REPAIRS

**8.1 Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, costing less than $50,000 in any twelve (12) month period that do not affect the structure or base building systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord ten (10) Business Days' prior notice. All alterations shall be performed by licensed contractors in a good and workmanlike manner and in compliance with law.

**8.2 Removal.** At the time Landlord consents to an alteration, Landlord shall state whether Tenant must remove it at the end of the Term. Tenant is not required to remove Landlord's Work or any alteration that Landlord does not designate for removal at the time of consent. Tenant shall remove its cabling installed after the Commencement Date and its trade fixtures, furniture and equipment, and shall repair any damage caused by removal.

**8.3 Repairs by Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, as part of Operating Expenses except where caused by Tenant's negligence, the structure, roof, foundation, exterior walls, Common Areas and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems.

**8.4 Repairs by Tenant.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises, including supplemental HVAC units, kitchen equipment and other systems exclusively serving the Premises and installed by or for Tenant, in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted.

**8.5 Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant, and shall discharge or bond over any such lien within twenty (20) days after notice of its filing.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**9.1 Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 9.3, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the request and reasonable information about the proposed transferee.

**9.2 Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from a subtenant or assignee in excess of the Rent payable under this Lease for the applicable space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer, including brokerage commissions, attorneys' fees, improvement allowances and free rent.

**9.3 Permitted Transfers.** Landlord's consent is not required for (a) an assignment of this Lease or a sublease to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant; (b) an assignment to an entity resulting from a merger or consolidation with Tenant or acquiring all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided that the successor's tangible net worth immediately after the transaction is not less than Tenant's tangible net worth on the Lease Date; or (c) any transfer of the stock or other ownership interests in Tenant, or in any entity that directly or indirectly controls Tenant (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"). Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of any Permitted Transfer under clause (a) or (b) within thirty (30) days after it occurs. A transfer under clause (c) is not an assignment of this Lease.

**9.4 No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord expressly agrees in writing.

## 10. INDEMNITY AND WAIVER

**10.1 By Tenant.** Subject to Section 11.4, Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord and its members, managers, employees and agents from all claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors in the Building, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors.

**10.2 By Landlord.** Subject to Section 11.4, Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Tenant and its officers, directors, employees and agents from all claims, losses and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors.

**10.3 Industrial Insurance Waiver.** Solely for the purpose of giving effect to the indemnities in this Section 10 and to the extent permitted by law, each party waives, as to the other party only, its immunity under the Industrial Insurance Act, Title 51 RCW. The parties acknowledge that this waiver was mutually negotiated.

**10.4 Survival.** This Section 10 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease.

## 11. INSURANCE

**11.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord, its property manager and any mortgagee designated by Landlord as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance on a special form basis covering Tenant's furniture, fixtures, equipment, product samples and alterations for their full replacement cost;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000; and

(d) business interruption insurance sufficient to cover not less than twelve (12) months of Rent.

**11.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on a special form basis covering the Building for its full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence. The cost of that insurance is an Operating Expense.

**11.3 Certificates.** Each party shall deliver to the other, before the Commencement Date and upon each renewal, certificates evidencing the insurance it is required to maintain.

**11.4 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent the loss is covered, or would be covered, by the property insurance the waiving party is required to maintain under this Lease, and each party shall cause its property insurers to waive subrogation accordingly.

## 12. DAMAGE AND CONDEMNATION

**12.1 Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building is damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, deliver to Tenant a reasonable estimate by an independent contractor of the time required to restore it. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 12.2, Landlord shall restore the base building and Landlord's Work with reasonable diligence.

**12.2 Termination.** Either party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate if the estimated restoration period exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, or if the casualty occurs during the last twelve (12) months of the Term and the estimated restoration period exceeds sixty (60) days.

**12.3 Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable by the casualty, from the date of the casualty until the earlier of Tenant's reoccupation of that part and the date that is ninety (90) days after Landlord substantially completes its restoration.

**12.4 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or of the Building's access or parking that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of taking. Landlord is entitled to the entire award, except that Tenant may make a separate claim for its moving expenses and its unamortized alterations paid for by Tenant.

## 13. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**13.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) Business Days after written notice from Landlord that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within that period, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that, if involuntary, is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**13.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may, in addition to its other remedies at law, terminate this Lease by written notice, or, without terminating this Lease, re-enter the Premises in accordance with law and relet them for Tenant's account. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages. Landlord may recover from Tenant the Rent due through the date of termination and the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for that period, discounted to present value at the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco plus one percent (1%).

**13.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or such longer period as is reasonably required if Landlord begins the cure within that period and diligently completes it, Tenant may pursue its remedies at law. If the failure relates to a repair within the Premises that materially interferes with Tenant's use of the Premises, Tenant may perform the repair after an additional ten (10) Business Days' notice and recover its reasonable costs from Landlord.

## 14. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure an Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore any amount applied within ten (10) Business Days after Landlord's written demand. Landlord is not required to keep the Security Deposit separate from its general funds, except as required by law. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the expiration of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises, together with the statement required by RCW 59.18 to the extent applicable. Provided no Event of Default has occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $64,470.00 on January 1, 2028, and Landlord shall refund the difference to Tenant within thirty (30) days after that date.

## 15. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

**15.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage or deed of trust now or later encumbering the Property, provided that the holder of each such instrument delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which the holder agrees not to disturb Tenant's possession so long as no Event of Default has occurred. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its existing lender within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

**15.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) Business Days after the other party's request, deliver a certificate confirming the Commencement Date and Expiration Date, the Rent then payable, whether this Lease is in full force and effect, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists. Neither party is required to deliver more than two (2) estoppel certificates in any calendar year.

## 16. SURRENDER

At the expiration or termination of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty, condemnation and Landlord's repair obligations excepted, with the items Tenant is required to remove under Section 8.2 removed.

## 17. PARKING AND SIGNAGE

**17.1 Parking.** Tenant may purchase up to the number of parking permits stated in the Basic Lease Information at the prevailing monthly rate charged to tenants of the Building, currently $285.00 per permit per month. Landlord may change the rate from time to time to the rates charged in comparable buildings in downtown Seattle.

**17.2 Signage.** Landlord shall provide, at its cost, Building-standard suite entry signage and a listing in the lobby directory. Tenant may install, at its cost and subject to Landlord's reasonable approval of design and to applicable law, its name and logo in the sixth (6th) floor elevator lobby.

## 18. LANDLORD'S ACCESS

Landlord may enter the Premises on not less than one (1) Business Day's notice, except in an emergency, to inspect them, to make repairs, and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, to show them to prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures, including the escort of Landlord's personnel in Tenant's design studio and product sample areas.

## 21. GENERAL

**21.1 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default has occurred, Tenant shall peaceably enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

**21.2 Transfer by Landlord.** Upon a transfer of the Building, the transferor Landlord is released from obligations first arising after the transfer, provided that the transferee assumes those obligations in writing and the Security Deposit is transferred to the transferee.

**21.3 Limitation on Landlord Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Property, including rents, insurance proceeds and sale and condemnation proceeds.

**21.4 Force Majeure.** Except for the payment of Rent, neither party is in default for any delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes not involving the delayed party's own employees, governmental action and epidemics, for the duration of the delay.

**21.5 Governing Law; Venue.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Washington. Any action arising out of this Lease shall be brought in the Superior Court of the State of Washington for King County.

**21.6 Attorneys' Fees.** In any action to enforce this Lease, the prevailing party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

**21.7 Amendments; Waivers.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing. Landlord's acceptance of Rent does not waive any default.

**21.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

**21.9 Memorandum of Lease.** At either party's request, the parties shall execute a memorandum of this Lease in recordable form. Tenant shall not record this Lease itself.

**21.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.

## EXHIBIT D – DETERMINATION OF FAIR MARKET RENT (SUMMARY)

**1. Definition.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rental rate per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay and a willing landlord would accept for comparable space in comparable Class A office buildings in downtown Seattle, for a term comparable to the Extension Term, taking into account all relevant concessions, including improvement allowances, free rent and brokerage commissions.

**2. Procedure.** Within thirty (30) days after Tenant exercises its option, Landlord shall notify Tenant of its determination of Fair Market Rent. If Tenant objects within fifteen (15) Business Days, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days. If they do not agree, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience leasing downtown Seattle office space, and the two brokers shall select a third. The third broker shall select whichever of the parties' final determinations is closer to Fair Market Rent, and that selection is binding. Each party bears the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.
