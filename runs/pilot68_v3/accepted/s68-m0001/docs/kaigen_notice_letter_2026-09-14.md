## 1. Pending Sale of Kaigen Group Holdings, Inc.

We are writing to inform you that Kaigen Group Holdings, Inc. ("**Holdings**"), the parent company of Kaigen, has entered into a definitive stock purchase agreement with Sorrelpeak Capital Partners, L.P. ("**Sorrelpeak**"). Under that agreement, Sorrelpeak will acquire all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Holdings (the "**Transaction**"). The Transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended. Closing is expected to occur on or about December 15, 2026.

When the Transaction closes, Kaigen will remain a wholly owned subsidiary of Holdings. Holdings will in turn be wholly owned by Sorrelpeak and its affiliated investment vehicles. Kaigen's corporate name, state of incorporation, principal place of business, federal employer identification number and legal existence will not change as a result of the Transaction.

## 2. Continuity of the Relationship

No action is required by Dreisur in connection with the Transaction. Kaigen will continue to be the Customer under the Agreement, and the Agreement will continue in accordance with its terms. We expect the Services to continue without interruption before, at and after closing. Kaigen's current executive leadership, including our Chief Operating Officer, Miriam Oyelaran-Hart, and our Vice President, Supply Chain, will continue to lead Kaigen's operations and its relationship with Dreisur after closing.

We are providing this notice as a matter of good relationship management. The Transaction is an important development for our business, and we wanted you to hear about it from us before any public announcement. We greatly value the work your teams have done at the Facilities over the past eighteen months, and we look forward to continuing that work together.

## 3. Background to the Transaction

**3.1 Sorrelpeak.** Sorrelpeak is a private investment firm that focuses on consumer, retail and branded products businesses. Sorrelpeak has told us that it intends to support Kaigen's continued growth in its direct-to-consumer and marketplace channels. It also intends to support the expansion of Kaigen's outerwear and technical gear categories.

**3.2 Structure.** The Transaction is structured as a purchase of the stock of Holdings. Kaigen is not a party to the stock purchase agreement. No assets, contracts or employees of Kaigen are being transferred to any other entity as part of the Transaction. Kaigen's existing contracts with its suppliers, service providers, wholesale customers and marketplaces remain in place with Kaigen.

**3.3 Timing.** The parties to the stock purchase agreement filed the required notifications under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act during the week of September 7, 2026. Subject to the expiration of the waiting period and the satisfaction of the other closing conditions, closing is expected to occur on or about December 15, 2026. If the expected timing changes materially, we will let you know.

**3.4 Financing.** We understand that Sorrelpeak will fund the purchase price with equity commitments from its funds and with debt financing arranged at the Holdings level. Kaigen will not be a borrower under that financing. We do not expect the Transaction to affect Kaigen's ability to pay the Fees when due under Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

## 5. Invoicing and Payment

**5.1 No Change to Payment Arrangements.** The Transaction will not change Kaigen's invoicing or payment arrangements. Please continue to submit invoices through Kaigen's electronic accounts payable portal in accordance with Section 7.2 of the Agreement as amended. Kaigen will continue to pay by ACH transfer to the bank account Dreisur has designated under Section 7.3 of the Agreement as amended.

**5.2 Payment Security.** Transactions of this kind sometimes attract fraudulent payment-redirection attempts. These often take the form of emails that appear to come from a company's finance or legal team and announce new remittance instructions "in connection with the acquisition." Kaigen will **not** change its billing entity, remittance procedures or accounts payable contacts as a result of the Transaction. Kaigen will also not ask Dreisur to change its own designated bank account. If you receive any communication that claims to come from Kaigen, Holdings or Sorrelpeak and requests a change in payment or invoicing details, please do not act on it. Instead, please verify it by telephone with our Vice President, Supply Chain or our accounts payable manager at the numbers you already have on file.

Similarly, Kaigen will continue to verify any request to change Dreisur's designated bank account in the manner described in Section 7.3 of the Agreement as amended.

**5.3 Accounts Payable Contacts.** Kaigen's accounts payable contacts remain unchanged. Questions about invoices, credits or payment status may continue to be directed to ap@kaigenoutdoor.com.
