# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Exhibit A to the Master Services Agreement**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). It forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Agreement, Supplier provides Fulfillment Services, Returns Services and Peak Surge Services to Customer. In doing so, Supplier Processes Personal Data of Customer's end customers and other individuals on behalf of Customer.

B. The parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will Process such Personal Data. They intend these terms to satisfy the requirements of Data Protection Laws for contracts between a business or controller and its service provider or processor.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this DPA:

"**Customer Personal Data**" means Personal Data that Supplier or any Sub-processor Processes on behalf of Customer in connection with the Agreement, including Personal Data contained in Orders, shipping labels, packing slips, return authorizations, customer service records and WMS records.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all Applicable Law relating to privacy, data protection, data security, breach notification or the Processing of Personal Data. This includes the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations (the "**CCPA**"); the consumer privacy laws of the other U.S. states, as in effect from time to time; state data breach notification and data security laws; and, to the extent applicable, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada) and comparable provincial laws.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Customer Personal Data relates.

"**Data Subject Request**" means a request by or on behalf of a Data Subject to exercise a right granted under Data Protection Laws. Such rights include access, correction, deletion, portability, and opting out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising.

"**Personal Data**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular natural person or household. It includes any information defined as "personal data," "personal information" or a similar term under Data Protection Laws.

"**Personal Data Breach**" means a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data. It also includes any other event that requires notification to Data Subjects, regulators or other third parties under Data Protection Laws.

"**Process**" and "**Processing**" mean any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. Such operations include collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

"**Security Measures**" means the administrative, technical and physical safeguards described in Section 5 and Annex 2.

"**Sub-processor**" means any person, other than an employee of Supplier, that Supplier engages to Process Customer Personal Data. This includes Supplier's Affiliates and any Subcontractor that has access to Customer Personal Data.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this DPA as if set out in full. References to Sections and Annexes in this DPA are to sections of, and annexes to, this DPA unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES OF THE PARTIES

**2.1 Scope.** This DPA applies to all Processing of Customer Personal Data by Supplier and its Sub-processors in connection with the Agreement and each Statement of Work.

**2.2 Roles.** As between the parties:

(a) Customer is the "business" or "controller" of Customer Personal Data under Data Protection Laws.

(b) Supplier is the "service provider," "contractor" or "processor" of Customer Personal Data, acting on behalf of Customer.

**2.3 Description of Processing.** Annex 1 sets out:

(a) the subject matter, nature and purpose of the Processing;

(b) the categories of Data Subjects and Customer Personal Data; and

(c) the duration of the Processing.

The parties may update Annex 1 by written agreement as Statements of Work are added or changed.

**2.4 Customer Obligations.** Customer shall ensure that it has provided all notices and obtained all consents required by Data Protection Laws for Supplier to Process Customer Personal Data in accordance with this DPA and Customer's instructions.

## 3. PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS AND RESTRICTIONS

**3.1 Documented Instructions.** Supplier shall Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA and each Statement of Work constitute Customer's complete instructions as of the Effective Date. Customer may issue additional or replacement written instructions consistent with the Agreement.

**3.2 Unlawful Instructions.** Supplier shall promptly inform Customer if, in Supplier's opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws. Supplier may then suspend Processing under that instruction until Customer confirms or modifies it.

**3.3 Restrictions.** Supplier shall not:

(a) sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined under the CCPA;

(b) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in Annex 1, including for any commercial purpose other than performing the Services;

(c) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Supplier and Customer;

(d) combine Customer Personal Data with Personal Data that Supplier receives from or on behalf of any other person, or collects from its own interactions with Data Subjects, except as expressly permitted by Data Protection Laws; or

(e) attempt to re-identify any de-identified data derived from Customer Personal Data.

**3.4 Compliance Certification.** Supplier certifies that it understands the restrictions in Section 3.3 and will comply with them. Supplier shall notify Customer within five (5) Business Days if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws. Customer may then take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized Processing.

**3.5 Location of Processing.** Supplier shall Process and store Customer Personal Data only within the United States and Canada. Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data to, or permit remote access to it from, any other country without Customer's prior written consent.

**3.6 Carriers and Marketplaces.** Customer acknowledges that Supplier must disclose shipping names, addresses and contact details to the parcel carriers and freight providers selected or approved by Customer in order to perform the Services. Such disclosures, made solely for delivery or return of Customer Products, are permitted under this DPA. Carriers that receive data solely for these purposes are not Sub-processors of Supplier.

## 4. SUPPLIER PERSONNEL

**4.1 Confidentiality.** Supplier shall ensure that every individual authorized to Process Customer Personal Data is subject to a written duty of confidentiality, or an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality, that survives the end of that individual's engagement.

**4.2 Access Limitation.** Supplier shall limit access to Customer Personal Data to Supplier Personnel who need it to perform the Services. Access shall be granted on a least-privilege basis and revoked promptly when no longer required.

**4.3 Training.** Before granting any Supplier Personnel access to Customer Personal Data, and at least annually after that, Supplier shall provide them with training on data protection, information security and the handling of printed materials containing Personal Data. This requirement applies to temporary workers engaged for Peak Surge Services.

## 5. SECURITY

**5.1 Security Program.** Supplier shall implement and maintain a comprehensive written information security program. The program shall include the Security Measures and be designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of Customer Personal Data and of the WMS and other systems on which Customer Personal Data is Processed.

**5.2 Standard.** The Security Measures shall be appropriate to the nature of the Customer Personal Data and the risks presented by the Processing. They shall be no less rigorous than:

(a) the standards required by Data Protection Laws; and

(b) the standards generally accepted in the third-party logistics industry.

**5.3 Minimum Measures.** Without limiting Section 5.1, the Security Measures shall include, at a minimum:

(a) encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit over public networks and at rest;

(b) role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for all remote and privileged access;

(c) logging and monitoring of access to Customer Personal Data;

(d) timely patching and vulnerability management;

(e) secure disposal of shipping labels, packing slips, return labels and other printed materials containing Personal Data; and

(f) physical access controls at each Facility.

**5.4 Independent Assessment.** Supplier shall maintain a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment covering the WMS and the systems used to Process Customer Personal Data. Supplier shall provide that report to Customer on request and shall promptly remediate any material exceptions identified in it.

**5.5 No Degradation.** Supplier may update the Security Measures from time to time. No update shall materially decrease the overall level of protection afforded to Customer Personal Data.

## 6. SUB-PROCESSORS

**6.1 Authorization.** Customer authorizes Supplier to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 3. Supplier shall not engage any other Sub-processor without Customer's prior written authorization.

**6.2 New Sub-processors.** Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging a new Sub-processor. The notice shall state:

(a) the Sub-processor's identity and location; and

(b) the Processing it will perform.

Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If Customer objects, the parties shall discuss Customer's concerns in good faith. Supplier shall not engage the proposed Sub-processor to Process Customer Personal Data unless and until the objection is resolved.

**6.3 Sub-processor Obligations.** Before a Sub-processor Processes any Customer Personal Data, Supplier shall enter into a written agreement with it that imposes data protection obligations no less protective of Customer Personal Data than those in this DPA. Supplier shall remain fully responsible to Customer for each Sub-processor's performance of those obligations, consistent with Section 4.4 of the Agreement.

**6.4 Temporary Staffing.** Temporary staffing agencies engaged under Section 4.4 of the Agreement are not Sub-processors where their workers:

(a) Process Customer Personal Data only under the direct supervision of Supplier's employees; and

(b) do so only on Supplier's systems.

Supplier shall nonetheless ensure that such workers comply with Section 4.

## 7. ASSISTANCE AND DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

**7.1 Data Subject Requests.** Supplier shall notify Customer within five (5) Business Days after receiving any Data Subject Request relating to Customer Personal Data. Supplier shall not respond to the request except to direct the Data Subject to Customer, unless Customer instructs otherwise in writing.

**7.2 Assistance with Requests.** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Supplier shall provide Customer with reasonable assistance, through appropriate technical and organizational measures, to enable Customer to respond to Data Subject Requests within the time periods required by Data Protection Laws. Such assistance includes retrieving, correcting or deleting records in the WMS.

**7.3 Assessments and Regulatory Inquiries.** Supplier shall provide Customer with information and assistance reasonably requested in connection with:

(a) any data protection assessment, risk assessment or cybersecurity audit that Customer is required to conduct under Data Protection Laws; and

(b) any inquiry, investigation or request from a governmental authority relating to Customer Personal Data.

**7.4 Legal Requests.** If Supplier receives a subpoena, court order or other legal demand for Customer Personal Data, Supplier shall promptly notify Customer, unless legally prohibited from doing so, and shall reasonably cooperate with Customer's efforts to limit or oppose the disclosure. Supplier shall disclose only the Customer Personal Data that it is legally required to disclose.

## 8. PERSONAL DATA BREACH

**8.1 Notification.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing of any Personal Data Breach without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it. Notice shall be sent to the contact designated in Annex 1, with a copy to Customer's notice address under Section 18.1 of the Agreement.

**8.2 Content of Notice.** The notice shall describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects.

Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**8.3 Response.** Supplier shall promptly investigate, contain and remediate each Personal Data Breach and preserve all relevant logs and evidence. Supplier shall also provide Customer with all information reasonably requested about the breach and cooperate with Customer's investigation.

**8.4 Notifications to Third Parties.** Customer shall determine, in its sole discretion, whether to notify any affected individuals, regulators, payment card brands, marketplaces or other third parties. Customer shall also determine the content and timing of any such notification. Supplier shall not make any such notification, except as required by Data Protection Laws, without Customer's prior written approval of its content.

**8.5 Costs.** Supplier shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs incurred by Customer in responding to any Personal Data Breach arising from a breach of this DPA by Supplier, its Sub-processors or Supplier Personnel. Such costs include:

(a) forensic investigation;

(b) notification of affected individuals;

(c) call-center support;

(d) credit monitoring and identity-protection services; and

(e) fines or penalties imposed by a governmental authority or payment card brand.

## 9. AUDIT AND RECORDS

**9.1 Records.** Supplier shall maintain records of its Processing activities on behalf of Customer to the extent required by Data Protection Laws. Supplier shall make those records available to Customer on request.

**9.2 Information and Audits.** Supplier shall make available to Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA. Supplier shall also permit and contribute to audits and inspections of its Processing of Customer Personal Data, conducted by Customer or an independent auditor appointed by Customer and bound by obligations of confidentiality. Audits under this Section 9.2 shall be conducted:

(a) not more than once in any twelve (12) month period, except following a Personal Data Breach or at the request of a governmental authority;

(b) on not less than fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice; and

(c) during normal business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Supplier's operations.

**9.3 Use of Reports.** Customer may first request Supplier's most recent SOC 2 Type II report and completed security questionnaires. Customer shall consider in good faith whether those materials reasonably satisfy the purpose of a proposed audit.

**9.4 Remediation.** Supplier shall promptly remediate any non-compliance with this DPA identified in an audit and shall confirm completion of the remediation to Customer in writing.

## 10. LIABILITY

Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), strict liability or otherwise, is subject to Section 11 of the Agreement. Any reference in Section 11 of the Agreement to liability under the Agreement includes liability under this DPA.

## 11. TERM; RETURN AND DELETION

**11.1 Term.** This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date. It remains in effect for so long as Supplier or any Sub-processor Processes Customer Personal Data, including during any Transition Period.

**11.2 Return and Deletion.** On Customer's written election, Supplier shall return all Customer Personal Data to Customer, in accordance with Section 8.4 of the Agreement, upon expiration or termination of the Agreement.

Supplier shall then securely delete all Customer Personal Data, and cause each Sub-processor to do the same, within thirty (30) days after the later of:

(a) the end of the Transition Period; and

(b) the end of the ninety (90) day period described in Section 8.4 of the Agreement.

Supplier shall certify the deletion to Customer in writing on request.

**11.3 Retention Required by Law.** Supplier may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent and for so long as Applicable Law requires. During any such retention, Supplier shall:

(a) continue to protect the retained data in accordance with this DPA; and

(b) Process it only for the purpose of complying with that legal requirement.

**11.4 Survival.** Sections 3.3, 4.1, 5, 8, 10, 11.3, 12 and 13 survive expiration or termination of this DPA for so long as Supplier or any Sub-processor retains any Customer Personal Data, and thereafter to the extent necessary to give them effect.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This DPA, and any dispute, claim or controversy arising out of or relating to it or to the Processing of Customer Personal Data, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware. This applies without regard to any choice-of-law or conflict-of-laws rule or principle that would result in the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for the resolution of any such dispute, claim or controversy. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in such courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

## 13. GENERAL

**13.1 Changes in Data Protection Laws.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires a modification of this DPA, the parties shall negotiate in good faith an Amendment that addresses the change.

**13.2 Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 18.1 of the Agreement. Notices of a Personal Data Breach shall also be given to the contact designated in Annex 1.

**13.3 Amendments and Waivers.** This DPA may be amended only in accordance with Section 18.4 of the Agreement.

**13.4 Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any respect, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible. The remaining provisions of this DPA shall continue in full force and effect.

**13.5 Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature in accordance with Section 18.11 of the Agreement.

## ANNEX 1 – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**1. Subject Matter.** Processing of Customer Personal Data by Supplier in the performance of the Services under the Agreement and each Statement of Work.

**2. Nature and Purpose of Processing (Business Purposes).** Supplier Processes Customer Personal Data for the following purposes:

- receiving and processing Orders;
- picking, packing and shipping Customer Products to end customers;
- generating shipping labels, packing slips, gift messages and return labels;
- handing off shipments to carriers;
- receiving, inspecting and processing returns, exchanges and refunds data;
- handling delivery exceptions and address corrections;
- providing order-status and returns data to Customer;
- maintaining WMS records; and
- performing related reporting, auditing, quality control and security activities.

**3. Categories of Data Subjects.**

(a) Customer's end customers, including purchasers placing Orders through Customer's e-commerce storefronts and through third-party online marketplaces.

(b) Gift recipients and other ship-to addressees.

(c) Individuals initiating returns or exchanges.

(d) Customer's employees and contractors who interact with Supplier's systems.

**4. Categories of Customer Personal Data.**

(a) Names, shipping and billing addresses, email addresses and telephone numbers.

(b) Order numbers, order contents, sizes and quantities, order dates and order values.

(c) Marketplace buyer identifiers.

(d) Gift messages and delivery instructions.

(e) Return reasons, return condition notes and refund or exchange status.

(f) Carrier tracking numbers and delivery confirmations.

(g) Business contact details and user credentials of Customer personnel.

Supplier does not receive payment card numbers, financial account numbers, government identification numbers, health information or precise geolocation data. Customer shall not transmit such data to Supplier.

**5. Frequency and Duration.** Processing occurs on a continuous basis for the Term and any Transition Period, and thereafter until deletion under Section 11.2 of this DPA.

**6. Retention.** Supplier retains Order and returns records in the WMS for the Term, subject to Customer's instructions and Section 11 of this DPA.

**7. Breach Notification Contacts.**

Customer: Director of Information Security, Kaigen Outdoor Co., security@kaigenoutdoor.com, with a copy to legal@kaigenoutdoor.com.

Supplier: Chief Information Security Officer, Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC, security@dreisur.com.

## ANNEX 2 – SECURITY MEASURES

**1. Governance.** Supplier maintains a designated information security officer, written security policies reviewed at least annually, and a documented risk assessment process.

**2. Access Control.** Supplier implements:

- unique user IDs;
- role-based access aligned to job function;
- multi-factor authentication for remote and privileged access;
- quarterly access reviews; and
- revocation of access within one (1) Business Day after a user's separation, or by the end of the shift for temporary workers.

**3. Encryption.** Supplier encrypts data:

- in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; and
- at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent industry-standard algorithm.

**4. Network and Application Security.** Supplier maintains:

- firewalls, network segmentation of WMS environments, and endpoint detection and response on servers and workstations;
- secure configuration baselines; and
- annual third-party penetration testing of internet-facing systems.

**5. Vulnerability Management.** Supplier applies critical security patches within fourteen (14) days and high-severity patches within thirty (30) days after release, subject to documented compensating controls where patching is not feasible.

**6. Logging and Monitoring.** Supplier centrally logs authentication events and access to Customer Personal Data, retains logs for at least twelve (12) months, and reviews alerts on a continuous basis.

**7. Physical Security.** Each Facility has:

- badge-controlled entry;
- visitor logs;
- CCTV coverage of docks, pack stations and server rooms; and
- restricted access to areas where printed materials containing Personal Data are stored.

**8. Printed Materials.** Supplier stores misprinted labels, voided packing slips and returned shipping documents in locked containers and destroys them by cross-cut shredding or certified destruction.

**9. Business Continuity.** Supplier maintains daily encrypted backups of WMS data, with tested restoration procedures, as part of the business continuity and disaster recovery plan required by Section 18.2 of the Agreement.

**10. Incident Response.** Supplier maintains a written incident response plan, tested at least annually, that addresses the notification obligations in Section 8 of this DPA.
