# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 2, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Kaigen Outdoor Co.**, a Washington corporation with its principal place of business at 1420 Ashcombe Street, Suite 600, Seattle, Washington 98101 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dreisur Fulfillment Services LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 300 Harborline Drive, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**").

Customer and Supplier are each referred to in this Amendment as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier provides e-commerce fulfillment, returns processing and peak surge services to Customer.

B. The Agreement includes as Exhibit A a Data Processing Addendum between the parties dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**DPA**") and as Exhibit B a Service Level Schedule between the parties dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**SLA Schedule**").

C. Section 18.4 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument that expressly refers to the Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

D. The parties have reviewed the operation of the Agreement during its first contract year. They now wish to amend the Agreement to consolidate certain data provisions, to update certain operational, invoicing and notice provisions, and to add provisions on packaging and supply chain compliance, in each case on the terms of this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Amendment where the context requires. References in this Amendment to Sections are to sections of the Agreement unless otherwise stated.

**1.3 References to the Agreement.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar import, and each reference to the Agreement in the DPA, the SLA Schedule, any Statement of Work or the Guaranty, means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 9

**2.1 Data Security.** Section 9.2 is deleted in its entirety. On and after the Amendment Effective Date, Supplier's obligations with respect to the security of Customer Data and of the systems on which Customer Data is Processed are governed exclusively by the DPA.

**2.2 Processing of Personal Data.** Section 9.3 is deleted and replaced with the following:

> **9.3 Processing of Personal Data.** The parties shall comply with the DPA in respect of all Personal Data Processed by Supplier on behalf of Customer under this Agreement. Supplier shall ensure that each Subcontractor that Processes Customer Data does so in accordance with the DPA.

**2.3 Heading.** The heading of Section 9 is deleted and replaced with "CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION."

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 3

**3.1 Packaging and Sustainability.** A new Section 3.8 is added to the Agreement immediately after Section 3.7, as follows:

> **3.8 Packaging and Sustainability.**
>
> (a) Supplier shall use the packaging materials, void fill, mailers and cartons specified in Customer's packaging guide as provided to Supplier in writing from time to time (the "**Packaging Guide**"). Supplier shall not substitute any packaging material without Customer's prior written approval, except that Supplier may, on notice to Customer, use a substitute of equal or better quality and equal or better recycled content where a specified material is temporarily unavailable.
>
> (b) Supplier shall right-size outbound cartons and mailers to the dimensions of each Order to the extent the packaging equipment at the applicable Facility permits, with the objective of minimizing dimensional weight and void fill.
>
> (c) Supplier shall separate, bale and recycle corrugated cardboard, stretch film and other recyclable packaging waste generated at each Facility in the performance of the Services, using recycling vendors licensed under Applicable Law.
>
> (d) Supplier shall deliver to Customer, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, a written report setting out, for that quarter and for each Facility, (i) the quantities of each packaging material used for Customer's Orders; (ii) the estimated recycled content of those materials; and (iii) the weight of packaging waste recycled and disposed of. Supplier shall provide such supporting data as Customer reasonably requests to support Customer's sustainability reporting and its compliance with extended producer responsibility laws for packaging.
>
> (e) Any change to the Packaging Guide that would materially increase Supplier's cost of performing the Services shall be handled as a change request under Section 2.4.

## 4. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 4

**4.1 Temporary Staffing Agencies.** Section 4.4 is amended by adding the following at the end of that Section:

> Supplier shall notify Customer in writing, no later than September 1 of each calendar year, of the identity of each temporary staffing agency that Supplier intends to engage in connection with Peak Surge Services in the upcoming year, and shall promptly notify Customer of any additional temporary staffing agency engaged after that date. Supplier shall ensure that each temporary staffing agency it engages complies with Applicable Law relating to wages and hours, workers' compensation and employment eligibility, and Section 4.3 shall apply to temporary workers supplied by such agencies as if they were Supplier Personnel employed by Supplier.

**4.2 Account Management.** Section 4.2 is amended by adding the following at the end of that Section:

> Supplier shall also designate, for each Facility, a site operations lead who shall be responsible for the day-to-day performance of the Services at that Facility and who shall be available to Customer's operations contacts during the Facility's operating hours.

## 5. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 7

**5.1 Invoicing.** Section 7.2 is deleted and replaced with the following:

> **7.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Services performed in the preceding calendar month. Each invoice shall set out the Services performed, the applicable rates and quantities by Facility, any service credits and other credits applied, any pass-through carrier charges and packaging materials billed at cost, and any applicable taxes, and shall be accompanied by such supporting data as Customer may reasonably require. Supplier shall submit each invoice, together with its supporting data, through Customer's electronic accounts payable portal or such other electronic means as Customer designates in writing. An invoice is received for the purposes of Section 7.3 on the date on which it is submitted in accordance with this Section 7.2. Supplier shall submit all invoices for Services performed in a calendar year no later than March 31 of the following calendar year, other than invoices for Services performed during the Transition Period.

**5.2 Method of Payment.** Section 7.3 is amended by adding the following at the end of that Section:

> Customer shall make all payments under this Agreement by ACH transfer to the bank account designated by Supplier in writing on its letterhead and signed by an authorized officer of Supplier. Supplier shall give Customer not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any change in its designated bank account, and Customer may verify any such change by telephone with Supplier's account manager or finance contact before making any payment to a new account.

**5.3 Invoice Disputes.** Section 7.4 is amended by adding the following at the end of that Section:

> If the parties are unable to resolve a payment dispute within the thirty (30) day period described in this Section 7.4, either party may refer the dispute to escalation under Section 17.3.

## 6. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 15

**6.1 Supply Chain Compliance.** Section 15.3 is amended by adding the following new clause (f) immediately after clause (e):

> (f) Supplier will maintain, and will provide to Customer on reasonable request, records sufficient to identify the temporary staffing agencies and Subcontractors engaged in the performance of the Services, and will cooperate with Customer, at Customer's reasonable request, in responding to inquiries from customs or other governmental authorities, marketplaces and wholesale customers concerning labor practices in Customer's supply chain to the extent such inquiries relate to the Services.

## 9. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

**9.1 Effectiveness.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment Effective Date. Except as expressly provided in this Amendment, the amendments made by this Amendment apply to Services performed on and after the Amendment Effective Date.

**9.2 Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement, the DPA, the SLA Schedule and each Statement of Work remain unchanged and in full force and effect in accordance with their terms, and are ratified and confirmed by the parties.

**9.3 Accrued Rights.** Nothing in this Amendment affects any right or remedy of either party that accrued before the Amendment Effective Date, or any invoice issued, payment made or service credit applied before the Amendment Effective Date.

**9.4 Implementation.** Supplier shall:

(a) deliver the first quarterly packaging report under new Section 3.8(d) for the calendar quarter ending September 30, 2026;

(b) deliver the first notice of temporary staffing agencies under amended Section 4.4 no later than September 1, 2026; and

(c) begin submitting invoices in accordance with amended Section 7.2 with the invoice for Services performed in June 2026, provided that Customer has given Supplier access to its electronic accounts payable portal no later than June 20, 2026. If Customer has not given such access by that date, Supplier shall continue to invoice in the manner previously used by the parties until fifteen (15) Business Days after access is given.

**9.5 Site Operations Leads.** Supplier shall designate the site operations leads required by amended Section 4.2 within ten (10) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date and shall notify Customer in writing of the name and contact details of each of them.
