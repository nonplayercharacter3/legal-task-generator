# COMPONENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Component Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Breiraind Lift Components Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Taliv Elevator Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 212 West 37th Street, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10018 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer is an elevator maintenance, repair and modernization contractor. It services vertical transportation equipment in commercial, residential and institutional buildings within its New York Metropolitan Area maintenance portfolio (the "**Metro Portfolio**").

B. Supplier designs, manufactures, sources and distributes replacement components for elevator and escalator equipment. These include door operators, controllers, traction machine parts and safety gear.

C. Customer wishes to obtain a reliable, priced source of replacement components to support the Metro Portfolio. Supplier wishes to supply those components on the terms of this Agreement.

D. As a condition of Supplier extending credit under this Agreement, Taliv Holdings LLC, the parent of Customer, is delivering a guaranty of Customer's payment obligations (the "**Parent Guaranty**").

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. Unless terminated earlier under Section 15, it continues for 36 months from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for a Renewal Term of two (2) years (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). It shall renew in the same way at the end of each Renewal Term. Renewal does not occur if either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the end of the then-current term. A notice of non-renewal is timely only if given at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and all Renewal Terms are together the "**Term**."

2.3 **Pricing for Renewal Terms.** The pricing in effect at the end of the Initial Term or a Renewal Term continues into the next Renewal Term, subject to adjustment under Section 5.3.

## 3. SUPPLY COMMITMENT

3.1 **Requirements.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase from Supplier all of its requirements of Covered Products for use in Serviced Units. Customer shall not purchase Covered Products for use in Serviced Units from any other source, except as permitted by Section 3.3.

3.2 **Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Customer shall purchase Covered Products with an aggregate net invoice value at least equal to the minimum annual purchase commitment stated in the Order Form.

(a) Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a statement of aggregate net purchases for that Contract Year.

(b) If aggregate net purchases fall short of the minimum annual purchase commitment, Customer shall pay Supplier the shortfall amount stated in the Order Form within forty-five (45) days after receipt of Supplier's invoice.

(c) Purchases that Customer cannot make because of a Supplier failure to supply, an allocation under Section 4.6, or a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier are credited toward the minimum annual purchase commitment.

3.3 **Permitted Alternative Sourcing.** Customer may purchase a Covered Product from a third party in any of the following cases:

(a) Supplier notifies Customer that it cannot supply that Covered Product within its Lead Time;

(b) Supplier fails to deliver that Covered Product within ten (10) Business Days after its scheduled delivery date;

(c) the Covered Product is required on an emergency basis to restore a Serviced Unit that has been removed from service, and Supplier cannot deliver within the emergency response time stated in the Order Form; or

(d) an owner, manager or authority having jurisdiction for a building requires a specific manufacturer's component.

Customer shall limit any purchase under this Section 3.3 to the quantity reasonably required to address the circumstance. Customer shall give Supplier written notice of each such purchase within five (5) Business Days after making it.

3.4 **Supply Obligation.** Supplier shall supply all Covered Products ordered by Customer under Purchase Orders that conform to this Agreement, within the applicable Lead Time.

3.5 **No Agency.** Nothing in this Section 3 makes either Party the agent, distributor or franchisee of the other.

## 4. FORECASTS AND ORDERING

4.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated requirements of Covered Products for the following six (6) months (each, a "**Forecast**"). Forecasts are for planning purposes only. Except as provided in Section 4.2, they do not commit Customer to purchase any quantity.

4.2 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall maintain at its distribution facility, at its own cost, a safety stock of each Covered Product identified in the Order Form as a "stocked item." The quantity of safety stock shall be at least the average monthly quantity in Customer's most recent three (3) Forecasts.

(a) If this Agreement expires or is terminated, other than by Customer under Section 15.2, Customer shall purchase at the then-current price any remaining safety stock of stocked items that Supplier maintained specifically for Customer.

(b) This purchase obligation is limited to one (1) month of forecast requirements.

4.3 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Covered Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier. Each Purchase Order shall specify:
   - the Covered Products by part number;
   - the quantities;
   - the requested delivery date;
   - the Delivery Point; and
   - for emergency orders, the affected Serviced Unit.

Customer may issue Purchase Orders through Supplier's electronic ordering portal, by electronic data interchange or by email to the address stated in the Order Form.

4.4 **Acceptance of Purchase Orders.** Supplier shall acknowledge each Purchase Order within two (2) Business Days after receipt, or within four (4) hours for emergency orders. A Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to the Lead Times is deemed accepted if Supplier does not reject it in writing within that period. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if:

(a) it specifies a delivery date shorter than the Lead Time; or

(b) Customer is then in payment default under Section 6.

4.5 **Changes and Cancellation.** Customer may change or cancel a Purchase Order without charge by written notice delivered before Supplier ships the Covered Products.

(a) For Covered Products that Supplier has manufactured or modified specifically to Customer's order, Customer shall reimburse Supplier's documented, non-cancellable costs incurred before receipt of the cancellation notice.

(b) Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate those costs.

4.6 **Allocation.** If Supplier's supply of a Covered Product is constrained, Supplier shall allocate available supply to Customer at a level not less favorable than that given to Supplier's other customers for the same product. The allocation shall be in proportion to Customer's average purchases over the preceding six (6) months. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any constraint and its expected duration.

4.7 **Terms of Purchase Orders.** This Agreement governs each Purchase Order. Any additional or different terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice, shipping document or portal terms are of no effect, except for the descriptive information required by Section 4.3.

## 5. PRICING

5.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay for Covered Products at the prices stated in the Order Form. Prices are in United States dollars. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, prices include:
   - standard packaging;
   - labeling;
   - Documentation; and
   - delivery to the Delivery Point under Section 7.1.

5.2 **Firm Pricing.** Prices are firm for the first Contract Year.

5.3 **Annual Adjustment.** Supplier may adjust the prices of Covered Products effective on the first day of each Contract Year after the first.

(a) Supplier shall give Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of any adjustment.

(b) No annual adjustment may exceed the lesser of:
   - three percent (3%); and
   - the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Elevator and Moving Stairway Manufacturing (or a reasonably equivalent successor index), published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, over the most recent twelve (12) months for which data is available.

(c) Supplier shall provide supporting index data with each notice of adjustment.

5.4 **Most Favored Pricing.** If, during the Term, Supplier sells a Covered Product to any other customer in the United States at a lower price on comparable volume and terms, Supplier shall offer that lower price to Customer. The lower price applies from the date of the comparable sale. Supplier shall issue a credit for any overpayment.

5.5 **New Products.** The Parties may agree in writing on pricing for additional components that Customer wishes to purchase. Any such component becomes a Covered Product when the Order Form is amended under Section 10.1.

## 6. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of Covered Products. Each invoice shall state:
   - the Purchase Order number;
   - part numbers;
   - quantities;
   - unit prices;
   - applicable Taxes; and
   - the Delivery Point.

6.2 **Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Payment shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

6.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier in writing of the basis for the dispute before the payment due date.

(a) Customer shall pay the undisputed portion of the invoice when due.

(b) The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

(c) Customer shall pay any amount found to be owing within ten (10) Business Days after resolution.

6.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest from the due date until paid at the lesser of:
   - one percent (1%) per month; and
   - the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law.

If any undisputed amount remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after its due date, Supplier may suspend shipment of further Covered Products after giving Customer ten (10) Business Days' written notice. Any suspension continues only until the amount is paid.

6.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, excise, value-added and similar taxes ("**Taxes**"). Customer shall pay all Taxes that are properly invoiced. Customer is not responsible for taxes based on Supplier's net income, property or employees. If Customer provides a valid resale or exemption certificate, Supplier shall not charge Taxes covered by that certificate.

6.6 **Set-off.** Customer may set off against amounts payable to Supplier any undisputed credit, refund or amount owed by Supplier to Customer under this Agreement.

6.7 **Parent Guaranty.** On or before the Effective Date, Customer shall deliver to Supplier the Parent Guaranty, duly executed by Taliv Holdings LLC.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver Covered Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Delivery Point. Supplier is responsible for freight, insurance and handling to the Delivery Point.

7.2 **Delivery Dates.** Supplier shall deliver each Purchase Order on the delivery date stated in it. Supplier shall notify Customer immediately if it expects any delay and shall state the revised delivery date. If a delay is caused by Supplier, Supplier shall ship by expedited means at its own cost when Customer requests it.

7.3 **Emergency Orders.** For Purchase Orders identified as emergency orders, Supplier shall ship within the emergency response time stated in the Order Form.

7.4 **Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier shall package Covered Products to prevent damage in transit and storage. Packaging shall comply with Applicable Law and any packaging requirements in the Quality Agreement. Each package shall be labeled with:
   - the part number;
   - the description;
   - the quantity;
   - the lot or serial number;
   - the date of manufacture; and
   - the Purchase Order number.

7.5 **Documentation.** Supplier shall ship each Covered Product with its current Documentation. For safety gear, governors, controllers and other components for which Applicable Law requires certification, Supplier shall also furnish the applicable certificates of conformity or listing.

7.6 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Covered Products pass to Customer upon delivery to the Delivery Point.

7.7 **Partial and Excess Shipments.** Supplier shall not make partial shipments without Customer's prior consent. Customer may return excess quantities at Supplier's cost and risk, or may keep them and pay for them at the contract price.

## 8. INSPECTION

8.1 **Incoming Inspection.** Customer may inspect Covered Products upon receipt at the Delivery Point. Incoming Inspection is conducted under the procedures in the Quality Agreement.

8.2 **Rejected Shipments.** Customer may reject Covered Products that fail Incoming Inspection by notifying Supplier in writing. The notice shall describe the reason for rejection.

(a) Supplier shall arrange to collect rejected Covered Products at its own cost.

(b) At Customer's election, Supplier shall either replace them within the Lead Time (or within five (5) Business Days for stocked items) or issue a credit for their price.

8.3 **No Obligation to Test.** Customer's inspection, or its failure to inspect, does not relieve Supplier of its obligations under this Agreement. Customer has no obligation to perform any test other than those required under the Quality Agreement.

8.4 **Supplier Quality Obligations.** Supplier shall maintain the quality management system, process controls and traceability records required by the Quality Agreement. Supplier shall permit audits as provided in the Quality Agreement.

This warranty applies for a period of 24 months following installation in a Serviced Unit, or thirty (30) months following delivery, whichever ends first. During that period Supplier shall:

(a) replace any Nonconforming Product (as defined in the Quality Agreement) at no charge; and

(b) reimburse Customer for the reasonable field labor and lift-truck, rigging and travel costs incurred in removing the Nonconforming Product and installing the replacement, at the labor rates stated in the Order Form.

Supplier shall ship replacements within the Lead Time, or within forty-eight (48) hours for stocked items.

9.2 **Title and Non-Infringement.** Supplier warrants that:

(a) it will convey good title to each Covered Product, free of all liens, security interests and encumbrances; and

To the extent permitted, Supplier shall pass through to Customer any manufacturer warranty that exceeds the warranties in this Section 9. On request, Supplier shall reasonably assist Customer in enforcing any such warranty.

## 10. PRODUCT CHANGES, OBSOLESCENCE AND RECALLS

10.1 **Additions and Deletions.** The Parties may add components to, or remove components from, the Covered Products by written amendment to the Order Form signed by both Parties.

10.2 **Engineering Changes.** Supplier shall not make any change to the following without at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to Customer:
   - the form, fit, function, materials, manufacturing site or source of supply of a Covered Product;
   - a change that affects its code compliance or certification; or
   - a change that affects its interchangeability with prior versions.

Where the Quality Agreement requires Customer's approval of a change, Supplier shall not implement it without that approval.

10.3 **Discontinuation.** Supplier shall give Customer at least twelve (12) months' prior written notice before discontinuing any Covered Product. After receiving the notice:

(a) Customer may place last-time-buy Purchase Orders for that Covered Product at the then-current price, in quantities reasonably related to its Forecasts; and

(b) Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to identify a code-compliant replacement and shall offer it at a price not exceeding the price of the discontinued product plus any demonstrable increase in cost.

10.4 **Spare Parts Availability.** For at least seven (7) years after Supplier's last shipment of a Covered Product to Customer, Supplier shall make available repair parts and service support for each controller and door operator model supplied under this Agreement.

10.5 **Recalls and Safety Notices.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing within two (2) Business Days after it becomes aware of any of the following:
   - a defect in a Covered Product that creates a risk of injury, entrapment or uncontrolled car movement;
   - a recall or safety bulletin issued by Supplier or a manufacturer; or
   - an inquiry by a governmental authority concerning the safety of a Covered Product.

Supplier shall bear the costs of any recall or field retrofit required because of a defect in a Covered Product. These costs include replacement parts, reasonable field labor at the rates stated in the Order Form, and notification costs. Customer shall cooperate reasonably in carrying out any recall.

Supplier shall provide country-of-origin information for each Covered Product on request.

11.3 **Conflict Minerals and Restricted Substances.** On Customer's reasonable request, Supplier shall provide declarations on the content of conflict minerals and restricted substances in the Covered Products.

Supplier shall retain these records for at least six (6) years after the end of the Contract Year to which they relate. On reasonable notice and not more than once per Contract Year, Customer or an independent auditor may audit records relating to pricing and invoicing.

(a) Supplier shall promptly refund any overcharge identified by the audit.

(b) If the overcharge exceeds three percent (3%) for the audited period, Supplier shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable audit costs.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **Termination for Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

15.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. Where this Agreement provides that a breach entitles a Party to terminate for cause, that Party may terminate under this Section 15.2 by written notice to the breaching Party.

15.3 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party does any of the following:
   - becomes insolvent;
