## 11. SUPPLEMENTAL INFORMATION ON PRODUCTS COVERAGE

11.1 **Products Covered.** Products-completed operations coverage under the Commercial General Liability policy applies to "your product" as defined in the policy. It includes components that the named insured sources from third-party manufacturers and sub-tier suppliers and resells or incorporates into its own products. The products shown in the named insured's current catalog are within the scope of coverage, including the categories below:

| Product Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Door equipment | Door operator assemblies, control boards, motor and encoder assemblies, door restrictors, light curtain door edges, hoistway door interlocks, hanger and track kits |
| Controllers and electrical components | Controller main boards, complete traction and hydraulic controllers, AC regenerative drives, serial communication boards, fixture interface boards, safety relays, hoistway position systems |
| Traction machine parts | Drive sheaves, brake coil assemblies, brake lining kits, encoders, thrust bearing and seal kits, hoist motors |
| Rope | Hoist rope and governor rope |
| Safety gear | Overspeed governors, governor tension sheaves, car safeties, rope grippers, buffers, final limit switches, pit stop switches and inspection stations |

11.2 **Territory.** The coverage territory for products-completed operations is worldwide for products sold for use in the United States, its territories and possessions, Puerto Rico and Canada. Suits must be brought in those places, or the policy's other territorial provisions must apply.

11.3 **Elevator Exclusions.** The Commercial General Liability policy does not contain an elevator, escalator or vertical transportation equipment exclusion applicable to the named insured's products. The policy's standard "elevator" provisions in the definition of "loading or unloading" and in the damage to property exclusion do not limit coverage for bodily injury or property damage caused by a defect in the named insured's products.

11.4 **Recall Expense.** The Commercial General Liability policy contains the standard exclusion for recall of products, work or impaired property. Product recall expense coverage, if any, is provided under a separate policy that is not described in this certificate.

## 12. SUPPLEMENTAL INFORMATION ON CYBER AND PRIVACY COVERAGE

12.1 **Third-Party Service Providers.** The Cyber and Privacy Liability policy defines "computer system" to include systems operated by a third-party service provider under written contract with the named insured to host, store or process data on the named insured's behalf.

12.2 **Contractual Liability.** The policy provides coverage for liability assumed under the named insured's written contracts for breach of confidentiality or data protection obligations, to the extent the named insured would have been liable in the absence of the contract. It also covers notification and remediation costs that the named insured is contractually required to reimburse, subject to the policy's terms and the limits in Section 5.6.

12.3 **Claims-Made Basis.** Because the Cyber and Privacy Liability policy is written on a claims-made basis, coverage applies to claims first made against the named insured during the policy period, or during any applicable extended reporting period, for wrongful acts on or after the retroactive date shown in Section 5.6.
