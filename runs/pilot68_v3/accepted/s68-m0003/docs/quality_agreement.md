# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Quality Agreement Effective Date**") by and between:

**Breiraind Lift Components Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Taliv Elevator Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 212 West 37th Street, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10018 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties have entered into a Component Supply Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 (as amended from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier supplies replacement elevator and escalator components to Customer.

B. The Agreement refers to this Quality Agreement for several matters:
   - the procedures for Incoming Inspection;
   - packaging requirements;
   - Supplier's quality management system, process controls and traceability records;
   - audits;
   - approval of certain changes; and
   - root cause analysis and corrective action plans.

C. The Parties are entering into this Quality Agreement to set out those requirements.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement. These include "Affiliate," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Covered Products," "Delivery Point," "Documentation," "Lead Time," "Order Form," "Purchase Order," "Serviced Units" and "Specifications."

1.2 **Certificate of Conformity.** "**Certificate of Conformity**" means a document signed by an authorized Supplier quality representative that:
   - identifies a shipment by Purchase Order number, part number and lot or serial number; and
   - certifies that the Covered Products in that shipment were manufactured, inspected and tested in accordance with the Specifications and this Quality Agreement.

For Covered Products that require third-party certification, listing or type-testing, the term includes the applicable certificates.

1.3 **Incoming Inspection.** "**Incoming Inspection**" means the receiving inspection that Customer performs under Section 8 on Covered Products delivered to Customer's receiving facility.

1.4 **Nonconforming Product.** "**Nonconforming Product**" means a Covered Product that fails Incoming Inspection at Customer's receiving facility within ten (10) Business Days of receipt.

1.5 **Critical Safety Component.** "**Critical Safety Component**" means any Covered Product in the Safety Gear category of the Product Schedule in the Order Form. The term also includes:
   - each complete controller and drive (Part Nos. CT-5100, CH-5200, CD-6020 and CD-6040);
   - each hoistway door interlock assembly; and
   - each safety relay.

1.6 **Corrective Action Plan.** "**Corrective Action Plan**" means a written plan that Supplier prepares under Section 10. It describes the root cause of a quality problem, the containment actions taken, the permanent corrective actions planned or taken, and the steps that will verify their effectiveness.

1.7 **Quality Manual.** "**Quality Manual**" means Supplier's documented quality manual describing its quality management system, as updated from time to time under Section 3.3.

1.8 **Sub-tier Supplier.** "**Sub-tier Supplier**" means any third-party manufacturer, subcontractor or vendor that supplies Supplier with a Covered Product or with a material, part or sub-assembly incorporated into a Covered Product.

1.9 **Field Performance Report.** "**Field Performance Report**" has the meaning given in Section 11.1.

## 2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

2.1 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Covered Products that Supplier supplies to Customer under the Agreement. This includes Covered Products sourced from Sub-tier Suppliers.

2.2 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the quality responsibilities of each Party for the Covered Products. It forms part of the arrangement between the Parties under the Agreement.

2.3 **Supplier Responsibility.** Supplier is responsible for the quality of the Covered Products, and for their conformance to the Specifications and to Applicable Law. Customer's performance of Incoming Inspection, or its approval of any document, sample or change under this Quality Agreement, does not transfer that responsibility to Customer.

## 3. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

3.1 **Certification.** Throughout the Term, Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015, or a successor standard, by an accredited registrar. The certification shall cover each facility at which Covered Products are manufactured, assembled, tested or stored for shipment to Customer.

3.2 **Notice of Certification Changes.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing within five (5) Business Days after any of the following:
   - the suspension, withdrawal or lapse of its certification;
   - a material reduction in the scope of its certification; or
   - a major nonconformity raised by its registrar that relates to the manufacture or testing of Covered Products.

3.3 **Quality Manual.** Supplier shall maintain a current Quality Manual. Supplier shall give Customer a copy on the Quality Agreement Effective Date and a copy of each revision within thirty (30) days after it takes effect.

3.4 **Process Controls.** Supplier shall maintain documented process controls for the manufacture, assembly, inspection and testing of each Covered Product. These include:

(a) work instructions for each manufacturing and assembly operation;

(b) inspection and test plans that identify the characteristics to be checked, the methods and the acceptance criteria;

(c) calibration of all inspection, measuring and test equipment, traceable to national standards;

(d) control of nonconforming material within Supplier's own operations, including segregation, disposition and records; and

(e) training and qualification records for personnel performing inspection, testing and adjustment of Critical Safety Components.

3.5 **Final Inspection and Testing.** Supplier shall perform final inspection and functional testing of each Covered Product before shipment, in accordance with its inspection and test plans. For Critical Safety Components, Supplier shall perform the following:
   - 100% functional testing; and
   - for overspeed governors and car safeties, the tripping-speed and performance tests required by ASME A17.1/CSA B44.

Supplier shall record the results of these tests by serial number.

## 4. SPECIFICATIONS AND DOCUMENTATION

4.1 **Specifications.** Supplier shall maintain controlled copies of the Specifications for each Covered Product. Supplier shall make the current revision of any Specification available to Customer on request. Specifications include:
   - drawings;
   - bills of material;
   - performance characteristics; and
   - code compliance requirements.

4.2 **Customer Requirements.** Customer may state additional technical requirements for a Covered Product in a Purchase Order. Examples include job survey data for configured controllers. Supplier shall review those requirements before accepting the Purchase Order. Once Supplier accepts the Purchase Order, those requirements form part of the Specifications for that order.

4.3 **Documentation Control.** Supplier shall ensure that the Documentation shipped with each Covered Product is the current revision applicable to that part number and configuration. Supplier shall notify Customer of each revision to installation, adjustment or wiring Documentation for controllers and door operators that affects field installation or maintenance procedures.

4.4 **Certificates of Conformity.** Supplier shall include a Certificate of Conformity with each shipment. For Critical Safety Components, Supplier shall also include the certification, listing or type-test documentation required by Section 7.5 of the Agreement and Section 4.5 of the Order Form.

## 5. CHANGE CONTROL

5.1 **Notified Changes.** Section 10.2 of the Agreement requires Supplier to give at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of certain engineering changes. Supplier shall give that notice using the change notification form attached as Exhibit A. The notice shall include:
   - the affected part numbers;
   - a description of the change and the reason for it;
   - the proposed effective date and the first affected lot or serial number;
   - an assessment of its effect on form, fit, function, interchangeability and code compliance; and
   - any qualification or test data supporting the change.

5.2 **Changes Requiring Customer Approval.** Supplier shall not implement any of the following changes without Customer's prior written approval:

(a) any change to a Critical Safety Component that affects its certification, listing, type-test or code compliance;

(b) any change in the manufacturing site for a Critical Safety Component or a door operator assembly;

(c) any change in the Sub-tier Supplier of a controller main board, drive, door operator motor and encoder assembly, or safety gear assembly; and

(d) any change that makes a Covered Product non-interchangeable with the prior version installed in Serviced Units.

5.3 **Approval Process.** Customer shall respond to a request for approval under Section 5.2 within thirty (30) days after it receives a complete change notification.

(a) Customer may approve the change, approve it subject to conditions, request additional information or samples, or withhold approval on reasonable technical grounds.

(b) If Customer requests samples, Supplier shall provide up to three (3) samples of the changed Covered Product at no charge.

(c) Customer's thirty (30) day response period begins again when Customer receives the requested information or samples.

5.4 **Identification of Changed Product.** Supplier shall identify the first shipment of each changed Covered Product in a way that allows Customer to distinguish it from prior versions. Supplier may do so with a revision letter on the label or a notation on the Certificate of Conformity.

## 6. TRACEABILITY AND RECORDS

6.1 **Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain traceability for each Covered Product. This means traceability from the shipment and Purchase Order back to the manufacturing lot or serial number, and from that lot or serial number back to the raw material lots and Sub-tier Supplier lots used. For Critical Safety Components, controllers and door operator assemblies, Supplier shall maintain traceability by individual serial number.

6.2 **Quality Records.** Supplier shall maintain the following records:
   - inspection and test results;
   - calibration records;
   - Certificates of Conformity;
   - change notifications and approvals;
   - Corrective Action Plans; and
   - internal nonconformance records.

Supplier shall maintain these records for the retention period stated in Section 11.5 of the Agreement. For Critical Safety Components, the records shall be kept for at least ten (10) years after shipment.

6.3 **Access to Records.** Supplier shall provide copies of quality records relating to specific Covered Products within five (5) Business Days after Customer's request. Where an authority having jurisdiction has requested the records in connection with an accident, entrapment or inspection, Supplier shall provide them within two (2) Business Days.

6.4 **Recall Support.** Supplier's traceability records shall be sufficient to identify, within two (2) Business Days, every shipment to Customer that contains a Covered Product from an affected lot or serial range. This supports notice and recall activities under Section 10.5 of the Agreement.

## 7. PACKAGING AND LABELING

7.1 **Packaging Requirements.** In addition to the requirements of Section 7.4 of the Agreement, Supplier shall package Covered Products as follows:

(a) electronic assemblies, including controller boards, drives and door operator control boards, shall be shipped in electrostatic discharge protective packaging;

(b) machined surfaces of sheaves, brake components and bearings shall be protected against corrosion and impact;

(c) hoist and governor rope shall be shipped on reels or in coils, lubricated and protected from moisture and contamination, with the rope construction, diameter, length and manufacturer's lot identified on each reel or coil; and

(d) each package weighing more than fifty (50) pounds shall be palletized or skidded and marked with its gross weight.

7.2 **Labels.** Each package label shall be legible and durable and shall carry a bar code encoding the part number and lot or serial number. For Critical Safety Components, the label shall also identify the package as containing safety equipment.

7.3 **Shipping Documents.** Each shipment shall include a packing list showing the Purchase Order number, part numbers, quantities and lot or serial numbers, together with the Certificate of Conformity and Documentation.

## 8. INCOMING INSPECTION

8.1 **Location.** Customer performs Incoming Inspection at its receiving facility. As of the Quality Agreement Effective Date, that facility is located at 47-25 34th Street, Long Island City, New York 11101, as stated in Section 9.2 of the Order Form.

8.2 **Inspection Procedure.** Incoming Inspection consists of the following:

(a) verification that the part numbers, quantities and lot or serial numbers received match the packing list and the Purchase Order;

(b) visual inspection of packaging and of the Covered Products for shipping damage, corrosion, missing parts and obvious defects;

(c) verification that the Certificate of Conformity, Documentation and any required certification or type-test documentation are present and refer to the Covered Products received;

(d) verification of labeling under Section 7; and

(e) for Covered Products identified in Exhibit B, the dimensional checks or bench tests listed for that part number.

8.3 **Sampling.** For Covered Products shipped in quantities greater than ten (10) units of a single part number, Customer may perform the checks in Section 8.2(e) on a sample basis, using the sampling plan in Exhibit B. If any sample fails, Customer may inspect the entire lot.

8.4 **Shipments to Job Sites.** For Covered Products delivered to a job site or another Delivery Point that is not Customer's receiving facility, Customer may perform the checks in Sections 8.2(a) through 8.2(d) at the Delivery Point.

8.5 **Inspection Records.** Customer shall keep a record of each Incoming Inspection showing:
   - the date of receipt;
   - the date of inspection;
   - the Covered Products inspected; and
   - the results.

Customer shall provide copies of these records to Supplier on request.

8.6 **No Further Testing.** Customer is not required to perform any test other than those described in this Section 8 and in Exhibit B.

## 9. HANDLING OF NONCONFORMING PRODUCT

9.1 **Notice.** Customer shall notify Supplier's Quality Manager of each Nonconforming Product by email to quality@breiraind.com, using the nonconformance report form attached as Exhibit C. The report shall identify:
   - the Purchase Order;
   - the part number;
   - the lot or serial number;
   - the quantity; and
   - the nature of the nonconformance.

Customer shall include photographs where practical.

9.2 **Segregation.** Customer shall segregate and tag each Nonconforming Product and hold it for collection by Supplier. Customer shall not install any Nonconforming Product in a Serviced Unit.

9.3 **Supplier Response.** Within two (2) Business Days after receiving a nonconformance report, Supplier shall:

(a) acknowledge the report and issue a returned-goods authorization;

(b) arrange collection of the Nonconforming Product under Section 8.2(a) of the Agreement; and

(c) confirm whether it will replace the Nonconforming Product or issue a credit, in accordance with Customer's election under Section 8.2(b) of the Agreement.

9.4 **Containment.** If the nonconformance could affect other units from the same lot or serial range, Supplier shall promptly do the following:

(a) quarantine the affected inventory in its own facilities, including safety stock maintained under Section 4.2 of the Agreement; and

(b) tell Customer which other shipments to Customer contain units from that lot or serial range.

9.5 **Supplier Analysis.** Supplier shall analyze each returned Nonconforming Product. Supplier shall give Customer a written summary of its findings within fifteen (15) Business Days after it receives the returned product.

## 10. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

10.1 **When Required.** Supplier shall perform a root cause analysis and deliver a Corrective Action Plan in each of the following cases:

(a) when required under Section 9.5 of the Agreement;

(b) when three (3) or more Nonconforming Products of the same part number are reported in any rolling ninety (90) day period;

(c) when any Critical Safety Component is reported as a Nonconforming Product; and

(d) when Customer reasonably requests one following a Field Performance Report under Section 11.

10.2 **Methodology.** Supplier shall use a structured problem-solving method for each root cause analysis, such as the 8D method or an equivalent. Supplier shall make the supporting analysis, test data and failure analysis reports available to Customer.

10.3 **Timing.** Supplier shall deliver the following:
   - a written description of containment actions within five (5) Business Days after the obligation arises; and
   - a complete Corrective Action Plan within thirty (30) days after the obligation arises.

For Critical Safety Components, the containment description is due within two (2) Business Days.

10.4 **Implementation and Verification.** Supplier shall implement each Corrective Action Plan on the schedule stated in it.

(a) Supplier shall report progress to Customer at least monthly until the corrective actions are complete.

(b) Supplier shall verify the effectiveness of the corrective actions. Verification may be by inspection, testing or monitoring of subsequent production, and Supplier shall report the results to Customer.

(c) Where a corrective action involves a change described in Section 5.2, Section 5 applies to that change.

10.5 **Escalation.** Customer may escalate the matter under Section 18.3 of the Agreement in either of the following cases:

(a) Supplier fails to deliver a Corrective Action Plan when due; or

(b) a Corrective Action Plan does not resolve the quality problem within the time stated in it.

## 11. FIELD PERFORMANCE REPORTING

11.1 **Field Performance Reports.** Customer may give Supplier a report describing the performance of Covered Products installed in Serviced Units (a "**Field Performance Report**"). A report may cover:
   - failures, shutdowns, entrapments or abnormal operation that Customer's technicians attribute to a Covered Product; and
   - recurring adjustment or maintenance problems that Customer's technicians attribute to a Covered Product.

Each Field Performance Report shall identify, where known:
   - the part number and serial or lot number;
   - the Serviced Unit;
   - the date of installation; and
   - the date and description of the event.

11.2 **Return for Analysis.** Customer may return to Supplier, for failure analysis, any Covered Product removed from a Serviced Unit and described in a Field Performance Report. Supplier shall pay the cost of returning it. Supplier shall give Customer a written failure analysis within twenty (20) Business Days after receiving the returned Covered Product.

11.3 **Trend Review.** At each quarterly business review under Section 12.2 of the Order Form, Supplier shall present a summary of the Field Performance Reports received and the failure analyses completed during the quarter. The summary shall identify any failure mode that has recurred in more than one Serviced Unit.

11.4 **Safety Events.** If a Field Performance Report describes an event involving injury, entrapment, uncontrolled car movement or an order of an authority having jurisdiction, Supplier shall treat it as a priority. Supplier shall also assess promptly whether notice is required under Section 10.5 of the Agreement.

## 12. AUDITS

12.1 **Quality Audits.** Customer, or a qualified third-party auditor acting for Customer and bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Supplier's compliance with this Quality Agreement at any facility where Covered Products are manufactured, tested or stored. Each audit shall be conducted:
   - on at least fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice;
   - during normal business hours; and
   - not more than once in any Contract Year.

12.2 **For-Cause Audits.** In addition to the audit in Section 12.1, Customer may conduct an audit on five (5) Business Days' notice in either of the following cases:

(a) after a Critical Safety Component is reported as a Nonconforming Product; or

(b) after Supplier gives a notice under Section 10.5 of the Agreement.

12.3 **Scope.** An audit may include:
   - review of the Quality Manual, process controls, inspection and test records, calibration records, traceability records and Corrective Action Plans; and
   - observation of manufacturing, inspection and testing of Covered Products.

An audit does not extend to Supplier's cost or pricing data. Section 11.5 of the Agreement governs audits of pricing and invoicing.

12.4 **Coordination.** Customer may coordinate an audit under this Section 12 with an audit under Section 9.2 of the Data Processing Addendum to reduce disruption to Supplier's operations.

12.5 **Findings.** Customer shall give Supplier a written summary of its audit findings within fifteen (15) Business Days after the audit. Supplier shall respond to each finding within thirty (30) days. For any finding of noncompliance, the response shall be a Corrective Action Plan.

12.6 **Costs.** Each Party bears its own costs of an audit.

12.7 **Sub-tier Supplier Facilities.** On Customer's request, Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to arrange access for Customer to the facilities of a Sub-tier Supplier of a Critical Safety Component, accompanied by Supplier.

## 13. SUB-TIER SUPPLIERS

13.1 **Flow-Down.** Supplier shall flow down to each Sub-tier Supplier the quality requirements of this Quality Agreement that are relevant to the materials, parts or sub-assemblies it supplies. These include the requirements for traceability, change notification and records.

13.2 **Approved Sources.** Supplier shall maintain an approved supplier list for the Covered Products. Supplier shall evaluate and monitor each Sub-tier Supplier on quality and delivery performance.

13.3 **Responsibility.** Supplier remains responsible for the quality of materials, parts and sub-assemblies furnished by Sub-tier Suppliers, consistent with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

13.4 **Counterfeit Parts.** Supplier shall maintain controls designed to prevent counterfeit or unauthorized parts from being incorporated into Covered Products. Supplier shall purchase electronic components only from original manufacturers or their authorized distributors.

## 14. QUALITY CONTACTS AND REVIEWS

14.1 **Contacts.** Each Party's quality contact is the person identified in Section 11 of the Order Form for "Quality and warranty," or any other person that Party designates by email notice to the other Party's quality contact.

14.2 **Quality Reviews.** The Parties' quality contacts shall review quality performance at each quarterly business review under Section 12.2 of the Order Form. The review shall cover:
   - Nonconforming Product reports;
   - Field Performance Reports;
   - open Corrective Action Plans;
   - pending change notifications; and
   - audit findings.

14.3 **Quality Metrics.** Supplier shall include in its monthly scorecard under Section 12.1 of the Order Form the number of Nonconforming Products reported by part number. For each part number, Supplier shall also show the rate in parts per million of units shipped.

## 15. TERM AND GENERAL

15.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the Quality Agreement Effective Date. It remains in effect for as long as the Agreement remains in effect, and thereafter for as long as Supplier supplies Covered Products to Customer, including during any transition period under Section 15.6 of the Agreement.

15.2 **Survival.** Sections 6, 10 and 11 survive expiration or termination of this Quality Agreement, to the extent they relate to Covered Products delivered before expiration or termination.

15.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties may update Exhibits A, B and C by written agreement of their quality contacts, confirmed by email.

15.4 **Application of Agreement Terms.** Except as stated in this Quality Agreement, the following Sections of the Agreement apply to this Quality Agreement:
   - Section 12 (Confidentiality);
   - Section 18.1 (Governing Law);
   - Section 18.2 (Jurisdiction and Venue);
   - Section 18.3 (Dispute Escalation);
   - Section 18.5 (Notices); and
   - Section 18.7 (Amendment and Waiver).

15.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Quality Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

15.6 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by PDF or by a recognized electronic signature service are effective as original signatures.
