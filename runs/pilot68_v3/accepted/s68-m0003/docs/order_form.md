## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Serviced Units.** "**Serviced Units**" means every elevator, escalator and moving walkway unit maintained by Customer or any of its Affiliates anywhere in the United States or Canada, whether under a full maintenance contract, an examination-and-lubrication contract, a time-and-materials arrangement or a repair or modernization project.

2.2 **Stocked Item.** "**Stocked Item**" means a Covered Product identified as "Stocked" in the "Stock Status" column of the Product Schedule in Section 4. Supplier maintains safety stock of each Stocked Item under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Made-to-Order Item.** "**Made-to-Order Item**" means a Covered Product identified as "MTO" in the "Stock Status" column of the Product Schedule. A Made-to-Order Item is manufactured, configured or modified to the requirements of a specific Purchase Order. Section 4.5(a) of the Agreement applies to it.

2.4 **Emergency Order.** "**Emergency Order**" means a Purchase Order that Customer designates as an emergency order and that identifies a Serviced Unit that is out of service, is subject to a shutdown or violation order of an authority having jurisdiction, or has been involved in an entrapment.

2.5 **Consumable Item.** "**Consumable Item**" means a Covered Product identified as "Consumable" in the "Category" column of the Product Schedule. Section 9.4(d) of the Agreement applies to it.

2.6 **List Price.** "**List Price**" means Supplier's published list price for a Covered Product in effect on the date a Purchase Order is issued.

## 3. COMMERCIAL TERMS

**Item 1 – Pricing.** The unit prices for the Covered Products are stated in the Product Schedule in Section 4. Prices are in United States dollars and include standard packaging, labeling, Documentation and delivery to the Delivery Point, as provided in Section 5.1 of the Agreement. Prices are firm for the first Contract Year under Section 5.2 of the Agreement. After that, they may be adjusted only under Section 5.3 of the Agreement.

**Item 2 – Discount for Components Not Listed.** For any replacement component that Supplier offers in its current catalog but that is not listed in the Product Schedule, Supplier shall price the component at a discount of twenty-two percent (22%) off List Price until the Parties amend this Order Form under Section 10.1 of the Agreement. Such a component becomes a Covered Product only when that amendment takes effect.

**Item 3 – Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** The minimum annual purchase commitment under Section 3.2 of the Agreement is $2,400,000 per contract year. Aggregate net purchases are measured on net invoice value, after deducting credits for rejected shipments, warranty replacements, most-favored pricing adjustments and audit refunds. Aggregate net purchases exclude Taxes, expedited freight charged under Section 7.2 of the Agreement and reimbursed field labor.

**Item 4 – Shortfall Amount.** If Customer's aggregate net purchases in a Contract Year fall short of the minimum annual purchase commitment, the shortfall amount payable under Section 3.2(b) of the Agreement is fifteen percent (15%) of the difference between the minimum annual purchase commitment and Customer's aggregate net purchases for that Contract Year. The calculation takes into account the credit provided under Section 3.2(c) of the Agreement. The Parties agree that the shortfall amount is a reasonable estimate of Supplier's lost contribution margin and is not a penalty.

**Item 5 – Volume Rebate.** If Customer's aggregate net purchases in a Contract Year exceed $3,000,000, Supplier shall issue Customer a rebate credit equal to two percent (2%) of the aggregate net purchases above $3,000,000. The rebate credit shall be issued within thirty (30) days after Supplier delivers the Contract Year statement under Section 3.2(a) of the Agreement. Customer may apply the credit against future invoices under Section 6.6 of the Agreement.

**Item 6 – Payment Terms.** Net forty-five (45) days from receipt of invoice, as provided in Section 6.2 of the Agreement. Payment shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated in Section 10 of this Order Form or in any later written designation by Supplier.

**Item 7 – Delivery Terms.** DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Delivery Point, as provided in Section 7.1 of the Agreement.

**Item 8 – Price Adjustment Index.** The index for annual price adjustments under Section 5.3(b) of the Agreement is the Producer Price Index for Elevator and Moving Stairway Manufacturing published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (series PCU333921333921). The comparison runs from the most recent month for which final data is available on the date of Supplier's adjustment notice to the same month in the prior year.

## 6. EMERGENCY ORDERS

6.1 **Emergency Response Time.** The emergency response time for purposes of Sections 3.3(c) and 7.3 of the Agreement is:

(a) for Stocked Items, shipment within four (4) hours after Supplier's acknowledgment of the Emergency Order, if the Emergency Order is received between 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day. Emergency Orders received outside those hours ship by 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the next day, including weekends and holidays; and

(b) for Made-to-Order Items, shipment within fifty percent (50%) of the applicable Lead Time, rounded up to the next whole Business Day.

6.2 **Emergency Hotline.** Customer may place Emergency Orders 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, by telephone to Supplier's emergency desk at (732) 555-0148, followed within two (2) hours by a written Purchase Order to the ordering email address in Section 9.

6.3 **Expedited Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Emergency Orders by same-day courier to Delivery Points within 150 miles of Supplier's Edison, New Jersey distribution facility. For other Delivery Points, Supplier shall ship by next-flight-out or priority overnight service. Expedited delivery costs for Emergency Orders are included in the unit price.

Supplier shall pay or credit approved claims within thirty (30) days after receipt.
