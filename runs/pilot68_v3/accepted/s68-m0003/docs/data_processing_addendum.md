# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Addendum Effective Date**") by and between:

**Breiraind Lift Components Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Taliv Elevator Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 212 West 37th Street, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10018 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties have entered into a Component Supply Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 (as amended from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier supplies replacement elevator and escalator components to Customer.

B. In performing the Agreement, Supplier may receive, access, store or otherwise process Customer Data. Customer Data includes information about Customer's technicians and about the owners, managers and occupants of buildings in which Serviced Units are located.

C. Section 13.2 of the Agreement requires Supplier to process Customer Data only in accordance with this Addendum and Customer's documented instructions. The Parties are entering into this Addendum to set out those terms.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Confidential Information," "Covered Products," "Customer Data" and "Serviced Units."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Addendum:

(a) "**Authorized Personnel**" means Supplier's employees, officers and individual contractors who need access to Customer Data to perform the Services and who are bound by the obligations in Section 4.3.

(b) "**Data Protection Laws**" means all Applicable Law relating to privacy, data security, data breach notification or the processing of personal information. These include, to the extent applicable:
   - the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act, including New York General Business Law Sections 899-aa and 899-bb;
   - the New Jersey Identity Theft Prevention Act; and
   - any other state, federal or local law of similar effect.

(c) "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Information relates.

(d) "**Personal Information**" means any Customer Data that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with a particular natural person or household. Examples include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, building access codes or credentials linked to an individual, and technician identifiers.

(e) "**Process**" and "**Processing**" mean any operation performed on Customer Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

(f) "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Customer Data in Supplier's possession, custody or control or in the possession, custody or control of a Subprocessor.

(g) "**Services**" means the supply of Covered Products and all related activities Supplier performs under the Agreement. These include order processing, emergency order fulfillment, warranty and recall administration, technical support, and any remote monitoring or diagnostic functions that Customer authorizes under Section 13.4 of the Agreement.

(h) "**Subprocessor**" means any third party, including any Affiliate of Supplier, that Supplier engages to Process Customer Data on its behalf.

(i) "**Unit Technical Data**" means Customer Data relating to the configuration, condition or operation of Serviced Units. This includes unit identifiers, controller configuration, fault logs and remote monitoring data, whether or not it contains Personal Information.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Scope.** This Addendum applies to all Customer Data that Supplier or any Subprocessor Processes in connection with the Agreement, in whatever form and on whatever medium.

2.2 **Roles of the Parties.** As between the Parties:
   - Customer is the owner of the Customer Data and determines the purposes and means of its Processing; and
   - Supplier Processes Customer Data solely on Customer's behalf as a service provider.

Supplier acquires no right, title or interest in Customer Data except the limited right to Process it as this Addendum permits.

2.3 **Description of Processing.** Schedule 1 describes the categories of Customer Data, the categories of Data Subjects, and the nature and purposes of the Processing. The Parties may update Schedule 1 by written agreement.

2.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall:

(a) provide Customer Data to Supplier only to the extent reasonably necessary for Supplier to perform the Services; and

(b) ensure that it has given any notices required under Data Protection Laws for Supplier to Process Personal Information as this Addendum contemplates.

## 3. PROCESSING RESTRICTIONS

3.1 **Documented Instructions.** Supplier shall Process Customer Data only:

(a) as necessary to perform the Services;

(b) in accordance with Customer's documented instructions, which include the Agreement, this Addendum and any written instructions Customer gives from time to time; and

(c) as required by Applicable Law. Where Applicable Law requires Processing, Supplier shall inform Customer of that requirement before Processing, unless the law prohibits it.

3.2 **Unlawful Instructions.** Supplier shall promptly inform Customer if, in Supplier's reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws. Supplier may suspend the affected Processing until Customer confirms or modifies the instruction.

3.3 **Prohibited Uses.** Supplier shall not:

(a) sell, rent, lease or otherwise make Customer Data available to any third party for monetary or other valuable consideration;

(b) use Customer Data to market products or services to building owners, managers or occupants, or to solicit any customer of Customer;

(c) combine Customer Data with personal information that Supplier receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as necessary to perform the Services;

(d) use Customer Data to build or improve profiles of Customer's service contracts, building portfolio or pricing; or

(e) retain, use or disclose Customer Data for any purpose other than the purposes stated in Section 3.1.

3.4 **Aggregated Product Data.** Supplier may use Unit Technical Data from which all Personal Information, building addresses and Customer-identifying information have been removed to analyze the reliability and performance of its own products, including for root cause analysis under Section 9.5 of the Agreement. Supplier shall not attempt to re-identify any such data. Supplier shall not disclose any such data to a third party in a form that identifies Customer, a building or a Serviced Unit.

3.5 **Building Access Information.** Supplier shall not use any building address, access code, key-box combination or similar access information included in Customer Data for any purpose other than delivering Covered Products to the Delivery Point stated in a Purchase Order, or as Customer specifically directs in writing.

## 4. SECURITY

4.1 **Security Program.** Supplier shall implement and maintain a written information security program containing administrative, technical and physical safeguards. The program shall be appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and the risks of the Processing, and shall meet the requirements of Data Protection Laws, including the reasonable safeguards requirements of New York General Business Law Section 899-bb. At a minimum, the program shall include the measures described in Schedule 2.

4.2 **Review and Updates.** Supplier shall review its security program at least annually and after any material change in its business practices or any Security Incident. Supplier may update its security measures from time to time, but no update may materially reduce the overall protection given to Customer Data.

4.3 **Personnel.** Supplier shall:

(a) limit access to Customer Data to Authorized Personnel;

(b) ensure that all Authorized Personnel are subject to written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Section 12 of the Agreement;

(c) provide Authorized Personnel with appropriate training on data security and the handling of Personal Information at onboarding and at least annually; and

(d) promptly revoke an individual's access when that individual no longer requires it.

4.4 **Remote Access.** Where a Covered Product includes remote monitoring or diagnostic functions, Supplier shall not enable or use those functions except as Customer authorizes in writing under Section 13.4 of the Agreement. Where Customer authorizes remote access, Supplier shall:

(a) access a Serviced Unit only through secure, encrypted connections using multi-factor authentication;

(b) log each remote session, including the user, date, time, duration and Serviced Unit accessed, and keep those logs for at least twelve (12) months;

(c) not make any change to controller parameters, safety circuits or operating settings of a Serviced Unit by remote means unless Customer specifically requests it in writing; and

(d) end each remote session promptly after completing the authorized task.

4.5 **Security Certifications.** On Customer's written request, made not more than once per Contract Year, Supplier shall provide its most recent independent security assessment report (such as a SOC 2 Type II report or an equivalent third-party assessment) together with a summary of any corrective actions taken. Reports provided under this Section 4.5 are Supplier's Confidential Information.

## 5. SUBPROCESSORS

5.1 **Authorized Subprocessors.** Customer authorizes Supplier to engage the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3 as of the Addendum Effective Date.

5.2 **New Subprocessors.** Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging any new Subprocessor or replacing an existing one. The notice shall identify the Subprocessor, its location and the Processing it will perform.

(a) Customer may object to the engagement on reasonable grounds relating to data protection by written notice within fifteen (15) days after receiving Supplier's notice.

(b) If Customer objects, the Parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. Supplier shall not engage the Subprocessor to Process Customer Data until the objection is resolved.

5.3 **Subprocessor Obligations.** Before a Subprocessor Processes any Customer Data, Supplier shall enter into a written agreement with it that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum. This is consistent with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

5.4 **Responsibility.** Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of each Subprocessor as if they were Supplier's own acts and omissions.

## 6. DATA LOCATION

6.1 **Storage Location.** Supplier shall store and Process Customer Data only in data centers and facilities located in the United States, unless Customer gives its prior written consent to Processing elsewhere.

6.2 **Remote Personnel Access.** Supplier shall not permit any Authorized Personnel or Subprocessor located outside the United States to access Customer Data without Customer's prior written consent.

## 7. SECURITY INCIDENTS

7.1 **Notice.** Supplier shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after Supplier becomes aware of a Security Incident. Notice shall be sent by email to privacy@talivelevator.com, with a copy to the Customer notice address in Section 18.5 of the Agreement. Notice shall also be given by telephone to Customer's designated security contact listed in Schedule 3.

7.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice shall include, to the extent then known:
   - a description of the nature of the Security Incident, including its date and time and how it was discovered;
   - the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;
   - any Serviced Units, buildings or remote access functions affected;
   - the likely consequences of the Security Incident; and
   - the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it.

Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

7.3 **Containment and Remediation.** Supplier shall promptly take all reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Where the Security Incident involves remote access functions or controller credentials, these steps include immediately disabling affected remote access, rotating credentials and assisting Customer in confirming the integrity of the configuration of affected Serviced Units.

7.4 **Cooperation.** Supplier shall cooperate fully with Customer in investigating each Security Incident. Supplier shall provide the information and assistance Customer reasonably requests to:

(a) assess whether notice to Data Subjects, building owners, regulators or other persons is required; and

(b) prepare and deliver any such notice.

7.5 **Third-Party Notices.** Supplier shall not notify any Data Subject, building owner, manager or regulator of a Security Incident involving Customer Data without Customer's prior written approval, except where Applicable Law requires Supplier to give that notice directly. In that case Supplier shall give Customer advance notice and a copy of the proposed notice where legally permitted.

7.6 **Costs.** To the extent a Security Incident results from Supplier's or a Subprocessor's breach of this Addendum, Supplier shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of:
   - investigating the Security Incident;
   - notices to Data Subjects and others required by Data Protection Laws;
   - credit monitoring or identity protection services for affected Data Subjects for up to twenty-four (24) months, where customary for the type of information involved; and
   - any field inspection of Serviced Units reasonably required to confirm their safe operation.

7.7 **No Acknowledgment.** Supplier's notification of or response to a Security Incident is not an acknowledgment by Supplier of any fault or liability.

## 8. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS AND ASSISTANCE

8.1 **Requests.** If Supplier receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise a right under Data Protection Laws relating to Customer Data, Supplier shall:

(a) notify Customer within five (5) Business Days after receipt;

(b) not respond to the request except to direct the Data Subject to Customer, unless Customer instructs otherwise in writing; and

(c) provide Customer with reasonable assistance in responding to the request.

8.2 **Governmental Requests.** If Supplier receives a subpoena, court order, warrant or other request from a governmental authority for Customer Data, Supplier shall promptly notify Customer, unless legally prohibited. Supplier shall disclose only the Customer Data it is legally required to disclose. Supplier shall cooperate reasonably with any effort by Customer to obtain a protective order or other appropriate remedy, consistent with Section 12.4 of the Agreement.

8.3 **General Assistance.** Supplier shall provide Customer with reasonable assistance in complying with Customer's obligations under Data Protection Laws, including in responding to inquiries from building owners and authorities concerning data handled in connection with Serviced Units.

## 9. AUDITS

9.1 **Information.** Supplier shall make available to Customer, on reasonable request, all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate Supplier's compliance with this Addendum.

9.2 **Audit Rights.** Customer, or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Supplier's compliance with this Addendum:
   - on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice;
   - during normal business hours; and
   - not more than once in any Contract Year, except following a Security Incident or where a regulator requires an audit.

Customer may coordinate an audit under this Section 9.2 with any quality audit under the Quality Agreement to minimize disruption.

9.3 **Conduct of Audits.** Audits shall be conducted so as not to interfere unreasonably with Supplier's operations. Customer shall bear its own audit costs. However, if an audit reveals a material breach of this Addendum, Supplier shall reimburse Customer's reasonable audit costs.

9.4 **Remediation.** Supplier shall promptly remediate any non-compliance identified by an audit. Within thirty (30) days after receiving the audit findings, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a written remediation plan with target completion dates.

## 10. RETURN AND DELETION

10.1 **On Termination or Expiration.** Within thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of the Agreement, and in accordance with Section 15.5(d) of the Agreement, Supplier shall, at Customer's election:

(a) return all Customer Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format; or

(b) securely delete all Customer Data in its and its Subprocessors' possession, custody or control.

10.2 **Transition Period.** If Supplier continues to supply Covered Products to Customer under Section 15.6 of the Agreement, Supplier may retain the Customer Data reasonably required for that purpose until the end of the transition period. Section 10.1 then applies from the end of that period.

10.3 **On Request.** During the Term, Supplier shall return or delete specific Customer Data within thirty (30) days after Customer's written request, unless the Customer Data is needed to perform an outstanding Purchase Order, warranty claim or recall.

10.4 **Retention Required by Law.** Supplier may retain Customer Data to the extent Applicable Law requires, including the traceability and quality records it must keep under Section 11.5 of the Agreement. Supplier may also retain Customer Data held in routine backup systems until those backups are overwritten in the ordinary course. Retained Customer Data remains subject to this Addendum for as long as it is retained. Supplier shall not Process it for any other purpose.

10.5 **Certification.** On Customer's request, an officer of Supplier shall certify in writing that Supplier has complied with this Section 10.

10.6 **Deletion Standard.** Supplier shall delete Customer Data using methods consistent with NIST Special Publication 800-88 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization) or an equivalent standard, so that the data cannot be practicably read or reconstructed.

## 11. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

11.1 **Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum is subject to Section 14 of the Agreement, including Section 14.8 of the Agreement.

11.2 **Indemnity.** Supplier's breach of this Addendum is a breach of Section 13 of the Agreement for purposes of Section 14.1(c) of the Agreement.

11.3 **Insurance.** Supplier shall maintain the cyber and privacy liability insurance required by Section 14.5(d) of the Agreement for as long as Supplier or any Subprocessor Processes Customer Data.

## 12. TERM

12.1 **Term.** This Addendum takes effect on the Addendum Effective Date. It remains in effect for as long as the Agreement remains in effect, and thereafter for as long as Supplier or any Subprocessor retains any Customer Data.

12.2 **Survival.** Sections 7, 8.2, 10, 11 and 13 survive expiration or termination of this Addendum.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Addendum forms part of the arrangement between the Parties under the Agreement. Except as stated in this Addendum, the terms of the Agreement apply to this Addendum, including Sections 18.1 (Governing Law), 18.2 (Jurisdiction and Venue), 18.3 (Dispute Escalation), 18.5 (Notices) and 18.7 (Amendment and Waiver) of the Agreement.

13.2 **Changes in Law.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires a change to this Addendum, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend it to the extent necessary to comply.

13.3 **Confidentiality.** Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information under Section 12 of the Agreement, in addition to the protections in this Addendum.

13.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Addendum is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

13.5 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by PDF or by a recognized electronic signature service are effective as original signatures.

## SCHEDULE 1 – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**1. Categories of Data Subjects**
- Customer's elevator mechanics, apprentices, helpers and other field technicians
- Customer's dispatchers, purchasing staff and account managers
- Owners, managing agents, superintendents and other contacts of buildings in which Serviced Units are located
- Building occupants, only to the extent their information appears incidentally in entrapment reports, fault logs or delivery instructions

**2. Categories of Customer Data**
- Unit Technical Data, including unit identifiers, state and city device numbers, controller configuration, fault logs and remote monitoring data
- Building addresses, loading dock and delivery instructions, and building access information
- Names, business telephone numbers, business email addresses and job titles of Customer personnel and building contacts
- Technician identifiers and license numbers, to the extent included in warranty claims or recall records
- Purchase Order, warranty claim and recall records that reference any of the above

**3. Sensitive Information.** Customer does not intend to provide, and Supplier shall not request, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, biometric data or health information. If any such information is provided inadvertently, Supplier shall notify Customer and shall delete it promptly or Process it only as Customer instructs.

**4. Nature and Purposes of Processing**
- Receiving, acknowledging and fulfilling Purchase Orders, including emergency orders
- Arranging delivery of Covered Products to Delivery Points
- Administering warranty claims, replacements and field labor reimbursements under Section 9 of the Agreement
- Carrying out recalls, safety notices and field retrofits under Section 10.5 of the Agreement
- Providing technical support and Documentation
- Remote monitoring and diagnostics, only where Customer has authorized them under Section 13.4 of the Agreement
- Maintaining traceability and quality records under the Agreement and the Quality Agreement

**5. Duration of Processing.** For the term of this Addendum under Section 12.1.

**6. Frequency.** Continuous, as Purchase Orders, warranty claims and support requests arise.

## SCHEDULE 2 – MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

Supplier shall maintain at least the following measures:

1. **Governance.** A designated employee responsible for coordinating the information security program, and documented security policies approved by Supplier's management.

2. **Risk Assessment.** Assessment, at least annually, of reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks to Customer Data, and of the sufficiency of safeguards to control those risks.

3. **Access Control.** Role-based access to systems containing Customer Data, unique user credentials, multi-factor authentication for remote and privileged access, and quarterly review of access rights.

4. **Encryption.** Encryption of Customer Data in transit over public networks using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encryption at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent industry standard.

5. **Network Security.** Firewalls, intrusion detection or prevention, and segregation of systems that support remote monitoring functions from Supplier's general corporate network.

6. **Vulnerability Management.** Timely application of security patches; vulnerability scanning at least quarterly; and penetration testing of internet-facing systems that Process Customer Data at least annually.

7. **Endpoint Protection.** Current anti-malware software on all workstations and servers that Process Customer Data, and full-disk encryption of laptops and portable media.

8. **Logging and Monitoring.** Logging of access to Customer Data and of administrative activity, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months and reviewed for suspicious activity.

9. **Physical Security.** Controlled access to facilities and equipment that store Customer Data, including visitor logs and secure disposal of paper records.

10. **Business Continuity.** Regular backups of Customer Data, tested restoration procedures, and a documented business continuity and disaster recovery plan.

11. **Vendor Management.** Due diligence on the security practices of each Subprocessor before engagement, and periodic review afterward.

12. **Incident Response.** A documented incident response plan that is tested at least annually and supports the notice obligations in Section 7.
