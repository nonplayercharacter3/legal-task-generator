# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Guaranty Date**") by **Taliv Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 212 West 37th Street, 9th Floor, New York, New York 10018 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Breiraind Lift Components Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and **Taliv Elevator Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Customer**"), are entering into a Component Supply Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 (as amended, supplemented or replaced from time to time in accordance with its terms, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier supplies replacement elevator and escalator components to Customer on credit terms.

B. The Guarantor is the sole member of Customer and will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the supply of Covered Products to Customer and from the credit Supplier extends to Customer under the Agreement.

C. Recital D and Section 6.7 of the Agreement require Customer to deliver this Guaranty to Supplier, duly executed by the Guarantor, on or before the Effective Date of the Agreement as a condition of Supplier extending credit under the Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, to induce Supplier to enter into the Agreement and to extend credit to Customer under it, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Guarantor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Contract Year," "Covered Products," "Order Form" and "Purchase Order."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Guaranty:

(a) "**Bankruptcy Event**" means, with respect to Customer, any of the events described in Section 15.3 of the Agreement, and any case or proceeding under the United States Bankruptcy Code or any other bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership or similar law.

(b) "**Enforcement Costs**" means all reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs, that Supplier incurs in enforcing this Guaranty against the Guarantor.

(c) "**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

(d) "**Payment Demand**" means a written demand for payment delivered by Supplier to the Guarantor in accordance with Section 3.1.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Guaranty, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Guaranty. References to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranteed Obligations.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Supplier, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment when due of all amounts that Customer owes to Supplier under the Agreement and the Order Form, whether now existing or arising later (the "**Guaranteed Obligations**"). The Guaranteed Obligations include:

(a) the price of Covered Products delivered under Purchase Orders, as invoiced under Section 6.1 of the Agreement and payable under Section 6.2 of the Agreement;

(b) any shortfall amount payable under Section 3.2(b) of the Agreement and the Order Form;

(c) amounts payable for safety stock under Section 4.2(a) of the Agreement;

(d) reimbursement of non-cancellable costs payable under Section 4.5(a) of the Agreement;

(e) Taxes properly invoiced under Section 6.5 of the Agreement;

(f) late payment interest under Section 6.4 of the Agreement; and

(g) any amount payable by Customer under Section 14.2 of the Agreement, to the extent finally determined to be owing.

2.2 **Payment Only.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment only. The Guarantor does not guarantee the performance by Customer of any non-monetary obligation under the Agreement, including Customer's obligations to issue Forecasts or Purchase Orders, to perform installation or maintenance work, or to comply with Sections 12 and 13 of the Agreement. Nothing in this Guaranty requires the Guarantor to purchase any Covered Product.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. Supplier is not required, as a condition of the Guarantor's liability, to:

(a) bring any action or proceeding against Customer;

(b) exhaust any remedy against Customer or any other person;

(c) enforce or realize on any security held for the Guaranteed Obligations; or

(d) join Customer in any action against the Guarantor.

2.4 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations that arise during the Term of the Agreement, including during any Renewal Term, and to all Guaranteed Obligations that arise after the end of the Term under provisions of the Agreement that survive expiration or termination, including Sections 15.5 and 15.6 of the Agreement.

2.5 **Defenses of Customer Preserved.** The Guarantor's liability for any Guaranteed Obligation is no greater than Customer's liability for that obligation under the Agreement. The Guarantor may assert against Supplier any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Customer could assert under the Agreement with respect to a Guaranteed Obligation, including:

(a) that the amount is disputed in good faith under Section 6.3 of the Agreement;

(b) that the amount is subject to set-off under Section 6.6 of the Agreement;

(c) that the amount is subject to a credit under Section 3.2(c), Section 5.4, Section 8.2 or Section 11.5(a) of the Agreement; and

(d) that the amount has been paid.

However, the Guarantor may not assert any defense arising from a Bankruptcy Event affecting Customer, from Customer's lack of capacity or authority, or from any discharge, limitation or stay of Customer's obligations in any proceeding described in Section 1.2(a).

## 3. DEMAND AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Payment Demand.** If Customer fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Supplier may deliver a Payment Demand to the Guarantor. Each Payment Demand shall:

(a) identify the Guaranteed Obligation by invoice number, Purchase Order number or other reasonable reference;

(b) state the amount due and the date on which it became due; and

(c) be accompanied by a copy of the relevant invoice or statement.

3.2 **Time for Payment.** The Guarantor shall pay the amount stated in a Payment Demand within ten (10) Business Days after receiving it, except to the extent the Guarantor disputes the amount in good faith under Section 2.5 by written notice to Supplier within that period describing the basis for the dispute. The Guarantor shall pay any undisputed portion within the ten (10) Business Day period.

3.3 **Disputed Amounts.** The Guarantor shall pay any disputed amount that is resolved in Supplier's favor, whether by agreement between Supplier and Customer, by agreement between Supplier and the Guarantor, or by a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, within ten (10) Business Days after the resolution.

3.4 **Manner of Payment.** The Guarantor shall make each payment under this Guaranty in United States dollars, in immediately available funds, by ACH or wire transfer to the account that Supplier designates for payments under the Agreement in accordance with the Order Form. Each payment shall be made without deduction for Taxes, except as required by Applicable Law.

3.5 **Multiple Demands.** Supplier may deliver more than one Payment Demand. The delivery of a Payment Demand for part of the Guaranteed Obligations does not prevent Supplier from delivering later Payment Demands for other or further Guaranteed Obligations.

3.6 **Application of Payments.** Each payment by the Guarantor shall be applied to the Guaranteed Obligations identified in the related Payment Demand. Any payment by the Guarantor reduces the Guaranteed Obligations, and Customer's corresponding obligations to Supplier, by the amount paid.

3.7 **Enforcement Costs.** The Guarantor shall reimburse Supplier for its Enforcement Costs, but only if Supplier prevails in an action to enforce this Guaranty. If the Guarantor prevails in any such action, Supplier shall reimburse the Guarantor's reasonable and documented attorneys' fees and court costs.

## 4. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Obligations Not Affected.** Subject to Sections 2.5 and 7, the Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of or consents to it:

(a) any extension of time for payment, renewal, indulgence or waiver granted by Supplier to Customer;

(b) any amendment, supplement or modification of the Agreement or the Order Form made in accordance with their terms, including any change in pricing under Section 5.3 of the Agreement or the addition of Covered Products under Section 10.1 of the Agreement;

(c) any renewal of the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement;

(d) any Bankruptcy Event affecting Customer, or any disaffirmance or rejection of the Agreement in any such proceeding;

(e) any change in the name, organizational form or ownership of Customer;

(f) any release of, or failure to perfect, any security or other guaranty for the Guaranteed Obligations; or

(g) any delay or failure by Supplier in exercising any right or remedy under the Agreement or this Guaranty.

4.2 **Material Amendments.** Supplier shall give the Guarantor a copy of each written amendment to the Agreement or the Order Form within ten (10) Business Days after it is signed. Supplier's failure to do so does not release the Guarantor but extends, by the period of the delay, the Guarantor's time to respond to any Payment Demand relating to an obligation arising under the amendment.

4.3 **Waivers.** To the extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Guarantor waives:

(a) presentment, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) notice of the creation, renewal or extension of any Guaranteed Obligation, other than the notices expressly required by this Guaranty;

(c) any right to require Supplier to proceed first against Customer or any other person, or to marshal assets; and

(d) any defense based on any statute of limitations to the extent the limitation period applicable to the corresponding Guaranteed Obligation against Customer has not expired.

4.4 **Reinstatement.** If any payment received by Supplier on account of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded, avoided or required to be returned by Supplier in any Bankruptcy Event affecting Customer or otherwise, this Guaranty shall be reinstated as to that payment as though it had not been made. Supplier shall give the Guarantor prompt notice of any claim for rescission or return of which it becomes aware and shall consult with the Guarantor in good faith regarding the defense of that claim.

## 5. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

5.1 **Subrogation.** On payment of any Guaranteed Obligation, the Guarantor is subrogated to Supplier's rights against Customer with respect to that obligation. The Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Customer until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full.

5.2 **Subordination of Intercompany Claims.** Any indebtedness of Customer to the Guarantor is subordinated in right of payment to the Guaranteed Obligations that are then due and unpaid following a Payment Demand. Until those Guaranteed Obligations have been paid, the Guarantor shall not demand or accept payment of any such indebtedness, except for payments made in the ordinary course of business while no Payment Demand is outstanding.

5.3 **Amounts Received in Trust.** If the Guarantor receives any amount in violation of Section 5.1 or 5.2, it shall hold that amount in trust for Supplier and shall promptly pay it to Supplier to be applied to the Guaranteed Obligations then due.

6.1 **Representations and Warranties.** The Guarantor represents and warrants to Supplier, as of the Guaranty Date, that:

(a) it is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full limited liability company power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary limited liability company action, and this Guaranty has been duly executed and delivered by the Guarantor;

(d) this Guaranty constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of the Guarantor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(e) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate the Guarantor's certificate of formation or limited liability company agreement, any Applicable Law, or any material agreement or instrument by which the Guarantor is bound;

(f) no consent, approval or authorization of, or filing with, any governmental authority or other person is required for the Guarantor's execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty, other than those that have been obtained;

(g) the Guarantor is the sole member of Customer; and

(h) the Guarantor is solvent, and will not be rendered insolvent by its execution and delivery of this Guaranty.

6.2 **Financial Information.** For as long as any Guaranteed Obligation may arise under this Guaranty, the Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier, on Supplier's written request made not more than once per Contract Year:

(a) its most recent annual financial statements, consolidated with those of its subsidiaries, prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States, reviewed or audited by an independent certified public accountant; and

(b) a certificate signed by an authorized officer or manager of the Guarantor confirming that, to the Guarantor's knowledge, the Guarantor is in compliance with this Guaranty.

6.3 **Confidentiality of Financial Information.** Supplier shall treat all financial information delivered under Section 6.2 as confidential. Supplier shall use it only to evaluate the credit it extends to Customer under the Agreement and to enforce this Guaranty, and shall protect it in the same manner as Confidential Information of Customer under Section 12 of the Agreement. Section 12 of the Agreement applies to that information as if the Guarantor were the Discloser.

6.4 **Notice of Material Events.** The Guarantor shall notify Supplier in writing within ten (10) Business Days after the Guarantor becomes subject to any of the events described in Section 15.3 of the Agreement, as if those events applied to the Guarantor.

## 7. TERM AND RELEASE

7.1 **Term.** This Guaranty takes effect on the Guaranty Date and remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid in full after the expiration or termination of the Agreement, including any Guaranteed Obligations arising during any transition period under Section 15.6 of the Agreement.

7.2 **Release.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Guarantor's written request following the expiration or termination of the Agreement and the payment in full of all Guaranteed Obligations, Supplier shall deliver to the Guarantor a written confirmation that this Guaranty has terminated and that the Guarantor has no further liability under it, subject to Section 4.4.

7.3 **Partial Release.** Supplier may, by written notice, release the Guarantor from any part of the Guaranteed Obligations without affecting the Guarantor's liability for the remainder.

Each consents to service of process by the means described in Section 8, in addition to any other means permitted by Applicable Law.

9.4 **Consolidation.** Supplier may bring an action under this Guaranty in the same proceeding as any action against Customer under the Agreement, and the Guarantor consents to the consolidation of any such actions.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors and benefits Supplier and its successors and permitted assigns under the Agreement. The Guarantor may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Guaranty without the prior written consent of Supplier.

10.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Guarantor and Supplier. A waiver is effective only if it is in writing and signed by the waiving party. No failure or delay by Supplier in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of that right, and no single or partial exercise of any right precludes any further exercise of it.

10.3 **Remedies Cumulative.** The rights and remedies of Supplier under this Guaranty are cumulative and are in addition to its rights and remedies under the Agreement and Applicable Law.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

10.5 **Limitation on Liability.** The Guarantor is not liable under this Guaranty for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, except to the extent those damages form part of a Guaranteed Obligation owed by Customer under the Agreement. The Guarantor's aggregate liability under this Guaranty does not exceed the aggregate amount of the Guaranteed Obligations, plus Enforcement Costs payable under Section 3.7 and interest on any amount not paid when due under Section 3.2 at the rate stated in Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

10.6 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Guaranty confers any right or remedy on any person other than Supplier, the Guarantor and their respective successors and permitted assigns. 10.8 **Further Assurances.** The Guarantor shall execute and deliver any further documents, and take any further actions, reasonably requested by Supplier to give effect to this Guaranty, provided that no such document or action enlarges the Guaranteed Obligations.
