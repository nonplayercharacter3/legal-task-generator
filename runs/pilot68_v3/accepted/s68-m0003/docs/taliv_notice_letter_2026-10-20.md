**Re: Component Supply Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 between Breiraind Lift Components Corp. and Taliv Elevator Services, Inc. – Notice of Non-Renewal and Related Matters**

Dear Counsel:

We write about the Component Supply Agreement entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**") by Breiraind Lift Components Corp. ("**Supplier**") and Taliv Elevator Services, Inc. ("**Customer**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

This letter sets out Customer's notice of non-renewal. It also gives Supplier an update on a transaction involving Customer's parent, and sets out Customer's requests for an orderly wind-down of the supply relationship.

## 1. Notice of Non-Renewal

By this letter, dated October 20, 2026, Customer gives Supplier written notice under Section 2.2 of the Agreement that Customer elects not to renew the Agreement. Customer does not wish the Agreement to continue into a Renewal Term. Customer intends the Agreement to expire at the end of the Initial Term.

This notice is delivered in accordance with Section 18.5 of the Agreement. It is being sent by nationally recognized overnight courier and by email to the Supplier notice address stated in that Section.

## 2. Continued Performance Through the End of the Initial Term

Customer values the relationship the Parties have built over the Initial Term. Customer will continue to perform its obligations under the Agreement through its expiration and expects Supplier to do the same. In particular:

(a) **Forecasts.** Customer will continue to deliver monthly Forecasts under Section 4.1 by the fifteenth (15th) day of each month through the end of the Initial Term. Because the Agreement is expiring, the Forecasts delivered for November and December 2026 and January 2027 will show anticipated requirements only through the expiration date. Any requirements beyond that date will appear in a separate line item for transition purposes under paragraph 4 below.

(b) **Purchase Orders.** Customer will continue to issue Purchase Orders in the ordinary course. Customer asks Supplier to continue acknowledging Purchase Orders within the periods stated in Section 4.4, including the four (4) hour acknowledgment period for emergency orders.

(c) **Lead Times and Emergency Response.** Customer asks Supplier to maintain the Lead Times and emergency response times stated in the Order Form through expiration. Customer's field operations depend on timely delivery of door operators, controllers, traction machine parts and safety gear, particularly during the winter months, when entrapment and out-of-service calls typically increase across the Metro Portfolio.

(d) **Pricing.** Customer understands that the prices currently in effect under the Order Form, as adjusted under Section 5.3 for the current Contract Year, will continue to apply to all Purchase Orders issued before expiration.

(e) **Accepted Purchase Orders.** Under Section 15.5(a), Customer elects to have Supplier fulfill, on the terms of the Agreement, all Purchase Orders that Supplier accepts before the effective date of expiration. This includes Purchase Orders with delivery dates after that date.

## 3. Update Regarding Taliv Holdings LLC

As a courtesy, and so that Supplier's records are current, we inform Supplier of a transaction involving Customer's parent company.

On September 30, 2026, Taliv Holdings LLC, Customer's sole member and the guarantor under the Parent Guaranty, entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement with Pennock Vertical Partners. Under the Membership Interest Purchase Agreement, Pennock Vertical Partners will acquire the membership interests of Taliv Holdings LLC. Closing is expected on December 15, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.

Taliv Elevator Services, Inc. will remain a New York corporation and will continue to operate under its current name. Customer's management team, field operations, dispatch center and notice address stated in Section 18.5 of the Agreement are expected to remain unchanged. Supplier's day-to-day contacts at Customer will not change, as described in paragraph 13 below.

We will provide a further update after closing. Please direct any questions about this paragraph to the undersigned.

## 4. Transition Assistance

Customer requests transition assistance under Section 15.6 of the Agreement for the period following expiration. Specifically, Customer requests that Supplier:

(a) continue to supply Covered Products, at the prices in effect at expiration, against Purchase Orders that Customer issues during the transition period. Customer currently expects to need transition supply for approximately four (4) months after expiration. Customer will confirm the expected duration in writing at least thirty (30) days before the end of the Initial Term;

(b) provide the Documentation, wiring diagrams, adjustment procedures and other technical information reasonably required for Customer to qualify alternative sources for the Covered Products. Customer's priority is the controller and door operator models currently installed in Serviced Units;

(c) provide a list of all Covered Products sourced by Supplier from third-party manufacturers, with the manufacturer's name and part number for each, so that Customer can coordinate any pass-through warranties under Section 9.7; and

(d) designate a transition coordinator who will serve as Customer's single point of contact for transition matters.

Customer proposes that the Parties' operations leads meet during the week of November 9, 2026 to agree on a written transition plan. We would be glad to host the meeting at Customer's offices or to hold it by videoconference.

## 5. Safety Stock

Section 4.2 requires Supplier to maintain safety stock of each Covered Product identified in the Order Form as a "stocked item." Section 4.2(a) addresses Customer's obligation to purchase remaining safety stock maintained specifically for Customer. In light of that Section, Customer requests that Supplier provide, by November 16, 2026, a written report showing, for each stocked item:

(a) the part number and description;

(b) the quantity currently held at Supplier's distribution facility;

(c) whether the quantity is held specifically for Customer or forms part of Supplier's general inventory; and

(d) the average monthly quantity in Customer's three (3) most recent Forecasts, as used to calculate the safety stock level.

Customer intends to reduce its Forecast quantities for stocked items over the remaining months of the Initial Term. This will help the Parties manage safety stock levels in an orderly way as expiration approaches. Customer will work with Supplier in good faith to reconcile the safety stock position, consistent with the one (1) month limit in Section 4.2(b).

## 6. Contract Year Reconciliation

Under Section 3.2(a), Supplier is to deliver a statement of aggregate net purchases for each Contract Year within thirty (30) days after the end of that Contract Year. To help both Parties' planning and year-end closing processes, Customer requests that Supplier provide by November 30, 2026 an interim statement of aggregate net purchases for the current Contract Year to date.

The interim statement should separately identify:

(a) credits issued for rejected shipments under Section 8.2;

(b) credits issued for warranty replacements under Section 9.1;

(c) any credits or refunds under Sections 5.4 and 11.5(a); and

(d) any purchases that Customer was unable to make because of a Supplier failure to supply, an allocation under Section 4.6 or a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier. Section 3.2(c) credits these purchases toward the minimum annual purchase commitment.

Customer's accounts payable team will reconcile the interim statement against Customer's records and will raise any discrepancy promptly so that the final statement can be issued without delay.

## 7. Open Invoices and Credits

Customer's records show several invoices currently within the forty-five (45) day payment period under Section 6.2, and several open credit memos for rejected shipments and warranty replacements. Customer requests a statement of account as of October 31, 2026, showing all open invoices and all open credits.

Customer will continue to pay undisputed invoices when due. Customer expects open credits to be applied in accordance with Section 6.6 before the final invoices under the Agreement are settled. Customer is not aware of any invoice currently in dispute under Section 6.3.

## 8. Warranty Claims and Recalls

Section 15.7 provides that Section 9 survives expiration. Customer's warranty rights for Covered Products delivered during the Term therefore continue after expiration in accordance with their terms.

(a) **Open Claims.** Enclosed as Attachment A is a schedule of warranty claims that Customer has submitted and that remain open as of the date of this letter. For each claim the schedule shows the Purchase Order number, part number, serial or lot number, Serviced Unit, and date of submission. Customer asks Supplier to confirm the status of each open claim and the expected ship date for each replacement.

(b) **Field Labor Reimbursement.** Several of the open claims include requests for reimbursement of field labor, lift-truck, rigging and travel costs at the labor rates stated in the Order Form. Customer asks that these reimbursements be processed in the ordinary course and reflected in the statement of account requested in paragraph 7.

(c) **Root Cause Analysis.** Customer previously raised a recurring failure mode affecting door operator assemblies installed in Serviced Units. Customer asks Supplier to confirm the status of the root cause analysis and corrective action plan under Section 9.5 and the Quality Agreement.

(d) **Recalls and Safety Notices.** Section 10.5 also survives expiration. Customer relies on Supplier's prompt notice of any defect, recall, safety bulletin or governmental inquiry affecting Covered Products installed in Serviced Units. Please confirm that Supplier's notification list for Section 10.5 purposes will continue to include Customer's operations and safety contacts after expiration.

## 9. Spare Parts and Long-Term Support

Section 10.4 requires Supplier to make repair parts and service support available for at least seven (7) years after its last shipment to Customer of each controller and door operator model supplied under the Agreement. To help Customer plan its long-term maintenance program, Customer requests that Supplier confirm:

(a) the controller and door operator models that Supplier has supplied to Customer during the Term;

(b) the date of last shipment for any model that Supplier no longer supplies to Customer; and

(c) the ordering procedure and contact that Customer should use for repair parts and service support after expiration.

Customer also asks Supplier to tell Customer of any Covered Product for which Supplier has given, or expects to give, a discontinuation notice under Section 10.3. Customer may wish to place last-time-buy Purchase Orders before expiration.

## 10. Customer Data

Section 15.5(d) of the Agreement and Section 10 of the Data Processing Addendum address the return and deletion of Customer Data. Customer will make its election between return and deletion under Section 10.1 of the Data Processing Addendum in writing before expiration.

In the meantime, Customer requests that Supplier:

(a) confirm the systems and Subprocessors in which Customer Data is currently held, including the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3 to the Data Processing Addendum and any other Subprocessor engaged under Section 5.2 of the Data Processing Addendum;

(b) confirm whether any remote monitoring or diagnostic functions are currently enabled for any Serviced Unit under Section 13.4 of the Agreement. If any are enabled, identify the Serviced Units concerned so that the Parties can plan to disable those functions on expiration, or at the end of any transition period under Section 10.2 of the Data Processing Addendum; and

(c) identify any Customer Data that Supplier expects to retain under Section 10.4 of the Data Processing Addendum, together with the legal basis for retention.

After the return or deletion is complete, Customer will ask for the officer's certificate contemplated by Section 10.5 of the Data Processing Addendum.

## 11. Confidential Information

On expiration, each Party's obligations under Section 12.5 of the Agreement will apply. Customer will return or destroy Supplier's Confidential Information in accordance with that Section and asks Supplier to do the same with Customer's Confidential Information. This includes Customer's pricing, Forecasts, building lists and unit data.

Customer notes that the confidentiality obligations in Section 12 continue for five (5) years after the Term ends, and longer for trade secrets, as provided in Section 12.6.

## 12. Insurance

Section 14.5 requires Supplier to maintain the insurance coverages described in that Section during the Term and for three (3) years after it ends. Customer and its Affiliates are to be named as additional insureds on the commercial general liability and umbrella or excess liability policies.

Customer requests that Supplier deliver a current certificate of insurance showing the required coverages, including products-completed operations coverage, and confirming Customer's additional insured status. Customer also asks Supplier to deliver renewal certificates on each policy renewal during the three (3) year post-Term period. These should be sent to the Customer notice address in Section 18.5, with a copy to Customer's risk manager identified in paragraph 13.
