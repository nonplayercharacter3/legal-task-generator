# CATERING MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Catering Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or the "**MSA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Catering MSA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Lebix Regional Airways, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4400 Terminal Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1250 Cargo Road, Building C, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19153 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates scheduled regional passenger air service and needs inflight catering, provisioning and related services for its flights.

B. Provider operates flight kitchens and provides airline catering, provisioning, galley handling and related services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to provide those services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" has the meaning given in Section 15.3.

"**Airside**" means any area of an airport beyond the security screening point, including aprons, ramps, taxiways, stands and aircraft parking positions.

"**Applicable Law**" means all laws, regulations, ordinances, codes, orders and binding guidance of any governmental or regulatory authority that apply to a Party or to the Services. This includes the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, regulations of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (including 21 C.F.R. Part 117 and the interstate conveyance sanitation rules), U.S. Department of Agriculture requirements, Federal Aviation Administration and Transportation Security Administration requirements, and applicable state and local health codes.

"**Catering Order**" means a written order, schedule or service plan issued by Customer and accepted by Provider under Section 2.2, specifying the Meals, provisioning, stations, flights and other Services to be provided.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

"**Customer Materials**" means all galley equipment, carts, trolleys, containers, serviceware, branded items, menus, recipes, specifications and other materials supplied or owned by Customer.

"**Fees**" means the fees and charges payable by Customer for the Services under Section 6 and the applicable Catering Order.

"**Food Safety Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected contamination, undeclared allergen, temperature excursion or foreign object in a Meal.

"**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

"**Meal**" means any food, beverage, snack, ingredient or other consumable item prepared, assembled, stored, handled, transported or loaded by or for Provider under this Agreement.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the Food Safety and Quality Agreement between Customer and Provider, as amended from time to time.

"**Services**" means the catering, provisioning, flight-kitchen, galley handling, delivery, loading, offloading, washing, storage and related services described in Section 2 and in each Catering Order.

"**Stations**" means the airports listed in Schedule A, as updated by Catering Order from time to time.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** If the documents comprising the Parties' arrangement conflict: (a) the Quality Agreement prevails over the MSA on food safety and quality matters, including related defined terms, including the meaning of any defined term relating thereto; and (b) the MSA prevails over Catering Orders.

## 2. SERVICES AND CATERING ORDERS

**2.1 Engagement.** Customer engages Provider to perform the Services at the Stations in accordance with this Agreement, the Quality Agreement and each Catering Order. This Agreement does not require Customer to purchase any minimum volume of Services.

**2.2 Catering Orders.** Customer may issue Catering Orders from time to time. Provider shall accept or reject each Catering Order in writing within five (5) business days of receipt. If Provider does not respond in that time, the Catering Order is deemed accepted. Each accepted Catering Order is governed by this Agreement.

**2.3 Changes.** Customer may change Meal counts, flight assignments and loading plans by notice through Provider's ordering system. The change cut-off times are set out in Schedule B. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate changes received after the cut-off, and may charge the late-change fees in Schedule C.

**2.4 Menus and Specifications.** Provider shall prepare Meals in accordance with the menus, recipes, portion specifications and presentation standards approved by Customer. Provider shall not substitute ingredients or suppliers that affect allergen content, nutritional labeling or religious or dietary certification without Customer's prior written approval.

## 3. STANDARDS AND FOOD SAFETY

**3.1 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with good industry practice for airline catering, and in compliance with Applicable Law, the Quality Agreement and Customer's reasonable written policies notified to Provider.

**3.2 Food Safety Program.** Provider shall maintain at each flight kitchen serving the Stations a written food safety plan, including hazard analysis and risk-based preventive controls, allergen controls, sanitation controls and cold-chain controls, that meets Applicable Law and the Quality Agreement.

**3.3 Food Safety Incidents.** Provider shall notify Customer of each Food Safety Incident in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Provider shall immediately quarantine affected Meals and implement any withdrawal or recall that Customer reasonably directs or that Applicable Law requires. At Customer's request, Provider shall cooperate in any investigation, root cause analysis and corrective action.

**3.4 Recall Costs.** Provider shall bear the reasonable costs of any withdrawal, recall, replacement provisioning and disposal arising from a Food Safety Incident, unless the Food Safety Incident was caused solely by Customer Materials or by Customer's acts or omissions.

**3.5 Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other of any regulatory inspection, warning letter, notice of violation or similar communication that relates to the Services or the Meals.

## 4. PROVISIONING, DELIVERY AND AIRSIDE ACCESS

**4.1 Delivery.** Provider shall deliver and load Meals and provisions onto Customer's aircraft at the times specified in the applicable Catering Order and the station loading plans. Provider shall remove and offload used galley equipment and waste.

**4.2 On-Time Performance.** Provider shall meet the on-time loading service levels in Schedule D. Service credits under Schedule D are Customer's sole monetary remedy for late loading that does not cause a flight delay. They do not limit Customer's other rights where a flight delay or cancellation results.

**4.3 Airside Access.** Customer shall support Provider's applications for airport security credentials and Airside access needed to perform the Services. Provider shall ensure that its personnel and vehicles comply with all airport rules, security programs and ramp safety procedures.

**4.4 Aircraft Damage.** Provider shall immediately report to Customer any contact between Provider's vehicles, equipment or personnel and Customer's aircraft, whether or not damage is apparent.

## 5. PERSONNEL

**5.1 Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign trained, qualified and appropriately certified personnel to perform the Services. This includes food handler certification and the security background checks that Applicable Law and airport rules require.

**5.2 Responsibility.** Provider is responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and subcontractors. Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services involving food preparation without Customer's prior written consent.

**5.3 Removal.** Customer may require Provider to remove from the Services any individual whom Customer reasonably considers unsuitable. Customer shall give Provider written reasons.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Schedule C and the applicable Catering Orders. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay where lawfully invoiced, other than taxes on Provider's net income.

**6.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Invoices shall show Meal counts and charges by flight and Station, and shall include supporting data reasonably requested by Customer.

**6.3 Payment.** Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receiving a correct invoice. Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith by giving written notice with reasonable detail. The Parties shall resolve disputed amounts under Section 18.4.

**6.4 Price Adjustments.** Fees may be adjusted once in each contract year, on the anniversary of the Catering MSA Effective Date, by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (Food Away from Home) over the preceding twelve months. No adjustment may exceed four percent (4%).

**6.5 Average Monthly Fees.** Where this Agreement refers to "average monthly fees," that means the total Fees paid or payable for the twelve (12) full calendar months immediately preceding the relevant date, divided by twelve. If fewer than twelve full calendar months have elapsed, it means the total Fees for all full calendar months elapsed, divided by the number of those months.

## 7. EQUIPMENT AND CUSTOMER MATERIALS

**7.1 Customer Materials.** Customer Materials remain Customer's property. Provider shall use them only for the Services, keep them in good condition (fair wear and tear excepted), maintain inventory records and return them on request or on termination.

**7.2 Provider Equipment.** Provider shall supply, at its own cost, all kitchen facilities, catering trucks, high-lift vehicles, refrigeration, tools and other equipment needed to perform the Services, except Customer Materials.

## 8. INSURANCE

**8.1 Provider Insurance.** Throughout the term and for two (2) years afterward, Provider shall maintain with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) aviation general liability insurance, including premises, products and completed operations, and contractual liability, with limits of not less than $25,000,000 per occurrence, which shall include hangarkeepers' coverage for aircraft in Provider's care, custody or control;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance, including Airside operations, with limits of not less than $10,000,000 per occurrence;

(c) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000; and

(d) product contamination and recall insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000.

**8.2 Endorsements.** The policies in Section 8.1(a), (b) and (d) shall name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds, shall be primary and non-contributory, and shall include a waiver of subrogation in Customer's favor. Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance on request.

**8.3 Customer Insurance.** Customer shall maintain aviation hull and liability insurance in amounts customary for regional airlines of similar size.

## 9. AUDIT AND RECORDS

**9.1 Records.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services, Fees, food safety monitoring, temperature logs, supplier approvals and corrective actions. Provider shall retain them for at least three (3) years, or longer if Applicable Law requires.

**9.2 Audits.** On reasonable notice, Customer and its designees may audit Provider's facilities, records and practices relating to the Services. Unannounced food safety audits may be conducted as provided in the Quality Agreement.

## 10. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**10.1 Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized and in good standing; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and (c) its performance will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

**10.2 Provider.** Provider further represents and warrants that: (a) it holds, and will maintain, all licenses, permits and registrations required to operate its flight kitchens and perform the Services; (b) the Meals will be wholesome, fit for human consumption, not adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of Applicable Law, and prepared in accordance with the approved specifications; and (c) the Services will comply with Applicable Law.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by: (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement or the Quality Agreement; (b) the negligence or wilful misconduct of Provider or its personnel or subcontractors; (c) any Meal that is adulterated, contaminated or misbranded; or (d) Provider's violation of Applicable Law.

**11.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to Services performed Airside or aboard Customer's aircraft, regardless of cause, including bodily injury, death and damage to property.

**11.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party in writing of the claim, although delay excuses the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is prejudiced; (b) allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement imposing liability or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may take part with counsel of its choice at its own cost.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), strict liability or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**12.2 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, even if advised of their possibility.

**12.3 Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's liability for fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct;

(b) Provider's liability for Losses arising from a Food Safety Incident;

(c) Customer's obligations under Section 11.2;

(d) a Party's breach of Section 13; or

(e) Customer's obligation to pay undisputed Fees.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

**13.1 Definition.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. This includes pricing, menus, recipes, flight schedules, passenger load data and security procedures.

**13.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services. It shall protect that information with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its personnel, advisors and Affiliates who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as these.

**13.3 Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or by a regulatory authority, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice where lawful.

**13.4 Duration.** This Section 13 survives for three (3) years after termination. For trade secrets, it survives for so long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 14. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND BRANDING

**14.1 Ownership.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Menus, recipes and specifications developed exclusively for Customer under this Agreement become Customer's property upon payment of the related Fees.

**14.2 Publicity.** Neither Party may use the other's name, trademarks or logos in any press release, advertising or public statement without the other's prior written consent. However, Provider may identify Customer as a client in customer lists.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

**15.1 Subcontracting.** Subject to Section 5.2, Provider may engage subcontractors for non-food-preparation Services on notice to Customer and remains liable for their performance.

**15.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any attempted assignment in breach of this Section 15.2 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**15.3 Change of Control.** A change of control of either Party, including by merger, consolidation, sale of equity or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, requires the other Party's prior written consent. "Control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies by contract or otherwise. A "change of control" occurs when any person or group that did not control a Party on the Catering MSA Effective Date acquires control of that Party. The Party undergoing the change of control shall request consent by written notice at least sixty (60) days before the change of control takes effect. If a change of control occurs without the other Party's prior written consent: (a) the non-consenting Party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice, given within one hundred twenty (120) days after it learns of the change of control; and (b) on such termination, the Party undergoing the change of control shall pay an early termination fee equal to six months' average monthly fees, payable by the Party undergoing the change of control within thirty (30) days after the termination takes effect.

## 16. TERM AND TERMINATION

**16.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Catering MSA Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five years from the Catering MSA Effective Date, unless terminated earlier under this Agreement. After that, it renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

**16.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

**16.3 Termination for Food Safety.** Customer may terminate this Agreement, or any Catering Order, immediately on written notice if: (a) a Food Safety Incident results in serious illness or death of any passenger or crew member; (b) Provider's license or permit to operate any flight kitchen serving the Stations is suspended or revoked; or (c) three (3) or more Food Safety Incidents occur at any one Station in any rolling ninety (90) day period.

**16.4 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**16.5 Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate any Catering Order for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 17. EFFECTS OF TERMINATION

**17.1 Transition.** On expiry or termination, Provider shall continue to perform the Services for up to ninety (90) days if Customer requests, at the then-current Fees. Provider shall cooperate in good faith to transition the Services to Customer or a successor provider.

**17.2 Return of Property.** Each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information. Provider shall return all Customer Materials.

**17.3 Accrued Rights; Survival.** Termination does not affect accrued rights or liabilities. Sections 3.4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15.3, 17 and 18 survive termination or expiry.

## 18. GENERAL

**18.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**18.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**18.3 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They shall be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy sent by courier, to the addresses below or to another address a Party designates by notice. A notice takes effect on receipt.

To Customer: Lebix Regional Airways, Inc., 4400 Terminal Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801; Attention: General Counsel; Email: legalnotices@lebixair.com

**18.4 Dispute Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than for injunctive relief, the Parties shall refer any dispute to their respective senior executives. Those executives shall meet within fifteen (15) business days of a written request to try in good faith to resolve the dispute.

**18.5 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**18.6 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action or airport closure. However, a force majeure event does not excuse payment obligations or Provider's compliance with food safety requirements for Meals actually delivered. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use reasonable efforts to mitigate.

**18.7 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

**18.8 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**18.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
