# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO CATERING MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Catering Master Services Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of July 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment No. 1 Effective Date**") by and between:

**Lebix Regional Airways, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4400 Terminal Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1250 Cargo Road, Building C, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19153 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Catering Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**MSA**").

B. Section 18.7 of the MSA provides that the MSA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

C. The Parties wish to amend the MSA as set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the MSA.

**1.2 References.** From the Amendment No. 1 Effective Date, each reference in the MSA to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning refers to the MSA as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.3

**2.1 Section 12.3(b).** Section 12.3(b) of the MSA is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "(b) Provider's liability for Losses arising from a Food Safety Incident caused by Provider's gross negligence or wilful misconduct;"

**2.2 Remaining Provisions.** Sections 12.3(a), 12.3(c), 12.3(d) and 12.3(e) of the MSA are unchanged.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SCHEDULE A (STATIONS)

**3.1 Additional Station.** Schedule A of the MSA is amended by adding the following Station:

> "Harrisburg International Airport (MDT), Middletown, Pennsylvania."

**3.2 Commencement of Services.** Provider shall begin performing the Services at the Station added by Section 3.1 on the date specified in the first Catering Order issued for that Station under Section 2.2 of the MSA. Fees for Services at that Station shall be those set out in Schedule C of the MSA, as adjusted under Section 6.4 of the MSA.

**3.3 Food Safety Requirements.** Before beginning Services at the Station added by Section 3.1, Provider shall have in place for each flight kitchen serving that Station a Food Safety Plan and the certification required under the Quality Agreement.

## 4. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

**4.1 Conflict.** If there is any conflict between this Amendment and the MSA, this Amendment controls.

**4.2 Ratification.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the MSA remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and the Parties ratify and confirm it.

**4.3 Effectiveness.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment No. 1 Effective Date.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS

Each Party represents and warrants to the other that: (a) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment; (b) this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it; and (c) this Amendment constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** Sections 18.1 and 18.2 of the MSA apply to this Amendment. This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**6.2 Incorporation.** Sections 18.3 (Notices), 18.7 (Amendment; Waiver), 18.8 (Severability) and 18.9 (Counterparts) of the MSA apply to this Amendment as if set out in full.

**6.3 Headings.** Headings in this Amendment are for convenience only.

**6.4 Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
