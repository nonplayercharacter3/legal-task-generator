# Dugein Inflight Provisions Opens New Flight Kitchen to Serve Central Pennsylvania

## 1. Headline Announcement

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**PHILADELPHIA, Pennsylvania, September 14, 2026.** Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC ("Dugein"), a provider of airline catering, provisioning and flight-kitchen services, today announced the opening of its new flight kitchen in Middletown, Pennsylvania. The facility is located minutes from Harrisburg International Airport (MDT). It is Dugein's second production facility in the Commonwealth and adds to the company's main flight kitchen at 1250 Cargo Road in Philadelphia.

## 2. The Facility

The Middletown kitchen covers about 38,000 square feet. It includes:

- separate hot-kitchen, cold-kitchen and bakery production areas;
- a dedicated allergen-controlled preparation room with its own entry, air handling and utensils;
- blast chillers and walk-in cold storage, with continuous temperature data logging;
- a cart and serviceware washing line; and
- loading docks for a fleet of refrigerated catering trucks and high-lift vehicles.

At full capacity, the facility can produce up to 9,000 meals a day for scheduled and charter flights departing central Pennsylvania.

## 3. Food Safety and Certification

Before production began, Dugein put a written food safety plan in place for the facility under 21 C.F.R. Part 117. The facility has also received certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI)-benchmarked scheme. All production staff completed food safety, allergen awareness and personal hygiene training before starting work.

"Food safety comes before everything else in our kitchens," said Corwin J. Halvey, Chief Executive Officer of Dugein Inflight Provisions. "We designed Middletown from the ground up around allergen segregation, cold-chain control and full lot-level traceability. That gives the carriers we serve the same standards in central Pennsylvania that they rely on from us in Philadelphia."

## 4. Jobs and Community

The facility opens with about 85 employees, including chefs, food safety technicians, drivers and ramp loading staff. Dugein expects to grow to about 120 employees over the next 18 months. The company is working with local workforce development programs to recruit and train culinary and logistics staff from the Harrisburg area.

## 5. Sustainability

The Middletown kitchen uses energy-efficient refrigeration and LED lighting throughout. It also runs a food-waste diversion program that sends unused, food-safe surplus to regional food banks where Applicable regulations permit. Dugein recycles galley waste streams through local partners.

## 6. About Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC

Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. It provides inflight catering, provisioning, galley handling and flight-kitchen services to regional and charter airlines in the Mid-Atlantic region. For more information, visit the company's website.
