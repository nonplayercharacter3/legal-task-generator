**Re: Catering Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 between Lebix Regional Airways, Inc. and Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC (as amended by Amendment No. 1 effective July 1, 2026, the "MSA"). Notice of Pending Transaction**

Dear Mr. Halvey:

We are writing to inform Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC ("Provider") of a pending corporate transaction involving Lebix Regional Airways, Inc. ("Lebix" or "Customer"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the MSA.

**1. The Transaction.** On October 17, 2026, Lebix entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Sablecrest Aviation Holdings, LLC ("Sablecrest") and a wholly owned subsidiary of Sablecrest formed for the transaction ("Merger Sub"). Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub will merge with and into Lebix. Lebix will continue as the surviving corporation and will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Sablecrest. Closing depends on customary conditions, including regulatory approvals. It is expected to occur on December 15, 2026.

**2. Effect on the MSA.** Lebix will survive the merger as the same legal entity. Lebix will remain the contracting party under the MSA, and the MSA will not be assigned or transferred. Section 15.2 of the MSA restricts assignment or transfer of the MSA, and no assignment or transfer will occur in connection with the transaction. Accordingly, no consent or other action is required of Provider under the MSA in connection with the merger. Lebix is giving this letter as a matter of courtesy and to support continued coordination between our teams.

**3. Continuity of Services.** Lebix expects no change to the Services, the Stations, the Catering Orders currently in effect, or Lebix's operational contacts as a result of the transaction. This includes the Harrisburg International Airport (MDT) Station added by Amendment No. 1. The Parties' Quality Representatives under the Food Safety and Quality Agreement will remain in place. Lebix asks that Provider continue to perform the Services in the ordinary course under the MSA, the Quality Agreement and the applicable Catering Orders.

**4. Invoicing and Notices.** Invoices should continue to be addressed to Lebix as they are today. Notices to Customer under Section 18.3 of the MSA should continue to be sent to the address and email shown in that Section. Lebix will notify Provider in writing if any of its notice or billing details change after closing.

**5. Confidentiality.** The information in this letter, including the existence and terms of the transaction, is Confidential Information of Lebix under Section 13 of the MSA. Lebix and Sablecrest will make a public announcement in due course. Until then, please limit disclosure of this letter to those of your personnel and advisors who need to know it.

**6. Contact.** Please direct any questions about this letter to the undersigned at legalnotices@lebixair.com.

We value our relationship with Dugein and look forward to continuing it.
