# FOOD SAFETY AND QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Food Safety and Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Quality Agreement Effective Date**") by and between:

**Lebix Regional Airways, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4400 Terminal Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dugein Inflight Provisions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1250 Cargo Road, Building C, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19153 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Catering Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, Provider performs inflight catering, provisioning, flight-kitchen and related services for Customer.

B. The MSA requires Provider to perform the Services in compliance with this Quality Agreement.

C. This Quality Agreement sets out the food safety and quality requirements, responsibilities and procedures that apply to the Meals and the Services.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA. In this Quality Agreement:

"**Allergen Matrix**" means the Customer-approved record of the major food allergens, as identified under Applicable Law, contained in each Meal and each component of a Meal.

"**Corrective Action Plan**" means a written plan prepared by Provider under Section 7. Each plan identifies the root cause of a non-conformance, the corrective and preventive actions to be taken, the responsible persons and the completion dates.

"**Critical Control Point**" means a point, step or procedure in Provider's food safety plan at which control can be applied and is essential to prevent or eliminate a food safety hazard or reduce it to an acceptable level.

"**Food Safety Incident**" means a confirmed contamination of a Meal that results in illness of a passenger or crew member and is reported to a regulatory authority.

"**Food Safety Plan**" means the written food safety plan that Provider maintains at each flight kitchen under Section 3.2 of the MSA and Section 3 of this Quality Agreement.

"**Quality Representative**" means the individual each Party appoints under Section 2.1.

"**Recall**" means the removal or correction of Meals that have been loaded onto Customer's aircraft or delivered to Customer, whether initiated by a Party or required by a regulatory authority.

"**Specifications**" means the menus, recipes, portion specifications, ingredient lists, Allergen Matrix, labeling and presentation standards approved by Customer under Section 2.4 of the MSA.

"**Withdrawal**" means the removal of Meals from Provider's facilities, catering trucks or staging areas before they are loaded onto Customer's aircraft.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the MSA applies to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full. References to Sections are to sections of this Quality Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. QUALITY GOVERNANCE

**2.1 Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall appoint a Quality Representative and notify the other Party of that person's name, title and 24-hour contact details. Provider's Quality Representative shall hold a recognized food safety qualification and shall be available, directly or through a designated deputy, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Either Party may replace its Quality Representative on written notice.

**2.2 Quality Reviews.** The Quality Representatives shall meet at least quarterly to review food safety and quality performance. The review shall cover audit findings, customer complaints, open Corrective Action Plans, supplier changes and regulatory developments. Provider shall circulate a written quality report at least five (5) business days before each meeting.

**2.3 Escalation.** Any food safety or quality matter that the Quality Representatives cannot resolve within ten (10) business days shall be escalated under Section 18.4 of the MSA.

## 3. FOOD SAFETY PLAN AND CONTROLS

**3.1 Food Safety Plan.** Provider shall maintain and implement a Food Safety Plan at each flight kitchen serving the Stations. The plan shall meet 21 C.F.R. Part 117 and other Applicable Law. It shall include at least:

(a) a hazard analysis covering biological, chemical, physical and allergen hazards;

(b) identified Critical Control Points and preventive controls, with critical limits, monitoring procedures and verification activities;

(c) allergen controls, including segregation, labeling, changeover cleaning and verification against the Allergen Matrix;

(d) sanitation controls and environmental monitoring, including pathogen testing of food-contact and non-food-contact surfaces;

(e) cold-chain and hot-holding controls under Section 5;

(f) foreign material controls, including metal detection or inspection where appropriate; and

(g) a supply-chain program for approval and monitoring of ingredient suppliers under Section 6.

**3.2 Review and Changes.** Provider shall review each Food Safety Plan at least annually and whenever a change in ingredients, processes, equipment or facilities could affect food safety. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' written notice of any material change to a Food Safety Plan that affects the Meals.

**3.3 Certification.** Provider shall maintain certification of each flight kitchen serving the Stations under a Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI)-benchmarked scheme. Provider shall deliver copies of current certificates and audit reports to Customer on request.

**3.4 Water and Ice.** Provider shall use only potable water and ice made from potable water in the preparation and handling of Meals. Provider shall test water sources as Applicable Law requires.

## 4. FOOD SAFETY INCIDENTS, WITHDRAWALS AND RECALLS

**4.1 Detection.** Provider shall maintain procedures to detect, investigate and record any potential food safety hazard affecting the Meals. This includes customer and crew complaints, monitoring deviations and laboratory results.

**4.2 Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer's Quality Representative by telephone and email within two (2) hours after Provider becomes aware of a Food Safety Incident. The notice shall identify, to the extent known: the affected Meals, lot or batch codes, production dates, flights and Stations; the nature of the Food Safety Incident; the number of persons affected; and the immediate containment actions taken. Provider shall give Customer written updates at least every twenty-four (24) hours until the Food Safety Incident is closed.

**4.3 Containment.** On becoming aware of a Food Safety Incident, Provider shall immediately quarantine all affected and potentially affected Meals, ingredients and production runs. Provider shall not release quarantined product without Customer's written approval.

**4.4 Withdrawals and Recalls.** Provider shall carry out a Withdrawal or Recall of Meals affected by a Food Safety Incident:

(a) at Customer's reasonable direction;

(b) where a regulatory authority requires it; or

(c) where Provider's Food Safety Plan requires it.

Provider shall coordinate the Withdrawal or Recall with Customer's operations control center so that affected Meals are removed from aircraft without avoidable flight delay. Provider shall supply replacement provisioning where reasonably practicable.

**4.5 Mock Recalls.** Provider shall conduct a mock recall at each flight kitchen at least twice in each contract year. Each mock recall shall trace at least one finished Meal back to its ingredient lots and forward to the flights on which it was loaded. Provider shall share the results with Customer within ten (10) business days.

**4.6 Regulatory Communications.** Provider shall not make any public statement about a Food Safety Incident involving Customer's flights without Customer's prior written approval, except where Applicable Law requires. In that case, Provider shall give Customer as much advance notice as is lawful. Each Party shall comply with Section 3.5 of the MSA.

**4.7 Costs.** Section 3.4 of the MSA governs the costs of any Withdrawal or Recall.

## 5. TEMPERATURE AND COLD-CHAIN CONTROL

**5.1 Temperature Limits.** Provider shall hold chilled Meals at or below 41°F (5°C) and hot-held Meals at or above 135°F (57°C) from production through loading. Provider shall chill cooked foods within the time and temperature limits set out in the FDA Food Code as adopted in the applicable jurisdiction.

**5.2 Monitoring.** Provider shall monitor and record temperatures at each Critical Control Point, in each cold storage unit and in each catering truck. Provider shall use calibrated devices and, for storage units and trucks, continuous data loggers. Provider shall retain temperature records under Section 9.1 of the MSA.

**5.3 Disposition.** Provider shall discard, and not load, any Meal held outside the limits in Section 5.1 beyond the time permitted by Applicable Law or the Food Safety Plan.

**5.4 Aircraft Loading.** Provider shall load chilled Meals into pre-chilled galley carts or with dry ice or other approved coolant, in accordance with the station loading plans. Provider shall load them as close to departure as the Catering Order permits.

## 6. INGREDIENTS AND SUPPLIERS

**6.1 Approved Suppliers.** Provider shall source ingredients and packaging only from suppliers approved under its supply-chain program. Each such supplier shall be subject to documented risk assessment, specifications and periodic verification.

**6.2 Supplier Changes.** Section 2.4 of the MSA applies to any substitution of ingredients or suppliers. Provider shall update the Allergen Matrix and obtain Customer's approval before any change that affects allergen content takes effect.

**6.3 Certifications.** For Meals designated as halal, kosher, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or other special meal types, Provider shall maintain the applicable certifications and segregation controls and provide evidence to Customer on request.

## 7. NON-CONFORMANCES AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

**7.1 Non-Conformances.** A non-conformance is any failure of the Meals or Services to meet the Specifications, the Food Safety Plan, this Quality Agreement or Applicable Law. Provider shall record each non-conformance and investigate its root cause.

**7.2 Corrective Action Plans.** Provider shall deliver a Corrective Action Plan to Customer:

(a) within five (5) business days after closure of any Food Safety Incident;

(b) within ten (10) business days after any audit finding classified as major or critical; and

(c) within ten (10) business days after Customer's written request regarding any other non-conformance.

Provider shall implement each Corrective Action Plan by the stated completion dates and report progress at each quality review under Section 2.2.

**7.3 Repeat Non-Conformances.** If the same non-conformance recurs at a Station within ninety (90) days after closure of a Corrective Action Plan, Provider's Quality Representative and a senior operations executive shall meet with Customer within five (5) business days to agree on additional measures.

## 8. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

**8.1 Scheduled Audits.** Customer and its designees may conduct food safety and quality audits of any flight kitchen, storage facility or vehicle used in the Services. Customer shall give at least five (5) business days' notice of a scheduled audit.

**8.2 Unannounced Audits.** Customer may conduct up to four (4) unannounced audits at each flight kitchen in each contract year. Customer may conduct additional unannounced audits following any Food Safety Incident or any audit finding classified as critical. Provider shall grant immediate access during operating hours, subject to reasonable safety and security requirements.

**8.3 Audit Findings.** Customer shall deliver a written audit report within ten (10) business days after each audit. Findings shall be classified as minor, major or critical, and Provider shall address them under Section 7.

**8.4 Third-Party and Regulatory Inspections.** Provider shall give Customer copies of all regulatory inspection reports, Form 483 observations, warning letters and certification audit reports relating to the flight kitchens serving the Stations within five (5) business days after receipt.

## 9. TRAINING AND HYGIENE

**9.1 Training.** Provider shall ensure that all personnel handling Meals receive documented food safety, allergen awareness and personal hygiene training before starting work, with refresher training at least annually.

**9.2 Health Policy.** Provider shall maintain and enforce an employee health policy that complies with Applicable Law. The policy shall include exclusion and restriction of personnel with reportable illnesses or symptoms.

**9.3 Pest Control.** Provider shall maintain a documented pest control program at each flight kitchen, administered by a licensed pest control operator.

## 10. RECORDS AND TRACEABILITY

**10.1 Traceability.** Provider shall maintain lot-level traceability for each Meal from ingredient receipt through production, storage and loading. The records shall identify the flight and aircraft on which each Meal was loaded. Provider shall be able to produce traceability records for any Meal within four (4) hours of Customer's request.

**10.2 Retention.** Provider shall retain all records required by this Quality Agreement in accordance with Section 9.1 of the MSA.

**10.3 Retained Samples.** Provider shall retain, under refrigeration or freezing as appropriate, a sample of each high-risk Meal component produced for Customer's flights for at least seventy-two (72) hours after loading.

## 11. TERM AND GENERAL

**11.1 Term.** This Quality Agreement begins on the Quality Agreement Effective Date and continues for so long as the MSA remains in effect, including any transition period under Section 17.1 of the MSA.

**11.2 Relationship to the MSA.** This Quality Agreement forms part of the Parties' arrangement under the MSA. Section 1.3 of the MSA governs the relationship between this Quality Agreement and the MSA.

**11.3 Incorporation.** Sections 13 (Confidentiality), 18.1 (Governing Law), 18.2 (Jurisdiction), 18.3 (Notices), 18.7 (Amendment; Waiver), 18.8 (Severability) and 18.9 (Counterparts) of the MSA apply to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full.

**11.4 Survival.** Sections 4, 7, 10 and 11 survive expiry or termination for so long as any Meal supplied under the MSA may be subject to a Withdrawal, Recall or regulatory inquiry.
