# JOINT PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## 1. Headline

**Vaikiv Therapeutics Completes Acquisition of Oravexin Extended-Release Tablet Franchise from Taigon Pharma Holdings**

*Transaction adds an established, once-daily commercial product to Vaikiv's portfolio*

## 2. Announcement

**WILMINGTON, Del., March 2, 2026.** Vaikiv Therapeutics, Inc. ("Vaikiv") and Taigon Pharma Holdings, LLC ("Taigon") today announced the completion of Vaikiv's acquisition of the Oravexin franchise from Taigon. The parties entered into the Asset Purchase Agreement for the transaction on February 9, 2026, and the closing occurred on March 2, 2026.

Under the agreement, Vaikiv has acquired the assets relating to Oravexin 40 mg extended-release tablets (NDA 214-883) for a purchase price of $186,000,000 in cash. The acquired assets include:

- the New Drug Application and related regulatory filings;
- the product's patents, the ORAVEXIN trademarks and related know-how;
- existing product inventory; and
- the contract manufacturing and supply relationship for the product.

## 3. Transition

The NDA transfer letters have been submitted to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Under a Transition Services Agreement entered into at closing, Taigon will provide Vaikiv with regulatory, pharmacovigilance, distribution, supply chain and quality services for Oravexin for a period ending nine months after the closing date. The aim is an orderly transfer of the franchise with continued supply to patients, pharmacies and wholesalers.

Healthcare providers and patients should continue to report adverse events and product complaints for Oravexin through the existing channels listed in the product labeling. They will be notified of any change to those channels.

## 4. Management Comments

"Oravexin is a well-established product with a durable patent position, and it fits squarely within our commercial strategy," said the Chief Executive Officer of Vaikiv. "We look forward to building on the franchise and working closely with Taigon to transition it smoothly."

"We are pleased to have completed this transaction with Vaikiv," said the Chief Executive Officer of Taigon. "We are committed to supporting Vaikiv throughout the transition period to ensure continuity for patients and customers."

## 5. About Oravexin

Oravexin 40 mg extended-release tablets are a once-daily oral therapy approved by the FDA under NDA 214-883. Please see the full Prescribing Information for Oravexin for indications, dosing and important safety information.
