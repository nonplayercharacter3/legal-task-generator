# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Taigon Pharma Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Vaikiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller the Purchased Assets relating to Oravexin 40 mg extended-release tablets (NDA 214-883) (the "**Product**").

B. Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under it, Seller will provide certain regulatory, pharmacovigilance, distribution, supply chain and quality services relating to the Product to Buyer for a transitional period.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**APA Closing Date**" means the Closing Date under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Applicable Law**" means all Laws applicable to the Product or to the performance of the Services, including the FDCA, current Good Manufacturing Practice requirements under 21 C.F.R. Parts 210 and 211, and the postmarketing reporting requirements under 21 C.F.R. Part 314.

"**CMO**" means Brennholt Contract Manufacturing, LLC, the counterparty to the Manufacturing Agreement.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 3.3.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule A.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the APA Closing Date and ending on the earlier of (a) the end date stated for that Service in Schedule A and (b) the expiration or termination of this Agreement under Section 7.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A and any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.3.

"**Transition Plan**" means the written plan for the migration of each Service from Seller to Buyer or its designee, to be agreed under Section 2.5.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Seller shall provide, or cause its Affiliates or permitted subcontractors to provide, the Services to Buyer and its Affiliates on the terms of this Agreement.

**2.2 Scope.** The Services shall be substantially similar in nature, scope, quality and priority to the corresponding services Seller provided for the Product during the twelve months before the Signing Date. Seller is not required to provide any Service in a manner that would require it to hire additional personnel, acquire new systems or violate Applicable Law. However, this sentence does not excuse Seller from providing any Service described in Schedule A.

**2.3 Additional Services.** If Buyer identifies a service that Seller provided for the Product before the Closing and that is reasonably necessary to operate the Business but is not described in Schedule A (an "**Additional Service**"), Buyer may request that Seller provide it. Seller shall provide that Additional Service on terms, including fees, that are consistent with Schedule A and that the Parties agree in writing in good faith. Once agreed, each Additional Service is a Service for all purposes of this Agreement.

**2.4 Subcontractors.** Seller may use subcontractors to perform the Services only to the extent it used them for the corresponding services before the Closing, or with Buyer's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Seller remains responsible for the performance of each subcontractor as if it were Seller's own performance.

**2.5 Transition Plan and Migration.** Within 30 days after the APA Closing Date, the Service Coordinators shall agree on a Transition Plan. Seller shall cooperate in good faith with Buyer to migrate each Service to Buyer or its designee before the end of its Service Period. As part of that cooperation, Seller shall extract and deliver to Buyer, in a commercially reasonable electronic format, all Product-specific data held in Seller's enterprise resource planning, quality management and safety database systems, as described in Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule. The data deliveries shall be made in the phases set out in Schedule A, and the final delivery shall be made no later than 30 days before the end of the applicable Service Period.

## 3. SERVICE STANDARDS AND GOVERNANCE

**3.1 Standard of Performance.** Seller shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, using personnel with appropriate qualifications and training, (b) with at least the same degree of care, skill and timeliness as Seller used in providing the corresponding services for the Product before the Closing, and (c) in compliance with Applicable Law and the Manufacturing Agreement.

**3.2 Records.** Seller shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services, including all batch-release documentation, adverse event intake records, distribution records and regulatory correspondence. Seller shall deliver copies to Buyer on request and in any event at the end of the applicable Service Period.

**3.3 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall designate a service coordinator (each, a "**Service Coordinator**") who will act as its primary point of contact for the Services. The Service Coordinators shall meet at least every two weeks during the first three months of the term and at least monthly after that to review performance, the Transition Plan and any open issues.

**3.4 Escalation.** Any dispute concerning the performance of the Services shall first be referred to the Service Coordinators. If they cannot resolve it within 10 Business Days, it shall be referred to a senior executive of each Party with authority to settle it. If those executives cannot resolve it within a further 10 Business Days, either Party may pursue its remedies under Section 9.3.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** In consideration of the Services, Buyer shall pay Seller the Service Fees. Buyer shall also reimburse Seller's reasonable documented out-of-pocket costs paid to third parties for the Services, without markup, to the extent stated in Schedule A or approved in advance in writing by Buyer.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Seller shall invoice Buyer monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Services provided and the reimbursable costs, with reasonable supporting documentation.

**4.3 Payment.** Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt. If Buyer disputes any part of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Seller in writing, pay the undisputed portion and work with Seller to resolve the dispute under Section 3.4.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value added and similar taxes, which Buyer shall bear, other than taxes based on Seller's net income.

## 5. REGULATORY AND QUALITY MATTERS

**5.1 NDA Holder Status.** Until the FDA acknowledges the transfer of the NDA to Buyer under Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall maintain the NDA for the benefit of Buyer and shall make all regulatory submissions for the Product as Buyer reasonably directs. After the FDA acknowledges the transfer, Buyer shall be responsible for all submissions as NDA holder. Seller shall continue to provide the regulatory support Services described in Schedule A.

**5.2 Pharmacovigilance.** During the applicable Service Period, Seller shall receive, process and report adverse events and product complaints for the Product in accordance with Applicable Law and the safety data exchange procedures in Schedule A. Seller shall forward to Buyer each adverse event report within two Business Days after receipt, and each serious and unexpected adverse event report within one Business Day after receipt.

**5.3 Batch Release.** Until the Quality Agreement has been assigned to Buyer and Buyer's quality unit has assumed batch release responsibility under the Transition Plan, Seller's quality unit shall review CMO batch records and release batches of the Product in accordance with the Quality Agreement and Applicable Law. Seller shall not release any batch that does not meet the NDA specifications. Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any out-of-specification result, deviation or investigation relating to any batch.

**5.4 Recalls and Field Actions.** Each Party shall notify the other within one Business Day after becoming aware of any information that may require a recall, market withdrawal, field alert report or field correction for the Product. Decisions on any such action relating to units manufactured or sold after the APA Closing Date shall be made by Buyer. Seller shall provide the recall support Services described in Schedule A. Responsibility for the costs of any such action is governed by Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.5 Inspections and Audits.** Seller shall notify Buyer promptly of any inspection or inquiry by a Governmental Authority relating to the Product or the Services. Buyer may attend, to the extent permitted by the Governmental Authority, and may review and comment on any response before it is submitted. On 10 Business Days' notice, and not more than once per calendar quarter unless for cause, Buyer may audit Seller's performance of the Services.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

**6.1 Confidentiality.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party received in connection with this Agreement and shall use it only to perform or receive the Services. Information relating to the Product and the Purchased Assets is Buyer's confidential information and is also subject to Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Ownership of Data.** All data and records relating to the Product that are generated, collected or processed in performing the Services are the property of Buyer. Seller shall hold them as custodian for Buyer during the applicable Service Period.

**6.3 Data Protection.** Seller shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Product data, including personal information contained in adverse event reports. Seller shall notify Buyer within 48 hours after discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of that data.

**6.4 Return of Data.** At the end of each Service Period, Seller shall deliver to Buyer all Product data relating to that Service that has not previously been delivered. Seller may then delete it from its systems, except for archival copies that Seller is required by Applicable Law to retain, which shall remain subject to Section 6.1.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until nine months after the APA Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under this Section 7. Buyer may extend the Service Period for any Service by up to 90 days by written notice to Seller given at least 30 days before the scheduled end of that Service Period. Fees for the extension period shall be the Service Fees for that Service in effect immediately before the extension.

**7.2 Termination of Individual Services by Buyer.** Buyer may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on 30 days' written notice to Seller. Service Fees for a terminated Service end on the effective date of termination. If a Service depends on another Service, Seller shall identify that dependency to Buyer in writing within five Business Days after receiving the notice.

**7.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing the breach. However, Seller may not terminate or suspend any regulatory, pharmacovigilance or batch release Service for Buyer's non-payment of fees that Buyer disputes in good faith under Section 4.3.

**7.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Buyer shall pay all undisputed Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Seller shall deliver all Product data and records as required by Sections 3.2 and 6.4. Sections 4, 6, 7.4, 8 and 9 survive expiration or termination.

## 8. LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnification by Seller.** Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of (a) Seller's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud in performing the Services, (b) Seller's breach of Section 5 or Section 6, or (c) Seller's violation of Applicable Law in performing the Services.

**8.2 Indemnification by Buyer.** Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of (a) Buyer's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud in receiving the Services, or (b) Seller's performance of a Service in accordance with Buyer's written direction, except to the extent of Seller's own negligence or breach.

**8.3 Limitation of Liability.** Except for Losses arising out of a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, breach of Section 6 or indemnification obligations under this Section 8 for third-party claims, neither Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. Neither Party shall be liable for consequential, indirect or punitive damages under this Agreement, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**8.4 Procedure.** Claims for indemnification under this Section 8 shall be made and defended in accordance with the procedures in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, applied with the necessary changes.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement. A copy of each notice relating to Services shall also be sent to the recipient's Service Coordinator.

**9.2 Independent Contractor.** Seller acts as an independent contractor in providing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

**9.3 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and any claim, controversy or dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement. Each Party agrees not to commence any such action in any other court, and waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that any such action has been brought in an inconvenient forum.

**9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.5 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. A force majeure event does not excuse Seller's obligations under Sections 5.2 and 5.4 to the extent they can be performed by alternative means.

**9.6 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, Buyer may assign this Agreement, without that consent, to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement under Section 11.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

**9.7 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against which it is asserted.

**9.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF, each of which shall be deemed an original. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
