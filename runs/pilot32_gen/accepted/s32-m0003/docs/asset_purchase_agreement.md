# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**") by and between **Vaikiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Taigon Pharma Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns and operates the business of developing, manufacturing (through a third-party contract manufacturer), marketing, distributing and selling the Product in the United States (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Cap**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Deductible**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a).

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association.

"**FDA**" means the United States Food and Drug Administration or any successor agency.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), 4.4 (Title to Purchased Assets) and 4.12 (Brokers), and Sections 5.1 (Organization), 5.2 (Authority; Enforceability) and 5.5 (Brokers).

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Buyer and Seller, substantially in the form attached as Exhibit B.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and accept from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed on Schedule 2.1 of the Disclosure Schedule, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) the NDA and all other regulatory approvals, filings and submissions relating to the Product;

(b) the Product Intellectual Property;

(c) the Inventory;

(d) the Manufacturing Agreement and the other contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(d) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(e) all books, records, batch records, stability data, pharmacovigilance and adverse event databases, complaint files and promotional materials relating exclusively to the Product; and

(f) all claims, causes of action and rights of recovery against third parties to the extent relating to the Purchased Assets, other than those relating to Excluded Liabilities.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include any assets of Seller other than those listed in Section 2.1, including cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable arising from sales of the Product before the Closing, Seller's corporate records and any assets listed on Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising from performance required after the Closing, but excluding any Liability arising from any breach or default by Seller before the Closing; and

(b) Liabilities arising from Buyer's ownership or operation of the Purchased Assets after the Closing, including product liability claims relating to units of the Product manufactured and sold by or on behalf of Buyer after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and remain responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller and its Affiliates other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all Liabilities arising from the ownership or operation of the Business or the Purchased Assets before the Closing;

(b) all Liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any pre-Closing tax period;

(c) all Liabilities arising out of or relating to product liability claims, recalls, market withdrawals, field corrections or similar actions with respect to units of the Product manufactured, distributed or sold before the Closing, except to the extent provided in Schedule 2.4(c);

(d) all Liabilities for returns, chargebacks, rebates, Medicaid and other governmental pricing program payments and wholesaler fees relating to units of the Product sold before the Closing;

(e) all Liabilities relating to employees, contractors or benefit plans of Seller or its Affiliates; and

(f) all Liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets.

**2.5 Non-Assignable Contracts.** If any Assigned Contract may not be assigned without the consent of a third party and that consent has not been obtained by the Closing, this Agreement shall not constitute an assignment of that Assigned Contract. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent and, pending receipt, shall provide Buyer the benefits of that Assigned Contract, and Buyer shall perform Seller's obligations under it to the extent of those benefits.

**2.6 Purchase Price.** The aggregate consideration for the Purchased Assets is $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.7 Escrow.** The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent as a source of recovery, but not the exclusive source, for Seller's obligations under Article IX, and shall be released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.8 Allocation.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a proposed allocation of the Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. The Parties shall resolve any disagreements in good faith and shall file all tax returns consistently with the final allocation.

**2.9 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on March 2, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(c) Seller's executed NDA Transfer Letter;

(d) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 3.2(d) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the consent of the counterparty to the Manufacturing Agreement;

(e) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(f) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments described in Section 2.6;

(b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer;

(c) Buyer's executed NDA Transfer Letter; and

(d) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Signing Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has all requisite power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has all requisite power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been, and each such Ancillary Agreement will be at the Closing, duly executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws and general principles of equity.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Purchased Assets, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, require any consent under, or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed on Schedule 2.1 of the Disclosure Schedule. The Purchased Assets, together with the services to be provided under the Transition Services Agreement, constitute all assets necessary to conduct the Business as conducted by Seller as of the Signing Date.

**4.5 Regulatory Matters.** (a) Seller is the sole holder of the NDA, which is in full force and effect. Seller has filed all annual reports, supplements, field alert reports and other submissions required to be filed with the FDA for the Product, and all such submissions were complete and accurate in all material respects when filed.

(b) Since January 1, 2022, the Product has been developed, manufactured, tested, labeled, stored, distributed, marketed and sold in compliance in all material respects with the FDCA and all other applicable Laws, including current Good Manufacturing Practice requirements.

(c) Except as set forth on Schedule 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule, since January 1, 2022, neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, the counterparty to the Manufacturing Agreement has received any FDA Form 483, warning letter, untitled letter, "it has come to our attention" letter or other written notice of noncompliance from any Governmental Authority with respect to the Product or any facility at which the Product is manufactured.

**4.6 Product Quality.** Each unit of the Product included in the Inventory (a) was manufactured in accordance with the NDA, its specifications and current Good Manufacturing Practice, (b) is not adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of the FDCA, and (c) has a remaining shelf life of not less than the minimum stated on Schedule 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule. Seller has made available to Buyer all stability data for the Product, and no out-of-specification or out-of-trend stability result for any commercial lot remains uninvestigated.

**4.7 Recalls.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule, since January 1, 2022, there has been no recall, market withdrawal, field correction or safety alert relating to the Product, whether voluntary or required by a Governmental Authority, and to Seller's knowledge no facts exist that would reasonably be expected to result in any such action.

**4.8 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 2.1(b) of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered Product Intellectual Property. Seller exclusively owns the Product Intellectual Property. To Seller's knowledge, the manufacture, use and sale of the Product do not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person, and no Person is infringing the Product Intellectual Property.

**4.9 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract, and Seller has not received written notice of termination of any Assigned Contract.

**4.10 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action, suit, investigation or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Product, the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.11 Inventory.** The Inventory consists of items of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course and is stored in accordance with the Product labeling and applicable Law.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the Signing Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Buyer has all requisite corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws and general principles of equity.

**5.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds available to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the Signing Date until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the NDA in good standing, maintain the Inventory at normal levels and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, amend or terminate any Assigned Contract or sell, license or encumber any Purchased Asset.

**6.2 Access.** From the Signing Date until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the books, records, regulatory files and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Regulatory Transfer.** On the Closing Date, the Parties shall submit the NDA Transfer Letters to the FDA. Until the FDA acknowledges the transfer of the NDA, Seller shall maintain the NDA for the benefit of Buyer and shall take such regulatory actions relating to the Product as Buyer reasonably directs.

**6.4 Notice of Recalls and Regulatory Events.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other of any recall, market withdrawal, adverse event trend or communication from a Governmental Authority relating to the Product of which it becomes aware, and the Parties shall cooperate in good faith in responding to it. Seller shall bear the cost of any such action to the extent it is an Excluded Liability.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, keep confidential all information relating to the Product and the Purchased Assets, except as required by Law.

**6.6 Non-Competition.** For three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly develop, market or sell in the United States any oral extended-release product containing the same active ingredient as the Product.

**6.7 Public Announcements.** The Parties shall issue a joint press release upon the Closing in a form agreed by both Parties. Otherwise, neither Party shall make any public announcement regarding this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except as required by Law or stock exchange rules.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party may reasonably request to carry out the intent of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b), and all deliverables listed in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b), and all deliverables listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of the Parties;

(b) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred by April 30, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close by that date; or

(c) by either Party, if the other Party has materially breached this Agreement and has failed to cure that breach within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect, except that Sections 6.5, 6.7 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a Party of Liability for Fraud or willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV, other than the Fundamental Representations, including the representations and warranties in Sections 4.5, 4.6 and 4.7, shall survive the Closing until the Release Date (as defined in the Escrow Agreement). The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall survive the Closing for the same periods as the corresponding representations of Seller. Covenants and agreements to be performed after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) may be brought unless written notice of that claim, describing it in reasonable detail, is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period. Any claim properly noticed before that expiration shall survive until finally resolved. The Parties intend the survival periods in this Section 9.1 to shorten the otherwise applicable statute of limitations in accordance with 10 Del. C. § 8106(c).

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any breach of or inaccuracy in any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any breach of or inaccuracy in any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** (a) A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice of the claim to the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure.

(b) For any claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within 20 days after receiving notice of the claim, except that Seller may not assume the defense of any claim involving a Governmental Authority, a recall or a criminal allegation relating to the Product. The Indemnified Party may participate with its own counsel at its own expense. Neither Party shall settle a third-party claim without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.** (a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2(a) exceed $1,395,000 (the "**Deductible**"), in which case Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Cap**"). Buyer's liability under Section 9.3(a) shall be subject to a deductible and cap of the same amounts.

(b) The Cap and the Deductible shall not apply to Losses arising out of or relating to (i) any Excluded Liability under Section 9.2(c), or (ii) Fraud.

(c) The Cap and the Deductible shall not apply to Losses arising out of any breach of a Fundamental Representation. The aggregate liability of Seller for such Losses, together with all other Losses under Section 9.2(a), shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, and for determining whether a breach has occurred, each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality qualifier in it.

**9.6 Sources of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Amount then held by the Escrow Agent, and thereafter directly by Seller. Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent as required by the Escrow Agreement to give effect to any final determination under this Article IX.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive monetary remedy of the Parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement, except for claims of Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.11.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** The Parties shall treat any indemnification payment under this Article IX as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEES AND TRANSITION

**10.1 No Transfer of Employees.** Buyer shall have no obligation to offer employment to any employee of Seller or its Affiliates. Seller shall retain all Liabilities relating to its employees.

**10.2 Transition Services.** At the Closing, the Parties shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide to Buyer certain regulatory, pharmacovigilance, distribution, supply chain and quality services relating to the Product for a period ending nine months after the Closing Date, on the terms set forth in that agreement.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action (whether in contract, tort or otherwise) arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY IT.

**11.11 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each Party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief to prevent breaches of this Agreement, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled, without proof of actual damages or the posting of any bond.

The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. The Disclosure Schedule is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement.
