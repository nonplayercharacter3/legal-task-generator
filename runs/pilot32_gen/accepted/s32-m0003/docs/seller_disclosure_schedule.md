### Schedule 2.4(c): Product Liability and Recall Liabilities

**2025 Voluntary Recall of Oravexin Lots OX-2511 through OX-2519**

1. *Description.* On September 17, 2025, Seller initiated a voluntary recall to the retail level of Oravexin 40 mg extended-release tablets, Lots OX-2511, OX-2512, OX-2513, OX-2514, OX-2515, OX-2516, OX-2517, OX-2518 and OX-2519 (the "**2025 Recall**"). The 2025 Recall followed out-of-specification dissolution results at the 12-month stability time point for Lot OX-2514, and a subsequent investigation identified a granulation moisture deviation at the CMO's facility affecting all nine lots. The FDA classified the 2025 Recall as a Class II recall on October 6, 2025 (Recall Event No. 94417). The recalled lots were manufactured by the CMO between November 2024 and March 2025 and distributed by Seller before the Signing Date.

2. *Status.* As of the Signing Date, Seller has completed notification of all direct consignees and has received returns representing approximately 81% of distributed units of the recalled lots. Seller has not received FDA notice of termination of the 2025 Recall. Seller has submitted its corrective and preventive action plan to the FDA, and the CMO implemented revised in-process moisture controls on July 28, 2025.

3. *Treatment.* Liabilities arising out of or relating to the 2025 Recall, including costs of notification, retrieval, destruction and replacement of recalled units, customer credits and reimbursements, regulatory response costs and any product liability claims relating to units from the recalled lots, are Excluded Liabilities. Seller's indemnification of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2(c) for Losses arising out of or relating to the 2025 Recall shall be subject to the Cap and the Deductible under Section 9.5 notwithstanding Section 9.5(b).

### Schedule 4.5: Regulatory Matters

1. On June 12, 2025, following a pre-approval and surveillance inspection conducted May 27 through June 6, 2025, the FDA issued a Form FDA 483 to the CMO for its facility in Raleigh, North Carolina (FEI No. 3011847720). The Form 483 contained three observations, relating to (a) in-process moisture monitoring during granulation, (b) the timeliness of deviation investigations and (c) equipment cleaning log documentation. The CMO submitted its written response on June 30, 2025. The FDA classified the inspection as Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI) by letter dated November 4, 2025.
