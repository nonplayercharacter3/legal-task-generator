# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and among **Vaikiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Taigon Pharma Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller the Purchased Assets relating to Oravexin 40 mg extended-release tablets (NDA 214-883) for a Purchase Price of $186,000,000.

B. Section 2.6(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount serves as a source of recovery for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND APPOINTMENT

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2(a).

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2, or an order or judgment for which the time to appeal has expired without an appeal being filed.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions executed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before the Release Date that have not been resolved and paid as of that date.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

**1.2 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

## 2. ESCROW DEPOSIT

**2.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $18,600,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day it is received.

**2.2 Segregated Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated escrow account in the Escrow Agent's name for the benefit of Buyer and Seller (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Fund shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent.

**2.3 No Interest in Escrow Fund.** Neither Buyer nor Seller may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer any interest in the Escrow Fund, except as provided in this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Fund shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party, except as required by applicable law.

## 3. INVESTMENT

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the Ostrander Government Obligations Money Market Fund (Institutional Class), or in such other investments as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instructions. If no investment direction is in effect, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make any disbursement under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall have no liability for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3 or from any liquidation of an investment made to fund a disbursement.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund form part of the Escrow Fund and shall be disbursed with it.

## 4. RELEASE DATE AND DISBURSEMENTS

**4.1 Release Date.** The "**Release Date**" means the date that is ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, or, if that date is not a Business Day, the next Business Day. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund until the Release Date. Within three Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the then-remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the Pending Claim Amount. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the retained Pending Claim Amount and shall disburse it under Section 4.3.

**4.2 Claims Against the Escrow Fund.**

(a) *Claim Notice.* At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has incurred or reasonably expects to incur Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount claimed or, if that amount is not then determinable, Buyer's good-faith estimate of it.

(b) *Objection.* Seller may deliver to Buyer and the Escrow Agent a written objection to all or any part of a Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**") within 20 Business Days after receiving the Claim Notice. Each Objection Notice shall state the amount in dispute and describe the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

(c) *No Objection.* If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 20-Business-Day period in Section 4.2(b), Seller shall be deemed to have accepted the Claim Notice. The Escrow Agent shall then disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Fund the amount stated in the Claim Notice within three Business Days after the end of that period.

(d) *Partial Objection.* If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer the undisputed amount within three Business Days after receiving the Objection Notice. The Escrow Agent shall retain the disputed amount until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**4.3 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse any amount retained for a disputed claim, or for a Claim Notice pending as of the Release Date, only in accordance with:

(a) Joint Written Instructions; or

(b) a Final Order. The Final Order must be delivered to the Escrow Agent together with a certificate of the presenting party, confirming that the order is final and non-appealable, on which the Escrow Agent may rely without further inquiry.

When all claims relating to a retained amount have been finally resolved and paid, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any remaining part of that retained amount to Seller.

**4.4 Joint Written Instructions.** Buyer and Seller shall promptly execute and deliver Joint Written Instructions as necessary to give effect to any final determination of a claim under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent may, at any time, disburse all or any part of the Escrow Fund in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**4.5 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in Schedule 1, or to such other account as the receiving party may designate by written notice that the Escrow Agent has confirmed by a call-back under Section 5.6.

**4.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to pay any amount due to Buyer under this Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the balance of the Escrow Fund to Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall have no further obligation for the shortfall. Any recovery of the shortfall from Seller is governed by the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**5.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall perform only the duties expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, is not bound by and has no duty to inquire into the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or delivered by the proper person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to verify the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**5.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent of its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**5.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult with legal counsel of its choosing. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**5.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may file an interpleader action in a court specified in Section 10.2 and deposit the disputed funds with that court, after which it shall be relieved of all further obligations with respect to those funds.

**5.6 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any funds-transfer instruction, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative listed on Schedule 2. The Escrow Agent may rely on the confirmation of any person purporting to be that Authorized Representative.

**5.7 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent and its officers, directors, employees and agents against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Escrow Agreement. This indemnity does not cover amounts resulting from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear 50% of any amount paid under this Section 5.7. However, a party whose breach of this Escrow Agreement or the Purchase Agreement caused the amount to be incurred shall bear the entire amount.

## 6. FEES AND EXPENSES

**6.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3, together with reimbursement of its reasonable documented out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in performing its duties.

**6.2 Allocation.** In accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each pay 50% of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Fund unless they remain unpaid for more than 30 days after invoice. In that case, the party that failed to pay shall reimburse the Escrow Fund for the amount deducted.

## 7. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**7.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving Buyer and Seller at least 30 days' prior written notice.

**7.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions given at least 30 days in advance.

**7.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The successor must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has accepted appointment within 30 days after the notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court specified in Section 10.2 to appoint a successor. Until a successor is appointed, the Escrow Agent's only duty shall be to hold the Escrow Fund. On the successor's acceptance of appointment, the Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Fund to it and shall be discharged from all further duties under this Escrow Agreement.

**7.4 Merger.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall succeed to its duties under this Escrow Agreement without further act.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state and local income tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Fund. Any amount disbursed to Seller shall be treated as paid by Buyer to Seller at the time of disbursement, consistent with Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**8.2 Tax Reporting.** Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required of it under applicable law with respect to the Escrow Fund.

**8.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any taxes it is required by law to withhold, and shall remit those taxes to the appropriate taxing authority.

## 9. NOTICES

**9.1 Delivery.** All notices, instructions and other communications under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing. They shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email in PDF form with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or such other address as a party may designate by notice):

If to Buyer: Vaikiv Therapeutics, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, with a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to Buyer's outside counsel.

If to Seller: Taigon Pharma Holdings, LLC, Attention: Chief Legal Officer, with a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to Seller's outside counsel.

If to the Escrow Agent: Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration (Vaikiv / Taigon).

**9.2 Effectiveness for Escrow Agent.** A notice or instruction to the Escrow Agent shall be effective only on actual receipt by the Escrow Agent. Notices received after 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day, or on a day that is not a Business Day, shall be deemed received on the next Business Day.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that an action has been brought in an inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

**10.4 Termination.** This Escrow Agreement shall terminate when the Escrow Fund has been fully disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 5.3, 5.7 and 6 shall survive termination and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**10.6 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as provided in Section 7.4. Buyer may assign its rights under this Escrow Agreement to any permitted assignee of Buyer under Section 11.5 of the Purchase Agreement on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, governmental action, failures of communications or computer systems, and interruptions of the Federal Reserve wire system.

**10.9 USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is required by federal law to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person that opens an account. Each of them shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with that requirement.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF, each of which shall be deemed an original. All counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.
