## SCHEDULE 1: COMMERCIAL TERMS

**1. Licensed Modules.** The Software includes the following TideRoute modules:

(a) TideRoute Voyage Optimizer (weather routing, speed and consumption planning);
(b) TideRoute Fleet Performance (hull, propeller and engine performance analytics);
(c) TideRoute Emissions Reporter (CII, EU ETS and IMO DCS reporting); and
(d) TideRoute Fleet Dashboard (shore-side monitoring and reporting).

**2. Covered Vessels.** At the Effective Date, the Software will be configured for the dry-bulk vessels owned by Licensee and listed in the onboarding plan agreed by the Parties. Licensee may add further Vessels on thirty (30) days' notice at the per-vessel rate in paragraph 3.

**3. License Fees.** During the Initial Term, the annual license fee is US$18,500 per Vessel per contract year, invoiced annually in advance on the Effective Date and each anniversary of it. Vessels added mid-year are invoiced pro rata.

**4. Implementation Fee.** A one-time implementation and onboarding fee of US$95,000, invoiced on the Effective Date, covers configuration, data integration and up to ten (10) days of training for Authorized Users.

**5. Renewal Fee Adjustment.** At the start of each Renewal Term, the per-Vessel annual license fee in effect immediately before that Renewal Term increases by seven percent (7%). The adjusted fee applies throughout that Renewal Term.

**6. Additional Services.** Professional services outside the scope of paragraph 4 are charged at US$1,650 per consultant day, subject to a written estimate approved by Licensee in advance.

## SCHEDULE 3: SERVICE LEVELS

**1. Availability.** Licensor shall make the hosted components of the Software available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, measured at Licensor's network boundary and excluding Scheduled Maintenance and outages caused by force majeure under Section 14 or by Licensee's onboard equipment or connectivity.

**2. Scheduled Maintenance.** Licensor shall carry out scheduled maintenance outside 06:00 to 22:00 UTC on Business Days where practicable, shall give at least five (5) Business Days' notice, and shall limit scheduled maintenance to eight (8) hours per calendar month ("**Scheduled Maintenance**").

**3. Support.** Licensor shall provide support by email, web portal and telephone, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents, and during 08:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time on Business Days for other incidents.

**4. Response and Resolution Targets.**

| Severity | Description | Response | Target Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Software unavailable or routing outputs unavailable for one or more Vessels at sea | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| 2 | Material function impaired with no reasonable workaround | 4 hours | 2 Business Days |
| 3 | Non-critical function impaired or workaround available | 1 Business Day | 10 Business Days |
| 4 | Questions, documentation issues, enhancement requests | 2 Business Days | Next scheduled release |

**5. Service Credits.** If availability in a calendar month falls below the level in paragraph 1, Licensee is entitled to a credit against the next invoice equal to the following percentage of one-twelfth of the annual license fee:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to below 99.5% | 5% |
| 98.0% to below 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 98.0% | 20% |

**6. Claims.** Licensee must claim a service credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the month concerned. Service credits for any month shall not exceed 20% of one-twelfth of the annual license fee.

**7. Chronic Failure.** If availability falls below 98.0% in any three (3) months in any rolling twelve (12) month period, Licensee may terminate this Agreement by written notice given within sixty (60) days after the third such month, without liability for early termination, and Licensor shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

**8. Reporting.** Licensor shall provide Licensee with a monthly service report showing availability, incidents by severity and service credits accrued.
