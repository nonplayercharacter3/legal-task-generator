# SCHEDULE 3: SERVICE LEVELS

**to the Software License Agreement between Lusen Maritime Software LLC ("Licensor") and Nipusk Bulk Shipping, Inc. ("Licensee") relating to the TideRoute voyage-optimization and fleet-performance platform**

---

## 1. Application and Interpretation

1.1 This Schedule 3 sets out the Service Levels that apply to the Software under Section 7 of the Agreement.

1.2 Capitalized terms used in this Schedule 3 and not defined in it have the meanings given in Section 1.1 of the Agreement. These include "Business Day", "Software", "Service Levels", "Vessel" and "Documentation".

1.3 In this Schedule 3:

"**Availability**" means the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the hosted components of the Software are accessible and materially functional, measured at Licensor's network boundary. Excluded Downtime is not counted.

"**Excluded Downtime**" means (a) Scheduled Maintenance; (b) outages caused by a force majeure event under Section 14 of the Agreement; and (c) outages caused by Licensee's onboard equipment or satellite connectivity.

"**Incident**" means any failure of the Software to perform materially in accordance with the Documentation.

"**Severity**" means the severity classification of an Incident under paragraph 5.

---

## 2. Availability

2.1 Licensor shall make the hosted components of the Software available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding Excluded Downtime.

2.2 Licensor shall measure Availability using automated monitoring tools. Licensor shall keep monitoring records for at least twelve (12) months and make them available to Licensee on reasonable request.

---

## 3. Scheduled Maintenance

3.1 Where practicable, Licensor shall carry out scheduled maintenance outside 06:00 to 22:00 UTC on Business Days.

3.2 Licensor shall give Licensee at least five (5) Business Days' notice of scheduled maintenance. Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed eight (8) hours per calendar month ("**Scheduled Maintenance**").

3.3 Licensor shall use reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance so that routing outputs for Vessels at sea are not interrupted.

---

## 4. Support

4.1 Licensor shall provide support by email, web portal and telephone. Severity 1 and Severity 2 Incidents are supported 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Other Incidents are supported from 08:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time on Business Days.

4.2 When Licensee reports an Incident, it shall give a description of the Incident, the affected Vessels or users and its proposed Severity. Licensor shall confirm or reasonably reclassify the Severity on first response.

---

## 5. Response and Resolution Targets

| Severity | Description | Response | Target Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Software unavailable or routing outputs unavailable for one or more Vessels at sea | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| 2 | Material function impaired with no reasonable workaround | 4 hours | 2 Business Days |
| 3 | Non-critical function impaired or workaround available | 1 Business Day | 10 Business Days |
| 4 | Questions, documentation issues, enhancement requests | 2 Business Days | Next scheduled release |

5.1 Response time runs from Licensor's receipt of the Incident report through a support channel listed in paragraph 4.1.

5.2 Licensor shall keep Licensee informed of progress on Severity 1 Incidents at intervals of no more than two (2) hours until the Incident is resolved or a workaround is in place.

---

## 6. Service Credits

6.1 If Availability in a calendar month falls below the level in paragraph 2.1, Licensee is entitled to a credit against the next invoice. The credit is the percentage shown below of one-twelfth of the annual license fee:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to below 99.5% | 5% |
| 98.0% to below 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 98.0% | 20% |

6.2 To receive a service credit, Licensee must claim it in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the month concerned.

6.3 Service credits for any month shall not exceed 20% of one-twelfth of the annual license fee.

---

## 7. Chronic Failure

7.1 Licensee may terminate the Agreement by written notice if Availability falls below 98.0% in any three (3) months in any rolling twelve (12) month period. The notice must be given within sixty (60) days after the third such month.

7.2 Licensee shall have no liability for early termination under paragraph 7.1. Licensor shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

---

## 8. Reporting and Review

8.1 Licensor shall give Licensee a monthly service report showing Availability, Incidents by Severity and service credits accrued.

8.2 At either party's request, the parties shall meet each quarter to review the monthly service reports and any recurring Incidents.
