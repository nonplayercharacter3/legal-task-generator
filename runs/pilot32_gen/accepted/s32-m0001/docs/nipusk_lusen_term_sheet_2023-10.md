# TERM SHEET

**Proposed License of the TideRoute Voyage-Optimization Platform**

**Lusen Maritime Software LLC / Nipusk Bulk Shipping, Inc.**

**Date: October 16, 2023**

---

## 1. Purpose and Status

1.1 This term sheet sets out the principal terms on which Lusen Maritime Software LLC ("**Lusen**") proposes to license its TideRoute voyage-optimization and fleet-performance platform ("**TideRoute**") to Nipusk Bulk Shipping, Inc. ("**Nipusk**") for use across Nipusk's dry-bulk fleet.

1.2 Except for Sections 11 (Confidentiality), 12 (Costs) and 13 (Governing Law and Forum), this term sheet is not legally binding. No binding obligation to license or acquire TideRoute arises until the parties sign a definitive software license agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

---

## 2. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Lusen Maritime Software LLC |
| Licensee | Nipusk Bulk Shipping, Inc. |

---

## 3. Software and Modules

3.1 Licensed software: TideRoute, in hosted form, together with documentation and all generally released updates.

3.2 Modules in scope:

(a) TideRoute Voyage Optimizer (weather routing, speed and consumption planning);
(b) TideRoute Fleet Performance (hull, propeller and engine performance analytics);
(c) TideRoute Emissions Reporter (CII, EU ETS and IMO DCS reporting); and
(d) TideRoute Fleet Dashboard (shore-side monitoring and reporting).

3.3 Covered vessels: Nipusk-owned dry-bulk vessels listed in an onboarding plan to be agreed, with the ability to add further vessels at the per-vessel rate.

---

## 4. License Scope

4.1 Non-exclusive, non-transferable license for Nipusk and its affiliates to use TideRoute for internal business purposes in operating the covered vessels.

4.2 Standard restrictions on copying, reverse engineering, sublicensing and bureau use.

---

## 5. Term and Renewal

5.1 Initial term: three years from the effective date of the Definitive Agreement. Target effective date: January 1, 2024.

5.2 Renewal: after the initial term, the agreement will renew automatically for one-year renewal terms, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current term.

5.3 License fees for any renewal term to be set out in the Definitive Agreement.

---

## 6. Fees

6.1 Annual license fee: US$18,500 per vessel per contract year, invoiced annually in advance. Vessels added mid-year to be invoiced pro rata.

6.2 One-time implementation and onboarding fee: US$95,000, covering configuration, data integration and up to ten (10) days of user training.

6.3 Additional professional services: US$1,650 per consultant day, subject to Nipusk's prior written approval of an estimate.

6.4 Payment terms: thirty (30) days from receipt of invoice. Good-faith disputed amounts may be withheld pending resolution.

---

## 7. Exclusivity

7.1 During the term, Nipusk will use TideRoute as its exclusive solution for voyage-optimization software only, for dry-bulk vessels owned by Nipusk.

7.2 Nipusk remains free to select and use other providers for all other functions and vessels.

---

## 8. Service Levels and Support

8.1 Target availability of 99.5% per calendar month for hosted components, excluding scheduled maintenance and outages caused by onboard equipment or connectivity.

8.2 24/7 support for critical incidents, with response and resolution targets, service credits and a chronic-failure termination right to be set out in a service level schedule.

---

## 9. Vessel Data

9.1 Nipusk will own all vessel data (including AIS, noon-report, fuel, route and performance data) processed through TideRoute. Lusen will receive a license to use vessel data only to provide and support the service, plus a right to use aggregated, de-identified data.

9.2 Lusen to maintain an ISO/IEC 27001-aligned security program, notify security incidents within 72 hours, host data in the United States or the European Economic Area, and return and delete vessel data on expiry or termination.

---

## 10. Liability, Insurance and Assignment

10.1 Mutual exclusion of indirect and consequential loss. Each party's liability capped at fees paid and payable in the relevant twelve-month period, with customary carve-outs including confidentiality, IP indemnity and fraud.

10.2 Lusen to provide an IP infringement indemnity and to maintain professional indemnity and cyber insurance of at least US$5,000,000 per claim.

10.3 Assignment: neither party may assign the Definitive Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

---

## 11. Confidentiality

The parties will keep the existence and contents of this term sheet and all information exchanged in the negotiations confidential, other than disclosure to their respective affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it.

---

## 12. Costs

Each party will bear its own costs in negotiating this term sheet and the Definitive Agreement.

---

## 13. Governing Law and Forum

13.1 This term sheet and the Definitive Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

13.2 Disputes will be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

---

## 14. Next Steps

Lusen to circulate a first draft of the Definitive Agreement, including commercial terms, vessel data terms and service level schedules, by November 15, 2023, with a target signing date before December 31, 2023.
