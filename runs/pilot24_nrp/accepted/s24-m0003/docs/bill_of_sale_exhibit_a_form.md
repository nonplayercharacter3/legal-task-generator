# EXHIBIT A

# FORM OF BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 20[●] (the "**Closing Date**"), by **Faiveirt Chemical Company**, a Texas corporation with its principal office in Pasadena, Texas ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Soupen Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets. The Purchased Assets are the assets used in Seller's business of blending and distributing water treatment chemicals, including coagulants, corrosion inhibitors and scale inhibitors, from the Pasadena, Texas blending facility (the "**Business**").

B. Section 3.2(a)(i) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. These include "Purchased Assets," "Excluded Assets," "Assumed Contracts," "Assumed Liabilities," "Excluded Liabilities," "Facility," "Lien," "Permitted Liens" and "Closing."

## 2. Conveyance of Purchased Assets

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Buyer, its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets. Buyer takes the Purchased Assets free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens. Buyer and its successors and assigns shall have and hold the Purchased Assets forever. The Purchased Assets include:

(a) all buildings, fixtures, storage tanks, blending and packaging equipment, laboratory equipment, loading racks and other tangible personal property located at or used in connection with the Facility, as described in Schedule 2.1(a) to the Purchase Agreement;

(b) all inventory of raw materials, feedstock, work in process, finished coagulants, corrosion inhibitors and scale inhibitors, packaging and supplies;

(c) the Assumed Contracts listed in Schedule 2.1(c) to the Purchase Agreement and in Annex 1 to this Bill of Sale, including the Supply Contract between Seller and Lakeport Municipal Water District (the "**Lakeport Supply Contract**");

(d) to the extent transferable, the permits, licenses and registrations listed in Schedule 4.8 to the Purchase Agreement;

(e) all product formulations, blending specifications, trade secrets and other intellectual property used exclusively in the Business. Any trademark or other registered intellectual property is also conveyed by the IP Assignment;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business arising after August 14, 2026; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists, supplier lists, quality control records and environmental monitoring records relating to the Business or the Facility.

**2.2 Real Property.** The owned real property comprising the Facility is conveyed by the special warranty deed delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv) of the Purchase Agreement. This Bill of Sale does not convey that real property.

## 3. Excluded Assets and Liabilities

**3.1 Excluded Assets.** This Bill of Sale does not sell, convey, assign, transfer or deliver any of the Excluded Assets. Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in the Excluded Assets.

**3.2 No Assumption of Liabilities.** Buyer does not assume any liability or obligation of Seller under this Bill of Sale. The Assumed Liabilities are assumed only under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Seller retains all Excluded Liabilities.

## 4. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller or of Buyer but on behalf of and for the benefit of Buyer. Under this power, Buyer may:

(a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) give receipts and releases for the Purchased Assets;

(c) institute and prosecute, at Buyer's expense, any proceeding that Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets; and

(d) endorse, in the name of Seller, any check or other instrument received in payment of any account receivable included in the Purchased Assets.

This appointment is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 5. Further Assurances

Consistent with Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall execute and deliver any further instruments of conveyance and transfer that Buyer reasonably requests from time to time after the Closing. Seller shall also take any other action that Buyer reasonably requests. These obligations exist to vest more effectively in Buyer, and to perfect, Buyer's title to and possession of the Purchased Assets. If Seller receives any payment, mail or other property after the Closing that relates to a Purchased Asset, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer and promptly remit it to Buyer.

## 6. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Purchase Agreement and is subject to its terms. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies the representations, warranties, covenants, agreements or indemnities of the parties in the Purchase Agreement. Those provisions are not merged into this Bill of Sale. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Bill of Sale and the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase Agreement prevails.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale is subject to Section 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.3 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by PDF or electronic signature. A signature delivered in that way is effective as an original.
