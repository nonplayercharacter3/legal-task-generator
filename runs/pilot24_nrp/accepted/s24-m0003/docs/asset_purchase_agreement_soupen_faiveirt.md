# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 14, 2026, by and between **Soupen Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Buyer**"), and **Faiveirt Chemical Company**, a Texas corporation with its principal office in Pasadena, Texas ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller blends and distributes water treatment chemicals, including coagulants, corrosion inhibitors and scale inhibitors, to municipal and industrial customers from its blending facility in Pasadena, Texas (the "**Business**").

B. Buyer and Seller signed a non-binding letter of intent dated May 12, 2026 (the "**Letter of Intent**").

C. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, substantially all of the assets used in the Business, and Buyer will assume certain specified liabilities of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

"**Assumed Contracts**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1(c).

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets used in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) the Pasadena, Texas blending facility, including the owned real property, buildings, fixtures, storage tanks, blending equipment and other tangible personal property described in Schedule 2.1(a);

(b) all inventory of raw materials, feedstock, work in process, finished coagulants, corrosion inhibitors and scale inhibitors, packaging and supplies;

(c) the contracts listed in Schedule 2.1(c) (the "**Assumed Contracts**");

(d) to the extent transferable, all permits, licenses and registrations held for the operation of the Business and the Facility, as listed in Schedule 4.8;

(e) all product formulations, blending specifications, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used exclusively in the Business;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business arising after the date of this Agreement; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists, supplier lists, quality control records and environmental monitoring records relating to the Business or the Facility.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include cash and cash equivalents, Seller's corporate records, Seller's insurance policies, Tax refunds for pre-Closing periods, any contract that is not an Assumed Contract, and the other assets listed in Schedule 2.2 (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay and perform only (a) the liabilities of Seller under the Assumed Contracts to the extent arising and relating to performance after the Closing, and (b) trade accounts payable of the Business incurred in the ordinary course after the date of this Agreement and reflected in the closing statement (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and remain responsible for, all liabilities of Seller and the Business other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including: (a) all Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities; (b) all liabilities for Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date; (c) all liabilities arising out of any breach or default by Seller under any Assumed Contract before the Closing; (d) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets; (e) all liabilities to current or former employees of Seller arising before the Closing; and (f) all product liability and warranty claims relating to products shipped before the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price by wire transfer of immediately available funds as follows: (a) $2,425,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") to the escrow agent named in the escrow agreement delivered under Section 3.2 (the "**Escrow Agreement**"); and (b) the balance to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) business days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and an allocation schedule prepared by Buyer within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date and reasonably agreed by Seller. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third business day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on another date the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Closing Deliveries.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (i) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller; (ii) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assumed Contracts (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (iii) an intellectual property assignment (the "**IP Assignment**"); (iv) a special warranty deed for the Facility real property; (v) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (vi) the third-party consents listed in Schedule 4.5; (vii) payoff letters and Lien releases for all Liens on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Liens; (viii) an IRS Form W-9; and (ix) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (i) the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.5; (ii) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (iii) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Facility in good operating condition, preserve its relationships with customers and suppliers, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, (a) sell or encumber any Purchased Asset other than inventory in the ordinary course, (b) amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assumed Contract, or (c) increase the compensation of any employee of the Business other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Facility, the books and records of the Business and the employees of the Business, including for the purpose of conducting a Phase I environmental site assessment.

**6.3 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business and the Purchased Assets, and all information received from Buyer under this Agreement, except as required by Law.

**6.4 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not required to, offer employment effective as of the Closing to employees of the Business. Seller shall pay all wages, bonuses, accrued vacation and other amounts owed to employees of the Business through the Closing Date.

**6.5 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed in Schedule 4.5, in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

**6.6 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the blending or distribution of coagulants, corrosion inhibitors or scale inhibitors for water treatment in the United States, or solicit any customer of the Business for those products.

**6.7 Transitional Supply.** For eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, Seller shall supply Buyer at the Facility with ferric chloride feedstock in the volumes Buyer orders, up to 115% of the Business's average monthly ferric chloride consumption during the twelve months before the Closing, at the prices and on the terms set out in Schedule 6.7. To permit Seller to plan production, Buyer shall provide Seller each month with a rolling three-month forecast of its feedstock requirements, which may be derived from Buyer's customer volume forecasts. Seller shall use those forecasts only to perform this Section 6.7.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**6.9 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws. Any liability arising from non-compliance is an Excluded Liability.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until twelve (12) months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after expiry of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants to be performed after the Closing survive until fully performed. All claims for indemnification under this Article VIII, including claims under Section 8.2(b), must be asserted by written notice delivered before the expiry of twelve (12) months after the Closing Date, other than claims for breach of a Fundamental Representation, which may be asserted at any time within the survival period of that Fundamental Representation. A claim asserted by timely written notice survives until finally resolved.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document;

(b) any Excluded Liability, including any Pre-Closing Environmental Liability; or

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, (b) any Assumed Liability, or (c) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**8.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller is not liable under Section 8.2(a) (other than for breach of a Fundamental Representation or for fraud) until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 8.2(a) exceed $485,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller is liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(b) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) (other than for breach of a Fundamental Representation or for fraud) shall not exceed $7,275,000. The aggregate liability of Seller under this Article VIII shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The Basket and the cap in Section 8.4(b) do not apply to claims under Section 8.2(b) or Section 8.2(c).

(d) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually recovered by the Indemnified Party in respect of the matter, less the costs of recovery.

**8.5 Claims Procedure.** A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. A delay in giving notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within thirty (30) days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Buyer shall control the investigation and remediation of any environmental condition at the Facility that is the subject of a claim under Section 8.2(b).

**8.6 Escrow.** Buyer may recover amounts owed to the Buyer Indemnitees under this Article VIII from the Indemnity Escrow Amount in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, without limiting its right to recover directly from Seller. Any portion of the Indemnity Escrow Amount not subject to a pending claim shall be released to Seller on the date that is twelve (12) months after the Closing Date.

**8.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud, specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article VIII is the Parties' sole and exclusive monetary remedy for breach of this Agreement after the Closing.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, unless the failure results from that Party's breach; (c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination under Section 9.1, this Agreement becomes void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.3, this Section 9.2 and Article XI survive, and no termination relieves a Party from liability for willful breach before termination.

**10.2 Proration.** Real and personal property Taxes on the Purchased Assets for the tax period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Buyer and Seller on a daily basis as of the Closing Date.

**10.3 Cooperation.** The Parties shall cooperate in the preparation of Tax returns and the conduct of any Tax audit relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.
