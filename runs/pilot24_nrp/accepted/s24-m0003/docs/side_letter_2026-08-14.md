Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into on August 14, 2026 by **Faiveirt Chemical Company**, a Texas corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Soupen Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are signing it together with the Asset Purchase Agreement between them dated as of the same date (the "**Agreement**"). This Side Letter is the "Side Letter" referred to in the Agreement. It records certain further agreements of the Parties about the transactions contemplated by the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions and Background

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. These terms include "Business," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Facility," "Governmental Authority," "Buyer Indemnitees," "Indemnity Escrow Amount" and "Escrow Agreement."

**1.2 Notice of Violation.** Seller has received from the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (the "**TCEQ**") a notice of violation relating to the Facility. The notice alleges exceedances of permitted discharge limits for total suspended solids and dissolved iron in stormwater discharged from the Facility's tank farm containment area during the first quarter of 2026. It also alleges related recordkeeping deficiencies. That notice, together with any enforcement action, agreed order, penalty assessment or proceeding of the TCEQ or any other Governmental Authority arising out of the same alleged violations, is the "**Pasadena NOV**." The Pasadena NOV is disclosed in Schedule 4.12 to the Agreement and remains unresolved on the date of this Side Letter.

## 2. Special Indemnity for the Pasadena NOV

**2.1 Indemnity.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Buyer Indemnitees from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to the Pasadena NOV. This obligation applies whether those Losses are assessed or paid before or after the Closing Date.

**2.2 Losses.** For all purposes of the Agreement and this Side Letter, "**Losses**" means only out-of-pocket fines and penalties paid to a Governmental Authority.

**2.3 Relationship to Article VIII.** Claims under this Section 2 are made in accordance with the claims procedure in Section 8.5 of the Agreement. They are not subject to the Basket or to the cap in Section 8.4(b) of the Agreement. Seller's obligations under this Section 2 survive the Closing until the Pasadena NOV is finally resolved and all amounts payable under this Section 2 have been paid. Buyer may recover amounts owed under this Section 2 from the Indemnity Escrow Amount in accordance with Section 8.6 of the Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

**2.4 Conduct of the Pasadena NOV.** Before the Closing, Seller shall continue to respond to the Pasadena NOV at its own cost. Seller shall keep Buyer reasonably informed of all material developments and promptly give Buyer copies of all material correspondence with the TCEQ. Seller shall not enter into any agreed order or settlement of the Pasadena NOV that imposes any operating restriction, corrective action or other non-monetary obligation on the Facility or the Business without Buyer's prior written consent, which Buyer shall not unreasonably withhold. After the Closing, Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in responding to the Pasadena NOV, and Section 8.5 of the Agreement applies to its defense and settlement.

## 3. Corrective Measures Before Closing

Before the Closing, Seller shall complete, at its own cost, the following measures at the Facility:

(a) repair of the secondary containment liner in the tank farm containment area identified in the Pasadena NOV; and

(b) the update of the Facility's stormwater pollution prevention plan.

Seller shall deliver to Buyer reasonable written evidence that these measures are complete, together with the officer's certificate required by Section 7.2(c) of the Agreement.

## 4. Confidentiality

The terms of this Side Letter are confidential information of the Parties subject to Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Either Party may disclose this Side Letter to the escrow agent under the Escrow Agreement, to its lenders and professional advisors, and to any Governmental Authority as required by Law.

## 5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Side Letter, and any dispute arising out of or relating to the Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts located in Harris County, Texas for any such dispute, and agrees not to bring any such action in any other court. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.
