# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit B to Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 14, 2026*

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**TSA**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Tripux Elevator Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Seles Lift Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as Seller, and Recipient, as Buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is purchasing substantially all of the assets of Provider's elevator and escalator maintenance, service and repair business (the "**Business**").

B. Before the Closing, Provider performed dispatch and after-hours emergency call answering for the Business using its own telephone systems, dispatch software and personnel. These functions are not being transferred to Recipient at the Closing.

C. Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this TSA at the Closing. Under this TSA, Provider will provide those functions to Recipient for a transitional period while Recipient establishes its own capability.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this TSA and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this TSA have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings:

"**After-Hours Period**" means each period from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day, and all hours on any day that is not a Business Day.

"**Business Day**" means any day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are open for the general transaction of business.

"**Customer**" means any customer of the Business under an Assigned Contract, and any other customer of Recipient for whom Recipient's personnel service Covered Units.

"**Dispatch Platform**" means Provider's computer-aided dispatch and work order software, together with the related telephone lines, call routing and recording systems, used by Provider to operate the Business before the Closing.

"**Entrapment Call**" means any call reporting that one or more persons are trapped in an elevator car or are otherwise unable to exit an elevator or escalator.

"**Exit Plan**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1.

"**Recipient Coordinator**" and "**Provider Coordinator**" have the meanings set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending on the termination date for that Service under Article 6.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this TSA unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide to Recipient the following services (each, a "**Service**" and collectively, the "**Services**"), as further described in Schedule A:

(a) *Daytime Dispatch.* Receiving service requests from Customers by telephone, email and the Customer web portal during hours other than the After-Hours Period. This Service also includes creating work orders in the Dispatch Platform and assigning them to Recipient's field technicians and route supervisors, in accordance with the dispatch protocols that Recipient provides under Section 2.4.

(b) *After-Hours Emergency Call Answering.* Answering all calls to the Business's emergency service lines during the After-Hours Period, including calls placed from elevator car emergency telephones that are monitored on those lines. This Service also includes recording each call and notifying Recipient's designated on-call technician in accordance with Section 2.3.

(c) *Records and Data.* Maintaining all call recordings, call logs and work order records generated in performing the Services. Provider shall deliver to Recipient a daily export of those records in the format described in Schedule A, and shall give Recipient read-only access to the Dispatch Platform for Business work orders.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

- in a manner substantially similar in scope, quality, priority and timeliness to that in which Provider performed the same functions for the Business during the 12 months before the Closing;
- with the same degree of care that Provider uses for its own operations; and
- in compliance with applicable law, including the Elevator Codes to the extent they govern emergency communications and response.

**2.3 Response Times.** Provider shall meet the following response standards, measured monthly:

(a) Provider shall answer at least 95% of calls during the After-Hours Period within 30 seconds, and shall answer every call within 60 seconds.

(b) For each Entrapment Call, Provider shall contact Recipient's designated on-call technician within 5 minutes after the call is received. If the on-call technician does not acknowledge the call within 10 minutes, Provider shall escalate it to the next contact on Recipient's escalation list. Where a person is reported injured or in distress, Provider shall also contact local emergency services immediately.

(c) For all other emergency calls, Provider shall notify Recipient's designated on-call technician within 15 minutes after the call is received.

(d) Provider shall create a work order in the Dispatch Platform for each daytime service request within 30 minutes after receiving it.

**2.4 Recipient Protocols.** No later than the Closing Date, Recipient shall deliver to Provider the following:

- its dispatch protocols;
- its on-call technician schedules; and
- its escalation lists.

Recipient shall update each of them in writing at least two Business Days before any change takes effect. In the case of an on-call schedule change caused by illness or emergency, Recipient shall update it as soon as practicable. Provider is entitled to rely on the most recent protocols, schedules and lists delivered by Recipient.

**2.5 Service Reports.** Within ten Business Days after the end of each calendar month, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a report showing, for that month:

- the number of calls received;
- performance against each response standard in Section 2.3; and
- each Entrapment Call and its handling.

**2.6 Additional Services.** If Recipient reasonably identifies a function that Provider performed for the Business before the Closing that is not described in Section 2.1, and that is needed for the transitional operation of the Business, Recipient may request that Provider provide that function. Provider shall consider the request in good faith. Any additional service that the parties agree on in writing shall become a Service, at a fee agreed in writing based on Provider's actual cost.

**2.7 No Obligation to Expand.** Provider is not required to provide any Service at a volume materially greater than the volume of the same function performed for the Business during the 12 months before the Closing, adjusted for any increase in the number of Covered Units under the Assigned Contracts, or to purchase any new hardware or software to provide the Services.

## 3. PERSONNEL AND SYSTEMS

**3.1 Provider Personnel.** Provider shall assign sufficient qualified personnel to perform the Services in accordance with this TSA. Provider shall be solely responsible for the compensation, benefits, supervision and employment taxes of its personnel. Provider personnel are not employees or agents of Recipient.

**3.2 Dispatch Personnel.** Recipient acknowledges that Provider's dispatchers and call-center staff who perform the Services may remain employees of Provider during the Service Period. Nothing in this TSA restricts Recipient from offering employment to any of those individuals effective at or after the end of the Service Period for the Service they perform. Recipient shall notify Provider in writing at least 15 days before any such offer takes effect during a Service Period.

**3.3 Access to Recipient Systems.** Recipient shall give Provider personnel access to Recipient's technician contact systems and work order tools to the extent reasonably required to perform the Services. Provider shall comply with Recipient's reasonable written security and access policies.

**3.4 Ownership of Data.** All Customer information, call recordings, call logs and work order records relating to the Business and generated in performing the Services are Recipient's property. They form part of the books and records of the Business acquired under Section 2.1(f) of the Purchase Agreement. Provider shall use that data only to perform the Services.

**3.5 Telephone Numbers.** The Business's main service and emergency telephone numbers are Purchased Assets. During the Service Period, Provider shall continue to route those numbers to the Dispatch Platform. At the end of the Service Period for the After-Hours Emergency Call Answering Service, Provider shall cooperate with Recipient and the telephone carrier to port those numbers to Recipient's systems.

## 4. GOVERNANCE

**4.1 Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint an individual to manage the Services and serve as its primary contact under this TSA. Provider's individual is the "**Provider Coordinator**," and Recipient's individual is the "**Recipient Coordinator**." Each party may replace its coordinator by written notice to the other party.

**4.2 Monthly Meetings.** The Provider Coordinator and the Recipient Coordinator shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least once each month during the Service Period to review the service reports, the progress of the Exit Plan and any performance concerns.

**4.3 Performance Failures.** If Provider fails to meet any response standard in Section 2.3 in any calendar month, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a written corrective plan within five Business Days after Recipient's request. Provider shall implement that plan promptly.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the following monthly fees for the Services:

| Service | Monthly Fee |
|---|---|
| Daytime Dispatch | $24,500 |
| After-Hours Emergency Call Answering | $18,000 |
| Records and Data | Included |

Fees for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider, at cost and without markup, for:

- telephone carrier charges for the Business's service and emergency lines; and
- any other third-party charges that Recipient approves in writing in advance and that are incurred solely to provide the Services.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail for pass-through costs. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**5.4 Disputed Invoices.** If Recipient disputes any invoice amount in good faith, Recipient shall give Provider written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, within 20 days after receiving the invoice. Recipient shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 9.2.

**5.5 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay any sales, use or similar taxes imposed on the Services, other than taxes based on Provider's income.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

**6.1 Term.** This TSA begins on the Closing Date. Each Service shall be provided until the date that is six months after the Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under this Article 6 or extended under Section 6.2.

**6.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Period for either or both of the Daytime Dispatch Service and the After-Hours Emergency Call Answering Service. Recipient may do so once, for up to three additional months, by giving Provider written notice at least 30 days before the scheduled end of that Service Period. Fees during any extension shall be the fees set forth in Section 5.1.

**6.3 Early Termination by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole, for convenience on at least 30 days' written notice to Provider. The Records and Data Service shall continue until the end of the last remaining Service Period, and for 30 days after that for the final data transfer under Section 7.2.

**6.4 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this TSA by written notice if the other party materially breaches this TSA and does not cure that breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. However, Provider may not terminate or suspend the After-Hours Emergency Call Answering Service for Recipient's non-payment unless:

- the amount is undisputed and more than 45 days past due; and
- Provider has given Recipient at least 15 days' further written notice of its intent to terminate.

**6.5 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration of any Service, Recipient shall pay all fees and pass-through costs accrued for that Service through the termination date. Sections 3.4, 5.3, 5.4, 7.2, 8, 9 and 10 survive termination or expiration of this TSA.

## 7. TRANSITION AND EXIT

**7.1 Exit Plan.** Within 30 days after the Closing Date, the Provider Coordinator and the Recipient Coordinator shall jointly prepare a written plan (the "**Exit Plan**") for the migration of the Services to Recipient or its designee. The Exit Plan shall include:

- a schedule for porting the Business's telephone numbers;
- the transfer of historical dispatch and call records; and
- a period of parallel operation before each Service ends.

Provider shall perform its obligations under the Exit Plan as part of the Services.

**7.2 Data Transfer.** Within 15 Business Days after the end of the last Service Period, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a complete export of all Business data held in the Dispatch Platform, in the format described in Schedule A. After Recipient confirms in writing that it has received a usable copy, Provider shall delete that data from its systems, except to the extent applicable law requires Provider to retain it.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other party that it receives in connection with this TSA. Each party shall use that information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this TSA. This Section 8.1 does not restrict disclosure required by law, or disclosure to a party's employees and advisers who need to know the information and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

**8.2 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Article 8 is in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. LIABILITY; DISPUTES

**9.1 Limitation of Liability.** Neither party shall be liable to the other under this TSA for indirect, special, punitive or consequential damages, or for lost profits, except to the extent arising from its gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Article 8. Provider's aggregate liability under this TSA shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under Section 5.1. This cap does not apply to Losses arising from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or from third-party claims for bodily injury or death caused by Provider's failure to perform the After-Hours Emergency Call Answering Service in accordance with Section 2.3.

**9.2 Dispute Resolution.** The Provider Coordinator and the Recipient Coordinator shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute under this TSA. If they do not resolve it within ten Business Days, either party may refer the dispute to Provider's President and Recipient's Chief Executive Officer. If those officers do not resolve it within a further ten Business Days, either party may pursue its remedies under Section 10.6. Provider shall continue to perform the Services while any dispute is pending, subject to Section 6.4.

**9.3 Insurance.** During the Service Period, Provider shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, and errors and omissions insurance covering the Services with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per claim. On request, Provider shall deliver certificates of that insurance to Recipient.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this TSA creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.

**10.2 Notices.** All notices under this TSA shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices shall be delivered to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, with a copy to the other party's coordinator, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**10.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this TSA without the prior written consent of the other party. However, Recipient may assign this TSA to an Affiliate to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, widespread telecommunications outages and acts of government. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During any such event, Provider shall implement its existing business continuity procedures for the emergency service lines, including call forwarding to backup lines.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This TSA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**10.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** This TSA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this TSA. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits instead to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this TSA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This TSA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

**Schedule A: Service Descriptions and Data Formats**

1. *Daytime Dispatch.* Intake channels are the main service line, the service email address and the Customer web portal. Work orders shall record the Customer, the Covered Unit identifier, the reported condition, the priority level and the assigned technician.

2. *After-Hours Emergency Call Answering.* The coverage lines are the emergency service line and the monitored elevator car emergency telephone lines. Each call record shall include the time received, the caller, the location and Covered Unit, whether the call is an Entrapment Call, each notification made and the time of technician acknowledgment.

3. *Data Formats.* Daily exports and the final export shall be delivered in comma-separated values (CSV) format. Call recordings shall be delivered in WAV or MP3 format, through a secure file transfer site designated by Recipient.
