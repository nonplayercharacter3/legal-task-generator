# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 14, 2026, by and between **Tripux Elevator Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Seles Lift Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a business of inspecting, maintaining, servicing, repairing and modernizing passenger and freight elevators, escalators and related vertical transportation equipment for commercial, residential and institutional customers (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

"**Business Day**" means any day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are open for the general transaction of business.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, as it may be supplemented in accordance with Section 6.5.

"**Elevator Codes**" means ASME A17.1/CSA B44 (Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators), ASME A17.3 (Safety Code for Existing Elevators and Escalators), and all state and municipal statutes, codes, ordinances and regulations governing the installation, inspection, testing, maintenance and operation of elevators, escalators and related equipment in each jurisdiction in which the Business operates.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Kestrow Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent, substantially in the agreed form attached as Exhibit A.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority) and Section 4.8 (Title to Purchased Assets).

"**General Representations**" means all representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV other than the Fundamental Representations.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, municipal or local government, or any agency, department, board, inspector or other authority thereof, including any state or municipal elevator inspection authority.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $1,875,000.

"**Key Employee Agreement**" means the Key Employee Agreement between Seller and Renata Oyelaran, Seller's Director of Field Operations.

"**Knowledge of Seller**" means the actual knowledge, after reasonable inquiry, of Seller's President, Chief Financial Officer and Director of Field Operations.

"**Liens**" means any mortgage, pledge, lien, charge, security interest, claim or other encumbrance.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, individually or in the aggregate, materially adverse to the Business, the Purchased Assets or the results of operations or condition (financial or otherwise) of the Business.

"**Permits**" means all licenses, permits, certificates, registrations and approvals issued by any Governmental Authority, including elevator contractor licenses, mechanic licenses and certificates of operation.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the transition services agreement between Seller and Buyer, substantially in the agreed form attached as Exhibit B, under which Seller will provide dispatch and after-hours emergency call answering services to Buyer for a transitional period after the Closing.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens listed in Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(b) all inventory, spare parts, controllers, components and supplies;

(c) all contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including all customer maintenance and service contracts, the vehicle leases for the Business's service fleet, and the Key Employee Agreement (collectively, the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all tools, testing equipment, load-testing weights, service vehicles, furniture and other tangible personal property;

(e) all Permits held by Seller for the operation of the Business, to the extent transferable under applicable law, including the municipal elevator contractor license;

(f) all customer lists, maintenance logs, inspection and test records, service histories, and other books and records of the Business; and

(g) all goodwill of the Business, including the right to use the name "Tripux Elevator" and all related trade names.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include: (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's corporate seal, minute books and stock records; (c) insurance policies of Seller; (d) all contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (e) all tax refunds relating to periods before the Closing; and (f) the assets listed in Section 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the following liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) trade accounts payable of the Business incurred in the ordinary course and reflected on the Closing statement; and (b) liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing, other than liabilities arising from any breach or default by Seller before the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume any liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including any liabilities for taxes of Seller, indebtedness, pre-Closing employment matters, and any fines, penalties or remediation costs relating to the condition or inspection status of any elevator or escalator serviced by the Business before the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,750,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

**2.6 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement as a source of recovery for claims under Article IX; and

(b) the balance of the Purchase Price to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.7 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and an allocation schedule prepared by Buyer and delivered to Seller within 90 days after the Closing Date. Seller and Buyer shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.8 Non-Assignable Assets.** If any Assigned Contract or Permit cannot be assigned without a consent that has not been obtained at Closing, this Agreement does not constitute an assignment of that item. Seller shall, until such consent is obtained, hold that item for the benefit of Buyer and cooperate with Buyer in any lawful arrangement designed to provide Buyer its benefits.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale and an assignment and assumption agreement, each duly executed by Seller; (b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (d) the consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule; (e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d); and (f) such other customary instruments of transfer as Buyer reasonably requests.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the payments described in Section 2.6; (b) the assignment and assumption agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action of Seller, including its board of directors and stockholders. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's certificate of incorporation or bylaws, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Business, or (c) except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, require the consent of any Person under, or result in a breach of or default under, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Consents.** No consent, approval, Permit or authorization of, or filing with, any Governmental Authority or other Person is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller, except for those listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule. Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the following required consent: the consent of the municipal licensing authority to the transfer of Seller's elevator contractor license to Buyer.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the balance sheets and statements of income of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2023 and December 31, 2024 and for the eleven months ended November 30, 2025. Those financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business for the periods indicated.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since November 30, 2025, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Business involving annual payments in excess of $50,000, each customer maintenance contract covering more than ten units, and each employment agreement with a key employee. Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any other party is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.8 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Liens other than the Permitted Liens listed in Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.9 Condition of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action, suit, proceeding or investigation pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets, other than as listed in Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws. Section 4.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all Permits held by Seller for the Business, each of which is valid and in full force and effect.

**4.12 Elevator Code Compliance and Inspections.** Each elevator, escalator and related unit that Seller maintains under an Assigned Contract (each, a "**Covered Unit**") has been maintained and serviced by Seller in compliance in all material respects with the Elevator Codes. Each Covered Unit holds a current certificate of operation or inspection certificate issued by the applicable Governmental Authority. All periodic safety tests required by the Elevator Codes for each Covered Unit, including Category 1 and Category 5 tests and full-load and governor tests, have been timely completed and properly recorded. There are no open inspection deficiency notices, violation notices or orders of any Governmental Authority with respect to any Covered Unit.

**4.13 Employees.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Business, with title, date of hire and current compensation. Seller is not a party to any collective bargaining agreement covering employees of the Business except as listed in Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.14 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and has paid all taxes shown as due on those returns. There are no Liens for taxes on any Purchased Asset other than Liens for taxes not yet due and payable.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement, and this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Business's customer relationships and workforce, and shall continue to perform all scheduled maintenance, inspections and periodic safety tests on the Covered Units. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, enter into, amend or terminate any Assigned Contract, other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, maintenance logs and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, the consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller, including by providing information required by the municipal licensing authority.

**6.4 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not required to, offer employment effective as of the Closing to employees of the Business. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts Buyer's offer, effective as of the Closing, and shall pay all wages, accrued vacation and other amounts owed to those employees through the Closing Date.

**6.5 Disclosure Schedule Supplements.** From time to time before the Closing, Seller may supplement or amend the Disclosure Schedule with respect to any matter arising or first becoming known to Seller after the date of this Agreement (each, a "**Schedule Supplement**"). Any matter disclosed in a Schedule Supplement shall be deemed to have cured any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty arising from that matter, for purposes of Section 7.2 and of the indemnification provisions of Article IX, unless Buyer delivers to Seller a written objection to that Schedule Supplement within 5 Business Days after Buyer's receipt of the supplement.

**6.6 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and Buyer shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business, except as required by law.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such additional instruments and take such further actions as the other party reasonably requests to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date (except that the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects);

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Seller shall have obtained each of the consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, each in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(d) Buyer shall have received a certificate signed by an officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(e) there shall not have occurred any Material Adverse Effect; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each of the items in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate signed by an officer of Buyer certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b) have been satisfied, and Buyer shall have made the deliveries in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred on or before May 15, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close by that date;

(c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and that breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and that breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect, without liability of any party, except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a party of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations shall survive the Closing until the date that is 90 days after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until the date that is six years after the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) for any inaccuracy in or breach of a representation or warranty may be made unless the Indemnified Party delivers written notice of that claim, describing it in reasonable detail, to the Indemnifying Party before the expiration of the applicable survival period. Any claim not noticed within that period is barred. A claim timely noticed shall survive until it is finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** Subject to this Article IX, from and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Subject to this Article IX, from and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations until the aggregate Losses exceed $93,750, after which Seller shall be liable for Losses from the first dollar.

(b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(c) Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) The limitations in Sections 9.4(a) and 9.4(b) shall not apply to Losses arising from fraud.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.** The party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice to the party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. For any claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to Buyer under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the funds then held by the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement and, to the extent those funds are insufficient, directly by Seller, subject to Section 9.4. Seller and Buyer shall promptly deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent as required by the Escrow Agreement to give effect to any amount finally determined to be owed under this Article IX.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, other than claims for fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Seller and Buyer.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement, except that the fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Seller and Buyer.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.8 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement or the Transition Services Agreement, this Agreement prevails over the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement.

**11.10 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns, and, except as provided in Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any right on any other Person.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
