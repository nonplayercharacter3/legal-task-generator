# ESCROW AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit A to Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 14, 2026*

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Seles Lift Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Tripux Elevator Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Kestrow Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase substantially all of the assets of Seller's elevator and escalator maintenance, service and repair business.

B. Section 2.6(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, as a source of recovery for claims under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Buyer and Seller have also agreed that a portion of the Purchase Price otherwise payable to Seller will be deposited with the Escrow Agent at the Closing and held as a special escrow in connection with Schedule Supplement No. 1, as described in Article III.

D. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings:

"**Business Day**" means any day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are open for the general transaction of business.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, collectively, the Indemnity Escrow Fund and the Special Escrow Fund.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 9.7, or an order or judgment for which the time to appeal has expired without an appeal being filed.

"**Indemnity Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest and earnings on it, less all disbursements made from it under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Indemnity Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Special Escrow Fund**" means the Special Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.2, together with all interest and earnings on it, less all disbursements made from it under this Escrow Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Articles are to those of this Escrow Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**2.1 Indemnity Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $1,875,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the Escrow Agent in Schedule 1. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Fund in a separate account and disburse it only in accordance with Article IV and Article V.

**2.2 Special Escrow Amount.** Under Section 2.6(b) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller designates the Escrow Agent's account in Schedule 1 to receive $640,000 of the balance of the Purchase Price otherwise payable to Seller at the Closing (the "**Special Escrow Amount**"). At the Closing, Buyer shall wire the Special Escrow Amount to that account on Seller's behalf. Buyer's payment of the Special Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent satisfies Buyer's obligation to pay that portion of the Purchase Price to Seller. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Special Escrow Fund in a separate account from the Indemnity Escrow Fund and disburse it only in accordance with Article III and Article V.

**2.3 Appointment and Acceptance.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of each deposit to Buyer and Seller in writing on the Business Day it is received.

**2.4 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Kestrow Government Obligations Money Market Fund, or in any other investment directed by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent shall have no liability for any loss on an investment made in accordance with this Section 2.4. Absent direction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account.

## 3. SPECIAL ESCROW

**3.1 Purpose.** The Special Escrow Fund is held as security for the matters disclosed in Schedule Supplement No. 1 described in Section 3.3. The Special Escrow Fund is separate from, and in addition to, the Indemnity Escrow Fund. Disbursements from the Special Escrow Fund do not reduce the Indemnity Escrow Fund.

**3.2 No Admission.** No party's agreement to the deposit, holding or release of the Special Escrow Fund is an admission by that party of any position stated in Section 3.3.

**3.3 Schedule Supplement No. 1.** On February 9, 2026, Seller delivered to Buyer a Schedule Supplement under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement, designated "Schedule Supplement No. 1." Schedule Supplement No. 1 disclosed open state inspection deficiency notices on 14 elevator units that are Covered Units under Section 4.12 of the Purchase Agreement. On February 23, 2026, Buyer delivered to Seller a written objection to Schedule Supplement No. 1.

(a) *Seller's Position.* Seller's position is that Buyer's objection was not delivered within the period required by Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement. Seller's position is therefore that the matters disclosed in Schedule Supplement No. 1 are deemed to have cured any inaccuracy in or breach of Section 4.12 of the Purchase Agreement for purposes of Section 7.2 and Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

(b) *Buyer's Position.* Buyer disputes Seller's position. Buyer's position is that the matters disclosed in Schedule Supplement No. 1 are not cured, and that Losses arising from the open state inspection deficiency notices on those 14 elevator units are indemnifiable by Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

(c) *Special Escrow Amount.* The Special Escrow Amount of $640,000 shall be held by the Escrow Agent pending resolution of the dispute described in this Section 3.3.

**3.4 Release of Special Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse the Special Escrow Fund as follows:

(a) *To Buyer.* The Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer the amount specified in Joint Written Instructions or in a Final Order. That amount shall be paid to Buyer within three Business Days after the Escrow Agent receives the Joint Written Instructions, or receives a copy of the Final Order together with Buyer's certificate that the Final Order has become final and non-appealable, as applicable. The Final Order must determine that Buyer is entitled to that amount with respect to the matters disclosed in Schedule Supplement No. 1.

(b) *To Seller.* On the Indemnity Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the entire balance of the Special Escrow Fund, unless before that date Buyer has obtained a Final Order in its favor, or Buyer and Seller have delivered Joint Written Instructions, in either case with respect to the matters disclosed in Schedule Supplement No. 1. If Buyer obtains such a Final Order or Joint Written Instructions are delivered, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Special Escrow Fund in accordance with them. Any balance of the Special Escrow Fund remaining after that disbursement shall be released to Seller.

(c) *Pending Proceeding.* If, on the Indemnity Escrow Release Date, Buyer has commenced an action under Section 9.7 relating to Schedule Supplement No. 1 that has not resulted in a Final Order, Buyer may deliver written notice of that action to the Escrow Agent and Seller before that date. If it does so, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Special Escrow Fund until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

## 4. INDEMNITY ESCROW

**4.1 Indemnity Escrow Release Date.** The Indemnity Escrow Fund shall be held until the date that is 15 months after the Closing Date (the "**Indemnity Escrow Release Date**"). Buyer may deliver Claim Notices against the Indemnity Escrow Fund at any time before the Indemnity Escrow Release Date. Within three Business Days after the Indemnity Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund. The Escrow Agent shall first subtract, and retain, the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before the Indemnity Escrow Release Date that remain unresolved on that date (each, a "**Pending Claim**").

**4.2 Claim Notices.** To make a claim against the Indemnity Escrow Fund, Buyer shall deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice must state:

- the amount claimed, or a good faith estimate of that amount;
- the section or sections of the Purchase Agreement under which the claim is made; and
- the facts giving rise to the claim, described in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If Seller does not deliver a written objection to the Escrow Agent and Buyer (a "**Counter-Notice**") within 20 Business Days after the Escrow Agent's receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount claimed to Buyer from the Indemnity Escrow Fund on the next Business Day.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** If Seller timely delivers a Counter-Notice disputing all or part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any undisputed portion to Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall retain the disputed portion until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, and shall disburse that portion in accordance with them.

**4.5 Release of Retained Amounts.** On resolution of any Pending Claim by Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for that claim as directed. Any amount retained for a Pending Claim that is not awarded to Buyer shall be released to Seller.

## 5. GENERAL DISBURSEMENT PROVISIONS

**5.1 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Escrow Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any portion of the Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions to give effect to any amount finally determined to be owed under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement or under Article III of this Escrow Agreement.

**5.2 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account of the recipient set forth in Schedule 1, or to another account designated by the recipient in a written notice. The Escrow Agent may confirm any such notice by a telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed in Schedule 2.

**5.3 Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent may rely on a Final Order delivered by either Buyer or Seller, together with that party's certificate that the order is final and non-appealable. The delivering party shall give a copy of the Final Order and certificate to the other party at the same time.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are limited to those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement and are ministerial in nature. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by or charged with notice of, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for indirect, special, punitive or consequential damages.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent from and against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance of this Escrow Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to losses caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 6.4. A party that is finally determined to have caused the loss shall instead bear the full amount.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may file an action in interpleader in a court described in Section 9.7 and deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with that court.

**6.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions given at least 30 days in advance. Resignation or removal takes effect on the appointment of a successor escrow agent by Buyer and Seller and the transfer of the Escrow Funds to that successor. If no successor is appointed within 30 days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one.

**6.7 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set forth in Schedule 3. Those fees, and the Escrow Agent's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**6.8 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the documentation it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering and customer identification requirements.

## 7. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Reporting.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Seller is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds. Seller shall report all interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds as its income, whether or not those earnings are distributed in that year. Any disbursement to Buyer shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**7.2 Tax Forms.** Before the Closing, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file all information returns required with respect to the Escrow Funds.

## 8. TERMINATION

This Escrow Agreement shall terminate when the Escrow Agent has disbursed all of the Escrow Funds in accordance with its terms. Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 6.7 shall survive termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt. Notices shall be delivered to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**9.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**9.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any successor to the Escrow Agent by merger or consolidation, or by transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall become the Escrow Agent under this Escrow Agreement without further action.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.5 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

**9.6 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**9.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Escrow Agreement. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits instead to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.
