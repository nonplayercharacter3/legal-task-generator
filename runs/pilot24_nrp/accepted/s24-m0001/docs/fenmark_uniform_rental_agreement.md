# UNIFORM RENTAL AND SERVICE AGREEMENT

This UNIFORM RENTAL AND SERVICE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 3, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Fenmark Workwear Rentals, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Fenmark**"), and **Tripux Elevator Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**").

## 1. SERVICES

**1.1 Rental Program.** Fenmark shall rent to Customer the uniform garments and related items listed in Schedule 1 (the "**Rental Items**") for wear by Customer's field technicians, mechanics, route supervisors and warehouse staff (each, a "**Wearer**"). Fenmark shall also launder, repair and replace the Rental Items as described in this Agreement (together with the rental, the "**Services**").

**1.2 Initial Fitting and Issue.** Within 21 days after the Effective Date, Fenmark shall measure each Wearer identified by Customer. Fenmark shall issue each Wearer the number of sets of Rental Items stated in Schedule 1, embroidered with the Wearer's name and Customer's logo as approved by Customer in writing.

**1.3 Weekly Service.** Fenmark shall pick up soiled Rental Items and deliver clean Rental Items once each week, on a scheduled service day agreed with Customer, at each Customer location listed in Schedule 2. Fenmark shall launder all Rental Items in accordance with its standard industrial laundering procedures.

**1.4 Repair and Replacement.** Fenmark shall repair, at no additional charge, any Rental Item that is damaged through normal wear. Fenmark shall replace, at no additional charge, any Rental Item that it reasonably determines cannot be repaired because of normal wear.

**1.5 High-Visibility and Flame-Resistant Items.** Rental Items designated in Schedule 1 as high-visibility shall meet ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 at the time of issue. Rental Items designated as flame-resistant shall meet NFPA 70E arc rating category 2 at the time of issue. Fenmark shall launder those items using processes that the garment manufacturer recommends for maintaining those ratings, and shall remove from service any item that no longer meets the applicable standard.

## 2. WEARER CHANGES

**2.1 Additions.** Customer may add Wearers at any time by written notice or through Fenmark's customer portal. Fenmark shall fit and issue Rental Items to each added Wearer within 14 days after notice.

**2.2 Removals.** Customer may remove Wearers by written notice. Customer shall return all Rental Items issued to a removed Wearer within 14 days after the notice. Charges for that Wearer end on the service week after the Rental Items are returned.

**2.3 Size Changes.** Fenmark shall exchange Rental Items for a different size at no charge, up to twice per Wearer in each Contract Year.

**2.4 Minimum Volume.** Customer shall maintain at least 40 Wearers in the program. If the number of Wearers falls below that minimum for more than two consecutive months, Fenmark may charge for 40 Wearers until the number of Wearers returns to the minimum.

## 3. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

**3.1 Weekly Charges.** Customer shall pay the weekly rental and service charges per Wearer stated in Schedule 1, together with the weekly charges for any additional items stated in Schedule 1.

**3.2 Loss and Damage Charges.** Customer shall pay the replacement charge stated in Schedule 1 for each Rental Item that is lost, not returned, or damaged beyond normal wear, including by cutting, burns from welding or torch work, paint, or chemical damage. Fenmark shall identify each such charge on its invoice by item and Wearer.

**3.3 Emblem and Name Charges.** Customer shall pay the one-time charges stated in Schedule 1 for name and logo emblems on Rental Items issued to added Wearers and for replacement emblems requested by Customer.

**3.4 Price Adjustments.** Fenmark may increase the charges in Schedule 1 once in each Contract Year, beginning with the second Contract Year, by written notice at least 30 days before the increase takes effect. No annual increase shall exceed 4% of the charges then in effect. "**Contract Year**" means each 12-month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

**3.5 Invoicing and Payment.** Fenmark shall invoice Customer monthly. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt. If Customer disputes an invoice amount in good faith, Customer shall notify Fenmark in writing within 30 days after receipt, describing the dispute, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

**3.6 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower, from the due date until paid.

**3.7 Taxes.** Customer shall pay any sales, use or similar taxes imposed on the Services, other than taxes based on Fenmark's income.

## 4. SERVICE STANDARDS

**4.1 Quality.** Fenmark shall deliver Rental Items that are clean, pressed or folded as appropriate, and free of tears, missing buttons and broken zippers.

**4.2 Service Failures.** If Fenmark misses a scheduled weekly delivery, or if more than 5% of the Rental Items delivered to a location in any week fail to meet Section 4.1, Customer may notify Fenmark. Fenmark shall correct the failure within two business days after notice. Customer shall receive a credit equal to that week's charges for the affected location for any failure not corrected within that time.

**4.3 Repeated Failures.** If Fenmark has service failures under Section 4.2 in any three weeks in a period of 12 consecutive weeks, and has not corrected the cause within 30 days after Customer's written notice describing them, Customer may terminate this Agreement under Section 6.3 without any charge under Section 6.4.

## 5. OWNERSHIP AND CARE

**5.1 Ownership.** The Rental Items remain Fenmark's property at all times. Customer shall not sell, pledge or permit any Lien on the Rental Items.

**5.2 Care.** Customer shall make reasonable efforts to ensure that Wearers use the Rental Items only for work purposes. Customer shall store soiled Rental Items in the containers Fenmark provides for pickup. Customer shall not launder Rental Items itself or through a third party.

**5.3 Customer Locations.** Customer shall give Fenmark reasonable access to each location in Schedule 2 during the scheduled service window. Customer may add or remove locations by 30 days' written notice.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

**6.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months. After that, it renews automatically for successive 12-month terms, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

**6.2 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

**6.3 Termination for Service Failure.** Customer may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if the conditions in Section 4.3 are met.

**6.4 Early Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 60 days' written notice. If Customer does so during the initial term, Customer shall pay a termination charge equal to the replacement charges in Schedule 1 for all Rental Items then issued, multiplied by the unamortized percentage stated in Schedule 1 for the number of months the Rental Items have been in service. No other termination charge or liquidated damages apply.

**6.5 Return of Rental Items.** Within 14 days after termination or expiration, Customer shall make all Rental Items available for pickup at the locations in Schedule 2. Customer shall pay the replacement charge under Section 3.2 for any Rental Item not returned.

**6.6 Survival.** Sections 3, 5.1, 6.4, 6.5, 7 and 8 survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 7. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

**7.1 Indemnity.** Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by the indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct in connection with this Agreement.

**7.2 Limitation of Liability.** Neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, special, punitive or consequential damages, or lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except for amounts payable under Section 7.1. Except for amounts payable under Section 7.1 and Customer's payment obligations under Section 3, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the charges paid and payable by Customer in the 12 months before the claim arose.

**7.3 Insurance.** Fenmark shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law. On request, Fenmark shall deliver certificates of that insurance to Customer.

**7.4 Garment Standards.** Fenmark makes no warranty that the Rental Items are suitable for any purpose other than the one described in Schedule 1. Customer remains responsible for determining the personal protective equipment that its Wearers require for each task.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**8.2 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. However, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party, if the successor assumes this Agreement in writing.

**8.3 Independent Contractor.** Fenmark is an independent contractor. Fenmark personnel are not employees or agents of Customer.

**8.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disasters and acts of government. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**8.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**8.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**8.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**8.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
