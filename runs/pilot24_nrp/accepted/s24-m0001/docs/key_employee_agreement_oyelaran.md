# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2022 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Tripux Elevator Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Company**"), and **Renata Oyelaran**, an individual ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. Company operates a business that inspects, maintains, services, repairs and modernizes passenger and freight elevators, escalators and related vertical transportation equipment for commercial, residential and institutional customers (the "**Business**").

B. Company wishes to employ Executive as its Director of Field Operations, and Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

C. In her position, Executive will have access to Company's confidential information, customer relationships, pricing, service histories and workforce. Company would not enter into this Agreement without the covenants in Sections 6 and 7.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, Executive's employment and the compensation described below, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**1.1 Position.** Company employs Executive as its Director of Field Operations. Executive reports to Company's President.

**1.2 Duties.** Executive is responsible for Company's field operations, including:

- supervising the field service technicians, mechanics and route supervisors;
- scheduling preventive maintenance, periodic safety testing and repair work;
- coordinating with state and municipal elevator inspection authorities on inspections, tests and deficiency notices;
- overseeing dispatch and emergency call response; and
- other duties consistent with her position that the President assigns from time to time.

**1.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote her full business time and efforts to Company. Executive may serve on civic, charitable or industry association boards, provided that this service does not interfere with her duties and is not with a competitor of Company.

**1.4 Location.** Executive's principal place of employment is Company's headquarters. Executive shall travel to customer sites and other locations as her duties reasonably require.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three years (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

**2.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms together are the "**Term**."

## 3. COMPENSATION

**3.1 Base Salary.** Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $185,000, payable in accordance with Company's regular payroll practices (the "**Base Salary**"). The President shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 25% of Base Salary. The bonus is based on criteria that the President sets each year after consulting Executive, including:

- field technician utilization;
- timely completion of periodic safety tests; and
- the number of open inspection deficiency notices.

Any bonus earned for a fiscal year shall be paid by March 15 of the following year, provided Executive is employed on the payment date, except as provided in Section 5.3.

**3.3 Retention Payment.** On each anniversary of the Effective Date during the Term, Company shall pay Executive a retention payment of $20,000, provided Executive is employed on that anniversary.

## 4. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**4.1 Benefit Plans.** Executive may participate in the health, dental, disability, life insurance and 401(k) plans that Company makes available to its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.2 Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to 20 days of paid time off each calendar year, in addition to Company holidays, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.3 Vehicle.** Company shall provide Executive with a Company vehicle for business use, together with fuel and maintenance, in accordance with Company's fleet policy.

**4.4 Expenses.** Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company's expense reimbursement policy.

## 5. TERMINATION

**5.1 Termination Events.** Executive's employment may be terminated:

(a) by Company for Cause, effective on written notice;

(b) by Company without Cause, on 30 days' written notice;

(c) by Executive for Good Reason, under Section 5.2(b);

(d) by Executive without Good Reason, on 60 days' written notice; or

(e) automatically on Executive's death or Disability.

**5.2 Definitions.**

(a) "**Cause**" means any of the following:

- Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony;
- Executive's fraud, embezzlement or willful misconduct that materially harms Company;
- Executive's willful falsification of any maintenance, inspection or safety test record;
- Executive's material breach of Section 6 or Section 7; or
- Executive's repeated failure to perform her material duties, if not cured within 30 days after written notice describing the failure.

(b) "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without Executive's written consent:

- a material reduction in Executive's Base Salary or target bonus;
- a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or
- relocation of Executive's principal place of employment by more than 50 miles.

To resign for Good Reason, Executive must give Company written notice within 60 days after the condition first occurs. Company then has 30 days to cure the condition. If Company does not cure it, Executive must resign within 30 days after the cure period ends.

(c) "**Disability**" means Executive's inability to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period, as determined by a physician reasonably acceptable to Company and Executive.

**5.3 Severance.** If Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, Company shall:

(a) continue to pay Executive's Base Salary for 12 months after the termination date (the "**Severance Period**");

(b) pay Executive a pro-rated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance, when bonuses are paid to other employees; and

(c) if Executive elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay the employer portion of the premiums for that coverage during the Severance Period.

Payment of severance is conditioned on Executive signing a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to Company, and on that release becoming effective within 60 days after the termination date. It is also conditioned on Executive's continued compliance with Sections 6 and 7.

**5.4 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, Company shall pay Executive her earned but unpaid Base Salary, accrued but unused paid time off to the extent required by Company policy or law, and unreimbursed business expenses.

## 6. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**6.1 Non-Competition.** During Executive's employment and for 18 months after her employment ends for any reason (the "**Restricted Period**"), Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, own, manage, operate, be employed by, or provide services to any Person that provides elevator or escalator maintenance, service, repair or modernization services within the States of Delaware, Maryland and New Jersey and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (the "**Territory**"). Executive may own up to 2% of any class of publicly traded securities as a passive investment.

**6.2 Customer Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, induce or attempt to induce any customer of Company to terminate, not renew, or reduce its maintenance or service relationship with Company, or to obtain elevator or escalator maintenance, service, repair or modernization services from any other Person. This Section 6.2 applies to any Person that was a customer of Company at any time during the 24 months before Executive's employment ends.

**6.3 Employee Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any field technician, mechanic, route supervisor or dispatcher who was employed by Company at any time during the 12 months before Executive's employment ends.

**6.4 Reasonableness.** Executive acknowledges the following:

- the covenants in this Section 6 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and activity;
- they are necessary to protect Company's goodwill, customer relationships and confidential information; and
- the compensation and benefits under this Agreement are adequate consideration for them.

**6.5 Tolling.** If Executive breaches Section 6.1, 6.2 or 6.3, the Restricted Period for that covenant is extended by the period of the breach.

**6.6 Reformation.** If a court determines that any covenant in this Section 6 is unenforceable as written, the court may modify the covenant to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND WORK PRODUCT

**7.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of Company, including:

- customer lists, contract terms and pricing;
- service histories, maintenance logs, and inspection and test records;
- technician schedules and compensation; and
- business plans and financial information.

**7.2 Non-Disclosure.** During and after her employment, Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, except as required to perform her duties or as required by law. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to a Governmental Authority or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws, including the immunity provisions of 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b).

**7.3 Return of Property.** When her employment ends, Executive shall return all Company property, including vehicles, tools, devices, documents and records, and all copies of Confidential Information.

**7.4 Work Product.** All work product, procedures, checklists and materials that Executive creates in the course of her employment belong to Company.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when delivered by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to Company shall be sent to its headquarters, to the attention of the President. Notices to Executive shall be sent to her most recent home address on file with Company.

**8.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**8.3 Assignment; Successors.** This Agreement is personal to Executive, and Executive may not assign it. Company may not assign this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of Executive, including in connection with any merger, consolidation or reorganization of Company or any sale of all or substantially all of the assets of Company or of the Business. Any purported assignment by Company without the prior written consent of Executive is void. If Company makes or purports to make such an assignment, the covenants in Section 6 terminate and are of no further force or effect. Subject to this Section 8.3, this Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective heirs, legal representatives, successors and permitted assigns.

**8.4 Remedies.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 6 or Section 7 would cause Company irreparable harm. Company is entitled to seek injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, in addition to any other remedy, without posting a bond.

**8.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**8.6 Severability.** Subject to Section 6.6, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**8.7 Withholding.** Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**8.8 Section 409A.** The parties intend that payments under this Agreement are exempt from, or comply with, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. This Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment of severance is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. Any payment conditioned on a release that could be paid in either of two calendar years shall be paid in the later year.

**8.9 Survival.** Sections 5.3, 5.4, 6, 7 and 8 survive the end of Executive's employment, subject to Section 8.3.

**8.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
