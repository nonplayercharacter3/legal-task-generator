# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This **KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Imelda Raskova** (the "**Executive**"). **Sonox AgroDrone LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), joins this Agreement solely for purposes of Section 7.4.

## RECITALS

A. The Company and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Seller on the date of this Agreement.

B. The Executive is the founder and managing member of Seller. She will receive substantial direct and indirect benefits from the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Executive's entry into this Agreement is a condition to the Closing under Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement. This Agreement is the "Key Employee Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the consummation of the Closing and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings given below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Business**" means the crop-imaging drone product line marketed as the Sonox Scout series, as conducted by Seller immediately before the Closing and by the Company after the Closing.

"**Cause**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Competing Business**" means any business, enterprise or activity that engages in, or is preparing to engage in, the Business, or that develops, manufactures, markets or sells products or services that are competitive with the Business.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of the Executive's employment with the Company and the 24 months after the Termination Date.

"**Restricted Territory**" means North America, South America and Europe.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which the Executive's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** Starting on the Effective Date, the Company shall employ the Executive as Chief Technology Officer, Agricultural Systems. She shall report to the Company's Chief Executive Officer.

**2.2 Duties.** The Executive shall perform the duties customarily associated with her position and any other duties consistent with that position that the Chief Executive Officer reasonably assigns. These include leading the integration of the acquired drone airframe, payload and FieldMind flight-control software teams into the Company.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** The Executive shall devote her full business time, attention and efforts to the Company. She may (a) manage her personal investments and (b) serve on civic, charitable or academic boards, as long as these activities do not materially interfere with her duties or breach Article 6. The Executive may wind up the affairs of Seller after the Closing. She shall not cause Seller to conduct any business operations.

**2.4 Location.** The Executive's principal place of work is the Company's agricultural systems facility. She will travel as reasonably required to perform her duties.

**2.5 Term.** The Executive's employment is "at will" and may be ended by either party at any time, subject to Article 4.

## 3. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $325,000, payable under the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board or its compensation committee will review the base salary each year. It may increase the base salary but may not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** The Executive is eligible for an annual target bonus of 40% of base salary, based on performance objectives set by the Chief Executive Officer after consulting the Executive. Any bonus for 2026 will be prorated from the Effective Date.

**3.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus of $750,000 in two equal installments. The first installment is due on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and the second on the second anniversary. Each installment is payable only if the Executive remains employed on that date. If the Company terminates her employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, before an installment is paid, the unpaid installments become payable under Section 4.4.

**3.4 Equity Award.** Subject to Board approval, the Company shall grant the Executive restricted stock units for 60,000 shares of the Company's common stock under the Company's equity incentive plan. The units vest in four equal annual installments, starting on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, if the Executive remains in continuous service. The plan and the award agreement govern the award.

**3.5 Benefits.** The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company makes generally available to its senior executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

**3.6 Paid Time Off.** The Executive is entitled to paid time off under the Company's policies for senior executives, and to no less than 25 days per calendar year.

**3.7 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses she incurs in performing her duties, under the Company's expense reimbursement policies.

## 4. TERMINATION

**4.1 Termination Generally.** The Executive's employment ends (a) on her death, (b) on written notice by the Company after her Disability, (c) on written notice by the Company with or without Cause, or (d) on written notice by the Executive with or without Good Reason. If the Executive resigns without Good Reason, she shall give at least 60 days' prior written notice. "**Disability**" means the Executive is unable to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days.

**4.2 Cause.** "**Cause**" means any of the following by the Executive:

(a) conviction of, or a plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony;

(b) fraud, embezzlement or willful dishonesty toward the Company;

(c) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties after written notice and 30 days to cure;

(d) a material breach of Article 5 or Article 6; or

(e) willful misconduct that causes material harm to the Company.

**4.3 Good Reason.** "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without the Executive's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in base salary;

(b) a material diminution in her title, authority or duties; or

(c) a relocation of her principal place of work by more than 50 miles.

To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give written notice within 60 days after the condition first occurs. The Company then has 30 days to cure. If it does not cure, she must resign within 30 days after the cure period ends.

**4.4 Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall:

(a) continue her base salary for 12 months after the Termination Date;

(b) pay any unpaid retention bonus installments under Section 3.3 within 60 days after the Termination Date; and

(c) pay the employer portion of COBRA premiums for her and her eligible dependents for up to 12 months.

These payments are subject to the Executive signing a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, and that release becoming effective within 60 days after the Termination Date, and to her continued compliance with Article 5 and Article 6.

**4.5 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive her earned but unpaid base salary, accrued but unused paid time off and unreimbursed business expenses through the Termination Date.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates or the Purchased Assets, including information Seller disclosed or developed before the Closing. It includes source code for the FieldMind flight-control software, airframe and payload designs, agronomic models, grower data, pricing and dealer terms. The Executive shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence during and after her employment. She shall not use or disclose it except in performing her duties for the Company or as required by law. Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any Governmental Authority. She also keeps the immunity provided by 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b).

**5.2 Assignment of Inventions.** The Executive hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in any invention, work of authorship, software, design, improvement or other Intellectual Property that she conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment and that relates to the Company's actual or anticipated business. This assignment does not cover any invention that qualifies for protection under Section 2870 of the California Labor Code or any similar applicable law.

**5.3 Prior Work.** The Executive confirms that she assigned to Seller all of her right, title and interest in all Intellectual Property she contributed to Seller's products, including the FieldMind flight-control software. To the extent any such right was not assigned to Seller, she hereby assigns it to the Company.

**5.4 Return of Property.** On the Termination Date, or earlier at the Company's request, the Executive shall return all Company property and all documents and media containing Confidential Information.

## 6. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**6.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the Restricted Territory, (a) engage in any Competing Business, or (b) own, manage, operate, control, finance, be employed by, consult for or provide services to any Person engaged in a Competing Business. Passive ownership of less than 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person does not violate this Section 6.1.

**6.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any customer, dealer, distributor or grower of the Company with whom she had material contact, or about whom she received Confidential Information, to stop or reduce doing business with the Company.

**6.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any employee or individual contractor of the Company, or any Person who held that role during the prior 12 months. General solicitations that do not target those individuals do not violate this Section 6.3.

**6.4 Non-Disparagement.** During and after her employment, the Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, its products or its directors or officers. This Section 6.4 does not restrict truthful testimony or statements required by law.

**6.5 Reasonableness.** The Executive acknowledges the following:
- the covenants in this Article 6 are reasonable in duration, territory and scope;
- they are necessary to protect the goodwill and Confidential Information that the Company acquired under the Purchase Agreement; and
- they are given in connection with the sale of a business in which she holds a material interest.

If a court finds any covenant unenforceable, it may change the covenant to the extent needed to make it enforceable.

**6.6 Remedies.** A breach of Article 5 or this Article 6 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy. If the Executive breaches Section 6.1, 6.2 or 6.3, the Restricted Period is extended by the length of the breach.

## 7. TRANSACTION MATTERS

**7.1 Purchase Agreement.** The Executive acknowledges the following:
- she has read the Purchase Agreement;
- she executed it on behalf of Seller as Seller's managing member; and
- the Company would not have entered into the Purchase Agreement without her agreement to the terms of this Agreement.

**7.2 No Conflicting Obligations.** The Executive represents that she is not bound by any agreement or obligation that would prevent her from performing her duties under this Agreement, other than obligations to Seller that the Closing has released or satisfied.

**7.3 Cooperation.** For 24 months after the Closing Date, the Executive shall reasonably cooperate with the Company in transitioning the Assigned Contracts, the dealer and grower relationships of Seller and the FAA certificates, waivers and exemptions included in the Purchased Assets. If her employment has ended, the Company shall compensate her at a reasonable hourly rate for this cooperation.

**7.4 Purchase Agreement Indemnification.** Under Section 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement, Section 9.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement is hereby amended so that the term "Fundamental Representations" shall not include Section 4.9 (Intellectual Property). As amended, the Fundamental Representations consist of the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority) and 4.14 (Brokers) of the Purchase Agreement. Except as amended by this Section 7.4, the Purchase Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**8.2 Section 409A.** The parties intend this Agreement to be exempt from, or to comply with, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and they shall interpret it on that basis. Each payment under this Agreement is a separate payment. Payments due on a termination of employment are made only on a "separation from service."

**8.3 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company go to the Attention of the General Counsel. Notices to the Executive go to her most recent address in the Company's records.

**8.4 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive, and she may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or of the Purchased Assets.

**8.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Company and the Executive. Section 7.4 may be amended only by a written instrument that the Company and Seller also sign. A waiver is effective only if it is in writing.

**8.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, subject to Section 6.5.

**8.7 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** The laws of the State of Delaware govern this Agreement, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

**8.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**8.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
