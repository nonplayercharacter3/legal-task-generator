# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This **ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026, by and between **Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Sonox AgroDrone LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the Business, including the development and sale of spray, seeding and multispectral imaging drone airframes, related payloads and the FieldMind flight-control software.

B. Dr. Imelda Raskova is the founder and managing member of Seller.

C. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets of the Business and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings given below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement, the Key Employee Agreement and each other agreement, certificate or instrument delivered under Section 3.2 or Section 3.3.

"**Assigned Contracts**" means the Contracts listed in Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule, including Seller's dealer agreements, distribution agreements and grower service contracts.

"**Basket**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a).

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of agricultural drones, payloads and flight-control and agronomic software, including spray, seeding and imaging platforms, as conducted by Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

"**Contract**" means any contract, agreement, lease, license, purchase order, commitment or other legally binding arrangement, whether written or oral.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, option or other encumbrance of any kind.

"**Fundamental Representations**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(b).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court or regulatory body, including the Federal Aviation Administration.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all patents, patent applications, trademarks, copyrights, software (including source code and object code), trade secrets, know-how, designs, data and other intellectual property rights anywhere in the world, together with all goodwill associated with them.

"**Key Employee**" means Dr. Imelda Raskova.

"**Key Employee Agreement**" means the employment agreement between Buyer and the Key Employee to be entered into on the Closing Date.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning given in Section 2.5.

"**Seller Intellectual Property**" means all Intellectual Property owned or purported to be owned by Seller, or used or held for use in the Business, including the FieldMind flight-control software and the patents and patent applications listed in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to Articles and Sections of, and Schedules to, this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in all assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all inventory, finished drone airframes, payloads, components, raw materials and work in process;

(b) the Assigned Contracts;

(c) the Seller Intellectual Property;

(d) all tooling, test equipment, ground-control stations, furniture and other tangible personal property;

(e) all FAA certificates, waivers and exemptions held by Seller, to the extent transferable;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(g) all books, records, customer lists, grower data and supplier lists; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) cash and cash equivalents, (b) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and tax records, (c) Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts, (d) Seller's employee benefit plans, and (e) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all liabilities arising from Buyer's operation of the Business or ownership of the Purchased Assets after the Closing; and

(b) all liabilities under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all liabilities other than Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**").

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller.

**2.6 Allocation.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller an allocation of the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. The Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.7 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax law. Amounts so withheld are treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on October 30, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 7. The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets, duly executed by Seller (the "**Bill of Sale**");

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and the Assumed Liabilities, duly executed by Seller (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**");

(c) an assignment of the Seller Intellectual Property in form suitable for recording with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office and the U.S. Copyright Office, duly executed by Seller (the "**IP Assignment Agreement**");

(d) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Key Employee;

(e) all third-party consents listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule;

(f) payoff letters and releases of all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets; and

(g) a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's managing member and members approving this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller (a) the Purchase Price, (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Buyer, (c) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Buyer, and (d) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors approving this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has full limited liability company power to own the Purchased Assets and to conduct the Business.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly executed by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller, or (c) except as listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, require the consent of any Person under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those to be released at Closing.

**4.5 Condition and Sufficiency of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The Purchased Assets are sufficient for the continued conduct of the Business immediately after the Closing in substantially the same manner as conducted before the Closing.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet and statements of income of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 and the unaudited interim statements for the six months ended June 30, 2026. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP, consistently applied, and fairly present the financial condition and results of operations of the Business in all material respects.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Assigned Contract. Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and in full force and effect. To Seller's knowledge, neither Seller nor any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.8 Inventory.** All inventory included in the Purchased Assets consists of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course of business, net of reserves reflected in the financial statements.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** (a) Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered and applied-for Seller Intellectual Property. (b) Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of all Seller Intellectual Property, free and clear of Encumbrances, and has valid licenses to use all other Intellectual Property used in the Business. (c) The conduct of the Business, including the design, manufacture and sale of Seller's drones and payloads and the use and licensing of the FieldMind flight-control software, does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person. (d) Each current and former employee and contractor who contributed to the development of Seller Intellectual Property, including the Key Employee, has executed a written assignment to Seller of all rights in that contribution. (e) No open-source software is incorporated into the FieldMind flight-control software in a manner that requires disclosure or licensing of its source code.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws, including 14 C.F.R. Parts 107 and 137 and applicable pesticide application regulations. Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all permits, certificates, waivers and exemptions held by Seller for the Business.

**4.11 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.12 Employees.** Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Business with title and current compensation. Seller has complied in all material respects with all applicable employment laws.

**4.13 Taxes.** Seller has filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and the Purchased Assets and has paid all taxes shown as due on them. There are no Encumbrances for taxes on the Purchased Assets.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes Buyer's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand or other sources of immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, sell, license or encumber any Purchased Asset other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Employees.** Buyer may offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to any employee of the Business. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts Buyer's offer, effective as of the Closing, and shall pay all wages, bonuses and accrued benefits owed to those employees through the Closing Date.

**6.4 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of 5 years after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (a) engage in the Business anywhere in North America, South America or Europe, (b) own, manage, operate or provide services to any Person engaged in the Business in those territories, (c) solicit any customer, dealer or grower of the Business to cease or reduce doing business with Buyer, or (d) solicit for employment any employee of Buyer who was an employee of the Business. Passive ownership of less than 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person does not violate this Section 6.4.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent that information becomes generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach of this Section 6.5 or is required to be disclosed by law.

**6.6 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out the purposes of this Agreement.

**6.7 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales laws. Any Loss arising from that waiver is an Excluded Liability.

## ARTICLE 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, (b) Seller having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing, (c) delivery of each item listed in Section 3.2, and (d) no material adverse effect on the Business having occurred since the date of this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing, and (c) delivery of each item listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties, (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, unless the failure results from that Party's breach, or (c) by either Party upon a material breach by the other Party that is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it becomes void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2, Section 6.5 and Article 11 survive, and no Party is relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.13 (Taxes) survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, members, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any Assumed Liability;

(b) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.** (a) A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. (b) The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by notice given within 30 days after receipt of the claim notice. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. (c) The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2(a) exceed $485,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 15% of the Purchase Price.

(b) The Basket and the cap in Section 9.5(a) do not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.9 (Intellectual Property) and 4.14 (Brokers) (the "**Fundamental Representations**"), or to fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of the Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The Basket and the cap in Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to claims under Section 9.3.

(d) Losses are calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party for the relevant matter, less costs of recovery.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, except for claims of fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.12.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** Indemnification payments under this Article 9 are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use, documentary and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date are apportioned between Seller and Buyer on a daily basis as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party bears its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses below (or another address a Party designates by notice):

If to Buyer: Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

If to Seller: Sonox AgroDrone LLC, Attention: Dr. Imelda Raskova, Managing Member.

**11.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Business without consent. Any permitted assignment does not relieve the assigning Party of its obligations.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. If any covenant in Section 6.4 is held unenforceable because of its duration, scope or territory, the court may reduce it to the maximum enforceable extent.

**11.5 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.7 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**11.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY IT.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement prevails, except that (a) the Key Employee Agreement governs the restrictive covenants of the Key Employee, and (b) an Ancillary Agreement governs to the extent it expressly amends a specified Section of this Agreement and is signed by both Buyer and Seller.

**11.10 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, modified or supplemented only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

**11.11 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and the Seller Indemnitees under Article 9, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.12 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement, including Section 6.4 or Section 6.5, were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.
