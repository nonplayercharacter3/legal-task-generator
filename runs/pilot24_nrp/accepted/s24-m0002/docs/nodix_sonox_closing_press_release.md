# Nodix Aerial Systems Completes Acquisition of Sonox AgroDrone Assets

**Acquisition adds spray, seeding and multispectral imaging drone platforms and FieldMind flight-control software to the Nodix agricultural portfolio**

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**October 30, 2026**

## 1. Announcement

Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc. ("Nodix") today announced that it has completed its acquisition of substantially all of the assets of Sonox AgroDrone LLC ("Sonox"), a maker of precision agriculture drones. The transaction closed on October 30, 2026, under an Asset Purchase Agreement the companies signed on September 15, 2026.

Nodix acquired the assets for a cash purchase price of $48,500,000 and also assumed certain liabilities. Nodix paid the purchase price from cash on hand.

## 2. The Acquired Business

Sonox designs, manufactures, sells and services agricultural drones, payloads and flight-control and agronomic software. Its product portfolio includes:

- spray and seeding drone airframes for crop protection and planting applications;
- multispectral imaging drones and payloads for crop monitoring and field analytics; and
- the FieldMind flight-control software, which supports autonomous mission planning and execution across Sonox platforms.

The acquired assets include:

- Sonox's product inventory;
- its intellectual property, including the FieldMind software and Sonox's drone patents and patent applications;
- its dealer, distribution and grower service relationships;
- its tooling and test equipment; and
- its transferable Federal Aviation Administration certificates, waivers and exemptions.

## 3. Leadership

Dr. Imelda Raskova, the founder and managing member of Sonox, has joined Nodix as Chief Technology Officer, Agricultural Systems, reporting to the Nodix Chief Executive Officer. Dr. Raskova will lead the integration of the Sonox drone airframe, payload and FieldMind software teams into Nodix.

"Sonox has built a strong set of agricultural platforms and a flight-control system that growers rely on in the field," said the Chief Executive Officer of Nodix. "We are pleased to welcome Dr. Raskova and the Sonox team to Nodix. Together we will expand the range of solutions we offer to dealers and growers."

"Joining Nodix gives our products and our people the scale to reach more growers," said Dr. Raskova. "I look forward to working with the Nodix team to bring the FieldMind platform and our spray, seeding and imaging drones to a broader market."

## 4. Customers and Dealers

Nodix will continue to support existing Sonox customers, dealers and growers during the transition. Nodix will contact dealers and grower service customers directly about the transfer of their agreements and about ongoing product support and servicing.

## 5. About Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc.

Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that develops unmanned aerial systems and related software for commercial applications.

## 6. About Sonox AgroDrone LLC

Sonox AgroDrone LLC is a Delaware limited liability company that was founded by Dr. Imelda Raskova. Before the closing, it designed, manufactured, sold and serviced precision agriculture drones, payloads and the FieldMind flight-control software.
