# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NODIX AERIAL SYSTEMS, INC.

**Dated as of September 14, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the board of directors (the "**Board**") of **Nodix Aerial Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and the Company's bylaws. The undersigned waive all notice requirements and adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly convened meeting of the Board.

## 1. BACKGROUND

**1.1 Proposed Transaction.** Management has proposed that the Company acquire substantially all of the assets of **Sonox AgroDrone LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Sonox**"). Sonox is engaged in the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of agricultural drones, payloads and flight-control and agronomic software. This includes spray, seeding and multispectral imaging drone airframes, related payloads and the FieldMind flight-control software (the "**Acquisition**").

**1.2 Materials Reviewed.** The Board has reviewed the following materials:

(a) the proposed Asset Purchase Agreement between the Company, as Buyer, and Sonox, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**");

(b) the proposed Key Employee Agreement between the Company and Dr. Imelda Raskova, the founder and managing member of Sonox (the "**Key Employee Agreement**");

(c) management's summary of the material terms of the Acquisition;

(d) the report of the Company's outside counsel on legal, intellectual property and regulatory due diligence, including FAA certificates, waivers and exemptions held by Sonox; and

(e) management's financial analysis of the Acquisition and its funding.

**1.3 Board Discussion.** The Board has discussed the Acquisition with the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and outside counsel. The discussion covered the strategic rationale and the valuation. It also covered the importance to the Company of the FieldMind flight-control software and the Sonox drone patents and patent applications, and the allocation of risk under the indemnification provisions of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Capitalized Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. MATERIAL TERMS APPROVED

The Board has been advised that the Purchase Agreement and the Key Employee Agreement contain the following material terms, among others:

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000, payable in cash at the Closing, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

**2.2 Signing and Closing.** The Purchase Agreement is to be signed on September 15, 2026. The Closing is scheduled for October 30, 2026, subject to satisfaction or waiver of the closing conditions.

**2.3 Purchased Assets.** The Company is to acquire substantially all of the assets used or held for use in the Business, free and clear of all Encumbrances. These include:
- inventory;
- the Assigned Contracts;
- the Seller Intellectual Property, including the FieldMind flight-control software;
- tooling and equipment;
- transferable FAA certificates, waivers and exemptions;
- accounts receivable;
- books and records; and
- goodwill.

Cash, Sonox's organizational and tax records, its employee benefit plans and its non-assigned Contracts are excluded.

**2.4 Business.** "Business" is defined as the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of agricultural drones, payloads and flight-control and agronomic software. The definition includes spray, seeding and imaging platforms.

**2.5 Survival.** General representations and warranties survive until 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations and the tax representations survive until 60 days after the applicable statute of limitations expires.

**2.6 Seller Indemnity; Cap and Basket.** Sonox's liability for breaches of general representations and warranties is subject to a basket of $485,000. It is capped at 15% of the Purchase Price.

**2.7 Fundamental Representations.** The Fundamental Representations are Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.9 (Intellectual Property) and 4.14 (Brokers). Claims for breach of the Fundamental Representations, and claims for fraud, are not subject to the basket or to the 15% cap. Sonox's liability for breaches of the Fundamental Representations is limited to the Purchase Price. As a result, claims relating to title to and non-infringement of the Seller Intellectual Property, including the FieldMind flight-control software and the Sonox drone patents, are not limited by the 15% cap.

**2.8 Seller Non-Compete.** Sonox is to agree not to engage in the Business in North America, South America or Europe for 5 years after the Closing Date. Sonox is also to agree not to solicit customers, dealers, growers or transferred employees during that period.

**2.9 Governing Law.** The Purchase Agreement is governed by Delaware law. Disputes are to be heard in the Delaware Court of Chancery, or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts in Delaware.

**2.10 Key Employee.** At the Closing, Dr. Raskova is to be employed as Chief Technology Officer, Agricultural Systems, reporting to the Chief Executive Officer. Her compensation is:
- an annual base salary of $325,000;
- a target annual bonus of 40% of base salary;
- a retention bonus of $750,000, payable in two equal installments on the first and second anniversaries of the Closing; and
- a grant of restricted stock units for 60,000 shares of common stock, vesting in four equal annual installments.

She is also to be bound by confidentiality, invention assignment, non-competition and non-solicitation covenants. These covenants apply during her employment and for 24 months after it ends, and are intended to protect the goodwill of the Business acquired by the Company.

## 3. RESOLUTIONS

### 3.1 Approval of the Acquisition

**RESOLVED**, that the Acquisition, on the terms described in Article 2 of this consent, is advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders, and is approved.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement are approved. The Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Company, in substantially the form presented to the Board. Any changes approved by the Authorized Officer executing it must be consistent with the material terms described in Article 2. Execution by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that approval.

### 3.2 Ancillary Agreements

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Company, each of the following (together, the "**Ancillary Agreements**"):
- the Bill of Sale;
- the Assignment and Assumption Agreement;
- the IP Assignment Agreement;
- the Key Employee Agreement; and
- each other agreement, certificate and instrument to be delivered by the Company under the Purchase Agreement.

Each Ancillary Agreement must be on terms consistent with the Purchase Agreement and the material terms described in Article 2.

### 3.3 Equity Award to Key Employee

**RESOLVED**, that, effective on and subject to the occurrence of the Closing and the commencement of Dr. Raskova's employment, the Board approves the grant to Dr. Raskova of restricted stock units for 60,000 shares of the Company's common stock under the Company's equity incentive plan. The units vest in four equal annual installments, starting on the first anniversary of the Closing Date, subject to her continuous service. The grant is subject to the terms of the plan and the Company's standard form of award agreement. The Board reserves 60,000 shares under the plan for issuance upon settlement of these units.

### 3.4 Funding

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to pay the Purchase Price from the Company's cash on hand at the Closing, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

### 3.5 Regulatory and Third-Party Matters

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers and the General Counsel are each authorized to take the following actions, in the name and on behalf of the Company:
- prepare, execute and file all notices, applications and filings with any Governmental Authority, including the Federal Aviation Administration, needed to transfer or reissue the FAA certificates, waivers and exemptions included in the Purchased Assets; and
- obtain any third-party consents required under the Assigned Contracts.

### 3.6 Secretary's Certificate

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary of the Company is authorized to certify these resolutions. That certification is to be delivered to Sonox at the Closing under Section 3.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

### 3.7 General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the officers of the Company are each authorized to take all further actions, pay all fees and expenses, and execute and deliver all further documents that they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions, consistent with the terms approved in this consent.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any officer or director of the Company in connection with the Acquisition that are consistent with these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved.

## 4. EFFECTIVENESS

**4.1 Counterparts.** This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

**4.2 Filing.** This consent is effective when signed by all of the directors. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
