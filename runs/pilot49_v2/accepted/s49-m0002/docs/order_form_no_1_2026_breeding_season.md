## 1. Services

**1.1 Scope.** Provider shall perform the following Services for Customer's 2026-27 breeding season:

| Line | Service | Crop | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Mid-density SNP genotyping | Corn | Genotyping of inbred and doubled-haploid lines on Provider's 3K corn SNP panel, including DNA extraction from leaf punches |
| B | Mid-density SNP genotyping | Soybean | Genotyping of breeding lines on Provider's 1.5K soybean SNP panel, including DNA extraction from leaf punches or seed chips |
| C | Seed purity and hybridity testing | Corn | Marker-based hybridity testing of hybrid seed lots, using a 96-seed bulk per lot and a Customer-approved diagnostic marker set |
| D | Trait-introgression support | Corn and soybean | Foreground and background selection analysis for Customer's backcross conversion programs, including recurrent-parent recovery reports for each backcross generation |

**1.2 Marker Panels.** For Lines A and B, Provider shall use the panel versions in effect on the Order Form Effective Date. For Line C, Provider shall use the diagnostic marker set that Customer approves in writing before the first Line C Batch. Any change to a panel or marker set is subject to Section 11 of the Quality Agreement.

**1.3 Deliverables.** For each Batch, Provider shall deliver:
(a) genotype call files in .csv and HapMap format, with per-sample call rates;
(b) for Line C, a purity certificate for each seed lot stating the percentage of off-types and selfs detected;
(c) for Line D, a selection report ranking individuals by foreground marker status and percentage of recurrent-parent genome recovered; and
(d) a plate-level quality control summary showing control performance against the acceptance criteria in Section 7.3 of the Quality Agreement.

Provider shall deliver all Deliverables through Customer's designated secure file transfer site.

**2.2 Submission Windows.** Customer expects to submit Batches in the following windows:
(a) **Window 1** (South American winter nursery): July 1 to September 30, 2026, about 40% of Lines A and B volume;
(b) **Window 2** (Iowa and Illinois fall harvest): October 1 to December 15, 2026, about 35% of Lines A and B volume and all of Line C volume; and
(c) **Window 3** (Hawaii and Puerto Rico off-season nurseries): January 5 to April 30, 2027, the remaining Lines A and B volume.

Customer shall give Provider at least ten business days' advance notice by email to Provider's Quality Contact of each Batch of more than 2,000 samples.

**2.3 Volume Variance.** Actual volumes may vary from the estimates in Section 2.1 by up to 15% for any Line without adjustment to the unit prices in Section 3. The Parties shall agree in writing any variance above 15% under Section 3.3 of the MSA.

## 5. Sample Requirements

**5.1 Formats.** Customer shall submit:
(a) for Lines A, B and D, four 6 mm leaf punches per sample in 96-well collection plates, with wells H11 and H12 left empty for Provider's controls; or, for soybean, seed chips of at least 10 mg per sample;
(b) for Line C, at least 200 seeds per seed lot in sealed, labeled envelopes; and
(c) a Sample Manifest for each Batch in the format set out in Schedule A of the Quality Agreement.

**5.2 Storage and Handling.** Provider shall store and handle all Samples under Section 5 of the Quality Agreement.

**5.3 Disposition.** Within 30 days after Provider releases the Deliverables for each Batch, Provider shall return or destroy the remaining Samples from that Batch under Section 6.4 of the Quality Agreement, as Customer instructs. If Customer gives no instruction, Provider shall hold the Samples until Customer instructs. This Order Form does not authorize Provider to keep any retention or reference portion of any Sample.
