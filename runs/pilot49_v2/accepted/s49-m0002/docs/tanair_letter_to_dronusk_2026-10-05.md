## 1. The Transaction

On September 28, 2026, Tanair Holdings Corp., the parent company of Tanair Seed Genetics, Inc. ("**Tanair**"), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Kelbrin Agronomics plc ("**Kelbrin**"). Under that agreement, a wholly owned subsidiary of Kelbrin will merge with and into Tanair Holdings Corp. Tanair Holdings Corp. will survive the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Kelbrin. The parties publicly announced the transaction on September 29, 2026.

## 2. Timing

The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 and approval by the stockholders of Tanair Holdings Corp. The transaction is currently expected to close on or about January 15, 2027.

## 3. Tanair as Contracting Party

The merger will take place at the level of Tanair Holdings Corp. Tanair will remain the same legal entity. It will keep its corporate name, its principal place of business at 2400 Prairie Research Parkway, Ames, Iowa, and its breeding operations. Tanair will continue as Customer under the MSA, the Quality Agreement and Order Form No. 1 dated June 15, 2026.

## 4. Operations During the 2026-27 Season

We do not expect any change to Tanair's breeding program, sample submission schedule or Quality Contact as a result of the transaction. Dr. Annika Ruehl remains Tanair's Quality Contact under the Quality Agreement. Batches for the 2026-27 breeding season will continue to ship to your Morrisville laboratory as scheduled under Order Form No. 1.

## 5. Confidentiality

Some of the information in this letter, including the expected closing timetable, has not been made public beyond the parties' announcement. Please treat it as Tanair's Confidential Information under Section 9 of the MSA.
