# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Preamble**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Tanair Seed Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2400 Prairie Research Parkway, Ames, Iowa 50010 ("**Customer**"); and

**Dronusk Genomic Services LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 715 Davis Drive, Suite 300, Morrisville, North Carolina 27560 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background**

A. Customer breeds and develops hybrid corn and soybean lines.

B. Provider operates laboratory facilities that provide molecular-marker genotyping, seed purity testing and trait-introgression support services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to perform those services from time to time under Order Forms. Provider is willing to perform them on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

**"Affiliate"** means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting securities of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies by contract or otherwise.

**"Applicable Law"** means all laws, regulations, and governmental orders that apply to a Party's performance under this Agreement. This includes laws on plant health, seed certification, biosafety, export control and data protection.

**"Change of Control"** means any transaction or series of related transactions by which any person or group of persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the voting securities of Customer or of any entity that directly or indirectly controls Customer. It includes acquisition by merger, consolidation, stock purchase, or otherwise.

**"Confidential Information"** has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

**"Customer Germplasm"** means Customer seed, plant tissue, DNA extracted from them, and genotypic data derived from them.

**"Customer Materials"** means all Customer Germplasm, pedigree records, breeding-program information, marker panels supplied by Customer, and other materials and information that Customer or its Affiliates provide to Provider.

**"Deliverables"** means the reports, data files, genotype calls, purity certificates, and other work product that Provider delivers to Customer under an Order Form.

**"Fees"** means the fees for the Services stated in an Order Form.

**"Order Form"** means an order form, in the form agreed by the Parties, that describes Services to be performed under this Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

**"Permitted Assignee"** means (a) any Affiliate of the assigning Party, or (b) any person that acquires all or substantially all of the assets or business to which this Agreement relates.

**"Provider Technology"** means Provider's laboratory protocols, assay designs, bioinformatics pipelines, software, equipment, know-how and other technology that Provider developed independently of this Agreement and without use of Customer Materials.

**"Quality Agreement"** means the Quality Agreement between Customer and Provider governing sample receipt, handling, storage, testing quality and related matters, as amended from time to time.

**"Services"** means the genotyping, seed purity testing, trait-introgression support and related laboratory services that Provider performs under an Order Form.

## 2. Structure of Agreement

**2.1 Order Forms.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Order Form. This Agreement governs each Order Form, and each Order Form forms part of this Agreement. Each Order Form will state the Services, sample volumes, turnaround times, Fees, and any additional terms the Parties agree for that engagement.

**2.2 No Minimum Commitment.** Customer has no obligation to enter into any Order Form unless an Order Form expressly provides otherwise.

**2.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Quality Agreement and the Order Forms may conflict or be inconsistent. If they do, the Quality Agreement shall prevail with respect to sample-handling and quality matters. Subject to that rule, the applicable Order Form shall prevail over the body of this Agreement.

## 3. Services

**3.1 Performance Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services:
(a) in a professional and workmanlike manner;
(b) in accordance with generally accepted practices for agricultural genomics laboratories;
(c) in accordance with the Quality Agreement and the applicable Order Form; and
(d) in compliance with Applicable Law.

**3.2 Turnaround Times.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the turnaround times stated in each Order Form. If Provider expects any delay in delivering results, it shall promptly notify Customer and state the cause and the expected revised delivery date.

**3.3 Changes.** Either Party may request changes to the Services under an Order Form. A change becomes binding only when the Parties record it in a written change order or amended Order Form signed by both Parties.

**3.4 Facilities.** Provider shall perform the Services at its Morrisville, North Carolina laboratory or at another facility that Customer approves in advance in writing.

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees stated in each Order Form. Fees do not include applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes, except taxes based on Provider's net income.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Services completed in the previous month, unless the Order Form states otherwise. Each invoice must identify the Order Form, the sample batches processed, and the Deliverables provided.

**4.3 Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Provider in writing of the reasons before the payment due date. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

**4.4 Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services and Fees for at least three years after the relevant Services are performed. Customer may audit those records no more than once per calendar year, on 15 days' notice.

## 5. Personnel

**5.1 Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign appropriately trained and qualified personnel to perform the Services. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Customer, provided that the quality of the Services does not decline as a result.

**5.2 Responsibility.** Provider is responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and permitted subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 6. Customer Responsibilities

**6.1 Samples.** Customer shall deliver samples in the quantities and condition, and with the identifying information, specified in the applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement.

**6.2 Information.** Customer shall provide reasonable information and cooperation that Provider requests to perform the Services. This includes marker panel specifications and sample manifests.

**6.3 Regulatory Documentation.** Customer shall provide any phytosanitary certificates, permits, or other documents that Applicable Law requires for Customer to ship samples to Provider.

## 7. Customer Germplasm and Samples

**7.1 Ownership.** As between the Parties, Customer owns and retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Germplasm and other Customer Materials. Provider obtains no rights in Customer Germplasm except the limited right to use it to perform the Services for Customer.

**7.2 Restrictions.** Provider shall not, and shall ensure that its personnel and subcontractors do not, do any of the following with Customer Germplasm:
(a) use it for any purpose other than performing the Services;
(b) propagate, grow out, cross, or reverse engineer it, except as an Order Form expressly requires;
(c) transfer or disclose it to any third party; or
(d) combine it with, or use it to develop or improve, germplasm, marker panels, or databases of Provider or any third party.

**7.3 Risk of Loss.** Provider bears risk of loss of, and damage to, Customer Germplasm from the time Provider receives it until Provider returns or destroys it in accordance with this Agreement.

**7.4 Segregation, Custody and Return.** Provider shall segregate Customer Germplasm from the materials of Provider and its other customers. Provider shall maintain chain of custody over all Customer Germplasm. On expiration or termination of this Agreement or the applicable Order Form, or at any earlier time on Customer's written request, Provider shall return or destroy all Customer Germplasm. Each of these obligations applies in each case as set out in the Quality Agreement.

**7.5 Contamination and Loss Events.** Provider shall notify Customer in writing within 48 hours after becoming aware of any actual or suspected loss, contamination, mix-up, or unauthorized access to or use of Customer Germplasm. Provider shall cooperate fully with Customer to investigate and remediate the event.

## 8. Intellectual Property

**8.1 Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Provider retains all rights in Provider Technology.

**8.2 Deliverables.** All Deliverables become the sole property of Customer when created. This includes all genotypic and phenotypic data, purity results, and analyses derived from Customer Germplasm. Provider hereby assigns to Customer all of its right, title and interest in the Deliverables.

**8.3 License to Provider Technology.** To the extent any Provider Technology is incorporated into a Deliverable, Provider grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use that Provider Technology as part of the Deliverable for Customer's internal breeding and commercial purposes.

**8.4 Improvements.** Provider may own improvements to Provider Technology that are of general application, provided that they do not incorporate or disclose Customer Confidential Information or Customer Germplasm.

## 9. Confidentiality

**9.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement. Disclosure may be oral, written, electronic or in any other form. Customer's Confidential Information includes Customer Materials, Deliverables, breeding pipeline information, and the identity and characteristics of Customer's germplasm.

**9.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient;
(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**9.3 Obligations.** The Recipient shall:
(a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement;
(b) disclose it only to its employees, contractors, and advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 9; and
(c) protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**9.4 Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order. Where legally permitted, the Recipient must first give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser can seek a protective order.

**9.5 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 continue during the term of this Agreement and for five years afterward. For trade secrets and Customer Germplasm, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret or Customer Germplasm.

## 10. Warranties

**10.1 Mutual.** Each Party warrants that:
(a) it is duly organized and in good standing;
(b) it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and
(c) this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

**10.2 Provider.** Provider warrants that:
(a) the Services and Deliverables will conform to the specifications in the applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement;
(b) it holds and will maintain all licenses, permits and accreditations required to perform the Services; and
(c) the Deliverables and Provider Technology, as provided by Provider, will not infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights.

**10.3 Remedy for Non-Conforming Services.** If any Services or Deliverables do not conform to Section 10.2(a), Provider shall re-perform the non-conforming Services at no charge, if Customer supplies replacement samples where re-testing requires them. If Provider cannot do so within a reasonable time, Provider shall refund the Fees paid for the non-conforming Services.

**10.4 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

**11.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility. This exclusion includes lost profits and lost revenue.

**11.2 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.3 Exclusions from Limitation.** Section 11.1 and Section 11.2 shall not apply to:
(a) a Party's breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality);
(b) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 12; or
(c) loss, contamination or misappropriation of Customer Germplasm by Provider, its personnel or its subcontractors.

## 12. Indemnification

**12.1 By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from and against all third-party claims, and the losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from them, to the extent the claims arise from:
(a) any allegation that the Deliverables or Provider Technology infringe or misappropriate third-party intellectual property rights;
(b) Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or
(c) Provider's violation of Applicable Law.

**12.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors and employees, from and against all third-party claims, and the losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from them, to the extent the claims arise from any allegation that Customer Materials, as supplied by Customer, infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

**12.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim, but it shall not settle any claim that imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The indemnified Party shall provide reasonable cooperation, at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 13. Insurance

During the term of this Agreement and for two years afterward, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, the following minimum coverage:
(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence;
(b) professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; and
(c) property insurance covering Customer Germplasm in Provider's custody.

Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 14. Term and Termination

**14.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years. After that, it renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. Any Order Form in effect at expiration continues to be governed by this Agreement until that Order Form is completed or terminated.

**14.2 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form by written notice if the other Party:
(a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice of it; or
(b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**14.3 Termination on Change of Control.** If Customer undergoes a Change of Control without Provider's prior written consent, Provider may terminate this Agreement and all Order Forms upon 30 days' written notice to Customer.

**14.4 Termination for Convenience by Customer.** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience on 60 days' written notice to Provider.

**14.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination:
(a) Customer shall pay Provider for Services properly performed up to the effective date of termination;
(b) Provider shall deliver to Customer all completed and in-progress Deliverables;
(c) Provider shall comply with Section 7.4; and
(d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 9.

**14.6 Survival.** Sections 1, 4.4, 7, 8, 9, 10.4, 11, 12, 14.5, 14.6, 16 and 17 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 15. Assignment and Subcontracting

**15.1 Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for all Services performed by its subcontractors.

**15.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, either Party may assign this Agreement in its entirety, without consent, to a Permitted Assignee by written notice to the other Party. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.

**15.3 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 16. Compliance

**16.1 Export Control and Biosafety.** Each Party shall comply with all applicable export control, plant health and biosafety laws in connection with the shipment, handling and storage of samples and data under this Agreement.

**16.2 Anti-Corruption.** Each Party shall comply with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and all other applicable anti-corruption laws.

However, if a force majeure event prevents Provider's performance for more than 30 days, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form on written notice.

**17.4 Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A failure or delay in exercising any right does not operate as a waiver of that right.

**17.5 Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.
