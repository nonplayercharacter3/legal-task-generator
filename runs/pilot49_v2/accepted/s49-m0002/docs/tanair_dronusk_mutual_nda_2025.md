# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 14, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Tanair Seed Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2400 Prairie Research Parkway, Ames, Iowa 50010 ("**Tanair**"); and

**Dronusk Genomic Services LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 715 Davis Drive, Suite 300, Morrisville, North Carolina 27560 ("**Dronusk**").

Tanair and Dronusk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible commercial relationship. Under that relationship, Dronusk would provide molecular-marker genotyping, seed purity testing and trait-introgression laboratory services to Tanair (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement governs the disclosure and use of information exchanged for the Purpose.

## 2. Definitions

**2.1 "Confidential Information"** means all non-public information that a Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") for the Purpose, whether before or after the Effective Date. Disclosure may be oral, written, electronic, visual or in any other form. Confidential Information includes:
(a) for Tanair, breeding program information, pedigree records, line names and codes, trait targets, marker panel specifications, sample volume forecasts and pricing information;
(b) for Dronusk, laboratory protocols, assay designs, bioinformatics methods, capacity information, quality system documentation and pricing information; and
(c) for each Party, the existence and terms of the Parties' discussions.

**2.2 "Representatives"** means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, contractors, and legal, financial and technical advisers.

**2.3 "Affiliate"** means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting securities of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies by contract or otherwise.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate by written records:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient or its Representatives;
(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure by the Discloser;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party that is not under a duty of confidentiality to the Discloser; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations of the Recipient

**4.1 Use.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

**4.2 Disclosure.** The Recipient shall not disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to anyone other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose. Each of those Representatives must be bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**4.3 Standard of Care.** The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**4.4 Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser promptly after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information. The Recipient shall cooperate reasonably with the Discloser to limit the effects of that use or disclosure.

**4.5 No Reverse Engineering.** The Recipient shall not analyze, decompile or reverse engineer any materials or data containing the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or court order. Where legally permitted, the Recipient must first give the Discloser prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser can seek a protective order or other remedy. The Recipient shall disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 6. Biological Materials

No seed, plant tissue, DNA or other biological material will be transferred under this Agreement. Any transfer of biological materials between the Parties requires a separate written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 7. Return or Destruction

Within 15 days after the Discloser's written request, or after termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Discloser in its possession or control, and shall confirm in writing that it has done so. However, the Recipient may keep:
(a) copies required by law or by its bona fide document retention policies; and
(b) copies stored in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible.

Any retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

## 8. Ownership; No License

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. This Agreement grants no license or other right to the Recipient in any Confidential Information, patent, trade secret, plant variety protection certificate or other intellectual property right of the Discloser, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 9. No Warranty; No Obligation

**9.1 No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Discloser makes no warranty, express or implied, as to its accuracy or completeness.

**9.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Neither Party is obligated to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either Party may end discussions at any time for any reason. A commercial relationship will arise only under a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 10. Term

**10.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two years, unless either Party terminates it earlier on 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

**10.2 Survival of Obligations.** The Recipient's obligations under Sections 3 through 8 continue for five years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement. For trade secrets, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 11. Remedies

A breach of this Agreement may cause the Discloser irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Discloser may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or stop a breach, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 12. General

**12.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier, to the address of the receiving Party stated at the beginning of this Agreement (Attention: General Counsel, for Tanair; Attention: Chief Executive Officer, for Dronusk), or to another address that a Party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt.

**12.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 12.2 is void.

**12.3 Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**12.4 Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A failure or delay in exercising any right does not operate as a waiver of that right.

**12.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

**12.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
