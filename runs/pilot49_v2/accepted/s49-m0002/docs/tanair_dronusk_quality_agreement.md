# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and between **Tanair Seed Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2400 Prairie Research Parkway, Ames, Iowa 50010 ("**Customer**"), and **Dronusk Genomic Services LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 715 Davis Drive, Suite 300, Morrisville, North Carolina 27560 ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose and Definitions

**1.1 Purpose.** This Quality Agreement is the Quality Agreement referred to in the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). It sets out the Parties' respective responsibilities for:
(a) sample receipt, identification, storage and handling;
(b) laboratory quality systems; and
(c) related quality matters for the Services.

**1.2 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA. In this Quality Agreement, the following terms have these meanings:

**"Batch"** means a group of Samples that Customer submits under a single Sample Manifest.

**"Chain of Custody Record"** means the written or electronic record described in Section 6.3.

**"Customer Germplasm"** means seed lots, leaf punches and other living plant tissue physically delivered by Customer to Provider for testing under an Order Form.

**"Deviation"** means any departure from this Quality Agreement, an SOP or an Order Form specification. This includes any sample mix-up, mislabeling, contamination, out-of-specification control result, or failure to meet storage conditions.

**"LIMS"** means Provider's laboratory information management system.

**"Quality Contact"** means the individual each Party designates under Section 3.2.

**"Sample"** means an individual unit of Customer Germplasm identified by a unique Customer sample identifier on a Sample Manifest.

**"Sample Manifest"** means the electronic manifest, in the format set out in Schedule A, that accompanies each Batch.

**"SOP"** means a written standard operating procedure of Provider that applies to the Services.

## 2. Scope

**2.1 Services Covered.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Services performed under the MSA and each Order Form. These include molecular-marker genotyping, seed purity testing (including grow-out and marker-based hybridity testing) and trait-introgression support services.

**2.2 Facilities.** Provider shall perform all Services at its Morrisville, North Carolina laboratory, as required by Section 3.4 of the MSA, unless Customer approves another facility in advance in writing.

## 3. Quality System and Responsibilities

Provider shall maintain its ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation for genotyping and seed purity testing throughout the term of the MSA. Provider shall notify Customer within five business days if any accreditation is suspended, withdrawn or materially limited in scope.

## 4. Sample Submission and Receipt

**4.1 Sample Preparation.** Customer shall package Samples in the containers, formats and quantities specified in the applicable Order Form. Customer shall label each Sample with a unique Customer sample identifier, which may be coded and need not disclose pedigree or line names.

**4.2 Sample Manifest.** Customer shall send the Sample Manifest for each Batch to Provider electronically before or at the time of shipment.

**4.3 Receipt Inspection.** Within two business days after a Batch arrives, Provider shall:
(a) inspect each shipping container for damage, temperature excursion and evidence of tampering;
(b) reconcile the Samples received against the Sample Manifest;
(c) log each Sample into the LIMS under its Customer sample identifier; and
(d) send Customer a receipt confirmation listing any discrepancies.

**4.4 Nonconforming Shipments.** If a Batch is damaged, incomplete or does not match its Sample Manifest, Provider shall quarantine the affected Samples and notify Customer's Quality Contact. Provider shall not process quarantined Samples until Customer gives written instructions.

## 5. Storage and Handling

**5.1 Storage Conditions.** Provider shall store Customer Germplasm under the following conditions unless an Order Form specifies otherwise:
(a) seed lots at 4°C to 10°C and no more than 40% relative humidity;
(b) leaf punches and lyophilized tissue at −20°C or below, with desiccant; and
(c) fresh tissue at −80°C or below if it is not processed within 24 hours after receipt.

**5.2 Monitoring.** Provider shall continuously monitor storage temperature and humidity, with alarms that alert on-call personnel at all times. Provider shall record and retain monitoring data under Section 10.

**5.3 Access.** Provider shall restrict access to storage areas containing Customer Germplasm to authorized personnel. Provider shall log each access, whether by badge or by key log.

**5.4 Handling.** Provider shall handle Customer Germplasm only under SOPs designed to prevent cross-contamination. These must include separate workspaces for seed grinding and DNA extraction, cleaning between Batches, and the use of no-template and known-genotype controls on every plate.

## 6. Segregation, Chain of Custody and Destruction

**6.1 Segregation.** Provider shall physically segregate Customer Germplasm from the materials of Provider and its other customers. Each storage unit, rack or shelf holding Customer Germplasm must be dedicated to Customer and clearly labeled. Provider shall not store Customer Germplasm in a common pool or with unlabeled materials.

**6.2 No Commingling.** Provider shall not process Customer Germplasm in the same grow-out plot, tray, grinding run or extraction plate as the materials of any other customer.

**6.3 Chain of Custody.** Provider shall maintain a Chain of Custody Record for each Sample of Customer Germplasm from receipt until return or destruction. The record must show:
(a) the date and time of each transfer;
(b) the identity of each person taking custody;
(c) each storage location; and
(d) each processing step.

Provider shall maintain the Chain of Custody Record in the LIMS and provide Customer with a copy within five business days after Customer's request.

**6.4 Return or Destruction.** Within 30 days after (a) completion of the Services under an Order Form, (b) expiration or termination of the MSA or the applicable Order Form, or (c) Customer's written request, Provider shall do one of the following with all Customer Germplasm in its possession or control:
(i) return it to Customer, at Customer's expense for shipping, to the address Customer designates; or
(ii) if Customer so instructs in writing, destroy it by autoclaving, incineration or another method that renders it non-viable.

**6.5 Certificate of Destruction.** Within ten business days after completing any destruction under Section 6.4, Provider shall deliver to Customer's Quality Contact a certificate of destruction signed by Provider's Head of Quality Assurance. The certificate must identify each Batch and Sample destroyed, the destruction method, the date of destruction, and the personnel who performed it.

**6.6 Retention Samples.** Provider shall not keep any retention or reference portion of Customer Germplasm after return or destruction under Section 6.4 unless the applicable Order Form expressly authorizes it.

## 7. Testing and Quality Control

**7.1 Validated Methods.** Provider shall perform all DNA extraction, genotyping and purity assays using methods that Provider has validated and documented in its SOPs. Provider shall not change a validated method for Customer's work except under Section 11.

**7.2 Controls.** Each genotyping plate must include at least two no-template controls and at least one known-genotype control. Each purity test must include the reference controls specified in the applicable Order Form.

**7.3 Acceptance Criteria.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Provider shall release genotyping results only if:
(a) all controls perform as expected;
(b) the call rate for the plate is at least 95%; and
(c) concordance on known-genotype controls is at least 99%.

**7.4 Data Review and Release.** A qualified analyst shall review each data set, and a second qualified person shall verify it, before Provider releases it to Customer. Provider shall deliver data through Customer's designated secure file transfer site in the format specified in the applicable Order Form.

**7.5 Data Integrity.** Provider shall maintain LIMS and instrument data in a manner that is attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate. Provider shall maintain audit trails and access controls on all systems used for the Services.

## 8. Deviations and Corrective Action

**8.1 Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer's Quality Contact of any Deviation that affects or may affect Customer Germplasm or Deliverables. Notice must be given within 48 hours after Provider becomes aware of the Deviation, consistent with Section 7.5 of the MSA.

**8.2 Investigation.** Provider shall investigate each Deviation, determine its root cause, and deliver a written investigation report to Customer within 15 business days after notification.

**8.3 Corrective and Preventive Action.** Provider shall propose and implement corrective and preventive actions for each Deviation. Customer shall approve closure of the Deviation, and that approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**8.4 Re-Testing.** If a Deviation makes any Deliverable unreliable, Provider shall re-perform the affected Services under Section 10.3 of the MSA.

## 9. Audits and Inspections

**9.1 Customer Audits.** Customer or its designee may audit Provider's facilities, quality system, SOPs and records relating to the Services once per calendar year on 15 business days' notice. Customer may also conduct an additional for-cause audit on five business days' notice after any Deviation that results in loss or contamination of Customer Germplasm.

**9.2 Regulatory Inspections.** Provider shall notify Customer within two business days after any governmental inspection that relates to the Services or to Customer Germplasm. Provider shall give Customer a summary of the findings that relate to them.

**9.3 Audit Findings.** Provider shall respond in writing to audit findings within 20 business days and shall complete agreed corrective actions within the agreed timelines.

## 10. Records

**10.1 Retention.** Provider shall retain the following for at least three years after the relevant Services are performed:
(a) Sample Manifests;
(b) receipt records;
(c) storage monitoring data;
(d) Chain of Custody Records;
(e) raw instrument data;
(f) analysis records;
(g) Deviation reports; and
(h) certificates issued under Section 6.5.

**10.2 Access.** Provider shall make these records available to Customer during audits under Section 9 and otherwise on reasonable request.

## 11. Change Control

**11.1 Notice of Changes.** Provider shall give Customer at least 30 days' prior written notice of any planned change that may affect the Services. This includes changes to:
(a) validated methods;
(b) genotyping platforms or marker chemistry;
(c) critical equipment;
(d) facilities; and
(e) key quality personnel.

**11.2 Customer Approval.** Provider shall not implement a change described in Section 11.1(a), (b) or (d) for Customer's work without Customer's prior written approval. Provider shall perform any bridging or revalidation studies that Customer reasonably requests.

## 12. Term and Amendment

**12.1 Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the date stated above. It remains in effect for as long as the MSA or any Order Form remains in effect and Provider holds any Customer Germplasm.

**12.2 Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties shall review this Quality Agreement at least once every two years.

**12.3 Notices.** Operational communications under this Quality Agreement may be exchanged between the Quality Contacts by email. All other notices must be given in accordance with Section 17.2 of the MSA.

## Schedule A – Sample Manifest Format

Each Sample Manifest must be submitted as a .csv or .xlsx file and include the following fields:
1. Order Form number;
2. Batch identifier;
3. Customer sample identifier;
4. crop (corn or soybean);
5. sample type (seed lot, leaf punch, or other tissue);
6. quantity (seed count or number of punches);
7. requested assay or marker panel;
8. plate and well position (if pre-plated by Customer); and
9. shipping date and courier tracking number.
