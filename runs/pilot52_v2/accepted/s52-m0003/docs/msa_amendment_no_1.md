# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Client and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider supplies staging, rigging, lighting and production crew services for Client's tours, festival dates and single events.

B. On July 13, 2026, the Parties signed Statement of Work No. 7 for the Ember Season 2027 North American Tour (the "**Tour SOW**").

C. Client is reviewing its touring and festival schedule for the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2027 (the "**2027 Contract Year**"). The Parties wish to give Client a one-time right to reduce the Minimum Annual Commitment for that Contract Year, and to make the related changes set out below.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. EFFECTIVE DATE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Effective Date.** This Amendment takes effect on August 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**").

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement. References in this Amendment to Sections are to sections of the Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. INVOICING AMENDMENTS

2.1 **Electronic Invoicing.** The following sentences are added at the end of Section 4.2 of the Agreement:

"Provider shall submit each invoice electronically, in PDF format, to the accounts payable address Client designates in writing. Each invoice must state the SOW number, the Engagement, the invoice period and the Contract Year to which the Fees count under Section 4.3. An invoice is received for purposes of this Section 4.2 on the business day it is delivered to that address."

2.2 **True-Up Statement.** The following sentence is added at the end of Section 4.4 of the Agreement:

"Each true-up invoice must be accompanied by a statement listing each SOW and the Fees invoiced under it that count toward the relevant Contract Year."

## 3. REDUCTION OF 2027 MINIMUM ANNUAL COMMITMENT

3.1 **Reduction Right.** Client may reduce the Minimum Annual Commitment for the 2027 Contract Year from $4,200,000 to $2,900,000 (the "**Reduced Commitment**") by delivering written notice to Provider that states that Client is exercising its right under this Section 3 (a "**Reduction Notice**"). A Reduction Notice must be delivered within 60 days after the Amendment Effective Date.

3.2 **Delivery.** A Reduction Notice must be delivered as provided in Section 18.2 of the Agreement. It is effective on receipt.

3.3 **One-Time Right.** The right in Section 3.1 may be exercised once. It applies only to the 2027 Contract Year. A Reduction Notice, once delivered, may not be withdrawn without Provider's written consent.

3.4 **Effect of Reduction.** If Client delivers a Reduction Notice in accordance with Sections 3.1 and 3.2:

(a) the Minimum Annual Commitment for the 2027 Contract Year is $2,900,000 for all purposes of the Agreement, including Section 4.4 and the definition of "Minimum Commitment Shortfall" in Section 1.1;

(b) the Minimum Annual Commitment for every other Contract Year remains $4,200,000 under Section 4.3; and

(c) the Fees under the Tour SOW continue to count toward the 2027 Contract Year under Section 4.3 and Section 1.3 of the Tour SOW.

3.5 **No Reduction.** If Client does not deliver a Reduction Notice in accordance with Sections 3.1 and 3.2, the Minimum Annual Commitment for the 2027 Contract Year remains $4,200,000.

## 4. TOUR SOW

4.1 **Ratification.** The Parties confirm that the Tour SOW is in full force and is a Statement of Work under the Agreement.

4.2 **No Change to Tour Fees.** Nothing in this Amendment changes the Fees, payment schedule or cancellation charges under the Tour SOW. Exercise of the right in Section 3 is not a cancellation of any Engagement under Section 13.3 of the Agreement.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Agreement Continues.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force. From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **Representations.** Each Party represents that it has authority to enter into this Amendment and that this Amendment has been duly signed by its authorized representative.

5.3 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Section 17 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of this Amendment.

5.4 **Confidentiality.** The terms of this Amendment are Confidential Information of both Parties under Section 8 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which form one instrument.
