# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 7

**Ember Season 2027 North American Tour**

This Statement of Work No. 7 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of July 13, 2026 (the "**SOW Date**") by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC** ("**Client**") and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.** ("**Provider**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement between Client and Provider dated March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). This SOW is governed by and forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 **Engagement.** This SOW covers the Ember Season 2027 North American Tour (the "**Tour**"), an arena tour headlined by the recording artist professionally known as Marisol Vey (the "**Artist**"). The Tour is an Engagement under the Agreement.

1.2 **Tour Period.** The Tour period runs from the first production rehearsal day on April 5, 2027 through the final load-out on July 3, 2027 (the "**Tour Period**").

1.3 **Contract Year.** The first show date of the Tour is April 16, 2027. Under Section 4.3 of the Agreement, all Fees under this SOW count toward the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2027.

1.4 **Client Contacts.** Client's tour production manager for the Tour is the individual Client designates in writing before the first rehearsal day (the "**Production Manager**"). Provider shall take day-to-day direction on the Services from the Production Manager.

## 2. SCHEDULE OF DATES AND VENUES

2.1 **Production Rehearsals.** Provider shall supply full production for rehearsals at the Kingsbridge Production Facility, Bronx, New York, from April 5, 2027 through April 13, 2027. This includes four days of build and lighting programming and five days of full production rehearsals.

2.2 **Show Dates.** The Tour consists of 38 arena show dates in the United States and Canada, as set out in the routing attached as Exhibit A (the "**Routing**"). The Routing as of the SOW Date is Client Confidential Information under Section 8 of the Agreement and is not public until the Tour is officially announced.

2.3 **Routing Changes.** Client may add, move or remove show dates by written notice to Provider. A change that adds show dates, or moves a show date by more than 14 days, requires a change order under Section 3.4 of the Agreement. Cancellation of a show date is governed by Section 6 of this SOW.

2.4 **Daily Schedule.** For each show date, unless the Production Manager issues a different schedule for that Venue:

| Milestone | Time (local) |
|---|---|
| Rigging call (points marked and motors hung) | 7:00 a.m. |
| Trim and focus complete | 1:00 p.m. |
| Show-ready (all systems tested) | 4:00 p.m. |
| Doors | 6:30 p.m. |
| Load-out complete and trucks loaded | 4 hours after show end |

## 3. SERVICES AND PERSONNEL

3.1 **Services.** Provider shall provide the following Services for the Tour:

(a) design support and technical drawings for the stage set, including adaptation of the Artist's creative design to each Venue;

(b) supply, assembly and operation of the main stage, B-stage and connecting thrust;

(c) rigging and flying of the lighting rig, video surfaces, PA hang support, the flown "ember canopy" scenic element and the Artist performer flying system;

(d) supply, programming and operation of the lighting system;

(e) pyrotechnic support, including installation, wiring and operation of Provider-supplied firing systems, and coordination with the licensed pyrotechnic operator and local fire authorities;

(f) load-in and load-out at each Venue; and

(g) trucking coordination for Provider equipment, as stated in Section 5.5.

3.2 **Rigging, Flying and Pyrotechnics.** All rigging, flying and pyrotechnic work on the Tour will be performed by Provider Personnel, including the Local Crew engaged by Provider as independent contractors. In this SOW, "**Local Crew**" means the up riggers, down riggers, fly operators and pyrotechnic technicians that Provider engages at or near each Venue to supplement the touring crew for load-in, show and load-out. Provider shall direct and supervise the Local Crew through the Head Rigger and the Pyrotechnics Supervisor named in Section 3.4.

3.3 **Touring Crew.** Provider shall supply a touring crew of not fewer than 22 persons for the Tour Period, as follows:

| Role | Number |
|---|---|
| Production Lead | 1 |
| Head Rigger | 1 |
| Touring riggers | 4 |
| Automation and flying technicians | 2 |
| Lighting Director | 1 |
| Lighting technicians | 5 |
| Pyrotechnics Supervisor | 1 |
| Pyrotechnic technician | 1 |
| Stage manager and carpenters | 4 |
| Crew chief / logistics coordinator | 2 |

3.4 **Key Personnel.** The following are key personnel for the Tour under Section 5.2 of the Agreement:

(a) Production Lead: Darnell Okafor;

(b) Head Rigger: Ilse Brandvold;

(c) Lighting Director: Tomás Reyes-Ibarra; and

(d) Pyrotechnics Supervisor: Grant Whitcombe.

3.5 **Local Labor.** For each Venue, Provider shall arrange Local Crew in the numbers shown on the Venue labor call issued by the Production Manager at least 21 days before the show date. Provider shall coordinate Local Crew with the Venue and with any applicable collective bargaining agreement, as Section 3.5 of the Agreement provides. Where a Venue's collective bargaining agreement requires use of house labor for any task, Provider shall use house labor for that task and coordinate it with the Venue.

3.6 **Certifications.** Before the first rehearsal day, Provider shall deliver to the Production Manager current certification records for the Head Rigger, the touring riggers, the automation and flying technicians and the Pyrotechnics Supervisor. Before each load-in, Provider shall confirm in writing that the riggers, fly operators and pyrotechnic technicians working at that Venue hold the certifications that the Venue and applicable law require.

## 4. EQUIPMENT

4.1 **Provider Equipment.** Provider shall supply the equipment listed in Exhibit B (the "**Equipment List**"). The Equipment List includes the main stage and B-stage decks, ground support, truss, chain motors and motor control, the performer flying system, the lighting rig and control, the pyrotechnic firing systems and all associated rigging hardware.

4.2 **Spares.** Provider shall carry on the Tour spare motors equal to at least 10% of the motors deployed, one complete spare lighting console, and spare rigging hardware and cabling sufficient to replace any single failed component without delay to the show.

4.3 **Client-Supplied Items.** Client or the Artist will supply the video surfaces and media servers, the audio system, the scenic soft goods and all pyrotechnic product. Provider is not responsible for the condition of Client-supplied items but shall install, rig and operate them as this SOW provides.

4.4 **Inspection.** Provider shall inspect all rigging hardware, motors and the performer flying system before the first rehearsal day and at intervals no longer than every seven show dates. It shall keep inspection logs available for review by the Production Manager and any Venue.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** The Fees for the Tour are as follows:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Production rehearsals (April 5 to April 13, 2027) | $186,000 |
| Show fee, per show date (38 show dates at $71,500) | $2,717,000 |
| Design support and technical drawings | $64,000 |
| Local Crew (estimated, billed at actual per Section 5.3) | $412,000 |
| **Total estimated Fees** | **$3,379,000** |

5.2 **Payment Schedule.** Provider shall invoice the Fees as follows:

(a) a deposit of 20% of the fixed Fees (excluding the Local Crew estimate), payable on signature of this SOW;

(b) a second deposit of 20% of the fixed Fees, invoiced on March 1, 2027;

(c) monthly invoices for the Local Crew charges incurred in each calendar month of the Tour Period; and

(d) the balance of the Fees, invoiced after the final show date under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

All invoices are payable under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Local Crew Charges.** Provider shall invoice Local Crew at the hourly rates actually paid by Provider plus a coordination fee of 8%. Each invoice must be supported by Venue labor sign-in sheets or equivalent time records.

5.4 **Per Diem and Lodging.** Client shall reimburse lodging for the touring crew at cost, and per diem at $65 per crew member per day, for each day of the Tour Period that the touring crew is away from New York City. These amounts are reimbursable expenses under Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Freight.** Provider shall supply up to nine 53-foot trucks for Provider equipment. Client shall reimburse trucking and fuel at cost, without mark-up, under Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

## 6. CANCELLATION

6.1 **Cancellation by Client.** If Client cancels the whole Tour or any show date under Section 13.3 of the Agreement, Client shall pay the following cancellation charges, measured from the date of Provider's receipt of the cancellation notice to the cancelled show date (or, for the whole Tour, to the first rehearsal day):

| Notice period | Cancellation charge |
|---|---|
| More than 120 days | Deposits paid to date are retained; no further charge |
| 61 to 120 days | 35% of the Fees for the cancelled show dates |
| 15 to 60 days | 60% of the Fees for the cancelled show dates |
| 14 days or fewer | 100% of the Fees for the cancelled show dates |

6.2 **Credit of Deposits.** Deposits paid are credited against any cancellation charge. Local Crew charges are payable only to the extent Provider has incurred them and cannot cancel them.

6.3 **Force Majeure.** If a Force Majeure Event causes cancellation of a show date, then for purposes of Section 15.2 of the Agreement: (a) if the cancellation occurs after load-in has begun, Client shall pay 50% of the show fee for that date plus Local Crew charges actually incurred; and (b) if the cancellation occurs before load-in, no show fee is payable for that date. Each Party otherwise bears its own costs.

## 7. RIGGING PLANS AND SAFETY

7.1 **Rigging Plans.** For each Venue, Provider shall deliver load calculations and the rigging plot to the Production Manager and the Venue at least 30 days before the show date, under Section 6.2 of the Agreement. The performer flying system and the ember canopy must be covered by a stamped engineering review before the first rehearsal day.

7.2 **Pyrotechnic Permits.** Client shall obtain pyrotechnic permits for each Venue, with Provider's assistance. Provider shall supply the technical information that permit applications require at least 30 days before the show date.

7.3 **Safety Standards.** The Services are subject to Section 6 of the Agreement and to the production quality and safety agreement between the Parties.

## 8. DELIVERABLES AND SERVICE LEVELS

8.1 **Deliverables.** The Deliverables under this SOW are the stage and scenic technical drawings, the rigging plots for each Venue, and the lighting cue files and show file for the Tour. Ownership passes under Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

8.2 **Service Levels.** The Service Level Schedule applies to the Tour. For that purpose the show-ready time in Section 2.4 is the show-readiness target, and the load-out completion time in Section 2.4 is the load-out target.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Insurance.** Before the first rehearsal day, Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance meeting Section 11 of the Agreement, naming Client and each Venue that requires it as additional insureds.

9.2 **Change Orders.** Any change to the Services, the Equipment List, the crew complement or the Fees requires a change order under Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

9.3 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
