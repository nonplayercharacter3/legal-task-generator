# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Schedule to the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 between Diril Live Presents LLC and Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE AND RELATIONSHIP TO THE AGREEMENT

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule is the Service Level Schedule referred to in Sections 1.1, 2.2 and 3.6 of the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). It sets out the service levels Provider shall meet in performing the Services and the service credits that apply when Provider fails to meet them.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Schedule:

"**Measurement Month**" means each calendar month during the Term in which Provider performs Services under any SOW.

"**Monthly Production Fees**" means the Fees for Services performed in a Measurement Month under all SOWs, excluding reimbursable expenses under Section 4.5 of the Agreement and taxes. Where a Fee covers Services performed over more than one calendar month, it is allocated to each Measurement Month in proportion to the show dates, rehearsal days or other days of Services in that month.

"**Service Date**" means each show date, rehearsal day, load-in day or load-out day on which Provider performs Services under a SOW.

"**Service Level**" means each performance standard in Section 2.

References to Sections are to sections of this Schedule unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Application.** This Schedule applies to every Engagement under every SOW. A SOW may set the specific target times for an Engagement, including the show-ready and load-out times. Where a SOW does not set a target time, the target time is the time stated in the production schedule Client issues for the Engagement under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

## 2. SERVICE LEVELS

2.1 **Show-Readiness.** On each show date, all Provider-supplied staging, rigging, lighting, automation and pyrotechnic firing systems shall be installed, tested and ready for the show by the show-readiness target for that show date. A system is ready when it has passed Provider's pre-show checks and Client's production manager has received confirmation from Provider's Production Lead or crew chief.

2.2 **Load-In Milestones.** On each load-in day, Provider shall complete the rigging call and the trim and focus milestones stated in the applicable SOW or production schedule by the stated times.

2.3 **Load-Out Completion.** After each show date, Provider shall complete load-out of Provider equipment, and have its trucks loaded, by the load-out target for that Venue.

2.4 **Equipment Availability.** During each show, Provider-supplied equipment shall operate without a failure that causes (a) a stop of the show, (b) the omission of a scheduled lighting, automation, flying or pyrotechnic cue sequence, or (c) a delay to the show start of more than 10 minutes.

2.5 **Rigging Documentation.** Provider shall deliver the load calculations and rigging plot for each Venue by the time required under the applicable SOW and Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

## 3. SERVICE CREDITS

3.1 **Credit Amounts.** If Provider fails to meet a Service Level, Client is entitled to the following service credit for each failure, calculated as a percentage of the Fees attributable to the affected Service Date:

| Service Level | Failure | Service credit |
|---|---|---|
| Show-readiness (Section 2.1) | Ready up to 30 minutes late, without delaying doors | 2% |
| Show-readiness (Section 2.1) | Ready more than 30 minutes late, or doors delayed | 5% |
| Load-in milestones (Section 2.2) | Any milestone missed by more than 60 minutes | 1% |
| Load-out completion (Section 2.3) | Completed up to 2 hours late | 1% |
| Load-out completion (Section 2.3) | Completed more than 2 hours late | 3% |
| Equipment availability (Section 2.4) | Omitted cue sequence or start delayed more than 10 minutes | 4% |
| Equipment availability (Section 2.4) | Show stop | 10% |
| Rigging documentation (Section 2.5) | Delivered late | 1% |

3.2 **Fees Attributable to a Service Date.** For a show date, the Fees attributable to the Service Date are the per-show fee stated in the applicable SOW. For a rehearsal day, load-in day or load-out day that is not a show date, they are the Fees the SOW states for that day or, if the SOW states a lump sum for a period, that lump sum divided equally by the number of days in the period. For a rigging documentation failure, the credit is calculated on the per-show fee for the show date to which the documentation relates.

3.3 **Single Credit per Event.** Where one event causes more than one Service Level failure on the same Service Date, Client is entitled only to the highest single credit applicable to that event.

3.4 **Exclusions.** No service credit applies to a failure to the extent caused by:

(a) Client, the artist, the Venue, or any of their employees or contractors, including late Venue access, late arrival of Client-supplied items, or Client-requested changes to the schedule or production on the day;

(b) a failure of Client-supplied items, including Client-supplied video surfaces, media servers, audio systems and soft goods;

(c) a Force Majeure Event;

(d) a stop-work under Section 6.4 of the Agreement that is not caused by Provider's failure to comply with the Agreement or the production quality and safety agreement; or

(e) a hold or instruction given by a Venue or public authority that is not caused by Provider.

## 4. CREDIT CAP

4.1 **Monthly Cap.** The total service credits for any Measurement Month shall not exceed 15% of monthly production fees for that Measurement Month.

4.2 **Calculation.** The cap in Section 4.1 is applied to the Monthly Production Fees for the Measurement Month in which the affected Service Dates occurred, regardless of when the relevant Fees are invoiced.

## 5. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

5.1 **Records.** For each Service Date, Provider's Production Lead or crew chief shall record the actual show-ready time, the load-in milestone times, the load-out completion time and any equipment failure during the show. The record shall be countersigned or confirmed by email by Client's production manager within two business days after the Service Date. If Client's production manager does not respond within that period, the record is treated as confirmed, unless Client later shows it to be inaccurate.

5.2 **Monthly Report.** Within 10 business days after the end of each Measurement Month, Provider shall deliver to Client a report showing, for each Engagement, each Service Level result, each failure, the credit calculated under Section 3 and the total credits for the month after applying Section 4.

5.3 **Disputes.** Client may dispute a monthly report by written notice within 30 days after receiving it. The Parties shall try to resolve the dispute through their production representatives. Any unresolved dispute shall be handled under Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

5.4 **Review.** Service Level results shall be reviewed at the post-engagement review meeting held under Section 9.1 of the production quality and safety agreement.

## 6. APPLICATION OF CREDITS

6.1 **Invoice Credit.** Provider shall apply each service credit shown in a monthly report against the next invoice it issues to Client under the Agreement. If no further invoice is due under any SOW, Provider shall pay the credit to Client within 30 days after the monthly report is delivered.

6.2 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** For the purposes of Sections 4.3 and 4.4 of the Agreement, Fees count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment before deduction of any service credit.

6.3 **Other Rights.** Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services. They are in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 9.3 of the Agreement and do not limit Client's rights under Section 10 or Section 13 of the Agreement.

## 7. CHRONIC FAILURE

7.1 **Remediation Plan.** If Provider fails the show-readiness Service Level or the equipment availability Service Level on three or more show dates of the same Engagement in any period of 30 consecutive days, Provider shall, within 10 business days after Client's request, deliver a written remediation plan. The plan shall identify the causes of the failures and the corrective steps, and Provider shall implement it on the timetable agreed with Client.

7.2 **Material Breach.** A failure of the show-readiness or equipment availability Service Level on five or more show dates of the same Engagement in any period of 60 consecutive days is a material breach of the Agreement for purposes of Section 13.1 of the Agreement.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Part of the Agreement.** This Schedule is a schedule under Section 2.2 of the Agreement and forms part of it.

8.2 **Review and Changes.** The Parties shall review the Service Levels at least once in each Contract Year. Any change to this Schedule must be made by written amendment signed by both Parties under Section 18.3 of the Agreement.

8.3 **Governing Law.** This Schedule is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

8.4 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which form one instrument.
