## 2. Purpose

2.1 The meeting reviewed pre-production progress for the Ember Season 2027 North American Tour under Statement of Work No. 7 dated July 13, 2026 (the "SOW"). It also confirmed the deliverables and deadlines leading to production rehearsals at the Kingsbridge Production Facility, which start on April 5, 2027.

2.2 Commercial terms were not discussed, except for the invoicing items in Section 8.

## 3. Design and Technical Drawings

3.1 Kevond reported that the base stage set drawings, covering the main stage, B-stage and connecting thrust, are at 80% completion. Venue-specific adaptations will begin once Diril releases the final Routing.

3.2 The artist creative team confirmed that the "ember canopy" scenic design is frozen. No further changes to its dimensions or weight are expected. Kevond noted that any later change affecting the Equipment List, the crew complement or the Fees would need a change order under Section 3.4 of the Agreement, as SOW Section 9.2 provides.

3.3 The Equipment List remains at Revision 3, dated July 8, 2026. No revision is proposed at this time.

**Action:** Kevond (D. Okafor) to circulate the 100% base set drawings by October 30, 2026.

## 4. Rigging and Engineering Review

4.1 Ms. Brandvold confirmed that the performer flying system and the ember canopy will be submitted for stamped engineering review. Kevond expects the review to be complete well ahead of the first rehearsal day, as SOW Section 7.1 requires. The canopy is expected to exceed 2,000 pounds on two of its pick points, so it falls within Section 4.2(c) of the production quality and safety agreement in any event.

4.2 Venue load calculations and rigging plots will be prepared on a rolling basis and delivered at least 30 days before each show date. Diril asked that the first six Venues' plots be delivered as a batch before rehearsals. Kevond agreed.

4.3 Kevond will load test the performer flying system at Kingsbridge before its first use, as Section 4.4 of the production quality and safety agreement requires. Diril will arrange for the artist's flying double to attend the test session.

**Action:** Kevond (I. Brandvold) to confirm the engineering firm and the review timetable by October 15, 2026.

## 5. Lighting

5.1 Mr. Reyes-Ibarra reported that the lighting plot is complete in draft. Programming will take place during the four build and programming days at Kingsbridge (April 5 to April 8, 2027).

5.2 Kevond confirmed that one complete spare lighting console will travel with the Tour, as SOW Section 4.2 requires.

5.3 Diril asked for a pre-visualization session with the artist's creative team in February 2027. Kevond agreed in principle, subject to confirming studio availability.

**Action:** Kevond (T. Reyes-Ibarra) to propose pre-visualization dates by December 1, 2026.

## 6. Pyrotechnics

6.1 Mr. Whitcombe confirmed that Kevond will supply the firing systems, and that pyrotechnic product will be supplied by Diril or the artist under SOW Section 4.3.

6.2 Diril will lead the permit applications for each Venue. Kevond will supply the technical information for each application at least 30 days before the show date, under SOW Section 7.2. Diril noted that two Canadian Venues are expected to need longer lead times. It will flag those Venues once the Routing is final.

6.3 Diril confirmed that the licensed pyrotechnic operator for the Tour will be engaged by the artist's production. Diril will introduce the operator to Mr. Whitcombe by the end of November 2026.

**Action:** Diril (M. Feld) to provide the pyrotechnic operator's contact details and license information by November 30, 2026.

## 7. Crew, Labor Calls and Credentials

7.1 Kevond confirmed the touring crew complement of 22 set out in SOW Section 3.3. Mr. Okafor confirmed that all four key personnel named in SOW Section 3.4 are committed for the full Tour Period.

7.2 Diril confirmed that it will issue the Venue labor call for each Venue at least 21 days before the show date, as SOW Section 3.5 provides. Diril will copy Kevond's crew chiefs on its correspondence with Venue labor stewards.

7.3 Kevond will deliver certification records for the touring rigging, flying and pyrotechnic crew to the Production Manager before the first rehearsal day, under SOW Section 3.6.

7.4 Crew accreditation for the Tour will run through the existing process under the data processing addendum. Diril asked that credential lists for Canadian show dates be sent only through the accreditation portal. Kevond confirmed that this is its current practice.

7.5 Diril confirmed that it will designate the Production Manager for the Tour in writing before the first rehearsal day, under SOW Section 1.4. Ms. Holloway will act as the day-to-day contact until then.

## 8. Invoicing and Insurance

8.1 Ms. Anand confirmed that Kevond's invoice for the first deposit under SOW Section 5.2(a) has been received. Kevond noted that the second deposit will be invoiced on March 1, 2027.

8.2 Kevond confirmed that it will submit all Tour invoices electronically, stating the SOW number, the Engagement, the invoice period and the Contract Year, in line with Section 4.2 of the Agreement as amended.

8.3 Kevond will deliver certificates of insurance naming Diril and each Venue that requires it as additional insureds before the first rehearsal day, as SOW Section 9.1 requires.
