# PRODUCTION QUALITY AND SAFETY AGREEMENT

This Production Quality and Safety Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE AND RELATIONSHIP TO THE AGREEMENT

1.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the quality, safety and documentation standards that apply to the Services. It is the production quality and safety agreement referred to in Sections 2.2 and 6.1 of the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**").

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement. References to Sections are to sections of this Quality Agreement unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Application.** This Quality Agreement applies to every Engagement under every SOW. An SOW may add safety or quality requirements specific to an Engagement. Where it does, Provider shall meet both the requirements of this Quality Agreement and the additional requirements of the SOW.

## 2. SAFETY MANAGEMENT

2.1 **Safety Program.** Provider shall maintain a written safety program covering rigging, working at height, fall protection, aerial lift and forklift operation, electrical distribution, pyrotechnic support, manual handling and heat and weather exposure. Provider shall provide a current copy to Client on request and shall notify Client in writing of any material change.

2.2 **Standards.** Provider shall perform the Services in compliance with:

(a) applicable federal, state, provincial and local occupational safety law, including OSHA regulations and the corresponding Canadian requirements for show dates in Canada;

(b) ANSI E1.2 (aluminum trusses and towers), ANSI E1.6-1 (powered hoist systems), ANSI E1.21 (temporary structures for outdoor events) and other ANSI E1 standards that apply to the work;

(c) NFPA 1126 for proximate pyrotechnics, as adopted by the local authority having jurisdiction;

(d) Venue rigging and safety rules made available to Provider; and

(e) manufacturer instructions for all equipment.

2.3 **Safety Officer.** For each Engagement, Provider shall designate a safety officer, who may be the Production Lead or Head Rigger named in the SOW. The safety officer is Client's point of contact on safety matters and has authority to stop any Provider activity under Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

2.4 **Daily Safety Briefing.** On each load-in and load-out day, Provider shall hold a safety briefing before work begins. The briefing shall cover the day's rigging and overhead work, exclusion zones, weather conditions and any open safety items. Provider shall keep a sign-in record of each briefing.

## 3. PERSONNEL QUALIFICATIONS

3.1 **Certification Requirements.** Provider shall ensure that Provider Personnel performing the following tasks hold the following current qualifications, or an equivalent qualification acceptable to the Venue and the authority having jurisdiction:

| Task | Minimum qualification |
|---|---|
| Head rigger | ETCP Certified Rigger (Arena) |
| Up rigging and motor operation | ETCP Certified Rigger (Arena) or documented equivalent experience accepted by the Venue |
| Performer flying | Manufacturer training on the flying system in use, plus 40 hours of supervised operation |
| Electrical distribution | ETCP Certified Entertainment Electrician |
| Aerial lift operation | Operator training compliant with ANSI A92.22 and A92.24 |
| Pyrotechnic support | License or permit required by the authority having jurisdiction |

3.2 **Records.** Provider shall keep certification and training records for Provider Personnel and shall make them available to Client within two business days after Client's request.

3.3 **Local Labor.** Where Provider arranges local labor under Section 3.5 of the Agreement, Provider shall give the Venue labor steward or crew chief a written scope of the tasks to be performed and shall confirm with the Venue, before load-in, the certifications the Venue requires for those tasks. Provider shall not assign local labor to performer flying or to pyrotechnic firing.

3.4 **Fitness for Duty.** Provider shall not permit any individual to perform rigging, flying, pyrotechnic or electrical work while impaired by alcohol, drugs or fatigue. Provider shall schedule work so that no member of the touring crew works more than 16 hours in any 24-hour period, except in an emergency affecting safety.

## 4. RIGGING AND STRUCTURES

4.1 **Rigging Plans.** The load calculations and rigging plots required by Section 6.2 of the Agreement shall show, for each rigging point, the expected static load, the dynamic allowance, the motor capacity and the Venue's rated capacity for that point. Provider shall deliver revised plans promptly after any change to the rig.

4.2 **Engineering Review.** Provider shall obtain review by a licensed professional engineer of (a) any performer flying system, (b) any ground-supported structure over 20 feet in height, (c) any flown element exceeding 2,000 pounds on a single point, and (d) any element for which the Venue or applicable law requires engineering review. Provider shall deliver the stamped review to Client before the element is first used.

4.3 **Inspection.** Provider shall inspect motors, rigging hardware, truss and soft goods in line with manufacturer requirements and ANSI E1.6-1. It shall inspect the full rig before the first show date of each Engagement and at the intervals the SOW states. Provider shall remove from service any item that fails inspection and shall tag it until repaired or replaced.

4.4 **Load Testing.** Before the first use of a performer flying system on each Engagement, and after any repair to it, Provider shall carry out a load test at not less than the test load the manufacturer specifies and shall record the result.

4.5 **Exclusion Zones.** During rigging, trim and load-out, Provider shall establish and enforce exclusion zones beneath overhead work. Provider shall coordinate those zones with the Venue and with Client's production manager.

## 5. OUTDOOR EVENTS AND WEATHER

5.1 **Wind Management Plan.** For each outdoor Engagement involving temporary structures, flown elements or LED surfaces, Provider shall prepare a written wind management plan. The plan shall state the wind speeds at which (a) monitoring increases, (b) flown or exposed elements are lowered or secured, and (c) the structure is evacuated. The plan shall reflect the engineering data for each structure.

5.2 **Monitoring.** Provider shall provide an anemometer at the highest practical point of each outdoor structure, and shall monitor weather forecasts and lightning detection services during the Engagement.

5.3 **Action.** Provider shall take the actions stated in the wind management plan when the stated thresholds are reached. It shall promptly inform Client's production manager and the Venue of those actions. Any cancellation or delay of a show for weather is governed by Section 15 of the Agreement and the applicable SOW.

## 6. PYROTECHNIC SUPPORT

6.1 **Scope.** Provider's pyrotechnic support consists of installation, wiring and operation of Provider-supplied firing systems and coordination with the licensed pyrotechnic operator, as the applicable SOW provides. Unless the SOW states otherwise, pyrotechnic product is supplied by Client or the artist.

6.2 **Firing System Checks.** Provider shall test each firing system for continuity and fault conditions before each show, with no product connected during system tests. Provider shall maintain a separate, keyed arming control under the control of the licensed operator.

6.3 **Clearances.** Provider shall install firing positions at the clearances shown in the approved permit and the manufacturer data for the product. It shall not change a firing position without the agreement of the licensed operator and, where required, the authority having jurisdiction.

6.4 **Storage.** Provider shall not store pyrotechnic product in its equipment or trucks unless the applicable SOW expressly provides for it and all required permits are in place.

## 7. EQUIPMENT QUALITY

7.1 **Maintenance Records.** Provider shall maintain a maintenance and inspection record for each motor, controller, performer flying system, lighting console and firing system that it supplies. The record shall identify the item by serial number.

7.2 **Condition on Delivery.** All equipment Provider supplies shall be clean, complete, tested and in working order on delivery to the rehearsal facility or first Venue. Lighting fixtures shall be lamped or LED-tested, and motor control shall be function-tested with all motors in the package.

7.3 **Failure Reporting.** Provider shall log each equipment failure during an Engagement, the cause where known, and the corrective action. Provider shall provide the log to Client's production manager at the end of each week of an Engagement and at the end of the Engagement.

## 8. INCIDENT REPORTING AND INVESTIGATION

8.1 **Immediate Notice.** Provider shall report each accident, injury, near miss or equipment failure as required by Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Where the incident involves serious injury, a fall from height, a dropped object from a flown element, or an unplanned pyrotechnic discharge, Provider shall notify Client's production manager by telephone as soon as practicable and in any event within one hour.

8.2 **Written Report.** Within 72 hours after any incident reportable under Section 8.1, Provider shall deliver to Client a written report. The report shall describe what happened, the persons and equipment involved, witnesses, immediate actions taken, and the preliminary cause.

8.3 **Root Cause Analysis.** For any incident involving serious injury or a dropped object from a flown element, Provider shall complete a root cause analysis and a corrective action plan within 15 business days after the incident. Provider shall implement the corrective actions on the timetable agreed with Client.

8.4 **Preservation.** Provider shall preserve equipment, logs, photographs and other records relating to any incident reportable under Section 8.1 until Client confirms in writing that they may be released, and shall cooperate with any investigation by Client, the Venue, an insurer or a public authority.

8.5 **Regulatory Notices.** Each Party shall make any notice to a public authority that applicable law requires of it. Each Party shall give the other a copy of any such notice relating to an Engagement promptly after it is made.

## 9. QUALITY REVIEW AND AUDIT

9.1 **Post-Engagement Review.** Within 30 days after the final show date of each touring Engagement, the Parties shall hold a review meeting covering safety performance, incidents, equipment reliability and any service level results under the Service Level Schedule.

9.2 **Safety Audit.** Client, or a safety consultant it appoints who is bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with Section 8 of the Agreement, may audit Provider's compliance with this Quality Agreement at any Venue or at Provider's shop on reasonable notice. Audits shall not interfere with load-in, rehearsals or shows.

9.3 **Corrective Action.** Provider shall correct any non-compliance identified in an audit within the period Client reasonably specifies, taking into account the safety risk involved. Any non-compliance that presents an immediate risk to persons shall be corrected before the affected work continues.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Part of the Agreement.** This Quality Agreement is an addendum under Section 2.2 of the Agreement and forms part of it.

10.2 **Term.** This Quality Agreement remains in effect for the Term and for any period after the Term during which an SOW remains in progress under Section 12.3 of the Agreement.

10.3 **Review.** The Parties shall review this Quality Agreement at least once in each Contract Year and shall update it by written amendment signed by both Parties to reflect changes in applicable law or industry standards.

10.4 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

10.5 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which form one instrument.
