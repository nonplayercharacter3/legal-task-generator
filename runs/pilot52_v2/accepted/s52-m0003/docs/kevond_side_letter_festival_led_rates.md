**Re: Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 between Diril Live Presents LLC and Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc. (the "Agreement"): Festival LED Wall and Video Package Rates**

Dear Counsel:

This letter sets out the rates and related terms the Parties have agreed for LED wall and festival video packages. It follows Section 3.4 of the term sheet dated November 14, 2023. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This letter applies to any SOW for a festival Engagement under which Provider supplies LED video wall, video processing, camera or IMAG (image magnification) equipment and related crew (a "**Festival Video Package**"). It applies only where the first show date of that Engagement falls within the Initial Term.

1.2 This letter does not apply to touring Engagements or single events, unless the applicable SOW expressly adopts it.

## 2. Package Rates

2.1 Unless the applicable SOW states a lower rate, Provider shall charge the following for each Festival Video Package:

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| LED wall panels (3.9 mm pixel pitch or finer), including rigging frames and ground support | $38.00 per square foot per show day |
| LED wall panels (above 3.9 mm pixel pitch) | $29.00 per square foot per show day |
| Video processing and media server package (primary and backup) | $4,750 per show day |
| IMAG camera package (up to four cameras, with switcher) | $6,200 per show day |
| Video crew (engineer, LED technicians, camera operators) | per Provider's rate card in effect on the SOW date, less 10% |

2.2 **Multi-day discount.** For a festival Engagement of three or more consecutive show days at a single Venue, the equipment rates in Section 2.1 for the third and each later show day are reduced by 20%.

2.3 **Non-show days.** Load-in, rehearsal and load-out days are charged at 50% of the equipment rates in Section 2.1. Crew time on those days is charged at the applicable crew rate.

2.4 **Rate hold.** The rates in Section 2.1 are fixed through February 28, 2026. After that date, Provider may increase them once per Contract Year, by written notice given at least 90 days before the start of the Contract Year. No increase may exceed 4% over the rates in effect for the prior Contract Year. An increase does not apply to an SOW signed before the increase takes effect.

## 3. Equipment Standards

3.1 LED panels supplied under a Festival Video Package must be rated IP65 or better for outdoor festival use. They must be supplied with ground support or flown rigging certified for the expected wind loads at the Venue.

3.2 Provider shall carry spare panels equal to at least 5% of the deployed wall area for each Festival Video Package.

3.3 Flown or ground-supported LED structures are subject to Section 6 of the Agreement and the production quality and safety agreement, including the requirements for rigging plans and wind management.

## 4. Fees and Invoicing

4.1 Fees for a Festival Video Package are Fees under the Agreement. They count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment under Section 4.3 of the Agreement in the Contract Year in which the first show date of the Engagement occurs.

4.2 Provider shall invoice Festival Video Package Fees under Section 4.2 of the Agreement, together with the other Fees for the Engagement.

4.3 Freight for LED and video equipment is reimbursable only where the applicable SOW provides for it, as set out in Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

## 5. Weather Cancellation

5.1 If a Venue or a public authority cancels a festival show day for severe weather, and Provider's equipment was installed and show-ready, Client shall pay 50% of the equipment rates under Section 2.1 for that day and the crew rates for hours actually worked. In all other respects, Section 15 of the Agreement and the applicable SOW govern.
