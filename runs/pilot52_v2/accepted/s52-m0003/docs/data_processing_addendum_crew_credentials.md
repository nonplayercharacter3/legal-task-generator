# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Crew Credentialing and Access Data**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into as of June 3, 2024 by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. PURPOSE AND RELATIONSHIP TO THE AGREEMENT

1.1 **Purpose.** Provider operates a crew accreditation process for Client's Engagements. Under that process, Provider collects identity and certification information from crew members, prepares credential and access lists, and sends those lists to Client's production office and to Venues. This Addendum sets out the terms on which Provider processes personal information for that purpose.

1.2 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Addendum is a data processing addendum under Section 2.2 of the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement.

1.3 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Addendum:

"**Credential Data**" means personal information that Provider processes on behalf of Client under this Addendum, as described in Schedule 1.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws that apply to the processing of Credential Data. These include the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act and its regulations, other U.S. state consumer privacy and data breach notification laws, and, for show dates in Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act and applicable provincial privacy laws.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable individual to whom Credential Data relates.

"**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Credential Data.

"**Subprocessor**" means any third party that Provider engages to process Credential Data.

References to Sections are to sections of this Addendum unless stated otherwise.

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

2.1 **Roles.** For Credential Data, Client is the business or controller and Provider is the service provider or processor, as those terms are used in the Data Protection Laws.

2.2 **Instructions.** Provider shall process Credential Data only on Client's documented instructions. The Agreement, the applicable SOW, this Addendum and Client's credential policies made available to Provider under Section 5.5 of the Agreement are Client's instructions. Provider shall promptly tell Client if, in its opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

2.3 **Restrictions.** Provider shall not:

(a) sell or share Credential Data, as those terms are defined in the Data Protection Laws;

(b) retain, use or disclose Credential Data for any purpose other than performing the Services and the accreditation process described in Schedule 1;

(c) retain, use or disclose Credential Data outside the direct business relationship between the Parties; or

(d) combine Credential Data with personal information it receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as Data Protection Laws permit for a service provider.

2.4 **Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with the Data Protection Laws that apply to it in connection with Credential Data. Client is responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions. Provider is responsible for giving any notice at collection that Data Protection Laws require when it collects Credential Data from crew members. That notice must be in a form Client has approved.

## 3. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Confidentiality.** Credential Data is Confidential Information of Client under Section 8 of the Agreement. Provider shall limit access to Credential Data to its employees and contractors who need that access to carry out the accreditation process and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

3.2 **Security.** Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Credential Data. Those measures must, at a minimum, meet the requirements in Schedule 2. Provider may update those measures from time to time, but it shall not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

3.3 **Data Minimization.** Provider shall collect only the Credential Data listed in Schedule 1. It shall not collect government identification numbers, health information or biometric information unless a Venue or public authority requires it for an Engagement and Client has approved the collection in writing.

3.4 **Disclosure to Venues.** Provider may disclose Credential Data to a Venue, a Venue's security contractor or a public authority only to the extent needed to issue credentials, control access or meet a legal requirement for that Engagement. Provider shall send credential lists only through the secure transfer methods described in Schedule 2.

3.5 **Data Subject Requests.** Provider shall notify Client within five business days after it receives any request from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws in relation to Credential Data. Provider shall not respond to the request except on Client's instructions. Taking into account the nature of the processing, Provider shall give Client reasonable assistance in responding to those requests.

3.6 **Assistance.** Provider shall give Client reasonable information and assistance to help Client meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws for Credential Data. This includes any risk assessment that Data Protection Laws require.

## 4. SUBPROCESSORS

4.1 **Authorized Subprocessors.** Client authorizes Provider to use the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3.

4.2 **New Subprocessors.** Provider shall give Client written notice at least 30 days before engaging a new Subprocessor. If Client objects on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days after receiving that notice, the Parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it, Provider shall not use the new Subprocessor for Credential Data.

4.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Provider shall bind each Subprocessor by a written contract that contains data protection obligations at least as protective as those in this Addendum. Provider remains responsible to Client for each Subprocessor's processing of Credential Data.

## 5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

5.1 **Notice.** Provider shall notify Client's General Counsel and production office without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

5.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures Provider has taken or proposes to take. Provider shall supplement the notice as more information becomes available.

5.3 **Response.** Provider shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remedy each Security Incident. It shall cooperate with Client in any notice to Data Subjects, regulators or Venues. Provider shall not notify any third party of a Security Incident involving Credential Data without Client's prior approval, unless the law requires it to do so.

5.4 **Costs.** Where a Security Incident results from Provider's breach of this Addendum, Provider shall reimburse Client's reasonable costs of notices to Data Subjects and regulators and of credit monitoring that Data Protection Laws require or that Client reasonably offers.

## 6. CROSS-BORDER TRANSFERS

6.1 **Location.** Provider shall store Credential Data in the United States or Canada.

6.2 **Canadian Show Dates.** Credential Data collected for show dates in Canada may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Provider shall protect that data as this Addendum requires, and Client shall give any notice of that transfer that applicable Canadian law requires.

## 7. AUDIT

7.1 **Information.** On Client's written request, and no more than once in any Contract Year, Provider shall give Client a written response to a reasonable security questionnaire and copies of any current third-party security assessment reports covering the systems used for Credential Data.

7.2 **Audit.** If the information in Section 7.1 does not reasonably show compliance with this Addendum, or after a Security Incident, Client may audit Provider's compliance with this Addendum on 15 days' notice. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours, in a manner that does not interfere with load-in, rehearsals or shows. Client shall bear its own audit costs.

## 8. RETENTION, RETURN AND DELETION

8.1 **Retention.** Provider shall delete Credential Data for each Engagement within 90 days after that Engagement's final load-out. However, Provider may keep certification records for its own employees as long as Section 3.2 of the production quality and safety agreement between the Parties requires.

8.2 **End of Term.** On expiration or termination of the Agreement, and subject to Section 12.3 of the Agreement, Provider shall return or delete all remaining Credential Data at Client's election, in line with Section 13.4 of the Agreement. On request, Provider shall certify the deletion in writing.

8.3 **Legal Holds.** Provider may keep Credential Data that the law requires it to keep, or that is subject to a preservation obligation under Section 8.4 of the production quality and safety agreement. It shall protect that data under this Addendum for as long as it keeps it.

## 9. LIABILITY

9.1 **Agreement Terms Apply.** Each Party's liability arising out of this Addendum is subject to Section 14 of the Agreement.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Term.** This Addendum remains in effect for as long as Provider processes Credential Data.

10.2 **Changes in Law.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires changes to this Addendum, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith and sign a written amendment under Section 18.3 of the Agreement.

10.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Addendum shall be given under Section 18.2 of the Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents under Section 5.1 may also be given by email and telephone to Client's General Counsel.

10.4 **Governing Law.** This Addendum is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Section 17 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of this Addendum.

10.5 **Counterparts.** This Addendum may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.

## SCHEDULE 1: DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Data Subjects | Provider's touring crew; local stagehands, riggers and technicians arranged under Section 3.5 of the Agreement; Client production staff; artist production staff whose credentials Provider handles at Client's request |
| Categories of Credential Data | Full name; employer or engaging company; role; mobile telephone number; work email; photograph for credential badges; emergency contact name and telephone number; certification type, number and expiry date; credential level and access zones; Venue sign-in and sign-out times |
| Purpose | Issuing credentials and access lists for each Engagement; checking that crew hold required certifications; Venue access control; incident and emergency response |
| Duration | For each Engagement, from the start of crew accreditation until deletion under Section 8.1 |
| Frequency | Continuous during each Engagement |

## SCHEDULE 2: MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

1. Credential Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
2. Access to the accreditation system uses unique user accounts and multi-factor authentication, and access is based on role.
3. Credential lists are sent to Venues only through the accreditation portal or by encrypted, password-protected files. Passwords are sent separately.
4. Printed credential lists are kept under the control of the crew chief or production office and are shredded after the show date.
5. Access to the accreditation system is logged, and logs are kept for at least 12 months.
6. User access is removed within two business days after a user no longer needs it.
7. Staff with access to the accreditation system receive annual privacy and security training.
8. Backups are encrypted, and the restoration process is tested at least annually.
