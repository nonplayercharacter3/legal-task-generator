# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Diril Live Presents LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Client promotes and produces concerts, tours and festival events in North America.

B. Provider supplies staging, rigging, lighting and production crew services for live entertainment events.

C. Client wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to provide those services under Statements of Work issued under this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with a Party.

"**Change of Control**" means, for Client, any of the following: (a) the acquisition by any person or group of more than 50% of the voting equity of Client; (b) the acquisition by any person or group of more than 50% of the voting equity of any entity that directly or indirectly Controls Client; (c) a merger, consolidation or reorganization of Client or of any entity that directly or indirectly Controls Client, after which the holders of its voting equity immediately before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting equity of the surviving entity; or (d) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of Client.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on March 1 during the Term. The first Contract Year begins on the Effective Date.

"**Control**" means the direct or indirect power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise. "**Controls**" and "**Controlled**" have corresponding meanings.

"**Deliverables**" means all designs, plots, drawings, rigging plans, cue files and other materials that Provider prepares specifically for Client under a Statement of Work.

"**Engagement**" means a tour, festival date, single event or other production for which Provider provides Services under a Statement of Work.

"**Fees**" means the amounts payable by Client for the Services as set out in a Statement of Work.

"**Minimum Annual Commitment**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Minimum Commitment Shortfall**" means, as of any date, the sum, for the Contract Year in progress and each remaining Contract Year in the then-current Term, of the amount by which the Minimum Annual Commitment for that Contract Year exceeds the Fees invoiced to Client for that Contract Year through that date.

"**Provider Personnel**" means individuals employed by Provider who perform Services.

"**Services**" means the staging, rigging, flying, lighting, video, pyrotechnic support, load-in, load-out and production crew services described in a Statement of Work.

"**Service Level Schedule**" means the schedule of service levels and service credits attached to or issued under this Agreement.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work that refers to this Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

"**Venue**" means any arena, stadium, amphitheater, festival site or other location at which Services are performed.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 **Statements of Work.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work. Each SOW shall describe the Engagement, the Services, the schedule of dates and Venues, the crew complement, the equipment to be supplied and the Fees. Each SOW is governed by and forms part of this Agreement.

2.2 **Schedules and Addenda.** The Service Level Schedule, the production quality and safety agreement, any data processing addendum and any other schedule or addendum the Parties sign that refers to this Agreement form part of this Agreement.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: MSA, then Amendment No. 1, then SOWs, then schedules and addenda, then side letters. In this Section 2.3, "MSA" means the body of this Agreement and "Amendment No. 1" means the first written amendment to this Agreement signed by both Parties.

2.4 **No Exclusivity.** Except as a SOW expressly provides, nothing in this Agreement obliges Client to engage Provider exclusively for any Engagement. Client remains subject to Section 4.3.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Performance Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in line with generally accepted standards of the live entertainment production industry, and in compliance with applicable law, Venue rules and the requirements of each SOW.

3.2 **Equipment.** Provider shall supply, transport, maintain and insure all staging, truss, motors, rigging hardware, lighting fixtures, control systems and other equipment that a SOW lists as Provider equipment. Provider shall keep all such equipment in safe working condition and current with manufacturer inspection and certification requirements.

3.3 **Production Schedule.** Provider shall meet the load-in, rehearsal, show-ready and load-out times stated in each SOW or in the production schedule Client issues for the Engagement. Provider shall notify Client's production manager promptly of any circumstance likely to cause a delay.

3.4 **Change Orders.** Either Party may request changes to the scope of a SOW. A change binds the Parties only when set out in a written change order signed by both Parties that states any adjustment to the Fees and schedule.

3.5 **Local Labor.** Where a SOW so provides, Provider may arrange for local stagehands, riggers and other labor at a Venue to support the Services. Provider shall coordinate any such labor with the Venue and with any applicable collective bargaining agreement.

3.6 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the service levels in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits for failures are calculated and applied as set out in the Service Level Schedule.

## 4. FEES, INVOICING AND MINIMUM COMMITMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay the Fees set out in each SOW. Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Client shall pay where applicable. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Unless a SOW states otherwise, Provider shall invoice deposits as stated in the SOW and the balance of the Fees for each Engagement after completion of the final show date of that Engagement. Client shall pay undisputed amounts within 30 days after receipt of a correct invoice. Client may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Provider in writing of the dispute and the reasons within 15 days after receipt of the invoice. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

4.3 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** Client commits to purchase Services under SOWs with aggregate Fees of not less than $4,200,000 per Contract Year (the "**Minimum Annual Commitment**"). Fees for an Engagement count toward the Contract Year in which the first show date of that Engagement occurs.

4.4 **Annual True-Up.** Within 45 days after the end of each Contract Year, Provider may invoice Client for the amount by which the Minimum Annual Commitment for that Contract Year exceeds the Fees invoiced for that Contract Year. The commitment is reduced by the Fees for any Engagement that Provider fails to perform for reasons other than Client's breach or a Force Majeure Event affecting Client. Client shall pay the true-up invoice under Section 4.2.

4.5 **Expenses.** Client shall reimburse travel, lodging, per diem and freight expenses only to the extent a SOW provides for them, at cost and without mark-up, against reasonable supporting documentation.

4.6 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month or, if lower, the maximum rate allowed by law.

4.7 **Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep accurate records supporting its invoices for three years after the relevant invoice date. Client may audit those records on 15 days' notice, no more than once per Contract Year.

## 5. PERSONNEL

5.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign Provider Personnel who are suitably trained, experienced and, where required by law or Venue rules, licensed or certified for the tasks they perform. This includes certification for rigging, fall protection, aerial lift operation and pyrotechnic handling, as applicable.

5.2 **Key Personnel.** Each SOW may name a production lead, head rigger, lighting director or other key personnel. Provider shall not replace named key personnel during an Engagement without Client's prior consent, which Client shall not unreasonably withhold, except for illness, injury, resignation or termination of employment.

5.3 **Removal.** Client may require Provider to remove from an Engagement any individual whose conduct is unsafe, unlawful or materially disruptive. Provider shall supply a qualified replacement promptly.

5.4 **Employment Matters.** Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, payroll taxes, workers' compensation and other employment obligations for Provider Personnel.

5.5 **Conduct.** Provider shall ensure that Provider Personnel comply with Client's artist and backstage conduct policies, credential policies and Venue rules made available to Provider.

## 6. HEALTH AND SAFETY

6.1 **Safety Obligations.** Provider shall perform the Services in compliance with applicable occupational safety law, ANSI E1 entertainment technology standards where applicable, Venue rigging requirements and the production quality and safety agreement between the Parties.

6.2 **Rigging Plans.** For each Engagement involving flown elements, Provider shall deliver load calculations and rigging plots to Client and the Venue within the time the SOW requires. Where the Venue or applicable law requires it, those plans must be reviewed by a qualified engineer.

6.3 **Incidents.** Provider shall report to Client's production manager any accident, injury, near miss or equipment failure during an Engagement immediately and in any event within 24 hours. Provider shall cooperate with any investigation.

6.4 **Stop Work.** Either Party may suspend any activity it reasonably considers unsafe. A suspension for genuine safety reasons is not a breach by the suspending Party.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its materials, know-how and intellectual property that exist before the Effective Date or are developed independently of this Agreement.

7.2 **Deliverables.** Upon payment in full of the Fees for the relevant Engagement, Provider assigns to Client all right, title and interest in the Deliverables, excluding Provider's pre-existing materials. Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Provider pre-existing materials incorporated in the Deliverables, solely in connection with the Deliverables.

7.3 **Artist Materials.** All artist names, likenesses, logos, show content and creative designs supplied by Client or its artists remain the property of Client or the relevant artist. Provider shall use them only to perform the Services.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential. It includes tour routing, unannounced dates, artist information, pricing and the terms of this Agreement.

8.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care. It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and disclose it only to its employees, advisors and Affiliates who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

8.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or (d) is independently developed.

8.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** A receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order. Where lawful, it shall give prompt notice so the disclosing Party may seek protective treatment.

8.5 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 8 continue for three years after the end of the Term.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has authority to enter into this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any other agreement binding on it.

9.2 **Provider.** Provider warrants that (a) the Services will conform to the applicable SOW, (b) all equipment Provider supplies will be fit for its intended use and properly maintained, inspected and certified, and (c) it holds and will maintain all licenses and permits required to perform the Services.

9.3 **Remedy.** If Services fail to conform to Section 9.2, Provider shall re-perform or correct them promptly at no charge. Where correction is not possible in time for the affected show, Provider shall credit the Fees attributable to the non-conforming Services. This remedy is in addition to any service credits and to Client's rights under Section 10.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client, its Affiliates and their respective officers, members, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property, to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or Provider Personnel.

10.2 **Intellectual Property.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client against third-party claims that the Deliverables or Provider's equipment or materials, as supplied by Provider, infringe any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret.

10.3 **By Client.** Client shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property, to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Client or its employees, or from artist or show content supplied by Client.

10.4 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, give the indemnifying Party control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 11. INSURANCE

11.1 **Provider Coverage.** During the Term, Provider shall maintain at its own cost, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate;

(b) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $10,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation as required by law and employer's liability of at least $1,000,000;

(d) commercial automobile liability of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit; and

(e) inland marine or equipment floater coverage for the full replacement value of Provider equipment.

11.2 **Certificates.** Provider shall name Client and any Venue that requires it as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 11.1(a), (b) and (d). Provider shall provide certificates of insurance before the first show date of each Engagement.

## 12. TERM

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date, March 1, 2024, and continues until February 29, 2028 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13 or Section 16.3.

12.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive three-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). A renewal does not occur if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal delivered not more than 120 days nor fewer than 90 days before the end of the then-current term. A notice delivered outside that period is of no effect. The terms of this Agreement, including the Minimum Annual Commitment under Section 4.3, apply to each Contract Year of each Renewal Term.

12.3 **SOWs in Progress.** Unless a SOW is terminated with this Agreement, any SOW in effect when this Agreement expires remains governed by this Agreement until the SOW is completed.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. For payment breaches the cure period is 10 days.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

13.3 **Engagement Cancellation.** Client may cancel any Engagement, in whole or in part, on written notice. Cancellation charges are payable as set out in the applicable SOW. Cancellation of an Engagement does not reduce the Minimum Annual Commitment.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Client shall pay Provider for Services performed through the effective date, and each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information. Sections 4.4, 4.7, 7, 8, 10, 14, 16.3, 17 and 18, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

14.1 **Exclusion.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

14.2 **Cap.** Each Party's total liability arising out of this Agreement in any Contract Year shall not exceed the greater of (a) the Fees paid and payable in that Contract Year and (b) $4,200,000.

14.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 14.1 and 14.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10, (b) breach of Section 8, (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, (d) Client's obligation to pay Fees, true-up amounts under Section 4.4 or the Minimum Commitment Shortfall under Section 16.3.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 **Excused Performance.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, severe weather, fire, flood, epidemic, government order, war, terrorism, civil disturbance or Venue closure (each, a "**Force Majeure Event**"). This does not apply to payment obligations for Services already performed.

15.2 **Notice and Mitigation.** The affected Party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate. If a Force Majeure Event causes cancellation of a show, the Parties shall allocate costs as set out in the applicable SOW.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

16.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall not assign or transfer this Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, without Client's prior written consent.

16.2 **By Client.** Client shall not assign or transfer this Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without Provider's prior written consent. Any Change of Control of Client is deemed an assignment for purposes of this Section 16.2. Client may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate that is not the subject of a Change of Control, provided that Client gives Provider written notice of the assignment.

16.3 **Remedy for Unconsented Assignment.** Any purported assignment in breach of Section 16.1 or Section 16.2 is void. If Client assigns or is deemed to have assigned this Agreement without Provider's prior written consent, Provider may terminate this Agreement by written notice given within 90 days after Provider learns of the assignment. Upon such termination, Client shall pay Provider, within 30 days after the termination date, (a) all Fees for Services performed through the termination date and (b) the Minimum Commitment Shortfall calculated as of the termination date for the remainder of the then-current term.

16.4 **Successors.** Subject to this Section 16, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 17. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

17.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

17.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than for injunctive relief, the Parties shall refer any dispute to their senior executives. Those executives shall meet within 15 days after either Party's written request.

17.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

18.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses stated on the signature page or another address a Party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt.

18.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

18.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

18.5 **Publicity.** Neither Party shall use the other's name or marks in publicity without prior written consent. Provider may not disclose artist or tour information before the official announcement.

18.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which form one instrument.
