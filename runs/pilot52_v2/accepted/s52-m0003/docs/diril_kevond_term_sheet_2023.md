# TERM SHEET

**Proposed Master Services Agreement: Staging, Rigging, Lighting and Production Crew Services**

**Date:** November 14, 2023

**Between:** Diril Live Presents LLC ("**Diril**") and Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc. ("**Kevond**")

This term sheet sets out the principal terms the parties have agreed for a master services agreement under which Kevond will provide production services to Diril (the "**MSA**"). Except as stated in Section 12, this term sheet is not binding. The parties will be bound only when the MSA is signed by both of them.

## 1. Parties

1.1 **Client:** Diril Live Presents LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, a wholly owned subsidiary of Diril Entertainment Holdings, Inc.

1.2 **Provider:** Kevond Staging & Production Services, Inc., a New York corporation.

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 Kevond will provide staging, rigging, flying, lighting, video, pyrotechnic support, load-in, load-out and production crew services for Diril's tours, festival dates and single events in North America.

2.2 Each tour, festival or event will be documented in a statement of work ("**SOW**") under the MSA. Each SOW will set out the schedule of dates and venues, the crew complement, the equipment supplied by Kevond and the fees.

2.3 Where an SOW provides for it, Kevond may arrange local labor at a venue. Kevond will coordinate that labor with the venue and with any applicable collective bargaining agreement.

## 3. Pricing and Payment

3.1 Fees will be set per SOW, based on Kevond's 2024 rate card as negotiated for each engagement.

3.2 Deposits will be invoiced as each SOW provides. The balance will be invoiced after the final show date of each engagement. Payment is due 30 days after receipt of a correct invoice. Diril may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith on written notice.

3.3 Travel, lodging, per diem and freight will be reimbursed at cost, without mark-up, only where an SOW provides for them.

3.4 Separate pricing for LED wall and festival video packages may be agreed by side letter.

## 4. Minimum Annual Commitment

4.1 Diril will commit to aggregate SOW fees of not less than $4,200,000 per Contract Year. A Contract Year is each twelve-month period beginning March 1.

4.2 Fees for an engagement count toward the Contract Year in which its first show date occurs.

4.3 Any shortfall against the commitment will be trued up after the end of each Contract Year. Engagements that Kevond fails to perform, other than because of Diril's breach or force majeure affecting Diril, will reduce the commitment by their fees.

## 5. Term and Renewal

5.1 **Initial Term:** From the MSA effective date, targeted for March 1, 2024, through February 29, 2028.

5.2 **Renewal:** After the Initial Term, the MSA may be extended by one-year renewals only by mutual written agreement. Before the end of the Initial Term or any renewal period, the parties will meet to discuss renewal pricing, the minimum commitment and the scope of services.

5.3 **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days after written notice (10 days for non-payment), or on the other party's insolvency.

## 6. Personnel and Safety

6.1 Kevond will assign trained, experienced and properly certified crew, including for rigging, fall protection, aerial lift operation and pyrotechnic handling.

6.2 Each SOW may name key personnel, such as the production lead, head rigger and lighting director. Kevond will not replace named key personnel mid-engagement without Diril's consent, which Diril will not unreasonably withhold.

6.3 The parties will sign a separate production quality and safety agreement covering safety standards, rigging plans, engineering review and incident reporting.

## 7. Service Levels

7.1 Kevond will meet service levels for show-readiness, load-in and load-out timing, and equipment availability. The service levels will be set out in a service level schedule to the MSA.

7.2 Service credits will apply for service level failures, subject to a monthly cap to be agreed in the service level schedule.

## 8. Indemnity, Insurance and Liability

8.1 Kevond will indemnify Diril against third-party bodily injury, death and property damage claims to the extent caused by negligence or willful misconduct in performing the services. Kevond will also indemnify Diril against intellectual property infringement claims arising from its deliverables and equipment.

8.2 Diril will indemnify Kevond against third-party bodily injury, death and property damage claims to the extent caused by Diril's negligence or willful misconduct, and against claims arising from artist or show content Diril supplies.

8.3 Kevond will carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate, umbrella coverage of at least $10,000,000, statutory workers' compensation, employer's liability, auto liability and equipment coverage. Diril will be named as additional insured.

8.4 There will be a mutual exclusion of consequential damages and an annual liability cap. Carve-outs will cover indemnities, confidentiality, gross negligence, fraud and willful misconduct, and payment obligations.

## 9. Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

9.1 Deliverables prepared specifically for Diril will be assigned to Diril on payment. Kevond will retain its pre-existing materials and will license them to Diril for use with the deliverables.

9.2 Customary mutual confidentiality obligations will apply. They will cover tour routing, unannounced dates, artist information and pricing, and will last for three years after the end of the term.

## 10. Assignment

10.1 Neither party may assign the MSA without the other's prior written consent. Assignment provisions will be settled in the MSA.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

11.1 The MSA will be governed by New York law. The parties will submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County, New York.

11.2 Disputes will first be escalated to senior executives before any litigation.

## 12. Binding Provisions

12.1 Only the following provisions of this term sheet are binding: this Section 12, Section 9.2 (as applied to information exchanged in negotiations), and Section 11.1 (as to governing law).

12.2 Each party will bear its own costs of negotiating and documenting the MSA.

12.3 Either party may end negotiations at any time by written notice, without liability.

## 13. Next Steps

13.1 Kevond's counsel will prepare the first draft of the MSA, with signing targeted for February 2024 and an effective date of March 1, 2024.
