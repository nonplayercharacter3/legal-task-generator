# ORDER FORM

**Polyaluminum Chloride Supply: Ostler Creek Water Treatment Plant**

This Order Form is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between **Zapeiv Chemical Corp.** ("**Supplier**") and **Felound Regional Water Authority Services, Inc.** ("**Customer**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Supply Agreement**").

## 1. GENERAL

1.1 **Incorporation.** This Order Form is one of the Supply Documents and is incorporated into the Supply Agreement under Section 17.1 of the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **References.** References in this Order Form to Sections are to Sections of this Order Form unless they refer expressly to the Supply Agreement or the Statement of Work.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** The "**Initial Term**" is five years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date.

2.2 **Renewal Terms.** At the end of the Initial Term, the Supply Agreement will renew automatically for successive five-year terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). Each Renewal Term begins on the day after the preceding term expires. The Supply Agreement continues to renew at the end of each Renewal Term unless a Party gives a non-renewal notice under Section 2.3.

2.3 **Non-Renewal Notice.** Either Party may prevent the next renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal no earlier than 120 and no later than 90 days before the then-current term expires. A non-renewal notice must be given in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Supply Agreement and is treated as given on the date of receipt. A notice is effective only if it is received no earlier than 120 days, and no later than 90 days, before the expiration of the then-current term.

2.4 **Terms During Renewal.** Each Renewal Term is on the terms of the Supply Documents as in effect at the end of the preceding term. Prices continue to be adjusted under Section 3.

## 3. PRICING

3.1 **Unit Price.** The price for the PACl Product is $1.42 per gallon, delivered.

3.2 **Included Charges.** The unit price in Section 3.1 includes freight to the Unloading Station, fuel surcharges, delivery labor, the telemetry units and monitoring services under Section 5 of the Statement of Work, maintenance of the Supplier Equipment under Section 6 of the Statement of Work, and the technical support under Section 8.4 of the Statement of Work. Supplier will not charge any additional fee for those items.

3.3 **Price Adjustment.** The unit price is fixed until the first anniversary of the Effective Date. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust the unit price once in each contract year, effective on an anniversary of the Effective Date, by giving Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. Each adjustment will be based on the percentage change, over the twelve (12) months ending three (3) months before the adjustment date, in the Producer Price Index for Industrial Inorganic Chemicals, Not Elsewhere Classified, published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. No upward adjustment may exceed three percent (3%) of the unit price in effect immediately before the adjustment. Supplier's notice will show the index values used and the calculation.

3.4 **Invoicing Basis.** Invoices will be calculated on the gallons stated on the bill of lading for each delivery under Section 4.5 of the Statement of Work.

3.5 **Estimated Volume.** Customer estimates that the Plant will use approximately 180,000 gallons of the PACl Product per contract year. This estimate is for planning only and does not obligate Customer to purchase any minimum quantity.

## 4. EXCLUSIVE SCOPE

4.1 **Exclusive Product.** The Exclusive Product under the Supply Documents is polyaluminum chloride (PACl) coagulant.

4.2 **Covered Site.** The Covered Site under the Supply Documents is the Ostler Creek Water Treatment Plant.

4.3 **Supply.** Supplier will supply the Exclusive Product to the Covered Site in accordance with the Statement of Work, and Customer will purchase its requirements of the Exclusive Product for use at the Covered Site from Supplier, subject to Sections 4.3 and 14.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 5. CONTACTS FOR THIS ORDER FORM

5.1 **Supplier.** Commercial matters under this Order Form, including price adjustment notices, should be directed to Supplier's account manager designated under Section 9.1 of the Statement of Work, with a copy to Supplier's General Counsel.

5.2 **Customer.** Commercial matters under this Order Form should be directed to Customer's Director of Procurement, with a copy to Customer's General Counsel. Formal notices must be given as required by Section 17.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 6. AMENDMENT

This Order Form may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.
