# STATEMENT OF WORK

**Polyaluminum Chloride Supply, Delivery and Tank Management Services: Ostler Creek Water Treatment Plant**

This Statement of Work (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between **Zapeiv Chemical Corp.** ("**Supplier**") and **Felound Regional Water Authority Services, Inc.** ("**Customer**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Supply Agreement**").

## 1. PURPOSE AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the Product requirements, delivery scheduling, inventory monitoring, tank management, site access and related services that Supplier will perform under Section 3.4 and Section 6.1 of the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **References.** References in this SOW to Sections are to Sections of this SOW unless they refer expressly to the Supply Agreement or the Order Form.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Product.** Supplier will supply polyaluminum chloride (PACl) coagulant (the "**PACl Product**") in accordance with this SOW.

2.2 **Covered Site.** Supplier will deliver the PACl Product and perform the services in this SOW at the Ostler Creek Water Treatment Plant, 1450 Ostler Creek Road, Delmar, New York 12054 (the "**Plant**").

2.3 **Services.** Supplier will:

(a) schedule and make bulk deliveries of the PACl Product to the Plant under Section 4;

(b) remotely monitor PACl inventory in the Plant's bulk storage tanks under Section 5;

(c) inspect and maintain Supplier-owned equipment at the Plant under Section 6;

(d) comply with the site access, safety and unloading procedures in Section 7; and

(e) provide the quality documentation and technical support described in Sections 3 and 8.

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS

3.1 **PACl Product Requirements.** Each delivery of the PACl Product must meet the following requirements: NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 certified PACl, minimum 10.0% Al2O3, 70% basicity.

3.2 **Certification Maintenance.** Supplier will keep the NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 certification for the PACl Product, and for each manufacturing location from which it ships the PACl Product to the Plant, current throughout the Term. Supplier will provide Customer with a copy of the current certification listing on the Effective Date and within ten (10) business days after each renewal.

3.3 **Certificates of Analysis.** Each certificate of analysis delivered under Section 6.4 of the Supply Agreement will state, for the applicable production lot: the lot number and manufacturing location; date of manufacture; aluminum content expressed as % Al2O3; basicity; specific gravity; pH; and the NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 certification reference.

3.4 **Change Notification.** Supplier will give Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of any change in the manufacturing location, raw material source or manufacturing process for the PACl Product.

## 4. DELIVERY

4.1 **Delivery Point.** Supplier will deliver the PACl Product by bulk tanker to the chemical unloading station at the Plant's Chemical Feed Building (the "**Unloading Station**").

4.2 **Delivery Quantities.** Each delivery will be a full bulk tanker load of approximately 4,500 gallons unless Customer specifies a different quantity in the applicable Purchase Order.

4.3 **Delivery Windows.** Supplier will make deliveries between 7:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding Customer holidays notified to Supplier in advance. Supplier will confirm each scheduled delivery with the Plant's operations supervisor at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance.

4.4 **Lead Time.** Supplier will deliver within three (3) business days after acceptance of a Purchase Order. For emergency requests that Customer designates as such, Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver within twenty-four (24) hours.

4.5 **Delivery Documentation.** Each delivery will be accompanied by a bill of lading, the certificate of analysis for the lot, and the current safety data sheet. The bill of lading will state the quantity delivered by weight and by gallons.

## 5. INVENTORY MONITORING AND REPLENISHMENT

5.1 **Storage Tanks.** The Plant has two (2) PACl bulk storage tanks, Tank PAC-1 and Tank PAC-2, each with a nominal working capacity of 8,000 gallons. The tanks are owned by Customer.

5.2 **Telemetry.** Supplier will install, at its own cost, tank-level telemetry units on Tanks PAC-1 and PAC-2 within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date. Supplier will give Customer read-only access to the telemetry portal at no charge.

5.3 **Replenishment Trigger.** Supplier will monitor tank levels each business day. When the combined inventory in the tanks falls to 6,000 gallons, Supplier will notify the Plant's operations supervisor and propose a delivery date. Customer will issue a Purchase Order for the proposed delivery or for an alternative date.

5.4 **Minimum Inventory.** Supplier will schedule deliveries so that combined PACl inventory at the Plant does not fall below 3,000 gallons, based on the most recent forecast under Section 3.3 of the Supply Agreement and the Plant's average daily feed rate.

5.5 **Reporting.** By the tenth (10th) business day of each month, Supplier will give Customer a report showing, for the prior month, deliveries made, quantities delivered, lot numbers, daily tank levels and any telemetry outages.

## 6. EQUIPMENT

6.1 **Supplier-Owned Equipment.** The telemetry units and any related communications hardware installed under Section 5.2 remain Supplier's property (the "**Supplier Equipment**"). Supplier will maintain and repair the Supplier Equipment at its own cost and will restore any failed unit within three (3) business days after it becomes aware of the failure.

6.2 **Customer-Owned Equipment.** Supplier will not modify, adjust or connect to Customer's tanks, feed pumps, piping or control systems except as Customer approves in writing.

6.3 **Removal.** On expiration or termination of the Supply Agreement, Supplier will remove the Supplier Equipment within the period stated in Section 12.3 of the Supply Agreement, at a time coordinated with the Plant's operations supervisor. Supplier will repair any damage caused by the removal.

## 7. SITE ACCESS, SAFETY AND UNLOADING

7.1 **Access.** Supplier's drivers and technicians will check in at the Plant's main gate, present identification and sign the visitor log on each visit. Customer may refuse access to any person who does not comply with this Section 7 or with Customer's site rules.

7.2 **Driver Qualifications.** Each driver delivering to the Plant will hold a valid commercial driver's license with hazardous materials endorsement and will have completed Supplier's chemical unloading training. Supplier will provide training records on request.

7.3 **Unloading Procedure.** For each delivery:

(a) a Plant operator will verify the bill of lading and certificate of analysis and confirm the receiving tank before unloading begins;

(b) the driver will connect only to the Unloading Station fill line labeled for PACl;

(c) the driver will remain at the tanker throughout unloading and will stop unloading immediately if a Plant operator so directs; and

(d) the driver and Plant operator will both sign the bill of lading after unloading is complete.

7.4 **Personal Protective Equipment.** Supplier's personnel will wear chemical-resistant gloves, splash goggles, face shields and other personal protective equipment appropriate to the PACl Product while connecting, unloading and disconnecting.

7.5 **Spills.** Supplier will equip each tanker with a spill kit suitable for the PACl Product. Supplier will immediately notify the Plant's operations supervisor of any spill or release during delivery, and will contain and clean up any spill or release caused by Supplier's personnel or equipment at Supplier's cost.

## 8. QUALITY AND ACCEPTANCE TESTING

8.1 **Sampling.** Customer may take a retained sample from each delivery, before or after unloading, in the presence of the driver.

8.2 **Acceptance Testing.** Customer's laboratory may test any retained sample for conformity to the Specifications. If testing shows that a delivery does not conform to the Specifications, Customer may reject the delivery under Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement.

8.3 **Split Samples.** At Supplier's request, Customer will provide a split of any retained sample on which Customer bases a rejection. Supplier may test the split sample at its own laboratory, but Supplier's testing does not delay Supplier's replacement obligations under Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement.

8.4 **Technical Support.** Supplier will make a qualified technical representative available by telephone during business hours to support Customer on dosage optimization, jar testing and feed system troubleshooting for the PACl Product. Supplier will provide on-site jar testing support at the Plant up to two (2) times per calendar year at no additional charge.

## 9. CONTACTS

9.1 **Supplier Contact.** Supplier will designate an account manager for the Plant and give Customer the account manager's name, telephone number and email address within five (5) business days after the Effective Date.

9.2 **Customer Contact.** The Plant's operations supervisor is Customer's day-to-day contact for delivery scheduling and site matters under this SOW. Notices under the Supply Agreement must be given as required by Section 17.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## 10. AMENDMENT

This SOW may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.
