# LABORATORY INSTRUMENT MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This Laboratory Instrument Maintenance Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Ostrevka Analytical Instruments LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 75 Commerce Drive, Latham, New York 12110 ("**Provider**"), and **Felound Regional Water Authority Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Reservoir Road, Albany, New York 12205 ("**Customer**"). Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates a central water quality laboratory that analyzes drinking-water samples to support treatment operations and regulatory compliance.

B. Provider services, calibrates and repairs laboratory analytical instruments.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to maintain certain of its laboratory instruments on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Covered Instruments**" means the laboratory instruments listed in Schedule A, as that schedule is updated under Section 2.3.

1.2 "**Laboratory**" means Customer's Central Water Quality Laboratory at 220 Reservoir Road, Albany, New York 12205.

1.3 "**Preventive Maintenance**" means the scheduled inspection, cleaning, calibration, adjustment and replacement of wear parts described in Section 3.1 and Schedule A.

1.4 "**Remedial Service**" means diagnosis and repair of a Covered Instrument that has failed or is not performing to the manufacturer's published performance specifications.

1.5 "**Services**" means the Preventive Maintenance, Remedial Service and other services Provider performs under this Agreement.

1.6 "**Service Year**" means each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 **Engagement.** Customer engages Provider, and Provider agrees, to perform the Services for the Covered Instruments at the Laboratory.

2.2 **Non-Exclusive.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. Customer may engage the instrument manufacturer or any other service provider for any instrument, including a Covered Instrument.

2.3 **Changes to Covered Instruments.** Customer may add or remove instruments by written notice to Provider. An added instrument becomes a Covered Instrument thirty (30) days after the notice, at the annual fee the Parties agree in writing. A removed instrument ceases to be a Covered Instrument at the end of the calendar month in which the notice is given, and the annual fee will be reduced pro rata.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Preventive Maintenance.** Provider will perform Preventive Maintenance on each Covered Instrument at the frequency stated in Schedule A, in accordance with the instrument manufacturer's recommended maintenance procedures. Provider will schedule each visit with the Laboratory Manager at least ten (10) business days in advance.

3.2 **Remedial Service.** Customer may request Remedial Service by telephone or email to Provider's service desk. Provider will:

(a) respond by telephone within four (4) business hours after the request;

(b) arrive on site within one (1) business day after the request for instruments designated "Priority" in Schedule A, and within three (3) business days for all other Covered Instruments; and

(c) use commercially reasonable efforts to restore the instrument to the manufacturer's performance specifications within five (5) business days after arriving on site.

3.3 **Calibration and Documentation.** After each Preventive Maintenance or Remedial Service visit, Provider will give Customer a written service report stating the instrument serial number, the work performed, parts replaced, calibration and verification results, and the standards used, with traceability to NIST where applicable. Reports will be delivered within five (5) business days after the visit.

3.4 **Parts.** Provider will use new parts that are genuine manufacturer parts or equivalents approved in writing by Customer. Parts listed as included in Schedule A are covered by the annual fee. Other parts will be invoiced at Provider's cost plus fifteen percent (15%), and Provider will obtain Customer's written approval before installing any part costing more than $1,000.

3.5 **Loaner Instruments.** If a Priority instrument cannot be restored within the period in Section 3.2(c), Provider will, where reasonably available, supply a loaner instrument of equivalent capability at no charge until the repair is complete.

## 4. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 **Access.** Customer will give Provider's technicians reasonable access to the Laboratory and the Covered Instruments during normal business hours, and outside those hours when Customer requests emergency service.

4.2 **Operation.** Customer will operate the Covered Instruments in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and will promptly notify Provider of any malfunction.

4.3 **Site Rules.** Customer will give Provider its Laboratory safety, chemical hygiene and security rules. Provider's personnel will comply with them while at the Laboratory.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Annual Fee.** Customer will pay the annual fee stated in Schedule A for the Covered Instruments, invoiced quarterly in advance in four equal installments.

5.2 **Additional Charges.** Remedial Service on Covered Instruments is included in the annual fee, except for repairs caused by Customer's misuse, accident or operation contrary to the manufacturer's instructions. Those repairs, and any services Customer requests that are outside this Agreement, will be charged at the hourly rate in Schedule A, plus parts under Section 3.4.

5.3 **Fee Adjustment.** Provider may increase the annual fee effective on any anniversary of the Effective Date by giving Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No increase may exceed three percent (3%) of the fee in effect immediately before the increase.

5.4 **Payment.** Customer will pay undisputed invoiced amounts within thirty (30) days after receiving a correct invoice. If Customer disputes any amount in good faith, it will notify Provider in writing with reasonable detail and pay the undisputed portion, and the Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales and use taxes. Customer will pay those taxes unless it provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 6. WARRANTIES

6.1 **Services Warranty.** Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner by trained technicians, consistent with the instrument manufacturer's procedures and generally accepted industry standards.

6.2 **Parts Warranty.** Provider warrants that parts it installs will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for ninety (90) days after installation.

6.3 **Remedy.** If Services or parts do not conform to Section 6.1 or Section 6.2, and Customer notifies Provider within ninety (90) days after performance or installation, Provider will promptly re-perform the nonconforming Services or replace the defective part at no charge.

6.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 **Coverage.** During the term of this Agreement, Provider will maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of $1,000,000;

(c) professional liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per claim; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance of $1,000,000.

7.2 **Certificates.** Provider will name Customer as an additional insured on the policy in Section 7.1(a), provide a certificate of insurance on the Effective Date and on each renewal, and give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **By Provider.** Provider will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its directors, officers and employees from all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of Provider's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, including bodily injury or damage to property at the Laboratory.

8.2 **By Customer.** Customer will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its members, officers and employees from all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

8.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, and delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is prejudiced. The indemnifying Party will control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party and may not settle a claim that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without that Party's prior written consent.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 **Exclusion.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 9.3, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

9.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 9.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF (a) THE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM AND (b) FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($500,000).

9.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 9.1 and 9.2 do not apply to a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, a Party's breach of Section 11, a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, or Customer's obligation to pay fees due.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for one (1) Service Year. It then renews for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Service Year.

10.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, on sixty (60) days' written notice to Provider.

10.3 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

10.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Customer will pay for Services performed through the effective date, and Provider will refund any prepaid fees for the period after that date, pro rata. Provider will deliver to Customer all service reports and calibration records for the Covered Instruments that it has not already delivered.

10.5 **Survival.** Sections 5.4, 6, 8, 9, 10.4, 11 and 13 survive expiration or termination.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

11.1 **Obligations.** Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information the other Party discloses in connection with this Agreement, use it only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, and protect it with at least reasonable care. This obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or is independently developed.

11.2 **Laboratory Data.** Provider will not access, copy or retain sample results or other data stored on any Covered Instrument or connected system, except to the extent strictly necessary to perform the Services. All such data remains Customer's property.

11.3 **Required Disclosure.** A Party may disclose confidential information as required by law, court order or public records law, and will give the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted. Provider acknowledges that Customer may be subject to the New York Freedom of Information Law.

## 12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING

12.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the other Party's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 12.1 is void.

12.2 **Subcontracting.** Provider may not subcontract any Services without Customer's prior written consent, other than to the instrument manufacturer for factory repairs. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

13.2 **Venue.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Albany County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses in the preamble (for Customer, to the attention of the General Counsel; for Provider, to the attention of its Managing Member), or to another address a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

14.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. The affected Party will promptly notify the other and resume performance as soon as practicable.

14.3 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

14.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties, except for notices given under Section 2.3. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

14.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

14.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic or PDF signature, each of which is an original.
