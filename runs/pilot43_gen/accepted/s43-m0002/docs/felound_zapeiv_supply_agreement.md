# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Zapeiv Chemical Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"), and **Felound Regional Water Authority Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Reservoir Road, Albany, New York 12205 ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and distributes chemicals used in the treatment of potable water.

B. Customer operates municipal drinking-water treatment plants and wishes to buy water treatment chemicals from Supplier on the terms of this Agreement, the Statement of Work and the Order Form.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement and in the other Supply Documents have the following meanings:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, ordinances, permits and orders that apply to a Party's performance under this Agreement. This includes the Safe Drinking Water Act and its implementing regulations, and the regulations of the New York State Department of Health.

1.3 "**Facility**" means any water treatment plant, pumping station or related installation owned or operated by Customer or any of its Affiliates.

1.4 "**Initial Term**" and "**Renewal Term**" have the meanings given in the Order Form.

1.5 "**Order Form**" means the Order Form executed by the Parties with reference to this Agreement, as amended from time to time by written agreement of the Parties.

1.6 "**Products**" means the Water Treatment Chemicals that Supplier supplies to Customer under this Agreement.

1.7 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products that Customer issues to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.8 "**Specifications**" means, for each Product, Supplier's standard product data sheets for that Product, as in effect from time to time.

1.9 "**Statement of Work**" means the Statement of Work executed by the Parties with reference to this Agreement, as amended from time to time by written agreement of the Parties.

1.10 "**Supply Documents**" means this Agreement, the Statement of Work and the Order Form.

1.11 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.12 "**Water Treatment Chemicals**" means chemicals used in the treatment, conditioning or distribution of potable water. These include coagulants and flocculants (including polyaluminum chloride, aluminum sulfate and ferric chloride), disinfectants (including sodium hypochlorite and chlorine), pH adjusters (including caustic soda and lime), and corrosion inhibitors (including orthophosphate and polyphosphate blends).

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term and any Renewal Terms stated in the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 12.

2.2 **Effect of Expiration.** Purchase Orders accepted before this Agreement expires or terminates remain subject to this Agreement until fully performed.

## 3. PURCHASE ORDERS AND FORECASTS

3.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer will order Products by issuing Purchase Orders. Each Purchase Order will state the Product, the quantity, the Facility and delivery point, and the requested delivery date. Each Purchase Order is subject to the Supply Documents. Any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other business form are void.

3.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier will accept or reject each Purchase Order within two (2) business days after receiving it. Supplier may not reject a Purchase Order that conforms to the Supply Documents and to the most recent forecast under Section 3.3. A Purchase Order that Supplier does not reject within that period is deemed accepted.

3.3 **Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar month, Customer will give Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements for the next three (3) months. Forecasts are for planning only and do not obligate Customer to purchase any quantity.

3.4 **Delivery and Tank Management.** Supplier will perform the delivery scheduling, inventory monitoring, tank management and related services described in the Statement of Work.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase one hundred percent (100%) of its requirements of all Water Treatment Chemicals for use at any Facility exclusively from Supplier, and shall not buy any Water Treatment Chemicals for use at any Facility from any third party.

4.2 **Supply Commitment.** During the Term, Supplier will maintain enough manufacturing capacity, inventory and logistics resources to meet Customer's forecasted requirements under Section 3.3, plus twenty percent (20%) of that forecast.

4.3 **Supply Shortfall.** If Supplier cannot or does not deliver conforming Products against an accepted Purchase Order by the confirmed delivery date, Customer may buy replacement products from a third party to cover the shortfall. Those purchases will not breach Section 4.1. Supplier will reimburse Customer for the amount by which the cost of the replacement products, including freight, exceeds the price that would have been payable under this Agreement.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Customer will pay for Products at the prices set out in Section 3 (Pricing) of the Order Form. Prices may be adjusted only as provided in the Order Form.

5.2 **Taxes.** Prices exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Customer will pay those taxes unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

5.3 **Invoices.** Supplier will invoice Customer after each delivery. Each invoice will reference the applicable Purchase Order, the Facility, the bill of lading and the quantity delivered.

5.4 **Payment.** Customer will pay undisputed invoiced amounts within thirty (30) days after receiving a correct invoice. If Customer disputes any amount in good faith, it will notify Supplier in writing with reasonable detail, and will pay the undisputed portion. The Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law.

## 6. DELIVERY, INSPECTION AND REJECTION

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier will deliver Products in bulk tanker or other approved containers to the delivery point stated in the applicable Purchase Order at the designated Facility, on the confirmed delivery date. Supplier will comply with the site access, safety and unloading procedures in the Statement of Work and with any reasonable site rules Customer provides.

6.2 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer when the Products are fully discharged into Customer's storage tank at the delivery point.

6.3 **Inspection and Rejection.** Customer may inspect, sample and test each delivery before or after unloading. Customer may reject any delivery, in whole or in part, that does not conform to the Specifications. To do so, Customer must give Supplier written notice within ten (10) business days after delivery or, for nonconformities that reasonable inspection would not reveal, within ten (10) business days after discovering the nonconformity. For rejected Products, Supplier will, at Customer's option and at Supplier's expense, either (a) remove the rejected Products and replace them with conforming Products within forty-eight (48) hours, or (b) remove the rejected Products and credit or refund their price. Supplier will also reimburse Customer for reasonable costs incurred in handling, segregating, removing or disposing of the rejected Products.

6.4 **Certificates of Analysis.** Each delivery will be accompanied by a certificate of analysis issued by Supplier's quality laboratory for the applicable production lot.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product delivered under this Agreement will, at delivery and for the shelf life stated on its certificate of analysis: (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be free from contamination and adulteration; (c) be manufactured, packaged, labeled and shipped in compliance with Applicable Law; and (d) be delivered with good title, free of all liens and encumbrances.

7.2 **Remedies.** If any Product breaches the warranty in Section 7.1, Supplier will, at Customer's option, promptly replace the nonconforming Product or credit or refund its price. Supplier will also reimburse Customer for reasonable costs of removal, disposal and system remediation caused by the nonconforming Product. These remedies are in addition to Customer's rights under Section 6.3 and Section 9.

7.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform the Supply Documents; and (c) its execution and performance of the Supply Documents does not violate any agreement or obligation binding on it.

7.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND SAFETY

8.1 **Compliance.** Each Party will comply with Applicable Law in performing its obligations under the Supply Documents.

8.2 **Safety Data Sheets.** Supplier will give Customer current safety data sheets for each Product before the first delivery, and will promptly provide an updated sheet whenever one is revised.

8.3 **Regulatory Cooperation.** Supplier will provide documentation, lot records and other information reasonably requested by Customer to support Customer's reporting to, and inspections by, regulators with jurisdiction over any Facility.

8.4 **Notice of Regulatory Events.** Supplier will notify Customer in writing within twenty-four (24) hours after it becomes aware of any recall, regulatory action, contamination event or other circumstance that could affect the safety, quality or regulatory status of Products delivered or to be delivered to Customer.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates, and their directors, officers, employees and agents from all third-party claims, losses, damages, penalties, fines, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of: (a) any breach of Section 7.1; (b) Supplier's negligence or willful misconduct, including during delivery and unloading at any Facility; or (c) Supplier's violation of Applicable Law.

9.2 **By Customer.** Customer will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates, and their directors, officers, employees and agents from all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of Customer's negligence or willful misconduct in storing, handling or using Products after title passes.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim. Any delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay prejudices it. The indemnifying Party will control the defense and settlement of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. It may not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without that Party's prior written consent. The indemnified Party will reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense and may join the defense with its own counsel at its own expense.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 10.3, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

10.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 10.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF (a) THE AMOUNTS PAID AND PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM AND (b) TWO MILLION DOLLARS ($2,000,000).

10.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 10.1 and 10.2 do not apply to: (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 9; (b) a Party's breach of Section 13; (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (d) Customer's obligation to pay amounts due for Products.

## 11. INSURANCE

11.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years after it ends, Supplier will maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best, at least the following coverage:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations, with limits of $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of $5,000,000, including MCS-90 endorsement for hazardous materials transport;

(c) pollution legal liability insurance with limits of $10,000,000 per claim; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance of $1,000,000.

11.2 **Additional Insureds; Certificates.** Supplier will name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 11.1(a) through 11.1(c). It will provide certificates of insurance on the Effective Date and on each policy renewal, and will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 12. TERMINATION

12.1 **Termination for Material Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. For a failure to pay undisputed amounts, the cure period is ten (10) days.

12.2 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party: (a) becomes insolvent or admits in writing that it cannot pay its debts as they become due; (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; (c) files, or has filed against it, a petition in bankruptcy or insolvency that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or (d) has a receiver, trustee or similar officer appointed for all or a substantial part of its assets.

12.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination: (a) Customer will pay for all conforming Products delivered before the effective date of termination; (b) Supplier will, at Customer's request, deliver Products ordered under accepted Purchase Orders; and (c) Supplier will, within thirty (30) days, remove any Supplier-owned equipment from each Facility in accordance with the Statement of Work.

12.4 **Transition Assistance.** If this Agreement expires or terminates for any reason other than Customer's uncured material breach, Supplier will, at Customer's request, continue supplying Products at the then-current prices for up to ninety (90) days so that Customer can move to a replacement supplier without interrupting treatment operations.

12.5 **Survival.** Sections 5, 7, 9, 10, 12.3, 12.4, 13, 16 and 17 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, operating data, process information and the terms of the Supply Documents. It does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

13.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient will use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform or exercise its rights under this Agreement. It will disclose that information only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13. It will protect that information with at least reasonable care.

13.3 **Required Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, regulation, court order or public records law. Where legally permitted, it will give the Discloser prompt notice so that the Discloser may seek protective treatment. Supplier acknowledges that Customer may be subject to the New York Freedom of Information Law and to regulatory reporting obligations in connection with its operations.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 **Excused Performance.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These include acts of God, flood, fire, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, and failures of public utilities (each a "**Force Majeure Event**"). Labor disputes involving the affected Party's own workforce, and changes in market conditions or raw material costs, are not Force Majeure Events.

14.2 **Notice and Mitigation.** The affected Party will promptly notify the other Party of the Force Majeure Event, use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its effects, and resume performance as soon as practicable. If Supplier's performance is affected, Supplier will allocate its available supply to Customer at least pro rata with its other customers. During the Force Majeure Event, Customer may buy replacement products from third parties without breaching Section 4.1.

14.3 **Extended Force Majeure.** If a Force Majeure Event prevents Supplier from performing for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, Customer may terminate this Agreement by written notice without liability.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

15.2 **Assignment.** Customer may not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, by operation of law or otherwise, without Supplier's prior written consent. Supplier may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the consent of Customer, to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or part of its business. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.

15.3 **Subcontracting.** Supplier may use qualified carriers to transport Products. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its carriers and subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation, either Party may refer a dispute to senior executives of each Party by written notice. Those executives will meet, in person or by videoconference, within fifteen (15) business days after the notice to try to resolve the dispute. This Section 16.2 does not prevent either Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief at any time.

16.3 **Venue.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Albany County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

16.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 17. MISCELLANEOUS

17.1 **Supply Documents.** The Supply Documents are to be read together as a single agreement. The Statement of Work and the Order Form are incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

17.2 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the Supply Documents, they govern in the following order of precedence: the Supply Agreement, then the Statement of Work, then the Order Form.

17.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy sent by courier, to the addresses stated in the preamble (to the attention of the General Counsel), or to any other address a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

17.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A failure or delay in exercising any right does not operate as a waiver of that right.

17.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

17.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.
