# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Glesar Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), and **Tamsin Okoro**, an individual ("**Employee**"). The Company and Employee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Company is an independent video game developer. It owns and operates the "Emberfall" action role-playing game franchise.

B. The Company wishes to employ Employee as its Studio Creative Director. Employee wishes to accept that employment on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

C. Before the Effective Date, Employee independently developed a proprietary engine toolkit known as "Wickline." The Company wishes to use Wickline in developing the Emberfall franchise, and Employee is willing to license Wickline to the Company on the terms of Section 7.2.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, Employee's employment by the Company, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Cause**" means any of the following by Employee:
   (i) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day opportunity to cure;
   (ii) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony;
   (iii) an act of fraud, embezzlement or material dishonesty against the Company; or
   (iv) material breach of Section 6, 7 or 8 that, if curable, is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

(b) "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the business, products, technology, finances, players, personnel and plans of the Company. This includes unreleased game designs, source code, build pipelines, lore, art, audio and marketing plans. Confidential Information does not include information that becomes publicly available other than through Employee's breach of this Agreement.

(c) "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, in each case without Employee's written consent:
   (i) a material reduction in Employee's Base Salary;
   (ii) a material diminution in Employee's title, authority or creative responsibilities for the Emberfall franchise; or
   (iii) relocation of Employee's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles.

   Good Reason exists only if all of the following occur:
   (A) Employee gives the Company written notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs;
   (B) the Company fails to cure the condition within thirty (30) days after that notice; and
   (C) Employee resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

(d) "**Wickline Toolkit**" means the engine toolkit known as "Wickline" described in Exhibit A. It includes all source code, object code, editors, shaders, scripting interfaces, plug-ins and documentation comprising it as of the Effective Date. It also includes all updates, versions and improvements to it that Employee makes outside the scope of her employment.

(e) "**Work Product**" means all inventions, works of authorship, software, designs, characters, lore, artwork, audio, documentation, know-how and other materials, and all Intellectual Property in any of them, that Employee conceives, creates, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment with the Company. This applies only to materials that (i) relate to the business of the Company or its actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, or (ii) result from work performed by Employee for the Company or with the Company's equipment, facilities or Confidential Information.

(f) "**Intellectual Property**" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of every kind, anywhere in the world. This includes copyrights, trademarks, patents, trade secrets, software, rights in data and moral rights (to the extent waivable).

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; POSITION; DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs Employee as its Studio Creative Director, reporting to the Company's Managing Member.

**2.2 Duties.** Employee shall have overall creative responsibility for the Emberfall franchise. This includes game direction, narrative, art direction and audio direction. Employee shall also perform such other duties, consistent with her position, as the Managing Member reasonably assigns.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** Employee shall devote her full business time and attention to the business of the Company. Employee may manage her personal investments and engage in civic, charitable and educational activities. Employee may also maintain and improve the Wickline Toolkit on her own time, provided these activities do not materially interfere with her duties or breach Section 6, 7 or 8.

**2.4 Place of Work.** Employee's principal place of work is the Company's studio in Brooklyn, New York, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** Employee's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues until terminated by either Party under Section 5 (the "**Term**").

**3.2 At-Will Employment.** Employee's employment is at will. Either Party may terminate it at any time, subject to the notice and payment provisions of Section 5.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary of $285,000 (the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Managing Member shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase it, but not decrease it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of thirty percent (30%) of the Base Salary. The bonus is based on milestones that the Managing Member and Employee agree in writing for each calendar year. Any bonus earned for a calendar year shall be paid no later than March 15 of the following year.

**4.3 Benefits.** Employee may participate in all employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to its senior employees generally, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.4 Paid Time Off.** Employee is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to Company holidays.

**4.5 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with the Company's expense policies.

**4.6 No Other Compensation.** The compensation and benefits described in this Section 4 are the only compensation payable to Employee for her services and for the rights granted in Section 7. Employee is not entitled to any royalty, revenue share or other payment based on the sale or exploitation of any Company product, including any Emberfall title.

## 5. TERMINATION

**5.1 Termination by the Company.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment:

(a) for Cause, effective immediately upon written notice; or

(b) without Cause, upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

**5.2 Termination by Employee.** Employee may terminate her employment:

(a) for Good Reason, in accordance with Section 1.1(c); or

(b) without Good Reason, upon sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period, in which case it shall pay Employee's Base Salary for the waived portion.

**5.3 Death or Disability.** Employee's employment terminates automatically upon her death. The Company may terminate Employee's employment upon written notice if Employee is unable to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve (12) month period.

**5.4 Accrued Obligations.** Upon any termination, the Company shall pay Employee:
(a) her earned but unpaid Base Salary;
(b) any earned but unpaid bonus for a completed calendar year;
(c) accrued but unused paid time off; and
(d) unreimbursed business expenses.

**5.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall pay Employee, in addition to the amounts in Section 5.4:
(a) continued Base Salary for twelve (12) months following the termination date; and
(b) the Company's share of COBRA premiums for the same period.

These severance payments are conditioned on Employee executing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company within sixty (60) days after the termination date.

**5.6 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment under Section 5.5 is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Non-Disclosure.** During and after her employment, Employee shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence. Employee shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information except in performing her duties for the Company or as required by Law. If disclosure is required by Law, Employee shall give the Company prompt written notice so that it may seek a protective order.

**6.2 Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from reporting possible violations of Law to any governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower provisions of applicable Law. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, Employee shall not be held liable under any trade secret Law for a disclosure made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of Law.

**6.3 Return of Property.** Upon termination of her employment, or earlier at the Company's request, Employee shall return to the Company all Company property and all materials containing Confidential Information. This includes development kits, devices, source code and access credentials. This Section 6.3 does not require Employee to return or delete her own copies of the Wickline Toolkit.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 Assignment of Work Product.** All Work Product is the sole and exclusive property of the Company. To the extent permitted by Law, all copyrightable Work Product is "work made for hire" under the U.S. Copyright Act. To the extent any Work Product is not work made for hire, Employee hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in and to that Work Product. This assignment does not cover any invention that qualifies for exclusion under Section 203-f of the New York Labor Law.

**7.2 Prior Inventions; Wickline License.**

(a) *Retained Ownership.* The Wickline Toolkit is a prior invention of Employee and is not Work Product. As between the Parties, Employee retains all right, title and interest in and to the Wickline Toolkit.

(b) *License.* Employee hereby grants to the Company a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable (except as provided in Section 12.4), royalty-free, fully paid-up license to use, reproduce, modify, create derivative works of, compile and incorporate the Wickline Toolkit. Under this license, the Company may also distribute the Wickline Toolkit in object-code form as embodied in the Company's games, in each case solely in connection with the development, publication, distribution, operation and exploitation of the Emberfall franchise and any sequels, expansions, ports or remasters of it.

(c) *Sublicensing.* The Company may sublicense the rights in Section 7.2(b) to its contractors, porting partners, platform holders and distributors, solely to the extent necessary for them to perform services for, or distribute the games of, the Company.

(d) *Modifications.* The Company owns all modifications to the Wickline Toolkit that are made by or for the Company within the scope of Employee's employment (the "**Company Modifications**"), subject to Employee's underlying rights in the Wickline Toolkit.

(e) *Transfer.* The license in Section 7.2(b) may be transferred only in accordance with Section 12.4.

**7.3 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by Law, Employee irrevocably waives, and agrees not to assert against the Company or its licensees, any moral rights she may have in any Work Product. The Company shall use reasonable efforts to credit Employee as Creative Director in the credits of each Emberfall title for which she serves in that role.

**7.4 Disclosure and Cooperation.** Employee shall promptly disclose all Work Product to the Company. During and after her employment, at the Company's request and expense, Employee shall execute all instruments and take all actions reasonably necessary to perfect, register and enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product.

**7.5 Power of Attorney.** If the Company is unable, after reasonable effort, to obtain Employee's signature on any instrument described in Section 7.4, Employee irrevocably appoints the Company as her attorney-in-fact solely to execute and file that instrument with respect to Work Product.

## 8. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**8.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During her employment and for twelve (12) months afterward, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any employee of the Company with whom she worked to leave the Company's employ. General solicitations not targeted at Company employees do not violate this Section 8.1.

**8.2 Non-Solicitation of Business Partners.** During her employment and for twelve (12) months afterward, Employee shall not use Confidential Information to solicit any publisher, platform holder or development partner of the Company to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

**8.3 Reasonableness.** Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 8 are reasonable in scope and duration and are necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests.

## 9. REPRESENTATIONS OF EMPLOYEE

Employee represents that:

(a) she is not bound by any agreement that would prevent or restrict her from performing her duties under this Agreement;

(b) she is the sole author and owner of the Wickline Toolkit and has the right to grant the license in Section 7.2; and

(c) she will not bring to the Company, or use in performing her duties, any confidential information or proprietary materials of any former employer or other third party.

## 10. REMEDIES

**10.1 Injunctive Relief.** Employee acknowledges that a breach of Section 6, 7 or 8 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is therefore entitled to seek injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

**10.2 Survival.** Sections 5.4 through 5.6 and Sections 6 through 12 survive the termination of Employee's employment.

## 11. WITHHOLDING

The Company may withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement all federal, state, local and foreign taxes that it is required to withhold under applicable Law.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**12.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) business day after it is sent by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the following addresses (or to such other address as a Party designates by notice):

If to the Company: Glesar Studios LLC, Attention: Managing Member, notices@glesar.example.

If to Employee: at the most recent home address and personal email address on file with the Company.

**12.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**12.3 Successors.** Subject to Section 12.4, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective heirs, legal representatives, successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Employee, and Employee may not assign it. The Company may not assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights under it, including the license granted in Section 7.2, without the prior written consent of Employee. This restriction applies whether the assignment or transfer occurs by operation of law, merger, conversion, change of control, sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, or otherwise. Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 12.4 is void, and upon any such purported assignment or transfer the license granted in Section 7.2 terminates.

**12.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**12.6 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York, County of Kings or County of New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**12.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. Any court of competent jurisdiction may modify the restrictive covenants in Section 8 to the minimum extent necessary to make them enforceable.

**12.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A – WICKLINE TOOLKIT

The "Wickline" engine toolkit was developed by Tamsin Okoro before the Effective Date. It consists of the following:

1. The Wickline level and encounter editor, including its node-based scripting interface.
2. The Wickline lighting and particle shader library.
3. The Wickline animation state-machine runtime and associated editor plug-ins.
4. The Wickline build and asset-cooking utilities.
5. All documentation, sample projects and test suites for items 1 through 4.

The version of the Wickline Toolkit existing on the Effective Date is identified by repository commit hash `wl-7c41e9a`, a copy of which Employee has deposited with the Company.
