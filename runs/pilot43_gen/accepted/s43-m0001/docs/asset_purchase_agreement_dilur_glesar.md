# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 24, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**"), by and between **Dilur Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Glesar Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is an independent video game developer. It owns and operates the "Emberfall" action role-playing game franchise, including its game code, trademarks, soundtrack and related assets (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) the Assigned IP, including the items listed on Schedule 2.1(a);

(b) all development hardware, build servers, dev kits and other tangible personal property used primarily in the Business, as listed on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all books, records, design documents, player data (to the extent transferable under applicable Law), marketing materials and files relating primarily to the Business; and

(e) all goodwill of the Business, including the goodwill associated with the Emberfall Marks.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include any of the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): Seller's cash and cash equivalents; Seller's organizational documents and tax records; any Contract that is not an Assigned Contract; any Intellectual Property not used or held for use in the Business; and the assets listed on Schedule 2.2.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the following Liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising and to be performed after the Closing, excluding any Liability arising from any breach or default by Seller occurring at or before the Closing; and

(b) Liabilities arising from Buyer's ownership or operation of the Purchased Assets after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for paying, performing and discharging, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Without limiting the foregoing, the Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all Liabilities for Taxes of Seller, or relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(b) all Liabilities relating to any current or former employee or contractor of Seller arising on or before the Closing Date, including wages, bonuses, royalties, severance and benefits;

(c) all Liabilities under any Contract that is not an Assigned Contract;

(d) all Liabilities arising from pre-Closing infringement by or use of the Assigned IP, which are retained by Seller;

(e) all Liabilities arising out of any action, suit or proceeding relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets to the extent arising from facts or circumstances existing on or before the Closing Date; and

(f) all indebtedness of Seller.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate consideration for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Seller designates in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Allocation.** Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller an allocation of the Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities among the Purchased Assets, prepared in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. The Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.7 Non-Assignable Assets.** If any Assigned Contract cannot be assigned without a consent that has not been obtained at or before the Closing, this Agreement shall not constitute an assignment of that Assigned Contract. In that case Seller shall, until the consent is obtained, provide Buyer the benefits of that Assigned Contract under any reasonable arrangement Buyer requests, and Buyer shall perform the corresponding obligations to the extent of the benefits received.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after the conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale for the tangible Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**"), duly executed by Seller;

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"), duly executed by Seller;

(c) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller, together with short-form trademark and copyright assignments suitable for recording;

(d) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.4;

(e) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying as to the matters set forth in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b);

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(g) complete copies of the Emberfall Game Code repositories, together with all access credentials.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.5;

(b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying as to the matters set forth in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b).

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall instead survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive until fully performed. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the applicable survival period expires shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates, and their respective members, managers, officers, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Procedures.**

(a) *Third-Party Claims.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any third-party claim for which indemnification is sought. Failure to give that notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, by written notice delivered within twenty (20) days after receipt of the claim notice. The indemnified party may participate in that defense at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money that the indemnifying party pays in full and includes a complete release of the indemnified party.

(b) *Direct Claims.* An indemnified party shall assert any claim not involving a third party by written notice to the indemnifying party. The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days to respond.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *General Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller for claims under Section 9.2(a), and the aggregate liability of Buyer for claims under Section 9.3(a), shall each not exceed 15% of the Purchase Price ($7,275,000) (the "**General Cap**").

(b) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under that Section exceed $242,500. Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.

(c) *Fundamental Representations.* The representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority) and 4.9 (Intellectual Property) (the "**Fundamental Representations**") are excluded from the General Cap and from the basket in Section 9.5(b). Seller's aggregate liability for breach of the Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Fraud.* None of the limitations in this Section 9.5 applies to Losses arising from fraud.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims arising from fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties with respect to any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

(e) by Seller, by written notice, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice of it.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any Party, except that (a) Sections 6.6, 10.2 and Article XI shall survive, and (b) no termination relieves a Party from liability for any willful breach of this Agreement before termination.
