# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Glesar Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Dilur Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as Buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 24, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Assignee, and Assignee agreed to purchase from Assignor, the Purchased Assets, including the Assigned IP, for a Purchase Price of $48,500,000.

B. Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to deliver this Agreement at the Closing to effect the assignment of the Assigned IP to Assignee.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Assigned IP," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Emberfall Game Code," "Emberfall Marks," "Emberfall Soundtrack," "Encumbrance," "General Cap," "Intellectual Property," "Losses" and "Transaction Documents." In addition:

(a) "**Assignee Indemnitees**" means Assignee, its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents.

(b) "**Assignor Indemnitees**" means Assignor, its members and managers, and their respective officers, employees and agents.

(c) "**Copyrights**" means all copyrights and works of authorship included in the Assigned IP, whether registered or unregistered, including all copyrights in the Emberfall Game Code and the Emberfall Soundtrack, and all registrations, applications, renewals and extensions of any of them.

(d) "**Marks**" means the Emberfall Marks and all other trademarks, service marks, logos, trade dress and domain names included in the Assigned IP, together with all registrations, applications, renewals and the goodwill associated with any of them.

(e) "**Short-Form Assignments**" means the short-form trademark assignment and the short-form copyright assignment in the forms attached as Exhibits 1 and 2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee hereby accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances. This includes the items listed on Schedule A and the following:

(a) the Emberfall Game Code, including all source code, object code, build scripts, tools, assets, engine modifications and related documentation for *Emberfall*, *Emberfall: Ashen Crown* and *Emberfall III*;

(b) the Marks, together with the goodwill of the Business symbolized by the Marks;

(c) the Emberfall Soundtrack, including all musical compositions, master recordings, stems and audio assets;

(d) all character designs, lore bibles, concept art, and marketing and promotional assets for the Emberfall franchise;

(e) all Copyrights;

(f) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the foregoing; and

(g) all rights to sue for and recover damages and other relief for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the foregoing, and all rights of priority and protection of interests in any of the foregoing anywhere in the world.

**2.2 Moral Rights.** To the extent Assignor holds any moral rights or similar rights of attribution or integrity in the Assigned IP, Assignor hereby irrevocably waives, and agrees not to assert, those rights against Assignee or its successors, assigns or licensees, to the extent permitted by applicable Law.

**2.3 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Marks under Section 2.1 includes the portion of the goodwill of the Business connected with the use of, and symbolized by, the Marks. Assignor shall not use any of the Marks, or any mark confusingly similar to any of them, after the Effective Date.

**2.4 Delivery.** On the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee complete copies of all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP in Assignor's possession or control, including the Emberfall Game Code repositories and all access credentials, as provided in Section 3.2(g) of the Purchase Agreement. Assignor shall not retain any copy of the Emberfall Game Code after the Effective Date, except as required by applicable Law.

## 3. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as of the Effective Date as follows:

**3.1 Authority.** Assignor has full limited liability company power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. Its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary limited liability company action of Assignor.

**3.2 Title.** Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances, and has not previously assigned, transferred or exclusively licensed any of the Assigned IP to any Person.

**3.3 Non-Infringement.** The Assigned IP, and its development, use and exploitation, do not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person.

**3.4 Purchase Agreement Representations.** The representations and warranties in Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement are incorporated by reference in this Agreement and are made by Assignor as of the Effective Date.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES; RECORDATION

**4.1 Short-Form Assignments.** Concurrently with this Agreement, Assignor shall execute and deliver to Assignee the Short-Form Assignments. The Short-Form Assignments are delivered solely for recordation and do not expand or limit the rights and obligations of the Parties under this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Recordation.** Assignee may record this Agreement or the Short-Form Assignments with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, the European Union Intellectual Property Office, the UK Intellectual Property Office, the Japan Patent Office and any other Governmental Authority or domain name registrar. Assignor authorizes each such authority and registrar to record Assignee as the owner of the applicable Assigned IP.

**4.3 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and expense, Assignor shall execute and deliver any further instruments, and take any further actions, that Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. This includes executing declarations, assignments and powers of attorney, and providing testimony in any proceeding.

**4.4 Power of Attorney.** Assignor hereby irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, deliver and file in Assignor's name any instrument described in Section 4.3 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest.

**4.5 Domain Names.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall take all actions required by the applicable registrar to transfer the domain emberfall.example and every other domain name included in the Marks to an account designated by Assignee.

## 5. INDEMNIFICATION

**5.1 Indemnification by Assignor.** From and after the Effective Date, Assignor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Assignee Indemnitees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Assignor in Section 3, or any breach of any covenant of Assignor in this Agreement, in accordance with Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Indemnification by Assignee.** From and after the Effective Date, Assignee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Assignor Indemnitees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the Assigned IP or the ownership, use, licensing, distribution or other exploitation of the Assigned IP, whether arising before or after the Effective Date. Assignee's obligations under this Section 5.2 are not subject to any limitation in the Purchase Agreement.

**5.3 Procedures.** Claims for indemnification under this Section 5 shall be made and handled in accordance with the procedures in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, which apply to this Agreement *mutatis mutandis*.

## 6. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**6.1 Purchase Agreement.** This Agreement is delivered under, and is subject to the terms of, the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement limits Assignee's rights to the Purchased Assets under the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Survival.** The representations and warranties in Section 3 survive the Effective Date for the survival period that applies to the Fundamental Representations under Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement. The covenants in Sections 2.3, 2.4 and 4 survive until fully performed.

**6.3 Liability for Intellectual Property Matters.** Assignor's liability for any inaccuracy in or breach of Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement or Section 3 of this Agreement shall be subject to the General Cap.

**6.4 No Double Recovery.** No Assignee Indemnitee shall recover more than once for the same Loss under this Agreement and the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**7.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Affiliate or to any successor to or acquirer of any of the Assigned IP without Assignor's consent.

**7.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**7.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.6 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**7.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
