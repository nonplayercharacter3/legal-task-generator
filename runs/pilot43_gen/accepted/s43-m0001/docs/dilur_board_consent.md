# DILUR INTERACTIVE, INC.

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Dated as of October 6, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Dilur Interactive, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), acting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and the Company's Bylaws, hereby waive all notice and adopt the following recitals and resolutions by unanimous written consent, without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as if the Board had adopted them unanimously at a duly called and held meeting.

---

## 1. RECITALS

**A. The Asset Purchase Agreement.** The Company, as Buyer, and Glesar Studios LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Glesar**"), as Seller, entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 24, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, the Company agreed to acquire the assets of the "Emberfall" action role-playing game franchise, including the Emberfall Game Code, the Emberfall Marks, the Emberfall Soundtrack and the Assigned Contracts. The Assigned Contracts include the Key Employee Agreement between Glesar and Tamsin Okoro. The Purchase Price is $48,500,000. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**B. Prior Board Approval.** Before the Purchase Agreement was executed, the Board approved it and the other Transaction Documents. The Board also authorized the Company's officers to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement on the Company's behalf and to perform the Company's obligations under it.

**C. Schedule Update.** On September 14, 2026, the Company received written notice from Glesar of a Schedule Update under Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement (the "**September Schedule Update**"). The September Schedule Update discloses a pending claim by the composer of the Emberfall Soundtrack. The claim alleges that Glesar's exploitation of certain compositions and master recordings in the Emberfall Soundtrack infringes the composer's copyrights, and it asserts a right to royalties for that exploitation (the "**Composer Claim**").

**D. Effect on Representations.** The Composer Claim bears on, among other provisions, Seller's representations and warranties in Section 4.7 (Litigation) and Section 4.9 (Intellectual Property) of the Purchase Agreement, and on Schedule 4.9(d), which lists no royalty obligations. Section 4.9 is a Fundamental Representation under Section 9.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

**E. Management Report.** The Board has received a report from the Company's Chief Executive Officer and General Counsel on the September Schedule Update and the Composer Claim. The report covered the Company's rights under Sections 2.4, 6.7, 7.2 and 10.1 and Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The Board has considered the report and the advice of the Company's outside counsel.

**F. Board Determination.** The Board has determined that the Company should not accept the September Schedule Update. The Board has further determined that the Company should object to it in writing and reserve all of its rights and remedies with respect to the Composer Claim.

---

## 2. RESOLUTIONS

### 2.1 Objection to the September Schedule Update

**RESOLVED**, that the Company does not accept the September Schedule Update. The Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each hereby authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to prepare, execute and deliver to Glesar a written notice under Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice shall state that the Company objects to the September Schedule Update and to the matters disclosed in it, including the Composer Claim.

### 2.2 Reservation of Rights

**RESOLVED**, that the Objection Notice shall expressly reserve all of the Company's rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement, the other Transaction Documents and applicable Law with respect to the September Schedule Update and the Composer Claim, including:

(a) the Company's rights to indemnification under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement for any inaccuracy in or breach of Seller's representations and warranties, including the Fundamental Representations;

(b) the Company's position that all Liabilities arising out of the Composer Claim are Excluded Liabilities under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement, including Section 2.4(d) and Section 2.4(e), retained by Seller and indemnifiable under Section 9.2(c);

(c) the Company's rights with respect to the conditions to Closing in Section 7.2 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(d) the Company's rights under Article X of the Purchase Agreement.

### 2.3 Negotiation with Seller

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to negotiate with Glesar concerning the Composer Claim. These negotiations may cover a special indemnity, an escrow or holdback of a portion of the Purchase Price, an amendment to the Purchase Agreement, or any other arrangement that the Authorized Officer considers in the best interests of the Company. However, no amendment to the Purchase Agreement, and no waiver of any of the Company's rights under it, shall be executed without further approval of the Board.

### 2.4 No Settlement by Seller

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized and directed to remind Glesar, in the Objection Notice or otherwise, that under Section 6.1 of the Purchase Agreement Glesar may not settle any claim relating to the Assigned IP, including the Composer Claim, without the Company's prior written consent.

### 2.5 Reporting

**RESOLVED**, that the General Counsel shall report to the Board on the status of the Objection Notice, the Composer Claim and any negotiations with Glesar no later than the Board's next regularly scheduled meeting, and more frequently as material developments occur.

### 2.6 General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all further actions and to execute and deliver all further notices, instruments and documents that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions. The Authorized Officer's taking of any such action or execution of any such document shall be conclusive evidence of that determination.

### 2.7 Ratification

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any officer or director of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects, to the extent consistent with these resolutions.
