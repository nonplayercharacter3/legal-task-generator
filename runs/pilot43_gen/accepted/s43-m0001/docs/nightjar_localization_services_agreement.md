# LOCALIZATION AND QUALITY ASSURANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This LOCALIZATION AND QUALITY ASSURANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Dilur Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Dilur**"), and **Nightjar Localization Ltd.**, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales ("**Vendor**"). Dilur and Vendor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Dilur publishes video games for console, PC and mobile platforms.

B. Vendor provides translation, localization, linguistic quality assurance and functional quality assurance services to the interactive entertainment industry.

C. Dilur wishes to engage Vendor to provide those services for certain Dilur titles, and Vendor wishes to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

(b) "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. For Dilur, this includes unreleased builds, scripts, source text, story content, release dates and marketing plans. Confidential Information does not include information that (i) is or becomes publicly available other than through the Receiving Party's breach, (ii) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure, (iii) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (iv) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

(c) "**Deliverables**" means all translated text, localized assets, voice-over scripts, glossaries, style guides, bug reports, test plans, test results and other materials that Vendor prepares or delivers for Dilur under a Statement of Work.

(d) "**Dilur Materials**" means all builds, source text, art, audio, tools, glossaries and other materials that Dilur provides to Vendor.

(e) "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

(f) "**Services**" means the localization, linguistic quality assurance, functional quality assurance and related services described in a Statement of Work.

(g) "**Statement of Work**" means a written statement of work that refers to this Agreement and is signed by both Parties.

(h) "**Vendor Tools**" means Vendor's proprietary translation-memory software, terminology management systems, test-management platforms and methodologies that exist independently of the Services.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Statements of Work.** Vendor shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work shall identify the applicable Dilur title, the target languages and platforms, the scope of Services, the delivery schedule, the acceptance criteria and the fees. Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement.

**2.2 Change Orders.** Either Party may request a change to the scope or schedule of a Statement of Work. No change is binding unless both Parties sign a written change order setting out its effect on fees and schedule.

**2.3 Personnel.** Vendor shall assign qualified, native-speaker linguists and trained testers to the Services. Vendor shall designate a project manager for each Statement of Work as Dilur's primary point of contact. Vendor is responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and approved subcontractors.

**2.4 Subcontractors.** Vendor shall not subcontract any Services without Dilur's prior written consent. Vendor shall bind each approved subcontractor in writing to confidentiality and intellectual property obligations at least as protective of Dilur as those in this Agreement.

**2.5 Standards.** Vendor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and in compliance with Dilur's style guides and the platform certification requirements identified in the applicable Statement of Work.

**2.6 Dilur Responsibilities.** Dilur shall provide Vendor with timely access to the Dilur Materials, builds and development hardware reasonably required to perform the Services. Vendor is excused from any delay to the extent caused by Dilur's failure to do so.

## 3. ACCEPTANCE

**3.1 Review.** Dilur shall have ten (10) business days after receiving each Deliverable to review it against the acceptance criteria in the applicable Statement of Work and to notify Vendor in writing of any nonconformity.

**3.2 Correction.** Vendor shall correct any nonconformity identified under Section 3.1 and redeliver the Deliverable within five (5) business days after Dilur's notice, at no additional charge. The review process in Section 3.1 then applies to the corrected Deliverable.

**3.3 Deemed Acceptance.** A Deliverable is accepted when Dilur notifies Vendor of acceptance, or when the review period in Section 3.1 ends without notice of nonconformity.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Dilur shall pay Vendor the fees set out in each Statement of Work. Unless a Statement of Work provides otherwise, fees are calculated at the rates in Exhibit A.

**4.2 Invoices.** Vendor shall invoice Dilur monthly in arrears for accepted Deliverables and time-based Services performed during the preceding month. Each invoice shall identify the applicable Statement of Work and include reasonable supporting detail.

**4.3 Payment.** Dilur shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, in U.S. dollars, by wire transfer. Dilur shall notify Vendor in writing of any good-faith dispute within thirty (30) days after receipt of the invoice. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

**4.4 Expenses.** Dilur shall reimburse only those travel and out-of-pocket expenses that it approves in writing in advance.

**4.5 Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Dilur shall pay where applicable, other than taxes based on Vendor's net income. If Dilur is required by law to withhold any tax from a payment, Dilur may do so and shall provide Vendor with evidence of the withholding.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**5.1 Dilur Materials.** Dilur retains all right, title and interest in and to the Dilur Materials. Dilur grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the term of each Statement of Work to use the Dilur Materials solely to perform the Services.

**5.2 Ownership of Deliverables.** Upon payment of the applicable fees, all Deliverables and all intellectual property rights in them are the sole and exclusive property of Dilur. To the extent permitted by law, all copyrightable Deliverables are "works made for hire" for Dilur. To the extent any Deliverable is not a work made for hire, Vendor hereby assigns to Dilur all of its right, title and interest in and to that Deliverable, effective upon payment. Vendor shall obtain from its personnel and approved subcontractors all assignments and waivers of moral rights necessary to give effect to this Section 5.2.

**5.3 Vendor Tools.** Vendor retains all right, title and interest in and to the Vendor Tools. To the extent any Vendor Tool is incorporated in a Deliverable, Vendor grants Dilur a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use it as part of that Deliverable. Translation memories and glossaries generated from Dilur Materials are Deliverables, not Vendor Tools.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITY

**6.1 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services, (b) disclose it only to its employees, approved subcontractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 6, and (c) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care.

**6.2 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by law to disclose Confidential Information, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, to the extent legally permitted, and reasonable assistance in seeking a protective order.

**6.3 Build Security.** Vendor shall store all unreleased builds and Dilur Materials in access-controlled environments, shall not remove them from approved facilities, and shall notify Dilur within forty-eight (48) hours after discovering any unauthorized access to, or leak of, Dilur Materials.

**6.4 Return.** Upon completion of each Statement of Work, or at Dilur's request, Vendor shall return or securely destroy all Dilur Materials and Dilur Confidential Information, and certify that it has done so in writing.

**6.5 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 6 continue for five (5) years after this Agreement ends. For trade secrets and unreleased game content, they continue for as long as the information remains confidential.

## 7. WARRANTIES

**7.1 Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

**7.2 Vendor.** Vendor represents and warrants that (a) the Services will conform to Section 2.5 and the applicable Statement of Work; (b) the Deliverables, excluding Dilur Materials, will be original and will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party; and (c) Vendor will comply with all applicable laws, including employment and data protection laws, in performing the Services.

**7.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 By Vendor.** Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Dilur and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim that (a) a Deliverable, excluding Dilur Materials, infringes the intellectual property rights of a third party, or (b) results from Vendor's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 6.

**8.2 By Dilur.** Dilur shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Vendor and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim that the Dilur Materials, as provided by Dilur and used by Vendor in accordance with this Agreement, infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party.

**8.3 Procedures.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim. Delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party controls the defense and settlement of the claim, but may not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense and may participate with its own counsel at its own expense.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

**9.2 Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable by Dilur under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

**9.3 Exceptions.** Sections 9.1 and 9.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, (b) a Party's breach of Section 6, or (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

**10.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years. It then renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

**10.2 Termination for Convenience.** Dilur may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

**10.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.

**10.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon any termination, Dilur shall pay Vendor for all Services performed and accepted Deliverables delivered through the termination date. Vendor shall promptly deliver to Dilur all Deliverables, whether complete or in progress, and comply with Section 6.4. Termination of this Agreement terminates all Statements of Work then in effect, unless Dilur elects in writing to have a Statement of Work continue under the terms of this Agreement until it is completed.

**10.5 Survival.** Sections 4, 5, 6, 7.3, 8, 9, 10.4, 10.5 and 11 survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**11.1 Independent Contractor.** Vendor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship. Vendor is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits and taxes of its personnel.

**11.2 Insurance.** During the term, Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance and professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance, each with limits of at least $2,000,000 per claim, and shall provide certificates of insurance to Dilur upon request.

**11.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) business day after they are sent by internationally recognized courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or to such other address as a Party designates by notice):

If to Dilur: Dilur Interactive, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, legal@dilur.example.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. However, either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other Party.

**11.5 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
