# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 22, 2026, by and between **Givand Battery Recovery, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Goulound Lithium Reclamation, Inc.**, a Nevada corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates the Business from the Facility in Sparks, Nevada.

B. Seller wishes to sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, all or substantially all of the assets of Seller's spent lithium-ion battery recycling business, being the Purchased Assets, together with the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

"**Business**" means the business of collection, discharge and mechanical processing of spent lithium-ion batteries into black mass, excluding any refining of black mass, as conducted by Seller at the Facility as of the date of this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 5.1.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances listed on Schedule 3.5, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to all assets, properties and rights of every kind, wherever located, used primarily in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all machinery, equipment, processing lines, tooling, vehicles, furniture and other tangible personal property, including the items listed on Schedule 2.1(a);

(b) all inventory, including spent batteries, feedstock, work in process, black mass and finished products;

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 3.9 (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) Seller's leasehold interest in the Facility;

(e) all Permits, including Environmental Permits, to the extent transferable under applicable Law;

(f) all intellectual property, know-how, process documentation and trade secrets used primarily in the Business;

(g) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(h) all books, records, customer lists, supplier lists, and environmental, health and safety records relating to the Business or the Facility; and

(i) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's corporate seal, minute books and stock records; (c) contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (d) insurance policies of Seller; (e) tax refunds relating to periods ending on or before the Closing Date; (f) any assets not used primarily in the Business; and (g) the rights of Seller under the Transaction Documents.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (a) the obligations of Seller under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing Date, excluding any liability arising from a breach or default occurring on or before the Closing Date, and (b) trade accounts payable of the Business to third parties incurred in the ordinary course and reflected in the Closing Statement (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including: (a) all Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities; (b) all liabilities for taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any period ending on or before the Closing Date; (c) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets; (d) all liabilities relating to employees or former employees of Seller arising on or before the Closing Date; (e) all indebtedness of Seller; and (f) all liabilities arising from any litigation pending or threatened as of the Closing Date.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.7. At Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Indemnity Escrow.** At Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in the Escrow Fund and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and Article IX.

**2.7 Working Capital Adjustment.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the closing working capital of the Business (the "**Closing Statement**"). If closing working capital exceeds the target working capital set forth on Schedule 2.7, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller; if it is less, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. Disputes not resolved by the Parties within 30 days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing selected jointly by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

**2.8 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, under an allocation prepared by Buyer and delivered to Seller within 120 days after the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE III. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**3.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Nevada and has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to conduct the Business.

**3.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**3.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth on Schedule 3.3, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller, the Business or the Purchased Assets, or (c) require the consent of or notice to any Person under, or conflict with, result in a breach of, or give rise to a right of termination under, any Assigned Contract or Permit.

**3.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited financial statements of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the unaudited financial statements for the six months ended June 30, 2026. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business.

**3.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 3.5 (the "**Permitted Encumbrances**").

**3.6 Condition and Sufficiency of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The Purchased Assets are sufficient for the continued conduct of the Business immediately after the Closing in substantially the same manner as conducted before the Closing.

**3.7 Real Property.** Schedule 3.7 lists the lease of the Facility. Seller holds a valid leasehold interest in the Facility, and the lease is in full force and effect, with no default by Seller or, to Seller's knowledge, the landlord.

**3.8 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 3.8 lists all registered intellectual property used in the Business. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**3.9 Material Contracts.** Schedule 3.9 lists each contract relating to the Business that (a) involves annual payments of more than $250,000, (b) is a supply, offtake or tolling agreement, (c) restricts the conduct of the Business, or (d) is otherwise material to the Business. Each such contract is valid and binding and in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach. Schedule 3.9 identifies each such contract that requires the consent of, or notice to, the counterparty in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**3.10 Permits.** Schedule 3.10 lists all Permits held by Seller for the operation of the Business, all of which are valid and in full force and effect.

**3.11 Environmental Matters.** Except as set forth on Schedule 3.11: (a) Seller and the Business are and have been in material compliance with all Environmental Laws and Environmental Permits; (b) there has been no Release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Facility in violation of Environmental Laws or requiring investigation or remediation; (c) Seller has not received any notice of any actual or alleged violation of Environmental Laws; and (d) Seller has made available to Buyer all environmental reports, audits and assessments relating to the Facility in its possession.

**3.12 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**3.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

**3.14 Employees.** Schedule 3.14 lists all employees of the Business, together with their titles and current compensation.

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**4.2 Authority.** Buyer has full limited liability company power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at Closing sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**4.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE V. CLOSING

**5.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, or on another date agreed by the Parties (the "**Closing Date**").

**5.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale (the "**Bill of Sale**"); (b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (c) an assignment of the Facility lease; (d) the Escrow Agreement, executed by Seller; (e) the Transition Services Agreement, executed by Seller; (f) evidence of the Required Consents; and (g) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b).

**5.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to Seller; (b) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each executed by Buyer; and (d) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, (a) conduct the Business only in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, (b) maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, (c) maintain all Permits in full force, (d) not amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract, and (e) not sell, lease or encumber any Purchased Asset other than inventory sold in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Governmental Filings and Third-Party Consents.**

(a) Each Party shall make all filings with Governmental Authorities required to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement and shall cooperate with the other Party in doing so.

(b) Seller shall give all notices to third parties required under the Assigned Contracts in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

(c) The consents, approvals and Permit transfers set forth on Schedule 6.2(c) are the "**Required Consents**."

**6.3 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Facility, the Purchased Assets, and the books, records and personnel of the Business, including for the purpose of conducting environmental assessments of the Facility (including, with Seller's consent not to be unreasonably withheld, invasive sampling).

**6.4 Efforts to Obtain Required Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each of the Required Consents before the Closing, on terms reasonably satisfactory to Buyer. Seller shall not be required to pay any consideration to obtain any consent other than customary administrative fees.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement concerning any sale of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent required by Law.

**6.7 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.**

(a) For five years after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (i) engage in the Business anywhere in the United States, Canada or Mexico, (ii) own, manage, operate or control any Person engaged in the Business in that territory, or (iii) solicit any customer or supplier of the Business to cease or reduce its business with Buyer.

(b) During the Restricted Period, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, solicit for employment any employee of the Business hired by Buyer, other than through general solicitations not directed at those employees.

(c) Seller acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 6.7 are reasonable and necessary to protect the goodwill acquired by Buyer. If any court finds any restriction unenforceable, the court may modify it to the extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**6.8 Transition Services.** At Closing, the Parties shall enter into a Transition Services Agreement under which Seller shall provide to Buyer, for up to nine months after the Closing Date, the accounting, IT, logistics and regulatory reporting services described in it, at Seller's cost.

**6.9 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to any employee of the Business listed on Schedule 3.14, effective as of the Closing Date. Seller shall be responsible for all compensation and benefits of its employees through the Closing Date.

**6.10 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws. Any liabilities arising from noncompliance are Excluded Liabilities.

**6.11 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver further instruments and take further action as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to close are subject to (a) the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, and (b) the receipt of any required approvals of Governmental Authorities.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to close are subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects (or, for those qualified by materiality or Material Adverse Effect, in all respects) as of the Closing Date, and the Fundamental Representations are true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred since the date of this Agreement;

(d) each of the Required Consents has been obtained and is in full force and effect; and

(e) Seller has delivered each of the items in Section 5.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to close are subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and (c) Buyer has delivered each of the items in Section 5.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, unless the failure is due to the terminating Party's breach; (c) by Buyer, if Seller breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 20 days after notice; or (d) by Seller, if Buyer breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 20 days after notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination, this Agreement shall become void and there shall be no liability of either Party, except that Section 6.6, this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a Party from liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date, except that (a) the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, and (b) the representations in Section 3.11 (Environmental Matters) survive until the sixth anniversary of the Closing Date. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms. Claims asserted in writing before the end of the applicable survival period survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement; (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; (c) any Excluded Asset; or (d) any Excluded Liability, including any Pre-Closing Environmental Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Basket.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses exceed $485,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

**9.5 Cap; Exceptions.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price.

(b) The limitations in Section 9.4 and Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising out of or relating to Fraud, breaches of Fundamental Representations, and Excluded Liabilities (including Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities).

**9.6 Indemnification Procedures.** A Buyer Indemnitee seeking indemnification shall give Seller written notice of the claim describing it in reasonable detail. For third-party claims, the indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, at its own cost, but may not settle any claim without the indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only payment of money by the indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the indemnified Party. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually prejudiced.

**9.7 Payments; Escrow.** Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund, to the extent available, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, and thereafter directly by Seller. Seller shall pay any amount due within ten Business Days after final determination.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedies.** Except for claims for Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.9, and the working capital adjustment under Section 2.7, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of this Agreement after the Closing.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use, documentary and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Proration.** Personal property and real property taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Buyer and Seller as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it. The fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth below each Party's signature, or to another address a Party designates by notice.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to modify the Agreement to effect their original intent as closely as possible.

**11.4 Assignment.** Buyer may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Seller. Seller may assign this Agreement and any of its rights and obligations under it, including its obligations under Article IX, Section 6.7 and Section 6.8 and the Transition Services Agreement, to any Person without the consent of Buyer. Upon any such assignment and the assumption in writing by the assignee of the assigned obligations, Seller shall be released from those obligations. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.8 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the state or federal courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.9 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**11.10 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and the Disclosure Schedule or the Escrow Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the Escrow Agreement shall control with respect to matters concerning the Escrow Fund.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
