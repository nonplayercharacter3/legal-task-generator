# OFFICE LEASE

**OAKHURST PLAZA**
**2200 Oakhurst Plaza Drive, Wilmington, Delaware 19803**

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Oakhurst Plaza Associates LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Givand Battery Recovery, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Tenant**").

**1.2 Definitions.** "**Lease Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday observed by banks in Wilmington, Delaware. "**Rent**" means Base Rent and Additional Rent together.

## 2. PREMISES

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants, to use the lobbies, elevators, corridors, restrooms, parking areas and other common areas of the Property (the "**Common Areas**").

**2.2 Rentable Area.** The rentable area of the Premises and the Building has been measured under the BOMA Office Buildings Standard Methods of Measurement (ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017). The Parties accept those measurements, and neither Party will remeasure them during the Term.

**2.3 Condition of Premises.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises to Tenant on the Commencement Date with Landlord's Work (as defined in Section 6.1) substantially complete, broom clean and free of all occupants. Landlord shall also deliver the Building systems serving the Premises in good working order. Except for Landlord's Work and Landlord's obligations under Section 9, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless it ends earlier under this Lease.

**3.2 Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises up to fifteen (15) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, cabling and equipment. All terms of this Lease apply during early access, except the obligation to pay Rent.

**3.3 Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term for one additional period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**"). To exercise this option, Tenant must give Landlord written notice at least nine (9) months before the Expiration Date, and Tenant must not then be in default beyond any applicable cure period. Base Rent for the Renewal Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the fair market rent for comparable office space in comparable buildings in the Wilmington, Delaware suburban office market. If the Parties cannot agree on fair market rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, each Party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in that market. The two brokers shall select a third broker, and fair market rent will be the average of the two determinations that are closest together.

## 4. RENT

**4.2 Abatement.** Base Rent for the second, third and fourth full calendar months of the Term is abated. Tenant shall pay Additional Rent during the abatement period.

**4.3 Additional Rent.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Pro Rata Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year ("**Additional Rent**").

(a) "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair and insure the Property in a manner consistent with comparable office buildings in the Wilmington, Delaware suburban office market. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures, except the annual amortized portion of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by Laws first enacted after the Effective Date. They also exclude leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, debt service, Landlord's income taxes, costs recovered from insurance or other tenants, and costs of correcting construction defects.

(b) "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Property for the applicable calendar year.

(c) Operating Expenses for the Base Year and each later year will be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy of the Building, if the Building is less than fully occupied.

(d) Tenant's Pro Rata Share of controllable Operating Expenses will not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative, compounding basis over the Base Year. Controllable Operating Expenses exclude insurance premiums, utility costs, snow removal and Taxes.

**4.4 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Before each calendar year, Landlord shall give Tenant a reasonable estimate of Additional Rent for that year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall give Tenant a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receiving the statement.

**4.5 Audit Right.** Within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving an annual statement, Tenant may inspect Landlord's records for that year at Landlord's office on reasonable notice. If the inspection shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's share by more than three percent (3%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable inspection costs, in addition to refunding the overpayment.

**4.6 Payment; Late Charge.** Tenant shall pay Rent without demand, deduction or offset, except as expressly provided in this Lease, by ACH transfer or check to the account or address that Landlord designates in writing. If any Rent is not paid within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. However, no late charge applies to the first late payment in any twelve-month period unless the payment remains unpaid for five (5) days after Landlord gives Tenant written notice.

**4.7 Partial Months.** Rent for any partial month at the beginning or end of the Term will be prorated on a daily basis.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant shall deliver the Security Deposit to Landlord on execution of this Lease. If Tenant defaults beyond any applicable cure period, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit toward the amounts owed, and Tenant shall restore it within ten (10) days after written demand. Landlord is not required to keep the Security Deposit separate from its general funds. Landlord shall return any unapplied balance to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Section 18.

## 6. IMPROVEMENTS AND ALTERATIONS

**6.1 Landlord's Work.** Before the Commencement Date, at Landlord's cost, Landlord shall complete the following work using Building-standard materials (together, "**Landlord's Work**"): new carpet and paint throughout the Premises; construction of one conference room and two private offices according to the plan attached to Exhibit A; and upgrade of the kitchenette. If Landlord's Work is not substantially complete by the Commencement Date because of Landlord's delay, the Commencement Date, the Expiration Date and the Base Rent schedule will be postponed day for day until it is substantially complete.

**6.2 Alterations.** Tenant may not make any alteration, addition or improvement to the Premises ("**Alterations**") without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. No consent is required for cosmetic Alterations, such as painting, carpeting and installing shelving, that cost less than $15,000 in any one instance, do not affect the Building structure or systems, and do not require a building permit. Tenant shall perform all Alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with Laws, using contractors reasonably approved by Landlord.

**6.3 Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant. Tenant shall discharge or bond over any such lien within twenty (20) days after it receives notice of the lien.

## 7. USE AND COMPLIANCE

**7.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use. Tenant shall not use the Premises in any way that violates any law, ordinance, regulation or order of any governmental authority ("**Laws**"). Tenant shall not create a nuisance or unreasonably interfere with other tenants of the Building.

**7.2 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Property any hazardous materials, except customary office and cleaning supplies in quantities used in compliance with Laws. Tenant shall not store or display at the Premises any batteries, battery components, black mass or other recycling materials, other than consumer batteries used in ordinary office equipment.

**7.3 Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the reasonable rules and regulations for the Building that Landlord gives Tenant in writing, as long as they apply on a non-discriminatory basis to all tenants and do not conflict with this Lease.

**7.4 Access.** Tenant has access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's security procedures.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**8.1 Landlord's Services.** Landlord shall provide the following, the cost of which is included in Operating Expenses:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on Business Days and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays;

(b) electricity for normal office use;

(c) passenger elevator service at all times;

(d) janitorial service five (5) nights per week to Building standard; and

(e) water for restroom and kitchenette use.

**8.2 After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC service outside the hours in Section 8.1(a) on Tenant's request, at Landlord's actual cost, currently $55 per hour per zone.

**8.3 Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If an interruption caused by Landlord's negligence makes the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive Business Days after Tenant gives Landlord written notice of it, Rent will abate from the sixth Business Day until the service is restored.

## 9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

**9.1 Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements and exterior walls of the Building, the Common Areas, and the Building's mechanical, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and life-safety systems up to the point of connection to the Premises.

**9.2 Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall repair any damage to the Property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or invitees.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**10.1 Consent Required.** Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises (each a "**Transfer**") without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days after it receives the request, together with the information about the proposed transferee that Landlord reasonably requires.

**10.2 Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) Business Days' prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to (a) an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, or (b) an entity that acquires all or substantially all of Tenant's assets or equity, or into which Tenant is merged, if that entity's tangible net worth immediately after the transaction is not less than Tenant's tangible net worth on the Effective Date (each a "**Permitted Transfer**").

**10.3 No Release.** No Transfer, including a Permitted Transfer, releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

**10.4 Excess Rent.** For any Transfer other than a Permitted Transfer, Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from the transferee that exceeds the Rent payable under this Lease for the transferred space, after Tenant recovers its reasonable costs of the Transfer.

## 11. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**11.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Throughout the Term, Tenant shall maintain, with insurers rated A-/VIII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds;

(b) umbrella liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000;

(c) special-form property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and Alterations, at full replacement cost; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law.

Tenant shall deliver certificates of this insurance to Landlord before the earlier of early access under Section 3.2 and the Commencement Date.

**11.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Throughout the Term, Landlord shall maintain special-form property insurance covering the Building at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence in the aggregate, including umbrella coverage.

**11.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives all claims against the other for damage to property to the extent the damage is covered, or would be covered, by the property insurance required under this Lease. Each Party shall cause its property insurers to waive subrogation accordingly.

**11.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 11.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Premises or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors. Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Common Areas or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**12.1 Casualty.** If fire or other casualty damages the Premises or the Building access to them, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant an estimate from a reputable contractor of the time needed to restore them. If the estimated restoration time exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days, either Party may terminate this Lease by written notice to the other within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate. If this Lease is not terminated, Landlord shall restore the Premises (excluding Tenant's personal property and Alterations) with reasonable diligence. Rent will abate in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable until restoration is complete.

**12.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that the remainder is not reasonably usable for the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates as of the date of the taking. If any lesser part is taken, Rent will be reduced in proportion to the rentable area taken. Landlord is entitled to the award, but Tenant may make a separate claim for its moving expenses and its personal property, as long as that claim does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 13. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**13.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five (5) days after Landlord gives Tenant written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after Landlord gives Tenant written notice of the failure. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, there is no Event of Default as long as Tenant begins the cure within that period and diligently completes it; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that, if involuntary, is not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

**13.2 Landlord's Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may, by written notice to Tenant, terminate this Lease or Tenant's right of possession. It may also recover the Rent due through the date of termination and damages as permitted by the Laws of the State of Delaware. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to relet the Premises and mitigate its damages. Landlord may not recover consequential damages from Tenant, except as provided in Section 18.2.

**13.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after Tenant gives Landlord written notice of the failure (or within a longer period that is reasonably necessary, if Landlord begins the cure within thirty (30) days and diligently completes it), Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

## 14. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL

**14.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Property. However, the subordination is effective only if the mortgagee enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement with Tenant (an "**SNDA**") providing that Tenant's possession will not be disturbed as long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver an SNDA from the current mortgagee of the Property within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date.

**14.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after a written request from either Party, the other Party shall deliver a certificate confirming the status of this Lease, the dates through which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 15. LANDLORD'S ACCESS

Landlord may enter the Premises on at least one (1) Business Day's prior notice, except in an emergency, to inspect them, make repairs or show them to prospective lenders or purchasers. During the last nine (9) months of the Term, Landlord may also show the Premises to prospective tenants, unless Tenant has exercised its renewal option under Section 3.3. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business, and Tenant may require that a Tenant representative accompany Landlord during any entry.

## 16. QUIET ENJOYMENT

As long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant may peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

## 17. SIGNAGE

Landlord shall provide, at its cost, Building-standard signage identifying Tenant in the main lobby directory and at the entrance to the Premises. Tenant may not install any other signage visible from outside the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

## 18. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

**18.1 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall remove its personal property and cabling. Tenant is not required to remove Landlord's Work or any Alteration, unless Landlord required its removal in writing at the time it consented to that Alteration.

**18.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. During the holdover, Tenant shall pay one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the monthly Base Rent last in effect, prorated on a daily basis, plus Additional Rent. If the holdover continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord gives Tenant written notice that Landlord has entered into a lease with a new tenant for the Premises, Tenant is also liable for Landlord's actual damages caused by the holdover.

## 20. MISCELLANEOUS

**20.1 Brokers.** Each Party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than the Brokers named in Section 1.1. Landlord shall pay the commissions of both Brokers under separate agreements. Each Party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through the indemnifying Party.

**20.2 Limitation of Landlord's Liability.** Tenant's recourse against Landlord for any obligation under this Lease is limited to Landlord's interest in the Property, including its rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds.

**20.3 Force Majeure.** Except for the payment of money, neither Party is in default for any delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, strikes, governmental action, pandemic or shortages of materials. The time for performance is extended for the period of the delay.

**20.4 Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**20.5 Successors.** This Lease binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, subject to Section 10.

**20.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**20.8 Authority.** Each Party represents that the individual signing this Lease on its behalf is authorized to do so.

**20.9 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
