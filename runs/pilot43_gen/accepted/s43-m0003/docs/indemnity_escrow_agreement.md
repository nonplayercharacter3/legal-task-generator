# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and among **Givand Battery Recovery, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Goulound Lithium Reclamation, Inc.**, a Nevada corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are together the "**Principal Parties**." Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent are each a "**Party**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 22, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is purchasing substantially all of the assets of Seller's spent lithium-ion battery recycling business conducted at the Facility in Sparks, Nevada.

B. Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The amount secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS; APPOINTMENT

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware or New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less all disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 9, accompanied by a certificate of counsel to the presenting Principal Party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $4,850,000.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions executed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

**1.2 Appointment.** The Principal Parties appoint the Escrow Agent as their escrow agent for the purposes of this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

## 2. DEPOSIT OF ESCROW FUND

**2.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in Schedule A. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt to the Principal Parties in writing on the day of receipt.

**2.2 Segregated Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent for the benefit of the Principal Parties. The Escrow Fund is not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party. It shall be held and disbursed only as provided in this Agreement.

**2.3 No Interest in Escrow Fund.** Neither Principal Party may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer or encumber any interest in the Escrow Fund. Any attempt to do so is void.

## 3. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUND

**3.1 Release Date.** On the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate Claim Amounts of all Claim Notices delivered on or before the Release Date that have not been paid or otherwise resolved. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent confirming the Closing Date and the amount to be released at least five Business Days before the Release Date.

**3.2 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained under Section 3.1 until the related Claim Notice is resolved under Section 4. When each Claim Notice is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall pay the amount due to Buyer, if any, in accordance with Section 4. It shall release to Seller the excess of the retained amount for that Claim Notice over the amount paid to Buyer.

**3.3 Other Disbursements.** The Escrow Agent shall also disburse all or any part of the Escrow Fund at any time (a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, or (b) in accordance with a Final Order.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

**4.1 Claim Notice.** At any time on or before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") asserting a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice must state:

(a) the nature and basis of the claim in reasonable detail, including the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which it is made; and

(b) the amount of Losses claimed or, if not then determinable, a good-faith estimate of those Losses (the "**Claim Amount**").

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a Claim Notice by delivering a written notice of objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent (an "**Objection Notice**") within 30 days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice. An Objection Notice must state the disputed portion of the Claim Amount and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Payment of Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period, Seller is deemed to have accepted the Claim Notice. On the fifth Business Day after the end of that period, the Escrow Agent shall pay the full Claim Amount to Buyer from the Escrow Fund. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a Claim Amount, the Escrow Agent shall pay the undisputed part to Buyer within five Business Days after it receives the Objection Notice. It shall retain the disputed part (the "**Disputed Amount**") in the Escrow Fund.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days after delivery of an Objection Notice to resolve the dispute. If they do so, they shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent reflecting the resolution. If they do not, either Principal Party may bring an action under Section 9. The Escrow Agent shall disburse a Disputed Amount only in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**4.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Fund is not sufficient to pay any Claim Amount in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay the balance of the Escrow Fund, and it has no further obligation for the unpaid part. Claim Notices shall be paid in the order in which the Escrow Agent receives them.

**4.6 Exclusive Source of Recovery.** The Escrow Fund is Buyer's sole and exclusive source of recovery for all Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, including Losses arising from Excluded Liabilities. This Section 4.6 does not apply to Losses arising from Fraud.

## 5. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUND

**5.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund only as directed in Joint Written Instructions in one or more of the following: (a) direct obligations of the United States of America with maturities of 90 days or less; (b) money market funds investing solely in such obligations; or (c) interest-bearing deposit accounts of the Escrow Agent insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limit. If no Joint Written Instructions are given, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in the investment described in Schedule A.

**5.2 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund become part of the Escrow Fund and are disbursed with it.

**5.3 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make any disbursement required under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss, fee or penalty resulting from the liquidation.

## 6. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly stated in this Agreement. These duties are ministerial and are not fiduciary. The Escrow Agent is not bound by and has no duty to review any other agreement, including the Purchase Agreement. It has no duty to determine whether any Party has complied with any other agreement.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and given by the proper person. It has no duty to investigate the accuracy of any statement in it.

**6.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its own gross negligence or willful misconduct, as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. The Escrow Agent is not liable in any event for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Advice of Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is fully protected for any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also deposit the Escrow Fund with a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 9 and bring an interpleader action. On that deposit, the Escrow Agent is released from all further obligations under this Agreement for the deposited funds.

**6.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving 30 days' written notice to the Principal Parties. The Principal Parties may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving 30 days' written notice to it. The resignation or removal takes effect on the appointment of a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions and the successor's acceptance in writing. If no successor is appointed within 30 days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one. The Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Fund and its records to the successor escrow agent.

**6.7 Authorized Representatives; Security Procedures.** Each Principal Party designates the individuals listed on Schedule B as its authorized representatives (each, an "**Authorized Representative**"). Any instruction to disburse funds that is not delivered in person shall be confirmed by the Escrow Agent by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative other than the person who signed the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until the call-back is completed.

## 7. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule C. It is also entitled to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses.

**7.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents and hold them harmless from all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including reasonable attorneys' fees and the costs of enforcing this Section 7.2. This indemnity does not apply to the extent any of them results from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.2, unless a court determines that the amount arose from the act or omission of one of them.

**7.3 Survival.** This Section 7 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Reporting.** For tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is disbursed. All earnings on the Escrow Fund shall be reported as income of Buyer on IRS Form 1099 or as otherwise required by law. Each disbursement shall be reported in accordance with Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**8.2 Tax Forms.** Each Principal Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Effective Date. The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any taxes it is required by law to withhold.

**8.3 Tax Distributions.** Within 30 days after the end of each calendar year, the Escrow Agent shall, at Buyer's written request, distribute to Buyer from the earnings of the Escrow Fund an amount equal to 40% of the earnings reported as Buyer's income for that year.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**9.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, City of New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement and for any dispute concerning the Escrow Fund, including whether a Claim Notice is valid or payable. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**9.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE ESCROW FUND.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed the entire Escrow Fund in accordance with its terms. Sections 6.3 and 7 survive termination.

**11.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.3 Assignment.** No Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or that acquires all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 USA PATRIOT Act.** The Principal Parties acknowledge that, under federal law, the Escrow Agent is required to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person that opens an account. Each Principal Party shall provide that information to the Escrow Agent on request.

**11.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
