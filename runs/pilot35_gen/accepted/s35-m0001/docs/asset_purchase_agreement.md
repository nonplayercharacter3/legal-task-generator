# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026, by and between **Stepair Outfitters, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Risel Alpine Gear, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller designs, develops, sources, markets and sells technical outerwear shell jackets, pants and related shell products (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

(b) "**Ancillary Documents**" means the Bill of Sale, the Lease Assignment and the other instruments of transfer and certificates delivered under Article III.

(c) "**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

(d) "**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in form reasonably acceptable to Buyer and Seller, by which Seller conveys the Purchased Assets to Buyer at the Closing.

(e) "**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, option, right of first refusal or other restriction of any kind.

(f) "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any political subdivision, agency, court or tribunal of any of them.

(g) "**Landlord**" means Kestrow Canal Properties LLC.

(h) "**Landlord Consent**" means the written consent of the Landlord to the assignment of the Studio Lease to Buyer.

(i) "**Lease Assignment**" means the assignment and assumption of the Studio Lease between Seller and Buyer, to which the Landlord Consent is attached.

(j) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(k) "**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, occurrence or development that, individually or in the aggregate, is or would reasonably be expected to be materially adverse to the Business, the Purchased Assets or the results of operations or financial condition of the Business.

(l) "**Membrane Technology License Agreement**" means the license dated March 3, 2023 between Fjellbrand AG and Seller described in Schedule 2.1(c).

(m) "**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

(n) "**Studio Lease**" means the lease between the Landlord, as landlord, and Seller, as tenant, for Suite 4B, 118 Carroll Basin Street, Brooklyn, New York.

(o) "**Transaction Documents**" means this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, properties and rights, to the extent used or held for use in the Business, free and clear of any Encumbrance (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all finished goods, work in process, raw materials, laminated fabrics, trims and other inventory of the Business;

(b) all intellectual property owned by Seller and used in the Business, including the trademarks, designs, patterns, technical specifications and trade secrets listed on Schedule 2.1(b), together with all goodwill associated with them;

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) (the "**Assigned Contracts**"), including the Membrane Technology License Agreement and the Studio Lease;

(d) all tooling, sample-room equipment, sewing and seam-taping machinery, furniture, fixtures and computer equipment of the Business listed on Schedule 2.1(d);

(e) all customer lists, supplier lists, wholesale account records, product testing data and other books and records of the Business; and

(f) all goodwill of the Business as a going concern.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include cash and cash equivalents, Seller's organizational documents, minute books and tax records, any insurance policies of Seller, any assets of Seller's insulated apparel and footwear lines, or any assets listed on Schedule 2.2 (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (a) the obligations of Seller under the Assigned Contracts arising and to be performed after the Closing, excluding any liability arising from a breach or default by Seller occurring at or before the Closing, and (b) the liabilities listed on Schedule 2.3 (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and pay, perform and discharge when due, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including all liabilities for taxes of Seller, all product warranty and product liability claims relating to products sold before the Closing, and all liabilities relating to Seller's employees for periods before the Closing.

**2.5 Third-Party Transfer Documents.** Seller shall deliver to Buyer at the Closing the Landlord Consent and such other transfer documents relating to the Assigned Contracts as Buyer reasonably requests.

**2.6 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) business days before the Closing.

**2.7 Allocation of Purchase Price.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets for tax purposes in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the allocation schedule that Buyer delivers to Seller within sixty (60) days after the Closing. Seller may object in writing within thirty (30) days after delivery. The Parties shall resolve any objection in good faith and shall file all tax returns consistently with the final allocation.

**2.8 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from the Purchase Price any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax law. Any amounts so withheld shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by the electronic exchange of documents and signatures on October 30, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Lease Assignment, duly executed by Seller, with the Landlord Consent attached;

(c) a certificate of the secretary or manager of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of managers authorizing the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents;

(d) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c);

(e) payoff letters and Encumbrance releases, in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, for all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets; and

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.6;

(b) the Lease Assignment, duly executed by Buyer;

(c) a certificate of the secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing the transactions contemplated by this Agreement; and

(d) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that the statements in this Article IV are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York, and has full limited liability company power and authority to own, operate and lease the Purchased Assets and to carry on the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents, to perform its obligations under them and to consummate the transactions contemplated by them. The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents have been duly authorized by all requisite action of Seller's board of managers and members. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents do not and will not (a) conflict with Seller's certificate of formation or operating agreement, (b) violate any law or order applicable to Seller, the Business or the Purchased Assets, or (c) result in a breach of, constitute a default under, or give rise to any right of termination, acceleration or modification of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Consents.** Except for the Landlord Consent and except as set forth in Schedule 4.4, no consent, approval, waiver or authorization of, or notice to, any Governmental Authority or other Person is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement or the Ancillary Documents, or the assignment of any Assigned Contract to Buyer.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in or license to use, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those to be released at or before the Closing.

**4.6 Sufficiency of Assets.** The Purchased Assets constitute all of the rights, property and assets necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach of or default under any Assigned Contract. Seller has delivered to Buyer complete and correct copies of each Assigned Contract, including all amendments.

**4.8 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 2.1(b) lists all registered and material unregistered intellectual property owned by Seller and used in the Business. The conduct of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property of any Person, and no claim to that effect is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened.

**4.9 Inventory.** The inventory included in the Purchased Assets consists of items of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course of the Business, and is not damaged, defective or obsolete beyond reserves reflected in Seller's books.

**4.10 Financial Statements.** The unaudited balance sheet and statement of income of the Business as of and for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, and for the six months ended June 30, 2026, delivered to Buyer, fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.11 Absence of Changes.** Since June 30, 2026, Seller has operated the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.12 Legal Proceedings.** There is no action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets, or that challenges the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**4.13 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has complied in all material respects with all laws applicable to the Business and the Purchased Assets, including laws relating to product labeling, chemical restrictions in textiles and import and customs matters.

**4.14 Taxes.** All tax returns required to be filed by Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets have been timely filed, and all taxes shown as due on them have been paid. There are no tax Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller that:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware, and has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Buyer and constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Legal Proceedings.** There is no action pending or, to Buyer's knowledge, threatened against Buyer that challenges the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and licensors of the Business. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Notice of Certain Events.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that has caused, or would reasonably be expected to cause, any representation or warranty of Seller to be untrue or any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.4 Efforts.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to satisfy the conditions to the Closing set forth in Article VII.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not required to, offer employment effective on the Closing Date to employees of Seller who work primarily in the Business. Seller shall be responsible for all wages, bonuses, accrued paid time off and other compensation of those employees through the Closing Date.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent that information becomes publicly available other than through Seller's breach.

**6.7 Bulk Sales Laws.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales law. Any liability arising from noncompliance is an Excluded Liability.

**6.8 Non-Competition.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (a) design, manufacture, market or sell technical outerwear shell products anywhere in North America or Europe, (b) solicit any customer or supplier of the Business to cease or reduce its business with Buyer, or (c) solicit for employment any employee of the Business hired by Buyer, other than through general solicitations not targeted at such employees. Seller acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 6.8 are reasonable and necessary to protect the goodwill acquired by Buyer.

**6.9 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of the Transaction Documents.

**6.10 Public Announcements.** No Party shall make any public announcement regarding this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the condition that no Governmental Authority has enacted or issued any law or order that restrains or prohibits the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's written waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer has received a certificate, signed by an authorized officer of Seller, certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) Seller has delivered the Landlord Consent;

(e) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred since the date of this Agreement; and

(f) Seller has delivered each of the items in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's written waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Seller has received a certificate, signed by an authorized officer of Buyer, certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b) have been satisfied; and

(d) Buyer has delivered each of the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.5 and 4.14 survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document, or (c) any Excluded Liability.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**8.4 Limitations.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) until the aggregate Losses exceed $184,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed $1,840,000. These limitations do not apply to Losses arising from fraud or from any inaccuracy in or breach of the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.5 or 4.14, as to which Seller's aggregate liability shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**8.5 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**8.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article VIII is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**8.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing (a) by mutual written agreement of the Parties; (b) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; (c) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or (d) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, unless the failure results from the terminating Party's breach.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination, this Agreement becomes void and there is no liability on the part of any Party, except that Sections 6.10 and Article XI survive, and no termination relieves a Party from liability for any willful breach.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use, documentary and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Proration.** Personal property taxes and similar periodic taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with the Transaction Documents.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or to such other address as a Party specifies by notice).

**11.3 Interpretation; Headings.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**11.5 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Buyer may not assign any of its rights or delegate any of its obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of Seller. Seller may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the consent of Buyer to any Affiliate or to any Person acquiring all or substantially all of Seller's remaining assets. Any such assignment shall include Seller's obligations under Article VIII (Indemnification) and Section 6.8 (Non-Competition), and upon such assignment Seller shall be released from those obligations. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 11.5 is void.

**11.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**11.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim of inconvenient forum.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any inconsistency between this Agreement and any Ancillary Document, the Ancillary Documents control as to matters specifically addressed therein; otherwise the Asset Purchase Agreement controls.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY IT.

**11.11 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of Section 6.6 or Section 6.8 were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that Buyer is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy, without posting bond.

**11.12 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article VIII, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in it confers any right or remedy on any other Person.

**11.13 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
