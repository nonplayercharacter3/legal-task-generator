# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made, executed and delivered on October 30, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**") by **Risel Alpine Gear, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Stepair Outfitters, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets, and Buyer has agreed to purchase them, for a purchase price of $18,400,000, plus Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale to Buyer at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

2.1 **Transfer of Purchased Assets.** Effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer and its successors and assigns, and Buyer accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Purchased Assets include:

(a) all finished goods, work in process, raw materials, laminated fabrics, trims and other inventory of the Business;

(b) all intellectual property owned by Seller and used in the Business, including the items listed on Schedule 2.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement, together with all associated goodwill;

(c) the Assigned Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) of the Purchase Agreement, including the Membrane Technology License Agreement, being the license dated March 3, 2023 between Fjellbrand AG and Seller;

(d) the tooling, sample-room equipment, sewing and seam-taping machinery, furniture, fixtures and computer equipment listed on Schedule 2.1(d) of the Purchase Agreement;

(e) all customer lists, supplier lists, wholesale account records, product testing data and other books and records of the Business; and

(f) all goodwill of the Business as a going concern.

2.2 **Studio Lease.** Seller's interest in the Studio Lease is transferred to Buyer by the Lease Assignment delivered at the Closing, with the Landlord Consent attached. This Bill of Sale also conveys that interest, to the extent not conveyed by the Lease Assignment.

2.3 **Excluded Assets.** Seller does not sell, assign, transfer or convey to Buyer, and Seller retains, all of its right, title and interest in, to and under the Excluded Assets.

## 3. NO ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITIES

This Bill of Sale transfers assets only. Buyer does not assume any liability or obligation of Seller under this Bill of Sale. Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities is governed by Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Seller retains all Excluded Liabilities as provided in Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. POWER OF ATTORNEY

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution. Buyer may act in the name of Seller, but for the benefit and at the expense of Buyer, to:

(a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) institute and prosecute any proceeding that Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets; and

(c) endorse any checks or other instruments payable to Seller that relate to the Purchased Assets.

This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES; PURCHASE AGREEMENT

5.1 **Further Assurances.** Consistent with Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall, at Buyer's request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such further deeds, assignments, endorsements and other instruments of transfer, and take such further actions, as Buyer reasonably requests to vest in Buyer good title to the Purchased Assets.

5.2 **Purchase Agreement.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of the parties in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale and survive its delivery as provided in the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and any claim, controversy or cause of action arising out of or relating to the conveyance of, or title to, any of the Purchased Assets, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

6.2 **Jurisdiction.** Any suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, or to the conveyance of, or title to, any of the Purchased Assets, shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. Seller and Buyer each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of that court and waive any objection to venue there and any claim of inconvenient forum.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

7.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns, and benefits Buyer and its successors and assigns.

7.2 **Headings.** Headings are for reference only and do not affect the interpretation of this Bill of Sale.

7.3 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
