# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF RISEL ALPINE GEAR, LLC

**Dated: September 11, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Risel Alpine Gear, LLC**, a New York limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act by written consent without a meeting under Section 407 of the New York Limited Liability Company Law and Section 6.7 of the Company's Amended and Restated Operating Agreement (the "**Operating Agreement**"). The undersigned consent to the adoption of the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if they had been duly adopted at a meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. RECITALS

1.1 **The Business.** The Company designs, develops, sources, markets and sells technical outerwear shell jackets, pants and related shell products (the "**Business**"). The Company also operates an insulated apparel line and a footwear line, which are not part of the Business.

1.2 **Proposed Transaction.** The Company has negotiated the sale of substantially all of the assets of the Business to **Stepair Outfitters, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). The sale is to be made under an Asset Purchase Agreement between the Company and Buyer, in substantially the form presented to the Board (the "**Asset Purchase Agreement**"). The aggregate purchase price is $18,400,000, payable in cash at closing, plus Buyer's assumption of certain specified liabilities. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Asset Purchase Agreement.

1.3 **Ancillary Documents.** The Asset Purchase Agreement contemplates that, at the Closing, the Company will deliver the following documents (together, the "**Ancillary Documents**"):

(a) a Bill of Sale conveying the Purchased Assets to Buyer;

(b) a Lease Assignment of the Studio Lease for Suite 4B, 118 Carroll Basin Street, Brooklyn, New York, with the written consent of Kestrow Canal Properties LLC, as landlord, attached; and

(c) the officer's and secretary's certificates and the other instruments of transfer described in Article III of the Asset Purchase Agreement.

1.4 **Membrane Technology License.** Among the Purchased Assets is the Company's interest in the license dated March 3, 2023 between Fjellbrand AG ("**Fjellbrand**") and the Company (the "**Membrane Technology License Agreement**"). This license grants the Company rights to the waterproof-breathable membrane technology incorporated in the shell product line.

(a) Under Section 14.2 of the Membrane Technology License Agreement, the Company may not assign the license, or any of its rights under it, without the prior written consent of Fjellbrand. This restriction applies to any assignment by operation of law and to any assignment made in connection with a sale of the shell business or of all or substantially all of the assets to which the license relates.

(b) Under Section 14.3 of the Membrane Technology License Agreement, Fjellbrand may terminate the license on written notice if any assignment is made without that consent.

1.5 **Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the following materials:

(a) the Asset Purchase Agreement and the forms of the Ancillary Documents;

(b) a summary of the principal terms of the transaction prepared by management; and

(c) the Membrane Technology License Agreement and the other Assigned Contracts.

The Board has also consulted with the Company's legal and financial advisors.

1.6 **Board Determination.** The Board has determined that the sale of the Business on the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement is advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its members.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions:

## 2. RESOLUTION 1: APPROVAL OF THE ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

2.1 **Approval.** The Asset Purchase Agreement, in substantially the form presented to the Board, and the transactions it contemplates are approved and adopted in all respects. These transactions include the sale, assignment and transfer of the Purchased Assets to Buyer for a purchase price of $18,400,000 and Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

2.2 **Execution.** The Chief Executive Officer, the President and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Asset Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Company. Execution by an Authorized Officer conclusively evidences that officer's approval of any changes from the form presented to the Board, provided those changes do not reduce the purchase price.

## 3. RESOLUTION 2: ANCILLARY DOCUMENTS

3.1 **Approval.** The Bill of Sale, the Lease Assignment and each other Ancillary Document, in the forms contemplated by the Asset Purchase Agreement, are approved.

3.2 **Execution.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver each Ancillary Document in the name and on behalf of the Company, with such changes as that officer approves. The officer's execution conclusively evidences that approval.

## 4. RESOLUTION 3: CLOSING MATTERS

4.1 **Consummation of the Closing.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized and directed to consummate the Closing on the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement. This authority includes the delivery of all items listed in Section 3.2 of the Asset Purchase Agreement and the receipt of the purchase price by wire transfer.

4.2 **Transfer of the Membrane Technology License Agreement.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to assign and convey the Membrane Technology License Agreement to Buyer at the Closing as a Purchased Asset under the Bill of Sale. The Authorized Officers may proceed to close without first obtaining the prior written consent of Fjellbrand, and may deliver the Bill of Sale and the other Ancillary Documents at the Closing on that basis.

4.3 **Studio Lease.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to request and obtain the Landlord Consent from Kestrow Canal Properties LLC, and to deliver the Lease Assignment at the Closing with the Landlord Consent attached.

4.4 **Closing Certificates.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver the officer's certificate required by Section 7.2(c) of the Asset Purchase Agreement. The Secretary of the Company is authorized to execute and deliver the certificate described in Section 3.2(c) of the Asset Purchase Agreement, certifying these resolutions.

4.5 **Encumbrance Releases.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to obtain payoff letters and releases of all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets, and to apply the proceeds of the purchase price to satisfy the related indebtedness at the Closing.

## 5. RESOLUTION 4: MEMBER APPROVAL

5.1 **Recommendation.** The Board recommends that the members of the Company approve the sale of the Business under the Asset Purchase Agreement, as required by Section 8.3 of the Operating Agreement.

5.2 **Solicitation of Member Consent.** The Secretary is directed to submit the transaction to the members for approval by written consent.

## 6. RESOLUTION 5: GENERAL AUTHORITY

6.1 **Further Actions.** Each Authorized Officer is authorized to take all further actions in the name and on behalf of the Company that the officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions. These actions include:

(a) executing and delivering all further agreements, instruments, certificates and notices;

(b) paying all fees and expenses; and

(c) making all filings with any Governmental Authority.

6.2 **Ratification.** All actions previously taken by any manager, officer or agent of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 7. EFFECTIVENESS; COUNTERPARTS

7.1 **Effectiveness and Filing.** This written consent is effective as of the date first written above. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

7.2 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This written consent may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
