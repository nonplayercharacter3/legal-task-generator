# OFFICE LEASE

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Kestrow Canal Properties LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Risel Alpine Gear, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Tenant**").

## 1. PREMISES AND BUILDING

1.1 **Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the space known as Suite 4B, 118 Carroll Basin Street, Brooklyn, New York (the "**Premises**"). The Premises are located on the fourth floor of the building at that address (the "**Building**"). The Premises contain approximately 7,800 rentable square feet, as shown on the floor plan attached as Exhibit A. The parties agree to that rentable area for all purposes of this Lease, and it is not subject to remeasurement.

1.2 **Common Areas.** Tenant has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobby, elevators, freight elevator, loading dock, stairways, restrooms on multi-tenant floors and other common areas of the Building (the "**Common Areas**"). This right is subject to the rules and regulations in Section 9.3.

1.3 **Condition of Premises.** Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date, except for the Landlord's Work described in Exhibit B. Landlord shall complete the Landlord's Work in a good and workmanlike manner before the Commencement Date.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Term.** The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") begins on March 1, 2024 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ends at 11:59 p.m. on February 28, 2031 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless terminated earlier under this Lease.

2.2 **Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term for one additional period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**"). To do so, Tenant must give Landlord written notice at least twelve (12) months before the Expiration Date, and Tenant must not be in default beyond any applicable cure period when it gives that notice. Base Rent for the first year of the Renewal Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the fair market rent for comparable space in comparable buildings in the Gowanus and Carroll Gardens submarkets of Brooklyn, as determined under Exhibit C. Base Rent will increase by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the start of the Renewal Term.

2.3 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the Term ends without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. Tenant shall pay use and occupancy at a monthly rate of one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect for the last month of the Term, prorated on a daily basis. Tenant is also liable for Landlord's actual damages arising from any holdover that continues more than sixty (60) days after Landlord gives Tenant written notice that it requires possession.

## 3. RENT

3.1 **Base Rent.** Beginning on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay monthly base rent of $21,750 ("**Base Rent**"), in advance, on the first day of each calendar month during the Term, without demand, setoff or deduction except as expressly provided in this Lease. Base Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

3.2 **Base Rent Adjustment.** Beginning on March 1, 2029, and on March 1, 2030, Base Rent will increase by three percent (3%) over the Base Rent in effect immediately before that date.

3.3 **Additional Rent.** All sums other than Base Rent that Tenant owes under this Lease are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent together are "**Rent**." Landlord has the same remedies for non-payment of Additional Rent as it has for non-payment of Base Rent.

3.4 **Real Estate Taxes.** Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Real Estate Taxes for each tax year exceed the Real Estate Taxes for the 2024/2025 tax year (the "**Base Tax Year**"). "**Tenant's Proportionate Share**" is 6.5%. "**Real Estate Taxes**" means all real property taxes and assessments imposed on the Building and the land on which it stands, net of any abatement or refund actually received by Landlord. Landlord shall deliver a statement of the amount due. Tenant shall pay that amount in equal monthly installments with Base Rent or, for amounts billed in arrears, within thirty (30) days after receipt of the statement.

3.5 **Operating Expenses.** Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses for calendar year 2024. "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, clean, maintain, repair and insure the Building and the Common Areas. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures (other than the annual amortized portion of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Effective Date), debt service, ground rent, leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, Landlord's income and franchise taxes, and costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses. Tenant may audit Landlord's records for that year on thirty (30) days' notice, within one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of the statement.

3.6 **Late Charge.** If Tenant does not pay any installment of Rent within five (5) business days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to four percent (4%) of the overdue amount.

3.7 **Payment.** Tenant shall pay Rent by ACH transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing, or by check delivered to Landlord at its notice address.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 **Amount.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord $65,250, equal to three (3) months of Base Rent, as security for Tenant's performance (the "**Security Deposit**").

4.2 **Holding and Application.** Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with Section 7-103 of the New York General Obligations Law. If Tenant defaults beyond any applicable cure period, Landlord may apply all or part of the Security Deposit to cure the default. Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within ten (10) days after Landlord's written demand.

4.3 **Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the Expiration Date and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in the condition required by Section 12.1.

4.4 **Transfer.** On an assignment of this Lease permitted under Article 8, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the account of the assignee, provided the assignee delivers a replacement security deposit or Tenant authorizes the transfer in writing.

## 5. USE

5.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for executive and administrative offices, apparel design studio, pattern-making and sample-room use (including sewing, cutting and seam-taping equipment customary for apparel sample production), product showroom use by appointment, and uses incidental to them (the "**Permitted Use**"). Tenant shall not conduct retail sales to the public or production manufacturing at the Premises.

5.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the structure of the Building, the Building systems and the Common Areas, except to the extent compliance is required because of Tenant's particular use or alterations.

5.3 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Premises any hazardous materials other than customary office and cleaning supplies and materials ordinarily used in apparel sample-room operations, in quantities customary for that use and in compliance with law.

5.4 **Access.** Tenant has access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's reasonable security procedures.

## 6. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

6.1 **Landlord Services.** Landlord shall provide (a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on business days and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays; (b) passenger elevator service at all times and freight elevator service on business days during the same hours; (c) cleaning of the Common Areas; and (d) water for ordinary lavatory and pantry purposes.

6.2 **Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is separately metered. Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly for all electricity consumed at the Premises.

6.3 **Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by repairs, accidents, labor disputes or events beyond Landlord's reasonable control. However, if an interruption caused by Landlord's negligence renders the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive business days, Rent abates from the sixth business day until the services are restored.

## 7. ALTERATIONS, REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

7.1 **Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations (such as painting and carpeting) costing less than $25,000 in any calendar year that do not affect the structure or Building systems. All alterations must be performed by licensed and insured contractors in compliance with law.

7.2 **Tenant Repairs.** Tenant shall keep the interior, non-structural portions of the Premises and Tenant's equipment in good order and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted.

7.3 **Landlord Repairs.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the roof, foundation, exterior walls, structural elements, Common Areas and base Building mechanical, electrical, plumbing and life-safety systems up to the point of connection to the Premises.

7.4 **Trade Fixtures.** Tenant's movable furniture, equipment and trade fixtures, including sewing machines, seam-taping machines, cutting tables, pattern plotters and computer equipment, remain Tenant's property. Tenant may remove them at any time, provided Tenant repairs any damage caused by their removal.

## 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease, or sublet all or any part of the Premises, without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its consent.

8.2 **Request for Consent.** Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of any proposed assignment or sublease at least twenty (20) days before its proposed effective date. The notice must identify the proposed assignee or subtenant and include its most recent financial statements, a description of its intended use and the principal terms of the transaction. Landlord shall respond within fifteen (15) days after receipt of a complete request.

8.3 **Sale of Business.** Landlord shall not withhold its consent to an assignment of this Lease to a Person acquiring all or substantially all of the assets of Tenant's business conducted at the Premises if (a) the assignee will use the Premises for the Permitted Use, (b) the assignee has a tangible net worth, immediately after the transaction, at least equal to that of Tenant on the Effective Date, and (c) the assignee assumes in writing all of Tenant's obligations under this Lease arising from and after the effective date of the assignment.

8.4 **Effect of Assignment.** Any assignment must be by a written instrument under which the assignee assumes Tenant's obligations under this Lease, and a fully executed copy must be delivered to Landlord. On an assignment made with Landlord's consent under Section 8.3, Tenant is released from obligations under this Lease first arising after the effective date of the assignment, but remains liable for all obligations arising before that date.

8.5 **Costs.** Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in reviewing any request for consent, not to exceed $2,500 per request.

## 9. INSURANCE; INDEMNITY; RULES

9.1 **Tenant Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain, throughout the Term, (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, plus umbrella coverage of at least $5,000,000, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's improvements, equipment and personal property for their full replacement cost; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

9.2 **Landlord Insurance; Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance on the Common Areas. Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other for any loss covered by the property insurance it is required to carry.

9.3 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the Building rules and regulations attached as Exhibit D, as Landlord may reasonably amend them on written notice, provided they are applied in a non-discriminatory manner and do not materially interfere with the Permitted Use.

9.4 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.2, each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other from all claims for bodily injury or property damage to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

10.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's improvements and property) with reasonable diligence, and Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable until restoration is substantially complete. If restoration cannot reasonably be completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after Landlord delivers its contractor's estimate of the restoration period. This Section 10.1 is an express agreement governing casualty under Section 227 of the New York Real Property Law.

10.2 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct the Permitted Use, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate claim for its moving expenses and trade fixtures, if that claim does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

11.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within that period, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant files a voluntary bankruptcy petition, or an involuntary petition is filed against Tenant and not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

11.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by giving Tenant at least ten (10) days' written notice, and may recover possession of the Premises by summary proceedings or other lawful means. Tenant remains liable for Rent through the date of termination and for damages equal to the Rent for the balance of the Term, less the net rents Landlord actually collects on reletting. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to relet the Premises to mitigate its damages.

11.3 **Landlord Default.** Landlord is in default if it fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, or within a longer period reasonably required if Landlord begins the cure within that period and diligently completes it.

## 12. SURRENDER; ESTOPPEL; SUBORDINATION

12.1 **Surrender.** On the Expiration Date or earlier termination, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good order, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall remove its trade fixtures and personal property.

12.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within ten (10) business days after the other's written request, deliver a certificate confirming whether this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates through which Rent has been paid, and whether, to the certifying party's knowledge, any default exists.

12.3 **Subordination and Non-Disturbance.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided the mortgagee delivers a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists.

12.4 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term, subject to the terms of this Lease.

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS

13.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Lease must be in writing and are effective on delivery by hand or nationally recognized overnight courier to the addresses below (or another address a party designates by notice):

*To Landlord:* Kestrow Canal Properties LLC, 118 Carroll Basin Street, Management Office, Brooklyn, New York, Attention: Property Manager.

*To Tenant:* Risel Alpine Gear, LLC, Suite 4B, 118 Carroll Basin Street, Brooklyn, New York, Attention: Chief Financial Officer.

13.2 **Brokers.** Each party represents that it dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than those identified in Exhibit E, whose commissions Landlord shall pay under separate agreement.

13.3 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

13.4 **Successors and Assigns.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

13.5 **Amendments.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant.

13.6 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Building, including the rents and proceeds from it.

13.7 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** LANDLORD AND TENANT EACH WAIVE TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS LEASE, OTHER THAN ANY CLAIM FOR PERSONAL INJURY OR PROPERTY DAMAGE.

13.8 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.

## EXHIBIT C: FAIR MARKET RENT DETERMINATION

Landlord shall notify Tenant of its determination of fair market rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's renewal notice. If they do not agree, each party shall appoint a licensed New York commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in Brooklyn office leasing, and the two brokers shall select a third. The third broker shall choose whichever of the parties' determinations is closer to fair market rent, and that choice is binding. Each party bears the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third.
