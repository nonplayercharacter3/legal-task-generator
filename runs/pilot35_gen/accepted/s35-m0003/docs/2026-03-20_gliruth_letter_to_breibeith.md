## 1. Notice of Non-Renewal

Under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, Gliruth gives Breibeith written notice of non-renewal of the Agreement. The Initial Term under Section 12.1 is three years, ending June 30, 2026. Gliruth does not intend to continue the Agreement beyond that date and will not accept a Renewal Term.

Gliruth disputes that any automatic renewal of the Agreement has occurred or will occur. Gliruth does not accept any invoice, fee adjustment notice under Section 4.4, or other communication that treats the Agreement as continuing after June 30, 2026. Gliruth reserves all of its rights on this point.

## 2. Transition and Return of Licensee Data

Gliruth expects to continue using the Software under the Agreement through June 30, 2026 and to keep paying undisputed amounts as they come due. Gliruth asks that Breibeith confirm in writing, within fifteen (15) business days after the date of this letter, the procedure and timing it will follow under Section 7.4 to make Licensee Data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format. Gliruth also asks Breibeith to name a point of contact for transition planning.

Gliruth's operations team would like to schedule a call in April 2026 to discuss export file formats for inventory records, event bookings, delivery schedules and dispatch history. Gliruth would also like to discuss the timing of the final export so that its peak spring and early-summer event season is not disrupted.

## 3. Corporate Information

For information only, Gliruth advises Breibeith that on March 12, 2026, Gliruth signed a merger agreement under which Gliruth is to be acquired by Ostrander Venue Group, Inc. ("**Ostrander**"). The transaction remains subject to customary closing conditions. Gliruth will continue to operate its event furniture and staging rental business in the ordinary course until closing.

We give this information in confidence. Breibeith should treat it as Gliruth's Confidential Information under Section 15 of the Agreement. Please do not disclose it beyond those of Breibeith's personnel and advisers who need to know it.
