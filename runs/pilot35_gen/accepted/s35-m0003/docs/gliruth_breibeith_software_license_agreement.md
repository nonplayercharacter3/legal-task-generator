## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops and licenses RentFlow, a hosted inventory, booking and dispatch platform for rental businesses.

## 4. FEES

4.1 **Annual License Fee.** Licensee shall pay Licensor a license fee of $384,000 per year (the "**Annual License Fee**"). The Annual License Fee covers up to 150 named Authorized Users and standard support under the Quality Agreement.

4.2 **Additional Users.** Licensee may add Authorized Users above the number in Section 4.1 at Licensor's then-current per-user rate, prorated for the remainder of the applicable contract year.

4.4 **Fee Adjustments.** Licensor may increase the Annual License Fee for any Renewal Term by giving written notice at least sixty (60) days before the Renewal Term begins. No single increase will exceed five percent (5%) of the Annual License Fee for the preceding contract year.

## 5. INVOICING, PAYMENT AND TAXES

5.1 **Invoicing.** Licensor will invoice the Annual License Fee annually in advance, on or about the first day of each contract year. Other fees will be invoiced monthly in arrears.

5.2 **Payment.** Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Overdue amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Licensor in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay all such taxes, other than taxes based on Licensor's net income, property or employees.

6.2 **Service Standards.** Licensor shall provide the Software and related support in accordance with the uptime, data-accuracy and support standards set out in the Quality Agreement.

6.3 **Updates.** Licensor shall make available to Licensee, at no additional charge, all updates and new releases of the Software that it makes generally available to its licensees.
