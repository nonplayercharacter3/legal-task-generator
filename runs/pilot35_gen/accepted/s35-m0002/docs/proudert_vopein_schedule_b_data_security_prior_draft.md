# SCHEDULE B – DATA SECURITY

**to the Software License Agreement between Vopein Systems LLC and Proudert Technical Fabrics, Inc.**

*Draft of February 20, 2026. Circulated for discussion. Not for execution.*

## 1. Purpose and Application

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule B sets out the data security requirements that Licensor shall meet under Section 8.2 of the Agreement. They apply whenever Licensor or its personnel access Licensee Systems or Licensee Data, whether remotely or at the Licensed Site.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule B have the meanings given in the Agreement. These include "Licensee Data," "Licensee Systems," "Licensed Site," "Services," "Software" and "Support Services." In this Schedule B:

(a) "**Licensor Personnel**" means Licensor's employees and any subcontractors permitted under Section 7, who access Licensee Systems or Licensee Data.

(b) "**Remote Access**" means access to Licensee Systems or Licensee Data from any location other than the Licensed Site.

(c) "**Security Incident**" means any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Licensee Data in Licensor's possession or control.

1.3 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Schedule B forms part of the Agreement as one of its Schedules. It adds to, and does not limit, Licensor's obligations under Sections 8 and 9 of the Agreement.

## 2. Information Security Program

2.1 **Program.** Licensor shall maintain a written information security program. The program shall include administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Licensee Data and to the Services and Support Services Licensor provides.

2.2 **Standards.** Licensor's program shall be consistent with a recognized industry framework, such as ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.

2.3 **Responsible Officer.** Licensor shall designate an individual responsible for its information security program. It shall give Licensee that individual's name and contact details on request.

2.4 **Review.** Licensor shall review its information security program at least once a year, and after any material change to its business or systems. It shall update the program as needed to address identified risks.

## 3. Personnel

3.1 **Need to Know.** Licensor shall limit access to Licensee Systems and Licensee Data to Licensor Personnel who need that access to provide the Services or the Support Services.

3.2 **Screening.** Before granting any Licensor Personnel access to Licensee Systems or Licensee Data, Licensor shall conduct background checks on them to the extent permitted by applicable law.

3.3 **Training.** Licensor shall ensure that all Licensor Personnel receive security awareness training before first access and at least once a year after that.

3.4 **Confidentiality.** All Licensor Personnel shall be bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than Section 9 of the Agreement.

3.5 **Removal of Access.** Licensor shall notify Licensee within two (2) business days when any Licensor Personnel with credentials to Licensee Systems no longer requires access. Licensee may then disable those credentials.

## 4. Access Controls

4.1 **Individual Accounts.** Each Licensor Personnel shall use individual credentials to access Licensee Systems. Shared or generic accounts shall not be used unless Licensee approves in writing.

4.2 **Authentication.** Licensor Personnel shall use multi-factor authentication for all Remote Access.

4.3 **Least Privilege.** Licensor shall request only the access rights needed for the task at hand. Elevated or administrative access shall be used only for the time needed to complete the relevant task.

4.4 **Licensee Procedures.** Licensor Personnel shall comply with Licensee's reasonable written security procedures for access to Licensee Systems, as Licensee provides them to Licensor in advance.

## 5. Remote Access

5.1 **Approved Methods.** Remote Access shall take place only through connection methods approved by Licensee, such as Licensee's virtual private network or a Licensee-approved remote support tool.

5.2 **Logging.** Licensor shall keep a log of Remote Access sessions by Licensor Personnel. The log shall record the individual, the date and time, and the purpose of each session. Licensor shall retain the log for at least twelve (12) months and provide a copy to Licensee on reasonable request.

5.3 **Endpoint Security.** Devices that Licensor Personnel use for Remote Access shall run supported operating systems, current security patches, anti-malware software and full-disk encryption.

## 6. Data Handling

6.1 **Use.** Licensor shall access and use Licensee Data only as permitted by Section 8.1 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Minimization.** Licensor shall not copy or extract Licensee Data from Licensee Systems unless doing so is reasonably necessary to provide the Services or the Support Services.

6.3 **Encryption.** Licensor shall encrypt any Licensee Data that it holds outside Licensee Systems, both in transit and at rest, using industry-standard encryption.

6.4 **Storage Location.** Licensor shall store any Licensee Data held outside Licensee Systems only in the United States.

6.5 **Return and Deletion.** Return and deletion of Licensee Data on expiration or termination are governed by Section 8.4 of the Agreement. Before then, Licensor shall delete any copies of Licensee Data held outside Licensee Systems once they are no longer needed for the purpose for which they were made.

## 7. Subcontractors

7.1 **Consent.** Licensor shall not permit any subcontractor to access Licensee Systems or Licensee Data without Licensee's prior written consent. Licensee shall not unreasonably withhold that consent.

7.2 **Flow-Down.** Licensor shall bind each approved subcontractor in writing to data security obligations no less protective than this Schedule B. Licensor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 8. Software Security

8.1 **Secure Development.** Licensor shall maintain secure software development practices for the Software. These shall include code review and testing for common vulnerabilities before each release.

8.2 **Vulnerability Management.** Licensor shall assess known vulnerabilities in the Software and make fixes available to Licensee as Updates under Section 4.1 of the Agreement. Fixes for critical vulnerabilities shall be made available as soon as reasonably practicable.

8.3 **Malicious Code.** Licensor's obligations regarding malicious code are set out in Section 10.3 of the Agreement.

## 9. Security Incidents

9.1 **Notice.** Licensor shall notify Licensee of any Security Incident without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of it, as required by Section 8.3 of the Agreement.

9.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice shall describe, to the extent known: (a) the nature of the Security Incident; (b) the categories of Licensee Data affected; (c) the steps Licensor has taken to contain it; and (d) a contact person at Licensor. Licensor shall update Licensee as further information becomes available.

9.3 **Cooperation.** Licensor shall reasonably cooperate with Licensee in investigating and remediating each Security Incident, as provided in Section 8.3 of the Agreement.

## 10. Verification

10.1 **Security Questionnaire.** Not more than once a year, Licensee may ask Licensor to complete a reasonable written security questionnaire. Licensor shall complete it within thirty (30) days.

10.2 **Third-Party Reports.** If Licensor holds a current independent audit report or certification covering its information security program, such as a SOC 2 Type II report or an ISO/IEC 27001 certificate, it shall provide a copy to Licensee on request. Licensee shall treat that copy as Licensor's Confidential Information.

10.3 **Remediation.** If a questionnaire response or report shows a material gap against this Schedule B, Licensor shall give Licensee a written remediation plan within thirty (30) days and carry it out within a reasonable time.
