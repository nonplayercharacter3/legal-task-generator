# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is dated September 8, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**"). It is entered into by **Vopein Systems LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("**Vopein**" or "**Licensor**"), and **Proudert Technical Fabrics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Proudert**" or "**Licensee**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensee licenses the LoomSight Software for use at the Licensed Site.

B. Licensor has made available the LoomSight Vision Inspection Module. This module provides automated, camera-based surface inspection of woven fabric.

C. Licensee wishes to add the Vision Inspection Module to the Software licensed under the Agreement. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement as set out in this Amendment.

In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Authorized User.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement is amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

> 1.2 "**Authorized User**," as used in the Agreement and this Amendment, means: (a) a Licensee employee at the Licensed Site issued individual LoomSight login credentials; (b) each contractor, consultant or other individual engaged by or on behalf of Licensee who has system access to the Software, whether through individual login credentials, shared credentials or an interface; and (c) each networked inspection camera or sensor station at the Licensed Site that captures, transmits or receives data through the Software.

1.2 **Vision Inspection Module.** The following definition is added to Section 1 of the Agreement as Section 1.14:

> 1.14 "**Vision Inspection Module**" means the LoomSight Vision Inspection Module described in item 5 of Schedule A.

1.3 **Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

## 2. VISION INSPECTION MODULE

2.1 **Addition to Schedule A.** Schedule A of the Agreement is amended by adding the following item 5:

> 5. LoomSight Vision Inspection Module: automated image capture of fabric surfaces during weaving and finishing, machine-learning defect detection and classification, defect mapping by roll position, and transfer of detected defects to LoomSight Quality for logging and roll grading.

From the Amendment Date, the Vision Inspection Module forms part of the Software and is licensed under Section 2.1 of the Agreement for use at the Licensed Site.

2.2 **Module Fee.** Licensee shall pay Licensor a fee for the Vision Inspection Module of $145 per Authorized User per month (the "**Module Fee**"). The Module Fee is payable in addition to the annual core license fee under Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Invoicing.** Licensor shall invoice the Module Fee quarterly in arrears, beginning with the calendar quarter in which the Amendment Date falls. Each invoice shall state the highest number of Authorized Users during each month of the quarter, as shown by the usage report described in Section 6.2 of the Agreement. The Module Fee for any partial month at the start of the module subscription shall be prorated on a daily basis.

2.4 **Payment and Taxes.** Payment of the Module Fee and invoice disputes are governed by Section 5.3 of the Agreement. Taxes are governed by Section 5.5 of the Agreement.

2.5 **Support.** The Support Services under Section 4 of the Agreement apply to the Vision Inspection Module from the Amendment Date. No separate support fee is payable for the Vision Inspection Module.

2.6 **Warranty.** The Software warranty in Section 10.1 of the Agreement applies to the Vision Inspection Module. For the Vision Inspection Module, the twelve (12) month warranty period begins on the date Licensee first uses the Vision Inspection Module in production at the Licensed Site.

2.7 **Hardware.** Cameras, lighting, mounting hardware, sensor stations and related network equipment for the Vision Inspection Module are Licensee Systems. They are provided and maintained by Licensee, except as otherwise agreed in a Statement of Work.

## 3. IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

3.1 Any installation, configuration, model training, integration or other Services that Licensor performs for the Vision Inspection Module shall be described in a Statement of Work or in a change order under Section 3.5 of the Agreement. Fees for those Services shall be stated in the applicable Statement of Work or change order.

## 4. GENERAL

4.1 **Effect of Amendment.** This Amendment is an amendment signed by both Parties within the meaning of Sections 15.5 and 15.7 of the Agreement. From the Amendment Date, references in the Agreement to "this Agreement" include this Amendment. Except as stated in this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

4.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 15.2 and 15.3 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment.

4.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
