## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. These include "Affiliate," "Authorized User," "Effective Date," "Fees," "Licensed Site," "Software" and "Term."

1.2 References to Sections of the Agreement are to the numbered sections of the Agreement as executed on the Effective Date.

## 2. Core License Fee Discount

2.1 **Discount.** In consideration of Licensee's commitments in Section 3, Licensor grants Licensee a discount of twelve percent (12%) on the annual core license fee payable under Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

2.2 **Application.** The annual core license fee for the Base Tier under Section 5.1 of the Agreement is $186,000. After the discount, Licensee will pay $163,680 per year for the Base Tier, which is a reduction of $22,320. If Licensee moves to a higher tier under Section 5.1 or Section 5.4 of the Agreement, the twelve percent (12%) discount will apply to the annual core license fee for that tier.

2.3 **Invoicing.** Licensor will show the discount as a separate line item on each invoice issued under Section 5.2 of the Agreement. The discount does not apply to services fees under any Statement of Work, to expenses under Section 5.6 of the Agreement, or to taxes under Section 5.5 of the Agreement.

2.4 **Duration of Discount.** The discount applies to each annual core license fee invoice issued during the Term.

## 3. Licensee Commitments

3.1 **Exclusive Use.** During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months after its expiration or termination, Licensee shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, license, develop or use any competing manufacturing execution or quality inspection software at any facility owned or operated by Licensee or any of its Affiliates, anywhere in the world.

3.2 **Scope.** For the purposes of Section 3.1, competing manufacturing execution or quality inspection software includes any software, platform or hosted service, whether obtained from a third party or developed internally, that provides production order management, machine or loom scheduling, work-in-process tracking, defect logging, product grading, statistical process control or automated inspection functions that are substantially similar to those of the Software.

3.3 **Reference Customer.** During the Term, Licensee agrees to act as a reference customer for Licensor. Licensee will take part in no more than four (4) reference calls or site visits per year, scheduled at mutually agreed times. Licensor shall not publish Licensee's name or logo in marketing materials without Licensee's prior written approval of each use.

## 4. Confidentiality

The terms of this Side Letter, including the discount in Section 2, are the Confidential Information of each Party and are subject to Section 9 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 15.2 and 15.3 of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter.
