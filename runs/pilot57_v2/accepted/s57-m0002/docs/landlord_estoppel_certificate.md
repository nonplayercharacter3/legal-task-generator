# LANDLORD ESTOPPEL AND CONSENT CERTIFICATE

**Date:** February 24, 2026

**To:**

**Kateix Dental Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**")

**Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation ("**Tenant**")

**Re:** Lease dated May 1, 2019, between Broadway Crossing Retail, LP, as landlord, and Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc., as tenant. The Lease covers Suite 110, Broadway Crossing Shopping Center, 5800 Broadway Street, San Antonio, Texas 78209 (Clinic 1, Mesquite Ridge – Alamo Heights).

---

**Broadway Crossing Retail, LP**, a Texas limited partnership ("**Landlord**"), delivers this Landlord Estoppel and Consent Certificate (this "**Certificate**") to Buyer and Tenant.

Landlord understands the following. Under a Stock Purchase Agreement to be entered into between Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), Buyer will acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant from Seller (the "**Transaction**"). Buyer and its lenders will rely on this Certificate in completing the Transaction.

## 1. THE LEASE

1.1 **Lease Documents.** The lease consists solely of the documents listed below. Together they are the "**Lease**." The Lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant for the premises described in it (the "**Premises**"). There is no other agreement, oral or written, between Landlord and Tenant affecting the Premises.

(a) Shopping Center Lease dated May 1, 2019, between Landlord and Tenant; and

(b) First Amendment to Lease dated September 15, 2023, between Landlord and Tenant.

1.2 **Premises.** The Premises consist of approximately 3,850 rentable square feet, known as Suite 110 of the Broadway Crossing Shopping Center, San Antonio, Texas.

1.3 **Full Force and Effect.** The Lease is valid and in full force and effect, and has not been modified, supplemented or amended except as listed in Section 1.1.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Commencement and Expiration.** The term of the Lease began on May 1, 2019 and expires on April 30, 2029, unless extended or terminated earlier under the Lease.

2.2 **Renewal Options.** Tenant has two (2) remaining options to extend the term of the Lease, each for five (5) years, on the terms set out in Section 3.3 of the Lease. Tenant has not exercised either option.

2.3 **Possession.** Tenant has accepted and is in possession of the entire Premises. All improvements that Landlord was required to construct or pay for under the Lease have been completed. All tenant improvement allowances payable by Landlord under the Lease, including under the First Amendment, have been paid in full.

## 3. RENT AND OTHER CHARGES

3.1 **Base Rent.** The current monthly base rent under the Lease is $13,794.17. Base rent has been paid through February 28, 2026. No base rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

3.2 **Additional Rent.** Tenant pays its pro rata share of common area maintenance charges, real estate taxes and insurance costs for the shopping center, as provided in the Lease. Tenant's current monthly estimated payment of those charges is $3,368.75. That amount has been paid through February 28, 2026. Landlord has completed its reconciliation of those charges for calendar year 2024. Neither party owes the other any amount under that reconciliation. Reconciliation for calendar year 2025 will be delivered in the ordinary course under the Lease.

3.3 **Security Deposit.** Landlord holds a security deposit of $13,000.00 under the Lease. Landlord holds no other deposit, letter of credit or guaranty for the Lease.

3.4 **No Offsets.** To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant has no present right of offset, abatement or credit against rent under the Lease.

## 4. NO DEFAULTS

4.1 **Tenant.** To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant is not in default under the Lease. No event has occurred that, with notice or lapse of time or both, would constitute a default by Tenant under the Lease. Landlord has not sent Tenant any notice of default that remains uncured.

4.2 **Landlord.** Landlord has not received any notice from Tenant claiming a default by Landlord under the Lease that remains uncured.

4.3 **No Termination.** Landlord has not given or received any notice of termination of the Lease.

## 5. USE

5.1 **Permitted Use.** The Lease permits the use of the Premises for the operation of a general dentistry practice and related dental services, including dental hygiene, diagnostic imaging and minor oral surgery, and for business office uses incidental to that practice.

5.2 **Exclusive Use.** Section 8.4 of the Lease grants Tenant the exclusive right within the shopping center to operate a general dentistry practice. Landlord has not leased, and will not lease, any other space in the shopping center in violation of that exclusive.

## 6. CONSENT TO TRANSACTION

6.1 **Consent.** Section 15.3 of the Lease treats a transfer of a controlling interest in Tenant as an assignment requiring Landlord's consent. Landlord consents to the Transaction, including the transfer of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant from Seller to Buyer and the resulting change in ownership of Tenant.

6.2 **No Release; No Conditions.** Tenant will remain the tenant under the Lease after the Transaction. The Transaction does not require any assumption agreement, increase in rent, additional security deposit, guaranty or other modification of the Lease. Landlord will not charge any consent, transfer or review fee for the Transaction. However, Tenant will reimburse Landlord's reasonable attorneys' fees in reviewing this Certificate, not to exceed $1,500.00, as provided in Section 15.6 of the Lease.

6.3 **Recapture.** Landlord waives any right under the Lease to recapture the Premises, or to terminate the Lease, because of the Transaction.

6.4 **Later Transfers.** This consent applies only to the Transaction. Any later assignment, sublease or transfer of a controlling interest in Tenant remains subject to the Lease.

## 7. OTHER MATTERS

7.1 **Purchase Rights.** Tenant has no option or right of first refusal to purchase the Premises or the shopping center.

7.2 **Mortgage.** The shopping center is encumbered by a deed of trust in favor of Alamo Commercial Bank. The Lease is subordinate to that deed of trust, subject to the Subordination, Non-Disturbance and Attornment Agreement dated June 12, 2019, among Alamo Commercial Bank, Landlord and Tenant.

7.3 **Environmental.** Landlord has not given Tenant any written notice that Tenant's use of the Premises violates any environmental law. Landlord acknowledges that Tenant's handling and disposal of regulated medical waste and dental amalgam, in compliance with applicable law, is permitted under the Lease.

7.4 **Notices.** Notices to Landlord under the Lease should be sent to: Broadway Crossing Retail, LP, Attention: Asset Manager, 300 Convent Street, Suite 1400, San Antonio, Texas 78205.

## 8. RELIANCE

8.1 **Reliance.** Landlord delivers this Certificate knowing that Buyer, Tenant and any lender providing financing to Buyer or Tenant in connection with the Transaction will rely on it. Each of them may rely on this Certificate.

8.2 **Effect.** Landlord will be estopped from asserting any claim or fact inconsistent with this Certificate against Buyer, Tenant or those lenders. This Certificate does not amend the Lease. The person signing this Certificate on behalf of Landlord is authorized to do so.

8.3 **Governing Law.** This Certificate is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

8.4 **Counterparts.** This Certificate may be signed by electronic signature and delivered by PDF, and each such copy is deemed an original.
