## 2. SCHEDULE 2.4 — ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES

2.1 **General.** Closing Net Working Capital is determined in accordance with GAAP. GAAP is applied using the same accounting methods, policies, practices, principles, procedures, classifications, judgments and estimation methods that were used in preparing the audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025 included in the Financial Statements. If GAAP and that consistent application conflict, consistent application controls, to the extent the result is consistent with GAAP.

2.2 **Specific Principles.** The following apply in calculating Closing Net Working Capital:

(a) *Patient accounts receivable.* Patient and payer accounts receivable are stated net of contractual allowances and an allowance for doubtful accounts. The allowance is computed using the aging-based reserve percentages applied in the December 31, 2025 audited balance sheet. Receivables aged more than 180 days from the date of service are reserved at 100%.

(b) *Payer credit balances.* Credit balances owed to patients or payers, including identified overpayments, are included as current liabilities.

(c) *Dental supplies inventory.* Clinical and dental supplies inventory is included at the lower of cost or net realizable value, based on a physical count taken within five (5) days before the Closing Date.

(d) *Prepaid expenses.* Prepaid expenses are included only to the extent the Company will receive the benefit after the Closing.

(e) *Accrued compensation.* Accrued wages, accrued paid time off, and accrued but unpaid dentist production bonuses for periods before the Closing are included as current liabilities.

(f) *Exclusions.* Closing Cash, Closing Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses, and all current and deferred income Tax assets and liabilities are excluded.

2.3 **Target Net Working Capital.** Target Net Working Capital is $2,150,000.

2.4 **Illustrative Calculation.** An illustrative calculation of net working capital as of December 31, 2025, prepared under these principles, is in Data Room folder 2.4.1. If the illustrative calculation conflicts with Sections 2.1 through 2.3 of this Schedule 2.4, those sections control.

## 5. SCHEDULE 4.5(b) — CONSENTS AND CHANGE OF CONTROL PROVISIONS

5.1 **Network Participation Agreement with Pellucid Dental Benefits Network** (Schedule 4.5(a), item 4.1).

(a) Section 14.2 of the agreement treats a change of control of the Company as an assignment of the agreement by the Company. "Change of control" includes any transfer of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the Company. Any such change of control of the Company requires the prior written consent of Pellucid.

(b) If a change of control occurs without that consent, Pellucid may terminate the agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice to the Company.

5.2 **Participating Dentist Agreement with Brightwater Dental Plans of Texas, Inc.** (Schedule 4.5(a), item 4.2). The Company must give Brightwater written notice of any change in ownership within thirty (30) days after the change.

5.3 **Dental Provider Agreement with Lonestar Kids Dental Managed Care, LLC** (Schedule 4.5(a), item 4.4). The Company must report any change in ownership or controlling interest to Lonestar Kids within the period required by the Texas Medicaid and CHIP enrollment rules. See also Schedule 4.4, Section 3.1(b).

5.4 **Clinic Leases.** Each Clinic Lease listed in Schedule 4.9 requires the landlord's consent to a transfer of a controlling interest in the Company.

5.5 **Indebtedness.** Each agreement listed in Schedule 4.5(a), Part 4 includes a change of control event of default. Each will be repaid and terminated at the Closing.
