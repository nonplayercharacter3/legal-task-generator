# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and among **Kateix Dental Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pinecrest Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $3,000,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Indemnity Escrow Amount is security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which national banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Closing Date**" means March 2, 2026.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest and other earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, together with a certificate of the presenting party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account titled "Pinecrest Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent for Kateix Dental Partners / Laleis Dental Holdings." The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the deposit to Buyer and Seller in writing.

**2.3 No Commingling.** The Escrow Funds will not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party, and will be held and disbursed only as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** Unless Buyer and Seller otherwise direct in Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits, or in a money market fund investing solely in obligations of the United States government. The Escrow Agent has no liability for any loss on an investment made in accordance with this Section 3.1.

**3.2 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds and will be disbursed together with them.

**3.3 Tax Reporting.** Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds for income Tax purposes until disbursement, and all earnings on the Escrow Funds will be reported as income of Buyer. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file any information returns required by applicable law.

## 4. DISBURSEMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Disbursement Generally.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds only:

(a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions;

(b) in accordance with a Final Order;

(c) to Buyer, as provided in Section 5.2 or Section 5.3; or

(d) to Seller, as provided in Section 4.2.

**4.2 Release.** On the date that is nine (9) months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller all Escrow Funds then remaining in the Escrow Account, less the aggregate amount of all claims set out in Claim Notices delivered on or before the Release Date that have not been paid or otherwise resolved as of the Release Date (the "**Pending Claim Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount after the Release Date. As each pending claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the portion of the Pending Claim Amount attributable to that claim in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, and shall disburse to Seller any part of that portion not payable to Buyer.

**4.3 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient. The Escrow Agent shall make each disbursement within two (2) Business Days after the date on which it becomes required under this Agreement.

**4.4 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any wire instruction other than an instruction set out in this Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the instructing party listed on Schedule A. The Escrow Agent may rely on that confirmation.

## 5. CLAIMS PROCEDURES

**5.1 Claim Notice.** If Buyer, in good faith, claims any amount from the Escrow Funds for Losses under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") to Seller and the Escrow Agent. A Claim Notice must state the amount claimed, or a good-faith estimate if the amount is not yet determinable, and describe the basis of the claim in reasonable detail.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). An objection must state the amount objected to and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail. If Seller does not deliver an objection within the Objection Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, on the first Business Day after the Objection Period ends, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice.

**5.3 Undisputed Amounts.** If Seller objects to only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed portion to Buyer on the first Business Day after the Objection Period ends.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall hold any disputed amount until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order directing its disbursement, and shall then disburse it accordingly.

**5.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay all claims, the Escrow Agent shall pay claims in the order in which it received the related Claim Notices. Nothing in this Agreement limits the rights or obligations of Buyer and Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties apply. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, is not bound by, and has no duty to review or interpret, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper party. The Escrow Agent has no duty to investigate the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its own gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages, even if advised of their possibility.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its acceptance of or performance under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear fifty percent (50%) of any amount paid under this Section 6.4, unless a court of competent jurisdiction determines that the loss was caused by one of them, in which case that party shall bear the full amount. This Section 6.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. The Escrow Agent may also interplead the Escrow Funds in a court of competent jurisdiction and will then be released from further obligations relating to the interpleaded funds.

**6.6 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and is fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of counsel.

**6.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller, acting together, may remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. In either case, Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Funds to the successor on its appointment. If no successor is appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint a successor. Until a successor is appointed, the Escrow Agent's only duty is to hold the Escrow Funds.

**6.8 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with any information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering, sanctions and customer identification laws.

## 7. FEES AND EXPENSES

**7.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set out in Schedule B, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay fifty percent (50%) of those fees and expenses.

**7.2 No Set-Off Against Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds unless they remain unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after invoice, in which case it may deduct the unpaid amount from the portion of the Escrow Funds next disbursable to the non-paying party.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 7 survive termination.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given (a) when delivered personally, (b) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. recipient's local time on a Business Day (and otherwise on the next Business Day), or (c) one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only on actual receipt. Notices shall be addressed as follows:

If to Buyer: Kateix Dental Partners, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address and email set forth on Buyer's signature page.

If to Seller: Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC, Attention: Chief Executive Officer, at the address and email set forth on Seller's signature page.

If to the Escrow Agent: Pinecrest Trust Company, N.A., Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, at the address and email set forth on the Escrow Agent's signature page.

**9.2 Authorized Representatives.** Schedule A lists the individuals authorized to sign instructions and confirm wire transfers on behalf of each of Buyer and Seller (each, an "**Authorized Representative**"). A party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the other parties.

**9.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless set forth in a writing signed by the waiving party.

**9.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further action.

**9.5 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms will remain in full force and effect.

**9.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**9.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the federal or state courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**9.8 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, or failure of communications or utility systems.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
