This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") is delivered by Kateix Dental Partners, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), to Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. The Closing occurred on March 2, 2026. Section 2.4(b) of the Agreement provides for Seller to prepare and deliver a Closing Statement to Buyer after the Closing, and for Buyer to dispute the Closing Statement by delivering a Notice of Disagreement.

B. Seller delivered the Closing Statement on March 30, 2026, by email to Buyer's General Counsel. In the Closing Statement, Seller calculated Closing Net Working Capital at $2,318,400. Buyer delivered a Notice of Disagreement to Seller on May 22, 2026, by email to Seller's Chief Executive Officer (the "**Buyer Notice**"). The Buyer Notice disputes Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital by approximately $1,200,000, as described in Section 1 below.

C. Buyer and Seller wish to record the items raised in the Buyer Notice and to set out the arrangements for an initial meeting between them to discuss those items.

## 1. ITEMS RAISED IN THE BUYER NOTICE

1.1 The Buyer Notice asserts that Closing Net Working Capital, properly determined under the Accounting Principles in Schedule 2.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, is approximately $1,118,400. That amount is approximately $1,200,000 less than the amount stated in the Closing Statement. The Buyer Notice identifies the following principal items:

(a) *Patient and payer accounts receivable.* Buyer asserts that the Closing Statement did not apply the aging-based reserve percentages required by Section 2.2(a) of Schedule 2.4. In particular, Buyer asserts that it did not fully reserve receivables aged more than 180 days from the date of service. Buyer estimates the overstatement at approximately $690,000.

(b) *Payer credit balances.* Buyer asserts that the Closing Statement omitted from current liabilities certain credit balances and identified overpayments owed to payers and patients, as required by Section 2.2(b) of Schedule 2.4. Buyer estimates the understatement at approximately $315,000.

(c) *Accrued compensation.* Buyer asserts that the Closing Statement did not include accrued but unpaid dentist production bonuses for periods before the Closing under the Associate Dentist Production Bonus Policy, as required by Section 2.2(e) of Schedule 2.4. Buyer estimates the understatement at approximately $195,000.

1.2 The description in Section 1.1 summarizes the Buyer Notice and is qualified in its entirety by the Buyer Notice itself. Seller has not accepted any of the items or amounts described in Section 1.1.

## 2. MEETING

2.1 Representatives of Buyer and Seller, including their respective financial advisors, shall meet in person or by video conference on or before June 12, 2026, at a time agreed by the parties, to discuss the items raised in the Buyer Notice.

2.2 Before the meeting, each party shall make available to the other party the work papers and supporting schedules it reasonably expects to rely on at the meeting. Buyer shall cause the Company to give Seller and its accountants reasonable access during normal business hours to the books and records of the Company relating to the items described in Section 1.1, consistent with Section 2.4(d) of the Agreement.

2.3 Buyer and Seller shall each bear their own costs of preparing for and attending the meeting.
