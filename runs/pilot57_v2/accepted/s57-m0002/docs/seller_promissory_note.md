# SECURED-FREE SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000.00**
**March 2, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Kateix Dental Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal amount stated in Section 1, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms of this Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

Maker issues this Note under Section 2.2(c) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Maker purchased all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation (the "**Company**"). This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. PRINCIPAL

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The original principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**").

**1.2 Payment of Principal.** Maker shall repay the Principal Amount in three (3) installments, each due on the date shown below (each, an "**Installment Date**"):

| Installment Date | Principal Installment |
|---|---|
| March 2, 2028 | $2,000,000 |
| March 2, 2029 | $2,000,000 |
| March 2, 2030 (the "**Maturity Date**") | All remaining unpaid principal |

All unpaid principal, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

## 2. INTEREST

**2.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the date of this Note until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the second day of each June, September, December and March, beginning June 2, 2026, and on the Maturity Date (each, an "**Interest Payment Date**").

**2.3 Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.4 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the unpaid principal balance and, to the extent permitted by law, any overdue interest bear interest at a rate equal to the rate under Section 2.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

**2.5 Usury Savings.** Interest under this Note will not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate will be applied to reduce the unpaid principal balance or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. PAYMENTS

**3.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Holder from time to time. Maker shall make each payment in full, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction.

**3.2 Business Days.** If any payment is due on a day that is not a business day, it is due on the next business day, and interest continues to accrue until paid.

**3.3 Application of Payments.** Each payment shall be applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 6.3, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to principal.

**3.4 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, on not less than five (5) business days' written notice to Holder. Each partial prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued interest on the amount prepaid and shall be applied to principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS OF MAKER

Maker represents to Holder that (a) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Note, (b) the execution, delivery and performance of this Note have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action, and (c) this Note is a legal, valid and binding obligation of Maker, enforceable against Maker in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

## 5. COVENANTS OF MAKER

While any amount under this Note remains unpaid, Maker shall:

(a) deliver to Holder, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Maker, the annual consolidated financial statements of Maker for that fiscal year;

(b) give Holder written notice within five (5) business days after an officer of Maker becomes aware of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default; and

(c) not sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of the shares of capital stock of the Company, or cause or permit the Company to sell all or substantially all of its assets, unless this Note is repaid in full at or before the closing of that transaction.

## 6. EVENTS OF DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**6.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal when due under this Note;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount when due under this Note, and the failure continues for ten (10) business days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(c) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 5, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(d) Maker or the Company (i) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, (ii) commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law, or (iii) admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(e) an involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker or the Company under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law, and it is not dismissed within sixty (60) days after its commencement; or

(f) Maker or the Company defaults in the payment of any indebtedness for borrowed money in an aggregate principal amount of more than $2,500,000, and the default results in the acceleration of that indebtedness.

**6.2 Remedies.** On the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 6.1(d) or Section 6.1(e), the unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, becomes immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder. While any other Event of Default is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, immediately due and payable. Holder may then exercise any right or remedy available to it under this Note or applicable law.

**6.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs in collecting any amount due under this Note after an Event of Default.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note, except for the notices expressly required by this Note.

**7.2 No Waiver by Holder.** No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right. No single or partial exercise of any right precludes any other or further exercise of that right or any other right. No waiver is effective unless set forth in a writing signed by Holder.

**7.3 Amendment.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**7.4 Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker, which may not be unreasonably withheld. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder. Any purported transfer or assignment in violation of this Section 7.4 is void. This Note binds and benefits Maker and Holder and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

**7.5 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss from Holder and, in the case of mutilation, surrender of this Note.

**7.6 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, with Maker treated as Buyer and Holder treated as Seller.

**7.7 Severability.** If any term of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms will remain in full force and effect, and the invalid term will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**7.8 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.9 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the federal or state courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Note. MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**7.10 Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed by electronic signature, and a PDF copy of the executed Note has the same effect as the original. Holder shall return the original Note to Maker, marked "Paid in Full," within ten (10) business days after payment in full of all amounts owing under this Note.
