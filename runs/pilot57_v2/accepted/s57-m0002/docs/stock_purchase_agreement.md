# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and between **Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Kateix Dental Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company owns and operates fourteen (14) general dentistry clinics (the "**Clinics**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. The parties are executing this Agreement and consummating the transactions contemplated by this Agreement at the same time.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

"**Clinic Leases**" means the real property leases under which the Company occupies the premises of the Clinics, as listed in Schedule 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Closing Cash**" means the aggregate cash and cash equivalents of the Company as of 11:59 p.m. Central Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens and encumbrances. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), (a) plus Closing Cash, (b) minus Closing Indebtedness, (c) minus Transaction Expenses, and (d) plus or minus the amount, if any, by which Closing Net Working Capital exceeds or falls short of Target Net Working Capital (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or deliver the following, based on the estimates in the Estimated Closing Statement:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount under Section 2.3;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses to the payees named in payoff letters and invoices delivered by Seller;

(c) to Seller, a promissory note of Buyer in the original principal amount of $6,000,000, in the form attached as Exhibit A (the "**Seller Note**"); and

(d) to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, an amount equal to the estimated Purchase Price minus the amounts described in clauses (a) and (c).

**2.3 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $3,000,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Indemnity Escrow Fund shall be held as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX and shall be held and disbursed by the Escrow Agent in accordance with this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

**2.4 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* Not less than three (3) business days before the Closing Date, Seller delivered to Buyer a written statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting out Seller's good-faith estimates of Closing Cash, Closing Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses and Closing Net Working Capital, and the resulting estimated Purchase Price.

(b) *Closing Statement; Notice of Disagreement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a written statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting out Seller's calculation of Closing Cash, Closing Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses and Closing Net Working Capital, and the resulting Purchase Price, together with reasonable supporting detail. Buyer may dispute the Closing Statement by delivering to Seller a written notice (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within forty-five (45) days after delivery of the Closing Statement. A Notice of Disagreement must specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute, and the basis for Buyer's position. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement will be final and binding on the parties. Any item not disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement will be final and binding on the parties.

(c) *Resolution.* Seller and Buyer shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any disputed items for thirty (30) days after delivery of a timely Notice of Disagreement. Any items still unresolved at the end of that period shall be submitted to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, shall consider only the disputed items, and shall resolve each item within the range of values asserted by the parties. Its determination will be final and binding on the parties absent manifest error. Its fees shall be allocated between Seller and Buyer in inverse proportion to the extent each prevails on the disputed amounts.

(d) *Access.* Buyer shall give Seller and its accountants reasonable access during normal business hours to the books, records and personnel of the Company, to the extent reasonably needed to prepare the Closing Statement.

(e) *Payment.* Within five (5) business days after the Purchase Price becomes final under this Section 2.4, (i) if the final Purchase Price exceeds the estimated Purchase Price, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller, and (ii) if the estimated Purchase Price exceeds the final Purchase Price, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. Each payment shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount it is required to deduct and withhold under applicable Tax law. Any amount so withheld will be treated as paid to the Person entitled to the payment.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on March 2, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), at the same time as the execution of this Agreement. The Closing will be deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Central Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(c) the written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(d) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness and releases of all related liens;

(e) the third-party consents described in Section 6.4;

(f) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the Texas Secretary of State, dated within ten (10) days before the Closing Date; and

(g) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments and the Seller Note described in Section 2.2, and the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Qualification.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Texas and is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where the conduct of its business requires it.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require the consent of any Governmental Authority.

(a) Schedule 4.5(a) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Company that (i) involves annual payments to or by the Company of more than $150,000, (ii) is a participation, provider or network agreement with any dental benefits plan, insurer or payer, (iii) limits the Company's freedom to compete in any line of business or geographic area, or (iv) relates to Indebtedness (collectively, the "**Material Contracts**").

(b) Schedule 4.5(b) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Material Contract that requires the consent of, or notice to, any counterparty in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, or that gives any counterparty a right to terminate or modify the contract on a change of control of the Company.

(c) Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except those (a) reflected or reserved against in the Financial Statements, (b) incurred in the ordinary course since December 31, 2025, or (c) disclosed in Schedule 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.9 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. Schedule 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Clinic Lease. Each Clinic Lease is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and the Company holds a valid leasehold interest in each leased premises.

**4.10 Taxes.** All income and other material Tax returns of the Company have been timely filed and are true, correct and complete in all material respects. All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid. No audit or other proceeding regarding Taxes of the Company is pending or threatened.

**4.11 Licenses and Permits.** Schedule 4.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material permit and license held by the Company, including each facility permit, radiation equipment registration and DEA registration. Each dentist, hygienist and dental assistant employed or engaged by the Company holds all licenses and certifications required by applicable law for the services that individual performs, and each such license is in good standing.

**4.12 Healthcare Compliance.**

(a) The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all Healthcare Laws.

(b) All claims submitted by or on behalf of the Company to any governmental health care program or private payer since January 1, 2023 were accurate and complete in all material respects, were supported by adequate documentation, and were submitted in accordance with the applicable payer's billing and coding requirements.

(c) Except as set forth in Schedule 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule, the Company has not received any written notice of any audit, investigation, recoupment, overpayment demand or claims review by any Governmental Authority or payer since January 1, 2023, and the Company holds no known overpayment that it has not refunded.

(d) The Company is in compliance in all material respects with HIPAA. The Company has entered into a business associate agreement with each Person that creates, receives, maintains or transmits protected health information on its behalf. There has been no breach of unsecured protected health information that required notification under HIPAA.

**4.13 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each such plan has been operated in material compliance with its terms and applicable law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.14 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company, including any malpractice claim.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from the Company or Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company, except to the extent the information (a) is generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach, or (b) must be disclosed under applicable law.

**6.2 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) own, manage or operate any general dentistry practice within fifteen (15) miles of any Clinic, or (b) solicit for employment any dentist or clinical employee of the Company. Seller acknowledges that these restrictions are reasonable and necessary to protect the goodwill acquired by Buyer.

**6.3 Books and Records.** For seven (7) years after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing, and to give Seller reasonable access to them for legitimate business purposes on reasonable notice.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller delivered to Buyer at the Closing the consents of the landlords under the Clinic Leases to the change in ownership of the Company contemplated by this Agreement, each in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

**6.5 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver any further instruments, and take any further actions, that the other party reasonably requests to carry out the transfer of the Shares under this Agreement.

**6.6 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement about this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by applicable law.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller is responsible for, and shall indemnify Buyer against, all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date, to the extent not included as a liability in the calculation of Closing Net Working Capital.

**7.2 Straddle Periods.** For any taxable period that begins before and ends after the Closing Date, Taxes shall be allocated to the pre-Closing portion (a) for property and similar ad valorem Taxes, on a per diem basis, and (b) for all other Taxes, as if the taxable period ended at the end of the Closing Date.

**7.3 Cooperation; Transfer Taxes.** The parties shall cooperate reasonably in preparing Tax returns and in any Tax audit of the Company. Seller and Buyer shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of any transfer, documentary, sales or similar Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares.

## ARTICLE VIII. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**8.1 Continuation of Employment.** Buyer shall cause the Company to continue to employ the employees of the Company immediately after the Closing, on terms determined by Buyer and the Company.

**8.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article VIII creates any right in any employee or other Person, or amends any benefit plan.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations and the representations and warranties in Section 4.10 (Taxes) survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification may be made for breach of a representation or warranty after its survival period ends. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** Subject to this Article IX, from and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including the Company after the Closing) and their respective representatives from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV; provided, however, that Seller will have no liability under this clause (a) for any matter disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Closing Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing or taken into account in the final Purchase Price; and

(d) any Taxes for which Seller is responsible under Section 7.1.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Subject to this Article IX, from and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) *Direct Claims.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. A failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, by written notice delivered within twenty (20) days after receiving notice of the claim. The indemnified party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.** The following limitations apply to Losses under this Article IX:

(a) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations until the aggregate Losses for those breaches exceed $363,750 (the "**Deductible**"), and then only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations will not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(c) Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX will not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3(a) will not exceed the Purchase Price.

(e) For purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), each representation and warranty will be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier.

(f) Losses will be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party for the matter, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Sources of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer indemnified party under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations shall be recovered solely and exclusively from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, and Buyer shall have no right of set-off against the Seller Note for those Losses. Any Losses payable to a Buyer indemnified party under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of Fundamental Representations or Section 4.10 (Taxes), or under Sections 9.2(b) through 9.2(d), shall be recovered first from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, to the extent of funds then available, and then directly from Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.11, and disputes under Section 2.4, which shall be resolved as provided in that Section, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX will be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.
