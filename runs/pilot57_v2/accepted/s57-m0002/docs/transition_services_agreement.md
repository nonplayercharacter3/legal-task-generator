# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Laleis Dental Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Kateix Dental Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as seller, and Recipient, as buyer, are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company owns and operates fourteen (14) general dentistry clinics (the "**Clinics**").

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided billing, practice-management software and information technology services to the Company and the Clinics.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires the parties to enter into this Agreement at the Closing, so that Provider will continue to provide those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Closing Date**" means March 2, 2026.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Patient Data**" means all patient demographic, clinical, treatment, imaging, scheduling, insurance and financial information of the Company or the Clinics, including protected health information within the meaning of HIPAA.

"**PM System**" means the practice-management and electronic dental records software platform used by the Company and the Clinics as of the Closing Date, together with its databases, interfaces and related clearinghouse connections.

"**Service Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of, and Schedules to, this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the date that is nine months after the Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under Article 10 (the "**Term**").

**2.2 Early Termination of Individual Services.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, on not less than thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Termination of a Service does not affect the remaining Services or the Service Fee, except as provided in Section 4.3.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause to be provided, the Services to Recipient and, at Recipient's direction, to the Company and the Clinics.

**3.2 Scope.** The Services shall be substantially similar in scope, volume and nature to the corresponding services provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date. Provider is not required to provide any service not described in Schedule A, or to provide any Service at a volume materially greater than that historical level, unless the parties agree in writing.

**3.3 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies a service that Provider provided to the Company before the Closing and that is not described in Schedule A, Recipient may request that Provider provide it. Provider shall consider each request in good faith. Any additional service and its fee shall be set out in a written amendment to Schedule A signed by both parties.

**3.4 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint a service coordinator to act as its primary contact for the Services. The coordinators shall meet by telephone or video conference at least every two weeks during the Term to review performance and the progress of the migration under Section 3.5.

**3.5 Migration.** Provider shall cooperate in good faith with Recipient to migrate the Services, the PM System data and the Patient Data to Recipient's own systems or to a vendor designated by Recipient before the end of the Term. That cooperation includes delivering complete data extracts in a commercially reasonable, non-proprietary format on Recipient's request. Recipient shall bear the reasonable out-of-pocket costs of third-party conversion tools used for the migration.

**3.6 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall cause the Company to provide Provider with the information, access to personnel and facilities, and approvals that Provider reasonably requires to perform the Services.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fee.** In consideration of the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider a fee of $85,000 per month (the "**Service Fee**"). The Service Fee for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**4.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Provider. If Recipient disputes any invoice in good faith, it shall notify Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date, and shall pay the undisputed portion.

**4.3 Fee Reduction.** If Recipient terminates a Service under Section 2.2, the parties shall negotiate in good faith an equitable reduction of the Service Fee reflecting the costs Provider no longer incurs.

**4.4 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider at cost, without markup, for third-party clearinghouse transaction fees, postage for patient statements, and per-user license fees for the PM System, in each case to the extent incurred in providing the Services and supported by reasonable documentation.

**4.5 Taxes.** The Service Fee excludes sales, use and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes imposed on the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 5. SERVICE STANDARDS

**5.1 Standard of Care.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, using the degree of skill and care it used in providing the corresponding services to the Company before the Closing.

**5.2 Compliance with Law.** Provider shall perform the Services in compliance with applicable law, including Healthcare Laws governing the submission of claims to governmental and private payers.

**5.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign personnel to the Services with the training and experience reasonably needed to perform them. Provider controls the hiring, supervision and compensation of its personnel.

**5.4 Availability.** Provider shall make the PM System available to the Clinics during each Clinic's normal business hours, other than scheduled maintenance performed outside those hours on at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore any unscheduled outage promptly.

## 6. DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Ownership of Data.** As between the parties, the Company owns all Patient Data and all billing, claims and remittance data relating to the Clinics, whether generated before or after the Closing. Provider acquires no right in that data except the limited right to use it to perform the Services.

**6.2 Patient Data.** Provider shall use and disclose Patient Data only as needed to perform the Services and as permitted by HIPAA. Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Patient Data that are no less protective than those it maintained before the Closing, and shall notify Recipient within five (5) business days after discovering any breach of unsecured protected health information involving Patient Data.

**6.3 Confidentiality.** Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other party, or of the Company, that it receives in connection with this Agreement. Each party shall use that information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, except to the extent the information (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through its breach, or (b) must be disclosed under applicable law.

**6.4 Return of Data.** At the end of the Term, or earlier on Recipient's request for any terminated Service, Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Patient Data and other Company data in its possession relating to that Service. Provider shall then delete its copies, except as required to be retained by applicable law.

## 7. DISCLAIMER

**7.1 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, concerning the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient, the Company and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider in performing the Services.

**8.2 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, managers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to any third-party claim arising out of or relating to the Services or the operation of the Clinics, including any claim by any patient, payer or Governmental Authority.

**8.3 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for which it seeks indemnification. A failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. The indemnified party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Cap.** Except for (i) obligations under Section 8.2, and (ii) a party's fraud, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid in the six (6) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**9.2 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility. This Section 9.2 does not limit amounts payable to a third party under Article 8.

**9.3 Purchase Agreement Unaffected.** Nothing in this Agreement limits or expands the rights or obligations of Provider, as Seller, or Recipient, as Buyer, under the Purchase Agreement.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. For a failure by Recipient to pay undisputed amounts, the cure period is fifteen (15) days.

**10.2 Termination for Convenience by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate this Agreement in its entirety on not less than thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, (a) Recipient shall pay all Service Fees and pass-through costs accrued through the effective date of termination, and (b) Provider shall comply with Sections 3.5 and 6.4. Articles 1, 6, 8, 9 and 11, Section 10.3 and Article 12 survive expiration or termination.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**11.1 Escalation.** The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement first through their service coordinators and then, if the dispute is not resolved within ten (10) business days, through senior executives of each party, for a further ten (10) business days.

**11.2 Continued Performance.** Provider shall continue to perform the Services during any dispute, provided that Recipient continues to pay undisputed amounts when due.

## SCHEDULE A: SERVICES

**A.1 Revenue-Cycle and Billing Services.**

(a) Insurance eligibility and benefits verification for scheduled patients at each Clinic.

(b) Preparation, coding review and electronic submission of dental claims to private payers, dental benefits networks and Texas Medicaid and CHIP dental managed care organizations, using the Company's provider identifiers.

(c) Posting of payer remittances and patient payments, denial management, appeals and resubmission of rejected claims.

(d) Preparation and mailing of patient statements, and patient billing inquiries handled through Provider's billing call center.

(e) Identification and reporting to Recipient of credit balances and overpayments, and processing of refunds approved by Recipient.

(f) Monthly accounts-receivable aging, collections and payer-mix reports for each Clinic.

**A.2 Practice-Management and Patient-Records System Hosting.**

(a) Hosting, operation and maintenance of the PM System, including the electronic dental records, imaging archive and scheduling modules, in Provider's data environment.

(b) Daily backups of the PM System databases, with backups retained for at least thirty (30) days.

(c) Management of user accounts and access permissions for Clinic personnel, as directed by Recipient.

(d) Maintenance of clearinghouse and payer connectivity through the PM System.

**A.3 Information Technology Support.**

(a) Help-desk support for Clinic personnel from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central Time, Monday through Saturday.

(b) Network, email and endpoint support for the computers, printers and imaging-sensor workstations at the Clinics.

(c) Management of the Clinics' firewall, endpoint security and antivirus tools as configured on the Closing Date.

(d) Coordination with third-party hardware and software vendors serving the Clinics.

**A.4 Migration Support.** The cooperation described in Section 3.5, including data mapping, test extracts and a final cut-over extract of all PM System data and Patient Data.
