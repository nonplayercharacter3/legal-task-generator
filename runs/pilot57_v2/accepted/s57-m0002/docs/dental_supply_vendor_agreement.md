# DENTAL SUPPLY PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This DENTAL SUPPLY PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Orrin Clinical Supply Co.**, a Texas corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Mesquite Ridge Dental Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer owns and operates general dentistry clinics in the State of Texas (each, a "**Clinic**").

B. Supplier distributes consumable dental and clinical supplies, small equipment and infection-control products.

C. Customer wishes to buy, and Supplier wishes to sell, those products for the Clinics on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person.

"**Contract Price**" means the price for a Product stated in the Price Schedule attached as Exhibit A, as adjusted under Section 4.2.

"**Delivery Location**" means the address of the Clinic stated in the applicable Purchase Order.

"**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product that fails to conform to Section 7.1.

"**Products**" means the dental and clinical supplies listed in Exhibit A, as updated from time to time under Section 2.3.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products submitted by or on behalf of Customer under Section 3.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of, and Exhibits to, this Agreement. Headings are for reference only.

## 2. SCOPE

**2.1 Supply.** During the Term, Supplier shall sell and deliver to Customer the Products ordered under Purchase Orders accepted under Section 3.2.

**2.2 No Minimum Purchase.** Customer is not required to purchase any minimum quantity of Products. Customer may buy products of the same kind from other suppliers.

**2.3 Product Changes.** Supplier may add products to Exhibit A at any time, and Customer may buy them at the price then quoted in writing by Supplier. Supplier may discontinue a Product on at least sixty (60) days' written notice. When it gives that notice, Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to identify a substitute product of equal or better quality, which Customer may accept or decline.

## 3. ORDERS

**3.1 Purchase Orders.** Customer, or any Clinic on Customer's behalf, may submit Purchase Orders through Supplier's online ordering portal, by email or by electronic data interchange. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products, quantities, Delivery Location and requested delivery date.

**3.2 Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within one (1) business day after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if a Product is discontinued under Section 2.3 or is subject to a manufacturer allocation or recall.

**3.3 Changes and Cancellation.** Customer may change or cancel any Purchase Order without charge before Supplier ships the Products.

**3.4 Conflicting Terms.** Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or portal page do not form part of this Agreement and have no effect.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Prices.** Customer shall pay the Contract Price for each Product. Contract Prices include packaging and standard ground freight to the Delivery Location.

**4.2 Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust Contract Prices once in each Contract Year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No adjustment for any Product may exceed the lesser of (a) four percent (4%) and (b) the percentage increase in the manufacturer's list price for that Product over the preceding twelve (12) months. "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

**4.3 Volume Rebate.** Supplier shall pay Customer a rebate equal to two percent (2%) of net purchases in each Contract Year in excess of $400,000. Supplier shall pay the rebate by credit memo within forty-five (45) days after the end of the Contract Year. Supplier shall deliver with each credit memo a statement of net purchases for the Contract Year by Clinic, in a form that supports Customer's reporting of discounts and rebates.

**4.4 Invoices and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer on shipment. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date by ACH or wire transfer. Customer may dispute any invoice in good faith by written notice before the due date. The parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 12.1, and Customer shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

**4.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

**4.6 Taxes.** Contract Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Customer shall pay any such taxes stated on Supplier's invoices, unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate. Each party bears its own income taxes.

## 5. DELIVERY

**5.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the Delivery Location within two (2) business days after acceptance of a Purchase Order received before 3:00 p.m. Central Time, and within three (3) business days for later orders. Supplier shall offer next-day delivery at the expedite charges stated in Exhibit A.

**5.2 Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer on delivery to the Delivery Location.

**5.3 Backorders.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any backordered Product and its expected availability date. Customer may cancel any backordered quantity without charge.

**5.4 Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier shall ship Products in their original manufacturer packaging, with lot numbers and expiration dates legible. Supplier shall not deliver any Product with less than six (6) months of remaining shelf life, unless the Product's total shelf life is shorter than twelve (12) months or Customer agrees in writing.

## 6. INSPECTION AND RETURNS

**6.1 Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within ten (10) business days after delivery and may reject any Nonconforming Product, or any shipment that is damaged or incorrect, by notice to Supplier through the ordering portal or by email.

**6.2 Remedy.** For any rejected Product, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, replace it within two (2) business days or credit Customer for its price. Supplier shall arrange and pay for the return shipment.

**6.3 Recalls.** Supplier shall notify Customer within one (1) business day after Supplier learns of any manufacturer recall, market withdrawal or safety notice affecting a Product sold to Customer, identifying the affected lot numbers and the Clinics to which they were delivered. Supplier shall provide a credit or replacement for recalled Products and shall reimburse Customer's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of complying with the recall.

## 7. WARRANTIES

**7.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Customer that each Product, when delivered, (a) conforms to the manufacturer's specifications and labeling, (b) is free from defects in materials and workmanship, (c) is not adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, and (d) if it is a medical device, has been cleared, approved or registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration as required by law.

**7.2 Pass-Through of Manufacturer Warranties.** Supplier assigns to Customer, to the extent assignable, all manufacturer warranties for the Products, and shall reasonably assist Customer in enforcing them.

**7.3 Compliance.** Supplier warrants that it holds, and will maintain during the Term, all licenses and registrations required by federal and Texas law to distribute the Products, including any Texas Department of State Health Services registration required for the distribution of devices or drugs. Supplier shall provide copies on Customer's reasonable request.

**7.4 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, Supplier makes no other warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees ("**Losses**"), arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by (a) any Nonconforming Product, (b) any breach of Section 7.3, or (c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel in performing this Agreement.

**8.2 By Customer.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel, including the improper storage, handling or clinical use of Products after delivery, or (b) Customer's breach of this Agreement.

**8.3 Procedures.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim. A failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure. The indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and the indemnified party may participate at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Except for obligations under Article 8 and breaches of Article 10, neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.2 Cap.** Except for obligations under Article 8, breaches of Article 10, and a party's fraud or willful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the total amounts paid and payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**9.3 Insurance.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, and shall name Customer as an additional insured. Supplier shall deliver a certificate of insurance on Customer's request.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY; COMPLIANCE

**10.1 Confidentiality.** Each party shall hold in confidence the terms of this Agreement, including the Contract Prices, and all non-public information of the other party that it receives under this Agreement. Each party shall use that information only to perform this Agreement, except to the extent the information (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through its breach, or (b) must be disclosed under applicable law.

**10.2 No Patient Information.** The parties do not intend that Supplier will create, receive, maintain or transmit any protected health information on Customer's behalf. Customer shall not include patient information in any Purchase Order or other communication to Supplier.

**10.3 Anti-Kickback.** No part of any payment, discount, rebate or other benefit under this Agreement is intended as an inducement for the referral of patients or the arrangement of any item or service paid for by any governmental health care program. The parties intend the rebate under Section 4.3 and any other price reduction to qualify as a discount under 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b)(3)(A) and 42 C.F.R. § 1001.952(h). Each party shall comply with the disclosure and reporting obligations that apply to it under those provisions.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

**11.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years. It then renews automatically for successive one-year terms, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term (the initial term and all renewal terms, together, the "**Term**").

**11.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on ninety (90) days' written notice to the other party.

**11.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**11.4 Effect of Termination.** Expiration or termination does not affect any Purchase Order accepted before the effective date of termination, which Supplier shall fill on the terms of this Agreement. Within forty-five (45) days after expiration or termination, Supplier shall pay Customer any rebate earned under Section 4.3 for the partial Contract Year, calculated on a pro rata basis. Articles 7, 8, 9 and 10, Section 11.4 and Article 12 survive expiration or termination.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Dispute Resolution.** The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement through good-faith negotiation between their account managers and then, if not resolved within fifteen (15) business days, between senior executives of each party. Either party may then pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given (a) when delivered personally, (b) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. recipient's local time on a business day (and otherwise on the next business day), or (c) one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the address and email set forth on the recipient's signature page or a later address designated by notice. Routine order communications may be made through the ordering portal.

**12.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which may not be unreasonably withheld. However, either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party. A change in the ownership or control of either party is not an assignment of this Agreement and does not require the consent of, or give any right of termination to, the other party. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

**12.5 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, manufacturer shortage or allocation, or failure of public utility or transportation systems. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During any such event, Supplier shall allocate available Products among its customers on a fair and reasonable basis, and Customer may buy affected Products from other sources.

**12.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties, except that Exhibit A may be updated as provided in Sections 2.3 and 4.2. No waiver is effective unless set forth in a writing signed by the waiving party.

**12.7 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms will remain in full force and effect, and the invalid term will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**12.8 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified parties under Article 8, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**12.9 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Bexar County, Texas, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**12.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A: PRICE SCHEDULE

**A.1 Product Categories.** The Contract Prices for the following categories are set out in the attached item-level price list (SKU, manufacturer, unit of measure and Contract Price):

(a) Infection control and sterilization: gloves, masks, gowns, surface disinfectants, sterilization pouches, biological indicators.

(b) Restorative: composites, bonding agents, glass ionomers, matrix systems, finishing and polishing products.

(c) Preventive and hygiene: prophy paste, fluoride varnish, sealants, disposable prophy angles, saliva ejectors.

(d) Anesthetics and pharmaceuticals: local anesthetic cartridges, topical anesthetics, hemostatic agents, in each case subject to applicable law.

(e) Impression and laboratory: impression materials, bite registration, trays, temporary crown and bridge materials.

(f) Endodontic and surgical: files, obturation materials, sutures, surgical blades.

(g) Small equipment and sundries: curing light tips, handpiece maintenance products, radiography sensor sleeves and barriers.

**A.2 Expedite Charges.** Next-day delivery: $25.00 per shipment. Same-day courier delivery, where available: $75.00 per shipment.

**A.3 Pricing Basis.** Contract Prices are fixed for the first Contract Year and are subject to adjustment only under Section 4.2.
