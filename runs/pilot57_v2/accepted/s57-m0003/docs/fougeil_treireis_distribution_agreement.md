# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells point-of-care chemistry and hematology analyzers for veterinary use, together with the reagents, test cartridges, controls and other consumables used with them.

B. Distributor is in the business of marketing, warehousing and distributing animal health products to veterinary practices and related customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory and the Field, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Form.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Competing Product**" means any analyzer, instrument, test, assay, reagent, cartridge or other consumable intended for use in veterinary diagnostics, whether for companion-animal, livestock, equine or other species, and whether for point-of-care or reference-laboratory use, other than the Products when sold to or through Distributor under this Agreement.

1.3 "**Contract Year**" means each 12-month period beginning July 1 during the Term. The first Contract Year begins on the Effective Date.

1.4 "**Customer**" means a veterinary practice, clinic, hospital, academic institution or other end user in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor.

1.5 "**Field**" means the field of use stated in Section 3 of the Order Form.

1.6 "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

1.7 "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**" means, for each Contract Year, the minimum net purchase amount for Products stated in the Order Form.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means the Order Form – Initial Appointment executed by the parties as of the Effective Date, as amended from time to time in a writing signed by both parties.

1.9 "**Products**" means the analyzers, reagents, consumables and accessories listed in the Order Form or in Supplier's then-current price list, as updated by Supplier under Section 5.2.

1.10 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under Section 4.1.

1.11 "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between the parties covering storage, handling, complaint handling, adverse event reporting and recall procedures for the Products, as amended from time to time.

1.12 "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

1.13 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule agreed by the parties setting out order fulfillment, technical support and field service levels for the Products.

1.14 "**Specifications**" means Supplier's published specifications for each Product, as in effect at the time of shipment.

1.15 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.16 "**Territory**" means the territory stated in Section 3 of the Order Form.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory and the Field during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

2.2 **Distributor Sales.** Distributor shall buy Products only from Supplier for resale and shall resell Products only to Customers in the Territory. Distributor shall not actively solicit orders for Products from outside the Territory, establish a warehouse or branch for the Products outside the Territory, or sell Products to any person that Distributor knows or has reason to believe intends to resell them outside the Territory.

2.3 **Supplier Exclusivity Covenant.** During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Supplier shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, sell, distribute, license or otherwise supply any Competing Product anywhere in the world, whether through its own sales force, any distributor, agent, reseller, original equipment manufacturer, online channel or any other channel, other than to or through Distributor under this Agreement.

2.4 **Sub-Distributors.** Distributor may appoint sub-distributors in the Territory with Supplier's prior written consent, which Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold. Distributor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of each sub-distributor as if they were Distributor's own.

2.5 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or franchise relationship. Distributor has no authority to bind Supplier.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term stated in the Order Form (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

3.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive renewal terms, each equal in length to the Initial Term (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

3.3 **Terms During Renewal.** The terms of this Agreement and the Order Form in effect at the end of the preceding term apply during each Renewal Term, except that pricing is subject to Section 5.2 and the Minimum Purchase Commitment for each Contract Year of a Renewal Term is the amount the parties agree in writing. If the parties have not agreed a Minimum Purchase Commitment before the start of a Contract Year, the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the preceding Contract Year applies.

## 4. ORDERS AND MINIMUM PURCHASES

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier stating the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery locations. Each Purchase Order is governed solely by this Agreement, and any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days after receipt. Supplier shall not reject a Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to the forecast under Section 4.3, except for Products that Supplier has discontinued under Section 5.3.

4.3 **Forecasts.** By the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Distributor shall deliver a rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its Product requirements. The first three (3) months of each forecast are binding, and the remaining months are non-binding and for planning purposes only.

4.4 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of at least the Minimum Purchase Commitment in each Contract Year. If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Commitment for a Contract Year, Supplier may, by written notice to Distributor given within sixty (60) days after the end of the applicable Contract Year, convert Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 to a non-exclusive appointment, effective thirty (30) days after the date of the notice. Purchases in one Contract Year in excess of the Minimum Purchase Commitment do not carry forward to a later Contract Year.

4.5 **Reporting.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a report of Product sales by Product, quantity and Customer category, and of Distributor's inventory of Products on hand.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices stated in the Order Form or, after the first Contract Year, in Supplier's then-current price list for distributors. Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and are FCA Supplier's facility, exclusive of taxes, duties and freight.

5.2 **Price Changes.** Supplier may change prices once per Contract Year by written notice given at least ninety (90) days before the start of the Contract Year in which the change takes effect. No single annual increase may exceed five percent (5%) for any Product, except for documented increases in the cost of raw materials or components outside Supplier's reasonable control.

5.3 **Product Changes.** Supplier may add Products, or discontinue or modify any Product, on at least one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice to Distributor. Supplier shall fill accepted Purchase Orders for discontinued Products and shall continue to supply reagents and consumables for any discontinued analyzer for at least five (5) years after the discontinuance date.

5.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice upon shipment. Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Late payments bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any good-faith dispute within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice and shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

5.5 **Taxes.** Distributor is responsible for all sales, use, excise and similar taxes on Products it buys, other than taxes on Supplier's net income. Distributor shall provide valid resale certificates where applicable.

5.6 **Credit.** Supplier may set a credit limit for Distributor and may require payment in advance or other security if Distributor exceeds that limit or fails to pay undisputed amounts when due.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the carrier designated by Distributor at Supplier's facility. Temperature-controlled reagents shall be packed according to Supplier's validated shipping configurations and the Quality Agreement.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

6.3 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect each shipment within five (5) business days after receipt and shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage, visible damage or temperature excursion indicated by the shipment monitors. Supplier shall replace or credit nonconforming Products reported within that period.

6.4 **Service Levels.** Supplier shall perform order fulfillment, technical support and field service in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Marketing.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to market and sell the Products throughout the Territory, maintain a trained sales force with knowledge of the Products, and participate in Supplier's sales training programs.

7.2 **Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store, handle and ship Products in accordance with the Specifications, the Quality Agreement and Supplier's written instructions, including cold-chain requirements for reagents and consumables. Distributor shall maintain temperature records for its storage facilities and make them available to Supplier on request.

7.3 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws that apply to its importation, storage, marketing, sale and distribution of the Products, including applicable regulations of the U.S. Department of Agriculture and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, and state veterinary and licensing requirements.

7.4 **Claims.** Distributor shall make no representation, warranty or claim about the Products that is inconsistent with Supplier's labeling, instructions for use or approved marketing materials.

7.5 **Records and Audit.** Distributor shall keep complete and accurate records of its purchases, sales and inventory of Products for at least three (3) years after the relevant transaction. Supplier may audit those records once per Contract Year on at least fifteen (15) business days' notice during normal business hours.

7.6 **Parent Guaranty.** On or before the Effective Date, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under this Agreement executed by Treireis Holdings, Inc.

## 8. QUALITY, COMPLAINTS AND RECALLS

8.1 **Quality Agreement.** The parties shall comply with the Quality Agreement.

8.2 **Complaints and Adverse Events.** Distributor shall notify Supplier of any Product complaint within two (2) business days, and of any adverse event involving an animal within one (1) business day, after Distributor becomes aware of it, and shall cooperate with Supplier's investigation.

8.3 **Recalls.** Supplier has sole authority to decide whether to recall or withdraw any Product. Distributor shall promptly carry out any recall at Supplier's direction. Supplier shall bear the reasonable, documented costs of a recall, except to the extent the recall results from Distributor's breach of this Agreement or the Quality Agreement.

## 9. TRADEMARKS

9.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks and trade names solely to market and sell the Products in the Territory, in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines.

9.2 **Ownership.** All goodwill from Distributor's use of Supplier's trademarks inures to Supplier. Distributor shall not register or use any mark, domain name or social media handle that is confusingly similar to Supplier's trademarks.

9.3 **No Alteration.** Distributor shall not alter, remove or obscure any label, serial number, lot number or trademark on the Products or their packaging.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that (a) each analyzer will conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after installation at the Customer site or eighteen (18) months after shipment, whichever is earlier; and (b) each reagent and consumable will conform to the Specifications until its labeled expiration date, if stored and handled in accordance with the Specifications.

10.2 **Remedy.** Supplier's sole obligation, and Distributor's exclusive remedy, for breach of Section 10.1 is the repair or replacement of the nonconforming Product or, if repair or replacement is not commercially practicable, a credit or refund of the price paid.

10.3 **Exclusions.** The warranty in Section 10.1 does not apply to Products that have been misused, improperly stored or handled, modified, or repaired by anyone other than Supplier or its authorized service providers, or used with reagents or consumables not supplied by Supplier.

10.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is its valid and binding obligation.

10.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 10, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **Distributor Indemnification.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Distributor's storage, handling or transportation of Products, including any failure to maintain cold-chain conditions; (b) any representation, warranty or claim about the Products made by Distributor or its sub-distributors that is not authorized by Supplier; (c) Distributor's violation of applicable law, including veterinary, licensing and regulatory requirements; or (d) the negligence or willful misconduct of Distributor or its sub-distributors.

11.2 **Supplier Indemnification.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by a defect in a Product as delivered by Supplier; (b) any allegation that a Product, as delivered by Supplier, infringes or misappropriates a U.S. patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party; or (c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced. The indemnifying party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim, but may not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified party without its written consent. The indemnified party shall reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying party's expense and may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense.

11.4 **Infringement Remedies.** If a Product becomes, or in Supplier's opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Supplier may, at its option, procure the right for Distributor to continue selling the Product, modify the Product so it is non-infringing without material loss of functionality, or discontinue the Product and repurchase Distributor's unsold inventory at the price paid.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR LOST REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF THOSE DAMAGES.

12.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY DISTRIBUTOR IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a party's obligations under Section 11.1;

(b) a party's breach of Section 14 (Confidentiality);

(c) a party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Distributor's obligation to pay for Products ordered and delivered.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after receiving written notice describing it, or within ten (10) days in the case of non-payment of undisputed amounts.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver or trustee appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Effect of Termination or Expiration.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement, (a) all amounts owed to Supplier become due; (b) Supplier shall fill accepted Purchase Orders, unless Supplier terminated under Section 13.1 or 13.2; (c) Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products for ninety (90) days in accordance with this Agreement, after which Supplier may repurchase any remaining saleable inventory at the price paid; and (d) Distributor shall cease using Supplier's trademarks except as needed to sell remaining inventory under clause (c).

13.4 **Customer Transition.** For ninety (90) days after termination or expiration, Distributor shall reasonably cooperate with Supplier to transition Customers, including by providing a list of Customers with installed analyzers and their service status.

13.5 **Survival.** Sections 2.3, 5.4, 7.5, 8.3, 10, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 13.5, 14, 15.4, 16 and 17 survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Obligations.** Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, shall use it only to perform this Agreement, and shall disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14.

14.2 **Exceptions.** Section 14.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was known to the receiving party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's information, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose confidential information as required by law or court order after giving the other party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective treatment.

14.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue for five (5) years after termination or expiration of this Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **Assignment.** Supplier shall not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without Distributor's prior written consent.

15.2 **Subcontracting.** Supplier may use contract manufacturers and component suppliers in the manufacture of the Products, provided that Supplier remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement.

15.3 **Void Transfers.** Any purported assignment in violation of Section 15.1 is void.

15.4 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Escalation.** Before starting any action, other than an action for injunctive relief, a party shall give the other party written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of each party shall meet within twenty (20) days to try to resolve it.

16.4 **Injunctive Relief.** Each party acknowledges that a breach of Section 9 or 14 may cause irreparable harm for which damages are an inadequate remedy, and that the non-breaching party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

16.5 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy sent by courier, to the address of the receiving party stated on the signature page or as later designated by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

17.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, labor disputes not involving the affected party's own employees, governmental action or failures of utilities or carriers. The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

17.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among the documents forming the parties' agreement, the following order of precedence applies: signed amendments and side letters; this Distribution Agreement; the Order Form; the Quality Agreement; the Service Level Schedule.

17.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

17.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

17.6 **Insurance.** Each party shall maintain, during the Term and for two (2) years after, commercial general liability insurance (including products liability for Supplier) with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

17.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
