# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2023 by and between **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is the Quality Agreement referred to in Sections 1.11 and 8.1 of the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Order Form.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out each party's quality responsibilities for the Products. It covers storage and handling, distribution, traceability, complaint handling, adverse event reporting, and recalls and field actions.

1.2 **Products Covered.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products supplied under the Agreement. This includes analyzers, reagents, consumables, controls and accessories, and any Products added under Section 5.3 of the Agreement. It also applies to Demo Units supplied to Distributor.

1.3 **Facilities Covered.** This Quality Agreement applies to Supplier's manufacturing and shipping facilities for the Products. It also applies to each distribution center and warehouse that Distributor uses to store Products in the Territory, and to each sub-distributor appointed under Section 2.4 of the Agreement.

## 2. QUALITY SYSTEMS

2.1 **Supplier Quality System.** Supplier will maintain a documented quality management system for the design, manufacture, release, labeling and shipment of the Products. That system will meet the requirements of ISO 13485 or an equivalent standard. Supplier is responsible for the release of each lot of Products against the Specifications before shipment.

2.2 **Distributor Quality System.** Distributor will maintain written procedures for the following:

(a) receipt, inspection, storage, picking and shipment of Products;

(b) temperature monitoring and control, including handling of temperature excursions;

(c) stock rotation on a first-expired, first-out basis;

(d) quarantine and disposition of damaged, expired, returned or recalled Products;

(e) lot and serial number traceability;

(f) receipt and forwarding of complaints and adverse event reports; and

(g) recall execution.

2.3 **Training.** Each party will ensure that its personnel who handle Products, or who receive complaints or adverse event information, are trained on the procedures that apply to their duties. Each party will keep records of that training.

2.4 **Quality Contacts.** Each party's quality contact is named in Section 1 of the Order Form. Each party may change its quality contact by written notice to the other party.

## 3. STORAGE AND HANDLING

3.1 **Storage Conditions.** Distributor will store each Product within the temperature, humidity and other conditions stated in its labeling and the Specifications. As of the date of this Quality Agreement, the storage conditions are:

| Product Category | SKUs | Storage Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Analyzers and accessories | FVD-C200, FVD-H50, FVD-ACC-PRN | 10°C to 30°C, dry |
| Chemistry panel rotors | FVD-C200-CP17, FVD-C200-PP10, FVD-C200-EL6 | 2°C to 8°C, refrigerated |
| Hematology reagent kit | FVD-H50-RK | 2°C to 30°C, do not freeze |
| Quality control sets | FVD-QC-CHEM, FVD-QC-HEM | 2°C to 8°C, refrigerated |

Supplier will notify Distributor in writing of any change to storage conditions, and of the storage conditions for any added Product, before the first shipment to which the change applies.

3.2 **Temperature Monitoring.** Distributor will monitor refrigerated storage areas continuously using calibrated monitoring equipment with alarms. Distributor will keep temperature records for its storage facilities, as required by Section 7.2 of the Agreement, and will make them available to Supplier on request.

3.3 **Excursions in Storage.** If Products in Distributor's storage are exposed to conditions outside their labeled range, Distributor will quarantine the affected Products. It will notify Supplier's quality contact within two (2) business days after it discovers the excursion. The notice will state the Products and lots affected, and the duration and range of the excursion. Supplier will assess the excursion and give written disposition instructions. Distributor will not sell affected Products unless Supplier releases them in writing.

3.4 **Inbound Shipments.** Supplier will ship temperature-controlled reagents in its validated shipping configurations with temperature monitors, as required by Section 6.1 of the Agreement. Distributor will review the shipment monitors on receipt and will report any shortage, visible damage or temperature excursion within the period stated in Section 6.3 of the Agreement.

3.5 **Outbound Shipments.** Distributor will ship temperature-controlled Products to Customers using shipping configurations and methods that Supplier has validated or approved in writing. Distributor will not ship refrigerated Products by a method with a transit time longer than the validated duration of the configuration used.

3.6 **Shelf Life.** Distributor will not ship to a Customer any reagent, consumable or control that has less than sixty (60) days of remaining shelf life, unless the Customer agrees in writing. Distributor will remove expired Products from saleable stock and dispose of them in accordance with applicable law.

## 4. TRACEABILITY AND RECORDS

4.1 **Traceability.** Distributor will keep records that allow each analyzer to be traced by serial number, and each reagent, consumable and control to be traced by lot number, to the Customer that received it. The records will show the date of shipment and the Customer's name and address.

4.2 **Installed Base.** Distributor will keep a current list of Customers with installed analyzers, showing the serial number and installation date of each analyzer. Supplier and its authorized service providers will share installation records with Distributor so that this list stays accurate.

4.3 **Supplier Records.** Supplier will keep device history records, lot release records and distribution records for each lot and serial number shipped to Distributor.

4.4 **Retention.** Each party will keep the records described in this Quality Agreement for at least three (3) years after the relevant transaction, or for any longer period that applicable law requires. For analyzers, records will be kept for at least the expected service life of the analyzer as stated in Supplier's labeling.

4.5 **Access.** Each party will provide copies of the records described in this Section 4 to the other party within five (5) business days after request. In the event of a recall or an investigation of a Priority 1 support case under the Service Level Schedule, it will do so within one (1) business day.

## 5. COMPLAINTS

5.1 **Definition.** A "**Complaint**" is any written, electronic or oral communication that alleges a deficiency in the identity, quality, durability, reliability, performance, labeling or safety of a Product after its release by Supplier.

5.2 **Receipt and Forwarding.** Distributor will record each Complaint it receives. It will notify Supplier's quality contact of each Complaint within two (2) business days after Distributor becomes aware of it, as required by Section 8.2 of the Agreement. The notice will include the following, to the extent known:

(a) the Customer's name and contact details;

(b) the Product, SKU, serial number and lot number;

(c) a description of the alleged deficiency and the date it occurred;

(d) any error codes or test results reported; and

(e) whether an animal was involved, and if so, any effect on the animal.

5.3 **Investigation.** Supplier is responsible for investigating each Complaint and determining its root cause and any corrective and preventive action. Distributor will cooperate with the investigation. This includes helping to retrieve the affected Product or samples from the Customer, where Supplier requests it. Supplier will bear the cost of shipping retrieved Product to Supplier.

5.4 **Customer Communication.** Distributor will handle first-line communication with the Customer, as contemplated by Section 3.1 of the Service Level Schedule. Supplier will provide Distributor with the information needed to respond to the Customer. Distributor will not give the Customer any conclusion about the cause of a Complaint until Supplier has completed its investigation or authorized the communication.

5.5 **Closure.** Supplier will notify Distributor in writing when it closes the investigation of a Complaint that Distributor forwarded. The notice will summarize the findings.

5.6 **Trending.** Supplier will review Complaint data at least quarterly for trends. It will share a summary of Complaint trends for the Products with Distributor at the business reviews held under Section 5.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

## 6. ADVERSE EVENTS

6.1 **Definition.** An "**Adverse Event**" is any event in which a Product is alleged to have caused or contributed to the injury, illness or death of an animal. This includes events in which an incorrect or delayed result from a Product is alleged to have led to inappropriate treatment.

6.2 **Reporting to Supplier.** Distributor will notify Supplier's quality contact of each Adverse Event within one (1) business day after Distributor becomes aware of it, as required by Section 8.2 of the Agreement. The notice will include the information listed in Section 5.2, together with the species and any available clinical details.

6.3 **Regulatory Reporting.** Supplier is responsible for deciding whether an Adverse Event must be reported to any governmental authority. Supplier is also responsible for making any such report. Distributor will provide the information Supplier reasonably needs to do so. If Distributor is itself required by applicable law to report an Adverse Event, it will give Supplier a copy of the report before submitting it, where practicable, and in any event promptly after submission.

6.4 **Handling as Complaint.** Each Adverse Event is also a Complaint and will be investigated under Section 5.

## 7. RECALLS AND FIELD ACTIONS

7.1 **Authority.** As provided in Section 8.3 of the Agreement, Supplier has sole authority to decide whether to recall or withdraw any Product. Supplier also has sole authority to decide whether to issue any field safety notice, correction or other field action.

7.2 **Notice to Distributor.** Supplier will notify Distributor's quality contact in writing of any recall or field action. The notice will identify the affected Products, lots and serial numbers, the reason for the action, the level to which it extends (distributor, Customer or end user), and the actions Distributor must take.

7.3 **Distributor Actions.** Within two (2) business days after receiving Supplier's notice, Distributor will do the following:

(a) quarantine all affected Products in its inventory and stop shipping them;

(b) identify, using the records described in Section 4, all Customers that received affected Products; and

(c) send those Customers the communication that Supplier provides or approves.

Distributor will then retrieve or correct affected Products as Supplier directs.

7.4 **Effectiveness Checks.** Distributor will track Customer responses to recall communications. It will report the status to Supplier weekly, or at any other interval Supplier reasonably requests, until Supplier closes the recall.

7.5 **Distributor-Identified Issues.** If Distributor becomes aware of information suggesting that a recall or field action may be needed, it will notify Supplier's quality contact within one (1) business day.

7.6 **Costs.** Recall costs are borne as provided in Section 8.3 of the Agreement.

7.7 **Mock Recalls.** Once each Contract Year, Supplier may require Distributor to carry out a mock recall of a selected lot or serial number. Distributor will identify all recipients of the selected Product within one (1) business day after the request.

## 8. RETURNS

8.1 **Authorization.** Distributor will not return Products to Supplier without a return authorization number issued by Supplier.

8.2 **Customer Returns.** Distributor will quarantine Products returned by Customers. It will not return refrigerated Products to saleable stock unless Distributor can show that the Products were kept within their labeled storage conditions at all times.

8.3 **Credits.** Credits for returned Products are issued under the Agreement and, where applicable, Section 6.2 of the Order Form.

## 9. AUDITS

9.1 **Supplier Audits of Distributor.** Supplier may audit Distributor's storage and distribution facilities and quality records relating to the Products once each Contract Year. Supplier will give at least fifteen (15) business days' notice, and the audit will take place during normal business hours. Supplier may also carry out an audit for cause on five (5) business days' notice following a significant storage excursion, a recall, or a trend in Complaints related to storage or handling.

9.2 **Audit Findings.** Supplier will provide a written report of its findings within thirty (30) days after the audit. Distributor will respond within thirty (30) days after receiving the report with a corrective action plan for each finding, and will carry out the plan with reasonable diligence.

9.3 **Sub-Distributors.** Distributor will ensure that each sub-distributor is bound by quality obligations at least as protective as this Quality Agreement, and that Supplier has audit rights over each sub-distributor equivalent to those in Section 9.1.

9.4 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each party will notify the other within two (2) business days after it learns of any governmental inspection or inquiry relating to the Products. It will share any resulting findings that relate to the Products, to the extent permitted by law.

## 10. CHANGES

10.1 **Product Changes.** Supplier will notify Distributor of changes to Products in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Agreement. Supplier will notify Distributor in writing of any change to labeling, storage conditions or instructions for use before the first shipment of Products bearing the change.

10.2 **Distributor Changes.** Distributor will notify Supplier in writing at least thirty (30) days before it begins to store Products at a new facility, or makes any material change to its temperature control systems for Products.

## 11. TERM AND GENERAL

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on July 1, 2023 and continues for the Term of the Agreement. Sections 4, 6.3 and 7 continue for as long as either party holds Products or has obligations relating to Products already distributed.

11.2 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Quality Agreement forms part of the Agreement. Section 14 (Confidentiality) of the Agreement applies to information exchanged under this Quality Agreement.

11.3 **Review and Amendment.** The parties' quality contacts will review this Quality Agreement at least once each Contract Year. This Quality Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties, as provided in Section 17.4 of the Agreement.

11.4 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement apply to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full in it.

11.5 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
