# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of July 1, 2023 by and between **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is the Service Level Schedule referred to in Sections 1.13 and 6.4 of the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Order Form.

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 **Services Covered.** This Schedule sets out the service levels that Supplier will meet for:

(a) order acknowledgment and fulfillment;

(b) technical support to Distributor and to Customers; and

(c) field service and warranty repair of analyzers installed at Customer sites in the Territory.

1.2 **Products Covered.** This Schedule applies to all Products supplied under the Agreement, including Products added under Section 5.3 of the Agreement.

1.3 **Measurement Period.** Service levels are measured for each calendar quarter during the Term (each, a "**Measurement Period**").

## 2. ORDER FULFILLMENT

2.1 **Order Acknowledgment.** Supplier will acknowledge receipt of each Purchase Order within one (1) business day. It will accept or reject the Purchase Order within the period stated in Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

2.2 **Lead Times.** Supplier will ship conforming Purchase Orders within the standard lead times stated in Section 7.2 of the Order Form:

| Product Category | Standard Lead Time (from acceptance) |
|---|---|
| Analyzers | 10 business days |
| Reagents, consumables and controls | 5 business days |
| Accessories | 5 business days |

2.3 **On-Time Shipment Target.** In each Measurement Period, Supplier will ship at least ninety-five percent (95%) of accepted order lines within the applicable standard lead time, or on the requested delivery date if that is later ("**On-Time Shipment Rate**").

2.4 **Order Accuracy Target.** In each Measurement Period, at least ninety-eight percent (98%) of order lines shipped will match the accepted Purchase Order in SKU, quantity and lot documentation ("**Order Accuracy Rate**").

2.5 **Shelf Life.** Reagents and consumables will be shipped with the minimum remaining shelf life required by Section 7.3 of the Order Form. An order line shipped with less shelf life, without Distributor's written agreement, counts as an inaccurate line under Section 2.4.

2.6 **Cold-Chain Shipments.** Temperature-controlled reagents will be shipped in Supplier's validated shipping configurations with temperature monitors, as required by Section 6.1 of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement. Supplier will replace or credit any shipment with a temperature excursion reported under Section 6.3 of the Agreement. The replacement will ship within two (2) business days after Supplier receives the report.

2.7 **Backorders.** If Supplier expects that it cannot ship an order line within the applicable lead time, it will notify Distributor within two (2) business days after acceptance. The notice will state the expected ship date. Distributor may cancel a backordered line without charge at any time before shipment.

2.8 **Exclusions.** The following are excluded from the calculation of the On-Time Shipment Rate:

(a) order lines delayed because of a carrier designated by Distributor;

(b) order lines that exceed the binding portion of Distributor's forecast under Section 4.3 of the Agreement by more than twenty percent (20%); and

(c) order lines delayed by a force majeure event under Section 17.2 of the Agreement.

## 3. TECHNICAL SUPPORT

3.1 **Support Tiers.** Distributor will provide first-line support to Customers. This covers installation scheduling, basic operation questions, consumable ordering and initial troubleshooting using Supplier's troubleshooting guides. Supplier will provide second-line support to Distributor's support personnel and, where Distributor escalates a case, directly to Customers.

3.2 **Support Hours.** Supplier will provide telephone and email technical support from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays. It will also provide emergency telephone support for Priority 1 cases at all other times.

3.3 **Priority Levels and Response Times.** Supplier will respond to escalated cases within the following times, measured from receipt of the escalation during support hours or, for Priority 1 cases, at any time:

| Priority | Description | Initial Response | Target Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyzer inoperable or producing results that cannot be relied on, with no workaround | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| 2 | Analyzer operating with material impairment, or a workaround is available | 4 support hours | 3 business days |
| 3 | Minor issue, documentation question or feature request | 1 business day | 10 business days |

3.4 **Response Target.** In each Measurement Period, Supplier will meet the Initial Response time for at least ninety-five percent (95%) of escalated cases at each priority level ("**Response Rate**").

3.5 **Case Records.** Supplier will log each escalated case in its case management system. Each record will show the priority, timestamps, actions taken and resolution. Supplier will give Distributor access to case status for cases Distributor escalates.

3.6 **Complaints and Adverse Events.** A support case that involves a Product complaint or an adverse event involving an animal will also be handled under Section 8.2 of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement. Nothing in this Schedule changes the reporting timelines in those provisions.

## 4. FIELD SERVICE AND WARRANTY REPAIR

4.1 **Installation.** Supplier or its authorized service provider will install and verify each analyzer at the Customer site. Installation will take place within ten (10) business days after the analyzer is delivered to the Customer, or on a later date the Customer requests.

4.2 **Warranty Repair.** For analyzers under warranty under Section 10.1 of the Agreement, Supplier will diagnose the fault remotely where possible. Within one (1) business day after the case is classified as requiring hardware repair, Supplier will either:

(a) ship a replacement analyzer or field-replaceable module to the Customer by priority overnight courier; or

(b) dispatch a field service engineer to arrive at the Customer site within two (2) business days.

4.3 **Loaner Analyzers.** If a warranty repair is not completed within three (3) business days after the hardware repair classification, Supplier will provide a loaner analyzer at no charge until the repair is complete.

4.4 **Field Service Target.** In each Measurement Period, Supplier will meet the times in Section 4.2 for at least ninety percent (90%) of warranty repair cases ("**Field Service Rate**").

4.5 **Out-of-Warranty Service.** Supplier will offer out-of-warranty repair and preventive maintenance to Customers at its then-current service rates. The times in Section 4.2 apply to those services as targets only.

4.6 **Software and Firmware.** Supplier will make analyzer software and firmware updates available to Distributor and Customers. Supplier will give Distributor at least thirty (30) days' advance notice of any update that changes the operating procedure or the reportable analyte menu, except for updates required for safety or regulatory compliance.

## 5. REPORTING AND REVIEW

5.1 **Quarterly Report.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Measurement Period, Supplier will deliver to Distributor a report showing, for that Measurement Period:

(a) the On-Time Shipment Rate and Order Accuracy Rate;

(b) the Response Rate by priority level;

(c) the Field Service Rate; and

(d) a summary of any missed service level, with its cause and the corrective action taken.

5.2 **Business Reviews.** Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each Measurement Period, the parties' commercial contacts named in Section 1 of the Order Form will meet in person or by video conference. They will review the report, Distributor's sales report under Section 4.5 of the Agreement and Distributor's forecast.

5.3 **Corrective Action Plan.** If Supplier misses the same service level in two (2) consecutive Measurement Periods, it will deliver a written corrective action plan within twenty (20) business days after Distributor requests one. Supplier will then implement the plan with reasonable diligence.

## 6. SERVICE CREDITS

6.1 **Credits.** If Supplier misses a service level in a Measurement Period, Distributor will be entitled to the following credits against amounts due for Products:

| Service Level Missed | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| On-Time Shipment Rate | 1% of net invoice value of Products shipped in the Measurement Period |
| Order Accuracy Rate | 0.5% of net invoice value of Products shipped in the Measurement Period |
| Response Rate (any priority) | $5,000 |
| Field Service Rate | $10,000 |

6.2 **Maximum.** Total service credits for any Measurement Period will not exceed five percent (5%) of the net invoice value of Products shipped to Distributor in that Measurement Period.

6.3 **Claiming Credits.** Distributor must claim service credits in writing within sixty (60) days after receiving the quarterly report for the relevant Measurement Period. Supplier will issue a credit note against Distributor's next invoice within thirty (30) days after it receives a valid claim.

6.4 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Service credits do not reduce the net invoice value of Products for purposes of the Minimum Purchase Commitment.

6.5 **Other Remedies.** Service credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the services. They do not limit Distributor's other rights or remedies under the Agreement.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR RESPONSIBILITIES

Supplier's performance of the service levels depends on Distributor doing the following:

(a) delivering forecasts and Purchase Orders as required by Sections 4.1 and 4.3 of the Agreement;

(b) maintaining trained first-line support personnel, as contemplated by Section 7.1 of the Agreement;

(c) escalating cases with the information reasonably required by Supplier's escalation form, including the analyzer serial number, error codes and the lot numbers of the consumables in use; and

(d) giving Supplier's personnel and authorized service providers reasonable access to Distributor's facilities and coordinating access to Customer sites.

A service level is not treated as missed to the extent the miss results from Distributor's failure to meet these responsibilities.

## 8. CHANGES

8.1 **Review.** The parties will review this Schedule at least once each Contract Year.

8.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be changed only in a writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties, as provided in Section 17.4 of the Agreement.

## 9. GENERAL

This Schedule forms part of the Agreement. Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement apply to this Schedule as if set out in full in it. This Schedule may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
