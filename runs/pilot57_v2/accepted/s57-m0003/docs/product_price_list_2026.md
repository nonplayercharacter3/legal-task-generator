## 1. Purpose

1.1 Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc. ("**Supplier**") issues this price list to Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC ("**Distributor**"). It is Supplier's then-current price list for distributors referred to in Section 5.1 of the Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2023, as amended (the "**Agreement**"), and in Section 5.2 of the Order Form – Initial Appointment dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Order Form**").

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this price list have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Order Form.

1.3 This price list is given as written notice of a price change under Section 5.2 of the Agreement. It is delivered more than ninety (90) days before the start of Contract Year 4, the Contract Year in which the prices take effect.

## 2. Effective Period

2.1 The prices in Section 3 apply to all Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027.

2.2 Purchase Orders accepted before July 1, 2026 are invoiced at the prices in effect on the date of acceptance, even if shipment occurs on or after July 1, 2026.

## 4. Ordering and Logistics

4.1 **Minimum Order.** Each Purchase Order must have a net value of at least $10,000, except for replacement analyzers ordered under a warranty claim, as provided in Section 7.1 of the Order Form.

4.2 **Lead Times.** Standard lead times are as stated in Section 7.2 of the Order Form and Section 2.2 of the Service Level Schedule:

| Product Category | Standard Lead Time (from acceptance) |
|---|---|
| Analyzers | 10 business days |
| Reagents, consumables and controls | 5 business days |
| Accessories | 5 business days |

4.3 **Shelf Life.** Reagents and consumables ship with at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life, as provided in Section 7.3 of the Order Form.

4.4 **Storage Conditions.** Storage conditions for each Product are as stated in Section 3.1 of the Quality Agreement. Chemistry panel rotors and quality control sets ship refrigerated in Supplier's validated shipping configurations.

## 5. Invoicing and Payment

5.1 Supplier invoices on shipment. Payment terms are as stated in Section 5.4 of the Agreement: undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

5.2 Payments are made by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing, as provided in Section 8 of the Order Form.

5.3 For purposes of the Minimum Purchase Commitment for Contract Year 4, net invoice value is calculated at the prices actually invoiced under this price list, as provided in Section 6.2 of the Order Form.
