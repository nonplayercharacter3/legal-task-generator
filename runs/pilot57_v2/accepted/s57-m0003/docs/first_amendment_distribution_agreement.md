# FIRST AMENDMENT TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This First Amendment to Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of February 15, 2024 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory and the Field.

B. During the first Contract Year, Distributor's Customers raised questions about the scope of Supplier's indemnity for claims relating to Product labeling, instructions for use and software embedded in the analyzers. The parties have also agreed to adjust the indemnification procedure.

C. The parties wish to restate the indemnification provisions of the Agreement as set out in this Amendment, under Section 17.4 of the Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT OF SECTION 11 (INDEMNIFICATION)

Section 11 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> ## 11. INDEMNIFICATION
>
> 11.1 **Supplier Indemnification.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by a defect in a Product as delivered by Supplier; (b) any allegation that a Product, as delivered by Supplier, including any software or firmware embedded in an analyzer, infringes or misappropriates a U.S. patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party; (c) any statement in Supplier's labeling, instructions for use or approved marketing materials for a Product that is alleged to be false or misleading or to violate applicable law; or (d) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier.
>
> 11.2 **Distributor Indemnification.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Distributor's storage, handling or transportation of Products, including any failure to maintain cold-chain conditions; (b) any representation, warranty or claim about the Products made by Distributor or its sub-distributors that is not authorized by Supplier; (c) Distributor's violation of applicable law, including veterinary, licensing and regulatory requirements; or (d) the negligence or willful misconduct of Distributor or its sub-distributors.
>
> 11.3 **Exclusions from Supplier Indemnity.** Supplier has no obligation under Section 11.1(b) for any claim to the extent it arises from (a) a modification of a Product by anyone other than Supplier or its authorized service providers; (b) the combination or use of a Product with reagents, consumables, equipment or software not supplied or approved in writing by Supplier, where the claim would not have arisen but for that combination or use; or (c) Distributor's continued sale of a Product after Supplier has notified Distributor of a modification or discontinuance under Section 11.5 that would have avoided the claim.
>
> 11.4 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, and in any event within thirty (30) days after the indemnified party receives a written demand, complaint or other formal notice of the claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced. The indemnifying party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, but may not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified party without its written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. The indemnified party shall reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying party's expense and may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense.
>
> 11.5 **Infringement Remedies.** If a Product becomes, or in Supplier's opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Supplier may, at its option, procure the right for Distributor to continue selling the Product, modify the Product so it is non-infringing without material loss of functionality, or discontinue the Product and repurchase Distributor's unsold inventory at the price paid.

## 3. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

3.1 **Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement and the Order Form remain unchanged and in full force and effect. The Agreement and this Amendment are to be read and construed as one instrument.

3.2 **Effective Date.** The amendments in Section 2 apply to all claims first asserted by a third party on or after the Amendment Effective Date. Claims first asserted before the Amendment Effective Date continue to be governed by Section 11 of the Agreement as in effect immediately before the Amendment Effective Date.

3.3 **Representations.** Each party represents to the other that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment, that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it, and that this Amendment is its valid and binding obligation.

## 4. GENERAL

4.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in this Amendment.

4.2 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

4.3 **Headings.** Headings in this Amendment are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
