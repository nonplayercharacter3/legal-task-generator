# ORDER FORM – INITIAL APPOINTMENT

This Order Form – Initial Appointment (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 2. INITIAL TERM

The Initial Term of the Agreement is sixty (60) months from the Effective Date.

## 3. TERRITORY AND FIELD

3.1 **Territory.** The Territory is the United States.

3.2 **Field.** The Field is companion-animal point-of-care chemistry and hematology analyzers and related consumables.

3.3 **Exclusive Appointment.** Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 of the Agreement is exclusive for the Products in the Territory and the Field.

6.2 **Calculation.** Net invoice value means the invoiced price of Products shipped to Distributor in the Contract Year, less credits issued for returns, shortages and nonconforming Products, and excluding freight, insurance and taxes.

6.3 **Supplier Supply Shortfalls.** The Minimum Purchase Commitment for a Contract Year is reduced by the net invoice value of any conforming Purchase Order that Supplier rejected, or failed to ship within sixty (60) days after the requested delivery date, other than because of a breach by Distributor.

## 7. ORDERING AND LOGISTICS

7.1 **Minimum Order.** Each Purchase Order must have a net value of at least $10,000, except for replacement analyzers ordered under a warranty claim.

7.2 **Lead Times.** Standard lead time is ten (10) business days for analyzers and five (5) business days for reagents and consumables, measured from Supplier's acceptance of the Purchase Order.

7.3 **Shelf Life.** Reagents and consumables will have at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life at the time of shipment, unless Distributor agrees otherwise in writing for a particular order.

7.4 **Shipping.** Distributor designates its primary distribution center for receipt of Products and will notify Supplier in writing of any additional delivery locations in the Territory. Temperature-controlled reagents ship in Supplier's validated shipping configurations in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

7.5 **Initial Stocking Order.** Distributor shall place an initial stocking Purchase Order within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date of not less than $750,000 net invoice value. That order counts toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the first Contract Year.

## 8. PAYMENT

Payment terms are as stated in Section 5.4 of the Agreement. Payments are made by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.
