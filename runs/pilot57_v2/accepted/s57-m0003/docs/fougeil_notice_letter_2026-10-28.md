Ladies and Gentlemen:

1. **Agreement.** We refer to the Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2023 (as amended by the First Amendment to Distribution Agreement dated as of February 15, 2024, the "**Agreement**") between Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC ("**Distributor**"). We also refer to the Order Form – Initial Appointment dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Order Form**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Order Form. This letter is a notice under Sections 4.4 and 17.1 of the Agreement.

2. **Shortfall and Conversion.** Section 6.1 of the Order Form sets the Minimum Purchase Commitment for Contract Year 3, the period from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026, at $7,500,000 in net invoice value. Supplier's records show that the net invoice value of Products shipped to Distributor in Contract Year 3, calculated under Section 6.2 of the Order Form, was $6,212,480. This leaves a shortfall of $1,287,520. Supplier has found no rejected or late-shipped conforming Purchase Orders in Contract Year 3 that would reduce the Minimum Purchase Commitment under Section 6.3 of the Order Form. Accordingly, Supplier hereby exercises its right under Section 4.4 of the Agreement to convert the appointment to non-exclusive. Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 of the Agreement will become a non-exclusive appointment for the Products in the Territory and the Field, effective thirty (30) days after the date of this notice, on November 27, 2026.

3. **Supporting Information.** Enclosed is a schedule of Supplier's invoices and credit notes for Contract Year 3, listed by invoice number, invoice date, SKU and net amount. The schedule reconciles to the net invoice value stated in Paragraph 2. If Distributor believes any amount in the schedule is incorrect, please send us the details and supporting documents within fifteen (15) business days after the date of this letter.

4. **Continuing Obligations.** Except for the change to Section 2.1 described in Paragraph 2, the Agreement, the Order Form and the related documents remain in full force and effect. Both parties' rights and obligations continue unchanged. In particular, Distributor remains bound by its forecasting, reporting, storage and handling, and payment obligations under Sections 4.3, 4.5, 5.4 and 7.2 of the Agreement. The Minimum Purchase Commitment for Contract Year 4 under Section 6.1 of the Order Form also continues to apply.

5. **Business Review.** Supplier remains committed to the U.S. companion-animal market and to its relationship with Distributor. We propose that the parties' commercial contacts named in Section 1 of the Order Form discuss Distributor's Contract Year 4 forecast and sales plan at the next quarterly business review under Section 5.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

6. **No Waiver.** This letter does not waive any of Supplier's rights or remedies under the Agreement or applicable law, all of which are reserved.
