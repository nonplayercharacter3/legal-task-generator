# GUARANTY

This Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of July 1, 2023 by **Treireis Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and **Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"), are entering into a Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2023. Under that agreement Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory and the Field. The Distribution Agreement, together with the Order Form and as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, is the "**Agreement**."

B. Distributor is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the transactions contemplated by the Agreement.

C. Section 7.6 of the Agreement requires Distributor to deliver to Supplier, on or before the Effective Date, a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under the Agreement executed by Guarantor. Guarantor is giving this Guaranty to satisfy that requirement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranteed Obligations.** "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all amounts that Distributor owes, now or in the future, to Supplier under or in connection with the Agreement, whether due at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise. They include:

(a) the price of all Products ordered under Purchase Orders and delivered;

(b) late payment interest under Section 5.4 of the Agreement;

(c) amounts that become due under Section 13.3(a) of the Agreement on termination or expiration;

(d) amounts payable by Distributor under Section 11 of the Agreement; and

(e) the costs of enforcement described in Section 7.

Guaranteed Obligations include amounts that would become due but for the operation of any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, and interest that accrues after the start of any such proceeding, whether or not allowed as a claim in it.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Supplier the full and punctual payment of the Guaranteed Obligations when due. This is a guaranty of payment and not of collection.

2.2 **Primary Obligation.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is primary, direct and independent of Distributor's obligations. Supplier may enforce this Guaranty against Guarantor without first:

(a) bringing any action against Distributor or any other person;

(b) pursuing any security; or

(c) exhausting any other remedy.

2.3 **Payment on Demand.** If Distributor fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay that amount to Supplier within ten (10) business days after receiving Supplier's written demand. Payment is made in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account Supplier designates. A demand need only state the amount due and identify the invoice or other obligation to which it relates.

2.4 **Multiple Demands.** Supplier may make more than one demand under this Guaranty.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations arising during the Term, including any Renewal Term. It remains in effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid in full, including those that arise or survive after termination or expiration of the Agreement.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** Guarantor's obligations are not released, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, with or without notice to or consent of Guarantor:

(a) any amendment, supplement, side letter, extension, renewal or other modification of the Agreement. This includes changes to prices, Products, payment terms, credit limits or Minimum Purchase Commitments;

(b) any waiver, forbearance, extension of time or indulgence granted to Distributor;

(c) any release, addition or substitution of any other guarantor or security;

(d) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Distributor or Guarantor, or any assignment of the Agreement by Distributor;

(e) the insolvency, bankruptcy, dissolution or reorganization of Distributor, or any rejection of the Agreement in any bankruptcy proceeding;

(f) any invalidity, irregularity or unenforceability of the Agreement or any Guaranteed Obligation against Distributor; or

(g) any other act, omission or circumstance that might otherwise discharge or provide a defense to a guarantor or surety, other than payment in full of the Guaranteed Obligations.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded, avoided or must be returned by Supplier, this Guaranty is reinstated for that amount. This applies in any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar proceeding, and it applies as though the payment had not been made.

## 4. WAIVERS

4.1 **Waiver of Notices.** Guarantor waives the following:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) notice of the creation, renewal, extension or accrual of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(c) presentment, demand for payment on Distributor, protest and notice of dishonor or nonpayment; and

(d) all other notices to which a guarantor might otherwise be entitled, other than the demand required by Section 2.3.

4.2 **Waiver of Defenses.** Guarantor waives any defense based on the following:

(a) any requirement that Supplier proceed first against Distributor or any security;

(b) any statute of limitations affecting Distributor's liability, to the fullest extent permitted by law; and

(c) any defense available to a guarantor or surety under applicable law, other than payment in full.

4.3 **Defenses of Distributor.** Guarantor may assert any defense that Distributor could assert under the Agreement, other than defenses arising from Distributor's insolvency, lack of capacity or authority, or the unenforceability of the Agreement against Distributor.

## 5. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

5.1 **Deferral of Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations are paid in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any of the following rights against Distributor that arise from payment under this Guaranty:

(a) subrogation;

(b) reimbursement;

(c) contribution; or

(d) indemnity.

5.2 **Subordination.** All present and future debts that Distributor owes to Guarantor are subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations. After Distributor defaults in paying a Guaranteed Obligation, Guarantor shall not accept any payment on those debts until the default is cured. If Guarantor receives such a payment, it holds the payment in trust for Supplier and shall promptly pay it over to Supplier.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND COVENANTS

6.1 **Representations.** Guarantor represents and warrants to Supplier that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally;

(d) executing and performing this Guaranty does not violate its organizational documents, any law that applies to it or any agreement binding on it;

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the membership interests in Distributor; and

(f) it has received a copy of the Agreement and has had the opportunity to review it with counsel.

6.2 **Financial Statements.** For so long as this Guaranty is in effect, Guarantor shall deliver the following to Supplier:

(a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, its consolidated annual financial statements, audited by an independent certified public accountant; and

(b) within sixty (60) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters, its unaudited consolidated quarterly financial statements, certified by its chief financial officer.

Supplier shall treat these statements as confidential information under Section 14 of the Agreement.

6.3 **Notice of Changes.** Guarantor shall notify Supplier in writing within ten (10) business days after any of the following:

(a) a change in its ownership of Distributor;

(b) any sale of all or substantially all of its assets; or

(c) any merger or consolidation in which Guarantor is not the surviving entity.

## 7. COSTS OF ENFORCEMENT

Guarantor shall pay on demand all reasonable costs and expenses that Supplier incurs in enforcing this Guaranty, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs.

## 8. PAYMENTS

8.1 **No Set-Off.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off, counterclaim or deduction. The only exception is withholding required by law. In that case, Guarantor shall pay the additional amounts needed for Supplier to receive the full amount it would have received without the withholding.

8.2 **Application.** Supplier may apply any amount received under this Guaranty to the Guaranteed Obligations in any order it chooses.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the law of another jurisdiction.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor and Supplier, by its acceptance of this Guaranty, each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of New York sitting in New York County and the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

9.3 **Service of Process.** Guarantor consents to service of process in any action described in Section 9.2 by delivery to its address for notices under Section 10.1, in addition to any other method permitted by law.

9.4 **Jury Waiver.** GUARANTOR AND SUPPLIER EACH WAIVE THE RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier. Notices to Supplier go to its address for notices under the Agreement. Notices to Guarantor go to the address stated below its signature, or to any other address Guarantor later designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

10.2 **Assignment.** This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and assigns. Guarantor may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Guaranty without Supplier's prior written consent. Supplier may assign its rights under this Guaranty together with an assignment of its rights under the Agreement.

10.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only in a writing signed by Guarantor and Supplier. No failure or delay by Supplier in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

10.5 **Cumulative Remedies.** Supplier's rights under this Guaranty are in addition to its rights under the Agreement and applicable law.

10.6 **Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Guaranty may be executed and delivered by electronic signature or electronic transmission. Each such signature has the same effect as an original.
