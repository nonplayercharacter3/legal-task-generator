**Re: Launch Support for the Products: Side Letter to Distribution Agreement and Order Form – Initial Appointment**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter (this "**Side Letter**") sets out the launch pricing, demonstration-unit allowance and marketing support that Fougeil Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc. ("**Supplier**") will provide to Treireis Animal Health Distribution, LLC ("**Distributor**"). It is given under Section 5.3 of the Order Form – Initial Appointment dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Order Form**"), issued under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of July 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Order Form.

## 1. Launch Period

1.1 The "**Launch Period**" is the period from the Effective Date through December 31, 2023.

1.2 This Side Letter applies only to Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier, and to activities carried out, during the Launch Period, except where a section states otherwise.

## 2. Launch Pricing

2.1 **Analyzer Introductory Discount.** For Purchase Orders accepted during the Launch Period, Supplier will invoice the analyzers listed below at the following introductory prices. These replace the prices in Section 5.1 of the Order Form for those Purchase Orders only:

| SKU | Product | Order Form Price (USD) | Launch Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FVD-C200 | Fougeil VetChem C200 Chemistry Analyzer | 9,850.00 | 8,865.00 |
| FVD-H50 | Fougeil VetHem H50 Hematology Analyzer | 11,400.00 | 10,260.00 |

2.2 **Quantity Limit.** The launch prices in Section 2.1 apply to no more than 150 units of FVD-C200 and 100 units of FVD-H50 in total. Supplier will invoice units above those quantities at the prices in Section 5.1 of the Order Form.

2.3 **Reagents and Consumables.** Launch pricing does not apply to reagents, consumables, controls or accessories. Those Products are invoiced at the prices in Section 5.1 of the Order Form.

2.4 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Analyzers purchased at launch prices count toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the first Contract Year at their invoiced launch price. This is consistent with the calculation of net invoice value in Section 6.2 of the Order Form.

## 3. Demonstration-Unit Allowance

3.1 **Allowance.** During the Launch Period, Supplier will supply to Distributor, at no charge, up to twenty (20) units of FVD-C200 and fifteen (15) units of FVD-H50 for use as demonstration and evaluation units (the "**Demo Units**"). Each shipment of Demo Units will include one (1) Chemistry Quality Control Set (FVD-QC-CHEM) or one (1) Hematology Control Set (FVD-QC-HEM), as applicable, also at no charge.

3.2 **Ordering.** Distributor will request Demo Units by Purchase Order marked "DEMO." Such Purchase Orders are not subject to the minimum order value in Section 7.1 of the Order Form.

3.3 **Use.** Distributor will use Demo Units only to demonstrate the Products to prospective Customers in the Territory, for evaluations at Customer sites lasting no more than thirty (30) days, and for Distributor's internal sales training. Distributor will not sell, lease or otherwise transfer a Demo Unit except as permitted by Section 3.5.

3.4 **Title and Care.** Supplier retains title to each Demo Unit. Risk of loss passes to Distributor on delivery to the carrier as provided in Section 6.2 of the Agreement. Distributor will store, handle and maintain Demo Units in accordance with the Specifications and the Quality Agreement, and will keep a log of each Demo Unit's serial number, location and evaluation history. Distributor will make that log available to Supplier on request.

3.5 **Conversion.** Distributor may buy any Demo Unit at any time during the Term at fifty percent (50%) of the price for that analyzer under Section 5.1 of the Order Form or Supplier's then-current price list for distributors, whichever applies at the time. The unit must first be refurbished and re-verified by Supplier or its authorized service provider at Supplier's cost.

3.6 **Exclusion from Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Demo Units and the controls supplied with them are not invoiced. They do not count toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment. A Demo Unit bought under Section 3.5 counts toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which it is invoiced, at its invoiced price.

3.7 **Return.** On termination or expiration of the Agreement, Distributor will return all Demo Units it has not bought to Supplier's facility at Distributor's cost, in the same condition as delivered, except for reasonable wear and tear.

## 4. Marketing Support

4.1 **Launch Marketing Fund.** Supplier will make available a launch marketing fund of $250,000 (the "**Fund**"). The Fund will reimburse Distributor's documented, out-of-pocket expenses for the following activities in the Territory during the Launch Period:

(a) trade show and veterinary conference participation featuring the Products;

(b) Customer seminars, continuing-education events and webinars about the Products;

(c) printed and digital advertising using Supplier's approved marketing materials; and

(d) sales incentive programs for Distributor's sales force related to the Products.

4.2 **Approval.** Distributor will submit a launch marketing plan to Supplier by July 31, 2023. Each activity to be reimbursed from the Fund requires Supplier's prior written approval of the plan or of the specific activity. Supplier will not unreasonably withhold that approval. All materials must comply with Section 7.4 of the Agreement and Supplier's brand guidelines under Section 9.1 of the Agreement.

4.3 **Claims and Payment.** Distributor will submit reimbursement claims, with receipts and reasonable supporting documentation, by January 31, 2024. Supplier will pay approved claims by credit note against Distributor's next invoice or, at Distributor's election, by ACH within thirty (30) days after approval. Any part of the Fund not claimed by January 31, 2024 lapses.

4.4 **Supplier Launch Resources.** During the Launch Period, Supplier will also provide at its own cost:

(a) two (2) field application specialists to accompany Distributor's sales representatives on Customer visits and installations;

(b) up to four (4) regional sales training sessions for Distributor's sales force, in addition to the training programs referred to in Section 7.1 of the Agreement; and

(c) launch marketing collateral, including product brochures, panel menus and comparison sheets, in quantities reasonably requested by Distributor.

## 5. Initial Stocking Order

5.1 Analyzers included in the initial stocking Purchase Order under Section 7.5 of the Order Form are eligible for the launch prices in Section 2.1, within the quantity limits in Section 2.2.

5.2 Section 7.5 of the Order Form requires the initial stocking order to have a net invoice value of not less than $750,000. That value is measured at the prices actually invoiced, including launch prices.

## 6. General

6.1 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Side Letter supplements the Order Form as contemplated by Section 5.3 of the Order Form. Except as stated in this Side Letter, the Agreement and the Order Form continue unchanged and in full force and effect.

6.2 **Confidentiality.** The terms of this Side Letter are confidential information of each party under Section 14 of the Agreement.

6.3 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it.

6.4 **Amendment.** This Side Letter may be amended only in a writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

6.5 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
