## 2. Schedule 4.4: No Conflicts; Consents

2.1 **Exclusive License Agreement.** Under Section 14.3 of the Exclusive License Agreement dated March 3, 2019, between the Company and Orlund BioSciences GmbH ("**Orlund**"), as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated November 18, 2021 and Amendment No. 2 dated February 9, 2024, the Company may not assign the Exclusive License Agreement, and no change of control of the Company may occur, without the prior written consent of Orlund. Under the Exclusive License Agreement, a "change of control" includes the sale of more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting securities of the Company. If a change of control of the Company occurs without that consent, Orlund may terminate the Exclusive License Agreement by written notice to the Company. The consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Agreement will constitute a change of control of the Company for purposes of Section 14.3 of the Exclusive License Agreement. The Company delivered written notice of the proposed transaction to Orlund on August 27, 2026.

2.2 **Governmental Approvals.** The sale of the Shares to Buyer requires the following approvals of change of ownership:

(a) New Jersey Board of Pharmacy: nonresident manufacturer license and wholesale distributor license;

(b) California State Board of Pharmacy: nonresident manufacturer license;

(c) Texas State Board of Pharmacy: manufacturer registration; and

(d) Florida Board of Pharmacy: manufacturer license.

2.3 **FDA Notifications.** Following the Closing, the Company will update its FDA establishment registration and drug listing information to reflect the change in ownership, as required by 21 C.F.R. Part 207. No prior FDA approval is required.

## 10. Schedule 4.15: Product Liability

10.1 **Company Products.** The Company currently markets Velnarix and Ostrevan. Until November 2024, the Company also manufactured and sold Lorvexa oral suspension. Lorvexa was discontinued and withdrawn from the market as described in Schedule 4.12, item 8.2.

10.2 **Pending Product Liability Claims (Lorvexa)**

(a) *Dunmore et al. v. Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.*, No. 2:25-cv-04417 (D.N.J.). Four plaintiffs allege dosing-related adverse events from Lorvexa oral suspension linked to alleged inconsistency in suspension concentration. The Company's motion to dismiss is pending.

(b) *Pereira v. Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.*, No. 25-CV-11902 (Pa. Ct. Com. Pl., Phila. Cnty.). A single plaintiff alleges pediatric adverse events from Lorvexa oral suspension. The case is in fact discovery.

(c) *Holcombe v. Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.*, No. 2026-CA-002251 (Fla. Cir. Ct., Hillsborough Cnty.). A single plaintiff alleges injury from Lorvexa oral suspension. The complaint was served on July 30, 2026, and no answer has yet been filed.

10.3 **Threatened Claims.** By a demand letter dated August 12, 2026, counsel for two additional individuals asserted claims relating to Lorvexa oral suspension. No action has been filed.

10.4 **Insurance.** The Company has tendered each matter in items 10.2 and 10.3 to its product liability insurer under the policy listed in Schedule 4.18, item 12.1(b). The insurer has accepted coverage subject to a reservation of rights.

10.5 **Recalls and Withdrawals.** The voluntary market withdrawal of Lorvexa oral suspension described in Schedule 4.12, item 8.2. There has been no other recall or market withdrawal of any Company Product.
