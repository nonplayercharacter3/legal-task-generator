FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (together with its permitted successors and assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal amount of TWENTY-FOUR MILLION DOLLARS ($24,000,000). This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026, between the Maker, as Buyer, and the Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Note:

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Products**" means the products listed on Exhibit A to this Note.

"**Credit Agreement**" means the Maker's existing credit agreement with its lenders, as amended, restated, supplemented, refinanced or replaced from time to time.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Losses**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Maturity Date**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Product Liability Claim**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Senior Debt**" means all obligations of the Maker and its subsidiaries under the Credit Agreement, including principal, interest (including interest accruing after the start of any insolvency proceeding, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses, indemnities and reimbursement obligations.

"**Senior Lenders**" means the lenders and agents under the Credit Agreement.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Note, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Note. Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Maturity; Payment of Principal

2.1 **Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal amount of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**").

2.2 **No Amortization.** No principal shall be payable before the Maturity Date, except as provided in Section 3.3 or Section 7.2.

2.3 **Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the Holder at least five Business Days before the payment date. If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day, and interest shall accrue for the additional days.

2.4 **Application of Payments.** Payments shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal.

## 3. Interest; Prepayment

3.1 **Rate.** The unpaid principal amount of this Note shall bear simple interest at a rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum, calculated on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed, from the Issue Date until paid in full.

3.2 **Interest Payment Dates.** Accrued interest shall be payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date after the Issue Date, and on the Maturity Date.

3.3 **Optional Prepayment.** The Maker may prepay this Note, in whole or in part, at any time without premium or penalty, together with accrued interest on the principal prepaid, on at least five Business Days' prior written notice to the Holder.

3.4 **Default Interest.** While an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid principal amount shall bear interest at the rate in Section 3.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

## 4. Set-Off

4.1 **Right of Set-Off.** The Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable under this Note the amount of any Losses arising from Product Liability Claims relating to Company Products for which any Buyer Indemnified Party is entitled to indemnification by the Holder under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

4.2 **Notice.** To exercise the right under Section 4.1, the Maker shall give the Holder written notice, at least ten Business Days before the payment date against which the set-off is to be applied, stating the amount to be set off and describing the Product Liability Claim and the Losses in reasonable detail.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** If the Holder disputes a set-off in writing within ten Business Days after receipt of the notice under Section 4.2, the Maker may withhold the disputed amount from the payment then due until the dispute is finally resolved by written agreement of the Maker and the Holder or by a final, non-appealable judgment under Section 10. Within five Business Days after final resolution, the Maker shall pay to the Holder any withheld amount determined not to be subject to set-off, together with interest on it at the rate in Section 3.1 from the original payment date.

4.4 **Effect of Set-Off.** Any amount set off under this Section 4 shall be treated as a payment of principal and interest under this Note, applied in accordance with Section 2.4, and as a satisfaction of the corresponding indemnification obligation of the Holder under the Purchase Agreement to the extent of the amount set off. A good-faith exercise of the right under this Section 4 shall not constitute an Event of Default.

## 5. Representations of the Maker

The Maker represents to the Holder that (a) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Note; (b) this Note has been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action of the Maker; and (c) this Note constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of the Maker, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws and general principles of equity.

## 6. Subordination

6.1 **Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner provided in this Section 6.

6.2 **Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or other event of default under the Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing, and the Senior Lenders have given the Maker and the Holder written notice of a payment block, the Maker may make, and the Holder may receive, regularly scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note. A payment block under clause (ii) above shall last no more than 179 days in any 360-day period.

6.3 **Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding of the Maker, the Senior Debt shall first be paid in full in cash before the Holder receives any payment under this Note.

6.4 **Turnover.** Any payment received by the Holder in violation of this Section 6 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lenders and promptly paid over to them.

6.5 **Subordination Agreement.** The Holder shall, on request of the Maker or the Senior Lenders, execute a subordination agreement with the Senior Lenders on terms consistent with this Section 6.

## 7. Events of Default

7.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, other than an amount withheld or set off under Section 4 or a payment blocked under Section 6, and the failure continues for ten Business Days after written notice from the Holder;

(b) the Maker breaches any other covenant in this Note and the breach continues for 30 days after written notice from the Holder;

(c) the Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or commences any voluntary bankruptcy, insolvency or reorganization proceeding; or

(d) an involuntary bankruptcy, insolvency or reorganization proceeding is commenced against the Maker and is not dismissed within 60 days.

7.2 **Remedies.** Subject to Section 6, (a) on the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c) or (d), the unpaid principal amount of this Note and all accrued interest shall automatically become immediately due and payable; and (b) on the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, the Holder may, by written notice to the Maker, declare the unpaid principal amount of this Note and all accrued interest immediately due and payable.

7.3 **Costs of Collection.** The Maker shall pay the reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by the Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. Transfer

8.1 **Restrictions.** The Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of the Maker, except to an Affiliate of the Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note, including Sections 4 and 6. Any transferee takes this Note subject to all rights of set-off of the Maker under Section 4.

8.2 **Assignment by Maker.** The Maker may assign its obligations under this Note to any successor to all or substantially all of its business, provided that the successor assumes this Note in writing.

## 9. Notices

All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, set forth in Section 11.2 (Notices) of the Purchase Agreement, with references to Buyer read as references to the Maker and references to Seller read as references to the Holder.

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, including any dispute regarding payment or set-off under it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

10.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Maker and the Holder irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note, and waive any objection to venue in that court.

10.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** THE MAKER AND THE HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Waivers by Maker.** The Maker waives presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor of this Note.

11.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder and, while any Senior Debt is outstanding, any amendment of Section 6 also requires the consent of the Senior Lenders.

11.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11.4 **Usury.** In no event shall interest under this Note exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to principal or refunded to the Maker.

11.5 **Cancellation.** On payment in full of this Note, including by set-off under Section 4, the Holder shall promptly return this Note to the Maker marked "paid in full."

11.6 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, the Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms on receipt of a customary affidavit and indemnity from the Holder.
