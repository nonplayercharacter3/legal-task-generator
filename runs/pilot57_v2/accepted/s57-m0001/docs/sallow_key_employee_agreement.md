# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**") by and between **Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Imogen Sallow** (the "**Executive**"). The Company and the Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Executive is employed by the Company as its Chief Scientific Officer under an employment letter dated January 6, 2020 (the "**Prior Employment Letter**").

B. On the Signing Date, Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Peitort**"), and Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Peitort will purchase all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from Seller (the "**Transaction**").

C. The Executive's continued service is important to the Company and to Peitort. As a condition to its willingness to enter into the Purchase Agreement, Peitort has required that the Executive enter into this Agreement, effective as of the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and the Executive's continued employment, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Effectiveness; Term

1.1 **Effective Date.** This Agreement shall become effective automatically on, and subject to, the occurrence of the Closing (as defined in the Purchase Agreement). The date on which the Closing occurs is the "**Effective Date**." Until the Effective Date, the Prior Employment Letter shall continue to govern the Executive's employment.

1.2 **Termination of Purchase Agreement.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect, and the Prior Employment Letter shall continue in effect.

1.3 **Term.** The Executive's employment under this Agreement shall begin on the Effective Date and continue until the third anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). Thereafter the term shall renew automatically for successive one-year periods, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. Employment may end earlier under Section 5. The period of employment under this Agreement is the "**Employment Period**."

## 2. Position and Duties

2.1 **Position.** During the Employment Period, the Executive shall serve as Chief Scientific Officer of the Company and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company. After the Effective Date, the Executive shall also hold the title of Senior Vice President, Specialty Injectables, of Peitort.

2.2 **Duties.** The Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customarily associated with the position of chief scientific officer of a specialty pharmaceutical company. These include oversight of research and development, formulation science, technical transfer, scientific support for regulatory submissions, and scientific liaison with the Company's licensors, including Orlund BioSciences GmbH under the Exclusive License Agreement covering Velnarix.

2.3 **Full Time and Attention.** The Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time and attention to the business of the Company. The Executive may (a) serve on the boards of up to two non-competing companies or non-profit organizations with the prior written consent of the Chief Executive Officer, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, (b) engage in academic, teaching and professional society activities, and (c) manage her personal investments. In each case, these activities must not materially interfere with her duties under this Agreement or breach Section 7.

2.4 **Place of Performance.** The Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's facility at 1150 Corporate Drive, Bridgewater, New Jersey, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. Compensation

3.1 **Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $465,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices (the "**Base Salary**"). The Base Salary shall be reviewed at least annually and may be increased, but not decreased, by the Company.

3.2 **Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Employment Period, the Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 45% of the Base Salary (the "**Target Bonus**"). The actual bonus shall be based on the achievement of corporate and individual performance goals set by the Company after consultation with the Executive. For the 2026 fiscal year, the Executive's bonus shall be determined under the Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc. Annual Incentive Bonus Plan (2026 plan year). Any bonus shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates, provided that the Executive is employed on the payment date, except as provided in Section 6.

3.3 **Signing Equity Award.** Within 60 days after the Effective Date, Peitort's compensation committee will be recommended to grant the Executive restricted stock units with a grant-date value of $1,200,000. The award shall be made under, and subject to, the terms of Peitort's equity incentive plan and award agreement. It shall vest in three equal annual installments beginning on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, subject to continued employment.

3.4 **Long-Term Incentives.** Beginning with Peitort's 2027 annual grant cycle, the Executive shall be eligible for annual long-term incentive awards on the same basis as similarly situated senior executives of Peitort.

## 4. Benefits and Expenses

4.1 **Benefits.** During the Employment Period, the Executive shall be eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans maintained by the Company or Peitort for similarly situated executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

4.2 **Paid Time Off.** The Executive shall be entitled to 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to Company holidays, in accordance with Company policy.

4.3 **Business Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in the performance of her duties in accordance with Company policy.

4.4 **Indemnification and Insurance.** The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by its organizational documents and applicable law. The Company shall also cover the Executive under its directors and officers liability insurance on the same basis as other officers of the Company.

## 5. Termination of Employment

5.1 **Death or Disability.** The Executive's employment shall end automatically on her death. The Company may terminate the Executive's employment on written notice if she has a Disability. "**Disability**" means the Executive's inability, due to physical or mental incapacity, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period.

5.2 **By the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means any of the following: (a) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of her duties that causes material harm to the Company; (c) a material breach of Section 7 or 8; (d) the Executive's debarment or exclusion under 21 U.S.C. § 335a or from any federal health care program; or (e) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties. Before termination for Cause under clause (b), (c) or (e), the Company shall give the Executive written notice describing the grounds, and the Executive shall have 30 days to cure, if the matter can be cured.

5.3 **By the Company Without Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause on 30 days' written notice.

5.4 **By the Executive for Good Reason.** The Executive may terminate her employment for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means any of the following without the Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in the Base Salary or Target Bonus; (b) a material diminution in her title, authority, duties or responsibilities; (c) a relocation of her principal place of employment by more than 40 miles; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company. To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give written notice within 60 days after the initial occurrence of the condition. The Company must then fail to cure within 30 days after that notice, and the Executive must resign within 30 days after the end of the cure period.

5.5 **By the Executive Without Good Reason.** The Executive may resign without Good Reason on 60 days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period, and the waiver shall not change the nature of the termination.

5.6 **Non-Renewal.** Non-renewal by the Company under Section 1.3 shall be treated as a termination without Cause. Non-renewal by the Executive shall be treated as a resignation without Good Reason.

## 6. Payments on Termination

6.1 **Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive (a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the termination date, (b) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid, (c) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent payable under Company policy, and (d) unreimbursed business expenses (together, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

6.2 **Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following in addition to the Accrued Obligations, subject to Section 6.3:

(a) continued payment of the Base Salary for 12 months after the termination date (18 months if the termination occurs within 12 months after the Effective Date);

(b) a pro-rated Target Bonus for the year of termination, paid when annual bonuses are paid to other executives;

(c) payment or reimbursement of COBRA premiums for the Executive and her eligible dependents for up to 12 months, ending earlier if she becomes eligible for comparable coverage from a new employer; and

(d) accelerated vesting of the portion of the award under Section 3.3 that would have vested within 12 months after the termination date.

6.3 **Release.** The severance in Section 6.2 is conditioned on the Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company, Peitort and their affiliates in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company. The release must become effective within 60 days after the termination date. Payments shall begin on the first payroll date after the release becomes effective. If the 60-day period spans two calendar years, payments shall begin in the second year.

6.4 **No Mitigation.** The Executive shall not be required to mitigate the amount of any payment under this Section 6. Except as stated in Section 6.2(c), no payment shall be reduced by compensation earned from other employment.

## 7. Restrictive Covenants

7.1 **Confidential Information.** The Executive shall not, during or after the Employment Period, use or disclose any Confidential Information except in the performance of her duties or as required by law. "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Company, Peitort and their affiliates. It includes formulations, manufacturing processes, analytical methods, clinical and regulatory data, supplier and customer information, and information received from licensors and other third parties under confidentiality obligations. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive shall not be held liable under any trade secret law for disclosures made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report a suspected violation of law, or made in a sealed filing in a lawsuit.

7.2 **Non-Competition.** During the Employment Period and for 12 months after it ends for any reason (the "**Restricted Period**"), the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, provide services in a scientific, research, development or executive capacity to any business that develops, manufactures or commercializes a sterile injectable product that competes with Velnarix or Ostrevan in the United States or Canada. The Executive may hold up to 2% of the publicly traded securities of any company.

7.3 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) solicit or hire any employee of the Company or Peitort with whom she worked or about whom she obtained Confidential Information, or (b) induce any licensor, supplier, distributor or customer of the Company to end or adversely change its relationship with the Company. General advertisements not targeted at Company employees do not violate clause (a).

7.4 **Reasonableness.** The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 7 are reasonable in scope and duration, are necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests, including its goodwill acquired by Peitort in the Transaction, and are supported by adequate consideration. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 8. Inventions and Intellectual Property

8.1 **Assignment.** The Executive hereby assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in any invention, discovery, formulation, process, data, work of authorship or improvement that she conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment with the Company and that relates to the business or actual or reasonably anticipated research of the Company, or results from work performed for the Company (together, "**Inventions**"). This assignment includes Inventions made under the Prior Employment Letter, to the extent not already assigned.

8.2 **Excluded Inventions.** This Section 8 does not apply to any invention that the Executive develops entirely on her own time without using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities or Confidential Information, unless it relates to the Company's business or results from work performed for the Company.

8.3 **Cooperation.** During and after the Employment Period, the Executive shall cooperate reasonably, at the Company's expense, in obtaining and enforcing patents and other protection for Inventions, including by signing applications and assignments.

8.4 **Licensor Obligations.** The Executive acknowledges that Inventions relating to Velnarix may be subject to obligations of the Company to Orlund BioSciences GmbH under the Exclusive License Agreement. She agrees to comply with the Company's reasonable instructions to enable it to meet those obligations.

## 9. Return of Property

On termination of employment, or earlier on request, the Executive shall return to the Company all Company property, including documents, laboratory notebooks, data and devices, and all copies of Confidential Information in her possession.

## 10. Section 409A

The Parties intend that payments under this Agreement be exempt from or comply with Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and this Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. References to termination of employment mean a "separation from service." If the Executive is a "specified employee" when she separates from service, any payment of deferred compensation that is subject to Section 409A and payable on that separation shall be delayed until the first business day after the six-month anniversary of the separation, to the extent required to avoid tax under Section 409A.

## 11. General Provisions

11.1 **Prior Employment Letter.** As of the Effective Date, this Agreement supersedes the Prior Employment Letter. Inventions and confidentiality obligations under the Prior Employment Letter for periods before the Effective Date shall remain in effect.

11.2 **Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign this Agreement to Peitort or to any successor to all or substantially all of the Company's business.

11.3 **Third-Party Beneficiary.** Peitort is an intended third-party beneficiary of Sections 7 and 8 and may enforce them.

11.4 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company shall be sent to Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc., 1150 Corporate Drive, Bridgewater, New Jersey 08807, Attention: Chief Executive Officer, with a copy after the Effective Date to Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc., 400 Riverside Commons, Suite 1200, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, Attention: General Counsel. Notices to the Executive shall be sent to the most recent home address in the Company's records.

11.5 **Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

11.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

11.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in effect, subject to Section 7.4.

11.8 **Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict of law rules. Any action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in New Jersey, and each Party submits to their jurisdiction. The Company may seek injunctive relief to enforce Sections 7 and 8 without posting bond.

11.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.

11.10 **Acknowledgment.** The Executive acknowledges that she has read this Agreement, has had the opportunity to consult counsel of her choice, and enters into it voluntarily.
