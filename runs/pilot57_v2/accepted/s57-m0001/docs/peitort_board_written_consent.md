# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PEITORT PHARMACEUTICAL HOLDINGS, INC.

**(a Delaware corporation)**

**Effective as of September 12, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), act by written consent without a meeting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and Section 3.9 of the Amended and Restated Bylaws of the Corporation (the "**Bylaws**"). The undersigned hereby waive all notice and adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if they had been duly adopted at a meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

---

## 1. Background

1.1 The Corporation has negotiated the acquisition of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**").

1.2 The Company is a specialty pharmaceutical company. Its lead product, Velnarix, is a sterile injectable manufactured and commercialized under an Exclusive License Agreement between the Company and Orlund BioSciences GmbH.

1.3 The Corporation and Seller entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated June 2026 regarding the proposed acquisition (the "**Letter of Intent**"). The Corporation has since conducted business, legal, regulatory, financial and tax due diligence on the Company under the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between the Corporation and Seller.

1.4 The Board has received a substantially final draft of the Stock Purchase Agreement between the Corporation, as buyer, and Seller (together with its exhibits and schedules, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). It has also received a substantially final draft of the Disclosure Schedule, the form of subordinated promissory note to be issued by the Corporation to Seller at the closing (the "**Seller Note**"), and the employment agreement between the Company and Dr. Imogen Sallow, the Company's Chief Scientific Officer (the "**Key Employee Agreement**").

1.5 At meetings held on August 20, 2026 and September 10, 2026, the Board received presentations on the proposed transaction (the "**Transaction**") from management, the Corporation's outside legal counsel and its financial advisor. The Board discussed those presentations with them.

---

## 2. Approval of the Transaction and the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has determined that the Transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders.

**RESOLVED**, that the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, and the Transaction are approved and authorized in all respects. This includes the purchase of the Shares for a base purchase price of $186,000,000, subject to the working capital adjustment provided in the Purchase Agreement, payable as follows:

(a) an amount in cash of $162,000,000, plus or minus the estimated working capital adjustment, at the closing; and

(b) the issuance to Seller at the closing of the Seller Note in the principal amount of $24,000,000.

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Corporation. Each may do so with such changes as that Authorized Officer, acting with the advice of counsel, approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Corporation. Execution and delivery of the Purchase Agreement by an Authorized Officer shall be conclusive evidence of that approval.

---

## 3. Approval of the Seller Note

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Seller Note are approved, substantially in the form presented to the Board. These include a principal amount of $24,000,000 and maturity 36 months after the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement).

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver the Seller Note at the closing in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, with such changes as that Authorized Officer approves consistent with the preceding resolution in Section 2. The Seller Note, once executed and delivered, shall constitute an obligation of the Corporation.

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has determined that the incurrence of indebtedness evidenced by the Seller Note is permitted under the Corporation's existing credit agreement. The Chief Financial Officer is authorized to deliver any notice or certificate to the Corporation's lenders that the credit agreement requires in connection with the Seller Note.

---

## 4. Funding of the Cash Consideration

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation is authorized to fund the cash portion of the purchase price and related transaction expenses from cash on hand and from borrowings under the revolving credit facility of the Corporation's existing credit agreement. The Chief Financial Officer is authorized to submit borrowing requests and take all related actions.

---

## 5. Ancillary Agreements and Closing Deliverables

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, each agreement, instrument and certificate to be delivered by the Corporation under the Purchase Agreement. This includes the officer's certificate described in Section 7.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary or any Assistant Secretary of the Corporation is authorized to certify these resolutions. Any such officer may deliver a certified copy to Seller at the closing as required by Section 3.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation, as the sole stockholder of the Company following the closing, is authorized to elect the directors of the Company designated by the Chief Executive Officer. It is also authorized to cause the Company to perform the Key Employee Agreement in accordance with its terms.

---

## 6. Regulatory Filings and Consents

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to cooperate with Seller and the Company in obtaining the Required Consents (as defined in the Purchase Agreement). In doing so, they may prepare, execute and file any applications, notices or other documents with the state boards of pharmacy or any other Governmental Authority (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) that they deem necessary or appropriate. They may also pay any customary filing fees in connection with those filings.

---

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to do and perform all such further acts, to execute and deliver all such further documents, and to pay all such fees and expenses as any of them deems necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions and to consummate the Transaction. The taking of any such action shall be conclusive evidence of that determination.

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer, employee or agent of the Corporation in connection with the Transaction and consistent with these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects. This includes the negotiation and execution of the Letter of Intent and the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement.

---

## 8. Effectiveness and Counterparts

8.1 This written consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or by PDF transmission. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.

8.2 This written consent shall be effective as of the date first written above upon the execution of counterparts by all of the members of the Board. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
