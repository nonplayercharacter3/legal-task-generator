# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026, by and between **Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3 (as adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Base Purchase Price as follows:

(a) an amount in cash equal to $162,000,000, plus or minus the estimated adjustment under Section 2.3(a), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(b) by issuing and delivering to Seller a subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $24,000,000, substantially in the form attached as Exhibit B (the "**Seller Note**").

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing, together with reasonable supporting detail. The cash payment under Section 2.2(a) shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which estimated net working capital exceeds or falls short of $14,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing. Seller may dispute the statement by written notice within 30 days after receipt. Disputes the Parties cannot resolve within 30 days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of that firm shall be borne by the Parties in inverse proportion to the extent each prevails.

(c) Any final adjustment shall be paid in cash within five Business Days after final determination.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. New York time on the third Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates for the Shares duly endorsed or accompanied by stock powers; (b) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (c) the certificate described in Section 7.2(c); (d) copies of each Required Consent; (e) an IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and (f) a good standing certificate of the Company dated within five Business Days before the Closing Date.

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the cash payment under Section 2.2(a); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the certificate described in Section 7.3(c); and (d) a certified copy of the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain its permits and FDA registrations, and not (a) amend its organizational documents, (b) issue or sell any equity, (c) incur indebtedness for borrowed money outside the ordinary course, (d) enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract, or (e) declare or pay any dividend other than cash dividends paid before the Closing.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to satisfy the conditions to Closing within its control and to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**6.4 Required Consents.** Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use reasonable best efforts to obtain each Required Consent before the Closing, and Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts. Neither Party shall be required to pay any consideration to obtain a Required Consent, other than customary filing fees.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** The Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller shall remain in effect until the Closing in accordance with its terms.

**6.6 Tax Matters.**

(a) Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all tax returns of the Company for Pre-Closing Tax Periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least 30 days before filing.

(b) Taxes for any Straddle Period shall be apportioned to the Pre-Closing Tax Period on a closing-of-the-books basis, except that property and similar ad valorem Taxes shall be apportioned on a daily basis.

(c) Transfer taxes arising from the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**6.7 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or any material part of its assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.8 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Buyer, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), (b) and (f) have been satisfied;

(d) each Required Consent shall have been obtained and shall be in full force and effect;

(e) the Key Employee Agreement shall be in full force and effect and Dr. Imogen Sallow shall not have repudiated it; and

(f) since the date of this Agreement, there shall not have occurred any Material Adverse Effect.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Seller, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Buyer certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and (b) have been satisfied.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 31, 2027, provided that the right to terminate under this clause is not available to a Party whose breach caused the failure to close; (c) by either Party if a final, non-appealable order of a Governmental Authority prohibits the Closing; or (d) by either Party upon a material breach by the other Party that would cause a condition in Section 7.2 or 7.3 not to be satisfied and that is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.5, this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a Party from liability for Fraud or willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until twelve (12) months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until the sixth anniversary of the Closing Date. Covenants to be performed after the Closing shall survive until fully performed. No claim for breach of a representation or warranty may be made after the expiration of its survival period, but any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Buyer Indemnified Parties from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes of the Company for any Pre-Closing Tax Period and any Taxes of Seller, provided that claims under this Section 9.2(c) may be made only during the survival period applicable to the representations and warranties in Section 4.11; and

(d) any Product Liability Claim relating to any Company Product, to the extent arising from the manufacture, sale or distribution of that Company Product before the Closing, including the pending claims set forth on Schedule 4.15.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Seller Indemnified Parties from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement or the Seller Note; and

(c) any payment or performance by any Seller Indemnified Party under any Seller Guarantee, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**9.4 Basket.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Section 9.2(a) exceed $1,395,000 (the "**Deductible**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The Deductible shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud or from any breach of a Fundamental Representation.

**9.5 Materiality Scrape.** For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier.

**9.6 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Base Purchase Price ($18,600,000) (the "**General Cap**").

(b) The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the General Cap.

(c) The General Cap shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud or any breach of a Fundamental Representation or Section 4.12 (Regulatory Compliance), and the aggregate liability of Seller for those Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party with respect to them, less the costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

(e) No Party shall be liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.7 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying Party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

(b) For any claim brought by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within 20 days after receipt of notice of the claim. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money, is fully paid by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

(c) Seller may not assume the defense of any third-party claim involving a Governmental Authority or seeking injunctive relief against the Company.

**9.8 Payments.** Any amount owed under this Article IX shall be paid within ten Business Days after final determination by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**9.9 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which any Buyer Indemnified Party is entitled under this Article IX against any amount otherwise payable by Buyer under the Seller Note, subject to the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.10 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement, except for claims of Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the determination of the Purchase Price under Section 2.3.

**9.11 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEES
