# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of May 4, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Peitort**"), and **Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Bratousk**"). Peitort and Bratousk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Bratousk owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Peitort would acquire the Company (the "**Proposed Transaction**"). To do so, each Party expects to disclose to the other non-public information about itself and, in the case of Bratousk, about the Company.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that is disclosed or made available by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives, whether before or after the Effective Date, in connection with the Proposed Transaction. Information disclosed by or on behalf of Bratousk includes information about the Company. Confidential Information includes:

(a) financial statements, projections, budgets and pricing information;

(b) formulations, manufacturing processes, analytical methods, specifications and know-how;

(c) clinical, regulatory and quality data, including FDA correspondence, inspection reports and responses;

(d) contracts, including license, supply, distribution and lease agreements, and information received from licensors, suppliers and other third parties under confidentiality obligations;

(e) information about employees, compensation and benefit plans;

(f) materials posted in any electronic data room maintained for the Proposed Transaction; and

(g) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

1.2 Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Parties are evaluating the Proposed Transaction, the existence and content of discussions between them, and the terms of any proposal (together, "**Transaction Information**").

1.3 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is or becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, if that source is not known by the Receiving Party, after reasonable inquiry, to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party or the Company; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

1.4 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and the directors, managers, officers, employees, agents, lenders and prospective financing sources, and professional advisors (including legal counsel, accountants, financial advisors and consultants) of that Party and its Affiliates. "**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party. For Bratousk, Affiliates do not include portfolio companies of investment funds managed by Bratousk, other than the Company, unless they have received Confidential Information. "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

## 2. Use and Non-Disclosure

2.1 **Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and complete the Proposed Transaction, and for no other purpose.

2.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential. It shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any Person, except to its Representatives who need to know the information for the purpose in Section 2.1, are informed of its confidential nature, and are directed to treat it in accordance with this Agreement. Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, other than Representatives who have signed a separate confidentiality agreement with the Disclosing Party.

2.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Third-Party Information.** The Receiving Party acknowledges that certain Confidential Information, including information relating to the Company's licensors and suppliers, may be subject to confidentiality obligations owed by the Company to third parties. The Receiving Party shall comply with any reasonable additional handling restrictions that the Disclosing Party communicates in writing for that information, including "clean team" restrictions for competitively sensitive pricing or customer information.

## 3. Legally Required Disclosure

3.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy, at the Disclosing Party's expense. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts.

3.2 If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the information will be treated confidentially.

3.3 Notice under Section 3.1 is not required for disclosures made in routine examinations by a regulatory or self-regulatory authority that are not targeted at the Disclosing Party, the Company or the Proposed Transaction.

## 4. Return and Destruction

4.1 Upon written request of the Disclosing Party at any time, the Receiving Party shall, within ten business days, return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an authorized officer of the Receiving Party shall confirm in writing that it has done so.

4.2 The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies required to be retained by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention or compliance policies, and (b) copies stored in automatic electronic archiving or back-up systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding expiration of the term in Section 9.1.

## 5. Non-Solicitation of Employees

5.1 For 18 months after the Effective Date, Peitort shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment or hire any officer of the Company, or any employee of the Company at the level of director or above, with whom Peitort or its Representatives first had contact, or of whom they first became aware, in connection with the Proposed Transaction.

5.2 Section 5.1 does not restrict (a) general solicitations, including through search firms or advertisements, that are not directed at Company employees, or hiring any Person who responds to them; (b) soliciting or hiring any Person whose employment with the Company ended at least six months earlier; or (c) any employment arrangement entered into with the Company's consent or in connection with a definitive agreement for the Proposed Transaction.

## 6. Contacts and Communications

6.1 Without the prior written consent of Bratousk, Peitort shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, contact any employee (other than those designated by Bratousk), licensor, supplier, distributor, customer or landlord of the Company regarding the Company or the Proposed Transaction. All requests for information, site visits and management meetings shall be submitted to the individuals Bratousk designates in writing.

6.2 Nothing in Section 6.1 restricts contacts made in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Proposed Transaction and not using Confidential Information.

## 7. No Representations; No Obligation

7.1 **No Representations.** Neither Party, nor any of its Representatives, makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information. Neither Party shall have any liability to the other resulting from the use of Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a definitive agreement for the Proposed Transaction.

7.2 **No Obligation.** Unless and until a definitive agreement for the Proposed Transaction has been executed and delivered, neither Party is under any legal obligation of any kind with respect to the Proposed Transaction by virtue of this Agreement, except for the matters specifically agreed in this Agreement. Each Party may end discussions at any time for any reason.

7.3 **No License.** No license or other right in any patent, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property is granted or implied by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

7.4 **Privilege.** To the extent any Confidential Information includes materials subject to the attorney-client privilege, work product doctrine or other applicable privilege, the Parties share a common legal and commercial interest in that information, and its disclosure is not intended to, and shall not, waive or diminish any privilege.

## 8. Remedies

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief for any actual or threatened breach, without proof of actual damages and without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

8.2 If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable judgment that a Party or its Representatives breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable legal fees and expenses incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 9. Term

9.1 This Agreement remains in effect until the second anniversary of the Effective Date. The obligations under Sections 2 through 4 for any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law shall continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

9.2 Expiration of this Agreement does not relieve either Party of liability for any breach occurring before expiration.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or such other address as a Party designates by notice):

If to Peitort: Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc., 400 Riverside Commons, Suite 1200, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, Attention: General Counsel.

If to Bratousk: Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC, 75 Rockefeller Plaza, 22nd Floor, New York, New York 10019, Attention: Managing Partner.

10.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Bratousk may assign its rights under this Agreement to any acquirer of the Company. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

10.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of that right.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

10.5 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

10.6 **Jurisdiction; Venue.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

10.7 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

10.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF transmission. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
