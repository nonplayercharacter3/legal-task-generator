**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc. (the "**Company**") from Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC ("**Seller**"). We refer to the acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

Except as stated in Section 10, this Letter is non-binding. It reflects our current understanding of the terms that would be included in a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Structure

Buyer would purchase all of the Shares from Seller, free and clear of all liens. At closing the Company would become a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer. The Company would keep all of its business, assets and contracts, including:

- the Exclusive License Agreement with Orlund BioSciences GmbH covering Velnarix;
- the lease of the Bridgewater, New Jersey facility; and
- the Company's supply arrangements.

## 2. Purchase Price

2.1 **Base Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares would be $186,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"). The price assumes the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of net working capital.

2.2 **Form of Consideration.** The Base Purchase Price would be paid at closing as follows:

(a) $162,000,000 in cash, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, adjusted as described in Section 2.3; and

(b) $24,000,000 by Buyer's issuance to Seller of a subordinated promissory note (the "**Seller Note**").

2.3 **Working Capital Adjustment.** The cash portion would be adjusted dollar for dollar for any excess or shortfall of closing net working capital against a target of $14,200,000. The adjustment would be based on an estimate at closing, followed by a customary post-closing true-up. Any dispute would be resolved by an independent accounting firm.

## 3. Seller Note

The Seller Note would have the following principal terms:

(a) **Principal:** $24,000,000.

(b) **Maturity:** 36 months after the closing date, with no amortization.

(c) **Interest:** simple interest at six percent (6.0%) per annum, payable quarterly in arrears.

(d) **Prepayment:** permitted at any time without premium or penalty.

(e) **Subordination:** subordinated to Buyer's senior credit facility on terms customary for seller financing and reasonably acceptable to Buyer's senior lenders.

(f) **Set-off:** Buyer would have a right of set-off against amounts payable under the Seller Note for amounts owed to Buyer under the indemnification provisions of the Definitive Agreement. The detailed set-off mechanics would be agreed in the Definitive Agreement and the Seller Note.

## 4. Representations and Warranties

The Definitive Agreement would contain representations and warranties of Seller customary for transactions of this type involving a specialty pharmaceutical manufacturer. These would include representations on:

- organization, authority, capitalization and title to the Shares;
- financial statements and undisclosed liabilities;
- material contracts;
- intellectual property, including the Company's rights under the Exclusive License Agreement;
- taxes;
- regulatory compliance, including compliance with FDA requirements and current Good Manufacturing Practice;
- product liability and recalls;
- litigation, employees and benefit plans, environmental matters, insurance and brokers.

Buyer would give customary representations on organization, authority and sufficiency of funds.

## 5. Indemnification

The parties currently contemplate the following indemnification terms:

(a) **Survival.** General representations and warranties would survive for 12 months after the closing date. Fundamental representations (organization, authority, capitalization and title, and brokers) would survive for six years.

(b) **Deductible.** Seller's liability for breaches of general representations would be subject to a deductible equal to 0.75% of the Base Purchase Price.

(c) **General Cap.** Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of general representations would be capped at 10% of the Base Purchase Price ($18,600,000).

(d) **Carve-outs.** The General Cap would not apply to Losses arising from:

- fraud;
- breaches of fundamental representations; or
- breaches of the regulatory compliance representation.

Seller's aggregate liability for these matters would be capped at the purchase price.

(e) **Specific Indemnities.** Seller would indemnify Buyer for:

- pre-closing taxes of the Company; and
- product liability claims arising from products manufactured, sold or distributed by the Company before closing, including the pending claims relating to the discontinued Lorvexa oral suspension identified in Buyer's preliminary diligence.

## 6. Key Employee

Buyer considers the continued service of Dr. Imogen Sallow, the Company's Chief Scientific Officer, to be important to the Transaction. Signing of the Definitive Agreement would be conditioned on the Company and Dr. Sallow entering into a new employment agreement, effective at closing, on terms acceptable to Buyer.

## 7. Conditions to Closing

Closing would be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement and related ancillary agreements;

(b) satisfactory completion of Buyer's confirmatory business, legal, regulatory, financial and tax due diligence;

(c) receipt of required governmental approvals and third-party consents to be identified in the Definitive Agreement, including state board of pharmacy change-of-ownership approvals;

(d) accuracy of representations and performance of covenants;

(e) absence of a material adverse effect on the Company; and

(f) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's board of directors.

## 8. Due Diligence; Timing

8.1 Seller would give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the Company's personnel, facilities, books, records and contracts. This includes regulatory correspondence, inspection history and the Company's material licensing and supply agreements.

8.2 The parties would aim to sign the Definitive Agreement by September 15, 2026 and to close as promptly as practicable after the required approvals are received.

## 9. Financing

Buyer expects to fund the cash portion of the purchase price from cash on hand and borrowings under its existing credit facility. The Transaction would not be subject to a financing condition.

## 10. Binding Provisions

Only the following provisions of this Letter are legally binding on the parties: this Section 10 and Sections 11 through 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**").

## 11. Exclusivity

From the date Seller countersigns this Letter until the earlier of (a) September 30, 2026 and (b) the date either party gives written notice ending negotiations, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and their respective representatives not to:

- solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer for the sale of the Company, its equity or any material part of its assets; or
- provide information to any such person for that purpose.

Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of that kind that it receives.

## 12. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, are subject to the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller, which remains in effect in accordance with its terms.

## 13. Expenses

Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 14. Governing Law

This Letter shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

## 15. Non-Binding Effect; Termination

15.1 Except for the Binding Provisions, this Letter is not a binding agreement. It does not create any obligation to negotiate or to enter into the Definitive Agreement or the Transaction. Any such obligation will arise only on execution and delivery of the Definitive Agreement.

15.2 Either party may end negotiations at any time by written notice. The Binding Provisions survive termination, except that Section 11 ends as provided in that Section.

15.3 This Letter may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF transmission.
