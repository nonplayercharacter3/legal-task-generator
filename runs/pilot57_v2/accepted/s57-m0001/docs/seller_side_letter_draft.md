Ladies and Gentlemen:

We refer to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), between Peitort Pharmaceutical Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Bratousk Healthcare Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). The Agreement relates to the purchase by Buyer of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Elmcrest Specialty Pharma, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

This letter (this "**Letter Agreement**") sets out certain agreements between Buyer and Seller in connection with the Agreement and the Closing.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used in this Letter Agreement and not defined in it have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Agreement applies to this Letter Agreement as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Letter Agreement.

## 2. Amendment to Section 9.6(c)

2.1 This Letter Agreement is entered into under Section 11.8 (Amendment and Waiver) of the Agreement. It is a written instrument signed by each Party and amends the Agreement as provided in this Section 2.

2.2 With effect from the date of this Letter Agreement, Section 9.6(c) of the Agreement is amended by deleting the words "or Section 4.12 (Regulatory Compliance)".

2.3 This Letter Agreement shall form part of the Agreement. From the date of this Letter Agreement, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement", and each reference in any Ancillary Agreement to the Agreement, shall mean the Agreement as amended by this Letter Agreement.

## 3. Management Services Agreement

3.1 Seller and Buyer acknowledge that the Management Services Agreement dated April 1, 2018, between Seller and the Company (the "**Management Services Agreement**"), disclosed on Schedule 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule, will terminate at the Closing.

3.2 Seller shall cause the Management Services Agreement to be terminated effective as of the Closing, without any liability to the Company. Any portion of the annual management fee attributable to the period through the Closing Date and not paid before the Closing shall be treated as a current liability of the Company in calculating net working capital under Section 2.3 of the Agreement. No management fee or other amount shall be payable by the Company to Seller under the Management Services Agreement for any period after the Closing.

3.3 At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written termination of the Management Services Agreement, executed by Seller and the Company. The termination shall include a release by Seller of the Company from all obligations under the Management Services Agreement, other than those described in Section 3.2.

## 4. Closing Coordination

4.1 Seller shall deliver the wire instructions required by Section 2.2(a) of the Agreement on Seller's letterhead, signed by an authorized officer of Seller. Seller shall also confirm those instructions by telephone with Buyer's Chief Financial Officer at a number known to Buyer, and not at a number provided in the instructions themselves.

4.2 The Parties shall cooperate reasonably to settle the forms of the closing certificates described in Sections 7.2(c) and 7.3(c) of the Agreement at least three Business Days before the anticipated Closing Date.

## 5. Effect of this Letter Agreement

5.1 Except as amended by Section 2, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.

5.2 This Letter Agreement does not constitute a waiver of any right or remedy of either Party under the Agreement, except as stated in it.

## 6. Miscellaneous

6.1 **Notices.** Section 11.2 (Notices) of the Agreement applies to notices under this Letter Agreement.

6.2 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Letter Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 11.11 (Jurisdiction; Venue) and Section 11.12 (Waiver of Jury Trial) of the Agreement apply to this Letter Agreement as if set out in full.

6.3 **Successors and Assigns.** This Letter Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, in accordance with Section 11.5 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Counterparts.** This Letter Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF transmission. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.
