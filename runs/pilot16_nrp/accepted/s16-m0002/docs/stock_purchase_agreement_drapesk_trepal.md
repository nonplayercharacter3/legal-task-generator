# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 by and between **Trepal Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Drapesk Materials Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the "**Shares**"), of **Kestrin Cell Reclamation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company operates lithium-ion battery collection, black-mass shredding and hydrometallurgical recovery facilities.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $184,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to Seller, an amount equal to the Purchase Price less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(b) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

**2.3 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $9,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account (the "**Escrow Fund**") and disbursed in accordance with Section 9.7 and the Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Fund shall serve as a source, but not the exclusive source, of recovery for amounts owed by Seller under Article IX.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from the Purchase Price any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions set forth in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company listed in Section 3.2(c) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(d) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors authorizing this Agreement;

(e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments set forth in Section 2.2;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer;

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties contained in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing and shall survive until the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed. No claim for indemnification under this Article IX in respect of any representation or warranty may be asserted unless a Claim Notice with respect to such claim is delivered prior to the expiration of the applicable survival period, provided that any claim asserted in a Claim Notice delivered prior to such expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, and subject to the other terms of this Article IX, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, following the Closing, the Company) and their respective directors, officers and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any Buyer Indemnitee arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any of the representations or warranties of Seller contained in Article IV or in any certificate delivered by Seller pursuant to this Agreement;

(b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant, agreement or obligation to be performed by Seller pursuant to this Agreement; or

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any period ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, and subject to the other terms of this Article IX, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any Seller Indemnitee arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any of the representations or warranties of Buyer contained in Article V;

(b) the ownership or operation of the Company or any of the Facilities, whether before or after the Closing; or

(c) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant, agreement or obligation to be performed by Buyer pursuant to this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller shall be liable pursuant to Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Cap**"). The Cap shall not apply to Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or arising out of Fraud.

(b) Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer Indemnitees for indemnification under Section 9.2(a), other than in respect of Fundamental Representations or Fraud, until the aggregate amount of all Losses in respect thereof exceeds $920,000 (the "**Basket**"), in which event Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(c) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer shall be liable pursuant to Section 9.3, other than with respect to Section 9.3(b), shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price.

(d) In no event shall any indemnifying party be liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent actually paid to a third party.

(e) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of such Losses, less the costs of recovery.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.**

(a) *Direct Claims.* An indemnified party shall assert any claim for indemnification by delivering to the indemnifying party a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim, the provisions of this Agreement under which the claim is made and, to the extent reasonably estimable, the amount of Losses claimed. The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of a Claim Notice to respond in writing.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* If any indemnified party receives notice of any action or claim by a third party that may give rise to a claim for indemnification, it shall promptly deliver a Claim Notice to the indemnifying party. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of such third-party claim with counsel reasonably satisfactory to the indemnified party, provided that it acknowledges in writing its obligation to indemnify. The indemnified party may participate in the defense at its own expense. No party shall settle a third-party claim without the consent of the other party, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

(c) *Failure to Give Notice.* Failure to give prompt notice of a third-party claim shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced thereby.

**9.6 Environmental Claims.** Buyer shall not deliver a Claim Notice with respect to any inaccuracy in or breach of Section 4.14 until thirty (30) days after Buyer's receipt of the Phase II Report (such period, the "**Environmental Review Period**"). During the Environmental Review Period, Buyer and Seller shall meet and confer in good faith regarding the findings of the Phase II Report and any investigation or remediation recommended therein. Any remediation undertaken in respect of an environmental claim shall be conducted to the least stringent standard permitted under applicable Environmental Law for the continued industrial use of the relevant Facility.

**9.7 Release of Escrow Fund.** Within five (5) Business Days after the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent to release to Seller the remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in any Claim Notice delivered prior to such date and not yet resolved, which retained amount shall be released upon final resolution of the applicable claims in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.8 Order of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund and, to the extent the Escrow Fund is insufficient or has been released, directly by Seller.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Agreement shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.10 Exclusive Remedies.** Subject to Section 6.10(c) and Section 11.11, from and after the Closing the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of the subject matter of this Agreement, other than claims arising from Fraud.
