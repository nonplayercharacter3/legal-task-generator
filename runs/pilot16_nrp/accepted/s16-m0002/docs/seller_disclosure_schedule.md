## Section 6.8 – Phase II Report Scope of Work

The Environmental Consultant will perform the Phase II environmental site assessment at each Facility. The scope of work is as follows.

1. **Henderson Facility.**
   - (a) Soil borings, and installation of at least six (6) groundwater monitoring wells, in and down-gradient of the black-mass storage building, the electrolyte drainage and collection areas, and the wastewater treatment units.
   - (b) Sampling and analysis of the stormwater retention basin sediments and leak detection system.
   - (c) Analysis for metals (including lithium, nickel, cobalt, manganese, copper and aluminum), fluoride, organic carbonate solvents, volatile organic compounds and per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances.

2. **Reno Facility.**
   - (a) Soil borings, and installation of at least three (3) groundwater monitoring wells, in and around the spent-battery receiving, discharge and storage areas.
   - (b) Analysis for the parameters described in paragraph 1(c).

3. **Chandler Facility.**
   - (a) Soil borings, and installation of at least four (4) groundwater monitoring wells, in and around the black-mass pre-processing area, the spent-battery storage areas and the diesel fuel tank.
   - (b) Analysis for the parameters described in paragraph 1(c), plus total petroleum hydrocarbons.

4. **All Facilities.**
   - (a) Comparison of results to applicable federal and state screening and cleanup standards.
   - (b) A summary of findings and recommendations for any further investigation or remediation.

The Environmental Consultant will deliver the Phase II Report to Buyer and Seller within the period set forth in Section 6.8 of the Agreement.
