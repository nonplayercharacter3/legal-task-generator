# DRAPESK MATERIALS GROUP, INC.

## UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN LIEU OF A MEETING

**Dated: March 6, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Drapesk Materials Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), acting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the Bylaws of the Corporation, hereby waive all notice and consent to the adoption of the following recitals and resolutions. These resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called and held meeting of the Board.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Corporation has negotiated a Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with Trepal Holdings Corp., a Delaware corporation (the "**Seller**"). Under it, the Corporation would purchase all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock (the "**Shares**") of Kestrin Cell Reclamation, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from the Seller (the "**Acquisition**").

1.2 The Company operates lithium-ion battery collection, black-mass shredding and hydrometallurgical recovery facilities. The Board has considered that the Company's principal liability exposure relates to environmental matters at its facilities. This includes the storage of spent batteries and black mass, and the handling of electrolyte and process residues.

1.3 The Board has received the following:

(a) a draft of the Purchase Agreement in substantially final form, together with a draft of the Seller's Disclosure Schedule;

(b) a summary of principal terms prepared by management and outside counsel; and

(c) presentations from management, outside counsel and the Corporation's financial and environmental advisors on the Acquisition, the results of due diligence and the allocation of environmental risk under the Purchase Agreement.

1.4 The Board has determined that the Acquisition is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders. This determination rests on the principal terms described in Section 2 of these resolutions.

## 2. Approval of the Acquisition and Principal Terms

**RESOLVED**, that the Acquisition is approved. The Purchase Agreement is approved substantially in the form presented to the Board, on the following principal terms:

(a) *Purchase Price.* The aggregate purchase price for the Shares shall be $184,000,000, payable in cash at the closing. Of this amount, $9,200,000 shall be deposited with Calloway Trust Bank, N.A., as escrow agent, as an indemnity escrow.

(b) *Seller Indemnity Cap.* The Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of its representations and warranties shall be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price. The cap shall not apply to breaches of Fundamental Representations or to fraud.

(c) *Fundamental Representations.* The Fundamental Representations, which are excluded from the cap and survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, include Section 4.14 (Environmental Matters) of the SPA, together with Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization) and 4.5 (Brokers).

(d) *Phase II Environmental Assessment.* Following the closing, the Seller shall cause an independent environmental consultant to conduct a Phase II environmental site assessment of each Company facility, at the Seller's expense.

(e) *Restrictive Covenants.* The Seller shall be bound by a five-year non-competition covenant and spent-battery feedstock supply covenant in favor of the Company.

(f) *Governing Law.* The Purchase Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

## 3. Authorization of Officers

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and any Vice President of the Corporation (each an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Corporation. Each Authorized Officer may approve changes to the Purchase Agreement, as shown by his or her signature, provided those changes are consistent with the principal terms in Section 2 of these resolutions.

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, the following:

(a) the escrow agreement contemplated by the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the officer's certificates and secretary's certificates required at closing; and

(c) all other agreements, instruments, certificates and documents that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or advisable to complete the Acquisition.

## 4. Funding and Regulatory Filings

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to fund the Purchase Price from the Corporation's cash on hand and available sources of immediately available funds.

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to prepare and file any notification required under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, and to pay all related filing fees. They are also authorized to make any filings with Governmental Authorities needed to transfer or reissue the Company's environmental permits.

## 5. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to take all further actions, and to pay all fees and expenses, that they consider necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer or agent of the Corporation in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 6. Effectiveness; Counterparts

6.1 This consent shall be effective as of the date first written above. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

6.2 This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF transmission. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.
