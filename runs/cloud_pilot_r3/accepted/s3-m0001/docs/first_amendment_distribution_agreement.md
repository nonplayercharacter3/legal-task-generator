# FIRST AMENDMENT TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This First Amendment to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Velloran Instruments, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Calder Ridge Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19713 ("**Supplier**"), and **Harrow Creek Distribution LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Fallon Mill Road, Suite 300, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to that certain Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. Supplier has introduced a line of accessory and consumable products for use with its precision measurement instruments. The Parties wish to add that line to the Products listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement and to update Exhibit B to the Agreement to set out distributor pricing for that line.

C. Section 19.2 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement, including its Exhibits, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Capitalized Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Accessory Products.** "**Accessory Products**" means the products in the Accessories product line described in Section 2.1 of this Amendment, as listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT OF EXHIBIT A (PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS)

2.1 **Addition of Accessories Product Line.** Effective as of the Amendment Effective Date, Exhibit A to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row at the end of the table:

| Product Line | Representative Models | Specifications |
|---|---|---|
| Accessories | VI-AC100 Probe and Stylus Kits; VI-AC210 Carrying and Storage Cases; VI-AC300 Calibration Software Licenses; VI-AC400 Replacement Anvils and Measuring Faces | Supplier Spec. Sheet AC-2026 |

2.2 **Status as Products.** From and after the Amendment Effective Date, the Accessory Products are "Products" for all purposes of the Agreement, including Section 2.1 (Exclusive Appointment), Section 4 (Pricing and Payment), Section 5 (Minimum Purchase Requirements), Section 6 (Orders and Delivery) and Section 10 (Warranties).

2.3 **Specifications.** Supplier shall deliver Supplier Spec. Sheet AC-2026 to Distributor within ten (10) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date. For purposes of Section 10.1 of the Agreement, the Accessory Products shall conform to the specifications in that Spec. Sheet.

2.4 **Restated Exhibit A.** For convenience, Exhibit A to the Agreement, as amended by this Amendment, is restated in full in Annex 1 to this Amendment.

## 3. AMENDMENT OF EXHIBIT B (PRICING)

3.1 **Part 1 – Pricing.** Effective as of the Amendment Effective Date, Part 1 of Exhibit B to the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **Part 1 – Pricing.** Distributor pricing for each Product is Supplier's U.S. list price in effect on the date of the Purchase Order, less a distributor discount of (a) thirty-two percent (32%) for Dimensional Metrology and Surface Analysis Products, (b) twenty-eight percent (28%) for Laboratory Balances and Calibration Standards Products, and (c) twenty-five percent (25%) for Accessories Products.

3.2 **Part 2 – Minimum Purchase Requirement.** Part 2 of Exhibit B to the Agreement is not amended by this Amendment and remains in effect as stated in the Agreement. Net purchases of Accessory Products under Purchase Orders accepted on or after the Amendment Effective Date count toward the Minimum Purchase Requirement for the contract year in which they are invoiced.

3.3 **Purchase Orders Before the Amendment Effective Date.** This Amendment does not affect the pricing of any Purchase Order accepted by Supplier before the Amendment Effective Date.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS

4.1 Each Party represents and warrants to the other that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it, and that this Amendment constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. From and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference to the Agreement in any other document, means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 18.1 and 18.2 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in this Amendment.

5.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
