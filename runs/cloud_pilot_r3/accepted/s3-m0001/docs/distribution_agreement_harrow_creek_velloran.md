# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Velloran Instruments, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Calder Ridge Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19713 ("**Supplier**"), and **Harrow Creek Distribution LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Fallon Mill Road, Suite 300, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures precision measurement instruments and related products.

B. Distributor is in the business of marketing, selling and distributing industrial and laboratory instrumentation to commercial and institutional customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of a Person or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

1.3 "**Change of Control**" means, with respect to Distributor, (a) any transaction or series of related transactions that results in the sale, transfer or other disposition, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the equity interests or voting power of Distributor; (b) any merger, consolidation or reorganization of Distributor after which the holders of Distributor's equity interests immediately before the transaction hold fifty percent (50%) or less of the equity interests or voting power of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of Distributor's assets.

1.4 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

1.5 "**Customer**" means any end user or reseller to which Distributor sells Products in the Territory.

1.6 "**Exhibits**" means the exhibits attached to this Agreement, as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 19.2.

1.7 "**First Amendment**" means the first written amendment to this Agreement executed by both Parties in accordance with Section 19.2.

1.8 "**Minimum Purchase Requirement**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

1.9 "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

1.10 "**Products**" means the products listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.11 "**Side Letter**" means any letter agreement executed by both Parties that supplements this Agreement with respect to exclusivity and non-competition.

1.12 "**Territory**" means the States of Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

1.13 "**Trademarks**" means the trademarks, trade names and logos of Supplier used in connection with the Products, as designated by Supplier from time to time.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment. During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or sales representative for the Products in the Territory, and shall not itself sell Products directly to Customers located in the Territory except as provided in Section 2.3.

2.2 **No Rights Outside the Territory.** Distributor has no right to market or sell Products outside the Territory, and Supplier reserves all rights to appoint distributors and sell Products outside the Territory. Distributor shall refer to Supplier any inquiries it receives for Products for delivery outside the Territory.

2.3 **Reserved Accounts.** Supplier may sell Products directly to the federal government accounts listed in Exhibit C (the "**Reserved Accounts**"), wherever located. Distributor shall receive no commission on sales to Reserved Accounts.

2.4 **Independent Contractor.** Distributor purchases Products for its own account and resells them in its own name. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship between the Parties, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years from the Effective Date, ending November 14, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 15.

3.2 **Renewal.** This Agreement shall renew for one (1) additional term of three (3) years (the "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") only if Distributor delivers to Supplier written notice of its election to renew (a "**Renewal Notice**") at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the Initial Term. This Agreement does not renew automatically. If Distributor does not deliver a Renewal Notice within the time required by this Section 3.2, this Agreement shall expire at the end of the Initial Term.

3.3 **Renewal Pricing.** Pricing for the Renewal Term shall be as set out in Exhibit B as in effect on the first day of the Renewal Term, subject to adjustment under Section 4.3.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for the Products set out in Exhibit B. Prices are stated in United States dollars and are exclusive of taxes, which are Distributor's responsibility other than taxes on Supplier's net income.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment. Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

4.3 **Price Changes.** Supplier may change the prices in Exhibit B once per calendar year on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice, provided that no increase in any contract year may exceed five percent (5%) for any Product. Price changes do not apply to Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of the change.

4.4 **Late Payment.** Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

## 5. MINIMUM PURCHASE REQUIREMENTS

5.1 **Minimum Purchases.** In each contract year of the Term, Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of not less than the amount stated for that contract year in Exhibit B (the "**Minimum Purchase Requirement**").

5.2 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Requirement in any contract year, Supplier may, as its sole remedy, by written notice delivered within sixty (60) days after the end of that contract year, (a) convert Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 to a non-exclusive appointment for the remainder of the Term, or (b) terminate this Agreement under Section 15.3.

5.3 **Reporting.** Within twenty (20) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a written report showing sales of Products by Product and by State within the Territory, and its inventory on hand.

## 6. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

6.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing written purchase orders ("**Purchase Orders**") to Supplier. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with this Agreement and Supplier's published lead times.

6.2 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in Newark, Delaware (Incoterms 2020), or to such other delivery location as stated in the applicable Purchase Order and agreed by Supplier.

6.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery in accordance with Section 6.2.

6.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice that are additional to or inconsistent with this Agreement are of no effect.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, market and sell the Products throughout the Territory.

7.2 Distributor shall maintain a trained sales and technical support staff sufficient to market the Products and provide first-line technical support to Customers.

7.3 Distributor shall maintain an inventory of Products sufficient to meet reasonably anticipated Customer demand in the Territory.

7.4 Distributor shall comply with all applicable laws in connection with its performance under this Agreement, including export control and anti-corruption laws.

7.5 Distributor shall not modify the Products or their labeling or packaging without Supplier's prior written consent.

## 8. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

8.1 Supplier shall provide Distributor, at no charge, reasonable quantities of sales literature, technical documentation and demonstration materials for the Products.

8.2 Supplier shall provide initial and periodic product training for Distributor's sales and technical personnel.

8.3 Supplier shall refer to Distributor all inquiries it receives from prospective Customers located in the Territory, other than Reserved Accounts.

8.4 Supplier shall give Distributor at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice of the discontinuation of any Product.

## 9. TRADEMARKS

9.1 Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Trademarks in the Territory solely to advertise and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's trademark guidelines.

9.2 Distributor acquires no ownership interest in the Trademarks. All goodwill arising from Distributor's use of the Trademarks inures to Supplier. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Distributor shall cease all use of the Trademarks, subject to Section 16.2.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor and to each Customer that the Products will conform to the specifications in Exhibit A and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after delivery to the Customer, not to exceed eighteen (18) months after delivery to Distributor.

10.2 **Remedy.** Supplier's sole obligation, and Distributor's exclusive remedy, for breach of the warranty in Section 10.1 is the repair or replacement of the nonconforming Product or, if repair or replacement is not commercially practicable, a refund or credit of the price paid.

10.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

10.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 10, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, officers, managers and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from (a) any claim that a Product infringes a third party's intellectual property rights, or (b) bodily injury or property damage caused by a defect in a Product, except to the extent caused by Distributor's modification or misuse of the Product.

11.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from (a) Distributor's breach of this Agreement, (b) any warranty or representation regarding the Products made by Distributor beyond those in Section 10, or (c) Distributor's negligence or willful misconduct.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing obligations on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, Customer lists and product roadmaps.

12.2 The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to perform this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party other than its employees, Affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 12.

12.3 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available without breach, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law (after prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful).

12.4 The obligations in this Section 12 survive for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 EXCEPT FOR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11 AND BREACHES OF SECTION 12, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

13.2 EXCEPT FOR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11 AND BREACHES OF SECTION 12, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY DISTRIBUTOR TO SUPPLIER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Supplier may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business relating to the Products. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void.

14.2 **Change of Control of Distributor.** Any Change of Control of Distributor shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Distributor and requires the prior written consent of Supplier.

14.3 **Termination on Unconsented Change of Control.** If a Change of Control of Distributor occurs without Supplier's prior written consent, Supplier may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to Distributor.

14.4 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

15.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for its business, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Minimum Purchase Shortfall.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice delivered in accordance with Section 5.2.

15.4 **Change of Control.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement in accordance with Section 14.3.

## 16. EFFECT OF EXPIRATION OR TERMINATION

16.1 Upon expiration or termination, all outstanding undisputed amounts become immediately due, and Supplier shall fill accepted Purchase Orders unless termination is by Supplier under Section 15.1 or 15.2.

16.2 Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory of Products in the Territory for ninety (90) days after expiration or termination, subject to the terms of this Agreement. Alternatively, Supplier may elect to repurchase that inventory, in saleable condition, at Distributor's net landed cost.

16.3 Sections 4.2, 4.4, 9.2, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17, 18 and 19 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.
