# WAREHOUSE AND FULFILLMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Warehouse and Fulfillment Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Saltmarsh Logistics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 615 Pigeon Point Road, New Castle, Delaware 19720 ("**Provider**"), and **Harrow Creek Distribution LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Fallon Mill Road, Suite 300, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Customer**"). Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider operates public and contract warehouse facilities and provides storage, order fulfillment and related logistics services.

B. Customer distributes industrial and laboratory instrumentation and wishes to engage Provider to store certain of its inventory and to fulfill certain customer orders, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Facility**" means Provider's warehouse located at 615 Pigeon Point Road, New Castle, Delaware 19720, or any other facility Customer approves in writing under Section 3.3.

1.2 "**Goods**" means the inventory that Customer or its suppliers deliver to the Facility for storage and handling under this Agreement.

1.3 "**Order**" means an instruction from Customer, transmitted through the WMS or by other agreed means, directing Provider to pick, pack and ship Goods to a consignee.

1.4 "**Rate Schedule**" means the schedule of fees attached as Schedule 1, as amended in accordance with Section 5.3.

1.5 "**Services**" means the receiving, storage, inventory management, order fulfillment, shipping and related services described in Section 2 and Schedule 1.

1.6 "**WMS**" means Provider's web-based warehouse management system.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Receiving.** Provider shall receive inbound shipments of Goods at the Facility during its normal receiving hours, inspect each shipment for visible damage and count discrepancies against the bill of lading or advance shipping notice, and record received Goods in the WMS within one (1) Business Day after receipt. Provider shall promptly notify Customer of any visible damage, shortage or overage.

2.2 **Storage.** Provider shall store the Goods in a clean, dry, secure and climate-controlled area of the Facility, maintained at a temperature of between 60°F and 80°F and a relative humidity not exceeding 60%, segregated from goods of Provider's other customers and identifiable as Customer's property.

2.3 **Order Fulfillment.** Provider shall pick, pack and tender for shipment each Order received by 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day on that same Business Day, and each other Order on the next Business Day. Provider shall ship Orders using the carriers and service levels designated by Customer.

2.4 **Packaging and Labeling.** Provider shall ship Goods in the manufacturer's original packaging, with the manufacturer's labeling intact, together with any packing slip or shipping label generated for the Order. Provider shall not open, repackage, relabel or otherwise alter Goods or their manufacturer packaging or labeling except as Customer specifically directs in writing.

2.5 **Returns.** Provider shall receive, inspect and record customer returns in accordance with Customer's written return procedures and hold returned Goods in a separate location pending Customer's disposition instructions.

2.6 **Inventory Reporting and Counts.** Provider shall give Customer continuous access to real-time inventory data through the WMS. Provider shall conduct a full physical inventory count once per calendar year on a date agreed with Customer, and cycle counts as reasonably requested by Customer, and shall reconcile any variances with Customer within ten (10) Business Days.

2.7 **Performance Standards.** Provider shall achieve (a) order accuracy of at least 99.5%, (b) on-time shipment of at least 99% of Orders under Section 2.3, and (c) annual inventory accuracy of at least 99.7% by unit, each measured monthly. If Provider fails to meet any standard in two (2) consecutive months, the Parties shall meet within ten (10) Business Days to agree on a corrective action plan.

## 3. FACILITY AND PERSONNEL

3.1 **Facility Standards.** Provider shall maintain the Facility in good repair and in compliance with applicable law, with alarm and fire-suppression systems, controlled access and video surveillance of storage and shipping areas.

3.2 **Personnel.** Provider shall perform the Services using its own trained employees or supervised contractors and is responsible for their acts and omissions.

3.3 **Relocation.** Provider shall not move Goods to any facility other than the Facility without Customer's prior written approval, except temporarily in an emergency to protect the Goods, in which case Provider shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours.

3.4 **Access and Audit.** Customer and its designees may inspect the Facility and Goods, and audit Provider's records relating to the Services, during normal business hours on two (2) Business Days' notice.

## 4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of one (1) year. It renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless terminated in accordance with this Section 4.

4.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, by giving the other Party at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

4.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, or immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

4.4 **Transition.** Upon expiration or termination for any reason, Provider shall continue to perform the Services for up to thirty (30) days at the then-current rates if Customer so requests, and shall cooperate in the orderly removal of the Goods. Provider shall release all Goods to Customer or its designee upon payment of all undisputed amounts then due.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the fees set out in the Rate Schedule. No other fees, surcharges or charges apply unless agreed in writing.

5.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Undisputed amounts are due within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Customer shall notify Provider in writing of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.3 **Rate Adjustments.** Provider may adjust the rates in the Rate Schedule once in each twelve (12) month period, beginning February 1, 2025, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No adjustment may exceed four percent (4%) of the rates then in effect.

## 6. TITLE, LIEN AND RISK OF LOSS

6.1 **Title.** Title to the Goods remains with Customer at all times. Provider acquires no ownership interest in the Goods.

6.2 **Warehouse Lien.** Provider's lien on the Goods under Article 7 of the Delaware Uniform Commercial Code is limited to undisputed charges for storage and handling of the specific Goods subject to the lien. Provider waives any general lien on other Goods.

6.3 **Warehouse Receipts.** The WMS receiving records constitute non-negotiable warehouse receipts for the Goods. Provider shall not issue negotiable warehouse receipts for any Goods.

6.4 **Risk of Loss.** Provider is responsible for loss of or damage to Goods from the time of their receipt at the Facility until they are tendered to the carrier designated for an Order, to the extent resulting from Provider's failure to exercise the care that a reasonably careful warehouse operator would exercise under like circumstances.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 Provider shall maintain, at its own cost, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) warehouse legal liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence; (c) automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in clauses (a) and (c) and furnish certificates of insurance on request.

7.2 Customer shall maintain property insurance on the Goods for their full replacement value.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its members, managers, officers and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Provider's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.

8.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its members, managers, officers and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Customer's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement, or from any defect in the Goods not caused by Provider.

8.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing obligations on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 EXCEPT FOR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 8 AND BREACHES OF SECTION 10, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS.

9.2 Provider's liability for loss of or damage to Goods under Section 6.4 is limited to Customer's landed cost of the Goods lost or damaged, not to exceed $5,000,000 per occurrence.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including Customer's inventory levels, pricing, supplier information and customer and consignee data, and shall use that information solely to perform this Agreement.

10.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available without breach, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law (after prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful). These obligations survive for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement.

14.2 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.
