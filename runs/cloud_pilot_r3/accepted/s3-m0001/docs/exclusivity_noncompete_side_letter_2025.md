# Re: Exclusivity and Non-Competition Side Letter – Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of November 15, 2023

Dear Mr. Esterly:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Distribution Agreement**") between Velloran Instruments, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Harrow Creek Distribution LLC ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." This Side Letter is the "Side Letter" referred to in Sections 1.11 and 19.4 of the Distribution Agreement. It sets out additional understandings between the Parties about Distributor's exclusive appointment and Distributor's non-competition obligations.

In consideration of Supplier's continued appointment of Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products, the mutual covenants below and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement, except that "**Territory**" means Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, New York and Virginia.

1.2 **Competing Products.** "**Competing Products**" means any precision measurement instruments, laboratory balances, calibration standards, or related accessories or consumables that are substantially similar in function to, or are marketed as substitutes for, any of the Products. Products manufactured or supplied by Supplier are not Competing Products.

1.3 **Restricted Period.** "**Restricted Period**" means the Term of the Distribution Agreement and the eighteen (18) month period immediately following the expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement for any reason.

## 2. Non-Competition

2.1 **Covenant.** During the Restricted Period, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, market, promote, sell, distribute or act as a sales agent or representative for any Competing Products in the Territory.

2.2 **Indirect Activities.** Distributor shall not avoid the restriction in Section 2.1 by acting through any Affiliate, member, manager, employee or other Person. Distributor shall not knowingly supply Competing Products to any Person for resale in the Territory during the Restricted Period.

2.3 **Passive Investments.** Section 2.1 does not prohibit Distributor or its Affiliates from owning, as a passive investment, two percent (2%) or less of any class of publicly traded securities of any Person.

2.4 **Reasonableness.** Distributor acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 2 are reasonable in scope, duration and geographic area. Distributor also acknowledges that these restrictions are necessary to protect Supplier's goodwill, Confidential Information and investment in the distribution of the Products, and that Supplier would not continue Distributor's exclusive appointment without them.

## 3. Exclusivity

3.1 **Confirmation of Appointment.** Supplier confirms that Distributor's appointment as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products continues as set forth in Section 2.1 of the Distribution Agreement, subject to Section 2.3 (Reserved Accounts) and Section 5.2 (Shortfall) of the Distribution Agreement. This Side Letter does not modify Section 2.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

3.2 **Minimum Performance.** Distributor acknowledges that its exclusive appointment remains conditioned on its satisfaction of the Minimum Purchase Requirement in accordance with Section 5 and Exhibit B of the Distribution Agreement.

3.3 **Notice of Competing Opportunities.** During the Term, Distributor shall promptly notify Supplier in writing of any request it receives from a Customer or prospective Customer to supply Competing Products in the Territory.

## 4. Remedies

4.1 **Equitable Relief.** Distributor acknowledges that a breach or threatened breach of Section 2 would cause Supplier irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Supplier may therefore seek temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief to restrain any such breach in the courts specified in Section 6, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

4.2 **Tolling.** If Distributor breaches Section 2, the post-term portion of the Restricted Period is extended by the period during which the breach continues.

4.3 **Termination Right.** A breach of Section 2 during the Term is a material breach of the Distribution Agreement for purposes of Section 15.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 5. Term and Survival

5.1 This Side Letter takes effect on the date first written above. It remains in effect for the Term of the Distribution Agreement and thereafter for so long as any obligation under Section 2 or Section 4 remains in effect.

5.2 Sections 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 survive the expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement.

## 6. Governing Law and Forum

6.1 This Side Letter and any dispute arising out of the exclusivity and non-competition arrangements between the parties are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

6.2 Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Side Letter or those arrangements. Each Party also waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

## 7. Miscellaneous

7.1 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Side Letter may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

7.2 **Severability.** If any provision of this Side Letter is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. A court of competent jurisdiction may modify the invalid provision, including by narrowing its duration or geographic scope, to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

7.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Side Letter shall be given in accordance with Section 17 of the Distribution Agreement.

7.4 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
