# MEMBERSHIP INTEREST PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 5, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**") by and among **Quillon Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"); **Martin J. Esterly**, an individual resident of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania; **Priya N. Hollister**, an individual resident of the State of New Jersey (each of Mr. Esterly and Ms. Hollister, a "**Seller**" and together, the "**Sellers**"); and **Harrow Creek Distribution LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"). Buyer, the Sellers and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Sellers together own all of the issued and outstanding membership interests of the Company (the "**Membership Interests**"). Mr. Esterly owns sixty percent (60%) and Ms. Hollister owns forty percent (40%).

B. The Company is the exclusive distributor in the mid-Atlantic region of precision measurement products manufactured by Velloran Instruments, Inc. ("**Velloran**").

C. The Sellers wish to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from the Sellers, all of the Membership Interests, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; CLOSING

2.1 **Purchase and Sale.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, each Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from each Seller, all of the Membership Interests owned by that Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances.

2.2 **Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Membership Interests (the "**Closing**") shall take place by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on November 30, 2026 (the "**Target Closing Date**"). If the conditions set out in Article 7 (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing) have not been satisfied or waived by the Target Closing Date, the Closing shall take place on the third (3rd) Business Day after all of those conditions are satisfied or waived, or on such other date as Buyer and the Sellers agree in writing. The date on which the Closing actually occurs is the "**Closing Date**." The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

2.3 **Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Membership Interests is Forty-Eight Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($48,500,000) (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.5.

2.4 **Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the lenders under the Credit Agreement listed on Schedule 3.6, the amount required to repay all indebtedness outstanding under that Credit Agreement, as set out in a customary payoff letter;

(b) to Delaware Trust Escrow Services, LLC (the "**Escrow Agent**"), Four Million Eight Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($4,850,000) (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"), to be held under an escrow agreement in a form reasonably acceptable to Buyer and the Sellers; and

(c) to each Seller, that Seller's pro rata share (based on the percentages stated in Recital A) of the Purchase Price, less the amounts paid under clauses (a) and (b) and any unpaid transaction expenses of the Company.

2.5 **Working Capital Adjustment.** The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which the Company's net working capital as of the Closing exceeds or falls short of Six Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($6,200,000). Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to the Sellers a closing statement setting out its calculation of closing net working capital. Any dispute that the Sellers and Buyer cannot resolve within thirty (30) days after the Sellers deliver a written objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by them, whose determination is final and binding.

2.6 **Closing Deliveries of the Sellers.** At the Closing, the Sellers shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) assignments of the Membership Interests, duly executed by each Seller;

(b) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of each manager and officer of the Company designated by Buyer;

(c) copies of the Required Consents, each in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(d) the payoff letter described in Section 2.4(a) and evidence of the release of all Encumbrances securing indebtedness under the Credit Agreement;

(e) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the Delaware Secretary of State dated within ten (10) days before the Closing Date; and

(f) the certificate described in Section 7.1(c).

2.7 **Closing Deliveries of Buyer.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.4 and the certificate described in Section 7.2(c).
