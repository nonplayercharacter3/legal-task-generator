## 2. Price Adjustment

2.1 Over the past eighteen months, Supplier has absorbed significant increases in the cost of membrane elements, activated carbon media, stainless steel housings and inbound freight, as well as higher labor costs at its manufacturing facility. To reflect those costs, Supplier is adjusting the Prices for all Products by fourteen percent (14%), effective November 1, 2026.

2.2 The adjusted Prices apply to all Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier on or after November 1, 2026. Purchase Orders accepted before that date will be invoiced at the Prices in effect on the date of acceptance.

2.3 This is the only adjustment to the Prices that Supplier will make in Contract Year 3 (July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027).
