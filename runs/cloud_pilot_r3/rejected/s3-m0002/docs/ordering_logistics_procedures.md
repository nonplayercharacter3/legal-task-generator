# SCHEDULE C — ORDERING AND LOGISTICS PROCEDURES

**to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2024 between Brightmere Instruments Inc. and Halvorsen Coastal Distribution LLC**

## 1. PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule C sets out the Ordering and Logistics Procedures referred to in Sections 1.1(h), 6.1, 6.3 and 6.4 of the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2024 between Brightmere Instruments Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Halvorsen Coastal Distribution LLC ("**Distributor**"), as amended from time to time in accordance with its terms (the "**Agreement**"). These procedures govern how Distributor orders Products and how Products are shipped, received, inspected and returned.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule C have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. In this Schedule C:

(a) "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

(b) "**Order Acknowledgment**" means Supplier's written acceptance of a Purchase Order under Section 3.

(c) "**Requested Ship Date**" means the date stated in a Purchase Order on which Distributor requests that the Products be made available to the carrier.

(d) "**RMA Number**" means a return material authorization number issued by Supplier under Section 7.

1.3 **Days.** Consistent with Section 1.3 of the Agreement, references to days in this Schedule C are to calendar days unless Business Days are expressly stated.

## 2. FORECASTS

2.1 **Rolling Forecast.** Distributor shall deliver the rolling forecast required by Section 4.4 of the Agreement on or before the fifteenth day of each calendar quarter, covering the following two calendar quarters, broken down by Part No. for each Product listed in Part 1 of Schedule A.

2.2 **Non-Binding Nature.** Forecasts are non-binding, as provided in Section 4.4 of the Agreement, and are used by Supplier for production planning only. A forecast does not constitute a Purchase Order.

2.3 **Format.** Forecasts shall be submitted by email to the Supplier order desk address in Section 9.1 using a spreadsheet in a format reasonably acceptable to both Parties.

## 3. PURCHASE ORDERS

3.1 **Content.** Each Purchase Order shall state:

(a) Distributor's Purchase Order number and date of issue;

(b) the Part No., description and quantity of each Product ordered;

(c) the applicable Price for each Product, as set out in Schedule B (as adjusted in accordance with Section 5 of the Agreement);

(d) the Requested Ship Date;

(e) the designated carrier and Distributor's carrier account number, if any; and

(f) the delivery address for the shipment, which shall be Distributor's warehouse at 215 Harbor Point Road, Port Jefferson, New York 11777, or such other location in the Territory as Distributor specifies in the Purchase Order.

3.2 **Submission.** Purchase Orders shall be submitted by email to the Supplier order desk address in Section 9.1, or through any electronic ordering portal that Supplier makes available and Distributor elects to use.

3.3 **Lead Time.** Distributor shall use reasonable efforts to state a Requested Ship Date not earlier than twenty-one (21) days after the date the Purchase Order is issued. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate shorter lead times where inventory permits.

3.4 **Acceptance Period.** For purposes of Section 6.1 of the Agreement, Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days after receipt by issuing an Order Acknowledgment or a written rejection stating the reason for rejection. Any Purchase Order that Supplier neither accepts nor rejects within that period is deemed accepted.

3.5 **Order Acknowledgment.** Each Order Acknowledgment shall confirm the Products, quantities, Prices and the scheduled ship date. If Supplier cannot meet the Requested Ship Date, the Order Acknowledgment shall state the earliest date on which Supplier can make the Products available to the carrier.

3.6 **Changes and Cancellations.** Distributor may change or cancel an accepted Purchase Order without charge by written notice delivered to the order desk at least ten (10) days before the scheduled ship date. Changes requested after that time are subject to Supplier's written agreement, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

3.7 **Terms of Purchase Orders.** As provided in Section 6.2 of the Agreement, any pre-printed or additional terms in any Purchase Order, Order Acknowledgment or invoice are of no force or effect.

## 4. SHIPPING

4.1 **Delivery Terms.** All Products are delivered FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

4.2 **Carrier Designation.** Distributor shall designate the carrier for each shipment in the applicable Purchase Order. If Distributor does not designate a carrier, Supplier may select a reputable common carrier on Distributor's behalf, in which case freight charges shall be billed to Distributor at Supplier's actual cost without markup and itemized separately on the invoice.

4.3 **Packaging.** Supplier shall pack Products in their original retail packaging, palletized and shrink-wrapped in a manner suitable for ground transportation and consistent with good commercial practice, so as to protect the Products from damage in ordinary handling.

4.4 **Labeling.** Each carton and pallet shall be labeled with Distributor's Purchase Order number, the Part No., the quantity and the manufacturing lot number of the Products it contains. Supplier shall not alter its product labeling or packaging in a manner inconsistent with Section 13.2 of the Agreement.

4.5 **Shipping Documents.** Each shipment shall be accompanied by a packing list stating the Purchase Order number, Part No., quantity and lot number of each Product, and a bill of lading. Supplier shall email a copy of the packing list and carrier tracking information to Distributor's receiving contact in Section 9.2 on the day of shipment.

4.6 **Partial Shipments.** Supplier may make partial shipments of an accepted Purchase Order only with Distributor's prior consent, which may be given by email. Each partial shipment shall be invoiced separately in accordance with Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

## 5. RECEIVING AND INSPECTION

5.1 **Receipt.** Distributor shall note on the carrier's delivery receipt any visible damage to, or shortage in, a shipment at the time of delivery.

5.2 **Inspection Period.** For purposes of Section 6.4 of the Agreement, Distributor shall inspect Products within fifteen (15) days after receipt at the delivery address.

5.3 **Notice of Non-Conformity.** If Distributor determines that any Products are non-conforming, damaged or short-shipped, Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing within the inspection period in Section 5.2, identifying the Purchase Order number, Part No., quantity, lot number and the nature of the non-conformity, with photographs where reasonably practicable.

5.4 **Supplier Response.** In accordance with Section 6.4 of the Agreement, Supplier shall, at its option and expense, promptly replace rejected Products or credit Distributor for their Price. Supplier shall issue an RMA Number for any rejected Products to be returned within five (5) Business Days after receiving Distributor's notice.

5.5 **Effect on Warranty.** Acceptance of Products following inspection does not limit Supplier's warranty obligations under Section 10 of the Agreement with respect to latent defects or any other non-conformity not reasonably discoverable on inspection.

## 6. INVOICING

6.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each Purchase Order, as provided in Section 5.4 of the Agreement. Each invoice shall reference the Purchase Order number and list the Part No., quantity, Price and any separately itemized freight charges and applicable taxes.

6.2 **Delivery of Invoices.** Invoices shall be sent by email to Distributor's accounts payable contact in Section 9.2.

6.3 **Payment and Disputes.** Payment terms, invoice disputes and late payment are governed by Sections 5.4 and 5.5 of the Agreement.

## 7. RETURNS

7.1 **RMA Required.** No Products may be returned to Supplier without an RMA Number. Distributor shall request an RMA Number by email to the Supplier returns contact in Section 9.1, identifying the Products, quantities, lot numbers and reason for return.

7.2 **Rejected Products and Warranty Returns.** For Products rejected under Section 5 or returned for breach of the warranty in Section 10.1 of the Agreement, Supplier shall bear all related shipping costs, consistent with Sections 6.4 and 10.3 of the Agreement, and shall provide a prepaid shipping label or arrange collection with the RMA Number.

7.3 **End-User Warranty Claims.** Customer and end-user claims under Supplier's standard Aquilon Limited Warranty referred to in Section 10.2 of the Agreement shall be processed in accordance with the claim instructions in that warranty. Distributor may consolidate end-user returns into periodic shipments to Supplier under a single RMA Number.

7.4 **Recalled Products.** Retrieval, return, storage and disposal of Products subject to a Recall shall be carried out as Supplier directs under Section 9.2 of the Agreement, and the related costs shall be reimbursed in accordance with Section 9.3 of the Agreement.

7.5 **Post-Term Repurchase.** Any repurchase of saleable Products by Supplier under Section 14.3(b) of the Agreement shall be processed under this Section 7, using an RMA Number issued for that purpose.

## 8. TRACEABILITY

8.1 **Lot Records.** Distributor shall record the lot number of each Product received and the lot numbers of Products sold to each Customer, so as to maintain the records required by Section 7.4 of the Agreement.

8.2 **Recall Support.** Upon a Recall, Distributor shall, within five (5) Business Days after Supplier's written request, provide Supplier with a list of Customers who purchased Products from the affected lots, to the extent reflected in the records maintained under Section 7.4 of the Agreement.
