# FIRST AMENDMENT TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This First Amendment to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Brightmere Instruments Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Concord Pike, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Halvorsen Coastal Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 215 Harbor Point Road, Suite 12, Port Jefferson, New York 11777 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Amendment as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of July 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Aquilon line of residential water-filtration systems in the Territory.

B. Supplier has introduced a new under-sink reverse osmosis model, and Distributor has requested an expansion of the Territory to include additional counties in the Hudson Valley region of the State of New York.

C. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement to update the Territory, the Products and the related Prices, and to make certain conforming changes, on the terms set out in this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar import shall mean the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. TERRITORY

2.1 **Expansion of Territory.** Part 2 of Schedule A (Products and Territory) is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **Part 2 — Territory**
>
> The following counties of the State of New York: Nassau, Suffolk, Kings, Queens, Richmond, Westchester, Rockland, Putnam, Dutchess and Orange.

2.2 **Existing Accounts.** Supplier shall, within thirty (30) days after the Amendment Effective Date, refer to Distributor in accordance with Section 3.2 of the Agreement all inquiries received from prospective Customers located in Dutchess County or Orange County.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.3

3.1 Section 12.3 is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to:
>
> (a) Distributor's obligations under Section 11.1; or
>
> (b) a Party's liability for its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 4. PRODUCTS AND PRICES

4.1 **Addition to Products.** Part 1 of Schedule A (Products and Territory) is hereby amended by adding the following rows at the end of the table:

| Part No. | Description |
|---|---|
| AQ-RO750 | Aquilon RO-750 Tankless Under-Sink Reverse Osmosis System |
| AQ-RC7S | Aquilon Replacement Cartridge Set (RO-750) |

4.2 **Prices for Added Products.** Part 1 of Schedule B (Prices and Minimum Annual Purchase Commitments) is hereby amended by adding the following rows at the end of the table:

| Part No. | Price |
|---|---|
| AQ-RO750 | $418.00 |
| AQ-RC7S | $62.00 |

4.3 **Existing Prices.** The Prices for the Products listed in Part 1 of Schedule B immediately before the Amendment Effective Date are not changed by this Amendment and remain subject to adjustment in accordance with Section 5 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Purchase Commitments.** Purchases of the Products added by Section 4.1 shall count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for each Contract Year in which they are purchased.

## 5. RATIFICATION

5.1 **Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and is hereby ratified and confirmed by each Party.

5.2 **No Waiver.** Nothing in this Amendment constitutes a waiver of any right or remedy of either Party under the Agreement with respect to any matter arising before the Amendment Effective Date.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Amendment and the Agreement as amended shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

6.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Amendment or the Agreement as amended, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

7.1 **Authority.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly executed by its authorized representative.

7.2 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

7.3 **Headings.** Headings in this Amendment are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
