# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Brightmere Instruments Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Concord Pike, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Halvorsen Coastal Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 215 Harbor Point Road, Suite 12, Port Jefferson, New York 11777 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures the Aquilon line of residential water-filtration systems and related replacement components.

B. Distributor is engaged in the wholesale distribution of residential plumbing, water-treatment and home-improvement products to dealers, installers and retailers in the State of New York.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

(b) "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on July 1 during the Term.

(c) "**Customer**" means any dealer, installer, retailer or other purchaser of Products from Distributor in the Territory.

(d) "**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**" means the minimum quantity of Products, expressed in dollar value at the then-current Prices, that Distributor shall purchase from Supplier in each Contract Year, as set out in Schedule B.

(e) "**Prices**" means the prices for the Products set out in Schedule B, as adjusted from time to time in accordance with Section 5.

(f) "**Products**" means the Aquilon residential water-filtration systems, replacement cartridges and accessories listed in Schedule A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

(g) "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement and in accordance with the Ordering and Logistics Procedures.

(h) "**Ordering and Logistics Procedures**" means the ordering, shipping, receiving and returns procedures set out in Schedule C.

(i) "**Recall**" means any recall, market withdrawal, field correction or safety notice relating to any Product, whether voluntary or required by a governmental authority.

(j) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

(k) "**Territory**" means the counties of the State of New York listed in Schedule A.

1.2 **Schedules.** The following Schedules form part of this Agreement: Schedule A (Products and Territory); Schedule B (Prices and Minimum Annual Purchase Commitments); and Schedule C (Ordering and Logistics Procedures).

1.3 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "including" and "includes" mean "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless otherwise stated. References to days are to calendar days.

1.4 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising this Agreement, the following order of precedence shall apply: written amendments, then the Agreement body, then Schedules and notices.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on July 1, 2024 and continues for an initial term of three years, ending June 30, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 14.

2.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party not more than 270 days nor less than 240 days before the expiration of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. The Minimum Annual Purchase Commitments set out in Schedule B, as adjusted under Section 4.3, shall apply in each Contract Year of each Renewal Term.

## 3. APPOINTMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

3.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or through any Affiliate or third party, sell the Products to any person for resale in the Territory, and shall refer to Distributor all inquiries received from prospective Customers located in the Territory.

3.3 **Distributor Restrictions.** Distributor shall not actively solicit Customers for the Products outside the Territory. Distributor shall not, during the Term, distribute in the Territory any residential water-filtration system that competes directly with the Products without Supplier's prior written consent.

3.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, agency or fiduciary relationship between the Parties. Distributor purchases Products for its own account and sets its own resale prices.

## 4. PURCHASE COMMITMENTS AND FORECASTS

4.1 **Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase Products having an aggregate value at least equal to the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for that Contract Year set out in Schedule B.

4.2 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment in any Contract Year, Supplier may, as its sole remedy for such shortfall, by written notice given within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year, either (a) convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive for the remainder of the Term, or (b) if the shortfall exceeds twenty-five percent (25%) of the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment, terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice.

4.3 **Renewal Term Commitments.** For each Contract Year of any Renewal Term, the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment shall equal the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the final Contract Year of the immediately preceding term, increased by five percent (5%).

4.4 **Forecasts.** On or before the fifteenth day of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated Product requirements for the following two calendar quarters.

## 5. PRICES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay Supplier the Prices for all Products purchased under this Agreement. Prices are stated in United States dollars and are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be borne by Distributor except for taxes based on Supplier's income.

5.2 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the Prices not more than once in any Contract Year by giving Distributor written notice of the adjustment (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**") not less than sixty (60) days before the adjusted Prices take effect. Each Price Adjustment Notice shall identify the affected Products, the adjusted Prices and the effective date of the adjustment.

5.3 **Objection to Price Adjustment.** Distributor may object to any increase in Prices stated in a Price Adjustment Notice by delivering written notice of objection to Supplier within 30 days after Distributor's receipt of a price adjustment notice. In its notice of objection, Distributor may elect either (a) to require the Parties to negotiate in good faith regarding the proposed increase for a period of thirty (30) days, during which the existing Prices shall continue to apply, or (b) to remove the affected Products from this Agreement, in which case the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment shall be reduced proportionately. If Distributor does not deliver a notice of objection within that period, Distributor shall be deemed to have accepted the adjusted Prices, which shall take effect on the date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice.

5.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each Purchase Order. Undisputed amounts are due within forty-five (45) days after Distributor's receipt of the invoice. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable supporting detail, within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

5.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.

## 6. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

6.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders in accordance with the Ordering and Logistics Procedures. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within the period stated in Schedule C; Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order consistent with Distributor's most recent forecast.

6.2 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice shall be of no force or effect.

6.3 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier designated in accordance with the Ordering and Logistics Procedures. Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

6.4 **Inspection and Acceptance.** Distributor shall inspect Products within the period stated in Schedule C following receipt and may reject any non-conforming Products by written notice to Supplier. Supplier shall, at its option and expense, promptly replace rejected Products or credit Distributor for their Price.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory and shall maintain a sales organization adequate for that purpose.

7.2 **Inventory.** Distributor shall maintain a reasonable inventory of Products sufficient to meet anticipated Customer demand in the Territory.

7.3 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its distribution of the Products, and shall not make any representation or warranty regarding the Products beyond those contained in Supplier's published product literature.

7.4 **Records.** Distributor shall maintain records of Product sales by Customer and lot number sufficient to enable the tracing of Products in the event of a Recall, and shall retain such records for at least three (3) years after the relevant sale.

## 8. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

8.1 **Supply.** Supplier shall manufacture and supply Products in quantities sufficient to fill Purchase Orders accepted under Section 6.1.

8.2 **Marketing Support.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, at no charge, reasonable quantities of product literature, technical documentation and training for Distributor's sales personnel.

8.3 **Regulatory Compliance.** Supplier shall be responsible for obtaining and maintaining all certifications, listings and approvals required for the Products to be lawfully sold in the Territory, including NSF/ANSI certifications claimed in Supplier's product literature.

## 9. PRODUCT RECALLS

9.1 **Notice.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other in writing upon becoming aware of any facts indicating that a Recall of any Product may be necessary or advisable.

9.2 **Conduct of Recall.** Supplier shall determine whether to initiate a Recall and shall direct its conduct. Distributor shall cooperate with Supplier in carrying out any Recall, including by using the records maintained under Section 7.4 to notify affected Customers.

9.3 **Recall Costs.** Unless the Recall results solely from Distributor's breach of this Agreement or Distributor's negligent storage or handling of Products, Supplier shall reimburse Distributor for all reasonable, documented costs and expenses incurred by Distributor in connection with any Recall, including costs of Customer notification, retrieval, shipping, storage and disposal of recalled Products, refunds or credits issued to Customers, and the Price paid by Distributor for recalled Products in Distributor's inventory. Supplier shall pay such amounts within thirty (30) days after receipt of Distributor's invoice and supporting documentation.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that, for twenty-four (24) months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to Supplier's published specifications, (b) be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship, and (c) comply with all applicable laws and the certifications referred to in Section 8.3.

10.2 **End-User Warranty.** Supplier shall extend to end users its standard limited warranty for the Products, a copy of which is included in Schedule A, and Distributor shall pass that warranty through to Customers without modification.

10.3 **Remedies.** For any breach of the warranty in Section 10.1, Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace the non-conforming Product or refund its Price, and shall bear all related shipping costs.

10.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its valid and binding obligation.

10.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **Indemnification by Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against any and all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim, suit or proceeding arising out of or relating to the distribution, marketing or resale of the Products.

11.2 **Indemnification by Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against any and all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim, suit or proceeding alleging (a) that any Product infringes or misappropriates any patent, trademark, copyright or trade secret of a third party, or (b) bodily injury or damage to tangible property caused by a manufacturing defect in a Product.

11.3 **Procedure.** The Party seeking indemnification shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, provided that failure to do so shall relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced; (b) permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the claim, provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR LOST REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

12.2 **Liability Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID BY DISTRIBUTOR TO SUPPLIER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to:

(a) Distributor's obligations under Section 11.1;

(b) Supplier's obligation to reimburse Recall costs under Section 9; or

(c) a Party's liability for its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 13. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

13.1 **Trademark License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the AQUILON trademark and Supplier's related trademarks in the Territory solely to advertise, promote and resell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's reasonable brand guidelines provided to Distributor in writing.

13.2 **Ownership.** All intellectual property rights in the Products and Supplier's trademarks remain with Supplier. Distributor shall not alter, remove or obscure any labeling, marking or packaging of the Products.

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

14.2 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for its business, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement: (a) Supplier shall fill all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of expiration or termination; (b) Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory of Products for a period of one hundred eighty (180) days, subject to the license in Section 13.1, or, at Distributor's election, Supplier shall repurchase saleable Products in their original packaging at the Prices paid by Distributor; and (c) all amounts owed by either Party shall become due in accordance with their terms.

14.4 **Survival.** Sections 5.4, 5.5, 7.4, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14.3, 14.4, 15, 16 and 17 shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 15. CONFIDENTIALITY

15.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including the Prices, shall use such information only to perform this Agreement, and shall not disclose it except to its employees, Affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as this Section 15.

15.2 **Exceptions.** The obligations in Section 15.1 do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed. A Party may disclose confidential information to the extent required by law or court order, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

15.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 15 continue during the Term and for three (3) years thereafter.

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods shall not apply.

17.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, upon written notice to the other Party.

18.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

18.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that it gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

18.4 **Insurance.** Each Party shall maintain, during the Term and for two (2) years thereafter, commercial general liability insurance (including products and completed operations coverage) with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, and shall provide a certificate of insurance on request.
