# INDUSTRIAL LEASE

This INDUSTRIAL LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made as of 16 January 2017 (the "**Lease Date**") by and between Ridgefold Properties LLC, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and Tessaline Filtration Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Basic Lease Provisions.**

| Item | Provision |
|---|---|
| Premises | The land and the single-tenant industrial building at 2210 Vista Ridge Boulevard, Sparks, Nevada 89431, containing approximately 185,000 rentable square feet, with the parking areas, truck courts and other improvements described in Exhibit A |
| Commencement Date | As stated in Section 2.1 |
| Initial Term | As stated in Section 2.2 |
| Base Rent | As stated in Section 3.1 |
| Security Deposit | $185,000 |
| Permitted Use | As stated in Section 6.1 |
| Renewal Option | As stated in Article 18 |

**1.2 Definitions.** In this Lease:

"**Additional Rent**" means all sums other than Base Rent payable by Tenant under this Lease, including Operating Expenses and Taxes.

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such person.

"**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance regulated as hazardous, toxic or a pollutant under any Environmental Law, including petroleum products, solvents and polymer processing by-products.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations and ordinances relating to the protection of the environment or human health.

"**Rent**" means Base Rent and Additional Rent.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term and, if validly exercised under Article 18, the Renewal Term.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Commencement Date.** The term of this Lease commences on 1 March 2017 (the "**Commencement Date**"). Tenant accepted possession of the Premises on the Commencement Date in their then "as-is" condition, subject only to Landlord's obligations under Section 8.2.

**2.2 Initial Term.** The initial term of this Lease is ten (10) years, beginning on the Commencement Date and expiring at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on 28 February 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless sooner terminated or extended in accordance with this Lease.

**2.3 Expiration.** Except as provided in Article 18, Tenant has no right to extend the Term or to remain in possession of the Premises after the expiration of the Initial Term.

**3.2 Late Charge.** Any installment of Rent not received within five (5) days after its due date bears a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount and interest at ten percent (10%) per annum from the due date until paid.

**3.3 Net Lease.** This Lease is a net lease. Except as expressly provided herein, Tenant shall pay all costs of operating, maintaining, repairing and insuring the Premises.

## 4. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**4.1 Operating Expenses.** Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, all reasonable costs incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining and insuring the Premises ("**Operating Expenses**"), excluding capital expenditures for the roof structure and foundation (except to the extent amortized over their useful life in accordance with GAAP), Landlord's financing costs, and costs caused by Landlord's negligence.

**4.2 Taxes.** Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, all real property taxes and assessments levied against the Premises for any period within the Term ("**Taxes**"), prorated for any partial year.

**4.3 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable monthly estimate of Operating Expenses and Taxes with each installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a reconciliation statement, and any overpayment shall be credited and any underpayment paid within thirty (30) days. Tenant may audit Landlord's records once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**5.1** Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore the amount applied within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance within thirty (30) days after the expiration of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 6. USE

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for the design, manufacture, assembly, testing, warehousing and distribution of industrial filtration systems, components and media, and related office and research uses (the "**Permitted Use**"), and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

**6.2 Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply, at its cost, with all laws, permits and insurance requirements applicable to its use of the Premises.

## 7. ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS

**7.1 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not use, store or release any Hazardous Materials on the Premises except in quantities customarily used in the Permitted Use and in compliance with all Environmental Laws.

**7.2 Baseline.** The parties acknowledge the Phase I environmental site assessment dated 9 December 2016 delivered to Tenant before the Lease Date (the "**Baseline Report**"). Tenant has no responsibility for any condition identified in the Baseline Report or otherwise existing before the Commencement Date.

**7.3 Environmental Indemnity.** Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims, costs and liabilities arising from any release of Hazardous Materials on the Premises during the Term caused by Tenant or its agents, employees or contractors. Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against all claims, costs and liabilities arising from any Hazardous Materials present on the Premises before the Commencement Date. This Section 7.3 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease.

## 8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

**8.1 Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall, at its cost, keep the Premises in good order, condition and repair, including the interior, building systems (HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire-suppression), dock equipment, parking areas and landscaping, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted.

**8.2 Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall, at its cost (subject to Section 4.1), maintain and repair the roof structure, foundation and exterior structural walls of the building.

## 9. ALTERATIONS

**9.1** Tenant may make non-structural alterations costing less than $250,000 per project without Landlord's consent on prior written notice. All other alterations require Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Tenant's trade fixtures, production equipment and machinery remain Tenant's property and may be removed by Tenant at the end of the Term, provided Tenant repairs any damage caused by their removal.

## 10. INSURANCE

**10.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, naming Landlord as additional insured; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and improvements at full replacement cost; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**10.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain all-risk property insurance on the building at full replacement cost, the premiums for which are included in Operating Expenses.

**10.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other for any loss covered by property insurance required to be maintained under this Lease.

## 11. INDEMNITY

**11.1** Subject to Section 10.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims arising from Tenant's use of the Premises or the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, and Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against all claims arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**12.1 Casualty.** If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the building with reasonable diligence, and Rent shall abate in proportion to the portion of the Premises rendered untenantable. If restoration cannot reasonably be completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within thirty (30) days after Landlord's estimate of the restoration period.

**12.2 Condemnation.** If all or a material part of the Premises is taken by eminent domain such that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct the Permitted Use, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its trade fixtures and relocation costs.

## 13. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**13.1 Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

**13.2 Change in Ownership.** Any transfer, in one or more transactions, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting stock of Tenant shall be treated as an assignment requiring Landlord's consent under Section 13.1. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receipt of the information reasonably required to evaluate it.

**13.3 Permitted Transfers.** Notwithstanding Section 13.1, Tenant may, on prior written notice but without consent, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an Affiliate of Tenant, provided the assignee assumes Tenant's obligations in writing.

**13.4 No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease unless Landlord agrees in writing.

## 14. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**14.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within ten (10) days after written notice of non-payment; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, does not begin to cure within that period and diligently complete the cure; or (c) Tenant becomes insolvent or the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**14.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may, in addition to any other remedy available at law, (a) terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises, and (b) recover from Tenant all Rent accrued to the date of termination and damages measured in accordance with applicable Nevada law. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**14.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, Tenant may perform the obligation and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after demand.

## 15. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATES

**15.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage or deed of trust on the Premises, provided the holder delivers a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists.

**15.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within ten (10) business days after request by the other, deliver a written statement certifying whether this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to the certifying party's knowledge, any default exists.

## 16. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

**16.1 Surrender.** At the expiration or termination of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in the condition required by Section 8.1, and shall remove its trade fixtures and personal property in accordance with Section 9.1.

**16.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration or termination of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect immediately before expiration, prorated on a daily basis, and shall indemnify Landlord against all losses resulting from the holdover, including claims of any succeeding tenant, if the holdover continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord's written notice to vacate.

## 17. LANDLORD'S ACCESS

**17.1** Landlord may enter the Premises on not less than forty-eight (48) hours' notice (except in an emergency) to inspect them, perform its obligations, and, during the last nine (9) months of the Term, show them to prospective tenants. Landlord shall comply with Tenant's reasonable safety and confidentiality procedures and shall minimize interference with Tenant's operations.

## 18. OPTION TO RENEW

**18.1 Grant of Option.** Landlord grants Tenant one (1) option to renew the Term for a single period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**"), commencing on the day after the expiration of the Initial Term, on the terms of this Lease except as provided in this Article 18. Tenant may exercise the option to renew only by written notice to Landlord (the "**Renewal Notice**") delivered not later than nine (9) months prior to the expiration of the Initial Term. The Renewal Notice must be delivered no later than nine months before expiry of the ten-year initial term, and any Renewal Notice delivered after that date is void and of no effect. Time is of the essence of this Section 18.1.

**18.2 Conditions.** Tenant may exercise the option to renew only if, at the time of delivering the Renewal Notice and on the first day of the Renewal Term, no Event of Default exists. The option to renew may be exercised by Tenant or any permitted assignee under Article 13.

**18.3 Renewal Rent.** Monthly Base Rent for the first lease year of the Renewal Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent for the Premises, and shall increase by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the first day of the Renewal Term. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the monthly base rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay, and a willing landlord would accept, for comparable industrial space in the Reno-Sparks market on a net basis for a comparable term. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Renewal Notice, each shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' industrial leasing experience in the Reno-Sparks market, and the two brokers shall appoint a third. Fair Market Rent shall be the average of the two closest of the three brokers' determinations, and the determination shall be binding on the parties.

**18.4 No Further Options.** There is no option to renew beyond the Renewal Term.

## 19. MISCELLANEOUS

**19.1 Notices.** All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) business day after dispatch by nationally recognized overnight courier, or three (3) business days after mailing by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth below each party's signature or such other address as a party may designate by notice.

**19.2 Governing Law.** This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Nevada.

**19.3 Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than those whose commissions are payable by Landlord under separate agreement, and shall indemnify the other against any claim by any other broker claiming through it.

**19.4 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or any person claiming through Landlord.

**19.5 Memorandum of Lease.** At either party's request, the parties shall execute a memorandum of this Lease in recordable form, which either party may record in the Official Records of Washoe County, Nevada.

**19.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**19.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**19.8 Successors.** Subject to Article 13, this Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**19.9 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
