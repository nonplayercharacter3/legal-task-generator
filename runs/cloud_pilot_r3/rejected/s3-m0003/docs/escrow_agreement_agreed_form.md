# ESCROW AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit A to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated 2 October 2026*

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among Halvorsen Industrial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Corvane Group LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Granite Ledger Trust Company, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are together the "**Principal Parties**" and each a "**Principal Party**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and, for limited purposes, Tessaline Filtration Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of 2 October 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from Seller.

B. Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount is security for Seller's obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent and to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2, accompanied by a written certification from the presenting Principal Party's counsel that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, as listed in Schedule 1.

"**Law**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Pending Claims Amount**" means, at any time, the aggregate amount of all claims in Claim Notices delivered on or before the Release Date that have not been resolved and paid under Section 4.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND DEPOSIT

**2.1 Deposit of Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account in Schedule 2, the sum of $14,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**").

**2.2 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated escrow account (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Agent shall hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds only as provided in this Agreement.

**2.3 Acknowledgment.** On receipt, the Escrow Agent shall confirm to the Principal Parties in writing that it has received the Escrow Amount.

**2.4 No Commingling.** The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with the Escrow Agent's own funds or any other funds. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, attachment, set-off or other claim by the Escrow Agent or its creditors, except as provided in Section 7.4.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds as directed in Joint Written Instructions in (a) direct obligations of the United States government maturing within one hundred eighty (180) days, (b) a money market fund registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940 that invests only in United States government obligations, or (c) an interest-bearing deposit account insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the extent of applicable limits. Without Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the investment described in clause (b).

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss arising from an investment made under this Section 3, or from any liquidation made to make a required disbursement.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds and shall be disbursed with the Escrow Funds under this Agreement.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Claim Notice.** At any time on or before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that Buyer or another indemnified party under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement has a claim for Losses. The Claim Notice must describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount claimed or, if not yet determinable, a good-faith estimate of it.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). The objection must state the amount in dispute and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Uncontested Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an objection within the Objection Period, the claim is conclusively deemed approved. Within five (5) Business Days after the end of the Objection Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Funds, the amount in the Claim Notice. If Seller objects to only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer within five (5) Business Days after receiving the objection.

**4.4 Contested Claims.** For any disputed amount, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold that amount in the Escrow Account. It shall disburse that amount only (a) under Joint Written Instructions or (b) under a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions promptly after any disputed claim is resolved by agreement. Disbursement shall occur within five (5) Business Days after the Escrow Agent receives the Joint Written Instructions or Final Order.

**4.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, the Escrow Agent shall hold that amount as part of the Pending Claims Amount. Buyer may amend the estimate by later written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. An increase in an estimate made after the Release Date is not effective against the Escrow Funds.

**4.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay all approved claims in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay the claims in the order in which the Escrow Agent received the related Claim Notices.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Release on Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, minus the Pending Claims Amount.

**5.2 Release of Pending Claims Amount.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claims Amount after the Release Date. As each pending claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount held for it under Section 4.4. Any part of that amount not payable to Buyer shall be disbursed to Seller.

**5.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Section 5 or Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse all or part of the Escrow Funds as directed in Joint Written Instructions.

**5.4 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account of the payee in Schedule 2, or to any other account the payee designates by written notice to the Escrow Agent. Any change to account details is subject to the call-back verification in Section 8.3.

## 6. TAX MATTERS

**6.1 Tax Owner.** The Principal Parties agree that, for United States federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed, in accordance with Proposed Treasury Regulation Section 1.468B-8. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Funds before disbursement.

**6.2 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer from the earnings on the Escrow Funds an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings attributable to that quarter, to fund Buyer's taxes on those earnings. Each such disbursement reduces the Escrow Funds.

**6.3 Tax Forms.** Each Principal Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8 before the Closing. The Escrow Agent shall file information returns as required by Law and may withhold from any disbursement any tax it is required by Law to withhold.

## 7. THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement, has no duties under it, and is not required to interpret it.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on any written notice, instruction or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and given by the proper person. The Escrow Agent may consult counsel and is fully protected in acting in good faith on that counsel's advice.

**7.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**7.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers and employees against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of its performance of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.4, unless the amount arises from the gross negligence or willful misconduct of one Principal Party, in which case that Principal Party shall bear it. The Escrow Agent has a first lien on the Escrow Funds only for amounts due to it under this Section 7.4 and Section 7.5 that remain unpaid thirty (30) days after written demand to the Principal Parties. This Section 7.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**7.5 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set forth in Schedule 3. Consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**7.6 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain about its duties, it may (a) continue to hold the Escrow Funds until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, or (b) deposit the Escrow Funds with the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware in an interpleader action. Its reasonable costs of any interpleader action are expenses under Section 7.5.

**7.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to the Principal Parties. The Principal Parties may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions on thirty (30) days' notice. A resignation or removal takes effect when a successor escrow agent, appointed by Joint Written Instructions, accepts appointment in writing. If no successor is appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2 to appoint one. On acceptance, the Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Funds to the successor, and the successor shall have all rights and duties of the Escrow Agent under this Agreement.

**7.8 Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall provide monthly statements of the Escrow Account to each Principal Party.

## 8. NOTICES AND INSTRUCTIONS

**8.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses in Schedule 1 or another address a party designates by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**8.2 Authorized Representatives.** Each Principal Party designates the individuals listed for it in Schedule 1 as authorized to sign instructions on its behalf. A Principal Party may change its authorized representatives by written notice signed by an existing authorized representative.

**8.3 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any funds-transfer instruction, or any change to payee account details, the Escrow Agent shall verify it by telephone call-back to an authorized representative of the instructing Principal Party, other than the person who signed the instruction where practicable, at the number in Schedule 1. The Escrow Agent may delay a transfer until verification is completed.

## 9. TERM

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed under this Agreement. Sections 7.3, 7.4 and 10 survive termination.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if and only if such court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and agrees that all claims in respect of any such action shall be heard and determined only in such court.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.2 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that (a) Buyer may assign its rights to any permitted assignee under Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement, on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent, and (b) any successor to the Escrow Agent by merger or consolidation, or by transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the Escrow Agent without further act.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns, and confers no rights on any other Person.

**11.5 USA PATRIOT Act.** Each Principal Party shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information reasonably requested to verify its identity under applicable anti-money laundering Laws.

**11.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Electronic signatures are deemed original signatures.
