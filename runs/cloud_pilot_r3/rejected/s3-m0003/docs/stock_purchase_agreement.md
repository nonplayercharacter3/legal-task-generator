# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of 2 October 2026, by and among Halvorsen Industrial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Corvane Group LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Article VI, Tessaline Filtration Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement and each other agreement, certificate or instrument delivered at the Closing under this Agreement.

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of industrial filtration systems, including aftermarket parts and replacement media.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Granite Ledger Trust Company.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent, substantially in the form attached as Exhibit A.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $14,850,000.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, court or tribunal thereof.

"**Landlord**" means Ridgefold Properties LLC.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $148,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX; and

(b) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, less the Estimated Closing Indebtedness, plus or minus the amount by which Estimated Working Capital exceeds or falls short of the Target Working Capital,

in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in writing by the recipient at least two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Purchase Price Adjustment.** Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's good-faith estimate of the Company's working capital ("**Estimated Working Capital**") and indebtedness ("**Estimated Closing Indebtedness**") as of the Closing. Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver a closing statement setting forth the actual amounts. Seller shall have forty-five (45) days to dispute the closing statement, and any unresolved dispute shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days of final determination. "**Target Working Capital**" means the amount set forth in Schedule 2.3.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on 30 November 2026, or, if the conditions set forth in Article VII have not been satisfied or waived by that date, on the third (3rd) Business Day after all such conditions have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time as Buyer and Seller may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer (a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers attached; (b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller (and, in the case of the Transition Services Agreement, the Company); (c) the third-party consents listed in Schedule 4.5; (d) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer; and (e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver (a) the payments described in Section 2.2; (b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware, and the Company is duly qualified to do business in the State of Nevada.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations hereunder and thereunder. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any capital stock of the Company.

**4.4 No Subsidiaries.** The Company has no subsidiaries and does not own any equity interest in any other Person.

**4.5 Consents.** Schedule 4.5 sets forth the consents, approvals and notices of third parties to be obtained or given by Seller or the Company in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement and the consummation of the transactions contemplated hereby. No consent, approval or filing with any Governmental Authority is required in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of 31 December 2024 and 31 December 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of 30 June 2026 and related statements for the six-month period then ended (collectively, the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of the respective dates and for the respective periods indicated, subject, in the case of interim statements, to normal year-end adjustments and the absence of notes.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since 30 June 2026, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists each contract to which the Company is a party that (a) involves annual payments in excess of $500,000, (b) is a sole-source or exclusive supply arrangement, (c) is a lease of real property, or (d) restricts the Company from competing in any line of business (collectively, the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and, to Seller's knowledge, the other parties thereto, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach of or default under any Material Contract.

**4.9 Real Property.** The Company does not own any real property. The Lease is the only lease of real property to which the Company is a party. The Lease is in full force and effect, including the renewal option set forth in Section 18.1 of the Lease, and the Company holds a valid leasehold interest in the Sparks Facility, free and clear of all liens other than liens for taxes not yet due and payable. The Company has not received any written notice of default under the Lease.

**4.10 Title to Assets.** The Company has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible personal property used in the Business, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens.

**4.11 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all intellectual property used in the conduct of the Business as currently conducted, and, to Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property of any Person.

**4.12 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Shares.

**4.13 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since 1 January 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, and holds all permits necessary for the conduct of the Business as currently conducted.

**4.14 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all applicable environmental Laws and has not received any written notice of any violation of environmental Law relating to the Sparks Facility.

**4.15 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.15 lists each material employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in material compliance with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.16 Taxes.** All income and other material tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and all taxes shown thereon have been paid.

**4.17 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price and all other amounts payable by it hereunder.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed), Seller shall cause the Company to (a) conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, (b) use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization, assets, relationships with customers, suppliers and employees, and (c) not amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Notice of Certain Events.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that has had or would reasonably be expected to have a Material Adverse Effect or that would cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, prior to the Closing, each of the consents listed in Schedule 4.5, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** From the date hereof until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, initiate, knowingly encourage or engage in discussions concerning any acquisition of the Company, the Shares or any material portion of the Company's assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business, except to the extent required by Law.

**6.7 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, execute and deliver the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.8 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (a) engage in, own, manage, operate or control any Person engaged in the Business anywhere in North America, Europe or any other jurisdiction in which the Company conducted the Business during the twelve (12) months preceding the Closing Date, or (b) solicit or hire any employee of the Company, or induce any customer or supplier of the Company to cease or adversely modify its relationship with the Company; provided that Seller may own, as a passive investment, up to two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any Person. Seller acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 6.8 are reasonable and necessary to protect the goodwill of the Business acquired by Buyer.

**6.9 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as required by Law.

**6.10 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to (a) no Governmental Authority having enacted or entered any Law or order that makes the transactions contemplated hereby illegal or otherwise restrains or prohibits their consummation, and (b) any waiting period applicable under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, having expired or been terminated, if applicable.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, if qualified by materiality or Material Adverse Effect, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed and complied in all material respects with all covenants required by this Agreement to be performed by it prior to or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) each of the consents listed in Schedule 4.5 shall have been obtained and shall be in full force and effect;

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed and complied in all material respects with all covenants required by this Agreement to be performed by it prior to or at the Closing;

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Buyer certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b) have been satisfied; and

(d) Buyer shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and such breach has not been cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice;

(c) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and such breach has not been cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or

(d) by Buyer or Seller, if the Closing has not occurred on or before 31 January 2027, provided that the right to terminate under this Section 8.1(d) is not available to a party whose breach caused the failure of the Closing to occur by that date.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that Sections 6.9, this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve any party of liability for fraud or willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.17 (the "**Fundamental Representations**") shall survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective representatives against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement, or (c) any indebtedness or transaction expenses of the Company outstanding at the Closing and not taken into account in the calculation of the Purchase Price.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.** (a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud) until aggregate Losses exceed $1,485,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount. (b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) (other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud) shall not exceed the Escrow Amount. (c) Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**9.5 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail, provided that failure to give such notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

**9.6 Escrow Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer indemnified party under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the funds held by the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement, and thereafter directly by Seller, subject to Section 9.4.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Subject to Section 11.10, and except in the case of fraud, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement following the Closing.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall be responsible for and shall indemnify Buyer against all taxes of the Company attributable to any tax period (or portion thereof) ending on or before the Closing Date, to the extent not reflected as a liability in the final determination of working capital under Section 2.3.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and similar taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of tax returns and any tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided herein, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby. The fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages hereto or such other address as a party may designate by notice.

**11.3 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, such invalidity shall not affect any other term of this Agreement or invalidate such term in any other jurisdiction.

**11.4 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail, except that an Ancillary Agreement shall prevail with respect to matters within its own subject matter.

**11.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**11.6 Assignment.** No party may assign its rights or delegate its obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates or, after the Closing, to any purchaser of all or substantially all of the equity or assets of the Company, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations hereunder.

**11.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns, and nothing herein shall confer any right or remedy upon any other Person.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.9 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if and only if such court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and agrees that all claims in respect of any such action shall be heard and determined only in such court. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission shall be deemed original signatures.
