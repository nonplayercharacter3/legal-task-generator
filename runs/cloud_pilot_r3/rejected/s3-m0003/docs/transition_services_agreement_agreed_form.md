# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit B to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated 2 October 2026*

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between Corvane Group LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and Tessaline Filtration Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Provider and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider, Halvorsen Industrial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and the Company are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of 2 October 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Provider is selling all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company to Buyer.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates have provided certain administrative, information technology and support services to the Company. Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and the Company to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing so that Provider continues to provide those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and in the Purchase Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Business**" means the manufacture of filtration systems as conducted at the Sparks Facility on the Closing Date. This definition of "Business" shall apply for all purposes of the Transaction Documents.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Data**" means all data and records of or relating to the Company or the Business, in any form, that are processed, stored or maintained by Provider or its Affiliates in providing the Services.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending on the date set forth for that Service in Schedule A, unless terminated earlier or extended under Section 7.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.3.

"**Sparks Facility**" means the Company's manufacturing facility located in Sparks, Nevada.

"**Transaction Documents**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates or permitted subcontractors to provide, each Service to the Company in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in substantially the same manner, scope, volume, priority and quality as those services were provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, (b) with no less care than Provider uses in providing similar services to its own businesses, and (c) in compliance with applicable Law.

**2.3 Additional Services.** If, within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, the Company identifies any service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company in the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date and that is not listed in Schedule A (an "**Omitted Service**"), the Company may request it by written notice. Provider shall then provide the Omitted Service on the terms of this Agreement, at a fee determined under Section 5.1. The Parties may also agree in writing to other services ("**Additional Services**"). Upon agreement, Schedule A shall be deemed amended to include each Omitted Service and Additional Service.

**2.4 Subcontractors.** Provider may engage subcontractors to perform any Service if they were engaged for substantially similar services before the Closing Date, or with the Company's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld. Provider remains responsible for the performance of any subcontractor as if it were Provider's own performance.

**2.5 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any third-party consents or licenses required to provide the Services. The costs of obtaining those consents shall be shared equally by the Parties. If a required consent cannot be obtained, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a commercially reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 3. COMPANY RESPONSIBILITIES

**3.1 Cooperation.** The Company shall provide Provider with information, access and assistance reasonably required to perform the Services and shall make timely decisions and approvals reasonably requested by Provider.

**3.2 Migration.** The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to transition each Service to its own internal resources or to a third-party provider before the end of the applicable Service Period. Provider shall provide reasonable migration assistance under Section 7.4.

## 4. GOVERNANCE

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall appoint a service coordinator (each a "**Service Coordinator**") who will be the primary point of contact for the day-to-day administration of this Agreement. Each Party may replace its Service Coordinator on written notice.

**4.2 Escalation.** Any dispute about the Services shall first be referred to the Service Coordinators. If they do not resolve it within ten (10) Business Days, it shall be referred to a senior executive of each Party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Business Days to resolve it in good faith before either Party starts proceedings under Section 12, other than proceedings for urgent injunctive relief.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** The Company shall pay Provider the fees for each Service set forth in Schedule A (the "**Service Fees**"). For any Omitted Service or Additional Service, the fee shall equal Provider's documented direct cost of providing the service, without mark-up.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** The Company shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket third-party costs incurred specifically to provide the Services, if those costs are identified in Schedule A or approved in advance in writing by the Company.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. The Company may withhold amounts disputed in good faith if it notifies Provider in writing of the dispute within the payment period. Disputed amounts shall be resolved under Section 4.2.

**5.4 Taxes.** Service Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be borne by the Company. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. DATA, CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**6.1 Company Data.** As between the Parties, all Company Data is the exclusive property of the Company. Provider shall use Company Data only to perform the Services. Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Company Data that are no less protective than those in effect before the Closing Date and that comply with applicable Law. Provider shall notify the Company without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or loss of Company Data.

**6.2 Return of Data.** At the end of each Service Period, and in any event upon termination of this Agreement, Provider shall deliver all Company Data relating to the terminated Service to the Company in a commonly used, machine-readable format. After the Company confirms receipt, Provider shall securely delete its remaining copies, except copies retained under applicable Law or automatic archival systems, which shall remain subject to Section 6.3.

**6.3 Confidentiality.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party received in connection with this Agreement, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those herein, or as required by Law. This Section 6.3 is in addition to, and does not limit, Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.4 Intellectual Property.** Each Party retains ownership of its intellectual property. Provider grants the Company, for the duration of the applicable Service Period, a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any systems, software or materials of Provider to the extent necessary to receive the Services. Any deliverables created specifically for the Company under this Agreement are owned by the Company.

**6.5 Access to Systems.** Each Party shall comply with the reasonable security policies of the other Party that are notified in writing when accessing the other Party's systems or premises.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Closing Date and continues until the expiry or termination of the last Service Period, unless terminated earlier under this Section 7.

**7.2 Extension.** The Company may extend the Service Period for any Service once, by up to ninety (90) days, by written notice to Provider at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current Service Period. During any extension, the Service Fees for the extended Service shall be the Service Fees in Schedule A increased by ten percent (10%).

**7.3 Termination.**

(a) The Company may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Service Fees for the terminated Service shall end on the effective date of termination. Unless otherwise stated in Schedule A, termination of a Service does not affect any other Service.

(b) Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Provider may terminate a Service for the Company's non-payment only if undisputed Service Fees for that Service remain unpaid for more than forty-five (45) days after written notice of non-payment.

**7.4 Migration Assistance.** Before the end of each Service Period, Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to transition the relevant Service to the Company or its designee, including providing knowledge transfer and making Company Data available under Section 6.2. Migration assistance shall be charged at Provider's documented direct cost.

**7.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiry or termination of any Service or this Agreement, the Company shall pay all Service Fees accrued up to the effective date of termination. Sections 5, 6, 7.5, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 13 survive any expiry or termination of this Agreement.

**8.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its valid and binding obligation.

**8.2 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, REGARDING THE SERVICES, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Company and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from (a) Provider's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct in providing the Services, (b) any breach by Provider of Section 6, or (c) any claim that the Services or any materials of Provider used to provide them infringe the intellectual property of a third party.

**9.2 By the Company.** The Company shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from the Company's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct in receiving the Services.

**9.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of such claim (provided that no settlement imposing any obligation on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion.** EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING UNDER SECTION 9, A BREACH OF SECTION 6, OR A PARTY'S GROSS NEGLIGENCE, FRAUD OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.2 Cap.** EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED MATTERS IN SECTION 10.1, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL SERVICE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Purchase Agreement.** Nothing in this Section 10 limits any right or remedy of Buyer or the Company under the Purchase Agreement.

## 11. FORCE MAJEURE

**11.1** Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, fire or flood, if the affected Party gives prompt written notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During any such event, Service Fees for any affected Service shall be suspended to the extent the Service is not provided. If a force majeure event affecting Provider continues for more than thirty (30) days, the Company may procure the affected Service from a third party and may terminate it under Section 7.3(a) without the notice period.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**12.1 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.**

(a) This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Services or any term defined in this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

(b) Notwithstanding anything in the Purchase Agreement, each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York County, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and agrees that all claims in respect of any such action shall be heard and determined only in such courts. Each Party waives any objection to venue in such courts and any claim that such courts are an inconvenient forum.

**12.2 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**12.3 Injunctive Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of Section 6 may cause irreparable harm. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

## 13. GENERAL

**13.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page or such other address as a Party may designate by notice. A copy of any notice to the Company shall be sent concurrently to Buyer.

**13.2 Independent Contractors.** Provider acts as an independent contractor in providing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**13.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that the Company may assign this Agreement without consent to Buyer, any Affiliate of Buyer, or any purchaser of all or substantially all of the equity or assets of the Company.

**13.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**13.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**13.6 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce the Company's rights hereunder. Otherwise, this Agreement confers no rights on any Person other than the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**13.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Electronic signatures are deemed original signatures.
