# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of 1 April 2022 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between Okonjo Polymer Works Ltd., a corporation having its principal place of business at 4400 Industrial Parkway, Akron, Ohio ("**Supplier**"), and Tessaline Filtration Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal manufacturing facility in Sparks, Nevada ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures specialty polymer resins, including resins formulated to Customer's proprietary specifications for use in industrial filtration media.

B. Customer designs, manufactures, sells and services industrial filtration systems and wishes to purchase from Supplier, and Supplier wishes to sell to Customer, such resins on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such person, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of a person or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 11.1.

"**Facility**" means Customer's manufacturing facility located in Sparks, Nevada, or such other facility in the United States as Customer may designate by written notice.

"**Forecast**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Products**" means the filtration-grade polymer resins listed in Schedule A, each manufactured in accordance with the applicable Specifications.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order issued by Customer for Products under Section 4.2.

"**Specifications**" means Customer's technical specifications for each Product set forth in or referenced in Schedule A, as amended from time to time under Section 6.3.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of five (5) years (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 13 or Section 14.

**2.2 Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of two (2) years each (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least twelve (12) months before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## 3. SUPPLY AND PURCHASE

**3.1 Supply Commitment.** During the Term, Supplier shall manufacture and sell to Customer, and Customer shall purchase from Supplier, the Products in the quantities ordered under Purchase Orders issued in accordance with Section 4.

**3.2 Sole Source.** Customer designates Supplier as its sole source of supply of the Products, and Customer shall purchase from Supplier not less than one hundred percent (100%) of its requirements of the Products for use at the Facility, except as provided in Section 3.4.

**3.3 Supply Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity sufficient to supply at least one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the volumes set forth in Customer's then-current Forecast.

**3.4 Supply Failure.** If Supplier fails to deliver at least ninety percent (90%) of the quantity of any Product ordered under conforming Purchase Orders in any two (2) consecutive calendar months, Customer may, without liability, purchase such Product from a third party for so long as the failure continues and for a qualification period of up to ninety (90) days thereafter. Supplier shall provide reasonable technical cooperation to enable such third party to manufacture the Product to the Specifications, subject to Section 11.

## 4. FORECASTS AND ORDERS

**4.1 Forecasts.** On or before the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Customer shall provide Supplier with a rolling twelve (12) month non-binding forecast of its anticipated requirements for each Product (each a "**Forecast**"). The first three (3) months of each Forecast shall be firm, and Customer shall issue Purchase Orders for at least ninety percent (90%) of the quantities shown in such firm period.

**4.2 Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders specifying the Product, quantity, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt. Supplier shall not reject any Purchase Order that is consistent with the firm period of the applicable Forecast and that calls for delivery not less than thirty (30) days after the date of the Purchase Order.

**4.3 Terms of Orders.** Each accepted Purchase Order is governed by the terms of this Agreement. Any additional or different terms in any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect unless agreed in writing by both Parties.

## 5. PRICE AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Prices.** The prices for the Products are set forth in Schedule B. Prices are stated in United States dollars and are exclusive of applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be borne by Customer.

**5.2 Price Adjustment.** Prices are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust prices once in each contract year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before such anniversary. Any increase shall not exceed the lesser of (a) five percent (5%) and (b) the percentage change in Supplier's documented raw-material costs for the Products over the preceding twelve (12) months.

**5.3 Most Favored Customer.** Supplier represents that the prices charged to Customer for each Product are no less favorable than the prices Supplier charges any other customer for the same or substantially similar product in comparable volumes.

**5.4 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Customer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith, provided that Customer notifies Supplier of the dispute in writing within the payment period.

## 6. QUALITY AND SPECIFICATIONS

**6.1 Quality Standards.** Supplier shall manufacture the Products in accordance with the Specifications, applicable Laws and Supplier's ISO 9001 certified quality management system. Supplier shall deliver a certificate of analysis with each shipment.

**6.2 Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test the Products within thirty (30) days after delivery and may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications. Supplier shall, at Customer's election, promptly replace rejected Products or credit their purchase price, and shall bear all costs of return and replacement.

**6.3 Changes.** Customer may propose changes to the Specifications on written notice. Supplier shall implement any such change within a reasonable period, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith any equitable adjustment to price or lead time. Supplier shall make no change to the formulation, raw materials, manufacturing site or process for any Product without Customer's prior written approval.

**6.4 Audit.** Upon at least ten (10) business days' prior notice, Customer may audit Supplier's manufacturing facilities and quality records relating to the Products not more than once in any twelve (12) month period, or at any time following a material quality failure.

## 7. DELIVERY

**7.1 Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver the Products FCA Supplier's plant (Incoterms 2020), packaged and labeled in accordance with Schedule A. Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery to Customer's designated carrier.

**7.2 Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly upon becoming aware of any anticipated delay in delivery. If a delivery is more than ten (10) days late, Supplier shall, at Customer's request, ship by expedited means at Supplier's cost.

**7.3 Safety Stock.** Supplier shall maintain at its own cost a safety stock of each Product equal to not less than sixty (60) days of Customer's average monthly requirements under the then-current Forecast.

## 8. WARRANTIES

**8.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for a period of eighteen (18) months after delivery, each Product shall (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship, (c) be manufactured in compliance with all applicable Laws, and (d) be delivered free and clear of all liens and encumbrances.

**8.2 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its valid and binding obligation.

**8.3 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from (a) any breach of Supplier's warranties under Section 8, (b) any defect in the Products, or (c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier.

**9.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from (a) any claim that the Specifications infringe the intellectual property of a third party, or (b) the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer.

**9.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of such claim (provided that no settlement imposing any obligation on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1** EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING UNDER SECTION 9, A BREACH OF SECTION 11, OR A PARTY'S GROSS NEGLIGENCE, FRAUD OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, HOWEVER CAUSED.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**11.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the Specifications, formulations, pricing, Forecasts and the terms of this Agreement.

**11.2 Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those herein. These obligations survive for five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

**11.3 Ownership of Specifications.** The Specifications and all intellectual property in them are and remain the exclusive property of Customer. Supplier shall not use the Specifications, or any formulation developed to meet them, to manufacture or supply resin to any person other than Customer.

## 12. FORCE MAJEURE

**12.1** Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, fire or flood, provided that the affected Party gives prompt written notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event affecting Supplier continues for more than sixty (60) days, Customer may terminate this Agreement on written notice, and Section 3.4 shall apply during the period of the event.

## 13. TERMINATION

**13.1 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**13.2 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**13.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination or expiry, (a) Supplier shall fulfil all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination, unless Customer elects otherwise, (b) Customer shall pay all amounts properly due for Products delivered, (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, and (d) Supplier shall, at Customer's election, sell to Customer all safety stock held under Section 7.3 at the then-current prices.

**13.4 Transition Assistance.** Upon any termination or expiry other than termination by Supplier under Section 13.1, Supplier shall continue to supply the Products on the terms of this Agreement for up to one hundred eighty (180) days at Customer's request to allow Customer to qualify an alternative source.

**13.5 Survival.** Sections 8, 9, 10, 11, 13.3, 13.4, 15 and 16 survive any termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

**14.1 Assignment by Customer.** Customer may not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14.1 is void.

**14.2 Change of Control of Customer.** Any transaction or series of related transactions, whether by merger, sale of stock, operation of law or otherwise, as a result of which any person or group of persons acquires, directly or indirectly, more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting stock of Customer shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer for purposes of Section 14.1 and shall require the prior written consent of Supplier. If any such transaction occurs without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Customer given at any time after Supplier becomes aware of such transaction, and Sections 13.4 shall not apply to such termination.

**14.3 Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may assign or otherwise transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, together with any of its rights and obligations hereunder, without the consent of Customer, to (a) any Affiliate of Supplier, (b) any successor to all or any part of Supplier's business or assets, whether by merger, sale of stock, sale of assets or otherwise, or (c) any other person.

**14.4 Binding Effect.** Subject to this Section 14, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

**15.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods shall not apply.

**15.2 Escalation.** Before commencing any proceeding (other than for urgent injunctive relief), a Party shall give the other written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within fifteen (15) business days to attempt in good faith to resolve it.

**15.3 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 16. GENERAL

**16.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) business day after dispatch by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set forth below each Party's signature or such other address as a Party may designate by notice.

**16.2 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**16.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**16.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**16.5 Insurance.** Supplier shall maintain, during the Term and for three (3) years thereafter, commercial general liability insurance including products liability coverage of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, and shall provide certificates of insurance to Customer on request.

**16.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Electronic signatures are deemed original signatures.
