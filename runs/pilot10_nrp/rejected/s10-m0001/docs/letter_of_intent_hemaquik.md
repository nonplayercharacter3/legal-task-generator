# LETTER OF INTENT

**KOLAIS ANIMAL HEALTH DIAGNOSTICS, INC.**

March 9, 2026

**CONFIDENTIAL**

Sounour Veterinary Systems, LLC
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re: Proposed Acquisition of the HemaQuik Business**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Kolais Animal Health Diagnostics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire from Sounour Veterinary Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), the assets of Seller's HemaQuik point-of-care companion-animal hematology analyzer business (the "**Transaction**"). Except as provided in Paragraph 13, this Letter is not binding on either party.

## 1. Business and Purchased Assets

The business to be acquired is the design, manufacture, marketing, sale, distribution and servicing of the HemaQuik point-of-care companion-animal hematology analyzer line and the associated HemaQuik reagent cartridges (the "**Business**"). Buyer would acquire substantially all of the assets used or held for use primarily in the Business, free and clear of all liens (the "**Purchased Assets**"). The Purchased Assets would include:

(a) all inventory of analyzers, reagent cartridges, spare parts, raw materials, work in process and finished goods;

(b) all patents, trademarks (including the HEMAQUIK mark), software, firmware, trade secrets, know-how and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business;

(c) the customer, distribution, supply and service contracts of the Business, including the exclusive distribution agreement between Seller and Fennwick Veterinary Hospitals, Inc. ("**Fennwick**") covering HemaQuik analyzers and cartridges (the "**Fennwick Distribution Agreement**");

(d) manufacturing equipment, tooling, molds and test fixtures;

(e) regulatory files, product registrations, technical documentation, quality records and complaint files; and

(f) customer lists, pricing information, books and records of the Business, and the related goodwill.

## 2. Retained Business and Excluded Assets

Seller would keep its veterinary reference laboratory business (the "**Retained Business**") and all of its assets. Seller would also keep cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable arising before Closing, and its organizational and tax records.

## 3. Liabilities

Buyer would assume only (a) liabilities arising from the operation of the Business and the Purchased Assets after Closing, and (b) obligations under the assigned contracts to be performed after Closing. Seller would keep all other liabilities. These include product liability, warranty and recall liabilities for products manufactured or sold before Closing, Seller's taxes and pre-Closing taxes of the Business, employee liabilities arising on or before Closing, liabilities of the Retained Business, and indebtedness for borrowed money.

## 4. Purchase Price

The purchase price for the Purchased Assets would be $48,600,000, payable in cash at Closing, plus the assumption of the assumed liabilities described in Paragraph 3. The purchase price assumes that the Business is delivered with a normalized level of inventory. It is subject to confirmatory due diligence.

## 5. Earnout

In addition to the purchase price, Buyer would pay Seller an earnout based on net sales of HemaQuik reagent cartridges for calendar years 2027 and 2028. Each annual payment would equal a percentage of net cartridge sales above an agreed annual threshold, and aggregate earnout payments would be capped at $6,000,000. The parties will agree the thresholds, the percentage, the statement and dispute procedures and Seller's verification rights in the Definitive Agreement.

## 6. Third-Party Consents; Fennwick

Seller has advised Buyer that Fennwick's prior written consent is required under the Fennwick Distribution Agreement for any assignment of that agreement or any change of control. The Fennwick Distribution Agreement is Seller's principal sales channel for the Business. Fennwick's consent to the assignment of the Fennwick Distribution Agreement to Buyer is required under the Fennwick Distribution Agreement, and obtaining it is a condition to Closing. Seller will be responsible for requesting and obtaining that consent, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, and Buyer will cooperate reasonably with Seller in that process. Seller will also identify in the Definitive Agreement any other third-party consents required to assign the contracts of the Business.

## 7. Indemnification and Escrow

The Definitive Agreement would contain representations, warranties and indemnities customary for a transaction of this type. Seller's indemnity for breaches of general representations and warranties would be subject to a deductible basket and a cap of 12% of the purchase price. Fundamental representations, fraud, covenant breaches and Excluded Liabilities would not be subject to the basket or the cap. At Closing, $4,860,000 of the purchase price would be deposited in escrow with a mutually acceptable escrow agent for 18 months after Closing, as a source of recovery for Buyer's indemnification claims.

## 8. Definitive Agreement and Ancillary Documents

The Transaction would be documented in an asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"), with a bill of sale, an assignment and assumption agreement, an intellectual property assignment, an escrow agreement and a transition services agreement. Buyer's counsel will prepare the first draft of the Definitive Agreement. The parties will work in good faith to sign the Definitive Agreement in May 2026.

## 9. Conditions to Closing

In addition to the condition in Paragraph 6, Buyer's obligation to close would be conditioned on:

(a) satisfactory completion of Buyer's business, financial, legal, regulatory, intellectual property and technical due diligence, including review of the recall history of HemaQuik analyzers;

(b) execution of the Definitive Agreement and the ancillary documents;

(c) accuracy of Seller's representations and performance of its covenants;

(d) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business; and

(e) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's board of directors.

## 10. Timing

The parties will target a Closing of June 30, 2026, or such other date as they may agree ("**Closing**").

## 11. Restrictive Covenants; Employees; Transition Services

(a) *Non-competition.* For five years after Closing, Seller and its affiliates would not design, manufacture, market or sell any point-of-care companion-animal hematology analyzer or reagent cartridge. This restriction would not limit the Retained Business as then conducted.

(b) *Employees.* Buyer may offer employment, effective at Closing, to Seller employees dedicated to the Business. Buyer and Seller will agree the list of those employees during due diligence.

(c) *Transition services.* Seller would provide information technology, laboratory information management system and data migration support, and related transition services, for up to nine months after Closing. The terms will be set out in a transition services agreement.

## 12. Access and Conduct of Business

Between the date of this Letter and Closing, Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the personnel, properties, contracts, books and records of the Business. Seller will also operate the Business in the ordinary course and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and distributors, including Fennwick.

## 13. Binding Provisions

Only this Paragraph 13 and the following provisions are binding on the parties:

(a) *Exclusivity.* Until the earlier of May 31, 2026 and the date on which either party gives written notice ending negotiations, Seller will not, and will cause its affiliates and representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any Person other than Buyer for any sale of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of that kind.

(b) *Confidentiality.* The terms of this Letter and the information exchanged in connection with the Transaction remain subject to the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between the parties dated January 22, 2026.

(c) *Expenses.* Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with the Transaction, whether or not it closes.

(d) *Governing Law.* This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to conflict of law principles.

(e) *No Obligation.* Neither party is obligated to complete the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is signed and delivered. Either party may end negotiations at any time by written notice, subject to its continuing obligations under this Paragraph 13.

(f) *Counterparts.* This Letter may be signed in counterparts and delivered by email in PDF format.

If this Letter reflects our mutual understanding, please sign and return a copy to the undersigned.
