# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** dated as of May 18, 2026 (this "**Agreement**"), between **SOUNOUR VETERINARY SYSTEMS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **KOLAIS ANIMAL HEALTH DIAGNOSTICS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of designing, manufacturing, marketing, selling, distributing and servicing the HemaQuik point-of-care companion-animal hematology analyzer line and the associated HemaQuik reagent cartridges (the "**Business**"). Seller is also engaged in the operation of veterinary reference laboratories (the "**Retained Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

"**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in the agreed form, to be delivered by Seller to Buyer at the Closing under Section 3.2(a).

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Cartridge Net Sales**" means the gross amounts invoiced by Buyer and its Affiliates for sales of HemaQuik reagent cartridges to third parties, less customary trade discounts, returns, allowances, rebates and freight charges actually given or incurred, determined in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer together with this Agreement.

"**Fennwick**" means Fennwick Veterinary Hospitals, Inc.

"**Fennwick Distribution Agreement**" means the exclusive distribution agreement between Seller and Fennwick relating to HemaQuik analyzers and cartridges, as listed on Schedule 2.1(c).

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights used or held for use primarily in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all inventory of HemaQuik analyzers, reagent cartridges, spare parts, raw materials, work in process and finished goods;

(b) all patents, trademarks (including the HEMAQUIK mark), copyrights, software, firmware, trade secrets, know-how and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business, as listed on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c), including the Fennwick Distribution Agreement and the customer, supply and service contracts of the Business (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all manufacturing equipment, tooling, molds and test fixtures used primarily in the Business;

(e) all regulatory files, product registrations, technical documentation, quality records and complaint files relating to the Business;

(f) all customer lists, pricing information, marketing materials and books and records relating primarily to the Business; and

(g) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include any assets of Seller other than those described in Section 2.1 (the "**Excluded Assets**"), including (a) all assets of the Retained Business, (b) cash and cash equivalents, (c) accounts receivable arising before the Closing, (d) Seller's organizational documents and Tax records, and (e) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all Liabilities arising out of the ownership or operation of the Business or the Purchased Assets after the Closing;

(b) all Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising before or after the Closing; and

(c) all Liabilities for Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period beginning after the Closing Date.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible for any Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Seller shall pay, perform and discharge all Excluded Liabilities when due. Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all Liabilities for product liability, warranty or recall arising from HemaQuik analyzers or cartridges manufactured or sold before the Closing, including all Liabilities relating to recalled HemaQuik analyzers;

(b) all Liabilities for Taxes of Seller, and all Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(c) all Liabilities relating to the Retained Business or the Excluded Assets;

(d) all Liabilities relating to employees or former employees of Seller, including compensation, benefits and severance, arising on or before the Closing Date; and

(e) all indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,600,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay (a) the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to Seller, and (b) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in writing by the payee at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,860,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held for 18 months after Closing under the Escrow Agreement as a source of recovery for claims by the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX. On the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the remaining Indemnity Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of any then-pending claims, which shall be retained until finally resolved.

**2.7 Earnout.**

(a) *Earnout Payments.* In addition to the Purchase Price, Buyer shall pay to Seller, for each of the calendar years ending December 31, 2027 and December 31, 2028 (each, an "**Earnout Period**"), an amount equal to 15% of the amount by which Cartridge Net Sales for that Earnout Period exceed $22,000,000 (for 2027) or $26,000,000 (for 2028) (each, an "**Earnout Payment**"). The aggregate Earnout Payments shall not exceed $6,000,000.

(b) *Earnout Statement.* Within 75 days after the end of each Earnout Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Cartridge Net Sales for that Earnout Period and its calculation of the Earnout Payment (an "**Earnout Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* If Seller disputes an Earnout Statement, Seller shall deliver written notice of the dispute within 45 days after receipt. If the parties cannot resolve the dispute within 30 days, they shall submit it to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. The fees of that firm shall be borne in inverse proportion to the relative success of each party.

(d) *Audit and Inspection Rights.* Throughout each Earnout Period and for 12 months thereafter, Seller and its representatives shall have the right, on ten Business Days' prior written notice and during normal business hours, to audit and inspect the books, records, invoices, customer-level sales data and pricing records of Buyer and its Affiliates relating to sales of HemaQuik reagent cartridges, to the extent reasonably necessary to verify Cartridge Net Sales and any Earnout Statement. Buyer shall cooperate with, and provide reasonable access to its personnel for, any such audit.

(e) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay each Earnout Payment within ten Business Days after the Earnout Statement becomes final.

**2.8 Allocation of Purchase Price.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and a schedule prepared by Buyer and delivered to Seller within 90 days after the Closing Date. The parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on June 30, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement covering the Assigned Contracts, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Intellectual Property Assignment, duly executed by Seller;

(d) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(f) an IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver (a) the payments described in Section 2.5, (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer, and (c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** Between the date of this Agreement and the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with the customers, suppliers and distributors of the Business. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Notice of Developments.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.4 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, design, manufacture, market or sell any point-of-care companion-animal hematology analyzer or reagent cartridge. Nothing in this Section 6.4 restricts the operation of the Retained Business as conducted on the date of this Agreement.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer may, in its discretion, offer employment effective as of the Closing Date to the employees of Seller identified on Schedule 6.5. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in connection with those offers.

**6.6 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and Buyer shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide to Buyer information technology, data migration and related support for the Business for up to nine months after the Closing Date.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the condition that no Governmental Authority shall have enacted, issued or entered any Law or order that is in effect and makes the transactions contemplated by this Agreement illegal or otherwise restrains or prohibits their consummation.

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate, dated the Closing Date and signed by an authorized officer of Seller, certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) no action or proceeding shall have been commenced against Buyer or Seller that would prevent the Closing; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, by written notice to Seller, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within 15 days after written notice;

(c) by Seller, by written notice to Buyer, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within 15 days after written notice; or

(d) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by July 31, 2026, unless the failure to close results from the terminating party's breach of this Agreement.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any party, except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and nothing in this Agreement relieves any party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.4 and 4.12 survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties survive the Closing in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document;

(b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document; or

(c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective managers, officers, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document, or any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement or any Ancillary Document; or

(b) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

(b) In the case of a claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of the claim at its expense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense with its own counsel at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (i) until the aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceed $486,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket, or (ii) for aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) in excess of 12% of the Purchase Price ($5,832,000) (the "**Cap**"). The Basket and the Cap do not apply to Losses arising from fraud or from any inaccuracy in or breach of the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.4 or 4.12.

(b) Seller indemnity for Excluded Liabilities is not subject to the cap. Seller's obligations under Sections 9.2(b) and 9.2(c) are not subject to the Basket or the Cap.

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of the claim, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Escrow Recovery.** Any amount payable by Seller to a Buyer Indemnified Party under this Article IX shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to the extent then available, and thereafter directly by Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, other than claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under Section 2.7.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic Taxes relating to the Purchased Assets for any period that begins before and ends after the Closing Date shall be apportioned between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date based on the number of days in that period.
