# BILL OF SALE

**AGREED FORM: FOR DELIVERY AT CLOSING**

This **BILL OF SALE** (this "**Bill of Sale**") is dated as of June 30, 2026. It is made and delivered by **SOUNOUR VETERINARY SYSTEMS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **KOLAIS ANIMAL HEALTH DIAGNOSTICS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## 1. Background and Definitions

1.1 Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of May 18, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets, and Buyer has agreed to purchase them.

1.2 This Bill of Sale is the Bill of Sale referred to in Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement. It is delivered at the Closing under that Section.

1.3 Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. These terms include "Business," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Excluded Assets," "Excluded Liabilities," "Assumed Liabilities," "Assigned Contracts," "Encumbrance" and "Purchased Assets."

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 For good and valuable consideration, Seller does hereby sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The consideration includes the payment of the Purchase Price of $48,600,000 under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, and Seller acknowledges its receipt and sufficiency. The Purchased Assets include all of the assets, properties and rights described in Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

2.2 Buyer is to have and to hold the Purchased Assets for its own use and benefit, from and after the Closing, forever.

2.3 The Assigned Contracts, including the Fennwick Distribution Agreement, are assigned to and assumed by Buyer under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement delivered at the Closing.

## 3. Excluded Assets

3.1 Seller is not selling, assigning, transferring, conveying or delivering to Buyer, and Buyer is not acquiring, any right, title or interest in, to or under the Excluded Assets. All Excluded Assets remain the property of Seller.

3.2 The Excluded Assets include all assets of the Retained Business.

## 4. Terms of the Purchase Agreement

4.1 This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement and is subject to its terms.

4.2 Seller's liability with respect to the Purchased Assets and the related obligations of Seller, including Excluded Liabilities, is subject to the limitations set forth in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.3 The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of Seller under the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale. They survive the delivery of this Bill of Sale to the extent provided in Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Further Assurances

At Buyer's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of sale, conveyance, transfer and assignment, and take such other actions, as Buyer may reasonably request. The purpose of these instruments and actions is to more effectively transfer, convey and assign to Buyer, and to confirm Buyer's title to, the Purchased Assets, consistent with Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Power of Attorney

6.1 Seller irrevocably constitutes and appoints Buyer, its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller but on behalf of and for the benefit of Buyer, to do the following:

(a) to demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) to institute and prosecute any proceeding that Buyer may deem proper in order to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets; and

(c) to defend or compromise any action, suit or proceeding in respect of any of the Purchased Assets.

6.2 The power granted in Section 6.1 is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable by Seller.

## 7. Successors and Assigns

This Bill of Sale is binding upon Seller and its successors and assigns, and inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

## 8. Governing Law

This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision.

## 9. Counterparts and Electronic Delivery

This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by email in PDF format. A signature so delivered is effective as an original.
