# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 31, 2026 by and among **Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Zoband Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Fiduciary Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller have entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), under which Buyer is purchasing from Seller a library of forty-one (41) independent feature films and related distribution rights, sublicenses and materials.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $2,760,000 of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, to be held and disbursed under this Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties and the Escrow Agent agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Closing Date**" means July 31, 2026.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $2,760,000.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" means the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, or an order or judgment for which the time to appeal has expired without an appeal having been filed, accompanied by a written certification of the delivering Party's counsel to that effect.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 10.1.

"**Losses**" means out-of-pocket damages actually paid or incurred by Buyer or its Affiliates, excluding attorneys' fees, diminution in value, lost profits, and consequential, incidental, special or punitive damages of any kind.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all Losses claimed in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been paid from the Escrow Fund or resolved under Section 4.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of, and has no duty to review, the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Fund shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party.

## 3. DEPOSIT AND INVESTMENT

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt to Buyer and Seller in writing promptly upon receipt.

**3.2 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the investment set out on **Schedule A**, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instructions. Absent instructions, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3.2, or for any failure to invest pending receipt of instructions.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund form part of the Escrow Fund and shall be disbursed with it in accordance with this Agreement.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

**4.1 Claim Notice.** At any time before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that Buyer or another indemnified person under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement has suffered or reasonably expects to suffer Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under the Purchase Agreement, specifying in reasonable detail the nature of the claim and the amount of Losses claimed (or, if not then determinable, a good-faith estimate of that amount).

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may, within thirty (30) days after its receipt of a Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"), deliver to Buyer and the Escrow Agent a written notice (an "**Objection Notice**") objecting to all or part of the claim, stating in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the amount in dispute.

**4.3 Disbursement on Claims.**

(a) If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice before the end of the Objection Period, the Escrow Agent shall, on the fifth (5th) Business Day after the end of the Objection Period, disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Fund the amount of Losses claimed in the Claim Notice.

(b) If the Escrow Agent receives a timely Objection Notice objecting to only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer the undisputed amount within five (5) Business Days after receipt of the Objection Notice, and shall continue to hold the amount in dispute (the "**Disputed Amount**").

(c) The Escrow Agent shall disburse any Disputed Amount only (i) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or (ii) in accordance with a Final Order, within five (5) Business Days after receipt of those instructions or that Final Order.

**4.4 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to pay any amount due under this Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the entire remaining Escrow Fund and shall have no further obligation in respect of the shortfall.

## 5. RELEASE OF THE ESCROW FUND

**5.1 Release on Escrow Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the balance of the Escrow Fund, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Escrow Release Date.

**5.2 Release of Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained under Section 5.1 until the related claims are resolved, and shall disburse that amount, in whole or in part, to Buyer or Seller in accordance with Section 4.3. Upon resolution of all pending claims, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any remaining balance of the Escrow Fund to Seller.

**5.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding Sections 4 and 5, the Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse all or any portion of the Escrow Fund in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**5.4 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the recipient in writing to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent may confirm any wire instructions by telephone call-back to a person designated on **Schedule B**, and may delay a disbursement until that confirmation is obtained.

## 6. DUTIES AND PROTECTION OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on any written notice, instruction, certificate or Final Order that it reasonably believes to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper person, and has no duty to investigate the validity or accuracy of any statement in it.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of its performance under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, any amount payable under this Section 6.4 shall be borne equally, unless the indemnified matter arises from the act or omission of one Party, in which case that Party shall bear it. This Section 6.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions regarding the Escrow Fund, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, or may interplead the Escrow Fund with a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.3 and thereupon be discharged from further duties with respect to the amount interpleaded.

**6.6 Advice of Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and is fully protected in acting in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**6.7 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not responsible for any delay or failure to perform resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemics, failures of communications or computer systems, or interruptions of the Federal Reserve wire system.

## 7. FEES AND EXPENSES

**7.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set out on **Schedule C** and reimbursed for its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement.

**7.2 Allocation.** All fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct any fee or expense from the Escrow Fund unless it remains unpaid thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent's written invoice to both Parties.

## 8. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**8.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice signed by both of them.

**8.3 Successor.** Within the notice period, Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which shall be a bank or trust company having combined capital and surplus of at least $250,000,000. If no successor has been appointed by the end of the notice period, the Escrow Agent may deliver the Escrow Fund into the registry of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.3 or petition that court to appoint a successor. The resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent takes effect only upon delivery of the Escrow Fund to the successor escrow agent or the court, and the Escrow Agent shall then be discharged from all further duties.

## 9. TAX MATTERS

**9.1 Tax Reporting.** Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund for income tax purposes until disbursement, and all interest and earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer. Each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date.

**9.2 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any taxes it is required by law to withhold, and shall remit those amounts to the appropriate taxing authority.

**9.3 Tax Distributions.** Upon written request of Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall distribute to Buyer, no later than April 1 of each year, an amount equal to thirty-five percent (35%) of the earnings on the Escrow Fund allocated to Buyer for the preceding calendar year.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices and instructions under this Agreement shall be in writing and are effective when delivered personally, one Business Day after dispatch by recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email in PDF format with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses or such other address as a recipient designates by notice:

If to Buyer: Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC, Attention: Chief Legal Officer, at the address set out on the signature page.

If to Seller: Zoband Releasing, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address set out on the signature page.

If to the Escrow Agent: Ostrander Fiduciary Trust Company, N.A., Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, at the address set out on the signature page.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

**10.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.3 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each of the Parties and the Escrow Agent irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, City of New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Fund, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**10.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF THE PARTIES AND THE ESCROW AGENT IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.5 Assignment.** No Party may assign this Agreement or any rights under it without the prior written consent of the other Party and the Escrow Agent, except that any assignment of rights by a Party under the Purchase Agreement shall be effective against the Escrow Agent only upon written notice to the Escrow Agent signed by both Buyer and Seller. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which succeeds to all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall succeed to the Escrow Agent's rights and duties under this Agreement without further act.

**10.6 Patriot Act Compliance.** The Parties acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is required under applicable law to obtain, verify and record information identifying each person that opens an account, and each Party shall provide that information upon request.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**10.8 Termination.** This Agreement terminates upon the disbursement of the entire Escrow Fund in accordance with its terms, except that Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 7 survive.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
