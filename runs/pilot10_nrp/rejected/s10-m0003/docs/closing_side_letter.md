Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter**") is delivered at the Closing under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between Zoband Releasing, Inc. ("**Seller**") and Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC ("**Buyer**"). It is the Side Letter referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Disputed Titles

1.1 **Identification.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that Buyer's review of the chain-of-title files under Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement has identified unresolved questions about Seller's rights in the following three Library Titles (each a "**Disputed Title**" and together the "**Disputed Titles**"):

(a) *The Hollow Orchard*: a competing claim by the estate of the author of the underlying novel that the option and literary purchase agreement lapsed before principal photography;

(b) *Northbound Freight*: an incomplete chain of assignment from the original production company to Seller, with no executed assignment on file for the period between the production company's dissolution and Seller's acquisition; and

(c) *Salt Lake Lullaby*: missing synchronization and master use licenses for four compositions shown on the music cue sheet, and an asserted claim by one music publisher.

1.2 **Schedule 4.7.** Seller confirms that each matter in Section 1.1 is described on Schedule 4.7 to the Purchase Agreement.

1.3 **Closing.** The Parties have agreed to proceed with the Closing on July 31, 2026 with the Disputed Titles included in the Purchased Assets, on the terms of this Letter.

## 2. Title Exclusion

2.1 **Exclusion Right.** Buyer retains the right to exclude any or all of the Disputed Titles from the Purchased Assets by delivering a Title Exclusion Notice to Seller within the period specified in Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement. Each Title Exclusion Notice shall identify the Disputed Title or Titles to be excluded and the defect described in Section 1.1 for each.

2.2 **Effect of Exclusion.** Upon delivery of a Title Exclusion Notice under Section 2.1:

(a) each Disputed Title named in that notice shall cease to be a Library Title and shall be an excluded asset under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the Purchase Price shall be reduced by the amount allocated to each excluded Disputed Title on Schedule 2.6 to the Purchase Agreement, and Seller shall pay that amount to Buyer by wire transfer of immediately available funds within ten (10) Business Days after delivery of the Title Exclusion Notice;

(c) Buyer shall reconvey to Seller, by quitclaim and without representation or warranty, all of its right, title and interest in each excluded Disputed Title and the related Library Materials, and shall assign to Seller any Assumed Sublicense relating solely to that title; and

(d) any receipts collected by Buyer from the exploitation of an excluded Disputed Title after the Closing Date, less distribution expenses actually incurred, shall be remitted to Seller within thirty (30) days after the reconveyance, and those receipts shall be excluded from Net Receipts under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

2.3 **Allocated Amounts.** For reference, the amounts allocated to the Disputed Titles on Schedule 2.6 to the Purchase Agreement are as set out on **Annex A** to this Letter.

## 3. Cooperation on Disputed Titles

3.1 **Seller Efforts.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts, at its own cost, to resolve each defect described in Section 1.1, including by:

(a) seeking a written acknowledgment or quitclaim from the estate claimant for *The Hollow Orchard*;

(b) obtaining confirmatory assignments from the former principals of the original production company for *Northbound Freight*, in a form suitable for recordation with the United States Copyright Office; and

(c) obtaining the missing synchronization and master use licenses, or settling the asserted publisher claim, for *Salt Lake Lullaby*.

3.2 **Information.** Seller shall promptly give Buyer copies of all correspondence with claimants and rights holders relating to the Disputed Titles, and shall not settle or compromise any such claim in a manner that restricts Buyer's exploitation of a Disputed Title without Buyer's prior written consent.

3.3 **Further Assurances.** Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement applies to all documents obtained under this Section 3.

## 4. Reservation of Rights

Buyer's agreement to close with the Disputed Titles included in the Purchased Assets does not waive or limit any right of Buyer under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The disclosures on Schedule 4.7 have the effect given to them by the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. General

5.1 **Purchase Agreement.** Except as expressly set out in this Letter, the Purchase Agreement remains unmodified and in full force and effect.

5.2 **Amendment.** This Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller.

5.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Letter, including any Title Exclusion Notice, shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.4 **Governing Law; Forum.** This Letter is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Sections 11.8 and 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Letter as if set out in full.

5.5 **Counterparts.** This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
