# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 14, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Lipaind**"), and **Zoband Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Zoband**"). Lipaind and Zoband are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Lipaind and Zoband are each engaged in the business of acquiring, licensing and distributing independent feature films.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Lipaind would acquire from Zoband certain feature films in Zoband's library, together with related distribution rights, sublicenses and materials (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

C. In connection with the Proposed Transaction, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. The Parties wish to protect that information on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"**Affiliate**" means, for any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party or its Representatives on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Proposed Transaction, including: (a) chain-of-title documents, option and literary purchase agreements, copyright registrations and assignments, music cue sheets, synchronization and master use licenses and other clearance files; (b) distribution, exhibition, broadcast, streaming and other sublicense agreements and their financial terms; (c) title-level revenue, expense, residual and participation records; (d) exhibitor, platform and customer lists and deal terms; (e) business plans, forecasts and valuations; and (f) all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing. Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Parties are discussing the Proposed Transaction and the status and terms of those discussions.

"**Disclosing Party**" means a Party that discloses Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means a Party that receives Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Representatives**" means, for a Party, its Affiliates and its and their respective directors, officers, managers, members, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors and actual or prospective financing sources, in each case who need to know the Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating or negotiating the Proposed Transaction.

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is or becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, provided that source is not known by the Receiving Party, after reasonable inquiry, to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

**2.1 Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating and, if applicable, consummating the Proposed Transaction, and for no other purpose.

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any Person other than its Representatives. Before disclosing Confidential Information to a Representative, the Receiving Party shall inform that Representative of its confidential nature and direct that Representative to treat it in accordance with this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**2.3 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.4 Competitively Sensitive Information.** Each Party may designate Confidential Information relating to current exhibitor, platform and sublicense deal terms or title-level pricing as "Outside Counsel Only" or "Clean Team Only." Information so designated may be disclosed only to the Receiving Party's outside legal counsel and to those other Representatives that the Parties identify in writing as members of a clean team, and shall not be disclosed to personnel of the Receiving Party with responsibility for pricing or licensing negotiations.

**2.5 Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information, and shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to regain possession of the Confidential Information and prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

## 3. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in seeking that remedy. If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to it.

## 4. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party at any time, the Receiving Party shall, within ten (10) business days, return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall confirm that destruction in writing signed by an officer of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies required to be retained by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policies, and (b) copies stored in automatic electronic archival or backup systems that are not readily accessible, in each case provided that the retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained.

## 5. NO SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

For a period of twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any officer or employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact in connection with the Proposed Transaction. This Section 5 does not prohibit general solicitations of employment not directed specifically at those individuals, or the hiring of any individual who responds to such a general solicitation or who has ceased to be employed by the other Party for at least six (6) months.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO LICENSE; NO OBLIGATION

**6.1 No Representations.** Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information it discloses. Neither Party shall have any liability to the other Party or its Representatives arising from the use of Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a definitive written agreement regarding the Proposed Transaction, if and when executed.

**6.2 No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in or to any Confidential Information, or any copyright, trademark or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party, including any right in any motion picture or related materials.

**6.3 No Obligation to Transact.** Neither Party is under any legal obligation of any kind with respect to the Proposed Transaction by virtue of this Agreement, except for the matters specifically agreed in this Agreement. No agreement providing for the Proposed Transaction shall be deemed to exist unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered by both Parties. Either Party may terminate discussions regarding the Proposed Transaction at any time and for any reason, by written notice to the other Party.

## 7. TERM

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for a period of two (2) years after the Effective Date. The obligations of the Receiving Party under Sections 2, 3 and 4 with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during that period survive until the date that is two (2) years after the expiry of that period, except that the obligations with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law survive for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the requirement to post a bond or other security, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. GENERAL

**9.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it, and no waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**9.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 9.3 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**9.5 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the federal or other state courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in that court.

**9.6 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
