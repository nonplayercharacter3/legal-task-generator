# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 2, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**") by and between **Zoband Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of acquiring, licensing and distributing independent feature films, and owns or controls distribution rights in the forty-one (41) feature-length motion pictures listed on **Schedule 1.1(a)** (the "**Library Titles**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, the Library Titles and related assets on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"**Affiliate**" means, for any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Assumed Sublicenses**" means the distribution, exhibition, broadcast, streaming and other sublicense agreements relating to the Library Titles listed on **Schedule 1.1(b)**.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required to close.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Ostrander Fiduciary Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent dated as of the Closing Date.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.4, together with any earnings on it.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3.

"**Library Materials**" means all negatives, interpositives, digital cinema packages, master files, trailers, key art, music cue sheets, publicity materials and other physical and digital elements of the Library Titles in Seller's possession or control.

"**Losses**" means any and all losses, liabilities, damages, judgments, settlements, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and diminution in value of Library Titles.

"**Net Receipts**" has the meaning given in Section 2.5(a).

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Side Letter**" means any letter agreement between Buyer and Seller delivered at the Closing relating to this Agreement.

"**Title Exclusion Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 6.4.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections, Articles and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 6.4, plus the Earnout Payments, if any, payable under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than those listed on **Schedule 2.2**, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to:

(a) the Library Titles, including all copyrights, distribution rights, ancillary rights and remake, sequel and derivative rights owned or controlled by Seller in them;

(b) the Assumed Sublicenses and all receivables accruing under them after the Closing Date;

(c) the Library Materials;

(d) all chain-of-title documents, clearance files, music licenses, guild and residual records and laboratory access letters relating to the Library Titles; and

(e) all claims and causes of action of Seller against third parties to the extent relating to the Library Titles

(together, the "**Purchased Assets**").

**2.3 Excluded Assets and Liabilities.** Buyer does not acquire any asset of Seller not listed in Section 2.2. Buyer assumes only obligations under the Assumed Sublicenses arising after the Closing Date. All other liabilities of Seller, including guild residuals and participations accrued for periods before the Closing Date, remain with Seller.

**2.4 Payment at Closing; Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall:

(a) deposit $2,760,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) pay to Seller the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

The balance of the Escrow Fund, less the amount of any pending claims, shall be released to Seller on the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Release Date**").

**2.5 Earnout.**

(a) For each of the two consecutive twelve-month periods beginning on the Closing Date (each, an "**Earnout Period**"), Buyer shall pay Seller fifteen percent (15%) of the gross receipts actually received by Buyer from the exploitation of the Library Titles, less distribution expenses actually incurred and third-party participations paid ("**Net Receipts**"), in excess of $2,500,000 for that Earnout Period (each, an "**Earnout Payment**").

(b) Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Earnout Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of Net Receipts for that period, together with any Earnout Payment due.

(c) For a period of one hundred twenty (120) days after delivery of each statement, Seller and its representatives may, on ten (10) Business Days' notice and during normal business hours, inspect and audit Buyer's books and records relating to the Library Titles, including Buyer's exhibitor, platform and sublicense deal terms and title-level revenue and expense records, to verify the statement. If an audit shows an underpayment of more than five percent (5%), Buyer shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Library Titles as set out in **Schedule 2.6**, and the Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on July 31, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale and an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assumed Sublicenses, each duly executed by Seller;

(b) short-form copyright assignments for each Library Title in a form suitable for recordation with the United States Copyright Office;

(c) laboratory access letters for all Library Materials held by third parties;

(d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; and

(e) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the matters in Sections 7.1(a) and 7.1(b).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments under Section 2.4, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and counterparts of the documents in Section 3.2(a) executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Signing Date and as of the Closing Date that:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. This Agreement has been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action and constitutes the valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Title to Tangible Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to the Library Materials, free and clear of all liens other than those listed on Schedule 2.2.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents, any law applicable to Seller, or any material contract to which Seller is a party.

**4.5 Assumed Sublicenses.** Schedule 1.1(b) lists each Assumed Sublicense. Each Assumed Sublicense is in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.6 Litigation.** Except as set out in **Schedule 4.6**, there is no action, claim or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Library Titles.

**4.7 Chain of Title; Clearances.** Except as set out in **Schedule 4.7**:

(a) Seller owns or controls, by valid and continuous chain of title, the rights in each Library Title described for that title on Schedule 1.1(a);

(b) all literary, underlying, music, performer and other rights necessary to exploit each Library Title in the media and territories described on Schedule 1.1(a) have been obtained and are in full force; and

(c) the exploitation of the Library Titles as described on Schedule 1.1(a) does not infringe the copyright, trademark, right of publicity or other rights of any Person.

**4.8 Residuals and Participations.** Schedule 4.8 lists all guild residual and third-party participation obligations relating to the Library Titles, and all amounts due under them for periods before the Closing Date have been paid.

**4.9 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee from Buyer in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller that:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement, which constitutes the valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the amounts due under Section 2.4.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct Before Closing.** Between the Signing Date and the Closing, Seller shall operate its business relating to the Library Titles in the ordinary course and shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, grant any new license of, or amend or terminate any Assumed Sublicense relating to, any Library Title.

**6.2 Access.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the Library Materials, chain-of-title files and Assumed Sublicenses.

**6.3 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, Seller shall execute such further assignments and instruments, and take such further action, as Buyer reasonably requests to vest in Buyer the Purchased Assets, including the recordation of copyright assignments.

**6.4 Title Exclusion.** If Buyer's review of the chain-of-title files identifies a defect in Seller's rights in any Library Title, Buyer may, by written notice to Seller identifying the affected title and the defect (a "**Title Exclusion Notice**") delivered no later than 30 days after the Signing Date, elect to exclude that Library Title from the Purchased Assets. Upon a timely Title Exclusion Notice, the affected title shall cease to be a Library Title and the Purchase Price shall be reduced by the amount allocated to that title on Schedule 2.6.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information relating to the Library Titles and the Assumed Sublicenses, and all information obtained under Section 2.5(c), except as required by law.

**6.6 Non-Solicitation of Licensees.** For two (2) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not induce any counterparty to an Assumed Sublicense to terminate or adversely modify it.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to:

(a) the representations of Seller being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing; and

(c) receipt of the deliveries in Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the representations of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date and receipt of the deliveries in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written agreement of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by August 31, 2026, unless the failure results from that Party's breach; or (c) by either Party upon the other Party's material breach that remains uncured fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be of no further effect, except that Section 6.5, this Section 8.2 and Article XI survive, and no Party is relieved of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of the Parties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months following the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations, being the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3, shall survive until the date that is six (6) years following the Closing Date. Covenants survive until fully performed. No claim for breach of a representation or warranty may be made after the expiry of its survival period, except a claim for which written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail was given before that expiry.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, managers, members and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of a representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of a covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any liability of Seller not assumed by Buyer under Section 2.3.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of any breach of a representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, or any obligation under the Assumed Sublicenses arising after the Closing Date.

**9.4 Limitations.** Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breach of the Fundamental Representations, (a) until aggregate Losses exceed $184,000, after which Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of that amount, or (b) for aggregate Losses in excess of the Escrow Amount. Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**9.5 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) unless the settlement involves only payment of money by the indemnifying party and a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to Buyer under Section 9.2(a), other than for breach of the Fundamental Representations, shall be satisfied solely from the Escrow Fund, which shall be Buyer's sole source of recovery for those claims. Losses payable to Buyer for breach of the Fundamental Representations or under Sections 9.2(b) and 9.2(c) shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund, to the extent available, and then directly by Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud and for specific performance under Section 11.9, this Article IX is the exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of this Agreement after the Closing.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** Any sales, use, transfer or similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller is responsible for all taxes relating to the Purchased Assets for periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided, each Party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement. Escrow Agent fees shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are effective when delivered personally, one Business Day after dispatch by recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses below or such other address as a Party designates by notice:

If to Seller: Zoband Releasing, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address set out on the signature page.

If to Buyer: Lipaind Pictures Group, LLC, Attention: Chief Legal Officer, at the address set out on the signature page.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**11.5 Assignment.** Seller may assign this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Buyer. Buyer may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Seller. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any right or remedy on any Person other than the Parties.

**11.7 Public Announcements.** No Party shall make any public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law.

**11.8 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the federal or other state courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in that court.

**11.9 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable harm would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**11.12 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among the transaction documents, the Side Letter shall prevail, then this Agreement, then the Escrow Agreement; provided that the Escrow Agreement shall control with respect to the disbursement of the Escrow Fund.
