# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Keisaiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Client**"), and **Kedoul Specialty Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." This DPA supplements the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Agreement**").

**RECITALS**

A. Under the Agreement, Provider performs third-party logistics, specialty distribution and Hub Services for Velnorix. In doing so, Provider receives, creates and processes Personal Data, including protected health information of patients enrolled in Client's patient support program.

B. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Provider processes Personal Data. Where HIPAA requires it, these terms also serve as a business associate agreement under 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e) and 164.504(e).

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Hub Services," "Personal Data," "Services," "Statement of Work" and "Velnorix."

1.2 "**Data Protection Laws**" means all Applicable Law relating to the privacy, security or processing of Personal Data. This includes HIPAA, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act and their implementing regulations, the Federal Trade Commission Act, and state laws on health data privacy, consumer privacy, data security and breach notification.

1.3 "**HIPAA**" has the meaning given in Section 1.2 of the Agreement.

1.4 "**PHI**" means protected health information as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information that Provider creates, receives, maintains or transmits on behalf of Client or Client's patient support program.

1.5 "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, alignment, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

1.6 "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, Personal Data. It also means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized interference with the systems used to Process Personal Data. It includes any "breach of unsecured protected health information" as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

1.7 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Provider to Process Personal Data on Provider's behalf.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Scope.** This DPA applies to all Personal Data Processed by Provider, its Affiliates or its Subprocessors in connection with the Services. This includes patient intake, benefits verification, prior authorization support, co-pay program administration, case management and coordination of shipments to patients and sites of care.

2.2 **Roles.** For the Personal Data, Client is the party that determines the purposes and means of Processing. Provider Processes Personal Data solely on Client's behalf. To the extent Client or Client's patient support program acts as or on behalf of a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA, Provider acts as Client's business associate or subcontractor business associate.

2.3 **Details of Processing.** The categories of data subjects, categories of Personal Data, and nature and purpose of the Processing are described in Annex A.

## 3. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Provider shall Process Personal Data only:
(a) to perform the Services;
(b) in accordance with the Agreement, this DPA and Client's other documented instructions; and
(c) as required by Data Protection Laws.

Provider shall promptly notify Client if, in Provider's reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Provider shall not:
(a) sell, rent or license Personal Data;
(b) share Personal Data for cross-context behavioral advertising;
(c) use Personal Data for Provider's own commercial purposes;
(d) combine Personal Data with data from other sources, except as necessary to perform the Services; or
(e) de-identify Personal Data for any purpose not authorized in writing by Client.

3.3 **Minimum Necessary.** Provider shall limit its use and disclosure of PHI, and its requests for PHI, to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b).

3.4 **Confidentiality of Personnel.** Provider shall ensure that every person authorized to Process Personal Data:
(a) is bound by written confidentiality obligations;
(b) receives privacy and security training at least annually; and
(c) has access only to the Personal Data required for that person's role.

3.5 **Permitted Disclosures.** Provider may disclose PHI only:
(a) to Client;
(b) to patients, health care providers, pharmacies, payers and other persons as required to perform the Hub Services under Client's approved program rules; or
(c) as required by law, subject to Section 10.3 of the Agreement.

## 4. SECURITY

4.1 **Safeguards.** Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards that:
(a) comply with the HIPAA Security Rule, 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C; and
(b) are appropriate to the risk presented by the Processing.

The minimum required measures are described in Annex B.

4.2 **Encryption.** Provider shall encrypt Personal Data in transit and at rest. Encryption shall use industry-standard algorithms consistent with guidance issued by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services and the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

4.3 **Risk Assessment.** Provider shall perform a documented security risk analysis at least annually and after any material change to the systems used to Process Personal Data. Provider shall remediate identified risks in a timely manner.

4.4 **Certification.** Provider shall maintain a SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent independent assessment, covering the systems used to Process Personal Data. Provider shall deliver the current report to Client on request.

## 5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

5.1 **Notification.** Provider shall notify Client in writing of any Security Incident without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after Provider discovers it. The notice shall include, to the extent then known:
(a) the nature of the Security Incident;
(b) the categories and approximate number of individuals and records affected;
(c) the identity of each affected individual;
(d) the likely consequences; and
(e) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it.

Provider shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

5.2 **Investigation and Remediation.** Provider shall promptly:
(a) investigate the Security Incident;
(b) take all reasonable steps to contain, mitigate and remediate it; and
(c) preserve all relevant logs and evidence.

Provider shall cooperate with Client and any forensic examiner Client engages.

5.3 **Notifications to Third Parties.** Client shall determine whether and how to notify affected individuals, regulators, the media or other third parties. Provider shall not make any such notification without Client's prior written approval, except where Data Protection Laws require it.

5.4 **Costs.** Provider shall reimburse Client's reasonable costs for any Security Incident arising from a breach of this DPA by Provider or its Subprocessors. These costs include:
(a) forensic investigation;
(b) notification to individuals and regulators;
(c) call center support;
(d) credit or identity monitoring for affected individuals for at least twenty-four (24) months; and
(e) any other remediation required by Data Protection Laws.

5.5 **Unsuccessful Attempts.** This Section 5 is satisfied as to unsuccessful attempts if Provider reports them in aggregate on Client's request. Unsuccessful attempts include pings, port scans and blocked log-in attempts that do not result in unauthorized access to Personal Data.

## 6. SUBPROCESSORS

6.1 **Consent.** Consistent with Section 7.2 of the Agreement, Provider shall not engage any Subprocessor without Client's prior written consent. Annex C lists the Subprocessors approved as of the Effective Date.

6.2 **Subprocessor Terms.** Provider shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor before the Subprocessor Processes any Personal Data. That agreement shall impose data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA. Where PHI is involved, it shall include a subcontractor business associate agreement that complies with 45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e).

6.3 **Responsibility.** Provider remains fully responsible to Client for the acts and omissions of each Subprocessor.

## 7. INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS AND ASSISTANCE

7.1 **Individual Requests.** Provider shall forward to Client, within five (5) Business Days of receipt, any request from an individual to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws. These include rights of access, amendment, accounting of disclosures, deletion or opt-out. Provider shall not respond to the request except as Client directs.

7.2 **HIPAA Rights.** Provider shall:
(a) make PHI available for access and amendment as required by 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.524 and 164.526; and
(b) document disclosures and provide the information required for an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.

In each case, Provider shall act within the time reasonably required for Client to meet its obligations.

7.3 **Assistance.** Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to Client with:
(a) privacy impact assessments;
(b) responses to regulatory inquiries; and
(c) compliance with Data Protection Laws relating to the Services.

7.4 **Books and Records.** Provider shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. This obligation applies for purposes of determining compliance with HIPAA.

## 8. DATA LOCATION AND TRANSFERS

8.1 **Location.** Provider shall store and Process Personal Data only within the United States. Provider shall not permit remote access to Personal Data from outside the United States without Client's prior written consent.

## 9. AUDIT

9.1 **Audit Rights.** Client may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA, either itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations. Audits may take place once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' written notice. Client may also audit at any time after a Security Incident or on a regulator's request.

9.2 **Remediation.** Provider shall promptly remediate any non-compliance identified in an audit. Provider shall report the remediation to Client in writing.

## 10. RETURN AND DELETION

10.1 **On Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of the Agreement, and after completion of any transition assistance under Section 12.6 of the Agreement, Provider shall, at Client's election:
(a) return all Personal Data to Client or Client's designee in a mutually agreed format; or
(b) securely destroy all Personal Data.

Provider shall certify the return or destruction in writing within thirty (30) days.

10.2 **Retention Required by Law.** If Applicable Law requires Provider to retain any Personal Data, the following applies:
(a) Provider shall extend the protections of this DPA to that Personal Data;
(b) Provider shall limit further use and disclosure to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible; and
(c) Provider shall destroy the Personal Data when the retention requirement ends.

## 11. TERM; LIABILITY

11.1 **Term.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Provider or any Subprocessor Processes Personal Data in connection with the Agreement.

11.2 **Termination for Breach.** Client may terminate the Agreement under Section 12.3 of the Agreement if Provider materially breaches this DPA and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after notice. If Client determines that cure is not possible, Client may terminate immediately on written notice.

11.3 **Liability and Indemnity.** Provider's obligations under Section 13.1 of the Agreement apply to claims arising out of this DPA. Each Party's liability under this DPA is subject to Section 14 of the Agreement.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This DPA is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The same law governs any dispute arising out of or relating to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement or this DPA.

12.2 Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this DPA or the Processing of Personal Data. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Amendment.** The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment to this DPA required to comply with changes in Data Protection Laws. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.2 **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this DPA shall be resolved to permit compliance with Data Protection Laws.

13.3 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 16.3 of the Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents shall also be sent by email to Client's privacy office at the address Client designates.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

### ANNEX A – DETAILS OF PROCESSING

**Data subjects:** The following persons, as they relate to Velnorix:
- patients and prospective patients enrolled in, or referred to, Client's patient support program;
- caregivers and authorized representatives;
- prescribing health care providers and their office staff;
- pharmacy and site-of-care personnel.

**Categories of Personal Data:**
- Patient identification data: names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses and dates of birth.
- Insurance and payer information, including member identifiers.
- Prescription, diagnosis and treatment information.
- Benefits verification and prior authorization records.
- Co-pay program enrollment and financial eligibility data.
- Shipment and delivery records.
- Case management notes.
- Adverse event and product complaint information.
- Business contact information of health care provider personnel.

**Nature and purpose:** Performance of the Hub Services and related distribution Services under the Agreement and the applicable Statement of Work. This includes patient intake, benefits verification, prior authorization support, co-pay administration, case management, coordination and tracking of Velnorix shipments, and adverse event and complaint reporting to Client.

**Duration:** The Term of the Agreement, any period of transition assistance under Section 12.6 of the Agreement, and any period of retention under Section 10.2 of this DPA.

### ANNEX B – MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

1. Role-based access controls, unique user IDs and multi-factor authentication for all access to systems containing Personal Data.
2. Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent).
3. Logging and monitoring of access to Personal Data, with logs retained for at least six (6) years.
4. Vulnerability scanning at least monthly, and independent penetration testing at least annually.
5. Endpoint protection, patch management and secure configuration standards.
6. Documented incident response, business continuity and disaster recovery plans, tested at least annually.
7. Background checks for personnel with access to Personal Data, to the extent permitted by law.
8. Secure disposal of media and paper records containing Personal Data.
