## 2. Purpose and Opening Remarks

2.1 The meeting was the operational kickoff following execution of the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 (the "MSA"), the Data Processing Addendum, the GDP Quality Agreement and the Service Level Schedule, each dated as of the same date, and the initial Statement of Work.

2.2 Ms. Okonjo-Hale summarized Keisaiv's launch plan for Velnorix injection in the United States and confirmed the target first commercial shipment date of April 15, 2024. Mr. Vessant confirmed Kedoul's readiness, subject to completion of the open items in Section 8.

2.3 The parties confirmed that Ms. Venkataraman and Mr. Delahaye are the relationship managers for purposes of MSA Section 7.1 and will chair the quarterly reviews under MSA Section 3.3.

## 3. Facility Readiness and Quality

3.1 Ms. Oyelaran reported that installation and operational qualification of the dedicated 2°C to 8°C cold room at the Memphis Facility is complete. Performance qualification, including the summer and winter temperature mapping required by Quality Agreement Section 4.1, is complete for the winter profile. The summer mapping report will be issued after the scheduled summer study. Ms. Lunde-Achebe asked that the winter mapping and IQ/OQ reports be sent to Keisaiv Quality for review before first receipt of inventory. Kedoul agreed.

3.2 Ms. Mallory confirmed that the backup generator was load-tested on March 6, 2024 and that the contingency storage location in Olive Branch, Mississippi has been qualified, consistent with Quality Agreement Section 4.5. The business continuity plan will be provided to Keisaiv Quality.

3.3 Alarm escalation: the 24-hour alarm response contact for Annex 1 of the Quality Agreement will be confirmed in writing by Kedoul. Ms. Lunde-Achebe asked that Keisaiv Quality's on-call number be added to the alarm call tree for notification purposes. Kedoul agreed.

3.4 The parties walked through the excursion and quarantine workflow under Quality Agreement Section 5.5, including the data package (temperature data, duration, minimum and maximum temperatures) that Kedoul will send to Keisaiv Quality with each notification. Keisaiv will provide a disposition decision request template.

3.5 Ms. Mallory confirmed that Kedoul's mock recall procedure is in place. The parties agreed to schedule the first annual mock recall under Quality Agreement Section 7.2 for Q4 2024.

## 4. Inbound Receipt and Inventory

4.1 First inbound shipment from Keisaiv's contract manufacturer is planned for the week of April 1, 2024. Keisaiv will send the advance shipping notice at least three (3) Business Days before arrival.

4.2 Received units will remain in Quarantine until receipt verification is complete and Keisaiv has released the Lot, per Quality Agreement Section 5.2. Keisaiv Quality will send Lot release notices to Kedoul Quality by email, with a copy to the relationship managers.

4.3 Minimum remaining shelf life at shipment will be as stated in the initial Statement of Work. Kedoul confirmed its warehouse management system is configured for first-expired, first-out allocation.

## 5. Order-to-Cash and Shipping

5.1 Mr. Delahaye confirmed the Order Cut-Off Time of 3:00 p.m. Central Time and the order fulfillment service level of 98% of Conforming Orders shipped on the same Business Day under Service Level Schedule Section 3.1.

5.2 Shipping configurations: Keisaiv has approved the summer and winter shipper configurations for the U.S. Lanes. Kedoul will ship only in those configurations, each with the data logger specified by Keisaiv (Quality Agreement Sections 6.1 and 6.3). Ms. Oyelaran noted that Hawaii and Alaska lanes will use the extended-duration configuration specified in the Statement of Work.

5.3 Consignee license verification will run as an automated check at order entry, with manual review of exceptions, per Quality Agreement Section 6.4.

5.4 Keisaiv's first rolling twelve-month forecast under MSA Section 5.1 was delivered on March 8, 2024. Updates will be provided by the fifth Business Day of each month.

## 6. Hub Services

6.1 Mr. Ebersole reviewed the patient support program rules, eligibility criteria and approved patient communications. Final versions will be delivered to Kedoul by March 22, 2024, as required by MSA Section 5.3. Mr. Whitcombe confirmed that hub staff will not use any communication that Keisaiv has not approved.

6.2 Hub operating hours will be as set out in the initial Statement of Work. Kedoul is completing hiring for the case manager team. Training on Velnorix program rules and adverse event recognition is scheduled for the week of April 1, 2024.

6.3 Adverse events, Product Complaints and medical information requests will be forwarded to Keisaiv's pharmacovigilance mailbox within one (1) Business Day of receipt (MSA Section 6.4; Service Level Schedule Section 3.9). Keisaiv will run a test case before go-live.

## 7. Data Protection and Systems

7.1 Ms. Marchetti confirmed that the approved Subprocessors listed in Annex C of the Data Processing Addendum are as identified in the initial Statement of Work. Kedoul confirmed that all hub case management data is hosted and accessed only within the United States, per DPA Section 8.1.

7.2 Kedoul will deliver its current SOC 2 Type II report under DPA Section 4.4 by March 29, 2024.

7.3 Ms. Marchetti requested the email address for Kedoul's security incident contact and provided the Keisaiv privacy office address for notices under DPA Section 13.3. Kedoul will confirm its contact in writing.

7.4 The parties agreed on the encrypted SFTP channel for the daily patient status file and shipment data feed. User acceptance testing is scheduled for April 3–5, 2024.

## 8. Reporting and Governance

8.1 Kedoul will deliver the first monthly performance report for April 2024 within ten (10) Business Days after month-end, in the format required by Service Level Schedule Section 4.2. Kedoul will circulate a draft template for Keisaiv's comment by April 5, 2024.

8.2 First quarterly business review is tentatively set for July 2024.

8.3 Invoices will be issued monthly in arrears under MSA Section 4.2 and sent to Keisaiv's accounts payable mailbox, with a copy to Ms. Venkataraman.
