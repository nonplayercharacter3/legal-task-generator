# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Keisaiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Halvorsen Parkway, Suite 900, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Client**"), and **Kedoul Specialty Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Corridor Way, Building C, Memphis, Tennessee 38118 ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Client is a specialty pharmaceutical company that commercializes Velnorix injection, a refrigerated biologic product, in the United States.

B. Provider operates temperature-controlled warehousing and distribution facilities and provides third-party logistics, specialty distribution and patient support hub services to pharmaceutical manufacturers.

C. Client wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to perform such services on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings set out below or elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of, or the power to direct the management of, such entity.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, guidance and orders applicable to a Party or the Services, including the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Drug Supply Chain Security Act, state wholesale distribution licensing laws, the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations ("**HIPAA**"), and federal and state anti-kickback and privacy laws.

1.3 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which national banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

1.4 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

1.5 "**Covered Products**" means any pharmaceutical product commercialized by Client or its Affiliates, now or in the future, including Velnorix.

1.6 "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date governing Provider's processing of Personal Data in connection with the Services, as amended from time to time.

1.7 "**Fees**" means the fees for the Services set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.8 "**Hub Services**" means the patient support hub services described in a Statement of Work, which may include benefits verification, prior authorization support, co-pay program administration, patient intake and case management, and coordination of shipments to patients and sites of care.

1.9 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including protected health information as defined under HIPAA, that Provider receives, creates or processes in connection with the Services.

1.10 "**Quality Agreement**" means the GDP Quality Agreement between the Parties governing the storage, handling and distribution of Velnorix, as amended from time to time.

1.11 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule attached to or executed under this Agreement setting out the service levels, measurement methods and service credits applicable to the Services.

1.12 "**Services**" means the third-party logistics, warehousing, order-to-cash, specialty distribution and Hub Services described in this Agreement and in each Statement of Work, together with any related services reasonably necessary for their performance.

1.13 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes Services to be performed and the applicable Fees.

1.14 "**Temperature Excursion**" means any deviation, of any duration, outside the 2°C to 8°C labeled storage range.

1.15 "**Territory**" means throughout the world.

1.16 "**Velnorix**" means Client's Velnorix (injection) product, in all strengths and presentations, as approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND SERVICES

2.1 **Services.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work in accordance with this Agreement, the Quality Agreement, the DPA, the Service Level Schedule and Applicable Law. The initial Statement of Work, executed concurrently with this Agreement, covers warehousing, distribution and Hub Services for Velnorix.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work is governed by and incorporated into this Agreement. Either Party may propose additional Statements of Work, and no Statement of Work is binding unless signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. A Statement of Work may be amended only by a written change order signed by both Parties.

2.3 **Related Agreements.** Concurrently with this Agreement, the Parties have entered into the Quality Agreement and the DPA. The Service Level Schedule forms part of this Agreement.

2.4 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Client appoints Provider as Client's exclusive provider of third-party logistics, specialty distribution and patient support hub services for the Covered Products in the Territory, and Client shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, engage any third party to perform services that are the same as or substantially similar to the Services for any Covered Product in the Territory. Client shall cause its Affiliates to comply with this Section 2.4 as if they were parties to this Agreement.

2.5 **Facilities.** Provider shall perform the Services at the facilities identified in the applicable Statement of Work. Provider shall not relocate the storage of Velnorix to a different facility without Client's prior written consent and completion of any qualification activities required under the Quality Agreement.

2.6 **Licenses and Registrations.** Provider shall obtain and maintain, at its own expense, all licenses, registrations and permits required under Applicable Law to perform the Services, including state wholesale distributor and third-party logistics provider licenses, and shall provide copies to Client on request.

## 3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 **Performance Standards.** Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the service levels set out in the Service Level Schedule.

3.2 **Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider shall issue the service credits set out in the Service Level Schedule, subject to the limits stated in that schedule. Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not Client's exclusive remedy for a material breach of this Agreement.

3.3 **Reporting and Reviews.** Provider shall deliver monthly performance reports in the form required by the Service Level Schedule. The Parties' relationship managers shall meet at least quarterly to review performance, open corrective actions and forecasts.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay the Fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Client shall pay other than taxes on Provider's net income.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Client monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize Fees by Statement of Work and shall reflect any service credits accrued in the preceding month.

4.3 **Payment.** Client shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Client may withhold amounts disputed in good faith by written notice describing the dispute, and the Parties shall work to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Fee Adjustments.** Provider may adjust the Fees once per contract year, effective on an anniversary of the Effective Date, by written notice delivered at least ninety (90) days in advance. No adjustment shall exceed the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers for the preceding twelve (12) months.

4.5 **Audit.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records supporting its invoices for at least three (3) years after the relevant invoice date. Client may audit those records once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 5. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

5.1 **Forecasts.** Client shall provide Provider with a rolling twelve (12) month non-binding forecast of inbound shipments and expected order volumes, updated monthly.

5.2 **Product Information.** Client shall provide Provider with the labeling, storage requirements, shipping specifications and other product information reasonably required to perform the Services, and shall notify Provider promptly of any change.

5.3 **Program Rules.** For Hub Services, Client shall provide written program rules, eligibility criteria and approved patient communications. Provider shall not deviate from approved program rules or communications without Client's written approval.

## 6. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS

6.1 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with good distribution practices and the standards of care generally observed by leading specialty pharmaceutical logistics providers in the United States.

6.2 **Compliance.** Provider shall comply with Applicable Law, the Quality Agreement and Client's written policies provided to Provider in advance. Provider shall not offer or pay any remuneration to any health care provider, patient or payer in connection with the Services other than as expressly directed by Client under an approved patient support program.

6.3 **Serialization and Traceability.** Provider shall maintain transaction information, transaction history and transaction statements for Velnorix as required by the Drug Supply Chain Security Act and shall respond to verification requests within the time required by Applicable Law.

6.4 **Adverse Events and Complaints.** Provider shall forward to Client any adverse event, product complaint or medical information request it receives regarding Velnorix within one (1) Business Day of receipt, in the manner set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

6.5 **Debarment.** Provider represents that neither it nor any person performing Services is debarred, excluded or suspended under 21 U.S.C. § 335a or from any federal health care program, and shall notify Client immediately if this representation ceases to be true.

## 7. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTORS

7.1 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified and appropriately trained personnel to perform the Services and may replace them on reasonable notice. Provider shall designate a relationship manager as Client's primary point of contact.

7.2 **Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract any portion of the Services involving the storage or physical handling of Velnorix or the processing of Personal Data without Client's prior written consent. Provider remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 **DPA.** Provider shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the DPA, Client's documented instructions and Applicable Law. To the extent required by HIPAA, the DPA operates as a business associate agreement between the Parties.

8.2 **Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Client of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use or disclosure of, Personal Data in accordance with the timelines and procedures set out in the DPA.

## 9. TITLE, RISK OF LOSS AND PRODUCT LOSS

9.1 **Title.** Title to Velnorix and all other Client inventory remains with Client at all times. Provider shall keep Client inventory free of liens and encumbrances arising through Provider and shall not hold itself out as the owner of any Client inventory.

9.2 **Custody.** Velnorix is in Provider's custody from the time Provider accepts delivery at its facility until delivery to the consignee designated in the applicable order, including while in transit with any carrier engaged by Provider.

9.3 **Product Loss.** Provider shall bear the replacement cost of any Velnorix that is lost, stolen, damaged, or subject to a Temperature Excursion while in Provider's custody, except to the extent such loss results from Client's negligence or a defect in Velnorix existing at the time Provider accepted delivery. Replacement cost means Client's fully burdened cost of goods for the affected units plus reasonable costs of investigation, quarantine and destruction. Provider shall pay or credit such amounts within thirty (30) days after Client's written demand.

9.4 **Notification and Quarantine.** Provider shall notify Client of any loss, damage or Temperature Excursion affecting Velnorix and shall quarantine affected units in accordance with the Quality Agreement pending Client's disposition decision.

9.5 **Inventory Counts.** Provider shall conduct cycle counts in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work and a full physical inventory at least once per calendar year, and shall reconcile any variance with Client within ten (10) Business Days.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including pricing, forecasts, patient program rules and business plans. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

10.2 **Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates, subcontractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Section 10.

10.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice where legally permitted and cooperates in seeking protective treatment.

10.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 10 continue for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely for trade secrets. Personal Data is governed by the DPA.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 **Pre-existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, systems and know-how.

11.2 **Client Materials and Deliverables.** All reports, patient program materials, data compilations and other deliverables prepared specifically for Client under a Statement of Work become the property of Client upon payment in full. Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any Provider pre-existing materials incorporated into such deliverables to the extent necessary to use the deliverables.

11.3 **Trademarks.** Provider shall not use Client's names, trademarks or logos without Client's prior written consent, except as required to perform the Services.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section 12.

12.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three (3) year renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party no earlier than two hundred ten (210) days and no later than one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. A notice of non-renewal delivered at any other time shall be of no force or effect.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

12.4 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.5 **Regulatory Termination.** Client may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Provider loses any license required to perform the Services, receives a warning letter or equivalent regulatory action relating to its handling of pharmaceutical products, or breaches Section 6.5.

12.6 **Transition Assistance.** Upon expiration or termination for any reason, Provider shall, for up to one hundred eighty (180) days at Client's request and at the rates then in effect, continue to perform the Services and cooperate in the orderly transfer of Client inventory, records and patient cases to Client or its designee.

12.7 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Client shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services performed through the effective date, and Provider shall return all Client inventory and Confidential Information and handle Personal Data as required by the DPA. Sections 4.5, 9, 10, 11, 12.6, 12.7, 13, 14 and 16 survive expiration or termination.

## 13. INDEMNIFICATION

13.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, penalties and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement, the Quality Agreement or the DPA; (b) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct; (c) Provider's violation of Applicable Law; or (d) any unauthorized use or disclosure of Personal Data in Provider's possession or control.

13.2 **By Client.** Client shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of (a) product liability claims relating to Velnorix, except to the extent caused by Provider's handling of Velnorix; (b) Client's negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Client's violation of Applicable Law.

13.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided no settlement imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

14.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except for liability under Sections 9.3, 10 and 13, or arising from a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

14.2 **Cap.** Except for liability under Sections 9.3, 10 and 13, or arising from a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 15. INSURANCE

15.1 Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, throughout the Term and for two (2) years thereafter: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence; (b) warehouse legal liability and cargo insurance covering the full replacement value of Client inventory in Provider's custody; (c) professional and technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; (d) cyber liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per claim; and (e) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Provider shall name Client as an additional insured where customary and deliver certificates of insurance on request.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

16.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation by courier, to the addresses stated on the signature page or such other address as a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

16.4 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among this Agreement and the related documents, the Quality Agreement shall govern with respect to quality, storage and handling matters; the DPA shall govern with respect to Personal Data; and otherwise this Agreement shall govern, followed by the Service Level Schedule, followed by the Statements of Work.

16.5 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of the business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other Party.

16.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

16.7 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Force majeure does not excuse Provider's obligations to maintain temperature-controlled storage, backup power and business continuity measures required by the Quality Agreement.

16.8 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

16.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.

16.10 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
