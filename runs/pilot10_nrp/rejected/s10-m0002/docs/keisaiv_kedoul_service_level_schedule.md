# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Keisaiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Client**"), and **Kedoul Specialty Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). It forms part of the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule sets out:
(a) the service levels that apply to the Services;
(b) how the service levels are measured and reported; and
(c) the service credits that apply under Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

1.2 **Scope.** This Schedule applies to the Services performed for Velnorix under the initial Statement of Work. It covers:
(a) outbound shipments from the Facilities to consignees located in the fifty states of the United States and the District of Columbia (the "**U.S. Lanes**"); and
(b) the Hub Services provided to patients and health care providers located in the United States.

1.3 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement or, where applicable, in the Quality Agreement.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "**Conforming Order**" means an order for Velnorix that meets all of the following conditions:
(a) Provider receives it through the agreed order channel by the Order Cut-Off Time on a Business Day;
(b) it contains complete ship-to, consignee license and product information;
(c) it is for released inventory available at the Facility; and
(d) it is not on credit, compliance or regulatory hold at Client's direction.

2.2 "**Order Cut-Off Time**" means 3:00 p.m. Central Time, or another time specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

2.3 "**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month.

2.4 "**Monthly Fees**" means the total Fees invoiced under the applicable Statement of Work for Services performed in the relevant Measurement Period. Pass-through freight, packaging and other reimbursable costs are excluded.

2.5 "**Service Level Failure**" means Provider's failure to achieve a Service Level in a Measurement Period.

2.6 "**Service Level**" means each performance standard set out in Section 3.

## 3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 **Order Fulfillment.** Provider shall ship 98% of Conforming Orders on the same Business Day. Measurement: the number of Conforming Orders shipped on the Business Day of receipt, divided by the total number of Conforming Orders received in the Measurement Period.

3.2 **Order Accuracy.** Provider shall ship at least 99.8% of order lines with no errors. An order line is in error if it has the wrong product, strength, presentation, quantity, Lot, consignee or shipping configuration. Measurement: the number of error-free order lines shipped, divided by the total number of order lines shipped in the Measurement Period, as confirmed by consignee discrepancy reports and Provider's investigation.

3.3 **On-Time Delivery.** At least 98.5% of shipments on the U.S. Lanes shall be delivered by the delivery date committed for the applicable service level selected for the order. Measurement: carrier proof-of-delivery data. Shipments delayed by events described in Section 6.1 are excluded.

3.4 **Shipping Configuration Compliance.** 100% of shipments of Velnorix shall be made in a qualified shipping configuration approved by Client for the applicable lane and season, as required by Section 6.1 of the Quality Agreement. Each shipment shall include the temperature indicator or data logger required by Section 6.3 of the Quality Agreement.

3.5 **Temperature Excursion Notification.** For 100% of Temperature Excursions affecting Velnorix in Provider's custody, Provider shall notify Client's Quality Unit and place the affected units in Quarantine. Notification shall be given within one (1) Business Day after discovery, in accordance with Section 5.5 of the Quality Agreement.

3.6 **Inventory Accuracy.** Provider shall maintain inventory record accuracy of at least 99.9% by unit, by Lot, as measured by cycle counts and by the annual physical inventory under Section 9.5 of the Agreement.

3.7 **Benefits Verification Turnaround.** Provider shall complete at least 95% of benefits verifications within two (2) Business Days after receipt of a complete patient enrollment form.

3.8 **Hub Call Answer Rate.** Provider shall answer at least 90% of inbound calls to the patient support program line within thirty (30) seconds. The call abandonment rate shall not exceed 5%. This Service Level applies during the hub operating hours specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

3.9 **Adverse Event and Complaint Forwarding.** Provider shall forward to Client 100% of adverse events, Product Complaints and medical information requests regarding Velnorix within one (1) Business Day of receipt, in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

## 4. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

4.1 **Data.** Provider shall measure each Service Level using its warehouse management, transportation, temperature monitoring and hub case management systems. Provider shall keep the underlying data for at least three (3) years, consistent with Section 4.5 of the Agreement, and shall make it available to Client on request.

4.2 **Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, Provider shall deliver a performance report to Client. The report shall show, for each Service Level:
(a) actual performance;
(b) any Service Level Failure and its root cause;
(c) corrective actions taken or planned; and
(d) the service credits accrued.

4.3 **Quarterly Review.** The Parties' relationship managers shall review the monthly reports, trends and open corrective actions at the quarterly reviews held under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Corrective Action.** For each Service Level Failure, Provider shall deliver a written corrective action plan within fifteen (15) Business Days after the end of the Measurement Period. Provider shall implement the plan within the timelines it sets out. Where a Service Level Failure is also a Deviation, the corrective action shall be managed as a CAPA under Section 8 of the Quality Agreement.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 **Credit Amounts.** For each Service Level Failure, Provider shall issue the service credit set out below as a percentage of Monthly Fees:

| Service Level | Section | Service Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Order Fulfillment | 3.1 | 3% |
| Order Accuracy | 3.2 | 2% |
| On-Time Delivery | 3.3 | 2% |
| Shipping Configuration Compliance | 3.4 | 3% |
| Temperature Excursion Notification | 3.5 | 3% |
| Inventory Accuracy | 3.6 | 1% |
| Benefits Verification Turnaround | 3.7 | 2% |
| Hub Call Answer Rate | 3.8 | 1% |
| Adverse Event and Complaint Forwarding | 3.9 | 3% |

5.2 **Application.** Provider shall show accrued service credits on the invoice for the month following the Measurement Period, as required by Section 4.2 of the Agreement. Any credits outstanding when the Agreement expires or terminates shall be paid to Client within thirty (30) days.

5.3 **Cap.** The total service credits for any Measurement Period shall not exceed 10% of monthly fees for the affected month.

5.4 **Other Remedies.** As provided in Section 3.2 of the Agreement, service credits are a price adjustment and are not Client's exclusive remedy for a material breach of the Agreement. Service credits do not limit Provider's obligations under Section 9.3 of the Agreement.

## 6. EXCLUSIONS

6.1 **Excused Performance.** A Service Level Failure is excused to the extent it is caused by any of the following:
(a) a force majeure event under Section 16.7 of the Agreement;
(b) Client's breach of the Agreement or failure to provide product information, program rules or approvals required under Section 5 of the Agreement; or
(c) an order volume in a Measurement Period exceeding 125% of Client's most recent forecast under Section 5.1 of the Agreement, unless Provider agreed in writing to support that volume.

6.2 **Notice.** Provider may rely on Section 6.1 only if it identifies the excusing event, and its effect on performance, in the monthly report for the affected Measurement Period.

## 7. REPEATED FAILURES

7.1 **Escalation.** If the same Service Level Failure occurs in any two (2) consecutive Measurement Periods, or in any three (3) Measurement Periods within a rolling twelve (12) month period, the matter shall be escalated. Within ten (10) Business Days, Provider's senior operations executive shall meet with Client to agree on a remediation plan.

7.2 **Material Breach.** A Service Level Failure of Section 3.4, 3.5 or 3.9 in any three (3) Measurement Periods within a rolling twelve (12) month period constitutes a material breach for purposes of Section 12.3 of the Agreement.

## 8. REVIEW AND AMENDMENT

8.1 **Annual Review.** The Parties shall review the Service Levels at least annually. They may also review them on any material change in the Services or the U.S. Lanes.

8.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 16.8 of the Agreement.
