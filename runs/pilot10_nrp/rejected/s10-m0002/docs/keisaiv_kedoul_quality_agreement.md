# GDP QUALITY AGREEMENT

This GDP Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Keisaiv Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Client**"), and **Kedoul Specialty Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Client and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." This Quality Agreement is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Agreement**").

**RECITALS**

A. Client holds the approved Biologics License Application for Velnorix and is responsible for the quality of Velnorix placed on the market.

B. Under the Agreement, Provider stores, handles and distributes Velnorix on Client's behalf. Provider also coordinates shipments of Velnorix to patients and sites of care as part of the Hub Services.

C. This Quality Agreement allocates the Parties' quality responsibilities for those activities in accordance with good distribution practices and Applicable Law.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Hub Services," "Services," "Statement of Work" and "Velnorix."

1.1 "**Applicable GDP Requirements**" means all Applicable Law and regulatory guidance governing the storage, handling and distribution of prescription drugs and biologics in the United States. This includes 21 C.F.R. Parts 203 and 205, the Drug Supply Chain Security Act, United States Pharmacopeia General Chapters <1079> and <659>, and state wholesale distribution and third-party logistics provider requirements.

1.2 "**CAPA**" means a corrective and preventive action.

1.3 "**Change**" means any proposed change to a Facility, to equipment or systems used to store, monitor or ship Velnorix, to shipping configurations or lanes, or to any procedure governing activities under this Quality Agreement.

1.4 "**Deviation**" means any departure from this Quality Agreement, an approved procedure or an Applicable GDP Requirement.

1.5 "**Facility**" means each Provider facility at which Velnorix is received, stored or shipped, as identified in the applicable Statement of Work and qualified under Section 4.

1.6 "**Lot**" means a specific quantity of Velnorix identified by a unique lot number assigned by Client or its manufacturer.

1.7 "**Product Complaint**" means any written, electronic or oral communication alleging a deficiency in the identity, quality, durability, reliability, safety, effectiveness or performance of Velnorix, including its packaging and labeling.

1.8 "**Quarantine**" means the physical and system segregation of Velnorix, with status controls, so that it cannot be shipped pending a disposition decision by Client.

1.9 "**Temperature Excursion**" means any deviation outside the 2°C to 8°C labeled storage range lasting more than sixty (60) consecutive minutes, as recorded by a qualified temperature monitoring device.

1.10 "**Quality Unit**" means, for each Party, the function responsible for quality assurance under this Quality Agreement, as identified in Annex 1.

## 2. SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to Velnorix injection, distributed solely in the United States. It covers receipt, storage, inventory control, order picking, packing, shipping, returns handling and the related records maintained by Provider under the Agreement.

2.2 **Client Responsibilities.** Client is responsible for:
(a) release of each Lot for distribution;
(b) all disposition decisions for Velnorix in Quarantine, including decisions following a Temperature Excursion;
(c) stability data and product-specific storage and shipping specifications;
(d) recalls, market withdrawals and regulatory reporting to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration; and
(e) approval of shipping configurations and packaging qualification protocols.

2.3 **Provider Responsibilities.** Provider is responsible for:
(a) storing and handling Velnorix in accordance with its labeling, Client's specifications and Applicable GDP Requirements;
(b) maintaining qualified Facilities, equipment and shipping systems;
(c) maintaining a quality management system adequate for the Services;
(d) identifying, documenting and investigating Deviations within its control; and
(e) cooperating with Client in recalls, investigations and regulatory inspections.

2.4 **Responsibility Matrix.** Annex 2 sets out a summary allocation of responsibilities. Annex 2 is a summary only and is to be read with the body of this Quality Agreement.

## 3. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

3.1 **Quality System.** Provider shall maintain a documented quality management system covering document control, training, Change control, Deviation management, CAPA, internal audits, supplier and carrier qualification, and management review.

3.2 **Procedures.** Provider shall maintain written standard operating procedures for all activities under this Quality Agreement. On request, Provider shall provide Client with copies of procedures that apply specifically to Velnorix.

3.3 **Training.** Provider shall train all personnel who handle Velnorix or its records on the applicable procedures before they perform those tasks, and at least annually after that. Provider shall keep training records.

3.4 **Internal Audits.** Provider shall perform internal quality audits of each Facility at least annually.

## 4. FACILITIES AND EQUIPMENT

4.1 **Qualification.** Before storing Velnorix at a Facility, Provider shall complete installation, operational and performance qualification of the refrigerated storage areas. This includes a temperature mapping study under both summer and winter conditions. Provider shall provide the qualification reports to Client for review.

4.2 **Requalification.** Provider shall repeat temperature mapping at least every three (3) years and after any Change that could affect temperature distribution.

4.3 **Monitoring Equipment.** Provider shall continuously monitor storage temperatures using calibrated sensors with alarm capability, traceable to national standards. Provider shall calibrate each sensor at least annually.

4.4 **Alarms.** Provider shall configure alarms to alert responsible personnel at all times, including outside business hours. Provider shall test alarm functionality at least quarterly.

4.5 **Backup Power and Continuity.** Each Facility shall have automatic backup power sufficient to maintain refrigerated storage for at least seventy-two (72) hours. Provider shall maintain a written business continuity plan for Velnorix storage and shipping, including contingency storage at a qualified alternate location. Provider shall test the plan at least annually.

4.6 **Pest Control, Security and Sanitation.** Provider shall maintain documented programs for pest control, cleaning and physical security of each Facility, with access to Velnorix storage areas restricted to authorized personnel.

## 5. STORAGE AND HANDLING

5.1 **Storage Conditions.** Provider shall store Velnorix refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C, protected from light in its original carton, and shall not freeze it.

5.2 **Receipt.** On receipt of each inbound shipment, Provider shall:
(a) verify the shipment against Client's advance shipping notice;
(b) review in-transit temperature data before placing Velnorix into available inventory;
(c) inspect for visible damage; and
(d) verify product identifiers as required by the Drug Supply Chain Security Act.

Velnorix shall remain in Quarantine until receipt is complete and Client has released the Lot.

5.3 **Inventory Control.** Provider shall manage inventory on a first-expired, first-out basis unless Client directs otherwise. Provider shall not ship any unit with less than the remaining shelf life specified by Client in the applicable Statement of Work.

5.4 **Temperature Records.** Provider shall keep continuous temperature records for each storage area and each shipment and shall make them available to Client on request.

5.5 **Temperature Excursions.** Provider shall notify Client's Quality Unit of any Temperature Excursion affecting Velnorix within one (1) Business Day after discovery. Provider shall promptly place all affected units in Quarantine and shall provide Client with the temperature data, the duration and the minimum and maximum temperatures recorded. Affected units shall remain in Quarantine until Client makes its disposition decision.

5.6 **Damaged and Suspect Product.** Provider shall place in Quarantine any Velnorix that is damaged, suspected of being counterfeit or illegitimate, or otherwise unfit for distribution. Provider shall notify Client within one (1) Business Day.

## 6. SHIPPING AND TRANSPORTATION

6.1 **Shipping Configurations.** Provider shall ship Velnorix only in shipping configurations qualified for the applicable lane and season and approved by Client.

6.2 **Carriers.** Provider shall use only carriers qualified under its supplier qualification procedure and approved by Client. Provider remains responsible for Velnorix while in transit with carriers it engages.

6.3 **In-Transit Monitoring.** Each shipment of Velnorix shall include a calibrated temperature indicator or data logger as specified by Client. Provider shall instruct consignees on how to report indicator alarms.

6.4 **Licensing of Consignees.** Provider shall verify, before each shipment, that each consignee is appropriately licensed or authorized under Applicable Law to receive Velnorix.

## 7. RETURNS, RECALLS AND COMPLAINTS

7.1 **Returns.** Provider shall receive returned Velnorix only as authorized by Client and shall place all returns in Quarantine. Returned Velnorix shall not be returned to saleable inventory without Client's written disposition.

7.2 **Recalls.** Client shall direct all recalls and market withdrawals. Within the timeframes Client specifies, Provider shall:
(a) provide distribution records identifying consignees by Lot;
(b) place remaining inventory of affected Lots in Quarantine; and
(c) support consignee notification and reconciliation of returned units.

Provider shall participate in a mock recall at least annually at Client's request.

7.3 **Product Complaints.** Provider shall forward Product Complaints to Client within one (1) Business Day of receipt, consistent with Section 6.4 of the Agreement. Provider shall investigate any Product Complaint relating to its own activities and report its findings to Client.

## 8. DEVIATIONS, CAPA AND CHANGE CONTROL

8.1 **Deviations.** Provider shall document and investigate each Deviation. Provider shall notify Client within one (1) Business Day of any Deviation that may affect the quality of Velnorix. Investigations shall be completed within thirty (30) days unless Client agrees to an extension, and shall identify root cause and proposed CAPA.

8.2 **CAPA.** Provider shall implement CAPA within agreed timelines and verify its effectiveness. Provider shall report the status of open CAPA in the quarterly reviews held under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

8.3 **Change Control.** Provider shall notify Client in writing at least sixty (60) days before implementing any Change that may affect Velnorix. Provider shall not implement the Change without Client's written approval. Emergency Changes required to protect Velnorix may be implemented immediately, with notice to Client within one (1) Business Day.

## 9. RECORDS

9.1 **Record Keeping.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of all activities under this Quality Agreement. This includes receipt, storage, temperature monitoring, shipping, returns, Deviations, CAPA, training and qualification records.

9.2 **Retention.** Provider shall retain records for at least the longer of:
(a) six (6) years after creation; and
(b) one (1) year after expiry of the relevant Lot.

Provider shall not destroy records without first offering them to Client.

9.3 **Electronic Records.** Electronic systems used to create or maintain quality records shall be validated and shall maintain secure, time-stamped audit trails consistent with 21 C.F.R. Part 11, to the extent applicable.

## 10. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

10.1 **Client Audits.** Client may audit each Facility and Provider's quality records once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice. Client may also audit at any time for cause, including after a significant Deviation or Temperature Excursion.

10.2 **Regulatory Inspections.** Provider shall notify Client within one (1) Business Day of any regulatory inspection or inquiry relating to Velnorix or to Facilities where Velnorix is stored. Provider shall provide copies of any resulting observations and its responses. Client may review those responses before submission, to the extent they relate to Velnorix.

## 11. TERM AND AMENDMENT

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement remains in effect for the Term of the Agreement and for so long after that as Provider holds any Velnorix or quality records relating to Velnorix.

11.2 **Review.** The Quality Units shall review this Quality Agreement at least every two (2) years and on any material Change in the Services.

11.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Quality Unit heads of both Parties. Annex 1 may be updated by written notice from either Party for its own contacts.
