# STABIND BREWING COMPANY INTRODUCES RIDGELINE HAZE, A NEW YEAR-ROUND HAZY IPA

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Rochester, New York, November 3, 2026.** Stabind Brewing Company, Inc. today announced Ridgeline Haze, a new hazy India Pale Ale. Ridgeline Haze will join the brewery's year-round lineup and will reach retailers, bars and restaurants across Stabind's distribution footprint beginning in late November 2026.

## 1. About Ridgeline Haze

1.1 **The Beer.** Ridgeline Haze is a soft, full-bodied IPA at 6.8% ABV. Stabind's brewers describe it as having notes of ripe stone fruit, tangerine and pine, with a smooth, lightly bitter finish. It is brewed with a base of pilsner malt, flaked oats and wheat. It is hopped generously in the whirlpool and dry-hopped twice.

1.2 **Hops.** Ridgeline Haze uses hop pellets grown and processed in Washington's Yakima Valley. The pellets are cold-stored and handled under refrigeration from the farm to Stabind's brewhouse to protect aroma and freshness.

1.3 **Development.** The recipe was developed over eighteen months of small-batch pilot brewing at Stabind's Rochester brewery. More than two dozen test versions were poured for guests in the Foundry Street taproom during that period.

## 2. Availability

2.1 **Formats.** Ridgeline Haze will be available in 16-ounce four-pack cans and on draft.

2.2 **Launch Event.** Stabind will host a release celebration at its taproom at 410 Foundry Street, Rochester, New York, on Saturday, November 21, 2026, from noon to 10:00 p.m. The event will feature live music and food trucks, and fresh draft pours of Ridgeline Haze.

2.3 **Distribution.** Ridgeline Haze will be sold through Stabind's existing wholesale distribution partners and at the taproom. Retailers interested in carrying Ridgeline Haze should contact their local Stabind distributor.

## 3. Comment from Stabind

3.1 "Ridgeline Haze is the beer our team has wanted to make since we first opened our doors," said Marguerite A. Stabind, Chief Executive Officer of Stabind Brewing Company. "It is bright, juicy and easy to drink, and it shows what our brewers can do with great Northwest hops. We can't wait for our neighbors in Rochester and our fans across the region to try it."

## 4. About Stabind Brewing Company

4.1 Stabind Brewing Company, Inc. is a regional craft brewery headquartered in Rochester, New York. Stabind brews a range of year-round, seasonal and limited-release beers and operates a taproom at its Foundry Street brewery.
