# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of February 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Stabind Brewing Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Stabind**"), and **Kogein Hop Farms, LLC**, a Washington limited liability company ("**Kogein**"). Stabind and Kogein are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

This DPA supplements the Hop Supply Agreement between Stabind and Kogein dated as of February 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It is the Data Processing Addendum referred to in Sections 1.1(e) and 9.4 of the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this DPA:

(a) "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the Processing of Stabind Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act, N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-aa and § 899-bb, the Washington data breach notification law, RCW 19.255.010, and, to the extent applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended.

(b) "**Kogein Group Member**" means Kogein; any Affiliate of Kogein; and any entity with which Kogein has a strategic supply or processing arrangement.

(c) "**Personnel**" means a Party's employees, officers and individual contractors.

(d) "**Process**" or "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Stabind Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

(e) "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Stabind Personal Data in Kogein's possession, custody or control.

(f) "**Stabind Personal Data**" means any information that identifies, relates to or could reasonably be linked with an identified or identifiable individual and that Kogein receives from or on behalf of Stabind, or Processes on Stabind's behalf, in connection with the Agreement.

(g) "**Subprocessor**" means any third party, other than a Kogein Group Member, that Kogein engages to Process Stabind Personal Data.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this DPA. References in this DPA to Sections are to sections of this DPA unless stated otherwise.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Scope.** This DPA applies to all Processing of Stabind Personal Data by Kogein in connection with the Agreement. The categories of Stabind Personal Data and data subjects, and the nature and purposes of Processing, are described in Annex 1.

2.2 **Roles.** Stabind is the business or controller of Stabind Personal Data. Kogein Processes Stabind Personal Data as Stabind's service provider or processor and only on Stabind's behalf.

2.3 **Stabind's Obligations.** Stabind shall ensure that it has any notices, consents and other legal bases required by Applicable Data Protection Law for Kogein to Process Stabind Personal Data as contemplated by the Agreement and this DPA.

## 3. KOGEIN'S PROCESSING OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Kogein shall Process Stabind Personal Data only to perform its obligations under the Agreement and in accordance with Stabind's documented instructions. The Agreement and this DPA are Stabind's complete instructions as of the Effective Date. Stabind may issue additional reasonable written instructions consistent with the Agreement. Kogein shall promptly notify Stabind if it believes an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Kogein shall not:

(a) sell or share Stabind Personal Data, as those terms are defined in Applicable Data Protection Law;

(b) retain, use or disclose Stabind Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Agreement, including any commercial purpose of Kogein;

(c) retain, use or disclose Stabind Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Stabind and Kogein; or

(d) combine Stabind Personal Data with personal data that Kogein receives from or on behalf of any other person or collects from its own interactions with individuals, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.3 **Personnel.** Kogein shall limit access to Stabind Personal Data to Personnel who need access to perform the Agreement and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations or an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality.

3.4 **Disclosure to Kogein Group Members.** Kogein may disclose Stabind Personal Data to a Kogein Group Member only to the extent necessary for that Kogein Group Member to perform obligations under the Agreement. Before any such disclosure, Kogein shall ensure that the Kogein Group Member is bound by written obligations at least as protective of Stabind Personal Data as those in this DPA. Kogein remains responsible to Stabind for any Processing by a Kogein Group Member.

3.5 **Legal Requests.** If Kogein receives a subpoena, court order or other governmental request for Stabind Personal Data, Kogein shall notify Stabind promptly, unless prohibited by law, so that Stabind may seek a protective order or other remedy. Kogein shall disclose only the Stabind Personal Data that it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. SUBPROCESSORS

4.1 **Authorization.** Stabind authorizes Kogein to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 2.

4.2 **New Subprocessors.** Kogein shall give Stabind at least 30 days' written notice before engaging a new Subprocessor. Stabind may object on reasonable data protection grounds by written notice within 15 days after receiving Kogein's notice. If Stabind objects, the Parties shall discuss Stabind's concerns in good faith, and Kogein shall not engage the new Subprocessor to Process Stabind Personal Data until the objection is resolved.

4.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Kogein shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations at least as protective as those in this DPA. Kogein remains liable to Stabind for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors.

## 5. SECURITY

5.1 **Security Program.** Kogein shall implement and maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of Stabind Personal Data and consistent with N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-bb. At a minimum, the program shall include the measures described in Annex 3.

5.2 **Review.** Kogein shall review and update its security program at least annually, and after any Security Incident, to address reasonably foreseeable internal and external risks.

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

6.1 **Notice.** Kogein shall notify Stabind in writing without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. The notice shall describe, to the extent known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate number of individuals affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it. Kogein shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.2 **Cooperation.** Kogein shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident and shall cooperate with Stabind in any investigation. Stabind shall decide whether and how to notify affected individuals, regulators or others, except where Applicable Data Protection Law requires Kogein to give notice directly.

6.3 **Costs.** Kogein shall reimburse Stabind's reasonable costs of investigation, notification and credit monitoring arising from a Security Incident caused by Kogein's or its Subprocessors' breach of this DPA.

## 7. ASSISTANCE AND INDIVIDUAL REQUESTS

7.1 **Individual Requests.** Kogein shall promptly forward to Stabind any request from an individual to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Law relating to Stabind Personal Data. Kogein shall not respond to the request except as Stabind instructs, and shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Stabind to respond.

7.2 **Other Assistance.** Kogein shall provide reasonable information and assistance to enable Stabind to comply with its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law, including any risk assessment relating to the Processing.

## 8. AUDIT

8.1 **Information.** On Stabind's written request, Kogein shall provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA, including summaries of any third-party security assessments.

8.2 **Audits.** Stabind may audit Kogein's compliance with this DPA as part of, and on the same notice and frequency as, the audits permitted under Section 7.3 of the Agreement. Stabind may conduct an additional audit within 30 days after any Security Incident.

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION

9.1 On expiration or termination of the Agreement, or earlier on Stabind's written request, Kogein shall, at Stabind's election, return or securely delete all Stabind Personal Data and shall certify deletion in writing within 30 days. Kogein may retain Stabind Personal Data only as required by law or in routine backup systems, consistent with Section 9.5 of the Agreement. Any retained Stabind Personal Data remains subject to this DPA for as long as Kogein retains it.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Liability.** Each Party's liability under this DPA is subject to Section 11 of the Agreement.

10.2 **Term.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Kogein Processes Stabind Personal Data.

10.3 **Changes in Law.** If Applicable Data Protection Law changes in a way that affects the Processing under this DPA, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendments needed to comply.

10.4 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

10.5 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this DPA.

10.6 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.

## ANNEX 3: MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

1. Role-based access controls and unique user credentials for all systems holding Stabind Personal Data.
2. Multi-factor authentication for remote access and administrative accounts.
3. Encryption of Stabind Personal Data in transit and at rest using industry-standard methods.
4. Annual security awareness training for Personnel with access to Stabind Personal Data.
5. Logging and monitoring of access to systems holding Stabind Personal Data.
6. Physical access controls at Kogein's Moxee facility, including controlled entry and visitor logs.
7. Secure disposal of paper and electronic records containing Stabind Personal Data.
