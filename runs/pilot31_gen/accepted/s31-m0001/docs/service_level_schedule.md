# SCHEDULE 1: SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is Schedule 1 to the Hop Supply Agreement between **Stabind Brewing Company, Inc.** ("**Stabind**") and **Kogein Hop Farms, LLC** ("**Kogein**") dated as of February 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Schedule:

(a) "**Chronic Failure**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

(b) "**Delivery Window**" means, for each Purchase Order line item, the period from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. local time at the delivery location on the delivery date stated in the accepted Purchase Order. Stabind may agree to a different delivery date or time in writing.

(c) "**Measurement Period**" means each calendar quarter during the Term. A quarter ends on March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31.

(d) "**On-Time Delivery**" means the percentage, calculated under Section 2.2, of Purchase Order line items in a Measurement Period that are delivered On Time and In Full.

(e) "**On Time and In Full**" means that a Purchase Order line item is delivered (i) within the Delivery Window, (ii) in the full quantity ordered, and (iii) in the packaging and at the temperature required by Section 5.2 of the Agreement.

(f) "**Performance Report**" means the quarterly report that Kogein delivers under Section 3.1.

(g) "**Service Credit**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

1.2 **References.** References in this Schedule to Sections are to sections of this Schedule unless stated otherwise.

## 2. ON-TIME DELIVERY SERVICE LEVEL

2.1 **Service Level.** Kogein shall achieve On-Time Delivery of at least 95% in each Measurement Period.

2.2 **Calculation.** On-Time Delivery for a Measurement Period equals the number of Purchase Order line items with a delivery date in that Measurement Period that were delivered On Time and In Full, divided by the total number of Purchase Order line items with a delivery date in that Measurement Period, multiplied by 100. The result is rounded to the nearest whole percentage point. The following rules apply to the calculation:

(a) A line item delivered in more than one shipment is On Time and In Full only if every shipment arrives within the Delivery Window.

(b) A line item rejected under Section 7.2 of the Agreement is not On Time and In Full, unless Kogein delivers a conforming replacement within the original Delivery Window.

(c) A line item is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator if its late or short delivery is caused solely by (i) Stabind's failure to make the delivery location available during the Delivery Window, (ii) a change to the Purchase Order that Stabind requests less than five business days before the delivery date, or (iii) a Force Majeure Event that Kogein has notified to Stabind under Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Service Credits.** If On-Time Delivery for a Measurement Period is below 95%, Kogein shall credit Stabind with an amount (a "**Service Credit**") equal to the following percentage of the Fees invoiced for Products delivered in that Measurement Period:

| On-Time Delivery for the Measurement Period | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 94% | 2% |
| 93% | 4% |
| 92% | 6% |
| Below 92% | 10% |

2.4 **Application of Service Credits.** Kogein shall apply each Service Credit against the next invoice issued after delivery of the Performance Report for the relevant Measurement Period. If no further invoice is to be issued, Kogein shall pay the Service Credit to Stabind within 30 days. Service Credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of late or short deliveries and are not a penalty. Service Credits do not limit Stabind's rights under Section 4 or under the Agreement.

## 3. REPORTING

3.1 **Performance Reports.** Within 10 business days after the end of each Measurement Period, Kogein shall deliver a Performance Report to Stabind's Chief Operating Officer. Kogein may deliver it by email. Each Performance Report shall set out:

(a) On-Time Delivery for the Measurement Period just ended and for the immediately preceding Measurement Period;

(b) each Purchase Order line item that was not delivered On Time and In Full, with the reason for the failure;

(c) each line item that Kogein has excluded under Section 2.2(c), with the basis for the exclusion;

(d) the Service Credit due, if any; and

(e) the corrective actions Kogein has taken or plans to take for any failure to meet the service level in Section 2.1.

3.2 **Supporting Data.** Kogein shall provide the delivery records, carrier tracking data and temperature logs supporting each Performance Report within five business days after Stabind requests them.

3.3 **Disputes over Performance Reports.** Stabind may dispute any calculation in a Performance Report by written notice to Kogein. Kogein's operations representatives shall meet with Stabind's operations representatives within 10 business days after that notice to reconcile the calculation. If they cannot agree, either Party may escalate the dispute under Section 5.2. A dispute over a calculation does not suspend any time period in this Schedule.

## 4. CHRONIC FAILURE

4.1 **Chronic Failure.** A "**Chronic Failure**" occurs if On-Time Delivery is below 92% in two consecutive calendar quarters.

4.2 **Stabind's Remedies.** If a Chronic Failure occurs, Stabind may elect one of the following by written notice to Kogein given under Section 16 of the Agreement within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Performance Report for the second of the two consecutive calendar quarters:

(a) reduce the Minimum Annual Volume under Section 3.2 of the Agreement by any amount up to 50%, starting with the Contract Year in which the notice is given and continuing for each later Contract Year in the Term; or

(b) terminate the Agreement under Section 13.3 of the Agreement. Termination takes effect on the date stated in the notice, which must be at least 30 days and not more than 180 days after the date of the notice.

4.3 **Contents of Notice.** A notice under Section 4.2 shall identify the two calendar quarters that make up the Chronic Failure and the remedy Stabind elects. If Stabind elects a reduction under Section 4.2(a), the notice shall also state the amount of the reduction.

4.4 **Effect of Reduction.** If Stabind reduces the Minimum Annual Volume under Section 4.2(a), the reduced Minimum Annual Volume replaces the amount stated in Section 3.2 of the Agreement for all purposes, including the calculation of any shortfall invoice. If the notice is given part of the way through a Contract Year, the reduced Minimum Annual Volume applies to the whole of that Contract Year.

4.5 **Further Chronic Failures.** Each Chronic Failure is a separate event. The quarters of one Chronic Failure may not be counted toward another. Stabind's election of a remedy for one Chronic Failure does not prevent it from electing a remedy for a later Chronic Failure.

4.6 **Remedies Cumulative.** Stabind's remedies under this Section 4 are in addition to Service Credits under Section 2.3 and to Stabind's other rights and remedies under the Agreement.

## 5. GOVERNANCE

5.1 **Quarterly Review.** Within 15 business days after delivery of each Performance Report, the Parties' operations representatives shall meet, in person or by video conference, to review Kogein's performance against the service level in Section 2.1, open corrective actions and Stabind's most recent Forecast.

5.2 **Escalation.** The Parties' operations representatives may not be able to resolve a dispute under this Schedule within 10 business days after either Party first raises it in writing. In that case, either Party may refer the dispute to Stabind's Chief Operating Officer and Kogein's President, who shall meet within 10 business days after the referral to try to resolve it in good faith. Escalation under this Section 5.2 does not limit either Party's rights under Section 17.2 of the Agreement and does not suspend any time period in this Schedule.

5.3 **Corrective Action.** If On-Time Delivery is below 95% in any Measurement Period, Kogein shall implement the corrective actions described in its Performance Report for that Measurement Period. Kogein shall report on their status at the next quarterly review under Section 5.1.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Records.** Kogein shall keep complete and accurate records of all deliveries, carrier data and temperature logs relating to the Products for at least three years after the end of the Measurement Period to which they relate. These records are subject to audit under Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

6.3 **Part of the Agreement.** This Schedule is the Service Level Schedule referred to in the Agreement, including Sections 1.1(o), 5.3 and 13.3 of the Agreement.
