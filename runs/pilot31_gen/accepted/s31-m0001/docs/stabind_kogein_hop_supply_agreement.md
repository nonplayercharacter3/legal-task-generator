# HOP SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Hop Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2025 by and between **Stabind Brewing Company, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Foundry Street, Rochester, New York 14608 ("**Stabind**"), and **Kogein Hop Farms, LLC**, a Washington limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Yakima Valley Highway, Moxee, Washington 98936 ("**Kogein**"). Stabind and Kogein are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Kogein grows, harvests, kilns, processes and pelletizes hops and supplies hop pellets to commercial brewers.

B. Stabind operates a regional craft brewery and wishes to buy hop pellets, including pellets processed to Stabind's proprietary blend specifications, from Kogein on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**Blend Specifications**" means the proprietary hop variety, blend ratio, alpha-acid, oil-content and processing parameters for Stabind's custom pellet blends, as set out in Exhibit A or later provided by Stabind in writing.

(c) "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

(d) "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period beginning on February 1 during the Term.

(e) "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

(f) "**Effective Date**" means February 1, 2025.

(g) "**Fees**" means all amounts payable by Stabind to Kogein under this Agreement for Products and Services.

(h) "**Forecast**" has the meaning given in Section 3.3.

(i) "**Lot**" means a quantity of Product from a single harvest, variety and processing run, identified by a unique lot number.

(j) "**Minimum Annual Volume**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

(k) "**Performance Report**" has the meaning given in the Service Level Schedule.

(l) "**Product Contamination Losses**" means Stabind's losses, costs and expenses arising from Products that are contaminated, adulterated or not in conformity with the Specifications. These include costs of product recall, market withdrawal and customer notification; the cost of beer, wort and packaging materials destroyed or rendered unsaleable; and the costs of cleaning, sanitizing and remediating brewing equipment and facilities.

(m) "**Products**" means the hop pellets listed in Exhibit A, including pellets processed to the Blend Specifications, as updated by the Parties in writing.

(n) "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Stabind under Section 5.1.

(o) "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule attached to this Agreement as Schedule 1.

(p) "**Services**" means hop storage, cold-chain handling, blending, pelletizing, testing and related processing services performed by Kogein under this Agreement.

(q) "**Specifications**" means the Blend Specifications and the product, quality, packaging and analytical specifications for each Product set out in Exhibit A.

(r) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Kogein Group Member.** "**Kogein Group Member**" has the meaning given in Section 1.1 of the Data Processing Addendum.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date, February 1, 2025. It continues for an initial term of four years, ending January 31, 2029 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.2 **Renewal.** On expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five-year Renewal Terms (each a "**Renewal Term**"), five years per renewal. Renewal ends only if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal not more than 210 days and not less than 180 days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. Pricing for each Renewal Term shall be Kogein's then-current list price for each Product in effect on the first day of that Renewal Term. All other terms of this Agreement, including the Minimum Annual Volume under Section 3.2, continue to apply during each Renewal Term.

2.3 **Effect of Expiration.** Expiration of this Agreement does not affect Purchase Orders accepted before the expiration date. Those Purchase Orders remain governed by this Agreement until fully performed.

## 3. SUPPLY AND PURCHASE COMMITMENTS

3.1 **Supply.** Kogein shall sell and deliver Products to Stabind, and perform the Services, in accordance with this Agreement, the Specifications and the Service Level Schedule.

3.2 **Minimum Annual Volume.** Stabind shall purchase at least 180,000 pounds of hop pellets per contract year (the "**Minimum Annual Volume**"). If Stabind's purchases in a Contract Year fall below the Minimum Annual Volume, Kogein may invoice Stabind, within 60 days after the end of that Contract Year, for the shortfall quantity at the weighted average price paid by Stabind for Products during that Contract Year. The Minimum Annual Volume may be reduced only as provided in the Service Level Schedule, in Section 14.3 or by written agreement of the Parties.

3.3 **Forecasts.** By the first business day of each calendar quarter, Stabind shall give Kogein a non-binding rolling twelve-month forecast of its anticipated Product requirements by variety and blend (each a "**Forecast**"). Kogein shall use Forecasts for crop allocation, contracting and processing planning. Forecasts are Stabind's Confidential Information.

3.4 **Crop Allocation.** Kogein shall reserve from each harvest enough hops of the varieties identified in Exhibit A to meet the Minimum Annual Volume and the most recent Forecast. Kogein shall notify Stabind promptly if it expects any harvest shortfall that may affect supply to Stabind.

## 4. SPECIFICATIONS AND BLEND SPECIFICATIONS

4.1 **Conformity.** All Products shall conform to the Specifications. Each Lot shall be accompanied by a certificate of analysis showing alpha-acid, beta-acid, total oil, moisture content and hop storage index results.

4.2 **Blend Specifications.** The Blend Specifications are the exclusive property of Stabind. Kogein shall use the Blend Specifications only to produce Products for Stabind and shall not produce for any other person a product made to, or derived from, the Blend Specifications.

4.3 **Changes.** Stabind may change the Blend Specifications on 90 days' written notice. If a change materially increases Kogein's processing costs, the Parties shall negotiate an equitable price adjustment in good faith.

## 5. ORDERING AND DELIVERY

5.1 **Purchase Orders.** Stabind shall order Products by Purchase Orders stating the Product, quantity, Lot requirements (if any), delivery date and delivery location. Kogein shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within three business days after receipt. A Purchase Order consistent with the most recent Forecast and this Agreement may not be rejected.

5.2 **Delivery.** Kogein shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the delivery location stated in the applicable Purchase Order. Products shall be shipped in nitrogen-flushed or vacuum-sealed foil packaging and maintained at or below 40°F throughout transit.

5.3 **Service Levels.** Kogein shall meet the service levels, including the On-Time Delivery service level, set out in the Service Level Schedule. Kogein shall deliver a Performance Report to Stabind each calendar quarter as provided in the Service Level Schedule. The remedies for failure to meet the service levels, including remedies for a Chronic Failure, are set out in the Service Level Schedule and are in addition to Stabind's other rights under this Agreement.

5.4 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Stabind on delivery at the delivery location.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Prices.** During the Initial Term, Stabind shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit B. Pricing during any Renewal Term is determined under Section 2.2.

6.2 **Invoices.** Kogein shall invoice Stabind on shipment of each Lot. Invoices shall reference the applicable Purchase Order and Lot numbers.

6.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within 45 days after receipt. Stabind may withhold any amount disputed in good faith by notifying Kogein in writing before the due date, with reasonable detail of the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve disputed amounts within 30 days.

6.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Stabind shall pay if properly invoiced, other than taxes based on Kogein's income.

## 7. QUALITY, TESTING AND INSPECTION

7.1 **Quality Systems.** Kogein shall maintain food safety and quality systems consistent with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's Current Good Manufacturing Practice requirements and with industry standards for hop processing. Kogein shall maintain Lot traceability from field to shipment.

7.2 **Inspection and Rejection.** Stabind may inspect and test each Lot within 30 days after delivery. Stabind may reject any Lot that does not conform to the Specifications by written notice to Kogein. At Stabind's election, Kogein shall promptly replace the rejected Lot at no charge or credit Stabind for its price, and shall bear the cost of return or disposal.

7.3 **Audits.** On 10 business days' notice, and not more than twice per Contract Year (unless a contamination event has occurred), Stabind may audit Kogein's facilities, records and quality systems relating to the Products.

7.4 **Contamination Notice.** Kogein shall notify Stabind within 24 hours after becoming aware of any actual or suspected contamination, adulteration or nonconformity affecting any Lot supplied or to be supplied to Stabind. Kogein shall cooperate fully with any investigation, recall or market withdrawal.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Kogein Warranties.** Kogein warrants that each Product at delivery and for twelve months after delivery (when stored under the conditions in Exhibit A): (a) conforms to the Specifications; (b) is free from contamination, adulteration, foreign material and pesticide residue above applicable legal limits; (c) has been grown, processed, packaged and stored in compliance with applicable law; and (d) is delivered with good title, free of liens.

8.2 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized, has authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a binding obligation enforceable against it.

8.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Section 8, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

9.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by a Party under this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Stabind's Confidential Information includes the Blend Specifications, Forecasts, Purchase Orders and ordering history.

9.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care. The receiving Party may disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 9.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, after giving prompt notice to the other Party where lawful.

9.4 **Data Processing.** The Parties' respective obligations for any personal data processed in connection with this Agreement are set out in the Data Processing Addendum.

9.5 **Return.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information on request, except copies retained under a routine backup system or as required by law.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Kogein.** Kogein shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Stabind and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from: (a) any Product that is contaminated, adulterated or does not conform to the Specifications; (b) Kogein's breach of this Agreement; or (c) Kogein's negligence or willful misconduct.

10.2 **By Stabind.** Stabind shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Kogein and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from: (a) Stabind's breach of this Agreement; or (b) Stabind's negligence or willful misconduct.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall notify the indemnifying Party promptly of any claim, give it sole control of the defense and settlement (except that no settlement may impose any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Stabind in the prior 12 months, measured from the date of the event giving rise to the claim.

11.2 **Exclusions from Cap.** The limitation in Section 11.1 does not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 9;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Product Contamination Losses. Product Contamination Losses are excluded from the liability cap.

11.3 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except for claims described in Section 11.2, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 During the Term and for two years afterward, Kogein shall maintain with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; (b) product contamination and recall insurance of at least $3,000,000 per occurrence; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Kogein shall name Stabind as an additional insured on the policies described in clause (a) and provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

13.3 **Service Level Termination.** Stabind may terminate this Agreement for a Chronic Failure as provided in the Service Level Schedule.

13.4 **Effect.** On termination, Stabind shall pay for Products delivered and accepted before the termination date. Kogein shall complete accepted Purchase Orders if Stabind so requests. The Minimum Annual Volume does not apply to any period after termination by Stabind under Section 13.1, 13.2 or 13.3.

13.5 **Survival.** Sections 4.2, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13.4, 13.5, 16 and 17, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, drought, hail, wildfire, epidemic, war, governmental action or failure of public utilities (a "**Force Majeure Event**"). The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of the Force Majeure Event.

14.2 A crop failure is a Force Majeure Event only to the extent it results from a regional weather or pest event affecting hop growers in the Yakima Valley generally. During any such shortfall, Kogein shall allocate available hops to Stabind no less favorably than to any other customer.

14.3 If a Force Majeure Event prevents Kogein from supplying Products for more than 60 days, Stabind may buy substitute products from third parties during the period of non-supply. Those quantities count toward the Minimum Annual Volume. If the Force Majeure Event continues for more than 120 days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

15.2 **Assignment.** Stabind may not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Kogein's prior written consent. Kogein may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Kogein Group Member on written notice to Stabind. Any other assignment by Kogein requires Stabind's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.
