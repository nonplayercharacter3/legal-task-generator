**Re: Hop Supply Agreement between Stabind Brewing Company, Inc. and Kogein Hop Farms, LLC dated as of February 1, 2025, including Schedule 1 (Service Level Schedule)**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered under the Hop Supply Agreement between Stabind Brewing Company, Inc. ("**Stabind**") and Kogein Hop Farms, LLC ("**Kogein**") dated as of February 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It records the Parties' agreement on the matters set out below.

## 1. Definitions and Background

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement, including the Service Level Schedule.

1.2 **Background.** The Parties acknowledge the following:

(a) On July 8, 2026, Kogein delivered to Stabind its Performance Report for the calendar quarter ended June 30, 2026 (the "**Q2 2026 Performance Report**").

(b) The Q2 2026 Performance Report showed On-Time Delivery of 88% for the calendar quarter ended March 31, 2026 ("**Q1 2026**") and 89% for the calendar quarter ended June 30, 2026 ("**Q2 2026**").

(c) On August 28, 2026, Stabind delivered to Kogein a written notice (the "**Election Notice**"). The Election Notice stated that a Chronic Failure had occurred for Q1 2026 and Q2 2026. It also stated Stabind's election under Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule to reduce the Minimum Annual Volume.

(d) Kogein has notified Stabind that it considers the Election Notice untimely under Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule. Stabind does not accept Kogein's position.

(e) The Parties have met to discuss Kogein's On-Time Delivery performance, the 2026 harvest and the other matters addressed in this Side Letter.

## 2. Chronic Failure Election; Reservation of Rights

2.1 **Positions Reserved.** The Parties have not resolved whether the Election Notice was timely or whether it was effective to reduce the Minimum Annual Volume. Each Party reserves all of its rights, remedies, claims and defenses under the Agreement and applicable law regarding the Election Notice, the Chronic Failure for Q1 2026 and Q2 2026, and the Minimum Annual Volume for the Contract Year beginning February 1, 2026 and later Contract Years.

2.2 **No Waiver.** Neither this Side Letter nor any discussion between the Parties about the Election Notice is a waiver by either Party of any right or position, or an admission by either Party. Section 17.3 of the Agreement continues to apply.

2.3 **Invoicing Pending Resolution.** Until the Parties resolve the matters in Section 2.1 by written agreement or final judgment, Kogein shall not issue any shortfall invoice under Section 3.2 of the Agreement for the Contract Year beginning February 1, 2026. This restriction ends 60 days after that resolution. Kogein's right to issue that invoice within the 60-day period is preserved.

## 3. Amendment to Section 11.2 of the Agreement

3.1 **Amendment.** Section 11.2(d) of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "(d) Product Contamination Losses, to the extent arising from Kogein's willful misconduct."

3.2 **Effective Date of Amendment.** The amendment in Section 3.1 takes effect on the date of this Side Letter.

## 4. On-Time Delivery Remediation Plan

4.1 **Remediation Plan.** Within 15 business days after the date of this Side Letter, Kogein shall deliver to Stabind a written remediation plan. The plan must describe the steps Kogein will take to restore On-Time Delivery to the service level required by the Service Level Schedule, including:

(a) cold-storage capacity at Kogein's Moxee facility;

(b) carrier allocation for deliveries to Stabind's Rochester brewery; and

(c) staffing of Kogein's pelletizing lines during the post-harvest processing period.

4.2 **Progress Meetings.** Until On-Time Delivery has met the required service level for two consecutive calendar quarters, the Parties' operations representatives shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least once each month to review progress under the remediation plan.

4.3 **No Effect on Service Levels.** The remediation plan does not modify the service levels, measurement methods, Performance Report obligations or remedies in the Service Level Schedule.

## 5. General

5.1 **Agreement Otherwise Unchanged.** Except as expressly amended by Section 3, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. This Side Letter is a signed side letter and an amendment for purposes of Sections 17.3 and 17.4 of the Agreement.

5.2 **Confidentiality.** This Side Letter and its terms are Confidential Information of each Party under Section 9 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this Side Letter.

5.4 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
