# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Addendum Effective Date**") by and between **Ludis Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sovex Seed Distribution, LLC**, an Iowa limited liability company ("**Customer**"). It supplements the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**RECITALS**

A. Under Section 9.1 of the Agreement, Customer may share Grower Data with Supplier to support agronomic services, product performance analysis and stewardship programs, and Supplier will process Grower Data only in accordance with this Addendum.

B. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will receive, use, protect and dispose of Grower Data.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Aggregated Data**" means Grower Data that has been combined with data from other sources and de-identified so that it cannot reasonably be used to identify any Grower, any individual or any specific field, and that Supplier has publicly committed not to re-identify.

1.2 "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations applicable to the processing of Grower Data under this Addendum, including the Iowa Consumer Data Protection Act (Iowa Code chapter 715D), the Nebraska Data Privacy Act, and applicable state data breach notification laws, each as amended from time to time.

1.3 "**Grower Data**" means all data relating to a Grower or a Grower's farming operation that Customer, or a Grower at Customer's direction, provides or makes available to Supplier in connection with the Agreement. It includes (a) Grower names, contact details, account numbers and other personal information; (b) field locations, field boundaries and legal land descriptions; (c) planting data, including planting dates, seeding rates, hybrid placement and population; (d) soil, fertility, tillage and crop protection data; (e) stand counts, emergence observations and scouting reports; and (f) harvest and yield data, including yield monitor and moisture data.

1.4 "**Permitted Purposes**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

1.5 "**Personnel**" means the employees, officers and individual contractors of Supplier and its Affiliates.

1.6 "**Process**" or "**processing**" means any operation performed on Grower Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

1.7 "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Grower Data in Supplier's possession or control, or in the possession or control of any Subprocessor.

1.8 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Supplier, other than its Personnel, that processes Grower Data on Supplier's behalf.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Scope.** This Addendum applies to all processing of Grower Data by or for Supplier in connection with the Agreement, from the Addendum Effective Date until Supplier has returned or destroyed all Grower Data under Section 11.

2.2 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Customer controls the Grower Data it shares, and Supplier processes Grower Data on Customer's behalf and on its documented instructions. To the extent Applicable Data Protection Law treats any personal information within Grower Data as personal data, Customer is the controller and Supplier is the processor of that personal data.

2.3 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, Grower Data remains the property of Customer or the relevant Grower, as applicable. Nothing in this Addendum or the Agreement grants Supplier any ownership interest in Grower Data.

2.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer is responsible for obtaining the Grower consents required under Section 9.2 of the Agreement and for the accuracy of the instructions it gives Supplier.

## 3. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Supplier will process Grower Data only on Customer's documented instructions, which are set out in this Addendum and the Agreement and in any further written instructions Customer reasonably gives. Supplier will promptly notify Customer if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction would violate Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 **Compliance.** Supplier will comply with Applicable Data Protection Law in its processing of Grower Data and will provide the same level of privacy protection as is required of processors under that law.

3.3 **No Sale.** Supplier will not sell, rent, license or otherwise disclose Grower Data to any third party for monetary or other valuable consideration, and will not use Grower Data for targeted advertising to Growers.

3.4 **No Competitive Use.** Supplier will not use Grower Data to solicit Growers for direct sales, to set Grower-specific prices, or to support any commodity trading or speculation.

## 4. PERMITTED PURPOSES

4.1 **Permitted Purposes.** Supplier may process Grower Data only for the following purposes (the "**Permitted Purposes**"):

(a) providing agronomic services and hybrid placement recommendations to Customer and Growers;

(b) analyzing the performance of Products, including emergence, stand and yield;

(c) investigating and responding to product quality concerns and Grower Claims;

(d) administering Supplier's stewardship programs, including refuge compliance; and

(e) complying with applicable law.

4.2 **Aggregated Data.** Supplier may create Aggregated Data from Grower Data and may use Aggregated Data for its internal research and product development. Supplier will not disclose Aggregated Data to any third party in a form that identifies Customer.

## 5. PERSONNEL

5.1 Supplier will limit access to Grower Data to Personnel who need access for the Permitted Purposes.

5.2 Supplier will ensure that all Personnel with access to Grower Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and have received appropriate training on data protection and security.

## 6. SECURITY

6.1 **Security Measures.** Supplier will implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Grower Data against Security Incidents, appropriate to the nature of the Grower Data. Those safeguards will include at a minimum:

(a) encryption of Grower Data in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption;

(b) role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication for systems that store Grower Data;

(c) logging and monitoring of access to Grower Data;

(d) regular vulnerability scanning and patching of systems that store Grower Data; and

(e) business continuity and backup procedures.

6.2 **Review.** Supplier will review its safeguards at least annually and will not materially reduce the overall level of protection they provide during the term of this Addendum.

## 7. SUBPROCESSORS

7.1 **Authorization.** Customer authorizes Supplier to engage Subprocessors to process Grower Data for the Permitted Purposes, subject to this Section 7. Supplier's current Subprocessors as of the Addendum Effective Date are listed in Annex 1.

7.2 **New Subprocessors.** Supplier will give Customer at least thirty (30) days' written notice before engaging any new Subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period, in which case the Parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, Supplier will not use the new Subprocessor to process Grower Data.

7.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Supplier will enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum. Supplier remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of those obligations.

7.4 **Location.** Supplier will not process Grower Data, or permit any Subprocessor to process Grower Data, outside the United States without Customer's prior written consent.

## 8. SECURITY INCIDENTS

8.1 **Notice.** Supplier will notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

8.2 **Content.** The notice will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate volume of Grower Data affected, the Growers affected, and the steps Supplier has taken or proposes to take to contain and remediate it. Supplier will update the notice as further information becomes available.

8.3 **Cooperation.** Supplier will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident and will cooperate with Customer in meeting any notification obligations to Growers or regulators under Applicable Data Protection Law. Supplier will not notify any Grower of a Security Incident without first consulting Customer, unless required by law.

8.4 **Costs.** Supplier will bear the reasonable costs of notifying affected Growers and regulators, and of credit monitoring where required by law, to the extent a Security Incident results from Supplier's or its Subprocessor's breach of this Addendum.

## 9. GROWER REQUESTS AND ASSISTANCE

9.1 **Grower Requests.** If Supplier receives a request from a Grower to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of Grower Data, Supplier will promptly forward the request to Customer and will not respond to it directly except as Customer instructs or as required by law.

9.2 **Assistance.** Supplier will provide reasonable assistance to Customer in responding to Grower requests and in meeting Customer's obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law, including any data protection assessments.

9.3 **Government Requests.** If Supplier receives a subpoena, court order or other governmental request for Grower Data, Supplier will, where lawful, notify Customer promptly so that Customer may seek a protective order, and will disclose only the Grower Data it is legally required to disclose.

## 10. RECORDS AND AUDIT

10.1 **Information.** Supplier will make available to Customer, on reasonable written request, the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this Addendum, including any third-party security audit reports or certifications Supplier holds.

10.2 **Audit.** No more than once in any twelve (12) month period, and additionally after any Security Incident, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations may audit Supplier's compliance with this Addendum, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, during normal business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Supplier's operations. Each Party bears its own costs of the audit.

## 11. RETURN AND DELETION

11.1 **On Request.** On Customer's written request, Supplier will delete specified Grower Data, including all Grower Data relating to a particular Grower, within thirty (30) days.

11.2 **On Termination.** Within sixty (60) days after expiration or termination of the Agreement, Supplier will, at Customer's election, return all Grower Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format or securely destroy it, and will certify the destruction in writing on request.

11.3 **Retention.** Supplier may retain Grower Data to the extent required by law, and may retain Aggregated Data, provided that any retained Grower Data remains subject to this Addendum for as long as it is retained.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Addendum, and any dispute arising out of or relating to this Addendum, the Agreement or any processing of Grower Data, is governed by the laws of the State of Iowa, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

12.2 Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Polk County, Iowa, for any such dispute, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 13. TERM AND GENERAL

13.1 **Term.** This Addendum takes effect on the Addendum Effective Date and continues for as long as Supplier or any Subprocessor processes Grower Data.

13.2 **Liability.** Each Party's liability under this Addendum is subject to Section 12 of the Agreement.

13.3 **Survival.** Sections 2.3, 3.3, 3.4, 8, 9.3, 11 and 12 survive expiration or termination of the Agreement.

13.4 **Amendment.** This Addendum may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties will negotiate in good faith any amendment reasonably required to comply with changes in Applicable Data Protection Law.

13.5 **Notices.** Notices under this Addendum will be given in accordance with Section 18.1 of the Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents must also be sent by email to legal@sovexseed.com.

13.6 **Counterparts.** This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
