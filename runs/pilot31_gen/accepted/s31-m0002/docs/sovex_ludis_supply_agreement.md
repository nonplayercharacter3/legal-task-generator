# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Ludis Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sovex Seed Distribution, LLC**, an Iowa limited liability company ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Supplier develops, produces and sells proprietary hybrid corn seed.

B. Customer is a regional seed distributor that resells seed to growers.

C. Customer wishes to buy, and Supplier wishes to sell, Supplier's proprietary hybrid corn seed for resale by Customer to growers, on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Forms entered into under it.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below. Other terms are defined where they first appear.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum entered into between the Parties covering Grower Data, as amended from time to time.

1.3 "**Delivery Date**" means, for any shipment of Products, the date on which the Products are delivered to the Delivery Location stated in the applicable Order Form.

1.4 "**Fees**" means the amounts payable by Customer for Products under this Agreement and the Order Forms.

1.5 "**Grower**" means any farmer, grower or other end user that buys or receives Products from or through Customer.

1.6 "**Grower Claims**" means any claim, demand, suit or proceeding brought by a Grower relating to the crop stand, emergence, yield or performance of Products, whatever the cause.

1.7 "**Grower Data**" has the meaning given in the Data Processing Addendum.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means a written order form, signed by both Parties, that refers to this Agreement and states the Products, quantities, prices, Delivery Dates, Delivery Location, Territory and season to which it applies.

1.9 "**Products**" means the varieties of Supplier's proprietary hybrid corn seed listed in Exhibit A to the applicable Order Form, including seed treated with any seed treatment stated in that Exhibit A.

1.10 "**Season**" means the planting season stated in an Order Form.

1.11 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product stated in the applicable Order Form and on the Product tag, including the minimum warm germination rate, purity and seed treatment.

1.12 "**Territory**" means the geographic area for resale of Products stated in the applicable Order Form.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until December 31, 2028 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive three (3) year Renewal Terms (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party not more than 150 days and not less than 120 days before the end of the then-current Term.

2.3 **Effect on Order Forms.** Any Order Form in effect at the expiration or termination of this Agreement will continue to be governed by this Agreement until the Products under that Order Form have been delivered and paid for, unless the Order Form is terminated under Section 15.

## 3. ORDER FORMS

3.1 **Ordering.** Customer will purchase Products by entering into an Order Form for each Season. Each Order Form is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement.

3.2 **Forecasts.** By May 1 of each calendar year, Customer will give Supplier a non-binding forecast of its expected Product requirements for the following Season.

3.3 **Acceptance.** An Order Form is binding when signed by both Parties. Supplier will not unreasonably refuse to sign an Order Form that is consistent with Customer's most recent forecast and the then-current price list.

3.4 **Changes.** Customer may request changes to quantities or varieties in a signed Order Form by written notice. Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate the change, subject to seed availability.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Exclusive Purchasing.** During the Term, Customer shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, purchase, sell or distribute any corn, soybean or sorghum seed from or on behalf of any person other than Supplier anywhere in the United States and Canada.

4.2 **Supplier Commitment.** During the Term, Supplier will not appoint any other distributor to resell the Products to Growers in the Territory, except for direct sales by Supplier to national accounts identified in the applicable Order Form.

4.3 **Marketing.** Customer will use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and resell the Products to Growers in the Territory, consistent with Supplier's reasonable brand guidelines provided in writing.

## 5. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

5.1 **Minimum Units.** For each Season, Customer will purchase at least the minimum number of units of Products stated in the applicable Order Form (the "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**").

5.2 **Shortfall.** If Customer purchases fewer units than the Minimum Purchase Commitment for a Season, Customer will pay Supplier, within thirty (30) days after invoice, a shortfall fee equal to the per-unit shortfall rate stated in the Order Form multiplied by the number of units of the shortfall.

5.3 **Adjustment for Supply Failure.** The Minimum Purchase Commitment for a Season will be reduced unit for unit to the extent Supplier fails to deliver ordered Products or delivers Products that are rejected under Section 8.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Prices.** Customer will pay the prices stated in the applicable Order Form. Supplier may change its price list for a future Season by giving Customer written notice at least ninety (90) days before the Order Form deadline for that Season.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier will invoice Customer on the Delivery Date for each shipment.

6.3 **Payment Terms.** Customer will pay undisputed invoices within the payment terms stated in the Order Form, or, if none are stated, within thirty (30) days after receipt of invoice. Payment is to be made in U.S. dollars by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

6.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving Supplier written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

6.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

6.6 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Customer will pay those taxes or provide a valid resale or exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

6.7 **Returns.** Customer may return unopened, undamaged Products in their original packaging for credit in accordance with the return policy stated in the applicable Order Form.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Delivery.** Supplier will deliver Products FCA (Incoterms 2020) Supplier's designated facility, for carriage to the delivery location stated in the applicable Order Form (the "**Delivery Location**"), on or before the Delivery Dates stated in that Order Form.

7.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier will notify Customer promptly of any expected delay. If Supplier fails to deliver any Products within ten (10) days after the scheduled Delivery Date, Customer may cancel the affected portion of the Order Form without liability.

7.3 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer on the Delivery Date.

7.4 **Storage and Handling.** Customer will store and handle Products in accordance with the storage and handling instructions on the Product tag and in Supplier's written stewardship guidelines.

## 8. SPECIFICATIONS, INSPECTION AND WARRANTY

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, on the Delivery Date, Products will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be labeled in compliance with all applicable federal and state seed laws; and (c) be free of any lien or encumbrance.

8.2 **Testing.** Supplier will test each lot of Products for warm germination and purity before shipment and will provide a certificate of analysis with each shipment.

8.3 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test Products after delivery, including by warm germination and cold germination testing at an accredited seed laboratory.

8.4 **Germination Claims.** Customer must give Supplier written notice of any claim that Products fail to meet the germination rate in the Specifications within 21 days after the Delivery Date, with reasonable supporting detail, including any laboratory results. If Customer does not give notice within that period, the Products are deemed accepted as to germination.

8.5 **Other Nonconformities.** Customer must give Supplier written notice of any visible damage, shortage or labeling nonconformity within ten (10) days after the Delivery Date.

8.6 **Remedies.** For any Products that fail to conform to the warranty in Section 8.1 and that are noticed under Section 8.4 or 8.5, Supplier will, at its option, (a) replace the nonconforming Products at no cost to Customer, or (b) issue a credit or refund of the Fees paid for them. Supplier will bear the cost of returning or disposing of nonconforming Products.

8.7 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 8, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. SUPPLIER DOES NOT WARRANT ANY PARTICULAR CROP YIELD, WHICH DEPENDS ON CONDITIONS OUTSIDE SUPPLIER'S CONTROL.

8.8 **Customer Statements to Growers.** Customer will not make any warranty or representation to Growers about the Products beyond those on the Product tag and in Supplier's published product literature.

## 9. GROWER DATA

9.1 **Sharing of Grower Data.** Customer may share Grower Data with Supplier to support agronomic services, product performance analysis and stewardship programs. Supplier will process Grower Data only in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum.

9.2 **Grower Consents.** Customer will obtain any Grower consents required by applicable law for the sharing of Grower Data with Supplier.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND STEWARDSHIP

10.1 **Ownership.** Supplier owns all right, title and interest in and to the Products' germplasm, genetics, traits, variety names and associated trademarks. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any of those rights to Customer.

10.2 **Limited License.** Supplier grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks and product literature solely to market and resell Products in the Territory.

10.3 **Grower Agreements.** Customer will ensure that each Grower receiving Products is bound by Supplier's standard limited use license as printed on the Product tag or bag, which prohibits saving, replanting, breeding with or reverse engineering the seed.

10.4 **Stewardship.** Customer will comply with Supplier's reasonable written stewardship requirements, including any refuge requirements and seed treatment handling requirements applicable to the Products.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier will defend Customer and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, against any third-party claim alleging that the Products, or Customer's resale of Products in accordance with this Agreement, infringe or misappropriate any patent, plant variety protection certificate, trademark or trade secret, and will indemnify them against any damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded or agreed in settlement in respect of that claim.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer will defend Supplier and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, against any claim, demand, suit or proceeding arising out of or relating to Customer's use, handling, storage, resale or planting of Products, including any Grower Claims, and will indemnify and hold them harmless from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from such claims.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, except that delay will relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced; (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, except that the indemnifying Party may not enter into any settlement that imposes any obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without its prior written consent; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own cost.

11.4 **Infringement Remedies.** If the Products become, or in Supplier's reasonable opinion are likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Supplier may, at its option, procure the right for Customer to continue reselling them, modify or replace them with non-infringing varieties of equivalent performance, or, if neither is commercially reasonable, accept return of the affected Products and refund the Fees paid for them.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Cap.** Subject to Section 12.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, will not exceed the Fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the liability.

12.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Subject to Section 12.3, neither Party will be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits or revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of those damages.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's obligations under Section 11;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 14;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Customer's obligation to pay Fees and any shortfall fee under Section 5.2.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 **Required Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, each Party will maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best, (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations liability coverage, with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, and (b) any workers' compensation insurance required by law.

13.2 **Additional Insured.** Customer will name Supplier as an additional insured under its commercial general liability policy, and Supplier will name Customer as an additional insured under its products liability coverage, in each case as respects the Products.

13.3 **Certificates.** Each Party will deliver a certificate of insurance evidencing the coverage required by Section 13.1 within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date and on each policy renewal. Each Party will give the other at least thirty (30) days' written notice of cancellation or material reduction of the required coverage.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including pricing, forecasts and the terms of this Agreement. Grower Data is governed by the Data Processing Addendum.

14.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party will use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and will protect it with at least reasonable care. The receiving Party may disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14.

14.3 **Exceptions.** This Section 14 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. The receiving Party may disclose information required by law or court order, after giving the disclosing Party prompt notice where lawful.

14.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue for three (3) years after the end of the Term, and for trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

15.2 **For Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Regulatory Change.** Either Party may terminate an affected Order Form on written notice if a change in law or a regulatory action prohibits the sale of a Product in the Territory.

15.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) Customer will pay all undisputed Fees for Products delivered; (b) Customer may sell off Products in its inventory for ninety (90) days, subject to Sections 10.3 and 10.4; and (c) each Party will return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, subject to the Data Processing Addendum as to Grower Data.

15.5 **Survival.** Sections 1, 6 (as to amounts accrued), 8.7, 10.1, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15.4, 15.5, 18 and 19 survive expiration or termination.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, drought, flood, crop disease, fire, epidemic, war, labor disputes (other than those of the affected Party's own workforce), governmental action or failure of carriers. The affected Party will notify the other promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If the event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either Party may terminate the affected Order Form by written notice.

16.2 If a force majeure event or crop shortfall limits Supplier's available supply of any Product, Supplier will allocate available supply among its customers fairly and on a non-discriminatory basis.

## 17. COMPLIANCE

17.1 Each Party will comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement, including federal and state seed laws, the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act as it applies to seed treatments, and applicable export control and anti-corruption laws.

17.2 Each Party will maintain accurate records relating to the Products, including lot numbers and sales records, for at least three (3) years and will cooperate in any product recall or regulatory inquiry.
