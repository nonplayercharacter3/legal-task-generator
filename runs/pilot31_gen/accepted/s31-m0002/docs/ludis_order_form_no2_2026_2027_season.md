# ORDER FORM NO. 2

**2026-2027 SEASON**

This Order Form No. 2 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of April 20, 2026 (the "**Order Form Date**") by and between **Ludis Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sovex Seed Distribution, LLC**, an Iowa limited liability company ("**Customer**"). It is entered into under the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**RECITALS**

A. Under Section 3.1 of the Agreement, Customer purchases Products by entering into an Order Form for each Season.

B. The Parties entered into Order Form No. 1 for the 2026 Season. The Delivery Date for the Products shipped under Order Form No. 1 was February 20, 2026. By letter dated March 30, 2026, Customer gave Supplier written notice under Section 8.4 of the Agreement that certain lots of Products delivered under Order Form No. 1 failed to meet the germination rate in the Specifications, with supporting laboratory results (the "**March 30 Claim**"). By letter dated April 3, 2026, Supplier rejected the notice as untimely.

C. The Parties now wish to set out the Products, quantities, prices and other terms for the 2026-2027 Season.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. SEASON

1.1 This Order Form applies to the 2026-2027 Season, covering Products delivered for planting in the spring of 2027 (the "**2026-2027 Season**").

1.2 This Order Form is incorporated into and governed by the Agreement under Section 3.1 of the Agreement.

## 2. PRODUCTS AND TERRITORY

2.1 **Products.** The Products under this Order Form are the varieties of Supplier's proprietary hybrid corn seed listed in Exhibit A, with the seed treatments stated in Exhibit A.

2.2 **Territory.** The Territory for resale of Products under this Order Form is Iowa and Nebraska.

2.3 **National Accounts.** For purposes of Section 4.2 of the Agreement, Supplier's national accounts for the 2026-2027 Season are: none.

## 3. QUANTITIES AND MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Ordered Quantities.** Customer orders the quantities of each Product stated in Exhibit A, totaling 48,000 units. One unit is one 80,000-kernel bag or its equivalent in bulk or ProBox packaging.

3.2 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** The Minimum Purchase Commitment for the 2026-2027 Season under Section 5.1 of the Agreement is 40,000 units.

3.3 **Shortfall Rate.** The per-unit shortfall rate for purposes of Section 5.2 of the Agreement is $42.00 per unit.

## 4. PRICES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** The per-unit prices for each Product are stated in Exhibit A. Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and exclude taxes, as provided in Section 6.6 of the Agreement.

4.2 **Early Order Discount.** Customer will receive a discount of four percent (4%) off the Exhibit A prices for all units ordered in this Order Form, reflecting placement of this order before June 1, 2026.

4.3 **Payment Terms.** Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days after receipt of invoice. Invoices will be issued on each Delivery Date under Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

## 5. DELIVERY

5.1 **Delivery Location.** The Delivery Location is Customer's warehouse at 2140 Industrial Parkway, Ames, Iowa 50010, or another location in the Territory that Customer designates in writing at least fourteen (14) days before the applicable Delivery Date.

5.2 **Delivery Dates.** Supplier will deliver Products in the following tranches:

| Tranche | Scheduled Delivery Date | Units |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | January 15, 2027 | 20,000 |
| 2 | February 12, 2027 | 18,000 |
| 3 | March 5, 2027 | 10,000 |
| **Total** | | **48,000** |

5.3 **Allocation of Varieties.** Customer will give Supplier its allocation of varieties among tranches by written notice no later than November 1, 2026. If Customer does not do so, Supplier will allocate each variety pro rata across the tranches.

## 6. SPECIFICATIONS AND RETURNS

6.1 **Specifications.** The Specifications for each Product include a minimum warm germination rate of ninety-five percent (95%), a minimum physical purity of ninety-nine percent (99%), and the seed treatment stated in Exhibit A, in each case as tested by Supplier under Section 8.2 of the Agreement and stated on the Product tag.

6.2 **Return Policy.** For purposes of Section 6.7 of the Agreement, Customer may return unopened, undamaged Products in their original packaging, up to ten percent (10%) of the total units delivered under this Order Form, by written notice given no later than June 30, 2027. Supplier will issue a credit at the net price paid, less a restocking charge of five percent (5%). Treated Products are returnable only if they remain within the treatment's labeled shelf life on the date of return.

## 7. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

7.1 Customer expressly reserves all of its rights and remedies in respect of the March 30 Claim described in Recital B, including under Sections 8.1 and 8.6 of the Agreement and applicable law.

7.2 Nothing in this Order Form, including Customer's execution of it, its placement of orders under it, or its payment of any invoice under it or under Order Form No. 1, waives, releases or otherwise affects the March 30 Claim.
