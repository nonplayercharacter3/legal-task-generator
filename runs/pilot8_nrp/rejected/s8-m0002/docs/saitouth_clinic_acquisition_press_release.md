# Saitouth Dental Partners Completes Acquisition of Trolind Family Dental's Four New Castle County Clinics

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**WILMINGTON, Delaware, March 3, 2026.** Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC ("Saitouth"), a dental service organization supporting community dental practices in the Mid-Atlantic region, announced today that it has completed its acquisition of substantially all of the assets of Trolind Family Dental, P.A. ("Trolind Family Dental"). Trolind Family Dental operates four general dentistry and orthodontic clinics in New Castle County, Delaware. The transaction closed on March 2, 2026.

## 1. Transaction Overview

Under an asset purchase agreement governed by Delaware law, Saitouth acquired the operations, equipment, clinic leases, patient relationships and goodwill of the four Trolind Family Dental clinics. The clinics provide general dentistry, hygiene and orthodontic services. The acquired clinics are located at:

- 1408 Pennsylvania Avenue, Wilmington, Delaware;
- 220 East Main Street, Newark, Delaware;
- 615 North Broad Street, Middletown, Delaware; and
- 1120 Pulaski Highway, Bear, Delaware.

The parties did not disclose the financial terms of the transaction.

## 2. Continuity of Patient Care

All four clinics remain open and continue to see patients at their current locations and hours. Patients do not need to take any action. Existing appointments, treatment plans and orthodontic treatment in progress will continue as scheduled. Patient records will remain available at each clinic in accordance with applicable privacy law, including HIPAA.

Saitouth offered employment to the dentists, hygienists, orthodontic staff and other team members of the clinics, effective at closing. Patients will continue to be cared for by the clinical teams they know.

## 3. Insurance and Payer Participation

Saitouth is working with Medicaid, the Delaware Children's Health Insurance Program and private dental benefits plans to transition payer enrollment for the clinics. Patients should continue to present their current insurance information at their next visit. Clinic staff will contact patients directly if any change affects their coverage.

## 4. Management Commentary

"Trolind Family Dental has built a strong reputation in New Castle County for family-centered general and orthodontic care," said the Chief Executive Officer of Saitouth Dental Partners. "We are pleased to welcome the Trolind clinical teams. We look forward to supporting them with the operational, technology and administrative resources they need to keep focusing on their patients."

"We chose Saitouth because it shares our commitment to accessible, high-quality care for families in our community," said the President of Trolind Family Dental. "This partnership positions our clinics and our staff for the next chapter of growth."

## 5. About Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC

Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company headquartered at 300 Delaware Avenue, Suite 900, Wilmington, Delaware 19801. Saitouth provides non-clinical business support to affiliated dental practices, including:

- billing and revenue cycle management;
- payer enrollment;
- human resources;
- technology; and
- facilities management.

Clinical decisions remain with licensed dental professionals.

## 6. About Trolind Family Dental

Trolind Family Dental has provided general dentistry, hygiene and orthodontic services to patients across New Castle County, Delaware through clinics in Wilmington, Newark, Middletown and Bear.
