# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and between **Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Trolind Family Dental, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns and operates four dental clinics in New Castle County, Delaware, through which it conducts the Business.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business, and Buyer will assume certain specified liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the IP Assignment, the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.4.

"**Business**" means general dentistry, hygiene and orthodontic services conducted at the Clinics.

"**Clinics**" means the dental clinics operated by Seller at (a) 1408 Pennsylvania Avenue, Wilmington, Delaware; (b) 220 East Main Street, Newark, Delaware; (c) 615 North Broad Street, Middletown, Delaware; and (d) 1120 Pulaski Highway, Bear, Delaware.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Section 4.1 (Organization and Good Standing), Section 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 4.5 (Title to Purchased Assets), Section 4.9 (Intellectual Property) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local government, regulatory or administrative agency, commission or court, including the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, the Delaware Division of Medicaid and Medical Assistance and the Delaware Board of Dentistry and Dental Hygiene.

"**Intellectual Property Assets**" means all trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, domain names, social media accounts, copyrights, software (including Seller's patient-recall and appointment-reminder software), databases and other intellectual property owned by Seller and used in the Business, including the items listed on Schedule 4.9.

"**IP Assignment**" means the intellectual property assignment agreement between Seller, as assignor, and Buyer, as assignee, in respect of the Intellectual Property Assets.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, recoupments, overpayment refunds, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Payer**" means any Governmental Authority health program (including Medicaid and the Delaware Children's Health Insurance Program), any dental benefits plan and any private insurer or third-party administrator that reimburses dental services.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller under Section 2.2(b).

"**Transferred Employees**" means each employee of Seller who accepts Buyer's offer of employment under Section 6.3 and commences employment with Buyer.

"**Transferred Employee Liabilities**" means all liabilities and obligations to or in respect of the Transferred Employees, whether arising before or after the Closing, including accrued and unused paid time off, wage and hour claims, bonus and commission obligations, benefit plan obligations and professional liability (malpractice) tail coverage and related exposure.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $8,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

**2.2 Payment of the Purchase Price.** Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $6,600,000 at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing; and

(b) $1,800,000 by delivery at the Closing of the Seller Note, in the principal amount of $1,800,000, payable on the terms set forth therein.

**2.3 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens, all of Seller's right, title and interest in the assets used in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including: (a) dental chairs, imaging equipment, sterilization equipment, orthodontic equipment, furniture and other tangible personal property located at the Clinics; (b) supplies and inventory; (c) the real property leases for the Clinics listed on Schedule 2.3(c); (d) the contracts listed on Schedule 2.3(d); (e) the Intellectual Property Assets; (f) patient records, charts and radiographs, to the extent transferable under applicable law; (g) telephone numbers and listings; and (h) goodwill of the Business.

**2.4 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (a) liabilities under the assigned contracts and leases arising after the Closing, and (b) the Transferred Employee Liabilities (together, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.5 Excluded Liabilities.** Other than the Assumed Liabilities, Buyer does not assume any liability of Seller, including any liability arising from Seller's billing, coding or claims submission to any Payer for services rendered before the Closing.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Schedule 2.6 and Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on March 2, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale; (b) an assignment and assumption agreement; (c) assignments of the Clinic leases with landlord consents; (d) the Transition Services Agreement; (e) a certificate of good standing of Seller; and (f) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying resolutions authorizing this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.2(a); (b) the executed Seller Note; (c) the assignment and assumption agreement; and (d) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying resolutions authorizing this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a professional association duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations thereunder. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents, any applicable law or any material contract, except as set forth on Schedule 4.3.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** The financial statements of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 delivered to Buyer were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens.

**4.6 Condition of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.7 Real Property.** Schedule 4.7 lists each lease for the Clinics. Each such lease is in full force and effect, and Seller is not in material default thereunder.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists all material contracts of the Business, including all Payer participation agreements. Each is valid and binding, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 4.9 lists all registered and material unregistered Intellectual Property Assets. Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of all Intellectual Property Assets, free and clear of all liens, and has the right to transfer them to Buyer. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any person, and no person is infringing any Intellectual Property Asset.

**4.10 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.10, there is no action, audit or investigation pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.11 Licenses and Permits.** Seller and each dentist and hygienist practicing at the Clinics hold all licenses, permits and registrations (including DEA registrations and radiation equipment registrations) required to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.12 Healthcare Billing and Payer Compliance.** For the six years preceding the Closing Date: (a) all claims submitted by or on behalf of Seller to any Payer were accurate and complete in all material respects, used correct procedure codes, and were supported by appropriate clinical documentation; (b) Seller has complied in all material respects with all Payer participation requirements, the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, the federal False Claims Act, the Delaware Medicaid program rules and all other applicable healthcare laws; (c) Seller has not received any notice of audit, recoupment, overpayment demand or exclusion from any Payer or Governmental Authority, except as set forth on Schedule 4.12; and (d) no credit balances or known overpayments are owed by Seller to any Payer.

**4.13 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.13 lists each employee of Seller with title, compensation, accrued paid time off and date of hire. Seller has complied in all material respects with all applicable employment laws.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, and this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price at the Closing.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets.

**6.2 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, own, operate or provide dental services through any dental practice located within ten miles of any Clinic, or solicit any Transferred Employee or any patient of the Clinics.

**6.3 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to each employee of Seller listed on Schedule 6.3 on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to those in effect immediately before the Closing.

**6.4 Patient Records.** Seller and Buyer shall comply with applicable law, including HIPAA and the Delaware dental records regulations, in the transfer and custody of patient records, and Buyer shall provide Seller reasonable access to such records as required for Seller's defense of any audit or claim.

**6.5 Payer Transition.** Following the Closing, Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in Buyer's enrollment with each Payer and shall promptly remit to Buyer any payment received by Seller for services rendered by Buyer after the Closing.

**6.6 Further Assurances; IP Assignment.** Following the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional instruments as the other Party reasonably requests to give effect to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement. Without limiting the foregoing, Seller shall execute and deliver the IP Assignment and such short-form recordable assignments as are necessary to record the transfer of the Intellectual Property Assets with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any domain name registrar.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to: (a) the accuracy in all material respects of Seller's representations and warranties; (b) Seller's performance in all material respects of its covenants; (c) receipt of the landlord consents listed on Schedule 4.3; and (d) delivery of the items in Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to: (a) the accuracy in all material respects of Buyer's representations and warranties; (b) Buyer's performance in all material respects of its covenants; and (c) delivery of the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, sales, use and similar taxes arising from the transactions contemplated by this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall be responsible for all taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller contained in this Agreement, including the representations and warranties in Section 4.12, shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months following the Closing Date; provided, that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The representations and warranties of Buyer shall survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months following the Closing Date. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim may be made in respect of any representation or warranty after its survival period expires, unless a valid Claim Notice was delivered under Section 9.4 before that expiration.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its affiliates, officers, managers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) the Transferred Employee Liabilities; and

(c) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement or any other Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) *Notice.* A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall deliver written notice of its claim (a "**Claim Notice**") to the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**").

(b) *Contents of Claim Notice.* A Claim Notice shall describe in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim and the provision of this Agreement under which indemnification is sought, and shall include invoices, remittance records or other documentation of Losses actually paid by the Indemnified Party. A Claim Notice that does not satisfy this Section 9.4(b) shall not be valid.

(c) *Third-Party Claims.* The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by notice given within thirty days after receipt of the Claim Notice. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own cost. Neither Party shall settle a third-party claim without the other Party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

(d) *Payment.* The Indemnifying Party shall pay any undisputed amount within thirty days after receipt of a valid Claim Notice. Disputed amounts shall be resolved under Section 11.7.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($840,000) (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Fundamental Representations and Fraud.* The Cap shall not apply to Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of the Fundamental Representations, including Section 4.9, or to fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for such Losses, other than in the case of fraud, shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) *Buyer Cap.* Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3, other than Section 9.3(b), shall not exceed the Cap.

(d) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breach of the Fundamental Representations, until the aggregate Losses exceed $84,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party.

(f) *Mitigation.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses.

**9.6 Set-off.** Buyer may set off amounts owed to it under this Article IX against amounts payable under the Seller Note solely in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under the Ancillary Agreements, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** Indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by March 31, 2026, unless caused by the terminating Party's breach; or (c) by either Party upon a material uncured breach by the other Party.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be of no further force or effect, except Section 6.1, this Section 10.2 and Article XI, and except that no Party is relieved of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier or email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page or such other address as a Party designates by notice.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.5 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to an affiliate or collaterally to its lenders.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware). EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.8 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall control, except that the IP Assignment shall control as to the Assigned IP (as defined in the IP Assignment).

**11.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
