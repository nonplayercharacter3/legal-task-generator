**Re: Proposed Acquisition of the Assets of Trolind Family Dental, P.A.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of Trolind Family Dental, P.A., a Delaware professional association ("**Seller**"). The proposed acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**." Except as stated in Paragraph 14, this Letter is not binding on either party and is intended only as a basis for negotiating a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Business and Clinics

Seller currently operates four dental clinics in New Castle County, Delaware. The clinics are located at 1408 Pennsylvania Avenue, Wilmington; 220 East Main Street, Newark; 615 North Broad Street, Middletown; and 1120 Pulaski Highway, Bear (the "**Clinics**"). Through the Clinics, Seller provides general dentistry, hygiene and orthodontic services (the "**Business**"). Buyer's proposal covers the Business as a whole, including the orthodontic practice and its related brands, equipment and patient-recall systems.

## 2. Purchased Assets and Assumed Liabilities

(a) Buyer will acquire, free and clear of all liens other than customary permitted liens, substantially all of Seller's assets used in the Business. These include:

- equipment, including orthodontic equipment, furniture and other tangible personal property;
- supplies and inventory;
- the Clinic leases;
- assigned contracts;
- patient records, to the extent transferable under applicable law;
- telephone numbers;
- goodwill; and
- all intellectual property used in the Business. This includes the TROLIND FAMILY DENTAL and related marks, domain names, social media accounts, and Seller's patient-recall and appointment-reminder software.

(b) Buyer will assume only (i) liabilities under assigned contracts and leases that arise after closing, and (ii) the obligations to transferred employees to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement. All other liabilities remain with Seller. In particular, Seller retains any liability arising from its billing, coding or claims submission to any payer for services rendered before closing.

## 3. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price will be $8,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the assumed liabilities described in Paragraph 2(b). The Purchase Price is based on the financial statements of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025. It assumes the Business is delivered with a customary level of working supplies and no material adverse change before closing.

## 4. Payment of the Purchase Price

The Purchase Price will be paid as follows:

(a) $6,600,000 in cash at closing; and

(b) $1,800,000 by a subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller at closing (the "**Seller Note**"). The Seller Note will bear interest at a fixed rate of six percent (6%) per year, will be payable in equal quarterly installments over three years, and may be prepaid without premium or penalty. The Seller Note will be subordinated to Buyer's senior institutional indebtedness on customary terms. Buyer will have the right to set off amounts owed to it for indemnifiable losses against payments due under the Seller Note, on terms to be set out in the Definitive Agreement and the Seller Note.

## 5. Representations and Warranties

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties customary for an acquisition of a dental practice of this size. These will include representations as to:

- organization;
- authority;
- title to assets;
- financial statements;
- condition of assets;
- leases;
- material contracts and payer participation agreements;
- intellectual property;
- litigation;
- licenses and permits (including DEA and radiation equipment registrations);
- employees; and
- brokers.

They will also include a representation on healthcare billing and payer compliance. That representation will cover claims accuracy, coding, clinical documentation and compliance with Medicaid, the Delaware Children's Health Insurance Program, private payer requirements and applicable federal and state healthcare laws, in each case for a six-year lookback period.

## 6. Survival

Seller's representations and warranties will survive for 18 months after closing. This includes the healthcare billing and payer compliance representations. Seller's representations as to organization, authority, title to assets, intellectual property and brokers (the "**Fundamental Representations**") will survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Buyer's representations and warranties will survive for the same general survival period as Seller's.

## 7. Indemnification

(a) Seller will indemnify Buyer for losses arising from:

- breaches of Seller's representations, warranties and covenants; and
- excluded liabilities, including pre-closing payer recoupments, overpayment refunds, penalties and fines.

(b) Seller's liability for breaches of representations and warranties will be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price ($840,000). This cap will not apply to breaches of the Fundamental Representations or to fraud. Seller's liability for breaches of the Fundamental Representations, other than fraud, will be capped at the Purchase Price.

(c) Seller will not be liable for breaches of representations and warranties, other than the Fundamental Representations, until aggregate losses exceed $84,000. After that threshold, Seller will be liable only for the excess.

(d) Buyer will indemnify Seller for breaches of Buyer's representations, warranties and covenants and for the assumed liabilities. Buyer's indemnity obligations will be subject to a cap on terms to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement.

(e) The indemnification procedures will be customary. A claim notice will describe the claim in reasonable detail. It may be given for losses that have been incurred or are reasonably anticipated, including losses arising from a pending audit or recoupment demand.

## 8. Employees

Buyer will offer employment, effective at closing, to the dentists, hygienists, orthodontic staff and other employees of Seller identified by Buyer. The offers will be on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to their current terms. The treatment of accrued paid time off, wage obligations, benefit plan obligations and professional liability tail coverage will be addressed in the Definitive Agreement.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

For five years after closing, Seller will not, directly or indirectly:

- own, operate or provide dental services through any dental practice within ten miles of any Clinic; or
- solicit any transferred employee or any patient of the Clinics.

## 10. Conditions to Closing

Closing will be subject to the following conditions:

- completion of Buyer's legal, financial, billing and operational due diligence to Buyer's satisfaction;
- negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and related ancillary agreements;
- receipt of landlord consents for the Clinic leases;
- arrangements satisfactory to Buyer for transition of payer enrollment;
- the absence of any material adverse change in the Business; and
- receipt of all required approvals.

## 11. Intellectual Property Transfer

At or after closing, Seller will execute an intellectual property assignment and short-form recordable assignments. These will transfer and record the transfer of all intellectual property used in the Business, including the general dentistry, hygiene and orthodontic brands and the patient-recall software, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and applicable domain name registrars.

## 12. Timing

The parties will use good faith efforts to execute the Definitive Agreement and close the Transaction on or about March 2, 2026.

## 13. Due Diligence and Access

Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the following, subject to applicable privacy law, including HIPAA:

- the Clinics;
- Seller's books, records, payer agreements, audit correspondence and claims data;
- Seller's employees and professional staff; and
- Seller's software and intellectual property documentation.

## 14. Binding Provisions

Only the following provisions of this Letter are legally binding on the parties: this Paragraph 14 and Paragraphs 15, 16, 17 and 18. No other binding obligation will arise with respect to the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time before execution of the Definitive Agreement by written notice to the other.

## 15. Exclusivity

From the date Seller countersigns this Letter until the earlier of (a) March 31, 2026 and (b) termination of negotiations under Paragraph 14, Seller will not, directly or indirectly, solicit, negotiate or accept any proposal for the sale of the Business, the Clinics or any material portion of Seller's assets with any person other than Buyer.

## 16. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the terms of this Letter and all information furnished in connection with the Transaction confidential, except as required by law or as disclosed to its advisors and financing sources who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## 17. Expenses

Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 18. Governing Law

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

If these terms are acceptable, please countersign and return a copy of this Letter. This proposal will expire if not countersigned by 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on January 16, 2026.
