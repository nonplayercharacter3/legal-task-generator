# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$1,800,000**
**Issue Date: March 2, 2026**
**Wilmington, Delaware**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Trolind Family Dental, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association ("**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest set out below, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). March 2, 2026 is the "**Issue Date**" of this Note. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. PRINCIPAL

1.1 **Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $1,800,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount is part of the Purchase Price.

1.2 **Maturity.** The entire unpaid Principal Amount, with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on March 2, 2029 (the "**Maturity Date**"), unless paid earlier under this Note.

## 2. INTEREST

2.1 **Rate.** Interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount from the Issue Date at a fixed rate of six percent (6%) per year, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

2.2 **Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount at a rate of eight percent (8%) per year.

2.3 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any interest paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount.

## 3. PAYMENTS

3.1 **Installments.** Maker shall pay the Principal Amount in twelve (12) equal quarterly installments of $150,000 each, on the second day of each June, September, December and March, beginning June 2, 2026 and ending on the Maturity Date (each, an "**Installment Date**").

3.2 **Interest Payments.** Maker shall pay accrued interest in arrears on each Installment Date.

3.3 **Manner of Payment.** Maker shall make all payments in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing from time to time.

3.4 **Business Days.** If a payment is due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest shall accrue for the additional days. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required to close.

3.5 **Application of Payments.** Payments shall be applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 8.2, then to accrued interest, and then to the Principal Amount in the order of the installments next due.

## 4. PREPAYMENT

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each partial prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued interest on the amount prepaid and shall be applied to the remaining installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. SET-OFF

5.1 **Right of Set-off.** Subject to Section 5.2, Maker may set off against any installment of principal or interest under this Note the amount of any Losses for which Maker is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, as provided in Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.2 **Set-off Notice.** Maker may exercise its right under Section 5.1 only if Maker delivers to Holder a written notice (a "**Set-off Notice**") within one hundred fifty (150) days after the Issue Date. Each Set-off Notice shall state the amount to be set off, identify the installment or installments against which the set-off will be applied, and attach or refer to the Claim Notice delivered under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement in respect of the related Losses.

5.3 **Disputed Amounts.** If Holder disputes an amount stated in a Set-off Notice, Holder shall notify Maker in writing within thirty (30) days after receipt of the Set-off Notice. Maker may withhold the disputed amount until the dispute is resolved by written agreement of Maker and Holder or by a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement. Within ten (10) Business Days after that resolution, Maker shall pay to Holder any withheld amount determined not to be owed to Maker, with interest at the rate in Section 2.1 from the date the amount would otherwise have been due.

5.4 **Effect of Set-off.** Any amount properly set off under this Section 5 reduces the Principal Amount and interest payable under this Note and shall be treated as a payment by Holder of the corresponding indemnification obligation under the Purchase Agreement. Except as provided in this Section 5, Maker's obligation to pay this Note is absolute and unconditional.

## 6. SUBORDINATION

6.1 **Subordination.** The payment of principal and interest under this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Indebtedness. "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owed to any bank, commercial finance lender or other institutional lender (each, a "**Senior Lender**"), including principal, interest, fees and expenses, whether existing on the Issue Date or incurred later.

6.2 **Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under the Senior Indebtedness and the Senior Lender has delivered written notice of it to Holder, Maker may make, and Holder may receive, regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

6.3 **Turnover.** Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 6 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly paid over to it.

6.4 **Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, on request, execute a subordination agreement with any Senior Lender consistent with this Section 6 and on terms customary for seller financing in transactions of this kind.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest within ten (10) Business Days after the date due, other than an amount withheld under Section 5;

(b) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law that, if involuntary, is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due; or

(c) Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets to a person that is not an affiliate of Maker, unless this Note is repaid in full at or before the completion of that transfer.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 **Acceleration.** Subject to Section 6, upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. Upon an Event of Default under Section 7(b), those amounts become due and payable automatically without notice.

8.2 **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in collecting any amount due and unpaid after an Event of Default.

8.3 **Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by this Note.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

9.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

9.2 **Transfer.** Holder may not assign, pledge or transfer this Note without Maker's prior written consent. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without Holder's prior written consent, except to an affiliate of Maker that assumes those obligations in writing, in which case Maker remains liable.

9.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. No delay in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right.

9.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

9.5 **Governing Law.** This Note is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.6 **Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Note as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Note.

9.7 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on identical terms after receiving an affidavit of loss and an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker.

9.8 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed and delivered by electronic signature, which is as effective as an original signature.
