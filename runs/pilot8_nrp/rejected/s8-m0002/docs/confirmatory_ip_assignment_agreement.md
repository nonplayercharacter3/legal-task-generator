# CONFIRMATORY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This CONFIRMATORY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of October 12, 2026 by and between **Trolind Family Dental, P.A.**, a Delaware professional association ("**Assignor**"), and **Saitouth Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as Buyer, are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), under which Assignor sold to Assignee substantially all of the assets used in the operation of Assignor's four dental clinics in New Castle County, Delaware.

B. Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Assignment and such short-form recordable assignments as are necessary to record the transfer of the intellectual property sold under the Purchase Agreement with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any domain name registrar.

C. The Parties are entering into this Assignment to confirm and record the transfer of the Assigned IP to Assignee.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Assignment and the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Assigned IP**" means all intellectual property owned by Assignor and used primarily in the Business, including:

(a) the trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress listed on Schedule A, together with all common-law rights therein, all registrations and applications for registration thereof, and all goodwill of the Business connected with the use thereof and symbolized thereby (the "**Assigned Marks**");

(b) the domain names and social media accounts listed on Schedule B (the "**Assigned Domains**");

(c) the software listed on Schedule C, including Assignor's patient-recall and appointment-reminder software, in source code and object code form, together with all documentation, configurations, templates and data structures relating thereto (the "**Assigned Software**");

(d) all copyrights in website content, patient education materials, marketing materials, forms and other works of authorship, whether registered or unregistered; and

(e) all databases and compilations, including patient-recall lists, to the extent transferable under applicable law.

"**Business**" means general dentistry and hygiene services conducted at the Clinics.

"**Cap**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Clinics**" has the meaning given in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all rights in and to the Assigned IP throughout the world, including all rights to sue for and collect damages and other remedies for past, present and future infringement, dilution, misappropriation or other violation, and all income, royalties and payments now or hereafter due with respect to the Assigned IP.

1.2 **Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

2.1 **Assignment.** Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and confirms its prior sale, assignment, transfer and conveyance to Assignee effective as of the Closing Date, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP and the Intellectual Property Rights, free and clear of all liens.

2.2 **Acceptance.** Assignee hereby accepts the assignment of the Assigned IP and the Intellectual Property Rights.

2.3 **Goodwill.** The assignment of the Assigned Marks includes the goodwill of the Business connected with the use of and symbolized by the Assigned Marks, and Assignor shall not use any Assigned Mark or any mark confusingly similar thereto after the date of this Assignment.

2.4 **Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor waives, and shall cause its employees and contractors who authored any Assigned IP to waive, all moral rights in the Assigned IP.

## 3. CONSIDERATION

The consideration for the assignment in Section 2 is included in the Purchase Price paid under the Purchase Agreement. No additional consideration is payable by Assignee under this Assignment.

## 4. DELIVERY; RECORDATION

4.1 **Delivery of Software and Materials.** Within ten (10) business days after the date of this Assignment, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee (a) complete copies of the source code and object code of the Assigned Software, together with all build instructions, administrator credentials and license keys, and (b) all files, specimens and correspondence relating to the Assigned Marks, in each case to the extent not previously delivered.

4.2 **Domain Names and Accounts.** Within five (5) business days after the date of this Assignment, Assignor shall take all actions required by the applicable registrar or platform to transfer the registrant and administrative control of each Assigned Domain to Assignee or its designee.

4.3 **Recordation.** Assignor authorizes Assignee to record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in the form of Exhibit 1, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office and any other Governmental Authority or registrar. Assignor shall execute any such short-form assignment upon Assignee's request.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES

5.1 **Further Instruments.** Assignor shall, at Assignee's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as are reasonably necessary to vest in Assignee full title to the Assigned IP and to perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights therein.

5.2 **Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, solely to execute and file any document described in Section 4.3 or Section 5.1 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) business days after Assignee's written request.

5.3 **Cooperation in Enforcement.** Assignor shall reasonably cooperate, at Assignee's expense, in any action brought by Assignee to enforce or defend the Assigned IP, including by providing testimony and documents.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

6.1 **Assignor.** Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee that:

(a) Assignor has full power and authority to enter into this Assignment and to perform its obligations hereunder, and this Assignment constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignor;

(b) the representations and warranties in Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement are true and correct as to the Assigned IP as of the date of this Assignment as though made on that date; and

(c) Assignor has not granted any license, option or other right in the Assigned IP to any person that remains in effect, other than as listed on Schedule 4.9 to the Purchase Agreement.

6.2 **Assignee.** Assignee represents and warrants to Assignor that it has full power and authority to enter into this Assignment, and this Assignment constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignee.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Assignor's aggregate liability for any inaccuracy in or breach of its representations and warranties with respect to the Assigned IP, whether under this Assignment or the Purchase Agreement, including Section 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement, shall not exceed the Cap. Claims under this Section 7 shall be made in accordance with the procedures in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Assignment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, except to the extent that the federal laws of the United States govern the assignment of registered trademarks and copyrights.

8.2 **Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Assignment as if set out in full herein, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Assignment.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

9.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Assignment shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

9.2 **Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Assignee may assign its rights under this Assignment to an affiliate, to any acquirer of the Assigned IP or collaterally to its lenders.

9.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

9.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Assignment is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

9.5 **Counterparts.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
