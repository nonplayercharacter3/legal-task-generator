We are writing about the Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 between Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc. ("**Ziteir**") and Lakix Grower Services, LLC ("**Lakix**") (the "**Agreement**"). This letter also concerns the related Data Processing Addendum and Statement of Work No. 001 (Fleet Deployment and Pilot Training). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. The Transaction

Ziteir has entered into a definitive merger agreement with Okonvale Agritech Holdings, Inc. ("**Okonvale**"). Under that agreement, a wholly owned subsidiary of Okonvale will merge with and into Ziteir. Ziteir will survive the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Okonvale, and Okonvale will acquire all of the outstanding equity of Ziteir (the "**Transaction**"). The Transaction is subject to customary closing conditions. The expected closing date is November 30, 2026.

## 2. Effect on the Agreement

Ziteir will remain the same legal entity after closing, and the Agreement will remain with Ziteir. Ziteir will not assign or transfer the Agreement, the Data Processing Addendum or any Statement of Work to Okonvale or any other person. Accordingly, no consent of Lakix is required in connection with the Transaction. This letter is provided for informational purposes only, and Lakix does not need to take any action in response to it.

## 3. Continuity of Supply and Services

After closing, Ziteir will continue to perform all of its obligations under the Agreement in the ordinary course. In particular:

(a) Ziteir will continue to accept and fulfill Purchase Orders for Drones and Spare Parts at the prices set out in Exhibit B to the Agreement.

(b) The ZiteirLink Platform will continue to operate under the service levels in Exhibit A to the Agreement, and existing subscriptions will remain in effect.

(c) Deployment, commissioning and pilot training under Statement of Work No. 001 will continue on the existing schedule, using the same Ziteir project team.

(d) Ziteir will continue to process Grower Data in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum. The Sub-processors listed in Annex 3 to the Data Processing Addendum will not change as a result of the Transaction. If any change to Sub-processors is proposed after closing, Ziteir will give notice in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Data Processing Addendum.
