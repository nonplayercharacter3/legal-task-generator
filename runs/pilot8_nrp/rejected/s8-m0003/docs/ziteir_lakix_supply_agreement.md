# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**" or "**Supply Agreement Effective Date**") between:

**Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Sorrel Ridge Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Ziteir**" or "**Supplier**"); and

**Lakix Grower Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1275 Harvest Mill Road, Dover, Delaware 19901 ("**Lakix**" or "**Customer**").

Ziteir and Lakix are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Ziteir designs and manufactures autonomous agricultural spray drones. It also operates a cloud-based fleet telemetry platform and provides related deployment and training services.

B. Lakix provides agronomy and application services to growers. It wishes to buy drones and subscribe to the telemetry platform for use in those services, and to engage Ziteir for deployment and training services.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Ziteir will supply, and Lakix will buy, those Products and Services.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting equity of an entity.

1.2 "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date. The DPA governs Ziteir's processing of Grower Data, as amended from time to time.

1.3 "**Documentation**" means Ziteir's published operator manuals, technical specifications and maintenance guides for the Products.

1.4 "**Drones**" means the ZX-40 autonomous crop-spraying drones described in Exhibit A, including the standard accessories listed there.

1.5 "**Grower Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

1.6 "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

1.7 "**Platform**" means Ziteir's ZiteirLink fleet telemetry platform, including its web and mobile interfaces, application programming interfaces and related hosted services described in Exhibit A.

1.8 "**Products**" means the Drones, Spare Parts and Platform subscriptions listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.9 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Lakix issues under this Agreement and that Ziteir accepts in accordance with Section 3.

1.10 "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

1.11 "**Services**" means the fleet deployment, commissioning, pilot training and related professional services described in a Statement of Work.

1.12 "**Spare Parts**" means the replacement components for the Drones listed in Exhibit A.

1.13 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work for Services that both Parties sign and that references this Agreement, including Statement of Work No. 001 (Fleet Deployment and Pilot Training).

1.14 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with all Renewal Terms.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues for an initial term of two years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date, ending February 28, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive three-year renewal terms (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. ORDERS

3.1 **Purchase Orders.** Lakix will order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Ziteir. Each Purchase Order will state the Products and quantities, the requested delivery dates and the delivery location.

3.2 **Acceptance.** Ziteir will accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five business days after receipt. If Ziteir does not respond within that period, the Purchase Order is deemed accepted. Ziteir will not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with the forecast provided under Section 3.3.

3.3 **Forecasts.** By the first day of each calendar quarter, Lakix will give Ziteir a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated Drone requirements for the following two quarters.

3.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Lakix will pay the prices for Products set out in Exhibit B. Fees for Services are as set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

4.2 **Fixed Pricing.** The prices set out in Exhibit B are fixed for the duration of the Term.

4.3 **Invoicing.** Ziteir will invoice Drones and Spare Parts upon shipment. Platform subscription fees are invoiced annually in advance from the activation date of each Drone on the Platform.

4.4 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within 30 days of receipt, in U.S. dollars, by wire transfer to the account Ziteir designates. Lakix will notify Ziteir in writing of any good-faith dispute within 15 days of receipt of the invoice, and the Parties will work to resolve it promptly. Late undisputed amounts bear interest at 1% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

4.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Lakix is responsible for those taxes, other than taxes based on Ziteir's net income.

## 5. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

5.1 **Delivery.** Ziteir will deliver Products FCA Ziteir's facility in Wilmington, Delaware (Incoterms 2020), unless a Purchase Order specifies a different delivery location that Ziteir accepts.

5.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Drones and Spare Parts pass to Lakix upon delivery. Title to the Platform and all software embedded in the Drones remains with Ziteir and is licensed under Section 7.

5.3 **Late Delivery.** Ziteir will notify Lakix promptly of any expected delay in delivery. If Ziteir delivers any Drone more than 30 days after the delivery date confirmed for it, Lakix may cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability.

## 6. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 Lakix will inspect Drones within ten business days after delivery. Lakix may reject any Drone that does not materially conform to the specifications in Exhibit A by giving written notice that describes the nonconformity.

6.2 Ziteir will repair or replace any rejected Drone at its cost within 20 business days after receipt of a rejection notice. Drones not rejected within the inspection period are deemed accepted, without prejudice to Lakix's rights under Section 9.

## 7. PLATFORM AND SOFTWARE

7.1 **Platform Access.** Subject to payment of the applicable fees, Ziteir grants Lakix a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to access and use the Platform for each subscribed Drone, solely for Lakix's operations and services to its grower clients.

7.2 **Embedded Software.** Ziteir grants Lakix a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the firmware and software embedded in each Drone solely to operate that Drone. The license continues for as long as Lakix or its permitted transferee owns the Drone.

7.3 **Restrictions.** Lakix will not reverse engineer, decompile or modify the Platform or embedded software, except to the extent applicable law permits despite this restriction.

7.4 **Availability.** Ziteir will make the Platform available in accordance with the service levels set out in Exhibit A.

## 8. SERVICES; DATA PROTECTION

8.1 **Services.** Ziteir will perform the Services described in each Statement of Work in a professional and workmanlike manner, using appropriately qualified personnel.

8.2 **Data Protection.** Each Party will comply with the Data Processing Addendum in connection with the processing of Grower Data through the Platform and the Drones.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Ziteir warrants that each Drone and Spare Part will be free from material defects in materials and workmanship. Ziteir also warrants that each will conform in all material respects to the specifications in Exhibit A and the Documentation for 12 months after delivery.

9.2 **Remedy.** If a Drone or Spare Part breaches Section 9.1, Ziteir will repair or replace it, at its option and expense, within 15 business days after notice. If Ziteir cannot repair or replace it, Ziteir will refund the price paid for it.

9.3 **Platform Warranty.** Ziteir warrants that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the Term.

9.4 **Exclusions.** The warranties in this Section 9 do not apply to defects caused by misuse, accident, unauthorized modification, failure to follow the Documentation, or use of chemicals or payloads outside the specifications in Exhibit A.

9.5 **Compliance.** Each Party will comply with all laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement. This includes, in the case of Lakix, Federal Aviation Administration regulations governing the operation of unmanned aircraft and applicable pesticide application laws.

9.6 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, ZITEIR MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. THIS INCLUDES ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Ziteir.** Ziteir will defend Lakix and its Affiliates against any third-party claim alleging that the Products infringe or misappropriate a U.S. patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret. Ziteir will indemnify them against resulting damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

10.2 **By Lakix.** Lakix will defend Ziteir and its Affiliates against any third-party claim arising from Lakix's operation of the Drones in breach of applicable law, or from Lakix's selection or application of chemicals. Lakix will indemnify them against resulting damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will:

(a) give prompt written notice of the claim;

(b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

The indemnifying Party may not settle a claim in a way that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party without that Party's consent.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Cap.** Except for liability under Section 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Lakix in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except for liability under Section 11.3, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits or crop loss, even if it was advised of their possibility.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) Lakix's payment obligations;

(b) a Party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct; or

(c) a Party's obligations under Section 10.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information the other Party discloses that is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential ("**Confidential Information**"). It will use that Confidential Information only to perform under this Agreement.

12.2 These obligations do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was already known to the receiving Party without restriction;

(c) is independently developed; or

(d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

12.3 A Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful. These obligations survive for three years after the end of the Term, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 13. INSURANCE

During the Term, Ziteir will maintain the following insurance with reputable insurers:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products liability, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) aviation liability insurance covering the Drones while Ziteir personnel operate them, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence; and

(c) cyber liability insurance of at least $3,000,000.

Ziteir will provide certificates of insurance upon request.

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

14.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) becomes insolvent;

(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) has a receiver appointed; or

(d) becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within 60 days.

14.3 **Effect.** On expiration or termination:

(a) Ziteir will fulfill accepted Purchase Orders unless Lakix cancels them;

(b) Lakix will pay all amounts due for Products delivered and Services performed;

(c) Platform access will end, subject to the data return provisions of the DPA; and

(d) Sections 5.2, 7.2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14.3 and 18 survive.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the other Party's prior written consent. Any purported assignment without that consent is void.

15.2 **Change of Control.** Any change of control of Supplier, including a merger, consolidation, sale of equity or other transaction in which more than 50% of Supplier's voting equity changes hands, is deemed an assignment of this Agreement. Any such assignment requires Lakix's prior written consent. Failure to obtain that consent is a material breach of this Agreement and entitles Lakix to terminate this Agreement by written notice and to recover its damages.

15.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, severe weather, epidemic, war, terrorism, governmental action, airspace restrictions and failures of public utilities or telecommunications networks.

16.2 The affected Party will notify the other Party promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than 90 days, either Party may terminate the affected Purchase Order or Statement of Work by written notice.

## EXHIBIT A: PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

**A.1 ZX-40 Autonomous Crop-Spraying Drone**

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Spray tank capacity | 40 liters |
| Effective swath width | 7 to 10 meters |
| Maximum takeoff weight | 92 kg |
| Flight time at full payload | 14 minutes (standard battery) |
| Positioning | RTK GNSS, ±2 cm horizontal accuracy |
| Obstacle avoidance | Forward and rear radar, downward terrain-following radar |
| Ingress protection | IP67 (core electronics) |
| Standard accessories | Two flight batteries, one rapid charger, one ground control tablet, one RTK base station per order of five or more Drones |

**A.2 Spare Parts**

Flight batteries, propeller sets, spray nozzle kits, pump assemblies, arm assemblies and radar modules, each as described in the Documentation.

**A.3 ZiteirLink Fleet Telemetry Platform**

The Platform provides:

- real-time flight tracking;
- spray application logs and as-applied maps;
- field boundary and prescription map import;
- fleet maintenance alerts; and
- data export through application programming interfaces.

**Service level:** 99.5% monthly availability, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance. If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, Lakix receives a service credit of 10% of that month's Platform fees for the affected Drones.
