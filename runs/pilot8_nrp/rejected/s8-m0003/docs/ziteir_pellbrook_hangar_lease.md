# HANGAR AND OFFICE LEASE

This Hangar and Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of January 2, 2024 (the "**Lease Date**") between:

**Pellbrook Airfield Properties, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office at 900 Runway View Drive, Middletown, Delaware 19709 ("**Landlord**"); and

**Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Sorrel Ridge Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Tenant**").

Landlord and Tenant are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. BASIC LEASE TERMS

1.1 **Property.** The "**Property**" is the Pellbrook Airfield general aviation facility at 900 Runway View Drive, Middletown, Delaware 19709. It includes the runways, taxiways, aprons, parking areas and common facilities that Landlord makes available to tenants (the "**Common Areas**").

1.2 **Premises.** The "**Premises**" are Hangar 6 at the Property. Hangar 6 consists of approximately 14,200 rentable square feet of hangar floor area and approximately 3,800 rentable square feet of attached office, workshop and storage space, together with the exclusive use of the adjacent apron area of approximately 6,000 square feet shown on Exhibit A.

1.3 **Term.** The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") begins on February 1, 2024 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ends on January 31, 2029 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless extended under Section 3.2 or terminated earlier under this Lease.

1.4 **Base Rent.** Base Rent is payable monthly as follows:

|---|---|---|
| February 1, 2024 to January 31, 2025 | $288,000.00 | $24,000.00 |

| February 1, 2027 to January 31, 2028 | $314,705.40 | $26,225.45 |

1.5 **Tenant's Share.** Tenant's share of Operating Expenses is 11.5%, being the ratio of the rentable area of the Premises to the total rentable area of the hangar and office buildings at the Property.

1.6 **Security Deposit.** $50,000.00.

1.7 **Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for the following purposes (the "**Permitted Use**"):

(a) the storage, assembly, maintenance, repair and testing of unmanned aircraft systems and related equipment;

(b) flight testing of unmanned aircraft in accordance with Section 6;

(c) training of operators and technicians; and

(d) general office use incidental to those activities.

## 2. GRANT

Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term, on the terms of this Lease. Tenant and its employees, contractors and invitees have the non-exclusive right to use the Common Areas, in common with other tenants, subject to the Airfield Rules described in Section 6.2.

## 3. TERM AND EXTENSION

3.1 **Delivery.** Landlord will deliver the Premises to Tenant on the Commencement Date, broom clean, with the hangar door, building systems and roof in good working order, and with Landlord's Work described in Exhibit B substantially complete.

3.2 **Option to Extend.** Provided that Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period, Tenant may extend the Term for one additional period of five years (the "**Extension Term**"). Tenant must exercise this option by written notice to Landlord given no earlier than 12 months and no later than nine months before the Expiration Date. If Tenant does not give that notice, the option lapses and this Lease ends on the Expiration Date.

3.3 **Extension Rent.** Base Rent for the Extension Term will be 95% of the fair market rent for comparable hangar and office space at general aviation airfields in New Castle County, Delaware, as agreed by the Parties. If the Parties cannot agree within 30 days after Tenant's exercise notice, fair market rent will be determined by a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten years' experience in aviation properties in Delaware, jointly appointed by the Parties, whose determination will be final. Each Party will bear one-half of the broker's fees.

## 4. RENT

4.1 **Payment.** Tenant will pay Base Rent in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, by ACH transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing.

4.2 **Operating Expenses.** Tenant will pay as additional rent Tenant's Share of the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, insure and repair the Common Areas and the structural elements of the buildings at the Property ("**Operating Expenses**"). Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital improvements, except to the extent they reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Lease Date, amortized over their useful lives;

(b) costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants;

(c) leasing commissions, legal fees and costs of disputes with other tenants;

(d) Landlord's debt service, depreciation and income taxes; and

(e) management fees exceeding 3% of gross rents at the Property.

4.3 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord will give Tenant a reasonable estimate of Operating Expenses before each calendar year, and Tenant will pay one-twelfth of its share monthly with Base Rent. Within 120 days after each calendar year, Landlord will deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses. Any overpayment will be credited against the next rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended, and Tenant will pay any underpayment within 30 days. Tenant may audit Landlord's records relating to each statement on 30 days' notice within one year after receiving it.

4.4 **Late Charge.** Any rent not paid within five business days after its due date bears a late charge of 3% of the overdue amount.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for the performance of its obligations. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any default by Tenant beyond any applicable cure period, and Tenant will restore any amount applied within ten business days after written demand. Landlord will return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within 30 days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 6. USE AND AIRFIELD OPERATIONS

6.1 **Compliance with Law.** Tenant will use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and in compliance with all applicable laws. This includes Federal Aviation Administration regulations governing the operation of unmanned aircraft, and environmental, fire and safety laws.

6.2 **Airfield Rules.** Tenant will comply with the rules and regulations for the Property that Landlord adopts and applies uniformly to all tenants (the "**Airfield Rules**"), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit C. Landlord will give Tenant at least 30 days' written notice of any change to the Airfield Rules. No change may materially restrict the Permitted Use or impose material additional costs on Tenant.

6.3 **Flight Testing.** Tenant may conduct flight testing of unmanned aircraft only within the designated test corridor shown on Exhibit A, during the hours set out in the Airfield Rules, and after coordinating with Landlord's airfield manager in the manner the Airfield Rules require. Tenant is solely responsible for obtaining any FAA authorizations, waivers or exemptions that its flight operations require.

6.4 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant may store and use at the Premises lithium-ion batteries, fuels, lubricants, cleaning agents and water-based test solutions in quantities reasonably necessary for the Permitted Use, in compliance with applicable law and the manufacturers' instructions. Tenant will not store or use agricultural chemicals or pesticides at the Premises, other than sealed sample containers kept in a fire-rated cabinet. Tenant will maintain battery charging stations in compliance with the National Fire Protection Association standards adopted by the local fire marshal.

## 7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 **Landlord.** Landlord will maintain in good condition and repair:

(a) the roof, foundation, exterior walls and structural elements of the Premises;

(b) the hangar door mechanism, except for damage caused by Tenant;

(c) the building systems serving the Premises up to the point of connection to the Premises; and

(d) the Common Areas, including runway and taxiway surfaces, lighting and snow removal.

7.2 **Tenant.** Tenant will maintain the interior of the Premises, including interior walls, floors, fixtures, and the heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems exclusively serving the office area, in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant will maintain a service contract for those HVAC systems with a licensed contractor.

7.3 **Alterations.** Tenant may make non-structural alterations costing less than $50,000 in any single project without Landlord's consent, on prior written notice to Landlord. Any other alteration requires Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Tenant may remove its trade fixtures, test equipment, charging stations and workbenches at the end of the Term and will repair any damage caused by their removal.

## 8. UTILITIES

Tenant will pay directly for electricity, gas, telecommunications and internet services separately metered to the Premises. Water, sewer and trash removal are included in Operating Expenses. Landlord will permit Tenant to install, at Tenant's cost, a rooftop RTK GNSS base station and antenna in a location Landlord reasonably approves.

## 9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

9.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant will maintain during the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) aviation liability insurance covering Tenant's unmanned aircraft operations at the Property of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(c) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and alterations at full replacement cost; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

Tenant's liability policies will name Landlord as an additional insured. Tenant will deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord on the Commencement Date and upon each renewal.

9.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord will maintain property insurance on the buildings at the Property at full replacement cost, and commercial general liability and airport premises liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence.

9.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives, and will cause its property insurers to waive, any right of recovery against the other Party for loss or damage covered by the property insurance that Party is required to carry under this Lease.

9.4 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.3, each Party will indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other Party from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage at the Property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying Party or its employees, contractors or invitees.

## 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

10.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord will restore them with reasonable diligence, and rent will abate in proportion to the portion of the Premises that is unusable until restoration is complete. If Landlord reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than 180 days, either Party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within 30 days after Landlord delivers that estimate.

10.2 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the Common Areas that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, either Party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its relocation costs and trade fixtures.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

11.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay.

11.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on written notice to Landlord within 30 days after the transaction:

(a) assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with Tenant;

(b) assign this Lease to an entity resulting from a merger or consolidation with Tenant, or to an entity acquiring all or substantially all of Tenant's assets; or

(c) undergo any transfer of its equity interests or any change of control, including by merger.

A transfer under this Section 11.2 is not a default under this Lease and does not require any consent or payment to Landlord.

## 12. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

12.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay rent within ten days after written notice that it is past due; or

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within 30 days after written notice describing the failure, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within 30 days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it.

12.2 **Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and pursue any remedy available under Delaware law, subject to Landlord's duty to use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

12.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within 30 days after written notice from Tenant, or immediately in an emergency, Tenant may perform the obligation and Landlord will reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within 30 days after invoice.

## 13. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

13.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant will surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear, casualty and alterations Landlord has approved for retention excepted.

13.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant will be a month-to-month tenant at 125% of the monthly Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term, and otherwise on the terms of this Lease.

## 14. QUIET ENJOYMENT; SUBORDINATION

14.1 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant will peaceably enjoy the Premises without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

14.2 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Property, provided that the mortgagee delivers a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default is continuing.

14.3 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each Party will, within ten business days after the other Party's written request, deliver a certificate confirming the status of this Lease, the rent then payable and whether any default is known to exist.

## 15. NOTICES

Notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the following addresses, or to any other address a Party designates by notice:

**To Landlord:** Pellbrook Airfield Properties, LLC, 900 Runway View Drive, Middletown, Delaware 19709, Attention: Property Manager

**To Tenant:** Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc., 4410 Sorrel Ridge Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803, Attention: General Counsel; with a copy to the Premises, Attention: Facilities Manager

Notices are effective upon receipt or refusal.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

16.2 **Brokers.** Each Party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Delmarva Commercial Realty, whose commission Landlord will pay under a separate agreement.

16.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing.

16.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

16.5 **Memorandum of Lease.** Either Party may record a memorandum of this Lease in a form reasonably acceptable to both Parties.

16.6 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.

## EXHIBIT C: AIRFIELD RULES

1. Unmanned aircraft flight testing is permitted only within the test corridor shown on Exhibit A, between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. local time, and not during the arrival or departure of manned aircraft.
2. Before each flight test session, the operator will notify the airfield manager by phone or radio and confirm that no conflicting manned operations are scheduled.
3. Unmanned aircraft may not be flown over the aprons, fuel farm, parking areas or other tenants' leased premises.
4. Vehicles on the airfield must display a Landlord-issued pass and yield to all aircraft.
5. Fueling, battery charging and hazardous materials storage must comply with the local fire marshal's requirements.
6. Tenants will promptly report any incident, accident or near-miss involving aircraft at the Property to the airfield manager.
