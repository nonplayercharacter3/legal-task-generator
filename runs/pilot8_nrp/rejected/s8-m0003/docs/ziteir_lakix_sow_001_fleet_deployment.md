# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 001

## FLEET DEPLOYMENT AND PILOT TRAINING

This Statement of Work No. 001 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") between **Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Ziteir**" or "**Supplier**"), and **Lakix Grower Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Lakix**" or "**Customer**"). Ziteir and Lakix are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

This SOW is issued under the Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** Lakix intends to deploy a fleet of ZX-40 Drones across its operating bases. It will use the fleet to provide crop-spraying and application services to Growers. Under this SOW, Ziteir will commission those Drones, integrate them with the ZiteirLink Platform, and train Lakix's pilots and fleet technicians (together, the "**Services**").

1.2 **Fleet.** The Parties anticipate deployment of up to 24 Drones in the phases described in Section 3. Lakix will buy Drones, Spare Parts and Platform subscriptions separately by Purchase Orders under Section 3 of the Agreement. They are priced under Exhibit B to the Agreement. This SOW does not itself obligate Lakix to purchase any Products.

1.3 **Operating Bases.** The Services will be performed at the following Lakix operating bases (each a "**Site**"), and at any additional Site the Parties agree in writing:

(a) Dover Base, 1275 Harvest Mill Road, Dover, Delaware 19901;

(b) Georgetown Base, Sussex County, Delaware; and

(c) Easton Base, Talbot County, Maryland.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Site Readiness Assessment.** Before each phase, Ziteir will visit each Site scheduled for that phase. During the visit Ziteir will assess:

(a) battery charging capacity;

(b) chemical mixing and loading areas;

(c) storage and maintenance facilities;

(d) cellular and RTK connectivity; and

(e) local airspace conditions.

Ziteir will deliver a written Site readiness report within ten business days after each visit, identifying any changes Lakix should make before commissioning.

2.2 **Commissioning.** For each Drone delivered under a Purchase Order, Ziteir will:

(a) unpack, assemble and inspect the Drone and its standard accessories;

(b) install current firmware and confirm the Drone's registration on the Platform;

(c) calibrate the spray system, flow meters and nozzles to the product labels Lakix supplies;

(d) set up and survey RTK base stations; and

(e) complete a test flight and test spray using water.

Commissioning does not replace Lakix's inspection rights under Section 6 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Platform Configuration.** Ziteir will configure Lakix's ZiteirLink account. This includes:

(a) user roles and permissions;

(b) import of Lakix's initial field boundaries and prescription map templates;

(c) maintenance alert thresholds; and

(d) export settings for Lakix's agronomy record systems.

Ziteir will process all Grower Data in connection with the Services in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum.

2.4 **Pilot Training.** Ziteir will deliver its ZX-40 Operator Course to Lakix pilots nominated by Lakix (each a "**Trainee**"). The course consists of:

(a) two days of classroom and simulator instruction covering flight planning, mission setup, emergency procedures, battery management and Platform use; and

(b) three days of supervised field flights at a Site.

Each training cohort will include no more than eight Trainees. Ziteir will issue a course completion certificate to each Trainee who passes the course's practical assessment.

2.5 **Technician Training.** Ziteir will deliver its ZX-40 Field Maintenance Course to Lakix technicians nominated by Lakix. The course covers routine maintenance, replacement of Spare Parts, nozzle and pump servicing, diagnostics and firmware updates. It runs for two days, with no more than six technicians per cohort.

2.6 **Season Support.** During each phase's spray season support window in Section 3, Ziteir will provide:

(a) a dedicated field support engineer available by phone and Platform chat from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days a week; and

(b) on-site support at a Site within 24 hours of Lakix's request where an issue cannot be resolved remotely.

2.7 **Annual Refresher.** Before the start of the 2026 spray season, Ziteir will deliver a one-day refresher course for all certified Trainees. The course will cover firmware and Platform updates released since the original training.

## 3. PHASES AND SCHEDULE

| Phase | Sites | Anticipated Drones | Services Window | Season Support Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Dover Base | Up to 8 | March 15, 2025 to May 31, 2025 | April 1, 2025 to September 30, 2025 |

3.1 **Scheduling.** The Parties will agree specific dates for Site visits, commissioning and training cohorts at least 15 business days in advance. Ziteir will use commercially reasonable efforts to complete commissioning of each Drone within ten business days after delivery under the applicable Purchase Order.

3.2 **Schedule Changes.** Either Party may request a change to the schedule by written notice to the other Party's Project Manager. Where Lakix requests a change less than five business days before a scheduled on-site visit, Lakix will reimburse Ziteir's reasonable non-refundable travel costs already incurred.

## 4. DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

4.1 **Deliverables.** The deliverables under this SOW are:

(a) Site readiness reports;

(b) a commissioning record for each Drone, signed by the Ziteir technician;

(c) the configured Platform account;

(d) training materials and course completion certificates; and

(e) an end-of-phase report for each phase summarizing Services performed and open items.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Lakix will review each end-of-phase report within ten business days after receipt. Lakix will either accept the phase in writing or describe in writing any material respect in which the Services for that phase did not conform to this SOW. Ziteir will correct any such nonconformity at no additional charge and resubmit the end-of-phase report. A phase is deemed accepted if Lakix does not respond within the review period.

## 5. RESPONSIBILITIES OF LAKIX

Lakix will:

(a) provide Ziteir personnel with safe and timely access to each Site, together with suitable power, workspace and secure storage;

(b) provide training fields and obtain landowner permission for training flights;

(c) supply the product labels and application rates needed for calibration;

(d) nominate Trainees who meet the eligibility requirements of the course; and

(e) designate a Project Manager with authority to make day-to-day decisions under this SOW.

Lakix remains responsible under Section 9.5 of the Agreement for compliance with Federal Aviation Administration regulations governing the operation of unmanned aircraft and with applicable pesticide application laws. This includes obtaining and maintaining any required remote pilot certificates, agricultural aircraft operator certificates, exemptions and applicator licenses for its operations. Ziteir's training supports, but does not substitute for, those certifications.

6.1 **Project Managers.** The initial Project Managers are:

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date. It continues until the Services for all phases have been completed and accepted, unless terminated earlier under this Section 8 or Section 14 of the Agreement.

8.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Lakix may terminate any phase that has not yet begun by giving at least 30 days' written notice before the start of that phase's Services Window. Lakix will pay for Services performed and non-refundable expenses incurred up to the effective date of termination.

8.3 **Effect.** Termination of this SOW does not terminate the Agreement or any accepted Purchase Order.
