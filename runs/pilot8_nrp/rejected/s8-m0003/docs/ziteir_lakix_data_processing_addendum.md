# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") between:

**Ziteir Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Sorrel Ridge Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Ziteir**" or "**Processor**"); and

**Lakix Grower Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1275 Harvest Mill Road, Dover, Delaware 19901 ("**Lakix**" or "**Controller**").

Ziteir and Lakix are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties have entered into a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Ziteir supplies ZX-40 autonomous crop-spraying drones, the ZiteirLink fleet telemetry platform and related Services to Lakix.

B. In providing the Platform, the Drones and the Services, Ziteir will collect, host and otherwise process Grower Data on behalf of Lakix and its grower clients.

C. Section 8.2 of the Agreement requires each Party to comply with this DPA in connection with the processing of Grower Data through the Platform and the Drones.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the processing of Grower Data under the Agreement. This includes, where applicable, the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and state data breach notification laws.

1.3 "**Grower**" means any farm operator, landowner or other client of Lakix for whom Lakix provides agronomy or application services using the Drones or the Platform.

1.4 "**Grower Data**" means all data and information that Lakix, a Grower, or any Drone operated by or for Lakix submits to, generates within, or uploads to the Platform or the Drones. Grower Data includes:

(a) field boundaries, prescription maps, yield and soil data, and crop and agronomic records;

(b) flight logs, spray application logs, as-applied maps and chemical application records;

(c) geolocation data associated with Growers' fields and operations; and

(d) Personal Data of Growers and their personnel.

1.5 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, or that is otherwise defined as "personal data," "personal information" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.6 "**Process**" or "**processing**" means any operation performed on Grower Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, erasure and destruction.

1.7 "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Grower Data while in the possession, custody or control of Processor or any Sub-processor.

1.8 "**Sub-processor**" means any third party that Processor engages to process Grower Data on its behalf.

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

2.1 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Lakix is the controller of Grower Data. Ziteir processes Grower Data solely as a processor or service provider on behalf of Lakix.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Processor will process Grower Data only as necessary to:

(a) provide, maintain and support the Platform and the Drones;

(b) perform the Services under any Statement of Work; and

(c) comply with Lakix's documented written instructions, including those given through the Platform's configuration settings.

2.3 **Details of Processing.** The subject matter, nature, purpose and categories of Grower Data are described in Annex 1. Processing will continue for the Term and for any data return period under Section 9.

2.4 **Lakix Responsibilities.** Lakix is responsible for obtaining any notices, consents and authorizations from Growers that Applicable Data Protection Law requires for Processor to process Grower Data under this DPA.

## 3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Processor will process Grower Data only on Lakix's documented instructions, unless applicable law requires otherwise. Processor will promptly inform Lakix if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Processor will not:

(a) sell or share Grower Data, as those terms are defined under Applicable Data Protection Law;

(b) retain, use or disclose Grower Data for any purpose other than the purposes in Section 2.2;

(c) combine Grower Data with data Processor receives from other customers, except as permitted under Section 3.3; or

(d) use Grower Data to market products or services to any Grower.

3.3 **Aggregated Data.** Processor may create aggregated and de-identified data from Grower Data to operate, secure and improve the Platform and the Drones. Processor will ensure that such data cannot reasonably be used to identify Lakix, any Grower, any individual or any specific field location, and will not attempt to re-identify it.

3.4 **Personnel.** Processor will ensure that each person it authorizes to process Grower Data is bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations and receives appropriate training in data protection.

3.5 **Compliance.** Processor will comply with the obligations that Applicable Data Protection Law imposes on processors and service providers.

## 4. SECURITY

4.1 **Security Measures.** Processor will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Grower Data against Security Incidents. These measures include, at a minimum, the measures described in Annex 2.

4.2 **Review.** Processor will review its security measures at least annually and update them as reasonably necessary to address evolving threats. Processor will not materially reduce the overall level of protection those measures provide during the Term.

## 5. SUB-PROCESSORS

5.1 **Authorization.** Lakix generally authorizes Processor to engage Sub-processors. The Sub-processors engaged as of the DPA Effective Date are listed in Annex 3.

5.2 **Notice of New Sub-processors.** Processor will give Lakix at least 30 days' written notice before engaging a new Sub-processor. Lakix may object on reasonable data protection grounds within 15 days after that notice. If Lakix objects, the Parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it within 30 days, Lakix may terminate the affected Platform subscriptions without penalty.

5.3 **Sub-processor Terms.** Processor will impose on each Sub-processor written data protection obligations no less protective of Grower Data than those in this DPA. Processor remains responsible for each Sub-processor's performance of those obligations.

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

6.1 **Notification.** Processor will notify Lakix without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

6.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice will describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate volume of Grower Data affected;

(c) the Growers likely to be affected; and

(d) the measures Processor has taken or proposes to take to contain and remediate it.

Processor will supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.3 **Cooperation.** Processor will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Processor will cooperate with Lakix in meeting any obligation to notify Growers, individuals or regulators.

6.4 **Public Statements.** Processor will not notify any Grower or make any public statement about a Security Incident that identifies Lakix or any Grower without Lakix's prior written approval, unless applicable law requires it.

## 7. ASSISTANCE AND AUDIT

7.1 **Data Subject Requests.** Processor will promptly forward to Lakix any request it receives from a Grower or individual relating to Grower Data. Processor will provide reasonable assistance to enable Lakix to respond to such requests.

7.2 **Assessments.** Processor will provide reasonable information and assistance to enable Lakix to carry out any data protection assessment that Applicable Data Protection Law requires in connection with the Platform.

7.3 **Audit.** Once in any 12-month period, Lakix may request that Processor provide its most recent independent third-party security assessment or certification report covering the Platform. If that report does not reasonably address Lakix's concerns, Lakix may, on at least 30 days' written notice, conduct an audit of Processor's relevant processing activities. Any such audit will take place during normal business hours, will be subject to Processor's reasonable confidentiality and security requirements, and will be at Lakix's expense.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **Data Incident Indemnity.** Processor will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Lakix, its Affiliates and each Grower from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, costs and expenses arising out of or relating to any Security Incident involving Grower Data. These include:

(a) regulatory fines and penalties;

(b) the costs of notifying affected Growers, individuals and regulators;

(c) credit monitoring, call center and forensic investigation costs; and

(d) amounts payable in respect of third-party claims, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

8.2 **Scope of Indemnity.** Processor's obligations under Section 8.1 apply to each Security Incident, whether or not caused by Processor's fault, and shall not be subject to any limitation of liability in the Agreement.

8.3 **Procedure.** The procedure in Section 10.3 of the Agreement applies to claims for indemnification under this Section 8.

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION OF GROWER DATA

9.1 **Export During the Term.** Lakix may export Grower Data at any time during the Term through the Platform's export functions and application programming interfaces.

9.2 **Return on Expiration or Termination.** For 60 days after expiration or termination of the Agreement, Processor will continue to make Grower Data available to Lakix for export. On Lakix's written request during that period, Processor will deliver Grower Data to Lakix in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

9.3 **Deletion.** Within 30 days after the end of the export period in Section 9.2, Processor will delete all Grower Data in its possession or control and will certify the deletion in writing on request. Processor may retain Grower Data to the extent applicable law requires, provided that it keeps that data confidential and processes it only for the purpose of that legal requirement.

## 10. TERM; GENERAL

10.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date and continues for as long as Processor processes Grower Data under the Agreement.

10.2 **Survival.** Sections 6, 8 and 9 survive expiration or termination of the Agreement for as long as Processor or any Sub-processor retains any Grower Data, and Section 8 survives as to any Security Incident occurring before that time.

10.3 **Agreement Provisions.** Sections 17 (Notices) and 18.1 (Governing Law) and 18.2 (Forum) of the Agreement apply to this DPA as if set out in full.

10.4 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

## ANNEX 2: SECURITY MEASURES

1. Encryption of Grower Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256.
2. Role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and quarterly access reviews.
3. Logical separation of each customer's Grower Data within the Platform.
4. Continuous logging and monitoring of Platform infrastructure, with alerting for anomalous activity.
5. Annual third-party penetration testing of the Platform and remediation of critical findings within 30 days.
6. Secure, signed firmware updates for the Drones and encrypted data links between Drones and the Platform.
7. Daily encrypted backups stored in a geographically separate region, with tested restoration procedures.
8. A documented incident response plan, tested at least annually.
9. Background checks for personnel with access to Grower Data, to the extent permitted by law.
