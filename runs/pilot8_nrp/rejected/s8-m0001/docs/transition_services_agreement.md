# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Glosiv Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Sorrelux Imaging, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider, Daikesk Imaging Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Recipient are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided certain administrative, technical and operational support to Recipient.

C. Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Provider will provide certain services to Recipient for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. After the Closing, Recipient is not an Affiliate of Provider.

"**Business**" means the manufacture of digital radiography detectors as conducted by Recipient as of the Closing Date.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Data**" means all data, records and information owned by, licensed to or held for Recipient. It includes customer lists, installed-base records, field-service histories, device performance data and protected health information processed by or for Recipient.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning Recipient or the Business that Provider or its Affiliates hold or receive in connection with this Agreement, including Company Data and Company Intellectual Property.

"**Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Omitted Services added under Section 2.3.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, to Recipient the transition services reasonably necessary to operate the Business. These services include the Services described in Schedule A.

**2.2 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner. Provider shall perform them with at least the same degree of care, priority, volume and timeliness that it provided to Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date (the "**Service Standard**"). Provider shall comply with all applicable laws in performing the Services, including HIPAA and FDA quality system regulations to the extent applicable.

**2.3 Omitted Services.** If, within 90 days after the Closing Date, Recipient identifies a service that meets both of the following conditions, Recipient may notify Provider in writing:

(a) Provider or its Affiliates provided the service to Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date; and

(b) the service is reasonably necessary to operate the Business but is not listed in Schedule A (an "**Omitted Service**").

The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to add the Omitted Service to Schedule A. Fees for the Omitted Service shall be determined in accordance with Section 5.1.

**2.4 Personnel.** Provider shall determine which of its personnel perform the Services. Provider shall ensure that those personnel have the skills and experience needed to perform the Services at the Service Standard.

**2.5 Third-Party Consents.** If Provider needs the consent of a third party to provide any Service, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent. Recipient shall bear the reasonable out-of-pocket cost of doing so. If Provider cannot obtain a consent after using those efforts, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to find a reasonable alternative arrangement.

**2.6 No Obligation to Improve.** Provider is not required to upgrade, enhance or modify the systems or processes it uses to provide the Services, except as required by law or as the Parties otherwise agree in writing.

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date and continues until 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Section 3.

**3.2 Early Termination of Individual Services.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for any reason by giving Provider at least 30 days' written notice. The Fees for any terminated Service end on the effective date of termination. Where Provider has notified Recipient in writing in advance that another Service depends on the terminated Service, Provider may terminate that dependent Service on the same date.

**3.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. Where the breach is Recipient's failure to pay undisputed Fees, the cure period is 15 days.

**3.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, or of any Service:

(a) Recipient shall pay all Fees accrued for Services performed through the effective date of termination;

(b) Provider shall return to Recipient, or at Recipient's election destroy, all Company Data in its possession or control, in accordance with Section 7.4; and

(c) Sections 1, 3.4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 13 survive.

## 4. ADMINISTRATION

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall appoint an individual to act as its primary contact for the administration of this Agreement (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). Each Party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice to the other Party.

**4.2 Reporting.** Provider shall give Recipient monthly reports on the performance of the Services against the Service Standard, in a form the Service Coordinators reasonably agree.

**4.3 Cooperation; Migration.** Each Party shall cooperate reasonably with the other in connection with the Services. Provider shall also provide reasonable assistance to Recipient and Buyer in migrating the Services and Company Data to Recipient's or Buyer's own systems or to a third-party provider before the end of the Term. That assistance includes making knowledgeable personnel available and delivering data in an industry-standard format.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider for each Service the amount set forth in Schedule A (the "**Fees**"). Fees are equal to Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the Service, without markup. Fully loaded cost means the direct and reasonably allocated indirect costs of providing the Service, calculated consistently with Provider's allocation practices for Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date.

**5.2 Third-Party Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider, without markup, for reasonable out-of-pocket costs that Provider pays to third parties specifically to provide the Services, if those costs are approved by Recipient in advance.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it gives Provider written notice of the dispute before the payment due date. The Service Coordinators shall seek to resolve the dispute within 30 days. Any disputed amount later determined to be owed shall be paid within ten Business Days after that determination.

**5.5 Taxes.** Fees do not include sales, use, value-added or similar taxes. Recipient shall pay any such taxes properly imposed on the Services, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 6. ACCESS TO SYSTEMS

**6.1 Access.** Where a Service requires one Party to access the other's systems, the accessing Party shall comply with the other Party's reasonable security policies and procedures that are provided to it in writing.

**6.2 Limited Use.** Provider shall access Recipient's systems and Company Data only to the extent necessary to perform the Services.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

**7.1 Confidentiality.** Provider shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and their respective representatives to, hold all Confidential Information in confidence. Provider shall use Confidential Information only to perform the Services, and shall not disclose it to any Person except to its personnel and subcontractors who need to know it to perform the Services and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 7.

**7.2 Exceptions.** Section 7.1 does not apply to information that becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement. Provider may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving prompt notice to Recipient so that Recipient may seek a protective order.

**7.3 Data Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Company Data no less protective than those it maintained for Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date and in any event consistent with applicable law. Provider shall notify Recipient within 48 hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or use or disclosure of, Company Data.

**7.4 Return of Company Data.** Within 30 days after the end of the Term, Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Company Data in its possession or control. Provider shall then permanently delete all copies, except as required by law, and certify that deletion in writing.

**7.5 Business Associate Agreement.** To the extent any Service involves protected health information, the Parties shall execute a business associate agreement complying with HIPAA on or before the Closing Date.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Ownership.** Each Party retains all rights in its own Intellectual Property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of any Intellectual Property between the Parties.

**8.2 Company Data and Deliverables.** As between the Parties, Recipient owns all Company Data and all work product created by Provider specifically for Recipient in performing the Services.

**8.3 License to Provider.** Recipient grants Provider a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Term to use Recipient's Intellectual Property and Company Data solely to perform the Services.

## 9. WARRANTIES

**9.1 Mutual.** Each Party represents that it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**9.2 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Provider makes no warranty regarding the Services, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**10.2 Cap on Liability.** Provider's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services shall not exceed the Fees paid by Recipient under this Agreement in the six months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Purchase Agreement, any claim arising from the use or disclosure by Provider or its Affiliates of Company Data or Confidential Information in connection with the Services is subject to, and limited by, this Section 10.2.

**10.3 Remedies for Service Failures.** If Provider fails to perform any Service at the Service Standard, Provider shall, at Recipient's request, promptly re-perform the Service at no additional charge, without limiting Recipient's other remedies under this Agreement.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify Recipient and its Affiliates against all Losses arising from third-party claims caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, or by its breach of Section 7.

**11.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify Provider and its Affiliates against all Losses arising from third-party claims caused by Recipient's gross negligence or willful misconduct in receiving or using the Services.

**11.3 Procedures.** The procedures in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement apply to claims under this Section 11, with conforming changes.

## 12. GENERAL

**12.1 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

**12.2 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for a failure or delay in performance (other than a payment obligation) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, if it promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. The Fees for any affected Service are suspended during the period of non-performance.

**12.3 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform Services that third parties performed for Recipient during the 12 months before the Closing Date. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors.

**12.4 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of Provider. Provider may assign this Agreement, or delegate the performance of any of the Services, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**12.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**12.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for Buyer, which may enforce Recipient's rights under Section 4.3 and Section 7, nothing in this Agreement confers any right on any Person other than the Parties.
