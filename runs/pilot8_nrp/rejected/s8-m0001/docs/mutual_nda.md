# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Daikesk Imaging Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Daikesk**"), and **Glosiv Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Glosiv**"). Daikesk and Glosiv are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Glosiv owns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Sorrelux Imaging, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Daikesk would acquire the Company or its business (the "**Transaction**").

C. In connection with the Transaction, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information, and the Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. For purposes of this Agreement, the Company is an Affiliate of Glosiv.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Disclosing Party or its Representatives furnish to a Receiving Party or its Representatives on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Transaction. It includes financial statements, forecasts, customer and supplier information, installed-base and field-service records, product designs, source code, regulatory submissions, intellectual property documentation, employee information and pricing. It also includes all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect that information, and the Transaction Information.

"**Disclosing Party**" means a Party, or its Affiliate, that discloses Confidential Information.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means a Party that receives Confidential Information, whether directly or through its Representatives.

"**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, agents, financing sources, and legal, financial, accounting and other professional advisors.

"**Transaction Information**" means the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, the fact that the Parties are discussing the Transaction, and the status and terms of those discussions.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation."

## 2. USE AND NON-DISCLOSURE

**2.1 Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and consummate the Transaction, and for no other purpose.

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall hold Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any Person, except to its Representatives who:

(a) need to know the Confidential Information for the purpose described in Section 2.1;

(b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and

(c) are bound by duties or obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

**2.3 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, other than Representatives who have entered into a separate confidentiality agreement directly with the Disclosing Party.

**2.4 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

**2.5 Competitively Sensitive Information.** The Parties acknowledge that each of them participates in the medical imaging industry. A Disclosing Party may designate particular Confidential Information, such as customer-specific pricing, cost data or product road maps, as "Clean Team Only." The Receiving Party shall limit access to designated information to the individuals and outside advisors that the Parties approve in writing, and shall comply with any reasonable clean-team protocol the Parties agree in writing.

**2.6 Personal Health Information.** No Party shall disclose protected health information, as defined under HIPAA, to the other Party under this Agreement unless the information has first been de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

**3.1 Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession on a non-confidential basis before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party or its Representatives, if that source is not known by the Receiving Party to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to Confidential Information.

**3.2 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule or legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, at the Disclosing Party's expense; and

(b) cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in seeking that remedy.

If no protective order is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment of it.

## 4. CONTACTS; NON-SOLICITATION

**4.1 Contacts.** Without Glosiv's prior consent, Daikesk shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, initiate contact concerning the Transaction or the Confidential Information with any employee (other than those designated by Glosiv), customer, supplier, distributor or group purchasing organization of the Company. This Section 4.1 does not restrict contacts in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Transaction.

**4.2 Non-Solicitation.** For 12 months after the Effective Date, Daikesk shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any officer or senior technical employee of the Company with whom Daikesk first had contact, or of whom Daikesk first became aware, in connection with the Transaction. This Section 4.2 does not prohibit (a) general solicitations, including through search firms, that are not targeted at Company employees, or hiring any person who responds to them, or (b) discussions with Company personnel to which Glosiv has given its prior consent.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

**5.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it has decided not to proceed with the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return to the Disclosing Party, or destroy, all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing.

**5.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention policy, or stored in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding expiration of the term under Section 8.1.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO LICENSE

**6.1 No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party nor its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of Confidential Information. Only the representations and warranties made in a definitive agreement for the Transaction, when executed and delivered, and subject to its terms, will have any legal effect.

**6.2 No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

**7.1 No Commitment.** Neither Party is obligated to pursue or complete the Transaction unless and until a definitive agreement has been executed and delivered, and then only on the terms of that definitive agreement. Either Party may end discussions at any time for any reason by written notice to the other Party.

**7.2 Effect of Closing.** If the Transaction is consummated, Daikesk's obligations under this Agreement with respect to Confidential Information concerning the Company or its business terminate at the closing of the Transaction. All other obligations under this Agreement continue in accordance with their terms.

## 8. TERM

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement continues in effect for two years after the Effective Date. The Receiving Party's obligations with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

**8.2 Accrued Rights.** Expiration of this Agreement does not relieve any Party of liability for a breach occurring before expiration.

## 9. REMEDIES

**9.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**9.2 Costs.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party or its Representatives breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable and documented legal fees and expenses incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are effective on delivery by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier or email (with confirmation of transmission), addressed:

If to Daikesk: Daikesk Imaging Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

If to Glosiv: Glosiv Medical Systems, Inc., Attention: Chief Legal Officer.

**10.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it, and no waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**10.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment without that consent is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**10.4 Securities Laws.** Each Party acknowledges that it is aware, and will advise its Representatives, that applicable securities laws restrict any Person who possesses material non-public information about an issuer from purchasing or selling that issuer's securities, or communicating that information to others in circumstances where it is reasonably foreseeable that they will trade in reliance on it.

**10.5 Privilege.** To the extent any Confidential Information includes materials subject to the attorney-client privilege, work product doctrine or other applicable privilege, the Parties share a common legal and commercial interest in those materials. Disclosure under this Agreement is not intended to waive any privilege or protection.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**10.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**10.8 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which constitute one instrument.
