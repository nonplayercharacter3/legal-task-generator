**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Sorrelux Imaging, Inc.: Non-Binding Letter of Intent**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Daikesk Imaging Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Sorrelux Imaging, Inc. (the "**Company**") from Glosiv Medical Systems, Inc. ("**Seller**") (the "**Transaction**"). Except as provided in Section 13, this Letter is not binding. It is intended only as a basis for continued due diligence and for negotiation of definitive agreements.

## 1. Structure

Buyer will purchase all of the Shares from Seller under a stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). At closing (the "**Closing**"), the Company will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer. Buyer will acquire the Company's operations as a going concern. Those operations comprise the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of digital radiography and ultrasound imaging systems (the "**Business**"), including the Company's field-service organization and installed-base service contracts.

## 2. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Shares will be $186,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), payable in cash at Closing. The Purchase Price assumes the following:

(a) the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis; and

(b) the Company has a normalized level of working capital, to be agreed by the parties during due diligence.

The Purchase Price will be subject to a customary post-Closing working capital adjustment.

## 3. Escrow

At Closing, Buyer will deposit 5% of the Purchase Price ($9,325,000) with a mutually acceptable escrow agent. The escrow will serve as a source of recovery for indemnification claims under the Definitive Agreement.

## 4. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for a transaction of this type. These will include representations about capitalization, title to the Shares, financial statements, material contracts, intellectual property, FDA regulatory compliance, data privacy and HIPAA, employees and taxes.

Buyer proposes the following indemnification terms:

(a) Seller's indemnification obligation for breaches of general representations and warranties will be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price;

(b) the cap in paragraph (a) will be subject to a deductible to be agreed; and

(c) fraud, fundamental representations and breaches of Seller's post-Closing confidentiality covenant will be excluded from the cap.

## 5. Transition Services

At Closing, Seller and the Company will enter into a transition services agreement (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"). Under it, Seller will provide the services that Seller or its Affiliates currently provide to the Company and that are reasonably necessary for the Company to continue to operate the Business as currently conducted. The term will be 12 months after the Closing Date. The services will include:

(a) information technology, ERP, network hosting, and data migration support;

(b) support for the Company's field-service operations, including dispatch, service-ticketing systems, spare-parts logistics and access to installed-base service records;

(c) support for the Company's ultrasound operations, including shared transducer sourcing, regulatory and complaint-handling systems, and distributor order processing;

(d) supply chain, procurement and shared-supplier arrangements for digital radiography detectors and components; and

(e) payroll, benefits administration, and finance and accounting support.

Services will be provided at Seller's fully loaded cost without markup, at a level of service consistent with that provided to the Company in the 12 months before Closing. The parties will set out a detailed services schedule in the Definitive Agreement process.

## 6. Key Personnel

Buyer places significant value on the Company's management and technical team. That team includes Dr. Tamsin Ferreol, the Company's Chief Technology Officer. During due diligence, Buyer expects to review the employment arrangements of senior Company personnel and any intellectual property licensed to the Company by those personnel. Buyer may also discuss retention arrangements with key personnel, with Seller's prior consent. Any such retention arrangements are to take effect at Closing.

## 7. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

For five years after Closing, Seller will not, and will cause its Affiliates not to, do either of the following:

(a) engage in the Business in North America or Europe; or

(b) solicit for employment senior employees of the Company, subject to customary exceptions.

## 8. Conditions

Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) satisfactory completion of Buyer's business, financial, legal, regulatory and intellectual property due diligence;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement on terms satisfactory to Buyer and Seller;

(c) expiration or termination of any waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976;

(d) receipt of required third-party consents identified in due diligence;

(e) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's board of directors; and

(f) the absence of any material adverse change in the Company or the Business.

Buyer's financing is in place, and the Transaction will not be subject to a financing condition.

## 9. Due Diligence and Access

From the date of this Letter, Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the following during normal business hours:

(a) the Company's books, records, contracts, facilities and personnel; and

(b) the Company's regulatory files and intellectual property documentation.

Buyer expects to complete its due diligence within 75 days after the date of this Letter.

## 10. Timing

The parties will use good-faith efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement by August 31, 2026. Closing will take place promptly after the closing conditions are satisfied.

## 11. Conduct of Business

Until the Definitive Agreement is signed or this Letter terminates, Seller will cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. During that period, the Company will not enter into, amend or terminate any material contract outside the ordinary course without Buyer's consent.

## 12. Expenses

Each party will bear its own fees and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 13. Binding Provisions

Only the following provisions of this Letter are binding on the parties (the "**Binding Provisions**"):

(a) Section 12 (Expenses);

(b) this Section 13;

(c) Section 14 (Exclusivity);

(d) Section 15 (Confidentiality); and

(e) Section 16 (Governing Law; Termination).

No other provision of this Letter creates any legally binding obligation. No party will have any obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered, and then only on the terms of the Definitive Agreement.

## 14. Exclusivity

For 90 days after the date of this Letter, Seller will not, and will cause the Company and their respective representatives not to, take any of the following actions with any person other than Buyer concerning any acquisition of the Company, the Shares or any material portion of the Company's assets:

(a) solicit, initiate or encourage any proposal;

(b) negotiate or furnish information in connection with any proposal; or

(c) enter into any agreement.

Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal it receives during that period.

## 15. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, are subject to the mutual non-disclosure agreement previously entered into between Buyer and Seller. That agreement remains in full force and effect.

## 16. Governing Law; Termination

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

This Letter will terminate on the earliest of:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(b) mutual written agreement of the parties; and

(c) 90 days after the date of this Letter.

Termination does not relieve any party of liability for a breach of the Binding Provisions before termination. Sections 12, 13, 15 and 16 survive termination.

## 17. Counterparts

This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

If this Letter accurately reflects our mutual understanding, please sign and return a copy by 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on May 11, 2026. After that time this proposal will lapse.
