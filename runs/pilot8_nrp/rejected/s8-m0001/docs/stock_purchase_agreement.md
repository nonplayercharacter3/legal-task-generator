# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**" or the "**Purchase Agreement**") is entered into as of August 14, 2026, by and among **Daikesk Imaging Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Glosiv Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Sorrelux Imaging, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company is engaged in the Business.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Transition Services Agreement and the Key Employee Agreement.

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of digital radiography and ultrasound imaging systems.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Company Data**" means all data, records and information owned by, licensed to or held for the Company, including customer lists, installed-base records, field-service histories, device performance data and protected health information processed by or for the Company.

"**Company Intellectual Property**" means all Intellectual Property owned by, or licensed to, the Company and used or held for use in the Business.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 6.7.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under Delaware law in the making of the representations and warranties in this Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization and Good Standing), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court or regulatory body, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

"**Intellectual Property**" means patents, patent applications, trademarks, copyrights, software, trade secrets, know-how and all other intellectual property rights.

"**Key Employee**" means Dr. Tamsin Ferreol, Chief Technology Officer of the Company.

"**Key Employee Agreement**" means the Employment Agreement between the Company and the Key Employee dated March 15, 2021.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, taken as a whole, other than effects arising from general economic conditions, changes in law or GAAP, or the announcement of this Agreement, except to the extent such effects disproportionately affect the Company relative to other participants in the medical imaging industry.

"**Material Contracts**" has the meaning given in Section 4.8.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement between Seller, as provider, and the Company, as recipient, in the form attached as Exhibit A.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) $9,325,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to Wilmington Escrow Services, LLC, as escrow agent, to be held under an escrow agreement in customary form as a source of recovery for claims under Article IX; and

(b) the Purchase Price, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, less the Escrow Amount, to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital. The Purchase Price paid at Closing shall be increased or decreased, as applicable, by the amount by which estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of $14,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**") (the "**Estimated Working Capital Adjustment**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual Closing Working Capital. Seller has 30 days to object in writing. Disputes not resolved within 30 days after an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties, whose determination is final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) Any amount owed following the final determination shall be paid within five Business Days by wire transfer.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means current assets of the Company (excluding cash and tax assets) less current liabilities of the Company (excluding indebtedness and tax liabilities), determined in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable tax law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on another date agreed by Buyer and Seller (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank, or stock powers duly executed;

(b) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller and the Company;

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(d) the certificate described in Section 7.2(c);

(e) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(f) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.5.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2 and the certificate described in Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has all requisite corporate power to own its assets and conduct its business as now conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000,000 shares are issued and outstanding, all of which are the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity of the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the certificate of incorporation or bylaws of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.5, result in a breach of, or give rise to any right of termination under, any Material Contract.

**4.5 Consents.** No consent, approval, waiver or authorization of, or notice to, any Governmental Authority or other Person is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller or the consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except those set forth on Schedule 4.5.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of June 30, 2026 and related statements for the six months then ended. The financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Company as of their dates.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists each contract to which the Company is a party that (a) involves annual payments in excess of $500,000, (b) is a license of Intellectual Property to or from the Company (other than off-the-shelf software), (c) is an employment agreement with any officer of the Company, (d) is a group purchasing, distribution or supply arrangement with a hospital system or group purchasing organization, or (e) restricts the Company from competing in any line of business (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns, or has a valid and enforceable right to use, all Company Intellectual Property necessary for the conduct of the Business as currently conducted. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the Intellectual Property of any Person.

**4.10 Regulatory Compliance.** The Company holds all FDA 510(k) clearances and other permits required for the products of the Business, and is in compliance in all material respects with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and applicable quality system regulations.

**4.11 Data Privacy.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with HIPAA and all other applicable laws relating to the privacy and security of Company Data. Since January 1, 2023, there has been no material breach of security involving Company Data.

**4.12 Employees.** Schedule 4.12 lists each employee of the Company with annual base compensation in excess of $200,000. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.13 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all material tax returns required to be filed and has paid all taxes shown as due on those returns.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from the Company or Seller in connection with this Agreement, other than Halvern Partners LLC, whose fees will be paid by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization; Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any law applicable to Buyer, and require no consent of any Governmental Authority other than under the HSR Act.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to distribution in violation of securities laws.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees. Without limiting the foregoing, the Company shall not amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VII. Each party shall file any notification required under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 (the "**HSR Act**") within ten Business Days after the date of this Agreement.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed on Schedule 4.5.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement with respect to the sale of the Company or its material assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and their respective representatives to, hold in confidence and not use or disclose any confidential or proprietary information concerning the Company or the Business, including Company Data and Company Intellectual Property ("**Confidential Information**"), except (a) to the extent the information becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Section 6.7, (b) as required by law, after prompt notice to Buyer, or (c) as necessary to perform the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.8 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) engage in the Business anywhere in North America or Europe, (b) own any interest in any Person engaged in the Business (other than passive holdings of less than 2% of a publicly traded company), or (c) solicit for employment any employee of the Company with annual base compensation in excess of $150,000, except through general solicitations not targeted at Company employees.

**6.9 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release regarding this Agreement without the prior consent of Buyer and Seller, except as required by law.

**6.10 Intercompany Arrangements.** At or before the Closing, Seller shall cause all intercompany accounts and agreements between the Company and Seller or its Affiliates to be settled and terminated, other than the Transition Services Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to satisfaction of the following conditions:

(a) any waiting period under the HSR Act shall have expired or been terminated; and

(b) no Governmental Authority shall have enacted or issued any law or order that prohibits the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to satisfaction, or waiver by Buyer, of the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and the other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), (b) and (d);

(d) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred since the date of this Agreement;

(e) the consents listed on Schedule 4.5 shall have been obtained; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered the items listed in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to satisfaction, or waiver by Seller, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an officer of Buyer to that effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer or Seller if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the delay;

(c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 30 days after notice; or

(d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 30 days after notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no party shall be relieved of liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for 18 months, except that the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, the Company and their Affiliates against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller, or (c) any taxes of the Company for pre-Closing periods.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($18,650,000). Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until aggregate Losses exceed $1,400,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

(b) The limitations in Section 9.4(a) do not apply to Losses arising out of Fraud, any breach of the Fundamental Representations, or any breach of Section 6.7 (Confidentiality). Seller's aggregate liability for those Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of Fraud.

(c) Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3 shall not exceed the amount set forth in Section 9.4(a), except for breaches of Section 5.1 and Fraud.

**9.5 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. For third-party claims, the indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and a full release.

**9.6 Escrow; Order of Recovery.** Buyer shall first seek recovery of Losses under Section 9.2(a) from the Escrow Amount, and then directly from Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.10, this Article IX is the exclusive remedy of the parties after the Closing for any breach of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare all tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date. Buyer shall prepare all other tax returns of the Company.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Taxes for any period beginning before and ending after the Closing Date shall be apportioned on a closing-of-the-books basis, except for property taxes, which shall be apportioned daily.

**10.3 Transfer Taxes.** Transfer, stamp and similar taxes arising from the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.4 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in the filing of tax returns and in any audit relating to taxes of the Company.

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided, each party bears its own expenses in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are effective on delivery by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier or email (with confirmation of transmission), addressed:

If to the Company before the Closing, to Seller; after the Closing, to Buyer.

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or, after the Closing, to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, provided Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.5 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified parties under Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any right on any Person other than the parties.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to be enforceable.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.9 Exclusive Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which constitute one instrument.

**11.12 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, the following order of precedence applies: the Purchase Agreement, then the Transition Services Agreement, then the Key Employee Agreement; each Ancillary Agreement controls as to its own subject matter.
