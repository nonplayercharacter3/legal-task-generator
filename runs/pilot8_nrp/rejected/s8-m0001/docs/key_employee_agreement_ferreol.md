# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 15, 2021 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Sorrelux Imaging, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Tamsin Ferreol**, an individual (the "**Employee**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company designs, manufactures, sells and services digital radiography and ultrasound imaging systems.

B. The Employee is the sole owner and named inventor of certain patents relating to the calibration of flat-panel digital radiography detectors. She conceived and reduced those inventions to practice before the Effective Date and outside any employment with the Company.

C. The Company wishes to employ the Employee as its Chief Technology Officer and to obtain a license to those patents. The Employee wishes to accept that employment and grant that license on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Base Salary**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Board**" means the Board of Directors of the Company.

"**Cause**" means the Employee's (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of her duties that causes material harm to the Company; (c) material breach of Section 8 or Section 10; or (d) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties, after written notice from the Board and a cure period of 30 days.

"**Change of Control**" means any of the following: (a) any Person or group of Persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, beneficial ownership of more than 50% of the outstanding voting stock of the Company, whether by purchase, merger or otherwise, including any sale of a majority of the Company's stock; (b) a merger or consolidation of the Company in which the holders of the Company's voting stock immediately before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting power of the surviving entity immediately after it; or (c) a sale, lease or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

"**Company Products**" means the digital radiography and ultrasound imaging systems, detectors, components, software and related service offerings that the Company designs, manufactures, sells or services from time to time.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Detector Calibration Patents**" means the patents and patent applications listed on Exhibit A, together with all continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, reexaminations and extensions of them, and all foreign counterparts.

"**Good Reason**" means, without the Employee's written consent, (a) a material reduction in the Base Salary; (b) a material diminution in her title, authority or duties; or (c) relocation of her principal place of work by more than 50 miles. Good Reason exists only if the Employee gives written notice within 60 days after the condition first arises, the Company fails to cure within 30 days after that notice, and the Employee resigns within 30 days after the cure period ends.

"**Inventions**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation."

## 2. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs the Employee as its Chief Technology Officer. She reports to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

**2.2 Duties.** The Employee shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for a chief technology officer of a medical imaging company. These include leadership of research and development, detector and transducer engineering, product software and regulatory design controls, together with any other duties the Chief Executive Officer reasonably assigns.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** The Employee shall devote substantially all of her business time and attention to the Company. She may serve on scientific advisory boards, hold academic appointments, and manage her personal investments, provided those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or breach Section 8 or Section 10.

**2.4 Place of Performance.** The Employee's principal place of work is the Company's headquarters, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Initial Term.** The term of employment begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years (the "**Initial Term**").

**3.2 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term and each anniversary after it, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods. Either party may prevent a renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the renewal date. The Initial Term and any renewals are the "**Term**."

## 4. COMPENSATION

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of $345,000 (the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board shall review the Base Salary at least annually and may increase it, but not decrease it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** The Employee is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 40% of Base Salary. The bonus is based on Company and individual performance objectives that the Board sets after consultation with the Employee. Any bonus is paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates.

**4.3 Long-Term Incentive.** The Employee is eligible to participate in any long-term incentive plan that the Company or its parent maintains for senior executives. Her participation is on terms no less favorable than those applicable to other senior executives of the Company.

## 5. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**5.1 Benefits.** The Employee may participate in all employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

**5.2 Vacation.** The Employee is entitled to 25 days of paid vacation per year, in addition to Company holidays.

**5.3 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**5.4 Patent Maintenance.** During the Term, the Company shall reimburse the Employee for the reasonable out-of-pocket costs of prosecuting and maintaining the Detector Calibration Patents.

## 6. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**6.1 Death or Disability.** Employment terminates automatically on the Employee's death. The Company may terminate employment on 30 days' written notice if the Employee is unable, because of physical or mental incapacity, to perform her essential duties with or without reasonable accommodation for 120 consecutive days.

**6.2 By the Company.** The Company may terminate employment for Cause, or without Cause on 30 days' written notice.

**6.3 By the Employee.** The Employee may resign for Good Reason, or without Good Reason on 60 days' written notice.

**6.4 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Employee:

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary;

(b) accrued but unused vacation;

(c) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(d) any vested benefits under Company plans.

These are the "**Accrued Obligations**."

## 7. SEVERANCE

**7.1 Qualifying Termination.** If the Company terminates the Employee's employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall pay or provide the following, subject to Section 7.3:

(a) continued Base Salary for 12 months;

(b) a pro-rated target bonus for the year of termination; and

(c) Company-paid COBRA premiums for up to 12 months.

**7.2 Change of Control Termination.** If the qualifying termination described in Section 7.1 occurs within 12 months after a Change of Control, the following enhancements apply:

(a) the salary continuation period becomes 18 months;

(b) the Employee also receives a lump sum equal to 1.5 times her target bonus; and

(c) all unvested equity awards held by the Employee vest in full.

**7.3 Release.** Severance under this Section 7 is conditioned on the Employee executing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, within 60 days after termination.

**7.4 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. It shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its affiliates, customers and suppliers. It includes product designs, source code, calibration data, regulatory submissions, pricing, customer lists and installed-base records. It does not include information that becomes publicly available other than through the Employee's breach.

**8.2 Non-Disclosure.** During the Term and at all times after it, the Employee shall hold Confidential Information in confidence. She shall use it only in performing her duties, except as required by law after prompt notice to the Company.

**8.3 Return of Materials.** On termination of employment, the Employee shall return or destroy all Company materials containing Confidential Information.

**8.4 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Employee from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency. It also does not limit any immunity under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b).

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**9.1 Assignment of Inventions.** The Employee hereby assigns to the Company all inventions, discoveries, improvements, works of authorship, software and know-how (collectively, "**Inventions**") that meet both of the following conditions:

(a) she conceives or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during the Term; and

(b) they relate to Company Products or result from work performed for the Company.

This assignment does not cover the Detector Calibration Patents or any invention excluded under applicable state law.

**9.2 Excluded Personal Patents.** The Company acknowledges the following:

(a) the Employee conceived and reduced to practice the inventions claimed in the Detector Calibration Patents before the Effective Date;

(b) the Employee is and remains their sole owner; and

(c) nothing in this Agreement assigns or transfers title to them to the Company.

**9.3 License of Detector Calibration Patents.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Employee grants to the Company an exclusive (including as to the Employee, except for non-commercial research), royalty-free, worldwide license under the Detector Calibration Patents to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import and service Company Products. The Company may not sublicense these rights, except to its contract manufacturers and service subcontractors acting on the Company's behalf. The license continues for the life of the last-to-expire Detector Calibration Patent, except as follows. The license and all rights of the Company under this Section 9.3 shall terminate automatically upon a Change of Control unless the Company has obtained the Employee's prior written consent to that Change of Control. The license shall not be affected by the termination of the Employee's employment for any reason other than Cause.

**9.4 Improvements.** Improvements to the Detector Calibration Patents conceived by the Employee during the Term in the course of her work for the Company are Inventions under Section 9.1. The Employee shall promptly disclose them to the Company.

**9.5 Further Assurances.** The Employee shall execute any documents reasonably requested by the Company to perfect its rights in Inventions. The Company bears all costs of doing so.

## 10. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**10.1 Non-Competition.** During the Term and for 12 months after it ends, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, be employed by, consult for or own any interest in any Person that develops or sells digital radiography or ultrasound imaging systems competitive with Company Products in North America or Europe. Passive holdings of less than 2% of a publicly traded company are permitted.

**10.2 Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for 18 months after it ends, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any of the following:

(a) any employee of the Company to leave the Company; or

(b) any customer of the Company to reduce its business with the Company.

**10.3 Reasonableness.** The Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 10 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**11.1 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are effective on delivery by hand, by overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices go to the Company at its headquarters, attention: Chief Executive Officer, and to the Employee at her most recent address in the Company's records.

**11.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Employee and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**11.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Employee, who may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement only with the Employee's written consent.

**11.4 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective heirs, legal representatives, successors and permitted assigns.

**11.5 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**11.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**11.8 Forum.** Each party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**11.9 Survival.** Sections 6.4, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 survive the termination of the Employee's employment, in accordance with their terms.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
