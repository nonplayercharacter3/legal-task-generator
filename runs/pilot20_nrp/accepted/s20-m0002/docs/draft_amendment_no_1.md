# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into and made effective as of **September 1, 2026** (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Peigirt Trailwear Co.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Peigirt**"); and

**Giseind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Giseind**").

Peigirt and Giseind are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Peigirt and Giseind are parties to a Master Services Agreement made effective as of March 1, 2025, including Schedule B (Service Level Schedule) (the "**Agreement**").

B. Under Section 2.3 of the Agreement, Giseind currently performs Returns Services only for Products returned by end customers in the United States.

C. The purpose of this Amendment is to add returns processing for Peigirt's Canadian e-commerce customers at Giseind's Mississauga, Ontario Facility.

D. Under Section 17.2 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended only by a written Amendment signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. CANADIAN RETURNS SERVICES

**1.1 Amendment of Section 2.3.** Section 2.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **2.3 Returns Services.** Giseind shall receive Products returned by end customers in the United States at its Reno, Nevada Facility, and Products returned by end customers in Canada at its Mississauga, Ontario Facility. For each returned item, Giseind shall inspect and grade it within three (3) Business Days of receipt, restock Products graded as "A-stock," and segregate all other returned Products for disposition as Peigirt directs in writing.

**1.2 Amendment of Section 3.2.** In Section 3.2 of the Agreement, the words "(c) Mississauga, Ontario (fulfillment)" are deleted and replaced with "(c) Mississauga, Ontario (fulfillment and returns)".

**1.3 Canadian Returns Processing.** For returns received at the Mississauga, Ontario Facility, Giseind shall:

(a) accept returns shipped using the prepaid return labels Peigirt issues to Canadian end customers, and any other return carriers Peigirt designates in writing;

(b) record each returned unit against the original Order in Giseind's warehouse management system and transmit the inspection grade and disposition status to Peigirt through the Integration within one (1) Business Day after grading;

(c) hold Products graded other than "A-stock" in a segregated area of the Facility for up to sixty (60) days pending Peigirt's written disposition instructions; and

(d) process returned Products imported into Canada from the United States, if any, in accordance with Peigirt's written instructions and applicable customs requirements, with any duties, taxes and brokerage fees passed through to Peigirt at Giseind's actual cost without markup.

**1.4 Service Levels.** The Returns Processing Service Level in Section 2.5 of the Service Level Schedule applies to returns received at the Mississauga, Ontario Facility from the Amendment Effective Date, measured with returns received at the Reno, Nevada Facility.

**1.5 Fees.** The returns processing Fee in Section 5.1 of the Agreement (USD 2.25 per returned unit) applies to returned units processed at the Mississauga, Ontario Facility. No setup or implementation fee is payable for the Canadian Returns Services.

**1.6 Transition.** Giseind shall begin accepting Canadian returns at the Mississauga, Ontario Facility no later than thirty (30) days after the Amendment Effective Date. Until then, Canadian returns shall continue to be handled under Peigirt's existing arrangements.

## 2. REPLACEMENT OF MSA SECTION 2.4

Section 2.4 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **2.4 Exclusivity.** During the Term, Peigirt shall use Giseind as its exclusive provider of all warehousing, fulfillment and distribution services for Products in any channel worldwide.

## 3. GENERAL

**3.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**3.2 Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

**3.3 Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement.

**3.4 Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
