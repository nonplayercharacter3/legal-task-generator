## 3. Actions from Q1 2026 Review

3.1 **Pack-out guidelines.** Peigirt issued updated packaging and branding guidelines in writing on April 22, 2026 (new tissue wrap and insert card for the base layer line). Giseind confirmed all three Facilities moved to the new pack-out by May 6, 2026. *Closed.*

## 4. Service Level Performance

4.1 Giseind presented the monthly reports for April, May and June 2026, delivered under Section 6.3 of the Agreement and Section 5.1 of the Service Level Schedule. Results, measured across all Facilities in aggregate:

| Service Level | Target | April | May | June |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dispatch (SLS 2.1) | 98% within 24 hours of Order Release | 98.6% | 98.9% | 98.4% |
| Order Accuracy (SLS 2.2) | 99.7% | 99.6% | 99.8% | 99.8% |
| Inventory Accuracy (SLS 2.3) | 99.5% | 99.7% | 99.6% | 99.7% |
| Receiving (SLS 2.4) | 98% within 2 Business Days | 98.3% | 98.8% | 99.1% |
| Returns Processing (SLS 2.5) | 97% within 3 Business Days | 97.9% | 98.2% | 97.4% |
| Integration Confirmations (SLS 2.6) | 99% within 1 hour | 99.5% | 99.6% | 99.4% |

4.2 **April Order Accuracy shortfall.** Giseind's root-cause analysis attributed the April miss mainly to size mis-picks on the men's softshell jacket at Columbus, where two adjacent bin locations held the same style in M and L. Giseind re-slotted the style and added a size scan at pack-out. May and June were back above target. Peigirt confirmed it had no further questions on the corrective action plan.

4.3 **Exclusions.** Giseind excluded 312 Orders from the June Dispatch calculation under Section 3.2(a) of the Service Level Schedule because of missing SKU barcodes on one inbound shipment. Peigirt's Supply Chain Manager confirmed the vendor labeling error and accepted the exclusion.

4.4 **Returns Processing in June.** June was above target but close to the line. Giseind attributed this to post-Father's Day return volume at Reno and has added a weekend shift through August.

## 5. Facility Updates

5.1 **Reno, Nevada.** No changes. Giseind confirmed the CCTV upgrade in the pack area was completed in May.

5.2 **Columbus, Ohio.** Re-slotting completed (see item 4.2). No relocation of Inventory is planned.

## 6. Integration

6.1 Giseind's Solutions Engineer reported one Integration latency incident on May 14, 2026, lasting about 40 minutes. It was caused by a certificate renewal on Giseind's side. Order acknowledgments queued and processed without loss. Shipment confirmations for the affected window are included in the May Integration Confirmations result above.

6.2 Peigirt asked for return grading and disposition status to be added to the Integration feed ahead of the Canadian returns launch. Giseind agreed to scope this.

## 7. Invoicing and Service Credits

7.1 **Service Credit for April.** For the April Order Accuracy shortfall, a Service Credit of 3% of April Monthly Fulfillment Fees is due under Section 4.1 of the Service Level Schedule. This is well within the 15% cap in Section 4.2. Giseind confirmed the credit was applied against the May invoice, consistent with Section 5.3 of the Agreement. Peigirt Finance confirmed receipt.

7.2 **No other Service Credits** were due for the quarter.

7.3 **Disputed amounts.** None outstanding. Peigirt Finance noted that packaging materials on the June invoice were billed at cost plus 10%, consistent with Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

## 8. Forecasts

8.1 Peigirt confirmed that the rolling 90-day forecasts under Section 2.5 of the Agreement were delivered on time in April, May and June. Actual Order volume for the quarter ran at 94% of forecast.

8.2 Peigirt will deliver its Peak Season forecast by September 1, 2026, as required by Section 2.5. The Supply Chain Manager flagged that the fall outerwear launch will bring inbound receipts forward by about two weeks compared with 2025. Giseind asked to receive the inbound receiving plan for that launch as early as possible so it can plan labor at Reno and Columbus.

8.3 Giseind and Peigirt agreed to schedule the annual physical inventory under Section 4.3 of the Agreement for a date outside Peak Season. Proposed dates are to be exchanged by email.

## 9. Canadian Returns

9.1 Peigirt restated its interest in having Canadian end-customer returns processed at the Mississauga, Ontario Facility instead of under its current arrangements. Peigirt's Customer Experience Lead said refund turnaround for Canadian customers is the most frequent complaint in post-purchase surveys.

9.2 Giseind confirmed it can support returns processing at Mississauga at the existing returns processing Fee of USD 2.25 per returned unit, using the same inspection, grading and disposition process as Reno.

9.3 Giseind will prepare a draft amendment to the Agreement adding Canadian returns processing at Mississauga and send it to Peigirt for review. Both sides noted that any change to the Agreement must be made by a written Amendment under Section 17.2.
