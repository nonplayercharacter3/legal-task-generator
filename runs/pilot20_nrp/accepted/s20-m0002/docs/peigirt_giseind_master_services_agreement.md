# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and made effective as of **March 1, 2025** (the "**MSA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Peigirt Trailwear Co.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Peigirt**"); and

**Giseind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Giseind**").

Peigirt and Giseind are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Peigirt designs, markets and sells outdoor apparel and equipment, including jackets, base layers and hiking gear, through its own e-commerce channels.

B. Giseind is a provider of third-party logistics, warehousing and e-commerce fulfillment services.

C. Peigirt wishes to engage Giseind to receive, store, pick, pack and ship Peigirt's direct-to-consumer e-commerce orders, and to process returns, on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Amendment**" means a written amendment to this Agreement signed by both Parties under Section 17.2.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in the State of Delaware.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**E-Commerce Order**" means an order for Products placed by an end consumer through a Peigirt-operated website, mobile application or online marketplace storefront and released to Giseind for fulfillment through the Integration.

"**Facility**" means each warehouse or distribution center operated by Giseind or its approved subcontractors from which Services are performed, as listed in Section 3.2.

"**Fees**" means the fees payable by Peigirt for the Services under Section 5.

"**Integration**" means the electronic data interface between Peigirt's order management system and Giseind's warehouse management system described in Section 3.4.

"**Inventory**" means Products received by Giseind at a Facility and held for Peigirt.

"**Order**" means an E-Commerce Order released to Giseind for fulfillment.

"**Peak Season**" means November 15 through December 31 of each year.

"**Peigirt Data**" means all data and information supplied by or on behalf of Peigirt, or generated by Giseind in performing the Services, including order data, SKU-level inventory and sales data, end-customer names, addresses, contact details and order histories, and returns data.

"**Products**" means all goods bearing any trademark, trade name or logo owned or licensed by Peigirt.

"**Returns Services**" means the receipt, inspection, grading, restocking, refurbishment routing or disposal of Products returned by end customers, as described in Section 2.3.

"**Services**" means the fulfillment services described in Section 2.2, the Returns Services, and any related services Giseind performs under this Agreement.

"**Service Level Schedule**" means Schedule B (Service Level Schedule) to this Agreement, which sets out the service levels for the Services and the service credits payable for failures to meet them.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term, as described in Section 12.

"**Territory**" means the United States and Canada.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Schedule B forms part of this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Engagement.** Peigirt engages Giseind, and Giseind agrees, to perform the Services in accordance with this Agreement and the Service Level Schedule.

**2.2 Fulfillment Services.** Giseind shall:

(a) receive inbound shipments of Products at the Facilities, count and inspect them against advance shipping notices, and record discrepancies in its warehouse management system within two (2) Business Days of receipt;

(b) store Inventory in a clean, secure and climate-appropriate environment, segregated from goods of other customers;

(c) pick, pack and label each Order in accordance with Peigirt's packaging and branding guidelines as provided to Giseind in writing from time to time;

(d) tender each Order to the carrier and service level designated in the Order data, and transmit tracking information to Peigirt through the Integration; and

(e) perform cycle counts and an annual physical inventory in accordance with Section 4.

**2.3 Returns Services.** Giseind shall receive Products returned by end customers in the United States at its returns Facility, inspect and grade each returned item within three (3) Business Days of receipt, restock Products graded as "A-stock," and segregate all other returned Products for disposition as Peigirt directs in writing.

**2.4 Exclusivity.** During the Term, Peigirt shall use Giseind as its exclusive provider for the fulfillment of E-Commerce Orders shipped to addresses in the Territory. Nothing in this Section 2.4 restricts Peigirt from using any other provider, or performing services itself, for any other channel, product or geography.

**2.5 Forecasts.** Peigirt shall provide Giseind with a rolling non-binding ninety (90) day forecast of expected Order volumes and inbound receipts on or before the fifth (5th) Business Day of each month, and a separate Peak Season forecast no later than September 1 of each year.

**2.6 Subcontracting.** Giseind may not subcontract the performance of any material part of the Services without Peigirt's prior written consent. Giseind remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 3. FACILITIES, PERSONNEL AND SYSTEMS

**3.1 Personnel.** Giseind shall assign qualified and appropriately trained personnel to perform the Services and shall designate an account manager who will serve as Peigirt's primary point of contact. Giseind may replace the account manager on not less than fifteen (15) days' written notice to Peigirt.

**3.2 Facilities.** As of the MSA Effective Date, the Services are performed from Giseind's facilities at (a) Reno, Nevada (fulfillment and returns), (b) Columbus, Ohio (fulfillment) and (c) Mississauga, Ontario (fulfillment). Giseind shall not relocate Inventory to a different facility without Peigirt's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**3.3 Security.** Giseind shall maintain at each Facility commercially reasonable physical security measures, including controlled access, CCTV coverage of storage and pack areas, and alarm monitoring.

**3.4 Integration.** Each Party shall bear its own costs of building and maintaining its side of the Integration. Giseind shall maintain the Integration so that Orders are received and acknowledged in near real time and shipment confirmations are returned to Peigirt within one (1) hour of carrier tender.

## 4. INVENTORY

**4.1 Title.** Title to all Inventory remains with Peigirt at all times. Giseind has no ownership interest in the Inventory and shall keep it free of liens, security interests and encumbrances arising by, through or under Giseind.

**4.2 Warehouse Lien Waiver.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Giseind waives any warehouse lien or similar statutory lien it may have over the Inventory.

**4.3 Counts.** Giseind shall perform cycle counts on a rolling basis so that each SKU is counted at least once per calendar quarter, and shall conduct a full physical inventory once per year at a date agreed with Peigirt outside Peak Season.

**4.4 Shrinkage.** Giseind is responsible for the replacement cost of Inventory lost, damaged or destroyed while in Giseind's care, custody or control, to the extent annual shrinkage exceeds one-quarter of one percent (0.25%) of the average value of Inventory held during the relevant contract year.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** Peigirt shall pay Giseind the following Fees for the Services:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Inbound receiving | USD 0.35 per unit |
| Storage | USD 18.00 per pallet position per month |
| Order fulfillment (first unit) | USD 2.85 per Order |
| Order fulfillment (each additional unit) | USD 0.55 per unit |
| Returns processing | USD 2.25 per returned unit |

Carrier charges are passed through to Peigirt at Giseind's actual cost without markup. Packaging materials supplied by Giseind are invoiced at cost plus ten percent (10%).

**5.2 Fee Adjustments.** Giseind may increase the Fees once per contract year, effective on an anniversary of the MSA Effective Date, by giving Peigirt at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No increase may exceed the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage increase in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve (12) months.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Giseind shall invoice Peigirt monthly in arrears. Peigirt shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. Service credits due under the Service Level Schedule shall be applied against the next invoice.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** Peigirt may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Giseind in writing of the basis of the dispute before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**5.5 Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, goods and services and similar taxes, which Peigirt shall pay where properly invoiced. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS

**6.1 Performance Standards.** Giseind shall perform the Services so as to meet or exceed the service levels set out in the Service Level Schedule.

**6.2 Service Credits.** If Giseind fails to meet a service level, Giseind shall credit Peigirt the service credits set out in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are without prejudice to Peigirt's rights under Section 13.2(c).

**6.3 Reporting.** Giseind shall deliver to Peigirt, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each month, a report showing its performance against each service level for that month.

**6.4 Business Reviews.** The Parties shall hold a business review meeting each calendar quarter to review performance, forecasts, open issues and improvement initiatives.

## 7. PEIGIRT RESPONSIBILITIES

Peigirt shall (a) deliver Products to the Facilities properly labeled with SKU barcodes and accompanied by advance shipping notices; (b) supply accurate Order data through the Integration; (c) provide packaging and branding guidelines in writing; and (d) respond promptly to Giseind's reasonable requests for disposition instructions and information.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA

**8.1 Peigirt Marks.** Peigirt grants Giseind a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Peigirt's trademarks solely as necessary to perform the Services, including on packing slips and branded packaging.

**8.2 Pre-existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, systems and know-how. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Giseind's warehouse management system or other proprietary tools.

**8.3 Peigirt Data.** As between the Parties, Peigirt owns all Peigirt Data. Giseind shall use Peigirt Data only to perform the Services, and shall not sell, aggregate, benchmark or otherwise use Peigirt Data for any other purpose.

**8.4 Data Protection.** Giseind shall process personal information contained in Peigirt Data in compliance with all applicable privacy and data protection laws in the Territory and shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of that information. Giseind shall notify Peigirt within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Peigirt Data.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

**9.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Peigirt Data, pricing, forecasts, product plans and the terms of this Agreement.

**9.2 Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9.

**9.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

**9.4 Return.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement, each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, except that Giseind shall return all Peigirt Data to Peigirt in a commonly used electronic format before destroying its copies.

## 10. INSURANCE

Giseind shall maintain during the Term, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) warehouse legal liability insurance covering the full replacement value of the Inventory; (c) cyber liability insurance of not less than USD 5,000,000 per claim; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Giseind shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 11. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

**11.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has authority to enter into this Agreement, and will comply with all applicable laws in performing it.

**11.2 Service Warranty.** Giseind warrants that it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards for e-commerce fulfillment providers.

**11.3 Indemnity by Giseind.** Giseind shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Peigirt and its Affiliates from all third-party claims, losses and expenses arising from (a) Giseind's negligence or willful misconduct; (b) Giseind's breach of Section 8.3, 8.4 or 9; or (c) bodily injury or property damage at any Facility.

**11.4 Indemnity by Peigirt.** Peigirt shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Giseind from all third-party claims, losses and expenses arising from (a) Peigirt's negligence or willful misconduct; or (b) any claim that the Products, or Giseind's use of Peigirt's trademarks in accordance with Section 8.1, infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

**11.5 Limitation of Liability.** Except for liability under Section 11.3, Section 11.4, a breach of Section 9, or a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, and each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement in any contract year is limited to the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 12. TERM

**12.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the MSA Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years from the MSA Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

**12.2 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five (5) year Renewal Terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party no earlier than 300 days and no later than 270 days before the expiration of the then-current Term.

## 13. TERMINATION

**13.1 Termination by Either Party.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it; or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**13.2 Termination by Peigirt.** Peigirt may terminate this Agreement on written notice to Giseind if:

(a) Giseind fails to pay any amount due to Peigirt and does not cure within thirty (30) days of notice;

(b) Giseind suffers an unauthorized disclosure of Peigirt Data caused by its breach of Section 8.4; or

(c) Giseind fails to meet the Dispatch Service Level set out in the Service Level Schedule in any three (3) months in any rolling six (6) month period.

**13.3 Termination by Giseind for Non-Payment.** Giseind may terminate this Agreement on written notice if Peigirt fails to pay undisputed Fees within forty-five (45) days after receiving written notice of non-payment.

## 14. TRANSITION

**14.1 Transition Assistance.** On expiry or any termination of this Agreement, Giseind shall, for up to one hundred twenty (120) days, continue to perform the Services at the then-current Fees and provide reasonable cooperation to transfer the Services to Peigirt or its designated replacement provider, including releasing all Inventory and Peigirt Data.

**14.2 Release of Inventory.** Giseind shall release Inventory for collection within ten (10) Business Days of Peigirt's written request, subject only to payment of undisputed Fees then due.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, government action, or failure of public utilities, provided that the affected Party promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Labor disputes involving Giseind's own workforce are not a force majeure event. If a force majeure event prevents Giseind's performance of a material part of the Services for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days, Peigirt may use alternative providers for the affected Services for the duration of the event without breach of Section 2.4, or terminate this Agreement on written notice.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

**16.1 General.** Neither Party may assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as provided in Section 16.3. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 16 is void.

**16.2 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

**16.3 Permitted Assignment.** Giseind may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without notice to or consent of Peigirt, to (a) any Affiliate of Giseind; (b) any successor to Giseind by merger, consolidation, reorganization or change of control; or (c) any acquirer of all or substantially all of its fulfillment business or assets.
