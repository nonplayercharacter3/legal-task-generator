# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule is Schedule B to the Master Services Agreement between **Peigirt Trailwear Co.** ("**Peigirt**") and **Giseind Fulfillment Services, LLC** ("**Giseind**") made effective as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Schedule:

"**Cut-Off Time**" means 2:00 p.m. local time at the Facility to which an Order is released.

"**Dispatch**" means, for an Order, the physical tender of the fully picked, packed and labeled Order to the designated carrier, as evidenced by a carrier scan or a Giseind manifest close event recorded in Giseind's warehouse management system. "**Dispatched**" has a corresponding meaning.

"**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month during the Term.

"**Monthly Fulfillment Fees**" means the Order fulfillment fees (first unit and each additional unit) invoiced under Section 5.1 of the Agreement for a Measurement Period. They exclude storage, receiving, returns processing, carrier pass-through and packaging charges.

"**Order Release**" means the time at which an Order is received and acknowledged by Giseind's warehouse management system through the Integration. Orders released after the Cut-Off Time are treated as released at 8:00 a.m. local time on the next Business Day.

"**Peak Season**" means September 1 through January 31 of each year.

"**Service Level**" means each performance standard set out in Section 2.

"**Service Credit**" means a credit against Fees calculated under Section 4.

## 2. SERVICE LEVELS

Giseind shall meet each of the following Service Levels in each Measurement Period, measured across all Facilities in aggregate.

**2.1 Dispatch Service Level.** 98% of Orders dispatched within 24 hours of order release (the "**Dispatch Service Level**").

**2.2 Order Accuracy.** At least 99.7% of Orders Dispatched in the Measurement Period are shipped with the correct SKUs, sizes, colors and quantities, as measured by end-customer mis-ship claims validated by Giseind against pack-station records.

**2.3 Inventory Accuracy.** Unit-level inventory accuracy of at least 99.5%, as measured by the cycle counts performed under Section 4.3 of the Agreement in the Measurement Period.

**2.4 Receiving.** At least 98% of inbound shipments are received, counted and available for allocation in Giseind's warehouse management system within two (2) Business Days of arrival at the Facility, consistent with Section 2.2(a) of the Agreement.

**2.5 Returns Processing.** At least 97% of returned units are inspected and graded within three (3) Business Days of receipt at the returns Facility, consistent with Section 2.3 of the Agreement.

**2.6 Integration Confirmations.** At least 99% of shipment confirmations are returned to Peigirt through the Integration within one (1) hour of carrier tender, consistent with Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

## 3. MEASUREMENT AND RELIEF

**3.1 Measurement.** Giseind shall measure performance against each Service Level using data from its warehouse management system and carrier systems. Peigirt may audit the underlying data on reasonable notice, not more than twice in any contract year.

**3.2 Excused Failures.** An Order, shipment or unit is excluded from the calculation of a Service Level to the extent the failure is caused by (a) Peigirt's failure to perform its responsibilities under Section 7 of the Agreement, including inaccurate Order data or missing advance shipping notices; (b) a force majeure event under Section 15 of the Agreement; (c) a failure of Peigirt's side of the Integration; or (d) Order volume in a Measurement Period exceeding one hundred thirty percent (130%) of the most recent forecast for that period provided under Section 2.5 of the Agreement, for the volume above that threshold only.

**3.3 Peak Season.** During Peak Season, the Dispatch Service Level does not apply and no Service Credits accrue. Giseind shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts to Dispatch Orders within the Dispatch Service Level and shall continue to report its performance under Section 5.

## 4. SERVICE CREDITS

**4.1 Calculation.** If Giseind fails to meet a Service Level in a Measurement Period, Giseind shall credit Peigirt the following percentage of the Monthly Fulfillment Fees for that Measurement Period:

| Service Level | Shortfall | Service Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch (Section 2.1) | Up to 2 percentage points below target | 5% |
| Dispatch (Section 2.1) | More than 2 percentage points below target | 10% |
| Order Accuracy (Section 2.2) | Any shortfall | 3% |
| Inventory Accuracy (Section 2.3) | Any shortfall | 2% |
| Receiving (Section 2.4) | Any shortfall | 2% |
| Returns Processing (Section 2.5) | Any shortfall | 2% |
| Integration Confirmations (Section 2.6) | Any shortfall | 1% |

**4.2 Cap.** The aggregate Service Credits payable for any Measurement Period shall not exceed 15% of monthly fulfillment fees for that Measurement Period.

**4.3 Application.** Service Credits shall be applied against the next invoice under Section 5.3 of the Agreement. Service Credits outstanding on expiry or termination of the Agreement shall be paid to Peigirt within thirty (30) days.

**4.4 Nature of Credits.** Service Credits are a price adjustment as described in Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

## 5. REPORTING

**5.1 Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, Giseind shall deliver the report required by Section 6.3 of the Agreement. The report shall show, for each Service Level and each Facility, (a) actual performance against target; (b) volumes measured and volumes excluded under Section 3.2, with the reason for each exclusion; and (c) the Service Credits due.

**5.2 Remediation.** If Giseind fails to meet any Service Level in a Measurement Period, Giseind shall include in the monthly report a root-cause analysis and a corrective action plan, and shall review progress on the plan at the next quarterly business review held under Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

## 6. CHRONIC FAILURE

Failure to meet the Dispatch Service Level in any three (3) months in any rolling six (6) month period entitles Peigirt to terminate the Agreement under Section 13.2(c) of the Agreement.

## 7. REVIEW OF SERVICE LEVELS

The Parties shall review the Service Levels at the first quarterly business review of each contract year. Any change to a Service Level or Service Credit is effective only if made by an Amendment under Section 17.2 of the Agreement.
