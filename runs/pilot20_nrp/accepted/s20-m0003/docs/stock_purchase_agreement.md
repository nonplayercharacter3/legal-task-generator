# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 2, 2026, by and among Seipal Thermal Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Gourain Industrial Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Kestrelle Immersion Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures, installs and services liquid and immersion cooling systems for hyperscale data centers (the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

D. The board of directors of the Company has approved this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or cause to be paid, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses identified in the payoff letters and invoices delivered under Section 3.2(e), as set forth in the closing statement delivered under Section 2.5(a); and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b), as adjusted under Section 2.5(a).

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority is treated as paid to the Person in respect of whom the withholding was made.

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $13,950,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account and disbursed in accordance with Article IX and the Escrow Agreement. On the first Business Day following the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the then remaining balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of all claims then pending under Article IX, which shall be retained until those claims are finally resolved.

**2.5 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, the Company shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting out its good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, Closing Indebtedness, Closing Cash and Transaction Expenses, with reasonable supporting detail, and the resulting estimated Purchase Price.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the actual amounts of the items described in Section 2.5(a). Seller has 45 days to deliver a written notice of objection. Any dispute not resolved by the parties within 30 days after delivery of that notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller, whose determination is final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) *Payment.* Any upward or downward adjustment shall be paid by the owing party within five Business Days after final determination. Any downward adjustment payable by Seller may, at Buyer's election, be recovered from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, in which case Seller shall promptly restore the Indemnity Escrow Fund by the amount so recovered.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time as Buyer and Seller agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver or cause to be delivered to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers in the form attached as Exhibit A;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(d) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer that each of the Required Consents has been obtained;

(e) customary payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness and final invoices for all Transaction Expenses;

(f) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c);

(g) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller; and

(h) a certificate of the secretary of the Company certifying the resolutions of the board of directors of the Company approving this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, if Seller or the Company has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within 20 days after written notice;

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any party, except that this Section 8.2, Section 6.6 and Article XI survive, and no termination relieves any party from liability for Fraud or willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that (a) the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after the expiration of the longest applicable statute of limitations, and (b) the representations in Section 4.13 (Taxes) survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations (giving effect to any waiver or extension). Covenants survive until fully performed. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller or the Company in Article IV or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller or, before the Closing, the Company in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date;

(d) any Closing Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing or taken into account in determining the Purchase Price; or

(e) any Fraud by Seller or the Company.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V, or (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail; failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, provided it acknowledges in writing its indemnification obligation for that claim. The indemnifying party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money fully paid by the indemnifying party and includes a complete release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *General Cap.* Except as provided in Section 9.5(b), the aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $18,600,000 (10% of the Purchase Price). Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations or Section 4.13) unless and until the aggregate Losses exceed $930,000, after which Seller is liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

(b) *Fundamental Representations, Taxes and Fraud.* The aggregate liability of Seller for Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of the Fundamental Representations or Section 4.13 (Taxes), or under Section 9.2(c), shall not exceed the Purchase Price. Liability for claims arising from Fraud is not subject to any cap or other limitation in this Section 9.5. Claims under Sections 9.2(b) and 9.2(d) are capped at the Purchase Price.

(c) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of a representation or warranty, and for determining whether a breach has occurred, any qualification as to materiality or Material Adverse Effect shall be disregarded.

(d) *Insurance.* Losses are calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of the claim, less costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

**9.6 Manner of Payment.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnified Party under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied (a) first, from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, by joint written instruction or final order delivered to the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement, to the extent of funds then available; and (b) thereafter, to the extent the Indemnity Escrow Fund is insufficient or has been released, directly by Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds within ten Business Days after final determination, subject to the limitations in Section 9.5.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Subject to Section 2.5, Section 11.10 and claims for Fraud, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to any breach of representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement after the Closing.
