# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

*[Agreed Form: Exhibit B to Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 2, 2026]*

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 20[●], by and among Seipal Thermal Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Gourain Industrial Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Larchmont Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Kestrelle Immersion Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $13,950,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Agent will hold that amount and disburse it under the Purchase Agreement and this Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings given below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Buyer Indemnified Parties**" has the meaning given in Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1(a).

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs, as confirmed to the Escrow Agent in the joint written notice delivered under Section 3.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1(c).

"**Final Order**" means a final and non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a final determination of the independent accounting firm under Section 2.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement. It must be accompanied by a certificate of counsel for the presenting party stating that the order, judgment, decree or determination is final and non-appealable.

"**Indemnity Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest and earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements under this Agreement.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Losses**" means out-of-pocket losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, Taxes, interest, penalties, fines, costs and expenses actually incurred, including reasonable attorneys' fees, excluding diminution in value, lost profits and consequential damages.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1(b).

"**Release Date**" means the first Business Day following the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Indemnity Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent, on or before the date of this Agreement, a certificate in the form of Schedule 1. The certificate shall name the individuals authorized to act on its behalf under this Agreement (each, an "**Authorized Representative**") and give their specimen signatures and telephone numbers. A party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the Escrow Agent and the other party.

## 3. ESCROW FUND

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a joint written notice confirming the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the deposit to Buyer and Seller in writing.

**3.2 Segregated Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Fund in a segregated account maintained in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Indemnity Escrow Fund shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent. It shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party.

**3.3 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Indemnity Escrow Fund in a U.S. government money market fund or other investment designated in a Joint Written Instruction. If no Joint Written Instruction is given, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Fund in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured to the extent permitted by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any investment loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3.3. All interest and earnings form part of the Indemnity Escrow Fund.

## 4. CLAIMS AND DISBURSEMENTS

**4.1 Claims Against the Indemnity Escrow Fund.**

(a) *Claim Notice.* At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). The Claim Notice shall state that a Buyer Indemnified Party has incurred, or reasonably expects to incur, Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. It shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of that amount.

(b) *Objection.* Seller may object to all or any part of a claim by delivering a written notice of objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall state in reasonable detail the basis of the objection and the amount in dispute. If the Escrow Agent receives no Objection Notice within that 30-day period, Seller is deemed to have accepted the claim. On the first Business Day after the 30-day period ends, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount claimed to Buyer from the Indemnity Escrow Fund.

(c) *Disputed Claims.* If Seller timely delivers an Objection Notice, the Escrow Agent shall promptly disburse to Buyer any portion of the claimed amount not disputed in the Objection Notice. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amount in dispute (the "**Disputed Amount**") in the Indemnity Escrow Fund. It shall disburse the Disputed Amount only on receipt of a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order.

**4.2 Purchase Price Adjustment.** If any downward adjustment is payable by Seller under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement and Buyer elects to recover it from the Indemnity Escrow Fund under Section 2.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall deliver a Joint Written Instruction to the Escrow Agent directing disbursement of that amount to Buyer. If the amount was determined by the independent accounting firm, Buyer may instead deliver a Final Order. Seller shall restore the Indemnity Escrow Fund by the amount so disbursed in accordance with Section 2.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.3 Recovery from Indemnity Escrow Fund.** The Indemnity Escrow Fund shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery of the Buyer Indemnified Parties for any Losses under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. No Buyer Indemnified Party shall have recourse against Seller, any Affiliate of Seller or any of their respective assets for any such Losses in excess of the funds then remaining in the Indemnity Escrow Fund. Claims against the Indemnity Escrow Fund shall be made and determined by reference to Losses as defined in this Agreement.

**4.4 Release.** On the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the then remaining balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund. Before doing so, it shall deduct the aggregate amount of all claims described in Claim Notices received before the Release Date that have not then been finally resolved and paid. The Escrow Agent shall retain each retained amount until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or Final Order with respect to the related claim. It shall then disburse that amount as directed, and shall promptly release to Seller any portion of the retained amount no longer subject to a pending claim.

**4.5 Joint Written Instructions and Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds from the Indemnity Escrow Fund only in accordance with Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.4, a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall promptly execute and deliver Joint Written Instructions as needed to give effect to any agreed resolution of a claim or to any Final Order. The Escrow Agent shall make each disbursement within two Business Days after receipt of the applicable instruction or order, by wire transfer to the account designated in it.

**4.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Indemnity Escrow Fund is insufficient to pay all amounts then due, the Escrow Agent shall pay claims in the order in which the related Claim Notices were received.

## 5. DUTIES AND RIGHTS OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**5.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent has only the duties expressly stated in this Agreement, which are purely ministerial. No implied duties or obligations apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not responsible for determining whether any party has complied with it.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by an Authorized Representative of the proper party. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into the validity or accuracy of any such document. The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and is fully protected for any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**5.3 Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except to the extent of its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, as determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, punitive or consequential damages.

**5.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to losses caused by the gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud of the Escrow Agent. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear 50% of any amount paid under this Section 5.4, except that a party whose breach of this Agreement caused the amount to be incurred shall bear it in full. This Section 5.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**5.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions concerning the Indemnity Escrow Fund, it may take either of the following actions until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order resolving the conflict:

(a) refrain from acting; or

(b) file an action in interpleader in a court described in Section 9.5 and deposit the Indemnity Escrow Fund with the clerk of that court.

**5.6 Security Procedures.** If the Escrow Agent receives wire transfer instructions other than in writing at the time of execution of this Agreement, it shall confirm them by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative listed on Schedule 1 before making the disbursement.

## 6. FEES AND EXPENSES

**6.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 2, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable counsel fees, incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement.

**6.2 Allocation.** In accordance with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer shall bear 50% and Seller shall bear 50% of all fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Indemnity Escrow Fund.

## 7. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Reporting.** Buyer is treated as the owner of the Indemnity Escrow Fund for federal and applicable state income Tax purposes until the Indemnity Escrow Fund is disbursed. All interest and earnings on the Indemnity Escrow Fund shall be reported as income of Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall issue IRS Forms 1099 or other applicable information returns accordingly.

**7.2 Tax Documentation.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent, on or before the date of this Agreement, a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8.

**7.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax law. It shall remit any amount withheld to the appropriate Governmental Authority, and each amount so remitted is treated as paid to the Person in respect of whom the withholding was made.

## 8. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL; TERMINATION

**8.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent within that period. If they fail to do so, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 9.5 to appoint a successor.

**8.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving it at least 15 days' written notice and appointing a successor escrow agent.

**8.3 Transfer to Successor.** On acceptance of appointment by a successor escrow agent, the resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall deliver the Indemnity Escrow Fund and all related records to the successor. The resigning or removed Escrow Agent is then discharged from further duties under this Agreement, but remains entitled to the benefit of Sections 5.3 and 5.4.

**8.4 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed the entire Indemnity Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth below each party's signature or to such other address as a party specifies by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**9.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**9.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any permitted assignee under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, succeeds to the Escrow Agent under this Agreement without further act.

**9.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**9.5 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the federal or state courts located in Wilmington, Delaware. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.6 Patriot Act.** The parties acknowledge that federal law requires the Escrow Agent to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account. Each party shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests for that purpose.

**9.7 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, governmental action, and failure of communications or utilities.

**9.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
