# KESTRELLE IMMERSION SYSTEMS, INC.

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**September 29, 2026**

The undersigned are all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Kestrelle Immersion Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Acting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the bylaws of the Company, they adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. Each resolution has the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called meeting of the Board.

## 1. RECITALS

1.1 **The Proposed Transaction.** Gourain Industrial Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), is the record and beneficial owner of all 4,200,000 issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**"). Seller has negotiated with Seipal Thermal Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), the sale to Buyer of all of the Shares (the "**Transaction**").

1.2 **The Purchase Agreement.** The Board has been presented with the proposed form of Stock Purchase Agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Company (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase the Shares for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement. At the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"), $13,950,000 of the purchase price will be deposited with Larchmont Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent, under an Indemnity Escrow Agreement substantially in the form attached to the Purchase Agreement as Exhibit B (the "**Escrow Agreement**").

1.3 **Company Obligations.** The Company is a party to the Purchase Agreement. It will give representations and warranties, agree to covenants about the conduct of its business before the Closing, and agree to deliver certain closing documents, as set out in the Purchase Agreement.

1.4 **Facility Lease.** The Company leases its manufacturing and service facility from Dunmore Realty Partners under the Lease Agreement dated as of June 1, 2021 (the "**Facility Lease**"). Section 18.2 of the Facility Lease requires the landlord's consent to a change of control of the Company.

1.5 **Ashgrove Supply Agreement.** The Company is a party to the Master Cooling Systems Supply Agreement dated March 14, 2024 (the "**Ashgrove Supply Agreement**") with Ashgrove Cloud Infrastructure LLC ("**Ashgrove**"). The Company designs, supplies, installs and services immersion cooling systems for Ashgrove's hyperscale data center campuses under that agreement. Management has advised the Board that the Ashgrove Supply Agreement accounted for approximately 38% of the Company's revenue for fiscal year 2025 and for the six months ended June 30, 2026. Section 14.3 of the Ashgrove Supply Agreement permits Ashgrove to terminate the agreement on written notice if a change of control of the Company occurs without Ashgrove's prior written consent. The sale of the Shares to Buyer will be a change of control for that purpose.

1.6 **Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement and has received a presentation from management and the Company's counsel on their terms. The Board has also reviewed the Company's principal commercial relationships. Having considered these matters, the Board has determined that the Transaction and the Company's entry into the Purchase Agreement are advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its sole stockholder.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions:

## 2. APPROVAL OF THE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

2.1 **RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the Company's execution, delivery and performance of it, are authorized, adopted and approved in all respects.

2.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Company. The Purchase Agreement may be executed substantially in the form presented to the Board, with any changes that the Authorized Officer executing it approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company. Execution by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. ESCROW AGREEMENT AND RELATED DOCUMENTS

3.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Board acknowledges the form of Escrow Agreement and the deposit of $13,950,000 of the purchase price with the escrow agent at the Closing, and approves the Company's performance of any obligations relating to the Escrow Agreement under the Purchase Agreement.

3.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Company, all certificates, instruments and other documents that the Company is required to deliver under the Purchase Agreement. These include the officer's certificate, the secretary's certificate certifying these resolutions, and the closing statement under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. THIRD-PARTY CONSENTS AND CUSTOMER MATTERS

4.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are directed to request, before the Closing, the consent of Dunmore Realty Partners to the change of control of the Company under Section 18.2 of the Facility Lease. They shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, as contemplated by Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, in light of the confidentiality obligations in the Purchase Agreement and the importance of the Ashgrove relationship, the Authorized Officers shall not contact Ashgrove about the Transaction before the Closing. After the Closing, the Authorized Officers, together with Buyer's representatives, are directed to approach Ashgrove about the change of control of the Company and to seek Ashgrove's written confirmation that the Ashgrove Supply Agreement will continue in effect.

4.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to make any other filings and give any other notices to Governmental Authorities and third parties that they determine are necessary or advisable to consummate the Transaction. These include any filing required under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended.

## 5. RESIGNATIONS AND TRANSITION

5.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Board acknowledges that, under Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer will deliver written resignations effective at the Closing. The Authorized Officers are authorized to take any action needed to give effect to those resignations and to the transition of the Company's management to Buyer.

## 6. GENERAL AUTHORITY

6.1 **RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer, the Secretary of the Company and any other officer designated by an Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all further actions to carry out the purposes of these resolutions. This includes executing and delivering all agreements, instruments and documents and paying all fees and expenses that such officer considers necessary or advisable.

6.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any director or officer of the Company in connection with the Transaction and the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

6.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary of the Company is directed to file this consent with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

## 7. EFFECTIVENESS

7.1 This consent is effective as of the date first written above. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
