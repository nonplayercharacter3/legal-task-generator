**Re: Proposed Acquisition of the Cervical and Lumbar Interbody Fusion Implant Business of Pisen Spine Technologies, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Muvux Orthopedics, LLC ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire from Pisen Spine Technologies, Inc. ("**Seller**") substantially all of the assets of Seller's business of designing, manufacturing, marketing, distributing and selling cervical and lumbar interbody fusion implants and related instrumentation (the "**Business**"). The transaction described in this Letter is referred to as the "**Transaction**."

Except as expressly provided in Paragraph 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the Parties' present intentions. It is intended only as a basis for negotiating a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Structure of the Transaction

The Transaction will be structured as a purchase of assets. Buyer will acquire the Purchased Assets described in Paragraph 3 and will assume only the Assumed Liabilities described in Paragraph 4. Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 2. Purchase Price

(a) The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets will be $68,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). It will be paid as follows:

(i) $54,000,000 in cash at the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"); and

(ii) $14,000,000 by a subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller (the "**Seller Note**").

(b) The Seller Note is expected to bear interest at 6.0% per annum, payable quarterly, and to mature on the fourth anniversary of the Closing Date. It will be subordinated to Buyer's senior indebtedness on terms to be agreed. The Seller Note will be subject to Buyer's right of set-off for indemnification amounts finally determined to be owed by Seller.

(c) The cash portion of the Purchase Price will be subject to a customary adjustment based on a physical count of inventory conducted shortly before Closing, against a target inventory value to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement.

## 3. Purchased Assets

The Purchased Assets will include all of Seller's right, title and interest in the assets, properties and rights used or held for use primarily in the Business, free and clear of all liens other than customary permitted liens. These include:

(a) inventory of implants, spacers, cages and instrumentation, including consigned inventory;

(b) 510(k) clearances, CE certificates and other regulatory authorizations, together with design history files, device master records, technical files and complaint files, to the extent transferable;

(c) patents, trademarks, know-how and other intellectual property;

(d) assigned customer, distribution, group purchasing organization and supply contracts;

(e) tooling, molds, machinery and equipment; and

(f) books, records, customer lists and supplier lists of the Business.

Cash, pre-Closing accounts receivable, Seller's corporate and tax records, insurance policies and employee benefit plans will be excluded.

## 4. Assumed Liabilities and Excluded Liabilities

(a) Buyer will assume only (i) liabilities under assigned contracts to the extent arising and relating to periods after the Closing, other than liabilities arising from any pre-Closing breach by Seller, (ii) trade payables of the Business incurred in the ordinary course after the Closing, and (iii) liabilities for product liability, personal injury, product warranty and field corrective action claims relating to products manufactured and sold by Buyer after the Closing (together, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

(b) Seller will retain all other liabilities of Seller and the Business (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Pre-closing product liability will be retained by Seller as an Excluded Liability. This includes all liabilities for product liability, personal injury, property damage, product warranty, recall and field corrective action claims relating to products manufactured or sold by Seller before the Closing. The Excluded Liabilities will also include pre-Closing taxes, pre-Closing environmental liabilities, liabilities relating to pre-Closing employment matters, Seller's indebtedness, and all liabilities arising out of the warning letter issued by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration to Seller, including the cost of all remediation and corrective and preventive actions.

## 5. Due Diligence

Following execution of this Letter, Seller will give Buyer and its advisers reasonable access to the books, records, facilities, personnel, regulatory files, complaint and adverse event records, and product liability claim history of the Business. Buyer's obligations under the Definitive Agreement will be conditioned on satisfactory completion of this review.

## 6. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnification provisions customary for transactions of this type. These will cover regulatory compliance, product liability claims, intellectual property, title and sufficiency of assets. Seller will indemnify Buyer for breaches of its representations, warranties and covenants and for all Excluded Liabilities. Survival periods, baskets and caps will be negotiated in the Definitive Agreement.

## 7. Covenants

The Definitive Agreement will include:

(a) customary covenants by Seller to operate the Business in the ordinary course before Closing;

(b) cooperation in transferring regulatory authorizations to Buyer;

(c) a five-year worldwide non-competition covenant by Seller covering cervical and lumbar interbody fusion implants; and

(d) a transition services agreement on terms to be agreed.

## 8. Employees

Buyer expects to offer employment, effective at Closing, to selected employees of the Business. Seller will remain responsible for all compensation and benefits owed to its employees through the Closing.

## 9. Conditions

Closing will be subject to:

(a) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and the related ancillary agreements;

(b) satisfactory completion of Buyer's due diligence;

(c) receipt of required third-party consents;

(d) approval by the boards of directors or managers of each Party and, as required, Seller's stockholders; and

(e) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

## 10. Timing

The Parties will use good faith efforts to execute the Definitive Agreement by September 15, 2026, and to complete the Closing promptly afterwards.

## 11. Expenses

Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only this Paragraph 12 and Paragraphs 11, 13, 14 and 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the Parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any binding obligation. No Party will have any obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered. The Binding Provisions will survive any termination of this Letter.

## 13. Exclusivity

For sixty (60) days after the date of Seller's countersignature, Seller will not, directly or indirectly, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer concerning any sale of the Business or any material portion of its assets, other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course.

## 14. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter are confidential. Neither Party will disclose them except to its professional advisers and financing sources who are bound by confidentiality obligations, or as required by law.

## 15. Governing Law; Termination

The Binding Provisions are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. This Letter will terminate on the earliest of:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(b) written notice of termination by either Party; or

(c) the expiration of the exclusivity period in Paragraph 13, if the Definitive Agreement has not been executed by then.
