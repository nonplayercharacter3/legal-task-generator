# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This **ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026, by and between **Muvux Orthopedics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Pisen Spine Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of designing, manufacturing, marketing, distributing and selling cervical and lumbar interbody fusion implants and related instrumentation (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. PURCHASE AND SALE

### 1.1 Definitions

For purposes of this Agreement:

(a) "**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each to be delivered at Closing.

(b) "**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

(c) "**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud by a Party in the making of a representation or warranty in this Agreement, with actual knowledge of its falsity and the intent to induce the other Party to rely on it.

(d) "**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization and Good Standing), 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), 4.3 (Title to Purchased Assets) and 4.9 (Brokers), and Sections 5.1 (Organization), 5.2 (Authority; Enforceability) and 5.6 (Brokers).

(e) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(f) "**Products**" means all cervical and lumbar interbody fusion implants, spacers, cages and associated instrumentation designed, manufactured, marketed, distributed or sold by or on behalf of Seller in the Business, including those listed in Section 1.1(f) of the Disclosure Schedule.

(g) "**Regulatory Authorizations**" means all 510(k) clearances, CE certificates, establishment registrations, device listings and other permits issued by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the "**FDA**") or any other governmental authority relating to the Products.

(h) "**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the form attached as Exhibit B, to be issued by Buyer to Seller and dated the Closing Date.

(i) "**Warning Letter**" means the warning letter issued by the FDA to Seller relating to Seller's quality system for the Products, as described in Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule.

### 1.2 Purchased Assets; Excluded Assets

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights used or held for use primarily in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(i) all inventory of Products, including finished goods, work-in-process, raw materials and consigned inventory held at hospitals and distributors;

(ii) all Regulatory Authorizations, design history files, device master records, technical files and complaint files relating to the Products, to the extent transferable;

(iii) all patents, trademarks, know-how and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business;

(iv) all contracts listed in Section 1.2(a)(iv) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(v) all tooling, molds, machinery and equipment used primarily in the manufacture of the Products; and

(vi) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists of the Business.

(b) The Purchased Assets exclude cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable arising before the Closing, Seller's corporate records, tax returns and tax refunds, insurance policies, employee benefit plans, and the assets listed in Section 1.2(b) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Excluded Assets**").

### 1.3 Assumed Liabilities

At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge when due only the following liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising and relating to periods after the Closing, other than any liability arising from a breach or default by Seller before the Closing;

(b) all trade payables of the Business to third parties incurred in the ordinary course after the Closing; and

(c) all liabilities for product liability, personal injury, property damage, product warranty and field corrective action claims relating to the Products, whether manufactured or sold before or after the Closing.

### 1.4 Excluded Liabilities

Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all liabilities for taxes of Seller, and all taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(b) all liabilities arising under environmental laws relating to the ownership or operation of the Business or Seller's facilities before the Closing;

(c) all liabilities arising out of or relating to the Warning Letter, including all costs of remediation, corrective and preventive actions, and any related FDA enforcement action;

(d) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets;

(e) all liabilities relating to current or former employees of Seller arising before the Closing, including under any employee benefit plan; and

(f) all indebtedness of Seller.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE PRICE

### 2.1 Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $68,000,000, of which $54,000,000 is payable in cash at Closing and $14,000,000 by Seller Note (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

### 2.2 Payment at Closing

At the Closing, Buyer shall (a) pay $54,000,000 to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, and (b) execute and deliver the Seller Note to Seller.

### 2.3 Inventory Adjustment

(a) Seller and Buyer shall jointly conduct a physical count of the inventory of Products within five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date. If the book value of inventory determined by that count is less than $6,200,000 (the "**Target Inventory Value**"), the cash portion of the Purchase Price payable at Closing shall be reduced dollar for dollar by the shortfall.

(b) Any dispute regarding the inventory count that the Parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date shall be referred to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. The fees of the accounting firm shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

### 2.4 Allocation

The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, under a schedule prepared by Buyer and delivered to Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. The Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

### 2.5 Withholding

Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be deducted and withheld under applicable tax law. Any amount so withheld shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

### 3.1 Closing

The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

### 3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables

At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller; (b) letters to the FDA transferring the Regulatory Authorizations to Buyer; (c) releases of all liens on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Liens; (d) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying board and stockholder resolutions approving this Agreement; and (e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

### 3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables

At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the cash payment under Section 2.2(a); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and (d) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule delivered by Seller concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

### 4.1 Organization and Good Standing

Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has all corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business as currently conducted.

### 4.2 Authority; Enforceability

Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

### 4.3 Title to Purchased Assets

Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. "**Permitted Liens**" means liens for taxes not yet due and payable and the liens listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

### 4.4 Sufficiency of Assets

The Purchased Assets, together with the services to be provided under the Transition Services Agreement, constitute all of the assets necessary to conduct the Business in all material respects as currently conducted.

### 4.5 Material Contracts

Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Business with annual payments in excess of $250,000, each distribution agreement and each group purchasing organization agreement. Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, each other party to it, and Seller is not in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

### 4.6 Intellectual Property

Seller owns or has a valid right to use all intellectual property used in the Business. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property of any Person.

### 4.7 Regulatory Matters

Except for the Warning Letter, Seller holds all Regulatory Authorizations required to manufacture and sell the Products as currently manufactured and sold, and has not received any FDA Form 483, warning letter, untitled letter or notice of recall relating to the Products within the three (3) years before the date of this Agreement. Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all field corrective actions and recalls of the Products within that period.

### 4.8 Product Liability

Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all product liability claims and lawsuits relating to the Products pending, or to Seller's knowledge threatened, as of the date of this Agreement, and all such claims resolved within the three (3) years before that date.

### 4.9 Brokers

No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

### 4.10 Taxes

Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and has paid all taxes shown as due on those returns.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

### 5.1 Organization

Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. Muvux Orthopedics Holdings, Inc. (the "**Parent**") is the sole member of Buyer.

### 5.2 Authority; Enforceability

Buyer has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

### 5.3 No Conflicts

The execution, delivery and performance by Buyer of this Agreement and the Seller Note do not violate Buyer's certificate of formation or limited liability company agreement or any law applicable to Buyer.

### 5.4 Sufficiency of Funds

Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

### 5.5 Parent Transaction

Parent has entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated August 27, 2026 with Ardentis Surgical Partners, L.P. (the "**Parent Merger Agreement**"), under which an affiliate of Ardentis Surgical Partners, L.P. will merge with and into Parent, with Parent surviving. The Parent Merger Agreement does not prohibit Buyer from entering into or performing this Agreement or the Seller Note, and no consent of any party to the Parent Merger Agreement is required for Buyer to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

### 5.6 Brokers

No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

### 6.1 Conduct of Business

Between the date of this Agreement and the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, maintain all Regulatory Authorizations in full force and effect, and not sell, license or encumber any Purchased Asset other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course.

### 6.2 Access

Between the date of this Agreement and the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Business.

### 6.3 Regulatory Transfer

The Parties shall cooperate to transfer the Regulatory Authorizations to Buyer as of the Closing Date and to notify the FDA and applicable notified bodies of that transfer. Seller shall continue, at its own cost, all corrective and preventive actions required under the Warning Letter until the FDA has issued a close-out letter.

### 6.4 Employees

Buyer may offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to the employees of Seller listed in Section 6.4 of the Disclosure Schedule. Seller shall pay all wages, bonuses and accrued paid time off owed to those employees through the Closing Date.

### 6.5 Non-Competition

For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, design, manufacture, market or sell any cervical or lumbar interbody fusion implant anywhere in the world.

### 6.6 Further Assurances

After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out the intent of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

### 7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations

The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any order or law that prohibits the Closing.

### 7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations

The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date (and the Fundamental Representations of Seller being true and correct in all respects); (b) Seller having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) delivery of a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the satisfaction of clauses (a) and (b); and (d) receipt of the third-party consents listed in Section 7.2(d) of the Disclosure Schedule.

### 7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations

The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and (c) delivery of a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the satisfaction of clauses (a) and (b).

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

### 8.1 Termination

This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either Party if the other Party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within twenty (20) days after written notice.

### 8.2 Effect of Termination

On termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a Party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

### 9.1 Survival

The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed.

### 9.2 Indemnification by Seller

From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

### 9.3 Indemnification by Buyer

From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty, or any breach of any covenant or agreement, of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(b) any Assumed Liability.

### 9.4 Basket

Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), and Buyer shall not be liable under Section 9.3(a) for breach of representations and warranties, until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $680,000, after which the indemnifying Party shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount. The basket in this Section 9.4 does not apply to Losses arising from inaccuracies in Fundamental Representations or from Fraud.

### 9.5 Caps

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2 shall not exceed 12% of the Purchase Price ($8,160,000) (the "**Seller Indemnity Cap**"), and the aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to the Seller Indemnity Cap.

(b) The Seller Indemnity Cap does not apply to Losses arising from Fundamental Representations, Fraud, and claims under Section 9.2(c) for Excluded Liabilities. Seller's aggregate liability for Losses arising from Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) No cap, basket or other limitation in this Article IX applies to claims under Section 9.3(b).

### 9.6 Claims Procedure

(a) An indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim for indemnification, describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

(b) The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party by written notice within twenty (20) days after receipt of notice of the claim. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified Party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

### 9.7 Set-Off

Buyer may set off any amount finally determined to be owed to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX against amounts then due or to become due under the Seller Note, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

### 9.8 Exclusive Remedy

From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, except for claims for Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief.

### 9.9 Tax Treatment

The Parties shall treat all indemnification payments under this Agreement as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PUBLICITY

### 10.1 Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and all non-public information received from the other Party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers and financing sources bound by confidentiality obligations. After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business.

### 10.2 Public Announcements

No Party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law or stock exchange rules.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

### 11.1 Expenses

Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

### 11.2 Notices

All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a Party designates by notice.

### 11.3 Amendment and Waiver

This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

### 11.4 Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to an affiliate or, after the Closing, to any purchaser of all or substantially all of the Business, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

### 11.5 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

### 11.6 Governing Law

This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

### 11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial

Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the federal or state courts located in the State of Delaware). EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

### 11.8 Specific Performance

The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

### 11.9 Order of Precedence

In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and the Seller Note, the Seller Note shall control. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any other Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall control. This Agreement supersedes the non-binding letter of intent between Buyer and Seller relating to the Business.

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
