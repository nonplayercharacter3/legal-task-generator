FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **MUVUX ORTHOPEDICS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **PISEN SPINE TECHNOLOGIES, INC.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its permitted successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest described below, on the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in, and is issued under Section 2.2(b) of, the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026, between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**").

## 1. Principal; Definitions

(a) **Principal.** The principal amount of this Note is $14,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). Maker shall pay the Principal Amount, to the extent not previously paid or reduced by set-off under Section 8, on the Maturity Date.

(b) **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

(i) "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

(ii) "**Change of Control**" means, with respect to any Person: (A) any transaction or series of related transactions as a result of which any Person or group of Persons acting together, other than the Persons holding such equity interests on the Closing Date, acquires beneficial ownership, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting equity interests of that Person; (B) any merger, consolidation or other business combination involving that Person, whether or not that Person is the surviving entity, after which the holders of that Person's voting equity interests immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity interests of the surviving or resulting entity or its ultimate parent; or (C) the sale, lease or transfer of all or substantially all of the assets of that Person.

(iii) "**Default Rate**" has the meaning given in Section 7(c).

(iv) "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

(v) "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all indebtedness for borrowed money of Maker owed to banks, insurance companies or other institutional lenders under any credit facility in effect on or after the Closing Date, together with all interest, fees and expenses in respect of that indebtedness, and any refinancing of it.

(vi) "**Senior Lender**" means any holder of Senior Indebtedness or any agent acting on its behalf.

## 2. Interest

(a) **Rate.** The outstanding Principal Amount bears interest from the date of this Note until paid in full at a rate of 6.0% per annum, payable quarterly.

(b) **Payment Dates.** Accrued interest is payable in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date after the date of this Note, and on the Maturity Date.

(c) **Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

(d) **Maximum Rate.** No interest or other amount payable under this Note shall exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount.

## 3. Maturity

The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, is due and payable in full on the fourth anniversary of the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

## 4. Payments; Prepayment

(a) **Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Holder at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date.

(b) **Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the Principal Amount.

(c) **Voluntary Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon not less than five (5) Business Days' written notice to Holder, together with accrued interest on the amount prepaid.

## 5. Subordination

(a) **Subordination.** The payment of principal and interest under this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness, to the extent and in the manner provided in this Section 5 and in any subordination agreement entered into among Holder, Maker and any Senior Lender on terms reasonably acceptable to Holder.

(b) **Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or other event of default under the Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing and the Senior Lender has given written notice of it to Holder, Maker may make, and Holder may receive, all regularly scheduled payments of interest under Section 2 and the payment of principal on the Maturity Date.

(c) **Turnover.** Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 5 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly paid over to it.

(d) **Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or reorganization proceeding involving Maker, all Senior Indebtedness shall be paid in full in cash before Holder is entitled to receive any payment under this Note.

(e) **No Impairment.** Nothing in this Section 5 impairs, as between Maker and Holder, the obligation of Maker to pay the amounts due under this Note in accordance with its terms.

## 6. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) **Payment.** Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest or other amount under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after the date it is due, in each case other than a payment prohibited by Section 5 or reduced by set-off under Section 8;

(b) **Covenants.** Maker fails to perform any other obligation under this Note, and that failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Holder;

(c) **Voluntary Insolvency.** Maker commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(d) **Involuntary Insolvency.** An involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(e) **Change of Control.** Maker, or any Person that directly or indirectly controls Maker, undergoes a Change of Control without the prior written consent of Holder; or

(f) **Acceleration of Senior Indebtedness.** The Senior Indebtedness is accelerated following an event of default under the agreements governing it, and that acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days.

## 7. Remedies

(a) **Acceleration.** On the occurrence of any Event of Default under Section 6(a), 6(b), 6(e) or 6(f), and at any time while it is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount of $14,000,000, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, immediately due and payable.

(b) **Automatic Acceleration.** On the occurrence of any Event of Default under Section 6(c) or 6(d), the entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, becomes immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder.

(c) **Default Interest.** From and after the occurrence of any Event of Default and for so long as it is continuing, the outstanding Principal Amount and, to the extent permitted by law, all overdue interest shall bear interest at a rate equal to four percent (4%) per annum above the rate set out in Section 2(a) (the "**Default Rate**"), payable on demand.

(d) **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in collecting any amount due under this Note after an Event of Default.

(e) **Subject to Subordination.** The exercise of remedies by Holder under this Section 7 is subject to Section 5.

## 8. Set-Off; Indemnification

(a) **Right of Set-Off.** In accordance with Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off against any principal or interest then due or to become due under this Note any amount finally determined to be owed to any Buyer Indemnitee under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. An amount is finally determined when it has been agreed in writing by Maker and Holder or determined by a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction. Maker shall give Holder written notice of any set-off, specifying the amount and the claim to which it relates. Each set-off is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the Principal Amount, in inverse order of maturity, and is treated as payment of that amount under this Note.

(b) **Limitation on Holder Liability.** Holder's aggregate liability for all claims under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement, including claims under Section 9.2(c) for Excluded Liabilities, whether recovered by set-off under this Section 8 or otherwise, shall not exceed the Seller Indemnity Cap, except for Losses arising from Fraud.

(c) **Pending Claims.** If a Buyer Indemnitee has delivered a notice of claim under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement that has not been finally determined, Maker may withhold from any payment then due under this Note an amount equal to Maker's good faith estimate of the Losses claimed, until the claim is finally determined. Any amount withheld and later determined not to be owed shall be paid to Holder within five (5) Business Days after that determination, together with interest at the rate set out in Section 2(a). Withholding in accordance with this Section 8(c) is not an Event of Default.

## 9. Covenants of Maker

So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker shall:

(a) maintain its existence as a limited liability company in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) deliver to Holder, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Maker's annual financial statements; and

(c) promptly notify Holder in writing of the occurrence of any Event of Default.

## 10. Waivers

Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note impairs that right or operates as a waiver of it, and no single or partial exercise of any right precludes any further exercise of it.

## 11. Assignment and Transfer

Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker, and any purported transfer without that consent is void. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, except to a permitted assignee of Maker under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement that assumes this Note in writing, in which case Maker remains liable for its obligations under this Note. This Note binds and benefits the parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 12. Notices

All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, set out in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 13. Amendment

This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

(b) Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the federal or state courts located in the State of Delaware) for any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note.

(c) EACH OF MAKER AND HOLDER WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 15. Severability

If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

## 16. Lost or Destroyed Note

On receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note, and of an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor and amount.
