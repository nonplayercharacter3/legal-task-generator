# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this **"Schedule"**) is entered into as of **March 1, 2025** between **Fouveirt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**), and **Pruran Distribution Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company (**"Distributor"**). It is made under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 1, 2025 (the **"Agreement"**). It sets out the performance standards that apply to Distributor's storage, handling and logistics services under Section 8 of the Agreement and the Statement of Work.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Statement of Work. These terms include "Products," "Specifications," "Recall," "Customer," "Contract Year," "Service Fees" and "Statement of Work."

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Schedule:

**"Facility"** means each warehouse or storage location that Distributor uses to store or handle Products, including each Facility identified in the Statement of Work.

**"Handling Failure"** means any of the following:

(a) physical damage to Products, or to their packaging, while the Products are in Distributor's care, custody or control;

(b) a failure to store Products in accordance with Section 3;

(c) a failure to segregate, quarantine or hold Products as required by Section 3.4 or the Statement of Work;

(d) a mis-pick, mis-shipment or lot-recording error that affects lot traceability; or

(e) any other failure to handle Products in accordance with the Specifications or Section 8 of the Agreement.

**"Measurement Period"** means each calendar month.

**"Order Accuracy"** means the percentage of Customer order lines shipped in a Measurement Period with the correct SKU, quantity and lot status.

**"On-Time Shipment"** means a Customer order that is shipped from a Facility within the time stated in Section 4.1.

**"Temperature Excursion"** means any period in which the recorded temperature or relative humidity in a storage zone, or in a trailer carrying Products, is outside the range required by Section 3 or by the Specifications. The excursion begins at the first out-of-range reading and ends at the first in-range reading after it.

## 2. GENERAL

**2.1 Performance Standard.** Distributor shall meet or exceed each service level in this Schedule in each Measurement Period.

**2.2 Measurement.** Distributor shall measure performance against each service level using data from the WMS and from the monitoring system described in Section 3.3 of the Statement of Work. Distributor shall report its performance in the monthly report required by Section 5.2 of the Statement of Work.

**2.3 Supplier Audit of Data.** Supplier may audit the data used to measure performance on five (5) business days' notice. If an audit shows that Distributor has overstated its performance, Distributor shall recalculate the service credits for the affected Measurement Periods. Distributor shall also reimburse Supplier's reasonable audit costs if the overstatement affected any service credit.

## 3. STORAGE AND TEMPERATURE STANDARDS

**3.1 Dry Products.** Distributor shall store dry Products at no more than 80°F (27°C) and at relative humidity of no more than 65%.

**3.2 Canned Products.** Distributor shall store canned Products in dedicated, temperature-mapped zones within the range stated in the Specifications. Where the Specifications do not state a range, the range is 40°F (4°C) to 85°F (29°C). Canned Products must never be exposed to freezing conditions.

**3.3 Transport.** For each shipment of Products from a Facility, Distributor shall:

(a) use only clean, dry, odor-free trailers in good repair;

(b) between November 1 and March 31, carry canned Products only in heated or insulated trailers that can maintain the range in Section 3.2 for the full transit time; and

(c) for any transit of more than four (4) hours, use trailers fitted with temperature recorders, and keep the recorder data with the shipment records.

**3.4 Quarantine.** Distributor shall place on hold in the WMS any Product affected by a Temperature Excursion or Handling Failure within two (2) hours of detecting it. Distributor shall keep the affected Product on hold until Supplier gives written disposition instructions.

**3.5 Excursion Notice.** Distributor shall notify Supplier's account manager of any Temperature Excursion lasting more than sixty (60) minutes. Distributor shall give that notice within four (4) hours of detecting the excursion and confirm it in writing within twenty-four (24) hours. This notice is in addition to Distributor's notice obligations under Section 8.5 of the Agreement.

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS

**4.1 Service Level Table.** Distributor shall meet the following service levels:

| No. | Service Level | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| SL-1 | Temperature compliance | At least 99.5% of logged storage-zone readings within range | Readings logged under Section 3.3 of the Statement of Work |
| SL-2 | Temperature Excursion duration | No Temperature Excursion longer than 4 consecutive hours | Per storage zone, per event |
| SL-3 | On-Time Shipment | At least 98% of Customer orders received by 12:00 noon Eastern Time shipped the same business day; all other orders shipped by the next business day | Per Measurement Period |
| SL-4 | Order Accuracy | At least 99.7% of order lines | Per Measurement Period |
| SL-5 | Inventory accuracy | At least 99.5% at the SKU and lot level | Monthly cycle counts |
| SL-6 | Receiving | 100% of inbound shipments received and recorded in the WMS within 24 hours | Per Measurement Period |
| SL-7 | Damage rate | No more than 0.15% of cases handled damaged while in Distributor's care, custody or control | Per Measurement Period |
| SL-8 | Recall hold | 100% of affected lots placed on hold within 2 hours of Supplier's Recall notice | Per Recall |
| SL-9 | Traceability report | Lot-level distribution report delivered within 24 hours of request | Per request |

**4.2 Exclusions.** A failure to meet a service level is excused to the extent it is caused by:

(a) Supplier's breach of the Agreement or the Statement of Work;

(b) a Customer order that exceeds Distributor's most recent forecast by more than twenty percent (20%), but only for SL-3; or

(c) a force majeure event under Section 16.6 of the Agreement.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS AND REMEDIES

**5.1 Service Credits.** If Distributor fails to meet a service level in a Measurement Period, Distributor shall credit Supplier the following percentage of the monthly Service Fee for that Measurement Period:

| Service Level | Credit per Failure |
|---|---|
| SL-1, SL-2, SL-7, SL-8 | 3% |
| SL-3, SL-4, SL-5, SL-6, SL-9 | 1.5% |

The total service credits for any Measurement Period shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the monthly Service Fee for that Measurement Period.

**5.2 Application of Credits.** Distributor shall apply service credits against the next monthly Service Fee invoice. Any credit outstanding at the end of the Statement of Work shall be paid to Supplier within thirty (30) days.

**5.3 Product Losses.** Distributor shall reimburse Supplier for Products damaged, destroyed or rendered unsaleable by a Temperature Excursion or Handling Failure. For Supplier-owned Inventory, reimbursement is at Supplier's standard cost. For Distributor-owned Inventory, Distributor bears the loss and is not entitled to any credit from Supplier. Distributor shall also pay the costs of disposal of those Products at Supplier's direction.

**5.4 Liability for Temperature Excursions and Handling Failures.** Distributor's aggregate liability for Temperature Excursions and Handling Failures, including any indemnity or recall costs, is subject to Section 12.2 of the Agreement, notwithstanding Section 12.3 of the Agreement.

**5.5 Corrective Action.** If Distributor fails to meet any service level, Distributor shall give Supplier a written root-cause analysis and corrective action plan within ten (10) business days after the end of the Measurement Period. Distributor shall implement the plan within the time stated in it, as Supplier reasonably approves.

**5.6 Chronic Failure.** A **"Chronic Failure"** occurs if Distributor:

(a) fails to meet SL-1, SL-2 or SL-8 in any two (2) Measurement Periods within any six (6) consecutive Measurement Periods; or

(b) fails to meet any three (3) service levels in the same Measurement Period.

A Chronic Failure is a material breach of the Statement of Work for the purposes of Section 8.2 of the Statement of Work and Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

## 6. REVIEW

**6.1 Quarterly Review.** The Parties shall review performance against this Schedule at each quarterly business review held under Section 5.3 of the Statement of Work.

**6.2 Changes.** This Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

## 7. GENERAL

**7.1 Incorporation.** This Schedule forms part of the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

**7.2 Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
