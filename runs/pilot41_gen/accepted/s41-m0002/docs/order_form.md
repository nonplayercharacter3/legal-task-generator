# ORDER FORM

**Order Form No.:** FPN-PDP-001
**Order Form Date:** March 1, 2025

This Order Form is entered into as of March 1, 2025 between **Fouveirt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**), and **Pruran Distribution Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company (**"Distributor"**). It is made under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 1, 2025 (the **"Agreement"**). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PRODUCTS

**"Products"** means all pet food manufactured by Supplier and sold under the Fouveirt brand. This includes the dry and canned dog and cat food listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement, including Veterinary Diet Products, and Supplier's freeze-dried dog and cat treats, as Supplier's product range is updated from time to time.

## 2. INITIAL TERM

The Initial Term is three years from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date. The Initial Term begins on March 1, 2025 and ends on February 28, 2028, unless the Agreement is renewed under Section 4.2 of the Agreement or terminated earlier under Section 14 of the Agreement.

## 3. TERRITORY AND CUSTOMERS

**3.1 Territory.** The Territory is New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania.

**3.2 House Accounts.** The House Accounts are those listed in Exhibit B to the Agreement.

## 4. MINIMUM ANNUAL PURCHASE COMMITMENT

**4.1 Commitment.** Distributor's minimum annual purchase commitment under Section 5.3 of the Agreement is $6,200,000 of Products per Contract Year.

**4.2 Calculation.** Purchases count toward the minimum annual purchase commitment based on the net invoiced price of Products shipped by Supplier to Distributor during the Contract Year, excluding taxes, freight and any credits issued under Section 10.3 of the Agreement. Sales to House Accounts do not count toward the minimum annual purchase commitment.

**4.3 Annual Reconciliation.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall give Distributor a written statement of Distributor's purchases for that Contract Year measured against the minimum annual purchase commitment.

## 5. ORDERING AND FORECASTS

**5.1 Minimum Order Quantity.** Each Purchase Order shall be for at least one (1) full truckload, or twenty-two (22) standard pallets, of Products, unless Supplier agrees otherwise in writing.

**5.2 Lead Time.** Distributor shall issue each Purchase Order at least fourteen (14) days before the requested delivery date.

**5.3 Forecasts.** Distributor shall deliver forecasts in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Agreement.

## 6. PRICING, PAYMENT AND DELIVERY

**6.1 Prices.** Prices are as set out in Supplier's price list in effect under Section 6.1 of the Agreement, as adjusted under Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

**6.2 Payment Terms.** Net thirty (30) days from receipt of invoice, by ACH transfer, as stated in Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

**6.3 Delivery Terms.** FCA Supplier's manufacturing facility in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020), as stated in Section 7.1 of the Agreement.
