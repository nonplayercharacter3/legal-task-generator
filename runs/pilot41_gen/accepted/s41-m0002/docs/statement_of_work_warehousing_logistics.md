# AMENDED AND RESTATED STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: WAREHOUSING AND TEMPERATURE-CONTROLLED LOGISTICS SERVICES

This Amended and Restated Statement of Work No. 1 (this **"Statement of Work"** or **"SOW"**) is entered into as of **February 16, 2026** (the **"SOW Restatement Date"**) between **Fouveirt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Supplier"**), and **Pruran Distribution Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company (**"Distributor"**). It is made under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 1, 2025 (the **"Agreement"**).

## 1. BACKGROUND

**1.1 Original Statement of Work.** The Parties entered into Statement of Work No. 1 as of March 1, 2025, together with the Agreement (the **"Original SOW"**). Under the Original SOW, Distributor provides warehousing, inventory management and temperature-controlled logistics services for the Products. The Parties now wish to amend and restate the Original SOW to:

(a) add a second storage facility;

(b) update the reporting requirements; and

(c) restate the Service Fee.

**1.2 2026 Price Adjustment Notice.** Supplier delivered a written notice of adjustment to Product prices for the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2026 to Distributor on **January 20, 2026** (the **"2026 Price Adjustment Notice"**). Distributor rejected the 2026 Price Adjustment Notice in writing as untimely under Section 6.4 of the Agreement by letter dated January 27, 2026. Supplier maintains that the 2026 Price Adjustment Notice is effective. The Parties enter into this Statement of Work **without prejudice** to either Party's position on the 2026 Price Adjustment Notice. Nothing in this Statement of Work amends Section 6 of the Agreement.

**1.3 Restatement.** From the SOW Restatement Date, this Statement of Work amends, restates and replaces the Original SOW in its entirety. Services performed and Service Fees accrued under the Original SOW before the SOW Restatement Date remain governed by the Original SOW.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

**2.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Statement of Work have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Products," "Specifications," "Recall," "Territory," "Contract Year" and "Service Level Schedule."

**2.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Statement of Work:

**"Facilities"** means the Primary Facility and the Secondary Facility.

**"Inventory"** means all Products held at the Facilities. This includes Products owned by Distributor, and any Products owned by Supplier that Supplier consigns to the Facilities for staging, House Account fulfillment or Recall handling.

**"Primary Facility"** means Distributor's warehouse at 220 Meadowlands Industrial Drive, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094.

**"Secondary Facility"** means Distributor's warehouse at 1650 Lehigh Valley Commerce Drive, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18106.

**"Services"** means the services described in Section 3.

**"WMS"** means Distributor's warehouse management system.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

**3.1 Receiving.** Distributor shall:

(a) receive Products at the Facilities;

(b) verify quantities, SKUs and lot codes against the bill of lading and the related Purchase Order or Supplier transfer advice;

(c) inspect each shipment in accordance with Section 7.3 of the Agreement; and

(d) record each lot in the WMS within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt.

**3.2 Storage.** Distributor shall store all Inventory in accordance with Section 8 of the Agreement, the Specifications and the Service Level Schedule. Distributor shall:

(a) keep canned Products in dedicated, temperature-mapped zones;

(b) keep dry Products at no more than 80°F (27°C) and at relative humidity of no more than 65%;

(c) store Products off the floor on clean pallets, with at least eighteen (18) inches of clearance from walls; and

(d) segregate Products from chemicals, non-food goods and any goods with strong odors.

**3.3 Monitoring.** Distributor shall keep calibrated, continuous temperature and humidity sensors in each storage zone, logging readings at least every fifteen (15) minutes. Sensors shall be calibrated at least every six (6) months against a NIST-traceable standard. Distributor shall give Supplier read-only online access to monitoring data for the Facilities.

**3.4 Inventory Management.** Distributor shall:

(a) manage Inventory on a first-expired, first-out basis by lot;

(b) perform monthly cycle counts and a full physical count at the end of each Contract Year;

(c) quarantine any lot that is damaged, nearing expiry (with less than ninety (90) days of shelf life remaining) or subject to a Supplier hold; and

(d) maintain lot traceability in accordance with Section 8.3 of the Agreement.

**3.5 Order Fulfillment.** Distributor shall pick, pack and ship Customer orders from the Facilities. For canned Products moving between November 1 and March 31, Distributor shall use carriers and trailers that meet the transport requirements of the Specifications and the Service Level Schedule, including heated or insulated trailers where required.

**3.6 House Account and Consignment Handling.** When Supplier requests in writing, Distributor shall stage and ship Supplier-owned Inventory to House Account locations in the Territory. That Inventory shall be held as Supplier's property, labeled as such in the WMS and kept separate from Distributor-owned stock.

**3.7 Recall Support.** In any Recall, Distributor shall perform the obligations in Section 8.6 of the Agreement. Distributor shall:

(a) place affected lots on hold in the WMS within two (2) hours of Supplier's notice;

(b) produce a lot-level distribution report within twenty-four (24) hours; and

(c) receive, segregate and hold returned or retrieved Products pending Supplier's disposition instructions.

**3.8 Pest Control and Sanitation.** Distributor shall keep a licensed pest control contract for each Facility, with at least monthly service. Distributor shall follow a written master sanitation schedule and keep records of each service and each sanitation activity.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fee.** In consideration of the Services, Supplier shall pay Distributor a monthly Service Fee of **$38,500** (the **"Service Fee"**). The Service Fee covers all Services described in Section 3 for Inventory volumes of up to 9,000 pallet positions across the Facilities.

**4.2 Additional Charges.** The following are charged in addition to the Service Fee, at the rates in Annex 1:

(a) pallet positions above 9,000 in any month;

(b) special labeling or kitting requested in writing by Supplier and consented to under Section 9.3 of the Agreement; and

(c) Recall handling labor, to the extent the Recall is not caused by Distributor's breach of Section 8 of the Agreement.

No other charges are payable without Supplier's prior written approval.

**4.3 Invoicing.** Distributor shall invoice the Service Fee monthly in advance, on or about the first (1st) business day of each month. Additional charges shall be invoiced monthly in arrears, with supporting detail. Supplier shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

**4.4 Disputes.** Supplier shall notify Distributor of any good-faith dispute about an invoice within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Supplier shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall resolve disputes in the manner described in Section 6.3 of the Agreement, applied with the necessary changes.

**4.5 Fee Review.** The Service Fee is fixed through February 28, 2027. Any later change to the Service Fee requires a written amendment signed by both Parties.

## 5. PERSONNEL AND GOVERNANCE

**5.1 Account Managers.** Each Party shall appoint an account manager for this Statement of Work.

- Supplier's initial account manager is its Director of Supply Chain, Northeast.
- Distributor's initial account manager is its Vice President, Warehouse Operations.

**5.2 Monthly Reporting.** By the tenth (10th) business day of each month, Distributor shall give Supplier a report for the previous month covering:

(a) Inventory on hand by SKU, lot and Facility;

(b) shipments by Customer;

(c) temperature and humidity performance, including each Temperature Excursion and Handling Failure (as defined in the Service Level Schedule);

(d) cycle count accuracy; and

(e) performance against each service level in the Service Level Schedule.

**5.3 Quarterly Business Reviews.** The account managers shall meet at least quarterly to review performance, forecasts, capacity and corrective actions.

**5.4 Qualified Personnel.** Distributor shall assign trained personnel to perform the Services. Distributor shall document food safety and handling training for all warehouse staff who handle Products, both on hire and annually after that.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS

Distributor shall perform the Services in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. Service credits and remedies for failure to meet service levels are as stated in the Service Level Schedule.

## 7. SUPPLIER RESPONSIBILITIES

Supplier shall:

(a) provide current Specifications for each Product and notify Distributor in writing of any change at least thirty (30) days before it takes effect;

(b) provide advance shipping notices for Supplier-owned Inventory; and

(c) give timely disposition instructions for quarantined, held or returned Products.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

**8.1 Term.** This Statement of Work continues from the SOW Restatement Date until the Agreement expires or terminates, unless it is terminated earlier under this Section 8.

**8.2 Termination.** This Statement of Work terminates automatically under Section 14.4 of the Agreement. Either Party may terminate this Statement of Work for the other Party's material breach under the procedure in Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

**8.3 Transition.** On expiry or termination, Distributor shall give reasonable transition assistance for up to sixty (60) days. That assistance includes:

(a) releasing Supplier-owned Inventory to Supplier or its designee; and

(b) delivering complete lot, temperature and Inventory records.

Supplier shall pay the Service Fee, prorated, for the transition period.

## 9. GENERAL

**9.1 Agreement Terms.** This Statement of Work is subject to the Agreement. Sections 8, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 16 of the Agreement apply to the Services.

**9.2 Amendment.** This Statement of Work may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**9.3 Counterparts.** This Statement of Work may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
