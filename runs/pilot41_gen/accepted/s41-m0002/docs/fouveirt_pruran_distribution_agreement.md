# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into and made effective as of **March 1, 2025** (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between:

**Fouveirt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Harborview Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (**"Supplier"**); and

**Pruran Distribution Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Meadowlands Industrial Drive, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 (**"Distributor"**).

Supplier and Distributor are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties."**

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and sells dry, canned and veterinary-diet pet food under the Fouveirt brand.

B. Supplier sells its Veterinary Diet Products through its own veterinary clinic channel, directly to licensed veterinary practices and their affiliated pharmacies.

C. Distributor operates temperature-controlled warehousing and distribution facilities. Distributor sells to pet specialty, grocery, mass-merchandise and independent retail accounts in the Northeastern United States.

D. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

E. Under the Statement of Work, Distributor will also provide warehousing and logistics services to Supplier for a monthly Service Fee.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

**"Applicable Law"** means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, orders and binding guidance that apply to a Party's performance under this Agreement. This includes the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and their implementing regulations, including 21 C.F.R. Part 507, and state commercial feed laws in the Territory.

**"Contract Year"** means each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

**"Customer"** means any retail, wholesale or e-commerce account in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor for resale.

**"Initial Term"** has the meaning given in the Order Form.

**"Order Form"** means the Order Form executed by the Parties with this Agreement. The Order Form sets out the Initial Term, the minimum annual purchase commitment and other commercial terms.

**"Products"** means dry and canned dog and cat food listed in Exhibit A, excluding Veterinary Diet Products.

**"Purchase Order"** means a written order for Products that Distributor issues to Supplier under Section 5.

**"Recall"** means any recall, market withdrawal, stock recovery or field correction of any Product, whether it is voluntary or required by a Governmental Authority.

**"Governmental Authority"** means any federal, state or local governmental, regulatory or administrative body, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and state departments of agriculture.

**"Service Fees"** means the fees payable by Supplier to Distributor under the Statement of Work.

**"Service Level Schedule"** means the Service Level Schedule executed by the Parties. It sets out the performance standards that apply to Distributor's storage, handling and logistics services.

**"Specifications"** means Supplier's written product, packaging, storage and handling specifications for each Product, as Supplier provides and updates them in writing.

**"Statement of Work"** means the Statement of Work for warehousing and logistics services entered into by the Parties under this Agreement.

**"Territory"** means New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania.

**"Veterinary Diet Products"** means Supplier's therapeutic and prescription-only pet food. This means any pet food marketed for use under the direction of a licensed veterinarian, in any format, including the products sold under Supplier's "Fouveirt VetCare" line.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement:

(a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation;

(b) "including" means "including without limitation";

(c) references to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement unless otherwise stated; and

(d) references to a document include that document as amended in accordance with its terms.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF THE AGREEMENT

**2.1 Contract Documents.** The Parties' relationship is governed by the following documents (together, the **"Contract Documents"**):

(a) this Agreement, including its Exhibits;

(b) the Order Form;

(c) the Service Level Schedule; and

(d) the Statement of Work.

**2.2 Incorporation.** The Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and the Statement of Work are each incorporated into and form part of this Agreement.

**2.3 Order of Precedence.** If the Contract Documents conflict or are inconsistent, they will prevail in the following order: the Order Form, then the Service Level Schedule, then the Distribution Agreement body, then the Statement of Work.

## 3. APPOINTMENT

**3.1 Exclusive Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment. During the Term, Supplier shall not:

(a) appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller for the Products in the Territory; or

(b) sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except as permitted by Section 3.3.

**3.2 Reserved Rights.** Supplier reserves all rights not expressly granted to Distributor. Distributor has no right to distribute, market or sell any goods of Supplier other than the Products, whether inside or outside the Territory.

**3.3 House Accounts.** Supplier may continue to sell Products directly to the national accounts listed in Exhibit B (the **"House Accounts"**), including for delivery to House Account locations in the Territory. Sales to House Accounts do not count toward Distributor's minimum annual purchase commitment.

**3.4 Territorial Restrictions.** Distributor shall not actively market or solicit sales of Products outside the Territory. Distributor shall not establish any warehouse or sales office for the Products outside the Territory without Supplier's prior written consent.

**3.5 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship. Neither Party may bind the other.

## 4. TERM

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term, which is three years from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date, as stated in the Order Form. It may be terminated earlier under Section 14. The Initial Term, together with any renewal term agreed by the Parties in writing, is the **"Term."**

**4.2 Renewal.** The Parties may renew this Agreement by written agreement signed by both Parties at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current Term.

## 5. ORDERS AND MINIMUM PURCHASES

**5.1 Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall purchase Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products, quantities, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days of receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with Distributor's most recent forecast.

**5.2 Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) business day of each month, Distributor shall give Supplier a rolling six (6) month non-binding forecast of its Product requirements.

**5.3 Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase at least the minimum annual purchase commitment stated in the Order Form, which is $6,200,000 of Products per Contract Year. If Distributor fails to meet the minimum in any Contract Year, Supplier may, as its sole remedies for that shortfall:

(a) terminate the exclusivity under Section 3.1 on thirty (30) days' written notice, so that Distributor's appointment continues on a non-exclusive basis; or

(b) terminate this Agreement under Section 14.2(c).

**5.4 Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed terms on a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Prices.** Distributor shall pay Supplier the prices for the Products set out in Supplier's price list in effect on the date Supplier accepts the relevant Purchase Order. The price list in effect on the Effective Date is attached as Exhibit A.

**6.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment. Distributor shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. Payment shall be made by ACH transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

**6.3 Disputed Invoices.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any good-faith dispute about an invoice within fifteen (15) days of receipt, giving reasonable detail of the dispute. Distributor shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**6.4 Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the prices of the Products once per Contract Year by written notice to Distributor. Supplier must deliver the notice at least 60 days before each anniversary of the Effective Date. A price adjustment takes effect on the anniversary that follows a timely notice. It applies to Purchase Orders accepted on or after that date. If Supplier does not deliver a notice within that period, prices for the following Contract Year remain unchanged.

**6.5 Late Payment.** Supplier may charge interest on overdue undisputed amounts at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law.

**6.6 Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Distributor is responsible for any such taxes, other than taxes based on Supplier's net income. Distributor shall provide valid resale exemption certificates for each state in the Territory.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

**7.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's manufacturing facility in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020), unless the Purchase Order states otherwise and Supplier accepts it.

**7.2 Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Products pass to Distributor on delivery under Section 7.1.

**7.3 Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect each shipment within five (5) business days of receipt at its facility. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage, visible damage or nonconformity within that period. If Distributor does not give notice within that period, the shipment is deemed accepted, except as to latent defects.

## 8. PRODUCT HANDLING AND FOOD SAFETY

**8.1 Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store, handle and transport all Products in accordance with:

(a) the Specifications;

(b) the Service Level Schedule; and

(c) Applicable Law, including current good manufacturing practice and preventive controls requirements for animal food.

**8.2 Temperature Control.** Distributor shall keep canned Products within the temperature ranges stated in the Specifications. Distributor shall keep dry Products in clean, dry, pest-free conditions at no more than 80°F (27°C) and at relative humidity of no more than 65%. Distributor shall continuously monitor and record storage temperatures and keep those records for at least two (2) years.

**8.3 Lot Traceability.** Distributor shall keep lot-level records of every Product it receives, stores and ships, sufficient to trace each lot one step forward to each Customer. Distributor shall produce those records to Supplier within twenty-four (24) hours of request.

**8.4 Facility Standards.** Distributor shall maintain a written food safety plan for each facility used for the Products. Distributor shall keep each such facility registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration where Applicable Law requires it. Distributor shall permit Supplier to audit each such facility on five (5) business days' notice, or without notice following a suspected food safety event.

**8.5 Notification.** Distributor shall notify Supplier within twenty-four (24) hours after becoming aware of any of the following:

(a) any deviation from Sections 8.1 to 8.4;

(b) any suspected contamination or adulteration of any Product;

(c) any Customer or consumer complaint alleging illness or injury to an animal; or

(d) any inquiry or inspection by a Governmental Authority relating to the Products.

**8.6 Recalls.** Supplier has sole authority to decide whether to initiate a Recall. Distributor shall cooperate fully in any Recall, including by giving notice to Customers, quarantining stock and retrieving affected Products. Distributor shall bear all costs of a Recall, and of any Customer chargebacks and contamination claims, to the extent caused by Distributor's breach of this Section 8. Supplier shall bear all other Recall costs.

## 9. MARKETING AND TRADEMARKS

**9.1 Marketing Efforts.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory. Distributor shall maintain a sales force sufficient to serve the Customers in the Territory.

**9.2 Trademark License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory. Distributor may use the trademarks solely to market and sell the Products, and only in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines. All goodwill arising from that use belongs to Supplier.

**9.3 No Alteration.** Distributor shall not repackage, relabel or alter any Product or its packaging without Supplier's prior written consent.

## 10. WARRANTIES

**10.1 Supplier Warranties.** Supplier warrants that each Product, when delivered under Section 7.1, will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) not be adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act or applicable state law; and

(c) have at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life.

**10.2 Distributor Warranties.** Distributor warrants that:

(a) it holds and will maintain all licenses, registrations and permits needed to perform this Agreement and the Statement of Work; and

(b) it will perform its obligations in a professional manner and in compliance with Applicable Law.

**10.3 Remedy.** If a Product breaches Section 10.1, Supplier shall, at its option, replace the Product or credit Distributor for it. Distributor shall make nonconforming Products available for return or destruction as Supplier directs.

**10.4 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. THIS INCLUDES ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents against all third-party claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees (**"Losses"**), to the extent arising from:

(a) any breach of Section 10.1;

(b) any claim that a Product, as delivered by Supplier, caused illness, injury or death to any animal or person; or

(c) any claim that Supplier's trademarks, as licensed under Section 9.2, infringe a third party's rights.

**11.2 By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents against all Losses to the extent arising from:

(a) any breach by Distributor of Section 8;

(b) the negligence or wilful misconduct of Distributor or its personnel or subcontractors; or

(c) any representation or warranty about the Products that Distributor makes and that Supplier has not authorized.

**11.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall:

(a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim;

(b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, but the indemnifying Party may not make any settlement that admits fault on the indemnified Party's behalf without that Party's consent; and

(c) give reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

A delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay prejudices it.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any of the following, however arising: indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or loss of profits or business opportunity.

**12.2 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the Service Fees paid under the Statement of Work in the 12 months before the claim.

**12.3 Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnity obligations under Section 11;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality);

(c) a Party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct; or

(d) Losses arising from Distributor's breach of Section 8 (Product Handling and Food Safety), including Recall and contamination costs and Customer chargebacks.

**12.4 Payment Obligations.** Section 12.2 does not limit Distributor's obligation to pay for Products it purchases or Supplier's obligation to pay Service Fees properly due.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

**13.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information the other Party discloses to it in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, formulations, Specifications, Customer data and forecasts (**"Confidential Information"**). Each Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement. Each Party may disclose it only to its employees, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13.

**13.2 Exceptions.** Section 13.1 does not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or

(d) must be disclosed by law, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice to the other Party where permitted.

**13.3 Duration.** This Section 13 survives for three (3) years after termination. Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under Applicable Law.

## 14. TERMINATION

**14.1 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**14.2 Additional Supplier Rights.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement by written notice to Distributor if:

(a) Distributor commits a material breach of Section 8 that results in a Recall;

(b) Distributor becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(c) Distributor fails to meet the minimum annual purchase commitment in any Contract Year.

**14.3 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry:

(a) all unpaid amounts become due;

(b) Distributor shall, at Supplier's option, sell its remaining saleable inventory of Products in the ordinary course for up to ninety (90) days, or return that inventory to Supplier for credit at Distributor's purchase price;

(c) Distributor shall cease all use of Supplier's trademarks; and

(d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

**14.4 Statement of Work.** Termination of this Agreement terminates the Statement of Work at the same time, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

**14.5 Survival.** Sections 6.2, 6.3, 8.3, 8.6, 11, 12, 13, 14.3, 15 and 16 survive termination or expiry.

## 15. INSURANCE

During the Term and for two (2) years afterwards, each Party shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence; and

(b) in Distributor's case, warehouse legal liability and cargo insurance of at least $3,000,000 per location and per conveyance, and product contamination and recall insurance of at least $5,000,000.

Each Party shall name the other as an additional insured on its general liability policy and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.
