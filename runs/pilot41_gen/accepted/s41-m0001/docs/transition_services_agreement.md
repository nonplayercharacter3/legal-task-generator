# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Trasirt Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Lalais Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as seller, and Recipient, as buyer, are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Recipient is acquiring from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Ostrevane Imaging Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided the Business with remote service, field service dispatch and ERP hosting. To support the orderly transition of the Business to Recipient, Recipient wishes Provider to continue to provide those services after the Closing, and Provider is willing to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

C. Section 3.2(c) of the Stock Purchase Agreement requires Provider to deliver this Agreement, duly executed, at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Applicable Law**" means any Law applicable to the performance or receipt of the Services, including the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the FDA's regulations at 21 C.F.R. Parts 803, 806 and 820.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement, in any form, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

"**Customer**" means any customer of the Business, including any hospital, imaging center, clinic or other Person that owns, leases or uses Company Products or installed-base equipment serviced by the Company.

"**Recipient Confidential Information**" means Confidential Information of Recipient or the Company. It includes all installed-base service records, Customer site data, equipment configuration and service history, service firmware and diagnostic software, complaint and service event records, and other data relating to the Business that Provider accesses, processes, hosts or generates in performing the Services.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data relating to the Business, including Customer data, that is entered into, stored in, processed by or generated through the Provider Systems in connection with the Services.

"**Provider Systems**" means the ERP platform, remote service platform, dispatch and ticketing systems, and related hardware, software and networks owned, licensed or operated by Provider or its Affiliates and used to provide the Services.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 6.3.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as modified under Section 3.4.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Stock Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Business," "Business Day," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Company Product," "FDA," "Law," "Losses" and "Person."

**1.3 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Closing Date and continues until 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 12.

**2.2 Service Periods.** Each Service shall be provided for the Term, unless Schedule A specifies a shorter period for that Service or the Service is terminated earlier under Section 12.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient and the Company in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A.

**3.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a professional and workmanlike manner;

(b) with substantially the same scope, priority, quality, timeliness and level of care as the corresponding services were provided to the Business during the 12 months before the Closing Date; and

(c) in compliance with Applicable Law.

**3.3 Regulatory Support.** Where a Service involves the receipt, logging or handling of any complaint, service event or report relating to a Company Product, Provider shall:

(a) record it in the Provider Systems in accordance with the Company's complaint-handling procedures in effect on the Closing Date; and

(b) forward it to Recipient's designated quality contact within one Business Day after receipt, or within 24 hours where it involves a death, serious injury or malfunction that may be reportable under 21 C.F.R. Part 803.

Recipient remains responsible for all regulatory determinations and submissions relating to Company Products.

**3.4 Changes to Services.** Either party may request a change to the scope of any Service by written notice to the other. No change is effective until set out in a written amendment to Schedule A signed by both parties. The amendment shall include any agreed adjustment to the fees.

**3.5 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, at its own cost, any consents from third-party licensors or vendors of the Provider Systems that are required for Provider to provide the Services to Recipient and the Company.

## 4. FEES

**4.1 Service Fee.** In consideration of the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider a service fee of $410,000 per month (the "**Monthly Service Fee**"). The Monthly Service Fee shall be prorated on a daily basis for any partial calendar month during the Term.

**4.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall also reimburse Provider for the following costs, which Provider incurs at Recipient's written request and outside the ordinary scope of the Services, in each case at Provider's actual cost without markup:

(a) replacement parts and consumables used in field service on Recipient's behalf; and

(b) reasonable out-of-pocket travel expenses of field service personnel for dispatches outside the service regions identified in Schedule A.

**4.3 Taxes.** The Monthly Service Fee is exclusive of sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Recipient shall pay those taxes as properly invoiced by Provider, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 5. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Invoices.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall show the Monthly Service Fee and any pass-through costs in reasonable detail, with supporting documentation for pass-through costs.

**5.2 Payment.** Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account Provider designates in writing.

**5.3 Disputes.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider in writing, with reasons, before the payment due date. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute under Section 13.8. Recipient shall pay any amount found to be owed within ten Business Days after the dispute is resolved.

**5.4 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by Law if lower, from the due date until paid.

## 6. PERSONNEL AND GOVERNANCE

**6.1 Personnel.** Provider shall assign to the Services appropriately qualified, trained and experienced personnel. This includes field service engineers trained and qualified on the Company Products and installed-base equipment covered by the Services. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the personnel who performed the corresponding services before the Closing assigned to the Services during the Term.

**6.2 Control of Personnel.** Provider's personnel remain under Provider's direction and control, and are not employees or agents of Recipient or the Company. Provider is responsible for their compensation, benefits and employment taxes.

**6.3 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint a service coordinator (each, a "**Service Coordinator**") who will serve as its primary contact for the day-to-day administration of this Agreement. Either party may replace its Service Coordinator on written notice. The Service Coordinators shall meet at least monthly to review performance, open service tickets and transition planning.

**6.4 Site Access.** When Provider's personnel are on the premises of Recipient, the Company or any Customer, they shall comply with that party's reasonable safety, security, radiation-safety and site-access rules communicated to Provider.

## 7. RECIPIENT OBLIGATIONS

**7.1 Cooperation.** Recipient shall, and shall cause the Company to, provide Provider with reasonable information, access and cooperation as reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services.

**7.2 Use of Services.** Recipient shall use the Services only for the operation of the Business and in compliance with Applicable Law.

**7.3 Transition Planning.** Recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to transition each Service to its own systems or a third-party provider before the end of the Term.

## 8. SYSTEMS, DATA AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the parties, all Recipient Data is the property of Recipient or the Company. Provider acquires no right, title or interest in any Recipient Data, other than a limited, non-exclusive right to use it during the Term solely to perform the Services.

**8.2 Provider Systems.** Provider retains all right, title and interest in the Provider Systems. Recipient acquires no rights in the Provider Systems other than the right to receive the Services.

**8.3 Data Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Recipient Data hosted in or processed by the Provider Systems. Those safeguards shall be no less protective than those Provider applied to the corresponding data of the Business before the Closing, and no less than reasonable industry standards. Provider shall notify Recipient within 48 hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Recipient Data.

**8.4 Data Migration and Return.** Before the end of the Term, or within 30 days after any earlier termination of a Service, Provider shall:

(a) provide reasonable assistance to migrate the Recipient Data relating to that Service to systems Recipient designates;

(b) deliver to Recipient a complete copy of that Recipient Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format; and

(c) after Recipient confirms receipt, delete the Recipient Data from the Provider Systems, except to the extent retention is required by Applicable Law.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

**9.1 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall:

(a) hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence, using at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care;

(b) use that Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; and

(c) not disclose it to any Person except as permitted by this Section 9.

**9.2 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(c) was independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

Recipient Confidential Information held by Provider before the Closing in connection with the Business is not excluded under clause (b) or (c) by reason of that prior holding.

**9.3 Permitted Disclosures.** The Receiving Party may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to its and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9. Provider may also disclose Recipient Confidential Information to its permitted assignees and delegates under Section 13.2, to the extent needed for them to perform the Services. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Section 9 by Persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

**9.4 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by Law to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, where legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion legally required.

**9.5 Survival.** This Section 9 survives the expiration or termination of this Agreement for five years. For trade secrets, service firmware and Customer data, it survives for as long as that information remains confidential.

## 10. WARRANTIES

**10.1 Mutual.** Each party represents that it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

**10.2 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, neither party makes any warranty, express or implied, with respect to the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**11.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify Recipient, the Company and their respective directors, officers and employees against all Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct, breach of Section 8.3 or Section 9, or violation of Applicable Law in performing the Services.

**11.2 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify Provider and its directors, officers and employees against all Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by Recipient's gross negligence, willful misconduct or violation of Applicable Law in receiving or using the Services.

**11.3 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising under this Agreement, except as payable to a third party under Section 11.1 or 11.2.

**11.4 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Monthly Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

**11.5 Exceptions.** Sections 11.3 and 11.4 do not apply to:

(a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 11.1 or 11.2;

## 12. TERMINATION

**12.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. For Recipient's failure to pay undisputed amounts, the cure period is 15 days.

**12.2 Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**12.3 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement:

(a) Recipient shall pay all fees accrued for Services performed through the effective date of termination;

(b) Provider shall perform its obligations under Section 8.4; and

(c) Sections 4.3, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12.3 and 13 survive.

## SCHEDULE A — SERVICES

### Part 1 — Remote Service

1. Operation of the remote service platform connecting to Company Products installed at Customer sites. This covers the OV-9 Mobile C-Arm System (K193318), the OV-12 Mobile C-Arm System with Flat-Panel Detector (K211472) and the DR-Prime Digital Radiography System (K224056), as well as the CT and MRI equipment in the Company's installed base that is enabled for remote connectivity.
2. Remote diagnostics, log retrieval, error-code analysis and remote software and firmware support. These shall be performed in accordance with the Company's service procedures.
3. Technical support hotline staffed during the hours, and with the escalation procedures, in effect for the Business before the Closing Date.
4. Logging of all service events and Customer complaints in the Provider Systems, and forwarding them in accordance with Section 3.3.

### Part 2 — Field Service Dispatch

1. Receipt, triage and scheduling of service requests from Customers, and dispatch of qualified field service engineers to Customer sites. This covers the service regions served by the Business before the Closing Date.
2. Preventive maintenance scheduling and dispatch under Customer service contracts of the Business.
3. Parts ordering and logistics coordination for field service, with pass-through costs governed by Section 4.2.
4. Maintenance of service records, service history and Customer site data for each serviced unit in the Provider Systems.

### Part 3 — ERP Hosting

1. Hosting, operation and maintenance of the ERP instance used by the Business, including order management, inventory, procurement, service contract billing and financial reporting modules.
2. User access administration for Recipient and Company personnel, as directed by Recipient's Service Coordinator.
3. Daily backups of Recipient Data held in the ERP instance, with restoration capability consistent with pre-Closing practice.
4. Support for the extraction and migration of Recipient Data under Section 8.4.
