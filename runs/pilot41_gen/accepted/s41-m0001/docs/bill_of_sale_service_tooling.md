# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Trasirt Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to **Ostrevane Imaging Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and **Lalais Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller holds certain service tooling, calibration phantoms and service manuals used in the Business. Section 3.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing, transferring those assets to the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Transfer of Assets

**1.1 Conveyance.** Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Ostrevane Imaging Corp., and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets described on Schedule A (the "**Transferred Assets**"). The transfer is effective as of the Effective Date and is free and clear of all Encumbrances.

**1.2 Transferee.** The transferee of the Transferred Assets under this Bill of Sale is Ostrevane Imaging Corp. No right, title or interest in any Transferred Asset is conveyed to Buyer or to any other Person under this Bill of Sale.

**1.3 Included Rights.** The Transferred Assets include all of the following, to the extent held by Seller and transferable:
- all manufacturer and vendor warranties relating to the Transferred Assets;
- all calibration certificates, traceability records and maintenance logs for the Transferred Assets; and
- all copyrights and other rights in the service manuals included in the Transferred Assets.

## 2. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement. These include "Business," "Closing," "Encumbrance" and "Person."

## 3. Delivery and Possession

**3.1 Delivery.** Seller shall deliver possession of the Transferred Assets to the Company on the Effective Date. Transferred Assets already located at the Company's facility at 2200 Westbridge Parkway, Rochester, New York, are delivered in place. Seller shall deliver any Transferred Assets at other Seller locations to that facility at Seller's expense within ten Business Days after the Effective Date.

**3.2 Electronic Materials.** For service manuals and related documentation held in electronic form, Seller shall do both of the following on the Effective Date:
- deliver complete copies to the Company in their native format; and
- after delivery, retain only such copies as Seller needs to perform the Transition Services Agreement or to comply with applicable Law.

## 4. Further Assurances

At the Company's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver further instruments of transfer and take such other actions as the Company reasonably requests to vest in the Company good title to the Transferred Assets. This includes delivering any certificates of title, calibration records or vendor notices.

## 5. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as Seller's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely for the following purposes:
- to demand and receive any of the Transferred Assets; and
- to institute and prosecute, in Seller's name and at the Company's expense, any proceeding the Company considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title in or to the Transferred Assets.

This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 6. Relationship to the Stock Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under Section 3.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of Seller in the Stock Purchase Agreement are not enlarged or diminished by this Bill of Sale.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.3 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and accepted in counterparts. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.

## SCHEDULE A — TRANSFERRED ASSETS

### Part 1 — Service Tooling

1. Twelve (12) field service tool kits for the OV-9 and OV-12 Mobile C-Arm Systems. Each kit includes a torque driver set, a collimator alignment fixture and C-arm rotation lock tools.
2. Six (6) field service tool kits for the DR-Prime Digital Radiography System. Each kit includes a detector tray alignment jig and a tube-stand counterweight adjustment set.
3. Four (4) high-voltage generator diagnostic test sets, with interface cables.
4. Eight (8) non-invasive kVp/mAs/exposure-time meters with dosimetry probes.
5. Service laptops loaded with Company diagnostic and service software, assigned to the field service personnel listed in the asset register delivered by Seller to Buyer before the Closing.
6. Specialized CT and MRI service tooling held at Seller's regional depots and used to service the Company's installed base of computed tomography and magnetic resonance imaging equipment. This includes gantry alignment fixtures, RF coil test fixtures and helium-level service tooling, as identified in that asset register.

### Part 2 — Calibration Phantoms

1. Ten (10) image quality test phantoms for fluoroscopic and radiographic image quality assessment.
2. Six (6) flat-panel detector uniformity and calibration phantoms for the OV-12 and DR-Prime systems.
3. Four (4) CT water and performance phantoms used in installed-base CT service.
4. Four (4) MRI uniformity and spatial-linearity phantoms used in installed-base MRI service.
5. All calibration certificates and traceability documentation for the phantoms listed in this Part 2.

### Part 3 — Service Manuals

1. Service manuals, installation manuals, field modification instructions and service bulletins for each of the following:
   - the OV-9 Mobile C-Arm System (K193318);
   - the OV-12 Mobile C-Arm System with Flat-Panel Detector (K211472); and
   - the DR-Prime Digital Radiography System (K224056).
2. Service manuals, procedures and technical documentation held by Seller for the CT and MRI equipment in the Company's installed base.
3. All revisions of the materials in items 1 and 2 of this Part 3 that Seller holds, in paper and electronic form.
