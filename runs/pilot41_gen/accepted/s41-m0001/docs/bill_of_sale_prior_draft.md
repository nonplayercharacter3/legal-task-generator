# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Trasirt Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to **Ostrevane Imaging Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and **Lalais Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Stock Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller holds certain service tooling, calibration phantoms and service manuals used in the Business. The Stock Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing to transfer those assets to the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Transfer of Assets

**1.1 Conveyance.** Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Ostrevane Imaging Corp., and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets described on Schedule A (the "**Transferred Assets**"). The transfer is effective as of the Effective Date and is free and clear of all Encumbrances.

**1.2 Transferee.** The transferee of the Transferred Assets under this Bill of Sale is Ostrevane Imaging Corp.

**1.3 Included Rights.** To the extent held by Seller and transferable, the Transferred Assets include:

(a) all manufacturer and vendor warranties relating to the Transferred Assets; and

(b) all calibration certificates, traceability records and maintenance logs for the Transferred Assets.

[*Note to Draft: Treatment of copyrights in service manuals under discussion.*]

## 2. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement.

## 3. Delivery and Possession

**3.1 Delivery.** Seller shall deliver possession of the Transferred Assets to the Company on the Effective Date. Transferred Assets already located at the Company's facility at 2200 Westbridge Parkway, Rochester, New York, are delivered in place. Any Transferred Assets at other Seller locations shall be delivered by Seller to that facility at Seller's expense within [ten (10)] Business Days after the Effective Date.

**3.2 Electronic Materials.** Seller shall deliver to the Company on the Effective Date complete copies, in native format, of all service manuals and related documentation that Seller holds in electronic form.

## 4. Further Assurances

At the Company's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver further instruments of transfer. Seller shall also take such other actions as the Company reasonably requests to vest in the Company good title to the Transferred Assets.

## 5. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as Seller's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely for two purposes:

(a) to demand and receive any of the Transferred Assets; and

(b) to institute and prosecute, in Seller's name and at the Company's expense, any proceeding to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title in or to the Transferred Assets.

This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 6. Relationship to the Stock Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Stock Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of Seller in the Stock Purchase Agreement are not enlarged or diminished by this Bill of Sale.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York. No choice or conflict of law rule shall be given effect that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.3 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and accepted in counterparts. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format are effective as originals.
