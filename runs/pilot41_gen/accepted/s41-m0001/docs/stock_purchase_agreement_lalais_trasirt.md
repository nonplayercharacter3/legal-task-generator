# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2026, by and between **Lalais Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Trasirt Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Ostrevane Imaging Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures and sells mobile C-arm and digital radiography systems, and provides service for an installed base of computed tomography and magnetic resonance imaging equipment (the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount; and

(b) to Seller, the Purchase Price, as estimated under Section 2.3(a), less the Escrow Amount.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting out its good faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Closing Working Capital**"). The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which the Estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of $14,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Closing Working Capital**"). Seller shall have 30 days to deliver any written objection. Any dispute not resolved by the parties within 30 days after delivery of an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding.

(c) Within five Business Days after the Closing Working Capital is finally determined, the paying party shall pay the other party the difference between the Closing Working Capital and the Estimated Closing Working Capital.

**2.4 Escrow.**

(a) At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $18,650,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with Citibank, N.A., or another escrow agent reasonably acceptable to both parties (the "**Escrow Agent**"), to be held under an escrow agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent as security for Seller's obligations under Article 9.

(b) On the date 90 days after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the then-remaining Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of all claims for indemnification asserted by Buyer in good faith under Article 9 that remain pending on the Escrow Release Date. Each retained amount shall be released to the party entitled to it promptly after the related claim is finally resolved.

(c) Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to the Person in respect of whom the withholding was made.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article 7 have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, transferring the service tooling, calibration phantoms and service manuals described in it to the Company;

(c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) the escrow agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(e) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 7.2(d);

(f) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(g) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents;

(h) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(i) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments required by Section 2.2;

(b) the Transition Services Agreement and the escrow agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).
