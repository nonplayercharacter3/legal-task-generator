# NON-BINDING TERM SHEET

**Proposed Logistics Services Relationship between Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC and Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc.**

**Date:** January 26, 2026

This Term Sheet sets out the principal terms on which **Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC** ("**Customer**") and **Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc.** ("**Provider**") propose to enter into a master services agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). Except as stated in Section 12, this Term Sheet does not create any legally binding obligation. Binding obligations will arise only when the parties sign the Definitive Agreement.

## 1. Parties

| Role | Entity |
|---|---|
| Customer | Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company |
| Provider | Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation |

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 Provider will perform the following services for Customer's event furniture rental business, including banquet seating, lounge sets, staging and tenting furniture (the "**Services**"):

(a) delivery of rental inventory to event venues;
(b) on-site installation;
(c) strike and teardown;
(d) return transport; and
(e) dispatch logistics.

2.2 Provider will perform dispatch through its proprietary Zouduth Dispatch Platform. Customer will have access to the platform to submit orders, track deliveries and retrieve reports.

2.3 Service territories, service levels, scheduling windows and rates will be set out in statements of work signed by both parties.

## 3. Term

3.1 The Definitive Agreement will have an initial term of three (3) years from its effective date.

3.2 After the initial term, it will renew automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives at least ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

## 4. Annual Minimum Commitment

4.1 Customer will commit to purchase Services with aggregate fees of not less than $1,850,000 per contract year.

4.2 Provider may invoice any shortfall after the end of the relevant contract year.

4.3 The commitment will be reduced pro rata for periods of Provider service-level failure and for force majeure events affecting Provider.

## 5. Pricing and Payment

5.1 Rates will be fixed for the first contract year. After that, Provider may increase rates once per contract year on ninety (90) days' notice. Each increase will be capped at three percent (3%).

5.2 Provider will invoice monthly in arrears. Customer will pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days.

5.3 Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith.

## 6. Data Protection

6.1 Customer will own all data Provider receives from or collects for Customer in performing the Services. This includes event-client contact details, venue access information and delivery sign-offs, whether entered into the Zouduth Dispatch Platform or collected by Provider crews on site.

6.2 The parties will enter into a data processing addendum. The addendum will cover:

(a) breach notification;
(b) subprocessor approval;
(c) security standards; and
(d) return and deletion of data on termination.

## 7. Assignment

7.1 Neither party may assign without consent, except to a successor that is not a Competitor.

7.2 "**Competitor**" means any person or entity, or any affiliate of a person or entity, that is engaged in the business of renting furniture, staging, tenting or related equipment for events.

## 8. Risk Allocation

8.1 Provider will be responsible for loss of or damage to rental inventory while it is in Provider's care, custody or control, excluding ordinary wear and tear.

8.2 Each party will indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its own negligence or willful misconduct. Provider will also indemnify Customer against claims that the Zouduth Dispatch Platform infringes third-party intellectual property rights.

8.3 Limitations of liability, including any cap and its exclusions, will be mutual. The parties will settle them in the Definitive Agreement.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 Provider will maintain insurance at levels customary for event logistics providers. This will include:

(a) commercial general liability insurance;
(b) automobile liability insurance;
(c) cargo and inland marine insurance;
(d) workers' compensation insurance; and
(e) cyber liability insurance.

9.2 Provider will name Customer as an additional insured on the general liability and automobile policies.

## 10. Governing Law and Disputes

10.1 The Definitive Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

10.2 Disputes will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

10.3 Before either party files suit, the parties will escalate the dispute to their senior executives.

## 11. Announcement

On signing the Definitive Agreement, the parties may issue a mutually approved joint announcement of the relationship.

## 12. Binding Provisions

12.1 Only Sections 12.1, 12.2 and 12.3 of this Term Sheet are binding.

12.2 Each party will keep the terms of this Term Sheet and all non-public information exchanged in connection with it confidential.

12.3 Each party will bear its own costs. These binding provisions are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

12.4 Either party may end discussions at any time by written notice.
