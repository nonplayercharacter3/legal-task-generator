# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and between **Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**").

This DPA forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Provider dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all United States federal and state laws and regulations that apply to the Processing of Customer Data under this DPA. This includes state consumer privacy laws, data breach notification laws and data security laws, each as amended from time to time.

1.2 "**Authorized Personnel**" means Provider's employees, crew members and approved Subprocessors who need access to Customer Data to perform the Services.

1.3 "**Customer Data**" means Personal Data uploaded by Customer to the Zouduth Dispatch Platform in connection with the Services, including order records, Event Client names and delivery addresses that Customer enters or imports into the Dispatch Platform.

1.4 "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates, including Event Clients, their guests and representatives, and venue staff.

1.5 "**Personal Data**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with a particular natural person or household, and any information treated as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.6 "**Processing**" means any operation performed on data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

1.7 "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Customer Data.

1.8 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party that Provider engages to Process Customer Data on Provider's behalf.

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 **Roles.** With respect to Customer Data, Customer is the business or controller and Provider is the service provider or processor, as those terms are used under Applicable Data Protection Law.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Provider shall Process Customer Data only for the following purposes:

(a) scheduling, routing and dispatching deliveries, installations, strikes and returns of Rental Inventory;
(b) communicating with Event Clients and venues about Customer orders;
(c) confirming delivery and completion of Services; and
(d) generating reports for Customer through the Dispatch Platform.

2.3 **Details of Processing.** The categories of Data Subjects, the types of Customer Data and the duration of Processing are described in Annex A.

## 3. Provider Obligations

3.1 **Instructions.** Provider shall Process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, each Statement of Work and this DPA constitute Customer's instructions. Provider shall promptly notify Customer if it believes an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Provider shall not:

(a) sell or share Customer Data, as those terms are defined under Applicable Data Protection Law;
(b) retain, use or disclose Customer Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, including for any commercial purpose of Provider;
(c) retain, use or disclose Customer Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and Provider; or
(d) combine Customer Data with personal data that Provider receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.3 **Compliance.** Provider shall comply with Applicable Data Protection Law in its Processing of Customer Data and shall provide the same level of privacy protection as that law requires of Customer.

3.4 **Personnel.** Provider shall ensure that all Authorized Personnel:

(a) are bound by written confidentiality obligations;
(b) receive appropriate training on the handling of Customer Data; and
(c) access Customer Data only to the extent necessary to perform the Services.

## 4. Security

4.1 **Security Measures.** Provider shall implement and maintain the administrative, technical and physical safeguards described in Annex B. These safeguards shall be designed to protect Customer Data against Security Incidents and shall be appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and the risks of the Processing.

4.2 **Platform Controls.** Provider shall maintain the following controls for the Dispatch Platform:

(a) role-based access controls;
(b) multi-factor authentication for administrative access;
(c) encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest using industry-standard methods; and
(d) logging of access to Customer Data, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.

4.3 **Mobile Devices.** Provider shall ensure that mobile devices that crews use to access Customer Data are enrolled in a mobile device management program. That program must support remote wipe and screen lock.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 **Authorization.** Customer authorizes Provider to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex C. Provider shall not engage any other Subprocessor without giving Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of the proposed Subprocessor.

5.2 **Objection.** Customer may object to a proposed Subprocessor on reasonable grounds relating to data protection within fifteen (15) days after receiving notice. If Customer objects, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it within thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

5.3 **Flow-Down.** Provider shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor. That agreement must impose data protection obligations at least as protective as those in this DPA. Provider remains fully liable to Customer for each Subprocessor's performance.

## 6. Security Incidents

6.1 **Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer in writing of any Security Incident without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours after Provider becomes aware of it.

6.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice shall describe, to the extent known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;
(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;
(c) the likely consequences of the Security Incident; and
(d) the measures Provider has taken or proposes to take to address it.

Provider shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.3 **Cooperation.** Provider shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer in meeting Customer's notification obligations to Data Subjects, regulators and others. Provider shall not notify any Data Subject or regulator of a Security Incident involving Customer Data without Customer's prior written approval, unless the law requires it.

6.4 **Costs.** Provider shall bear the reasonable costs of investigation, remediation and notification, including credit monitoring where appropriate, to the extent the Security Incident results from Provider's or its Subprocessors' breach of this DPA.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

7.1 Provider shall promptly forward to Customer, and in any event within five (5) business days, any request it receives from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Law with respect to Customer Data. Provider shall not respond to any such request except on Customer's instructions.

7.2 Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to Data Subject requests. Such assistance includes access to, correction of and deletion of Customer Data held in the Dispatch Platform.

## 8. Return and Deletion

8.1 **On Termination.** Within thirty (30) days after the expiration or termination of the Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, return all Customer Data to Customer in a commonly used machine-readable format, or securely delete it. Provider shall delete all remaining copies within that period, unless Applicable Data Protection Law requires retention.

8.2 **Certification.** On Customer's request, an officer of Provider shall certify the deletion in writing.

8.3 **Retained Data.** Any Customer Data that Provider retains as required by law remains subject to this DPA for as long as it is retained.

## 9. Audits and Assessments

9.1 **Information.** On Customer's reasonable request, Provider shall make available the information needed to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA. This includes Provider's most recent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment for the Dispatch Platform.

9.2 **Audits.** Customer may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA no more than once in any twelve (12) month period. Customer must give at least thirty (30) days' written notice, and the audit must take place during normal business hours. Customer may conduct additional audits following a Security Incident. Customer shall bear its own audit costs, unless the audit reveals a material breach of this DPA.

## 10. Data Location and Transfers

10.1 Provider shall store and Process Customer Data only within the United States, unless Customer gives prior written consent.

## 11. Liability

11.1 Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in Section 11 of the Agreement.

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 This DPA and all disputes arising out of or relating to it, including disputes concerning the Processing of Customer Data or any Security Incident, are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts of New York County, New York, for the resolution of any such dispute. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 13. General

13.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the date of the Agreement. It remains in effect for as long as Provider Processes Customer Data.

13.2 **Amendment.** The parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment to this DPA reasonably required to comply with changes in Applicable Data Protection Law. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

## Annex B – Security Measures

1. Written information security program, reviewed annually.
2. Encryption of Customer Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
3. Role-based access and multi-factor authentication for the Dispatch Platform.
4. Background checks for Authorized Personnel, where permitted by law.
5. Annual penetration testing of the Dispatch Platform, with remediation of critical findings.
6. Business continuity and backup procedures, with backups encrypted and tested at least annually.
