# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Preamble**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

**Recitals**

A. Customer rents event furniture, including banquet seating, lounge sets, staging and tenting furniture, to its clients for weddings, corporate functions and other events.

B. Provider operates a logistics business and a proprietary dispatch platform, and provides delivery, on-site installation, strike and teardown, and dispatch services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to perform these services for Customer's event rental business, and Provider wishes to perform them, on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Competitor**" means any person or entity, or any Affiliate of a person or entity, that is engaged in the business of renting furniture, staging, tenting or related equipment for events.

1.3 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

1.4 "**Customer Data**" means all data Provider receives from or collects for Customer in performing Services, including event-client contact details. Customer Data also includes venue access information, delivery sign-offs, and any data entered into or generated by the Dispatch Platform in connection with Customer's orders.

1.5 "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the parties that governs Provider's Processing of Personal Data on behalf of Customer, as amended from time to time.

1.6 "**Dispatch Platform**" means the Zouduth Dispatch Platform, which is Provider's proprietary software system for scheduling, routing, crew assignment and order tracking, together with its mobile applications.

1.7 "**Event Client**" means any customer of Customer that rents Rental Inventory for an event.

1.8 "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

1.9 "**Rental Inventory**" means all furniture, staging, tenting furniture, décor and related equipment that Customer owns or leases and rents to Event Clients, together with associated hardware, fasteners and packaging.

1.10 "**Services**" means the delivery, on-site installation, strike and teardown, return transport, and dispatch logistics services described in this Agreement and in each Statement of Work.

1.11 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work that refers to this Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 2. Services and Structure of Agreement

2.1 **Services.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work. Provider shall do so in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with good industry practice for event logistics, and in compliance with all applicable laws.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement. Each SOW shall specify, as applicable:

(a) the service territory;
(b) the categories of Rental Inventory covered;
(c) the service levels;
(d) the scheduling windows; and
(e) the applicable rates.

No SOW is binding until both parties sign it.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents that make up the parties' agreement, the following order of precedence applies: DPA (for Personal Data matters only), then MSA, then Statements of Work.

2.4 **Dispatch Platform Access.** Provider grants Customer and its authorized personnel a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to access and use the Dispatch Platform. This right is limited to submitting orders, tracking deliveries and retrieving reports in connection with the Services.

## 3. Provider Obligations

3.1 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign trained and qualified personnel to perform the Services. Provider shall ensure that each crew lead holds all licenses required for the vehicles and equipment used. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice. Provider shall promptly remove from Customer's account any individual whom Customer reasonably objects to.

3.2 **Handling of Rental Inventory.** Provider shall load, transport, install, strike and return Rental Inventory in accordance with:

(a) Customer's written handling and installation guidelines; and
(b) the manufacturer's specifications that Customer provides to Provider.

3.3 **Damage and Loss.** Provider is responsible for loss of, or damage to, Rental Inventory while it is in Provider's care, custody or control. This excludes ordinary wear and tear. Provider shall reimburse Customer for the repair cost or the replacement value of the affected items, whichever is lower.

3.4 **Site Conduct.** Provider's crews shall:

(a) comply with venue rules, load-in and load-out schedules, and reasonable directions from Event Clients and venue staff; and
(b) present themselves as providing services on behalf of Customer.

3.5 **Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract any material part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 4. Customer Obligations

4.1 Customer shall submit orders through the Dispatch Platform in accordance with the lead times stated in the applicable SOW.

4.2 Customer shall provide accurate event details, venue information and installation specifications for each order.

4.3 Customer shall ensure that Rental Inventory tendered to Provider is in serviceable condition and suitably packaged for transport.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Customer is responsible for those taxes, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

5.2 **Annual Minimum Commitment.** Customer commits to purchase Services with aggregate fees of not less than $1,850,000 per contract year (the "**Annual Minimum**"). If the fees invoiced in a Contract Year are less than the Annual Minimum, Provider may invoice Customer for the shortfall within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year. The Annual Minimum is reduced pro rata for:

(a) any period during which Provider fails to meet the service levels in the applicable SOW; and
(b) any period of a Force Majeure Event affecting Provider.

5.3 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days after receipt of a correct invoice.

5.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith, provided it gives Provider written notice of the dispute with reasonable detail. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.5 **Rate Adjustments.** Rates are fixed for the first Contract Year. After that, Provider may increase rates once per Contract Year on at least ninety (90) days' written notice. Any such increase may not exceed three percent (3%) over the rates then in effect.

## 6. Service Levels

6.1 Provider shall meet the on-time delivery, installation completion and strike completion service levels set out in each SOW.

6.2 If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider shall issue the service credits specified in the applicable SOW.

6.3 If Provider fails the same service level in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period, that failure is a material breach for purposes of Section 14.3.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement and that is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential. Customer's Confidential Information includes Customer Data, Customer's pricing, Event Client lists and booking information.

7.2 **Obligations.** Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care. Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. Each party may disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisors who:

(a) need to know it for those purposes; and
(b) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 7.

7.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party;
(b) was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or
(d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information.

7.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** A receiving party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order. Before doing so, it shall give prompt notice to the disclosing party where lawful, and shall reasonably cooperate with the disclosing party's efforts to obtain protective treatment.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 **Ownership of Customer Data.** As between the parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Provider shall use Customer Data only to perform the Services. Provider shall not sell, license or otherwise make available Customer Data to any third party, except as permitted by this Agreement.

8.2 **DPA.** Customer Data that is Personal Data shall be processed in accordance with the DPA.

8.3 **Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data. These safeguards apply to the Dispatch Platform, to mobile devices used by Provider crews, and to any paper records collected on site.

8.4 **Return and Deletion.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, and at Customer's written request, Provider shall return or securely delete all Customer Data in its possession or control, except as required by law.

## 9. Intellectual Property

9.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. Provider retains all rights in the Dispatch Platform. Customer retains all rights in its trademarks, designs and installation guidelines.

9.2 **Reports.** Reports and data exports that the Dispatch Platform generates for Customer become Customer's property on delivery, to the extent they contain Customer Data.

9.3 **Use of Marks.** Neither party may use the other's name or marks without prior written consent. The parties may, however, issue a mutually approved announcement of their relationship.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim to the extent it arises from:

(a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider, its personnel or subcontractors in performing the Services;
(b) any claim that the Dispatch Platform infringes or misappropriates a third party's intellectual property rights;
(c) Provider's violation of applicable law, including employment, wage and hour, and motor vehicle laws; or
(d) any vehicle accident involving Provider's vehicles or personnel.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim arising from or relating to the Rental Inventory. This includes claims for bodily injury, death or property damage occurring at any event at which Rental Inventory is used, regardless of whether caused in part by Provider's installation or handling of the Rental Inventory.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall:

(a) promptly notify the indemnifying party in writing of any claim, although delay relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is prejudiced;
(b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified party may be made without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; and
(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

The indemnified party may participate in the defense through counsel of its own choosing, at its own expense.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, even if advised of their possibility.

11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct;
(b) Customer's obligation to pay fees due under this Agreement; or
(c) Customer's obligations under Section 10.2.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 During the Term and for one (1) year afterward, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;
(b) commercial automobile liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 combined single limit;
(c) cargo and inland marine insurance covering Rental Inventory in transit and in Provider's custody, of not less than $500,000 per conveyance;
(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and
(e) cyber liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000.

12.2 Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the general liability and automobile policies. Provider shall furnish certificates of insurance on request.

## 13. Warranties

13.1 Each party represents and warrants that:

(a) it is duly organized and in good standing;
(b) it has full authority to enter into this Agreement; and
(c) this Agreement is a binding obligation enforceable against it.

13.2 Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 2.1 and the applicable SOW. Provider further warrants that it holds, and will maintain, all permits and licenses required to perform the Services.

13.3 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 14. Term and Termination

14.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the MSA Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Section 14.

14.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**," and together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"). Either party may prevent renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

14.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

14.4 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party:

(a) becomes insolvent;
(b) makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors; or
(c) becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.5 **Transition Assistance.** On expiration or termination, Provider shall complete all scheduled strikes and returns of Rental Inventory then in progress. For up to ninety (90) days, Provider shall also provide reasonable transition assistance at the rates then in effect.

14.6 **Survival.** The following survive expiration or termination of this Agreement: Sections 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 14.5, 14.6, 16 and 17, together with any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive.

## 15. Assignment

15.1 **By Customer.** Customer may not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Provider's prior written consent. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15.1 is void.

15.2 **By Provider.** Provider may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Customer's consent, to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of Provider's business or assets to which this Agreement relates, whether by merger, sale of stock, sale of assets or otherwise. Provider shall give Customer written notice of the assignment within thirty (30) days after it takes effect.

15.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. Governing Law and Disputes

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

16.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Escalation.** Before filing suit, other than for injunctive relief, the parties shall refer any dispute to their respective senior executives. Those executives shall meet within fifteen (15) days to attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith.

16.4 **Equitable Relief.** Each party may seek injunctive relief to prevent an actual or threatened breach of Section 7 or Section 8.

## 17. General

17.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses stated on the signature page or to any updated address a party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt.

17.2 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship. Provider is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits and supervision of its personnel.

17.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control (each, a "**Force Majeure Event**"), including severe weather, natural disaster, epidemic, war, terrorism, governmental action or labor disputes not involving the affected party's own workforce. This Section 17.3 does not apply to payment obligations. The affected party shall give prompt notice of the Force Majeure Event and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

17.4 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the DPA and all Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter. It supersedes all prior and contemporaneous proposals, term sheets, negotiations, understandings and agreements, whether written or oral.

17.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing. A waiver on one occasion does not operate as a waiver on any other occasion.

17.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

17.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
