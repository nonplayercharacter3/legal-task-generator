# Nonaisk Event Rentals and Zouduth Logistics Group Announce Multi-Year Logistics Services Agreement

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**March 4, 2026**

## 1. Announcement

Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC ("Nonaisk") and Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc. ("Zouduth") today announced a multi-year agreement for delivery, installation and dispatch services. Nonaisk rents furniture for weddings, corporate functions and other events. Zouduth provides logistics services and runs its own dispatch technology.

Under a Master Services Agreement dated March 2, 2026, Zouduth will provide the following services for Nonaisk's event rental business:

- delivery;
- on-site installation;
- strike and teardown;
- return transport; and
- dispatch logistics.

The agreement has an initial term of three years.

## 2. About the Relationship

Nonaisk's inventory includes banquet seating, lounge sets, staging and tenting furniture. Zouduth crews will deliver, set up and break down that inventory at venues. They will work to Nonaisk's handling and installation standards. Scheduling, routing and order tracking will run through the Zouduth Dispatch Platform. Nonaisk's team will use the platform to submit orders, follow deliveries in real time and retrieve service reports.

Service territories, service levels and scheduling windows will be set out in statements of work agreed between the companies. The first statements of work are expected to cover Nonaisk's core metropolitan markets during the 2026 event season.

## 3. Executive Comments

"Our clients plan events months in advance and expect every chair, lounge set and stage deck to be in place before the doors open," said the Chief Executive Officer of Nonaisk Event Rentals. "Zouduth brings trained crews and a dispatch platform built for tight load-in windows. That lets our team focus on design, inventory and client service."

"Event logistics is about precision. A late truck or an unfinished install is not an option," said the President of Zouduth Logistics Group. "We are proud that Nonaisk has chosen us. Our crews will treat Nonaisk's inventory and Nonaisk's clients with the care they expect."

## 4. Client Experience and Data Care

Nonaisk's event clients will receive delivery-window notifications and on-site service on Nonaisk's behalf. The companies have entered into a data processing addendum covering how client information is handled in providing the services. Under the addendum, that information is stored within the United States and protected by security safeguards.

## 5. About Nonaisk Event Rentals, LLC

Nonaisk Event Rentals is a Delaware limited liability company. It rents event furniture, including banquet seating, lounge sets, staging and tenting furniture, to clients for weddings, corporate functions, galas and other events.

## 6. About Zouduth Logistics Group, Inc.

Zouduth Logistics Group is a Delaware corporation. It provides delivery, on-site installation, strike and teardown, and dispatch services. It runs these services on its proprietary Zouduth Dispatch Platform, which handles scheduling, routing, crew assignment and order tracking.
