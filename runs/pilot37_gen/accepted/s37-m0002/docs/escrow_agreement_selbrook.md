# ESCROW AGREEMENT

**THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 19, 2026, by and among **Pralor Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Brimain Shipping Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Selbrook Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kalvesund Tankers, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.2(a) and Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement require Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at Closing. That amount will be held and disbursed under this Agreement and is available to satisfy claims of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. On June 12, 2026, Buyer delivered to Seller a Vessel Condition Notice under Section 6.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement for M/T Kalvesund Cygnet (IMO No. 9712368) (the "**Cygnet Notice**"). The Cygnet Notice identifies defects in the vessel's hull and cargo system and estimates the repair cost at $2,400,000. Seller asserts that the Cygnet Notice is untimely under Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement and disputes Buyer's claim. Buyer disputes Seller's position. Under Section 6.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller have agreed that the Disputed Repair Amount will be deposited with the Escrow Agent at Closing out of the Purchase Price and held pending resolution of that dispute.

D. Buyer and Seller wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and disburse the Escrow Funds, and the Escrow Agent is willing to act, on the terms of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Cygnet Dispute**" means the dispute between Buyer and Seller described in Recital C, including any dispute over the existence of the defects, the estimated repair cost or the validity of the Cygnet Notice.

"**Disputed Repair Account**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the funds held by the Escrow Agent in the Indemnity Escrow Account and the Disputed Repair Account, together with any interest and earnings on them.

"**Escrow Release Date**" means the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Escrow Release Date is the next Business Day.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or another court of competent jurisdiction under Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement, together with a certificate of counsel to the party delivering it confirming that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Indemnity Escrow Account**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before the Escrow Release Date that have not then been resolved and paid, or withdrawn, under Section 4.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Escrow Deposits.** At Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) $9,300,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"), under Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(b) $2,400,000 (the "**Disputed Repair Amount**"), out of the Purchase Price otherwise payable to Seller, under Section 6.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement for the Cygnet Dispute.

**2.2 Separate Accounts.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Amount in a segregated account (the "**Indemnity Escrow Account**"). It shall hold the Disputed Repair Amount in a separate segregated account (the "**Disputed Repair Account**"). Funds in one account shall not be used to satisfy any payment due from the other.

**2.3 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds only in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.4 Confirmation of Receipt.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of each deposit to Buyer and Seller by email on the Business Day it is received.

**2.5 No Other Duties.** The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller. Its duties are limited to those expressly set out in this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a U.S. government money market fund or interest-bearing deposit account offered by the Escrow Agent, as directed in writing by Buyer and Seller jointly. Absent written direction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing account insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limit.

**3.2 Earnings.** Interest and earnings on each account form part of that account and shall be distributed with the principal of that account.

**3.3 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss resulting from that liquidation or from any investment made in accordance with this Section 3.

## 4. INDEMNITY ESCROW: CLAIMS

**4.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). The Claim Notice must state that a Buyer Indemnified Party has a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount of Losses claimed or, if not then known, a good-faith estimate.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after Seller receives the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice must state the amount disputed and the grounds for the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period, the Escrow Agent shall pay Buyer, from the Indemnity Escrow Account, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice on the fifth Business Day after the end of that period. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall pay the undisputed part on the same basis.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall hold any disputed amount in the Indemnity Escrow Account until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It shall then disburse that amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instruction or Final Order within five Business Days after receipt.

**4.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the amounts claimed exceed the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Account, the Escrow Agent shall disburse funds in the order in which payment becomes due under this Section 4. The Escrow Agent has no obligation to pay any amount beyond the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Account.

## 5. RELEASE OF INDEMNITY ESCROW

**5.1 Release on Escrow Release Date.** The Indemnity Escrow Amount shall be held until the Escrow Release Date. Within five Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Seller the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Account, less the Pending Claim Amount.

**5.2 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount after the Escrow Release Date. It shall disburse each part of that amount under Section 4 as each pending claim is resolved. When a pending claim is resolved, any part of the amount retained for it that is not paid to Buyer shall be paid to Seller within five Business Days.

**5.3 Joint Instructions.** Buyer and Seller may at any time direct the release of all or any part of the Indemnity Escrow Account by Joint Written Instruction.

## 6. DISPUTED REPAIR ACCOUNT

**6.1 Holding.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Disputed Repair Amount in the Disputed Repair Account pending resolution of the Cygnet Dispute. Section 5 does not apply to the Disputed Repair Account. Its funds shall not be released on the Escrow Release Date by reason of that date alone.

**6.2 Release.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse the Disputed Repair Account only:

(a) in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction stating the amount, if any, payable to Buyer and the amount, if any, payable to Seller; or

(b) in accordance with a Final Order determining the Cygnet Dispute.

The Escrow Agent shall make the disbursement within five Business Days after receipt of the Joint Written Instruction or Final Order.

**6.3 Payment to Buyer.** Any amount paid to Buyer from the Disputed Repair Account shall be treated by Buyer and Seller as a reduction of the Purchase Price under Section 6.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement. Any amount paid to Seller shall be treated as payment of the Purchase Price.

**6.4 No Determination by Escrow Agent.** The Escrow Agent has no duty to determine, and shall not determine, the merits of the Cygnet Dispute, including whether the Cygnet Notice was timely.

## 7. THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial. They are limited to those expressly set out in this Agreement, and no implied duties apply. The Escrow Agent is not a trustee or fiduciary for any party.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature that it in good faith believes to be genuine and delivered by the proper party. It need not inquire into the truth or accuracy of any statement in it.

**7.3 Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except for its own gross negligence or willful misconduct, as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, punitive or consequential damages.

**7.4 Indemnity of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each bears one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.4, unless a court determines that the loss arose from the conduct of only one of them. This Section 7.4 survives termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**7.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain of its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the affected Escrow Funds with the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware by interpleader.

**7.6 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is fully protected in acting in good faith on that counsel's advice.

**7.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on 30 days' written notice. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent within that period. If no successor is appointed, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one. The resignation or removal takes effect when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Funds are transferred to it.

**7.8 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set out in Schedule B. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, as provided in Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. Buyer shall report all income on the Escrow Funds and pay any resulting tax, subject to adjustment between Buyer and Seller when funds are disbursed.

**8.2 Tax Forms.** Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 before Closing. The Escrow Agent shall file any information returns required by Law for income earned on the Escrow Funds.

**8.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount it is required by Law to withhold.

## 9. AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVES; SECURITY PROCEDURES

**9.1 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall designate on Schedule A the individuals authorized to sign instructions and notices on its behalf (each an "**Authorized Representative**"). A party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the Escrow Agent signed by an existing Authorized Representative.

**9.2 Call-Back Verification.** Before making any disbursement other than under Section 5.1, the Escrow Agent may confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of each instructing party at the number listed on Schedule A.

**9.3 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with all information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering, sanctions and know-your-customer Laws. The Escrow Agent need not accept any deposit until it has completed those procedures.

## 10. TERMINATION

This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 7.3, 7.4 and 7.8 survive termination.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

If to Seller: Brimain Shipping Group, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**11.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which succeeds to all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**11.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**11.5 Jurisdiction.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

**11.6 Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party waives, to the fullest extent permitted by Law, any right to trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of this Agreement.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
