# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**SPA Signing Date**"), by and between **Brimain Shipping Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Pralor Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kalvesund Tankers, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company owns or bareboat charters-in, and operates, a fleet of seven product tankers.

C. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Aldmoor Facility**" means the fleet credit facility agreement between the Company, as borrower, and Aldmoor Bank, as lender, together with the related mortgages, guarantees and security documents.

"**Bareboat Charters**" means the bareboat charter parties between Torvenne Ship Leasing Ltd., as owner ("**Torvenne**"), and the Company, as charterer, for M/T Kalvesund Aurora and M/T Kalvesund Borealis, each as described in Schedule 4.7.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Classification Society**" means, for each Company Vessel, the classification society identified for that vessel on Schedule 1.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Company Vessels**" means the seven vessels owned or bareboat chartered-in by the Company listed on Schedule 1.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the SPA Signing Date, including the numbered schedules attached to this Agreement.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all applicable Laws relating to pollution, the protection of the marine environment, or the discharge of oil, noxious liquid substances, sewage, garbage, ballast water or air emissions from ships. This includes MARPOL, the International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships' Ballast Water and Sediments, the U.S. Oil Pollution Act of 1990 and the U.S. Clean Water Act.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Selbrook Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent, to be entered into before or at Closing substantially on the terms described in Section 2.3.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization), 4.12 (Taxes) and 4.14 (Brokers), and in Sections 5.1 and 5.2.

"**ISM Code**" means the International Management Code for the Safe Operation of Ships and for Pollution Prevention.

"**Law**" means any statute, regulation, ordinance, rule, treaty, convention, order or other requirement of any Governmental Authority, flag state or Classification Society.

"**Liens**" means any mortgage, pledge, lien, maritime lien, charge, security interest or other encumbrance.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**MARPOL**" means the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, 1973, as modified by the Protocols of 1978 and 1997, as amended.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company. General changes in the product tanker market, freight rates or bunker prices are excluded unless they affect the Company disproportionately.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" or "**TSA**" means the transition services agreement between Seller and the Company in the agreed form attached as Exhibit B.

"**Transition Services**" means the services to be provided by Seller under the Transition Services Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer the Shares to Buyer, free and clear of all Liens, and Buyer shall purchase the Shares from Seller. The aggregate consideration for the Shares is $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Section 2.4 and Section 6.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, $9,300,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"), to be held under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the amounts needed to repay in full any indebtedness under the Aldmoor Facility that Buyer elects to have repaid at Closing, as set out in a payoff letter delivered under Section 3.2(a)(v); and

(c) to Seller, the balance of the Purchase Price after the deductions in clauses (a) and (b) and any adjustment under Section 2.4 or Section 6.4.

**2.3 Escrow.** The Indemnity Escrow Amount shall be held and disbursed by the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement. It is available to satisfy claims of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX. Any amount held back or deposited under Section 6.4(d) shall also be held under the Escrow Agreement. Seller and Buyer shall each pay one-half of the Escrow Agent's fees.

**2.4 Working Capital Adjustment.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing. The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased dollar-for-dollar by the amount by which that net working capital is more or less than $14,500,000. Disputes shall be resolved by an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller. Its determination is final and binding absent manifest error.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article VIII are satisfied or waived, other than conditions to be satisfied at Closing, or on another date agreed in writing by the parties (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Closing Deliveries.**

(a) At Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(ii) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company that Buyer designates;

(iii) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iv) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller, if not already executed;

(v) a payoff letter or consent from Aldmoor Bank, as applicable under Schedule 6.3;

(vi) copies of the third-party consents obtained under Section 6.3; and

(vii) the certificate required by Section 8.2(d).

(b) At Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments under Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer if not already executed, and the certificate required by Section 8.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the SPA Signing Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller and the Company are each duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. This Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The Shares are all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company. They are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable, and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller free and clear of all Liens. No options, warrants or other rights to acquire capital stock of the Company exist.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** Except as set out in Schedule 4.4, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require any consent under, or give rise to any right of termination under, any Material Contract.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** The audited financial statements of the Company for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 fairly present, in all material respects, the Company's financial position and results of operations in accordance with U.S. GAAP.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course, and no Material Adverse Effect has occurred.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.7 lists each contract to which the Company is a party that is material to its business (the "**Material Contracts**"). These include each bareboat charter, time charter of more than 12 months, ship management agreement, pooling agreement and credit agreement. Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.8 Company Vessels.**

(a) Schedule 1.1 accurately lists each Company Vessel, with its IMO number, flag, Classification Society and whether it is owned or bareboat chartered-in by the Company.

(b) The Company is the sole registered and beneficial owner of each owned Company Vessel, free and clear of all Liens other than those under the Aldmoor Facility. The Company is the bareboat charterer of M/T Kalvesund Aurora and M/T Kalvesund Borealis under the Bareboat Charters.

(c) Each Company Vessel is classed with its Classification Society, with class maintained without overdue recommendations, conditions of class or memoranda affecting class. Each Company Vessel holds all trading certificates required by its flag state and the applicable international conventions, valid and unexpired.

(d) Each Company Vessel is seaworthy, in good operating condition and repair (ordinary wear and tear excepted), and has been maintained in accordance with its Classification Society's rules and its planned maintenance system.

(e) In the past three years, no Company Vessel has been detained by any port state control authority or has been involved in a casualty, grounding, collision or pollution incident.

**4.9 Environmental and Regulatory Matters.** The Company and each Company Vessel are, and for the past five years have been, in compliance in all material respects with Environmental Laws, the ISM Code and the International Ship and Port Facility Security Code. Each Company Vessel holds a valid International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate, International Ballast Water Management Certificate and Safety Management Certificate. The Company or its ship manager holds a valid Document of Compliance. No claim, notice of violation or investigation under any Environmental Law is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or any Company Vessel.

**4.10 Litigation.** No action, arbitration or proceeding is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company, other than claims fully covered by the Company's P&I or hull and machinery insurance.

**4.11 Insurance.** Each Company Vessel is entered with a P&I club that is a member of the International Group of P&I Clubs and is insured for hull and machinery risks at not less than its market value. All premiums and calls due have been paid.

**4.12 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed and has paid all taxes due.

**4.13 Employees and Crew.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with the Maritime Labour Convention, 2006 and with all applicable crew employment agreements and collective bargaining agreements.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee from the Company or Buyer in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly formed, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. This Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 Financing.** At Closing, Buyer will have sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to any distribution in violation of securities Laws.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** Between the SPA Signing Date and the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate its business and the Company Vessels in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall cause the Company not to (a) sell, charter out on a bareboat basis or encumber any Company Vessel; (b) enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract; (c) incur indebtedness other than under the Aldmoor Facility in the ordinary course; or (d) declare or pay any dividend.

**6.2 Access.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the books, records, class records, planned maintenance records and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent, waiver and approval listed on Schedule 6.3 (the "**Required Consents**"), in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller in obtaining the Required Consents.

**6.4 Vessel Inspection.**

(a) *Inspection Period.* Buyer and its surveyors may inspect each Company Vessel, including a superintendent's inspection, and review its class records, during an inspection period of 45 days after the SPA Signing Date (the "**Inspection Period**"). Inspections shall be carried out at Buyer's cost, without drydocking and without interference with the vessel's commercial operations. Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer reasonable access to each Company Vessel at a port of call during the Inspection Period.

(b) *Vessel Condition Notice.* If Buyer's inspection reveals any defect, damage or deficiency in a Company Vessel that would reasonably require repair at a cost above $250,000 per vessel, Buyer may deliver a written notice to Seller identifying the defect and Buyer's good-faith estimate of the repair cost (a "**Vessel Condition Notice**"). Any Vessel Condition Notice must be delivered within 20 days after the last day of the Inspection Period.

(c) *Deemed Acceptance.* If Buyer does not deliver a Vessel Condition Notice for a Company Vessel within the period in Section 6.4(b), Buyer shall be deemed to have accepted the condition of that Company Vessel. Buyer shall also be deemed to have waived any claim for repair, or for a reduction of the Purchase Price, under this Section 6.4 for that vessel.

(d) *Resolution.* Within 15 Business Days after receipt of a timely Vessel Condition Notice, Seller shall elect by written notice to Buyer either (i) to cause the defect to be repaired to the satisfaction of the Classification Society before Closing, at Seller's cost, or (ii) to reduce the Purchase Price by the estimated repair cost stated in the Vessel Condition Notice. If Seller disputes the existence of the defect, the estimated cost or the validity of the Vessel Condition Notice, the disputed amount shall be deposited with the Escrow Agent at Closing out of the Purchase Price. It shall be held under the Escrow Agreement until the dispute is resolved by written agreement of Buyer and Seller or by final determination under Section 11.7.

**6.5 Transition Services.** At Closing, Seller and the Company shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.6 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any casualty, detention, class condition or pollution incident affecting any Company Vessel between the SPA Signing Date and the Closing.

**6.7 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Shares or any Company Vessel to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.8 Confidentiality; Public Announcements.** Neither party shall issue any public announcement about this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except as required by Law.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller is responsible for all taxes of the Company attributable to periods (or portions of periods) ending on or before the Closing Date, to the extent not reflected in net working capital under Section 2.4.

**7.2 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in preparing tax returns and in any tax audit relating to the Company.

**7.3 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of any transfer, documentary, stamp or registration taxes arising from the sale of the Shares.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each party to close are subject to: (a) no Law or order prohibiting the Closing; and (b) the expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976.

**8.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to satisfaction, or written waiver by Buyer, of the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations are true and correct in all respects, and the other representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects, in each case at Closing;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before Closing;

(c) each Required Consent listed on Schedule 6.3 has been obtained and is in full force and effect;

(d) Seller has delivered a certificate of an officer of Seller confirming satisfaction of Sections 8.2(a), (b) and (e);

(e) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred, and no Company Vessel has become an actual or constructive total loss; and

(f) Seller has delivered each item listed in Section 3.2(a).

**8.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to: (a) Buyer's representations and warranties being true and correct in all material respects at Closing; (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects its pre-Closing covenants; and (c) Buyer having delivered an officer's certificate confirming clauses (a) and (b).

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The Fundamental Representations survive the Closing until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. All other representations and warranties of the parties survive the Closing until the Escrow Release Date (as defined in the Escrow Agreement). The covenants of the parties survive in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any taxes for which Seller is responsible under Section 7.1;

provided, however, that (d) Seller shall have no liability under Section 9.2(a) for any Losses arising from any matter disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Notice of Claims; Time Bar.** A party seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying party written notice of the claim, describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to it and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses. No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3 in respect of a representation or warranty may be made unless that notice is delivered before the expiration of the survival period applicable to that representation or warranty under Section 9.1. Any claim noticed in time survives until finally resolved.

**9.5 Limitations.** Except for claims for breach of Fundamental Representations or for fraud:

(a) Seller shall have no liability under Section 9.2(a) until aggregate Losses exceed $930,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses above that amount; and

(b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

Seller's aggregate liability for breach of Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Claims of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Section 9.2(a), other than for breach of Fundamental Representations or for fraud, shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

**9.7 Third-Party Claims.** The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It may not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.8 Insurance; Mitigation.** Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually recovered, including under P&I and hull and machinery cover. Each indemnified party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its Losses.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, this Article IX is the exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement. This does not affect claims for fraud, claims for specific performance, or the procedures under Sections 2.4 and 6.4.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before Closing:

(a) by mutual written agreement of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by September 30, 2026, unless the failure results from that party's breach;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller materially breaches this Agreement such that a condition in Section 8.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 30 days after notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer materially breaches this Agreement such that a condition in Section 8.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 30 days after notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect. Sections 6.8, this Section 10.2 and Article XI survive. No party is relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other. However, Buyer may assign its rights to an Affiliate or, as collateral, to its financing sources.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns, and of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**11.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

**11.10 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

## SCHEDULE 4.7. MATERIAL CONTRACTS
