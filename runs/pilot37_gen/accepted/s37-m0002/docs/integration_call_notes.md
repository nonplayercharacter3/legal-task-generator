## 2. Purpose of Call

This was the third weekly integration call. The group reviewed workstreams for the period from Closing through the end of the Transition Services Agreement term, and identified pre-Closing tasks that need owners. Transaction document negotiation was not on the agenda. The Escrow Agreement was signed on June 19, 2026, and counsel confirmed that no further drafting is needed for this call.

## 3. Closing Timeline and Logistics

3.1 Counsel summarized the closing mechanics under SPA Article III. Closing occurs on the third Business Day after the Article VIII conditions are satisfied or waived, or on another agreed date, and takes place remotely. The SPA outside date is September 30, 2026 (Section 10.1(b)). The working target remains a Closing in the second half of July 2026, subject to the conditions.

3.2 HSR status: counsel confirmed the filing was made. Counsel will report on the waiting period at next week's call.

3.3 Finance confirmed that the Closing funds flow will follow SPA Section 2.2:
- $9,300,000 to Selbrook Trust Company, N.A. as the Indemnity Escrow Amount;
- the Aldmoor Facility payoff, if Buyer elects repayment at Closing; and
- the balance to Seller, after any adjustments.

Finance will also provide Form W-9 and KYC materials to Selbrook in line with Escrow Agreement Sections 8.2 and 9.3.

3.4 Finance has not yet decided whether to refinance or to seek lender consent under the Aldmoor Facility. Either route is provided for in Schedule 6.3. Finance will confirm its preference by July 1 so that counsel can request the payoff letter or consent letter from Aldmoor Bank.

## 4. Replacement Technical Manager

4.1 Under TSA Section 9.1, the TSA runs for six months from the Closing Date, with one optional extension of up to three months. The extension requires notice at least 30 days before the end of the initial term. The VP, Fleet Operations wants all vessels moved to Pralor's in-house management, or to a third-party manager, within the initial six-month term.

4.2 Shortlisting of external managers is in progress, as a fallback to in-house management. Two candidates have been asked for indicative management fees and DOC coverage for MR and LR1 tonnage.

4.3 Under TSA Section 4.1, the Migration Plan is due within 30 days after the Effective Date. HSQE will prepare a first draft before Closing so that it can be agreed promptly once the TSA is signed.

## 5. Discussion with Seller Operations (Seller attendees joined)

5.1 Seller's Head of Fleet Management confirmed the following about current operating practice:

Pralor asked for a crew list by rank and nationality, and for a schedule of crew contract end dates, to support planning for crew transfers under TSA Section 6.2. 5.3 Seller confirmed that vessel operating budgets will be ready for delivery within 15 days after the Effective Date, as required by TSA Section 3.4.

5.4 Seller's attendees left the call at 10:50 a.m.

## 6. Commercial Contracts

6.1 **Time charter.** M/T Kalvesund Gannet remains on its 24-month time charter that started in January 2026. The VP, Fleet Operations does not expect the charterer to require anything for the share sale beyond a notification letter. Counsel will review the charter for any notice requirement and report back.

6.2 **Pool.** M/T Kalvesund Eider and M/T Kalvesund Fulmar trade in the Nordwake LR1 Pool, which is terminable on 90 days' notice. Pralor is still deciding whether to keep both vessels in the pool after Closing. No notice will be given before Closing. SPA Section 6.1(b) restricts amendment or termination of Material Contracts before Closing without Buyer's consent.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 Pralor's insurance broker is preparing quotes to bring the Company's hull and machinery and war risks cover onto Pralor's fleet program at the next renewal. P&I entries will remain with the existing International Group club for now.

7.2 TSA Section 2.5(d) requires Provider to be named as co-assured to the extent customary for a ship manager. The broker will arrange this endorsement for the TSA term.

## 8. IT, Finance and Systems

8.1 IT will set up read-only access to Seller's planned maintenance and crewing systems for Pralor HSQE, to run during the TSA term. Data export formats will be confirmed as part of the Migration Plan.

8.2 Finance will open segregated operating accounts in the Company's name so that Provider can operate them under TSA Section 5.3. Signatory arrangements are still to be agreed.

8.3 Finance noted that the working capital statement under SPA Section 2.4 is due within 90 days after the Closing Date. Finance will set up the closing balance sheet procedures in advance.
