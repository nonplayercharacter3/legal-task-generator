# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form – Exhibit B to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026*

**THIS TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Brimain Shipping Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Kalvesund Tankers, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as seller, and Pralor Maritime Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Before the Closing, Provider has provided technical management, crew management and safety management services to the Company under an intra-group services arrangement. That arrangement terminates at the Closing.

C. Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and the Company to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Provider will continue to provide those services for a transitional period while the Company and Buyer arrange their replacement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Classification Society**" means, for each Company Vessel, the classification society with which that vessel is classed on the Effective Date.

"**Company Vessels**" means the vessels owned by the Company and registered in its name, and "**Company Vessel**" means any of them.

"**Crew**" means the master, officers and ratings of each Company Vessel.

"**DOC**" means the Document of Compliance issued under the ISM Code to Provider, as the "Company" within the meaning of the ISM Code, covering the Company Vessels.

"**Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Flag State**" means, for each Company Vessel, the state whose flag that vessel is entitled to fly.

"**ISM Code**" means the International Management Code for the Safe Operation of Ships and for Pollution Prevention.

"**ISPS Code**" means the International Ship and Port Facility Security Code.

"**Migration Plan**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**MLC 2006**" means the Maritime Labour Convention, 2006, as amended.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**SMC**" means the Safety Management Certificate issued under the ISM Code for each Company Vessel.

"**SMS**" means the safety management system maintained by Provider under the ISM Code.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Transition Services**" means the services described in Section 2.1 and Schedule A.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. TRANSITION SERVICES

**2.1 Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide the following services to the Company for the Company Vessels, as further described in Schedule A:

(a) *Technical management.* This includes superintendence, planned maintenance, arranging repairs, drydockings and surveys, maintaining class and statutory certificates, procuring stores, spares and lubricants, and arranging attendance by the Classification Society and Flag State surveyors;

(b) *Crew management.* This includes selecting, engaging, training and paying the Crew, arranging crew changes, travel and repatriation, administering crew employment agreements and any applicable collective bargaining agreements, and maintaining compliance with MLC 2006; and

(c) *ISM and ISPS services.* This includes acting as the "Company" under the ISM Code, maintaining the DOC and the SMS, maintaining the SMC and the International Ship Security Certificate for each Company Vessel, appointing the designated person ashore and company security officer, and conducting internal audits.

**2.2 Ancillary Services.** On the Company's reasonable request, Provider shall also provide the Company with reasonable assistance during the Term in arranging insurance renewals, handling P&I and hull and machinery claims, and reporting to the Company's lenders and charterers. In each case this assistance shall be provided consistent with Provider's practice in the 12 months before the Effective Date.

**2.3 Additional Services.** If the Company requests a service that is not described in Section 2.1 or Schedule A, Provider shall consider the request in good faith. Any additional service agreed by the parties in writing becomes a Transition Service, at a fee agreed at the time.

**2.4 Personnel and Subcontractors.** Provider may use its own employees and those of its Affiliates to perform the Transition Services. It may subcontract crew recruitment and travel arrangements to manning agents it used in the ordinary course before the Effective Date. Provider remains responsible for the performance of each subcontractor.

**2.5 Company Responsibilities.** The Company shall:

(a) give Provider timely access to the Company Vessels and their records;

(b) fund the operating accounts under Section 5.3;

(c) give Provider prompt notice of each employment and voyage order affecting the Company Vessels; and

(d) maintain hull and machinery, war risks and P&I insurance on each Company Vessel, naming Provider as co-assured to the extent customary for a ship manager.

## 3. STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

**3.1 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Transition Services (a) with the same degree of skill, care and diligence it applied in providing the corresponding services to the Company in the 12 months before the Effective Date, and (b) in any event in accordance with sound ship management practice (the "**Service Standard**").

**3.2 Compliance.** In performing the Transition Services, Provider shall comply with, and shall manage the Company Vessels so that they comply with, the requirements of the Classification Society, the Flag State, the ISM Code, the ISPS Code, MLC 2006 and Environmental Laws.

**3.3 Records and Reporting.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Transition Services. It shall deliver to the Company:

(a) a monthly report on each Company Vessel's technical condition, class and certificate status, crew complement and operating expenses against budget; and

(b) prompt written notice of any casualty, port state control detention or deficiency, condition of class, non-conformity under the SMS or pollution incident.

**3.4 Budgets.** Within 15 days after the Effective Date, Provider shall deliver to the Company a vessel operating budget for each Company Vessel for the remainder of the Term. Provider shall not incur expenditure on any Company Vessel of more than $100,000 above budget in any calendar month without the Company's prior written approval, except in an emergency affecting the safety of the vessel, its Crew or the environment.

## 4. MIGRATION

**4.1 Migration Plan.** Within 30 days after the Effective Date, the parties shall agree a written plan (the "**Migration Plan**") for the transfer of the Transition Services to the Company or its designated replacement manager. The Migration Plan shall cover the transfer of the SMS documentation, the issue of an interim DOC and interim SMCs to the replacement manager, the novation or re-engagement of Crew, and the transfer of planned maintenance and class records.

**4.2 Cooperation.** Provider shall cooperate in good faith with the Company and any replacement manager to implement the Migration Plan. In particular, it shall:

(a) make its superintendents and designated person ashore reasonably available;

(b) attend handover meetings and audits by the Flag State or the Classification Society; and

(c) deliver all vessel records, certificates, manuals and data held by it in relation to the Company Vessels.

**4.3 Records.** All records relating to the Company Vessels created or held by Provider in performing the Transition Services are the property of the Company. Provider may retain copies to the extent required by Law or the ISM Code.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** For the Transition Services, the Company shall pay Provider the management fees set out in Schedule B (the "**Fees**"). The Fees are payable monthly in advance for each Company Vessel. They are prorated for any partial month.

**5.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice the Fees on or before the first Business Day of each month. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within 15 days after receipt. The Company may withhold any disputed amount in good faith, with written notice to Provider stating the grounds for the dispute.

**5.3 Operating Expenses.** The Fees do not include the operating expenses of the Company Vessels, which are for the Company's account. These include crew wages and costs, stores, spares, lubricants, repairs, survey fees, insurance premiums and port costs. Provider shall pay those expenses from a segregated operating account for each Company Vessel. The Company shall fund each account monthly in advance in the budgeted amount. Provider shall not commingle the operating accounts with its own funds.

**5.4 Taxes.** The Fees are exclusive of any sales, use or similar taxes, which are for the Company's account. Each party bears its own income taxes.

**5.5 Audit.** On 10 Business Days' notice, the Company or its auditors may inspect Provider's books and records relating to the Fees and the operating accounts. The Company may make no more than one such inspection in any three-month period.

## 6. CREW

**6.1 Crew Employment.** Provider shall remain the employer, or shall cause its manning agents to remain the employer, of the Crew during the Term. It is responsible for the Crew's compliance with MLC 2006, Flag State manning requirements and STCW certification requirements.

**6.2 Crew Transfer.** In accordance with the Migration Plan, Provider shall cooperate with the Company and the replacement manager to transfer the employment of those members of the Crew whom the Company wishes to retain. Provider shall not solicit any member of the Crew to leave service on a Company Vessel during the Term, except for rotation in the ordinary course.

## 7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

**7.1 Limitation of Provider's Liability.** Provider is not liable to the Company for any loss, damage, delay or expense arising out of the Transition Services unless it results from Provider's negligence, gross negligence or willful misconduct, or that of its employees, agents or subcontractors. In that case, Provider's liability for each incident or series of incidents giving rise to a claim shall not exceed ten times the annual Fees for the affected Company Vessel. The limit does not apply to loss resulting from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**7.2 Indemnity by the Company.** Except to the extent of Provider's liability under Section 7.1, the Company shall indemnify Provider against all claims, costs and liabilities that Provider incurs in performing the Transition Services in accordance with this Agreement.

**7.3 Crew Liabilities.** Provider shall indemnify the Company against claims by Crew members or their dependants arising from Provider's breach of its obligations as employer, to the extent not covered by the Company's P&I insurance.

**7.4 No Consequential Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of hire or any indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, except in the case of fraud or willful misconduct.

**7.5 Himalaya Clause.** No employee, agent or subcontractor of Provider is liable to the Company for any loss arising out of the Transition Services. Each of them may rely on the limitations and exclusions in this Section 7 as if it were a party.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Confidential Information.** Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party under this Agreement. It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement, unless disclosure is required by Law or by a Governmental Authority, Flag State or Classification Society.

**8.2 Vessel Data.** Provider shall not use any operational, commercial or technical data relating to the Company Vessels for the benefit of any other vessel or Person after the Term.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Closing Date and continues for six months (the "**Term**"). The Company may extend the Term once, for up to three additional months, by written notice to Provider at least 30 days before the end of the initial Term.

**9.2 Partial Termination.** The Company may terminate the Transition Services for any Company Vessel, or any category of Transition Services, on 30 days' written notice to Provider. The Fees for that vessel or category end on the effective date of termination.

**9.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 15 days after written notice. However, Provider may not terminate this Agreement for non-payment of disputed amounts withheld in accordance with Section 5.2.

**9.4 Termination for Loss.** The Transition Services for a Company Vessel terminate automatically if that vessel becomes an actual or constructive total loss or is sold.

**9.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement, or of the Transition Services for any Company Vessel:

(a) Provider shall promptly deliver to the Company all records, certificates, funds in the relevant operating accounts and other property relating to the affected Company Vessels;

(b) Provider shall maintain the DOC and SMS coverage for each affected Company Vessel until an interim DOC and interim SMC have been issued to the replacement manager, for no longer than 30 days; and

(c) the Company shall pay all Fees accrued to the date of termination and reimburse all operating expenses properly incurred.

Sections 4.3, 7, 8 and 10 survive expiry or termination.

**10.5 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce the Company's rights under it. Except as provided in Section 7.5, no other Person has any rights under this Agreement.

## SCHEDULE A. DESCRIPTION OF TRANSITION SERVICES

**Part 1 – Technical Management**

1. Provide a dedicated superintendent for the Company Vessels, with at least one attendance on board each Company Vessel during the Term.
2. Operate and update the planned maintenance system for each Company Vessel.
3. Arrange and supervise all class, statutory and Flag State surveys falling due during the Term, and close out any recommendations or memoranda.
4. Arrange voyage repairs, and any drydocking scheduled during the Term, subject to Section 3.4.
5. Procure stores, spares, lubricants and chemicals through Provider's existing supplier arrangements.
6. Manage compliance with MARPOL, the Ballast Water Management Convention and vessel vetting requirements, including preparation for SIRE and charterer inspections.

**Part 2 – Crew Management**

1. Maintain full Crew complement in accordance with each Company Vessel's minimum safe manning document.
2. Administer Crew payroll, allotments, social contributions and P&I crew cover requirements.
3. Arrange crew changes, travel, medical examinations and visas.
4. Maintain Crew certification, training records and hours-of-rest records.

**Part 3 – ISM and ISPS Services**

1. Maintain the DOC and the SMS, and keep each Company Vessel covered by them.
2. Maintain the SMC and the International Ship Security Certificate for each Company Vessel.
3. Provide the designated person ashore and the company security officer on a 24-hour basis.
4. Conduct internal ISM and ISPS audits, and manage non-conformities and corrective actions.
5. Maintain the shipboard oil pollution emergency plan, the vessel response plan and qualified individual arrangements for U.S. calls.
