# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 3, 2026 by **Lavas Technologies Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 4100 Riverbend Parkway, Suite 300, Austin, Texas 78746 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Namaith Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 2250 Industrial Drive, Toledo, Ohio 43612 ("**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Lavas Glazing Software, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and Beneficiary are entering into a Software License Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Under it, Licensor licenses the GlazeCore facade-engineering and tempering-control software to Beneficiary.

B. Licensor is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the License Agreement.

C. Beneficiary requires delivery of this Guaranty as a condition to entering into the License Agreement. Guarantor is delivering this Guaranty to induce Beneficiary to do so.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the License Agreement.

1.2 "**Licensed Software**" means the GlazeCore platform and the modules listed in Schedule A to the License Agreement as in effect on the date hereof, in object code form, together with the related Documentation and Updates.

1.3 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations, liabilities and undertakings of Licensor to Beneficiary under the License Agreement with respect to the Licensed Software, whether now existing or arising later, and whether for payment or performance. They include Licensor's obligations:

(a) to deliver, implement, support and maintain the Licensed Software under Section 3 and Schedule B of the License Agreement;

(b) under the warranties in Section 8 of the License Agreement, including the refund obligations in Section 8.2;

(c) to defend and indemnify under Section 9.1 of the License Agreement;

(d) under Section 7 (Confidentiality) and Section 13.4 (Effect of Termination) of the License Agreement; and

(e) to pay any damages, refunds, costs or other amounts payable by Licensor under the License Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

2.2 **Performance on Default.** If Licensor fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall, within ten (10) business days after written demand from Beneficiary, either pay the amount due or perform the obligation, or cause it to be performed. Guarantor may cause performance through Licensor, an Affiliate of Guarantor or a qualified third party reasonably acceptable to Beneficiary.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Beneficiary is not required to proceed first against Licensor, to exhaust any remedy against Licensor or to pursue any other security before enforcing this Guaranty.

## 3. EXTENT OF LIABILITY

3.1 **Defenses of Licensor.** Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Licensor could assert under the License Agreement, other than defenses arising from Licensor's insolvency, bankruptcy, dissolution or lack of capacity or authority.

3.2 **Limitations Under the License Agreement.** Guarantor's liability for any Guaranteed Obligation is no greater than Licensor's liability for that obligation under the License Agreement, including Section 10 of the License Agreement.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS UNCONDITIONAL

4.1 Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by:

(a) any extension of time, indulgence or waiver granted to Licensor;

(b) any amendment or modification of the License Agreement made in accordance with its terms;

(c) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of Licensor, or any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting Licensor;

(d) any assignment of the License Agreement by Licensor to an Affiliate under Section 14.1 of the License Agreement; or

(e) any failure or delay by Beneficiary in exercising any right against Licensor or Guarantor.

4.2 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Beneficiary on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Licensor, as though the payment had not been made.

## 5. WAIVERS

Guarantor waives presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, and notice of the creation, renewal or extension of any Guaranteed Obligation. Guarantor also waives any right to require Beneficiary to proceed against Licensor or any other person before making demand on Guarantor. Nothing in this Section 5 waives the demand required by Section 2.2.

## 6. SUBROGATION

Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Licensor arising from payment or performance under this Guaranty.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Beneficiary that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize them;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws and to general principles of equity;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement to which it is a party; and

(e) it directly or indirectly owns all of the equity interests in Licensor.

## 8. COVENANTS

8.1 **Financial Information.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year during the Term, Guarantor shall deliver to Beneficiary, on request, its annual consolidated financial statements. Beneficiary shall treat them as Confidential Information under Section 7 of the License Agreement.

8.2 **Notice of Certain Events.** Guarantor shall notify Beneficiary in writing within ten (10) business days after Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the equity interests in Licensor.

## 9. PAYMENTS

All payments by Guarantor under this Guaranty shall be made in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account Beneficiary designates in writing. They shall be made without set-off or deduction, except as permitted by Section 3.1.

## 10. EXPENSES

Guarantor shall reimburse Beneficiary on demand for the reasonable attorneys' fees and other costs Beneficiary incurs in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

## 11. TERM

This Guaranty remains in full force until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that survive termination or expiry of the License Agreement under Section 13.5 of the License Agreement.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor and Beneficiary each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of New York sitting in the Borough of Manhattan and the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

12.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** GUARANTOR AND BENEFICIARY EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of the License Agreement. 13.3 **Amendments and Waivers.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Guarantor and Beneficiary. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

13.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.
