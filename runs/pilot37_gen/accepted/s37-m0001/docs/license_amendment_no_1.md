# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Software License Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Lavas Glazing Software, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office at 4100 Riverbend Parkway, Suite 300, Austin, Texas 78746 ("**Licensor**"), and **Namaith Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 2250 Industrial Drive, Toledo, Ohio 43612 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses the GlazeCore facade-engineering and tempering-control software to Licensee.

B. Licensee wishes to license, and Licensor is willing to license, the ThermaStress Analysis Module as part of the Licensed Software.

C. The Parties also wish to restate Section 10.3 of the Agreement.

D. Licensee has informed Licensor that Namaith Holdings Corp., the parent company of Licensee, has signed a definitive agreement under which Namaith Holdings Corp. will be acquired by Orvand Partners Fund III, L.P.

E. Under Section 16.2 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. ADDITION OF THERMASTRESS ANALYSIS MODULE

1.1 **Schedule A.** Schedule A to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row immediately after the row numbered A-5:

| No. | Module | Description |
|---|---|---|
| A-6 | ThermaStress Analysis Module | Thermal stress and thermal breakage risk analysis for monolithic, laminated and insulating glass lites, including edge-condition, shading and frame-absorption modeling, and integration with the FacadeDesign Module and TemperControl Module |

1.2 **Licensed Software.** From the Amendment Effective Date, the ThermaStress Analysis Module is part of the Licensed Software for all purposes of the Agreement, including Sections 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9 of the Agreement.

1.3 **Delivery.** Licensor shall deliver the ThermaStress Analysis Module, and license keys for it at each Licensed Site, within ten (10) business days after the Amendment Effective Date.

## 2. RESTATEMENT OF SECTION 10.3

Section 10.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 10.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 10.1 and 10.2 shall not apply to:
>
> (a) Licensee's indemnification obligations under Section 9.2;
>
> (b) Licensee's breach of Section 7; or
>
> (c) Licensee's obligation to pay fees due under Section 4.

## 3. THERMASTRESS FEES AND SERVICES

3.1 **Module Fee.** A new Section 4.7 is added to the Agreement as follows:

> 4.7 **ThermaStress Module Fee.** In addition to the Annual License Fee, Licensee shall pay Licensor a module fee of $165,000 per contract year for the ThermaStress Analysis Module (the "**ThermaStress Module Fee**"). For the contract year in which the Amendment Effective Date falls, the ThermaStress Module Fee is prorated on a daily basis from the Amendment Effective Date to the end of that contract year. Licensor shall invoice the prorated amount on the Amendment Effective Date and shall invoice the ThermaStress Module Fee for each later contract year together with the Annual License Fee under Section 4.2. The ThermaStress Module Fee includes support and maintenance of the ThermaStress Analysis Module under Section 3.3.

3.2 **Annual License Fee.** The Annual License Fee under Section 4.1 of the Agreement remains $1,240,000 per contract year.

3.3 **Implementation.** Schedule B to the Agreement is amended by adding the following paragraph after paragraph B.1:

> **B.1A ThermaStress Implementation.** Licensor shall install and configure the ThermaStress Analysis Module at each Licensed Site, integrate it with the FacadeDesign Module and TemperControl Module, and provide up to sixteen (16) hours of remote Authorized User training. These services are to be completed within forty-five (45) days after the Amendment Effective Date and are included in the ThermaStress Module Fee for the contract year in which the Amendment Effective Date falls.

3.4 **Support.** The support hours, severity levels and response times in paragraphs B.2 and B.3 of Schedule B apply to the ThermaStress Analysis Module.

## 4. REFERENCES AND DEFINED TERMS

4.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

4.2 From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning refers to the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 5. NO WAIVER OR CONSENT

Except as expressly set out in this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. Nothing in this Amendment is a waiver of, or a consent under, any provision of the Agreement, and nothing in this Amendment limits or impairs any right or remedy of either Party under the Agreement.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Amendment is governed by Section 15 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

6.3 **Authority.** Each Party represents that the individual signing this Amendment on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.
