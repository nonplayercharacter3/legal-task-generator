# TERM SHEET

## GlazeCore Software License: Lavas Glazing Software, LLC / Namaith Facade Systems, Inc.

**Date:** January 22, 2026

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Lavas Glazing Software, LLC proposes to license the GlazeCore platform to Namaith Facade Systems, Inc. Except for Item 12, this term sheet is not binding. A binding obligation will arise only when the parties sign a definitive software license agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

---

## 1. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Lavas Glazing Software, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, Austin, Texas |
| Licensee | Namaith Facade Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation, Toledo, Ohio |
| Guarantor | Lavas Technologies Holdings, Inc., parent of Licensor |

## 2. Licensed Software

The GlazeCore facade-engineering and tempering-control platform, licensed in object code, with Documentation and all generally released Updates. The license will cover the following modules:

- GlazeCore Core Platform
- FacadeDesign Module
- TemperControl Module
- CutOptimizer Module
- QualityTrace Module

The parties may add modules later by amending the module schedule.

## 3. Scope of License

- Non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license for Licensee's internal facade-engineering, design, fabrication and tempering-control operations.
- Licensed Sites: Toledo, Ohio and Joliet, Illinois. Licensee may add further facilities by notice.
- Up to 150 named Authorized Users.
- Licensee may integrate the software with its tempering furnaces, cutting tables and inspection systems through the documented interfaces.

## 4. Term

Initial term of five (5) contract years from the effective date of the Definitive Agreement. After that, the license renews automatically for one-year renewal terms unless either party gives at least 180 days' notice of non-renewal.

## 5. Fees

- Annual License Fee of $1,240,000 per contract year, invoiced annually in advance and payable within 30 days of receipt.
- The Annual License Fee includes support and maintenance.
- Implementation services for the first contract year are included. These cover installation, TemperControl furnace integration, data migration and up to 40 hours of on-site training.
- Any additional services will be charged at $245 per hour for engineers and $310 per hour for senior architects.

## 6. Support and Service Levels

- 24x7 support for Severity 1 issues, meaning a production stoppage or a tempering-control failure.
- Severity 1 response within 1 hour, with a workaround or fix within 8 hours.
- The full severity matrix will be set out in a schedule to the Definitive Agreement.

## 7. Indemnities and Limitation of Liability

**Licensor IP indemnity.** Licensor will defend and indemnify Licensee against third-party claims that the Licensed Software infringes or misappropriates any intellectual property right. Licensor will also have customary procure, modify or replace remedies, with a refund remedy if those are not commercially reasonable. The usual exclusions will apply for unauthorized modifications and for combinations with items Licensor did not supply. Licensor's IP infringement indemnity is excluded from the liability cap (uncapped).

**Licensee indemnity.** Licensee will indemnify Licensor against third-party claims. Licensee's indemnity is limited to claims from Licensee's negligence or modifications; subject to liability cap.

**Liability cap.** Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable by Licensee in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**Consequential damages.** There will be a mutual exclusion of indirect, consequential and punitive damages and lost profits.

**Exceptions.** The following will also be excluded from the cap and from the consequential-damages exclusion:

- breaches of confidentiality; and
- Licensee's obligation to pay fees due.

## 8. Warranties

- Licensor warrants that the software will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the term.
- If a nonconformity is not corrected within 60 days, Licensee may terminate and receive a pro rata refund of prepaid fees.
- Licensor warrants that the software contains no malicious code.
- Licensor warrants that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

## 9. Insurance

Licensor will maintain the following coverage, with insurers rated A- VII or better:

- commercial general liability of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate; and
- technology errors and omissions insurance, including cyber coverage, of at least $5,000,000.

Licensor will deliver certificates after signing and on each renewal.

## 10. Parent Guaranty

Lavas Technologies Holdings, Inc. will deliver a guaranty of Licensor's obligations under the Definitive Agreement at signing.

## 11. Governing Law and Forum

The Definitive Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The parties will submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware. Before litigation, senior executives will meet to try to resolve the dispute. Each party will waive jury trial.

## 12. Confidentiality; Non-Binding Effect

The terms of this term sheet and the discussions between the parties are confidential. Each party will keep them confidential on the terms of the parties' existing mutual non-disclosure agreement. This Item 12 is binding. No other provision of this term sheet creates a legal obligation, and either party may end negotiations at any time before the Definitive Agreement is signed.

## 13. Target Timing

The parties are targeting signing of the Definitive Agreement and the Parent Guaranty in early March 2026.
