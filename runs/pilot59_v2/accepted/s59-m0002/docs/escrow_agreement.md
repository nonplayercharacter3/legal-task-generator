# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and among **Treikaith Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Mateo, California ("**Buyer**"), **Daibaind Games, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company based in Austin, Texas ("**Seller**"), and **Pellmont Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer will acquire from Seller the "Emberfall Saga" franchise business and related assets.

B. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,800,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Buyer and Seller wish to appoint the Escrow Agent, and the Escrow Agent agrees to act, to hold, invest and disburse the Indemnity Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Appointment

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" means a written notice from Buyer to Seller and the Escrow Agent under Section 4.3(a).

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under the Purchase Agreement. Buyer and Seller shall confirm that date to the Escrow Agent in writing on the Closing Date.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Indemnity Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any interest and earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements under this Agreement.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 9.2. It includes any order that is not appealed within the time allowed.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims stated in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been paid or finally resolved under Section 4.

1.2 **Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Indemnity Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

## 2. Deposit and Escrow Account

2.1 **Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount of $4,800,000 with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller on the date of receipt.

2.2 **Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Funds in a segregated account maintained in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Indemnity Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, attachment or claim of any creditor of a party, and the Escrow Agent shall not commingle them with its own funds. They may be used only as provided in this Agreement.

2.3 **No Other Duties.** The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by, the Purchase Agreement. It has no duty to determine whether any party has complied with it.

## 3. Investment

3.1 **Permitted Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Indemnity Escrow Funds in the Pellmont Government Obligations Money Market Fund, or in another investment named in a Joint Written Instruction. Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the investment is not insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

3.2 **Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Indemnity Escrow Funds form part of the Indemnity Escrow Funds. They shall be disbursed with the principal in accordance with Section 4.

3.3 **Losses.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss on an investment made in accordance with this Section 3, or for any liquidation of an investment needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement.

## 4. Disbursements

4.1 **Joint Written Instructions and Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Indemnity Escrow Funds within two (2) Business Days after it receives either (a) a Joint Written Instruction or (b) a copy of a Final Order, together with a certificate from the party presenting it confirming that the order is final and non-appealable. Each disbursement shall be made in accordance with that instruction or order.

4.2 **Release Date.** On the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release all remaining Indemnity Escrow Funds to Seller, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the retained Pending Claim Amount. As each claim is resolved under Section 4.3, the Escrow Agent shall (a) pay to Buyer the amount owed to Buyer in respect of that claim and (b) release to Seller the excess of the amount retained for that claim over the amount paid to Buyer. Buyer and Seller shall deliver a Joint Written Instruction confirming the Release Date and the amount to be released at least three (3) Business Days before the Release Date. If they fail to do so, the Escrow Agent may still make the release under this Section 4.2 on the basis of the Claim Notices it has received.

4.3 **Claims Procedure.**

(a) *Claim Notice.* Before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver a Claim Notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent for any claim for Losses under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice must state in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which it is made and the amount claimed. If the amount is not then determinable, the notice must give a good faith estimate of it.

(b) *Objection.* Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within twenty (20) Business Days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). The objection must state in reasonable detail the basis for it.

(c) *No Objection.* If the Escrow Agent receives no objection within the Objection Period, it shall disburse the claimed amount to Buyer on the second (2nd) Business Day after the Objection Period ends.

(d) *Partial Objection.* If Seller objects to only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer on the second (2nd) Business Day after the Objection Period ends. It shall continue to hold the disputed part.

(e) *Disputed Claims.* The Escrow Agent shall hold any disputed amount until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order resolving the claim. Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days after an objection to resolve the dispute. After that period, either party may commence proceedings under Section 9.2.

4.4 **Insufficient Funds.** If the Indemnity Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay a claim in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay the funds then held. The Escrow Agent has no obligation to make any payment beyond the Indemnity Escrow Funds then held.

4.5 **Payment Method.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer to the accounts designated in Schedule 1, or to another account designated by a party in writing. Any change of account is subject to the Escrow Agent's call-back verification procedures under Section 5.5.

## 5. The Escrow Agent

5.1 **Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement and are purely ministerial. No implied duties or fiduciary obligations will be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent.

5.2 **Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed by the proper person. It has no duty to inquire into the validity or accuracy of any such document.

5.3 **Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except for its own gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect or consequential damages.

5.4 **Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, unless a court determines that the amount resulted from the actions of one party alone.

5.5 **Security Procedures.** The Escrow Agent may confirm any funds transfer instruction by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed in Schedule 2. It may delay a disbursement until that confirmation is obtained.

5.6 **Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, it may refrain from acting until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the disputed funds with a court under Section 9.2 by interpleader.

## 6. Fees and Expenses

6.1 **Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set out in Schedule 3, together with its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of those fees and expenses.

6.2 **No Deduction.** The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Indemnity Escrow Funds unless they remain unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 7. Tax Matters

7.1 **Tax Reporting.** For tax reporting purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Indemnity Escrow Funds, and all earnings on them are allocated to Buyer until disbursed. Earnings disbursed to Seller are allocated to Seller in the year of disbursement. Each party shall deliver a properly completed IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent on or before the Effective Date.

7.2 **Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold any tax required by law from any disbursement and shall report and remit that tax as required.

## 8. Resignation, Removal and Termination

8.1 **Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving Buyer and Seller at least thirty (30) days' written notice. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving it at least thirty (30) days' written notice. In either case, the resignation or removal takes effect only when a successor escrow agent appointed by Buyer and Seller has accepted the appointment and received the Indemnity Escrow Funds. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court under Section 9.2 to appoint one.

8.2 **Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Indemnity Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with Section 4. Sections 5.3, 5.4 and 6 survive termination and any resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. General Provisions

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

9.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware.

9.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

9.4 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is effective when delivered personally, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. However, a notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only when it is actually received. Notices shall be addressed as follows:

If to Buyer: Treikaith Interactive, Inc., San Mateo, California, Attention: General Counsel.

If to Seller: Daibaind Games, LLC, Austin, Texas, Attention: Managing Member.

If to the Escrow Agent: Pellmont Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration.

9.5 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

9.6 **Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as provided in Section 8.1. A merger or consolidation of the Escrow Agent, or a transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, is not an assignment requiring consent.

9.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

9.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

9.9 **USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the identifying information that it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification Laws.
