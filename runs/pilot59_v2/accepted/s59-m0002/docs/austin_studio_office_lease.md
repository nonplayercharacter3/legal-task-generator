# OFFICE LEASE

## 2. Premises and Term

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the Building, including lobbies, elevators, restrooms, loading areas and the parking garage, in common with other tenants.

2.2 **Measurement.** The Rentable Area of the Premises and of the Building have been determined under the BOMA Office Standard (ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017). Those figures are stipulated for all purposes of this Lease and are not subject to remeasurement.

2.3 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless it ends earlier or is extended under this Lease.

2.4 **Early Access.** Beginning on the later of the Effective Date and Landlord's delivery of the Premises with Landlord's Work substantially complete, Tenant may enter the Premises to install furniture, fixtures, cabling, servers and equipment. During early access, all terms of this Lease apply except the obligation to pay Base Rent and Operating Expenses.

2.5 **Delivery Condition.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises with the work described in Section 9.1 ("**Landlord's Work**") substantially complete and with Building systems serving the Premises in good working order. If Landlord's Work is not substantially complete by December 15, 2026, then (a) the Commencement Date is postponed day for day until it is substantially complete and (b) the Abatement Period, Expiration Date and rent schedule shift accordingly. Landlord and Tenant shall confirm any adjusted dates in a written commencement memorandum.

## 3. Rent

3.1 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay monthly base rent ("**Base Rent**") in advance on the first day of each calendar month, as follows:

| Months of Term | Annual Rate per RSF | Monthly Base Rent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 – 12 | $56.00 | $86,333.33 |
| 13 – 24 | $57.68 | $88,923.33 |
| 25 – 36 | $59.41 | $91,590.42 |
| 37 – 48 | $61.19 | $94,334.58 |
| 49 – 60 | $63.03 | $97,171.25 |
| 61 – 64 | $64.92 | $100,085.00 |

3.2 **Abatement.** Base Rent is abated in full for the Abatement Period. Operating Expenses are not abated. If Tenant is in monetary default beyond any applicable cure period and Landlord terminates this Lease as a result, the unamortized portion of the abated Base Rent becomes immediately due.

3.3 **Operating Expenses.** Beginning on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses for each calendar year. Payments are made monthly in advance, based on Landlord's reasonable annual estimate. "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs of operating, maintaining, insuring and repairing the Building and its land, including real estate taxes, common area utilities, janitorial service for common areas, security and a management fee not exceeding three percent (3%) of gross Building revenue. Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital expenditures, except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by Laws enacted after the Effective Date, in each case amortized over their useful life;

(b) leasing commissions, tenant improvement costs and marketing costs;

(c) principal, interest and other debt service on any mortgage;

(d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(e) costs arising from Landlord's negligence or violation of Law; and

(f) costs of remediating Hazardous Materials not introduced by Tenant.

3.4 **Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses other than real estate taxes, insurance premiums, utilities and snow and ice removal ("**Controllable Expenses**") shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year, on a non-cumulative basis, over the Controllable Expenses for the prior calendar year.

3.5 **Reconciliation and Audit.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver to Tenant a statement of actual Operating Expenses for that year. Any overpayment is credited against the next amounts due or, after the end of the Term, refunded within thirty (30) days. Any underpayment is due within thirty (30) days after the statement. Tenant may audit Landlord's records for any calendar year within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving the statement. If the audit shows that Operating Expenses were overstated by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs.

3.6 **Late Charge.** Any amount not paid within five (5) days after its due date bears a late charge of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve (12) month period if Tenant pays the overdue amount within five (5) days after written notice.

## 4. Security Deposit

4.1 **Deposit.** Tenant shall deliver the Security Deposit to Landlord within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any Event of Default. Tenant shall then restore the Security Deposit within ten (10) days after written demand.

4.2 **Return.** Landlord shall return any unapplied portion of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises. The Security Deposit may be commingled with Landlord's other funds and earns no interest for Tenant.

4.3 **Transfer.** If Landlord transfers its interest in the Building, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the transferee. Landlord is then released from liability for its return.

## 5. Use and Compliance

5.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

5.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all Laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all Laws applicable to the structure, the Building systems and the common areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act and the Texas Architectural Barriers Act. "**Laws**" means all statutes, ordinances, codes, regulations and orders of any governmental authority.

5.3 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the rules and regulations attached as Exhibit B, and with reasonable, non-discriminatory amendments that Landlord gives Tenant in writing. If those rules conflict with this Lease, this Lease controls.

5.4 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring Hazardous Materials onto the Premises, except customary office, cleaning and electronics supplies used in compliance with Law. "**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance regulated as hazardous or toxic under any environmental Law.

5.5 **After-Hours Access.** Tenant and its employees may access the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's access-card security system.

## 6. Services and Utilities

6.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall furnish the following:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding Building holidays;

(b) passenger elevator service at all times and freight elevator service on reasonable advance request;

(c) janitorial service to the Premises five (5) nights per week, to a standard comparable to similar Class A buildings in central Austin; and

(d) electricity to the Premises sufficient for general office use and the Permitted Use.

6.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside the hours in Section 6.1(a) on Tenant's request, at Landlord's actual cost. That cost is currently $65.00 per hour per floor.

6.3 **Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is separately submetered. Tenant shall pay for that electricity at Landlord's actual cost, without markup, within thirty (30) days after invoice.

6.4 **Supplemental Cooling.** Tenant may install, at its cost, a supplemental HVAC unit to serve its server and build room. It may connect the unit to the Building's condenser water loop, at a tap fee of $750.00 per ton per year.

6.5 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may select its own telecommunications and internet providers. Landlord shall give those providers reasonable access to the Building's risers, conduits and telecommunications rooms. Tenant may also install up to two (2) satellite or microwave dishes on the Building roof, in locations Landlord reasonably designates and at no rent.

6.6 **Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. However, if any interruption (a) is caused by Landlord's negligence and (b) makes the Premises untenantable for more than three (3) consecutive Business Days after Tenant's notice, Base Rent and Operating Expenses abate from the fourth (4th) Business Day until service is restored.

## 7. Repairs and Maintenance

7.1 **Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements, exterior walls, common areas, parking garage and Building systems up to their point of connection to the Premises.

7.2 **Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. This includes interior finishes, Tenant's supplemental HVAC unit and any fixtures Tenant installs.

## 8. Alterations

8.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations costing less than $50,000 per project that do not affect the structure or Building systems. Tenant must give Landlord ten (10) days' prior notice of any such cosmetic alteration.

8.2 **Performance.** Alterations must be performed by licensed contractors, in a good and workmanlike manner and in compliance with Law. Tenant shall keep the Building free of mechanics' liens arising from Tenant's work. Tenant shall discharge or bond over any such lien within twenty (20) days after notice of its filing.

8.3 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall remove its cabling and any alteration that Landlord designated for removal at the time of consent. Tenant need not remove Landlord's Work or the Tenant Improvements.

## 9. Improvements

9.1 **Landlord's Work.** At Landlord's sole cost, Landlord shall (a) demise the fourth floor as a single-tenant floor, (b) deliver code-compliant restrooms on the floor, and (c) deliver the ceiling grid, base building lighting and HVAC distribution in good working order.

9.2 **Tenant Improvement Allowance.** Landlord shall provide an allowance of $65.00 per rentable square foot ($1,202,500.00) toward the cost of Tenant's initial improvements to the Premises (the "**Tenant Improvements**"). The Tenant Improvements may include sound-isolated audio and motion capture rooms, a server and build room, and an open studio layout. Tenant may apply up to $10.00 per rentable square foot of the allowance to cabling, signage, furniture and move costs. Landlord shall disburse the allowance monthly on progress draws, against invoices and lien waivers. Any allowance not requested by December 31, 2027 is forfeited.

9.3 **Plans.** Tenant shall submit plans for the Tenant Improvements to Landlord for approval. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold approval and shall respond within ten (10) Business Days after receipt. Landlord's construction management fee for the Tenant Improvements is two percent (2%) of their hard costs.

## 10. Assignment and Subletting

10.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 10.2, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay that consent. Landlord shall respond to any request within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the information it reasonably requires.

10.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's consent, but with written notice to Landlord within ten (10) Business Days after the transfer, to any of the following (each, a "**Permitted Transferee**"):

(a) an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant;

(b) an entity resulting from a merger or consolidation with Tenant; or

(c) an entity that acquires all or substantially all of Tenant's assets or stock, or all or substantially all of the business conducted at the Premises.

10.3 **Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received under a sublease or assignment, other than to a Permitted Transferee, that exceeds the rent payable under this Lease. This is calculated after Tenant recovers its reasonable transfer costs.

10.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees in writing.

## 11. Insurance and Indemnity

11.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain, throughout the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, plus umbrella coverage of at least $5,000,000;

(b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and the Tenant Improvements at full replacement cost; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law.

Landlord, its property manager and its mortgagee shall be named as additional insureds on Tenant's liability policies.

11.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building at full replacement cost, together with commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings in Austin, Texas.

11.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all claims against the other party for loss or damage to its property, to the extent covered or required to be covered by property insurance under this Lease.

11.4 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 11.3, each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage, to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 12. Casualty and Condemnation

12.1 **Casualty.** If a fire or other casualty damages the Premises or Building access, Landlord shall restore them with reasonable diligence. Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable. Either party may terminate this Lease by written notice if (a) Landlord's contractor reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, or (b) the casualty occurs during the last twelve (12) months of the Term and restoration will take more than sixty (60) days.

12.2 **Condemnation.** If any taking renders a material part of the Premises or parking unusable for the Permitted Use, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving costs and its unamortized Tenant Improvement costs paid from its own funds.

## 13. Default and Remedies

13.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay any rent within five (5) days after written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation, and does not cure that failure within thirty (30) days after written notice. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant has a longer period, so long as it begins the cure within that period and pursues it diligently; or

(c) Tenant files for bankruptcy, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has an involuntary bankruptcy petition filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.2 **Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may (a) terminate this Lease by written notice, or (b) terminate Tenant's right of possession without terminating this Lease. In either case, Landlord may recover its damages as permitted by Texas law. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages, as required by Section 91.006 of the Texas Property Code.

13.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, Landlord is in default. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Landlord has a longer period, so long as it begins the cure within that period and pursues it diligently. If the failure materially interferes with Tenant's use of the Premises and Landlord does not cure it within an additional ten (10) Business Days after a second notice, Tenant may perform the obligation within the Premises. Landlord shall then reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 14. Renewal Option

14.1 **Option.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years. To exercise it, Tenant must give written notice to Landlord at least twelve (12) months, but not more than eighteen (18) months, before the Expiration Date. No Event of Default may then be continuing.

14.2 **Rent.** Base Rent for the extension term is ninety-five percent (95%) of the prevailing market rate for comparable renewal space in comparable buildings in the East Austin and Central Business District submarkets. If the parties do not agree on that rate within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, it is determined by baseball arbitration between two licensed Texas commercial real estate brokers, each having at least ten (10) years' office leasing experience in Austin.

## 15. Subordination, Estoppel and Surrender

15.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any present or future mortgage on the Building. That subordination is conditioned on the mortgagee's delivery of a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement. Under that agreement, Tenant's possession may not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default is continuing. Landlord shall deliver that agreement from its current mortgagee within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date.

15.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten (10) Business Days after a party's request, the other party shall deliver a certificate confirming (a) the status of this Lease, (b) the dates to which rent has been paid, and (c) whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

15.3 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and alterations Tenant need not remove excepted.

15.4 **Holdover.** If Tenant holds over without Landlord's consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. It shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term, prorated daily. Tenant is liable for consequential damages arising from a holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord's written notice that Landlord has signed a lease with a successor tenant for the Premises.

## 16. General Provisions

16.1 **Business Day.** "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a legal holiday in Austin, Texas.

16.2 **Notices.** All notices under this Lease must be in writing and sent to the addresses in Section 1.1. A notice is effective when delivered personally, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with a copy by one of the foregoing methods.

16.3 **Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Larkspur Commercial Realty, LLC, which represents Landlord, and Halden Tenant Advisors, Inc., which represents Tenant. Landlord shall pay both brokers' commissions under separate agreements. Each party shall indemnify the other against any claim by any other broker claiming through it.

16.4 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant may peaceably enjoy the Premises without hindrance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

16.5 **Signage.** Tenant may install, at its cost, its name and logo on the fourth-floor elevator lobby and suite entry, and on the Building directory. Tenant may also place its name on one (1) panel of the Building's monument sign, subject to Law and Landlord's reasonable approval of the design.

16.6 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Building, including its rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds.

16.7 **Governing Law and Venue.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any action arising out of this Lease shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Travis County, Texas.

16.8 **Confidentiality.** Tenant shall keep the economic terms of this Lease confidential. However, Tenant may disclose them to its attorneys, accountants, lenders, investors, prospective transferees and as required by Law.

16.9 **Memorandum.** Neither party shall record this Lease. At either party's request, the parties shall execute and record a short-form memorandum of lease that omits the economic terms.

16.10 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

16.11 **Entire Agreement.** This Lease, including its Exhibits, is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant concerning the leasing of the Premises. It may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both of them.

16.12 **Authority.** Each party represents that the individual signing this Lease on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.

## EXHIBIT A: DESCRIPTION OF PREMISES

The Premises consist of the entire fourth (4th) floor of the Comal Yard Office Building, 501 Comal Street, Austin, Texas 78702, known as Suite 400, containing 18,500 rentable square feet. The Premises are the area within the demising walls of the fourth floor shown cross-hatched on the floor plan initialed by the parties on the Effective Date. That area excludes the Building core, the elevator shafts, the stairwells and the Building's mechanical and telecommunications rooms on that floor. Tenant has access to those rooms only as provided in Section 6.5.

## EXHIBIT B: RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. Sidewalks, entrances, lobbies, corridors and stairways may not be obstructed or used for any purpose other than access to and from the Premises.
2. Tenant may not change or add locks on Building entry doors. Tenant may install its own access-control system on the suite entry doors if it gives Landlord the means of entry for emergencies.
3. Deliveries of furniture, equipment and bulk supplies must use the freight elevator and loading dock, at times Landlord reasonably schedules.
4. Smoking and vaping are prohibited throughout the Building and within twenty-five (25) feet of any entrance.
5. Tenant shall not operate equipment that produces noise or vibration that unreasonably disturbs other tenants. Audio and motion capture work must be confined to rooms built with sound isolation.
6. Bicycles may be stored only in the Building bicycle room or within the Premises.
7. Tenant shall comply with the Building's reasonable emergency evacuation procedures and participate in scheduled fire drills.
8. Pets are not permitted in the Building, except service animals.
