# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into by and between **Treikaith Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Mateo, California (the "**Company**"), and **Lioba Fenwright**, an individual resident in Austin, Texas ("**Executive**"). It takes effect on the Effective Date defined in Section 1.1.

## RECITALS

A. The Company and Daibaind Games, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("**Daibaind**"), are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will acquire the "Emberfall Saga" franchise business of Daibaind, including its intellectual property, source code and build tools, its Austin development team and certain related contracts (the "**Business**").

B. Executive is the Studio Creative Director of the Business. Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement provides that this Agreement will be entered into at or before the closing of that acquisition (the "**Closing**").

C. The Company wishes to employ Executive, and Executive wishes to be employed by the Company, from and after the Closing on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Effectiveness and Term

1.1 **Effective Date.** This Agreement becomes effective on the date on which the Closing occurs (the "**Closing Date**" or "**Effective Date**"). If the Purchase Agreement terminates before the Closing, this Agreement is void and of no further effect.

1.2 **At-Will Employment.** Executive's employment with the Company is "at will." Either party may terminate it at any time, with or without Cause or Good Reason, subject to the notice and severance provisions of Section 5. The period from the Effective Date until the termination of Executive's employment is the "**Employment Period**."

## 2. Position and Duties

2.1 **Position.** During the Employment Period, Executive shall serve as Studio Creative Director, Emberfall Saga, reporting to the Company's Chief Creative Officer.

2.2 **Duties.** Executive is responsible for the creative direction of the Emberfall Saga franchise. This includes game design, narrative, art direction and the creative leadership of the Austin development team. Executive shall also perform the other duties consistent with that position that the Company reasonably assigns.

2.3 **Location.** Executive's principal place of work is the Company's studio office in Austin, Texas. Executive shall travel to the Company's San Mateo, California headquarters and elsewhere as reasonably required by the Business.

2.4 **Exclusive Service.** During the Employment Period, Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time and efforts to the Company. Executive may (a) manage personal investments, (b) serve on civic or charitable boards and (c) give occasional talks at industry events, in each case only if these activities do not interfere with Executive's duties or breach Section 6 or Section 7.

## 3. Compensation

3.1 **Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $310,000. The salary is payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices and is subject to annual review by the Company.

3.2 **Annual Bonus.** Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of thirty percent (30%) of base salary. The bonus is based on franchise milestones and on individual and Company performance goals set by the Company. For calendar year 2026, the bonus will be prorated from the Effective Date. Except as provided in Section 5, a bonus is payable only if Executive is employed on the payment date.

3.3 **Retention Award.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $450,000, in two equal installments. The first installment is payable on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and the second on the second anniversary. Each installment is payable only if Executive remains employed on that date, except as provided in Section 5.2.

3.4 **Equity.** Subject to approval by the Company's Board of Directors, Executive will receive restricted stock units covering shares of the Company's common stock with a grant-date value of $1,200,000. The units vest in equal annual installments over four (4) years from the Effective Date and are governed by the Company's equity incentive plan and the applicable award agreement.

## 4. Benefits and Expenses

4.1 **Benefits.** Executive is eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to similarly situated employees, subject to the terms of those plans. For eligibility and vesting purposes, Executive's service with Daibaind will be credited to the extent permitted by those plans.

4.2 **Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to paid time off in accordance with Company policy, and to not less than twenty (20) days per year.

4.3 **Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing Executive's duties, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy.

## 5. Termination

5.1 **Definitions.**

(a) "**Cause**" means any of the following by Executive: (i) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony; (ii) fraud, embezzlement or material dishonesty toward the Company; (iii) willful and material breach of this Agreement or of a written Company policy that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or (iv) willful refusal to perform lawful duties after written notice.

(b) "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without Executive's written consent: (i) a material reduction in base salary; (ii) a material diminution in Executive's creative authority over the Emberfall Saga franchise; or (iii) relocation of Executive's principal place of work more than fifty (50) miles from Austin, Texas. Good Reason exists only if (A) Executive gives notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs, (B) the Company fails to cure within thirty (30) days after that notice, and (C) Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

5.2 **Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall pay Executive the following. Payment is conditioned on Executive signing a general release of claims in a form provided by the Company, and that release becoming effective:

(a) continued base salary for twelve (12) months;

(b) any unpaid installment of the retention bonus under Section 3.3; and

(c) Company-paid continuation coverage under COBRA for up to twelve (12) months.

5.3 **Other Terminations.** If Executive's employment ends for any other reason, the Company shall pay only accrued but unpaid salary, accrued and unused paid time off and unreimbursed expenses (the "**Accrued Obligations**").

5.4 **Notice.** Executive shall give the Company at least sixty (60) days' written notice of any resignation without Good Reason. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period.

## 6. Restrictive Covenants

6.1 **Non-Competition.** During the Employment Period and for a period of 24 months after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or the European Union (the "**Territory**"), do either of the following:

(a) own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, consult for or otherwise provide services to any Person that develops or publishes premium action role-playing or fantasy adventure video games for console or PC platforms (a "**Competing Business**"), in any role similar to the roles Executive held with Daibaind or holds with the Company; or

(b) develop, design or direct any video game that is substantially similar in setting, characters or core mechanics to any Emberfall Saga title.

Executive may hold up to two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any company as a passive investment.

6.2 **Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire any person who is or was during the preceding six (6) months an employee or individual contractor of the Business or of the Company. Executive also shall not encourage any such person to leave the Company. General advertisements not targeted at those persons are not a breach of this Section 6.2.

6.3 **Non-Solicitation of Business Partners.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, induce any platform holder, licensor, developer or other business partner of the Business to terminate or adversely change its relationship with the Company.

6.4 **Acknowledgments.** Executive acknowledges that the covenants in this Section 6 are given in connection with the Company's acquisition of the Business and its goodwill under the Purchase Agreement. Executive further acknowledges that Executive's role gives Executive access to the Company's confidential information and to the creative and technical assets of the Emberfall Saga franchise.

6.5 **Tolling.** If Executive breaches Section 6.1, 6.2 or 6.3, the Restricted Period is extended by the period of that breach.

## 7. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

7.1 **Confidential Information.** Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, either during or after the Employment Period, except as required to perform Executive's duties or as required by law. "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Company or the Business, including source code, build tools, unreleased game designs, sequel builds, story and lore documents, player data, financial information and business plans.

7.2 **Assignment of Inventions.** Executive hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in any works of authorship, inventions, designs, code, characters, art and other intellectual property that Executive creates, alone or with others, during the Employment Period and within the scope of Executive's employment (the "**Work Product**"). To the extent permitted by law, all Work Product is a "work made for hire." Executive waives all moral rights in the Work Product to the extent permitted by law.

7.3 **Return of Property.** When Executive's employment ends, Executive shall return all Company property, including devices, documents, repository credentials and copies of Confidential Information.

7.4 **Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive will not be held liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting a suspected violation of law.

## 8. Remedies

8.1 **Injunctive Relief.** Executive agrees that a breach of Section 6 or Section 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is therefore entitled to injunctive relief for any such breach, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

8.2 **Forfeiture.** If Executive materially breaches Section 6 or Section 7, the Company's obligations under Section 5.2 end. Any unpaid severance or retention installments are then forfeited.

## 9. General Provisions

9.1 **Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of Los Angeles, California. Each party consents to the personal jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

9.2 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is effective when delivered personally, one (1) business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its San Mateo, California headquarters, Attention: General Counsel. Notices to Executive go to the most recent home address on file with the Company.

9.3 **Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to applicable tax withholding.

9.4 **Section 409A.** The parties intend this Agreement to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. It shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

9.5 **Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by Executive. The Company may assign this Agreement to an affiliate, or to any successor to all or substantially all of the Business.

9.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing.

9.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

9.8 **Survival.** Sections 6, 7, 8 and 9 survive the termination of Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

9.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

9.10 **Advice of Counsel.** Executive acknowledges that Executive has had the opportunity to review this Agreement with independent counsel of Executive's choice.
