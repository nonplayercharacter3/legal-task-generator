# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026, by and between **Treikaith Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Mateo, California ("**Buyer**"), and **Daibaind Games, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company based in Austin, Texas ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller develops and publishes the video game franchise known as "Emberfall Saga" and operates an independent game development studio in Austin, Texas (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the following assets (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all development hardware, devkits, servers, workstations and other tangible personal property used in the Business and listed on Schedule 2.1(a);

(b) all intellectual property owned or purported to be owned by Seller and used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased IP**"). The Purchased IP includes: (i) the Emberfall Saga name, logos, characters, storylines, lore and associated trademarks and trade dress; (ii) all source code, object code, build tools, scripts, pipelines and development tools; (iii) all registered and unregistered copyrights; (iv) all art assets, audio assets, animations, models and design documents; (v) all unreleased sequel builds, prototypes and works in progress; (vi) all domain names and social media accounts; and (vii) all goodwill associated with any of the foregoing, together with all rights to sue for past, present and future infringement;

(c) all Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c), including platform publishing agreements, engine licenses and developer agreements (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all books, records, player data (to the extent transferable under applicable Law), files and correspondence relating to the Business; and

(e) all goodwill of the Business as a going concern.

**2.2 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price less the Indemnity Escrow Amount to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account Seller designates in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,800,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Funds and disburse them in accordance with the Escrow Agreement. The Indemnity Escrow Funds secure Seller's obligations under Article IX.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer is not assuming, and Seller shall retain and timely pay, perform and discharge, all liabilities and obligations of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all liabilities arising out of the operation of the Business or the ownership of the Purchased Assets before the Closing;

(b) all liabilities for Taxes of Seller, or relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any pre-Closing period;

(c) all liabilities relating to employees of Seller arising before the Closing, including accrued compensation, bonuses and severance;

(d) all liabilities arising out of any action, suit or proceeding pending or threatened as of the Closing, including the matters set forth on Schedule 2.4; and

(e) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets.

**2.5 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only those liabilities of Seller under the Assigned Contracts that arise and are to be performed after the Closing. Any liability arising out of a breach or default by Seller before the Closing is not assumed (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.6 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) cash and cash equivalents, (b) Seller's organizational documents and minute books, (c) Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts, (d) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, or (e) the assets listed on Schedule 2.6 (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.7 Allocation.** Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller an allocation of the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986. The parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived. Conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing must still be satisfied or waived at that time. The parties may agree in writing on another date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(b) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Key Employee;

(e) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.5;

(f) a certificate of Seller's secretary certifying the resolutions of Seller's members and managers approving this Agreement; and

(g) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Sections 2.2 and 2.3 and counterparts of each Ancillary Agreement to which Buyer is a party, duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Texas. Seller has all requisite power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations under them. All necessary action of Seller's members and managers has duly authorized that execution, delivery and performance. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's certificate of formation or company agreement, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.5, require consent under, conflict with, result in a breach of, or give rise to any right of termination under, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Financial Information.** Schedule 4.4 sets forth the unaudited revenue, royalty income and operating statements of the Business for fiscal years 2024 and 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026. That information was prepared from Seller's books and records and fairly presents, in all material respects, the results of operations of the Business for those periods.

**4.5 Consents.** Schedule 4.5 sets forth each consent, approval or notice of or to any Person required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller, including under any Assigned Contract (the "**Required Consents**").

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since June 30, 2026, Seller has conducted the Business in the ordinary course, and no Material Adverse Effect has occurred.

**4.7 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.7, there is no action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.8 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has operated the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. This includes consumer protection, data privacy and child online privacy Laws and Laws governing randomized in-game purchases.

**4.9 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.9 lists each Contract material to the Business, including each platform publishing agreement, engine license, middleware license, developer agreement and music or talent license (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract. Seller has made true and complete copies of each Material Contract available to Buyer.

**4.10 Employees.** Schedule 4.10 lists each employee and individual independent contractor of the Business, with title, compensation and work location. Seller has complied in all material respects with all applicable employment Laws.

**4.11 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and the Purchased Assets and has paid all Taxes shown as due on them. There are no Encumbrances for Taxes on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.12 Intellectual Property.**

(a) Seller has good and valid title to all Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

(b) Schedule 4.12 lists all registrations and applications for registration included in the Purchased IP. Each registration is subsisting and, to the Knowledge of Seller, valid and enforceable.

(c) To the Knowledge of Seller, the operation of the Business as currently conducted does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property rights of any Person. Except as set forth on Schedule 4.12, Seller has received no written claim alleging any such infringement.

(d) Each current and former employee and contractor who contributed to the development of any Emberfall Saga title has executed a written agreement presently assigning to Seller that individual's rights in those contributions.

(e) The source code for the Emberfall Saga titles has not been disclosed to any Person other than employees and contractors bound by written confidentiality obligations, and is not subject to any source code escrow.

(f) No Emberfall Saga title incorporates open-source software in a manner that requires the disclosure or licensing of any proprietary source code of the Business.

**4.13 Platform Compliance.** Each released Emberfall Saga title has passed the certification requirements of each platform holder party to an Assigned Contract. Seller has not received any written notice of material non-compliance from any such platform holder.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement for which Buyer could become liable.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. All necessary corporate action has duly authorized that execution, delivery and performance.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Independent Investigation.** Buyer has conducted its own independent investigation of the Business. In entering into this Agreement, Buyer has relied solely on that investigation and on the representations and warranties in Article IV.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Purchased Assets and its relationships with platform holders, licensors, employees and players. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not:

(a) sell, license or encumber any Purchased Asset;

(b) amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract; or

(c) increase the compensation of any employee of the Business other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access.** Before the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records, Contracts, source code repositories and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Notification; Schedule Supplements.**

(a) Before the Closing, Seller may, and shall promptly upon becoming aware of any relevant matter, supplement or amend the Disclosure Schedule by written notice to Buyer (each, a "**Schedule Supplement**"). A Schedule Supplement shall cover any matter arising or discovered after the date of this Agreement that, if existing on that date, would have been required to be set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, or that renders any representation or warranty of Seller inaccurate.

(b) Buyer may object to a Schedule Supplement by written notice delivered to Seller within five (5) Business Days after receipt by Buyer of that Schedule Supplement. If Buyer does not deliver a written objection within that period, the Schedule Supplement is deemed accepted by Buyer. Upon that deemed acceptance, the Schedule Supplement is treated as having cured any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty disclosed in it, for purposes of Article IX and of the condition in Section 7.2(a).

**6.4 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each Required Consent before the Closing, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer. Buyer shall reasonably cooperate with those efforts. No party is required to pay any consent fee to a third party other than as set forth on Schedule 4.5.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any Person other than Buyer concerning the sale of the Business or any material Purchased Asset.

**6.6 Employees.** Before the Closing, Buyer may offer employment to the employees of the Business listed on Schedule 4.10, on terms Buyer determines. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer's offers, shall not discourage any employee from accepting an offer, and shall release each accepting employee from any non-competition or confidentiality obligation that would restrict employment with Buyer.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except as required by Law.

**6.8 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by Law.

**6.9 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further action, as the other party reasonably requests to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement. This includes the transfer of any repositories, accounts and credentials comprising the Purchased IP.

**6.10 Key Employee.** At or before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Key Employee to enter into an employment agreement with Buyer, in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer (the "**Key Employee Agreement**"). The Key Employee Agreement shall include covenants not to compete with the Business, and not to solicit employees of the Business, for a period of 24 months after the Closing Date. The parties acknowledge that the Key Employee holds a substantial economic interest in Seller and will receive substantial value from the Purchase Price. The parties further acknowledge that those covenants are necessary to protect the goodwill of the Business acquired by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligation of each party to consummate the Closing is subject to the following: no Governmental Authority shall have enacted, issued or entered any Law or order that is in effect and prohibits the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of each of the following:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and the General Representations shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date as though made on that date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required by this Agreement to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate, signed by an authorized officer of Seller, certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) each Required Consent set forth on Schedule 4.5 shall have been obtained and shall be in full force and effect;

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred since the date of this Agreement; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each item listed in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of each of the following:

(a) Buyer's representations and warranties shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before or at the Closing; and

(c) Buyer shall have delivered each item listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026. This right is not available to a party whose breach caused the failure of the Closing to occur by that date; or

(c) by either party, upon a material breach by the other party that would cause a condition in Article VII not to be satisfied and that is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void and of no further effect, except for this Section 8.2, Section 6.8 and Article XI. Termination does not relieve any party from liability for Fraud or willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations shall survive the Closing until eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability or Excluded Asset.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, members and managers from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) An indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of any claim to the indemnifying party, describing the claim in reasonable detail. A failure to give notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent the indemnifying party is materially prejudiced by that failure.

(b) The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, by written notice within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the claim notice. The indemnified party may participate in that defense at its own expense. No settlement that imposes any obligation on the indemnified party, other than the payment of money fully indemnified under this Agreement, may be entered into without the indemnified party's consent.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller for all Losses under Section 9.2 shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,800,000) (the "**Cap**"). Seller has no liability under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations until the aggregate Losses exceed $240,000, after which Seller is liable from the first dollar.

(b) The Cap and the threshold in Section 9.5(a) do not apply to Losses arising out of (i) Fraud, (ii) any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, or (iii) Seller's obligations in respect of Excluded Liabilities pursuant to Section 9.2(c). Seller's aggregate liability for Losses under clause (ii) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Losses are calculated net of insurance proceeds actually recovered by the indemnified party, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** The Indemnity Escrow Funds then held by the Escrow Agent are the sole and exclusive source of recovery for any Losses of Buyer under Section 9.2(a) arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any General Representation, except in the case of Fraud. For Losses arising out of Fraud, any breach of a Fundamental Representation, or Section 9.2(b) or 9.2(c), Buyer may recover first from the Indemnity Escrow Funds then held and then directly from Seller. Disbursements of the Indemnity Escrow Funds shall be made in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. This Section 9.7 does not limit claims for Fraud or the right to specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.12.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of all sales, use, transfer and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property Taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer on a per diem basis.

**10.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall reasonably cooperate in the preparation of Tax returns and in any audit relating to the Purchased Assets.

Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or the posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy.
