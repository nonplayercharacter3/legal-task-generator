# SIDE LETTER

**Re: Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026, between Treikaith Interactive, Inc. and Daibaind Games, LLC: Amendment No. 1**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into by Treikaith Interactive, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Daibaind Games, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("**Seller**"). It refers to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026, between Buyer and Seller (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. Background and Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **Recitals.** The parties record the following:

(a) Since the date of the Agreement, Buyer and Seller have reviewed the indemnification provisions of Article IX in preparation for the Closing. They have agreed to make the conforming clarification to Section 9.5 of the Agreement set out in Section 2 of this Side Letter.

(b) On October 2, 2026, Seller delivered Supplement No. 1 to the Disclosure Schedule. Supplement No. 1 relates to the claim of Orvenna Sound Collective concerning the Emberfall Saga soundtrack.

(c) On October 16, 2026, Buyer delivered to Seller a written objection to Supplement No. 1 under Section 6.3(b) of the Agreement.

(d) Seller contends that Supplement No. 1 was deemed accepted by Buyer under Section 6.3(b) of the Agreement. Buyer disputes that contention. Each party reserves all of its rights and positions on that matter.

## 2. Amendment of Section 9.5(b)

2.1 **Restated Section 9.5(b).** Section 9.5(b) of the Agreement is hereby amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

> "(b) The Cap and the threshold in Section 9.5(a) do not apply to Losses arising out of Fraud or breach of any Fundamental Representation. Seller's aggregate liability for Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation shall not exceed the Purchase Price."

2.2 **Effect.** The amendment in Section 2.1 is effective as of the date of this Side Letter. From that date, each reference in the Agreement to Section 9.5(b) refers to that Section as restated by this Side Letter.

## 3. Reservation of Rights

Nothing in this Side Letter is a waiver, acceptance or admission by either party concerning Supplement No. 1, Buyer's objection of October 16, 2026, or the claim of Orvenna Sound Collective. Each party reserves all of its rights under the Agreement on those matters.

## 4. Amendment Under Section 11.9

4.1 **Amendment.** This Side Letter is an amendment of the Agreement. It is executed in accordance with Section 11.9 of the Agreement and is signed by Buyer and Seller.

4.2 **No Other Changes.** Except as expressly amended by this Side Letter, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the date of this Side Letter, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" refers to the Agreement as amended by this Side Letter.

## 5. General Provisions

5.1 **Governing Law.** This Side Letter, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware. Conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction do not apply.

5.2 **Forum.** Section 11.7 (Exclusive Forum) and Section 11.8 (Waiver of Jury Trial) of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter as if set out in it in full, with conforming changes.

5.3 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
