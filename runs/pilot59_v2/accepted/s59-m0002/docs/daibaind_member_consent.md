# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE MEMBERS OF DAIBAIND GAMES, LLC

**A Texas Limited Liability Company**

**In Lieu of a Meeting**

**Dated: September 14, 2026**

The undersigned are all of the members (each, a "**Member**," and together, the "**Members**") of **Daibaind Games, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company (the "**Company**"). Acting under Section 101.359 of the Texas Business Organizations Code and Section 7.6 of the Company Agreement of the Company dated March 1, 2018, as amended (the "**Company Agreement**"), they consent to the adoption of the following resolutions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Members at a duly called meeting.

## 1. Background

1.1 **The Business.** The Company develops and publishes the video game franchise known as "Emberfall Saga" and operates an independent game development studio in Austin, Texas (the "**Business**").

1.2 **The Proposed Transaction.** The Company proposes to enter into an Asset Purchase Agreement with **Treikaith Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Mateo, California (the "**Buyer**"), to be dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will sell to the Buyer the franchise intellectual property, source code and build tools, related tangible assets and the assigned platform, engine and developer contracts of the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"). The Buyer will also assume the Assumed Liabilities. The Buyer will offer employment to the Austin development team (together, the "**Transaction**").

1.3 **Consideration.** The aggregate purchase price under the Purchase Agreement is $48,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, $4,800,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") will be deposited with Pellmont Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent. The deposit secures the Company's indemnification obligations under the Purchase Agreement.

1.4 **Member Approval Required.** Section 7.4(b) of the Company Agreement requires the approval of Members holding a majority of the outstanding Membership Interests for any sale, lease or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company outside the ordinary course of business. The Purchased Assets are all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

1.5 **Review.** The Members have received and reviewed (a) the final form of the Purchase Agreement, (b) the Disclosure Schedule, and (c) the forms of the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. Together, items (c) are the "**Ancillary Agreements**." The Members have also had the opportunity to consult counsel and other advisers of their choice.

1.6 **Interested Member.** Lioba Fenwright, a Member and the Company's Studio Creative Director, has disclosed the following to the other Members. It is a condition of the Transaction that she enter into an employment agreement with the Buyer at or before the Closing. That agreement will include covenants not to compete and not to solicit employees. Under it, she will receive compensation, a retention award and equity from the Buyer. The other Members have considered those arrangements in giving this consent.

## 2. Approval of the Transaction

2.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Transaction and the Company's entry into the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, substantially in the forms reviewed by the Members, are approved, authorized and adopted in all respects. Those agreements may include such changes as the Managing Member approves, with that approval conclusively evidenced by the Managing Member's execution of them.

2.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the sale, conveyance, assignment, transfer and delivery of the Purchased Assets to the Buyer, on the terms of the Purchase Agreement, are approved as a disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company for purposes of Section 7.4(b) of the Company Agreement.

2.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Members find the Transaction, including the Purchase Price, to be fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its Members.

## 3. Authorization of Officers

3.1 **RESOLVED**, that Halric Venmoor, as Managing Member of the Company (the "**Managing Member**"), is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take the following actions:

(a) execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement, the Disclosure Schedule and each Ancillary Agreement;

(b) prepare and deliver any Schedule Supplement required or permitted under Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement;

(c) seek the Required Consents set forth on Schedule 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule, and deliver the notices required under the Assigned Contracts;

(d) execute and deliver the closing certificate required by Section 7.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(e) execute and deliver every other certificate, instrument and document that the Managing Member considers necessary or advisable to consummate the Transaction.

3.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Managing Member and the Company's Chief Financial Officer are each authorized to take the following actions in connection with the Closing:

(a) designate in writing the Company's account for receipt of the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) arrange for the repayment in full of the Company's revolving credit facility with Brazos Commerce Bank, and obtain the release of the related liens at or before the Closing; and

(c) deliver to the Buyer and the escrow agent the tax forms, wire instructions and identifying information required under the Escrow Agreement.

3.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary of the Company is authorized to certify these resolutions to the Buyer under Section 3.2(f) of the Purchase Agreement, and to certify the incumbency of each person signing on behalf of the Company.

## 4. Employees

4.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Company shall cooperate with the Buyer's offers of employment to the employees of the Business, as required by Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement. Effective as of the Closing, the Company releases each employee who accepts employment with the Buyer from any non-competition or confidentiality obligation owed to the Company that would restrict that employment.

4.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Lioba Fenwright to enter into the Key Employee Agreement with the Buyer at or before the Closing, as required by Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. General Authority

5.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Managing Member and each other officer of the Company is authorized to take all further actions, pay all fees and expenses, and execute and deliver all further documents that the Managing Member or that officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

5.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by the Managing Member or any officer of the Company in connection with the negotiation of the Transaction, consistent with these resolutions, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 6. Effectiveness

6.1 **Effective Date.** This written consent is effective when it has been signed by all of the Members. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Members.

6.2 **Counterparts.** This written consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or by PDF. All counterparts together are one instrument.

6.3 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used and not defined in this written consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.
