# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Daibaind Games, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company based in Austin, Texas ("**Assignor**"), and **Treikaith Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation headquartered in San Mateo, California ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as Buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Assignor has agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Assignee the "Emberfall Saga" franchise business and related assets, including the Purchased IP.

B. Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to deliver this Agreement, duly executed, at the Closing. This Agreement is the IP Assignment Agreement referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is delivered in the form of Exhibit C to it.

C. The parties are entering into this Agreement to record the assignment of the Assigned IP with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, the European Union Intellectual Property Office and any other applicable Governmental Authority.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Assigned IP.** "**Assigned IP**" means all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the following, in each case to the extent registered or applied-for and set forth on Exhibit A:

(a) the trademarks, service marks, logos and trade dress, together with all registrations and applications for registration of them, and all goodwill of the Business associated with them and symbolized by them (the "**Assigned Marks**"); and

(b) the copyright registrations and applications for copyright registration, together with the copyrights in the works that are the subject of those registrations and applications (the "**Assigned Copyrights**").

1.2 **Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 **Assignment.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee hereby accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

2.2 **Included Rights.** The assignment in Section 2.1 includes, to the extent relating to the Assigned IP:

(a) all renewals, extensions, restorations and reversions of any of the Assigned IP;

(b) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable in respect of any of the Assigned IP;

(c) all rights to sue for and collect damages for, and to obtain injunctive and other relief for, any past, present or future infringement, dilution, misappropriation or other violation of any of the Assigned IP, and to retain all amounts recovered; and

(d) all rights of priority and protection of interests in any of the Assigned IP under the laws of any jurisdiction.

2.3 **Intent-to-Use Applications.** For each intent-to-use trademark application included in the Assigned Marks, Assignor assigns that application together with the portion of the Business to which the mark pertains, which is ongoing and existing, in accordance with 15 U.S.C. § 1060(a)(1).

## 3. Recordation and Further Assurances

3.1 **Recordation.** Assignor authorizes Assignee to record this Agreement, or a short-form confirmation of it, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, the European Union Intellectual Property Office and any other Governmental Authority. Assignor requests each such Governmental Authority to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned IP. Assignee shall bear the costs of recordation.

3.2 **Further Assurances.** At Assignee's reasonable request and without further consideration, Assignor shall promptly execute and deliver such further instruments, declarations and documents, and take such further action, as Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. This obligation is consistent with Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

3.3 **Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee, and any officer of Assignee, as Assignor's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file in Assignor's name any document described in Section 3.2 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

3.4 **Files and Records.** On the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, or to counsel designated by Assignee, the prosecution files, registration certificates, docketing records and correspondence relating to the Assigned IP in Assignor's possession or control.

3.5 **Pending Deadlines.** Assignor shall notify Assignee in writing on the Effective Date of any filing, response, maintenance or renewal deadline for any of the Assigned IP that falls within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date.

## 4. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

4.1 **Purchase Agreement.** This Agreement is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnities of the parties relating to the Assigned IP are set forth in the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. General Provisions

5.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Matters of the validity, registration and recordation of any Assigned IP are governed by the Laws of the jurisdiction in which that Assigned IP is registered or applied for.

5.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware.

5.3 **Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

5.4 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee.

5.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

5.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
