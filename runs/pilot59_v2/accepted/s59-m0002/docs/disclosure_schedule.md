# DISCLOSURE SCHEDULE

**to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026, by and between Treikaith Interactive, Inc. ("Buyer") and Daibaind Games, LLC ("Seller")**

## 1. Preliminary Statements

1.1 This Disclosure Schedule is delivered by Seller to Buyer under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026, between Buyer and Seller (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 Section and schedule numbers in this Disclosure Schedule correspond to the sections of the Agreement. A matter disclosed in one schedule is deemed disclosed for any other schedule only where the agreement itself names that other schedule, or where the matter is cross-referenced in it.

1.3 Some matters are included for information only. Their inclusion does not mean the matter is material, or that it is outside the ordinary course of business. It is also not an admission of liability to any third party.

1.4 This Disclosure Schedule may be supplemented or amended under Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Supplement No. 1 is attached as Part 3.

### Schedule 2.4: Excluded Liabilities

The following matters are Excluded Liabilities under Section 2.4(d) of the Agreement:

1. *Calloway, et al. v. Daibaind Games, LLC*, Case No. 1:26-cv-00814, United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, Austin Division. This is a putative consumer class action filed April 22, 2026. It alleges that the randomized "Ember Cache" in-game purchase mechanics in Emberfall Saga: Ashen Crown and Emberfall Saga: Tidebreaker violate the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices–Consumer Protection Act and the consumer protection laws of other states. It also asserts claims for unjust enrichment. Plaintiffs seek restitution, statutory damages, attorneys' fees and injunctive relief on behalf of a putative nationwide class of purchasers. This matter, together with any related or successor action arising out of the same or similar facts, is the "**Pre-Closing Player Litigation**." Seller's motion to dismiss is pending.
2. All liabilities of Seller under its revolving credit facility with Brazos Commerce Bank. That facility will be repaid in full, and the related liens released, at or before the Closing.
3. All accrued but unpaid 2026 bonuses of employees of the Business attributable to periods before the Closing.

4.2 *Schedule 4.7 (Litigation).* Schedule 4.7 is supplemented by adding the following new Item 3:

> 3. On September 28, 2026, Seller received a demand letter from counsel to Orvenna Sound Collective, a music production collective based in Portland, Oregon. The letter asserts that Orvenna Sound Collective owns the copyright in certain compositions and sound recordings contained in the Emberfall Saga soundtrack, including the main theme and the in-game combat suite. It asserts this on the basis of an alleged 2019 co-writing arrangement with members of Hallorin Bridge. The letter alleges that the Emberfall Saga titles infringe those copyrights. It demands that Seller cease distribution of the affected tracks. It also demands an accounting and payment of a share of revenue, and states that Orvenna Sound Collective will seek preliminary and permanent injunctive relief if the demand is not met within thirty (30) days. Seller disputes the claim and has notified Hallorin Bridge under the Music Composition and License Agreement.

4.3 *Schedule 4.12(c) (Infringement Claims).* Schedule 4.12(c) is amended by deleting "None" and replacing it with: "The claim of Orvenna Sound Collective described in Schedule 4.7, Item 3."

4.4 *Schedule 4.9 (Material Contracts).* Schedule 4.9, Item 8, is supplemented by adding: "The ownership of portions of the score is the subject of the claim of Orvenna Sound Collective described in Schedule 4.7, Item 3."
