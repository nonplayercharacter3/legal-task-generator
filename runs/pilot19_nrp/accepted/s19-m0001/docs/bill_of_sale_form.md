# FORM OF BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by [NAME OF SELLER SUBSIDIARY], a [●] [corporation/limited liability company] ("**Transferor**"), in favor of **Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. Background

1.1 Transferor is a Subsidiary of **Gliteis Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**").

1.2 Seller and **Voutuv Infrastructure Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller will sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

1.3 Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause Transferor, before the Closing, to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to transfer the Transferred Test Assets to the Company free and clear of all Encumbrances.

1.4 Transferor executes and delivers this Bill of Sale in satisfaction of that obligation.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Closing," "Encumbrance," "Intellectual Property," "Subsidiary" and "Transferred Test Assets."

2.2 For purposes of this Bill of Sale, the Transferred Test Assets consist of the following (collectively, the "**Transferred Assets**"):

(a) the thermal-test equipment listed in Part 1 of Schedule A, including all test rigs, chilled-water and liquid-loop test stands, flow benches, environmental chambers, sensors, data-acquisition hardware, spare parts, tooling and accessories used with that equipment (the "**Test Equipment**");

(b) the Intellectual Property listed in Part 2 of Schedule A, together with all test protocols, calibration records, validation data, software, firmware, source code, drawings, specifications and know-how embodied in or relating to the Test Equipment or the thermal testing of the Company's cooling systems (the "**Transferred IP**");

(c) all manufacturer and vendor warranties, service contracts and maintenance records relating to the Test Equipment, to the extent transferable; and

(d) all rights to sue for and to collect damages for any past, present or future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of the Transferred IP, and all income, royalties and payments due or payable with respect to the Transferred IP.

## 3. Conveyance

3.1 **Transfer.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Transferor acknowledges, Transferor hereby sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances, to have and to hold forever.

3.2 **Possession.** Transferor shall deliver physical possession of the Test Equipment to the Company at the Company's Austin, Texas facility, or at such other location as the Company designates, on or before the Effective Date. Transferor shall deliver to the Company, on or before the Effective Date, all tangible embodiments of the Transferred IP, including electronic copies of all source code, test data and documentation in Transferor's possession or control.

3.3 **No Assumed Liabilities.** The Company does not assume, and Transferor retains, all liabilities and obligations of Transferor and its Affiliates arising from the ownership, operation or use of the Transferred Assets before the Effective Date.

## 4. Intellectual Property Recordation

4.1 To the extent any Transferred IP is registered or the subject of a pending application with any Governmental Authority, Transferor shall, on or promptly after the Effective Date, execute and deliver short-form assignments in recordable form in favor of the Company.

4.2 Transferor authorizes the Company to record this Bill of Sale or any such short-form assignment with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office and any other Governmental Authority.

## 5. Power of Attorney

5.1 Transferor irrevocably appoints the Company, and its successors and assigns, as its true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Transferor but for the benefit of the Company, to:

(a) demand and receive any of the Transferred Assets;

(b) give receipts and releases for the Transferred Assets; and

(c) institute and prosecute proceedings that the Company considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Transferred Assets.

5.2 This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 6. Further Assurances

6.1 From time to time after the Effective Date, at the Company's request and without further consideration, Transferor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to:

(a) execute and deliver such further instruments of sale, conveyance, transfer and assignment; and

(b) take such other actions,

in each case as the Company may reasonably request to vest more effectively in the Company title to the Transferred Assets and to put the Company in possession of them.

6.2 If, after the Effective Date, Transferor or any of its Affiliates receives any payment or holds any asset that forms part of the Transferred Assets, it shall hold that payment or asset in trust for the Company and promptly remit or deliver it to the Company.

## 7. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

7.1 This Bill of Sale is delivered under Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

7.2 This Bill of Sale is an Ancillary Agreement for purposes of the Purchase Agreement.

7.3 Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies any representation, warranty, covenant or agreement of Seller in the Purchase Agreement, including the representations in Section 4.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. Miscellaneous

8.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Transferor and its successors and assigns. It benefits the Company and its successors and assigns.

8.2 **Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Bill of Sale and may enforce it.

8.3 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

8.4 **Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought in the courts specified in Section 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement. Section 11.10 applies to this Bill of Sale as if set out in full in it.

8.5 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. A signature delivered electronically is deemed an original.
