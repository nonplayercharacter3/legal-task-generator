# FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT

This FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Shipper**"), and **Ostrander Freight Logistics, Inc.**, a Texas corporation ("**Carrier**"). Shipper and Carrier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Background

1.1 Shipper designs, manufactures, installs and services liquid and chilled-water cooling systems for data centers. It ships finished units, components and spare parts from its facility in Austin, Texas to customer sites and warehouses in the United States.

1.2 Carrier is a motor carrier and freight logistics provider. It holds operating authority issued by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration ("**FMCSA**").

1.3 Shipper wishes to engage Carrier to transport and handle its freight on the terms of this Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement:

"**Accessorial Charges**" means the charges for services other than line-haul transportation listed in Part 2 of Schedule A.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday in the United States.

"**Cargo**" means the goods tendered by Shipper to Carrier for transportation under this Agreement.

"**Loss or Damage**" means physical loss of, damage to, or delay in delivery of Cargo while it is in Carrier's possession, custody or control.

"**Rates**" means the rates and charges set forth in Schedule A, as adjusted under Section 5.4.

"**Shipment**" means Cargo moving under a single bill of lading from one origin to one destination.

"**Shipping Order**" means a request for transportation issued by Shipper under Section 3.2.

## 3. Services

3.1 **Scope.** Carrier shall provide the following services for Shipper (the "**Services**"):

(a) full-truckload and less-than-truckload transportation of Cargo by motor carrier within the contiguous United States;

(b) dedicated air-ride equipment for crated cooling distribution units, chillers and other sensitive equipment;

(c) liftgate, inside delivery, white-glove placement and other accessorial services listed in Part 2 of Schedule A; and

(d) shipment tracking and proof-of-delivery reporting through Carrier's online portal.

3.2 **Shipping Orders.** Shipper shall request Services by issuing Shipping Orders through Carrier's portal, by email or by telephone confirmed in writing. Each Shipping Order shall state the origin, destination, requested pickup and delivery dates, and the description, weight, dimensions and number of pieces of the Cargo. Carrier shall accept or reject each Shipping Order within four (4) business hours. Carrier is deemed to have accepted any Shipping Order it does not reject within that time.

3.3 **No Minimum Volume.** Shipper does not commit to tender any minimum volume of Cargo. Carrier does not commit to accept any particular volume of Shipping Orders, except as set out in Section 3.2.

3.4 **Equipment.** Carrier shall furnish clean, dry and well-maintained equipment suitable for the Cargo described in each Shipping Order. Equipment for crated cooling units shall be air-ride and fitted with load bars or straps adequate to secure the Cargo.

3.5 **On-Time Performance.** Carrier shall use reasonable efforts to pick up and deliver each Shipment on the dates stated in the accepted Shipping Order. Carrier shall notify Shipper promptly of any anticipated delay. Carrier shall report its on-time pickup and delivery performance to Shipper each calendar quarter.

## 4. Term

4.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date. It continues for three (3) years (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 11.

4.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

## 5. Rates and Payment

5.1 **Rates.** Shipper shall pay Carrier for the Services at the Rates. Fuel surcharges shall be calculated weekly under Part 3 of Schedule A.

5.2 **Invoices.** Carrier shall invoice each Shipment within thirty (30) days after delivery. Each invoice shall reference the Shipping Order number and the bill of lading, and attach a signed proof of delivery.

5.3 **Payment.** Shipper shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Shipper may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Carrier in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve any disputed amount within thirty (30) days after the notice.

5.4 **Rate Adjustments.** Carrier may adjust the line-haul Rates once in each contract year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before that anniversary. No annual adjustment may exceed four percent (4%) of the line-haul Rates in effect immediately before it.

5.5 **Annual Spend.** The Parties estimate that Shipper's spend under this Agreement will be about $1,400,000 per contract year. This estimate is for planning purposes only and does not bind either Party.

5.6 **Taxes.** Rates are exclusive of any sales or similar taxes that may apply to the Services. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. Shipping Documents

6.1 **Bills of Lading.** Each Shipment shall move under a bill of lading or electronic shipping document issued by Carrier. The terms of this Agreement apply to each Shipment. Any term printed on a bill of lading, delivery receipt or other shipping document that adds to or differs from this Agreement has no effect.

6.2 **Receipts.** Carrier shall obtain the signature of the consignee, with the date and time of delivery and any exceptions noted, at each delivery. Carrier shall make proof of delivery available on its portal within one (1) Business Day after delivery.

## 7. Cargo Liability

7.1 **Liability Standard.** Carrier is liable for Loss or Damage to Cargo under 49 U.S.C. § 14706 (the Carmack Amendment), subject to Section 7.2.

7.2 **Limit.** Carrier's liability for Loss or Damage to any Shipment shall not exceed the lesser of (a) the actual invoice value of the lost or damaged Cargo, and (b) $250,000 per Shipment. Shipper may declare a higher value for any Shipment in its Shipping Order. If Shipper does so, and Carrier accepts that Shipping Order, the limit for that Shipment is the declared value, and Shipper shall pay the excess value charge set forth in Part 2 of Schedule A.

7.3 **Claims.** Shipper shall file any claim for Loss or Damage in writing within nine (9) months after delivery or, for a Shipment not delivered, within nine (9) months after the date delivery should reasonably have occurred. Carrier shall acknowledge each claim within thirty (30) days after receipt and shall pay, decline or make a firm settlement offer within one hundred twenty (120) days after receipt.

7.4 **Salvage.** Carrier shall not sell or dispose of any damaged Cargo without Shipper's prior written consent. Shipper may require Carrier to return damaged Cargo to Shipper. Any salvage value Shipper receives shall be credited against Carrier's liability.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 During the Term and for one (1) year afterward, Carrier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) motor carrier cargo insurance of at least $250,000 per Shipment;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit per occurrence, meeting FMCSA requirements;

(c) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate;

(d) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $5,000,000; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000.

8.2 Carrier shall name Shipper as an additional insured under the policies in Sections 8.1(b), 8.1(c) and 8.1(d). Carrier shall deliver certificates of insurance to Shipper on request and shall give Shipper at least thirty (30) days' notice before any of these policies is cancelled or materially reduced.

## 9. Compliance and Indemnity

9.1 **Compliance.** Carrier shall maintain all operating authority, registrations, permits and licenses required to provide the Services. Carrier shall comply with all federal, state and local laws and regulations that apply to the Services, including FMCSA safety, hours-of-service and drug and alcohol testing requirements. Carrier shall keep a safety rating that is not "Unsatisfactory."

9.2 **Hazardous Materials.** Shipper shall not tender hazardous materials, including any refrigerant or coolant regulated as a hazardous material, unless the Shipping Order identifies them and they are classified, packaged, marked and labeled as required by Law. Carrier may reject any Shipping Order that includes hazardous materials it is not authorized to carry.

9.3 **Indemnity by Carrier.** Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Shipper and its officers, directors and employees from all claims, losses, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury, death or damage to property (other than Cargo) to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Carrier, its employees or its subcontractors, or by Carrier's breach of Section 9.1. Carrier's liability for Cargo is governed by Section 7.

9.4 **Indemnity by Shipper.** Shipper shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Carrier and its officers, directors and employees from all claims, losses, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury, death or damage to property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Shipper or its employees, or by Shipper's breach of Section 9.2.

9.5 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall notify the indemnifying Party promptly of any claim, allow it to control the defense and settlement, and cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes an obligation on the indemnified Party without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

9.6 **Consequential Damages.** Except for obligations under Sections 9.3 and 9.4 and liability for breach of Section 10, neither Party is liable for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party received under this Agreement, including Rates, customer names and delivery locations, and shall use that information only to perform this Agreement.

10.2 Section 10.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, that the receiving Party already knew without a duty of confidence, or that a Party is required to disclose by Law. A Party may disclose this Agreement in confidence to its actual or prospective investors, acquirers, lenders and professional advisors.

10.3 This Section 10 survives for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## 11. Termination

11.1 **Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice to the other Party.

11.2 **Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

11.3 **Immediate Termination.** Shipper may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Carrier loses its operating authority, receives an "Unsatisfactory" safety rating, or fails to maintain the insurance required by Section 8.

11.4 **Effect.** Termination does not affect any Shipment tendered and accepted before the termination date, which Carrier shall complete on the terms of this Agreement. Shipper shall pay for all Services performed up to the termination date. Sections 5, 7, 9, 10, 11.4 and 12 survive termination.

## 12. General

12.1 **Independent Contractor.** Carrier is an independent contractor. Carrier has exclusive control over its drivers, equipment and operations. Carrier's personnel are not employees of Shipper.

12.2 **Subcontracting.** Carrier shall not broker, interline or subcontract any Shipment to another carrier without Shipper's prior written consent. Carrier remains responsible for any approved subcontractor as if it were its own performance.

12.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. However, either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets. A change in the ownership or control of a Party is not an assignment for purposes of this Section 12.3 and does not require the consent of the other Party. In this Section 12.3, "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

12.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. Notices are deemed given when delivered by hand, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the address set forth on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

12.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disaster, road closures, war, terrorism or acts of government. The affected Party must notify the other Party promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Carrier from performing for more than ten (10) days, Shipper may use other carriers for the affected Shipments during that period.

12.6 **Waiver of Rights.** To the extent permitted by 49 U.S.C. § 14101(b), the Parties expressly waive all rights and remedies under 49 U.S.C. Subtitle IV, Part B that conflict with this Agreement, except for the registration, insurance and safety fitness requirements and the Carmack Amendment as modified by Section 7.

12.7 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by applicable federal law and, to the extent not governed by federal law, by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

12.8 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

12.9 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

12.10 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

12.11 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
