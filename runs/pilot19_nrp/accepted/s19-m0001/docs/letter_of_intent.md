**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Voutuv Infrastructure Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire from Gliteis Industrial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). We refer to this acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

Except as provided in Section 9, this Letter is not binding on either party. It is intended only as a basis for negotiating a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and related ancillary agreements.

## 1. Transaction Structure

1.1 Buyer will purchase all of the Shares from Seller. As a result, the Company will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer.

1.2 The Company designs, manufactures, installs and services liquid and chilled-water cooling systems for hyperscale data centers (the "**Business**").

1.3 Before closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"), Seller will cause its subsidiary that holds the thermal-test equipment and related intellectual property used in the Business to transfer those assets to the Company under a bill of sale. Those assets will be transferred free and clear of all liens and encumbrances.

## 2. Purchase Price

2.1 The aggregate purchase price for the Shares will be $214,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price assumes that the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of working capital.

2.2 The Purchase Price will be payable in cash at Closing. It will be subject to a customary post-closing working capital adjustment against a target to be agreed on the basis of Buyer's financial due diligence.

2.3 A portion of the Purchase Price, in an amount to be agreed, will be deposited in escrow at Closing to secure the working capital adjustment.

## 3. Representations and Warranties

3.1 The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties of Seller that are customary for a transaction of this type. These will include representations on:

(a) organization, authority and capitalization;

(b) financial statements;

(c) material contracts and customer and supplier relationships;

(d) product warranty;

(e) environmental matters, including the handling, recovery and disposal of refrigerants;

(f) intellectual property;

(g) litigation and compliance with laws;

(h) taxes; and

(i) brokers.

3.2 Representations on organization, authority, capitalization and brokers will be treated as fundamental representations.

## 4. Indemnification

4.1 **Survival.** The general representations and warranties of Seller will survive Closing for 18 months after Closing. Fundamental representations will survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

4.2 **Deductible.** Seller will not be liable for breaches of general representations until aggregate losses exceed a deductible equal to 0.5% of the Purchase Price. After that, Seller will be liable only for losses in excess of the deductible.

4.3 **Cap.** Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of general representations will be capped at 10% of the Purchase Price. Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of fundamental representations, breaches of covenants and pre-closing taxes will be capped at the Purchase Price.

4.4 **Fraud.** These limitations will not apply to fraud.

4.5 **Exclusive Remedy.** Indemnification will be the exclusive post-Closing remedy, subject to customary exceptions for fraud, equitable relief and the working capital adjustment.

## 5. Transition Services

5.1 Seller will provide transition services to the Company after Closing under a transition services agreement on terms to be negotiated. These services will include ERP, payroll and field-service dispatch.

5.2 The services will be provided for 9 months after the Closing Date. Pricing will be at Seller's cost, without mark-up.

## 6. Due Diligence

6.1 Buyer's proposal is subject to satisfactory completion of business, financial, legal, tax, environmental and technical due diligence.

6.2 Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the Company's facilities, books, records, contracts and management. This will include the Company's principal customer agreements and its facility in Austin, Texas.

## 7. Conditions to Closing

The Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) execution of the Definitive Agreement and related ancillary agreements satisfactory to both parties;

(b) expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended;

(c) receipt of required third-party consents to be identified in the Definitive Agreement;

(d) the absence of any material adverse effect on the Company; and

(e) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's investment committee.

## 8. Timing

The parties intend to use good-faith efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement by September 15, 2026. They intend to close as promptly as practicable after the closing conditions have been satisfied.

## 9. Binding Provisions

Only the provisions of this Section 9 are legally binding on the parties.

9.1 **Exclusivity.** For sixty (60) days after Seller countersigns this Letter, Seller will not, and will cause the Company and its respective representatives not to, do any of the following with any person other than Buyer:

(a) solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or any material portion of its assets or equity; or

(b) provide information to any person for the purpose described in paragraph (a).

9.2 **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of this Letter are subject to the confidentiality agreement between Buyer and Seller dated May 28, 2026, which remains in full force and effect.

9.3 **Expenses.** Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

9.4 **No Obligation.** Unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed, neither party will have any obligation with respect to the Transaction, except under this Section 9. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time by written notice. Upon such termination, Sections 9.2 through 9.6 will survive.

9.5 **Governing Law.** This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.6 **Counterparts.** This Letter may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
