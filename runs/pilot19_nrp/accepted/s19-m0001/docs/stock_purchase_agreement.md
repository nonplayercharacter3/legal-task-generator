# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026, by and between **Gliteis Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Voutuv Infrastructure Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures, installs and services liquid and chilled-water cooling systems for hyperscale data centers (the "**Business**").

C. Before the Closing, a Subsidiary of Seller will transfer to the Company certain thermal-test equipment and related Intellectual Property under the Bill of Sale.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances, for an aggregate purchase price of $214,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to an escrow account maintained by the escrow agent named in the escrow agreement, $2,000,000 (the "**Adjustment Escrow Amount**");

(b) on behalf of the Company, the amounts necessary to repay the indebtedness of the Company set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv); and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b), plus or minus the amount by which Seller's good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, delivered not less than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, exceeds or falls short of Target Working Capital.

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and remitted are treated as paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

**2.4 Closing Statement; Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) Within 30 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting detail.

(b) Seller has thirty (30) days after receipt of the Closing Statement to deliver to Buyer a written notice of disagreement specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item. If Seller does not deliver a notice of disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement is final and binding.

(c) If Seller timely delivers a notice of disagreement, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve the disputed items. Any items not resolved within that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"), acting as an expert and not an arbitrator, whose determination is final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the extent each prevails on the disputed items.

(d) If final Closing Working Capital exceeds the estimate used under Section 2.2(c), Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If final Closing Working Capital is less than that estimate, the shortfall shall be paid to Buyer from the Adjustment Escrow Amount and, to the extent the Adjustment Escrow Amount is insufficient, by Seller. Any payment under this Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination, and any remaining Adjustment Escrow Amount shall be released to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iii) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer that the Bill of Sale has been executed and delivered and that the Transferred Test Assets have been transferred to the Company;

(iv) customary payoff letters for all indebtedness of the Company for borrowed money;

(v) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(vi) the third-party consents listed in Schedule 7.2(c);

(vii) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(viii) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller the payments required by Section 2.2, the Transition Services Agreement duly executed by the Company, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Subject to this Article IX, from and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective representatives from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement and (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

(c) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) The limitations in this Section 9.4 do not apply to Losses arising from fraud.
