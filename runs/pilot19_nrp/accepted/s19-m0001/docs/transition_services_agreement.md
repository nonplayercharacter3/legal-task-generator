# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Gliteis Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Kelthorn Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Provider and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Background

1.1 Provider and **Voutuv Infrastructure Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Provider is selling to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

1.2 Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided the Company with enterprise resource planning, payroll and field-service dispatch support. After the Closing, the Company will need these services while it moves the relevant functions to its own systems or to systems of Buyer.

1.3 The Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to deliver this Agreement at the Closing. This Agreement is the "Transition Services Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is an Ancillary Agreement for purposes of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Business," "Business Day," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Law" and "Person."

2.2 In this Agreement:

"**Company Data**" means all data and information of or relating to the Company, its employees, customers, suppliers or the Business that is processed, stored or generated in the course of providing the Services, including payroll records, financial records, work orders and service tickets.

"**Migration**" means the transfer of the functions supported by the Services, and of the related Company Data, from Provider's systems to systems operated by or for the Company.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 4.3.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Closing Date. Unless terminated earlier under Section 10, it ends 9 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**").

3.2 **Service Periods.** Each Service shall be provided for the period stated for it in Schedule A. If no period is stated, the Service shall be provided for the full Term. No Service period may extend beyond the Term unless the Parties agree in writing.

3.3 **Extension.** The Company may ask Provider to extend any Service for up to three (3) additional months by written notice given at least thirty (30) days before the end of the applicable Service period. Provider shall consider any such request in good faith and shall not unreasonably withhold its agreement. Any extended Service shall be provided at the Service Fees then in effect.

## 4. Services

4.1 **Provision of Services.** Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to the Company during the applicable Service period.

4.2 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall provide each Service:

(a) in substantially the same manner, scope, priority, volume and quality as Provider or its Affiliates provided that Service to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date;

(b) with at least the same degree of care that Provider uses in providing similar services to its own businesses; and

(c) in compliance with applicable Law.

4.3 **Additional Services.** If, within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, the Company identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company before the Closing and that is not described in Schedule A, the Company may request that Provider provide that service (an "**Additional Service**"). Provider shall provide that Additional Service on the terms of this Agreement at a fee calculated under Section 5.1. The Parties shall document each Additional Service in a written supplement to Schedule A.

4.4 **Subcontractors.** Provider may use third-party subcontractors to provide any Service, but only to the extent it used those subcontractors for that service before the Closing or with the Company's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the performance of each subcontractor as if it were its own.

4.5 **Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any consent of a third-party licensor or vendor required for Provider to provide the Services to the Company after the Closing. Provider shall bear the cost of obtaining any such consent. If a required consent cannot be obtained, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to put in place a reasonable alternative arrangement that provides the Company with substantially equivalent service.

4.6 **Company Cooperation.** The Company shall provide Provider with information, access and assistance reasonably necessary for Provider to perform the Services. Provider is excused from performance to the extent the Company's failure to provide that cooperation prevents performance, provided Provider promptly notifies the Company of the failure.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Service Fees.** The Company shall pay Provider for each Service the fees stated in Schedule A (the "**Service Fees**"). The Service Fees are equal to Provider's actual cost of providing the Services, without mark-up. Cost includes allocated personnel costs, third-party charges passed through without mark-up, and a reasonable allocation of system and license costs, in each case calculated consistently with Provider's allocation to the Company before the Closing.

5.2 **Invoices.** Provider shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Fees by Service and include reasonable supporting detail for any pass-through charges.

5.3 **Payment.** The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. The Company may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, if it notifies Provider of the dispute in writing and in reasonable detail before the payment due date. The Parties shall resolve disputed amounts under Section 11.

5.4 **Taxes.** Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar Taxes. The Company shall pay any such Taxes properly invoiced by Provider, other than Taxes on Provider's income.

5.5 **Partial Months.** Service Fees for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

## 6. Governance

6.1 **Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall appoint a representative to coordinate the Services (each, a "**Service Coordinator**") and shall notify the other Party of that person's name and contact details within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Date. A Party may replace its Service Coordinator by notice to the other Party.

6.2 **Meetings.** The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least every two weeks during the Term to review the performance of the Services, the status of the Migration and any open issues.

6.3 **Escalation.** Any issue that the Service Coordinators cannot resolve within five (5) Business Days shall be escalated under Section 11.

## 7. Migration

7.1 **Migration Plan.** Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, the Parties shall agree in good faith a written plan for the Migration of each Service. The plan shall include milestones, responsibilities and a target cut-over date for each Service.

7.2 **Migration Assistance.** Provider shall provide the Company with the cooperation and assistance reasonably requested to complete the Migration, including:

(a) the extraction and delivery of Company Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format;

(b) reasonable access to Provider personnel who are knowledgeable about the systems and processes used to provide the Services; and

(c) parallel running of systems for a reasonable period before cut-over.

Migration assistance shall be charged at cost under Section 5.1.

7.3 **Return of Company Data.** At the end of each Service period, Provider shall deliver to the Company all Company Data relating to that Service. Within sixty (60) days after the end of the Term, Provider shall delete all remaining Company Data from its systems, except to the extent retention is required by Law, and shall certify that deletion in writing on request.

## 8. Data, Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

8.1 **Ownership of Company Data.** As between the Parties, the Company owns all Company Data. Provider shall use Company Data only to provide the Services.

8.2 **Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Company Data that are no less protective than those Provider applies to its own data of a similar nature, and that comply with applicable data protection Law. Provider shall notify the Company without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Company Data.

8.3 **Confidentiality.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all confidential information of the other Party received under this Agreement and use it only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. This Section 8.3 does not apply to information that becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, or that a Party is required to disclose by Law. This Section 8.3 is in addition to Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

8.4 **Intellectual Property.** Each Party retains all rights in its own Intellectual Property. Provider grants the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Term to use Provider's systems, software and materials to the extent necessary to receive the Services. Any reports, deliverables or work product created specifically for the Company under this Agreement belong to the Company.

## 9. Liability

9.1 **Warranty Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, Provider makes no warranty regarding the Services, express or implied.

9.2 **Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement, except to the extent arising from a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, a breach of Section 8, or amounts payable to a third party.

9.3 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement, except for liability arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud or a breach of Section 8.

9.4 **Indemnity.** Provider shall indemnify the Company against Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services. The Company shall indemnify Provider against Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by the Company's gross negligence or willful misconduct in receiving the Services.

## 10. Termination

10.1 **Termination of Services by the Company.** The Company may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. If a Service depends on another Service, Provider shall identify that dependency within five (5) Business Days after receiving the notice, and the Parties shall agree in good faith how to address it.

10.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it. Provider may not terminate any Service for non-payment unless the undisputed amount remains unpaid for thirty (30) days after the Company receives a written notice of non-payment.

10.3 **Effect of Termination.** On termination of any Service, the Company shall pay the Service Fees accrued for that Service up to the effective date of termination. Sections 7.3, 8, 9, 10.3, 11 and 12 survive any termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 11. Dispute Escalation

11.1 Either Party may refer a dispute arising under this Agreement to senior executives of each Party by written notice. The executives shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after the notice and attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute.

11.2 If the dispute is not resolved within twenty (20) Business Days after the notice, either Party may bring proceedings under Section 12.2.

11.3 Nothing in this Section 11 prevents either Party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

## 12. General

12.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in writing in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages of this Agreement.

12.2 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims, disputes and causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Harris County, Texas, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, and agrees not to commence any such action in any other court. Sections 11.9 and 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement do not apply to this Agreement, and this Section 12.2 expressly provides otherwise for purposes of Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

12.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE SERVICES.

12.4 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties. Personnel of Provider who perform the Services remain employees of Provider.

12.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing a Service for more than fifteen (15) days, the Company may terminate that Service immediately by notice, and the Service Fees for that Service shall be suspended during the period of non-performance.

12.6 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, the Company may assign this Agreement to Buyer or to any Affiliate of Buyer without consent.

12.7 **Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce it.

12.8 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

12.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

12.10 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
