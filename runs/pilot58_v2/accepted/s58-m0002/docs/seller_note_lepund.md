# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**THIS NOTE AND THE OBLIGATIONS EVIDENCED BY IT ARE SUBORDINATED TO THE PRIOR PAYMENT IN FULL OF SENIOR INDEBTEDNESS (AS DEFINED BELOW) TO THE EXTENT AND IN THE MANNER SET FORTH IN SECTION 6. THIS NOTE HAS NOT BEEN REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED, OR ANY STATE SECURITIES LAW. IT MAY NOT BE SOLD, PLEDGED OR OTHERWISE TRANSFERRED EXCEPT AS PERMITTED BY SECTION 9.**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal sum of Six Million Dollars ($6,000,000), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

Maker issues this Note under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of November 6, 2026, between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Maker is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal Amount; Maturity Date

1.1 **Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). It represents the deferred portion of the Purchase Price payable under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Issue Date.** This Note is issued on, and dated as of, the Closing Date (the "**Issue Date**").

1.3 **Maturity Date.** The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts then owing under this Note, shall be due and payable in full on the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Issue Date (the Closing Date) (the "**Maturity Date**"). If that date is not a Business Day, payment shall be made on the next succeeding Business Day.

## 2. Interest

2.1 **Rate.** The unpaid Principal Amount shall bear interest from the Issue Date until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum.

2.2 **Computation.** Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Interest shall accrue but shall not compound.

2.3 **Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest shall be payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date occurring at least sixty (60) days after the Issue Date. All remaining accrued and unpaid interest shall be payable on the Maturity Date.

2.4 **Default Interest.** While an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid Principal Amount and any overdue interest shall bear interest at a rate of two percent (2.0%) per annum above the rate in Section 2.1.

2.5 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest payable under this Note shall exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. Payments

3.1 **Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Holder at least five (5) Business Days before the relevant payment date.

3.2 **Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the unpaid Principal Amount.

3.3 **Business Day.** "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

## 4. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five (5) Business Days' written notice to Holder. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by all accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Amounts prepaid may not be reborrowed.

## 5. Right of Set-Off

5.1 **Set-Off.** Maker may set off against any amount then due or thereafter becoming due under this Note (whether principal or interest) any amount to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, as contemplated by Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.2 **Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any set-off under Section 5.1. The notice shall state the amount set off and describe in reasonable detail the claim to which it relates.

5.3 **Pending Claims.** If, on any payment date, a Buyer Indemnitee has delivered a claim notice under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement that has not been finally resolved, Maker may withhold from the payment then due an amount equal to Maker's good faith estimate of the Losses claimed. Maker shall pay the amount withheld, or the portion of it not owed to the Buyer Indemnitee, within ten (10) Business Days after final resolution of the claim, together with interest at the rate in Section 2.1 from the original payment date.

5.4 **Effect.** Any amount set off under this Section 5 shall be treated as a payment of this Note in that amount and shall not constitute a failure to pay or an Event of Default.

## 6. Subordination

6.1 **Senior Indebtedness.** "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries for borrowed money owed to any bank, commercial lender or other institutional lender under any credit facility in effect from time to time, including principal, interest (including interest accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding), fees, expenses and indemnities.

6.2 **Subordination.** The payment of the Principal Amount and interest on this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness.

6.3 **Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or other event of default under the Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing and the holder of the Senior Indebtedness has given Maker and Holder written notice of it, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, regularly scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note.

6.4 **Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding of Maker, the holders of Senior Indebtedness shall be entitled to receive payment in full in cash before Holder receives any payment on this Note.

6.5 **Turnover.** Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 6 shall be held in trust for, and promptly paid over to, the holders of Senior Indebtedness.

6.6 **Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall execute such subordination agreement as any holder of Senior Indebtedness may reasonably request, provided that its terms are no less favorable to Holder in any material respect than this Section 6.

## 7. Events of Default; Remedies

7.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after the date due, other than an amount set off or withheld in accordance with Section 5;

(b) Maker commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its assets; or

(c) an involuntary case is commenced against Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

7.2 **Remedies.** Subject to Section 6, upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a), Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued and unpaid interest immediately due and payable. Upon an Event of Default under Section 7.1(b) or 7.1(c), those amounts shall automatically become immediately due and payable without notice.

7.3 **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. Waivers

Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note shall operate as a waiver of that right. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by Holder.

## 9. Transfer

Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker. Maker may assign its obligations under this Note to any Affiliate that assumes them in writing, provided that Maker remains liable for them. Any purported transfer in violation of this Section 9 shall be void. Any permitted transferee takes this Note subject to Sections 5 and 6.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

10.2 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

10.3 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Note shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Maker and Holder each submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware.

10.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS NOTE.

10.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

10.6 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms upon receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from Holder.

10.7 **Successors.** This Note shall bind Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and shall benefit Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.
