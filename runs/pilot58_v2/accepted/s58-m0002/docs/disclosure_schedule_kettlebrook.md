## 9. Schedule 4.17 – Environmental Matters

### Part A – Disclosed Environmental Matters

The following matters are disclosed against Section 4.17 of the Agreement (the "**Disclosed Environmental Matters**"):

1. **Ridgeview Farm – Groundwater Nitrate Contamination.**

   (a) Routine monitoring of the on-site drinking water wells at Ridgeview Farm in 2025 found nitrate-nitrogen concentrations above the federal maximum contaminant level of 10 mg/L.

   (b) The Company engaged Champlain Environmental Consultants, LLC to perform a Phase I and a Phase II environmental site assessment of Ridgeview Farm.

   (c) The Phase II Environmental Site Assessment Report dated August 18, 2026 (the "**Ridgeview Phase II Report**") found groundwater nitrate concentrations of 14 to 31 mg/L across a plume extending beneath the main barn complex, the former heifer lot and the eastern crop fields. The report attributes the plume primarily to historic manure application and the former unlined heifer-lot runoff area.

   (d) The Ridgeview Phase II Report estimates the cost of remediation and long-term monitoring at between $3,400,000 and $7,800,000. The range depends on the remedial approach the regulator requires.

   (e) The Vermont Agency of Natural Resources, Department of Environmental Conservation, issued a Notice of Groundwater Exceedance dated September 29, 2026, and has asked the Company to submit a corrective action work plan by January 31, 2027.

   (f) The Company has supplied bottled water to the two (2) farm employee residences served by the affected wells since October 2025.

   (g) The Ridgeview Phase II Report and the Notice of Groundwater Exceedance are posted in the Data Room at folder 4.17.A.1.

2. **Ashgrove Farm – Nutrient Management Plan Update.**

   (a) The New Hampshire Department of Agriculture, Markets and Food sent the Company a letter dated May 12, 2026 asking it to update the Ashgrove Farm nutrient management plan to reflect an expanded herd size.

   (b) The updated plan was submitted on June 30, 2026 and approved on August 14, 2026. No penalty was assessed.

3. **Plant – Wastewater Pretreatment Permit.** In March 2026 the Plant exceeded its biochemical oxygen demand limit under Middlebury Industrial Pretreatment Permit No. IP-022 on one occasion. The Company paid a $2,500 surcharge and installed an additional equalization tank in April 2026. No further exceedances have occurred.

### Part B – Remediation Matters

1. **Hollis Farm – Decommissioned Manure Storage Pond.**

   (a) An earthen manure storage pond at Hollis Farm, with a capacity of approximately 1.2 million gallons, was taken out of service in 2023 and replaced with a concrete-lined storage structure.

   (b) Closure of the former pond has not been completed. Closure requires sludge removal, liner soil sampling and backfilling under the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation CAFO General Permit and the NRCS Conservation Practice Standard 360 (Waste Facility Closure).

   (c) A closure plan prepared by Hudson Valley Agronomic Engineering, PC, dated February 3, 2026, estimates the closure cost at $640,000.

   (d) The closure plan is posted in the Data Room at folder 4.17.B.1.

### Part C – Environmental Reports Made Available

2. Ridgeview Phase II Report, dated August 18, 2026.
3. Phase I Environmental Site Assessment, Hollis Farm, Hudson Valley Agronomic Engineering, PC, dated April 2, 2026.
