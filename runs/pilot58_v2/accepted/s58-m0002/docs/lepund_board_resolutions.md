# LEPUND CREAMERY HOLDINGS, INC.

## RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Adopted at a Special Meeting Held November 4, 2026**

---

## 1. Meeting and Quorum

1.1 A special meeting of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), was held on November 4, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time. Directors attended in person at the Corporation's offices and by videoconference. Every participant could hear and speak with every other participant.

1.2 Notice of the meeting was given to each director in accordance with the Corporation's Bylaws. Each director present waived any further notice.

1.3 A majority of the directors then in office were present, which constitutes a quorum under the Bylaws. The Chair of the Board presided and the Secretary of the Corporation recorded the proceedings.

1.4 Tobias Brennan, Chief Financial Officer, and representatives of Harlow Stevens LLP, counsel to the Corporation, attended at the Board's invitation.

## 2. Background

2.1 Management and counsel presented the proposed acquisition by the Corporation of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Seller**") (the "**Acquisition**").

2.2 The Company operates three certified-organic dairy farms, Ridgeview Farm, Hollis Farm and Ashgrove Farm. It also operates a fluid milk and yogurt processing facility in Middlebury, Vermont, together with a farmstead cheese production line.

2.3 The Board has reviewed the following materials, copies of which were provided to each director before the meeting:

(a) the letter of intent between the Corporation and the Seller dated September 14, 2026;

(b) the substantially final draft of the Stock Purchase Agreement between the Seller and the Corporation (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), together with the draft Disclosure Schedule;

(c) the substantially final drafts of the Subordinated Promissory Note to be issued by the Corporation to the Seller (the "**Seller Note**") and the Transition Services Agreement between the Seller, as provider, and the Corporation, as recipient (the "**Transition Services Agreement**");

(d) the substantially final draft of the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement between Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC, as assignor, and the Company, as assignee (the "**IP Assignment Agreement**");

(e) management's summary of the business, financial, environmental, tax and organic certification due diligence on the Company; and

(f) management's financial model and funding plan for the Acquisition.

2.4 Management reported on the principal terms of the Acquisition, including the following:

(a) an aggregate purchase price of $46,200,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to a post-closing net working capital adjustment;

(b) payment of $40,200,000 of the Purchase Price in cash at closing, less the Company's estimated indebtedness, which will be repaid at closing;

(c) payment of the remaining $6,000,000 of the Purchase Price through the issuance of the Seller Note. The Seller Note will bear interest at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum, payable quarterly. It will mature eighteen (18) months after the closing date, will be subordinated to the Corporation's senior institutional indebtedness, and will be subject to the Corporation's right of set-off for indemnification claims under the Purchase Agreement;

(d) the indemnification, survival and limitation provisions of the Purchase Agreement as reflected in the draft presented;

(e) the provision by the Seller of milk hauling, organic certification support and producer payroll services under the Transition Services Agreement for a term of 9 months after the closing date; and

(f) the assignment to the Company, at or before closing, of trademarks, recipes, starter cultures and other intellectual property held by Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC under the IP Assignment Agreement.

2.5 Management reported that the cash portion of the Purchase Price will be funded from the Corporation's cash on hand and available borrowings.

2.6 After discussion, and after questions from the directors were answered by management and counsel, the Board determined that the Acquisition is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders.

## 3. Resolutions

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the Board unanimously adopted the following resolutions:

### 3.1 Approval of the Acquisition and the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Acquisition, on the terms described to the Board, is authorized and approved. The form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, are approved, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it may approve, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by that officer's execution of the Purchase Agreement.

### 3.2 Approval of the Seller Note

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation is authorized to issue the Seller Note to the Seller at closing in the principal amount of $6,000,000, substantially in the form presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it may approve. The Corporation is authorized to perform its obligations under the Seller Note, including the payment of principal and interest.

### 3.3 Approval of the Transition Services Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Transition Services Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, are approved, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it may approve. The Corporation is authorized to enter into and perform the Transition Services Agreement and to pay the fees under it.

### 3.4 Ancillary Documents

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to take all actions necessary to cause the Company, following closing, to accept and perform the IP Assignment Agreement. They are also authorized to execute and deliver all certificates, instruments, short-form assignments, payoff acknowledgments, funds flow memoranda and other documents contemplated by the Purchase Agreement or reasonably necessary to complete the Acquisition (together with the Purchase Agreement, the Seller Note and the Transition Services Agreement, the "**Transaction Documents**").

### 3.5 Funding and Payments

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price and all related fees and expenses, and to direct the wire transfer of funds in accordance with the Purchase Agreement. This authority includes the direct payment of the Company's indebtedness to its lender at closing. The Authorized Officers are further authorized to make any payments due under the post-closing working capital adjustment in Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

### 3.6 Post-Closing Matters

**RESOLVED**, that, effective upon closing, the Authorized Officers are authorized:

(a) to designate the directors and officers of the Company;

(b) to designate authorized signatories on the Company's bank accounts; and

(c) to give all notices to customers, governmental authorities and certifying agents required in connection with the change of control of the Company.

### 3.7 Authorized Officers

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Secretary of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Transaction Documents in the name and on behalf of the Corporation. Each Authorized Officer is also authorized to take any further action that the officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

### 3.8 Ratification

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer or agent of the Corporation in connection with the Acquisition are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects. This includes the execution of the letter of intent and the conduct of due diligence.

## 4. Adjournment

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:35 a.m. Eastern Time.
