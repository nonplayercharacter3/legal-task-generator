# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 6, 2026, by and between **Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement.

"**Assigned IP**" has the meaning given in the IP Assignment Agreement.

"**Business**" means the production, processing, marketing and sale of organic fluid milk, yogurt, butter and cheese, as conducted by the Company as of the date of this Agreement.

"**Cap**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.5(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Environmental Law**" means any applicable federal, state or local law, regulation, permit or order relating to pollution, the protection of the environment, natural resources, groundwater or surface water, or the generation, storage, handling, discharge or release of Hazardous Materials, including animal waste and nutrients.

"**Farms**" means the certified-organic dairy farms owned or operated by the Company, known as Ridgeview Farm, Hollis Farm and Ashgrove Farm.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under Delaware common law with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and 4.20 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court or other body thereof, including any accredited certifying agent under the USDA National Organic Program.

"**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance regulated under Environmental Law, including petroleum, pesticides, nitrates, phosphates and manure.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement to be entered into at or before the Closing between Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC, as assignor, and the Company, as assignee.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' and consultants' fees and costs of investigation and remediation.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Plant**" means the Company's fluid milk and yogurt processing facility and the associated farmstead cheese production line.

"**Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities**" means the environmental conditions and matters set forth in Part B of Schedule 4.17 of the Disclosure Schedule, to the extent arising from the ownership or operation of the Farms or the Plant before the Closing.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and the portion of any Straddle Period ending on the Closing Date.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $6,000,000 to be issued by Buyer to Seller at the Closing.

"**Straddle Period**" means any taxable period beginning on or before and ending after the Closing Date.

"**Tax**" or "**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, property, payroll, employment, withholding, franchise and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions thereto.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Seller, as provider, and Buyer, as recipient.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement and the Disclosure Schedule unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $46,200,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $40,200,000, less the Estimated Indebtedness, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(b) $6,000,000 by the issuance and delivery to Seller of the Seller Note.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing. If closing net working capital is less than $3,150,000 (the "**Target**"), Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer; if it exceeds the Target, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. Disputes not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be withheld under applicable Tax law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VIII, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank, or stock powers; (b) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC and the Company; (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (d) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (e) payoff letters for all Indebtedness; and (f) a certificate of good standing of the Company.

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.2(a); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware, and the Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where the Farms and the Plant are located.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The Shares constitute all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company, are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable, and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, any law applicable to them, or any Material Contract, except as set forth in Schedule 4.4.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows, and the unaudited balance sheet as of September 30, 2026. Such financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Company as of their dates.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since September 30, 2026, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course and there has not been any material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.7 Real Property.** Schedule 4.7 lists all real property owned or leased by the Company, including the Farms and the Plant. The Company has good and marketable fee simple title to each owned parcel.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists each contract of the Company involving annual payments in excess of $250,000, each milk supply or producer agreement, each co-packing agreement and each customer agreement with a top-twenty customer (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is in full force and effect and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.9 Organic Certification.** Each of the Farms and the Plant holds a current organic certification under the USDA National Organic Program, as listed in Schedule 4.9, and no certifying agent has issued any notice of noncompliance, proposed suspension or proposed revocation that remains unresolved.

**4.10 Food Safety.** The Company holds all permits required under the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance and applicable state dairy laws, and there has been no product recall or market withdrawal of any Company product within the past three (3) years.

**4.11 Livestock and Inventory.** The dairy herds and inventory of the Company are of a quality usable and saleable in the ordinary course of the Business.

**4.12 Intellectual Property.** Upon the execution and delivery of the IP Assignment Agreement, the Company will own all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens. To Seller's knowledge, the use of the Assigned IP by the Company does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person, and no claim alleging such infringement is pending or threatened.

**4.13 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.13, there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.14 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid. No audit or examination of any Tax return of the Company is pending, and the Company has not waived any statute of limitations in respect of Taxes.

**4.15 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.15 lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each plan has been operated in material compliance with its terms and applicable law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.16 Compliance with Laws.** The Company is, and for the past three (3) years has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws.

**4.17 Environmental Matters.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.17: (a) the Company and the Farms and the Plant are, and for the past five (5) years have been, in material compliance with Environmental Law, including all nutrient management plans and concentrated animal feeding operation permits; (b) there has been no release of Hazardous Materials at, on or under the Farms or the Plant requiring investigation or remediation under Environmental Law; and (c) Seller has made available to Buyer all environmental site assessments and reports in its possession relating to the Farms and the Plant.

**4.18 Insurance.** Schedule 4.18 lists all insurance policies maintained by the Company, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.19 Customers and Suppliers.** Schedule 4.19 lists the ten largest customers and ten largest milk suppliers of the Company for fiscal year 2025, and no such customer or supplier has notified the Company of an intention to terminate or materially reduce its relationship.

**4.20 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement, the Seller Note and the Transition Services Agreement.

**5.2 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities laws.

**5.3 Financing.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain organic certification of the Farms and the Plant, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, incur Indebtedness, sell material assets, or enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access.** Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement relating to a sale of the Company or its assets to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company, including producer pay prices, milk supply terms, herd records and customer data.

**6.5 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the Business within the States of Vermont, New York, New Hampshire, Massachusetts or Maine, other than through the performance of the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.6 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and Buyer shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide milk hauling, organic certification support and producer payroll services for a term of 9 months after the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, at Seller's expense, all income Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date and shall submit each such return to Buyer for review not less than thirty (30) days before filing. Buyer shall prepare all Tax returns for Straddle Periods.

**7.2 Straddle Periods.** Taxes for any Straddle Period shall be apportioned, in the case of property Taxes, on a per diem basis and, in the case of all other Taxes, on a closing-of-the-books basis as of the end of the Closing Date.

**7.3 Cooperation; Tax Contests.** The parties shall cooperate in the filing of Tax returns and in any audit or proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company. Buyer shall control any Tax contest relating to a period beginning before the Closing Date, provided that Seller may participate at its own expense.

**7.4 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales and similar Taxes arising from the transactions contemplated hereby shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller's performance in all material respects of its covenants; (c) delivery of each item in Section 3.2; (d) no certifying agent having suspended or revoked the organic certification of any Farm or the Plant; and (e) receipt of all third-party consents listed in Schedule 4.4.

**8.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer's performance in all material respects of its covenants; and (c) delivery of each item in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties of the parties, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until 15 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(b) The obligations of Seller under Section 9.2(b) shall survive until the Maturity Date (as defined in the Seller Note). The obligations of Seller under Section 9.2(c) shall survive until the sixth (6th) anniversary of the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the parties to be performed after the Closing shall survive in accordance with their terms.

(c) No claim may be brought after the expiration of the applicable survival period, provided that any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before such expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any Pre-Closing Taxes;

(c) any liability under Environmental Law, or any release or presence of Hazardous Materials, arising from the ownership or operation of the Farms or the Plant before the Closing; and

(d) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, but may not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller for Losses under Sections 9.2(a) and 9.2(c) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,620,000) (the "**Cap**").

(b) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses thereunder exceed $346,500 (the "**Deductible**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(c) Losses arising from Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities, from any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation, or from Fraud shall not be subject to the Cap or the Deductible. The aggregate liability of Seller under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of Fraud.

(d) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Buyer Indemnitees in respect thereof, less the costs of recovery.

(e) For purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), any materiality qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**9.6 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article IX against any amount then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to any breach of representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement, except for claims of Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by February 26, 2027, provided the terminating party is not then in material breach; or (c) by either party upon a material breach by the other that is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except for Sections 6.4 and Article XI, and except that no party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided herein, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages or such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights hereunder to any Affiliate or, as collateral, to its lenders, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.5 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any rights upon any Person other than the parties.

**11.7 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware.

**11.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.9 Ancillary Agreements.** Each Ancillary Agreement controls as to its subject matter; otherwise this Agreement controls.

**11.10 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.11 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**11.12 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
