# LETTER OF INTENT

**LEPUND CREAMERY HOLDINGS, INC.**

September 14, 2026

**PRIVATE AND CONFIDENTIAL**

Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC
Attention: Managing Member

**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). This acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**". Except as provided in Section 11, this Letter is not binding on either party.

## 1. Transaction Structure

1.1 Buyer would purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**"), free and clear of all liens. The Transaction would be documented in a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and related ancillary agreements.

1.2 Buyer understands that the Company conducts the production, processing, marketing and sale of organic fluid milk, yogurt, butter and cheese (the "**Business**"). The Business is carried on at three certified-organic dairy farms, Ridgeview Farm, Hollis Farm and Ashgrove Farm (the "**Farms**"), and at the Company's fluid milk and yogurt processing facility in Middlebury, Vermont, together with its farmstead cheese production line (the "**Plant**").

## 2. Purchase Price

2.1 The aggregate purchase price for the Shares would be $46,200,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price assumes that the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of net working capital. It would be subject to a customary post-closing working capital adjustment against a target to be agreed on the basis of Buyer's financial due diligence.

2.2 The Purchase Price would be paid at closing (the "**Closing**") as follows:

(a) $40,200,000 in cash, less the Company's indebtedness outstanding at Closing, by wire transfer of immediately available funds; and

(b) $6,000,000 by the issuance by Buyer to Seller of a subordinated promissory note (the "**Seller Note**"). The interest rate, maturity, subordination and other terms of the Seller Note would be agreed in the Definitive Agreement. The Seller Note would be subject to Buyer's right of set-off for amounts owed to Buyer under the indemnification provisions of the Definitive Agreement.

## 3. Intellectual Property

3.1 Buyer understands that certain trademarks, recipes, starter cultures and other intellectual property used in the Business are held by Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC, an affiliate of Seller ("**IP Holdco**"). These include the "Kettlebrook Organic" and "Brouloul Farmstead" brands, the Company's yogurt and cheese recipes, and its proprietary starter cultures.

3.2 At or before Closing, IP Holdco would assign to the Company, free and clear of all liens and licenses, all intellectual property owned by IP Holdco and used or held for use in the Business. This assignment would be made under an intellectual property assignment agreement in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

## 4. Representations, Warranties and Covenants

4.1 The Definitive Agreement would contain representations, warranties and covenants of Seller customary for a transaction of this type and size. These would include representations on organization and authority, capitalization and title to the Shares, financial statements, real property, material contracts, organic certification, food safety, intellectual property, taxes, employee benefits, environmental matters and customers and suppliers.

4.2 The Definitive Agreement would also include customary interim operating covenants, a post-closing confidentiality covenant, and a five (5) year non-competition covenant from Seller and its affiliates covering the Business in the northeastern United States.

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 **General Representations.** Seller's representations and warranties, other than fundamental representations, would survive Closing for 15 months after the Closing Date. Fundamental representations, consisting of organization, authority, capitalization and title, and brokers, would survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

5.2 **Taxes.** Seller would indemnify Buyer for all taxes of the Company for pre-closing periods, including the pre-closing portion of any straddle period. This tax indemnity would survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

5.3 **Environmental Matters.** Seller would indemnify Buyer for all liabilities under environmental law, and for any release or presence of hazardous materials, arising from the ownership or operation of the Farms or the Plant before Closing. Buyer's due diligence has identified groundwater nitrate contamination at Ridgeview Farm, which is described in the Phase II Environmental Site Assessment Report dated August 18, 2026. Buyer has also identified the uncompleted closure of the decommissioned manure storage pond at Hollis Farm. The parties agree that pre-closing environmental liabilities, including Ridgeview Farm nitrate contamination, not subject to the general cap. Seller's environmental indemnity would survive until the sixth (6th) anniversary of the Closing Date.

5.4 **Limitations.** Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of general representations and warranties would be subject to a cap of 10% of the Purchase Price and a deductible of 0.75% of the Purchase Price. Claims for breaches of fundamental representations and claims for fraud would not be subject to the cap or the deductible. Seller's aggregate indemnification liability would not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of fraud.

5.5 **Set-Off.** Buyer would be entitled to set off indemnifiable losses against amounts due under the Seller Note.

## 6. Transition Services

At Closing, Seller and Buyer would enter into a transition services agreement. Under it, Seller would provide milk hauling, organic certification support and producer payroll services to Buyer for a term of 9 months after the Closing Date, at cost-based fees to be agreed. The agreement would include customary confidentiality and data protection obligations for the Company's producer, pricing and customer information.

## 7. Conditions to Closing

The Closing would be subject to the following conditions, among others customary for a transaction of this type:

(a) completion of Buyer's business, financial, legal, environmental, tax and organic certification due diligence to Buyer's satisfaction;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and the ancillary agreements described in this Letter;

(c) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's board of directors;

(d) receipt of required third-party consents, including the consent of any lender to the Company and of key customers whose contracts require consent to a change of control;

(e) no suspension or revocation of the organic certification of any Farm or the Plant; and

(f) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

## 8. Due Diligence and Access

From the date of countersignature of this Letter until the earlier of the execution of the Definitive Agreement and the termination of this Letter, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the Farms, the Plant, and the Company's books, records, contracts, personnel, environmental reports and organic certification files.

## 9. Timing

The parties intend to use good faith efforts to execute the Definitive Agreement by November 6, 2026, and to complete the Closing as soon as practicable thereafter.

## 10. Expenses

Each party shall bear its own fees and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 11. Binding Provisions

11.1 **Exclusivity.** For a period of sixty (60) days after the date of Seller's countersignature of this Letter, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company, IP Holdco and their respective representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer relating to any sale of the Company, its stock or any material portion of its assets. Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of that kind that it receives.

11.2 **Confidentiality.** The terms of this Letter and the existence of discussions between the parties are subject to the Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller dated June 22, 2026, which remains in full force and effect.

11.3 **Non-Binding Effect.** Except for Sections 8, 10 and 11, which are binding on the parties, this Letter is an expression of intent only. No binding obligation to complete the Transaction shall arise unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered by both parties.

11.4 **Termination.** The binding provisions of this Letter shall terminate on the earliest of (a) the execution of the Definitive Agreement, (b) the expiration of the exclusivity period in Section 11.1, and (c) the written agreement of the parties. Section 10 and Section 11.2 shall survive any termination.

11.5 **Governing Law.** This Letter shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## 12. Acceptance

If the foregoing reflects Seller's understanding, please sign and return a copy of this Letter to the undersigned by 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on September 18, 2026. If it is not countersigned by that time, this proposal shall lapse.

We look forward to working with you to complete the Transaction.
