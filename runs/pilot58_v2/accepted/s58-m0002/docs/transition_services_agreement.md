# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement referred to below) between **Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as seller, and Recipient, as buyer, are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of November 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided milk hauling, organic certification support and producer payroll services to the Company.

C. Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires the parties to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that Provider will continue to provide those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Company Data**" means all data and information of or relating to the Company or the Business that Provider or any Service Personnel receives, accesses or generates in performing the Services. Company Data includes producer pay prices, milk supply contracts, herd records, milk quality and component test results, organic system plans and certification files, payroll records and customer orders.

"**Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule A.

"**Losses**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Service Personnel**" means the employees, agents and subcontractors of Provider or its Affiliates who perform any of the Services.

"**Services**" means the services described in Section 3 and Schedule A.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Closing Date and continues until 9 months after the Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under Section 10 (the "**Term**").

**2.2 Service Periods.** Each Service shall be provided for the full Term unless Schedule A states a shorter period for that Service or Recipient terminates that Service early under Section 10.2.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Milk Hauling.** Provider shall collect raw organic milk from the Farms and from the independent producers listed in Part B of Schedule 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule, and deliver it to the Plant, on the routes and pickup schedules in effect immediately before the Closing. Provider shall use bulk tank trucks that are dedicated to certified-organic milk, are cleaned and sanitized in accordance with the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance, and are operated by drivers holding current bulk milk hauler and sampler licenses in each applicable state.

**3.2 Organic Certification Support.** Provider shall assist the Company in maintaining the organic certifications listed in Schedule 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule. This assistance includes preparing and updating organic system plans, maintaining grazing-season and pasture records, tracking input and feed purchases, preparing for annual inspections, and responding to inquiries from certifying agents.

**3.3 Producer Payroll.** Provider shall calculate and process payments to the Company's milk producers, including the Farms' internal accounting transfers. Provider shall apply component, quality and organic premiums under each producer's milk supply agreement, process hauling deductions and other authorized deductions, issue producer settlement statements, and remit payments from Company accounts on the Company's customary semi-monthly settlement dates.

**3.4 Omitted Services.** If, within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Recipient identifies any service related to the Services that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company in the twelve (12) months before the Closing and that is not described in Schedule A, Provider shall provide that service at a fee determined on the same cost basis as the Fees. The parties shall amend Schedule A to reflect any such service.

## 4. STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

**4.1 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in substantially the same manner, scope, priority and quality as provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing, (b) in compliance with applicable law, including the USDA National Organic Program regulations, the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance and applicable state dairy and wage payment laws, and (c) with the degree of care that Provider applies to its own comparable operations (the "**Service Standard**").

**4.2 Service Personnel.** Provider shall assign suitably qualified and licensed Service Personnel. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of all Service Personnel.

**4.3 Service Managers.** Each party shall designate a service manager to coordinate the Services and resolve operational issues. The service managers shall meet, in person or by telephone, at least every two weeks during the Term.

**4.4 Reports.** Provider shall deliver to Recipient within ten (10) days after the end of each month a report summarizing milk volumes hauled by route and producer, any rejected or diverted loads, the status of organic certification matters, and the producer payments processed in that month.

## 5. ACCESS AND COOPERATION

**5.1 Access to Company Data and Systems.** To enable Provider to perform the Services, Recipient shall cause the Company to give Provider and the Service Personnel access to the Company Data and to the Company's systems, facilities and personnel reasonably necessary for the Services. This access includes the Company's producer pay prices, milk supply contracts, herd records and customer orders for producer payroll and hauling route planning.

**5.2 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall cause the Company to provide timely information, approvals and payment authorizations reasonably requested by Provider in connection with the Services.

**5.3 Facility Rules.** While at any Farm or the Plant, Service Personnel shall comply with the Company's biosecurity, food safety and site safety rules made known to them.

**5.4 Ownership of Company Data.** As between the parties, all Company Data is and shall remain the property of the Company. Provider acquires no right in the Company Data except the limited right to use it to perform the Services during the Term.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Fees for each Service as set forth in Schedule A. The Fees are intended to reimburse Provider's actual cost of providing the Services, without markup.

**6.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for fuel surcharges, tolls and third-party laboratory testing fees incurred in providing the Services, at Provider's actual cost and without markup, to the extent supported by reasonable documentation.

**6.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by written notice describing the dispute in reasonable detail. The parties shall resolve any dispute under Section 11.

**6.4 Taxes.** The Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Recipient shall bear to the extent applicable. Each party shall bear its own income taxes.

**6.5 Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep accurate records supporting the Fees and pass-through costs. Recipient may audit those records once during the Term and once within six (6) months after its end, on reasonable notice and during normal business hours.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1 Obligations.** Provider shall hold all Company Data in confidence and shall use it only to perform the Services. Provider shall not disclose Company Data to any Person other than Service Personnel who need to know it to perform the Services and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 7.

**7.2 Security.** Provider shall maintain commercially reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect the Company Data against unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Provider shall notify Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Company Data.

**7.3 Return.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of the Term or the earlier termination of any Service, Provider shall deliver to the Company all Company Data relating to the terminated Services in Provider's possession, in a commonly used electronic format, and shall then destroy all remaining copies and certify that destruction in writing.

**7.4 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Section 7 is in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient, the Company and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in the performance of the Services or Provider's breach of Section 7.

**8.2 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates and their respective officers, members, managers, employees and Service Personnel from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the provision of the Services or Recipient's use of the Services, except to the extent caused by Provider's willful misconduct.

**8.3 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any consequential, incidental, indirect, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, except to the extent paid to a third party in respect of a third-party claim.

**8.4 Limitation of Liability.** The aggregate liability of each party arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid by Recipient under this Agreement. The limitations in Section 8.3 and this Section 8.4 shall not apply to Recipient's obligations under Section 8.2 or to Recipient's obligation to pay the Fees.

**8.5 Procedures.** Claims for indemnification under this Section 8 shall be made and handled in accordance with the procedures set forth in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, applied with such changes as the context requires.

## 9. INSURANCE

During the Term, Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, (b) commercial automobile liability insurance covering all vehicles used in the milk hauling Services with a combined single limit of at least $1,000,000, (c) cargo insurance covering raw milk in transit, and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. On request, Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing this coverage.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it. Provider may not terminate for nonpayment of any amount disputed in good faith under Section 6.3.

**10.2 Early Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or by route or category, on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Fees for a terminated Service shall cease to accrue as of the effective date of termination.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, Recipient shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date. Sections 5.4, 6.3 through 6.5, 7, 8, 11 and 12 shall survive expiration or termination.

**10.4 Transition Assistance.** During the last sixty (60) days of the Term, and for any Service terminated early, Provider shall cooperate reasonably with Recipient and any successor provider in the orderly transfer of the Services, including by providing route sheets, producer pickup schedules, payroll calculation files and organic certification records.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**11.1 Escalation.** The parties' service managers shall first attempt to resolve any dispute under this Agreement. If they cannot resolve it within ten (10) Business Days, either party may refer it to a senior executive of each party, who shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after the referral.

**11.2 Continued Performance.** Pending resolution of any dispute, Provider shall continue to perform the Services and Recipient shall continue to pay undisputed Fees.

**11.3 Litigation.** If the senior executives do not resolve the dispute within twenty (20) Business Days after referral, either party may bring proceedings in accordance with Section 12.6.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties. Service Personnel shall remain employees or contractors of Provider or its Affiliates.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**12.3 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Provider, except that Recipient may assign its rights hereunder to the Company or any other Affiliate of Recipient. Provider may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, or delegate the performance of any Service to any Person upon written notice to Recipient and without the consent of Recipient. This Agreement shall bind and benefit the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, road closures, fire, flood, epidemic or animal disease quarantine, provided that the affected party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents the milk hauling Services for more than seventy-two (72) hours, Recipient may engage a substitute hauler for the affected routes, and the Fees for those routes shall be suspended for that period.

**12.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**12.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware.

**12.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**12.8 Third-Party Beneficiary.** The Company is an express third-party beneficiary of Sections 3, 4, 5.4, 7 and 8.1 and may enforce them directly against Provider. Except as stated in this Section 12.8 and Section 8, nothing in this Agreement confers any rights on any Person other than the parties.

**12.9 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together shall constitute one instrument.
