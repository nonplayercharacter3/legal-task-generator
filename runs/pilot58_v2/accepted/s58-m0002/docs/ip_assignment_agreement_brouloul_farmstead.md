# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into effective as of the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement referred to below). It is made by and between **Brouloul Farmstead IP Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Kettlebrook Organic Creamery, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Brouloul Family Dairy Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Lepund Creamery Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of November 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignor is an Affiliate of Seller. Assignor holds title to certain intellectual property that Assignee has used in the conduct of its business.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires the execution and delivery of this Agreement at or before the Closing. Assignor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Assigned IP**" means all Intellectual Property owned or purported to be owned by Assignor, in whole or in part, that is used or held for use in the Business. Assigned IP includes:

(a) the Trademarks listed in Schedule A, together with all other Trademarks used in the Business, and all goodwill associated with any of them;

(b) all Recipes and Know-How used in the Business;

(c) all Starter Cultures used in the Business, including all master stocks, working stocks and propagation records for them;

(d) all Copyrights in labels, packaging artwork, website content and marketing materials used in the Business;

(e) all domain names and social media accounts and handles used in the Business; and

(f) all rights to sue for and recover damages for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or dilution of any of the foregoing.

"**Business**" means the production, processing and sale of organic fluid milk, as conducted by Assignee as of the date of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Copyrights**" means all works of authorship, whether or not registered, and all registrations and applications for them.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights in any jurisdiction, including Trademarks, Copyrights, Recipes and Know-How, Starter Cultures, trade secrets, domain names and all registrations, applications, renewals and extensions of any of the foregoing.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Recipes and Know-How**" means all formulas, recipes, ingredient specifications, standardization parameters, processing methods, pasteurization and homogenization protocols, quality control procedures, shelf-life data and related technical information, whether or not reduced to writing, together with all trade secret rights in them.

"**Starter Cultures**" means all proprietary bacterial, yeast and mold cultures, strains and blends, and all derivatives, isolates and subcultures of them, together with all related strain characterization data and propagation methods.

"**Trademarks**" means all trademarks, service marks, trade names, brand names, logos, trade dress and other source identifiers, whether or not registered, and all registrations and applications for them.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee hereby accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP. The assignment is free and clear of all liens, licenses and encumbrances.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The assignment of each Trademark included in the Assigned IP includes the goodwill of the Business symbolized by that Trademark.

**2.3 Recordation.** Assignee may record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment in a form reasonably requested by Assignee, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office and any other registry or governmental authority. Assignor shall execute any such short-form assignment at or promptly after the Closing.

## 3. DELIVERY

**3.1 Documentation.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP in Assignor's possession or control. These include written Recipes and Know-How, batch records, laboratory notebooks, strain characterization files, label and packaging artwork files, and registration certificates and prosecution files for the registered Trademarks.

**3.2 Starter Culture Stocks.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Assignee at the Plant all master and working stocks of the Starter Cultures included in the Assigned IP. Delivery shall be made under temperature-controlled conditions consistent with Assignor's past practice. Assignor shall also deliver all propagation and viability records for those stocks. Assignor shall not retain any sample, isolate or subculture of any such Starter Culture.

**3.3 Domain Names and Accounts.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing, Assignor shall transfer to Assignee administrative control of each domain name and social media account included in the Assigned IP. Assignor shall also provide all credentials necessary for that transfer.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Assignor is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. Assignor has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignor, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Title.** Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens, licenses and encumbrances. Assignor has not granted any license, option or other right in the Assigned IP to any Person other than Assignee.

**4.3 Validity.** Each registered Trademark listed in Schedule A is subsisting and, to Assignor's knowledge, valid and enforceable. All maintenance and renewal fees due on or before the Closing Date have been paid.

**4.4 Non-Infringement.** To Assignor's knowledge, the use of the Assigned IP in the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or dilute the Intellectual Property of any Person. No claim alleging any such infringement, misappropriation or dilution is pending or, to Assignor's knowledge, threatened.

**4.5 Trade Secrets.** Assignor has taken commercially reasonable measures to protect the confidentiality of the Recipes and Know-How and the Starter Cultures included in the Assigned IP. To Assignor's knowledge, none of them has been disclosed to any Person except under a written obligation of confidentiality.

## 5. COVENANTS

**5.1 Further Assurances.** From and after the Closing, Assignor shall, at Assignee's request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as Assignee may reasonably request to vest, perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP.

**5.2 Power of Attorney.** Assignor hereby irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file any document described in Section 5.1 if Assignor fails to do so within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

**5.3 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Assignor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all Recipes and Know-How and all information relating to the Starter Cultures included in the Assigned IP. Assignor shall not use or disclose any of them, except as required by law. In that case Assignor shall give Assignee prompt prior notice and cooperate with Assignee in seeking a protective order.

**5.4 No Challenge.** Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, challenge the validity, enforceability or ownership of any Assigned IP. Assignor shall not register or attempt to register any Trademark confusingly similar to any Trademark included in the Assigned IP.

**5.5 Payments Received.** If Assignor receives any payment or recovery relating to the Assigned IP after the Closing, Assignor shall hold it in trust for Assignee. Assignor shall remit it to Assignee within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

## 6. INDEMNIFICATION

**6.1 Indemnity.** Assignor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Assignee and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Assignor in Section 4, or (b) any breach of any covenant of Assignor in this Agreement.

**6.2 Procedures.** Claims under this Section 6 shall be made and handled in accordance with the procedures set forth in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, applied with such changes as the context requires.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing. Notices shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page or such other address as a party designates by notice.

**7.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**7.3 Assignment; Successors.** Assignor may not assign this Agreement or any of its obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Assignee. Assignee may assign its rights hereunder to Buyer, to any Affiliate of Buyer or to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Business. This Agreement shall bind and benefit the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**7.4 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce it directly against Assignor.

**7.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware.

**7.6 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**7.7 Specific Performance.** Assignor acknowledges that any breach of Sections 3 or 5 would cause Assignee irreparable harm. Assignee shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**7.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**7.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together shall constitute one instrument.
