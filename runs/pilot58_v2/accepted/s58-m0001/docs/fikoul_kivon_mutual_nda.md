# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of January 20, 2026 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Kivon Systems LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 1200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Kivon**"), and **Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal offices at 75 Clinton Square, Rochester, New York 14604 ("**Fikoul**"). Kivon and Fikoul are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Fikoul would license, and Kivon would host, implement and support, Kivon QualiTrace, a cloud-hosted electronic quality management system (the "**Purpose**"). This NDA governs information the Parties exchange for the Purpose.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

2.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Affiliates disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Affiliates in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes product roadmaps, software architecture, security documentation, pricing, quality procedures, manufacturing information, regulatory correspondence, and the fact and content of the Parties' discussions.

2.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, and legal, financial and technical advisors.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no breach of this NDA by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before the Disclosing Party disclosed it;

(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or

(d) is rightfully received from a third party that is not bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party.

## 4. Obligations

4.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

4.2 **Protection.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, and no less care than it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature.

4.3 **Disclosure to Representatives.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

4.4 **Patient and Personal Information.** The Parties do not intend to exchange protected health information or other personal information about patients for the Purpose. Fikoul shall de-identify any sample quality records it provides to Kivon for evaluation. If either Party discovers that it has received such information, it shall promptly notify the other Party and return or securely delete it.

4.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly after it becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and shall cooperate reasonably to limit its effects.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, court order, or a regulatory authority, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Where legally permitted, it shall first give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's expense, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 6. Return and Destruction

Within thirty (30) days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and confirm this in writing. The Receiving Party may retain copies (a) as required by law or its regulatory record retention obligations, and (b) in automatic electronic backups that are not readily accessible, provided those copies remain subject to this NDA for as long as they are retained.

## 7. No License; No Warranty

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any license or other right under any patent, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property right, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

7.2 Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no warranty about its accuracy or completeness. Only representations and warranties stated in a signed definitive agreement will bind a Party.

## 8. No Obligation to Proceed

This NDA does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice. Each Party remains free to discuss similar transactions with third parties, provided it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in doing so.

## 9. Term

9.1 This NDA covers Confidential Information disclosed during the period beginning on the NDA Effective Date and ending two (2) years later, unless either Party ends it earlier on thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.2 The Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 4 through 7 continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period. Obligations for Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 10. Remedies

Unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive relief to enforce this NDA, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, without posting a bond.

## 11. General

11.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

11.3 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses stated in the preamble (to the attention of the General Counsel of Kivon and the Chief Legal Officer of Fikoul), or to another address a Party designates by notice.

11.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the other Party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of all or substantially all of its relevant assets.

11.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

11.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

11.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
