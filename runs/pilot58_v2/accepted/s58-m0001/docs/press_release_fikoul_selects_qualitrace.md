# Fikoul Orthopedics Selects Kivon QualiTrace to Modernize Quality Management for Hip and Knee Implant Manufacturing

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**ROCHESTER, N.Y. and WILMINGTON, Del., May 12, 2026.** Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc., a manufacturer of orthopedic hip and knee implants, and Kivon Systems LLC, a provider of cloud software for regulated life sciences manufacturers, today announced that Fikoul has selected Kivon QualiTrace as its electronic quality management system (eQMS). The companies have signed a software license agreement under which Kivon will host, implement and support QualiTrace for Fikoul, starting at Fikoul's manufacturing facility in Warsaw, Indiana.

## 1. About the Agreement

Kivon QualiTrace is a cloud-hosted eQMS. Fikoul will use it to manage device history records, complaint files, and nonconformance and corrective and preventive action (CAPA) workflows. The deployment includes the QualiTrace Core, Complaints and NC/CAPA modules. It also includes migration of Fikoul's legacy quality records from its Warsaw, Indiana facility, together with validation support and Premier Support.

Implementation began in May 2026, and the system is scheduled to go into production at the Warsaw facility in June 2026. Customer data will be hosted in U.S. data centers. The platform's electronic records and signature controls are designed to support compliance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11.

The financial terms of the agreement were not disclosed.

## 2. Executive Comments

"Our surgeons and patients rely on the traceability of every implant we make," said the Chief Operating Officer of Fikoul Orthopedics. "Moving our device history records, complaint handling and CAPA processes onto a single validated cloud platform will give our quality teams in Warsaw better visibility and faster investigations. It will also give us a stronger foundation for regulatory inspections."

"Fikoul is exactly the kind of manufacturer QualiTrace was built for," said the Chief Executive Officer of Kivon Systems. "Orthopedic implant makers operate under some of the most demanding quality and data integrity requirements in the industry. We look forward to supporting Fikoul's team through implementation, validation and beyond."

## 3. About Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc.

Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc. designs and manufactures hip and knee implant systems for orthopedic surgeons and hospitals. The company is headquartered in Rochester, New York, and manufactures its products at its facility in Warsaw, Indiana.

## 4. About Kivon Systems LLC

Kivon Systems LLC develops cloud software for quality and compliance in regulated manufacturing. Its flagship product, Kivon QualiTrace, helps medical device and life sciences companies manage quality records, nonconformances, CAPAs and complaints in a validated, hosted environment. Kivon is based in Wilmington, Delaware.
