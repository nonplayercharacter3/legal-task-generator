# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**QA Effective Date**") by and between **Kivon Systems LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 1200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**"), and **Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal offices at 75 Clinton Square, Rochester, New York 14604 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of May 4, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**") and Order Form No. KQ-2026-0417 under it (the "**Order Form**"). Under them, Licensee licenses Kivon QualiTrace, a cloud-hosted electronic quality management system, for use in its operations, including at its manufacturing facility in Warsaw, Indiana (the "**Warsaw Facility**").

B. Licensee uses the Software to create and maintain records required under the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's Quality Management System Regulation, 21 C.F.R. Part 820, and its electronic records and signatures regulation, 21 C.F.R. Part 11. These records include device history records, complaint files, and nonconformance and CAPA records for orthopedic hip and knee implants. Licensee therefore must validate the Software for its intended use and exercise appropriate controls over Licensor as a supplier.

C. On August 3, 2026, Licensee delivered to Licensor a written notice rejecting the Software under Section 4.2 of the License Agreement. Licensor contends that the notice was untimely and that the Software has been deemed accepted. Licensee disputes Licensor's position. Neither Party waives any right, remedy or position with respect to that notice by entering into this Agreement.

D. This Agreement is the Quality Agreement referred to in the License Agreement. It sets out the quality, validation, change control, data integrity and regulatory support responsibilities of each Party for the Software and the Hosted Services.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the License Agreement.

1.2 "**Applicable Regulations**" means 21 C.F.R. Parts 11 and 820, ISO 13485:2016, and other laws, regulations and standards that apply to Licensee's use of the Software as part of its quality management system, each as amended.

1.3 "**CAPA**" means corrective and preventive action.

1.4 "**GxP Change**" means any change to the Software, the Hosted Services or Licensor's hosting infrastructure that could affect validated functionality, data integrity, electronic signatures, audit trails, security controls or the availability of Customer Data.

1.5 "**Nonconformance**" means any failure of the Software or the Hosted Services to conform to the Documentation, the approved configuration design, the acceptance criteria in the Order Form, the Applicable Regulations as they relate to the Software, or the requirements of this Agreement.

1.6 "**Quality Matters**" means all matters addressed in this Agreement, including the quality, validation, change control, data integrity, audit, inspection support and record retention responsibilities of each Party for the Software and the Hosted Services, and any dispute arising from software validation, Nonconformances or CAPA.

1.7 "**Quality Representative**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 2.3.

1.8 "**Validation Package**" means the validation documentation Licensor provides for each release of the Software, including the installation qualification and operational qualification summaries, requirements traceability matrix, test scripts and executed results, and release notes.

## 2. SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 **Scope.** This Agreement applies to the Software, the Hosted Services and the Data Migration Services provided under the License Agreement and the Order Form, at the Warsaw Facility and any other site where Licensee or its Affiliates use the Software.

2.2 **Allocation of Responsibilities.** The Parties' responsibilities are allocated in the responsibility matrix in Schedule 1. Licensee retains ultimate responsibility for its quality management system and for validating the Software for its intended use. Licensor is responsible for the quality of the Software and Hosted Services as supplied, and for the activities assigned to it in this Agreement.

2.3 **Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Representative within ten (10) business days after the QA Effective Date and shall notify the other Party in writing of any change. Licensee's initial Quality Representative is its Director, Quality Systems. Licensor's initial Quality Representative is its Head of Quality and Compliance, Life Sciences.

2.4 **Licensor Quality System.** Licensor shall maintain a documented quality management system for the development, testing, release, hosting and support of the Software. That system shall be consistent with ISO 9001:2015 or ISO 13485:2016 and with GAMP 5 principles for computerized systems. Licensor shall maintain written procedures for software development life cycle, configuration management, change control, problem management, CAPA, training and document control.

2.5 **Personnel and Training.** Licensor shall ensure that personnel who develop, test, host or support the Software, or who have access to Customer Data, are qualified and trained in the relevant procedures and in the Applicable Regulations to the extent relevant to their roles. Licensor shall maintain training records and make them available to Licensee on request.

## 3. SOFTWARE VALIDATION

3.1 **Licensor Validation Package.** For each release deployed to Licensee's production environment, Licensor shall provide a complete Validation Package at least thirty (30) days before production deployment, consistent with Section 5.3 of the License Agreement and Section 5.5 of the Order Form.

3.2 **Licensee Validation.** Licensee is responsible for performing performance qualification and any user acceptance testing it considers necessary to establish that the Software is fit for its intended use. Licensor shall provide reasonable support for that work, as described in Section 4.4 of the License Agreement, including access to the validation environment and assistance from knowledgeable Licensor personnel.

3.3 **Validation Environment.** Licensor shall maintain a validation environment that mirrors the production configuration. Licensor shall document and resolve any variance between the two environments before production deployment of a release.

3.4 **Validation Deficiencies.** If Licensee identifies a deficiency in a Validation Package, Licensee shall notify Licensor in writing. Licensor shall correct the deficiency and reissue the affected documentation within fifteen (15) business days after notice, or within a longer period the Quality Representatives agree in writing.

3.5 **Periodic Review.** The Quality Representatives shall meet at least annually to review the validated state of the Software, open Nonconformances and CAPAs, GxP Changes during the period, and audit findings.

## 4. CHANGE CONTROL

4.1 **GxP Changes.** Licensor shall manage all GxP Changes under its documented change control procedure. That procedure shall include impact assessment, risk classification, testing, approval and documentation.

4.2 **Notice.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any GxP Change that materially changes validated functionality, in accordance with Section 5.3 of the License Agreement. The notice shall include a description of the change, Licensor's impact assessment and the updated Validation Package.

4.3 **Emergency Changes.** Licensor may implement an emergency GxP Change without prior notice where needed to correct a Severity 1 issue or respond to a Security Incident. Licensor shall notify Licensee within twenty-four (24) hours after implementation and shall provide the change documentation and Validation Package within ten (10) business days.

4.4 **Infrastructure and Subprocessors.** Licensor shall treat any change of hosting provider, data center location or subprocessor with access to Customer Data as a GxP Change. Licensor shall also comply with Section 8 of the Order Form.

## 5. DATA INTEGRITY AND ELECTRONIC RECORDS

5.1 **ALCOA+ Principles.** Licensor shall design, host and maintain the Software so that Customer Data remains attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring and available throughout the applicable retention period.

5.2 **Part 11 Controls.** Licensor shall maintain the Software's technical controls for electronic records and electronic signatures in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11 as described in the Documentation. These controls include secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails that users cannot modify or disable; unique user identification; signature manifestations; and the linking of signatures to their records.

5.3 **Access Controls.** Licensor shall limit its personnel's access to Licensee's production environment and Customer Data to what is necessary to provide the Hosted Services and support. Licensor shall log all such access and make the logs available to Licensee on request.

5.4 **Backup and Recovery.** Licensor shall perform backups and disaster recovery testing in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Order Form. Licensor shall verify the integrity of restored data during each disaster recovery test and report the results to Licensee.

5.5 **Security.** Licensor's security obligations for Customer Data are set out in Section 7 of the License Agreement.

## 6. NONCONFORMANCES

6.1 **Identification and Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other Party's Quality Representative in writing of any Nonconformance it identifies. Licensor shall notify Licensee of any Nonconformance that could affect the integrity, completeness or availability of Customer Data, or the validated state of the Software, within two (2) business days after identifying it.

6.2 **Classification.** Licensor shall classify each Nonconformance by severity in accordance with Section 5.4 of the Order Form and shall assess its impact on Customer Data and validated functionality. Licensee may reasonably request a reclassification, and the Quality Representatives shall resolve any disagreement in good faith.

6.3 **Investigation.** Licensor shall investigate each Nonconformance it is responsible for, determine its root cause and document the investigation. Licensor shall provide Licensee with an investigation report within twenty (20) business days after notice for Severity 1 and Severity 2 Nonconformances, and on request for other Nonconformances.

6.4 **Correction.** Licensor shall correct Nonconformances within the resolution targets in Section 5.4 of the Order Form. Licensor shall provide Licensee with documentation sufficient to support Licensee's assessment of whether re-validation is required.

6.5 **Data Impact.** If a Nonconformance has corrupted, altered or lost Customer Data, Licensor shall identify the affected records, restore them from backup where possible, and provide Licensee with a written data impact assessment to support Licensee's own evaluation under its quality system.

## 7. CAPA

7.1 **Licensor CAPA.** Licensor shall open a CAPA for any Nonconformance that is Severity 1 or Severity 2, recurs, or results from a systemic failure in Licensor's quality system. Licensor shall also open a CAPA for any audit finding Licensee classifies as major or critical.

7.2 **CAPA Plan.** Licensor shall provide Licensee with a written CAPA plan within thirty (30) days after opening a CAPA. The plan shall include root cause, corrective and preventive actions, responsible persons, target completion dates and effectiveness checks.

7.3 **Tracking and Closure.** Licensor shall report the status of open CAPAs to Licensee monthly. A CAPA is closed when Licensor has completed the actions and verified their effectiveness, and has provided the closure documentation to Licensee.

7.4 **Licensee CAPA.** Licensor shall cooperate reasonably with any CAPA Licensee opens under its own quality system that relates to the Software or the Hosted Services.

## 8. COMPLAINTS AND REGULATORY REPORTING SUPPORT

8.1 **Licensee Responsibility.** Licensee is solely responsible for handling complaints about its medical devices and for evaluating and submitting medical device reports and other regulatory reports.

8.2 **Licensor Support.** If a Nonconformance or Security Incident affects complaint files or other records that Licensee may need for complaint handling or regulatory reporting, Licensor shall promptly provide the information Licensee reasonably needs to meet its reporting deadlines.

8.3 **Software Complaints.** Licensor shall maintain a procedure to receive, log and investigate complaints from its customers about the Software, and shall notify Licensee of any such complaint that indicates a defect likely to affect Licensee's use of the Software.

## 9. AUDITS

9.1 **Supplier Audits.** Licensee or its designated auditor may audit Licensor's quality system, facilities, records and procedures relating to the Software and Hosted Services, in accordance with Section 13.2 of the License Agreement. Licensor shall make relevant personnel and documents reasonably available during each audit.

9.2 **Remote and Documentary Audits.** Licensee may conduct an audit remotely or through a written questionnaire. Licensor shall respond to a supplier questionnaire within thirty (30) days after receipt.

9.3 **Findings.** Licensee shall provide Licensor with a written audit report within thirty (30) days after an audit. Licensor shall respond within thirty (30) days after receipt, with a CAPA plan for each major or critical finding under Section 7.

9.4 **Third-Party Reports.** Licensor shall provide Licensee each year with its most recent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent, together with any ISO certificates relevant to the Software.

## 10. REGULATORY INSPECTIONS

10.1 **Support.** Licensor shall support Licensee during regulatory inspections and audits relating to the Software or Customer Data, in accordance with Section 7.8 of the License Agreement. This includes making knowledgeable personnel available, on reasonable notice and by remote means if needed, to explain the Software's controls, validation and change history.

10.2 **Inspections of Licensor.** If a regulatory authority notifies Licensor of an inspection or request for information that relates to the Software as used by Licensee, Licensor shall notify Licensee within two (2) business days where legally permitted. Licensor shall provide Licensee with copies of any resulting observations that relate to the Software.

## 11. RECORDS AND RETENTION

11.1 **Licensor Records.** Licensor shall retain its validation, change control, Nonconformance, CAPA, training and audit records relating to the Software for the period stated in Section 13.1 of the License Agreement.

11.2 **Customer Data Retention.** Licensor shall retain Customer Data in the Hosted Services during the Term as required to allow Licensee to meet its record retention obligations under the Applicable Regulations. Licensor's obligations on expiration or termination are set out in Sections 7.5 and 12.4 of the License Agreement.

## 12. SUBCONTRACTORS

12.1 Licensor shall qualify and monitor any subcontractor or subprocessor that performs activities affecting the quality of the Software or the Hosted Services. Licensor shall impose on them written obligations consistent with this Agreement and with Section 5.4 of the License Agreement.

12.2 Licensor remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors and subprocessors under this Agreement.

## 13. TERM

13.1 This Agreement begins on the QA Effective Date and continues for as long as the License Agreement remains in effect.

13.2 Sections 5, 10 and 11 survive expiration or termination to the extent necessary for Licensee to retain access to Customer Data and meet its regulatory obligations.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

14.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to Quality Matters, are governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

14.2 **Venue.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts located in Kosciusko County, Indiana for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any Quality Matter, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

14.3 **Escalation.** Before starting any action under Section 14.2, the Party raising a dispute shall refer it to the Quality Representatives. If they do not resolve it within fifteen (15) business days, either Party may refer it to a senior executive of each Party, who shall meet within ten (10) business days. This Section does not prevent either Party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 15 of the License Agreement, with a copy to the other Party's Quality Representative. Routine operational communications between the Quality Representatives may be given by email.

15.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement, including Schedule 1, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. The Quality Representatives may update contact details in Schedule 1 by written notice.

15.3 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
