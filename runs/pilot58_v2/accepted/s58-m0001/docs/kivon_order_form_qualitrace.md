## 2. Subscription Term and Renewal

2.1 **Initial Term.** The initial Subscription Term begins on the Delivery Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Order Form renews automatically for successive thirty-six (36) month renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal not more than one hundred fifty (150) days and not less than one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. Notice of non-renewal must be given in accordance with Section 15 of the License Agreement.

2.3 **Renewal Pricing.** The Annual Subscription Fee for each year of a Renewal Term may increase by no more than three percent (3%) over the Annual Subscription Fee in effect for the immediately preceding year. Licensor shall give Licensee written notice of any increase at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the start of the Renewal Term.

## 5. Service Levels and Support

5.1 **Availability.** Licensor shall make the production environment available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month. Scheduled maintenance is excluded, provided it is performed within the maintenance window of Saturday 10:00 p.m. to Sunday 4:00 a.m. Eastern Time and notified at least five (5) business days in advance.

5.2 **Service Credits.** If monthly availability falls below the commitment in Section 5.1, Licensor shall credit Licensee against the next invoice as follows:

| Monthly Availability | Credit (% of monthly portion of Annual Subscription Fee) |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% but at least 99.5% | 5% |
| Below 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% | 25% |

5.3 **Backup and Disaster Recovery.** Licensor shall back up Customer Data at least every four (4) hours to a geographically separate U.S. data center. The recovery point objective is four (4) hours and the recovery time objective is twenty-four (24) hours. Licensor shall test its disaster recovery plan at least annually and provide Licensee with a summary of the results.

5.4 **Premier Support.** Licensor shall provide 24x7 support for Severity 1 issues and support from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days for all other issues, with the following response targets:

| Severity | Description | Initial Response | Target Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Production down or Customer Data integrity at risk | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| 2 | Major function impaired, no workaround | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| 3 | Minor function impaired or workaround available | 1 business day | Next scheduled release |
| 4 | Question or enhancement request | 2 business days | As agreed |

5.5 **Releases.** Licensor shall deploy major releases to the validation environment at least thirty (30) days before production deployment, consistent with Section 5.3 of the License Agreement.

Licensor's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to the Hosted Services, Data Migration Services or Customer Data under this Order Form shall not exceed the Subscription Fees paid by Licensee under this Order Form in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Implementation Plan and Acceptance Criteria

7.1 **Implementation Timetable.**

| Milestone | Target Date |
|---|---|
| Project kickoff | May 11, 2026 |
| Configuration design sign-off | May 29, 2026 |
| Validation environment build complete | June 5, 2026 |
| Data migration trial load and reconciliation | June 12, 2026 |
| Final migration and production cutover | June 19–21, 2026 |
| Delivery Date | June 22, 2026 |

7.2 **Data Migration Scope.** Licensor shall migrate the following legacy records from Licensee's existing quality systems at the Warsaw, Indiana facility: (a) device history records for lots manufactured from January 1, 2019; (b) open and closed complaint files from January 1, 2016; (c) nonconformance and CAPA records from January 1, 2019; and (d) associated attachments and audit trail metadata, to the extent the legacy systems can export it.

7.3 **Migration Reconciliation.** Licensor shall deliver a reconciliation report showing record counts, field-level mapping, exceptions and checksum verification for each migrated record set.

7.4 **Acceptance Criteria.** For the purposes of Section 4.2 of the License Agreement, the Software meets the acceptance criteria if:

(a) all configured workflows for device history records, complaints, nonconformance and CAPA execute in the production environment in accordance with the approved configuration design and the Documentation;
(b) the migration reconciliation report shows that at least 99.95% of in-scope records migrated without data loss, and Licensor has corrected or documented every exception;
(c) electronic signatures and audit trails function in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11 as described in the Documentation;
(d) the Licensor validation package listed in Section 3.2(c) is complete for the deployed release; and
(e) no Severity 1 or Severity 2 issue remains open.
