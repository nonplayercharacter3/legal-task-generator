# NON-BINDING TERM SHEET

## Kivon QualiTrace eQMS License and Hosted Services

**Date:** March 18, 2026

**Parties:** Kivon Systems LLC ("**Kivon**") and Fikoul Orthopedics, Inc. ("**Fikoul**")

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms the parties have discussed for Fikoul's license of Kivon QualiTrace. Except as stated in Section 14 (Binding Provisions), it is not binding. It is intended only as a basis for negotiating definitive agreements.

## 1. Transaction Overview

Kivon will license Kivon QualiTrace, a cloud-hosted electronic quality management system (eQMS), to Fikoul. Kivon will also provide hosting, implementation, data migration, validation support and ongoing support. Fikoul will use the system for device history records, complaint files, nonconformance and CAPA workflows for its orthopedic hip and knee implants. Implementation will begin at Fikoul's manufacturing facility in Warsaw, Indiana.

## 2. Definitive Agreements

The parties expect to sign the following definitive agreements:

(a) a Software License Agreement setting out the general terms of the license, hosted services, data security, warranties, liability and indemnities;

(b) an Order Form specifying the modules, user counts, fees, delivery date, service levels and implementation plan; and

(c) a Quality Agreement allocating quality, validation, change control, data integrity and regulatory support responsibilities, to be signed after go-live.

## 3. Licensed Products and Services

| Item | Proposed Scope |
|---|---|
| Software modules | QualiTrace Core; Complaints Module; NC/CAPA Module |
| Hosted environments | Production, validation and sandbox (U.S. region) |
| Authorized Users | Approximately 450 named users |
| Support | Premier Support tier |
| Data migration | Legacy device history, complaint, nonconformance and CAPA records at the Warsaw, Indiana facility |

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** $386,000 per year, covering the modules, hosted services, Premier Support and up to 450 Authorized Users.

4.2 **Implementation Fees.** One-time fees for configuration, data migration, and validation support and training, estimated at approximately $150,000. The final amount will be stated in the Order Form.

4.3 **Payment.** Subscription fees will be invoiced annually in advance. Undisputed invoices will be payable within 45 days.

## 5. Implementation and Delivery

Kivon will propose a detailed implementation timetable, targeting go-live at the Warsaw, Indiana facility in the second quarter of 2026. The Order Form will fix the delivery date and the acceptance criteria.

## 6. Acceptance

Fikoul will have a period after delivery to test the software against the documentation and the agreed acceptance criteria and to reject it for material nonconformities. Kivon will correct nonconformities and redeliver. If the software repeatedly fails acceptance, Fikoul may terminate and receive a refund of implementation fees and prepaid subscription fees. The definitive agreements will set the length of the acceptance period.

## 7. Term and Renewal

7.1 **Initial Term.** Thirty-six (36) months from the delivery date.

7.2 **Renewal.** One-year renewal terms; either party may decline renewal on 60 days' notice.

7.3 **Renewal Pricing.** Annual increases on renewal will be capped at a percentage to be agreed in the definitive agreements.

## 8. Exclusivity

During the term, Fikoul will use Kivon as its exclusive provider of eQMS software only, at Fikoul's Warsaw, Indiana manufacturing facility only. Fikoul and its affiliates remain free to select any vendor for other software categories and for other facilities.

## 9. Data Security and Regulatory Compliance

9.1 Fikoul will own all customer data. Kivon will host customer data only in U.S. data centers.

9.2 Kivon will maintain an information security program meeting industry standards for regulated cloud systems. The program will include encryption in transit and at rest, 21 C.F.R. Part 11-compliant audit trails, and annual SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent) audits.

9.3 Kivon will give prompt notice of security incidents, cooperate in any notifications, and reimburse Fikoul's related costs where the incident results from Kivon's breach of its security obligations.

9.4 Kivon will support Fikoul's software validation, supplier audits and FDA inspections.

## 10. Service Levels

Kivon will commit to 99.9% monthly availability for production, with service credits for shortfalls. It will also commit to a four-hour recovery point objective and a 24-hour recovery time objective, and to severity-based support response targets.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at fees paid or payable in the 24 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Customary exclusions will apply to the cap and to the waiver of consequential damages. These include indemnification obligations, Kivon's breach of its data security and confidentiality obligations, gross negligence, fraud and willful misconduct, and Fikoul's obligation to pay fees due.

## 12. Indemnities

Kivon will indemnify Fikoul against third-party intellectual property infringement claims and claims arising from security incidents. Fikoul will indemnify Kivon against third-party claims arising from customer data that Fikoul provides in violation of law or third-party rights.

## 13. Governing Law and Forum

The definitive agreements will be governed by the laws of the State of New York. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York will have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 14. Binding Provisions

14.1 **Non-Binding Effect.** This term sheet does not create any legally binding obligation, except for this Section 14. Neither party is obligated to enter into any transaction unless and until the parties sign definitive agreements. Either party may end negotiations at any time without liability.

14.2 **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of this term sheet are confidential. They are subject to the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the parties.

14.3 **Costs.** Each party will bear its own costs of negotiating and preparing the definitive agreements.

14.4 **Governing Law.** This Section 14 is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
