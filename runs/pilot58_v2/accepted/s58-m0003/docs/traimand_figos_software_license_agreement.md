# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Software License Agreement is entered into as of **January 15, 2024** (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Figos Analytics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 410 Marquette Avenue, Suite 1200, Minneapolis, Minnesota 55401 ("**Licensor**"), and **Traimand Seed Genetics, Inc.**, an Iowa corporation with its principal place of business at 2250 Prairie Ridge Road, Ankeny, Iowa 50023 ("**Customer**"). Licensor and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor has developed and operates a proprietary genomic selection software platform that computes genomic estimated breeding values and related analytics from genotypic, phenotypic and pedigree data.

B. Customer conducts corn and soybean breeding programs and wishes to license the platform to support selection decisions in those programs.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Licensor will make the platform and related services available to Customer.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this definition, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Authorized Users**" means Customer's and its Affiliates' employees and individual contractors whom Customer authorizes to access the Platform under Section 2.2.

"**Change of Control**" means, for a Party: (a) any merger, consolidation, share exchange or similar transaction after which the Party's equity holders immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the surviving entity or its parent; (b) the acquisition by any person or group of more than fifty percent (50%) of the Party's voting interests; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the Party's assets.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.4.

"**Customer Data**" means all data Customer provides, including all Derived Data generated from it by the Platform. Customer Data includes all genotypic, phenotypic, pedigree, trial, environmental and germplasm data that Customer or its Authorized Users upload, transmit or otherwise make available to Licensor or the Platform.

"**Derived Data**" means all outputs, results and data generated by or through the Platform from Customer-provided data. Derived Data includes genomic estimated breeding values ("**GEBVs**"), marker-effect estimates, genomic relationship matrices, prediction accuracies, and any statistical or machine-learning models trained or calibrated on that data.

"**Documentation**" means Licensor's user guides, technical specifications and methodology notes for the Platform, as updated from time to time.

"**Fees**" means the subscription fees, professional services fees and other amounts payable under an Order Form or Statement of Work.

"**Order Form**" means an ordering document referencing this Agreement and signed by both Parties that specifies the Platform modules, subscription term, Fees and related commercial terms. The first Order Form is Order Form No. OF-001.

"**Platform**" means Licensor's hosted genomic selection software platform, including the modules identified in an Order Form, the Documentation, and all Updates.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between the Parties covering data handling, data integrity and model validation for the Platform, as amended from time to time.

"**Security Incident**" means any unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction of, Customer Data in Licensor's possession, custody or control.

"**Services**" means implementation, training, data migration, transition and other professional services that Licensor performs under a Statement of Work.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work referencing this Agreement and signed by both Parties that describes Services to be performed by Licensor.

"**Subscription Term**" means the Initial Subscription Term and any Renewal Term, each as described in the applicable Order Form and Section 12.

"**Updates**" means bug fixes, patches, enhancements and new releases of the Platform that Licensor makes generally available to its customers.

**1.2 Agreement.** "**Agreement**" means this Software License Agreement together with each Order Form, each Statement of Work, and the Quality Agreement, each as executed and amended from time to time. References to "this Agreement" include each of those documents.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising this Agreement, the documents control in the following order: the Order Form; then the Quality Agreement (for quality and data-handling matters); then this License Agreement; then SOWs.

**1.4 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this License Agreement unless otherwise stated.

**9.1 Security Program.** Licensor shall maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against Security Incidents. **9.2 Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. Licensor shall promptly investigate, contain and remediate the Security Incident. It shall provide Customer with information that Customer reasonably requests about the incident, and shall bear the reasonable costs of remediation.

**9.3 Data Use, Retention and Deletion.** Licensor shall use, retain, return and delete Customer Data in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Licensor shall not use Customer Data for any purpose other than providing the Platform and Services to Customer, except as the Quality Agreement permits.

**9.4 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**9.5 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, and with no less care than it uses for its own similar information. It shall use the information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement. It shall not disclose the information except to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these. A receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, if it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective treatment.

**9.6 Audit.** Once per contract year, on thirty (30) days' notice, Customer may review Licensor's most recent third-party security audit reports and supporting documentation. Customer may also conduct an audit following a Security Incident.

**12.2 Subscription Term and Renewal.** Each Order Form has an initial subscription term as specified in that Order Form (the "**Initial Subscription Term**"). At the end of the Initial Subscription Term, and at the end of each Renewal Term, the Order Form automatically renews for the renewal period specified in the Order Form (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). It does not renew if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term. Fees for a Renewal Term are as stated in the applicable Order Form.

**12.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form or Statement of Work on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Either Party may also terminate immediately on notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**12.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of an Order Form, Customer's right to access the affected Platform modules ends. Return and deletion of Customer Data is governed by Section 9.3. If Licensor terminates under Section 12.3, Customer shall pay all unpaid Fees for the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term. If Customer terminates under Section 12.3, Licensor shall refund prepaid Fees for the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term.

**12.5 Survival.** Sections 1, 5 (as to accrued amounts), 7, 8.4, 9, 10, 11, 12.4, 12.5, 14 and 15 survive expiration or termination.
