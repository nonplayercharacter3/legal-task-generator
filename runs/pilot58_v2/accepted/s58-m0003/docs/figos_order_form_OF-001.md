## 2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

2.1 **Subscription Start Date:** February 1, 2024.

2.2 **Initial Subscription Term:** 36 months, beginning on the Subscription Start Date.

2.3 **Renewal Term:** The Initial Subscription Term automatically renews for successive Renewal Terms of 24 months each, in accordance with Section 12.2.

2.4 **Renewal Fees:** Subscription Fees for each Renewal Term are charged at Licensor's then-current list pricing for the Platform Modules and user seats subscribed at the time of renewal.

## 3. PLATFORM MODULES AND USAGE

3.1 **Licensed Modules.** Customer subscribes to the following Platform modules (the "**Platform Modules**"):

| **Module** | **Description** | **Crops** |
|---|---|---|
| GEBV Engine | Computation of genomic estimated breeding values using GBLUP, ssGBLUP and Bayesian marker-effect models | Corn, soybean |
| Pedigree and Relationship Module | Pedigree management, numerator and genomic relationship matrices, and parentage verification | Corn, soybean |
| Trial Data Manager | Ingestion, quality control and curation of multi-environment phenotypic trial data | Corn, soybean |
| Cross Planner | Mate allocation and cross prediction using GEBVs and genetic diversity constraints | Corn, soybean |

3.2 **Authorized User Seats:** 40 named Authorized User seats.

3.3 **Usage Allowance.** The subscription includes genotyping data for up to 250,000 genotyped lines per contract year and up to 60,000 SNP markers per line. If Customer exceeds the allowance in any contract year, Licensor will notify Customer. The Parties will then discuss in good faith an increase in the allowance at the rates in Section 4.3 of this Order Form.

3.4 **Hosting Region.** Licensor will host Customer Data in data centers located in the United States.

## 5. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 Licensor will invoice the Annual Subscription Fee for each contract year annually in advance on or about the first day of that contract year.

5.2 Licensor will invoice the onboarding fee on the Subscription Start Date.

5.3 Licensor will invoice additional usage annually in arrears.

5.4 Invoices are payable in accordance with Section 5.2. Payment is due in U.S. dollars by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated on Licensor's invoice.

## 6. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 **Availability.** Licensor will make the Platform available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month. Scheduled maintenance, notified to Customer at least 48 hours in advance and performed outside 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. U.S. Central Time on business days, is excluded.

6.2 **Support.** Licensor will provide email and telephone support from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. U.S. Central Time on business days. For Severity 1 issues (Platform unavailable or GEBV computation failing for all users), Licensor will respond within two hours, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

6.3 **Service Credits.** If availability falls below 99.5% in a calendar month, Customer will receive a credit against the next invoice equal to 5% of the monthly equivalent of the Annual Subscription Fee for each full 0.5% shortfall, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly equivalent.

## SPECIAL TERMS

**1. Breeding Season Change Freeze.** Licensor will not deploy Updates that change GEBV calculation methodology or default model parameters between August 1 and October 31 of any year without Customer's prior written approval. Security patches and bug fixes are not subject to this freeze.

**2. Model Validation.** Before any Update that changes GEBV calculation methodology is deployed to Customer's production environment, Licensor will validate that Update in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

**3. Named Account Scientist.** Licensor will designate a named quantitative geneticist as Customer's account scientist. That person will attend quarterly model review meetings with Customer's breeding informatics team.

**4. Data Security Liability.** Section 11.3(b) shall apply only to Licensor's willful disclosure of Customer Data in breach of Section 9. All other breaches of Section 9 by Licensor are subject to the cap in Section 11.1.

**5. Publicity.** Neither Party will use the other Party's name or logo in any press release, customer list or marketing material without the other Party's prior written consent.
