# QUALITY AGREEMENT

**DairyLedger Traceability and Cold-Chain Platform**

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**QA Effective Date**") by and between:

**TILIL SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"); and

**KUNOUX CREAMERY, INC.**, a Wisconsin corporation ("**Licensee**").

Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to the Software-as-a-Service License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Under the License Agreement, Licensor provides the DairyLedger platform and related Implementation Services to Licensee.

B. Licensee uses the Subscription Services to create and keep records that support its food safety, traceability, organic certification and recall obligations as an organic dairy processor.

C. Section 6.5 of the License Agreement provides that the Quality Agreement sets out how the parties divide responsibility for the regulatory record-keeping and recall support functions of the Subscription Services. Section 8.9 of the License Agreement provides that the Quality Agreement sets the retention periods for trace and cold-chain records.

D. This Quality Agreement is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in the License Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below and in the License Agreement, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1 Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out each party's responsibilities for:

(a) the regulatory record-keeping and recall support functions of the Subscription Services;

(b) the integrity, retention and retrieval of Quality Records;

(c) the validation and documentation of Food Safety Functions;

(d) change control for updates to the Subscription Services that affect Food Safety Functions;

(e) Licensor's support during a Recall Event or a Regulatory Trace Request; and

(f) Licensee's right to audit the Licensor quality management processes that relate to the Subscription Services.

**1.2 Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to the Subscription Services, the Platform Software and the Licensor-supplied hardware listed in Section 7.1 of the Order Form, as used at each Covered Facility at which the Subscription Services are in production use.

**1.3 Relationship to the Agreement.** This Quality Agreement is part of the Agreement. It is governed by the License Agreement. It does not change the allocation of risk or the remedies set out in the License Agreement.

**1.4 Nature of Licensor's Role.** Licensor provides a software service and related hardware and support. Licensor does not manufacture, process, pack, hold or distribute food for Licensee. Licensor does not make food safety decisions on Licensee's behalf, such as decisions on the acceptance or rejection of raw milk, product release, product holds or the initiation or scope of any recall.

## 2. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**2.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the License Agreement or, where the context relates to the Implementation Services, in the SOW. In this Quality Agreement:

"**Applicable Food Laws**" means the federal, state and local laws, regulations and binding guidance that apply to Licensee's dairy processing operations. These include the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and its implementing regulations, including 21 C.F.R. Part 117 and, to the extent applicable to Licensee's products, 21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart S; the Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance as adopted in the states where Licensee operates; the dairy and food regulations of the Wisconsin Department of Agriculture, Trade and Consumer Protection; and the USDA National Organic Program regulations at 7 C.F.R. Part 205.

"**Food Safety Functions**" means the functions of the Subscription Services listed in Schedule 1.

"**Quality Record**" means any record created, stored or generated by the Subscription Services in the operation of a Food Safety Function. This includes farm pickup records, tanker load records, receiving records, laboratory results received through the interfaces described in the SOW, load release and override records, silo and lot genealogy records, cold-chain readings, alerts and corrective action records, organic certification records, consumer-complaint records and the audit trail for each of those records.

"**Quality Representative**" means the person designated by a party under Section 13.1.

"**Recall Event**" means any actual or proposed recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery of a Licensee product, whether initiated by Licensee or requested by a regulator.

"**Regulatory Trace Request**" means any request by a food safety regulator, an organic certifier or a public health authority for traceability, cold-chain or supplier records relating to Licensee products or raw milk.

"**Release**" means any update, upgrade, patch, configuration change or new release of the Platform Software or the hosting environment that Licensor deploys to the production environment of the Subscription Services.

"**Validation Package**" means the documentation described in Section 6.2.

**2.2 Interpretation.** Section 16.17 of the License Agreement applies to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full, with references to "this License Agreement" read as references to this Quality Agreement.

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES

**3.1 Licensee Responsibilities.** In accordance with Section 6.5 of the License Agreement, Licensee is responsible for its compliance with Applicable Food Laws. Licensee is responsible for:

(a) its food safety plan, hazard analysis, preventive controls, sanitation programs and supplier approval program;

(b) deciding which records it must keep under Applicable Food Laws and configuring the Subscription Services to capture them, with Licensor's support under the SOW;

(c) the accuracy of Licensee Data entered by Licensee and its Authorized Users, as provided in Section 6.2 of the License Agreement;

(d) setting temperature alert thresholds, escalation rules, load release test requirements and override roles;

(e) responding to cold-chain alerts, test failures and deviations, and recording corrective actions;

(f) all decisions on raw milk acceptance, product holds, product release and recalls;

(g) communicating with regulators, organic certifiers, customers and consumers about Recall Events and Regulatory Trace Requests; and

(h) maintaining any records required by Applicable Food Laws that the Subscription Services do not capture.

**3.2 Licensor Responsibilities.** In accordance with Section 6.2 of the License Agreement, Licensor is responsible for the accurate processing, storage and reporting of Licensee Data by the Subscription Services in accordance with the Documentation. Licensor is also responsible for:

(a) operating the Food Safety Functions in accordance with the Documentation and the functional specifications described in Section 3.1 of the SOW;

(b) maintaining the integrity, availability and retrievability of Quality Records as described in Sections 4 and 5;

(c) validating Food Safety Functions and maintaining the Validation Package under Section 6;

(d) following the change control procedure in Section 7 for Releases;

(e) providing recall and trace support under Section 8;

(f) maintaining and calibrating Licensor-supplied hardware under Section 11; and

(g) supporting Licensee's audits and regulatory inspections under Section 12.

## 4. QUALITY RECORDS AND RETENTION

**4.1 Retention Periods.** Under Section 8.9 of the License Agreement, Licensor shall keep Quality Records within the Subscription Services, available to Licensee for online retrieval and export, for at least the retention periods in Schedule 2, measured from the date each record is created. These retention periods apply during the Term.

**4.2 No Deletion by Licensor.** During the Term, Licensor shall not delete, archive offline or otherwise remove from online retrieval any Quality Record before the end of its retention period in Schedule 2, unless Licensee instructs it in writing to do so.

**4.3 Records Beyond the Retention Period.** At the end of the retention period for a Quality Record, the record shall remain in the Subscription Services unless Licensee removes it through the functions of the Subscription Services or instructs Licensor in writing to remove it. Licensee may set longer retention periods for any category of Quality Record through the configuration of the Subscription Services.

**4.4 Records Holds.** If Licensee notifies Licensor that a Quality Record, or a category of Quality Records, is subject to a regulatory, litigation or certifier hold, Licensor shall preserve those records in accordance with the notice until Licensee tells Licensor in writing that the hold has ended.

**4.5 Migrated Records.** Historical records migrated into the Subscription Services under Section 2.4 of the SOW are Quality Records. Their retention periods are measured from the original record date shown in the migrated data.

**4.6 End of Term.** On expiration or termination of the Agreement, Section 8.8 of the License Agreement applies to the export and deletion of Quality Records. Licensor shall make Quality Records available for export in a format that keeps lot genealogy and trace linkages, as provided in that Section. Licensee is responsible for keeping exported Quality Records for any remaining period required by Applicable Food Laws.

**4.7 Personal Data.** Where Quality Records contain Personal Data, Licensor shall process that Personal Data in accordance with the DPA. Retention under this Section 4 is a documented instruction of Licensee, as stated in Section 9.4 of the DPA.

## 5. DATA INTEGRITY

**5.1 Audit Trail.** Licensor shall maintain, for each Quality Record, the time-stamped audit trail described in Attachment 1 to the SOW. The audit trail shall record the user, the date and time, the action taken and, for modifications, the prior value. Users shall not be able to edit or delete the audit trail.

**5.2 Time Synchronization.** Licensor shall synchronize the clocks of the Subscription Services, the mobile applications and the Licensor-supplied gateways to a recognized time source. Each record shall show its time-stamp in Central Time, together with the coordinated universal time value.

**5.3 Offline Capture.** Records captured offline under Section A.2 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement shall keep their original time-stamps after synchronization. The audit trail shall show the time of capture and the time of synchronization for each such record.

**5.4 Corrections.** Corrections to Quality Records shall be made only by Authorized Users with a role that permits corrections. Each correction shall require the user to enter a reason. The original value shall remain visible in the audit trail.

**5.5 Load Release Overrides.** Each override of a load release control under Section 3 of Attachment 1 to the SOW shall record the user, the reason entered and the tests outstanding at the time of override. Licensor shall make available a standard report of all overrides by Covered Facility and by period.

**5.6 Electronic Records.** Licensor shall operate the Subscription Services so that Quality Records are attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, and can be produced in human-readable and electronic form on request.

## 6. VALIDATION AND DOCUMENTATION

**6.1 Validation by Licensor.** Licensor shall validate each Food Safety Function before it is first deployed to the production environment for Licensee and after each Release classified as a Category A or Category B change under Section 7.2. Validation shall show that the Food Safety Function performs in accordance with the Documentation and the functional specifications described in Section 3.1 of the SOW.

**6.2 Validation Package.** Licensor shall maintain a Validation Package for the Food Safety Functions. The Validation Package shall include:

(a) a description of each Food Safety Function and its intended use;

(b) the functional requirements traced to test cases;

(c) executed test cases with results, the version tested and the date of testing;

(d) a record of deviations found and how they were resolved; and

(e) a validation summary signed by an authorized Licensor representative.

**6.3 Access to Validation Package.** Licensor shall make the current Validation Package available to Licensee on request, subject to Section 10 of the License Agreement. Licensee may show the Validation Package to regulators and organic certifiers in an inspection or audit, in accordance with Section 10.4 of the License Agreement.

**6.4 Licensee Acceptance Testing.** Licensee's user acceptance testing of the configured Food Safety Functions at each Implementation Site shall take place under Section 6 of the SOW. The recall simulation exercise under Section 2.6(d) of the SOW forms part of that testing for the Trace and Recall module.

**6.5 Documentation.** Licensor shall keep the Documentation for the Food Safety Functions current. Licensor shall update it no later than the date on which a Release that changes a Food Safety Function is deployed.

## 7. CHANGE CONTROL

**7.1 Application.** This Section 7 applies to each Release that affects a Food Safety Function. It supplements Section 5.4 of the License Agreement.

**7.2 Classification.** Licensor shall classify each Release that affects a Food Safety Function as follows:

| Category | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Category A | Changes the logic, calculation, data structure or output of a Food Safety Function | Changes to lot genealogy logic, alert threshold evaluation, load release controls or trace report content |
| Category B | Changes the user interface, workflow or configuration options of a Food Safety Function without changing its logic or output | New screens, changed field labels, new filter options for trace reports |
| Category C | Does not change a Food Safety Function, but is deployed to components that support it | Security patches, performance improvements, infrastructure updates |

**7.3 Notice.** Licensor shall give Licensee's Quality Representative written notice of each Category A Release at least 30 days before deployment and of each Category B Release at least 10 Business Days before deployment. The notice shall include release notes describing the change, the Food Safety Functions affected and a summary of Licensor's validation results. Category C Releases shall be described in the monthly report under Section A.8 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement.

**7.4 Sandbox Access.** Licensor shall make each Category A Release available in the non-production environment at least 15 Business Days before deployment to production, so that Licensee may carry out its own testing if it wishes.

**7.5 Licensee Concerns.** If Licensee reasonably believes that a Category A Release would impair a Food Safety Function or Licensee's ability to meet Applicable Food Laws, Licensee may notify Licensor in writing before deployment. The Quality Representatives shall discuss the concern promptly and in good faith. Licensor shall consider deferring deployment for Licensee, configuring the change off for Licensee, or providing a workaround, where it can do so without affecting the security of the Subscription Services.

**7.6 Emergency Releases.** Licensor may deploy a Release without the notice periods in Section 7.3 where the Release is needed to address a security vulnerability or a Severity 1 incident. Licensor shall notify Licensee's Quality Representative as soon as practicable, and in any event within one Business Day after deployment, and shall complete any validation required by Section 6.1 within 10 Business Days after deployment.

**7.7 Timing.** Licensor shall not deploy a Category A Release during a Recall Event notified under Section 8.1, unless the Release is an emergency Release under Section 7.6 or Licensee agrees.

**7.8 Licensee Configuration Changes.** Licensee shall document changes it makes to thresholds, escalation rules, load release requirements and user roles for Food Safety Functions through its own change control procedures. The audit trail of the Subscription Services shall record each such change.

## 8. RECALL AND REGULATORY TRACE SUPPORT

**8.1 Notice.** Licensee may notify Licensor of a Recall Event or a Regulatory Trace Request by telephone to Licensor's 24 x 7 support line, followed by a support ticket. The notice shall identify the products, lots, farm suppliers or periods concerned, to the extent known.

**8.2 Severity.** Licensor shall treat each notice under Section 8.1 as a Severity 1 incident for purposes of Section 5.2 of, and Section A.5 of Exhibit A to, the License Agreement. Licensor shall respond within 30 minutes, on a 24 x 7 basis.

**8.3 Recall Support Coordinator.** Within two hours after receiving a notice under Section 8.1, Licensor shall assign a named support engineer who is familiar with Licensee's configuration to act as recall support coordinator. The coordinator shall remain available to Licensee until Licensee confirms that support is no longer needed.

**8.4 Support Provided.** During a Recall Event or Regulatory Trace Request, Licensor shall:

(a) confirm that the Trace and Recall module and the underlying Quality Records are available and complete, including records captured offline and awaiting synchronization;

(b) help Licensee run trace-back and trace-forward reports for the products, lots or suppliers concerned, and export them in PDF and CSV formats;

(c) help Licensee prepare an electronic sortable spreadsheet of relevant traceability records within 24 hours after Licensee's request, where a regulator requires one;

(d) produce, on request, the audit trail for any Quality Record relevant to the Recall Event or Regulatory Trace Request;

(e) investigate and report promptly on any apparent gap, inconsistency or error in the Quality Records; and

(f) make Licensor personnel available to explain the operation of the Subscription Services to Licensee's recall team.

**8.5 Licensee Decisions.** Licensee shall make all decisions about the Recall Event or the response to the Regulatory Trace Request. Licensor shall not contact any regulator, certifier, customer or consumer about a Recall Event or Regulatory Trace Request unless Licensee asks it to or the law requires it to.

**8.6 Continuity of Functions.** Sections 3.3 and 7.5 of the License Agreement apply to the recall and regulatory trace functions of the Subscription Services.

**8.7 Mock Recalls.** After the Implementation Services are complete, Licensee may carry out up to two mock recall exercises each Subscription Year using the Subscription Services. Licensor's customer success manager shall coordinate Licensor's support for those exercises, as described in Section 10.2(c) of the Order Form. Licensee shall give at least 10 Business Days' notice of a scheduled exercise. Licensee may also carry out unannounced exercises, in which case Licensor shall provide support through its standard support channels.

**8.8 Post-Event Review.** Within 15 Business Days after Licensee confirms that a Recall Event or Regulatory Trace Request has closed, the Quality Representatives shall meet to review the performance of the Subscription Services. Licensor shall prepare a written summary of any incidents, gaps or improvements identified, and of the actions Licensor will take.

**8.9 Fees.** The support described in this Section 8 forms part of the support included in the Subscription Fees under Section 2.4(c) of the Order Form.

## 9. COLD-CHAIN ALERTS AND DEVIATIONS

**9.1 Alert Delivery.** Licensor shall deliver cold-chain alerts by on-screen notification, email and SMS, and shall escalate unacknowledged alerts, in accordance with the escalation rules configured by Licensee and Section 5 of Attachment 1 to the SOW.

**9.2 Alert Delivery Failures.** If Licensor becomes aware that alerts for any Covered Facility have not been generated or delivered as configured, Licensor shall treat the failure as a Severity 1 incident and shall notify Licensee's Quality Representative and designated support contacts. Licensor shall identify, in the root cause analysis under Section A.9 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement, the alerts affected and the period concerned.

**9.3 Sensor Faults.** The Subscription Services shall flag readings from a Licensor-supplied sensor or probe that has stopped reporting or is reporting values outside its valid range. Licensee shall decide how to treat affected product or raw milk.

**9.4 Corrective Actions.** Licensee shall record its corrective actions for cold-chain deviations in the Subscription Services. Licensor shall provide standard reports of deviations and corrective actions by Covered Facility and by period.

## 10. SUPPLIER ORGANIC CERTIFICATION

**10.1 Licensee Responsibility.** Licensee is responsible for keeping the organic certification status and expiry date of each farm supplier current in the Subscription Services and for its supplier approval decisions.

**10.2 Licensor Responsibility.** Licensor is responsible for the alerts and pickup flags described in Section 7 of Attachment 1 to the SOW operating as configured.

**10.3 Certifier Inspections.** Licensor shall support Licensee in producing organic certification, pickup and lot records for inspection by Licensee's organic certifier, in the same way as for a Regulatory Trace Request under Section 8.4(b) and (d).

## 11. CALIBRATION AND HARDWARE

**11.1 Calibration Certificates.** Licensor shall supply a certificate of calibration for each sensor and probe on delivery, as required by Section 7.6 of the Order Form. Licensor shall upload each certificate to the Subscription Services and link it to the relevant device record.

**11.2 Recalibration.** Licensor shall recalibrate or replace each sensor and probe in accordance with Section 7.6 of the Order Form. Licensor shall record the date, result and certificate of each recalibration in the Subscription Services.

**11.3 Out-of-Tolerance Findings.** If a sensor or probe is found out of tolerance at recalibration or when returned for repair, Licensor shall notify Licensee's Quality Representative within five Business Days. The notice shall state the device, its location or tanker assignment, the deviation found and the date of the last in-tolerance calibration, so that Licensee can assess the effect on affected records.

**11.4 Repair and Replacement.** Repair and replacement of hardware are governed by Section 7.4 of the Order Form.

**11.5 Licensee Instruments.** Licensee is responsible for calibrating its own instruments, scales and plant historian sensors whose readings are received through the interfaces described in Section 2.3 of the SOW.

## 12. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

**12.1 Quality Audits.** Licensee may audit the Licensor quality management processes that relate to the Food Safety Functions. This includes Licensor's validation, change control, release management, calibration and incident management processes. Audits shall take place no more than once in any 12-month period, except following a Severity 1 incident affecting a Food Safety Function or at a regulator's request.

**12.2 Conduct of Audits.** Licensee shall give at least 30 days' written notice of an audit, or such shorter notice as a regulator requires. Audits may be carried out by Licensee or by an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations. They shall take place during normal business hours, may be held remotely or at Licensor's offices, and shall not unreasonably interfere with Licensor's operations or compromise the security of other customers' data. Each party shall bear its own costs of an audit.

**12.3 Findings.** Licensee shall give Licensor a written summary of any findings within 20 Business Days after the audit. Licensor shall respond within 20 Business Days after receiving the findings with a corrective action plan, including target dates, for each finding that Licensor accepts. The Quality Representatives shall discuss any finding that Licensor does not accept.

**12.4 Coordination with DPA Audits.** Where practicable, the parties shall coordinate audits under this Section 12 with audits under Section 7.4 of the DPA, so as to avoid duplication.

**12.5 Regulatory Inspections.** If a regulator or organic certifier inspects Licensee and asks about the Subscription Services, Licensor shall provide reasonable support. This may include supplying the Validation Package, Documentation and relevant audit trail records, and making a knowledgeable representative available by video conference during the inspection on reasonable notice. Licensor shall notify Licensee promptly if a regulator contacts Licensor directly about Licensee's records, unless the law prohibits notice.

**12.6 Regulator Accounts.** Licensee may provide read-only auditor and regulator accounts within the entitlement in Section 5.1 of the Order Form, and may disclose Quality Records to regulators as permitted by Section 10.4 of the License Agreement.

## 13. QUALITY GOVERNANCE

**13.1 Quality Representatives.** Each party shall designate a Quality Representative, as stated in Schedule 3. Either party may change its Quality Representative by written notice to the other party's Quality Representative.

**13.2 Quality Reviews.** The Quality Representatives shall meet at least once each calendar quarter, which may be in conjunction with the quarterly business review under Section 10.2(b) of the Order Form. Each review shall cover:

(a) Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents affecting Food Safety Functions, and their root cause analyses;

(b) Releases deployed and planned, by category;

(c) cold-chain alert delivery performance;

(d) calibration status of Licensor-supplied hardware;

(e) open audit findings and corrective action plans; and

(f) any Recall Events, Regulatory Trace Requests and mock recall exercises in the period.

**13.3 Escalation.** Any matter under this Quality Agreement that the Quality Representatives cannot resolve within 10 Business Days shall be escalated to Licensor's Vice President of Customer Delivery and Licensee's Vice President of Quality Assurance. Matters they cannot resolve shall be referred to executive negotiation under Section 16.2 of the License Agreement.

**13.4 Review of Quality Agreement.** The Quality Representatives shall review this Quality Agreement at least once each Subscription Year, and following any change in Applicable Food Laws that affects the Food Safety Functions. Any changes agreed shall be made by amendment under Section 16.11 of the License Agreement.

Routine communications, notices under Sections 7.3, 9.2 and 11.3, and notices under Section 8.1 may be given to the Quality Representatives and through the support channels described in Section A.6 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement.

Sections 16.2 and 16.3 of the License Agreement apply to any dispute arising out of or relating to this Quality Agreement.

**14.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Quality Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions of this Quality Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.
