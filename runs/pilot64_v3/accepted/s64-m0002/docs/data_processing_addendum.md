# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**DairyLedger Traceability and Cold-Chain Platform**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between:

**TILIL SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"); and

**KUNOUX CREAMERY, INC.**, a Wisconsin corporation ("**Licensee**").

Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to the Software-as-a-Service License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Under the License Agreement, Licensor provides the DairyLedger platform and related Implementation Services to Licensee.

B. In providing the Subscription Services and the Implementation Services, Licensor will process certain personal data contained in the Licensee Data on behalf of Licensee. This includes contact and identification details of farm suppliers, records of milk haulers and drivers, and consumer-complaint records.

C. This DPA is the "DPA" referred to in the License Agreement. It sets out the terms on which Licensor processes that personal data.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below and in the License Agreement, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the License Agreement. In this DPA:

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, each as amended or replaced from time to time. This includes the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations, other comprehensive state consumer privacy laws, state data breach notification laws and state data security laws, in each case to the extent applicable.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates. This includes farm suppliers who are individuals or sole proprietors, owners and employees of farm suppliers, haulers, drivers, consumers who submit complaints, and Licensee personnel who are Authorized Users.

"**Data Subject Request**" means a request by a Data Subject to exercise a right under Data Protection Laws. This includes a request to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of Personal Data, or to opt out of or limit any processing.

"**Personal Data**" means any Licensee Data that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with a Data Subject, and that is "personal information," "personal data" or an equivalent term under Data Protection Laws.

"**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

"**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data that Licensor or any Subprocessor transmits, stores or otherwise Processes.

"**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Licensor, or by another Subprocessor, that Processes Personal Data on behalf of Licensee in connection with the Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 16.17 of the License Agreement applies to this DPA as if set out in full, with references to "this License Agreement" read as references to this DPA.

**1.3 Incorporation.** This DPA is incorporated into the Agreement in accordance with Section 8.3 of the License Agreement and forms part of it.

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

**2.1 Roles of the Parties.** As between the parties, Licensee is the controller or "business" of the Personal Data, and Licensor is the processor or "service provider" Processing Personal Data on behalf of Licensee. Where Licensee Processes Personal Data on behalf of an Affiliate, Licensor is a Subprocessor of that Affiliate's data, and Licensee shall be the sole point of contact with Licensor for that Affiliate.

**2.2 Scope of Processing.** The subject matter, nature and purpose of the Processing, the categories of Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects are described in Schedule 1. The duration of the Processing is the Term, together with any transition period under Section 14.6 of the License Agreement and the period during which Licensor retains Licensee Data under Section 8.8 of the License Agreement.

**2.3 Licensee Instructions.** Licensor shall Process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Licensee. The Agreement, including this DPA, and Licensee's configuration and use of the Subscription Services are Licensee's complete documented instructions as of the DPA Effective Date. Licensee may give additional written instructions consistent with the Agreement. Licensor shall promptly inform Licensee if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws, and Licensor may suspend Processing in accordance with that instruction until Licensee confirms or modifies it.

**2.4 Licensee Obligations.** Licensee shall ensure that its instructions comply with Data Protection Laws. Licensee shall provide any notices to Data Subjects, and obtain any consents, required under Data Protection Laws for the Processing described in this DPA, including the collection of location data from drivers and haulers through the mobile applications.

## 3. LICENSOR OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Restrictions on Processing.** Licensor shall not:

(a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined under Data Protection Laws;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than providing the Subscription Services and the Implementation Services and performing its other obligations under the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose other than those services;

(c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Licensor and Licensee; or

(d) combine Personal Data with personal data that Licensor receives from or on behalf of any other person, or collects from its own interactions with a Data Subject, except as permitted by Data Protection Laws for service providers.

**3.2 Aggregated Data.** Licensor's compilation of statistical and performance information under Section 8.2 of the License Agreement shall not include Personal Data. Licensor shall not attempt to re-identify any Data Subject from that information.

**3.3 Compliance.** Licensor shall comply with the obligations that apply to it as a processor or service provider under Data Protection Laws. It shall provide the same level of privacy protection as those laws require. Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws or this DPA. Upon that notice, Licensee may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorized Processing.

**3.4 Certification.** Licensor certifies that it understands the restrictions in Section 3.1 and will comply with them.

**3.5 Personnel.** Licensor shall ensure that each person authorized to Process Personal Data is subject to a written duty of confidentiality, has received appropriate training on data protection and security, and accesses Personal Data only as necessary to perform the Agreement.

**3.6 Data Location.** Licensor shall Process and store Personal Data only in the United States, in accordance with Section 8.6 of the License Agreement, unless Licensee agrees otherwise in writing. Remote access to Personal Data by Licensor personnel or Subprocessors located outside the United States requires Licensee's prior written approval.

## 4. SUBPROCESSORS

**4.1 Authorization.** Licensee authorizes Licensor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3. Licensee also gives a general authorization for Licensor to engage further Subprocessors, subject to this Section 4 and Section 15.2 of the License Agreement.

**4.2 Notice of New Subprocessors.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least 30 days' written notice before a new Subprocessor begins to Process Personal Data. The notice shall state the Subprocessor's name, location, and the nature of the Processing it will perform.

**4.3 Objection.** Licensee may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by written notice within 20 days after receiving Licensor's notice. The parties shall discuss Licensee's objection in good faith. If Licensor cannot reasonably accommodate the objection within 30 days after Licensee's notice, Licensee may terminate the affected Subscription Services by written notice, and Licensor shall refund any prepaid Subscription Fees attributable to the terminated Subscription Services for the period after termination.

**4.4 Subprocessor Terms.** Licensor shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective of Personal Data than those in this DPA. Licensor remains responsible for the performance of each Subprocessor as provided in Section 15.2 of the License Agreement.

**4.5 Emergency Replacement.** Where Licensor must replace a Subprocessor urgently for reasons outside its reasonable control, such as the Subprocessor's insolvency or a security event affecting it, Licensor may engage a replacement Subprocessor without the advance notice in Section 4.2. Licensor shall notify Licensee of the replacement as soon as practicable, and Section 4.3 shall then apply.

## 5. SECURITY

**5.1 Security Measures.** Licensor shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Schedule 2. These measures are designed to protect Personal Data against Security Incidents, having regard to the nature of the Personal Data and the risks of the Processing. They supplement Licensor's obligations under Section 8.4 of the License Agreement.

**5.2 Updates.** Licensor may update the measures in Schedule 2 from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall level of protection of Personal Data.

**5.3 Licensee Responsibilities.** Licensee is responsible for its use of the security features of the Subscription Services, including administering user roles, enforcing single sign-on for its employees, and controlling the use of Personal Data in the non-production environment as described in Section 11.2 of the Order Form.

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

**6.1 Notification.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident, consistent with Section 8.5 of the License Agreement. Notice shall be given to Licensee's Legal Department and to its designated support contacts.

**6.2 Content of Notice.** To the extent the information is available, the notice shall describe:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;

(b) the likely consequences of the Security Incident;

(c) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate the Security Incident; and

(d) a contact point at Licensor for further information.

Where Licensor cannot provide all of this information at once, it shall provide it in phases as it becomes available.

**6.3 Cooperation.** Licensor shall cooperate with Licensee and take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Licensor shall provide information and assistance reasonably requested by Licensee to meet its notification obligations to Data Subjects, regulators and other persons under Data Protection Laws.

**6.4 Notifications to Third Parties.** Licensor shall not notify any Data Subject, regulator or other third party of a Security Incident affecting Personal Data without Licensee's prior written approval, except where Licensor is required by law to do so.

**6.5 No Acknowledgment of Fault.** Licensor's notification of or response to a Security Incident is not an acknowledgment by Licensor of any fault or liability for the Security Incident.

## 7. ASSISTANCE, AUDITS AND RECORDS

**7.1 Data Subject Requests.** Licensor shall promptly, and in any event within five Business Days, forward to Licensee any Data Subject Request that Licensor receives relating to Personal Data. Licensor shall not respond to the request except to direct the Data Subject to Licensee. Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Licensor shall assist Licensee by appropriate technical and organizational measures in responding to Data Subject Requests, including through the export, correction and deletion functions of the Subscription Services.

**7.2 Assessments.** Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance to Licensee, on request, with any data protection or risk assessment that Licensee is required to carry out under Data Protection Laws in relation to the Processing.

**7.3 Audit Reports.** Licensor shall make available to Licensee, on request and subject to Section 10 of the License Agreement, its most recent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment under Section 8.4 of the License Agreement, together with a summary of any material findings and Licensor's remediation of them.

**7.4 Audits.** If the information provided under Section 7.3 is not sufficient to demonstrate Licensor's compliance with this DPA, or if a Security Incident has occurred, or if a regulator requires it, Licensee may audit Licensor's compliance with this DPA. Audits shall take place no more than once in any 12-month period, except following a Security Incident or at a regulator's request. Licensee shall give at least 30 days' written notice, or such shorter notice as a regulator requires. Audits may be carried out by Licensee or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations. They shall take place during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with Licensor's operations or compromise the security of other customers' data. Each party shall bear its own costs of an audit.

**7.5 Records.** Licensor shall maintain records of its Processing of Personal Data as required by Data Protection Laws and shall make them available to Licensee on request.

**7.6 Regulatory Inquiries.** Licensor shall notify Licensee promptly of any inquiry, complaint or investigation by a regulator relating to the Processing of Personal Data, unless prohibited by law, and shall cooperate with Licensee in responding to it.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Licensee Indemnity.** Licensee shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Licensor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and Subprocessors from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, settlements, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to Licensee Data or Licensee's processing instructions under this DPA, including any claim by a Data Subject, regulator or other third party.

**8.2 Application of Limitations.** The obligations of Licensee under Section 8.1 shall not be subject to any limitation of liability in the Agreement, including Section 12 of the License Agreement.

**8.3 Licensor Liability.** Licensor's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to Section 12 of the License Agreement.

**8.4 Procedure.** The procedure in Section 13.5 of the License Agreement applies to any claim for which indemnification is sought under Section 8.1.

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION

**9.1 Return.** Upon expiration or termination of the Agreement, Licensor shall make Personal Data available for export in accordance with Section 8.8 of the License Agreement.

**9.2 Deletion.** After the 60-day period described in Section 8.8 of the License Agreement, Licensor shall delete Personal Data from its production systems, and from its backups in accordance with its standard backup rotation, and shall require each Subprocessor to do the same. Licensor shall certify deletion in writing on Licensee's request.

**9.3 Retained Data.** To the extent Licensor is required by law to retain any Personal Data, or retains Personal Data in backups pending deletion, Licensor shall continue to protect that Personal Data in accordance with this DPA, shall Process it only for the purpose of that retention, and shall delete it when the retention period ends.

**9.4 Records Under the Quality Agreement.** Licensor's retention of trace and cold-chain records during the Term under Section 8.9 of the License Agreement and the Quality Agreement is a documented instruction of Licensee for purposes of Section 2.3.

## 10. TERM AND GENERAL

**10.1 Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date. It continues for as long as Licensor or any Subprocessor Processes Personal Data in connection with the Agreement.

**10.2 Changes in Data Protection Laws.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires a change to this DPA, the parties shall negotiate in good faith an amendment to give effect to that requirement. Any amendment shall be made in accordance with Section 16.11 of the License Agreement.

**10.3 Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 16.5 of the License Agreement, except that notices of Security Incidents shall also be given as stated in Section 6.1.

**10.4 Governing Law and Disputes.** This DPA, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware in accordance with Section 16.1 of the License Agreement. Sections 16.2 and 16.3 of the License Agreement apply to any dispute arising out of or relating to this DPA.

**10.5 Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions of this DPA shall continue in full force and effect.

**10.6 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature in accordance with Section 16.18 of the License Agreement.

## SCHEDULE 1 — DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**1. Subject Matter.** The Processing of Personal Data contained in the Licensee Data by Licensor in providing the Subscription Services and the Implementation Services under the Agreement.

**2. Nature and Purpose of Processing.** Hosting, storage, backup and recovery; recording and tracking of farm pickups, tanker loads, plant receiving, silo and lot genealogy and cold-chain readings; generation of trace-back, trace-forward and recall reports; tracking of organic certification status; recording and tracking of consumer complaints; supplier and hauler onboarding and support; data migration from Licensee's legacy records; operation of the interfaces described in the SOW; technical support; and the other activities needed to perform the Agreement.

**3. Categories of Data Subjects.**

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Farm suppliers | Individuals and sole proprietors that supply raw milk to Licensee, and owners, managers and employees of farm suppliers |
| Haulers and drivers | Milk haulers, tanker drivers and their dispatch personnel |
| Consumers | Individuals who submit complaints or inquiries about Licensee products |
| Licensee personnel | Employees, contractors and agents of Licensee and its Affiliates who are Authorized Users |
| Regulator and auditor users | Individuals holding read-only auditor and regulator accounts |

**4. Categories of Personal Data.**

| Data Subject Category | Personal Data |
|---|---|
| Farm suppliers | Name, farm name, business and mailing address, telephone number, email address, supplier identifier, bulk tank identifier, organic certification number and status, pickup volumes, quality test results by supplier, pricing and premium data, and mobile application login credentials |
| Haulers and drivers | Name, employer, telephone number, email address, driver identifier, tanker assignments, route schedules, pickup records, GPS location at the time of recording pickups, time-stamps, and mobile application login credentials |
| Consumers | Name, address, telephone number, email address, product and lot purchased, place and date of purchase, description of complaint, any health-related information the consumer chooses to provide, and investigation notes |
| Licensee personnel | Name, job title, work location, work email and telephone number, user role, single sign-on identifier, and audit trail records of activity in the Subscription Services |
| Regulator and auditor users | Name, organization, work email address and access logs |

**5. Sensitive Personal Data.** Licensee does not intend to submit sensitive personal data to the Subscription Services, other than (a) precise geolocation of drivers collected at the time of recording a farm pickup, and (b) any health-related information that a consumer voluntarily includes in a complaint. Licensee shall configure the consumer complaint module to limit free-text collection of health information to what is needed for the investigation of a complaint.

**6. Frequency.** Continuous, for the duration described in Section 2.2 of this DPA.

## SCHEDULE 2 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL SECURITY MEASURES

**1. Governance.** Licensor maintains a written information security program approved by its senior management, with a designated security officer responsible for its implementation. The program is reviewed at least annually.

**2. Independent Assessment.** Licensor maintains a SOC 2 Type II report covering the security, availability and confidentiality trust services criteria for the Subscription Services, renewed annually.

**3. Access Control.** Access to Personal Data is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed at least quarterly. Licensor personnel with administrative access to production systems authenticate with multi-factor authentication. Access rights are removed within one Business Day after a person leaves Licensor or no longer needs access.

**4. Authentication for Users.** The Subscription Services support single sign-on using SAML 2.0 for Licensee employees and DairyLedger-managed credentials with multi-factor authentication for farm suppliers, haulers and drivers, as described in Section 11.3 of the Order Form.

**5. Encryption.** Personal Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard. Encryption keys are managed through a dedicated key management service, with access to keys restricted and logged. Data stored locally on mobile devices and Licensor-supplied gateways for offline capture is encrypted at rest.

**6. Network Security.** Production environments are segregated from corporate and non-production networks. Licensor uses firewalls, web application firewalls and intrusion detection to protect the Subscription Services, and monitors security events continuously.

**7. Vulnerability Management.** Licensor performs automated vulnerability scanning at least weekly and commissions an independent penetration test of the Subscription Services at least annually. Critical vulnerabilities are remediated within 15 days and high vulnerabilities within 30 days after identification, or mitigated by compensating controls within those periods.

**8. Secure Development.** Licensor follows a secure software development lifecycle, including code review, static analysis and dependency scanning before release, and separation of development, test and production environments.

**9. Logging and Monitoring.** Licensor logs administrative and user access to Personal Data and retains security logs for at least 12 months. The Subscription Services maintain a time-stamped audit trail of record creation, modification and deletion that cannot be edited by users.

**10. Backup and Recovery.** Personal Data is backed up and recoverable in accordance with Section A.7 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement. Backups are encrypted and stored in a geographically separate data center in the United States.

**11. Physical Security.** Personal Data is hosted in data centers operated by the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3 that maintain SOC 2 Type II or ISO/IEC 27001 attestations, with controlled entry, video surveillance and environmental protections.

**12. Personnel Security.** Licensor conducts background checks on personnel with access to Personal Data, to the extent permitted by law, and requires annual security and privacy training.

**13. Incident Response.** Licensor maintains a documented incident response plan, tested at least annually, that supports the notification and cooperation obligations in Section 6 of this DPA.

**14. Device Security.** Licensor-supplied gateways and sensors receive firmware updates remotely under Section 7.5 of the Order Form. Gateways authenticate to the Subscription Services using device certificates, and lost or stolen devices can be revoked remotely.
