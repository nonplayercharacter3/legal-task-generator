## 4. SUBSCRIBED MODULES

**4.1 Modules.** Licensee subscribes to the following modules of the DairyLedger platform:

| Module Code | Module | Description |
|---|---|---|
| DL-FP | Farm Pickup | Mobile capture of farm pickups, bulk tank identifiers, tank temperatures, volumes, sample identifiers and GPS location, with offline capture |
| DL-TT | Tanker Tracking | Linking of tanker loads to farm pickups and recording of in-transit temperatures from Licensor-supplied sensors |
| DL-RCV | Plant Receiving | Receiving time, scale weights, receiving temperatures and laboratory results, with load release controls and authorized overrides |
| DL-LOT | Silo and Lot Genealogy | Silo fill and draw tracking, lot code assignment and finished product lot genealogy |
| DL-CC | Cold-Chain Monitoring | Temperature threshold configuration, alerting by on-screen notification, email and SMS, escalation rules and corrective action logging |
| DL-TRC | Trace and Recall | Trace-back, trace-forward and recall reporting, with PDF and CSV export |
| DL-ORG | Organic Certification | Supplier organic certification tracking, expiry alerts and lapsed-certification pickup flags |
| DL-CMP | Consumer Complaints | Consumer complaint recording, lot linkage and investigation tracking |
| DL-API | Integration Services | Access to the DairyLedger application programming interfaces and standard connectors used for the interfaces described in the SOW |
| DL-RPT | Reporting and Analytics | Standard operational dashboards, scheduled reports and report builder |

**4.2 Functional Specifications.** The functional specifications for the modules listed in Section 4.1 are set out in the Documentation and in Attachment 1 to the SOW, as provided in Section 3.1 of the SOW.

**4.3 Platform Features.** Each module includes the platform features made generally available to DairyLedger subscribers, including role-based access control, single sign-on using SAML 2.0, a time-stamped audit trail that cannot be edited by users, and English and Spanish user interfaces for the mobile applications.

## 6. OVERAGES

**6.1 Overage Rates.** If Licensee's use exceeds an entitlement in Section 5 and the parties do not agree on an amendment within the period in Section 3.2 of the License Agreement, Licensor may invoice the excess use at the following rates:

| Category | Overage Rate |
|---|---|
| Named plant and office users | $480 per user per Subscription Year, prorated monthly |
| Farm supplier users | $60 per user per Subscription Year, prorated monthly |
| Hauler and driver users | $90 per user per Subscription Year, prorated monthly |
| Raw milk received | $0.00025 per pound in excess of the annual entitlement |
| Tanker loads recorded | $1.50 per load in excess of the annual entitlement |
| Monitored temperature points | $180 per point per Subscription Year, prorated monthly |
| API calls | $40 per additional 100,000 calls in the relevant calendar month |

**6.2 Invoicing of Overages.** Overages for user, temperature point and storage entitlements will be invoiced quarterly in arrears. Overages for raw milk and tanker load entitlements will be invoiced annually in arrears following the end of each Subscription Year. API call overages will be invoiced quarterly in arrears.

**6.3 No Penalty for Temporary Peaks.** Excess use of the API call entitlement in any calendar month during the Peak Season, as that term is used in the License Agreement as amended from time to time, or in any calendar month in which Licensee is responding to a product recall or a regulatory trace request, will not be invoiced as an overage if Licensee's total API calls over the relevant Subscription Year do not exceed twelve times the monthly entitlement.

## 7. HARDWARE

**7.1 Licensor-Supplied Hardware.** Licensor shall supply the following hardware for deployment under the SOW:

| Item | Description | Quantity | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| HW-TS | In-tanker temperature sensor with cellular backhaul, mounted to tanker manway or outlet valve | 48 | Assigned to tankers serving the Implementation Sites |
| HW-SS | In-silo temperature probe with wireless transmitter | 28 | Eau Claire Plant: 16; Sheboygan Plant: 12 |
| HW-GW | Plant gateway with local storage and battery backup for offline capture | 6 | Eau Claire Plant: 3; Sheboygan Plant: 3 |
| HW-SP | Spare sensor kit (4 in-tanker sensors and 2 in-silo probes) | 2 | One per Implementation Site |

**7.2 Charges.** The hardware listed in Section 7.1, including delivery to the Implementation Sites, is supplied for the Subscription Term at no charge beyond the Annual Subscription Fee and the Implementation Fees. Configuration and commissioning of the hardware are part of the Implementation Services under Section 2.2(e) of the SOW.

**7.3 Title and Risk.** Title to the hardware remains with Licensor. Risk of loss of or damage to the hardware passes to Licensee on delivery to the relevant Covered Facility, except for loss or damage caused by Licensor or its personnel. Licensee shall keep the hardware free of liens and shall not remove the identification labels.

**7.4 Replacement and Additional Hardware.** Licensor shall repair or replace, at no charge, any hardware that fails during the Subscription Term because of a defect in materials or workmanship or ordinary wear, within five Business Days after notice, using the spare sensor kits where available. Hardware that is lost, or damaged by misuse, accident or tanker collision, will be replaced at the following prices:

| Item | Replacement or Additional Unit Price |
|---|---|
| HW-TS | $640 |
| HW-SS | $520 |
| HW-GW | $1,850 |

The same prices apply to additional hardware ordered by Licensee during the Initial Term. Additional hardware is subject to the temperature point entitlement in Section 5.2.

**7.5 Firmware.** Licensor shall maintain and update the firmware on the hardware remotely as part of the Subscription Services. Licensee shall not modify or replace the firmware.

**7.6 Calibration.** Licensor shall deliver each sensor and probe with a certificate of calibration traceable to a recognized national standard. Licensor shall recalibrate or replace each sensor and probe at least once every 24 months during the Subscription Term at no charge, on a schedule agreed with Licensee to avoid disruption to milk receiving.

**7.7 Return.** Within 60 days after the end of the Subscription Term, Licensee shall make the hardware available for collection by Licensor at the Covered Facilities where it is located. Licensor shall bear the cost of collection. Licensee shall not be charged for normal wear of returned hardware.

**7.8 Cellular Connectivity.** The cellular data plans for the in-tanker sensors are included in the Annual Subscription Fee for the quantities in Section 7.1, and for additional sensors ordered under Section 7.4.
