# Kunoux Creamery Selects Tilil Systems' DairyLedger Platform for Farm-to-Plant Milk Traceability and Cold-Chain Monitoring

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

*Organic dairy processor to replace paper pickup tickets and spreadsheet receiving logs at its Eau Claire and Sheboygan, Wisconsin plants with a hosted traceability system linking every finished product lot to the farms that supplied it*

## 1. Announcement

**EAU CLAIRE, Wis. and WILMINGTON, Del., March 10, 2026.** Kunoux Creamery, Inc. ("Kunoux"), an organic dairy processor, and Tilil Systems, Inc. ("Tilil"), a provider of supply chain traceability software for the dairy industry, today announced a five-year subscription agreement. Under the agreement, Kunoux will deploy Tilil's DairyLedger traceability and cold-chain platform at its processing plants in Eau Claire and Sheboygan, Wisconsin.

The agreement took effect on March 1, 2026. Implementation work has started at the Eau Claire plant, with project kickoff scheduled for this month.

DairyLedger is a cloud-based software service. It records raw milk from the moment it is picked up from a farm's bulk tank, through tanker transport, plant receiving, silo storage and processing, to the finished product lot. It monitors temperatures throughout that journey and alerts plant teams when a reading falls outside the limits they set. When a question arises about a product, DairyLedger can produce a trace-back report showing every farm, tanker load and silo behind a finished lot, or a trace-forward report showing every lot that contains milk from a given farm or load.

At Kunoux, DairyLedger will replace the paper pickup tickets and spreadsheet-based receiving logs now used at the two Wisconsin plants that receive raw milk directly from the company's farm supplier network.

## 2. Why Kunoux Is Making the Change

Kunoux buys organic milk from family farms across Wisconsin. The milk is collected by haulers and processed into fluid milk, cultured products and cheese. Each pickup produces a set of records: the farm, the bulk tank, the volume, the tank temperature, the sample taken for testing, and the driver and tanker that collected it. Each tanker load combines milk from several farms. Each silo at the plant combines milk from several tanker loads. Each finished product lot is drawn from one or more silos.

Today much of that chain is held on paper tickets carried in tanker cabs and in spreadsheets kept at each plant. The records are complete, but connecting them takes time. In a mock recall, the Kunoux quality assurance team has to bring together pickup tickets, receiving logs, laboratory results and production records by hand to work out which farms and loads stand behind a lot.

"Our farmers produce milk to the highest standards, and our customers expect us to be able to show where every carton and every wheel of cheese came from," said Helena Brandvold, Chief Executive Officer of Kunoux Creamery. "Our people have always been able to answer that question. With DairyLedger they will be able to answer it in minutes rather than hours, from a single system, at any time of day. That matters for food safety, it matters for our organic certification, and it matters for the trust our customers place in our brand."

The project grew out of a review that Kunoux's operations, quality assurance and plant systems teams began in 2025. The review looked at how the company could strengthen traceability ahead of expanding regulatory expectations for food traceability records, and how it could reduce manual work at the receiving bays during the spring flush, when milk volumes peak.

## 3. The DairyLedger Platform

Kunoux has subscribed to ten DairyLedger modules:

1. **Farm Pickup.** Drivers record each pickup on a mobile application, including the farm, bulk tank identifier, tank temperature, volume and sample identifier. The application captures location at the time of recording.
2. **Tanker Tracking.** Each tanker load is linked to the farm pickups that make it up. Temperatures are recorded in transit by sensors fitted to the tanker.
3. **Plant Receiving.** Receiving times, scale weights, receiving temperatures and laboratory results are captured at the plant. Loads cannot be released to a silo until the required tests are entered and passed, unless an authorized user records an override with a reason.
4. **Silo and Lot Genealogy.** Silo fills and draws are tracked, lot codes are assigned, and each finished product lot is linked to the silos, loads and pickups behind it.
5. **Cold-Chain Monitoring.** Plant teams set temperature thresholds. The system issues alerts by on-screen notification, email and text message, escalates alerts that are not acknowledged, and logs corrective actions.
6. **Trace and Recall.** Trace-back, trace-forward and recall reports can be produced for any finished product lot or farm supplier and exported in PDF and spreadsheet formats.
7. **Organic Certification.** Each farm supplier's organic certification status and expiry date are tracked. Users are alerted ahead of expiry, and pickups from a supplier whose certification has lapsed are flagged.
8. **Consumer Complaints.** Complaints are recorded, linked to finished product lots where the lot is known, and tracked through investigation and closure.
9. **Integration Services.** DairyLedger connects with Kunoux's existing business systems, including its enterprise resource planning system, laboratory information management system, receiving bay scales and plant temperature monitoring systems.
10. **Reporting and Analytics.** Operational dashboards, scheduled reports and a report builder give plant and quality teams a current view of receiving, temperatures and supplier performance.

Every module includes role-based access controls and a time-stamped audit trail that users cannot edit, so that the history of every record is preserved.

"Dairy is a supply chain where minutes matter, both for product quality and for the response when something goes wrong," said Rafael Mendoza-Kiel, Chief Executive Officer of Tilil Systems. "We built DairyLedger around the way milk actually moves: farm to tanker, tanker to plant, plant to silo, silo to lot. Kunoux runs exactly the kind of operation the platform was designed for, with an organic supplier network, multiple product lines and a quality culture that takes record-keeping seriously. We are proud that they have chosen us."

## 4. Rollout Plan

The implementation will take place in two phases.

**Phase 1: Eau Claire.** Tilil's implementation team will begin with discovery workshops at the Eau Claire plant, covering farm pickup and hauling routes, receiving, silo management, lot coding, cold-chain monitoring points, laboratory testing, product release and recall procedures. The workshops will lead to a solution design that sets out how each module will be configured for Kunoux. Configuration, system connections, data migration and testing will follow. Go-live at Eau Claire is targeted for August 2026, ahead of the late-summer receiving peak.

**Phase 2: Sheboygan.** The Sheboygan plant will follow. Go-live there is targeted for later in 2026.

In each phase, Tilil will migrate Kunoux's active farm supplier, hauler and driver records, together with recent raw milk receiving and test history and recent consumer-complaint records. That way the new system holds a useful history from the first day of use. Before go-live at each plant, Kunoux's recall team will run a full recall simulation on DairyLedger, producing trace-back and trace-forward reports for a sample finished product lot.

Tilil consultants will be on site at each plant for the first days after go-live. A 30-day hypercare period will follow, during which Tilil will hold daily check-ins with the Kunoux project team before handing each site over to its standard support organization.

"We have deliberately sequenced this so that we learn at Eau Claire and carry those lessons to Sheboygan," said Marcus Lindqvist, Director of Plant Systems at Kunoux and the company's project manager for the implementation. "Our receiving, laboratory and quality teams have been involved from the start, and they will be the ones testing the system before it goes live. Nothing goes into production until they are satisfied that it works the way our plants work."

Tilil's implementation will be led by Dana Okonkwo-Reyes, a project manager with experience of DairyLedger deployments at dairy processors and cooperatives.

## 5. Sensors and Offline Capture

As part of the subscription, Tilil will supply and commission in-tanker temperature sensors, in-silo temperature probes and plant gateways for the two plants. The in-tanker sensors use cellular connections to report temperatures while milk is in transit. The in-silo probes report temperatures from storage at the plant.

Rural routes do not always have reliable mobile coverage. The DairyLedger mobile applications and plant gateways therefore store pickup, receiving and temperature records locally when a connection is lost, and synchronize them automatically when the connection returns. Records keep their original time-stamps, so the history shows when each event actually happened. The platform is designed to hold at least 72 hours of records locally.

Each sensor and probe is delivered with a certificate of calibration. Tilil will recalibrate the devices on a regular schedule agreed with Kunoux so that the work does not disrupt milk receiving.

## 6. Farm Suppliers and Haulers

The farms that supply Kunoux, and the haulers and drivers who collect their milk, are central to the project. Drivers will use the DairyLedger mobile application at each pickup. Farm suppliers will be able to view their pickup and quality records through the supplier portal.

To support the transition, Tilil will provide an onboarding kit with quick-start guides in English and Spanish. It will also run live webinar sessions for farm suppliers and haulers, scheduled with Kunoux around milking and hauling routines, and provide a dedicated support queue for suppliers and haulers in the weeks before and after go-live at each plant. The mobile applications are available in English and Spanish.

"Our farm families and the haulers who serve them are part of the Kunoux team," said Ms. Brandvold. "We want this change to make their days easier, not harder. A driver should be able to record a pickup in less time than it takes to fill out a paper ticket, and a farmer should be able to see their own records whenever they want. We will be talking with our suppliers and haulers throughout the rollout, and we will listen to what they tell us."

Kunoux will contact its farm suppliers and haulers directly over the coming months with details of training sessions and onboarding timing for each plant.

## 7. Data Stewardship and Security

Kunoux will own the data that it and its suppliers, haulers and employees enter into DairyLedger, and the reports generated from that data. Tilil will use the data only to provide the service to Kunoux. Tilil will not sell Kunoux's data or share benchmarking or market information derived from it.

Kunoux data will be hosted in data centers located in the United States, with backups held in a separate U.S. data center. Tilil maintains an independently audited SOC 2 Type II report covering the DairyLedger platform. Data is encrypted both when stored and when transmitted. Farm supplier and hauler users sign in with multi-factor authentication, and Kunoux employees will sign in through the company's own single sign-on system.

"Supplier records, volumes and quality results are sensitive information for a dairy processor and for the farms it buys from," said Mr. Mendoza-Kiel. "Our customers own their data. We hold it securely, we use it only to run the service for them, and they can export it whenever they choose."

## 8. Service and Support

Tilil will provide the DairyLedger production environment with a 99.9 percent monthly availability commitment. Kunoux will receive Tilil's Enterprise support tier, under which any incident that prevents milk receiving, product release or a response to a trace or recall request is treated as the highest severity and receives an initial response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Tilil will assign Kunoux a named customer success manager. The customer success manager will hold monthly service reviews and quarterly business reviews with Kunoux's operations and quality assurance leadership, and will coordinate Tilil's support for mock recall exercises after the implementation is complete.

The two companies have also entered into a quality agreement. It sets out how they share responsibility for the record-keeping and recall support functions of the platform, how software updates that affect food safety functions are validated and communicated before deployment, and how Tilil will support Kunoux during an actual recall or a request for records from a regulator or organic certifier.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**What changes for farm suppliers?**
Drivers will record pickups on a mobile application instead of on paper tickets. Farm suppliers will be able to view their pickup and quality records online. Kunoux will contact each supplier directly about onboarding and training before go-live at the plant that receives their milk.

**What changes for haulers and drivers?**
Drivers will use the DairyLedger mobile application to record each pickup. The application works without a mobile signal and synchronizes when coverage returns. Tilil will provide quick-start guides and live training sessions in English and Spanish, and a dedicated support queue during the transition.

**Will the change affect product availability?**
No. The rollout has been planned so that the plants continue normal receiving and production throughout. Each plant will go live only after testing by Kunoux's own teams and a recall simulation.

**Who owns the data?**
Kunoux owns the data entered into DairyLedger for its operations and the reports generated from it. Tilil uses the data only to provide the service to Kunoux.

**Where is the data stored?**
In data centers located in the United States.

**How does DairyLedger help with organic certification?**
DairyLedger tracks each farm supplier's organic certification status and expiry date, alerts Kunoux ahead of expiry, and flags any pickup from a supplier whose certification has lapsed. It also helps Kunoux produce pickup and lot records for review by its organic certifier.

**How will consumers benefit?**
Faster and more precise traceability means that if a question arises about a product, Kunoux can identify the affected lots and their sources more quickly. That supports a faster, better-targeted response and helps protect consumers.
