## 3. Introductions and Roles

**3.1** Ms. Okonkwo-Reyes opened the meeting and confirmed that she is Tilil's named implementation project manager under Section 7.1 of the SOW. She introduced the Tilil team: the solution architect, integration engineer, data migration specialist, field device technician and training lead.

**3.2** Mr. Lindqvist confirmed that he is Kunoux's Project Manager under Section 7.2 of the SOW. He introduced the quality assurance, receiving and IT integration leads for each Implementation Site, as SOW Section 7.2 requires.

**3.3** The Kunoux Vice President of Operations described the business objectives for the project:

(a) to replace paper pickup tickets and spreadsheet receiving logs at the Eau Claire Plant and the Sheboygan Plant;
(b) to cut the time needed to produce trace-back and trace-forward reports in a mock or actual recall from hours to minutes; and
(c) to reduce tanker waiting time at the receiving bays, especially during the spring flush.

**3.4** The Tilil Vice President of Customer Delivery confirmed Tilil's commitment to the phased plan. He confirmed that Tilil will not reassign the Project Manager or solution architect away from the project before Milestone M7, except as permitted by Section 7.3 of the SOW.

## 4. Project Governance, Reporting and Escalation

**4.1 Weekly Status Reports.** Ms. Okonkwo-Reyes confirmed that she will issue a written status report each week under Section 5.4 of the SOW. The first report will be issued on Friday, March 20, 2026. Each report will cover progress against the project plan, upcoming activities, open risks and issues, decisions required, and the status of change requests.

**4.2 Weekly Project Meeting.** The Project Managers agreed to hold a one-hour project meeting every Tuesday at 10:00 a.m. Central Time by video conference. Site leads will attend as needed.

**4.3 Steering Committee.** The Steering Committee under Section 7.4 of the SOW comprises the two Project Managers, Tilil's Vice President of Customer Delivery, and Kunoux's Vice President of Operations and Vice President of Quality Assurance. The first Steering Committee meeting was set for April 8, 2026. Monthly meetings will follow on the second Wednesday of each month. Additional meetings will be called at key decision points, including approval of the Solution Design Document.

**4.4 Escalation Contacts.** The attendees confirmed the escalation contacts in Section 7.5 of the SOW. Kunoux noted that its Level 2 contact is the Director of Plant Systems. This means Mr. Lindqvist holds both Level 1 and Level 2 for Kunoux. Kunoux will consider whether to nominate a different Level 1 contact after Go-Live at the Eau Claire Plant. Any update will be given by written notice to Tilil's Project Manager, as SOW Section 7.5 provides.

**4.5 Change Control.** Ms. Okonkwo-Reyes summarized the change control procedure in Section 10 of the SOW:

(a) change requests go in writing to the other party's Project Manager;
(b) Tilil provides a written impact assessment within five Business Days;
(c) change orders up to $25,000 may be signed by the Project Managers; and
(d) change orders above that amount, or that move Milestone M4 or M6 by more than 20 Business Days, require Steering Committee approval and signature by an authorized officer of each party.

The attendees agreed that the weekly status report will include a change request log.

**4.6 Project Repository.** Tilil will set up a shared project repository for Deliverables, workshop materials, the defect log, the RAID log (risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies) and meeting minutes. Kunoux's IT integration lead for the Eau Claire Plant will confirm which Kunoux users need access.

Kunoux's IT integration lead will confirm with the system vendor whether a test instance can be made available. If not, the parties will document the staging approach in the Test Plan.

**7.3 Scales.** The receiving lead for the Eau Claire Plant confirmed that both receiving bay scales use the same indicator model, which supports a serial-to-network connection. The Sheboygan receiving lead will confirm the scale model at that site.

The field device technician noted that integrated plant historian tags count toward the monitored temperature point entitlement in Section 5.2 of the Order Form, together with Tilil-supplied sensors.

**7.5 Other Systems.** Kunoux asked whether DairyLedger could also receive hauler route schedules from its transport management system. Ms. Okonkwo-Reyes noted that this interface is not among those listed in Section 2.3 of the SOW, so it is outside scope under Section 2.8(b). Kunoux may raise a change request if it wishes to pursue it. Kunoux will consider this after the discovery workshops.

## 8. Data Migration

**8.1 Scope.** The data migration specialist reviewed the records to be migrated under Section 2.4 of the SOW:

(a) active farm supplier master records, including organic certification status and expiry dates;
(b) hauler, route and driver master records;
(c) raw milk receiving and test records for the 24 months before the SOW Effective Date; and
(d) open consumer-complaint records, and complaint records closed within the 12 months before the SOW Effective Date.

**8.2 Source Data.** Kunoux confirmed that receiving records are held in one spreadsheet workbook per plant per month, and that test results are held in the LIMS. Kunoux will provide extracts in the format agreed in the Solution Design Document. Kunoux acknowledged its responsibility under Section 2.4 and Section 8(c) of the SOW to cleanse the source data and resolve exceptions shown in the reconciliation reports.

**8.3 Migration Cycles.** Tilil will perform up to two trial migrations and one production migration for each Implementation Site. For the Eau Claire Plant, the first trial migration is planned for the week of June 8, 2026 and the second for the week of June 29, 2026. Each migration will be followed by a reconciliation report (DEL-6) showing record counts and exceptions.

**8.4 Data Transfer.** The data migration specialist asked that all data extracts be transferred through the secure file transfer location in the project repository, not by email. Kunoux agreed. The extracts contain farm supplier, driver and consumer-complaint information.

## 11. Data Protection and Security Items

**11.1 Personal Data in the Project.** Kunoux's Legal Department representative joined for this item. The attendees noted that the migrated records and the live Licensee Data include personal data of farm suppliers, haulers and drivers, and consumers who submit complaints. That personal data is processed under the Data Processing Addendum dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**DPA**").

**11.2 Driver Location Data.** The Legal Department representative noted that the Farm Pickup module captures GPS location at the time each pickup is recorded. She also noted that Kunoux is responsible under Section 2.4 of the DPA for providing any required notices to drivers and haulers. Kunoux will prepare a short driver notice for inclusion in the onboarding kit. Tilil's training lead will reserve space for it.

**11.3 Consumer Complaint Free Text.** The Kunoux Vice President of Quality Assurance confirmed that complaint investigators will be told to limit free-text health information to what is needed for the investigation, consistent with Schedule 1, paragraph 5 of the DPA. The solution architect will include guidance text on the complaint entry screen in the Solution Design Document.

**11.4 Sandbox Data.** The attendees agreed that the non-production environment used for training will be loaded with masked or synthetic supplier and driver data where practicable. Any production data copied to that environment will be used only for testing and training, in line with Section 11.2 of the Order Form.

**11.5 Single Sign-On.** Kunoux's IT integration lead will provide the identity provider metadata for single sign-on by April 10, 2026. Farm supplier, hauler and driver users will sign in with DairyLedger-managed credentials and multi-factor authentication, as described in Section 11.3 of the Order Form.

**11.6 Security Documentation.** Kunoux confirmed that it has received Tilil's SOC 2 Type II report and has no open questions from its security review.
