# TERM SHEET

**DairyLedger Traceability and Cold-Chain Platform: Proposed Subscription by Kunoux Creamery, Inc.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | January 23, 2026 |
| **Proposed Licensor** | Tilil Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Tilil**") |
| **Proposed Licensee** | Kunoux Creamery, Inc., a Wisconsin corporation ("**Kunoux**") |
| **Proposed Guarantor** | Kunoux Holdings, L.P. |
| **Status** | Non-binding except as stated in Section 14 |

This term sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") summarizes the principal terms on which Tilil and Kunoux propose to enter into a subscription to Tilil's DairyLedger platform. It reflects discussions between the parties' commercial, operations and quality assurance teams from October 2025 through January 2026. It is intended as the basis for preparing definitive agreements (the "**Definitive Agreements**").

## 1. TRANSACTION OVERVIEW

**1.1 Purpose.** Kunoux wishes to replace the spreadsheet-based receiving logs and paper pickup tickets now used at its Wisconsin processing plants with a hosted system for farm-to-plant milk traceability and cold-chain monitoring. Tilil will provide the DairyLedger platform as a software-as-a-service offering, together with implementation services, Tilil-supplied sensors and gateways, and ongoing support.

**1.2 Definitive Agreements.** The parties expect the transaction to be documented in the following agreements:

(a) a Software-as-a-Service License Agreement (the "**License Agreement**");

(b) an Order Form setting out the subscribed modules, facilities, entitlements, hardware and fees;

(c) a Statement of Work for the implementation services (the "**SOW**");

(d) a Data Processing Addendum covering personal data processed by Tilil on Kunoux's behalf (the "**DPA**");

(e) a Quality Agreement allocating food safety, regulatory record-keeping and recall support responsibilities; and

(f) a guaranty of Kunoux's payment obligations by Kunoux Holdings, L.P.

**1.3 Target Signing and Effective Date.** The parties will work toward signing the Definitive Agreements on or about February 27, 2026, with an effective date of March 1, 2026, so that discovery workshops can begin in March and Go-Live at the first plant can occur before the late-summer receiving peak.

**1.4 First Drafts.** Tilil will prepare first drafts of the License Agreement, Order Form, SOW and DPA based on its standard enterprise forms, adapted to reflect this Term Sheet. Kunoux will prepare the first draft of the Quality Agreement. Kunoux Holdings, L.P. will review the guaranty with its own counsel.

## 2. PLATFORM AND MODULES

**2.1 Subscription Services.** Tilil will provide access to the production environment and one non-production (sandbox) environment of the DairyLedger platform. This includes hosting, storage, backup, support, maintenance, and all updates and new releases that Tilil makes generally available to its subscribers for the subscribed modules.

**2.2 Modules.** Kunoux intends to subscribe to the following modules:

| Module | Summary of Function |
|---|---|
| Farm Pickup | Mobile capture of farm pickups, bulk tank identifiers, tank temperatures, volumes, sample identifiers and GPS location, with offline capture |
| Tanker Tracking | Linking of tanker loads to farm pickups and in-transit temperature recording |
| Plant Receiving | Receiving times, scale weights, receiving temperatures and laboratory results, with load release controls |
| Silo and Lot Genealogy | Silo fill and draw tracking, lot coding and finished product lot genealogy |
| Cold-Chain Monitoring | Threshold configuration, alerting, escalation and corrective action logging |
| Trace and Recall | Trace-back, trace-forward and recall reporting |
| Organic Certification | Supplier organic certification tracking and expiry alerts |
| Consumer Complaints | Complaint recording, lot linkage and investigation tracking |
| Integration Services | Access to APIs and standard connectors |
| Reporting and Analytics | Operational dashboards, scheduled reports and report builder |

**2.3 Mobile Applications.** The farm pickup and driver mobile applications will be available in English and Spanish. They will store records locally during any loss of connectivity and synchronize when connectivity returns. Kunoux has asked that offline storage cover at least 72 hours, and Tilil has indicated that this is consistent with its current product.

**2.4 Platform Features.** Role-based access control, single sign-on for Kunoux employees, multi-factor authentication for supplier and hauler users, and a time-stamped audit trail that users cannot edit will be included in all modules.

**2.5 Hosting.** Kunoux data will be hosted in data centers located in the United States, with backups stored in a geographically separate U.S. data center.

## 3. FACILITIES AND IMPLEMENTATION

**3.1 Initial Plants.** The platform will be implemented at Kunoux's processing plants in Eau Claire, Wisconsin and Sheboygan, Wisconsin. These are the two plants that receive raw milk directly from Kunoux's farm supplier network. The parties expect a phased rollout, with Eau Claire first and Sheboygan second.

**3.2 Implementation Scope.** Tilil's implementation services under the SOW will include:

(a) discovery and design workshops at each plant, leading to a Solution Design Document;

(b) configuration of the subscribed modules;

(c) interfaces with Kunoux's enterprise resource planning system, laboratory information management system, receiving bay scales, plant historian/SCADA system and supplier payroll and premium system;

(d) migration of active farm supplier, hauler, route and driver master records, 24 months of raw milk receiving and test records, and recent consumer-complaint records;

(e) delivery and commissioning of in-tanker sensors, in-silo probes and plant gateways;

(f) supplier and hauler onboarding materials and webinars;

(g) administrator, super-user and role-based training, and a recall simulation exercise at each plant before Go-Live; and

(h) cutover, on-site Go-Live support and a 30-day hypercare period at each plant.

**3.3 Indicative Timeline.** Subject to the project plan to be agreed in the SOW:

| Activity | Indicative Timing |
|---|---|
| Kickoff and project plan | March 2026 |
| Solution Design Document approved | May 2026 |
| Eau Claire Go-Live | August 2026 |
| Sheboygan Go-Live | November 2026 |
| Final acceptance | December 2026 |

**3.4 Acceptance.** The SOW will include a test plan, defined acceptance criteria, and a written acceptance procedure with deemed acceptance only after a stated review period. Kunoux expects no milestone to be accepted while Severity 1 or Severity 2 defects remain open.

**3.5 Additional Facilities.** Rollout to any other Kunoux facility is not part of this transaction. Kunoux may later request implementation at other facilities through an additional order form or a further statement of work on terms to be agreed at that time.

**3.6 Licensee Responsibilities.** Kunoux will provide timely access to subject matter experts, plant networks, systems and test environments. It will supply cleansed legacy data extracts, carry out user acceptance testing, and invite its farm suppliers, haulers and drivers to the platform. Kunoux will be responsible for plant network cabling and wireless coverage at receiving bays, silo areas and laboratories.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

**4.1 Scope.** In recognition of Tilil's pricing and implementation commitments, Kunoux will agree that the exclusivity commitment in the Definitive Agreements will cover milk traceability software at the Eau Claire and Sheboygan, Wisconsin plants only. During the Initial Term, Kunoux will not use a third-party milk traceability product in place of DairyLedger at either of those plants.

**4.2 Excluded Systems.** The exclusivity commitment will not restrict Kunoux's use of general-purpose enterprise resource planning, laboratory information management, accounting or spreadsheet software for purposes other than milk traceability.

**4.3 Release of Exclusivity.** The exclusivity commitment will cease to apply to any module that Tilil withdraws or is unable to continue to provide. It will also cease during any post-termination transition period, so that Kunoux can implement replacement software.

## 5. TERM AND RENEWAL

**5.1 Initial Term.** The License Agreement will have an initial term of five years from the License Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

**5.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the License Agreement will renew automatically for successive one-year renewal terms. Either party may elect not to renew by written notice given at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

**5.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate for the other party's uncured material breach after a 30-day cure period, or upon the other party's insolvency. Kunoux will also have a termination right for chronic failure of the availability commitment.

## 6. FEES

**6.1 Annual Subscription Fee.** The subscription fee for the modules, entitlements and hardware described in this Term Sheet will be $486,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. The first invoice will be issued on the effective date of the License Agreement.

**6.2 Fixed Pricing.** The annual subscription fee will be fixed for the Initial Term. Any increase for a renewal term will require at least 120 days' notice and will be capped at the lesser of 5% and the CPI-U increase for the preceding 12 months.

**6.3 Implementation Fees.** Tilil has proposed a fixed implementation fee of $312,000, invoiced on milestones defined in the SOW, with a meaningful portion held back until Go-Live at each plant and final acceptance. Reimbursable travel expenses will be subject to a cap to be stated in the SOW and to Kunoux's travel policy.

**6.4 Hardware.** The initial quantity of in-tanker sensors, in-silo probes, plant gateways and spare sensor kits needed for the two plants will be supplied at no charge beyond the subscription and implementation fees. This includes cellular data plans for the in-tanker sensors. Title to the hardware will remain with Tilil. Tilil will repair or replace defective hardware at no charge and will recalibrate sensors at least every 24 months. Replacement units for lost or damaged hardware will be priced in the Order Form.

**6.5 Entitlements and Overages.** The Order Form will set out user entitlements (plant and office users, supplier users, hauler and driver users, and read-only auditor accounts) and volume entitlements (supplier records, pounds of raw milk received, tanker loads, monitored temperature points, API calls and storage). The parties have discussed entitlements sized to Kunoux's 2025 volumes at the two plants, with headroom for approximately 20% growth. Overage rates will be stated in the Order Form. Overages will be invoiced only after a 30-day good-faith discussion period. API call peaks during the spring flush season or a recall will not be charged if annual usage remains within twelve times the monthly entitlement.

**6.6 Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices will be payable within 30 days after receipt. Kunoux may withhold amounts disputed in good faith, provided it pays the undisputed portion. Tilil will not suspend service for disputed amounts. Tilil will never suspend recall or regulatory trace functions for non-payment.

**6.7 Taxes.** Fees will exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Kunoux will provide any exemption certificates on which it relies.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS AND SUPPORT

**7.1 Availability.** Tilil will commit to 99.9% monthly availability of the production environment, excluding scheduled maintenance in an agreed weekend window and force majeure events.

**7.2 Service Credits.** Service credits will apply on a tiered basis where availability falls below the commitment, up to 30% of the monthly subscription fee.

**7.3 Support.** Kunoux will receive Tilil's Enterprise support tier. Severity 1 incidents, including any incident that prevents milk receiving, product release or a trace or recall response, will receive a 30-minute initial response on a 24 x 7 basis. Tilil will provide root cause analyses for Severity 1 incidents and monthly service reports.

**7.4 Recovery Objectives.** Tilil will commit to a four-hour recovery point objective and an eight-hour recovery time objective, and will test its business continuity and disaster recovery plan at least annually.

**7.5 Seasonal Considerations.** Kunoux has asked that Tilil avoid scheduling maintenance during peak milk receiving periods. Tilil has agreed to use commercially reasonable efforts to do so. The parties may discuss enhanced support during the spring flush season as part of a later amendment or account plan.

**7.6 Customer Success.** Tilil will assign a named customer success manager, hold monthly service reviews and quarterly business reviews, and provide up to 24 hours of remote refresher training each subscription year.

## 8. DATA AND SECURITY

**8.1 Data Ownership.** As between the parties, Kunoux will own all data entered into or generated by the platform for Kunoux. This includes farm supplier, pickup, tanker, driver, laboratory, cold-chain, lot, pricing and premium, and consumer-complaint records, and all reports generated from that data. Tilil will receive a limited license to use that data only to provide the services.

**8.2 Aggregated Data.** Tilil may compile anonymized system usage and performance metrics. It may not disclose benchmarking or market information derived from Kunoux data, or information from which Kunoux's volumes, pricing, supplier identities or quality results could be derived.

**8.3 Security.** Tilil will maintain industry-standard security safeguards and a SOC 2 Type II report, or equivalent, covering the platform. It will notify Kunoux of any security incident affecting Kunoux data within 48 hours.

**8.4 Personal Data.** The parties will enter into a DPA covering supplier contact details, driver records, consumer-complaint records and other personal data processed by Tilil. The DPA will address processing instructions, subprocessors, security, incident notification, audits, and return and deletion of data.

**8.5 Data Export and Deletion.** Kunoux may export its data at any time during the term. For 60 days after termination or expiration, Tilil will make the data available in a machine-readable format that preserves lot genealogy and trace linkages. Tilil will then delete the data and certify deletion on request.

**8.6 Record Retention.** The Quality Agreement will set the retention periods for trace and cold-chain records needed to support Kunoux's regulatory record-keeping obligations.

## 9. QUALITY AND REGULATORY

**9.1 Quality Agreement.** The parties will enter into a Quality Agreement that addresses:

(a) the allocation of responsibilities for the regulatory record-keeping and recall support functions of the platform;

(b) change control for platform updates affecting food safety functions;

(c) Tilil's support obligations during an actual product recall or regulatory trace request, including response times for producing trace reports;

(d) validation and documentation of the platform's food safety functions; and

(e) Kunoux's right to audit the quality management processes relevant to the platform.

**9.2 Regulatory Responsibility.** Kunoux will remain responsible for its own compliance with the food safety, organic certification and traceability laws that apply to its operations. Tilil will be responsible for the platform functioning as documented.

**9.3 Regulator Access.** Kunoux may give food safety regulators and organic certifiers access to trace records and read-only accounts without restriction.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**10.1 Platform.** Tilil will retain ownership of the platform, its documentation, and all modifications and extensions of them.

**10.2 Kunoux Deliverables.** Kunoux will own Kunoux-specific standard operating procedures, training content that incorporates Kunoux information, and data mapping documents describing Kunoux's systems. Ownership will pass on payment of the related implementation fees.

**10.3 Infringement Indemnity.** Tilil will defend and indemnify Kunoux against third-party claims that the platform infringes or misappropriates intellectual property rights. Mitigation options will include a refund of prepaid fees if neither a license nor a non-infringing modification is commercially reasonable. Kunoux will give a reciprocal indemnity for third-party claims that its data infringes intellectual property rights.

## 11. RISK ALLOCATION

**11.1 Warranties.** Tilil will warrant that the platform will perform materially in accordance with its documentation and the functional specifications in the SOW, that implementation services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner, and that it will use industry-standard measures to prevent malicious code. Kunoux will warrant that it has the rights and consents needed for Tilil to process Kunoux data.

**11.2 Liability Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Indirect and consequential damages will be mutually excluded.

**11.3 Exclusions from Cap.** The cap and exclusion of indirect damages will not apply to fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct, the infringement indemnities, Kunoux's obligation to pay fees due, or liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

**11.4 Insurance.** Tilil will maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, technology errors and omissions insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per claim, cyber liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**11.5 Parent Guaranty.** Kunoux Holdings, L.P. will provide a guaranty to Tilil of Kunoux's obligations under the Definitive Agreements, in a form to be agreed. Delivery of the guaranty on or before the effective date will be a condition of the License Agreement.

## 12. OTHER COMMERCIAL TERMS

**12.1 Publicity.** Neither party will issue a press release or public announcement about the transaction without the other party's prior written approval of its content. The parties expect to agree a joint launch announcement after signing.

**12.2 Subcontractors.** Tilil may use subcontractors, including hosting providers, to perform its obligations. Tilil will remain responsible for them and will bind them to confidentiality and security obligations no less protective than those in the Definitive Agreements.

**12.3 Transition Assistance.** On termination or expiration, other than termination by Tilil for Kunoux's failure to pay, Tilil will continue to provide the services for up to 90 days at the then-current prorated rate and will provide reasonable transition assistance.

**12.4 Site Rules.** Tilil personnel working at Kunoux plants will comply with Kunoux's site safety, sanitation, food defense and good manufacturing practice rules.

**12.5 Governing Law and Disputes.** The Definitive Agreements will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Disputes will be referred first to executive negotiation and then to the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, with a mutual jury trial waiver.

## 14. LEGAL EFFECT

**14.1 Non-Binding.** Except for this Section 14, this Term Sheet is a statement of the parties' present intentions only. It does not create any legally binding obligation on either party, and neither party will be bound unless and until the Definitive Agreements are signed. Either party may end negotiations at any time for any reason, without liability to the other party, subject to the binding provisions of this Section 14.

**14.2 Binding Provisions.** The following provisions of this Section 14 are legally binding on the parties when this Term Sheet is countersigned.

(a) **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of this Term Sheet, and all non-public information exchanged in connection with the proposed transaction, will be treated as confidential under the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the parties dated October 14, 2025. That agreement remains in effect.

(b) **Costs.** Each party will bear its own costs and expenses, including legal fees, in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreements, whether or not the Definitive Agreements are signed.

(c) **Pricing Hold.** Tilil will hold the pricing in Section 6 open until March 31, 2026.

(d) **Governing Law.** This Section 14 is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

(e) **Counterparts.** This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

**14.3 Expiry.** If the Definitive Agreements have not been signed by March 31, 2026, this Term Sheet will expire, except for Sections 14.2(a), 14.2(b) and 14.2(d), which will survive.
