# PARENT GUARANTY

**DairyLedger Traceability and Cold-Chain Platform**

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2026 by **KUNOUX HOLDINGS, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **TILIL SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Kunoux Creamery, Inc., a Wisconsin corporation ("**Licensee**"), are entering into a Software-as-a-Service License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Under the License Agreement, Licensor will provide the DairyLedger platform and related Implementation Services to Licensee.

B. In connection with the License Agreement, Licensor and Licensee are also entering into Order Form No. 1, Statement of Work No. 1, a Data Processing Addendum and a Quality Agreement. Each of these forms part of the "Agreement" as defined in the License Agreement.

C. The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding equity interests of Licensee. The Guarantor expects to derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from Licensor's entry into the Agreement and its performance of the Agreement.

D. Section 16.8 of the License Agreement requires Licensee to deliver to Licensor, on or before the Effective Date, a guaranty in favor of Licensor executed by Kunoux Holdings, L.P. in the form agreed by the parties. This Guaranty is that guaranty.

NOW, THEREFORE, to induce Licensor to enter into the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Guarantor agrees as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the License Agreement. In this Guaranty:

"**Agreement**" has the meaning given in the License Agreement and includes the License Agreement, the Order Form, the SOW, the DPA and the Quality Agreement, each as amended, supplemented, restated or replaced from time to time.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Licensee to Licensor under or in connection with the Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, whether actual or contingent, and whether for payment or performance. This includes:

(a) the payment of all Fees, including Subscription Fees, Implementation Fees, overage charges, hardware replacement charges and reimbursable expenses;

(b) the payment of all amounts that become due and payable under Section 14.4 or Section 14.5 of the License Agreement;

(c) the payment of interest on late payments under Section 7.5 of the License Agreement and of Taxes payable by Licensee under Section 7.7 of the License Agreement;

(d) all indemnification, defense and hold harmless obligations of Licensee under the Agreement;

(e) all damages and other amounts for which Licensee is liable for breach of the Agreement; and

(f) the performance of all other covenants and obligations of Licensee under the Agreement.

"**Insolvency Proceeding**" means any voluntary or involuntary proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation, dissolution or similar law, in any jurisdiction, in respect of Licensee or the Guarantor.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 16.17 of the License Agreement applies to this Guaranty as if set out in full, with references to "this License Agreement" read as references to this Guaranty. References to the License Agreement or the Agreement are to it as amended, supplemented, restated or replaced from time to time, whether or not the Guarantor has consented to the change.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Licensor, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment when due, whether at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise, and the full and punctual performance of all Guaranteed Obligations.

**2.2 Payment on Demand.** If Licensee fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall pay that amount to Licensor within 10 Business Days after receipt of Licensor's written demand. The demand shall identify the unpaid amount and the provision of the Agreement under which it is due. If Licensee fails to perform any Guaranteed Obligation other than a payment obligation, the Guarantor shall, within 10 Business Days after receipt of Licensor's written demand, perform that obligation or cause it to be performed. Alternatively, the Guarantor shall pay Licensor the damages and other amounts for which Licensee is liable as a result of the failure.

**2.3 Guaranty of Payment, Not of Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Licensor is not required, as a condition of the Guarantor's liability, to do any of the following:

(a) make any demand on Licensee;

(b) bring any action or proceeding against Licensee or any other person;

(c) exhaust any remedy against Licensee or any other person; or

(d) enforce any security or other guaranty held by it for any Guaranteed Obligation.

**2.4 Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It extends to all Guaranteed Obligations, whenever arising, and remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full. No intermediate payment or satisfaction of any part of the Guaranteed Obligations discharges this Guaranty.

**2.5 Payments.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account Licensor designates in writing. Payments shall be made without set-off, counterclaim, deduction or withholding, except for withholding required by law. If withholding is required by law, the Guarantor shall pay such additional amounts as are needed so that Licensor receives the amount it would have received without the withholding.

**2.6 Interest on Late Payment.** Any amount demanded under Section 2.2 and not paid within the period stated there shall accrue interest from the end of that period until paid. Interest shall accrue at the rate stated in Section 7.5 of the License Agreement.

**2.7 Costs of Enforcement.** The Guarantor shall reimburse Licensor on demand for all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Licensor incurs in enforcing this Guaranty against the Guarantor. This applies to the extent Licensor prevails in that enforcement.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTOR'S OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Obligations Absolute.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are absolute and unconditional. They shall not be released, discharged, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of it or has consented to it:

(a) any amendment, supplement, restatement, replacement, extension, renewal or waiver of or under the Agreement. This includes any additional Order Form, statement of work, change order or amendment that increases the Fees, extends the Term or adds facilities, modules, entitlements or services;

(b) any extension of time, indulgence or forbearance granted by Licensor to Licensee, or any compromise or settlement with Licensee;

(c) any invalidity, illegality, unenforceability or lack of authority affecting any obligation of Licensee under the Agreement. Any Guaranteed Obligation that is invalid or unenforceable against Licensee for such a reason shall nonetheless be recoverable from the Guarantor as principal obligor;

(d) any Insolvency Proceeding in respect of Licensee, or any stay, discharge, disallowance or limitation of any Guaranteed Obligation in that proceeding;

(e) any rejection or disaffirmance of the Agreement in an Insolvency Proceeding;

(f) any change in the name, organization, structure, ownership or business of Licensee or the Guarantor, or any merger, consolidation or reorganization of either of them;

(g) any assignment or transfer of the Agreement by Licensor or Licensee in accordance with Section 15 of the License Agreement;

(h) any release of, or failure to obtain, perfect or enforce, any other guaranty or security for any Guaranteed Obligation;

(i) any delay or failure by Licensor in exercising any right or remedy against Licensee or any other person;

(j) any claim, set-off or defense that Licensee may have against Licensor, other than the defense that a Guaranteed Obligation has been paid or performed in full; or

(k) any other act, omission or circumstance that might, but for this Section 3.1, operate as a legal or equitable discharge of a guarantor or surety.

**3.2 Reinstatement.** This Guaranty shall continue to be effective, or shall be reinstated, if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned. This applies whether the payment was made by Licensee, by the Guarantor or by any other person, and whether the rescission or return arises in an Insolvency Proceeding or otherwise. In each case, the Guaranty applies as though the payment had not been made.

**3.3 Acceleration in Insolvency.** If an Insolvency Proceeding in respect of Licensee prevents Licensor from demanding or accelerating payment of any Guaranteed Obligation, that obligation shall be payable by the Guarantor under this Guaranty on Licensor's demand. This applies as if the obligation had become due and payable under the Agreement.

## 4. WAIVERS

**4.1 Suretyship Waivers.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Guarantor waives each of the following:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) notice of the creation, renewal, extension, amendment or accrual of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(c) presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of protest and notice of dishonor or non-performance, other than the demand required by Section 2.2;

(d) any requirement that Licensor proceed first against Licensee or any other person, or against any security;

(e) any right to require Licensor to marshal assets; and

(f) all other suretyship defenses and rights available to a guarantor under applicable law that would otherwise limit or condition the Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty.

**4.2 Subrogation and Contribution.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right that it may acquire by way of subrogation, reimbursement, indemnity, contribution or otherwise against Licensee arising from payment or performance under this Guaranty. If the Guarantor receives any amount in respect of those rights before then, it shall hold that amount in trust for Licensor. The Guarantor shall promptly pay that amount to Licensor, to be applied against the Guaranteed Obligations.

**4.3 Subordination.** All indebtedness of Licensee to the Guarantor, whether now existing or arising later, is subordinated in right of payment to the Guaranteed Obligations. This subordination applies after Licensor has made a demand under Section 2.2 that remains unsatisfied, or while an Insolvency Proceeding in respect of Licensee is pending. While either circumstance continues, the Guarantor shall not demand or accept any payment of that indebtedness. This Section 4.3 does not restrict ordinary course distributions, intercompany charges or repayments by Licensee to the Guarantor at any other time.

**4.4 Information.** The Guarantor confirms that it has adequate means to obtain information about the financial condition of Licensee on a continuing basis. Licensor has no duty to provide the Guarantor with any information about Licensee, the Agreement or the Guaranteed Obligations, except as expressly stated in this Guaranty.
