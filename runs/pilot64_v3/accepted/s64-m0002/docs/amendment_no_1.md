# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE LICENSE AGREEMENT AND STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**DairyLedger Traceability and Cold-Chain Platform**

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**TILIL SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"); and

**KUNOUX CREAMERY, INC.**, a Wisconsin corporation ("**Licensee**").

Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**party**" and together as the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to the Software-as-a-Service License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**") and Statement of Work No. 1 dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**SOW**"), under which Licensor provides the DairyLedger platform and related Implementation Services to Licensee.

B. Kunoux Holdings, L.P., the parent of Licensee, signed an equity purchase agreement on September 18, 2026 to sell the equity of its general partner and operating subsidiaries to Ostrander Dairy Partners III, L.P. Closing of that transaction is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

C. Phase 1 of the Implementation Services at the Eau Claire Plant reached Go-Live on August 17, 2026. Licensee has informed Licensor that the plant network upgrade at the receiving bays and silo areas of the Sheboygan Plant will finish later than planned. The parties therefore wish to revise the Phase 2 schedule.

D. The parties also wish to add an interface and additional supplier and hauler onboarding support to the Implementation Services, to provide for enhanced support during the spring flush season, to update the confidentiality and notice provisions of the License Agreement, and to make certain related changes, in each case as set out in this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the License Agreement or, where the context relates to the Implementation Services, in the SOW.

**1.2 References.** From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the License Agreement to "this License Agreement," and each reference in the Agreement to the License Agreement, means the License Agreement as amended by this Amendment. Each reference in the Agreement to the SOW means the SOW as amended by this Amendment.

**1.3 Interpretation.** Section 16.17 of the License Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full, with references to "this License Agreement" read as references to this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENTS TO THE LICENSE AGREEMENT

**2.1 Confidentiality — Permitted Recipients.** Section 10.3(b) of the License Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "(b) not disclose it to any third party except to (i) its and its Affiliates' employees, officers, directors, contractors, legal and financial advisers, auditors, insurers and actual or prospective financing sources, and (ii) any bona fide prospective acquirer of, or investor in, the Recipient or any entity controlling the Recipient, and that person's legal, financial and technical advisers, in each case who need to know it for those purposes or for the purpose of evaluating the relevant transaction and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 10. Disclosure under clause (ii) shall not include Licensor's source code, security architecture documentation or SOC 2 Type II reports, or any Licensee Data that identifies an individual, and the Recipient shall be responsible for any breach of this Section 10 by a person to whom it discloses Confidential Information under clause (ii); and"

**2.2 Spring Flush Support.** A new Section 5.7 is added to the License Agreement immediately after Section 5.6:

> "**5.7 Peak Season Support.** Each year during the Term, from April 1 through June 30 (the "**Peak Season**"), Licensor shall:
>
> (a) schedule no maintenance under Section 5.3, other than emergency maintenance needed to address a security vulnerability or a Severity 1 incident, between 4:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. Central Time on any day;
>
> (b) assign a named support engineer familiar with Licensee's configuration as Licensee's primary support contact, available during Licensee's milk receiving hours as notified by Licensee;
>
> (c) hold a weekly support review call with Licensee's Project Manager or other designated representative to review open incidents, alert volumes and system performance; and
>
> (d) at Licensee's request made at least 30 days before the start of the Peak Season, perform a capacity review of the Subscription Services against Licensee's forecast receiving volumes and report its findings to Licensee in writing before April 1.
>
> The support described in this Section 5.7 is included in the Subscription Fees."

**2.3 Status Page.** A new Section A.10 is added to Exhibit A of the License Agreement immediately after Section A.9:

> "**A.10 Service Status Page.** Licensor shall maintain an online service status page for the Subscription Services, accessible to Licensee's administrators, showing the current Availability of the production environment, scheduled maintenance windows and the status of any open incident affecting multiple customers. Licensor shall update the status page within 30 minutes after it becomes aware of any Severity 1 incident and at least every two hours until the incident is resolved. Licensee may subscribe its administrators to email and SMS notifications of status page updates."

**2.4 Monthly Reporting.** Section A.8 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end:

> "Each monthly report shall also state, for each Covered Facility at which the Subscription Services are in production use, the number of cold-chain alerts generated, the median time to acknowledgement of those alerts, and the number of registered and active farm suppliers, haulers and drivers."

**2.5 Notices to Licensee.** The notice details for Licensee on the signature page of the License Agreement are amended by adding the following after Licensee's email address:

> "With a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to:
> Halvorsen Brandt LLP
> Attention: Technology Transactions Group
> 411 East Wisconsin Avenue, Suite 1900
> Milwaukee, Wisconsin 53202
> Email: notices@halvorsenbrandt.com"

**2.6 Notices to Licensor.** The notice details for Licensor on the signature page of the License Agreement are amended by replacing the email address "legal@tililsystems.com" with "contracts.legal@tililsystems.com". Notices sent to the former email address before the Amendment Effective Date remain validly given.

## 3. AMENDMENTS TO THE SOW

**3.1 Revised Phase 2 Milestones.** The rows for Milestones M6 and M7 in the table in Section 5.2 of the SOW are deleted and replaced with the following:

| Milestone | Description | Target Date |
|---|---|---|
| M6 | Phase 2 user acceptance testing passed and Go-Live at the Sheboygan Plant | January 18, 2027 |
| M7 | Phase 2 Hypercare close-out and final acceptance | February 17, 2027 |

The parties agree that the revised target dates in this Section 3.1 are made through the change control procedure in Section 10 of the SOW. This Amendment constitutes the written change order required by Section 10.3 of the SOW and Section 4.6 of the License Agreement for these changes. The revision of Milestones M6 and M7 has been approved by the Steering Committee.

**3.2 Phase 1 Status.** The parties acknowledge that:

(a) Milestones M1, M2 and M3 have been accepted;

(b) Milestone M4 was accepted on August 28, 2026, following Go-Live at the Eau Claire Plant on August 17, 2026; and

(c) Milestone M5 was accepted on September 25, 2026, with three Severity 3 defects recorded in the remediation plan referenced in the Acceptance Certificate for that Milestone.

Licensor shall close the Severity 3 defects referred to in clause (c) in a scheduled release no later than December 15, 2026.

**3.3 Network Readiness at the Sheboygan Plant.** For Phase 2 only, the date by which Licensee must meet its obligation under Section 8(d) of the SOW is November 30, 2026. Licensee shall give Licensor's Project Manager written confirmation when the plant network connectivity and wireless coverage at the receiving bays, silo areas and laboratory of the Sheboygan Plant meet the minimum technical requirements in the Documentation. Licensor shall begin field device deployment at the Sheboygan Plant within 10 Business Days after receiving that confirmation. If Licensee gives the confirmation after November 30, 2026, Section 5.3 of the SOW and Section 4.3 of the License Agreement apply to any resulting delay.

**3.4 Additional Interface.** The following row is added to the table in Section 2.3 of the SOW after the row for INT-5:

| No. | Licensee System | Direction | Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| INT-6 | Transport management system | Inbound to DairyLedger | Hauler route schedules, tanker assignments and planned pickup sequences |

Licensor shall build and test INT-6 for both Implementation Sites. For the Eau Claire Plant, INT-6 shall be deployed in a scheduled release no later than December 15, 2026. For the Sheboygan Plant, INT-6 shall be deployed before Go-Live. The Test Plan shall be updated to include test scripts for INT-6, and Licensor shall deliver the updated Test Plan to Licensee at least 15 Business Days before the start of user acceptance testing for Phase 2. Deliverable DEL-4 is amended to read "Interfaces INT-1 through INT-6 (DairyLedger connectors and configurations)".

**3.5 Additional Onboarding Support.** Section 2.5(b) of the SOW is amended by replacing "up to six live webinar sessions" with "up to ten live webinar sessions, of which at least four shall be held after the Amendment Effective Date and directed to farm suppliers and haulers serving the Sheboygan Plant". Section 2.5(a) of the SOW is amended by adding at the end: ", and a one-page laminated in-cab reference card for drivers in English and Spanish, supplied in a quantity of up to 250 cards".

**3.6 Additional Implementation Fees.** Section 9.1 of the SOW is amended as follows:

(a) The first sentence is deleted and replaced with: "The Implementation Fees for the Implementation Services are a fixed fee of $334,500, invoiced on the following Milestones and events:"

(b) The following rows are added at the end of the table:

| Milestone or Event | Amount |
|---|---|
| Deployment of INT-6 at the Eau Claire Plant and acceptance under Section 6 | $15,000 |
| Completion of the additional onboarding support under Section 2.5, as amended | $7,500 |

The percentages and amounts stated for Milestones M1, M2, M4, M6 and M7 remain unchanged.

**3.7 Expense Cap.** In the second sentence of Section 9.4 of the SOW, "$38,000" is replaced with "$44,000". The increase reflects the additional on-site visits to the Sheboygan Plant needed under the revised Phase 2 schedule. All other requirements of Section 9.4 of the SOW continue to apply.

**3.8 Key Personnel.** In Section 7.3 of the SOW, the reference to Milestone M7 is read as a reference to Milestone M7 as revised by Section 3.1 of this Amendment. Licensor confirms that Dana Okonkwo-Reyes will remain its Project Manager through Milestone M7.

**3.9 Hypercare at the Sheboygan Plant.** Because Go-Live at the Sheboygan Plant will now occur in winter, Licensor shall include in the cutover plan for the Sheboygan Plant a contingency procedure for the loss of connectivity at receiving bays during severe weather. The procedure shall confirm that the offline capture functions in Section A.2 of Exhibit A to the License Agreement are operating on all Licensor-supplied gateways before Go-Live. During the first five Business Days after Go-Live, at least one of the two on-site consultants required under Section 2.7 of the SOW shall be the field device technician.
