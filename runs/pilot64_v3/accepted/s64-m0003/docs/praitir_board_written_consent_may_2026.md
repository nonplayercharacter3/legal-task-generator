# PRAITIR BAKING HOLDINGS, LLC

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS

**Dated: May 12, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Praitir Baking Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. They do so under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (the "**Act**") and Section 7.8 of the Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Company dated as of January 20, 2026 (the "**LLC Agreement**"). The undersigned waive all requirements of notice, and direct that this consent be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

## 1. RECITALS

**A. The Acquisition.** The Company entered into the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with Glailirt Artisan Breads, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Glailirt**"). Under it, the Company agreed to acquire substantially all of the assets of Glailirt's wholesale bread, rolls and laminated pastry business (the "**Business**"), including the bakery production plant located at 1180 Oak Point Avenue, Bronx, New York (the "**Bronx Plant**"), for a Base Purchase Price of $18,400,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement. The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurred on March 2, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"). On the Closing Date the Company and Glailirt entered into the Transition Services Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"), the Bill of Sale and the other Ancillary Agreements. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**B. Prior Board Authorization.** The Board approved the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements by unanimous written consent dated February 6, 2026 (the "**February Consent**"). The Board authorized the Company's officers to consummate the Closing by the certificate delivered under Section 3.2(b)(iii) of the Purchase Agreement.

**C. Closing Statement.** Section 2.6(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Glailirt to deliver a Closing Statement within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date. That statement sets forth Glailirt's calculation of the Closing Inventory Value and the resulting Purchase Price. Glailirt delivered the Closing Statement on March 30, 2026, by email from Glailirt's Vice President, Finance to the Company's Chief Financial Officer, with a hard copy sent by overnight courier. The Closing Statement states a Closing Inventory Value of $1,326,450. The Estimated Closing Inventory Value delivered before the Closing was $1,214,800. On the basis of the Closing Statement, Glailirt has asserted that the Company owes an additional payment of $111,650 under Section 2.6(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

**D. Inventory Review.** The Company's finance and operations personnel, with the assistance of Marlowe Veitch LLP, the Company's outside accountants, have reviewed the Closing Statement against the physical count of Inventory taken at the Bronx Plant on the night of March 1–2, 2026, the receiving records, and the lot and shelf-life records for the ingredients and finished goods included in the Inventory. The Chief Financial Officer has reported the following to the Board in a memorandum dated May 5, 2026 (the "**Inventory Memorandum**"):

(i) the Closing Statement values approximately 41,000 pounds of butter and laminating fat sheets at replacement cost rather than at the lower of cost or net realizable value required by the Inventory Principles;

(ii) the Closing Statement includes finished laminated pastry goods and par-baked rolls that had passed, or were within seven (7) days of, their stated shelf life at the Closing and were later discarded;

(iii) the Closing Statement includes packaging materials bearing Glailirt trademarks that are not usable by the Company after the period allowed under Section 6.14 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(iv) the Closing Statement does not reflect the count variances recorded by the Company's count team for flour and specialty grain silos.

On the basis of the Inventory Memorandum, management estimates that the Closing Inventory Value, properly determined under the Inventory Principles, is approximately $1,048,900. On that basis Glailirt would owe the Company approximately $165,900 rather than the Company owing Glailirt any additional amount.

**E. Proposed Sale of the Company.** Praitir Holdco, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership and the sole member of the Company (the "**Member**"), has negotiated a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (the "**MIPA**") with Copperline Fund IV, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership ("**Copperline**"). Under the MIPA, the Member will sell to Copperline, and Copperline will purchase, 100% of the issued and outstanding membership interests of the Company (the "**Copperline Sale**"). The Company is a party to the MIPA for limited purposes, including the interim operating covenants, the access and cooperation covenants and the closing deliverables to be delivered by the Company. The closing of the Copperline Sale is expected to occur on or about July 15, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in the MIPA.

**F. Financing Arrangements.** The Company financed a portion of the Purchase Price under the Credit Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Credit Agreement**") among the Company, as borrower, the lenders party to it and Hudson Commerce Bank, N.A., as administrative agent (the "**Agent**"). The Credit Agreement provides a senior secured term loan facility and a revolving credit facility. The Company also entered into the Master Equipment Lease Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Equipment Lease**") with Tidewater Capital Equipment Finance, LLC ("**Tidewater**"). The Equipment Lease covers a spiral freezer and an automated bagging line installed at the Bronx Plant after the Closing. Each of the Credit Agreement and the Equipment Lease contains a change of control provision that would be triggered by the Copperline Sale.

**G. Board Determination.** The Board has reviewed the Inventory Memorandum, a draft of the MIPA dated May 8, 2026, a summary of the consents to be obtained in connection with the Copperline Sale prepared by the Company's counsel, and the other materials presented to it. The Board has determined that the actions set forth in this consent are advisable and in the best interests of the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions:

## 2. RESOLUTION 1 – RATIFICATION OF THE ACQUISITION

**1.1** RESOLVED, that the execution, delivery and performance by the Company of the Purchase Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Intellectual Property Assignment and each other Ancillary Agreement are approved, ratified and confirmed in all respects. The same applies to the consummation of the Closing on March 2, 2026 and the payment of the amounts payable by the Company at the Closing under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that all actions taken before the date of this consent by any Manager or officer of the Company in connection with the Closing are approved, ratified and confirmed, to the extent those actions are consistent with the February Consent and the purposes of these resolutions. These actions include the offers of employment made under Section 6.8(a) of the Purchase Agreement, the filings made with the United States Food and Drug Administration and the New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets, and the filings made with the New York City Department of Environmental Protection.

## 3. RESOLUTION 2 – DISPUTE OF THE CLOSING INVENTORY VALUE

**2.1** RESOLVED, that the Board acknowledges that Glailirt delivered the Closing Statement on March 30, 2026, and that the Company does not accept the Closing Inventory Value set forth in the Closing Statement. The Company disputes that figure and the resulting Purchase Price calculation for the reasons summarized in Recital D and set forth in the Inventory Memorandum.

**2.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized and directed, acting alone, to prepare, execute and deliver to Glailirt, on behalf of the Company, a Notice of Disagreement under Section 2.6(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Notice of Disagreement shall:

(a) identify each item of the Closing Statement that the Company disputes, including each of the items described in Recital D;

(b) state the amount in dispute for each item and the Company's calculation of the Closing Inventory Value;

(c) describe in reasonable detail the basis for each disagreement; and

(d) be accompanied by the count sheets, receiving records, lot and shelf-life records and other supporting documentation referred to in the Inventory Memorandum.

**2.3** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are directed to deliver the Notice of Disagreement in the manner required by Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Delivery shall be made by email of a PDF document and by nationally recognized overnight courier to Glailirt, with a copy to Glailirt's counsel.

**2.4** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are directed not to make any payment to Glailirt under Section 2.6(e) of the Purchase Agreement on the basis of the Closing Statement as delivered. They are further directed to request from Glailirt the working papers and supporting records used in preparing the Closing Statement.

**2.5** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized, acting alone, to negotiate in good faith with Glailirt to resolve the disputed items. If the disputed items are not resolved by agreement, they may submit them to an Independent Accountant under Section 2.6(d) of the Purchase Agreement. That authority includes the authority to propose and agree on the identity of the Independent Accountant and to execute an engagement letter with it. However, any written resolution of the disputed items that results in a Closing Inventory Value greater than $1,140,000 shall require the further approval of the Board.

**2.6** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Company's engagement of Marlowe Veitch LLP to assist in the preparation of the Notice of Disagreement and in any proceedings before the Independent Accountant is approved. The Authorized Officers may engage Bellmont Arcaro LLP, as legal counsel, for the same purposes.

## 4. RESOLUTION 3 – APPROVAL OF THE COPPERLINE SALE

**3.1** RESOLVED, that the Board approves the Copperline Sale and the Company's participation in it, on substantially the terms described in the draft MIPA presented to the Board. The Board recommends that the Member approve the Copperline Sale to the extent the Member's approval is required under the LLC Agreement.

**3.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the MIPA, to the extent applicable to the Company, are approved. The Authorized Officers are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the MIPA on behalf of the Company, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves as necessary or advisable. The Authorized Officer's execution and delivery of the MIPA shall be conclusive evidence of that approval, provided that no such change shall:

(a) increase the Company's obligations under the MIPA beyond those described in the draft presented to the Board; or

(b) require the Company to make any payment or incur any indebtedness, other than customary transaction expenses.

**3.3** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to execute and deliver, on behalf of the Company, each certificate, instrument and agreement that the MIPA requires the Company to deliver at or before the closing of the Copperline Sale (the "**Company Sale Documents**"). The Company Sale Documents include:

(a) an officer's certificate as to the Company's interim covenants;

(b) a secretary's certificate as to the Company's organizational documents and incumbency;

(c) resignations of the Managers designated by the Member, effective as of the closing of the Copperline Sale; and

(d) payoff letters or consents, as applicable, in respect of the Company's indebtedness.

**3.4** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to provide Copperline and its representatives, financing sources and advisors with access to the Bronx Plant and to the books, records, contracts, personnel and data of the Company. That access is to be provided to the extent required by the MIPA, subject to customary confidentiality undertakings and to the Company's good manufacturing practice, food safety and site security policies.

## 5. RESOLUTION 4 – CONSENTS REQUIRED FOR THE COPPERLINE SALE

**4.1** RESOLVED, that the Board has reviewed the summary of required consents prepared by the Company's counsel. The Board acknowledges that the following consents are required in connection with the Copperline Sale (the "**Required Consents**"), as listed on **Exhibit A** to this consent:

(a) the consent of the Agent and the Required Lenders (as defined in the Credit Agreement) to the change of control of the Company under Section 7.11 of the Credit Agreement. In the alternative, if Copperline elects to refinance the Credit Agreement at the closing of the Copperline Sale, a customary payoff letter from the Agent providing for the repayment in full of the obligations under the Credit Agreement and the release of all liens securing them; and

(b) the consent of Tidewater to the change of control of the Company under Section 18.3 of the Equipment Lease.

**4.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized and directed, acting alone, to request, negotiate and obtain each Required Consent on terms reasonably acceptable to the Authorized Officer. That authority includes the authority to execute and deliver consent letters, amendments and fee letters, provided that the aggregate consent fees payable by the Company for all Required Consents shall not exceed $75,000 without the further approval of the Board.

**4.3** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers shall report to the Board, not less than once every two (2) weeks until the closing of the Copperline Sale, on the status of each Required Consent and on any condition to the closing of the Copperline Sale that the Authorized Officers believe may not be satisfied by July 15, 2026.

## 6. RESOLUTION 5 – CONTINUED OPERATION OF THE BUSINESS

**5.1** RESOLVED, that, pending the closing of the Copperline Sale, the Authorized Officers are directed to cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course and to comply with the interim operating covenants of the MIPA. This includes maintaining the Company's Permits for the Bronx Plant, its food safety plan, allergen control program, supplier verification program and environmental monitoring program, and its insurance coverage.

**5.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Chief Operating Officer, as the Company's Service Coordinator under the Transition Services Agreement, shall continue to administer the Company's receipt of the Services. The Chief Operating Officer shall report to the Board at each regular meeting on:

(a) the performance of the Services;

(b) the status of the migration of the Services and Buyer Data to the Company or its designated service providers; and

(c) any invoice disputes under the Transition Services Agreement.

**5.3** FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to negotiate and enter into agreements with third-party providers of enterprise resource planning, payroll, route accounting and logistics services in order to replace the corresponding Services. Each such agreement shall have an aggregate first-year cost not exceeding $350,000 and shall otherwise be on terms the Authorized Officer considers commercially reasonable.

## 7. RESOLUTION 6 – CAPITAL EXPENDITURES AT THE BRONX PLANT

**6.1** RESOLVED, that the following capital expenditures at the Bronx Plant are approved, in an aggregate amount not exceeding $920,000, to be funded from cash on hand and availability under the revolving credit facility under the Credit Agreement, to the extent permitted by it and by the MIPA:

(a) the upgrade of the dust collection system serving the flour silos and mixing room, in accordance with the recommendations of the Company's combustible dust consultant;

(b) the replacement of the grease interceptor serving the laminating and sanitation areas; and

(c) the installation of a second metal detection and check-weighing station on the bread packaging line.

**6.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that, if the MIPA requires Copperline's consent to any such expenditure, the Authorized Officers are directed to request that consent before committing to it.

## 10. RESOLUTION 9 – GENERAL AUTHORITY

**9.1** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer and the Secretary are each authorized, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all such further actions as that officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the intent and purposes of the foregoing resolutions. That authority includes the authority to:

(a) execute and deliver all such agreements, certificates, instruments, notices and other documents;

(b) make all such filings with Governmental Authorities; and

(c) pay all such fees and expenses.

The taking of any such action, or the execution and delivery of any such document, shall be conclusive evidence of that officer's authority to do so.

**9.2** FURTHER RESOLVED, that all actions previously taken by any Manager or officer of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions are approved, ratified and confirmed in all respects, to the extent consistent with those resolutions.
