# BILL OF SALE

**(Bronx Plant – Tangible Personal Property)**

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made, executed and delivered as of March 2, 2026, by **Glailirt Artisan Breads, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Praitir Baking Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## 1. RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (as it may be amended, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer has agreed to purchase from Seller, the Purchased Assets, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement.

B. The Closing under the Purchase Agreement is occurring on March 2, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"). Under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement, the Closing is deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date (the "**Effective Time**").

C. Section 3.2(a)(i) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, at the Closing. This Bill of Sale is in the form attached to the Purchase Agreement as Exhibit A.

D. The Bronx Plant is being conveyed to Buyer at the Closing by a separate bargain and sale deed with covenant against grantor's acts delivered under Section 3.2(a)(v) of the Purchase Agreement. The Assigned Contracts are being assigned to Buyer by the Assignment and Assumption Agreement. The Intellectual Property included in the Purchased Assets is being assigned to Buyer by the Intellectual Property Assignment.

NOW, THEREFORE, for the consideration set forth in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 2. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**2.1 Purchase Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including the terms "Bronx Plant," "Business," "Encumbrance," "Excluded Assets," "Inventory," "Permitted Encumbrances," "Purchased Assets" and "Tangible Personal Property."

**2.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Bill of Sale:

"**Conveyed Property**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Fixture Equipment**" means all items of production equipment, refrigeration equipment, utility equipment and other tangible personal property included in the Purchased Assets that are installed in, attached to or affixed to the buildings and structures at the Bronx Plant, to the extent those items constitute personal property rather than real property under applicable Law.

"**Records**" means the books and records described in Section 2.1(k) of the Purchase Agreement, in whatever form or medium they exist, including paper files, binders, logbooks, chart recorder output and electronic files maintained on local storage devices located at the Bronx Plant.

"**Vehicles**" means the motor vehicles, trailers and powered industrial trucks included in the Tangible Personal Property, including those listed in Part 3 of Schedule 1.

**2.3 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Bill of Sale, with references in that Section to "this Agreement" read as references to this Bill of Sale. References in this Bill of Sale to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Bill of Sale unless otherwise stated. Schedule 1 forms part of this Bill of Sale.

## 3. CONVEYANCE

**3.1 Sale and Transfer.** Effective as of the Effective Time, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, its successors and assigns, and Buyer accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, in each case to the extent included in the Purchased Assets (collectively, the "**Conveyed Property**"):

(a) all Tangible Personal Property described in Section 2.1(d) of the Purchase Agreement, including the ovens, proofers, mixers, dough sheeters, laminating lines, depositors, slicers, bagging and packaging equipment, spiral freezers, refrigeration systems, racks, pans, tools, vehicles, furniture and office equipment used in the Business, and including each item listed on Section 2.1(d) of the Disclosure Schedule and in Schedule 1;

(b) all Fixture Equipment;

(c) all Inventory described in Section 2.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement, including flour, butter, yeast, sugar and other ingredients and raw materials, packaging materials, work in process, finished goods and supplies, wherever located;

(d) all Records, together with the files, cabinets, storage media and other physical items in which the Records are kept at the Bronx Plant, other than the items retained by Seller under Section 2.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(e) all spare parts, replacement parts, change parts, dies, cutters, molds, screens, belts, blades and other maintenance stores held for use with any item of Tangible Personal Property, wherever located;

(f) all manuals, drawings, specifications, operating instructions, calibration certificates, maintenance logs and service histories relating to any item of Tangible Personal Property, to the extent in Seller's possession or control; and

(g) all rights of Seller under any manufacturer's, vendor's, supplier's or installer's warranty, guarantee or service commitment relating to any of the foregoing, to the extent transferable and to the extent included in the Purchased Assets under Section 2.1(i) of the Purchase Agreement.

**3.2 Excluded Assets.** Notwithstanding Section 3.1, the Conveyed Property does not include, and Seller is not selling, assigning, transferring, conveying or delivering to Buyer, any Excluded Asset. Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in, to and under the Excluded Assets.

**3.3 Title.** Seller conveys the Conveyed Property to Buyer free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances. Encumbrances on any Conveyed Property securing Closing Indebtedness are released at the Closing upon payment of the amounts specified in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(vi) of the Purchase Agreement, and Seller shall cause the applicable UCC-3 termination statements and other release instruments to be filed or delivered as provided in those payoff letters.

**3.4 To Have and to Hold.** TO HAVE AND TO HOLD the Conveyed Property unto Buyer, its successors and assigns, to and for its and their own use and behalf forever.

## 4. NO ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITIES

**4.1 No Assumption.** This Bill of Sale is an instrument of conveyance only. Buyer does not assume, and nothing in this Bill of Sale shall be construed as an assumption by Buyer of, any Liability of Seller or any of its Affiliates. Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities is made solely under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement.

**4.2 Excluded Liabilities.** The Excluded Liabilities remain the sole responsibility of Seller, and Seller shall pay and satisfy them in due course as provided in Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. DELIVERY AND POSSESSION

**5.1 Possession.** Seller shall deliver possession of the Conveyed Property to Buyer at the Effective Time. Conveyed Property located at the Bronx Plant shall be delivered in place, and no physical removal or relocation of any item is required for delivery to be complete. Seller shall deliver to Buyer at the Closing all keys, access cards, alarm codes, combinations, passwords, software license keys and service credentials in Seller's possession that are necessary to operate or access any item of Conveyed Property.

**5.2 Conveyed Property Located Elsewhere.** To the extent any Conveyed Property is in the possession of a carrier, warehouse operator, maintenance contractor, ingredient supplier or other third party at the Effective Time, Seller shall, at Buyer's request, deliver to that third party a written notice of the transfer to Buyer and a direction to hold the Conveyed Property for the account of Buyer, and shall otherwise cooperate with Buyer to obtain possession of it.

**5.3 Vehicle Titles and Registrations.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer the original certificate of title for each titled Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer and free of any noted lien other than liens released at the Closing under Section 3.3, together with a properly completed odometer disclosure statement where required by Law. Seller shall execute any power of attorney, application or other form that the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles or any other Governmental Authority reasonably requires to record the transfer of title or registration of any Vehicle to Buyer. Seller shall surrender or cancel the license plates for each Vehicle in accordance with applicable Law, and Seller shall cancel its insurance coverage for the Vehicles only after Buyer has confirmed in writing that Buyer's coverage is in effect.

**5.4 Records.** Seller may retain copies of Records to the extent permitted by Section 10.1 of the Purchase Agreement, subject to the confidentiality obligations in Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement. Records relating to the Business that are maintained on Seller's enterprise systems and are not located at the Bronx Plant shall be made available and migrated to Buyer as part of the Services under the Transition Services Agreement.

## 6. FURTHER ASSURANCES; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**6.1 Further Assurances.** At any time and from time to time after the Closing, at Buyer's request and without further consideration, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, execute, acknowledge and deliver such other instruments of sale, transfer, conveyance and assignment, provide such materials and information, and take such other actions as Buyer may reasonably request to more effectively transfer, convey and assign to Buyer, and to confirm Buyer's title to, all of the Conveyed Property, to put Buyer in actual possession and operating control of the Conveyed Property, and to assist Buyer in exercising all rights with respect to the Conveyed Property.

**6.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably constitutes and appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller or Buyer but on behalf and for the benefit of Buyer:

(a) to demand and receive from time to time any and all of the Conveyed Property and to make endorsements and give receipts and releases for and in respect of the Conveyed Property;

(b) to institute, prosecute, compromise and settle any and all Actions that Buyer may consider proper in order to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Conveyed Property; and

(c) to defend or compromise any and all Actions in respect of any of the Conveyed Property.

Seller acknowledges that the foregoing powers are coupled with an interest and are irrevocable by Seller, including by its dissolution. Buyer shall not exercise the power of attorney in this Section 6.2 in respect of any matter unless Seller has failed to take the requested action within ten (10) Business Days after Buyer's written request.

**6.3 Wrong Pockets.** If, after the Closing, Seller or any of its Affiliates holds or receives any Conveyed Property, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer and shall promptly transfer and deliver it to Buyer, for no additional consideration, in accordance with Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement. If any Excluded Asset has been transferred to Buyer under this Bill of Sale, Buyer shall promptly transfer it to Seller in accordance with that Section.

## 7. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**7.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under, and in accordance with, the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnities of Seller and Buyer contained in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale, and the rights and remedies of the Parties with respect to them are set forth in the Purchase Agreement.

**7.2 No Additional Representations.** Seller makes no representation or warranty in this Bill of Sale other than the warranty of title expressed in Section 3.3. Nothing in this Bill of Sale is intended to create any representation or warranty in addition to those set forth in the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. MISCELLANEOUS

**8.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale is binding upon Seller and its successors and assigns and inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

**8.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Bill of Sale is for the sole benefit of Buyer and Seller and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in this Bill of Sale confers upon any other Person any legal or equitable right, benefit or remedy.

**8.3 Notices.** All notices and other communications under this Bill of Sale shall be in writing and shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**8.4 Severability.** If any term or provision of this Bill of Sale is invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall not affect any other term or provision of this Bill of Sale or render that term or provision unenforceable in any other jurisdiction.

**8.5 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended, modified or supplemented only by a written instrument signed by Seller and Buyer.

**8.6 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**8.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Sections 11.7 and 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement apply to any suit, Action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, as if set forth in full in this Bill of Sale.

**8.8 Headings.** The headings in this Bill of Sale are for reference only and do not affect its interpretation.

**8.9 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together constitute one instrument. A signed copy of this Bill of Sale delivered by email or other means of electronic transmission, including through DocuSign or a similar platform, has the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.
