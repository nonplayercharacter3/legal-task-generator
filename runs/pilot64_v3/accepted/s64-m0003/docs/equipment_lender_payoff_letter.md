## 1. Background and Purpose

**1.1 The Financing.** Kestrel Point Equipment Finance, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Kestrel Point**" or "**we**"), is the lender and secured party under the Master Loan and Security Agreement No. KP-22-0614 dated as of June 14, 2022 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified, the "**Master Agreement**"), with Glailirt Artisan Breads, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Borrower**"). The following Equipment Schedules are issued under the Master Agreement and are listed in Annex A:

(a) Equipment Schedule No. 001 dated June 14, 2022;

(b) Equipment Schedule No. 002 dated September 30, 2022; and

(c) Equipment Schedule No. 003 dated April 11, 2023.

Each is referred to as a "**Schedule** ," and together they are the "**Schedules**." The Master Agreement, the Schedules, the promissory notes issued under them and each related security agreement, certificate and instrument are, together, the "**Loan Documents**."

**1.2 The Transaction.** We understand the following:

(a) Borrower has agreed to sell substantially all of the assets of its wholesale bread, rolls and laminated pastry business to Praitir Baking Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Purchaser**"), under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 between Borrower, as seller, and Purchaser, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**");

(b) the sale includes the equipment and vehicles that secure Borrower's obligations to us;

(c) the closing of the sale (the "**Closing**") is scheduled for March 2, 2026; and

(d) at the Closing, the obligations owing to us under the Loan Documents will be paid in full from the closing proceeds by wire transfer made on Borrower's behalf.

**1.3 Purpose of this Letter.** Borrower has asked us to state the following in this letter (this "**Payoff Letter**"):

(a) the amount required to pay in full all obligations of Borrower under the Loan Documents;

(b) our wire instructions; and

(c) the actions we will take to release our security interests and liens upon receipt of that amount.

We understand that this Payoff Letter will be delivered at the Closing as a payoff letter from the holder of Closing Indebtedness, within the meaning of the Purchase Agreement. We have not reviewed the Purchase Agreement, and nothing in this Payoff Letter makes us a party to it.

## 5. Effect of Payment

**5.1 Satisfaction of Obligations.** Upon receipt of the Payoff Amount, and any applicable Per Diem Amount, in accordance with Section 4, automatically and without further action by any Person:

(a) all Obligations shall be paid and satisfied in full;

(b) the Loan Documents shall terminate and be of no further force or effect, except for those provisions that by their terms survive repayment, as described in Section 5.3;

(c) all security interests, liens and other encumbrances granted to or held by Kestrel Point in or on the Collateral, or any other property of Borrower, shall be released and terminated; and

(d) Kestrel Point shall have no further right, title or interest in or to any of the Collateral.

**5.2 No Further Claims Against Collateral.** After the Payoff Time, Kestrel Point will not assert any claim against the Collateral, or against Purchaser as the owner of the Collateral, in respect of any Obligations or the Loan Documents.

**5.3 Surviving Provisions.** The following provisions of the Loan Documents survive the Payoff Time, but only as obligations of Borrower and only to the extent they relate to periods before the Payoff Time:

(a) Borrower's obligations to reimburse Kestrel Point for taxes assessed on the Collateral for periods before the Payoff Time; and

(b) Borrower's indemnification obligations under Section 12 of the Master Agreement.

No surviving provision gives Kestrel Point any security interest or lien in any of the Collateral or any recourse to Purchaser or Purchaser's assets.

**5.4 Reinstatement.** If any payment of the Payoff Amount is subsequently avoided, rescinded or required to be returned by Kestrel Point under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, the Obligations so returned shall be reinstated against Borrower, but only to the extent of the amount returned. Any such reinstatement shall be a claim against Borrower only. It shall not revive any security interest or lien on any Collateral that has been sold to Purchaser, and it shall not create any claim against Purchaser.
