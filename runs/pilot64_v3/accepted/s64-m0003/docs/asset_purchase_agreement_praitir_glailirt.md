# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 9, 2026, by and between **Glailirt Artisan Breads, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Praitir Baking Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of producing, packaging and distributing wholesale bread, rolls and laminated pastry products to foodservice, grocery and institutional customers from its production plant in the Bronx, New York (as conducted by Seller on the date of this Agreement, the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. At the Closing, Seller and Buyer will enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller will provide certain transitional services to Buyer after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

### 2.5 Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), plus or minus, as applicable, the amount by which the Closing Inventory Value exceeds or is less than the Target Inventory Value set forth on Schedule 2.6 (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to the holders of Closing Indebtedness, on behalf of Seller, the amounts set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(vi); and

(b) to Seller, an amount equal to the Base Purchase Price, plus or minus the amount by which the Estimated Closing Inventory Value exceeds or is less than the Target Inventory Value, less the amounts paid under Section 2.5(a),

in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in writing by Seller or the applicable payee at least two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date.

### 2.6 Purchase Price Adjustment

(a) *Estimated Inventory; Closing Statement.* At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Closing Inventory Value (the "**Estimated Closing Inventory Value**"), together with reasonable supporting detail. Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's calculation of the Closing Inventory Value and the resulting Purchase Price (the "**Closing Statement**"). Buyer shall give Seller and its Representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Inventory, the physical count records and the books and records of the Business as necessary for Seller to prepare the Closing Statement, including through the Services provided under the Transition Services Agreement.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with any item in the Closing Statement, Buyer may deliver to Seller, within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Closing Statement (the "**Review Period**"), a written notice (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for the disagreement, together with reasonable supporting documentation. During the Review Period, Seller shall give Buyer and its Representatives reasonable access to the working papers and supporting records used by Seller in preparing the Closing Statement.

(c) *Effect of No Objection.* If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within the Review Period, the Closing Statement and the Closing Inventory Value set forth in it shall be final and binding on the Parties. Any item not specifically disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement shall be final and binding on the Parties.

(d) *Resolution.* If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve the disputed items. Any disputed items resolved in writing by the Parties shall be final and binding. Any disputed items not so resolved shall be submitted to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to Buyer and Seller (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator, shall consider only the items in dispute, and shall determine each disputed item within the range of the amounts proposed by Buyer and Seller. The Independent Accountant shall deliver its written determination within thirty (30) days after its engagement, and that determination shall be final and binding on the Parties absent manifest error. The fees and expenses of the Independent Accountant shall be borne by Seller and Buyer in inverse proportion to the relative amounts of the disputed items determined in favor of each.

(e) *Payment.* Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Inventory Value becomes final and binding, (i) if the Purchase Price as finally determined exceeds the amount paid at Closing under Section 2.5, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller, and (ii) if the amount paid at Closing exceeds the Purchase Price as finally determined, Seller shall pay the excess to Buyer, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

(f) *Treatment.* Any payment under this Section 2.6 shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

### 2.7 Allocation of Purchase Price

The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Code and the methodology set forth on Schedule 2.7 (the "**Allocation Schedule**"). Buyer shall deliver a draft Allocation Schedule to Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. If Seller objects within thirty (30) days after receipt, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the objection and, failing resolution, shall refer the dispute to the Independent Accountant. Buyer and Seller shall file all Tax Returns, including IRS Form 8594, consistently with the Allocation Schedule as finally determined.

### 2.8 Withholding

Buyer may deduct and withhold from the Purchase Price any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under any applicable Tax Law. Any amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to Seller.

### 2.9 Third-Party Consents
