# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and between **Glailirt Artisan Breads, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Praitir Baking Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of February 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller substantially all of the assets of Seller's wholesale bread, rolls and laminated pastry business conducted from the Bronx Plant.

B. To ensure an orderly transition of the Business to Buyer, Seller has agreed to provide Buyer with certain services for a limited period after the Closing, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. The execution and delivery of this Agreement is a deliverable at the Closing under Section 3.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Defined Terms

The following terms have the meanings specified in this Section 1.1:

"**Additional Services**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3.

"**Buyer Data**" means all data and records relating to the Business, the Purchased Assets or Buyer's operations that are created, received, stored or processed by Seller in providing the Services, including production, sanitation, lot and traceability records, customer order data and payroll data relating to Transferred Employees.

"**Force Majeure Event**" has the meaning set forth in Section 12.7.

"**Monthly Service Fee**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Pass-Through Costs**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Seller Personnel**" means the employees, agents and subcontractors of Seller or its Affiliates who perform any Services.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.

"**Service Fees**" means, collectively, the Monthly Service Fee, the Pass-Through Costs, the amounts payable under Section 4.3 and any fees agreed for Additional Services.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as that Schedule may be amended under Section 2.3, together with any Additional Services.

"**Transition Period**" means the period of 180 days beginning on the Closing Date.

### 1.2 Purchase Agreement Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

### 1.3 Interpretation

In this Agreement: (a) the words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation"; (b) the word "or" is not exclusive; (c) references to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated; (d) headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation; and (e) references to days are to calendar days unless Business Days are specified. The Schedules form part of this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

### 2.1 Provision of Services

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, during the Transition Period Seller shall provide, or cause its Affiliates or permitted subcontractors to provide, the Services to Buyer. Each Service shall be provided for the period stated for that Service in Schedule A or, if no period is stated, for the Transition Period.

### 2.2 Scope

Seller is not required to provide any service that is not a Service, or to provide any Service (a) in a volume or at a frequency materially greater than that at which the corresponding service was provided to the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, or (b) in a manner that would require Seller to violate any applicable Law, Permit or Contract to which Seller is a party. If Seller believes any Service would cause such a violation, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer, and the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to identify a lawful alternative means of providing the Service.

### 2.3 Additional Services

If Buyer identifies a service that was provided to the Business by Seller or its Affiliates during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date and that is reasonably necessary for the operation of the Business but is not described in Schedule A, Buyer may request that Seller provide that service. Seller shall consider each request in good faith and, if Seller agrees, the Parties shall document the service, its duration and its fee in a written amendment to Schedule A (each, an "**Additional Service**"). The fee for any Additional Service shall be determined on a basis consistent with the methodology used to determine the Monthly Service Fee.

### 2.4 Cooperation

Buyer shall provide Seller with such information, decisions and approvals as Seller reasonably requests in order to perform the Services, and shall make available to Seller Personnel reasonable access to the Bronx Plant and Buyer's systems and personnel to the extent required. Seller shall not be responsible for any failure to perform a Service to the extent caused by Buyer's failure to perform its obligations under this Section 2.4.

### 2.5 Migration

During the Transition Period, Seller shall cooperate in good faith with Buyer and its Representatives to plan and implement the migration of each Service, and of all Buyer Data, to Buyer or its designated service providers. That cooperation includes providing reasonable knowledge transfer, documentation of processes and systems, and data extracts in a commercially reasonable, industry-standard format. Seller shall not charge any fee for migration assistance in addition to the Service Fees, except for reasonable out-of-pocket costs approved in advance by Buyer in writing.

## 3. SERVICE STANDARDS

### 3.1 Standard of Performance

Seller shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) with substantially the same degree of care, skill, priority and diligence, and at substantially the same level of quality, timeliness and volume, as Seller provided the corresponding services to the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and (c) in compliance with all applicable Laws, including the Food Laws and Environmental Law.

### 3.2 Food Safety Services

Without limiting Section 3.1, to the extent any Service involves the production, sanitation, quality assurance, environmental monitoring, allergen control or supplier verification functions at the Bronx Plant, Seller shall perform that Service in accordance with the written food safety plan, allergen control program, supplier verification program and environmental monitoring program delivered to Buyer under Section 4.19(d) of the Purchase Agreement, as those programs may be updated by Buyer and communicated to Seller in writing. Seller shall promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, notify Buyer of any positive pathogen result, allergen cross-contact event, foreign material finding or other food safety deviation identified by Seller Personnel in performing the Services.

### 3.3 Records

Seller shall maintain complete and accurate records of the Services performed, including time records for Seller Personnel and supporting documentation for all Pass-Through Costs and other amounts invoiced under Section 4. Seller shall make those records available to Buyer for inspection and copying on reasonable notice during the Transition Period and for one (1) year after its end.

### 3.4 Disclaimer

Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement or in the Purchase Agreement, Seller makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, with respect to the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 4. SERVICE FEES AND PAYMENT

### 4.1 Monthly Service Fee

In consideration of the Services described in Schedule A, Buyer shall pay Seller a fee of $142,000 per month (the "**Monthly Service Fee**") for each calendar month or portion of a month during the Transition Period. The Monthly Service Fee for any partial month shall be prorated on the basis of the number of days in that month during which Services are provided. If Buyer terminates any Service before the end of the Transition Period under Section 5.2, the Monthly Service Fee shall be reduced from the effective date of that termination by the amount allocated to that Service in Schedule A.

### 4.2 Pass-Through Costs

In addition to the Monthly Service Fee, Buyer shall reimburse Seller for the reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs actually incurred by Seller to third parties in providing the Services, without markup, to the extent those costs are identified as pass-through costs in Schedule A or are approved in advance by Buyer in writing (the "**Pass-Through Costs**"). Pass-Through Costs include third-party software license and maintenance fees allocable to the Services, third-party carrier charges for deliveries made on Buyer's behalf and consumable supplies purchased for the Business at Buyer's request.

### 4.3 Occupancy Costs

During the Transition Period, Buyer shall reimburse Seller for all rent, utilities (including electricity, natural gas, water and sewer charges), real estate taxes, insurance premiums allocable to the premises, and repair and maintenance costs of the Facilities, to the extent paid or incurred by Seller and attributable to the Transition Period. Amounts payable under this Section 4.3 shall form part of the Service Fees, shall be invoiced monthly in arrears together with the Monthly Service Fee, and shall be accompanied by copies of the underlying landlord, utility, taxing authority and vendor invoices. Amounts attributable to a period that falls only partly within the Transition Period shall be prorated on a daily basis.

### 4.4 Invoicing and Payment

Seller shall invoice Buyer monthly in arrears, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, for the Service Fees payable for that month. Each invoice shall set forth in reasonable detail the Monthly Service Fee, each category of Pass-Through Costs and each category of amounts under Section 4.3, with supporting documentation. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller. Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall bear interest from the due date until paid at a rate per annum equal to the prime rate as published in *The Wall Street Journal* on the due date, plus two percent (2%).

### 4.5 Invoice Disputes

If Buyer disputes any amount in an invoice in good faith, Buyer shall notify Seller in writing before the payment due date, specifying the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Service Coordinators shall attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days. Any dispute not so resolved shall be escalated under Section 10.1. Seller shall not suspend any Service by reason of a good-faith invoice dispute.

### 4.6 Taxes

The Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value added and similar Taxes. Buyer shall pay any such Taxes properly imposed on the provision of the Services, to the extent Seller separately states them on its invoices. Each Party shall bear its own income, franchise and similar Taxes. The Parties shall cooperate to minimize, to the extent permitted by Law, any Taxes imposed on the Services.

### 4.7 No Set-Off

Except for amounts disputed under Section 4.5, Buyer shall not set off any amount owed to it by Seller under the Purchase Agreement or any other Ancillary Agreement against the Service Fees.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 5.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the Closing Date and, unless earlier terminated under this Section 5 or Section 12.2, shall continue until the expiration of the Transition Period.

### 5.2 Termination of Services by Buyer

Buyer may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Seller. Schedule A identifies any Service that depends on another Service and cannot be terminated separately from it. Termination of one or more Services shall not affect the provision of the remaining Services.

### 5.3 Termination for Breach

Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure that breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice describing it in reasonable detail. Seller may not terminate this Agreement or suspend any Service for non-payment unless an undisputed invoice remains unpaid for forty-five (45) days after its due date and Buyer has failed to pay it within fifteen (15) days after a second written notice from Seller.

### 5.4 Extension

At Buyer's written request delivered at least thirty (30) days before the end of the Transition Period, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith an extension of any Service for up to an additional ninety (90) days, at a fee consistent with the methodology used to determine the Monthly Service Fee. No extension shall be effective unless set forth in a written amendment signed by both Parties.

### 5.5 Effect of Termination

Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, (a) Buyer shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, (b) Seller shall promptly deliver to Buyer all Buyer Data in its possession in the format described in Section 2.5, and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 8.3. Sections 3.3, 4.4, 4.5, 4.6, 5.5, 8, 9, 10 and 11, and Section 12 other than Section 12.2, shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 6. GOVERNANCE AND PERSONNEL

### 6.1 Service Coordinators

Each Party shall designate an individual to act as its primary point of contact for the administration of this Agreement (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). The initial Service Coordinators are identified in Schedule B. Either Party may replace its Service Coordinator on written notice to the other Party. The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least every two (2) weeks during the Transition Period to review the performance of the Services, the status of migration and any open issues.

### 6.2 Seller Personnel

Seller shall determine the Seller Personnel who will perform the Services, provided that Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make available the individuals identified for each Service in Schedule A and shall not reassign any of those individuals away from the Services without reasonable prior notice to Buyer. Seller Personnel shall remain employees or contractors of Seller or its Affiliates, and Seller shall be solely responsible for their compensation, benefits, withholding and employment-related Taxes.

### 6.3 Site Rules

While at the Bronx Plant, Seller Personnel shall comply with Buyer's reasonable safety, security, good manufacturing practice, hygiene, allergen and visitor policies as communicated to Seller in writing, including policies relating to protective clothing, hand washing, glass and brittle plastics and combustible dust control.

### 6.4 Subcontracting

Seller may use subcontractors to perform any Service that was performed by a subcontractor for the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date. Seller may not otherwise subcontract any Service without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Seller remains responsible for the performance of each Service by any subcontractor as if Seller had performed it directly.

### 6.5 Independent Contractor

Seller is an independent contractor in performing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

## 7. SYSTEMS ACCESS AND DATA

### 7.1 Systems Access

To the extent any Service requires Buyer personnel to access Seller's information technology systems, including Seller's enterprise resource planning, order management, warehouse management, route accounting and payroll systems, Seller shall grant that access, limited to the functionality and data relating to the Business. Buyer shall cause its personnel to comply with Seller's reasonable information security policies communicated in writing.

### 7.2 Buyer Data

As between the Parties, all Buyer Data is the property of Buyer. Seller shall use Buyer Data only to provide the Services, shall segregate Buyer Data from Seller's own data to the extent reasonably practicable, and shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect Buyer Data against unauthorized access, use or disclosure.

### 7.3 Security Incidents

Seller shall notify Buyer in writing within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Buyer Data, and shall cooperate with Buyer in investigating and remediating the incident and in making any notification required by Law.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 8.1 Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement, including Buyer Data, recipes, formulas, production processes, customer and pricing information and the terms of this Agreement. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure and, in the case of information concerning the Business held by Seller, was not included in the Purchased Assets, (c) is lawfully received from a third party not under a duty of confidentiality, or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

### 8.2 Obligations

The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its Representatives who need to know it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 8. If the Receiving Party is required by Law to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt notice, to the extent legally permitted, and disclose only the portion legally required.

### 8.3 Return or Destruction

Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except that the Receiving Party may retain copies required by Law or by bona fide record retention policies, which shall remain subject to this Section 8. This Section 8 supplements, and does not limit, Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 9.1 Ownership

Nothing in this Agreement transfers any ownership interest in either Party's Intellectual Property. Any work product created by Seller Personnel specifically for Buyer in performing the Services, including reports, process documentation, production schedules and data extracts, shall be owned by Buyer, and Seller assigns to Buyer all of its right, title and interest in that work product.

### 9.2 License

Seller grants Buyer a non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable license during the Transition Period to use any Intellectual Property of Seller that is not a Purchased Asset, solely to the extent necessary for Buyer to receive and use the Services.

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

### 10.1 Escalation

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be referred to the Service Coordinators. If the Service Coordinators do not resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days, either Party may refer it to the President of Seller and the Chief Executive Officer of Buyer, who shall meet and attempt in good faith to resolve it within fifteen (15) Business Days after referral. If the dispute is not so resolved, either Party may pursue any remedy available to it under Section 12.10. Nothing in this Section 10.1 prevents a Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief at any time.

### 10.2 Limitation of Liability

Neither Party shall be liable to the other Party under this Agreement for any punitive or exemplary damages, or for any consequential damages that were not reasonably foreseeable, except to the extent paid to a third party under a Third-Party Claim. The aggregate liability of Seller under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. The limitations in this Section 10.2 shall not apply to a Party's breach of Section 7 or Section 8, its indemnification obligations under Section 11, or its fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

### 11.1 By Seller

Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective Representatives from and against all Losses arising out of (a) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Seller or Seller Personnel in performing the Services, (b) any violation of Law by Seller or Seller Personnel in performing the Services, or (c) any claim by Seller Personnel relating to their employment or engagement by Seller or its Affiliates.

### 11.2 By Buyer

Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective Representatives from and against all Losses arising out of (a) Buyer's use of the Services in violation of Law or of this Agreement, or (b) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Buyer or its personnel in connection with this Agreement.

### 11.3 Procedures

The procedures set forth in Sections 9.4(b) and 9.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement shall apply, with conforming changes, to any claim for indemnification under this Section 11.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

### 12.1 Notices

All notices and other communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, with a copy of any notice of breach, termination or suspension to each Party's Service Coordinator.

### 12.2 Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. For purposes of this Section 12.2, any change of control of Buyer shall be deemed an assignment, including any merger or consolidation of Buyer with or into another Person, any transfer of a majority of the equity interests or voting power of Buyer, or any transfer of the right to direct the management and policies of Buyer, in each case whether in one transaction or a series of related transactions. If any assignment or deemed assignment occurs without Seller's prior written consent, Seller may terminate this Agreement upon ten (10) days' notice to Buyer. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 12.2 is void. Subject to this Section 12.2, this Agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### 12.3 Amendment; Waiver

This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended, modified or supplemented only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless set forth in writing and signed by the Party so waiving, and no failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of it.

### 12.4 Severability

If any term or provision of this Agreement is invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall not affect any other term or provision of this Agreement or render that term or provision unenforceable in any other jurisdiction, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to modify this Agreement so as to effect their original intent as closely as possible.

### 12.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries

Except as provided in Section 11, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in this Agreement confers upon any other Person any legal or equitable right, benefit or remedy.

### 12.6 Insurance

During the Transition Period, Seller shall maintain commercial general liability, product liability, automobile liability and workers' compensation insurance covering the activities of Seller Personnel in performing the Services, in amounts not less than those maintained by Seller for the Business immediately before the Closing Date, and shall furnish certificates of that insurance to Buyer on request.

### 12.7 Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance under this Agreement, other than an obligation to pay money, to the extent caused by fire, flood, storm, earthquake, epidemic, act of war or terrorism, civil unrest, governmental action, widespread utility or telecommunications failure, or any other event beyond its reasonable control (a "**Force Majeure Event**"). The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party of the Force Majeure Event and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Service Fees for any Service that is not provided during a Force Majeure Event shall abate for the period of non-performance, and if a Force Majeure Event prevents Seller from providing any Service for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days, Buyer may procure that Service from a third party for the duration of the Force Majeure Event.

### 12.8 Further Assurances

Each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

### 12.9 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

### 12.10 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial

Sections 11.7 and 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement shall apply to any suit, Action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, as if set forth in full in this Agreement.

### 12.11 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall be one and the same agreement. A signed copy of this Agreement delivered by email or other means of electronic transmission, including through DocuSign or a similar platform, shall have the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.
