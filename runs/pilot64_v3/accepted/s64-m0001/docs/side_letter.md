**Re: Side Letter to Software License Agreement – Brewhouse Integration Module and Related Implementation Matters**

Dear Ms. Dribas:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between Brikon Systems, Inc. ("**Licensor**") and Dribas Brewing Company, LLC ("**Licensee**"). It records certain commitments that Licensor has made to Licensee in connection with the implementation of the BrewLedger platform at Licensee's Wilmington and Dover breweries, including the delivery and Acceptance of the Brewhouse Integration Module.

Licensee has told Licensor that the integration of the Licensed Software with its brewhouse control systems, tank sensors and packaging line controllers is a principal reason for its decision to license the BrewLedger platform, and that Licensee is retiring its legacy production database and spreadsheet systems in reliance on that integration. Licensor and Licensee are therefore entering into this Side Letter at the same time as the Agreement. In consideration of the mutual covenants in the Agreement and in this Side Letter, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Interpretation

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. References in this Side Letter to Sections of the Agreement are to the Sections of the Agreement as in effect on the date of this Side Letter.

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Side Letter:

(a) "**Acceptance Deadline**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

(b) "**BIM**" means the Brewhouse Integration Module, as described in Section 4.5 of the Agreement and Sections A.1, B.3 and B.4 of the Schedules to the Agreement.

(c) "**BIM Readiness Notice**" means the written notice that Licensor gives under Section 4.3 of the Agreement stating that the BIM is ready for acceptance testing at a Site.

(d) "**Integration Project Lead**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

(e) "**Termination Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

(f) "**Termination Right**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

1.3 **Construction.** Section 15.16 of the Agreement applies to this Side Letter as if references in it to "this Agreement" were references to this Side Letter.

## 2. Brewhouse Integration Module Acceptance and Termination Right

2.1 **Acceptance Deadline.** Licensor shall complete the configuration and integration of the BIM at both Sites, and the BIM shall be Accepted in accordance with Section 4.3 of the Agreement, no later than June 30, 2026 (the "**Acceptance Deadline**"). Acceptance of the BIM requires that the BIM meet the Acceptance Criteria in Section B.4 of Schedule B to the Agreement at each of the Wilmington Site and the Dover Site. Acceptance at one Site alone does not constitute Acceptance of the BIM for purposes of this Section 2.

2.2 **Termination Right.** If the BIM has not been Accepted on or before the Acceptance Deadline, Licensee may terminate the Agreement in its entirety, without payment of any termination fee, early termination charge or other penalty (the "**Termination Right**"), by written notice to Licensor (a "**Termination Notice**") delivered no later than thirty (30) days after the Acceptance Deadline. The Termination Notice must be given in accordance with Section 15.5 of the Agreement and must state the date on which termination is to take effect, which must be no earlier than the date of the Termination Notice and no later than ninety (90) days after it.

2.3 **No Cure Period.** The Termination Right is not subject to the notice and cure procedure in Section 12.2 of the Agreement or the escalation procedure in Section 14.1 of the Agreement. Licensee need not show that Licensor has materially breached the Agreement in order to exercise the Termination Right.

2.4 **Relationship to Section 4.4 of the Agreement.** The Termination Right is in addition to Licensee's rights under Section 4.4 of the Agreement with respect to the BIM. Licensee's election of a remedy under Section 4.4(a) or 4.4(b) of the Agreement before the Acceptance Deadline does not waive the Termination Right, unless the BIM is Accepted on or before the Acceptance Deadline.

2.5 **Fees Pending Acceptance.** Until the BIM is Accepted, Licensor shall not invoice the Implementation Services Fees milestone payable on Acceptance of the BIM under Section A.5 of Schedule A to the Agreement.

## 3. Consequences of Exercise of the Termination Right

3.1 **Refunds.** If Licensee exercises the Termination Right, Licensor shall refund to Licensee, within thirty (30) days after the effective date of termination:

(a) all annual subscription Fees paid by Licensee for the period after the effective date of termination, calculated on a daily pro rata basis; and

(b) all Implementation Services Fees paid by Licensee under Section A.5 of Schedule A to the Agreement, including the milestone payment made on signature of the Agreement.

3.2 **No Further Fees.** On exercise of the Termination Right, Licensee is released from any obligation to pay Fees attributable to any period after the effective date of termination and any Implementation Services Fees not yet invoiced. Licensee remains responsible for undisputed annual subscription Fees attributable to the period up to the effective date of termination and for pre-approved expenses under Section 3.8 of the Agreement incurred before the date of the Termination Notice.

3.3 **Customer Data.** Section 7.8 of the Agreement applies on termination under this Side Letter. In addition, during the period between the date of the Termination Notice and the effective date of termination, Licensor shall on request provide Licensee with up to two (2) full exports of Customer Data in CSV and JSON formats, together with the data dictionary and schema documentation for those exports, at no charge.

3.4 **Transition Assistance.** Section 12.5 of the Agreement applies on termination under this Side Letter. However, the migration assistance described in Section 12.5(b) of the Agreement shall be provided at no charge for up to forty (40) hours. Any assistance beyond that amount shall be charged at the rates in Section A.6 of Schedule A to the Agreement.

3.5 **Equipment Interfaces.** Within fifteen (15) business days after the effective date of termination, Licensor shall remove or disable any connector, gateway, agent or other software that Licensor or its subcontractors installed on Licensee's brewhouse control systems, tank sensors, packaging line controllers or Site networks in performing the Implementation Services. Licensor shall return those systems to an operating configuration no less functional than their configuration before the installation, at Licensor's expense.

3.6 **Survival.** Sections 3, 8 and 9 of this Side Letter survive the termination of the Agreement under this Side Letter.

## 4. Implementation Commitments

4.1 **Implementation Plan.** Within twenty (20) business days after the date of this Side Letter, the Parties' project managers shall agree the Implementation Plan contemplated by Section 4.1 of the Agreement. The Implementation Plan shall include a schedule of activities for the BIM at each Site that is designed to achieve Acceptance of the BIM before the Acceptance Deadline. It shall also include:

(a) an inventory of the brewhouse control systems, sensors and line controllers listed in Section B.3 of Schedule B to the Agreement, identifying for each device the make, model, firmware version and data interface to be used;

(b) the dates on which Licensor will deliver each BIM Readiness Notice;

(c) the dates on which Licensor personnel will attend each Site for installation, configuration and testing; and

(d) the dependencies on Licensee, its equipment vendors and its network providers that Licensor has identified, with the dates by which each must be satisfied.

4.2 **Target Dates for Other Modules.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver the BIM Readiness Notice for the Wilmington Site no later than April 30, 2026 and for the Dover Site no later than May 15, 2026. Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve Acceptance of the Production Scheduling Module and the Tank Management Module before it delivers the first BIM Readiness Notice. The target dates in this Section 4.2 are planning targets. Licensor's failure to meet them is not, of itself, a breach of the Agreement, and the target dates do not affect the Acceptance Deadline.

4.3 **Equipment Vendor Coordination.** Licensor shall be responsible for engaging directly with the technical contacts of Licensee's equipment vendors identified under Section B.5 of Schedule B to the Agreement. Licensor shall bear its own costs of that engagement. Licensee shall make introductions to those contacts within ten (10) business days after Licensor's request and shall use reasonable efforts to procure their participation. Licensee is not responsible for any fees charged by an equipment vendor at Licensor's request unless Licensee has approved those fees in advance in writing.

4.4 **Connectivity Assessment.** Within thirty (30) days after the date of this Side Letter, Licensor shall conduct an on-site assessment of the network connectivity at each Site against the minimum requirements in the Documentation. Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a written report identifying any upgrades that Licensor considers necessary for the BIM to meet the Acceptance Criteria. The assessment and report are included in the Implementation Services Fees.

4.5 **Test Environment.** Licensor shall make available to Licensee a non-production test environment of the Licensed Software, populated with a representative sample of Licensee's migrated data, for the duration of acceptance testing for each Module and for at least sixty (60) days after Acceptance of the BIM. Use of the test environment by Authorized Users does not count toward live production use for purposes of Section 4.3(c) of the Agreement.

4.6 **Parallel Operation.** Until the BIM is Accepted at a Site, Licensor shall not require Licensee to decommission its legacy production database or spreadsheet systems at that Site as a condition of performing any Implementation Services. Licensor shall support Licensee's parallel operation of those systems with the Licensed Software where reasonably required for continuity of Licensee's brewing operations and regulatory recordkeeping.

## 5. Project Staffing

5.1 **Integration Project Lead.** Licensor designates Priya Raman, Senior Solution Architect, as the Licensor lead for the BIM (the "**Integration Project Lead**"). The Integration Project Lead shall devote at least fifty percent (50%) of her working time to Licensee's implementation from the date of this Side Letter until Acceptance of the BIM. She shall attend each Site in person for the installation and acceptance testing of the BIM at that Site.

5.2 **Replacement.** Licensor shall not remove or reassign the Integration Project Lead before Acceptance of the BIM except for reasons of death, disability, resignation, termination of employment for cause or extended leave. If the Integration Project Lead ceases to act, Licensor shall within ten (10) business days propose a replacement with comparable experience in brewhouse equipment integration. Licensee may interview the proposed replacement and may reasonably object to the appointment. Licensor shall bear the cost of transferring knowledge to the replacement, and no such time shall be charged to Licensee.

5.3 **Subcontractors.** Licensor shall identify in the Implementation Plan any subcontractor that will perform integration work on Licensee's equipment at either Site. Section 15.4 of the Agreement applies to each such subcontractor. Any subcontractor personnel attending a Site shall comply with Licensee's site safety, sanitation and food-safety rules as provided to Licensor, including rules governing entry to brewhouse, cellar and packaging areas.

## 6. Reporting and Governance

6.1 **Weekly Status Reports.** From the date the Implementation Plan is agreed until Acceptance of the BIM, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee's project manager a written status report each week. Each report shall describe progress against the Implementation Plan, open issues and risks, any Errors identified during configuration or testing with their severity levels, and any Licensee dependency that Licensor considers to be at risk.

6.2 **Steering Meetings.** The Parties' project managers, together with the Integration Project Lead and a Licensee representative with operational responsibility for each Site, shall meet at least every two weeks until Acceptance of the BIM. A senior executive of each Party shall attend a steering meeting at least monthly. Meetings may be held by video conference, except that at least one meeting each calendar quarter shall be held in person at a Site.

6.3 **Delay Notices.** Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days, after Licensor becomes aware of any circumstance that Licensor reasonably expects will prevent Acceptance of the BIM by the Acceptance Deadline. The notice shall describe the circumstance, its expected effect and the steps Licensor proposes to take in response. Licensor's notice, or its failure to give notice, under this Section 6.3 does not affect the Acceptance Deadline or the Termination Right.

6.4 **Notices under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.** Any notice that Licensor gives under Section 4.2 of the Agreement relating to the BIM shall be copied to Licensee's Chief Executive Officer at the address in Section 15.5 of the Agreement and shall be raised at the next steering meeting under Section 6.2.

## 7. Training and Go-Live Support

7.1 **Brewhouse Operator Training.** In addition to the training described in Section B.1(e) of Schedule B to the Agreement, Licensor shall provide one (1) on-site training session at each Site, of no less than four (4) hours, for Licensee's brewers and cellar staff on the use of the BIM. The session shall cover monitoring of live brewhouse and tank data, handling of sensor alarms and the reconciliation of BIM data with batch records. These sessions are included in the Implementation Services Fees.

7.2 **Extended Go-Live Support.** For the BIM only, the go-live support described in Section B.1(f) of Schedule B to the Agreement shall extend to fifteen (15) business days following the cutover date at each Site. During the first five (5) business days after cutover at each Site, a Licensor consultant shall be present on site during Licensee's brewing shifts.

7.3 **Brewing Calendar.** Licensor shall schedule cutover at each Site in consultation with Licensee. Licensor shall not schedule cutover during the week preceding or following any seasonal release that Licensee identifies to Licensor at least thirty (30) days in advance.
