# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Software License Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Brikon Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Market Street, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**"), and **Dribas Brewing Company, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 415 South Madison Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its BrewLedger brewery production, tank management and compliance software platform to Licensee for use at Licensee's breweries in Wilmington and Dover, Delaware. On the same date the Parties also entered into a side letter relating to the Brewhouse Integration Module and related implementation matters (the "**Side Letter**") and the Data Processing Addendum.

B. Licensor delivered the BIM Readiness Notice for the Wilmington Site on May 22, 2026 and for the Dover Site on June 19, 2026. The Brewhouse Integration Module had not been Accepted at either Site as of the Acceptance Deadline. Acceptance testing of the Brewhouse Integration Module is continuing at both Sites under Section 4.4 of the Agreement.

C. By letter dated August 14, 2026, which Licensee delivered to Licensor on August 14, 2026 (the "**August Notice**"), Licensee gave notice that it was exercising the Termination Right under Section 2.2 of the Side Letter, with termination to take effect on November 12, 2026. By letter dated August 28, 2026, Licensor stated that it disputes the effectiveness of the August Notice. Licensee maintains that the August Notice is effective. Each Party reserves all of its rights and remedies with respect to the August Notice, as set out in Section 6.

D. Licensee has asked Licensor to license an additional Module that supports the preparation and tracking of label approval applications, formula submissions and related alcohol-regulatory records. Licensor is willing to license that Module on the terms of this Amendment.

E. Licensee's use of the additional Module will involve Licensor hosting and processing label artwork, formula information and regulatory compliance data that Licensee supplies. The Parties have agreed to restate the indemnification provisions of the Agreement to allocate responsibility for that data.

F. The Parties also wish to record a remediation plan for the Brewhouse Integration Module and to make certain other changes to the Agreement, in each case as set out in this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and in the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement or, where the context requires, in the Side Letter.

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Amendment:

(a) "**BIM**" means the Brewhouse Integration Module.

(b) "**BIM Remediation Plan**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

(c) "**Label and Formula Compliance Module**" or "**LFC Module**" means the Module of the Licensed Software described in Section 2.1.

(d) "**Regulatory Submission Data**" means label artwork, label text, statements of composition, formula information, ingredient and processing descriptions, alcohol content data, and other information that Licensee or its Authorized Users submit to the Licensed Software for the preparation, tracking or filing of label approval applications, formula approvals or other submissions to the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau, the Delaware Alcoholic Beverage Control Commissioner, or any other federal, state or local authority that regulates the production, labeling, distribution or sale of alcoholic beverages.

1.3 **References.** References in this Amendment to Sections and Schedules of the Agreement are to the Sections of and Schedules to the Agreement as in effect immediately before the Amendment Effective Date, unless otherwise stated. Section 15.16 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if references in it to "this Agreement" were references to this Amendment.

## 2. LABEL AND FORMULA COMPLIANCE MODULE

2.1 **Description.** The LFC Module is a Module of the Licensed Software that allows Licensee to:

(a) prepare, store and version label artwork and label text for each of Licensee's products;

(b) generate draft label approval applications and formula submissions from product, recipe and production records held in the Licensed Software;

(c) track the status, approval numbers, expiration and renewal of label approvals and formula approvals for each product and package format; and

(d) maintain a record of distributor and state registration requirements associated with each approved label.

2.2 **Addition to Schedule A.** The table in Section A.1 of Schedule A to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row after the row for the Brewhouse Integration Module:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Label and Formula Compliance Module | Preparation, versioning and tracking of label artwork, label approval applications, formula submissions and related state registration records, as described in Section 2.1 of Amendment No. 1 |

2.3 **Definition of Module.** Section 1.13 of the Agreement is amended by deleting the words "and the Brewhouse Integration Module" and replacing them with "the Brewhouse Integration Module and the Label and Formula Compliance Module".

2.4 **Specifications.** The Specifications for the LFC Module are those set out in the BrewLedger Label and Formula Compliance Functional Specification, version 1.3, as made available to Licensee before the Amendment Effective Date, together with the configurations documented in the Implementation Plan as updated under Section 2.6.

2.5 **Acceptance.** The LFC Module is subject to acceptance testing and Acceptance under Sections 4.3 and 4.4 of the Agreement. In addition to the Acceptance Criteria in Section B.4(a) and B.4(b) of Schedule B to the Agreement, the LFC Module meets the Acceptance Criteria when it produces, for at least five (5) of Licensee's current products selected by Licensee, draft label approval applications and formula submissions that contain all fields required by the applicable federal forms in effect on the date of testing, populated from Licensee's records in the Licensed Software without material error.

2.6 **Implementation.** Licensor shall configure the LFC Module and migrate Licensee's existing label approval records, as provided by Licensee in spreadsheet and PDF form, into the LFC Module. Within fifteen (15) business days after the Amendment Effective Date, the Parties' project managers shall update the Implementation Plan to include a schedule of activities for the LFC Module. Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver its notice under Section 4.3 of the Agreement that the LFC Module is ready for acceptance testing no later than December 15, 2026.

2.7 **Regulatory Updates.** Section 6.4 of the Agreement applies to the LFC Module as it applies to the Compliance Reporting Module. Licensor shall update the LFC Module within a commercially reasonable time to reflect changes to federal label approval and formula submission forms and to the electronic filing requirements of the relevant authorities.

2.8 **Licensee Responsibility for Submissions.** Licensee is responsible for reviewing each label approval application, formula submission and other regulatory filing generated using the LFC Module before it is submitted, and for the accuracy and completeness of each submission that Licensee makes. The LFC Module does not submit any filing to a regulatory authority without the action of an Authorized User.

## 3. INDEMNIFICATION

Section 10 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> ## 10. INDEMNIFICATION
>
> 10.1 **Licensee Indemnity.** Licensee shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Licensor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Licensor Indemnitees**") from and against any claim, suit or proceeding brought by a third party or by any governmental or regulatory authority (a "**Licensee-Data Claim**"), and all damages, costs, settlements, fines, penalties and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) finally awarded against the Licensor Indemnitees or agreed in a settlement approved by Licensee in connection with any Licensee-Data Claim, to the extent the Licensee-Data Claim arises from (a) any label, label artwork, label text, formula, statement of composition or other Regulatory Submission Data that Licensee or its Authorized Users submit to the Licensed Software, (b) any filing or submission that Licensee makes to a regulatory authority using output of the Licensed Software, or (c) Licensee's failure to comply with laws governing the production, labeling, advertising, distribution or sale of alcoholic beverages. In this Section 10, "**Regulatory Submission Data**" has the meaning given in Amendment No. 1 to this Agreement.
>
> 10.2 **Infringement Indemnity.** Licensor shall defend Licensee, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, managers, members, employees and agents (the "**Licensee Indemnitees**") against any claim, suit or proceeding brought by a third party alleging that the Licensed Software, or Licensee's use of it in accordance with this Agreement, infringes, misappropriates or otherwise violates any Intellectual Property Right of that third party (an "**Infringement Claim**"), and shall indemnify and hold harmless the Licensee Indemnitees from and against all damages, costs, settlements, fines and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) finally awarded against them or agreed in a settlement approved by Licensor in connection with any Infringement Claim.
>
> 10.3 **Exclusions.** Licensor has no obligation under Section 10.2 to the extent an Infringement Claim arises from (a) modification of the Licensed Software by anyone other than Licensor or its subcontractors, (b) combination of the Licensed Software with any product, data or process not supplied, specified or approved by Licensor, where the claim would not have arisen but for the combination, (c) Customer Data, or (d) Licensee's continued use of an allegedly infringing version of the Licensed Software after Licensor has made available at no charge a non-infringing version with materially equivalent functionality and has given Licensee a reasonable period to implement it. Licensee has no obligation under Section 10.1 to the extent a Licensee-Data Claim arises from an Error in the Licensed Software, from Licensor's breach of this Agreement, or from Licensor's alteration of Regulatory Submission Data without Licensee's instruction.
>
> 10.4 **Mitigation.** If the Licensed Software becomes, or in Licensor's reasonable opinion is likely to become, the subject of an Infringement Claim, Licensor may at its option and expense (a) procure for Licensee the right to continue using the Licensed Software, (b) modify or replace the Licensed Software so that it becomes non-infringing while providing materially equivalent functionality, or (c) if neither (a) nor (b) is commercially reasonable, terminate this Agreement with respect to the affected Licensed Software and refund to Licensee all prepaid Fees for the period after termination, together with all Implementation Services Fees paid for the affected Module, reduced on a straight-line basis over a three-year period from the date of Acceptance of that Module.
>
> 10.5 **Procedure.** The Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, provided that a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay materially prejudices its defense, (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, provided that the indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation or admission of liability on the Indemnified Party without its prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense.
>
> 10.6 **Regulatory Proceedings.** Where a Licensee-Data Claim is brought by a governmental or regulatory authority, Licensor shall not communicate with that authority concerning the Licensee-Data Claim except as required by law or with Licensee's prior written consent, and shall promptly provide Licensee with copies of any correspondence it receives from that authority relating to Licensee.

## 5. BREWHOUSE INTEGRATION MODULE REMEDIATION

5.1 **Remediation Plan.** Within ten (10) business days after the Amendment Effective Date, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a written plan (the "**BIM Remediation Plan**") for completing acceptance testing of the BIM at each Site. The BIM Remediation Plan shall:

(a) identify each failure to meet the Acceptance Criteria in Section B.4(c) of Schedule B to the Agreement that Licensee has notified to Licensor under Section 4.4 of the Agreement, including the data latency and data loss failures recorded for the fermentation tank sensors at the Dover Site and the canning line controller at the Wilmington Site;

(b) describe the root cause of each failure, as determined by Licensor, and the corrective action Licensor will take;

(c) identify any firmware, gateway or network changes Licensor considers necessary, and state which Party is responsible for each; and

(d) set out the dates on which Licensor will redeliver the BIM for retesting at each Site.

5.2 **Staffing.** Licensor shall continue to assign the Integration Project Lead, or a replacement appointed in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Side Letter, to the BIM until the BIM is Accepted or the Agreement ends. In addition, Licensor shall assign one additional integration engineer to the BIM at each Site for the period of remediation and retesting.

5.3 **Costs.** Licensor shall bear its own costs of preparing and performing the BIM Remediation Plan. No time spent by Licensor personnel on the BIM Remediation Plan shall be charged to Licensee as additional professional services under Section A.6 of Schedule A to the Agreement.

5.4 **Reporting.** Section 6 of the Side Letter continues to apply until the BIM is Accepted. Each weekly status report under Section 6.1 of the Side Letter shall describe progress against the BIM Remediation Plan.

5.5 **Implementation Fee Milestone.** Section 2.5 of the Side Letter continues to apply. Licensor shall not invoice the Implementation Services Fees milestone payable on Acceptance of the BIM until the BIM has been Accepted at both Sites.

5.6 **Parallel Operation.** Section 4.6 of the Side Letter continues to apply at each Site until the BIM is Accepted at that Site.

5.7 **No Effect on Rights.** Licensee's participation in the BIM Remediation Plan, its continued acceptance testing of the BIM, and its continued use of the Licensed Software are without prejudice to the positions described in Recital C and are subject to Section 6.

## 6. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

6.1 **August Notice.** Licensor disputes that the August Notice validly exercised the Termination Right and disputes that the Agreement will terminate on November 12, 2026 or on any other date by reason of the August Notice. Licensee maintains that the August Notice is effective in accordance with its terms. Nothing in this Amendment resolves that dispute.

6.2 **General Reservation.** Each Party reserves all of its rights, remedies, claims and defenses under the Agreement, the Side Letter and applicable law with respect to the August Notice, the Termination Right and the failure of the BIM to be Accepted by the Acceptance Deadline. No Party waives, releases or is estopped from asserting any of them by reason of entering into this Amendment, performing under it, or accepting or making any payment under the Agreement after the date of the August Notice.

6.3 **Continued Performance.** Pending resolution of the dispute described in Section 6.1, each Party shall continue to perform its obligations under the Agreement in accordance with Section 14.3 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Dispute Resolution.** Either Party may refer the dispute described in Section 6.1 to the escalation procedure in Section 14.1 of the Agreement at any time after the Amendment Effective Date.
