# TERM SHEET

## BrewLedger Software License: Brikon Systems, Inc. and Dribas Brewing Company, LLC

**Date:** December 8, 2025

**Status:** Summary of principal terms for discussion. Non-binding except as stated in Section 15.

This term sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") summarizes the principal terms agreed in principle between Brikon Systems, Inc. ("**Brikon**" or "**Licensor**") and Dribas Brewing Company, LLC ("**Dribas**" or "**Licensee**") for the license of the BrewLedger platform and related services. The Parties will negotiate the definitive documentation described in Section 14 in good faith on the basis of this Term Sheet. Brikon and Dribas are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Parties

1.1 **Licensor.** Brikon Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 1200 Market Street, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801. Brikon develops and makes available the BrewLedger brewery production, tank management and compliance software platform.

1.2 **Licensee.** Dribas Brewing Company, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 415 South Madison Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801. Dribas operates craft breweries in Wilmington and Dover, Delaware.

1.3 **Contacts for Negotiation.** Brikon: Marcus T. Halloran, Chief Executive Officer, with Daniel Okafor, Implementation Manager, for implementation matters. Dribas: Elena V. Dribas, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Member, with Rachel Steen, Director of Operations, for operational and implementation matters.

## 2. Licensed Software and Modules

2.1 **Platform.** Brikon will license to Dribas the BrewLedger platform, delivered as a hosted software-as-a-service offering with object-code components where needed for on-site connectivity. The license will include the Documentation and all generally available updates, patches and new releases at no additional charge.

2.2 **Modules.** The license will cover the following modules:

| Module | Summary of Function |
|---|---|
| Production Scheduling Module | Brew calendar, batch planning, brewhouse and cellar scheduling, and capacity forecasting across both breweries |
| Tank Management Module | Fermentation, conditioning and bright tank allocation, gravity and temperature logging, cleaning-in-place records and tank dashboards |
| Compliance Reporting Module | Draft federal Brewer's Reports of Operations, excise tax return worksheets and Delaware state reports generated from production and removal records |
| Inventory and Packaging Module | Raw material, packaging material and finished goods inventory, lot tracking, packaging runs and keg tracking |
| Brewhouse Integration Module | Integration with brewhouse control systems, tank sensors and packaging line controllers at both breweries |

2.3 **License Scope.** The license will be non-exclusive and non-transferable, except in connection with a permitted assignment. It will permit use by Dribas and its Affiliates for internal business purposes in brewing, cellaring, packaging, warehousing, distribution and compliance functions, subject to the agreed user and site counts.

2.4 **Customary Restrictions.** The definitive agreement will contain customary restrictions on copying, reverse engineering, sublicensing, service-bureau use and use to build a competing product. It will also require compliance with applicable law, including laws governing the production, labeling and sale of alcoholic beverages.

2.5 **Future Modules.** Brikon has described a label and formula compliance module under development. The Parties expect to discuss adding it by order form or amendment once it is generally available. It is not included in the pricing in Section 4.

## 3. Sites and Users

3.1 **Sites.** The license will cover two sites:

(a) Dribas's brewery and taproom at 415 South Madison Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801; and

(b) Dribas's brewery at 88 Bay Road Industrial Park, Dover, Delaware 19901.

Additional sites may be added by order form at Brikon's then-current per-site pricing.

3.2 **Users.** Up to forty (40) named users across both sites. Additional seats will be available in blocks of five (5) at $1,200 per block per year, prorated for partial years.

## 4. Fees and Pricing

4.1 **Annual Subscription.** $96,000 per year for all modules listed in Section 2.2, two sites and forty users. Hosting, backup, disaster recovery and standard support are included.

4.2 **Pricing Basis.** The annual subscription reflects a discount of approximately twenty percent (20%) from Brikon's list pricing for a comparable deployment. Brikon has offered this discount in consideration of the exclusivity commitment described in Section 5.

4.3 **Implementation Fee.** A fixed fee of $64,000, payable in four equal milestone installments of $16,000 each:

| Milestone | Amount |
|---|---|
| Signature of the definitive agreement | $16,000 |
| Acceptance of the Production Scheduling Module and the Tank Management Module | $16,000 |
| Acceptance of the Compliance Reporting Module and the Inventory and Packaging Module | $16,000 |
| Acceptance of the Brewhouse Integration Module | $16,000 |

4.4 **Additional Professional Services.** Services outside the agreed implementation scope, if Dribas requests them in writing, will be charged at $185 per hour for consultants and $225 per hour for solution architects.

4.5 **Invoicing and Payment.** Annual subscription fees will be invoiced annually in advance from the effective date. Undisputed invoices will be payable within thirty (30) days after receipt. Dribas may withhold amounts disputed in good faith if it gives notice before the due date and pays the undisputed balance.

4.6 **Late Payment and Suspension.** Interest on late undisputed amounts will accrue at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum lawful rate. Suspension will be permitted only for undisputed amounts more than forty-five (45) days overdue, after at least fifteen (15) days' written notice.

4.7 **Renewal Pricing.** Increases on renewal will be capped at the lesser of five percent (5%) and the change in CPI-U for the preceding twelve months. Brikon must give at least ninety (90) days' notice before the end of the current term.

4.8 **Expenses.** Dribas will reimburse reasonable, documented travel expenses for on-site implementation work that Dribas has pre-approved in writing, in accordance with Dribas's travel policy.

4.9 **Taxes.** Fees will be exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes. Dribas will bear those taxes, other than taxes on Brikon's net income, property or employees.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 **Scope.** In consideration of the pricing in Section 4.2, Dribas will agree that, during the Term, it will not license or use production-scheduling software other than BrewLedger for production-scheduling software at Licensee's Wilmington and Dover breweries only. Stated as an agreed scope, the exclusivity covers production-scheduling software at Licensee's Wilmington and Dover breweries only.

5.2 **Functions and Locations Outside Scope.** Dribas will remain free to select, license, use and develop software of its choice for all other functions and at all other locations. These include its taproom point-of-sale, distributor and sales management, accounting, and raw material and finished goods inventory systems. The covenant will bind Dribas only, and not its Affiliates.

5.3 **Transition.** Dribas will have until the Acceptance of the Production Scheduling Module at each brewery to retire its existing production-scheduling spreadsheets at that brewery. Parallel use during implementation and acceptance testing will not breach the exclusivity covenant.

5.4 **Effect of Termination.** The exclusivity covenant will end automatically on expiration or termination of the definitive agreement for any reason.

## 6. Implementation and Acceptance

6.1 **Implementation Services.** Brikon will provide the following implementation services:

(a) project kickoff and an agreed implementation plan;
(b) configuration of each module for Dribas's recipes, batch sizes, tank inventory, packaging formats and site layouts;
(c) migration of up to five (5) years of historical production, inventory and compliance records from Dribas's spreadsheets and legacy production database;
(d) integration of the Brewhouse Integration Module;
(e) training of up to ten (10) administrators and forty (40) end users; and
(f) go-live support for ten (10) business days at each site.

6.2 **Project Managers.** Each Party will appoint a project manager within ten (10) business days after signature of the definitive agreement.

6.3 **Acceptance Testing.** Each module will be subject to a thirty (30) day testing period against written acceptance criteria. Acceptance will occur on any of the following:

(a) Dribas's written confirmation that the module meets the criteria;
(b) expiry of the testing period without a written failure notice; or
(c) thirty (30) consecutive days of live production use other than for testing.

6.4 **Failures.** Brikon will correct notified failures and redeliver for retesting within thirty (30) days. If a module fails after the second retest, Dribas may extend the correction period, accept the module with an equitable fee reduction, or terminate as to that module with a refund of all fees paid for it.

6.5 **Brewhouse Integration Scope.** The Brewhouse Integration Module will be integrated with:

(a) the 30-barrel brewhouse control system at Wilmington and the 15-barrel brewhouse control system at Dover;
(b) temperature and pressure sensors on all fermentation and bright tanks at both breweries; and
(c) the canning line controller at Wilmington and the kegging line controller at Dover.

Acceptance criteria will include near real-time data capture, with latency of no more than sixty (60) seconds, over a continuous test period of at least seventy-two (72) hours at each site. Data loss during that period must not exceed one percent (1%) of expected readings.

6.6 **Brewhouse Integration Commitment.** Integration with Dribas's brewhouse equipment is a principal reason for Dribas's selection of BrewLedger. The Parties will record the following commitments in a side letter signed together with the definitive agreement:

(a) the Brewhouse Integration Module will be Accepted at both breweries by June 30, 2026;
(b) if it is not, Dribas may terminate the entire agreement without any termination fee, by written notice delivered within thirty days after that date, and will receive refunds of prepaid subscription fees and implementation fees paid; and
(c) Brikon will provide associated staffing, reporting and transition commitments.

6.7 **Integration Lead.** Brikon expects to designate Priya Raman, Senior Solution Architect, as its lead for the Brewhouse Integration Module. Her role and replacement protections will be set out in the side letter.

6.8 **Dribas Responsibilities.** Dribas will:

(a) provide reasonable access to its sites, personnel, systems and equipment;
(b) provide network connectivity meeting the documented minimum requirements;
(c) introduce Brikon to its equipment vendors' technical contacts; and
(d) provide personnel for acceptance testing.

Brikon will not be responsible for delay caused by Dribas's failure to cooperate if Brikon gives prompt written notice of that failure.

## 7. Hosting, Service Levels and Support

7.1 **Hosting Location.** Brikon will host the platform and Dribas's data in the United States. Brikon will not relocate the data outside the United States without Dribas's prior written consent.

7.2 **Availability.** Brikon will commit to monthly availability of 99.5%, excluding notified scheduled maintenance outside 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days. Service credits will apply as follows:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.5% but at or above 99.0% | 5% of monthly fees |
| Below 99.0% but at or above 98.0% | 10% of monthly fees |
| Below 98.0% but at or above 95.0% | 20% of monthly fees |
| Below 95.0% | 30% of monthly fees |

7.3 **Chronic Failure.** If Brikon misses the availability commitment in three (3) months in any rolling six-month period, Dribas may terminate and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the period after termination.

7.4 **Offline Capability.** The Production Scheduling Module and the Tank Management Module will include local caching. This will allow brew schedules and tank status to be viewed at each site during a connectivity interruption.

7.5 **Support.** Standard support will be available from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days. A 24x7 emergency line will be available for Severity 1 issues. Response and resolution targets will be:

| Severity | Response Time | Resolution or Workaround Target |
|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| Severity 2 | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Severity 3 | 1 business day | 10 business days |
| Severity 4 | 3 business days | Next scheduled release |

7.6 **Regulatory Updates.** Brikon will update the Compliance Reporting Module within a commercially reasonable time to reflect changes to Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau and Delaware Alcoholic Beverage Control Commissioner reporting requirements.

7.7 **No Reduction of Functionality.** No update will materially reduce the functionality of any licensed module.

7.8 **Account Management.** Brikon will assign a customer success manager, who will hold quarterly service reviews with Dribas.

## 8. Customer Data and Security

8.1 **Customer Data.** "Customer Data" will mean all data Licensee submits, including recipes, formulations, production records and distributor sales data, together with reports and outputs generated from that data. This applies whether the data is entered manually, uploaded or transmitted from connected equipment.

8.2 **Ownership.** Dribas will own all Customer Data. Brikon will receive a limited license to use the Customer Data only to provide the platform and services to Dribas.

8.3 **Use Restrictions.** Brikon will not use Customer Data for any other purpose. In particular, it will not sell or disclose Customer Data. It will also not use Customer Data to develop, train, benchmark or improve products or services for any other person. Brikon may collect technical usage metrics that contain no Customer Data and do not identify Dribas or its users.

8.4 **Security.** Brikon will maintain the following security measures:

(a) industry-standard administrative, physical and technical safeguards;
(b) encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent);
(c) multi-factor authentication for administrative access;
(d) logical tenant segregation;
(e) monthly vulnerability scans and an annual independent penetration test; and
(f) a current SOC 2 Type II report, to be provided on request once a year.

8.5 **Security Incidents.** Brikon will notify Dribas without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of unauthorized access to, or loss or corruption of, Customer Data.

8.6 **Backups.** Brikon will perform daily backups with thirty (30) day retention. The recovery point objective will be twenty-four (24) hours and the recovery time objective will be eight (8) hours. Restoration procedures will be tested at least twice a year.

8.7 **Personal Data.** The Parties will enter into a data processing addendum covering any personal data that Brikon processes on Dribas's behalf, such as user account details and employee information contained in batch records.

8.8 **Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the agreement ends, Brikon will make Customer Data available for export in machine-readable formats, including CSV and JSON. Brikon will then delete the data, with written certification of deletion on request.

## 9. Term and Termination

9.1 **Initial Term.** Three (3) years from the effective date of the definitive agreement.

9.2 **Renewal.** The agreement will renew automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives at least ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

9.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice. Either Party may also terminate immediately on the other Party's insolvency.

9.4 **Additional Dribas Termination Rights.** Dribas will also have the following termination rights:

(a) chronic availability failure, as described in Section 7.3;
(b) uncorrected warranty breach as to an affected module;
(c) failure of a module to meet acceptance criteria after the second retest, as described in Section 6.4;
(d) the brewhouse integration termination right described in Section 6.6; and
(e) a force majeure event preventing service for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days.

9.5 **Transition Assistance.** For up to one hundred twenty (120) days after expiry or termination, Brikon will provide continued access at pro rata fees and reasonable migration assistance at its standard rates. This will not apply where Brikon terminates for Dribas's uncured non-payment of undisputed fees.

9.6 **Bankruptcy Protection.** The licenses will be treated as licenses of intellectual property for purposes of Section 365(n) of the United States Bankruptcy Code.

## 10. Warranties and Indemnities

10.1 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party will give customary warranties of organization, authority and enforceability.

10.2 **Brikon Warranties.** Brikon will warrant that:

(a) the platform will perform materially in accordance with its documentation and specifications during the Term;
(b) its services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner;
(c) it will not knowingly introduce malicious code;
(d) the platform will contain no disabling devices; and
(e) it will comply with applicable law.

10.3 **Warranty Remedies.** Brikon will correct nonconformities or re-perform services within thirty (30) days after notice. If it does not, Dribas may terminate as to the affected module and receive a refund of prepaid fees.

10.4 **Dribas Warranty.** Dribas will warrant that it has the right to submit Customer Data to the platform.

10.5 **IP Indemnity.** Brikon will defend and indemnify Dribas, its Affiliates and their personnel against third-party claims that the platform, or Dribas's authorized use of it, infringes or misappropriates intellectual property rights. Exclusions will be customary: unauthorized modifications, combinations not supplied or approved by Brikon, Customer Data, and continued use of an infringing version after a free, materially equivalent replacement has been made available.

10.6 **IP Mitigation.** Brikon may procure continued rights or modify the platform to be non-infringing. If neither is commercially reasonable, Brikon may terminate as to the affected software and refund prepaid fees and depreciated implementation fees.

10.7 **Indemnification Procedure.** The definitive agreement will include customary notice, control-of-defense, cooperation and settlement-consent provisions.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Consequential Damages.** Neither Party will be liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages or for lost profits, subject to the exceptions in Section 11.3.

11.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim, subject to the exceptions in Section 11.3.

11.3 **Exceptions.** The exclusion and the cap will not apply to:

(a) Brikon's IP infringement indemnity under Section 10.5;
(b) breach of confidentiality obligations;
(c) Brikon's breach of the Customer Data use restrictions or security obligations;
(d) gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud;
(e) Dribas's obligation to pay fees properly due; or
(f) a Party's infringement or misappropriation of the other Party's intellectual property.

11.4 **Service Credits.** Service credits will count toward the cap. They will not limit Dribas's termination rights.

11.5 **Insurance.** During the Term and for two (2) years after it, Brikon will maintain the following coverage with insurers rated A- or better:

(a) commercial general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate;
(b) technology errors and omissions of $3,000,000;
(c) cyber liability of $3,000,000; and
(d) statutory workers' compensation.

Insurance will not limit Brikon's liability.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 **Mutual Obligations.** Customary mutual confidentiality obligations will apply. Customer Data will be Dribas's confidential information. The platform and non-public documentation will be Brikon's confidential information.

12.2 **Duration.** Obligations will continue for five (5) years after the agreement ends. Obligations covering trade secrets, including Dribas's recipes and formulations, and Customer Data will last for as long as the information remains a trade secret or is held by the recipient.

12.3 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party will be entitled to seek injunctive relief for actual or threatened breach, without posting bond.

12.4 **This Term Sheet.** The existence and terms of this Term Sheet and the Parties' negotiations are confidential under Section 15.2.

## 13. Governing Law and Disputes

13.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** The definitive documentation will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction. The Parties will waive jury trial.

13.2 **Escalation.** Before starting proceedings, other than for injunctive relief, the Parties will escalate disputes first to their project managers and then to senior executives, within defined periods.

13.3 **Continued Performance.** Each Party will continue to perform during any dispute. Brikon will not suspend service on account of a dispute, except for undisputed overdue amounts as described in Section 4.6.

## 14. Definitive Documentation and Timetable

14.1 **Documents.** The transaction will be documented in:

(a) a Software License Agreement with schedules covering modules, sites, users and fees; implementation services, specifications and acceptance criteria; service levels and support; and security requirements;
(b) the side letter described in Section 6.6; and
(c) the data processing addendum described in Section 8.7.

14.2 **Drafting.** Brikon will circulate a first draft of the Software License Agreement based on its standard form, conformed to this Term Sheet, by December 19, 2025. Dribas's counsel will circulate first drafts of the side letter and comments on the data processing addendum.

14.3 **Target Signing.** The Parties are targeting signature of all definitive documents on or about January 15, 2026.

14.4 **Approvals.** Execution by Dribas is subject to approval by its managing member and members in accordance with its operating agreement. Execution by Brikon is subject to approval by its Chief Executive Officer.

14.5 **Due Diligence.** Before signing, Brikon will provide:

(a) its most recent SOC 2 Type II report;
(b) the BrewLedger Functional Specification, version 4.2;
(c) a list of third-party hosting providers and subcontractors expected to have access to Customer Data; and
(d) certificates of insurance.

Dribas will provide an inventory of the brewhouse equipment and controllers to be integrated.

## 15. Binding and Non-Binding Provisions

15.1 **Non-Binding Terms.** Except for this Section 15 and Section 16, this Term Sheet is a non-binding expression of the Parties' current intentions. It does not create any legally binding obligation to license, purchase or provide any software or services. Neither Party will be bound unless and until definitive documentation is executed and delivered by both Parties.

15.2 **Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep confidential the existence and terms of this Term Sheet, the negotiations and all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with them. Disclosure is permitted to its Affiliates, personnel and professional advisors who need to know the information and are bound by duties of confidentiality, and as required by law.

15.3 **Good Faith.** Each Party will negotiate the definitive documentation in good faith on the basis of this Term Sheet until January 31, 2026, unless the Parties agree to extend that date in writing.

15.4 **Termination of Negotiations.** Either Party may end negotiations at any time by written notice. Neither Party will have any liability to the other for doing so, other than for breach of the binding provisions of this Term Sheet.

15.5 **Governing Law of Binding Provisions.** The binding provisions of this Term Sheet are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## 16. Costs and Counterparts

16.1 **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own legal, advisory and other costs in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the definitive documentation.

16.2 **Counterparts.** This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts and delivered by PDF or electronic signature.
