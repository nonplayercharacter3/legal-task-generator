# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Addendum Effective Date**") by and between **Brikon Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Market Street, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**"), and **Dribas Brewing Company, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 415 South Madison Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its BrewLedger brewery production, tank management and compliance software platform to Licensee and provides related hosting, implementation and support services.

B. In providing the Licensed Software and those services, Licensor will Process certain Personal Data on behalf of Licensee. This includes account information for Authorized Users, employee and contractor details recorded in batch, cellar and packaging records, and contact details for Licensee's distributors, suppliers and taproom customers.

C. Section 7.7 of the Agreement provides that the Parties shall comply with this Addendum to the extent Licensor Processes Personal Data on behalf of Licensee. The Parties are entering into this Addendum to set out their respective obligations with respect to that Processing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Addendum and in the Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

The following terms have the meanings set out below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Section 1 have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.1 "**Customer Data**" means Personal Data that Licensor Processes on behalf of Licensee in connection with the Agreement, in whatever form or medium and whether provided by Licensee, by its Authorized Users, or by brewhouse equipment, systems or third parties connected to the Licensed Software by or for Licensee.

1.2 "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the Processing of Customer Data under the Agreement. These include, to the extent applicable, the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act, the Delaware data breach notification statute at Title 6, Chapter 12B of the Delaware Code, and any comparable state consumer privacy, data security or breach notification law, in each case as amended or replaced from time to time.

1.3 "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Customer Data relates.

1.4 "**Data Subject Request**" means a request from a Data Subject, or from a person acting on a Data Subject's behalf, to exercise a right granted under Applicable Data Protection Law. These rights include the right of access, correction, deletion, portability or opt-out.

1.5 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, or that is linked or reasonably linkable to such a person, and any information defined as "personal data," "personal information" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.6 "**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration or unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Data that Licensor or any Subprocessor transmits, stores or otherwise Processes.

1.7 "**Process**," "**Processes**," "**Processed**" and "**Processing**" mean any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. Examples are collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

1.8 "**Controller**" means the person that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the Processing of Personal Data. Where Applicable Data Protection Law uses the term "business" or a similar term, Controller includes that term.

1.9 "**Processor**" means the person that Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller. Where Applicable Data Protection Law uses the term "service provider" or a similar term, Processor includes that term.

1.10 "**Subprocessor**" means any person, other than an employee of Licensor, that Licensor engages to Process Customer Data on Licensor's behalf in connection with the Agreement. This includes any hosting provider engaged for the Hosted Environment.

1.11 "**Documented Instructions**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES OF THE PARTIES

2.1 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Licensee is the Controller of Customer Data, and Licensor is a Processor that Processes Customer Data on behalf of Licensee.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Licensor shall Process Customer Data only to the extent necessary to provide the Licensed Software, the Implementation Services and the Support Services to Licensee under the Agreement. Annex 1 describes the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the Processing, the types of Customer Data and the categories of Data Subjects.

2.3 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Addendum forms part of the agreement between the Parties described in Section 15.6 of the Agreement. Except as set out in this Addendum, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

2.4 **Compliance with Law.** Each Party shall comply with the obligations that Applicable Data Protection Law imposes on it in connection with the Processing of Customer Data under the Agreement.

## 3. LICENSEE OBLIGATIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** The Agreement, this Addendum, and Licensee's configuration and use of the Licensed Software constitute Licensee's complete documented instructions to Licensor for the Processing of Customer Data (the "**Documented Instructions**"). Licensee may give Licensor additional written instructions consistent with the Agreement. Licensor may charge for any additional instructions that require work outside the scope of the Agreement at the rates in Section A.6 of Schedule A to the Agreement, if Licensor first gives Licensee a written estimate and Licensee approves it in writing.

3.2 **Lawfulness of Instructions.** Licensee shall ensure that its Documented Instructions comply with Applicable Data Protection Law. Licensor shall promptly inform Licensee if, in Licensor's reasonable opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law. In that case Licensor may suspend Processing under that instruction until Licensee confirms or modifies it in writing.

3.3 **Notices and Consents.** Licensee is responsible for providing any notices and obtaining any consents that Applicable Data Protection Law requires for Licensor to Process Customer Data as contemplated by the Agreement. This includes notices to Licensee's employees and contractors whose names, credentials or shift records are entered into the Licensed Software.

3.4 **Data Minimization.** Licensee shall not submit to the Licensed Software any Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, payment card data, health information, biometric data or government identification numbers. The only exception is where the Specifications expressly require that data for a function Licensee uses. The Licensed Software is not designed to Process payment card data, and Licensee shall not configure it to do so.

## 4. LICENSOR OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Processing on Instructions.** Licensor shall Process Customer Data only on Licensee's Documented Instructions, unless Applicable Data Protection Law requires otherwise. In that case Licensor shall inform Licensee of the legal requirement before Processing, unless the law prohibits Licensor from doing so.

4.2 **Prohibited Activities.** Licensor shall not:

(a) sell or share Customer Data, as those terms are defined under Applicable Data Protection Law;

(b) retain, use or disclose Customer Data for any purpose other than performing the services specified in the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose other than those services;

(c) retain, use or disclose Customer Data outside the direct business relationship between Licensor and Licensee; or

(d) combine Customer Data with Personal Data that Licensor receives from or on behalf of any other person, or that Licensor collects from its own interactions with a Data Subject, except as Applicable Data Protection Law permits for a Processor.

4.3 **Certification.** Licensor certifies that it understands the restrictions in Section 4.2 and will comply with them.

4.4 **Location of Processing.** Licensor shall Process and store Customer Data only in the United States, in accordance with Section 5.1 of the Agreement. Licensor shall not transfer Customer Data outside the United States, or permit any Subprocessor to access it from outside the United States, without Licensee's prior written consent.

4.5 **Assistance.** Taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to Licensor, Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance to Licensee in meeting Licensee's obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law. These obligations include responding to Data Subject Requests, conducting data protection assessments, and notifying authorities and Data Subjects of a Personal Data Breach.

4.6 **Notice of Inability to Comply.** Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing within five (5) business days after it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law or this Addendum. Licensee may then take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized Processing.

4.7 **De-identified Data.** If Licensor receives de-identified data from Licensee in connection with the Agreement, Licensor shall take reasonable measures to ensure the data cannot be associated with a natural person. Licensor shall publicly commit to maintain and use the data only in de-identified form and shall not attempt to re-identify it.

## 5. LICENSOR PERSONNEL

5.1 **Confidentiality.** Licensor shall ensure that each person it authorizes to Process Customer Data is subject to a written duty of confidentiality or an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality with respect to that Customer Data.

5.2 **Access Limitation.** Licensor shall limit access to Customer Data to those personnel who need it to perform Licensor's obligations under the Agreement, in accordance with Section D.1 of Schedule D to the Agreement.

5.3 **Training.** Licensor shall provide its personnel with access to Customer Data with training on their data protection and security obligations at least annually, in accordance with Section D.5 of Schedule D to the Agreement.

## 6. SECURITY

6.1 **Security Measures.** Licensor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Data against Personal Data Breaches. These measures shall take into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of the Processing. As a minimum, they shall include the measures in Section 7.4 of, and Schedule D to, the Agreement.

6.2 **Changes to Security Measures.** Licensor may update its security measures from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall level of protection afforded to Customer Data.

6.3 **Independent Assessments.** Licensor shall maintain the SOC 2 Type II report described in Section 7.4 of the Agreement. Licensor shall promptly inform Licensee of any qualified opinion or material exception in that report that relates to controls protecting Customer Data, and shall describe the remediation Licensor is taking.

6.4 **Licensee Security Responsibilities.** Licensee is responsible for the following:

(a) managing the credentials of its Authorized Users;

(b) configuring user roles and permissions within the Licensed Software appropriately;

(c) securing the devices and networks at the Sites through which Authorized Users access the Licensed Software; and

(d) securing Licensee's brewhouse equipment, sensors and controllers, except for any connector, gateway or agent software that Licensor or its subcontractors install on that equipment.

## 7. SUBPROCESSORS

7.1 **Authorized Subprocessors.** Licensee authorizes Licensor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 2 as of the Addendum Effective Date.

7.2 **New Subprocessors.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging a new Subprocessor or replacing an existing one. The notice shall state the Subprocessor's name and location and describe the Processing it will perform.

7.3 **Objection.** Licensee may object to a new or replacement Subprocessor on reasonable grounds relating to data protection by written notice to Licensor within fifteen (15) days after receiving Licensor's notice. If Licensee objects, the Parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it within thirty (30) days after Licensee's objection, Licensor shall do one of the following:

(a) refrain from using the Subprocessor to Process Customer Data; or

(b) allow Licensee to terminate the affected Module by written notice, without penalty, and refund any prepaid Fees for that Module attributable to the period after termination.

7.4 **Subprocessor Terms.** Licensor shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective of Customer Data than those in this Addendum. These obligations include the restrictions in Section 4.2 and the security requirements in Section D.8 of Schedule D to the Agreement.

7.5 **Responsibility.** Licensor remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of its obligations and for its compliance with this Addendum, as if the Subprocessor's acts and omissions were Licensor's own, in accordance with Section 15.4 of the Agreement.

## 8. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

8.1 **Notification.** If Licensor receives a Data Subject Request relating to Customer Data, Licensor shall forward it to Licensee within five (5) business days after receipt. Licensor shall not respond to the Data Subject Request itself, except to acknowledge receipt and refer the requester to Licensee, unless Licensee authorizes Licensor in writing to respond or Applicable Data Protection Law requires a response.

8.2 **Tools and Assistance.** The Licensed Software includes administrator functions that allow Licensee to search for, export, correct and delete Customer Data relating to an individual Data Subject. Where Licensee cannot fulfill a Data Subject Request using those functions, Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance on Licensee's written request, at no additional charge for up to ten (10) hours in each calendar year. Beyond that, assistance shall be charged at the rates in Section A.6 of Schedule A to the Agreement.

8.3 **Batch and Compliance Records.** Before deleting or altering any Customer Data contained in batch, production or compliance records in response to a Data Subject Request, Licensee is responsible for confirming that the deletion or alteration is consistent with Licensee's recordkeeping obligations under the laws governing the production and sale of alcoholic beverages.

## 9. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

9.1 **Notification.** Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. Notice shall be given to the contacts set out in Section 15.5 of the Agreement and, by telephone, to Licensee's project manager.

9.2 **Content of Notice.** Licensor's notice shall describe the following, to the extent the information is then known to Licensor:

(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;

(b) the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach;

(c) the measures Licensor has taken or proposes to take to address the Personal Data Breach and mitigate its possible adverse effects; and

(d) the name and contact details of a Licensor contact from whom Licensee can obtain further information.

Where Licensor does not have all of this information at the time of its initial notice, it shall provide the information in phases as it becomes available, without undue further delay.

9.3 **Investigation and Remediation.** Licensor shall promptly investigate each Personal Data Breach and take reasonable steps to contain and remediate it. Licensor shall cooperate with Licensee and provide information that Licensee reasonably requests to assess the Personal Data Breach and to meet any notification obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law.

9.4 **Notifications to Third Parties.** Licensee is responsible for determining whether to notify affected Data Subjects, regulators, law enforcement or other third parties of a Personal Data Breach. Licensor shall not notify any such person of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data without Licensee's prior written approval, unless Applicable Data Protection Law requires Licensor to do so.

9.5 **Costs.** Where a Personal Data Breach results from Licensor's or a Subprocessor's breach of the Agreement or this Addendum, Licensor shall reimburse Licensee's reasonable, documented costs of notifying affected Data Subjects and regulators, and of providing credit monitoring or identity protection services where Applicable Data Protection Law requires them or where it is customary for the type of Personal Data concerned.

9.6 **No Admission.** Licensor's notification of or response to a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability with respect to the Personal Data Breach.

9.7 **Relationship to Security Incidents.** A Personal Data Breach is also a Security Incident for purposes of Section 7.5 of the Agreement. Notice given by Licensor in accordance with Section 9.1 of this Addendum also satisfies the notice requirement in Section 7.5 of the Agreement for that event.

## 10. AUDITS AND INFORMATION

10.1 **Information.** On Licensee's written request, Licensor shall make available to Licensee the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate Licensor's compliance with this Addendum and Applicable Data Protection Law. This includes Licensor's most recent SOC 2 Type II report, summaries of its most recent penetration test, and its written information security policies relevant to the Hosted Environment.

10.2 **Audits.** If the information provided under Section 10.1 does not reasonably demonstrate Licensor's compliance, or if a Personal Data Breach has occurred, Licensee may conduct an audit of Licensor's Processing of Customer Data. Licensee may conduct the audit itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Section 8 of the Agreement. Audits are subject to the following:

(a) Licensee shall give at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, except following a Personal Data Breach, when ten (10) business days' notice is sufficient;

(b) audits shall be conducted during Licensor's normal business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Licensor's operations;

(c) audits shall be limited to the systems, records and personnel involved in the Processing of Customer Data;

(d) except following a Personal Data Breach, Licensee may not conduct more than one audit in any twelve-month period; and

(e) Licensee shall bear its own costs of the audit, unless it reveals a material breach of this Addendum by Licensor, in which case Licensor shall reimburse Licensee's reasonable, documented audit costs.

10.3 **Hosting Providers.** Where an audit would require access to the facilities of a hosting provider Subprocessor, Licensor may satisfy the audit by providing that Subprocessor's most recent independent audit reports. Those reports must cover the controls relevant to the Hosted Environment.

10.4 **Remediation.** Licensor shall promptly remediate any material non-compliance with this Addendum identified in an audit. Licensor shall give Licensee a written remediation plan within thirty (30) days after receiving the audit findings.

## 11. RETURN AND DELETION

11.1 **Return and Deletion.** On expiration or termination of the Agreement, Licensor shall return and delete Customer Data in accordance with Section 7.8 of the Agreement.

11.2 **Retention Required by Law.** Where Applicable Data Protection Law requires Licensor to retain any Customer Data after the period described in Section 7.8 of the Agreement, the following applies:

(a) Licensor shall notify Licensee of the requirement;

(b) Licensor shall retain only the Customer Data that the law requires it to retain, and only for as long as the law requires;

(c) Licensor shall continue to protect that Customer Data in accordance with this Addendum; and

(d) Licensor shall Process that Customer Data only for the purposes for which the law requires its retention.

11.3 **Subprocessors.** Licensor shall ensure that each Subprocessor deletes Customer Data within the period that applies to Licensor under Section 7.8 of the Agreement. Licensor shall include confirmation of Subprocessor deletion in any certification of deletion it provides under that Section.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Addendum and any claim, controversy or dispute arising out of or relating to this Addendum or to the Processing of Customer Data shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Addendum or to the Processing of Customer Data. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts, including on the ground of inconvenient forum.

12.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY AND VOLUNTARILY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ADDENDUM.

12.4 **Escalation.** Section 14.1 of the Agreement applies to disputes under this Addendum. This does not prevent either Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent an actual or threatened unauthorized use or disclosure of Customer Data.

## 13. TERM

13.1 **Term.** This Addendum takes effect on the Addendum Effective Date and continues for as long as Licensor or any Subprocessor Processes Customer Data on behalf of Licensee in connection with the Agreement.

13.2 **Survival.** Sections 9, 10, 11 and 12 and any other provision of this Addendum that by its nature is intended to survive, survive the expiration or termination of the Agreement until Licensor and all Subprocessors have completed the deletion of Customer Data in accordance with Section 11.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Limitation of Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum is subject to Section 11 of the Agreement.

14.2 **Changes in Law.** If Applicable Data Protection Law changes in a way that requires changes to this Addendum, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Addendum to the extent reasonably necessary to comply with that change. Section 15.8 of the Agreement applies to any such amendment.

14.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Addendum shall be given in accordance with Section 15.5 of the Agreement.

14.4 **Assignment.** This Addendum may be assigned only together with the Agreement and in accordance with Section 15.3 of the Agreement.

14.5 **Severability.** Section 15.9 of the Agreement applies to this Addendum.

14.6 **Third-Party Beneficiaries.** No Data Subject or other third party is a beneficiary of this Addendum.

14.7 **Interpretation.** Section 15.16 of the Agreement applies to this Addendum as if references in it to "this Agreement" were references to this Addendum.

14.8 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by PDF, DocuSign or other electronic means are effective as originals.

## ANNEX 1 – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**1. Subject Matter.** Licensor's provision of the BrewLedger platform, hosting, implementation, data migration, equipment integration and support services to Licensee under the Agreement.

**2. Duration.** The Term of the Agreement, any transition period under Section 12.5 of the Agreement, and the period until deletion of Customer Data under Section 11 of this Addendum.

**3. Nature and Purpose of Processing.** The Processing consists of hosting, storage, backup and recovery, transmission, display, reporting and technical support of Customer Data. It also includes migration of historical records during the Implementation Services, all as necessary to provide the Licensed Software and services under the Agreement.

**4. Categories of Data Subjects.**

(a) Authorized Users, including Licensee's and its Affiliates' employees and individual contractors.

(b) Brewers, cellar staff, packaging staff and other personnel identified in batch, tank, cleaning-in-place, packaging and quality records.

(c) Individual contacts at Licensee's distributors, wholesalers, retailers, suppliers and equipment vendors.

(d) Individual taproom customers, keg deposit holders and event contacts, to the extent their details are entered into the Inventory and Packaging Module for keg tracking or into distributor and sales records.

**5. Types of Customer Data.**

(a) Names, job titles, work email addresses, work telephone numbers and employee identifiers.

(b) User credentials, role assignments, login records, IP addresses and audit-trail entries.

(c) Shift, sign-off and operator records associated with brewing, cellaring and packaging activities.

(d) Business contact details for distributor, supplier and vendor personnel.

(e) Names, contact details and deposit records of keg deposit holders and similar individual customers.

**6. Sensitive Data.** None. Licensee shall not submit sensitive data to the Licensed Software, as described in Section 3.4 of this Addendum.

**7. Processing Locations.** The Hosted Environment and backup storage, each located in the United States, and Licensor's offices at 1200 Market Street, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801.
