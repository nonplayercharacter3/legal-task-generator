## SCHEDULE D: SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

**D.1 Access Control.** Licensor shall apply need-to-know access to Customer Data and require multi-factor authentication for all administrative access.

**D.2 Encryption.** Licensor shall encrypt Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard.

**D.3 Vulnerability Management.** Licensor shall conduct monthly vulnerability scans and an annual independent penetration test.

**D.4 Tenant Segregation.** Licensor shall logically segregate Customer Data from the data of its other customers.

**D.5 Business Continuity.** Licensor shall maintain a tested disaster recovery plan, with geographically separate backup storage within the United States.
