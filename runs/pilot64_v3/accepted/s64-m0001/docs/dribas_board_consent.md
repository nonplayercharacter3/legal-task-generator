# JOINT WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS AND THE MEMBERS OF DRIBAS BREWING COMPANY, LLC

**In Lieu of a Special Meeting**

**Dated as of January 12, 2026**

The undersigned are all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") and all of the Members (the "**Members**") of **Dribas Brewing Company, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"). Acting under the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (the "**Act**") and the Company's Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement, as amended to date (the "**LLC Agreement**"), they consent to, approve and adopt the following recitals and resolutions. This written consent (this "**Consent**") has the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Board and of the Members at a meeting duly called and held. The undersigned waive all requirements of notice, meeting and quorum that would otherwise apply to the actions taken in this Consent.

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 **The Company.** The Company operates craft breweries in Wilmington and Dover, Delaware:

(a) the brewery and taproom at 415 South Madison Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Wilmington Brewery**"); and

(b) the brewery at 88 Bay Road Industrial Park, Dover, Delaware 19901 (the "**Dover Brewery**" and, together with the Wilmington Brewery, the "**Breweries**").

1.2 **Current Systems.** The Company currently manages production scheduling, tank allocation, batch records, inventory and federal and state compliance reporting through a combination of spreadsheets and a legacy production database. In the Board's judgment, these systems no longer support the Company's production volumes or its two-site operations adequately.

1.3 **Vendor Selection.** In the fourth quarter of 2025, the Company's management evaluated brewery operations software platforms offered by several vendors. Management recommended to the Board that the Company license the BrewLedger brewery production, tank management and compliance software platform (the "**BrewLedger Platform**") developed by **Brikon Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Brikon**"). The recommendation rested principally on the following:

(a) the BrewLedger Platform's ability to integrate with the Company's brewhouse control systems, fermentation tank sensors and packaging line controllers;

(b) its compliance reporting functions for the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau and the Delaware Alcoholic Beverage Control Commissioner; and

(c) the pricing Brikon offered.

1.4 **Term Sheet.** On December 8, 2025, the Company and Brikon signed a term sheet summarizing the principal terms under discussion (the "**Term Sheet**"). Except for certain confidentiality, good-faith negotiation, cost and governing-law provisions, the Term Sheet is non-binding. Its Section 14.4 provides that the Company's execution of the definitive documentation is subject to approval by its managing member and members in accordance with the LLC Agreement.

1.5 **Definitive Documents.** The Company's counsel and management have since negotiated with Brikon the definitive documents listed in Exhibit A (together, the "**Transaction Documents**"). The Board and the Members have received and reviewed final or substantially final forms of each Transaction Document. They have also received:

(a) a written summary of the principal commercial terms prepared by management;

(b) a summary of the implementation timetable and the integration commitments for the Brewhouse Integration Module;

(c) a budget for the first contract year; and

(d) the opportunity to ask questions of management and the Company's counsel.

1.6 **Reason for Approval.** The Transaction Documents together involve a multi-year financial commitment, the licensing of software that will become the Company's system of record for production and compliance functions, and the hosting of the Company's production, recipe and compliance records by a third party. The Board and the Members have therefore determined that the Company's entry into the Transaction Documents should be approved by the Board and the Members, as contemplated by the LLC Agreement and the Term Sheet.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board and the Members adopt the following resolutions.

## 2. APPROVAL OF THE SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

2.1 **Approval.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Software License Agreement between Brikon, as licensor, and the Company, as licensee, to be dated as of January 15, 2026, together with its Schedules A through D (the "**License Agreement**"), substantially in the form presented to the Board and the Members, are approved in all respects.

2.2 **Finding.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company's entry into and performance of the License Agreement are advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its Members.

2.3 **Commercial Terms Noted.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board and the Members note, among other terms of the License Agreement as presented, the following:

(a) **Modules.** The license covers the Production Scheduling Module, the Tank Management Module, the Compliance Reporting Module, the Inventory and Packaging Module and the Brewhouse Integration Module.

(b) **Sites and Users.** The license covers the Wilmington Brewery and the Dover Brewery as the licensed Sites, with up to forty (40) named Authorized Users.

(c) **Subscription Fees.** Annual subscription Fees are $96,000, with hosting, backup, disaster recovery and standard support included.

(d) **Implementation Fees.** Implementation Services Fees are a fixed $64,000, payable in four milestone installments of $16,000 each. The first is due on signature. The remaining three are due on Acceptance of the Modules specified in Section A.5 of Schedule A to the License Agreement.

(e) **Term.** The initial term is three (3) years from the Effective Date. Thereafter the License Agreement renews automatically for one-year renewal terms unless either party gives at least ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

(f) **Price Increases.** Renewal price increases are capped as provided in Section 3.7 of the License Agreement.

2.4 **Execution Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer (as defined in Section 9.1) is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the License Agreement. The License Agreement may be executed with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company. Execution and delivery by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. APPROVAL OF THE SIDE LETTER

3.1 **Approval.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the letter agreement from Brikon to the Company relating to the Brewhouse Integration Module and related implementation matters, to be dated January 15, 2026 and countersigned by the Company (the "**Side Letter**"), substantially in the form presented to the Board and the Members, are approved in all respects.

3.2 **Purpose.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board and the Members note that the Side Letter records commitments that management identified as important to the Company's decision to proceed. These include:

(a) the Acceptance Deadline for the Brewhouse Integration Module of June 30, 2026;

(b) the Company's right to terminate the License Agreement without a termination fee if the Brewhouse Integration Module is not Accepted by that date, exercisable by written notice within the period stated in the Side Letter;

(c) related refund and transition commitments;

(d) the designation of Brikon's Integration Project Lead; and

(e) weekly reporting and steering-meeting obligations.

3.3 **Execution Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to countersign and deliver the Side Letter, with such changes as the Authorized Officer approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company.

## 4. APPROVAL OF THE DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

4.1 **Approval.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Data Processing Addendum between Brikon and the Company, to be dated as of January 15, 2026, together with its Annexes 1 and 2 (the "**DPA**"), substantially in the form presented to the Board and the Members, are approved in all respects.

4.2 **Execution Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the DPA, with such changes as the Authorized Officer approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company.

4.3 **Employee Notices.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Director of Operations, in consultation with the Company's counsel, is directed to prepare and deliver any notices to the Company's employees and individual contractors that are appropriate in connection with the entry of their names, credentials, shift records and sign-off records into the BrewLedger Platform. Those notices should be delivered before the Company's Authorized Users begin live use of the BrewLedger Platform.

## 5. BUDGET AND FUNDING

5.1 **Budget Approval.** RESOLVED, that the following budget for the BrewLedger implementation for the Company's 2026 fiscal year (the "**Implementation Budget**") is approved:

| Item | Budgeted Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual subscription Fees (first contract year) | $96,000 |
| Implementation Services Fees (all four milestones) | $64,000 |
| Pre-approved travel expenses of Brikon personnel for on-site implementation | $8,000 |
| Site network connectivity upgrades (Wilmington Brewery and Dover Brewery) | $15,000 |
| Internal staff overtime and backfill during acceptance testing and cutover | $12,000 |
| Contingency | $10,000 |
| **Total** | **$205,000** |

5.2 **Funding.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Implementation Budget shall be funded from the Company's operating cash and, to the extent necessary, from availability under the Company's existing revolving credit facility, as the Chief Executive Officer and the Controller determine.

5.3 **Spending Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Executive Officer is authorized to approve expenditures within the Implementation Budget. Any of the following requires the prior approval of the Board:

(a) any single expenditure, or series of related expenditures, that would cause total spending to exceed the Implementation Budget by more than ten percent (10%); and

(b) any Order Form under the License Agreement that adds Modules, Sites or user seats and has an annual cost of more than $25,000.

5.4 **Lender Notice.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Controller is directed to confirm, before the License Agreement is executed, whether the Company's revolving credit facility requires notice to or consent from the lender for the Company's entry into the Transaction Documents. If notice or consent is required, the Controller shall give or obtain it.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION GOVERNANCE

6.1 **Project Manager.** RESOLVED, that Rachel Steen, Director of Operations, is designated as the Company's project manager for purposes of Section 4.1 of the License Agreement. She is authorized to:

(a) agree the Implementation Plan with Brikon's project manager;

(b) coordinate the Company's personnel, equipment vendors and network providers in support of the Implementation Services; and

(c) receive the weekly status reports and attend the steering meetings contemplated by the Side Letter.

6.2 **Site Leads.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the head brewer of the Wilmington Brewery and the brewery manager of the Dover Brewery are each designated as the Company's operational representative for that Brewery. Each shall:

(a) participate in the steering meetings contemplated by the Side Letter;

(b) participate in the acceptance testing of each Module at that Brewery; and

(c) confirm to the project manager whether each Module meets the Acceptance Criteria at that Brewery before the Company gives any notice of Acceptance or failure under Section 4.3 or Section 4.4 of the License Agreement.

6.3 **Acceptance Decisions.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that no notice confirming that a Module meets the Acceptance Criteria shall be given on behalf of the Company unless it has been approved in writing by the project manager and by the Chief Executive Officer. The project manager shall track each Testing Period so that any notice of failure to meet the Acceptance Criteria can be given within that Testing Period.

6.4 **Reporting to the Board.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that until the Brewhouse Integration Module has been Accepted at both Breweries, the Chief Executive Officer shall report to the Board on the status of the implementation at each regular meeting of the Board. The Chief Executive Officer shall also report promptly between meetings on any of the following:

(a) any delay notice received from Brikon under the Side Letter;

(b) any Severity 1 Error affecting either Brewery; and

(c) any matter that, in the Chief Executive Officer's judgment, is reasonably likely to affect the Company's rights under the Side Letter.

6.5 **Calendar of Key Dates.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the project manager, with the assistance of the Company's counsel, shall maintain a calendar of the dates and notice periods under the Transaction Documents. The calendar shall include:

(a) the Acceptance Deadline and the notice period for the termination right under the Side Letter;

(b) the Testing Period for each Module;

(c) the deadline for requesting service credits under Schedule C to the License Agreement;

(d) the non-renewal notice date under Section 12.1 of the License Agreement; and

(e) any objection period for new Subprocessors under the DPA.

The project manager shall circulate the calendar to the Chief Executive Officer and the Controller, and shall update it whenever a relevant date changes.

## 7. LEGACY SYSTEMS AND OPERATIONAL CONTINUITY

7.1 **Parallel Operation.** RESOLVED, that management is directed to keep the Company's legacy production database and production spreadsheets operational at each Brewery, and to maintain parallel records in them, until the Brewhouse Integration Module has been Accepted at that Brewery. This direction applies unless the Chief Executive Officer determines, in consultation with the project manager and the relevant site lead, that earlier retirement at a Brewery is appropriate.

7.2 **Regulatory Recordkeeping.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Compliance and Quality Manager is directed to confirm, before the cutover at each Brewery, that the Company's records of production, removals, inventory and excise tax will continue to meet the Company's federal and state recordkeeping requirements during and after the transition to the BrewLedger Platform.

7.3 **Historical Records.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that before decommissioning any legacy system, management shall confirm that the historical records migrated to the BrewLedger Platform under Section 4.6 of the License Agreement have been reviewed and reconciled. Management shall also retain an archival copy of the legacy data in a format accessible to the Company independently of the BrewLedger Platform.

7.4 **Cutover Scheduling.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Director of Operations shall identify to Brikon, at least thirty (30) days in advance, the Company's seasonal releases during 2026. This will allow cutover at each Brewery to be scheduled in accordance with the Side Letter.

## 8. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND DATA OVERSIGHT

8.1 **Records Owner.** RESOLVED, that the Compliance and Quality Manager is designated as the Company's internal owner of the Company's data held in the BrewLedger Platform. In that role, the Compliance and Quality Manager shall:

(a) administer Authorized User accounts, roles and permissions;

(b) review the Authorized User list at least quarterly and promptly disable the access of any departing employee or contractor; and

(c) serve as the Company's point of contact for Data Subject Requests and Personal Data Breach notices received from Brikon.

8.2 **Recipes and Formulations.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company's recipes, formulations and production methods remain among its most valuable trade secrets. Management is directed to limit access to recipe and formulation records within the BrewLedger Platform to those Authorized Users whose responsibilities require it, using the role and permission functions of the BrewLedger Platform.

8.3 **Security Reports.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that management shall request Brikon's most recent SOC 2 Type II report under Section 7.4 of the License Agreement at least once in each contract year and shall report any material exception noted in that report to the Board.

8.4 **Incident Response.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that if the Company receives notice from Brikon of a Security Incident or a Personal Data Breach, the Chief Executive Officer shall promptly inform the Board and engage the Company's counsel. The Chief Executive Officer is authorized to take the actions that the Company's counsel advises are necessary or appropriate in response, including any notifications that the Company determines to make.

8.5 **Insurance Review.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Controller is directed to review, with the Company's insurance broker, whether the Company's existing insurance program, including any cyber liability coverage, should be adjusted in light of the Company's reliance on a hosted platform for its production and compliance records. The Controller shall report the broker's recommendations to the Board at its next regular meeting.

## 9. AUTHORIZED OFFICERS AND GENERAL AUTHORITY

9.1 **Authorized Officers.** RESOLVED, that each of the following is designated an "**Authorized Officer**" for purposes of this Consent:

(a) Elena V. Dribas, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Member; and

(b) Jonas R. Dribas, Chief Financial Officer.

9.2 **General Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all actions that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes and intent of the foregoing resolutions. These actions include:

(a) executing and delivering all certificates, instruments, notices and other documents contemplated by or required in connection with the Transaction Documents;

(b) paying all Fees, expenses and other amounts within the Implementation Budget, subject to Section 5.3; and

(c) giving any notice, approval or consent that the Company is required or permitted to give under the Transaction Documents in the ordinary course of their performance.

9.3 **Reserved Matters.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each of the following actions under the Transaction Documents requires the prior approval of the Board:

(a) a notice of termination of the License Agreement given by the Company, other than a termination limited to a single Module under Section 4.4 or Section 9.3 of the License Agreement;

(b) a notice of non-renewal under Section 12.1 of the License Agreement;

(c) any amendment of the License Agreement, the Side Letter or the DPA that increases the Company's financial commitment, extends the Term or limits the Company's rights; and

(d) the commencement of any proceeding against Brikon.

However, where the Chief Executive Officer determines that a notice must be given before the Board can reasonably act, the Chief Executive Officer may give the notice after consulting the Company's counsel and at least one other member of the Board. The Chief Executive Officer shall then report the action to the Board promptly.

9.4 **Third-Party Reliance.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that Brikon and any other person may rely on a certificate signed by an Authorized Officer as to:

(a) the adoption of these resolutions;

(b) the incumbency of the Authorized Officers; and

(c) the authority of the Authorized Officers to act on behalf of the Company in connection with the Transaction Documents.
