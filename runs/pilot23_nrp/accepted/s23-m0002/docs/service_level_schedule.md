# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Service Level Schedule** to the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the **"Agreement"**) between **Prupox Genomics, Inc.** (**"Provider"**) and **Staidaix Seed Genetics, LLC** (**"Customer"**).

This Service Level Schedule is the "Service Level Schedule" referred to in the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Schedule:

**"Batch"** means a group of Samples received by Provider under a single sample manifest and assigned a single Provider lot identifier, for example "SG-26-117".

**"Call Rate"** means, for a Batch, the number of valid genotype calls returned divided by the total number of genotype calls attempted across all passing Samples and markers in the Batch, expressed as a percentage.

**"Concordance Rate"** means the percentage of genotype calls on Provider's internal control samples and Customer-designated check lines in a Batch that match the established reference genotype for those samples.

**"Customer Portal"** means Provider's secure, password-protected web portal through which Provider makes Deliverables available to Customer.

**"Data Release Date"** means, for a Batch, the date on which Provider posts the final genotype data for that Batch to the Customer Portal. Posting of preliminary, partial or quality-control-only files does not constitute a Data Release Date.

**"Receipt Date"** means the Business Day on which a Batch is physically received and logged at Provider's laboratory with a complete sample manifest. A Batch received after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time is treated as received on the next Business Day.

**"Sample Failure Rate"** means, for a Batch, the number of Samples that fail Provider's DNA quality thresholds or return a per-Sample call rate below ninety percent (90%), divided by the total number of Samples in the Batch, expressed as a percentage, excluding Samples that Provider shows failed due to Customer-caused defects under Section 5.

**"Service Level"** means each performance standard set out in Section 2.

**"Turnaround Time"** means the number of Business Days from the Receipt Date to the Data Release Date for a Batch.

## 2. SERVICE LEVELS

**2.1 Turnaround Time.** Provider shall achieve the following Turnaround Times for each Batch:

| Service Category | Standard Turnaround Time | Expedited Turnaround Time (where ordered) |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted SNP marker panel genotyping (up to 384 markers) | 10 Business Days | 5 Business Days |
| Mid-density SNP array genotyping | 15 Business Days | 8 Business Days |
| Genotyping-by-sequencing and low-pass sequencing | 20 Business Days | Not available |
| Trait-screening assays (herbicide tolerance, disease resistance, quality traits) | 12 Business Days | 6 Business Days |

Expedited Turnaround Time applies only to Batches designated as expedited on the sample manifest and accepted by Provider in writing, subject to any expedited volume limits and surcharges in the applicable Order Form.

**2.2 Data Quality.** For each Batch, Provider shall achieve:

| Metric | Service Level |
|---|---|
| Call Rate | at least 97.0% |
| Concordance Rate | at least 99.5% |
| Sample Failure Rate | no more than 3.0% |

**2.3 Portal Availability.** Provider shall make the Customer Portal available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified to Customer at least three (3) Business Days in advance and performed outside 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central Time on Business Days.

**2.4 Data Release Notification.** On each Data Release Date, Provider shall send an automated notice to Customer's designated program contact identifying the Batch, the files posted and the Data Release Date. The Data Release Date is the date of posting to the Customer Portal, regardless of when the notice is sent or opened.

## 3. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

**3.1 Measurement.** Provider shall measure performance against each Service Level using its laboratory information management system and Customer Portal logs. Customer may verify Provider's measurements against Provider's records in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Agreement.

**3.2 Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, Provider shall deliver a written report showing, for each Batch with a Data Release Date in that month, the Receipt Date, Data Release Date, Turnaround Time, Call Rate, Concordance Rate and Sample Failure Rate. The report will also show monthly Customer Portal availability and any service credits accrued.

**3.3 Quarterly Review.** The Parties shall meet each calendar quarter to review performance under this Schedule, forecasts under Section 2.3 of the Agreement, and any open quality matters under the Quality Agreement.

## 4. SERVICE CREDITS

**4.1 Turnaround Time Credits.** If Provider fails to meet the applicable Turnaround Time for a Batch, Customer will be entitled to a service credit equal to the following percentage of the Fees for that Batch:

| Delay beyond applicable Turnaround Time | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 Business Days | 5% |
| 4 to 7 Business Days | 10% |
| 8 or more Business Days | 20% |

**4.2 Data Quality Credits.** If a Batch fails to meet any data quality Service Level in Section 2.2, Customer will be entitled to a service credit of ten percent (10%) of the Fees for that Batch. This credit is in addition to any re-run or other remedy available under the Quality Agreement for the same Batch, but no more than one data quality credit applies per Batch.

**4.3 Portal Availability Credits.** If Customer Portal availability falls below the Service Level in Section 2.3 in any calendar month, Customer will be entitled to a service credit of two percent (2%) of the Fees invoiced for that month for each full one percent (1%) shortfall, up to ten percent (10%).

**4.4 Cap.** Total service credits in any calendar month will not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of the Fees invoiced under the applicable Order Form for that month.

**4.5 Application.** Service credits are applied in accordance with Section 4.7 of the Agreement. Any credits outstanding when an Order Form expires or terminates will be paid to Customer within thirty (30) days.

**4.6 Chronic Failure.** If Provider fails to meet the same Service Level in any three (3) calendar months within a rolling six (6) month period, that failure will be treated as a material breach of the applicable Order Form for the purposes of Section 12.2 of the Agreement.

## 5. EXCLUSIONS

Provider will not be responsible for a failure to meet a Service Level to the extent the failure is caused by:

(a) Samples that do not comply with Provider's sample submission guidelines or the Quality Agreement, including insufficient tissue or DNA, contamination or degradation before receipt, or mislabeling by Customer;

(b) incomplete or inaccurate sample manifests or marker panel specifications provided by Customer;

(c) Batch volumes above the capacity limits in Section 3.5 of the Agreement;

(d) a force majeure event under Section 15.5 of the Agreement; or

(e) a change requested by Customer after the Receipt Date.

Provider shall notify Customer within two (2) Business Days after the Receipt Date of any Sample-related defect it relies on under clause (a) or (b).

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Relationship to the Agreement.** This Schedule forms part of the Agreement. Service credits under this Schedule are Customer's remedies for the failures to which they apply as provided in Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

**6.2 Changes.** This Schedule may be amended only in writing signed by authorized representatives of both Parties in accordance with Section 15.6 of the Agreement.
