# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This **Quality Agreement** is entered into as of March 1, 2025 under the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the **"Agreement"**) between **Prupox Genomics, Inc.** (**"Provider"**) and **Staidaix Seed Genetics, LLC** (**"Customer"**).

This Quality Agreement is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement or the Service Level Schedule.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1 Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the quality responsibilities of Provider and Customer for the Services. It covers laboratory quality standards, Sample submission and receipt, chain of custody, data acceptance criteria, nonconformance procedures, re-run remedies, and record and Sample retention.

**1.2 Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Services performed under the Agreement and every Order Form, at every Provider facility at which Samples are received, stored or processed.

**1.3 Responsibilities.** Provider is responsible for the quality of the Services and Deliverables. Customer is responsible for the quality of Samples as submitted, for the accuracy of sample manifests and marker panel specifications, and for the timely review of Deliverables under Section 7.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

In this Quality Agreement:

**"Acceptance Criteria"** means the data acceptance criteria in Section 6.2.

**"CAPA"** means a corrective and preventive action plan prepared under Section 8.3.

**"Deviation"** means any departure from a Provider standard operating procedure, validated assay protocol or this Quality Agreement during the receipt, storage, handling or processing of Samples, or the generation of data for a Batch.

**"Nonconformance"** means a failure of a Batch or its Deliverables to meet the Acceptance Criteria or the specifications in the applicable Order Form.

**"Nonconformance Notice"** means a written notice from Customer to Provider that meets the requirements of Section 7.3.

**"Quality Contact"** means each Party's designated representative for quality matters under Section 3.3.

**"Re-run"** means the reprocessing of a Batch, or of affected plates or Samples within a Batch, using retained DNA or, where retained DNA is insufficient, replacement Samples.

**"SOPs"** means Provider's written standard operating procedures for the Services.

## 3. QUALITY SYSTEM

**3.1 Quality Management System.** Provider shall maintain a documented quality management system for the Services that is consistent with ISO/IEC 17025 or an equivalent recognized laboratory standard. The system shall include document control, personnel training records, instrument qualification and calibration, reagent lot control, internal audit and management review.

**3.2 Accreditation.** Provider shall keep in force each laboratory accreditation it holds as of the date of this Quality Agreement for the facilities performing the Services. Provider shall notify Customer in writing within five (5) Business Days after any suspension, withdrawal or material adverse finding affecting that accreditation.

**3.3 Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact and notify the other Party of any change in writing. On the date of this Quality Agreement, Provider's Quality Contact is its Director, Quality Assurance (quality@prupoxgenomics.com) and Customer's Quality Contact is its Lab Operations Manager (labops@staidaix.com).

**3.4 Training.** Provider shall ensure that all personnel handling Samples or processing data for the Services are trained on the applicable SOPs before performing that work, and shall keep training records available for Customer review under Section 10.

## 4. SAMPLE SUBMISSION AND RECEIPT

**4.1 Submission Requirements.** Customer shall submit Samples in accordance with Provider's sample submission guidelines then in effect. Each shipment must include a complete sample manifest identifying each Sample by Customer identifier and plate position, the crop, the marker panel or assay requested, whether expedited processing is requested, and the location of any Customer-designated check lines.

**4.2 Sample Formats.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Samples shall be submitted as leaf tissue punches or seed chips in 96-well plates in the configuration specified in the submission guidelines, or as extracted DNA meeting the concentration and purity thresholds in the submission guidelines.

**4.3 Receipt Inspection.** On receipt, Provider shall inspect each shipment, verify it against the sample manifest, record shipment temperature and package condition, and log the Batch in its laboratory information management system. Provider shall notify Customer of any Sample-related defect within the period stated in Section 5 of the Service Level Schedule.

**4.4 Rejected Samples.** Provider may decline to process Samples that are materially non-compliant with the submission guidelines. Provider shall hold any declined Samples for Customer's instructions for at least thirty (30) days.

## 5. CHAIN OF CUSTODY AND HANDLING

**5.1 Chain of Custody.** Provider shall maintain an unbroken, documented chain of custody for every Sample from receipt through extraction, processing, storage, and return or destruction. Each transfer of custody, storage location and processing step shall be recorded in Provider's laboratory information management system with date, time and responsible personnel.

**5.2 Segregation and Security.** Provider shall store Samples and extracted DNA in access-controlled areas, segregated from material of other customers, and shall label all containers with Provider lot identifiers that can be traced to Customer's sample manifest.

**5.3 Storage Conditions.** Provider shall store tissue and extracted DNA under the temperature conditions specified in its SOPs and shall monitor storage equipment continuously with alarm notification. Provider shall notify Customer within two (2) Business Days after any temperature excursion that may affect Samples.

**5.4 Sample Use.** Provider shall handle Samples only as permitted by Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

## 6. LABORATORY CONTROLS AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

**6.1 Process Controls.** For each plate in a Batch, Provider shall include positive controls, no-template controls and its internal reference samples as specified in the applicable validated assay protocol. Customer may designate check lines within its Samples, and Provider shall report results for those check lines separately.

**6.2 Acceptance Criteria.** A Batch meets the Acceptance Criteria if:

(a) the Call Rate for the Batch is at least 97.0%;

(b) the Concordance Rate for the Batch is at least 99.5%;

(c) the Sample Failure Rate for the Batch is no more than 3.0%;

(d) all controls on each plate perform within the limits in the applicable assay protocol; and

(e) the Deliverables are complete, in the format specified in the applicable Order Form, and correctly mapped to Customer's sample identifiers.

**6.3 Provider Release Review.** Before the Data Release Date for a Batch, Provider's quality personnel shall review the Batch data against the Acceptance Criteria. If Provider identifies a Nonconformance before release, Provider shall notify Customer and either initiate a Re-run or release the data with a written summary of the Nonconformance, as Customer directs.

**6.4 Deviations.** Provider shall document every Deviation, assess its impact on the affected Batch, and notify Customer within three (3) Business Days after identifying any Deviation that may affect data for a Customer Batch.

## 7. CUSTOMER REVIEW AND NONCONFORMANCE NOTICE

**7.1 Customer Review.** Following each Data Release Date, Customer shall review the Deliverables for the Batch against the Acceptance Criteria.

**7.2 Notice Period.** Customer shall give Provider a Nonconformance Notice for a Batch within ten (10) Business Days after the Data Release Date for that Batch. If Customer does not give a Nonconformance Notice for a Batch within that period, the Batch and its Deliverables are deemed accepted, and Customer will have no right to a Re-run, replacement data, service credit under Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule, or refund in respect of that Batch.

**7.3 Content and Delivery.** A Nonconformance Notice must be in writing, identify the Batch by Provider lot identifier, identify the affected plates, Samples or markers, and describe the Nonconformance with reasonable supporting data. Customer shall send the Nonconformance Notice by email to Provider's Quality Contact, with a copy to Provider's account manager.

**7.4 Acknowledgement.** Provider shall acknowledge each Nonconformance Notice in writing within two (2) Business Days after receipt and assign it a nonconformance reference number.

## 8. INVESTIGATION AND REMEDIES

**8.1 Investigation.** Within ten (10) Business Days after receiving a Nonconformance Notice, Provider shall investigate the reported Nonconformance, including review of control results, instrument logs, reagent lots and chain-of-custody records, and deliver a written investigation report to Customer stating whether the Batch meets the Acceptance Criteria and the root cause of any confirmed Nonconformance.

**8.2 Re-run.** If Provider confirms a Nonconformance, or if the investigation does not show that the Nonconformance resulted from a Customer-caused defect described in Section 5(a) or (b) of the Service Level Schedule, Provider shall perform a Re-run of the affected plates or Samples at no charge to Customer. Provider shall post Re-run data to the Customer Portal within the standard Turnaround Time for the applicable Service Line, measured from the date of Provider's investigation report or, where replacement Samples are needed, from their Receipt Date.

**8.3 CAPA.** For any confirmed Nonconformance attributable to Provider, Provider shall prepare a CAPA identifying the root cause, corrective actions and preventive actions, with target completion dates, and deliver it to Customer with the investigation report. Provider shall report on CAPA progress at each quarterly review under Section 3.3 of the Service Level Schedule.

**8.4 Credits and Refund.** For a Batch subject to a timely Nonconformance Notice, the data quality service credit in Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule applies. If Re-run data for the affected plates or Samples again fails the Acceptance Criteria, Provider shall refund or credit, at Customer's election, the Fees for those plates or Samples.

**8.5 Disputes.** If the Parties disagree about the results of an investigation, either Party's Quality Contact may escalate the matter for resolution under Section 15.2 of the Agreement. Fees for a Batch subject to a pending Nonconformance Notice may be disputed under Section 4.4 of the Agreement.

## 9. RECORDS AND SAMPLE RETENTION

**9.1 Records.** Provider shall retain laboratory records for the Services in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Agreement, including raw intensity and sequence files, genotype call files, control results, Deviation records, investigation reports and CAPAs.

**9.2 Residual Sample Retention.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Provider shall retain:

(a) residual extracted DNA for each Batch for six (6) months after the Data Release Date; and

(b) residual tissue and seed chips for each Batch for ninety (90) days after the Data Release Date.

**9.3 Extended Retention.** For any Batch subject to a Nonconformance Notice, Provider shall retain residual DNA and tissue until the later of the end of the periods in Section 9.2 and sixty (60) days after the matter is closed.

**9.4 Return or Destruction.** At the end of the applicable retention period, Provider shall return residual Samples to Customer at Customer's request and expense, or otherwise destroy them by a validated method and provide a certificate of destruction on request.

## 10. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

**10.1 Quality Audits.** In addition to site visits under Section 5.3 of the Agreement, Customer may conduct a quality audit of Provider's facilities, SOPs and records relating to the Services once in each Contract Year on at least ten (10) Business Days' notice, and at any time on reasonable notice following a confirmed Nonconformance attributable to Provider.

**10.2 Findings.** Provider shall respond in writing to audit findings within twenty (20) Business Days, including a CAPA for each significant finding.

**10.3 Regulatory Inspections.** Provider shall notify Customer promptly of any governmental inspection of its facilities that relates to the handling of Customer Samples, and shall provide Customer with a summary of any findings relating to the Services.

## 11. CHANGES

**11.1 Change Notification.** Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any material change to the validated assay protocols, marker panels, instrument platforms, genotype calling software or processing sites used for the Services.

**11.2 Validation.** Provider shall validate each material change before implementing it for Customer Batches and, on request, provide Customer with a summary of the validation results, including concordance with data generated under the prior process on a set of Customer check lines.

**11.3 Customer Marker Panels.** Provider shall not modify any Customer marker panel without Customer's prior written approval.

## 12. GENERAL

**12.1 Relationship to the Agreement.** This Quality Agreement forms part of the Agreement and is subject to its terms.

**12.2 Review.** The Quality Contacts shall review this Quality Agreement at least once in each Contract Year and propose any updates for approval.

**12.3 Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by authorized representatives of both Parties in accordance with Section 15.6 of the Agreement.
