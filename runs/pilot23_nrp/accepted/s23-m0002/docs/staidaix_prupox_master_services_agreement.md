# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into and made effective as of **March 1, 2025** (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between:

**Prupox Genomics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Meridian Research Parkway, Durham, North Carolina 27703 (**"Provider"**); and

**Staidaix Seed Genetics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Prairie Gate Road, Ames, Iowa 50010 (**"Customer"**).

Provider and Customer are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties."**

## RECITALS

A. Provider operates accredited laboratory facilities and provides marker-assisted genotyping, sequencing and trait-screening services to plant breeding organizations.

B. Customer conducts soybean and corn breeding programs and wishes to buy laboratory services from Provider, and Provider wishes to provide those services, on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Forms executed under it.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies, whether by contract or otherwise.

**"Applicable Law"** means all laws, regulations, rules and orders of any governmental authority that apply to a Party's performance under this Agreement, including laws governing the import, movement and handling of plant material.

**"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day observed as a federal holiday in the United States.

**"Change of Control"** means, for any entity: (a) the acquisition, directly or indirectly, by any person or group of persons acting in concert, of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of that entity; (b) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction involving that entity in which the holders of its voting equity immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the surviving or resulting entity immediately after it; (c) the sale of all or substantially all of that entity's assets; or (d) any event described in clauses (a) to (c) occurring for any parent entity that directly or indirectly controls that entity, such that control of that entity changes indirectly.

**"Confidential Information"** has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

**"Customer Data"** means all genotype calls, marker data, sequence reads, trait-screening results, reports and other data generated by Provider from Samples in performing the Services, together with all pedigree, line, field and other information that Customer provides to Provider.

**"Customer Germplasm"** means all seed, tissue, DNA and other plant material, and all genetic lines, varieties, hybrids and inbreds, owned or controlled by Customer or its Affiliates.

**"Data Release Date"** has the meaning given in the Service Level Schedule.

**"Deliverables"** means the data files, reports and other outputs that Provider delivers to Customer under an Order Form.

**"Fees"** means the fees stated in an Order Form for the Services.

**"Order Form"** means an ordering document that both Parties execute and that references this Agreement, specifying Services, volumes, Fees, commitments and related terms.

**"Provider Technology"** means Provider's laboratory protocols, assay designs, marker panels (excluding any marker panel designed by or for Customer), software, pipelines, instruments and know-how that existed before the Effective Date or that Provider develops independently of Customer Confidential Information.

**"Quality Agreement"** means the quality agreement between the Parties governing laboratory quality standards, sample handling, data acceptance criteria and nonconformance procedures for the Services, as amended from time to time.

**"Samples"** means Customer Germplasm in any form that Customer or its designee submits to Provider for testing.

**"Service Level Schedule"** means the service level schedule between the Parties setting out turnaround times, data quality metrics, service credits and related definitions, as amended from time to time.

**"Services"** means the genotyping, sequencing, trait-screening and related laboratory and data services that Provider performs under this Agreement and any Order Form.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. A reference to a document includes that document as amended in accordance with its terms.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Quality Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and each Order Form are intended to be read together. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, the following order of precedence applies: this Agreement, then the Quality Agreement, then the Service Level Schedule, then Order Forms. A term in an Order Form will prevail over a conflicting term of this Agreement only if the Order Form expressly identifies, by section number, the provision of this Agreement that it is intended to override, and then only for the Services under that Order Form.

## 2. SERVICES AND ORDER FORMS

**2.1 Performance of Services.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Order Form in accordance with this Agreement, the Quality Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and Applicable Law.

**2.2 Order Forms.** Each Order Form is governed by and incorporates this Agreement. An Order Form becomes binding only when signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Each Order Form will specify, as applicable, the Services, the crops and marker panels to be used, estimated Sample volumes, Fees, any minimum commitment, the order term and any additional terms.

**2.3 Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Provider with a non-binding rolling quarterly forecast of anticipated Sample volumes not later than fifteen (15) days before the start of each calendar quarter. Forecasts do not create any purchase obligation except as stated in an Order Form.

**2.4 Changes.** Either Party may request a change to the scope of Services under an Order Form. No change is binding until documented in a written change order or amended Order Form signed by both Parties.

## 3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS; SAMPLES; EXCLUSIVITY

**3.1 Sample Submission.** Customer shall prepare, label and ship Samples in accordance with Provider's sample submission guidelines and the Quality Agreement. Customer is responsible for obtaining any phytosanitary certificates, permits or other approvals required for the shipment of Samples to Provider's facilities.

**3.2 Information and Cooperation.** Customer shall provide Provider with sample manifests, marker panel specifications and other information reasonably required to perform the Services, and shall designate a program contact for day-to-day communications.

**3.3 Ownership of Samples.** As between the Parties, Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to all Samples and Customer Germplasm. Provider shall use Samples solely to perform the Services and shall not sequence, propagate, reverse engineer, transfer or otherwise exploit any Sample for any other purpose. Provider shall return or destroy residual Samples in accordance with the retention periods in the Quality Agreement.

**3.4 Exclusivity.** During the term of this Agreement, Customer and its Affiliates shall procure all genotyping, sequencing and trait-screening services for any crop species in any territory exclusively from Provider, and shall not engage any third-party laboratory or service provider, or perform those services internally, except with Provider's prior written consent. Provider will have a reasonable opportunity to quote for any such services before Customer or any Affiliate seeks consent to procure them elsewhere.

**3.5 Capacity.** Provider shall maintain laboratory capacity sufficient to process Customer's forecast Sample volumes within the turnaround times in the Service Level Schedule, provided that actual volumes do not exceed one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the most recent forecast.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Order Form. Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Fees are calculated per Sample per marker panel at the rates in that Order Form.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Services for which the Data Release Date has occurred during the preceding month, together with any other amounts that become due under an Order Form.

**4.3 Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Payments shall be made in U.S. dollars by electronic funds transfer to the account Provider designates in writing.

**4.4 Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider in writing before the payment due date with reasonable detail of the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Customer shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

**4.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.

**4.6 Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer is responsible for those taxes, other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

**4.7 Service Credits.** Service credits accrued under the Service Level Schedule shall be applied against the next invoice issued under the applicable Order Form.

## 5. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTORS

**5.1 Personnel.** Provider shall assign appropriately trained and qualified personnel to perform the Services and shall designate an account manager as Customer's primary point of contact. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Customer.

**5.2 Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract the laboratory processing of Samples without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its permitted subcontractors as if they were its own.

**5.3 Site Visits.** On at least ten (10) Business Days' notice, and not more than once per Contract Year except for cause, Customer may visit Provider's facilities during normal business hours to observe Sample handling and data processing related to the Services, subject to Provider's reasonable safety and confidentiality procedures.

## 6. QUALITY AND SERVICE LEVELS

**6.1 Quality Agreement.** Provider shall perform the Services in compliance with the Quality Agreement, including its sample handling, chain-of-custody, data acceptance and nonconformance procedures. Customer shall comply with its obligations under the Quality Agreement, including the timing requirements for notices of nonconformance.

**6.2 Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the service levels in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits under the Service Level Schedule, together with the re-run and other remedies in the Quality Agreement, are Customer's remedies for the failures to which they apply, without prejudice to Customer's rights under Section 12.

**6.3 Records.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate laboratory records for all Services, including instrument logs, control results and chain-of-custody records, for at least three (3) years after the relevant Data Release Date, and shall make them available to Customer on reasonable request.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA

**7.1 Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in and to its pre-existing intellectual property. Provider retains ownership of the Provider Technology. Customer retains ownership of Customer Germplasm and all intellectual property in it.

**7.2 Customer Data and Deliverables.** All Customer Data and Deliverables are the exclusive property of Customer from the time of creation. Provider hereby assigns to Customer all right, title and interest it may have in Customer Data and Deliverables. Provider shall not use Customer Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, and shall not aggregate, de-identify or combine Customer Data with other data for its own purposes.

**7.3 Customer Marker Panels.** Any marker panel, SNP set or assay that Provider designs for Customer, or that is based on Customer's sequence data or Confidential Information, is Customer's property and may not be offered to any third party.

**7.4 License to Provider.** Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the term of this Agreement to use Samples, Customer Data and Customer Confidential Information solely as needed to perform the Services.

**7.5 Improvements.** Improvements to Provider Technology of general application that Provider develops in performing the Services, and that do not incorporate or reveal Customer Data or Customer Confidential Information, belong to Provider.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Definition.** **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the **"Discloser"**) to the other (the **"Recipient"**) in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data, Customer Germplasm, pedigrees and breeding program information are Customer's Confidential Information. The terms of each Order Form are the Confidential Information of both Parties.

**8.2 Obligations.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity and in no event less than reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

**8.3 Exclusions.** These obligations do not apply to information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**8.4 Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, if it gives the Discloser prompt notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

**8.5 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 8 continue during the term of this Agreement and for five (5) years after it ends, except that obligations relating to Customer Germplasm, Customer Data and trade secrets continue for so long as that information remains confidential.

## 9. WARRANTIES

**9.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

**9.2 Provider Warranties.** Provider warrants that (a) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with good laboratory practice in the agricultural genomics industry; (b) the Services and Deliverables will conform to the specifications in the applicable Order Form and the data acceptance criteria in the Quality Agreement; (c) Provider holds and will maintain all licenses, accreditations and permits required to perform the Services; and (d) the Provider Technology, as used to perform the Services, does not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

**9.3 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. PROVIDER DOES NOT WARRANT ANY PARTICULAR BREEDING, AGRONOMIC OR COMMERCIAL OUTCOME FROM CUSTOMER'S USE OF THE DELIVERABLES.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**10.1 By Provider.** Provider shall defend Customer and its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim arising from (a) an allegation that the Provider Technology or the Services infringe or misappropriate any intellectual property right; (b) Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Provider's breach of Section 3.3 or Section 8, and shall indemnify them against resulting damages, settlements and reasonable attorneys' fees.

**10.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend Provider and its officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim arising from (a) Customer's failure to obtain permits required for the shipment of Samples; or (b) Customer's gross negligence or willful misconduct, and shall indemnify them against resulting damages, settlements and reasonable attorneys' fees.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**11.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR LOST REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

**11.2 Cap.** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER ALL ORDER FORMS DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

**11.3 Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10; (b) a Party's breach of Section 8; (c) Provider's breach of Section 3.3 or Section 7.2, or any loss of, damage to or misuse of Customer Germplasm; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (e) Customer's obligation to pay Fees and other amounts due under this Agreement or any Order Form.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

**12.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five (5) years. It will then renew automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term. If an Order Form remains in effect when this Agreement expires, this Agreement will continue to govern that Order Form until it expires or is terminated.

**12.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any affected Order Form by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or that Order Form and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

**12.3 Termination by Provider for Assignment.** Provider may terminate this Agreement and any or all Order Forms immediately by written notice to Customer if Customer assigns or transfers, or purports to assign or transfer, this Agreement or any Order Form in breach of Section 14. A termination under this Section 12.3 is a termination for Customer's breach for all purposes of this Agreement and each Order Form.

**12.4 Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for a substantial part of its assets, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**12.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Order Form: (a) Customer shall pay all Fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination, together with any other amounts that become payable under the applicable Order Form on termination; (b) Provider shall, at Customer's election, complete processing of Samples already received or return them; (c) Provider shall deliver all Customer Data in its possession in the format specified in the applicable Order Form within thirty (30) days; and (d) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, subject to Section 6.3.

**12.6 Survival.** Sections 3.3, 4, 6.3, 7, 8, 9.3, 10, 11, 12.5, 12.6, 14 and 15, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. INSURANCE

During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years after it ends, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) professional errors and omissions liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

**14.1 General.** Neither Party may assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any Order Form, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent. Any purported assignment or transfer in breach of this Section 14 is void. Subject to this Section 14, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**14.2 Change of Control.** A Change of Control of Customer is deemed an assignment of this Agreement and each Order Form by Customer and requires Provider's prior written consent under Section 14.1. Customer shall give Provider written notice of any proposed Change of Control of Customer as soon as reasonably practicable and, where lawful, before it occurs. Provider may withhold or condition its consent in its discretion.

**14.3 Provider Assignment.** Provider may assign this Agreement without Customer's consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its genotyping laboratory business, provided that the successor agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement and Provider gives Customer written notice within ten (10) Business Days after the assignment.

## 15. GENERAL

**15.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**15.2 Disputes.** The Parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between their senior executives for thirty (30) days after either Party gives written notice of the dispute. If the dispute is not resolved, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts. Nothing in this Section 15.2 prevents a Party from seeking urgent injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

**15.3 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses stated on the signature page (or any updated address a Party designates by notice). Notices are effective on receipt, or if received after 5:00 p.m. local time or on a day that is not a Business Day, on the next Business Day.

**15.4 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**15.5 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure to perform (other than a payment obligation) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, acts of government and widespread utility failures, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may obtain the affected Services elsewhere during the period of delay.

**15.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**15.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions will remain in full force.

**15.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
