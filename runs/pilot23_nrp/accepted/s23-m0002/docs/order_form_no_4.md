## 1. SERVICES

**1.1 Services Ordered.** During the Order Term, Provider shall perform the following Services for Customer's breeding programs, on Samples Customer submits in accordance with the Agreement and the Quality Agreement:

| Service Line | Crop | Marker Panel / Assay | Standard Turnaround Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Targeted SNP marker panel genotyping | Soybean | Staidaix SoyCore-384 (Customer marker panel) | 10 Business Days |
| Mid-density SNP array genotyping | Soybean | Prupox GlyMax 6K array | 15 Business Days |
| Targeted SNP marker panel genotyping | Corn | Staidaix ZeaSelect-192 (Customer marker panel) | 10 Business Days |
| Trait-screening assays | Soybean | Herbicide tolerance event zygosity; SCN resistance (rhg1/Rhg4) | 12 Business Days |

**1.2 Facilities.** Provider shall perform the Services at its laboratory at 4410 Meridian Research Parkway, Durham, North Carolina 27703. Section 5.2 of the Agreement applies to any change of processing site.

**1.3 Forecasts.** Customer shall continue to provide rolling quarterly forecasts under Section 2.3 of the Agreement. Customer's current forecast for the first Contract Year is approximately 410,000 soybean Samples and 65,000 corn Samples, distributed across the Service Lines above.

## 2. EXCLUSIVE SERVICES

**2.1 Exclusive Services Scope.** The Parties agree that the exclusive services scope for this Order Form is: Soybean marker-assisted genotyping services performed for Customer in the United States (the **"Exclusive Services"**).

**2.2 Right to Quote.** Before Customer submits Samples for any Exclusive Services to a third-party laboratory, Customer shall give Provider written notice of the proposed volume and turnaround requirements, and Provider will have ten (10) Business Days to submit a quotation.

**2.3 Capacity for Exclusive Services.** Provider shall reserve laboratory capacity for the Exclusive Services sufficient to process Customer's forecast volumes within the Turnaround Times in the Service Level Schedule, in accordance with Section 3.5 of the Agreement.

## 3. MINIMUM ANNUAL COMMITMENT

**3.1 Commitment.** Customer commits to purchase Services under this Order Form with Fees of at least $1,850,000 per Contract Year for a three-year Order Term (the **"Minimum Annual Commitment"**).

**3.2 Contract Year.** **"Contract Year"** means each consecutive twelve (12) month period during the Order Term, the first beginning on the Order Form Effective Date.

**3.3 Counting Toward the Commitment.** Fees invoiced for Services with a Data Release Date in a Contract Year count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment for that Contract Year. Expedited surcharges count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment. Service credits do not reduce the amount counted.

**3.4 Shortfall.** If the Fees counted for a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Annual Commitment, Provider may invoice Customer for the difference (the **"Shortfall Amount"**) within thirty (30) days after the end of that Contract Year. The Shortfall Amount is payable in accordance with Section 4.3 of the Agreement.

**3.5 No Carry-Forward.** Fees in excess of the Minimum Annual Commitment in any Contract Year do not carry forward to any later Contract Year.

## 4. FEES

**4.1 Per-Sample Fees.** The following Fees apply for the first Contract Year:

| Service Line | Fee per Sample (Standard) | Expedited Surcharge |
|---|---|---|
| Soybean SoyCore-384 targeted SNP genotyping | $3.15 | +40% |
| Soybean GlyMax 6K mid-density array | $9.80 | +35% |
| Corn ZeaSelect-192 targeted SNP genotyping | $2.85 | +40% |
| Soybean trait-screening assay (per assay) | $1.95 | +40% |

**4.2 Price Adjustment.** On the first day of the second and third Contract Years, Provider may increase the Fees by up to the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage increase in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve (12) months, on at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

**4.3 Expedited Volume.** Expedited Batches may not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of Customer's Samples in any calendar quarter without Provider's written agreement.

**4.4 Invoicing and Payment.** Fees are invoiced and payable in accordance with Section 4 of the Agreement.

## 5. TERMINATION AND ACCELERATION

**5.1 Termination.** This Order Form may be terminated only in accordance with Section 12 of the Agreement.

**5.2 Acceleration on Termination for Customer's Breach.** If Provider terminates this Order Form for Customer's breach, the unpaid Minimum Annual Commitment for the then-current Contract Year and each remaining Contract Year of the Order Term (less Fees already invoiced and counted toward the Minimum Annual Commitment for the then-current Contract Year) becomes immediately due and payable, without further notice or demand. Provider may invoice that amount on or after the effective date of termination, and Customer shall pay it within thirty (30) days after receipt of the invoice.

**5.3 Termination by Customer for Provider's Breach.** If Customer terminates this Order Form under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, the Minimum Annual Commitment will not apply to the Contract Year in which termination takes effect or any later Contract Year.

## 6. LOT SG-26-117

**6.1 Background.** Lot SG-26-117 is a Batch of 18,432 soybean Samples submitted by Customer under Order Form No. 3 for SoyCore-384 targeted SNP genotyping. The Data Release Date for Lot SG-26-117 was September 14, 2026. Customer's Nonconformance Notice for Lot SG-26-117 is dated October 2, 2026, and reports a Call Rate and Concordance Rate below the Service Levels in Section 2.2 of the Service Level Schedule across 23 of 48 plates.

**6.2 Parties' Positions.** Provider's position is that Customer's Nonconformance Notice for Lot SG-26-117 was not given within the period required by the Quality Agreement, and that Lot SG-26-117 has been accepted. Customer disputes Provider's position that the notice was untimely, has not accepted Lot SG-26-117, and reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement, the Quality Agreement and the Service Level Schedule in respect of Lot SG-26-117, including re-run, service credit and refund remedies.

**6.3 Invoice.** Customer has disputed Provider's invoice for Lot SG-26-117 under Section 4.4 of the Agreement. Fees for Lot SG-26-117 do not count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment under this Order Form.

**6.4 Discussions.** The Parties shall continue to discuss Lot SG-26-117 through their senior executives under Section 15.2 of the Agreement.

## 7. DELIVERABLES AND DATA

**7.1 Format.** Provider shall post final genotype data for each Batch to the Customer Portal as (a) a genotype call matrix in tab-delimited text and HapMap formats; (b) per-Sample and per-marker call rate summaries; and (c) control and check-line concordance reports.

**7.2 Return of Data.** For the purposes of Section 12.5(c) of the Agreement, Customer Data will be delivered in the formats in Section 7.1 together with raw intensity files, on encrypted media or by secure file transfer.
