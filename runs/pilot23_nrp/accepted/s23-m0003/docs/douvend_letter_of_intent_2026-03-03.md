# DOUVEND ENTERTAINMENT GROUP, INC.

**March 3, 2026**

**CONFIDENTIAL**

Bikas Interactive Holdings, Inc.
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Emberline Forge Studios, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter of Intent**") sets out the principal terms on which Douvend Entertainment Group, Inc. ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Emberline Forge Studios, Inc. (the "**Company**") from Bikas Interactive Holdings, Inc. ("**Seller**"), its sole stockholder (the "**Transaction**"). Except as stated in Paragraph 12, this Letter of Intent is a non-binding statement of the parties' current intentions. It will serve as the basis for negotiating a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and related ancillary agreements.

## 1. Transaction Structure

Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, so that the Company becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer at the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**").

## 2. Purchase Price

The purchase price for the Shares will consist of:

(a) a base purchase price of $84,000,000 in cash (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), payable at Closing and subject to a customary working capital adjustment against a target to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement; and

(b) the Earnout described in Paragraph 3.

## 3. Earnout

In addition to the Base Purchase Price, Buyer will pay Seller contingent consideration of up to $30,000,000 in the aggregate (the "**Earnout**"), based on the net revenue of the Company's title "Ashen Vanguard" during the three years following the Closing Date. The Earnout will be measured and paid annually, with revenue thresholds, calculation methodology, reporting, audit rights and dispute resolution to be set out in the Definitive Agreement. During the earnout period, Buyer will operate the title in good faith and will not take any action with the primary purpose of avoiding or reducing the Earnout.

## 4. Retained Business and Cinderloom Engine

(a) *Retained Business.* Seller will retain its mobile casual game publishing business, operated as "Bikas Pocket," which is not part of the Transaction.

(b) *Cinderloom Engine.* Seller will retain all right, title and interest in its proprietary "Cinderloom" game engine. At Closing, Seller will grant the Company a non-exclusive license to use the Cinderloom engine for the development, maintenance and publishing of Ashen Vanguard and the Company's other titles under development at Closing (the "**Retained Engine License**"). The Retained Engine License will contain customary restrictions on sublicensing, disclosure, reverse engineering and use of Seller's engine technology and confidential information outside the licensed scope.

(c) *Transition Services.* Seller will provide limited transition services to the Company for an agreed period following Closing, on terms and at cost-based fees to be agreed.

## 5. Representations and Warranties; Seller Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer customary for a transaction of this type. Seller's indemnification for breaches of its general representations and warranties will survive for 18 months after Closing and will be subject to a cap of 10% of the Base Purchase Price ($8,400,000), a basket of 1% of the Base Purchase Price and a customary de minimis threshold. Breaches of fundamental representations and fraud will be excluded from the cap, with Seller's liability for breaches of fundamental representations limited to the Base Purchase Price.

## 6. Buyer Indemnification and Liability Cap

Buyer will indemnify Seller for breaches of Buyer's representations, warranties and covenants in the Definitive Agreement and for the operation of the Company after Closing. Buyer's aggregate indemnification liability will be subject to the same cap that applies to Seller's general indemnification obligations, except that the following will be excluded from Buyer's liability cap: fraud, Earnout payment obligations, and breaches of the Retained Engine License.

## 7. Key Personnel

Buyer considers the continued involvement of the Company's studio head, Teodor Vasquez-Ilunga, important to the Transaction. Buyer may, with Seller's cooperation, discuss with Mr. Vasquez-Ilunga the terms of his continued role with the Company following Closing. Seller and Buyer will cooperate to identify any other key employees of the Company.

## 8. Restrictive Covenants

For three years following the Closing Date, Seller will not, and will cause its controlled affiliates not to, engage in the development or publishing of tactical role-playing games for console and PC only. The restriction will not apply to the Bikas Pocket business described in Paragraph 4(a), to Seller's continued ownership, development and licensing of the Cinderloom engine, or to passive ownership of less than 5% of the publicly traded securities of any person. Seller will also agree to a customary two-year non-solicitation of the Company's key employees, subject to an exception for general solicitations.

## 9. Conditions to Closing

The Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including (a) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement and ancillary agreements satisfactory to both parties, (b) completion of Buyer's confirmatory due diligence, (c) receipt of any required third-party consents and governmental approvals, (d) the absence of a material adverse effect on the Company, and (e) approval of each party's board of directors.

## 10. Due Diligence and Timing

Following signature of this Letter of Intent, Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the books, records, contracts, personnel and facilities of the Company. The parties intend to sign the Definitive Agreement within approximately 75 days after the date of this Letter of Intent and to close as soon as practicable thereafter. Buyer's counsel will prepare the initial draft of the Definitive Agreement.

## 11. Expenses

Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter of Intent and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only this Paragraph 12 and Paragraphs 11, 13, 14 and 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding. No other provision of this Letter of Intent creates any legally binding obligation, and no obligation to complete the Transaction will arise unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered by both parties.

## 13. Exclusivity

For 60 days following the date of this Letter of Intent, Seller will not, and will cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer regarding any sale of the Shares or any material portion of the Company's assets. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of that kind that it receives.

## 14. Confidentiality

The terms of this Letter of Intent and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction are subject to the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller dated January 20, 2026, which remains in full force and effect.

## 15. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter of Intent is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

If this Letter of Intent reflects our mutual understanding, please sign and return a copy to the undersigned. This Letter of Intent will expire if not countersigned by 5:00 p.m. (New York time) on March 10, 2026.
