# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Emberline Forge Studios, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Teodor Vasquez-Ilunga**, an individual ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company develops and publishes tactical role-playing video games, including the title known as "Ashen Vanguard."

B. Executive has substantial experience in game direction, combat systems design and character animation. Executive personally developed and owns an animation toolkit known as "Kestrel Motion," which he created before joining the Company.

C. The Company wishes to employ Executive as its Studio Head and to obtain a license to use the Kestrel Motion toolkit in its games. Executive wishes to accept that employment and grant that license, in each case on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the following meanings in this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Company.

"**Board**" means the Board of Directors of the Company.

"**Cause**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

"**Change of Control**" means any of the following: (a) the sale, transfer or other disposition, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of a majority of the outstanding equity securities of the Company, whether by the Company's stockholders or otherwise; (b) a merger, consolidation or reorganization of the Company after which the holders of the Company's voting securities immediately before the transaction hold less than a majority of the voting power of the surviving or resulting entity; or (c) the sale, lease or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

"**Company Games**" means Ashen Vanguard and every other video game, including all versions, ports, expansions, downloadable content and sequels, developed or published by the Company during the Term.

"**Competing Business**" means the development, publishing or distribution of any video game in any genre, on any platform.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

"**Kestrel Motion**" means the animation toolkit known as "Kestrel Motion" owned by Executive, including its procedural animation libraries, rigging and retargeting tools, motion-blending modules, source code, object code, documentation and all updates, improvements and modifications made by Executive, whether before or after the Effective Date.

"**Kestrel Motion License**" means the license granted in Section 7.3(a).

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental body or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the Term and the twelve months following the termination of Executive's employment for any reason.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.

"**Work Product**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs Executive as Studio Head, reporting to the Board. Executive will have overall creative and operational responsibility for the Company's development studio, including the direction of Ashen Vanguard and other Company Games, and such other duties consistent with that position as the Board may assign.

**2.2 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote substantially all of his business time and attention to the Company. Executive may serve on the boards of non-profit organizations and manage his personal investments, provided those activities do not interfere with his duties and do not involve any Competing Business.

**2.3 Place of Performance.** Executive will perform his duties principally at the Company's studio premises, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. TERM

Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of three years (the "**Initial Term**"). It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current period. The Initial Term and any renewal periods, ending on the date Executive's employment terminates, are the "**Term**." Executive's employment may be terminated earlier under Section 5.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company will pay Executive an annual base salary of $325,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board will review the base salary each year and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 40% of base salary, based on studio and title milestones set by the Board after consulting Executive. Any bonus will be paid by March 15 of the year after the year to which it relates, subject to Executive's continued employment through the payment date except as provided in Section 5.5.

**4.3 Title Royalty Pool.** Executive will participate in the Company's development team royalty pool for Ashen Vanguard on the terms of the pool plan adopted by the Board, as amended from time to time.

**4.4 Benefits.** Executive may participate in all employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.5 Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to 25 days of paid time off each year, in addition to Company holidays.

**4.6 Expenses.** The Company will reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing his duties, in accordance with Company policy.

## 5. TERMINATION

**5.1 General.** Either party may terminate Executive's employment at any time in accordance with this Section 5. On termination for any reason, Executive will receive accrued but unpaid base salary, accrued unused paid time off and unreimbursed expenses (the "**Accrued Obligations**").

**5.2 Termination by the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means Executive's (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence that materially harms the Company; (c) material breach of Section 6, 7 or 8; or (d) willful and continued failure to perform his material duties after written notice from the Board and a 30-day opportunity to cure.

**5.3 Termination by Executive for Good Reason.** Executive may terminate his employment for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means, without Executive's consent, (a) a material reduction in his base salary; (b) a material reduction in his duties, authority or responsibilities as Studio Head; or (c) relocation of his principal place of work by more than 50 miles. Executive must give written notice within 60 days after the condition first arises, the Company must fail to cure within 30 days after that notice, and Executive must resign within 30 days after the cure period ends.

**5.4 Death or Disability.** Executive's employment terminates on his death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment if he is unable to perform his essential duties, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 days in any twelve-month period.

**5.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment other than for Cause, death or disability, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, subject to Executive signing and not revoking a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, the Company will (a) continue Executive's base salary for twelve months, (b) pay a pro rata portion of the annual bonus for the year of termination based on actual performance, and (c) pay the employer portion of COBRA premiums for up to twelve months.

**5.6 Resignation from Offices.** On termination of employment for any reason, Executive will resign from all offices and directorships held with the Company and its Affiliates.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates and their games, including source code, game designs, unreleased content, engine and tool technology licensed to the Company, player data, financial information, business plans and the terms of the Company's contracts. Confidential Information does not include information that becomes publicly available other than through Executive's breach.

**6.2 Obligations.** During and after the Term, Executive shall hold Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform his duties for the Company, and not disclose it to any Person except as required by law or as authorized by the Company.

**6.3 Return of Materials.** On termination of employment, Executive shall return to the Company all documents, devices and materials containing Confidential Information, other than materials relating solely to Kestrel Motion.

**6.4 Protected Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to a governmental agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, Executive will not be held liable for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or made under seal in a legal proceeding.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 Work Product.** All inventions, works of authorship, software, designs, characters, artwork, audio, game mechanics and other materials that Executive creates, alone or with others, within the scope of his employment or using Company resources during the Term ("**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. To the extent permitted by law, Work Product is "work made for hire." Executive assigns to the Company all of his right, title and interest in any Work Product that is not work made for hire. Work Product does not include Kestrel Motion.

**7.2 Excluded Prior Works.** Executive retains all right, title and interest in Kestrel Motion and in the other works listed on Schedule A, each of which Executive created before the Effective Date. Improvements and modifications to Kestrel Motion made by Executive during the Term remain Executive's property and form part of Kestrel Motion, but any Company Game assets created using Kestrel Motion (including animation sets, rigs and motion data specific to a Company Game) are Work Product.

**7.3 Kestrel Motion License.**

(a) *Grant.* Executive grants to the Company a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license during the term of the Kestrel Motion License to use, reproduce, modify and integrate Kestrel Motion in the development, maintenance, porting and publishing of Company Games, and to distribute Kestrel Motion in object code form solely as embedded in Company Games.

(b) *Restrictions.* The Company shall not sublicense Kestrel Motion, other than to contractors working on Company Games under written confidentiality obligations, and shall not use Kestrel Motion for any purpose other than Company Games. The Company shall treat the source code of Kestrel Motion as Executive's confidential information.

(c) *Term of License.* The Kestrel Motion License begins on the Effective Date and continues until the earliest of (i) termination by mutual written agreement, (ii) termination by Executive if the Company materially breaches Section 7.3(b) and fails to cure within 30 days after written notice, and (iii) the date set out in Section 7.3(d). The Kestrel Motion License does not terminate on the termination of Executive's employment.

(d) *Change of Control.* The Kestrel Motion License will terminate automatically upon a Change of Control of the Company, unless before the Change of Control Executive has consented in writing to the continuation of the Kestrel Motion License following that Change of Control.

(e) *Effect of Termination.* On termination of the Kestrel Motion License, the Company shall cease all use and distribution of Kestrel Motion, including in any Company Game, and shall return or destroy all copies of Kestrel Motion in its possession.

(f) *Warranty.* Executive represents that he is the sole owner of Kestrel Motion and that, to his knowledge, Kestrel Motion does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**7.4 Further Assistance.** During and after the Term, Executive shall, at the Company's expense, sign documents and take other reasonable actions needed to perfect the Company's rights in Work Product.

**7.5 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Executive waives any moral rights in Work Product.

## 8. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**8.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, as an owner, employee, consultant, director or otherwise, engage in or provide services to any Competing Business. Passive ownership of less than 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person does not breach this Section 8.1.

**8.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any employee of the Company, or any individual who was an employee of the Company within the prior six months, to leave the Company or to work for any other Person.

**8.3 Non-Solicitation of Partners.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, induce any platform holder, publisher, distributor or licensor of the Company to terminate or adversely change its relationship with the Company.

**8.4 Reasonableness; Reformation.** Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 8 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's goodwill, Confidential Information and trade secrets. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**8.5 Remedies.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 6, 7 or 8 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief to prevent any such breach, in addition to any other remedy.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are effective when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company must be addressed to the Chair of the Board at the Company's principal office. Notices to Executive must be sent to his most recent address in the Company's records.

**9.2 Assignment.** Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. Section 7.3 is not affected by any such assignment.

**9.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.5 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and will be interpreted accordingly. Each severance installment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

**9.6 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required by law.

**9.7 Survival.** Sections 5.5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 survive termination of Executive's employment in accordance with their terms.

**9.8 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one agreement.
