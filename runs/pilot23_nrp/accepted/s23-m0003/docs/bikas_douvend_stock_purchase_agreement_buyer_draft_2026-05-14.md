# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**BUYER DRAFT — May 14, 2026**
*Privileged and Confidential. Subject to further review and comment. Not an offer capable of acceptance.*

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**" or the "**SPA**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 by and between **Bikas Interactive Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Douvend Entertainment Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Emberline Forge Studios, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company develops and publishes tactical role-playing video games, including the title known as "Ashen Vanguard."

C. Seller owns the proprietary game engine known as "Cinderloom," which is used by the Company. Seller will retain the Cinderloom engine and will license it to the Company at the Closing under the Retained Engine License.

D. Seller also conducts, and will retain after the Closing, Seller's mobile casual game publishing business, operated as "Bikas Pocket" (the "**Seller Retained Business**").

E. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Base Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate cash purchase price of $84,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"). The Base Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Section 2.4. Buyer will also pay any Earnout Payments that become payable under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller the Base Purchase Price, plus or minus the Estimated Adjustment Amount, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** At least five Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting out Seller's good faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital and the resulting difference from the Target Working Capital of $3,250,000 (the "**Estimated Adjustment Amount**").

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual Closing Working Capital. Seller will have 45 days to dispute the statement. The parties shall negotiate any dispute in good faith. Any dispute not resolved within 30 days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm jointly selected by the parties, whose determination will be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five Business Days after final determination.

**2.5 Earnout.**

(a) *Earnout Payments.* In addition to the Base Purchase Price, Buyer shall pay to Seller contingent consideration of up to $30,000,000 in the aggregate (the "**Maximum Earnout Amount**"), based on the Net Revenue of Ashen Vanguard during the three years following the Closing Date (the "**Earnout Period**"). The Earnout Period is divided into three consecutive twelve-month periods (each, an "**Earnout Year**").

(b) *Calculation.* For each Earnout Year in which Net Revenue of Ashen Vanguard equals or exceeds $40,000,000, Buyer shall pay to Seller $10,000,000 (each, an "**Earnout Payment**"). If Net Revenue for an Earnout Year is at least $30,000,000 but less than $40,000,000, the Earnout Payment for that Earnout Year will be an amount equal to $10,000,000 multiplied by a fraction, the numerator of which is the amount by which Net Revenue exceeds $30,000,000 and the denominator of which is $10,000,000. Aggregate Earnout Payments will not exceed the Maximum Earnout Amount. "**Net Revenue**" means gross revenue recognized by the Company and its Affiliates from the sale, licensing and in-game monetization of Ashen Vanguard, less platform fees, refunds, chargebacks and sales taxes, determined in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

(c) *Earnout Statement.* Within 60 days after the end of each Earnout Year, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of Net Revenue for that Earnout Year and the resulting Earnout Payment, with reasonable supporting detail. Seller and its representatives will have reasonable access to the relevant books and records of the Company. Disputes will be resolved under the procedure in Section 2.4, applied with the necessary changes.

(d) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay each Earnout Payment within 15 Business Days after the Earnout Statement for that Earnout Year becomes final.

(e) *Operation of the Business.* During the Earnout Period, Buyer shall, and shall cause the Company to, (i) act in good faith and not take any action with the primary purpose of avoiding or reducing any Earnout Payment, (ii) continue to make Ashen Vanguard commercially available on the platforms on which it is available at the Closing, and (iii) maintain separate books and records sufficient to calculate Net Revenue.

(f) *Acceleration.* If, during the Earnout Period, Buyer sells all or substantially all of the assets of the Company relating to Ashen Vanguard, the unpaid portion of the Maximum Earnout Amount for any Earnout Year not yet completed will become immediately due and payable, unless the acquirer assumes Buyer's obligations under this Section 2.5 in writing.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax law. Any amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority will be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after the conditions in Article 7 are satisfied or waived, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business relationships. Without Buyer's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, the Company will not amend its organizational documents, issue equity securities, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, or enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company.

**6.3 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company, except as required by law. From and after the Closing, Buyer shall, and shall cause the Company to, keep confidential all non-public information concerning Seller, the Seller Retained Business and the Cinderloom engine, except as required by law.

**6.4 Non-Competition.** For three years following the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, own, manage, operate or engage in any Competing Business anywhere in the world. For purposes of this Section 6.4, "**Competing Business**" has the meaning given in Section 1.1 of the Key Employee Agreement, being the development, publishing or distribution of any video game in any genre, on any platform. Ownership of less than 5% of the publicly traded securities of any Person will not by itself breach this Section 6.4.

**6.5 Non-Solicitation.** For two years following the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, solicit for employment any employee of the Company listed on Schedule 4.9. General solicitations not directed at Company employees will not breach this Section 6.5.

**6.6 Retained Engine License.** At the Closing, Seller shall grant to the Company, and Buyer shall cause the Company to accept, a non-exclusive license to use the Cinderloom engine in the development, maintenance and publishing of Ashen Vanguard and the Company's other titles under development at the Closing, on the terms of the Retained Engine License. Seller retains all right, title and interest in the Cinderloom engine.

**6.7 Restrictions on Use of Cinderloom Engine.** Buyer shall not, and shall cause the Company and its other Affiliates not to: (a) use the Cinderloom engine or any of its source code other than as permitted by the Retained Engine License; (b) sublicense, disclose or make available the Cinderloom engine, its source code or related documentation to any Person other than as permitted by the Retained Engine License; (c) reverse engineer, decompile or create derivative engines from the Cinderloom engine; or (d) use any confidential information relating to the Cinderloom engine to develop or improve any competing game engine.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the parties; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by August 31, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either party if the other party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it will become void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.3, this Section 8.2 and Article 11 will survive, and no party will be relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after expiry of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed. A claim made in writing with reasonable detail before the end of the applicable survival period will survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article 4;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any taxes of the Company attributable to any period ending on or before the Closing Date; and

(d) any third-party claim alleging that any Company IP, or the development, publishing or exploitation of any game title of the Company, infringes, misappropriates or otherwise violates any Intellectual Property of any Person, whether known or unknown, and whether arising before or after the Closing. Seller's obligations under this Section 9.2(d) apply without regard to the Cap, the Basket or the survival periods in Section 9.1.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article 5;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, including Buyer's obligations under Section 2.5 and Section 6.7; and

(c) the operation of the Company after the Closing.

**9.4 Limitations on Seller's Liability.**

(a) *Cap.* Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) will not exceed 10% of the Base Purchase Price ($8,400,000) (the "**Cap**"), except that the Cap does not apply to Losses arising out of any breach of a Fundamental Representation or out of fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of Fundamental Representations will not exceed the Base Purchase Price.

(b) *Basket.* Seller will not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, until the aggregate Losses exceed $840,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller will be liable only for Losses above the Basket.

(c) *De Minimis.* No individual claim, or series of related claims, under Section 9.2(a) involving Losses below $50,000 may be asserted, and those Losses will not count toward the Basket.

**9.5 Limitations on Buyer's Liability.**

(a) *Basket.* Buyer will not be liable under Section 9.3(a) until the aggregate Losses exceed the Basket, after which Buyer will be liable only for Losses above the Basket.

(b) *Cap.* Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3 shall not exceed the Cap, except for Losses arising out of Excluded Buyer Obligations.

**9.6 Indemnification Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall promptly notify the party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") in writing of any claim, describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice will not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. For third-party claims, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and the Indemnified Party may participate at its own expense. Neither party will settle a third-party claim without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

**9.7 Treatment of Indemnification Payments.** All indemnification payments under this Article 9 will be treated as adjustments to the Base Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any and all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it, except for claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.10.
