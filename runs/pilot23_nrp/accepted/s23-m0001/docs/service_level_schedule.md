# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026 by and between **Stepisk Furnishings Manufacturing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Taisund Event Rentals, LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is made under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement between the parties dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

This Schedule is the Service Level Schedule referred to in Sections 1.7 and 2.2 of the Agreement. It applies to Supplier's supply of Products under the Agreement and to the Services under the Statement of Work dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**SOW**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement or the SOW.

## 1. Purpose and Definitions

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule sets out the following matters for Supplier's performance under the Agreement and the SOW:

(a) the service levels;

(b) how those service levels are measured and reported; and

(c) the service credits that apply when Supplier fails to meet a service level.

1.2 **Definitions.** In this Schedule:

(a) "**Business Day**" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays observed in the United States.

(b) "**Damage Report**" means a report of damaged Products made by Distributor under Section 2.1.

(c) "**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month during the Term.

(d) "**Monthly Services Fees**" means the fees invoiced under Section 6 of the SOW for the relevant Measurement Period.

(e) "**Service Level**" means each performance standard set out in Sections 2 through 6.

(f) "**Service Credit**" means a credit calculated under Section 8.

## 2. Damaged Units

2.1 **Damage Reports.** Distributor shall report damaged Products under Section 9.3 of the Agreement and Section 5.5 of the SOW. Each report shall be sent by email to Supplier's operations lead and to service@stepiskfurnishings.com, or through any online portal Supplier makes available. Each Damage Report shall state:

(a) the Product codes and quantities affected;

(b) the Depot where the Products are located;

(c) a short description of the damage, with photographs where practicable; and

(d) whether the damage was found on delivery or in Distributor's rental fleet.

A Damage Report is made when Supplier receives it. If Supplier receives a Damage Report outside 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, it is treated as received at 6:00 a.m. on the next day.

2.2 **Replacement Time.** Supplier shall deliver a conforming replacement unit for each damaged unit identified in a Damage Report to the Depot stated in the Damage Report. The replacement shall be delivered within 48 hours after Distributor's damage report (the "**Replacement Time**").

2.3 **Source of Replacement Units.** Supplier may source replacement units from new stock or from Distributor's buffer inventory held at the Supplier Warehouse.

(a) If a replacement unit is drawn from buffer inventory, Supplier shall restore buffer inventory to the quantities in the current buffer plan within 10 Business Days.

(b) Replacement units for Products rejected under Section 7.3 of the Agreement, or covered by Section 8.1 of the Agreement, are provided at no charge, as provided in Section 6.2 of the SOW and Section 3.4 of the SOW.

2.4 **Collection of Damaged Units.** Supplier shall collect the damaged units on the delivery that brings the replacement units, or on the next scheduled run to that Depot, at Distributor's option.

2.5 **Measurement.** The Replacement Time Service Level is met for a Measurement Period if at least 98% of the damaged units reported in that period are replaced within the Replacement Time.

## 3. Delivery Service Levels

3.1 **On-Time Delivery of Purchase Orders.** Supplier shall deliver at least 97% of the Product units ordered under accepted Purchase Orders in each Measurement Period to the stated Depot on the delivery date in the accepted Purchase Order. A unit delivered late, short-shipped or rejected under Section 7.3 of the Agreement is not delivered on time.

3.2 **Scheduled Runs.** Supplier shall complete at least 98% of the scheduled delivery runs required by Section 5.1 of the SOW in each Measurement Period. Each run shall arrive within the receiving hours of the Depot in Section 2.1 of the SOW, within a two-hour window agreed with the Depot.

3.3 **Expedited Deliveries.** Supplier shall complete each expedited delivery requested under Section 5.2 of the SOW within the following times after Supplier receives the request:

| Depot | Expedited Delivery Time |
|---|---|
| D-PHL, D-CHL | 24 hours |
| D-ABE, D-NCS | 36 hours |

The Expedited Delivery Service Level is met for a Measurement Period if at least 95% of expedited deliveries in that period are completed within these times.

3.4 **Delivery Accuracy.** At least 99% of delivery notes in each Measurement Period shall correctly state the following, as confirmed on unloading:

(a) the Product codes;

(b) the quantities; and

(c) the condition status of the Products delivered.

## 4. Refurbishment Service Levels

4.1 **Collection.** Supplier shall collect Products tendered for refurbishment under Section 3.1 of the SOW on the next scheduled run to the relevant Depot. If that run is less than 24 hours after Supplier receives the refurbishment request, Supplier shall collect the Products on the following scheduled run.

4.2 **Turnaround.** Supplier shall redeliver refurbished Products to the Depot of collection within the following times after collection:

| Product Category | Product Codes | Turnaround Time |
|---|---|---|
| Chiavari chairs | SFM-CC-100, SFM-CC-110 | 7 Business Days |
| Banquet and cocktail tables | SFM-BT-60R, SFM-BT-72R, SFM-BT-8RC, SFM-CT-30, SFM-CT-36 | 7 Business Days |
| Lounge seating | SFM-LS-200, SFM-LS-210, SFM-LS-220 | 12 Business Days |

The Refurbishment Turnaround Service Level is met for a Measurement Period if at least 95% of units redelivered in that period are redelivered within these times.

4.3 **Beyond Economic Repair Notices.** Supplier shall give any notice required by Section 3.3 of the SOW within three Business Days after collection of the affected Product.

4.4 **Refurbishment Quality.** No more than 2% of refurbished units redelivered in a Measurement Period shall be returned by Distributor within 30 days for failure to meet the standard in Section 3.2 of the SOW.

## 5. Warehousing and Inventory Service Levels

5.1 **Inventory Reports.** Supplier shall deliver the weekly inventory report required by Section 4.5 of the SOW by 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the first Business Day of each week.

5.2 **Inventory Accuracy.** Each physical count under Section 4.5 of the SOW shall show inventory accuracy of at least 99.5% by Product code against the most recent inventory report.

5.3 **Buffer Availability.** Supplier shall maintain buffer inventory at the Supplier Warehouse at not less than 95% of the quantities in the current buffer plan, measured at the close of each Business Day. Units drawn under Section 2.3 and awaiting restoration within the 10-Business-Day period stated there are counted as available.

## 6. Response Times

6.1 **Operational Inquiries.** Supplier shall acknowledge each operational inquiry from Distributor's operations lead or Depot personnel within the times below and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve it promptly. An operational inquiry includes an inquiry about delivery status, inventory status or a refurbishment request.

| Priority | Description | Acknowledgment Time |
|---|---|---|
| Priority 1 | Missed or failed delivery affecting a Customer event within 72 hours | 1 hour |
| Priority 2 | Other delivery, replacement or refurbishment issue | 4 business hours |
| Priority 3 | General inquiry | 1 Business Day |

6.2 **Contact.** Supplier shall staff a service desk from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days a week, for Priority 1 inquiries.

## 7. Measurement and Reporting

7.1 **Performance Report.** Supplier shall measure its performance against each Service Level for each Measurement Period. It shall include the results in the performance report circulated under Section 7.2 of the SOW. The report shall include:

(a) actual performance against each Service Level;

(b) each Service Level failure, with root cause and corrective action; and

(c) the Service Credits due.

7.2 **Records.** Supplier shall keep accurate records supporting each performance report for at least 24 months. On reasonable notice, Supplier shall make those records available to Distributor for review.

7.3 **Disputes.** Distributor may dispute any measurement or Service Credit calculation by notice to Supplier's operations lead within 30 days after receiving the performance report. The operations leads shall attempt to resolve the dispute at the next monthly review. Unresolved disputes are handled under Section 18.3 of the Agreement.

## 8. Service Credits

8.1 **Credit Amounts.** If Supplier fails to meet a Service Level in a Measurement Period, Supplier shall credit Distributor the following percentage of the Monthly Services Fees for that period:

| Service Level | Section | Service Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Replacement Time | 2.5 | 5% |
| On-Time Delivery of Purchase Orders | 3.1 | 4% |
| Scheduled Runs | 3.2 | 2% |
| Expedited Deliveries | 3.3 | 2% |
| Refurbishment Turnaround | 4.2 | 3% |
| Refurbishment Quality | 4.4 | 2% |
| Inventory Reports and Accuracy | 5.1, 5.2 | 1% |
| Buffer Availability | 5.3 | 2% |

8.2 **Maximum Credits.** Total Service Credits for any Measurement Period shall not exceed 20% of the Monthly Services Fees for that period.

8.3 **Application.** Supplier shall apply Service Credits against the invoice for the Measurement Period following the period in which they accrue. On termination or expiry of the SOW, Supplier shall pay any unapplied Service Credits to Distributor within 30 days.

8.4 **Other Rights.** Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting reduced service value. They are without prejudice to Distributor's other rights and remedies under the Agreement and the SOW, including:

(a) rejection and replacement under Section 7.3 of the Agreement;

(b) warranty remedies under Section 8.3 of the Agreement; and

(c) termination under Section 15.3 of the Agreement.

## 9. Exclusions

9.1 **Excused Failures.** A failure to meet a Service Level is excused to the extent it is caused by any of the following:

(a) an event described in Section 20.2 of the Agreement, provided Supplier complies with that Section;

(b) Distributor's failure to give access to a Depot during receiving hours under Section 2.3 of the SOW;

(c) a Purchase Order or expedited request that exceeds 120% of the forecast quantity for the relevant month; or

(d) a Damage Report or refurbishment request that omits information required by this Schedule or the SOW, until the missing information is supplied.

9.2 **Notice of Excused Failure.** Supplier shall identify any excused failure, and the reason for it, in the relevant performance report.

## 10. Repeated Service Failures

10.1 **Corrective Action Plan.** If Supplier fails to meet the same Service Level in two consecutive Measurement Periods, Supplier shall deliver a written corrective action plan to Distributor within 10 Business Days. The plan shall identify the root cause, corrective steps and target dates. Supplier shall implement the plan as agreed by the operations leads.

10.2 **Termination Right.** Failures to meet a Service Level count toward Distributor's termination right under Section 15.3 of the Agreement. This applies whether or not a corrective action plan has been delivered or implemented. Excused failures under Section 9 do not count.

## 11. General

11.1 **Incorporation.** This Schedule is incorporated into and forms part of the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

11.2 **Review.** The operations leads shall review the Service Levels annually and may recommend changes. No change is binding unless made under Section 11.3.

11.3 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties, including a change order under Section 7.3 of the SOW.

11.4 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
