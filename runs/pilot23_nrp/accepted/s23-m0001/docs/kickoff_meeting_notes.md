## 2. Purpose of Meeting

2.1 This was the first operating meeting under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "Agreement"). It also covered the Statement of Work dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "SOW") and the Service Level Schedule dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "Schedule").

2.2 The goals were to confirm contacts, walk through day-to-day procedures and agree the first set of action items before the first Forecast and Purchase Orders are issued. No changes to the contract documents were proposed or agreed. Any future changes will go through the change request process in SOW Section 7.3.

## 3. Contacts and Governance

3.1 Operations leads were confirmed as named in SOW Section 7.1: Tobias Renwald for Stepisk and Marisol Quennet for Taisund.

3.2 The monthly review under SOW Section 7.2 will be held on the second Thursday of each month at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time, alternating between D-PHL and the Supplier Warehouse, with a video link. The first review is set for April 9, 2026.

3.3 Stepisk confirmed that it will circulate its performance report against the Schedule at least three business days before each monthly review, as required by SOW Section 7.2 and Schedule Section 7.1.

3.4 The Stepisk service desk for Priority 1 inquiries will be staffed from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days a week, per Schedule Section 6.2. Tobias Renwald will circulate the desk phone number with the contact sheet (see Section 9).

## 4. Depots and Delivery Runs

4.1 The four Depots in SOW Section 2.1 were confirmed: D-PHL, D-ABE, D-CHL and D-NCS. Receiving hours are as listed in the SOW table. Taisund has no current plans to add, close or relocate a Depot.

4.2 Scheduled runs under SOW Section 5.1 were confirmed: D-PHL and D-CHL three times per week, and D-ABE and D-NCS twice per week. Proposed run days were as follows:
- D-PHL and D-CHL: Monday, Wednesday, Friday
- D-ABE and D-NCS: Tuesday, Thursday

4.3 Each Depot manager will agree a two-hour arrival window with Annika Feldshaw for each run, within the Depot's receiving hours, as contemplated by Schedule Section 3.2.

4.4 Devin Hallorough walked the Stepisk team through the D-PHL dock and the designated staging area for unloading under SOW Section 5.4. Stepisk drivers will complete Taisund's site safety orientation before their first run (SOW Section 2.3).

4.5 Expedited delivery requests under SOW Section 5.2 will be sent by email to Tobias Renwald and Annika Feldshaw, with a call to the service desk where a Customer event is within 72 hours.

## 5. Forecasts and Purchase Orders

5.1 Calvin Medrano confirmed that Taisund's first rolling six-month Forecast, by Product and Depot, will be delivered on April 1, 2026, the first business day of the month, as required by Agreement Section 5.3.

5.2 Purchase Orders will be issued by email from Taisund's purchasing mailbox and will state Products, quantities, the delivery Depot and requested delivery dates (Agreement Section 5.4). Stepisk will confirm acceptance or rejection by return email within five business days.

5.3 Initial spring season demand is expected to be strongest for SFM-CC-100 chiavari chairs, SFM-BT-60R round banquet tables and SFM-CT-30 cocktail tables. Lounge seating (SFM-LS-200, SFM-LS-210 and SFM-LS-220) volumes are expected to pick up from May for corporate events.

## 6. Refurbishment

6.1 Lionel Barrish summarized the refurbishment standard in SOW Section 3.2 by Product category. He also described the shop's intake process at the Supplier Warehouse.

6.2 Refurbishment requests will use the form Stepisk will circulate (see Section 9). The form will capture Product codes, quantities, Depot of collection and the condition observed, as required by SOW Section 3.1.

6.3 The turnaround times in Schedule Section 4.2 were noted: 7 Business Days for chiavari chairs and for banquet and cocktail tables, and 12 Business Days for lounge seating.

6.4 Stepisk confirmed that beyond economic repair notices under SOW Section 3.3 will include photographs and a short damage description. They will be sent within three Business Days after collection (Schedule Section 4.3). Taisund will make its scrap or replace election within the five business days allowed by SOW Section 3.3.

6.5 Items returned for warranty conditions will be flagged on the refurbishment request so that they are handled under Agreement Section 8.3 without fees, per SOW Section 3.4.

## 7. Damaged Units

7.1 Damage Reports will be emailed to Tobias Renwald and to service@stepiskfurnishings.com. Each will contain the information listed in Schedule Section 2.1, including photographs where practicable.

7.2 The team confirmed that the replacement time is 48 hours after Distributor's damage report (Schedule Section 2.2). Reports received outside 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time are treated as received at 6:00 a.m. the next day.

7.3 Annika Feldshaw confirmed that replacements will normally be drawn from buffer inventory at the Supplier Warehouse, with buffer stock restored within 10 Business Days (Schedule Section 2.3(a)). Damaged units will be collected with the replacement delivery or on the next scheduled run, at Taisund's option (Schedule Section 2.4).

## 8. Warehousing and Inventory

8.1 Buffer inventory will be held at the Supplier Warehouse at 300 Commodore Distribution Way, Bridgeport, New Jersey 08014 (SOW Section 4.1).

8.2 The operations leads will agree the initial buffer plan by April 3, 2026, using the April 1 Forecast. The plan will be reviewed each calendar quarter against the Forecast (SOW Section 4.2).

8.3 Stepisk will issue its first weekly inventory report on Monday, April 6, 2026, by 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time (Schedule Section 5.1). The report will show Product code, condition status and location (SOW Section 4.5).

8.4 Taisund intends to conduct its first quarterly physical count at the Supplier Warehouse in late June 2026, on 48 hours' notice as provided in SOW Section 4.5.
