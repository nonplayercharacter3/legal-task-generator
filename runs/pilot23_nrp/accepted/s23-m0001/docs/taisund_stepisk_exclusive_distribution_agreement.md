# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Stepisk Furnishings Manufacturing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Kettering Mill Road, High Point, North Carolina 27265 ("**Supplier**"), and **Taisund Event Rentals, LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2250 Delancey Industrial Parkway, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19146 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## Recitals

A. Supplier designs and manufactures commercial event furniture and provides related refurbishment, warehousing and logistics services.

B. Distributor operates an event furniture rental and distribution business serving event venues, caterers, planners and corporate customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's exclusive distributor and rental operator for the Products in the Territory, on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Customer**" means any person to whom Distributor sells, rents or otherwise supplies Products.

1.3 "**Depot**" means each warehouse or staging facility operated by Distributor in the Territory and identified in the Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Forecast**" means the rolling forecast of Distributor's Product requirements delivered under Section 5.3.

1.5 "**Products**" means the commercial event furniture listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the parties in writing from time to time.

1.6 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.7 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule agreed by the parties under Section 2.2, setting out the service levels applicable to Supplier's performance.

1.8 "**Specifications**" means Supplier's published specifications for each Product in effect on the date of the applicable Purchase Order.

1.9 "**Statement of Work**" means the Statement of Work agreed by the parties under Section 2.2 under which Supplier refurbishes, warehouses and delivers Product inventory to the Depots.

1.10 "**Territory**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

## 2. Agreement Structure

2.1 **Contract Documents.** The relationship of the parties is governed by this Agreement, the Statement of Work and the Service Level Schedule (together, the "**Contract Documents**").

2.2 **Statement of Work and Service Level Schedule.** The parties shall enter into the Statement of Work and the Service Level Schedule under this Agreement. Each is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement upon signature by both parties.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the Contract Documents, the Contract Documents shall prevail in the following order: Statement of Work, then Service Level Schedule, then Distribution Agreement.

## 3. Appointment and Territory

3.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor and rental operator for the Products in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware (the "**Territory**"), and Distributor accepts the appointment.

3.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or through any Affiliate or third party, (a) sell, rent or otherwise supply Products to any person for use, resale or rental in the Territory other than through Distributor, or (b) appoint any other distributor, dealer or rental operator for the Products in the Territory.

3.3 **Customer Referrals.** Supplier shall refer to Distributor all inquiries it receives for Products for delivery or use in the Territory.

3.4 **Distributor Sales Outside the Territory.** Distributor may fulfill orders from Customers located in the Territory for events held outside the Territory, but shall not actively solicit Customers located outside the Territory.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

4.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 5. Supply, Forecasts and Orders

5.1 **Supply Obligation.** Supplier shall manufacture and supply Products to Distributor in the quantities set out in Purchase Orders accepted under Section 5.4.

5.2 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net price of not less than $1,200,000 in each contract year of the Term. If Distributor fails to meet the minimum in two consecutive contract years, Supplier's sole remedy is to convert the appointment in Section 3.1 to a non-exclusive appointment on 90 days' written notice.

5.3 **Forecasts.** On or before the first business day of each calendar month, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a non-binding rolling Forecast of its Product requirements for the following six months, by Product and Depot.

5.4 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall issue Purchase Orders specifying Products, quantities, the delivery Depot and requested delivery dates. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five business days of receipt. Supplier shall not reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with the most recent Forecast and does not exceed 120% of the forecast quantity for the relevant month. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

5.5 **Terms of Purchase Orders.** The terms of this Agreement apply to each Purchase Order. Any additional or different terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 6. Pricing and Payment

6.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit A. Supplier may adjust prices once per contract year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, on at least 90 days' written notice, by an amount not exceeding the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Furniture and Related Products published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics over the preceding 12 months.

6.2 **Most Favored Pricing.** Supplier shall not sell any Product to any other distributor or rental operator in the United States at a net price lower than the price then charged to Distributor for comparable volumes.

6.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon delivery of Products to the applicable Depot. Fees for services under the Statement of Work shall be invoiced as provided in the Statement of Work.

6.4 **Payment.** Undisputed amounts are due within 30 days of Distributor's receipt of a correct invoice. Distributor may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier of the dispute in writing before the due date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any disputed amount within 30 days.

6.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Distributor shall pay unless Distributor provides a valid resale or exemption certificate.

## 7. Delivery, Title and Risk

7.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the Depot stated in each Purchase Order on the delivery date stated in the accepted Purchase Order. Delivery, refurbishment and warehousing services relating to the Products are further described in the Statement of Work and are subject to the Service Level Schedule.

7.2 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Distributor upon delivery to the applicable Depot and completion of unloading.

7.3 **Inspection.** Distributor may inspect Products within 10 business days after delivery and reject any Products that are damaged or do not conform to the Specifications. Supplier shall replace rejected Products at its cost in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

## 8. Warranties

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that, for 36 months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship, and (c) when used in accordance with Supplier's published load ratings and instructions, be fit for commercial event rental use.

8.2 **Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products, as delivered, comply with all applicable laws, regulations and voluntary industry standards identified in the Specifications, including applicable ANSI/BIFMA standards for seating and tables.

8.3 **Remedy.** For any Product that breaches Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at Distributor's election, repair or replace the Product, or refund the price paid for it.

8.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Section 8, Supplier makes no other warranties, express or implied.

## 9. Distributor Obligations

9.1 **Rental Operations.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, rent and sell the Products in the Territory and shall maintain adequate Depot facilities, delivery vehicles and trained setup staff.

9.2 **Instructions.** Distributor shall provide Customers with Supplier's published load ratings, stacking limits and setup instructions for the Products.

9.3 **Damage Reporting.** Distributor shall report damaged Products to Supplier in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

9.4 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its distribution and rental of the Products.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **Indemnification by Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against any and all claims, demands, suits, losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the distribution, rental, setup or use of the Products by Distributor, any Customer or any other person, regardless of whether caused by a defect in the Products.

10.2 **Indemnification by Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against any and all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) alleging that any Product, as supplied by Supplier, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall (a) give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, provided that delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced, (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on the indemnified party without its prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The indemnified party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 **Supplier Insurance.** During the Term and for three years afterward, Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate.

11.2 **Distributor Insurance.** During the Term, Distributor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, and commercial automobile liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 combined single limit.

11.3 **Certificates.** Each party shall provide certificates of insurance to the other upon request.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

12.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.2, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable under the Agreement in the 12 months before the claim.

12.2 **Exclusions from Cap.** The limitation in Section 12.1 shall not apply to a party's obligations under Section 10, to a party's breach of Section 14, or to a party's fraud or willful misconduct.

12.3 **Consequential Damages.** Except for a party's obligations under Section 10 and a party's breach of Section 14, neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 13. Trademarks and Intellectual Property

13.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks and product names in the Territory solely to market, rent and sell the Products.

13.2 **Ownership.** Supplier retains all rights in its trademarks, designs and other intellectual property. Distributor retains all rights in its own trademarks, Customer lists and Customer data.

13.3 **Brand Standards.** Distributor shall use Supplier's trademarks in accordance with Supplier's reasonable brand guidelines provided in writing.

## 14. Confidentiality

14.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, Forecasts, Purchase Orders, Customer identities, Customer delivery schedules and locations, and business plans.

14.2 **Obligations.** The receiving party shall use the disclosing party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations and exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall not disclose it except to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 14, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.

14.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, (b) was known to the receiving party without restriction before disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information, or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the disclosing party prompt notice where lawful.

14.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue during the Term and for five years afterward.

## 15. Termination

15.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

15.2 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for its business or assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

15.3 **Repeated Service Failures.** Distributor may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if Supplier fails to meet the same service level in the Service Level Schedule in three or more months in any rolling 12-month period.

## 16. Effects of Termination

16.1 **Accrued Rights.** Termination or expiry does not affect rights or obligations accrued before the effective date of termination.

16.2 **Open Orders.** Supplier shall fulfill all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination, unless Distributor terminates under Section 15 and elects to cancel them.

16.3 **Sell-Off Period.** For 12 months after termination or expiry, Distributor may continue to rent and sell its existing inventory of Products and use Supplier's trademarks for that purpose in accordance with Section 13.

16.4 **Return of Confidential Information.** Upon termination or expiry, each party shall return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information, except as required to be retained by law or under Section 16.3.

16.5 **Survival.** Sections 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 19 survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 17. Assignment

17.1 **Assignment by Distributor.** Distributor shall not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 17.1 is void.

17.2 **Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any person, including by merger, consolidation, reorganization or sale of all or any part of its assets or business, without the consent of Distributor, upon written notice to Distributor.

17.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 18. Governing Law and Disputes

18.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

18.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

18.3 **Escalation.** Before commencing any action, other than for injunctive relief, a party shall give the other written notice of the dispute. Senior executives of each party shall meet within 15 business days after the notice to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

18.4 **Injunctive Relief.** Each party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent a breach of Section 13 or Section 14 without first complying with Section 18.3.

## 20. General

20.1 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, agency or employment relationship.

20.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Supplier's performance for more than 60 days, Distributor may procure substitute products from third parties during the period of non-performance without breaching this Agreement.

20.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.
