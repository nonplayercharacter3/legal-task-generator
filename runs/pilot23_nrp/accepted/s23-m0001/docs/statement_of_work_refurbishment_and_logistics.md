# STATEMENT OF WORK: REFURBISHMENT, WAREHOUSING AND LOGISTICS SERVICES

This Statement of Work (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Stepisk Furnishings Manufacturing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Taisund Event Rentals, LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is made under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement between the parties dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

This SOW is the Statement of Work referred to in Sections 1.9 and 2.2 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Scope and Purpose

1.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the refurbishment, warehousing and delivery services that Supplier will perform for Distributor's Product inventory (the "**Services**"). It also sets out the Depots, the fees for the Services and the related operating procedures.

1.2 **Products Covered.** The Services apply to all Products listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement. This includes Products purchased by Distributor under the Agreement and Products in Distributor's rental fleet that are returned to Supplier for refurbishment.

1.3 **Service Levels.** Supplier shall perform the Services in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. Service credits, response times and replacement times for damaged units are as stated in the Service Level Schedule.

2.3 **Depot Access.** Distributor shall give Supplier's drivers and personnel reasonable access to each Depot during receiving hours. Supplier's personnel shall comply with Distributor's site safety rules while on Depot premises.

## 3. Refurbishment Services

3.1 **Intake.** Distributor may tender Products from its rental fleet to Supplier for refurbishment by issuing a refurbishment request identifying the Product codes, quantities, Depot of collection and the condition observed. Supplier shall collect tendered Products from the identified Depot on the next scheduled delivery run, or as stated in the Service Level Schedule.

3.2 **Refurbishment Standard.** Supplier shall restore each refurbished Product to a condition that conforms to the Specifications in all respects affecting safety, structural integrity and load rating, and that is cosmetically suitable for commercial event rental use. Refurbishment includes, as applicable:

(a) for chiavari chairs (SFM-CC-100 and SFM-CC-110), inspection of joints, spindles and feet, re-gluing or replacement of loose components, touch-up or refinishing, and replacement of seat pads;

(b) for banquet and cocktail tables (SFM-BT-60R, SFM-BT-72R, SFM-BT-8RC, SFM-CT-30 and SFM-CT-36), inspection and replacement of folding mechanisms, locking hinges, leg glides and damaged tops, and frame straightening or replacement;

(c) for lounge seating (SFM-LS-200, SFM-LS-210 and SFM-LS-220), cleaning, re-upholstery of damaged panels, frame inspection and replacement of legs and glides.

3.3 **Beyond Economic Repair.** If Supplier determines that a Product cannot be refurbished to the standard in Section 3.2 for a cost below 60% of its then-current net price under Exhibit A to the Agreement, Supplier shall notify Distributor with photographs and a short description of the damage. Distributor may then elect, within five business days, to (a) have the Product scrapped, or (b) have it replaced at the net price in Exhibit A to the Agreement under a Purchase Order. If Distributor does not elect within that period, Supplier shall hold the Product for a further 30 days and then may scrap it.

3.4 **Warranty Items.** Products returned for a condition covered by the product warranty in Section 8.1 of the Agreement are repaired or replaced under Section 8.3 of the Agreement. Supplier shall not charge refurbishment, collection or delivery fees for them.

3.5 **Refurbishment Warranty.** Supplier warrants that its refurbishment work will be free from defects in workmanship for 12 months after redelivery of the refurbished Product to a Depot.

## 4. Warehousing Services

4.1 **Facility.** Supplier shall store Distributor's buffer inventory of Products at Supplier's regional warehouse at 300 Commodore Distribution Way, Bridgeport, New Jersey 08014 (the "**Supplier Warehouse**"), or at another facility within the Territory approved in writing by Distributor.

4.2 **Buffer Inventory.** Supplier shall hold buffer inventory at the Supplier Warehouse in the quantities stated in the most recent buffer plan agreed by the parties' operations leads. The buffer plan shall be reviewed each calendar quarter against the Forecast.

4.3 **Storage Standards.** Supplier shall store Products indoors in a clean, dry, climate-controlled environment, on racking or pallets appropriate to each Product type, and in accordance with Supplier's stacking limits. Upholstered lounge seating shall be wrapped and stored off the floor.

4.4 **Title and Risk in Storage.** Products held at the Supplier Warehouse that have been delivered to and accepted by Distributor and then returned to Supplier for storage or refurbishment are held by Supplier as bailee. Supplier bears risk of loss of those Products while they are in its possession, custody or control, including in transit between a Depot and the Supplier Warehouse.

4.5 **Inventory Reporting.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with a weekly inventory report by Product code, condition status (available, in refurbishment, awaiting decision under Section 3.3) and location. Supplier shall permit Distributor to conduct a physical count of its inventory at the Supplier Warehouse on 48 hours' notice, not more than once per calendar quarter except to investigate a discrepancy.

## 5. Delivery Services

5.1 **Scheduled Runs.** Supplier shall operate scheduled delivery runs from the Supplier Warehouse to each Depot as follows: D-PHL and D-CHL, three times per week; D-ABE and D-NCS, twice per week. Scheduled runs carry new Products under accepted Purchase Orders, refurbished Products and buffer replenishment, and collect Products tendered for refurbishment.

5.2 **Expedited Deliveries.** Distributor may request an expedited delivery outside the scheduled runs. Supplier shall perform expedited deliveries within the times stated in the Service Level Schedule.

5.3 **Delivery Documentation.** Each delivery shall be accompanied by a delivery note listing Product codes, quantities, Purchase Order or refurbishment request numbers and condition status. Distributor's signature on a delivery note confirms receipt of the stated quantities only. It does not waive Distributor's inspection and rejection rights under Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

5.4 **Unloading.** Supplier's drivers shall unload Products at the Depot dock and place them in the staging area designated by Distributor. Title and risk of loss for new Products pass as provided in Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Damage Reports.** Distributor shall report damaged Products found on delivery or in its fleet in accordance with Section 9.3 of the Agreement and the Service Level Schedule. Supplier shall replace damaged units within the replacement time stated in Section 2.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

| Scheduled delivery run – D-ABE or D-NCS | per run | $360.00 |

6.2 **Exclusions.** No fee is payable for (a) warranty items under Section 3.4, (b) replacement of Products rejected under Section 7.3 of the Agreement, or (c) any delivery run or expedited delivery made to correct a Supplier error or shortage.

6.3 **Fee Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the fees in Section 6.1 once per contract year, at the same time and subject to the same notice period and index limit as price adjustments under Section 6.1 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Services monthly in arrears, itemized by Service, Depot and refurbishment request or Purchase Order number. Invoices are payable, and may be disputed, in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

## 7. Governance

7.1 **Operations Leads.** Each party shall appoint an operations lead for this SOW. The initial operations leads are Tobias Renwald for Supplier and Marisol Quennet for Distributor. Either party may replace its operations lead on written notice.

7.2 **Monthly Review.** The operations leads shall meet at least monthly to review service level performance, inventory reports, refurbishment backlog and the buffer plan. Supplier shall circulate a performance report against the Service Level Schedule at least three business days before each meeting.

7.3 **Change Requests.** Either party may propose changes to the Services by written change request. No change is binding unless set out in a written change order signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for the Term of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier under this Section 8.

8.2 **Termination.** This SOW terminates automatically on termination or expiry of the Agreement. Distributor may also terminate this SOW for Supplier's material breach of it on the same terms as Section 15.1 of the Agreement.

8.3 **Transition.** On termination or expiry, Supplier shall, at Distributor's direction, complete refurbishment work in progress and deliver all of Distributor's inventory held at the Supplier Warehouse to the Depots designated by Distributor within 30 days. Fees for the transition period are payable at the rates in Section 6.1.
