## 1. Purpose of this Notice

1.1 This letter is written notice under Sections 17.2 and 19.1 of the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 between Stepisk Furnishings Manufacturing, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Taisund Event Rentals, LLC ("**Distributor**") (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 Supplier will assign the Agreement to Orlenne Event Group LLC ("**Orlenne**"). The assignment will include the Statement of Work dated as of March 16, 2026 and the Service Level Schedule dated as of March 16, 2026, each of which forms part of the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

## 2. The Assignee

2.1 Orlenne is a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 655 Brandywine Gateway Boulevard, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406.

2.2 Orlenne operates an event furniture rental business. It serves venues, caterers, planners and corporate customers in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware. As part of a broader commercial arrangement, Orlenne will take over Supplier's regional distribution and logistics relationships in the Mid-Atlantic, including the Agreement.

## 3. Scope and Effective Date of the Assignment

3.1 Supplier will assign all of its rights and obligations under the Agreement, the Statement of Work and the Service Level Schedule to Orlenne. The assignment will take effect on December 1, 2026 (the "**Assignment Date**").

3.2 From the Assignment Date, references to "Supplier" in the Contract Documents will be references to Orlenne. Orlenne will be entitled to all of Supplier's rights under the Contract Documents, including the right to receive Forecasts, Purchase Orders, inventory reports and payments. Orlenne will assume Supplier's obligations accruing from and after the Assignment Date.

3.3 Supplier will continue to manufacture the Products. From the Assignment Date, Supplier will supply them to Orlenne for fulfillment under the Agreement.

## 4. Transfer of Records and Information

4.1 On or before the Assignment Date, Supplier will deliver to Orlenne the records it holds under the Contract Documents so that Orlenne can perform without interruption. These records include:

(a) current pricing under Exhibit A to the Agreement and the fees under Section 6.1 of the Statement of Work;

(b) Distributor's Forecasts and open Purchase Orders;

(c) the current buffer plan and weekly inventory reports for the Supplier Warehouse;

(d) Depot schedules, delivery notes and Customer delivery schedules and locations provided in connection with expedited and scheduled deliveries; and

(e) Service Level performance reports and supporting records kept under Section 7.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

4.2 Confidential Information transferred to Orlenne will remain subject to Section 14 of the Agreement. Orlenne will be bound by that Section as Supplier's successor.

## 5. Operations Before and After the Assignment Date

5.1 Until the Assignment Date, Supplier will continue to perform under the Contract Documents in the ordinary course. Distributor should continue to submit Forecasts, Purchase Orders, refurbishment requests and Damage Reports to Supplier as it does now.

5.2 Supplier intends that scheduled delivery runs, refurbishment turnaround and damaged unit replacement continue without interruption through the transition. Supplier will coordinate with Orlenne on the handover of the Supplier Warehouse and delivery operations.

5.3 Invoices issued by Supplier for Products delivered and Services performed before the Assignment Date remain payable to Supplier under Section 6.4 of the Agreement. Invoices for periods after the Assignment Date will be issued by Orlenne. Payment instructions will follow under separate cover.
