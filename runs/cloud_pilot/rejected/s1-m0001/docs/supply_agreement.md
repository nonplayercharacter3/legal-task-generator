# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2027 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Rochester, New York 14624 ("**Customer**"), and **Brightwater Polymer Works LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Riverside Commerce Drive, Akron, Ohio 44311 ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells elastomeric and polymer sealing components for industrial fluid-power applications.

B. Customer manufactures hydraulic systems and wishes to buy certain sealing components from Supplier, and Supplier wishes to sell them to Customer, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 14.1.

1.3 "**Defect**" means any failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications or to the warranties in Section 9.1, including any defect in material or workmanship.

1.4 "**Delivery Point**" means the Customer facility or other location stated in the applicable Purchase Order.

1.5 "**Exhibits**" means Exhibit A (Products and Specifications) and Exhibit B (Pricing), each attached to and forming part of this Agreement.

1.6 "**Products**" means: Hydraulic seals, gaskets and replacement parts listed in Exhibit A, as Exhibit A may be updated from time to time by written agreement of the Parties.

1.7 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Customer to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.8 "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications, drawings, material requirements and performance criteria for each Product set out or referenced in Exhibit A, together with any written changes approved under Section 8.

1.9 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. FORECASTS AND ORDERS

3.1 **Forecasts.** Customer shall give Supplier, by the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar quarter, a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product purchases for the following twelve (12) months. Forecasts are for planning purposes only and do not obligate Customer to buy any quantity of Products.

3.2 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that state the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and Delivery Point. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days of receipt. Supplier may not reject a Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to the most recent forecast. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

3.3 **Lead Times.** Supplier shall deliver Products within the lead times set out in Exhibit A.

3.4 **Purchase Order Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other business form of either Party are of no force or effect, except for the commercial details described in Section 3.2.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for Products set out in Exhibit B. Prices are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term. After that, Supplier may propose a price adjustment no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, by written notice given at least ninety (90) days in advance. No adjustment may exceed the percentage increase in the Producer Price Index for Plastics Material and Resins Manufacturing published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics over the preceding twelve (12) months, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment Terms.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of each order. Each invoice must reference the applicable Purchase Order number. Payment terms: Undisputed invoices due within 45 days of receipt. Customer shall pay by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days. Withholding a disputed amount under this Section 4.3 is not a breach of this Agreement.

4.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Supplier shall separately state on its invoices and Customer shall pay, unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

4.5 **Set-Off.** Customer may set off against any amount owed to Supplier any amount Supplier owes to Customer under this Agreement.

## 5. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

5.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Delivery Point, packaged and labeled in accordance with the Specifications and Customer's reasonable written packaging instructions.

5.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly if it expects any delivery to be late. If Supplier fails to deliver by the confirmed delivery date for reasons other than a Force Majeure Event or Customer's act or omission, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, ship by expedited freight at Supplier's cost, or Customer may cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability.

5.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer upon delivery at the Delivery Point.

## 6. EXCLUSIVITY

6.1 **Exclusive Purchasing.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase, and shall cause each of its Affiliates to purchase, all of its requirements for sealing products, wherever used worldwide, exclusively from Supplier.

6.2 **Supply Commitment.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient manufacturing capacity, raw materials and inventory to meet Customer's forecast requirements for Products and to fill all Purchase Orders accepted or deemed accepted under Section 3.2.

6.3 **Supply Failure.** If Supplier is unable to fill any accepted Purchase Order in full and on time, Customer may buy the affected quantities from a third party for the duration of the shortfall. Those purchases do not breach Section 6.1, and Supplier shall reimburse Customer for any amount by which the third-party price exceeds the price in Exhibit B.

## 7. QUALITY AND INSPECTION

7.1 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 (or a successor standard) throughout the Term and shall provide a certificate of conformance with each shipment.

7.2 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within thirty (30) days after delivery and may reject any Product that has a Defect. Customer's inspection, acceptance or payment does not waive any warranty or other right under this Agreement.

7.3 **Rejected Products.** Supplier shall, at Customer's election, replace or credit any rejected Product within ten (10) business days of notice of rejection, and shall bear all costs of return shipment.

7.4 **Audits.** On reasonable advance notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period (or at any time following a material quality failure), Customer or its designee may audit Supplier's facilities, processes and records relating to the Products.

## 8. CHANGES

8.1 **Specification Changes.** Supplier shall not change the Specifications, materials, manufacturing process, manufacturing site or sub-suppliers for any Product without Customer's prior written approval.

8.2 **Customer Changes.** Customer may request changes to the Specifications by written notice. Supplier shall respond within fifteen (15) business days with any resulting effect on price or lead time. No change takes effect until both Parties agree to it in writing.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for a period of 18 months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be free from defects in material and workmanship; (c) be merchantable and fit for the purposes described in the Specifications; and (d) be free of all liens and encumbrances.

9.2 **Remedies.** If any Product breaches Section 9.1, Supplier shall, at Customer's election and at Supplier's cost, promptly repair or replace the Product or refund its purchase price. Supplier shall also reimburse Customer for its reasonable costs of removal, reinstallation, shipping and handling associated with the nonconforming Product.

9.3 **Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that it will manufacture and supply the Products in compliance with all applicable laws, including laws on product safety, hazardous substances, export control and labor.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 10. INSURANCE

10.1 During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, Supplier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; and (b) product recall insurance of at least $2,000,000. Supplier shall name Customer as an additional insured on its general liability policy and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising out of (a) any Defect in or failure of a Product, including claims for bodily injury, death or property damage; (b) any allegation that a Product, or its manufacture, sale or use, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property right of a third party, except to the extent the claim results from Supplier's compliance with a design furnished by Customer; or (c) Supplier's breach of Section 9.3.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising out of (a) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct; or (b) any allegation that a design furnished by Customer infringes the intellectual property rights of a third party.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim (although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced), shall allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Subject to Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, however caused and on any theory of liability.

12.2 **Liability Cap.** Subject to Section 12.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the amounts paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to the following liability cap carve-outs: Indemnification obligations under Section 11 and breach of confidentiality under Section 14.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for its business or assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, fill any Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination on the terms of this Agreement. Each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 14.4.

13.4 **Last-Time Buy.** Upon any expiration or termination other than by Supplier under Section 13.1, Customer may place a final Purchase Order for up to twelve (12) months of its forecast requirements of Products, which Supplier shall fill on the terms of this Agreement.

13.5 **Survival.** Sections 4, 9, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 14, 15, 17, 18 and 19, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement, whether before or after the Effective Date and in any form, including Specifications, drawings, designs, forecasts, pricing, and business and technical information.

14.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was rightfully known to the Recipient without restriction before receipt from the Discloser; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

14.3 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14. The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective treatment.

14.4 **Return or Destruction.** Upon the Discloser's written request or upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backup systems or as required by law, which remain subject to this Section 14.

14.5 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue during the Term and for five (5) years afterward, and, for trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

14.6 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Section 14 may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

## 15. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

15.1 **Customer Materials.** All Specifications, drawings, designs and tooling furnished or paid for by Customer ("**Customer Materials**") are and remain Customer's property. Supplier shall use Customer Materials solely to manufacture Products for Customer and shall not use them to make products for any third party.

15.2 **Supplier IP.** Except as stated in Section 15.1, each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license to the other Party's intellectual property except as needed to perform this Agreement.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, flood, fire, war, terrorism, epidemic, government action or widespread utility failure (each a "**Force Majeure Event**"). The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a Force Majeure Event prevents Supplier's performance for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may buy affected Products from third parties for the duration of the event without breaching Section 6.1, and if it continues for more than ninety (90) days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

## 17. NOTICES

17.1 All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out in the preamble (or to any other address a Party designates by notice). Notices to Customer must be marked "Attention: General Counsel," and notices to Supplier "Attention: President." Notices are effective upon receipt.

## 18. ASSIGNMENT

18.1 **By Customer.** Customer may not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 18.1 is void.

18.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the consent of Customer, (a) to any Affiliate of Supplier; or (b) to any successor to Supplier's business by merger, consolidation, change of control, or sale of all or substantially all of Supplier's assets or of the assets of the business unit to which this Agreement relates. Supplier shall give Customer written notice of the assignment within thirty (30) days after it takes effect.

18.3 **Binding Effect.** Subject to Sections 18.1 and 18.2, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

18.4 **Subcontracting.** Supplier shall not subcontract the manufacture of any Product without Customer's prior written consent and remains responsible for the performance of any permitted subcontractor.

## 19. GENERAL

19.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

19.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

19.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

19.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the documents that make up this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: Written amendments, then this Agreement, then Exhibits, then Purchase Orders.

19.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship between them.

19.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.
