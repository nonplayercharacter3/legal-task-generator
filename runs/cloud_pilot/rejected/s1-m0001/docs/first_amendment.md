# FIRST AMENDMENT TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This First Amendment to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of March 1, 2027 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Rochester, New York 14624 ("**Customer**"), and **Brightwater Polymer Works LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Riverside Commerce Drive, Akron, Ohio 44311 ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2027 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier supplies certain sealing components to Customer.

B. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement to clarify certain definitions, conform the limitation of liability provisions and update certain administrative provisions, in each case on the terms of this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 References in the Agreement to "this Agreement" mean the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 1 (DEFINITIONS)

2.1 Section 1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Section 1.10 immediately after Section 1.9:

> 1.10 "**Products**" means, for all purposes of this Agreement, the hydraulic seals and gaskets listed in Exhibit A, comprising finished goods only and excluding components and replacement parts, as Exhibit A may be updated from time to time by written agreement of the Parties.

2.2 Section 1.5 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "References to Exhibit A or Exhibit B include any updated version of that Exhibit signed by both Parties."

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12 (LIMITATION OF LIABILITY)

3.1 Section 12.3 is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> 12.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to (a) liability for breach of Section 14 (Confidentiality); or (b) liability that cannot be limited or excluded under applicable law.

3.2 Sections 12.1 and 12.2 of the Agreement remain unchanged, and each continues to be "subject to Section 12.3" as amended by this Amendment.

## 4. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 10 (INSURANCE)

4.1 Section 10.1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "Supplier shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date and within thirty (30) days after each policy renewal, and shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage."

## 5. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 17 (NOTICES)

5.1 Section 17.1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "Notices sent by email must be sent, in the case of Customer, to legalnotices@halvorsenfluid.com and, in the case of Supplier, to contracts@brightwaterpolymer.com, or to any other email address a Party designates by notice."

## 6. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

6.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

6.2 This Amendment is a written amendment signed by authorized representatives of both Parties for purposes of Section 19.3 of the Agreement and forms part of the Agreement.

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Governing Law.** This Amendment and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

7.2 **Jurisdiction.** Section 19.2 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full.

7.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Amendment to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Amendment Effective Date.
