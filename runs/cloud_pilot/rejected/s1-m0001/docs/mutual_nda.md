# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of October 5, 2026 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Industrial Parkway, Rochester, New York 14624 ("**Halvorsen**"), and **Brightwater Polymer Works LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Riverside Commerce Drive, Akron, Ohio 44311 ("**Brightwater**"). Halvorsen and Brightwater are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. The Parties wish to evaluate a potential commercial relationship under which Brightwater would supply hydraulic seals, gaskets and related sealing components to Halvorsen (the "**Purpose**").

B. In connection with the Purpose, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public business and technical information, and the Parties wish to protect that information on the terms of this NDA.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this NDA, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and whether disclosed orally, visually, in writing, electronically or by inspection of samples or facilities. Confidential Information includes specifications, drawings, designs, material formulations, manufacturing processes, test data, forecasts, volumes, pricing, cost information, customer and supplier information, and other business and technical information. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, professional advisers and prospective financing sources.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show by competent evidence:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient or its Representatives;

(b) was rightfully known to the Recipient without restriction before receipt from the Discloser;

(c) is rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

3.1 **Use.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

3.2 **Standard of Care.** The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.3 **Permitted Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

3.4 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Recipient shall not analyze, reverse engineer, decompile or determine the composition of any samples, prototypes or other tangible materials provided by the Discloser, except as the Discloser expressly authorizes in writing.

3.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser promptly in writing on becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information and shall reasonably cooperate with the Discloser to limit the consequences.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena or court order, provided that the Recipient (where legally permitted) gives the Discloser prompt written notice of the requirement, reasonably cooperates with the Discloser's efforts to obtain a protective order or other confidential treatment, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

5.1 Upon the Discloser's written request, or upon either Party's written notice that it has decided not to proceed with the Purpose, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession and, on request, certify the destruction in writing by an officer. The Recipient may retain copies held in routine electronic backup systems or as required by law or by its bona fide document retention policy, which copies remain subject to this NDA.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY

6.1 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this NDA grants the Recipient any license or other right in the Discloser's Confidential Information, patents, trademarks or other intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 **No Warranty.** CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS." NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AS TO THE ACCURACY OR COMPLETENESS OF ITS CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

7.1 This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any further agreement. Each Party may end discussions at any time for any reason. Any supply or other commercial relationship between the Parties will arise only under a separate written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 8. TERM AND DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

8.1 **Term.** This NDA begins on the NDA Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless either Party terminates it earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other.

8.2 **Survival.** The Recipient's obligations under this NDA with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during the term of this NDA continue for five (5) years after the expiration or termination of this NDA, and, for trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this NDA may cause the Discloser irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance, without the requirement to post a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 10. NOTICES

10.1 All notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out in the preamble (or to any other address a Party designates by notice). Notices to Halvorsen must be marked "Attention: General Counsel," and notices to Brightwater "Attention: President." Notices are effective upon receipt.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

11.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this NDA relates, provided the successor agrees in writing to be bound by this NDA and is not a competitor of the other Party. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 11.3 is void.

11.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
