# TERM SHEET

## Proposed Supply Arrangement – Hydraulic Seals and Related Parts

**Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc. / Brightwater Polymer Works LLC**

**Date:** January 15, 2027

**Status:** Non-binding summary of principal terms. For discussion purposes only.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which **Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Brightwater Polymer Works LLC** ("**Supplier**") propose to enter into a definitive supply agreement (the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under the Supply Agreement, Supplier would supply, and Customer would buy, certain sealing components for Customer's hydraulic systems.

1.2 Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 2. Parties

| Role | Entity | Jurisdiction | Address |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | Halvorsen Fluid Systems, Inc. | Delaware corporation | 4100 Industrial Parkway, Rochester, New York 14624 |
| Supplier | Brightwater Polymer Works LLC | Ohio limited liability company | 220 Riverside Commerce Drive, Akron, Ohio 44311 |

## 3. Products

3.1 The Products are hydraulic seals, gaskets and replacement parts listed in Exhibit A to the Supply Agreement. The initial list is set out in the Schedule to this term sheet.

3.2 The Parties may add products to Exhibit A only by written agreement.

## 4. Term

4.1 **Target Effective Date:** March 1, 2027.

4.2 **Initial Term:** 36 months from the Effective Date.

4.3 **Renewal:** The agreement would renew automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either Party gives at least 90 days' notice of non-renewal.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 **Agreed Scope:** Industrial hydraulic seals only, for Customer's facilities in the United States.

5.2 Gaskets, replacement parts and any other sealing goods are not exclusive. Customer may continue to source them from other suppliers. Purchases by Customer's facilities outside the United States, and purchases by Customer's Affiliates, are not exclusive.

5.3 The exclusivity would not apply during any period in which Supplier cannot fill accepted orders in full and on time, or is affected by a force majeure event.

5.4 Supplier would commit to maintain enough capacity and inventory to meet Customer's rolling forecasts.

## 6. Forecasts and Orders

6.1 Customer would provide a non-binding 12-month rolling forecast each quarter.

6.2 Customer would order through Purchase Orders. Supplier would accept or reject each Purchase Order within 5 business days. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period would be deemed accepted. Supplier could not reject a conforming Purchase Order that is within forecast.

6.3 Lead times would be as stated in Exhibit A to the Supply Agreement.

## 7. Pricing and Payment

7.1 **Prices:** As set out in the Schedule. Prices would be fixed for the first 12 months.

7.2 **Price Adjustments:** After the first 12 months, Supplier could propose no more than one adjustment per 12-month period, on 90 days' notice. Any adjustment would be capped at the PPI change for Plastics Material and Resins Manufacturing.

7.3 **Payment Terms:** Undisputed invoices due within 45 days of receipt.

7.4 **Disputes and Set-Off:** Customer could withhold amounts it disputes in good faith and could set off amounts Supplier owes to it.

## 8. Delivery

8.1 Delivery would be DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Customer facility named in each Purchase Order.

8.2 Title and risk of loss would pass to Customer on delivery.

## 9. Quality and Warranty

9.1 Supplier would maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification and provide a certificate of conformance with each shipment.

9.2 Customer would have a 30-day inspection and rejection right and an annual audit right.

9.3 **Warranty Period:** 18 months after delivery. The warranty would cover all Products, including replacement parts.

9.4 **Warranty Remedies:** At Customer's election, Supplier would repair, replace or refund. Supplier would also reimburse Customer's removal, reinstallation and shipping costs.

9.5 Supplier would make no change to specifications, materials, processes, manufacturing sites or sub-suppliers without Customer's prior written approval.

## 10. Insurance

10.1 Supplier would maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate.

10.2 Supplier would maintain product recall insurance of $2,000,000.

10.3 Customer would be named as an additional insured.

## 11. Indemnification

11.1 **By Supplier:** Supplier would indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from product defects (including bodily injury, death and property damage), intellectual property infringement (other than infringement caused by Customer-furnished designs), and Supplier's non-compliance with law.

11.2 **By Customer:** Customer would indemnify Supplier against third-party claims arising from Customer's negligence or willful misconduct, and from infringement claims based on Customer-furnished designs.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

12.1 **Exclusion:** Each Party would exclude indirect, consequential and similar damages, and lost profits.

12.2 **Liability Cap:** Amounts paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.3 **Carve-outs:** Indemnification obligations under Section 11 and breach of confidentiality under Section 14. Both the damages exclusion and the cap would be subject to these carve-outs.

## 13. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

13.1 Confidentiality terms consistent with the Parties' existing mutual non-disclosure agreement would be included in the Supply Agreement. Those obligations would survive for 5 years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

13.2 Customer specifications, drawings and tooling would remain Customer's property. Supplier would use them only for Customer.

## 14. Assignment

14.1 Neither Party could assign the Supply Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent.

14.2 Any assignment to a competitor of the other Party would require that Party's consent in all cases.

14.3 Supplier could not subcontract manufacture without Customer's consent.

## 15. Termination

15.1 Either Party could terminate for an uncured material breach, after a 30-day cure period.

15.2 Either Party could terminate on the other Party's insolvency.

15.3 Customer would have a last-time-buy right covering up to 12 months of forecast requirements.

## 16. Governing Law

16.1 **Governing Law:** State of New York.

16.2 **Forum:** Exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in Manhattan.

## 17. Non-Binding Effect

17.1 This term sheet is not a binding agreement. It does not create any obligation to negotiate or to enter into the Supply Agreement.

17.2 No Party will be bound unless and until a definitive Supply Agreement has been executed and delivered by both Parties.

17.3 Section 17 and the Parties' existing mutual non-disclosure agreement are the only binding provisions relating to this term sheet.
