# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or this "**Master Supply Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tessaline Polymers LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), and **Harrowgate Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and sells food-grade flexible packaging film and related converted products.

B. Customer produces packaged food products and wishes to buy packaging film from Supplier for use in its operations.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will supply, and Customer will purchase, such products. These terms are supplemented by one or more Order Forms and by the Quality Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** The following terms have the following meanings in this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**Artwork**" means all graphics, text, logos, trademarks, nutritional and ingredient statements, allergen declarations, regulatory statements and other printed matter that Customer provides or approves for printing on Products.

(c) "**Change of Control**" means, for any Party: (i) the acquisition by any person or group, directly or indirectly, of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of that Party; (ii) any merger, consolidation, share exchange or similar transaction involving that Party, whether or not that Party is the surviving entity, after which the holders of that Party's voting equity immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the surviving or resulting entity or its ultimate parent; or (iii) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of that Party.

(d) "**Customer Materials**" means Artwork, Customer's trademarks, and any specifications, designs or other materials Customer provides to Supplier for use in making Products.

(e) "**Filled Product**" means any food or other product that Customer or its designee packages in, fills into or seals with Products.

(f) "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from a Third-Party Claim.

(g) "**Order Form**" means Order Form No. 1 dated as of the Effective Date between the Parties, and any later order form signed by both Parties that references this Agreement. Each Order Form sets out, among other things, the term, pricing and volume commitments for the Products covered by it.

(h) "**Products**" means the packaging film products described in the applicable Order Form and the Specifications.

(i) "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Customer under this Agreement and an Order Form.

(j) "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date, which sets out the Specifications and the inspection, acceptance, rejection and warranty-claim procedures for Products, as amended from time to time.

(k) "**Specifications**" means the technical, material, performance and food-contact specifications for each Product set out in or incorporated into the Quality Agreement.

(l) "**Third-Party Claim**" means any claim, suit, action or proceeding brought by a person other than a Party or its Affiliates.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. References to "days" mean calendar days unless "business days" is specified.

1.3 **Order of Precedence.** The Order Form and the Quality Agreement are incorporated into this Agreement by reference. If the terms of these documents conflict, they shall govern in the following order of precedence: Order Form, then Master Supply Agreement, then Quality Agreement. The pre-printed terms of any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or similar document shall have no effect, even if accepted or signed.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term, and any Renewal Term, stated in the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

2.2 **Survival of Orders.** If this Agreement expires or is terminated, any Purchase Order accepted before the effective date of expiration or termination shall remain governed by this Agreement until fulfilled, unless the Purchase Order is cancelled under Section 14.

## 3. FORECASTS AND ORDERS

3.1 **Forecasts.** On or before the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar month, Customer shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements for the following six (6) months.

3.2 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that state the Product, quantity, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within three (3) business days after receipt. Supplier may not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that matches the most recent forecast and the lead times in the Order Form. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

3.3 **Changes.** Customer may change the quantity or delivery date of an accepted Purchase Order by written notice at least ten (10) business days before the scheduled ship date. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate shorter-notice changes.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay for Products at the prices set out in the applicable Order Form. Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and include standard packaging for shipment. Prices may be adjusted only as provided in the Order Form.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of each order. Customer shall pay each invoice within 45 days after receipt of undisputed invoice. Payment shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Supplier in writing of the disputed amount and the reasons before the payment due date. Customer shall timely pay any undisputed portion. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.

4.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, excise, value-added and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay unless it provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income, property and employees.

## 5. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

5.1 **Delivery.** Unless the Order Form states otherwise, Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier Customer designates, on the delivery date stated in the accepted Purchase Order.

5.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly if it expects any delay in delivery. If Supplier fails to deliver within five (5) business days after the scheduled date for reasons other than Customer's acts or a Force Majeure Event, Customer may (a) cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability, or (b) require Supplier to ship by expedited means at Supplier's cost.

5.3 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery to the carrier in accordance with Section 5.1.

5.4 **Packaging and Labeling for Shipment.** Supplier shall package, mark and palletize Products in accordance with the Specifications and good industry practice for food-contact materials, so as to prevent contamination and damage in transit.

## 6. QUALITY

6.1 **Quality Agreement.** Supplier shall manufacture, test, store and ship Products in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Inspection, acceptance, rejection of nonconforming lots, and warranty-claim procedures are governed by the Quality Agreement.

6.2 **Regulatory Compliance.** Supplier shall ensure that Products, and the materials used to make them, comply with all laws applicable to food-contact packaging in the United States, including 21 C.F.R. Parts 174 through 186 and applicable FDA food-contact notifications.

6.3 **Change Control.** Supplier shall not change any raw material, resin or additive supplier, manufacturing site or material process for a Product without at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to Customer and Customer's written approval, as further provided in the Quality Agreement.

6.4 **Audits.** On reasonable prior notice and during normal business hours, Customer or its designee may audit Supplier's facilities, records and quality systems relating to Products, no more than once per calendar year except for cause.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product, upon delivery and for the warranty period stated in the Quality Agreement, will (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship, (c) be manufactured in compliance with applicable law and current good manufacturing practices, and (d) be suitable for direct food contact as described in the Specifications.

7.2 **Title.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products, free of all liens and encumbrances.

7.3 **Customer Warranties.** Customer warrants that (a) it has the right to provide the Customer Materials to Supplier for use under this Agreement, and (b) the Artwork, as provided or approved by Customer, will comply with applicable labeling laws.

7.4 **Remedies.** Customer's remedies for breach of Section 7.1 are set out in the Quality Agreement, together with any remedies available under Section 10.1.

7.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT OR THE QUALITY AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. CUSTOMER MATERIALS AND ARTWORK

8.1 **Supply of Artwork.** Customer shall provide Artwork in the format specified by Supplier and shall review and approve each proof in writing before Supplier begins production.

8.2 **Accuracy.** Supplier shall reproduce approved Artwork faithfully within the print tolerances stated in the Specifications. Supplier is not responsible for the content of approved Artwork, including any errors in ingredient, allergen or nutritional statements.

8.3 **Tooling.** Printing plates, cylinders and other tooling made specifically for Customer's Artwork and paid for by Customer are Customer's property. Supplier shall hold them in good condition and return them to Customer on request.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 **Ownership.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its own intellectual property. Customer owns all rights in the Customer Materials. Supplier owns all rights in its film formulations, processes and know-how.

9.2 **License to Supplier.** Customer grants Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Customer Materials solely to manufacture and supply Products to Customer.

9.3 **No Other Use.** Supplier shall not sell or supply to any third party any product bearing Customer's Artwork or trademarks.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of any Third-Party Claim to the extent arising from (a) any defect in a Product or failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications or applicable law, (b) any claim that a Product, excluding Customer Materials, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party, or (c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of any Third-Party Claim to the extent arising from (a) any claim that the Artwork or other Customer Materials infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party, (b) the content of any labeling or Artwork, including ingredient, allergen, nutritional and regulatory statements, (c) the contents of any Filled Product, including any product liability, personal injury, illness or recall claim relating to a Filled Product, except to the extent caused by a Product's failure to conform to the Specifications, or (d) the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel.

10.3 **Procedure.** The Party seeking indemnification shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the Third-Party Claim, though a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced, (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, but the indemnifying Party may not settle any claim that admits fault or imposes non-monetary obligations on the indemnified Party without its written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense.

10.4 **Infringement Remedies.** If a Product becomes, or in Supplier's reasonable opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim covered by Section 10.1(b), Supplier may at its option and expense (a) procure the right for Customer to continue using the Product, or (b) modify or replace the Product so that it is non-infringing while still conforming to the Specifications.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 11.3 AND SECTION 11.4, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS OR LOST REVENUE, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, HOWEVER CAUSED AND UNDER ANY THEORY OF LIABILITY, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

11.2 **Cap on Liability.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 11.3 AND SECTION 11.4, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE fees paid or payable in the prior 12 months BY CUSTOMER TO SUPPLIER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT, MEASURED FROM THE DATE OF THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's liability for fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct, (b) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality), or (c) Customer's obligation to pay amounts due for Products under Section 4.

11.4 **Customer Indemnity.** Notwithstanding Section 11.2 or Section 11.1, Customer's obligations under Section 10.2 shall not be subject to any limitation of liability or exclusion of damages set out in this Agreement.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, each Party shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; (b) product recall insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

12.2 **Certificates.** Each Party shall provide certificates of insurance to the other on request and shall give at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including pricing, Specifications, formulations and forecasts ("**Confidential Information**"). The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and financing sources who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13.

13.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information excludes information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information, or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

13.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order, if it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

13.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 13 continue during the Term and for five (5) years afterward, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

14.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for its business, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination: (a) Customer shall pay for all Products delivered before the effective date and for any finished Products and Customer-specific raw materials held by Supplier for Customer within the forecast and lead-time limits in the Order Form; (b) Supplier shall return all tooling and Customer Materials; and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

14.4 **Survival.** Sections 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14.3, 14.4, 15 and 17, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

15.1 **Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the consent of Customer, to any person, and may delegate any of its obligations under this Agreement to any Affiliate or subcontractor. Supplier shall notify Customer in writing within thirty (30) days after any such assignment.

15.2 **Assignment by Customer.** Customer may not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier.

15.3 **Change of Control of Customer.** Any Change of Control of Customer, including by merger in which Customer is the surviving entity, shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer requiring the prior written consent of Supplier under Section 15.2. If a Change of Control of Customer occurs without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may, at any time within one hundred eighty (180) days after it learns of the Change of Control, terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice to Customer. In that event, the unpaid balance of the minimum annual purchase commitment under the Order Form for the remainder of the then-current term shall become immediately due and payable by Customer.

15.4 **Void Transfers; Binding Effect.** Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 15 is void. Subject to this Section 15, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
