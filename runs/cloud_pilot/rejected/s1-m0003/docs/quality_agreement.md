# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tessaline Polymers LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), and **Harrowgate Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). It is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in the Master Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Master Supply Agreement**").

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the Specifications and the quality responsibilities of the Parties for the Products. It covers manufacturing controls, inspection, acceptance, rejection of nonconforming lots, warranty-claim procedures, change control, complaints and recalls.

1.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products supplied under the Master Supply Agreement and any Order Form. As of the Effective Date, the Products are Part Nos. TP-LF-2140, TP-BF-3325, TP-PR-1875 and TP-SW-1200, as listed in Section 4.1 of Order Form No. 1.

1.3 **Relationship to Master Supply Agreement.** This Quality Agreement is incorporated into the Master Supply Agreement by reference under Section 1.3 of the Master Supply Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Master Supply Agreement.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Certificate of Analysis**" or "**CoA**" means a document issued by Supplier for each Lot that states the test results for the Lot against each release parameter in the applicable Product Specification Sheet.

(b) "**Deviation**" means any departure from the Specifications, approved processes or this Quality Agreement during manufacture, testing, storage or shipment of a Product.

(c) "**Latent Defect**" means a nonconformity that could not reasonably be detected by the incoming inspection described in Section 6.1.

(d) "**Lot**" means a quantity of a single Part No. made in one continuous production run from the same raw material lots and identified by a unique lot number.

(e) "**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product that does not conform to the Specifications or to the warranties in Section 7.1 of the Master Supply Agreement.

(f) "**Product Specification Sheet**" means the specification sheet for a Part No. described in Section 3.1, as approved in writing by both Parties.

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS

3.1 **Product Specification Sheets.** The Specifications for each Product consist of the Product Specification Sheet for that Part No., attached as Schedule A, together with the general requirements in Section 3.2. Each Product Specification Sheet states at least the following: resin and layer structure; nominal gauge and gauge tolerance; width and roll dimensions; core size; tensile strength; elongation; seal strength and seal initiation temperature; oxygen and moisture vapor transmission rates, where applicable; coefficient of friction; haze and gloss; and, for TP-PR-1875, color standards and print registration tolerances.

3.2 **General Requirements.** All Products shall:

(a) be manufactured only from resins, additives, inks, adhesives and coatings that comply with 21 C.F.R. Parts 174 through 186 or are the subject of an effective FDA food-contact notification for the intended conditions of use;

(b) be free of foreign matter, gels and carbon specks above the limits in the Product Specification Sheet, pinholes, delamination and odor or taint transfer;

(c) for TP-PR-1875, meet a residual solvent limit of not more than 10 mg/m² total retained solvents; and

(d) be wound, wrapped and palletized as required by Section 5.4 of the Master Supply Agreement and the Product Specification Sheet.

3.3 **Changes to Specifications.** A Product Specification Sheet may be changed only by a written revision signed by the quality representatives of both Parties named in Section 10. Each revision shall bear a new revision number and effective date.

## 4. MANUFACTURING AND QUALITY SYSTEM

4.1 **Quality Management System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to a Global Food Safety Initiative-benchmarked standard for packaging manufacturers, such as BRCGS Packaging Materials or FSSC 22000, and shall keep that certification current throughout the Term. Supplier shall notify Customer within five (5) business days if its certification is suspended, withdrawn or downgraded.

4.2 **Good Manufacturing Practices.** Supplier shall manufacture, test, store and ship Products in accordance with current good manufacturing practices for food-contact packaging, including documented hygiene, pest control, glass and brittle plastics, foreign-matter and allergen-segregation programs.

4.3 **Release Testing.** Supplier shall test each Lot against the release parameters in the applicable Product Specification Sheet before shipment. Supplier shall not ship any Lot that fails a release parameter, unless Customer has approved a written deviation under Section 4.5.

4.4 **Certificates of Analysis.** Supplier shall provide a CoA for each Lot by email no later than the time of shipment. Each roll and pallet shall be labeled with the Part No., Lot number, date of manufacture and roll number.

4.5 **Deviations.** Supplier shall record and investigate every Deviation. Supplier shall not ship any Product affected by a Deviation that may affect conformity to the Specifications unless Customer's quality representative has approved the Deviation in writing before shipment. Customer's approval of a Deviation applies only to the Lots identified in the approval.

## 5. WARRANTY MECHANICS

5.1 **Warranty Period.** The warranty period for the purposes of Section 7.1 of the Master Supply Agreement is 18 months after delivery of the applicable Lot. The warranty period applies to Products stored by Customer in accordance with the storage conditions stated in the Product Specification Sheet.

5.2 **Warranty Claims.** Customer may make a warranty claim for any Nonconforming Product, including a Latent Defect, discovered at any time during the warranty period. Customer shall submit each claim in writing to Supplier's quality representative within fifteen (15) business days after discovering the nonconformity. The claim shall identify the Part No., Lot number and nature of the nonconformity and, where practicable, include photographs or samples.

5.3 **Investigation.** Supplier shall acknowledge each warranty claim within two (2) business days and shall deliver a written root-cause analysis and corrective and preventive action plan within twenty (20) business days after receipt of the claim. Customer shall make retained samples available to Supplier on request.

5.4 **Remedies.** For any Nonconforming Product, Supplier shall, at Customer's election: (a) replace the Nonconforming Product at Supplier's cost within ten (10) business days after Customer's election; or (b) issue a credit or refund of the price paid for the Nonconforming Product. In either case Supplier shall bear the cost of return freight and disposal of the Nonconforming Product. These remedies are in addition to any remedies available under Section 10.1 of the Master Supply Agreement, as provided in Section 7.4 of the Master Supply Agreement.

## 6. INSPECTION, ACCEPTANCE AND REJECTION

6.1 **Incoming Inspection.** Customer may inspect each Lot upon receipt. Incoming inspection may include review of the CoA, visual inspection of rolls and packaging, and sample testing of the parameters in the Product Specification Sheet.

6.2 **Rejection Window.** Customer may reject any Lot that is nonconforming by written notice to Supplier within 10 business days after delivery. The notice shall state the reasons for rejection. A Lot not rejected within that period is deemed accepted for purposes of incoming inspection only. Acceptance does not affect Customer's rights under Section 5 for Latent Defects or for any nonconformity discovered during the warranty period.

6.3 **Disputed Rejections.** If Supplier disputes a rejection, the Parties' quality representatives shall confer within five (5) business days. If they cannot agree, the Parties shall jointly submit retained samples to an independent laboratory accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 that is acceptable to both Parties. The laboratory's results are binding on the Parties as to conformity. The Party whose position is not supported by the results shall pay the laboratory's fees.

6.4 **Handling of Rejected Lots.** Customer shall quarantine rejected Lots and hold them for Supplier's inspection for up to twenty (20) business days after the rejection notice. Supplier shall then arrange for collection or authorize disposal at its cost. Supplier shall replace rejected Lots in accordance with Section 5.4.

## 7. CHANGE CONTROL

7.1 **Notified Changes.** In accordance with Section 6.3 of the Master Supply Agreement, Supplier shall give Customer at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of, and obtain Customer's written approval for, any change to: (a) any raw material, resin, additive, ink, adhesive or coating, or the supplier of any of them; (b) the manufacturing site or production line; or (c) any material process step or test method.

7.2 **Qualification.** Each change notice shall include a description of the change, the reason for it, an updated food-contact compliance statement and proposed validation data. On request, Supplier shall supply qualification samples from at least three (3) production Lots made under the changed conditions. Customer shall respond to a complete change notice within sixty (60) days and shall not unreasonably withhold approval.

## 8. COMPLAINTS, NOTIFICATIONS AND RECALLS

8.1 **Notification of Quality Events.** Each Party shall notify the other within twenty-four (24) hours after becoming aware of any information suggesting that a Product shipped to Customer may present a food-safety risk, including contamination, migration above regulatory limits or a material Deviation.

8.2 **Recalls.** Customer has sole authority to decide whether to conduct a recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery of any Filled Product. Supplier shall cooperate fully with Customer in any recall, including by providing Lot traceability data, retained samples and test results within twenty-four (24) hours after Customer's request. Responsibility for the costs of a recall is determined under the Master Supply Agreement.

8.3 **Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain traceability from each Lot to the raw material lots used in it, and shall be able to complete a full trace within four (4) hours. Supplier shall conduct a mock recall at least once each calendar year and share the results with Customer.

## 9. AUDITS AND RECORDS

9.1 **Audits.** Customer's audit rights are set out in Section 6.4 of the Master Supply Agreement. For any audit, Supplier shall make available its quality manual, batch records, CoAs, Deviation and complaint records, calibration records and third-party audit reports. Supplier shall respond in writing to audit findings within thirty (30) days with a corrective action plan.

9.2 **Records Retention.** Supplier shall retain all batch records, CoAs, test data and Deviation records for each Lot for at least three (3) years after the end of the warranty period for that Lot.

9.3 **Retained Samples.** Supplier shall retain a representative sample of each Lot, stored under the conditions in the Product Specification Sheet, until the end of the warranty period for that Lot.

## 10. QUALITY REPRESENTATIVES

10.1 **Designation.** Each Party designates the following quality representative. A Party may change its representative by written notice to the other Party.

10.2 **Quality Reviews.** The quality representatives shall meet at least twice each calendar year to review quality metrics, complaints, Deviations, open corrective actions and planned changes.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement remains in effect for as long as the Master Supply Agreement is in effect and afterward until the warranty period for the last Lot delivered has expired.

11.2 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties, except that Product Specification Sheets may be revised as provided in Section 3.3.

11.3 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
