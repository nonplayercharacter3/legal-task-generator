# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Under the Master Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026**

This Order Form No. 1 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tessaline Polymers LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), and **Harrowgate Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Master Supply Agreement between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Master Supply Agreement**").

## 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Incorporation.** This Order Form is an "Order Form" as defined in Section 1.1(g) of the Master Supply Agreement. It is incorporated into the Master Supply Agreement by reference in accordance with Section 1.3 of the Master Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Master Supply Agreement. In this Order Form:

(a) "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period during the Term. The first Contract Year begins on the Effective Date.

(b) "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

(c) "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

(d) "**Resin Index**" means the monthly average contract price for low-density polyethylene film-grade resin, as published by the resin price reporting service the Parties designate in writing, or a substantially equivalent successor index the Parties agree on in writing.

(e) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of the Master Supply Agreement and this Order Form begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14 or Section 15.3 of the Master Supply Agreement.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, the Master Supply Agreement and this Order Form shall automatically renew for successive five (5) year Renewal Terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). They shall continue to renew at the end of each Renewal Term unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal. A notice of non-renewal is effective only if it is delivered in accordance with Section 16 of the Master Supply Agreement no earlier than sixty (60) days and no later than thirty (30) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. A notice delivered outside that period is of no effect, and the Master Supply Agreement and this Order Form shall renew as provided in this Section 2.2. Except as the Parties otherwise agree in writing, all terms of this Order Form, including the minimum annual purchase commitment in Section 3.1 and the pricing mechanism in Section 4, shall apply during each Renewal Term.

## 3. VOLUME COMMITMENTS

3.1 **Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** During each Contract Year, Customer shall purchase Products from Supplier with an aggregate invoiced value, net of taxes, freight and credits for rejected Products, of at least $2,400,000 (the "**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**").

3.2 **Credit Toward Commitment.** The following count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which they would have shipped: (a) Products ordered under an accepted Purchase Order that Supplier fails to deliver for any reason other than Customer's breach; (b) Products cancelled by Customer under Section 5.2 or Section 17.3 of the Master Supply Agreement; and (c) Products rejected as nonconforming under the Quality Agreement and not replaced by Supplier within the time required by the Quality Agreement.

3.3 **Shortfall.** Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a statement showing the invoiced value of Products purchased during that Contract Year, as adjusted under Section 3.2. If the adjusted amount is less than the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment, Supplier may invoice Customer for twenty percent (20%) of the difference (the "**Shortfall Payment**"). The Shortfall Payment is payable in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Master Supply Agreement. The Parties agree that the Shortfall Payment is a reasonable estimate of Supplier's lost margin and is not a penalty.

3.4 **Capacity Reservation.** Supplier shall reserve production capacity sufficient to supply Customer's forecast requirements up to one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment in each Contract Year, prorated by month. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to supply requirements above that level.

## 4. PRODUCTS AND PRICING

4.1 **Products and Prices.** The Products covered by this Order Form and their initial prices are set out below. All Products shall conform to the Specifications in the Quality Agreement.

| Part No. | Product Description | Gauge | Unit | Price per Unit (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP-LF-2140 | Clear LDPE/LLDPE coextruded lidding film, heat-seal coated | 2.0 mil | 1,000 sq. ft. | $38.50 |
| TP-BF-3325 | High-barrier EVOH multilayer forming film, thermoform grade | 3.25 mil | 1,000 sq. ft. | $71.20 |
| TP-PR-1875 | Reverse-printed PET/PE laminate rollstock, up to 8 colors | 1.875 mil | 1,000 sq. ft. | $54.75 |
| TP-SW-1200 | Anti-fog polyolefin shrink overwrap film | 1.2 mil | 1,000 sq. ft. | $22.90 |

4.2 **Plate and Cylinder Charges.** Supplier shall charge Customer for new printing plates and cylinders for TP-PR-1875 at Supplier's documented cost plus ten percent (10%), quoted in writing and approved by Customer before ordering. Tooling paid for by Customer is Customer's property under Section 8.3 of the Master Supply Agreement.

4.3 **Price Adjustment.** Prices are fixed for the first Contract Year. After that, either Party may request a price adjustment once per calendar quarter, by written notice with supporting data, if the Resin Index average for the preceding calendar quarter has moved by more than five percent (5%) up or down from the Resin Index average for the quarter on which current prices were based. The adjustment shall equal the percentage change in the Resin Index multiplied by the resin share of the Product price, which the Parties agree is fifty-five percent (55%) for each Product. Adjusted prices apply to Purchase Orders issued thirty (30) days or more after the notice. No other price increase is permitted without Customer's written agreement.

4.4 **Most Favored Pricing.** If, during the Term, Supplier sells a substantially similar product to another customer in comparable volumes and on comparable terms at a lower unit price, Supplier shall promptly offer that lower price to Customer for the corresponding Product.

## 5. ORDERING, LEAD TIMES AND DELIVERY

5.1 **Lead Times.** Supplier's standard lead times from acceptance of a Purchase Order to shipment are: (a) fifteen (15) business days for TP-LF-2140 and TP-SW-1200; (b) twenty (20) business days for TP-BF-3325; and (c) twenty (20) business days for TP-PR-1875 using existing approved Artwork, or thirty (30) business days for new Artwork after proof approval.

5.2 **Minimum Order Quantities.** The minimum quantity per Purchase Order line is 250 units (1,000 sq. ft. each) per Part No., or 400 units for any new print run of TP-PR-1875.

5.3 **Forecast Liability.** Customer is responsible under Section 14.3(a) of the Master Supply Agreement only for (a) finished Products made to fill accepted Purchase Orders, and (b) Customer-specific raw materials, including printed inventory and custom-formulated resin, purchased by Supplier to meet the first two (2) months of Customer's most recent forecast, up to a maximum value equal to two (2) months of average monthly purchases over the preceding six (6) months.

5.4 **Delivery.** Products shall be delivered FCA Supplier's facility at 4410 Brandywine Commerce Drive, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 (Incoterms 2020), or another Supplier facility approved by Customer under Section 6.3 of the Master Supply Agreement, to the carrier Customer designates.

5.5 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall maintain at its cost a safety stock of finished TP-LF-2140 and TP-BF-3325 equal to at least two (2) weeks of Customer's average forecast requirements for each. Supplier shall rotate safety stock on a first-in, first-out basis so that no Product is shipped more than six (6) months after its date of manufacture.

## 6. ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

6.1 **Contacts.** Each Party shall designate an account manager responsible for the day-to-day relationship under this Order Form. The account managers shall meet, in person or by videoconference, at least once each calendar quarter to review forecasts, delivery performance, quality metrics and pricing.

6.2 **Performance Reporting.** Supplier shall provide Customer a monthly report showing on-time-in-full delivery performance, open Purchase Orders, safety stock levels and any quality events reported under the Quality Agreement.

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Effect.** Except as expressly provided in this Order Form, the Master Supply Agreement remains in full force and effect and governs the purchase and sale of the Products listed in Section 4.1.

7.2 **Amendment.** This Order Form may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties, in accordance with Section 17.5 of the Master Supply Agreement.

7.3 **Counterparts.** This Order Form may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
