## II. ISSUES

### Issue 1: Renewal notice is untimely and Halvorsen may have no renewal (CRITICAL)

- **Where:** DA §2.2 (renewal "at least 90 days before Initial Term expiry"; if not timely, the Agreement "will expire at the end of the Initial Term"); DA §2.1 (Initial Term ends Dec. 31, 2026); DA §17.1 (courier notice effective one Business Day after dispatch); Letter §§1, 3.
- **Analysis:** Ninety days before Dec. 31, 2026 is **Oct. 2, 2026**. The Letter is dated Oct. 15 and is effective Oct. 16 at the earliest, assuming it was dispatched that day. The Letter is otherwise correctly formed. It goes to the §17.1 address, to the General Counsel, by courier, and it correctly says the email copy is not notice (§17.2). The only defect is timing. Section 2.2 states the consequence expressly, and §19.1 requires any waiver to be in a signed writing. The Letter does not reserve rights or argue timeliness.
- **Why it matters:** If the notice is ineffective, Halvorsen loses exclusivity (DA §3.1, "during the Term"), the trademark license (DA §10.1, "during the Term"), and the three-year Renewal Term. That also undermines the value Corvane is paying for (Issue 9).
- **Remedies are limited:** DA §13.1 bars lost-profit and consequential damages "arising out of" the Agreement, with exceptions only for Sections 11 and 12. If Brightwater refuses to honor the renewal, a damages claim looks weak. Realistic relief is declaratory or equitable, in New York County (DA §18.2; jury waived, §18.3). Whether a New York court would excuse a 14-day-late option exercise, or find waiver or estoppel, needs research. I have not done that research and would not rely on it.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Do not argue or concede lateness in writing until we settle strategy.
  2. Get the courier waybill and dispatch and delivery records now, since the exact dispatch date matters.
  3. Ask the client about all Brightwater communications since Sept. 1, including any oral assurances or conduct suggesting Brightwater would treat the renewal as valid.
  4. Seek a **signed written amendment or waiver under DA §19.1** in which Brightwater accepts the Oct. 15 notice as timely, or grants a fresh renewal on the DA terms. Package this with the change-of-control consent (Issue 3) and the confirmations in Issue 5.
  5. Prepare a fallback plan (Issue 2).

### Issue 2: If the DA expires on Dec. 31, 2026, Halvorsen's protections are narrow

- **Where:** DA §§3.1, 10.1, 16.2, 16.3, 16.4, 8.4, 9; Am. §5.5.
- **Analysis:** After expiry, Halvorsen keeps:
  - A right to require Brightwater to repurchase all new, unused, resalable Products at net landed cost, if Halvorsen elects it within 60 days (DA §16.2). This is available on expiration, but not if Brightwater terminated under §15.1, 15.2 or 15.3.
  - A 180-day sell-off and service period for existing Customers (DA §16.3).
  - Surviving warranty, indemnity and liability terms (DA §16.4).
- **What it loses:** Exclusivity, since Brightwater may appoint another distributor or sell direct on Jan. 1, 2027. It also loses the right to receive new supply, and the 2027 earn-out base (LOI §2.3(b)).
- **Recommendation:** Prepare an inventory count and a §16.2 repurchase election now. Hold the election in reserve for use right after Dec. 31. Note that §16.2 covers "Products" as defined in DA §1.1, meaning the Exhibit A models. It may not cover spare parts and accessories, which the Amendment's broader definition reaches only for Am. §§3–5. Coordinate with Tidemark (Issue 11).

### Issue 3: The Corvane deal is a deemed assignment requiring Brightwater's prior written consent (CRITICAL)

- **Where:** DA §14.2; DA §14.3; DA §15.3; DA §16.2; LOI §§1.1, 1.2, 4.1, 6; LOI Sched. 1.
- **Analysis:** Corvane will buy 100% of Halvorsen's membership interests. DA §14.2 deems any transfer of more than 50% of the equity, or of control, to be an assignment requiring Brightwater's **prior written consent**. The request must go in writing at least 30 days before the proposed transfer.
  - **No reasonableness standard.** Unlike the Tidemark agreement (WSA §16.1), §14.2 has no "not unreasonably withheld" language. Brightwater can withhold consent and extract concessions.
  - **Consequence of closing without consent.** The transfer is void under §14.3, and Brightwater may terminate immediately under §15.3. A §15.3 termination also cancels the §16.2 inventory repurchase.
  - **LOI misdescribes the position.** LOI §1.2 says contracts "remain with the Company," which is true of the entity but irrelevant to §14.2. LOI §6 lists no third-party consent condition.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Send the consent request by **Oct. 29**, or get a signed written waiver of the 30-day period.
  2. Before sending, get Corvane's written consent to disclose the deal, because LOI §10 and the Aug. 12, 2026 Confidentiality Agreement may restrict disclosure (Issue 4).
  3. Ask for a consent that expressly covers the Corvane transaction and Mr. Halvorsen's new employment role.
  4. In the Definitive Agreement, make Brightwater's written consent and a valid renewal an express closing condition, and resist any covenant that makes Sellers guarantee them.
  5. Do not close until consent is in hand.

### Issue 4: Sharing the DA with Corvane in diligence may breach DA §11

- **Where:** DA §§11.1, 11.2; LOI §§5.1–5.3, 10; compare WSA §11.1.
- **Analysis:** LOI §5.2 gives Corvane access to the Distribution Agreement. DA §11.1 treats "pricing, customer lists and product specifications" as Brightwater Confidential Information, and Exhibit B is pricing. DA §11.2 permits disclosure only to "employees, advisors and financing sources." A prospective acquirer is not on that list. The WSA by contrast expressly permits disclosure to "actual or prospective investors or acquirers" (WSA §11.1).
- **Why it matters:** An unauthorized disclosure is a breach that Brightwater could use as a termination or leverage point at the very moment Halvorsen needs its cooperation.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Pause sharing Exhibit B and other Brightwater-sourced data until Brightwater consents.
  2. Fold a confidentiality consent into the Issue 3 request.
  3. Meanwhile use redacted copies or a counsel-only data room.
  4. Check what Corvane has already received.
  5. Use LOI §5.3, a binding provision that bars Corvane from contacting suppliers without Halvorsen's consent. It means Halvorsen controls the Brightwater approach.

### Issue 5: Renewal binds Halvorsen to the $2.4M Annual Minimum for three more years; prior-year compliance is unknown

- **Where:** Am. §§1.2, 1.3, 2.1, 3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.2, 5.1–5.3, 5.5; DA §§2.3, 5.2.
- **Analysis:**
  - **Renewal terms.** The Renewal Term is "on the terms of this Agreement then in effect" (DA §2.3). That includes the Amendment, so renewal carries about $7.2M of minimum commitments over 2027–2029. Prices may rise up to 4% a year under DA §5.2, while the Amendment's minimum is a fixed dollar figure.
  - **2025 minimum.** The Amendment is dated March 1, 2025, but "Contract Year" starts Jan. 1, 2025 (Am. §1.2). Proration under §3.2 applies only to a Contract Year shorter than 12 months, so a full $2.4M minimum arguably applied for 2025.
  - **Shortfall consequences.** A Shortfall that is not cured within 90 days of final determination allows conversion to non-exclusive (Am. §5.2). A Shortfall over 25% of the minimum (more than $600,000, so Net Purchases below $1.8M), or uncured Shortfalls in two consecutive years, allows termination without further cure (Am. §5.3). Cure purchases do not count toward the minimum for the year in which they are made (Am. §5.1).
  - **Exclusive remedy.** Am. §5.5 makes these Brightwater's exclusive remedies, which helps Halvorsen.
  - **Counting.** Net Purchases count goods "shipped" during the year (Am. §1.3). They exclude freight, taxes, and amounts credited under DA §§6.3 and 9.2. They are reduced where Brightwater rejected or late-delivered a conforming order (Am. §3.3). There is no carryover (Am. §3.4).
  - **Survival gap.** DA §16.4 does not list the Amendment's reconciliation provisions as surviving expiry. That is a point for Halvorsen if the DA expires.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Get Halvorsen's 2025 and 2026 year-to-date Net Purchases, and any reconciliation, shortfall, or cure correspondence.
  2. If 2026 is tracking short, evaluate placing orders that ship by Dec. 31. Weigh this against inventory build-up, storage cost, and repurchase exposure.
  3. As part of the renewal and consent package, request an **estoppel confirming** no Shortfall or default for 2025 or 2026. Also consider renegotiating the minimum for the Renewal Term, since renewal otherwise locks it in.
  4. Check whether Brightwater delivered the 2025 reconciliation due Feb. 14, 2026 (Am. §4.2).

### Issue 6: The Amendment removes the inventory repurchase right if Brightwater terminates for a Shortfall

- **Where:** Am. §§5.3, 5.4; DA §§16.1, 16.2.
- **Analysis:** A termination for Shortfall is deemed a §15.1 termination "for purposes of Sections 16.1 and 16.2." DA §16.2 excludes terminations by Supplier under §15.1 from the repurchase right. Brightwater could therefore terminate for a Shortfall and Halvorsen would be left with unsold inventory. Section 16.1 would still require Brightwater to fill accepted orders, because only the non-payment form of §15.1 is carved out there.
- **Recommendation:** In any renewal amendment, negotiate to restore repurchase rights on a Shortfall termination. In the meantime avoid building inventory to chase the minimum without understanding this exposure.

### Issue 11: Tidemark liability cap is low relative to likely inventory value

- **Where:** WSA §§9.1, 9.2, 9.3, 10.1(b), 10.2, Ex. B (valuation charge).
- **Analysis:** Tidemark's liability for Goods is capped at documented landed cost, up to **$250,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 per year** (WSA §9.2). Its warehouse legal liability coverage is $1M per occurrence (WSA §10.1(b)). Halvorsen buys at least $2.4M a year from Brightwater, and unit prices run $9,850–$17,900 (DA Ex. B). A fire or flood could exceed the cap many times over. Halvorsen must insure Goods at full value and waive subrogation against Tidemark except up to Tidemark's liability (WSA §10.2). A claim notice is due within 60 days of discovery and no later than nine months after release or loss (WSA §9.4).
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Get the inventory value at Secaucus and Halvorsen's property policy.
  2. Confirm the policy covers goods at a third-party warehouse with no gap.
  3. Consider raising the limits for specified Goods by paying the valuation charge, which is $0.10 per $100 of declared value per month (WSA §9.2, Ex. B).
  4. Calendar the claim deadlines.

### Issue 12: Tidemark term and exit mechanics do not line up with the Brightwater timeline

- **Where:** WSA §§2.1, 2.2, 3.2, 13.3, 14.1; Ex. B; DA §§16.2, 16.3.
- **Analysis:**
  - **Commitment.** The WSA runs to May 31, 2028 and auto-renews yearly unless either party gives 90 days' notice of non-renewal. For the Initial Term that notice falls on or about **March 2, 2028**.
  - **Cost.** Halvorsen reserves at least 12,000 sq ft at $1.15 per sq ft per month, which is $13,800 a month (WSA §3.2, Ex. B).
  - **Exit.** After the first anniversary (passed, June 1, 2026), Halvorsen may terminate for convenience on 120 days' written notice, paying Dedicated Space charges through the effective date (WSA §13.3). That is roughly $55,000 for four months.
  - **Timing conflict.** On any termination Halvorsen must remove all Goods within 30 days (WSA §14.1). That conflicts with the 180-day DA wind-down (DA §16.3, to June 29, 2027) and with the 60-day repurchase window (DA §16.2, to Mar. 1, 2027).
- **Recommendation:**
  1. Do not give a §13.3 notice yet.
  2. If renewal fails, give notice shortly after Dec. 31 so the effective date (about early May 2027) and the 30-day removal period fit within the wind-down.
  3. Coordinate any Brightwater repurchase logistics, since delivery under DA §6.1 is FCA Allentown, with Goods stored in Secaucus, New Jersey.
  4. Calendar the March 2028 non-renewal date.

### Issue 13: Tidemark is not a constraint on the Corvane deal, with one lender item

- **Recommendation:** Keep Tidemark invoices current, and raise the bailee waiver with Corvane's financing team.
