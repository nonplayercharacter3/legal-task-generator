**Re: Proposed Acquisition of Halvorsen Coffee Distribution LLC**

Dear Mr. Halvorsen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Corvane Hospitality Partners LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire Halvorsen Coffee Distribution LLC, a New York limited liability company (the "**Company**"), from the holders of its membership interests (each a "**Seller**" and together the "**Sellers**"). Buyer, the Company and the Sellers are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the Parties' present intentions. It creates no legally binding obligation to complete the transaction described in it (the "**Transaction**").

## 1. Transaction Structure

1.1 **Purchase of Interests.** At the Closing (defined in Section 4), Buyer will purchase from the Sellers, and the Sellers will sell to Buyer, 100% of the membership interests in the Company (the "**Interests**"). The Interests will be free and clear of all liens, pledges, options and other encumbrances.

1.2 **Effect.** As a result of the Transaction, the Company will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer. The Company will continue as a separate legal entity, and its contracts, licenses, permits, customer relationships and other assets will remain with the Company.

1.3 **Business.** The Company's business includes the marketing, sale, installation and servicing of commercial coffee equipment to food service operators in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. This business includes its distribution of Brightwater commercial espresso machines under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 1, 2024 between Brightwater Espresso Systems Inc. and the Company, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated as of March 1, 2025 (as amended, the "**Distribution Agreement**") (the "**Business**").

## 2. Purchase Price

2.1 **Base Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Interests will be $18,500,000 (the "**Base Price**"). The Base Price assumes the Company will be delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of net working capital.

2.2 **Adjustments.** The Base Price will be adjusted at Closing, dollar for dollar, for (a) cash and cash equivalents held by the Company, (b) indebtedness and unpaid transaction expenses of the Company, and (c) the amount by which closing net working capital exceeds or falls short of a target to be agreed after Buyer's due diligence. A customary post-closing true-up will apply within ninety (90) days after Closing.

2.3 **Payment.** At Closing, Buyer will pay the Base Price, as adjusted, in cash by wire transfer, except as follows:

(a) $1,850,000 (10% of the Base Price) will be deposited with a mutually acceptable escrow agent for eighteen (18) months to secure the Sellers' indemnification obligations; and

(b) up to $2,000,000 will be payable as an earn-out if the Company's Gross Profit for calendar year 2027 equals or exceeds a target to be set out in the Definitive Agreement.

2.4 **Allocation Among Sellers.** The Sellers will allocate the purchase price among themselves in proportion to their respective Interests, as shown on Schedule 1, unless they direct otherwise in writing.

## 3. Definitive Agreement

3.1 **Preparation.** Buyer's counsel will prepare the first draft of a membership interest purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). Buyer will deliver that draft within twenty-one (21) days after the Sellers countersign this Letter.

3.2 **Terms.** The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants, indemnities and closing deliverables customary for transactions of this type and size. The terms of this Letter will be reflected in it.

3.3 **Indemnification.** General representations will survive for eighteen (18) months after Closing. Fundamental representations and tax representations will survive until sixty (60) days after the applicable statute of limitations expires. The Sellers' aggregate liability for breaches of general representations will be capped at 10% of the Base Price, subject to a deductible of 0.75% of the Base Price. These limits will not apply to fraud.

## 4. Closing

4.1 **Target Closing Date.** The Parties will use commercially reasonable efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement by October 30, 2026. The closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**") is targeted for November 30, 2026, or another date the Parties agree.

4.2 **Simultaneous Sign and Close.** If all conditions in Section 6 are satisfied or waived when the Definitive Agreement is signed, the Parties may sign and close simultaneously.

## 5. Due Diligence

5.1 **Access.** Beginning on the date the Sellers countersign this Letter, the Company will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access during normal business hours to the Company's books, records, contracts, facilities, financial statements, tax returns and personnel. This access will be subject to reasonable advance notice and will be coordinated through Mr. Halvorsen.

5.2 **Scope.** Buyer's due diligence will include financial, accounting, tax, legal, commercial, employment, environmental and operational review of the Business. It will include review of the Company's material contracts, including the Distribution Agreement and the Company's warehouse, lease, customer and service arrangements.

5.3 **No Contact.** Buyer will not contact any supplier, customer, landlord or employee of the Company about the Transaction (other than Mr. Halvorsen and personnel he designates) without the Company's prior consent.

## 6. Conditions to Closing

Buyer's obligation to complete the Transaction will be subject to the following conditions, along with the other conditions customary in the Definitive Agreement:

(a) completion of Buyer's due diligence with results satisfactory to Buyer in its sole discretion;

(b) negotiation, execution and delivery of the Definitive Agreement on terms satisfactory to Buyer and the Sellers;

(c) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's investment committee;

(d) the accuracy, in all material respects, of the Sellers' representations and warranties in the Definitive Agreement at signing and Closing, and the Sellers' performance in all material respects of their pre-Closing covenants;

(e) no material adverse change in the Business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company between the date of this Letter and Closing;

(f) delivery of audited financial statements of the Company for fiscal year 2025 and reviewed interim financial statements for the period ending September 30, 2026;

(g) execution by Mr. Halvorsen of a two (2) year employment agreement and a restrictive covenant agreement with the Company, on the terms described in Section 7;

(h) repayment in full at Closing of the Company's revolving credit facility and release of all related liens; and

(i) no injunction, order or pending proceeding restraining or prohibiting the Transaction.

## 7. Employment Matters

7.1 **Key Employee.** At Closing, Mr. Halvorsen will enter into an employment agreement with the Company to serve as President for a term of two (2) years. His annual base salary will be no less than his current compensation, and he will be eligible to participate in Buyer's management incentive plan.

7.2 **Restrictive Covenants.** Each Seller will agree not to compete with the Business in the Territory described in Section 1.3, or to solicit its customers or employees, for three (3) years after Closing.

7.3 **Employees.** Buyer intends to retain the Company's existing employees, including its certified service technicians, on substantially similar terms of employment.

## 8. Conduct of Business Before Closing

From the date of this Letter until Closing or termination of this Letter, the Company will operate the Business in the ordinary course, consistent with past practice. It will use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business relationships and goodwill. Without Buyer's prior written consent, which Buyer will not unreasonably withhold, the Company will not:

(a) make any distribution to the Sellers other than tax distributions consistent with past practice;

(b) enter into, materially amend or terminate any contract outside the ordinary course; or

(c) incur indebtedness other than borrowings under its existing revolving credit facility in the ordinary course.

## 9. Exclusivity

From the date the Sellers countersign this Letter until the earlier of (a) December 15, 2026 and (b) the date Buyer notifies the Sellers in writing that it is ending negotiations (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), neither the Company nor any Seller will take any of the following actions, directly or through any representative:

(a) solicit, encourage, negotiate or accept any proposal from any person other than Buyer for the acquisition of any equity interest in the Company or of any material portion of its assets; or

(b) furnish any information to any person for that purpose.

The Sellers will promptly notify Buyer of any such proposal they receive during the Exclusivity Period.

## 10. Confidentiality

The Confidentiality Agreement dated August 12, 2026 between Buyer and the Company continues to apply to all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction. No Party will publicly disclose the existence or terms of this Letter without the other Parties' prior written consent, except as required by law.

## 11. Expenses

Each Party will bear its own legal, accounting, advisory and other expenses incurred in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed. Expenses of the Company that are not paid before Closing will be treated as transaction expenses under Section 2.2.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 5.3, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 of this Letter (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the Parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any legally binding obligation. Except for the Binding Provisions, no Party will have any obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered. Either Party may end negotiations at any time before then for any reason, without liability.

## 13. Termination

This Letter will terminate automatically at the end of the Exclusivity Period if the Definitive Agreement has not been executed by then. It may also be terminated earlier by mutual written agreement. The Binding Provisions in Sections 10, 11, 13 and 14 survive termination.

## 14. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
