# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment No. 1**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Brightwater Espresso Systems Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"); and

**Halvorsen Coffee Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. The Agreement does not contain a minimum purchase requirement. In consideration of the continuation of Distributor's exclusive appointment under Section 3.1 of the Agreement, the Parties wish to establish an annual minimum purchase commitment and related reporting and remedy provisions.

C. Section 19.1 of the Agreement permits the Agreement to be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment No. 1 have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Contract Year.** "**Contract Year**" means each calendar year during the Term, beginning with the calendar year commencing January 1, 2025. If the Term ends on a date other than December 31, the final Contract Year is the period from January 1 of that year to the last day of the Term.

1.3 **Net Purchases.** "**Net Purchases**" means the aggregate net invoice price paid or payable by Distributor to Supplier for goods shipped to Distributor during a Contract Year under accepted Purchase Orders. Net Purchases exclude taxes, freight and insurance charges, credits for returned or nonconforming goods, and any amounts credited or reimbursed under Sections 6.3 or 9.2 of the Agreement.

1.4 **References.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder" or words of similar import means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment No. 1.

## 2. RECITALS AND APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Confirmation of Appointment.** Supplier confirms that Distributor's exclusive appointment under Section 3.1 of the Agreement continues in full force, subject to Distributor's satisfaction of the Annual Minimum as provided in Section 3 of this Amendment No. 1.

2.2 **No Change to Territory.** The Territory remains New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

## 3. ANNUAL MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Annual Minimum.** For each Contract Year, Distributor shall make Net Purchases of Products from Supplier of not less than $2,400,000 (the "**Annual Minimum**"). For purposes of this Section 3, Section 4 and Section 5 of this Amendment No. 1, "**Products**" means all goods offered by Supplier from time to time, including spare parts and accessories, whether or not listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement.

3.2 **Proration.** If a Contract Year is shorter than twelve (12) months, the Annual Minimum for that Contract Year will be reduced pro rata based on the number of days in that Contract Year.

3.3 **Supplier Failure to Supply.** The Annual Minimum for a Contract Year will be reduced by the net invoice price of any goods ordered under a Purchase Order that conforms to the Agreement and that Supplier (a) rejects in breach of Section 4.2 of the Agreement, or (b) fails to deliver within sixty (60) days after the requested delivery date, other than because of a force majeure event affecting Supplier under Section 19.3 of the Agreement.

3.4 **No Carryover.** Net Purchases in excess of the Annual Minimum in any Contract Year do not carry over to, and may not be credited against, the Annual Minimum for any other Contract Year.

## 4. REPORTING AND RECONCILIATION

4.1 **Quarterly Statement.** Together with each quarterly report delivered under Section 7.5 of the Agreement, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a statement of its cumulative Net Purchases of Products for the then-current Contract Year.

4.2 **Annual Reconciliation.** Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall deliver to Distributor a written reconciliation showing Distributor's total Net Purchases of Products for that Contract Year, any adjustment under Section 3.2 or Section 3.3, and the amount of any shortfall against the Annual Minimum (a "**Shortfall**"). Distributor may dispute the reconciliation by written notice, with reasonable detail, within thirty (30) days after receipt, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

## 5. CONSEQUENCES OF SHORTFALL

5.1 **Cure Period.** If a Shortfall is finally determined under Section 4.2, Distributor may cure the Shortfall by placing Purchase Orders for Products, in addition to its ordinary requirements, with an aggregate net invoice price at least equal to the Shortfall within ninety (90) days after that determination (the "**Cure Period**"). Purchases made to cure a Shortfall do not count toward the Annual Minimum for the Contract Year in which they are made.

5.2 **Conversion to Non-Exclusive.** If Distributor does not cure a Shortfall within the Cure Period, Supplier may, by written notice given within sixty (60) days after the end of the Cure Period, convert Distributor's appointment under Section 3.1 of the Agreement to a non-exclusive appointment, effective thirty (30) days after the notice. Upon that conversion, Section 3.2 and the second sentence of Section 3.3 of the Agreement will cease to apply.

5.3 **Termination.** If a Shortfall exceeding twenty-five percent (25%) of the Annual Minimum occurs in any Contract Year and is not cured within the Cure Period, or if an uncured Shortfall occurs in two (2) consecutive Contract Years, the failure constitutes a material breach of the Agreement that is not capable of further cure. Supplier may then terminate the Agreement by written notice under Section 15.1 of the Agreement without any additional cure period.

5.4 **Repurchase.** A termination by Supplier under Section 5.3 of this Amendment No. 1 is a termination by Supplier under Section 15.1 of the Agreement for purposes of Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Exclusive Remedies.** The remedies in this Section 5 are Supplier's sole and exclusive remedies for Distributor's failure to meet the Annual Minimum.

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS

6.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly modified by this Amendment No. 1, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. The Parties ratify and confirm the Agreement as amended by this Amendment No. 1.

6.2 **Representations.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Amendment No. 1, and that this Amendment No. 1 has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it and is its valid and binding obligation.

6.3 **Governing Law.** This Amendment No. 1 is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Sections 18.2 and 18.3 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment No. 1.

6.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment No. 1 may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

6.5 **Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
