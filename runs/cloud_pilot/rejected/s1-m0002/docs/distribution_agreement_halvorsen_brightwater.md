# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Brightwater Espresso Systems Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"); and

**Halvorsen Coffee Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures commercial espresso machines for the hospitality and food service industries.

B. Distributor is engaged in the business of marketing, selling, installing and servicing commercial coffee equipment to restaurants, hotels, cafés and other food service operators.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings given below or where first defined in the text.

1.1 "**Products**" means the commercial espresso machines listed in Exhibit A.

1.2 "**Territory**" means New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

1.3 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under Section 4.

1.4 "**Customer**" means any end user of Products located in the Territory that purchases or leases Products from Distributor for its own use and not for resale.

1.5 "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1, and "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2. The Initial Term together with any Renewal Term is the "**Term**."

1.6 "**Marks**" means the trademarks, trade names, logos and service marks of Supplier used in connection with the Products, including BRIGHTWATER and the Brightwater wave logo.

1.7 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 11.1.

1.8 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 15, continues until December 31, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 **Renewal Option.** Distributor may renew this Agreement for one additional term of three (3) years (the "**Renewal Term**") by giving Supplier written notice at least 90 days before Initial Term expiry. Notice of renewal must be given in accordance with Section 17. If Distributor does not give timely notice under this Section 2.2, this Agreement will expire at the end of the Initial Term.

2.3 **Terms of Renewal.** The Renewal Term will be on the terms of this Agreement then in effect, except that the prices in Exhibit B may be adjusted under Section 5.2.

## 3. APPOINTMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

3.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or indirectly, sell Products to any person in the Territory, or appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller for Products in the Territory, other than through Distributor. Supplier may continue to sell directly to the national accounts listed in Part 2 of Exhibit A, provided that Supplier pays Distributor a service referral fee equal to five percent (5%) of the net invoice price of each such sale delivered into the Territory.

3.3 **Distributor Restrictions.** Distributor shall not actively solicit sales of Products outside the Territory. During the Term, Distributor shall not distribute any commercial espresso machine manufactured by a competitor of Supplier that is substantially similar in function and price point to any Product, without Supplier's prior written consent.

3.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, agency or employment relationship between them. Distributor purchases Products for its own account and sets its own resale prices.

## 4. ORDERS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by submitting Purchase Orders to Supplier. Each Purchase Order must state the Products, model numbers, quantities, requested delivery date and delivery location.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days of receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Forecasts.** By the tenth (10th) day of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated Product requirements for the following two (2) quarters.

4.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for Products set out in Exhibit B. Supplier shall invoice upon shipment.

5.2 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the prices in Exhibit B once per calendar year, effective January 1, by giving Distributor at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No single annual increase may exceed four percent (4%) over the prices then in effect.

5.3 **Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.4 **Late Payment.** Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Distributor shall pay or for which it shall provide a valid resale exemption certificate.

## 6. DELIVERY

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in Allentown, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020), unless the Purchase Order specifies another delivery location agreed by Supplier.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery under Section 6.1.

6.3 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect Products within ten (10) Business Days after receipt and shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage or visible damage within that period. Supplier shall promptly replace or credit nonconforming Products.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, market and sell the Products throughout the Territory.

7.2 **Installation and Service.** Distributor shall maintain at least two (2) Supplier-certified service technicians in each state within the Territory and shall provide installation, preventive maintenance and warranty repair services to Customers in accordance with Supplier's published service standards.

7.3 **Inventory.** Distributor shall maintain a reasonable inventory of Products sufficient to meet anticipated Customer demand in the Territory.

7.4 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its resale, installation and servicing of the Products in the Territory.

7.5 **Reports.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a report of Product sales by model and state for that quarter.

## 8. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

8.1 **Supply.** Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to fill all accepted Purchase Orders by the requested delivery date.

8.2 **Training and Materials.** Supplier shall provide, at no charge, initial and annual technical training for Distributor's service technicians and reasonable quantities of sales literature and marketing materials.

8.3 **Leads.** Supplier shall promptly refer to Distributor all inquiries it receives regarding the purchase of Products in the Territory, other than inquiries from the national accounts identified in Part 2 of Exhibit A.

8.4 **Discontinuation.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice before discontinuing any Product, and shall make spare parts for any discontinued Product available to Distributor for at least seven (7) years after discontinuation.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor and to each Customer that the Products will conform to the specifications in Exhibit A and will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after installation at the Customer's premises, but no more than eighteen (18) months after delivery to Distributor.

9.2 **Remedy.** Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace any Product that does not conform to Section 9.1, and shall reimburse Distributor for warranty labor performed by its certified technicians at the rates set out in Exhibit B.

9.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is its valid and binding obligation.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. TRADEMARKS

10.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Marks in the Territory solely to advertise and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines.

10.2 **Ownership.** All rights in the Marks remain with Supplier. Distributor shall not register or use any trademark, trade name or domain name confusingly similar to the Marks.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, customer lists and product specifications.

11.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party other than its employees, advisors and financing sources who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 11.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Section 11.2 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice where lawful.

11.4 **Survival.** The obligations in this Section 11 survive for three (3) years after the end of the Term.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its members, managers, officers and employees from third-party claims arising out of (a) any defect in the design or manufacture of the Products, (b) any claim that the Products or the Marks infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party, or (c) Supplier's breach of this Agreement.

12.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its directors, officers and employees from third-party claims arising out of (a) Distributor's negligent installation or servicing of Products, (b) any warranty or representation regarding the Products made by Distributor beyond those in Section 9, or (c) Distributor's breach of this Agreement.

12.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without its consent.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTIONS 11 AND 12, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

13.2 Supplier maintains, and shall continue to maintain during the Term, product liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, naming Distributor as an additional insured.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent. Supplier may, however, assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its espresso machine business that assumes Supplier's obligations in writing.

14.2 **Change of Control of Distributor.** Any transfer, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the equity or membership interests in Distributor, or of the power to direct its management and policies, whether by sale, merger, conversion or otherwise, is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Distributor and requires Supplier's prior written consent. Distributor shall request that consent in writing at least thirty (30) days before the proposed transfer.

14.3 **Void Transfers.** Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 14 is void.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **Material Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, or within ten (10) days in the case of non-payment of undisputed amounts.

15.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Unauthorized Assignment or Change of Control.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Distributor assigns or purports to assign this Agreement, or undergoes a transfer described in Section 14.2, without Supplier's prior written consent.

## 16. EFFECT OF TERMINATION OR EXPIRATION

16.1 **Outstanding Orders.** Upon expiration or termination, Supplier shall fill all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination, unless Supplier terminated under Section 15.1 for non-payment or under Section 15.2.

16.2 **Repurchase of Inventory.** Within sixty (60) days after expiration or termination other than by Supplier under Sections 15.1, 15.2 or 15.3, Supplier shall repurchase, at Distributor's option, all new, unused and resalable Products in Distributor's inventory at Distributor's net landed cost.

16.3 **Wind-Down.** Distributor may continue to sell Products remaining in its inventory, and to perform warranty and service work for existing Customers, for one hundred eighty (180) days after expiration or termination.

16.4 **Survival.** Sections 9, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17, 18 and 19 survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

18.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

18.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

19.2 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: Amendment No. 1, then the Agreement body, then the Exhibits.

19.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

19.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision will be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.
