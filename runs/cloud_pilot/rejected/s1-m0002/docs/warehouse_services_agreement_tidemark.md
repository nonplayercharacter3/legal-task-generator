# WAREHOUSE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Warehouse Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Tidemark Logistics LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Warehouse Provider**"); and

**Halvorsen Coffee Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**").

Warehouse Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Warehouse Provider operates a public and contract warehouse facility located at 600 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 (the "**Facility**").

B. Customer markets, sells, installs and services commercial coffee equipment, and wishes to engage Warehouse Provider to receive, store, handle and release its inventory at the Facility.

C. Warehouse Provider wishes to provide those services on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings given below or where first defined in the text.

1.1 "**Goods**" means the commercial coffee equipment, spare parts, accessories, packaging and related merchandise that Customer tenders to Warehouse Provider for storage at the Facility, as identified in Warehouse Receipts.

1.2 "**Services**" means the receiving, storage, handling, inventory control, order picking, packing, loading and related services described in Section 3 and Exhibit A.

1.3 "**Warehouse Receipt**" means the non-negotiable warehouse receipt or electronic receiving record issued by Warehouse Provider under Section 4.2 for Goods received at the Facility.

1.4 "**Release Order**" means a written or electronic instruction from Customer to Warehouse Provider to release, pick, pack or ship specified Goods.

1.5 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

1.6 "**WMS**" means Warehouse Provider's web-based warehouse management system, through which Customer may submit Release Orders and view inventory records.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 13, continues until May 31, 2028 (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 **Automatic Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods (each a "**Renewal Period**") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Period.

2.3 **Rates on Renewal.** Warehouse Provider may adjust the rates in Exhibit B effective at the start of any Renewal Period by giving Customer at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No adjustment may exceed five percent (5%) over the rates then in effect.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Scope.** Warehouse Provider shall perform the Services at the Facility in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with this Agreement, the operating procedures in Exhibit A and generally accepted standards of the warehousing industry in the New York metropolitan area.

3.2 **Dedicated Space.** Warehouse Provider shall make available to Customer not less than 12,000 square feet of dry, enclosed, racked storage space within the Facility (the "**Dedicated Space**"), together with shared access to dock doors and staging areas. Customer may request additional space on thirty (30) days' notice, subject to availability, at the rates in Exhibit B.

3.3 **Hours.** Warehouse Provider shall receive and release Goods between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Business Days. Services outside those hours are available on twenty-four (24) hours' notice at the after-hours rates in Exhibit B.

3.4 **Order Fulfillment.** Warehouse Provider shall pick, pack and stage for carrier pickup all Goods covered by a Release Order received by 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day by the close of that Business Day, and all other Release Orders by the close of the following Business Day.

3.5 **Personnel.** Warehouse Provider shall furnish all labor, supervision, material handling equipment and racking necessary to perform the Services. All personnel performing the Services are employees or subcontractors of Warehouse Provider, and Warehouse Provider is solely responsible for their wages, benefits, withholdings and supervision.

3.6 **Independent Contractor.** Warehouse Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

## 4. RECEIPT AND STORAGE OF GOODS

4.1 **Advance Notice.** Customer shall give Warehouse Provider advance shipping notice, by WMS or email, of each inbound shipment at least one (1) Business Day before its scheduled arrival, stating the carrier, number of units, model or part numbers and any special handling requirements.

4.2 **Receiving.** Warehouse Provider shall unload, count and visually inspect each inbound shipment upon arrival and shall issue a Warehouse Receipt within one (1) Business Day. Warehouse Provider shall note on the carrier's delivery receipt, and report to Customer within one (1) Business Day, any shortage, overage or visible damage. Warehouse Receipts are non-negotiable and are issued solely in the name of Customer.

4.3 **Storage Conditions.** Warehouse Provider shall store Goods in the Dedicated Space in a clean, dry, secure and climate-moderated environment, maintained between 40°F and 95°F, and shall follow the stacking and orientation markings on Goods packaging.

4.4 **Segregation.** Warehouse Provider shall keep Goods physically segregated from goods of other customers and identified as Customer's property by location labels in the WMS.

4.5 **Hazardous Materials.** Customer shall not tender any hazardous materials for storage without Warehouse Provider's prior written consent. Customer shall disclose any cleaning chemicals, descaling agents or other regulated substances included with Goods.

## 5. INVENTORY CONTROL

5.1 **Records.** Warehouse Provider shall maintain accurate, real-time inventory records of all Goods in the WMS, by stock-keeping unit and serial number where applicable, and shall give Customer continuous read-only access to those records.

5.2 **Cycle Counts.** Warehouse Provider shall perform cycle counts of Goods monthly and a full physical inventory once each calendar year at a date agreed by the Parties. Customer may observe any count.

5.3 **Inventory Accuracy.** Warehouse Provider shall maintain an inventory accuracy rate of at least 99.5%, measured by units at each annual physical inventory. Warehouse Provider shall credit Customer for any unexplained shortage exceeding that tolerance at the lesser of Customer's documented landed cost and the limit in Section 9.2.

## 6. RELEASE AND SHIPMENT

6.1 **Release Orders.** Warehouse Provider shall release Goods only upon a Release Order submitted by an authorized representative of Customer through the WMS or by email from a designated Customer address. Customer shall maintain and update the list of authorized representatives in the WMS.

6.2 **Carriers.** Warehouse Provider shall load Goods onto carriers designated by Customer. Customer is responsible for selecting carriers and paying freight charges unless Customer requests that Warehouse Provider arrange freight, in which case Warehouse Provider shall invoice freight at cost plus the handling fee in Exhibit B.

6.3 **Delivery to Carrier.** Warehouse Provider's responsibility for Goods ends when they are loaded onto the carrier's vehicle and the carrier signs a bill of lading.

## 7. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Rates.** Customer shall pay the storage, handling and accessorial charges set out in Exhibit B.

7.2 **Invoicing.** Warehouse Provider shall invoice storage charges monthly in advance and handling and accessorial charges monthly in arrears, with supporting activity detail from the WMS.

7.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Customer shall notify Warehouse Provider in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within fifteen (15) days of receipt, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Customer shall timely pay all undisputed portions of any invoice.

7.4 **Late Payment.** Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

7.5 **Warehouse Lien.** Warehouse Provider has a warehouse lien on Goods in its possession under Section 7-209 of the Uniform Commercial Code solely for undisputed charges relating to those Goods that remain unpaid more than thirty (30) days after their due date. Warehouse Provider shall give Customer at least fifteen (15) days' written notice before enforcing that lien.

## 8. TITLE AND RISK

8.1 **Title.** Title to all Goods remains with Customer at all times. Warehouse Provider has no ownership interest in the Goods and shall not pledge, encumber or permit any lien on them other than the lien described in Section 7.5.

8.2 **Customer Financing.** Warehouse Provider acknowledges that Goods may be subject to security interests granted by Customer to its lenders. Upon Customer's request, Warehouse Provider shall execute a customary landlord or bailee waiver in favor of Customer's lenders.

## 9. LIABILITY FOR GOODS

9.1 **Standard of Care.** Warehouse Provider is liable for loss of or damage to Goods while in its care, custody or control caused by its failure to exercise the care that a reasonably careful warehouse operator would exercise under like circumstances, consistent with Section 7-204 of the Uniform Commercial Code.

9.2 **Limit.** Warehouse Provider's liability for loss of or damage to Goods is limited to Customer's documented landed cost of the affected Goods, up to a maximum of $250,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 in the aggregate in any calendar year. Customer may increase these limits for specified Goods by written request and payment of the additional valuation charge in Exhibit B.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Warehouse Provider is not liable for loss or damage caused by (a) concealed damage or defects in Goods existing before receipt, (b) inadequate packaging furnished by Customer or its suppliers, or (c) a force majeure event under Section 16.3, except to the extent Warehouse Provider's negligence contributed to the loss.

9.4 **Claims.** Customer shall give Warehouse Provider written notice of any claim for loss or damage within sixty (60) days after Customer learns of the loss or damage, and in any event within nine (9) months after the Goods are released or reported lost.

## 10. INSURANCE

10.1 **Warehouse Provider Insurance.** During the Term, Warehouse Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) warehouse legal liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and (d) automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit. Warehouse Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policy in clause (a).

10.2 **Customer Insurance.** Customer is responsible for insuring Goods against first-party property loss at their full value. Customer shall cause its property insurers to waive subrogation against Warehouse Provider except to the extent of Warehouse Provider's liability under Section 9.

10.3 **Certificates.** Each Party shall provide certificates of insurance to the other upon request and shall give at least thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation or material reduction of required coverage.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including inventory data, customer and supplier identities, order volumes and pricing, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement. A Party may disclose that information to its employees, advisors, lenders and actual or prospective investors or acquirers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 11.

11.2 **Exceptions.** Section 11.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice where lawful.

11.3 **Survival.** The obligations in this Section 11 survive for two (2) years after the end of the Term.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **By Warehouse Provider.** Warehouse Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its members, managers, officers and employees from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage (other than to Goods, which is governed by Section 9) arising out of the negligence or willful misconduct of Warehouse Provider or its personnel at the Facility.

12.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Warehouse Provider and its members, managers, officers and employees from third-party claims arising out of (a) any defect in the Goods, or (b) Customer's failure to disclose hazardous or regulated substances under Section 4.5.

12.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and reasonably cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense.

12.4 **Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTIONS 11 AND 12.1 THROUGH 12.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **Material Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, or within ten (10) days in the case of non-payment of undisputed amounts.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Convenience.** After the first anniversary of the Effective Date, Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice, subject to payment of storage charges for the Dedicated Space through the effective date of termination.

## 14. EFFECT OF TERMINATION OR EXPIRATION

14.1 **Removal of Goods.** Upon expiration or termination, Customer shall remove all Goods from the Facility within thirty (30) days, and Warehouse Provider shall cooperate in an orderly transition, including loading Goods onto Customer's designated carriers at the standard handling rates in Exhibit B.

14.2 **Final Accounting.** Warehouse Provider shall deliver a final inventory report and final invoice within fifteen (15) days after all Goods are removed.

14.3 **Survival.** Sections 7.5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15 and 16 survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

16.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Either Party may, however, assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates. A change in the ownership or control of a Party is not an assignment for purposes of this Section 16.1.

Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

16.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, severe weather, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees, and acts of government, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

16.4 **Amendments; Waivers.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

16.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision will be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## EXHIBIT A: OPERATING PROCEDURES

1. **Receiving Window.** Inbound appointments are scheduled through the WMS in two-hour windows. Unscheduled arrivals will be received as dock capacity permits.
2. **Storage Configuration.** Palletized espresso machines are stored on selective pallet racking, no more than one pallet high per rack position. Spare parts and accessories are stored in bin shelving by part number.
3. **Serial Number Capture.** Warehouse Provider shall scan and record serial numbers of all espresso machines upon receipt and release.
4. **Damaged Goods.** Goods found damaged upon receipt or in storage are moved to a quarantine location and photographed, and Customer is notified within one (1) Business Day.
5. **Reporting.** Warehouse Provider shall deliver a monthly operations report showing receipts, releases, on-hand inventory, order cycle times and inventory accuracy.
