# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$7,000,000.00**
**Issue Date: October 30, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (together with its permitted successors and assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal sum of SEVEN MILLION DOLLARS ($7,000,000.00), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms set out in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the "Seller Note" issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), among the Maker, as Buyer, the Holder, as Seller, and Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc. (the "**Company**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal; Maturity Date

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The original principal amount of this Note is $7,000,000.00.

**1.2 Maturity Date.** The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date, being October 30, 2029 (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

**1.3 Scheduled Principal Payments.** The Maker shall pay principal in installments as follows:

(a) $1,750,000.00 on the date that is 12 months after the Closing Date;

(b) $1,750,000.00 on the date that is 24 months after the Closing Date; and

(c) the remaining unpaid principal balance on the Maturity Date.

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Rate.** The unpaid principal balance of this Note bears simple interest at the rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum from the Issue Date until paid in full.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each January, April, July and October, beginning January 29, 2027, and on the Maturity Date.

**2.3 Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.4 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the unpaid principal balance bears interest at the rate of eight percent (8.0%) per annum.

**2.5 Maximum Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount received in excess of that rate is applied to reduce principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to the Maker.

## 3. Payments

**3.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account the Holder designates in writing at least five Business Days before the payment date.

**3.2 Application.** Each payment is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal.

**3.3 Set-Off.** All payments of principal and interest under this Note are subject to the Maker's right of set-off under Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement. Any amount set off in accordance with that Section is treated as paid on this Note on the date of the set-off, to the extent of the amount set off. The Maker shall give the Holder written notice of each set-off, describing the claim in reasonable detail.

## 4. Prepayment

The Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments are applied to scheduled principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Subordination

**5.1 Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all indebtedness of the Maker and its subsidiaries for borrowed money owed to any bank, institutional lender or other financial institution, whether outstanding now or incurred later, including all principal, interest, fees and expenses (the "**Senior Debt**").

**5.2 Permitted Payments.** The Maker may make regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note unless a payment default or other event of default under the Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing and the holder of the Senior Debt has given written notice of that default to the Maker and the Holder (a "**Blockage Notice**"). A Blockage Notice suspends payments under this Note for up to 180 days after delivery, or until the default is cured or waived, if earlier. Only one Blockage Notice may be given in any 360-day period.

**5.3 Subordination Agreement.** On request of the Maker or any holder of Senior Debt, the Holder shall execute a subordination agreement consistent with this Section 5 and in form reasonably acceptable to the holder of the Senior Debt.

**5.4 Turnover.** Any payment received by the Holder in violation of this Section 5 is held in trust for the holders of the Senior Debt and shall be promptly paid over to them.

## 6. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within 15 Business Days after the date due, other than a failure resulting from a set-off under Section 3.3 or a suspension under Section 5.2;

(b) the Maker becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or commences any voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law;

(c) an involuntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law is commenced against the Maker and is not dismissed within 60 days; or

(d) the Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the shares of the Company to any Person that is not an Affiliate of the Maker, unless this Note is paid in full at the closing of that transfer.

The exercise in good faith by the Maker of its right of set-off under Section 3.3 is not an Event of Default.

## 7. Remedies

**7.1 Acceleration.** If an Event of Default under Section 6(b) or 6(c) occurs, the unpaid principal and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without notice or demand. If any other Event of Default occurs and is continuing, the Holder may, by written notice to the Maker and subject to Section 5, declare the unpaid principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable.

**7.2 Costs of Collection.** The Maker shall pay the Holder's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

**7.3 Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Section 5, the Holder's rights and remedies under this Note are cumulative and in addition to any other rights available at law or in equity.

## 8. Waivers

The Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest. No delay or omission by the Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right. A waiver on one occasion does not bar any right on any future occasion.

## 9. Transfer; Assignment

**9.1 By the Holder.** The Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, or any interest in it, without the prior written consent of the Maker, except to an Affiliate of the Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note, including Sections 3.3 and 5. Any transferee takes this Note subject to all rights of set-off and defenses of the Maker existing at the time of transfer.

**9.2 By the Maker.** The Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the Holder's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its assets that assumes this Note in writing.

**9.3 Register.** The Maker shall keep a register of the holder of this Note. The Maker may treat the Person listed in the register as the owner of this Note for all purposes.

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction.** The Maker and the Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of New York sitting in the Borough of Manhattan and the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** THE MAKER AND THE HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 11. Miscellaneous

**11.1 Notices.** Notices under this Note must be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**11.2 Amendment.** This Note may be amended or modified only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.4 Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds the Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits the Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

**11.5 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, the Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from the Holder.

**11.6 Cancellation.** When all amounts owed under this Note have been paid in full or satisfied by set-off, the Holder shall surrender this Note to the Maker for cancellation.

**11.7 Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
