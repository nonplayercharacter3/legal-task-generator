# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BATAIRT STUDENT TRANSIT HOLDINGS, INC.

**Dated: October 5, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting, as permitted by Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the Corporation's Bylaws. The undersigned waive any notice or other requirement for a meeting. They adopt the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present.

## 1. Background

**1.1 The Proposed Acquisition.** Management has presented to the Board a proposal for the Corporation to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), from **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Seller**"). The Company operates a fleet of approximately 420 school buses and provides pupil transportation services to public school districts under district pupil transportation contracts.

**1.2 Transaction Documents.** The Board has reviewed substantially final drafts of the following documents (together, the "**Transaction Documents**"):

(a) the Stock Purchase Agreement, to be dated October 6, 2026, among the Corporation, as Buyer, the Seller and, for limited purposes, the Company (the "**Purchase Agreement**");

(b) the Subordinated Promissory Note to be issued by the Corporation to the Seller at the Closing under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Seller Note**");

(c) the Transition Services Agreement, to be dated as of the Closing Date, between the Seller, as Provider, and the Corporation, as Recipient (the "**Transition Services Agreement**");

(d) the letter agreement between the Corporation and the Seller regarding District change-of-control consents, to be dated as of the date of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Side Letter**");

(e) the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement, to be dated as of the Closing Date, under which the Seller assigns the Routing Software to the Company (the "**IP Assignment Agreement**"); and

(f) the Key Employee Agreement, to be dated as of the Closing Date, between the Company and Marisol Okonkwo-Reyes, the Company's Chief Operating Officer (the "**Key Employee Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Principal Terms.** Management has advised the Board that, under the Purchase Agreement:

(a) the aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**");

(b) the Purchase Price is payable at the Closing as $41,500,000 in cash and $7,000,000 by issuance of the Seller Note, which matures 36 months after the Closing Date;

(c) the Closing is scheduled to occur on October 30, 2026; and

(d) the Seller will provide indemnification to the Corporation on the terms set out in Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Review.** The Board has reviewed the Transaction Documents, management's presentation, the financial, operational and legal due diligence reports prepared by the Corporation's advisors, and the proposed financing for the cash portion of the Purchase Price. The Board has had the opportunity to ask questions of management and counsel.

## 2. Approval of the Acquisition and the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Board determines that the acquisition of the Shares on the terms of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Acquisition**") is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the Corporation's performance of its obligations under it, are authorized and approved in all respects;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and the Secretary of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, in substantially the form presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by his or her execution of it; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the payment of the Purchase Price in the amounts and manner provided in Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement is authorized and approved.

## 3. Approval of the Seller Note

**RESOLVED**, that the issuance by the Corporation to the Seller of the Seller Note, in the original principal amount of $7,000,000, maturing 36 months after the Closing Date and subordinated to the Corporation's Senior Debt as provided in the Seller Note, is authorized and approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Seller Note at the Closing in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, in substantially the form presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to make all payments of principal and interest under the Seller Note as they become due, and to give any notice and take any action on behalf of the Corporation in connection with its rights under the Seller Note and Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. Approval of the Ancillary Agreements

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of each of the Transition Services Agreement, the Side Letter and the IP Assignment Agreement, and of each other agreement, certificate and instrument contemplated by the Purchase Agreement to which the Corporation is a party, are authorized and approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver each of those documents in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, in substantially the form presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the payment of fees and pass-through costs under the Transition Services Agreement, as set out in Schedule A to that agreement, is authorized and approved.

## 5. Post-Closing Matters Relating to the Company

**RESOLVED**, that, effective upon the Closing, the Corporation, as sole stockholder of the Company, is authorized to cause the Company to execute, deliver and perform the Key Employee Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, effective upon the Closing, the Corporation, as sole stockholder of the Company, shall elect as directors of the Company the individuals designated by the Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation, to serve until their successors are duly elected and qualified, and each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute a written consent of the sole stockholder of the Company for that purpose; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Compensation Committee of the Board is authorized to consider and approve, under the Corporation's equity incentive plan, the equity award to Marisol Okonkwo-Reyes contemplated by Section 4.4 of the Key Employee Agreement, on such terms as the Compensation Committee determines.

## 6. Financing

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to fund the Closing Cash Payment from the Corporation's cash on hand and from borrowings under the Corporation's existing senior credit facility, and to deliver any borrowing request, notice or certificate required under that facility in connection with the Acquisition.

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to take all further actions, to execute and deliver all further agreements, certificates, instruments and documents, to make all filings, and to pay all fees and expenses, that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions and to complete the Acquisition, the taking of any such action to be conclusive evidence of that determination;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary of the Corporation is authorized to deliver to the Seller at the Closing a certificate certifying these resolutions, as required by Section 3.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any officer or director of the Corporation in connection with the Acquisition and the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions, including the negotiation of the Transaction Documents and the conduct of due diligence under the mutual nondisclosure agreement between the Corporation and the Seller, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness; Counterparts

**8.1 Effectiveness.** This consent is effective as of the date first written above, when it has been signed by all of the directors of the Corporation.

**8.2 Counterparts.** This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**8.3 Filing.** This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
