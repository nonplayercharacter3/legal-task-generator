# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and between **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), among Recipient, as Buyer, Provider, as Seller, and Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), Recipient is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company from Provider. The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs on the Effective Date.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates performed driver background-check, drug and alcohol testing administration and route planning functions for the Company on a centralized basis.

C. Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under it, Provider will continue to provide those functions for the benefit of Recipient and the Company for a transitional period while Recipient establishes its own capabilities.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Applicable Law**" means all Laws applicable to the Services. This includes the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration regulations at 49 C.F.R. Parts 40, 382 and 391, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, state school bus driver certification and fingerprinting requirements, and state student privacy Laws.

"**Background Check Services**" means the services described in Part 1 of Schedule A.

"**Drug and Alcohol Testing Services**" means the services described in Part 2 of Schedule A.

"**Recipient Group**" means Recipient and its subsidiaries, including the Company after the Closing.

"**Route Planning Services**" means the services described in Part 3 of Schedule A.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period starting on the Effective Date and ending on the date stated for that Service in Schedule A, as extended or earlier terminated under Section 3.

"**Services**" means the Background Check Services, the Drug and Alcohol Testing Services and the Route Planning Services, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.4.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Transition Services Agreement.

## 2. Services

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates or approved subcontractors to provide, the Services to the Recipient Group, as described in Schedule A.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a manner, scope, volume and level of priority substantially similar to the manner in which Provider and its Affiliates performed the same functions for the Company during the 12 months before the Effective Date;

(b) in compliance with Applicable Law; and

(c) using personnel with the qualifications, certifications and training required by Applicable Law, including, for the Drug and Alcohol Testing Services, a designated employer representative and access to a certified medical review officer and qualified substance abuse professionals.

**2.3 Limitations.** Provider is not required to (a) provide any Service at a volume materially above the volume provided to the Company before the Effective Date, (b) hire additional personnel or acquire additional systems, or (c) provide any Service in a manner that would violate Applicable Law.

**2.4 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company in the 12 months before the Effective Date and that is not listed in Schedule A, Recipient may request it in writing. Provider shall consider each request in good faith. Any service so agreed (an "**Additional Service**") shall be documented in a written amendment to Schedule A stating its scope, Service Period and fee.

**2.5 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform the Services, including consortium/third-party administrators, collection sites and laboratories certified by the Department of Health and Human Services for drug testing, and consumer reporting agencies for background checks. Schedule A lists the subcontractors used as of the Effective Date. Provider shall not engage a new subcontractor for any Service without Recipient's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors.

**2.6 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall cause the Recipient Group to provide Provider with information, access and cooperation reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services. This includes timely delivery of driver applicant information, authorizations and consents required under the Fair Credit Reporting Act and 49 C.F.R. Part 40, District bell schedules, student enrollment and stop data, and fleet assignments.

## 3. Term and Termination

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Period, unless terminated earlier under this Section 3.

**3.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Period for any Service once, by up to 90 days, by giving written notice to Provider at least 30 days before the Service Period ends. The fees during any extension are the fees stated in Schedule A increased by 10%.

**3.3 Termination of Individual Services.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on at least 30 days' written notice to Provider. Upon termination of a Service, the fees for that Service cease to accrue as of the effective date of termination, prorated for any partial month.

**3.4 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing it in reasonable detail. Provider may not terminate this Agreement or suspend any Service for nonpayment of fees disputed in good faith under Section 5.4.

**3.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, Recipient shall pay all undisputed fees accrued through the effective date. Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 4. Governance

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall designate a service coordinator (a "**Service Coordinator**") with authority to act for it on day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Recipient's initial Service Coordinator is the Chief Operating Officer of the Company. Each party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice.

**4.2 Reporting.** Provider shall deliver to Recipient, by the tenth Business Day of each month, a report for the prior month listing (a) background checks initiated, completed and pending, with any adverse results; (b) drug and alcohol tests conducted, by test type, with any verified positive results, refusals and return-to-duty status; (c) the random testing selection rates achieved against the minimum annual rates required by 49 C.F.R. Part 382; and (d) route changes implemented and any route-related service failures reported by a District.

**4.3 Urgent Notifications.** Provider shall notify Recipient's Service Coordinator promptly, and in any event within the time required by Applicable Law, of any verified positive drug test, alcohol test result of 0.04 or greater, refusal to test, or disqualifying background-check result for any driver or applicant.

## 5. Fees and Payment

**5.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the fees for each Service stated in Schedule A. The fees are intended to reimburse Provider's costs of providing the Services, including the fees of third-party subcontractors, without markup.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider, at cost, for third-party laboratory, collection site, medical review officer and consumer reporting agency charges incurred in performing the Services, to the extent those charges are not included in the fees under Schedule A.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice must include reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**5.4 Disputes.** If Recipient disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, it shall notify Provider in writing within 20 days after receipt, describing the dispute in reasonable detail, and pay the undisputed portion. The Service Coordinators shall attempt to resolve the dispute within 15 Business Days.

**5.5 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 6. Records and Data

**6.1 Ownership of Records.** All driver qualification files, background-check reports, drug and alcohol testing records, route data and other records created or maintained by Provider in performing the Services for the Recipient Group ("**Service Records**") are the property of the Recipient Group.

**6.2 Recordkeeping.** Provider shall maintain the Service Records in accordance with Applicable Law, including the retention periods in 49 C.F.R. § 382.401 and 49 C.F.R. § 391.51. Provider shall report information to the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse as required of a consortium/third-party administrator acting for the Company.

**6.3 Transfer of Records.** Upon expiration or termination of each Service, Provider shall deliver to Recipient, in a reasonably usable electronic format, all Service Records relating to that Service, subject to the confidentiality requirements of 49 C.F.R. Part 40 and the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

**6.4 Routing Software and Systems.** In performing the Route Planning Services, Provider's personnel may access and use the Routing Software and other systems of the Recipient Group solely to perform the Services. Provider acquires no right, title or interest in the Routing Software or any route data by reason of that access. Provider shall comply with the Recipient Group's reasonable security and access policies made known to it in writing.

## 7. Confidentiality

**7.1 Obligations.** Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other party, or of the Recipient Group, received in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"). Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. Confidential Information may be disclosed only to employees, Affiliates, subcontractors and advisors who need to know it for those purposes and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 7.

**7.2 Student and Driver Information.** Provider shall handle student information, and driver background-check and testing information, only as permitted by Applicable Law and the District Contracts. Provider shall implement reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect that information. Provider shall notify Recipient within 48 hours after discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of that information.

**7.3 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the receiving party without restriction before disclosure, or (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information. A party may disclose Confidential Information as required by Law or legal process, after giving the other party prompt notice where lawful.

## 8. Indemnification; Limitation of Liability

**8.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Recipient and the other members of the Recipient Group, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of Provider's willful misconduct in performing the Services or Provider's breach of Section 7.2, subject to Section 8.4.

**8.2 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and subcontractors, from and against all Losses arising from or relating to the Services or this Agreement, including Losses caused by Provider's negligence. This obligation extends to Losses arising out of any claim by a driver, applicant, student, parent, District or Governmental Authority relating to the Background Check Services, the Drug and Alcohol Testing Services or the Route Planning Services.

**8.3 Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification under this Section 8 shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. A delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. For any third-party claim, the indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. The indemnified party may participate at its own expense. The indemnifying party may not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**8.4 Limitation of Liability.** The aggregate liability of Provider arising out of or relating to this Agreement and the Services, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid by Recipient to Provider under this Agreement. Provider shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenues. This Section 8.4 shall not limit Recipient's obligations under Section 8.2.

**8.5 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 9. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

**9.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties. Provider's personnel are not employees of the Recipient Group.

**9.2 No Effect on Purchase Agreement Rights.** Nothing in this Agreement limits any representation, warranty, covenant or indemnification obligation of Provider, as Seller, under the Purchase Agreement for matters occurring before the Closing.

## 10. General

**10.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, with a copy of any notice to Recipient sent to the attention of Recipient's Service Coordinator.

**10.2 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemic, government action, labor disputes not involving the affected party's own employees, or failure of utilities. The affected party shall notify the other party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents performance of a Service for more than 15 consecutive days, Recipient may terminate that Service on written notice.

**10.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. However, Recipient may assign this Agreement to any member of the Recipient Group or to any successor to all or substantially all of the business of the Company.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Section 8 and the members of the Recipient Group, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**10.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.8 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any Proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**10.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## SCHEDULE A: SERVICES, SERVICE PERIODS AND FEES

### Part 1. Background Check Services

**Scope.** For each driver applicant, current driver, bus monitor and other safety-sensitive employee of the Company:

(a) pre-employment criminal history checks, including state and FBI fingerprint-based checks where required by state Law or a District Contract;

(b) sex offender registry searches;

(c) motor vehicle record requests and annual reviews under 49 C.F.R. § 391.25;

(d) previous employer safety performance history inquiries under 49 C.F.R. § 391.23, including Clearinghouse pre-employment queries; and

(e) periodic re-checks required by state Law or the District Contracts.

**Subcontractor.** Ridgeline Screening Services, Inc. (consumer reporting agency).

**Service Period.** Through April 30, 2027.

**Fee.** $38 per completed check package, plus pass-through state and FBI fingerprint fees.

### Part 2. Drug and Alcohol Testing Services

**Scope.** Administration of the Company's controlled substances and alcohol testing program under 49 C.F.R. Parts 40 and 382, including:

(a) enrollment of the Company's covered drivers in Provider's random testing consortium;

(b) random selection at not less than the minimum annual percentage rates published by the FMCSA;

(c) scheduling of pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, return-to-duty and follow-up tests;

(d) coordination of collection sites, certified laboratories and medical review officer services;

(e) Clearinghouse reporting and annual queries; and

(f) preparation of the annual MIS report.

**Subcontractors.** Tidewater Occupational Health Network (collection sites); Clearpath Toxicology Laboratories, LLC (HHS-certified laboratory); Meridian MRO Services, P.C. (medical review officer).

**Service Period.** Through April 30, 2027.

**Fee.** Consortium administration fee of $6,500 per month, plus pass-through collection, laboratory and medical review officer charges.

### Part 3. Route Planning Services

**Scope.**

(a) Annual and mid-year route design for each District served by the Company, using the Routing Software;

(b) processing of District stop additions, deletions and bell-time changes;

(c) special-needs and out-of-district route planning;

(d) field trip and athletic trip scheduling support; and

(e) knowledge transfer and training of Company routing personnel, so that Company personnel can perform route planning independently by the end of the Service Period.

**Service Period.** Through January 31, 2027.

**Fee.** $24,000 per month.
