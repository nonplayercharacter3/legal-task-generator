# LETTER AGREEMENT REGARDING DISTRICT CHANGE-OF-CONTROL CONSENTS

**FAPUL FAMILY TRANSPORTATION HOLDINGS, LLC**

October 6, 2026

**Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**
Attention: General Counsel

**Re: Stock Purchase Agreement for Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.: District Consents**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of the date of this Side Letter (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, for limited purposes, **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

Buyer and Seller wish to set out how the parties will approach the Districts served by the Company about the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, and certain related matters. In consideration of the mutual covenants in the Purchase Agreement and this Side Letter, Buyer and Seller agree as follows:

## 1. District Consent Process

**1.1 Consent Requests.** Within five Business Days after the date of this Side Letter, Seller shall cause the Company to deliver a written request for consent to each District whose consent is listed on Schedule 4.4 to the Purchase Agreement. Each request must be in a form reasonably acceptable to Buyer. Seller shall cause the Company to submit any additional materials the District reasonably requests, including any form of consent, assignment or assumption the District customarily uses.

**1.2 Notices to Other Districts.** Seller shall cause the Company to give each other District that is party to a District Contract a courtesy notice of the transactions. Buyer and Seller shall agree the form of that notice. The notice shall be given no earlier than the date the parties announce the transactions publicly. It shall describe the change of control and confirm that the Company will continue to perform its District Contract without interruption.

**1.3 Buyer Cooperation.** Buyer shall reasonably cooperate with Seller and the Company in seeking the consents described in Section 1.1. Buyer's cooperation includes:

(a) providing information about Buyer's ownership, management, safety record and financial condition that a District reasonably requests;

(b) providing certificates of insurance, or evidence of committed coverage, showing that the Company's auto liability, general liability and umbrella coverage will continue after the Closing at levels no less than those required by the applicable District Contract; and

(c) making appropriate Buyer officers available to attend District board meetings or meet with District transportation staff, as reasonably requested.

**1.4 No Payments or Amendments.** Neither Seller nor the Company shall pay any fee, offer any rate concession or agree to any amendment of a District Contract to obtain a consent without Buyer's prior written consent. Buyer is not required to pay any fee or other consideration, or to agree to any amendment of a District Contract, to obtain a consent.

## 2. Material District Contracts

For purposes of Section 9.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement, references to "Material District Contracts" mean the District Contracts listed on Annex A to this Side Letter.

## 3. Communications with Districts

**3.1 Coordination.** Before the Closing, neither party shall communicate with any District about the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement except as provided in Section 1 or as the parties otherwise agree in writing. Seller shall give Buyer a reasonable opportunity to review and comment on any written communication to a District about the transactions before it is sent. Seller shall promptly give Buyer copies of all written responses received from any District.

**3.2 Participation.** Buyer may attend, and Seller shall give Buyer reasonable advance notice of, any meeting or call with a District about the transactions, including any public board meeting at which a consent request is on the agenda.

**3.3 Status Updates.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer a weekly written update on the status of each consent request. Each update shall state any conditions a District has proposed and any concerns a District has raised.

## 4. Post-Closing Cooperation

**4.1 Outstanding Consents.** If any consent requested under Section 1.1 has not been obtained by the Closing Date, Seller shall continue to cooperate reasonably with Buyer and the Company after the Closing in seeking it. This includes making Seller's personnel with existing relationships with the applicable District reasonably available.

**4.2 Notices from Districts.** If a District gives the Company or Seller written notice of termination, non-renewal or default under any District Contract after the Closing that relates to the change of control of the Company, the party receiving the notice shall promptly deliver a copy to the other party.

**4.3 Claims.** Any claim under Section 9.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement shall be made and resolved under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. General

**5.1 Effect.** Except as expressly provided in this Side Letter, the Purchase Agreement remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms. This Side Letter is an Ancillary Agreement for purposes of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Notices.** Notices under this Side Letter must be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**5.4 Assignment.** This Side Letter may be assigned only together with, and to the same extent as, the Purchase Agreement under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.5 Confidentiality.** The terms of this Side Letter are confidential. Neither party shall disclose them to any District or other third party except as required by Law or as needed to obtain the consents described in Section 1.1.

**5.6 Governing Law.** This Side Letter, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**5.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any Proceeding arising out of or relating to this Side Letter. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**5.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS SIDE LETTER.

**5.9 Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
