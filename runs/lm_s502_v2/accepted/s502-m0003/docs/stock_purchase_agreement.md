# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**" or the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**") is dated October 6, 2026. It is entered into by and among **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Articles IV and VI, **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company operates a fleet of approximately 420 school buses and provides pupil transportation services to public school districts under the District Contracts.

C. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

"**Closing Cash Payment**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2(a).

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares free and clear of all Liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment of Purchase Price.** Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows:

(a) $41,500,000 (the "**Closing Cash Payment**"), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least two Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(b) $7,000,000, by the issuance and delivery to Seller of a subordinated promissory note of Buyer in that principal amount (the "**Seller Note**"). The Seller Note shall mature 36 months after the Closing Date. Payments under it are subject to Section 9.7.

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Taxing Authority is treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on October 30, 2026, or at such other time or place as Buyer and Seller may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) counterparts of each Ancillary Agreement to which Seller or the Company is a party, duly executed;

(c) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Marisol Okonkwo-Reyes;

(d) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(e) the District consents and the landlord estoppel certificate obtained under Section 6.4;

(f) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, dated within five Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(g) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the Closing Cash Payment, in accordance with Section 2.2(a);

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;

(c) counterparts of each Ancillary Agreement to which Buyer is a party, duly executed; and

(d) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Qualification.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where its operations require qualification.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all Liens. No options, warrants or other rights to acquire equity of the Company are outstanding.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller and the Company do not (a) violate the certificate of incorporation or bylaws of the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require the consent of, or notice to, any Person under any Contract to which the Company is a party, except as set forth on Schedule 4.4.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026, and the related statements of income and cash flows for the fiscal years then ended (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except (a) those reflected or reserved against in the June 30, 2026 balance sheet, and (b) those incurred in the ordinary course of business since June 30, 2026, none of which is material.

**4.7 Absence of Certain Changes.** Since June 30, 2026, the Company has operated in the ordinary course of business, and no event has occurred that has had or would reasonably be expected to have a Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since July 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. This includes Laws governing commercial motor vehicle operation, school bus inspection and pupil transportation. The Company holds all permits, operating authorities and certificates material to its business, and each is in full force and effect.

**4.9 Litigation.** There is no Proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company. This includes any Proceeding arising out of any vehicle accident or student injury, other than claims fully covered by insurance and listed in the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.10 Taxes.**

(a) The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it. All such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects.

(b) The Company has timely paid all Taxes due and owing, whether or not shown on any Tax return. The Company has timely withheld and remitted all payroll, employment and withholding Taxes required to have been withheld and remitted in connection with amounts paid to any employee or independent contractor, including its drivers and monitors.

(c) The Company has properly reported and paid all federal and state fuel excise Taxes and has properly claimed any exemptions or refunds for fuel used in school bus operations.

(d) No audit or other Proceeding by any Taxing Authority is pending or threatened with respect to the Company. The Company has not waived any statute of limitations in respect of Taxes.

**4.11 Employees; Driver Qualification.** Each driver employed by the Company holds a valid commercial driver's license with the passenger and school bus endorsements required by applicable Law. Each driver has completed the pre-employment and periodic background checks, and is enrolled in the drug and alcohol testing program, required by applicable Law and the District Contracts. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.12 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.12 lists each Material District Contract and each other Contract of the Company providing for payments in excess of $250,000 per year. Each such Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any such Contract. No District has given written notice of termination or non-renewal of any District Contract.

**4.13 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. The Disclosure Schedule lists each lease under which the Company occupies any bus depot, maintenance facility or office (the "**Real Property Leases**"). Each Real Property Lease is in full force and effect and the Company is not in default under it.

**4.14 Fleet.** The Disclosure Schedule lists each school bus and other vehicle owned or leased by the Company. Each vehicle has passed its most recent state-required inspection and is in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

**4.15 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all intellectual property used in its business. Upon the execution and delivery of the IP Assignment Agreement, the Company will own the Routing Software free and clear of all Liens.

**4.16 Insurance.** The Disclosure Schedule lists all insurance policies maintained by or for the benefit of the Company, including auto liability, general liability, umbrella and workers' compensation policies. All such policies are in full force and effect.

**4.17 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not permit the Company to (a) amend or terminate any Material District Contract, (b) issue any equity securities, (c) incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, or (d) sell or dispose of any school bus other than in scheduled fleet replacement.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Company's properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel. Information furnished under this Section 6.2 is subject to the mutual nondisclosure agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or its assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, (a) each consent listed on Schedule 4.4, and (b) an estoppel certificate from the landlord under each Real Property Lease covering a bus depot. Buyer shall reasonably cooperate in those efforts.

**6.5 Employees.** For 12 months after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each continuing employee with base wages no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the Closing.

**6.6 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are filed after the Closing Date. Seller shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least 30 days before filing.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either Buyer or Seller if the Closing has not occurred by November 30, 2026, unless the failure results from a breach of this Agreement by the terminating party; or

(c) by Buyer or Seller if the other party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it becomes void and of no further effect, except for this Section 8.2 and Article XI, and except that no party is relieved of liability for any willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, survive the Closing until 12 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The Fundamental Representations survive the Closing until the sixth anniversary of the Closing Date.

(c) The obligations of Seller under Section 9.2(b) survive the Closing for the same period as the representations in Section 4.10.

(d) The obligations of Seller under Section 9.2(d) survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the parties to be performed after the Closing survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(e) No claim for indemnification under this Article IX may be asserted, and no Losses are recoverable under this Article IX, unless a Proceeding is commenced with respect to the claim before the expiration of the applicable survival period set forth in this Section 9.1.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any Pre-Closing Taxes;

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(d) the termination, or written notice of termination, of any Material District Contract by the applicable District within 12 months after the Closing Date, to the extent the termination is based on the failure to obtain the District's consent to the change of control of the Company effected by this Agreement.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice to the party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"), describing the claim in reasonable detail. A failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure.

(b) For any claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by giving written notice within 20 days after receipt of notice of the claim. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party may not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000). This limit does not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation or from fraud. The aggregate liability of Seller under this Article IX shall in no event exceed the Purchase Price.

(b) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud, until the aggregate Losses exceed $242,500, after which Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(c) The aggregate liability of Buyer for indemnification under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price.

(d) The limitations in this Section 9.5 apply only to indemnification under this Article IX.

(e) Losses are calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party with respect to the matter.

**9.6 Payments.** The Indemnifying Party shall pay any amount finally determined to be owed under this Article IX within ten Business Days after the determination, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**9.7 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any Losses to which it or any other Buyer indemnitee is entitled under this Article IX against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note. Buyer shall give Seller written notice of any set-off, describing the claim in reasonable detail. The exercise of the right of set-off by Buyer in good faith does not constitute an event of default under the Seller Note.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, this Article IX and Section 9.7 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement. This Section 9.8 does not apply to claims for fraud or for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.10.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.
