# LANDLORD ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE

**Date: October 22, 2026**

**To:**

**Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**
Attention: General Counsel

**Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**
Attention: Chief Operating Officer

**Re:** Lease of the bus depot and maintenance facility at 1180 Tallow Creek Road, New Castle, Delaware 19720 (the "**Premises**")

The undersigned, **Corwin Ridge Industrial Properties, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), delivers this Landlord Estoppel Certificate (this "**Certificate**") about the Lease described in Section 1. **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, is the tenant under the Lease ("**Tenant**").

Landlord understands the following. **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), has agreed to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of Tenant from **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). That acquisition is made under a Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2026 (the "**Transaction**"), and its closing is scheduled for October 30, 2026. Landlord also understands that Buyer, Tenant and Buyer's lenders will rely on this Certificate in completing the Transaction.

Landlord certifies to Buyer, Tenant and Buyer's lenders as follows:

## 1. Lease Documents

**1.1 Lease.** The Premises are leased to Tenant under the Industrial Lease dated March 1, 2019, between Landlord and Tenant. The Industrial Lease was amended by the First Amendment to Lease dated July 15, 2023. Together, these are the "**Lease**." Exhibit A lists every Lease document.

**1.2 Complete Agreement.** The Lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant about the Premises. It has not been modified, supplemented or amended except as stated in Exhibit A. No other agreement, oral or written, between Landlord and Tenant affects Tenant's occupancy of the Premises.

**1.3 Validity.** The Lease is in full force and effect. It is a valid and binding obligation of Landlord and, to Landlord's knowledge, of Tenant.

## 2. Premises and Term

**2.1 Premises.** The Premises consist of approximately 9.4 acres of land, together with the following:

(a) an approximately 22,500 square-foot maintenance building with eight service bays;

(b) an approximately 4,200 square-foot dispatch and office building; and

(c) a paved and lighted bus parking area, a fueling island and a bus wash.

**2.2 Term.** The current term of the Lease began on March 1, 2019 and expires on February 28, 2034.

**2.3 Renewal Options.** Tenant has two remaining options to extend the term, each for five years. Each option may be exercised by written notice to Landlord at least 12 months before the then-current term expires. Tenant has no right of first refusal or option to purchase the Premises.

**2.4 Acceptance and Possession.** Tenant has accepted and is in possession of the entire Premises. All improvements that Landlord was required to construct or pay for under the Lease have been completed and paid for. No allowance, credit or reimbursement is owed to Tenant.

## 3. Rent and Security Deposit

**3.1 Base Rent.** The current monthly base rent is $61,250.00. It is payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month. Base rent increases by 2.5% on each March 1 during the term.

**3.2 Additional Rent.** Tenant pays, as additional rent, its share of real estate taxes, insurance and common area charges under the Lease. Tenant currently pays these in estimated monthly installments of $8,900.00. The reconciliation for calendar year 2025 has been completed, and no amount is owed by either party for that year.

**3.3 Rent Paid.** Base rent and additional rent have been paid through October 31, 2026. No rent has been paid more than one month in advance.

**3.4 Security Deposit.** Landlord holds a security deposit of $122,500.00 under the Lease. Landlord has not applied any part of it to any obligation of Tenant. Tenant has delivered no letter of credit or other security.

## 4. No Defaults; No Claims

**4.1 No Default.** Tenant is not in default under the Lease. To Landlord's knowledge, no event has occurred that, with notice or the passage of time or both, would become a default by Tenant. Landlord has not given Tenant any notice of default that remains uncured.

**4.2 Landlord Performance.** To Landlord's knowledge, Landlord has performed all of its obligations under the Lease that were required to be performed through the date of this Certificate. Landlord has received no written notice from Tenant claiming a default by Landlord.

**4.3 No Offsets.** To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant has no existing defense, offset or counterclaim against the payment of rent or the performance of its other obligations under the Lease.

**4.4 Environmental.** Landlord has not given Tenant written notice of any violation of environmental Law at the Premises. This includes any violation relating to the fueling island, the underground storage tanks or the maintenance building. Landlord is not aware of any such violation.

**4.5 Termination.** Landlord has not given and has not received any notice of termination of the Lease. Neither party has any right to terminate the Lease before the scheduled expiration of the term, except on default or casualty or condemnation as provided in the Lease.

## 5. Transaction

**5.1 No Consent Required.** Section 14.1 of the Lease restricts assignment of the Lease and subletting of the Premises. Under Section 14.4 of the Lease, a transfer of the shares of Tenant is not an assignment of the Lease. Landlord's consent is not required for the Transaction, and the Transaction will not be a default under the Lease.

**5.2 Continuing Lease.** After the Transaction closes, the Lease will continue in full force and effect in accordance with its terms, with Tenant remaining the tenant under it.

## 6. Mortgage

The Premises are subject to a mortgage held by Brandywine Mutual Savings Bank, which is Landlord's only mortgagee. Tenant and that mortgagee are parties to a Subordination, Non-Disturbance and Attornment Agreement dated March 1, 2019, and that agreement remains in full force and effect.

## 7. Authority; Reliance

**7.1 Authority.** The individual signing this Certificate is authorized to sign it on behalf of Landlord.

**7.2 Reliance.** Buyer, Tenant, Buyer's lenders and their respective successors and assigns may rely on this Certificate. This Certificate binds Landlord and its successors and assigns.

**7.3 Effect.** This Certificate does not amend the Lease. Landlord is estopped from asserting any claim or fact inconsistent with the statements in this Certificate against any party entitled to rely on it.

**7.4 Governing Law.** This Certificate is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
