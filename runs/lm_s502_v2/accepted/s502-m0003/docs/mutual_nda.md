# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of June 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and between **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Batairt**"), and **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Fapul**"). Batairt and Fapul are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Fapul owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company provides pupil transportation services to public school districts.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Batairt would acquire the Company (the "**Transaction**"). In doing so, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that a Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Transaction, whether before, on or after the Effective Date. Confidential Information includes:

(a) financial statements, projections, budgets and Tax information;

(b) district contracts, pricing, bid and proposal information, and correspondence with school districts;

(c) route data, bell schedules, stop locations and routing software, including source code and documentation;

(d) fleet, maintenance, inspection, accident and insurance records;

(e) employee, payroll and labor information;

(f) Protected Records, as defined in Section 1.3; and

(g) all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

Information concerning the Company disclosed by Fapul, the Company or their Representatives is Fapul's Confidential Information.

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession without confidentiality restriction before its disclosure under this Agreement;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source that is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

The exclusions in this Section 1.2 do not apply to Protected Records.

**1.3 Protected Records.** "**Protected Records**" means personally identifiable information concerning students, parents or guardians, and the criminal history, background-check, motor vehicle record, medical and drug and alcohol testing records of drivers, bus monitors and applicants.

**1.4 Representatives.** "**Representatives**" means a Party's affiliates and its and their directors, managers, officers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors and actual or prospective financing sources.

## 2. Use and Nondisclosure

**2.1 Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and complete the Transaction (the "**Purpose**").

**2.2 Nondisclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential. It may disclose that information only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose, who are informed of its confidential nature and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**2.3 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.4 Protected Records.** Fapul shall provide Protected Records only to the extent reasonably necessary for Batairt's due diligence, and in de-identified or summary form where practicable. Batairt shall:

(a) access Protected Records only through the electronic data room or other secure means designated by Fapul;

(b) limit access to Representatives directly engaged in operational, safety or legal due diligence;

(c) handle Protected Records in compliance with applicable law, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, 49 C.F.R. Part 40 and applicable state student privacy laws; and

(d) notify Fapul within 48 hours after discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Protected Records.

**2.5 Transaction Information.** Without the other Party's prior written consent, neither Party shall disclose to any person, other than its Representatives, the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, that discussions about the Transaction are taking place, or any of the terms of the Transaction.

## 3. Contact with Districts, Employees and Counterparties

Without Fapul's prior written consent, Batairt shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, contact any school district, district employee, Company employee (other than those designated by Fapul), landlord, supplier or insurer of the Company about the Company or the Transaction. This Section 3 does not restrict contact in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Transaction.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, subpoena or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall reasonably cooperate in that effort at the Disclosing Party's expense. If no protective order is obtained, the Receiving Party may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment of it.

## 5. Return and Destruction

Within ten business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it will not proceed with the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing. The Receiving Party may retain copies (a) required to be kept by law, regulation or bona fide internal compliance policy, and (b) stored in automatic electronic backups that are not readily accessible, provided that retained copies remain subject to this Agreement. Protected Records shall be destroyed and not retained under clause (b).

## 6. No Representations; No Obligation

**6.1 No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty about the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement for the Transaction.

**6.2 No Obligation.** Neither Party is obligated to proceed with the Transaction unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered. Either Party may end discussions at any time for any reason.

**6.3 No License.** No license or other right in any Confidential Information, including the routing software, is granted by this Agreement.

## 7. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for two years after the Effective Date. Obligations concerning Protected Records and trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a Protected Record or a trade secret under applicable law. If the Transaction is completed, Batairt's obligations under this Agreement concerning Confidential Information of the Company end on the closing of the Transaction. Fapul's obligations concerning Batairt's Confidential Information continue for the full term.

## 8. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. Securities Law Compliance

Each Party acknowledges that it is aware, and will advise its Representatives, that United States securities laws restrict any person who has material non-public information about a company from purchasing or selling securities of that company, or from communicating that information to others in circumstances where it is reasonably foreseeable that they will trade in reliance on it.

## 10. General

**10.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the address set out below a Party's signature, or to another address a Party designates by notice.

**10.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

**10.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**10.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.6 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**10.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
