# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and between **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Marisol Okonkwo-Reyes** ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Parent**"), is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company from **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC** ("**Seller**"). The closing of that acquisition (the "**Closing**") occurs on the Effective Date.

B. Executive serves as Chief Operating Officer of the Company. Executive has substantial knowledge of the Company's District Contracts, driver workforce, fleet operations and route planning.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires the Company and Executive to enter into this Agreement concurrently with the Closing. Parent regards Executive's continued service, and Executive's agreement to the covenants in Sections 7 through 9, as a material inducement to completing the acquisition.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business**" means providing pupil transportation services to public school districts, private schools and other educational institutions. This includes school bus operation, route planning, driver recruitment and training, and fleet maintenance.

"**Cause**" means any of the following by Executive:

(a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud, dishonesty or harm to a minor;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company;

(c) material breach of this Agreement or of any written Company policy, including policies on driver qualification, student safety or drug and alcohol testing, which is not cured within 15 days after written notice, if curable; or

(d) willful failure to perform Executive's material duties, other than through Disability, which is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

"**Disability**" means Executive's inability, due to physical or mental illness or incapacity, to perform the essential functions of Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 90 consecutive days or 120 days in any 12-month period.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following without Executive's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in Base Salary, other than an across-the-board reduction of no more than 10% that applies to all senior executives of Parent and its subsidiaries;

(b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or

(c) relocation of Executive's principal place of work by more than 50 miles.

Good Reason exists only if Executive gives written notice within 60 days after the condition first occurs, the Company fails to cure within 30 days after that notice, and Executive resigns within 30 days after the cure period ends.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of Executive's employment and the 18 months after the Termination Date.

"**Restricted Territory**" means each state in which the Company provides pupil transportation services, or has submitted a bid or proposal to provide them, during the 12 months before the Termination Date.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which Executive's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

## 2. Employment; Position and Duties

**2.1 Position.** The Company employs Executive as its Chief Operating Officer, effective on the Effective Date. Executive reports to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company or another officer designated by Parent.

**2.2 Duties.** Executive has the duties and authority customary for a chief operating officer of a pupil transportation business of the Company's size, and other duties consistent with that position that the Company assigns. Those duties include oversight of daily fleet operations, depot management, driver hiring and compliance, and the integration of the Company's route planning and driver screening functions following the Closing.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time and attention to the Company. Executive may serve on civic, charitable or industry association boards, and manage personal investments, if those activities do not materially interfere with Executive's duties or breach Sections 7 through 9.

**2.4 Location.** Executive's principal place of work is the Company's principal office. Executive will travel to the Company's depots and District sites as reasonably required.

## 3. Term

**3.1 Initial Term.** The term of employment begins on the Effective Date and continues until the third anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless ended earlier under Section 5.

**3.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term together with any renewal is the "**Term**."

## 4. Compensation and Benefits

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $285,000 ("**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Company's board of directors (the "**Board**") will review Base Salary at least annually and may increase, but not decrease (except as allowed under clause (a) of the definition of Good Reason), Base Salary.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 40% of Base Salary. The actual bonus depends on achievement of performance objectives set by the Board after consulting with Executive. These objectives will include operating safety, on-time performance and District Contract retention metrics. The bonus for the fiscal year that includes the Effective Date will be prorated. Any bonus earned is paid no later than March 15 of the year after the fiscal year to which it relates. Except as provided in Section 6, Executive must be employed on the payment date to earn the bonus.

**4.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $150,000, in two equal installments of $75,000. The first installment is payable within 30 days after the first anniversary of the Effective Date, and the second within 30 days after the second anniversary. Each installment is payable only if Executive remains employed on the applicable anniversary, except as provided in Section 6.2.

**4.4 Equity Award.** Subject to approval by Parent's board of directors, Executive will receive an award under Parent's equity incentive plan. The award's terms are governed by that plan and the applicable award agreement.

**4.5 Benefits.** Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company or Parent maintains for its senior executives generally, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.6 Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to 25 days of paid time off per year, in addition to Company holidays, under the Company's policies.

**4.7 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing Executive's duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.8 Vehicle.** The Company shall provide Executive with a monthly vehicle allowance of $900, given the travel required among the Company's depots and District sites.

## 5. Termination of Employment

**5.1 Death or Disability.** Executive's employment ends automatically on Executive's death. The Company may end Executive's employment upon Disability by written notice.

**5.2 By the Company.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause or without Cause at any time by written notice. A notice of non-renewal by the Company under Section 3.2 is a termination without Cause effective at the end of the then-current term.

**5.3 By Executive.** Executive may resign for Good Reason under the procedure in the definition of that term. Executive may resign without Good Reason on at least 60 days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period without the waiver being treated as a termination by the Company.

**5.4 Resignation from Positions.** When Executive's employment ends for any reason, Executive is deemed to resign from all officer and director positions with the Company, Parent and their affiliates.

## 6. Payments on Termination

**6.1 Accrued Obligations.** When Executive's employment ends for any reason, the Company shall pay Executive:

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the Termination Date;

(b) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid;

(c) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent payable under Company policy; and

(d) unreimbursed business expenses properly incurred.

These are the "**Accrued Obligations**."

**6.2 Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following in addition to the Accrued Obligations:

(a) continued payment of Base Salary for 12 months after the Termination Date, in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices;

(b) a prorated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance, payable when bonuses are paid to other executives;

(c) any unpaid installment of the retention bonus under Section 4.3, payable within 60 days after the Termination Date; and

(d) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment of the employer portion of premiums for up to 12 months, or until Executive becomes eligible for group health coverage from another employer, if earlier.

**6.3 Release.** The payments and benefits in Section 6.2 are conditioned on Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, with the release becoming effective within 60 days after the Termination Date. They are also conditioned on Executive's continued compliance with Sections 7 through 9. Payments that would otherwise be made before the release becomes effective will be paid in a lump sum on the first payroll date after it does. If the 60-day period spans two calendar years, payment begins in the second year.

**6.4 Other Terminations.** If Executive's employment ends because of death or Disability, termination for Cause or resignation without Good Reason, Executive receives only the Accrued Obligations.

## 7. Confidential Information

**7.1 Nondisclosure.** Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, during or after employment, except as required to perform Executive's duties or as required by Law. "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information of the Company, Parent or their affiliates. It includes District pricing and bid information, route data, driver and student records, safety and testing records, the Routing Software, financial information and business plans. It does not include information that becomes publicly available other than through Executive's breach.

**7.2 Student and Driver Records.** Executive shall handle student information and driver background-check and drug and alcohol testing records only in compliance with applicable Law, the District Contracts and Company policy.

**7.3 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of Law to any Governmental Authority, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower Laws. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret Law for disclosing a trade secret (a) in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of Law, or (b) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

**7.4 Return of Property.** When Executive's employment ends, Executive shall return all Company property and Confidential Information in Executive's possession.

## 8. Work Product

All inventions, software, route designs, processes and other work product that Executive creates, alone or with others, in the course of employment and relating to the Business ("**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, Executive assigns it to the Company. Executive shall sign documents reasonably requested to perfect the Company's rights. This Section 8 does not apply to any invention that qualifies fully under Section 2870 of the California Labor Code or any similar applicable Law.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

**9.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, within the Restricted Territory, own, manage, operate, be employed by or provide services to any Person engaged in the Business. This Section 9.1 does not prohibit passive ownership of up to 2% of a publicly traded company.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation of Districts.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any District that was a party to a District Contract during the 12 months before the Termination Date to terminate, not renew or reduce its business with the Company. Executive also shall not provide pupil transportation services to any such District for anyone other than the Company.

**9.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any employee of the Company, including any driver, monitor, dispatcher or mechanic, who was employed by the Company during the 12 months before the solicitation or hiring. General advertisements not targeted at Company employees do not breach this Section 9.3.

**9.4 Non-Disparagement.** During and after employment, Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, Parent or their affiliates, officers or directors, subject to Section 7.3.

**9.5 Reasonableness; Reformation.** Executive acknowledges that the covenants in this Section 9 are reasonable and necessary to protect the goodwill of the Company acquired by Parent under the Purchase Agreement. If a court finds any covenant unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**9.6 Remedies.** A breach of Sections 7 through 9 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, in addition to any other remedy, without posting bond. The Restricted Period is extended by any period during which Executive is in breach of Section 9.

## 10. Section 409A

This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and will be interpreted accordingly. Each payment under this Agreement is a separate payment. A termination of employment means a "separation from service" under Section 409A. If Executive is a "specified employee" on the Termination Date, any payment of deferred compensation that is payable upon separation from service and would otherwise be paid within six months after it will instead be paid on the first business day after the six-month period ends. Reimbursements are paid no later than the end of the year after the year in which the expense is incurred.

## 11. General

**11.1 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all Taxes required to be withheld by Law.

**11.2 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered by hand, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its principal office, attention: Chief Executive Officer, with a copy to Parent. Notices to Executive go to the most recent address in the Company's records.

**11.3 Assignment.** Executive may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign it to Parent, to any affiliate of Parent or to any successor to all or substantially all of the Company's business.

**11.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**11.5 Severability.** Subject to Section 9.5, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**11.6 Survival.** Sections 6 through 11 survive the termination of Executive's employment.

**11.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

**11.8 Jurisdiction.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**11.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
