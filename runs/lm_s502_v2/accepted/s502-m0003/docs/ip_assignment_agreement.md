# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and between **Fapul Family Transportation Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Kestrelbrook Student Transportation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), among **Batairt Student Transit Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as the Company, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of Assignee from Assignor. The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs on the Effective Date.

B. Assignor owns the Routing Software. Assignee has used the Routing Software in operating its pupil transportation business.

C. Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing and to cooperate with Buyer and Assignee in transferring all source code, documentation and data relating to the Routing Software.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Assigned IP**" means the following, in each case anywhere in the world:

(a) the Routing Software, including all versions, releases, modifications, enhancements and derivative works of it in existence on or before the Effective Date;

(b) all copyrights and copyrightable works in the Routing Software, all registrations and applications for registration of those copyrights, and all renewals and extensions of them;

(c) all trade secrets, know-how, algorithms, models, methods, processes and other confidential information embodied in or relating to the Routing Software;

(d) all inventions, whether or not patentable, conceived or reduced to practice in developing the Routing Software, and all patents and patent applications claiming any of them;

(e) all Route Data;

(f) all Documentation;

(g) all rights to sue for and recover damages and other remedies for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the foregoing; and

(h) all income, royalties and payments now or later due in respect of any of the foregoing.

"**Documentation**" means all user manuals, technical specifications, design documents, architecture diagrams, configuration files, build scripts, release notes, training materials and other materials describing or relating to the design, operation, use, maintenance or support of the Routing Software.

"**Route Data**" means all route maps, stop locations, bell-time schedules, run and tier configurations, geocoding and street network data, District-specific parameters and other data created, compiled or maintained in or for the Routing Software in connection with Assignee's business, in each case to the extent owned by Assignor.

"**Source Code**" means the human-readable form of the computer code of the Routing Software, together with all comments, programmer notes, libraries and other materials necessary for a reasonably skilled programmer to understand, maintain, modify, compile and deploy it.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably and unconditionally sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Liens.

**2.2 Recordation.** Assignee may record this Agreement, or a short-form confirmatory assignment consistent with it, with the United States Copyright Office, the United States Patent and Trademark Office or any other Governmental Authority. Assignor shall execute any short-form assignment reasonably requested by Assignee for that purpose.

**2.3 Moral Rights.** To the extent any moral rights or similar rights in the Assigned IP cannot be assigned under applicable Law, Assignor waives, and shall cause its personnel to waive, those rights and agrees not to assert them against Assignee, Buyer or their successors, assigns or licensees.

**2.4 No Retained Rights.** Assignor retains no right, title, interest or license in or to the Assigned IP. Any access to or use of the Routing Software by Assignor's personnel after the Effective Date is governed by Section 6.4 of the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. Delivery

**3.1 Delivery at Closing.** On the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee:

(a) a complete and current copy of the Source Code and object code of the Routing Software, including the version in production use by Assignee on the Effective Date;

(b) all Documentation; and

(c) all Route Data, in its native format and in a reasonably usable export format.

**3.2 Repositories and Credentials.** On the Effective Date, Assignor shall transfer to Assignee administrative control of all source code repositories, issue-tracking systems, build environments, hosting accounts and domain registrations used exclusively for the Routing Software. Assignor shall deliver all passwords, encryption keys, access credentials and license keys needed to access and operate them.

**3.3 Copies.** After completing delivery under Sections 3.1 and 3.2, Assignor shall not retain any copy of the Source Code, Documentation or Route Data, except as needed to perform the Route Planning Services under the Transition Services Agreement. Within 30 days after the end of the Service Period for the Route Planning Services, Assignor shall destroy any remaining copies and certify that destruction to Assignee in writing.

## 4. Further Assurances; Cooperation

**4.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request, and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as Assignee reasonably requests to vest, perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. Assignee shall reimburse Assignor's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of doing so.

**4.2 Personnel.** Assignor shall cause each of its current and former employees and contractors who contributed to the development of the Routing Software to execute any confirmatory assignment reasonably requested by Assignee, to the extent Assignor has the right to require it.

**4.3 Knowledge Transfer.** Assignor shall make its personnel with knowledge of the Routing Software reasonably available to answer questions from Assignee's personnel about the Source Code and Documentation. Knowledge transfer and training relating to route planning are provided as part of the Route Planning Services under Part 3 of Schedule A to the Transition Services Agreement.

**4.4 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely to execute and file any instrument described in Section 2.2 or Section 4.1 if Assignor fails to do so within 15 Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

## 5. Confidentiality

From and after the Effective Date, Assignor shall hold in confidence all trade secrets and other confidential information included in the Assigned IP. Assignor shall not use or disclose that information, except to perform the Route Planning Services under the Transition Services Agreement or as required by Law, after giving Assignee prompt notice where lawful.

## 6. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

**6.1 Representations and Indemnification.** The representations and warranties of Assignor concerning the Routing Software are set out in the Purchase Agreement, including Section 4.15. The remedies for any inaccuracy in or breach of them are governed by Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 No Additional Consideration.** The consideration for the assignment in this Agreement is included in the Purchase Price. No additional payment is due from Assignee or Buyer.

## 7. General

**7.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignee are to be sent to Buyer's address.

**7.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign this Agreement, and any of the Assigned IP, without Assignor's consent.

**7.3 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce it.

**7.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**7.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**7.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Federal Law governs questions concerning the registration and recordation of copyrights and patents.

**7.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any Proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**7.8 Specific Performance.** Assignor acknowledges that its failure to deliver the Assigned IP under Section 3, or to perform its obligations under Sections 4 and 5, would cause Assignee irreparable harm. Assignee is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief to enforce those obligations, in addition to any other remedy.

**7.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**7.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## SCHEDULE 1: ROUTING SOFTWARE

**Part A. Software**

1. The route planning and optimization application used by Assignee for annual and mid-year route design, including the route optimization engine, tiering and bell-time modeling modules, and the special-needs and out-of-district routing module.
2. The stop management and geocoding module, including the street network and boundary data layers maintained for each District served by Assignee.
3. The trip scheduling module used for field trip and athletic trip scheduling.
4. The driver run-sheet generation and export tools, and the interfaces between the Routing Software and Assignee's dispatch and payroll systems.
5. All related databases, scripts, configuration files and administrative tools.

**Part B. Route Data**

Route Data for each District served by Assignee, including the Districts party to the District Contracts listed on Schedule 4.12 to the Purchase Agreement, for the 2024–2025, 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 school years.

**Part C. Repositories and Accounts**

All source code repositories, build environments, hosting accounts and domain registrations used exclusively for the Routing Software, as identified in the delivery memorandum delivered by Assignor under Section 3.2.
