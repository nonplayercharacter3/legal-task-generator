# ORDER FORM NO. 3

**Order Form No. 3** (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of January 1, 2026 (the "**Order Form Date**") between **Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC** ("**Supplier**").

It is issued under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**") and is an Order Form for the purposes of Sections 1.1 and 3.2 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. ORDER FORM PERIOD

**1.1 Period.** This Order Form applies to Purchase Orders issued from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026 (the "**Order Form Period**"). Purchase Orders accepted during the Order Form Period continue to be governed by this Order Form until fully performed.

**1.2 Prior Order Forms.** Order Form No. 2 expires on December 31, 2025. Purchase Orders accepted under Order Form No. 2 before that date continue to be governed by Order Form No. 2.

## 3. PRODUCTS AND PRICING

**3.1 Reagent.** Supplier shall supply Reagent meeting the specifications in Section 3.2 of the Quality Agreement at the following prices:

| Delivery Location | Delivery Method | Price (USD per metric ton, 50% w/w NaOH) |
|---|---|---|
| Rotterdam / Antwerp | Barge or truck to Vessel | 545 |
| Singapore | Barge to Vessel | 520 |
| Houston / New York / Savannah | Truck to Vessel or terminal | 560 |
| Other ports | DAP Vessel location | Rotterdam price + documented freight, at cost |

**3.2 Reagent Price Adjustment.** The Reagent prices in Section 3.1 are fixed until June 30, 2026. On July 1, 2026, each price may be adjusted by the percentage change in the ICIS caustic soda (liquid, 50%) contract price assessment for the relevant region between the December 2025 and the June 2026 assessments. The adjustment may not exceed plus or minus 8%. Supplier shall give Customer written notice of any adjustment, with supporting calculations, by June 15, 2026.

**3.3 Minimum Delivery Quantities.** The minimum quantity per Reagent delivery is 20 metric tons for barge deliveries and 10 metric tons for truck deliveries. Deliveries below those quantities carry a small-delivery surcharge of USD 1,250 per delivery.

**3.4 Spare Parts.** Supplier shall supply EGCS spare parts at Supplier's published list prices in effect on the Order Form Date (Laguth Price List LMT-PL-2026-01), less a discount of 18%. Supplier shall not increase list prices for Customer during the Order Form Period.

## 4. SERVICES AND RATES

**4.1 Services.** Supplier shall perform Services for the Vessels under this Order Form in accordance with Section 6 of the Agreement and the Service Level Schedule.

**4.2 Day Rates.**

| Personnel | Standard Day Rate (USD) | Overtime / Weekend / Holiday Day Rate (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Service Engineer | 1,650 | 2,150 |
| Service Engineer | 1,350 | 1,750 |
| Commissioning / Controls Specialist | 1,800 | 2,350 |
| Remote Technical Support | 175 per hour | 225 per hour |

A day is any period of up to ten hours on board a Vessel or in transit. Travel days are charged at 75% of the standard day rate.

**4.3 Expenses.** Supplier may invoice reasonable travel, accommodation, launch and visa expenses at cost, without mark-up, supported by receipts. Air travel is economy class for flights under eight hours.

**4.4 Annual Inspection Program.** Supplier shall perform one annual onboard inspection of each Vessel's EGCS during the Order Form Period, at a fixed fee of USD 9,800 per Vessel inclusive of personnel time and excluding expenses under Section 4.3. Customer shall nominate the port and approximate date for each inspection at least 30 days in advance.

**4.5 Crew Training.** Supplier shall provide up to two onboard crew training sessions per Vessel during the Order Form Period, which may be combined with the annual inspection, at no additional charge beyond expenses under Section 4.3.

**4.6 Retrofit Work.** Retrofit and other project-based Services are not covered by this Order Form and are performed only under an SOW in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

## 5. ORDERING AND INVOICING

**5.1 Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders in accordance with Section 3.3 of the Agreement. Each Purchase Order shall identify the Vessel by name and IMO number, the delivery location and the requested delivery date.

**5.2 Forecast.** Customer's initial 12-month non-binding forecast under Section 3.4 of the Agreement estimates Reagent consumption of approximately 14,500 metric tons across the Vessels during the Order Form Period.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Agreement. Payment terms are as set out in Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Invoices shall be sent to accountspayable@nironoceanlines.com with a copy to Customer's fleet technical department.
