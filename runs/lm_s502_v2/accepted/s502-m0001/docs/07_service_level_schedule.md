# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 between:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 ("**Customer**"); and

**Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Houston, Texas 77029 ("**Supplier**").

This Schedule supplements the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement and is the Service Level Schedule referred to in Sections 1.1 and 6.2 of the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Schedule:

"**Critical Spare Part**" means a spare part identified as critical in the applicable Order Form.

"**Incident**" means any failure, malfunction or degraded performance of an EGCS on a Vessel, or any shortfall in Products, that Customer reports to the Service Desk.

"**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month during the Term.

"**Response Time**" means the period from the time Customer reports an Incident to the Service Desk until a qualified Supplier service engineer begins remote diagnosis of it with the Vessel's crew or Customer's fleet technical department.

"**Service Credit**" means a credit calculated under Section 5.

"**Service Desk**" means the Laguth Global Service Desk, which Supplier operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

"**Severity Level**" means the classification of an Incident under Section 2.

"**Vessel Fees**" means, for a Vessel and a Measurement Period, the Fees invoiced for Products delivered to, and Services performed for, that Vessel in that Measurement Period.

"**Vessels**" means the vessels listed in Annex 1 to this Schedule.

## 2. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION

**2.1 Severity Levels.** Customer shall assign a Severity Level to each Incident when it reports it, acting reasonably:

| Severity Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Severity 1 (Critical) | The EGCS is inoperable or cannot maintain compliance with MARPOL Annex VI or applicable washwater discharge limits, so that the Vessel must switch to compliant fuel, deviate, or faces detention or loss of trading. |
| Severity 2 (Major) | The EGCS operates with significantly degraded performance, or a monitoring or control component has failed, so that compliance is at risk within 72 hours if not corrected. |
| Severity 3 (Minor) | The EGCS operates in compliance, but a component is faulty or performance is degraded in a way that does not put compliance at risk in the short term. |
| Severity 4 (Request) | Requests for information, documentation, training or planned maintenance. |

**2.2 Reclassification.** Supplier may propose a different Severity Level by notice to Customer's fleet technical department. Customer's classification applies unless Customer agrees to the change in writing, and the Parties shall resolve any disagreement through their technical contacts.

## 3. SERVICE LEVELS

**3.1 Response Times.** Supplier shall meet the following Response Times for each Incident on a Vessel:

| Severity Level | Response Time | Target Remote Resolution or Action Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | 1 hour | Written action plan within 4 hours |
| Severity 2 | 4 hours | Written action plan within 24 hours |
| Severity 3 | 1 Business Day | Resolution or action plan within 5 Business Days |
| Severity 4 | 2 Business Days | As agreed |

**3.2 Onboard Attendance.** If a Severity 1 or Severity 2 Incident on a Vessel cannot be resolved remotely, Supplier shall have a qualified service engineer attend the Vessel as follows:

(a) at the Vessel's current or next port of call in Northern Europe, the U.S. Gulf or East Coast, Singapore or Hong Kong: within 48 hours after Customer's request for Severity 1, and within 72 hours for Severity 2; and

(b) at any other port of call: within 96 hours after Customer's request for Severity 1, and within 120 hours for Severity 2,

or, in each case, on the Vessel's arrival at that port if later. Supplier's obligations under this Section 3.2 are subject to Customer arranging access to the Vessel under Section 6.3 of the Agreement.

**3.3 Critical Spares Availability.** Supplier shall maintain the stock of Critical Spare Parts required by Section 3.5 of the Agreement and the applicable Order Form so that, in each Measurement Period, at least 98% of Purchase Order lines for Critical Spare Parts for the Vessels are dispatched from a Supplier depot within 24 hours after acceptance of the Purchase Order.

**3.4 Critical Spares Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Critical Spare Parts for a Vessel to the location stated in the Purchase Order within the following times after acceptance of the Purchase Order:

(a) Houston, Rotterdam or Singapore, or a port within 500 nautical miles of one of them: 48 hours; and

(b) any other port: 96 hours.

**3.5 Reagent On-Time Delivery.** In each Measurement Period, Supplier shall deliver at least 97% of Reagent deliveries for the Vessels within the delivery window stated in the relevant Purchase Order. No Reagent delivery for a Vessel may be more than 24 hours late.

**3.6 Service Desk Availability.** The Service Desk shall answer at least 95% of telephone calls within 60 seconds in each Measurement Period, and shall acknowledge each email Incident report within 30 minutes.

## 4. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

**4.1 Records.** Supplier shall log each Incident, Purchase Order and Reagent delivery for the Vessels in its service management system. Each log shall record the time of report or acceptance, the Severity Level, each Response Time, attendance and delivery time, and the time of resolution.

**4.2 Monthly Report.** Within ten Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a report that:

(a) states Supplier's performance against each service level in Section 3, by Vessel;

(b) identifies each failure to meet a service level, with its cause and the corrective action taken;

(c) calculates the Service Credits due for that Measurement Period; and

(d) lists open Severity 1 and Severity 2 Incidents.

**4.3 Verification.** Customer may verify any report against its own records and request supporting data, which Supplier shall provide within five Business Days. The Parties shall correct any error in a report in the next monthly report.

**4.4 Quarterly Review.** The Parties' technical contacts shall meet at least once each quarter to review performance under this Schedule. They may hold this meeting together with the quarterly meeting under Section 2.2 of the Quality Agreement.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

**5.1 Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet a service level for a Vessel, Supplier shall credit Customer the following Service Credits:

| Service Level | Failure | Service Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Response Time (Severity 1) | Each Incident not met | 5% of Vessel Fees for the Measurement Period, and a further 2% for each additional full hour of delay |
| Response Time (Severity 2) | Each Incident not met | 3% of Vessel Fees for the Measurement Period |
| Onboard Attendance (Section 3.2) | Each attendance not met | 10% of Vessel Fees for the Measurement Period, and USD 2,500 for each additional 24 hours of delay |
| Critical Spares Delivery (Section 3.4) | Each Purchase Order line not met | 10% of the price of the line, and a further 5% for each additional 24 hours of delay |
| Reagent Delivery (Section 3.5) | Each delivery more than 24 hours late | USD 3,000 per delivery |

**5.2 Fleet-Wide Service Levels.** If Supplier fails to meet the service level in Section 3.3, 3.5 (as to the 97% threshold) or 3.6 in a Measurement Period, Supplier shall credit Customer 2% of the aggregate Vessel Fees for all Vessels for that Measurement Period for each service level missed.

**5.3 Monthly Cap.** The aggregate Service Credits payable for any Measurement Period shall not exceed 20% of the aggregate Vessel Fees for all Vessels for that Measurement Period.

**5.4 Application.** Supplier shall apply Service Credits against the next invoice issued after the monthly report in which they are calculated. Any Service Credits outstanding on termination or expiry of the Agreement shall be paid to Customer within 30 days.

**5.5 Exceptions.** Supplier is not responsible for a failure to meet a service level to the extent it is caused by:

(a) a force majeure event under Section 16.3 of the Agreement;

(b) Customer's failure to provide access to a Vessel under Section 6.3 of the Agreement, or a Vessel's master refusing attendance for safety or operational reasons; or

(c) Customer's failure to provide information reasonably requested by the Service Desk to diagnose an Incident,

provided that Supplier notifies Customer of the relevant cause promptly after becoming aware of it.

**5.6 Other Remedies.** Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Products and Services. They are without prejudice to Customer's other rights and remedies under the Agreement, including under Sections 5.3, 9, 11 and 14.1 of the Agreement. Any damages recovered for a failure shall be reduced by the Service Credits paid for that failure.

## 6. PERSISTENT FAILURE

**6.1 Remediation Plan.** If Supplier fails to meet the same service level in any two consecutive Measurement Periods, or in any three Measurement Periods within a rolling 12-month period, Supplier shall deliver to Customer within ten Business Days a written remediation plan. Supplier shall implement the plan at its own cost.

**6.2 Material Breach.** If Supplier fails to meet a Severity 1 Response Time or onboard attendance service level in any three Measurement Periods within a rolling 12-month period, that failure is a material breach of the Agreement for the purposes of Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

## 7. GENERAL

**7.1 Liability.** Service Credits count toward Supplier's aggregate liability under Section 12.3 of the Agreement.

**7.2 Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 16.5 of the Agreement.

**7.3 Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Schedule is governed by the law, and subject to the jurisdiction, set out in Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement.

**7.4 Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
