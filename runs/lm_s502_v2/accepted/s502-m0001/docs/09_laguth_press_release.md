# Laguth Marine Technologies Expands Scrubber Reagent Supply Network to Algeciras and Busan

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**HOUSTON, Texas – June 17, 2026** – Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC ("Laguth Marine"), a supplier of exhaust gas cleaning system (EGCS) spare parts, alkaline reagent and technical services to the global shipping industry, today announced that it is extending its reagent supply network to the ports of Algeciras, Spain and Busan, South Korea. Laguth Marine is a subsidiary of Laguth Group Holdings, Inc.

## 1. New Supply Locations

From July 1, 2026, Laguth Marine will offer barge delivery of sodium hydroxide reagent (50% w/w) to vessels calling at Algeciras and Busan. The new locations add to Laguth Marine's existing depots in Houston, Rotterdam and Singapore.

Algeciras serves vessels transiting the Strait of Gibraltar. Busan is a major transshipment hub for transpacific and intra-Asia container services. Both ports are frequent bunkering and stores calls for scrubber-fitted container ships and bulk carriers.

Supply at the new locations will run from local storage arrangements operated by Laguth Marine and its affiliates. It will follow the same quality controls that apply across the Laguth Marine network, including batch testing at an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory and a certificate of analysis with each delivery.

## 2. Why It Matters for Operators

"Our customers increasingly ask us to deliver reagent where their vessels actually call, rather than build voyages around our depots," said Daniel R. Kessler, President of Laguth Marine Technologies. "Adding Algeciras and Busan cuts deviation and small-lot deliveries for operators on the Asia–Europe and transpacific trades. It also helps crews keep closed-loop and hybrid systems running in waters where open-loop discharge is restricted."

The expansion supports Laguth Marine's growing base of hybrid and closed-loop EGCS installations. Those systems use alkaline reagent to neutralize washwater and keep it within discharge limits while maintaining compliance with MARPOL Annex VI.

## 3. Laguth Marine Service Network

Laguth Marine's integrated service offering includes:

- **Critical spares stock** held at the Houston, Rotterdam and Singapore depots, including washwater pumps, dosing pumps, spray nozzles, analyzer modules and control system components;
- **The Laguth Global Service Desk**, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for remote troubleshooting of EGCS incidents;
- **Onboard technical services**, including annual inspections, commissioning support and crew training; and
- **Retrofit engineering**, including open-loop to hybrid conversions carried out during scheduled drydockings.

## 4. Availability

Operators can order reagent deliveries at Algeciras and Busan through their usual Laguth Marine commercial contact or through the Laguth Global Service Desk. Pricing and delivery terms for existing customers are set by their supply arrangements with Laguth Marine.
