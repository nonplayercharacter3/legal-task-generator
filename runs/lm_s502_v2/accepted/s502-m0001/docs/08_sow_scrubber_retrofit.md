# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: EGCS HYBRID CONVERSION RETROFIT

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026 (the "**SOW Date**") between **Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC** ("**Supplier**").

It is issued under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 1, 2025 (as amended, the "**Agreement**"). It is an SOW for the purposes of Sections 1.1 and 6.4 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**1.1 Purpose.** Customer wishes to convert the open-loop EGCS on two Vessels to hybrid (open-loop and closed-loop) operation. The conversion will allow those Vessels to keep using their EGCS in ports and coastal waters where open-loop washwater discharge is restricted.

**1.2 Retrofit Vessels.** This SOW covers the following Vessels (each a "**Retrofit Vessel**"):

| No. | Vessel | IMO Number | Flag | Existing EGCS Type | Converted EGCS Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Niron Galatea | 9728417 | Panama | Open-loop | Hybrid |
| 2 | Niron Halcyon | 9728429 | Panama | Open-loop | Hybrid |

**1.3 Project.** Supplier shall perform the engineering, supply, installation, commissioning, documentation and training Services described in Section 2 for each Retrofit Vessel (the "**Project**").

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

**2.1 Engineering.** For each Retrofit Vessel, Supplier shall:

(a) carry out a pre-retrofit onboard survey, including a 3D laser scan of the EGCS room, funnel casing and relevant tank spaces;

(b) prepare the basic and detailed design for the conversion. This includes the process and instrumentation diagrams, piping and electrical layouts, structural reinforcement drawings, and the arrangement of the process tank, the alkali storage and dosing system and the washwater treatment unit;

(c) prepare stability and load calculations to the extent affected by the conversion; and

(d) submit all drawings and calculations requiring approval to the Vessel's classification society and flag administration, and obtain those approvals before installation begins.

**2.2 Equipment Supply.** Supplier shall supply for each Retrofit Vessel the equipment listed in Annex A (the "**Retrofit Equipment**"). The Retrofit Equipment includes a closed-loop circulation module, a washwater treatment unit with bleed-off monitoring, an alkali dosing system, sludge handling equipment, the associated pumps, valves, instrumentation and piping, and an upgraded EGCS control system. Retrofit Equipment is a Product for the purposes of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement.

**2.3 Installation.** Supplier shall install the Retrofit Equipment during the drydock periods described in Section 3. Supplier shall provide supervising engineers and installation personnel. It shall also coordinate with the shipyard on hot work, lifting, piping, cabling and structural work in accordance with the approved design.

**2.4 Commissioning and Testing.** Supplier shall commission the converted EGCS on each Retrofit Vessel and carry out:

(a) harbor acceptance tests at the shipyard; and

(b) sea trials demonstrating operation in open-loop and closed-loop modes, including emission ratio and washwater discharge monitoring, in accordance with the test program in Annex B.

**2.5 Documentation.** For each Retrofit Vessel, Supplier shall deliver:

(a) a revised EGCS Technical Manual and Onboard Monitoring Manual reflecting hybrid operation, approved by the classification society or flag administration as required;

(b) as-built drawings and an updated spare parts list;

(c) updated SOx Emission Compliance Plan inputs for Customer's use; and

(d) the certificates of conformity, class approvals and test reports required by Section 7.2 of the Agreement and Section 4.2 of the Quality Agreement.

**2.6 Training.** Supplier shall provide onboard training on hybrid operation, closed-loop maintenance, alkali handling and sludge management. The training shall be given to each Retrofit Vessel's engineering officers during sea trials, and to Customer's fleet technical department at Customer's offices or by video conference.

**2.7 Exclusions.** The following are outside the scope of this SOW: shipyard dock fees, berthing and general yard services; gas-freeing and tank cleaning not required by the conversion; and repairs to existing equipment found to be defective during the survey, unless agreed under Section 6.

Customer shall confirm each drydock period at least 60 days in advance and shall notify Supplier promptly of any change.

**3.2 Milestones.** Supplier shall achieve the following milestones for each Retrofit Vessel:

| Milestone | Target Date |
|---|---|
| M1: Pre-retrofit survey completed | 30 days after the SOW Date |
| M2: Design approved by class and flag | 60 days before the drydock start |

**3.4 Delay.** If Supplier expects to miss a milestone, it shall notify Customer immediately with a recovery plan. If installation for a Retrofit Vessel is not completed within the planned drydock duration for reasons attributable to Supplier, Supplier shall reimburse Customer the shipyard's documented daily charges for the extended drydock. Supplier shall also pay liquidated damages of USD 15,000 for each day of extension, up to a maximum of 10% of the Contract Price for that Retrofit Vessel. These liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of Customer's loss of use of the Retrofit Vessel. They are Customer's sole monetary remedy for that delay, other than reimbursement of shipyard charges, unless the delay exceeds 30 days.

**7.2 Acceptance Certificate.** Customer shall issue an acceptance certificate within five Business Days after the acceptance criteria are met. ## ANNEX A: RETROFIT EQUIPMENT (PER RETROFIT VESSEL)

1. Closed-loop process tank with level and temperature instrumentation.
2. Closed-loop circulation pumps (2 × 100%), with motors and variable frequency drives.
3. Plate heat exchanger for process water cooling.
4. Alkali dosing system, including dosing pumps (2 × 100%), alkali storage tank connections and day tank.
5. Washwater treatment unit (hydrocyclone separation) with bleed-off monitoring (pH, PAH, turbidity).
6. Sludge tank connections and sludge transfer pump.
7. Mode-changeover valves with actuators, and associated piping and fittings.
8. Upgraded EGCS control system with hybrid-mode software and integration with the existing CEMS.
9. Cabling, junction boxes and local control panels.
10. Commissioning spares and a two-year operational spares kit.

## ANNEX B: TEST PROGRAM SUMMARY

1. **Harbor acceptance tests:** function tests of all pumps, valves, alarms and interlocks; control system mode changeover; dosing system calibration; instrument loop checks.
2. **Sea trials, open-loop mode:** continuous operation for at least 4 hours at 50%, 75% and 85% MCR; SO₂/CO₂ emission ratio within the limit equivalent to 0.50% m/m sulphur fuel; washwater pH, PAH and turbidity within applicable discharge limits.
3. **Sea trials, closed-loop mode:** continuous operation for at least 4 hours at 50%, 75% and 85% MCR; emission ratio as above; bleed-off monitoring within applicable limits; alkali consumption recorded.
4. **Mode changeover:** at least two changeovers in each direction under load, without loss of compliance.
5. **Reporting:** Supplier shall deliver a signed test report to Customer and the classification society within five Business Days after completion of sea trials.
