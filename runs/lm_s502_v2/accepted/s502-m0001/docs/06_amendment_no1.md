# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 3, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") between:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 ("**Customer**"); and

**Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Houston, Texas 77029 ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to a Supply Agreement dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), and to Order Form No. 3 under the Agreement dated January 1, 2026 ("**Order Form No. 3**").

B. Section 4.1 of the Agreement provides that Reagent prices may be adjusted only as stated in the applicable Order Form or by written amendment. Section 3.2 of Order Form No. 3 provides for an index-based adjustment of Reagent prices on July 1, 2026.

C. The Parties have agreed to replace that index-based adjustment with fixed Reagent prices for the remainder of the Order Form Period, to add delivery locations, and to make certain other changes to the Agreement, in each case as set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement or, where the context requires, in Order Form No. 3.

**1.2 Amendment.** This Amendment is a written instrument amending the Agreement for the purposes of Section 16.5 of the Agreement. It is also a written amendment for the purposes of Section 4.1 of the Agreement.

## 2. REAGENT PRICING

**2.1 Revised Reagent Prices.** With effect from July 1, 2026, the table in Section 3.1 of Order Form No. 3 is deleted and replaced with the following:

| Delivery Location | Delivery Method | Price (USD per metric ton, 50% w/w NaOH) |
|---|---|---|
| Rotterdam / Antwerp | Barge or truck to Vessel | 572 |
| Singapore | Barge to Vessel | 541 |
| Houston / New York / Savannah | Truck to Vessel or terminal | 588 |
| Algeciras | Barge to Vessel | 579 |
| Busan | Barge to Vessel | 553 |
| Other ports | DAP Vessel location | Rotterdam price + documented freight, at cost |

**2.2 Price Adjustment Mechanism.** Section 3.2 of Order Form No. 3 is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "**3.2 Reagent Prices Fixed.** The Reagent prices in Section 3.1 are fixed from July 1, 2026 through the end of the Order Form Period. No index-based or other adjustment applies to those prices during the Order Form Period."

**2.3 Purchase Orders.** The revised prices in Section 2.1 apply to all Reagent delivered on or after July 1, 2026, including Reagent delivered under Purchase Orders accepted before that date. Reagent delivered before July 1, 2026 is invoiced at the prices in effect on the date of delivery.

**2.4 Notice.** Supplier's obligation under Section 3.2 of Order Form No. 3 (as in effect before this Amendment) to give notice of an index-based adjustment by June 15, 2026 is released.

## 3. DELIVERY AND SMALL DELIVERIES

**3.1 New Delivery Locations.** Supplier shall supply Reagent at Algeciras and Busan from depots or local storage arrangements of Supplier or its Affiliates. Deliveries at those locations are subject to Section 5 of the Agreement, the Quality Agreement and Section 3.6 of Order Form No. 3.

**3.2 Small-Delivery Surcharge.** In Section 3.3 of Order Form No. 3, "USD 1,250" is deleted and replaced with "USD 1,100".

**3.3 Forecasts.** Within 15 Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, Customer shall provide Supplier with an updated non-binding forecast under Section 3.4 of the Agreement that identifies expected Reagent requirements at Algeciras and Busan for the remainder of the Order Form Period.

## 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**4.1 Exclusions.** Section 12.4 is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "**12.4 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.3 shall not apply to Supplier's obligations under Section 11.1, or to liability arising from a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct."

**4.2 Section References.** References in the Agreement to Section 12.4 are references to Section 12.4 as amended by this Section 4.

## 5. NOTICES

**5.1 Supplier Notice Details.** In Section 16.4 of the Agreement, the Supplier notice details are amended to add, after "Attention: Chief Commercial Officer, with a copy to Legal Department", the words ", and by email to legalnotices@laguthmarine.com".

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Effect of Amendment.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement and Order Form No. 3 remain unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Effective Date, references in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and references in any other document to the Agreement, are references to the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

**6.2 Representations.** Each Party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it.

**6.3 Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Amendment and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the law, and subject to the jurisdiction, set out in Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement.

**6.4 Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
