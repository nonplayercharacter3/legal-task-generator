# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 between:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 ("**Customer**"); and

**Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Houston, Texas 77029 ("**Supplier**").

This DPA supplements the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement and is the Data Processing Addendum referred to in Sections 1.1 and 13.5 of the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this DPA:

"**Customer Personal Data**" means any Personal Data that Supplier or its Sub-processors process on behalf of Customer or its Affiliates in performing the Agreement.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations on privacy, data protection and data security that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data. These include, where applicable, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "**GDPR**"), the GDPR as it forms part of the law of the United Kingdom (the "**UK GDPR**"), the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012, and applicable United States federal and state privacy laws.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Customer Personal Data relates.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to a Data Subject, and includes "personal data" and "personal information" as defined in Data Protection Laws.

"**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.

"**Processing**" has the meaning given in Data Protection Laws, and "**process**" and "**processed**" are construed accordingly.

"**Standard Contractual Clauses**" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, together with, where the UK GDPR applies, the International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK Information Commissioner.

"**Sub-processor**" means any person engaged by Supplier to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

**2.1 Roles.** As between the Parties, Customer (or its relevant Affiliate) is the controller and Supplier is the processor of Customer Personal Data.

**2.2 Scope.** Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only to the extent necessary to supply the Products and perform the Services under the Agreement, each Order Form and each SOW. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the categories of Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects are described in Annex 1.

**2.3 Relationship to Vessel Data.** Vessel Data that contains Personal Data (for example, crew names in EGCS logbooks or onboard monitoring records) is Customer Personal Data for the purposes of this DPA. Supplier's obligations under this DPA are in addition to its obligations under Section 13 of the Agreement.

**2.4 Customer Instructions.** Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA and Customer's written directions given in accordance with the Agreement constitute Customer's instructions. Supplier shall promptly inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws, and may suspend the affected processing until Customer confirms or modifies the instruction.

**2.5 Customer Obligations.** Customer shall ensure that it has a lawful basis for the processing it instructs Supplier to carry out and that it has given any notices required under Data Protection Laws to Data Subjects, including crew members serving on the Vessels.

## 3. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Compliance.** Supplier shall comply with Data Protection Laws in processing Customer Personal Data.

**3.2 Confidentiality of Personnel.** Supplier shall ensure that each person authorized to process Customer Personal Data (a) is subject to a written duty of confidentiality or an appropriate statutory duty, and (b) has access only to the Customer Personal Data needed for that person's role.

**3.3 Security.** Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against Personal Data Breaches, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing. Those measures shall include at least the measures in Annex 2.

**3.4 Onboard Access.** When Supplier personnel attend a Vessel, they shall access onboard systems containing Customer Personal Data only as needed to perform the Services and in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Supplier shall not remove Customer Personal Data from a Vessel on portable media unless the media is encrypted.

**3.5 No Sale or Secondary Use.** Supplier shall not sell or share Customer Personal Data, or retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose of its own.

## 4. SUB-PROCESSORS

**4.1 Consent.** Supplier shall not engage a Sub-processor without Customer's prior written consent, consistent with Section 15.4 of the Agreement. Annex 3 lists the Sub-processors that Customer has approved as of the date of this DPA.

**4.2 New Sub-processors.** Supplier shall give Customer at least 30 days' written notice before engaging a new Sub-processor, identifying the Sub-processor, its location and the processing it will perform. Customer shall respond within that period and shall not unreasonably withhold consent.

**4.3 Sub-processor Terms.** Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor, by written contract, data protection obligations that are no less protective of Customer Personal Data than those in this DPA. Supplier remains fully responsible to Customer for each Sub-processor's performance of those obligations.

## 5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

**5.1 Transfers.** Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the country in which it was collected, or permit a Sub-processor to do so, except in compliance with Data Protection Laws.

**5.2 Standard Contractual Clauses.** To the extent Supplier processes Customer Personal Data originating in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom in a country that has not been recognized as providing an adequate level of protection, the Parties agree that the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two: controller to processor) are incorporated into this DPA by reference. For those clauses: (a) the optional docking clause in Clause 7 applies; (b) Option 2 of Clause 9(a) applies, with the notice period set out in Section 4.2 of this DPA; (c) the optional language in Clause 11 does not apply; (d) Clauses 17 and 18 are governed by and refer to the law and courts of Ireland; and (e) Annexes I and II are completed by Annexes 1 and 2 of this DPA.

## 6. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS AND ASSISTANCE

**6.1 Data Subject Requests.** Supplier shall promptly, and in any event within five Business Days, forward to Customer any request it receives from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data. Supplier shall not respond to the request except on Customer's instructions.

**6.2 Assistance.** Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in (a) responding to Data Subject requests, (b) carrying out data protection impact assessments, (c) consulting with supervisory authorities, and (d) meeting Customer's security obligations under Data Protection Laws.

## 7. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

**7.1 Notification.** Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. The notice shall be sent to the Customer notice address in Section 16.4 of the Agreement, with a copy by email to Customer's data protection contact identified in Annex 1.

**7.2 Content.** The notice shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**7.3 Response.** Supplier shall take all reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remedy each Personal Data Breach, and shall cooperate with Customer in its response. Supplier shall not notify any Data Subject, regulator or third party of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data without Customer's prior written approval, unless required by law.

## 8. AUDIT

**8.1 Information.** Supplier shall make available to Customer on request all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA, including summaries of its most recent independent security audit reports or certifications.

**8.2 Audits.** Customer, or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations and appointed by Customer, may audit Supplier's compliance with this DPA once in any 12-month period, or at any time after a Personal Data Breach or where required by a supervisory authority. Customer shall give at least 30 days' notice of an audit (except after a Personal Data Breach), shall conduct it during normal business hours, and shall bear its own costs.

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION

**9.1 Return or Deletion.** On termination or expiry of the Agreement, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, return all Customer Personal Data to Customer or securely delete it, and delete existing copies, within 60 days. Supplier's obligation to return Vessel Data under Section 14.4(c) of the Agreement applies to Vessel Data that is Customer Personal Data.

**9.2 Retention by Law.** Supplier may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent required by law, provided that it keeps that data confidential, processes it only for the purpose for which retention is required, and deletes it when no longer required.

**9.3 Certification.** On Customer's request, Supplier shall certify in writing that it has complied with this Section 9.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or in connection with this DPA is subject to Section 12 of the Agreement.

**10.2 Term.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Supplier or any Sub-processor processes Customer Personal Data.

**10.3 Changes in Law.** If a change in Data Protection Laws requires a change to this DPA, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend it.

**10.4 Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Except as provided in Section 5.2(d), this DPA is governed by the law, and subject to the jurisdiction, set out in Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement.

**10.5 Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## ANNEX 1: DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**Subject matter and duration:** Processing incidental to the supply of Products and performance of Services under the Agreement, for the Term and the period described in Section 9.

**Nature and purpose:** Remote monitoring and troubleshooting of EGCS; onboard technical attendance; crew training; maintenance of EGCS compliance records; coordination of deliveries with Vessels and port agents.

**Categories of Data Subjects:** Masters, officers and crew of the Vessels; Customer and Affiliate employees and contractors; port agents and their personnel.

**Categories of Personal Data:** Names, ranks and positions; business contact details; seafarer certificate and training records; entries in EGCS logbooks and monitoring records; system access credentials and access logs.

**Special categories of Personal Data:** None intended. Supplier shall not request health or other sensitive data.

**Data protection contact (Customer):** Privacy Officer, Niron Ocean Lines, Inc., 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311.

## ANNEX 2: TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

1. Encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent).
2. Role-based access controls, unique user credentials and multi-factor authentication for remote access to EGCS monitoring platforms.
3. Logging and review of access to systems containing Customer Personal Data.
4. Network segregation between Supplier remote-support systems and Vessel operational technology networks, consistent with IMO Resolution MSC.428(98) on maritime cyber risk management.
5. Regular vulnerability scanning and timely patching of Supplier systems.
6. Backup and recovery procedures, tested at least annually.
7. Annual data protection and security training for Supplier personnel with access to Customer Personal Data.
8. Secure deletion of Customer Personal Data in accordance with recognized standards (for example, NIST SP 800-88).
