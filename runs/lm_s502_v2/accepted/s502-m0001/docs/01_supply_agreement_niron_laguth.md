# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 ("**Customer**"); and

**Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Houston, Texas 77029 ("**Supplier**").

Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates a fleet of container vessels, many of which are fitted with exhaust gas cleaning systems.

B. Supplier designs, manufactures and supplies spare parts for exhaust gas cleaning systems, supplies alkaline reagent for those systems, and provides onboard and remote technical services.

C. Customer wishes to buy, and Supplier wishes to supply, those products and services for Customer's fleet on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to a person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting securities of, or the power to direct the management and policies of, the relevant person.

"**Applicable Law**" means all laws, regulations, conventions and binding regulatory requirements that apply to a Party, the Products, the Services or the Vessels. This includes the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships (MARPOL) and its Annexes, flag state requirements, and port state and coastal state requirements.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

"**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the data processing addendum between the Parties governing the processing of personal data under this Agreement.

"**EGCS**" means an exhaust gas cleaning system (scrubber) installed or to be installed on a Vessel, including its washwater treatment and monitoring equipment.

"**Fees**" means all amounts payable by Customer for Products and Services under this Agreement and any Order Form or SOW.

"**Order Form**" means an order form executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and specifies the Vessels, Products, Services, quantities, prices and delivery terms for a particular period.

"**Products**" means (a) EGCS spare parts and components, (b) alkaline reagent, including sodium hydroxide solution, for use in EGCS ("**Reagent**"), and (c) any other goods supplied by Supplier under this Agreement.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products or Services issued by Customer under an Order Form.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between the Parties setting out quality control, inspection, certification and nonconformance procedures for the Products.

"**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule between the Parties setting out response times, parts-availability levels and service credits.

"**Services**" means onboard and remote technical services for EGCS, including commissioning support, troubleshooting, maintenance, crew training and retrofit work.

"**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes project-based Services.

"**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out in the applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement, together with the EGCS manufacturer's specifications and Applicable Law.

"**Vessel Data**" means operational, performance, emissions, washwater and location data relating to any Vessel or EGCS that Supplier collects, receives or accesses in performing this Agreement.

"**Vessels**" means all vessels owned, operated or chartered by Customer or its Affiliates from time to time.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. A reference to a document includes that document as amended from time to time.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Order Forms, the Quality Agreement, the Service Level Schedule, each SOW and the DPA are to be read together. If they conflict, the following order of precedence applies: Amendments; Service Level Schedule (as to service levels and credits); Supply Agreement; DPA; Quality Agreement; SOW; Order Form. Accordingly, the Service Level Schedule prevails over this Agreement as to service levels and service credits, and any written amendment executed by both Parties prevails over each of the other documents.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

**2.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

## 3. SUPPLY OF PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

**3.1 Scope.** Supplier shall supply Products and perform Services for the Vessels in accordance with this Agreement, each Order Form, each SOW and each accepted Purchase Order.

**3.2 Order Forms.** The Parties shall agree Order Forms from time to time. Each Order Form forms part of this Agreement but does not amend it unless the Order Form expressly states the Section being amended and is signed by an authorized officer of each Party.

**3.3 Purchase Orders.** Customer may issue Purchase Orders under an Order Form. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within two Business Days of receipt. A Purchase Order that conforms to the applicable Order Form is deemed accepted if not rejected in that period. Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order or acknowledgment have no effect.

**3.4 Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling 12-month forecast of Reagent and spare-parts requirements each quarter. Forecasts do not create a purchase commitment.

**3.5 Critical Spares Stock.** Supplier shall maintain at its own cost a stock of the critical spare parts identified in the applicable Order Form at its Houston, Rotterdam and Singapore depots, in the quantities stated in that Order Form.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices set out in the applicable Order Form or SOW. Reagent prices may be adjusted only as stated in the applicable Order Form or by written amendment.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Products upon delivery and Services monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall identify the Vessel, Purchase Order number and Products or Services concerned.

**4.3 Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within 45 days of receipt. Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith by giving notice with reasonable detail before the due date. The Parties shall work promptly to resolve any dispute.

**4.4 Taxes.** Prices exclude value added, sales and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay where properly invoiced. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 5. DELIVERY

**5.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the port, agent or vessel location stated in each Purchase Order, by the delivery date stated in that Purchase Order.

**5.2 Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer on delivery in accordance with Section 5.1.

**5.3 Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer immediately if it expects any delay. If delivery of Reagent or a critical spare part is more than 48 hours late, Customer may obtain substitute goods from a third party. Supplier shall reimburse Customer the reasonable excess cost of those goods, without prejudice to Customer's other remedies.

**5.4 Reagent Handling.** Supplier shall deliver Reagent in containers and with documentation that comply with the IMDG Code and Applicable Law, and shall provide current safety data sheets with each delivery.

## 6. SERVICES

**6.1 Performance.** Supplier shall perform the Services with appropriately qualified and certified personnel, in a professional and workmanlike manner and in accordance with good industry practice.

**6.2 Service Levels.** Supplier shall meet the service levels set out in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits are payable as set out there.

**6.3 Onboard Attendance.** Supplier personnel attending a Vessel shall comply with the master's instructions, the Vessel's safety management system and Applicable Law. Customer shall arrange reasonable access to the Vessel.

**6.4 Retrofit Work.** Project-based Services, including EGCS retrofits, shall be performed under an SOW.

## 7. QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE

**7.1 Quality Agreement.** Supplier shall comply with the Quality Agreement.

**7.2 Certification.** Supplier shall supply each Product with the certificates of conformity, class approvals and test reports required by the Quality Agreement and Applicable Law.

**7.3 Regulatory Support.** Supplier shall provide Customer with all information reasonably required for Customer's EGCS compliance records, including the EGCS Technical Manual, Onboard Monitoring Manual and washwater records.

## 8. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

**8.1 Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within 30 days after delivery. Customer may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications by notice to Supplier.

**8.2 Rejected Products.** Supplier shall, at Customer's option, replace or repair rejected Products, or credit their price, within the time required by the Quality Agreement. Supplier shall bear all costs of returning, replacing and disposing of rejected Products.

## 9. WARRANTIES

**9.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product will, for 18 months after delivery (or, for Reagent, until its stated expiry date), (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials, workmanship and design, and (c) be fit for use in the EGCS for which it is supplied.

**9.2 Services Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 6.1. It also warrants that any work performed will be free from defects for 12 months after completion.

**9.3 Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products, when installed and used in accordance with Supplier's instructions, will enable the EGCS to operate in compliance with MARPOL Annex VI and applicable washwater discharge limits.

**9.4 Remedies.** If a Product or Service breaches a warranty, Supplier shall promptly repair, replace or re-perform it at its own cost, including the cost of attendance on the Vessel. This is without prejudice to Customer's other rights under this Agreement.

**9.5 Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, each Party disclaims all other warranties, express or implied.

## 10. INSURANCE

**10.1 Coverage.** During the Term and for two years afterward, Supplier shall maintain with reputable insurers: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 5,000,000 per occurrence; (b) products and completed operations liability insurance of at least USD 10,000,000 in the aggregate; (c) pollution legal liability insurance of at least USD 10,000,000 per claim; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**10.2 Evidence.** Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request and shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in Section 10.1(a) to (c).

## 11. INDEMNITIES

**11.1 Third-Party Claims.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) death, personal injury or damage to property caused by the Products or Services, (b) any claim that the Products or Services infringe a third party's intellectual property rights, or (c) Supplier's breach of Applicable Law.

**11.2 Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, employees and agents from and against all losses arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from death or personal injury caused by Customer's gross negligence or willful misconduct on board a Vessel.

**11.3 Environmental Indemnity.** Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates from and against all Environmental Losses. "**Environmental Losses**" means all fines, penalties, port detention costs, off-hire, deviation costs and related expenses incurred by Customer or its Affiliates, to the extent arising from nonconforming Products, or Services not performed in accordance with this Agreement, that cause any Vessel or EGCS to breach MARPOL Annex VI, applicable washwater discharge limits, or any related flag state, port state or coastal state requirement.

**11.4 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt notice of any claim, reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's cost and, for third-party claims, control of the defense. The indemnifying Party may not settle a claim in a way that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent. Delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1 Excluded Damages.** Except as stated in Section 12.4, neither Party is liable for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for loss of profit, arising under this Agreement. Off-hire, detention and deviation costs are direct damages for the purposes of this Agreement.

**12.2 Customer Liability.** Customer's aggregate liability under this Agreement, other than its obligation to pay Fees and its obligations under Section 11.2, shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**12.3 Supplier Liability.** Supplier's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**12.4 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.3 shall not apply to: (a) Supplier's obligations under Section 11.1; (b) liability arising from a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Supplier's obligations under Section 11.3.

**12.5 Non-Excludable Liability.** Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited under Applicable Law.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY AND VESSEL DATA

**13.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party under this Agreement that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. It includes Vessel Data, pricing and the terms of this Agreement.

**13.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement. It shall disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13. It shall protect that information with at least reasonable care.

**13.3 Exceptions.** Section 13.2 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to it before disclosure, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law (subject to prompt notice where lawful).

**13.4 Vessel Data.** As between the Parties, Customer owns all Vessel Data. Supplier may use Vessel Data only to perform the Services and to support Customer's compliance obligations. Supplier shall not sell, license or disclose Vessel Data to any third party except its subcontractors bound by this Section 13.

**13.5 Personal Data.** Each Party shall comply with the DPA.

**13.6 Duration.** This Section 13 survives for five years after termination or expiry, and indefinitely for Vessel Data.

## 14. TERMINATION

**14.1 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving notice describing it.

**14.2 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within 60 days.

**14.3 Guaranty.** Customer may terminate this Agreement by notice if the parent guaranty given by Laguth Group Holdings, Inc. in favor of Customer ceases to be in full force and effect and Supplier does not provide a replacement guaranty reasonably acceptable to Customer within 30 days.

**14.4 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry: (a) Customer shall pay for Products delivered and Services performed up to the termination date; (b) Supplier shall, at Customer's request, continue to supply Reagent and critical spare parts on the terms of this Agreement for up to 180 days to allow orderly transition; and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, except that Supplier shall return all Vessel Data to Customer.

**14.5 Survival.** Sections 9, 11, 12, 13, 14.4, 14.5 and 16, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive termination or expiry.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

**15.1 Assignment by Customer.** Customer may not assign, novate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Supplier, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**15.2 Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may assign, novate or otherwise transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any person without the consent of Customer, upon written notice to Customer.

**15.3 Change of Control of Customer.** Customer shall not undergo a Change of Control without the prior written consent of Supplier. "**Change of Control**" means any transaction or series of related transactions, including a merger, consolidation, share acquisition or sale of all or substantially all assets, as a result of which any person or group of persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the voting power of Customer, or the power to direct its management and policies. If a Change of Control of Customer occurs without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice to Customer.

**15.4 Subcontracting.** Supplier may subcontract Services only with Customer's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Supplier remains responsible for all acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

**15.5 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 15 is void.

**16.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

**16.2 Jurisdiction.** The state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement. Each Party submits to that jurisdiction and waives any objection to venue there.

**16.3 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These include war, terrorism, piracy, natural disaster, epidemic, port closure or governmental action, but exclude Supplier's labor disputes and supply-chain shortages that a prudent supplier would have mitigated. The affected Party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate. If a force majeure event prevents supply of Reagent or critical spare parts for more than 14 days, Customer may obtain substitute supply without liability to Supplier.

**16.4 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by internationally recognized courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses below (or as later notified):

- Customer: Niron Ocean Lines, Inc., 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311, Attention: General Counsel.

**16.5 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument that refers to this Agreement and is signed by an authorized officer of each Party.

**16.6 Waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it. A waiver is effective only if in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**16.7 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**16.8 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency.

**16.9 Third-Party Rights.** Except for Customer's Affiliates under Sections 11.1 and 11.3, no person other than the Parties has any right to enforce this Agreement.
