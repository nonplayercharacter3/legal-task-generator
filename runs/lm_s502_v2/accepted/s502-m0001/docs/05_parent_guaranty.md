# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2025 by:

**Laguth Group Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Suite 800, Houston, Texas 77029 (the "**Guarantor**"),

in favor of:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 (the "**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. The Beneficiary and Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Supplier**"), have entered into a Supply Agreement dated March 1, 2025 (as amended from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, the Supplier supplies exhaust gas cleaning system spare parts, alkaline reagent and onboard and remote technical services for the Beneficiary's fleet.

B. The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the membership interests in the Supplier. It will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement.

C. The Beneficiary entered into the Agreement on the basis that the Guarantor would give this Guaranty. This Guaranty is the parent guaranty referred to in Section 14.3 of the Agreement.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Guaranty:

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all present and future obligations and liabilities of the Supplier to the Beneficiary under or in connection with the Agreement and each Order Form, SOW, Purchase Order and other document forming part of the Agreement. This covers obligations that are actual or contingent, and those owed alone or jointly. It includes the obligations to supply Products, perform Services, pay service credits, indemnify and pay damages.

"**Insolvency Event**" means, as to any person, that the person (a) becomes insolvent or is unable to pay its debts as they fall due, (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, (c) has a receiver, trustee or custodian appointed over all or a substantial part of its assets, or (d) becomes subject to any proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**1.3 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Guaranty as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Guaranty.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the due and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

**2.2 Payment.** If the Supplier fails to pay any amount forming part of the Guaranteed Obligations when due, the Guarantor shall pay that amount to the Beneficiary within ten Business Days after written demand.

**2.3 Performance.** If the Supplier fails to perform any non-monetary Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall, within ten Business Days after written demand, either perform that obligation or procure its performance. The Guarantor shall also indemnify the Beneficiary against all losses, costs and expenses that the Beneficiary incurs as a result of the failure. Where the failure concerns the supply of Reagent or Critical Spare Parts to a Vessel, the Guarantor shall act as promptly as is necessary to avoid or minimize disruption to that Vessel's operations.

**2.4 Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. The Beneficiary need not first (a) make any demand on, or bring any action against, the Supplier or any other person, (b) enforce any other security or guaranty, or (c) exhaust any other remedy before making a demand on the Guarantor.

**2.5 Extent of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty does not exceed the liability that the Supplier has, or would have had but for any of the matters in Section 3, under the Agreement. The Guarantor may raise any defense that the Supplier could raise under the Agreement, except a defense arising from any matter in Section 3. This Section 2.5 does not limit Section 6.3.

**2.6 Demands.** A demand under this Guaranty must be in writing. It must describe in reasonable detail the Guaranteed Obligation concerned and the Supplier's failure. The Beneficiary may make more than one demand.

## 3. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It extends to the ultimate balance of the Guaranteed Obligations, regardless of any intermediate payment or discharge in whole or in part.

**3.2 Obligations Not Affected.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not reduced, released or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not known to the Guarantor:

(a) any amendment, supplement, extension or renewal of the Agreement, or any new Order Form, SOW or Purchase Order, including any that increases the Guaranteed Obligations;

(b) any time, waiver, consent or indulgence granted to the Supplier;

(c) any release of, or failure to enforce, any security or other guaranty for the Guaranteed Obligations;

(d) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of any obligation of the Supplier;

(e) any Insolvency Event affecting the Supplier, or any change in the Supplier's ownership, constitution or legal status;

(f) any set-off, counterclaim or defense that the Supplier has against the Beneficiary, other than payment or performance of the relevant Guaranteed Obligation; or

(g) any other act, omission or circumstance that, but for this Section 3.2, might operate to discharge a guarantor or surety.

**3.3 Waivers.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Guarantor waives (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, (b) notice of the incurring of any Guaranteed Obligation, (c) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor or default, except as stated in Section 2.6, and (d) any right to require the Beneficiary to proceed first against the Supplier or any other person or security.

**3.4 Reinstatement.** If any payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation is avoided, rescinded or must be returned, including in any Insolvency Event affecting the Supplier, this Guaranty continues or is reinstated as if that payment or performance had not been made.

**3.5 Subrogation and Subordination.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations that have fallen due have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution or indemnity against the Supplier arising from its performance of this Guaranty. Any amount that the Guarantor receives in breach of this Section 3.5 shall be held in trust for the Beneficiary and paid to it promptly. Any indebtedness of the Supplier to the Guarantor is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations while the Supplier is in default under the Agreement.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary, as of the date of this Guaranty, that:

(a) it is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize that execution, delivery and performance;

(c) this Guaranty constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not breach its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law binding on it, or any agreement or instrument binding on it or its assets;

(e) no Insolvency Event has occurred in relation to it; and

(f) it directly or indirectly owns all of the membership interests in the Supplier.

## 5. COVENANTS

**5.1 Financial Information.** The Guarantor shall provide the Beneficiary with copies of its annual consolidated audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, and with its unaudited quarterly consolidated financial statements within 60 days after the end of each fiscal quarter. The Beneficiary shall treat those statements as Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Agreement, unless the Guarantor has made them public.

**5.2 Notice of Certain Events.** The Guarantor shall notify the Beneficiary in writing promptly, and in any event within five Business Days, after (a) any Insolvency Event affecting the Guarantor or the Supplier, or (b) any event that would make any representation in Section 4 untrue if repeated at that time.

## 6. PAYMENTS

**6.1 Currency and Account.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in United States dollars, in immediately available funds, to the account notified by the Beneficiary in its demand.

**6.2 No Deductions.** The Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off or counterclaim, and free and clear of any deduction or withholding for taxes, unless required by law. If a deduction or withholding is required by law, the Guarantor shall increase the payment so that the Beneficiary receives the amount it would have received had no deduction or withholding been required.

**6.3 Default Interest and Costs.** Any amount not paid by the Guarantor when due under this Guaranty bears interest from the due date until the date of actual payment at the rate of 2% per annum above the prime rate published in *The Wall Street Journal* on the due date. The Guarantor shall also pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Beneficiary incurs in enforcing this Guaranty.

## 7. TERM AND RELEASE

**7.1 Term.** This Guaranty remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that arise after termination or expiry of the Agreement under Sections 9, 11, 12, 13 and 14.4 of the Agreement.

**7.2 No Revocation.** The Guarantor may not revoke or terminate this Guaranty, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the Beneficiary.

**7.3 Replacement.** If the Supplier provides a replacement guaranty under Section 14.3 of the Agreement and the Beneficiary accepts it in writing, the Beneficiary shall release the Guarantor in writing from obligations arising after the date of that release. The release does not affect any Guaranteed Obligation that arose before it.

## 8. GENERAL

**8.1 Assignment.** The Guarantor may not assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Guaranty without the prior written consent of the Beneficiary. The Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any person to whom it assigns the Agreement in accordance with Section 15.1 of the Agreement. This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors.

**8.2 Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered in the manner set out in Section 16.4 of the Agreement. Notices to the Beneficiary go to its address in that Section. Notices to the Guarantor go to: Laguth Group Holdings, Inc., 1200 Port Industrial Way, Suite 800, Houston, Texas 77029, Attention: General Counsel, with a copy to the Supplier.

**8.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Guarantor and the Beneficiary. No failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of it. A waiver is effective only if in writing and signed by the Beneficiary.

**8.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**8.5 Governing Law.** This Guaranty and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

**8.6 Jurisdiction.** The state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Guaranty. The Guarantor submits to that jurisdiction and waives any objection to venue there.

**8.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF THE GUARANTOR AND THE BENEFICIARY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS GUARANTY.

**8.8 Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
