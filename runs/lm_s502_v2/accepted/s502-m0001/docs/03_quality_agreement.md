# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 between:

**Niron Ocean Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harborside Plaza, Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 ("**Customer**"); and

**Laguth Marine Technologies, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Port Industrial Way, Houston, Texas 77029 ("**Supplier**").

This Quality Agreement supplements the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement and is the Quality Agreement referred to in Sections 1.1, 7.1, 7.2 and 8.2 of the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE

**1.1 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Products," "Reagent," "Services," "Specifications," "Vessels," "EGCS" and "Vessel Data."

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

"**Batch**" means a quantity of Reagent produced or blended in a single production run and identified by a unique batch number.

"**CAPA**" means a corrective and preventive action plan prepared under Section 7.

"**Certificate of Conformity**" means a document issued by Supplier certifying that a Product or Batch conforms to the Specifications, in the form described in Annex A.

"**Critical Spare Part**" means a spare part identified as critical in the applicable Order Form.

"**Nonconformance**" means any failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications, this Quality Agreement or the warranties in Section 9 of the Agreement, and includes a defect in labeling, packaging or documentation.

"**Quality Representative**" means the individual appointed by each Party under Section 2.2.

**1.3 Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products supplied for the Vessels under the Agreement, each Order Form and each Purchase Order, and to spare parts and materials installed by Supplier in performing Services, including under any SOW.

## 2. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

**2.1 Certification.** Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 (or any successor standard) by an accredited certification body, covering the design, manufacture, procurement, storage and dispatch of the Products at each facility and depot used to supply Customer, including its Houston, Rotterdam and Singapore depots. Supplier shall notify Customer within five Business Days if any certification is suspended, withdrawn or not renewed.

**2.2 Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall appoint a Quality Representative and notify the other Party of that person's name and contact details. The Quality Representatives shall be the primary contacts for all matters under this Quality Agreement and shall meet (in person or by video conference) at least once each quarter to review quality performance.

**2.3 Personnel.** Supplier shall ensure that personnel involved in manufacturing, testing, handling and dispatching Products, and in installing Products on board the Vessels, are trained and competent for their tasks. Supplier shall keep training records and make them available to Customer on request.

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS AND CHANGE CONTROL

**3.1 Compliance with Specifications.** Supplier shall manufacture, procure, test, store, package and deliver each Product in accordance with the Specifications.

**3.2 Reagent Specifications.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Reagent shall be sodium hydroxide solution meeting the following minimum requirements, verified for each Batch:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NaOH concentration | 50% w/w (± 1.0%) |
| Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃) | ≤ 0.2% w/w |
| Sodium chloride (NaCl) | ≤ 0.01% w/w |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 10 mg/kg |
| Heavy metals (as Pb) | ≤ 5 mg/kg |
| Appearance | Clear, free of visible suspended matter |

**3.3 Spare Parts.** Spare parts shall be new, unused, and either genuine parts of the EGCS manufacturer or parts approved by the EGCS manufacturer and, where required, by the relevant classification society. Supplier shall not supply reconditioned, refurbished or substitute parts without Customer's prior written approval.

**3.4 Change Control.** Supplier shall not make any change that could affect the form, fit, function, performance, composition, safety or regulatory status of a Product without first giving Customer at least 90 days' written notice and obtaining Customer's written approval. This applies to changes in raw materials, sub-suppliers, manufacturing site, manufacturing process, test methods, packaging and labeling. The notice shall describe the change, the reason for it, and the validation data supporting it. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold approval of a change that Supplier demonstrates has no adverse effect on the Product or on the compliance of the EGCS.

**3.5 Obsolescence.** Supplier shall give Customer at least 12 months' written notice before discontinuing any Product and shall offer Customer a last-time-buy opportunity on the terms of the Agreement.

## 4. INSPECTION, TESTING AND DOCUMENTATION

**4.1 Supplier Testing.** Supplier shall inspect and test each Product before dispatch in accordance with its approved inspection and test plan. Supplier shall test each Batch of Reagent for the parameters in Section 3.2 at an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory, which may be Supplier's own laboratory if so accredited.

**4.2 Delivery Documentation.** Supplier shall deliver with each consignment:

(a) a Certificate of Conformity;

(b) for Reagent, a certificate of analysis for each Batch and a current safety data sheet, consistent with Section 5.4 of the Agreement;

(c) for spare parts subject to class approval, a copy of the relevant class approval certificate or type approval; and

(d) any other documents required by Applicable Law or the applicable Order Form.

**4.3 Labeling and Traceability.** Supplier shall label each Product with its part number or product code, description, quantity, serial number or Batch number, date of manufacture and, for Reagent, the expiry date. Supplier shall maintain traceability of each Product to its raw materials, sub-suppliers, production records and test results.

**4.4 Reagent Shelf Life.** Reagent delivered to Customer shall have at least 80% of its stated shelf life remaining on the delivery date.

**4.5 Customer Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test Products in accordance with Section 8.1 of the Agreement. Customer's inspection, testing or acceptance does not relieve Supplier of its obligations under this Quality Agreement or the Agreement.

## 5. AUDITS

**5.1 Quality Audits.** Customer, or a qualified third party appointed by Customer and bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Supplier's facilities, depots, quality records and processes relating to the Products once in any 12-month period on at least 30 days' notice. Customer may conduct additional audits on reasonable notice following a significant Nonconformance or a recall under Section 8.

**5.2 Sub-suppliers.** Supplier shall manage the quality of its sub-suppliers of materials and components used in the Products and shall, on request, procure that Customer may participate in Supplier's audits of those sub-suppliers.

**5.3 Findings.** Supplier shall respond in writing to audit findings within 30 days with a CAPA for each finding and shall implement it within the agreed timetable.

## 6. NONCONFORMANCE

**6.1 Notification.** Each Party shall notify the other's Quality Representative promptly, and in any event within two Business Days, after becoming aware of a Nonconformance or suspected Nonconformance in any Product delivered or to be delivered under the Agreement.

**6.2 Quarantine.** Customer may quarantine any Product subject to a suspected Nonconformance pending investigation. Supplier shall provide disposition instructions within five Business Days after notice.

**6.3 Replacement Times.** For the purposes of Section 8.2 of the Agreement, Supplier shall replace or repair rejected Products, or credit their price at Customer's option, within the following times after notice of rejection:

(a) Reagent: delivery of conforming replacement Reagent within 72 hours, to the port or location nominated by Customer;

(b) Critical Spare Parts: delivery of a conforming replacement within five Business Days; and

(c) all other Products: delivery of a conforming replacement within 15 Business Days.

**6.4 Costs.** Supplier shall bear all costs of investigating Nonconformances and of returning, replacing and disposing of nonconforming Products, consistent with Section 8.2 of the Agreement, including the cost of removing nonconforming Reagent from a Vessel's tanks and disposing of it in accordance with Applicable Law.

**6.5 Other Remedies.** This Section 6 is without prejudice to Customer's rights under Sections 9 and 11 of the Agreement.

## 7. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS AND CAPA

**7.1 Investigation.** For each confirmed Nonconformance, Supplier shall investigate the root cause and deliver to Customer a written root cause analysis within 15 Business Days after notice.

**7.2 CAPA.** The root cause analysis shall include a CAPA describing the corrective actions taken, the preventive actions proposed, the responsible persons and an implementation timetable. Supplier shall report progress at each quarterly meeting under Section 2.2 until each CAPA is closed.

**7.3 Repeated Nonconformance.** If the same or a substantially similar Nonconformance occurs three or more times in any 12-month period, Supplier shall, at Customer's request, carry out additional inspection and testing of the affected Products at its own cost until Customer is reasonably satisfied that the root cause has been eliminated.

## 8. RECALLS AND FIELD ACTIONS

**8.1 Notice.** Supplier shall notify Customer immediately if it becomes aware of any defect, safety issue or regulatory concern affecting Products of the same type, Batch or production lot as Products supplied to Customer, whether or not those Products have shown a Nonconformance on a Vessel.

**8.2 Recall.** If a recall or field action is required by Applicable Law, a classification society or the EGCS manufacturer, or is reasonably requested by Customer to protect the safety or compliance of any Vessel or EGCS, Supplier shall carry it out promptly and at its own cost, in coordination with Customer so as to minimize disruption to Vessel operations.

## 9. EGCS COMPLIANCE SUPPORT

**9.1 Compliance Information.** Supplier shall provide Customer with all quality and test data relating to the Products that Customer reasonably requires for the EGCS Technical Manual, the Onboard Monitoring Manual, washwater records and any port state, flag state or class inspection, consistent with Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

**9.2 Performance Monitoring.** Where Supplier has remote access to EGCS monitoring data under the Agreement, Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any trend in Vessel Data indicating that a Product may be affecting EGCS performance, washwater quality or emissions compliance.

## 10. RECORDS

**10.1 Retention.** Supplier shall keep all production, inspection, test, traceability, Nonconformance and CAPA records relating to the Products for at least the longer of (a) five years after delivery of the relevant Product and (b) the period required by Applicable Law.

**10.2 Access.** Supplier shall provide copies of those records to Customer within five Business Days after request.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Term.** This Quality Agreement remains in effect for the Term of the Agreement and for so long afterward as Supplier supplies Products to Customer, including under Section 14.4(b) of the Agreement. Sections 8 and 10 survive termination or expiry.

**11.2 Confidentiality.** Information exchanged under this Quality Agreement is Confidential Information and is subject to Section 13 of the Agreement.

**11.3 Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or in connection with this Quality Agreement is subject to Section 12 of the Agreement.

**11.4 Review.** The Quality Representatives shall review this Quality Agreement at least once each year. Any change to it shall be made in accordance with Section 16.5 of the Agreement.

**11.5 Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the law, and subject to the jurisdiction, set out in Sections 16.1 and 16.2 of the Agreement.

**11.6 Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## ANNEX A: CERTIFICATE OF CONFORMITY – MINIMUM CONTENT

1. Supplier name and address, and the manufacturing site of the Product.
2. Customer Purchase Order number and Order Form reference.
3. Vessel name and IMO number, and delivery port or location.
4. Product description, part number or product code, and quantity.
5. Serial numbers or Batch numbers.
6. For Reagent: date of manufacture, expiry date and certificate of analysis reference.
7. Statement that the Product conforms to the Specifications and this Quality Agreement.
8. Reference to any applicable class approval or type approval.
9. Name, title and signature of Supplier's authorized quality signatory, and date.
