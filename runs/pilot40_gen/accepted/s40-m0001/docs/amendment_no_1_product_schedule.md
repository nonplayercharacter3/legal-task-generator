# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between:

**Rirux Linen Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Rirux**"); and

**Brisosk Laundry Solutions, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Brisosk**").

Rirux and Brisosk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Rirux and Brisosk are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). Under Section 1.7 of the Agreement, Exhibit A may be updated by Amendment.

B. On December 10, 2025, Rirux gave Brisosk written notice of non-renewal of the Agreement under Section 9.2 of the Agreement (the "**Non-Renewal Notice**").

C. Brisosk contends that the Non-Renewal Notice was untimely under Section 9.2 of the Agreement and that the Agreement therefore renewed for a Renewal Term. Rirux disputes Brisosk's position. The Parties have not resolved this disagreement, and each Party reserves its rights as set out in Section 4.

D. Rirux has begun manufacturing a line of folding and finishing equipment for commercial laundry operations. The Parties wish to add that equipment to the Products and to set its distributor pricing and support terms.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 "**SOW No. 1**" means Statement of Work No. 1: Field Engineering Services between the Parties, dated as of March 1, 2024.

1.3 "**New Products**" means the product lines added to Exhibit A by Section 2.1 of this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT OF EXHIBIT A (PRODUCTS)

2.1 **Additional Product Lines.** Exhibit A to the Agreement is amended to add the following rows immediately before the row for "Accessories and Spare Parts":

| Product Line | Description |
|---|---|
| RX-FF Series Flatwork Finishing Systems | Flatwork ironers, 1 to 4 roll, 32-inch to 48-inch roll diameter, with integrated feeders and cross-folders |
| RX-SP Series Small-Piece Folders and Stackers | Automatic small-piece folders, towel folders, blanket folders and stacking conveyors |

2.2 **Accessories and Spare Parts.** The row for "Accessories and Spare Parts" in Exhibit A covers the standard accessories, replacement components and spare parts for the New Products.

2.3 **Specifications.** The specifications for the New Products are as published by Rirux on the Amendment Date. Rirux may update them from time to time by giving notice to Brisosk, as stated in Exhibit A.

2.4 **Effect.** From the Amendment Date, the New Products are "Products" for all purposes of the Agreement.

## 3. PRICING AND SUPPORT FOR NEW PRODUCTS

3.1 **Distributor Prices.** Paragraph 1 of Exhibit B to the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "The distributor price for RX-FF Series Flatwork Finishing Systems and RX-SP Series Small-Piece Folders and Stackers is Rirux's then-current U.S. list price less a distributor discount of twenty-eight percent (28%)." Spare parts for the New Products are discounted from list at the spare parts rate in Exhibit B.

3.2 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Purchases of New Products count toward the minimum purchase amount in Paragraph 2 of Exhibit B to the Agreement. This Amendment does not change that minimum purchase amount.

3.3 **Preventive Maintenance.** The table in Section 4.1 of SOW No. 1 is amended to add the following rows:

| Product Line | Preventive Maintenance Interval |
|---|---|
| RX-FF Series Flatwork Finishing Systems | Quarterly |
| RX-SP Series Small-Piece Folders and Stackers | Semi-annually |

3.4 **Monthly Support Fee.** The table in Section 6.1 of SOW No. 1 is amended to add the following rows:

| Product Line | Monthly Fee per Unit |
|---|---|
| RX-FF Series Flatwork Finishing Systems | $425 |
| RX-SP Series Small-Piece Folders and Stackers | $95 |

3.5 **Registration.** Brisosk shall register each New Product installed at a Customer site in the Territory under Section 2.3 of SOW No. 1. Within thirty (30) days after the Amendment Date, Brisosk shall register any New Product installed before the Amendment Date.

3.6 **Training.** Within ninety (90) days after the Amendment Date, Rirux shall give Brisosk's technical personnel initial training on the installation, operation and maintenance of the New Products under Section 6.3 of the Agreement.

## 4. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

4.1 This Amendment does not amend Section 9 of the Agreement.

4.2 Neither the execution of this Amendment nor any performance under it is an admission by either Party, or a waiver of any position of either Party, about the following:
(a) the timeliness or effect of the Non-Renewal Notice; or
(b) whether the Agreement renewed at the end of the Initial Term.

4.3 Each Party reserves all of its rights and remedies concerning the matters described in Recital C and Section 4.2.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Agreement Otherwise Unchanged.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, and subject to Section 4, the terms of the Agreement and SOW No. 1 are unchanged. References in the Agreement to "this Agreement" and to Exhibit A or Exhibit B include the changes made by this Amendment.

5.2 **Amendment.** This Amendment is an "Amendment" as defined in Section 1.2 of the Agreement. To the extent it amends SOW No. 1, it is also a written instrument under Section 8.2 of SOW No. 1.

5.3 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Section 14.1 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment.

5.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
