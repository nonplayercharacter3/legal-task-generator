**Re: Volume Rebates and Co-Marketing Funds under the Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024**

Dear Sir or Madam:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter**") is between Rirux Linen Systems, LLC ("**Rirux**") and Brisosk Laundry Solutions, Inc. ("**Brisosk**"). It relates to the Distribution Agreement between Rirux and Brisosk dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). This Letter is a Side Letter as defined in Section 1.11 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. In the term sheet negotiated before the Agreement was signed, the Parties agreed that Brisosk's exclusivity would cover Healthcare Laundry Accounts in Pennsylvania and New Jersey only.

B. Section 4.6 of the Agreement provides that any volume rebates or co-marketing funds payable to Brisosk shall be as agreed by the Parties in writing.

C. The Parties wish to set out in this Letter the volume rebates and co-marketing funds that Rirux will make available to Brisosk.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period during the Term that begins on March 1.

1.2 "**Marketing Fund**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

1.3 "**Net Purchases**" means the aggregate net invoice value of Products that Brisosk buys from Rirux in a Contract Year and pays for in full. Net Purchases exclude taxes, freight, insurance, returns and credits, and fees for Services under any SOW.

1.4 "**Rebate**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

## 2. VOLUME REBATES

2.1 **Rebate Tiers.** For each Contract Year in which Brisosk's Net Purchases exceed the minimum purchase amount in Exhibit B to the Agreement, Rirux shall pay Brisosk a rebate (the "**Rebate**"). The Rebate is calculated on all Net Purchases for that Contract Year at the following rates:

| Net Purchases in the Contract Year | Rebate Rate |
|---|---|
| $3,500,000 or less | 0% |
| More than $3,500,000 up to and including $5,000,000 | 1.5% |
| More than $5,000,000 up to and including $7,000,000 | 2.5% |
| More than $7,000,000 | 3.5% |

2.2 **Spare Parts.** Purchases of spare parts count toward Net Purchases when Rirux determines the applicable tier. No Rebate is payable on spare parts.

2.3 **Calculation Statement.** Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each Contract Year, Rirux shall give Brisosk a written statement. The statement shall show Net Purchases for that Contract Year, the applicable tier and the Rebate amount.

2.4 **Payment.** Rirux shall pay the Rebate within thirty (30) days after it delivers the calculation statement. Rirux may pay the Rebate as a credit memo against Brisosk's future invoices or by wire transfer, at Brisosk's election. Rirux may set off against the Rebate any undisputed amount that is past due from Brisosk under the Agreement or any SOW.

2.5 **Disputes.** If Brisosk disputes a calculation statement, it shall notify Rirux in writing within thirty (30) days after receiving the statement. The notice shall state the basis of the dispute. The Parties shall then work in good faith to resolve it. Rirux shall pay any undisputed portion of the Rebate when due.

2.6 **Partial Contract Years.** If the Agreement expires or terminates part-way through a Contract Year, the tier thresholds in Section 2.1 shall be reduced in proportion to the number of days of that Contract Year that have elapsed. No Rebate is payable for a Contract Year in which Rirux terminates the Agreement under Section 9.3 of the Agreement.

## 3. CO-MARKETING FUNDS

3.1 **Accrual.** Brisosk shall accrue a co-marketing fund (the "**Marketing Fund**") equal to two percent (2%) of its Net Purchases in each calendar quarter. Rirux shall report the accrued amount to Brisosk within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter.

3.2 **Eligible Activities.** Brisosk may use the Marketing Fund only for the following activities that promote the Products in the Territory:
(a) trade show attendance and booth costs;
(b) print, digital and direct-mail advertising;
(c) Customer demonstrations, open houses and training events;
(d) product literature and sales collateral; and
(e) other activities that Rirux approves in advance in writing.

3.3 **Pre-Approval.** For any single activity that costs more than $10,000, Brisosk shall submit a written proposal to Rirux before committing any spend. Rirux shall approve or reject the proposal within ten (10) business days and shall not unreasonably withhold approval.

3.4 **Brand Compliance.** All materials paid for with the Marketing Fund shall comply with Section 8.2 of the Agreement and Rirux's brand guidelines.

3.5 **Claims and Reimbursement.** Brisosk shall claim reimbursement from the Marketing Fund by giving Rirux a written claim with invoices, proof of performance and copies of the materials used. Rirux shall reimburse each approved claim within thirty (30) days after receipt, up to the balance of the Marketing Fund then accrued.

3.6 **Expiry of Unused Funds.** Marketing Fund amounts accrued in a Contract Year expire if Brisosk does not claim them within ninety (90) days after the end of that Contract Year. Amounts in the Marketing Fund have no cash value, and Rirux has no obligation to pay them except as reimbursement of approved claims.

## 4. RECORDS AND AUDIT

4.1 **Records.** Brisosk shall keep accurate records of all activities paid for with the Marketing Fund for two (2) years after the end of the Contract Year in which it incurs the expense.

4.2 **Audit.** Once per Contract Year, on at least fifteen (15) business days' written notice, Rirux may audit those records during normal business hours. If an audit shows that Brisosk claimed amounts for ineligible activities, Brisosk shall repay those amounts within thirty (30) days.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Term.** This Letter takes effect on the Effective Date of the Agreement and continues for the Term. It ends automatically when the Agreement expires or terminates. Sections 2.4, 2.6, 3.5 and 4 survive expiry or termination for the purpose of settling amounts that accrued before that date.

5.2 **Confidentiality.** The terms of this Letter are Confidential Information under Section 10 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Governing Law.** This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Section 14.1 of the Agreement applies to this Letter.

5.4 **Amendment.** This Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

5.5 **Counterparts.** This Letter may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
