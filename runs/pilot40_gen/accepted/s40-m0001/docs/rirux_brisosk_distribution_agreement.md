# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Rirux Linen Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Rirux**"); and

**Brisosk Laundry Solutions, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Brisosk**").

Rirux and Brisosk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Rirux designs and manufactures industrial washer-extractors, tunnel washers and automated chemical dosing systems for commercial laundry operations.

B. Brisosk resells and supports commercial laundry equipment for institutional laundry operators.

C. Rirux wishes to appoint Brisosk as a distributor of the Products, and Brisosk wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Amendment**" means a written amendment to this Agreement signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

1.3 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

1.4 "**Critical Failure**" means a failure rendering a Product wholly inoperable.

1.5 "**Customer**" means any person or entity that owns, operates or manages a commercial, institutional or on-premises laundry facility. This includes healthcare, hospitality, industrial, uniform-rental, correctional, educational and government laundry operators.

1.6 "**Healthcare Laundry Account**" means a Customer whose laundry facility mainly processes linen for hospitals, long-term care facilities, outpatient surgical centers or other licensed healthcare providers.

1.7 "**Products**" means the commercial laundry equipment and systems listed in Exhibit A, as Exhibit A may be updated by Amendment. It includes standard accessories and spare parts for that equipment.

1.8 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Brisosk issues to Rirux under Section 3.

1.9 "**Services**" means the field engineering, installation support, preventive maintenance, repair and technical support services that Rirux provides under an SOW.

1.10 "**Service Levels**" means the response times, resolution targets, service credits and related performance standards for the Services set out in an SOW.

1.11 "**Side Letter**" means any letter agreement between the Parties that relates to this Agreement and is not designated as an Amendment.

1.12 "**SOW**" means a statement of work for Services that references this Agreement and is signed by both Parties.

1.13 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms, as described in Section 9.

1.14 "**Territory**" means the States of Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland and New York.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Rirux appoints Brisosk as its exclusive distributor of all Products to all Customers in the Territory during the Term, and Brisosk accepts that appointment. During the Term, Rirux shall not sell, lease, market or distribute any Products to any Customer in the Territory, including Rirux's national accounts. This restriction applies whether Rirux acts directly, through an Affiliate, or through any other distributor, dealer, agent or sales representative. Rirux shall refer to Brisosk any inquiry for Products that it receives from a Customer located in the Territory.

2.2 **Sales Outside the Territory.** Brisosk shall not actively solicit Customers for Products outside the Territory, establish a sales office or warehouse for Products outside the Territory, or appoint sub-distributors outside the Territory, unless Rirux has given its prior written consent.

2.3 **Sub-Distributors.** Brisosk may appoint sub-distributors within the Territory only with Rirux's prior written consent, which Rirux shall not unreasonably withhold. Brisosk remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its sub-distributors.

2.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, agency or fiduciary relationship between them. Brisosk shall resell Products in its own name and for its own account.

2.5 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each contract year of the Term, Brisosk shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of at least the minimum purchase amount set out in Exhibit B.

## 3. ORDERS

3.1 **Purchase Orders.** Brisosk shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Rirux. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products and quantities ordered, the requested delivery date, and the delivery location within the Territory.

3.2 **Acceptance.** A Purchase Order is binding only when Rirux accepts it in writing. Rirux shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt and shall not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement.

3.3 **Forecasts.** By the tenth (10th) day of each calendar quarter, Brisosk shall give Rirux a non-binding rolling twelve-month forecast of its anticipated Product requirements.

3.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice that add to or conflict with this Agreement have no effect.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Brisosk shall pay the distributor prices for Products set out in Exhibit B. Rirux may change those prices once per calendar year by giving Brisosk at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. Price changes do not apply to Purchase Orders that Rirux has accepted before the change takes effect.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Rirux shall invoice Brisosk when it ships the Products.

4.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

4.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Brisosk shall notify Rirux in writing of any good-faith dispute about an invoice within fifteen (15) days of receipt. The notice shall state the basis of the dispute. Brisosk shall pay the undisputed portion of the invoice when due.

4.5 **Taxes.** Prices do not include sales, use, excise and similar taxes. Brisosk is responsible for those taxes, except taxes based on Rirux's net income. If Brisosk holds valid resale exemption certificates, it shall provide them to Rirux.

4.6 **Rebates and Marketing Funds.** Any volume rebates or co-marketing funds payable to Brisosk shall be as agreed by the Parties in writing.

## 5. DELIVERY

5.1 **Delivery Terms.** Rirux shall deliver Products FCA Rirux's manufacturing facility (Incoterms 2020), unless the accepted Purchase Order states otherwise.

5.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to Products and risk of loss pass to Brisosk on delivery under Section 5.1.

5.3 **Inspection.** Brisosk shall inspect Products within ten (10) business days after they arrive at the delivery location. Within that period, Brisosk shall notify Rirux in writing of any shortage, visible damage or nonconformity. Products for which Brisosk gives no notice in that period are deemed accepted, except for latent defects covered by Section 7.

## 6. FIELD ENGINEERING AND SUPPORT

6.1 **Services.** Rirux shall provide Services for Products installed at Customer sites in the Territory as described in one or more SOWs. Each SOW shall describe the scope of the Services, the applicable Service Levels and the fees for the Services.

6.2 **Brisosk Support Obligations.** Brisosk shall provide first-line support to its Customers. This covers intake of service calls, basic troubleshooting, and escalation to Rirux under the procedures in the applicable SOW.

6.3 **Training.** At no additional charge, Rirux shall give Brisosk's technical personnel reasonable initial and annual training on the installation, operation and maintenance of the Products.

6.4 **Spare Parts.** For at least seven (7) years after Rirux discontinues a Product, Rirux shall keep spare parts for that Product available for purchase by Brisosk.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 **Product Warranty.** Rirux warrants that each Product will conform to Rirux's published specifications in Exhibit A and will be free from defects in materials and workmanship. This warranty lasts twelve (12) months from the earlier of installation at the Customer site and ninety (90) days after delivery to Brisosk.

7.2 **Remedy.** If a Product breaches the warranty in Section 7.1, Rirux shall, at its option, repair or replace the nonconforming Product or component. If Rirux cannot do so within a commercially reasonable time, it shall refund the price Brisosk paid for that Product. Rirux shall give priority to warranty claims arising from a Critical Failure.

7.3 **Exclusions.** The warranty in Section 7.1 does not apply to defects resulting from any of the following:
(a) misuse or negligence;
(b) installation, operation or maintenance that does not follow Rirux's instructions;
(c) use of chemicals or consumables not approved by Rirux;
(d) modification or repair by anyone other than Rirux or Rirux-trained personnel; or
(e) normal wear of consumable parts.

7.4 **Pass-Through.** Brisosk may pass the warranty in Section 7.1 through to its Customers. Brisosk shall not give any warranty on Rirux's behalf beyond that warranty.

7.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, RIRUX MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. THIS INCLUDES ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. MARKETING AND TRADEMARKS

8.1 **Promotion.** Brisosk shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory. It shall maintain a qualified sales and technical staff for that purpose.

8.2 **Trademark License.** Rirux grants Brisosk a non-exclusive, non-transferable license, for the Term, to use Rirux's trademarks and logos in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products. Brisosk shall follow Rirux's reasonable brand guidelines.

8.3 **No Other Rights.** All goodwill from Brisosk's use of Rirux's trademarks benefits Rirux. Brisosk shall not register any trademark, trade name or domain name that is confusingly similar to Rirux's trademarks.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

9.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for a further term (each, a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry of the then-current term. To be effective, such notice must be received no later than ninety (90) days prior to the last day of the then-current term. Each Renewal Term is three years, automatic absent timely non-renewal notice.

9.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party if:
(a) the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it; or
(b) the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

9.4 **Effect of Expiry or Termination.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement:
(a) all amounts owed by either Party become due;
(b) Rirux shall fulfil all Purchase Orders accepted before the date of expiry or termination, unless Rirux terminated under Section 9.3;
(c) Brisosk may sell its remaining inventory of Products for one hundred twenty (120) days, and the license in Section 8.2 continues for that purpose only; and
(d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

9.5 **Survival.** Sections 4.3, 7, 9.4, 9.5, 10, 11, 12 and 14 survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, pricing and customer information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

10.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement. It shall protect that information with at least reasonable care and shall disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 10.

10.3 **Exceptions.** Section 10.2 does not apply to information that the receiving Party can show:
(a) is or becomes publicly available without its fault;
(b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or
(d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law. Where permitted, it shall first give the disclosing Party prompt notice of the requirement.

10.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 10 last for the Term and three (3) years afterward. For trade secrets, they last for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Rirux.** Rirux shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Brisosk and its Affiliates against third-party claims, and related losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent they arise from either of the following:
(a) bodily injury or damage to tangible property caused by a defect in a Product; or
(b) any allegation that a Product, as delivered by Rirux, infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark.

11.2 **By Brisosk.** Brisosk shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Rirux and its Affiliates against third-party claims, and related losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent they arise from any of the following:
(a) Brisosk's negligence or willful misconduct;
(b) any warranty or representation that Brisosk makes about the Products beyond those authorized in Section 7; or
(c) Brisosk's breach of applicable law.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall:
(a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party of the claim;
(b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, except that the indemnifying Party may not settle the claim in a way that imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without its consent; and
(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Except for indemnification obligations under Section 11 and breaches of Section 10, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising from this Agreement, even if it was advised of the possibility of such damages.

12.2 **Cap.** Except for indemnification obligations under Section 11, breaches of Section 10, and Brisosk's payment obligations, each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts that Brisosk paid to Rirux under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 During the Term, each Party shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:
(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; and
(b) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

On request, each Party shall provide the other with certificates of insurance.

The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.
