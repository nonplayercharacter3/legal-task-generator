# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: FIELD ENGINEERING SERVICES

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Rirux Linen Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Rirux**"), and **Brisosk Laundry Solutions, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Brisosk**").

This SOW is issued under the Distribution Agreement between Rirux and Brisosk dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). It is an "SOW" as defined in Section 1.12 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** Under Section 6.1 of the Agreement, Rirux will provide Services for Products installed at Customer sites in the Territory. This SOW sets out the scope of those Services, the Service Levels and the fees.

1.2 **Covered Equipment.** This SOW covers all Products listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement that meet both of the following conditions (the "**Covered Equipment**"):
(a) Brisosk sold or installed the Product at a Customer site in the Territory; and
(b) Brisosk has registered the Product with Rirux under Section 2.3.

1.3 **Services.** Rirux shall provide the following Services for the Covered Equipment:
(a) installation support and commissioning assistance;
(b) scheduled preventive maintenance under Section 4;
(c) on-site corrective repair and fault diagnosis;
(d) second-line remote technical support to Brisosk's service personnel; and
(e) software and firmware updates for RX-DS Series Chemical Dosing Systems and for tunnel washer controllers.

1.4 **Exclusions.** The Services do not include any of the following:
(a) repairs needed because of an event described in Section 7.3 of the Agreement;
(b) relocation of Covered Equipment;
(c) supply of chemicals or other consumables; or
(d) work on equipment that is not Covered Equipment.

Rirux may perform excluded work on request at the rates in Section 6.3.

## 2. SERVICE REQUESTS AND ESCALATION

2.1 **First-Line Support.** Under Section 6.2 of the Agreement, Brisosk shall provide first-line support to its Customers. Brisosk shall take in all Customer service calls, carry out basic troubleshooting under Rirux's published troubleshooting guides, and escalate to Rirux any fault it cannot resolve.

2.2 **Escalation.** Brisosk shall escalate a fault to Rirux by logging a service request through Rirux's service portal or by calling Rirux's service line. Each service request shall include:
(a) the Customer name and site address;
(b) the model and serial number of the affected Covered Equipment;
(c) a description of the fault and any error codes displayed; and
(d) the troubleshooting steps already taken.

2.3 **Registration.** Within fifteen (15) business days after each Product is installed, Brisosk shall register it with Rirux. Registration shall state the Customer, the site address, the serial number and the installation date.

2.4 **Priority Classification.** When Rirux receives a service request, it shall classify the request as a Critical Failure, a Major Fault or a Minor Fault under Section 3. Brisosk may ask Rirux to reconsider a classification. Rirux shall consider any such request in good faith.

## 3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 **Fault Categories.** For the purposes of this SOW:
(a) "**Critical Failure**" means any malfunction of Covered Equipment that stops the equipment from operating, or that reduces the rated throughput of the equipment by ten percent (10%) or more, measured against Rirux's published specifications for that equipment;
(b) "**Major Fault**" means a malfunction, other than a Critical Failure, that materially impairs a function of the Covered Equipment but does not reduce its throughput as described in Section 3.1(a); and
(c) "**Minor Fault**" means any other malfunction, error or request for technical assistance.

3.2 **Response Times.** Rirux shall meet the following response times, measured from the time Rirux receives a complete service request under Section 2.2:

| Fault Category | Remote Response | On-Site Response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical Failure | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| Major Fault | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Minor Fault | 1 business day | Scheduled at next preventive maintenance visit or as agreed |

For each Critical Failure where Rirux does not meet the on-site response time in this Section 3.2, Rirux shall credit Brisosk five percent (5%) of the monthly support fee for that month under Section 6.1 (a "**Service Credit**").

3.3 **Coverage Hours.** Critical Failure response is available twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Response for Major Faults and Minor Faults is available from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays ("**business hours**").

3.4 **Restoration Target.** For each Critical Failure, Rirux shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore the affected Covered Equipment to operation within twenty-four (24) hours after the on-site response. Where a permanent repair needs parts that are not in stock locally, Rirux shall apply a temporary workaround if one is reasonably available.

3.5 **Service Credit Mechanics.** Rirux shall apply Service Credits against the next monthly invoice issued under Section 6. Brisosk shall claim each Service Credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the month in which the missed response occurred. Rirux shall pay Brisosk the amount of any Service Credit outstanding when this SOW expires or terminates within thirty (30) days after expiry or termination.

3.6 **Excused Delays.** A response time is not missed to the extent that Rirux's failure to meet it is caused by any of the following:
(a) Brisosk or the Customer failing to provide site access;
(b) inaccurate information in the service request; or
(c) an event described in Section 14.6 of the Agreement.

3.7 **Reporting.** Within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month, Rirux shall give Brisosk a report for that month. The report shall show service requests received by category, response times achieved, open requests and Service Credits accrued.

## 4. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

4.1 **Schedule.** Rirux shall perform preventive maintenance on Covered Equipment at the following intervals:

| Product Line | Preventive Maintenance Interval |
|---|---|
| RX-WE Series Washer-Extractors | Semi-annually |
| RX-TW Series Tunnel Washers | Quarterly |
| RX-DS Series Chemical Dosing Systems | Quarterly, including calibration of pumps and flow meters |

4.2 **Scheduling.** Rirux shall agree on preventive maintenance dates with Brisosk at least ten (10) business days in advance. Where reasonably practicable, Rirux shall schedule visits to minimize disruption to Customer operations.

4.3 **Visit Records.** After each preventive maintenance visit, Rirux shall give Brisosk a written record of the work performed, any parts replaced and any recommended corrective action.

## 5. PERSONNEL AND SITE REQUIREMENTS

5.1 **Field Engineers.** Rirux shall perform the Services through factory-trained field engineers employed or engaged by Rirux. Rirux remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor it uses.

5.2 **Site Rules.** While at Customer sites, Rirux personnel shall comply with the Customer's reasonable safety, infection-control and security rules, as long as those rules are provided to Rirux in advance.

5.3 **Brisosk Cooperation.** Brisosk shall arrange site access for Rirux personnel. It shall also make sure that Customer staff are available to give operational information when reasonably needed.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Monthly Support Fee.** Brisosk shall pay Rirux a monthly support fee for the Covered Equipment registered on the first day of each calendar month, calculated at the following rates:

| Product Line | Monthly Fee per Unit |
|---|---|
| RX-WE Series Washer-Extractors | $85 |
| RX-TW Series Tunnel Washers | $1,250 |
| RX-DS Series Chemical Dosing Systems | $60 |

6.2 **Parts.** During the warranty period under Section 7.1 of the Agreement, Rirux shall supply replacement parts for warranty repairs at no charge. Outside the warranty period, Brisosk shall pay for parts at the distributor prices in Exhibit B to the Agreement.

6.3 **Out-of-Scope Work.** Rirux shall charge for out-of-scope work at $165 per hour for field engineering time, plus reasonable travel expenses. Rirux shall perform such work only after Brisosk gives written approval.

6.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Rirux shall invoice the monthly support fee in advance on the first day of each month. Sections 4.3, 4.4 and 4.5 of the Agreement apply to all invoices under this SOW.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for as long as the Agreement remains in effect.

7.2 **Termination.** This SOW ends automatically when the Agreement expires or terminates. Either Party may terminate this SOW for material breach on the terms of Section 9.3(a) of the Agreement.

7.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination of this SOW, Brisosk shall pay all fees accrued up to the date of expiry or termination, and Rirux shall pay any Service Credits still outstanding under Section 3.5.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Incorporation.** This SOW forms part of the Agreement. Sections 10 through 14 of the Agreement apply to this SOW.

8.2 **Amendment.** This SOW may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
