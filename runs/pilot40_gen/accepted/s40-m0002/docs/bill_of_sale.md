# BILL OF SALE

**Exhibit A to Asset Purchase Agreement: Agreed Form**

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of December 15, 2026, by **Diseil Animal Health Laboratories, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company** ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Soulor Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc., a Delaware corporation** ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver certain assets of the Business to Buyer for the Purchase Price of $18,400,000.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS; CONVEYANCE

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. For purposes of this Bill of Sale, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Seller Facility**" means Seller's laboratory and reference laboratory premises located in Fort Collins, Colorado.

"**Purchased Assets**" means all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets used in the Business that are located at the Seller Facility, including:

- (a) all Inventory, including raw materials, reagents, work-in-process, finished test kits, packaging and supplies;
- (b) all equipment, instruments, analyzers, furniture and other tangible personal property, including the items listed on Schedule 1.1(b) to the Purchase Agreement;
- (c) all books, records, customer lists, supplier lists, validation data and quality files of the Business; and
- (d) all other tangible personal property of every kind used in the Business.

**1.2 Conveyance.** Seller hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 2.1 to the Purchase Agreement. Buyer shall have and hold the Purchased Assets forever.

## 2. EXCLUDED ASSETS

Seller does not sell, assign, transfer, convey or deliver to Buyer any of the Excluded Assets described in Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in the Excluded Assets.

## 3. NO ASSUMPTION OF LIABILITIES

This Bill of Sale is an instrument of conveyance only. By accepting this Bill of Sale, Buyer does not assume any liability or obligation of Seller. Buyer assumes the Assumed Liabilities solely under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement delivered at the Closing.

## 4. CONTRACTS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Assigned Contracts are assigned to Buyer under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement. The Intellectual Property used in the Business is assigned to Buyer under the IP Assignment. Both instruments are delivered at the Closing under Section 3.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. POWER OF ATTORNEY

Seller hereby irrevocably constitutes and appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in Seller's name and stead but on Buyer's behalf and for Buyer's benefit, to:

- (a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets from time to time;
- (b) give receipts and releases for the Purchased Assets;
- (c) institute and prosecute, in Seller's name or otherwise, any proceeding that Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets; and
- (d) defend and compromise any action, suit or proceeding relating to any of the Purchased Assets.

Seller acknowledges that this power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable. It does not terminate on Seller's dissolution.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Seller makes no representation or warranty in this Bill of Sale. The representations and warranties of Seller concerning the Purchased Assets are set forth in Article 4 of the Purchase Agreement and survive in accordance with Section 8.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**7.1 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Colorado, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.2 Jurisdiction.** Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, or to the conveyance of the Purchased Assets, must be brought exclusively in the District Court for Larimer County, Colorado. Seller, and Buyer by its acceptance of this Bill of Sale, each irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of that court and waives any objection to venue there.

## 8. SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS

This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

## 9. COUNTERPARTS; ELECTRONIC DELIVERY

This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature and electronic transmission. Delivery in that manner has the same effect as delivery of a manually signed original.
