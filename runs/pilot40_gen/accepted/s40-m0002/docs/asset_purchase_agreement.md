# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026, by and between **Soulor Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc., a Delaware corporation** ("**Buyer**"), and **Diseil Animal Health Laboratories, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company** ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a business that develops, manufactures, markets and sells companion-animal diagnostic assays, reagents and test kits. The business includes the CanineScreen panel and related products and the operation of a reference laboratory (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business. Buyer will also assume certain specified liabilities. In each case the terms and conditions of this Agreement apply.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale of Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 2.1.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's limited liability company records, tax returns and minute books; (c) any Contract that is not an Assigned Contract; (d) insurance policies of Seller; and (e) the other assets listed on Schedule 2.2.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the following liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) obligations under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing, other than any obligation arising from a breach or default occurring on or before the Closing; and (b) trade accounts payable of the Business listed on Schedule 2.3.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume any liability of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities. All other liabilities remain the sole responsibility of Seller, including all liabilities for taxes relating to periods before the Closing, product liability claims for products sold before the Closing, and employee liabilities.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

- (a) $1,840,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to the escrow agent named in the escrow agreement to be entered into at Closing, to be held as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article 8; and
- (b) the balance to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Allocation.** Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller an allocation of the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. The Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.7 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable tax Law.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on December 15, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 7.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

- (a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;
- (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Seller;
- (c) the IP Assignment, duly executed by Seller;
- (d) copies of the Required Consents;
- (e) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b);
- (f) a certificate of the secretary of Seller attaching the resolutions of Seller's managers and members authorizing this Agreement; and
- (g) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

- (a) the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.5;
- (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and
- (c) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b).

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents or (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller.

**4.4 Consents.** Except for the Required Consents set forth on Schedule 4.4, no consent, approval or notice of or to any Person is required in connection with the execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 2.1.

**4.6 Sufficiency of Assets.** The Purchased Assets constitute all of the assets necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.7 Inventory.** All Inventory, wherever held, consists of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course of business. The Inventory is not adulterated, expired or misbranded. Schedule 4.7 lists all locations at which Inventory is held as of the date of this Agreement.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 1.1(c) lists each Contract material to the Business. Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any other party is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** Seller owns or has the valid right to use all Intellectual Property used in the Business. To the Knowledge of Seller, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the Intellectual Property of any Person.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller conducts the Business in material compliance with all applicable Laws. Seller holds all permits material to the operation of the Seller Facility and the Business.

**4.11 Litigation.** No action, suit or proceeding is pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.12 Financial Statements.** The financial statements of the Business delivered to Buyer were prepared in accordance with GAAP, consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business as of their dates.

**4.13 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course and no Material Adverse Effect has occurred.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall (a) operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and (b) use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Purchased Assets and its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not sell, transfer or encumber any Purchased Asset, other than sales of Inventory in the ordinary course. Seller shall also not amend or terminate any Assigned Contract without that consent.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the Seller Facility, the Kestrin Warehouse, and the books, records and personnel of the Business, during normal business hours and on reasonable advance notice.

**6.3 Consents.** Seller shall use its reasonable best efforts to obtain each of the Required Consents before the Closing Date, at Seller's cost. Buyer shall reasonably cooperate with Seller in that effort.

**6.4 Notice of Developments.** Before the Closing, each Party shall promptly notify the other in writing of any fact or event that causes or would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Article 7 not to be satisfied.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to employees of the Business. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in communicating with those employees. Seller shall remain responsible for all compensation and benefits accrued through the Closing Date.

**6.6 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in any business that competes with the Business in North America. During the same period, Seller shall not solicit any customer or employee of the Business.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except as required by Law.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**6.9 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws. Seller shall indemnify Buyer against any Losses arising from that non-compliance.

## ARTICLE 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the following conditions: (a) no Law or order of any Governmental Authority prohibits the Closing; and (b) no action is pending before any Governmental Authority seeking to enjoin the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:

- (a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article 4 are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;
- (b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;
- (c) Seller has obtained and delivered to Buyer each of the Required Consents, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;
- (d) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred since the date of this Agreement; and
- (e) Seller has delivered each of the items in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions:

- (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article 5 are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;
- (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and
- (c) Buyer has delivered each of the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for 18 months. Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.5 (the "**Fundamental Representations**") are an exception and survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller, or (c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer, or (b) any Assumed Liability.

**8.4 Limitations.** Seller has no liability under Section 8.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, until aggregate Losses exceed $184,000. Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller is liable from the first dollar. Seller's aggregate liability under Section 8.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, shall not exceed $2,760,000. These limitations do not apply to fraud.

**8.5 Procedures.** A Party seeking indemnification shall give prompt written notice of the claim to the indemnifying Party. Failure to give notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party.

**8.6 Escrow.** Buyer shall first seek recovery of Losses under Section 8.2 from the Escrow Amount, to the extent available.

## ARTICLE 9. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

- (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties;
- (b) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 31, 2027, unless the terminating Party's breach caused the failure to close;
- (c) by Buyer, if Seller materially breaches this Agreement and the breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice; or
- (d) by Seller, if Buyer materially breaches this Agreement and the breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it becomes void and no Party has further liability, except for liability for willful breach before termination. Sections 6.7 and Article 10 survive termination.

**10.1 Expenses.** Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement. Seller shall bear all transfer, sales and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets.

**10.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.11 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each Party is entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**10.12 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
