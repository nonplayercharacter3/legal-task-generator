**Re: Letter of Intent: Proposed Acquisition of the Companion-Animal Diagnostic Assay Business of Diseil Animal Health Laboratories, LLC**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Soulor Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire the companion-animal diagnostic assay business (the "**Business**") of Diseil Animal Health Laboratories, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("**Seller**"). The proposed acquisition is the "**Transaction**." Except as stated in Section 12, this Letter is not binding on either party.

## 1. Structure of the Transaction

Buyer will acquire substantially all of the assets used in the Business. The acquisition will be made under a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and customary ancillary conveyance and assignment documents. Buyer will assume only the liabilities specifically identified in the Definitive Agreement.

## 2. Purchased Assets

The assets to be acquired (the "**Purchased Assets**") will include all assets used in the Business, wherever located. Based on Buyer's diligence to date, they include:

- (a) all reagents, raw materials, work-in-process, finished test kits, packaging and supplies, including finished-kit inventory held at the Reno, Nevada warehouse operated by Kestrin Logistics, LLC;
- (b) the equipment, instruments and analyzers at Seller's laboratory and reference laboratory in Fort Collins, Colorado;
- (c) the Key Contracts described in Section 5 and the customer distribution agreements of the Business;
- (d) all intellectual property used in the Business, including assay protocols, formulations, validation data and the CanineScreen name and marks;
- (e) transferable permits, licenses and registrations;
- (f) accounts receivable; and
- (g) books, records, customer and supplier lists and quality files.

Cash, Seller's company records, tax returns, insurance policies and contracts not designated for assignment will be excluded.

## 3. Purchase Price

The purchase price for the Purchased Assets will be $18,400,000, payable in cash at closing (the "**Purchase Price**"). Buyer expects that ten percent of the Purchase Price will be deposited with a mutually acceptable escrow agent. That amount will serve as security for Seller's indemnification obligations. The Purchase Price assumes delivery of inventory at normalized levels consistent with Seller's trailing twelve-month average and the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

## 4. Due Diligence

From the date of this Letter, Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the facilities, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business. This access includes the Fort Collins laboratory and the Kestrin warehouse in Reno, Nevada. Buyer's diligence will cover financial, legal, regulatory, quality, intellectual property and commercial matters, including the Key Contracts.

## 5. Key Contracts

Buyer has identified the following contracts as key to the value of the Business (the "**Key Contracts**"). Buyer expects each of them to be assigned to Buyer at closing.

**5.1 Exclusive Reagent Supply Agreement with Pellwyn Reagents, Inc.** Seller is party to an Exclusive Reagent Supply Agreement with Pellwyn Reagents, Inc. ("**Pellwyn**"). Under it, Pellwyn is the sole source of the reagents used in Seller's flagship CanineScreen panel. Under the Pellwyn supply agreement, Pellwyn prior written consent is required for assignment, including by asset sale or change of control. This covers any sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Business. Seller has advised that it has not previously sought Pellwyn's consent to any assignment.

**5.2 Fort Collins Laboratory Lease.** Seller occupies its laboratory and reference laboratory in Fort Collins, Colorado under a lease. Assignment of the lease requires the landlord's consent.

**5.3 Kestrin Warehousing and Fulfillment Services Agreement.** Seller holds finished-kit inventory at the Reno, Nevada warehouse of Kestrin Logistics, LLC under a warehousing and fulfillment services agreement. Seller has advised that it may assign the agreement on written notice to Kestrin.

**5.4 Customer Distribution Agreements.** Seller will provide a list of its customer distribution agreements, together with any assignment restrictions in them, during diligence.

## 6. Conditions to Closing

The Definitive Agreement will contain conditions to closing customary for a transaction of this kind. They will include the following:

- (a) completion of Buyer's due diligence to its satisfaction;
- (b) receipt of all third-party consents required to assign the Key Contracts;
- (c) accuracy of Seller's representations and performance of its covenants;
- (d) absence of any material adverse change in the Business; and
- (e) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's board of directors.

## 7. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreement will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for an asset acquisition of this kind. These will cover title to and sufficiency of the Purchased Assets, the condition and location of inventory, the Key Contracts, intellectual property, compliance with laws, litigation and financial statements. Buyer expects general representations to survive for 18 months after closing.

## 8. Non-Competition

At closing, Seller will agree not to compete with the Business in North America, and not to solicit its customers or employees, for five years after closing.

## 9. Employees

Buyer may offer employment to selected employees of the Business on terms Buyer determines. Seller will remain responsible for all compensation and benefits accrued through closing.

## 10. Timing

The parties will use good-faith efforts to sign the Definitive Agreement by mid-September 2026. They will target closing in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to satisfaction of the closing conditions.

## 11. Expenses

Each party will bear its own expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction.

## 12. Binding Provisions

Only Sections 11, 12, 13 and 14 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are binding on the parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any legal obligation. A binding agreement for the Transaction will arise only if the parties sign the Definitive Agreement. Either party may end negotiations at any time before then, for any reason, without liability. The Binding Provisions survive any such termination.

## 13. Exclusivity

Seller will not, for 60 days after the date it countersigns this Letter, solicit, encourage, negotiate or accept any proposal from any person other than Buyer for the acquisition of the Business or any material portion of its assets. This restriction applies to Seller and to its members, managers, officers and advisors.

## 14. Confidentiality; Governing Law

**14.1 Confidentiality.** The terms of this Letter and the parties' discussions are confidential. They are subject to the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the parties dated May 4, 2026.

**14.2 Governing Law.** The Binding Provisions are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
