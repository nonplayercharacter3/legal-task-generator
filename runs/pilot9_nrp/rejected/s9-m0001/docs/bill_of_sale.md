# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is dated as of April 6, 2026, and is made by **Zanoul Equipment Leasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), in favor of **Lindqvist Linen Services, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Transferor is an Affiliate of Zanoul Holdings LLC ("**Seller**"). It owns the tunnel washers, ironers, folding lines and related equipment described in Exhibit A (the "**Transferred Equipment**"). The Company has used the Transferred Equipment at its industrial laundry plant at 410 Paterson Plank Road, Carlstadt, New Jersey (the "**Plant**") under the Equipment Lease Agreement dated January 10, 2022 between Transferor and the Company (the "**Equipment Lease**").

B. Seller, Paibon Textile Care Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and the Company have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of April 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller sells all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company to Buyer.

C. Section 2.6(a)(iii) of the Purchase Agreement requires delivery of this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Transferor, at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the benefits Transferor will derive as an Affiliate of Seller from the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Transferor agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Transferor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and the Company accepts, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the Transferred Equipment, free and clear of all liens, security interests, claims and encumbrances.

**2.2 Included Items.** The Transferred Equipment includes the following, to the extent they relate to the items listed on Exhibit A:
- all attachments, accessories, spare parts, tooling and components;
- all control systems, programmable logic controllers, software licenses and firmware embedded in or used to operate the equipment, to the extent transferable;
- all manufacturer and vendor warranties, service contracts and maintenance records; and
- all manuals, drawings and operating documentation.

**2.3 Delivery.** The Transferred Equipment is located at the Plant and is deemed delivered to the Company at the Closing without physical removal or relocation. Risk of loss passes to the Company at the Closing.

## 3. Termination of Equipment Lease

**3.1 Termination.** Effective as of the Closing, the Equipment Lease is terminated. Neither Transferor nor the Company has any further obligation under it, except for obligations stated in Section 3.2.

**3.2 Rent.** Rent and other amounts accrued under the Equipment Lease for periods before the Closing and unpaid as of the Closing are included as current liabilities in Closing Working Capital. Transferor waives any rent for periods after the Closing.

**3.3 Release of Filings.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing, Transferor shall file, or authorize the Company to file, UCC-3 termination statements releasing any financing statement filed in connection with the Equipment Lease or otherwise covering the Transferred Equipment.

## 4. Representations of Transferor

Transferor represents and warrants to the Company as of the Closing as follows:

(a) Transferor is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Bill of Sale.

(b) Transferor is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Transferred Equipment and has good and marketable title to it. That title is free and clear of all liens, security interests, claims and encumbrances, other than liens to be released at the Closing under payoff letters delivered under the Purchase Agreement.

(c) The execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale do not violate Transferor's organizational documents, any Law applicable to Transferor, or any agreement to which Transferor is a party.

## 5. Further Assurances

At the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Transferor shall execute and deliver further instruments of transfer and conveyance, and shall take any other action reasonably required, to vest in the Company title to the Transferred Equipment. This includes the assignment or transfer of manufacturer warranties and software licenses described in Section 2.2.

## 6. Power of Attorney

Transferor irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely to:
- demand and receive any of the Transferred Equipment; and
- give receipts and releases for the Transferred Equipment.

This power is coupled with an interest.

## 7. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

**7.1 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and all claims arising out of or relating to the Transferred Equipment, are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey. This includes claims relating to title, condition, fitness, warranties and the termination of the Equipment Lease. This choice applies without regard to any conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**7.2 Jurisdiction.** Each of Transferor and the Company irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of New Jersey, Bergen County, for any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale or the Transferred Equipment. Each waives any objection to venue in that court.

**7.3 Effect.** This Section 7 expressly supersedes the Stock Purchase Agreement as to governing law and forum for any matter relating to the Transferred Equipment.

## 8. Miscellaneous

**8.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Transferor and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**8.2 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Transferor and the Company.

**8.3 Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner provided in Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Transferor shall be given care of Seller.

**8.4 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. Electronic signatures have the same effect as original signatures.
