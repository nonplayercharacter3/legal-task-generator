# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is dated as of April 6, 2026, and is entered into by and among **Paibon Textile Care Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Zanoul Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Lindqvist Linen Services, Inc., a New Jersey corporation (the "**Company**"), have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of April 6, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company.

B. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount and the Adjustment Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to hold and disburse those amounts on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Adjustment Claim**" means a written claim by Buyer against the Adjustment Escrow Fund for any amount payable to Buyer under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Adjustment Escrow Amount**" means $350,000.

"**Adjustment Escrow Fund**" means the Adjustment Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest and earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Closing Date**" means April 6, 2026.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, collectively, the Indemnity Escrow Fund and the Adjustment Escrow Fund.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $2,400,000.

"**Indemnity Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest and earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Indemnity Release Date**" means the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a certificate of the presenting party to that effect.

"**Reference Date**" means December 31, 2025, the date of the Balance Sheet.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of, and has no duties under, the Purchase Agreement, and is bound only by the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

## 2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**2.2 Separate Accounts.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Fund and the Adjustment Escrow Fund in separate, segregated accounts. Neither Escrow Fund may be applied to satisfy a claim against the other.

## 3. Deposit and Investment

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds (a) the Indemnity Escrow Amount of $2,400,000, and (b) the Adjustment Escrow Amount of $350,000. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of each deposit to Buyer and Seller by email on the date received.

**3.2 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a U.S. Government money market fund or a deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured to the extent of FDIC limits, as Buyer and Seller may jointly direct in writing. Absent a direction, the Escrow Funds shall be held uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any investment loss or for any failure to achieve a particular rate of return.

**3.3 Ownership for Tax Purposes.** Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds for income Tax purposes until disbursed. All interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds are allocated to Buyer and reported on IRS Form 1099 to Buyer. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver a properly completed IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent at or before the Closing.

## 4. Release of Escrow Funds

**4.1 Release of Indemnity Escrow Fund.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Indemnity Release Date, which is 18 months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered on or before the Indemnity Release Date that remain unresolved on that date (the "**Retained Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Retained Amount and shall release each portion of it upon resolution of the related claim in accordance with Section 5.3.

**4.2 Joint Written Instructions and Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse any portion of the Escrow Funds (a) in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction, or (b) in accordance with a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver a Joint Written Instruction whenever either is entitled to a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement or the Purchase Agreement.

**4.3 Adjustment Escrow Fund.**

(a) Buyer shall deliver any Adjustment Claim to Seller and the Escrow Agent no later than ninety (90) days after the Reference Date. The Adjustment Claim shall state the amount claimed and describe in reasonable detail the basis for the claim.

(b) If Buyer timely delivers an Adjustment Claim, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Adjustment Escrow Fund and shall disburse it only in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order.

(c) If no Adjustment Claim has been delivered to the Escrow Agent by the deadline in Section 4.3(a), the Escrow Agent shall, within three (3) Business Days after receipt of Seller's written request, release the entire Adjustment Escrow Fund to Seller. No consent or instruction of Buyer is required for that release.

(d) Any balance of the Adjustment Escrow Fund remaining after payment of all amounts finally determined to be payable to Buyer in respect of a timely Adjustment Claim shall be released to Seller.

**4.4 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the recipient in writing. The Escrow Agent may confirm wire instructions by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule 1 before making any disbursement.

## 5. Claims Against the Indemnity Escrow Fund

**5.1 Claim Notice.** If any Buyer Indemnified Party asserts a claim for indemnification under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating the amount claimed (or a good faith estimate, if not then determinable) and describing the claim in reasonable detail.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a claim set forth in a Claim Notice by delivering a written notice of objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after receipt of the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). If Seller does not deliver an objection within the Objection Period, the claim is deemed accepted, and the Escrow Agent shall pay the amount claimed to Buyer from the Indemnity Escrow Fund on the third (3rd) Business Day after the end of the Objection Period.

**5.3 Disputed Claims.** If Seller timely objects, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold an amount equal to the disputed portion of the claim until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order, and shall then disburse that amount accordingly. The Escrow Agent shall pay any undisputed portion of the claim to Buyer on the third (3rd) Business Day after the end of the Objection Period.

**5.4 Exclusive Source of Recovery.** The Indemnity Escrow Fund is the sole and exclusive source of recovery for all claims by the Buyer Indemnified Parties under the Purchase Agreement, other than claims for Fraud. Buyer and Seller agree that the Purchase Agreement is hereby amended accordingly.

## 6. Duties and Protections of the Escrow Agent

**6.1 Limited Duties.** The duties of the Escrow Agent are only those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into or verify the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**6.3 Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, punitive or consequential damages.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of its performance under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount. This Section 6.4 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands, it may refrain from acting until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order, or it may interplead the Escrow Funds in a court of competent jurisdiction described in Section 9.2.

**6.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. Upon delivery of the Escrow Funds to a successor escrow agent jointly appointed by Buyer and Seller, the resigning or removed Escrow Agent is discharged from further duties. If no successor is appointed within the notice period, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint a successor.

**6.7 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering, sanctions and customer identification requirements.

## 7. Fees and Expenses

The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 2 and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds without a Joint Written Instruction.

## 8. Termination

This Escrow Agreement terminates when all of the Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms, except that Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 7 survive termination.

## 9. Miscellaneous

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to any conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**9.2 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**9.3 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses below (or such other address as a party may designate by notice). Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

If to Buyer: Paibon Textile Care Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address set forth on its signature page to the Purchase Agreement.

If to Seller: Zanoul Holdings LLC, Attention: Managing Member, at the address set forth on its signature page to the Purchase Agreement.

If to the Escrow Agent: Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration – Paibon/Zanoul.

**9.4 Amendment.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**9.5 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that any successor to the Escrow Agent by merger or conversion becomes the Escrow Agent without further act.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
