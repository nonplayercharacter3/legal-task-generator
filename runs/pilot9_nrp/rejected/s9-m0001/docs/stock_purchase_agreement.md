# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of April 6, 2026, and is entered into by and among **Paibon Textile Care Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Zanoul Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Lindqvist Linen Services, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company operates commercial and healthcare laundry and linen services in the State of New Jersey, including from an industrial laundry plant located at 410 Paterson Plank Road, Carlstadt, New Jersey (the "**Plant**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

D. At the Closing, Zanoul Equipment Leasing LLC, an Affiliate of Seller ("**Equipment Affiliate**"), will transfer to the Company certain tunnel washers, ironers and folding lines used in the business of the Company, pursuant to the Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Documents**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Bill of Sale and each other agreement, certificate and instrument delivered under this Agreement.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the Bill of Sale dated as of the Closing Date by Equipment Affiliate in favor of the Company with respect to the Transferred Equipment.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company (excluding cash and income Tax assets) minus the current liabilities of the Company (excluding Indebtedness and income Tax liabilities), in each case as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date and determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

"**Accounting Principles**" means GAAP, applied consistently with the methodologies used in preparing the Balance Sheet.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Escrow Agreement dated as of the Closing Date among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Excluded Taxes**" means (a) all Taxes of the Company for any Pre-Closing Tax Period, (b) all Taxes of Seller or any of its Affiliates (other than the Company) for any period, and (c) all Taxes of any Person imposed on the Company as a transferee or successor, by contract or under Treasury Regulations Section 1.1502-6 or any similar provision of state or local Law, which Taxes relate to an event or transaction occurring before the Closing.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under Delaware common law with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV or Article V.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.3 (Capitalization), Section 4.11 (Taxes), Section 4.15 (Brokers), Section 5.1 (Organization and Authority of Buyer) and Section 5.4 (Brokers).

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, permit or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, payroll, employment, property, franchise, withholding and other taxes, together with any interest and penalties.

"**Transferred Equipment**" means the tunnel washers, ironers, folding lines and related equipment described in Exhibit A to the Bill of Sale.

**1.2 Other Definitions.** Other capitalized terms have the meanings given to them where they are defined in this Agreement.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $24,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount and the Adjustment Escrow Amount, under Section 2.3;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 2.6(a)(v);

(c) on behalf of the Company or Seller, the unpaid transaction expenses set forth in the Estimated Closing Statement; and

(d) to Seller, the Purchase Price, plus or minus the amount by which the Estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or is less than the Target Working Capital, less the amounts paid under clauses (a) through (c) (the "**Closing Payment**").

**2.3 Escrow.**

(a) *Indemnity Escrow.* At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $2,400,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account (the "**Indemnity Escrow Fund**") as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article VIII and released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

(b) *Adjustment Escrow.* At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $350,000 (the "**Adjustment Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account (the "**Adjustment Escrow Fund**") as security for any amount payable to Buyer under Section 2.4 and released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.4 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* Not less than two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller delivered to Buyer a statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting forth its good faith estimate of Closing Working Capital ("**Estimated Closing Working Capital**"), Indebtedness and unpaid transaction expenses.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital, prepared in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

(c) *Notice of Disagreement.* Seller may dispute the Closing Statement by delivering to Buyer, within forty-five (45) days after receipt, a written notice specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the dispute (a "**Notice of Disagreement**"). If Seller does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement is final and binding.

(d) *Resolution.* Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve any dispute set out in a Notice of Disagreement. Any items remaining in dispute after that period shall be submitted to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually agreed by Buyer and Seller (the "**Independent Accountant**"), acting as an expert and not as an arbitrator, which shall resolve only the items in dispute within thirty (30) days after its engagement. The determination of the Independent Accountant is final and binding absent manifest error. Fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated in inverse proportion to the relative success of each party.

(e) *Payment.* If finally determined Closing Working Capital is less than Estimated Closing Working Capital, Buyer and Seller shall jointly instruct the Escrow Agent to pay the shortfall to Buyer from the Adjustment Escrow Fund, and Seller shall pay any shortfall in excess of the Adjustment Escrow Fund to Buyer within five (5) Business Days. If finally determined Closing Working Capital exceeds Estimated Closing Working Capital, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller within five (5) Business Days. Any balance of the Adjustment Escrow Fund remaining after payment of any shortfall shall be released to Seller.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any Taxes required to be withheld under applicable Law. Any amount so withheld is treated as paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

**2.6 Closing Deliveries.**

(a) *Seller Deliveries.* At the Closing, Seller shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iii) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Equipment Affiliate;

(iv) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 2.6;

(v) customary payoff letters for all Indebtedness, together with lien releases;

(vi) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(vii) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(viii) a certificate of the Secretary of the Company certifying its organizational documents, board resolutions and good standing.

(b) *Buyer Deliveries.* At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2 and the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by the electronic exchange of documents and signatures on April 6, 2026, concurrently with the execution of this Agreement (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Effective Time.** The Closing is deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of organization. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 1,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants, rights or other securities convertible into or exercisable for equity of the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.4, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require any consent under, or result in a breach of or give rise to any right of termination under, any Material Contract.

**4.5 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.5 lists each of the following contracts to which the Company is a party (the "**Material Contracts**"): (a) each customer contract providing for annual revenue in excess of $250,000; (b) each real property lease; (c) each contract for the purchase or lease of equipment in excess of $100,000; and (d) each contract containing a non-competition or exclusivity covenant binding on the Company. Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025 (the "**Balance Sheet**") and the related statements of income and cash flows for the fiscal year then ended. The financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since the date of the Balance Sheet, the Company has operated in the ordinary course and there has not been any event that has had a material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.8 Title to Assets; Equipment.** The Company has good title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible assets used in its business. Upon delivery of the Bill of Sale, the Company will hold good title to the Transferred Equipment, free and clear of all liens. The Transferred Equipment is in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

**4.9 Real Property.** The Company does not own any real property. The Company leases the Plant under the lease listed on Schedule 4.5 (the "**Plant Lease**").

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, and holds all permits necessary to conduct its business.

**4.11 Taxes.** All Tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true, correct and complete in all material respects. All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid. No audit or proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company is pending or threatened.

**4.12 Environmental Matters.**

(a) The Company is, and since January 1, 2016 has been, in compliance with all Environmental Laws.

(b) The Company holds, and is in compliance with, all permits required under Environmental Laws, including its New Jersey Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit and its industrial wastewater discharge permit issued by the applicable sewerage authority for discharges from the Plant, and no such permit will be terminated or require modification as a result of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

(c) The Company has stored, handled, used and disposed of all solvents, detergents, chemicals and other Hazardous Materials, and all lint, sludge and filter residues, in compliance with Environmental Laws.

(d) There has been no release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Plant or any other property currently or formerly operated by the Company, and there is no contamination of soil, sediment, surface water or groundwater at the Plant requiring investigation or remediation under Environmental Law.

(e) The Company has not received any notice of violation, information request or claim under any Environmental Law, and has delivered to Buyer all environmental reports, sampling data and assessments in its possession relating to the Plant.

**4.13 Employees.** Schedule 4.13 lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each plan has been operated in compliance in all material respects with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not a party to any collective bargaining agreement except as set forth on Schedule 4.13.

**4.14 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority of Buyer.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.3 Investment Purpose.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Further Assurances.** Following the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

**6.2 Books and Records.** For six (6) years after the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing and to afford Seller reasonable access to them on reasonable notice.

**6.3 Transition of Routes and Accounts.** For ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall cooperate reasonably with Buyer in transitioning the Company's delivery routes, customer contacts and vendor accounts that were administered by Seller or its Affiliates.

**6.4 Release.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller, on behalf of itself and its Affiliates, releases the Company from all claims arising before the Closing, other than claims under this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare and file all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date. Buyer shall prepare and file all other Tax returns of the Company.

**7.2 Straddle Periods.** Taxes for any taxable period beginning before and ending after the Closing Date shall be allocated to the Pre-Closing Tax Period (a) for property Taxes, ratably on a daily basis, and (b) for all other Taxes, on a closing-of-the-books basis.

**7.3 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use and similar Taxes arising from the transactions contemplated by this Agreement shall be borne by Seller.

**7.4 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date (the "**General Survival Date**"), except as provided in Sections 8.1(b) and 8.1(c).

(b) The Fundamental Representations survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations (giving effect to any waiver or extension).

(c) The representations and warranties in Section 4.12 (Environmental Matters) survive the Closing until the General Survival Date.

(d) The covenants and agreements of the parties survive the Closing in accordance with their terms. No claim may be brought for breach of a representation or warranty unless written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period, in which case the claim survives until finally resolved. The parties intend that the survival periods in this Section 8.1 replace any otherwise applicable statute of limitations.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV; (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; (c) any Excluded Taxes; and (d) any Indebtedness or transaction expenses not paid at or before the Closing; *provided, however,* that Seller shall have no obligation to indemnify the Buyer Indemnified Parties under clause (a) with respect to any matter disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V, and (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**8.4 Claims Procedure.**

(a) *Direct Claims.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail and stating the estimated amount of Losses, if known. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party by written notice within twenty (20) days after receiving notice of the claim. The indemnified party may participate with its own counsel at its own expense. Neither party may settle a third-party claim without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

**8.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) until aggregate Losses exceed $240,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed $2,400,000, an amount equal to the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(b) The limitations in Section 8.5(a) do not apply to Losses arising out of Fraud, breaches of Fundamental Representations, and Excluded Taxes, for which the aggregate liability of Seller shall not exceed the Purchase Price (except in the case of Fraud).

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party, less costs of recovery.

**8.6 Source of Recovery.** Any amount payable by Seller under this Article VIII shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, to the extent then available, and thereafter directly by Seller.

**8.7 Tax Treatment.** Indemnification payments under this Article VIII are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE IX. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**9.1 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the business of commercial or healthcare laundry and linen services in the States of New Jersey, New York or Pennsylvania.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, solicit any customer or employee of the Company to terminate or diminish its relationship with the Company.

**9.3 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company, except to the extent disclosure is required by Law.

**9.4 Remedies.** Seller acknowledges that a breach of this Article IX would cause irreparable harm to Buyer, and that Buyer is entitled to equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to any other remedy.
