# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Gaiport Clinical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Meridian Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Provider**"); and

**Daitend Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1250 Harbor View Drive, Suite 210, Annapolis, Maryland 21401 ("**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** Provider operates a cloud-based dental practice-management, scheduling, billing and imaging platform. Customer operates a group of dental clinics and wishes to subscribe to that platform for use across its locations. The Parties therefore agree as follows.

---

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings given below.

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

"**Authorized User**" means an employee, dentist, hygienist, contractor or other personnel of Customer or of a Clinic Location whom Customer authorizes to access the Platform.

"**Clinic Location**" means each dental clinic operated by Customer or its Affiliates that is listed in a Statement of Work, as updated from time to time under Section 2.4.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Customer Data**" means all data, records, files, images and other content that Customer, its Affiliates or Authorized Users upload to, enter into or generate through the Platform, including Patient Data.

"**Customer Data Claims**" has the meaning given in Section 10.2.

"**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated as of the Effective Date, which also serves as the Parties' business associate agreement under HIPAA, as amended from time to time.

"**Documentation**" means Provider's then-current user guides, technical specifications and help materials for the Platform.

"**Fees**" means the fees payable by Customer under Section 4 and each Statement of Work.

"**HIPAA**" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act, and their implementing regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164, each as amended.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

"**Patient Data**" means all patient health information, including radiographs, intraoral scans and other diagnostic images.

"**Platform**" means Provider's hosted dental practice-management, scheduling, billing and imaging software-as-a-service offering, including the modules identified in a Statement of Work, together with all updates, upgrades and Documentation that Provider makes generally available to its customers.

"**Professional Services**" means implementation, data migration, configuration, training and other services described in a Statement of Work.

"**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.2.

"**Services**" means access to the Platform and the Professional Services.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work or order form signed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

"**Successor**" means any entity that acquires all or any part of the Platform business of Provider, or any of Provider's customer contracts, whether by merger, sale of stock, sale of assets or otherwise.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

---

## 2. Services and Statements of Work

**2.1 Provision of Services.** Provider shall make the Platform available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Term, and shall perform the Professional Services, in each case in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and the applicable Statement of Work.

**2.2 Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement. Each Statement of Work shall identify the Platform modules, Clinic Locations, Authorized User counts, Professional Services and any Fees in addition to those set out in Section 4.

**2.3 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming the Parties' arrangement, the documents shall prevail in the following order: the DPA first, then this Agreement, then Statements of Work.

**2.4 Additional Clinic Locations.** Customer may add Clinic Locations to the Platform by written request to Provider. Provider shall onboard each additional Clinic Location under a Statement of Work or a written change order signed by both Parties, at the per-location rates then set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

**2.5 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors and hosting providers to perform the Services, provided that Provider remains responsible for their performance and that any subcontractor that accesses Patient Data is bound by obligations consistent with the DPA.

**2.6 Updates.** Provider may update the Platform from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the functionality, security or availability of the Platform as described in the Documentation at the Effective Date.

---

## 3. Customer Responsibilities

**3.1 Use.** Customer shall use the Platform only for the internal clinical, scheduling, billing and administrative operations of Customer and its Clinic Locations, and in accordance with the Documentation and applicable law.

**3.2 Authorized Users.** Customer is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of Authorized User credentials and for all activity under those credentials. Customer shall promptly notify Provider of any known unauthorized use of the Platform.

**3.3 Restrictions.** Customer shall not, and shall not permit any person to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Platform; (b) reverse engineer or decompile the Platform, except to the extent permitted by applicable law; (c) sublicense, resell or provide the Platform to any third party other than Authorized Users; or (d) use the Platform to build a competing product.

**3.4 Cooperation.** Customer shall provide Provider with timely access to information, personnel and systems reasonably required for the Professional Services.

---

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1 Platform Fee.** In consideration for access to the Platform for the Clinic Locations listed in the initial Statement of Work, Customer shall pay Provider a platform fee of $18,400 per month (the "**Platform Fee**"). Additional Clinic Locations, Authorized Users and Platform modules shall be charged at the rates set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

**4.2 Professional Services Fees.** Professional Services shall be charged as set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

**4.3 Renewal Pricing.** Fees for any Renewal Term shall be Provider's then-current rates for the Platform, as notified by Provider to Customer in writing at least sixty (60) days before the start of that Renewal Term. If Provider does not give such notice, the Fees in effect at the end of the then-current term shall continue to apply.

**4.4 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice the Platform Fee monthly in advance. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. Customer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith by notifying Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the due date, and the Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute.

**4.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

**4.6 Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay all such taxes other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

---

## 5. Data Protection

**5.1 Customer Data.** As between the Parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, limited license to host, process, transmit and display Customer Data solely to provide the Services and as otherwise permitted by the DPA.

**5.2 DPA.** Provider shall process Patient Data in accordance with the DPA and HIPAA. The DPA is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

**5.3 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for the Platform as described in the DPA.

**5.4 Aggregated Data.** Provider may compile de-identified, aggregated statistics about use of the Platform, provided that such statistics do not identify Customer, any Clinic Location or any individual and are de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514.

---

## 6. Service Levels and Support

**6.1 Availability.** Provider shall make the Platform available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance and performed outside 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days.

**6.2 Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet the availability commitment in Section 6.1 in any month, Customer shall be entitled to a credit against the next monthly Platform Fee equal to five percent (5%) of that month's Platform Fee for each full one-half percent (0.5%) shortfall, up to a maximum of twenty-five percent (25%) of that month's Platform Fee.

**6.3 Support.** Provider shall provide telephone and online support from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days, and emergency support for Severity 1 issues on a 24/7 basis, as further described in the Documentation.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

**7.1 Provider IP.** Provider and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in and to the Platform, the Documentation and all related intellectual property. No rights are granted to Customer except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

**7.2 Customer Materials.** Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data and any materials Customer provides to Provider.

**7.3 Feedback.** Provider may use any suggestions or feedback provided by Customer regarding the Platform without obligation to Customer, provided that Provider does not identify Customer or disclose Customer's Confidential Information.

**7.4 Deliverables.** Any configuration, templates or reports created specifically for Customer under a Statement of Work may be used by Customer for its internal purposes during the Term. Underlying Provider tools and methodologies remain Provider's property.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

**8.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including the terms of this Agreement, pricing, business plans and, in the case of Customer, Customer Data. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was known to the receiving Party before disclosure without restriction; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. Patient Data is governed by the DPA and HIPAA regardless of these exclusions.

**8.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its employees, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 8.

**8.3 Compelled Disclosure.** A receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

---

## 9. Warranties

**9.1 Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

**9.2 Provider.** Provider warrants that (a) the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation; (b) the Professional Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner; and (c) Provider will not knowingly introduce any virus, malware or other harmful code into the Platform.

**9.3 Remedy.** If Provider breaches Section 9.2(a) or 9.2(b), Provider shall, at its option, correct the non-conformity or re-perform the affected Services. If Provider cannot do so within thirty (30) days after written notice, Customer may terminate the affected Statement of Work and receive a refund of prepaid Fees for the unused portion.

**9.4 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, each Party disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. Provider does not provide clinical advice, and Customer remains responsible for all clinical decisions made using the Platform.

## 10. Indemnification

**10.1 By Provider.** Provider shall defend Customer and its Affiliates against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Provider and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates any United States patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claim. Provider has no obligation under this Section 10.1 to the extent a claim arises from Customer Data, modifications not made by Provider, or combination of the Platform with items not supplied by Provider. If the Platform becomes subject to such a claim, Provider may procure the right for Customer to continue using it, modify it to be non-infringing, or, if neither is commercially reasonable, terminate the affected Services and refund prepaid Fees for the unused portion.

**10.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against any and all claims, demands, investigations, regulatory proceedings, fines, penalties, losses, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to any Customer Data uploaded to, entered into or stored on the Platform by or on behalf of Customer, including any enforcement action, civil money penalty or corrective action plan under HIPAA or state health privacy law, whether or not caused or contributed to by any act or omission of Provider (collectively, "**Customer Data Claims**").

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced; (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement imposing any admission or obligation on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

**11.1 Cap.** Subject to Section 11.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.2 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Subject to Section 11.3, neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, revenue or goodwill, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 shall not apply to (a) Customer's obligation to pay Fees due under this Agreement; (b) amounts payable under Section 10.2; or (c) a Party's liability for fraud or willful misconduct.

---

## 12. Term and Termination

**12.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months from the Effective Date, ending December 31, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section 12.

**12.2 Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms of thirty-six (36) months each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal no earlier than 120 and no later than 90 days before the current term ends.

**12.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

**12.4 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**12.5 Suspension.** Provider may suspend access to the Platform if any undisputed invoice remains unpaid more than thirty (30) days after written notice of non-payment, or if suspension is reasonably necessary to prevent a security threat to the Platform. Provider shall limit any suspension to the extent and duration reasonably necessary and shall restore access promptly once the cause is resolved.

---

## 13. Effect of Termination

**13.1 Fees.** On expiry or termination, Customer shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination. If Customer terminates under Section 12.3 or 12.4, Provider shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

**13.2 Transition Assistance.** For up to ninety (90) days after expiry or termination, Provider shall continue to provide read-only access to the Platform and shall, on Customer's request, export Customer Data in an industry-standard, non-proprietary format (including DICOM format for diagnostic images), at Provider's then-current Professional Services rates.

**13.3 Return and Deletion.** Following the transition period, Provider shall return or delete Customer Data in accordance with the DPA.

**13.4 Survival.** Sections 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 16 and 17 survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

---

## 14. Insurance

**14.1** During the Term, Provider shall maintain, with reputable insurers, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; and (c) cyber liability insurance, including coverage for privacy breach response and regulatory defense, of at least $5,000,000 per claim. Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on Customer's reasonable request.

---

## 15. Assignment

**15.1 Consent Required.** Subject to Section 15.3, neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15 is void.

**15.2 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**15.3 Provider Assignment.** Provider may assign this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, to any Successor without the consent of Customer, by written notice to Customer given within thirty (30) days after the assignment takes effect. Upon such assignment, the Successor shall assume Provider's obligations under this Agreement and the DPA with respect to the assigned rights.

---

## 16. Governing Law and Disputes

**16.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

**16.2 Escalation.** Before starting any proceeding (other than for urgent injunctive relief), a Party shall give the other written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within fifteen (15) business days to attempt in good faith to resolve it.

**16.3 Forum.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**16.4 Equitable Relief.** Each Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to protect its Confidential Information or intellectual property without first complying with Section 16.2.
