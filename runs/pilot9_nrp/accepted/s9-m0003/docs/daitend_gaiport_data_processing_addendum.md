# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM AND BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Addendum and Business Associate Agreement (the "**DPA**") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Gaiport Clinical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Daitend Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"). Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** The Parties have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of the Effective Date (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider makes its cloud-based dental practice-management, scheduling, billing and imaging platform available to Customer and its Clinic Locations. Customer is a Covered Entity under HIPAA. To provide the Services, Provider will create, receive, maintain or transmit Protected Health Information on Customer's behalf and will act as Customer's Business Associate. This DPA sets out the terms on which Provider will process Patient Data, and it serves as the Parties' business associate agreement under 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.308(b), 164.314(a) and 164.504(e).

---

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this DPA, the following terms have the meanings given below.

"**Breach**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

"**Business Associate**" and "**Covered Entity**" have the meanings given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

"**Patient Data**" means Protected Health Information relating to patients of Customer or its Clinic Locations that is contained in Customer's scheduling and billing records created, received, maintained or transmitted by Provider through the Platform, excluding diagnostic images.

"**Protected Health Information**" or "**PHI**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

"**Security Incident**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.304.

"**Subcontractor**" means any subcontractor or hosting provider engaged by Provider under Section 2.5 of the Agreement that creates, receives, maintains or transmits Patient Data on Provider's behalf.

"**Unsecured PHI**" has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

**1.2 Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA, including "Authorized User," "Clinic Location," "Customer Data," "HIPAA," "Platform," "Services" and "Statement of Work," have the meanings given in the Agreement. Terms used but not defined in this DPA or the Agreement have the meanings given in HIPAA.

---

## 2. Scope and Roles

**2.1 Roles.** Customer is the Covered Entity and Provider is its Business Associate with respect to Patient Data. Provider processes Patient Data only on behalf of Customer and in accordance with Customer's documented instructions, the Agreement and this DPA.

**2.2 Incorporation.** This DPA is incorporated into the Agreement under Section 5.2 of the Agreement and applies for as long as Provider or any Subcontractor holds Patient Data.

**2.3 Compliance with Law.** Each Party shall comply with HIPAA and applicable state health privacy and data security laws in performing its obligations under this DPA.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

**3.1 Services.** Provider may use and disclose Patient Data only as necessary to perform the Services under the Agreement and each Statement of Work, and as required by law.

**3.2 Provider Administration.** Provider may use Patient Data for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities. Provider may disclose Patient Data for those purposes only if (a) the disclosure is required by law; or (b) Provider obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the Patient Data will be held confidentially, used or further disclosed only as required by law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Provider of any instance in which the confidentiality of the Patient Data has been breached.

**3.3 De-identification.** Provider may de-identify Patient Data in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514 solely to compile the aggregated statistics permitted by Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

**3.4 Minimum Necessary.** Provider shall limit its uses and disclosures of, and requests for, Patient Data to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b).

**3.5 Prohibited Uses.** Provider shall not (a) use or disclose Patient Data in any manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Customer; (b) sell Patient Data; or (c) use or disclose Patient Data for marketing or fundraising purposes.

---

## 4. Subcontractors

**4.1 Flow-Down.** Provider shall ensure, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), that each Subcontractor agrees in writing to restrictions, conditions and requirements that are at least as protective of Patient Data as those that apply to Provider under this DPA.

**4.2 Responsibility.** Provider remains responsible to Customer for the acts and omissions of each Subcontractor with respect to Patient Data as if they were Provider's own.

**4.3 Subcontractor List.** Provider shall maintain a current list of Subcontractors that host or have access to Patient Data and shall provide it to Customer on written request. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' written notice before engaging a new Subcontractor to host Patient Data.

---

## 5. Security and Breach Notification

**5.1 Safeguards.** Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of Patient Data, and shall comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with respect to electronic Patient Data.

**5.2 Specific Controls.** Without limiting Section 5.1, Provider shall, with respect to Patient Data:

(a) encrypt Patient Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard;

(b) host Patient Data only in data centers located in the United States that hold a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO/IEC 27001 certification;

(c) apply role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and access logging retained for at least six (6) years;

(d) conduct an annual risk analysis under 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A), and an annual independent penetration test of the Platform, and remediate identified high and critical findings within a commercially reasonable time;

(e) maintain daily backups of Patient Data with a recovery point objective of twenty-four (24) hours and a recovery time objective of eight (8) hours, and test its disaster recovery plan at least annually; and

(f) provide security and HIPAA training to all personnel with access to Patient Data at onboarding and at least annually.

**5.3 Reporting.** Provider shall report to Customer in writing:

(a) any use or disclosure of Patient Data not permitted by this DPA, within five (5) business days after Provider becomes aware of it;

(b) any Security Incident affecting Patient Data, within five (5) business days after discovery, provided that this DPA serves as notice of, and no further report is required for, unsuccessful attempts such as pings, port scans, denied log-in attempts and denial-of-service attacks that do not result in unauthorized access to Patient Data; and

(c) any Breach of Unsecured PHI within the Patient Data, without unreasonable delay and in no event later than ten (10) calendar days after discovery within the meaning of 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(a)(2).

**5.4 Breach Report Contents.** Each report under Section 5.3(c) shall include, to the extent known, the identity of each individual whose Unsecured PHI has been, or is reasonably believed to have been, accessed, acquired, used or disclosed, a description of the Breach, the types of Patient Data involved, the steps Provider has taken to contain and mitigate it, and any other information Customer reasonably requires to meet its notification obligations under 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.404 through 164.408. Provider shall supplement the report as further information becomes available.

**5.5 Mitigation and Cooperation.** Provider shall mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to Provider of a use or disclosure of Patient Data in violation of this DPA. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation of the Breach. Provider shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of notifying affected individuals, regulators and the media, and of providing credit monitoring where appropriate, to the extent the Breach results from Provider's or a Subcontractor's breach of this DPA.

**5.6 Notification Responsibility.** Customer shall determine whether, and how, to notify affected individuals, the Secretary of Health and Human Services and the media of a Breach. Provider shall not give such notices without Customer's prior written approval, except as required by law.

---

## 6. Individual Rights and Regulatory Access

**6.1 Access.** Within ten (10) business days after Customer's request, Provider shall make Patient Data in a Designated Record Set available to Customer as necessary for Customer to meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524.

**6.2 Amendment.** Within ten (10) business days after Customer's request, Provider shall make Patient Data in a Designated Record Set available for amendment and incorporate any amendments as directed by Customer under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526.

**6.3 Accounting.** Provider shall document disclosures of Patient Data, and make that information available to Customer within ten (10) business days after request, as necessary for Customer to provide an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.

**6.4 Direct Requests.** If an individual submits a request under this Section 6 directly to Provider, Provider shall forward it to Customer within five (5) business days and shall not respond to the individual except as Customer directs.

**6.5 Books and Records.** Provider shall make its internal practices, books and records relating to the use and disclosure of Patient Data available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services for purposes of determining Customer's compliance with HIPAA.

**6.6 Covered Entity Obligations.** To the extent Provider carries out any of Customer's obligations under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Provider shall comply with the requirements of that Subpart that apply to Customer in performing those obligations.

---

## 7. Return and Deletion

**7.1 Return.** On expiry or termination of the Agreement, and during the transition period described in Section 13.2 of the Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, return all Patient Data to Customer in an industry-standard, non-proprietary format.

**7.2 Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the transition period, Provider shall delete or destroy all Patient Data, including copies held by Subcontractors, in accordance with NIST Special Publication 800-88. Provider shall certify the deletion in writing to Customer on request.

**7.3 Infeasibility.** If return or destruction of any Patient Data is infeasible, Provider shall notify Customer of the conditions that make return or destruction infeasible. Provider shall extend the protections of this DPA to that Patient Data, and shall limit further uses and disclosures of it to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for so long as Provider maintains it.

**7.4 Backups.** Patient Data held in backup systems shall be overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course of Provider's backup rotation, which shall not exceed ninety (90) days. Pending deletion, that Patient Data remains subject to this DPA.

---

## 8. Audit and Assurance

**8.1 Reports.** Once in each calendar year, on Customer's written request, Provider shall provide Customer with a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent independent audit report, covering the Platform and the systems that host Patient Data.

**8.2 Questionnaires.** Provider shall respond, within thirty (30) days, to Customer's reasonable written security questionnaires. Customer may send no more than one questionnaire in any twelve-month period, unless it sends a further questionnaire following a Breach.

**8.3 Audit.** If Provider suffers a Breach of Unsecured PHI within the Patient Data, or if a regulator requires it, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA. The audit shall take place on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, during normal business hours, and in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt Provider's operations.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

**9.1 Term.** This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date. It remains in effect until the Agreement has expired or been terminated and all Patient Data has been returned or destroyed in accordance with Section 7.

**9.2 Termination for Violation.** If Customer determines that Provider has violated a material term of this DPA, Customer may give Provider written notice describing the violation. If Provider does not cure the violation within thirty (30) days after that notice, Customer may terminate the Agreement and this DPA by written notice under Section 12.3 of the Agreement. If cure is not possible, Customer may terminate immediately on written notice.

**9.3 Survival.** Sections 5.5, 6.5, 7 and 10 survive expiry or termination of this DPA.

---

## 10. General

**10.1 Amendment for Changes in Law.** The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this DPA as necessary to comply with any change in HIPAA or other applicable law. No amendment is effective unless it is in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

**10.2 Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this DPA shall be resolved to permit the Parties to comply with HIPAA.

**10.3 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this DPA confers any rights on any person other than the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**10.4 Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 17.1 of the Agreement. Reports under Section 5.3 shall also be sent by email to Customer's Privacy Officer at privacy@daitenddental.com.

**10.5 Governing Law.** This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules, except to the extent HIPAA applies.

**10.6 Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
