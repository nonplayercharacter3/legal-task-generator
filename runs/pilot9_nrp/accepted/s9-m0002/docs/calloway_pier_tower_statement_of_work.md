# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: CALLOWAY PIER TOWER

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 18, 2024 by and between **Meideis Glass Fabrication Co.** ("**Supplier**") and **Besouth Facade Systems, Inc.** ("**Buyer**") under the Glass Supply Agreement between Supplier and Buyer dated as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). This SOW is a Statement of Work under the Agreement and forms part of it under Section 4.1 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PROJECT

1.1 **Project.** Calloway Pier Tower, a 31-story mixed-use residential and office tower at 1400 Calloway Pier Road, Baltimore, Maryland 21231 (the "**Project**").

1.2 **Project Parties.**

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Owner | Calloway Pier Owner LLC |
| General Contractor | Tidewater Hallcroft Builders, Inc. |
| Architect of Record | Norquist Lavelle Architects, P.C. |
| Curtainwall Subcontractor | Besouth Facade Systems, Inc. |
| Glass Fabricator | Meideis Glass Fabrication Co. |

1.3 **Buyer's Scope.** Buyer holds the subcontract for design, fabrication and installation of the unitized curtainwall system for the Project's tower levels 3 through 31 and the podium storefront at levels 1 and 2. Supplier will fabricate and supply the glass described in Section 3 for incorporation into Buyer's units.

## 2. EXCLUSIVITY AND PROJECT COMMITMENT

2.1 **Exclusivity.** Supplier will be Buyer's exclusive source of insulated glass units for the Calloway Pier Tower project, Baltimore, Maryland. Buyer will not purchase insulated glass units for the Project from any other source while this SOW is in effect.

2.2 **Estimated Quantities.** Buyer estimates its Project requirements at approximately 186,000 sq. ft. of insulated glass units, broken down in Section 3.2. These quantities are estimates for planning purposes. Buyer's purchase commitment is set by the Purchase Orders it issues under this SOW.

2.3 **Duration.** This SOW takes effect on the date above and continues until Supplier has delivered all Products ordered under it and the warranty periods in Section 7 have run, or until it is terminated earlier under the Agreement.

## 3. PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

3.1 **Products.** The Products under this SOW are the following items from Exhibit A to the Agreement, in the makeups described below:

| Type | Exhibit A Item | Makeup | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| IGU-1 | A-1 / A-5 | 1" overall dual-pane: 1/4" heat-strengthened low-E (#2 surface) / 1/2" argon-filled airspace / 1/4" heat-strengthened clear | Tower vision, levels 3–31 |
| IGU-2 | A-1 / A-3 / A-5 | 1-5/16" overall dual-pane: 1/4" heat-strengthened low-E (#2 surface) / 1/2" airspace / 9/16" laminated heat-strengthened clear, 0.060" PVB | Podium storefront, levels 1–2 |
| IGU-3 | A-1 / A-6 | 1" overall dual-pane: 1/4" heat-strengthened low-E (#2 surface) / 1/2" airspace / 1/4" heat-strengthened with ceramic frit (#4 surface) | Tower spandrel, levels 3–31 |
| IGU-4 | A-2 / A-5 | Triple-pane, 1-3/4" overall, low-E #2 and #5 surfaces, argon fill at 90% minimum | Mechanical penthouse, level 31 acoustic zone |

3.3 **Specifications.** Products must conform to the following, which together with Exhibit A to the Agreement are the Specifications for this SOW:

(a) Project Specification Section 08 44 13 (Glazed Aluminum Curtain Walls) and Section 08 81 00 (Glass Glazing), Issue for Construction set dated January 26, 2024;

(b) Buyer's approved shop drawings and glass schedule, as revised and approved by the Architect of Record from time to time;

(c) low-E coating: Solarban 70 or approved equal, with performance values within the tolerances stated in Specification Section 08 81 00;

(d) spacer: warm-edge stainless steel, black finish; secondary seal: structural silicone compatible with Buyer's structural glazing sealant, with compatibility and adhesion test results delivered before first production; and

(e) labeling: each unit to bear a permanent IGCC/IGMA certification label and a removable label showing the Project mark number from Buyer's glass schedule.

3.4 **Mock-Up.** Before production, Supplier will supply glass for Buyer's performance mock-up within six weeks after Buyer's Purchase Order for mock-up glass. Production will not begin until the Architect of Record approves the mock-up and visual samples.

## 4. PRICING

4.1 **Prices.** Prices for Products under this SOW are the base prices in Exhibit B to the Agreement, adjusted as follows:

| Type | Price (USD per sq. ft.) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| IGU-1 | $27.75 | A-1 base $24.50 + A-5 low-E upcharge $3.25 |
| IGU-2 | $46.10 | A-1 base $24.50 + A-5 low-E upcharge $3.25 + laminated lite $18.35 (Project volume rate) |
| IGU-3 | $33.40 | A-1 base $24.50 + A-5 low-E upcharge $3.25 + frit lite $5.65 |
| IGU-4 | $45.25 | A-2 base $38.75 + two low-E surfaces at $3.25 each |

4.2 **Oversize Units.** Units over 50 sq. ft. carry a surcharge of $4.10 per sq. ft. on the whole unit area. Units over 85 sq. ft. or over 144 inches on either dimension will be priced by written quote before Buyer issues the Purchase Order.

4.3 **Mock-Up Glass.** Mock-up glass is priced at the rates in Section 4.1 plus a one-time setup charge of $3,500.

4.4 **Price Adjustment.** Prices under this SOW are subject to adjustment only as provided in Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

4.5 **Freight.** Supplier will arrange freight to the Delivery Point under Section 5.1 of the Agreement, and Buyer will reimburse freight at cost.

## 5. DELIVERY

5.1 **Delivery Point.** Buyer's unitizing facility at 2950 Broening Highway, Baltimore, Maryland 21224, unless a Purchase Order designates the Project site for direct-to-site storefront glass.

5.2 **Release Schedule.** Buyer will release Purchase Orders by floor sequence according to the Project schedule it delivers to Supplier, as updated monthly. Buyer will release each tower floor sequence at least eight weeks before the required delivery date.

5.3 **Lead Times.** The lead times in Section 5.2 of the Agreement apply, except that IGU-2 and IGU-4 units have a lead time of twelve weeks after Supplier accepts the Purchase Order.

5.4 **Delivery Sequencing.** Supplier will deliver units in mark-number sequence according to Buyer's floor release. Each rack must carry a packing list showing Project mark numbers, unit sizes and type designations.

5.5 **Breakage and Remakes.** Supplier will give priority to remakes for broken or rejected units. It will ship remakes for nonconforming units within three weeks after Buyer's notice under Section 8.2 of the Agreement.

## 6. QUALITY

6.1 **Testing.** Supplier will deliver to Buyer, before first production shipment, the following:

(a) current IGCC/IGMA certification for each unit construction;

(b) ASTM E2190 test reports; and

(c) structural silicone adhesion and compatibility reports referred to in Section 3.3(d).

6.2 **Visual Quality.** Visual quality will be assessed under ASTM C1036 and C1048 at the viewing distances and conditions stated in Specification Section 08 81 00.

6.3 **Site Access.** Supplier will make a technical representative available on request for up to four Project site or unitizing facility visits during production, at no charge.

## 7. WARRANTY

The warranties in Section 9 of the Agreement apply to Products under this SOW, including the ten-year insulated glass seal warranty and the five-year laminated glass delamination warranty in Section 9.2 of the Agreement. On Buyer's request, Supplier will issue a Project-specific warranty certificate in favor of the Owner on the same terms, for inclusion in Buyer's close-out submittals.
