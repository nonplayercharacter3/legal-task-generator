# GLASS SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Glass Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 by and between **Meideis Glass Fabrication Co.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal office at 2200 Furnace Street, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18103 ("**Supplier**"), and **Besouth Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 715 Harborline Drive, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Buyer**"). Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier fabricates insulated, laminated and tempered architectural glass products.

B. Buyer designs, fabricates and installs unitized and stick-built curtainwall and facade systems for commercial construction projects.

C. Buyer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, architectural glass products on the terms of this Agreement and the Statements of Work issued under it.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to any person, any other person that now or hereafter directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting equity of a person or the power to direct its management and policies, whether by contract or otherwise.

"**Amendment**" means a written amendment to this Agreement signed by both Parties under Section 18.3.

"**Change of Control**" means, as to Buyer, any transaction or series of related transactions in which (a) any person or group of persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the voting equity of Buyer or of any direct or indirect parent of Buyer; (b) Buyer or any direct or indirect parent of Buyer merges or consolidates with another person and the equity holders of Buyer or that parent immediately before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting equity of the surviving entity immediately after it; or (c) Buyer sells all or substantially all of its assets.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

"**Delivery Point**" means the project site, Buyer facility or other location stated in the applicable Purchase Order.

"**Effective Date**" means the date first written above.

"**Products**" means the architectural glass products listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing or by a Statement of Work.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Buyer issues under this Agreement and a Statement of Work.

"**Specifications**" means the technical requirements for Products in Exhibit A and in the applicable Statement of Work, including referenced ASTM standards, project drawings and approved shop drawings.

"**Statement of Work**" means a project-specific statement of work signed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any extension under Section 2.2.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement takes effect on February 1, 2024 (the Supply Agreement Effective Date) and continues for an initial term of three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15 or Section 16.3.

2.2 **Extension.** The Parties may extend the Term by mutual written agreement signed before the Initial Term expires.

2.3 **Effect on Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work in effect on expiry or termination of this Agreement continues under its own terms until completion of the project it covers, and this Agreement continues to govern that Statement of Work until then.

## 3. SUPPLY COMMITMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Purchase.** During the Term, Buyer shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, purchase from Supplier all of its requirements for architectural glass products throughout North America, including insulated glass units, laminated glass, heat-strengthened and fully tempered glass, and coated and spandrel glass, whether for curtainwall, storefront, window wall or other facade applications. Buyer and its Affiliates shall not purchase these products from any other source during the Term.

3.2 **Supply Obligation.** Supplier shall accept and fill Purchase Orders that conform to this Agreement and the applicable Statement of Work, subject to the lead times in Section 5.2.

3.3 **Forecasts.** Buyer shall give Supplier a rolling non-binding six-month forecast of anticipated Product requirements by the tenth day of each calendar quarter.

## 4. STATEMENTS OF WORK AND PURCHASE ORDERS

4.1 **Statements of Work.** The Parties will set out project-specific requirements, including Specifications, quantities, pricing adjustments, delivery schedules and project contacts, in a Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work forms part of this Agreement.

4.2 **Purchase Orders.** Buyer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that identify the applicable Statement of Work, Products, quantities, Delivery Point and requested delivery dates. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five business days of receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it does not conform to this Agreement or the applicable Statement of Work.

4.3 **Conflicting Terms.** Pre-printed or standard terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgement, invoice or other business form are of no effect.

## 5. DELIVERY

5.1 **Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020), packed on Supplier's returnable racks, to the carrier Buyer designates. If a Statement of Work requires delivery to the Delivery Point, Supplier shall arrange freight, and Buyer shall reimburse freight at cost.

5.2 **Lead Times.** Unless a Statement of Work states otherwise, Supplier shall deliver standard insulated glass units within six weeks, and laminated or custom-coated units within ten weeks, after it accepts the Purchase Order.

5.3 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Buyer promptly of any anticipated delay. If Supplier fails to deliver by the confirmed delivery date for reasons other than Force Majeure or Buyer's act or omission, Supplier shall, at Buyer's election, expedite shipment at its own cost.

5.4 **Racks.** Buyer shall return Supplier's racks in good condition within 60 days after delivery, failing which Supplier may invoice Buyer the replacement cost stated in Exhibit B.

## 6. TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer on delivery of Products to the carrier under Section 5.1, or, where Supplier arranges freight to the Delivery Point, on unloading at the Delivery Point.

## 7. PRICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Prices.** Buyer shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit B, as adjusted by any Statement of Work for project-specific Products.

7.2 **Price Adjustments.** Prices are fixed for the first 12 months of the Initial Term. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust prices once per contract year on 60 days' written notice, by no more than the percentage change in the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Producer Price Index for flat glass (PPI series WPU1311) over the previous 12 months.

7.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice on shipment. Undisputed invoices are due within 45 days of receipt. Buyer shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasons, within 15 days of receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 17.

7.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month or the highest lawful rate, whichever is lower.

7.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Buyer shall pay unless it provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 8. QUALITY AND INSPECTION

8.1 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system for Product fabrication. Its insulated glass units must be certified under the IGCC/IGMA certification program, and its laminated glass must comply with ANSI Z97.1 and 16 CFR 1201 where applicable.

8.2 **Inspection.** Buyer shall inspect Products within 10 business days after delivery and shall notify Supplier in writing of any visible nonconformity or shipping damage within that period. Acceptance does not waive Buyer's rights under Section 9 for latent defects.

8.3 **Rejection.** Supplier shall replace rejected Products at its cost within the lead times in Section 5.2, or within a shorter period agreed in writing where the project schedule requires it.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that Products will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship; and (c) be free of liens and encumbrances on delivery.

9.2 **Seal Warranty.** Supplier further warrants that insulated glass units will be free from seal failure, material obstruction of vision from film formation or dust, and internal moisture for ten years after delivery. Supplier warrants that laminated glass will be free from delamination for five years after delivery.

9.3 **General Warranty Period.** Except as stated in Section 9.2, the warranties in Section 9.1 apply for 24 months after delivery.

9.4 **Remedies.** For any Product that breaches a warranty, Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace it, and shall reimburse Buyer's reasonable documented costs of removal and reinstallation, up to the amount stated in Section 11.2.

9.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by defective Products or by the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier, and from claims that Products infringe a third party's patent, trademark or copyright.

10.2 **By Buyer.** Buyer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors and employees from third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Buyer in handling, glazing or installing Products.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall notify the indemnifying Party of the claim promptly, shall allow it sole control of the defense and settlement (except that no settlement may impose liability on the indemnified Party without its consent), and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's cost.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion.** Except for liability under Section 10 or Section 12, or for gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, including lost profits.

11.2 **Cap.** Except for liability under Section 10 or Section 12, or for gross negligence or willful misconduct, each Party's total liability under a Statement of Work is limited to the total amounts paid and payable under that Statement of Work.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information that one Party discloses to the other under this Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including pricing, Specifications, shop drawings and project information.

12.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall not disclose it except to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers and subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as this Section 12.

12.3 **Exceptions.** This Section 12 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice where lawful.

12.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 12 survive for three years after the Term ends.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 **Supplier Coverage.** Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:
(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations, of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate;
(b) umbrella liability insurance of at least $5,000,000;
(c) automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit; and
(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000.

13.2 **Certificates.** Supplier shall name Buyer and its Affiliates as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 13.1(a) and 13.1(b), and shall deliver certificates of insurance to Buyer on the Effective Date and on each policy renewal.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, epidemic, war, governmental action, labor disputes not involving its own workforce, and failure of float glass supply from primary manufacturers ("**Force Majeure**"). The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a Force Majeure event continues for more than 90 days, either Party may terminate the affected Statement of Work on written notice.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

15.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

15.3 **Effect.** On termination, Buyer shall pay for all Products delivered and for work in process under accepted Purchase Orders that Supplier cannot reasonably mitigate. Sections 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 17 and 18 survive termination or expiry.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

16.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 16 is void.

16.2 **Change of Control.** Any Change of Control of Buyer, including a Change of Control occurring at the level of any direct or indirect parent of Buyer, is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Buyer and requires Supplier's prior written consent under Section 16.1. Buyer shall notify Supplier in writing of any proposed Change of Control at least 30 days before it is completed.

16.3 **Termination Right.** If a Change of Control of Buyer occurs without Supplier's prior written consent, Supplier may terminate this Agreement and any or all Statements of Work on 30 days' written notice to Buyer, given at any time within 12 months after Supplier learns of the Change of Control.

16.4 **Successors.** Subject to this Section 16, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

17.1 **Escalation.** The Parties shall first try to resolve any dispute under this Agreement by referring it to their respective senior executives, who shall meet within 15 days after either Party's written request.

17.2 **Courts.** If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days after that request, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts.

17.3 **Injunctive Relief.** Nothing in this Section 17 prevents a Party from seeking interim injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses below or to another address a Party designates by notice:

18.3 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

18.4 **Waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

18.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision is to be replaced by a valid provision that comes closest to the Parties' intent.

18.6 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

18.7 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.
