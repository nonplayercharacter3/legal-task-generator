# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO GLASS SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 15, 2026 by and between **Meideis Glass Fabrication Co.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Besouth Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). It amends the Glass Supply Agreement between Supplier and Buyer dated as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties entered into the Agreement for the supply of architectural glass products by Supplier to Buyer, and entered into Statement of Work No. 1 – Calloway Pier Tower dated as of March 18, 2024 under it.

B. Under Section 2.1 of the Agreement, the Initial Term continues for three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date of February 1, 2024.

C. The Parties wish to provide for renewal of the Agreement after the Initial Term and to set pricing for any renewal period. Under Section 18.3 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **New Definition.** Section 1.1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following definition in alphabetical order:

"**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2."

1.3 **Term.** The definition of "Term" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with each Renewal Term and any extension under Section 2.4."

1.4 **Amendment as Amendment.** This Amendment is an "Amendment" for the purposes of the Agreement.

## 2. RENEWAL

Section 2.2 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following Sections 2.2, 2.3 and 2.4, and existing Section 2.3 of the Agreement (Effect on Statements of Work) is renumbered as Section 2.5:

"2.2 **Renewal.** On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive renewal terms of 24 months each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before expiry of the then-current term.

2.3 **Notice of Non-Renewal.** A notice of non-renewal must be given under Section 18.1 and takes effect on expiry of the then-current term. A notice of non-renewal does not affect any Statement of Work then in effect, which continues under Section 2.5.

2.4 **Extension.** The Parties may also extend the Term by mutual written agreement signed before the then-current term expires."

## 3. PRICING FOR RENEWAL TERMS

3.1 **Renewal Price Increase.** Section 7 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following Section 7.6:

"7.6 **Renewal Term Pricing.** With effect from the first day of each Renewal Term, the prices in Exhibit B, and the prices in each Statement of Work for Products delivered during that Renewal Term, increase by 7% over the prices in effect on the last day of the preceding term. Any adjustment under Section 7.2 during a Renewal Term applies to the prices as increased under this Section 7.6."

3.2 **Exhibit B.** Exhibit B to the Agreement is amended by adding the following note after the pricing table:

"Base prices in this Exhibit B are subject to the Renewal Term increase in Section 7.6."

## 4. GENERAL

4.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement and Statement of Work No. 1 – Calloway Pier Tower remain unchanged and in full force and effect. From the date of this Amendment, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

4.2 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules, and Section 17 of the Agreement applies to any dispute under it.

4.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
