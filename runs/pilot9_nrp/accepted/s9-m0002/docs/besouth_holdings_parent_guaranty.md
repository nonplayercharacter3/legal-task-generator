# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of October 5, 2026 by **Besouth Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office at 715 Harborline Drive, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Meideis Glass Fabrication Co.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal office at 2200 Furnace Street, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18103 (the "**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. The Supplier and **Besouth Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Buyer**"), are parties to the Glass Supply Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024. That agreement was amended by Amendment No. 1 dated as of June 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment**"). In this Guaranty, the Glass Supply Agreement as amended by the Amendment and as further amended from time to time is the "**Supply Agreement**."

B. The Buyer and the Supplier have entered into Statement of Work No. 1 – Calloway Pier Tower dated as of March 18, 2024 under the Supply Agreement. They may enter into further Statements of Work under it.

C. Under Section 2.1 of the Supply Agreement, the Initial Term runs for three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date of February 1, 2024. Under Section 2.2 of the Supply Agreement, as amended by the Amendment, the Supply Agreement will renew for a Renewal Term of 24 months beginning February 1, 2027.

D. The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding equity of the Buyer. It will benefit from the Supplier's continued supply of Products to the Buyer during the Renewal Term.

E. As a condition of continuing to extend trade credit to the Buyer during the Renewal Term, the Supplier has requested that the Guarantor give this Guaranty.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranteed Obligations.** "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all payment obligations of the Buyer to the Supplier under the Supply Agreement, each Statement of Work and each Purchase Order accepted under them, whether now existing or arising later and whether due or not yet due. These include:

(a) the price of Products delivered;

(b) freight reimbursements;

(c) rack replacement charges under Section 5.4 of the Supply Agreement;

(d) interest on late payments under Section 7.4 of the Supply Agreement; and

(e) amounts payable on termination under Section 15.3 of the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless stated otherwise.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** Subject to Section 3, the Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Supplier the full and punctual payment of the Guaranteed Obligations when due. This is a guaranty of payment and not of collection.

2.2 **Payment on Demand.** If the Buyer fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, and that amount remains unpaid for 10 business days after the Supplier gives the Buyer written notice of the failure, the Guarantor shall pay that amount to the Supplier within 15 business days after the Supplier's written demand. Each demand must:

(a) identify the unpaid invoices or other amounts;

(b) state the amount demanded; and

(c) attach copies of the relevant invoices.

2.3 **No Performance Guaranty.** This Guaranty covers payment obligations only. The Guarantor does not guarantee the Buyer's performance of any non-monetary obligation under the Supply Agreement.

## 3. MAXIMUM LIABILITY

The Guarantor's total aggregate liability under this Guaranty is limited to $4,500,000, plus the Supplier's reasonable costs of enforcement recoverable under Section 10.2. Payments by the Guarantor under this Guaranty reduce the amount available under this Section 3. Payments by the Buyer do not reduce that amount.

## 4. DEFENSES

4.1 **Defenses of the Buyer.** The Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that the Buyer could assert against the Supplier regarding a Guaranteed Obligation, including a good-faith invoice dispute raised under Section 7.3 of the Supply Agreement. The Guarantor may not assert a defense based on the Buyer's insolvency, bankruptcy or lack of capacity.

4.2 **Waivers.** To the extent permitted by law, the Guarantor waives:

(a) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor;

(b) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; and

(c) any requirement that the Supplier first proceed against the Buyer or any other person or security before making demand on the Guarantor, other than the notice to the Buyer required under Section 2.2.

4.3 **Changes to the Supply Agreement.** The Guarantor's obligations are not released or reduced by any of the following:

(a) any amendment, extension or renewal of the Supply Agreement or any Statement of Work, including any Renewal Term;

(b) any price adjustment under Section 7.2 or Section 7.6 of the Supply Agreement; or

(c) any waiver or indulgence the Supplier grants to the Buyer.

Section 3 continues to limit the Guarantor's liability in each case.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Supplier, as of the date of this Guaranty, that:

(a) it is a limited liability company duly formed, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has the power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all limited liability company action necessary to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its certificate of formation or limited liability company agreement, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement binding on it; and

(e) no consent or approval of any governmental authority is required for it to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty.

## 6. SUBROGATION

Until the Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement or contribution against the Buyer arising from payments under this Guaranty.

## 7. REINSTATEMENT

This Guaranty is reinstated if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by the Supplier on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of the Buyer. Reinstatement applies to the extent of the payment so rescinded or returned.

## 8. CONTINUING GUARANTY; CHANGE IN OWNERSHIP

8.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This is a continuing guaranty. It remains in effect until the later of:

(a) expiry or termination of the Supply Agreement; and

(b) completion of each Statement of Work then in effect under Section 2.5 of the Supply Agreement.

After that date, it continues only for Guaranteed Obligations that arose before that date and remain unpaid.

8.2 **Change of Control of the Guarantor.** The Guarantor may undergo a change of control, including a sale of all or a majority of its equity to a third party. A change of control of the Guarantor does not release, reduce or otherwise affect the Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty. This Guaranty continues to bind the Guarantor after any change of control. The Guarantor shall give the Supplier written notice of any change of control of the Guarantor within 10 business days after it is completed.

8.3 **Change in Ownership of the Buyer.** If the Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the equity of the Buyer, this Guaranty continues for Guaranteed Obligations arising before that event. The Guarantor may then terminate this Guaranty for Guaranteed Obligations arising after that event by written notice to the Supplier.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors and benefits the Supplier and its successors and permitted assigns under the Supply Agreement. The Guarantor may not assign its obligations under this Guaranty without the Supplier's prior written consent.

10.2 **Costs.** The Guarantor shall reimburse the Supplier's reasonable documented legal costs of enforcing this Guaranty against the Guarantor, if the Supplier prevails.

10.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Guarantor and the Supplier. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

10.5 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

10.6 **Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any proceeding arising out of this Guaranty.

10.7 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
