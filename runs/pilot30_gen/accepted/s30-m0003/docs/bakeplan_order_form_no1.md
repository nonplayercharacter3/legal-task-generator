## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Authorized User**" means a named employee of Licensee who is located at a Licensed Facility and has been issued unique login credentials to access the Software. "**Authorized Users**" has a corresponding meaning.

1.2 "**Subscription Period**" means the period stated in Section 2.3.

1.3 "**Subscription Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period during the Subscription Period, beginning on the Subscription Start Date or an anniversary of it.

1.4 "**Subscription Start Date**" means March 2, 2026.

## 2. Software, Quantities and Subscription Period

2.1 **Software Modules.** Licensee subscribes to the following BakePlan modules:

| **Module** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| BakePlan Production Scheduler | Line-level production scheduling, batch sequencing, oven and proofer capacity planning, and shift planning |
| BakePlan Recipe Manager | Recipe formulation management, ingredient specifications, scaling, version control and allergen data management |

2.2 **Licensed Quantity.** 150 Authorized Users.

2.3 **Subscription Period.** Thirty-six (36) months, beginning on the Subscription Start Date and ending on March 1, 2029, unless terminated earlier under the Agreement.

2.4 **Environments.** One (1) production environment and one (1) non-production (test and training) environment. Use of the non-production environment does not count toward the Licensed Quantity.

## 5. Implementation Services

5.1 **Scope.** Under Section 11.3 of the Agreement, Licensor shall provide the following services for each Licensed Facility:

(a) configuration of production lines, equipment capacities and shift patterns in the Production Scheduler module;

(b) migration of up to 2,500 existing recipe formulations and associated ingredient and allergen records from Licensee's spreadsheet-based tools into the Recipe Manager module, with validation reports for Licensee review;

(c) up to two (2) standard integrations with Licensee's enterprise resource planning system for production orders and inventory levels; and

(d) on-site administrator training and train-the-trainer sessions at each plant.

5.2 **Go-Live.** Go-live dates for each Licensed Facility will be agreed in writing by the Parties' project managers under Section 11.3 of the Agreement. The Parties' current target is to complete go-live at all Licensed Facilities within six (6) months after the Order Date.

5.3 **Project Managers.** Each Party shall designate a project manager for the implementation and notify the other Party of that designation within ten (10) days after the Order Date.
