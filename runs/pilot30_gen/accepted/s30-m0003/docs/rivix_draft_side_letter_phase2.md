**Re: Side Letter: BakePlan Phase 2 Plant Rollout, Software License Agreement dated March 2, 2026**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into between Rivix Systems, Inc. ("**Licensor**") and Veilound Bakeries, Inc. ("**Licensee**"). It refers to the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. This Side Letter is a Side Letter for purposes of the Agreement.

Licensee has told Licensor that it intends to extend use of the Software beyond the Licensed Facilities identified in Order Form No. 1 to additional bakery plants as part of its second-phase rollout ("**Phase 2**"). In connection with Phase 2, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Phase 2 Commercial Terms

1.1 **Phase 2 Order Form.** The Parties intend to execute a further Order Form covering the Phase 2 facilities, Software modules and Authorized User quantities (the "**Phase 2 Order Form**"). The Phase 2 Order Form will be governed by the Agreement.

1.2 **Phase 2 Discount.** For the initial subscription period of the Phase 2 Order Form, Licensor shall apply a discount of fifteen percent (15%) to its then-current list price for each Authorized User and each Software module ordered under the Phase 2 Order Form.

1.3 **Implementation Credit.** Licensor shall provide Licensee with a one-time credit of $40,000. The credit applies against implementation, configuration and training Fees for the Phase 2 facilities under Section 11.3 of the Agreement. Any part of the credit that is not used within twelve (12) months after the date of the Phase 2 Order Form expires.

1.4 **Price Hold.** Licensor shall not increase the per-user rate stated in Order Form No. 1 for the remainder of its initial subscription period.

## 2. Exclusivity

2.1 Section 2.6 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "2.6 **Exclusivity.** In consideration of the pricing in Order Form No. 1 and the Phase 2 commercial terms, Licensee agrees that neither Licensee nor any of its Affiliates shall license, develop or use any production planning, inventory management, recipe management or warehouse management software from any provider other than Licensor, whether at the Licensed Facilities or at any facility worldwide, during the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter."

2.2 Licensee shall cause each of its Affiliates, including Veilound Wholesale Distribution LLC, to comply with Section 2.6 of the Agreement as amended by this Paragraph 2.

## 3. Phase 2 Implementation

3.1 **Project Plan.** Within thirty (30) days after the Phase 2 Order Form is signed, Licensor shall deliver a written implementation plan for the Phase 2 facilities. The plan will include proposed configuration milestones, data migration steps and training sessions. Go-live dates for each Phase 2 facility will be agreed in writing by the Parties' project managers under Section 11.3 of the Agreement.

3.2 **Dedicated Resources.** During Phase 2 implementation, Licensor shall assign a named implementation lead and at least one solutions engineer with experience in commercial bakery recipe and allergen configuration.

3.3 **Support.** The support and availability commitments in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 of the Agreement apply to each Phase 2 facility from its go-live date.

## 4. Amendment to Section 9.3

Section 9.3(b) of the Agreement is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "(b) a Party's breach of Section 6.1 (Confidentiality);"

## 5. General

5.1 **Effect.** Except as amended by this Side Letter, the Agreement and Order Form No. 1 remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

5.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Side Letter is governed by Section 15 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.

5.4 **Effectiveness.** This Side Letter becomes binding when executed by authorized representatives of both Parties, as required by Section 14.1 of the Agreement.
