## 1. Background

1.1 This letter is a notice of audit findings under Sections 4.2, 4.3 and 13 of the Software License Agreement between Rivix Systems, Inc. ("**Rivix**" or "**Licensor**") and Veilound Bakeries, Inc. ("**Veilound**" or "**Licensee**"), entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). It also concerns BakePlan Order Form No. 1 dated March 2, 2026 ("**Order Form No. 1**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement and Order Form No. 1.

1.2 On September 14, 2026, Rivix gave Veilound written notice under Section 4.2 of the Agreement that it would audit Veilound's use of the Software. The audit began on October 15, 2026. Rivix conducted the audit remotely during normal business hours using the usage and credential reports generated by the Software, so no on-site visit was required at any Licensed Facility. Rivix has borne all costs of the audit, as required by Section 4.2.

1.3 Order Form No. 1 licenses Veilound for 150 Authorized Users of the bundled BakePlan Production Scheduler and Recipe Manager modules. The annual fee is $1,800 per Authorized User.

## 2. Audit Findings

2.1 Section 1.1 of Order Form No. 1 defines an "Authorized User" as a named employee of Licensee who is located at a Licensed Facility and has been issued unique login credentials to access the Software. Rivix has measured Veilound's licensed usage against that definition.

2.2 The Software's credential reports for the audit period of March 2, 2026 through October 15, 2026 show 212 individuals holding active login credentials to the production environment. The reports exclude the non-production environment under Section 2.4 of Order Form No. 1. Of those 212 individuals:

(a) 150 are named employees of Veilound Bakeries, Inc. located at a Licensed Facility. These users fall within the Licensed Quantity;

(b) 34 are seasonal agency staff and independent contractors engaged at the Allentown Bread Plant, the Reading Bun and Roll Plant and the Hagerstown Sweet Goods Plant. They are not employees of Licensee; and

(c) 28 are personnel of Veilound Wholesale Distribution LLC working at the Lehigh Valley Distribution Center. They are not employees of Licensee.

2.3 The 62 individuals described in Paragraphs 2.2(b) and 2.2(c) are therefore not counted within the 150 Authorized Users licensed under Order Form No. 1. Each of them has accessed the Software at least once during the audit period and has been issued an unlicensed credential. A schedule identifying each credential by user ID, facility, first login date and most recent login date is enclosed as Attachment A.

## 3. True-Up Demand

Under Section 4.3(a) of the Agreement and Sections 3.1 and 3.2 of Order Form No. 1, Rivix demands payment of **$111,600 for 62 excess Authorized Users**. This amount is calculated at $1,800 per Authorized User for the current Subscription Year. Rivix will issue an invoice for this amount under separate cover. Payment is due within thirty (30) days after Veilound's receipt of this letter, as Section 4.3 of the Agreement provides.

## 4. Alternative Remedy

As an alternative to paying for the excess Authorized Users on an ongoing basis, Section 4.3(b) of the Agreement allows Veilound to reduce its Authorized Users to the Licensed Quantity and pay the applicable Fees for the period of excess use. If Veilound chooses this option, please tell us in writing within the same thirty (30) day period which credentials will be deactivated. Rivix will then provide a revised calculation of the Fees for the period of excess use.

## 5. Future Usage

From the date of this letter, Rivix asks that Veilound not issue further credentials beyond the Licensed Quantity unless it gives notice under Section 3.5 of the Agreement. Any additional Authorized Users will be billed at the rate stated in Section 3.2 of Order Form No. 1.
