# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Rivix Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Veilound Bakeries, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops and provides BakePlan, a cloud-hosted production-scheduling and recipe-management software platform for commercial food manufacturers.

B. Licensee operates commercial bakery plants and wishes to license BakePlan for use in planning production, managing recipe formulations and maintaining allergen records across its facilities.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Licensor will make BakePlan available to Licensee, with the specific products, quantities, facilities and fees to be stated in one or more Order Forms.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity. Licensee's Affiliates include Veilound Wholesale Distribution LLC.

1.2 "**Authorized Users**" means employees, agents and contractors of Licensee and its Affiliates who are authorized by Licensee to access and use the Software under this Agreement.

1.3 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Licensee Data is Licensee's Confidential Information.

1.4 "**Documentation**" means the user guides, technical specifications and online help materials for the Software that Licensor makes generally available to its customers.

1.5 "**Fees**" means the fees set out in an Order Form.

1.6 "**Licensed Facilities**" means the Licensee and Affiliate plants, warehouses and offices identified as licensed facilities in an Order Form.

1.7 "**Licensee Data**" means all data, content and materials that Licensee or its Authorized Users upload to, enter into or generate through the Software, including recipe formulations, ingredient specifications, allergen data, production volumes and supplier information.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means an ordering document referencing this Agreement and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties, including Order Form No. 1.

1.9 "**Security Incident**" means any actual unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction of, Licensee Data while in the possession, custody or control of Licensor or its subcontractors.

1.10 "**Side Letter**" means a letter agreement that references this Agreement and has been executed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

1.11 "**Software**" means the BakePlan hosted software, including the production-scheduling and recipe-management modules identified in an Order Form, together with all updates, upgrades and Documentation that Licensor provides under this Agreement.

1.12 "**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

2.1 **License.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of the applicable Fees, Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 16.2) license during the Term for its Authorized Users to access and use the Software for the internal business operations of Licensee and its Affiliates, in the quantities stated in the applicable Order Form.

2.2 **Affiliates.** Licensee may permit its Affiliates to use the Software through Authorized Users. Licensee is responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement as if that Affiliate were Licensee.

2.3 **User Credentials.** Each Authorized User will receive unique login credentials. Credentials may not be shared between individuals. Licensee may reassign a credential from one individual to another on a permanent basis, for example on termination of employment or completion of a contractor engagement.

2.4 **Restrictions.** Licensee shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Software; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Software, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits this despite this restriction; (c) sublicense, sell, rent or lease the Software to any person other than its Affiliates; (d) use the Software to provide service-bureau or time-sharing services to third parties; or (e) remove any proprietary notices from the Software.

2.5 **Third-Party Components.** The Software may include open-source or third-party components. These are licensed under their own terms, which Licensor shall identify in the Documentation. Those terms do not limit Licensee's rights under this Agreement.

2.6 **Exclusivity.** In consideration of the pricing in Order Form No. 1, Licensee agrees that it shall license and use no competing production-scheduling software at the Licensed Facilities during the Term. This Section 2.6 does not restrict Licensee's use of enterprise resource planning, accounting, inventory, warehouse or maintenance software, or of Licensee's existing spreadsheet-based planning tools during any implementation or transition period.

## 3. ORDER FORMS, FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Order Forms.** Licensee may purchase subscriptions to the Software by executing Order Forms. Each Order Form will specify the Software modules, the number of Authorized Users, the Licensed Facilities, the Fees and the subscription period.

3.2 **Fees.** Licensee shall pay the Fees set out in each Order Form. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, annual Fees are invoiced annually in advance.

3.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Licensor in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any such dispute within thirty (30) days.

3.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay those taxes, except taxes based on Licensor's net income, property or employees.

3.5 **Additional Users.** Licensee may add Authorized Users during a subscription period by notice to Licensor. Additional Authorized Users will be billed at the per-user rate stated in the applicable Order Form, prorated for the remainder of the then-current subscription year.

## 4. RECORDS AND AUDIT

4.1 **Records.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months after it ends, Licensee shall keep reasonably accurate records of the number of Authorized Users who have been issued credentials.

4.2 **Audit.** No more than once in any twelve (12) month period, and on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Licensor may audit Licensee's use of the Software to verify compliance with the quantities licensed under the applicable Order Form. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with Licensee's operations, and may rely on usage reports generated by the Software. Licensor shall bear the costs of each audit.

4.3 **Audit Results.** Licensor shall deliver its audit findings in writing, with reasonable supporting detail. If an audit shows that the number of Authorized Users exceeds the licensed quantity, Licensee shall, within thirty (30) days after receipt of the findings and subject to Section 3.3, either (a) pay the applicable Fees for the excess Authorized Users at the per-user rate in the applicable Order Form, or (b) reduce its Authorized Users to the licensed quantity and pay the applicable Fees for the period of excess use.

5.1 **Licensor Property.** As between the Parties, Licensor owns all right, title and interest in and to the Software, the Documentation and all related intellectual property rights. No rights are granted to Licensee other than those expressly set out in this Agreement.

Licensee grants Licensor a non-exclusive license during the Term to host, process and use Licensee Data solely to provide the Software and support services to Licensee.

5.3 **Feedback.** Licensor may use any suggestions or feedback that Licensee provides about the Software without restriction, provided that Licensor does not identify Licensee or disclose Licensee's Confidential Information.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY

6.1 **Confidentiality.** The Recipient shall: (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar nature, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 6.1. These obligations do not apply to information that the Recipient can show (i) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient, (ii) was known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure, (iii) is received from a third party without breach of any obligation of confidentiality, or (iv) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information. The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

6.2 **Security Program.** Licensor shall maintain a written information security program with administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of Licensee Data. The program shall be consistent with industry standards for hosted business software, including encryption of Licensee Data in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, logging and monitoring, and annual independent third-party audit (SOC 2 Type II or equivalent). Licensor shall provide its most recent audit report to Licensee on request.

6.3 **Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. Licensor shall (a) promptly investigate, contain and remediate the Security Incident; (b) provide Licensee with information reasonably requested about the nature and scope of the Security Incident and the Licensee Data affected; (c) cooperate with Licensee in any notifications to regulators, customers or other third parties that Licensee reasonably determines are required; and (d) reimburse Licensee for the reasonable costs of investigation, notification, remediation and restoration of Licensee Data, to the extent the Security Incident results from Licensor's breach of Section 6.2.

6.4 **Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term, Licensor shall make all Licensee Data available to Licensee for export in a commonly used, machine-readable format. Licensor shall then delete Licensee Data from its systems within sixty (60) days, except for copies retained in routine backups, which remain subject to this Section 6 until overwritten.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years. It then renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term (the initial term and all renewal terms together, the "**Term**"). Each Order Form remains in effect for its stated subscription period, and this Agreement continues to govern any Order Form that remains in effect.

10.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an affected Order Form on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

10.3 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

10.4 **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry, all licenses granted under this Agreement end, and Section 6.4 applies. If Licensee terminates under Section 10.2 or Section 10.3, Licensor shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after termination.

10.5 **Survival.** Sections 4.1, 5, 6, 7.4, 8, 9, 10.4, 10.5, 13, 14, 15 and 16 survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

11.2 **Availability.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Software available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance and performed outside 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time.

11.3 **Implementation.** Licensor shall provide implementation, configuration and training services for each Licensed Facility as described in the applicable Order Form. The Parties shall agree go-live dates for each Licensed Facility in writing through their respective project managers.
