# TERM SHEET

## Nimbus Platform License: Fatesk Interactive Entertainment, LLC / Drupuv Systems, Inc.

**Date:** November 6, 2026

**Status:** Non-binding summary of principal terms, except as stated in Section 15.

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Drupuv Systems, Inc. would license its Nimbus live-service game backend platform to Fatesk Interactive Entertainment, LLC. Drupuv would also perform the related implementation and integration services. The parties intend to set these terms out in definitive agreements: a Platform License Agreement, a Statement of Work for implementation services and a Service Level Schedule (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**").

## 1. Parties

1.1 **Licensor:** Drupuv Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Drupuv**").

1.2 **Licensee:** Fatesk Interactive Entertainment, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Fatesk**").

## 2. Licensed Platform

2.1 **Platform.** The Platform is Drupuv's Nimbus live-service backend platform. It covers matchmaking, player login and authentication, cross-platform accounts, and in-game commerce and storefront services, together with the related SDKs, APIs, tools, documentation and all generally available updates (the "**Platform**").

2.2 **License.** The license would be non-exclusive and worldwide. It would cover Fatesk and its affiliates, and would permit use of the Platform to develop, operate, support and commercialize Fatesk's online titles. It would also permit Fatesk to embed SDK components in those titles in object-code form.

2.3 **Initial Licensed Titles.** The initial Licensed Titles are:

(a) *Ironveil: Frontier* (PC, PlayStation, Xbox);

(b) the *Ironveil: Frontier* mobile companion application (iOS, Android); and

(c) *Project Halcyon* (working title; PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo).

Fatesk may add titles by notice, with no change to the annual license fee, provided aggregate use stays within the Licensed Concurrency.

2.4 **Licensed Concurrency.** The license would cover 750,000 peak concurrent users across all Licensed Titles. Drupuv would continue to serve all End Users during any overage. Additional capacity would be priced at the overage rate in Section 4.3.

## 3. Implementation Services

3.1 **Scope.** Drupuv would perform discovery and integration design, SDK integration, data migration of existing *Ironveil: Frontier* account and entitlement data, and launch-readiness work, including load testing at the Licensed Concurrency and 72-hour launch support.

3.2 **Fee.** The implementation fee would be a $420,000 fixed fee. It would be payable against milestones, with no additional time-and-materials charges for the agreed scope.

3.3 **Acceptance.** Each milestone would be subject to acceptance testing by Fatesk against agreed criteria.

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Annual License Fee.** The annual license fee would be $1,840,000 per contract year, invoiced quarterly in advance.

4.2 **Payment Terms.** Payment would be due net 45 days. Fatesk could withhold amounts it disputes in good faith.

4.3 **Overage.** Additional Licensed Concurrency would be available in blocks of 50,000 peak concurrent users at $96,000 per block per contract year, pro-rated.

## 5. Service Levels and Incident Response

5.1 **Service Levels.** Availability and performance commitments and service credits would be set out in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits would not be Fatesk's exclusive remedy.

5.2 **Severity 1 Incident.** A "Severity 1 Incident" would be an incident making matchmaking, login or in-game purchases unavailable to 10% or more of End Users.

5.3 **Response.** Drupuv would respond to each Severity 1 Incident within one hour, provide hourly status updates and deliver a root cause analysis within five business days after resolution.

5.4 **Chronic Failure.** Fatesk could terminate if three or more Severity 1 Incidents occur in any rolling 90-day period. On that termination, prepaid fees would be refunded.

5.5 **Launch Windows.** Fatesk could designate up to four launch windows per contract year. Drupuv would not perform scheduled maintenance during a launch window and would provide elevated on-call staffing.

## 6. Data and Security

6.1 Fatesk would own all Fatesk Data, including account, entitlement, transaction, matchmaking and telemetry data.

6.2 Drupuv would maintain industry-standard security, including encryption in transit and at rest and an annual SOC 2 Type II audit. Drupuv would notify Fatesk within 48 hours after becoming aware of a security incident.

6.3 Fatesk Data would be available for export during the term and for 90 days afterward, and would then be deleted with written certification.

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 Drupuv would retain ownership of the Platform. Fatesk would retain ownership of its titles and Fatesk Data.

7.2 Fatesk would own the implementation deliverables created specifically for its titles once the implementation fee is paid in full.

## 8. Term and Renewal

8.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term would be three years from the effective date of the Platform License Agreement.

8.2 **Renewal.** The agreement would provide for one-year renewals; either party may decline renewal on 90 days' notice before term end.

## 9. Termination and Transition

9.1 Either party could terminate for an uncured material breach (30-day cure period) or for the insolvency of the other party.

9.2 On expiration or termination, Fatesk could elect a transition period of up to 180 days on existing terms at a pro-rated fee. During that period, Drupuv would provide migration assistance.

## 10. Assignment

10.1 **Fatesk.** Fatesk may not assign without Drupuv's consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

10.2 **Drupuv.** Drupuv may not assign to a Fatesk competitor without Fatesk's prior written consent.

## 11. Liability

11.1 Each party's liability would be capped at the fees paid and payable in the preceding 12 months. Neither party would be liable for consequential damages.

11.2 The cap and the exclusion would not apply to indemnification obligations, data and confidentiality breaches, or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11.3 Drupuv would indemnify Fatesk against third-party IP infringement claims relating to the Platform.

## 12. Governing Law and Forum

The Definitive Agreements would be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. They would provide for the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

## 13. Target Timetable

The parties are targeting signature of the Definitive Agreements with an effective date of February 1, 2027.

## 14. Confidentiality

The existence and contents of this term sheet are confidential. Neither party may disclose them except to its advisors who need to know them.

## 15. Non-Binding Effect

This term sheet is not a binding agreement and does not create any obligation to enter into the Definitive Agreements. Only Section 14 and this Section 15 are binding. No other obligation will arise unless and until the Definitive Agreements are signed by both parties.
