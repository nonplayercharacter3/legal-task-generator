# PLATFORM LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Platform License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2027 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Drupuv Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, with its principal place of business at 1100 Mission Ridge Parkway, Suite 400, Austin, Texas 78746 ("**Drupuv**" or "**Licensor**"); and

**Fatesk Interactive Entertainment, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 300 Delaware Avenue, Suite 900, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Fatesk**" or "**Licensee**").

Drupuv and Fatesk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** Drupuv develops and operates the Nimbus live-service game backend platform. Fatesk publishes online video games and wishes to license the Platform for use with its online titles. Drupuv will also perform implementation and integration services under a separate Statement of Work.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms have the meanings set out below or where they are first defined in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Documentation**" means Drupuv's then-current technical documentation, API references, SDK guides and integration manuals for the Platform, as made available to Fatesk.

1.3 "**End User**" means any individual who accesses or uses a Licensed Title through an account that is created, authenticated or managed through the Platform.

1.4 "**Fatesk Data**" means all data that Fatesk, its Affiliates or End Users submit to, or that is generated through, the Platform in connection with the Licensed Titles. This includes End User account data, profile data, entitlements, purchase and transaction records, matchmaking data and telemetry.

1.5 "**Implementation Services**" means the implementation, SDK integration, data migration and launch-readiness services described in the Statement of Work.

1.6 "**Licensed Concurrency**" means 750,000 peak concurrent users across all Licensed Titles.

1.7 "**Licensed Titles**" means the online video game titles published by Fatesk or its Affiliates that are listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.8 "**Permitted Transfer**" means any merger, change of control, or sale of all or any part of the business or assets to which this Agreement relates, to any person.

1.9 "**Platform**" means Drupuv's Nimbus live-service backend platform. This includes its matchmaking, player login and authentication, cross-platform account, and in-game commerce and storefront services, together with the related SDKs, APIs, tools and Documentation, and all Updates.

1.10 "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.2.

1.11 "**Severity 1 Incident**" means an incident making matchmaking, login or in-game purchases unavailable to 10% or more of End Users.

1.12 "**SLA Schedule**" means the Nimbus Service Level Schedule agreed by the Parties and attached to this Agreement.

1.13 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means the Nimbus Implementation Statement of Work entered into by the Parties, together with any later statement of work the Parties sign that references this Agreement.

1.14 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.15 "**Updates**" means all patches, fixes, upgrades, new versions and enhancements to the Platform that Drupuv makes generally available to its licensees.

## 2. License Grant

2.1 **License.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of the applicable fees, Drupuv grants Fatesk and its Affiliates a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 15) worldwide license during the Term to:

(a) access and use the Platform to develop, test, operate, support and commercialize the Licensed Titles;

(b) integrate the SDKs and APIs into the Licensed Titles and distribute those SDK components in object-code form as embedded in the Licensed Titles; and

(c) use and reproduce the Documentation in support of the foregoing.

2.2 **Licensed Concurrency.** Fatesk's use of the Platform is licensed up to the Licensed Concurrency. If the measured peak concurrent users across all Licensed Titles exceed the Licensed Concurrency in any calendar month, Drupuv will continue to serve all End Users. The Parties will then meet in good faith to agree an increase to the Licensed Concurrency at the overage rate set out in Exhibit B.

2.3 **Contractors.** Fatesk may permit its third-party contractors and co-development studios to use the Platform solely on Fatesk's behalf. Fatesk remains responsible for their compliance with this Agreement.

2.4 **Additional Titles.** Fatesk may add online titles to Exhibit A by written notice to Drupuv. Adding a title does not change the annual license fee, provided aggregate use remains within the Licensed Concurrency.

## 3. Restrictions

3.1 Except as expressly permitted in this Agreement, Fatesk shall not:

(a) sublicense, sell, lease or otherwise make the Platform available to any third party other than End Users through the Licensed Titles;

(b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Platform, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits it despite this restriction;

(c) use the Platform to build a competing backend platform; or

(d) remove or alter any proprietary notices on the Platform or Documentation.

3.2 Drupuv reserves all rights not expressly granted in this Agreement.

## 4. Implementation Services

4.1 **Statement of Work.** Drupuv shall perform the Implementation Services described in the Statement of Work. The Statement of Work sets out the scope, milestones, acceptance criteria and fees for the Implementation Services.

4.2 **Personnel.** Drupuv shall assign qualified personnel with experience in live-service game backends to perform the Implementation Services. Drupuv may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Fatesk. Any replacement must have equivalent qualifications.

4.3 **Cooperation.** Fatesk shall provide timely access to the build environments, source branches, personnel and information reasonably required for Drupuv to perform the Implementation Services, as described in the Statement of Work.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Annual License Fee.** Fatesk shall pay Drupuv an annual license fee of $1,840,000 per contract year. A "contract year" is each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

5.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Drupuv shall invoice the annual license fee in equal quarterly installments in advance. Fatesk shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Fatesk may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, if it notifies Drupuv in writing of the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve any such dispute.

5.3 **Renewal Fee Adjustment.** At the start of each Renewal Term, Drupuv may increase the annual license fee by up to nine percent (9%) over the annual license fee in effect for the final contract year of the preceding term. Drupuv must give Fatesk written notice of any such increase at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the start of the Renewal Term.

5.4 **Implementation Fees.** Fees for the Implementation Services are set out in the Statement of Work.

5.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Fatesk shall pay. Taxes based on Drupuv's net income, property or employees are excluded from Fatesk's obligation.

5.6 **Service Credits.** Service credits that accrue under the SLA Schedule shall be applied against the next quarterly installment of the annual license fee. Service credits accrued in the final quarter of the Term shall be paid to Fatesk in cash within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term.

## 6. Platform Operation

6.1 **Hosting and Operation.** Drupuv shall host, operate, maintain and secure the Platform in accordance with this Agreement, the Documentation and the SLA Schedule.

6.2 **Updates.** Drupuv shall make all Updates available to Fatesk at no additional charge. Drupuv shall not make any Update that materially reduces the functionality of the matchmaking, login, cross-platform account or in-game commerce services. Drupuv shall give Fatesk at least one hundred twenty (120) days' notice before deprecating any API on which a Licensed Title relies.

6.3 **Launch Windows.** Fatesk may designate up to four (4) launch windows per contract year, of up to fourteen (14) days each, by notice given at least thirty (30) days in advance. During a designated launch window, Drupuv shall not perform scheduled maintenance affecting the Licensed Titles. Drupuv shall also provide the elevated on-call staffing described in the SLA Schedule.

## 7. Support and Incident Response

7.1 **Support.** Drupuv shall provide technical support to Fatesk twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, through the support channels described in the SLA Schedule.

7.2 **Severity 1 Response.** For each Severity 1 Incident, Drupuv shall:

(a) respond within one (1) hour after the earlier of Drupuv's detection of the incident or Fatesk's report of it;

(b) assign senior engineering resources to work continuously on the incident until it is resolved or a workaround is in place; and

(c) provide Fatesk with status updates at least every hour until resolution.

7.3 **Other Incidents.** Drupuv shall respond to and resolve all other incidents within the times set out in the SLA Schedule.

7.4 **Root Cause Analysis.** Within five (5) business days after resolving any Severity 1 Incident, Drupuv shall deliver a written root cause analysis to Fatesk. The analysis shall describe the cause, the remediation taken and the steps Drupuv will take to prevent recurrence.

## 8. Service Levels

8.1 **Service Levels.** Drupuv shall meet the availability and performance service levels set out in the SLA Schedule.

8.2 **Measurement and Reporting.** Drupuv shall measure its performance against the service levels and deliver a monthly report to Fatesk within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month.

8.3 **Service Credits.** If Drupuv fails to meet a service level, Fatesk shall be entitled to the service credits set out in the SLA Schedule. Service credits do not limit Fatesk's other rights and remedies under this Agreement.

8.4 **Termination for Chronic Failure.** If three (3) or more Severity 1 Incidents occur in any rolling ninety (90) day period, Fatesk may terminate this Agreement on written notice to Drupuv. Fatesk must give that notice within sixty (60) days after the third such incident. Upon any such termination, Drupuv shall refund all prepaid fees for the period after the termination date. The provisions of Section 12.5 shall also apply.

## 9. Data and Security

9.1 **Ownership of Fatesk Data.** As between the Parties, Fatesk owns all Fatesk Data. Drupuv shall process Fatesk Data only to provide the Platform and the Implementation Services, and only in accordance with Fatesk's documented instructions.

9.2 **Security.** Drupuv shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for the Platform and Fatesk Data that are consistent with industry standards. These include, at a minimum, encryption of Fatesk Data in transit and at rest, and an annual SOC 2 Type II audit, the report of which Drupuv shall provide to Fatesk on request.

9.3 **Security Incidents.** Drupuv shall notify Fatesk without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Fatesk Data. Drupuv shall cooperate with Fatesk's investigation and remediation of the incident.

9.4 **Privacy Laws.** Each Party shall comply with all privacy and data protection laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement. The Parties shall enter into any data processing addendum reasonably required under those laws.

9.5 **Return of Data.** During the Term and for ninety (90) days afterward, Drupuv shall make all Fatesk Data available to Fatesk for export in a standard, machine-readable format. After that period, Drupuv shall delete the Fatesk Data and certify the deletion in writing.

## 10. Intellectual Property

10.1 **Drupuv Materials.** Drupuv retains all right, title and interest in and to the Platform, the Documentation and its pre-existing materials, including all Updates and improvements.

10.2 **Fatesk Materials.** Fatesk retains all right, title and interest in and to the Licensed Titles, Fatesk Data and its pre-existing materials, including all game code, art, characters and trademarks.

10.3 **Deliverables.** The ownership of deliverables created under the Statement of Work is set out in the Statement of Work.

10.4 **Feedback.** Drupuv may use any suggestions Fatesk provides about the Platform. Drupuv may not identify Fatesk as the source of those suggestions without Fatesk's consent.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other Party's confidential information with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care. Each Party shall use the other's confidential information only to perform under this Agreement and shall disclose it only to employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as these.

11.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was already known to the receiving Party, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

11.3 **Unreleased Titles.** Information concerning unreleased Licensed Titles, release dates, game content and Fatesk's player metrics is Fatesk's confidential information whether or not it is marked.

11.4 **Duration.** These obligations continue for five (5) years after the end of the Term. Obligations concerning trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Agreement.

12.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive three (3) year Renewal Terms. It shall not renew if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal. A notice of non-renewal is effective only if delivered no earlier than one hundred fifty (150) days and no later than one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

12.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.5 **Effect of Termination; Transition.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement:

(a) at Fatesk's request, Drupuv shall continue to provide the Platform for a transition period of up to one hundred eighty (180) days on the terms of this Agreement, at a pro-rated portion of the annual license fee;

(b) during that transition period, Drupuv shall provide reasonable transition assistance to help Fatesk migrate End User accounts, entitlements and Fatesk Data to a successor platform;

(c) Section 9.5 shall apply to the return and deletion of Fatesk Data; and

(d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's confidential information, subject to Section 9.5.

12.6 **Survival.** Sections 5 (as to accrued amounts), 9, 10, 11, 12.5, 12.6, 13.3, 14 and 17 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. Warranties

13.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

13.2 **Drupuv.** Drupuv warrants that:

(a) the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation;

(b) the Implementation Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards; and

(c) the Platform, as delivered, will not contain any virus, malicious code or undisclosed disabling device.

13.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 14. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability

14.1 **Drupuv Indemnity.** Drupuv shall defend Fatesk and its Affiliates against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform infringes or misappropriates any intellectual property right. Drupuv shall pay any damages, settlements and costs finally awarded against them on such a claim.

14.2 **Fatesk Indemnity.** Fatesk shall defend Drupuv against any third-party claim alleging that the Licensed Titles, excluding the Platform, infringe or misappropriate any intellectual property right. Fatesk shall pay any damages, settlements and costs finally awarded against Drupuv on such a claim.

14.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of the claim, reasonable cooperation and sole control of the defense to the indemnifying Party. The indemnifying Party may not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

14.4 **Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits arising out of this Agreement.

14.5 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

14.6 **Exceptions.** Sections 14.4 and 14.5 do not apply to a Party's indemnification obligations, breach of Section 9 or Section 11, or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 15. Assignment

15.1 **By Fatesk.** Fatesk may not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Drupuv's prior written consent. Drupuv shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay that consent.

15.2 **By Drupuv.** Drupuv may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Fatesk's consent in connection with a Permitted Transfer. Drupuv shall give Fatesk written notice of the assignment within thirty (30) days after it takes effect.

15.3 **Effect.** Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. Notices

16.1 All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They shall be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation sent by one of those methods, to the addresses stated on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

16.2 A notice is effective on receipt. Notices to Fatesk shall be copied to its General Counsel at the address stated on the signature page.

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

17.2 **Forum.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

17.3 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to protect its intellectual property or confidential information. A Party seeking such relief is not required to post a bond.

17.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, they shall prevail in the following order: the SOW (as to Implementation Services), then the SLA Schedule (as to service levels and incident classification), then this License Agreement.

17.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events do not include failures of Drupuv's subcontractors or hosting providers that Drupuv's business continuity and redundancy measures would reasonably have prevented. Any such event does not excuse Drupuv's obligations under Section 7.2 or Section 9.

17.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

17.7 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing. A Party's failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of that provision.

17.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions shall remain in effect.

17.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
