## 2. Premises

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the premises known as Suite 900 on the ninth (9th) floor of the Building, as shown on Exhibit A (the "**Premises**"). Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the lobbies, elevators, corridors, restrooms, loading dock and other common areas of the Building (the "**Common Areas**") in common with other tenants, subject to the Building Rules.

2.2 **Measurement.** The rentable area of the Premises and of the Building have been measured in accordance with the ANSI/BOMA Z65.1 office measurement standard and are stipulated for all purposes of this Lease. Neither Party may remeasure them during the Term except in connection with an expansion or reduction of the Premises.

2.3 **Condition of Premises.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises on the Commencement Date with the Landlord Work described in Section 2.4 substantially complete and with the base Building systems serving the Premises in good working order. Except for the Landlord Work and latent defects of which Tenant notifies Landlord within twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition.

2.4 **Landlord Work; Allowance.** Landlord shall, at its cost, perform the base building work described in Exhibit A (the "**Landlord Work**"). Landlord shall also provide Tenant an improvement allowance of $65.00 per rentable square foot of the Premises (the "**Allowance**") toward the cost of Tenant's initial improvements, including the studio build-out, server room, sound-isolated audio rooms and motion-capture space. Landlord shall disburse the Allowance monthly against invoices, lien waivers and an architect's certification of completed work. Any portion of the Allowance not requested within twelve (12) months after the Commencement Date shall lapse.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Term.** The term of this Lease begins on April 1, 2025 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ends on March 31, 2032 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless extended under Section 3.2 or terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease (the "**Term**").

3.2 **Renewal Option.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for a period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease except that Base Rent shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent. Tenant shall exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given not less than nine (9) months and not more than fifteen (15) months before the Expiration Date. The option may be exercised only if Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay and a willing landlord would accept for comparable space in comparable Class A office buildings in downtown Wilmington, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the Parties cannot agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's exercise notice, it shall be determined by the appraisal process in Section 3.3.

3.3 **Appraisal.** Each Party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the Wilmington office market within fifteen (15) days after either Party's request. If the two brokers' determinations differ by five percent (5%) or less, Fair Market Rent is their average. Otherwise, the two brokers shall jointly appoint a third broker, who shall select whichever of the first two determinations is closer to the third broker's own view of Fair Market Rent. Each Party shall bear the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.

| 1 (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2026) | $31.50 |
| 2 (April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027) | $32.45 |

| 4 (April 1, 2028 – March 31, 2029) | $34.42 |

Base Rent for Lease Year 1 is $448,875.00, payable in monthly installments of $37,406.25.

4.2 **Abatement.** Base Rent for the first three (3) full calendar months after the Commencement Date is abated. Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes during that period to the extent they exceed the Base Year amounts.

4.3 **Payment.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, by ACH transfer to the account Landlord designates by notice. Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis. All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease are "**Rent**."

4.4 **Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) business days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve (12) month period if Tenant pays within five (5) business days after written notice of the late payment.

## 5. Operating Expenses and Taxes

5.1 **Tenant's Share.** Beginning January 1, 2026, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year. "**Tenant's Share**" is 5.0%, being the rentable area of the Premises divided by the rentable area of the Building.

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair and insure the Building and the Common Areas, in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude: capital expenditures, except those that reduce Operating Expenses (amortized over their useful life, to the extent of the savings) or that are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date; costs of leasing, brokerage commissions and tenant improvements for other tenants; debt service and ground rent; costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants; Landlord's general corporate overhead; and management fees above three percent (3%) of gross Building revenues. Controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than insurance, utilities, snow removal and Taxes) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis over the Base Year.

5.3 **Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied against the Building and the land on which it stands, excluding Landlord's income, franchise, estate and transfer taxes. If Landlord obtains a refund of Taxes for any year, Landlord shall credit Tenant's Share of the refund, net of reasonable costs of obtaining it.

5.4 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied in any calendar year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.5 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Tenant shall pay monthly, with Base Rent, one-twelfth (1/12) of Landlord's reasonable estimate of Tenant's Share of excess Operating Expenses and Taxes. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receipt of the statement.

5.6 **Audit.** Tenant may audit Landlord's books and records for any calendar year by notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving the annual statement. If the audit shows that Tenant was overcharged by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs in addition to the overcharge.

## 6. Security Deposit

On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit $74,812.50 with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance (the "**Security Deposit**"). Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any default by Tenant that continues beyond the applicable cure period, and Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within ten (10) days after notice. Landlord may commingle the Security Deposit with its general funds, and no interest accrues on it. Landlord shall return any unapplied balance to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Section 18.1.

## 7. Use

7.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for general office use and for video game development studio use, including software development, art and animation production, audio recording, motion capture, quality assurance testing and the operation of servers and network equipment for those purposes, and for no other purpose without Landlord's consent.

7.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, base Building systems and Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act, except to the extent compliance is required by Tenant's alterations or particular use.

7.3 **Building Rules.** Tenant shall comply with the Building rules in Exhibit B and with any reasonable, non-discriminatory changes to them of which Landlord gives Tenant at least thirty (30) days' notice. If any Building rule conflicts with an express provision of the body of this Lease, the provision of the body of this Lease applies.

7.4 **Access.** Tenant and its employees may access the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's security procedures.

## 8. Services and Utilities

8.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall provide the following services, the cost of which is included in Operating Expenses:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding Building holidays;

(b) passenger elevator service at all times, and freight elevator service during business hours and at other times by reasonable arrangement;

(c) janitorial service five (5) nights a week to Class A office standards;

(d) electricity to the Premises sufficient for a connected load of at least eight (8) watts per usable square foot, exclusive of base Building HVAC; and

(e) water for restrooms, kitchenettes and drinking fountains.

8.2 **After-Hours and Supplemental HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside the hours in Section 8.1(a) on Tenant's request at Landlord's actual cost, currently $65.00 per hour per floor. Tenant may install, at its cost, a supplemental HVAC unit for its server room, connected to the Building condenser water system, with Landlord's consent to the plans, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold.

8.3 **Electricity Submetering.** Electricity consumed in the Premises shall be measured by a submeter that Landlord installs as part of the Landlord Work. Tenant shall pay for that electricity at Landlord's actual cost, without markup, within thirty (30) days after invoice.

8.4 **Parking.** Landlord shall make available to Tenant thirty (30) unreserved parking spaces in the Building garage at the prevailing monthly rate for the garage, currently $185.00 per space per month.

8.5 **Interruption.** If any service Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than three (3) consecutive business days for a reason within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable, Rent shall abate in proportion to the untenantable area from the fourth (4th) business day until the service is restored.

8.6 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may select its own telecommunications and fiber providers. Landlord shall provide reasonable access to the Building risers and telecommunications rooms for that purpose, subject to Landlord's reasonable requirements.

## 9. Alterations

9.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations (such as painting, carpeting and furniture installation) costing less than $50,000 in any single project that do not affect the Building structure or systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord at least ten (10) business days' prior notice.

9.2 **Performance.** All alterations shall be performed in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with law, by licensed and insured contractors reasonably approved by Landlord.

9.3 **Removal.** At the time Landlord consents to an alteration, Landlord shall state whether Tenant must remove it at the end of the Term. Tenant need not remove any alteration that Landlord did not so designate, or any part of Tenant's initial improvements other than the motion-capture rigging and the supplemental server room HVAC unit.

9.4 **Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Building free from mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant. Tenant shall discharge or bond over any such lien within thirty (30) days after notice of it.

## 10. Repairs and Maintenance

10.1 **Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good condition and to the standard of comparable Class A office buildings in downtown Wilmington, the structure and roof of the Building, the Common Areas, and the base Building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire and life safety and elevator systems.

10.2 **Tenant.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall repair any damage to the Building caused by Tenant's negligence or willful misconduct, subject to Section 11.4.

10.3 **Entry by Landlord.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, or without notice in an emergency, to inspect, perform repairs or show the Premises to prospective lenders and purchasers, and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, to prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security and confidentiality procedures, including escort requirements for areas where unreleased game content is in development.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain during the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance on Tenant's personal property and alterations, on a special form causes of loss basis, for their full replacement cost; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and employer's liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000.

11.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building, excluding Tenant's personal property and alterations, on a special form causes of loss basis for its full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings.

11.3 **Certificates.** Each Party shall deliver certificates of its insurance to the other before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

11.4 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent covered by the property insurance it is required to carry under this Lease, and each Party shall cause its property insurer to waive subrogation accordingly.

## 12. Indemnity

12.1 **By Tenant.** Subject to Section 11.4, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents.

12.2 **By Landlord.** Subject to Section 11.4, Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents.

12.3 **Consequential Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for lost profits or consequential damages, except for Tenant's liability under Section 18.2.

## 13. Casualty

13.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building is damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant a contractor's estimate of the time needed to restore it. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 13.2, Landlord shall diligently restore the Building and the Premises, excluding Tenant's alterations and personal property.

13.2 **Termination.** Either Party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after receipt of the estimate if the estimated restoration period exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, or if the casualty occurs during the last eighteen (18) months of the Term and the estimated restoration period exceeds ninety (90) days.

13.3 **Abatement.** Rent shall abate in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable from the date of the casualty until Landlord completes its restoration.

## 14. Condemnation

If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the Building's access or parking that Tenant cannot reasonably continue its business in the Premises, is taken by eminent domain, either Party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. If this Lease is not terminated, Rent shall be reduced in proportion to the area taken. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving expenses and its unamortized alterations, provided it does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 15. Assignment and Subletting

15.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to any request within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information it reasonably requires.

15.2 **Permitted Transfers by Tenant.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) business days' prior notice, assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, or to a successor to Tenant by merger or by acquisition of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided the successor's net worth is not less than Tenant's net worth on the Lease Date.

15.3 **Profit Sharing.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received under a sublease or assignment requiring consent under Section 15.1 that exceeds the Rent attributable to the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer.

15.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.

15.5 **Transfer by Landlord.** On a sale of the Building, Landlord is released from obligations arising after the sale, provided the purchaser assumes them in writing and the Security Deposit is transferred to the purchaser.

## 16. Default and Remedies

16.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice of the failure, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

16.2 **Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by notice to Tenant, or may terminate Tenant's right of possession without terminating this Lease, and may recover possession through judicial process in accordance with Delaware law. Landlord may recover the Rent due through the date of termination, the reasonable costs of reletting, and the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for that period, discounted to present value at the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia plus one percent (1%).

16.3 **Mitigation.** Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages, including by reletting the Premises.

16.4 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after notice from Tenant (or such longer period as is reasonably required, if Landlord begins the cure within that period and diligently completes it), Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity. If the failure concerns a repair within the Premises that Landlord is required to make and materially interferes with Tenant's use, Tenant may perform the repair after a further five (5) business days' notice, and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 17. Subordination and Estoppel

17.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided the mortgagee enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement with Tenant. Under that agreement, Tenant's possession and rights under this Lease shall not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

17.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each Party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other's request, deliver a certificate confirming the status of this Lease, the Rent payable and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 18. Surrender and Holdover

18.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and condemnation excepted, and shall remove its personal property, its cabling and any alterations required to be removed under Section 9.3.

18.2 **Holdover.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's consent, Tenant shall be a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days of the holdover, and one hundred fifty percent (150%) of that rate thereafter. Tenant is liable for Landlord's consequential damages caused by a holdover only if the holdover continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord notifies Tenant that it has signed a lease with a new tenant for the Premises.

## 19. Notices

All notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a copy sent by one of those methods, to the following addresses or to any other address a Party designates by notice:

**Landlord:** Christina Landing Office Partners, LLC, c/o Riverfront Property Management, 300 Delaware Avenue, Suite 110, Wilmington, Delaware 19801, Attn: Asset Manager.

**Tenant:** Fatesk Interactive Entertainment, LLC, 300 Delaware Avenue, Suite 900, Wilmington, Delaware 19801, Attn: General Counsel.

A notice is effective on receipt or on refusal of delivery.

## 20. Miscellaneous

20.1 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably enjoy the Premises during the Term without interference from Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

20.2 **Brokers.** Each Party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Wilmington Commercial Realty Advisors, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement. Each Party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through it.

20.3 **Signage.** Tenant may, at its cost, install its name and logo on the ninth (9th) floor elevator lobby and on the Building directory, in accordance with the Building's signage standards.

20.4 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring hazardous materials onto the Premises, other than ordinary office, cleaning and electronics maintenance supplies used in compliance with law. Landlord represents that, to its knowledge, there are no hazardous materials in the Premises in violation of law on the Lease Date.

20.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is in default for a delay in performing a non-monetary obligation caused by events beyond its reasonable control, for the duration of the delay.

20.6 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

20.7 **Amendment.** This Lease may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties.

20.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

20.9 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
