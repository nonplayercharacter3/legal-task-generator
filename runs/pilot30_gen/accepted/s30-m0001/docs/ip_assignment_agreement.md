# CONFIRMATORY TRADEMARK AND RECIPE ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This CONFIRMATORY TRADEMARK AND RECIPE ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 22, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and among the following parties:

- **Glegond Dairy Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**");
- **Sorrelfield Organic Dairy, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"); and
- solely for purposes of Recital C and Section 8, **Rilux Foods Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

Seller and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer purchased from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company. The Closing occurred on April 1, 2026.

B. Before the Closing, Seller and its Affiliates registered, maintained and in some cases held record title to the SORRELFIELD trademarks. They also held certain formulations, recipes and process specifications for the Company's organic fluid-milk and yogurt products. Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to execute and deliver this Agreement following the Closing to confirm the Company's title to those assets.

C. On April 30, 2026, Seller delivered to Buyer the Closing Statement under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement. On June 12, 2026, Buyer delivered to Seller a Notice of Disagreement with respect to the Closing Statement. Seller asserts that the Notice of Disagreement was not delivered within the period required by Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement and that the Closing Statement has become final and binding. Buyer disputes Seller's position and reserves all of its rights. As of the Effective Date, the Parties and Buyer have not resolved this dispute.

D. The Parties wish to confirm the assignment of the Assigned IP to the Company, and the Company wishes to grant Seller a license to use certain of the Assigned IP in connection with Seller's retained product lines, in each case on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Assigned IP**" means the Marks and the Formulations, together with:
- all goodwill associated with the Marks;
- all registrations, applications and renewals of the Marks;
- all rights to sue for and recover damages and other remedies for past, present and future infringement, dilution or misappropriation of any of the foregoing; and
- all income, royalties and payments now or hereafter due with respect to any of the foregoing.

"**Formulations**" means the recipes, formulations, ingredient specifications, culture blends, process parameters and quality specifications for the products listed in Part II of **Schedule 1**, in any form or medium, including all related know-how and trade secrets.

"**Licensed IP**" means the Marks and the Formulations identified as licensed in Part III of **Schedule 1**.

"**Marks**" means the trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress listed in Part I of **Schedule 1**, together with all common-law rights in them and all variations and derivatives incorporating the term SORRELFIELD.

"**Retained Product Lines**" means the dairy products manufactured, marketed or sold by Seller or its Affiliates as of the Effective Date, together with reasonable line extensions of those products.

## 2. Confirmation and Assignment

**2.1 Assignment.** To the extent Seller or any of its Affiliates holds any right, title or interest in, to or under the Assigned IP, Seller, on behalf of itself and its Affiliates, hereby irrevocably confirms, sells, assigns, transfers and conveys that right, title and interest to the Company. The Company accepts the assignment. This assignment is effective as of the Closing Date for all purposes.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Marks includes the goodwill of the business connected with and symbolized by the Marks. The Parties intend that the Company shall be the sole owner of the Marks and that goodwill from and after the Closing Date.

**2.3 Recordation.** The Company may record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment in the form of **Exhibit A**, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any other Governmental Authority. Seller authorizes the Commissioner for Trademarks to record the Company as owner of the Marks.

**2.4 Delivery of Formulations.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, Seller shall deliver to the Company complete copies of all documents and electronic files in Seller's or its Affiliates' possession that embody the Formulations. Seller may retain copies of the Licensed IP solely to exercise its rights under Section 4.

## 3. Representations and Warranties of Seller

Seller represents and warrants to the Company as follows:

(a) Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement on behalf of itself and its Affiliates.

(b) Seller has not granted any license, lien or other right in the Assigned IP to any Person other than the Company. The only exception is the license granted to Seller under Section 4.

(c) To Seller's knowledge, no Person is infringing, diluting or misappropriating any of the Assigned IP.

(d) The Formulations delivered under Section 2.4 are complete and accurate in all material respects and are the formulations used by the Company in the Business as of the Closing Date.

## 4. License to Seller

**4.1 Grant.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Company hereby grants to Seller a non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free license, under the Company's rights in the Licensed IP, to use:
- the Marks; and
- the yogurt Formulations identified in Part III of Schedule 1,

in each case in the United States, solely in connection with the manufacture, marketing, distribution and sale of the Retained Product Lines.

**4.2 Sublicensing.** Seller may sublicense its rights under Section 4.1 to its Affiliates and to contract manufacturers and co-packers producing Retained Product Lines for Seller. Seller remains responsible for each sublicensee's compliance with this Agreement.

**4.3 Term.** The license in Section 4.1 commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under Section 4.6.

**4.4 Quality Control.** Seller shall maintain the quality of all products sold under the Marks at a level at least equal to the quality of the corresponding products sold by Seller as of the Closing Date. Seller shall comply with all applicable law in using the Licensed IP, including the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance and applicable labeling requirements. On reasonable prior notice and not more than twice in any calendar year, the Company may inspect samples of products bearing the Marks to confirm compliance with this Section 4.4.

**4.5 Trademark Usage.** Seller shall use the Marks in a form consistent with the Company's reasonable trademark usage guidelines, as provided to Seller in writing from time to time. All use of the Marks by Seller inures to the benefit of the Company. Seller shall not challenge the Company's ownership of, or the validity of, the Marks.

**4.6 Termination.** The Company may terminate the license in Section 4.1 by written notice to Seller in either of the following cases:
- Seller materially breaches Section 4.4 or Section 4.5 and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after receiving written notice of it; or
- Seller ceases to sell all Retained Product Lines bearing the Marks for a continuous period of twelve (12) months.

**4.7 Effect of Termination.** On termination of the license, Seller shall cease all use of the Licensed IP within ninety (90) days. During that period Seller may sell off existing inventory and packaging bearing the Marks.

## 5. Confidentiality of Formulations

**5.1 Confidentiality.** Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and sublicensees to, hold the Formulations in confidence. Seller shall use the Formulations only as permitted by Section 4. Seller shall protect the Formulations with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own trade secrets of similar importance, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**5.2 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Section 5 is in addition to Seller's obligations under Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Further Assurances

**6.1 Further Instruments.** From time to time after the Effective Date, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the Company may reasonably request. These may include declarations, powers of attorney and assignment forms required by any trademark office or registry, to the extent needed to perfect, record or enforce the Company's rights in the Assigned IP.

**6.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file in Seller's name any document described in Section 6.1 that Seller fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after the Company's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest.

## 7. Prosecution and Enforcement

**7.1 Prosecution.** From and after the Effective Date, the Company has the sole right to prosecute, maintain and renew the Marks, at its own cost.

**7.2 Enforcement.** The Company has the sole right to enforce the Assigned IP against third parties. Seller shall promptly notify the Company of any infringement of the Licensed IP of which it becomes aware and shall reasonably cooperate in any enforcement action, at the Company's expense.

## 8. Purchase Price Adjustment

**8.1 Reservation of Rights.** Nothing in this Agreement, and no execution or performance of it, constitutes an admission, waiver or acceptance by Seller or Buyer of any position regarding the matters described in Recital C. Each of Seller and Buyer reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement with respect to the Closing Statement and the Notice of Disagreement.

**8.2 No Set-Off.** No Party shall withhold or delay performance of its obligations under this Agreement by reason of the matters described in Recital C.

## 9. Miscellaneous

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**9.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**9.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and assigns.

**9.4 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be sent to 410 Industrial Park Road, St. Albans, Vermont 05478.

**9.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Equitable Relief.** Seller acknowledges that a breach of Section 5 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief to restrain any such breach, in addition to any other remedy.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
