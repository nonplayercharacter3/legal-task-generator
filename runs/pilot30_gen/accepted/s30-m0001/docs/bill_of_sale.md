# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of April 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), by **Glegond Dairy Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Sorrelfield Organic Dairy, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"). Seller and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Rilux Foods Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller holds title to certain bottling-line equipment and bulk milk tankers used in the Business. Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Seller and the Company agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2** "**Conveyed Assets**" means the bottling-line equipment and bulk milk tankers described on **Schedule A**, together with:
- all spare parts, tooling, attachments, accessories and replacement components for those items;
- all manuals, maintenance logs, sanitation and wash records, and calibration records for those items; and
- to the extent assignable, all manufacturer and vendor warranties relating to those items.

**1.3** "**Liabilities**" means any and all debts, liabilities, obligations, claims, Losses, fines, penalties and expenses of any kind, whether known or unknown, accrued or unaccrued, absolute or contingent, and whether in contract, tort, strict liability or otherwise.

## 2. Conveyance

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller hereby sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Conveyed Assets. The Company accepts the Conveyed Assets.

**2.2 Title Documents.** Each bulk milk tanker listed in Part II of Schedule A is titled with a motor vehicle authority. For each such tanker, Seller has delivered to the Company at the Closing:
- the original certificate of title, duly endorsed for transfer to the Company; and
- any odometer disclosure statements and lien releases required by applicable law.

**2.3 Possession.** The Conveyed Assets are located at the St. Albans Facility or are otherwise in the possession of the Company as of the Closing. Possession passes to the Company at the Closing without further delivery.

## 3. Title; Condition of Assets

**3.1 Title.** Seller represents and warrants to the Company that Seller has good and marketable title to the Conveyed Assets and that the Conveyed Assets are free and clear of all liens, security interests and encumbrances. This Bill of Sale vests in the Company good title to the Conveyed Assets.

**3.2 Condition.** Except as set forth in Section 3.1, the Conveyed Assets are conveyed "AS IS, WHERE IS." SELLER MAKES NO OTHER REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AS TO THE CONDITION OF THE CONVEYED ASSETS. Without limitation, Seller makes no warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 4. Further Assurances

**4.1 Further Instruments.** From time to time after the Closing, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of conveyance and transfer as the Company may reasonably request to vest in the Company title to the Conveyed Assets. This includes any applications for certificates of title or registration with the Vermont Department of Motor Vehicles or any other Governmental Authority.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller hereby irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, for two purposes:
- to demand and receive any of the Conveyed Assets; and
- to endorse, in Seller's name, any certificate of title or registration document relating to the Conveyed Assets.

This power of attorney is coupled with an interest.

## 5. Indemnification

**5.1 Indemnity.** The Company shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective members, managers, officers, directors and employees, from and against any and all Liabilities arising out of or relating to the Conveyed Assets or their ownership, operation, maintenance or use. This indemnity applies to Liabilities whether arising before, on or after the date hereof. It includes Liabilities relating to:
- product contamination, recall or adulteration associated with the bottling-line equipment;
- the operation of the bulk milk tankers, including collisions, spills and personal injury or property damage claims; and
- compliance of the Conveyed Assets with the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance, motor carrier requirements and other applicable law.

**5.2 Amount.** The obligations of the Company under Section 5.1 apply without limitation as to amount.

**5.3 Procedure.** Seller shall give the Company prompt written notice of any claim for which it seeks indemnification under this Section 5. Any delay in giving notice relieves the Company of its obligations only to the extent the Company is materially prejudiced by the delay.
- The Company may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to Seller.
- Neither Party shall settle any such claim without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits Seller and the Company and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**6.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**6.3 Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be sent to the address of the Company set forth on its signature page below.

**6.4 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company.

**6.5 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
