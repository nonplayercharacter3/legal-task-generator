# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 16, 2026, by and between **Glegond Dairy Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Rilux Foods Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Sorrelfield Organic Dairy, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company processes and sells organic fluid milk and yogurt.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale and the IP Assignment Agreement.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale under which Seller conveys to the Company the bottling-line equipment and bulk milk tankers held by Seller and used in the Business (the "**Conveyed Assets**").

"**Business**" means the processing, packaging, marketing and sale of organic fluid milk, cream and yogurt products, as conducted by the Company as of the date hereof.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.4.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company minus its current liabilities as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date. It is calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles set forth in Schedule 2.4.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any agency, court, tribunal or other instrumentality thereof.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the confirmatory assignment agreement under which Seller confirms and assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the SORRELFIELD trademarks and the Company's product formulations.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and, for any taxable period that begins on or before and ends after the Closing Date, the portion of that period ending on the Closing Date.

"**Required Consents**" means the consents, approvals and notices set forth on Schedule 7.2(d).

"**Target Working Capital**" means $6,200,000.

"**Tax**" or "**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, franchise, payroll, property, excise and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions thereto.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to Articles, Sections and Schedules of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Base Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $84,500,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), adjusted as provided in Section 2.4 (as adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the following by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to Seller, the Base Purchase Price, plus or minus the amount by which Seller's good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of Target Working Capital (the "**Estimated Closing Payment**"), less the Indebtedness Payoff Amount; and

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness Payoff Amount, to the holders of Indebtedness in accordance with the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(d).

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written statement of its good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, with reasonable supporting detail.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital and the resulting Purchase Price. The Closing Statement shall be prepared in accordance with the Accounting Principles and shall be accompanied by reasonable supporting schedules.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with the Closing Statement, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written notice of disagreement (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the dispute. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the Parties.

(c) *Resolution.* If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve the disputed items. Either Party may then submit any items that remain unresolved to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually agreed by the Parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. Its determination shall be limited to the disputed items and shall fall within the range of values claimed by the Parties. Its determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the extent each prevails.

(d) *Payment.* Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Statement becomes final:
- if the Purchase Price as finally determined exceeds the Estimated Closing Payment plus the Indebtedness Payoff Amount, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller; and
- if it is less, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third Business Day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived, or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(c) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(d) customary payoff letters for all Indebtedness of the Company (the aggregate amount payable under those letters, the "**Indebtedness Payoff Amount**");

(e) copies of the Required Consents;

(f) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the Vermont Secretary of State not more than ten (10) days before the Closing Date; and

(g) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Section 2.2 and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Vermont. The Company has all requisite corporate power to own its assets and carry on the Business.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations hereunder and thereunder. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, no par value, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller. All of the Shares are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, together with the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.5 Material Contracts; Consents.**

(a) Schedule 4.5(a) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party that is material to the Business, including each contract for the supply of raw milk (each, a "**Material Contract**"). Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

(b) Except as set forth on Schedule 4.5(a) of the Disclosure Schedule, no consent of, or notice to, any counterparty to a Material Contract is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement.

**4.6 Real Property.** The Company does not own any real property. The Company leases its processing facility located in St. Albans, Vermont (the "**St. Albans Facility**") under a lease that is valid, binding and in full force and effect.

**4.7 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns, or will own upon the consummation of the IP Assignment Agreement, all right, title and interest in and to the SORRELFIELD trademarks and the formulations used in the Company's fluid-milk and yogurt products. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**4.8 Compliance; Organic Certification.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws. This includes the USDA National Organic Program regulations at 7 C.F.R. Part 205 and the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance as adopted in Vermont. The Company holds a valid organic handler certification from an accredited certifying agent.

**4.9 Taxes.** All income and other material Tax returns required to be filed by or with respect to the Company have been timely filed, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been timely paid. No audit or other proceeding by any Governmental Authority is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened with respect to any Taxes of the Company. The Company has not waived any statute of limitations with respect to Taxes.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Business.

**4.11 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each such plan has been administered in material compliance with its terms and applicable law.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from the Company in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** Between the date hereof and the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, except as consented to in writing by Buyer. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Company's relationships with its suppliers, customers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Between the date hereof and the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** Between the date hereof and the Closing, Seller shall not solicit, initiate or engage in discussions regarding any sale of the Company or the Business to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Consents.** Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the Required Consents before the Closing. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller in doing so.

**6.5 Tax Matters.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least twenty (20) days before filing. Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business.

**6.7 Employees.** For twelve (12) months following the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each continuing employee with base compensation no less favorable than that provided immediately before the Closing.

**6.8 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of 3 years after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly:
- engage in, own, manage or operate any business that processes or sells organic fluid milk or organic yogurt in the United States; or
- solicit for employment any employee of the Company.

Seller's retained conventional (non-organic) dairy operations, as conducted on the date hereof, shall not violate this Section 6.8.

**6.9 Post-Closing Intellectual Property Confirmation.** Following the Closing, Seller shall execute and deliver the IP Assignment Agreement to confirm the Company's title to the SORRELFIELD trademarks and product formulations.

**6.10 Further Assurances.** Each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party may reasonably request to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

(d) the consents, approvals and notices set forth on Schedule 7.2(d) shall have been obtained or given; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each item set forth in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:
- (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;
- (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing; and
- (c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer to that effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of the Parties contained in this Agreement, including those in Section 4.9, shall survive the Closing until six (6) months following the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the Parties that by their terms contemplate performance after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. The Parties specifically intend that the survival periods in this Section 8.1 shall replace any otherwise applicable statute of limitations, as permitted by 10 Del. C. § 8106(c).

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes, provided that a Claim Notice with respect thereto is delivered prior to the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 8.1; and

(d) any Indebtedness of the Company outstanding as of the Closing and not paid at the Closing.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:
- (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V; and
- (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**8.4 Claims Procedure.** A Person seeking indemnification under this Article VIII shall deliver to the indemnifying Party a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses. No claim may be made under Section 8.2(a) or Section 8.3(a) unless a Claim Notice is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 8.1. A claim for which a Claim Notice is timely delivered shall survive until finally resolved.

**8.5 Third-Party Claims.** The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, provided that it acknowledges in writing its obligation to indemnify. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**8.6 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) until the aggregate amount of Losses thereunder exceeds $845,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(b) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller shall be liable under this Article VIII shall not exceed 10% of the Base Purchase Price ($8,450,000).

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified Party with respect thereto.

**8.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article VIII is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, other than claims for fraud or for specific performance under Section 11.8.

**8.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article VIII shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:
- (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties;
- (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before June 30, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or
- (c) by either Party if the other Party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void and of no further effect. However, Sections 6.6, 11.6 and 11.7 shall survive, and no Party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach.
