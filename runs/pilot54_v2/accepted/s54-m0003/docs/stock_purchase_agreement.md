# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and between **Giraisk Linen Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Lakemont Textile Care, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company operates the Business from its plants in Yonkers, New York and Newark, New Jersey.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount under Section 2.3;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the outstanding indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company identified in payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(d); and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b), as adjusted by the Estimated Closing Working Capital under Section 2.4(a).

**2.3 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold it, together with any earnings on it (the "**Escrow Funds**"), as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX and Section 2.4. The Escrow Funds shall be held and released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.4 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital ("**Estimated Closing Working Capital**"). If it exceeds the target working capital of $3,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), the payment to Seller shall be increased by the excess. If it is less, the payment shall be reduced by the shortfall.

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller its calculation of Closing Working Capital. Seller has 30 days to deliver a written objection. Any dispute not resolved within 30 days after the objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties. That firm's determination is final and binding, absent manifest error.

(c) Any amount owed to Buyer under this Section 2.4 shall be paid by Seller within five Business Days. If Seller does not pay it, Buyer may recover it from the Escrow Funds. Any amount owed to Seller shall be paid by Buyer within five Business Days.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third Business Day after all conditions in Article VIII have been satisfied or waived, other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Company and Renata Szabo-Whitlock;

(d) customary payoff letters for all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company;

(e) the Bill of Sale and the IP Assignment Agreement, each duly executed by Pinaix Equipment Finance, LLC;

(f) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer; and

(g) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.1(a) and 8.1(b).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments under Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.2(a) and 8.2(b).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct the Business only in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall cause the Company to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization, customer relationships and assets. Without limiting the foregoing, the Company shall not (a) amend its organizational documents, (b) issue any equity interest, (c) incur indebtedness for borrowed money outside the ordinary course, (d) enter into, materially amend or terminate any Material Contract, or (e) increase compensation of any officer other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VIII. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consents set forth on Schedule 4.5 before the Closing.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business.

**6.5 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) engage in the Business anywhere in the States of New York, New Jersey or Connecticut, or (b) solicit any customer of the Company to cease or reduce its business with the Company, or solicit for employment any employee of the Company.

**6.6 Retained Credit Support.** Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller to be fully and unconditionally released from the Retained Credit Support on or before October 31, 2026. Buyer may do this by substituting its own guaranty or bond, or that of one of its Affiliates, or by other arrangements reasonably acceptable to the beneficiary. Until such release, Buyer shall cause the Company not to amend, extend or renew the Yonkers plant lease or the Saint Odile Health System contract in any manner that increases Seller's obligations under the Retained Credit Support without Seller's prior written consent.

**6.7 Tax Matters.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date. Seller shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least 30 days before filing. Buyer shall cooperate in the conduct of any Tax audit relating to Pre-Closing Taxes. Seller may participate in any such audit at its own expense.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**7.1 Continuing Employees.** For twelve months after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each employee of the Company who remains employed after the Closing with base pay no less favorable than that in effect immediately before the Closing.

**7.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article VII confers any right on any employee or other Person, or amends any benefit plan.

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing;

(c) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred;

(d) the consents set forth on Schedule 4.5 have been obtained; and

**8.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Buyer has delivered each item listed in Section 3.3.

**8.3 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent, (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by May 29, 2026, provided the terminating party's breach is not the cause, or (c) by either party upon a material uncured breach by the other party that would cause a condition in Section 8.1 or 8.2 to fail.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, survive the Closing until 12 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization of Seller), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Organization of the Company), 4.4 (Capitalization), 4.19 (Brokers), 5.1 (Organization and Authority) and 5.5 (Brokers) (the "**Fundamental Representations**") survive the Closing until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(c) The covenants and agreements of the parties survive the Closing in accordance with their terms, or, if no term is specified, until fully performed. The obligations of Seller under Section 9.2(c) survive until the end of the survival period applicable to the representations in Section 4.16.

(d) A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before expiration of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Seller under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes, to the extent not reflected as a liability in Closing Working Capital as finally determined.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Buyer under this Agreement; and

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer, or, after the Closing, of the Company, in this Agreement.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) *Third-Party Claims.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any third-party claim. Failure to give notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It may not settle the claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

(b) *Direct Claims.* A claim not involving a third party shall be asserted by written notice to the indemnifying party. The indemnifying party has 30 days to respond. If it does not respond within that period, it is deemed to have rejected the claim, and the indemnified party may pursue the remedies available to it under this Agreement.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a), and the aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer is liable under Section 9.3(a), shall not exceed $4,850,000 (the "**Cap**"). The Cap does not apply to Losses arising from inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, or from fraud. Those Losses are limited to the Purchase Price.

(b) *Deductible.* Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a), and Buyer is not liable under Section 9.3(a), until the aggregate amount of all Losses under that Section exceeds $485,000 (the "**Deductible**"), and then only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(c) *Application.* The Cap and the Deductible shall apply only to claims under Section 9.2(a) and Section 9.3(a).

(d) *Mitigation; Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party with respect to them. Each indemnified party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses after becoming aware of the event giving rise to them.

(e) *No Consequential Damages.* No party is liable for punitive damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Funds, to the extent available, and then directly by Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. This Section 9.7 does not limit claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, or the procedures in Section 2.4.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.
