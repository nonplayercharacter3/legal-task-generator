# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Lakemont Textile Care, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Renata Szabo-Whitlock** ("**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Giraisk Linen Group, Inc. ("**Parent**") and Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC, Parent is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company. The closing under the Purchase Agreement (the "**Closing**") is occurring on the Effective Date.

B. Executive has served as President of the Company. Through that role she has gained substantial knowledge of the Company's operations, customers, pricing, employees and confidential information.

C. The Company wishes to continue to employ Executive after the Closing, and Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms of this Agreement. Executive acknowledges that the covenants in Sections 6 and 7 are a material inducement to Parent's willingness to complete the Closing, and that she will receive substantial benefits under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. Parent and its subsidiaries are Affiliates of the Company.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Business**" means commercial laundry and linen services provided to hotels, resorts and restaurants, including linen rental, laundering, pressing and the delivery and collection of linens and related textile products.

"**Cause**" means any of the following by Executive: (a) a material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured, if curable, 30 days after written notice from the Company; (b) conviction of, or a plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (c) willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company or its Affiliates; (d) willful failure to follow a lawful and reasonable directive of the Board, not cured within 15 days after written notice; or (e) a material violation of a written policy of the Company concerning harassment, discrimination or financial controls.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following without Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in Base Salary, other than an across-the-board reduction of no more than 10% applicable to senior executives of Parent and its subsidiaries generally; (b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or (c) relocation of Executive's principal place of work by more than 40 miles from the Company's Yonkers, New York plant. Good Reason exists only if Executive gives the Company written notice within 60 days after the condition first occurs, the Company fails to cure it within 30 days after that notice, and Executive resigns within 30 days after the cure period ends.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of Executive's employment with the Company and the 24 months after the Termination Date.

"**Restricted Territory**" means the States of New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which Executive's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

## 2. Employment; Duties

**2.1 Position.** The Company shall employ Executive as its President, reporting to the Board and to the Chief Operating Officer of Parent. Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for that position and such other duties consistent with that position as the Board assigns.

**2.2 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote her full business time and attention to the Company. She may serve on civic, charitable or professional boards, and manage her personal investments, if those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or breach Sections 6 or 7.

**2.3 Place of Performance.** Executive's principal place of work is the Company's Yonkers, New York plant. She shall travel to the Company's Newark, New Jersey plant and elsewhere as reasonably required.

## 3. Term

**3.1 Initial Term.** Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the third anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

**3.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any renewal periods are referred to as the "**Term**."

## 4. Compensation and Benefits

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $385,000 ("**Base Salary**"), in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board shall review Base Salary annually and may increase, but not decrease (other than as permitted under the definition of Good Reason), it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 40% of Base Salary. The bonus will be based on achievement of performance goals set by the Board after consultation with Executive. Any bonus is payable no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates, subject to Executive's continued employment through the payment date except as provided in Section 5.3.

**4.3 Retention Award.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention award of $250,000, in two equal installments, on the first and second anniversaries of the Effective Date. Each installment is subject to Executive's continued employment through the applicable payment date, except as provided in Section 5.3.

**4.4 Equity.** Executive will be recommended for an award under Parent's equity incentive plan, on terms set by Parent's compensation committee and the applicable award agreement.

**4.5 Benefits.** Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans made available to senior executives of the Company, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.6 Vacation.** Executive is entitled to four weeks of paid vacation per year, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.7 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

## 5. Termination

**5.1 Termination Events.** Executive's employment may be terminated:

(a) by the Company for Cause, effective on written notice;

(b) by the Company without Cause, on 30 days' written notice (or pay in lieu of notice);

(c) by Executive for Good Reason, in accordance with the definition of Good Reason;

(d) by Executive without Good Reason, on 60 days' written notice, which the Company may waive in whole or in part;

(e) upon Executive's death; or

(f) by the Company upon Executive's Disability. "**Disability**" means Executive's inability, due to physical or mental incapacity, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period.

**5.2 Accrued Obligations.** Upon any termination, the Company shall pay Executive (a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the Termination Date, (b) accrued but unused vacation to the extent required by Company policy or law, (c) unreimbursed business expenses, and (d) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans (together, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

**5.3 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall, in addition to the Accrued Obligations, and subject to Section 5.4:

(a) continue to pay Base Salary for 12 months after the Termination Date;

(b) pay any annual bonus earned for the fiscal year ended before the Termination Date but not yet paid;

(c) pay any unpaid installment of the retention award under Section 4.3; and

(d) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay the employer portion of the premiums for Executive and her covered dependents for up to 12 months after the Termination Date.

**5.4 Release.** Payments under Section 5.3 are conditioned on Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company and its Affiliates, in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, within 60 days after the Termination Date. They are also conditioned on Executive's continued compliance with Sections 6 and 7. Payments begin on the first payroll date after the release becomes irrevocable. The first payment includes any amounts that would otherwise have been paid before that date.

**5.5 Resignation from Positions.** Upon termination for any reason, Executive shall resign from all positions she holds as an officer or director of the Company or any of its Affiliates.

## 6. Confidentiality; Work Product

**6.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company or its Affiliates, including customer lists and contract terms, pricing, route and logistics data, financial information, employee information, supplier terms, processes and the source code and data of the LinenLoop RFID linen tracking software. It does not include information that becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach.

**6.2 Non-Disclosure.** During and after her employment, Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, except as required in performing her duties for the Company or as required by law. If disclosure is required by law, Executive shall give the Company prompt notice (where legally permitted) so that it may seek a protective order.

**6.3 Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive will not be held liable under any trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or made in a filing in a lawsuit under seal.

**6.4 Return of Property.** Upon termination, or earlier on request, Executive shall return all property and Confidential Information of the Company in her possession, including all copies.

**6.5 Work Product.** All inventions, works of authorship, processes and improvements that Executive conceives or develops during her employment, and that relate to the business of the Company or result from her work for it, are the sole property of the Company. Executive assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in them. This Section 6.5 does not apply to any invention that qualifies for protection under Section 203-f of the New York Labor Law.

## 7. Restrictive Covenants

**7.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the Restricted Territory, own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, consult for or otherwise participate in any Person that engages in the Business. Executive may own up to 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person as a passive investment.

**7.2 Customer Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) solicit or accept business from any customer of the Company for the purpose of providing services that are part of the Business, or (b) induce any customer of the Company to cease or reduce its purchases of services that are part of the Business from the Company. For this Section 7.2, "customer" means any Person that was a customer of the Company, or to which the Company submitted a written proposal, during the 24 months before the Termination Date.

**7.3 Employee Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any person who is, or within the preceding six months was, an employee of the Company. General advertisements not targeted at Company employees do not violate this Section 7.3.

**7.4 Non-Disparagement.** Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, Parent or their respective officers, directors or services. Nothing in this Section 7.4 limits truthful testimony or the disclosures permitted under Section 6.3.

**7.5 Reasonableness.** Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 7 are reasonable in scope, duration and geography and are necessary to protect the goodwill of the Company and the value of the shares acquired by Parent. If a court determines that any restriction is unenforceable, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**7.6 Tolling.** The Restricted Period is extended by any period during which Executive is in breach of this Section 7.

**7.7 Remedies.** Executive agrees that a breach of Section 6 or this Section 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

## 8. Section 409A

The parties intend that payments under this Agreement are exempt from, or comply with, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. This Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. If Executive is a "specified employee" on the Termination Date, any payment of deferred compensation triggered by her separation from service is delayed until the first business day after the six-month anniversary of the Termination Date, to the extent required by Section 409A. Any reimbursement shall be paid no later than the end of the year following the year in which the expense was incurred.

## 9. Miscellaneous

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices to the Company shall be sent to its Yonkers, New York plant, Attention: Chair of the Board, with a copy to Parent, Attention: General Counsel. Notices to Executive shall be sent to the most recent home address on file with the Company.

**9.2 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required by law to be withheld.

**9.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign it to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets.

**9.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company other than Executive. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**9.5 Severability.** Subject to Section 7.5, if any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**9.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.8 Survival.** Sections 5, 6, 7 and 9 survive the termination of Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
