# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 8, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Giraisk Linen Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Giraisk**"), and **Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Pinaix**"). Each of Giraisk and Pinaix is a "**Party**," and together they are the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Pinaix owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Lakemont Textile Care, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company operates a commercial laundry and linen-rental business from plants in Yonkers, New York and Newark, New Jersey.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Giraisk would acquire the Company (the "**Transaction**"). The Parties refer to their discussions as "Project Pressline."

C. In connection with that evaluation, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information. The Parties wish to protect that information on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Affiliate.** "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. For Pinaix, the term includes the Company and Pinaix Equipment Finance, LLC.

**1.2 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Party or any of its Affiliates or Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses or makes available to the other Party or any of its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Transaction. It includes:

(a) financial statements, forecasts, budgets and tax information;

(b) customer and supplier lists, contract terms, pricing, route and logistics data and revenue by customer or segment;

(c) information about employees, compensation, benefit plans and labor relations;

(d) information about real property, equipment, permits and environmental matters;

(e) software, source code, data structures and technical information, including information concerning the LinenLoop RFID linen tracking software;

(f) all materials made available in any electronic data room for Project Pressline; and

(g) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

**1.3 Transaction Information.** Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Parties are considering the Transaction, that Confidential Information has been made available, and the status and terms of any discussions between the Parties ("**Transaction Information**").

**1.4 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by reasonable evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in the Receiving Party's possession on a non-confidential basis before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, if that source is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**1.5 Person.** "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

**1.6 Representatives.** "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, officers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, consultants and, in the case of Giraisk, prospective debt financing sources, in each case who need to know the Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating or financing the Transaction.

## 2. Use and Non-Disclosure

**2.1 Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, financing and completing the Transaction (the "**Purpose**") and for no other purpose.

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential. It shall not disclose that information to any Person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are informed of its confidential nature. The Receiving Party shall direct its Representatives to treat the Confidential Information in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.3 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives, other than a Representative who has entered into a separate confidentiality agreement directly with the Disclosing Party.

**2.4 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.5 Competitively Sensitive Information.** The Parties acknowledge that Giraisk and the Company compete in certain markets. Pinaix may designate customer-specific pricing, contract terms and similar competitively sensitive information as "Clean Team Only." Information so designated shall be made available only to the outside counsel, outside advisors and limited employees of Giraisk who are not directly responsible for pricing or sales and who are identified to Pinaix in writing in advance. The Parties shall comply with applicable antitrust laws in exchanging any such information.

## 3. Legally Required Disclosure

**3.1 Notice.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice. The Disclosing Party may then seek, at its own expense, a protective order or other appropriate remedy. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts.

**3.2 Limited Disclosure.** If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be given to the information disclosed.

**3.3 Regulatory Examinations.** Disclosure to a banking, securities or other regulatory authority in the course of a routine examination not targeted at the Disclosing Party or the Transaction does not require notice under Section 3.1.

## 4. Return and Destruction

**4.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it has terminated discussions about the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, including Derivative Materials. An officer of the Receiving Party shall certify the return or destruction in writing on request.

**4.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies required to be retained by law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention policies, and (b) copies stored on automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Retained copies remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained, notwithstanding the expiration of the term in Section 8.1.

## 5. Non-Solicitation

**5.1 Employees.** For 18 months after the Effective Date, Giraisk shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment or hire any officer or salaried management employee of the Company with whom Giraisk had contact, or of whom it became aware, in connection with the Transaction.

**5.2 Exceptions.** Section 5.1 does not prohibit (a) general solicitations, advertisements or search-firm engagements not targeted at Company employees, or hiring any person who responds to them, or (b) soliciting or hiring any person whose employment with the Company ended at least six months before the first contact.

**5.3 Effect of Acquisition.** Section 5.1 ceases to apply on the closing of the Transaction.

## 6. No Representations; No Obligation

**6.1 No Warranty.** Neither Party, nor any of its Representatives, makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information. Neither Party has any liability to the other resulting from the use of Confidential Information, except as set forth in a definitive agreement for the Transaction.

**6.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** No agreement providing for the Transaction exists unless and until a definitive agreement has been executed and delivered. Either Party may terminate discussions at any time for any reason or no reason, without liability, except for its obligations under this Agreement.

**6.3 No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any Confidential Information or intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use it for the Purpose.

**6.4 Contact with Company Personnel.** Without Pinaix's prior consent, Giraisk shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, contact any employee, customer, supplier, landlord or lender of the Company about the Transaction, other than through individuals designated by Pinaix. This Section 6.4 does not restrict contacts in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Transaction.

## 7. Remedies

**7.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that money damages may not be a sufficient remedy for a breach of this Agreement. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**7.2 Costs.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable judgment that a Party breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in the litigation.

## 8. Term

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement and the obligations under it expire two years after the Effective Date, except that Section 4.2 continues to apply as stated in it.

**8.2 Definitive Agreement.** If the Parties enter into a definitive agreement for the Transaction, the confidentiality provisions of that agreement govern the treatment of information concerning the Company from and after the closing under it.

## 9. Miscellaneous

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices to Giraisk shall be sent to the attention of its General Counsel. Notices to Pinaix shall be sent to the attention of its Chief Executive Officer. In each case notices shall be sent to the address set forth in that Party's signature block, or to such other address as the Party designates by notice.

**9.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**9.3 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York. Each Party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**9.6 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any attempted assignment without that consent is void.

**9.7 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, that term shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining terms shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
