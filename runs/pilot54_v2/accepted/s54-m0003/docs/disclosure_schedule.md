## Preliminary Statements

1. **Delivery.** Seller delivers this Disclosure Schedule (this "**Disclosure Schedule**") to Buyer under the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), between Giraisk Linen Group, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**"). Delivery is made concurrently with the execution of the Agreement.

2. **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. "**Company**" means Lakemont Textile Care, Inc.

3. **Numbering.** The section numbers below correspond to the sections of the Agreement. Under Section 11.8 of the Agreement, a disclosure in one section qualifies other sections only to the extent its relevance to them is reasonably apparent on its face.

4. **Summaries.** Descriptions of contracts and other documents in this Disclosure Schedule are summaries. Each is qualified by the full text of the document described, and a true and complete copy of each has been made available to Buyer in the electronic data room titled "Project Pressline."

5. **No Admission.** Including an item in this Disclosure Schedule is not an admission that the item is material, outside the ordinary course of business, or a breach or violation of any contract or Law.

## Schedule 4.16 — Taxes

1. The New York State Department of Taxation and Finance has commenced a sales and use tax field audit of the Company for the periods March 1, 2023 through November 30, 2024 (Audit Case No. X-418827364). The audit covers the taxability of linen rental charges and of certain equipment and chemical purchases at the Yonkers plant. The auditor issued an information document request on January 12, 2026. 2. The Company's federal, New York and New Jersey income and franchise tax returns for taxable years 2022 through 2025 remain open to examination under the applicable statutes of limitations. Except as described in Item 1, no audit of any of those returns is pending.
