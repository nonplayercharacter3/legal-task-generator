# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of April 15, 2026, by and between **Pinaix Equipment Finance, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Lakemont Textile Care, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Giraisk Linen Group, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**") are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Assignor is an Affiliate of Seller. Assignor owns the LinenLoop RFID linen tracking software, which the Company uses in the operation of the Business under an intercompany arrangement with Assignor.

C. Section 3.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Assignment, duly executed by Assignor, at the Closing. Section 4.13 of the Purchase Agreement contemplates that, upon delivery of this Assignment, the Company will own all right, title and interest in and to the LinenLoop RFID linen tracking software.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Assigned IP**" means all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the following:

(a) the Software;

(b) all Intellectual Property Rights in the Software, in all jurisdictions;

(c) all registrations of, applications for and renewals and extensions of any of those Intellectual Property Rights;

(d) all goodwill associated with the Software, including the name "LinenLoop" as used in connection with it;

(e) all rights to sue for, and to collect and retain damages and other remedies for, any past, present or future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the foregoing; and

(f) all income, royalties and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the foregoing.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all copyrights, works of authorship, trade secrets, know-how, inventions (whether or not patentable), patents, database rights, trademarks, service marks, trade names and other intellectual property and proprietary rights.

"**Intercompany Arrangement**" means the arrangement between Assignor and the Company under which the Company has used the Software before the Closing, whether written or unwritten.

"**Software**" means the LinenLoop RFID linen tracking software described on Exhibit A, in all versions and releases, including:

(a) all source code, object code, scripts, build files and configuration files;

(b) all databases and data structures, including data schemas, compiled by or for the Software (other than data owned by customers of the Company);

(c) the related RFID reader integration modules, application programming interfaces and interfaces to third-party systems;

(d) all user, technical and administrative documentation, specifications, flow charts and design materials; and

(e) all modifications, enhancements, derivative works and work in progress relating to any of the foregoing.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment.** Assignor irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of the Assigned IP. The Company accepts the assignment.

**2.2 Effective Time.** The assignment under Section 2.1 is effective at the Closing on the date of this Assignment.

**2.3 Delivery.** On the date of this Assignment, Assignor shall deliver to the Company, by secure electronic transfer to a repository designated by the Company:

(a) a complete and current copy of the source code of the Software, together with all build tools, scripts and instructions reasonably necessary to compile, install and maintain it;

(b) all documentation included in the Software; and

(c) all credentials, keys and passwords for repositories, hosting accounts and code-signing certificates used exclusively for the Software.

Assignor shall transfer to the Company, within ten Business Days after the date of this Assignment, administrative control of any account with a third-party service provider that is used exclusively for the Software.

**2.4 Termination of Intercompany Arrangement.** The Intercompany Arrangement terminates as of the Closing. No fee, charge or other amount is payable by the Company under the Intercompany Arrangement for any period, and neither party has any further obligation under it.

**2.5 No Retained Copies.** After completing the deliveries under Section 2.3, Assignor shall not retain, and shall cause its Affiliates not to retain, any copy of the source code of the Software. Assignor shall certify the deletion of any such copies in writing on the Company's request. Archival copies held on backup media in the ordinary course may be retained until overwritten in the ordinary course, if they are not accessed or used.

**2.6 Excluded Liabilities.** The Company does not assume any liability or obligation of Assignor or any of its Affiliates, including any obligation to any developer, contractor or licensor arising before the Closing.

## 3. Representations and Warranties

**3.1 Assignor Representations.** Assignor represents and warrants to the Company that:

(a) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Liens;

(b) Assignor has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Assignment and to assign the Assigned IP;

(c) Assignor has not granted any license, option or other right in the Assigned IP to any Person other than the Company under the Intercompany Arrangement;

(d) each current and former employee and contractor of Assignor or its Affiliates who contributed to the development of the Software has assigned to Assignor, by written agreement or by operation of law, all of that Person's rights in his or her contributions;

(e) Exhibit A lists each third-party software component and open-source component incorporated in the Software, and the license under which it is used. No such component is used in a manner that requires the Software's source code to be disclosed, licensed or distributed to any Person; and

(f) upon delivery of this Assignment, the Company will own all right, title and interest in and to the Software.

**3.2 Purchase Agreement Rights.** The rights of Buyer and the other Buyer Indemnitees under the Purchase Agreement, including under Section 4.13 and Article IX, with respect to the Assigned IP are not affected by this Assignment.

## 4. Moral Rights; Confidentiality

**4.1 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Assignor waives, and shall cause its Affiliates and their employees and contractors to waive, any moral rights or rights of attribution or integrity in the Software.

**4.2 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, the source code, data structures and other non-public information concerning the Software are confidential information of the Company. Assignor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold that information in confidence consistent with Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Further Assurances; Power of Attorney

**5.1 Further Assurances.** From time to time after the date of this Assignment, at the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably required to vest in the Company, and to perfect, record and enforce, the Company's rights in the Assigned IP. This includes executing any short-form assignment for recording with the U.S. Copyright Office or any other Governmental Authority, and providing reasonable access to personnel with knowledge of the Software for knowledge-transfer purposes for 90 days after the date of this Assignment.

**5.2 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute in Assignor's name any instrument reasonably necessary to give effect to this Assignment, if Assignor fails to do so within ten Business Days after the Company's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

**5.3 Payments Received.** If Assignor or any of its Affiliates receives, after the date of this Assignment, any payment relating to the Assigned IP, Assignor shall hold it in trust for the Company and remit it to the Company within ten Business Days after receipt.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns. The Company may assign its rights under this Assignment to any Affiliate or to any successor to the Business without Assignor's consent.

**6.2 Notices.** All notices under this Assignment shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices to Assignor shall be sent to it in care of Seller at the address for Seller under Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be sent to it in care of Buyer at the address for Buyer under that Section.

**6.3 Governing Law.** This Assignment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**6.4 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Assignment shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York. Each party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**6.5 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ASSIGNMENT.

**6.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**6.7 Severability.** If any term of this Assignment is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms shall remain in full force and effect.

**6.8 Counterparts.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A

### Software

**1. Description.** LinenLoop is a proprietary RFID-based linen tracking and inventory management system. It is used by the Company at its Yonkers, New York and Newark, New Jersey plants and at customer sites. It records the issue, return, wash-cycle count and replacement of tagged linens and textile products. It also generates par-level, loss-rate and invoicing reports for customers.

**2. Components.**

| Item | Component | Current Version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | LinenLoop Core (server application and tracking engine) | 4.2.3 |
| 2 | LinenLoop Customer Portal (web application) | 3.8.0 |
| 3 | LinenLoop Route (mobile application for delivery route drivers) | 2.5.1 |
| 4 | RFID reader integration modules (tunnel, portal and handheld readers) | 1.9.4 |
| 5 | Invoicing export interface to Sage Intacct | 1.3.0 |
| 6 | Route data interface to RouteWise Pro | 1.1.2 |
| 7 | LinenLoop database schema and reporting module | 4.2.3 |

**3. Third-Party and Open-Source Components.**

| Component | License | Use |
|---|---|---|
| PostgreSQL database server | PostgreSQL License | Database (unmodified) |
| React | MIT License | Customer Portal user interface |
| Apache Kafka | Apache License 2.0 | Event messaging (unmodified) |
| RFID reader vendor SDKs | Commercial license of the reader manufacturer, transferable with the reader hardware | Reader integration modules |

**4. Excluded Items.** The Software does not include the RouteWise Pro or Sage Intacct software. Those remain licensed to the Company under its own subscription agreements, as described in Schedule 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule. It also does not include RFID tags or reader hardware, or data owned by customers of the Company.
