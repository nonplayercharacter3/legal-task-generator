# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of April 15, 2026, by **Pinaix Equipment Finance, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), to and in favor of **Lakemont Textile Care, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Giraisk Linen Group, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**") are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Transferor is an Affiliate of Seller. Transferor owns the tunnel-washer equipment described on Exhibit A, which is installed at, and used by the Company in the operation of the Business at, the Company's Newark, New Jersey plant.

C. Section 3.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Transferor, at the Closing. Section 4.11 of the Purchase Agreement contemplates that, upon delivery of this Bill of Sale, the Company will have good title to that equipment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Transferor agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. "**Transferred Equipment**" means:

(a) the equipment described on Exhibit A;

(b) all controllers, programmable logic controllers, chemical-injection systems, sensors, conveyors, shuttles, slings, spare parts and accessories installed on or held for use with that equipment;

(c) all operating manuals, maintenance logs, wiring diagrams, inspection reports and other records relating to that equipment; and

(d) to the extent transferable, all manufacturer and vendor warranties, service contracts and similar rights relating to that equipment.

## 2. Transfer

**2.1 Conveyance.** Transferor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the Transferred Equipment. The Company takes the Transferred Equipment to have and to hold forever.

**2.2 Effective Time.** The transfer under Section 2.1 is effective at the Closing on the date of this Bill of Sale.

**2.3 Delivery and Possession.** The Transferred Equipment is located at the Company's Newark, New Jersey plant and is in the possession of the Company. Delivery is made in place. No physical removal, relocation or reinstallation is required.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** The Company does not assume any liability or obligation of Transferor or any of its Affiliates, including any obligation under any financing arrangement, lease or other agreement under which Transferor acquired or financed the Transferred Equipment.

## 3. Title

**3.1 Title Warranty.** Transferor represents and warrants to the Company that:

(a) Transferor has good and marketable title to the Transferred Equipment, free and clear of all Liens;

(b) Transferor has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to transfer the Transferred Equipment; and

(c) upon delivery of this Bill of Sale, the Company will have good and marketable title to the Transferred Equipment, free and clear of all Liens.

**3.2 Release of Filings.** Transferor shall, at its own cost, cause to be terminated or amended, within ten Business Days after the date of this Bill of Sale, any UCC financing statement filed by or against Transferor that covers any of the Transferred Equipment.

**3.3 Purchase Agreement Rights.** The rights of Buyer and the other Buyer Indemnitees under the Purchase Agreement, including under Section 4.11 and Article IX, with respect to the Transferred Equipment are not affected by this Bill of Sale.

## 4. Further Assurances; Power of Attorney

**4.1 Further Assurances.** From time to time after the date of this Bill of Sale, at the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Transferor shall execute and deliver such further instruments of transfer, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably required to vest in the Company good title to the Transferred Equipment and to put the Company in full possession and control of it. This includes the assignment of any manufacturer or vendor warranty that requires a separate instrument.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Transferor irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive the Transferred Equipment and any rights relating to it, and to execute in Transferor's name any instrument reasonably necessary to give effect to this Bill of Sale. This power is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Payments Received.** If Transferor or any of its Affiliates receives, after the date of this Bill of Sale, any payment, refund or warranty proceeds relating to the Transferred Equipment, Transferor shall hold it in trust for the Company and remit it to the Company within ten Business Days after receipt.

## 5. Miscellaneous

**5.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Transferor and its successors and assigns and benefits the Company and its successors and assigns.

**5.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**5.3 Jurisdiction.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the County of New York, New York. Transferor irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**5.4 Severability.** If any term of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms shall remain in full force and effect.

**5.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronically signed copy is deemed an original.
