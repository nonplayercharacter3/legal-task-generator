**Re: Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 – Retained Credit Support and Related Matters**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") is delivered with reference to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Giraisk Linen Group, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer acquired all of the issued and outstanding shares of Lakemont Textile Care, Inc. (the "**Company**"). The Closing occurred on April 15, 2026.

Buyer has informed Seller that it has not yet obtained Seller's full and unconditional release from the Retained Credit Support. Buyer has requested additional time to do so. The parties are also exchanging correspondence about a pending claim against the Escrow Funds. This Letter Agreement records their agreement on the credit support and their respective positions on that claim.

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. "**Escrow Agreement**," "**Escrow Agent**" and "**Escrow Funds**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. "**Claim Notice**" and "**Initial Release Amount**" have the meanings given in the Escrow Agreement.

## 2. Status of Retained Credit Support

Buyer and Seller acknowledge the status of the Retained Credit Support as of the date of this Letter Agreement:

(a) *Yonkers Lease Guaranty.* Seller's guaranty of the Company's obligations under the lease of the Company's Yonkers, New York plant remains outstanding. The lease runs through June 30, 2034. Base rent is currently approximately $1,150,000 per year, plus additional rent, taxes, operating expenses and other charges payable by the tenant. The landlord has advised Buyer that it is reviewing Buyer's financial information in connection with a proposed substitute guaranty. It has not yet agreed to release Seller.

(b) *Saint Odile Performance Bond.* The performance bond in the amount of $3,000,000 under the Company's linen services agreement with Saint Odile Health System remains outstanding. Seller's indemnity agreement in favor of the surety continues to support the bond. Buyer has applied to its surety for a replacement bond. Saint Odile Health System has not yet consented to the substitution.

## 3. Extension of Release Deadline; Buyer Indemnity

(a) *Extension.* Seller agrees that the date "October 31, 2026" in Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement is replaced with "April 30, 2027" (the "**Extended Release Date**"). Buyer shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller to be fully and unconditionally released from each item of the Retained Credit Support on or before the Extended Release Date. The restrictions in the last sentence of Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement continue to apply until each such release is obtained.

(b) *Indemnity.* In consideration of the extension in Section 3(a), Buyer shall indemnify Seller and the other Seller Indemnitees against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or resulting from the Retained Credit Support. This includes any amount paid or payable by Seller or any of its Affiliates under the Yonkers lease guaranty, and any amount paid or payable to the surety under the Saint Odile performance bond or any related indemnity agreement, in each case whether the claim arises before or after the Extended Release Date. Buyer's obligations under this Section 3(b) are deemed an obligation of Buyer under Section 9.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Claims under this Section 3(b) shall be asserted and resolved in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

(c) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay any amount due under Section 3(b) within ten Business Days after Seller's written demand. The demand must be accompanied by reasonable supporting documentation of the amount paid or demanded under the Retained Credit Support.

(d) *Cooperation.* Seller shall cooperate reasonably with Buyer's efforts to obtain the releases contemplated by Section 3(a). This includes delivering information about Seller's existing guaranty and surety arrangements that the landlord, Saint Odile Health System or any surety reasonably requests. Seller is not required to pay any fee or incur any out-of-pocket cost that Buyer does not reimburse.

## 4. Information Rights

Until each item of the Retained Credit Support has been released, Buyer shall cause the Company to give Seller, within five Business Days after receipt, copies of the following:

(a) any written notice of default under the Yonkers plant lease or the Saint Odile Health System linen services agreement; and

(b) any written demand or claim under the Yonkers lease guaranty or the Saint Odile performance bond.

## 5. Pending Escrow Claim

(a) *Claim Notice.* Buyer delivered to Seller and the Escrow Agent a Claim Notice dated October 19, 2026. The notice asserts a claim for $1,300,000 of Losses arising from credits owed to Saint Odile Health System for overbilling by the Company for linen services invoiced before the Closing. Buyer's position is that the overbilling constitutes a breach of the representations in Sections 4.7 and 4.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

(b) *Seller's Position.* Seller disputes the claim. Seller has demanded that Buyer join in joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent directing release of the Initial Release Amount to Seller, without reduction for the amount stated in the Claim Notice.

(c) *Buyer's Position.* Buyer disputes Seller's demand. Buyer has not agreed to deliver joint written instructions for release of the Initial Release Amount. Buyer reserves its position that $1,300,000 must be withheld from any release of Escrow Funds pending final resolution of the claim.

(d) *Reservation of Rights.* Nothing in this Letter Agreement is an admission by either party about the merits of the claim or about either party's entitlement to any portion of the Escrow Funds. Each party reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement with respect to the claim and the Escrow Funds. The parties shall continue to discuss the claim in good faith.

## 6. Effect of Letter Agreement

Except as expressly set forth in this Letter Agreement, the Purchase Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. This Letter Agreement is an amendment to the Purchase Agreement for purposes of Section 11.5 of the Purchase Agreement. References in the Purchase Agreement to "this Agreement" include this Letter Agreement.
