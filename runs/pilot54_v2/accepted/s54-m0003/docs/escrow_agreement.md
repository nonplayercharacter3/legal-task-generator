# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 15, 2026, by and among **Giraisk Linen Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Pinaix Service Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Fernhill Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Lakemont Textile Care, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Closing under the Purchase Agreement is occurring on April 15, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**").

C. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit is security for Seller's obligations under Article IX and Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

D. Buyer and Seller wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and release the Escrow Funds, and the Escrow Agent is willing to serve, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" means a written notice delivered by Buyer to Seller and the Escrow Agent under Section 5.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" means, at any time, the aggregate amount stated in all Claim Notices that have not been paid to Buyer or otherwise resolved under Section 4.1.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any amounts released under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a final determination of the independent accounting firm under Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement. It must be accompanied by a certificate of the party delivering it confirming that it is final and non-appealable.

"**Final Release Date**" means the date that is 12 months after the Closing Date.

"**Initial Release Amount**" means $2,425,000, being 50% of the Escrow Amount.

"**Initial Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, as identified on Schedule 1.

## 2. Appointment; Deposit

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and release the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated on Schedule 2 (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller on the Business Day of receipt.

**2.3 Segregation.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in the Escrow Account, separate from its own funds and from funds held for any other Person. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any Lien, attachment or claim of any creditor of Buyer, Seller or the Escrow Agent. They may be released only as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. Investment

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Fernhill Treasury Obligations Money Market Fund or another investment designated in Joint Written Instructions (each, a "**Permitted Investment**"). Absent such instructions, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any Permitted Investment as needed to make a release under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3, or from any liquidation of it.

**3.3 Earnings.** Interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds. They shall be released together with the final release under Section 4.3.

## 4. Release of Escrow Funds

**4.1 Joint Written Instructions; Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall release Escrow Funds within three Business Days after receipt of either of the following:

(a) Joint Written Instructions directing the release; or

(b) a Final Order directing the release, delivered by either Buyer or Seller, with a copy to the other.

Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions to give effect to any settlement of a claim or any determination under the Purchase Agreement that entitles a party to Escrow Funds.

**4.2 Initial Release.** On the date that is 180 days after the Closing Date (the "**Initial Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the Initial Release Amount, less the aggregate Disputed Amount under all Claim Notices delivered before the Initial Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall make the release without further instruction from Buyer or Seller. Any amount withheld under this Section 4.2 shall remain in the Escrow Account and be released only in accordance with Section 4.1.

**4.3 Final Release.** On the Final Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller all remaining Escrow Funds, less the aggregate Disputed Amount under all Claim Notices delivered before the Final Release Date. Any amount withheld under this Section 4.3 shall remain in the Escrow Account and be released only in accordance with Section 4.1.

**4.4 Working Capital Payments.** Buyer may recover from the Escrow Funds any amount owed to it under Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement that Seller has not paid when due. To do so, Buyer shall deliver a Claim Notice stating the amount and the basis for the claim under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement. The procedures in Section 5 apply to that Claim Notice.

**4.5 Method of Payment.** All releases shall be made by wire transfer to the account of the recipient set forth on Schedule 2, or to another account designated by that party in a written notice to the Escrow Agent that has been confirmed by call-back under Section 6.6.

## 5. Claims Procedure

**5.1 Claim Notice.** Buyer may assert a claim against the Escrow Funds by delivering a Claim Notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. Each Claim Notice must:

(a) state the amount of the Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate if the amount is not yet determined;

(b) describe in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim; and

(c) identify the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which the claim is made.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after receipt of the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice must state the amount disputed and the basis for the dispute in reasonable detail.

**5.3 Uncontested Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period, the Escrow Agent shall release to Buyer the amount stated in the Claim Notice on the fifth Business Day after that period ends. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of the claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed portion to Buyer on the same timetable.

**5.4 Contested Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount disputed in an Objection Notice until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order with respect to that amount.

## 6. The Escrow Agent

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are solely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or fiduciary obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement, is not bound by it, and has no duty to interpret it or determine compliance with it.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and given by the proper Person. It has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any special, indirect or consequential damages.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and its officers, directors, employees and agents against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance of this Agreement. This indemnity does not cover losses caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 6.4, unless a court determines that one of them caused the loss. This Section 6.4 survives termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands concerning the Escrow Funds, or is uncertain about its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also interplead the Escrow Funds into a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 8.2. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any delay resulting from such action.

**6.6 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any funds-transfer instruction, other than a release under Section 4.2 or 4.3 to an account already on Schedule 2, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule 1 who did not sign the instruction.

**6.7 Resignation; Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions on 30 days' notice. Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions within that period. If no successor has been appointed when the period ends, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one. The resignation or removal takes effect when the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to the successor.

## 7. Fees; Tax Matters

**7.1 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3, and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds.

**7.2 Tax Reporting.** For tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds, and all earnings on them are reported as income of Buyer, until released. Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file the information returns required of it with respect to earnings on the Escrow Funds.

**7.3 Tax Distributions.** The Escrow Agent shall not make any distribution to Buyer in respect of taxes on earnings on the Escrow Funds unless directed by Joint Written Instructions.

## 8. Miscellaneous

**8.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the addresses set forth on Schedule 1 (or such other address as a party designates by notice). Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**8.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Funds, including any dispute over entitlement to any release of Escrow Funds, shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the action shall be brought in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**8.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**8.4 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been released in accordance with its terms. Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 7.1 survive termination.

**8.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**8.6 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither Buyer nor Seller may assign its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other and written notice to the Escrow Agent. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**8.7 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms shall remain in full force and effect.

**8.8 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account. Each of them shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**8.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
