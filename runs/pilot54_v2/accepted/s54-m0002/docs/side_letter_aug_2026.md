This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered concurrently with the execution of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of the date of this Side Letter (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). This Side Letter is the "Side Letter" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Installer Classification Matter

1.1 Seller has informed Buyer that in May 2026 the Wage and Hour Division of the U.S. Department of Labor opened an audit of Seller. The audit concerns Seller's engagement of field installers, electricians and installation crew leads as independent contractors rather than employees during the period from January 1, 2023 to the present. It also concerns related wage, overtime and recordkeeping matters under the Fair Labor Standards Act. That audit, any related or successor inquiry, investigation, assessment or proceeding by any Governmental Authority, and any claim by any individual engaged by Seller arising from the same facts, are referred to in this Side Letter as the "**Installer Classification Matter**."

1.2 Seller has delivered to Buyer copies of the initial document request issued by the U.S. Department of Labor and Seller's written response to it. Seller has also delivered the list of individuals engaged by Seller as independent contractors in its installation operations since January 1, 2023.

## 2. Treatment of the Installer Classification Matter

The Parties agree that any Losses incurred by any Buyer Indemnitee arising out of or relating to the Installer Classification Matter shall be recoverable by Buyer under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement solely as Losses arising from a breach of the representations and warranties of Seller in Section 4.13(c) of the Purchase Agreement. This applies whether or not the Installer Classification Matter, or any fact or circumstance relating to it, is disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule, in this Side Letter or otherwise, or is known to Buyer or its representatives at or before the Closing. No disclosure of the Installer Classification Matter shall be deemed to qualify Section 4.13(c) for purposes of any such recovery.

## 3. Conduct of the Installer Classification Matter

3.1 Until the Closing, Seller shall continue to respond to the Installer Classification Matter in the ordinary course. Seller shall (a) keep Buyer reasonably informed of material developments, (b) deliver to Buyer copies of all material written correspondence with any Governmental Authority within three (3) Business Days after sending or receiving it, and (c) consult in good faith with Buyer before making any material submission.

3.2 Seller shall not settle, compromise or consent to the entry of any judgment or order regarding the Installer Classification Matter without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed, if the settlement, compromise, judgment or order would (a) impose any obligation, restriction or admission on Buyer or the Business after the Closing, or (b) require any change to the classification or engagement terms of any individual whom Buyer engages after the Closing.

3.3 After the Closing, the conduct of any third-party claim forming part of the Installer Classification Matter shall be governed by Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement. Each Party shall make available to the other, on reasonable request, the books, records and personnel within its control that are reasonably necessary to respond to the Installer Classification Matter.

## 4. Engagement of Installers by Buyer

Nothing in this Side Letter obligates Buyer to engage, on any terms, any individual engaged by Seller as an independent contractor. Any offer of engagement or employment by Buyer to any such individual shall be made at Buyer's discretion under Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. General

5.1 *Confidentiality.* The Parties shall treat this Side Letter and its contents as confidential information under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement. A Party may disclose them to its Affiliates, lenders, insurers and professional advisers who are bound by obligations of confidentiality, or as required by Law.

5.2 *Amendment and Waiver.* This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

5.3 *Termination.* If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing in accordance with Section 8.1 of the Purchase Agreement, this Side Letter shall automatically terminate at the same time and be of no further force or effect.

5.4 *Governing Law; Jurisdiction.* This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Side Letter.

5.5 *Counterparts.* This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
