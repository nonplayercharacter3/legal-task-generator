## 8. Schedule 4.9: Installed Systems

**Part A: Defined Term**

"**Specified Warranty Period**" means the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, during which Seller continues to administer warranty claims under Customer Contracts that were submitted to Seller before the Closing Date.

**Part B: Exceptions to Section 4.9**

1. *Roof penetration claims (resolved).* During the period from January 1, 2023 through July 31, 2026, Seller received 112 customer reports of roof leaks or water intrusion at residential Installed Systems. Seller determined that 71 were attributable to the Installed System. All 71 were repaired at Seller's cost, at an aggregate cost of $286,400, and each claim has been closed. The claim log is in Data Room folder 4.9.B.1.

2. *Open warranty claims.* As of July 31, 2026, Seller had 38 open warranty claims under residential Customer Contracts. None of them exceeds $10,000 individually. The open claim log is in Data Room folder 4.9.B.2.

3. *Commercial warranty claim.* A commercial customer under a 2021 engineering, procurement and construction agreement has given notice of a claim relating to inverter pad drainage and conduit water intrusion at a 410 kW carport installation. Seller estimates the cost of remediation at approximately $46,000. Seller has scheduled the remediation work for September 2026.

4. *Permit close-out.* As of July 31, 2026, final inspection sign-off had not been recorded for 23 residential Installed Systems completed in 2025 and 2026. Each of these systems has received utility permission to operate. Seller is scheduling re-inspection. The list is in Data Room folder 4.9.B.4.

5. *Racking recall.* In March 2025, a racking manufacturer issued a voluntary field notice for a rail splice component. Seller identified 214 residential Installed Systems using the affected component. As of July 31, 2026, Seller had completed the manufacturer-funded retrofit on 196 of them. The remaining 18 are scheduled for completion by October 31, 2026.

## 11. Schedule 4.13(c): Employment Compliance

1. In May 2026, the Wage and Hour Division of the U.S. Department of Labor opened an audit of Seller. The audit concerns Seller's engagement of field installers, electricians and installation crew leads as independent contractors rather than employees during the period from January 1, 2023 to the present. It also concerns related wage, overtime and recordkeeping matters under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Seller received an initial document request and delivered its written response in July 2026. No findings or assessment have been issued. Since January 1, 2023, Seller has engaged 137 individuals as independent contractors in its installation operations. The list of these individuals is in Data Room folder 4.13.C.

2. In February 2025, Seller paid $8,200 in back overtime to four (4) non-exempt call center employees following an internal payroll review. No claim was asserted.
