# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

*[Draft of July 24, 2026. Buyer's counsel draft for discussion purposes. Subject to further review and comment by Buyer. Not an offer capable of acceptance. No agreement will exist until definitive documentation is executed and delivered.]*

This **ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and between **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the Business.

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. As an inducement to Buyer to enter into this Agreement, Tobias Renfeld, Seller's founder, Chief Executive Officer and majority shareholder, has agreed to enter into the Key Employee Agreement with Buyer, effective as of the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale of Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights of every kind used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(b) all inventory, including panels, inverters, batteries, racking, mounting hardware and other components;

(c) all Customer Contracts and all other Contracts set forth in Schedule 4.7 (together, the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all intellectual property used in the Business, including the Monitoring Platform and all software, trademarks, trade names (including the name "Rebous Rooftop Energy") and domain names;

(e) all vehicles, tools, equipment, furniture and fixtures;

(f) all permits and licenses held by Seller to the extent transferable, including contractor and electrical licenses;

(g) all books, records, customer lists, lead lists, supplier lists, system design files and permitting files; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's corporate seal, minute books and stock records; (c) all employee benefit plans of Seller and the assets of them; (d) all insurance policies of Seller; (e) all Tax refunds relating to pre-Closing periods; (f) the Contracts listed in Schedule 2.2; and (g) the rights that accrue to Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) trade accounts payable of Seller to third parties in connection with the Business that remain unpaid and are not delinquent as of the Closing Date; and (b) liabilities in respect of the Assigned Contracts, but only to the extent such liabilities are required to be performed after the Closing, were incurred in the ordinary course and do not relate to any failure to perform, improper performance, warranty or other breach by Seller on or before the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible for any liabilities or obligations of Seller of any kind other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Seller shall pay and satisfy when due all Excluded Liabilities. Without limiting the foregoing, the Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) any liabilities arising out of or relating to Seller's ownership or operation of the Business and the Purchased Assets before the Closing Date, except the Assumed Liabilities;

(b) any liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any pre-Closing Tax period;

(c) any liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets;

(d) any liabilities arising out of or relating to Seller's employment or engagement of any individual on or before the Closing Date, including liabilities for wages, overtime, benefits, payroll Taxes, workers' compensation and any classification of any individual as an independent contractor rather than an employee;

(e) any liabilities under any employee benefit plan of Seller;

(f) any liabilities arising out of any action pending or threatened as of the Closing Date; and

(g) any liabilities of Seller to its shareholders or Affiliates.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $38,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $3,850,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX. The Indemnity Escrow Amount is not Buyer's exclusive source of recovery under Article IX.

**2.7 Allocation of Purchase Price.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, under an allocation schedule prepared by Buyer and delivered to Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.8 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as Buyer is required to deduct and withhold under applicable Tax Law.

**2.9 Third-Party Consents.** To the extent any Assigned Contract is not assignable without the consent of a third party and such consent has not been obtained at Closing, this Agreement shall not constitute an assignment of it, and Seller shall, for a period of up to twelve (12) months after the Closing, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain such consent and, pending consent, provide Buyer the economic benefits and burdens of such Assigned Contract.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than those to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (e) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Tobias Renfeld; (f) the third-party consents listed in Schedule 4.4; (g) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors and shareholders approving this Agreement; (h) an IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and (i) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the Purchase Price less the Indemnity Escrow Amount to Seller; (b) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; (d) a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of managers approving this Agreement; and (e) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers, installers and employees. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, enter into, amend or terminate any material Contract, sell or encumber any Purchased Asset outside the ordinary course, or change its standard warranty terms.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Until the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Business, including installation and permitting files and Monitoring Platform data.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, initiate or engage in discussions regarding any acquisition of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets by any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment effective as of the Closing to employees of Seller engaged in the Business. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts such an offer, effective as of the Closing, and shall pay all wages, accrued paid time off and other amounts owed to such employees through the Closing Date.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives to, hold in confidence all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent such information becomes generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach.

**6.6 Non-Competition by Seller.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the Business anywhere in the United States, or solicit any customer of the Business or any employee hired by Buyer under Section 6.4.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

**6.8 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws. Any liabilities arising from non-compliance are Excluded Liabilities.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to no Governmental Authority having enacted or issued any Law or order that restrains or prohibits the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of the following: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of clauses (a) and (b); (d) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; (e) the Key Employee Agreement shall be in full force and effect; and (f) Seller shall have delivered the items in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of the following: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of clauses (a) and (b); and (d) Buyer shall have delivered the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the cause of the failure to close; (c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and such breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and such breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and there shall be no liability on the part of either Party, except that Section 6.5, this Article VIII and Article XI shall survive, and nothing relieves a Party from liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date; provided that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the Parties to be performed after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. The Parties specifically intend that the survival periods in this Section 9.1 replace any otherwise applicable statute of limitations.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective managers, officers and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Seller under it;

(b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement to be performed by Seller under this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its officers and directors from and against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer under this Agreement, or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall deliver written notice to the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") describing the claim in reasonable detail, including the amount of Losses if then known. No claim may be made under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) unless notice of such claim is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period in Section 9.1, and any claim so noticed shall survive until finally resolved. For third-party claims, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party within fifteen (15) days after notice, and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 15% of the Purchase Price, and the aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer is liable under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the same amount.

(b) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate amount of all Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceeds $385,000 (the "**Basket**"), in which event Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(c) *Exceptions.* The limitations in Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation or from fraud, and shall not apply to claims under Section 9.2(b) or Section 9.2(c). The aggregate liability of Seller for all Losses under this Article IX, other than in the case of fraud, shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualification in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of them, less costs of recovery.

**9.6 Payments; Escrow.** Any amount owed by Seller under this Article IX shall be paid first from the Indemnity Escrow Amount in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and, to the extent the Indemnity Escrow Amount is insufficient, directly by Seller within ten (10) Business Days after final determination. On the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the remaining Indemnity Escrow Amount less the aggregate amount of all pending claims, as provided in the Escrow Agreement.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp, registration and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic Taxes for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be apportioned between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date based on the number of days in such period.

**10.3 Cooperation.** The Parties shall cooperate as reasonably requested in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.
