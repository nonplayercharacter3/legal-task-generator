# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

*[Agreed Form. To be executed and delivered at the Closing under Sections 3.2(c) and 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.]*

This **TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Recipient, as buyer, and Provider, as seller, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Provider used in the Business.

B. To support the orderly transition of the Business to Recipient, Recipient has asked Provider to provide certain services after the Closing for a limited period, and Provider has agreed to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

C. This Agreement is the "Transition Services Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Customer Data**" means all data relating to customers of the Business or to the Installed Systems, including customer names, addresses, contact details, account and payment information, system design and permitting files, and system production and performance data, including all such data held in or generated by the Monitoring Platform.

"**Losses**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Provider Personnel**" means the employees, contractors and agents of Provider who perform the Services.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as modified from time to time under this Agreement.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of, and Schedules to, this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Closing Date and continues until the date that is 6 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 10.

**2.2 Service Periods.** Each Service shall be provided for the period stated for it in Schedule A or, if no period is stated, for the Term. No Service shall be provided after the end of the Term unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

**2.3 Extension.** Recipient may request an extension of any Service by written notice to Provider at least thirty (30) days before the scheduled end of that Service. Provider shall consider any such request in good faith. Any extension, and the fees applicable to it, shall be agreed in writing by the Parties.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** Provider shall provide the Services to Recipient during the Term. Provider shall perform the Services (a) in substantially the same manner, scope, priority and quality as it performed them for the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, (b) with reasonable skill and care, and (c) in compliance with all applicable Laws (together, the "**Service Standard**").

**3.2 Personnel.** Provider shall assign Provider Personnel with appropriate skill and experience to perform the Services. Provider is responsible for the compensation, benefits and supervision of all Provider Personnel. Provider Personnel are not employees of Recipient.

**3.3 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall appoint a service coordinator, identified in Schedule A, to act as its primary contact for the Services. Each Party may replace its service coordinator by written notice to the other Party.

**3.4 Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with the information, access and decisions reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services. Provider shall not be responsible for any failure to perform to the extent caused by Recipient's failure to do so.

**3.5 Changes to Services.** Either Party may propose changes to the scope of any Service. No change is effective unless agreed in writing by both Parties, including any adjustment to the Service Fees.

**3.6 Migration Assistance.** Throughout the Term, Provider shall cooperate in good faith with Recipient to migrate the Services, and all Customer Data, to Recipient or its designee. This includes providing data extracts in a commercially standard format and reasonable access to knowledgeable Provider Personnel.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the monthly fees for each Service set forth in Schedule A (the "**Service Fees**"). Service Fees for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**4.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider, at cost and without markup, for third-party costs that Provider reasonably incurs in providing the Services and that are identified in Schedule A or approved in advance in writing by Recipient, including permit filing fees paid to Governmental Authorities on behalf of Recipient.

**4.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice to Provider, with reasonable detail of the dispute, before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**4.5 Taxes.** Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes properly invoiced by Provider, other than Taxes based on Provider's income.

## 5. CUSTOMER DATA AND SYSTEMS

**5.1 Ownership.** As between the Parties, all Customer Data and the Monitoring Platform are owned by Recipient from and after the Closing. Provider acquires no right in any of them under this Agreement.

**5.2 Use of Customer Data.** Provider shall access, use and process Customer Data solely as necessary to perform the Services, and in accordance with Recipient's reasonable written instructions and applicable Laws.

**5.3 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Customer Data that are no less protective than those Provider maintained before the Closing Date. Provider shall notify Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Customer Data.

**5.4 Return of Data.** When the applicable Service ends, Provider shall return to Recipient all Customer Data relating to that Service and then, on Recipient's written instruction, permanently delete all copies in Provider's possession, except as required by Law.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Confidentiality Obligations.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party that it receives in connection with this Agreement, and shall use that information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. Customer Data is confidential information of Recipient.

**6.2 Exceptions.** Section 6.1 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (c) is required to be disclosed by Law, provided the disclosing Party gives the other Party prompt prior notice where legally permitted.

**6.3 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Section 6 is in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against any and all Losses arising out of or relating to the Services, Recipient's receipt or use of the Services, or Recipient's operation of the Business, including Losses arising from Provider's negligence in performing the Services, except to the extent such Losses result from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**7.2 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates and their respective managers, officers and employees from and against any and all Losses to the extent arising out of Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services.

**7.3 Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification under this Section 7 shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. For third-party claims, the indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim without the indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

## 8. LIMITATIONS OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, except to the extent paid to a third party under an indemnification obligation in Section 7.

**8.2 Cap.** The aggregate liability of each Party arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid by Recipient to Provider under this Agreement.

**8.3 Exceptions.** The limitation in Section 8.2 shall not apply to Recipient's obligations under Section 7.1, or to Recipient's obligation to pay Service Fees and pass-through costs under Section 4.

**8.4 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the Services.

## 9. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, severe weather, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, or widespread failure of utilities or telecommunications. The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents performance of any Service for more than fifteen (15) days, Recipient may terminate that Service under Section 10.2 without cost.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Expiration.** This Agreement expires at the end of the Term, unless terminated earlier under this Section 10.

**10.2 Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on fifteen (15) days' written notice to Provider. The Service Fees for that Service shall be reduced or eliminated accordingly from the effective date of termination.

**10.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice of it. Provider may not terminate any Service for non-payment of amounts disputed in good faith under Section 4.4.

**10.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date, and Provider shall comply with Sections 3.6 and 5.4. Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10.4, 11 and 12 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**11.1 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute under this Agreement through their service coordinators. If the dispute is not resolved within ten (10) Business Days, either Party may refer it to a senior executive of each Party, who shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after the referral.

**11.2 Equitable Relief.** Nothing in this Section 11 prevents a Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief to protect Customer Data or confidential information.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**12.1 Relationship of the Parties.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**12.3 Assignment.** Provider may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, or delegate the performance of any of the Services, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Provider. This Agreement is binding on and inures to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**12.5 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, it shall not affect any other term or invalidate it in any other jurisdiction.

**12.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified Persons under Section 7, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**12.8 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**12.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
