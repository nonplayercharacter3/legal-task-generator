# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF BRUKAISK SOLAR HOLDINGS, LLC

**In Lieu of a Meeting**

**Dated as of August 21, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act by written consent without a meeting under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (the "**Act**") and the Company's limited liability company agreement, as amended (the "**LLC Agreement**"). By signing below, they waive any notice requirements and adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called and held meeting of the Board.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Board has reviewed a proposed acquisition by the Company of substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of **Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Seller is engaged in the design, sale, installation, maintenance and monitoring of residential and commercial solar energy and battery storage systems (the "**Acquisition**").

1.2 The Acquisition is to be made under an Asset Purchase Agreement between the Company, as buyer, and Seller, to be dated as of August 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Board has received and reviewed the substantially final form of the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.3 The Board has also received and reviewed the substantially final forms of the following documents, each of which is to be entered into at signing or at the Closing in connection with the Acquisition:

(a) the Side Letter between the Company and Seller, to be dated as of the date of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the Key Employee Agreement between the Company and Tobias Renfeld, Seller's founder, Chief Executive Officer and majority shareholder, to be dated as of the date of the Purchase Agreement and effective as of the Closing;

(c) the Transition Services Agreement between Seller, as provider, and the Company, as recipient, with a term ending 6 months after the Closing Date;

(d) the Escrow Agreement among the Company, Seller and the Escrow Agent;

(e) the Bill of Sale and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement; and

(f) the Disclosure Schedule to be delivered by Seller.

The documents in clauses (a) through (e), together with each other agreement, instrument and certificate to be delivered by the Company under the Purchase Agreement, are referred to in this consent as the "**Transaction Documents**."

1.4 The Board has had the opportunity to ask questions of the Company's officers and its legal, financial and tax advisers about the Acquisition and the Transaction Documents, including the due diligence findings on the Business. It has considered the matters it deems relevant.

## 2. Approval of the Acquisition and the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has determined that the Acquisition, on the terms of the Purchase Agreement, is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the Company's performance of its obligations under it, are approved and adopted in all respects. This includes:

(a) payment of an aggregate Purchase Price of $38,500,000, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities; and

(b) deposit at the Closing of $3,850,000 of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent as the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, with any changes that the Authorized Officer executing it approves. Such officer's execution and delivery of the Purchase Agreement will be conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. Approval of the Transaction Documents

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of each Transaction Document, and the Company's performance of its obligations under each of them, are approved in all respects;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to execute and deliver each Transaction Document, substantially in the form presented to the Board, with any changes that the Authorized Officer executing it approves. Such officer's execution and delivery of a Transaction Document will be conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 4. Employment of Tobias Renfeld

**RESOLVED**, that the Board approves the employment of Tobias Renfeld as President of the Company's Rebous Rooftop Energy division, effective as of the Closing Date, on the terms of the Key Employee Agreement. This includes the base salary, annual bonus opportunity and retention award set forth in it;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any grant of profits interests to Mr. Renfeld contemplated by Section 4.4 of the Key Employee Agreement remains subject to separate approval by the Board under the Company's equity incentive plan, and nothing in this consent constitutes that approval.

## 5. Funding

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to fund the Purchase Price and the Company's other payment obligations at the Closing from the Company's cash on hand and available sources of funds. They may also arrange the related wire transfers to Seller and to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Sections 2.5, 2.6 and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Closing Certificates

**RESOLVED**, that any Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver at the Closing the following, as required by Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement:

(a) a certificate certifying these resolutions; and

(b) the officer's certificate required by Section 7.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the Secretary and any other officer of the Company designated in writing by the Chief Executive Officer (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take the following actions as they consider necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions and to consummate the Acquisition:

(a) execute, deliver and file all agreements, certificates, instruments, notices, consents and other documents;

(b) pay all fees and expenses; and

(c) take all other actions;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to exercise or waive any of the Company's rights under the Transaction Documents before or after the Closing. This authority does not extend to any amendment of the Purchase Agreement or the Side Letter that changes the Purchase Price or the Indemnity Escrow Amount. Any such amendment requires the further approval of the Board;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any manager, officer, employee or agent of the Company in connection with the Acquisition or the matters contemplated by these resolutions, which are consistent with them, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness; Counterparts

8.1 This consent is effective when signed by all members of the Board. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

8.2 This consent may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
