# BILL OF SALE

*[Agreed Form. To be executed and delivered by Seller at the Closing under Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.]*

This **BILL OF SALE** (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026, by **Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets, and Buyer has agreed to purchase them, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement.

B. This Bill of Sale is the "Bill of Sale" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Seller is delivering it under Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Purchase Price paid under the Purchase Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets. The conveyance is free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens. To Have and to Hold the Purchased Assets unto Buyer, its successors and assigns, forever.

2.2 Without limiting Section 2.1, the Purchased Assets include the following, as more fully described in Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement:

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(b) all inventory, including panels, inverters, batteries, racking, mounting hardware and other components;

(c) all Assigned Contracts, subject to Section 4 of this Bill of Sale;

(d) all intellectual property used in the Business, including the Monitoring Platform and all software, trademarks, trade names (including the name "Rebous Rooftop Energy") and domain names;

(e) all vehicles, tools, equipment, furniture and fixtures;

(f) all permits and licenses held by Seller, to the extent transferable, including contractor and electrical licenses;

(g) all books, records, customer lists, lead lists, supplier lists, system design files and permitting files; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

## 3. Excluded Assets

Seller does not sell, assign, transfer, convey or deliver to Buyer, and Buyer does not acquire, any right, title or interest in, to or under the Excluded Assets. All Excluded Assets remain the property of Seller.

## 4. Non-Assignable Contracts

This Bill of Sale does not constitute an assignment of any Assigned Contract that is not assignable without the consent of a third party if that consent has not been obtained at the Closing. Section 2.9 of the Purchase Agreement governs each such Assigned Contract. When the required consent is obtained, the Assigned Contract is assigned to Buyer under this Bill of Sale without further action by either party.

## 5. No Assumption of Liabilities

This Bill of Sale conveys assets only. Buyer does not assume any liability or obligation of Seller by this Bill of Sale. Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities is made only under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. The Excluded Liabilities remain the liabilities of Seller under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Purchase Agreement and is subject to its terms. The representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnities of the parties in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale. They survive its delivery to the extent, and for the periods, provided in the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies any of them.

## 7. Further Assurances

At Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver further instruments of sale, conveyance, transfer and assignment, and take such other actions, as Buyer reasonably requires to vest in Buyer good and valid title to the Purchased Assets and to put Buyer in actual possession and operating control of them, in accordance with Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement. These include the following:

(a) executing vehicle title certificates and registration transfer documents for the vehicles included in the Purchased Assets;

(b) executing short-form assignments of trademarks, domain names and other registered intellectual property in a form suitable for recording with the applicable registry; and

(c) giving notices to customers under residential Customer Contracts, and to counterparties under other Assigned Contracts, as listed in Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

## 8. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller but on behalf and for the benefit of Buyer, to take the following actions:

(a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) give receipts and releases for any of the Purchased Assets;

(c) endorse checks and other instruments payable to Seller that relate to accounts receivable included in the Purchased Assets; and

(d) institute and prosecute, at Buyer's expense, any proceeding that Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title to the Purchased Assets.

This power of attorney is coupled with an interest. It is not revocable by Seller, and it does not terminate on the dissolution of Seller.

## 9. Successors and Assigns

This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

## 10. Governing Law

This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Bill of Sale as if set out in full in it, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Bill of Sale.

## 11. Counterparts; Electronic Delivery

This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
