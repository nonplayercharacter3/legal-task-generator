# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This **KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 21, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**"), by and between **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), and **Tobias Renfeld**, an individual ("**Executive**"). The Company and Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, the Company, as buyer, and Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), have entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of the Signing Date (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will acquire substantially all of the assets of Seller, including Seller's goodwill.

B. Executive is the founder, Chief Executive Officer and majority shareholder of Seller. Executive will receive substantial direct and indirect economic benefit from the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Company desires to employ Executive, and Executive desires to be employed by the Company, beginning on the closing date under the Purchase Agreement (the "**Closing Date**"), on the terms of this Agreement.

D. Executive's entry into this Agreement, including the covenants in Article VII, is a material inducement to the Company to enter into the Purchase Agreement and to pay the purchase price under it. The Company would not have entered into the Purchase Agreement without those covenants.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with, such Person.

"**Board**" means the board of managers of the Company.

"**Business**" means the installation of residential solar photovoltaic systems.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.3.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1.

"**Customer**" means any Person that purchased, leased or contracted for products or services of the Business from Seller or the Company at any time during the twenty-four (24) months before the date of determination, and any Person that Seller or the Company actively solicited for the Business during the twelve (12) months before that date.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.4.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending on the later of (a) the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Closing Date and (b) the second (2nd) anniversary of the date Executive's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

"**Territory**" means the United States of America.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## ARTICLE II. EFFECTIVENESS; EMPLOYMENT

**2.1 Effectiveness.** This Agreement becomes effective on the Closing Date, conditioned on the occurrence of the closing under the Purchase Agreement. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the closing occurs, this Agreement automatically terminates and is void, and neither Party has any obligation under it.

**2.2 Employment.** Starting on the Closing Date, the Company shall employ Executive, and Executive accepts employment with the Company, on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

**2.3 Term.** Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Closing Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years (the "**Initial Term**"). After the Initial Term, it renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Employment may end earlier under Article VI.

## ARTICLE III. POSITION AND DUTIES

**3.1 Position.** Executive shall serve as President of the Company's Rebous Rooftop Energy division and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

**3.2 Duties.** Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for that position, and any other duties consistent with that position that the Chief Executive Officer or the Board assigns from time to time. These include integrating the operations, personnel and customer relationships acquired from Seller into the Company.

**3.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time, attention and efforts to the Company. Executive may manage personal investments and serve on civic or charitable boards, provided those activities do not interfere with Executive's duties and do not violate Article VII or Article VIII. Executive may serve on the board of any for-profit entity only with the prior written consent of the Board.

**3.4 Place of Performance.** Executive's principal place of employment is the Company's offices in the metropolitan area where Seller's headquarters were located immediately before the Closing Date. Executive will travel as the business reasonably requires.

## ARTICLE IV. COMPENSATION

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $375,000 ("**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board shall review the Base Salary at least once a year and may increase it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target amount of fifty percent (50%) of Base Salary. The bonus will be based on achievement of performance goals that the Board sets after consulting with Executive. The bonus for the fiscal year in which the Closing Date occurs will be prorated. Except as provided in Section 6.5, any bonus is payable only if Executive remains employed through the payment date, and in any event no later than March 15 of the year following the fiscal year to which it relates.

**4.3 Retention Award.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention award of $500,000 in two equal installments, on the first and second anniversaries of the Closing Date. Each installment is payable only if Executive remains employed on the applicable anniversary, except as provided in Section 6.5.

**4.4 Equity Incentive.** Subject to Board approval, the Company shall grant Executive profits interests in the Company under its equity incentive plan, in an amount and on vesting terms set forth in a separate award agreement.

## ARTICLE V. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**5.1 Benefits.** Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans and programs that the Company maintains for its senior executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

**5.2 Paid Time Off.** Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off each calendar year, in accordance with Company policy.

**5.3 Business Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for all reasonable business expenses incurred in performing Executive's duties, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policies.

## ARTICLE VI. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**6.1 Death or Disability.** Executive's employment ends automatically on Executive's death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment on written notice if Executive has a disability that prevents Executive from performing the essential functions of the position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period.

**6.2 Termination by the Company.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment at any time, with or without Cause, by written notice to Executive.

**6.3 Cause.** "**Cause**" means Executive's:

(a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing Executive's duties that causes material harm to the Company;

(c) material breach of Article VII or Article VIII, or of any other material provision of this Agreement, that, if curable, is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice;

(d) willful failure to follow a lawful directive of the Chief Executive Officer or the Board that is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(e) fraud, embezzlement or misappropriation involving the Company or Seller.

**6.4 Good Reason.** Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in Base Salary, other than an across-the-board reduction that applies to all senior executives of the Company; (b) a material diminution of Executive's title, authority or duties; or (c) relocation of Executive's principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles. Good Reason exists only if (i) Executive gives the Company written notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs, (ii) the Company fails to cure the condition within thirty (30) days after receiving that notice, and (iii) Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**6.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause (other than under Section 6.1), or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall:

(a) continue to pay Executive's Base Salary for twelve (12) months;

(b) pay any unpaid installment of the retention award under Section 4.3 within sixty (60) days after the termination date; and

(c) pay the COBRA premiums for Executive and Executive's dependents for up to twelve (12) months.

These payments are conditioned on Executive executing a general release of claims in favor of the Company, in a form the Company provides, and the release becoming irrevocable within sixty (60) days after termination. They are also conditioned on Executive's continued compliance with Articles VII and VIII.

**6.6 Accrued Obligations.** Whenever Executive's employment ends, the Company shall pay Executive (a) earned but unpaid Base Salary, (b) accrued but unused paid time off to the extent required by Company policy or law, and (c) unreimbursed business expenses properly incurred.

**6.7 Resignation from Positions.** When Executive's employment ends, Executive shall resign from all officer, manager and other positions with the Company and its Affiliates.

## ARTICLE VII. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as an owner, principal, partner, member, shareholder, officer, director, employee, consultant, agent, lender or otherwise:

(a) engage in the Business anywhere in the Territory, or own, manage, operate, control, finance, be employed by, provide services to, or participate in any Person that engages in the Business anywhere in the Territory;

(b) solicit, induce or attempt to induce any Customer to purchase products or services of the Business from any Person other than the Company or its Affiliates, or to reduce or terminate its business with the Company or its Affiliates in the Business;

(c) solicit, recruit, hire or engage, or attempt to solicit, recruit, hire or engage, any individual who is or was, during the preceding twelve (12) months, an employee of or installer or contractor engaged by the Company or its Affiliates in the Business, or induce any such individual to end that relationship; or

(d) induce or attempt to induce any supplier, dealer, channel partner or other business relation of the Company or its Affiliates in the Business to cease doing business with them, or to materially and adversely change its relationship with them.

Executive may own, as a passive investment, up to two percent (2%) of any class of publicly traded securities of any Person without violating this Section 7.1.

**7.2 Non-Disparagement.** During the Term and for three (3) years afterward, Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, its Affiliates, or their products, services, managers or officers. This Section 7.2 does not restrict truthful testimony required by law or communications with any governmental authority.

**7.3 Acknowledgments.** Executive acknowledges that:

(a) the covenants in this Article VII are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and subject matter, and are necessary to protect the goodwill, Confidential Information and customer relationships that the Company is acquiring under the Purchase Agreement;

(b) Executive is entering into these covenants in connection with the sale of a business and its goodwill, in which Executive has a substantial ownership interest; and

(c) Executive has had the opportunity to consult with independent counsel before signing this Agreement.

**7.4 Tolling.** If Executive breaches Section 7.1, the Restricted Period is extended by the period of the breach.

**7.5 Reformation.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any covenant in this Article VII is unenforceable because of its duration, geographic scope or subject matter, the court may modify the covenant to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates or the assets acquired from Seller. It includes customer lists and data, system design files, permitting files, pricing, supplier terms, installer rates, financial information, business plans, and the software, source code and data of the remote monitoring platform acquired from Seller. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach.

**8.2 Non-Disclosure.** During and after Executive's employment, Executive shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence. Executive shall not use or disclose it except as required in performing Executive's duties to the Company or as required by law. If disclosure is required by law, Executive shall give the Company prompt prior written notice, where legally permitted.

**8.3 Return of Property.** When Executive's employment ends, or earlier if the Company requests, Executive shall return to the Company all Company property and all documents and media containing Confidential Information, and shall not keep any copies.

**8.4 Work Product.** All inventions, works of authorship, software, designs, processes and improvements that Executive conceives or develops, alone or with others, during employment and that relate to the actual or anticipated business of the Company ("**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. Executive assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in all Work Product. Executive shall execute any documents the Company reasonably requests to perfect its rights. This Section 8.4 does not apply to any invention that qualifies for exclusion under Section 805 of Title 19 of the Delaware Code.

**8.5 Protected Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive will not be held criminally or civilly liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a complaint or other filing made under seal in a lawsuit.

## ARTICLE IX. REMEDIES

**9.1 Injunctive Relief.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Article VII or Article VIII would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is entitled to temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief and specific performance to enforce those Articles, without posting bond and without proving actual damages, in addition to any other remedy.

**9.2 Forfeiture.** If Executive materially breaches Article VII, the Company's obligations under Section 6.5 end, and Executive shall repay any amounts paid under Section 6.5(a).

## ARTICLE X. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required by law to be withheld.

**10.2 Section 409A.** The Parties intend that payments under this Agreement are exempt from or comply with Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and this Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. Any payment conditioned on a release that could be paid in either of two calendar years will be paid in the later year.

**10.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its principal office, attention: Chief Executive Officer. Notices to Executive go to the most recent address on file with the Company.

**10.4 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive, and Executive may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets. This Agreement binds and benefits the Company's successors and assigns.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing. No waiver of any breach is a waiver of any other or later breach.

**10.6 Severability.** Subject to Section 7.5, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.8 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.9 Survival.** Articles VII, VIII and IX, Sections 6.5 and 6.6, and this Article X survive the end of Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
