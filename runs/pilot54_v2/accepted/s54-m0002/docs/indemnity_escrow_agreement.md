# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

*[Agreed Form. To be executed and delivered at the Closing under Sections 3.2(d) and 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.]*

This **INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Brukaisk Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Rebous Rooftop Energy, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Corvane Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Seller used in the Business.

B. Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $3,850,000 of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. That amount will be held as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. This Escrow Agreement is the "Escrow Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less all amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, in substantially the form of Exhibit A.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $3,850,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds, in accordance with Sections 2.6 and 3.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.2 Appointment and Escrow Account.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent. It shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds and shall disburse them only in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**2.3 Confirmation of Receipt.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Indemnity Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the Business Day it is received.

**2.4 No Interest in Escrow Funds.** No Party may assign, pledge or otherwise encumber its interest in the Escrow Funds, and the Escrow Funds are not subject to any Lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of either Party, except as provided in this Escrow Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT

**3.1 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a money market deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limit, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller direct by Joint Written Instructions. Absent such instructions, the Escrow Funds shall remain so invested.

**3.2 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds form part of the Escrow Funds and are disbursed with them under Sections 4 and 5.

**3.3 Investment Losses.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss on any investment made in accordance with this Section 3, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Claim Notices.** If a Buyer Indemnitee claims indemnification from Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to the Escrow Agent and Seller a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail, as required by Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, and state the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of them if not then known.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written notice to the Escrow Agent and Buyer (an "**Objection Notice**") within thirty (30) days after Seller receives the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall state the amount in dispute and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within that thirty (30) day period, it shall disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Funds the amount stated in the Claim Notice on the fifth (5th) Business Day after the period ends. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed amount within the same time.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any disputed amount until it receives (a) Joint Written Instructions directing its disbursement, or (b) a copy of a Final Order, together with a certificate from the Party presenting it stating that the order is final and non-appealable. The Escrow Agent shall disburse in accordance with those instructions or that order within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

**4.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, Buyer may deliver an updated Claim Notice when the amount of Losses becomes known. Sections 4.2 through 4.4 apply to any increase in the amount claimed.

**4.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay any amount owed to Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available, and Seller remains liable for the balance in accordance with Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Release on the Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate amount of all claims stated in Claim Notices delivered before the Release Date that have not been paid or otherwise resolved (each, a "**Pending Claim**").

**5.2 Pending Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amount of each Pending Claim after the Release Date and disburse it under Section 4. When a Pending Claim is finally resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller any amount retained for it that is not payable to Buyer, within five (5) Business Days after receiving the applicable Joint Written Instructions or Final Order.

**5.3 Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**5.4 Termination.** This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed, except that Sections 6.3, 7 and 10 survive termination.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The duties of the Escrow Agent are only those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties apply. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, bound by, or required to interpret the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper person. Before acting on any disbursement instruction delivered other than by original signed document, the Escrow Agent shall confirm it by telephone call-back to a person listed on Exhibit B, other than the person who delivered it.

**6.3 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, unless the indemnified matter results from the breach of one Party, in which case that Party shall bear it.

**6.4 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, it may continue to hold the Escrow Funds affected until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also interplead the affected Escrow Funds in a court under Section 10.2.

**6.5 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. In either case, Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, and the Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Funds to the successor on its appointment. If no successor is appointed within that period, the Escrow Agent may petition a court under Section 10.2 to appoint one.

## 7. FEES AND EXPENSES

The Escrow Agent's fees are set forth in Exhibit C. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. No fee or expense of the Escrow Agent shall be paid from the Escrow Funds.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Owner.** The Parties agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed, in accordance with Treasury Regulations Section 1.468B-8. All interest and earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer until disbursed. Any amount disbursed to Seller shall be treated as a payment of Purchase Price when disbursed.

**8.2 Tax Documentation.** Each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required by Law with respect to the Escrow Funds.

**8.3 Treatment of Disbursements.** Any disbursement to Buyer from the Escrow Funds shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, in accordance with Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement, unless otherwise required by Law.

## 9. NOTICES

All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to Buyer and Seller shall be given to the addresses provided under Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be given to: Corvane Trust Company, N.A., Escrow Services, [address], Attention: [●], email: [●]. A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only on actual receipt.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Escrow Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.3 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** As between Buyer and Seller, nothing in this Escrow Agreement limits or expands Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, or Buyer's right to recover from Seller directly under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights under it to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement, on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its escrow business, succeeds to it under this Escrow Agreement without further act.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.6 Severability.** If any term of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, it shall not affect any other term or invalidate it in any other jurisdiction.

**10.7 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering and customer identification Laws.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
