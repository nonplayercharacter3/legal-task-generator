This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") sets out the terms on which Halvorsen Optics Inc. ("**Supplier**") will make cooperative marketing funds available to Brightwater Medical Supply LLC ("**Distributor**") for calendar year 2026. It is entered into under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of January 15, 2024, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated as of September 1, 2025 (as so amended, the "**Agreement**").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Program Year**" means the period from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

1.3 "**Qualifying Purchases**" means the net invoice value of Products invoiced by Supplier to Distributor during the Program Year, excluding taxes, freight, insurance and other shipping charges, and less credits issued for returns or pricing adjustments.

1.4 "**Co-op Funds**" means the cooperative marketing funds that accrue to Distributor under Section 2.

## 2. Accrual of Co-op Funds

2.1 **Accrual Rate.** Co-op Funds accrue at the rate of 2% of Qualifying Purchases of capital equipment (HO-OCT-500, HO-FC-220, HO-AR-310 and HO-TN-140) and 1% of Qualifying Purchases of accessory kits and parts (HO-ACC-KIT).

2.2 **Annual Maximum.** Total Co-op Funds accrued for the Program Year shall not exceed $90,000.

2.3 **Statements.** Within 15 business days after the end of each calendar quarter in the Program Year, Supplier shall give Distributor a written statement of Co-op Funds accrued, claimed, approved and available as of the end of that quarter.

## 3. Eligible Activities

3.1 **Eligible Activities.** Distributor may use Co-op Funds only to reimburse its documented out-of-pocket costs paid to third parties for the following activities, carried out in the Territory during the Program Year to promote the Products:

(a) print and digital advertising, including placements in ophthalmology and optometry trade publications;

(b) booth space, booth graphics and shipping of demonstration Products for regional trade shows and professional society meetings held in the Territory;

(c) direct mail and email campaigns directed to Customers and prospective Customers;

(d) Product-specific brochures, sell sheets and other collateral; and

(e) educational and clinical training events for Customer personnel on the proper use of the Products, excluding meals, travel and lodging for attendees.

3.2 **Excluded Costs.** Co-op Funds may not be used for (a) Distributor's salaries, commissions, overhead or internal staff costs, (b) materials that promote any product other than the Products, (c) any payment, discount, rebate, gift, meal, travel or other item of value provided to any Customer, any healthcare professional or any person in a position to refer, order or recommend the Products, or (d) any activity directed specifically to customers outside the Territory.

3.3 **Compliance.** Distributor shall carry out all activities funded under this Letter Agreement in compliance with Section 6.5 of the Agreement and all applicable laws, including the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, applicable state anti-kickback and fee-splitting laws and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's requirements on device labeling and promotion. Distributor shall not make any claim about a Product that is inconsistent with its cleared labeling or with materials supplied by Supplier.

## 4. Approval of Materials

4.1 All advertising, promotional and training materials funded under this Letter Agreement that use any Halvorsen Mark are subject to Supplier's prior written approval under Section 8.3 of the Agreement and must comply with Section 8 of the Agreement and Supplier's brand guidelines.

4.2 For any trade show or event to be funded under Section 3.1(b) or 3.1(e), Distributor shall submit a brief written description of the event, its date and location and the estimated cost to Supplier's Director of Channel Marketing at least 20 business days before the event. Supplier shall confirm or decline eligibility in writing within ten business days after receipt.

## 5. Claims and Reimbursement

5.1 **Claim Submission.** Distributor shall submit each claim for reimbursement within 60 days after completing the funded activity, and in any event no later than January 31, 2027. Each claim must include (a) copies of third-party invoices and proof of payment, (b) a copy or photograph of each funded advertisement, piece of collateral or booth display, and (c) for events, an attendance summary that does not include any patient information.

5.2 **Review.** Supplier shall approve or reject each claim, in whole or in part, by written notice within 30 days after receiving a complete claim, stating the reasons for any rejection. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject a claim that complies with this Letter Agreement.

5.3 **Method of Reimbursement.** Supplier shall reimburse approved claims, at its election, by issuing a credit memo applicable against Distributor's next invoice or by payment within 45 days after approval. Credit memos issued under this Letter Agreement do not reduce net invoice value for purposes of Section 4.4 of the Agreement and Exhibit B to the Agreement.

5.4 **Limit.** Supplier is not obligated to reimburse any amount that exceeds the Co-op Funds accrued and unclaimed at the time the claim is approved.

## 6. Expiry of Unused Funds

6.1 Co-op Funds that have not been claimed by January 31, 2027 expire and do not carry forward to any later period. Co-op Funds have no cash value except as provided in Section 5.3.

6.2 If the Agreement expires or is terminated during the Program Year, Co-op Funds accrued before the effective date of expiration or termination remain available for claims for activities completed before that date, provided that the claims are submitted within 60 days after that date.

## 7. Records and Audit

7.1 Distributor shall keep complete and accurate records supporting each claim for at least three years after the end of the Program Year. On at least ten business days' written notice, and not more than once during the Program Year and once during the following year, Supplier or its independent auditor may inspect those records during Distributor's normal business hours.

7.2 If an audit shows that Distributor received reimbursement for any amount that did not comply with this Letter Agreement, Distributor shall repay that amount, or Supplier may offset it against future Co-op Funds, within 30 days after written notice.

## 8. General

8.1 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This Letter Agreement supplements the Agreement and does not amend it. Except as stated in this Letter Agreement, the Agreement remains in full force and effect and the terms of the Agreement apply to the activities funded under this Letter Agreement.

8.2 **No Price Concession.** Co-op Funds are provided solely to reimburse Distributor's marketing costs for the Products. They are not a discount on or reduction of the prices set out in Exhibit B to the Agreement, and Distributor shall not pass any Co-op Funds through to Customers.

8.3 **No Future Commitment.** This Letter Agreement applies only to the Program Year. Supplier is not obligated to offer a cooperative marketing program for any later period.

8.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Letter Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 18.3 of the Agreement. Claims, event descriptions and routine program correspondence under Sections 4 and 5 may be sent by email to Supplier's Director of Channel Marketing at coop@halvorsenoptics.com.

8.5 **Governing Law.** This Letter Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

8.6 **Counterparts.** This Letter Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
