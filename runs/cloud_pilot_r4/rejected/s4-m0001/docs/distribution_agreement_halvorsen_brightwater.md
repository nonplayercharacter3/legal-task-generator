# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Halvorsen Optics Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Lindstrom Parkway, Rochester, New York 14623 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Brightwater Medical Supply LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 88 Harborview Drive, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures ophthalmic diagnostic devices and related accessories and consumables.

B. Distributor is in the business of marketing and distributing medical equipment to ophthalmology and optometry practices, ambulatory surgery centers and hospitals.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting securities of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Competing Product**" means any ophthalmic diagnostic device, including any optical coherence tomography system, fundus camera, autorefractor, keratometer, tonometer or visual field analyzer, that is not manufactured by or for Supplier and that performs substantially the same function as any Product.

1.3 "**Customer**" means any ophthalmology or optometry practice, ambulatory surgery center, hospital, clinic or other healthcare provider located in the Territory that purchases Products for its own use and not for resale.

1.4 "**Halvorsen Marks**" means the trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress of Supplier listed in Exhibit C, and any other marks that Supplier uses on or in connection with the Products.

1.5 "**Landed Cost**" means the price paid by Distributor to Supplier for a Product, plus freight, insurance, duties and other shipping charges actually paid by Distributor to deliver that Product to Distributor's facility.

1.6 "**Products**" means the ophthalmic diagnostic devices, accessories and consumables listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.7 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.8 "**Territory**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania (the "**Territory**"), and Distributor accepts that appointment.

2.2 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, sales agent or reseller for the Products in the Territory and shall not itself sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except that Supplier may sell directly to (a) any national group purchasing organization or integrated delivery network with which Supplier has a contract in effect on the Effective Date and that is listed in Exhibit D, and (b) any Customer for which Distributor has declined in writing to fulfill an order.

2.3 **No Sales Outside Territory.** Distributor shall not actively solicit Customers, establish any branch or warehouse for the Products, or advertise the Products specifically targeted outside the Territory.

2.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Neither Party is the agent, partner or joint venturer of the other, and neither Party has authority to bind the other.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 16.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms of one year each (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. PURCHASE ORDERS AND FORECASTS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall purchase Products by issuing Purchase Orders that state the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five business days after receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order that is consistent with Distributor's most recent forecast under Section 4.3.

4.2 **Purchase Order Terms.** The terms of this Agreement govern each Purchase Order. Any additional or different terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect unless signed by both Parties.

4.3 **Forecasts.** By the fifth business day of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated Product requirements for the following four calendar quarters.

4.4 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of not less than the annual minimum stated in Exhibit B for each contract year. If Distributor fails to meet the annual minimum in any contract year, Supplier's sole remedy is to convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive by written notice given within 60 days after the end of that contract year.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit B. Supplier may change the prices in Exhibit B no more than once in any contract year on at least 90 days' prior written notice, and no single increase may exceed five percent.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each order.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within 45 days after the invoice date. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within 15 days after receipt of the invoice and shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

5.4 **Late Payment.** Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent per month and the highest rate permitted by law.

5.5 **Resale Prices.** Distributor may set its own resale prices for the Products.

## 6. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

6.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory and shall maintain a sales force and clinical support staff adequate to do so.

6.2 **Installation and Support.** Distributor shall provide installation, user training and first-line technical support to Customers in accordance with Supplier's then-current service guidelines. Distributor shall maintain at least two field service technicians certified by Supplier on the Products.

6.3 **Records.** Distributor shall maintain accurate records of all Product sales, including the serial or lot number, Customer name and address and date of sale for each unit, for at least seven years after the sale, and shall make those records available to Supplier on reasonable request.

6.4 **Non-Competition.** During the Term, Distributor and its Affiliates shall not, directly or indirectly, manufacture, distribute, market, promote or sell any Competing Product in the Territory, or act as sales agent or representative for any manufacturer of a Competing Product in the Territory.

6.5 **Compliance with Laws.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement, including the federal Anti-Kickback Statute and state anti-kickback and fee-splitting laws.

## 7. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS; DELIVERY

7.1 **Supply.** Supplier shall manufacture and supply Products ordered under accepted Purchase Orders in accordance with the specifications in Exhibit A.

7.2 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in Rochester, New York (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier designated by Distributor. Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

7.3 **Marketing Support.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, at no charge, with reasonable quantities of Product literature, demonstration materials and sales training.

7.4 **Product Changes.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least 90 days' prior written notice of any discontinuation of a Product or any material change to a Product's specifications.

## 8. TRADEMARK AND IP RESTRICTIONS

8.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Halvorsen Marks in the Territory solely to identify, advertise and promote the Products in accordance with this Agreement and Supplier's brand guidelines as provided to Distributor from time to time.

8.2 **Restrictions.** Distributor shall not:

(a) alter, remove, obscure or add to any Halvorsen Mark, label, serial number, warning or instruction on or in any Product or its packaging;

(b) use any Halvorsen Mark in combination with any other trademark, trade name or logo, including Distributor's own, in a manner that suggests co-branding, private labeling or joint origin, without Supplier's prior written approval;

(c) register or attempt to register any Halvorsen Mark, or any mark, trade name or domain name confusingly similar to any Halvorsen Mark, in any jurisdiction;

(d) use any Halvorsen Mark in connection with any product other than the Products; or

(e) repackage, relabel or reverse engineer any Product.

8.3 **Approval of Materials.** Distributor shall submit to Supplier for prior written approval all advertising, promotional and training materials that use any Halvorsen Mark. Supplier shall approve or reject the materials within ten business days after receipt.

8.4 **Ownership.** All right, title and interest in the Halvorsen Marks and in all patents, copyrights, software, know-how and other intellectual property in the Products belong to Supplier. All goodwill arising from Distributor's use of the Halvorsen Marks inures to the benefit of Supplier.

8.5 **Infringement.** Distributor shall promptly notify Supplier of any suspected infringement of the Halvorsen Marks or Supplier's other intellectual property in the Territory. Supplier has the sole right to control any enforcement action, and Distributor shall cooperate at Supplier's expense.

8.6 **Cessation of Use.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, Distributor shall cease all use of the Halvorsen Marks, except as necessary to sell any Products that Supplier does not repurchase under Section 16.4.

## 9. REGULATORY MATTERS

9.1 **Regulatory Approvals.** Supplier shall obtain and maintain all clearances and approvals from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration required to market the Products in the United States.

9.2 **Complaints and Adverse Events.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any complaint, malfunction or adverse event relating to a Product within two business days after Distributor becomes aware of it, and of any event involving death or serious injury within 24 hours.

9.3 **Recalls.** Supplier shall decide whether to conduct any recall, field correction or market withdrawal of a Product. Distributor shall cooperate fully in any recall, including by using the records maintained under Section 6.3 to identify affected Customers. Supplier shall bear the reasonable, documented costs of any recall except to the extent caused by Distributor's breach of this Agreement or negligence.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product will conform to the specifications in Exhibit A and will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for 12 months after delivery to the Customer, but not more than 18 months after delivery to Distributor. Supplier's sole obligation for breach of this warranty is to repair or replace the non-conforming Product or, if repair or replacement is not commercially practicable, to refund the price paid.

10.2 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

10.3 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION 10, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim alleging (a) bodily injury or property damage caused by a defect in a Product, (b) that a Product or the Halvorsen Marks, used as permitted by this Agreement, infringe any third-party intellectual property right, or (c) Supplier's breach of this Agreement or violation of law.

11.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim arising from (a) Distributor's breach of Section 8 or any other provision of this Agreement, (b) any warranty or representation about the Products made by Distributor beyond those authorized by Supplier in writing, (c) Distributor's negligence or willful misconduct, including in installation or servicing of Products, or (d) Distributor's violation of law.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the claim, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Except for the matters listed in Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

12.2 **Cap.** Except for the matters listed in Section 12.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable under the Agreement in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 11;

(b) Distributor's breach of Section 8 (Trademark and IP Restrictions), including unauthorized use, alteration or co-branding of Halvorsen Marks; or

(c) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality).

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement and marked or reasonably understood to be confidential ("**Confidential Information**"), shall use it only to perform this Agreement, and shall disclose it only to its employees, advisers and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13.

13.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party, or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order after giving the other Party prompt notice, where lawful, and a reasonable opportunity to seek protective treatment.

13.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 13 continue during the Term and for five years afterward, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 Each Party shall maintain, during the Term and for three years afterward, commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate. Supplier shall also maintain products liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence. Each Party shall provide a certificate of insurance to the other on request.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

15.1 **Assignment by Distributor.** Distributor shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, in whole or in part, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without Supplier's prior written consent. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.1 is void.

15.2 **Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without Distributor's prior written consent, which Distributor shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay.

15.3 **Change of Control of Supplier.** Any change of control of Supplier, including any merger, consolidation or reorganization of Supplier or any sale or transfer, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of more than 50% of Supplier's outstanding voting stock, is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Supplier requiring Distributor's prior written consent under Section 15.2, which Distributor shall not unreasonably withhold. Supplier shall deliver a written request for that consent, identifying the acquiring person and the anticipated closing date, at least 30 days before closing of the change of control. If Supplier completes a change of control without having obtained Distributor's prior written consent, Distributor may terminate this Agreement by written notice to Supplier given within 90 days after Distributor learns of the closing, and Supplier shall repurchase Distributor's inventory of Products in accordance with Section 16.4.

15.4 **Successors.** Subject to this Section 15, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail, or within ten days in the case of failure to pay undisputed amounts.

16.2 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

16.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) all unpaid amounts owed to Supplier for delivered Products become due, (b) Supplier shall fulfill any Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination unless Supplier terminated under Section 16.1 or 16.2, and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

16.4 **Inventory Repurchase.** If this Agreement is terminated by Distributor under Section 15.3 or Section 16.1, or by Supplier for any reason other than under Section 16.1 or 16.2, Distributor may require Supplier, by written notice given within 30 days after the effective date of termination, to repurchase all Products then held in Distributor's inventory that are unused, in their original packaging and within their labeled shelf life, at Distributor's Landed Cost. Supplier shall pay the repurchase price within 30 days after receipt of the returned Products and shall bear the cost of return shipping.

16.5 **Survival.** Sections 6.3, 8.4, 8.6, 9.3, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16.3, 16.4, 16.5 and 18 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

17.1 **Escalation.** The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement by referring it to senior executives of each Party, who shall meet within 15 business days after either Party's written request.

17.2 **Equitable Relief.** Nothing in this Section 17 prevents either Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief at any time to protect its intellectual property or Confidential Information or to enforce Section 6.4 or Section 8.

## 18. GENERAL

18.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

18.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

18.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated in the preamble (in the case of Supplier, to the attention of the General Counsel, and in the case of Distributor, to the attention of the President), or to any other address a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

18.4 **Amendments and Waivers.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

18.5 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict, executed amendments prevail over the Agreement; the Agreement prevails over Exhibits and Purchase Orders.

18.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

18.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
