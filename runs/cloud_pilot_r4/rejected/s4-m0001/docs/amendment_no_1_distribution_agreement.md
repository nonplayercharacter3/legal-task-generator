# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 1, 2025 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Halvorsen Optics Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Lindstrom Parkway, Rochester, New York 14623 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Brightwater Medical Supply LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 88 Harborview Drive, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 15, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. The Parties have reviewed the Agreement following its first full contract year and wish to make certain conforming and administrative amendments to the Agreement.

C. Section 18.4 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Capitalized Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Additional Definition.** Section 1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Section 1.9 immediately after Section 1.8:

> 1.9 "**Permitted Transferee**" means any person that acquires all or substantially all of Distributor's business or assets relating to the Territory, whether by purchase of assets, purchase of equity, merger, consolidation or otherwise.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT BY DISTRIBUTOR

2.1 Section 15.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 15.1 **Assignment by Distributor.** Distributor may assign or otherwise transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, together with any of its rights and obligations under it, without Supplier's consent to any Permitted Transferee. Distributor shall give Supplier written notice of any such assignment within 30 days after it becomes effective, identifying the Permitted Transferee and its notice address. Except as permitted by this Section 15.1, Distributor shall not assign this Agreement without Supplier's prior written consent.

## 3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

3.1 Section 12.3(b) is deleted, and the remaining clauses shall be renumbered accordingly.

## 4. NOTICES

4.1 Section 18.3 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end of that Section:

> A copy of any notice to Distributor, which does not itself constitute notice, shall also be sent by email to legal@brightwatermedical.com.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "herein" or words of similar import means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

5.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
