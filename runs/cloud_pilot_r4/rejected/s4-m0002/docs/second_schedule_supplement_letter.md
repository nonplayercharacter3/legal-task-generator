## ANNEX A

### Second Schedule Supplement to Disclosure Schedule

**Section 4.10 (Litigation)**

1. On September 29, 2026, a former temporary staffing agency worker filed a complaint in the Court of Common Pleas against the Company. The complaint alleges personal injury from a forklift incident at the Company's machining facility on May 6, 2026. It seeks damages of $175,000. The Company has tendered the claim to its commercial general liability insurer. The insurer acknowledged the tender on October 7, 2026, subject to a customary reservation of rights. The Company maintains coverage of $5,000,000 per occurrence, subject to a $25,000 retention.

**Section 4.11 (Compliance with Laws; Permits)**

2. On September 22, 2026, a state environmental agency inspector conducted a routine inspection of the Company's machining facility and noted two recordkeeping deficiencies under the facility's air permit. These deficiencies concern the logging of coolant mist collector maintenance for the months of March and April 2026. The Company submitted the missing maintenance records and a revised logging procedure on October 2, 2026. As of the date of this Second Schedule Supplement, no notice of violation or penalty has been issued.

**Section 4.6 (Absence of Changes)**

3. On October 1, 2026, the Company's Plant Controller resigned to accept a position with an unaffiliated company. The Company's Assistant Controller has assumed the Plant Controller's duties on an interim basis. The Company has engaged a recruiting firm to identify a permanent replacement. No severance or retention payment was made or is owed in connection with the resignation.

**Section 4.7 (Material Contracts)**

4. On September 18, 2026, the Company renewed its existing one-year maintenance and calibration services agreement for its coordinate measuring machines on substantially the same terms as the prior agreement. The annual fee is $268,000, compared with $259,000 for the prior term. The renewal was entered into in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice.
