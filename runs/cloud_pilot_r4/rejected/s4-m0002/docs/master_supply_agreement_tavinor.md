# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between Tavinor Alloys Corp., a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Supplier**"), and Coldbrook Precision Components, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures specialty nickel-cobalt and precipitation-hardened stainless alloys in bar, billet and strip form, including alloys produced to proprietary melt specifications.

B. Buyer manufactures precision-machined components for automotive, industrial and aerospace customers, and requires a reliable supply of specialty alloy meeting the specifications set out in Schedule A.

C. Supplier has qualified its melt and processing routes for the Products with Buyer and with certain of Buyer's customers, and the Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will sell, and Buyer will purchase, the Products.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** As used in this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person, where "control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct the management and policies of a Person, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise.

"**Annual Forecast**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Contract Price**" means the price for each Product set out in Schedule B, as adjusted under Section 6.

"**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period beginning on February 1 during the Term.

"**Firm Period**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**List Price**" means Supplier's then-current published price list for the applicable alloy grade and form, as issued to its general customer base from time to time.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Products**" means the specialty alloy products described in Schedule A, each manufactured to the applicable Specifications.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Buyer under Section 3.

"**Specifications**" means the chemical, mechanical, dimensional and certification requirements for each Product set out in Schedule A, as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 7.4.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

## 2. SUPPLY RELATIONSHIP

**2.1 Supply.** During the Term, Supplier shall manufacture and sell to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Supplier, the Products ordered under Purchase Orders issued in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Requirements.** Buyer shall purchase from Supplier not less than eighty-five percent (85%) of Buyer's total annual requirements for alloy grades of the type described in Schedule A, measured by weight on a Contract Year basis. Buyer may purchase the balance of its requirements from other sources, provided that any such source has been qualified under Buyer's quality system.

**2.3 Capacity Reservation.** Supplier shall reserve melt and finishing capacity sufficient to supply one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the volume set out in the then-current Annual Forecast and shall give Buyer allocation priority no less favorable than that given to any other customer purchasing comparable volumes of the same alloy grades.

**2.4 Non-Exclusivity for Supplier.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Supplier from selling any alloy, including alloys of the same grade as the Products, to any other Person, except that Supplier shall not sell to any third party any Product manufactured to a Specification that incorporates Buyer's proprietary process parameters identified as such in Schedule A.

## 3. ORDERING

**3.1 Purchase Orders.** Buyer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier stating the Product, quantity, requested delivery date, delivery location and applicable Contract Price.

**3.2 Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days after receipt. Supplier shall not reject any Purchase Order that (a) is consistent with the Firm Period of the then-current forecast, (b) specifies a delivery date no earlier than the lead time set out in Schedule A, and (c) does not cause the aggregate quantity ordered in a calendar quarter to exceed one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the forecast quantity for that quarter. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

**3.3 Changes and Cancellation.** Buyer may reschedule or reduce any accepted Purchase Order without charge by written notice delivered at least forty-five (45) days before the scheduled delivery date. Changes requested within forty-five (45) days of the scheduled delivery date are subject to Supplier's reasonable consent, and Buyer shall reimburse Supplier for the cost of work-in-process and of raw material procured specifically for the affected order that cannot reasonably be used for other customers.

**3.4 Terms.** Each Purchase Order accepted by Supplier is governed by this Agreement. Additional or different terms proposed in any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or similar document are not binding on either Party unless signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

## 4. FORECASTS

**4.1 Annual Forecast.** Not later than November 1 of each year, Buyer shall deliver to Supplier a non-binding forecast of its anticipated requirements for each Product for the following Contract Year, broken down by calendar quarter (the "**Annual Forecast**").

**4.2 Rolling Forecast.** On or before the fifth Business Day of each calendar month, Buyer shall deliver to Supplier a rolling forecast of its requirements for the following six (6) months. The quantities forecast for the first ninety (90) days of each rolling forecast (the "**Firm Period**") are binding on Buyer, and Buyer shall issue Purchase Orders for those quantities.

**4.3 Raw Material Commitments.** Supplier may procure nickel, cobalt and other raw materials in reliance on the Firm Period. Buyer shall have no liability for raw material procured in anticipation of forecast quantities outside the Firm Period.

## 5. DELIVERY

**5.1 Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's mill at Washington, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020), unless the Parties agree otherwise in a Purchase Order. Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer upon delivery to Buyer's designated carrier.

**5.2 On-Time Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver each shipment no earlier than five (5) days before and no later than the scheduled delivery date. Supplier shall notify Buyer promptly upon becoming aware of any anticipated delay and shall state the cause and the revised delivery date.

**5.3 Late Delivery.** If Supplier fails to deliver any shipment within ten (10) days after the scheduled delivery date for reasons other than Force Majeure or Buyer's act or omission, Supplier shall, at Buyer's election, ship the delayed Products by expedited freight at Supplier's cost. If Supplier's on-time delivery performance falls below ninety percent (90%) for two consecutive calendar quarters, the Parties shall meet within fifteen (15) Business Days to agree a corrective action plan.

**5.4 Packaging and Documentation.** Supplier shall package, mark and label the Products in accordance with Schedule A and shall include with each shipment a packing list and a mill test certificate certifying conformity with the applicable Specifications and identifying the heat number of each lot.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Contract Price.** Buyer shall pay the Contract Price for each Product. The Contract Price consists of a base conversion price and a raw material surcharge calculated in accordance with Schedule B.

**6.2 Surcharge Adjustment.** The raw material surcharge shall be adjusted monthly by reference to the published indices identified in Schedule B. The base conversion price is fixed for the first Contract Year and thereafter may be adjusted once per Contract Year, effective February 1, by an amount not exceeding the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Steel Mill Products published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics for the preceding calendar year, by written notice from Supplier delivered at least sixty (60) days before the adjustment takes effect.

**6.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Buyer upon shipment. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Buyer may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith by written notice describing the dispute in reasonable detail, and the Parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 15.

**6.4 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the rate of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid.

**6.5 Taxes.** The Contract Price excludes sales, use and similar taxes, which Buyer shall pay or reimburse to the extent applicable, other than taxes on Supplier's income.

## 7. QUALITY

**7.1 Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 and, for Products designated in Schedule A as aerospace-grade, AS9100, and shall maintain such certifications throughout the Term.

**7.2 Inspection.** Buyer may inspect the Products within thirty (30) days after receipt and may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications by written notice to Supplier describing the nonconformity. Acceptance of Products does not waive Buyer's rights under Section 8 with respect to latent defects.

**7.3 Nonconforming Products.** At Buyer's election, Supplier shall promptly replace rejected Products or credit Buyer the Contract Price paid for them, and shall bear the cost of return freight. Supplier shall deliver a written root-cause analysis and corrective action report within twenty (20) Business Days after notice of any nonconformity affecting more than one heat.

**7.4 Changes to Specifications and Processes.** Supplier shall not change the melt practice, raw material source, processing route or production facility for any Product without at least one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice to Buyer and Buyer's written approval, which Buyer shall not unreasonably withhold where the change does not require requalification by Buyer's customers. Any amendment to the Specifications requires the written agreement of both Parties.

**7.5 Audit.** Upon ten (10) Business Days' notice, Buyer and, where required by Buyer's customer contracts, Buyer's customers may audit Supplier's facilities, records and quality processes relating to the Products during normal business hours, not more than twice per Contract Year except following a quality escape.

## 8. WARRANTIES

**8.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for a period of eighteen (18) months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the applicable Specifications and mill test certificate, (b) be free from defects in material and workmanship, and (c) be delivered free and clear of all liens and encumbrances.

**8.2 Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products will be manufactured in compliance with all applicable laws, including laws relating to conflict minerals, export control and restricted substances, and Supplier shall provide such supply chain declarations as Buyer reasonably requests.

**8.3 Remedies.** For any breach of Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at Buyer's election, repair, replace or refund the Contract Price of the affected Products and shall reimburse Buyer's reasonable, documented costs of removal, sorting and reinstallation incurred as a result of the breach, subject to Section 10.

**8.4 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 8, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), arising out of (a) any breach of Section 8 by Supplier, (b) personal injury or property damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier, or (c) any claim that the Products, as manufactured by Supplier, infringe any patent, trademark or trade secret of a third party, except to the extent the claim arises from a Specification furnished by Buyer.

**9.2 By Buyer.** Buyer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), arising out of (a) personal injury or property damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Buyer, or (b) any claim that a Specification furnished by Buyer infringes any patent, trademark or trade secret of a third party.

**9.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party other than the payment of money without its prior written consent.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion.** EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, BREACH OF SECTION 11, OR LIABILITY ARISING FROM GROSS NEGLIGENCE, FRAUD OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF THOSE DAMAGES.

**10.2 Cap.** EXCEPT FOR THE OBLIGATIONS AND LIABILITIES EXCLUDED IN SECTION 10.1, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN ANY CONTRACT YEAR SHALL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY BUYER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

**11.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public technical, commercial and financial information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including the Specifications, pricing and forecasts ("**Confidential Information**"), and shall use it only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement.

**11.2 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, (b) was known to the receiving Party before disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information, or (d) is received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful, and to its Affiliates, professional advisers, financing sources and bona fide prospective acquirers who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section 11.

**11.3 Survival.** The obligations in this Section 11 survive for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

## 12. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**12.1 Ownership.** Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Buyer owns all rights in the proprietary process parameters identified as Buyer's in Schedule A, and Supplier shall use them solely to manufacture Products for Buyer.

**12.2 License.** Buyer grants Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Buyer's Specifications solely to manufacture and supply the Products to Buyer.

## 13. TERM AND TERMINATION

**13.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five (5) years, and thereafter renews automatically for successive two (2) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least twelve (12) months before the end of the then-current term (the initial term and all renewal terms, the "**Term**").

**13.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.

**13.3 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files or has filed against it a petition in bankruptcy that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets.

**13.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, (a) Supplier shall complete delivery of all accepted Purchase Orders within the Firm Period unless Buyer instructs otherwise, (b) Buyer shall pay for all Products delivered and for raw material procured in reliance on the Firm Period under Section 4.3, and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information. Sections 6.3, 6.4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13.4, 14 and 15 survive expiration or termination.

**13.5 Transition Supply.** Upon expiration or any termination other than by Supplier under Section 13.2 or 13.3, Supplier shall, at Buyer's request, continue to supply the Products at the Contract Price for up to twelve (12) months to permit Buyer to qualify an alternative source.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

**14.1 General.** Neither Party may assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate that assumes all of the assigning Party's obligations in writing, provided the assigning Party remains liable for the Affiliate's performance. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void.

**14.2 Change of Control.** Any transaction or series of related transactions by which any Person or group of Persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting equity of Buyer, whether by sale, transfer or issuance of equity, merger, consolidation or otherwise, shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Buyer and shall require the prior written consent of Supplier. If any such transaction is consummated without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may, at its election and by written notice to Buyer delivered at any time within one hundred twenty (120) days after Supplier receives written notice of the consummation of the transaction, either (a) terminate this Agreement effective thirty (30) days after delivery of that notice, or (b) replace the Contract Price for all Products ordered after delivery of that notice with the List Price, in which case Sections 6.1 and 6.2 shall cease to apply to those Products and Section 13.5 shall not apply.

**14.3 Successors.** Subject to this Section 14, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**15.1 Escalation.** The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement through negotiation between their respective vice presidents responsible for supply chain, and, failing resolution within twenty (20) Business Days after written notice of the dispute, between their respective chief executive officers for a further twenty (20) Business Days.

**15.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

**15.3 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement that is not resolved under Section 15.1. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**15.4 Continued Performance.** Pending resolution of any dispute, each Party shall continue to perform its obligations under this Agreement, including Supplier's obligation to deliver Products under accepted Purchase Orders and Buyer's obligation to pay undisputed amounts.

## 16. GENERAL

**16.1 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees, or failure of public utilities ("**Force Majeure**"), provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During any Force Majeure affecting Supplier, Supplier shall allocate its available capacity among its customers on a basis no less favorable to Buyer than pro rata to purchases over the preceding twelve (12) months, and Buyer may purchase substitute Products from other sources without reduction of its obligations under Section 2.2 for the affected period. If a Force Majeure continues for more than ninety (90) days, the Party not affected may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

**16.2 Insurance.** Each Party shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability insurance (including products and completed operations coverage) with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, and shall provide a certificate of insurance on request.

**16.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if followed by a copy delivered under clause (a) or (b), in each case to the addresses set out on the signature page or such other address as a Party designates by notice.

**16.4 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship between them.

**16.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party so waiving, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**16.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

**16.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature are deemed original signatures.

## SCHEDULE B

### Pricing

1. **Base Conversion Price.** The base conversion price for each Product for the first Contract Year is set out in the pricing table attached as Exhibit B-1, stated in U.S. dollars per pound, FCA Supplier's mill.

3. **Surcharge Notice.** Supplier shall notify Buyer of the applicable surcharge for each calendar month no later than the last Business Day of the preceding month, together with reasonable supporting calculations.
