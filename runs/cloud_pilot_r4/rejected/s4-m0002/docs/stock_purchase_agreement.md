# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 3, 2026, by and among Meridale Capital Partners II, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), Harrowgate Industrial Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and, solely for purposes of Article IV and Article V, Coldbrook Precision Components, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement, as supplemented from time to time under Section 5.7.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Northbank Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Indemnity Escrow Agreement to be entered into at the Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any interest or earnings on it.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.13 and Sections 6.1 and 6.2.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court or other body exercising governmental, regulatory or judicial authority.

"**Lien**" means any mortgage, pledge, lien, charge, security interest or other encumbrance.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that, individually or in the aggregate, is or would reasonably be expected to be materially adverse to the business, results of operations or financial condition of the Company, other than effects arising from (a) general economic or industry conditions, (b) changes in Law or GAAP, or (c) the announcement of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except, in the case of clauses (a) and (b), to the extent the Company is disproportionately affected.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Purchase and Sale.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for the Purchase Price.

**2.3 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) on behalf of the Company, the amount specified in the Payoff Letter to the Lender;

(b) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent under Section 2.4; and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, plus or minus the estimated adjustment under Section 2.5(a), less the amounts paid under Sections 2.3(a) and 2.3(b).

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account and disbursed under the terms of this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Funds shall serve as a source of recovery for amounts owed to the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX.

**2.5 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital, and the Purchase Price payable at Closing shall be increased or decreased dollar-for-dollar by the amount by which that estimate exceeds or falls short of $6,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the final Closing Working Capital. Seller shall have 30 days to dispute that statement. The parties shall negotiate any dispute in good faith for 20 days, after which unresolved items shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five Business Days after the final determination.

(c) "**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company (excluding cash and income tax assets) less the current liabilities of the Company (excluding Indebtedness and income tax liabilities), determined as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day before the Closing Date in accordance with GAAP applied consistently with the Company's audited financial statements for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent are entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable tax Law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as Buyer and Seller agree in writing.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Payoff Letter and evidence of the release of all Liens securing the Indebtedness;

(d) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(e); and

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 from Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Section 2.3, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction in which such qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding, all of which are the Shares and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each of the following contracts to which the Company is a party (the "**Material Contracts**"): (a) each contract involving aggregate payments to or by the Company in excess of $250,000 per year; (b) each contract with a Material Customer or Material Supplier; (c) each contract relating to Indebtedness; (d) each lease of real property; and (e) each contract that limits the Company's ability to compete in any line of business or geographic area. **4.8 Customers and Suppliers.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the ten largest customers of the Company by revenue (the "**Material Customers**") and the ten largest suppliers of the Company by spend (the "**Material Suppliers**") for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026. **4.10 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or against Seller relating to the Shares or the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, and holds all material permits required for the conduct of its business.

**4.12 Taxes.** All income and other material tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true, complete and correct in all material respects, and all taxes shown as due on them have been paid.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer (which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed), Seller shall cause the Company to conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization and its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees. Without limiting the foregoing, the Company shall not (a) amend its organizational documents, (b) issue or sell any equity interests, (c) incur Indebtedness other than borrowings under the Loan Agreement in the ordinary course, (d) enter into, materially amend or terminate any Material Contract, or (e) declare or pay any dividend other than cash dividends paid before the Closing.

**5.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**5.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the conditions in Article VII to be satisfied as promptly as practicable. Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent and deliver each notice set forth on Annex 7.2(d).

**5.4 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, initiate, knowingly encourage or engage in discussions regarding any proposal for the acquisition of the Company or its assets, other than in the ordinary course.

**5.5 Notice of Certain Events.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that has had or would reasonably be expected to have a Material Adverse Effect or that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**5.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company, except to the extent required by Law.

**5.7 Schedule Supplements.**

(a) From time to time before the Closing, Seller may supplement or amend the Disclosure Schedule with respect to any matter first arising, or of which Seller first becomes aware, after the date of this Agreement (each, a "**Schedule Supplement**"), by written notice to Buyer.

(b) If any Schedule Supplement discloses a matter that, individually or together with other matters, would result in the failure of the condition in Section 7.2(a), Buyer may terminate this Agreement under Section 8.1(e) by written notice to Seller delivered within 10 Business Days after Buyer's receipt of a Schedule Supplement.

(c) If Buyer does not terminate this Agreement within the period set forth in Section 5.7(b), Buyer shall be deemed to have irrevocably waived its right to terminate this Agreement with respect to the matters disclosed in that Schedule Supplement, and that Schedule Supplement shall be deemed to have cured any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty arising from the matters disclosed in it for purposes of Article IX and for purposes of determining whether the condition in Section 7.2(a) has been satisfied.

**5.8 Payoff of Indebtedness.** Seller shall cause the Company to obtain and deliver to Buyer at least three Business Days before the Closing the Payoff Letter, together with customary Lien release documentation, providing for the release of all Liens securing the Indebtedness upon payment of the amount specified in it.

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of any Governmental Authority that restrains, enjoins or prohibits the consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction (or waiver by Buyer) of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller and the Company in Article IV (other than the Fundamental Representations) shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date as though made on that date (except those made as of a specified date, which shall be true and correct in all material respects as of that date), and the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller and the Company shall have performed and complied in all material respects with all covenants required by this Agreement to be performed or complied with by them before or at the Closing;

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by the mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer or Seller, by written notice to the other, if the Closing has not occurred on or before November 30, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**"); provided that the right to terminate under this Section 8.1(b) is not available to a party whose breach of this Agreement was the principal cause of the failure of the Closing to occur by the Outside Date;

(c) by Buyer, by written notice to Seller, if Seller or the Company has breached any representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement such that a condition in Section 7.2(a) or 7.2(b) would not be satisfied, and that breach has not been cured within 20 days after written notice from Buyer (or, if earlier, by the Outside Date);

(d) by Seller, by written notice to Buyer, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement such that a condition in Section 7.3(a) or 7.3(b) would not be satisfied, and that breach has not been cured within 20 days after written notice from Seller (or, if earlier, by the Outside Date); or

(e) by Buyer, in accordance with Section 5.7(b).

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any party, except that (a) this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive termination, and (b) no termination shall relieve any party from liability for any willful breach of this Agreement before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.12 shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants in this Agreement shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred or sustained by them arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller or the Company in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller or the Company in this Agreement;

(c) any Indebtedness outstanding as of the Closing not paid at or before the Closing; and

(d) any taxes of the Company attributable to periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, partners, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred or sustained by them arising out of or resulting from (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to the Fundamental Representations and Section 4.12) unless and until the aggregate Losses in respect of such claims exceed $485,000 (the "**Deductible**"), in which event Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(b) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to the Fundamental Representations and Section 4.12) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(c) The aggregate liability of Seller under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price, and the aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(d) The limitations in this Section 9.4 shall not apply to Losses arising from fraud.

(e) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualification in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.**

(a) An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for indemnification, describing the claim in reasonable detail and including a good-faith estimate of the Losses, to the extent known. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure.

(b) The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably satisfactory to the indemnified party by written notice within 20 days after receipt of notice of the claim. The indemnified party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.6 Manner of Payment.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied (a) first, from the Escrow Funds, by joint written instruction of Buyer and Seller or final non-appealable order delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, and (b) thereafter, to the extent the Escrow Funds are insufficient or have been released and subject to Section 9.4, directly by Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds within ten Business Days after final determination. Upon the expiration of the escrow period under the Escrow Agreement, the remaining Escrow Funds, less the amount of any pending claims, shall be released to Seller in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement, except for claims arising from fraud, claims for specific performance under Section 11.10, and claims under Section 2.5.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all income tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are filed after the Closing Date, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review and comment at least 30 days before filing.

**10.2 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of tax returns and any tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

**10.3 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and similar taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.
