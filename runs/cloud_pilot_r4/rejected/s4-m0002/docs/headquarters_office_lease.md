# OFFICE LEASE

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of April 1, 2022 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between 1180 Elm Ridge Associates LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and Coldbrook Precision Components, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

**1.1 Basic Terms.** The following terms are used in this Lease with the meanings given below:

(a) **Building:** the four-story office building known as 1180 Elm Ridge Road, together with its parking areas, driveways, walkways and landscaped areas (the "**Property**").

(b) **Premises:** Suite 300 on the third floor of the Building, containing approximately 14,250 rentable square feet, as shown on Exhibit A.

(c) **Commencement Date:** June 1, 2022.

(d) **Expiration Date:** May 31, 2032, subject to extension under Section 3.2.

(e) **Term:** the period beginning on the Commencement Date and ending on the Expiration Date, as it may be extended under Section 3.2.

(f) **Base Rent:** as set out in Section 4.1.

(g) **Tenant's Share:** 23.75%, being the rentable area of the Premises divided by the 60,000 rentable square feet of the Building.

(h) **Security Deposit:** $72,000.

(i) **Permitted Use:** general office use, including executive, administrative, engineering, sales and related purposes, and no other use.

(j) **Parking:** 48 unreserved spaces and 4 reserved spaces in the Building's surface parking lot.

**1.2 Exhibits.** Exhibit A (Premises) and Exhibit B (Rules and Regulations) form part of this Lease.

## 2. PREMISES

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease, together with the non-exclusive right to use the lobbies, corridors, elevators, restrooms, loading areas and other common areas of the Property (the "**Common Areas**") in common with other tenants.

**2.2 Condition.** Tenant accepted the Premises on the Commencement Date in their then-current condition, with the improvements completed by Landlord under the work letter dated April 1, 2022. Landlord warranted that the Building's structural elements and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing and life-safety systems were in good working order on the Commencement Date.

**2.3 Measurement.** The rentable area of the Premises and the Building stated in Section 1.1 is stipulated for all purposes of this Lease and is not subject to remeasurement.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier under this Lease.

**3.2 Extension Option.** Tenant has one option to extend the Term for a single period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), exercisable by written notice to Landlord delivered not earlier than fifteen (15) months and not later than nine (9) months before the Expiration Date, provided that no Event of Default is continuing when the notice is delivered. Base Rent for the first year of the Extension Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent, increasing by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the start of the Extension Term. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay, and a willing landlord would accept, for comparable office space in comparable buildings in the same submarket, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the submarket, and the two brokers shall appoint a third; Fair Market Rent shall be the average of the two determinations closest to each other.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**5.1 Payment.** For each calendar year during the Term after calendar year 2022 (the "**Base Year**"), Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

**5.2 Definitions.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs actually incurred by Landlord to operate, maintain, repair and insure the Property in a manner consistent with comparable office buildings in the submarket, excluding capital expenditures (other than those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first enacted after the Commencement Date, amortized over their useful lives), leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, debt service, depreciation, Landlord's general corporate overhead, and costs reimbursed by insurance or by other tenants. "**Taxes**" means all real estate taxes and assessments levied against the Property, excluding income, franchise, estate and transfer taxes of Landlord.

**5.3 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall deliver to Tenant before each calendar year a reasonable estimate of Tenant's payment under Section 5.1, payable monthly with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due (or refunded if the Term has ended), and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days after delivery of the statement.

**5.4 Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses other than Taxes, insurance premiums, utilities and snow removal shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis over the Base Year amount.

**5.5 Audit.** Tenant may, by notice within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving an annual statement, inspect Landlord's records for that year. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Operating Expenses by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs in addition to refunding the overpayment.

## 6. USE AND COMPLIANCE

**6.1 Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and in compliance with all applicable laws and the Rules and Regulations attached as Exhibit B, as they may be reasonably modified by Landlord on notice and applied in a non-discriminatory manner.

**6.2 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring onto the Property any hazardous materials other than customary office and cleaning supplies in quantities normal for office use and in compliance with applicable law. Landlord represents that, to Landlord's knowledge, there were no hazardous materials in the Premises or the Building on the Effective Date in violation of applicable law, and Landlord shall remain responsible for any hazardous materials not introduced by Tenant or its agents.

**6.3 Compliance with Laws.** Landlord shall, as part of Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 5.2, cause the Common Areas and the Building structure and systems to comply with applicable laws, including the Americans with Disabilities Act. Tenant shall comply with laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**7.1 Services.** Landlord shall furnish heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding holidays; electricity sufficient for normal office use; passenger elevator service at all times; janitorial service five nights per week in accordance with Landlord's standard specifications; and water for restroom and pantry use. After-hours HVAC shall be provided on request at Landlord's actual cost, currently $55 per hour per zone.

**7.2 Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is separately metered and Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly.

**7.3 Interruption.** If any service that Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than five (5) consecutive Business Days for a reason within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable, Rent shall abate in proportion to the untenantable area from the sixth Business Day until the service is restored. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a legal holiday on which national banks in the State in which the Property is located are closed.

## 8. MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS AND ALTERATIONS

**8.1 Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the structure, roof, foundation, exterior walls, Common Areas and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems in good condition and repair, consistent with comparable office buildings in the submarket.

**8.2 Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall repair any damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or invitees.

**8.3 Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed, except that Tenant may make cosmetic alterations (such as painting, carpeting and furniture installation) costing less than $50,000 in the aggregate in any twelve-month period, and that do not affect the Building structure or systems, on ten (10) days' prior notice to Landlord. At the time Landlord consents to any alteration, Landlord shall notify Tenant whether that alteration must be removed at the end of the Term; if Landlord does not so notify Tenant, removal is not required.

**8.4 Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant and shall cause any such lien to be released or bonded within thirty (30) days after notice of its filing.

## 9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**9.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds, (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and alterations at full replacement cost, and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord on request.

**9.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance covering the Building at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings, the premiums for which are included in Operating Expenses.

**9.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance, and each party shall cause its property insurance policies to permit that waiver.

**9.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Premises or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, and Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Common Areas or from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents. These obligations survive the expiration or termination of this Lease.

## 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**10.1 Casualty.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, deliver to Tenant a contractor's estimate of the time required to restore them. If the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days from the date of the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by notice within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate. Otherwise, Landlord shall restore the Premises and the Building with reasonable diligence. Rent shall abate in proportion to the portion of the Premises rendered untenantable until restoration is substantially complete.

**10.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the parking areas as renders the remainder unsuitable for the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving expenses and personal property, provided it does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**11.1 Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises (each, a "**Transfer**") without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the request and reasonable information about the proposed transferee.

**11.2 Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from a transferee under a Transfer requiring Landlord's consent in excess of the Rent allocable to the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the Transfer.

**11.3 Permitted Transfers; Ownership Changes.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent, assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to (a) any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with Tenant, or (b) any entity that succeeds to all or substantially all of Tenant's business or assets by merger, consolidation or purchase, provided in each case that Tenant gives Landlord written notice within thirty (30) days after the Transfer and, in the case of an assignment, the assignee assumes Tenant's obligations under this Lease in writing. A sale, issuance or other transfer of the stock or other equity interests of Tenant, or of any direct or indirect owner of Tenant, is not a Transfer, and requires neither the consent of nor notice to Landlord.

**11.4 No Release.** No Transfer releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

## 12. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**12.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five (5) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding, or of an involuntary proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

**12.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may, by written notice to Tenant, terminate this Lease or terminate Tenant's right of possession, and may recover from Tenant the Rent due through the date of termination, plus the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for the same period, each discounted to present value at the rate of six percent (6%) per year, together with Landlord's reasonable costs of reletting. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**12.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or such longer period as is reasonably required if Landlord begins the cure within that period and diligently pursues it), Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

## 13. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**13.1 Deposit.** Tenant has deposited the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for the performance of its obligations under this Lease. Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may apply all or part of the Security Deposit to amounts owed by Tenant, and Tenant shall restore the Security Deposit within ten (10) Business Days after notice of that application. Landlord shall return the unapplied Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the expiration or termination of this Lease and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

**13.2 Transfer by Landlord.** Upon a sale of the Building, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the purchaser and shall then be released from liability for its return.

## 14. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL

**14.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Property, provided that the holder of that mortgage delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which Tenant's possession and rights under this Lease will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default is continuing.

**14.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the other's request, deliver a certificate confirming, to the extent true, that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to the certifying party's knowledge, any default exists. Neither party may make more than two such requests in any twelve-month period except in connection with a sale or financing.

## 15. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

**15.1 Surrender.** On the expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and alterations not required to be removed under Section 8.3 excepted, and shall remove its personal property.

**15.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration or termination of this Lease without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be a tenant at sufferance and shall pay, for each month or part of a month of holdover, one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the monthly Base Rent in effect immediately before the holdover, plus Additional Rent. Tenant shall be liable for consequential damages arising from a holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord notifies Tenant that Landlord has entered into a lease with a successor tenant for the Premises.

## 16. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**16.1 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or any person claiming through Landlord.

**16.2 Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice (except in an emergency) during normal business hours to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective purchasers, lenders or, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, prospective tenants, and shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business.

**16.3 Limitation of Landlord's Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Property, including rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds.

**16.4 Brokers.** Each party represents that it dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Elm Ridge Realty Advisors, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement, and each party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through it.

**16.5 Notices.** All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) three Business Days after mailing by certified mail, return receipt requested, addressed to Landlord at the address designated by Landlord for the payment of Rent and to Tenant at the Premises, Attention: Chief Executive Officer, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**16.6 Governing Law.** This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State in which the Property is located.

**16.7 Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and Landlord's acceptance of Rent does not waive any default.

**16.8 Successors.** Subject to Section 11, this Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**16.9 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**16.10 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature are deemed original signatures.
