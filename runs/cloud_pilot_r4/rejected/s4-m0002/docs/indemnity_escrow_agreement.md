# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026, by and among Harrowgate Industrial Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), Meridale Capital Partners II, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), and Northbank Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Coldbrook Precision Components, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,850,000 with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, to be held and disbursed as a source of recovery for amounts owed to the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent and to hold and disburse the Escrow Funds on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** As used in this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Buyer Indemnitees**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less all amounts disbursed from the Escrow Account in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Escrow Period**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a written certification from counsel for the party presenting it that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $4,850,000.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 9.

"**Losses**" means actual out-of-pocket losses, liabilities, damages, judgments, settlements, fines and penalties paid or incurred by a Buyer Indemnitee, excluding diminution in value, lost profits, consequential damages, and attorneys' fees and other costs of investigation, defense or enforcement.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before that date that have not been resolved and paid, or withdrawn, in accordance with Section 4.

"**Authorized Representative**" means, for each of Buyer and Seller, an individual designated on Schedule 1, as that Schedule may be updated by the applicable party by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement, including in the recitals, have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the Escrow Agent on Schedule 2.

**2.2 Appointment and Acceptance.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller, and shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Indemnity Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the Business Day it is received.

**2.3 No Interest of Parties Before Disbursement.** The Escrow Funds shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party and shall be held and disbursed solely for the purposes of, and in accordance with, this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Northbank Government Obligations Money Market Fund (Institutional Class), or such other investment as Buyer and Seller direct by Joint Written Instruction. The Escrow Agent shall have no liability for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3.1, or for any failure to achieve any particular rate of return.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as necessary to make any disbursement required under this Agreement and shall not be liable for any loss, penalty or reduction in return resulting from that liquidation.

**3.3 Earnings and Tax Reporting.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds shall be added to and form part of the Escrow Funds. For tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until disbursed, and all earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall deliver to Buyer a quarterly distribution of earnings sufficient to pay the income tax attributable to those earnings, calculated at an assumed rate of 25%, within ten Business Days after the end of each calendar quarter.

## 4. DISBURSEMENT

**4.1 Escrow Period; Release.** The Escrow Funds shall be held during the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending on the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Period**"). Within five Business Days after the expiration of the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the expiration of the Escrow Period. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold an amount equal to the Pending Claim Amount until each unresolved claim is resolved in accordance with Section 4.2, and shall disburse any portion of that amount that is no longer subject to a pending claim to Seller within five Business Days after that claim is resolved.

**4.2 Claims Procedure.**

(a) At any time before the expiration of the Escrow Period, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has incurred or reasonably expects to incur Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, describing the claim in reasonable detail and stating the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of that amount, to the extent known.

(b) Seller may object to all or any part of a claim by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"), stating the basis of the objection in reasonable detail and the amount in dispute.

(c) If Seller does not deliver an objection within the Objection Period, the Escrow Agent shall, on the first Business Day after the end of the Objection Period, disburse to Buyer the amount set forth in the Claim Notice, subject to Section 4.3.

(d) If Seller delivers a timely objection to a portion of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, subject to Section 4.3, the portion of the claim to which Seller has not objected, and shall continue to hold the disputed amount until it receives either a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order directing the disbursement of that amount.

(e) Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days after delivery of any objection to resolve the dispute. Following resolution, Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver a Joint Written Instruction to the Escrow Agent reflecting that resolution. If the dispute is not resolved within that period, either party may seek resolution in accordance with Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.3 Amounts Disbursable to Buyer.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Funds only amounts constituting Losses, and each Claim Notice, Joint Written Instruction and Final Order directing a disbursement to Buyer shall state the amount of Losses to be disbursed. The Escrow Agent shall disburse amounts to Buyer under this Section 4 only upon (a) the expiration of an Objection Period under Section 4.2(c), (b) receipt of a Joint Written Instruction, or (c) receipt of a Final Order, and in each case only to the extent of the Escrow Funds then available.

**4.4 Disbursement Upon Joint Written Instruction.** At any time, the Escrow Agent shall disburse all or any portion of the Escrow Funds in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction within two Business Days after receipt.

**4.5 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the recipient on Schedule 2, or to such other account as the recipient designates by written notice to the Escrow Agent, subject to Section 5.6.

**4.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay any amount required to be disbursed, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available and shall have no further obligation with respect to the shortfall. Disbursement of the full Escrow Funds does not limit any obligation of Seller under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. THE ESCROW AGENT

**5.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature, and no implied duties. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and has no duties or obligations under, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller, and is not required to determine whether any party has complied with the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on any written notice, instruction, certificate or order believed by it in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper person, and shall not be required to inquire into the truth or accuracy of any statement in it.

**5.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent of its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for any special, indirect or consequential damages.

**5.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify the Escrow Agent and its officers, directors, employees and agents against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, other than those resulting from the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Escrow Agent. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 5.4, except that any amount arising from the breach of this Agreement by a party shall be borne by that party. This Section 5.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**5.5 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice and shall be fully protected in any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**5.6 Security Procedures.** Before effecting any wire transfer to an account other than one listed on Schedule 2, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the party directing the transfer, other than the individual who delivered the instruction, at the telephone number listed on Schedule 1.

**5.7 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands with respect to the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order, or may interplead the Escrow Funds with a court of competent jurisdiction in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, and upon doing so shall be released from all further obligation with respect to the interpleaded funds.

**5.8 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign upon 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent upon 30 days' written notice. A resignation or removal is effective only upon the appointment of a successor escrow agent, which shall be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000, designated by Joint Written Instruction, and the delivery of the Escrow Funds to that successor. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one.

## 6. FEES AND EXPENSES

**6.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3 and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement. Those fees and expenses shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller and shall not be paid from the Escrow Funds.

## 7. TERMINATION

**7.1 Termination.** This Agreement shall terminate upon the disbursement of all Escrow Funds in accordance with its terms, except that Sections 5.3, 5.4 and 6.1 survive termination.

## 8. KNOW-YOUR-CUSTOMER

**8.1 Identification.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering, sanctions and customer identification laws, and acknowledges that the Escrow Agent may not open the Escrow Account until that information has been provided.

## 9. NOTICES

**9.1 Notices.** All notices, instructions and other communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when sent by email in PDF format with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day, and otherwise on the next Business Day, or (c) one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case addressed as follows, or to such other address as a party designates by notice:

If to Buyer: Harrowgate Industrial Holdings LLC, Attention: General Counsel, at the address set forth on Schedule 1.

If to Seller: Meridale Capital Partners II, L.P., c/o Meridale Capital GP II, LLC, Attention: Managing Partner, at the address set forth on Schedule 1.

If to the Escrow Agent: Northbank Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration, at the address set forth on Schedule 1.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver of any provision is effective unless in writing and signed by the party so waiving.

**10.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that (a) Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any Person to which it has assigned its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement, and (b) any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which succeeds to all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall be the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**10.5 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining terms remain in full force and effect.

**10.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature are deemed original signatures.
