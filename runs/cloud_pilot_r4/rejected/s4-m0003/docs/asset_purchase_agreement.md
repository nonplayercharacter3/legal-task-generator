# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026, by and between **Harrowgate Climate Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Tellmar Controls LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the Business (as defined below).

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings specified below:

"**Accounting Principles**" means GAAP, applied using the same accounting methods, policies, practices, principles, procedures, classifications, judgments and estimation methodologies used in preparing the Balance Sheet, as further described in Schedule 1.1(a).

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with, such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Intellectual Property Assignment, the Transition Services Agreement and each other agreement, instrument or certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3(c).

"**Balance Sheet**" means the unaudited balance sheet of the Business as of November 30, 2025, included in the Financial Statements.

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of commercial and residential building climate control systems, as conducted by Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Net Working Capital**" means Net Working Capital as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

"**Closing Statement**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.4(a).

"**Disclosure Schedules**" means the disclosure schedules delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, easement, encroachment or other similar encumbrance, other than Permitted Encumbrances.

"**Excluded Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3(b).

"**Excluded Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3(d).

"**Financial Statements**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.4.

"**GAAP**" means United States generally accepted accounting principles in effect from time to time, consistently applied.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision thereof, or any agency, court or tribunal of such government or political subdivision.

"**Independent Accountant**" means an impartial nationally recognized firm of independent certified public accountants, other than the regular accountants of Seller or Buyer, mutually appointed by the Parties.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs or expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Net Working Capital**" means (a) the current assets of the Business included in the Purchased Assets, consisting solely of the line items specified on Schedule 1.1(b), less (b) the current liabilities of the Business included in the Assumed Liabilities, consisting solely of the line items specified on Schedule 1.1(b), in each case determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

"**Notice of Disagreement**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.4(b).

"**Permitted Encumbrances**" means (a) liens for Taxes not yet due and payable, (b) mechanics', carriers', workmen's and similar liens arising in the ordinary course of business for amounts not yet delinquent, and (c) the matters listed on Schedule 1.1(c).

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.2.

"**Target Net Working Capital**" means $6,200,000.

"**Tax**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, transfer, property, payroll, employment, withholding, excise and other taxes, together with any interest, additions or penalties with respect thereto.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement between Seller, as provider, and Buyer, as recipient, dated as of the Closing Date.

**1.2 Other Defined Terms.** Other capitalized terms are defined where they first appear in this Agreement.

**1.3 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires: (a) "days" means calendar days; (b) "including" means "including without limitation"; (c) references to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement; (d) words in the singular include the plural and vice versa; (e) "hereof," "herein" and "hereunder" refer to this Agreement as a whole; and (f) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. The Disclosure Schedules and Exhibits form part of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.4 (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**"). At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller the Base Purchase Price, plus or minus the amount by which the Estimated Closing Net Working Capital exceeds or is less than the Target Net Working Capital, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three Business Days before the Closing Date. "**Estimated Closing Net Working Capital**" means Seller's good faith estimate of Closing Net Working Capital, delivered to Buyer in a certificate at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.2 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all of the assets, properties and rights of every kind, wherever located, that are used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(b) all inventory, raw materials, work in progress, finished goods, spare parts and supplies;

(c) all contracts listed on Section 2.2(c) of the Disclosure Schedules (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all intellectual property owned by Seller and used or held for use in the Business, including the product designs, firmware, software and trademarks listed on Section 2.2(d) of the Disclosure Schedules;

(e) all tangible personal property, including machinery, equipment, tooling, vehicles, furniture and computer hardware;

(f) all permits held by Seller that are required for the conduct of the Business, to the extent transferable;

(g) all prepaid expenses, credits, advance payments, deposits and rights to refunds relating to the Business;

(h) all warranty rights against third parties relating to the Purchased Assets; and

(i) originals or copies of all books and records of the Business, including customer lists, supplier lists, service records, product engineering files and quality control records.

**2.3 Excluded Assets; Assumed and Excluded Liabilities.**

(a) *General.* Notwithstanding Section 2.2, the Purchased Assets do not include the Excluded Assets.

(b) *Excluded Assets.* "**Excluded Assets**" means (i) cash and cash equivalents; (ii) contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (iii) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and Tax returns; (iv) insurance policies of Seller and rights thereunder; (v) all employee benefit plans and assets attributable thereto; (vi) the assets listed on Section 2.3(b) of the Disclosure Schedules; and (vii) the rights that accrue to Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

(c) *Assumed Liabilities.* At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (i) liabilities arising under the Assigned Contracts after the Closing, excluding any liability arising from a breach or default by Seller occurring at or before the Closing; (ii) trade accounts payable of Seller to third parties in connection with the Business that remain unpaid at the Closing, to the extent included in Closing Net Working Capital; and (iii) product warranty obligations for products of the Business sold before the Closing, up to the amount of the warranty reserve included in Closing Net Working Capital (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

(d) *Excluded Liabilities.* Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and pay when due, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including all liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for pre-Closing periods, all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets, and all liabilities relating to employees of Seller arising at or before the Closing.

**2.4 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within 30 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good faith calculation of Closing Net Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting documentation, prepared in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital in the Closing Statement, Buyer may, within 30 days after Buyer's receipt of the Closing Statement (the "**Review Period**"), deliver to Seller a written notice of disagreement (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis therefor, together with reasonable supporting documentation. During the Review Period, Seller shall give Buyer and its accountants reasonable access to the books, records and working papers used in preparing the Closing Statement. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement before the end of the Review Period, the Closing Statement, including Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital, shall be final and binding on the Parties. Any item not disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement shall be final and binding on the Parties.

(c) *Resolution of Disputes.* If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed items within 30 days after Seller's receipt of the Notice of Disagreement. Any disputed items so resolved in writing shall be final and binding. Any disputed items not so resolved shall be submitted to the Independent Accountant, which shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator, and which shall resolve only the remaining disputed items, within the range of values assigned to each such item by the Parties in the Closing Statement and the Notice of Disagreement, respectively. The Independent Accountant shall deliver its written determination within 30 days after its engagement, and that determination shall be final and binding on the Parties absent manifest error. The fees and expenses of the Independent Accountant shall be borne by Seller and Buyer in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed items, as determined by the Independent Accountant.

(d) *Post-Closing Adjustment.* The "**Post-Closing Adjustment**" is the amount (which may be positive or negative) equal to final Closing Net Working Capital less Estimated Closing Net Working Capital. If the Post-Closing Adjustment is positive, Buyer shall pay that amount to Seller. If the Post-Closing Adjustment is negative, Seller shall pay the absolute value of that amount to Buyer.

(e) *Payment.* Any payment under Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five Business Days after Closing Net Working Capital becomes final and binding, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, and shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**2.5 Allocation of Purchase Price.** Within 90 days after Closing Net Working Capital becomes final and binding, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a proposed allocation of the Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. Seller may object within 30 days after receipt, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any objection. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with the allocation as finally agreed.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as Buyer is required to deduct and withhold under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on February 2, 2026, or at such other time, date or place as the Parties may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing actually occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, transferring the tangible personal property included in the Purchased Assets; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) the Intellectual Property Assignment, duly executed by Seller; (d) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (e) the third-party consents listed on Section 3.2(e) of the Disclosure Schedules; (f) payoff letters and lien releases for all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Encumbrances; (g) a certificate of the managing member of Seller certifying the resolutions authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby; and (h) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the payment described in Section 2.1; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (d) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedules, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that the statements in this Article IV are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full limited liability company power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to carry out its obligations hereunder and thereunder. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not (a) conflict with Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Business, (c) except as set forth in Section 4.2 of the Disclosure Schedules, require the consent of or notice to any Person under any Assigned Contract, or (d) result in any Encumbrance on the Purchased Assets.

**4.3 Title to and Sufficiency of Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances. The Purchased Assets, together with the services to be provided under the Transition Services Agreement, constitute all of the assets, properties and rights necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Complete copies of the unaudited financial statements of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2023 and December 31, 2024 and for the eleven-month period ended November 30, 2025 (the "**Financial Statements**") have been delivered to Buyer. The Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition of the Business as of the respective dates thereof and the results of operations for the periods indicated.

**4.5 Absence of Changes.** Since the date of the Balance Sheet, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, and there has not been any event, occurrence or development that has had, or would reasonably be expected to have, a material adverse effect on the Business.

**4.6 Material Contracts.** Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedules lists each contract of the Business involving annual payments in excess of $250,000 and each contract with any of the twenty largest customers or suppliers of the Business. Each such contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, the other party, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach thereof.

**4.7 Intellectual Property.** Section 2.2(d) of the Disclosure Schedules lists all registered intellectual property and material unregistered intellectual property used in the Business. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property of any Person.

**4.8 Inventory and Accounts Receivable.** All inventory of the Business consists of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course, except for obsolete items that have been written off or written down to net realizable value. All accounts receivable of the Business arose from bona fide transactions in the ordinary course.

**4.9 Product Warranty.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedules sets forth the standard terms of warranty for the products of the Business. There are no claims pending against Seller alleging that any product of the Business is defective other than routine warranty claims in the ordinary course.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller has complied in all material respects with all Laws applicable to the Business, and holds all permits required to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.11 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.12 Taxes.** All Tax returns required to be filed by Seller with respect to the Business have been timely filed, and all Taxes due and owing by Seller with respect to the Business have been timely paid.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Buyer of this Agreement do not conflict with Buyer's organizational documents or violate any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand or other sources of immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve the Purchased Assets and the goodwill and relationships of the Business with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to the employees of the Business listed on Section 6.3 of the Disclosure Schedules. Seller shall be responsible for all compensation and benefits of such employees accrued through the Closing Date.

**6.4 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) engage in the Business anywhere in the United States or Canada, (b) solicit any customer of the Business to purchase products or services competitive with the Business, or (c) solicit for employment any employee hired by Buyer under Section 6.3.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent such information becomes generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach.

**6.6 Receivables and Mail.** After the Closing, if Seller receives any payment in respect of any Purchased Asset, Seller shall promptly remit that payment to Buyer. Seller shall promptly forward to Buyer any mail or other communications relating to the Business.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and give effect to the transactions contemplated hereby.

## ARTICLE VII. INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.3, 4.12, 4.13, 5.1 and 5.4 survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

**7.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective representatives against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement, or (c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**7.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates and their respective representatives against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**7.4 Limitations.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 7.2(a) (other than for breaches of the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.3, 4.12 and 4.13) until the aggregate Losses exceed $485,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount, and Seller's aggregate liability under Section 7.2(a) (other than for such representations) shall not exceed $4,850,000.

**7.5 Claims Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party.

**7.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the determination of Closing Net Working Capital under Section 2.4, the indemnification provisions of this Article VII are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to (a) the accuracy of Seller's representations and warranties in all material respects, (b) Seller's performance in all material respects of its covenants required to be performed before the Closing, and (c) delivery of each item listed in Section 3.2.

**8.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to (a) the accuracy of Buyer's representations and warranties in all material respects, (b) Buyer's performance in all material respects of its covenants required to be performed before the Closing, and (c) delivery of each item listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE IX. TAX MATTERS

**9.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and registration Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**9.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar ad valorem Taxes relating to the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be apportioned between Seller and Buyer based on the number of days in such period before and after the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before March 31, 2026, provided that the terminating Party is not then in material breach of this Agreement; or (c) by either Party upon a material breach of this Agreement by the other Party that is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 10.1, this Agreement shall become void, without liability of any Party, except that Section 6.5, this Section 10.2 and Article XI survive, and no termination relieves any Party from liability for willful breach.
