### 2.1 Notice

This letter is the Notice of Disagreement of Harrowgate Climate Systems, Inc. ("**Buyer**") under Section 2.4(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**APA**"), between Buyer and Tellmar Controls LLC ("**Seller**"). It responds to the Closing Statement transmitted under Seller's letter dated March 2, 2026, which Buyer received on March 3, 2026.

Buyer disagrees with Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital of $6,940,000. The disputed items, the amount in dispute for each and the basis for each are set out in Section 2.2 below.

### 2.2 Disputed Items

**(a) Inventory obsolescence reserve. Amount in dispute: $410,000.** Seller's reserve analysis applies a 25% reserve to inventory with no movement for more than 18 months. The Balance Sheet, and the reserve methodology described in Schedule 1.1(a), apply a 100% reserve to inventory with no movement for more than 12 months. Applying the Accounting Principles to the Closing Date inventory count summary increases the reserve by $410,000. In particular, the reserve should cover discontinued legacy thermostat housings and controller boards for superseded rooftop unit models.

**(b) Allowance for doubtful accounts. Amount in dispute: $185,000.** Seller's aging report shows $612,000 of receivables more than 120 days past due. Consistent with the methodology used in preparing the Balance Sheet, the allowance should cover 100% of balances more than 120 days past due and 50% of balances more than 90 days past due. Seller's allowance is $185,000 below the amount that methodology produces.

**(c) Product warranty reserve. Amount in dispute: $290,000.** Seller's warranty reserve calculation applies a claims rate derived from calendar year 2023 experience. The Accounting Principles require the trailing twelve-month claims rate used in preparing the Balance Sheet. That rate reflects elevated claims on the residential zoning control product line in the second half of 2025. Applying it increases the current portion of the warranty reserve by $290,000.

**(d) Accrued liabilities. Amount in dispute: $275,000.** Seller's accrued liabilities rollforward omits (i) $168,000 of supplier invoices for components received before the Closing Date and invoiced after it, and (ii) $107,000 of accrued freight and utilities attributable to periods before the Closing Date. These amounts are current liabilities of the Business included in the Assumed Liabilities and should be accrued in Closing Net Working Capital.

### 2.3 Buyer's Calculation

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Seller's Closing Net Working Capital | $6,940,000 |
| Adjustment 2.2(a): Inventory obsolescence reserve | ($410,000) |
| Adjustment 2.2(b): Allowance for doubtful accounts | ($185,000) |
| Adjustment 2.2(c): Product warranty reserve | ($290,000) |
| Adjustment 2.2(d): Accrued liabilities | ($275,000) |
| **Buyer's Closing Net Working Capital** | **$5,780,000** |
| Estimated Closing Net Working Capital | $6,350,000 |
| **Post-Closing Adjustment (payable by Seller to Buyer)** | **($570,000)** |

On Buyer's calculation, Closing Net Working Capital is $5,780,000, which is $420,000 below the Target Net Working Capital of $6,200,000. The Post-Closing Adjustment is negative $570,000, and its absolute value is payable by Seller to Buyer under Section 2.4(d) of the APA.

### 2.4 Supporting Documentation

Buyer encloses: (a) a schedule of the inventory items subject to adjustment 2.2(a), with last-movement dates; (b) a recalculation of the allowance for doubtful accounts from Seller's aging report; (c) a recalculation of the warranty reserve using the trailing twelve-month claims rate; and (d) copies of the supplier invoices and freight and utility statements supporting adjustment 2.2(d).

### 2.5 Resolution

Under Section 2.4(c) of the APA, Buyer proposes that the Parties begin good faith negotiations on the disputed items. If any disputed items remain unresolved within 30 days after Seller's receipt of this Notice of Disagreement, Buyer intends to submit them to the Independent Accountant. Buyer reserves all of its rights under the APA, including under Article VII.
