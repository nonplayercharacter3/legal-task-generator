# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Tellmar Controls LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Harrowgate Climate Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as Seller, and Recipient, as Buyer, are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient acquired the Purchased Assets and assumed the Assumed Liabilities.

B. The Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to enter into this Agreement at the Closing as a condition to the Closing.

C. Recipient requires certain transitional services from Provider following the Closing, and Provider is willing to provide those services on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings specified below:

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture, sale and servicing of commercial building climate control systems.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information relating to the Business, the Purchased Assets or Recipient, including customer, supplier, pricing, product engineering, service and employee data, that is stored on, processed by or transmitted through the Provider Systems or otherwise received or generated by Provider in performing the Services.

"**Provider Personnel**" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of Provider who perform the Services.

"**Provider Systems**" means the information technology systems, software, networks, enterprise resource planning platform, telephony and related infrastructure owned, licensed or operated by Provider and used to provide the Services.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.3.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the earlier of (a) the end date for that Service set forth in Schedule A and (b) the expiration or earlier termination of this Agreement or of that Service under Section 5.

"**Services**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.3 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, *mutatis mutandis*, except that references in that Section to "this Agreement" are to this Agreement, and references to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement. Schedule A (Services) and Schedule B (Service Fees) form part of this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause to be provided, to Recipient the services described in Schedule A, together with any ancillary services that are reasonably necessary to operate the Business and that were provided by or on behalf of Provider to the Business during the twelve months before the Closing Date (collectively, the "**Services**").

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) with at least the same degree of care, skill, priority and diligence, and at substantially the same volume and level of quality, as Provider performed such services for the Business during the twelve months before the Closing Date, and (c) in compliance with all applicable Laws.

**2.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign Provider Personnel having the skill, training and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Provider shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of all Provider Personnel. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain continuity of the individuals who performed comparable services for the Business before the Closing Date.

**2.4 Third-Party Consents.** If the provision of any Service requires the consent of a third party under a license or other contract to which Provider is a party, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent promptly after the Effective Date. Reasonable out-of-pocket costs of obtaining any such consent shall be borne equally by the Parties. Pending receipt of a required consent, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a commercially reasonable alternative arrangement that provides Recipient with substantially the same benefit.

**2.5 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies, within 60 days after the Effective Date, any service that was provided by or on behalf of Provider to the Business before the Closing Date and that is not listed in Schedule A, Recipient may request that Provider provide that service. Provider shall consider any such request in good faith, and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith an amendment to Schedule A setting out the scope, Service Period and fee for that service, with fees determined on the same basis as set forth in Schedule B.

**2.6 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall cooperate with Recipient and Recipient's designees, at Recipient's reasonable request, to migrate the Business and the Recipient Data from the Provider Systems to Recipient's own systems before the end of the applicable Service Period, including by providing data extracts in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

## 3. ACCESS, SYSTEMS AND GOVERNANCE

**3.1 Access to Provider Systems.** Provider shall grant designated Recipient personnel access to the Provider Systems to the extent necessary for Recipient to receive and use the Services. Recipient shall comply with Provider's reasonable written information security and acceptable use policies provided to Recipient in advance.

**3.2 Ownership and Protection of Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, all Recipient Data is the property of Recipient. Provider shall (a) use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services, (b) maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Recipient Data no less protective than those Provider uses for its own data of a similar nature and in any event consistent with industry standards, (c) logically segregate Recipient Data from Provider's other data to the extent reasonably practicable, and (d) notify Recipient within 48 hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or use or disclosure of Recipient Data. Within 30 days after the end of the last Service Period, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a complete copy of all Recipient Data and, after Recipient confirms receipt, permanently delete all Recipient Data from the Provider Systems, except as required by Law.

**3.3 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall appoint a service coordinator (each, a "**Service Coordinator**") who shall serve as that Party's primary point of contact for the day-to-day administration of this Agreement. The Service Coordinators shall meet (in person or by video conference) at least every two weeks during the Term to review performance of the Services and migration progress.

**3.4 Dispute Escalation.** Any dispute relating to the Services shall first be referred to the Service Coordinators. If the Service Coordinators do not resolve the dispute within ten Business Days, either Party may refer the dispute to a senior executive of each Party, who shall meet within ten Business Days after referral. If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days after referral to the senior executives, either Party may pursue any remedy available under Section 10.2.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** In consideration for the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider the fees set forth in Schedule B (the "**Service Fees**"). The Service Fees are intended to reimburse Provider for its fully loaded cost of providing the Services, without mark-up.

**4.2 Expenses.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Provider in providing the Services, to the extent approved in advance in writing by Recipient's Service Coordinator.

**4.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears for the Service Fees and approved expenses for the preceding month, with reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt. If Recipient disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, it shall notify Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date, pay the undisputed portion, and the Parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 3.4.

**4.4 Taxes.** Recipient shall bear any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the provision of the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's income. Provider shall separately state any such Taxes on its invoices.

**4.5 Records and Audit.** Provider shall maintain accurate records supporting the Service Fees and expenses invoiced under this Agreement for at least two years after the end of the Term. Upon reasonable notice, Recipient may audit such records once during the Term and once within twelve months after the Term.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

**5.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless earlier terminated under this Section 5, continues until the date that is 9 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"). The Term may be extended only by a written instrument signed by each Party.

**5.2 Termination of Individual Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience upon 30 days' written notice to Provider. Upon termination of a Service, the Service Fees for that Service shall cease to accrue as of the effective date of termination, and the Service Fees for any partially terminated Service shall be equitably reduced. Termination of a Service does not affect the provision of any other Service, except to the extent the other Service depends on the terminated Service, as identified in Schedule A.

**5.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure that breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail; provided that Provider may terminate for Recipient's failure to pay only if undisputed Service Fees remain unpaid for more than 30 days after written notice of non-payment.

**5.4 Effect of Expiration or Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, (a) Recipient shall pay all Service Fees and approved expenses accrued through the effective date of expiration or termination, and (b) Provider shall continue to comply with Sections 2.6 and 3.2. Sections 3.2, 4.3 through 4.5, 5.4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Confidential Information.** Each Party shall hold in confidence, and shall not use except to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, all non-public information of the other Party disclosed or made available to it in connection with this Agreement, including Recipient Data. These obligations do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through the receiving Party's breach, (b) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information.

**6.2 Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose confidential information to the extent required by Law, provided that it gives the other Party prompt prior written notice, to the extent legally permitted, and cooperates with the other Party's efforts to obtain a protective order or other confidential treatment.

**6.3 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** The obligations in this Section 6 are in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 Provider Intellectual Property.** Except as provided in the Purchase Agreement, Provider retains all right, title and interest in and to the Provider Systems. Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Provider Systems solely to receive and use the Services.

**7.2 Work Product.** All data, reports and other deliverables created by Provider specifically for Recipient in performing the Services, and all Recipient Data, are owned by Recipient, and Provider hereby assigns to Recipient all of its right, title and interest therein.

## 8. WARRANTIES, INDEMNIFICATION AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

**8.2 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement or in the Purchase Agreement, Provider makes no other representation or warranty, express or implied, with respect to the Services.

**8.3 Indemnification.** Each Party shall indemnify the other Party and its Affiliates and their respective representatives against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from the indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 3.2 or Section 6.

**8.4 Limitation of Liability.** Except for liability arising from a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, breach of Section 3.2 or Section 6, or its indemnification obligations under Section 8.3, (a) neither Party is liable to the other for any punitive, exemplary or special damages, or for lost profits that were not reasonably foreseeable, and (b) each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

## 9. GENERAL

**9.1 Relationship of the Parties.** Provider is an independent contractor in performing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

**9.2 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that it promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from providing any Service for more than 15 days, Recipient may terminate that Service without liability and the Service Fees for that Service shall be suspended during the period of non-performance.

**9.3 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or delegate any of its obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of Provider. Provider may assign or delegate this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, including the performance of any Service, in whole or in part, to any Person upon written notice to Recipient. Any purported assignment or delegation in violation of this Section 9.3 is null and void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**9.4 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement to the addresses of the Parties specified in the Purchase Agreement, with a copy to the other Party's Service Coordinator.

**9.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, including its Schedules, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless explicitly set forth in writing and signed by the Party so waiving.

**9.6 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that holding shall not affect any other term of this Agreement or invalidate that term in any other jurisdiction.

**9.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Section 8.3, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in .pdf format or by electronic signature service are deemed original signatures.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND REMEDIES

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if Provider failed to perform the Services in accordance with this Agreement, and that Recipient is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled at law or in equity.
