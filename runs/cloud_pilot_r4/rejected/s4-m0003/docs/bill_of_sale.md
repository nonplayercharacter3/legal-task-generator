# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of February 2, 2026 by **Tellmar Controls LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Harrowgate Climate Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Purchased Assets to Buyer, and Buyer agreed to purchase them from Seller.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing, duly executed by Seller, transferring the tangible personal property included in the Purchased Assets.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the payment made by Buyer at the Closing under Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement and the other covenants and agreements in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Business," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Encumbrance," "Excluded Assets," "Permitted Encumbrances" and "Purchased Assets."

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.3 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Bill of Sale, *mutatis mutandis*.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Transfer of Tangible Personal Property.** Effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date, Seller hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all tangible personal property included in the Purchased Assets (the "**Transferred Property**"), including:

(a) all inventory, raw materials, work in progress, finished goods, spare parts and supplies of the Business, as described in Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) all machinery, equipment, tooling, vehicles, furniture and computer hardware used or held for use in the Business, as described in Section 2.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(c) all books and records of the Business in tangible form, as described in Section 2.2(i) of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Transferred Property does not include, and Seller does not sell, assign, transfer, convey or deliver to Buyer under this Bill of Sale, any of the Excluded Assets, which Seller retains.

**2.3 No Assumption of Liabilities.** This Bill of Sale is an instrument of conveyance only. Buyer does not assume, and this Bill of Sale does not cause Buyer to assume, any liability or obligation of Seller. The Assumed Liabilities are assumed by Buyer solely under the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Purchase Agreement.

**2.4 Possession.** Seller shall deliver to Buyer possession of the Transferred Property at the locations at which it is held as of the Closing Date. To the extent any Transferred Property remains at Seller's Dayton, Ohio facility after the Closing Date in connection with the warehousing and logistics services under the Transition Services Agreement, Seller holds that Transferred Property for the account and benefit of Buyer, as bailee for Buyer, and title to it vests in Buyer as of the Closing Date.

## 3. POWER OF ATTORNEY

**3.1 Appointment.** Seller hereby irrevocably constitutes and appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller but on behalf and for the benefit of Buyer, to demand and receive any of the Transferred Property, to institute and prosecute any proceeding that Buyer reasonably deems proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Transferred Property, and to defend or compromise any action relating to the Transferred Property.

**3.2 Coupled with an Interest.** The power of attorney in Section 3.1 is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable, and it is not affected by the subsequent dissolution or bankruptcy of Seller.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**4.1 Further Actions.** In accordance with Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall, at Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such additional instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably required to vest in Buyer good and valid title to the Transferred Property and to put Buyer in actual possession and operating control of it.

**4.2 Misdirected Property.** If, after the Closing, Seller or any of its Affiliates holds or comes into possession of any Transferred Property, Seller shall hold that Transferred Property in trust for Buyer and promptly deliver it to Buyer.

## 5. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**5.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies the representations, warranties, covenants, agreements or indemnities of Seller or Buyer in the Purchase Agreement, each of which survives the execution and delivery of this Bill of Sale in accordance with the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns and inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

**6.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Bill of Sale is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Section 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any action arising out of this Bill of Sale, *mutatis mutandis*.

**6.3 Severability.** If any term of this Bill of Sale is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that holding shall not affect any other term of this Bill of Sale or invalidate that term in any other jurisdiction.

**6.4 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, and signatures delivered by email in .pdf format or by electronic signature service are deemed original signatures.
