# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: 2027 PRODUCTION SEASON

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into under the Supply Agreement dated as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between **Braport Seed Genetics, Inc.** ("**Customer**" or "**Braport**") and **Traidir Seed Production, LLC** ("**Supplier**" or "**Traidir**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 **Production Scope.** Under this SOW, Supplier will produce hybrid corn seed from Braport parent lines, grown in Iowa and Illinois. Supplier will produce that seed from Parent Seed delivered by Customer, and will Condition and deliver it as Contract Seed for the 2027 Production Season.

1.2 **Services.** Supplier will perform the following services under this SOW:

(a) receipt, secure storage and chain-of-custody handling of Parent Seed under Article 9 of the Agreement;

(b) field preparation, planting, pollination management (including detasseling), roguing and field inspection on the Production Fields;

(c) harvest, drying, husking, shelling, sizing, treating, bagging and labeling of Contract Seed;

(d) lot testing and issue of certificates of analysis under Section 8.1 of the Agreement and the Service Level Schedule; and

(e) storage and delivery of Conditioned Contract Seed under Section 5 of this SOW.

1.3 **Production Season.** This SOW covers the 2027 Production Season, beginning on the date Supplier receives the first shipment of Parent Seed and ending when Supplier has delivered all Contract Seed under this SOW and completed the Parent Seed reconciliation under Section 9.4 of the Agreement.

## 3. PRODUCTION VOLUME

3.1 **Production Volume.** The target production volume for the 2027 Production Season is 180,000 units of 80,000 kernels each. For this SOW, a "Unit" means 80,000 kernels of Conditioned Contract Seed.

3.2 **Allocation by Hybrid.** The production volume is allocated among the hybrids as follows:

| Hybrid Designation | Units |
|---|---|
| BSG-4120 | 70,000 |
| BSG-4385 | 62,000 |
| BSG-4610 | 48,000 |
| **Total** | **180,000** |

3.3 **Acreage.** Supplier will plant enough acreage to produce the production volume, based on its yield history for comparable female lines, and in any case not less than 3,150 female-row acres in total. Supplier will report actual planted acreage by Production Field to Customer within ten Business Days after planting is completed.

3.4 **Yield Updates.** Supplier will give Customer written yield estimates by hybrid on August 15, 2027 and again on September 15, 2027. If Supplier expects to produce less than 90% of the Units allocated to any hybrid, it will notify Customer promptly, and the Parties will discuss in good faith whether to reallocate volume among hybrids.

## 4. PRODUCTION FIELDS AND SCHEDULE

4.1 **Production Fields.** The Production Fields for this SOW are located in the following counties:

| State | County | Hybrids | Approximate Female Acres |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iowa | Story | BSG-4120 | 720 |
| Iowa | Boone | BSG-4120, BSG-4385 | 840 |
| Iowa | Hamilton | BSG-4385 | 510 |
| Illinois | Henry | BSG-4610 | 610 |
| Illinois | Bureau | BSG-4385, BSG-4610 | 470 |

Supplier will provide Customer with legal descriptions and GPS boundaries for each Production Field by February 15, 2027. Supplier must obtain Customer's prior written approval before substituting or adding any field.

4.2 **Isolation.** Supplier will maintain the isolation distances and other isolation requirements set out in the Specifications for each Production Field.

4.3 **Schedule.** Supplier will perform production according to the following schedule:

| Activity | Target Window |
|---|---|
| Planting | April 20 to May 25, 2027 |
| Detasseling and roguing | July 1 to July 31, 2027 |
| Harvest | September 1 to October 20, 2027 |
| Conditioning and lot testing | September 15 to December 15, 2027 |
| Final delivery | By January 15, 2028 |

Supplier will notify Customer promptly if weather or field conditions are likely to move any activity outside its target window.

4.4 **Field Inspections.** Customer may inspect the Production Fields under Section 5.4 of the Agreement. During the pollination period, Supplier will give Customer at least 48 hours' notice of the planned start of detasseling on each Production Field.

## 5. CONDITIONING, PACKAGING AND DELIVERY

5.1 **Conditioning.** Supplier will Condition Contract Seed at its facility in Ames, Iowa. Contract Seed from the Illinois Production Fields will be moved to that facility in sealed, identified conveyances. Supplier will record each transfer in its chain-of-custody records.

5.2 **Treatment.** Supplier will apply the seed treatment package that Customer designates in writing by February 1, 2027. Customer will supply the treatment materials, or Supplier will purchase them and Customer will reimburse them at Supplier's cost without markup.

5.3 **Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier will package Contract Seed in 50-Unit bulk boxes and single-Unit bags in the proportions Customer specifies by November 1, 2027. Supplier will label all packaging with Customer's hybrid designations, trademarks and lot numbers, and the labels must comply with applicable seed labeling laws.

5.4 **Delivery Locations.** Supplier will deliver Conditioned Contract Seed FCA Supplier's Ames, Iowa facility, for collection by Customer's designated carriers, or to such other locations within Iowa and Illinois as Customer may designate in writing. Supplier will give Customer five Business Days' notice that each lot is ready for collection.

5.5 **Documentation.** Each lot will be accompanied by a certificate of analysis under Section 8.1 of the Agreement, a packing list and a bill of lading.

## 6. QUALITY

6.1 **Specifications.** Contract Seed must meet the Specifications in the Service Level Schedule, including its minimum germination, genetic purity and physical purity standards.

6.2 **Acceptance.** Customer may test and accept or reject lots of Contract Seed as provided in Article 8 of the Agreement.

## 7. FEES

7.1 **Production Fee.** Customer will pay Supplier the following fee for each Unit of Contract Seed accepted under Article 8 of the Agreement:

| Hybrid Designation | Fee per Unit |
|---|---|
| BSG-4120 | $34.50 |
| BSG-4385 | $35.75 |
| BSG-4610 | $36.25 |

7.2 **Included Costs.** The production fee covers all land, labor, equipment, inputs (other than seed treatment materials under Section 5.2), field operations, Conditioning, packaging, testing, storage until collection, and transport of Contract Seed from the Illinois Production Fields to the Ames, Iowa facility.

7.3 **Grower Advances.** On request, Customer will advance to Supplier up to 20% of the estimated production fee for each hybrid on July 15, 2027. Each advance will be credited against the first invoices issued for that hybrid.

7.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier will invoice and Customer will pay in accordance with Article 6 of the Agreement, including payment of undisputed amounts within 45 days from receipt of invoice. Service credits under the Service Level Schedule will be applied against invoices issued under this SOW.
