# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into under the Supply Agreement dated as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between **Braport Seed Genetics, Inc.** ("**Customer**" or "**Braport**") and **Traidir Seed Production, LLC** ("**Supplier**" or "**Traidir**"). This Schedule is the Service Level Schedule referred to in Sections 1.1 and 2.2 of the Agreement. It applies to Supplier's performance under the Agreement and each Statement of Work. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Schedule:

"**Certified Laboratory**" means a seed testing laboratory accredited by the Association of Official Seed Analysts or the Society of Commercial Seed Technologists, or another laboratory approved in writing by Customer.

"**Lot**" means a uniform, identified quantity of Conditioned Contract Seed of a single hybrid, produced and Conditioned together and assigned a single lot number.

"**Performance Failure**" means any failure by Supplier to meet a Service Level in Section 4 or a Specification in Section 2.

"**Seed Quality**" means the germination, vigor, genetic purity, physical purity, moisture, sizing, treatment and freedom-from-contamination attributes of Contract Seed described in Section 2.

"**Service Credit**" means a credit against fees calculated under Section 5.

"**Service Level**" means each performance standard set out in Section 4.

## 2. SEED QUALITY SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 **Germination.** Each Lot must achieve a minimum of 95% warm germination, tested under Section 3.

2.2 **Vigor.** Each Lot must achieve a minimum of 88% cold germination under the saturated cold test method in Section 3.2.

2.3 **Genetic Purity.** Each Lot must achieve a minimum genetic purity of 98.0%, with female selfs not exceeding 1.0% and off-types not exceeding 1.0%, determined by grow-out or by molecular marker analysis performed by a Certified Laboratory on Customer's behalf. Supplier will not itself perform any marker analysis of Braport Germplasm, as provided in Section 9.2(c) of the Agreement.

2.4 **Physical Purity.** Each Lot must contain at least 99.0% pure seed by weight, not more than 1.0% inert matter, no weed seed of any species designated as noxious under the seed laws of Iowa or Illinois, and no other crop seed.

2.5 **Moisture.** Contract Seed must be dried to a moisture content between 10.0% and 13.0% before bagging.

2.6 **Sizing.** Contract Seed must be sized into the size grades designated by Customer for each hybrid. Not more than 2.0% of seed in any sized Lot may fall outside the designated grade.

2.7 **Adventitious Presence.** Each Lot must test below 0.5% adventitious presence of any transgenic event not contained in the applicable hybrid, as measured by a Certified Laboratory using methods approved by Customer.

2.8 **Treatment.** Seed treatment must be applied at the rates designated by Customer under the applicable Statement of Work, within plus or minus 10% of the target loading for each active ingredient.

2.9 **Isolation.** Supplier will maintain, for each Production Field, isolation of at least 660 feet from any other corn of a different kernel color, type or genetic background. Isolation may be reduced by border rows or differential planting dates only as approved in writing by Customer for that Production Field.

## 3. TESTING PROTOCOLS

3.1 **Sampling.** Supplier will draw official samples from each Lot during bagging in accordance with the Association of Official Seed Analysts Rules for Testing Seeds. Supplier will retain a file sample of at least 1,000 grams from each Lot for two years after delivery and will make it available to Customer on request.

3.2 **Test Methods.** Warm germination, cold germination and physical purity tests will be performed by a Certified Laboratory under the Association of Official Seed Analysts Rules for Testing Seeds. Cold germination will be tested at 10°C for seven days in unsterilized field soil, followed by four days at 25°C.

3.3 **Certificates of Analysis.** The certificate of analysis delivered with each Lot under Section 8.1 of the Agreement will state the Lot number, hybrid designation, test dates, the laboratory that performed each test and the results for each Specification in Section 2.

3.4 **Customer Testing.** Customer may test any Lot under Section 8.2 of the Agreement. If Customer's test results and Supplier's test results for a Lot differ, a third Certified Laboratory selected jointly by the Parties will retest the retained file sample. The retest result is binding on the Parties for purposes of acceptance under Section 8.2 of the Agreement. The Party whose result differed more from the retest result will bear the cost of the retest.

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 **On-Time Delivery.** Supplier will make each Lot available for collection, or deliver it, by the delivery date set out in the applicable Statement of Work. The Service Level is 100% of Units by the final delivery date.

4.2 **Volume.** Supplier will deliver, for each hybrid, at least 95% of the Units allocated to that hybrid in the applicable Statement of Work. Shortfalls caused by a Force Majeure Event under Article 17 of the Agreement are excluded from this Service Level.

4.3 **First-Pass Quality.** At least 97% of Lots delivered in each Production Season must meet all Specifications in Section 2 on first test, without reconditioning.

4.4 **Reporting.** Supplier will deliver each progress report required by the applicable Statement of Work within two Business Days after its due date.

4.5 **Germplasm Notification.** Supplier will notify Customer of each Germplasm Loss Event within the 24-hour period required by Section 9.5 of the Agreement.

4.6 **Chain-of-Custody Records.** Supplier's chain-of-custody records under Section 9.3 of the Agreement must reconcile to within 0.5% by weight of all Parent Seed received for each parent line.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 **Calculation.** Supplier will issue Service Credits for Performance Failures as follows:

| Service Level | Performance Failure | Service Credit |
|---|---|---|
| On-Time Delivery (4.1) | Each Unit made available after the delivery date | 1% of the fee for that Unit for each week or part week of delay, up to 10% |
| Volume (4.2) | Units delivered below 95% of allocation for a hybrid | $4.00 per Unit of shortfall below the 95% threshold |
| First-Pass Quality (4.3) | First-pass rate below 97% | 2% of the fees for the Production Season for each full percentage point below 97% |
| Reporting (4.4) | Each late report | $500 per report |
| Germplasm Notification (4.5) | Each late notification | $25,000 per notification |
| Chain-of-Custody Records (4.6) | Reconciliation variance above 0.5% | $10,000 per parent line |

5.2 **Application.** Service Credits will be applied against the next invoice issued under the applicable Statement of Work. Supplier will pay Customer any Service Credits that remain unapplied at the end of a Production Season within 45 days.

5.3 **Reporting of Credits.** Supplier will include in each invoice a statement of all Service Credits accrued and applied during the invoice period.

5.4 **Other Remedies.** Service Credits are in addition to Customer's rights to reject Lots under Article 8 of the Agreement and to Customer's other rights and remedies under the Agreement.

## 6. REMEDIES AND LIABILITY

6.1 **Non-Conforming Lots.** If a Lot fails to meet any Specification in Section 2, Customer may require Supplier to recondition the Lot at Supplier's cost and resubmit it for testing within 20 Business Days. If the reconditioned Lot again fails, Customer may reject it under Section 8.3 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Germplasm Loss Events.** Notwithstanding Section 12.3 of the Agreement, Supplier's liability for any Germplasm Loss Event, including any related Performance Failure, is subject to the limit in Section 12.2 of the Agreement.

6.3 **Corrective Action.** For any Performance Failure, Supplier will deliver to Customer, within ten Business Days after Customer's request, a written root-cause analysis and corrective action plan. Supplier will carry out the plan as approved by Customer.

6.4 **Chronic Failure.** If Supplier fails the same Service Level in two consecutive Production Seasons, the failure is a material breach for the purposes of Section 16.1 of the Agreement.

## 7. GERMPLASM SECURITY MEASURES

7.1 **Storage.** Supplier will store Parent Seed in locked, access-controlled storage separate from all other seed. Only personnel named on an access list approved by Customer may enter it. Contract Seed will be stored in segregated, labeled bays within Supplier's Ames, Iowa facility.

7.2 **Access Logs and Surveillance.** Supplier will keep electronic access logs and continuous video surveillance of all Parent Seed storage areas. Supplier will retain the logs and recordings for at least 12 months and provide them to Customer on request.

7.3 **Field Controls.** At the end of planting, Supplier will clean all planter units used for Parent Seed on the Production Field. Supplier will collect and return to secure storage, or destroy under Customer's supervision, all leftover Parent Seed and spilled seed.

7.4 **Transport.** All Parent Seed and Contract Seed in transit must move in sealed conveyances with recorded seal numbers. Each transfer must be recorded in Supplier's chain-of-custody records.

7.5 **Personnel.** Supplier will ensure that all personnel with access to Braport Germplasm have signed confidentiality undertakings consistent with Article 15 of the Agreement and have completed Supplier's germplasm stewardship training.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Law and Forum.** This Schedule, and any dispute concerning Performance Failures, Service Credits or Seed Quality, is governed by the laws of the State of Iowa, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any such dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Polk County, Iowa, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

8.2 **Review.** The Parties' quality leads will meet within 60 days after final delivery for each Production Season to review Service Level performance and propose any changes to the Specifications or Service Levels for the next Production Season.

8.3 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 20.3 of the Agreement.

8.4 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
