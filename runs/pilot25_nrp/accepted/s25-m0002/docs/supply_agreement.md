# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Braport Seed Genetics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**" or "**Braport**"), and **Traidir Seed Production, LLC**, an Iowa limited liability company ("**Supplier**" or "**Traidir**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer develops and owns proprietary parent-line germplasm and supplies parent seed to contract growers for the production of commercial seed.

B. Supplier is in the business of growing, harvesting, conditioning, treating and packaging seed on behalf of seed companies.

C. Customer wishes to engage Supplier to produce, condition and supply seed from Customer's parent seed, and Supplier wishes to accept that engagement, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

"**Braport Germplasm**" means all plant genetic material owned, licensed or controlled by Customer, including Parent Seed, inbred and parent lines, breeding populations, plant tissue, pollen, and any progeny, derivative or genetic information derived from any of them.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized to close.

"**Conditioning**" means drying, husking, shelling, cleaning, sizing, treating, bagging, labeling and storing seed, and "**Condition**" has a corresponding meaning.

"**Contract Seed**" means any seed of any crop produced from Braport Germplasm.

"**Germplasm Loss Event**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5.

"**Parent Seed**" means seed of Customer's parent lines delivered by or on behalf of Customer to Supplier for planting under this Agreement.

"**Production Fields**" means the fields identified in a Statement of Work or approved in writing by Customer for planting Parent Seed.

"**Production Season**" means the growing, harvest and Conditioning cycle for a crop year covered by a Statement of Work.

"**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule entered into by the Parties under this Agreement, as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 20.3.

"**Specifications**" means the seed quality standards, testing protocols and packaging requirements for Contract Seed set out in the Service Level Schedule and the applicable Statement of Work.

"**Statement of Work**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties under Section 2.1 that describes production for one or more Production Seasons.

"**Territory**" means the United States and Canada.

"**Unit**" means the quantity of Contract Seed described as a unit in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles and Sections are to Articles and Sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 **Statements of Work.** The Parties will set out the production plan for each Production Season in a Statement of Work, which will specify, at a minimum, the hybrids to be produced, the production volume, the Production Fields, the planting and delivery schedule, and the pricing applicable to that Production Season. A Statement of Work becomes binding only when signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

2.2 **Service Level Schedule.** The Service Level Schedule sets out the Specifications, performance standards, service credits and related procedures that apply to Supplier's performance under this Agreement and each Statement of Work.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming this Agreement, the documents will govern in the following order: the Service Level Schedule, then this Supply Agreement, then each Statement of Work.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Article 16.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the then-current term.

3.3 **Statements of Work in Progress.** If this Agreement expires while a Statement of Work is in progress, this Agreement will continue to govern that Statement of Work until Supplier has completed delivery of all Contract Seed for the Production Season concerned and Customer has paid all amounts due for it.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, Customer shall purchase all of its requirements of Contract Seed in the Territory exclusively from Supplier, and shall not produce Contract Seed in the Territory itself or through any other grower.

4.2 **Capacity Commitment.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient acreage, isolation, equipment, Conditioning capacity and personnel to produce the volumes set out in each Statement of Work.

4.3 **Forecasts.** By July 1 of each year, Customer will provide Supplier with a non-binding forecast of its anticipated production requirements for the following Production Season. Forecasts do not bind Customer to execute a Statement of Work for any specific volume.

## 5. PRODUCTION

5.1 **Parent Seed.** Customer will deliver Parent Seed to Supplier in the quantities and at the times set out in the applicable Statement of Work. Supplier shall use Parent Seed solely to produce Contract Seed for Customer in accordance with this Agreement.

5.2 **Agronomic Practices.** Supplier shall plant, cultivate, detassel or otherwise manage pollination, rogue, harvest and Condition Contract Seed in accordance with good industry practice, the Specifications and Customer's written production protocols as provided to Supplier from time to time.

5.3 **Isolation and Field Records.** Supplier shall maintain isolation distances required by the Specifications and keep complete field records for each Production Field, including planting dates, inputs applied, inspection results and harvest data. Supplier shall provide those records to Customer on request.

5.4 **Inspection Rights.** Customer and its designees may enter Production Fields and Supplier's Conditioning and storage facilities on reasonable notice during normal business hours to inspect production, take samples and audit Supplier's compliance with this Agreement.

5.5 **Subcontracting.** Supplier shall not subcontract any growing or Conditioning of Contract Seed without Customer's prior written consent. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractor.

5.6 **Compliance with Laws.** Supplier shall comply with all applicable laws, including seed certification, labeling, pesticide and environmental laws, and shall hold all permits needed to perform its obligations.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Customer will pay Supplier the fees set out in the applicable Statement of Work for Contract Seed accepted under Article 8, subject to any service credits under the Service Level Schedule.

6.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon acceptance of each lot of Contract Seed. Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within 45 days from receipt of invoice.

6.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier of the dispute and its basis before the due date. The Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

6.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer will pay where applicable, other than taxes on Supplier's income, property or employees.

6.5 **Records and Audit.** Supplier shall keep accurate books and records relating to fees charged under this Agreement for at least three years after the relevant Production Season. Customer may audit those records once per year on 15 Business Days' notice.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Conditioned Contract Seed to the locations and on the schedule set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

7.2 **Title.** Title to Parent Seed and Braport Germplasm remains with Customer at all times. Title to Contract Seed vests in Customer as the Contract Seed is produced, and Supplier holds all Contract Seed as bailee for Customer.

7.3 **Risk of Loss.** Supplier bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, Parent Seed and Contract Seed from receipt by Supplier until delivery to Customer under Section 7.1.

## 8. QUALITY AND ACCEPTANCE

8.1 **Testing.** Supplier shall test each lot of Contract Seed in accordance with the Specifications and deliver a certificate of analysis with each lot.

8.2 **Acceptance.** Customer may test any lot of Contract Seed and may reject any lot that does not meet the Specifications by notice to Supplier within 30 days after delivery. Lots not rejected within that period are deemed accepted.

8.3 **Rejected Lots.** Supplier shall not sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of any rejected lot except as directed in writing by Customer. At Customer's direction, Supplier shall recondition, destroy or return rejected lots at Supplier's cost, and Customer is not obliged to pay for any rejected lot.

## 9. GERMPLASM PROTECTION

9.1 **Ownership.** Customer owns all right, title and interest in and to Braport Germplasm. Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses any right in Braport Germplasm to Supplier, other than the limited right to plant Parent Seed and produce Contract Seed for Customer under this Agreement.

9.2 **Restrictions.** Supplier shall not, and shall ensure that its personnel and subcontractors do not: (a) use Parent Seed or Contract Seed for any purpose other than performance of this Agreement; (b) propagate, multiply, self, backcross or breed with any Braport Germplasm except as expressly directed by Customer; (c) conduct any genetic analysis, fingerprinting or sequencing of Braport Germplasm; or (d) sell, transfer, give or make available any Braport Germplasm to any third party.

9.3 **Security and Segregation.** Supplier shall store Parent Seed and Contract Seed in secure, segregated and clearly identified facilities, maintain chain-of-custody records for all Parent Seed received, planted, remaining and returned, and implement the security measures required by the Specifications.

9.4 **Reconciliation and Return.** Within 60 days after the end of each Production Season, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a reconciliation of all Parent Seed received and used. Supplier shall return or, at Customer's written direction, destroy all unused Parent Seed and certify the return or destruction in writing.

9.5 **Germplasm Loss Events.** Supplier shall notify Customer within 24 hours after becoming aware of any loss, theft, diversion, unauthorized propagation or unauthorized transfer of any Braport Germplasm in the possession or control of Supplier or its subcontractors (each, a "**Germplasm Loss Event**"). Supplier shall cooperate fully with Customer to investigate and remedy the Germplasm Loss Event and to recover any diverted material.

9.6 **Equitable Relief.** Supplier acknowledges that a breach of this Article 9 would cause Customer irreparable harm for which damages would be an inadequate remedy, and that Customer is entitled to injunctive relief to prevent or restrain any such breach, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 **Customer Property.** All intellectual property rights in Braport Germplasm, Contract Seed, Customer's production protocols, hybrid designations and trademarks belong to Customer.

10.2 **Improvements.** Any improvement, selection or derivative of Braport Germplasm made by or for Supplier belongs to Customer upon creation, and Supplier hereby assigns all rights in it to Customer.

10.3 **Supplier Know-How.** Supplier retains ownership of its general agronomic and Conditioning know-how that does not incorporate Braport Germplasm or Customer's Confidential Information.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 **Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a binding obligation enforceable against it.

11.2 **Supplier Warranties.** Supplier warrants that: (a) all Contract Seed delivered will conform to the Specifications and be free from contamination by off-types and adventitious presence beyond the tolerances in the Specifications; (b) Contract Seed will be produced only from Parent Seed supplied by Customer; and (c) Supplier will perform its obligations in a good and workmanlike manner in accordance with good industry practice.

11.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

12.2 **Liability Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) Supplier's breach of Article 9, including any loss, diversion or unauthorized propagation of Parent Seed or other Braport Germplasm;

(b) a Party's indemnification obligations under Article 13;

(c) a Party's breach of Article 15; or

(d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

## 13. INDEMNIFICATION

13.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent resulting from: (a) Supplier's breach of this Agreement; (b) Supplier's negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Supplier's violation of applicable law.

13.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent resulting from: (a) any allegation that Braport Germplasm infringes a third party's intellectual property rights; or (b) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

13.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 Supplier shall maintain, during the term and for two years afterwards, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) crop and property insurance covering Parent Seed and Contract Seed in its care, custody and control at full replacement value; and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 15. CONFIDENTIALITY

15.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Braport Germplasm, production protocols, hybrid identities, volumes, pricing and field locations.

15.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to employees and approved subcontractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article 15.

15.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can show is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to it before disclosure, or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, after giving the disclosing Party prompt notice where lawful.

15.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Article 15 survive for five years after expiration or termination, and indefinitely for Braport Germplasm and trade secrets.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice of it.

16.2 **For Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

16.3 **For Germplasm Breach.** Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if a Germplasm Loss Event occurs that results from Supplier's breach of Article 9.

16.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination: (a) Supplier shall promptly deliver to Customer all Parent Seed, Contract Seed and Braport Germplasm in its possession or control, and all related field and chain-of-custody records; (b) Customer shall pay for all Contract Seed accepted before the termination date; and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information.

16.5 **Survival.** Articles 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15 and 19, Sections 6.5, 16.4 and 16.5, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 17. FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including drought, flood, hail, windstorm, fire, epidemic, war, government action or labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own workforce (a "**Force Majeure Event**"). The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party, use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects, and resume performance as soon as practicable. A Force Majeure Event does not excuse Supplier's obligations under Article 9 or any payment obligation for Contract Seed already accepted.

17.2 If a Force Majeure Event prevents Supplier from performing a material part of a Statement of Work for more than 60 days, Customer may terminate that Statement of Work by written notice without liability.

## 18. ASSIGNMENT

18.1 **By Customer.** Customer shall not assign or transfer this Agreement or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, without Supplier's prior written consent.

18.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, without the consent of Customer, on 30 days' written notice to Customer, including by merger, consolidation, change of control or sale of all or part of its seed production assets.

18.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Any purported assignment in violation of this Article 18 is void.

## 19. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

19.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

19.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

19.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than for injunctive relief, a Party will give the other Party written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of both Parties will meet within 15 Business Days after the notice to attempt in good faith to resolve it.

19.4 **Jury Waiver.** Each Party waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law, any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 20. GENERAL

20.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses each Party designates in writing. Notices are effective on receipt.

20.2 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

20.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and any Statement of Work may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

20.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

20.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
