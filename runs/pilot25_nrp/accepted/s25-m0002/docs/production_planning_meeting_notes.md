# PRODUCTION PLANNING MEETING NOTES: 2027 PRODUCTION SEASON

**Matter:** Braport Seed Genetics, Inc. / Traidir Seed Production, LLC
**Meeting date:** November 4, 2026
**Location:** Traidir Seed Production, LLC, 2410 Industrial Parkway, Ames, Iowa (some attendees by video)
**Prepared by:** Braport Seed Operations
**Status:** Internal working notes. Not a contract document.

## 1. Attendees

**Braport Seed Genetics, Inc. ("Braport")**
- Director, Seed Operations
- Manager, Seed Quality Assurance
- Manager, Germplasm Stewardship

**Traidir Seed Production, LLC ("Traidir")**
- General Manager, Production
- Quality Laboratory Supervisor
- Facility Security Manager

## 2. Purpose

This was the first joint planning meeting since the Supply Agreement became effective on October 1, 2026. The meeting walked through the timeline under Statement of Work No. 1 for the 2027 Production Season, confirmed responsibilities and agreed internal action items. No changes to the Supply Agreement, the Service Level Schedule or the SOW were proposed or agreed.

## 3. Production Scope and Volume

3.1 Both sides confirmed that the 2027 program covers hybrid corn seed from Braport parent lines, grown in Iowa and Illinois, as set out in SOW Section 1.1.

3.2 The target volume is 180,000 units of 80,000 kernels each, allocated as follows: BSG-4120, 70,000 Units; BSG-4385, 62,000 Units; BSG-4610, 48,000 Units.

3.3 Traidir confirmed that its yield history for the three female lines (BF-217, BF-231, BF-244) supports the minimum of 3,150 female-row acres. Traidir's General Manager expects planted acreage to track closely to the county table in SOW Section 4.1.

## 4. Production Fields

4.1 Traidir walked through the five counties: Story, Boone and Hamilton in Iowa, and Henry and Bureau in Illinois. Traidir has secured grower commitments on all planned fields. It does not expect to request any field substitution.

4.2 Traidir will deliver legal descriptions and GPS boundaries for each Production Field by February 15, 2027, as SOW Section 4.1 requires.

4.3 Braport's Germplasm Stewardship lead raised isolation on the Boone County fields, where BSG-4120 and BSG-4385 will be grown in the same county. Traidir confirmed that every field will meet the 660-foot minimum in Service Level Schedule Section 2.9. Traidir does not plan to seek any reduction for border rows or differential planting dates.

## 5. Parent Seed Delivery and Custody

5.1 Braport confirmed that all five parent lines remain on schedule for delivery to the Ames facility by March 15, 2027, in the quantities in SOW Section 2.2.

5.2 Traidir's Facility Security Manager described the segregated Parent Seed storage room. It has badge access, an electronic access log and continuous video surveillance. Traidir will send Braport the proposed access list for approval under Service Level Schedule Section 7.1.

5.3 Traidir will confirm each delivery in writing within two Business Days after receipt, with lot numbers, weights and condition, under SOW Section 2.3.

5.4 Both sides noted the 0.5% reconciliation tolerance by parent line in Service Level Schedule Section 4.6. They also noted that the Parent Seed reconciliation is due within 60 days after the end of the Production Season under Section 9.4 of the Supply Agreement.

## 6. Seed Quality and Testing

6.1 Braport's Quality Assurance lead reviewed the key Specifications: 95% warm germination, 88% cold germination, 98.0% genetic purity, 99.0% physical purity, moisture between 10.0% and 13.0%, and adventitious presence below 0.5%.

6.2 Traidir confirmed that warm germination, cold germination and physical purity tests will be run by a Certified Laboratory. Genetic purity marker work will be performed by a Certified Laboratory on Braport's behalf, consistent with Service Level Schedule Section 2.3. Traidir will not perform any marker analysis itself.

6.3 Traidir will retain a 1,000-gram file sample for each Lot for two years after delivery.

## 7. Treatment, Packaging and Delivery

7.1 Braport will designate the seed treatment package by February 1, 2027. Braport has not yet decided whether to supply the treatment materials itself or have Traidir purchase them at cost.

7.2 Braport will give the bulk box and single-Unit bag proportions by November 1, 2027.

7.3 The delivery plan is FCA the Ames facility for collection by Braport's carriers. Final delivery is due by January 15, 2028. Traidir will give five Business Days' notice that each Lot is ready for collection.

## 8. Reporting and Invoicing

8.1 Traidir will issue progress reports every two weeks from planting through final delivery.

8.2 Traidir will invoice on acceptance of each Lot. Braport Accounts Payable will be reminded that payment is due within 45 days from receipt of invoice, and that each invoice should show accrued and applied Service Credits.

8.3 Braport noted that it may request a grower advance of up to 20% of the estimated production fee on July 15, 2027, under SOW Section 7.3. No decision has been made.
