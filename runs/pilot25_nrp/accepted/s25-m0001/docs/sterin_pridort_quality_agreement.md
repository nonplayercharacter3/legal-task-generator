# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of June 1, 2025 (the "**QA Effective Date**") by and between **Pridort Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sterin Field Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. The Distribution Agreement provides for a quality agreement governing Product specifications, incoming inspection, nonconformance, corrective action, traceability and recalls. This Quality Agreement is that quality agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the respective quality responsibilities of Supplier and Distributor for the Products supplied under the Distribution Agreement.

1.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products listed in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement, including the AX-60 and AX-40 spray drones, the AX-M2 mapping drone, the AX-B60 intelligent flight battery and the AX-GS ground station and charging hub, and to all spare parts, payload modules and embedded software supplied for them.

1.3 **Relationship to Distribution Agreement.** This Quality Agreement supplements the Distribution Agreement. It is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in Section 1.8 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement, including "Business Day," "Customer," "Governmental Authority," "Products," "Purchase Order," "Recall," "Recall Costs," "Specifications" and "Territory."

2.2 "**CAPA**" means a corrective and preventive action under Section 7.4.

2.3 "**Certificate of Conformance**" means a document issued by Supplier for each Lot certifying that the Products in the Lot were manufactured, tested and released in accordance with the Specifications and this Quality Agreement.

2.4 "**Critical Nonconformity**" means a Nonconformity that creates, or could reasonably be expected to create, a risk of injury, property damage, loss of aircraft control, uncontrolled chemical discharge, battery thermal event or non-compliance with law.

2.5 "**Latent Nonconformity**" means a Nonconformity that could not reasonably be discovered through the incoming inspection procedures in Section 6.1.

2.6 "**Lot**" means a quantity of Products of a single model shipped under a single Supplier lot or shipment number.

2.7 "**Nonconformity**" means any failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications, to this Quality Agreement or to applicable law, or any defect in design, materials or workmanship.

2.8 "**Quality Contact**" means the individual designated by each Party under Section 3.3.

## 3. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

3.1 **Supplier.** Supplier shall maintain throughout the term of the Distribution Agreement a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 covering the design, manufacture, testing and release of the Products, and shall notify Distributor within ten (10) Business Days if that certification is suspended, withdrawn or not renewed.

3.2 **Distributor.** Distributor shall maintain documented procedures for receiving, inspection, storage, handling, field service and warranty administration of the Products that are consistent with Supplier's published handling and storage instructions, including the lithium battery storage and transport instructions for the AX-B60.

3.3 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact responsible for communications under this Quality Agreement. The initial Quality Contacts are set out in Schedule 1. Either Party may change its Quality Contact by written notice.

3.4 **Quality Reviews.** The Quality Contacts shall meet at least quarterly, in person or by video conference, to review incoming inspection results, field failure data, open CAPAs and any Recall activity.

## 4. SPECIFICATIONS AND CHANGE CONTROL

4.1 **Specifications.** Supplier shall manufacture each Product in conformance with its Specifications. Supplier shall provide Distributor with current technical data sheets, inspection criteria and acceptance standards for each Product.

4.2 **Change Notification.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of any change to a Product's form, fit, function, firmware, critical components, manufacturing site or sub-tier supplier of flight controllers, batteries, motors or nozzle flow-controller modules that could affect performance, safety, regulatory status or interchangeability of parts.

4.3 **Firmware Updates.** Supplier shall release firmware and FieldLink application updates with release notes describing changes affecting flight safety, spray application rates or data accuracy, and shall provide Distributor with the updates and release notes before general release to Customers.

4.4 **Regulatory Changes.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other of any change in law or Governmental Authority requirement known to it that affects the Specifications or the lawful sale or operation of the Products in the Territory.

## 5. MANUFACTURING, TESTING AND RELEASE

5.1 **Testing.** Supplier shall perform final functional testing of each spray and mapping drone, including flight controller calibration, nozzle flow-rate verification for spray models and sensor calibration for mapping models, before release.

5.2 **Battery Testing.** Supplier shall test each AX-B60 battery for capacity, cell balance and insulation resistance before shipment and shall ship batteries at a state of charge compliant with applicable transport regulations.

5.3 **Release Documentation.** Supplier shall deliver with each Lot a Certificate of Conformance, a packing list identifying each serial number in the Lot and a summary of final test results.

5.4 **Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier shall package and label the Products in accordance with the Specifications and applicable law, including hazardous materials labeling for batteries.

## 6. INCOMING INSPECTION AND REJECTION

6.1 **Incoming Inspection.** Distributor shall perform incoming inspection of each Lot in accordance with the inspection criteria and sampling plan provided by Supplier under Section 4.1, consisting of verification of quantities and serial numbers, review of the Certificate of Conformance, visual inspection for shipping damage and the functional checks set out in Schedule 2.

6.2 **Rejection Notice.** Distributor shall reject any Product or Lot having a Nonconformity discoverable at incoming inspection by written notice to Supplier's Quality Contact within ten (10) Business Days after delivery, describing the Nonconformity and identifying the affected serial numbers. Any Product not rejected within that period shall be deemed accepted, and Distributor waives all claims with respect to any Nonconformity in that Product that was discoverable at incoming inspection, including warranty claims under Section 9 of the Distribution Agreement.

6.3 **Supplier Response.** Supplier shall acknowledge each rejection notice within two (2) Business Days after receipt and, within ten (10) Business Days after receipt, shall either accept the rejection or provide a written explanation of its basis for disputing it.

6.4 **Rejected Products.** For each rejected Product accepted by Supplier as nonconforming, Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, repair or replace the Product within twenty (20) Business Days or credit Distributor the price paid, and shall bear all return freight and handling costs.

6.5 **Disputed Rejections.** If Supplier disputes a rejection, the Quality Contacts shall meet within five (5) Business Days to attempt to resolve the dispute. Either Party may refer an unresolved dispute to an independent testing laboratory reasonably acceptable to both Parties, whose findings on conformance are binding on the Parties for purposes of this Section 6. The non-prevailing Party shall bear the laboratory's fees.

## 7. NONCONFORMANCE AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

7.1 **Latent Nonconformities.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any Latent Nonconformity within thirty (30) days after Distributor becomes aware of it. Claims for Latent Nonconformities are governed by Section 9 of the Distribution Agreement.

7.2 **Field Failure Reporting.** Distributor shall report to Supplier monthly all field failures, Customer complaints and warranty claims relating to the Products, using the reporting template in Schedule 2.

7.3 **Critical Nonconformities.** Each Party shall notify the other by telephone and email within twenty-four (24) hours after becoming aware of any actual or suspected Critical Nonconformity, any incident involving injury or property damage, or any inquiry by a Governmental Authority concerning the Products.

7.4 **CAPA.** For any Critical Nonconformity, any recurring Nonconformity, or any Lot in which more than two percent (2%) of units are rejected, Supplier shall conduct a root-cause investigation and deliver to Distributor a written CAPA plan within twenty (20) Business Days after notice, and shall implement the CAPA plan within the time stated in it.

7.5 **Returned Product Analysis.** Supplier shall perform failure analysis on returned Products as reasonably requested by Distributor and shall share the results with Distributor within thirty (30) days after receipt of the returned Product.

## 8. TRACEABILITY

8.1 **Supplier Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain records linking each Product serial number to its Lot, manufacturing date, firmware version, final test results and the lot numbers of its batteries, flight controllers and nozzle flow-controller modules.

8.2 **Distributor Traceability.** Distributor shall maintain the records required by Section 7.4 of the Distribution Agreement and shall provide them to Supplier within two (2) Business Days after request in connection with any Recall or Critical Nonconformity.

## 9. RECALLS

9.1 **Recall Decision.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other if it believes a Recall may be required. The Parties shall consult in good faith before initiating any voluntary Recall. If a Governmental Authority requires a Recall, or if either Party reasonably determines that a Recall is necessary to address a safety risk, that Party may initiate the Recall after notice to the other.

9.2 **Conduct of Recall.** Supplier shall prepare the Recall strategy, Customer communications and technical remedy. Distributor shall, using its records under Section 8.2, notify affected Customers in the Territory, coordinate retrieval or field correction of affected Products and report Recall progress to Supplier weekly until the Recall is closed.

9.3 **Regulatory Communications.** Supplier shall be responsible for communications with Governmental Authorities concerning any Recall, and shall provide Distributor with copies of all material submissions and correspondence.

9.4 **Recall Cost Reimbursement.** Supplier shall reimburse Distributor for Recall Costs in accordance with Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement within thirty (30) days after receipt of Distributor's invoice and reasonable supporting documentation. Supplier's aggregate liability for Recall Costs is subject to the limitation set forth in Section 12.1 of the Distribution Agreement, and all amounts paid or payable by Supplier in respect of Recall Costs shall be counted toward that limitation.

9.5 **Mock Recall.** At either Party's request, not more than once per contract year, the Parties shall conduct a mock Recall exercise to test traceability and Customer notification procedures.

## 10. AUDITS

10.1 **Distributor Audit Right.** Distributor may, on at least fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice and not more than once per contract year, audit Supplier's manufacturing facility and quality records relating to the Products during normal business hours. Distributor may conduct additional for-cause audits following any Critical Nonconformity or Recall.

10.2 **Supplier Audit Right.** Supplier may, on the same terms, audit Distributor's receiving, storage, service and traceability practices relating to the Products.

10.3 **Audit Findings.** The audited Party shall respond in writing to any audit findings within twenty (20) Business Days and shall implement agreed corrective actions within the time agreed by the Quality Contacts.

10.4 **Confidentiality.** Information obtained in an audit is Confidential Information of the audited Party under Section 15 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 11. RECORDS

11.1 Each Party shall retain all records required by this Quality Agreement for at least five (5) years after the date of the last sale of the Product to which they relate, and shall make them available to the other Party on reasonable request.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 This Quality Agreement takes effect on the QA Effective Date and remains in effect for so long as the Distribution Agreement is in effect and thereafter for so long as either Party has obligations under Section 16 of the Distribution Agreement in respect of Products.

12.2 Sections 7, 8, 9 and 11 survive termination or expiration of this Quality Agreement with respect to Products delivered before termination or expiration.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 19 of the Distribution Agreement, with a copy to the receiving Party's Quality Contact. Operational communications between Quality Contacts may be given by email.

13.2 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Sections 20.1 and 20.2 of the Distribution Agreement apply to this Quality Agreement.

13.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Quality Contacts may update Schedules 1 and 2 by written agreement signed by both of them.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
