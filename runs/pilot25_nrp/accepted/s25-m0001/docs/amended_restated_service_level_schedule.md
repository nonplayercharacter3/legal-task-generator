# AMENDED AND RESTATED SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Schedule 2 to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025**

This Amended and Restated Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of October 20, 2026 (the "**Restatement Date**") by and between **Pridort Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sterin Field Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. BACKGROUND AND RESTATEMENT

1.1 **Distribution Agreement.** Supplier and Distributor are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Section 1.11 of the Distribution Agreement defines the Service Level Schedule as the schedule attached as Schedule 2, as amended or amended and restated by the Parties from time to time.

1.2 **Amendment and Restatement.** As of the Restatement Date, this Schedule amends and restates in its entirety the Service Level Schedule attached to the Distribution Agreement on its execution (the "**Original Schedule**"). Service credits accrued under the Original Schedule for periods before the Restatement Date remain payable as calculated under the Original Schedule.

1.3 **Purpose.** This Schedule sets out the lead times, fill-rate commitments and delivery performance measures referred to in Sections 4.4 and 6.3 of the Distribution Agreement, the second-line support, parts and training referred to in Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement, and certain operational matters agreed by the Parties as of the Restatement Date.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement or, where applicable, the Quality Agreement, including "Business Day," "Customer," "Lot," "Nonconformity," "Products," "Purchase Order," "Quality Agreement" and "Territory."

2.2 "**Accepted Purchase Order**" means a Purchase Order accepted or deemed accepted under Section 4.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

2.3 "**Committed Ship Date**" means the ship date for each line item of an Accepted Purchase Order determined under Section 3.2.

2.4 "**Dealer Portal**" means Supplier's web-based ordering, warranty registration, parts lookup and support ticketing system made available to Distributor.

2.5 "**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month.

2.6 "**Peak Season**" means the period from March 1 through July 31 of each calendar year.

2.7 "**Support Ticket**" means a request for second-line technical support submitted by Distributor through the Dealer Portal or the support hotline listed in Annex A.

## 3. LEAD TIMES AND ORDER ACKNOWLEDGMENT

3.1 **Standard Lead Times.** Supplier shall ship Products within the following lead times after the date the Purchase Order is accepted:

| Product | Standard Lead Time | Peak Season Lead Time |
|---|---|---|
| AX-60 spray drone | 30 days | 45 days |
| AX-40 spray drone | 30 days | 45 days |
| AX-M2 mapping drone | 21 days | 30 days |
| AX-B60 intelligent flight battery | 14 days | 21 days |
| AX-GS ground station and charging hub | 21 days | 30 days |
| Spare parts (Annex A, Part 3) | 3 Business Days | 3 Business Days |

3.2 **Committed Ship Date.** The Committed Ship Date for each line item is the later of (a) the requested delivery date stated in the Purchase Order, less reasonable transit time, and (b) the end of the applicable lead time in Section 3.1. Supplier shall state the Committed Ship Date in its acceptance of each Purchase Order.

3.3 **Forecast Reliance.** Supplier shall plan production capacity against Distributor's rolling forecasts delivered under Section 4.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Quantities ordered for Peak Season that exceed one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the quantity forecast for the relevant month in the forecast delivered at least ninety (90) days earlier are excluded from the fill-rate measure in Section 4.1, but Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ship them by the Committed Ship Date.

3.4 **Expedited Orders.** Distributor may designate up to ten percent (10%) of the units ordered in any month as expedited. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ship expedited units within half of the applicable lead time, and Distributor shall bear any incremental freight cost for expedited shipments.

## 4. DELIVERY PERFORMANCE

4.1 **On-Time Fill Rate.** Supplier shall achieve, for each Measurement Period, an on-time fill rate of at least ninety-five percent (95%), calculated as the number of units shipped complete on or before the Committed Ship Date divided by the number of units with a Committed Ship Date in that Measurement Period.

4.2 **Spare Parts Availability.** Supplier shall ship at least ninety-eight percent (98%) of spare parts order lines within the lead time in Section 3.1 in each Measurement Period.

4.3 **Exclusions.** Delays caused by Distributor's failure to designate a carrier, by changes to a Purchase Order requested by Distributor after acceptance, or by a force majeure event under Section 18 of the Distribution Agreement are excluded from the measures in Sections 4.1 and 4.2.

4.4 **Reporting.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, Supplier shall deliver to Distributor a report of on-time fill rate, spare parts availability, open backorders and Support Ticket performance for that Measurement Period.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 **Calculation.** If Supplier fails to meet a service level in Section 4.1 or 4.2 for any Measurement Period, Supplier shall issue to Distributor the service credit set out in Annex A, Part 2.

5.2 **Application.** Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice issued after the monthly report under Section 4.4. Any service credit outstanding on termination or expiration of the Distribution Agreement shall be paid to Distributor within thirty (30) days.

5.3 **Monthly Maximum.** The total service credits for any Measurement Period shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the amounts invoiced by Supplier for Products shipped in that Measurement Period.

5.4 **Other Rights.** Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting reduced service value. They do not limit Distributor's other rights and remedies under the Distribution Agreement in respect of the same failure, except that any damages recovered for a late delivery shall be reduced by the service credits issued for it.

5.5 **Chronic Failure.** If Supplier fails to meet the service level in Section 4.1 in any three (3) Measurement Periods within any rolling twelve (12) month period, the failure is a material breach of the Distribution Agreement for purposes of Section 16.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 6. SECOND-LINE TECHNICAL SUPPORT

6.1 **Scope.** Distributor provides first-line technical support to Customers under Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier shall provide second-line support to Distributor's trained service personnel, covering diagnosis of flight controller, propulsion, spray system, battery, sensor and firmware issues that Distributor cannot resolve at first line.

6.2 **Hours.** Supplier shall staff the support hotline and respond to Dealer Portal tickets from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central Time on Business Days and, during Peak Season, from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central Time on Saturdays.

6.3 **Response and Resolution Targets.**

| Severity | Description | Initial Response | Target Workaround or Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | Aircraft grounded fleet-wide at a Customer, safety risk, or suspected Critical Nonconformity | 1 hour | 1 Business Day |
| Severity 2 | Single aircraft grounded or spray application rate outside Specifications | 4 business hours | 3 Business Days |
| Severity 3 | Degraded function, FieldLink data or reporting issue | 1 Business Day | 10 Business Days |
| Severity 4 | General inquiry or documentation request | 2 Business Days | As agreed |

6.4 **Critical Nonconformities.** The response targets in Section 6.3 do not replace the notification obligations in Section 7.3 of the Quality Agreement.

## 7. SPARE PARTS AND TRAINING

7.1 **Parts Stocking.** Supplier shall maintain at its Fort Collins, Colorado facility a stock of the spare parts listed in Annex A, Part 3 sufficient to meet the service level in Section 4.2.

7.2 **Distributor Parts Inventory.** Distributor shall maintain at its West Des Moines, Iowa facility the recommended initial spare parts inventory published by Supplier for the installed fleet in the Territory, updated by Supplier not more than twice per contract year.

7.3 **Training.** Supplier shall provide, at no charge, (a) one five-day certified technician course per contract year for up to eight (8) Distributor personnel at Supplier's facility or Distributor's facility, at Distributor's election, and (b) online update training within thirty (30) days after the release of any new Product or any firmware update affecting flight safety or spray application rates. Distributor shall bear its personnel's travel expenses.

7.4 **Certification.** Distributor shall ensure that each person performing warranty service on the Products holds a current Supplier technician certification.

## 8. DISTRIBUTOR CORPORATE TRANSACTION; DEALER PORTAL TRANSITION

8.1 **Pending Transaction.** Distributor has informed Supplier that Sterin Holdings, Inc., the parent of Distributor, entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated September 22, 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**") with Kestrova Agritech, Inc. ("**Kestrova**"), under which Sterin Holdings, Inc. will sell one hundred percent (100%) of the equity of Distributor to Kestrova. Closing under the Stock Purchase Agreement is expected to occur on January 15, 2027.

8.2 **Information.** Distributor shall keep Supplier reasonably informed of the status of the transaction described in Section 8.1, including any change to the expected closing date, and shall give Supplier written notice of closing within two (2) Business Days after it occurs.

8.3 **Dealer Portal Transition.** At Distributor's written request, Supplier shall provide reasonable support for the migration of Distributor's Dealer Portal user accounts, order history, warranty registrations and Support Ticket history to new user credentials and, where technically feasible, integration with Kestrova's enterprise resource planning system, at no charge for up to forty (40) hours of Supplier personnel time. Nothing in this Section 8 constitutes Supplier's consent under Section 17.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 9. GOVERNANCE

9.1 **Monthly Operations Review.** The Parties' operations leads shall meet monthly by video conference to review the report under Section 4.4, open backorders, Support Ticket trends and the open matters listed in Annex B.

9.2 **Annual Review.** The Parties shall review the service levels in this Schedule at least once per contract year and may agree adjusted service levels for the following contract year by written amendment under Section 10.2.

9.3 **Escalation.** Any operational matter not resolved at the monthly review within thirty (30) days shall be escalated to Supplier's Vice President of Sales and Distributor's President. This Section 9.3 does not affect the dispute procedures in Section 6.5 of the Quality Agreement.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Distribution Agreement.** Except as amended and restated by this Schedule, the Distribution Agreement remains in full force and effect. This Schedule is Schedule 2 to the Distribution Agreement and forms part of it.

10.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

10.3 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Sections 20.1 and 20.2 of the Distribution Agreement apply to this Schedule.

10.4 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
