# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 18, 2024 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Pridort Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Pridort**"), and **Sterin Field Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Sterin**"). Pridort and Sterin are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Pridort designs, manufactures and sells the AX-series line of precision agriculture spray and mapping drones and related batteries, payload modules, ground equipment and software.

B. Sterin markets, sells and services agricultural equipment to growers, cooperatives and custom applicators in the Midwestern United States.

C. The Parties wish to exchange certain confidential information to evaluate and negotiate a potential distribution relationship for the AX-series products in the Midwestern United States (the "**Purpose**").

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this definition, "control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, financial and commercial information that one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Representatives disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, on or after the NDA Effective Date. The disclosure may be in writing, orally, visually, electronically or by inspection of facilities or equipment. Confidential Information includes product designs, specifications, firmware, test data, pricing, cost data, margins, sales volumes, forecasts, customer and dealer lists, marketing plans and the terms of any proposal exchanged by the Parties. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, managers, employees, attorneys, accountants, financial advisers and lenders.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by competent evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was known to the Receiving Party, without an obligation of confidentiality, before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or

(d) is received by the Receiving Party from a third party that is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, under a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

3.2 **Nondisclosure.** The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence. It shall disclose that information only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.4 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Receiving Party shall not disassemble, decompile or reverse engineer any demonstration unit, prototype, sample, firmware or software provided by the Disclosing Party, or analyze its composition, except as the Disclosing Party authorizes in writing.

3.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit the consequences.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where lawful, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice. This allows the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other remedy, and the Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably, at the Disclosing Party's expense, with those efforts. The Receiving Party may then disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for it.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall confirm in writing that it has done so. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

5.2 The Receiving Party may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by law or its bona fide document retention policies, and copies held in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY

6.1 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 **No Warranty.** CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

7.1 This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction, or to disclose any particular information. Either Party may end discussions concerning the Purpose at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other.

7.2 Unless and until the Parties enter into a definitive written agreement, neither Party has any obligation to the other concerning the Purpose, except for the obligations expressly set out in this Agreement.

7.3 Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from independently developing, acquiring or marketing products or services that are similar to or compete with those of the other Party, provided it does not use the other Party's Confidential Information in doing so.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

8.1 For twelve (12) months after the NDA Effective Date, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact in connection with the Purpose. General solicitations not targeted at the other Party's employees, such as job postings and recruiter searches, do not breach this Section 8.1. Neither does hiring any person who responds to them.

## 9. TERM

9.1 **Disclosure Period.** This Agreement applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period beginning on the NDA Effective Date and ending two (2) years after the NDA Effective Date. Either Party may end that period earlier by written notice to the other.

9.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period under Section 9.1. For Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations continue for as long as it remains a trade secret.

## 10. REMEDIES

10.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or restrain any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts. Either Party may, however, seek equitable relief under Section 10.1 in any court of competent jurisdiction.

12.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, that agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

12.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

12.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

12.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
