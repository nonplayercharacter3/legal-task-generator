# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 3, 2025 by **Pridort Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Sterin Field Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**").

## RECITALS

A. Pridort Aerial Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor.

B. Supplier and Distributor have entered into an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025 (as amended from time to time, the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the AX-series Products in the Territory.

C. Section 14.1 of the Distribution Agreement requires Supplier to cause Guarantor to deliver this Guaranty to Distributor concurrently with the execution of the Distribution Agreement.

D. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Distribution Agreement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Documents**" means the following documents, each as amended, supplemented or amended and restated from time to time in accordance with its terms:

(a) the Distribution Agreement;

(b) the Quality Agreement;

(c) the Service Level Schedule; and

(d) each Purchase Order accepted by Supplier.

1.3 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Distributor, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, two things:

(a) the full and punctual payment when due of all amounts payable by Supplier to Distributor under the Guaranteed Documents; and

(b) the full and punctual performance of all other obligations of Supplier to Distributor under the Guaranteed Documents.

Amounts payable under clause (a) include warranty credits, Recall Costs, service credits, indemnification payments and repurchase amounts. Together, the obligations in clauses (a) and (b) are the "**Guaranteed Obligations**."

2.2 **Extent of Guaranteed Obligations.** The Guaranteed Obligations are the obligations of Supplier as they exist under, and as limited by, the Guaranteed Documents read together in accordance with their terms. Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed the liability that Supplier would have to Distributor under the Guaranteed Documents for the same matter. Every limitation, exclusion and defense available to Supplier under the Guaranteed Documents is available to Guarantor, except the defenses described in Section 4.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not of collection. Distributor is not required to take any of the following steps before demanding payment or performance from Guarantor:

(a) proceed first against Supplier;

(b) exhaust any remedy against Supplier; or

(c) resort to any security or other credit support.

2.4 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations arising during the Term of the Distribution Agreement. It also applies to all Guaranteed Obligations that survive termination or expiration of the Distribution Agreement, including under Sections 9, 11, 16.3 and 16.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 3. DEMAND AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Demand.** If Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Distributor may deliver a written demand to Guarantor. The demand shall describe the failure in reasonable detail. Guarantor shall pay the amount demanded, or cause the obligation to be performed, within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of the demand.

3.2 **Payments.** All payments by Guarantor shall be made in United States dollars in immediately available funds, to the account Distributor designates in writing. Payments shall be made without set-off or counterclaim, except for set-offs and counterclaims that Supplier would be entitled to assert under the Guaranteed Documents.

3.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** Guarantor shall reimburse Distributor for the reasonable attorneys' fees and costs Distributor incurs in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

## 4. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS; WAIVERS

4.1 **Obligations Unconditional.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by any of the following:

(a) any amendment, supplement or amendment and restatement of any Guaranteed Document, or any waiver, consent or extension granted under it;

(b) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization, dissolution or similar proceeding of Supplier, or any stay or discharge of Supplier's obligations in that proceeding;

(c) any change in the ownership, corporate structure or existence of Supplier or Guarantor, including any assignment by Supplier permitted under Section 17.1 of the Distribution Agreement;

(d) any lack of authority of Supplier, or any invalidity or unenforceability of the Guaranteed Obligations arising from Supplier's lack of capacity; or

(e) any failure or delay by Distributor in exercising any right against Supplier or Guarantor.

4.2 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives the following:

(a) diligence, presentment, protest and notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) notice of the creation, renewal or extension of any Guaranteed Obligation, other than the demand required by Section 3.1; and

(c) any defense based on suretyship or impairment of collateral.

4.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment by Supplier or Guarantor of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned on the insolvency or bankruptcy of Supplier, this Guaranty shall be reinstated for that payment as though it had not been made.

4.4 **Subrogation.** Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution or reimbursement against Supplier until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid or performed in full.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Distributor as of the date of this Guaranty as follows:

(a) **Organization.** Guarantor is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

(b) **Authority.** Guarantor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty. Its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action.

(c) **Enforceability.** This Guaranty is the legal, valid and binding obligation of Guarantor, enforceable in accordance with its terms. This is subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally, and to general principles of equity.

(d) **No Conflict.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate Guarantor's certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any applicable law, or any material agreement to which Guarantor is a party.

(e) **Ownership.** Guarantor directly owns one hundred percent (100%) of the outstanding equity of Supplier.

(f) **Solvency.** Guarantor is solvent and will not be rendered insolvent by entering into this Guaranty.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Financial Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Guarantor shall deliver its annual consolidated financial statements to Distributor on request, so long as any Guaranteed Obligation may arise. Those statements are Confidential Information of Guarantor, and Distributor shall treat them in accordance with Section 15 of the Distribution Agreement as if Guarantor were a Party.

6.2 **Notice of Change.** Guarantor shall give Distributor written notice within thirty (30) days after it ceases to Control Supplier.

## 7. TERM

7.1 This Guaranty remains in full force until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full. This includes all Guaranteed Obligations that survive termination or expiration of the Distribution Agreement.

## 8. ASSIGNMENT

8.1 Distributor may assign its rights under this Guaranty only to a permitted assignee of the Distribution Agreement under Section 17 of the Distribution Agreement.

8.2 Guarantor may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Guaranty without Distributor's prior written consent.

8.3 This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and assigns, and benefits Distributor and its permitted successors and assigns.
