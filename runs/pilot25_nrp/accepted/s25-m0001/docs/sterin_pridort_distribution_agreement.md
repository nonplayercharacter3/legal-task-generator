# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Pridort Aerial Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Sterin Field Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells the AX-series line of precision agriculture spray and mapping drones and related batteries, payload modules, ground equipment and software.

B. Distributor markets, sells and services agricultural equipment to growers, cooperatives and custom applicators in the Midwestern United States.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with a Party.

1.2 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

1.3 "**Control**" (and its correlative forms "**Controlling**" and "**Controlled**") means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether by contract, ownership of voting securities or otherwise.

1.4 "**Customer**" means any end user or reseller that purchases Products from Distributor in the Territory.

1.5 "**Losses**" means all losses, liabilities, damages, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, and, for the purposes of Section 11, includes lost profits and direct losses whether or not arising out of a third-party claim.

1.6 "**Products**" means the AX-series products listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.7 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.8 "**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between Supplier and Distributor governing Product specifications, incoming inspection, nonconformance, corrective action, traceability and recalls, as entered into by the Parties and as amended from time to time.

1.9 "**Recall**" means any recall, field correction, market withdrawal, stop-sale or safety notice relating to the Products, whether voluntary or required by a Governmental Authority.

1.10 "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local governmental, regulatory or administrative body, including the Federal Aviation Administration and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.

1.11 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule attached as Schedule 2, as amended or amended and restated by the Parties from time to time.

1.12 "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications for each Product set out in Exhibit A and the Quality Agreement.

1.13 "**Territory**" means the States of Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota and Wisconsin.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory for the Term, and Distributor accepts the appointment.

2.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, appoint any other distributor, dealer or agent for the Products in the Territory or sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except for sales to national accounts listed in Exhibit C.

2.3 **Distributor Restrictions.** Distributor shall not actively solicit sales of the Products outside the Territory, and shall not, without Supplier's prior written consent, market or sell any agricultural spray or mapping drone that competes directly with the Products.

2.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, fiduciary or agency relationship between them.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years (the "**Initial Term**").

3.2 **Renewal.** This Agreement renews automatically for successive two (2) year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. ORDERS AND MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENTS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders specifying Product, quantity, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) Business Days after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.2 **Forecasts.** Distributor shall provide Supplier with a rolling twelve (12) month non-binding forecast of Product requirements on or before the fifth Business Day of each calendar quarter.

4.3 **Minimum Purchase Commitments.** Distributor shall purchase during each contract year at least the minimum quantities of Products set out in Exhibit D (the "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**"). If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Commitment for any contract year, Supplier may, as its sole right under this Section 4.3 on written notice given within ninety (90) days after the end of that contract year, convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive for the remainder of the Term.

4.4 **Supply Obligation.** Supplier shall supply all Products ordered under accepted Purchase Orders in accordance with the lead times and fill-rate commitments in the Service Level Schedule.

4.5 **Purchase Order Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit B. Supplier may adjust prices once per contract year on not less than ninety (90) days' written notice, and no adjustment shall exceed five percent (5%) or apply to Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of the adjustment.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice upon shipment.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Distributor may withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith by written notice describing the dispute in reasonable detail.

5.4 **Taxes.** Prices are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes. Distributor shall provide Supplier with valid resale certificates for each state in the Territory.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in Fort Collins, Colorado (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier designated by Distributor, for shipment to the delivery location stated in each Purchase Order.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

6.3 **Late Delivery.** Supplier's delivery performance is measured, and service credits for late delivery are calculated, under the Service Level Schedule.

## 7. MARKETING AND DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Marketing.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory and shall maintain a trained sales and service staff.

7.2 **Service and Support.** Distributor shall provide first-line technical support, field service and warranty administration to Customers in the Territory. Supplier shall provide second-line support, parts and training as set out in the Service Level Schedule.

7.3 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all applicable laws in marketing and reselling the Products, including laws regarding pesticide application equipment, unmanned aircraft operation and export control.

7.4 **Records.** Distributor shall maintain records of the serial numbers, Customers and delivery dates of all Products it sells, sufficient to enable traceability under the Quality Agreement, and shall retain those records for at least five (5) years after sale.

## 8. INSPECTION AND QUALITY

8.1 **Incoming Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect Products upon receipt in accordance with the incoming inspection procedures of the Quality Agreement, and shall give notice of any rejection in the manner and within the period stated in the Quality Agreement.

8.2 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 and shall comply with the Quality Agreement.

8.3 **Regulatory Status.** Supplier shall maintain, at its cost, all Governmental Authority approvals, registrations and exemptions required for the lawful sale and operation of the Products in the Territory.

## 9. WARRANTIES AND RECALLS

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that each Product will, for twelve (12) months after delivery, (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship, and (c) comply with all applicable laws.

9.2 **Title and Non-Infringement.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products free of liens and that the Products and their use as intended do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

9.3 **Warranty Remedies.** For any Product that fails to comply with Section 9.1, Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, repair or replace the Product or credit Distributor the price paid for it, and shall bear all freight and handling costs.

9.4 **Pass-Through Warranty.** Distributor may extend to Customers the end-user warranty published by Supplier in Exhibit E, and Supplier shall honor that warranty directly to Customers.

9.5 **Recalls.** If a Recall is required by a Governmental Authority or is reasonably determined by either Party to be necessary because of a defect, nonconformity or safety risk in any Product, the Parties shall cooperate to carry out the Recall in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Except to the extent the Recall is caused by Distributor's modification or mishandling of the Products, Supplier shall reimburse Distributor for all reasonable and documented costs incurred in conducting the Recall, including Customer notification, retrieval, shipping, field labor, replacement Products and parts, and refunds or credits to Customers ("**Recall Costs**").

9.6 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 **Trademark License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks listed in Exhibit F in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines.

10.2 **Software.** Embedded software and the Pridort FieldLink application are licensed to Customers under Supplier's end-user license terms. Distributor acquires no ownership of any Supplier intellectual property.

10.3 **Reservation.** All rights not expressly granted are reserved to Supplier.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim alleging that any Product, or its use in accordance with the Specifications, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret.

11.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Supplier Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any breach by Distributor of this Agreement, (b) Distributor's marketing, promotion and resale of the Products, including any representation or warranty made by Distributor to a Customer that is not authorized by Supplier, and (c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Distributor or its personnel.

11.3 **Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any third-party claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an admission of fault or a non-monetary obligation on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Cap.** Except as set out in Section 12.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable by Distributor in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as set out in Section 12.3, neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to:

(a) a Party's obligations under Section 11;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 15 (Confidentiality);

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Supplier's recall cost reimbursement under Section 9.5, which is excluded from the Section 12.1 cap.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 Supplier shall maintain during the Term and for three (3) years after it commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence, aviation liability insurance covering the Products of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence, and product recall insurance of at least $2,000,000. Distributor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence.

13.2 Each Party shall name the other as an additional insured on its liability policies and provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 14. PARENT GUARANTY

14.1 Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, Supplier shall cause Pridort Holdings, Inc. to deliver to Distributor a guaranty of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement in form reasonably satisfactory to Distributor.

## 15. CONFIDENTIALITY

15.1 **Obligation.** Each Party shall hold the other Party's non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**") in confidence, use it only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers and financing sources who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section 15.

15.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available without breach, was known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

15.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 15 survive for three (3) years after termination or expiration, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it, except as provided in Section 17.2.

16.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

16.3 **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration, (a) Supplier shall fulfill all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination unless terminated by Supplier under Section 16.1, 16.2 or 17.2, (b) Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory for one hundred eighty (180) days, and (c) Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, repurchase Distributor's unsold, undamaged inventory at the price paid.

16.4 **Continuing Support.** Supplier shall make spare parts and second-line technical support available for the Products for at least five (5) years after termination or expiration.

16.5 **Survival.** Sections 9, 11, 12, 15, 16.3, 16.4 and 20, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive termination or expiration.

## 17. ASSIGNMENT

17.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Supplier may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business relating to the Products that assumes Supplier's obligations in writing. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 17 is void.

17.2 **Change of Control of Distributor.** Any Change of Control of Distributor shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Distributor requiring Supplier's prior written consent under Section 17.1. "**Change of Control**" means (a) any transaction or series of related transactions by which any person or group acquires, directly or indirectly, more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of Distributor or of any entity Controlling Distributor, (b) any merger, consolidation or reorganization of Distributor or any entity Controlling Distributor following which the holders of its voting equity immediately before the transaction hold fifty percent (50%) or less of the voting equity of the surviving entity, or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of Distributor's assets. A Change of Control without Supplier's prior written consent is a material breach of this Agreement, and Supplier may, on written notice, immediately terminate this Agreement and suspend shipments of Products under any Purchase Order, without opportunity to cure.

17.3 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 18. FORCE MAJEURE

18.1 Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, labor disputes (other than those of the affected Party's own workforce) or governmental action, provided it gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, the unaffected Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice.

18.2 During any period in which Supplier's capacity to supply is reduced by a force majeure event, Supplier shall allocate available Products to Distributor no less favorably than to any other customer.

In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, they shall prevail in the following order of precedence: Quality Agreement (quality, nonconformance and recall matters only), then Distribution Agreement, then Service Level Schedule, then Purchase Orders.
