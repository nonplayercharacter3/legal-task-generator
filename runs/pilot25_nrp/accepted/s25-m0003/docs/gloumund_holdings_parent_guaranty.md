# GUARANTY

This Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of January 12, 2026 by **Gloumund Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 275 Doremus Avenue, Newark, New Jersey 07105 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Droupourt Chemical Corporation**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 4100 Riverbend Industrial Parkway, Baton Rouge, Louisiana 70805 ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Gloumund Water Solutions, LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Distributor**"), have entered into a Distribution Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Distribution Agreement**").

B. Distributor is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the Distribution Agreement.

C. Section 16 of the Distribution Agreement requires Guarantor to deliver this Guaranty to Supplier on or before the effective date of the Distribution Agreement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "**Products**" means all products sold by Supplier or any of its Affiliates to Distributor or any of its Affiliates, together with all related packaging, totes, drums, deposits, freight and services.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all present and future payment obligations of Distributor and its Affiliates for Products. These include the purchase price of Products, invoiced charges, tote deposits, taxes payable by the purchaser, late charges and other amounts payable for Products, whether arising under the Distribution Agreement, any Purchase Order or otherwise, and whether due, not yet due, absolute or contingent.

1.3 **Defined Terms.** "**Affiliate**" and "**Purchase Order**" have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement. Terms defined in this Guaranty control for purposes of this Guaranty, notwithstanding any different definition in the Distribution Agreement.

1.4 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Supplier the full and punctual payment of the Guaranteed Obligations when due, whether at maturity, by acceleration or otherwise.

2.2 **Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. Supplier is not required to proceed against Distributor or any other person, to enforce any security, or to pursue any other remedy before making a demand on Guarantor.

2.3 **Demand and Payment.** If any Guaranteed Obligation is not paid when due, Guarantor shall pay that amount to Supplier within ten (10) business days after receipt of Supplier's written demand. Each demand shall identify the unpaid invoices or other amounts and the date each became due. Guarantor shall make payments in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, without set-off or counterclaim.

2.4 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations, whenever incurred, until it terminates under Section 9.

## 3. MAXIMUM LIABILITY

3.1 Guarantor's aggregate liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed $2,000,000.

3.2 Each payment made by Guarantor under this Guaranty reduces the amount available under Section 3.1 by the amount of that payment. Payments made by Distributor or any other person do not reduce the amount available under Section 3.1, except to the extent they are applied to amounts that Guarantor has already paid.

## 4. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Obligations Not Affected.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or reduced by:

(a) any amendment, extension, renewal or modification of the Distribution Agreement or any Purchase Order;

(b) any extension of time for payment or other indulgence granted to Distributor;

(c) any release, compromise or settlement of any Guaranteed Obligation, other than payment in full;

(d) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Distributor; or

(e) any insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Distributor.

4.2 **Defenses.** Guarantor may assert any defense to payment that Distributor could assert under the terms of the applicable sale, other than defenses arising from Distributor's insolvency, bankruptcy, lack of capacity or lack of authority.

4.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Supplier in any insolvency or bankruptcy proceeding of Distributor, this Guaranty is reinstated as to that payment as though it had not been made. Any reinstated amount remains subject to Section 3.

## 5. WAIVERS

5.1 Guarantor waives:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor;

(c) notice of the incurrence of any Guaranteed Obligation; and

(d) any requirement that Supplier exhaust its remedies against Distributor before demanding payment from Guarantor.

5.2 Guarantor does not waive the demand required by Section 2.3.

## 6. SUBROGATION

6.1 Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement or contribution against Distributor for amounts paid under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full. After that time, Guarantor is subrogated to Supplier's rights against Distributor to the extent of Guarantor's payments.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Guarantor represents and warrants to Supplier that, as of the date of this Guaranty:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(c) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action and do not violate its organizational documents, any law that applies to it, or any material agreement to which it is a party;

(d) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity; and

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the membership interests in Distributor.

## 8. FINANCIAL INFORMATION

8.1 Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier, on Supplier's written request, a copy of its annual consolidated financial statements. Supplier shall treat those statements as confidential and shall use them only to evaluate Guarantor's credit under this Guaranty.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Guaranty remains in effect until the later of:

(a) the expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement; and

(b) the payment in full of all Guaranteed Obligations incurred on or before that expiration or termination.

9.2 Guarantor may terminate this Guaranty as to Guaranteed Obligations incurred after the effective date of termination by giving Supplier at least ninety (90) days' written notice. Termination under this Section 9.2 does not affect Guarantor's liability for Guaranteed Obligations incurred before the effective date of termination. If Guarantor gives notice under this Section 9.2, Distributor shall be in breach of Section 16.1 of the Distribution Agreement unless a replacement guaranty reasonably acceptable to Supplier is delivered before the termination takes effect.
