# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Droupourt Chemical Corporation**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 4100 Riverbend Industrial Parkway, Baton Rouge, Louisiana 70805 ("**Supplier**"), and **Gloumund Water Solutions, LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal office at 275 Doremus Avenue, Newark, New Jersey 07105 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and packages coagulant chemicals used in municipal and industrial water treatment.

B. Distributor resells water treatment chemicals to municipal and industrial customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory. Distributor wishes to accept that appointment on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means either of the following:

(a) ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity; or

(b) the power to direct the management and policies of an entity.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, permits and orders that apply to a Party or to the Products. This includes environmental, hazardous materials transportation, worker safety and drinking water laws.

1.3 "**Claim**" means any claim, demand, action, suit, proceeding, investigation or order brought or issued by a third party or a Governmental Authority.

1.4 "**Competing Products**" means any polyaluminum chloride or ferric sulfate coagulant that is not supplied by Supplier under this Agreement.

1.5 "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, municipal or other governmental or regulatory body, agency or court.

1.6 "**Losses**" means all of the following:

(a) losses, damages, liabilities, fines and penalties;

(b) costs of investigation, cleanup and remediation;

(c) judgments and settlements; and

(d) costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' and consultants' fees.

1.7 "**Products**" means the polyaluminum chloride and ferric sulfate coagulants listed in Exhibit A, as the Parties may amend Exhibit A from time to time by written agreement.

1.8 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Distributor issues to Supplier under Section 4.

1.9 "**Territory**" means the States of New York, New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

1.10 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out in Exhibit A. These include grade, concentration, NSF/ANSI Standard 60 certification and packaging requirements.

1.11 "**Parent Guaranty**" means the guaranty delivered by Gloumund Holdings, Inc. under Section 16.

1.12 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term, as described in Section 3.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products during the Term. The appointment covers sales to municipal and industrial water and wastewater treatment customers in the Territory. Distributor accepts the appointment.

2.2 **Supplier Restraint.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or sales representative for the Products in the Territory. Supplier shall refer to Distributor all inquiries for Products that it receives from customers located in the Territory.

2.3 **Sub-Distributors.** Distributor may not appoint sub-distributors for the Products without Supplier's prior written consent. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold that consent.

2.4 **Non-Competition.** During the Term, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, purchase for resale, market, distribute or sell any Competing Products in the Territory. This Section 2.4 does not restrict Distributor from selling any other water or wastewater treatment chemical, in the Territory or elsewhere.

2.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Neither Party is the agent, partner or joint venturer of the other. Neither Party may bind the other.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues for five years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless it is terminated earlier under Section 10.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal. The notice must be given at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. FORECASTS AND PURCHASE ORDERS

4.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Distributor shall give Supplier a rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements for the following six (6) months. Forecasts are not binding.

4.2 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders. Each Purchase Order shall state the following:

(a) the Products and quantities;

(b) the packaging;

(c) the requested delivery date; and

(d) the delivery location.

Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within three (3) business days after receipt. Supplier may not reject a Purchase Order that conforms to Distributor's most recent forecast within a variance of twenty percent (20%).

4.3 **Purchase Order Terms.** The terms of this Agreement apply to every Purchase Order. Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order or acknowledgment have no effect. This does not apply to the commercial details listed in Section 4.2.

4.4 **Minimum Purchase.** For each contract year, Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate invoiced value of at least the annual minimum stated in Exhibit B.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for the Products set out in Exhibit B. Supplier may adjust prices once in each contract year by giving Distributor at least ninety (90) days' written notice. A single adjustment may not exceed the sum of:

(a) the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Industrial Chemicals over the preceding twelve (12) months; and

(b) two percent (2%).

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each order.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days after receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount before its due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Distributor shall pay those taxes, other than taxes based on Supplier's income. If Distributor gives Supplier a valid resale exemption certificate, Supplier shall not charge sales tax covered by that certificate.

## 6. DELIVERY, PACKAGING AND TITLE

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's shipping point (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier that Distributor designates. If Distributor does not designate a carrier, Supplier may do so.

6.2 **Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier shall package the Products in bulk tankers, intermediate bulk container totes or drums, as the applicable Purchase Order specifies. Supplier shall label all packaging in accordance with the Specifications and Applicable Law. This includes the U.S. Department of Transportation hazardous materials regulations and the OSHA Hazard Communication Standard. Supplier is responsible for the design, condition and integrity of all totes and drums that it fills.

6.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier under Section 6.1.

6.4 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect Products within ten (10) business days after receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage, visible damage or nonconformity with the Specifications that a reasonable inspection would reveal. Notice under this Section 6.4 does not limit Supplier's warranty under Section 8.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Marketing.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to market and sell the Products in the Territory. Distributor shall maintain technically qualified sales and service personnel.

7.2 **Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store, handle and transport the Products in accordance with Applicable Law and with the safety data sheets that Supplier provides.

7.3 **Records.** Distributor shall keep records of its sales of Products by customer and lot number for at least three (3) years. On reasonable request, Distributor shall make those records available to Supplier to support product recalls and regulatory inquiries.

7.4 **Customer Complaints.** Distributor shall notify Supplier within two (2) business days of any customer complaint about Product quality, performance or safety.

## 8. SUPPLIER WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for twelve (12) months after delivery, the Products will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) be free from defects in materials and manufacture;

(c) be certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 60 for drinking water treatment where Exhibit A so states; and

(d) be manufactured, packaged and labeled in compliance with Applicable Law.

8.2 **Title.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products, free of liens and encumbrances.

8.3 **Remedies.** If a Product breaches Section 8.1, Supplier shall replace the Product or credit or refund its price, at Distributor's option. Supplier shall also pay the reasonable costs of returning, removing or disposing of the nonconforming Product.

8.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 8, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. REGULATORY MATTERS

9.1 **Safety Data Sheets.** Supplier shall provide current safety data sheets for each Product. Supplier shall provide updated sheets promptly after any revision.

9.2 **Permits.** Each Party shall obtain and maintain the permits, registrations and licenses that Applicable Law requires for its activities under this Agreement.

9.3 **Recalls.** Distributor shall cooperate in any recall of a Product that either:

(a) a Governmental Authority requires; or

(b) Supplier reasonably decides to undertake.

Supplier shall bear the reasonable costs of a recall caused by a breach of Section 8.1.

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement. The breaching Party must first receive written notice describing the breach and fail to cure it within thirty (30) days.

10.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) becomes insolvent;

(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(c) becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

10.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination:

(a) Distributor shall pay all amounts due for Products delivered;

(b) Supplier shall fill all accepted Purchase Orders, unless Supplier terminated under Section 10.1 or 10.2; and

(c) Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory of Products for ninety (90) days.

10.4 **Survival.** Sections 5, 8, 11, 12, 14, 17 and 18 and this Section 10.4 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from and against all Claims and Losses, including environmental and regulatory Claims and Losses, to the extent arising out of any of the following:

(a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Distributor or its employees, agents or carriers in the storage, handling, transport or resale of Products after title passes to Distributor;

(b) Distributor's breach of Section 7.2; or

(c) any warranty or representation about the Products that Distributor makes to its customers beyond those in Section 8.1.

Distributor has no obligation under this Section 11.1 to the extent a Claim or Loss is caused by a breach of Section 8.1 or by the design, condition or integrity of packaging for which Supplier is responsible under Section 6.2.

11.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from and against all Claims and Losses, including environmental and regulatory Claims and Losses, to the extent arising out of any of the following:

(a) any defect in the Products;

(b) any failure of the Products to conform to the Specifications or the warranties in Section 8.1;

(c) any failure of packaging to meet Supplier's obligations under Section 6.2;

(d) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its employees or agents; or

(e) any Claim that the Products, or their manufacture, infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party.

11.3 **Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification shall:

(a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the Claim, although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay prejudices it;

(b) allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the Claim; and

(c) give reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

The indemnifying Party may not settle any Claim in a way that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without the indemnified Party's prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

11.4 **Comparative Responsibility.** If both Parties contribute to a Claim or Loss, each Party's obligation under this Section 11 is limited to its proportionate share of responsibility.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any of the following damages arising out of this Agreement:

(a) indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages; or

(b) lost profits.

This applies even if the Party has been advised of the possibility of those damages.

12.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the amounts paid by Distributor to Supplier in the 12 months before the claim.

12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to any of the following:

(a) either Party's indemnification obligations for third-party Claims under Section 11;

(b) either Party's breach of Section 14;

(c) either Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Distributor's obligation to pay for Products delivered.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, each Party shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) pollution legal liability insurance with limits of at least $5,000,000 per claim; and

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 per accident.

13.2 **Certificates.** Each Party shall provide certificates of insurance on request. Each certificate shall name the other Party as an additional insured under the policies in Sections 13.1(a) and 13.1(b).

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information that the other Party discloses under this Agreement, including pricing, formulations, customer lists and forecasts. Each Party shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.

14.2 **Exceptions.** Section 14.1 does not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information; or

(d) must be disclosed by law or by order of a Governmental Authority, provided that the receiving Party gives prompt notice where lawful.

14.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement. For trade secrets, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure to perform, other than a payment obligation, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These events include the following:

(a) fire, flood, storm and epidemic;

(b) war and terrorism;

(c) labor disputes not involving that Party's employees;

(d) failure of public utilities; and

(e) acts of Governmental Authorities.

The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

## 16. PARENT GUARANTY

16.1 As a condition of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement, Distributor shall cause its parent, Gloumund Holdings, Inc., to deliver a guaranty to Supplier on or before the Effective Date. The guaranty shall cover Distributor's payment obligations and shall be in the form agreed between Supplier and Gloumund Holdings, Inc.
