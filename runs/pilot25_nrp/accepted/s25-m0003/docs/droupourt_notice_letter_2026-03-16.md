## 1. Purpose of This Letter

1.1 This firm acts as in-house counsel to Droupourt Chemical Corporation ("**Supplier**"). We write about the Distribution Agreement dated as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between Supplier and Gloumund Water Solutions, LLC ("**Distributor**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 This letter is a notice under Section 17.1 of the Agreement. It does two things:

(a) it gives Distributor written notice under Section 10.1 of Distributor's material breach of Section 2.4 of the Agreement; and

(b) it gives Distributor written notice under Section 11.3(a) of a Claim for which Supplier demands defense and indemnification under Section 11.1 of the Agreement.

## 2. Breach of the Non-Competition Covenant

2.1 **The covenant.** Section 2.4 of the Agreement provides that, during the Term and for eighteen (18) months after its expiration or termination, Distributor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, manufacture, purchase for resale, market, promote, distribute or sell any Competing Products anywhere in North America. Section 1.4 defines "Competing Products" as any chemical used in the treatment of water or wastewater, whether or not it competes with the Products, other than Products purchased from Supplier under the Agreement.

2.2 **The conduct.** Supplier has learned that, since at least February 2026, Distributor has been purchasing sodium hypochlorite disinfectant from a third-party manufacturer for resale. Distributor has been reselling that product to municipal and regional water utilities in the State of Connecticut. Our information comes from bid tabulations published by at least two Connecticut water utilities. Those tabulations identify Distributor as the awarded supplier of sodium hypochlorite solution for calendar year 2026. Supplier also has reports from its own field sales personnel.

2.3 **The breach.** Sodium hypochlorite is a chemical used in the treatment of water and wastewater. It is not a Product purchased from Supplier under the Agreement. It is therefore a Competing Product under Section 1.4. Connecticut is within North America. Distributor's purchase for resale, marketing and sale of sodium hypochlorite to Connecticut utilities is a material breach of Section 2.4.

2.4 **Demand.** Supplier demands that Distributor, within thirty (30) days after receipt of this letter:

(a) cease all purchase for resale, marketing, promotion, distribution and sale of sodium hypochlorite and any other Competing Products, whether directly or through any Affiliate;

(b) give Supplier written confirmation, signed by an officer of Distributor, that it has done so; and

(c) give Supplier a written list of each customer to which Distributor or any Affiliate has sold Competing Products since the Effective Date, with the products, quantities and invoiced amounts.

2.5 **Reservation of termination rights.** If Distributor does not cure the breach within the thirty (30) day period provided in Section 10.1, Supplier may terminate the Agreement by written notice. Supplier expressly reserves that right. Supplier also reserves its rights under Section 10.3(b) to decline to fill accepted Purchase Orders following any such termination. In addition, Supplier reserves the right to seek injunctive relief and damages in the courts identified in Section 18.3. Under Section 10.4, Section 2.4 survives any termination, and Distributor will remain bound by it for eighteen (18) months afterward.

## 3. Tender of the Newark Warehouse Claim

3.1 **The incident.** On or about February 27, 2026, a 330-gallon IBC tote of DC-FS-50 liquid ferric sulfate leaked inside Distributor's warehouse at 275 Doremus Avenue, Newark, New Jersey. Supplier had filled and shipped that tote to Distributor under a Purchase Order. Supplier understands that the released material reached a floor drain. It also understands that Distributor reported the discharge to the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection ("**NJDEP**") hotline the same day.

3.2 **The Claim.** On March 9, 2026, NJDEP issued a notice to Supplier and to Distributor. The notice requires investigation and cleanup of the release under the New Jersey Spill Compensation and Control Act. It also requests information about the source of the discharge. On March 11, 2026, the Passaic Valley Sewerage Commission notified Supplier that it is reviewing a possible discharge to its system from the Newark premises. Together, these matters, and any related Claims by any Governmental Authority or third party, are referred to in this letter as the "**Newark Claim**." Copies of both notices are enclosed.

3.3 **Indemnification obligation.** Section 11.1 of the Agreement requires Distributor to defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from and against all Claims and Losses, including environmental and regulatory Claims and Losses, arising out of or relating to the storage, handling, transport or resale of Products by or on behalf of Distributor, regardless of the cause. The Newark Claim arises out of the storage and handling of Products at Distributor's warehouse. It falls within Section 11.1.

3.4 **Tender.** Supplier hereby tenders the defense of the Newark Claim to Distributor and demands indemnification under Section 11.1. Supplier asks that Distributor confirm in writing, within ten (10) business days after receipt of this letter, that it accepts the tender and assumes the defense of the Newark Claim under Section 11.3(b). In that confirmation, Distributor should identify its counsel and environmental consultant.

3.5 **Losses to date.** Supplier has already incurred Losses in responding to the Newark Claim. These include the fees of outside environmental counsel and the costs of a consultant retained to review the NJDEP notice. Supplier will provide an itemized statement on request and will update it periodically. Supplier reserves the right to participate in the defense with counsel of its choice, as Section 11.3 permits.

3.6 **Preservation.** Distributor must preserve the leaking tote, its contents and labeling, any fragments or fittings, and all related records. Those records include receiving and inspection records under Section 6.4, storage logs, warehouse video, incident reports and communications with NJDEP and the Passaic Valley Sewerage Commission. Distributor may not dispose of, repair or alter the tote without Supplier's prior written consent. Supplier requests access to inspect the tote and the release area at a mutually convenient time within the next ten (10) business days.

3.7 **No settlement.** Distributor may not settle any part of the Newark Claim in a way that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, Supplier without Supplier's prior written consent, as provided in Section 11.3.
