# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this **"DPA"**) is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"DPA Effective Date"**) by and between:

**Gaiport Practice Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Gaiport"**); and

**Daitend Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**).

This DPA supplements the Master Services Agreement between Gaiport and Customer dated March 1, 2024 (the **"Agreement"**) and forms part of it. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Customer Personal Data"** means any personal data relating to Customer's or its Affiliates' employees, dentists, hygienists, front-office staff, contractors and other Authorized Users that Gaiport processes on behalf of Customer in providing the Services. This includes names, business contact details, login credentials, user identifiers, role and permission settings, and audit-log data. Customer Personal Data is defined excluding Patient Health Information.

1.2 **"Data Protection Laws"** means all United States federal and state laws and regulations on privacy, data security and the processing of personal data that apply to a party's performance under the Agreement.

1.3 **"Patient Health Information"** means information about a patient of Customer or its Affiliates that relates to the patient's past, present or future physical or dental health or condition, the provision of dental care to the patient, or payment for that care. This includes patient charts, treatment records, radiographic images and intraoral photographs.

1.4 **"Personal Data"** means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to an identified or identifiable natural person.

1.5 **"Processing"** means any operation performed on data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, erasure and destruction. **"Process"** and **"Processed"** have corresponding meanings.

1.6 **"Security Incident"** means a confirmed breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data Processed by Gaiport or its Subprocessors.

1.7 **"Subprocessor"** means any third party that Gaiport engages to Process Customer Personal Data on its behalf.

---

## 2. ROLES AND SCOPE

2.1 **Roles.** For Customer Personal Data, Customer acts as the business or controller, and Gaiport acts as the service provider or processor acting on Customer's behalf.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Gaiport shall Process Customer Personal Data only to provide the Services, including user account administration, authentication, access control, support and maintenance, and as otherwise documented in Customer's written instructions. Annex A describes the subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the Processing.

2.3 **Duration.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Gaiport Processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

---

## 3. CUSTOMER INSTRUCTIONS AND OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Gaiport shall Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA and Customer's configuration of the Services constitute Customer's complete instructions as of the DPA Effective Date. Gaiport shall inform Customer promptly if, in Gaiport's opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer is responsible for providing any notices to, and obtaining any consents from, its personnel that Data Protection Laws require for Gaiport to Process Customer Personal Data under this DPA.

3.3 **Prohibited Uses.** Gaiport shall not sell or share Customer Personal Data, retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the Services, or combine it with personal data Gaiport receives from other sources, except as Data Protection Laws permit for service providers.

---

## 4. GAIPORT PERSONNEL

4.1 **Confidentiality.** Gaiport shall ensure that its personnel authorized to Process Customer Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations or are under an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality.

4.2 **Access Limitation.** Gaiport shall limit access to Customer Personal Data to personnel who need that access to perform the Services. It shall provide those personnel with appropriate privacy and security training at least annually.

---

## 5. SECURITY OF CUSTOMER PERSONAL DATA

5.1 **Security Measures.** Gaiport shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, physical and technical measures to protect Customer Personal Data against Security Incidents. These measures shall include, at a minimum, the controls described in Annex B.

5.2 **Encryption.** Gaiport shall encrypt Customer Personal Data in transit over public networks using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent industry-standard algorithm.

5.3 **Testing.** Gaiport shall regularly test and evaluate the effectiveness of the measures in Section 5.1. It shall engage an independent third party to conduct a penetration test of the Platform at least once every twelve (12) months.

5.4 **Audit Reports.** On Customer's written request, made no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, Gaiport shall provide Customer with a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent third-party assessment, covering the systems that Process Customer Personal Data. That report is Gaiport's Confidential Information.

---

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

6.1 **Notice.** Gaiport shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data.

6.2 **Content of Notice.** To the extent known at the time, Gaiport's notice shall describe (a) the nature of the Security Incident; (b) the categories and approximate number of individuals and records concerned; (c) the likely consequences; and (d) the measures taken or proposed to address the Security Incident and mitigate its effects. Gaiport shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.3 **Cooperation.** Gaiport shall take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the Security Incident. It shall cooperate reasonably with Customer in investigating the Security Incident and in meeting any notification obligations that Data Protection Laws impose on Customer for Customer Personal Data.

6.4 **No Admission.** Gaiport's notification of or response to a Security Incident is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

---

## 7. SUBPROCESSORS

7.1 **Authorization.** Customer generally authorizes Gaiport to engage Subprocessors to Process Customer Personal Data. The Subprocessors engaged as of the DPA Effective Date are listed in Annex C.

7.2 **Changes.** Gaiport shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' notice before adding or replacing a Subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period. The parties shall then discuss the objection in good faith.

7.3 **Subprocessor Obligations.** Gaiport shall impose on each Subprocessor written data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA. Gaiport remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance, consistent with Section 15.2 of the Agreement.

---

## 8. ASSISTANCE AND REQUESTS

8.1 **Individual Requests.** Gaiport shall promptly forward to Customer any request it receives from an individual to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws over Customer Personal Data. Gaiport shall not respond except as Customer instructs or as the law requires.

8.2 **Assistance.** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Gaiport shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in responding to those requests and in meeting Customer's obligations under Data Protection Laws for Customer Personal Data.

8.3 **Government Requests.** If a governmental authority requests access to Customer Personal Data, Gaiport shall, where lawful, notify Customer promptly and give Customer a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or other remedy.

---

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION

9.1 **Return.** During the export period described in Section 7.4 of the Agreement, Gaiport shall make Customer Personal Data available to Customer for export on Customer's written request.

9.2 **Deletion.** Within sixty (60) days after the end of that export period, Gaiport shall delete Customer Personal Data from its production systems. It shall delete Customer Personal Data from backup systems in the ordinary course of its backup rotation, not to exceed one hundred eighty (180) days. Until that deletion, Gaiport shall continue to protect Customer Personal Data in accordance with Section 5.

9.3 **Certification.** On Customer's written request, an officer of Gaiport shall certify in writing that deletion under Section 9.2 has been completed.

9.4 **Legal Retention.** Gaiport may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent Data Protection Laws or other applicable law require it to do so. This DPA continues to apply to any retained Customer Personal Data.

---

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Liability.** Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to Section 10 of the Agreement.

10.2 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date and continues until the later of expiration or termination of the Agreement and completion of Gaiport's obligations under Section 9.

10.3 **Amendment.** The parties may amend this DPA only by a written instrument signed by both parties. If Data Protection Laws change in a way that affects Processing under this DPA, the parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendments reasonably necessary to comply.

10.4 **Governing Law.** This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, consistent with Section 16.1 of the Agreement.

## ANNEX B: SECURITY MEASURES

1. Role-based access controls and least-privilege access for Gaiport personnel.
2. Multi-factor authentication for administrative access to production systems.
3. Encryption in transit and at rest as described in Section 5.2.
4. Logging and monitoring of access to production systems, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.
5. Vulnerability management, including timely patching of critical vulnerabilities.
6. Documented incident response plan, tested at least annually.
7. Business continuity and disaster recovery procedures, including geographically separate backups.
8. Background checks for personnel with access to production systems, where lawful.
9. Physical security controls at hosting facilities, provided through Subprocessors certified to SOC 2 or ISO/IEC 27001.
