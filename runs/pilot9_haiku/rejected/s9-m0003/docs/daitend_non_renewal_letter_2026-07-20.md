## 1. Notice of Non-Renewal

This letter concerns the Master Services Agreement dated March 1, 2024 (the **"Agreement"**) between Gaiport Practice Systems, Inc. (**"Gaiport"**) and Daitend Dental Partners, LLC (**"Daitend"**). Pursuant to Section 12.2 of the Agreement, Daitend hereby gives notice of non-renewal of the Agreement. The Agreement will therefore expire at the end of the Initial Term on August 31, 2026, and will not renew for a Renewal Term. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 2. Services Through Expiration

Daitend expects Gaiport to continue providing the Services for all 22 Clinic Locations, including the Imaging Module, in accordance with the Agreement through August 31, 2026. Daitend will continue to pay the Subscription Fee of $18,400 per month for 22 clinic locations, as invoiced under Section 4.2, for the remaining months of the Initial Term.

## 3. Return of Customer Data

Under Section 7.4 of the Agreement, Daitend requests that Gaiport make all Customer Data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format promptly after expiration. This includes patient records, appointment and scheduling data, treatment plans, billing and insurance information, and radiographic images in DICOM format. Please identify a Gaiport contact to coordinate the export schedule and file transfer method with Daitend's IT team. Daitend also requests the officer's certification of deletion described in Section 9.3 of the Data Processing Addendum once deletion is complete.

## 4. Transition Contact

Please direct all communications about this notice and the transition to the undersigned, with a copy to Daitend's Legal department at the notice address stated in the Agreement.

## 5. Reservation of Rights

Nothing in this letter waives any of Daitend's rights or remedies under the Agreement, the Data Processing Addendum or applicable law, all of which are expressly reserved.
