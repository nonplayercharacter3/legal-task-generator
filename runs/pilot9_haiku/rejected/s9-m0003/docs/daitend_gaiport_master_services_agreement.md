# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between:

**Gaiport Practice Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Gaiport"**); and

**Daitend Dental Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Customer"**).

Gaiport and Customer are each a **"party"** and together the **"parties."**

**Background.** Gaiport provides a cloud-hosted dental practice-management, patient scheduling and dental-imaging platform. Customer operates a group of dental clinics and wishes to subscribe to that platform for its clinic locations on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

1.1 **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 **"Authorized Users"** means Customer's and its Affiliates' employees, dentists, hygienists, front-office staff and contractors whom Customer authorizes to access the Services.

1.3 **"Clinic Location"** means a physical dental clinic operated by Customer or its Affiliate and listed in an Order Form.

1.4 **"Customer Data"** means all data, information and content submitted to, stored in or generated through the Services by or for Customer or its Authorized Users. This includes patient records, appointment and scheduling data, treatment plans, billing and insurance information, protected health information (as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103), and radiographic images, intraoral photographs and other diagnostic images.

1.5 **"DPA"** means the Data Processing Addendum between the parties dated March 1, 2024, as amended from time to time.

1.6 **"Documentation"** means Gaiport's then-current user guides, technical specifications and help materials for the Services that Gaiport makes available to Customer.

1.7 **"Fees"** means the Subscription Fee and any other amounts payable under this Agreement or an Order Form.

1.8 **"Imaging Module"** means the component of the Platform that captures, stores, displays and manages radiographic and other diagnostic images, including integration with supported imaging sensors and devices.

1.9 **"Order Form"** means an ordering document signed by both parties that references this Agreement and specifies the Services, Clinic Locations and Fees.

1.10 **"Permitted Assignee"** means (a) any Affiliate of Gaiport; (b) any person or entity that acquires all or any portion of its assets or business, whether by merger, sale of stock, sale of assets, reorganization or otherwise; or (c) any lender, investor or other financing party of Gaiport or its Affiliates, including any agent or trustee acting for such parties.

1.11 **"Platform"** means Gaiport's cloud-hosted dental practice-management, scheduling and imaging software, including the Imaging Module, together with all updates, upgrades and modifications made generally available by Gaiport.

1.12 **"Services"** means access to and use of the Platform, the related hosting, support and maintenance services, and any implementation, training or professional services described in an Order Form.

1.13 **"Subscription Fee"** has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

1.14 **"Term"** means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms, as described in Section 12.

---

## 2. SERVICES AND ORDER FORMS

2.1 **Provision of Services.** Subject to this Agreement, Gaiport shall make the Services available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Term for the Clinic Locations identified in each Order Form.

2.2 **Order Forms.** Each Order Form is governed by and incorporated into this Agreement. Customer may add Clinic Locations or modules by executing an additional Order Form.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** The DPA shall control with respect to the processing of personal data. In all other respects, this Agreement shall prevail over any Order Form.

2.4 **Affiliates.** Customer's Affiliates may use the Services under this Agreement. Customer is responsible for their compliance with this Agreement.

---

## 3. ACCESS AND USE

3.1 **Grant of Access.** Gaiport grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted in Section 15) right during the Term for its Authorized Users to access and use the Services for Customer's internal business purposes in operating the Clinic Locations.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Customer shall not, and shall not permit any person to: (a) sublicense, resell or otherwise make the Services available to any third party other than Authorized Users; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Platform, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits this despite this restriction; (c) use the Services to build a competing product; or (d) use the Services in violation of applicable law.

3.3 **Account Security.** Customer shall keep Authorized User credentials confidential. Customer shall notify Gaiport promptly of any unauthorized use of an account of which Customer becomes aware.

3.4 **Support.** Gaiport shall provide technical support for the Services by telephone and online portal from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days. Gaiport shall provide emergency support for Severity 1 incidents twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, in accordance with the Documentation.

3.5 **Availability.** Gaiport shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance and outages caused by events described in Section 16.4.

---

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Subscription Fee.** Customer shall pay Gaiport a subscription fee of $18,400 per month for 22 clinic locations (the **"Subscription Fee"**). Fees for any additional Clinic Locations or modules shall be as set out in the applicable Order Form.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Gaiport shall invoice the Subscription Fee monthly in advance. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Gaiport in writing before the due date, with reasonable detail. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

4.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer is responsible for those taxes, other than taxes based on Gaiport's net income, property or employees.

---

## 5. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 Customer is responsible for the accuracy and legality of Customer Data and for obtaining any patient consents or authorizations that applicable law requires for Customer to submit Customer Data to the Services.

5.2 Customer shall maintain compatible hardware, imaging sensors, workstations and internet connectivity at each Clinic Location as described in the Documentation.

5.3 Customer shall designate a primary administrator responsible for managing Authorized User access and serving as Gaiport's point of contact.

---

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 **Gaiport Property.** Gaiport and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in and to the Platform, the Documentation and all related intellectual property. No rights are granted except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

6.2 **Customer Data.** As between the parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Customer grants Gaiport a non-exclusive license during the Term to host, process and transmit Customer Data solely to provide the Services and as permitted by the DPA.

6.3 **Feedback.** Gaiport may use any suggestions or feedback provided by Customer about the Services without obligation to Customer.

---

## 7. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

7.1 **Security Program.** Gaiport shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of Customer Data, as further described in the DPA.

7.2 **Use Limitation.** Gaiport shall access and use Customer Data only to provide, maintain and support the Services, to prevent or address technical problems, or as required by law.

7.3 **Security Incidents.** Gaiport shall notify Customer of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Customer Data in Gaiport's possession or control, in accordance with the DPA. Gaiport shall cooperate reasonably with Customer's investigation of the incident.

7.4 **Return of Data.** For thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, Gaiport shall, on Customer's written request, make Customer Data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format, including radiographic images in DICOM or another industry-standard format. After that period, Gaiport shall delete Customer Data in accordance with the DPA.

7.5 **Data Processing Addendum.** The parties shall comply with the DPA, which forms part of this Agreement.

---

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 **Definition.** **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

8.2 **Obligations.** The receiving party shall use the disclosing party's Confidential Information only to perform under this Agreement. It shall protect that information with at least reasonable care and shall disclose it only to its personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

8.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was known to the receiving party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

8.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law. Where lawful, it shall give the disclosing party prompt notice and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

---

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Gaiport and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, actions, demands, regulatory investigations, fines, penalties, damages, losses, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to (a) Customer Data, including any unauthorized access to, use of or disclosure of Customer Data, regardless of whether caused by Gaiport; (b) Customer's or its Authorized Users' use of the Services; or (c) Customer's breach of Section 3.2 or Section 5.

9.2 **By Gaiport.** Gaiport shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by Gaiport and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark or misappropriates a trade secret. Gaiport shall pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement of that claim. Gaiport has no obligation for claims arising from (a) Customer Data; (b) combination of the Platform with hardware, software or data not supplied by Gaiport; or (c) modifications not made by Gaiport. If the Platform becomes, or in Gaiport's opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Gaiport may procure the right for Customer to continue using it, modify it to be non-infringing, or terminate the affected Services and refund any prepaid Fees for the terminated portion.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall (a) notify the indemnifying party promptly of the claim; (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement may impose any obligation on the indemnified party without its consent; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Cap.** Except as set out in Section 10.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

10.2 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as set out in Section 10.3, neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue or goodwill, even if advised of their possibility.

10.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 10.1 and 10.2 shall not apply to:

(a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 9;

(b) Customer's obligation to pay Fees; or

(c) a party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

---

11.1 **Mutual.** Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

11.2 **Services.** Gaiport warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation. As Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty, Gaiport shall use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity. If Gaiport cannot do so within thirty (30) days after notice, Customer may terminate the affected Services and receive a refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

11.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided "as is," and each party disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. The Platform is an administrative and imaging tool and does not provide clinical diagnoses. Customer's dentists remain responsible for all clinical decisions.

---

## 12. TERM AND RENEWAL

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date of March 1, 2024 and continues for an initial term of thirty (30) months from the Effective Date, ending August 31, 2026 (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

12.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive renewal terms of thirty-six (36) months each (each, a **"Renewal Term"**), at the Subscription Fee and other Fees then in effect, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal not more than one hundred twenty (120) days and not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

---

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Suspension.** Gaiport may suspend access to the Services if any undisputed amount remains unpaid more than thirty (30) days after written notice of non-payment. Gaiport shall restore access promptly once payment is received.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Customer's right to access the Services ends, subject to Section 7.4. Customer shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination.

13.5 **Survival.** Sections 1, 4 (as to accrued Fees), 6, 7.4, 8, 9, 10, 13.4, 13.5, 15 and 16 survive expiration or termination.

---

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 During the Term, Gaiport shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, technology errors and omissions insurance, and cyber liability insurance with reputable insurers. Each policy shall have limits customary for providers of comparable services. Gaiport shall provide certificates of insurance on Customer's reasonable request.

---

## 15. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS; ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

15.2 **Subcontractors.** Gaiport may use subcontractors and hosting providers to perform the Services. Gaiport remains responsible for their performance under this Agreement.

15.3 **Assignment.** Except as permitted under Section 15.4, neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15.3 is void.

15.4 **Assignment by Gaiport.** Gaiport may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, together with any associated rights, obligations and Customer Data, to any Permitted Assignee without Customer's consent and without notice to Customer.

15.5 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated on the signature page or another address that a party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt. A copy sent by email does not by itself constitute notice.

16.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.
