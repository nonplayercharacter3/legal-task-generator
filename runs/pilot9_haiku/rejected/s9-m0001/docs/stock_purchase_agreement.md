# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Signing Date**"), by and between **Zanoul Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Paibon Textile Care Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kestrin Linen Services, Inc.**, a California corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company provides commercial laundry and healthcare linen services and operates an industrial laundry plant in Stockton, California (the "**Stockton Plant**").

C. Seller desires to sell to Buyer, and Buyer desires to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Documents**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Bill of Sale, and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in the agreed form, under which Zanoul Equipment Leasing LLC, an Affiliate of Seller ("**Equipment Affiliate**"), conveys to the Company at Closing the tunnel washers, flatwork ironers and route trucks used in the business of the Company.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Disclosure Schedules**" means the disclosure schedules delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all Laws relating to pollution, the protection of the environment or natural resources, or the generation, storage, treatment, discharge or release of Hazardous Materials, including wastewater discharge requirements.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement to be entered into among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent, in form reasonably acceptable to Buyer and Seller.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any earnings thereon.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares), 4.13 (Taxes) and 4.18 (Brokers).

"**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance, chemical or waste regulated under any Environmental Law, including laundry detergents, solvents, bleaching agents and petroleum products.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.10.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and the portion of any straddle period ending on the Closing Date.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, property, payroll, withholding and other taxes, together with any interest and penalties.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $42,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as set forth in Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price by wire transfer of immediately available funds as follows:

(a) to the holders of Closing Indebtedness, the amounts set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv);

(b) to the Escrow Agent, $4,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"), to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and Article IX; and

(c) to Seller, the balance of the Purchase Price, as adjusted by the Estimated Closing Statement.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.** Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital and Closing Indebtedness. Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver a final closing statement. Any dispute that the parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on August 14, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), or on such other date as Buyer and Seller may agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in Article VII.

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers;

(ii) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iii) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Equipment Affiliate;

(iv) customary payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness;

(v) the Required Consents;

(vi) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(vii) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b); and

(viii) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedules, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Signing Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of organization and has all requisite corporate power to own its assets and carry on its business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations thereunder. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company or any applicable Law and, except as disclosed in the Disclosure Schedules, do not require any consent under, or give rise to any right of termination under, any Material Contract.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024, and the related statements of income and cash flows, have been prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Company as of their dates.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities other than those reflected in the financial statements, incurred in the ordinary course since December 31, 2025, or disclosed in the Disclosure Schedules.

**4.8 Title to Assets.** The Company has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible assets used in its business. Upon delivery of the Bill of Sale at Closing, the Company will hold good title to the tunnel washers, flatwork ironers and route trucks described therein.

**4.9 Real Property.** The Company does not own any real property. The Company leases the Stockton Plant under the lease described in the Disclosure Schedules (the "**Stockton Plant Lease**"), which is in full force and effect.

**4.10 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.10 lists each contract to which the Company is a party involving annual payments in excess of $250,000 (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and, to Seller's Knowledge, the counterparty. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's Knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.11 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.12 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws and holds all material permits required for the conduct of its business.

**4.13 Taxes.** All Tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true and complete in all material respects, and all Taxes due have been paid. The Company is a member of the affiliated group of corporations of which Seller is the common parent and that files a consolidated U.S. federal income tax return.

**4.14 Environmental Matters.** (a) The Company and its operations at the Stockton Plant are, and have been, in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws, including all wastewater discharge permits and pretreatment requirements applicable to the Stockton Plant. (b) All Hazardous Materials used or stored at the Stockton Plant, including detergents, solvents and bleaching agents, have been stored and handled in compliance with Environmental Laws. (c) There has been no release of Hazardous Materials at, on or under the Stockton Plant, and there is no contamination of the soil or groundwater at the Stockton Plant, that requires investigation or remediation under Environmental Law.

**4.15 Employees and Benefit Plans.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all Laws relating to employment and with the terms of each employee benefit plan.

**4.16 Insurance.** The Company maintains insurance policies in amounts customary for its business, all of which are in full force and effect.

**4.17 Customers.** Schedule 4.10 identifies the Company's ten largest customers by revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. No such customer has notified the Company in writing of an intention to terminate or materially reduce its business with the Company.

**4.18 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 Tax Classification.** Buyer is classified as an association taxable as a corporation for U.S. federal income tax purposes.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the Signing Date until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to preserve its business relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company.

**6.3 Notice of Developments.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Article VII not to be satisfied.

**6.4 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VII. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the Required Consents before Closing.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** From the Signing Date until the earlier of the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit or negotiate any alternative acquisition proposal relating to the Company.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information concerning the Company, except as required by Law.

**6.7 Post-Closing Environmental Investigation.** Following the Closing, Buyer may cause an environmental consultant of its choosing to conduct a Phase II subsurface investigation of the Stockton Plant, including soil and groundwater sampling (the "**Phase II Investigation**"). Seller shall cooperate with Buyer's consultant and shall provide access to historical records relating to the Stockton Plant. Buyer shall cause the Phase II Investigation to be completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the Closing Date and shall deliver a copy of the final report to Seller promptly upon receipt.

**6.8 Non-Competition.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, engage in the commercial laundry or healthcare linen services business within the State of California.

**6.9 Employee Matters.** For twelve (12) months after the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each continuing employee with base compensation no less favorable than that in effect immediately before the Closing.

**6.10 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare and file all consolidated Tax returns of the Seller group that include the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date and shall pay all Taxes shown as due thereon.

**6.11 Section 338(h)(10) Election.** If Buyer delivers to Seller written notice of its election to make an election under Section 338(h)(10) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (and any corresponding election under state or local Law) with respect to the purchase of the Shares no later than forty-five (45) days after the Signing Date, Seller shall join with Buyer in making such election and shall deliver to Buyer at Closing an IRS Form 8023, duly executed by Seller. The parties shall allocate the aggregate deemed sale price among the assets of the Company in accordance with Section 338 of the Code and the Treasury Regulations thereunder. If Buyer does not deliver such notice within such period, Seller's obligations under this Section 6.11 shall lapse and be of no further force or effect.

**6.12 Bill of Sale.** Seller shall cause Equipment Affiliate to execute and deliver the Bill of Sale at Closing and to take any further action reasonably requested by Buyer to vest in the Company title to the equipment and vehicles conveyed thereunder, including delivery of endorsed certificates of title for the route trucks.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated hereby.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of each of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at Closing;

(c) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred since the Signing Date;

(d) Seller shall have obtained, and delivered to Buyer copies of, each of the consents set forth on Schedule 7.2(d) (the "**Required Consents**"), each in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2(a).

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of each of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at Closing; and

(c) Buyer shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2(b).

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by September 30, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of such failure; or (c) by either party upon a material breach by the other party that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and there shall be no liability on the part of any party, except for liability for willful breach and except that Sections 6.6 and Article XI shall survive.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of the parties shall survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date; provided that (a) the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, and (b) the representations and warranties in Section 4.14 (Environmental Matters) shall survive until the date that is one hundred eighty (180) days after the Closing Date. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim for breach of a representation or warranty may be brought after the expiration of the applicable survival period, except for claims for which a written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail was delivered before such expiration.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates and the Company (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes;

(d) any Closing Indebtedness not paid at or before the Closing; and

(e) any claim by Equipment Affiliate or any creditor of Equipment Affiliate relating to the assets conveyed under the Bill of Sale.

(f) Seller shall not be liable under this Section 9.2 for any Losses arising out of the failure to obtain any consent, approval or waiver disclosed on the Disclosure Schedules.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Source of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Funds and, to the extent the Escrow Funds are insufficient or have been released, directly by Seller, subject to Section 9.6.

**9.6 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(b) The limitation in Section 9.6(a) shall not apply to Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation, any Pre-Closing Taxes, or fraud, for which the aggregate liability of Seller shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to Fundamental Representations) until the aggregate Losses exceed $315,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, except for claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.3.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**10.1 Further Assurances.** Following the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages (or such other address as a party may designate by notice).

**11.2 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided herein, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses. Fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or, collaterally, to its financing sources.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.7 Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if such court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby.

**11.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.9 Ancillary Documents.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Document, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the Escrow Agreement shall control with respect to the release of and recovery from the Escrow Funds.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
