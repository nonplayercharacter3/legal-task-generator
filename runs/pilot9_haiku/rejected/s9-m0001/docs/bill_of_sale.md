# BILL OF SALE

*[Agreed Form. To be executed and delivered at Closing under Sections 3.2(a)(iii) and 6.12 of the Purchase Agreement.]*

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of August 14, 2026, by **Zanoul Equipment Leasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), to and in favor of **Kestrin Linen Services, Inc.**, a California corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. Background

1.1 Transferor is an Affiliate of **Zanoul Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**").

1.2 Seller and **Paibon Textile Care Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

1.3 Transferor owns certain tunnel washers, flatwork ironers and route trucks that the Company uses in its commercial laundry and healthcare linen services business. The Company has used these assets under the Equipment Lease and Services Agreement between the Company and Transferor (the "**Equipment Lease**"), which is listed as item 3 on Schedule 4.10 to the Purchase Agreement. Some of these assets are located at or operated from the Company's industrial laundry plant in Stockton, California (the "**Stockton Plant**").

1.4 Section 6.12 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause Transferor to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at Closing.

## 2. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. Conveyance

3.1 **Transfer.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Transferor acknowledges, Transferor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, effective as of the Closing, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the following (together, the "**Conveyed Assets**"):

(a) the tunnel washers described in Part A of Schedule A;

(b) the flatwork ironers described in Part B of Schedule A;

(c) the route trucks described in Part C of Schedule A, together with all keys, fobs and registration materials for them;

(d) all accessions, attachments, spare parts, tooling and controls installed on or held for use with any of the foregoing; and

(e) to the extent transferable, all manufacturer and vendor warranties, service records, operating manuals and maintenance logs relating to any of the foregoing.

3.2 **Title.** The Conveyed Assets are transferred free and clear of all liens, security interests, encumbrances and claims of any Person.

3.3 **Location and Possession.** The Company already has possession of the Conveyed Assets under the Equipment Lease. No physical delivery is required. Title and risk of loss pass to the Company at the Closing.

## 4. Representations of Transferor

Transferor represents to the Company as of the date of this Bill of Sale that:

(a) Transferor is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) Transferor has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to convey the Conveyed Assets;

(c) Transferor is the sole legal and beneficial owner of each Conveyed Asset and holds good and valid title to it, free and clear of all liens, security interests and encumbrances; and

(d) no consent of, or filing with, any Person is required for Transferor to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale. The only exception is the filing of certificates of title and registration transfers for the route trucks with the California Department of Motor Vehicles.

## 5. Certificates of Title; Further Assurances

5.1 **Route Trucks.** At Closing, Transferor shall deliver to the Company the original certificate of title for each route truck listed in Part C of Schedule A. Each certificate shall be duly endorsed for transfer to the Company and accompanied by any odometer disclosure statement and release of lienholder interest required by applicable Law.

5.2 **Lien Releases.** Before or at Closing, Transferor shall deliver UCC-3 termination statements, or authorize the Company to file them, for each financing statement that names Transferor as debtor and covers any Conveyed Asset.

5.3 **Further Assurances.** From time to time after the Closing, at the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Transferor shall execute and deliver further instruments of transfer and take such other actions as are reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Conveyed Assets. These actions include actions necessary to complete re-registration of the route trucks.

## 6. Equipment Lease

Effective upon the Closing, the Equipment Lease is terminated, as contemplated by Schedule 4.10 to the Purchase Agreement. No rent, fee or other amount is payable by the Company under the Equipment Lease for any period after the Closing. Transferor releases the Company from all obligations under the Equipment Lease relating to the Conveyed Assets.

## 7. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is an Ancillary Document delivered under the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies any right or obligation of Seller or Buyer under the Purchase Agreement, including Section 9.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. Miscellaneous

8.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Transferor and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

8.2 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

8.3 **Forum.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware.

8.4 **Counterparts; Electronic Signature.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronic signature has the same effect as an original.
