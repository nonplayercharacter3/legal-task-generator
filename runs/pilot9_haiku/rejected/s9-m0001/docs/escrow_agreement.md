# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of July 28, 2026, by and among **Paibon Textile Care Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Zanoul Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## 1. Background

1.1 Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Kestrin Linen Services, Inc.**, a California corporation (the "**Company**").

1.2 Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit with the Escrow Agent at the Closing $4,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent will hold and disburse the Indemnity Escrow Amount under this Escrow Agreement to secure Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

1.3 Buyer and Seller have asked the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent has agreed to do so.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Escrow Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The Closing is scheduled to occur on August 14, 2026.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, as described in Section 10.4, together with a certificate of the presenting party's counsel stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions executed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 6.2.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set out in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been paid or finally resolved under Section 6.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

2.2 **Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of those meanings or of any provision of the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. Appointment; Deposit

3.1 **Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

3.2 **Escrow Account.** Before the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall establish a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent, for the benefit of Buyer and Seller, designated "Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent for Paibon / Zanoul Indemnity Escrow."

3.3 **Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the Escrow Account. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt to Buyer and Seller by email on the Business Day of receipt.

3.4 **No Commingling.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds separate from its own funds and from funds held for any other Person. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, claim or right of set-off of the Escrow Agent.

## 4. Investment of Escrow Funds

4.1 **Permitted Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Ostrander Government Obligations Money Market Fund, or in another investment that Buyer and Seller designate by Joint Written Instructions. Without such instructions, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured to the extent provided by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

4.2 **Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds and are disbursed with them.

4.3 **Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement. It has no liability for any loss, fee or penalty arising from a liquidation made in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

4.4 **No Investment Advice.** The Escrow Agent does not give investment advice. It has no liability for any decline in the value of the Escrow Funds resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 4.

## 5. Release of Escrow Funds

5.1 **Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Release Date.

5.2 **Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained for a Pending Claim Amount under Section 5.1 until that claim is resolved under Section 6. When a claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall pay the amount due to Buyer, if any, and shall release to Seller any portion of the retained amount that exceeds the remaining Pending Claim Amount.

5.3 **Joint Written Instructions.** At any time, the Escrow Agent shall disburse all or any part of the Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

5.4 **Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Final Order delivered to it by Buyer or Seller, within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

5.5 **Payment Mechanics.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient. The Escrow Agent shall confirm by telephone call-back any wire instructions, and any change to wire instructions, with a person listed on Exhibit A for the relevant party.

## 6. Claims Against Escrow Funds

6.1 **Claim Notice.** If any Buyer Indemnitee claims indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") that:

(a) describes the claim in reasonable detail, including the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which it is made; and

(b) states the amount of Losses claimed or, if not yet known, a good-faith estimate of the amount.

6.2 **Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of the claim in a Claim Notice by delivering to Buyer and the Escrow Agent, within thirty (30) days after Seller receives the Claim Notice, a written notice (an "**Objection Notice**") describing the basis for its objection in reasonable detail and stating the amount in dispute.

6.3 **Payment of Claims.**

(a) If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the thirty (30) day period in Section 6.2, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer from the Escrow Funds, within five (5) Business Days after the end of that period, the amount stated in the Claim Notice.

(b) If the Escrow Agent receives an Objection Notice covering only part of the claim, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer the undisputed portion within five (5) Business Days after receipt of the Objection Notice.

(c) The Escrow Agent shall hold any disputed amount until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, and shall then disburse that amount in accordance with them.

(d) If the amount of a claim was stated as an estimate, Buyer shall deliver an updated Claim Notice when the amount of Losses is determined. The Escrow Agent shall pay no more than the amount stated in the most recent Claim Notice.

6.4 **Source of Recovery.** The Escrow Funds shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery for all claims of the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, except claims for actual fraud. Payment to Buyer of amounts from the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Section 6 satisfies Seller's obligations in respect of the paid claim to the extent of the amount paid.

6.5 **Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay a claim in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer the Escrow Funds then available, and has no further obligation in respect of that claim.

6.6 **Order of Payment.** The Escrow Agent shall pay claims in the order in which the relevant Claim Notices were received.

## 7. The Escrow Agent

7.1 **Duties.** The Escrow Agent has only the duties expressly set out in this Escrow Agreement. Those duties are ministerial. No implied duties or obligations may be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and has no duties under, the Purchase Agreement.

7.2 **Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or delivered by the proper person. It need not investigate any statement in such a document.

7.3 **Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

7.4 **Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and is fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

7.5 **Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an interpleader action in a court described in Section 10.4 and deposit the Escrow Funds with that court, after which it is discharged from further obligations with respect to the deposited funds.

7.6 **Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. Resignation or removal takes effect when a successor escrow agent appointed by Joint Written Instructions accepts its appointment in writing and receives the Escrow Funds. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 10.4 to appoint a successor.

7.7 **Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its acceptance of or performance under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount. This Section 7.7 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 8. Fees and Expenses

8.1 **Fees.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set out on Exhibit B. Consistent with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

8.2 **No Deduction.** The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds unless they remain unpaid for more than sixty (60) days after invoice to Buyer and Seller.

## 9. Tax Matters

9.1 **Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. All income on the Escrow Funds is reported as income of Buyer for each year in which it is earned.

9.2 **Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file IRS Form 1099 reports of earnings on the Escrow Funds as required by law. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent an IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date.

9.3 **Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount required by law to be withheld, and shall remit any withheld amount to the appropriate taxing authority.
