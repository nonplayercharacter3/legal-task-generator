## 3. Issues

### A. Supply Agreement: Term, Exclusivity, and Exit

**A-1. Auto-renewal and locked-in exclusivity.**
- **Where:** SA §§3.1, 3.2, 5.1, 17.2.
- **Why it matters:** The Initial Term runs four years from April 1, 2023. Renewal is automatic for three-year periods unless a notice is effective 180 days before expiration. Renewal is "on the terms of this Agreement then in effect, including Section 5," so exclusivity and the Amendment's expanded Products definition carry forward. The next non-renewal deadline is on or about October 2–3, 2029.
- **Fix:** Confirm in writing whether any notice was sent. If none was, calendar the 2029 deadline (use October 2, 2029 to be conservative). If the client wants out before 2030, the only route without Meideis's agreement is for-cause termination with a 30-day cure (SA §15.1). Otherwise, negotiate a shorter renewal or exit right.

**A-2. No exit for the Committed Quantity.**
- **Where:** SA §§2.2, 2.3, 15.1; SOW §§3.1, 3.3, 8.2.
- **Why it matters:** Besouth must buy the full Committed Quantity. It may reduce only with Meideis's written consent (SA §2.3). There is no termination-for-convenience or project-cancellation right. If Calloway is cancelled, delayed, or Besouth loses the package, Besouth still owes about $1.23 million for the IG Committed Quantity, and Holdings guarantees it (PG §1.2(b)).
- **Fix:** Negotiate a project-cancellation or scope-reduction clause with a defined cancellation fee, and an express reduction right for Owner or architect design changes. Ask Meideis to add this to SOW §3.3 for Calloway.

**A-3. Narrow exclusivity cover rights.**
- **Where:** SA §§5.1, 5.2, 17.5.
- **Why it matters:** Besouth may cover only the affected quantity under the specific PO, and only after Meideis notifies a shortfall or fails to deliver within 15 Business Days after the confirmed delivery date. Cover purchases "count toward any Committed Quantity" (SA §5.2), but the drafting does not say whether this reduces Besouth's commitment dollar-for-dollar or only satisfies quantity. Late delivery damages are largely excluded (SA §12.1; see D-3). Besouth has no right to cover for late delivery on other projects.
- **Fix:** Clarify that cover purchases reduce the Committed Quantity obligation dollar-for-dollar. Shorten the cure period for late delivery. Add a termination right for repeated late delivery.

**A-4. Change of control: broad trigger and harsh payment.**
- **Where:** SA §§1.3, 1.5, 16.1, 16.2; PG §§1.2(e), 4.1(c).
- **Why it matters:**
  - Change of Control includes any transfer of more than 50% of the equity of Buyer or any entity that controls Buyer, including an internal transfer (SA §1.3(a)).
  - "Control" includes power "by contract or otherwise" (SA §1.5). A lender's control rights on default could therefore trigger a Change of Control.
  - Under SA §16.2, a Change of Control without consent lets Meideis terminate and requires Besouth to pay the Price of all remaining Committed Quantities "whether or not" ordered, manufactured, or delivered, within 30 days. Holdings guarantees that payment (PG §1.2(e)).
  - The consent in SA §16.2 has no reasonableness standard. The reasonableness standard in SA §16.1 applies only to general assignments.
  - The "whether or not delivered" payment may be challenged as an unenforceable penalty under UCC §2-718 as adopted in Pennsylvania (SA §17.1). This requires research and should not be relied on.
- **Fix:** Before any transaction, reorganization, or financing with control rights, obtain Meideis's written consent. In any renegotiation, add a reasonableness standard, carve-outs for affiliate transfers and lender enforcement, and a payment limited to documented costs net of resale and avoided costs.

**A-5. Annual price increases.**
- **Where:** SA §§6.1, 6.2, 6.3.
- **Why it matters:** Meideis may raise Exhibit B prices once a year by up to the greater of 4% or the PPI change, on 60 days' notice, effective each April 1. Project Prices are fixed only for the Committed Quantity (SA §6.3; SOW §4.1). Non-Calloway orders, attic stock, and quantities above the Committed Quantity are exposed. The next adjustment date is April 1, 2027, so notice would be due by about January 31, 2027.
- **Fix:** Confirm current Exhibit B pricing and whether any §6.2 notice was received. In renegotiation, seek a cap or fixed pricing through the Renewal Term.

- **Fix:** Obtain the endorsement and check for incorporation or ingredient exclusions. Ask the broker, in writing, to confirm coverage for Besouth as a contractor incorporating the product.
