# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of October 20, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between **Meideis Glassworks, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Furnace Road, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18109 ("**Supplier**"), and **Besouth Facade Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 410 Wharf Street, Camden, New Jersey 08103 ("**Buyer**"). Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Buyer are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of April 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**").

B. Supplier and Buyer are parties to Statement of Work No. 2026-03 under the Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026, for the Calloway Street Tower facade, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (the "**Calloway SOW**").

C. The Architect of Record for the Calloway Project has issued a revision to the Project's glass glazing specification calling for laminated heat-strengthened spandrel glass at the floor-line and column-cover zones of the unitized curtain wall. Buyer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, that spandrel glass under the Agreement and the Calloway SOW.

D. Under Section 17.3 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference in any Statement of Work to "the Agreement," means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENTS TO THE AGREEMENT

2.1 **Products.** Section 1.7 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following, which applies for all purposes of the Agreement:

> 1.7 "**Products**" means all architectural glass products manufactured, fabricated or supplied by Supplier, including the low-E coated insulating glass units described in Exhibit A and the laminated spandrel glass described in Exhibit A-1.

2.2 **Specifications.** Section 1.10 of the Agreement is amended by replacing the words "set out in Exhibit A" with "set out in Exhibit A and Exhibit A-1."

2.3 **New Exhibit A-1.** The Agreement is amended by adding Exhibit A-1 (Laminated Spandrel Glass), in the form attached to this Amendment, immediately following Exhibit A.

2.4 **Pricing.** Exhibit B of the Agreement is amended by adding the following rows to the pricing table, immediately following the row for "Units exceeding 50 sq. ft. (oversize upcharge)":

| Product Configuration | Price per sq. ft. (FCA Allentown) |
|---|---|
| SG-1 laminated HS spandrel, clear/clear, 0.060 PVB, opacifier on No. 4 surface | $21.85 |
| SG-1 ceramic frit (in lieu of opacifier), per lite | $3.10 |

2.5 **Labeling.** Section 8.4 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end: "Each laminated spandrel lite shall bear a permanent label identifying Supplier, the date of manufacture and the applicable safety glazing certification under ANSI Z97.1 and 16 CFR 1201."

2.6 **Spandrel Warranty.** Section 10 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Section 10.5:

> 10.5 **Laminated Glass Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each laminated spandrel lite will, for a period of five years from the date of manufacture, be free from delamination, edge separation exceeding 1/4 inch from the glass edge, and bubbles or discoloration of the interlayer visible from a distance of ten feet under normal use and installation in accordance with Supplier's published glazing guidelines. Sections 10.3 and 10.4 apply to claims under this Section 10.5. Sections 10.1(c) and 10.2 do not apply to laminated spandrel glass.

## EXHIBIT A-1
### LAMINATED SPANDREL GLASS

1. **Product Description.** Laminated heat-strengthened spandrel glass for opaque zones of commercial curtain-wall and window-wall systems, consisting of:
   - (a) outboard lite: 6 mm heat-strengthened clear float glass;
   - (b) interlayer: 1.52 mm (0.060 inch) polyvinyl butyral (PVB), clear;
   - (c) inboard lite: 6 mm heat-strengthened clear float glass; and
   - (d) No. 4 surface treatment: water-based silicone opacifier or, where ordered, ceramic frit fired during heat treatment, in a color selected from Supplier's standard spandrel color range or custom-matched to an approved control sample.
2. **Standards.** Laminated glass shall conform to ASTM C1172 and shall meet the Category II impact requirements of 16 CFR 1201 and ANSI Z97.1. Heat-strengthened substrates shall conform to ASTM C1048.
3. **Size Limits.** Maximum lite size 72 inches by 144 inches; minimum lite size 12 inches by 24 inches.
4. **Lead Times.** Standard lead time is eight weeks from Purchase Order acceptance, including custom color-matched opacifier; replacement lites for nonconforming Products, four weeks.
