# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Meideis Glassworks, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Furnace Road, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18109 ("**Supplier**"), and **Besouth Facade Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 410 Wharf Street, Camden, New Jersey 08103 ("**Buyer**"). Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and fabricates coated architectural glass, including low-E coated insulating glass units, for commercial building envelopes.

B. Buyer designs, fabricates and installs curtain-wall, window-wall and facade systems for commercial and institutional construction projects.

C. Buyer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, insulating glass units for incorporation into Buyer's facade systems on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that Party.

1.2 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania are authorized or required to close.

1.3 "**Change of Control**" means (a) any direct or indirect transfer, sale or other disposition, whether in a single transaction or a series of related transactions and whether by merger, stock or equity sale, operation of law or otherwise, of more than 50% of the equity interests of Buyer or of any entity that Controls Buyer; or (b) any transaction or series of related transactions as a result of which any person or group of persons acquires the power, directly or indirectly, to direct the management and policies of Buyer.

1.4 "**Committed Quantity**" means the quantity of Products that Buyer commits to purchase for a Project, as stated in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.5 "**Control**" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether through ownership of voting securities or membership interests, by contract or otherwise; "**Controls**" and "**Controlled**" have correlative meanings.

1.6 "**Price**" means the unit price for a Product determined in accordance with Section 6 and Exhibit B.

1.7 "**Products**" means low-E coated insulating glass units described in Exhibit A.

1.8 "**Project**" means a construction project for which Buyer supplies facade systems incorporating Products.

1.9 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Buyer under this Agreement and, where applicable, a Statement of Work.

1.10 "**Specifications**" means the technical, performance and dimensional requirements for the Products set out in Exhibit A, together with any Project-specific requirements stated in a Statement of Work or Purchase Order and accepted by Supplier.

1.11 "**Statement of Work**" means a written statement of work for a Project, executed by both Parties and referencing this Agreement, that sets out the Project, the Committed Quantity, the delivery schedule and any Project-specific Specifications.

## 2. SCOPE; STATEMENTS OF WORK

2.1 **Supply.** During the Term, Supplier shall manufacture and sell Products to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase Products from Supplier, in accordance with this Agreement, each applicable Statement of Work and each accepted Purchase Order.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** The Parties may from time to time enter into Statements of Work for particular Projects. Each Statement of Work is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement. A Statement of Work remains "open" from its execution until Supplier has delivered, and Buyer has paid for, the full Committed Quantity under it.

2.3 **Committed Quantities.** Buyer shall purchase the full Committed Quantity stated in each Statement of Work. Buyer may increase a Committed Quantity by Purchase Order, subject to Supplier's acceptance, but may reduce a Committed Quantity only with Supplier's written consent.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for four years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). For purposes of this Section 3, the "Supply Agreement Effective Date" is the Effective Date.

3.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, and upon expiration of each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of three years (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party at least 180 days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. A notice of non-renewal is effective only if delivered in accordance with Section 17.2 at least 180 days before expiration of the then-current term. Each Renewal Term shall be on the terms of this Agreement then in effect, including Section 5.

3.3 **Open Statements of Work.** Expiration of this Agreement does not affect any Statement of Work then open, which shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until completed.

## 4. ORDERING

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Buyer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that identify the Project, Product type, glass makeup, quantity, dimensions, requested delivery dates and delivery location.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five Business Days after receipt. Supplier shall not reject a Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to an open Statement of Work. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Forecasts.** On or before the fifth Business Day of each calendar quarter, Buyer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling twelve-month forecast of its anticipated requirements for Products.

4.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect.

## 5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 **Requirements.** During the Term, Buyer shall purchase from Supplier 100% of Buyer's requirements for Products for all Projects, and shall not purchase Products from any other source, except as provided in Section 5.2.

5.2 **Supply Failure.** If Supplier notifies Buyer that it cannot supply a Product in the quantity or by the delivery date required by an accepted Purchase Order, or fails to deliver conforming Products within fifteen Business Days after the confirmed delivery date, Buyer may purchase the affected quantity from a third party for that Purchase Order only, and those purchases shall count toward any Committed Quantity.

5.3 **Owner-Directed Sources.** Section 5.1 does not apply to any Product that a Project owner or architect requires, by written specification issued before Buyer's bid, to be purchased from a named source other than Supplier.

## 6. PRICING

6.1 **Prices.** Prices for Products are set out in Exhibit B. Prices are stated per square foot of finished unit area, FCA Supplier's facility in Allentown, Pennsylvania, and exclude applicable sales and use taxes.

6.2 **Annual Adjustment.** Supplier may adjust the Prices once per contract year, effective on each anniversary of the Effective Date, by written notice delivered at least sixty days in advance. No annual adjustment may exceed the greater of 4% or the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Flat Glass (BLS series PCU327211327211) for the preceding twelve months.

6.3 **Project Pricing.** Prices stated in an executed Statement of Work are fixed for the Committed Quantity under that Statement of Work and are not subject to adjustment under Section 6.2.

## 7. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Buyer upon shipment of each delivery.

7.2 **Payment.** Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five days after receipt. Amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower.

7.3 **Disputes.** Buyer shall notify Supplier in writing of any good-faith invoice dispute within twenty days after receipt, stating the basis of the dispute, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty days.

## 8. DELIVERY; TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

8.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020), packed on Supplier-owned A-frame or L-frame racks, to the carrier designated by Buyer, or, where a Purchase Order so requests, shall arrange freight to the delivery location at Buyer's cost.

8.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer upon delivery to the carrier.

8.3 **Racks.** Glass racks remain Supplier's property. Buyer shall return empty racks to Supplier within sixty days after delivery or pay Supplier's then-current replacement charge.

8.4 **Labeling.** Each insulating glass unit shall bear a permanent label identifying Supplier, the date of manufacture and the applicable IGCC certification.

## 9. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

9.1 **Inspection.** Buyer shall inspect Products within ten Business Days after arrival at the delivery location and notify Supplier in writing of any visible damage, breakage or nonconformity.

9.2 **Nonconforming Products.** Supplier shall, at its option and cost, replace or credit any nonconforming Product notified under Section 9.1 or Section 10.3, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver replacements within the lead times set out in Exhibit A.

## 10. WARRANTY

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications at delivery; (b) be free from defects in material and workmanship; and (c) for a period of ten years from the date of manufacture, be free from material obstruction of vision caused by film formation or dust collection on the interior glass surfaces resulting from failure of the hermetic seal, under normal use and installation in accordance with Supplier's published glazing guidelines.

10.2 **Coating Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the low-E coating on each Product will not peel, crack or deteriorate for a period of ten years from the date of manufacture.

10.3 **Remedy.** Buyer shall notify Supplier of any warranty claim within sixty days after discovery. Supplier's obligation under this Section 10 is to furnish a replacement Product, delivered to the Project site, and to reimburse Buyer's reasonable documented labor costs to remove the defective unit and install the replacement, up to an aggregate amount per unit equal to two times the Price of that unit.

10.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 10, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

12.1 **Exclusion.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except for liability under Section 11 or Section 14, or for a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

12.2 **Cap.** Except for liability under Section 11 or Section 14, for a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or for Buyer's payment obligations for Products, each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed the total Prices paid and payable by Buyer for Products in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## EXHIBIT A
### PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

1. **Product Description.** Factory-sealed, dual-pane low-E coated insulating glass units for commercial curtain-wall, window-wall and storefront applications, consisting of:
   - (a) outboard lite: 6 mm heat-strengthened or fully tempered clear or low-iron float glass, with sputtered (soft-coat) low-E coating on the No. 2 surface, from Supplier's standard coating series MG-50, MG-62 and MG-70;
   - (b) airspace: 12.7 mm (1/2 inch), 90% argon fill, with warm-edge stainless steel or structural silicone-compatible spacer;
   - (c) inboard lite: 6 mm heat-strengthened, fully tempered or annealed clear float glass;
   - (d) dual seal: polyisobutylene primary seal and structural silicone secondary seal.
2. **Standards.** Products shall be certified by the Insulating Glass Certification Council (IGCC) to ASTM E2190, and glass substrates shall conform to ASTM C1036 and, where heat-treated, ASTM C1048.
3. **Size Limits.** Maximum unit size 84 inches by 144 inches; minimum unit size 10 inches by 24 inches.
4. **Lead Times.** Standard lead time is six weeks from Purchase Order acceptance; replacement units for nonconforming Products, three weeks.
