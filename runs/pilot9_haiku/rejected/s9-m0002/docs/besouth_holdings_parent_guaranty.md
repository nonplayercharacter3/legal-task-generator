# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of April 1, 2023 by **Besouth Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Wharf Street, Camden, New Jersey 08103 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Meideis Glassworks, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Furnace Road, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18109 ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and **Besouth Facade Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of April 1, 2023 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under the Supply Agreement, Supplier sells low-E coated insulating glass units to Buyer for incorporation into Buyer's facade systems.

B. Guarantor is Besouth Holdings Corp., sole member of Besouth Facade Systems, LLC, and owns all of the issued and outstanding membership interests of Buyer.

C. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the supply arrangements between Supplier and Buyer under the Supply Agreement and the Statements of Work entered into under it.

D. As a condition to entering into the Supply Agreement and extending credit terms to Buyer under Section 7 of the Supply Agreement, Supplier requires that Guarantor deliver this Guaranty.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranteed Obligations.** "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations of Buyer to pay money to Supplier under the Supply Agreement and each Statement of Work, whether now existing or arising later, including (a) the Price of all Products delivered to Buyer or in production under accepted Purchase Orders; (b) amounts payable for Committed Quantities under any Statement of Work; (c) interest on late payments under Section 7.2 of the Supply Agreement; (d) rack replacement charges under Section 8.3 of the Supply Agreement; (e) amounts payable by Buyer upon expiration or termination of the Supply Agreement, including under Sections 15.3 and 16.2 of the Supply Agreement; and (f) amounts payable by Buyer under Section 11.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Supplier, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment when due, whether at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise, of all Guaranteed Obligations.

2.2 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. Supplier is not required to proceed first against Buyer, any other guarantor or any security before enforcing this Guaranty against Guarantor.

2.3 **Payment Obligations Only.** This Guaranty covers only Buyer's obligations to pay money. Guarantor does not guarantee Buyer's performance of any non-monetary obligation under the Supply Agreement or any Statement of Work, including Buyer's design, fabrication or installation of facade systems.

2.4 **Defenses of Buyer.** Guarantor may assert against Supplier any defense, setoff or counterclaim that Buyer could assert under the Supply Agreement or a Statement of Work with respect to a Guaranteed Obligation, including any good-faith invoice dispute properly raised under Section 7.3 of the Supply Agreement, other than defenses arising from Buyer's insolvency, bankruptcy, lack of capacity or lack of authority.

## 3. DEMAND AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Demand.** If Buyer fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, and the failure continues for ten Business Days after Supplier gives Buyer written notice of it, Supplier may make written demand on Guarantor. Each demand shall identify the unpaid Guaranteed Obligations, the applicable invoices or Statement of Work, and the amount demanded.

3.2 **Payment.** Guarantor shall pay the amount demanded within fifteen Business Days after receipt of a demand that complies with Section 3.1, in immediately available funds, to the account Supplier designates in writing.

3.3 **No Deductions.** Except as permitted by Section 2.4, Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without setoff, counterclaim or deduction.

## 4. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Obligations Unconditional.** Except as provided in Sections 2.4 and 7, Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or affected by:

- (a) any extension, renewal, amendment or modification of the Supply Agreement or any Statement of Work, or the execution of any additional Statement of Work;
- (b) any waiver, forbearance or extension of time granted by Supplier to Buyer;
- (c) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of Buyer, or any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting Buyer;
- (d) the release, impairment or non-perfection of any security for, or any other guaranty of, the Guaranteed Obligations; or
- (e) any invalidity or unenforceability of a Guaranteed Obligation resulting from Buyer's lack of capacity or authority.

4.2 **Consent to Modifications.** Guarantor consents to any amendment, renewal or extension of the Supply Agreement and any Statement of Work in accordance with their terms, without further notice to or consent of Guarantor.

4.3 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives presentment, protest, notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, notice of dishonor, and, except as required by Section 3.1, all other notices and demands of any kind relating to the Guaranteed Obligations.

4.4 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if at any time any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must otherwise be returned by Supplier upon the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Buyer, as though the payment had not been made.

## 5. SUBROGATION

5.1 **Deferral.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Buyer arising from payments made under this Guaranty.

5.2 **Subordination.** Any indebtedness of Buyer to Guarantor is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations then due and unpaid. Guarantor may receive payments on that indebtedness in the ordinary course unless Buyer is then in default in payment of a Guaranteed Obligation that Supplier has demanded under Section 3.1.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Supplier that, as of the date of this Guaranty:

6.1 **Organization.** Guarantor is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

6.2 **Authority.** Guarantor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action.

6.3 **Enforceability.** This Guaranty is the legal, valid and binding obligation of Guarantor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

6.4 **No Conflict.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate Guarantor's certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law applicable to Guarantor, or any material agreement binding on Guarantor.

6.5 **Ownership.** Guarantor is the sole member of Buyer.

6.6 **Benefit.** Guarantor has received reasonably equivalent value and fair consideration for this Guaranty.

## 7. TERM AND RELEASE

7.1 **Term.** This Guaranty remains in effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid in full, including Guaranteed Obligations under any Statement of Work that remains open after expiration or termination of the Supply Agreement.

7.2 **Release on Substitute Credit Support.** Supplier shall release Guarantor from this Guaranty, by written instrument, upon delivery to Supplier of an irrevocable standby letter of credit or a replacement guaranty from a creditworthy guarantor, in each case in form and amount reasonably acceptable to Supplier, covering the Guaranteed Obligations.

## 8. FINANCIAL INFORMATION

8.1 **Annual Statements.** Within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year of Guarantor, Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier its consolidated balance sheet and statements of income and cash flows for that fiscal year, which may be unaudited if audited statements are not otherwise prepared.

8.2 **Confidentiality.** Supplier shall treat all financial information delivered under this Section 8 as confidential information of Buyer under Section 14 of the Supply Agreement.

9.4 **Expenses.** Guarantor shall reimburse Supplier for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

9.6 **Successors and Assigns.** This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Supplier and its successors and permitted assigns under the Supply Agreement. Guarantor may not assign its obligations under this Guaranty without Supplier's prior written consent.
