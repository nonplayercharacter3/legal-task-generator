# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 2026-03

## Calloway Street Tower Facade

This Statement of Work No. 2026-03 (this "**Statement of Work**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**SOW Date**") by and between **Meideis Glassworks, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Besouth Facade Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). It is entered into under the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Buyer dated as of April 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Statement of Work have the meanings given in the Agreement. Under Section 2.2 of the Agreement, this Statement of Work is incorporated into and governed by the Agreement.

## 1. PROJECT

1.1 **Project.** The Project is the Calloway Street Tower facade, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (the "**Calloway Project**").

1.2 **Project Description.** The Calloway Project is a 31-story mixed-use office and residential tower at 1650 Calloway Street, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19103. Buyer is the curtain-wall subcontractor and will design, fabricate and install a unitized curtain-wall system on all four elevations from Level 3 through the roof parapet.

1.3 **Project Parties.**

| Role | Entity |
|---|---|
| Owner | Calloway Street Partners, LP |
| General Contractor | Delmont Keane Builders, Inc. |
| Architect of Record | Varga Holloway Architects |
| Curtain-Wall Subcontractor | Besouth Facade Systems, LLC |
| Glass Supplier | Meideis Glassworks, Inc. |

1.4 **Buyer's Project Reference.** Buyer Job No. B-2611. Buyer shall cite this number on every Purchase Order issued under this Statement of Work.

## 2. PRODUCTS AND PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 **Products.** Supplier shall supply Products of the following configurations for the Calloway Project, each conforming to Exhibit A of the Agreement:

| Type | Configuration | Location |
|---|---|---|
| IG-1 | MG-62 low-E IGU, clear/clear, 6 mm HS outboard / 12.7 mm argon-filled airspace / 6 mm HS inboard | Typical vision glass, Levels 3–31 |
| IG-2 | MG-70 low-E IGU, low-iron/clear, 6 mm HS outboard / 12.7 mm argon-filled airspace / 6 mm FT inboard | Podium and lobby vision glass, Levels 3–5 |

2.2 **Project-Specific Specifications.** In addition to Exhibit A of the Agreement, Products supplied under this Statement of Work shall comply with the following, which Supplier accepts as Specifications for the Calloway Project:

- (a) Architect's Specification Section 08 44 13 (Glazed Aluminum Curtain Walls) and Section 08 81 00 (Glass Glazing), Issued for Construction set dated January 9, 2026, to the extent applicable to insulating glass units;
- (b) center-of-glass U-factor of 0.29 Btu/hr·ft²·°F or lower for IG-1 and 0.28 or lower for IG-2, calculated per NFRC 100;
- (c) heat-strengthened lites to have a roller-wave distortion not exceeding 0.003 inch peak-to-valley, with rollerwave oriented horizontally;
- (d) warm-edge spacers in black finish; and
- (e) structural silicone secondary seal compatible with Buyer's structural glazing sealant, as confirmed by Supplier's adhesion and compatibility test report to be furnished before the first production run.

2.3 **Mock-Up.** Supplier shall supply, at the Prices in Section 4, units for Buyer's visual mock-up and performance mock-up, within four weeks after Buyer's Purchase Order for them. Mock-up quantities count toward the Committed Quantity.

## 3. COMMITTED QUANTITY

3.1 **Committed Quantity.** The Committed Quantity under this Statement of Work is 42,000 square feet of insulating glass units, allocated by type as follows:

| Type | Committed Quantity (sq. ft. of finished unit area) |
|---|---|
| IG-1 | 36,500 |
| IG-2 | 5,500 |
| **Total** | **42,000** |

3.2 **Measurement.** Quantities are measured as finished unit area, in accordance with Section 6.1 of the Agreement, with each unit rounded up to the nearest whole square foot.

3.3 **Changes.** The Committed Quantity may be increased or reduced only as provided in Section 2.3 of the Agreement. Buyer may reallocate quantities between IG-1 and IG-2 by written notice to Supplier before release of the affected Purchase Order to production, without changing the total Committed Quantity.

## 4. PRICING

4.1 **Project Prices.** The following Prices apply to the Committed Quantity under this Statement of Work and, under Section 6.3 of the Agreement, are fixed and not subject to adjustment under Section 6.2 of the Agreement:

| Type | Price per sq. ft. (FCA Allentown) |
|---|---|
| IG-1 | $28.40 |
| IG-2 | $35.15 |

4.2 **Upcharges.** The fully tempered upcharge and oversize upcharge in Exhibit B of the Agreement apply at the rates stated there. The Project Prices above already include the fully tempered inboard lite for IG-2.

4.3 **Freight.** At Buyer's request under Section 8.1 of the Agreement, Supplier shall arrange freight to Buyer's fabrication facility at 410 Wharf Street, Camden, New Jersey 08103, at Buyer's cost, billed at carrier's rates without markup.

## 5. DELIVERY SCHEDULE

5.1 **Release Schedule.** Buyer shall issue Purchase Orders in releases tied to its unit fabrication sequence. The target schedule is:

| Release | Scope | Target Delivery to Buyer's Facility |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mock-up units | May 2026 |
| 2 | Levels 3–8 (IG-1 and all IG-2) | August 2026 |
| 3 | Levels 9–16 | November 2026 |
| 4 | Levels 17–24 | February 2027 |
| 5 | Levels 25–31 and parapet | May 2027 |

5.2 **Lead Times.** Purchase Orders under this Statement of Work are subject to the standard lead times in Exhibit A of the Agreement. Buyer shall issue each release Purchase Order at least the standard lead time before the requested delivery date.

5.3 **Schedule Adjustments.** Buyer may defer any release by up to sixty days by written notice given at least three weeks before the confirmed delivery date. Deferral does not reduce the Committed Quantity.

5.4 **Attic Stock.** Buyer's Purchase Orders will include attic stock as required by the Project specifications. Attic stock units count toward the Committed Quantity.

## 6. PROJECT SUBMITTALS AND QUALITY

6.1 **Submittals.** Within fifteen Business Days after the SOW Date, Supplier shall furnish to Buyer, for submission to the Architect: product data sheets; NFRC performance calculations for IG-1 and IG-2; IGCC certification; glass samples (12 inches by 12 inches) of each type; and Supplier's standard warranty forms reflecting Section 10 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Quality Control.** Supplier shall retain production inspection records for each release for the warranty periods in Section 10 of the Agreement and make them available to Buyer on reasonable request.

6.3 **Project Labeling.** In addition to the labeling required by Section 8.4 of the Agreement, each unit shall bear a removable label stating Buyer's Job No. B-2611, the unit mark number and the type designation.
