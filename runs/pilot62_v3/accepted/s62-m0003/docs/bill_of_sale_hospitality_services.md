# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of the Closing Date (as defined below) by **Bruneis Hospitality Services LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), to and in favor of **Sablewick Inn Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Transferee**"). Transferor and Transferee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (as it may be amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Transferee.

B. Transferor is an Affiliate of Seller. Transferor holds certain assets used in the operation of the five boutique hotels owned and operated by Transferee.

C. Section 5.8 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause Transferor to convey the Hotel Operating Assets to Transferee at the Closing by executing and delivering this Bill of Sale. Delivery of this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Transferor, is a Closing deliverable under Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

D. Transferor wishes to transfer the Hotel Operating Assets to Transferee, and Transferee wishes to accept them, on the terms of this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for the consideration described in Section 3 and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Transferor and Transferee agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Incorporated Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Bill of Sale, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Annex A**" means Annex A attached to this Bill of Sale, as it may be supplemented under Section 6.3.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given to it in Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Effective Time**" means 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

"**Hotel Operating Assets**" means all furniture, fixtures, equipment and inventory owned by Transferor that are located at the Hotels and listed on Annex A.

"**Hotels**" has the meaning given to it in the Purchase Agreement, being the five boutique hotels owned and operated by Transferee and listed on Schedule 1.1(a) to the Purchase Agreement.

"**Retained Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Transfer Taxes**" has the meaning given to it in Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

1.3 **Interpretation.** The rules of interpretation set forth in Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Bill of Sale, *mutatis mutandis*. References to Sections and Annexes are to Sections of, and Annexes to, this Bill of Sale unless otherwise stated.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

2.1 **Transfer.** Effective as of the Effective Time, Transferor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Transferee, its successors and assigns, and Transferee accepts, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Hotel Operating Assets, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens. Transferee and its successors and assigns shall have and hold the Hotel Operating Assets forever.

2.2 **Manufacturer and Vendor Warranties.** To the extent assignable without the consent of any third party, the transfer in Section 2.1 includes all of Transferor's rights under any express or implied warranty, guaranty, indemnity or service commitment of any manufacturer, supplier, vendor or installer relating to any of the Hotel Operating Assets. To the extent any such right is not assignable, Transferor shall, at Transferee's request and expense for reasonable out-of-pocket costs, enforce that right for the benefit of Transferee and remit to Transferee any recovery obtained.

2.3 **Alcoholic Beverage Inventory.** Inventory of alcoholic beverages included in the Hotel Operating Assets is transferred only to the extent transferable under applicable Law and the Permits for the applicable Hotel. If the transfer of any such inventory requires the approval of a liquor licensing authority that has not been obtained by the Effective Time, title to that inventory shall pass to Transferee upon receipt of that approval. Until then, Transferor shall hold that inventory at the applicable Hotel for the benefit of Transferee and shall not remove, sell or otherwise dispose of it, except for sale and service at the applicable Hotel as permitted by applicable Law, the proceeds of which shall belong to Transferee.

2.4 **Possession.** The Hotel Operating Assets are located at the Hotels and are, as of the Effective Time, in the physical possession of Transferee in the ordinary course of the operation of the Hotels. Transferor shall not be required to make any separate physical delivery of any Hotel Operating Asset. Delivery shall be deemed made at the location of each Hotel Operating Asset as of the Effective Time.

2.5 **Risk of Loss.** Risk of loss of, or damage to, the Hotel Operating Assets passes to Transferee at the Effective Time.

## 3. CONSIDERATION

3.1 **Consideration.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered in consideration of the Purchase Price payable under the Purchase Agreement and the mutual covenants of the Parties in this Bill of Sale. No separate or additional consideration is payable by Transferee or Buyer to Transferor for the Hotel Operating Assets.

3.2 **Allocation.** Transferor and Transferee shall not take any position on any Tax Return that attributes to the Hotel Operating Assets any value inconsistent with any allocation agreed between Buyer and Seller for Tax purposes, except as required by applicable Law.

## 4. LIABILITIES

4.1 **No Assumption.** Transferee does not assume, and shall not be responsible to pay, perform or discharge, any liability or obligation of Transferor or any of its Affiliates of any kind or nature, whether known or unknown, accrued or contingent, and whether arising before, on or after the Effective Time (collectively, the "**Retained Liabilities**"). The Retained Liabilities include:

(a) any liability arising out of the ownership or use of the Hotel Operating Assets by Transferor before the Effective Time;

(b) any liability for Taxes of Transferor or any of its Affiliates, other than Transfer Taxes to be borne as provided in Section 7.1;

(c) any liability of Transferor under any Contract under which Transferor acquired, financed or leased any of the Hotel Operating Assets, including any liability for the unpaid purchase price of any of them;

(d) any liability to any current or former employee, officer, manager or independent contractor of Transferor; and

(e) any liability of Transferor arising under the Hotel Services Agreement dated January 1, 2018 between Transferor and Transferee, including any fee, reimbursable cost or termination payment.

4.2 **Discharge.** Transferor shall pay, perform and discharge the Retained Liabilities as and when they become due.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF TRANSFEROR

Transferor represents and warrants to Transferee, as of the Effective Time, that:

(a) Transferor is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware, and has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to perform its obligations under it;

(b) the execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale by Transferor have been duly authorized by all requisite limited liability company action, and this Bill of Sale constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Transferor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(c) Transferor has good and valid title to each of the Hotel Operating Assets, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens, and has not previously sold, assigned, transferred, pledged or otherwise disposed of any of them;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale by Transferor do not conflict with or violate the certificate of formation or limited liability company agreement of Transferor or any Law applicable to Transferor; and

(e) no consent of, notice to or filing with any Person or Governmental Authority is required for the transfer of the Hotel Operating Assets to Transferee under this Bill of Sale, other than the approvals of liquor licensing authorities described in Section 2.3.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Further Assurances.** From and after the Effective Time, Transferor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, without further consideration, promptly execute and deliver such further deeds, bills of sale, endorsements, assignments, certificates of title and other instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take such further actions, as Transferee may reasonably request to vest in Transferee, perfect or confirm Transferee's title to the Hotel Operating Assets, or otherwise give effect to this Bill of Sale.

6.2 **Titled Assets.** To the extent any Hotel Operating Asset is a motor vehicle or other asset whose title is evidenced by a certificate of title issued by a Governmental Authority, Transferor shall deliver to Transferee at the Closing the original certificate of title for that asset, duly endorsed for transfer to Transferee, together with any odometer disclosure statement and other document required by applicable Law to register the transfer.

6.3 **Supplement to Annex A.** If, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the Closing Date, either Party identifies any item of furniture, fixtures, equipment or inventory owned by Transferor and located at a Hotel at the Effective Time that is not listed on Annex A, the Parties shall execute a supplement to Annex A listing that item, and the item shall be a Hotel Operating Asset for all purposes of this Bill of Sale effective as of the Effective Time.

6.4 **Assets Received After Closing.** If, after the Effective Time, Transferor or any of its Affiliates receives any payment, refund, rebate or other amount relating to any Hotel Operating Asset that belongs to Transferee under this Bill of Sale, including any insurance proceeds for loss of or damage to a Hotel Operating Asset occurring after the Effective Time, Transferor shall hold that amount in trust for Transferee and shall remit it to Transferee within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

6.5 **Records.** Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Transferor shall deliver to Transferee copies of all purchase records, invoices, warranty documentation, operating manuals, maintenance logs and service records in Transferor's possession or control relating to the Hotel Operating Assets, to the extent not already located at the Hotels.

6.6 **Power of Attorney.** Transferor irrevocably appoints Transferee, and any officer of Transferee, as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Transferor but on behalf and for the benefit of Transferee, (a) to demand and receive from time to time any of the Hotel Operating Assets and to give receipts and releases for them; (b) to institute and prosecute any proceeding that Transferee may deem proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Hotel Operating Assets; and (c) to execute any instrument described in Section 6.1 or Section 6.2 that Transferor fails to execute and deliver within ten (10) Business Days after Transferee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 7. TAXES

7.1 **Transfer Taxes.** Any Transfer Taxes arising from the transfer of the Hotel Operating Assets under this Bill of Sale shall be borne as provided in Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

7.2 **Resale Certificates.** Transferee shall deliver to Transferor, to the extent available under applicable Law, any resale certificate or other exemption certificate reasonably requested by Transferor in respect of any inventory included in the Hotel Operating Assets that is held for resale in the ordinary course of business of the Hotels. Transferor shall cooperate reasonably with Transferee in claiming any available occasional-sale, casual-sale or similar exemption from sales or use Taxes.

## 8. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

8.1 **Delivery Under the Purchase Agreement.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under Sections 3.2(c) and 5.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

8.2 **No Duplication.** Transferee shall not be entitled to recover under this Bill of Sale and under the Purchase Agreement more than once in respect of the same Loss.

8.3 **No Other Representations.** Except for the representations and warranties contained in Section 5, in the Purchase Agreement and in any certificate delivered under the Purchase Agreement, Transferor makes no express or implied representation or warranty regarding the Hotel Operating Assets.

Transferee may assign any of its rights under this Bill of Sale, together with any of the Hotel Operating Assets, to any Person that acquires those Hotel Operating Assets or any Hotel, and may collaterally assign its rights under this Bill of Sale to any lender providing financing to Transferee or Buyer. Transferor may not assign this Bill of Sale or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Transferee. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 9.2 shall be null and void.

9.3 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Bill of Sale is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in this Bill of Sale shall confer upon any other Person any legal or equitable right, benefit or remedy; provided, that Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of Section 4 and Section 6 and may enforce them directly.

9.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Bill of Sale may be amended, modified or supplemented only by an instrument in writing signed by each Party. No waiver of any provision of this Bill of Sale shall be effective unless explicitly set forth in writing and signed by the Party so waiving. No failure to exercise, or delay in exercising, any right or remedy under this Bill of Sale shall operate as a waiver of it.

9.5 **Severability.** If any term or provision of this Bill of Sale is invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall not affect any other term or provision of this Bill of Sale or invalidate or render unenforceable that term or provision in any other jurisdiction. Upon such a determination, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to modify this Bill of Sale so as to effect their original intent as closely as possible.

9.6 **Governing Law; Submission to Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.**

(a) This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction; provided, that the registration of title to any motor vehicle included in the Hotel Operating Assets shall be governed by the Laws of the jurisdiction in which that vehicle is titled, to the extent mandatorily applicable.

(b) Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Bill of Sale shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware, and each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

(c) EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY LEGAL ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS BILL OF SALE.

9.7 **Specific Performance.** Transferor agrees that irreparable damage would occur if it failed to perform its obligations under Sections 2 and 6 in accordance with their terms, and that Transferee shall be entitled to specific performance of those obligations, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled at law or in equity, without the necessity of proving the inadequacy of money damages or posting any bond.

9.8 **Headings.** The headings in this Bill of Sale are for convenience of reference only and do not affect its interpretation.

9.9 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall be deemed one and the same instrument. A signed copy of this Bill of Sale delivered by e-mail or other means of electronic transmission shall be deemed to have the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.
