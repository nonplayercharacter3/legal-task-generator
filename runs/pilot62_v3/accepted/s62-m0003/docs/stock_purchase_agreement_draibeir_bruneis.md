# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026, by and between **Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Sablewick Inn Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation owning five boutique hotels (the "**Company**").

B. Bruneis Brand Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Seller (the "**Brand Affiliate**"), owns the Hotel Marks. At the Closing it will assign the Hotel Marks to the Company under the IP Assignment Agreement.

C. Bruneis Hospitality Services LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Seller (the "**Service Affiliate**"), holds certain assets used in the operation of the Hotels. At the Closing it will convey those assets to the Company under the Bill of Sale.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

### 2.1 Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $148,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as set forth in Section 2.4.

### 2.2 Purchase and Sale of the Shares

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for the consideration specified in this Article II.

### 2.3 Payments at Closing

At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or cause to be paid, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to Seller, an amount (the "**Closing Payment**") equal to (i) the Purchase Price, (ii) minus the Escrow Amount, (iii) minus the Estimated Closing Indebtedness, (iv) minus the Estimated Transaction Expenses, and (v) plus the amount, if any, by which the Estimated Working Capital exceeds the Working Capital Target, or minus the amount, if any, by which the Working Capital Target exceeds the Estimated Working Capital;

(b) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, for deposit into the escrow account established under the Escrow Agreement;

(c) on behalf of the Company, to the holders of the Indebtedness listed on the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(e), the amounts specified in those payoff letters; and

(d) on behalf of the Company, to the payees of the Estimated Transaction Expenses, the amounts specified in the invoices delivered under Section 3.2(f).

### 2.4 Purchase Price Adjustment

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* No later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's good-faith estimates of the Working Capital (the "**Estimated Working Capital**"), the Indebtedness (the "**Estimated Closing Indebtedness**") and the Transaction Expenses (the "**Estimated Transaction Expenses**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of the actual Working Capital, Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses as of immediately before the Closing (the "**Closing Statement**").

(c) *Review and Disputes.* Seller shall have forty-five (45) days after receipt of the Closing Statement to review it, during which Buyer shall provide Seller and its Representatives reasonable access to the relevant books, records and personnel of the Company. If Seller disagrees with the Closing Statement, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written notice of disagreement specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the disagreement. Any item not so disputed shall be final and binding. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve any disputed items. Any items remaining in dispute at the end of that period shall be submitted to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the Parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"), which shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator, shall resolve only the items remaining in dispute, and shall render its determination within thirty (30) days. The determination of the Independent Accountant shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed items.

(d) *Payment.* If the Purchase Price as finally determined under this Section 2.4 exceeds the Purchase Price as estimated at Closing, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If the Purchase Price as estimated at Closing exceeds the Purchase Price as finally determined, Seller shall pay the excess to Buyer. Any payment under this Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

### 2.5 Escrow

(a) *Deposit.* At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $14,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX and the obligations of Seller under Section 2.4(d).

(b) *Release.* On the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent directing the release to Seller of the then-remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of all claims for indemnification set forth in any claim notice delivered by Buyer in accordance with Article IX on or before the Release Date that remain unresolved as of that date. Any amount retained for an unresolved claim shall be released, upon final resolution of that claim, to Buyer or Seller, as the case may be, in accordance with that resolution.

(c) *Fees.* The fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller.

(d) *Tax Treatment.* For income Tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is released, and all interest and earnings on the Escrow Fund shall be reported as income of Buyer, to the extent required by applicable Law.

### 2.6 Withholding

Buyer, the Company and the Escrow Agent shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under the Code or any provision of applicable Tax Law. Any amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the deduction and withholding was made. Seller shall deliver an IRS Form W-9 at Closing, upon receipt of which Buyer shall not withhold from payments to Seller except as required by a change in applicable Law.
