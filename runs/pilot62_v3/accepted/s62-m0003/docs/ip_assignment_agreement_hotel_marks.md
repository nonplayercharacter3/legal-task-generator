# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (as defined below) by and among:

- **Bruneis Brand Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**");
- **Sablewick Inn Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**"); and
- **Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Guarantor**"), solely for purposes of Section 6.4 and Article VIII.

Assignor, Assignee and Guarantor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and Guarantor, as buyer, are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (as it may be amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Guarantor all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignor is an Affiliate of Seller. Assignor owns the trademarks, service marks, trade names and logos used to identify the five boutique hotels owned and operated by Assignee, together with the registrations and applications for them and the goodwill associated with them.

C. Section 5.9 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause Assignor to assign the Hotel Marks to Assignee at the Closing by executing and delivering this Agreement. Execution and delivery of this Agreement by each Party is a deliverable at the Closing under Sections 3.2(b) and 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

D. Guarantor will receive substantial direct and indirect benefits from the assignment of the Hotel Marks to Assignee and from the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement. Guarantor is willing to guarantee certain obligations of Assignee under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Assigned Marks**" means (a) the Hotel Marks, including the trademarks, service marks, trade names and logos listed on Schedule A, which reproduces Schedule 1.1(c) to the Purchase Agreement; (b) all registrations and applications for registration of any of them, and all renewals and extensions of those registrations; (c) all common-law rights in any of them; (d) all goodwill of the business connected with the use of, and symbolized by, any of them; and (e) the Associated Rights.

"**Assignment Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Associated Rights**" means (a) all rights to sue for and recover damages, profits and other remedies for past, present and future infringement, dilution, misappropriation or other violation of any of the Hotel Marks, (b) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the Hotel Marks, and (c) all rights of priority and protection of interests in any of the Hotel Marks under the Laws of any jurisdiction.

"**Assignor Indemnitees**" means Assignor, its Affiliates and their respective Representatives.

"**Assignee Indemnitees**" means Assignee, Guarantor, their respective Affiliates and their respective Representatives.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given to it in Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.4(a).

"**PTO**" means the United States Patent and Trademark Office.

"**Wind-Down Period**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1(b).

### 1.2 Interpretation

The rules of interpretation set forth in Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Agreement, *mutatis mutandis*. References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to Articles and Sections of, and Schedules to, this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. ASSIGNMENT

### 2.1 Assignment of the Assigned Marks

Effective as of the Assignment Date, Assignor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, its successors and assigns, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned Marks, free and clear of all Liens. The Assigned Marks are to be held and enjoyed by Assignee, its successors and assigns, as fully and entirely as they would have been held and enjoyed by Assignor had this assignment not been made.

### 2.2 Goodwill

The assignment in Section 2.1 includes the goodwill of the business connected with the use of, and symbolized by, the Assigned Marks. Assignor acknowledges that, upon the Assignment Date, the business of operating the Hotels under the Assigned Marks is conducted by Assignee, and that the goodwill symbolized by the Assigned Marks passes with that business.

### 2.3 Intent-to-Use Applications

To the extent any application listed on Schedule A was filed on an intent-to-use basis under 15 U.S.C. § 1051(b) and no statement of use or amendment to allege use has been filed for it, Assignor assigns that application to Assignee as part of the business to which the mark pertains, which business is ongoing and existing, as required by 15 U.S.C. § 1060(a)(1).

### 2.4 Enforcement of Past Claims

Assignee has the exclusive right, in its own name, to bring, prosecute, settle and retain the proceeds of any claim or action for infringement, dilution or other violation of any of the Assigned Marks, whether that infringement, dilution or violation occurred before, on or after the Assignment Date. Assignor shall reasonably cooperate, at Assignee's expense for reasonable out-of-pocket costs, in any such claim or action, including by joining as a party where required for standing.

### 2.5 Excluded Rights

This Agreement does not assign any right, title or interest in any trademark, service mark, trade name or logo that is not a Hotel Mark, including the corporate names "Bruneis" and "Bruneis Hotel Holdings" and any mark consisting of or containing the word "Bruneis" (the "**Retained Names**"). Assignee shall have no right to use any Retained Name after the Assignment Date.

## ARTICLE III. CONSIDERATION

### 3.1 Consideration

The assignment in Article II is made in consideration of the Purchase Price payable under the Purchase Agreement and the mutual covenants of the Parties in this Agreement. No separate or additional consideration is payable by Assignee or Guarantor to Assignor for the Assigned Marks.

### 3.2 Transfer Taxes

Any Transfer Taxes arising from the assignment of the Assigned Marks shall be borne as provided in Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. RECORDATION AND FURTHER ASSURANCES

### 4.1 Recordation

Assignor authorizes Assignee to record this Agreement, or a short-form confirmatory assignment in the form attached as Schedule B (a "**Confirmatory Assignment**"), with the PTO and with any other registry or Governmental Authority in any jurisdiction in which any of the Assigned Marks is registered or the subject of an application. Assignor shall execute and deliver to Assignee, on the Assignment Date, an original Confirmatory Assignment for recordation with the PTO. Assignee shall bear the official recordation fees.

### 4.2 Further Assurances

From and after the Assignment Date, Assignor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, without further consideration, promptly execute and deliver such further instruments of assignment, declarations, powers of attorney and other documents, and take such further actions, as Assignee may reasonably request to perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned Marks, consistent with Section 5.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

### 4.3 Delivery of Files

Within ten (10) Business Days after the Assignment Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, in electronic form where available, copies of all prosecution files, registration certificates, specimens of use, docketing records, watch reports, cease-and-desist correspondence, coexistence and consent agreements, and other material documents in Assignor's possession or control relating to the Assigned Marks.

### 4.4 Docketed Deadlines

Assignor shall, within five (5) Business Days after the Assignment Date, deliver to Assignee a list of all filing, renewal, maintenance and response deadlines for the Assigned Marks falling within the twelve (12) months after the Assignment Date. Before the Assignment Date, Assignor shall have taken all actions required to be taken in respect of any such deadline falling on or before the Assignment Date.

### 4.5 Power of Attorney

Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee, and any officer of Assignee, as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, deliver and record, in the name of Assignor, any instrument described in Section 4.2 that Assignor fails to execute and deliver within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

### 5.4 Relationship to Purchase Agreement

## ARTICLE VI. INDEMNIFICATION; GUARANTEE

### 6.1 Indemnification by Assignor

From and after the Assignment Date, Assignor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Assignee Indemnitees from and against any and all Losses incurred or sustained by any of them based upon, arising out of or resulting from (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Assignor in Section 5.1, or (b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Assignor in this Agreement, including any use of the Assigned Marks by Assignor or its Affiliates after the Assignment Date other than as permitted by Section 7.1.

### 6.2 Indemnification by Assignee

From and after the Assignment Date, and subject to the limitations set forth in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, Assignee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Assignor Indemnitees from and against any and all Losses incurred or sustained by any of them based upon, arising out of or resulting from (a) the ownership, use or exploitation of the Assigned Marks by or on behalf of Assignee, its Affiliates, licensees or successors from and after the Assignment Date, including any claim by a third party that any such use or exploitation of the Assigned Marks infringes, dilutes or otherwise violates the trademark or other Intellectual Property rights of that third party; (b) the operation of the Hotels under the Assigned Marks from and after the Assignment Date; and (c) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Assignee in this Agreement.

### 6.3 Indemnification Procedures

Claims for indemnification under this Article VI, including the giving of notice, the assumption and conduct of the defense of any Third-Party Claim, and the settlement of claims, shall be made and conducted in accordance with the procedures set forth in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement, which are incorporated into this Agreement *mutatis mutandis*, with each reference in them to the "Indemnified Party" and the "Indemnifying Party" read as a reference to the Party seeking and the Party providing indemnification under this Article VI. Any payment due under this Article VI shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds within ten (10) Business Days after the final determination of the amount owed.

### 6.4 Guarantee

(a) *Guarantee.* Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to the Assignor Indemnitees, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the due and punctual payment and performance of all obligations of Assignee under Section 6.2, as and when they become due (the "**Guaranteed Obligations**").

(b) *Nature of Guarantee.* This guarantee is a guarantee of payment and performance and not of collection. Assignor shall not be required to proceed first against Assignee, to exhaust any remedy against Assignee, or to resort to any security before making a demand on or proceeding against Guarantor. Guarantor shall pay any Guaranteed Obligation within ten (10) Business Days after written demand by Assignor stating that Assignee has failed to pay that Guaranteed Obligation when due.

(c) *Waivers.* Guarantor waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of acceptance of this guarantee, notice of non-payment and all other notices and formalities to which it might otherwise be entitled, and any defense arising by reason of any disability or lack of authority of Assignee, other than the defense of payment or performance of the Guaranteed Obligations in full.

(d) *Obligations Unconditional.* The obligations of Guarantor under this Section 6.4 shall not be released, discharged or otherwise affected by (i) any amendment, modification, extension or waiver of any provision of this Agreement agreed with Assignee; (ii) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of Assignee; (iii) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting Assignee; or (iv) any sale, assignment or other transfer of any of the Assigned Marks by Assignee.

(e) *Reinstatement.* This guarantee shall continue to be effective, or be reinstated, if at any time any payment of any Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must otherwise be returned by any Assignor Indemnitee upon the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Assignee or otherwise, as though that payment had not been made.

(f) *Subrogation.* Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement or contribution against Assignee in respect of any payment made under this Section 6.4 until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full.

### 6.5 Coordination with the Purchase Agreement

No Assignee Indemnitee shall be entitled to recover under this Agreement and under the Purchase Agreement more than once in respect of the same Loss.

## ARTICLE VII. COVENANTS

### 7.1 Cessation of Use by Assignor

(a) From and after the Assignment Date, Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, use, register or seek to register any of the Assigned Marks, or any trademark, service mark, trade name, domain name or logo confusingly similar to any of them, in any jurisdiction, except as permitted by Section 7.1(b).

(b) For a period of sixty (60) days after the Assignment Date (the "**Wind-Down Period**"), Assignor and its Affiliates may continue to use the Assigned Marks solely to the extent they appear on existing corporate stationery, internal documents, archived marketing materials and websites not directed at the public, provided that Assignor and its Affiliates shall not hold themselves out as owning or operating any Hotel and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to remove the Assigned Marks from those materials before the end of the Wind-Down Period.

### 7.2 No Challenge

Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, contest, challenge or assist any third party in contesting or challenging the validity, enforceability or Assignee's ownership of any of the Assigned Marks, or file any petition to cancel or oppose any registration or application of the Assigned Marks.

### 7.3 Termination of Existing Licenses

Any license, sublicense or other permission granted by Assignor or any of its Affiliates to Assignee to use any of the Assigned Marks before the Assignment Date terminates automatically on the Assignment Date, without further action by any Party and without any liability of Assignee for any fee, royalty or other payment under it accruing after the Assignment Date.

### 7.4 Forwarding of Correspondence

Assignor shall promptly, and in any event within ten (10) Business Days after receipt, forward to Assignee any correspondence, notice, official action, demand or inquiry it or any of its Affiliates receives after the Assignment Date relating to any of the Assigned Marks, including any communication from the PTO or any other registry and any assertion by a third party relating to the Assigned Marks.

### 7.5 Payments Received

If Assignor or any of its Affiliates receives any payment that constitutes an Associated Right after the Assignment Date, Assignor shall hold that payment in trust for Assignee and shall remit it to Assignee within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

### 7.6 Confidentiality

Assignor shall treat all non-public information relating to the Assigned Marks, including enforcement strategy and watch reports, as confidential information of the Company subject to Section 5.6 of the Purchase Agreement.
