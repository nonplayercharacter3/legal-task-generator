# JOINT PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Draibeir Hospitality Partners to Acquire Sablewick Inn Group, Owner and Operator of Five Boutique Hotels, from Bruneis Hotel Holdings for $148.5 Million**

*Transaction adds 392 guest rooms across five historic East Coast markets to Draibeir's independent lodging platform*

**NEW YORK, NY and WILMINGTON, DE — September 14, 2026** — Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC ("Draibeir") and Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc. ("Bruneis") today announced that they have signed a definitive Stock Purchase Agreement. Under the agreement, Draibeir will acquire all of the outstanding stock of Sablewick Inn Group, Inc. ("Sablewick"), a Delaware corporation that owns and operates five boutique hotels. The purchase price is $148,500,000, subject to customary adjustments for working capital, indebtedness and transaction expenses at closing.

---

## 1. Transaction Highlights

1.1 **Acquisition of a complete operating platform.** Draibeir will acquire Sablewick as a going concern. This includes the five hotels, their restaurants and bars, and their established teams of hotel associates.

1.2 **Purchase price.** The aggregate purchase price is $148,500,000. Draibeir will fund the purchase price from cash on hand and other available sources of funds. The transaction is not subject to a financing condition.

1.3 **Brand continuity.** At closing, an affiliate of Bruneis will assign the Sablewick family of hotel marks to Sablewick. These include SABLEWICK, HOTEL PELLAM, THE CORVEN HOUSE, LISSET HOTEL, HALDEN ROW and THE PELLAM BAR, together with the associated goodwill. Each hotel will continue to operate under its current name.

1.4 **Portfolio scale.** The five hotels contain a combined 392 guest rooms in Baltimore, Maryland; Charleston, South Carolina; Savannah, Georgia; Providence, Rhode Island; and Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

1.5 **Expected timing.** The parties expect the transaction to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. Closing is subject to customary closing conditions, including expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 and approval of the change in ownership by the liquor licensing authorities with jurisdiction over each hotel.

---

## 2. Transaction Overview

2.1 Under the Stock Purchase Agreement, Draibeir will purchase from Bruneis all of the issued and outstanding shares of Sablewick. The agreement is governed by Delaware law. Because the transaction is structured as a stock purchase, Sablewick will remain the owner of its hotels, contracts, permits and employment relationships after closing, and the hotels will continue to operate without interruption.

2.2 Bruneis is Sablewick's sole stockholder and has owned Sablewick since 2017. Over that period Bruneis grew Sablewick from a single property, Hotel Pellam in Baltimore's Fells Point waterfront district, to a portfolio of five independent boutique hotels. Each hotel is known for its architecture, its food and beverage program and its ties to its neighborhood.

2.3 At closing, two Bruneis affiliates will transfer assets to Sablewick under separate closing instruments. Bruneis Brand Holdings LLC will assign the hotel marks. Bruneis Hospitality Services LLC, which currently provides centralized services to the hotels, will convey certain operating assets to Sablewick. Draibeir and Bruneis will work together on an orderly transition of the centralized services Sablewick currently receives from the Bruneis group.

2.4 Halloran Brook Capital LLC is serving as exclusive financial advisor to Bruneis. Sablewick's existing term loan will be repaid in full at closing.

## 3. The Sablewick Portfolio

Sablewick owns and operates five hotels. Each is an independent, design-led property with a strong local identity and a loyal repeat-guest base.

### 3.1 Hotel Pellam — Baltimore, Maryland

Hotel Pellam stands at 1180 Thames Quay on the Baltimore waterfront and is the portfolio's flagship and largest property, with 142 guest rooms. It occupies a converted early-twentieth-century warehouse and houses The Pellam Bar, a neighborhood destination known for its harbor-view terrace and seasonal cocktail program. The hotel is in the middle of a guest-room renovation of floors four through seven, scheduled for completion in early 2027. Sablewick acquired Hotel Pellam in 2017.

### 3.2 The Corven House — Charleston, South Carolina

The Corven House is a 61-room hotel at 44 Legare Street in Charleston's historic South of Broad neighborhood. It is set in a restored antebellum residence and its garden annex. The hotel's restaurant, Corven Table, has been recognized regionally for its Lowcountry menu. The Corven House joined the portfolio in 2019 and participates in the Historic Lodging Collective marketing consortium.

### 3.3 Lisset Hotel — Savannah, Georgia

The Lisset Hotel is an 88-room property at 309 East Bay Street, overlooking the Savannah River. It is known for its rooftop bar and its meeting and event spaces, which host weddings, corporate retreats and social gatherings throughout the year. Sablewick acquired the Lisset Hotel in 2020, and it also participates in the Historic Lodging Collective.

### 3.4 Halden Row — Providence, Rhode Island

Halden Row is a 54-room hotel on Benefit Street in Providence's College Hill historic district. It opened in 2022 after the adaptive reuse of a row of nineteenth-century commercial buildings. The hotel draws guests visiting the city's universities, arts institutions and dining scene, and its restaurant and courtyard serve both guests and local residents.

### 3.5 Mereweather Inn — Portsmouth, New Hampshire

The Mereweather Inn is a 47-room inn at 115 Ceres Street on the Portsmouth waterfront. It is the most recent addition to the portfolio, acquired in 2024. The inn offers breakfast service, a parlor and porch overlooking the Piscataqua River, and easy access to Portsmouth's historic downtown.

## 4. Strategic Rationale

4.1 **Complementary markets.** The Sablewick hotels sit in coastal and historic markets that complement Draibeir's existing independent lodging portfolio. These markets combine leisure demand with steady weekday business and group travel. The acquisition extends Draibeir's presence along the Eastern Seaboard, from New England to the Lowcountry.

4.2 **Independent positioning.** Each Sablewick hotel operates independently and is not affiliated with a national franchise system. That fits Draibeir's strategy of owning distinctive properties whose identity comes from their buildings, neighborhoods and people, not from a brand standard. Draibeir intends to keep the individual character of each hotel and to keep the Sablewick name as the portfolio's umbrella identity.

4.3 **Food and beverage strength.** Corven Table, The Pellam Bar and the Lisset Hotel's rooftop bar each serve local patrons as well as hotel guests. Draibeir considers these outlets central to the guest experience and to the hotels' standing in their communities, and plans continued investment in them.

4.4 **Reinvestment opportunity.** Draibeir intends to complete the Hotel Pellam guest-room renovation now underway. It also intends to evaluate further capital projects across the portfolio over the next several years, including guest-room refreshes, public-space improvements and technology upgrades.

4.5 **Operational continuity.** Draibeir values the experience of the general managers, department heads and associates who run the Sablewick hotels day to day. Draibeir expects the hotels' leadership teams to remain in place after closing and plans to work with them on Sablewick's next phase.

## 6. Guests, Associates and Partners

6.1 **Guests.** All five hotels remain open and are operating normally. Existing reservations, gift certificates and event bookings will be honored as booked. Guests do not need to take any action because of this announcement. Guests may continue to book through each hotel's existing website, reservations line and current online travel partners.

6.2 **Associates.** Sablewick's associates will remain employed by Sablewick when the transaction closes, because the transaction is a purchase of Sablewick's stock and not a sale of individual hotels. Draibeir has committed that, for twelve months after closing, each Sablewick associate who remains employed will receive base salary or hourly wage rates no less favorable than those in effect immediately before closing. Sablewick's leadership will share more information with associates through the hotels' normal internal channels as the transaction progresses.

6.3 **Vendors and partners.** Sablewick's contracts with suppliers, service providers and partners remain with Sablewick. Vendors should continue to work with their usual contacts at each hotel. Any changes to centralized procurement, accounting or other shared services after closing will be communicated directly to the affected vendors in advance.

6.4 **Communities.** Draibeir and Sablewick intend to continue the hotels' existing community partnerships, local sourcing relationships and charitable programs in each market.

---

## 7. Conditions and Expected Timing

7.1 The parties expect the transaction to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of customary conditions, including:

(a) expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976;

(b) approval by the liquor licensing authorities in Baltimore, Charleston, Savannah, Providence and Portsmouth of the change in ownership and control of Sablewick, as the holder of each hotel's liquor license; and

(c) other customary closing conditions set out in the Stock Purchase Agreement.

7.2 Each party will make its required filing under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act promptly after signing. Sablewick and Draibeir have begun preparing the applications required by the local liquor licensing authorities, so that alcoholic beverage service at each hotel can continue without interruption after closing.

7.3 Until closing, Bruneis will continue to own Sablewick, and Sablewick will continue to operate the hotels in the ordinary course.

## 9. About Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC

Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company headquartered at 75 Wexley Avenue, New York, New York. Draibeir acquires, owns and invests in independent and boutique lodging properties in gateway, coastal and historic markets in the United States. Its approach is to preserve what makes each hotel distinctive while providing the capital, operating resources and long-term ownership perspective needed for each property to grow. Draibeir works closely with on-property leadership teams and places a high priority on guest experience, associate development and community engagement.

---

## 10. About Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc.

Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc. is a Delaware corporation headquartered at 400 Carrow Street, Wilmington, Delaware. Bruneis owns and manages a group of hospitality businesses, including Sablewick Inn Group, Inc. Through its affiliates, Bruneis has provided brand management, centralized reservations, revenue management, sales and marketing, accounting, technology and procurement services to its hotels. Bruneis has owned Sablewick since 2017 and over that time has grown the portfolio from one hotel to five.

## 11. About Sablewick Inn Group, Inc.

Sablewick Inn Group, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that owns and operates five boutique hotels: Hotel Pellam in Baltimore, The Corven House in Charleston, the Lisset Hotel in Savannah, Halden Row in Providence and the Mereweather Inn in Portsmouth. Sablewick's hotels are known for thoughtful design, locally rooted food and beverage programs and personal service. Its tagline, "Small House, Long Stay," reflects the portfolio's focus on intimate hotels that invite guests to linger and to return.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

12.1 **Will the hotels change their names?** No. Each hotel will continue to operate under its current name. The Sablewick family of marks will be assigned to Sablewick at closing, and Draibeir intends to continue using them.

12.2 **Will existing reservations be honored?** Yes. All existing reservations, event contracts and gift certificates will be honored as booked, before and after closing.

12.3 **Will the hotels remain open during the transition?** Yes. All five hotels remain open and are operating normally. The transaction is not expected to affect day-to-day operations at any hotel.

12.4 **Will the Hotel Pellam renovation continue?** Yes. The guest-room renovation of floors four through seven at Hotel Pellam is proceeding on schedule, and Draibeir intends to see it through to completion. The affected floors are taken out of service in phases to limit the impact on guests.

12.5 **What happens to Sablewick's associates?** Sablewick will remain the employer of its associates after closing. Draibeir has committed to maintaining base pay rates for continuing associates for twelve months after closing, as described in Section 6.2.

12.6 **When will the transaction close?** The parties expect closing in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions.

12.7 **Will the hotels continue to serve alcoholic beverages?** Yes. The parties are working with the relevant liquor licensing authorities so that alcoholic beverage service at each hotel can continue without interruption.
