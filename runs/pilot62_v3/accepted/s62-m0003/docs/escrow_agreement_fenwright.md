# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (as defined below) by and among **Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Fenwright Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Principal**" and together the "**Principals**." Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (as it may be amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Sablewick Inn Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $14,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount secures the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement and the obligations of Seller under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Principals wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to receive, hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement. The Escrow Agent is willing to act on those terms.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" means a written notice of a claim for indemnification delivered by Buyer to Seller in accordance with Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, a copy of which Buyer has delivered to the Escrow Agent under Section 4.3.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated, non-interest-bearing or interest-bearing account, as the case may be, established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount as deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a final determination of the Independent Accountant (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) made under Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement, accompanied by a written certification from the Principal presenting it that it is final and that any time to appeal it has expired.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller, delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 10.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Release Date is the next succeeding Business Day.

"**Reserved Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2(a).

1.2 **Authorized Representatives.** "**Authorized Representative**" means, for each Principal, each individual identified on Schedule 1 as authorized to give instructions on behalf of that Principal, as Schedule 1 may be updated by that Principal by written notice to the Escrow Agent and the other Principal.

1.3 **Interpretation.** In this Agreement, the words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation"; references to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of and Schedules to this Agreement; references to "$" are to United States dollars; and headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

2.1 **Appointment.** The Principals appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 **Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall establish the Escrow Account in its name, as escrow agent for the benefit of the Principals, under the account title "Fenwright Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent f/b/o Draibeir Hospitality Partners, LLC and Bruneis Hotel Holdings, Inc. — Project Wick Escrow." The Escrow Fund shall be held in the Escrow Account separate and apart from all other funds of the Escrow Agent and shall not be commingled with any other funds.

2.3 **Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the Escrow Account, in accordance with Section 2.5(a) and Section 2.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to each Principal in writing, which may be by e-mail, on the Business Day on which it is received.

2.4 **No Other Deposits.** The Escrow Agent shall have no duty to solicit, and shall have no responsibility for the collection of, the Escrow Amount or any other amount. The Escrow Agent shall not be responsible for any amount until it has actually received that amount in the Escrow Account in immediately available funds.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF THE ESCROW FUND

3.1 **Permitted Investment.** Unless otherwise directed by Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the Fenwright Treasury Obligations Money Market Fund (Institutional Class), a money market fund investing solely in United States Treasury obligations and repurchase agreements collateralized by them, or in a successor fund of substantially similar character. The Principals may, by Joint Written Instructions, direct the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Fund uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account or to invest it in direct obligations of the United States government maturing within ninety (90) days.

3.2 **Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as necessary to make any disbursement required under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss, penalty or reduction in value resulting from any investment made in accordance with this Section 3 or from any liquidation of an investment made to effect a disbursement.

3.3 **Earnings.** All interest, dividends and other earnings on the Escrow Fund shall be added to and form part of the Escrow Fund and shall be disbursed with the Escrow Fund in accordance with Section 5, except as provided in Section 7.3.

3.4 **Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall provide each Principal with a monthly statement of the Escrow Account, showing all deposits, investments, earnings and disbursements during the period covered and the balance of the Escrow Fund at its end. The Escrow Agent shall make account activity available to the Authorized Representatives through its online portal.

3.5 **Acknowledgment.** The Principals acknowledge that investments in a money market fund are not deposits of the Escrow Agent, are not insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, and may lose value. The Escrow Agent may receive fees from the fund described in Section 3.1 in its capacity as investment adviser or service provider, in accordance with the fund's prospectus.

## 4. CLAIMS

4.1 **Purpose.** The Escrow Fund is held as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement and the obligations of Seller under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement. Under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement, indemnification of the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement is to be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund, to the extent of the funds then remaining in it.

4.2 **Payment of Agreed Amounts.** If Buyer and Seller agree that any amount is owed to Buyer, or to any Buyer Indemnitee, under Article IX or Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement, they shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent directing the disbursement of that amount from the Escrow Fund, and the Escrow Agent shall make that disbursement in accordance with Section 5.4.

4.3 **Copies of Claim Notices.** Whenever Buyer delivers a Claim Notice to Seller, Buyer shall concurrently deliver a copy of it to the Escrow Agent. Each such copy shall state the amount claimed, or Buyer's good-faith estimate of the amount claimed, and the date on which the Claim Notice was delivered to Seller. The Escrow Agent shall have no duty to review any Claim Notice or to determine whether it complies with the Purchase Agreement, and its only obligation in respect of any Claim Notice is to retain the amount stated in it as provided in Section 5.2.

4.4 **Disputed Claims.** If Seller disputes any claim set forth in a Claim Notice, the claim shall be resolved between Buyer and Seller in accordance with the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall disburse any amount held in respect of that claim only upon receipt of Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

## 5. RELEASE AND DISBURSEMENT

5.1 **Disbursement Only on Instruction.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds from the Escrow Fund only (a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, (b) in accordance with a Final Order, or (c) as provided in Section 7.3 and Section 8.

5.2 **Release on the Release Date.**

(a) On the Release Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent, in accordance with Section 2.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement, directing the release to Seller of the then-remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in any Claim Notice delivered by Buyer on or before the Release Date that remain unresolved as of that date (the aggregate of those amounts, the "**Reserved Amount**").

(b) The Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount specified in the Joint Written Instructions to Seller within two (2) Business Days after receipt, and shall continue to hold the Reserved Amount in the Escrow Fund.

(c) If either Principal fails to deliver Joint Written Instructions required by Section 5.2(a) within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the other Principal may seek a Final Order directing the release, and the Escrow Agent shall disburse funds in accordance with that Final Order.

5.3 **Release of the Reserved Amount.** The portion of the Reserved Amount held in respect of any claim shall be released, upon final resolution of that claim, to Buyer or Seller, as the case may be, in accordance with that resolution, as evidenced by Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order delivered to the Escrow Agent. When all claims included in the Reserved Amount have been finally resolved and all related disbursements made, the Escrow Agent shall release any remaining balance of the Escrow Fund to Seller upon receipt of Joint Written Instructions.

5.4 **Manner of Disbursement.** The Escrow Agent shall make each disbursement under this Agreement by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in the applicable Joint Written Instructions or Final Order or, if none is designated, to the account of the applicable Principal set forth on Schedule 2. Unless the applicable instructions provide otherwise, each disbursement shall be made within two (2) Business Days after the Escrow Agent's receipt of the Joint Written Instructions or Final Order directing it. Any instruction received after 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day shall be deemed received on the next Business Day.

5.5 **Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to make any disbursement in full, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the balance then available and shall have no liability for the shortfall. Nothing in this Agreement limits any obligation of Seller to pay any amount owed under the Purchase Agreement in excess of the Escrow Fund.

5.6 **Final Order Procedure.** Any Principal delivering a Final Order to the Escrow Agent shall concurrently deliver a copy of it to the other Principal. The Escrow Agent may conclusively rely on any Final Order and the accompanying certification without inquiry into its validity or finality.

## 6. CONCERNING THE ESCROW AGENT

6.1 **Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Agreement, which are purely ministerial in nature. No implied duties, covenants or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement, is not bound by it, and shall have no duty to inquire into or determine any matter arising under it, including whether any claim is valid or whether any condition to disbursement under the Purchase Agreement has been satisfied. References in this Agreement to the Purchase Agreement are for the convenience of the Principals only.

6.2 **Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely, and shall be protected in acting or refraining from acting, upon any written notice, instruction, certificate, Final Order or other document believed by it in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent shall have no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

6.3 **Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable judgment that its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud was the primary cause of a loss to a Principal. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, even if it has been advised of their possibility. The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemics, governmental action, or failure of communications, utility or payment systems.

6.4 **Advice of Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult with legal counsel of its choice, and any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel shall be full protection to the Escrow Agent.

6.5 **Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or claims from the Principals, or is in good-faith doubt as to its duties, it may (a) refrain from taking any action, other than continuing to hold the Escrow Fund, until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order resolving the conflict, or (b) file an action in interpleader in a court described in Section 11.2 and deposit the Escrow Fund with the clerk of that court, whereupon it shall be released from all further obligations under this Agreement with respect to the amount so deposited.

6.6 **Indemnification of the Escrow Agent.** The Principals, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or the performance of the Escrow Agent's duties under it, except to the extent determined by a court of competent jurisdiction in a final, non-appealable judgment to have resulted from the gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud of the Escrow Agent. As between the Principals, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 6.6, except that a Principal whose breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement gave rise to the indemnified matter shall bear the full amount, and the other Principal may recover from it any amount paid in excess of its share. The Escrow Agent shall not set off any amount owed to it under this Section 6.6 against the Escrow Fund.

6.7 **Security Procedures.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm any funds-transfer instruction, other than Joint Written Instructions delivered in original signed form, by a telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the applicable Principal other than the individual who signed the instruction, at the telephone number set forth on Schedule 1. The Escrow Agent may delay any disbursement until that confirmation is obtained. The Principals acknowledge that these procedures are commercially reasonable. The Escrow Agent may rely on the account number or other identifying number of a beneficiary or a beneficiary's bank provided in any instruction, even if it identifies a Person other than the named beneficiary.

6.8 **Know-Your-Customer.** Each Principal shall provide the Escrow Agent with the documentation and information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering, sanctions and customer-identification Laws, including the USA PATRIOT Act. The Escrow Agent shall not be required to accept the Escrow Amount until it has completed those procedures to its reasonable satisfaction.

## 7. FEES AND TAXES

7.1 **Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3 and to reimbursement of its reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in the performance of its duties. In accordance with Section 2.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement, those fees and expenses shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller. The acceptance fee and the first annual administration fee are payable on the Closing Date.

7.2 **Tax Reporting.** For income Tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is released, and all interest and earnings on the Escrow Fund shall be reported as income of Buyer, to the extent required by applicable Law. The Escrow Agent shall report all such interest and earnings to the Internal Revenue Service on IRS Form 1099 or other applicable form in the name of Buyer, using the taxpayer identification number set forth on the IRS Form W-9 delivered by Buyer. Each Principal shall deliver a properly completed IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date.

7.3 **Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter during which the Escrow Fund has earned income, and upon written request of Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the earnings on the Escrow Fund for that quarter, an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of those earnings, to permit Buyer to pay Taxes on them. No such disbursement shall be made from the principal of the Escrow Amount.

7.4 **Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any disbursement any amount it is required to deduct and withhold under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, or any other applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

## 8. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL; SUCCESSOR

8.1 **Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Principals.

8.2 **Removal.** The Principals may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions given at least thirty (30) days before the effective date of removal.

8.3 **Successor.** Upon any resignation or removal, the Principals shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The successor shall be a bank or trust company organized under the laws of the United States or any state of it, with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed and has accepted appointment within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, the resigning or removed Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 11.2 to appoint a successor. Until a successor is appointed and the Escrow Fund is transferred to it, the Escrow Agent's sole obligation shall be to hold the Escrow Fund and to make disbursements under Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

8.4 **Transfer.** Upon the successor's written acceptance of appointment, the resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Fund, together with copies of all records relating to it, to the successor, after deduction of any fees and expenses then due to it and unpaid by the Principals. The successor shall then have all the rights and obligations of the Escrow Agent under this Agreement.

8.5 **Merger.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or with which it is consolidated, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall be the successor Escrow Agent without further act of any Party.

## 9. TERMINATION

This Agreement shall terminate when the entire Escrow Fund has been disbursed in accordance with this Agreement. Sections 6.3, 6.6, 7.1 and 11 shall survive the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

11.2 **Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware, and each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY LEGAL ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

11.3 **Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement shall be binding upon and shall inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. No Principal may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement except to a Person to which it has assigned its corresponding rights or obligations under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement, and then only upon written notice to the other Parties accompanied by the documentation required under Section 6.8. The Escrow Agent may not assign this Agreement except as provided in Section 8.

11.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, modified or supplemented only by an agreement in writing signed by each Party. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless explicitly set forth in writing and signed by the Party so waiving.

11.5 **Severability.** If any term or provision of this Agreement is invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall not affect any other term or provision of this Agreement or invalidate or render unenforceable that term or provision in any other jurisdiction.

11.6 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in this Agreement shall confer upon any other Person any legal or equitable right, benefit or remedy.

11.7 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall be deemed one and the same agreement. A signed copy of this Agreement delivered by e-mail or other means of electronic transmission shall be deemed to have the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.

11.8 **Publicity.** No Principal shall use the name of the Escrow Agent in any press release or public statement without the Escrow Agent's prior written consent, except as required by applicable Law.
