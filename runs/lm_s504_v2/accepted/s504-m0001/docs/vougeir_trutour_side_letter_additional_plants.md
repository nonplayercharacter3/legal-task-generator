This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") sets out the terms on which Trutour Systems, LLC ("**Licensor**") will add Licensed Plants and Seats to the Licensed Software for Vougeir Linen Services, Inc. ("**Licensee**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## Recitals

A. Licensor licenses the TruRoute Linen Suite RFID linen tracking and route management platform to Licensee under the Agreement. Licensee currently uses the Licensed Software at the five Licensed Plants listed in Part 1 of Schedule A, with up to 150 Seats.

B. Licensee has informed Licensor of the proposed acquisition of Licensee by Ostrander Linen Group, Inc. ("**Ostrander**") under an agreement and plan of merger signed September 28, 2026. Closing is expected in January 2027.

C. In anticipation of the combined business after closing, Licensee has asked Licensor to add two processing facilities as Licensed Plants and to increase the number of Seats. Licensee intends to consolidate route planning and linen tracking for those facilities on the Licensed Software.

D. Section 2.2 of the Agreement provides that additional Seats or Licensed Plants may be added by written agreement of the Parties at Licensor's then-current rates, prorated for the remainder of the then-current contract year.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Additional Licensed Plants

1.1 **Addition.** With effect from the date of this Side Letter, the following facilities are added to Part 1 of Schedule A to the Agreement as Licensed Plants:

| Plant | Location |
|---|---|
| Bethlehem Plant | Bethlehem, Pennsylvania |
| Cherry Hill Plant | Cherry Hill, New Jersey |

1.2 **Implementation.** Licensor shall configure the Licensed Software for each additional Licensed Plant, integrate it with the RFID readers and handheld scanners at that plant, migrate existing route and inventory data for that plant, and train its Authorized Users, in each case under Section 3.1 of the Agreement and a mutually agreed implementation plan. The parties' target is go-live at each additional Licensed Plant by February 15, 2027. Go-live at each additional Licensed Plant occurs as provided in Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

1.3 **Service Levels.** The Quality Agreement applies to each additional Licensed Plant from the date the Licensed Software is deemed live at that plant under Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

## 2. Additional Seats

2.1 **Increase.** With effect from the date of this Side Letter, Part 2 of Schedule A to the Agreement is amended to read: "Up to 210 Seats in aggregate across all Licensed Plants."

2.2 **Allocation.** Licensee may allocate the Seats among the Licensed Plants as it chooses, subject to the aggregate limit in Section 2.1.

## 3. Fees

3.1 **Additional Plant and Seat Fees.** For the additional Licensed Plants and Seats, Licensee shall pay Licensor:

(a) an additional plant fee of $38,000 per Licensed Plant per contract year; and

(b) an additional seat fee of $1,350 per Seat per contract year for the 60 Seats added under Section 2.1.

These fees are in addition to the Annual License Fee.

3.2 **Proration.** For the contract year ending February 28, 2027, the fees in Section 3.1 are prorated from the date of this Side Letter to the end of that contract year. Licensor shall invoice the prorated amount on or after the date of this Side Letter. For each later contract year, Licensor shall invoice the fees in Section 3.1 annually in advance together with the Annual License Fee.

3.3 **Implementation Fees.** Licensee shall pay a one-time implementation fee of $12,500 for each additional Licensed Plant, payable on go-live at that plant under Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

3.4 **Payment.** Section 4.3 (Payment Terms) and Section 4.5 (Taxes) of the Agreement apply to all amounts payable under this Side Letter.

## 4. Effect of this Side Letter

4.1 **Agreement Continues.** Except as expressly set out in this Side Letter, the Agreement and the Quality Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. This Side Letter amends Schedule A to the Agreement for purposes of Section 1.1(f) and Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

4.2 **No Consent or Waiver.** Nothing herein constitutes a consent or waiver under the Agreement, and the execution of this Side Letter does not limit or affect any right or remedy of either Party under the Agreement or the Quality Agreement.

4.3 **Incorporation.** This Side Letter forms part of the Agreement. Sections 16 (Notices) and 18 (Governing Law and General) of the Agreement apply to this Side Letter as if set out in full.
