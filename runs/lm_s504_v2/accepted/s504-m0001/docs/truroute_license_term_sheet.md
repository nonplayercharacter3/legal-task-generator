# TERM SHEET

**TruRoute Linen Suite: Software License**

**Trutour Systems, LLC and Vougeir Linen Services, Inc.**

**Date:** January 17, 2025

## 1. Purpose and Status

1.1 This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Trutour Systems, LLC ("**Trutour**") proposes to license the TruRoute Linen Suite to Vougeir Linen Services, Inc. ("**Vougeir**"). It is the basis for preparing definitive agreements.

1.2 This term sheet is non-binding. Neither party has any obligation regarding the proposed transaction unless and until the parties sign definitive agreements. The only exceptions are Section 13 (Confidentiality) and Section 14 (Costs), which are binding when this term sheet is signed.

## 2. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Trutour Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company |
| Licensee | Vougeir Linen Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation |

## 3. Definitive Documents

3.1 **Software License Agreement.** A software license agreement covering the license grant, fees, data, confidentiality, intellectual property, warranties, indemnities, liability, term, termination and assignment.

3.2 **Quality Agreement.** A separate quality agreement covering availability commitments, data-processing commitments, support and incident response, service credits, reporting and chronic-failure termination rights.

3.3 **Target Signing and Effective Date.** The parties will aim to sign by late February 2025, with an effective date of March 1, 2025.

## 4. Licensed Software

4.1 The license covers the TruRoute Linen Suite RFID linen tracking and route management platform, hosted by Trutour. It includes the documentation and all updates and upgrades Trutour makes generally available to its customers.

4.2 The license is non-exclusive. It is for Vougeir's internal business operations at its processing facilities, and Vougeir's affiliates may use it under Vougeir's account.

## 5. Plants and Seats

5.1 **Initial Plants.** Allentown, PA; Reading, PA; Trenton, NJ; Wilmington (New Castle), DE; and Scranton, PA.

5.2 **Seats.** Up to 150 named-user seats in aggregate across all plants.

5.3 **Additions.** Vougeir may add plants or seats by written agreement at Trutour's then-current rates, prorated for the remainder of the contract year.

## 6. Fees

6.1 **Annual License Fee.** $486,000 per contract year, invoiced annually in advance.

6.2 **Implementation Fees.** $90,000 in total, payable as follows:

(a) $45,000 on completion of data migration for all plants; and

(b) $45,000 on go-live at all plants.

6.3 **Payment Terms.** Net 30 days. Vougeir may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith if it gives written notice before the due date.

6.4 **Fee Adjustments.** Trutour may propose increases to the annual license fee effective on an anniversary of the effective date. Trutour must give advance written notice of any proposed increase. Vougeir will have an objection right, and the parties will negotiate any objected increase in good faith. The notice and objection mechanics will be set out in the definitive license agreement.

## 7. Implementation

7.1 Trutour will configure the platform, integrate it with Vougeir's RFID readers and handheld scanners, migrate existing route and inventory data, and train Vougeir users under a mutually agreed implementation plan.

7.2 Go-live at a plant occurs on Trutour's written notice that the platform is operational in production at that plant.

## 8. Service Levels

8.1 **Availability.** The proposed availability commitment is 99.5% monthly, with tiered service credits against the monthly fee.

8.2 **Data Processing.** RFID scan events will be processed within five minutes. Next-day route sheets will be available by 3:00 a.m. local time. Data will be backed up every four hours.

8.3 **Support.** Support will be available 24/7 for Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents. A Severity 1 incident will receive a response within 30 minutes and a resolution or workaround within four hours.

8.4 **Chronic Failure.** Vougeir may terminate for repeated or severe availability failures, on thresholds to be agreed in the quality agreement.

## 9. Data and Security

9.1 Vougeir owns all of its data, including customer lists, routes, pricing, linen inventory, RFID scan records and linen-volume data. Trutour may use that data solely to provide the service.

9.2 Trutour will maintain industry-standard security safeguards and will give notice of any security incident within 72 hours.

9.3 Trutour may use only de-identified, aggregated statistics that do not identify Vougeir, its customers or its pricing.

9.4 After expiration or termination, Vougeir's data will be returned in a machine-readable format and then deleted.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 **Initial Term.** Three years from the effective date.

10.2 **Renewal.** The license renews automatically for one-year periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

10.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days after notice, and either party may terminate for the other party's insolvency.

10.4 **Transition Assistance.** Trutour will provide up to 90 days of transition assistance after expiration or termination.

## 11. Assignment

11.1 Neither party may assign without the other party's prior written consent.

## 12. Risk Allocation and Governing Law

12.1 **Indemnity.** Trutour will provide a customary IP infringement indemnity. Vougeir will indemnify Trutour for third-party claims arising from Vougeir data.

12.2 **Liability Cap.** Liability will be capped at 12 months' fees. The cap will not apply to indemnities, confidentiality breaches, data breaches, or gross negligence and willful misconduct.

12.3 **Insurance.** Trutour will carry commercial general liability insurance and technology errors and omissions insurance, including cyber coverage, of not less than $5,000,000 per claim.

12.4 **Governing Law.** The governing law will be Delaware, with the courts in Wilmington, Delaware as the forum.

## 13. Confidentiality (Binding)

13.1 Each party will keep confidential the terms of this term sheet and all non-public information it receives from the other party in connection with the proposed transaction. Each party will use that information only to evaluate and negotiate the proposed transaction.

## 14. Costs (Binding)

14.1 Each party will bear its own costs of negotiating and preparing this term sheet and the definitive agreements.
