# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

**TruRoute Linen Suite**

**DRAFT v2: February 11, 2025**
**Trutour draft for discussion, incorporating Vougeir comments of February 4, 2025. Subject to contract. Not for signature.**

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Trutour Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and **Vougeir Linen Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Licensor develops and makes available the TruRoute Linen Suite, a hosted software platform for RFID-based linen tracking and delivery route management.

B. Licensee operates industrial laundry and linen-rental facilities and wishes to license the TruRoute Linen Suite for use in its business.

C. Concurrently with this Agreement, the Parties are entering into a Quality Agreement setting out service levels and service credits for the Licensed Software.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this definition, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**Authorized Users**" means employees and individual contractors of Licensee who are authorized by Licensee to access the Licensed Software on Licensee's behalf.

(c) "**Change of Control**" means (i) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction involving a Party, after which the holders of that Party's voting equity immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the surviving entity; (ii) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of a Party's voting equity; or (iii) the sale of all or substantially all of a Party's assets.

(d) "**Documentation**" means Licensor's user guides, technical specifications and release notes for the Licensed Software, as updated from time to time.

(e) "**Licensee Data**" means all data and information submitted to or generated in the Licensed Software by or for Licensee, including customer lists, delivery routes, route schedules, pricing, linen inventory, RFID scan records and linen-volume data.

(f) "**Licensed Plants**" means the Licensee processing facilities listed in Schedule A, as that Schedule may be amended in writing by the Parties.

(g) "**Licensed Software**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

(h) "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated as of the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

(i) "**Service Levels**" and "**Service Credits**" have the meanings given in the Quality Agreement.

(j) "**Seat**" means a named-user access credential for one Authorized User.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

1.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict between this Agreement and the Quality Agreement, this Agreement prevails, except that the Quality Agreement controls on Service Levels and Service Credits.

## 2. License Grant

2.1 **License.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of the fees under Section 4, Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 14), non-sublicensable right during the Term to access and use the TruRoute Linen Suite RFID linen tracking and route management platform, together with its Documentation and all updates and upgrades Licensor makes generally available to its customers (collectively, the "**Licensed Software**"), for Licensee's internal business operations at the Licensed Plants.

2.2 **Seats.** Licensee may permit up to the number of Seats stated in Schedule A to access the Licensed Software. Additional Seats or Licensed Plants may be added by written agreement of the Parties at Licensor's then-current rates, prorated for the remainder of the then-current contract year.

2.3 **Affiliates.** Licensee's Affiliates may use the Licensed Software at the Licensed Plants under Licensee's account, provided that Licensee remains responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement.

2.4 **Restrictions.** Licensee shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Licensed Software; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Licensed Software, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits it; (c) sell, rent, lease or provide the Licensed Software to third parties on a service-bureau basis; or (d) circumvent any access control or usage limit in the Licensed Software.

2.5 **Reservation of Rights.** All rights not expressly granted to Licensee in this Agreement are reserved by Licensor.

## 3. Implementation and Support

3.1 **Implementation.** Licensor shall configure the Licensed Software, integrate it with Licensee's RFID readers and handheld scanners at the Licensed Plants, migrate Licensee's existing route and inventory data, and provide initial training to Licensee's Authorized Users, in each case under a mutually agreed implementation plan.

3.2 **Go-Live.** The Licensed Software is deemed live at a Licensed Plant when Licensor notifies Licensee in writing that the Licensed Software is operational in production at that Licensed Plant.

3.3 **Support.** Licensor shall provide maintenance and technical support for the Licensed Software as described in the Quality Agreement.

3.4 **Personnel.** Licensor shall assign qualified personnel to perform implementation and support and may replace them on reasonable notice to Licensee.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Annual License Fee.** Licensee shall pay Licensor an annual license fee of $486,000 (the "**Annual License Fee**") for each contract year of the Term, invoiced annually in advance on the Effective Date and on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

4.2 **Implementation Fees.** Licensee shall pay the one-time implementation fees stated in Schedule A upon completion of the milestones stated there.

4.3 **Payment Terms.** Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Licensor in writing of the disputed amount and the basis of the dispute before the payment due date. Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower.

4.4 **Fee Adjustments.** Licensor may propose an increase to the Annual License Fee, effective on any anniversary of the Effective Date, by giving Licensee written notice of the proposed fee at least ninety (90) days before that anniversary. Licensee may object to the proposed fee by written notice to Licensor given within 30 days after the date of Licensor's fee adjustment notice. If Licensee objects, the Parties shall negotiate the proposed fee in good faith, and if they do not agree before the stated anniversary, the Annual License Fee then in effect continues to apply for the following contract year.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay all such taxes, other than taxes on Licensor's net income, payroll or property.

## 5. Licensee Data

5.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, Licensee owns all Licensee Data. Licensee grants Licensor a non-exclusive license during the Term to host, process and use Licensee Data solely to provide the Licensed Software and support to Licensee.

5.2 **Security.** Licensor shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Licensee Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss, consistent with industry standards for hosted software services.

5.3 **Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Licensee Data, and shall cooperate with Licensee to investigate and remediate it.

5.4 **Aggregated Data.** Licensor may compile de-identified, aggregated statistics on use of the Licensed Software, provided that such statistics do not identify Licensee, its customers or its pricing.

5.5 **Return of Data.** Within thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, Licensor shall make all Licensee Data available to Licensee for export in a standard, machine-readable format, and shall then delete it from its systems, except for archival backup copies retained under Licensor's ordinary retention policies, which remain subject to Section 6.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including Licensee Data and the terms of this Agreement.

6.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 6, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.

6.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

6.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

6.5 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 6 survive for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely for Licensee Data and trade secrets.

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 **By Licensor.** Licensor shall defend Licensee and its Affiliates against any third-party claim alleging that the Licensed Software infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret, and shall pay any damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

9.2 **Exclusions.** Section 9.1 does not apply to claims arising from (a) modifications not made by or for Licensor; (b) combination with items not supplied or approved by Licensor, if the claim would not have arisen without the combination; or (c) use in breach of this Agreement.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

10.2 **Cap.** Each Party's total liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid and payable by Licensee in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

10.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 10.1 and 10.2 do not apply to a Party's indemnification obligations, breach of Section 6, Licensor's breach of Section 5, or a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 Licensor shall maintain during the Term commercial general liability insurance and technology errors and omissions insurance (including cyber liability coverage) with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per claim, and shall provide certificates of insurance on Licensee's request.

## 12. Term and Renewal

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the License Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

12.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 13. Termination

13.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Chronic Service Failure.** Licensee may terminate this Agreement as provided in the Quality Agreement.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Licensee's rights under Section 2 end, Licensor shall comply with Section 5.5, and Licensor shall refund any prepaid fees for the period after the effective date of termination, unless Licensor terminated under Section 13.1.

13.5 **Transition Assistance.** On Licensee's request, Licensor shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days after expiration or termination at its then-current professional services rates.

13.6 **Survival.** Sections 1, 4 (as to accrued amounts), 5.5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 13.4 through 13.6, and 18 survive expiration or termination.

## 14. Assignment

14.1 **Consent Required.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent.

14.2 **Change of Control.** A Change of Control of a Party is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by that Party for purposes of Section 14.1.

14.3 **Void Assignments.** Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void.

14.4 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. Subcontracting

16.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses stated on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice.
