# NON-BINDING TERM SHEET

## Proposed Software License: Veterinary LIMS and Instrument-Interface Platform

**Date:** January 20, 2026

**Licensor:** Rotounde Labware Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Rotounde**" or "**Licensor**")

**Licensee:** Pupeska Animal Diagnostics, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Pupeska**" or "**Licensee**")

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Licensor and Licensee (each a "**Party**") propose to enter into a software license agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). Except for Sections 10, 11 and 12, this term sheet is not binding. It is for discussion purposes only.

## 1. Transaction Overview

1.1 **Products.** Licensor will license to Licensee its cloud-hosted veterinary laboratory information management system ("**LIMS**"), its analyzer-interface platform, and its client portal (together, the "**Software**"). The license will cover use at Licensee's reference laboratory sites (the "**Licensed Sites**").

1.2 **Initial Licensed Sites.** Albany, New York (central reference laboratory); Rochester, New York; Hartford, Connecticut; and Allentown, Pennsylvania. Additional sites may be added by written amendment.

1.3 **Partner Clinic Access.** Independent veterinary clinics that submit specimens to Licensee ("**Partner Clinics**") may use the client portal to submit requisitions and view results. Licensee will be responsible for Partner Clinic compliance with the portal terms of use.

1.4 **Target Effective Date.** April 1, 2026.

## 2. Initial Term

2.1 The initial term will be 36 months from the effective date of the Definitive Agreement.

## 3. Renewal

3.1 After the initial term, the Definitive Agreement will continue for 12-month renewal terms, terminable on 60 days' notice before expiry.

3.2 Pricing for renewal terms will be set out in the Definitive Agreement.

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** $684,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

4.2 **Licensed Instrument Count.** The annual subscription fee covers up to 120 laboratory analyzers at the Licensed Sites that are interfaced with the Software.

4.3 **Instrument Overage.** $1,800 per interfaced analyzer per contract year above the licensed instrument count.

4.4 **Payment Terms.** Net 30 days from invoice date. Licensee may withhold amounts disputed in good faith.

4.5 **Implementation Services.** Implementation services will be priced under a separate statement of work.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 In consideration of the pricing in Section 4, Licensee will agree to an exclusivity commitment covering LIMS software only, at Licensed Sites in the United States, during the term only.

5.2 The commitment will not restrict Licensee's choice of diagnostic imaging, practice management or other non-LIMS software, and it will not apply to facilities outside the United States.

## 6. Service Levels and Support

6.1 **Availability.** 99.9% monthly availability, excluding scheduled maintenance notified in advance.

6.2 **Service Credits.** Credits against the subscription fee for missed availability, subject to a monthly cap to be agreed.

6.3 **Support.** 24x7 support for Severity 1 issues. Business-hours support for all other issues.

## 7. Data and Security

7.1 Licensee will own all Licensee data. Licensor will use that data only to provide the Software and related services.

7.2 Licensor will maintain industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest and an annual SOC 2 Type II audit.

7.3 On expiration or termination, Licensor will make Licensee data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format.

## 8. Indemnities and Liability

8.1 **Licensor Indemnity.** Licensor will defend Licensee against third-party claims that the Software infringes or misappropriates intellectual property rights.

8.2 **Licensee Indemnity.** Licensee will defend Licensor against third-party claims arising from Licensee's use of the Software in breach of the license restrictions.

8.3 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Customary exclusions from the cap will apply, including Licensor's intellectual property indemnity, breach of confidentiality, and gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud.

8.4 **Consequential Damages.** There will be a mutual exclusion of indirect and consequential damages, subject to the same exclusions.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 The Definitive Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts located in New York County, New York will have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 This term sheet and the discussions relating to it are confidential information under the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties dated December 2, 2025.

## 11. Costs

11.1 Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this term sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreement.

## 12. Non-Binding Effect

12.1 This term sheet is an expression of the Parties' current intentions only. It does not create any legally binding obligation, except under Sections 10, 11 and 12. No Party will be bound to complete the proposed transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement has been executed and delivered by both Parties.

12.2 Either Party may end negotiations at any time, for any reason, without liability to the other Party.
