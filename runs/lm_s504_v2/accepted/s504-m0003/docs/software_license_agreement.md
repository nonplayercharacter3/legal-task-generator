# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Rotounde Labware Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 410 Harrow Point Drive, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Licensor**"), and **Pupeska Animal Diagnostics, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 85 Calder Street, Albany, New York 12207 ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** Licensor develops and provides a cloud-hosted veterinary laboratory information management system and an analyzer-interface platform. Licensee operates a network of veterinary reference laboratories and wishes to license that platform for use at its laboratory sites on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months from the Effective Date, ending March 31, 2029 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive Renewal Terms. It will continue to renew at the end of each Renewal Term unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal during the Non-Renewal Window for the then-current term. A notice of non-renewal given outside the Non-Renewal Window has no effect for that term.

2.3 **Terms of Renewal.** Each Renewal Term is governed by this Agreement as then in effect, subject to the Fee adjustments in Section 4.6.

3.2 **Partner Clinic Access.** Licensee may permit Partner Clinics to access the client portal to submit test requisitions and view results for specimens they submit to Licensee. Licensee is responsible for each Partner Clinic's compliance with the portal terms of use in the Documentation.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** Licensee shall pay Licensor a subscription fee of $684,000 per year (the "**Annual Subscription Fee**"). Licensor will invoice it annually in advance at the start of each Contract Year.

4.2 **Professional Services.** Fees for implementation and other professional services are set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

4.3 **Payment Terms.** Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after the invoice date, in U.S. dollars, by wire or ACH transfer to the account Licensor designates. Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

4.4 **Licensed Instrument Count; Overage.** The Annual Subscription Fee covers up to 120 Connected Instruments (the "**Licensed Instrument Count**"). Licensor will measure the number of Connected Instruments on the last day of each calendar quarter. If the highest quarterly count in a Contract Year exceeds the Licensed Instrument Count, Licensee shall pay an overage fee of $1,800 per Connected Instrument per contract year above the Licensed Instrument Count. Licensor will invoice the overage fee in arrears at the end of the Contract Year, calculated per Connected Instrument in excess of the Licensed Instrument Count, without proration.

4.5 **Disputed Invoices.** Licensee may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Licensor in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

4.6 **Renewal Pricing.** At the start of each Renewal Term, and at the start of each Contract Year within a Renewal Term, the Annual Subscription Fee and the per-instrument overage fee will each increase by 7% over the amount in effect for the immediately preceding Contract Year.

4.7 **Taxes.** The Fees exclude sales, use, value-added, and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay those taxes, other than taxes on Licensor's net income, unless Licensee provides a valid exemption certificate.

4.8 **Suspension.** If any undisputed amount remains unpaid more than 45 days after its due date, Licensor may suspend access to the Software after giving Licensee at least 15 days' further written notice. Licensor shall restore access promptly once payment is received.

## 7. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

7.1 **Support.** Licensor shall provide telephone, email, and portal support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for Severity 1 issues, and from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days for all other issues.

7.2 **Availability.** Licensor shall make the Software available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least 72 hours in advance and performed between 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m. Eastern Time.

7.3 **Service Credits.** If availability falls below 99.9% in a month, Licensee will receive a credit of 5% of the monthly portion of the Annual Subscription Fee for each 0.5% (or part of it) below the target, up to 25% of that monthly portion. Service credits are applied against the next invoice.

7.4 **Updates.** Licensor shall provide all Updates to Licensee at no additional charge during the Term.

## 8. DATA AND SECURITY

8.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, Licensee owns all Licensee Data. Licensee grants Licensor a non-exclusive license to host, process, and use Licensee Data only to provide the Software and services under this Agreement.

8.2 **Security.** Licensor shall maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with industry standards for hosted laboratory systems. These safeguards include encryption of Licensee Data in transit and at rest, and an annual SOC 2 Type II audit, the report of which Licensor shall provide on request.

8.3 **Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee within 48 hours after confirming any unauthorized access to Licensee Data, and shall cooperate reasonably in investigating and remediating the incident.

8.4 **Return of Data.** For 60 days after expiration or termination, Licensor shall make Licensee Data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format. Licensor shall then delete Licensee Data, except as required by law.
