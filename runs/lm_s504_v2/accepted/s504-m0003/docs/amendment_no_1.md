# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Rotounde Labware Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and **Pupeska Animal Diagnostics, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Licensee**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to a Software License Agreement dated as of April 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

B. Licensee has asked Licensor to provide a module that prepares and transmits reports of reportable animal diseases to governmental animal-health authorities. Licensor is willing to provide that module on the terms of this Amendment.

C. The Parties also wish to amend and restate the indemnification provisions of the Agreement to reflect the addition of that module.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS; REGULATORY REPORTING MODULE

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **New Definitions.** Section 1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following definitions:

"**Reportable Disease**" means any animal disease, pathogen, or test result that applicable federal, state, or local law requires a veterinary laboratory to report to a governmental animal-health or public-health authority.

"**Reporting Authority**" means any governmental authority to which a Reportable Disease must be reported, including state animal-health officials, state public-health departments, and the United States Department of Agriculture.

"**Regulatory Reporting Module**" means the component of the Software that identifies results that may indicate a Reportable Disease, based on rule sets configured with Licensee, and that formats and transmits reports of those results to Reporting Authorities in the electronic formats those authorities accept, as described in Schedule 1 to Amendment No. 1 and in the Documentation.

1.3 **Software.** Section 1.16 of the Agreement is amended and restated as follows:

1.16 "**Software**" means Licensor's hosted veterinary laboratory information management system, the Instrument Interface Module, the client portal, the Regulatory Reporting Module, and all Updates, as described in the Documentation.

1.4 **Availability of the Module.** Licensor shall make the Regulatory Reporting Module available in production for use at all Licensed Sites no later than December 1, 2026. Licensor shall perform the configuration, rule-set development, and Reporting Authority connectivity testing described in Schedule 1 at no additional charge.

## 3. FEES FOR THE REGULATORY REPORTING MODULE

3.1 **Module Fee.** Beginning on the date the Regulatory Reporting Module is first made available in production under Section 1.4 of this Amendment, Licensee shall pay Licensor a fee of $72,000 per year for the Regulatory Reporting Module (the "**Module Fee**"). For the Contract Year in which that date falls, the Module Fee will be prorated on a daily basis to the end of that Contract Year and invoiced on that date. For each later Contract Year, the Module Fee will be invoiced annually in advance with the Annual Subscription Fee.

3.2 **Treatment as Fees.** The Module Fee is part of the Fees and is payable under Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Section 4.6 of the Agreement applies to the Module Fee in the same way it applies to the Annual Subscription Fee.

3.3 **Service Levels.** The Regulatory Reporting Module is part of the Software for purposes of Section 7 of the Agreement.

## 4. REGULATORY REPORTING

4.1 Section 15.2 of the Agreement is amended and restated as follows:

15.2 Licensee remains responsible for determining which results must be reported to Reporting Authorities and for the clinical review and release of each result before it is reported. Licensor shall operate the Regulatory Reporting Module so that results Licensee has released, and that the configured rule sets identify as reportable, are transmitted to the applicable Reporting Authority within the time stated in Schedule 1. Licensor shall promptly notify Licensee of any failed or rejected transmission of which it becomes aware.

4.2 Licensor shall update the rule sets and transmission formats of the Regulatory Reporting Module within 30 days after Licensee notifies Licensor of a change in the reporting requirements of a Reporting Authority. Licensor shall also make those updates within 30 days after a Reporting Authority publishes a change in its accepted electronic format.

## SCHEDULE 1: REGULATORY REPORTING MODULE

**1. Functionality.** The Regulatory Reporting Module will:

(a) apply rule sets, configured with Licensee, to released results in order to flag results that may indicate a Reportable Disease, based on test code, organism, species, and the jurisdiction of the submitting Partner Clinic or Licensed Site;

(b) generate reports in the electronic formats accepted by each Reporting Authority in New York, Connecticut, and Pennsylvania, and by the United States Department of Agriculture, as listed in the Documentation;

(c) transmit each report to the applicable Reporting Authority within 4 hours after the result is released, or within any shorter period that applicable law requires and that Licensee identifies to Licensor in writing; and

(d) keep an audit log of each report, including the time it was transmitted, the acknowledgment or rejection received, and the user who released the underlying result.

**2. Implementation.** Licensor shall complete the following by December 1, 2026:

(a) configuration of the initial rule sets for each Licensed Site;

(b) connectivity and test-message validation with each Reporting Authority listed in paragraph 1(b); and

(c) training for up to 20 Authorized Users designated by Licensee.

**3. Acceptance.** Licensee will have 15 business days after Licensor notifies it that the Regulatory Reporting Module is ready for production to confirm that test reports were successfully received by each Reporting Authority listed in paragraph 1(b). Licensee may notify Licensor in writing of any failure during that period. Licensor shall correct any failure so notified and resubmit the module for confirmation.
