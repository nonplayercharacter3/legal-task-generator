# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of April 1, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Rotounde Labware Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and **Pupeska Animal Diagnostics, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Licensee**"). It is entered into under the Software License Agreement between the Parties dated as of April 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

This SOW is a Statement of Work under Section 5.1 of the Agreement and is governed by the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 **Overview.** Licensor shall implement the Software for production use at the four Licensed Sites listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement (the "**Services**"). The Services consist of:

(a) project planning and governance;

(b) configuration of the Software;

(c) migration of historical data from Licensee's legacy laboratory system;

(d) interfacing of laboratory analyzers at the Licensed Sites through the Instrument Interface Module;

(e) set-up of the client portal for Partner Clinic access under Section 3.2 of the Agreement;

(f) testing, training and go-live support.

1.2 **Configuration.** Licensor shall configure the Software to Licensee's requirements as documented in the Configuration Workbook (Deliverable D-2). Configuration covers:

(a) test menus, test codes, reference ranges by species, and panel definitions;

(b) specimen accessioning and barcode label formats;

(c) result review and release workflows, including the clinical review steps described in Section 6.1(d) of the Agreement;

(d) user roles and permissions for Authorized Users;

(e) billing codes and report templates.

1.3 **Data Migration.** Licensor shall extract, transform, and load the following data from Licensee's legacy laboratory system into the Software:

(a) client and Partner Clinic master records;

(b) patient (animal) records;

(c) up to seven years of finalized result history.

Licensee shall provide the legacy data extracts in the format described in the Data Migration Plan (Deliverable D-3). Licensor shall perform up to two trial migrations and one production migration for each Licensed Site.

1.4 **Analyzer Interfaces.** Licensor shall interface the laboratory analyzers at the Licensed Sites listed in Appendix 1 with the Software through the Instrument Interface Module. Licensor shall use its standard interface drivers for analyzers listed in the Documentation as supported devices. Bidirectional interfaces (order download and result upload) will be configured wherever the analyzer supports them. If an analyzer is not a supported device or needs custom interface development, Licensor shall notify Licensee, and that work will be handled through a Change Order under Section 6.

1.5 **Client Portal.** Licensor shall configure the client portal for Partner Clinic requisition submission and result viewing. This includes branding, requisition forms, and result-notification settings. Licensee is responsible for enrolling Partner Clinics and issuing their portal credentials.

1.6 **Training.** Licensor shall provide:

(a) administrator training for up to 10 Licensee personnel;

(b) end-user training for up to 80 Authorized Users, delivered on site at each Licensed Site or remotely;

(c) recorded training modules that Licensee may use internally during the Term.

1.7 **Go-Live Support.** For each Licensed Site, Licensor shall provide on-site support for the first five business days after go-live. It shall then provide dedicated remote hypercare support for a further 20 business days. After the hypercare period, support is provided under Section 7 of the Agreement.

1.8 **Exclusions.** The Services do not include:

(a) procurement, installation, or maintenance of analyzers, workstations, label printers, or network equipment;

(b) remediation of errors in Licensee's legacy data beyond the transformation rules agreed in the Data Migration Plan;

(c) interfaces to third-party billing, accounting, or practice management systems.

Any of these may be added by Change Order.

## 3. ACCEPTANCE

3.1 **Acceptance Procedure.** This Section 3 sets out the acceptance procedures referred to in Section 5.3 of the Agreement. When a Deliverable or go-live milestone is complete, Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing. Licensee will then have 10 business days (the "**Acceptance Period**") to review or test it against the requirements of this SOW and the approved Configuration Workbook.

3.2 **Rejection.** Licensee may reject a Deliverable or milestone by written notice during the Acceptance Period. The notice must describe each material non-conformity in reasonable detail. Licensor shall correct the non-conformities and resubmit within 10 business days, and the procedure in this Section 3 will apply again.

3.3 **Deemed Acceptance.** A Deliverable or milestone is accepted on the earlier of:

(a) Licensee's written acceptance;

(b) the end of the Acceptance Period without a rejection notice;

(c) Licensee's use of the relevant Licensed Site's configuration to release results to Partner Clinics in production for more than 10 business days, other than during a correction period under Section 3.2.

3.4 **Repeated Failure.** If a go-live milestone is rejected three times for the same material non-conformity, Licensee may treat the failure as a material breach for purposes of Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

## 4. LICENSEE RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 Licensee shall:

(a) appoint a project manager with authority to make day-to-day project decisions;

(b) make subject-matter experts from each Licensed Site reasonably available;

(c) provide legacy data extracts, test menus, and analyzer inventories by the dates in the Project Plan;

(d) provide Licensor's personnel with reasonable on-site access, workspace, and network connectivity at the Licensed Sites;

(e) ensure that analyzers, workstations, and network infrastructure meet the requirements in the Documentation;

(f) arrange for analyzer manufacturers' field service support where it is needed to enable an interface.

## 6. CHANGE ORDERS

6.1 Either Party may request a change to the scope, Deliverables, or schedule of the Services. Within 10 business days after a request, Licensor shall provide a written change proposal. The proposal must describe the change, its effect on the schedule, and any additional fees.

6.2 A change becomes binding only when both Parties' project managers or other authorized representatives sign a change order describing it (a "**Change Order**"). Until then, Licensor shall continue to perform the Services as described in this SOW.

## 7. PERSONNEL AND GOVERNANCE

7.1 **Personnel.** Section 5.2 of the Agreement applies to Licensor's personnel. Licensor shall name a dedicated project manager and a lead interface engineer in the Project Plan. Licensor shall not reassign either of them during the period from kickoff through milestone M-4, except for resignation, leave, or termination of employment, without Licensee's prior written consent. That consent may not be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 **Site Rules.** While at a Licensed Site, Licensor's personnel shall comply with Licensee's safety, biosafety, and security rules as notified to Licensor in advance.

7.3 **Status Reporting.** The Parties' project managers shall meet weekly. Licensor shall provide a written status report each week covering progress against the Project Plan, open risks and issues, and pending Change Orders.

7.4 **Escalation.** Any issue the project managers cannot resolve within five business days will be escalated to Licensee's Chief Operating Officer and Licensor's Vice President of Customer Delivery.
