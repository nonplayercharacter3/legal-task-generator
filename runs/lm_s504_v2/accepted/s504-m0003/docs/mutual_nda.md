# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of December 2, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Rotounde Labware Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 410 Harrow Point Drive, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Rotounde**"), and **Pupeska Animal Diagnostics, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 85 Calder Street, Albany, New York 12207 ("**Pupeska**"). Rotounde and Pupeska are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate a possible business relationship. Under that relationship, Rotounde would license its veterinary laboratory information management system and analyzer-interface platform to Pupeska for use at Pupeska's reference laboratories (the "**Purpose**"). Each Party is willing to disclose information for the Purpose on the terms of this NDA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party or its Affiliates (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Affiliates (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date, and in any form. Confidential Information includes:

(a) software, source code, system architecture, interface specifications, product roadmaps, and technical documentation;

(b) pricing, proposals, financial information, and business plans;

(c) laboratory operations, test menus, specimen volumes, instrument inventories, and information about clinics and other customers; and

(d) the existence and content of the Parties' discussions about the Purpose.

Information disclosed in writing or electronically is Confidential Information if it is marked "confidential" or with a similar legend. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure. In either case, information is also Confidential Information if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, and lenders.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

2.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any person except to its Representatives who meet both of the following conditions:

(a) they need to know the information for the Purpose; and

(b) they are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA, whether by written agreement or by professional duty.

The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

2.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event with less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing on becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably to regain possession of the information and to prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

2.5 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software, prototype, or other tangible item provided to it as Confidential Information.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to any information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no act or omission of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before it was received from the Disclosing Party;

(c) is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order. Where lawful, the Receiving Party shall first give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's expense, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. PROTECTED HEALTH AND CLIENT INFORMATION

4.1 The Parties do not intend to exchange specimen-level or client-identifying laboratory data for the Purpose. If either Party needs sample data to evaluate interface compatibility, Pupeska will provide de-identified or synthetic data where practicable. Any client-identifying data that is disclosed remains Pupeska's Confidential Information and may be used only for the Purpose.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within 15 days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession or control, including copies, notes, and extracts. It shall confirm in writing, through an officer, that it has done so.

5.2 The Receiving Party may retain:

(a) copies kept in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible; and

(b) one archival copy where required by law or by its bona fide document-retention policy.

Any retained information remains subject to this NDA for as long as it is retained.

## 6. TERM

6.1 This NDA applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period from the NDA Effective Date until the second anniversary of the NDA Effective Date. Either Party may end that period earlier by giving 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

6.2 The Receiving Party's obligations for Confidential Information disclosed during that period continue for three years after the period ends. For trade secrets, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 7. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY; NO OBLIGATION

7.1 **Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right in any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark, or other intellectual property is granted or implied by this NDA, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

7.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "AS IS." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty about its accuracy or completeness. The Disclosing Party has no liability arising from the Receiving Party's use of it, except as stated in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

7.3 **No Obligation to Proceed.** This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information or to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either Party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or restrain any actual or threatened breach. It need not post a bond, and this right is in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing Law; Venue.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or relating to this NDA, and each Party submits to that jurisdiction.

9.2 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered to the addresses on the signature page. Delivery may be by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt. Notices are effective on receipt.

9.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the other Party's prior written consent. However, either Party may assign this NDA without consent to a successor in a merger or a sale of all or substantially all of its assets, on written notice to the other Party.

9.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

9.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

9.6 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
