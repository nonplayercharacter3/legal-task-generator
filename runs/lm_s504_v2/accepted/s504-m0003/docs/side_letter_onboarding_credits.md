## 1. Purpose

1.1 Licensee has told Licensor that it intends to interface a significant number of laboratory and diagnostic devices with the Software during the first year of the Term. These include devices used by the independent veterinary clinics that submit specimens to Licensee. Licensor wishes to encourage early and complete onboarding. It therefore agrees to grant Licensee the onboarding credits described in this Side Letter.

## 2. Onboarding Credit

2.1 **Definition.** "**Connected Instrument**" means any analyzer, workstation, or other device that transmits data to the Software, whether directly or through the Instrument Interface Module, the client portal, or any middleware approved by Licensor. This includes analyzers at the Licensed Sites, including point-of-care devices at Partner Clinics.

2.2 **Credit Amount.** Licensor shall grant Licensee a one-time credit of $250 for each Connected Instrument that is interfaced with the Software and transmitting data in production on or before December 31, 2026 (the "**Onboarding Credit**").

2.3 **Expected Volume.** Licensee currently expects to interface approximately 340 Connected Instruments located at Partner Clinics in addition to the analyzers at the Licensed Sites. Licensee shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide Licensor with the device information reasonably needed to complete those interfaces before December 31, 2026.

2.4 **Verification.** By January 15, 2027, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a report listing each Connected Instrument that qualifies for the Onboarding Credit. For each one, the report shall show its location, device type, and the date it first transmitted data in production. Licensee may raise any objection to the report in writing within 30 days after receiving it. The Parties shall resolve any objection in good faith. Licensor's interface logs will be the primary evidence of the date on which a device first transmitted data.

## 3. Application of Credit

3.1 The total Onboarding Credit, as shown in the report under Section 2.4 and adjusted for any objections resolved under that Section, will be applied against the invoice for the Annual Subscription Fee for the second Contract Year, beginning April 1, 2027.

3.2 If the total Onboarding Credit exceeds the amount of that invoice, Licensor shall apply the remaining balance against the next invoices issued under the Agreement until it is exhausted.

3.3 The Onboarding Credit has no cash value. It is not refundable, except that Licensor shall pay any unapplied balance to Licensee within 30 days after expiration or termination of the Agreement, other than termination by Licensor under Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

## 4. Implementation Support

4.1 Licensor shall make its interface engineering team available, at no additional charge, to help Licensee and its Partner Clinics connect devices through December 31, 2026. This assistance covers configuring standard interface drivers for devices listed in the Documentation as supported devices. Interfaces for devices that are not supported, or that require custom development, will be scoped and priced under a Statement of Work.
