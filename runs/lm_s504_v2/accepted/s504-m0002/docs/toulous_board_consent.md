# TOULOUS AQUACHEM LLC

## UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS IN LIEU OF A MEETING

**Dated: September 10, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act under the Company's Amended and Restated Operating Agreement (the "**Operating Agreement**") and the New York Limited Liability Company Law. They waive all requirements of notice and of a meeting. They consent to, adopt and approve the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if duly adopted at a meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. Recitals

**1.1 The Business.** The Company formulates, blends, certifies, markets and supplies coagulants and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal water and wastewater utilities. The Company conducts that business in part at its blending, storage and packaging facility at 1180 Mill Race Road, Ashby Falls, New York (the "**Ashby Falls Facility**", and the business as a whole, the "**Business**").

**1.2 The Proposed Transaction.** The Company has negotiated with **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Prodeth**"), a proposed Asset Purchase Agreement between Prodeth, as buyer, and the Company, as seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). A draft dated September 9, 2026 has been presented to the Board. Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company would sell to Prodeth substantially all of the assets used in the Business, including:

(a) the Ashby Falls Facility;

(b) the Business's NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 product certifications;

(c) the Business's formulations and other intellectual property; and

(d) the Business's municipal supply contracts.

Prodeth would also assume certain liabilities of the Business (the "**Transaction**").

**1.3 Consideration.** The aggregate purchase price under the Purchase Agreement is $42,000,000, subject to a working capital adjustment, payable as follows:

(a) cash at closing, of which $3,150,000 would be deposited with Pellucid Bank & Trust, N.A., as escrow agent, under an indemnity escrow agreement (the "**Escrow Agreement**"); and

(b) a subordinated promissory note made by Prodeth in favor of the Company in the principal amount of $6,000,000, maturing 36 months after the closing date (the "**Seller Note**").

**1.4 Ancillary Documents.** The Purchase Agreement contemplates that the Company will execute and deliver at closing a bill of sale and assignment and assumption agreement, the Escrow Agreement, and such deeds, certificates and other instruments as the Purchase Agreement requires (together with the Purchase Agreement, the "**Transaction Documents**"). It also contemplates that Prodeth will offer employment to certain employees of the Business, including Dr. Oriel Fannick, the Company's Vice President, Regulatory & Product Stewardship.

**1.5 Ashcombe Supply Agreement.** The Company is party to a Coagulant Supply Agreement with the Ashcombe County Water Authority (the "**Authority**"), together with all amendments, renewals and purchase orders issued under it (the "**Ashcombe Supply Agreement**"). The Ashcombe Supply Agreement accounted for approximately 31% of the Business's revenue for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. Under its terms, the Company may not assign the Ashcombe Supply Agreement, or effect any sale of all or substantially all of the assets used in its performance, without the prior written consent of the Authority.

**1.6 Other Counterparty Consents.** Certain other municipal supply contracts of the Business, including the Company's supply agreements with the Brennock Regional Water Commission and the Tillery Municipal Utilities District, also require counterparty consent to assignment.

**1.7 Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the draft Purchase Agreement and the forms of the other Transaction Documents. It has received the advice of the Company's management, legal counsel and financial advisors. The Board has determined that the Transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members.

## 2. Resolutions

**Resolution 1 – Approval of the Transaction.**

RESOLVED, that the Transaction, on substantially the terms described in the draft Purchase Agreement presented to the Board, is approved; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement and each other Transaction Documents are approved, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing them approves, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by that execution.

**Resolution 2 – Execution and Delivery.**

RESOLVED, that each of the Managing Member and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is authorized and directed, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement and each other Transaction Document in the name and on behalf of the Company, and to cause the Company to perform its obligations under them.

**Resolution 3 – Third-Party Consents.**

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to seek the prior written consent of the Authority to the assignment of the Ashcombe Supply Agreement to Prodeth and to the sale of all or substantially all of the assets used in its performance. To that end, they may:

(a) deliver such notices and requests for consent as they consider appropriate;

(b) meet and correspond with representatives of the Authority; and

(c) provide the Authority with such information concerning Prodeth and the Transaction as the Purchase Agreement and the Confidentiality Agreement between the Company and Prodeth permit; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed to seek and obtain the consents of the counterparties to the Company's other contracts to be assigned under the Purchase Agreement, including the Brennock Regional Water Commission and the Tillery Municipal Utilities District, and to deliver those consents to Prodeth as the Purchase Agreement requires.

**Resolution 4 – Seller Note and Escrow.**

RESOLVED, that the Company's acceptance of the Seller Note as part of the purchase price is approved. The Authorized Officers are authorized:

(a) to acknowledge the Seller Note on behalf of the Company;

(b) to execute any subordination agreement with Prodeth's senior lenders on customary terms, as the Seller Note contemplates; and

(c) to execute the Escrow Agreement and provide the escrow agent with the information it requires to open the escrow account.

**Resolution 5 – Employees.**

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to take such actions as are necessary or appropriate in connection with the transfer of employees of the Business to Prodeth, including:

(a) the release of Dr. Oriel Fannick and other transferring employees from their employment with the Company effective as of the closing; and

(b) the payment of all compensation and benefits accrued by those employees through the closing.

**Resolution 6 – Member Approval.**

RESOLVED, that the Board recommends that the members of the Company approve the Transaction as a sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets, as the Operating Agreement requires. The Authorized Officers are directed to submit the Transaction to the members for approval by written consent.

**Resolution 7 – Use of Proceeds.**

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to apply the net cash proceeds of the Transaction received at closing in the following order:

(a) first, to the repayment in full of the Company's indebtedness for borrowed money secured by the Purchased Assets, and to obtain the release of all related liens; and

(b) thereafter, in accordance with the Operating Agreement.

**Resolution 8 – General Authority.**

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to:

(a) take all further actions;

(b) execute and deliver all further certificates, instruments and documents, including the secretary's certificate and officer's certificate to be delivered at closing; and

(c) pay all fees and expenses,

in each case as they consider necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that all actions previously taken by any manager or officer of the Company in connection with the Transaction and consistent with these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved.

## 3. Effectiveness; Counterparts

This consent is effective as of the date first written above. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
