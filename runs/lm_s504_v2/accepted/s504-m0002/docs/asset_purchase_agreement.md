# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026, by and between **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of formulating, blending, certifying, marketing and supplying coagulants and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal water and wastewater utilities. Seller conducts that business in part at its blending facility located in Ashby Falls, New York (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

"**Ashby Falls Facility**" means the blending, storage and packaging facility located at 1180 Mill Race Road, Ashby Falls, New York, together with the land, buildings, fixtures and improvements on it.

"**Ashcombe Supply Agreement**" means the Coagulant Supply Agreement between Seller and the Ashcombe County Water Authority, together with all amendments, renewals and purchase orders issued under it.

"**Cap Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances (each as defined in the Disclosure Schedule), all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Ashby Falls Facility and all real property interests related to it;

(b) all inventory, raw materials, work in process, finished goods, packaging and supplies of the Business;

(c) all contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Ashcombe Supply Agreement, the Brennock Supply Agreement and the Tillery Supply Agreement (collectively, the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all NSF Certifications and all other permits, licenses and registrations held for the Business, to the extent transferable;

(e) all formulations, recipes, specifications, know-how, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used in the Business;

(f) all tangible personal property, including blending vessels, tanks, pumps, laboratory equipment and vehicles;

(g) all accounts receivable of the Business arising after the Closing; and

(h) all books, records, customer lists, quality and regulatory files, and certification correspondence of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include: (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's organizational documents and minute books; (c) accounts receivable arising before the Closing; (d) insurance policies of Seller and rights under them; (e) Tax refunds relating to pre-Closing periods; (f) Seller's employee benefit plans; and (g) the assets listed in Section 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) trade accounts payable of the Business to third parties incurred in the ordinary course and reflected in the Closing Working Capital Statement;

(b) Liabilities relating to the employment of Transferred Employees arising after the Closing; and

(c) all Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities;

(b) all Liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any pre-Closing period;

(c) all Liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets;

(d) all Liabilities relating to Seller's indebtedness for borrowed money;

(e) all Liabilities relating to employees of Seller who do not become Transferred Employees; and

(f) all Liabilities arising out of any action, suit or proceeding pending or threatened against Seller as of the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $42,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.7.

**2.6 Payment of Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Buyer shall:

(a) pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, an amount equal to the Purchase Price, less the principal amount of the Seller Note, less the Escrow Amount, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment;

(b) deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX; and

(c) execute and deliver to Seller the Seller Note in the principal amount of $6,000,000.

**2.7 Working Capital Adjustment.** Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital. Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a closing statement setting out its calculation of Closing Working Capital (the "**Closing Working Capital Statement**"). The Parties shall resolve any dispute regarding that statement through an independent accounting firm in accordance with Section 2.7 of the Disclosure Schedule. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.8 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale and Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) a deed for the Ashby Falls Facility in recordable form;

(d) the Required Consents;

(e) a certificate of Seller's secretary attaching the resolutions of Seller's board of managers authorizing this Agreement;

(f) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d); and

(g) such other instruments of transfer as Buyer reasonably requests.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.6, together with the Seller Note, the Bill of Sale and Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall maintain the Purchased Assets, preserve its relationships with customers and suppliers and maintain all NSF Certifications in good standing.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the Ashby Falls Facility, the books and records of the Business and its personnel, subject to the Confidentiality Agreement.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the Required Consents before the Closing.

**6.4 Notice of Developments.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement concerning any sale of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to the employees of the Business listed in Section 6.6 of the Disclosure Schedule. Each such employee who accepts is a "**Transferred Employee**." Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Dr. Oriel Fannick to enter into the Key Employee Agreement.

**6.7 Confidentiality; Public Announcements.** After the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business. No Party shall issue any press release or public announcement about this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except for a joint press release in the form agreed by the Parties or as required by Law.

**6.8 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the manufacture or sale of coagulants or polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal customers in the United States.

**6.9 Bulk Sales; Further Assurances.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Law. After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments as the other Party reasonably requests to give effect to this Agreement.

**6.10 NSF Recertification.** Buyer shall cause the transfer of the NSF Certifications to Buyer to be submitted to NSF International promptly after the Closing. Buyer shall cause the recertification audit of the transferred Products and the Ashby Falls Facility to be completed within twelve (12) months after the Closing Date. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in connection with that transfer and audit, including by providing formulation records, supplier certifications and batch histories in its possession.

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction of the following conditions: (a) no Governmental Authority shall have enacted or issued any Law or order that makes the transactions contemplated by this Agreement illegal or otherwise prohibits them; and (b) any waiting period under applicable antitrust Laws shall have expired or been terminated.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and (c) Buyer shall have delivered an officer's certificate to that effect and each of the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and real property transfer Taxes arising from the transactions contemplated by this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Straddle Periods.** Real property and personal property Taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated on a per diem basis.

**8.3 Cooperation.** The Parties shall cooperate as reasonably requested in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit relating to the Business.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, survive the Closing until nine (9) months after the Closing Date. No claim may be made in respect of any of them unless written notice of the claim is given before that date.

(b) The Fundamental Representations survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(c) The covenants and agreements of the Parties survive in accordance with their terms. Claims under Sections 9.2(c) and 9.3(b) may be made at any time.

(d) Any claim asserted in good faith with reasonable specificity in writing before the expiration of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any Assumed Liability; and

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give that notice does not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. For third-party claims, the indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It may not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under it exceed $210,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar. The aggregate liability of Seller under Sections 9.2(a) and 9.2(c) shall not exceed $4,200,000 (the "**Cap Amount**").

(b) The limitations in Section 9.5(a): (i) shall not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, which are limited to the Purchase Price; (ii) shall not apply to Losses arising from fraud or intentional misrepresentation; and (iii) shall not apply to Losses constituting Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities, whether claimed under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.2(c).

(c) The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3 shall not exceed the Cap Amount.

(d) No limitation in this Section 9.5 shall apply to Buyer's obligations under Section 9.3(b).

(e) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of them, less costs of recovery.

**9.6 Escrow Recovery.** Any amount payable to a Buyer Indemnified Party under this Article IX shall be satisfied first from the funds then held under the Escrow Agreement. To the extent those funds are insufficient, it shall be satisfied directly by Seller, subject to Section 9.8.

**9.7 Mitigation.** Each indemnified party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses after becoming aware of any event that would reasonably be expected to give rise to them.

**9.8 Set-Off.** Buyer shall have the right to set off against any principal, interest or other amount payable under the Seller Note any amount to which any Buyer Indemnified Party is entitled under this Article IX. That right covers amounts finally determined and amounts that are the subject of a pending claim made in good faith under Section 9.4. If Buyer withholds an amount in respect of a pending claim, it shall pay any amount subsequently determined not to be owed, without interest, within ten (10) Business Days after final determination. The exercise of that right in good faith does not constitute a default under the Seller Note.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the procedures in Section 2.7, the remedies in this Article IX are the Parties' sole and exclusive remedies with respect to this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either Party, by written notice, if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 15, 2027 (the "**Outside Date**"), provided that this right is not available to a Party whose breach of this Agreement caused the failure of the Closing to occur by that date;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice;

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(e) by either Party, if any Governmental Authority has issued a final, non-appealable order prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1, it becomes void without liability of any Party, except that (a) this Section 10.2, Section 6.7 and Article XI survive, and (b) nothing relieves any Party of liability for any willful breach before termination.
