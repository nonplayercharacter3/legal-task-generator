Ladies and Gentlemen:

We refer to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Toulous Aquachem LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"). This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") sets out certain agreements of Buyer and Seller in connection with the Closing. These agreements concern the environmental conditions identified at the Ashby Falls Facility and Seller's undertaking to remediate them. This Letter Agreement is entered into under Section 11.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Identified Environmental Conditions**" means the recognized environmental conditions at the Ashby Falls Facility identified in the Phase I Report and listed in Annex A.

"**Phase I Report**" means the Phase I Environmental Site Assessment of the Ashby Falls Facility prepared by Kestrel Ridge Environmental Consultants, P.C., dated July 22, 2026, a copy of which Seller delivered to Buyer before the date of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Remedial Work**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

## 2. Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities

The definition of "Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities" in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement is hereby amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

> "**Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities**" means all Liabilities arising under Environmental Laws to the extent arising from the Identified Environmental Conditions, including the costs of investigation, sampling, monitoring, removal and remediation of those conditions and any fines or penalties imposed by any Governmental Authority in respect of them.

## 3. Remediation Undertaking

**3.1 Remedial Work.** At its sole cost and expense, Seller shall investigate and remediate each Identified Environmental Condition. The work shall be done to the standards required by applicable Environmental Laws and by the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (the "**Department**") for continued industrial use of the Ashby Falls Facility (the "**Remedial Work**"). Seller shall engage a qualified environmental consultant reasonably acceptable to Buyer to design and carry out the Remedial Work.

**3.2 Work Plan and Schedule.** Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written work plan for the Remedial Work. Seller shall consider in good faith any comments Buyer provides within twenty (20) Business Days after receiving it. Seller shall begin the Remedial Work within thirty (30) days after the work plan is finalized. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete it, and to obtain any no-further-action letter or equivalent written determination from the Department, within twenty-four (24) months after the Closing Date.

**3.3 Access.** Buyer shall give Seller and its consultants and contractors reasonable access to the Ashby Falls Facility during normal business hours, on at least five (5) Business Days' prior notice, as reasonably necessary to perform the Remedial Work. Seller shall conduct the Remedial Work so as to minimize interference with the operation of the Ashby Falls Facility. Seller shall comply with Buyer's reasonable site safety, security and NSF quality-control procedures. On completion of each phase of the Remedial Work, Seller shall restore any affected area to substantially its prior condition.

**3.4 Reporting.** Seller shall provide Buyer with copies of all sampling data, reports, submissions to and correspondence with the Department relating to the Remedial Work promptly after Seller generates, delivers or receives them. Seller shall give Buyer a reasonable opportunity to review and comment on any submission to the Department before it is delivered.

**3.5 Insurance.** Seller shall cause each contractor performing the Remedial Work to maintain commercial general liability insurance and contractor's pollution liability insurance, each with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming Buyer as an additional insured.

## 4. Mill Race Road Permits

Seller shall cooperate with Buyer, at Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, in transferring to Buyer, or obtaining reissuance in Buyer's name of, the State Pollutant Discharge Elimination System stormwater permit and the air facility registration held for the Ashby Falls Facility. This includes signing any transferor notices or certifications that the Department requires.

## ANNEX A – Identified Environmental Conditions

The following recognized environmental conditions at the Ashby Falls Facility are identified in Section 8 of the Phase I Report:

| No. | Location | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Former fuel storage area, northeast corner of the site | Former 4,000-gallon underground diesel storage tank, removed in 2009, with no closure documentation or confirmatory soil sampling on file with the Department. |
| 2 | Aluminum sulfate and polyaluminum chloride bulk tank farm | Staining and corrosion on the concrete secondary containment pad and adjoining soils. The Phase I Report attributes these to historic overfills and containment cracking. |
| 3 | Historic drum storage area, south of Building 2 | Stained soils and stressed vegetation in an area used until approximately 2014 for storage of drummed polymer feedstocks, including acrylamide-based and epichlorohydrin-based materials. |
| 4 | Stormwater Outfall 001 to Mill Race Brook | Discolored sediments at and immediately downstream of the outfall. These are consistent with historic discharges of ferric and aluminum salt residues. |
| 5 | Rail spur loading area | Surface staining at the former ferric chloride railcar unloading station. The station has been out of service since 2017. |
