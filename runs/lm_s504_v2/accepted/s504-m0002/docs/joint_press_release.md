# JOINT PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Prodeth Water Solutions to Acquire Toulous Aquachem's Municipal Coagulant and Polymer-Blend Business**

*Acquisition adds Ashby Falls, New York blending facility and NSF/ANSI/CAN 60-certified product line to Prodeth's municipal treatment chemicals portfolio*

**STAMFORD, Connecticut and ASHBY FALLS, New York – September 14, 2026** – Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc. ("Prodeth") and Toulous Aquachem LLC ("Toulous") today announced that they have entered into a definitive asset purchase agreement. Under it, Prodeth will acquire Toulous's municipal coagulant and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals business (the "Business").

## 1. Transaction Overview

Under the agreement, Prodeth will acquire the assets used in the Business. These include:

- the blending, storage and packaging facility in Ashby Falls, New York;
- the Business's NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 product certifications;
- its formulations and related know-how; and
- its municipal supply contracts.

The aggregate purchase price is $42,000,000, subject to a customary working capital adjustment. It is payable in cash at closing, a portion of which will be held in escrow, and a $6,000,000 subordinated seller note issued by Prodeth.

The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. Closing is subject to customary closing conditions, including the receipt of certain third-party consents.

## 2. Strategic Rationale

The Business supplies aluminum- and iron-based coagulants and polymer-blend products to municipal drinking water and wastewater utilities in the northeastern United States. The acquisition extends Prodeth's municipal treatment chemicals division with an established regional blending platform and a portfolio of products certified for use in potable water treatment.

"Toulous has built a strong reputation with municipal utilities for consistent product quality and responsive service," said a spokesperson for Prodeth. "The Ashby Falls facility and the Toulous team give us additional blending capacity and certified products close to the utilities we serve. We look forward to welcoming the Business's employees to Prodeth."

"We are proud of the business we have built in Ashby Falls," said Lisette M. Toulous, a manager of Toulous Aquachem LLC. "Prodeth has the scale and technical resources to support the Business's customers and employees in its next phase, and we are committed to a smooth transition."

## 3. Employees and Operations

Prodeth expects to offer employment to employees of the Business, effective at closing. This includes Dr. Oriel Fannick, Vice President, Regulatory & Product Stewardship, who will continue to lead product certification and stewardship for the Business as part of Prodeth's municipal treatment chemicals division.

Prodeth intends to continue operations at the Ashby Falls facility after closing. Until closing, Toulous will continue to operate the Business in the ordinary course, and customers should continue to place orders and direct inquiries through their existing Toulous contacts.

## 4. About Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.

Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc., headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, supplies water treatment chemicals, technical services and process support to municipal and industrial water and wastewater customers across the United States.

## 5. About Toulous Aquachem LLC

Toulous Aquachem LLC, based in Ashby Falls, New York, formulates, blends, certifies, markets and supplies coagulants and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal water and wastewater utilities.
