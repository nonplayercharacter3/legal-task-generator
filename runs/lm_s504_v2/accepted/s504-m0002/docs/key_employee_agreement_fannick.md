# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026, by and between **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Oriel Fannick**, an individual (the "**Executive**"). The Company and the Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Company and Toulous Aquachem LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Toulous**"), are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, the Company will acquire the municipal coagulant and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals business of Toulous (the "**Business**"), including the blending facility located in Ashby Falls, New York (the "**Ashby Falls Facility**").

B. The Executive is employed by Toulous as Vice President, Regulatory & Product Stewardship. In that role the Executive has primary responsibility for the Business's product certifications, regulatory compliance and product stewardship programs.

C. The Company wishes to employ the Executive, and the Executive wishes to be employed by the Company, from the Closing Date on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Cause**" means the Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of the Executive's duties that causes material harm to the Company; (c) willful and continued failure to perform the Executive's material duties, other than because of Disability, after written notice and a cure period of thirty (30) days; (d) material breach of Section 8, 9 or 10; or (e) knowing falsification of any certification record, formulation on file, test result or regulatory submission.

"**Disability**" means the Executive's inability, because of a physical or mental impairment, to perform the essential functions of the Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following without the Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in Base Salary; (b) a material diminution in the Executive's title, authority or duties; (c) relocation of the Executive's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles from the Ashby Falls Facility; or (d) the Company's material breach of this Agreement. Good Reason exists only if: the Executive gives the Company written notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs; the Company fails to cure the condition within thirty (30) days after receiving that notice; and the Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the end of the cure period.

"**Restricted Business**" means the formulation, blending, certification, marketing or supply of coagulants or polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal water or wastewater utilities.

"**Restricted Period**" means the Executive's employment with the Company and the twelve (12) months after it ends for any reason.

## 2. Effectiveness; Term

**2.1 Condition.** This Agreement becomes effective on, and subject to, the occurrence of the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Agreement is void and of no effect, and neither Party has any obligation under it.

**2.2 Term.** The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until it ends under Section 6 (the "**Term**"). The Executive's employment is at will. Either Party may end it at any time, subject to the notice and payment provisions of Sections 6 and 7.

**2.3 Prior Employment.** The Executive acknowledges that the Executive's employment with Toulous will end at the Closing. Toulous remains responsible for all compensation, benefits and other amounts owed to the Executive for service before the Closing.

## 3. Position and Duties

**3.1 Position.** The Executive will serve as Vice President, Regulatory & Product Stewardship of the Company's municipal treatment chemicals division and will report to the division's President.

**3.2 Duties.** The Executive's duties will include:

(a) managing the transfer of the NSF Certifications to the Company and the recertification audit of the transferred Products and the Ashby Falls Facility contemplated by Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) maintaining the Products' conformity with their certified formulations on file and with NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 requirements;

(c) overseeing raw-material supplier qualification, certification correspondence, batch records and product labeling;

(d) overseeing product stewardship, safety data sheets and regulatory reporting for the Business; and

(e) such other duties consistent with the Executive's position as the Company reasonably assigns.

**3.3 Time and Attention.** The Executive will devote substantially all of the Executive's business time and attention to the Company. The Executive may serve on industry standards committees and professional association bodies, and may manage personal investments, if those activities do not materially interfere with the Executive's duties or breach Section 9.

**3.4 Place of Work.** The Executive's principal place of work will be the Ashby Falls Facility, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 4. Compensation

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company will pay the Executive an annual base salary of $238,000 ("**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Base Salary will be reviewed annually and may be increased, but not decreased, except as part of an across-the-board reduction that applies to the Company's senior management generally.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** The Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus under the Company's management incentive plan, with a target of thirty percent (30%) of Base Salary. The actual bonus depends on Company and individual performance against objectives set by the Company. For the year in which the Effective Date occurs, the bonus will be prorated. Any bonus is payable no later than March 15 of the year after the year to which it relates, provided the Executive is employed on the payment date, except as provided in Section 7.

**4.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company will pay the Executive a retention bonus of $120,000. It is payable in two equal installments: the first on the Company's first regular payroll date after the date on which the recertification audit referred to in Section 3.2(a) is completed, and the second on the second anniversary of the Effective Date. Each installment is payable only if the Executive is employed on the applicable payment date or the Executive's employment has earlier been ended by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason.

**4.4 Equity Award.** Subject to approval by the Company's board of directors, the Executive will receive, as soon as practicable after the Effective Date, restricted stock units under the Company's equity incentive plan with a grant-date value of $150,000. The units vest in three equal annual installments, subject to the terms of the plan and the applicable award agreement.

## 5. Benefits and Expenses

**5.1 Benefits.** The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to similarly situated employees, subject to the terms of those plans. The Company will credit the Executive's service with Toulous for purposes of eligibility, vesting and vacation accrual under those plans, to the extent permitted by the plans.

**5.2 Paid Time Off.** The Executive is entitled to twenty-two (22) days of paid time off per year, in addition to Company holidays, in accordance with Company policy.

**5.3 Expenses.** The Company will reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing the Executive's duties, in accordance with Company policy. This includes the cost of maintaining professional licenses and memberships relevant to the Executive's position.

## 6. Termination of Employment

**6.1 Death or Disability.** The Executive's employment ends automatically on the Executive's death. The Company may end it on written notice upon the Executive's Disability.

**6.2 By the Company.** The Company may end the Executive's employment for Cause, effective immediately on written notice, or without Cause on thirty (30) days' written notice. The Company may pay Base Salary in lieu of all or part of that notice.

**6.3 By the Executive.** The Executive may resign for Good Reason in accordance with the definition of that term, or without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period, in which case the resignation takes effect on the date the Company specifies.

**6.4 Resignation from Positions.** When the Executive's employment ends, the Executive will resign from any office or directorship held with the Company or its Affiliates.

## 7. Payments on Termination

**7.1 Accrued Amounts.** When employment ends for any reason, the Company will pay the Executive: earned but unpaid Base Salary; accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent payable under Company policy; any annual bonus earned for a completed year but not yet paid; and unreimbursed business expenses (together, the "**Accrued Amounts**").

**7.2 Severance.** If the Company ends the Executive's employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, in addition to the Accrued Amounts and subject to Section 7.3, the Company will:

(a) continue to pay the Executive's Base Salary for twelve (12) months after the termination date in accordance with its regular payroll practices;

(b) pay the Executive a prorated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance, at the time bonuses are paid to other executives;

(c) pay the unpaid installments of the retention bonus under Section 4.3 within sixty (60) days after the termination date; and

(d) if the Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay the employer portion of the premiums for up to twelve (12) months.

**7.3 Release.** The payments under Section 7.2 are conditioned on the Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within sixty (60) days after the termination date, and on the Executive's continued compliance with Sections 8, 9 and 10.

## 8. Confidential Information

**8.1 Non-Disclosure.** During and after employment, the Executive will not use or disclose any confidential or proprietary information of the Company or its Affiliates, except as required to perform the Executive's duties. This includes the formulations, recipes, specifications, certification files, supplier data, customer and pricing information and other information acquired with the Business. The obligation does not apply to information that becomes generally available to the public other than through the Executive's breach.

**8.2 Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Executive from: reporting possible violations of Law to any Governmental Authority; making disclosures protected under whistleblower Laws; or responding to a lawful subpoena or order. The Executive is notified that under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual is not liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit.

**8.3 Return of Property.** When employment ends, the Executive will return all Company property and records, including all certification, formulation and regulatory files, in any form.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

**9.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive will not, directly or indirectly, own, manage, operate, be employed by or provide services to any Person engaged in the Restricted Business in the United States, in any role similar to the Executive's role with the Company or in which the Executive would be reasonably likely to use the Company's confidential information. The Executive may own up to two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any company.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive will not, directly or indirectly, solicit any municipal customer of the Business with which the Executive had material contact during the last twenty-four (24) months of employment, for the purpose of supplying products competitive with the Products.

**9.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive will not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any employee of the Company who works in the Business. General solicitations not targeted at those employees do not breach this Section 9.3.

**9.4 Reasonableness.** The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 9 are reasonable in scope and duration and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests, including the goodwill of the Business that the Company is acquiring. If a court finds any restriction unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 10. Intellectual Property

All inventions, formulations, processes, improvements, works of authorship and know-how that the Executive conceives or develops, alone or with others, during employment and that relate to the Company's business ("**Work Product**") belong to the Company. The Executive assigns to the Company all of the Executive's rights in the Work Product and will sign any documents reasonably requested to perfect that assignment. This Section 10 does not apply to any invention that qualifies for protection under Section 203-f of the New York Labor Law.

## 11. Section 409A

The Parties intend that payments under this Agreement comply with, or are exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and this Agreement will be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. Any payment conditioned on termination of employment will be made only upon a "separation from service." If the release period under Section 7.3 spans two calendar years, payment will begin in the second year.

## 12. General

**12.1 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes and other amounts required by Law.

**12.2 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc., 400 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 900, Stamford, Connecticut 06902, Attention: General Counsel. Notices to the Executive go to the Executive's most recent address on the Company's records.

**12.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive and may not be assigned by the Executive. The Company may assign it to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of the Business.

**12.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**12.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**12.6 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-law rules. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement must be brought exclusively in the State or federal courts located in New York County, New York, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**12.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original.
