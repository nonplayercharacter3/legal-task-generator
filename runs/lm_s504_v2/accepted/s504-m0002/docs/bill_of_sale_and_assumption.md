# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT (this "**Instrument**") is dated as of ____________, 20__ (the "**Closing Date**"). It is made by and between **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Seller has agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets, and Buyer has agreed to assume the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Instrument at the Closing, and Section 3.3 requires Buyer to deliver it.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the consideration paid under Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Instrument have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Sale and Conveyance of Purchased Assets

**2.1 Conveyance.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Buyer and its successors and assigns, and Buyer accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances. The Purchased Assets include the assets described in Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement, namely:

(a) the Ashby Falls Facility and all real property interests related to it, to the extent not conveyed by the deed delivered under Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) all inventory, raw materials, work in process, finished goods, packaging and supplies of the Business;

(c) the Assigned Contracts, including the Ashcombe Supply Agreement, the Brennock Supply Agreement and the Tillery Supply Agreement, and the other contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(d) all NSF Certifications and all other permits, licenses and registrations held for the Business, to the extent transferable;

(e) all formulations, recipes, specifications, know-how, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used in the Business;

(f) all tangible personal property, including blending vessels, tanks, pumps, laboratory equipment and vehicles;

(g) all accounts receivable of the Business arising after the Closing; and

(h) all books, records, customer lists, quality and regulatory files, and certification correspondence of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** Seller does not sell, convey, assign, transfer or deliver to Buyer, and Seller retains, all of its right, title and interest in, to and under the Excluded Assets.

## 3. Assignment and Assumption

**3.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller assigns and transfers to Buyer all of Seller's rights under the Assigned Contracts.

**3.2 Assumption.** Effective as of the Closing, Buyer assumes and agrees to pay, perform and discharge when due the Assumed Liabilities, as described in Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**3.3 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer does not assume, and Seller retains and remains responsible for, the Excluded Liabilities, including all Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities.

## 4. Further Assurances

**4.1 Further Instruments.** From time to time after the Closing, at the request of either Party and without further consideration, the other Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments of sale, conveyance, transfer, assignment and assumption, and take such further action, as may reasonably be requested to vest in Buyer title to the Purchased Assets and to give effect to Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, in each case in accordance with Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller appoints Buyer as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets in Seller's name and to endorse in Seller's name any check or other instrument received in payment of any account receivable included in the Purchased Assets. This appointment is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**4.3 Receipts After Closing.** If Seller receives any payment or other property that is a Purchased Asset after the Closing, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer and remit or deliver it to Buyer promptly, and in any event within ten (10) Business Days after receipt. If Buyer receives any payment or other property that is an Excluded Asset, Buyer shall remit or deliver it to Seller within the same period.

## 5. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Instrument is delivered under, and is subject to all of the terms of, the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnities in the Purchase Agreement are not enlarged, merged, extinguished or otherwise affected by this Instrument.

## 6. General

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Instrument binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**6.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Instrument is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Nothing in it confers any right, benefit or remedy on any other Person.

**6.3 Amendment.** This Instrument may be amended only by an instrument in writing signed by each Party.

**6.4 Governing Law.** This Instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**6.5 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Instrument shall be instituted exclusively in the State or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts.

**6.6 Counterparts.** This Instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
