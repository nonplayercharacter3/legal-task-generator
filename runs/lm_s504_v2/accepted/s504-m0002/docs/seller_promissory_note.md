# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**

Dated as of ____________, 20__ (the "**Closing Date**")

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest set out below, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in, and is delivered under Sections 2.6(c) and 3.3 of, the Asset Purchase Agreement dated September 14, 2026 between Maker, as buyer, and Toulous Aquachem LLC, as seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal Amount

Maker shall pay to Holder the principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"), or so much of it as remains outstanding, together with accrued and unpaid interest, in accordance with this Note.

## 2. Maturity

The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts then owing under this Note, is due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

## 3. Interest

**3.1 Rate.** The outstanding Principal Amount bears simple interest at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum from the Closing Date until paid in full. Interest is calculated on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**3.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date occurring at least thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, and on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the outstanding Principal Amount and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, any overdue interest bear interest at the rate in Section 3.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

**3.4 Maximum Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 4. Payments

**4.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account that Holder designates by written notice to Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the relevant payment date.

**4.2 Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to costs and expenses payable under Section 9.3, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to the outstanding Principal Amount.

## 5. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note, in whole or in part, at any time without premium or penalty, on at least five (5) Business Days' prior written notice to Holder. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Amounts prepaid may not be reborrowed.

## 6. Subordination

**6.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to all Senior Debt. "**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owing to banks, institutional lenders or other financial institutions under any credit facility of Maker in effect from time to time, together with refinancings of that indebtedness.

**6.2 Payment Blockage.** While any payment default under Senior Debt is continuing, or for up to one hundred eighty (180) days after Holder receives written notice from the agent or holder of Senior Debt that any other event of default under the Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment of principal under this Note. Regularly scheduled interest payments are not blocked except during a payment default under the Senior Debt.

**6.3 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute and deliver a subordination agreement with the holder of Senior Debt on terms customary for seller financing and consistent with this Section 6.

## 7. Reporting

While any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, Maker's unaudited consolidated balance sheet as of the end of that month and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and for the fiscal year to date, certified by Maker's chief financial officer as fairly presenting in all material respects Maker's financial position and results of operations;

(b) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Maker's audited consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year, together with the report of its independent certified public accountants;

(c) together with the statements under paragraphs (a) and (b), a customer-concentration report identifying each customer of Maker, including each municipal water or wastewater utility customer of the Business, that accounted for more than five percent (5%) of Maker's consolidated revenue in the relevant period, with the revenue attributable to that customer and the contract under which it is supplied; and

(d) promptly after Holder's reasonable request, such other information concerning the financial condition and operations of Maker as Holder reasonably requests.

## 8. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest or other amount under this Note within five (5) Business Days after it is due;

(b) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 7, and that failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due;

(d) any involuntary case or proceeding of that kind is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(e) any Senior Debt in a principal amount exceeding $5,000,000 is accelerated and that acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days; or

(f) Maker sells, transfers or otherwise disposes of all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets, other than to an Affiliate of Maker that assumes this Note in writing.

## 9. Remedies

**9.1 Acceleration.** Subject to Section 6, if an Event of Default under Section 8(a), (b), (e) or (f) occurs and is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount, all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note immediately due and payable. If an Event of Default under Section 8(c) or (d) occurs, those amounts become immediately due and payable automatically, without notice or any other action by Holder.

**9.2 Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Section 6, Holder may exercise all rights and remedies available to it under this Note and applicable law. No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**9.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs in collecting any amount due under this Note after an Event of Default.

**9.4 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by this Note.

## 10. Transfer and Assignment

**10.1 Transfer by Holder.** Holder may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, in whole or in part, and any of its rights under this Note, to any Person, without the consent of Maker. Each transferee shall succeed to all of the rights of Holder under this Note to the extent of the interest transferred.

**10.2 Notice and Register.** Holder shall give Maker written notice of any transfer, stating the name and notice address of the transferee and the portion of the Principal Amount transferred. Maker shall maintain a register of the holders of this Note and may treat the Person shown in that register as the owner of this Note for all purposes until it receives that notice. On surrender of this Note in connection with a partial transfer, Maker shall issue replacement notes, in the same form, in the respective principal amounts transferred and retained.

**10.3 Maker.** Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, except to an Affiliate of Maker that acquires all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets and assumes this Note in writing, in which case Maker is not released from its obligations.

**10.4 Lost Note.** On receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note and, in the case of loss, theft or destruction, an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor.

## 11. Notices

All notices under this Note shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or such other address as a party designates by notice):

If to Maker: Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc., 400 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 900, Stamford, Connecticut 06902, Attention: General Counsel.

If to Holder: Toulous Aquachem LLC, 22 Canal Street, Ashby Falls, New York 12890, Attention: Managing Member, or, for any transferee, the address shown in the register maintained under Section 10.2.

## 12. Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and any claim or dispute arising out of or relating to this Note, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts and waive any objection to venue in them.

**12.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ALL RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION, PROCEEDING OR COUNTERCLAIM ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 13. Miscellaneous

**13.1 Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by an instrument in writing signed by Maker and Holder.

**13.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**13.3 Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits Holder and its successors and assigns.

**13.4 Business Day.** If any payment under this Note falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is payable on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue to the date of payment.

**13.5 Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
