# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Prodeth**"), and **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Toulous**"). Prodeth and Toulous are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties are considering a possible negotiated transaction in which Prodeth would acquire all or part of Toulous's business of formulating, blending, certifying, marketing and supplying coagulants and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals to municipal water and wastewater utilities (the "**Business**"), including its blending facility in Ashby Falls, New York (the "**Transaction**").

B. To evaluate, negotiate and, if agreed, complete the Transaction, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. Each Party wishes to protect that information on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that a Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Transaction. It includes:

(a) financial statements, projections, pricing, margins and cost data;

(b) customer and supplier identities, contracts, volumes and terms, including municipal supply contracts and bid information;

(c) product formulations, recipes, specifications, certified formulations on file, NSF/ANSI/CAN 60 certification files and audit reports, batch records and raw-material supplier qualifications;

(d) environmental, health, safety and permit information relating to any facility, including site assessments and sampling data;

(e) employee and compensation information; and

(f) all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Parties are discussing the Transaction and the status and terms of those discussions ("**Transaction Information**").

**1.2** "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, officers, members, employees, lenders and prospective financing sources, attorneys, accountants, environmental and technical consultants and financial advisors.

**1.3** "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person. "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure under this Agreement;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, if that source is not known by the Receiving Party, after reasonable inquiry, to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. Use and Disclosure

**3.1 Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and complete the Transaction, and for no other purpose.

**3.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any Person, except:

(a) to its Representatives who need to know it for the purpose in Section 3.1, who are informed of its confidential nature and who are bound by duties of confidentiality no less protective than this Agreement;

(b) with the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; or

(c) as permitted by Section 4.

**3.3 Third-Party Consents.** With the prior written consent of Prodeth (which may be given by email), Toulous may disclose Transaction Information, and such information concerning Prodeth as Prodeth approves for that purpose, to any counterparty to a contract of the Business whose consent or notice is required or requested in connection with the Transaction, solely for the purpose of seeking that consent or giving that notice.

**3.4 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, other than Representatives who have entered into a separate confidentiality agreement directly with the Disclosing Party.

**3.5 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

**3.6 Competitively Sensitive Information.** The Parties shall comply with applicable antitrust Laws in exchanging Confidential Information. Either Party may designate competitively sensitive information, including current customer-specific pricing and bid strategy, as "Clean Team Only." Information so designated may be disclosed only to the Receiving Party's outside counsel, outside advisors and those employees who are not directly involved in pricing, bidding or sales of competing products, and who are identified to the Disclosing Party in writing in advance.

## 4. Legally Required Disclosure

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by Law, regulation, subpoena, court order or the rules of any securities exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy at its own expense. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts. If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that it will be accorded confidential treatment. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits any Person from reporting possible violations of Law to any governmental authority or making disclosures protected under whistleblower Laws.

## 5. Return and Destruction

Within ten (10) Business Days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it is no longer pursuing the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, including Derivative Materials, and shall confirm that destruction in writing on request. The Receiving Party and its Representatives may, however, retain copies (a) to the extent required by Law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention or compliance policies, and (b) in automatic electronic back-up systems, if those copies are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

## 6. Non-Solicitation of Employees

For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any officer or management-level employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Transaction. This Section 6 does not restrict: (a) general solicitations not specifically targeted at those employees, or hiring anyone who responds to them; (b) soliciting or hiring any individual whose employment with the other Party ended at least six (6) months earlier; or (c) any offer of employment, employment agreement or hiring made by Prodeth in accordance with any definitive agreement for the Transaction.

## 7. No Representations; No Obligation

**7.1 No Representations.** Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information. Neither Party has any liability to the other or its Representatives arising from the use of Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement for the Transaction.

**7.2 No Obligation.** Neither Party is under any legal obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered, and then only as set out in it. Either Party may end discussions at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other.

**7.3 No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right in any Confidential Information, formulation, trademark, patent or other intellectual property is granted under this Agreement.

## 8. Term

This Agreement continues in effect until the second (2nd) anniversary of the Effective Date. A Party's obligations regarding Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable Law, including product formulations and recipes, continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret. If a definitive agreement for the Transaction is entered into, this Agreement continues in effect in accordance with its terms, except to the extent that definitive agreement expressly provides otherwise.

## 9. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 10. General

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or such other address as a Party designates by notice):

**10.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.3 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the State or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**10.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ALL RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION, PROCEEDING OR COUNTERCLAIM ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.5 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Prodeth may assign it to an Affiliate that acquires the Business.

**10.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by an instrument in writing signed by each Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
