# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of ____________, 20__ (the "**Closing Date**"). It is entered into by and among **Prodeth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Toulous Aquachem LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pellucid Bank & Trust, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring from Seller the Purchased Assets used in Seller's municipal coagulant and polymer-blend water treatment chemicals business.

B. Section 2.6(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount is held as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent has agreed to act as escrow agent and to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Appointment; Definitions

**1.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

**1.2 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a written certification of counsel to the Party delivering it that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is nine (9) months after the Closing Date.

## 2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, the sum of $3,150,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall acknowledge receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day of receipt. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent, as escrow agent for Buyer and Seller (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Fund is not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party, and shall be held and disbursed only as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. Investment of Escrow Fund

**3.1 Permitted Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in an interest-bearing, FDIC-insured deposit account maintained with the Escrow Agent, unless Buyer and Seller direct otherwise by Joint Written Instructions. Those instructions may direct investment only in direct obligations of, or obligations fully guaranteed by, the United States of America with a maturity of ninety (90) days or less, or in a money market fund investing exclusively in those obligations.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as necessary to make any disbursement required under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss arising from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3 or from any liquidation of that kind.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund form part of the Escrow Fund and shall be disbursed with it in accordance with this Agreement.

## 4. Claims Against the Escrow Fund

**4.1 Claim Notice.** If Buyer, on its own behalf or on behalf of any Buyer Indemnified Party, asserts a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). A Claim Notice shall: (a) describe the claim in reasonable detail, to the extent then known; (b) state the section or sections of the Purchase Agreement under which the claim is made; and (c) state the amount of Losses claimed or, if not then determinable, a good-faith estimate of that amount (the "**Claimed Amount**").

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a Claimed Amount by delivering a written notice of objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within twenty (20) Business Days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). The notice of objection shall state the portion of the Claimed Amount disputed (the "**Disputed Amount**") and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive a notice of objection within the Objection Period, the Claimed Amount is deemed undisputed. On the third (3rd) Business Day after the end of the Objection Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Fund the Claimed Amount, or, if less, the balance of the Escrow Fund. If a notice of objection disputes only part of a Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed portion to Buyer within three (3) Business Days after receiving that notice.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any Disputed Amount in the Escrow Fund until it receives either: (a) Joint Written Instructions directing the disbursement of that amount; or (b) a copy of a Final Order determining the claim. The Escrow Agent shall disburse the Disputed Amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instructions or Final Order within three (3) Business Days after receiving them. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions to give effect to any settlement of, or Final Order regarding, a Disputed Amount.

**4.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states a good-faith estimate of Losses not yet incurred, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the estimated amount, whether or not disputed, until it is disbursed under Section 4.3 or 4.4 or under Joint Written Instructions confirming the amount of Losses actually incurred.

## 5. Release of Escrow Fund

**5.1 Release on Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate of all Claimed Amounts under Claim Notices delivered before the Release Date that have not then been paid to Buyer or otherwise resolved (the "**Retained Amount**").

**5.2 Release of Retained Amount.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Retained Amount after the Release Date. When any claim included in the Retained Amount is finally resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse: (a) to Buyer, the amount payable to it in respect of that claim; and (b) to Seller, the excess, if any, of the portion of the Retained Amount held for that claim over the amount paid to Buyer. In each case the Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**5.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding Sections 4 and 5.1, the Escrow Agent shall disburse all or any part of the Escrow Fund at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**5.4 Manner of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient. The Escrow Agent shall confirm each funds transfer instruction received in the name of Buyer or Seller by a telephone call-back to an authorized representative named in Schedule A, other than the individual who delivered the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until that confirmation is obtained.

## 6. Tax Matters

**6.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Fund as its income for each taxable year in which the income is earned, whether or not distributed.

**6.2 Tax Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall report earnings on the Escrow Fund to the Internal Revenue Service and applicable state authorities in the name of Buyer, on IRS Form 1099 or other appropriate form. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver a properly completed IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date.

**6.3 Tax Distributions.** Within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of a written request from Buyer delivered after the end of any calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer from the earnings on the Escrow Fund an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings credited to the Escrow Fund for that quarter. No tax distribution shall be made from the Escrow Amount itself.

## 7. The Escrow Agent

**7.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement, and they are purely ministerial. No implied duties or obligations apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, is not bound by and has no duty to inquire into the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction, certificate or other document it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or delivered by the proper Person. It need not investigate any fact or matter stated in that document. The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice, and it is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**7.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**7.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents against all losses, claims, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or the Escrow Agent's performance under it, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.4, except that a Party whose breach of this Agreement gave rise to the amount shall bear all of it.

**7.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions regarding the Escrow Fund, it may hold the affected funds until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also, at its option, interplead the affected funds into a court of competent jurisdiction described in Section 10.2, and it is then discharged from further obligations regarding those funds.

**7.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving Buyer and Seller at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving it at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent before the effective date of the resignation or removal. If they do not, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction described in Section 10.2 to appoint a successor. The Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Fund to the successor escrow agent and is then discharged from further obligations under this Agreement. Until then, its only duty is to hold the Escrow Fund in safekeeping.

**7.7 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set out in Schedule B and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Fund.

**7.8 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with such information as it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering, sanctions and customer identification Laws. The Escrow Agent is not required to accept the Escrow Amount until it has received that information.

## 8. Termination

This Agreement terminates when the entire Escrow Fund has been disbursed in accordance with it. Sections 7.3, 7.4 and 7.7 survive termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 10. General

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the State or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ALL RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION, PROCEEDING OR COUNTERCLAIM ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by an instrument in writing signed by each Party.

**10.5 Assignment.** No Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties. However, any successor to the Escrow Agent by merger, conversion or consolidation, or by transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the Escrow Agent under this Agreement without further action.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Nothing in it confers any right, benefit or remedy on any other Person.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
