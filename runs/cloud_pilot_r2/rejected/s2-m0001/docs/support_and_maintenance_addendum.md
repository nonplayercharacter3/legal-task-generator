## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Authorized Users**" means the employees, contractors and agents of Licensee and its Affiliates whom Licensee has authorized to access and use the Licensed Software.

1.2 "**Business Hours**" means 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

1.3 "**Error**" means a reproducible failure of the Licensed Software to perform materially in accordance with the Documentation.

1.4 "**Incident**" means a single Error or a single request for assistance reported by Licensee to Licensor through the Support Channels.

1.5 "**Severity Level**" means the classification of an Incident under Section 4.1.

1.6 "**Support Channels**" means Licensor's online support portal, its support email address and its support telephone line, as identified in Section 9.

1.7 "**Support Contacts**" means the Authorized Users whom Licensee designates under Section 3.2 to submit Incidents and communicate with Licensor's support personnel.

1.8 "**Support Services**" means the support and maintenance services described in this Addendum.

1.9 "**Support Year**" means each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Addendum Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

1.10 "**Workaround**" means a temporary procedure or configuration change that restores the affected functionality of the Licensed Software in a manner that does not materially impair Licensee's use, pending a permanent correction.

## 2. SCOPE OF SUPPORT SERVICES

2.1 **Support Services.** During the Term, and provided Licensee has paid the applicable support Fees, Licensor shall provide the following Support Services for the Licensed Software:

(a) telephone, email and portal-based assistance to Authorized Users regarding installation, configuration and use of the Licensed Software;

(b) diagnosis of reported Errors and the provision of corrections or Workarounds in accordance with Section 4;

(c) Updates, as and when Licensor makes them generally available to its licensees who are current on support Fees, together with the related release notes and Documentation; and

(d) access to Licensor's online knowledge base, support portal and published technical bulletins.

2.2 **Supported Versions.** Licensor shall provide Support Services for the then-current release of the Licensed Software and the immediately preceding minor release within version 7.x. Licensor shall give Licensee at least twelve (12) months' written notice before ending Support Services for any supported release.

2.3 **Installation of Updates.** Licensee is responsible for installing Updates in its environment. On Licensee's request, Licensor shall provide remote guidance to Licensee during installation of an Update during Business Hours at no additional charge.

2.4 **Exclusions.** Support Services do not include support for, and Licensor has no obligation to correct, any Error caused by:

(a) modification of the Licensed Software by anyone other than Licensor or its subcontractors;

(b) use of the Licensed Software other than in accordance with the Agreement and the Documentation;

(c) hardware, operating systems, databases, networks or third-party software not specified in the Documentation as compatible with the Licensed Software;

(d) Licensee's failure to install an Update that Licensor made available at least ninety (90) days earlier and that would have corrected the Error; or

(e) any release of the Licensed Software that is no longer a supported release under Section 2.2.

2.5 **Additional Services.** Licensor may, at Licensee's request, provide assistance outside the scope of Section 2.1, including on-site support, custom development or support for excluded matters under Section 2.4. Such assistance will be performed under a Statement of Work at the rates agreed in it.

## 3. LICENSEE RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 **Cooperation.** Licensee shall provide Licensor with information reasonably necessary to reproduce and diagnose each Incident, including error messages, log files, system configuration details and a description of the steps leading to the Error.

3.2 **Support Contacts.** Licensee shall designate up to ten (10) Support Contacts, who must be trained in the use of the Licensed Software. Licensee may change its Support Contacts by written notice to Licensor through the Support Channels.

3.3 **Remote Access.** Where Licensor reasonably requires remote access to Licensee's environment to diagnose or correct a Severity 1 or Severity 2 Incident, Licensee shall provide secure remote access, subject to Licensee's reasonable security policies as communicated to Licensor in writing. Any access to Licensee Data is subject to Section 6.2 and Section 8 of the Agreement.

3.4 **Backups.** Licensee is responsible for maintaining current backups of Licensee Data and of its installation of the Licensed Software.

## 4. SEVERITY LEVELS AND RESPONSE TIMES

4.1 **Severity Levels.** Licensee shall assign an initial Severity Level to each Incident when it is reported. Licensor may reasonably reclassify an Incident after notifying Licensee, and the Parties shall discuss any disagreement in good faith.

| **Severity Level** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| Severity 1 (Critical) | The Licensed Software is inoperable in production, or a core ledger or reconciliation function is unavailable, with no Workaround. |
| Severity 2 (High) | A major function of the Licensed Software is materially impaired in production, and no reasonable Workaround is available. |
| Severity 3 (Medium) | A function of the Licensed Software is impaired, but a reasonable Workaround is available, or the Error affects a non-production environment. |
| Severity 4 (Low) | A minor Error, cosmetic issue, Documentation inquiry or request for enhancement. |

4.2 **Response and Resolution Targets.**

| **Severity Level** | **Availability** | **Initial Response** | **Target for Workaround or Correction** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | 24 x 7 by telephone | 1 hour | Continuous efforts until a Workaround is provided; permanent correction within 10 business days |
| Severity 2 | Business Hours | 4 Business Hours | Workaround within 2 business days; permanent correction in the next scheduled Update |
| Severity 3 | Business Hours | 1 business day | Correction in a future Update |
| Severity 4 | Business Hours | 3 business days | At Licensor's discretion |

4.3 **Initial Response.** The initial response time runs from Licensor's receipt of an Incident through the Support Channels and is met when a qualified member of Licensor's support personnel acknowledges the Incident and begins diagnosis. Severity 1 Incidents must be reported by telephone.

4.4 **Escalation.** If Licensor does not meet a target in Section 4.2 for a Severity 1 or Severity 2 Incident, Licensee may escalate the Incident to Licensor's Director of Customer Support and, if unresolved within a further two (2) business days, to Licensor's Chief Technology Officer.

## 5. SERVICE LEVEL CREDITS

5.1 **Credits.** If, in any calendar quarter, Licensor fails to meet the initial response time in Section 4.2 for more than one (1) Severity 1 Incident, or for more than three (3) Severity 2 Incidents, Licensee will be entitled to a credit equal to five percent (5%) of the support Fees attributable to that calendar quarter.

5.2 **Maximum Credits.** Credits under Section 5.1 will not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the support Fees attributable to any Support Year.

5.3 **Claiming Credits.** Licensee must request a credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the calendar quarter to which it relates. Licensor shall apply approved credits against the next invoice for support Fees. Credits have no cash value, except that any credit outstanding at the end of the Term will be refunded within thirty (30) days.

5.4 **Sole Remedy.** The credits in this Section 5 are Licensee's sole monetary remedy for Licensor's failure to meet the response times in Section 4.2. This Section 5.4 does not limit Licensee's rights under Section 10.3 of the Agreement.

## 6. SUPPORT FEES

6.1 **Annual Support Fee.** The annual support Fee for the Support Services is USD 96,000 per year for each Support Year of the Initial Term.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Licensor will invoice the annual support Fee annually in advance, on the Addendum Effective Date and on each anniversary of it during the Term.

6.3 **Payment.** Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice net thirty (30) days from invoice date, in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Agreement. Sections 5.3, 5.4 and 5.5 of the Agreement apply to support Fees.

6.4 **Renewal Terms.** Licensor may adjust the annual support Fee for any Renewal Term by written notice given at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the start of that Renewal Term, provided that any increase does not exceed five percent (5%) over the annual support Fee for the preceding Support Year.
