# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of October 15, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Analytics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Halvorsen**"), and **Brightmoor Logistics Group LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Brightmoor**"). Halvorsen and Brightmoor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** The Parties wish to exchange information so that they can evaluate and negotiate a possible license by Halvorsen to Brightmoor of the Corvane Ledger Platform, an on-premises financial ledger and reconciliation software product, together with related support, maintenance and implementation services (the "**Proposed Transaction**"). Each Party is willing to disclose confidential information to the other for that purpose on the terms of this NDA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that one Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") on or after the NDA Effective Date in connection with the Purpose, and that is marked or identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes business plans, pricing, financial information, customer and supplier information, product roadmaps, technical specifications, software (in source or object code form), documentation, demonstration environments and login credentials.

1.3 "**Purpose**" means evaluating, negotiating and, if the Parties so agree, documenting the Proposed Transaction.

1.4 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, professional advisors (including attorneys, accountants and financial advisors) and, with the Discloser's prior written consent, contractors.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate by written records:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure by the Discloser, without a duty of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received by the Recipient from a third party that is not under a duty of confidentiality to the Discloser; or

(d) is independently developed by or for the Recipient without use of, or reference to, the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

3.1 **Use.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

3.2 **Standard of Care.** The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like kind, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.3 **Disclosure to Representatives.** The Recipient may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information only to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Recipient is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this NDA if committed by the Recipient.

3.4 **Software and Demonstration Materials.** Without limiting Section 3.1, the Recipient shall not copy, modify, decompile, disassemble or reverse engineer any software, demonstration environment or technical materials furnished by the Discloser, and shall not use them to build a competing product or service.

3.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser promptly in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information and shall cooperate reasonably with the Discloser to limit its effects.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or court order, provided that the Recipient (a) gives the Discloser prompt prior written notice of the requirement where legally permitted; (b) provides reasonable cooperation, at the Discloser's expense, if the Discloser seeks a protective order or other remedy; and (c) discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

5.1 Upon the Discloser's written request, or upon expiration or termination of this NDA, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information in its possession or control, and on request shall certify the return or destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain copies held in routine electronic backups or as required by law or its bona fide document retention policies, and any retained copies remain subject to this NDA for as long as they are retained.

## 6. OWNERSHIP AND NO LICENSE

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or other right in any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property is granted under this NDA, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose as stated in Section 3.1.

6.2 **No Warranty.** ALL CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS." NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY AS TO THE ACCURACY OR COMPLETENESS OF ITS CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

7.1 This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information or to enter into the Proposed Transaction or any other agreement. Either Party may end discussions about the Proposed Transaction at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other.

7.2 Each Party may continue to develop, acquire and market products and services that are similar to the other Party's, provided that it does so without use of the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this NDA.

## 8. TERM AND DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

8.1 **Term.** This NDA begins on the NDA Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless either Party terminates it earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other.

8.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Recipient's obligations under Sections 3, 4 and 5 for Confidential Information disclosed during the term of this NDA continue for five (5) years after the expiration or termination of this NDA, except that obligations relating to software, source code and any trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

8.3 **Survival.** Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8.2, 8.3, 9 and 10 survive expiration or termination of this NDA.

## 9. REMEDIES, GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

9.1 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Discloser may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in the courts identified in Section 9.3, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

9.2 **Governing Law.** This NDA and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

9.3 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses stated on the signature page or another address a Party designates by notice.

10.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or otherwise transfer this NDA without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 10.2 is void. This NDA binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

10.3 **Export Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with all applicable export control and sanctions laws in connection with any technical information or software it receives under this NDA.

10.4 **Independent Parties.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this NDA creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

10.5 **Publicity.** Neither Party shall disclose the existence or subject matter of the Parties' discussions, or use the other Party's name or marks, without the other Party's prior written consent.

10.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

10.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

10.8 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
