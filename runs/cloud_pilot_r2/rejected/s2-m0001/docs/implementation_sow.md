## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** Licensor shall perform the implementation, configuration and training services described in this SOW (the "**Implementation Services**"). These services will enable Licensee to deploy the Licensed Software, being Corvane Ledger Platform, version 7.x, in object code form, in the environments permitted under Section 1.3 of Order Form No. 1.

1.2 **In Scope.** The Implementation Services consist of:

(a) a project kickoff and a requirements and environment readiness review;

(b) installation support for the Licensed Software in one (1) production environment and up to two (2) non-production environments (testing and disaster recovery) on servers owned or controlled by Licensee;

(c) configuration of the chart of accounts, ledger structures, reconciliation rules and user roles within the standard configuration options described in the Documentation;

(d) assistance with the migration of opening balances and up to twenty-four (24) months of historical ledger data from Licensee's legacy system, using the standard import utilities of the Licensed Software;

(e) support for user acceptance testing;

(f) training for Authorized Users as described in Section 4; and

(g) go-live support and a hand-off to Support Services under the Support Addendum.

1.3 **Out of Scope.** The following are outside the scope of this SOW: custom development or modification of the Licensed Software; integrations beyond the standard file-based import and export interfaces described in the Documentation; procurement or administration of Licensee's hardware, operating systems, databases or network infrastructure; and data cleansing of Licensee's legacy records. Licensor may provide out-of-scope work under a separate Statement of Work or under a Change Order in accordance with Section 8.

## 3. ACCEPTANCE

3.1 **Acceptance Review.** Within ten (10) business days after Licensor delivers a Deliverable, Licensee shall review it. Licensee shall then either accept it in writing or notify Licensor in writing of any material non-conformity with this SOW, giving reasonable detail.

3.2 **Correction.** Licensor shall correct any notified material non-conformity and redeliver the Deliverable within ten (10) business days. The review process in Section 3.1 then applies again to the redelivered Deliverable.

3.3 **Deemed Acceptance.** A Deliverable is deemed accepted if Licensee does not give notice under Section 3.1 within the review period, or if Licensee uses that Deliverable in production.

## 4. TRAINING

4.1 **Administrator Training.** Licensor shall provide up to three (3) days of remote administrator training for up to ten (10) Authorized Users designated by Licensee. This training covers system administration, user role management, installation of Updates and backup procedures.

4.2 **End-User Training.** Licensor shall provide up to four (4) remote train-the-trainer sessions of half a day each for Authorized Users. These sessions cover ledger entry, reconciliation workflows and standard reporting.

4.3 **Materials.** Training materials are part of the Documentation for purposes of Section 2.4 of the Agreement. Licensee may reproduce them solely for internal training of Authorized Users.

## 5. LICENSEE RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 **Environment.** In accordance with Section 4.4 of the Agreement, Licensee shall procure and maintain the hardware, operating systems, databases and network infrastructure required to run the Licensed Software, as specified in the Documentation. Licensee shall make these available by the dates set out in the project plan.

5.2 **Project Manager.** Licensee shall appoint a project manager with authority to make decisions and to approve Deliverables on Licensee's behalf.

5.3 **Information and Data.** Licensee shall provide timely access to the information, personnel and legacy data reasonably required for the Implementation Services. Licensee is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the legacy data it provides.

5.4 **Remote Access.** Where Licensor reasonably requires remote access to Licensee's environment, Licensee shall provide secure remote access. That access is subject to Licensee's reasonable security policies, as communicated to Licensor in writing. Any access by Licensor to Licensee Data is subject to Section 6.2 and Section 8 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Backups.** Licensee shall maintain current backups of Licensee Data before and during data migration activities.

## 6. PROJECT GOVERNANCE AND PERSONNEL

6.1 **Licensor Personnel.** Licensor shall assign a project manager and qualified implementation consultants who are experienced in the Licensed Software. Licensor may replace assigned personnel on reasonable notice to Licensee. Licensor shall ensure that any replacement has comparable qualifications.

6.2 **Status Meetings.** The Parties' project managers shall meet by video conference at least every two (2) weeks to review progress, risks and open issues. Licensor shall circulate a written status report before each meeting.

6.3 **Subcontractors.** Licensor may use subcontractors to perform the Implementation Services. Licensor remains responsible for their performance, and each subcontractor must be bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Section 8 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Escalation.** Project issues that the project managers cannot resolve within five (5) business days will be escalated to a senior executive of each Party. Disputes are resolved in accordance with Section 15 of the Agreement.
