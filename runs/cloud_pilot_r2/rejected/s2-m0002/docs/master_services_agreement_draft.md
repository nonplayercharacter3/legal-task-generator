# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**DRAFT: Provider Draft for Discussion. Subject to Negotiation.**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2027 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business in New York, New York ("**Customer**"), and **Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC**, a limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer is a retailer of furniture and housewares and requires warehousing, freight transportation and delivery services in support of its retail and e-commerce operations.

B. Provider is in the business of providing warehousing, freight transportation and last-mile delivery services.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Provider will perform services for Customer under one or more statements of work.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Customer Goods**" means all merchandise, inventory, fixtures and other goods of Customer or its Affiliates that are tendered to, received by, or in the possession, custody or control of Provider in connection with the Services.

1.3 "**Customer Data**" means all data and information, including Personal Data, that Customer or its end customers provide to Provider, or that Provider collects, generates or processes, in connection with the Services.

1.4 "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum entered into between the Parties in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

1.5 "**Fees**" means the fees and charges payable by Customer for the Services as set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.6 "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the Data Processing Addendum.

1.7 "**Services**" means the warehousing, freight transportation, last-mile delivery and related services described in a Statement of Work.

1.8 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties that references this Agreement and describes Services to be performed by Provider, including Statement of Work No. 1.

1.9 "**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 14.2.

## 2. Structure of Agreement

2.1 **Statements of Work.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Each Statement of Work is governed by and incorporates the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 **Contents of Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work shall describe, as applicable, the Services, the facilities and service area, the start date, service levels, the Fees and any assumptions or Customer dependencies.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents making up the Parties' agreement, the following order of precedence shall apply: the Data Processing Addendum, then this Agreement, then the Statement of Work.

2.4 **Change Orders.** Either Party may request a change to the scope of Services under a Statement of Work. No change shall be binding unless set out in a written change order signed by both Parties that states any resulting adjustment to the Fees or schedule.

## 3. Services

3.1 **Performance Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services in a diligent, professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work, good industry practice for third-party logistics providers, and all applicable laws, regulations and permits.

3.2 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Customer appoints Provider as Customer's exclusive provider of all warehousing, freight transportation and delivery services required by Customer and its Affiliates throughout the United States. During the Term, Customer shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, engage, contract with or use any third party to perform, or perform itself, any warehousing, freight transportation or delivery services throughout the United States.

3.3 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet or exceed the service levels set out in each Statement of Work. If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider shall (a) promptly notify Customer, (b) investigate and report the root cause, and (c) implement a corrective action plan reasonably acceptable to Customer. Any service credits set out in a Statement of Work shall be credited against the next invoice.

3.4 **Facilities and Equipment.** Unless otherwise stated in a Statement of Work, Provider shall furnish at its own cost all facilities, vehicles, equipment, materials, systems and personnel necessary to perform the Services, and shall maintain all such facilities, vehicles and equipment in safe, clean and good working order.

3.5 **Reporting.** Provider shall provide Customer with the inventory, shipment, delivery and exception reports described in each Statement of Work, and such other reports as Customer may reasonably request.

## 4. Customer Responsibilities

4.1 Customer shall provide Provider with accurate forecasts, shipping instructions, delivery addresses and product information reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services.

4.2 Customer shall ensure that Customer Goods tendered to Provider are properly packaged and labeled in accordance with the specifications in the applicable Statement of Work and do not include hazardous materials except as disclosed to and accepted by Provider in writing.

4.3 Customer shall designate a relationship manager who will serve as Provider's primary point of contact for the Services.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which shall be invoiced separately and paid by Customer, other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears unless a Statement of Work provides otherwise. Each invoice shall include reasonable supporting detail for all Fees and pass-through charges.

5.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

5.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider in writing of the dispute and the basis for it before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Provider shall continue performing the Services during any such dispute.

5.5 **Audit.** Provider shall maintain complete and accurate records relating to the Fees and the Services for at least three (3) years after the relevant invoice. Customer or its independent auditor may audit such records once per calendar year on reasonable advance notice during normal business hours.

## 6. Customer Goods

6.1 **Title.** Title to Customer Goods shall at all times remain with Customer. Provider has no right, title or interest in Customer Goods, and Provider shall keep Customer Goods free of all liens, claims and encumbrances arising by, through or under Provider, other than any lien that cannot be waived under applicable law.

6.2 **Care and Custody.** Provider shall exercise such care with respect to Customer Goods in its possession, custody or control as a reasonably careful warehouse operator and carrier would exercise under like circumstances, and shall store Customer Goods segregated from or readily identifiable apart from goods of other customers.

6.3 **Loss and Damage.** Provider shall be responsible for loss of or damage to Customer Goods occurring while in Provider's possession, custody or control, except to the extent caused by Customer's acts or omissions or by inherent defect in the Customer Goods. Provider shall reimburse Customer for such loss or damage at Customer's landed cost, as further described in the applicable Statement of Work.

6.4 **Inventory Counts.** Provider shall conduct cycle counts and an annual physical inventory as set out in the applicable Statement of Work, and Customer may attend any physical inventory.

## 7. Personnel and Subcontractors

7.1 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified, trained and properly licensed personnel to perform the Services. Provider shall conduct background checks, consistent with applicable law, on all personnel who will enter end customers' residences.

7.2 **Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract any material portion of the Services without Customer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Provider shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were Provider's own.

7.3 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, taxes and supervision of its personnel, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, agency or joint venture relationship between the Parties.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data and the terms of this Agreement are Customer's Confidential Information.

8.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, (b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care, and (c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 8.

8.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient, (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure, (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

8.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

8.5 **Return or Destruction.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement, or on the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, subject to the Data Processing Addendum in the case of Customer Data.

## 9. Data Protection

9.1 Provider shall process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

9.2 Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction, as further described in the Data Processing Addendum.

9.3 Provider shall notify Customer of any security incident affecting Customer Data within the time and in the manner set out in the Data Processing Addendum.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel or subcontractors in performing the Services, (b) Provider's violation of applicable law, or (c) any claim by Provider's personnel or subcontractors for wages, benefits or employment-related amounts.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, members and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to any third-party claim arising out of or relating to (a) Customer Goods, including any claim that Customer Goods are defective, unsafe or infringe any third-party right, (b) any Customer premises or end-customer premises at which Provider performs Services, or (c) Customer's use of the Services.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, provided that any delay shall relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced, (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Liability Cap.** Except as set out in Section 11.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement and all Statements of Work, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as set out in Section 11.3, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

11.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 shall not apply to Customer's obligations under Section 10.2, which shall apply without limitation. The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 shall also not apply to either Party's liability for fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct, or to Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 During the Term and for one (1) year thereafter, Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best, at least the following insurance:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles with a combined single limit of not less than $5,000,000 per accident;

(c) warehouse legal liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(d) motor truck cargo insurance with limits of not less than $250,000 per vehicle;

(e) workers' compensation insurance as required by applicable law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident; and

(f) cyber liability insurance with limits of not less than $3,000,000 per claim.

12.2 Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies described in Sections 12.1(a) and 12.1(b), and shall provide Customer with certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage before the start date of the first Statement of Work and on each renewal of such coverage. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction of any required coverage.

## 13. Intellectual Property

13.1 Each Party retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing materials, systems, software and intellectual property.

13.2 Customer owns all Customer Data and all reports prepared by Provider specifically for Customer under this Agreement. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Term to use any Provider portal or system made available to Customer in connection with the Services.

13.3 Customer grants Provider a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Customer's names and marks solely as necessary to perform the Services, including on delivery documentation.

## 14. Term and Termination

14.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 2 years from the MSA Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section 14.

14.2 **Renewal.** On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms of three (3) years each (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party during the fifteen (15) day period ending one hundred twenty (120) days before the expiry of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. Notice of non-renewal delivered at any other time shall be of no effect.

14.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or that Statement of Work and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

14.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.5 **Effect on Statements of Work.** Termination or expiration of this Agreement shall terminate all Statements of Work then in effect, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 15. Effect of Termination; Transition

15.1 On termination or expiration of this Agreement or any Statement of Work, Provider shall (a) promptly release and make available for collection all Customer Goods in its possession, custody or control, (b) return Customer Data in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum, and (c) provide final inventory reconciliation reports.

15.2 At Customer's request, Provider shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days after termination or expiration to enable an orderly transfer of the Services to Customer or its designee, at the rates set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

15.3 Customer shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination or expiration.

15.4 Sections 5.5, 6.1, 8, 10, 11, 13.1, 15, 17 and 18, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 16. Representations and Warranties

16.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that (a) it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization, (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and (c) its execution and performance of this Agreement do not conflict with any other agreement by which it is bound.

16.2 **By Provider.** Provider represents and warrants that (a) it holds, and will maintain throughout the Term, all licenses, registrations, operating authorities and permits required to perform the Services, including any required federal motor carrier operating authority, and (b) it will perform the Services in compliance with all applicable laws.

16.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 17. General

17.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated on the signature page, with a copy by email. Notices are effective on receipt. Either Party may change its notice address by notice to the other Party.

17.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, flood, fire, epidemic, war, terrorism, civil unrest or governmental action, provided that the affected Party promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing a material portion of the Services for more than fifteen (15) days, Customer may procure substitute services for the duration of the event.

17.3 **Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with all applicable laws in performing this Agreement, including anti-corruption, export control and employment laws.

17.4 **Assignment.** Customer shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any Statement of Work, or any of its rights or obligations under them, whether voluntarily, by operation of law, by merger or change of control or otherwise, without Provider's prior written consent. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 17.4 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

17.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

17.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

17.7 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Section 10, nothing in this Agreement confers any right or remedy on any person other than the Parties.

17.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## 18. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

18.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with New York law, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County, New York, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

18.2 **Escalation.** Before commencing any proceeding (other than for urgent injunctive relief), a Party shall give written notice of the dispute to the other Party, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within fifteen (15) business days in a good-faith attempt to resolve it.

18.3 **Injunctive Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of Section 8 or Section 9 may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that the non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

18.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY AND VOLUNTARILY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
