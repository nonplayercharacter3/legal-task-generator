## 3. Additional Insured and Notice of Cancellation

3.1 Section 12.2 of the Agreement requires Harrowgate to be named as an additional insured on the commercial general liability and commercial automobile liability policies. The insurers have confirmed that the endorsements will be issued on these terms on execution of the Agreement. On that basis, the Certificate names **Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc.** as an additional insured on both policies. We will forward copies of the endorsements once issued.

3.2 Each policy listed in Section 2.1 will be endorsed to provide Harrowgate, as certificate holder, with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction in coverage. This is consistent with Section 12.2 of the Agreement.

## 4. Renewal and Updated Certificates

4.1 Section 12.2 of the Agreement requires certificates of insurance before the start date of the first Statement of Work. Schedule A to Statement of Work No. 1 sets a target date of January 11, 2027 for delivery of those certificates. We will issue an updated Certificate on or before that date confirming that the endorsements described in Section 3 are in place.

4.2 The current policies expire on July 1, 2027. We will deliver renewal certificates to Harrowgate on or before that expiry date. We will do the same for each later renewal while Bellweather's obligations under Section 12.1 of the Agreement continue.

## 5. Nature of the Certificate

5.1 The Certificate is issued as a matter of information only. It summarizes, and does not amend, extend or alter, the coverage afforded by the policies listed. The policies' terms, conditions and exclusions govern the coverage. Copies of the policies and endorsements are available to Harrowgate on request through Bellweather.
