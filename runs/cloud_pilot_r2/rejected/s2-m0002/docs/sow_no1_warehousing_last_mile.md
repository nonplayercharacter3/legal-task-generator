# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**Warehousing and Last-Mile Delivery Services**

**DRAFT: Provider Draft for Discussion. Subject to Negotiation.**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into under the Master Services Agreement dated as of January 1, 2027 (the "**Agreement**") by and between **Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC** ("**Provider**"). This SOW is governed by and incorporates the terms of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the warehousing, fulfillment and last-mile home delivery Services that Provider will perform for Customer in support of Customer's retail store replenishment and e-commerce operations in the Service Area.

1.2 **In-Scope Services.** The Services under this SOW consist of:

(a) receiving, inspecting, storing and managing Customer Goods at the Facility (Section 3);

(b) order fulfillment, including picking, packing, staging and loading of Customer Goods for store replenishment and for delivery to end customers (Section 3);

(c) last-mile home delivery of Customer Goods to end customers within the Service Area, including threshold, room-of-choice and white-glove delivery options (Section 4);

(d) basic assembly, debris removal and haul-away of packaging, where ordered by Customer for a particular delivery (Section 4); and

(e) reporting, inventory control and returns processing as described in this SOW.

1.3 **Out-of-Scope Services.** Any service not described in this SOW, including line-haul transportation outside the Service Area, is outside the scope of this SOW and may be added only by a change order under Section 2.4 of the Agreement or by a separate Statement of Work.

## 2. Start Date and Term

2.1 **Start Date.** Provider shall commence the Services on January 15, 2027 (the "**Start Date**").

2.2 **Implementation.** Between the Effective Date of the Agreement and the Start Date, Provider shall complete the implementation activities set out in Schedule A, including warehouse management system configuration, integration with Customer's order management system, and receipt of the initial inventory transfer from Customer's current facility.

2.3 **Term of SOW.** This SOW continues from the Start Date until the expiration or termination of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 14 of the Agreement.

## 3. Warehousing and Fulfillment

3.1 **Facility.** Provider shall perform the warehousing and fulfillment Services at its distribution center located at 400 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey (the "**Facility**"). Provider shall not relocate the Services to another facility without Customer's prior written consent.

3.2 **Dedicated Space.** Provider shall make available to Customer not less than 180,000 square feet of racked and floor storage space at the Facility, of which not less than 40,000 square feet shall be suitable for oversized furniture items. Customer Goods shall be stored segregated from or readily identifiable apart from goods of other customers in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

3.3 **Receiving.** Provider shall unload, count and visually inspect all inbound shipments of Customer Goods and post receipts to the warehouse management system within twenty-four (24) hours after arrival at the Facility. Provider shall note any visible damage or shortage on the carrier's delivery receipt and report it to Customer within one (1) business day.

3.4 **Packaging and Labeling.** Customer Goods tendered to Provider shall be packaged and labeled in accordance with Customer's Vendor Packaging and Labeling Guide, as provided to Provider before the Start Date and updated by Customer on reasonable notice, for purposes of Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

3.5 **Order Fulfillment.** Provider shall pick, pack and stage (a) e-commerce orders received by 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day for delivery on the scheduled delivery date, and (b) store replenishment orders in accordance with the weekly replenishment schedule agreed by the Parties' relationship managers.

3.6 **Inventory Counts.** For purposes of Section 6.4 of the Agreement, Provider shall conduct (a) daily cycle counts such that every storage location is counted at least once per calendar quarter, and (b) one annual physical inventory during January of each year on a date agreed with Customer at least thirty (30) days in advance.

3.7 **Returns.** Provider shall receive, inspect and grade returned Customer Goods within two (2) business days after receipt, and shall restock, quarantine or dispose of returned Customer Goods in accordance with Customer's written disposition instructions.

## 4. Last-Mile Delivery

4.1 **Service Area.** Provider shall perform last-mile home delivery to end customers located within the five boroughs of New York City and Nassau, Suffolk, Westchester and Rockland Counties, New York, and Bergen, Hudson, Essex and Union Counties, New Jersey (the "**Service Area**").

4.2 **Delivery Options.** Provider shall offer the following delivery service levels, as selected by Customer or its end customer at the time of order:

(a) **Threshold:** delivery to the first dry area inside the residence entrance;

(b) **Room of Choice:** delivery to a room selected by the end customer, including carrying up to three (3) flights of stairs; and

(c) **White Glove:** room-of-choice delivery plus unpacking, placement, basic assembly of items that do not require tools beyond those carried on the vehicle, and removal of all packaging debris.

4.3 **Scheduling.** Provider shall contact each end customer at least forty-eight (48) hours before the scheduled delivery date to confirm a four (4) hour delivery window, and shall send a text or email notification when the delivery vehicle is within approximately thirty (30) minutes of the end customer's address.

4.4 **Delivery Personnel.** Each delivery shall be performed by a crew of at least two (2) persons for any item weighing more than seventy-five (75) pounds. All personnel entering end customers' residences shall have passed background checks in accordance with Section 7.1 of the Agreement and shall wear Provider uniforms and identification.

4.5 **Proof of Delivery.** Provider shall obtain electronic proof of delivery, including the end customer's signature and time-stamped photographs of each delivered item in place, and shall make proof of delivery available to Customer through the Provider portal within two (2) hours after delivery.

4.6 **Damaged or Refused Items.** If an item is damaged on delivery or refused by the end customer, Provider shall return the item to the Facility, document the condition, and notify Customer within one (1) business day.

## 5. Fees

5.1 **Monthly Service Fee.** In consideration of the Services described in this SOW, Customer shall pay Provider a fixed monthly service fee of $184,500 per month (the "**Monthly Service Fee**"). The Monthly Service Fee covers (a) the dedicated storage space described in Section 3.2, (b) receiving, fulfillment and returns processing for up to the Baseline Volumes, and (c) last-mile delivery of up to the Baseline Volumes.

5.2 **Baseline Volumes.** The "**Baseline Volumes**" for each calendar month are: (a) 2,400 inbound pallets received; (b) 9,000 e-commerce orders fulfilled; and (c) 6,500 last-mile home deliveries completed, of which not more than 1,500 are White Glove deliveries.

5.3 **Excess Volume Charges.** Volumes in excess of the Baseline Volumes in any calendar month shall be charged at the following unit rates:

| Activity | Unit Rate |
|---|---|
| Inbound pallet received | $9.50 per pallet |
| E-commerce order fulfilled | $4.25 per order |
| Threshold delivery | $68.00 per delivery |
| Room of Choice delivery | $89.00 per delivery |
| White Glove delivery | $129.00 per delivery |
| Haul-away of end customer's existing furniture (where ordered) | $45.00 per item |

5.4 **Pro-Ration.** The Monthly Service Fee for January 2027 shall be pro-rated based on the number of days from the Start Date through January 31, 2027.

5.5 **Pass-Through Charges.** Bridge and tunnel tolls and congestion pricing charges incurred on deliveries within the Service Area shall be passed through to Customer at cost, without mark-up, and supported by itemized detail on the monthly invoice in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Agreement.

5.6 **Transition Assistance Rates.** Transition assistance requested by Customer under Section 15.2 of the Agreement shall be charged at the following rates: (a) operations manager, $115 per hour; (b) warehouse associate, $48 per hour; and (c) systems and data support, $140 per hour. Outbound handling of Customer Goods on release from the Facility shall be charged at the inbound pallet rate in Section 5.3.

5.7 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice the Monthly Service Fee, excess volume charges and pass-through charges monthly in arrears, and Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Agreement.

## 6. Service Levels and Service Credits

6.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the following service levels, measured monthly:

| Service Level | Target |
|---|---|
| Receipt posting within 24 hours of arrival | 99.0% |
| Order fulfillment accuracy (correct item and quantity) | 99.5% |
| On-time delivery within confirmed four-hour window | 97.0% |
| Damage-free delivery | 99.0% |
| Inventory record accuracy (by location, at cycle count) | 99.5% |

6.2 **Service Credits.** For each service level that Provider fails to meet in a calendar month, Provider shall credit Customer an amount equal to two percent (2%) of the Monthly Service Fee for that month, up to a maximum aggregate credit of ten percent (10%) of the Monthly Service Fee for that month. Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice in accordance with Section 3.3 of the Agreement and are without prejudice to Customer's other rights and remedies under the Agreement.

6.3 **Loss and Damage.** For purposes of Section 6.3 of the Agreement, "landed cost" means Customer's purchase price for the affected Customer Goods plus inbound freight, duties and customs charges actually incurred by Customer, as shown in Customer's records. Provider shall pay or credit any amount due for loss or damage within thirty (30) days after Customer's written claim with reasonable supporting documentation.

## 7. Reporting and Governance

7.1 **Reports.** Provider shall provide Customer with the following reports through the Provider portal, in addition to those required under Section 3.5 of the Agreement:

(a) daily inventory-on-hand and receiving reports;

(b) daily delivery completion and exception reports, including failed and rescheduled deliveries;

(c) weekly damage and claims reports; and

(d) a monthly service level scorecard showing performance against each service level in Section 6.1 and any resulting service credits.

7.2 **Governance.** Each Party's relationship manager shall meet weekly during the first ninety (90) days after the Start Date and monthly thereafter to review performance, forecasts and open issues. Senior representatives of each Party shall hold a quarterly business review.

7.3 **Customer Data.** Provider shall process end customer names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses and delivery instructions only as necessary to perform the Services under this SOW and in accordance with Section 9 of the Agreement and the Data Processing Addendum.

## 8. Subcontracting and Assignment

8.1 **Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any material portion of the Services under this SOW, including any last-mile delivery route, without Customer's prior written consent in accordance with Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

8.2 **Assignment by Provider.** Provider may assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this SOW, and any of its rights and obligations under this SOW and under the Agreement, in whole or in part, to any third party, without Customer's consent. Provider shall give Customer written notice of any such assignment within thirty (30) days after it becomes effective.

## 9. Customer Dependencies and Assumptions

9.1 Customer shall provide Provider with a rolling thirteen (13) week volume forecast, updated weekly, for inbound pallets, e-commerce orders and deliveries.

9.2 Customer shall transmit e-commerce orders to Provider through the agreed system integration and shall be responsible for the accuracy of end customer delivery addresses and contact information it provides.

9.3 Customer shall arrange and pay for inbound transportation of Customer Goods to the Facility, except to the extent covered by a separate Statement of Work.

9.4 The Fees assume that no single item of Customer Goods exceeds 350 pounds or requires disassembly for delivery. Items exceeding these parameters shall be handled under a change order.
