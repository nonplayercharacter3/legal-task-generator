# TERM SHEET

**Warehousing and Last-Mile Delivery Services**

**Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc. / Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC**

**Dated: September 22, 2026**

This term sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") summarizes the principal terms on which **Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC** ("**Provider**") propose that Provider will provide warehousing, fulfillment and last-mile delivery services to Customer. Except as stated in Section 12, this Term Sheet is non-binding. It is intended only as a basis for preparing definitive agreements.

## 1. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Customer | Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc. |
| Provider | Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC |

## 2. Transaction Structure

2.1 The Parties will enter into a master services agreement (the "**MSA**") setting out the general terms of the relationship. Specific services will be described in one or more statements of work under the MSA, the first being Statement of Work No. 1 ("**SOW No. 1**"). The Parties will also enter into a data processing addendum governing Provider's processing of Customer's end-customer personal data (together with the MSA and SOW No. 1, the "**Definitive Agreements**").

2.2 Provider will prepare the initial drafts of the Definitive Agreements for Customer's review.

## 3. Services

3.1 **Warehousing and Fulfillment.** Receiving, storage, inventory control, order fulfillment, store replenishment and returns processing for Customer goods at Provider's distribution center at 400 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey.

3.2 **Last-Mile Home Delivery.** Threshold, room-of-choice and white-glove home delivery of Customer goods to Customer's end customers within the New York City metropolitan area, including basic assembly and debris removal where ordered.

3.3 **Service Levels.** SOW No. 1 will include monthly service levels for receipt posting, fulfillment accuracy, on-time delivery, damage-free delivery and inventory accuracy, supported by service credits.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 Customer will engage Provider on an exclusive basis for last-mile home delivery within the New York City metropolitan area only.

4.2 No other services are subject to exclusivity. Customer remains free to use other providers, or to perform services itself, for all warehousing, freight transportation, line-haul and delivery services outside the scope of Section 4.1.

## 5. Key Dates

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| MSA effective date | January 1, 2027 |
| SOW No. 1 start date (go-live) | January 15, 2027 |
| Initial term of MSA | 2 years from the MSA effective date |

## 6. Fees

6.1 **Monthly Service Fee.** A fixed monthly service fee of $184,500 per month, covering dedicated storage space and baseline monthly volumes of inbound pallets, e-commerce orders and last-mile deliveries to be set out in SOW No. 1.

6.2 **Excess Volumes.** Volumes above the baseline will be charged at unit rates to be set out in SOW No. 1.

6.3 **Payment.** Monthly invoicing in arrears. Customer to pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt and may withhold amounts disputed in good faith.

## 7. Customer Goods

Title to Customer goods remains with Customer at all times. Provider will be responsible for loss of or damage to Customer goods in its possession, custody or control, reimbursed at Customer's landed cost.

## 8. Liability and Insurance

8.1 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, subject to customary exceptions to be agreed in the Definitive Agreements.

8.2 **Indemnities.** To be negotiated in the Definitive Agreements.

8.3 **Insurance.** Provider will maintain commercial general liability, automobile liability, warehouse legal liability, motor truck cargo, workers' compensation and cyber liability coverage at levels to be set out in the MSA, and will name Customer as an additional insured where customary.

## 9. Data Protection

Provider will process end-customer personal data only on Customer's instructions, solely to perform the services, within the United States, and in accordance with the data processing addendum. Provider will notify Customer promptly of any security incident affecting Customer data.

## 10. Term, Renewal and Assignment

10.1 Initial term as stated in Section 5.

10.2 Renewal, termination and assignment terms to be negotiated in the Definitive Agreements.

## 11. Governing Law

The Definitive Agreements will be governed by New York law, with disputes resolved in the state and federal courts in New York County, New York.

## 12. Non-Binding Effect; Binding Provisions

12.1 This Term Sheet does not create any legally binding obligation on either Party to enter into the Definitive Agreements or to perform or purchase any services. Binding obligations will arise only on execution of the Definitive Agreements.

12.2 Despite Section 12.1, the following provisions are binding on the Parties:

(a) **Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep the terms of this Term Sheet and all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with the proposed transaction confidential. Each Party will use such information only to evaluate and negotiate the proposed transaction.

(b) **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreements.

(c) **Governing Law.** This Section 12 is governed by New York law.

12.3 Either Party may end negotiations at any time by written notice to the other Party, without liability, except under the binding provisions in Section 12.2, which survive for two (2) years.
