# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**DRAFT: Provider Draft for Discussion. Subject to Negotiation.**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into by and between **Harrowgate Home Goods, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Bellweather Freight Logistics LLC** ("**Provider**"), in connection with the Master Services Agreement dated as of January 1, 2027 between Customer and Provider (the "**Agreement**"). This DPA forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Data Protection Laws**" means all U.S. federal, state and local laws and regulations that apply to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include, as applicable, the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-aa and § 899-bb), the New Jersey Identity Theft Prevention Act and the New Jersey Data Privacy Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and comparable state privacy and data breach notification laws.

1.2 "**Personal Data**" means any information included in Customer Data that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with an identified or identifiable natural person or household. This includes end customer names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, delivery instructions, signatures and proof-of-delivery photographs.

1.3 "**Process**" or "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

1.4 "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data or other Customer Data in Provider's or its Subprocessors' possession, custody or control.

1.5 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party, including any subcontractor or Provider Affiliate, that Provider engages to Process Personal Data on Provider's behalf.

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 **Roles.** Customer is the controller, or "business," of the Personal Data. Provider is a processor, or "service provider," that Processes Personal Data solely on Customer's behalf.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Provider shall Process Personal Data only to the extent necessary to perform the Services described in the applicable Statement of Work. Annex 1 describes the categories of data subjects, the categories of Personal Data, and the nature and purposes of the Processing.

2.3 **Duration.** Provider shall Process Personal Data for the Term of the Agreement and, after that, only as needed to complete the return or deletion obligations in Section 8.

## 3. Provider Obligations

3.1 **Instructions.** Provider shall Process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, the applicable Statement of Work and this DPA constitute those instructions. Provider shall promptly inform Customer if, in Provider's opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Provider shall not:

(a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined in Data Protection Laws;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, including for any commercial purpose of Provider or for marketing to end customers;

(c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and Provider; or

(d) combine Personal Data with personal information that Provider receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as permitted by Data Protection Laws.

3.3 **Compliance.** Provider shall comply with all Data Protection Laws applicable to it as a service provider or processor. Provider shall provide the same level of privacy protection as those laws require. Provider shall notify Customer within five (5) business days if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws or this DPA.

3.4 **Personnel.** Provider shall ensure that all personnel authorized to Process Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations. Such personnel may access Personal Data only to the extent needed to perform the Services.

3.5 **Location.** Provider shall Process and store Personal Data only within the United States. It may do so elsewhere only with Customer's prior written consent.

## 4. Security

4.1 **Safeguards.** For purposes of Section 9.2 of the Agreement, Provider shall implement and maintain a written information security program. That program shall include the administrative, technical and physical safeguards described in Annex 2. The safeguards shall be no less rigorous than the reasonable safeguards required by N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-bb.

4.2 **Devices and Delivery Records.** Personal Data on handheld devices, driver applications and delivery vehicles' electronic systems shall be encrypted. Those devices shall be capable of remote wipe. Printed manifests and delivery paperwork containing Personal Data shall be collected at the end of each route. They shall then be securely shredded or retained under lock.

4.3 **Review.** Provider shall review and test its safeguards at least annually. It shall promptly remediate any material deficiency identified.

## 5. Security Incidents

5.1 **Notice.** For purposes of Section 9.3 of the Agreement, Provider shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

5.2 **Contents of Notice.** The notice shall describe, to the extent known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate number of individuals and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it.

Provider shall supplement the notice as more information becomes available.

5.3 **Cooperation.** Provider shall take all reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer's investigation and with any notifications Customer elects or is required to make.

5.4 **Notifications by Customer.** Customer shall determine whether to notify affected individuals, regulators or other third parties of a Security Incident, and the content of any such notice. Provider shall not make any such notification without Customer's prior written approval, except where Provider is independently required to do so by law.

5.5 **Costs.** Provider shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of investigating and responding to any Security Incident caused by a breach of this DPA by Provider or its Subprocessors. These costs include notification, call center and credit monitoring costs.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 **Consent.** Provider shall not engage any Subprocessor without Customer's prior written consent, consistent with Section 7.2 of the Agreement. Annex 3 lists the Subprocessors approved as of the date of this DPA.

6.2 **Subprocessor Terms.** Provider shall impose on each Subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

6.3 **Responsibility.** Provider shall remain responsible for each Subprocessor's acts and omissions as if they were Provider's own.

## 7. Assistance, Records and Audits

7.1 **Consumer Requests.** Provider shall notify Customer within five (5) business days of receiving any request from an individual to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws. Provider shall not respond to the request except on Customer's instructions. Provider shall provide reasonable assistance so that Customer can respond to such requests.

7.2 **Regulatory Inquiries.** Provider shall promptly notify Customer of any inquiry from a governmental authority relating to Personal Data, to the extent legally permitted. Provider shall cooperate with Customer in responding.

7.3 **Audit.** On reasonable advance notice, Provider shall make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. This includes any current third-party security assessment or audit report. Customer or its independent auditor may conduct an on-site audit of Provider's Processing once per calendar year, and at any time following a Security Incident. Any such audit shall take place during normal business hours and shall be subject to reasonable confidentiality obligations.

7.4 **Remediation.** If Customer reasonably determines that Provider is Processing Personal Data in an unauthorized manner, Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate that Processing. Provider shall cooperate with those steps.

## 8. Return and Deletion

8.1 **On Termination.** For purposes of Sections 8.5 and 15.1 of the Agreement, Provider shall return Customer Data to Customer within thirty (30) days after termination or expiration of the Agreement or the applicable Statement of Work. The return shall be made in a commonly used, machine-readable format reasonably specified by Customer.

8.2 **Deletion.** After returning Customer Data, Provider shall securely delete all remaining copies of it, including copies held by Subprocessors. Provider shall certify the deletion in writing at Customer's request.

8.3 **Legal Retention.** Provider may retain Customer Data only to the extent required by applicable law. Any retained Customer Data shall remain subject to this DPA and the confidentiality obligations of the Agreement for as long as it is retained.

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** This DPA and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, including any dispute relating to the Processing of Personal Data or any Security Incident, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 10. General

10.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date of the Agreement. It remains in effect for as long as Provider or any Subprocessor Processes Personal Data.

10.2 **Amendment.** The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment to this DPA reasonably required to comply with changes in Data Protection Laws. This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10.3 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## Annex 2: Security Measures

1. Role-based access controls, unique user IDs and multi-factor authentication for remote access and for administrative access to systems Processing Personal Data.
2. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
3. Logging and monitoring of access to systems Processing Personal Data, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.
4. Timely patching of systems, plus endpoint protection on all workstations and mobile devices.
5. Physical access controls at the Facility, including badge access and visitor logs for areas where Personal Data is stored.
6. Annual security awareness training for all personnel with access to Personal Data.
7. A documented incident response plan, tested at least annually.
8. Business continuity and backup procedures, with backups encrypted and tested periodically.
9. Secure disposal of media and paper records containing Personal Data.
