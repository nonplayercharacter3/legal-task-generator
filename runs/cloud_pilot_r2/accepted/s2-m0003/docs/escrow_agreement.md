# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of November 20, 2026, by and among **Marrowind Packaging Corp.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), **Tessaly Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Corbel Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Seller, Buyer and the Escrow Agent are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring the Purchased Assets and assuming the Assumed Liabilities of the Business for a Purchase Price of $18,400,000.

B. Section 2.6(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $1,840,000 of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit serves as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Section 3.2(c) and Section 3.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement require Seller and Buyer to execute and deliver this Escrow Agreement at the Closing. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

(a) "**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

(b) "**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

(c) "**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less all amounts distributed from the Escrow Account under this Escrow Agreement.

(d) "**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a certificate of counsel for the presenting Party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

(e) "**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an authorized representative of each of Seller and Buyer.

(f) "**Objection Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.2.

(g) "**Release Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT; INVESTMENT

**2.1 Appointment and Deposit.** Seller and Buyer appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $1,840,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Seller and Buyer in writing promptly after receipt.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent for the benefit of Seller and Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall hold and disburse the Escrow Funds only as provided in this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party, except as provided in a Final Order.

**2.3 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a United States Treasury money market fund or interest-bearing deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured to the extent available by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, as Seller and Buyer jointly designate in Schedule B. Seller and Buyer may change the investment by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 2.3, or from the liquidation of any investment needed to make a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement.

**2.4 Tax Matters.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are distributed, consistent with Proposed Treasury Regulation Section 1.468B-8. All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds shall be reported to Buyer on IRS Form 1099 or other applicable form. Before the Closing Date, each of Seller and Buyer shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9. The Escrow Agent may withhold any tax required by law to be withheld from distributions under this Escrow Agreement.

## 3. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Claim Notice.** If Buyer, on its own behalf or on behalf of any other Buyer indemnitee, asserts a claim for indemnification under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to the Escrow Agent and Seller, on or before the Release Date, a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice shall state:

(a) the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of them if they are not yet determinable; and

(b) in reasonable detail, the nature of the claim and the provision of the Purchase Agreement on which it is based.

**3.2 Objection Notice.** Seller may object to all or any part of a claim set out in a Claim Notice by delivering a written notice of objection to the Escrow Agent and Buyer within 30 days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). Each Objection Notice shall specify the amount in dispute and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

**3.3 Payment of Uncontested Amounts.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period in Section 3.2, then on the fifth Business Day after the end of that period the Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer, from the Escrow Funds, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall pay the undisputed portion to Buyer within five Business Days after receiving the Objection Notice.

**3.4 Disputed Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount disputed in an Objection Notice until it receives either:

(a) Joint Written Instructions directing the disposition of that amount; or

(b) a Final Order directing the disposition of that amount.

The Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instructions or Final Order within five Business Days after receiving it.

**3.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, Buyer may later deliver an updated Claim Notice to the Escrow Agent and Seller stating the amount of Losses as finally determined. Sections 3.2 through 3.4 apply to the updated Claim Notice.

**3.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay any claim in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay the amount available. Any remaining amount is recoverable by Buyer directly from Seller as provided in Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement, subject to the terms of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent has no responsibility for any such remaining amount.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Release Date.** On the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), or if that date is not a Business Day, on the next Business Day, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate amount of all claims stated in Claim Notices delivered on or before the Release Date that have not been paid or resolved as of the Release Date (the "**Pending Claims Amount**").

**4.2 Release of Pending Claims Amount.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claims Amount after the Release Date. When each pending claim is resolved under Section 3, the Escrow Agent shall:

(a) pay to Buyer the amount, if any, due to Buyer on that claim; and

(b) release to Seller the amount, if any, by which the portion of the Pending Claims Amount held for that claim exceeds the amount paid to Buyer.

**4.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Escrow Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**4.4 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the recipient in Schedule A, or in a later written notice signed by the recipient and delivered to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent may confirm any wire instructions, other than those in Schedule A, by telephone call-back to a person listed in Schedule A for the Party giving the instructions.

**4.5 Termination.** This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 5 and 6 survive termination or the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 5. THE ESCROW AGENT

**5.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and has no duty to inquire into the terms of, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Seller and Buyer.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice and is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with that counsel's advice.

**5.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**5.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Seller and Buyer, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent against any loss, liability, claim or expense, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Seller and Buyer, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 5.4. A Party whose conduct gave rise to the indemnified amount shall bear all of it.

**5.5 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth in Schedule C, together with reimbursement of its reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses. Seller and Buyer shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses.

**5.6 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions concerning the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with a court of competent jurisdiction described in Section 7.2 and file an action in interpleader.

**5.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving Seller and Buyer at least 30 days' written notice. Seller and Buyer may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions delivered at least 30 days before the effective date of removal. Seller and Buyer shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The resignation or removal becomes effective when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to it. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction described in Section 7.2 to appoint one.

**5.8 Successor by Merger.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

## 6. KNOW-YOUR-CUSTOMER; AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVES

**6.1 Customer Identification.** Seller and Buyer shall provide the Escrow Agent with information reasonably requested to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification laws.

**6.2 Authorized Representatives.** Schedule A lists the individuals authorized to sign instructions on behalf of Seller and Buyer, together with specimen signatures and telephone numbers for call-back confirmation. A Party may update its information by written notice to the Escrow Agent signed by an authorized representative.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the State and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

**7.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

**7.4 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to Seller or Buyer shall be sent to the addresses provided in Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to Corbel Trust Company, N.A., 225 Liberty Street, 18th Floor, New York, New York 10281, Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**7.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**7.6 Assignment.** No Party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties, except as provided in Sections 5.7 and 5.8.

**7.7 Successors and Assigns.** This Escrow Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**7.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**7.9 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
