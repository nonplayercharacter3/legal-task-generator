# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 30, 2026, by and between **Marrowind Packaging Corp.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Tessaly Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller designs, manufactures, converts and sells corrugated packaging products, including corrugated sheets, boxes, displays and protective inserts (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

(b) "**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Transition Services Agreement, the Bill of Sale and the Escrow Agreement.

(c) "**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3.

(d) "**Bill of Sale**" means the Bill of Sale and Assignment and Assumption Agreement to be delivered by Seller and Buyer at the Closing.

(e) "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

(f) "**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

(g) "**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

(h) "**Escrow Agent**" means Corbel Trust Company, N.A.

(i) "**Escrow Agreement**" means the Indemnity Escrow Agreement to be entered into at the Closing among Seller, Buyer and the Escrow Agent.

(j) "**Escrow Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.6.

(k) "**Excluded Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.2.

(l) "**Excluded Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.4.

(m) "**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization and Good Standing), Section 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 4.5 (Title to Purchased Assets) and Section 4.12 (Brokers).

(n) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(o) "**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, individually or in the aggregate, materially adverse to the Purchased Assets or the results of operations or financial condition of the Business, other than effects arising from general economic conditions, changes in law or GAAP, or conditions generally affecting the corrugated packaging industry, in each case that do not disproportionately affect the Business.

(p) "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

(s) "**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Seller and Buyer.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed in Section 2.1 of the Disclosure Schedule, all of Seller's right, title and interest in all assets used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets (the "**Purchased Assets**"). The Purchased Assets include:

(a) all inventory, raw materials (including linerboard and medium), work in process, finished goods and supplies;

(b) all machinery, corrugators, converting equipment, die cutters, printers, tooling, vehicles, furniture and other tangible personal property;

(c) all contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all intellectual property used or held for use in the Business, including customer dies and print plates;

(e) all accounts receivable arising from the Business;

(f) all permits, to the extent transferable; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists relating to the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** Notwithstanding Section 2.1, the Purchased Assets do not include, and Seller shall retain, the following assets (the "**Excluded Assets**"): Seller's cash, the Albany manufacturing facility, and the HOLLIS trademark.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only (a) liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing, other than liabilities arising from any breach by Seller before the Closing, and (b) trade accounts payable of the Business incurred in the ordinary course and reflected in the Closing statement under Section 2.7 (together, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall remain responsible for, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets, all pre-Closing taxes of Seller, and all liabilities arising from the employment of any Person by Seller before the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.7.

**2.6 Payment at Closing; Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $1,840,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX; and

(b) the balance of the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least two Business Days before the Closing.

**2.7 Working Capital Adjustment.** Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the net working capital of the Business as of the Closing. If the net working capital is less than $2,150,000, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer; if it exceeds $2,150,000, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. Seller has 30 days to object to the statement. Disputes not resolved within 30 days after an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

**2.8 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code, as set forth in an allocation schedule prepared by Buyer within 90 days after the Closing Date and reasonably acceptable to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on November 20, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying board and shareholder resolutions authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it;

(e) the third-party consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule; and

(f) an IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments described in Section 2.6;

(b) the Bill of Sale, the Transition Services Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying resolutions of Buyer authorizing this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents, any applicable law, or any material contract to which Seller is a party.

**4.4 Consents.** Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each consent required to assign the Assigned Contracts to Buyer.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens.

**4.6 Condition of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.7 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheet of the Business as of December 31, 2025, and the unaudited balance sheet as of September 30, 2026, together with the related statements of income, fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Business as of their dates, in accordance with GAAP consistently applied.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Business involving annual payments in excess of $150,000. Each such contract is valid and in full force, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.9 Intellectual Property.** The operation of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property of any Person, and, to Seller's knowledge, no Person is infringing the intellectual property included in the Purchased Assets.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Environmental Matters.** The Business is conducted in material compliance with applicable laws, including environmental laws, and Seller holds all material permits required to operate the Business.

**4.11 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Business.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions to Closing, and Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**6.4 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective on the Closing Date, to the employees of the Business listed in Section 6.4 of the Disclosure Schedule on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to their current terms.

**6.5 Non-Competition.** For three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the manufacture or sale of corrugated packaging products within the United States, except through the ownership of the Excluded Assets in a manner that does not compete with the Business.

**6.6 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and Buyer shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide certain services to Buyer for a term of six months after the Closing Date.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** Following the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before Closing; (c) no Material Adverse Effect having occurred; and (d) delivery of the items in Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before Closing; and (c) delivery of the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, provided the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either Party upon a material breach by the other Party that is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void without liability of any Party, except for liability for willful breach before termination and except that Article X shall survive.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, managers and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any breach of or inaccuracy in any representation or warranty made by Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Seller under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to: (a) any breach of or inaccuracy in any representation or warranty made by Buyer in this Agreement; (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and (c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2 shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price.

(b) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses exceed $184,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(c) *Exceptions.* The limitations set forth in Section 9.4(a) and Section 9.4(b) shall not apply to any claim under Section 9.2(a), or to any claim arising from fraud.

(d) *Buyer Cap.* The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of the applicable claim.

**9.5 Claims Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice to the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any amount payable to a Buyer indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the funds then held by the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement and thereafter directly by Seller.

**9.7 Escrow Release.** The Escrow Agent shall release the remaining Escrow Amount, less the amount of any pending claims, to Seller 18 months after the Closing Date, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims arising from fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.
