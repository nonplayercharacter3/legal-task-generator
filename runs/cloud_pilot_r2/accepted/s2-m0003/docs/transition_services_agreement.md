# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 20, 2026, by and between **Marrowind Packaging Corp.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Tessaly Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring the Purchased Assets and assuming the Assumed Liabilities of the Business.

B. Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Seller will provide certain transitional services to Buyer so that Buyer can operate the Business in an orderly manner after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

(a) "**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.4.

(b) "**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule A.

(c) "**Service Recipient Data**" means all data and records relating to the Business that Seller processes, stores or generates in providing the Services.

(d) "**Services**" means the transitional services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 3.3.

(e) "**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

## 2. TERM AND TERMINATION

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date and continues until the date that is 6 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"). The Term may end earlier under Section 2.2 or Section 2.3.

**2.2 Early Termination of Individual Services by Buyer.** Buyer may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, by giving Seller at least 30 days' written notice. The Service Fees for a terminated Service end on the effective date of termination. If a Service was paid in advance, Seller shall refund the prorated unused portion within 30 days.

**2.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice of it. If the breach is a failure to pay undisputed Service Fees, the cure period is 15 days.

**2.4 Effect of Termination.** When this Agreement or any Service terminates:

(a) Buyer shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination; and

(b) Seller shall deliver to Buyer all Service Recipient Data relating to the terminated Services, in the format in which Seller maintains it or another format reasonably requested by Buyer.

Sections 2.4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Seller shall provide, or cause its Affiliates or third-party contractors to provide, the Services to Buyer. Seller remains responsible for the performance of any Affiliate or contractor it uses.

**3.2 Standard of Performance.** Seller shall provide each Service substantially as follows:

(a) in a manner, scope, nature, priority and quality consistent with how Seller provided the Service to the Business during the 12 months before the Closing Date; and

(b) with at least the same degree of care Seller uses for its own operations.

Seller is not required to provide any Service at a volume materially greater than the volume provided to the Business during that 12-month period unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

**3.3 Additional Services.** Buyer may ask Seller to provide services that are not described in Schedule A but that Seller provided to the Business during the 12 months before the Closing Date ("**Additional Services**"). Seller shall consider each request in good faith. Any Additional Services and their fees shall be documented in a written amendment to Schedule A signed by both Parties.

**3.4 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall designate an individual to act as its primary contact for the administration of this Agreement (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). Seller's initial Service Coordinator is its Vice President, Finance. Buyer's initial Service Coordinator is its Director of Integration. Either Party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice. The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least every two weeks during the Term.

**3.5 Migration Assistance.** During the Term, Seller shall cooperate reasonably with Buyer to transition each Service to Buyer or to Buyer's designated provider. This includes giving reasonable access to Seller personnel who are knowledgeable about the Services. Buyer shall reimburse Seller's reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs of this cooperation.

**3.6 Third-Party Consents.** If providing a Service requires the consent of a third party, Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent. Buyer shall bear the reasonable, documented cost of obtaining it, subject to prior written approval by Buyer. If the consent cannot be obtained, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to find a reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 4. BUYER OBLIGATIONS

**4.1 Cooperation.** Buyer shall provide Seller with information, access and cooperation reasonably necessary for Seller to perform the Services.

**4.2 Systems Access.** If Buyer personnel are given access to Seller's information technology systems, Buyer shall ensure that those personnel:

(a) comply with Seller's written security and acceptable-use policies that have been provided to Buyer; and

(b) use the access solely to receive the Services.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** Buyer shall pay Seller the Service Fees for each Service as set forth in Schedule A. The Service Fees are intended to reflect Seller's fully loaded cost of providing the Services, without markup.

**5.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Seller shall invoice Buyer monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Fees and any reimbursable expenses. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt, by wire transfer to an account designated by Seller.

**5.3 Disputed Invoices.** If Buyer disputes any invoice in good faith, Buyer shall:

(a) notify Seller in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute within 20 days after receipt of the invoice; and

(b) pay the undisputed portion when due.

The Service Coordinators shall attempt to resolve the dispute within 15 days after the notice.

**5.4 Taxes.** Buyer shall pay any sales, use or similar taxes imposed on the Services, other than taxes based on Seller's income.

**5.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the rate of 1% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower, from the due date until paid.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

**6.1 Confidentiality.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party that it receives under this Agreement. Each Party shall use that information only to perform or receive the Services. These obligations do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed by the receiving Party; or

(d) must be disclosed under law or court order, provided that the receiving Party gives prompt notice to the disclosing Party where legally permitted.

**6.2 Service Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, all Service Recipient Data is owned by Buyer as part of the Purchased Assets. Seller shall:

(a) maintain the Service Recipient Data using reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards;

(b) not use it except to provide the Services; and

(c) return or destroy it, at Buyer's election, within 30 days after the end of the Term.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Seller makes no representations or warranties, express or implied, regarding the Services. This includes any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

**7.2 Limitation of Liability.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any consequential, special, indirect or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement, except in the case of gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of Section 6. Seller's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement, except in the case of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**7.3 Indemnification.** Each Party shall indemnify the other Party against Losses arising from third-party claims to the extent caused by the indemnifying Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing or receiving the Services. The procedures in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement apply to these claims, with necessary changes.

## 8. FORCE MAJEURE

**8.1 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, fire, flood, pandemic, war, terrorism, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees, or failure of public utilities. The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Service Fees for any affected Service are suspended for the period during which the Service is not provided.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement. A copy of each notice shall also be sent to the other Party's Service Coordinator.

**9.2 Relationship of the Parties.** Seller is an independent contractor in providing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

**9.3 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

**9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**9.6 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to an Affiliate that acquires all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets, provided Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**9.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**9.8 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
