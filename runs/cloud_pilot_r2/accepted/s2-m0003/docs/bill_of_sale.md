# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and entered into as of November 20, 2026, by and between **Marrowind Packaging Corp.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Tessaly Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of October 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Purchased Assets to Buyer. Buyer has agreed to purchase the Purchased Assets and to assume the Assumed Liabilities.

B. Section 3.2(a) and Section 3.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement require the Parties to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the payment of the Purchase Price, the mutual covenants in the Purchase Agreement, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Assigned Contracts**" means the contracts listed in Section 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule.

(b) "**Purchased Assets**" means all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights of every kind and nature, whether real, personal or mixed, tangible or intangible, wherever located, used or held for use in the Business. This includes:

> (i) all inventory, raw materials (including linerboard and medium), work in process, finished goods and supplies;
>
> (ii) all machinery, corrugators, converting equipment, die cutters, printers, tooling, vehicles, furniture and other tangible personal property;
>
> (iii) the Assigned Contracts;
>
> (iv) all intellectual property, including trademarks, trade names, customer dies and print plates;
>
> (v) all accounts receivable;
>
> (vi) all permits, to the extent transferable; and
>
> (vii) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists relating to the Business.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "include" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation."

## 2. CONVEYANCE OF PURCHASED ASSETS

**2.1 Sale and Transfer.** Effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date, Seller hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and Buyer hereby accepts, all of the Purchased Assets. The Purchased Assets are conveyed free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed in Section 2.1 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**2.2 Delivery of Possession.** Seller shall deliver possession of the tangible Purchased Assets to Buyer at the locations where those assets are located on the Closing Date.

**2.3 Power of Attorney.** Seller hereby constitutes and appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller but on behalf and for the benefit of Buyer, to:

(a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) give receipts and releases for and in respect of the Purchased Assets;

(c) institute and prosecute any proceedings that Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets; and

(d) endorse, without recourse, any checks or other instruments payable to Seller in respect of the Purchased Assets.

Seller acknowledges that this power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 3. ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION

**3.1 Assignment of Assigned Contracts.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller hereby assigns, transfers and conveys to Buyer all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned Contracts.

**3.2 Assumption of Assumed Liabilities.** Effective as of the Closing, Buyer hereby accepts the assignment in Section 3.1 and assumes and agrees to pay, perform and discharge, when due, the Assumed Liabilities.

**3.3 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer does not assume, and shall have no responsibility for, any Excluded Liability. Seller shall remain responsible for all Excluded Liabilities.

**3.4 Non-Assignable Contracts.** This Bill of Sale does not assign any Assigned Contract if an attempted assignment without the consent of a third party would constitute a breach of it, until that consent is obtained. Until then, Seller shall hold the Assigned Contract for the benefit of Buyer. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in any reasonable arrangement designed to provide Buyer with the benefits of it. Buyer shall perform Seller's obligations under that contract arising after the Closing, to the extent those obligations would have been Assumed Liabilities. Seller shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, as required by Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**4.1 Further Actions.** From time to time after the Closing, at Buyer's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of sale, conveyance, transfer and assignment, and take such other actions, as Buyer may reasonably request to vest in Buyer title to the Purchased Assets. These may include separate assignments of intellectual property for recording with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and certificates of title for vehicles.

**4.2 Receipt of Payments.** If Seller receives any payment, remittance or other amount in respect of any Purchased Asset after the Closing, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer. Seller shall pay it over to Buyer within 10 Business Days after receipt.

## 5. MISCELLANEOUS

**5.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and assigns.

**5.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Bill of Sale confers any right or remedy on any Person other than the Parties and their respective successors and assigns.

**5.3 Notices.** All notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.4 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**5.5 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York. This applies without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**5.6 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the State and federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale.

**5.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**5.8 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
