# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2022 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Glogair Artisan Bakeries, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Nulind Foodservice Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures par-baked breads, rolls and laminated pastry products at its commercial bakeries.

B. Distributor operates a foodservice distribution business serving restaurants, institutional kitchens, hotels and other foodservice operators.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings given below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. For this definition, "control" means the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of a person, or the power to direct the management and policies of a person, whether by contract or otherwise.

(b) "**Assign**" means to assign, transfer, delegate or otherwise convey this Agreement or any right or obligation under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law, by merger or consolidation, or otherwise. With respect to Supplier, "Assign" includes any Change of Control of Supplier. "**Assignment**" has a correlative meaning.

(c) "**Change of Control**" means (i) the sale, transfer or other disposition, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of a majority of the outstanding voting stock of Supplier; (ii) any merger, consolidation or reorganization of Supplier after which the holders of Supplier's voting stock immediately before the transaction hold less than a majority of the voting power of the surviving entity; or (iii) the sale of all or substantially all of Supplier's assets.

(d) "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 11.1.

(e) "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

(f) "**Products**" means all baked goods manufactured by or for Supplier or any of its Affiliates, including par-baked breads, rolls and laminated pastry, in each case as offered by Supplier from time to time.

(g) "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement.

(h) "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of January 1, 2022, as amended from time to time.

(i) "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

(j) "**Specifications**" means the product specifications for each Product, as set out in or maintained under the Quality Agreement.

(k) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

(l) "**Territory**" means the States of New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. APPOINTMENT AND EXCLUSIVITY

2.1 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Supplier and its Affiliates shall not sell, offer for sale or otherwise distribute, directly or indirectly, any Products in the Territory through any channel of trade except through Distributor. Supplier shall refer to Distributor all inquiries for Products received from customers located in the Territory.

2.2 **Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor accepts that appointment. Distributor may resell Products in the Territory to such customers as it determines in its discretion.

2.3 **Distributor Efforts.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products in the Territory, maintain adequate frozen and ambient warehouse capacity for the Products, and maintain a trained sales force familiar with the Products.

2.4 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship between the Parties, and neither Party may bind the other.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five years, ending December 31, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

3.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five-year terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal not more than 210 nor fewer than 180 days before expiration of the then-current term.

## 4. ORDERS AND FORECASTS

4.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifth business day of each calendar month, Distributor shall provide Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its anticipated Product requirements for the following three months.

4.2 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by Purchase Order, which shall state the Products, quantities, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within two business days of receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order consistent with the most recent forecast.

4.3 **Conflicting Terms.** The terms of this Agreement govern each Purchase Order. Any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect unless signed by both Parties.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for Products set out in Supplier's distributor price list in effect on the date of the applicable Purchase Order. Supplier may revise its distributor price list no more than once in each calendar year on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Distributor.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each order.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.4 **Late Payment.** Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Distributor shall pay unless it provides a valid resale or exemption certificate.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's bakery (Incoterms 2020) or to such other delivery location as is stated in the applicable accepted Purchase Order, in temperature-controlled conveyance meeting the requirements of the Quality Agreement.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery.

6.3 **Inspection.** Distributor may inspect Products upon receipt and shall notify Supplier of any visible damage, shortage or temperature excursion within two (2) business days after delivery. Products not rejected within that period are deemed accepted, without prejudice to Distributor's rights under Section 8.

## 7. QUALITY AND SPECIFICATIONS

7.1 **Quality Agreement.** Each Party shall comply with the Quality Agreement. Supplier shall manufacture, package, label, store and ship all Products in accordance with the Specifications and the Quality Agreement.

7.2 **Regulatory Compliance.** Supplier shall manufacture the Products in compliance with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and their implementing regulations, and all applicable state and local food safety laws. Distributor shall store and handle Products in compliance with applicable law and the Quality Agreement.

7.3 **Changes.** Supplier shall not change the formulation, ingredients, allergen profile or packaging of any Product without at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Distributor, except where required by law.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, upon delivery and through the shelf life stated in the Specifications, each Product (a) conforms to the Specifications; (b) is not adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act; and (c) may lawfully be introduced into interstate commerce.

8.2 **Remedy.** For any Product that breaches Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, replace the Product or credit Distributor the price paid, and shall reimburse Distributor's reasonable costs of return or destruction.

8.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any agreement to which it is a party.

8.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 8, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. RECALLS

9.1 **Recall Procedure.** If either Party becomes aware of information indicating that a Product may be adulterated, misbranded or otherwise unsafe, it shall promptly notify the other Party. The Parties shall cooperate in conducting any recall or market withdrawal in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

9.2 **Recall Costs.** Supplier shall bear the reasonable costs of any recall or withdrawal to the extent caused by Supplier's breach of this Agreement or the Quality Agreement, and Distributor shall bear such costs to the extent caused by Distributor's improper storage or handling of Products.

## 10. TRADEMARKS

10.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except with an Assignment permitted under Section 16), royalty-free license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory solely to advertise, promote and sell the Products.

10.2 **Ownership.** All goodwill arising from Distributor's use of Supplier's trademarks inures to Supplier. Distributor shall not register or use any mark confusingly similar to Supplier's trademarks.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, customer lists, recipes, formulations and manufacturing processes.

11.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and the Quality Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its employees, agents and advisors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section 11.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or is independently developed. The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the disclosing Party prompt notice where lawful.

11.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 11 survive for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from (a) any Product that breaches Section 8.1; (b) any claim of bodily injury, illness or death caused by a Product, except to the extent caused by Distributor's improper storage or handling; or (c) any claim that Supplier's trademarks infringe a third party's rights.

12.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from (a) Distributor's improper storage, handling or transportation of Products after delivery; or (b) Distributor's negligence or willful misconduct.

12.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim imposing any obligation on the indemnified Party without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 **Exclusion.** EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11 OR SECTION 12, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, each Party shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best. Supplier shall also maintain product contamination and recall insurance of not less than $2,000,000. Each Party shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **Termination by Either Party.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it; or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.2 **Termination by Distributor.** Distributor may terminate this Agreement upon written notice to Supplier if:

(a) Supplier fails to fill accepted Purchase Orders in full and on time in three or more calendar months within any twelve-month period;

(b) Supplier initiates a recall of any Product classified by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration as a Class I recall more than once in any twelve-month period; or

(c) Supplier undertakes any Assignment without Distributor's prior written consent under Section 16.2.

15.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, (a) Distributor shall pay all amounts due for Products delivered; (b) Supplier shall fill all Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination unless terminated by Supplier under Section 15.1; (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (d) Distributor may sell off its remaining inventory of Products for ninety (90) days.

15.4 **Survival.** Sections 5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15.3, 15.4 and 18 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

16.1 **By Distributor.** Distributor may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any person without the consent of Supplier, upon written notice to Supplier.

16.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall not Assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Distributor. Any purported Assignment by Supplier without that consent is void.

16.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 17. NOTICES AND FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the address of the receiving Party below (or any other address that Party designates by notice). Notices are effective upon receipt.

If to Supplier: Glogair Artisan Bakeries, Inc., Attention: Chief Executive Officer, with a copy to its General Counsel.

If to Distributor: Nulind Foodservice Distribution LLC, Attention: President, with a copy to its General Counsel.

17.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, epidemic, labor disputes, governmental action or failure of utilities, provided the affected Party promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Supplier from supplying Products for more than thirty (30) days, Distributor may obtain substitute products from third parties for the duration of the event without breach of this Agreement.

## 18. MISCELLANEOUS

18.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and all disputes arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

18.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

18.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

18.4 **Order of Precedence.** This Agreement prevails over the Quality Agreement in any conflict between them.

18.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

18.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
