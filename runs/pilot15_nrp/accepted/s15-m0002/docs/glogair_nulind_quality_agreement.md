# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2022 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Glogair Artisan Bakeries, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Nulind Foodservice Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor have entered into an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 1, 2022 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. Section 7.1 of the Distribution Agreement requires each Party to comply with this Quality Agreement. It also requires Supplier to manufacture, package, label, store and ship all Products in accordance with the Specifications and this Quality Agreement.

C. The Parties enter into this Quality Agreement to set out their respective responsibilities for food safety, product quality, traceability and recalls of the Products.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products that Supplier manufactures and supplies to Distributor under the Distribution Agreement. It covers manufacture, packaging, labeling, storage and shipment by Supplier, and receipt, storage, handling and onward distribution by Distributor.

1.2 **Relationship to Distribution Agreement.** This Quality Agreement supplements the Distribution Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Products," "Purchase Order," "Specifications," "Term" and "Territory."

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Applicable Food Law**" means the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and their implementing regulations, including 21 C.F.R. Part 117. It also includes all applicable state and local food safety laws in the Territory and in each jurisdiction where Supplier manufactures Products.

(b) "**Batch**" means a quantity of a Product produced on a single production line in a single production run and identified by a single lot code.

(c) "**Certificate of Analysis**" means a document issued by Supplier for a Batch that states the results of the tests required by the applicable Specification.

(d) "**Food Safety Plan**" means Supplier's written food safety plan for each bakery, prepared in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 117, Subpart C.

(e) "**GFSI Certification**" means a current certification under a scheme benchmarked by the Global Food Safety Initiative, such as SQF or BRCGS.

(f) "**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product that fails to conform to its Specification, to this Quality Agreement or to Applicable Food Law.

(g) "**Quality Contact**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 3.3.

(h) "**Temperature Excursion**" means any period during which a Product is held outside the storage temperature range stated in its Specification.

## 3. GENERAL RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 **Supplier.** Supplier is responsible for:

(a) manufacturing the Products in accordance with the Specifications, its Food Safety Plan and Applicable Food Law;

(b) maintaining GFSI Certification for each bakery that manufactures Products;

(c) qualifying and monitoring its ingredient and packaging suppliers; and

(d) releasing each Batch for shipment only after it has been reviewed against the applicable Specification.

3.2 **Distributor.** Distributor is responsible for:

(a) receiving, storing, handling and transporting Products after delivery in accordance with the Specifications, Section 6 and Applicable Food Law;

(b) maintaining traceability records for Products it receives and ships; and

(c) promptly reporting customer complaints and suspected Nonconforming Product to Supplier.

3.3 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact responsible for matters under this Quality Agreement. It shall notify the other Party in writing of the Quality Contact's name and contact details and of any change. Each Party's Quality Contact, or a qualified designee, shall be reachable at all times for food safety emergencies.

## 4. SPECIFICATIONS AND CHANGE CONTROL

4.1 **Specifications.** Supplier shall maintain a written Specification for each Product. Each Specification shall state, as applicable:

(a) ingredients and their declaration order;

(b) allergen profile;

(c) net weight and dimensions;

(d) par-bake parameters and finishing instructions;

(e) microbiological and physical limits;

(f) frozen or ambient storage temperature range;

(g) shelf life; and

(h) packaging and labeling requirements.

Supplier shall provide current Specifications to Distributor's Quality Contact. Supplier shall also provide a revised Specification at least sixty (60) days before it takes effect.

4.2 **Change Control.** Supplier shall not change the formulation, ingredients, allergen profile or packaging of any Product except in accordance with Section 7.3 of the Distribution Agreement. For each change, Supplier shall deliver to Distributor a revised Specification and updated labeling. Where an allergen is added, Supplier shall deliver a written allergen statement.

4.3 **Labeling.** Supplier shall ensure that all Product labels comply with Applicable Food Law. This includes allergen declarations under the Food Allergen Labeling and Consumer Protection Act and the FASTER Act. Each case label shall bear the Product name, lot code, production date and best-by or use-by date.

## 5. FOOD SAFETY PROGRAMS

5.1 **Food Safety Plan.** Supplier shall maintain and implement a Food Safety Plan for each bakery. A preventive controls qualified individual shall review each Food Safety Plan at least every three years. Supplier shall also review the plan whenever a significant change in a process or Product could create a new hazard.

5.2 **Prerequisite Programs.** Supplier shall maintain the following written programs:

(a) sanitation;

(b) environmental monitoring, including for *Listeria* species where Products are exposed post-bake;

(c) allergen control, including segregation, changeover and validated cleaning;

(d) pest control;

(e) foreign material control, including metal detection or x-ray inspection of all finished Products;

(f) glass and brittle plastics control; and

(g) employee hygiene and training.

5.3 **Testing.** Supplier shall test each Batch as the applicable Specification requires. It shall retain test results and Certificates of Analysis for each Batch and provide them to Distributor on request within two (2) business days.

5.4 **Certification.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with a copy of each GFSI Certification and each annual certification audit report summary. If any GFSI Certification is suspended, withdrawn or downgraded, Supplier shall notify Distributor within two (2) business days.

## 6. STORAGE, TRANSPORT AND TEMPERATURE CONTROL

6.1 **Supplier Shipments.** Supplier shall ship Products in temperature-controlled conveyance that maintains the storage temperature range in the applicable Specification. Each conveyance shall have a continuous temperature recorder. Supplier shall comply with the FDA Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule at 21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart O, to the extent it acts as shipper, loader or carrier.

6.2 **Distributor Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store and handle Products in the following manner:

(a) frozen Products at or below 0°F (-18°C), and ambient Products in accordance with the applicable Specification;

(b) in clean, pest-free warehouses and vehicles;

(c) segregated from non-food items and from products that could cause cross-contamination; and

(d) on a first-expired, first-out basis.

Distributor shall not repackage, relabel or alter any Product.

6.3 **Temperature Excursions.** If a Party becomes aware of a Temperature Excursion affecting Products in its custody, it shall quarantine the affected Products. It shall notify the other Party's Quality Contact within one (1) business day. The Parties shall jointly determine whether the affected Products may be released, reworked or destroyed. Supplier's assessment governs on product safety.

6.4 **Receipt.** Distributor shall review temperature records upon receipt of each delivery. It shall report any Temperature Excursion, visible damage or shortage within the period stated in Section 6.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 7. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

7.1 **Audit Rights.** Distributor and its successors and permitted assigns may audit and inspect Supplier's bakeries, warehouses and other facilities used to manufacture, store or ship Products. They may also audit Supplier's quality systems and records. Audits may be conducted directly or through a qualified third-party auditor. Each audit shall take place during normal business hours on at least ten (10) business days' prior written notice, and not more than once in any calendar year. Where a food safety event or a Class I recall has occurred, an audit may be conducted at any time on reasonable notice.

7.2 **Scope of Audit.** An audit under Section 7.1 may cover the following:

(a) the Food Safety Plan and prerequisite programs;

(b) Batch, testing and release records;

(c) supplier approval and ingredient records;

(d) sanitation and environmental monitoring results; and

(e) the recipes and formulations of the Products, including ingredient percentages, process parameters and par-bake profiles. These may be reviewed to verify conformity with the Specifications and allergen declarations.

7.3 **Supplier Cooperation.** Supplier shall give auditors reasonable access to the relevant facilities, personnel and records. It shall answer reasonable questions during the audit.

7.4 **Audit Findings.** Distributor shall deliver a written audit report to Supplier within thirty (30) days after the audit is completed. Supplier shall respond within thirty (30) days after receiving the report with a corrective action plan for each finding. Supplier shall carry out each corrective action plan within the agreed timeframes.

7.5 **Confidentiality.** All information obtained during an audit is Supplier's Confidential Information. It is subject to Section 11 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 8. NONCONFORMING PRODUCT AND COMPLAINTS

8.1 **Notification.** Each Party shall notify the other Party's Quality Contact of any suspected Nonconforming Product. Notice shall be given within one (1) business day where the issue may affect food safety, and within five (5) business days in all other cases.

8.2 **Quarantine and Disposition.** Distributor shall quarantine suspected Nonconforming Product in its custody pending Supplier's investigation. Supplier shall give its written disposition within five (5) business days. Remedies for Nonconforming Product are as set out in Section 8.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

8.3 **Customer Complaints.** Distributor shall forward to Supplier each customer complaint concerning a Product within two (2) business days of receipt. A complaint alleging illness, injury, allergen exposure or foreign material shall be forwarded within twenty-four (24) hours. Each forwarded complaint shall include the Product, lot code, quantity, customer details and any available samples or photographs. Supplier shall investigate each complaint and provide a written response. Where the complaint is substantiated, the response shall include its root cause analysis and corrective actions.

## 9. RECALLS AND MARKET WITHDRAWALS

9.1 **Decision.** Supplier is responsible for deciding whether to initiate a recall or market withdrawal of any Product and for any communications with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and other regulators concerning it. Distributor may initiate a withdrawal of Products from its own inventory where it reasonably believes it is required to do so for food safety reasons. In that case, it shall notify Supplier immediately.

9.2 **Execution.** Upon notice of a recall or market withdrawal, Distributor shall, within twenty-four (24) hours:

(a) stop shipment of the affected Products;

(b) identify and notify all customers that received affected lots, using the recall communication Supplier approves; and

(c) quarantine and account for all affected inventory.

Distributor shall provide Supplier with reconciliation reports and effectiveness checks as Supplier reasonably requests.

9.3 **Mock Recalls.** Each Party shall conduct at least one mock recall each calendar year that traces a Product one step forward and one step back. Each Party shall share the results with the other on request.

9.4 **Costs.** Recall costs are allocated as provided in Section 9.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 10. RECORDS AND TRACEABILITY

10.1 **Supplier Records.** Supplier shall maintain the following records for each Batch for at least two (2) years after the Batch's expiration date, or longer if Applicable Food Law requires:

(a) production records;

(b) ingredient lot records;

(c) testing records and Certificates of Analysis;

(d) release records; and

(e) shipping records.

10.2 **Distributor Records.** Distributor shall maintain records of the following for at least two (2) years after the applicable expiration date. These records shall be sufficient to identify, within four (4) hours of a request, every customer that received any lot of Product:

(a) receipt of each lot of Product;

(b) storage temperatures; and

(c) each shipment to customers by lot code.

10.3 **Food Traceability Rule.** To the extent any Product or ingredient is on the FDA Food Traceability List, each Party shall maintain the key data elements and critical tracking events required by 21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart S.

## 11. REGULATORY INSPECTIONS

11.1 **Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other within one (1) business day after any regulatory inspection, inquiry, sampling, warning letter or Form FDA 483 that relates to the Products.

11.2 **Cooperation.** Each Party shall provide the other with copies of any related observations and its responses, redacted as necessary for unrelated information. Each Party shall cooperate reasonably in responding to regulators.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for the Term of the Distribution Agreement.

12.2 **Survival.** Sections 7.5, 9, 10 and 11 survive expiration or termination for so long as any Product supplied under the Distribution Agreement remains within its shelf life or remains subject to a recall, and for the record retention periods in Section 10.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Review.** The Quality Contacts shall meet at least once each calendar year to review this Quality Agreement, quality performance, complaints and corrective actions. Either Party may propose amendments.

13.2 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. Specifications may be updated in accordance with Section 4.

13.3 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
