**Re: Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 1, 2022 between Glogair Artisan Bakeries, Inc. and Nulind Foodservice Distribution LLC: Notice of Pending Acquisition and Notice of Non-Renewal**

Dear Sir or Madam:

## 1. Purpose of this Letter

We refer to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of January 1, 2022 (the "**Agreement**") between Glogair Artisan Bakeries, Inc. ("**Glogair**" or "**Supplier**") and Nulind Foodservice Distribution LLC ("**Nulind**" or "**Distributor**"). We also refer to the companion Quality Agreement between the same parties dated as of January 1, 2022. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

This letter is formal notice under Section 17.1 of the Agreement. It concerns two matters: the pending acquisition of Glogair, and Glogair's intention not to renew the Agreement.

## 2. Pending Acquisition by Kestrune Baking Holdings, LLC

Glogair has entered into a definitive agreement with Kestrune Baking Holdings, LLC ("**Kestrune**"). Under that agreement, Kestrune will acquire all of the outstanding stock of Glogair. The transaction is expected to close on December 1, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.

The transaction is structured as a purchase of stock from Glogair's shareholders. After closing, Glogair will continue as the same legal entity and will remain the Supplier under the Agreement. No contract will be transferred. Because the transaction is not an assignment of the Agreement by Glogair, no consent of Nulind is required under Section 16 of the Agreement. We are providing this notice as a courtesy and in the interest of a smooth transition.

## 3. About Kestrune

Kestrune is a privately held baking company. It operates commercial bakeries that supply grocery retailers in New Jersey and New York. After closing, Glogair will operate as a wholly owned subsidiary of Kestrune. Glogair's bakery operations, production staff and quality personnel will remain in place.

## 4. Notice of Non-Renewal

Glogair hereby gives Nulind written notice under Section 3.2 of the Agreement that it does not intend to renew the Agreement. Glogair intends for the Agreement to expire at the end of the Initial Term on December 31, 2026, and does not accept any renewal of the Agreement for a Renewal Term.

Until that date, Glogair will continue to perform its obligations under the Agreement and the Quality Agreement. This includes accepting and filling Purchase Orders in accordance with Section 4 of the Agreement.

## 5. Transition Matters

We would welcome a meeting in the coming weeks to discuss the following:

(a) the wind-down of Purchase Orders and forecasts for the remainder of the Initial Term;

(b) Nulind's sell-off of its remaining inventory of Products under Section 15.3(d); and

(c) the return or destruction of Confidential Information under Section 15.3(c).

## 6. Reservation and Contact

Nothing in this letter waives any right or remedy of Glogair under the Agreement or the Quality Agreement. All such rights and remedies are expressly reserved.
