# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 15, 2026, by and between **Miriv Provisioning Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Breidoun Aviation Holdings, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Ostrander Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company operates commissaries that supply inflight meals, galley provisioning and crew meals to regional air carriers.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale of Shares.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests and other encumbrances.

**2.2 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is the amount set forth in Schedule 2.2 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), as adjusted under Section 2.4 (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.3 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least two Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to the Base Purchase Price, (a) plus the amount, if any, by which Seller's good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, or (b) minus the amount, if any, by which the Target Working Capital exceeds that estimate.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within 45 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a written statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting detail.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with any item in the Closing Statement, Buyer may deliver to Seller a written notice of disagreement (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within 30 days after delivery of the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for Buyer's position. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement and Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital shall be final and binding on the Parties.

(c) *Resolution.* If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 20 days to resolve the disputed items. Any items not resolved within that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by Buyer and Seller (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, shall limit its review to the disputed items and shall resolve each item within the range of values asserted by the Parties. The determination of the Independent Accountant shall be final and binding on the Parties absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be borne by Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed items.

(d) *Access.* During the review period under Section 2.4(b) and any resolution period under Section 2.4(c), Buyer shall give Seller and its representatives reasonable access to the books, records and personnel of the Company used in preparing the Closing Statement.

(e) *Payment.* Within five Business Days after Closing Working Capital becomes final under this Section 2.4, (i) if final Closing Working Capital exceeds the estimate used under Section 2.3, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller, and (ii) if the estimate exceeds final Closing Working Capital, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on July 1, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the date on which the Closing actually occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Key Employee and the Company;

(iii) resignations, effective as of the Closing, of the directors and officers of the Company listed on Schedule 3.2(a)(iii);

(iv) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(v) a certificate of the Secretary of the Company certifying its organizational documents and good standing; and

(vi) the certificate required by Section 7.1(c).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller the payment required by Section 2.3, the Transition Services Agreement duly executed by the Company, and the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of New York and is qualified to do business in each jurisdiction in which its operations require qualification.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and each Ancillary Agreement to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes, and each such Ancillary Agreement when executed will constitute, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The Shares constitute all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all encumbrances. No options, warrants or other rights to acquire capital stock of the Company are outstanding.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, any Law applicable to them, or any Material Contract, and require no consent of or filing with any Governmental Authority except as listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule contains the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet as of March 31, 2026. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except those reflected in the March 31, 2026 balance sheet and those incurred in the ordinary course of business since that date.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course and no event has occurred that has had or would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party that involves annual payments in excess of $100,000, each customer contract with a regional air carrier, and each contract with a supplier of food, linen, equipment or galley services (each, a "**Material Contract**"). Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.9 Customers and Suppliers.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the ten largest customers and ten largest suppliers of the Company for the year ended December 31, 2025. No such customer or supplier has notified the Company of an intention to terminate or materially reduce its relationship with the Company.

**4.10 Real Property.** Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all real property leased by the Company, including each commissary facility. The Company holds a valid leasehold interest in each such property.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, including food safety, sanitation and airport access requirements. The Company holds all permits, including health department and FDA registrations, required to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.12 Intellectual Property.** (a) Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered and material unregistered Company Intellectual Property. (b) The Company is the sole and exclusive owner of all Company Intellectual Property, including the Recipes and Specifications and the Ostrander Marks, free and clear of all encumbrances, and neither Seller nor any of its Affiliates holds any right, title or interest in any Company Intellectual Property. (c) The conduct of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person, and no claim alleging any such violation is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened. (d) The Company has taken commercially reasonable measures to protect the confidentiality of the Recipes and Specifications.

**4.13 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each plan has been maintained in compliance in all material respects with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed and has paid all taxes shown as due on them. No tax audit or proceeding is pending against the Company.

**4.15 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.16 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all environmental Laws applicable to its commissary operations.

**4.17 Insurance.** Section 4.17 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all insurance policies maintained by the Company, including product liability coverage. All such policies are in full force and effect.

**4.18 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization; Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of New York, and has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of securities Laws.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the amounts payable by it under this Agreement.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its customer, supplier and employee relationships.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Notice of Certain Events.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.1 not to be satisfied.

**6.4 Exclusivity.** Before the Closing or the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company, the Shares or a material portion of the Company's assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, keep confidential all information concerning the Company and the Business, including the Recipes and Specifications, except as required by Law.

**6.6 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation by Seller.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) engage in the Business anywhere in the United States, or (b) solicit any customer of the Company or hire any employee of the Company.

**6.7 Customer Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, any consents of regional air carrier customers required under Material Contracts in connection with the change of control of the Company.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party may reasonably request to carry out the purposes of this Agreement.

**6.9 Key Employee Agreement.** Seller shall cause the Key Employee to execute and deliver, at or before the Closing, the Key Employee Agreement with the Company, which shall include a covenant by the Key Employee not to engage in the Business, and not to solicit customers or employees of the Company, for a period of not less than two years after termination of her employment with the Company.

**6.10 Confirmatory IP Assignment.** Without limiting Section 4.12, if after the Closing Buyer determines that Seller or any of its Affiliates holds or may hold any right, title or interest in any Company Intellectual Property, Seller shall, at Buyer's request and without further consideration, promptly execute and deliver to the Company a confirmatory assignment of all such right, title and interest in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) Buyer has received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the matters in clauses (a) and (b); and (d) Seller has made the deliveries required by Section 3.2(a).

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) Seller has received a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the matters in clauses (a) and (b); and (d) Buyer has made the deliveries required by Section 3.2(b).

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall be responsible for and shall pay all taxes of the Company attributable to periods (or portions of periods) ending on or before the Closing Date.

**8.2 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of tax returns and the conduct of any tax audit relating to the Company.

**8.3 Transfer Taxes.** Transfer, documentary, sales and similar taxes arising from the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that (a) the Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.12 survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, and (b) the representations in Section 4.14 survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement, (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement, and (c) any taxes for which Seller is responsible under Section 8.1.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, and (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** A Person seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Exclusions.* The Cap does not apply to Losses arising out of (i) Fraud, (ii) any breach of a Fundamental Representation, or (iii) any breach of the representations and warranties in Section 4.12 (Intellectual Property). Seller's aggregate liability for the matters in clauses (ii) and (iii) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for the matters described in Section 9.5(b)) until the aggregate Losses exceed 0.75% of the Base Purchase Price, after which Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(d) *Mitigation; Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified Person in respect of the claim.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and determinations under Section 2.4, indemnification under this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by July 31, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the cause; or (c) by either Party if the other Party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within 10 days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it becomes void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.5, this Section 10.2 and Article XI survive, and no Party is relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a Party designates by notice).

**11.2 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**11.3 Assignment.** No Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or to any lender as collateral.

**11.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party.

**11.5 Waiver.** No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against which it is asserted.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**11.8 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for disputes under any Transaction Document, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**11.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY TRANSACTION DOCUMENT.

**11.10 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, the Ancillary Agreement controls as to its subject matter; otherwise this Agreement controls.

**11.11 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.12 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of Section 6.5, Section 6.6 or Section 6.10 were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that the non-breaching Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.
