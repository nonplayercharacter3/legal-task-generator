# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ostrander Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Renata Solvig**, an individual ("**Employee**"). The Company and Employee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Miriv Provisioning Group, LLC ("**Seller**") and Breidoun Aviation Holdings, Inc. ("**Buyer**") are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company, with the closing to take place on July 1, 2026 (the "**Closing**").

B. Employee serves as Executive Chef and General Manager of the Company. In that role she has had access to the Company's recipes, menu specifications, customer relationships and other confidential information, and she will continue to do so after the Closing.

C. Employee will benefit directly from the transactions under the Purchase Agreement. The Company and Buyer would not proceed with the Closing without Employee's agreement to the covenants in this Agreement. This Agreement is the Key Employee Agreement referred to in Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of Employee's continued employment, the compensation and benefits described below, and the other mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Business**" means the preparation of hot inflight meals for regional air carriers.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates or their businesses, in any form, including the Recipes and Specifications, customer and pricing information, carrier contract terms, supplier terms, cost data, galley loading plans, food safety and sanitation procedures, business plans and personnel information. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach by Employee.

"**Customer**" means any regional air carrier or other Person to which the Company provided products or services at any time during the 24 months before the Termination Date.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Recipes and Specifications**" means all recipes, menu specifications, plating guides, portion standards, galley loading plans and related documentation used by the Company.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of Employee's employment with the Company and the two years following the Termination Date.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which Employee's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

"**Territory**" means the United States.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; POSITION AND DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** The Company shall continue to employ Employee as Executive Chef and General Manager, beginning on the Effective Date. Employee shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company or another officer designated by the Company's Board of Directors.

**2.2 Duties.** Employee shall oversee all culinary, commissary and operational functions of the Company. These include menu development, food production, food safety compliance, staffing of the commissary facilities and management of carrier customer relationships. Employee shall perform such other duties consistent with her position as the Company may reasonably assign.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** Employee shall devote her full business time and attention to the Company. She may manage personal investments and take part in professional, civic and charitable activities if those activities do not interfere with her duties or breach Section 6 or Section 7.

**2.4 Location.** Employee's principal place of work is the Company's primary commissary facility. Employee shall travel to the Company's other facilities and to customer locations as her duties reasonably require.

## 3. TERM

Employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under Section 5. Employment is at will, subject to the notice and severance provisions of Section 5. This Agreement takes effect only upon the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement terminates before the Closing, this Agreement is void and of no effect.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary of $215,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board of Directors shall review the base salary annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 30% of base salary. The bonus will be based on Company and individual objectives that the Board sets after consulting Employee. Any bonus for a year is payable by March 15 of the following year, if Employee remains employed on the payment date, except as provided in Section 5.4.

**4.3 Retention Payment.** The Company shall pay Employee a retention payment of $90,000, in two equal installments. The first installment is payable on the first regular payroll date after the six-month anniversary of the Effective Date. The second is payable on the first regular payroll date after the first anniversary of the Effective Date. Each installment requires that Employee remain employed on the applicable anniversary or that her employment has been terminated earlier by the Company without Cause or by Employee for Good Reason.

**4.4 Benefits.** Employee may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company makes available to its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.5 Paid Time Off.** Employee is entitled to 20 days of paid time off each calendar year, in addition to Company holidays, under the Company's policies.

**4.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

## 5. TERMINATION

**5.1 Termination Generally.** Either Party may terminate Employee's employment at any time. Employee shall give at least 60 days' written notice of resignation without Good Reason. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period, in which case it shall pay Employee base salary for the waived portion.

**5.2 Cause.** "Cause" means Employee's (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony; (b) fraud, embezzlement or theft against the Company; (c) willful and material violation of food safety Laws or Company food safety policies that is not cured within 10 days after written notice, if curable; (d) material breach of Section 6 or Section 7; or (e) willful failure to perform her material duties that continues for 15 days after written notice.

**5.3 Good Reason.** "Good Reason" means, without Employee's consent, (a) a material reduction in her base salary or target bonus; (b) a material reduction in her duties, authority or responsibilities; or (c) a relocation of her principal place of work by more than 50 miles. Good Reason exists only if Employee gives written notice within 60 days after the condition first occurs, the Company fails to cure within 30 days after that notice, and Employee resigns within 30 days after the cure period ends.

**5.4 Severance.** If the Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall (a) continue Employee's base salary for 12 months after the Termination Date; (b) pay a pro-rated portion of the annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance; and (c) pay the employer portion of COBRA premiums for up to 12 months. These payments require that Employee sign, and not revoke, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within 60 days after the Termination Date, and that she comply with Sections 6 and 7.

**5.5 Accrued Amounts.** On any termination, the Company shall pay Employee her earned but unpaid base salary, accrued but unused paid time off as required by Company policy or applicable law, and unreimbursed business expenses.

**5.6 Return of Property.** On the Termination Date, or earlier at the Company's request, Employee shall return to the Company all Company property and all documents and materials containing Confidential Information, including all copies of the Recipes and Specifications in any form.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY; INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**6.1 Confidentiality.** During her employment and at all times afterward, Employee shall keep all Confidential Information in confidence. She shall not disclose it to any Person or use it for any purpose other than performing her duties for the Company. Employee may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, if she gives the Company prompt notice (where legally permitted) so that it may seek a protective order.

**6.2 Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Employee from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency or from making other disclosures protected by whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Employee will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

**6.3 Work Product.** All recipes, menus, specifications, preparation methods, plating designs, written materials and other work product that Employee creates, alone or with others, in the course of her employment or using Company resources ("**Work Product**") belong solely to the Company. To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, Employee assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in it.

**6.4 Prior Work.** Employee confirms that all recipes, menu specifications and other materials she developed for the Company before the Effective Date are owned by the Company. To the extent she holds any interest in them, she assigns that interest to the Company.

**6.5 Further Assurances.** At the Company's request, during and after her employment, Employee shall sign any documents reasonably necessary to confirm or record the Company's ownership of the Work Product. The Company shall reimburse her reasonable out-of-pocket costs of doing so.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the Territory, own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, consult for or otherwise provide services to any Person engaged in the Business, in any capacity in which she would perform services similar to those she performed for the Company or would be reasonably likely to use or disclose Confidential Information. Employee may own, as a passive investment, up to 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person.

**7.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) solicit or induce any Customer to purchase from any Person other than the Company any products or services of the Business, or (b) induce any Customer to end or reduce its relationship with the Company.

**7.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire any person who is, or was during the 12 months before the solicitation, an employee of the Company, or induce any such person to leave the Company. General advertisements not directed at Company employees do not breach this Section 7.3, but any hire resulting from them during the Restricted Period does.

**7.4 Non-Disparagement.** During her employment and for two years after the Termination Date, Employee shall not make any statement to a Customer, supplier or the public that disparages the Company, Buyer or their products or services. This Section 7.4 does not restrict truthful testimony or any disclosure protected under Section 6.2.

**7.5 Reasonableness.** Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 7 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and subject matter. She acknowledges that they are necessary to protect the goodwill acquired by Buyer under the Purchase Agreement and the Company's Confidential Information, and that she has received adequate consideration for them, including her proceeds from and benefits under the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement and the retention payment in Section 4.3.

**7.6 Tolling.** If Employee breaches Section 7.1, 7.2 or 7.3, the Restricted Period is extended by the period of the breach.

**7.7 Notice to New Employers.** During the Restricted Period, Employee shall give a copy of this Section 7 to any prospective employer or client before accepting employment or engagement with it. The Company may also notify any such Person of Employee's obligations under this Agreement.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Equitable Relief.** Employee acknowledges that a breach of Section 6 or Section 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is entitled to temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**8.2 Forfeiture.** If Employee materially breaches Section 6 or Section 7, the Company may stop any unpaid severance under Section 5.4, without affecting the release she has given.

**8.3 Reformation.** If a court finds any restriction in Section 7 unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

## 9. EMPLOYEE REPRESENTATIONS

Employee represents that she is not bound by any agreement that would prevent her from performing her duties under this Agreement, and that she will not use or disclose to the Company any confidential information belonging to any former employer.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW; FORUM

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**10.2 Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its principal office, attention: Chief Executive Officer, with a copy to Buyer. Notices to Employee go to her most recent address in the Company's records.

**11.2 Assignment.** Employee may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to Buyer, to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business. Any such assignee may enforce Sections 6, 7 and 8.

**11.3 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of Sections 6, 7 and 8 and may enforce them directly.

**11.4 Section 409A.** The Parties intend this Agreement to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and it shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

**11.5 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Employee and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against which it is asserted.

**11.7 Severability.** Subject to Section 8.3, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Survival.** Sections 5.4 through 5.6 and Sections 6 through 11 survive the termination of Employee's employment.

**11.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
