# LINEN AND GALLEY-CART WASHING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This LINEN AND GALLEY-CART WASHING SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 6, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ostrander Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Customer**"), and **Pellacourt Linen Services LLC**, a New York limited liability company (the "**Vendor**"). The Customer and the Vendor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Customer operates commissary facilities that prepare and supply inflight meals, galley provisioning and crew meals to regional air carriers.

B. The Vendor provides commercial laundering of linens and washing and sanitizing of galley carts, trolleys, trays and related equipment.

C. The Customer wishes to engage the Vendor to provide those services at and for the Customer's commissary facilities, and the Vendor wishes to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement:

"**Cart Equipment**" means galley carts, trolleys, standard units, drawers, ovens inserts, trays, casseroles and other reusable galley equipment delivered by the Customer to the Vendor for washing.

"**Facilities**" means the Customer's commissary facilities listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Customer by written notice from time to time.

"**Linens**" means tablecloths, napkins, tray mats, aprons, chef coats, towels and other textile items delivered by the Customer to the Vendor for laundering.

"**Services**" means the services described in Section 2 and Exhibit B.

"**Service Standards**" means the cleanliness, turnaround and handling standards set forth in Exhibit B.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Linen Services.** The Vendor shall collect soiled Linens from each Facility, launder, dry, press and fold them, and return them to the Facility in sealed, food-grade packaging.

**2.2 Cart Washing Services.** The Vendor shall collect Cart Equipment from each Facility, or receive it at the Vendor's washing plant, and shall wash, sanitize, dry and inspect it in accordance with the Service Standards before returning it to the Facility.

**2.3 Schedule.** The Vendor shall collect and deliver on the schedule set forth in Exhibit B. The Customer may request additional collections on not less than 24 hours' notice, and the Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet those requests.

**2.4 Damaged Items.** The Vendor shall set aside and report to the Customer any Linens or Cart Equipment that are torn, stained beyond recovery, broken or otherwise unfit for service. The Vendor shall not discard any Cart Equipment without the Customer's written approval.

**2.5 Personnel.** The Vendor shall provide all personnel, vehicles, chemicals and equipment needed to perform the Services. Vendor personnel entering a Facility or any airport area shall comply with the Customer's site rules and with all applicable airport security and badging requirements.

## 3. STANDARDS AND COMPLIANCE

**3.1 Sanitation.** The Vendor shall perform the Services in compliance with all applicable federal, state and local laws, including food-contact sanitation requirements of the United States Food and Drug Administration and applicable state and local health departments.

**3.2 Chemicals.** The Vendor shall use only detergents and sanitizers approved for food-contact surfaces and shall rinse all Cart Equipment so that no residue remains. On request, the Vendor shall provide safety data sheets for all chemicals used.

**3.3 Records; Inspection.** The Vendor shall keep records of wash cycles, water temperatures and sanitizer concentrations for at least two years. The Customer, its air carrier customers and any governmental authority may inspect the Vendor's plant and records on reasonable notice during normal business hours.

**3.4 Nonconforming Services.** If any Linens or Cart Equipment returned to the Customer do not meet the Service Standards, the Vendor shall, at the Customer's option, promptly rewash them at no charge or credit the applicable charges.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** The Customer shall pay the Vendor the fees set forth in Exhibit C.

**4.2 Price Adjustments.** The Vendor may increase the fees once in each contract year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, by giving at least 60 days' prior written notice. No increase may exceed the lesser of 4% or the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the New York–Newark–Jersey City area over the preceding 12 months.

**4.3 Invoices.** The Vendor shall invoice the Customer monthly in arrears, with itemized counts by Facility. The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**4.4 Disputed Amounts.** The Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying the Vendor in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

**4.5 Taxes.** The fees exclude applicable sales and use taxes, which the Customer shall pay as invoiced. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 5. LOSS AND DAMAGE

**5.1 Risk of Loss.** The Vendor bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, Linens and Cart Equipment from the time it collects them until it returns them to the Facility, other than ordinary wear and tear.

**5.2 Replacement.** The Vendor shall reimburse the Customer for the replacement cost of any Linens or Cart Equipment lost or damaged while in the Vendor's custody, as reflected in the replacement schedule in Exhibit C.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

**6.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three years. It then renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

**6.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing the breach.

**6.3 Termination for Repeated Service Failures.** The Customer may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if the Services fail to meet the Service Standards on three or more occasions in any period of 90 days, each failure having been notified to the Vendor in writing.

**6.4 Termination for Convenience.** After the first anniversary of the Effective Date, either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on 120 days' written notice.

**6.5 Effect of Termination.** On termination, the Vendor shall return all Linens and Cart Equipment in its possession within five business days, and the Customer shall pay all undisputed fees for Services performed through the termination date.

## 7. INSURANCE

**7.1 Coverage.** During the term, the Vendor shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations, of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000; and (d) bailee's customer or property coverage sufficient to cover Linens and Cart Equipment in its custody.

**7.2 Additional Insured; Certificates.** The general liability and automobile policies shall name the Customer as an additional insured. The Vendor shall deliver certificates of insurance on the Effective Date and on each renewal, and shall give the Customer 30 days' prior notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnification by Vendor.** The Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Customer and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of the Vendor or its personnel, (b) any breach of Section 3, or (c) any bodily injury, illness or property damage caused by the Services.

**8.2 Indemnification by Customer.** The Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Vendor and its officers, members and employees from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of the negligence or willful misconduct of the Customer or its personnel at any Facility.

**8.3 Procedures.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, and cooperate reasonably. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on the indemnified Party without its written consent.

**8.4 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except for obligations under Section 8.1 or Section 8.2 for third-party claims, and breaches of Section 9, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party received in connection with this Agreement, including the Customer's customer identities, flight schedules, volumes and pricing, and shall use it only to perform or receive the Services. This obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, or that must be disclosed by law, in which case the receiving Party shall give prompt notice where legally permitted. This Section 9 survives for three years after termination.

## 10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

The Vendor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment or agency relationship. The Vendor is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, taxes and supervision of its personnel.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses on the signature page or another address a Party designates by notice.

**11.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, except that the Customer may assign it without consent to any affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or the Facilities.

**11.3 Subcontracting.** The Vendor shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent. The Vendor remains responsible for any subcontractor's performance.

**11.4 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, utility failures, epidemics, labor actions not involving its own employees, and acts of government, if it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents the Vendor from performing for more than 10 days, the Customer may obtain substitute services during that period without liability to the Vendor.

**11.5 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against which it is asserted.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A — FACILITIES

1. Primary commissary facility.
2. Each additional commissary facility designated by the Customer by written notice under Section 1.

## EXHIBIT B — SERVICE SCHEDULE AND SERVICE STANDARDS

1. **Collection and Delivery.** Linens: collection and delivery six days per week. Cart Equipment: collection twice daily, with return within 12 hours of collection.
2. **Linen Standards.** Linens returned free of visible soil, stains and odors; pressed and folded to the Customer's specification; packaged in sealed, food-grade wrap labeled by item and count.
3. **Cart Washing Standards.** Wash water at not less than 160°F, or chemical sanitization at manufacturer-specified concentration; final rinse with potable water; Cart Equipment returned fully dry, free of residue, food particles and labels from prior service; latches, wheels and brakes inspected and defects reported.
4. **Count Reconciliation.** Signed collection and delivery tickets at each Facility, reconciled monthly with the invoice.

## EXHIBIT C — FEES AND REPLACEMENT SCHEDULE

1. **Linen Services.** Per-pound rate for flatwork; per-piece rate for garments, as set forth in the Vendor's rate card agreed by the Parties on the Effective Date.
2. **Cart Washing Services.** Per-unit rate for full carts, half carts, standard units and trays, as set forth in that rate card.
3. **Additional Collections.** Flat fee per additional collection requested under Section 2.3.
4. **Replacement Schedule.** Replacement cost of Linens and Cart Equipment as listed in the Customer's current equipment cost schedule delivered to the Vendor, as updated annually.
