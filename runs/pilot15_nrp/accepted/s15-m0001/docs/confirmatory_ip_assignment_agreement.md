# CONFIRMATORY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This CONFIRMATORY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 25, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Miriv Provisioning Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**" or "**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Skyline Catering, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Assignee**" or the "**Company**"). **Breidoun Aviation Holdings, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Buyer**"), joins this Agreement for the purposes set forth in Section 9. Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer purchased from Seller all of the issued and outstanding Shares of the Company. The Closing took place on July 1, 2026.

B. Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller, at Buyer's request and without further consideration, to execute and deliver to the Company a confirmatory assignment of any right, title or interest that Seller or any of its Affiliates holds or may hold in any Company Intellectual Property. Buyer has made that request. This Agreement is the IP Assignment Agreement delivered under Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered the Closing Statement to Buyer on August 3, 2026.

D. Buyer delivered a Notice of Disagreement to Seller on September 18, 2026. Seller has notified Buyer that it rejects the Notice of Disagreement as untimely under Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Buyer expressly reserves its position with respect to the Notice of Disagreement and the Closing Statement.

E. Assignor wishes to confirm and, to the extent not previously effected, to assign to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Business," "Cap," "Closing Statement," "Company Intellectual Property," "Intellectual Property," "Losses," "Notice of Disagreement," "Ostrander Marks," "Person," "Purchase Price," "Recipes and Specifications" and "Shares."

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Assigned IP**" means all right, title and interest that Assignor or any of its Affiliates holds or may hold, anywhere in the world, in and to (a) the Recipes and Specifications, (b) the Ostrander Marks, together with the goodwill of the Business associated with them, (c) the registrations, applications and domain names listed on Schedule A, and (d) all other Company Intellectual Property, in each case whether arising before, on or after the Closing Date.

"**Associated Rights**" means all rights to sue for and collect damages, profits and other remedies for any past, present or future infringement, misappropriation, dilution or other violation of the Assigned IP; all rights of priority and protection of interests in the Assigned IP under the laws of any jurisdiction; and all income, royalties and payments now or later due in respect of the Assigned IP.

"**Assignor Group**" means Assignor and each of its Affiliates.

## 2. CONFIRMATION AND ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Confirmation.** Assignor confirms that, as of the Closing, the Company was and is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP, and that no member of the Assignor Group retains any right, title or interest in any Assigned IP.

**2.2 Assignment.** To the extent any member of the Assignor Group holds any right, title or interest in the Assigned IP, Assignor, on behalf of itself and each other member of the Assignor Group, irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all such right, title and interest, together with all Associated Rights, free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests and other encumbrances. This assignment is effective as of the Closing Date, or, if later, as of the date on which the relevant member of the Assignor Group acquired the relevant interest.

**2.3 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Ostrander Marks under Section 2.2 includes the goodwill of the Business symbolized by the Ostrander Marks, and Assignor shall not take any action that would separate that goodwill from the Ostrander Marks.

**2.4 No Further Consideration.** The Parties acknowledge that the consideration for this Agreement was paid under the Purchase Agreement and that no additional amount is payable by Assignee or Buyer for the Assigned IP.

## 3. TRADE SECRETS; DELIVERY OF MATERIALS

**3.1 Delivery.** Within 10 Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all tangible and electronic embodiments of the Recipes and Specifications in the possession or control of any member of the Assignor Group. These include recipe cards, menu specifications, plating guides, portion standards, galley loading plans, supplier specifications and nutrition and allergen data.

**3.2 No Retained Copies.** After that delivery, Assignor shall cause each member of the Assignor Group to destroy all remaining copies of the Recipes and Specifications. An officer of Assignor shall certify the destruction to Assignee in writing. Copies kept solely in automatic electronic backup systems that are not accessed in the ordinary course need not be destroyed, but they remain subject to Section 3.3.

**3.3 Confidentiality.** Assignor shall, and shall cause each member of the Assignor Group to, keep the Recipes and Specifications strictly confidential and not use them for any purpose, consistent with Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**4.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and without further consideration, Assignor shall, and shall cause each member of the Assignor Group to, execute and deliver all further instruments and take all further actions reasonably necessary to perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. This includes executing short-form assignments for filing with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, domain name registrars and any other registry.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, deliver and file, in the name of Assignor, any instrument described in Section 4.1 that Assignor fails to execute within 10 Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest.

## 5. RECORDATION

**5.1 Recordation.** Assignee may record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment substantially in the form of Schedule B, with any Governmental Authority or registry. Assignor consents to that recordation.

**5.2 Costs.** Assignee shall bear the filing fees for recordation. Assignor shall bear its own costs of performing under Sections 3 and 4.

## 6. NO LICENSE TO ASSIGNOR

No member of the Assignor Group has, or will be deemed to have, any license or other right to use any Assigned IP after the Effective Date, whether by implication, estoppel or otherwise.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee and Buyer as of the Effective Date that:

**7.1 Authority.** Assignor has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement on behalf of itself and each other member of the Assignor Group. This Agreement is the legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**7.2 No Prior Transfer.** No member of the Assignor Group has assigned, licensed, pledged or otherwise transferred or encumbered any interest in the Assigned IP to or in favor of any Person other than the Company.

**7.3 No Conflict.** The execution and performance of this Agreement do not conflict with any agreement or instrument binding on any member of the Assignor Group.

**7.4 Purchase Agreement Representations.** The representations and warranties in Section 4.12 of the Purchase Agreement were true and correct as of the date of the Purchase Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION; LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnification.** Assignor shall indemnify Assignee, Buyer and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees against all Losses arising out of (a) any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Assignor in this Agreement, or (b) any claim by any member of the Assignor Group, or by any Person claiming through any of them, to any right, title or interest in the Assigned IP.

**8.2 Procedures.** Claims under Section 8.1 shall be made and administered in accordance with the procedures set forth in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, which apply to this Agreement *mutatis mutandis*.

**8.3 Limitation of Liability.** Assignor's aggregate liability for Losses arising out of any breach of this Agreement or of the representations and warranties in Section 4.12 of the Purchase Agreement, whether claimed under this Agreement or under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, shall be subject to the Cap set forth in Section 9.5(a) of the Purchase Agreement, notwithstanding Section 9.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Amounts paid under this Agreement count toward the Cap, and the Cap is not increased by this Agreement. This Section 8.3 does not limit Assignor's liability for Fraud.

**8.4 Equitable Relief.** Assignor acknowledges that a breach of Section 2, 3 or 4 would cause Assignee irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. Assignee is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

## 9. BUYER JOINDER

**9.1 Joinder.** Buyer joins this Agreement to acknowledge that this Agreement is the confirmatory assignment requested by Buyer under Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement, to accept the benefit of Sections 7 and 8, and to agree to Sections 9, 10 and 11.

**9.2 Rights of Buyer.** Buyer may enforce Sections 3, 4, 7 and 8 directly against Assignor, whether or not Assignee also does so.

**9.3 No Assumption.** Buyer assumes no obligation of Assignee under this Agreement.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it or to the Assigned IP, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**10.2 Exclusive Forum.** Each of Assignor, Assignee and Buyer irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Assigned IP. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF ASSIGNOR, ASSIGNEE AND BUYER WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignee shall be sent in care of Buyer.

**11.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties, Buyer and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Assignee and Buyer may assign their rights under this Agreement to any Affiliate, to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Business, or to any lender as collateral. Assignor may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Assignee and Buyer.

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor, Assignee and Buyer. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Person against which it is asserted.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**11.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
