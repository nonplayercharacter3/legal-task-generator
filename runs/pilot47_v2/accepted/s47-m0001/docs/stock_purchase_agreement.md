# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 17, 2026, by and between **Prudond Media Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in the theatrical, home entertainment and digital distribution of independent motion pictures.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Bill of Sale, the Transition Services Agreement and each other agreement, certificate or instrument delivered under this Agreement.

"**Basket**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.5(b).

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale and contribution agreement between Seller and the Company described in Section 6.8.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Cap**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.5(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement to be entered into at Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $3,450,000.

"**Escrow Funds**" has the meaning given in the Escrow Agreement.

"**Film Library**" means all released and unreleased motion picture titles that Seller or any of its Affiliates distribute, including the 2026 Release Slate. The term includes all copyrights, distribution rights, physical and digital elements, artwork, trailers and other advertising materials, and all rights to receive revenue from any of those titles.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under the Laws of the State of New York with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV or Article V.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.3 (Capitalization), Section 4.11 (Taxes) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**General Representations**" means all representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV other than the Fundamental Representations.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any court, agency or other body exercising governmental or regulatory authority.

"**Initial Release Date**" has the meaning given in the Escrow Agreement.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

"**Lien**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim or other encumbrance.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event or condition that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, taken as a whole.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Seller Guarantees**" means each guarantee, letter of credit, keep-well, surety bond or other credit support that Seller or any of its Affiliates has given for any obligation of the Company, whether arising before or after the Closing. The term includes Seller's guarantees of the Company's minimum guarantee payment obligations to producers and licensors of motion pictures.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, withholding, franchise, payroll, property and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the transition services agreement to be entered into at Closing between Seller, as provider, and the Company, as recipient.

"**2026 Release Slate**" means the motion pictures scheduled by Seller or the Company for initial theatrical release in calendar year 2026, as listed in Section 1.1 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $46,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the following by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount, to the account Seller designates in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority is treated as paid to the Person entitled to it.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on March 31, 2026, or on any other date the parties agree in writing (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**"). This date is subject to satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII. The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(c) a copy of the executed Bill of Sale;

(d) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of each director of the Company;

(e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(f) the consent described in Section 7.2(d).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the payments described in Section 2.2;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly executed by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding. All of those shares are the Shares and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Subsidiaries.** The Company has no subsidiaries and holds no equity interest in any other Person.

**4.5 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the certificate of incorporation or bylaws of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) result in a breach of or default under any material contract to which the Company is a party.

**4.6 Consents.** No consent, approval or waiver of any Person is required for the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement or the consummation of the transactions it contemplates, other than the consent of Kestrelwave Streaming, Inc. under its output agreement with the Company.

**4.7 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the year then ended. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates.

**4.8 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course, and no Material Adverse Effect has occurred.

**4.9 Material Contracts.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each of the following to which the Company is a party: (a) each output, license or distribution agreement involving annual payments over $250,000; (b) each producer agreement involving a minimum guarantee; (c) each exhibitor master agreement; and (d) each employment agreement with an officer of the Company. Each listed contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any other party is in material breach of any of them.

**4.10 Film Library.** Upon completion of the contribution under Section 6.8, the Company will own or hold valid and enforceable distribution rights in each title in the Film Library, free and clear of all Liens other than the rights of producers and licensors under the contracts listed in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule. Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each title in the Film Library, the territory and media of the Company's rights, and the term of those rights.

**4.11 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns it was required to file, and all those returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. The Company has paid all Taxes due and owing. No Tax audit or proceeding is pending or threatened against the Company.

**4.12 Participations and Residuals.** The Company has accurately computed, accounted for and paid, when due, all participations, royalties, overages and residuals payable to producers, talent, guilds and other participants in the Film Library. Producer participation statements are rendered within 90 days after each June 30 and December 31. Each statement rendered since January 1, 2023 was accurate in all material respects. No participant has delivered written notice of audit or dispute that remains unresolved. All amounts receivable from exhibitors and sub-distributors shown on the Company's books are bona fide and arise from settlements in the ordinary course.

**4.13 Litigation.** No action, suit or proceeding is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Film Library.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from the Company or Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

**4.15 Compliance with Laws.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient funds available to pay the Purchase Price at Closing.

**5.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to their distribution in violation of any securities Law.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall cause the Company not to (a) issue or sell any equity interest, (b) incur indebtedness over $500,000, (c) enter into, amend or terminate any contract listed in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule, or (d) acquire or dispose of any title in the Film Library.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Company's properties, books, records, contracts and personnel.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VII. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consent described in Section 4.6 before the Closing.

**6.4 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the earlier of the Closing and termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or any material portion of its assets to any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information about the Company and the Film Library, except as required by Law.

**6.7 Seller Guarantees.** After the Closing, Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller and its Affiliates to be released from the Seller Guarantees. Until released, Seller shall keep each Seller Guarantee in effect according to its terms.

**6.8 Contribution of Film Library.** Before the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, contribute, assign and transfer to the Company all of their right, title and interest in and to the Film Library by the Bill of Sale. The Bill of Sale shall be executed and delivered no later than the Business Day immediately preceding the Closing Date.

**6.9 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and the Company shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement. Under it, Seller will provide theatrical booking, participation accounting and digital cinema delivery services to the Company for nine months after the Closing Date.

**6.10 Tax Matters.** Seller shall prepare and timely file all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date. Seller shall pay all Taxes of the Company attributable to periods, or portions of periods, ending on or before the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction of the following conditions: (a) no Law or order of any Governmental Authority prohibits the Closing; and (b) no action by any Governmental Authority seeking to enjoin the Closing is pending.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) The Fundamental Representations are true and correct in all respects. All other representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date.

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before the Closing, including the contribution under Section 6.8.

(c) Buyer has received a certificate signed by an officer of Seller confirming the matters in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b).

(d) Seller has obtained and delivered to Buyer the written consent of Kestrelwave Streaming, Inc. under its output agreement with the Company.

(e) No Material Adverse Effect has occurred.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) Buyer's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date.

(b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before the Closing.

(c) Seller has received a certificate signed by an officer of Buyer confirming the matters in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b).

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by April 30, 2026, unless the failure results from the terminating party's breach;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer breaches any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 15 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it becomes void and no party has further liability, except for Section 6.6, this Section 8.2 and Article XI, and except for liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations shall survive the Closing until the Initial Release Date. The Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. A claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** Subject to this Article IX, after the Closing Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to periods, or portions of periods, ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** Subject to this Article IX, after the Closing Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnified Parties**") from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) any Seller Guarantee, including any payment made or obligation incurred by Seller or any of its Affiliates under any Seller Guarantee.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall promptly give written notice to the party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). The notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. For any claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party. It may not settle any claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,600,000) (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Basket.* Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $230,000 (the "**Basket**"). After that, Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket.

(c) *Buyer Limitations.* The aggregate amount of Losses for which Buyer is liable under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Cap.

(d) *Carve-Outs.* The Cap and the Basket shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud, breaches of Fundamental Representations, or Taxes.

(e) *Seller Guarantees.* The Cap, the Basket and the other limitations in this Section 9.5 shall not apply to claims under Section 9.3(c).

(f) *Mitigation; Insurance.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its Losses. The amount of any Loss is reduced by any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party with respect to it.

**9.6 Escrow.** Any amount owed to a Buyer Indemnified Party under this Article IX shall first be satisfied from the Escrow Funds in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the parties' exclusive remedy for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement. This Section 9.7 does not limit (a) claims for Fraud, (b) claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.10, or (c) remedies under any Ancillary Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** The parties shall treat any indemnification payment under this Article IX as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEES

**10.1 Continuation of Employment.** After the Closing, the Company will continue to employ its employees under their existing terms of employment, subject to the Company's right to modify those terms in accordance with applicable Law and the applicable employment agreements.

**10.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article X confers any right or remedy on any employee of the Company or any other Person.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses a party designates in writing to the other.

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent. However, Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any Affiliate or, as collateral, to its lenders. Buyer remains liable for its obligations after any such assignment.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

**11.9 Ancillary Agreements.** If any provision of this Agreement conflicts with any provision of an Ancillary Agreement, the Ancillary Agreement governs with respect to its own subject matter.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy available to it, without proof of actual damages or posting of a bond.

**11.11 Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnified Parties and the Seller Indemnified Parties under Article IX, this Agreement confers no rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.
