**Prepared by:** Buyer's counsel
**Draft circulated:** March 24, 2026
**Scheduled Closing Date:** March 31, 2026
**Distribution:** Working group (Buyer, Seller, their respective counsel and Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.)

*Confidential. For use by the working group in connection with the Closing only.*

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This memorandum sets out the flow of funds at the Closing under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 17, 2026 (the "**SPA**"), between **Prudond Media Group, Inc.** ("**Seller**") and **Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC** ("**Buyer**"). In the SPA, Buyer purchases all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.** (the "**Company**").

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this memorandum have the meanings given in the SPA.

1.3 This memorandum is an operational tool to coordinate the Closing payments. It does not amend, supplement or interpret the SPA or any Ancillary Agreement. If any figure in this memorandum differs from the SPA or the Escrow Agreement, the parties shall correct this memorandum before any funds are released.

## 4. Uses of Funds

4.1 Under Section 2.2 of the SPA, Buyer will make the following payments at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

| No. | Payee | Amount | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent (Escrow Account) | $3,450,000 | SPA Section 2.2(a); Escrow Agreement Section 2.1 |
| 2 | Prudond Media Group, Inc. | $42,550,000 | SPA Section 2.2(b) |
| | **Total Uses** | **$46,000,000** | |

4.2 **Reconciliation.** The payment to Seller equals the Purchase Price of $46,000,000 less the Escrow Amount of $3,450,000, which is $42,550,000. Total Uses equal Total Sources.

4.3 **Withholding.** Seller will deliver a properly completed IRS Form W-9 at or before the Closing. On that basis, Buyer does not expect to withhold any amount under Section 2.3 of the SPA. If Buyer determines before the Closing that withholding is required, it will notify Seller at least two Business Days before the Closing Date, and the parties will revise this memorandum.

## 5. Payments Outside This Funds Flow

5.1 **Transaction expenses.** Under Section 11.1 of the SPA, each party pays its own costs and expenses. Neither party's advisor fees are paid out of the Purchase Price, and none appear in Section 4.

5.2 **Escrow Agent fees.** Under Section 9.2 of the Escrow Agreement, Buyer and Seller each pay one-half of the Escrow Agent's fees and expenses directly on invoice, as set out in Schedule B to the Escrow Agreement. No portion of those fees is deducted from the Escrow Amount at the Closing.

## 6. Wire Instructions

6.1 **Seller.** Seller will deliver its written wire instructions at least three Business Days before the Closing Date, or no later than March 26, 2026, as required by Section 2.2(b) of the SPA. As agreed on the March 10, 2026 deal call, Buyer's Vice President, Finance will confirm the instructions by telephone with a named Seller officer, using a telephone number Seller gave before the written instructions were received.

6.2 **Escrow Agent.** Ostrander Trust Company, N.A. will provide wire instructions for the Escrow Account directly to Buyer. Buyer will confirm them by telephone call-back to the Ostrander relationship manager.

6.3 **Changes.** Any change to wire instructions after their initial delivery must be made in writing and confirmed by telephone call-back before Buyer acts on it. No change will be accepted by email alone.

## 7. Closing Sequence

7.1 **Pre-funding conditions.** Buyer will not initiate any wire until all of the following have occurred:

(a) the Bill of Sale has been executed and delivered no later than March 30, 2026, as required by Section 6.8 of the SPA, and a copy has been delivered to Buyer under Section 3.2(c);

(b) Seller has delivered the written consent of Kestrelwave Streaming, Inc. under Section 7.2(d) of the SPA;

(c) all signature pages for the SPA closing deliveries, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement are held in escrow by counsel and are ready for release;

(d) each officer's certificate under Sections 7.2(c) and 7.3(c) of the SPA has been delivered in agreed form;

(e) the Escrow Agent has confirmed that it has received each party's know-your-customer materials, IRS Form W-9 and authorized representatives for Schedule A to the Escrow Agreement; and

(f) counsel for each party has confirmed by email that, apart from payment of the amounts in Section 4, all conditions to that party's obligations in Article VII of the SPA are satisfied or waived.

7.2 **Release and funding.** On the Closing Date:

1. Counsel will hold a closing call to confirm satisfaction of the items in Section 7.1.
2. Buyer will initiate Wire 1 (Escrow Agent) and Wire 2 (Seller) at the same time.
3. Buyer will email the Federal Reserve reference numbers for each wire to the working group.
4. The Escrow Agent will confirm in writing its receipt of the Escrow Amount, as required by Section 2.2 of the Escrow Agreement.
5. Seller will confirm in writing its receipt of the payment under Section 2.2(b) of the SPA.
6. When both receipts are confirmed, counsel will release all signature pages, and the Closing will be complete.

7.3 **Timing.** Buyer intends to initiate the wires by 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time on March 31, 2026 so that both are received the same day.

## 8. Post-Closing Items

8.1 Seller will deliver the stock certificates for the Shares, with stock powers duly executed in blank, and the director resignations under Sections 3.2(a) and 3.2(d) of the SPA. Electronic copies will be delivered at the Closing, and originals will follow by overnight courier.

8.2 The Escrow Funds will be held and disbursed only in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

8.3 Service Fees under the Transition Services Agreement will be invoiced monthly in arrears by Seller, as Provider, to the Company under Section 5.3 of that agreement. They are not part of this funds flow.
