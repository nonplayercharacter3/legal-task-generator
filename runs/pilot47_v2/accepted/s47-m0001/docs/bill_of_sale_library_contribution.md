# BILL OF SALE AND CONTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND CONTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is entered into as of March 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Prudond Media Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Seller (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 17, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller, before the Closing, to contribute, assign and transfer certain motion picture assets to the Company by this Bill of Sale.

C. Seller wishes to contribute those assets to the Company as a capital contribution, and the Company wishes to accept them, on the terms of this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller and the Company agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Contributed Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Contributed Contracts**" means the producer agreements, license agreements, sub-distribution agreements, laboratory access letters and other contracts under which Seller or any of its Affiliates holds rights in, or receives revenue from, any Film Library title, to the extent those contracts relate to a Film Library title.

"**Elements**" means all physical and digital materials relating to a title, including digital cinema packages, digital source masters, original negatives, interpositives, soundtracks, music and effects tracks, subtitle and dubbing files, closed-caption files, and all laboratory access rights to any of them.

"**Film Library**" means all motion picture titles, whether feature-length or short-form, that Seller or any of its Affiliates owns or holds distribution rights in and that were theatrically released on or before December 31, 2024, together with all prequels, sequels, remakes and derivative rights in those titles to the extent owned or controlled by Seller or its Affiliates.

"**Library Receivables**" means all accounts receivable, overages, minimum guarantee installments and other amounts payable to Seller or any of its Affiliates in respect of any Film Library title, whether earned before or after the Effective Date.

"**Marketing Materials**" means all key art, one-sheets, trailers, teasers, television and radio spots, digital and social media assets, electronic press kits, stills and other advertising and publicity materials relating to a title.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Bill of Sale, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Bill of Sale unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Contribution and Transfer.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Seller, for itself and on behalf of each of its Affiliates, contributes, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and the Company accepts, all of the right, title and interest of Seller and its Affiliates in and to the following (collectively, the "**Contributed Assets**"):

(a) the Film Library, including each title listed on Schedule A;

(b) all copyrights, copyright registrations and applications, and renewals and extensions of them, in each Film Library title and in the underlying literary, musical and dramatic material to the extent owned by Seller or its Affiliates;

(c) all theatrical, non-theatrical, home entertainment, television, digital and other distribution and exploitation rights in each Film Library title, in all territories and media in which Seller or its Affiliates hold those rights;

(d) all Elements and Marketing Materials relating to each Film Library title;

(e) the Contributed Contracts;

(f) the Library Receivables; and

(g) all claims, causes of action and rights of recovery against third parties relating to any of the foregoing, including claims for past infringement.

**2.2 Retained Assets.** Seller and its Affiliates retain all assets not included in the Contributed Assets. Nothing in this Bill of Sale transfers to the Company any asset, right or interest of Seller or its Affiliates other than the Contributed Assets.

**2.3 Capital Contribution.** The contribution under Section 2.1 is a contribution to the capital of the Company. The Company issues no shares or other consideration for it.

**2.4 Non-Assignable Contracts.** If any Contributed Contract cannot be assigned without the consent of a third party that has not been obtained by the Effective Date, this Bill of Sale does not constitute an assignment of that contract. Until the consent is obtained, Seller shall hold that contract for the benefit of the Company, pass through to the Company all benefits under it, and act at the Company's reasonable direction in enforcing it. The Company shall perform Seller's obligations under that contract to the extent arising after the Effective Date. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each required consent.

## 3. ASSUMPTION OF OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Assumed Obligations.** The Company assumes and agrees to perform the obligations of Seller and its Affiliates under the Contributed Contracts, to the extent those obligations first arise after the Effective Date and do not result from any breach by Seller or its Affiliates before the Effective Date.

**3.2 Excluded Liabilities.** The Company does not assume any liability of Seller or its Affiliates other than the obligations described in Section 3.1.

## 4. DELIVERY OF ELEMENTS

**4.1 Laboratory Access.** Within ten Business Days after the Effective Date, Seller shall deliver to each laboratory, vault and digital storage provider holding Elements of any Film Library title a written notice directing that provider to recognize the Company as the owner of those Elements and to grant the Company access to them.

**4.2 Digital Assets.** Within ten Business Days after the Effective Date, Seller shall transfer to the Company, or to storage accounts designated by the Company, all digital Elements and Marketing Materials for each Film Library title held on systems controlled by Seller or its Affiliates.

**4.3 Records.** Seller shall deliver to the Company copies of all chain-of-title documents, copyright registrations, participation and residual statements, and accounting records relating to each Film Library title.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**5.1 Further Instruments.** At the Company's request and without further consideration, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, execute and deliver any further instruments of conveyance, short-form copyright assignments, notices of assignment and other documents, and take any other action the Company reasonably requests, to vest in the Company title to the Contributed Assets and to record that transfer with the United States Copyright Office or any other registry.

**5.2 Receipts After the Effective Date.** If Seller or any of its Affiliates receives any payment in respect of a Contributed Asset after the Effective Date, it shall hold that payment in trust for the Company and remit it to the Company within ten Business Days after receipt.

**5.3 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute any document required under Section 5.1 that Seller fails to execute within ten Business Days after the Company's written request, and to endorse and collect any check or other instrument payable to Seller in respect of a Contributed Asset. This power is coupled with an interest.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS

**6.1 Seller Representations.** Seller represents to the Company that (a) Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to perform its obligations under it, (b) this Bill of Sale has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, and (c) Seller and its Affiliates have conveyed to the Company the Contributed Assets free and clear of all Liens, other than the rights of producers and licensors under the Contributed Contracts.

**6.2 Purchase Agreement.** The representations, warranties and indemnities given by Seller to Buyer with respect to the Film Library are set forth in the Purchase Agreement. This Bill of Sale does not expand or limit them.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits Seller, the Company and their respective successors and assigns. Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Bill of Sale and may enforce it after the Closing.

**7.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company and, after the date of the Purchase Agreement and before its termination, consented to in writing by Buyer. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**7.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**7.4 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**7.5 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Bill of Sale.

**7.6 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
