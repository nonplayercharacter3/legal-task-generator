# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PRUDOND MEDIA GROUP, INC.

**Dated as of February 16, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of **Prudond Media Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the bylaws of the Corporation. The resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called and held meeting of the Board.

## 1. BACKGROUND

**1.1 The Company.** The Corporation owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in the theatrical, home entertainment and digital distribution of independent motion pictures.

**1.2 The Proposed Transaction.** The Corporation has negotiated the sale of all of the Shares to **Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Buyer**"). The sale will be made under a Stock Purchase Agreement between the Corporation and the Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Board has received a substantially final draft of the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in that draft.

**1.3 Principal Terms.** The Board has reviewed the following principal terms of the Purchase Agreement, among others:

(a) the aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $46,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), payable at Closing under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) $3,450,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") will be deposited at Closing with **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, as escrow agent, under an escrow agreement among the Corporation, the Buyer and the escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**");

(c) the Closing is scheduled to take place on March 31, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII of the Purchase Agreement;

(d) before the Closing, the Corporation will contribute the Film Library to the Company by a bill of sale and contribution agreement between the Corporation and the Company, as required by Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Bill of Sale**");

(e) at the Closing, the Corporation and the Company will enter into a transition services agreement under which the Corporation will provide theatrical booking, participation accounting and digital cinema delivery services to the Company for 9 months after the Closing Date (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"); and

(f) the Purchase Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

**1.4 Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the drafts of the Purchase Agreement and the forms of the Escrow Agreement, the Bill of Sale and the Transition Services Agreement (together with the Purchase Agreement, the "**Transaction Documents**"). The Board has also received presentations from management and the Corporation's outside counsel on the terms of the Transaction Documents. The Board has had the opportunity to ask questions of management and counsel.

## 2. APPROVAL OF THE TRANSACTION

**2.1 Approval of the Purchase Agreement.** RESOLVED, that the Board determines that the sale of the Shares to the Buyer on the terms of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Transaction**") is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders; and further

RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, and the Corporation's performance of its obligations under it, are authorized and approved in all respects.

**2.2 Approval of the Ancillary Agreements.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of each of the Escrow Agreement, the Bill of Sale and the Transition Services Agreement, substantially in the forms presented to the Board, and the Corporation's performance of its obligations under each of them, are authorized and approved in all respects.

**2.3 Contribution of the Film Library.** RESOLVED, that the contribution by the Corporation to the capital of the Company of the assets described in the Bill of Sale, to be completed no later than the Business Day immediately preceding the Closing Date, is authorized and approved; and further

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers (as defined below) are authorized to cause any Affiliate of the Corporation that holds rights in any asset to be contributed under the Bill of Sale to join in or take any action needed to complete that contribution.

## 3. ACTIONS AS SOLE STOCKHOLDER OF THE COMPANY

**3.1 Stockholder Actions.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation, as the sole stockholder of the Company, is authorized to approve the Company's execution, delivery and performance of the Bill of Sale and the Transition Services Agreement, and to take any other action as stockholder of the Company that is necessary or appropriate to carry out the Transaction; and further

RESOLVED, that any Authorized Officer may execute a written consent of the sole stockholder of the Company for that purpose on behalf of the Corporation.

**3.2 Director Resignations.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to obtain from each director of the Company a written resignation effective as of the Closing, for delivery under Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. AUTHORIZED OFFICERS

**4.1 Designation.** RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver each Transaction Document to which the Corporation is a party, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation. Each Authorized Officer may approve any change to a Transaction Document that the Authorized Officer, with the advice of counsel, determines to be necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Corporation. The Authorized Officer's execution of the Transaction Document is conclusive evidence of that approval.

**4.2 Closing Deliveries.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, the stock certificates or stock powers for the Shares, the officer's certificate under Section 7.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, wire instructions for the payment under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement, and every other certificate, instrument and document required to be delivered by the Corporation at or before the Closing.

**4.3 Escrow Matters.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to (a) designate the authorized representatives of the Corporation for purposes of the Escrow Agreement, (b) deliver the Corporation's IRS Form W-9 and know-your-customer materials to the escrow agent, and (c) execute and deliver any Joint Written Instruction or other notice under the Escrow Agreement.

**4.4 Third-Party Consents.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to request and obtain, on behalf of the Corporation and the Company, the consent of Kestrelwave Streaming, Inc. under its output agreement with the Company, and any other consent, approval or notice that the Authorized Officers determine to be necessary or appropriate in connection with the Transaction.

## 5. GENERAL AUTHORITY

**5.1 Further Actions.** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to take any further action, execute and deliver any further document, and pay any fee or expense, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, that the Authorized Officer determines to be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions and to consummate the Transaction.

**5.2 Secretary's Certification.** RESOLVED, that the Secretary or any Assistant Secretary of the Corporation is authorized to certify these resolutions and the incumbency and signatures of the Authorized Officers to the Buyer, the escrow agent and any other Person.

**5.3 Prior Actions.** RESOLVED, that any action taken before the date of this consent by any officer or director of the Corporation in connection with the negotiation of the Transaction Documents, consistent with the purposes of these resolutions, is ratified, confirmed and approved.

## 6. EFFECTIVENESS

**6.1 Counterparts.** This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**6.2 Filing.** This consent is effective as of the date first written above when signed by all of the directors of the Corporation. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
