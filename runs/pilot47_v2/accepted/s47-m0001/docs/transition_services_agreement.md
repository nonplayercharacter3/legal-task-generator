# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 31, 2026, by and between **Prudond Media Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 17, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the Closing, Provider performed theatrical booking, participation accounting and digital cinema delivery functions for Recipient's business. Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that Provider will continue to perform those functions for a transitional period.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a party, or obtained by the other party in performing or receiving the Services. For Recipient, this includes exhibitor terms, film rental and settlement data, release calendars, booking strategies, participation statements and participant data, and the Film Library titles' delivery and Elements information.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and records relating to Recipient or its business that Provider creates, receives, processes or stores in performing the Services.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth on Schedule A.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.2.

"**Services**" means the services described in Section 3.1 and on Schedule A.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date. It continues until the date that is 9 months after the Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under Section 9 (the "**Term**").

**2.2 Service Periods.** Each Service is provided for the Term, unless Schedule A states a shorter period for that Service or Recipient terminates that Service early under Section 9.2.

**2.3 Extension.** Recipient may extend the period for any Service once, by up to 60 days, by written notice to Provider at least 30 days before the period would otherwise end. The Service Fees for any extension period are those on Schedule A plus 10%.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide to Recipient the following Services, as further described on Schedule A:

(a) *Theatrical Booking.* Booking of Recipient's motion pictures with exhibitors in the United States and Canada, including negotiation of playdates, screen counts and holdover terms within parameters set by Recipient; preparation of booking reports; and collection and settlement of film rentals with exhibitors.

(b) *Participation Accounting.* Preparation and rendering of participation, royalty and overage statements to producers, licensors, talent and other participants in the Film Library; calculation of residuals and related guild reporting; maintenance of participation ledgers; and support for participant audits.

(c) *Digital Cinema Delivery.* Mastering, encryption and delivery of digital cinema packages and key delivery messages to exhibitors; management of Recipient's accounts with satellite and hard-drive delivery vendors; and tracking of Elements held at laboratories and storage providers.

**3.2 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) with at least the same degree of care, skill, priority and timeliness with which Provider performed them for Recipient's business during the 12 months before the Closing Date, and (b) in compliance with applicable Law and the terms of Recipient's exhibitor, producer and vendor agreements made available to Provider (the "**Service Standard**").

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign to the Services qualified personnel with appropriate experience. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep assigned the personnel who performed the corresponding functions before the Closing. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel.

**3.4 Recipient Direction.** Recipient retains all decision-making authority over its business. Provider shall follow Recipient's reasonable written instructions in performing the Services, including instructions on release dates, booking parameters, settlement terms and participant communications. Provider shall not enter into any agreement in Recipient's name without Recipient's prior written approval.

**3.5 Service Managers.** Each party shall appoint a service manager to coordinate the Services and resolve operational issues. The service managers shall meet, by telephone or video conference, at least every two weeks during the Term.

**3.6 Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with information, access and assistance reasonably necessary for Provider to perform the Services. Provider is excused from any failure to perform a Service to the extent caused by Recipient's failure to do so, if Provider promptly notifies Recipient of that failure.

## 4. MIGRATION

**4.1 Migration Plan.** Within 30 days after the Closing Date, the service managers shall agree on a written plan for migrating each Service to Recipient or its designee by the end of the Term.

**4.2 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to carry out the migration plan, including knowledge transfer sessions with Provider personnel, delivery of process documentation, and cooperation with Recipient's replacement vendors. Migration assistance is included in the Service Fees.

**4.3 Delivery of Recipient Data.** At the end of the period for each Service, and at any other time on Recipient's request, Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Recipient Data relating to that Service in a commonly used electronic format reasonably requested by Recipient.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees for each Service. Except as provided in Section 2.3, the Service Fees are equal to Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the Services, without mark-up.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable out-of-pocket third-party costs incurred in performing the Services, including vendor delivery charges and laboratory fees, without mark-up, if those costs are listed on Schedule A or approved in advance by Recipient in writing.

**5.3 Invoices.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Fees and pass-through costs by Service. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**5.4 Disputes.** Recipient may dispute any invoiced amount in good faith by written notice within 30 days after receipt of the invoice. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Recipient shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

**5.5 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's income.

## 6. COLLECTIONS AND FUNDS

**6.1 Film Rentals and Receipts.** All film rentals, settlements and other amounts that Provider collects from exhibitors, sub-distributors or others on Recipient's behalf are Recipient's property. Provider shall hold those amounts in trust for Recipient in a segregated account and remit them to Recipient within five Business Days after receipt, together with a settlement report.

**6.2 No Set-Off.** Provider may not set off any amount it holds for Recipient against any amount Recipient owes Provider under this Agreement or otherwise.

**6.3 Participant Payments.** Provider shall pay participant amounts on Recipient's behalf only from funds Recipient provides for that purpose and only in accordance with participation statements that Recipient has approved.

## 7. DATA, RECORDS AND AUDIT

**7.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider may use Recipient Data only to perform the Services.

**7.2 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for Recipient Data at least as protective as those it maintains for its own comparable data. Provider shall notify Recipient within 48 hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Recipient Data.

**7.3 Records.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services, the Service Fees, all amounts collected under Section 6.1, and all participation calculations.

**7.4 Audit.** On 10 Business Days' written notice, Recipient or its independent accountants may audit Provider's records relating to the Services during normal business hours, no more than twice during the Term and once in the 12 months after it ends. If an audit reveals an overcharge, Provider shall promptly refund it.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Obligations.** Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its employees and advisors who need to know it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

**8.2 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) the receiving party lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality obligation, or (c) the receiving party independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information. A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law, after giving the disclosing party prompt notice where legally permitted.

**8.3 Survival.** This Section 8 survives for three years after the end of the Term.

## 9. TERMINATION

**9.1 Expiration.** This Agreement expires at the end of the Term.

**9.2 Termination of Individual Services.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on 30 days' written notice to Provider. The Service Fees for that Service are reduced accordingly from the effective date of termination.

**9.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice describing it. Provider may not terminate this Agreement or suspend any Service for non-payment of disputed amounts.

**9.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) Recipient shall pay all undisputed Service Fees accrued through the effective date, (b) Provider shall remit all amounts held under Section 6.1 and deliver all Recipient Data under Section 4.3, and (c) Sections 4.3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12 survive.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement, except for breaches of Section 8, Provider's obligations under Section 6, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**10.2 Cap.** Except for the matters excluded in Section 10.1, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Recipient and its officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 6, 7 or 8.

**11.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by Provider's performance of the Services in accordance with Recipient's written instructions, except to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.

**11.3 Procedures.** The procedures in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement apply to claims under this Section 11.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties, except the limited agency needed to collect amounts under Section 6.1.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses a party designates in writing to the other. A copy of each notice to Recipient shall also be sent to Buyer.

**12.3 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without Provider's prior written consent. Provider may assign this Agreement, or delegate the performance of any Service, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations and obligations under Section 6.1, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic or failure of public utilities or communications networks. The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**12.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**12.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**12.8 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**12.9 Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce Recipient's rights under it. Otherwise, this Agreement confers no rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
