# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 31, 2026, by and among **Semeith Pictures Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Prudond Media Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February 17, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, to be held and disbursed under this Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1.

"**Claimed Amount**" means the amount of Losses stated in a Claim Notice.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other income earned on that amount, less all disbursements made in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a certificate of counsel for the presenting party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Initial Release Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.2.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate Claimed Amount of all Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been paid, withdrawn or finally resolved under Section 7.3.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; ESCROW DEPOSIT

**2.1 Escrow Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $3,450,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds, in accordance with Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.2 Appointment; Escrow Account.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent and shall disburse them only as provided in this Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall promptly confirm to Buyer and Seller in writing its receipt of the Escrow Amount.

**2.3 No Interest in Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Funds are held in trust for the benefit of Buyer and Seller as their interests appear under this Agreement. They are not subject to any lien, attachment, set-off or other judicial process of any creditor of any party. No party may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer any interest in the Escrow Funds, except as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** Unless Buyer and Seller otherwise direct by Joint Written Instruction, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a U.S. government money market fund rated in the highest category by a nationally recognized rating agency, or in a demand deposit account at the Escrow Agent insured to the extent available by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make any disbursement under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3, or from any liquidation of an investment before maturity to make a required disbursement.

**3.3 Income.** All interest and other income earned on the Escrow Funds becomes part of the Escrow Funds and is disbursed with them.

## 4. TAX MATTERS

**4.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state income Tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed, and all income earned on the Escrow Funds is reported as income of Buyer.

**4.2 Tax Reporting.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 at or before the Closing. The Escrow Agent shall report income earned on the Escrow Funds on IRS Form 1099 or other applicable form as required by Law.

**4.3 Tax Distributions.** Within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter, at Buyer's written request, the Escrow Agent shall distribute to Buyer from the Escrow Funds an amount equal to 40% of the income earned on the Escrow Funds during that quarter, to cover Buyer's Taxes on that income.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Initial Release Date.** The "**Initial Release Date**" is the date that is 120 days following the Closing Date, or if that date is not a Business Day, the next Business Day.

**5.2 Release to Seller.** Within three Business Days after the Initial Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then held, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Initial Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the retained amount under this Agreement.

**5.3 Release of Retained Amounts.** When any Claim Notice pending on the Initial Release Date is finally resolved under Section 7.3, the Escrow Agent shall, within three Business Days after receipt of the applicable Joint Written Instruction or Final Order, (a) disburse to Buyer the amount payable to Buyer in respect of that claim, and (b) disburse to Seller the excess, if any, of the amount retained for that claim over the amount paid to Buyer.

**5.4 Termination of Escrow.** This Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed all Escrow Funds in accordance with its terms. Sections 8, 9 and 11 survive termination.

## 6. DISBURSEMENT MECHANICS

**6.1 Disbursement Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds only (a) in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction, (b) in accordance with a Final Order, or (c) as expressly provided in Sections 4.3, 5.2, 5.3 and 7.2.

**6.2 Payment Method.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the receiving party. Each party shall confirm its wire instructions to the Escrow Agent at or before the Closing.

**6.3 Security Procedures.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm any change to wire instructions, and any disbursement instruction over $50,000, by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule A who is not the person who delivered the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until the call-back is completed.

**6.4 Joint Instructions.** Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver to the Escrow Agent a Joint Written Instruction whenever any amount becomes payable from the Escrow Funds under this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. CLAIMS AGAINST ESCROW FUNDS

**7.1 Claim Notice.** At any time before the Initial Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnified Party is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the Claimed Amount, which may be a good-faith estimate.

**7.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a Claimed Amount by delivering to Buyer and the Escrow Agent a written notice (an "**Objection Notice**") within 20 Business Days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall state the basis of the objection in reasonable detail. If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within that period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Claimed Amount to Buyer on the fifth Business Day after the period expires. If Seller objects to only part of a Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the unobjected portion to Buyer on that date.

**7.3 Resolution of Disputed Claims.** Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days after delivery of an Objection Notice to resolve the disputed claim. The Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount only in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction reflecting the parties' resolution or a Final Order. Either party may seek resolution of a disputed claim in the courts specified in Section 11.6.

**7.4 Exclusive Source of Recovery.** The Escrow Funds shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery of the Buyer Indemnified Parties for all Losses under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, whether or not subject to the Cap. Payments to the Buyer Indemnified Parties under this Section 7 shall be made solely from the Escrow Funds, as contemplated by Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.5 Order of Payment.** If Claim Notices for more than one claim are pending, the Escrow Agent shall pay finally resolved claims in the order in which the Claim Notices were received.

## 8. THE ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller, and has no duty to determine whether any party has complied with it.

**8.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature it reasonably believes to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper party. It may consult with counsel and is fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of counsel.

**8.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**8.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each bears one-half of any amount paid under this Section 8.4, unless a court finds that one of them caused the loss.

**8.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or is uncertain as to its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the Escrow Funds with the clerk of a court specified in Section 11.6 and commence an interpleader action, after which it is released from all further obligations for the deposited funds.

**8.6 Compliance with Orders.** The Escrow Agent may comply with any order, judgment or process of any court affecting the Escrow Funds. It is not liable to any party for that compliance, even if the order is later reversed or vacated.

**8.7 Know Your Customer.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification Laws.

## 9. FEES AND EXPENSES

**9.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule B, plus reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement.

**9.2 Allocation.** Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the Escrow Agent's fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds unless a party fails to pay its share within 30 days after invoice, in which case the deducted amount is charged to that party's interest in the Escrow Funds.

## 10. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**10.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instruction delivered at least 30 days before the effective date of removal.

**10.2 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $250,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court specified in Section 11.6 to appoint one. The resignation or removal takes effect only when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent transfers the Escrow Funds to it.

**10.3 Merger.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which acquires all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on Schedule A or any other address a party designates by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**11.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as provided in Section 10.3. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.6 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.7 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, interruption of communications or computer systems, or failure of any funds transfer system.

**11.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

**11.9 Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement confers no rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.
