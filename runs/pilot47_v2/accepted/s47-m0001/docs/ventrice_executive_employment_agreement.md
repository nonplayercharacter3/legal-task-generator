# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Ashgrove Film Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Marisol Ventrice**, an individual (the "**Executive**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company is engaged in the theatrical, home entertainment and digital distribution of independent motion pictures.

B. The Company wishes to employ the Executive as its President, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Base Salary**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Board**" means the Board of Directors of the Company.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.3.

"**Change of Control**" has the meaning set forth in Section 10.1.

"**Executive Titles**" means the motion pictures listed on Exhibit A, as updated under Section 6.2. Each is a title that the Executive originated, sourced or brought to the Company and that the Company acquired or distributes through the Executive's efforts.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.4.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Release**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.3.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues until June 1, 2027, unless terminated earlier under Section 7 (the "**Term**").

**2.2 Renewal.** The Term renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current Term.

## 3. POSITION AND DUTIES

**3.1 Position.** The Company employs the Executive as its President. The Executive reports directly to the Board.

**3.2 Duties.** The Executive has the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for the president of an independent motion picture distribution company. These include overall management of acquisitions, theatrical release planning, exhibitor relations, marketing and ancillary distribution. The Executive also has any other duties consistent with her position that the Board reasonably assigns.

**3.3 Full Time.** The Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time and efforts to the Company. The Executive may (a) serve on civic, charitable and industry association boards, and (b) manage her personal investments, if those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or violate Section 9.

**3.4 Place of Performance.** The Executive's principal place of employment is the Company's offices in New York, New York, subject to customary business travel, including attendance at film festivals and markets.

## 4. COMPENSATION

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $425,000 (as adjusted, the "**Base Salary**"). The Base Salary is payable in accordance with the Company's normal payroll practices. The Board shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, the Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus. The target bonus is 50% of the Base Salary, based on the achievement of performance goals that the Board sets after consulting the Executive. Any bonus earned is paid no later than March 15 of the year after the year to which it relates. Except as provided in Section 8, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive the bonus.

**4.3 Acquisition Incentive.** For each motion picture that the Company acquires during the Term at the Executive's recommendation, the Executive is entitled to an incentive payment equal to 1% of the Company's net receipts from that title, as reported in the Company's participation statements. The payment is made within 30 days after each statement is rendered, for the life of the Company's distribution rights in the title.

**4.4 Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to applicable tax withholding and other deductions required by law.

## 5. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**5.1 Benefit Plans.** The Executive may participate in all employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior executives, subject to the terms of those plans.

**5.2 Vacation.** The Executive is entitled to four weeks of paid vacation each calendar year, in addition to Company holidays.

**5.3 Business Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for all reasonable business expenses she incurs in performing her duties, in accordance with the Company's expense policies. Those policies include business-class air travel for flights over four hours.

**5.4 Indemnification; D&O Insurance.** The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by its certificate of incorporation, bylaws and applicable law. During the Term, and for six years after it ends, the Company shall maintain directors' and officers' liability insurance covering the Executive on terms no less favorable than those provided to any other officer or director.

## 6. EXECUTIVE TITLES; CREDIT

**6.1 Credit.** For each Executive Title, and for each other title that the Company acquires during the Term at the Executive's recommendation, the Executive is entitled to an "Executive Producer" or "Presented by" credit in the form customary for the Company's releases. Any casual or inadvertent failure to give that credit is not a breach of this Agreement if the Company cures it prospectively after written notice.

**6.2 Updates to Exhibit A.** The Company and the Executive shall update Exhibit A by written agreement within 30 days after the end of each calendar year to add any title that has become an Executive Title.

**6.3 Ownership.** Subject to Section 10.2, the Company owns or controls all rights in the Executive Titles that it acquires under its agreements with producers and licensors. The Executive's services in connection with the Executive Titles are works made for hire for the Company to the extent permitted by law.

## 7. TERMINATION

**7.1 Death or Disability.** The Executive's employment ends automatically on her death. The Company may end the Executive's employment by written notice if she is unable to perform her essential duties, with or without reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period because of physical or mental illness.

**7.2 Termination by the Company.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment at any time with or without Cause by written notice approved by the Board.

**7.3 Cause.** "**Cause**" means the Executive's (a) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing her duties that materially harms the Company; (c) material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured 30 days after written notice; or (d) fraud or embezzlement against the Company.

**7.4 Good Reason.** "**Good Reason**" means any of the following without the Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in her authority, duties or responsibilities, or a change in her reporting relationship to the Board; (b) a reduction in her Base Salary or target bonus; (c) relocation of her principal place of employment by more than 35 miles; (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company; or (e) any event described in Section 10.2. For clauses (a) through (d), the Executive must give the Company written notice within 60 days after the event first occurs. The Company then has 30 days to cure. If the Company does not cure, the Executive must resign within 30 days after the cure period ends.

**7.5 Resignation Without Good Reason.** The Executive may resign without Good Reason on at least 60 days' written notice to the Board.

**7.6 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive (a) any earned but unpaid Base Salary, (b) any earned but unpaid bonus for a completed fiscal year, (c) accrued but unused vacation, (d) unreimbursed business expenses, and (e) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans (collectively, the "**Accrued Obligations**"). The Executive's rights under Section 4.3 survive any termination other than termination for Cause.

## 8. SEVERANCE

**8.1 Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall pay or provide the following in addition to the Accrued Obligations, subject to Section 8.3:

(a) continued payment of the Base Salary for 12 months after the termination date;

(b) a pro-rated target bonus for the year of termination; and

(c) payment of COBRA premiums for the Executive and her eligible dependents for 12 months after the termination date, or, if earlier, until she becomes eligible for comparable coverage from a new employer.

**8.2 Change of Control Severance.** If the Executive resigns for Good Reason under Section 10.2, Section 10.2 governs the amount of her cash severance in place of Section 8.1(a). Sections 8.1(b) and 8.1(c) continue to apply.

**8.3 Release.** The Company's obligations under Sections 8.1 and 10.2 (other than the Accrued Obligations) are conditioned on the Executive signing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company (the "**Release**"). The Release must become effective within 60 days after the termination date. The Release shall not require the Executive to release her rights under Sections 4.3, 5.4 or 10.2.

**8.4 No Mitigation.** The Executive has no duty to mitigate the payments under this Section 8 or Section 10.2. Those payments are not reduced by any compensation she earns from other employment.

## 9. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**9.1 Confidentiality.** During and after the Term, the Executive shall keep confidential all non-public information of the Company. This includes exhibitor terms, release calendars, acquisition pipelines, participation data and financial information. The Executive shall not use that information except in performing her duties.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for 12 months after it ends, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any employee of the Company to leave the Company's employment.

**9.3 Non-Interference.** During the Term and for 12 months after it ends, the Executive shall not knowingly induce any producer, licensor or exhibitor to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company. This Section 9.3 does not restrict the Executive's exploitation of any rights that revert to her under Section 10.2.

**9.4 Return of Property.** When her employment ends, the Executive shall return all Company property and documents in her possession, other than her personal contacts and compensation records.

**9.5 Remedies.** The Executive acknowledges that a breach of this Section 9 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company may seek injunctive relief to enforce this Section 9 in addition to any other remedy.

## 10. CHANGE OF CONTROL

**10.1 Definition.** "**Change of Control**" means any of the following:

(a) any sale, transfer or other disposition, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of more than 50% of the outstanding voting stock of the Company;

(b) any merger, consolidation or reorganization of the Company after which the holders of the Company's voting stock immediately before the transaction hold less than 50% of the voting power of the surviving entity; or

(c) any sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

**10.2 Consent; Effect of Change of Control.** The Company shall not undergo a Change of Control without the Executive's prior written consent. If a Change of Control occurs without that consent:

(a) the Change of Control constitutes Good Reason, and the Executive may resign for Good Reason by written notice to the Company at any time within 90 days after the Change of Control. The notice and cure provisions of Section 7.4 do not apply;

(b) on that resignation, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and the benefits under Sections 8.1(b) and 8.1(c), the Company shall pay the Executive severance equal to 24 months of her Base Salary, at the rate in effect immediately before the Change of Control. The severance is payable in a lump sum within 10 days after the Release becomes effective; and

(c) effective on the date of that resignation, all of the Company's right, title and interest in and to the Executive Titles reverts to the Executive. This includes the Company's distribution rights, its interest in Elements and marketing materials, and its rights to receive revenue from those titles after that date. The Company shall, within 30 days after the resignation, execute and deliver any assignments, laboratory access letters and other instruments the Executive reasonably requests to give effect to the reversion, and shall deliver all physical and digital materials for the Executive Titles in its possession or control.

**10.3 Excise Tax.** If any payment under this Agreement would be subject to the excise tax under Section 4999 of the Internal Revenue Code, the payments shall be reduced to the extent needed to avoid that tax, but only if the reduction results in a greater after-tax amount for the Executive.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Section 409A.** The parties intend that payments under this Agreement comply with, or are exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. Each payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. If the Executive is a "specified employee" when her employment ends, any payment of deferred compensation that would otherwise be made in the first six months after separation is instead paid on the first business day after that period.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to the attention of the Chair of the Board at the Company's principal office. Notices to the Executive go to her most recent home address in the Company's records.

**11.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive and she may not assign it. The Company may not assign this Agreement without the Executive's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business that assumes this Agreement in writing.

**11.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company other than the Executive, acting with Board approval. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**11.8 Legal Fees.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for her reasonable legal fees in negotiating this Agreement, up to $15,000.

**11.9 Survival.** Sections 4.3, 5.4, 7.6, 8, 9, 10.2 and 11 survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
