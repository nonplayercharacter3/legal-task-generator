# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of June 30, 2026, by and among **Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Pemberly Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are together the "**Parties**." Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent are together the "**Signatories**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer acquired the refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business of Seller.

B. The Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit a portion of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit secures certain indemnification obligations of Seller.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, the Signatories agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which the Escrow Agent's offices in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Closing Date**" means June 30, 2026, the date of the Closing under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Account**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" means the date that is ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Escrow Release Date is the next Business Day.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction. Any order or judgment for which the time to appeal has expired without an appeal being filed is treated as non-appealable.

"**Indemnity Claim**" means a claim by Buyer for indemnification for Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty under Section 9.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before that date that have not been paid or resolved under Section 4.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

2.1 **Deposit of Escrow Amount.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall acknowledge receipt of the Escrow Amount in writing to the Parties.

2.2 **Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Account**"). It shall disburse the Escrow Funds only in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

2.3 **No Other Use.** The Escrow Funds shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Signatory. They shall be held and disbursed only for the purposes stated in this Escrow Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

3.1 **Permitted Investment.** Unless the Parties direct otherwise by Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Pemberly Treasury Obligations Money Market Fund, Institutional Class, or a successor fund investing solely in obligations of the United States government.

3.2 **Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as required to make a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement. It shall have no liability for any loss resulting from an investment or liquidation made in accordance with this Section 3.

3.3 **Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall deliver to the Parties monthly statements of the Escrow Account showing all deposits, investments, earnings and disbursements.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUNDS

4.1 **Claim Notice.** At any time before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to the Escrow Agent and to Seller a written notice of an Indemnity Claim (a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice shall (a) describe the Indemnity Claim in reasonable detail, (b) state the amount of Losses claimed or, if not yet determinable, a good-faith estimate of them, and (c) certify that a copy has been delivered to Seller at the same time.

4.2 **Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of the amount claimed in a Claim Notice by delivering a written notice to the Escrow Agent and Buyer (an "**Objection Notice**") within twenty (20) days after Seller receives the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall state the amount objected to and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

4.3 **Payment of Uncontested Amounts.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the period in Section 4.2, it shall pay to Buyer, on the fifth (5th) Business Day after that period ends, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice. If the Escrow Agent receives an Objection Notice for only part of the amount claimed, it shall pay to Buyer within the same period the amount not objected to.

4.4 **Contested Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount subject to a timely Objection Notice. It shall disburse that amount only (a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, or (b) in accordance with a Final Order delivered to it by either Party, together with a certificate of that Party's counsel that the order is a Final Order.

4.5 **Withholding.** The Escrow Agent shall withhold Escrow Funds from release only against an Indemnity Claim set out in a Claim Notice delivered before the Escrow Release Date in accordance with Section 4.1.

4.6 **Estimated Amounts.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, Buyer may update the estimate by a further notice to the Escrow Agent and Seller before the Escrow Release Date. Section 4.2 applies to any increase in the amount claimed.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

5.1 **Release on Escrow Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Seller the Escrow Funds, minus the Pending Claim Amount as of the Escrow Release Date.

5.2 **Subsequent Releases.** After the Escrow Release Date, as each Indemnity Claim included in the Pending Claim Amount is resolved under Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall pay the amount determined to be owed to Buyer. Within five (5) Business Days after that resolution, it shall pay to Seller any part of the retained amount for that Indemnity Claim that is not payable to Buyer.

5.3 **Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

5.4 **Earnings.** Earnings on the Escrow Funds shall follow the principal to which they relate and shall be paid to the Party receiving that principal.

5.5 **Payment Mechanics.** All payments shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated by the Parties on Schedule 1, or to such other account as a Party designates by written notice to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall confirm any change in wire instructions by telephone call-back to a person listed on Schedule 2 before acting on it.

## 6. TERMINATION

This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 7 and 8 survive termination and any resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 7. FEES AND EXPENSES

7.1 **Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set out on Schedule 3. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

7.2 **Reimbursement Between Parties.** If either Party pays more than its share under Section 7.1, the other Party shall reimburse it on demand.

## 8. THE ESCROW AGENT

8.1 **Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction, certificate or other document it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper Person. It need not inquire into the truth or accuracy of any statement in any such document.

8.2 **Duties Limited.** The duties and obligations of the Escrow Agent are determined solely by this Escrow Agreement. They are purely ministerial in nature. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and has no duty to review or interpret, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between the Parties. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent.

8.3 **Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

8.4 **Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, damages and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, unless the amount arises from the breach of this Escrow Agreement by one Party, in which case that Party shall bear it.

8.5 **Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands concerning the Escrow Funds that this Escrow Agreement does not resolve, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an interpleader action in a court described in Section 10.2 and deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with that court.

8.6 **Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

8.7 **Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the Parties. The Parties may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions given thirty (30) days in advance. In either case, the resignation or removal takes effect when a successor escrow agent appointed by the Parties accepts its appointment in writing and receives the Escrow Funds. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 10.2 to appoint one.

8.8 **Successor by Merger.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall be the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

## 9. TAX MATTERS

9.1 **Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Funds, whether or not distributed.

9.2 **Tax Forms.** Each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date a properly completed IRS Form W-9. The Escrow Agent shall file information returns on the earnings of the Escrow Funds as required by law.

9.3 **Tax Distributions.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer from the earnings on the Escrow Funds an amount equal to thirty percent (30%) of the earnings for that quarter, unless the Parties direct otherwise by Joint Written Instructions.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice-of-law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

10.2 **Forum.** Each Signatory irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, each Signatory submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware.

10.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH SIGNATORY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

10.4 **Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing. Notices shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt. Notices shall be sent to the addresses below, or to such other address as a Signatory designates by notice:

If to Buyer: Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC, Attention: Chief Financial Officer, at the address set out on the signature page of the Purchase Agreement.

If to Seller: Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address set out on the signature page of the Purchase Agreement.

If to the Escrow Agent: Pemberly Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Teivil/Sasos Escrow, 1100 North Market Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19890.

10.5 **Assignment.** No Signatory may assign this Escrow Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Signatories, except as provided in Section 8.8. This Escrow Agreement binds and benefits the Signatories and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

10.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Signatories. No waiver shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Signatory against which it is to be enforced.

10.7 **Know Your Customer.** The Parties shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering and customer identification requirements.

10.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

10.9 **Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be effective as originals.
