**Re: Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

Seller and Buyer agree as follows:

## 1. Customer Contracts

1.1 **Customer Notification.** On or promptly after the Closing Date, Seller and Buyer shall send a joint notice to each customer under a Customer Contract. The notice shall be substantially in the form attached as Exhibit A. It shall inform the customer of the transfer of the Business to Buyer and give remittance instructions for Buyer. This Paragraph 1.1 supplements, and does not limit, Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Customer Portal Credentials.** Seller shall cause the customer-facing inventory portal described in Section 2.1(a) of the Transition Services Agreement to display Buyer's name and customer service contacts no later than fifteen (15) Business Days after the Closing Date.

## 2. Assumed Liabilities

2.1 **Amendment.** Seller and Buyer amend the Purchase Agreement under Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement as set out in this Paragraph 2.

2.2 **Assumed Liabilities.** The definition of "Assumed Liabilities" in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "**Assumed Liabilities**" means (a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts, other than the Customer Contracts, arising after the Closing, but only to the extent such Liabilities do not relate to any breach, default or violation by Seller occurring on or before the Closing; and (b) all Liabilities under the Customer Contracts, whether arising before, at or after the Closing, including all cargo loss, product spoilage and temperature-excursion claims relating to goods received, stored, handled or transported under the Customer Contracts.

2.3 **Excluded Liabilities.** Clause (ii) of the definition of "Excluded Liabilities" in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> (ii) all cargo loss, product spoilage and temperature-excursion claims relating to goods received, stored or transported on or before the Closing, other than claims under the Customer Contracts,

## 3. Claims Files and Records

3.1 **Delivery of Claims Files.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer copies of all open customer claim files under the Customer Contracts. Each file shall include the related correspondence, bills of lading, receiving records and temperature, humidity and alarm logs.

3.2 **Cooperation.** For twelve (12) months after the Closing Date, Seller shall make available to Buyer, on reasonable notice and during normal business hours, those former employees of Seller who handled customer claims for the Business. Seller shall also give Buyer reasonable access to any records retained by Seller relating to those claims. Buyer shall reimburse Seller's reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs of that cooperation.

3.3 **Insurance Information.** Seller shall provide Buyer, on request, with the policy numbers, carriers and claims contacts for the cargo legal liability and warehouse legal liability policies of Seller in effect before the Closing.

## 4. General

4.1 **Effect.** Except as amended by Paragraph 2, the Purchase Agreement remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms. References in the Purchase Agreement to "this Agreement" shall be read as references to the Purchase Agreement as amended by this Side Letter.

4.2 **Governing Law; Forum.** This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware. Sections 11.10 and 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it.

4.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Side Letter shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.4 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be effective as originals.
