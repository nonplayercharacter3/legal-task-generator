# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT is entered into as of June 30, 2026, by and between **Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business. The business consists of three temperature-controlled distribution centers, a fleet of refrigerated tractors and trailers, and related customer cold-storage and transportation contracts (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business. Buyer will also assume certain specified liabilities. In each case the sale and assumption are on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. The Parties are signing this Agreement and consummating the Closing on the same date.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

### 2.1 Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Sections 2.3 and 2.4. It is also payable together with Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) Buyer shall pay the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) Buyer shall issue the Seller Note to Seller; and

(c) Buyer shall pay the balance of $36,650,000 to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Seller designates in writing.

### 2.2 Purchased Assets; Excluded Assets; Liabilities

(a) **Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, free and clear of all liens other than permitted liens listed on Schedule 2.2(a) (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):
(i) the Assigned Contracts;
(ii) Seller's leasehold interests in the Distribution Centers;
(iii) the Reefer Fleet;
(iv) all refrigeration plant, compressors, evaporators, racking, dock equipment, forklifts and other tangible personal property located at the Distribution Centers;
(v) the Closing Inventory;
(vi) the Accounts Receivable;
(vii) all permits held for the Business, to the extent transferable;
(viii) all books, records, customer lists, lane and pricing data and temperature logs of the Business; and
(ix) all goodwill of the Business.

(b) **Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include cash and cash equivalents, Seller's corporate records, insurance policies, Tax refunds for pre-Closing periods, or the other assets listed on Schedule 2.2(b) (the "**Excluded Assets**").

(c) **Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge the Assumed Liabilities when due.

(d) **Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer is not assuming and shall not be responsible for any Excluded Liabilities. Seller shall remain responsible for all Excluded Liabilities and shall pay, perform and discharge them when due.

### 2.3 Working Capital Adjustment

The Purchase Price shall be increased dollar for dollar by any amount by which the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital. It shall be decreased dollar for dollar by any amount by which the Target Working Capital exceeds the Closing Working Capital. "**Closing Working Capital**" means the Closing Inventory plus the Accounts Receivable, net of reserves, minus the current liabilities included in the Assumed Liabilities, each as of the Closing. Closing Working Capital shall be determined in accordance with GAAP applied consistently with the accounting principles set out on Schedule 2.3.

### 2.4 Closing Statement

(a) **Delivery.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**"). The Closing Statement shall set out Seller's good-faith calculation of the Closing Working Capital, including the value of the Closing Inventory and the Accounts Receivable, together with reasonable supporting detail. Buyer shall give Seller reasonable access to the books, records and personnel of the Business to prepare the Closing Statement.

(b) **Disputes.** Buyer may dispute the Closing Statement by delivering to Seller a written notice (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the dispute. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the Parties. Any item not specifically disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement shall also be final and binding.

(c) **Resolution.** If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve the disputed items. Any items still unresolved at the end of that period shall be submitted to a nationally recognized independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the Parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. It shall resolve only the disputed items, and within the range of values asserted by the Parties. Its determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The Independent Accountant's fees shall be borne by Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed items.

(d) **Payment.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Working Capital becomes final, the owing Party shall pay any adjustment amount under Section 2.3 by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

### 2.5 Indemnity Escrow

The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent as security for all Indemnity Claims. It shall be disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and Article IX. Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of the fees of the Escrow Agent.

### 2.6 Allocation

The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and the methodology set out on Schedule 2.6. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

### 3.1 Closing

The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on June 30, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), simultaneously with the execution of this Agreement. No refrigerant release requiring a report to any Governmental Authority has occurred at any Distribution Center during that period.

Schedule 1.1(b) lists each Customer Contract. Each Customer Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Seller has performed in all material respects all obligations required to be performed by it under each Customer Contract. Except as set out on Schedule 4.10, during the past two (2) years no customer has asserted any cargo loss, product spoilage or temperature-excursion claim exceeding $50,000, and no customer has given notice of termination or material reduction of volume.

### 4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits

Seller holds all permits required to operate the Business, including food-facility registrations and motor carrier operating authority. Seller is in material compliance with each such permit and with all applicable Laws, including the Food Safety Modernization Act sanitary transportation rule.

### 4.12 Litigation

Except as set out on Schedule 4.12, there is no Action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

### 9.1 Survival

(a) The representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until the Escrow Release Date (as defined in the Escrow Agreement). The representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing for the same period. No claim for breach of any such representation or warranty may be made unless written notice of the claim is delivered under Section 9.4 before that date.

(b) The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(c) The covenants and agreements of the Parties shall survive the Closing until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(d) Any claim for which notice is properly given before the end of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

### 9.2 Indemnification by Seller

From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective managers, officers and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:
(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;
(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or
(c) any Excluded Liability.

### 9.3 Indemnification by Buyer

From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective directors, officers and employees, from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:
(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;
(b) any Assumed Liability; or
(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

### 9.4 Claims Procedure

(a) A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give written notice to the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") promptly after becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the claim. The notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the estimated amount of Losses, if reasonably determinable. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the delay. If the claim is an Indemnity Claim, Buyer shall deliver a copy of the notice to the Escrow Agent at the same time.

(b) For any third-party claim, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by giving written notice within fifteen (15) days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

### 9.5 Limitations

(a) **Basket.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $485,000 (the "**Basket**"). Once that amount is exceeded, Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket. The same limitation applies to Buyer's liability under Section 9.3(a).

(b) **Cap.** The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $4,850,000 (the "**Cap**"). The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Cap.

(c) **Exceptions.** The Cap and the Basket shall not apply to Losses arising from fraud or from any breach of a Fundamental Representation, or to any claim under Section 9.2(b) or Section 9.2(c). The aggregate liability of Seller for those Losses and claims shall not exceed the Purchase Price. The Cap and the Basket shall not apply to any claim under Section 9.3(b).

(d) **Materiality Scrape.** For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

(e) **Insurance.** Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of the claim, less the costs of recovery.

### 9.6 Source of Recovery

Losses payable to Buyer in respect of an Indemnity Claim shall be satisfied first from the funds then held under the Escrow Agreement. To the extent those funds are insufficient or have been released, Losses shall then be satisfied by setoff under Section 9.7. Any remaining amount shall be paid directly by Seller.

### 9.7 Right of Setoff

Buyer may set off any amount to which it is entitled under this Article IX against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable under the Seller Note. Any amount set off shall reduce the amount owed under the Seller Note dollar for dollar. If Buyer withholds a payment under the Seller Note in good faith for a claim that has been noticed under Section 9.4 but not finally resolved, the withholding shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note. Buyer shall pay any withheld amount, with accrued interest at the rate in the Seller Note, within five (5) Business Days after final resolution of the claim in Seller's favor.

### 9.8 Exclusive Remedy

From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of this Agreement. This Section 9.8 does not limit (a) claims for fraud, (b) the procedures under Section 2.4, or (c) a Party's right to specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.12.

### 9.9 Tax Treatment

All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

### 10.1 Non-Competition

For five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly own, operate or provide services to any temperature-controlled warehousing or refrigerated trucking business within three hundred (300) miles of any Distribution Center.

### 10.2 Non-Solicitation

For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, (a) solicit any customer under a Customer Contract to cease or reduce its business with Buyer, or (b) solicit for employment any employee hired by Buyer under Section 7.1.

### 10.3 Reformation

If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any covenant in this Article X is unenforceable in duration or scope, the court may reduce the duration or scope to the extent necessary to make the covenant enforceable.
