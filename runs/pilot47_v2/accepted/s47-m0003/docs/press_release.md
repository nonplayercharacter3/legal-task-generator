# Sasos Reefer Logistics Completes Acquisition of Teivil Cold Chain's Refrigerated Warehousing and Reefer Trucking Business

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**WILMINGTON, Delaware, July 1, 2026.** Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC ("Sasos") today announced that it has acquired the refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business of Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc. ("Teivil"). The parties signed and closed the transaction on June 30, 2026 under an asset purchase agreement.

## 1. Transaction Overview

1.1 Sasos acquired substantially all of the assets used in Teivil's cold chain business. These include:

- three temperature-controlled distribution centers;
- Teivil's fleet of refrigerated tractors, trailers and transport refrigeration units; and
- Teivil's customer cold-storage, warehousing, handling and refrigerated transportation contracts.

1.2 The parties did not disclose the financial terms of the transaction.

1.3 The acquired business continues to operate from the same facilities, with the same operating teams. Most Teivil employees who supported the business have joined Sasos.

## 2. Continuity for Customers

2.1 Customers of the acquired business will continue to receive storage, handling and transportation services without interruption. Sasos and Teivil are jointly notifying each customer of the transfer and providing updated remittance instructions.

2.2 Teivil will provide transition support to Sasos for a limited period while Sasos moves the business onto its own systems. That support covers warehouse management system hosting, temperature telemetry and alarm monitoring. During the transition, customers will continue to have access to the customer inventory portal they use today, updated to show Sasos customer service contacts.

## 3. Management Commentary

3.1 The Chief Executive Officer of Sasos said: "This acquisition expands our temperature-controlled footprint and adds an experienced team and a well-maintained reefer fleet to our network. Our priority over the coming months is a seamless transition for every customer. That means the same people, the same facilities and the same attention to product integrity, from receiving dock to final delivery."

3.2 A spokesperson for Teivil said: "We are pleased to have found a buyer committed to the customers and employees who built this business. We will support Sasos throughout the transition."

## 4. Advisors

4.1 Pemberly Trust Company, N.A. is acting as escrow agent in connection with the transaction.

## 5. About Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC

5.1 Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company. It provides refrigerated and frozen warehousing, cross-docking and temperature-controlled truckload transportation to food, beverage and life-sciences shippers.

## 6. About Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.

6.1 Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc. is a Delaware corporation. Before the transaction, it operated a refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business serving customers in the food and consumer products sectors.
