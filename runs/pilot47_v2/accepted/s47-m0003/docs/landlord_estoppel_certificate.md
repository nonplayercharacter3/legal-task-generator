# LANDLORD ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE

**Date: June 24, 2026**

**To:**

**Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignee**")

**Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**")

**Re:** Lease of temperature-controlled distribution center located at [Premises Address] (the "**Premises**")

The undersigned (the "**Landlord**") is the landlord under the lease described in Section 1. The Landlord understands that Tenant and Assignee have entered into, or expect to enter into, an Asset Purchase Agreement. Under that agreement Tenant will assign its interest in the Lease to Assignee at the closing of the transaction (the "**Closing**"). The Landlord further understands that Assignee, its Affiliates and its lenders will rely on this certificate in completing the Closing and accepting the assignment.

The Landlord certifies to Assignee and Tenant as follows:

## 1. The Lease

1.1 **Lease Documents.** The Premises are leased to Tenant under a lease dated [Lease Date], as amended by the documents listed in Exhibit A (together, the "**Lease**"). The Lease is the only agreement between the Landlord and Tenant relating to the Premises. A true, correct and complete copy of the Lease is attached as Exhibit B.

1.2 **Validity.** The Lease is in full force and effect. It has not been modified, supplemented or amended except as listed in Exhibit A.

1.3 **Premises.** The Premises consist of the land and the temperature-controlled warehouse building described in the Lease. The building includes cold-storage and freezer rooms, refrigerated docks and the associated refrigeration plant and equipment rooms. Tenant is in possession of the entire Premises. Tenant has not sublet any part of the Premises, and to the Landlord's knowledge no other Person is in possession of any part of the Premises.

## 2. Term and Rent

2.1 **Term.** The current term of the Lease began on [Commencement Date] and expires on [Expiration Date], unless extended or terminated earlier in accordance with the Lease.

2.2 **Renewal Options.** Tenant has the renewal options set out in the Lease. No renewal option has been exercised or has lapsed.

2.3 **Base Rent.** The current monthly base rent under the Lease is $[Amount]. Base rent has been paid through June 30, 2026. No base rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

2.4 **Additional Rent.** Tenant pays its share of real estate taxes, insurance and common area charges as additional rent, as provided in the Lease. All additional rent billed to Tenant through the date of this certificate has been paid. The Landlord has not delivered any reconciliation statement for a prior lease year that shows an amount still owed by Tenant.

2.5 **Security Deposit.** The Landlord holds a security deposit of $[Amount] under the Lease. The Landlord has not applied any part of the security deposit to any obligation of Tenant. The Landlord shall hold the security deposit for the benefit of Assignee after the Closing, in accordance with the Lease.

## 3. Status of Performance

3.1 **No Tenant Default.** Tenant is not in default under the Lease. To the Landlord's knowledge, no event has occurred that, with the giving of notice or the passage of time or both, would constitute a default by Tenant under the Lease.

3.2 **No Landlord Default.** The Landlord has not received any notice from Tenant claiming a default by the Landlord under the Lease that remains uncured.

3.3 **Improvements.** All improvements, alterations and work required to be performed by the Landlord under the Lease have been completed. All tenant improvement allowances or other contributions owed by the Landlord to Tenant have been paid in full.

3.4 **No Offsets.** To the Landlord's knowledge, Tenant has no existing right of offset, deduction or abatement against rent under the Lease.

3.5 **Environmental and Compliance Notices.** The Landlord has not delivered to Tenant, and has not received from any Governmental Authority, any written notice of a violation of Law relating to the Premises that remains uncured. This includes any notice relating to the refrigeration systems, refrigerants or ammonia handling at the Premises.

3.6 **No Pending Actions.** No condemnation, eminent domain or similar proceeding is pending or, to the Landlord's knowledge, threatened against the Premises. The Landlord has not received written notice of any such proceeding.

## 4. Mortgage and Ownership

4.1 **Ownership.** The Landlord is the fee owner of the Premises. The Landlord has full power and authority to execute and deliver this certificate.

4.2 **Mortgage.** Except as listed in Exhibit A, the Premises are not subject to any mortgage or deed of trust. If any mortgage is listed in Exhibit A, the Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain from the mortgagee a subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement in favor of Assignee, in the mortgagee's customary form, within sixty (60) days after Assignee's written request.

4.3 **Sale of Premises.** The Landlord has not entered into any contract to sell the Premises. To the Landlord's knowledge, no Person has any option or right of first refusal to purchase the Premises.

## 5. Consent to Assignment

5.1 **Consent.** The Landlord consents to the assignment of the Lease by Tenant to Assignee, effective as of the Closing. The Landlord also consents to Assignee's assumption of Tenant's obligations under the Lease arising after the Closing. This consent satisfies all requirements of the Lease for the Landlord's consent to the assignment.

5.2 **No Fee or Rent Increase.** No assignment fee, transfer premium, increase in rent or other payment is due to the Landlord in connection with the assignment.

5.3 **Notices After Closing.** After the Closing and the Landlord's receipt of a copy of the executed assignment, the Landlord shall send all notices under the Lease to Assignee at the address Assignee designates in writing.

## 6. Tenant Equipment

6.1 **Trade Fixtures and Equipment.** The racking, forklifts, dock equipment, transport refrigeration units and other movable equipment and trade fixtures of Tenant located at the Premises are the property of Tenant. The Landlord claims no ownership interest in them.

6.2 **No Landlord Lien.** The Landlord waives any contractual landlord's lien on the movable equipment and trade fixtures of Tenant, and of Assignee after the Closing. The Landlord also waives any statutory landlord's lien on them to the extent permitted by Law. The Landlord does not waive its rights to any fixtures or improvements that are part of the realty or that the Lease requires to remain at the Premises when the Lease ends.

## 7. Reliance

7.1 **Reliance.** Assignee, its Affiliates, its lenders and their respective successors and assigns may rely on this certificate. The Landlord shall be bound by the statements in this certificate as to all such Persons.

7.2 **No Modification.** This certificate does not modify or amend the Lease. If this certificate conflicts with the Lease, the Lease governs as between the Landlord and Tenant. The Landlord remains estopped from denying the truth of the statements in this certificate as against any Person relying on it under Section 7.1.

7.3 **Defined Terms.** "Affiliate," "Governmental Authority," "Law" and "Person" have the meanings given in the Asset Purchase Agreement between Tenant and Assignee.
