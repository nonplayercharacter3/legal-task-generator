# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Service Provider**"), and **Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Recipient**"). Service Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Service Provider, as seller, and Recipient, as buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient acquired the refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business of Service Provider (the "**Business**"), including the Distribution Centers and the Reefer Fleet.

B. Before the Closing, the Business relied on Service Provider's warehouse management system and its temperature telemetry and alarm monitoring platform. Recipient needs those services for a transition period while it migrates the Business to its own systems.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to deliver this Agreement at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Data**" means all data relating to the Business that is processed, stored or generated in the course of the Services. This includes customer records, inventory and lot records, lane and pricing data, order and shipment history, and temperature, humidity and alarm logs.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Migration Plan**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Service Levels**" means the performance standards for each Service set out in Schedule A.

"**Services**" means the services described in Section 2.1 and Schedule A, as modified under Section 2.4.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

1.2 **Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** During the Term, Service Provider shall provide to Recipient the following Services for the Distribution Centers and the Reefer Fleet:

(a) **Warehouse Management System Hosting.** Hosting, operation, maintenance and support of the warehouse management system used in the Business as of the Closing Date. This includes receiving, putaway, slotting, picking, shipping, cycle-count and customer billing modules, and the customer-facing inventory portal.

(b) **Temperature Telemetry.** Continuous collection, storage and display of temperature and humidity readings from the sensors installed in the cold-storage rooms, freezers, docks and blast cells of the Distribution Centers, and from the transport refrigeration units in the Reefer Fleet.

(c) **Alarm Monitoring.** Twenty-four-hour monitoring of temperature-excursion, door-open, power-loss and refrigerant-detection alarms. This includes escalation to the on-call contacts Recipient designates in accordance with the call tree in Schedule A.

(d) **Help Desk and Data Services.** Tier 1 and Tier 2 help desk support for the systems described above. Service Provider shall also provide standard reports and data extracts reasonably requested by Recipient.

2.2 **Standard of Performance.** Service Provider shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) at a level of quality, priority and care no less than that at which it performed them for the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and (c) in accordance with the Service Levels and applicable Law.

2.3 **Personnel.** Service Provider shall assign personnel with appropriate qualifications and experience to perform the Services. Service Provider shall designate a service manager who will be Recipient's primary point of contact for the Services.

2.4 **Changes.** Either Party may request a change to the scope of the Services by written notice. No change shall be effective unless set out in a written change order signed by both Parties. The change order shall state any adjustment to the Fees.

2.5 **Third-Party Consents.** Service Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any consent of a third-party software or hardware vendor required to provide the Services to Recipient. Recipient shall reimburse the reasonable, documented out-of-pocket cost of those consents, up to the amount pre-approved by Recipient in writing.

## 3. MIGRATION

3.1 **Migration Plan.** Within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date, the Parties shall agree in good faith on a written plan for migrating the Business Data and the Business's operations to systems selected by Recipient (the "**Migration Plan**").

3.2 **Cooperation.** Service Provider shall cooperate with Recipient and its vendors in carrying out the Migration Plan. This includes providing complete extracts of the Business Data in the formats specified in the Migration Plan, parallel running of systems, and reasonable cut-over support.

3.3 **Final Data Delivery.** No later than ten (10) Business Days before the end of the Term, Service Provider shall deliver to Recipient a complete and final copy of all Business Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Recipient shall pay Service Provider the monthly fees for each Service set out in Schedule A (the "**Fees**"). The Fees are intended to reflect Service Provider's fully allocated cost of providing the Services, without markup.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Service Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Fees by Service and any reimbursable expenses. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice to Service Provider, with reasonable detail, before the payment due date. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Service Credits.** If Service Provider fails to meet a Service Level, Recipient shall be entitled to the service credits set out in Schedule A. Service credits shall be applied against the next invoice.

4.5 **Taxes.** The Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes properly invoiced by Service Provider.

## 5. BUSINESS DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, all Business Data is the property of Recipient. Service Provider shall use the Business Data only to perform the Services.

5.2 **Security.** Service Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for the Business Data that are no less protective than those it maintained for the Business before the Closing Date, and no less than industry-standard practice. Service Provider shall notify Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or loss of Business Data.

5.3 **Confidentiality.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party received under this Agreement. Neither Party shall disclose that information except to its employees and advisors who need to know it for purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by confidentiality obligations. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, or that is required to be disclosed by Law. These obligations are in addition to Service Provider's obligations under Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.4 **Return of Data.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term, and after completing the delivery required by Section 3.3, Service Provider shall delete all Business Data in its possession. It shall certify the deletion in writing to Recipient.

## 6. ACCESS AND RECORDS

6.1 **System Access.** Service Provider shall provide Recipient's designated personnel with user access to the systems used to provide the Services, as reasonably required for Recipient to operate the Business.

6.2 **Records.** Service Provider shall keep accurate records of the Services performed and the Fees charged. Recipient may inspect those records on reasonable notice during normal business hours.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the date that is 9 months after the APA Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Section 7. For purposes of this Agreement, the "APA Closing Date" is the Closing Date under the Purchase Agreement.

7.2 **Early Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, on thirty (30) days' written notice to Service Provider. The Fees shall be reduced to reflect the terminated Service from the effective date of termination.

7.3 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it. Service Provider may terminate for Recipient's nonpayment only if the unpaid amount is undisputed and remains unpaid thirty (30) days after Recipient receives notice of nonpayment.

7.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Recipient shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date. Sections 3.3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10 and 11 shall survive expiration or termination.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement. This exclusion does not apply to a breach of Section 5, or to a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. This limitation does not apply to a breach of Section 5, to Recipient's payment obligations, or to a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Service Provider.** Service Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of (a) Service Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, or (b) any breach by Service Provider of Section 5.

9.2 **By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify and hold harmless Service Provider and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of Recipient's use of the Services in breach of this Agreement.

9.3 **Procedure.** Claims under this Section 9 shall follow the procedure in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, applied with the necessary changes.

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 **Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute under this Agreement through their service managers. If the dispute is not resolved within ten (10) Business Days, it shall be escalated to a senior executive of each Party.

10.2 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice-of-law rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

10.3 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any Action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware.

10.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement. They shall be sent to the addresses of the Parties set out in the Purchase Agreement.

11.2 **Independent Contractor.** Service Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.

11.3 **Assignment.** Service Provider may, without the consent of Recipient, assign this Agreement or delegate any Services to any Person. Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Service Provider. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

11.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for a failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents performance of a material Service for more than fifteen (15) days, Recipient may terminate that Service on written notice.

11.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against which it is to be enforced.

11.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be effective as originals.
