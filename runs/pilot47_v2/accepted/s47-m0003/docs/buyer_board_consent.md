# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF SASOS REEFER LOGISTICS, LLC

**Dated: October 20, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. They do so under Section 18-302(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and the Company's Limited Liability Company Agreement, as amended (the "**LLC Agreement**"). The undersigned waive all notice requirements for a meeting and consent to the adoption of the resolutions below, effective as of the date set out above.

## 1. RECITALS

1.1 **The Acquisition.** The Company, as buyer, and Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, the Company acquired the refrigerated warehousing and reefer trucking business of Seller, including the Distribution Centers, the Reefer Fleet and the Customer Contracts. The Closing occurred on June 30, 2026. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Purchase Price Adjustment.** Under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase Price of $48,500,000 is subject to adjustment dollar for dollar by the amount by which the Closing Working Capital differs from the Target Working Capital of $6,200,000. Closing Working Capital includes the Closing Inventory and the Accounts Receivable, net of reserves.

1.3 **Delivery of the Closing Statement.** Under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered the Closing Statement on September 2, 2026. The Closing Statement sets out Seller's calculation of the Closing Working Capital, including the value of the Closing Inventory and the Accounts Receivable.

1.4 **Management Review.** The Company's Chief Financial Officer and the finance team reviewed the Closing Statement and its supporting detail. They compared it with the Company's physical counts at the Distribution Centers, its review of the refrigerant, spare parts and fuel stock, and its collections experience on the Accounts Receivable since the Closing. Management reported its findings to the Board. Management believes that the Closing Statement overstates the value of the Closing Inventory and the Accounts Receivable. In management's view the overstatement results in particular from:

(a) the inclusion in Closing Inventory of packaging and supplies that are obsolete or unusable, and of spare parts for transport refrigeration units no longer in the Reefer Fleet;

(b) the valuation of refrigerant stock at replacement cost rather than in accordance with the accounting principles set out on Schedule 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(c) the absence of adequate reserves against Accounts Receivable that are more than ninety (90) days past due, or that are subject to customer offsets for temperature-excursion credits.

1.5 **Board Determination.** The Board has considered management's report and the advice of the Company's counsel and accounting advisors. It has determined that disputing the Closing Statement is advisable and in the best interests of the Company.

## 2. RESOLUTIONS

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT:

**Resolution 1. Dispute of the Closing Statement.**

RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") be, and each of them acting alone is, authorized and directed to dispute the Closing Statement on behalf of the Company under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement. This includes the values assigned in it to the Closing Inventory and the Accounts Receivable, and any other item that the Authorized Officers, with the advice of counsel, determine should be disputed. In particular, the Authorized Officers are authorized to prepare, execute and deliver to Seller a Notice of Disagreement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify each disputed item and the basis for the dispute in reasonable detail.

**Resolution 2. Negotiation and Independent Accountant.**

RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to negotiate with Seller in good faith to resolve the disputed items. If any items remain unresolved, each Authorized Officer is authorized to agree with Seller on the selection of an Independent Accountant and to submit those items to the Independent Accountant under Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement. Each Authorized Officer is further authorized to prepare and deliver all submissions, supporting schedules and other materials required in that process.

**Resolution 3. Engagement of Advisors.**

RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to engage, or to continue the engagement of, legal counsel and independent accounting and valuation advisors to assist the Company in connection with the matters described in Resolutions 1 and 2. Each Authorized Officer is also authorized to pay the reasonable fees and expenses of those advisors.

**Resolution 4. Settlement Authority.**

RESOLVED, that any settlement with Seller of the disputed items that would result in a net payment by the Company to Seller, or in a reduction of less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the aggregate overstatement asserted in the Notice of Disagreement, shall require the further approval of the Board.

**Resolution 5. Reporting.**

RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers shall report to the Board on the status of the dispute at each regular meeting of the Board, and more often as material developments occur.

**Resolution 6. General Authority.**

RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to take all further actions that he or she considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions, including executing and delivering all related notices, certificates and instruments. Any such action is conclusively evidenced by the taking of it.

**Resolution 7. Ratification.**

RESOLVED, that all actions previously taken by any officer of the Company that are consistent with the purposes of the foregoing resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.
