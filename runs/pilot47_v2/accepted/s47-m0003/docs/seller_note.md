# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$7,000,000**
**Dated: June 30, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Sasos Reefer Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Teivil Cold Chain Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal amount set out in Section 1, together with interest as provided in this Note. Payment is due at the times and in the manner set out below.

Maker issues this Note under Section 2.1(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026, between Maker, as buyer, and Holder, as seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. PRINCIPAL

1.1 **Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $7,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount forms part of the Purchase Price under Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 **Outstanding Principal.** The "**Outstanding Principal**" at any time is the Principal Amount less all payments, prepayments and setoffs applied to principal under this Note through that time.

## 2. INTEREST

2.1 **Rate.** Interest shall accrue on the Outstanding Principal from the date of this Note at a fixed rate of eight percent (8.0%) per annum (the "**Interest Rate**").

2.2 **Computation.** Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

2.3 **Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, the Outstanding Principal and any overdue interest shall bear interest at the Interest Rate plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

2.4 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest shall be payable under this Note above the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid above that rate shall be applied to reduce the Outstanding Principal.

## 3. PAYMENTS

3.1 **Interest Payments.** Maker shall pay accrued interest quarterly in arrears on each March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31. The first payment is due on September 30, 2026. Each such date is an "**Interest Payment Date**."

3.2 **Principal Payments.** Maker shall repay the Principal Amount in installments of $875,000 each on each Interest Payment Date. Installments begin on September 30, 2027 and continue through the Maturity Date.

3.3 **Maturity.** The entire Outstanding Principal, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable on June 30, 2029 (the "**Maturity Date**").

3.4 **Business Days.** If any payment is due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day. Interest shall continue to accrue for the additional days.

3.5 **Manner of Payment.** Maker shall make all payments in U.S. dollars, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date.

3.6 **Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 8.2, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to Outstanding Principal in inverse order of maturity.

## 4. PREPAYMENT

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments shall be applied to the remaining principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. SETOFF

5.1 **Setoff Right.** Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest payable under this Note any amount to which Maker is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, as provided in Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.2 **Effect.** Any amount set off shall reduce the amounts owed under this Note dollar for dollar. Maker shall give Holder written notice of each setoff, stating the amount set off and the claim to which it relates.

## 6. SUBORDINATION

6.1 **Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set out in this Section 6. "**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owed to any bank, commercial lender or other institutional lender, together with interest, fees and expenses, and any refinancing of that indebtedness.

6.2 **Payment Blockage.** If a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under any Senior Debt, Maker shall make no payment under this Note until that default has been cured or waived or the Senior Debt has been paid in full. Any regularly scheduled payment not made because of this Section 6.2 shall be due on the first Business Day after the blockage ends.

6.3 **Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or similar proceeding of Maker, the holders of Senior Debt shall be paid in full before Holder receives any payment under this Note.

6.4 **Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment in violation of this Section 6, Holder shall hold it in trust for the holders of Senior Debt and promptly pay it over to them.

6.5 **Subordination Agreement.** At the request of any holder of Senior Debt, Holder shall execute a subordination agreement on that holder's customary terms consistent with this Section 6.

6.6 **No Impairment.** As between Maker and Holder, nothing in this Section 6 impairs Maker's absolute obligation to pay this Note in accordance with its terms.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

7.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, other than a payment that Section 6.2 prohibits or that Maker withholds or sets off under Section 5;

(b) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due;

(c) Maker commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law;

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, and it is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(e) Maker sells all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets to a Person that is not an Affiliate of Maker, unless this Note is paid in full at or before that sale.

7.2 **Remedies.** If an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) or 7.1(e) occurs and is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. If an Event of Default under Section 7.1(b), 7.1(c) or 7.1(d) occurs, the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest shall become immediately due and payable without notice. Holder's remedies are subject to Section 6.

## 8. WAIVERS; COSTS

8.1 **Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by this Note.

8.2 **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable, documented costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred after an Event of Default.

8.3 **No Waiver by Holder.** No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note shall operate as a waiver of that right.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice-of-law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of New York sitting in the County of New York and the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Note, including any action to collect amounts payable under this Note and any claimed setoff against those amounts. Maker and Holder each waive any objection to venue in those courts and any claim of inconvenient forum.

9.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

10.2 **Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without Maker's prior written consent. Any transferee takes this Note subject to Sections 5 and 6. Any purported transfer in violation of this Section 10.2 is void.

10.3 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

10.4 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor. Before it does so, Holder shall deliver to Maker an affidavit of loss and a reasonable indemnity.

10.5 **Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and benefits Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

10.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
