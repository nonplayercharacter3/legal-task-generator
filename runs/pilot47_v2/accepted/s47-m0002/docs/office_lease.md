# INDUSTRIAL AND OFFICE LEASE

This INDUSTRIAL AND OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of April 1, 2019 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Harrowgate Industrial Properties, L.P.**, a Pennsylvania limited partnership ("**Landlord**"), and **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE TERMS

**1.1 Summary.** The following basic terms apply under this Lease. Each is subject to the more detailed provisions of this Lease.

| Term | Provision |
|---|---|
| Premises | The building of approximately 86,000 rentable square feet at 2200 Keystone Industrial Drive, Bristol Township, Bucks County, Pennsylvania. It comprises a laundry plant of approximately 74,000 square feet and associated offices of approximately 12,000 square feet. The Premises include the land, truck court, loading docks and parking areas described in Exhibit A (together, the "**Property**"). |
| Commencement Date | April 1, 2019 |
| Initial Term | Twelve (12) years, ending March 31, 2031 |
| Renewal Options | Two (2) options of five (5) years each, under Section 3.2 |
| Base Rent | Under Section 4.1 and Exhibit B |
| Security Deposit | $185,000 |
| Permitted Use | Under Section 6.1 |

**1.2 Exhibits.** Exhibit A (Description of Property), Exhibit B (Base Rent Schedule) and Exhibit C (Tenant's Equipment) form part of this Lease.

## 2. DEMISE

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant has the exclusive right to use the Property throughout the Term.

**2.2 Condition.** Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date. However, Landlord warrants that on the Commencement Date the roof, structure, and the building's electrical, plumbing and fire-sprinkler systems are in good working order.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Initial Term.** The initial term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on March 31, 2031, unless terminated earlier or extended under this Lease. The initial term and each Renewal Term are together the "**Term**."

**3.2 Renewal Options.** Tenant may extend the Term for two (2) successive periods of five (5) years each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). To exercise an option, Tenant shall give Landlord written notice at least twelve (12) months before the then-current Term expires. Tenant may exercise an option only if no Event of Default is then continuing. Base Rent for each Renewal Term is ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent, determined under Section 4.3.

## 4. RENT

**4.1 Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord base rent in the amounts set out in Exhibit B ("**Base Rent**"). Base Rent is payable in equal monthly installments in advance, on the first day of each calendar month, without demand.

**4.2 Escalation.** Base Rent increases by two and one-half percent (2.5%) on each anniversary of the Commencement Date during the initial term, as reflected in Exhibit B.

**4.3 Fair Market Rent.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent that a willing tenant would pay, and a willing landlord would accept, for comparable industrial and office space in Bucks County, Pennsylvania, on a renewal basis. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant exercises a renewal option, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the local industrial market. The two brokers shall appoint a third. Fair Market Rent is the average of the two determinations closest to one another.

**4.4 Net Lease; Additional Rent.** This is a net lease. Tenant shall pay, as additional rent, all real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Property, the cost of property insurance maintained by Landlord under Section 9.2, and all utilities serving the Premises (together with Base Rent, "**Rent**"). Landlord shall deliver tax and insurance invoices to Tenant, and Tenant shall pay them within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.5 Late Payment.** Any installment of Rent not paid within five (5) days after it is due bears interest at eight percent (8%) per annum from the due date until paid.

**4.6 Security Deposit.** Tenant has deposited $185,000 with Landlord as security for its performance under this Lease. Landlord may apply the deposit to cure any Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the Term expires and Tenant surrenders the Premises.

## 5. UTILITIES AND WASTEWATER

**5.1 Utilities.** Tenant shall contract directly with the providers of electricity, natural gas, water, sewer and telecommunications services to the Premises, and shall pay for them when due.

**5.2 Wastewater.** Tenant shall obtain and maintain, in its own name, all industrial wastewater discharge permits required for its use of the Premises. Tenant shall comply with them and with all pretreatment requirements of the local sewer authority. Tenant may maintain and operate the wastewater pretreatment system located at the Premises, which is part of Tenant's Equipment.

## 6. USE

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for commercial laundry, linen-rental and textile-care operations, including the receiving, sorting, washing, drying, finishing, storage and distribution of linens and textiles, together with associated offices, vehicle staging and any lawful related use (the "**Permitted Use**").

**6.2 Compliance with Law.** Tenant shall use the Premises in compliance with all applicable laws, ordinances, regulations and permits.

**6.3 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant may use and store at the Premises detergents, alkalis, bleaches, sours, softeners and other chemicals customarily used in the Permitted Use, in quantities reasonably necessary for it and in compliance with applicable environmental laws. Tenant shall not otherwise bring hazardous substances onto the Property without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**6.4 Environmental Responsibility.** Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all losses, costs and liabilities arising from any release of hazardous substances at the Property caused by Tenant or its agents, employees or contractors during the Term. Tenant's obligations under this Section 6.4 survive the expiration or termination of this Lease. Nothing in this Lease makes Landlord responsible for, or releases any person from, any environmental condition at the Property.

## 7. MAINTENANCE, ALTERATIONS AND EQUIPMENT

**7.1 Tenant's Maintenance.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, the loading docks, the paved areas and all building systems serving only the Premises in good order and repair. Ordinary wear and tear and casualty are excepted.

**7.2 Landlord's Maintenance.** Landlord shall maintain, repair and, when necessary, replace the roof, foundation and structural elements of the building at its own cost, except to the extent the damage is caused by Tenant's negligence.

**7.3 Alterations.** Tenant may make non-structural alterations costing less than $150,000 per project without Landlord's consent, on prior written notice. All other alterations require Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

**7.4 Tenant's Equipment.** All tunnel washers, washer-extractors, dryers, ironers, folders, conveyors, chemical dosing and injection systems, boilers, the wastewater pretreatment system and other trade fixtures and equipment installed by Tenant, including those listed in Exhibit C ("**Tenant's Equipment**"), remain Tenant's property. Tenant may remove them at any time. Tenant shall repair any damage to the Premises that removal causes. Landlord waives any statutory or common-law landlord's lien on Tenant's Equipment and shall, at Tenant's request, deliver a customary landlord waiver to Tenant's lenders.

## 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**8.1 Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 8.2, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

**8.2 Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on written notice to Landlord within fifteen (15) days after the transfer, (a) assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with Tenant, or (b) assign this Lease to a successor by merger or to a purchaser of all or substantially all of Tenant's business. A sale or other transfer of the stock or other equity interests of Tenant, in whole or in part, is not an assignment of this Lease and does not require Landlord's consent.

**8.3 No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees in writing.

## 9. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**9.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured, (b) property insurance covering Tenant's Equipment at full replacement cost, and (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**9.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the building at full replacement cost.

**9.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent covered by property insurance required under this Lease. Each party shall cause its insurers to waive their rights of subrogation accordingly.

**9.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 9.3, each party shall indemnify the other against all third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage at the Property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 10. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**10.1 Casualty.** If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the building with reasonable diligence, and Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered unusable. If restoration cannot reasonably be completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after Landlord notifies Tenant of its estimate of the restoration period.

**10.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that Tenant cannot reasonably continue the Permitted Use, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for Tenant's Equipment and relocation costs.

## 11. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**11.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within ten (10) days after Landlord gives written notice that it is past due; or

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Landlord gives written notice of it. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, there is no Event of Default so long as Tenant begins the cure within that period and diligently completes it.

**11.2 Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises through legal process, and may recover its damages as allowed by Pennsylvania law. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages. Tenant does not confess judgment under this Lease.

**11.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, Tenant may perform it and recover the reasonable cost from Landlord.

## 12. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATES

**12.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later placed on the Property, provided that the mortgagee delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement. Under that agreement, Tenant's possession shall not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default is continuing.

**12.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten (10) business days after either party's written request, the other party shall execute and deliver a certificate addressed to the requesting party and any prospective purchaser, lender or investor it identifies. The certificate shall state (a) whether this Lease is in full force and effect and has been modified, (b) the dates through which Rent has been paid, (c) whether, to the certifying party's knowledge, any default exists under this Lease, and (d) any other factual matters reasonably requested, including whether the certifying party objects to any proposed transfer described in the request. Landlord shall deliver such certificates no more than three (3) times in any calendar year without charge.

## 13. SURRENDER; HOLDING OVER

**13.1 Surrender.** On expiration or termination of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and Landlord's repair obligations excepted. Tenant shall remove Tenant's Equipment under Section 7.4.

**13.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the Term ends without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. Monthly Base Rent during the holdover is one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the last monthly Base Rent in effect.

## 14. GENERAL

**14.1 Notices.** All notices under this Lease shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, or one (1) business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, to the address set out below a party's signature, or to another address a party designates by notice.

**14.2 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises throughout the Term without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

**14.3 Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice, during normal business hours and accompanied by Tenant's representative, to inspect them or perform its obligations. In an emergency, Landlord may enter on such notice as is reasonable. Landlord shall comply with Tenant's reasonable safety and infection-control rules for the plant floor.

**14.4 Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease and shall indemnify the other against any claim by a broker claiming through it.

**14.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**14.6 Successors.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**14.7 Memorandum.** At either party's request, the parties shall execute a memorandum of this Lease in recordable form.

**14.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**14.9 Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

**14.10 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
