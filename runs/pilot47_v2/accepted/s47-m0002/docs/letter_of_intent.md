**Re: Letter of Intent: Proposed Acquisition of Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**"), from **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). We refer to this acquisition as the "**Transaction**."

The Company provides commercial laundry, linen-rental and textile-care services to hospitals, health systems, hotels and hospitality customers (the "**Business**"). Buyer has valued the Business on the basis of the information Seller and the Company have provided to date. Buyer believes the Transaction can be documented and closed on an expedited basis.

Except for Paragraphs 8 through 13, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' current intentions. Paragraph 14 governs the effect of this Letter.

## 1. Structure

Buyer would purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**"), free and clear of all liens. Buyer would acquire the Company on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of working capital. At closing, Seller would pay or cause the Company to pay all indebtedness for borrowed money and all unpaid transaction expenses of the Company, or these amounts would be deducted from the purchase price.

## 2. Purchase Price and Payment

**(a) Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares would be $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to the adjustments described in Paragraph 1.

**(b) Seller Note.** $6,000,000 of the Purchase Price would be paid by Buyer's issuance to Seller of a subordinated promissory note (the "**Seller Note**"). The Seller Note would bear interest at six percent (6%) per annum, payable quarterly. Principal would amortize beginning after the second anniversary of closing and would mature on the fourth anniversary of closing. The Seller Note would be subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing on customary terms. Buyer would have the right to set off indemnifiable losses against amounts owing under it.

**(c) Escrow.** $4,850,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") would be deposited at closing with a nationally recognized escrow agent reasonably acceptable to both parties. The Escrow Amount would secure Seller's indemnification obligations. Remaining escrow funds, less pending claims, would be released to Seller at the end of the survival period for the general representations described in Paragraph 4.

**(d) Cash at Closing.** The balance of the Purchase Price, after the adjustments described in Paragraph 1, would be paid in cash at closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

## 3. Definitive Agreement

The Transaction would be documented in a stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**") and related ancillary agreements, including an escrow agreement, the Seller Note and a transition services agreement, each in form and substance satisfactory to Buyer and Seller. Buyer's counsel would prepare the initial draft of the Definitive Agreement. The Definitive Agreement would contain representations, warranties, covenants, conditions and indemnities customary for transactions of this type and size. These would include representations regarding financial statements, customer contracts and rebate arrangements, intellectual property (including the Company's wash-chemistry formulations and dosing software), environmental matters, employees and taxes.

## 4. Survival and Indemnification

The representations and warranties of Seller other than fundamental representations (the "**General Representations**") would survive closing for 18 months after closing. The representations regarding organization, authority, capitalization, taxes and brokers (the "**Fundamental Representations**") would survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Seller would indemnify Buyer for losses arising from:

(a) breaches of representations, warranties and covenants;

(b) pre-closing taxes;

(c) pre-closing environmental conditions and violations of environmental law; and

(d) unpaid indebtedness and transaction expenses.

Indemnification for breaches of General Representations would be subject to a deductible equal to one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the Purchase Price and a cap equal to the Escrow Amount. The deductible and cap would not apply to Fundamental Representations, fraud or the specific indemnities described above. Losses would be recovered first from the Escrow Amount, then at Buyer's election by set-off against the Seller Note, and then directly from Seller.

## 5. Transition Services

At closing, Seller would provide the Company with billing and collections, route dispatch, customer-portal hosting, payroll and IT support services for a transitional period of up to nine (9) months. Fees would be set at Seller's actual cost. The transition services agreement would contain customary protections for the confidentiality and return of Company and customer data.

## 6. Restrictive Covenants

For three (3) years after closing, Seller and its affiliates would not engage in commercial laundry or linen-rental services in any state in which the Company operates at closing. During the same period, they would not solicit the Company's customers or employees.

## 7. Conditions

Buyer's obligation to close would be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) satisfactory completion of Buyer's business, financial, legal, environmental and intellectual property due diligence;

(b) receipt of all third-party consents that Buyer identifies in due diligence as necessary to close the Transaction;

(c) completion of a Phase I environmental site assessment of the Company's plant premises, at Seller's expense, and resolution satisfactory to Buyer of any matters it identifies;

(d) absence of any material adverse change in the Business;

(e) execution of the Definitive Agreement and the ancillary agreements; and

(f) approval by Buyer's Board of Managers.

Buyer intends to fund the cash portion of the Purchase Price with equity contributions from its members and a senior credit facility. Buyer does not expect the Definitive Agreement to contain a financing condition.

## 8. Due Diligence and Access

From the date of this Letter until the Exclusivity Period ends, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the Company's premises, books, records, contracts, personnel and advisors. Buyer shall coordinate all customer, supplier and landlord contacts through Seller and shall not contact any of them without Seller's prior consent.

## 9. Exclusivity

From the date of this Letter until 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the sixtieth (60th) day after the date of this Letter (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and their respective representatives not to, directly or indirectly:

(a) solicit, encourage or initiate any proposal from any person other than Buyer for the acquisition of the Company, the Shares or any material portion of the Company's assets;

(b) negotiate or furnish information in connection with any such proposal; or

(c) enter into any agreement regarding any such proposal.

Seller shall promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal received during the Exclusivity Period. The Exclusivity Period ends earlier if Buyer notifies Seller in writing that it has ceased pursuing the Transaction.

## 10. Conduct of Business

During the Exclusivity Period, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. In particular, Seller shall cause the Company not to grant customer rebates or pricing concessions, or dispose of material assets, outside the ordinary course.

## 11. Confidentiality

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, are subject to the Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller dated April 14, 2026, which remains in full force and effect.

## 12. Expenses

Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is consummated. The cost of the Phase I environmental site assessment is borne by Seller as described in Paragraph 7(c).

## 13. Governing Law; Miscellaneous

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. This Letter may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic transmission. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument. This Letter may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

## 14. Effect of this Letter

Paragraphs 8 through 13 and this Paragraph 14 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the parties. No other paragraph of this Letter creates any legally binding obligation. Neither party has any obligation regarding the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered, and then only as set out in it. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time for any reason. Termination does not affect any liability for a prior breach of the Binding Provisions.
