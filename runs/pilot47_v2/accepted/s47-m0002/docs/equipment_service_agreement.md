# EQUIPMENT SERVICE AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This EQUIPMENT SERVICE AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Kessling Laundry Systems Service, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation ("**Service Provider**"), and **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates a commercial laundry, linen-rental and textile-care plant at 2200 Keystone Industrial Drive, Bristol Township, Bucks County, Pennsylvania (the "**Facility**").

B. Service Provider is in the business of servicing, maintaining and repairing industrial laundry equipment.

C. Customer wishes to engage Service Provider to provide preventive maintenance, repair and emergency services for certain equipment at the Facility, and Service Provider is willing to do so, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Covered Equipment**" means the equipment listed in Schedule A, as Schedule A may be amended under Section 2.5.

"**Customer Materials**" means all wash programs, chemical formulations, dosing parameters, process settings, software, configuration files, production data and other information of Customer that Service Provider accesses or receives in performing the Services.

"**Emergency Service**" means Service performed in response to a Covered Equipment failure that stops or materially reduces production on a tunnel washer line, a flatwork ironer line, a boiler or the wastewater pretreatment system.

"**Fees**" means the fees set out in Schedule B.

"**Parts**" means replacement parts, components and consumables furnished by Service Provider in performing the Services.

"**Preventive Maintenance**" means the scheduled inspection, adjustment, lubrication, calibration, cleaning and replacement of wear parts described in Schedule A.

"**Services**" means the Preventive Maintenance, repair services and Emergency Service described in Section 2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The word "including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Preventive Maintenance.** Service Provider shall perform Preventive Maintenance on the Covered Equipment at the intervals set out in Schedule A. The parties' service managers shall agree on a schedule each calendar quarter. Service Provider shall schedule work to minimize disruption to Customer's production, including by performing work on weekends or during non-production shifts when Customer reasonably requests.

**2.2 Repair Services.** On Customer's request, Service Provider shall diagnose and repair any malfunction of the Covered Equipment. For a non-emergency request, Service Provider's technician shall arrive at the Facility within two (2) Business Days after the request.

**2.3 Emergency Service.** Service Provider shall make Emergency Service available twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. For an Emergency Service request, Service Provider shall respond by telephone within one (1) hour and shall have a qualified technician on site at the Facility within four (4) hours after the request.

**2.4 Service Reports.** Within two (2) Business Days after each service visit, Service Provider shall deliver to Customer a written report describing the work performed, the Parts installed, the time spent and any recommended further work.

**2.5 Changes to Covered Equipment.** Customer may add equipment to or remove equipment from Schedule A on thirty (30) days' written notice. Fees for added equipment shall be determined at the rates in Schedule B for equipment of comparable type. Fees for removed equipment cease on the effective date of removal.

**2.6 Excluded Work.** The Services do not include repair of damage caused by (a) misuse, abuse or operation of the Covered Equipment other than in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions, (b) fire, flood or other casualty, or (c) modifications or repairs made by any person other than Service Provider or Customer's own maintenance staff. Service Provider shall perform excluded work on Customer's request at the time-and-materials rates in Schedule B.

## 3. STANDARDS OF PERFORMANCE

**3.1 Workmanship.** Service Provider shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the manufacturers' recommended maintenance procedures and generally accepted industry practice, and in compliance with applicable law.

**3.2 Personnel.** Service Provider shall assign technicians trained and qualified to service the Covered Equipment, including licensed boiler technicians where required by law. Customer may require Service Provider to remove from the Facility any technician who fails to comply with Section 3.3, and Service Provider shall promptly provide a qualified replacement.

**3.3 Facility Rules.** While at the Facility, Service Provider personnel shall comply with Customer's safety, lockout/tagout, infection-control and security rules made known to them, including the separation of soiled and clean areas of the plant floor.

**3.4 Parts.** Service Provider shall use new original-equipment-manufacturer Parts or Parts of equal quality, unless Customer approves otherwise in writing.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay Service Provider the Fees set out in Schedule B. The annual Preventive Maintenance fee covers all labor for Preventive Maintenance and the wear parts listed in Schedule A.

**4.2 Repairs and Parts.** Labor for repair services and Emergency Service, and Parts not covered by the annual Preventive Maintenance fee, are charged at the rates in Schedule B. Service Provider shall obtain Customer's written approval before performing any single repair whose estimated cost exceeds $15,000, except where delay would endanger persons or property.

**4.3 Price Adjustment.** On each anniversary of the Effective Date, Service Provider may increase the Fees by no more than the lesser of (a) three percent (3%) and (b) the percentage increase over the preceding twelve (12) months in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), U.S. City Average, published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Service Provider shall give at least sixty (60) days' written notice of any increase.

**4.4 Invoicing and Payment.** The annual Preventive Maintenance fee is invoiced in equal monthly installments in advance. All other Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears, with copies of the relevant service reports. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.5 Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith by giving Service Provider written notice of the dispute in reasonable detail before the payment is due. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days. Service Provider shall continue to perform the Services while a dispute is pending.

**4.6 Taxes.** Customer shall pay any sales, use or similar taxes imposed on the Services or Parts, other than taxes on Service Provider's net income.

## 5. WARRANTY

**5.1 Services Warranty.** Service Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 3.1. If any Service fails to conform to this warranty within ninety (90) days after it is performed, Service Provider shall re-perform it at no charge.

**5.2 Parts Warranty.** Service Provider warrants that Parts will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for one hundred eighty (180) days after installation, or for the manufacturer's warranty period if longer. Service Provider shall replace any defective Part, including the labor to install it, at no charge, and shall pass through to Customer the benefit of all manufacturers' warranties.

**5.3 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 5, SERVICE PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY; CUSTOMER MATERIALS

**6.1 Ownership.** Customer owns all Customer Materials. Service Provider acquires no right, title, license or interest in any Customer Materials under this Agreement.

**6.2 Use and Protection.** Service Provider shall use Customer Materials solely to perform the Services. Service Provider shall hold Customer Materials, and all other non-public information concerning Customer's business, customers, pricing and operations that it receives under this Agreement, in strict confidence. It shall not disclose that information to any person other than its personnel who need to know it to perform the Services and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 6.

**6.3 Chemical Dosing Systems.** In servicing the chemical dosing and injection systems, Service Provider shall not copy, extract, download or retain any wash program, chemical formulation or dosing parameter. It shall not change any of them except at the written direction of Customer's chemical and process engineering personnel.

**6.4 Exceptions.** Section 6.2 does not apply to information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through Service Provider's breach. It also does not apply to disclosures required by law, provided that Service Provider gives Customer prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

**6.5 Return.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, or at any time on Customer's request, Service Provider shall return or destroy all Customer Materials in its possession and certify that it has done so in writing.

## 7. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Insurance.** Throughout the term of this Agreement, Service Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of at least $1,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000; and

(d) umbrella liability insurance with limits of at least $5,000,000.

The general liability, automobile and umbrella policies shall name Customer as an additional insured. Service Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance to Customer on request.

**7.2 Indemnification by Service Provider.** Service Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and related losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Service Provider or its personnel, or by Service Provider's breach of Section 6.

**7.3 Indemnification by Customer.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Service Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and related losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel.

**7.4 Procedures.** The indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, and shall permit the indemnifying party to control its defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on the indemnified party, other than the payment of money funded by the indemnifying party, without the indemnified party's consent.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, or lost profits, arising out of this Agreement. This Section 8.1 does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations under Section 7, Service Provider's breach of Section 6, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**8.2 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed the total Fees paid and payable under this Agreement for that contract year. This Section 8.2 does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations under Section 7, Service Provider's breach of Section 6, a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or Customer's obligation to pay Fees.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years, ending December 31, 2027. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive one-year terms, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the then-current term ends.

**9.2 Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for any reason on ninety (90) days' written notice to Service Provider, without penalty or termination fee.

**9.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it. Customer may also terminate this Agreement on written notice if Service Provider fails to meet the Emergency Service response time in Section 2.3 on three (3) or more occasions in any period of six (6) consecutive months.

**9.4 Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**9.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Customer shall pay all Fees accrued for Services performed through the effective date, and Service Provider shall refund any prepaid Preventive Maintenance fee for periods after that date. Sections 4.5, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.5 and 10 survive expiration or termination.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Independent Contractor.** Service Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Service Provider is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits, supervision and employment taxes of its personnel.

**10.2 Subcontracting.** Service Provider shall not subcontract any of the Services without Customer's prior written consent, except for the use of manufacturers' factory technicians for warranty or specialized repair work. Service Provider remains responsible for the performance of any permitted subcontractor.

**10.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. However, Customer may assign this Agreement without consent to any affiliate, to a successor by merger, or to a purchaser of all or substantially all of its business or of the Facility. A sale or other transfer of the stock or other equity interests of either party is not an assignment of this Agreement and does not require the other party's consent. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**10.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemics, war, terrorism, labor disputes not involving the affected party's own employees, or failure of utilities. The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Customer may obtain substitute services from a third party during any such delay without liability to Service Provider.

**10.5 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the address set out below a party's signature or to another address a party designates by notice.

**10.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
