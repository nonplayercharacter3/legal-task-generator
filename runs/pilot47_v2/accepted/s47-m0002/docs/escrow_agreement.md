# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ostrander Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 20, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,850,000 with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit secures Seller's obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Buyer and Seller wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and disburse that deposit, and the Escrow Agent is willing to act, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $4,850,000.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Escrow Amount together with all interest and earnings on it, less all disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, or an order or judgment for which the time to appeal has expired without an appeal being filed.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set out in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that remain unresolved or unpaid.

"**Release Date**" means the date on which the survival period for the General Representations under Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement expires.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The word "including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Duties Limited.** The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not bound by it. It has no duty to know or interpret the Purchase Agreement. Its duties are solely those expressly set out in this Agreement, and no implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against it.

## 3. ESCROW DEPOSIT

**3.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller (the "**Escrow Account**").

**3.2 Acknowledgment.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day it is received.

**3.3 No Commingling.** The Escrow Agent shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with its own funds or with funds held for any other Person.

## 4. INVESTMENT

**4.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the investment selected by Buyer and Seller in Schedule 2 or as they otherwise direct by Joint Written Instructions. Absent a direction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits.

**4.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement required by this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss, fee or penalty resulting from that liquidation or from any investment made in accordance with this Section 4.

**4.3 Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds and are disbursed with them.

## 5. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Claim Notice.** If Buyer, on behalf of any Buyer Indemnitee, asserts a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver a written notice to the Escrow Agent and Seller (a "**Claim Notice**"). The Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount claimed or, if not yet determinable, a good-faith estimate of that amount.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written objection to the Escrow Agent and Buyer within twenty (20) Business Days after Seller receives the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice shall state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the amount disputed.

**5.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the period in Section 5.2, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, on the next Business Day, the amount set out in the Claim Notice. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer on the next Business Day after receiving the Objection Notice.

**5.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any disputed amount until it receives either (a) Joint Written Instructions directing its disbursement, or (b) a copy of a Final Order determining the disputed claim, together with a certificate of the party delivering it stating that the order is a Final Order. The Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount in accordance with those instructions or that order within two (2) Business Days after receiving them.

## 6. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**6.1 Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions whenever either of them is entitled to a disbursement under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement or this Agreement.

**6.2 Release on Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions directing the Escrow Agent to disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount. The Escrow Agent shall retain the Pending Claim Amount until each related claim is resolved under Section 5. As each such claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for it in accordance with the resolution, and any excess to Seller.

**6.3 Final Disbursement.** When no Escrow Funds remain, this Agreement terminates, except for Sections 7, 8 and 12.

**6.4 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in Schedule 1 for the receiving party, or to another account that party designates by written notice to the Escrow Agent, subject to Section 10.3.

## 7. THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and delivered by the proper party. It has no duty to verify the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**7.2 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**7.3 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is fully protected in any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**7.4 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions concerning the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an action in interpleader in a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 12.6 and deposit the Escrow Funds with that court.

**7.5 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, failure of communications or utility systems, or governmental action.

## 8. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Fees.** Buyer and Seller shall each pay fifty percent (50%) of the Escrow Agent's fees set out in Schedule 3 and of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement.

**8.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to the extent any of these items is caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. As between Buyer and Seller, each bears fifty percent (50%) of any amount paid under this Section 8.2, except that a party whose breach of this Agreement or of the Purchase Agreement caused the amount shall bear it in full.

**8.3 No Set-Off by Escrow Agent.** The Escrow Agent has no right of set-off or lien against the Escrow Funds for its fees, expenses or indemnity, except to the extent Buyer and Seller fail to pay any of them within thirty (30) days after written demand.

**8.4 Survival.** This Section 8 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**9.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**9.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving it at least ten (10) days' written notice.

**9.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which shall be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $250,000,000. Resignation or removal takes effect only when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent transfers the Escrow Funds to it. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one.

## 10. TAX MATTERS; KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER

**10.1 Tax Reporting.** For tax reporting purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds, and all interest and earnings are reportable by Buyer, until disbursed. Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 at or before the Closing.

**10.2 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall each provide to the Escrow Agent all information that it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering, sanctions and customer-identification laws.

**10.3 Security Procedures.** Before any disbursement to an account not listed in Schedule 1, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the payment instructions by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the receiving party listed in Schedule 1. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until that confirmation is completed.

## 11. NOTICES; AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVES

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the addresses set out in Schedule 1. A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only on actual receipt.

**11.2 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller designates the individuals listed in Schedule 1 as its authorized representatives (each, an "**Authorized Representative**"). A party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the other parties.

## 12. GENERAL

**12.1 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all of the parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**12.2 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, succeeds to the Escrow Agent under this Agreement without further act.

**12.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**12.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.

**12.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, The City of New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Funds. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.
