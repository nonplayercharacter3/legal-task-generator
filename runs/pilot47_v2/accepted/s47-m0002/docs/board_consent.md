# TRETORT LINEN HOLDINGS, LLC

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS

**Dated as of September 10, 2026**

The undersigned are all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"). Acting under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and Section 6.8 of the Company's Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "**LLC Agreement**"), they consent to the adoption of the following resolutions without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as a unanimous vote at a duly called meeting of the Board.

## 1. RECITALS

WHEREAS, on July 20, 2026, the Company, as Buyer, entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will purchase all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Pelmont**"). The Board approved the Company's entry into the Purchase Agreement by unanimous written consent dated July 17, 2026 (the "**Prior Consent**");

WHEREAS, under Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement, the aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). Under Section 2.2, a portion of the Purchase Price is to be paid by the Company's issue to Seller of a subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**"). Under Section 2.3, the Company is to deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with Ostrander Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"), under an escrow agreement among the Company, Seller and the Escrow Agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**");

WHEREAS, Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement provides that, at the Closing, Seller and Pelmont will enter into a transition services agreement (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"). Under it, Seller will continue to provide billing, route-dispatch, customer-portal hosting and related administrative services to Pelmont for a transitional period;

WHEREAS, Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to cause a Phase I environmental site assessment of Pelmont's plant premises (the "**Phase I Report**") to be completed and delivered to the Company;

WHEREAS, Seller delivered the Phase I Report to the Company on August 3, 2026. The Phase I Report identifies recognized environmental conditions at Pelmont's plant premises, including conditions associated with the historical use and storage of perchloroethylene in former dry-cleaning operations at the plant, and with historical wastewater discharge practices;

WHEREAS, after review of the Phase I Report by the Company's officers, environmental counsel and environmental consultant, the Company's officers delivered to Seller, on September 4, 2026, a written Environmental Objection Notice under Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement. The notice describes the recognized environmental conditions to which the Company objects (the "**Objection Notice**");

WHEREAS, Seller has informed the Company that it may take the position that the Company has accepted the environmental conditions identified in the Phase I Report, and the Company does not agree with that position;

WHEREAS, under Section 10.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement, either party may terminate the Purchase Agreement if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**");

WHEREAS, the Board has reviewed the current forms of the Escrow Agreement, the Seller Note and the Transition Services Agreement (together, the "**Closing Documents**"). It has also reviewed a summary of the proposed senior credit facility to be entered into by the Company and Pelmont at or about the Closing (the "**Senior Credit Facility**"). The Board has received the advice of the Company's officers and legal counsel regarding these matters; and

WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members to take the actions described below.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. RATIFICATION OF PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**RESOLVED**, that the execution and delivery of the Purchase Agreement by the Company's officers on July 20, 2026, as approved by the Prior Consent, and the Company's performance of the Purchase Agreement since that date, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 3. ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS

**RESOLVED**, that the delivery of the Objection Notice by the Company's officers on September 4, 2026 is ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company disputes any assertion that it has accepted, or is deemed to have accepted, the environmental conditions identified in the Phase I Report. The Company reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement in respect of those conditions, including its rights to indemnification under Section 9.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized to negotiate with Seller regarding the objected matters. These negotiations may cover any Phase II investigation, any remediation before the Closing, any specific indemnity, and any related amendment to the Purchase Agreement. No amendment or waiver that reduces the Company's rights in respect of environmental matters shall be executed without further approval of the Board; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to engage environmental consultants and counsel, at the Company's expense, to the extent they consider advisable in connection with these matters.

## 4. CLOSING DOCUMENTS

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of each of the Escrow Agreement and the Seller Note, substantially in the forms presented to the Board, and the Company's performance of its obligations under each of them, are approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board acknowledges the form of the Transition Services Agreement, to be entered into at the Closing by Seller and Pelmont, as presented to the Board, and approves the Company's causing Pelmont to enter into and perform it after the Closing;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Company, each Closing Document to which the Company is a party, and each certificate and instrument required to be delivered by the Company under Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Each Closing Document may include such changes as the executing Authorized Officer approves, and that approval is conclusively evidenced by his or her execution of it. No change may increase the Purchase Price, the principal amount of the Seller Note or the Escrow Amount; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to cause the Company to pay, at the Closing, the amounts required under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement and to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent.

## 5. FINANCING

**RESOLVED**, that the Company's entry into the Senior Credit Facility, on terms substantially consistent with the summary presented to the Board, is approved. The aggregate principal amount of the Senior Credit Facility shall not exceed $30,000,000 without further approval of the Board;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to negotiate, execute and deliver the credit agreement, security agreements, guaranties, intercreditor and subordination agreements and other documents relating to the Senior Credit Facility, in each case in the name and on behalf of the Company; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to request, and to call for, the capital contributions from the Company's members contemplated by Section 4.2 of the LLC Agreement, in the amount required, together with the proceeds of the Senior Credit Facility, to fund the Company's obligations at the Closing.

## 6. CLOSING

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to take all actions they consider necessary or advisable to satisfy the conditions in Article VII of the Purchase Agreement and to consummate the Closing as promptly as practicable, and in any event before the Outside Date;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers shall report to the Board on the status of the Closing conditions and the matters described in Section 3 of this consent at least every two (2) weeks until the Closing; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that upon the Closing, the following individuals are designated as the Company's nominees to serve as directors of Pelmont, and the Authorized Officers are each authorized to execute, as sole stockholder of Pelmont, a written consent electing them: [●], [●] and [●].

## 7. GENERAL AUTHORITY

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers, and each of them acting alone, are authorized to execute and deliver all further agreements, certificates, instruments and documents, to make all filings, to pay all fees and expenses, and to take all further actions they consider necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any manager or officer of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions, to the extent consistent with them, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that this consent may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic transmission. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument. This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
