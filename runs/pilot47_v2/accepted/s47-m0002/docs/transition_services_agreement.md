# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as Seller, and Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 20, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates provided certain billing, route-dispatch, customer-portal and related administrative services to Recipient. Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that those services continue for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information of or relating to Recipient or its customers that Provider receives, processes, hosts or generates in performing the Services. Recipient Data includes customer lists, contact details, contract terms, pricing, rebate and credit information, invoices, delivery schedules, route data, linen inventory and usage data, and portal user credentials.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 2.5.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set out in Schedule A.

"**Service Term**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the date set out for that Service in Schedule A, as that period may be extended or shortened under Section 3.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.4.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The word "including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in a manner, scope, volume, priority and quality substantially consistent with the way they were performed for Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, (b) with at least the same degree of care Provider uses for its own operations, and (c) in compliance with applicable Law.

**2.3 Systems and Access.** Provider shall continue to make available to Recipient the billing platform, dispatch and routing software, and customer-portal hosting environment used to provide the Services before the Closing, including the Recipient-facing portal at its existing web address. Provider shall give Recipient's designated personnel the access to those systems reasonably necessary to receive and use the Services.

**2.4 Additional Services.** If Recipient reasonably requests services that Provider or its Affiliates provided to Recipient before the Closing but that are not listed in Schedule A, Provider shall negotiate in good faith to provide them ("**Additional Services**"). Fees for Additional Services shall be determined on the same cost basis as the Service Fees. Additional Services become Services once the parties agree to them in writing.

**2.5 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint an individual to act as its primary contact for the Services (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least every two (2) weeks during the Service Term to review performance and migration progress.

**2.6 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall cooperate reasonably and in good faith with Recipient and Buyer to migrate each Service, and all related Recipient Data, to Recipient's or Buyer's own systems or to a third-party provider designated by Recipient, by the end of the applicable Service Term. Migration assistance includes delivery of Recipient Data in the format described in Section 6.4, knowledge-transfer sessions with Provider personnel, and parallel running of systems for up to thirty (30) days.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Term, unless terminated earlier under Section 10.

**3.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Term for any Service once, by up to ninety (90) days, by giving Provider written notice at least thirty (30) days before the Service Term ends. Service Fees during any extension shall be the Service Fees in Schedule A plus ten percent (10%).

**3.3 Early Termination of a Service.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, on at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Service Fees for a terminated Service cease on the effective date of termination. If a Service depends on another Service, Provider shall promptly tell Recipient so that Recipient can terminate both at the same time.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees for each Service in Schedule A. The Service Fees are intended to equal Provider's actual cost of providing the Services, without mark-up, and include all personnel, licensing and hosting costs.

**4.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs paid to third parties to provide the Services, other than costs already reflected in the Service Fees. These costs must be approved in advance by Recipient's Service Coordinator if they exceed $10,000 in any month.

**4.3 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Fees and pass-through costs by Service. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.4 Disputes.** Recipient may withhold payment of any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith, by giving Provider written notice of the dispute in reasonable detail before the payment is due. The Service Coordinators shall attempt to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days. Provider shall continue to perform the Services while any dispute is pending.

**4.5 Taxes.** Recipient shall pay any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the Services, other than Taxes on Provider's net income.

## 5. PERSONNEL

**5.1 Provider Personnel.** Provider shall assign personnel with appropriate skill and experience to perform the Services. To the extent reasonably practicable, Provider shall use the same personnel who performed the Services before the Closing.

**5.2 Control.** Provider's personnel remain employees of Provider or its Affiliates. Provider is responsible for their compensation, benefits, supervision and employment taxes.

**5.3 Recipient Policies.** When on Recipient's premises or using Recipient's systems, Provider personnel shall comply with Recipient's reasonable safety, security and information-technology policies made known to them.

## 6. RECIPIENT DATA; CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Ownership.** As between the parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider acquires no right, title or interest in Recipient Data under this Agreement.

**6.2 Use.** Provider shall use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services.

**6.3 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for Recipient Data at least as protective as those in place before the Closing and consistent with industry standards. Provider shall notify Recipient in writing within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Recipient Data, and shall cooperate reasonably with Recipient's investigation and remediation.

**6.4 Return of Data.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Service Term, and at any other time on Recipient's request, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a complete copy of the Recipient Data relating to that Service, in a commonly used, machine-readable format. After Recipient confirms receipt, Provider shall delete all Recipient Data relating to that Service from its systems and certify the deletion in writing, except for back-up copies deleted in the ordinary course.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** Each party shall hold in confidence all confidential information of the other party it receives under this Agreement, including, in the case of Provider, all Recipient Data. Neither party shall disclose that information except to its employees and advisors who need to know it for purposes of this Agreement, or as required by Law. This Section 6.5 does not apply to information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through the receiving party's breach. This Section 6.5 supplements, and does not limit, Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 No Transfer.** Except as provided in Section 6.1, each party retains all of its Intellectual Property in the systems, software and materials it uses under this Agreement.

**7.2 License to Recipient.** Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Service Term to use the systems, software and materials made available under Section 2.3, solely to receive and use the Services.

**7.3 Third-Party Software.** If Provider needs a third party's consent to provide any Service to Recipient after the Closing, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain it. Provider shall bear the cost of obtaining it, up to $25,000 in the aggregate; Recipient shall bear any excess.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other under this Agreement for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, or lost profits, except for damages arising from a breach of Section 6, fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**8.2 Cap.** Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under it, except for liability arising from a breach of Section 6, fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of (a) Provider's breach of Section 6, or (b) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel in performing the Services.

**9.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of Recipient's gross negligence or willful misconduct in using the Services.

**9.3 Procedures.** The claims procedures in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement apply to claims under this Section 9, with references to the Indemnified Party and the Indemnifying Party read accordingly.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it. Provider may not terminate for non-payment of any amount Recipient is disputing in good faith under Section 4.4.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the date of expiration or termination. Sections 2.6, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10.2 and 11 survive expiration or termination.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Provider go to Seller's address under the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Recipient go to Buyer's address under the Purchase Agreement, marked for the attention of Recipient.

**11.2 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.

**11.3 Assignment.** Provider may assign this Agreement or delegate any Services, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Provider. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemics, war, terrorism, labor disputes or failure of utilities. The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Recipient may terminate an affected Service without liability if the delay continues for more than fifteen (15) days.

**11.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns, except that Buyer may enforce Recipient's rights under it.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.9 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
