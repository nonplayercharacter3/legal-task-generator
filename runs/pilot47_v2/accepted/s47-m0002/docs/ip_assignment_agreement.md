# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 15, 2021 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Odile Fenwright**, an individual ("**Assignor**"), and **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor founded the Company. Before and after its formation, Assignor personally developed certain wash-chemistry formulations and process-control and dosing software used in the Company's commercial laundry, linen-rental and textile-care business.

B. Assignor wishes to assign, and the Company wishes to acquire, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to that intellectual property, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Assigned IP**" means, collectively, (a) the Formulas, (b) the Dosing Software, (c) all Intellectual Property Rights in the Formulas and the Dosing Software, and (d) all improvements, modifications and derivative works of any of the foregoing made by Assignor before the Effective Date. The Assigned IP includes all goodwill associated with it, all rights to sue for and recover damages for past, present and future infringement or misappropriation, and all income, royalties and payments due or payable on or after the Effective Date.

"**Change of Control**" means any transaction or series of related transactions, whether by sale, transfer, merger, consolidation, issuance or otherwise, as a result of which any Person or group of Persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting stock of the Company. A Change of Control also includes any sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company.

"**Dosing Software**" means the software known internally as "PelDose," in source code and object code form, including all versions, modules, algorithms, interfaces, databases, configuration files and documentation. PelDose controls the automated metering and injection of detergents, alkalis, bleaches, sours and softeners into tunnel washers and washer-extractors.

"**Formulas**" means the wash-chemistry formulations, recipes, wash programs, temperature and pH profiles, chemical ratios and related process specifications developed by Assignor for the cleaning, disinfection and finishing of healthcare and hospitality linens. The Formulas include those described in Schedule A, together with all laboratory notebooks, test data and supporting documentation.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all patents, patent applications, copyrights, copyright registrations, trade secrets, know-how, confidential information, trademarks, service marks, software rights, database rights and all other intellectual property and proprietary rights of any kind throughout the world, whether registered or unregistered.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The word "including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Subject to Section 8.3, Assignor irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys to the Company, and the Company accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens, security interests and encumbrances.

**2.2 Delivery.** Within ten (10) days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to the Company all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP in Assignor's possession or control. These include all source code, object code, build scripts, documentation, laboratory notebooks and formulation records.

**2.3 Recordation.** The Company may record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment consistent with it, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office or any other governmental authority.

## 3. CONSIDERATION

**3.1 Payment.** In consideration of the assignment in Section 2.1, the Company shall pay Assignor $350,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds within five (5) business days after the Effective Date.

**3.2 Sufficiency.** Assignor acknowledges that the payment in Section 3.1, together with the other covenants of the Company in this Agreement, constitutes full and adequate consideration for the Assigned IP. Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, no further royalties or other amounts are payable to Assignor for the Assigned IP.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES; MORAL RIGHTS

**4.1 Further Assurances.** At the Company's request and expense, Assignor shall execute and deliver any further instruments, and take any further actions, that the Company reasonably requests to perfect, record, register, maintain, enforce or defend the Company's rights in the Assigned IP.

**4.2 Attorney-in-Fact.** If Assignor fails to execute any instrument described in Section 4.1 within fifteen (15) days after the Company's written request, Assignor appoints the Company and its authorized officers as Assignor's attorney-in-fact, solely to execute and file that instrument. This appointment is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor waives, and agrees not to assert, any moral rights or rights of attribution or integrity she may have in the Assigned IP.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**5.1 By Assignor.** Assignor represents and warrants to the Company that:

(a) Assignor is the sole author, inventor and owner of the Assigned IP;

(b) Assignor has not previously assigned, licensed or granted any right in the Assigned IP to any Person;

(c) the Assigned IP is free of any lien, security interest or encumbrance;

(d) to Assignor's knowledge, the Assigned IP does not infringe or misappropriate the Intellectual Property Rights of any Person;

(e) the Dosing Software does not incorporate any open-source software under terms that would require disclosure or licensing of its source code; and

(f) Assignor has taken commercially reasonable measures to protect the confidentiality of the Formulas and the source code of the Dosing Software.

**5.2 By the Company.** The Company represents and warrants to Assignor that it is duly organized and in good standing under the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. It also represents that it has taken all corporate action necessary to authorize the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Assignor's Obligations.** From and after the Effective Date, Assignor shall hold the Formulas, the source code of the Dosing Software and all other confidential elements of the Assigned IP in strict confidence. Assignor shall not use or disclose them except for the benefit of the Company and as the Company authorizes in writing.

**6.2 Exceptions.** Section 6.1 does not apply to information that becomes generally available to the public other than through Assignor's breach. It also does not apply to disclosures required by law, provided that Assignor gives the Company prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

## 7. COVENANTS OF THE COMPANY

**7.1 Maintenance.** The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the confidentiality of the Formulas and the source code of the Dosing Software as trade secrets.

**7.2 Attribution.** The Company may, but is not required to, identify Assignor as the original developer of the Formulas and the Dosing Software in internal technical documentation.

**7.3 Notice of Certain Transactions.** The Company shall give Assignor written notice of any proposed Change of Control at least thirty (30) days before it is consummated.

## 8. TERM; REACQUISITION RIGHTS

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date. It continues in effect for so long as any Intellectual Property Rights in the Assigned IP subsist.

**8.2 Reversion on Dissolution.** If the Company dissolves, liquidates or winds up its business, or permanently ceases to use the Assigned IP in the conduct of its business for a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months, then on Assignor's written request all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP shall be reassigned to Assignor. This Section 8.2 does not apply to a sale of the Company or its business as a going concern.

**8.3 Reacquisition on Change of Control.** If the Company undergoes a Change of Control without Assignor's prior written consent, Assignor may reacquire the Assigned IP from the Company for a purchase price of one dollar ($1.00). Assignor may exercise this right by delivering written notice to the Company at any time within twelve (12) months after the consummation of the Change of Control. Within ten (10) days after receiving that notice, the Company shall execute and deliver all instruments necessary to assign to Assignor all of the Company's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP. It shall also deliver to Assignor all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens, security interests and encumbrances.

**8.4 Effect of Reacquisition.** On any reassignment under Section 8.2 or 8.3, the Company's rights in the Assigned IP terminate. The Company shall then cease all use of the Assigned IP, except as Assignor otherwise agrees in writing. Sections 6, 8.4 and 9 survive any reassignment.

## 9. GENERAL

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the address set out below a party's signature or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**9.2 Assignment of this Agreement.** The Company may not assign this Agreement without Assignor's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. Assignor may not assign this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent, except that Assignor's rights under Sections 8.2 and 8.3 pass to her heirs and estate. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**9.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.5 Equitable Relief.** Each party acknowledges that a breach of Section 2, 6 or 8 would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The non-breaching party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

**9.6 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.7 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Assigned IP. It supersedes all prior understandings between them on that subject.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
