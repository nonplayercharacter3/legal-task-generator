Ladies and Gentlemen:

Reference is made to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 20, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). The Purchase Agreement covers all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. This letter sets out certain additional agreements between Seller and Buyer that relate to the Closing.

## 1. Key Employee Retention Bonuses

**1.1 Retention Pool.** Before the date of this letter, Seller caused the Company to enter into retention bonus letters with the employees listed in Annex 1 (the "**Key Employees**"). The aggregate amount payable under those letters is $600,000 (the "**Retention Pool**"). Each Key Employee's allocation is set out in Annex 1.

**1.2 Payment Timing.** Under its retention bonus letter, each Key Employee is entitled to the following, provided the Key Employee remains employed by the Company on the applicable payment date:

(a) fifty percent (50%) of the Key Employee's allocation on the Closing Date; and

(b) the remaining fifty percent (50%) on the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date.

**1.3 Closing Installment.** Any amount of the Retention Pool payable on the Closing Date, together with the employer portion of any related payroll Taxes, is an unpaid transaction expense of the Company for purposes of Section 2.4(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Post-Closing Installment.** Seller shall pay the Company, in immediately available funds, the amount of each post-Closing installment, together with the employer portion of any related payroll Taxes. Seller shall make this payment no later than five (5) Business Days before the installment is payable. The Company shall pay the installment to the Key Employee through its regular payroll, less applicable withholding.

**1.5 Forfeited Amounts.** If a Key Employee forfeits any post-Closing installment, Seller has no obligation to fund the forfeited amount. A Key Employee forfeits by ceasing to be employed by the Company before the installment is payable, other than through a termination by the Company without cause. Any amount Seller has already funded for that installment shall be returned to Seller within ten (10) Business Days.

## 2. Directors' and Officers' Tail Coverage

**2.1 Purchase of Tail Policy.** Before the Closing, Seller shall obtain, at Seller's expense, a prepaid "tail" policy under the Company's existing directors' and officers' liability insurance policy. The tail policy shall have the following terms:

(a) a claims-reporting period of six (6) years after the Closing Date;

(b) coverage for acts or omissions occurring at or before the Closing; and

(c) coverage limits, retentions and terms no less favorable than those of the existing policy.

**2.2 Maintenance.** After the Closing, Buyer shall not cause the Company to cancel or amend the tail policy in any manner adverse to the insured persons.

## 3. Replacement of Seller Credit Support

**3.1 Scheduled Credit Support.** Annex 2 lists each guaranty, performance bond, letter of credit and other credit support that Seller or any of its Affiliates has provided for the obligations of the Company under customer contracts (the "**Seller Credit Support**"). These include the performance bonds that support the Company's linen-rental contracts with health-system customers.

**3.2 Replacement.** Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the Seller Credit Support to be released, or replaced with credit support provided by Buyer or the Company, at or as promptly as practicable after the Closing. Buyer's obligation is limited as follows:

(a) Buyer is not required to pay any consideration to any counterparty, other than customary fees for issuing replacement instruments; and

(b) Buyer is not required to agree to any amendment of the underlying customer contract.

**3.3 Interim Period.** Until each item of Seller Credit Support is released or replaced, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates from and against all amounts drawn or paid under that item. This indemnity applies only to the extent the draw or payment arises from an obligation of the Company that accrues after the Closing.

## 4. Insurance Claims for Pre-Closing Occurrences

**4.1 Access to Seller Policies.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts, at Buyer's request and expense, to pursue claims on the Company's behalf under Seller's occurrence-based general liability and automobile liability policies. This applies to policies under which the Company was an insured before the Closing, and only to claims arising from occurrences before the Closing.

**4.2 Proceeds.** Seller shall promptly remit to the Company any proceeds it receives on those claims, net of any deductible or retention borne by Seller.

## 5. General

**5.1 Covenants.** Seller's obligations under Sections 1, 2 and 4 of this letter are covenants of Seller under the Purchase Agreement for purposes of Section 9.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Buyer's obligations under Sections 2.2 and 3 of this letter are covenants of Buyer under the Purchase Agreement for purposes of Section 9.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Effect on Purchase Agreement.** Except as set out in this letter, the Purchase Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

**5.3 Termination.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1 of the Purchase Agreement before the Closing, this letter terminates automatically at the same time.

**5.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and Buyer. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**5.5 Assignment.** Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this letter as if it were set out here in full.

**5.6 Governing Law; Forum.** This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Sections 11.8 and 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this letter as if they were set out here in full.

**5.7 Counterparts.** This letter may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic transmission. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
