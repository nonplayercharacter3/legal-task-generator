# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**

**Issue Date:** [●], 2026

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its permitted successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal sum of **SIX MILLION DOLLARS ($6,000,000)**, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2 of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 20, 2026 between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Purchase Agreement concerns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. As used in this Note:

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus two percent (2%) per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 7.

"**Interest Rate**" means six percent (6%) per annum.

"**Losses**" means out-of-pocket damages actually paid or payable by a Buyer Indemnitee that are finally awarded by a non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction in favor of that Buyer Indemnitee and against Holder on a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, excluding attorneys' fees, consultants' fees, costs of investigation, sampling, monitoring, removal, remediation or other environmental response, diminution in value, lost profits, and any amount agreed or settled between Maker and Holder that has not been reduced to such a judgment.

"**Maturity Date**" means the fourth (4th) anniversary of the Issue Date.

"**Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such day to occur at least ninety (90) days after the Issue Date.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness, obligations and liabilities of Maker or the Company to the Senior Lender under the Senior Loan Documents, including principal, interest (including interest accruing after the filing of any insolvency proceeding), fees, expenses and indemnities.

"**Senior Lender**" means the lender or lenders, and any agent for them, under the Senior Loan Documents.

"**Senior Loan Documents**" means the credit agreement entered into by Maker and the Company at or about the Closing to finance the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, the related security and guaranty documents, and any refinancing, replacement or amendment of any of them.

## 2. INTEREST

**2.1 Accrual.** Interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the Issue Date at the Interest Rate. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Maker shall pay accrued and unpaid interest in arrears on each Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

**2.3 Default Interest.** While any Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) or 7.1(c) continues, the unpaid principal balance bears interest at the Default Rate.

**2.4 Maximum Rate.** No interest charged under this Note may exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. PRINCIPAL

**3.1 Amortization.** Beginning with the first Payment Date after the second (2nd) anniversary of the Issue Date, Maker shall pay principal in equal quarterly installments of $375,000 on each Payment Date.

**3.2 Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, subject to the Senior Loan Documents. Each prepayment is applied first to accrued interest and then to principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 4. PAYMENTS

**4.1 Manner.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing to Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the relevant payment.

**4.2 Business Days.** If any payment is due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue until paid.

**4.3 Application.** Except as provided in Section 3.3, payments are applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 9.2, then to accrued interest, and then to principal.

## 5. SET-OFF

**5.1 Right of Set-Off.** Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under this Note the amount of any Losses. Any amount set off under this Section 5.1 is treated as a payment under this Note on the date of the set-off, applied first to the next scheduled installments of interest and principal in order of maturity.

**5.2 Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any set-off under Section 5.1 no later than the date on which the payment affected by it is due. The notice shall identify the judgment on which the set-off is based and state the amount set off.

**5.3 No Other Set-Off.** Except as provided in this Section 5, Maker shall make all payments under this Note without set-off, counterclaim or deduction.

**5.4 Effect.** No exercise of the right of set-off under this Section 5 in good faith constitutes an Event of Default.

## 6. SUBORDINATION

**6.1 Subordination.** The payment of principal, interest and all other amounts under this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set out in this Section 6.

**6.2 Permitted Payments.** Maker may make regularly scheduled payments of interest and principal under Sections 2 and 3 unless a payment or financial covenant default under the Senior Loan Documents has occurred and is continuing and the Senior Lender has given Maker and Holder written notice of it (a "**Blockage Notice**"). Following a Blockage Notice, Maker shall make no payment under this Note until the earlier of (a) the cure or waiver of the default, and (b) one hundred eighty (180) days after the Blockage Notice is delivered. No more than one Blockage Notice may be delivered in any period of three hundred sixty (360) consecutive days.

**6.3 Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or reorganization of Maker, the Senior Lender is entitled to receive payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt before Holder receives any payment under this Note.

**6.4 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment under this Note in violation of this Section 6, Holder shall hold it in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly pay it over to the Senior Lender.

**6.5 Intercreditor Agreement.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute a subordination or intercreditor agreement with the Senior Lender on customary terms consistent with this Section 6.

**6.6 Set-Off Not Restricted.** Nothing in this Section 6 limits Maker's rights under Section 5.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, other than a payment that is prohibited by Section 6.2 or is set off under Section 5;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(c) Maker commences any voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has an involuntary case commenced against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(d) the Senior Lender accelerates the Senior Debt following an event of default under the Senior Loan Documents, and the acceleration is not rescinded.

**7.2 Remedies.** On the occurrence and during the continuance of an Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the unpaid principal balance of this Note and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. On an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c), that balance and interest become immediately due without notice. Holder's exercise of remedies is subject in all respects to Section 6.

## 8. COVENANTS OF MAKER

**8.1 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any Event of Default within five (5) Business Days after an authorized officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

**8.2 Information.** Until this Note is paid in full, Maker shall deliver to Holder, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, a certificate of an authorized officer of Maker stating the unpaid principal balance of this Note and the amount of any set-off made under Section 5 during that fiscal year.

## 9. GENERAL

**9.1 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note.

**9.2 Costs of Collection.** If an Event of Default occurs and Holder obtains a final judgment against Maker for amounts due under this Note, Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable costs of collection awarded by the court.

**9.3 Assignment.** Holder may not assign, transfer, pledge or participate this Note, or any interest in it, without Maker's prior written consent. Maker may assign its obligations under this Note to any Affiliate that assumes them in writing, but no assignment relieves Maker of its obligations. Any purported transfer in violation of this Section 9.3 is void. This Note binds and benefits Maker and Holder and their permitted successors and assigns.

**9.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. No delay in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of it.

**9.5 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, to the addresses of Buyer and Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**9.8 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Note. EACH OF MAKER AND HOLDER WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**9.9 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor. Before it does so, Holder shall deliver an affidavit of loss and an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker.

**9.10 Cancellation.** When all amounts due under this Note have been paid or satisfied in full, including by set-off under Section 5, Holder shall surrender this Note to Maker marked "Paid in Full."
