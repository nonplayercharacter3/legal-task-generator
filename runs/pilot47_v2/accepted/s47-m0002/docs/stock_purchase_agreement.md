# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 20, 2026, by and between **Drusisk Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Tretort Linen Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). It concerns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Pelmont Textile Care, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. The Company is engaged in commercial laundry, linen-rental and textile-care services for hospitals, health systems, hotels and hospitality customers (the "**Business**").

B. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver the Shares to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase the Shares from Seller, free and clear of all Liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price is payable as provided in this Article II.

**2.2 Seller Note.** At the Closing, Buyer shall issue to Seller a subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**"), substantially in the form of Exhibit B. The principal amount of the Seller Note is applied against the Purchase Price.

**2.3 Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold and disburse the Escrow Amount under the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX.

**2.4 Closing Payment.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to:

(a) the Purchase Price; *less*

(b) the principal amount of the Seller Note; *less*

(c) the Escrow Amount; *less*

(d) the amount of all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company outstanding at Closing, which Buyer shall pay on the Company's behalf to the holders under customary payoff letters; *less*

(e) all unpaid transaction expenses of the Company, which Buyer shall pay on the Company's behalf.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amounts so withheld are treated as paid to the Person entitled to them.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller (and, in the case of the Transition Services Agreement, by the Company);

(c) the Required Consents;

(d) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(e) a certificate of good standing of the Company dated within five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(g) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) to Seller, the payment required by Section 2.4;

(b) to Seller, the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;

(c) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount;

(d) to Seller, the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and

(e) to Seller, the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of organization. Each has all requisite corporate power to own its assets and carry on its business as now conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. There are no options, warrants, convertible securities or other rights to acquire capital stock of the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company or any Law applicable to them.

**4.5 Governmental Consents.** No consent, approval or filing with any Governmental Authority is required in connection with this Agreement, other than filings that, if not made, would not have a Material Adverse Effect.

**4.6 Subsidiaries.** The Company has no subsidiaries and holds no equity interest in any other Person.

**4.7 Financial Statements.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule contains the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the fiscal years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet as of June 30, 2026 and related statements for the six months then ended. These financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company for the dates and periods indicated. The Company's fiscal year ends on December 31.

**4.8 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except those reflected or reserved against in the June 30, 2026 balance sheet and those incurred in the ordinary course of business since that date.

**4.9 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course and there has been no Material Adverse Effect.

**4.10 Material Contracts.** Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Company involving annual payments in excess of $250,000. Each such contract is valid and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.11 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all intellectual property used in the Business, including its wash-chemistry formulations, dosing and process-control software, and customer portal. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**4.12 Customer Contracts and Rebates.** Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists the Company's twenty-five (25) largest customers by revenue for fiscal year 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026. It also lists all rebate, volume-discount, most-favored-pricing and linen-loss credit arrangements with those customers. All rebates and credits accrued through the date of the most recent balance sheet are properly reflected in the financial statements described in Section 4.7. No such customer has given written notice of termination, non-renewal or a material reduction in volume.

**4.13 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. The Lease is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and the Company holds a valid leasehold interest under it.

**4.14 Environmental Matters.** The Company is, and has been for the past five (5) years, in material compliance with all Environmental Laws. It holds and complies with all permits required under Environmental Laws, including wastewater discharge permits. To Seller's knowledge, there has been no release of Hazardous Materials at, on or under the Company's premises that would reasonably be expected to require Remediation Costs.

**4.15 Employees.** The Company is in material compliance with all applicable Laws relating to employment, wages and hours, and occupational safety. No labor union represents any employees of the Company, except as set out in Section 4.15 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.16 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed and has paid all Taxes due. No Tax audit or proceeding is pending or threatened against the Company.

**4.17 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to their distribution in violation of securities Laws.

**5.4 Financing.** At the Closing, Buyer will have sufficient funds to pay the amounts required by Section 2.4.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, which Buyer shall not unreasonably withhold, Seller shall cause the Company not to:

(a) issue, sell or redeem any equity securities;

(b) incur indebtedness for borrowed money other than under existing facilities;

(c) enter into, materially amend or terminate any contract listed in Section 4.10 or 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule;

(d) grant any rebate, credit or pricing concession to any customer outside the ordinary course; or

(e) sell, license or otherwise dispose of any material asset, including any intellectual property.

**6.2 Access.** Until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the Company's premises, books, records and personnel.

**6.3 Efforts.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VII and to consummate the Closing as promptly as practicable.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the Required Consents before the Closing, at Seller's expense.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and their representatives not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement regarding any sale of the Company, the Shares or a material portion of the Company's assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business, including customer lists, pricing and route information. This obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach.

**6.7 Environmental Assessment.** Seller shall cause the Phase I Report to be completed and delivered to Buyer promptly after the date of this Agreement, at Seller's expense. Buyer may object to any recognized environmental condition or other matter identified in the Phase I Report by delivering a written notice to Seller (an "**Environmental Objection Notice**") within fifteen (15) Business Days after Seller delivers the Phase I Report. The Environmental Objection Notice shall describe the objected matters in reasonable detail. If Buyer does not deliver an Environmental Objection Notice within that period, Buyer is deemed to have accepted the environmental conditions identified in the Phase I Report. Buyer also waives any right to indemnification under Section 9.2(d) for those conditions. If Buyer timely delivers an Environmental Objection Notice, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for ten (10) Business Days regarding any Phase II investigation, remediation before Closing, or a specific indemnity for the objected matters.

**6.8 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller shall enter into, and shall cause the Company to enter into, the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.9 Notification.** Until the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that causes or would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.10 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the business of commercial laundry or linen rental services in any state in which the Company provides services at the Closing. During the same period, they shall not solicit any customer or employee of the Company.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to no Law or order of any Governmental Authority being in effect that restrains, enjoins or prohibits the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Buyer, of the following conditions:

(a) *Representations.* The Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects. All other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date.

(b) *Covenants.* Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing.

(c) *Officer's Certificate.* Buyer shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), (b) and (e) have been satisfied.

(d) *Required Consents.* Required Consents means the following, each of which shall have been obtained and delivered to Buyer in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer: an estoppel certificate from the Landlord, dated no earlier than fifteen (15) days before the Closing Date. The estoppel certificate shall confirm that the Lease is in full force and effect, that no default exists under it, and that the Landlord does not object to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

(e) *No Material Adverse Effect.* Since the date of this Agreement, no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred.

(f) *Deliveries.* Seller shall have made each delivery required by Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Seller, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the matters in Sections 7.3(a) and (b), and the deliveries required by Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller is responsible for and shall pay all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date. Seller is also responsible for the portion of any straddle period ending on the Closing Date, determined on a closing-of-the-books basis.

**8.2 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, consistent with past practice. Seller shall submit each such return to Buyer for review and comment at least twenty (20) days before filing.

**8.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate reasonably in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

**8.4 Transfer Taxes.** Seller and Buyer shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of any transfer, documentary, sales, use or stamp Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations survive the Closing until nine (9) months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties survive in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed. No claim for breach of any representation or warranty may be made after the expiration of its survival period. However, any claim asserted by written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail before that expiration survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes for which Seller is responsible under Article VIII;

(d) any presence or release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or migrating from any premises owned, leased or operated by the Company that existed or occurred before the Closing, or any violation of Environmental Law by the Company before the Closing, in each case subject to Section 6.7; and

(e) any indebtedness or transaction expenses of the Company not paid at or before the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) *Deductible.* Seller has no liability under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations until the aggregate Losses exceed $242,500. Once that amount is exceeded, Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of it.

(b) *Cap.* Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of General Representations shall not exceed $4,850,000. The limitations in Sections 9.4(a) and (b) do not apply to Losses arising from Fundamental Representations, fraud or Sections 9.2(b) through (e). Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) *Financial Statement and Customer Claims.* No Buyer Indemnitee may assert any claim under Section 9.2(a) for breach of Section 4.7 (Financial Statements) or Section 4.12 (Customer Contracts and Rebates) until Buyer has delivered to Seller audited financial statements of the Company for the first full fiscal year beginning after the Closing Date. Buyer shall cause those audited financial statements to be delivered within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of that fiscal year.

(d) *Mitigation; Insurance.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses. Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received, less costs of recovery.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice of the claim to the party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. For any third-party claim, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by giving written notice within twenty (20) days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnifying Party may not settle a third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.6 Set-Off.** Buyer may set off the amount of any Losses for which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification under this Article IX, including Losses consisting of diminution in value, attorneys' fees or Remediation Costs. The set-off may be made against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note. Buyer shall give Seller written notice of the set-off describing the claim in reasonable detail. If the claim is finally determined to be less than the amount set off, Buyer shall pay the difference to Seller, with interest at the rate under the Seller Note, within ten (10) Business Days after that determination.

**9.7 Source of Recovery.** Losses payable by Seller under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied in the following order:

(a) first, from the Escrow Amount, under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) second, at Buyer's election, by set-off under Section 9.6; and

(c) third, directly from Seller.

Seller and Buyer shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent as required by the Escrow Agreement to give effect to this Article IX.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement. This Section 9.8 does not limit claims for fraud or for specific performance or injunctive relief.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**"). This right is not available to a party whose breach of this Agreement caused the failure to close by the Outside Date;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination under Section 10.1, this Agreement becomes void with no liability of any party. However, this Section 10.2 and Article XI survive termination, and no termination relieves any party from liability for willful breach before termination.
