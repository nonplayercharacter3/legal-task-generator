**Re: Certificates of Insurance for Lockwen Lift Services, Inc. Following Change of Ownership**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

## 1. Purpose of This Letter

1.1 Pellucid Risk Partners LLC ("**Pellucid**") acts as insurance broker to Lockwen Lift Services, Inc. (the "**Company**"). We understand that Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC ("**Stipuv**") acquired all of the outstanding stock of the Company from Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc. under a Stock Purchase Agreement that closed on June 30, 2026.

1.2 At the Company's request, we enclose updated certificates of insurance that reflect the change of ownership. They also add Stipuv as a certificate holder and, where the policy terms allow, as an additional insured.

## 2. Enclosed Certificates

2.1 We enclose the following documents:

(a) ACORD 25 Certificate of Liability Insurance, covering commercial general liability, business automobile liability, umbrella/excess liability and workers' compensation and employers' liability;

(b) ACORD 25 Certificate of Liability Insurance, covering contractor's professional and pollution liability;

(c) evidence of New York State Disability Benefits and Paid Family Leave coverage (Form DB-120.1); and

(d) evidence of New York State Workers' Compensation coverage (Form C-105.2).

2.2 The policies, carriers, policy numbers, policy periods and limits are as stated on each certificate. Each policy listed was in force on June 30, 2026 and remains in force on the date of this letter. All premiums billed to date have been paid.

## 3. Additional Insured and Certificate Holder Status

3.1 The carriers have endorsed the commercial general liability and umbrella/excess policies to add Stipuv as an additional insured. The endorsement applies on a primary and non-contributory basis with respect to the Company's operations, as provided in the endorsements listed on the certificates.

3.2 The business automobile policy has been endorsed in the same way, as respects liability arising from autos owned, hired or used by the Company.

3.3 Stipuv is named as certificate holder on each enclosed certificate. Notice of cancellation will be delivered in accordance with the policy provisions.

## 4. Customer and Owner Certificates

4.1 Many of the Company's maintenance, repair, inspection and modernization contracts require the Company to name building owners, managing agents or general contractors as additional insureds. We will continue to issue those certificates on request in the ordinary course.

4.2 Requests should be sent to our certificates desk at certs@pellucidrisk.com. Please include a copy of the relevant contract insurance requirements with each request.

4.3 Certificates issued for the Company's customers before the Closing remain valid for the stated policy periods. Those certificates do not need to be reissued because of the change of ownership.

## 5. Status of Certificates

5.1 Each certificate is issued as a matter of information only. It confers no rights on the certificate holder.

5.2 A certificate does not amend, extend or alter the coverage provided by the policies described in it. Coverage is subject to all the terms, conditions and exclusions of those policies.

5.3 Copies of the policies and endorsements are available on request.
