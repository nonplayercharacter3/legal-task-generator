# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 30, 2026 by and between **Lockwen Lift Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Darnell Okafor-Wyse**, an individual ("**Executive**"). **Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Parent**"), joins this Agreement solely for the purposes of Sections 5(d) and 13.

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), Parent is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company from Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc. ("**Seller**"). Delivery of this Agreement, duly executed by Executive, is a Closing deliverable under Section 3.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

B. Executive is currently employed by the Company as its Director of Field Operations. In that role Executive has substantial knowledge of the Company's field personnel, maintenance routes, customer relationships and code compliance practices.

C. The Company wishes to continue Executive's employment after the Closing, and Executive wishes to accept that continued employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business**" means the inspection, maintenance, repair and modernization of elevators, escalators and related vertical transportation equipment.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date. If the Closing does not occur, this Agreement shall be void and of no effect.

"**Restricted Territory**" means the State of New York and each county in the States of New Jersey and Connecticut in which the Company performed services under any customer contract during the twelve (12) months before the Date of Termination.

## 2. Term

(a) *Initial Term.* The employment of Executive under this Agreement shall begin on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of two years from the Closing Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 6.

(b) *Renewal.* After the Initial Term, the term shall renew automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

## 3. Position and Duties

(a) *Position.* Executive shall serve as Director of Field Operations of the Company and shall report to the President of the Company, or to such other officer as the Chief Executive Officer of Parent may designate.

(b) *Duties.* Executive shall be responsible for the direction and supervision of the Company's field service, inspection, testing, repair and modernization operations. These include scheduling of mechanic crews, oversight of periodic inspections and Category 1 and Category 5 testing, filings with the New York City Department of Buildings and the New York State Department of Labor, field safety programs and coordination with the Company's union-represented workforce. Executive shall perform such other duties consistent with that position as the Company may reasonably assign.

(c) *Licenses.* Executive shall maintain in good standing, at the Company's expense, each professional license and certification Executive holds on the Effective Date that is used in the Business, including any elevator agency director or elevator mechanic license, and shall promptly notify the Company of any notice of suspension, revocation or disciplinary proceeding relating to any such license.

(d) *Full Time and Attention.* Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time and attention to the Company. Executive may manage personal investments and participate in civic, charitable and industry association activities, provided those activities do not materially interfere with Executive's duties or violate Section 8 or Section 9.

(e) *Location.* Executive's principal place of work shall be the Company's offices in the New York metropolitan area, subject to reasonable travel to job sites and customer premises.

## 4. Compensation

(a) *Base Salary.* The Company shall pay Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $245,000 ("**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Base Salary shall be reviewed at least annually and may be increased, but not decreased, at the Company's discretion.

(b) *Annual Bonus.* For each fiscal year during the Term, Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target amount of thirty percent (30%) of Base Salary. The bonus shall be based on the achievement of performance objectives established by the Company after consultation with Executive, which shall include objectives relating to field productivity, customer retention, code compliance and safety. The bonus for fiscal year 2026 shall be prorated from the Effective Date. Any bonus earned shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates, subject to Executive's continued employment through December 31 of the year to which it relates.

(c) *Retention Bonus.* The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $150,000, in two equal installments of $75,000 each, payable within thirty (30) days after the first anniversary and the second anniversary of the Effective Date, subject in each case to Executive's continued employment through the applicable anniversary, except as provided in Section 7(b).

## 5. Benefits and Expenses

(a) *Benefit Plans.* Executive shall be eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans and programs the Company makes available to its similarly situated management employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

(b) *Paid Time Off.* Executive shall be entitled to twenty (20) days of paid vacation per calendar year, prorated for partial years, in addition to Company holidays, in accordance with Company policy.

(c) *Vehicle and Expenses.* The Company shall provide Executive with the use of a Company vehicle and mobile device for business purposes, and shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing Executive's duties, in accordance with Company policy.

(d) *Incentive Equity.* Parent shall recommend to its Board of Managers a grant to Executive of profits interests in Parent under Parent's incentive equity plan, on terms and subject to vesting conditions no less favorable than those applicable to other members of the Company's senior management, with the grant to be considered within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date.

## 6. Termination

(a) *Death or Disability.* Executive's employment shall terminate automatically upon Executive's death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment upon Executive's Disability. "**Disability**" means Executive's inability, due to physical or mental incapacity, to perform the essential functions of Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period, as determined in accordance with applicable law.

(b) *By the Company for Cause.* The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means Executive's (i) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (ii) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of Executive's duties that causes material harm to the Company; (iii) willful falsification of any inspection, test or filing record submitted to any Governmental Authority; (iv) material breach of Section 8 or Section 9; or (v) willful and continued failure to perform Executive's material duties after written notice from the Company. Before any termination under clause (ii), (iv) or (v), the Company shall give Executive written notice describing the conduct and, if it is curable, thirty (30) days to cure.

(c) *By the Company without Cause.* The Company may terminate Executive's employment without Cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

(d) *By Executive for Good Reason.* Executive may terminate employment for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means, without Executive's written consent, (i) a material reduction in Base Salary or target bonus; (ii) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or (iii) relocation of Executive's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles. Executive must give the Company written notice of the Good Reason condition within sixty (60) days after its initial occurrence, the Company shall have thirty (30) days to cure, and Executive must resign within thirty (30) days after the end of the cure period if the condition is not cured.

(e) *By Executive without Good Reason.* Executive may resign without Good Reason upon sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period, in which case it shall pay Executive Base Salary for the waived portion.

(f) *Date of Termination.* "**Date of Termination**" means the date on which Executive's employment ends under this Section 6.

## 7. Payments on Termination

(a) *Accrued Obligations.* Upon any termination, the Company shall pay Executive (i) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the Date of Termination, (ii) accrued but unused vacation to the extent required by Company policy or applicable law, (iii) unreimbursed business expenses, and (iv) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans in accordance with their terms (the "**Accrued Obligations**").

(b) *Severance.* If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall, in addition to the Accrued Obligations and subject to Section 7(c): (i) continue to pay Executive's Base Salary for twelve (12) months after the Date of Termination; (ii) pay any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year that remains unpaid; (iii) pay any unpaid installment of the retention bonus under Section 4(c); and (iv) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay the employer portion of the premiums for that coverage for up to twelve (12) months.

(c) *Release.* The payments under Section 7(b) are conditioned on Executive's execution, and non-revocation, of a general release of claims in favor of the Company, Parent and their Affiliates in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, which must become effective within sixty (60) days after the Date of Termination, and on Executive's continued compliance with Sections 8 and 9.

(d) *Other Terminations.* Upon termination for Cause, by reason of death or Disability, by Executive without Good Reason, or by expiration of the Term following notice of non-renewal, Executive shall be entitled only to the Accrued Obligations.

## 8. Confidential Information

(a) Executive shall not, during the Term or at any time afterward, use or disclose any Confidential Information except as required in the performance of Executive's duties or by law. "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, Parent and their Affiliates, including customer lists, contract terms and pricing, renewal dates, building and equipment data, inspection and test records, maintenance routes, labor costs, bidding strategies and financial information.

(b) Upon termination of employment, Executive shall promptly return to the Company all Company property and all documents and data containing Confidential Information.

(c) Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any Governmental Authority, from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws, or from disclosing information about unlawful acts in the workplace. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), Executive shall not be held liable for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed filing in a lawsuit.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

(a) *Non-Competition.* During the Term and for twelve (12) months after the Date of Termination, Executive shall not, within the Restricted Territory, directly or indirectly serve as an officer, director, manager, employee, consultant or owner of any Person engaged in the Business, other than passive ownership of less than two percent (2%) of a publicly traded company.

(b) *Non-Solicitation of Customers.* During the Term and for eighteen (18) months after the Date of Termination, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any customer of the Company with which Executive had material contact during the twenty-four (24) months before the Date of Termination to obtain services competitive with the Business, or to terminate or reduce its business with the Company.

(c) *Non-Solicitation of Employees.* During the Term and for eighteen (18) months after the Date of Termination, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or hire any employee of the Company, or any individual who was an employee of the Company within the six (6) months before the solicitation, to leave the employ of the Company. General advertisements not targeted at Company employees shall not breach this Section 9(c).

(d) *Reasonableness; Reformation.* Executive acknowledges that these covenants are reasonable in duration, geography and scope and are necessary to protect the Company's goodwill and Confidential Information, including the goodwill acquired by Parent under the Purchase Agreement. If a court determines that any covenant is unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

(e) *Tolling.* The post-termination periods in this Section 9 shall be extended by any period during which Executive is in breach of this Section 9.

## 10. Work Product

All work product, procedures, schedules, software configurations and other materials created by Executive in the course of employment shall be the sole property of the Company. Executive hereby assigns to the Company all rights in that work product and shall execute any documents reasonably requested to confirm that ownership.

## 11. Remedies

Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 8 or Section 9 would cause the Company irreparable harm. The Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, without the posting of a bond.

## 12. Section 409A

This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each payment under this Agreement shall be treated as a separate payment. Any payment conditioned on a termination of employment that constitutes deferred compensation shall be paid only upon a "separation from service." If the release consideration period spans two calendar years, payments conditioned on the release shall begin in the second calendar year.

## 13. Parent Acknowledgment

Parent acknowledges this Agreement and agrees to perform its obligations under Section 5(d). Parent shall have the right to enforce Sections 8 through 11 as a third-party beneficiary.

## 14. General Provisions

(a) *Withholding.* All payments under this Agreement shall be subject to applicable tax withholding.

(b) *Assignment.* This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by Executive. The Company may assign this Agreement to Parent, to any Affiliate of Parent, or to any successor to all or substantially all of the business of the Company.

(c) *Notices.* Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by email with confirmation of transmission, or by nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices to the Company or Parent shall be sent to Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC, 1200 Avenue of the Americas, 22nd Floor, New York, New York 10036, Attention: Chief Executive Officer. Notices to Executive shall be sent to the most recent home address in the Company's records.

(d) *Governing Law; Jurisdiction.* This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

(e) *Amendment and Waiver.* This Agreement may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company.

(f) *Severability.* Subject to Section 9(d), if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

(g) *Survival.* Sections 7 through 14 shall survive the termination of Executive's employment.

(h) *Counterparts.* This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.
