# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$7,500,000**
**Dated: June 30, 2026**
**New York, New York**

THIS SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE (this "**Note**") is issued by **Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), to **Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026, between Seller and Maker (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. "**Holder**" means Seller and any other Person who from time to time holds this Note.

## 1. Promise to Pay; Principal Amount

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, Maker promises to pay to the order of Holder the principal sum of **$7,500,000** (SEVEN MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS), or so much of it as remains outstanding, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, in the amounts, at the times and in the manner set forth in this Note. This Note is the Seller Note referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is delivered as part of the Purchase Price for the Shares.

## 2. Payment Schedule; Interest

(a) *Principal Installments.* Maker shall pay the principal of this Note in three (3) annual installments as follows:

| Installment | Payment Date | Principal Amount |
|---|---|---|
| First | December 31, 2027 | $2,500,000 |
| Second | December 31, 2028 | $2,500,000 |
| Third | December 31, 2029 (the "**Maturity Date**") | $2,500,000, or all remaining principal |

(b) *Interest.* The unpaid principal balance of this Note shall bear interest from the date of this Note at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Accrued and unpaid interest shall be payable in arrears on each principal installment date set out in Section 2(a) and on the Maturity Date.

(c) *Default Interest.* While an Event of Default is continuing, the outstanding principal balance shall bear interest at a rate equal to two percent (2.0%) per annum above the rate otherwise applicable under Section 2(b).

(d) *Maturity.* All outstanding principal and accrued and unpaid interest shall be due and payable in full on the Maturity Date.

## 3. Manner of Payment

(a) All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account that Holder designates in writing to Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable payment date.

(b) If any payment date falls on a day that is not a Business Day, the payment shall be due on the next Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue until that payment is made.

(c) Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal.

## 4. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon not less than five (5) Business Days' prior written notice to Holder. Each partial prepayment shall be applied to the principal installments in inverse order of maturity, together with interest accrued on the prepaid amount.

## 5. Right of Set-Off

(a) Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest payable to Seller under this Note any amount to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement. This right applies only to indemnification claims that a Buyer Indemnitee has timely asserted by a Claim Notice delivered under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

(b) If an indemnification claim described in Section 5(a) remains unresolved on a payment date, Maker may withhold from the payment due to Seller on that date an amount equal to Maker's good-faith estimate of the Losses claimed. Maker shall pay the withheld amount, together with interest at the rate under Section 2(b), to Seller within ten (10) Business Days after final resolution of the claim, to the extent the claim is resolved in favor of Seller.

(c) Maker shall give Seller written notice of each set-off or withholding under this Section 5 no later than the applicable payment date, identifying the related Claim Notice and the amount set off or withheld.

(d) Any amount set off under this Section 5 shall be treated as a payment of principal and interest under this Note in the amount set off, applied in the order set forth in Section 3(c). The exercise in good faith of the right of set-off under this Section 5 shall not constitute an Event of Default.

## 6. Subordination

(a) The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all Senior Indebtedness. "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker or the Company for borrowed money owed to any bank, institutional lender or other financial institution, whether outstanding on the date of this Note or incurred later, including all principal, interest, fees and expenses relating to those obligations.

(b) If a payment default under any Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing, or the maturity of any Senior Indebtedness has been accelerated, Maker shall make no payment under this Note until that default has been cured or waived or the accelerated Senior Indebtedness has been paid in full. Interest shall continue to accrue under this Note during any such period.

(c) Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute a subordination agreement with any holder of Senior Indebtedness on terms customary for seller financing in transactions of this type and consistent with this Section 6.

## 7. Information Rights

Until this Note has been paid in full, Maker shall deliver, or cause the Company to deliver, to Holder:

(a) within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of the end of that month and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and the year to date, certified by the chief financial officer of Maker as fairly presenting in all material respects the financial condition of the Company;

(b) within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, a schedule of the Company's Material Customer Contracts and other maintenance, service, repair, inspection and modernization contracts, showing for each contract the counterparty, the building location, the annual contract value, the expiration date and the renewal or termination notice date;

(c) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the annual financial statements of the Company, reviewed by an independent certified public accountant; and

(d) promptly after any officer of Maker obtains knowledge of it, written notice of any Event of Default or any event that, with notice or lapse of time, would constitute an Event of Default.

## 8. Transfer and Assignment

(a) Holder may sell, assign, transfer, pledge or negotiate this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker. Holder shall give Maker written notice of any transfer within ten (10) Business Days after it is made, stating the name and notice address of the transferee.

(b) Upon surrender of this Note to Maker in connection with a transfer of part of this Note, Maker shall execute and deliver to the transferee and the transferor new notes in the same form as this Note in principal amounts that together equal the unpaid principal balance of this Note.

(c) Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, except to a successor to all or substantially all of the assets of Maker that assumes this Note in writing.

(d) This Note shall be binding upon Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and shall inure to the benefit of Holder and its successors and assigns.

## 9. Events of Default

Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, and that failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder, other than any amount set off or withheld in good faith under Section 5;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other obligation under this Note, and that failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder;

(c) Maker or the Company makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy, or has an order for relief entered against it in an involuntary bankruptcy case that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(d) Maker sells or otherwise disposes of all or substantially all of the Shares or of the assets of the Company, unless this Note is paid in full at or before that disposition.

## 10. Remedies

(a) Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default described in Section 9(c), all unpaid principal and accrued interest under this Note shall automatically become due and payable without notice or demand. Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare all unpaid principal and accrued interest immediately due and payable.

(b) The remedies of Holder under this Section 10 are subject to Section 6.

(c) Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 11. Waivers

Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of protest of this Note. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note shall operate as a waiver of that right. No waiver of any right on one occasion shall be a waiver of that right on any other occasion. No amendment or waiver of any provision of this Note shall be effective unless in writing and signed by Maker and Holder.

## 12. Notices

All notices under this Note shall be in writing and shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, addressed as follows, or to such other address as a party designates by notice:

If to Maker: Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC, 1200 Avenue of the Americas, 22nd Floor, New York, New York 10036, Attention: Chief Executive Officer.

If to Seller: Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc., 410 Park Avenue South, 14th Floor, New York, New York 10016, Attention: General Counsel.

If to any other Holder: to the address stated in the notice of transfer delivered under Section 8(a).

## 13. Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver

(a) This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the law of any other jurisdiction.

(b) Maker irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Note.

(c) MAKER AND, BY ITS ACCEPTANCE OF THIS NOTE, HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 14. Miscellaneous

(a) *Usury Savings.* In no event shall the interest payable under this Note exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any interest paid in excess of that maximum shall be applied to reduce principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

(b) *Lost Note.* Upon receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note, and of an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker, Maker shall execute and deliver a replacement note of like tenor.

(c) *Severability.* If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

(d) *Headings.* The headings in this Note are for reference only and do not affect its interpretation.
