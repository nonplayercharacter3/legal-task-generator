# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of June 30, 2026, by and between **Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock (the "**Shares**") of **Lockwen Lift Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in the inspection, maintenance, repair and modernization of elevators, escalators and related vertical transportation equipment in the State of New York (the "**Business**").

B. Seller desires to sell to Buyer, and Buyer desires to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

C. The parties are signing this Agreement and consummating the Closing on the same date.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS; PURCHASE AND SALE

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Accounting Principles**" means GAAP, applied on a basis consistent with the accounting principles, practices, methodologies and policies used in preparing the Financial Statements, as further described in Schedule 1.1(a).

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means (a) the current assets of the Company, less (b) the current liabilities of the Company, in each case determined as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date in accordance with the Accounting Principles and the illustrative calculation set forth in Schedule 1.1(b), and excluding cash, Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses and income Tax assets and liabilities.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under the laws of the State of New York by a party in the making of the representations and warranties set forth in Article 4 or Article 5.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties set forth in Section 4.1 (Organization and Qualification), Section 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 4.3 (Capitalization) and Section 4.20 (Brokers).

"**GAAP**" means United States generally accepted accounting principles, consistently applied.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local government or political subdivision thereof, or any agency, department, board or instrumentality thereof, including the New York City Department of Buildings and the New York State Department of Labor.

"**Indebtedness**" means, without duplication, all obligations of the Company for borrowed money, capital leases, deferred purchase price of property or services (other than trade payables incurred in the ordinary course), and all accrued interest, prepayment premiums and penalties thereon.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, but excluding punitive damages except to the extent actually paid to a third party.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Seller Guarantees**" means the parent performance guarantees, payment guarantees, surety bonds, performance bonds and other credit support issued, procured or provided by Seller or any of its Affiliates (other than the Company) for the benefit of any building owner, managing agent, general contractor or other counterparty under any elevator maintenance, service, repair or modernization contract to which the Company is a party, including those listed in Schedule 6.8.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the original principal amount of $7,500,000 issued by Buyer to Seller at the Closing pursuant to Section 2.2(b).

"**Target Working Capital**" means $3,100,000.

"**Transaction Documents**" means this Agreement, the Seller Note, the Key Employee Agreement between the Company and Darnell Okafor-Wyse, and each other agreement, certificate and instrument delivered at the Closing pursuant to this Agreement.

"**Transaction Expenses**" means all fees and expenses incurred by the Company or Seller in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, to the extent unpaid as of the Closing.

**1.2 Purchase and Sale of Shares.** At the Closing, upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests and other encumbrances.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as set forth in Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay and deliver the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) an amount in cash equal to $41,000,000, less Closing Indebtedness and less Transaction Expenses (the "**Closing Cash Payment**"), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer, in the original principal amount of $7,500,000; and

(c) on behalf of the Company, the amounts set forth in the payoff letters for Closing Indebtedness and the invoices for Transaction Expenses, to the payees thereof.

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** Not less than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller delivered to Buyer a good-faith written estimate of Closing Working Capital, Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses, together with reasonable supporting detail (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**"). If the estimated Closing Working Capital exceeded the Target Working Capital, the Closing Cash Payment was increased by the amount of the excess. If it was less than the Target Working Capital, the Closing Cash Payment was reduced by the amount of the shortfall.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within forty-five (45) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good-faith calculation of Closing Working Capital, Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses, prepared in accordance with the Accounting Principles, together with reasonable supporting documentation. Buyer shall cause the Company to give Seller and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the books and records of the Company for the purpose of preparing the Closing Statement.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with any item in the Closing Statement, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written notice of disagreement (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within 30 days after Buyer's receipt of the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis therefor, with reasonable supporting documentation. If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within that period, the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the parties. Any item not specifically disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement shall be final and binding.

(c) *Resolution of Disputes.* During the thirty (30) days after delivery of a timely Notice of Disagreement, Seller and Buyer shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed items. Any items still in dispute at the end of that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national or regional standing mutually agreed by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. It shall decide only the disputed items, within the range of values asserted by the parties, based solely on the written submissions of the parties and the terms of this Agreement, and shall deliver its written determination within thirty (30) days after its engagement. That determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be borne by Seller and Buyer in inverse proportion to the relative extent to which each prevails on the disputed items.

(d) *Payment of Adjustment.* If the Closing Working Capital, as finally determined, exceeds the estimate used in the Estimated Closing Statement, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If it is less than that estimate, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. Corresponding adjustments shall be made for Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses. Any payment under this Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the deduction and withholding was made.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on June 30, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank; (b) the written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (c) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness; (d) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the New York Department of State dated within ten (10) days before the Closing Date; (e) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Darnell Okafor-Wyse; (f) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller; and (g) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors authorizing this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the Closing Cash Payment; (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the payments contemplated by Section 2.2(c); and (d) a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of managers authorizing this Agreement and the Seller Note.

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Qualification.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York. The Company has all requisite corporate power and authority to own and operate its properties and to carry on the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws and general principles of equity.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 1,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. There are no options, warrants, convertible securities or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the certificate of incorporation or bylaws of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, require the consent of or notice to any Person under any Material Customer Contract.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of May 31, 2026 and the related statements for the five-month period then ended (collectively, the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates, subject, in the case of the interim statements, to normal year-end adjustments and the absence of notes.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except (a) those reflected or reserved against in the May 31, 2026 balance sheet, (b) those incurred in the ordinary course of business since that date, none of which results from a breach of contract or violation of law, and (c) Transaction Expenses.

**4.7 Absence of Certain Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has operated the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and there has not been any event or condition that has had a material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.8 Title to Assets; Vehicles and Equipment.** The Company has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of its tangible assets, including the service vehicles, testing equipment and tools listed in Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule, free and clear of encumbrances other than those to be released at the Closing.

**4.9 Material Customer Contracts.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each maintenance, service, repair, inspection and modernization contract of the Company with annual revenue in excess of $150,000 or with a remaining term of more than one year (each, a "**Material Customer Contract**"). Each Material Customer Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of or default under any Material Customer Contract. No counterparty has given written notice of termination, non-renewal or a material reduction in scope. All prepaid maintenance fees under the Material Customer Contracts are set forth in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.10 Real Property.** The Company does not own any real property. Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each lease of real property to which the Company is a party.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all laws applicable to the Business.

**4.12 Elevator Code Compliance and Permits.** The Company holds all licenses, permits, registrations and certifications required to conduct the Business, including the elevator agency director and elevator mechanic licenses and approvals required by the New York City Department of Buildings and the New York State Department of Labor (collectively, "**Permits**"), all of which are listed in Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule and are in full force and effect. All inspection, maintenance, repair and testing work performed by the Company since January 1, 2023 has been performed in compliance in all material respects with ASME A17.1, the New York City Building Code, the New York City Fire Code and all other applicable elevator safety codes. All periodic inspections and Category 1 and Category 5 tests for which the Company was responsible have been timely performed and filed. There are no outstanding violations, notices of violation or civil penalties issued to the Company by any Governmental Authority with respect to the Business.

**4.13 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.14 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all income and other material Tax returns required to be filed and has paid all Taxes shown as due on such returns. There are no Tax liens on any assets of the Company other than for Taxes not yet due and payable.

**4.15 Employees and Labor.** Section 4.15 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Company and his or her title and base compensation. The Company is party to the collective bargaining agreement with Local 1 of the International Union of Elevator Constructors described in that Section and is in compliance in all material respects with that agreement.

**4.16 Employee Benefit Plans.** Section 4.16 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each such plan has been operated in compliance in all material respects with its terms and applicable law.

**4.17 Insurance.** Section 4.17 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each insurance policy maintained by or for the benefit of the Company. All such policies are in full force and effect and all premiums due have been paid.

**4.18 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all applicable environmental laws, including those relating to the handling and disposal of hydraulic fluids and lubricants.

**4.19 Affiliate Transactions.** Except for the Seller Guarantees and as set forth in Section 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule, there are no contracts or arrangements between the Company, on the one hand, and Seller or any of its Affiliates, on the other hand.

**4.20 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any brokerage, finder's or other fee in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Access to Records.** For a period of six (6) years after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing and to give Seller reasonable access to them, upon reasonable notice and for any reasonable business purpose.

**6.2 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business, except to the extent such information becomes generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach or is required to be disclosed by law.

**6.3 Non-Competition.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the inspection, maintenance, repair or modernization of elevators or escalators in the State of New York, or own or manage any Person so engaged, other than passive ownership of less than two percent (2%) of a publicly traded company.

**6.4 Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, (a) solicit for employment any employee of the Company, or (b) solicit any customer under a Material Customer Contract to terminate or reduce its business with the Company.

**6.5 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

**6.6 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

**6.7 Intercompany Arrangements.** Effective as of the Closing, all intercompany accounts between the Company, on the one hand, and Seller and its Affiliates, on the other hand, have been settled or terminated without further liability to the Company, other than the Seller Guarantees.

**6.8 Seller Guarantees.** Following the Closing, Buyer shall use reasonable efforts to cause the Company to obtain the release of Seller and its Affiliates from the Seller Guarantees, including by offering to the beneficiaries of the Seller Guarantees replacement guarantees or bonds from Buyer or the Company. Until each Seller Guarantee is released, Seller shall maintain that Seller Guarantee in effect in accordance with its terms. Buyer shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, extend, renew or amend any contract supported by a Seller Guarantee in a manner that increases Seller's obligations under that Seller Guarantee without Seller's prior written consent.

**6.9 Insurance.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall reasonably cooperate with Buyer and the Company in pursuing claims arising from pre-Closing occurrences under any occurrence-based insurance policy of Seller under which the Company was an insured.

## ARTICLE 7. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**7.1 Continuation of Employment.** Buyer shall cause the Company to continue the employment of each employee of the Company immediately following the Closing on terms consistent with the collective bargaining agreement described in Section 4.15, where applicable.

**7.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article 7 shall confer any rights on any employee or other Person, or constitute an amendment of any employee benefit plan.

## ARTICLE 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Pre-Closing Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare and file, at Seller's expense, all income Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

**8.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Shares shall be borne equally by Seller and Buyer.

**8.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE 9. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. All other representations and warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until six (6) months after the Closing Date. The covenants and agreements of the parties that by their terms are to be performed at or after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. No claim may be brought in respect of a representation or warranty after its survival date, except a claim for which a Claim Notice was delivered in accordance with Section 9.4 before that survival date, which shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article 9, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, managers, officers and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller contained in Article 4;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller contained in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes of the Company attributable to any taxable period, or portion thereof, ending on or before the Closing Date, to the extent not included as a liability in Closing Working Capital; and

(d) any Closing Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing or deducted in determining the Purchase Price.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article 9, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, directors, officers and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer contained in Article 5, or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer contained in this Agreement; and

(b) any Seller Guarantee, including any demand, draw or claim made by any beneficiary under any Seller Guarantee and any payment or performance by Seller or any of its Affiliates thereunder, whether arising before, on or after the Closing Date.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) *Claim Notice.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for indemnification under this Article 9 (a "**Claim Notice**"), describing the claim in reasonable detail and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses. A Claim Notice relating to a representation or warranty must be delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period set forth in Section 9.1. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party by written notice delivered within twenty (20) days after receipt of the Claim Notice. The indemnified party may participate in that defense at its own expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any third-party claim without the indemnified party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

(c) *Direct Claims.* The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of a Claim Notice for a direct claim to respond in writing. If it does not respond within that period, it shall be deemed to have rejected the claim, and the indemnified party may pursue any remedy available under this Agreement.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller shall be liable under Section 9.2(a), and the aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer shall be liable under Section 9.3, shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000).

(b) *Basket.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses in respect of all claims thereunder exceed $485,000, in which event Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(c) *Exclusions.* The limitations set forth in Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud or from any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, and the limitations in this Section 9.5 shall not apply to Losses under Section 9.3(b). The aggregate liability of Seller for breaches of Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Insurance and Tax Benefits.* The amount of any Losses shall be reduced by any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of those Losses, net of the costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

(e) *No Double Recovery.* No indemnified party shall be entitled to recover more than once for the same Loss, including to the extent that Loss was taken into account in the final determination of Closing Working Capital.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, other than claims for Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.11, and disputes governed by Section 2.4.

**9.7 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article 9 against any amount of principal or interest then payable by Buyer to Seller under the Seller Note, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note. The exercise of that right of set-off in good faith shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note. Set-off against the Seller Note is in addition to, and not in limitation of, any other remedy available to the Buyer Indemnitees under this Article 9.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article 9 shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.
