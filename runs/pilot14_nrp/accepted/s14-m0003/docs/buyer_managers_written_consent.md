# STIPUV VERTICAL HOLDINGS, LLC

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS

**Dated: September 14, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Stipuv Vertical Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**" or "**Buyer**"), act by written consent without a meeting. They do so under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and Section 6.7 of the Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Company (the "**LLC Agreement**"). The undersigned waive all notice requirements and consent to the adoption of the following recitals and resolutions.

## 1. RECITALS

**A. The Acquisition.** The Company, as Buyer, and **Braveirt Elevator Group, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company acquired all of the issued and outstanding shares of **Lockwen Lift Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Lockwen**"). The Closing occurred on June 30, 2026, for a Purchase Price of $48,500,000. The Purchase Price was paid partly in cash and partly by delivery of the Seller Note in the original principal amount of $7,500,000. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**B. Receipt of the Closing Statement.** Under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered to the Company the Closing Statement. The Closing Statement sets out Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital, Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses. The Company received the Closing Statement on August 4, 2026.

**C. Proposed Dispute of Closing Working Capital.** The Closing Statement reflects Closing Working Capital in excess of the estimate used in the Estimated Closing Statement. The resulting surplus of $1,240,000 would be payable by the Company to Seller under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement. The Company's finance team and its outside accounting advisors have reviewed the Closing Statement and the supporting documentation delivered with it. They have reported to the Board that Seller excluded from current liabilities the unearned deferred revenue attributable to prepaid maintenance fees under Lockwen's maintenance and service contracts, including the Material Customer Contracts. Those prepaid fees relate to maintenance services to be performed by Lockwen after the Closing. In the view of management and its advisors, the Accounting Principles require that the unearned portion be recorded as a current liability. If that liability is included, Closing Working Capital is reduced and the claimed surplus is eliminated or substantially reduced. The Company has not yet delivered a Notice of Disagreement to Seller under Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**D. Board Determination.** The Board has reviewed the Closing Statement, the report of management and the Purchase Agreement. It has determined that disputing the Closing Statement and taking the related actions described below are advisable and in the best interests of the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions:

## 2. RESOLUTIONS

**Resolution 1. Acknowledgment of Review.** RESOLVED, that the Board acknowledges and ratifies the review of the Closing Statement conducted by the Company's officers, finance team and outside accounting advisors, and all actions taken by them before the date of this consent in connection with that review.

**Resolution 2. Notice of Disagreement.** RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to prepare, execute and deliver to Seller a Notice of Disagreement under Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Notice of Disagreement shall dispute the Closing Working Capital set out in the Closing Statement and the $1,240,000 surplus claimed by Seller. Its stated basis shall be the exclusion from current liabilities of unearned deferred revenue attributable to prepaid maintenance contracts. The Authorized Officers may include in the Notice of Disagreement any other item they determine, with the advice of counsel and the Company's accounting advisors, should be disputed. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for it, with reasonable supporting documentation, and shall be delivered in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**Resolution 3. Negotiation and Independent Accountant.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to negotiate in good faith with Seller to resolve the disputed items under Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement. If any item remains in dispute at the end of the negotiation period, the Authorized Officers may agree with Seller on, and engage, an Independent Accountant and prepare and submit the Company's written submissions to it. Any settlement of the disputed items that results in a payment by the Company to Seller of more than $250,000 shall require the further approval of the Board.

**Resolution 4. Engagement of Advisors.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to engage, or continue the engagement of, legal counsel and accounting advisors to assist the Company in connection with the Notice of Disagreement and any proceedings under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement, on terms the Authorized Officer considers reasonable.

**Resolution 5. Adjustment Payment.** RESOLVED, that no payment shall be made to Seller under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement in respect of the Closing Statement except upon the final determination of Closing Working Capital and with the approval of an Authorized Officer, and any such payment shall be made from the Company's operating funds by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**Resolution 6. Reporting.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers shall report to the Board on the status of the Notice of Disagreement and any related negotiations or proceedings at each regular meeting of the Board, and promptly upon any material development.

**Resolution 7. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver all documents, give all notices and take all other actions that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the foregoing resolutions, and that all actions previously taken by any officer or manager of the Company consistent with the purposes of these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved.

## 3. GENERAL

**3.1 Effectiveness.** This consent shall be effective as of the date first written above when it has been signed by all of the members of the Board, and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

**3.2 Counterparts.** This consent may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be effective as originals.
