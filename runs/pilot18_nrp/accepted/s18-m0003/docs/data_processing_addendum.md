# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**" or "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by and between **Vaigaind Data Halls, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Trugeith Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"). It is made under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), as contemplated by Sections 1.1(e), 8.5 and 14.4 of the Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. Under the Agreement, Supplier provides Remote Monitoring Services for the Products installed at the Project Site through Supplier's hosted monitoring platform.

B. In providing the Remote Monitoring Services and related Services, Supplier processes certain Personal Data on behalf of Customer.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms that apply to that processing.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**1.2 Definitions.** In this Addendum:

(a) "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the New Jersey Data Privacy Act, the New York SHIELD Act and any other applicable state consumer privacy, data security or breach-notification law.

(b) "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

(c) "**Monitoring Platform**" means Supplier's hosted monitoring platform through which the Remote Monitoring Services are delivered. It includes its hosting environment, databases, dashboards, ticketing portal and alarm-delivery functions, and any network connection between the Products and that platform.

(d) "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, or that is otherwise defined as "personal data," "personal information" or a similar term under Data Protection Laws, that Supplier processes on behalf of Customer in connection with the Agreement.

(e) "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, alteration and deletion. "**Process**" and "**processed**" have corresponding meanings.

(f) "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data in Supplier's or its Sub-processors' possession, custody or control.

(g) "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by Supplier to process Personal Data on Supplier's behalf.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

**2.1 Roles.** Customer is the controller (or "business," as applicable) of the Personal Data. Supplier is a processor (or "service provider," as applicable) that processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

**2.2 Categories of Data Subjects.** The Personal Data processed under this Addendum relates to the following categories of Data Subjects:

(a) Customer's employees, contractors and operations personnel;

(b) individuals granted badge or escort access to the Project Site, including Supplier and subcontractor personnel and tenant representatives; and

(c) tenant contacts designated to receive alarms, notifications or reports.

**2.3 Categories of Personal Data.** The Personal Data comprises:

(a) names, job titles, employer, work email addresses and work and mobile telephone numbers;

(b) Monitoring Platform user credentials, user identifiers and access logs;

(c) badge-access records, including badge identifiers and entry and exit time stamps for Hall C and the associated mechanical gallery, where those records are linked to Incidents, maintenance visits or alarm events; and

(d) tenant contact details and alarm-routing and escalation preferences.

**2.4 Nature and Purpose.** Supplier processes Personal Data only to provide the Remote Monitoring Services and the other Services. That includes alarm delivery, Incident management, reporting, scheduling of Planned Maintenance and site access coordination under the Agreement, the applicable SOW and the Service Level Schedule.

**2.5 Duration.** Supplier processes Personal Data for the duration of the Remote Monitoring Services and for the period after that which is needed to comply with Section 9.

## 3. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

**3.1 Instructions.** Supplier shall process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, each SOW, the Service Level Schedule and this Addendum constitute Customer's instructions. Supplier shall promptly notify Customer if it believes an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

**3.2 Restrictions.** Supplier shall not:

(a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined under Data Protection Laws;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, including for any commercial purpose other than the Services;

(c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and Supplier; or

(d) combine Personal Data with personal data that Supplier receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as permitted by Data Protection Laws.

**3.3 Compliance.** Supplier shall comply with all obligations that Data Protection Laws impose on processors and service providers, and shall provide the same level of privacy protection as those laws require. Supplier shall notify Customer in writing if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws or this Addendum.

**3.4 Confidentiality of Personnel.** Supplier shall ensure that each person it authorizes to process Personal Data is bound by an obligation of confidentiality and receives appropriate training in data protection and security.

**3.5 Location.** Supplier shall store and process Personal Data only within the United States, unless Customer gives its prior written consent.

## 4. SECURITY

**4.1 Security Measures.** Supplier shall implement and maintain the safeguards required by Section 14.4 of the Agreement. Supplier shall also implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Annex 1 to protect Personal Data against Security Incidents.

**4.2 Certifications.** Supplier shall maintain, for the Monitoring Platform, a SOC 2 Type II report or ISO/IEC 27001 certification, renewed at least annually. On request, Supplier shall provide Customer with a copy of its most recent report or certificate.

**4.3 Operational Technology Segregation.** Supplier shall keep the Monitoring Platform logically segregated from Supplier's corporate networks and from the systems of Supplier's other customers. Supplier shall not initiate any remote connection from the Monitoring Platform into Customer's building management system or other Customer systems except through the connection method approved by Customer in writing.

## 5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

**5.1 Notice.** Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. This is consistent with Section 14.4 of the Agreement. The notice must be sent to Customer's designated security contact and copied to Customer's General Counsel.

**5.2 Content.** The notice must describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it.

Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**5.3 Cooperation.** Supplier shall:

(a) take prompt action to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident;

(b) preserve relevant logs and forensic evidence; and

(c) provide Customer with the information and assistance that Customer reasonably requests to meet its notification obligations to Data Subjects, tenants, regulators and others.

**5.4 Communications.** Supplier shall not notify any Data Subject, tenant or regulator of a Security Incident on Customer's behalf without Customer's prior written approval, unless required by law.

**5.5 Costs.** Supplier shall reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of investigating and responding to a Security Incident to the extent caused by Supplier's or its Sub-processors' breach of this Addendum. Those costs include notification, call-center, credit-monitoring and forensic costs.

## 6. SUB-PROCESSORS

**6.1 Authorized Sub-processors.** Customer authorizes Supplier to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 2.

**6.2 New Sub-processors.** Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging a new Sub-processor. If Customer objects on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period, the Parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If the objection is not resolved, Supplier shall not use that Sub-processor to process Personal Data.

**6.3 Sub-processor Terms.** Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors. Supplier's use of subcontractors to perform Services at the Project Site remains subject to Section 8.4 of the Agreement.

## 7. ASSISTANCE AND AUDIT

**7.1 Data Subject Requests.** Supplier shall promptly forward to Customer any request from a Data Subject relating to Personal Data, and shall not respond to it except on Customer's instructions. Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to such requests within the periods required by Data Protection Laws.

**7.2 Assessments.** Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance with any data protection assessment that Customer is required to conduct concerning the processing under this Addendum.

**7.3 Audit.** Once in any twelve (12) month period, and at any time after a Security Incident, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations may audit Supplier's compliance with this Addendum on at least fifteen (15) business days' notice. Supplier shall make available its relevant personnel, records and facilities. Supplier may satisfy an audit request first by providing its most recent report or certificate under Section 4.2, together with written responses to Customer's reasonable security questionnaires. Customer may proceed with an on-site audit if those materials do not reasonably address its request.

## 8. LIABILITY

**8.1 Responsibility.** Each Party is responsible for its own compliance with Data Protection Laws and this Addendum. Supplier is liable for any breach of this Addendum by Supplier or its Sub-processors.

**8.2 Limitation.** Notwithstanding Section 12.3(b) of the Agreement, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum or any Security Incident, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is subject to the limitation of liability in Section 12.2 of the Agreement, being the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**8.3 Insurance.** Supplier shall maintain the cyber liability insurance required by Section 11(e) of the Agreement.

## 9. RETURN AND DELETION

**9.1 Return or Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Remote Monitoring Services, or earlier on Customer's written request, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, return all Personal Data to Customer in a commonly used machine-readable format or securely delete it. Supplier shall certify the return or deletion in writing.

**9.2 Retained Copies.** Supplier may retain Personal Data only to the extent required by law. Any retained Personal Data remains subject to this Addendum for as long as it is retained.

**9.3 Telemetry.** Section 9.1 does not limit Supplier's obligation to retain Product telemetry data under Section 3.3 of the Service Level Schedule. To the extent that telemetry data contains Personal Data, Supplier shall process it only for that purpose.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Term.** This Addendum takes effect on the date stated above and remains in effect for as long as Supplier processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

**10.2 Incorporation.** This Addendum forms part of the Agreement. Matters not addressed in this Addendum are governed by the Agreement.

**10.3 Changes in Law.** If Data Protection Laws change in a way that requires changes to this Addendum, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to make the required changes.

**10.4 Governing Law.** This Addendum is governed by the law that governs the Agreement under Section 18.1 of the Agreement, being the laws of the State of New York. Section 18 of the Agreement applies to any dispute under this Addendum.

**10.5 Amendment.** This Addendum may be amended only in accordance with Section 19.3 of the Agreement.

**10.6 Counterparts.** This Addendum may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## ANNEX 1: TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

1. **Access control.** Supplier uses role-based access to the Monitoring Platform on a least-privilege basis. All administrative and Customer user access requires multi-factor authentication. Supplier reviews access rights at least quarterly and removes them within one (1) business day after a user's role ends.

2. **Encryption.** Supplier encrypts Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Supplier encrypts Personal Data at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard.

3. **Logging and monitoring.** Supplier logs access to Personal Data and administrative actions on the Monitoring Platform. Supplier retains those logs for at least twelve (12) months and monitors them for anomalous activity.

4. **Vulnerability management.** Supplier conducts vulnerability scans of the Monitoring Platform at least monthly and an independent penetration test at least annually. Supplier remediates critical vulnerabilities within fifteen (15) days and high vulnerabilities within thirty (30) days.

5. **Network security.** Product-to-platform connections are outbound-only from the Products, use authenticated and encrypted channels, and terminate in a segregated environment.

6. **Business continuity.** Supplier maintains backups of the Monitoring Platform in a geographically separate U.S. location. Supplier tests restoration at least annually.

7. **Personnel.** Supplier conducts background checks on personnel with access to Personal Data, as permitted by law, and provides security awareness training at least annually.

8. **Physical security.** Hosting facilities used for the Monitoring Platform have controlled access, video surveillance and environmental protections.
