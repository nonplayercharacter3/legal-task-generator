# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 2

## Phase 2 Liquid-Cooling Installation, Hall C, Secaucus Campus

This Statement of Work No. 2 (this "**SOW**" or "**SOW No. 2**") is entered into as of August 24, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Vaigaind Data Halls, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Trugeith Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"). It is issued under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to the Agreement. Under the Agreement, Supplier supplies liquid-cooling equipment and related installation, commissioning and Remote Monitoring Services for the Project Site, in phases, under Statements of Work.

B. Under Statement of Work No. 1 ("**SOW No. 1**"), Supplier supplied and installed the first phase of coolant distribution units and rear-door heat exchangers for the east half of Hall C, Rows C1 through C8 ("**Phase 1**").

C. The Phase 1 CDUs were Delivered to the Project Site on June 8, 2026. On July 10, 2026, Customer delivered to Supplier a written notice of nonconformity concerning leaking quick-disconnect couplings on the Phase 1 CDUs. Supplier has responded in writing that it rejects the notice as untimely under Section 7.3 of the Agreement. The Parties have not resolved the matter and reserve their respective positions regarding the Phase 1 CDUs.

D. Customer now wishes to engage Supplier to supply, install and commission the second phase of liquid-cooling equipment for the west half of Hall C ("**Phase 2**"), on the terms of this SOW.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Incorporation.** This SOW is governed by and incorporated into the Agreement. This SOW does not amend any Section of the Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this SOW:

(a) "**Phase 2 Area**" means the west half of Hall C at the Project Site, comprising Rows C9 through C16 and the associated mechanical gallery, as shown on Customer drawing VDH-SEC-HC-M-201, Revision D.

(b) "**Phase 2 Products**" means the Products listed in Section 3.1.

(c) "**Phase 2 Services**" means the Services described in Section 4.

(d) "**Substantial Completion**" means the date on which all Phase 2 Products have been installed, the commissioning tests in Section 8 have been passed, and Supplier has delivered the commissioning report required by Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

## 2. COVERED SITES

For purposes of the Agreement, including Section 3 of the Agreement, "**Covered Sites**" means all data center facilities owned, leased or operated by Customer or any of its Affiliates in the United States and Canada, including the Project Site.

**3.1 Phase 2 Products.** Supplier shall supply the following Products for installation in the Phase 2 Area:

| Item | Description | Model | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In-row coolant distribution unit, 1.2 MW, N+1 redundant pumps, dual-stage filtration | TTS-CDU-1200 | 6 |

The Design Basis is attached to this SOW as Exhibit A. Each CDU must maintain secondary-loop supply temperature within ±0.5°C of setpoint across the full load range stated in the Design Basis.

**3.3 Firmware.** Each CDU and rear-door heat exchanger must ship with controller firmware version 4.2 or later. The firmware must be compatible with Supplier's monitoring platform and with Customer's building management system over BACnet/IP.

## 4. SERVICES

**4.1 Scope of Phase 2 Services.** Supplier shall perform the following Services in the Phase 2 Area:

(a) site survey and verification of field dimensions, structural supports and facility water connections;

(b) receipt, unloading and rigging of the Phase 2 Products to the Hall C staging area in accordance with Section 6.1 of the Agreement;

(c) setting, anchoring and leveling of the CDUs;

(d) mounting of the rear-door heat exchangers on Customer-furnished cabinets;

(e) installation of secondary-loop manifolds, piping, quick-disconnect couplings and leak detection;

(f) flushing, filling and pressure testing of the secondary loop;

(g) controls integration with Customer's building management system;

(h) commissioning in accordance with Section 8;

(i) training for up to twelve (12) Customer operations personnel, in two sessions; and

(j) delivery of as-built drawings, operation and maintenance manuals and the commissioning report.

**4.2 Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall:

(a) provide facility water supply and return connections, with isolation valves, at the points shown in the Design Basis;

(b) provide electrical feeds to each CDU location;

(c) furnish and install the cabinets on which the rear-door heat exchangers are mounted; and

(d) provide reasonable access to the Phase 2 Area during the working hours set out in Section 6.3.

**4.3 Exclusions.** The Phase 2 Services do not include work on Customer's primary chilled-water plant, cabinet installation, or IT equipment installation.

## 8. COMMISSIONING

**8.1 Commissioning Tests.** Supplier shall perform the following commissioning tests in the presence of Customer's representatives, in accordance with Section 7.2 of the Agreement:

(a) a hydrostatic pressure test of the secondary loop at 1.5 times design pressure for four (4) hours, with no measurable pressure loss;

(b) a leak inspection of all quick-disconnect couplings and manifold connections under design flow;

(c) a load bank test of each CDU at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of rated capacity, demonstrating compliance with the supply temperature tolerance in Section 3.2;

(d) pump failover testing on each CDU; and

(e) end-to-end alarm verification from each CDU and leak detection controller through the monitoring platform and Customer's building management system.

**8.2 Test Procedures.** Supplier shall submit commissioning test procedures to Customer for approval at least twenty (20) days before the start of commissioning.

**8.3 Retest.** If any commissioning test fails, Supplier shall correct the cause and repeat the test at Supplier's cost.

**8.4 Inspection and Acceptance.** Sections 7.1, 7.3, 7.4 and 7.5 of the Agreement apply to the Phase 2 Products.
