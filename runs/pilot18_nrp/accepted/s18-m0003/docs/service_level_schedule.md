# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is made under the Supply Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between **Vaigaind Data Halls, LLC** ("**Customer**") and **Trugeith Thermal Systems, Inc.** ("**Supplier**"). It sets out the service levels, service credits and support obligations that apply to the Products and Services, as contemplated by Sections 1.1(j), 8.1 and 9.3 of the Agreement.

## 1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Scope.** This Schedule applies to all Products installed at the Project Site under any SOW and to all Services performed for those Products. That includes the Phase 1 Products under SOW No. 1 and the Phase 2 Products under SOW No. 2. Each Product becomes subject to this Schedule from the date on which commissioning of that Product is complete under the applicable SOW (the "**Service Commencement Date**").

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Schedule:

(a) "**Cooling Availability**" means, for a calendar month, the percentage calculated in accordance with Section 2.2.

(b) "**Cooling Outage**" means any period during which a CDU, or any group of CDUs serving a common secondary loop, fails to deliver secondary-loop coolant at the flow rate and within the supply temperature tolerance stated in the Specifications to the rows it serves. A Cooling Outage begins at the earlier of (i) the time the condition is first recorded by the monitoring platform or by Customer's building management system, and (ii) the time Customer reports the condition to Supplier. It ends when the condition has been corrected and confirmed in the monitoring platform data.

(c) "**Incident**" means any fault, alarm, failure or degradation of a Product or of the Remote Monitoring Services.

(d) "**Monthly Remote Monitoring Fee**" means the Remote Monitoring Services fees payable for the relevant calendar month under the applicable SOW, being, for the Phase 2 Products, the fee stated in Section 7.4 of SOW No. 2.

(e) "**Planned Maintenance**" means maintenance carried out in accordance with Section 7.

(f) "**Service Credit**" means a credit calculated in accordance with Section 5.

(g) "**Service Desk**" means Supplier's 24x7x365 technical support center, reachable by telephone, email and the monitoring platform's ticketing portal.

## 2. COOLING AVAILABILITY SERVICE LEVEL

**2.1 Service Level.** Supplier shall ensure that Cooling Availability for the Products at the Project Site is at least 99.99% in each calendar month (the "**Cooling Availability Service Level**").

**2.2 Calculation.** Cooling Availability is calculated separately for each secondary loop at the Project Site, as follows:

Cooling Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes − Outage Minutes) ÷ Total Minutes) × 100

In this formula:

- "**Total Minutes**" means the total number of minutes in the calendar month after the Service Commencement Date for the Products serving that loop; and
- "**Outage Minutes**" means the total number of minutes of Cooling Outage affecting that loop in the month, excluding minutes attributable to an exclusion under Section 8.

**2.3 Measurement.** Cooling Availability is measured using data from the monitoring platform, cross-checked against Customer's building management system logs. If the two data sources differ, the Parties shall review both in good faith. Until the difference is resolved, the data source that records the longer Cooling Outage applies.

## 3. REMOTE MONITORING SERVICE LEVELS

**3.1 Platform Availability.** Supplier shall ensure that the monitoring platform is available to receive telemetry from the Products, generate alarms and give Customer access to its dashboards and reports at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding Planned Maintenance ("**Platform Availability Service Level**").

**3.2 Alarm Delivery.** Supplier shall ensure that alarms generated by a Product are delivered through the monitoring platform to Customer's designated contacts and to Customer's building management system within sixty (60) seconds after the alarm condition is recorded, for at least 99.5% of alarms in each calendar month.

**3.3 Telemetry Retention.** Supplier shall retain Product telemetry data for at least twenty-four (24) months and make it available to Customer on request, in a commonly used machine-readable format.

**3.4 Personal Data.** Supplier's processing of Personal Data in connection with the Remote Monitoring Services is governed by the DPA, in accordance with Section 8.5 of the Agreement.

## 4. INCIDENT RESPONSE AND RESOLUTION

**4.1 Priority Levels.** Supplier shall classify each Incident on receipt, in consultation with Customer, as follows:

| Priority | Description |
|---|---|
| P1 (Critical) | A Cooling Outage, any coolant leak, or any condition presenting an immediate risk to Customer's or its tenants' IT equipment |
| P2 (High) | Loss of redundancy (for example, failure of one pump in an N+1 configuration), loss of alarm delivery, or degradation of supply temperature control without a Cooling Outage |
| P3 (Medium) | A fault that does not affect cooling capacity or redundancy, including a non-critical sensor or reporting fault |
| P4 (Low) | A request for information, a documentation request or a minor cosmetic defect |

Customer may reasonably reclassify an Incident to a higher priority. Supplier shall then apply the response and resolution times for that higher priority.

**4.2 Response and Resolution Times.** Supplier shall meet the following times, measured from the earlier of the time the Incident is logged by the monitoring platform and the time it is reported to the Service Desk:

| Priority | Remote Response | On-Site Attendance | Resolution or Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 15 minutes | 4 hours | 8 hours |
| P2 | 30 minutes | 8 hours | 24 hours |
| P3 | 4 hours | Next business day, if required | 5 business days |
| P4 | 1 business day | Not applicable | 10 business days |

**4.3 Permanent Fix.** Where Supplier resolves a P1 or P2 Incident by a workaround, Supplier shall implement a permanent fix within thirty (30) days after the workaround, unless Customer agrees otherwise in writing.

**4.4 Root Cause Analysis.** Supplier shall deliver a written root cause analysis for each P1 Incident within ten (10) business days after resolution. The analysis must describe the cause, the corrective actions taken and the preventive measures that will be implemented.

**4.5 Spare Parts.** Supplier shall maintain at or within a two-hour drive of the Project Site a stock of critical spare parts sufficient to meet the On-Site Attendance and Resolution times for P1 and P2 Incidents. Critical spare parts include pumps, controllers, sensors, seals and quick-disconnect couplings. This stock is in addition to any spare parts kit supplied to Customer under an SOW.

**4.6 Site Access.** Supplier personnel attending the Project Site shall comply with Section 8.2 of the Agreement.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

**5.1 Cooling Availability Credits.** If Cooling Availability for any secondary loop falls below the Cooling Availability Service Level in a calendar month, Supplier shall credit Customer the following percentage of the Monthly Remote Monitoring Fee for the CDUs serving that loop:

| Cooling Availability in the Month | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.99% but at or above 99.95% | 10% |
| Below 99.95% but at or above 99.9% | 25% |
| Below 99.9% but at or above 99.5% | 50% |
| Below 99.5% | 100% |

**5.2 Platform and Alarm Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet the Platform Availability Service Level or the alarm delivery service level in Section 3.2 in a calendar month, Supplier shall credit Customer 10% of the total Monthly Remote Monitoring Fee for that month for each service level missed.

**5.3 Response Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet the On-Site Attendance or Resolution time for a P1 Incident, Supplier shall credit Customer 5% of the total Monthly Remote Monitoring Fee for that month for each such failure.

**5.4 Monthly Maximum.** The total Service Credits for any calendar month shall not exceed 100% of the total Monthly Remote Monitoring Fee for that month.

**5.5 Application.** Supplier shall show Service Credits in the monthly report under Section 6 and apply them against the next invoice for Remote Monitoring Services. If no further invoice is due, Supplier shall pay the amount of any outstanding Service Credits to Customer within forty-five (45) days.

**5.6 Other Remedies.** Service Credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the Services. They are without prejudice to Customer's other rights and remedies under the Agreement, including under Sections 7, 9 and 15 of the Agreement.

## 6. REPORTING AND REVIEW

**6.1 Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month, Supplier shall deliver a report to Customer that includes:

(a) Cooling Availability for each secondary loop;

(b) platform availability and alarm delivery performance;

(c) a log of all Incidents, with their priority, response and resolution times;

(d) any Service Credits due; and

(e) trend data on supply temperature, flow, pressure and filter differential pressure for each CDU.

**6.2 Quarterly Review.** The Parties' project managers or other designated representatives shall meet each calendar quarter to review service performance, open Incidents, root cause analyses, firmware updates and the forecast under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

**6.3 Audit of Measurements.** On reasonable notice, Customer may review the raw data and calculations underlying any report. Supplier shall correct any error and adjust the related Service Credits on the next invoice.

## 7. PLANNED MAINTENANCE AND FIRMWARE

**7.1 Preventive Maintenance.** Supplier shall perform preventive maintenance on the Products in accordance with its operation and maintenance manuals, at least quarterly. Preventive maintenance includes filter replacement, coolant quality testing, pump inspection, coupling and seal inspection and calibration of sensors.

**7.2 Scheduling.** Supplier shall schedule all on-site Planned Maintenance at least ten (10) business days in advance, in accordance with Customer's change-management and method-of-procedure requirements. Supplier shall perform Planned Maintenance on only one CDU on any secondary loop at a time, and only while the redundant equipment serving that loop is available.

**7.3 Platform Maintenance.** Supplier shall perform planned maintenance on the monitoring platform outside the hours of 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days, on at least five (5) business days' notice. Platform maintenance may not exceed eight (8) hours in any calendar month. During platform maintenance, the Products must continue to operate and deliver local alarms to Customer's building management system.

**7.4 Firmware Updates.** Supplier shall make security and stability updates to Product firmware available without additional charge during the warranty period under Section 9.1 of the Agreement and while Remote Monitoring Services are provided. Supplier shall not install any firmware update on a Product without Customer's prior approval through Customer's change-management process.

## 8. EXCLUSIONS

**8.1 Excluded Events.** A Cooling Outage, platform unavailability or failure to meet a response or resolution time is excluded from the service level calculations to the extent it is caused by:

(a) failure of Customer's facility water supply, primary chilled-water plant or electrical feeds to meet the conditions stated in the Design Basis or other Specifications under the applicable SOW;

(b) Customer's breach of its responsibilities under the applicable SOW, or modification or repair of the Products by anyone other than Supplier or its subcontractors;

(c) Customer's refusal of access to the Project Site, or its refusal of approval for a requested change, where the refusal prevented Supplier from performing; or

(d) an event of force majeure under Section 16 of the Agreement.

**8.2 Conditions.** Planned Maintenance carried out in accordance with Section 7 is excluded only to the extent that it does not cause a Cooling Outage. Supplier bears the burden of showing that an exclusion applies.

## 9. CHRONIC FAILURE

**9.1 Chronic Failure.** A "**Chronic Failure**" occurs if:

(a) Supplier fails to meet the Cooling Availability Service Level for any secondary loop in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve (12) month period; or

(b) three (3) or more P1 Incidents arise from the same root cause within a rolling six (6) month period.

**9.2 Remediation Plan.** Within fifteen (15) business days after a Chronic Failure, Supplier shall deliver to Customer, at Supplier's cost, a written remediation plan. The plan must include a timetable for implementation, and Supplier shall implement the plan as approved by Customer.

**9.3 Material Breach.** A Chronic Failure is a material breach of the Agreement for purposes of Section 15.1 of the Agreement.

## 10. CONTACTS AND ESCALATION

**10.1 Service Desk.** Supplier shall maintain the Service Desk at all times. Supplier shall provide Customer with current telephone, email and portal contact details and update them promptly on any change.

**10.2 Escalation Path.** If a P1 or P2 Incident is not resolved within the applicable Resolution time, Supplier shall escalate it as follows:

- first, to Supplier's regional service manager;
- after a further four (4) hours, to Supplier's Vice President of Field Service; and
- after a further eight (8) hours, to Supplier's Chief Operating Officer.

Supplier shall keep Customer informed at each stage.

**10.3 Disputes.** Any dispute about the measurement of a service level or the calculation of a Service Credit is subject to Section 18 of the Agreement.

## 11. GENERAL

**11.1 Changes.** This Schedule may be amended only in accordance with Section 19.3 of the Agreement.

**11.2 Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
