# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Vaigaind Data Halls, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Trugeith Thermal Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures, installs and supports liquid-cooling equipment for data center environments. That equipment includes coolant distribution units and rear-door heat exchangers. Supplier also provides remote thermal monitoring services for that equipment.

B. Customer operates colocation data center facilities. Customer wishes to procure liquid-cooling equipment, together with related installation, commissioning and remote-monitoring services, for Hall C at Customer's Secaucus, New Jersey campus (the "**Project Site**").

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will supply such equipment and services, in one or more phases, under Statements of Work issued under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**CDU**" means a coolant distribution unit manufactured or supplied by Supplier, including its pumps, heat exchangers, filtration, controls, manifolds, quick-disconnect couplings and embedded firmware.

(c) "**Covered Products**" means CDUs, rear-door heat exchangers and any other liquid-cooling equipment of a type identified as a Product in any SOW.

(d) "**Covered Sites**" has the meaning given in the applicable SOW.

(e) "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties covering Supplier's processing of Personal Data in connection with the Remote Monitoring Services, as amended from time to time.

(f) "**Delivery**" means delivery of Products to the Project Site, or to such other delivery location as is specified in the applicable SOW, in accordance with Section 6.1. "**Delivered**" has a corresponding meaning.

(g) "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

(h) "**Products**" means the CDUs, rear-door heat exchangers and other equipment, parts, components, firmware and documentation that Supplier supplies under an SOW.

(i) "**Remote Monitoring Services**" means Supplier's remote thermal monitoring, alerting and reporting services for the Products, delivered through Supplier's hosted monitoring platform.

(j) "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule agreed between the Parties under this Agreement, which sets out the service levels, service credits and support obligations that apply to the Products and Services.

(k) "**Services**" means the installation, commissioning, testing, training, maintenance, support and Remote Monitoring Services that Supplier performs under this Agreement or any SOW.

(l) "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications, performance criteria and documentation for the Products set out or referenced in the applicable SOW.

(m) "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties that references this Agreement, including Statement of Work No. 2 for Phase 2 installation at the Project Site.

**1.2 Statements of Work.** Each SOW is governed by and incorporated into this Agreement. An SOW may not amend this Agreement unless it expressly identifies the Section of this Agreement to be amended and is signed by an authorized officer of each Party.

**1.3 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. "Days" means calendar days.

**1.4 Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, they take precedence in the following order:

- first, the DPA, as to Personal Data only;
- second, the body of this Agreement;
- third, the Service Level Schedule; and
- fourth, each SOW.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues for five years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 15.

**2.2 Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive five (5) year Renewal Terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by delivering written notice of non-renewal to the other Party during the thirty (30) day period ending two hundred seventy (270) days before the expiration of the then-current Term.

**2.3 Effect on SOWs.** Expiration or termination of this Agreement does not affect any SOW then in effect. Each such SOW continues to be governed by this Agreement until it is completed or terminated in accordance with its terms.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY

**3.1 Exclusive Purchasing.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase all of its requirements for Covered Products for all Covered Sites exclusively from Supplier. Customer shall not solicit, purchase or install Covered Products from any third party for use at any Covered Site.

**3.2 Supplier Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient manufacturing and installation capacity to meet Customer's forecast requirements for Covered Products. If Supplier notifies Customer in writing that it cannot supply a Covered Product within the lead times in the applicable SOW, Customer may procure that Covered Product from a third party, for the affected quantity only, without breaching Section 3.1.

**3.3 Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its anticipated requirements for Covered Products. Customer shall update the forecast quarterly. Forecasts are for planning purposes only and do not constitute purchase commitments.

## 4. ORDERING

**4.1 SOWs and Purchase Orders.** Customer orders Products and Services by executing SOWs and by issuing purchase orders under an executed SOW. Each purchase order must reference the applicable SOW. Any pre-printed or additional terms in a purchase order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

**4.2 Changes.** Either Party may request a change to the scope, schedule or Specifications under an SOW. A change is binding only when documented in a written change order signed by both Parties. The change order must state the effect of the change on price and schedule.

**4.3 Lead Times.** Supplier shall meet the lead times and delivery dates stated in each SOW. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly in writing of any anticipated delay, its cause, and the steps Supplier is taking to mitigate it.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for Products and Services set out in the applicable SOW. Prices are fixed for the duration of each SOW. Prices include all packaging, documentation and standard tooling.

**5.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Products upon Delivery and Services upon completion of the milestones stated in the applicable SOW. Supplier shall invoice Remote Monitoring Services monthly in arrears.

**5.3 Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier in writing of the basis of the dispute. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve any such dispute.

**5.4 Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay where Supplier is required to collect them. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

**6.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DDP (Incoterms 2020) to the Project Site or to the other delivery location stated in the applicable SOW, on the delivery dates stated in that SOW. Supplier is responsible for freight, unloading and rigging to the designated staging area, unless the applicable SOW provides otherwise.

**6.2 Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to Products passes to Customer on Delivery. Risk of loss passes to Customer on Delivery. However, where Supplier is responsible for installation, risk of loss for Products in Supplier's care, custody or control remains with Supplier until completion of installation.

**6.3 Packaging and Documentation.** Supplier shall package Products to prevent damage in transit and storage. Each shipment must include a packing list identifying the SOW, purchase order, serial numbers and quantities.

## 7. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

**7.1 Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test Products after Delivery to determine whether they conform to the Specifications and are free of visible damage or defects.

**7.2 Commissioning.** Where an SOW provides for commissioning, Supplier shall perform the commissioning tests described in that SOW in the presence of Customer's representatives. Supplier shall deliver a commissioning report to Customer.

**7.3 Rejection.** Customer may reject any Products that do not conform to the Specifications by giving Supplier written notice of rejection within 20 days after Delivery. The notice must describe the nonconformity in reasonable detail. If Customer does not give notice of rejection within that period, the Products are deemed accepted.

**7.4 Remedy for Rejected Products.** For Products properly rejected under Section 7.3, Supplier shall, at Customer's option and at Supplier's cost, either:

(a) repair or replace the Products within fifteen (15) days after the notice of rejection; or

(b) refund the price paid for the Products.

Supplier bears all freight, removal and reinstallation costs for rejected Products.

**7.5 Effect of Acceptance.** Acceptance of Products does not waive Customer's rights under Section 9.

## 8. INSTALLATION AND SERVICES

**8.1 Performance.** Supplier shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the applicable SOW and the Service Level Schedule.

**8.2 Site Rules.** While at the Project Site, Supplier personnel shall comply with Customer's site access, security, safety and change-management policies as made available to Supplier. Supplier shall ensure that its subcontractors comply with the same policies.

**8.3 Personnel.** Supplier shall assign qualified personnel to perform the Services. Supplier shall replace any individual whose performance or conduct Customer reasonably finds unsatisfactory.

**8.4 Subcontractors.** Supplier may not subcontract any material portion of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold that consent. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

**8.5 Remote Monitoring Services.** Supplier shall provide the Remote Monitoring Services for the period stated in the applicable SOW. Supplier's processing of Personal Data in connection with the Remote Monitoring Services is governed by the DPA.

## 9. WARRANTIES

**9.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for thirty-six (36) months after the later of Delivery and completion of commissioning, the Products will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) be free from defects in materials, workmanship and design; and

(c) be new and free of liens and encumbrances.

**9.2 Services Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 8.1 and in compliance with applicable law.

**9.3 Warranty Remedy.** If any Product or Service breaches a warranty, Supplier shall promptly repair or replace the Product, or re-perform the Service, at Supplier's cost. If Supplier fails to do so within a reasonable time, Customer may obtain a refund of the price paid for the nonconforming Product or Service. These remedies are in addition to Customer's other rights and remedies under this Agreement and the Service Level Schedule.

**9.4 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**10.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and the resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of:

(a) any claim that the Products or Services infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret;

(b) bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property caused by the Products or by the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its subcontractors; or

(c) Supplier's violation of applicable law.

**10.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and the resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, arising out of bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall:

(a) give prompt written notice of the claim;

(b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement of the claim, except that no settlement may impose any obligation on the indemnified Party without its consent; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. INSURANCE

During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, Supplier shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) products and completed operations coverage of at least $5,000,000 in the aggregate;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law;

(d) professional liability insurance of at least $5,000,000; and

(e) cyber liability insurance of at least $10,000,000.

Supplier shall name Customer as an additional insured on the general liability policy and provide certificates of insurance upon request.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

**12.2 Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

**12.3 Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10;

(b) breach of Section 14 (Confidentiality and Data Security), which is excluded from the cap;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Customer's obligation to pay undisputed fees.

## 13. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**13.1 Supplier IP.** Supplier retains all intellectual property rights in the Products, firmware, monitoring platform and documentation. Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the firmware and documentation embedded in or supplied with the Products, in connection with the operation, maintenance and transfer of the Products.

**13.2 Customer Data.** As between the Parties, Customer owns all data relating to Customer's facilities, operations, personnel and tenants that Supplier collects or generates in performing the Services ("**Customer Data**"). Supplier may use Customer Data only to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY

**14.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether disclosed orally, in writing or electronically. Confidential Information includes Customer Data, facility layouts, power and cooling designs, security arrangements and pricing.

**14.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall:

(a) use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform or exercise its rights under this Agreement;

(b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; and

(c) disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14.

**14.3 Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure;

(c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or

(d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

A Party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, provided that it gives the other Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

**14.4 Data Security.** Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards that meet or exceed industry standards to protect Customer Data and Customer's systems against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These safeguards include safeguards for the monitoring platform and for any network connection between the Products and that platform. Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Customer Data or Customer's systems. Supplier shall comply with the DPA in its processing of Personal Data.

**14.5 Return or Destruction.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement, or on request, the receiving Party shall return or destroy the disclosing Party's Confidential Information and certify that it has done so.

## 15. TERMINATION

**15.1 For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.

**15.2 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy or similar proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**15.3 Effect of Termination.** On termination, Customer shall pay for Products Delivered and Services performed through the termination date. Sections 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 17, 18 and 19 survive termination or expiration.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, or labor disputes other than those involving the affected Party's own workforce. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use reasonable efforts to mitigate. If a force majeure event continues for more than sixty (60) days, the other Party may terminate the affected SOW by written notice. This Section 16 does not excuse payment obligations.

## 17. NOTICES

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the address of the receiving Party set out below its signature, or to such other address as a Party designates by notice. Notices to Customer must be copied to Customer's General Counsel.

## 18. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

**18.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

**18.2 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between their senior executives, for a period of thirty (30) days after written notice of the dispute.

**18.3 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each Party waives trial by jury to the extent permitted by law.

## 19. GENERAL

**19.1 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent. However, either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate, or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates.

**19.2 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

**19.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing, and no waiver of one breach is a waiver of any other breach.

**19.4 Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions continue in full force.

**19.5 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
