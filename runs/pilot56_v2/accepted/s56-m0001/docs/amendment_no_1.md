# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

**LaneCast Platform**

This Amendment No. 1 to Software License Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of May 29, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Draibav Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Logith Auto Auctions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Licensee are parties to the Software License Agreement dated as of June 16, 2025, including the Order Form and the Service Level Schedule (together, the "**Agreement**"), under which Licensor licenses the LaneCast Platform to Licensee for use at the Licensed Sites.

B. Licensee has informed Licensor that Logith Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation and the parent of Licensee ("**Holdings**"), has entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 20, 2026 with Ostrander Remarketing Partners, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership ("**Ostrander**"), and that the transactions contemplated by that agreement are expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.

C. Licensor's monthly reports under Section 2.4 of the Service Level Schedule recorded Monthly Availability of 98.1% for January 2026, 97.6% for February 2026 and 98.4% for March 2026. Licensee delivered to Licensor a written notice dated May 4, 2026, stating that a Chronic Failure had occurred and that Licensee was terminating the Agreement under Section 5.2 of the Service Level Schedule with effect from October 31, 2026 (the "**Termination Notice**"). By letter dated May 11, 2026, Licensor disputed the effectiveness of the Termination Notice.

D. The Parties wish to strengthen the data security obligations under the Agreement and to provide for remediation of Platform availability, on the terms of this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND EFFECT

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **Effect.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment Effective Date. On and after that date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 8.1

2.1 In the second sentence of Section 8.1 of the Agreement, the words "which is governed by Sections 7 and 8.4 through 8.7" are deleted and replaced with "which is governed by Sections 7 and 8A".

## 3. DATA SECURITY

3.1 **Deletion.** Sections 8.4 through 8.7 of the Agreement are deleted in their entirety.

3.2 **Insertion.** The following new Section 8A is inserted in the Agreement immediately after Section 8:

> ## 8A. DATA SECURITY
>
> 8A.1 **Security Program.** Licensor shall implement, maintain and regularly update a written information security program containing administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Licensee Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction. The program shall conform to ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, as current from time to time, and shall be no less protective than industry standards for hosted transactional platforms processing financial and personal information.
>
> 8A.2 **Technical Controls.** Without limiting Section 8A.1, Licensor shall (a) encrypt Licensee Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard; (b) require multi-factor authentication for all administrative access to the Platform and to systems storing Licensee Data; (c) restrict access to Licensee Data to personnel who need it to perform the Agreement and who have completed background checks and annual security training; (d) maintain logging and monitoring of access to Licensee Data and retain logs for at least twelve (12) months; and (e) apply critical and high-severity security patches within fifteen (15) days and thirty (30) days, respectively, after release.
>
> 8A.3 **Data Location.** Licensor shall store and process Licensee Data only in data centers located in the United States, unless Licensee consents otherwise in writing.
>
> 8A.4 **Subprocessors.** Licensor shall not permit any subcontractor to access Licensee Data unless the subcontractor is bound by written obligations at least as protective as this Section 8A. Licensor shall maintain a current list of such subcontractors, give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any addition, and remain responsible for each subcontractor's compliance.
>
> 8A.5 **Audits and Testing.** Licensor shall obtain, at least annually, a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Platform from an independent auditor and shall provide a copy to Licensee within thirty (30) days after its issuance. Licensor shall engage an independent firm to conduct penetration testing of the Platform at least annually and shall provide Licensee with an executive summary of the results. Licensor shall remediate any critical or high-risk findings within thirty (30) days and any other material deficiencies within ninety (90) days.
>
> 8A.6 **Security Incident Notification.** Licensor shall notify Licensee in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to or acquisition, use or disclosure of Licensee Data (a "**Security Incident**"). The notice shall describe, to the extent known, the nature and scope of the Security Incident, the Licensee Data and the Bidders and Consignors affected, and the containment and remedial steps taken or planned. Licensor shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.
>
> 8A.7 **Incident Response.** Licensor shall promptly investigate, contain and remediate each Security Incident; preserve relevant evidence; cooperate with Licensee, its insurers and any regulator or law enforcement authority; and not notify any Bidder, Consignor or third party (other than law enforcement or as required by law) that identifies Licensee without Licensee's prior written approval. Licensor shall reimburse Licensee's reasonable costs of legally required notifications to Bidders, Consignors and regulators, forensic investigation, credit monitoring and identity protection services for affected individuals for at least twenty-four (24) months, and call-center support, to the extent the Security Incident results from Licensor's breach of the Agreement.
>
> 8A.8 **Security Contact.** Each Party shall designate a security contact available twenty-four (24) hours a day for matters under this Section 8A and shall notify the other Party of any change.

## 4. AVAILABILITY REMEDIATION

4.1 **Corrective Action Plan.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a written corrective action plan identifying the root causes of the Downtime recorded for January, February and March 2026 and the measures Licensor will take to meet the Availability Commitment, with target completion dates.

4.2 **Implementation and Reporting.** Licensor shall implement the corrective action plan in accordance with its terms and shall report on its progress at each quarterly review under Section 7.1 of the Service Level Schedule and, until the plan is completed, by written status report to Licensee on the fifteenth (15th) day of each month.

4.3 **No Effect on Service Levels.** Nothing in this Section 4 modifies the Availability Commitment, Service Credits or any other service level under the Service Level Schedule.

## 5. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.8

5.1 In Section 12.8 of the Agreement, the words "7.1, 7.3, 8, 9, 10" are deleted and replaced with "7.1, 7.3, 8, 8A (for so long as Licensor or any subcontractor retains any Licensee Data), 9, 10".

## 6. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

6.1 Each Party reserves all of its rights, remedies, claims and defenses with respect to the Termination Notice, including as to whether a Chronic Failure occurred, whether the Termination Notice was timely and effective, and the consequences of the Termination Notice under the Agreement.

6.2 Neither the execution of this Amendment nor the performance of any obligation under it, including Section 4, constitutes an acceptance or rejection of, or an admission by either Party regarding, the Termination Notice or Licensor's letter dated May 11, 2026.

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Ratification.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and the Parties ratify and confirm it.

7.2 **No Waiver.** Except as expressly set out in this Amendment, nothing in this Amendment waives, releases or modifies any right, remedy, obligation or provision of the Agreement, and Section 16.4 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment.

7.3 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules, and Sections 14.2 and 14.3 of the Agreement apply to any dispute arising out of it.

7.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
