# TERM SHEET

**LaneCast Platform License: Draibav Systems, Inc. / Logith Auto Auctions, LLC**

**Date:** May 9, 2025

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which **Draibav Systems, Inc.** ("**Draibav**") proposes to license its LaneCast simulcast bidding, online auction and vehicle condition reporting platform (the "**Platform**") to **Logith Auto Auctions, LLC** ("**Logith**"). Except as stated in Section 12, this term sheet is not binding. It is intended to form the basis for a definitive software license agreement, order form and service level schedule (together, the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. PARTIES

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Draibav Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 4410 Commerce Park Drive, Suite 300, Columbus, Ohio 43219 |
| Licensee | Logith Auto Auctions, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 2275 Interchange Boulevard, Lebanon, Tennessee 37090 |

## 2. LICENSED PLATFORM AND SITES

2.1 **Modules.** LaneCast Simulcast, LaneCast Online Auction, LaneCast Mobile, LaneCast Condition Reports and LaneCast Connect API, with all updates and new releases made generally available to Draibav's licensees.

2.2 **Licensed Sites.** Logith's six (6) auction facilities in Nashville (Lebanon), Memphis, Louisville, Birmingham (Bessemer), Atlanta South (Fairburn) and Charlotte (the "**Licensed Sites**"). Logith may add facilities by supplemental order at Draibav's then-current pricing.

2.3 **Users.** Unlimited Logith authorized users. No per-bidder or per-consignor charges.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** Sixty (60) months from a subscription start date of July 1, 2025.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the initial term, the license will continue for successive 12-month renewal terms, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 During the term, Logith will use the Platform as its exclusive provider of simulcast bidding software at the six Licensed Sites only.

4.2 Nothing in this Section 4 restricts Logith or its affiliates in respect of any other software or any other facility.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Subscription Fees.** Monthly subscription fees of $78,000 in total for all six Licensed Sites, allocated by site in the order form and invoiced monthly in advance. Fees for each site begin on the later of the subscription start date and that site's go-live date.

5.2 **Implementation Fee.** One-time fee of $96,000, payable in milestones tied to site go-live.

5.3 **Price Protection.** Subscription fees are fixed during the initial term. Any increase for a renewal term is subject to ninety (90) days' prior notice and capped at four percent (4%).

5.4 **Payment.** Net thirty (30) days. Good-faith disputed amounts may be withheld pending resolution.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 **Availability.** 99.9% monthly availability, with no scheduled maintenance during auction hours.

6.2 **Service Credits.** Tiered credits for availability shortfalls, with additional credits for extended Severity 1 outages during auction hours.

6.3 **Termination Right.** Logith may terminate, in whole or by site, without early termination fees, for chronic availability failure or repeated Severity 1 outages, on terms to be set out in the service level schedule.

6.4 **Disaster Recovery.** Geographically redundant U.S. hosting, with a four (4) hour recovery time objective and a fifteen (15) minute recovery point objective.

## 7. DATA

7.1 **Ownership.** Logith will own all data submitted to, collected by or generated by the Platform in connection with Logith's auctions, including bidder and consignor registration data, bid histories, hammer and reserve prices, condition reports and transaction data.

7.2 **Draibav Use.** Draibav may use Logith's data only to provide the Platform and services to Logith. Draibav may use Platform operational and performance data that does not identify Logith, its sites, bidders or consignors.

7.3 **Security.** Draibav will maintain an industry-standard written security program, obtain an annual SOC 2 Type II report, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and give prompt notice of security incidents.

7.4 **Return.** Data export at no charge for sixty (60) days following expiration or termination, followed by certified deletion.

## 8. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

8.1 **Cap.** Each party's liability capped at fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

8.2 **Exclusions from Cap.** IP indemnity obligations; breach of confidentiality and data security obligations; gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud; and payment obligations.

8.3 **Indemnity.** Draibav will defend and indemnify Logith against third-party IP infringement claims relating to the Platform.

## 9. INSURANCE

Draibav will maintain commercial general liability, technology errors and omissions, cyber liability and workers' compensation insurance in amounts to be agreed in the Definitive Agreement, and will provide certificates on request.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT

Neither party may assign without the other's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Draibav may assign to a successor to its Platform business that assumes its obligations in writing. Treatment of a change of control to be addressed in the Definitive Agreement.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

Delaware law. Courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, following thirty (30) days of executive escalation.

## 12. BINDING PROVISIONS

12.1 Only this Section 12 is binding. Neither party is obligated to enter into the Definitive Agreement unless and until it is executed by both parties.

12.2 The parties' discussions and the terms of this term sheet are subject to the mutual nondisclosure agreement between Draibav and Logith.

12.3 Each party will bear its own costs in connection with this term sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreement.

12.4 This Section 12 is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
