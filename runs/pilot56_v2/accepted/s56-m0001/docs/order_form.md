## 4. DEFINITIONS

4.1 **Licensee Data.** "**Licensee Data**" means data and information uploaded to the Platform by Licensee's Authorized Users in connection with Licensee's use of the Platform.

4.2 **Lane.** "**Lane**" means a single auction block at a Licensed Site at which vehicles are offered for sale through the simulcast bidding module.

## 5. PLATFORM MODULES

5.1 **Included Modules.** Licensee's subscription includes the following modules of the Platform at all Licensed Sites:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| LaneCast Simulcast | Live audio and video simulcast of in-lane sales with real-time online and mobile bidding |

5.3 **Bidders and Consignors.** There is no per-Bidder or per-Consignor charge for access to the Platform by Bidders and Consignors in Licensee's auctions.

## 7. IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

7.1 **Scope.** Licensor shall perform the following services (the "**Implementation Services**"):

(a) configuration of the Platform for each Licensed Site, including Lane setup, sale calendars, fee schedules and user roles;

(b) installation support and testing of simulcast audio and video equipment supplied by Licensee at each Licensed Site;

(c) migration of Licensee's existing Bidder and Consignor registration records, open run lists and historical condition reports from Licensee's legacy systems, in a format agreed in the implementation plan;

(d) integration of the Platform with Licensee's auction management, title and accounting systems through the LaneCast Connect API;

(e) on-site go-live support at each Licensed Site for its first two (2) Auction Days; and

(f) administrator training for up to twenty (20) Licensee personnel and train-the-trainer sessions at each Licensed Site.

7.2 **Implementation Plan.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Effective Date, Licensor shall deliver a written implementation plan setting out tasks, responsibilities and target Go-Live Dates for each Licensed Site. The plan becomes effective on Licensee's written approval.

7.3 **Acceptance.** Licensee shall have ten (10) Business Days after Licensor notifies Licensee that a Licensed Site's configuration is complete to test it and either accept it in writing or identify material non-conformities with the Documentation or the implementation plan. Licensor shall correct identified non-conformities and resubmit the configuration for testing.
