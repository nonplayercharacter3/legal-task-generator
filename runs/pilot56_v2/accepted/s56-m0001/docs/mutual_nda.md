# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of April 14, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Draibav Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Commerce Park Drive, Suite 300, Columbus, Ohio 43219 ("**Draibav**"), and **Logith Auto Auctions, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Interchange Boulevard, Lebanon, Tennessee 37090 ("**Logith**"). Draibav and Logith are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Draibav provides the LaneCast simulcast bidding, online auction and vehicle condition reporting platform. Logith operates wholesale used-vehicle auction facilities.

B. The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible license of the LaneCast platform for use in Logith's auction operations (the "**Purpose**"). Each Party is willing to disclose information for the Purpose on the terms of this NDA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party or its Affiliates (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and in any form. It includes:

(a) product roadmaps, software, system architecture and security documentation;
(b) pricing, proposals and commercial terms;
(c) business plans, financial information and auction volumes;
(d) Bidder, Consignor and dealer information; and
(e) the existence and status of the Parties' discussions.

Information qualifies as Confidential Information if it is marked confidential or if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants, financial advisers and actual or prospective financing sources.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no breach of this NDA by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;
(b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before it was disclosed under this NDA;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party that is not under a confidentiality obligation; or
(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

3.2 **Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information from unauthorized use and disclosure. It shall use at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.3 **Disclosure to Representatives.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose. Each such Representative must be bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

3.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall reasonably cooperate to regain possession of the information and to prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

3.5 **Personal Information.** If either Party discloses personal information, including information about Bidders or Consignors, the Receiving Party shall:

(a) process it only for the Purpose;
(b) comply with applicable privacy and data protection laws; and
(c) disclose it no more widely than reasonably necessary.

Each Party shall disclose personal information only to the extent reasonably necessary for the Purpose.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena or court order, on the following conditions:

(a) to the extent legally permitted, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice;
(b) it provides reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in seeking a protective order or other remedy; and
(c) it discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within fifteen (15) days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession or control and certify in writing that it has done so.

5.2 Despite Section 5.1, the Receiving Party may retain:

(a) copies required to comply with law or its bona fide document retention policies; and
(b) copies stored in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible.

Any retained copies remain subject to this NDA for as long as they are retained.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY; NO OBLIGATION

6.1 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right in any intellectual property is granted under this NDA, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "AS IS." The Disclosing Party makes no warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. Any warranties will be only those set out in a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

6.3 **No Obligation.** This NDA does not obligate either Party to:

(a) disclose any particular information;
(b) continue discussions; or
(c) enter into any further agreement.

Either Party may end discussions at any time.

6.4 **Independent Activities.** This NDA does not restrict either Party from independently developing or acquiring products or services that compete with those of the other Party, provided it does not use the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this NDA.

## 7. TERM

7.1 **Term.** This NDA applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period of two (2) years after the NDA Effective Date. Either Party may end that period earlier by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other.

7.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Receiving Party's obligations under this NDA continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period under Section 7.1. For any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

9.2 **Venue.** Any dispute arising out of this NDA shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts. Either Party may also seek injunctive relief under Section 8.1 in any court of competent jurisdiction.

9.3 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses shown on the signature page. Notices are effective on receipt.

9.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this NDA relates. This NDA binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

9.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

9.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

9.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
