# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and between **Laivoul Student Transit, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Boliv Coachlines, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a pupil transportation business in the State of New York. Through that business it provides school bus transportation services to school districts in the Hudson Valley region from a depot located in Dutchess County, New York (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the Business, and Buyer will assume certain specified liabilities of the Business. The terms and conditions of the sale and assumption are set forth in this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances (as defined in Schedule 4.5), all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) the Purchased Vehicles, together with all titles, registrations, inspection records, maintenance logs and warranties relating thereto;

(b) Seller's leasehold interest under the Depot Lease and all fixtures, lifts, fueling equipment and improvements located at the depot;

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all tools, parts inventory, radios, GPS and camera equipment, and other tangible personal property used in the Business and located at the depot;

(e) the routing data, route maps, stop lists and historical ridership data of the Business, and the intellectual property assigned under the IP Assignment Agreement;

(f) to the extent transferable under applicable Law, the Permits set forth on Schedule 2.1(f); and

(g) all books, records and files relating to the Purchased Assets.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include any of the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): cash and cash equivalents; accounts receivable for services rendered before the Closing; Seller's operating authority and USDOT number; Seller's corporate records; any Contract not listed on Schedule 2.1(c); insurance policies; and the assets set forth on Schedule 2.2.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only (a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts and the Depot Lease to the extent arising and relating to periods after the Closing, other than Liabilities arising from any breach or default by Seller occurring on or before the Closing, and (b) Operational Liabilities (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include all Liabilities arising out of the operation of the Business or the Purchased Assets before the Closing, all Taxes of Seller, all employee and benefit plan Liabilities relating to periods before the Closing, and all Liabilities for overbilling or refund claims of any school district relating to services rendered before the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to the prorations set forth in Section 2.8.

**2.6 Payment of Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $14,200,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(b) $4,200,000 by the execution and delivery of the Seller Note.

**2.7 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the allocation schedule to be agreed by the Parties within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.8 Prorations.** Rent and other charges under the Depot Lease, utilities, and vehicle registration fees shall be prorated between Buyer and Seller as of 12:01 a.m. on the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement; (c) an assignment and assumption of the Depot Lease (the "**Lease Assignment**"); (d) the IP Assignment Agreement; (e) the Transition Services Agreement; (f) the Driver Retention Side Letter; (g) certificates of title for each Purchased Vehicle, duly endorsed; (h) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d); and (i) such other instruments of transfer as Buyer may reasonably request. Each of the foregoing shall be duly executed by Seller where applicable.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the cash payment under Section 2.6(a); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) counterparts of each Ancillary Agreement to which Buyer is a party; and (d) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. Seller is duly qualified to do business in the State of New York.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes, and each such Ancillary Agreement when executed will constitute, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Purchased Assets, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.4, require any Consent under, conflict with, or result in a breach of or default under any Assigned Contract or the Depot Lease.

**4.4 Consents.** Schedule 4.4 sets forth each Consent of any Person required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement and the assignment of the Assigned Contracts and the Depot Lease to Buyer.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and marketable title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than the Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.6 Vehicles; Inspections.** Schedule 2.1(a) sets forth a true and complete list of the Purchased Vehicles, consisting of 212 school buses, with the VIN, model year, seating capacity and most recent NYSDOT inspection date of each. Each Purchased Vehicle (a) has passed its most recent NYSDOT semi-annual inspection without an out-of-service defect remaining uncorrected; (b) is in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted; and (c) is equipped and maintained in compliance with all applicable Laws, including 15 NYCRR Part 721 and the Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards. No Purchased Vehicle is subject to an open manufacturer recall.

**4.7 Real Property.** Seller does not own any real property used in the Business. The Depot Lease is in full force and effect, and Seller has delivered a true and complete copy thereof to Buyer. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, the landlord is in default thereunder.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists each Contract to which Seller is a party relating to the Business and involving annual payments in excess of $100,000, including each District Contract (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, each counterparty, and is in full force and effect. Seller is not in material breach of any Material Contract. Seller has received no written notice of termination, non-renewal or intent to rebid from any counterparty.

**4.9 District Billing.** All invoices, mileage reports, route sheets and reimbursement claims submitted by Seller to any school district under the District Contracts during the past three (3) years were true, accurate and complete in all material respects. They were prepared in accordance with the applicable District Contract and the transportation aid requirements of the New York State Education Department. No school district has asserted any claim for refund, audit adjustment or credit.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. These include Laws governing pupil transportation, driver qualification and drug and alcohol testing, Article 19-A of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law, and hours of service.

**4.11 Permits.** Seller holds all Permits necessary to operate the Business as currently conducted, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.12 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.12, there is no action, suit, claim or investigation pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.13 Employees.** Schedule 4.13 lists each driver, monitor, mechanic and dispatcher employed in the Business, with title, hire date, CDL class and endorsements, and current rate of pay. Seller is not party to any collective bargaining agreement covering employees of the Business, except as set forth on Schedule 4.13.

**4.14 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns and paid all Taxes required to be filed or paid relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets. No Encumbrance for Taxes exists on any Purchased Asset.

**4.15 Environmental.** The depot premises and Seller's operations thereon comply in all material respects with applicable environmental Laws. To Seller's knowledge, no release of fuel or hazardous substances has occurred at the depot requiring remediation.

**4.16 Intellectual Property.** Seller owns or has the right to use all routing data and other intellectual property included in the Purchased Assets, free of any claim of infringement.

**4.17 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 Financing.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient funds to pay the amount set forth in Section 2.6(a).

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall maintain the Purchased Vehicles in their current condition and keep all required inspections current. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, amend, terminate or waive any right under any Assigned Contract or the Depot Lease, or sell or encumber any Purchased Asset.

**6.2 Access.** Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the depot, the Purchased Vehicles, the books and records of the Business, and the employees of the Business.

**6.3 Efforts.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated hereby as promptly as practicable.

**6.4 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, at its sole cost, each Consent listed on Schedule 4.4 before the Closing. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller in obtaining each such Consent, including by furnishing information regarding Buyer's qualifications and insurance.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the expiration of the Transition Services Agreement, to those employees of the Business identified in, and on the terms set forth in, the Driver Retention Side Letter.

**6.6 Operating Authority.** Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain its own operating authority and the Permits required to operate the Business. Until it does so, Seller shall provide drivers and operating authority to Buyer in accordance with the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.7 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any Person other than Buyer concerning the sale of the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets.

**6.8 Confidentiality; Non-Solicitation.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business and the Purchased Assets, except as required by Law. During the same period, Seller shall not solicit any school district party to a District Contract to award pupil transportation services within New York to Seller or its Affiliates.

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of any Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated hereby.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and the other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) each Consent listed on Schedule 4.4 shall have been obtained and delivered to Buyer, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(d) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying as to the matters in Sections 7.2(a) and (b);

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each item set forth in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying as to the matters in Sections 7.3(a) and (b), and Buyer shall have delivered each item set forth in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of the Parties;

(b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by [●], 2027, provided the terminating Party's breach is not the cause of such failure; or

(c) by either Party upon a material breach by the other Party that would cause a condition in Article VII not to be satisfied and that is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.8 and Article XI shall survive. No Party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach occurring before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. All other representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the Survival Date, being the first Scheduled Payment Date under the Seller Note. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) may be made unless written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period. A claim timely noticed shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, members, managers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability or Excluded Asset.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, directors, officers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Deductible.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses thereunder exceed $184,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar. This Section 9.4 shall not apply to breaches of the Fundamental Representations or to fraud.

**9.5 Cap.** The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a), and of Buyer under Section 9.3, shall not exceed 12% of the Purchase Price. The foregoing limitation shall not apply to (a) breaches of the Fundamental Representations, which shall be limited to the Purchase Price, (b) fraud, or (c) Buyer's obligations under Section 9.3(c) with respect to Operational Liabilities.

**9.6 Procedures.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any third-party claim. Failure to give such notice shall not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified Party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

**9.7 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which it is entitled under this Article IX against any amount otherwise payable by Buyer under the Seller Note. To exercise this right, Buyer shall deliver written notice to Seller specifying the claim and the amount set off in reasonable detail no later than five (5) Business Days before the applicable payment date. Exercise of the right of set-off in good faith, whether or not ultimately determined to be justified, shall not constitute an event of default under the Seller Note. If the claim is finally determined in Seller's favor, Buyer shall pay the amount set off, with interest at the rate provided in the Seller Note, within ten (10) Business Days after such determination.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All sales, use, transfer and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets, including New York vehicle titling fees, shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Cooperation.** The Parties shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of Tax returns and in any audit relating to the Purchased Assets.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.9 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in such courts.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.11 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each Party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy at law or in equity.

**11.12 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall control. However, the Seller Note shall control with respect to the payment, enforcement and collection of amounts owing thereunder.
