# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Boliv Coachlines, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Laivoul Student Transit, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as seller, and Recipient, as buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring from Provider substantially all of the assets used in Provider's New York pupil transportation business, including the Purchased Vehicles, the Depot Lease and the District Contracts.

B. Provider's USDOT number, operating authority and NYSDOT motor carrier registrations are Excluded Assets under the Purchase Agreement. Recipient is applying for its own operating authority and the related Permits.

C. Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement contemplates that, until Recipient obtains its own operating authority, Provider will provide drivers and operating authority to Recipient under this Agreement. Delivery of this Agreement at the Closing is required by Section 3.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and in the Purchase Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Routes**" means the home-to-school, special education, out-of-district, athletic and field-trip routes performed under the District Contracts, as such routes may be modified by the applicable school district from time to time.

"**Rider Data**" means student rosters, stop assignments, special needs and medical information, parent and guardian contact information, and other information identifying any student transported on the Routes.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees payable by Recipient for the Services under Section 4.

"**Service Personnel**" means the drivers, monitors, attendants, dispatchers and routers employed by Provider and assigned to perform the Services.

"**Services**" means the services described in Section 3 and in Exhibit A.

"**Transition Period**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of and Exhibits to this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Transition Period.** Provider shall provide the Services during the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending 150 days after the Closing Date (the "**Transition Period**"), unless this Agreement is terminated earlier in accordance with Section 10.

**2.2 Expiration.** This Agreement shall expire automatically at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the last day of the Transition Period. Sections 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12, and any payment obligation accrued before expiration or termination, shall survive.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Driver and Operating Services.** During the Transition Period, Provider shall furnish Service Personnel holding valid commercial driver's licenses with the passenger (P) and school bus (S) endorsements required by Law, qualified under Article 19-A of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law, to operate the Purchased Vehicles on the Routes in accordance with the schedules, bell times and service standards of the District Contracts.

**3.2 Operating Authority.** Provider shall operate the Purchased Vehicles on the Routes under Provider's USDOT number, operating authority and NYSDOT motor carrier registrations. Provider shall keep that authority and those registrations in full force and effect throughout the Transition Period. Provider shall display its USDOT number on the Purchased Vehicles as required by Law and shall remove it promptly upon expiration or termination of this Agreement.

**3.3 Dispatch and Routing.** Provider shall provide dispatch, routing and scheduling services for the Routes from the Depot. Provider shall give Recipient's designated personnel access to Provider's routing and dispatch software platforms, on a read-and-export basis, for the purpose of migrating the routing data included in the Purchased Assets to Recipient's systems.

**3.4 Rider Data.** Provider shall maintain and update the Rider Data for each Route in the ordinary course. Provider shall deliver the then-current Rider Data to Recipient upon request and in any event upon expiration or termination of this Agreement.

**3.5 Compliance Programs.** Provider shall maintain each Service Person in its DOT drug and alcohol testing program. Provider shall also conduct the driver physical performance tests, annual reviews, defensive driving instruction and other driver qualification activities required by Law and by 8 NYCRR Section 156.3.

**3.6 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in compliance with applicable Law and with the operational requirements of the District Contracts, and at a level of care, skill and timeliness substantially consistent with Provider's performance of comparable services for the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date.

**3.7 Excluded Services.** The Services do not include vehicle maintenance, repair, inspection, fueling or cleaning, which Recipient shall perform or procure at its own cost using the Depot facilities and the shop personnel it engages. Provider is not required to provide any service not described in this Section 3 or in Exhibit A.

## 4. SERVICE FEES; PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** In consideration for the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees set forth in Exhibit B, consisting of (a) Provider's fully loaded cost of the Service Personnel, including wages, overtime, payroll taxes, workers' compensation premiums and benefits, plus a markup of eight percent (8%); and (b) a fixed administrative fee of $42,500 per calendar month, prorated for partial months, covering dispatch, compliance and the use of Provider's operating authority.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Recipient bi-weekly in arrears, consistent with Provider's payroll cycle. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Personnel hours by Route and include reasonable supporting documentation.

**4.3 Payment.** Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within fifteen (15) days after receipt, by wire transfer to an account designated by Provider. Recipient may dispute any invoiced amount in good faith by written notice describing the dispute in reasonable detail. The Parties shall resolve any dispute through the procedure in Section 11.1.

**4.4 District Revenues.** All amounts payable by school districts under the District Contracts for services rendered on and after the Closing Date belong to Recipient. Provider shall remit to Recipient any such amount it receives within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

## 5. SERVICE PERSONNEL

**5.1 Employment.** The Service Personnel shall remain employees of Provider during the Transition Period. Provider shall be solely responsible for their wages, benefits, withholding and employment taxes, workers' compensation coverage and compliance with employment Laws. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship between Recipient and any Service Person.

**5.2 Supervision.** Provider shall supervise and direct the Service Personnel in the performance of the Services. Recipient may request the removal of any Service Person from the Routes for reasonable cause relating to safety or student welfare. Provider shall promptly reassign any such Service Person and furnish a qualified replacement.

**5.3 Retention.** The offer of employment to Service Personnel at the end of the Transition Period is governed by Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement and the Driver Retention Side Letter.

## 6. RECORDS; CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Records.** Provider shall maintain complete and accurate records relating to the Services, including driver logs, hours of service, accident reports, drug and alcohol testing records and Route mileage. It shall retain them for the periods required by Law. Recipient may inspect and copy such records on reasonable notice during normal business hours.

**6.2 Confidentiality.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement and use it only to perform or receive the Services. Provider shall handle Rider Data in compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, Section 2-d of the New York Education Law and the data privacy requirements of the District Contracts. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement, or that must be disclosed by Law.

## 7. INSURANCE

**7.1 Provider Insurance.** During the Transition Period, Provider shall maintain automobile liability insurance covering the Purchased Vehicles in the amounts required by Law for Provider's operating authority and by the District Contracts, together with workers' compensation and employer's liability insurance covering the Service Personnel. Provider shall cause Recipient and each school district party to a District Contract to be named as additional insureds under its automobile liability policy. Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance to Recipient on the Closing Date. The premium cost attributable to the Purchased Vehicles shall be included in the Service Fees.

**7.2 Recipient Insurance.** Recipient shall maintain property insurance on the Purchased Vehicles and the Depot and general liability insurance in amounts customary for a pupil transportation business.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, directors, officers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the Services or the operation of the Purchased Vehicles during the Transition Period, except to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**8.2 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates, members, managers and employees from and against all Losses to the extent arising out of (a) Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, (b) any claim by a Service Person relating to wages, benefits or employment by Provider, or (c) any failure by Provider to maintain its operating authority as required by Section 3.2.

**8.3 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any consequential, incidental, indirect, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except to the extent paid to a third party under an indemnification obligation in this Section 8.

**8.4 Limitation of Liability.** The aggregate liability of each Party arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid by Recipient under this Agreement. Recipient's obligations under Section 8.1 apply to all Losses except to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct. The limitations in this Section 8.4 and in Section 8.3 shall not apply to Operational Liabilities (as defined in the Purchase Agreement), to a Party's fraud or willful misconduct, or to Recipient's obligation to pay the Service Fees.

**8.5 Procedures.** Claims for indemnification under this Section 8 shall be made and defended in accordance with the procedures set forth in Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement, which are incorporated herein, *mutatis mutandis*.

## 9. NO WARRANTIES

Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, with respect to the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Early Termination by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate this Agreement in whole, or terminate the Services for any one or more District Contracts, upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Recipient shall give such notice no later than the date on which Recipient obtains its own operating authority and Permits sufficient to perform the applicable Routes.

**10.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within ten (10) days after receiving written notice of it. If the breach is a failure to pay undisputed Service Fees, Provider may not suspend Services affecting student transportation on fewer than ten (10) days' prior written notice to Recipient and each affected school district.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Provider shall (a) deliver to Recipient all Rider Data, routing data and records relating to the Routes; (b) cooperate in the orderly transfer of the Routes to Recipient's operating authority; and (c) remove its USDOT markings from the Purchased Vehicles. Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION; GOVERNING LAW

**11.1 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through good faith discussion between their respective operations managers. If the dispute is not resolved within ten (10) Business Days, it shall be escalated to a senior executive of each Party.

**11.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.3 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in such courts.

**11.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**12.2 Relationship of the Parties.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.

**12.3 Assignment.** Provider may assign, delegate or subcontract this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. Recipient may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Provider. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**12.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Section 8, nothing in this Agreement confers any rights or remedies on any Person other than the Parties.

**12.7 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by severe weather, road closures, acts of God, epidemics or other events beyond its reasonable control. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Weather-related school closures declared by a school district shall not be treated as a failure to perform.

**12.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
