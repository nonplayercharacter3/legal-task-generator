## 8. Schedule 4.8: Material Contracts

Each Contract listed below is a Material Contract. Each District Contract is an Assigned Contract listed on Schedule 2.1(c).

1. **Hollis Ridge Contract.** Hollis Ridge Central School District; approximately 61 home-to-school routes, plus athletic and field-trip service.
   - Annual contract value: approximately $3,100,000 (2025–2026 school year).
   - Term: through June 30, 2028, with two one-year extension options exercisable by the District.
   - Pricing: daily route rates, escalated annually by the CPI-U increase capped at the statutory percentage permitted for school bus contract extensions.
   - Assignment and change of control (Section 14): the Contractor may not assign, transfer or sublet the contract or any part of it, and no change in control of the Contractor may occur, without the prior written approval of the District's Board of Education.
   - Insurance: the Contractor must maintain $10,000,000 of combined automobile liability coverage naming the District as additional insured.

2. **Dunmore Contract.** Dunmore Union Free School District; approximately 38 home-to-school routes.
   - Annual contract value: approximately $2,050,000.
   - Term: through June 30, 2029.
   - Assignment (Article 17): the District's written consent to assignment is required.

3. **Taconic Falls Contract.** Taconic Falls Central School District; approximately 44 home-to-school and BOCES routes.
   - Annual contract value: approximately $2,380,000.
   - Term: through June 30, 2027; the District may extend under Education Law Section 305(14).
   - Assignment: the contract contains no restriction on assignment by the Contractor.

4. **Verbank Ridge Contract.** Verbank Ridge Union Free School District; special education and out-of-district routes using the Type A fleet.
   - Annual contract value: approximately $910,000.
   - Term: through June 30, 2027.
   - Assignment: the contract contains no restriction on assignment by the Contractor.

5. **Bulk Diesel Fuel Supply Agreement** with Hudson Mid-State Petroleum, Inc.
   - Annual purchases: approximately $1,450,000 at current volumes.
   - Pricing: OPIS rack index plus a fixed margin.
   - Termination: either party may terminate on 60 days' notice.

6. **Vehicle Camera and GPS Telematics Services Agreement** with Ridgeline Fleet Systems, LLC.
   - Annual fees: approximately $186,000.
   - Term: through December 31, 2027.
   - Assignment: permitted upon written notice in connection with a sale of the vehicles served.

7. **Depot Lease** with Ashgrove Depot Realty LLC.
   - Annual base rent: approximately $412,000.
   - Assignment (Section 12.1): landlord consent required.
