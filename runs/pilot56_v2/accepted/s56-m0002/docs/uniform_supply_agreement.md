# UNIFORM AND SAFETY APPAREL SUPPLY AGREEMENT

**This UNIFORM AND SAFETY APPAREL SUPPLY AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Wexcombe Workwear Co.**, a New York corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Laivoul Student Transit, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates a pupil transportation business in the Hudson Valley region of New York.

B. Supplier manufactures and distributes work uniforms, high-visibility safety apparel and related accessories.

C. Customer wishes to purchase uniforms and safety apparel from Supplier for its drivers, monitors, mechanics and depot personnel, and Supplier wishes to supply them, on the terms set forth in this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Customer Marks**" means the names, logos and trademarks of Customer furnished by Customer to Supplier for application to the Products.

"**Delivery Location**" means the Customer facility in Dutchess County, New York designated in the applicable Purchase Order, or another location in New York designated by Customer in writing.

"**Products**" means the uniform garments, high-visibility safety vests and outerwear, and accessories described in Exhibit A, as Exhibit A may be amended by the Parties in writing.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Customer under Section 3.

"**Specifications**" means the descriptions, sizing, materials, colors, embroidery and performance standards for each Product set forth in Exhibit A.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of and Exhibits to this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless terminated earlier under Section 11.

**2.2 Renewal.** This Agreement renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. ORDERS

**3.1 Purchase Orders.** Customer may order Products from time to time by issuing a Purchase Order specifying the Products, sizes, quantities, Delivery Location and requested delivery date. Customer has no minimum purchase obligation under this Agreement.

**3.2 Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within three (3) Business Days after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it requests a delivery date shorter than the lead time stated in Exhibit A.

**3.3 Changes.** Customer may change or cancel a Purchase Order without charge by written notice given before Supplier begins embroidery or other customization of the affected Products. Customer shall pay for customized Products completed or in process at the time of cancellation at the prices in Exhibit B.

**3.4 Governing Terms.** Each Purchase Order is governed by this Agreement. Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 4. SUPPLY AND DELIVERY

**4.1 Lead Times.** Supplier shall deliver Products within the lead times set forth in Exhibit A. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly if it anticipates any delay.

**4.2 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Delivery Location, packed and labeled by employee name or size as specified in the Purchase Order. Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery.

**4.3 Inspection and Acceptance.** Customer may inspect Products within fifteen (15) Business Days after delivery and reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications or the Purchase Order. Supplier shall, at its cost and at Customer's election, replace any rejected Product within ten (10) Business Days or credit Customer for its price.

**4.4 Fitting Days.** At Customer's request, and up to four (4) times in each contract year without charge, Supplier shall send a representative to the Delivery Location for size fittings of new personnel.

**4.5 Inventory Program.** Supplier shall maintain at its warehouse an inventory of the stock Products identified in Exhibit A in quantities sufficient to fill reasonably anticipated reorders within the stated lead times.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Prices.** Prices for the Products are set forth in Exhibit B and are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of the term. Supplier may thereafter adjust prices once per contract year on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. No increase may exceed four percent (4%) of the then-current price, except for documented increases in the cost of raw materials affecting high-visibility fabric, which Supplier shall pass through at cost.

**5.2 Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon delivery. Each invoice shall reference the applicable Purchase Order.

**5.3 Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith by notifying Supplier in writing of the dispute in reasonable detail. The Parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 12.1.

**5.4 Taxes.** Prices exclude sales and use taxes, which Supplier shall separately state on each invoice and Customer shall pay, unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 6. PRODUCT STANDARDS

**6.1 Specifications.** All Products shall conform to the Specifications.

**6.2 Safety Apparel.** Each high-visibility vest and outerwear Product shall be certified as compliant with ANSI/ISEA 107-2020 for the performance class stated in Exhibit A. Supplier shall keep certification documentation current and furnish copies to Customer upon request.

**6.3 Changes to Specifications.** Supplier shall not change the materials, manufacturer or construction of any Product without Customer's prior written consent.

## 7. WARRANTIES

**7.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for twelve (12) months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and (c) for safety apparel, retain its ANSI/ISEA 107 retroreflective and fluorescent performance when laundered in accordance with the care instructions.

**7.2 Remedy.** Supplier shall repair or replace any Product that breaches Section 7.1 at no charge, or, if repair or replacement is not commercially practicable, refund its price.

**7.3 Compliance.** Supplier warrants that the Products are manufactured, labeled and sold in compliance with applicable Law, including textile labeling and flammability requirements.

**7.4 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION 7, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. CUSTOMER MARKS

**8.1 License.** Customer grants Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to reproduce the Customer Marks on the Products solely to fill Customer's Purchase Orders during the term.

**8.2 Restrictions.** Supplier shall not sell or supply any Product bearing the Customer Marks to any other Person. Upon expiration or termination, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, deliver to Customer or destroy all embroidery files, transfers and inventory bearing the Customer Marks, and Customer shall pay for any such finished inventory it elects to receive.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer and its members, managers and employees from and against all third-party claims, and related losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising out of (a) any defect in a Product, (b) any breach of Section 7.3, or (c) any claim that a Product, other than the Customer Marks, infringes a third party's intellectual property rights.

**9.2 By Customer.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Supplier and its directors, officers and employees from and against all third-party claims, and related losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising out of any claim that the Customer Marks infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

**9.3 Procedures.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's cost. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party, other than the payment of money funded by the indemnifying Party, without the indemnified Party's consent.

**9.4 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for consequential, incidental, indirect, special or punitive damages, or lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except to the extent paid to a third party under Section 9.1 or 9.2.

**9.5 Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total amounts paid and payable by Customer under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This Section 9.5 does not apply to the Parties' obligations under Sections 9.1 and 9.2, to Customer's obligation to pay for Products delivered, or to a Party's fraud or willful misconduct.

## 10. INSURANCE

During the term and for one (1) year thereafter, Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Customer as an additional insured. Supplier shall deliver a certificate of insurance to Customer upon request.

## 11. TERMINATION

**11.1 For Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice to Supplier.

**11.2 For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

**11.3 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**11.4 Effect.** Expiration or termination does not affect Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of termination, which the Parties shall complete under this Agreement unless Customer cancels them under Section 3.3. Sections 5.3, 7, 8.2, 9, 12 and 13 survive expiration or termination.

## 12. DISPUTES; GOVERNING LAW

**12.1 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good faith discussion between their respective account managers and, if the dispute is not resolved within ten (10) Business Days, between senior officers of each Party.

**12.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**12.3 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Dutchess County, New York, or, where federal jurisdiction requires, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS

**13.1 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page or such other address as a Party may designate by notice.

**13.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates, upon written notice to the other Party. Supplier may not subcontract the manufacture or embroidery of Products bearing the Customer Marks without Customer's prior written consent.

**13.3 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

**13.4 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event delays Supplier's performance for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may cancel the affected Purchase Orders without liability.

**13.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**13.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**13.7 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including its Exhibits and the Purchase Orders issued under it, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings with respect to that subject matter.

**13.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
