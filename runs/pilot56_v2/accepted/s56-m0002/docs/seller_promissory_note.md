# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$4,200,000**

**Issue Date: the Closing Date under the APA**

**FOR VALUE RECEIVED**, **Laivoul Student Transit, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Boliv Coachlines, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal amount of **Four Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,200,000)**. Maker shall also pay interest on the unpaid principal balance, in each case in the amounts, at the times and in the manner set forth in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

## 1. Purchase Agreement; Principal Amount; Issue Date

1.1 This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026, between Maker, as buyer, and Holder, as seller (the "**APA**" or "**Purchase Agreement**"). It is issued and delivered by Maker at the Closing under Sections 2.6(b) and 3.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement in partial payment of the Purchase Price.

1.2 The original principal amount of this Note is **$4,200,000** (the "**Principal Amount**").

1.3 This Note is dated and issued as of **the Closing Date under the APA** (the "**Issue Date**").

1.4 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Payment of Principal

2.1 **Installments.** Maker shall repay the Principal Amount in eight (8) consecutive installments of $525,000 each (each, a "**Principal Installment**"). Each Principal Installment is due on a Scheduled Payment Date.

2.2 **Scheduled Payment Dates.** The first Principal Installment shall be due and payable on the date that is **120 days after the Issue Date**. Each later Principal Installment shall be due and payable on the date that is three (3) calendar months after the preceding Scheduled Payment Date. Each date on which a Principal Installment is due under this Section 2.2 is a "**Scheduled Payment Date**." The last Scheduled Payment Date is the "**Maturity Date**." On the Maturity Date, all unpaid principal and all accrued and unpaid interest under this Note shall be due and payable in full.

2.3 **Business Days.** If any Scheduled Payment Date or other payment date under this Note falls on a day that is not a Business Day, the payment shall be due on the next Business Day. Interest shall continue to accrue for the period of the extension.

## 3. Interest

3.1 **Rate.** The outstanding principal balance of this Note shall bear interest from the Issue Date until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum.

3.2 **Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest shall be payable in arrears on each Scheduled Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

3.3 **Computation.** Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

3.4 **Default Rate.** While an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) is continuing, the outstanding principal balance shall bear interest at a rate equal to the rate in Section 3.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum. Interest at the default rate is payable on demand.

3.5 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 4. Manner and Application of Payments

4.1 **Manner.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable payment date. Payments shall be made without deduction, except as provided in Section 5.

4.2 **Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest, then to the Principal Installment then due, and then to any remaining principal in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Set-Off

Maker may set off against any amount payable under this Note any amount to which Maker is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, in the manner provided in Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement. Any amount so set off shall reduce the payment then due under this Note by the amount set off.

## 6. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, together with interest accrued on the amount prepaid to the date of prepayment. Partial prepayments shall be applied to Principal Installments in inverse order of maturity and shall not postpone or reduce any Principal Installment next coming due.

## 7. Events of Default; Remedies

7.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any Principal Installment or interest when due under this Note, and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after Maker receives written notice of the failure from Holder;

(b) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(c) Maker commences a voluntary case under the United States Bankruptcy Code or any other insolvency law, or seeks the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its assets;

(d) an involuntary case is commenced against Maker under any insolvency law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(e) Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the Purchased Assets to a Person other than an Affiliate of Maker, other than a collateral assignment to Maker's lenders.

7.2 **Remedies.** If an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) or (e) occurs and is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. If an Event of Default under Section 7.1(b), (c) or (d) occurs, the unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest shall become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action by Holder. Holder's rights under this Section 7.2 are subject to Section 8.

7.3 **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. Subordination

8.1 **Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owed to any bank, commercial finance company or other institutional lender that provides financing for the acquisition of the Purchased Assets or the working capital of Maker, together with all refinancings of that indebtedness (the "**Senior Debt**").

8.2 **Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall execute and deliver any subordination agreement reasonably requested by a holder of Senior Debt consistent with this Section 8. Such an agreement shall permit Maker to pay regularly scheduled principal and interest under this Note so long as no payment default on the Senior Debt is continuing.

8.3 **Turnover.** Any payment that Holder receives in violation of this Section 8 shall be held in trust for the holders of the Senior Debt and promptly paid over to them.

## 9. Waivers; Amendment; Transfer

9.1 **Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest, except for the notices expressly required by this Note.

9.2 **No Waiver by Holder.** No delay or failure by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right. No single or partial exercise of a right precludes any further exercise of it.

9.3 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

9.4 **Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker. Any transferee takes this Note subject to Maker's rights under Sections 5 and 8. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder.

9.5 **Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

9.6 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor. Before issuing it, Maker shall receive a customary affidavit of loss and an indemnity from Holder, or the mutilated Note.

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

10.2 **Jurisdiction.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that such an action has been brought in an inconvenient forum.

10.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and benefits Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

11.2 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11.3 **Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

11.4 **Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed by electronic signature. An electronically signed copy of this Note is effective as an original.
