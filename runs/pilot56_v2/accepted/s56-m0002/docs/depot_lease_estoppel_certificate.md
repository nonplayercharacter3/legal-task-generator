# LANDLORD ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATE

**Premises:** Bus Depot and Maintenance Facility, [●], Town of [●], Dutchess County, New York (the "**Premises**")

**Landlord:** Ashgrove Depot Realty LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**")

**Tenant:** Boliv Coachlines, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Tenant**")

**Proposed Assignee:** Laivoul Student Transit, LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Assignee**")

**Date:** [●], 2026

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Landlord understands that Tenant and Assignee are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026, under which Tenant will assign to Assignee, and Assignee will assume, Tenant's interest as tenant under the Lease described in Section 2. The assignment will take effect on the closing of that transaction (the "**Closing**").

1.2 Landlord delivers this certificate to Tenant and Assignee at Tenant's request. Landlord acknowledges that Assignee, and any lender providing financing to Assignee for the acquisition (an "**Assignee Lender**"), will rely on the statements in this certificate in completing the transaction.

## 2. The Lease

2.1 The lease of the Premises consists solely of the following documents (together, the "**Lease**"):

(a) Lease Agreement dated as of August 1, 2019, between Landlord, as landlord, and Tenant, as tenant; and

(b) First Amendment to Lease dated as of March 15, 2023, between Landlord and Tenant.

2.2 A true, correct and complete copy of the Lease is attached as Exhibit A. The Lease has not been modified, supplemented or amended except as listed in Section 2.1. There are no other agreements, written or oral, between Landlord and Tenant relating to the Premises.

2.3 The Lease is in full force and effect and represents the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Premises.

## 3. Premises and Permitted Use

3.1 The Premises consist of approximately [●] acres of land, together with the bus maintenance garage, bus wash, dispatch and office building, fueling island, diesel storage tanks, paved bus parking area and related improvements located on the land.

3.2 The permitted use of the Premises under the Lease is the parking, storage, dispatch, fueling, washing, maintenance and repair of school buses and other passenger vehicles, together with related office and administrative uses. Landlord confirms that Tenant's current use of the Premises as a school bus depot complies with the permitted use clause of the Lease.

3.3 Tenant has accepted possession of the Premises and is in occupancy. All improvements that Landlord was required to construct or pay for under the Lease have been completed and paid for. No tenant improvement allowance or other payment is owed by Landlord to Tenant.

## 4. Term

4.1 The current term of the Lease commenced on August 1, 2019, and expires on July 31, 2034.

4.2 Tenant has two (2) remaining options to extend the term of the Lease, each for five (5) years, on the terms set forth in the Lease. Neither option has been exercised or has lapsed.

## 5. Rent and Other Charges

5.1 The current annual base rent under the Lease is $412,000, payable in equal monthly installments of $34,333.33 in advance on the first day of each calendar month. Base rent is subject to adjustment on each August 1 as provided in the Lease.

5.2 Tenant pays, as additional rent, all real estate taxes, insurance premiums and utilities attributable to the Premises, as provided in the Lease.

5.3 Base rent and additional rent have been paid through [●], 2026. No rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

5.4 Tenant has deposited with Landlord a security deposit of $68,666.66, which Landlord holds under the Lease. No portion of the security deposit has been applied by Landlord.

5.5 Tenant is not entitled to any rent abatement, concession, credit or free rent period under the Lease.

## 6. No Defaults

6.1 To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant is not in default under the Lease. No event has occurred that, with the giving of notice or the passage of time or both, would constitute a default by Tenant.

6.2 Landlord has not delivered to Tenant any notice of default that remains uncured.

6.3 To Landlord's knowledge, Landlord is not in default under the Lease. Landlord has not received from Tenant any notice of default by Landlord that remains uncured.

6.4 To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant has no defense, offset or counterclaim against Landlord for the payment of rent or the performance of its obligations under the Lease.

## 7. Environmental and Compliance Matters

7.1 Landlord has not received any written notice from any governmental authority of any violation of any law, ordinance or regulation affecting the Premises, including environmental laws, that remains uncorrected.

7.2 Landlord has received no written notice of any release of petroleum or hazardous substances at the Premises requiring remediation under applicable law.

7.3 Landlord acknowledges that the diesel storage tanks at the Premises are registered under Petroleum Bulk Storage Registration No. 3-6[●] issued by the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation. Landlord has no objection to the transfer of that registration to Assignee in connection with the assignment of the Lease.

## 8. Ownership; No Transfers

8.1 Landlord is the fee owner of the Premises and the sole holder of the landlord's interest under the Lease. Landlord has not assigned, transferred or pledged its interest in the Lease or the rents payable under it, except to the holder of the Mortgage described in Section 8.2.

8.2 The Premises are encumbered by a mortgage held by [●] (the "**Mortgage**"). The Mortgage holder has delivered to Tenant a subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement dated as of [●], 2019, which remains in effect and binds any successor to Landlord's interest by foreclosure or deed in lieu of foreclosure.

8.3 Landlord has not received notice of any pending or threatened condemnation or eminent domain proceeding affecting any part of the Premises.

8.4 No person other than Tenant holds any option to purchase, right of first refusal or right of first offer with respect to the Premises or any part of it.

## 9. Consent to Assignment

9.1 Under Section 12.1 of the Lease, the assignment of the Lease by Tenant requires Landlord's consent. Landlord consents to the assignment of the Lease by Tenant to Assignee, and to the assumption of the Lease by Assignee, effective on the Closing.

9.2 Landlord has reviewed such information regarding Assignee's financial condition, insurance and qualifications as Landlord has requested. Landlord confirms that no assignment fee, rent increase, additional security deposit or other consideration is payable as a condition of its consent, other than reimbursement of Landlord's reasonable attorneys' fees in reviewing the assignment, not to exceed $5,000, which Tenant shall pay.

9.3 Following the Closing, Landlord shall send all notices under the Lease to Assignee at the address set forth in the assignment and assumption of the Lease, with a copy to any Assignee Lender that has given Landlord written notice of its address.

9.4 Landlord shall transfer the security deposit described in Section 5.4 to the account of Assignee as tenant under the Lease, effective on the Closing, upon written direction from Tenant. Landlord shall continue to hold the security deposit under the Lease for the benefit of Assignee.

## 10. Leasehold Financing

10.1 Landlord acknowledges that Assignee may grant to an Assignee Lender a collateral assignment of, or leasehold mortgage on, its interest under the Lease. Landlord consents to that grant to the extent permitted by the Lease.

10.2 Landlord shall give any Assignee Lender that has delivered its address to Landlord a copy of any notice of default given to Assignee under the Lease. The Assignee Lender shall have the same period to cure the default as Assignee has under the Lease, plus an additional thirty (30) days.

## 11. Reliance; Effect

11.1 This certificate may be relied upon by Tenant, Assignee and any Assignee Lender, and by their respective successors and assigns.

11.2 The statements in this certificate are made as of its date. Landlord is not required to update this certificate.

11.3 Nothing in this certificate modifies or amends the Lease.

11.4 The individual signing this certificate on behalf of Landlord is duly authorized to do so.
