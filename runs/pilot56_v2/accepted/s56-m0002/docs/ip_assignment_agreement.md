# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, being the Closing Date (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Boliv Coachlines, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Assignor**"), and **Laivoul Student Transit, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as seller, and Assignee, as buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignee is acquiring from Assignor substantially all of the assets used in Assignor's New York pupil transportation business conducted from the depot in Dutchess County, New York (the "**Business**").

B. Section 2.1(e) of the Purchase Agreement includes in the Purchased Assets the routing data, route maps, stop lists and historical ridership data of the Business, and the intellectual property assigned under this Agreement. Delivery of this Agreement at the Closing is required by Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the Purchase Price paid under the Purchase Agreement and the mutual covenants set forth herein, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Assigned IP**" means the Routing Materials, the Business Documentation and all Intellectual Property Rights in either of them, in each case as described more fully in Exhibit A.

"**Business Documentation**" means the written materials prepared by or for Assignor exclusively for use in the Business, including depot operating procedures, emergency and evacuation plans for the Purchased Vehicles, driver route instructions, district-specific service protocols and bell-time schedules, and the forms and templates used to prepare route sheets and mileage reports under the District Contracts.

"**Excluded IP**" means (a) the "Boliv" and "Boliv Coachlines" names, logos and trademarks, and any domain names incorporating them; (b) Assignor's enterprise licenses for its routing, dispatch and payroll software platforms, and the software itself; and (c) any other intellectual property of Assignor that is an Excluded Asset under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement and Schedule 2.2 to the Disclosure Schedule.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all rights of every kind in any jurisdiction in and to copyrights and works of authorship (whether or not registered), databases and compilations, trade secrets and confidential know-how, and all other proprietary rights, together with all rights to sue for and recover damages for past, present and future infringement or misappropriation of any of the foregoing.

"**Rider Data**" has the meaning given in the Transition Services Agreement.

"**Routing Materials**" means the routing data, route maps, stop lists, stop coordinates, turn-by-turn route directions, run and tier schedules, and historical ridership and mileage data of the Business for the Routes, in whatever form or medium held, including all exports, extracts and copies of such data generated by Assignor's routing and dispatch software platforms for the Business.

"**Routes**" has the meaning given in the Transition Services Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of and Exhibits to this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Closing, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**2.2 Accrued Rights.** The assignment in Section 2.1 includes all rights of Assignor to income, royalties and payments relating to the Assigned IP accruing after the Closing, and all claims and causes of action relating to the Assigned IP, whether arising before, on or after the Closing, including the right to sue for and retain damages for past infringement or misappropriation.

**2.3 Excluded IP.** The Excluded IP is not assigned under this Agreement and remains the property of Assignor. Data generated by Assignor's routing, dispatch and payroll software platforms for the Business, including the Routing Materials, is not Excluded IP, consistent with Schedule 2.2 to the Disclosure Schedule.

**2.4 Rider Data.** To the extent any Rider Data is contained in or associated with the Routing Materials, it is transferred to Assignee as part of the Routing Materials solely for use in providing pupil transportation services under the District Contracts. Each Party shall handle Rider Data in compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, Section 2-d of the New York Education Law and the data privacy requirements of the District Contracts. Assignor's maintenance and delivery of Rider Data during the Transition Period are governed by Section 3.4 of the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. DELIVERY

**3.1 Delivery at Closing.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, in electronic form reasonably acceptable to Assignee, a complete copy of the Business Documentation and of all Routing Materials then held by Assignor in exportable form.

**3.2 Data Migration.** Assignee's access to Assignor's routing and dispatch software platforms for the purpose of migrating the Routing Materials to Assignee's systems shall be provided under Section 3.3 of the Transition Services Agreement. Upon expiration or termination of the Transition Services Agreement, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee the then-current Routing Materials as required by Section 10.3 of that agreement.

**3.3 Retained Copies.** After the later of the Closing and the delivery required by Section 3.2, Assignor shall not retain any copy of the Routing Materials or Business Documentation, except (a) copies required to be retained by Law or by the records retention requirements of the District Contracts, (b) copies needed to perform the Services during the Transition Period, and (c) archival copies held in routine electronic back-up systems that are not readily accessible. Assignor shall hold any retained copies in confidence under Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement and shall not use them for any other purpose.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**4.1 Further Acts.** At Assignee's reasonable request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably necessary to vest, perfect, record or confirm Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP.

**4.2 Cooperation.** Assignor shall make available to Assignee, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours, those of its personnel with knowledge of the Routing Materials, for the purpose of explaining the structure, coding conventions and history of the Routing Materials. Assignee shall reimburse Assignor's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of any such cooperation requested after the Transition Period.

**4.3 Power of Attorney.** If Assignor fails to execute any instrument required by Section 4.1 within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request, Assignor appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, solely to execute and deliver that instrument in Assignor's name.

## 5. COVENANTS

**5.1 No Use.** From and after the Closing, Assignor shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, use the Assigned IP, except to the extent necessary to perform the Services under the Transition Services Agreement.

**5.2 Representations.** Assignor's representations and warranties with respect to the Assigned IP are set forth in Section 4.16 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.3 Covenants for Purposes of Article IX.** Each Party's obligations under this Agreement are covenants for the purposes of Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Ancillary Agreement.** This Agreement is the "IP Assignment Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is an Ancillary Agreement.

**6.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**6.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Assignee may collaterally assign its rights hereunder to its lenders and may assign its rights hereunder to any successor to the Business. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**6.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**6.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**6.7 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in such courts.

**6.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**6.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
