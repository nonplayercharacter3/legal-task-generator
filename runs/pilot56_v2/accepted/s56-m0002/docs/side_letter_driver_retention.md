**Re: Driver Retention Side Letter: Asset Purchase Agreement between Laivoul Student Transit, LLC and Boliv Coachlines, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is the "Driver Retention Side Letter" referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Laivoul Student Transit, LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and Boliv Coachlines, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Seller**"). It is delivered at the Closing under Section 3.2(f) of the Purchase Agreement. It sets out the terms on which Buyer will offer employment to employees of the Business under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 5.3 of the Transition Services Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement or, where indicated, the Transition Services Agreement.

1.2 In this Side Letter:

"**Covered Employees**" means the Service Personnel (as defined in the Transition Services Agreement) listed on Annex A. They consist of the school bus drivers, bus monitors and attendants, and dispatchers and routers employed in the Business and identified on Schedule 4.13 to the Disclosure Schedule. Annex A will be updated under Section 2.3.

"**Transfer Date**" means the day immediately following the last day of the Transition Period. If the Transition Services Agreement is terminated earlier in whole under Section 10 of that agreement, it means the day immediately following the effective date of that termination. If the Transition Services Agreement is terminated as to the Services for one or more District Contracts under Section 10.1 of that agreement, the Transfer Date for the Covered Employees assigned to the affected Routes is the day immediately following the effective date of that partial termination.

"**Transferred Employee**" means each Covered Employee who accepts Buyer's offer of employment and commences employment with Buyer on the applicable Transfer Date.

## 2. Offers of Employment

2.1 **Offers.** No later than thirty (30) days before the scheduled expiration of the Transition Period, Buyer shall make a written offer of employment to each Covered Employee who is then actively employed by Seller and assigned to the Services. Each offer shall be effective as of the applicable Transfer Date. If a partial or early termination is noticed under Section 10.1 of the Transition Services Agreement, Buyer shall make the offers to the affected Covered Employees no later than ten (10) Business Days before the applicable Transfer Date.

2.2 **Terms of Offer.** Each offer shall provide for:

(a) a position substantially comparable to the Covered Employee's position with Seller, at the Depot or another location within twenty-five (25) miles of the Depot;

(b) a base hourly rate or salary not less than the Covered Employee's rate of pay as of the Closing Date, as shown in the employee census in Data Room folder 7.2;

(c) credit for the Covered Employee's length of service with Seller for purposes of route bidding, seniority, paid time off accrual and eligibility under Buyer's benefit plans, to the extent permitted by those plans and applicable Law; and

(d) eligibility to participate in Buyer's group health plan from the Transfer Date, with waiting periods and pre-existing condition limitations waived to the extent permitted by the plan and its insurer.

2.3 **Updates to Annex A.** Seller shall deliver an updated Annex A to Buyer no later than forty-five (45) days before the scheduled expiration of the Transition Period, and promptly after any notice of termination under Section 10.1 of the Transition Services Agreement. The update shall reflect hires, separations and reassignments of Service Personnel during the Transition Period that occurred in the ordinary course and in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Transition Services Agreement.

2.4 **Conditions.** Buyer's offers may be conditioned on the Covered Employee's satisfaction of the following:

(a) the qualification requirements applicable to Buyer's position as a pupil transportation employer, including Article 19-A of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law, 8 NYCRR Section 156.3, and the fingerprinting and criminal history review required for school bus drivers and monitors;

(b) a pre-employment controlled substances test under 49 CFR Part 382, as applicable; and

(c) completion of Buyer's standard new-hire documentation, including Form I-9.

Seller shall cooperate with Buyer, to the extent permitted by Law and with any required employee authorization, in transferring driver qualification files, testing histories and training records to Buyer so that Covered Employees can be qualified efficiently.

## 3. Retention Bonus

3.1 **Amount.** Buyer shall pay a retention bonus to each Transferred Employee who is a school bus driver holding CDL Class B with P and S endorsements and who remains continuously employed by Buyer for ninety (90) days after the Transfer Date. The bonus is $1,500, less applicable withholdings. It shall be paid in the first regular payroll after that ninetieth (90th) day.

3.2 **Monitors and Dispatchers.** Buyer shall pay a retention bonus of $750, less applicable withholdings, on the same terms to each other Transferred Employee.

3.3 **Cost.** Buyer bears the retention bonuses under this Section 3. They are not Service Fees and are not reimbursable by Seller.

## 4. Seller Obligations

4.1 **Accrued Compensation.** Seller shall pay each Covered Employee, in accordance with applicable Law, all wages, overtime, accrued and unused paid time off (to the extent payable under Seller's policies or Law), and other compensation earned through the day before the applicable Transfer Date. Those amounts are Excluded Liabilities. To the extent they relate to the Transition Period, they are included in the Service Fees under Section 4.1 of the Transition Services Agreement.

4.2 **Benefit Plans.** Seller retains all Liabilities under its employee benefit plans, including COBRA continuation coverage for Covered Employees and their beneficiaries who experience a qualifying event on or before the applicable Transfer Date.

4.3 **Non-Interference.** During the Transition Period, Seller shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, induce or encourage any Covered Employee to decline Buyer's offer. Seller shall not offer any Covered Employee a transfer to Seller's other operations effective on or after the Transfer Date without Buyer's prior written consent. Seller shall make Covered Employees reasonably available, during non-driving hours and at Buyer's cost for any time paid, to attend Buyer's orientation and information sessions.

4.4 **Non-Solicitation.** For two (2) years after the applicable Transfer Date, Seller shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, solicit for employment any Transferred Employee. This Section 4.4 does not restrict general solicitations not targeted at Transferred Employees, or hiring any person whose employment with Buyer ended at least six (6) months earlier.

4.5 **Non-Accepting Employees.** Seller retains all Liabilities relating to Covered Employees who do not accept Buyer's offer or who do not commence employment with Buyer, including severance and any notice obligations under the federal Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act or the New York State WARN Act. However, if Buyer fails to make an offer to a Covered Employee as required by Section 2, Buyer shall reimburse Seller for severance and notice Liabilities attributable to that Covered Employee.

## 5. Buyer Obligations

5.1 **Transferred Employees.** From the applicable Transfer Date, Buyer is responsible for all wages, benefits and other Liabilities relating to the employment of Transferred Employees by Buyer.

5.2 **Employer Status.** Nothing in this Side Letter makes Buyer the employer of any Covered Employee before the applicable Transfer Date. Section 5.1 of the Transition Services Agreement continues to govern the employment of Service Personnel during the Transition Period.

## 6. Communications

6.1 The Parties shall agree on the initial written communication to Covered Employees about the transaction and the offers of employment. Neither Party shall make any written communication to Covered Employees about their post-Transfer Date employment that is inconsistent with this Side Letter.

6.2 Each Party shall keep the employee census and personal information of Covered Employees confidential. Each shall use that information only for the purposes of this Side Letter and in compliance with applicable Law.

## 7. No Third-Party Rights

Nothing in this Side Letter (a) confers any right, benefit or remedy on any Covered Employee or other Person other than the Parties, (b) guarantees employment for any period, or (c) restricts Buyer's right to terminate the employment of, or change the terms of employment of, any Transferred Employee after the Transfer Date, subject to Section 3 and applicable Law.

## 8. General

8.1 **Ancillary Agreement.** This Side Letter is an Ancillary Agreement. Each Party's obligations under it are covenants for the purposes of Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

8.2 **Notices.** All notices under this Side Letter shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

8.3 **Amendment; Assignment.** This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign it without the prior written consent of the other Party.

8.4 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Side Letter.

8.5 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
