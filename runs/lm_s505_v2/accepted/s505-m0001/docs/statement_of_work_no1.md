# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**Under the Supply Agreement dated April 1, 2024**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of April 1, 2024 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc.** ("**Customer**") and **Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC** ("**Supplier**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. Purpose and Definitions

1.1 This SOW describes the Products, Specifications, pricing, delivery locations, capacity commitment, safety stock and related terms for Customer's purchases of filter media and filter bags for the Covered Facilities.

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.3 "**Covered Facilities**" means the Customer manufacturing plants listed in Section 4.1.

## 2. SOW Period

2.1 This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for the Term of the Agreement, unless the Agreement or this SOW is terminated earlier under the Agreement.

2.2 Section 2.3 of the Agreement applies to this SOW.

## 3. Products and Specifications

3.1 **Products.** The Products under this SOW are:

| Item No. | Product | Description | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NF-101 | PTFE-membrane laminated polyester needlefelt, 16 oz/yd² | Roll goods, 60 in. and 72 in. widths, singed and heat-set, ePTFE membrane laminated to the dust-cake side | Linear yard |
| NF-102 | PTFE-membrane laminated aramid needlefelt, 16 oz/yd² | Roll goods, 60 in. width, for high-temperature service up to 400°F continuous | Linear yard |
| FB-201 | Finished filter bag, polyester/ePTFE (NF-101 media) | 6 in. dia. × 120 in., snap-band top, sewn bottom, thread compatible with media | Each |
| FB-202 | Finished filter bag, polyester/ePTFE (NF-101 media) | 6.25 in. dia. × 144 in., snap-band top, sewn bottom | Each |
| FB-203 | Finished filter bag, aramid/ePTFE (NF-102 media) | 5.75 in. dia. × 120 in., snap-band top, sewn bottom, PTFE thread | Each |

3.2 **Specifications.** Each Product shall conform to Customer Specification Sheets NFS-SPEC-101 (Rev. C), NFS-SPEC-102 (Rev. B) and NFS-SPEC-200 (Rev. D) (Filter Bag Fabrication), copies of which Customer has delivered to Supplier and which are incorporated in this SOW by reference. Key performance criteria include:

(a) air permeability of 3 to 8 CFM/ft² at 0.5 in. w.g., measured under ASTM D737;

(b) minimum filtration efficiency of 99.99% at 0.3 µm under Customer's test protocol in NFS-SPEC-101 and NFS-SPEC-102;

(c) minimum membrane peel strength as stated in the applicable Specification Sheet; and

(d) dimensional tolerances for finished bags of ±0.125 in. on diameter and ±0.5 in. on length.

3.3 **Custom Products.** Items FB-201, FB-202 and FB-203 are made to Customer's proprietary design for purposes of Section 13.2 and Section 13.3 of the Agreement.

3.4 **Changes.** Any change to a Specification Sheet requires a written revision signed by both parties. Section 7.4 of the Agreement applies to any change proposed by Supplier.

## 4. Covered Facilities and Delivery

4.1 **Covered Facilities.** Customer will order Products for delivery to the following Covered Facilities:

| Facility | Address | Receiving Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Cleveland Plant | 4100 Commerce Parkway, Cleveland, Ohio 44135 | Receiving Supervisor, Dock 3 |
| Louisville Plant | 9800 Preston Crossing Drive, Louisville, Kentucky 40229 | Materials Manager |
| Tulsa Plant | 7525 East 46th Street, Tulsa, Oklahoma 74145 | Receiving Supervisor |

4.2 **Delivery Terms.** Products shall be delivered FCA Supplier's facility at 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052 (Incoterms 2020), in accordance with Section 6.1 of the Agreement. Supplier shall arrange carriage to the applicable Covered Facility using Customer's designated carrier and Customer's freight account.

4.3 **Lead Times.** Standard lead times from Supplier's acceptance of a Purchase Order are:

(a) roll goods (NF-101, NF-102): fifteen (15) business days; and

(b) finished filter bags (FB-201, FB-202, FB-203): twenty (20) business days.

4.4 **Packaging.** Roll goods shall be shipped on 3 in. cores, wrapped in polyethylene and labeled with item number, lot number, roll length and Purchase Order number. Finished bags shall be packed in cartons of no more than fifty (50) bags, with each carton labeled with item number, lot number, quantity and Purchase Order number. A certificate of conformance shall accompany each shipment under Section 6.4 of the Agreement.

## 5. Pricing

5.1 **Prices.** The prices for Products are:

| Item No. | Unit Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| NF-101 | $14.85 per linear yard |
| NF-102 | $27.40 per linear yard |
| FB-201 | $38.60 each |
| FB-202 | $44.25 each |
| FB-203 | $71.90 each |

5.2 **Volume Pricing.** If Customer's aggregate purchases under this SOW in a contract year exceed $3,500,000, Supplier shall apply a two percent (2%) rebate to all purchases in that contract year above that amount. Supplier shall issue the rebate as a credit against the next invoice within forty-five (45) days after the end of the contract year.

5.3 **Price Adjustments.** Prices are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of this SOW. Later adjustments are governed by Section 5.2 of the Agreement.

5.4 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice under Section 5.3 of the Agreement and send invoices to Customer's Accounts Payable department at the Cleveland Plant address, with a copy by email to the address Customer designates in writing.

## 6. Capacity Commitment and Safety Stock

6.1 **Capacity Commitment.** For purposes of Section 3.2 of the Agreement, Supplier commits to the following monthly capacity for Customer:

| Product Group | Monthly Capacity Commitment |
|---|---|
| Roll goods (NF-101 and NF-102 combined) | 40,000 linear yards |
| Finished filter bags (FB-201, FB-202 and FB-203 combined) | 12,000 bags |

6.2 **Safety Stock.** For purposes of Section 8.2 of the Agreement, Supplier shall hold at its Gastonia, North Carolina facility, at its own cost, finished Products equal to at least four (4) weeks of Customer's then-current forecasted demand for each item number. Supplier shall report safety stock levels to Customer monthly.

6.3 **Forecasts.** Customer shall deliver the quarterly forecasts required by Section 4.4 of the Agreement broken out by item number and Covered Facility.

## 7. Quality

7.1 **First Article.** Before the first production shipment of each item number, Supplier shall submit first-article samples with full test data for Customer's approval. Customer shall approve or reject first articles within fifteen (15) business days after receipt.

7.2 **Lot Testing.** Supplier shall test each production lot for basis weight, air permeability and membrane peel strength and shall keep lot test records for at least five (5) years. Supplier shall provide lot test records to Customer on request.

7.3 **Inspection.** Inspection, rejection and remedies are governed by Sections 7.2 and 7.3 of the Agreement.

## 8. Account Management

8.1 **Supplier Contacts.** Supplier designates its Director of Key Accounts as primary contact for this SOW and its Quality Manager as quality contact.

8.2 **Customer Contacts.** Customer designates its Director of Strategic Sourcing as primary contact for this SOW and its Supplier Quality Engineer as quality contact.

8.3 **Business Reviews.** The parties shall hold quarterly business reviews covering delivery performance, quality, forecasts, capacity and continuous improvement. Supplier shall target on-time delivery of at least ninety-eight percent (98%) and a reject rate below five hundred (500) parts per million.

8.4 Contacts are for day-to-day coordination only. Formal notices must be given under Section 19 of the Agreement.

## 9. General

9.1 This SOW is governed by the Agreement. Except as stated in this SOW, all terms of the Agreement apply to the Products ordered under it.

9.2 This SOW may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

9.3 This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
