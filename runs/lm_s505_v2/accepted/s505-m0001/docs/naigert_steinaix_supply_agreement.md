# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Commerce Parkway, Cleveland, Ohio 44135 ("**Customer**"), and **Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052 ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer designs, manufactures and sells industrial dust collection and air filtration equipment.

B. Supplier manufactures PTFE-membrane laminated needlefelt filter media, finished filter bags and other technical fabrics.

C. Customer wishes to buy, and Supplier wishes to sell, such products on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all laws, regulations, ordinances and orders that apply to a party's performance under this Agreement.

1.3 "**Customer Personal Data**" means any Personal Data that Supplier receives from or on behalf of Customer, or otherwise Processes, in connection with this Agreement.

1.4 "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the parties governing Supplier's Processing of Customer Personal Data, as amended from time to time.

1.5 "**End Products**" means dust collectors, baghouses, cartridge collectors, filtration systems and related equipment and replacement parts that Customer or its Affiliates manufacture, assemble or sell and that incorporate any Products.

1.6 "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

1.7 "**Personal Data**" and "**Processing**" (and "**Process**") have the meanings given in the DPA.

1.8 "**Products**" means the PTFE-membrane laminated needlefelt filter media and finished filter bags described in a Statement of Work, together with any other goods that the parties add to a Statement of Work in writing.

1.9 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products that Customer issues under this Agreement and a Statement of Work.

1.10 "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications, drawings and performance criteria for each Product set out in or referenced by the applicable Statement of Work.

1.11 "**Statement of Work**" means a statement of work signed by both parties that refers to this Agreement and describes Products, Specifications, pricing, delivery locations and related terms.

1.12 "**Technical Textiles**" means any woven, nonwoven, knitted or coated industrial fabric, in roll goods, cut-piece or fabricated form, including filter media, filter bags, filter sleeves and membranes.

1.13 "**Territory**" means throughout the world.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 16.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, and at the end of each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for a further term of two (2) years (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"). Renewal does not occur if either party gives the other party written notice of non-renewal at least six (6) months before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work stays in effect for the period stated in it. No Statement of Work extends beyond the end of the Term unless the parties expressly agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY AND SUPPLY COMMITMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Purchase.** During the Term, Customer shall buy from Supplier all of its requirements for Technical Textiles in the Territory, and shall cause its Affiliates to do the same.

3.2 **Supply Commitment.** Supplier shall accept and fill all Purchase Orders that comply with this Agreement and the applicable Statement of Work, up to the capacity commitment set out in that Statement of Work.

3.3 **Supplier Inability to Supply.** If Supplier gives notice that it cannot supply a quantity of Products ordered in a compliant Purchase Order within the agreed lead time, Customer may buy that quantity from a third party for that order only. Supplier shall reimburse Customer for any reasonable price difference.

## 4. STATEMENTS OF WORK AND PURCHASE ORDERS

4.1 **Statements of Work.** The parties may sign one or more Statements of Work. Each Statement of Work is governed by and forms part of this Agreement.

4.2 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that state the Product, quantity, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall acknowledge or reject each Purchase Order within three (3) business days of receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it does not comply with this Agreement or the applicable Statement of Work. A Purchase Order that Supplier does not reject within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Forms.** Any preprinted terms on a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other business form have no effect, even if signed or accepted.

4.4 **Forecasts.** Customer shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its Product requirements each calendar quarter. Forecasts are for planning purposes only and do not obligate Customer to buy any quantity.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for Products set out in the applicable Statement of Work. Prices include all packaging, labeling and loading charges.

5.2 **Price Adjustments.** Prices are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of each Statement of Work. After that, either party may request a price adjustment no more than once per contract year, based on documented changes in the cost of PTFE resin, polyester and aramid fibers, or energy. Adjustments take effect only when agreed in writing.

5.3 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment. Each invoice shall reference the Purchase Order number.

5.4 **Payment.** Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, and shall give Supplier written notice of the dispute with reasonable detail. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay where properly invoiced. Each party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) unless the applicable Statement of Work states otherwise, on the delivery dates in accepted Purchase Orders.

6.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any expected delay. If a delivery is more than ten (10) business days late for reasons within Supplier's control, Supplier shall ship by expedited means at its own cost.

6.3 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery in accordance with Section 6.1.

6.4 **Packaging and Labeling.** Supplier shall pack and label Products in accordance with the Specifications and Customer's reasonable written instructions, and shall include a certificate of conformance with each shipment.

## 7. QUALITY AND INSPECTION

7.1 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 or an equivalent standard throughout the Term.

7.2 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within thirty (30) days after delivery and may reject any Products that do not conform to the Specifications. Payment for Products is not acceptance of them.

7.3 **Rejected Products.** At Customer's option, Supplier shall repair or replace rejected Products within ten (10) business days or refund their price. Supplier bears the cost of returning rejected Products.

7.4 **Change Control.** Supplier shall not change the raw materials, membrane, scrim, finish, manufacturing process or manufacturing location for any Product without at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to Customer and Customer's written approval.

7.5 **Audit.** On reasonable prior notice and no more than once per calendar year (except following a quality failure), Customer may audit Supplier's facilities and records relating to the Products.

## 8. CAPACITY AND CONTINUITY

8.1 **Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity sufficient to meet Customer's forecasted requirements plus fifteen percent (15%).

8.2 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall hold at its own cost the safety stock of finished Products specified in each Statement of Work.

8.3 **Allocation.** If Supplier's capacity is constrained for any reason, Supplier shall allocate available supply to Customer no less favorably than to any other customer.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for eighteen (18) months after delivery, all Products will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship;

(c) be merchantable; and

(d) be delivered free of liens and encumbrances.

9.2 **Remedies.** For any breach of Section 9.1, Supplier shall, at Customer's option, repair or replace the non-conforming Products or refund their price. Supplier shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of removing and replacing non-conforming Products installed in End Products.

9.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full power to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is binding on it.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 10. COMPLIANCE

10.1 **Laws.** Each party shall comply with Applicable Law in performing this Agreement.

10.2 **Materials Compliance.** Supplier shall ensure that Products comply with applicable restricted-substance laws, including laws governing per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS). Supplier shall give Customer the material declarations and test data that Customer reasonably requests to support its regulatory reporting.

10.3 **Trade Compliance.** Each party shall comply with applicable export control, sanctions and customs laws.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent arising from:

(a) any defect in the Products or breach of Section 9.1;

(b) any allegation that the Products infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret;

(c) Supplier's violation of Applicable Law; or

(d) bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim arising out of or relating to the design, manufacture, marketing, sale or use of End Products, including product liability claims, whether or not the Products contributed to the Loss.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall:

(a) notify the indemnifying party promptly of any claim, although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced;

(b) allow the indemnifying party to control the defense and settlement of the claim; and

(c) give reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim in a way that admits fault on behalf of, or imposes any obligation on, the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent. The indemnified party may take part in the defense with counsel of its own choosing and at its own cost.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Cap.** Each party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer to Supplier under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

12.2 **Excluded Damages.** Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct;

(b) Customer's obligations under Section 11.2; or

(c) Supplier's breach of Section 14.

## 13. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

13.1 **Ownership.** Each party keeps all rights in its intellectual property existing on the Effective Date or developed independently of this Agreement.

13.2 **Customer Materials.** Any drawings, designs, tooling or specifications that Customer provides remain Customer's property. Supplier may use them only to supply Products to Customer.

13.3 **Custom Products.** Where a Statement of Work identifies a Product as made to Customer's proprietary design, Supplier shall not sell that Product, or any product made from Customer's design, to any third party.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY

14.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, Specifications, forecasts, designs and Customer Personal Data. Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;

(b) the Recipient already lawfully knew without restriction;

(c) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction; or

(d) the Recipient develops independently without use of the Discloser's information.

14.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and shall not disclose it except to its employees, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14. The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where Applicable Law requires, after giving the Discloser prompt notice where lawful.

14.3 **Customer Personal Data.** Supplier shall Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions and only to perform this Agreement, and in accordance with the DPA.

14.4 **Security.** Supplier shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the risk to protect Customer Personal Data and Customer's other Confidential Information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

14.5 **Security Incidents.** Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or disclosure of Customer Personal Data or other Customer Confidential Information. Supplier shall cooperate with Customer to investigate and remediate the incident.

14.6 **Return.** On termination or expiry, or on request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies kept under routine backup procedures or as Applicable Law requires, which remain subject to this Section 14.

14.7 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue for five (5) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement, and for trade secrets and Customer Personal Data for as long as the information remains a trade secret or is held by Supplier.

## 15. INSURANCE

15.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterwards, Supplier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;

(b) cyber liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per claim; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

15.2 **Certificates.** Supplier shall name Customer as an additional insured under the commercial general liability policy and shall give Customer certificates of insurance on request.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 **For Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it.

16.2 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

16.3 **Effect.** Termination or expiry does not affect accepted Purchase Orders, which the parties shall complete under this Agreement unless Customer cancels them. Customer shall pay for Products delivered before termination. At Customer's request, Supplier shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to six (6) months.

16.4 **Survival.** Sections 1, 9, 11, 12, 14, 16.3, 16.4, 19 and 20, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive termination or expiry.

## 17. FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 **Excused Delay.** Neither party is liable for failure or delay in performance (other than payment) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, terrorism, epidemic, government action or failure of public utilities, provided that it promptly notifies the other party and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.

17.2 **Extended Events.** If a force majeure event prevents Supplier from supplying Products for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may buy affected Products from third parties for the duration of the event.

17.3 **Termination.** If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either party may terminate the affected Statement of Work by written notice.

## 18. ASSIGNMENT

18.1 **By Customer.** Customer may not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without Supplier's prior written consent.

18.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, or any of its rights or obligations under it, to any person upon written notice to Customer and without Customer's consent.

18.3 **Effect.** Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 18 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 19. NOTICES

19.1 **Form.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses in the preamble (or another address a party designates by notice). A copy by email does not by itself constitute notice.

19.2 **Copies.** Notices to Customer shall be marked to the attention of its General Counsel. Notices to Supplier shall be marked to the attention of its President.

19.3 **Effectiveness.** Notices are effective on receipt or on refusal of delivery.

## 20. GENERAL

20.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

20.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

20.3 **Parent Guaranty.** Supplier shall cause Steinaix Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, to deliver a guaranty of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement to Customer on or before the Effective Date.

20.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, they shall govern in the following order: (a) any amendment to this Agreement; then (b) the DPA, as to Personal Data Processing; then (c) the body of this Agreement; then (d) the Statement of Work.

20.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party. A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it.

20.6 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

20.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

20.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all together are one instrument.
