# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of April 1, 2024 by **Steinaix Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Commerce Parkway, Cleveland, Ohio 44135 ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company, have entered into a Supply Agreement dated as of April 1, 2024 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under it, Customer buys PTFE-membrane laminated needlefelt filter media and finished filter bags.

B. Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the membership interests in Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Supply Agreement.

C. Section 20.3 of the Supply Agreement requires that Guarantor deliver this Guaranty to Customer on or before the Effective Date of the Supply Agreement. Customer would not have entered into the Supply Agreement without it.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND GUARANTY

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Guaranty:

(a) "**Supplier**" means solely Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC.

(b) "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Supplier to Customer under or arising out of the Supply Agreement, each Statement of Work, each Purchase Order accepted under the Supply Agreement and the DPA. These include, without limitation, Supplier's obligations to supply Products, perform warranty and remedy obligations, indemnify, pay, reimburse and credit amounts, and protect Confidential Information and Customer Personal Data. They include such obligations whether now existing or arising later, and whether absolute or contingent.

(c) Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranty.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Customer, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

1.3 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. If Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall, within ten (10) business days after Customer's written demand, pay that amount or perform or cause the performance of that obligation.

1.4 **Defenses of Supplier.** Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Supplier could assert under the Supply Agreement against the Guaranteed Obligations, other than defenses arising from Supplier's insolvency, bankruptcy, lack of power or authority, or the matters listed in Section 3.

## 2. NATURE OF GUARANTY

2.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that survive termination or expiry of the Supply Agreement under Section 16.4 of the Supply Agreement.

2.2 **Independent Obligation.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are independent of Supplier's obligations. Customer may bring a separate action against Guarantor whether or not it brings any action against Supplier or joins Supplier in any action.

2.3 **No Prior Recourse.** Customer need not first proceed against Supplier, exhaust any remedy, or enforce any security before demanding payment or performance from Guarantor.

2.4 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Customer on Supplier's insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization, as though the payment had not been made.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS UNCONDITIONAL

3.1 **Consents.** Guarantor consents that, without notice to or further consent of Guarantor, and without affecting its obligations under this Guaranty:

(a) Customer and Supplier may amend, supplement, extend, renew or otherwise modify the Supply Agreement, any Statement of Work or the DPA;

(b) Customer may grant Supplier any extension of time, indulgence or waiver; and

(c) Customer may compromise, settle or release any Guaranteed Obligation, in whole or in part, by agreement with Supplier, in which case Guarantor's liability is reduced only to the extent of the amount so released.

3.2 **No Discharge.** Guarantor's obligations are not released, discharged or affected by:

(a) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of Supplier or Guarantor;

(b) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting Supplier;

(c) any invalidity or unenforceability of any Guaranteed Obligation by reason of Supplier's lack of power or authority; or

(d) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable discharge of a surety or guarantor, other than full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

## 4. WAIVERS

4.1 Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor or non-performance, other than the demand required by Section 1.3;

(c) any right to require Customer to proceed first against Supplier or any other person; and

(d) any defense based on suretyship or impairment of collateral.

4.2 **Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution, reimbursement or indemnity against Supplier arising from payments made or performance rendered under this Guaranty.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Customer that:

(a) it is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement binding on it; and

(e) it is solvent and will not be rendered insolvent by entering into this Guaranty.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Financial Information.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Guarantor shall deliver to Customer, on Customer's written request, its consolidated annual financial statements for that fiscal year. Customer shall treat them as Confidential Information under Section 14 of the Supply Agreement.

6.2 **Ownership.** Guarantor shall notify Customer in writing within thirty (30) days after Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the voting interests in Supplier.

## 7. PAYMENTS

7.1 **Manner.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in U.S. dollars in immediately available funds to the account Customer designates in writing.

7.2 **No Deductions.** Except as permitted by Section 1.4, Guarantor shall make all payments without set-off, counterclaim or deduction.

7.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** Guarantor shall pay Customer's reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Guaranty, if Customer prevails.

## 8. NOTICES

8.1 **Form.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the following addresses (or another address a party designates by notice):

(a) if to Guarantor: Steinaix Holdings, Inc., 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052, Attention: Chief Financial Officer; and

(b) if to Customer: Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc., 4100 Commerce Parkway, Cleveland, Ohio 44135, Attention: General Counsel.

8.2 **Effectiveness.** Notices are effective on receipt or on refusal of delivery.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty. Guarantor waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

9.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF GUARANTOR AND CUSTOMER, BY ITS ACCEPTANCE OF THIS GUARANTY, WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

9.4 **Assignment.** Guarantor may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Guaranty without Customer's prior written consent. Customer may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of its rights under the Supply Agreement. This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Customer and its successors and permitted assigns.

9.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Guarantor and Customer. A failure or delay by Customer in exercising any right under this Guaranty is not a waiver of it.

9.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

9.7 **Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Guaranty may be executed and delivered by electronic signature. An electronic signature has the same effect as an original.
