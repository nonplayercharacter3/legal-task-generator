# Steinaix Expands Coating Capacity at Ramos Arizpe Facility to Serve North American Filtration Market

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**GASTONIA, North Carolina, and RAMOS ARIZPE, Coahuila, Mexico — September 22, 2025.** Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC, a manufacturer of technical filter media and finished filter bags, today announced that its affiliate, Steinaix Textiles Mexico, S. de R.L. de C.V., has finished commissioning a second coating line at its facility in Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila. The new line roughly doubles the site's capacity for dip-, foam- and spray-coated filter media. The site serves dust collection and air pollution control equipment manufacturers across North America.

## 1. About the Expansion

1.1 The new line can apply PTFE, acrylic and other functional coatings and finishes to woven fiberglass and polyester needlefelt substrates. It has inline basis-weight and coating add-on monitoring and a dedicated curing oven.

1.2 The Ramos Arizpe facility also runs a filter bag fabrication cell for bags made from coated media. The fabrication cell includes PTFE-thread sewing for high-temperature applications.

1.3 The facility runs under an ISO 9001-certified quality management system. Steinaix said the new line is now included in the facility's certification scope.

## 2. Management Comments

2.1 "Our customers in cement, asphalt, metals and power generation keep asking for coated media that can handle higher temperatures and harsher chemistry," said Margit L. Steinaix, President of Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC. "This second line lets us meet that demand from a North American plant, with shorter transit times to customers in the United States and Mexico."

2.2 "This investment shows the confidence our group has in the team and the workforce in Coahuila," said Jorge E. Villarreal Garza, General Manager of Steinaix Textiles Mexico. "We have added more than forty production and quality positions this year. We expect to add more as volumes grow."

2.3 Konrad A. Steinaix, Chief Executive Officer of Steinaix Holdings, Inc., the parent company of both operating businesses, said the expansion is part of a multi-year capital plan to strengthen the group's position in membrane and coated filtration media.

## 3. Products and Markets

3.1 Gastonia will keep making the group's PTFE-membrane laminated needlefelt filter media and the finished filter bags made from that media. Ramos Arizpe will focus on coated media and bags made from coated media.

3.2 Coated products made in Ramos Arizpe are designed for baghouse and pulse-jet dust collector applications. These include high-temperature service of up to 500°F continuous for PTFE-coated fiberglass media.

3.3 Steinaix said it keeps working with customers and suppliers on material declarations and testing to support regulatory reporting on per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS).
