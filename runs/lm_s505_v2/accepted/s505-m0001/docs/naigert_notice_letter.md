This letter refers to the Supply Agreement entered into as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**") between Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc. ("**Customer**") and Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC ("**Supplier**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Notice of Non-Renewal

Under Section 2.2 of the Agreement, Customer hereby gives notice of non-renewal of the Agreement. The Agreement will therefore expire at the end of the Initial Term, effective March 31, 2027, and will not renew for a Renewal Term. Statement of Work No. 1 dated April 1, 2024 will end on the same date in accordance with Section 2.3 of the Agreement and Section 2.1 of Statement of Work No. 1.

## 2. Performance Through Expiry

Until the Agreement expires, Customer expects both parties to continue performing their obligations in full. This includes Supplier's capacity commitment, safety stock and lead-time obligations under Statement of Work No. 1. Customer will continue to issue Purchase Orders and quarterly forecasts in the ordinary course. Under Section 16.3 of the Agreement, Purchase Orders accepted before expiry will be completed on the terms of the Agreement unless Customer cancels them.

## 3. Transition Assistance

Under Section 16.3 of the Agreement, Customer requests that Supplier provide reasonable transition assistance for up to six (6) months following expiry. Customer's Director of Strategic Sourcing will contact Supplier's Director of Key Accounts to agree on a transition plan. The plan will cover the run-down of safety stock held at Supplier's Gastonia facility, open Purchase Orders, and the transfer of lot test records and first-article documentation for item numbers NF-101, NF-102, FB-201, FB-202 and FB-203.

## 4. Customer Materials and Confidential Information

On expiry, Customer requests that Supplier:

(a) return all Customer drawings, designs, tooling and Specification Sheets (including NFS-SPEC-101, NFS-SPEC-102 and NFS-SPEC-200) in accordance with Section 13.2 of the Agreement; and

(b) return or destroy Customer's Confidential Information, including any Customer Personal Data, in accordance with Section 14.6 of the Agreement and the DPA, and confirm in writing that it has done so.

Supplier remains bound by Sections 13.3 and 14 of the Agreement after expiry.

## 5. Reservation of Rights

This letter does not waive or limit any of Customer's rights or remedies under the Agreement, Statement of Work No. 1, the DPA or Applicable Law, all of which are expressly reserved.
