# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of June 3, 2024 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between **Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). It supplements the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This DPA sets out the terms on which Supplier Processes Customer Personal Data in connection with the Agreement. It is the "DPA" referred to in Section 1.4 of the Agreement.

1.2 This DPA applies to all Customer Personal Data that Supplier, its Affiliates or its Subprocessors Process in connection with the Agreement, any Statement of Work or any Purchase Order.

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the Processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. These include, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations, and other U.S. state consumer privacy, data security and breach notification laws.

2.2 "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

2.3 "**Personal Data**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with a Data Subject or household, including "personal information" and "personal data" as defined under Data Protection Laws.

2.4 "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, transmission, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

2.5 "**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Customer Personal Data.

2.6 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party, including any Supplier Affiliate, that Supplier engages to Process Customer Personal Data.

## 3. Roles and Instructions

3.1 **Roles.** For Customer Personal Data, Customer is the business or controller and Supplier is the service provider or processor, as those terms are used in Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Instructions.** Supplier shall Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, this DPA and Customer's Purchase Orders are Customer's complete instructions as of the DPA Effective Date. Customer may give further reasonable written instructions consistent with the Agreement. Supplier shall promptly tell Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3.3 **Restrictions.** Supplier shall not:

(a) sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in Data Protection Laws;

(b) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Agreement, including any commercial purpose of its own;

(c) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and Supplier; or

(d) combine Customer Personal Data with Personal Data that Supplier receives from or on behalf of any other person or collects from its own interactions with Data Subjects, except as Data Protection Laws permit.

3.4 **Certification.** Supplier certifies that it understands and will comply with the restrictions in Section 3.3.

## 4. Details of Processing

4.1 **Subject Matter and Duration.** Supplier Processes Customer Personal Data to supply Products under the Agreement for the Term and for any period after it during which Supplier holds Customer Personal Data.

4.2 **Nature and Purpose.** Supplier Processes Customer Personal Data to:

(a) receive, acknowledge and fill Purchase Orders;

(b) coordinate delivery, carriage and receiving at Customer's facilities;

(c) issue invoices and handle payments and disputes;

(d) manage quality, first-article, inspection and return matters; and

(e) give Customer users access to Supplier's online order and shipment-tracking portal.

4.3 **Categories of Data Subjects.** These are Customer's employees, contractors and agents, and personnel of Customer's designated carriers.

4.4 **Categories of Personal Data.** These are names, job titles, business email addresses, business telephone numbers, business addresses, portal user credentials and log data, and signatures on delivery and quality documents. Supplier shall not ask for, and Customer does not intend to provide, sensitive personal information.

## 5. Supplier Personnel

5.1 Supplier shall limit access to Customer Personal Data to personnel who need it to perform the Agreement.

5.2 Supplier shall ensure that those personnel are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate training in data protection and security.

## 6. Security

6.1 **Safeguards.** In addition to its obligations under Section 14.4 of the Agreement, Supplier shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Annex A. Supplier may update those measures as long as the update does not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

6.2 **Testing.** Supplier shall regularly test, assess and evaluate the effectiveness of its security measures. It shall do so at least once a year.

## 7. Security Incidents

7.1 **Notice.** Supplier shall notify Customer of any Security Incident without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it, in accordance with Section 14.5 of the Agreement.

7.2 **Content.** The notice shall describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures Supplier has taken or proposes to take to contain and remedy it.

Supplier shall provide additional information as it becomes available.

7.3 **Cooperation.** Supplier shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. It shall cooperate with Customer in any notification to Data Subjects, regulators or others that Data Protection Laws require. Supplier shall not notify any third party of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data without Customer's prior written approval, except as required by law.

## 8. Subprocessors, Assistance and Audit

8.1 **Subprocessors.** Customer authorizes Supplier to use the Subprocessors listed in Annex B. Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any new Subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If Customer objects, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith, and Supplier shall not use that Subprocessor for Customer Personal Data until the objection is resolved. Supplier shall bind each Subprocessor by written terms at least as protective as this DPA. Supplier remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance.

8.2 **Data Subject Requests.** Supplier shall promptly forward to Customer any request it receives from a Data Subject about Customer Personal Data. It shall not respond except on Customer's instructions. Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to those requests.

8.3 **Assistance.** Supplier shall provide reasonable information and assistance to Customer for risk assessments, regulatory inquiries and other obligations under Data Protection Laws that relate to Supplier's Processing.

8.4 **Audit.** On reasonable prior notice, Supplier shall make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to show its compliance with this DPA. Customer may audit Supplier's compliance under Section 7.5 of the Agreement. Customer may also audit at any time following a Security Incident.

8.5 **Location.** Supplier shall Process and store Customer Personal Data only in the United States, unless Customer agrees otherwise in writing.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA, including any Security Incident and any breach of Data Protection Laws, is subject to the limitations set out in Section 12.1 of the Agreement.

9.2 Supplier is liable for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors as if they were its own.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 On termination or expiry of the Agreement, or on Customer's earlier written request, Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Personal Data, at Customer's option. Supplier shall do so within thirty (30) days and shall certify completion in writing.

10.2 Section 14.6 of the Agreement applies to copies kept under routine backup procedures or as Applicable Law requires. Supplier shall continue to protect those copies in accordance with this DPA.

## 11. General

11.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date. It continues for as long as Supplier or any Subprocessor Processes Customer Personal Data.

11.2 **Changes in Law.** If Data Protection Laws change in a way that affects this DPA, the parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendments reasonably needed to comply.

11.3 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given under Section 19 of the Agreement. Security Incident notices under Section 7.1 shall also be sent by email to Customer's General Counsel and Director of Information Security.

11.4 **Governing Law.** Sections 20.1 and 20.2 of the Agreement apply to this DPA.

11.5 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## ANNEX A: Technical and Organizational Measures

1. **Access control:** role-based access, unique user IDs, multi-factor authentication for remote and administrative access, and prompt removal of access when personnel leave.
2. **Encryption:** encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent).
3. **Network security:** firewalls, endpoint protection, timely patching and logging of security events.
4. **Business continuity:** daily backups, a tested disaster recovery plan and documented recovery time objectives.
5. **Vendor management:** security due diligence on Subprocessors before engagement and periodically after that.
6. **Physical security:** controlled access to Supplier's Gastonia, North Carolina facility and server rooms.
7. **Incident response:** a documented incident response plan, tested at least once a year.
