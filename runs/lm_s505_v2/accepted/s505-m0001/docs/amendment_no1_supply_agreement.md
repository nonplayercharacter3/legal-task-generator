# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of July 15, 2025 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and among **Naigert Filtration Systems, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Commerce Parkway, Cleveland, Ohio 44135 ("**Customer**"), **Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052 ("**Steinaix Fabrics**"), and **Steinaix Textiles Mexico, S. de R.L. de C.V.**, a sociedad de responsabilidad limitada de capital variable organized under the laws of the United Mexican States, with its principal place of business at Boulevard Industria Automotriz No. 1450, Parque Industrial Santa Mónica, Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila 25904, Mexico ("**Steinaix Mexico**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Steinaix Fabrics are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of April 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"), and to Statement of Work No. 1 under the Agreement dated April 1, 2024 ("**SOW No. 1**").

B. Steinaix Mexico is an Affiliate of Steinaix Fabrics. It operates a coating and fabrication facility in Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila, Mexico.

C. Customer wishes to buy coated filter media and filter bags made from coated filter media for the Covered Facilities. Steinaix Fabrics wishes to have those products supplied by Steinaix Mexico.

D. The parties wish to amend the Agreement and SOW No. 1 on the terms of this Amendment, in accordance with Section 20.5 of the Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Interpretation.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement, as amended by this Amendment.

1.2 **New Definitions.** Section 1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Sections 1.14 and 1.15:

> 1.14 "**Coated Products**" means the Products identified as Coated Products in a Statement of Work, being filter media to which a PTFE, acrylic or other chemical coating or finish is applied by dip, foam or spray coating, and finished filter bags made from such filter media.
>
> 1.15 "**Supplying Affiliate**" means Steinaix Textiles Mexico, S. de R.L. de C.V., and any other Affiliate of Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC that the parties designate as a Supplying Affiliate in a written amendment to this Agreement signed by Customer, Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC and that Affiliate.

## 2. AMENDMENT OF "SUPPLIER"

2.1 **Preamble.** The definition of "**Supplier**" in the preamble to the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> "**Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Mill Ridge Road, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052, and each Supplying Affiliate (together, "**Supplier**")."

2.2 **References.** From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement, SOW No. 1 and any other Statement of Work to "Supplier" means Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC and each Supplying Affiliate, except that:

(a) references to Supplier's facility in Section 6.1 of the Agreement mean, for Coated Products, the facility of the Supplying Affiliate set out in the applicable Statement of Work; and

(b) notices to Supplier under Section 19 of the Agreement shall be given as set out in Section 6 of this Amendment.

2.3 **Joinder.** Steinaix Mexico joins the Agreement as a party, as a Supplier, and agrees to be bound by the Agreement, as amended by this Amendment, as it applies to Supplier, with effect from the Amendment Effective Date.

## 3. SUPPLY OF COATED PRODUCTS

3.1 **Supplying Entity.** Steinaix Mexico shall accept and perform all Purchase Orders for Coated Products. Customer shall address Purchase Orders for Coated Products to Steinaix Mexico, and Steinaix Fabrics shall continue to accept and perform all Purchase Orders for all other Products.

3.2 **Purchase Orders.** Section 4.2 of the Agreement applies to Purchase Orders for Coated Products. Steinaix Mexico shall acknowledge or reject each Purchase Order for Coated Products within three (3) business days of receipt, measured by business days in the United States.

3.3 **Performance.** Steinaix Mexico shall manufacture, coat, fabricate, pack, label and deliver Coated Products, and shall perform the warranty, remedy, indemnity, compliance, insurance and other obligations of Supplier under the Agreement for the Coated Products it supplies.

3.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Steinaix Mexico shall invoice Customer for Coated Products under Section 5.3 of the Agreement. All invoices shall be issued in U.S. dollars. Customer shall pay those invoices under Section 5.4 of the Agreement to the U.S. dollar account that Steinaix Mexico designates in writing.

3.5 **Customs and Trade.** Steinaix Mexico shall act as exporter of record for Coated Products and shall prepare and deliver, with each shipment, a commercial invoice, packing list and a certification of origin meeting the requirements of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement for any Coated Product that qualifies as originating. Section 10.3 of the Agreement applies to all shipments of Coated Products.

3.6 **Quality System.** Steinaix Mexico shall maintain its Ramos Arizpe facility under a quality management system certified to ISO 9001 or an equivalent standard, as required by Section 7.1 of the Agreement, and shall make the facility available for audit under Section 7.5 of the Agreement.

## 4. AMENDMENT OF STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

4.1 **Additional Products.** The table in Section 3.1 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding the following rows, each of which is a Coated Product:

| Item No. | Product | Description | Unit |

4.2 **Specifications.** Section 3.2 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding Customer Specification Sheet NFS-SPEC-300 (Rev. A) (Coated Media), which Customer has delivered to Steinaix Mexico and which is incorporated in SOW No. 1 by reference. NFS-SPEC-200 (Rev. D) applies to Items CB-401 and CB-402. The key performance criteria in Section 3.2(a) and (d) of SOW No. 1 apply to Coated Products. Minimum filtration efficiency and coating add-on weight for Coated Products are as stated in NFS-SPEC-300.

4.3 **Custom Products.** Section 3.3 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding Items CB-401 and CB-402 to the list of Products made to Customer's proprietary design.

4.4 **Delivery.** Section 4.2 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding the following at the end:

> "Coated Products shall be delivered FCA the Supplying Affiliate's facility at Boulevard Industria Automotriz No. 1450, Parque Industrial Santa Mónica, Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila 25904, Mexico (Incoterms 2020). The Supplying Affiliate shall arrange carriage to the applicable Covered Facility using Customer's designated carrier and customs broker and Customer's freight account."

4.5 **Lead Times.** Section 4.3 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding the following:

> "(c) coated roll goods (CF-301, CF-302): twenty (20) business days; and
>
> (d) finished filter bags made from coated media (CB-401, CB-402): twenty-five (25) business days."

4.6 **Prices.** The table in Section 5.1 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding the following rows:

| Item No. | Unit Price (USD) |
|---|---|

| CF-302 | $16.20 per linear yard |

Purchases of Coated Products count toward the volume threshold in Section 5.2 of SOW No. 1, and any rebate on them shall be credited by Steinaix Mexico.

4.7 **Capacity Commitment.** The table in Section 6.1 of SOW No. 1 is amended by adding the following rows:

| Product Group | Monthly Capacity Commitment |

> "For Coated Products, the Supplying Affiliate shall hold the safety stock at its Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila facility on the same terms."

4.9 **First Article.** Section 7.1 of SOW No. 1 applies to each Coated Product item number. No production shipment of a Coated Product may be made until Customer has approved its first article.

## 5. MANUFACTURING LOCATION

5.1 **Approval.** For purposes of Section 7.4 of the Agreement, Customer approves the Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila facility of Steinaix Mexico as the manufacturing location for Coated Products only.

5.2 **Other Products.** This Amendment does not approve any change in the manufacturing location of Items NF-101, NF-102, FB-201, FB-202 or FB-203, which remain subject to Section 7.4 of the Agreement.

## 6. NOTICES

Section 19.2 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following at the end:

> "Notices to Steinaix Textiles Mexico, S. de R.L. de C.V. shall be delivered to Boulevard Industria Automotriz No. 1450, Parque Industrial Santa Mónica, Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila 25904, Mexico, marked to the attention of its General Manager, with a copy to Steinaix Industrial Fabrics, LLC at the address in the preamble, marked to the attention of its President."

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement and SOW No. 1 remain unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference in SOW No. 1 to "the Agreement," means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

7.2 **Representations.** Each party represents to the others that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, and that this Amendment is binding on it.

7.3 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Sections 20.1 and 20.2 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment. Steinaix Mexico submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to the Agreement or this Amendment.

7.4 **Language.** This Amendment is executed in English. Any translation is for convenience only.

7.5 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all together are one instrument.
