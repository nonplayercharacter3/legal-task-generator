## 1. Amendment to Section 4.13 (Litigation)

Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule is supplemented by adding the following as new item 4:

> 4. *Calloway Pre-Owned Motors, LLC, individually and on behalf of all others similarly situated v. Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.*, United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, Case No. [●], filed August 27, 2026. The complaint was served on the Company on September 1, 2026.
>
> The plaintiff is a licensed used-vehicle dealer that purchased vehicles at the Auctions. It asserts claims under the federal Odometer Act, 49 U.S.C. Chapter 327, and under New York General Business Law Section 349. It alleges that, for vehicles sold through the Company's auction lanes from January 2023 to date, the Company (a) failed to obtain or transmit complete odometer disclosure statements, (b) accepted and passed through odometer readings marked "actual" on vehicles whose title or service histories indicated mileage discrepancies, and (c) failed to announce odometer-discrepancy and "not actual mileage" brands on the auction block before sale. The plaintiff seeks to represent a putative nationwide class of buying dealers that purchased affected vehicles at the Auctions, together with a New York subclass.
>
> The complaint seeks statutory damages of three times actual damages or $10,000 per violation, whichever is greater, under 49 U.S.C. Section 32710, together with rescission, attorneys' fees and costs. No class has been certified. The Company's response to the complaint is due October 22, 2026. The Company has retained counsel and believes it has meritorious defenses. On September 4, 2026, the Company gave notice of the action to the carrier under its garage liability and errors and omissions policies. The carrier has not yet issued a coverage position.
