**Re: Objection to Supplement No. 1 to the Disclosure Schedule under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026, between Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc. and Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

## 1. Notice of Objection

This letter is delivered by Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC ("**Buyer**") to Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc. ("**Seller**") under Sections 6.8 and 11.3 of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026, between Seller and Buyer (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

Buyer objects in writing to Supplement No. 1 to the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Supplement**"), in its entirety. Buyer does not accept the Supplement, and does not accept the matters it discloses, as curing any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller. This applies both to the conditions in Section 8.2 and to the indemnification provisions of Article IX.

## 2. Matters Disclosed in the Supplement

The Supplement adds new item 4 to Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule. That item describes the putative class action captioned *Calloway Pre-Owned Motors, LLC, individually and on behalf of all others similarly situated v. Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.*, pending in the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York (the "**Calloway Action**"). The Supplement also adds new item 4 to Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule, covering any liability of the Company arising from the Calloway Action. It further cross-references the Calloway Action for purposes of Section 4.10 of the Agreement.

The Calloway Action asserts claims under the federal Odometer Act, 49 U.S.C. Chapter 327, and New York General Business Law Section 349. It concerns vehicles sold through the Company's auction lanes from January 2023 onward. The plaintiff seeks to represent a putative nationwide class of buying dealers and a New York subclass. It seeks statutory damages under 49 U.S.C. Section 32710 of three times actual damages or $10,000 per violation, whichever is greater, together with rescission, attorneys' fees and costs.

## 3. Grounds for Objection

Buyer objects to the Supplement on the following grounds, without limitation:

(a) **Section 4.10 (Title and Odometer Compliance).** The Calloway Action alleges conduct that, if established, would mean the Company did not process odometer disclosure statements and title-branding disclosures in material compliance with the federal Truth in Mileage Act, its implementing regulations and applicable state Law. The alleged conduct covers a period beginning more than three years before the date of the Agreement. The Supplement describes the Calloway Action as a matter that arose after the date of the Agreement. The underlying conduct, however, did not arise after that date. Seller may not use Section 6.8 to qualify Section 4.10 retroactively for conduct that occurred before signing.

(b) **Section 4.13 (Litigation) and Section 4.5 (Undisclosed Liabilities).** The Calloway Action is a putative nationwide class action seeking statutory treble or per-violation damages. Its potential exposure is materially different in kind and scale from the ordinary-course matters listed in Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule as delivered on the date of the Agreement. Buyer does not accept the new item 4 to Section 4.5, which purports to schedule an unquantified category of liability, as an exception to Section 4.5.

(c) **Closing Conditions.** Buyer reserves the right to determine that the conditions in Sections 8.2(a) and 8.2(c) are not satisfied by reason of the Calloway Action and the matters underlying it, including any effect on the Licenses relevant to Section 8.2(d).

## 4. Reservation of Rights

Buyer expressly reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement, the Ancillary Agreements and applicable Law, including:

(a) its rights to indemnification under Article IX, including under Section 9.2(a) for any inaccuracy in or breach of Sections 4.5, 4.10 and 4.13, and under Section 9.2(d) for Title and Odometer Losses, which apply regardless of whether disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule;

(b) its right of setoff under Section 9.7;

(c) its rights under Article VIII, including the right to decline to consummate the Closing if any condition in Section 8.2 is not satisfied; and

(d) its rights of termination under Section 10.1.

Neither this letter, nor any continued performance by Buyer under the Agreement, nor any delay or omission in exercising any right, is a waiver of any right or remedy of Buyer. Under Section 11.7, no waiver by Buyer is effective unless in writing and signed by Buyer.

## 5. Information Requests

Under Section 6.2 of the Agreement, Buyer requests that Seller cause the Company to deliver the following to Buyer and its counsel within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this letter:

1. a complete copy of the complaint in the Calloway Action, all exhibits to it, and all pleadings, motions and correspondence with plaintiff's counsel filed or exchanged since service;
2. copies of the notice given to the Company's garage liability and errors and omissions carrier, and of all correspondence with the carrier concerning coverage;
3. the identity of defense counsel and the proposed defense strategy and budget;
4. a schedule of vehicles sold at the Auctions since January 1, 2023 for which an odometer exception, "not actual mileage" brand or odometer-discrepancy designation was recorded in the TitleTrack database, with the related sale dates and buying dealers;
5. any internal audit, compliance review, regulatory inquiry or dealer complaint concerning odometer disclosure or title-brand announcements at the Auctions since January 1, 2023; and
6. any other demand, arbitration or claim by a buying dealer, consignor, floorplan lender, retail purchaser or Governmental Authority that relates to the matters alleged in the Calloway Action.

## 6. Conduct of the Calloway Action Before Closing

Under Section 6.1 of the Agreement, Buyer requests that Seller cause the Company not to settle, compromise, consent to any judgment in, or stipulate to class certification in the Calloway Action without Buyer's prior written consent. Buyer also requests that Seller keep Buyer reasonably informed of all material developments, including the Company's response to the complaint, which is due October 22, 2026.
