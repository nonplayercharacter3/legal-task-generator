# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**
**Dated: [●], 2026** (the "**Issue Date**")

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal sum of SIX MILLION DOLLARS ($6,000,000), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, in the amounts, at the times and in the manner set out in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued at the Closing under Section 2.2(c) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026, between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Maker is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. Principal Amount; Definitions

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The original principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**").

**1.2 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Defined Terms.** In this Note:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Maturity Date**" means the fourth (4th) anniversary of the Issue Date.

"**Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such day to occur after the Issue Date.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness for borrowed money of Maker or the Company owed to any bank, institutional lender or other financing source (each, a "**Senior Lender**") under any credit facility used to finance the cash portion of the Purchase Price, or under any refinancing or replacement of that facility, including principal, interest, fees, expenses and all other obligations under it.

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Rate.** The unpaid Principal Amount accrues interest from the Issue Date at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued and unpaid interest is payable in arrears on each Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

**2.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default under Section 6.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid Principal Amount and any overdue interest shall accrue interest at a rate of eight percent (8.0%) per annum.

**2.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. Payment of Principal

**3.1 Amortization.** Maker shall repay the Principal Amount in installments of $375,000 on each Payment Date beginning with the first Payment Date that occurs on or after the second (2nd) anniversary of the Issue Date.

**3.2 Maturity.** The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Business Days.** If any payment under this Note falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day, and interest shall accrue for the additional days.

## 4. Payments Generally

**4.1 Method of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the relevant payment date. Until Holder designates another account, payments shall be made to the account to which the cash portion of the Purchase Price was paid at the Closing.

**4.2 Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to the unpaid Principal Amount.

**4.3 Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note at any time, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments shall be applied to installments of principal in the inverse order of maturity.

**4.4 Change of Control Prepayment.** If Maker sells all or substantially all of the equity interests in or assets of the Company to a Person that is not an Affiliate of Maker, the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued and unpaid interest shall become due and payable on the closing date of that sale.

## 5. Subordination

**5.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set out in this Section 5 and in any subordination agreement entered into among Holder, Maker and any Senior Lender (a "**Subordination Agreement**"). Holder shall execute and deliver a Subordination Agreement on customary terms reasonably requested by any Senior Lender, consistent with this Section 5.

**5.2 Permitted Payments.** So long as no payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under the Senior Debt, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note.

**5.3 Payment Blockage.** If a payment default or acceleration under the Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing, Maker shall make no payment under this Note, and Holder shall take no action to collect it, until that default has been cured or waived or the Senior Debt has been paid in full in cash. Any payment not made because of this Section 5.3 shall continue to accrue interest at the rate in Section 2.1 and shall be paid promptly after the payment blockage ends.

**5.4 Turnover.** Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 5 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lenders and promptly paid over to them.

**5.5 Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or similar proceeding of Maker, the Senior Debt shall first be paid in full in cash before Holder receives any payment under this Note.

## 6. Events of Default

**6.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after Maker's receipt of written notice from Holder;

(b) Maker (i) commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iii) consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its assets; or

(c) an involuntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**6.2 Remedies.** Subject to Section 5, while an Event of Default under Section 6.1(a) is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued and unpaid interest immediately due and payable. Upon an Event of Default under Section 6.1(b) or 6.1(c), the entire unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued and unpaid interest shall automatically become due and payable without notice or other action by Holder.

**6.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 7. Transfer; Waivers; Amendment

**7.1 Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without Maker's prior written consent, and any transfer made without that consent is void. Any permitted transferee takes this Note subject to Section 5 and to any Subordination Agreement. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without Holder's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of Maker's assets that assumes this Note in writing.

**7.2 Note Register.** Maker shall keep a register recording the name of the holder of this Note. Maker may treat the Person in whose name this Note is registered as its owner for all purposes.

**7.3 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, protest and notice of dishonor and of protest. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right.

**7.4 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder and, to the extent required by any Subordination Agreement, consented to by the applicable Senior Lender.

**7.5 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor. Before it does so, Holder shall deliver an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity (and the mutilated Note, if applicable), without bond.

## 8. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

**8.1 Governing Law.** This Note shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**8.2 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note shall be brought exclusively in the courts of the State of Delaware located in New Castle County or, if those courts lack subject-matter jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding, and waive any objection to venue in them.

**8.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 9. Miscellaneous

**9.1 Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner provided in Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement, to the addresses of Buyer (for Maker) and Seller (for Holder) set out in the Purchase Agreement, or to another address designated by notice.

**9.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

**9.4 Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

**9.5 Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed by electronic signature, which shall be deemed an original signature.
