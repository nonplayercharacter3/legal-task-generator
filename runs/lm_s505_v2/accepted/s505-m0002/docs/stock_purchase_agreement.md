# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 3, 2026, by and between **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), which owns and operates wholesale used-vehicle auction facilities serving licensed motor vehicle dealers.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings below:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Bill of Sale.

"**Auctions**" means the wholesale vehicle auctions conducted by the Company at its auction facilities and through its online simulcast and timed-bid platforms.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in the agreed form, under which Seller conveys to the Company certain tangible personal property used in the operation of the Auctions and held in Seller's name.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Consignor Funds**" means all amounts received by the Company from buyers at the Auctions that are payable to consignors of vehicles sold at the Auctions.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement, as it may be supplemented under Section 6.8.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Cindral Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement, in the agreed form, among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning given in the Escrow Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization and Authority), 4.2 (Capitalization; Title to Shares), 4.14 (Brokers) and 5.1 (Organization and Authority of Buyer).

"**General Representations**" means all representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV other than the Fundamental Representations and the representations and warranties in Section 4.12 (Taxes).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court, tribunal or regulatory body of any of them, including any state motor vehicle department or dealer licensing board.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $3,150,000.

"**Indemnity Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount as held by the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement, together with any earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under the Escrow Agreement.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the intellectual property assignment agreement, in the agreed form, between Seller and the Company.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Lien**" means any mortgage, pledge, lien, charge, security interest, claim or other encumbrance.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event or change that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, results of operations or financial condition of the Company, taken as a whole, other than changes arising from general economic conditions or conditions generally affecting the wholesale used-vehicle remarketing industry that do not disproportionately affect the Company.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note, in the agreed form, issued by Buyer to Seller at the Closing in the principal amount of $6,000,000.

"**Services**" has the meaning given in the Transition Services Agreement.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the transition services agreement, in the agreed form, between Seller, as provider, and the Company, as recipient.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $42,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall:

(a) pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to the Purchase Price, less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, less the principal amount of the Seller Note, and less the Estimated Closing Indebtedness;

(b) deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement;

(c) issue and deliver the Seller Note to Seller; and

(d) pay, on behalf of the Company, the Estimated Closing Indebtedness to the holders of that indebtedness in accordance with payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv).

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** At least five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting out Seller's good-faith estimate of the Company's indebtedness for borrowed money and floorplan obligations outstanding as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Closing Indebtedness**") and of the Company's net working capital, with reasonable supporting detail.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a closing statement setting out the actual Closing indebtedness and net working capital. Any dispute that the parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days after Seller's written notice of disagreement shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. Any resulting amount owed shall be paid within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable tax Law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VIII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or at another time or place agreed in writing by the parties. The date on which the Closing actually occurs is the "**Closing Date**."

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Escrow Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Bill of Sale, each duly executed by Seller (and, as applicable, the Company);

(iii) the resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(iv) customary payoff letters for all Estimated Closing Indebtedness, with releases of related Liens;

(v) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.2(a) and 8.2(b); and

(vi) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments and the Seller Note described in Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.3(a) and 8.3(b).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of organization. Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, and this Agreement constitutes Seller's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The Shares constitute all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, any Law applicable to them, or any material contract to which either is a party, and require no consent of any Governmental Authority other than as set out in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Complete copies of the Company's audited financial statements as of and for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, and unaudited financial statements as of and for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026, have been delivered to Buyer. They were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.5 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except those reflected in the most recent balance sheet, those incurred in the ordinary course since that date, and those set out in Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.6 Consignor Trust Funds.** All Consignor Funds have been held and remitted in accordance with applicable Law, the Company's auction rules and its contracts with consignors. No Consignor Funds have been commingled or used for any other purpose, and no consignor is owed any amount past its customary payment date.

**4.7 Dealer and Auction Licenses.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists every dealer, auctioneer, wholesale auction and motor vehicle license, bond and permit held by the Company (the "**Licenses**"). Each License is valid and in full force and effect, the Company is in material compliance with each License, and no proceeding to suspend, revoke or limit any License is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened.

**4.8 Auction Receivables.** All accounts receivable of the Company, including amounts owed by buying dealers and floorplan lenders for vehicles sold at the Auctions, arose from bona fide transactions in the ordinary course, are not subject to valid setoff or counterclaim, and are collectible in the ordinary course, net of reserves shown in the financial statements.

**4.9 Floorplan and Lender Arrangements.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each floorplan financing, auction access or dealer credit arrangement to which the Company is a party. The Company is not in material default under any of them.

**4.10 Title and Odometer Compliance.** The Company has processed title transfers, odometer disclosure statements and title-branding disclosures for vehicles sold at the Auctions in material compliance with the federal Truth in Mileage Act, 49 U.S.C. Chapter 327, its implementing regulations and applicable state Law.

**4.11 Material Contracts.** Section 4.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material contract of the Company. Each is valid and binding, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.12 Taxes.** All tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true and complete in all material respects, and all taxes due have been paid.

**4.13 Litigation.** Except as set out in Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement, the Seller Note and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price and to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Licenses, hold and remit Consignor Funds in accordance with Section 4.6, and not, without Buyer's prior written consent, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, amend its organizational documents, issue equity, or enter into, amend or terminate any material contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, title files and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VIII, including making all notices and filings with state motor vehicle and dealer licensing authorities required in connection with the change of control of the Company.

**6.4 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit or negotiate any proposal for the acquisition of the Company or any material part of its business.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company, including consignor, buyer and dealer data.

**6.6 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, own, operate or manage any wholesale vehicle auction within 150 miles of any auction facility of the Company, or solicit any consignor or employee of the Company, except in the performance of the Services.

**6.7 Notification of Certain Matters.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that causes or would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 8.2 not to be satisfied.

**6.8 Disclosure Schedule Supplements.** From time to time before the Closing, Seller may supplement or amend the Disclosure Schedule with respect to any matter arising or first becoming known to Seller after the date of this Agreement (each, a "**Schedule Supplement**"). Each Schedule Supplement shall be delivered to Buyer in writing in accordance with Section 11.3. Unless Buyer delivers to Seller a written objection to a Schedule Supplement within ten (10) Business Days after receipt of that Schedule Supplement, the Schedule Supplement shall be deemed accepted by Buyer, and the matters disclosed in it shall be deemed to have cured any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty to which they relate, both for purposes of determining whether the conditions in Section 8.2 have been satisfied and for purposes of the indemnification provisions of Article IX.

**6.9 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall be responsible for and pay all taxes of the Company attributable to taxable periods (or portions of periods) ending on or before the Closing Date.

**7.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use and similar taxes incurred in connection with the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**7.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate reasonably in the filing of tax returns and the conduct of any tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to (a) the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, and (b) receipt of the approvals of Governmental Authorities listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**8.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of the following:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and all other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred since the date of this Agreement;

(d) each License shall remain in full force and effect and shall not be subject to any pending suspension or revocation proceeding; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each of the items in Section 3.2(a).

**8.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of the following:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Buyer shall have delivered the payments, the Seller Note and the other items in Sections 2.2 and 3.2(b).

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations shall survive the Closing until the Escrow Release Date. The Fundamental Representations and the representations and warranties in Section 4.12 (Taxes) shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms. The obligations under Section 9.2(d) shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the statute of limitations applicable to the underlying claim. No claim may be brought for breach of a representation or warranty after the end of its survival period, except that any claim asserted in good faith with reasonable specificity in writing before that time shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any taxes of the Company for which Seller is responsible under Section 7.1, and any Closing indebtedness not paid at or before the Closing; and

(d) any defect in title, failure to deliver title, title-branding nondisclosure, or odometer misstatement or odometer disclosure violation with respect to any vehicle sold through the Auctions before the Closing, including any claim by a buying dealer, consignor, floorplan lender, retail purchaser or Governmental Authority, regardless of whether disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule (collectively, "**Title and Odometer Losses**").

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) *Third-Party Claims.* An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any third-party claim, but a delay in notice shall relieve the indemnifying party only to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party by written notice within fifteen (15) days after receiving notice, and the indemnified party may participate at its own expense. The indemnifying party may not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money fully indemnified hereunder and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

(b) *Direct Claims.* Any claim not involving a third party shall be asserted by written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail. The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days to respond, after which the indemnified party may pursue the remedies available under this Agreement.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) for breaches of the General Representations (i) until the aggregate Losses exceed $210,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount, or (ii) for Losses in excess of $4,200,000 in the aggregate.

(b) The limitations in Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from fraud, from breaches of the Fundamental Representations or of Section 4.12 (Taxes), or under Sections 9.2(b), 9.2(c) or 9.2(d), including Title and Odometer Losses. Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX (other than for fraud) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually recovered by the indemnified party, less costs of recovery and any resulting premium increases.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Losses payable to the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2(a) in respect of the General Representations shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, by joint written instruction or final order delivered to the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement, and thereafter directly by Seller, subject to Section 9.5. Losses under the other clauses of Section 9.2 may be recovered, at Buyer's election, from the Indemnity Escrow Fund or directly from Seller.

**9.7 Right of Setoff.** Buyer shall be entitled to set off the amount of any Losses for which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification under this Article IX against any payments due under the Seller Note, including principal and accrued interest. Buyer shall give Seller written notice of any setoff describing the related claim in reasonable detail. Exercise of this right of setoff in good faith, whether or not ultimately determined to be justified, shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note.

**9.8 Transition Services Matters.** Any Losses arising out of or relating to the subject matter of the Services shall be recovered exclusively under, and subject to the limitations of, the Transition Services Agreement.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedies.** Except for claims arising from fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.10, and disputes under Section 2.4, the remedies in this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedies of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement.

**9.10 Tax Treatment of Indemnification Payments.** All indemnification payments under this Agreement shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by Buyer or Seller by written notice if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 18, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**"), provided that this right shall not be available to a party whose breach of this Agreement has been the principal cause of the failure of the Closing to occur by that date;

(c) by Buyer by written notice if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 8.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice from Buyer;

(d) by Seller by written notice if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 8.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice from Seller; or

(e) by either party if a Governmental Authority has issued a final, non-appealable order permanently prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any party, except that this Section 10.2 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve any party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement, except that the fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.2 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by Law.

**11.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. New York time on a Business Day, and otherwise on the next Business Day, or (c) one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the addresses set out on the signature pages (or such other address as a party designates by notice).

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to an Affiliate or, collaterally, to its financing sources.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and the other indemnified Persons under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.

**11.7 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.9 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York, and each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled.

**11.11 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS CONTEMPLATED BY IT.

**11.12 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the provisions of this Agreement and those of any Ancillary Agreement (the Escrow Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Bill of Sale), the provisions of this Agreement shall prevail.

**11.13 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
