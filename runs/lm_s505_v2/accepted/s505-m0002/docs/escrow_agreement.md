# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Cindral Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will acquire from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, to be held and disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. APPOINTMENT; DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as their escrow agent for the purposes of this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Escrow Agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs, as certified to the Escrow Agent by Buyer and Seller in the joint notice delivered under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, accompanied by a written certification of the presenting party's counsel that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction to the Escrow Agent signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

**1.3 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement, shall have no duty to inquire into or construe its terms, and shall be bound only by this Escrow Agreement.

## 2. DEPOSIT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**2.1 Indemnity Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $3,150,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall hold the Indemnity Escrow Amount in a segregated escrow account (the "**Escrow Account**") and shall acknowledge receipt to Buyer and Seller in writing on the Business Day it is received.

**2.2 Closing Notice.** On the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a joint notice confirming that the Closing has occurred and stating the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent may rely on that notice conclusively.

**2.3 No Other Deposits.** The Escrow Agent shall have no obligation to accept any deposit other than the Indemnity Escrow Amount and any earnings on it.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the [●] money market deposit account maintained by the Escrow Agent, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instruction (each, a "**Permitted Investment**"). Absent a Joint Written Instruction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any Permitted Investment as necessary to make any disbursement required under this Escrow Agreement and shall not be liable for any loss, fee or penalty resulting from that liquidation or from any investment made in accordance with this Section 3.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds shall form part of the Escrow Funds and shall be disbursed with them.

**3.4 Tax Reporting.** For tax reporting purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds and all earnings on them until disbursed, and shall report those earnings as its income, except to the extent that applicable law requires otherwise. Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or W-8, as applicable, before the Closing Date.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Escrow Release Date.** "**Escrow Release Date**" means the date that is ninety (90) days after the Closing Date or, if that date is not a Business Day, the next Business Day.

**4.2 Release on Escrow Release Date.** Within three (3) Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller, by wire transfer to the account designated by Seller in Schedule B, the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate amount of all claims stated in Claim Notices delivered to the Escrow Agent on or before the Escrow Release Date that remain unresolved on that date (the "**Pending Claim Amount**").

**4.3 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount after the Escrow Release Date. As each claim included in it is resolved under Section 5, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for that claim in accordance with the applicable Joint Written Instruction or Final Order. Any portion of the retained amount not payable to Buyer shall then be disbursed to Seller.

**4.4 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding the dates in this Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse all or any part of the Escrow Funds in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction.

## 5. CLAIMS AGAINST ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Claim Notices.** At any time on or before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has incurred, or reasonably expects to incur, Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The notice shall state the amount claimed (or a good-faith estimate of it) and describe the claim in reasonable detail.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a Claim Notice by written notice to Buyer and the Escrow Agent (an "**Objection Notice**") delivered within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent's receipt of the Claim Notice. If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within that period, it shall disburse to Buyer, on the fifth (5th) Business Day after the end of the period, the amount stated in the Claim Notice (or, if the Escrow Funds are less, the Escrow Funds then remaining).

**5.3 Disputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent receives a timely Objection Notice, it shall disburse the uncontested portion of the claimed amount, if any, to Buyer as provided in Section 5.2. It shall continue to hold the contested portion until it receives either a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order directing its disbursement, and shall then disburse that amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instruction or Final Order within five (5) Business Days.

**5.4 Good-Faith Resolution.** Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any disputed claim and, upon resolution, shall promptly deliver a Joint Written Instruction to the Escrow Agent.

## 6. ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are solely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set out in this Escrow Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a fiduciary for any party.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively, and shall be protected in acting or refraining from acting, on any written notice, instruction, certificate or Final Order that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice and shall be fully protected in acting in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted under this Escrow Agreement except to the extent a court of competent jurisdiction determines that its gross negligence or willful misconduct was the primary cause of a loss. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 6.4, except that a party whose breach of this Escrow Agreement or the Purchase Agreement gave rise to the indemnified matter shall bear the entire amount. This Section 6.4 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or is otherwise uncertain of its duties, it may refrain from taking any action, other than holding the Escrow Funds, until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the Escrow Funds with a court described in Section 9.8 in an interpleader action.

**6.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent within that period. If they fail to do so, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 9.8 to appoint a successor. The resignation or removal takes effect when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to it.

**6.7 Fees and Expenses.** Buyer and Seller shall pay the Escrow Agent the fees set out in Schedule C and reimburse its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, those fees and expenses shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds.

**6.8 Know-Your-Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering, sanctions and customer identification laws.

## 7. AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVES; SECURITY PROCEDURES

**7.1 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller designates in Schedule A the individuals authorized to sign notices and instructions on its behalf (each, an "**Authorized Representative**"). Either party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the Escrow Agent signed by an existing Authorized Representative of that party.

**7.2 Call-Back Verification.** Before disbursing funds on any instruction other than a Final Order, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the instructing party (other than the individual who signed the instruction, where practicable) at the telephone number listed in Schedule A. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until that confirmation is obtained.

**7.3 Wire Details.** The Escrow Agent may rely on the account number or other identifying number of a beneficiary or beneficiary's bank stated in any instruction, even if it identifies a Person or bank other than the one named.

## 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms, except that Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 6.7 survive termination.

**8.2 Termination of Purchase Agreement.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Escrow Agreement shall be of no effect. Any amount deposited with the Escrow Agent shall be returned to Buyer on Buyer's written request.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. New York time on a Business Day, and otherwise on the next Business Day, or (c) one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices shall be addressed to the parties at the addresses set out in Schedule A or to another address designated by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

**9.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Escrow Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither Buyer nor Seller may assign its rights under this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other and of the Escrow Agent. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall succeed to the Escrow Agent's rights and obligations without further act.

**9.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Escrow Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**9.6 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, labor disputes, failure of communications or utilities, or the unavailability of the Federal Reserve wire system.

**9.7 Governing Law.** This Escrow Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**9.8 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**9.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

**9.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
