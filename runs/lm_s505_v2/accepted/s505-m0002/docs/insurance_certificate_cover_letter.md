## 3. Certificate Holder

Each certificate names Buyer as certificate holder, at the address shown above. The certificates are issued for information only. As is customary, they confer no rights on the certificate holder and do not amend, extend or alter the coverage afforded by the policies listed. Buyer is not named as an additional insured on any policy at this time.

## 4. Surety Bonds

The Company's dealer and auction surety bonds are not insurance policies and are not covered by the enclosed certificates. Those bonds are issued by Marrowind Surety Company and listed in Section 4.7, Part B of the Disclosure Schedule. We understand that the Company will request any bond riders needed for the change of control described in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule as part of the regulatory filings under Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement. On written request we will coordinate with Marrowind Surety Company on those riders.

## 5. Change of Control

Several of the policies listed in Section 2 contain change-of-control provisions that require notice to the insurer, and some permit the insurer to re-underwrite or adjust premium following a change in ownership of the named insured. Seller's combined-account policies will also require that the Company be endorsed off Seller's program, or that stand-alone coverage be placed, effective as of the Closing. We will be glad to work with Buyer's broker on any replacement placement or endorsement before the Closing Date. Please give us reasonable advance notice of the anticipated Closing Date for that purpose.
