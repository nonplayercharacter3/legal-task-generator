# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will acquire from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Certain tangible personal property used in the operation of the Auctions is titled or held in Seller's name rather than the Company's.

C. Section 3.2(a)(ii) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing so that the Company owns that property when Buyer acquires the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Bill of Sale:

"**Conveyed Assets**" means all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following tangible personal property, wherever located:

(a) the auction lane equipment, auction block podiums, lane lighting, public address and sound systems, lane display boards and vehicle-flow gates located at the Company's auction facilities and listed in Part A of Schedule 1;

(b) the simulcast cameras, encoders, servers, networking equipment, workstations, tablets and handheld bid-capture devices used in conducting the Auctions and listed in Part B of Schedule 1, but excluding any software or data residing on them that is Assigned IP or Excluded IP;

(c) the yard and lot vehicles, including shuttle vans, lot tractors and utility carts, listed in Part C of Schedule 1, together with the certificates of title to them (the "**Titled Vehicles**");

(d) the reconditioning, detail, inspection and condition-report equipment and tools listed in Part D of Schedule 1; and

(e) the office furniture, fixtures (to the extent personal property), safes, title-storage cabinets and supplies located at the Company's auction facilities and listed in Part E of Schedule 1.

"**Excluded Assets**" means:

(a) all Intellectual Property, including the Assigned IP, which is conveyed only under the IP Assignment Agreement, and the Excluded IP, which Seller retains;

(b) any vehicle consigned for sale at the Auctions, which is not the property of Seller;

(c) all signage, stationery and other materials bearing the "PRITOUTH" name or mark; and

(d) Seller's enterprise general ledger, accounts payable and payroll hardware located outside the Company's auction facilities.

"**Assigned IP**," "**Excluded IP**" and "**Intellectual Property**" have the meanings given in the IP Assignment Agreement.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Conveyance.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to the Company, and the Company accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Conveyed Assets, free and clear of all Liens, to have and to hold for the Company and its successors and assigns forever.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Excluded Assets are not conveyed under this Bill of Sale.

**2.3 Titled Vehicles.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to the Company the original certificate of title for each Titled Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to the Company, together with any odometer disclosure statement, lien release and other document required by the applicable state motor vehicle department to register the Titled Vehicle in the Company's name.

**2.4 Possession.** The Conveyed Assets are located at, and shall remain at, the Company's auction facilities. Possession passes to the Company at the Closing without further act. If any Conveyed Asset is located elsewhere at the Closing, Seller shall deliver it to the Company's auction facility designated by the Company within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date, at Seller's expense.

**2.5 No Assumption of Liabilities.** The Company does not assume, and this Bill of Sale does not transfer to the Company, any liability or obligation of Seller.

## 3. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**3.1 Representations and Indemnity.** Seller's representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities relating to the Conveyed Assets are set out in the Purchase Agreement. Claims for any inaccuracy in or breach of them shall be brought by Buyer and the other Buyer Indemnitees under, and subject to, Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**4.1 Further Assurances.** Consistent with Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall, at the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute and deliver all further instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take all further actions, reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Conveyed Assets. These include duplicate or corrected certificates of title and filings with any state motor vehicle department.

**4.2 Misdirected Assets and Payments.** If Seller holds or receives any Conveyed Asset, or any payment, warranty claim proceeds or insurance proceeds relating to a Conveyed Asset, after the Closing, Seller shall hold it for the Company and promptly deliver it to the Company.

## 5. MISCELLANEOUS

**5.1 Effectiveness.** This Bill of Sale becomes effective only upon the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Bill of Sale shall be void and of no effect.

**5.2 Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the Company in care of Buyer, with a copy to Buyer.

**5.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns and benefits the Company and its successors and assigns.

**5.4 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Bill of Sale and may enforce it directly.

**5.5 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company and consented to in writing by Buyer.

**5.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**5.7 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**5.8 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Seller and the Company each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**5.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** SELLER AND THE COMPANY EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS BILL OF SALE.

**5.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
