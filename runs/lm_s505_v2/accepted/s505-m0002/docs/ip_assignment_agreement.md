# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignor**"), and **Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor and Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will acquire from Assignor all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignor owns certain intellectual property that is used or held for use in the operation of the Auctions, and that property is held in Assignor's name rather than Assignee's.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Assignment at the Closing, so that Assignee holds the Assigned IP when Buyer acquires Assignee.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Assignment and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Assignment:

"**Assigned IP**" means all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under:

(a) the trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress listed in Part A of Schedule 1, together with all common-law rights in them, all registrations and applications for them, and the goodwill of the business connected with their use and symbolized by them (the "**Assigned Marks**");

(b) the internet domain names and social media accounts listed in Part B of Schedule 1 (the "**Assigned Domains**");

(c) the software, source code, object code, databases and related documentation listed in Part C of Schedule 1, including the Auction lane management, simulcast bidding, timed-bid, condition-report and title-tracking applications, and all copyrights in them (the "**Assigned Software**");

(d) all consignor, buying-dealer, bidder and floorplan-lender lists, records and data generated in, or collected through, the operation of the Auctions (the "**Auction Data**");

(e) all trade secrets, know-how, auction procedures, arbitration and sale rules, pricing methodologies and other confidential information used exclusively or primarily in the operation of the Auctions; and

(f) all rights to sue for, and to collect and retain damages and other relief for, past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the foregoing, and all income, royalties and payments now or later due in respect of any of the foregoing.

"**Excluded IP**" means the intellectual property listed in Schedule 2, including the "PRITOUTH" name and mark and Assignor's enterprise accounting and payroll systems.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights of every kind throughout the world, whether registered or unregistered.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Closing, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Liens, for Assignee's own use and enjoyment and for the use and enjoyment of its successors, assigns and legal representatives.

**2.2 Excluded IP.** The Excluded IP is not assigned under this Assignment and remains the property of Assignor.

**2.3 Recordation.** Assignor authorizes the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, each applicable domain name registrar and any other Governmental Authority or registry to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned IP. Assignee may record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in substantially the form of Exhibit A, with any of them. Assignee shall bear the filing fees for recordation.

**2.4 Domain Name and Account Transfers.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Date, Assignor shall initiate the transfer of each Assigned Domain to the registrar account designated by Assignee, deliver all authorization codes, and transfer administrative credentials for each social media account listed in Part B of Schedule 1.

**2.5 Delivery of Software and Data.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, in the form maintained by Assignor in the ordinary course, complete copies of the source code and object code for the Assigned Software and of all Auction Data. To the extent any Assigned Software or Auction Data is hosted on systems that Assignor retains, Assignor shall hold it on Assignee's behalf as Assignee's property. Assignor shall not use or disclose it except as Assignee directs in writing or as permitted by the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**3.1 Assignor Representations.** Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee that:

(a) Assignor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Assignment, and this Assignment is Assignor's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms;

(b) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Liens, and has not previously assigned, transferred, licensed or encumbered any of the Assigned IP; and

(c) the Assigned IP, together with the Intellectual Property otherwise owned by or licensed to Assignee, constitutes all of the Intellectual Property necessary to operate the Auctions as conducted on the date of the Purchase Agreement, other than the Excluded IP.

**3.2 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** The representations and warranties in Section 3.1 are made in furtherance of the Purchase Agreement. Claims for any inaccuracy in or breach of them shall be brought by Buyer and the other Buyer Indemnitees under, and subject to, Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. COVENANTS

**4.1 Further Assurances.** Consistent with Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement, Assignor shall, at Assignee's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute and deliver all further instruments and take all further actions reasonably necessary to perfect, record, register or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. These include declarations, powers of attorney and confirmatory assignments in the form required by any Governmental Authority or registry.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** If Assignor fails to execute any instrument described in Section 4.1 within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request, Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, to execute and file that instrument in Assignor's name, solely for the purposes of Section 4.1.

**4.3 Cessation of Use.** From and after the Closing, Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, use any Assigned Mark or any name or mark confusingly similar to it, or use or disclose any Auction Data or other Assigned IP, except in the performance of the Services under the Transition Services Agreement. Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Assignor shall remove each Assigned Mark from all signage, websites, stationery and promotional materials under its control.

**4.4 Confidentiality.** Assignor's obligations under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement apply to the Auction Data and to all trade secrets and confidential information included in the Assigned IP.

**4.5 Correspondence and Payments.** Assignor shall promptly forward to Assignee any correspondence, notices, inquiries or payments that Assignor receives after the Closing relating to the Assigned IP.

## 5. MISCELLANEOUS

**5.1 Effectiveness.** This Assignment becomes effective only upon the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Assignment shall be void and of no effect.

**5.2 Notices.** Notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignee shall be addressed to Assignee in care of Buyer, with a copy to Buyer.

**5.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign its rights under this Assignment, in whole or in part, to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or any part of the business of the Auctions. Assignor may not assign its obligations under this Assignment without Assignee's prior written consent.

**5.4 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Assignment and may enforce it directly.

**5.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee and consented to in writing by Buyer. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**5.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Assignment is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**5.7 Governing Law.** This Assignment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**5.8 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Assignment shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**5.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ASSIGNMENT.

**5.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
