# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Pritouth Dealer Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Stonebarrow Vehicle Auctions, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Vailous Auto Remarketing Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will acquire from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the Closing, Provider supplied title-office processing support, payroll, accounting and information technology services to Recipient under an intercompany shared services arrangement. That arrangement terminates at the Closing.

C. Section 3.2(a)(ii) of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing so that Recipient continues to receive those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Additional Service**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and records of Recipient that Provider or its personnel access, receive, process or store in performing the Services. Recipient Data includes title files, odometer disclosure statements, consignor, buying-dealer, bidder and floorplan-lender data, Auction sale results and Auction Data (as defined in the IP Assignment Agreement).

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set out in Schedule B, as adjusted under Section 5.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the date stated for that Service in Schedule A, as extended under Section 4.2 or shortened under Section 11.2.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A.

**2.2 Scope.** Except as stated in Schedule A or agreed under Section 2.3, Provider is not required to provide any service that it did not provide to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date. Provider is also not required to provide any service in a volume materially greater than the volume it provided during that period, other than increases reasonably resulting from the ordinary-course growth of the Auctions.

**2.3 Additional Services.** If Recipient reasonably requires a service that Provider provided to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date and that is not listed in Schedule A, Recipient may request it in writing. Provider shall provide that service (each, an "**Additional Service**") on terms, including fees determined on the basis set out in Schedule B, that the parties agree in a written amendment to Schedule A.

**2.4 Third-Party Consents.** If any Service requires the consent of a third-party licensor or vendor, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain it. Recipient shall reimburse Provider's reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs of doing so. If a consent cannot be obtained, the parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a commercially reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 3. SERVICE STANDARDS

**3.1 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) with substantially the same degree of care, skill, priority and timeliness with which it performed the same services for Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and (c) in compliance with applicable Law, including Laws governing motor vehicle titling and odometer disclosure.

**3.2 Licensed Functions.** Where a Service involves an act that applicable Law requires to be performed under one of Recipient's Licenses, Provider shall perform it in Recipient's name, under Recipient's direction, and in accordance with Recipient's written procedures. Recipient remains the licensee of record.

**3.3 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with the information, access to facilities and systems, and decisions that Provider reasonably requires to perform the Services, in a timely manner.

**3.4 Service Reports.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a report summarizing the Services performed during that month. For title and odometer Services, the report shall include the number of titles received, transferred and outstanding, and the number of title and odometer exceptions opened and closed.

## 4. TERM

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Period (the "**Term**").

**4.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Period for any Service once, by up to ninety (90) days, by written notice to Provider delivered at least thirty (30) days before that Service Period ends. During any extension, the Service Fees for the extended Service shall increase by ten percent (10%).

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees for each Service during its Service Period. The Service Fee for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider, without markup, for the reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs that Provider incurs in performing the Services and that are identified as pass-through costs in Schedule B. These include state title and registration fees, courier charges for title documents and third-party vehicle history report charges.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith by written notice to Provider describing the dispute in reasonable detail. The parties shall resolve the dispute under Section 12.

**5.5 Taxes.** Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Recipient shall pay to the extent applicable to the Services. Each party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 6. PERSONNEL AND GOVERNANCE

**6.1 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall appoint an individual (a "**Service Coordinator**") to oversee the Services and act as its primary point of contact. Each party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice to the other.

**6.2 Personnel.** Provider shall determine the personnel who perform the Services. Those personnel remain employees or contractors of Provider and are not employees of Recipient. Provider is solely responsible for their compensation, benefits and employment taxes.

**6.3 Site Rules.** While at Recipient's auction facilities or using Recipient's systems, Provider personnel shall comply with Recipient's reasonable safety, security and information technology policies that are provided to Provider in writing.

**6.4 Monthly Review.** The Service Coordinators shall meet at least monthly during the Term to review the performance of the Services and the progress of Recipient's migration plan under Section 11.4.

## 7. DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider shall use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services and shall not sell, license or disclose it, or use it for its own account or for the benefit of any other Person.

**7.2 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Recipient Data at least as protective as those it maintained for that data before the Closing and as required by applicable Law. These include the safeguards required under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the FTC Safeguards Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 314, for customer financial information.

**7.3 Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Recipient in writing within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or acquisition, loss or disclosure of, Recipient Data, and shall cooperate with Recipient in investigating and remediating the incident.

**7.4 Confidentiality.** Each party shall hold in confidence all confidential information of the other party received in connection with this Agreement and use it only to perform or receive the Services. This Section 7.4 does not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, or that is required to be disclosed by Law, provided that the disclosing party gives the other party prompt notice of the requirement where lawful. Provider's obligations under this Section 7.4 are in addition to its obligations under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.5 Return of Data.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the end of the Service Period for each Service, Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Recipient Data relating to that Service in a commonly used, machine-readable format. Provider shall then securely delete all copies, except archival copies that it must retain by Law, which remain subject to this Section 7.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Provider Systems.** Provider retains all right, title and interest in its systems, software and processes used to perform the Services, including the Excluded IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any of them to Recipient.

**8.2 Recipient Property.** Recipient retains all right, title and interest in the Assigned IP and the Recipient Data. To the extent the Services require it, Recipient grants Provider a non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable license during the Term to use the Assigned Software and Recipient Data solely to perform the Services.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO REPRESENTATIONS OR WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, CONCERNING THE SERVICES, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

**9.2 Cap on Liability.** The aggregate liability of Provider and its Affiliates arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the Service Fees paid in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The aggregate liability of Recipient under this Agreement, other than for its obligation to pay Service Fees and pass-through costs, is subject to the same limit. This Section 9.2 does not apply to a party's liability for fraud or willful misconduct.

**9.3 Excluded Damages.** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY PUNITIVE, EXEMPLARY, SPECIAL, INDIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, EXCEPT TO THE EXTENT PAID TO A THIRD PARTY IN RESPECT OF A CLAIM FOR WHICH INDEMNIFICATION IS AVAILABLE UNDER SECTION 10.

**9.4 Re-Performance.** If Provider fails to perform any Service in accordance with Section 3.1, Provider shall, at Recipient's request and at no additional charge, promptly re-perform that Service.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**10.1 By Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement, (b) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services, or (c) any violation of applicable Law by Provider or its personnel in performing the Services.

**10.2 By Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by (a) Recipient's breach of this Agreement or (b) Provider's performance of the Services in accordance with Recipient's written instructions.

**10.3 Procedures.** Claims under this Section 10 shall be made and defended in accordance with the procedures in Section 9.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, which apply to this Agreement as if set out in it, with references to the indemnifying and indemnified parties construed accordingly.

**10.4 Limitation.** The indemnities in this Section 10 are subject to Section 9.

## 11. TERMINATION

**11.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, except that Provider may terminate for non-payment only if the undisputed amount remains unpaid for forty-five (45) days after that notice.

**11.2 Early Termination of Services.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience by giving Provider at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. The Service Fees shall be reduced accordingly from the effective date of termination. If a terminated Service is interdependent with another Service as indicated in Schedule A, Provider shall notify Recipient within five (5) Business Days, and the parties shall agree in good faith on any adjustment.

**11.3 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, Recipient shall pay all Service Fees and pass-through costs accrued through the effective date. Sections 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 13 survive.

**11.4 Migration Assistance.** During the Term and for thirty (30) days after the last Service Period ends, Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to transition the Services to Recipient or its designee. This assistance includes knowledge transfer, delivery of Recipient Data under Section 7.5 and reasonable cooperation with Recipient's replacement vendors. Assistance beyond twenty (20) hours per month shall be charged at the hourly rates in Schedule B.

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**12.1 Escalation.** The Service Coordinators shall first attempt to resolve any dispute under this Agreement. If they cannot resolve it within ten (10) Business Days, either party may refer it to a senior executive of each party, who shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after the referral.

**12.2 Continued Performance.** Pending resolution of any dispute, Provider shall continue to perform the Services, and Recipient shall continue to pay all undisputed Service Fees.

**12.3 Litigation.** If the senior executives do not resolve the dispute within twenty (20) Business Days after the referral, either party may pursue its remedies in accordance with Sections 13.9 and 13.10.

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS

**13.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship between the parties.

**13.2 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Provider shall be sent to Seller's address under the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Recipient shall be addressed to Recipient in care of Buyer, with a copy to Buyer.

**13.3 Assignment and Delegation.** Provider may assign this Agreement, or delegate or subcontract the performance of any Services, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Provider. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**13.4 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, severe weather, pandemic, war, terrorism, labor disputes, or failure of utilities or communications networks. The affected party shall notify the other promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents performance of a Service for more than thirty (30) days, Recipient may terminate that Service without liability.

**13.5 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce it directly. Otherwise, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.

**13.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, including its Schedules, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Provider and Recipient. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**13.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**13.8 Effectiveness.** This Agreement becomes effective only upon the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void and of no effect.

**13.9 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**13.10 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**13.11 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY LEGAL PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**13.12 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
