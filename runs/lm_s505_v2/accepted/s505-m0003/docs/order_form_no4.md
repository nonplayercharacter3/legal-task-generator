# ORDER FORM NO. 4

**Thornfall Reckoning – QA Testing, Localization and Launch Support**

This Order Form No. 4 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into under the Master Services Agreement dated as of January 15, 2025 (the "**Agreement**") between **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Draibouth Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). This Order Form is dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Order Form Effective Date**"). This copy is a conformed copy that incorporates Change Order No. 1 dated September 4, 2026.

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 **Customer Title.** This Order Form covers Services for *Thornfall Reckoning*, an action role-playing game published by Customer (the "**Title**"). The Title is a Customer Title under the Agreement.

1.2 **Development Phase.** Since the Order Form Effective Date, Provider has performed functional, compatibility, certification pre-check and localization testing on the alpha, beta and release candidate Builds of the Title under Section 3 of this Order Form.

1.3 **Gold Master Build.** Provider Delivered the gold master Build of the Title, version 1.0.0.4471 (the "**Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build**"), through the Delivery Portal on September 1, 2026, together with its Delivery Package.

1.4 **Change Order No. 1.** Under Section 2.3 of the Agreement, the Parties signed Change Order No. 1 dated September 4, 2026. That change order added the day-one patch testing and launch live-operations support Services described in Sections 3.4 and 3.5 of this Order Form, and the related Fees in Section 5.

## 2. ORDER FORM TERM

2.1 **Term.** This Order Form begins on the Order Form Effective Date. It continues until the later of (a) March 31, 2027 and (b) completion of all Services described in Section 3, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 15 of the Agreement.

2.2 **Agreement Governs.** This Order Form is governed by and forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Agreement or the Quality Agreement.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Functional and Compatibility Testing.** Provider performs functional, regression and compatibility testing of each Build against the test plan approved under Section 3.2 of the Quality Agreement. Testing covers all game modes, including the single-player campaign, cooperative multiplayer and the "Reckoning Trials" endgame mode.

3.2 **Certification Pre-Checks.** For each release candidate and gold master Build, Provider completes certification pre-checks under Section 3.5 of the Quality Agreement for each Target Platform listed in Section 4.1.

3.3 **Localization.** Provider translates and localizes in-game text, user interface, subtitles and store listings from English into the Target Languages listed in Section 4.2, and performs linguistic quality assurance testing under Section 7 of the Quality Agreement.

3.4 **Day-One Patch Testing.** Provider performs functional and regression testing of the day-one patch Build and any hotfix Builds issued in the ninety (90) days after commercial release of the Title.

3.5 **Launch Live-Operations Support.** For one hundred twenty (120) days after commercial release of the Title, Provider provides player support through ticketing and email channels, community moderation on Customer's official forums and Discord server, and bug triage and escalation from the live environment. Provider performs these Services in English, French, German and Spanish, using scripts and escalation procedures approved by Customer under Section 10.2 of the Agreement.

## 4. PLATFORMS, LANGUAGES AND REQUIREMENTS

4.1 **Target Platforms.** PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Windows PC (Steam and Epic Games Store).

4.2 **Target Languages.** French, Italian, German, Spanish (Spain), Spanish (Latin America), Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

4.3 **Platform Holder Requirements.** For purposes of Section 3.2 of the Agreement, the platform holders are Sony Interactive Entertainment, Microsoft Corporation, Valve Corporation and Epic Games, Inc. Customer provides the applicable platform development kits under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Personal Data.** The Services in Sections 3.4 and 3.5 involve the processing of player Personal Data. Provider processes that Personal Data in accordance with Section 10 of the Agreement and the DPA.

## 5. DELIVERABLES, MILESTONES AND FEES

5.1 **Milestones.** Provider delivers the following Deliverables against the following Milestones:

| **Milestone** | **Deliverable** | **Milestone Date** | **Milestone Fee** |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 – Alpha | Alpha test report; approved test plan | April 15, 2026 | $145,000 |
| M2 – Beta | Beta Build test report; localized beta text (all Target Languages) | June 15, 2026 | $210,000 |
| M3 – Release Candidate | Release candidate Build with Delivery Package; certification pre-check results | August 3, 2026 | $265,000 |
| M4 – Gold Master | Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build with Delivery Package; final LQA reports; translation memories | September 1, 2026 | $420,000 |

5.2 **Time-and-Materials Services.** Customer pays for the Services in Sections 3.4 and 3.5 at the following rates, invoiced monthly in arrears under Section 5.1 of the Agreement:

| **Role** | **Hourly Rate** |
|---|---|
| QA Tester | $38 |
| Senior QA Tester / Lead | $55 |
| Localization Specialist | $62 |
| Player Support Agent | $34 |
| Community Moderator | $36 |
| Project Manager | $85 |

5.3 **Time-and-Materials Cap.** Fees for the Services in Sections 3.4 and 3.5 will not exceed $380,000 in total without Customer's prior written approval.

5.4 **Invoicing of Milestone Fees.** Provider invoices each Milestone Fee in accordance with Section 5.1 of the Agreement. Customer pays undisputed invoices in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Expenses.** No expenses are pre-authorized under this Order Form. Section 5.2 of the Agreement applies to any expenses.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 **Acceptance Criteria.** The Acceptance Criteria for each Build are the release thresholds in Section 6.3 of the Quality Agreement, the approved test plan, and the applicable platform holder requirements identified in Section 4.3. For the Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build, the Acceptance Criteria also require that all Target Language text is complete and integrated.

6.2 **Procedure.** Acceptance and rejection of each Deliverable under this Order Form are governed by Section 6 of the Agreement and Section 5 of the Quality Agreement.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS AND STAFFING

7.1 **Service Levels.** The Service Level Schedule applies to all Services under this Order Form. For the Services in Section 3.5, the player support response times and moderation coverage hours stated in the Service Level Schedule for live-operations support apply.

7.2 **Minimum Staffing.** During the period from the M3 Milestone Date through thirty (30) days after commercial release, Provider dedicates at least twenty-four (24) QA Testers and four (4) Senior QA Testers to the Title.

7.3 **Key Personnel.** For purposes of Section 4.2 of the Agreement, the key personnel are:

(a) Provider's QA Project Lead for the Title;

(b) Provider's Localization Manager for the Title; and

(c) Provider's Live-Operations Supervisor for the Title.
