## 6. DESCRIPTION OF OPERATIONS

6.1 **Operations Covered.** Outsourced video game quality assurance testing, localization and live-operations support services, including player support and community moderation. These include services the Insured performs for the Certificate Holder under the Agreement and each Order Form under it.

6.2 **Additional Insured Status.** Lebun Interactive, Inc., its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees are included as additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability policy (Insurer A). The coverage applies to liability arising out of the Insured's operations or completed operations performed for the additional insureds. It is provided by Endorsement GSC-AI-2010 (Additional Insured – Owners, Lessees or Contractors – Scheduled Person or Organization) and Endorsement GSC-AI-2037 (Additional Insured – Completed Operations). The additional insured coverage is primary and non-contributory with any other insurance available to the additional insureds, where required by written contract.

6.3 **Umbrella.** The Umbrella Liability policy (Insurer D) follows form over the Commercial General Liability policy. It extends to the additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability policy.

6.4 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Waiver of subrogation in favor of the Certificate Holder applies under the Commercial General Liability and Workers' Compensation policies, where required by written contract. It is provided by Endorsement GSC-WS-2404 and Endorsement EMI-WC-000313.

6.5 **Cyber Coverage.** The Technology Errors and Omissions and Cyber Liability policy (Insurer B) includes network security and privacy liability, data breach response costs, regulatory defense and penalties where insurable by law, and media liability. Each of these is subject to the policy limit shown in Section 5.2.

6.6 **Extended Coverage.** The Insured has confirmed to the Producer that it will maintain the coverages shown in this certificate, or replacement coverage with substantially the same limits, for the period required by Section 16.1 of the Agreement. The Producer will issue a renewal certificate on each policy renewal.

## 8. CANCELLATION

8.1 **Notice of Cancellation.** If any of the policies described above is cancelled before its expiration date, notice will be delivered in accordance with the policy provisions. Under Endorsements GSC-NC-0130, HSI-NC-0130, EMI-NC-0130 and GSE-NC-0130, each insurer will mail or deliver written notice to the Certificate Holder at the address in Section 7 at least thirty (30) days before the cancellation takes effect. For cancellation for non-payment of premium, the notice period is ten (10) days.

8.2 **Material Change.** Under the endorsements listed in Section 8.1, the issuing insurer will give the Certificate Holder at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any non-renewal or material reduction in the coverage described in this certificate.
