# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Draibouth Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer publishes and distributes video games and other interactive entertainment products.

B. Provider provides outsourced game quality assurance testing, localization and live-operations support services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to provide those services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Build**" means any version, iteration, candidate or release of a Customer Title, or any component of one, that Provider delivers or makes available under an Order Form. This includes alpha, beta, release candidate and gold master builds and localized versions.

"**Change of Control**" means, for Customer, (a) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction in which Customer's stockholders immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the surviving entity immediately after it; (b) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of Customer's voting securities; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of Customer's assets.

"**Competitor**" means any person or entity, or any Affiliate of one, that provides outsourced game testing, localization or player-support services to third parties as a material line of its business.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Covered Services**" means all services of the type described in Schedule A or in any Order Form, including functional, compatibility, compliance, performance and localization testing, linguistic translation and cultural adaptation, community management, player support and other live-operations support services, whether performed before or after a Customer Title is commercially released.

"**Customer Materials**" means all Customer Titles, Builds, source and object code, art assets, scripts, text, audio, design documents, trademarks, player data and other materials that Customer or its Affiliates provide to Provider.

"**Customer Title**" means any video game, application, downloadable content, expansion or other interactive entertainment product, on any platform, that is developed, published, co-published or distributed by Customer or its Affiliates, including any sequel, port, remaster or localized version of one.

"**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the data processing addendum entered into between the Parties in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

"**Deliverables**" means all reports, bug databases, test plans, test cases, translated text, localized assets, Builds and other work product that Provider prepares or delivers to Customer under an Order Form.

"**Delivery**" has the meaning given in the Quality Agreement.

"**Fees**" means the fees stated in an Order Form.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

"**Order Form**" means an order form, statement of work or similar ordering document that refers to this Agreement and that both Parties have signed.

"**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement entered into between the Parties that sets out the quality standards, Delivery requirements, acceptance and rejection procedures and defect classification for Deliverables, as amended from time to time.

"**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.2.

"**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule entered into between the Parties that sets out the service levels and service credits that apply to the Services, as amended from time to time.

"**Services**" means the Covered Services that Provider performs under an Order Form.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with all Renewal Terms.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES AND ORDER FORMS

2.1 **Performance of Services.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Order Form in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and the Quality Agreement.

2.2 **Order Forms.** Customer may order Services from time to time by Order Form. Each Order Form shall state, as applicable, the Customer Title, the Services, the Deliverables, any milestones and milestone dates, the Fees and the payment schedule. Each Order Form is governed by and forms part of this Agreement. Provider shall have no obligation under an Order Form until both Parties have signed it.

2.3 **Change Requests.** Either Party may request a change to the scope of an Order Form in writing. Provider shall respond within five (5) business days with a written estimate of the effect of the change on the Fees and schedule. A change is binding only when both Parties have signed a written change order.

2.4 **Affiliates.** Customer's Affiliates may order Services under this Agreement by signing an Order Form with Provider. For that Order Form, references to "Customer" include the ordering Affiliate, and that Affiliate is responsible for its own obligations under it.

## 3. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS; EXCLUSIVITY

3.1 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with generally accepted industry standards for outsourced video game services, and in compliance with all applicable laws.

3.2 **Platform Requirements.** Provider shall comply with the technical, certification and submission requirements of each platform holder that Customer identifies in an Order Form, as those requirements are made available to Provider.

3.3 **Facilities and Equipment.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Provider shall provide at its own cost all facilities, development kits, test hardware, software licenses and network connectivity needed to perform the Services. Customer shall provide any platform development kits that a platform holder licenses only to publishers, and Provider shall hold them in accordance with the applicable platform holder's requirements.

3.4 **Security.** Provider shall keep administrative, physical and technical safeguards in place to protect Customer Materials against unauthorized access, copying, disclosure or leakage. These safeguards must include access-controlled test facilities, controlled distribution of Builds, and watermarking or other tracking of pre-release Builds where Customer requests it. Provider shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours after it becomes aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized access to or disclosure of any pre-release Build.

3.5 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Customer shall not engage, and shall cause its Affiliates not to engage, any third party to perform Covered Services for any Customer Title, and shall not perform Covered Services for any Customer Title through an internal team created after the Effective Date for that purpose. In return, Provider shall give Customer priority in scheduling and staffing over Provider's other customers, consistent with the capacity commitments in the Service Level Schedule.

3.6 **Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any of the Services without Customer's prior written approval, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 4. PERSONNEL

4.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign personnel who have the skills, training and language proficiency needed to perform the Services. Personnel performing localization must be native or near-native speakers of the target language.

4.2 **Key Personnel.** Each Order Form may designate key personnel. Provider shall not reassign or remove key personnel without at least fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to Customer, except where the individual has resigned, has been terminated for cause, or is unavailable because of illness or leave. In each case Provider shall promptly propose a qualified replacement.

4.3 **Removal.** Customer may require, on reasonable grounds and by written notice, that Provider remove any individual from performing the Services. Provider shall do so promptly and shall replace that individual with qualified personnel at no additional cost to Customer.

4.4 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Provider is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits, taxes and supervision of its personnel. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.

4.5 **Confidentiality and Assignment Agreements.** Before any individual performs Services, Provider shall ensure that the individual has signed a written agreement that (a) protects Confidential Information on terms at least as protective as Section 9 and (b) assigns to Provider all rights in work product sufficient for Provider to comply with Section 8.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the Fees set out in each Order Form. Unless an Order Form states otherwise, Fees for time-and-materials Services are invoiced monthly in arrears, and milestone Fees are invoiced on acceptance or deemed acceptance of the applicable Deliverable under Section 6.

5.2 **Expenses.** Customer shall reimburse reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses only if they are authorized in an Order Form or approved in advance by Customer in writing.

5.3 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Customer in U.S. dollars. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receiving it. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Provider in writing of the dispute and the reasons for it before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

5.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay all such taxes that apply to the Services, except taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees. If applicable law requires Customer to withhold any tax, Customer may deduct it from the payment and shall give Provider evidence of the withholding.

5.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.6 **Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of time and expenses billed under this Agreement for at least three (3) years after the relevant invoice. On reasonable prior notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, Customer may audit those records.

## 6. DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 **Delivery.** Provider shall deliver each Deliverable in accordance with the applicable Order Form and the Delivery requirements of the Quality Agreement.

6.2 **Acceptance.** Acceptance and rejection of Deliverables, including Builds, are subject to the acceptance testing, rejection, remediation and deemed acceptance procedures of the Quality Agreement. Those procedures, and the periods stated in them, apply to every Deliverable unless the applicable Order Form expressly states a different procedure for that Deliverable.

6.3 **Effect of Acceptance.** Acceptance or deemed acceptance of a Deliverable does not waive Customer's rights under Section 11 with respect to that Deliverable.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS

7.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet or exceed the service levels set out in the Service Level Schedule.

7.2 **Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider shall issue the service credits stated in the Service Level Schedule. Service credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the Services. They are not Customer's sole remedy for a failure that also constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.

7.3 **Reporting.** Provider shall give Customer monthly reports on its performance against each service level, in the form required by the Service Level Schedule.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Customer Materials.** As between the Parties, Customer and its licensors own all right, title and interest in and to the Customer Materials. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Customer Materials solely to perform the Services.

8.2 **Deliverables.** All Deliverables, and all intellectual property rights in them, are the sole property of Customer from creation. To the extent any Deliverable does not vest in Customer by operation of law, Provider hereby assigns to Customer all right, title and interest in and to it. Provider shall sign any documents and take any actions that Customer reasonably requests to perfect that assignment.

8.3 **Provider Tools.** Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing and independently developed testing tools, automation frameworks, translation memories that do not contain Customer content, methods and know-how ("**Provider Tools**"). To the extent any Provider Tool is incorporated into a Deliverable, Provider grants Customer and its Affiliates a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, reproduce and modify that Provider Tool as part of the Deliverable.

8.4 **Translation Memories.** All translation memories, glossaries and terminology databases that contain Customer content are Deliverables. Provider shall deliver them to Customer in a standard industry format on request and on termination or expiration of the applicable Order Form.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement, whether before or after the Effective Date and in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer's Confidential Information includes all Customer Materials, unreleased Customer Titles, Builds, release dates and the terms of each Order Form.

9.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

9.3 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of similar importance, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 9.

9.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided that, where legally permitted, it gives the Discloser prompt prior notice and reasonable assistance in seeking a protective order.

9.5 **Return.** On the Discloser's request or on termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information and certify in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain copies required by law or held in routine backup systems, and those copies remain subject to this Section 9.

9.6 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 continue during the Term and for five (5) years afterwards. For trade secrets and unreleased Customer Titles, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret or the title remains unreleased.

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 **DPA.** To the extent Provider processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer in performing the Services, including in player support and live-operations Services, the Parties shall comply with the DPA.

10.2 **Player Communications.** Provider shall communicate with players only through channels and using scripts, policies and escalation procedures that Customer has approved. Provider shall not make any statement on Customer's behalf about refunds, account sanctions or legal matters except as those procedures authorize.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that (a) it is duly organized and in good standing; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and (c) its performance of this Agreement will not breach any agreement to which it is a party.

11.2 **Provider Warranties.** Provider represents and warrants that (a) the Services will be performed in accordance with Section 3.1; (b) each Deliverable will conform in all material respects to the applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement for ninety (90) days after acceptance or deemed acceptance; (c) the Deliverables and Provider Tools will not infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights; and (d) Provider will not introduce any virus, malicious code or unauthorized disabling mechanism into any Build or Customer system.

11.3 **Remedy.** If a Deliverable breaches Section 11.2(b), Provider shall promptly correct or re-perform the non-conforming Deliverable at no additional cost. If Provider fails to do so within thirty (30) days after Customer's written notice, Customer may terminate the applicable Order Form and recover the Fees paid for the non-conforming Deliverable.

11.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose or non-infringement.

## 12. TERM

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 15.

12.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive three (3) year Renewal Terms (each a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal not less than one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

12.3 **Order Forms.** Each Order Form remains in effect for the term stated in it. If this Agreement expires or is terminated while an Order Form remains in effect, this Agreement continues to govern that Order Form until the Order Form expires or is terminated, unless this Agreement provides otherwise.

## 13. INDEMNIFICATION

13.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim, and shall indemnify them against resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent the claim arises from (a) an allegation that any Deliverable or Provider Tool infringes or misappropriates a third party's intellectual property rights; (b) Provider's breach of Section 3.4, Section 9 or the DPA; (c) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel; or (d) any claim by Provider's personnel or subcontractors for compensation, benefits or employment-related rights.

13.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend Provider and its officers, directors and employees against any third-party claim, and shall indemnify them against resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent the claim alleges that Customer Materials, used by Provider as authorized under this Agreement, infringe or misappropriate a third party's intellectual property rights.

13.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) notify the indemnifying Party promptly of the claim, although delay relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced; (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

14.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except for the Excluded Claims, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits or revenues, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

14.2 **Cap.** Except for the Excluded Claims, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid and payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

14.3 **Excluded Claims.** "**Excluded Claims**" means (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 13; (b) a Party's breach of Section 9; (c) Provider's breach of Section 3.4 or the DPA; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; and (e) Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate any Order Form for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Customer shall pay Provider for Services performed and accepted Deliverables up to the effective date of termination, and for any non-cancellable commitments that the Order Form expressly identifies.

15.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form by written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement or that Order Form and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of it; or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Change of Control.** If Customer undergoes a Change of Control in which Customer is acquired by a Competitor, or merges with or into a Competitor, without Provider's prior written consent, Provider may terminate this Agreement and all Order Forms by giving Customer thirty (30) days' written notice. Provider may give that notice at any time within ninety (90) days after it receives written notice of the Change of Control from Customer. Customer shall notify Provider in writing of any agreement providing for a Change of Control within fifteen (15) days after signing it.

15.4 **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement or an Order Form:

(a) Provider shall stop performing the affected Services, except as needed for transition under Section 15.5;

(b) Provider shall deliver to Customer all completed and in-progress Deliverables, including Builds, bug databases and translation memories, for which Customer has paid or will pay;

(c) each Party shall comply with Section 9.5; and

(d) Customer shall pay all undisputed Fees for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

15.5 **Transition Assistance.** For up to ninety (90) days after termination or expiration, at Customer's request and at the rates in the applicable Order Form, Provider shall give reasonable assistance to transfer the Services to Customer or a replacement provider. This includes knowledge transfer and handover of test plans, test cases, open defect lists and localization assets.

15.6 **Survival.** Sections 5, 8, 9, 10, 11.4, 13, 14, 15.4, 15.5, 15.6 and 17, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 16. INSURANCE

16.1 **Required Coverage.** During the Term and for one (1) year afterwards, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, at least the following insurance:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) technology errors and omissions and cyber liability insurance, with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per claim and in the aggregate;

(c) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits, and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000; and

(d) umbrella or excess liability insurance, with limits of not less than $5,000,000.

16.2 **Certificates.** Provider shall have Customer named as an additional insured on the policy in Section 16.1(a). Provider shall give Customer certificates of insurance showing the required coverage on the Effective Date and on each policy renewal, and shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

17.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Either Party may, however, seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its intellectual property or Confidential Information.

17.3 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses stated on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt. Notices of breach, termination or non-renewal must also be sent by overnight courier.

17.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents that make up the Parties' agreement, the following order of precedence applies: signed amendments, then the DPA (as to Personal Data), then this Agreement, the Order Forms, the Service Level Schedule, and the Quality Agreement.

17.5 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate, or to a successor in a merger, reorganization or sale of all or substantially all of its assets or business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other Party. This Section 17.5 does not limit Section 15.3. Any attempted assignment in breach of this Section 17.5 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

17.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it.

17.7 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure to perform, other than a payment obligation, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing a material part of the Services for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form on written notice.

17.8 **Publicity.** Provider shall not identify Customer or any Customer Title as a client or project in any publicity, marketing or credits without Customer's prior written approval, except for in-game credits that Customer provides.

17.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

17.10 **Equitable Relief.** A breach of Section 3.4, Section 8 or Section 9 may cause irreparable harm for which damages are not an adequate remedy. The non-breaching Party may therefore seek injunctive relief without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

17.11 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.

## SCHEDULE A – SERVICES DESCRIPTION

**A.1 Quality Assurance Testing.** Functional testing, regression testing, compatibility testing across hardware and operating system configurations, platform certification and compliance pre-checks, performance and load testing, network and multiplayer testing, and test plan and test case development.

**A.2 Localization.** Translation of in-game text, user interface, subtitles, store listings and marketing copy; cultural adaptation; linguistic quality assurance testing; voice-over script adaptation; and management of translation memories and glossaries.

**A.3 Live-Operations Support.** Player support through ticketing, email and chat channels; community management and moderation; live-event monitoring; bug triage and reporting from live environments; and escalation to Customer's development and operations teams.

**A.4 Ordering.** Provider shall perform the specific Services for each Customer Title only as set out in an Order Form.
