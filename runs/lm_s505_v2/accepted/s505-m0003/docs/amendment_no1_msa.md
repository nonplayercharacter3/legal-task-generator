# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of October 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Draibouth Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of January 15, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Provider performs game quality assurance testing, localization and live-operations support services for Customer under Order Forms.

B. On September 30, 2026, Customer entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the "**Merger Agreement**") with Fennick Pine Entertainment Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a publisher of console and mobile games ("**Fennick Pine**"), and certain of Fennick Pine's subsidiaries. Under the Merger Agreement, Customer will merge with a wholly owned subsidiary of Fennick Pine and survive as a wholly owned subsidiary of Fennick Pine (the "**Merger**"), subject to the conditions stated in the Merger Agreement.

C. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement and to record certain related matters, as set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning, and each reference to the Agreement in any Order Form, the Service Level Schedule, the Quality Agreement or the DPA, means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 1.1

2.1 **Competitor.** The definition of "Competitor" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "**Competitor**" means any person or entity, or any Affiliate of one, that (a) provides outsourced game testing, localization or player-support services to third parties as a material line of its business, or (b) develops, publishes or distributes interactive entertainment software, including video games for console, personal computer, mobile or online platforms.

2.2 **No Other Changes to Definitions.** Except as stated in Section 2.1, the definitions in Section 1.1 of the Agreement are unchanged.

## 3. NOTICE OF MERGER AGREEMENT

3.1 **Acknowledgment of Receipt.** Provider acknowledges that it has received Customer's written notice of the Merger Agreement under the last sentence of Section 15.3 of the Agreement, and that the notice was delivered within the period required by that sentence.

3.2 **Information.** Customer shall notify Provider in writing within five (5) business days after the closing of the Merger, or after any termination of the Merger Agreement, and shall respond to Provider's reasonable requests for non-confidential information about the expected timing of the closing.

3.3 **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of the Merger Agreement, to the extent not publicly announced by Customer or Fennick Pine, are Customer's Confidential Information under Section 9 of the Agreement.

## 4. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

4.1 **Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build.** By letter dated September 25, 2026 (the "**Rejection Letter**"), Customer gave Provider written notice rejecting the Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build delivered under Order Form No. 4, on the grounds stated in the Rejection Letter. Provider has disputed the rejection, and the Parties have not resolved the matters raised in the Rejection Letter as of the Amendment Effective Date.

4.2 **Customer's Position.** Customer maintains the rejection stated in the Rejection Letter and continues to dispute that the Thornfall Reckoning Gold Master Build conforms to Order Form No. 4 and the Quality Agreement, and that any milestone Fee related to it is payable.

4.3 **No Extension or Waiver.** Nothing in this Amendment extends, tolls or waives any period under the Agreement, the Quality Agreement or any Order Form, or constitutes an admission by either Party about the matters described in Section 4.1.

4.4 **Rights Reserved.** Each Party reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement, the Quality Agreement, Order Form No. 4 and applicable law with respect to the matters described in Section 4.1. The Parties shall continue to discuss those matters in good faith.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement, each Order Form, the Service Level Schedule, the Quality Agreement and the DPA remain unchanged and in full force and effect. This Amendment forms part of the Agreement.

5.2 **Representations.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, signed and delivered by it.

5.3 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this Amendment.

5.4 **Notices.** Notices relating to this Amendment shall be given in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.
