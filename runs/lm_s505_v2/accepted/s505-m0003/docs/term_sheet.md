# SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

**Proposed Outsourced Game Services Relationship**
**Lebun Interactive, Inc. / Draibouth Studios LLC**

**Date:** November 22, 2024

**Status:** Non-binding, except as stated in Section 14

## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Summary of Principal Terms (this "**Term Sheet**") sets out the principal terms on which **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Lebun**"), proposes to engage **Draibouth Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Draibouth**"), to provide outsourced game services. Lebun and Draibouth are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

1.2 The Parties intend to record these terms in a master services agreement (the "**MSA**"), together with order forms, a service level schedule, a quality agreement and a data processing addendum (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**").

## 2. PARTIES

| **Role** | **Party** |
|---|---|
| Customer | Lebun Interactive, Inc., a video game publisher |
| Provider | Draibouth Studios LLC, a provider of outsourced game quality assurance testing, localization and live-operations support services |

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Service Lines.** Draibouth will offer the following services, which Lebun may order title by title:

(a) **Quality Assurance Testing:** functional, regression, compatibility, certification pre-check, performance and multiplayer testing;

(b) **Localization:** translation and cultural adaptation of in-game text, user interface, subtitles and store listings, with linguistic quality assurance testing; and

(c) **Live-Operations Support:** player support, community moderation, live-event monitoring and bug triage from live environments.

3.2 **Order Forms.** Draibouth will perform services only as described in signed order forms. Each order form will state the title, services, deliverables, milestones and fees. Lebun's affiliates may sign their own order forms.

3.3 **No Minimum Commitment.** Lebun will have no minimum volume or spend commitment.

## 4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 **Initial Term.** The MSA will have an initial term of three (3) years from its effective date.

4.2 **Renewal.** After the initial term, the MSA will renew automatically for one-year renewal terms. Either Party may opt out of any renewal by giving the other Party written notice at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 **Order Forms.** Each order form will run for the term stated in it. The MSA will continue to govern any order form that remains in effect when the MSA expires or terminates.

## 5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 **Scope.** During the term, Lebun will use Draibouth on an exclusive basis for functional QA testing of Lebun-published console titles in North America only.

5.2 **Excluded Services and Titles.** The exclusivity will not extend to compatibility, performance, certification or other testing; to localization or live-operations support; to PC, mobile or online-only titles; to titles of Lebun's affiliates; or to work performed outside North America. Lebun may continue to use its internal teams and other vendors for those services and titles.

5.3 **Provider Priority.** In return, Draibouth will give Lebun scheduling and staffing priority over its other customers, consistent with capacity commitments to be set out in the service level schedule.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Fees will be set in each order form, either as milestone fees or as time-and-materials rates. Draibouth's indicative 2025 hourly rate card is attached as Annex 1.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Time-and-materials fees will be invoiced monthly in arrears. Milestone fees will be invoiced on acceptance or deemed acceptance of the related deliverable.

6.3 **Payment.** Lebun will pay undisputed invoices within 45 days after receipt, in U.S. dollars. Lebun may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith on written notice before the due date.

6.4 **Expenses.** Expenses will be reimbursed only if authorized in an order form or approved in advance in writing.

## 7. QUALITY, DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

7.1 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties will sign a quality agreement covering test coverage, bug reporting, delivery packages, defect severity levels and release thresholds.

7.2 **Delivery.** Builds will be delivered through a Lebun-designated access-controlled portal with a delivery package and a written delivery notice.

7.3 **Acceptance.** Lebun will have 15 calendar days after delivery to accept or reject a deliverable by written notice. A deliverable not rejected in that period will be deemed accepted. Draibouth will remediate rejected deliverables within 10 days.

## 8. SERVICE LEVELS

8.1 A service level schedule will set response times for bug reporting and player support, moderation coverage hours, staffing and capacity commitments, and service credits for failures. Service credits will not be Lebun's sole remedy for a material breach.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Deliverables.** Lebun will own all deliverables, including bug databases and translation memories that contain Lebun content.

9.2 **Provider Tools.** Draibouth will keep its pre-existing tools and know-how. Lebun will receive a perpetual, royalty-free license to any tools incorporated in deliverables.

9.3 **Confidentiality and Security.** The MSA will include mutual confidentiality obligations and Draibouth security obligations for pre-release builds, including access-controlled facilities, build watermarking on request and 24-hour leak notification.

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 Draibouth will process player personal data only as Lebun's processor and under a data processing addendum.

## 11. WARRANTIES, INDEMNITIES AND LIABILITY

11.1 **Warranty.** Deliverables will materially conform to their specifications for 90 days after acceptance or deemed acceptance, with correction or re-performance as the remedy.

11.2 **Indemnities.** Draibouth will indemnify for intellectual property infringement by deliverables and tools, breach of security, confidentiality or data protection obligations, gross negligence or willful misconduct, and personnel claims. Lebun will indemnify for intellectual property infringement by Lebun materials.

11.3 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's liability will be capped at fees paid and payable in the 12 months before the claim, with customary exclusions for indemnities, confidentiality, data and security breaches, gross negligence, fraud, willful misconduct and payment of fees.

## 12. TERMINATION

12.1 **Convenience.** Lebun may terminate any order form on 30 days' notice.

12.2 **Cause.** Either Party may terminate for uncured material breach (30-day cure period) or insolvency.

12.3 **Change of Control.** Draibouth may terminate on 30 days' notice if Lebun is acquired by, or merges with, a provider of outsourced game testing, localization or player-support services without Draibouth's consent. Lebun will notify Draibouth of any signed change-of-control agreement within 15 days.

12.4 **Transition.** Draibouth will provide up to 90 days of transition assistance at order form rates.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 Draibouth will maintain commercial general liability ($2,000,000 per occurrence / $4,000,000 aggregate), technology errors and omissions and cyber ($5,000,000), workers' compensation and employer's liability ($1,000,000), and umbrella ($5,000,000) coverage, with Lebun as additional insured on the general liability policy.

## 14. LEGAL EFFECT

14.1 **Non-Binding.** This Term Sheet is a statement of intent only. It does not create a legally binding obligation for either Party, except under Sections 14.2 and 14.3, and no obligation to provide or purchase services will arise until the Definitive Agreements are signed.

14.2 **Confidentiality.** The existence and contents of this Term Sheet are confidential. Neither Party will disclose them except to its advisers who need to know them.

14.3 **Governing Law.** Sections 14.2 and 14.3 are binding and governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

14.4 **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs of negotiating the Definitive Agreements.

14.5 **Target Signing.** The Parties intend to sign the Definitive Agreements on or about January 15, 2025.
