# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Draibouth Studios LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of January 15, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider performs game quality assurance testing, localization and live-operations support services for Customer under Order Forms.

B. Section 10.1 of the Agreement requires the Parties to comply with this DPA to the extent Provider processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer in performing the Services, including in player support and live-operations Services.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. SCOPE AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Application.** This DPA applies to all Personal Data that Provider or its Sub-processors process on behalf of Customer or its Affiliates in connection with the Agreement or any Order Form.

1.2 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This DPA forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. Section 17.4 of the Agreement governs any conflict between this DPA and the other documents that make up the Parties' agreement.

1.3 **Affiliates.** Where an Affiliate of Customer signs an Order Form under Section 2.4 of the Agreement, this DPA applies to Personal Data processed under that Order Form, and references to "Customer" include that Affiliate.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Defined Terms.** In this DPA:

"**CCPA**" means the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations.

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the CCPA and other U.S. state consumer privacy laws, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, in each case as amended or replaced from time to time.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates, including players, community members and Customer personnel.

"**GDPR**" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to a Data Subject that Provider or its Sub-processors process on behalf of Customer or its Affiliates in connection with the Agreement, and includes "personal information" and "personal data" as defined in applicable Data Protection Laws.

"**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

"**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**processed**" have corresponding meanings.

"**Standard Contractual Clauses**" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, together with, where applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK Information Commissioner.

"**Sub-processor**" means any subcontractor or other third party that Provider engages to process Personal Data.

"**UK GDPR**" means the GDPR as it forms part of the law of the United Kingdom.

## 3. ROLES AND INSTRUCTIONS

3.1 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Customer is the controller (or "business") and Provider is the processor (or "service provider") of Personal Data.

3.2 **Instructions.** Provider shall process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. Those instructions consist of the Agreement, this DPA, each Order Form, and the scripts, policies and escalation procedures that Customer approves under Section 10.2 of the Agreement, together with any other written instructions Customer gives that are consistent with them. Provider shall promptly inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.

3.3 **Legally Required Processing.** If applicable law requires Provider to process Personal Data other than on Customer's instructions, Provider shall inform Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it.

3.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of the Personal Data it provides or makes available to Provider and for providing any notices and obtaining any consents that Data Protection Laws require for the processing described in Annex 1.

## 4. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Purpose Limitation.** Provider shall process Personal Data only to perform the Services. Provider shall not (a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined in the CCPA; (b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, including any commercial purpose; (c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the Parties; or (d) combine Personal Data with personal data it receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as Data Protection Laws permit for a service provider.

4.2 **Confidentiality of Personnel.** Provider shall ensure that every individual who processes Personal Data is subject to a written duty of confidentiality that meets Section 4.5 of the Agreement, has received appropriate data protection training, and has access only to the Personal Data needed to perform his or her duties.

4.3 **Children's Data.** Where a Customer Title is directed to children, or Customer notifies Provider that a Data Subject is a child, Provider shall process that child's Personal Data only as Customer's approved procedures permit and shall not request additional Personal Data from the child.

4.4 **CCPA Certification.** Provider certifies that it understands and will comply with the restrictions in Section 4.1. Provider shall notify Customer if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under the CCPA, and Customer may then take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized processing.

## 5. SECURITY

5.1 **Security Measures.** Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including the measures described in Annex 2. These measures are in addition to Provider's obligations under Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

5.2 **Review.** Provider shall review and test the effectiveness of its security measures at least once a year and shall not materially reduce the overall level of protection they provide during the term of this DPA.

## 6. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

6.1 **Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after Provider or any Sub-processor becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach. Where a Personal Data Breach also involves a pre-release Build, the shorter notice period in Section 3.4 of the Agreement applies to the Build.

6.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice must describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Provider shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.3 **Response.** Provider shall promptly take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Personal Data Breach and shall cooperate with Customer in its response. Provider shall not notify any regulator or Data Subject of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data without Customer's prior written approval, unless the law requires it.

## 7. SUB-PROCESSORS

7.1 **Approval.** Consistent with Section 3.6 of the Agreement, Provider shall not engage any Sub-processor without Customer's prior written approval, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold. The Sub-processors approved as of the DPA Effective Date are listed in Annex 3.

7.2 **Sub-processor Terms.** Before a Sub-processor processes Personal Data, Provider shall enter into a written agreement with it that imposes data protection obligations at least as protective as those in this DPA.

7.3 **Responsibility.** Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of each Sub-processor as if they were its own.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

8.1 **Transfer Locations.** Provider shall not process Personal Data, or permit it to be processed, outside the United States and the locations identified in Annex 1 without Customer's prior written approval.

8.2 **Transfer Mechanism.** To the extent Personal Data subject to the GDPR, the UK GDPR or Swiss data protection law is transferred to Provider or a Sub-processor in a country that does not provide an adequate level of protection, the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two, controller to processor, or Module Three, processor to processor, as applicable) are incorporated into this DPA by reference. For that purpose: (a) Customer is the data exporter and Provider is the data importer; (b) the optional docking clause in Clause 7 applies; (c) Option 2 of Clause 9 applies, subject to Section 7.1 of this DPA; (d) Clauses 17 and 18 select the law and courts of Ireland; and (e) Annexes 1, 2 and 3 of this DPA complete the corresponding annexes to the Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 9. ASSISTANCE

9.1 **Data Subject Requests.** Provider shall promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days, forward to Customer any request from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws that Provider receives, except player support inquiries that Customer's approved procedures authorize Provider to handle. Provider shall not respond to a rights request except as Customer instructs and shall give Customer reasonable assistance in responding.

9.2 **Assessments and Regulators.** Provider shall give Customer reasonable assistance with data protection impact assessments, prior consultations with regulators and responses to regulator inquiries relating to the processing under this DPA.

## 10. AUDIT

10.1 **Information.** Provider shall make available to Customer on request all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including copies of any current third-party security certifications or audit reports.

10.2 **Audits.** On fifteen (15) business days' prior written notice, and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period unless following a Personal Data Breach or a regulator's request, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA. Audits shall take place during business hours and in a manner that minimizes disruption to Provider's operations. Each Party shall bear its own costs of an audit, except that Provider shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs if the audit reveals a material non-compliance.

## 11. RETURN AND DELETION

11.1 **On Termination.** On termination or expiration of the applicable Order Form or the Agreement, or earlier on Customer's written request, Provider shall at Customer's election return or securely delete all Personal Data and certify in writing that it has done so, within thirty (30) days.

11.2 **Retention.** Provider may retain Personal Data to the extent applicable law requires or where it is held in routine backup systems until those backups are overwritten in the ordinary course. Retained Personal Data remains subject to this DPA and Section 9 of the Agreement, and Provider shall not process it for any other purpose.

## 12. LIABILITY

12.1 **Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to Sections 13 and 14 of the Agreement.

## 13. TERM

13.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date and remains in effect for as long as Provider or any Sub-processor processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer.

13.2 **Survival.** Sections 4.1, 6, 11 and 12 survive termination or expiration of this DPA to the extent Provider or any Sub-processor retains Personal Data.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment needed to comply with changes in Data Protection Laws.

14.2 **Notices.** Notices of Personal Data Breaches under Section 6 must be given by email and telephone to Customer's designated privacy contact, with a copy sent in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement. Other notices under this DPA must be given in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

14.3 **Governing Law.** Except as stated in Section 8.2 for the Standard Contractual Clauses, this DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this DPA.

14.4 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.

## ANNEX 1 – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

| **Item** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Performance of the Services under the Agreement and each Order Form |
| Duration | The term of the applicable Order Form, plus the period in Section 11 |
| Nature and purpose | Player support through ticketing, email and chat channels; community management and moderation; live-event monitoring; bug triage and escalation from live environments; and incidental processing of tester and Customer personnel data in quality assurance and localization Services |
| Categories of Data Subjects | Players and users of Customer Titles; members of Customer's official forums and community channels; Customer and Affiliate personnel |
| Categories of Personal Data | Names, usernames and gamertags; email addresses; account and platform identifiers; IP addresses and device identifiers; support ticket and chat content; gameplay, crash and telemetry data linked to accounts; moderation records; limited transaction references (no full payment card data) |
| Special categories | None intended. Provider shall not request special category data and shall handle any received in support communications as Customer's procedures direct |
| Processing locations | United States; Canada; Ireland |
| Frequency of transfer | Continuous, for the duration of the applicable Services |

## ANNEX 2 – TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

1. Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or equivalent).
2. Role-based access controls, unique user credentials and multi-factor authentication for all systems that hold Personal Data.
3. Access logging and quarterly review of access rights, with prompt revocation on reassignment or departure of personnel.
4. Access-controlled facilities, visitor logging and clean-desk policies at all locations where Personal Data is processed.
5. Restrictions on removable media, personal devices and screen capture at player support workstations.
6. Endpoint protection, vulnerability management and timely patching of systems.
7. Documented incident response procedures, tested at least once a year.
8. Business continuity and backup arrangements for systems that hold Personal Data.
9. Annual data protection and security training for all personnel who process Personal Data.
