# OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of February 2, 2024 (the "**Lease Date**") by and between **Calloway Ridge Properties LP**, a Texas limited partnership ("**Landlord**"), and **Lebun Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

1.1 **Summary.** The following basic lease information is part of this Lease. If it conflicts with any other provision of this Lease, the other provision controls.

| **Item** | **Term** |
|---|---|
| Building | Calloway Ridge Tower, 4100 Bluffstone Avenue, Austin, Texas 78746 |
| Premises | Suite 1200, comprising the entire twelfth (12th) floor of the Building |
| Rentable Area of Premises | 18,450 rentable square feet |
| Rentable Area of Building | 221,400 rentable square feet |
| Tenant's Share | 8.33% |
| Commencement Date | April 1, 2024 |
| Expiration Date | March 31, 2031 |
| Base Rent | As set out in Section 4.1 |
| Base Year for Operating Expenses | Calendar year 2024 |
| Security Deposit | $92,250 |
| Permitted Use | General office use, including video game development, design, testing and related administrative uses |
| Parking | 55 unreserved and 5 reserved spaces in the Building garage |

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used in this Lease have the meanings given in Section 1.1 or elsewhere in this Lease. "**Project**" means the Building, the land on which it stands, the parking garage and the common areas serving them.

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the Project, subject to the rules and regulations in Exhibit B.

2.2 **Condition of Premises.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises with Landlord's Work (as defined in Exhibit A) substantially complete and with the Building systems serving the Premises in good working order. Except for Landlord's Work, latent defects and Landlord's obligations under Section 8.1, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition.

2.3 **Measurement.** The rentable areas in Section 1.1 are stipulated and are not subject to remeasurement during the initial Term.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Term.** The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier or extended in accordance with this Lease.

3.2 **Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises beginning thirty (30) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, equipment, cabling and security systems, provided that Tenant has delivered the insurance certificates required by Section 10.3. Early access is subject to all terms of this Lease except the obligation to pay Base Rent and Operating Expense Rent.

3.3 **Option to Extend.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease, at Fair Market Rent. Tenant must exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given not less than nine (9) months and not more than fifteen (15) months before the Expiration Date, and Tenant must not then be in default beyond any applicable cure period. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing tenant would pay and a willing landlord would accept for comparable space in comparable buildings in the Austin, Texas central and southwest submarkets, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the Parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, it shall be determined by baseball arbitration by a single licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years of office leasing experience in Austin, Texas.

## 4. RENT

4.1 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay annual base rent ("**Base Rent**") in equal monthly installments, in advance, on the first day of each calendar month, as follows:

| **Period** | **Annual Rate per RSF** | **Monthly Installment** |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1 – 12 | $42.00 | $64,575.00 |
| Months 13 – 24 | $43.26 | $66,512.25 |
| Months 25 – 36 | $44.56 | $68,511.00 |
| Months 37 – 48 | $45.90 | $70,571.25 |
| Months 49 – 60 | $47.28 | $72,693.00 |
| Months 61 – 72 | $48.70 | $74,876.25 |
| Months 73 – 84 | $50.16 | $77,121.00 |

4.2 **Abatement.** Base Rent for months 2 through 5 of the Term is abated. Operating Expense Rent is not abated.

4.3 **Proration.** Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

4.4 **Payment.** Tenant shall pay all rent in U.S. dollars, without demand, deduction or set-off except as this Lease expressly provides, by electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing.

4.5 **Late Charge.** If Tenant does not pay any rent within five (5) days after Landlord's written notice that it is past due, Tenant shall pay a late charge of three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. Landlord is not required to give that notice more than twice in any twelve (12) month period.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

5.1 **Operating Expense Rent.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year ("**Operating Expense Rent**").

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable and customary costs of operating, maintaining, repairing, insuring and managing the Project, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures (except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Lease Date, amortized over their useful life), leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, debt service, ground rent, costs recovered from insurance or warranties, Landlord's income and franchise taxes, and management fees above three percent (3%) of gross Project revenues.

5.3 **Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means all real property taxes and assessments levied on the Project, including the Texas business margin tax to the extent attributable to the Project, and the reasonable costs of contesting them.

5.4 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied in any year, including the Base Year, variable Operating Expenses shall be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.5 **Controllable Expenses Cap.** Controllable Operating Expenses included in Operating Expense Rent shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis over the Base Year amount. Taxes, insurance, utilities, snow and ice removal and costs of complying with laws are not controllable.

5.6 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable estimate of Operating Expense Rent in monthly installments with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall give Tenant a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next rent due or, at the end of the Term, refunded within thirty (30) days. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receiving the statement.

5.7 **Audit.** Tenant may audit Landlord's records for any calendar year by written notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving the annual statement. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Operating Expense Rent by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit.

## 6. USE

6.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and in compliance with all applicable laws and the rules and regulations in Exhibit B.

6.2 **Access.** Tenant and its employees and invitees have access to the Premises, the Building and the parking garage twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to Landlord's reasonable security procedures.

6.3 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous materials onto the Project other than customary office and cleaning supplies and batteries for computer and gaming equipment, used and stored in compliance with law.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

7.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall provide heating, ventilation and air conditioning between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 9:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding Building holidays; electricity sufficient for normal office use; passenger elevator service at all times; janitorial service five (5) days a week; and water for restrooms and kitchens.

7.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside the hours in Section 7.1 on Tenant's request at Landlord's actual cost, currently $55 per hour per floor.

7.3 **Supplemental Cooling.** Tenant may install supplemental HVAC units to serve its server and device test rooms, connected to the Building condenser water loop, at Tenant's cost. Electricity for those units shall be separately metered and paid by Tenant at Landlord's actual cost without markup.

7.4 **Interruption.** If an interruption of essential services caused by Landlord's negligence or by matters within Landlord's reasonable control makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than three (3) consecutive business days, rent shall abate for the untenantable portion from the fourth (4th) business day until the services are restored.

7.5 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may select its own telecommunications and internet providers, and Landlord shall give them reasonable access to the Building risers and telecommunications rooms.

## 8. REPAIRS AND ALTERATIONS

8.1 **Landlord's Repairs.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the structure, roof, foundation, exterior walls, common areas and Building systems serving the Premises, in a manner consistent with comparable Class A office buildings in Austin, Texas. Costs are included in Operating Expenses to the extent Section 5.2 permits.

8.2 **Tenant's Repairs.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good condition, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall repair any damage caused by Tenant or its employees, contractors or invitees.

8.3 **Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, that cost less than $50,000 in any twelve (12) month period and do not affect the structure or Building systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord ten (10) days' prior notice.

8.4 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall remove its furniture, equipment and cabling. Tenant is not required to remove any alterations unless Landlord stated, when it consented to them, that removal would be required.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

9.1 **Consent Required.** Except as stated in Section 9.3, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to a request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving it, together with the information about the proposed transferee that Landlord reasonably requests.

9.2 **Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from a subtenant or assignee in excess of the rent payable under this Lease for the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer, including brokerage commissions, legal fees, improvement costs and free rent.

9.3 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on written notice to Landlord within thirty (30) days after the transfer, assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to (a) an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant; (b) a successor to Tenant by merger, consolidation or other reorganization; or (c) a purchaser of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided that, in the case of clauses (b) and (c), the transferee has a tangible net worth immediately after the transfer at least equal to Tenant's tangible net worth on the Lease Date. A transfer of Tenant's stock is not an assignment of this Lease.

9.4 **No Release.** Tenant remains liable under this Lease after any assignment or sublease, unless Landlord releases it in writing.

## 10. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

10.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain during the Term (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, with umbrella coverage of not less than $5,000,000; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and alterations at full replacement cost; and (c) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits.

10.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance covering the Building at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance on the Project with limits customary for comparable Class A office buildings in Austin, Texas.

10.3 **Certificates.** Tenant shall name Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds on its commercial general liability policy and shall deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord before entering the Premises and on each policy renewal.

10.4 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for damage to property to the extent covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance, and each party shall obtain a waiver of subrogation endorsement from its property insurer.

10.5 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 10.4, each party shall indemnify and defend the other against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage arising in the Premises (in Tenant's case) or the common areas (in Landlord's case), to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its agents, employees or contractors.

## 11. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

11.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant its contractor's estimate of the time needed to restore them. If the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate. Otherwise, Landlord shall restore the Premises and the Building with reasonable diligence. Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable until restoration is complete.

11.2 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that the remainder is not reasonably usable for the Permitted Use, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. Tenant may make a separate claim for its moving costs and its personal property.

## 12. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

12.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay rent within five (5) days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured in that period, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and complete it diligently; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

12.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may exercise any remedy available at law or in equity, including terminating this Lease or Tenant's right of possession. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages as required by Section 91.006 of the Texas Property Code.

12.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or such longer period as is reasonably needed if Landlord begins the cure within that period and completes it diligently, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law.

## 13. SECURITY DEPOSIT

13.1 **Deposit.** Tenant has delivered the Security Deposit to Landlord. Landlord may apply it to cure any Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore it within ten (10) business days after Landlord's notice of application.

13.2 **Reduction.** If no Event of Default has occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $46,125 on the first day of month 37 of the Term.

13.3 **Return.** Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, less amounts properly applied, within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 14. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

14.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any existing or future mortgage on the Project, provided that the mortgagee signs a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement agreeing not to disturb Tenant's possession while no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

14.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other's request, sign a certificate confirming the status of this Lease and any known defaults.

## 15. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

15.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, reasonable wear and tear, casualty and alterations that Tenant is not required to remove excepted.

15.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days of the holdover, and two hundred percent (200%) after that.

## 16. SIGNAGE AND PARKING

16.1 **Signage.** Landlord shall provide, at Landlord's cost, Building-standard signage for Tenant in the lobby directory and at the twelfth-floor elevator lobby. Tenant may install its logo in the elevator lobby, subject to Landlord's reasonable approval of the design.

16.2 **Parking.** Tenant may use the parking spaces stated in Section 1.1 at no charge for the first twenty-four (24) months of the Term, and afterwards at the prevailing monthly rates for the Building garage, not to exceed $150 per unreserved space and $225 per reserved space during the initial Term.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to Landlord at 4100 Bluffstone Avenue, Suite 150, Austin, Texas 78746, Attn: Asset Manager, and to Tenant at the Premises, Attn: General Counsel, or to any other address a party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt or refusal.

17.2 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

17.3 **Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Halberd Commercial Advisors (for Landlord) and Westlake Tenant Partners (for Tenant). Landlord shall pay both brokers' commissions under separate agreements.

17.4 **Quiet Enjoyment.** As long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

17.5 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Project, including rents, insurance proceeds and sale and condemnation proceeds.

17.6 **Consequential Damages.** Except under Section 15.2, neither party is liable to the other for consequential, special or punitive damages.

17.7 **Amendment.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

17.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

17.9 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and together they form one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A – WORK LETTER

A.1 **Landlord's Work.** Landlord shall, at its cost, deliver the Premises with (a) Building-standard restrooms on the twelfth floor in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act and the Texas Architectural Barriers Act; (b) Building systems distributed to the floor in good working order; and (c) demolition of the prior tenant's improvements completed ("**Landlord's Work**").

A.2 **Tenant Improvement Allowance.** Landlord shall provide an allowance of $75.00 per rentable square foot ($1,383,750) toward the cost of Tenant's initial improvements, including design, permitting, construction, cabling and security systems. Tenant may apply up to $10.00 per rentable square foot of any unused allowance to Base Rent first due after the Commencement Date. Landlord shall disburse the allowance monthly against contractor invoices and lien waivers. Landlord's construction management fee shall not exceed two percent (2%) of hard construction costs.

## EXHIBIT B – RULES AND REGULATIONS

B.1 Tenant shall not obstruct the sidewalks, entrances, corridors, elevators or stairways of the Building.

B.2 Tenant shall not install any additional locks on Building doors without giving Landlord a key or access credential, except for secure rooms that Tenant designates for confidential pre-release materials, for which Landlord's access shall be on twenty-four (24) hours' notice except in an emergency.

B.3 Deliveries of furniture and bulky equipment must be made through the freight elevator at times Landlord reasonably approves.

B.4 Smoking and vaping are prohibited throughout the Building and within twenty-five (25) feet of any Building entrance.

B.5 Landlord may amend these rules on reasonable notice, provided the amendments are applied in a non-discriminatory manner and do not materially interfere with Tenant's Permitted Use. If these rules conflict with the body of this Lease, the body of this Lease controls.
