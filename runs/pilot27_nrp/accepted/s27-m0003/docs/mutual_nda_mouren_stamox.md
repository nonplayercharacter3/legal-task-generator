# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of December 8, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and between:

- **Mouren Building Systems Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Mouren**"); and
- **Stamox Vertical Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Stamox**").

Mouren and Stamox are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Stamox owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Ascendra Lift Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Ascendra**"). Ascendra maintains, repairs and inspects elevators and escalators for commercial buildings in the Mid-Atlantic region of the United States.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Mouren would acquire Ascendra (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

C. In connection with the Proposed Transaction, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. The Parties are entering into this Agreement to protect that information.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Affiliate.** "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that person. For purposes of this Agreement, Ascendra is an Affiliate of Stamox.

**1.2 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Party or any of its Affiliates (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Proposed Transaction. It includes the following:

- (a) financial statements, projections, pricing and cost information;
- (b) customer lists, Service Contract terms, building portfolios and customer account histories;
- (c) maintenance control programs, maintenance logs, inspection reports, certificates of operation, citations and corrective-action records;
- (d) information concerning elevator contractor, mechanic and inspection licenses and the qualifying individuals for those licenses;
- (e) employee, labor and collective bargaining information;
- (f) incident, claim and litigation information, including information on entrapment, injury and property-damage claims;
- (g) software, source code, data models, fault-code libraries and technical documentation, including those relating to the LiftSense remote-monitoring software; and
- (h) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing.

The fact that the Parties are discussing the Proposed Transaction, and the terms of those discussions, are also Confidential Information of each Party.

**1.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

- (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;
- (b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
- (c) is or becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, is not bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or
- (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**1.4 Representatives.** "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, agents, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, actual or prospective financing sources, and insurance advisors.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

**2.1 Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and, if applicable, consummate the Proposed Transaction.

**2.2 Nondisclosure.** The Receiving Party shall hold the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in confidence. It shall not disclose that information to any person other than its Representatives who:

- (a) need to know the information for the purpose described in Section 2.1;
- (b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and
- (c) are directed to treat it in accordance with this Agreement, or are otherwise bound by professional or written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

**2.3 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.4 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**2.5 Source Code.** Source code and other technical materials relating to the LiftSense remote-monitoring software shall be made available only in a secure virtual data room or by supervised review. The Receiving Party shall not copy, download, decompile or reverse engineer those materials.

**2.6 Clean Team Information.** The Disclosing Party may designate certain competitively sensitive information, including customer-specific pricing and Service Contract renewal terms, as "**Clean Team Only**." Clean Team Only information may be disclosed only to the outside advisors and specifically identified employees of the Receiving Party listed on a clean-team roster agreed in writing by the Parties. Those individuals shall not have day-to-day responsibility for pricing or sales.

## 3. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or the rules of any securities exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted:

- (a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek, at its expense, a protective order or other appropriate remedy;
- (b) cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in seeking that remedy; and
- (c) disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for it.

## 4. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

**4.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten (10) business days after written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession. Upon request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing.

**4.2 Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention policies, or stored in automatic electronic backup systems. Retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained.

## 5. NON-SOLICITATION

**5.1 Employees.** For eighteen (18) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any officer, field supervisor or licensed elevator mechanic of the other Party or of Ascendra with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Proposed Transaction.

**5.2 Exceptions.** Section 5.1 does not prohibit:

- (a) general solicitations, including through advertisements or search firms, that are not specifically directed at those individuals; or
- (b) the hiring of any individual who responds to a general solicitation or whose employment with the other Party or Ascendra ended at least six (6) months before the first contact.

**5.3 Customers.** Mouren shall not use any Confidential Information to solicit any customer of Ascendra for elevator or escalator maintenance, repair or inspection services.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**6.1 No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to its accuracy or completeness. Only those representations and warranties made in a definitive written agreement for the Proposed Transaction, when, as and if executed, and subject to the limitations in it, shall have any legal effect.

**6.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Neither Party is obligated to pursue or consummate the Proposed Transaction unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice.

**6.3 No License.** No license or other right in any Confidential Information or intellectual property is granted under this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information under Section 2.1.

## 7. TERM

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years after the Effective Date.

**7.2 Trade Secrets.** The Receiving Party's obligations with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, including LiftSense source code, shall continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**8.2 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice.** Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.
