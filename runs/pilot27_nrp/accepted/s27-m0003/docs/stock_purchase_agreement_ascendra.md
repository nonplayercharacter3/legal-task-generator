# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of March 2, 2026. It is entered into by and between **Stamox Vertical Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Mouren Building Systems Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Ascendra Lift Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company maintains, repairs and inspects elevators and escalators for commercial buildings in the Mid-Atlantic region of the United States.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. At the Closing, the following will occur:

1. Seller will assign the LiftSense remote-monitoring software to the Company under the IP Assignment Agreement.
2. Stamox Fleet Services, LLC, an Affiliate of Seller, will convey certain field service vehicles and tooling to the Company under the Bill of Sale.
3. Buyer will issue the Seller Note to Seller.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; CLOSING

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer the Shares to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase the Shares from Seller. The Shares shall be free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests and other encumbrances. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment of Purchase Price.** Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows:

(a) **Cash.** Buyer shall pay $41,000,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, less the following amounts:
   - (i) Closing Indebtedness, which Buyer shall pay on behalf of the Company to the holders thereof in accordance with payoff letters delivered under Section 2.5(a)(iv); and
   - (ii) unpaid transaction expenses of the Company.

(b) **Seller Note.** Buyer shall issue to Seller the Seller Note in the principal amount of $7,500,000. The Seller Note shall mature 36 months after the Closing Date and shall be in the form attached as Exhibit A. The Seller Note is subject to Buyer's right of setoff under Section 9.8.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimate.* Not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Closing Working Capital**"). If it exceeds $3,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), the cash portion of the Purchase Price shall be increased by the excess. If it is less than the Target Working Capital, the cash portion shall be reduced by the shortfall.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver a statement of actual closing net working capital.

(c) *Disputes.* Disputes not resolved within thirty (30) days after Seller's notice of objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to the parties. That firm's determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting adjustment shall be paid in cash within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.4 Closing.**

(a) The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on September 30, 2026 (the "**Scheduled Closing Date**").

(b) If, on the Scheduled Closing Date, any condition in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing) has not been satisfied or waived, the Closing shall instead take place on the third (3rd) Business Day after that condition has been satisfied or waived.

(c) The date on which the Closing actually occurs is the "**Closing Date**." The parties acknowledge that the Scheduled Closing Date has been set to allow for the transfer or reissuance of the Contractor Licenses in accordance with Section 6.4.

**2.5 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

- (i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;
- (ii) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller and the Company;
- (iii) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Stamox Fleet Services, LLC;
- (iv) customary payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness;
- (v) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;
- (vi) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer of the transfer or reissuance of the Contractor Licenses;
- (vii) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying board resolutions authorizing this Agreement; and
- (viii) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

- (i) the cash payment required by Section 2.2(a);
- (ii) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;
- (iii) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and
- (iv) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing in any of the following ways:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after notice; or

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and there shall be no liability on the part of any party. However, the following shall survive termination:

- (b) Section 6.5, this Section 8.2 and Article XI.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until the date that is twelve (12) months after the date of this Agreement.

(b) The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(c) Covenants and agreements shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(d) No claim may be brought in respect of any representation or warranty after the expiration of its survival period, unless a Claim Notice for that claim has been delivered before expiration in accordance with Section 9.6. Any claim asserted in a timely Claim Notice shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from any of the following:

- (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article III or Article IV;
- (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;
- (c) any Closing Indebtedness or unpaid transaction expenses of the Company not deducted from the Purchase Price under Section 2.2(a); or
- (d) any taxes of the Company for any period ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from any of the following:

- (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or
- (b) the ownership or operation of the Company or its business after the Closing.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) *Third-Party Claims.* An indemnified party shall promptly notify the indemnifying party of any third-party claim. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of that claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

(b) *Settlement.* The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement:

- (i) involves only the payment of money; and
- (ii) includes a full release of the indemnified party.

(c) *Effect of Delay.* Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of Losses for which Seller shall be liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $4,850,000 (10% of the Purchase Price).

(b) *Deductible.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $242,500, and then only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(c) *Exclusions.* The limitations in Section 9.5(a) and Section 9.5(b) shall not apply to:

- (i) Losses arising from breach of any Fundamental Representation;
- (ii) Losses arising from fraud; or
- (iii) Losses arising under Section 9.3.

Seller's aggregate liability for Losses described in clauses (i) and (ii) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of the Losses.

(e) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**9.6 Claim Notices.**

(a) Any claim for indemnification shall be made by written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses.

(b) A Claim Notice in respect of any representation or warranty must be delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1.

(c) The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of a Claim Notice to dispute the claim in writing.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. This Section 9.7 does not limit:

- (a) claims for fraud;
- (b) claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.9; or
- (c) the purchase price adjustment procedures in Section 2.3.

**9.8 Right of Setoff.**

(a) Buyer may set off any amount to which it is entitled under this Article IX against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter payable under the Seller Note.

(b) For a claim that has been asserted in a Claim Notice but not finally resolved, Buyer may withhold from payments under the Seller Note an amount equal to Buyer's good-faith estimate of the Losses claimed, pending final resolution.

(c) Any amount withheld and later determined not to be owed shall be paid to Seller within five (5) Business Days after final resolution, together with interest at the rate provided in the Seller Note.

(d) The exercise of setoff in good faith shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.
