# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is dated as of September 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is made by **Stamox Fleet Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), in favor of **Ascendra Lift Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Stamox Vertical Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and Mouren Building Systems Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from Seller.

B. Transferor is an Affiliate of Seller. Transferor holds title to certain field service vehicles, hoisting equipment, test weights and tooling that the Company has used in its elevator and escalator maintenance, repair and inspection business.

C. Section 2.5(a)(iii) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale, duly executed by Transferor, at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Transferor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Fleet Assets.** "**Fleet Assets**" means all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the following:

- (a) the motor vehicles listed in Part 1 of Schedule A, together with all accessories, racking, shelving, ladders and upfit equipment installed in or on them (the "**Vehicles**");
- (b) the hoisting equipment, chain hoists, rigging, beam clamps and load-test equipment listed in Part 2 of Schedule A;
- (c) the certified test weights and weight carts listed in Part 3 of Schedule A;
- (d) the hand tools, power tools, diagnostic meters, controller programming devices and other tooling listed in Part 4 of Schedule A;
- (e) all spare parts and consumables carried in the Vehicles on the Effective Date; and
- (f) to the extent transferable, all manufacturer and vendor warranties, calibration certificates, load-test certificates, maintenance records and owner's manuals relating to any of the foregoing.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns forever, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the Fleet Assets. The Fleet Assets are conveyed free and clear of all liens, security interests, leases and other encumbrances.

**2.2 Consideration.** The conveyance in Section 2.1 is made as part of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement. No additional consideration is payable by the Company or Buyer under this Bill of Sale.

**2.3 Release of Liens.** On or before the Effective Date, Transferor shall deliver to the Company UCC-3 termination statements, lien releases and other instruments reasonably necessary to evidence the release of every lien or security interest on any Fleet Asset.

## 3. VEHICLE TITLES AND REGISTRATIONS

**3.1 Certificates of Title.** On the Effective Date, Transferor shall deliver to the Company the original certificate of title for each Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to the Company. Transferor shall also deliver any odometer disclosure statements and other documents required by the motor vehicle authority of the state in which the Vehicle is titled.

**3.2 Registration.** Transferor shall cooperate with the Company to retitle and reregister each Vehicle in the Company's name as promptly as practicable after the Effective Date. Until the reregistration of a Vehicle is complete, Transferor shall maintain that Vehicle's registration in effect and permit the Company to use the Vehicle in the ordinary course.

**3.3 Tolling Devices and Fuel Cards.** Transferor shall cancel all toll transponders, fuel cards and telematics subscriptions in Transferor's name associated with the Vehicles effective as of the Effective Date. Transferor shall remain responsible for all charges incurred under them before the Effective Date.

## 4. LIABILITIES

**4.1 No Assumption.** The Company does not assume, and nothing in this Bill of Sale shall be construed as an assumption by the Company of, any liability or obligation of Transferor or Seller of any kind.

**4.2 Pre-Effective Date Liabilities.** Transferor retains all liabilities arising out of the ownership, operation or use of the Fleet Assets before the Effective Date. These include liabilities for traffic citations, parking violations, motor vehicle accidents, property taxes and personal property taxes attributable to periods before the Effective Date.

## 5. RELATIONSHIP TO PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**5.1 Representations.** The representations and warranties concerning the Fleet Assets are those made by Seller in the Purchase Agreement, including Section 4.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Claims.** Any claim relating to the Fleet Assets or to this Bill of Sale shall be brought and resolved under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.3 Transfer Taxes.** Transfer, sales, use, titling and similar taxes and fees incurred in connection with the conveyance of the Fleet Assets shall be borne as provided in Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. FURTHER ASSURANCES; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**6.1 Further Assurances.** At the Company's request and without further consideration, Transferor shall execute and deliver further instruments of conveyance and take such other actions as are reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good title to the Fleet Assets.

**6.2 Power of Attorney.** Transferor irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to do the following in Transferor's name:

- (a) endorse and file certificates of title, registration applications and similar documents relating to the Vehicles; and
- (b) demand and receive any Fleet Asset that is not delivered on the Effective Date.

This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**6.3 Assets Held After the Effective Date.** If Transferor or any of its Affiliates holds or receives any Fleet Asset after the Effective Date, it shall hold that asset in trust for the Company. It shall deliver that asset to the Company promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Notices.** Notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Transferor shall be sent to Seller's address under that Section. Notices to the Company shall be sent to Buyer's address under that Section, with a copy addressed to the Company.

**7.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Transferor and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**7.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**7.4 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles. Each of Transferor and the Company irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Delaware Court of Chancery for any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, as provided in Section 11.8(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.5 Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each of Transferor and the Company irrevocably waives any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale.

**7.6 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.
