# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is dated as of September 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"). It is entered into by and among the following parties:

- **Stamox Vertical Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**");
- **Ascendra Lift Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"); and
- **Mouren Building Systems Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller, as seller, and Buyer, as buyer, are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from Seller.

B. Seller owns the LiftSense remote-monitoring and diagnostic software platform. The Company has used that platform in its elevator and escalator maintenance, repair and inspection business.

C. Section 2.5(a)(ii) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Agreement at the Closing. Under it, Seller assigns the LiftSense Software to the Company, and the Company grants Seller a license back for use in Seller's retained modernization and new-installation business.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below.

"**Assigned IP**" means all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the following, as further described in Schedule A:

- (a) the LiftSense Software;
- (b) all Intellectual Property Rights in the LiftSense Software;
- (c) all versions, releases, updates, modifications and derivative works of the LiftSense Software in existence on the Effective Date;
- (d) all data models, sensor-integration protocols, fault-code libraries and diagnostic algorithms embodied in or used with the LiftSense Software; and
- (e) all claims and causes of action relating to any of the foregoing, whether accruing before, on or after the Effective Date, including the right to sue for and collect damages for past, present and future infringement or misappropriation.

"**Improvements**" means any modification, enhancement or derivative work of the LiftSense Software made by or for Seller after the Effective Date in the exercise of the License.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all of the following rights, throughout the world:

- (a) copyrights and copyright registrations and applications;
- (b) patents, patent applications and inventions, whether or not patentable;
- (c) trade secrets, know-how and confidential information;
- (d) trademarks, service marks and trade names, including the LIFTSENSE mark, together with the goodwill associated with them; and
- (e) all other intellectual property and proprietary rights.

"**License**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Retained Business**" means the elevator and escalator modernization and new-installation business of Seller, as described in Section 6.6(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Source Materials**" means the source code, build scripts, technical documentation, design documents and developer notes for the LiftSense Software.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Seller irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to the Company, and the Company accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP. The Assigned IP is conveyed free and clear of all liens, security interests and other encumbrances, other than the License.

**2.2 Consideration.** The assignment in Section 2.1 is made as part of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement. No additional consideration is payable under this Agreement.

**2.3 Recordation.** Seller authorizes the Company to record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment in the form of Schedule B, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office and any other Governmental Authority.

**2.4 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by law, Seller waives, and shall cause its employees and contractors to waive, any moral rights in the Assigned IP.

## 3. DELIVERY; FURTHER ASSURANCES

**3.1 Delivery.** On the Effective Date, Seller shall deliver to the Company complete copies of the Source Materials and object code for the current production release of the LiftSense Software. Delivery shall be by secure electronic transfer or by transfer of administrative control of the code repositories listed in Schedule A.

**3.2 Retained Copies.** Seller may retain copies of the Source Materials and object code solely as necessary to exercise the License.

**3.3 Further Assurances.** At the Company's request and expense, Seller shall execute and deliver further instruments, and take such other actions, as are reasonably necessary to perfect, record or enforce the Company's rights in the Assigned IP.

**3.4 Transition Cooperation.** For ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, Seller shall make available to the Company, at no charge, up to two of its personnel familiar with the LiftSense Software. Those personnel shall answer reasonable questions concerning its architecture, deployment and maintenance.

## 4. LICENSE BACK

**4.1 Ownership.** As between the parties, the Company owns the Assigned IP. Except for the License, Seller retains no right, title or interest in the Assigned IP.

**4.2 License Grant.** Effective as of the Effective Date, the Company grants to Seller a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, fully paid-up license (the "**License**") to use, reproduce, modify, create derivative works of, and distribute in object-code form the LiftSense Software. The License may be exercised in the conduct of the Retained Business, including for the monitoring and diagnosis of elevators and escalators that Seller modernizes or installs.

**4.3 Sublicensing.** Seller may grant sublicenses under the License to its Affiliates, contractors and customers to the extent necessary for the conduct of the Retained Business. Seller shall remain responsible for each sublicensee's compliance with this Agreement.

**4.4 Improvements.** Seller shall own the Improvements, subject to the Company's ownership of the underlying Assigned IP. Seller shall have no obligation to deliver Improvements to the Company.

**4.5 Trademarks.** The License does not include any right to use the LIFTSENSE mark. Seller may, however, make factual reference to the LiftSense Software in technical documentation provided to customers of the Retained Business.

**4.6 No Support Obligation.** The Company has no obligation to provide maintenance, support, updates or hosting to Seller under the License.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY OF SOURCE MATERIALS

**5.1 Protection.** Seller shall protect the Source Materials it retains with at least the degree of care it uses for its own most sensitive source code, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**5.2 Disclosure.** Seller shall not disclose the Source Materials except to its employees, contractors and permitted sublicensees who need access to exercise the License and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 5.

**5.3 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Section 5 is in addition to Seller's obligations under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**6.1 Seller.** Seller represents and warrants to the Company and Buyer as follows:

- (a) Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP and has the right to assign it under this Agreement.
- (b) Seller has not granted any license, option or other right in the Assigned IP that remains in effect, other than non-exclusive licenses to the Company and to customers in the ordinary course.
- (c) Each current and former employee and contractor of Seller who contributed to the LiftSense Software has assigned his or her rights in that contribution to Seller under a written agreement.

**6.2 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** The representations in Section 6.1 are made in addition to those in Section 4.8 of the Purchase Agreement. Claims for breach of Section 6.1 shall be brought and resolved under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.3 Mutual.** Each party represents that it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is its legal, valid and binding obligation.

## 7. ENFORCEMENT

**7.1 Company Right to Enforce.** The Company has the sole right, but not the obligation, to enforce the Assigned IP against infringers and to retain all recoveries.

**7.2 Notice.** Seller shall promptly notify the Company of any infringement or misappropriation of the Assigned IP of which it becomes aware.

**7.3 Cooperation.** Seller shall provide reasonable cooperation in any enforcement action, at the Company's expense.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

**8.1 Assignment Irrevocable.** The assignment under Section 2.1 is irrevocable and is not subject to termination.

**8.2 License.** The License continues in perpetuity in accordance with Section 4.2.

**8.3 Survival.** Sections 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 survive any termination of the Purchase Agreement following the Closing.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be sent to Buyer's address under that Section, with a copy addressed to the Company.

**9.2 Assignment.**

(a) Seller may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, together with the License and its other rights and obligations under this Agreement, without the consent of any other party. Any such assignment may be made to any Person, including to any successor by merger, consolidation or other business combination, or to any purchaser of all or any portion of Seller's assets or equity.

(b) Neither Buyer nor the Company may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Seller.

(c) Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 9.2 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**9.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Delaware Court of Chancery for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, as provided in Section 11.8(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**9.6 Specific Performance.** Each party acknowledges that a breach of Sections 2, 3 or 5 would cause irreparable harm. The non-breaching party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**9.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably waives any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together shall constitute one instrument.
