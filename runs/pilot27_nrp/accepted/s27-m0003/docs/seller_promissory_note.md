# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$7,500,000**
**Dated: September 30, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Mouren Building Systems Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Stamox Vertical Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal sum of SEVEN MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($7,500,000). Maker shall also pay interest on the unpaid principal balance as provided in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Maker is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Ascendra Lift Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Interest Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December. The first Interest Payment Date is December 31, 2026.

"**Maturity Date**" has the meaning given in Section 2.

"**Senior Credit Agreement**" means any credit agreement, loan agreement or similar instrument under which Maker or any of its subsidiaries borrows money from one or more banks or other institutional lenders. It includes any refinancing or replacement of such an instrument.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" means all principal, interest, fees, expenses and other obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries owing under the Senior Credit Agreement.

"**Senior Lender**" means each lender, agent or other holder of Senior Indebtedness.

## 2. PRINCIPAL; MATURITY

The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment shall be made on the next succeeding Business Day.

## 3. INTEREST

**3.1 Rate.** The unpaid principal balance of this Note shall bear interest from the Closing Date until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum.

**3.2 Payment.** Accrued interest shall be payable in arrears on each Interest Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Computation.** Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**3.4 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default under Section 8.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid principal balance shall bear interest at a rate of two percent (2.0%) per annum above the rate in Section 3.1.

**3.5 Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to the reduction of principal.

## 4. PAYMENTS

**4.1 Manner.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Payment shall be made to the account Holder designates in writing to Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable payment date.

**4.2 Application.** Payments shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal.

**4.3 Withholding.** Maker may deduct and withhold from any payment the amounts it is required to deduct and withhold under applicable tax law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to Holder.

## 5. PREPAYMENT

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Partial prepayments shall be in a minimum amount of $250,000.

## 6. RIGHT OF SETOFF

**6.1 Setoff.** This Note is subject to Maker's right of setoff under Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement. Maker may set off any amount to which it is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter payable under this Note.

**6.2 Withholding Pending Resolution.** For a claim asserted in a Claim Notice but not finally resolved, Maker may withhold from payments under this Note an amount equal to Maker's good-faith estimate of the Losses claimed, pending final resolution.

**6.3 Release of Withheld Amounts.** Any amount withheld and later determined not to be owed shall be paid to Holder within five (5) Business Days after final resolution. Maker shall also pay interest on that amount, from the date it would otherwise have been paid, at the rate in Section 3.1.

**6.4 No Default.** The exercise of setoff or withholding in good faith under this Section 6 shall not constitute a default or an Event of Default under this Note.

## 7. SUBORDINATION

**7.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness.

**7.2 Payment Blockage.** If Maker receives written notice from a Senior Lender that a payment or financial covenant default has occurred and is continuing under the Senior Credit Agreement, then Maker shall make no payment of principal under this Note until the earlier of:

- (a) the cure or waiver of that default; and
- (b) the date that is one hundred eighty (180) days after delivery of that notice.

Regularly scheduled payments of interest may continue during any blockage period unless the Senior Indebtedness has been accelerated.

**7.3 Turnover.** Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 7 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lenders and paid over to them promptly upon demand.

**7.4 Subordination Agreement.** At the request of any Senior Lender, Holder shall execute a subordination agreement on customary terms consistent with this Section 7.

## 8. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

- (a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, other than by reason of setoff or withholding under Section 6 or a payment blockage under Section 7.2;
- (b) Maker commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;
- (c) an involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or
- (d) Maker sells all or substantially all of the Shares, or the Company sells all or substantially all of its assets, to a Person that is not an Affiliate of Maker, unless this Note is repaid in full at the closing of that sale.

**8.2 Notice.** Maker shall notify Holder in writing within five (5) Business Days after becoming aware of any Event of Default.

## 9. REMEDIES

**9.1 Acceleration.** Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 8.1(b) or Section 8.1(c), the entire unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest shall automatically become immediately due and payable.

**9.2 Optional Acceleration.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, Holder may declare the entire unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker.

**9.3 Subject to Subordination.** Holder's rights under this Section 9 are subject to Section 7.

**9.4 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 10. TRANSFER

**10.1 Restrictions.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker. However, Holder may transfer this Note to an Affiliate of Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note and the Purchase Agreement provisions applicable to it.

**10.2 Transferee Bound.** Any transferee of this Note takes it subject to Maker's rights of setoff and withholding under Section 6 and to Section 7.

**10.3 Securities Laws.** This Note has not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law. It may not be transferred except in compliance with those laws.

## 11. NOTICES

Notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Maker shall be sent to Buyer's address under that Section. Notices to Holder shall be sent to Seller's address under that Section.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** Each of Maker and Holder irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of New York sitting in the County of New York and the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Note. Each of Maker and Holder waives any objection to venue in those courts, including any objection based on inconvenient forum.

**12.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each of Maker and Holder irrevocably waives any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Note.

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS

**13.1 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest.

**13.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. No delay by Holder in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver of that right.

**13.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**13.4 Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and assigns. It inures to the benefit of Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

**13.5 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on identical terms. Holder must first deliver to Maker an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity, or in the case of mutilation, surrender the mutilated Note.

**13.6 Cancellation.** Upon payment in full of all amounts owing under this Note, Holder shall surrender this Note to Maker for cancellation.

**13.7 Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed by electronic signature, which shall have the same effect as an original signature.
