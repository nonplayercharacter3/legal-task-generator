# UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GLAROL RECYCLING TECHNOLOGIES, INC.

**Dated: August 27, 2026**

The undersigned are all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of **Glarol Recycling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Acting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and the Bylaws of the Company, they adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted at a duly called meeting of the Board.

## 1. Recitals

**A. The Acquisition.** On March 16, 2026, the Company, as buyer, entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with **Mourend Battery Materials LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company acquired the lithium-ion battery recycling business of Seller, including the black-mass processing facility at 2250 Cortez Industrial Way, Tilden, Nevada (the "**Facility**"). The Closing occurred on June 1, 2026, and the Company paid the Base Purchase Price of $46,500,000. Of that amount, $4,200,000 was deposited with Pellmont Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent, as the Indemnity Escrow Amount. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**B. Closing Statement and Adjustment Mechanism.** Section 2.5(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to prepare and deliver to the Company a Closing Statement setting forth Seller's calculation of the Closing Inventory Value and the resulting adjustment to the Base Purchase Price. Section 2.5(b) permits the Company to dispute the Closing Statement by delivering a Notice of Disagreement to Seller within 30 days after Seller delivers the Closing Statement. Section 2.5(c) provides for good-faith negotiation and, if needed, determination by an Independent Accountant.

**C. Receipt of the Closing Statement.** The Company's General Counsel has reported to the Board that Seller's Closing Statement, dated July 17, 2026, was received by email on July 20, 2026 at the Company's notice address, notices@glarol.com. The Closing Statement included supporting assay summaries and the metal pricing references Seller used. It reports a Closing Inventory Value that exceeds the Target Inventory Value set forth on Schedule 2.5 to the Purchase Agreement. On that basis, Seller asserts that the Company owes Seller an upward adjustment payment under Section 2.5(d).

**D. Management's Review.** The Company's Chief Financial Officer and its operations team, with the assistance of the Company's outside accounting advisors, have reviewed the Closing Statement. They have also reviewed the Company's own post-Closing sampling and assay of the black mass stockpiles at the Facility. Management has advised the Board that it disputes Seller's black mass inventory valuation, for three reasons:

(i) Seller's assays overstate the recoverable nickel and cobalt content of a significant portion of the black mass lots on hand at the Closing;

(ii) Seller applied metal pricing references for a period other than the one required by the Inventory Principles; and

(iii) Seller included in finished inventory certain partially processed material that does not meet the specifications under the Black Mass Offtake Agreement.

Management estimates that correcting these items would reduce the Closing Inventory Value by approximately $1,900,000 from the figure in the Closing Statement. As of the date of this consent, the Company has not delivered a Notice of Disagreement or any other written objection to the Closing Statement to Seller. The Company has not accepted the Closing Statement.

**E. Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the Closing Statement, management's written analysis dated August 24, 2026, and the related materials presented to it. The Board has determined that it is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders to dispute the Closing Statement as set forth below.

## 2. Resolutions

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT:

**Resolution 1. Dispute of the Closing Statement.** RESOLVED, that the Board approves the position of management that the Closing Statement overstates the Closing Inventory Value. The Company shall dispute the Closing Statement as it relates to the black mass inventory valuation and to each other item identified in management's analysis.

**Resolution 2. Delivery of Notice of Disagreement.** RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized and directed, acting alone, to prepare, execute and deliver to Seller on behalf of the Company a Notice of Disagreement under Section 2.5(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify each disputed item, including the black mass inventory valuation, and the Company's alternative calculation of the Closing Inventory Value. It shall be delivered promptly following the date of this consent by email to legalnotices@mourend.com and by nationally recognized overnight courier to Seller's notice address under Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**Resolution 3. Negotiation and Independent Accountant.** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to take the following actions on behalf of the Company:

(a) negotiate with Seller in good faith regarding the disputed items under Section 2.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) if any items remain unresolved, agree with Seller on the selection of an Independent Accountant, engage that firm and submit the unresolved items to it; and

(c) settle any disputed item, provided that any settlement that would result in a net payment by the Company to Seller under Section 2.5(d) of more than $250,000 shall require the further approval of the Board.

**Resolution 4. Engagement of Advisors.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to engage, or continue to engage, outside counsel, accounting advisors and independent assay laboratories as they consider necessary in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions. The Company shall pay their reasonable fees and expenses.

**Resolution 5. Reservation of Rights.** RESOLVED, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to reserve, in the Notice of Disagreement and in any related correspondence, all rights and remedies of the Company under the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Documents. This includes the Company's rights under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement in respect of the Inventory representations in Section 4.9.

**Resolution 6. General Authority.** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver all further documents, notices and instruments, and to take all further actions, as that officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions. That officer's execution or action is conclusive evidence of that determination.

**Resolution 7. Ratification.** RESOLVED, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer or representative of the Company in connection with the review of the Closing Statement and the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 3. Effectiveness; Counterparts

This consent is effective as of the date first written above upon execution by all members of the Board. It may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument. This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
