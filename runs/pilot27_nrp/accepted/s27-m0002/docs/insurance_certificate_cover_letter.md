## 3. Pollution Legal Liability Policy

The Pollution Legal Liability policy is written on a claims-made basis. Its three-year term matches the term the insurer quoted for this location, and it names the Facility as a scheduled location. Its terms and conditions, including the retroactive date, any exclusions for known conditions, and the reporting requirements, are set out in the policy itself. We will deliver the policy to you when the insurer issues it. Please send us any notice of a claim or pollution condition promptly so that we can report it to the insurer within the policy's reporting requirements.

## 4. Certificate Holder

At your request, the certificate names Glarol as certificate holder for its own records. If a lender, landlord, customer or other counterparty asks for a certificate naming it as holder or as additional insured, please send us the request together with the relevant contract language. We will then confirm what the policies permit and issue a separate certificate.

## 5. Nature of the Certificate

The certificate is issued as a matter of information only. It confers no rights on the certificate holder. It does not amend, extend or alter the coverage afforded by the policies listed. The policies, and not the certificate, govern the coverage, and each policy remains subject to all of its terms, exclusions and conditions. Limits shown may have been reduced by paid claims.

## 6. Cancellation and Renewal

If any listed policy is cancelled before its expiration date, the insurer will deliver notice in accordance with the policy provisions. We will diary each policy for renewal and will contact you at least ninety (90) days before expiration to begin the renewal process.
