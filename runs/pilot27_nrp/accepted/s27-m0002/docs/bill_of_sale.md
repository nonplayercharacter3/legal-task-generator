# BILL OF SALE

This **BILL OF SALE** (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of June 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**") by **Mourend Battery Materials LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and for the benefit of **Glarol Recycling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller agreed to sell, assign, transfer and deliver the Purchased Assets to Buyer at the Closing.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the payment of the Base Purchase Price under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance

Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer and its successors and assigns all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets. The Purchased Assets are conveyed free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens. They include, without limitation:

(a) all equipment, shredding and hydrometallurgical pre-treatment lines, discharge systems, dust collection and scrubbing systems, rolling stock, tooling and other tangible personal property used in the Business, located at or used in connection with the Facility at 2250 Cortez Industrial Way, Tilden, Nevada;

(b) all inventory, including black mass, feedstock and work in process;

(c) the Assigned Contracts listed on Annex A, subject to Section 4;

(d) to the extent transferable, the Hazardous Waste Permit and the other permits listed on Schedule 2.1(d) to the Purchase Agreement, subject to Section 7.6 of the Purchase Agreement;

(e) all intellectual property, process know-how, and books and records of the Business; and

(f) all goodwill of the Business.

## 3. Excluded Assets

The Excluded Assets described in Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement are not sold, assigned or transferred under this Bill of Sale. Seller retains them.

## 4. Non-Assignable Contracts

This Bill of Sale does not assign, and shall not be construed as an assignment or attempted assignment of, any Assigned Contract if a third party's consent to its assignment is required and that consent has not been obtained at or before the Closing. Each such Assigned Contract shall be governed by Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. No Assumption of Liabilities

This Bill of Sale conveys assets only. Buyer does not assume any liability of Seller under this Bill of Sale. The Assumed Liabilities are assumed only under the assignment and assumption agreement delivered under Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets in the name of Seller and for the benefit of Buyer, and to endorse in Seller's name any check or other instrument received in payment for any Purchased Asset. This power is coupled with an interest.

## 7. Further Assurances

At Buyer's reasonable request, Seller shall execute and deliver further instruments of transfer and take further actions to vest in Buyer title to the Purchased Assets, as provided in Section 7.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 8. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under the Purchase Agreement. It does not enlarge, reduce or otherwise modify any representation, warranty, covenant, indemnity or other right or obligation of Seller or Buyer under the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. Successors and Assigns

This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns, and benefits Buyer and its successors and assigns.

## 10. Governing Law

This Bill of Sale shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

## 11. Counterparts; Electronic Signature

This Bill of Sale may be executed and delivered by electronic signature and electronic transmission. Each is effective as delivery of an original.
