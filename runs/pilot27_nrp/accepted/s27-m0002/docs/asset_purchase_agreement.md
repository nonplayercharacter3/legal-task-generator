# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**"), dated as of March 16, 2026, is entered into by and between **Mourend Battery Materials LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Glarol Recycling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a lithium-ion battery recycling business. The business collects, discharges, shreds and processes end-of-life battery packs and cells into black mass at Seller's facility located in Tilden, Nevada (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings indicated:

"**Ancillary Documents**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Escrow Agreement, the Assignment and Assumption of Lease and each other agreement, instrument and certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Las Vegas, Nevada are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.4.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Closing Inventory Value**" means the value of the black mass, intermediate and finished inventory of the Business as of 12:01 a.m. Pacific time on the Closing Date. It is determined in accordance with the Inventory Principles set forth on Schedule 2.5.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all applicable federal, state and local laws, regulations, permits and orders relating to pollution, protection of the environment or natural resources, or the generation, handling, storage, treatment, transport, disposal or Release of Hazardous Materials. This includes the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act, and Chapters 444, 445A and 459 of the Nevada Revised Statutes.

"**Environmental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller set forth in Section 4.12.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pellmont Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the escrow agreement to be entered into at Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any earnings on it.

"**Facility**" means the black-mass processing facility, together with the related land, buildings and improvements, located at 2250 Cortez Industrial Way, Tilden, Nevada.

"**Facility Lease**" means the Industrial Lease Agreement under which Seller leases the Facility, as listed as item 1 on Schedule 2.1(c).

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties set forth in Sections 4.1 (Organization), 4.2 (Authority), 4.6 (Title to Purchased Assets) and 4.15 (Brokers).

"**Hazardous Materials**" means any substance, material or waste regulated under Environmental Laws. This includes lithium compounds, cobalt, nickel, manganese, electrolyte solvents, fluorinated compounds, heavy metals and petroleum products.

"**Hazardous Waste Permit**" means Nevada Hazardous Waste Management Facility Permit No. NEV-HW-0417, issued to Seller by the Nevada Division of Environmental Protection ("**NDEP**") for the Facility.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Permit Transfer Approval**" means the written approval by NDEP of the transfer of the Hazardous Waste Permit to Buyer, or the issuance to Buyer of a replacement permit on substantially equivalent terms.

"**Release**" means any release, spill, emission, leaking, discharge, disposal, leaching or migration into the environment.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all equipment, shredding and hydrometallurgical pre-treatment lines, discharge systems, dust collection and scrubbing systems, rolling stock, tooling and other tangible personal property used in the Business;

(b) all inventory, including black mass, feedstock and work in process;

(c) the contracts set forth on Schedule 2.1(c) (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) to the extent transferable, the Hazardous Waste Permit and the other permits listed on Schedule 2.1(d);

(e) all intellectual property, process know-how, and books and records of the Business; and

(f) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets; Assumed Liabilities.** The Purchased Assets exclude cash, Seller's organizational records, Tax refunds for pre-Closing periods and the assets listed on Schedule 2.2. Buyer assumes only those liabilities arising under the Assigned Contracts after the Closing, other than liabilities arising from any breach by Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"). Seller retains all other liabilities of the Business. This includes all liabilities arising under Environmental Laws from conditions existing at or before the Closing.

**2.3 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is Forty-Six Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($46,500,000) (the "**Base Purchase Price**"). It is subject to adjustment under Section 2.5 (as adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**"). At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Base Purchase Price as follows:

(a) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent under Section 2.4; and

(b) the balance to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit Four Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,200,000) (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX. On the first Business Day after the expiration of the general survival period under Section 9.1, the Escrow Agent shall release the remaining Escrow Fund to Seller, less the aggregate amount of all claims then pending under Claim Notices.

**2.5 Closing Statement; Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) Within forty-five (45) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good-faith calculation of the Closing Inventory Value and the resulting adjustment to the Base Purchase Price. Seller shall attach reasonable supporting detail, including assay results and the metal pricing references used.

(b) Buyer may dispute the Closing Statement by delivering to Seller a written notice (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within 30 days after Seller delivers the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item and Buyer's alternative calculation. During that period Seller shall give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the books, records, samples and personnel used in preparing the Closing Statement.

(c) If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within the period specified in Section 2.5(b), the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the parties. If Buyer timely delivers a Notice of Disagreement, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days. They shall then submit any items that remain in dispute to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, and shall resolve only the disputed items within the range of the parties' positions. Its determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The parties shall bear the fees of the Independent Accountant in inverse proportion to their relative success.

(d) If the Closing Inventory Value as finally determined exceeds the Target Inventory Value set forth on Schedule 2.5, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If it is less, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. In each case payment is due within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.6 Non-Assignable Contracts.** This Agreement shall not constitute an assignment of any Assigned Contract if an attempted assignment without the consent of a third party would constitute a breach of it, and that consent has not been obtained at or before the Closing. For ninety (90) days after the Closing, Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each such consent. Seller is not required to pay any consent fee or commence any litigation. During that ninety (90) day period, and until the consent is obtained, Seller shall hold the Assigned Contract for the benefit of Buyer. Seller shall also pass through to Buyer the economic benefits and burdens of the contract, to the extent permitted by the contract and applicable law. Once the consent is obtained, the Assigned Contract shall be deemed assigned to Buyer without further consideration. The failure to obtain any such consent shall not entitle Buyer to any adjustment of the Purchase Price.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VIII, other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing, or on another date the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**");

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities;

(c) an assignment and assumption of the Facility Lease;

(d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(e) the consents listed on Schedule 8.2(d);

(f) the certificate required by Section 8.2(c); and

(g) an IRS Form W-9.

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Section 2.3, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, counterparts of each Ancillary Document to which Buyer is party, and the certificate required by Section 8.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is party. This Agreement constitutes Seller's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents or any applicable law. Except as set forth in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, they do not require the consent of any person under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheet and income statement of the Business for fiscal year 2025, and the unaudited financial statements of the Business for the two months ended February 28, 2026, have been prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position of the Business as of their dates.

**4.5 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course, and there has not been any material adverse effect on the Business.

**4.6 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all Purchased Assets, free and clear of all liens other than Permitted Liens.

**4.7 Condition of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Business involving annual payments exceeding $250,000, including each feedstock supply agreement. Each Assigned Contract is valid and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.9 Inventory.** The inventory of the Business consists of quality usable and saleable in the ordinary course. It has been sampled and assayed in accordance with the Inventory Principles.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with applicable laws. Section 4.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all permits held for the Business, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.12 Environmental Matters.**

(a) Seller and the Business are, and for the past five (5) years have been, in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws, including the Hazardous Waste Permit.

(b) There has been no Release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Facility in violation of Environmental Laws, or in quantities or concentrations requiring investigation or remediation under Environmental Laws.

(c) Seller has not received any notice, demand, request for information or order from any governmental authority or other person alleging a violation of, or liability under, Environmental Laws relating to the Business or the Facility.

(d) There are no underground storage tanks, unlined surface impoundments, or disposal areas for battery electrolyte, process water or residues at the Facility.

(e) Seller has delivered to Buyer all environmental site assessments, sampling data, audits and reports in Seller's possession relating to the Facility.

**4.13 Employees.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all employees of the Business and their current compensation. Seller is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns relating to the Business and paid all Taxes shown as due. There are no liens for Taxes on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Liens.

**4.15 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE VII. COVENANTS

**7.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall maintain the Purchased Assets and the Hazardous Waste Permit, and shall not amend or terminate any Assigned Contract without Buyer's prior written consent.

**7.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Facility, books, records and personnel of the Business. Before the Closing, that access is subject to Section 7.6.

**7.3 Efforts; Third-Party Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VIII. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consents listed on Schedule 8.2(d) before the Closing.

**7.4 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to the employees of the Business listed on Schedule 7.4, on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to their current terms.

**7.5 Confidentiality; Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing, Seller shall not engage in lithium-ion battery recycling within the States of Nevada, California, Arizona, Utah or Oregon. Seller shall also keep confidential all information relating to the Business.

**7.6 Hazardous Waste Permit Transfer; Site Access.**

(a) Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the Permit Transfer Approval. Seller shall prepare and file all applications, Class 1 and Class 2 permit modification requests and financial assurance submissions required by NDEP. Seller may pursue the Permit Transfer Approval for up to twelve (12) months after the Closing Date. Until the Permit Transfer Approval is obtained, Seller shall remain the permittee of record under the Hazardous Waste Permit, and Buyer shall operate the Facility as Seller's designated operator under the transitional operating arrangement described on Schedule 7.6.

(b) Buyer shall cooperate with Seller's efforts under Section 7.6(a), including by posting replacement financial assurance on the Permit Transfer Approval.

(c) Before the Permit Transfer Approval is obtained, Buyer and its consultants shall not conduct any invasive or subsurface sampling at the Facility. This includes soil borings, groundwater monitoring well installation, sub-slab vapor sampling, and sampling of process sumps or containment areas. Buyer may, however, conduct visual inspections and non-invasive Phase I-type review. After the Permit Transfer Approval, Buyer may conduct sampling at the Facility in accordance with Environmental Laws and the Hazardous Waste Permit.

**7.7 Bulk Sales.** The parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales laws.

**7.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver any further instruments that the other party reasonably requests to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is nine (9) months following the Closing Date. This applies to the Environmental Representations. The Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.14 (Taxes) are excepted: they shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants in this Agreement shall survive in accordance with their terms. A claim asserted by a Claim Notice delivered under Section 9.4 before the end of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved. The parties intend the survival periods in this Section 9.1 to replace any otherwise applicable statute of limitations, in accordance with 10 Del. C. § 8106(c).

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against all Losses arising from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its affiliates against all Losses arising from any breach of Buyer's representations, warranties or covenants in this Agreement, or from any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Claim Procedures.** An indemnified party shall assert a claim by delivering written notice to the indemnifying party (a "**Claim Notice**"). Where the Escrow Fund is to be drawn upon, it shall also deliver the Claim Notice to the Escrow Agent. The Claim Notice shall describe the basis of the claim in reasonable detail, including the facts giving rise to it, the provisions of this Agreement under which it is made and, to the extent reasonably estimable, the amount of Losses claimed. The indemnifying party shall have thirty (30) days after receipt to object in writing. Any objection shall be resolved in accordance with this Agreement and, as to the Escrow Fund, the Escrow Agreement. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, until the aggregate Losses exceed $465,000. Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.

(b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, shall not exceed $6,975,000. Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The limitations in this Section 9.5 do not apply in the case of fraud.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Buyer Indemnitees shall first seek recovery of Losses under Section 9.2(a) from the Escrow Fund, to the extent of available funds. They may then recover directly from Seller, subject to Section 9.5.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.5, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties after the Closing for any claim arising under this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** The parties shall treat any indemnification payment as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by July 15, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach did not cause the failure; or

(c) by either party, if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall be void, except for this Section 10.2 and Article XI. No party shall have liability except for willful breach before termination.
