# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This **ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of June 1, 2026, by and among **Glarol Recycling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Mourend Battery Materials LLC**, a Nevada limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pellmont Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring substantially all of the assets of Seller's lithium-ion battery recycling business, including the black-mass processing facility located at 2250 Cortez Industrial Way, Tilden, Nevada.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit Four Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,200,000) with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The deposit secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to receive, hold, invest and disburse that deposit on the terms of this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent is willing to serve on those terms.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Escrow Agreement, the following terms have the meanings indicated:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York or Las Vegas, Nevada are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" means a written notice of a claim delivered by Buyer under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 5.1 of this Escrow Agreement.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, less any disbursements made under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction. The Party delivering it shall deliver it to the Escrow Agent with a certificate stating that the order is final and non-appealable. The Escrow Agent may rely on that certificate without further inquiry.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means Four Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,200,000).

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means a written instruction to the Escrow Agent signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Release Date**" means the first Business Day after the date that is nine (9) months following the Closing Date.

"**Authorized Representative**" means, for each Party, the persons listed on Schedule B, as updated from time to time by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of, and has no duties under, the Purchase Agreement. Its definitions are used only for convenience of reference.

## 2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**2.2 Know-Your-Customer.** Each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests to comply with the USA PATRIOT Act, applicable anti-money laundering laws and its customer identification procedures. The Escrow Agent need not accept the Indemnity Escrow Amount until it has received that information.

## 3. Deposit of Escrow Funds

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated on Schedule A.

**3.2 Separate Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent. The Escrow Fund shall not be commingled with the Escrow Agent's own funds. The Escrow Fund is not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party, except as provided in this Escrow Agreement.

**3.3 Acknowledgment.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Indemnity Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing promptly after receipt.

## 4. Investment of Escrow Fund

**4.1 Permitted Investment.** Unless otherwise directed by Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the Pellmont Government Obligations Money Market Fund, or a successor fund investing only in direct obligations of the United States government. The Parties may by Joint Written Instructions direct the Escrow Agent to invest in another investment acceptable to the Escrow Agent.

**4.2 No Liability for Investment Losses.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 4. It is also not liable for any failure to achieve a particular rate of return, or for any loss resulting from the sale of an investment to make a required disbursement.

**4.3 Earnings.** All earnings on the Escrow Fund form part of the Escrow Fund. They shall be disbursed together with the principal in accordance with Section 6.

## 5. Claims Against the Escrow Fund

**5.1 Delivery of Claim Notice.** If Buyer asserts a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement against the Escrow Fund, Buyer shall deliver a Claim Notice to Seller and to the Escrow Agent on or before the Release Date. The Claim Notice shall state the amount claimed, or a good-faith estimate of it (the "**Claimed Amount**"). It shall also describe the basis of the claim in reasonable detail, as required by Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent's sole obligation regarding the content of a Claim Notice is to confirm that it states a Claimed Amount.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a Claimed Amount by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after Seller receives the Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"). The objection shall state the portion of the Claimed Amount disputed (the "**Disputed Amount**") and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

**5.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent receives no objection within the Objection Period, it shall disburse the full Claimed Amount to Buyer from the Escrow Fund on the third (3rd) Business Day after the Objection Period ends. If Seller objects to only part of a Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed portion to Buyer on the same timetable.

**5.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any Disputed Amount in the Escrow Fund. It shall disburse the Disputed Amount only in accordance with:

(a) Joint Written Instructions; or

(b) a Final Order, delivered by either Party to the Escrow Agent and the other Party.

**5.5 Escrow Fund Insufficient.** If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to pay a Claimed Amount in full, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the entire remaining Escrow Fund. It shall have no further obligation regarding that claim. Buyer's right to recover any balance directly from Seller is governed by the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Release of the Escrow Fund

**6.1 Release on the Release Date.** On the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release the remaining Escrow Fund to Seller, less the aggregate amount of all Claimed Amounts then pending under Claim Notices delivered on or before the Release Date. A Claimed Amount is pending until it has been disbursed or released under Section 5 or this Section 6.

**6.2 Release of Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amounts retained under Section 6.1 until each pending claim is resolved by Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. When a pending claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount payable to Buyer, if any. It shall release to Seller any amount retained for that claim that is not payable to Buyer.

**6.3 Joint Written Instructions.** At any time, the Escrow Agent shall disburse all or any part of the Escrow Fund in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**6.4 Method of Disbursement.** The Escrow Agent shall make all disbursements by wire transfer to the accounts set forth on Schedule B, or to another account a Party designates by written notice. A Party may change its account only by a written notice signed by its Authorized Representative and confirmed by telephone call-back under Section 6.5.

**6.5 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any instruction to disburse funds, or on any change in wire instructions, the Escrow Agent may confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the instructing Party, at a number listed on Schedule B. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until it completes that confirmation, and it is not liable for that delay.

## 7. Duties of the Escrow Agent

**7.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial. They are limited to those expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement, and no implied duties or fiduciary obligations shall be read into it.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely, and is protected in acting or refraining from acting, on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature it reasonably believes to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper person. It need not investigate any fact or matter stated in any such document.

**7.3 Advice of Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**7.4 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands regarding the Escrow Fund, or is in reasonable doubt as to any action to be taken, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an action in interpleader in a court specified in Section 12.1 and deposit the Escrow Fund with that court. Upon that deposit, the Escrow Agent is released from all further obligations regarding the deposited funds.

**7.5 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent of its own gross negligence or willful misconduct, as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, even if advised of their possibility.

**7.6 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Those events include acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, interruption of communications or computer systems, and failure of the Federal Reserve wire system.

## 8. Fees; Indemnification of the Escrow Agent

**8.1 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule C, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable counsel fees. Buyer and Seller shall each pay fifty percent (50%) of those fees and expenses. As between Buyer and Seller, a Party that pays more than its share may recover the excess from the other Party.

**8.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. This does not apply to the extent those losses arise from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct, as determined by a Final Order. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear fifty percent (50%) of any amount paid under this Section 8.2, unless the losses arose from the acts or omissions of one Party, in which case that Party shall bear them.

**8.3 No Set-Off Against Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Agent shall not set off its fees or any indemnification amount against the Escrow Fund, except for amounts that remain unpaid more than sixty (60) days after the Escrow Agent invoices them.

**8.4 Survival.** This Section 8 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. Tax Matters

**9.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Fund until disbursement.

**9.2 Tax Forms.** On or before the Closing Date, each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9. The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file all required information returns, including IRS Form 1099, regarding income earned on the Escrow Fund.

**9.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any taxes it is required by law to withhold. It shall remit those taxes to the appropriate taxing authority.

## 10. Resignation and Removal of the Escrow Agent

**10.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Parties.

**10.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Escrow Agent.

**10.3 Successor.** Within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. If they do not appoint a successor within that period, the Escrow Agent may petition a court specified in Section 12.1 to appoint one. Upon the successor's written acceptance, the Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Fund to the successor and shall be discharged from all further duties. Until then, the Escrow Agent's sole duty is to hold the Escrow Fund safely.

## 11. Termination

This Escrow Agreement terminates when the entire Escrow Fund has been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 7, 8 and 12 survive termination.
