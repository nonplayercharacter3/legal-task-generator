# BILL OF SALE

## (Lagoon Assets)

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of April 30, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), by **Brilal Foods Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Sunfold Creamery, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Ratel Dairy Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company.

B. Seller holds title to the wastewater lagoon system serving the Company's plant in St. Albans, Vermont (the "**St. Albans Plant**"). Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to convey the Lagoon Assets to the Company at the Closing under this Bill of Sale, free and clear of all Liens.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Conveyed Assets**" means the Lagoon Assets, including the assets described in Schedule 1, together with all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to (a) all manufacturer and contractor warranties relating to the Lagoon Assets, to the extent transferable, and (b) all as-built drawings, engineering reports, operating and maintenance manuals, inspection records, sampling and monitoring data and other books and records relating to the Lagoon Assets.

"**Effective Time**" means 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Bill of Sale unless otherwise stated.

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Transfer of Title.** Effective as of the Effective Time, Seller hereby sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company, and its successors and assigns forever, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Conveyed Assets, free and clear of all Liens.

**2.2 Possession.** Possession of the Conveyed Assets is delivered to the Company at the Effective Time at their present location at the St. Albans Plant. The Conveyed Assets shall remain in place, and Seller shall not remove or disconnect any Conveyed Asset.

**2.3 Records.** Seller shall deliver to the Company, within ten Business Days after the Closing Date, all books and records included in the Conveyed Assets that are in Seller's possession and not already located at the St. Albans Plant. Seller may retain copies of those records to the extent required by Law or for purposes of Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. TITLE; WARRANTIES

**3.1 Title.** Seller represents and warrants to the Company that Seller is the sole owner of, and has good and marketable title to, the Conveyed Assets, free and clear of all Liens, and has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to convey the Conveyed Assets to the Company.

**3.2 Warranty of Title.** Seller warrants and shall defend title to the Conveyed Assets unto the Company, and its successors and assigns, against the lawful claims of all Persons claiming by, through or under Seller.

**3.3 Purchase Agreement Representations.** Except as set forth in Section 3.1 and in the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV of the Purchase Agreement, Seller makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, concerning the Conveyed Assets, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 4. CLAIMS

**4.1 Recovery.** All Losses arising out of or relating to the Conveyed Assets or the underlying real property on which the Conveyed Assets are located, including any Environmental Liability, shall be recoverable by the Company, Buyer and the other Buyer Indemnitees solely under, and in accordance with the procedures of, Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, and subject to the Cap.

**4.2 No Duplication.** No Buyer Indemnitee shall be entitled to recover more than once in respect of the same Loss under this Bill of Sale and the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**5.1 Further Instruments.** At the Company's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take such other actions, as the Company may reasonably request to vest in the Company good title to the Conveyed Assets and to put the Company in actual possession and operating control of them.

**5.2 Permits.** Seller shall cooperate reasonably with the Company in connection with any filing with, or notice to, the Vermont Agency of Natural Resources required in respect of the Conveyed Assets or the St. Albans Plant wastewater discharge permit, including the notice listed as item 3 on Schedule 4.5 to the Purchase Agreement.

**5.3 Power of Attorney.** Seller hereby irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive any of the Conveyed Assets and to give receipts and releases for them in the name of Seller. This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale shall be binding upon Seller and its successors and assigns and shall inure to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**6.2 Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company.

**6.3 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction, except to the extent the Laws of the State of Vermont mandatorily govern the transfer of title to personal property located in Vermont.

**6.4 Jurisdiction.** Section 12.11 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, mutatis mutandis.

**6.5 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed and accepted in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## SCHEDULE 1: LAGOON ASSETS

The wastewater lagoon system serving the St. Albans Plant, St. Albans, Vermont, including:

1. All lagoon cells, including the primary treatment cell and the secondary storage cell, together with their berms, embankments, inlet and outlet structures and spillways.
2. All lagoon liners, leak detection systems and related geotextile and drainage layers.
3. All surface and submerged aerators, blowers, diffusers, mixers and related electrical controls and panels.
4. All influent, transfer, recirculation and effluent pumps, lift stations, wet wells and related motors and controls.
5. All process wastewater piping, force mains, valves, flow meters and manholes running from the St. Albans Plant to the lagoon cells and from the lagoon cells to the permitted discharge or land-application points.
6. All groundwater monitoring wells, piezometers, sampling ports and related monitoring equipment.
7. All spare parts, tools, sampling equipment and related equipment used exclusively in connection with the foregoing.
