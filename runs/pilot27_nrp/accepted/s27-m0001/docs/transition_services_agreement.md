# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Brilal Foods Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**" or "**Seller**"), and **Sunfold Creamery, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"). Provider and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Ratel Dairy Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company.

B. Before the Closing, Seller and its Affiliates provided laboratory testing, organic certification compliance support and cold-chain logistics services to the Company. Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that it continues to provide those services to the Company for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Closing Date is April 30, 2026.

"**Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Provider Personnel**" means the employees, agents and subcontractors of Provider and its Affiliates who perform the Services.

"**Service Period**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Service Standard**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as that schedule may be amended under Section 2.3.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to the Company in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A. The Services consist of:

(a) **Laboratory Testing Services**: microbiological, compositional, antibiotic residue and somatic cell testing of incoming raw milk and finished products, performed at Provider's laboratory in Burlington, Vermont;

(b) **Organic Certification Compliance Support**: maintenance of the Company's organic system plan, preparation for and attendance at inspections by the Company's USDA-accredited organic certifying agent, supplier organic certificate tracking, and recordkeeping required under the USDA National Organic Program; and

(c) **Cold-Chain Logistics Services**: refrigerated transport of finished products from the St. Albans Plant to customer distribution centers, route scheduling, temperature monitoring and carrier management, using Provider's fleet and contracted carriers.

**2.2 Scope and Volume.** Except as set forth in Schedule A, Provider shall provide the Services in substantially the same manner, scope, volume and priority as provided to the Company during the 12 months before the Closing Date. Provider is not required to provide any service that is not described in Schedule A.

**2.3 Additional Services.** If the Company identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company during the 12 months before the Closing Date and that is not included in Schedule A, the Company may request that Provider provide that service. Provider shall consider the request in good faith. Any agreed additional service, together with its fee, shall be set out in a written amendment to Schedule A signed by both Parties.

**2.4 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform the Services, provided that Provider remains responsible for the performance of the Services by its subcontractors as if Provider had performed them.

**2.5 Third-Party Consents.** If any consent of a third party is required for Provider to provide any Service, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent. The Company shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Service Period.** Provider shall provide each Service from the Closing Date until 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Service Period**"), unless that Service is terminated earlier under Section 9.

**3.2 Extension.** The Company may, by written notice delivered at least 60 days before the end of the Service Period, request an extension of the Service Period for any Service for up to three additional months. Provider shall not unreasonably withhold its agreement to the extension. During any extension the Fees for the extended Service shall increase by 5%.

**3.3 Term of Agreement.** This Agreement shall remain in effect until the expiration or termination of all Services, except as provided in Section 9.4.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** The Company shall pay Provider the fees for each Service set forth in Schedule A (the "**Fees**"). The Fees reflect Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the Services, without markup. Provider shall be reimbursed for reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in providing the Services if those expenses are of a type set forth in Schedule A or approved in advance in writing by the Company.

**4.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Services provided and the expenses incurred during the preceding month. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt.

**4.3 Disputed Amounts.** If the Company disputes any invoiced amount in good faith, it shall notify Provider in writing within 15 days after receipt of the invoice and pay the undisputed portion when due. The Service Coordinators shall attempt to resolve the dispute under Section 10.1.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Fees do not include sales, use or similar Taxes, which shall be borne by the Company. Each Party shall bear its own income Taxes.

## 5. STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

**5.1 Service Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) with at least the same degree of care, skill, diligence and priority as it exercised in providing the same services to the Company during the 12 months before the Closing Date, (b) in compliance with applicable Law, including the USDA National Organic Program regulations, the Pasteurized Milk Ordinance, the Food Safety Modernization Act and the Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule, and (c) in accordance with the performance levels set forth in Schedule A (together, the "**Service Standard**").

**5.2 Laboratory Accreditation.** Provider shall maintain throughout the Service Period all accreditations and certifications of its Burlington laboratory that are required for the Laboratory Testing Services, including its certification under the Vermont Agency of Agriculture, Food and Markets laboratory program.

**5.3 Cold-Chain Requirements.** Provider shall maintain product temperatures at or below 41°F throughout transport and shall provide the Company with temperature logs for each shipment within two Business Days after delivery.

**5.4 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO REPRESENTATIONS OR WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, CONCERNING THE SERVICES, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 6. COOPERATION; RECORDS

**6.1 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall designate a service coordinator (each, a "**Service Coordinator**") who will be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. The Service Coordinators shall meet at least monthly during the Service Period to review performance of the Services.

**6.2 Company Cooperation.** The Company shall provide Provider with information, access to the St. Albans Plant and samples reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services, including raw milk samples, supplier organic certificates, production schedules, customer order data and delivery requirements.

**6.3 Records and Reports.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services, including test results, chain-of-custody documentation, certification compliance files and shipment records. Provider shall deliver copies of those records to the Company promptly on request and, in any event, at the end of the Service Period.

**6.4 Migration Assistance.** During the last 90 days of the Service Period, Provider shall provide the Company with reasonable assistance in transitioning each Service to the Company or to a replacement provider, including the transfer of test methods, organic system plan documentation and carrier contact information.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement, in written, electronic or other tangible form, that is marked as confidential at the time of disclosure. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party, (b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, (c) is received by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction, or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**7.2 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, (b) not disclose that Confidential Information to any Person other than its and its Affiliates' employees, agents and subcontractors who need to know it for those purposes and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 7, and (c) protect that Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**7.3 Information Received in Performing the Services.** Provider's obligations with respect to information of the Company received by Provider or Provider Personnel in performing the Services are governed exclusively by this Section 7.

**7.4 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by Law to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, to the extent legally permitted, and cooperate reasonably with any effort of the Disclosing Party to obtain a protective order. The Receiving Party may disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information it is legally required to disclose.

**7.5 Return or Destruction.** Within 30 days after the end of the Service Period, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its possession, except for copies retained in automatic electronic backups or as required by Law. Retained copies remain subject to this Section 7.

**7.6 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 7 continue for three years after the end of the Service Period.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Limitation of Liability.** The aggregate liability of Provider under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any consequential, incidental, special, punitive or exemplary damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement. The limitations in this Section 8.1 do not apply to (a) a Party's breach of Section 7, (b) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, or (c) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 8.2 or Section 8.3 for third-party claims.

**8.2 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Company and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent resulting from (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement, (b) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or Provider Personnel in performing the Services, or (c) any product spoilage, contamination or recall caused by Provider's failure to meet the Service Standard.

**8.3 Indemnification by the Company.** The Company shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent resulting from the Company's breach of this Agreement or the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Company in connection with its receipt of the Services.

**8.4 Procedures.** The procedures in Section 9.4(a) and Section 9.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement apply to claims for indemnification under this Section 8.

## 9. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination by the Company for Convenience.** The Company may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on 30 days' written notice to Provider. The Fees for any terminated Service shall cease as of the effective date of termination. Termination of a Service shall not affect any other Service, except where Schedule A identifies Services as dependent on each other.

**9.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any affected Service by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail. Provider may terminate under this Section 9.2 for nonpayment only if the unpaid amount is undisputed and remains unpaid 30 days after the notice.

**9.3 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**9.4 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, the Company shall pay all Fees and expenses accrued for Services performed before the effective date. Sections 4, 6.3, 7, 8, 9.4, 10 and 12 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**10.1 Escalation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through their Service Coordinators. If the Service Coordinators do not resolve the dispute within 15 days, either Party may refer it to a senior executive of each Party. The executives shall meet within ten Business Days after the referral.

**10.2 Continued Performance.** Pending resolution of any dispute, Provider shall continue to perform the Services, and the Company shall continue to pay undisputed Fees, in accordance with this Agreement.

**10.3 Litigation.** If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days after referral under Section 10.1, either Party may bring an action in accordance with Section 12.6.

## 11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR; PERSONNEL

**11.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

**11.2 Personnel.** Provider Personnel remain employees or contractors of Provider or its Affiliates. Provider is solely responsible for their compensation, benefits, withholding and supervision. While at the St. Albans Plant, Provider Personnel shall comply with the Company's food safety, sanitation, biosecurity and site safety rules made known to Provider.

**11.3 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure to perform under this Agreement (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, severe weather, epidemic, animal disease outbreak, labor disputes not involving its own employees, or acts of a Governmental Authority. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents performance of any Service for more than 30 days, the Company may terminate that Service on written notice.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a Party designates by notice). Copies of notices to the Company shall also be delivered to Buyer.

**12.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**12.3 Assignment.** Assignment of this Agreement and of any rights or obligations under it is governed by Section 12.4 of the Purchase Agreement, mutatis mutandis, with Provider in the position of Seller and the Company in the position of Buyer. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their successors and permitted assigns. Nothing in this Agreement confers any right or remedy on any other Person, except the indemnified Persons under Section 8.

**12.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principle that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction.

**12.6 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**12.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**12.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
