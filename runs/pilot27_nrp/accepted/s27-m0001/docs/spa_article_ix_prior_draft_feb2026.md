## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The following exceptions apply:

(i) the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after the applicable statute of limitations expires;

(ii) the representations and warranties in Section 4.8 (Environmental Matters) survive until five years after the Closing Date; and

(iii) the representations and warranties in Section 4.13 (Taxes) survive until 60 days after the applicable statute of limitations expires.

(c) Claims under Section 9.2(c) may be made until seven years after the Closing Date.

(d) Covenants and agreements survive in accordance with their terms.

(e) No claim may be made after the applicable survival period ends. However, any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before that period expires survives until it is finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article IX, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate Seller delivers under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) the Specified Environmental Matter, including:

(i) all investigation, remediation, monitoring and closure costs; and

(ii) all third-party claims and governmental penalties relating to it; and

(d) any of the following:

(i) any indebtedness of the Company outstanding at the Closing and not repaid at the Closing; and

(ii) any Taxes of the Company attributable to periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article IX, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(b) any payment or performance by Seller or any of its Affiliates under a Company Guaranty, to the extent the payment or performance relates to an obligation of the Company that first arises and is attributable to the period after the Closing.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) **Notice of Claims.** A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing the claim in reasonable detail. A failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the Indemnifying Party is materially prejudiced by that failure.

(b) **Third-Party Claims.**

(i) The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim by written notice within 20 days after receiving notice of the claim. Defense counsel must be reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party.

(ii) If the Indemnifying Party assumes the defense, the Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense.

(iii) The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, unless the settlement:

(A) involves only the payment of money;

(B) is fully paid by the Indemnifying Party; and

(C) includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

(c) **Specified Environmental Matter.** Seller shall control the investigation and remediation of the Specified Environmental Matter, in consultation with Buyer. Seller shall use qualified environmental consultants and shall carry out that work so as not to unreasonably interfere with operations at the St. Albans Plant.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) **Deductible and Cap.**

(i) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceed 0.75% of the Purchase Price (the "**Deductible**"). After that, Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(ii) Seller is not liable for Losses under Section 9.2(a) in excess of 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Cap**").

(iii) Buyer is not liable under Section 9.3 for Losses in excess of the Cap.

(b) **Exclusions from the Deductible and Cap.** The Deductible and the Cap do not apply to Losses arising out of:

(i) any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or of the representations and warranties in Section 4.13;

(ii) fraud; or

(iii) the Specified Environmental Matter, whether claimed under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.2(c).

Seller's aggregate liability for Losses described in clause (i) of this Section 9.5(b) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) **Insurance.** Losses are calculated net of any insurance proceeds the Indemnified Party actually receives for the Loss, less the costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

(d) **Punitive Damages.** No Party is liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

(e) **Materiality Scrape.** In calculating Losses, each representation and warranty is read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier. This reading does not apply in determining whether a breach has occurred.

(f) **Mitigation.** Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate any Loss once it becomes aware of the event giving rise to the Loss.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising under this Agreement. This Section 9.6 does not apply to:

(a) claims of fraud;

(b) claims under Section 2.3; or

(c) claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 12.11.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless Law requires otherwise.
