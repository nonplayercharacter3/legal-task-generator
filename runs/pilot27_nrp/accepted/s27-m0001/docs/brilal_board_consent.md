# BRILAL FOODS GROUP, INC.

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Dated as of March 16, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of **Brilal Foods Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), acting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and the Bylaws of the Corporation, hereby waive any notice requirements and consent to the adoption of the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if they had been duly adopted at a meeting of the Board duly called and held at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. RECITALS

**1.1 The Company.** The Corporation owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the "**Shares**"), of **Sunfold Creamery, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company processes organic dairy products at its plant in St. Albans, Vermont (the "**St. Albans Plant**").

**1.2 The Proposed Transaction.** Management has presented to the Board a Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") to be entered into between the Corporation, as seller, and **Ratel Dairy Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Buyer**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Corporation will sell all of the Shares to the Buyer for an aggregate purchase price of $86,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to the working capital adjustment provided in the Purchase Agreement (the "**Transaction**"). The Purchase Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

**1.3 Ancillary Agreements.** The Purchase Agreement contemplates that, at the Closing (as defined in the Purchase Agreement), the Corporation will enter into:

(a) a Transition Services Agreement with the Company (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"), under which the Corporation will provide laboratory testing, organic certification compliance support and cold-chain logistics services to the Company for a period of 12 months after the Closing Date; and

(b) a Bill of Sale (the "**Bill of Sale**"), under which the Corporation will convey to the Company the wastewater lagoon system serving the St. Albans Plant, including the lagoon cells, liners, aerators, pumps, piping, monitoring wells and related equipment (the "**Lagoon Assets**").

The Transition Services Agreement, the Bill of Sale and each other agreement, instrument or certificate to be delivered by the Corporation under the Purchase Agreement at the Closing are referred to as the "**Ancillary Agreements**."

**1.4 Indemnification and Guaranties.** The Board has been advised that, under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, the Corporation will indemnify the Buyer for, among other things, breaches of its representations and warranties and for contamination originating from the St. Albans Plant wastewater lagoon system, subject to the limitations set forth in the Purchase Agreement, and that the Corporation's existing guaranties of the Company's obligations under the lease of the St. Albans Plant and certain Company supply contracts (the "**Company Guaranties**") will remain in place after the Closing, with the Buyer indemnifying the Corporation in respect of them as provided in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.5 Third-Party Consents.** Management has reported to the Board that the consummation of the Transaction will require (a) the consent of the landlord under the lease of the St. Albans Plant, (b) notice to the Company's USDA-accredited organic certifying agent, and (c) notice to the Vermont Agency of Natural Resources in respect of the Company's wastewater discharge permit, each of which is listed on Schedule 4.5 to the Purchase Agreement.

**1.6 Raw Milk Supply Agreement.** Management has further reported to the Board that the Company is party to a Raw Milk Supply Agreement (the "**Raw Milk Supply Agreement**") with **Kettle Ridge Organic Dairy Cooperative** ("**Kettle Ridge**"), under which Kettle Ridge supplies approximately 40% of the Company's raw milk requirements. Under the Raw Milk Supply Agreement, a change in control of the Company requires the prior written consent of Kettle Ridge, failing which Kettle Ridge may terminate the Raw Milk Supply Agreement on 30 days' notice. The sale of the Shares to the Buyer will constitute a change in control of the Company for purposes of the Raw Milk Supply Agreement.

**1.7 Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the terms of the Purchase Agreement and the forms of the Ancillary Agreements, has received the advice of management, the Corporation's financial advisor and outside counsel, and has determined that the Transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders.

## 2. RESOLUTIONS

### Resolution 1: Approval of the Purchase Agreement and the Transaction

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, and the Transaction, including the sale of the Shares to the Buyer for the Purchase Price, are hereby authorized, adopted and approved in all respects; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and any Vice President of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each hereby authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it may approve, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by that execution.

### Resolution 2: Approval of the Ancillary Agreements

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Transition Services Agreement and the Bill of Sale, substantially in the forms attached as Exhibit A and Exhibit B to the Purchase Agreement, and each other Ancillary Agreement, are hereby authorized, adopted and approved; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is hereby authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to execute and deliver each Ancillary Agreement at the Closing, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it may approve, and to cause the Corporation to perform its obligations under each Ancillary Agreement, including the provision of the services under the Transition Services Agreement and the conveyance of the Lagoon Assets to the Company under the Bill of Sale.

### Resolution 3: Schedule 4.5 Consents and Notices

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is hereby authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed on Schedule 4.5 to the Purchase Agreement, including the consent of the landlord under the lease of the St. Albans Plant, and to deliver, or cause the Company to deliver, the notices to the Company's organic certifying agent and the Vermont Agency of Natural Resources described in Recital 1.5.

### Resolution 4: Raw Milk Supply Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that, with respect to the change-in-control consent of Kettle Ridge under the Raw Milk Supply Agreement described in Recital 1.6, the Authorized Officers are, and each of them is, hereby authorized to request such consent following the Closing, in cooperation with the Buyer and the Company, and to execute and deliver such correspondence and other documents in connection with that request as the Authorized Officer may deem necessary or appropriate.

### Resolution 5: Closing Deliverables

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is hereby authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to execute and deliver at the Closing the stock certificates and stock powers for the Shares, the officer's certificate required by Section 7.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, an IRS Form W-9 of the Corporation, and each other certificate, instrument and document required to be delivered by the Corporation under Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the Secretary or any Assistant Secretary of the Corporation is hereby authorized and directed to certify these resolutions to the Buyer at the Closing as required by Section 3.2(a)(vii) of the Purchase Agreement.

### Resolution 6: Resignations of Company Directors and Officers

**RESOLVED**, that each officer or employee of the Corporation who serves as a director or officer of the Company and who is designated by the Buyer under Section 3.2(a)(v) of the Purchase Agreement is hereby authorized and requested to deliver a written resignation from that position, effective as of the Closing.

### Resolution 7: General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is hereby authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to take all such further actions, to make all such filings, and to execute and deliver all such further agreements, instruments, certificates and documents, and to pay all such fees and expenses, as the Authorized Officer may deem necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions and to consummate the Transaction, the taking of any such action to be conclusive evidence of that determination.

### Resolution 8: Ratification

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer, employee or agent of the Corporation in connection with the Transaction and the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions, including the negotiation of the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 3. EFFECTIVENESS

**3.1 Counterparts.** This written consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or by PDF, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

**3.2 Effective Date; Filing.** This written consent shall be effective as of the date first written above upon execution by all of the directors of the Corporation, and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
