# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026, by and between **Brilal Foods Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ratel Dairy Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the "**Shares**"), of **Sunfold Creamery, Inc.**, a Vermont corporation (the "**Company**"), which processes organic dairy products at its plant in St. Albans, Vermont (the "**St. Albans Plant**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $86,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay (a) to the payees designated in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv), the amounts required to repay the Company's indebtedness for borrowed money, and (b) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the amounts paid under clause (a) and less the Estimated Working Capital Shortfall, if any, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in writing by the applicable payee at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Working Capital**"), prepared in accordance with the principles set forth on Schedule 2.3. If the Estimated Working Capital is less than $9,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), the shortfall (the "**Estimated Working Capital Shortfall**") shall reduce the amount payable at the Closing.

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing. Seller shall have 45 days to object in writing. Any dispute not resolved by the Parties within 30 days after delivery of an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. The fees of that firm shall be allocated in inverse proportion to the relative success of each Party.

(c) If the final net working capital exceeds the Estimated Working Capital, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller; if it is less, Seller shall pay the deficiency to Buyer, in each case within five Business Days after final determination.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to the Person in respect of which it was withheld.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iii) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(iv) customary payoff letters for all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company, together with Lien releases;

(v) the written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(vi) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.5;

(vii) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of the board of directors of Seller authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it;

(viii) a good standing certificate of the Company issued by the Vermont Secretary of State dated within ten days before the Closing Date;

(ix) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(x) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments required by Section 2.2, a counterpart of the Transition Services Agreement duly executed by the Company, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Vermont. Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 10,000 shares are issued and outstanding, all of which are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants, convertible securities or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company. The Company has no subsidiaries.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.5, require consent under, conflict with, result in a breach of or give rise to any right of termination or acceleration under any Material Contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended. Those financial statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.5 Consents.** No consent, approval, waiver or authorization of, or notice to, any Person (including any Governmental Authority) is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement or the consummation of the transactions contemplated by it, except as set forth on Schedule 4.5.

**4.6 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.6 lists each of the following Contracts to which the Company is a party (the "**Material Contracts**"): (a) each Contract for the purchase of raw milk or other dairy inputs; (b) each Contract with a customer accounting for more than $1,000,000 of revenue in fiscal year 2025; (c) the lease of the St. Albans Plant; (d) each Contract with a Governmental Authority or organic certifying agent; and (e) each other Contract involving payments in excess of $500,000 per year. Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.7 Real Property; Assets.** The Company holds a valid leasehold interest in the St. Albans Plant. Upon delivery of the Bill of Sale, the Company will own good title to the Lagoon Assets. The assets of the Company, together with the Lagoon Assets and the services to be provided under the Transition Services Agreement, are sufficient for the conduct of the Company's business as currently conducted.

**4.8 Environmental Matters.** Except for the Specified Environmental Matter, (a) the Company is and has been in material compliance with all Environmental Laws, (b) the Company holds and is in compliance with all permits required under Environmental Law, including its wastewater discharge permits, and (c) there has been no release of Hazardous Materials at the St. Albans Plant that would reasonably be expected to give rise to a material Environmental Liability.

**4.9 Organic Certification.** The Company holds a current organic handler certification under the USDA National Organic Program for each product sold as organic and has not received any notice of noncompliance, proposed suspension or proposed revocation in the past three years.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is in material compliance with all applicable Laws, including food safety Laws administered by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the Vermont Agency of Agriculture, Food and Markets, and holds all material permits necessary to conduct its business.

**4.11 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Shares.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

**4.13 Taxes.** All income and other material Tax returns of the Company have been timely filed, and all Taxes shown as due on them have been paid.

**4.14 Employees.** Schedule 4.14 lists each employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each plan has been operated in material compliance with its terms and applicable Law.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to (a) conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, (b) maintain its organic certifications and environmental permits, (c) preserve its relationships with suppliers, customers and employees, and (d) not amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the conditions to Closing in Article VII to be satisfied. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed on Schedule 4.5.

**6.4 Lagoon Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall convey the Lagoon Assets to the Company pursuant to the Bill of Sale, free and clear of all Liens.

**6.5 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement with the Company, under which Seller shall provide laboratory testing, organic certification compliance support and cold-chain logistics services to the Company following the Closing.

**6.6 Intercompany Arrangements.** Except for the Transition Services Agreement, the Bill of Sale and the Company Guaranties, all Contracts between the Company, on the one hand, and Seller or any of its Affiliates, on the other, shall be terminated at or before the Closing without further liability to the Company.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives to, hold in strict confidence and not use or disclose any Company Confidential Information, except (a) as required by Law, after giving Buyer prompt notice and a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order, or (b) to the extent the information becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Section 6.7. Seller acknowledges that Company Confidential Information includes information that is not marked or identified as confidential.

**6.8 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) own, operate or manage any business that processes or sells organic fluid milk, yogurt or cheese in the States of Vermont, New Hampshire, Maine, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Rhode Island or New York, or (b) solicit any employee of the Company or any supplier of raw milk to the Company to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

**6.9 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing or termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement concerning any sale of the Company or its assets other than to Buyer.

**6.10 Public Announcements.** No Party shall issue any press release or public statement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by Law.

**6.11 Books and Records.** For seven years after the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing and to afford Seller reasonable access to them for legitimate business purposes.

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an executive officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), 7.2(b) and 7.2(e);

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before June 30, 2026, provided that the right to terminate under this clause (b) is not available to a Party whose breach caused the failure of the Closing to occur; (c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2, Section 6.10 and Article XII shall survive, and no termination shall relieve any Party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date; provided that (a) the Fundamental Representations shall survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations, (b) the representations and warranties in Section 4.8 (Environmental Matters) shall survive until five years after the Closing Date, and (c) the representations and warranties in Section 4.13 (Taxes) shall survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Claims under Section 9.2(c) may be made until seven years after the Closing Date. Covenants and agreements shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim may be made after the applicable survival period, except that any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before expiration of that period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article IX, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Seller under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) the Specified Environmental Matter, including all investigation, remediation, monitoring and closure costs and all third-party claims and governmental penalties relating to it; and

(d) any indebtedness of the Company outstanding at the Closing and not repaid at the Closing, and any Taxes of the Company attributable to periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, and subject to this Article IX, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(b) the Company Guaranties, including any payment, performance or other obligation of Seller or any of its Affiliates under any Company Guaranty, whether arising before or after the Closing, and notwithstanding Section 9.5.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the Party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

(b) The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within 20 days after receipt of notice of the claim. If it does so, the Indemnified Party may participate at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money, is fully paid by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

(c) Seller shall control the investigation and remediation of the Specified Environmental Matter, in consultation with Buyer, using qualified environmental consultants and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with operations at the St. Albans Plant.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (i) until the aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceed 0.75% of the Purchase Price (the "**Deductible**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible, or (ii) for Losses under Section 9.2(a) in excess of 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Cap**"). Buyer shall not be liable under Section 9.3(a) for Losses in excess of the Cap.

(b) The Deductible and the Cap shall not apply to Losses arising out of (i) any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation or the representations and warranties in Section 4.13, (ii) fraud, or (iii) the Specified Environmental Matter, whether claimed under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.2(c). The aggregate liability of Seller for Losses described in clause (i) of this Section 9.5(b) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of the Loss, less the costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

(d) No Party shall be liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

(e) For purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising under this Agreement, except for claims of fraud, claims under Section 2.3, and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 12.11.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date, consistent with past practice, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least 20 days before filing.

**10.3 Cooperation; Transfer Taxes.** The Parties shall cooperate reasonably in connection with the preparation of Tax returns and any Tax audit. **11.2 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article XI shall create any right in any employee or other Person or amend any employee benefit plan.
