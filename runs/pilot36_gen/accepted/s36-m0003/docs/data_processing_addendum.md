# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Drulun Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Carnegie Center Drive, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Drulun**"); and

**Pruvax Biologics Manufacturing, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2100 Ellis Road, Durham, North Carolina 27703 ("**Pruvax**").

Drulun and Pruvax are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Drulun and Pruvax are parties to a Manufacturing and Supply Agreement effective February 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under the Supply Agreement, Pruvax manufactures, labels, serializes and supplies finished Product to Drulun.

B. In performing Sections 8.1 and 8.2 of the Supply Agreement, Pruvax may process Personal Data on Drulun's behalf. This includes patient and reporter information contained in complaint, adverse event and serialization records.

C. Section 9.1 of the Supply Agreement requires the Parties to enter into this DPA to govern Pruvax's processing of Personal Data on Drulun's behalf.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Supply Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this DPA:

(a) "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this DPA. These include, where applicable, the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations, the Federal Trade Commission Act, state consumer health data and consumer privacy laws, state data breach notification laws, and FDA regulations governing adverse event and complaint records.

(b) "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates. Data Subjects include patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals and other persons who report complaints or adverse events.

(c) "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to a Data Subject that Pruvax processes on Drulun's behalf in connection with the Supply Agreement. It includes information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with a Data Subject, including health information.

(d) "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. It includes collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, alignment, restriction, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

(e) "**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data in the possession, custody or control of Pruvax or any Subprocessor.

(f) "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Pruvax to Process Personal Data on Drulun's behalf.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Drulun determines the purposes and means of the Processing of Personal Data. Pruvax Processes Personal Data solely as Drulun's service provider and processor.

2.2 **Scope of Processing.** Pruvax shall Process Personal Data only to the extent necessary to:

(a) receive, log, triage and forward product complaints and adverse event reports concerning the Product under Section 8.2 of the Supply Agreement;

(b) generate, maintain and exchange serialization and aggregation data, and investigate serialization exceptions, under Section 8.1 of the Supply Agreement, to the extent those records contain Personal Data; and

(c) retain the records described in Section 8.3 of the Supply Agreement.

2.3 **Details of Processing.** Schedule 1 describes the categories of Data Subjects, the types of Personal Data, and the nature, purpose and duration of the Processing.

## 3. PRUVAX OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Instructions.** Pruvax shall Process Personal Data only on Drulun's documented instructions. The Supply Agreement and this DPA constitute those instructions, together with any further written instructions Drulun gives that are consistent with them. Pruvax shall promptly notify Drulun if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Pruvax shall not:

(a) sell or share Personal Data, or disclose it for any commercial purpose other than performing the Supply Agreement;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the Parties;

(c) combine Personal Data with personal data it receives from or on behalf of any other person; or

(d) attempt to re-identify any Personal Data that Drulun has de-identified or pseudonymized.

3.3 **Compliance.** Pruvax shall comply with all Data Protection Laws that apply to it as a processor or service provider. It shall promptly notify Drulun if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under this DPA or Data Protection Laws.

3.4 **Confidentiality of Personnel.** Pruvax shall ensure that every person authorized to Process Personal Data is bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality. Access must be limited to personnel who need it to perform the Supply Agreement and who have received appropriate training in data protection and pharmacovigilance data handling.

## 4. SECURITY

4.1 **Security Measures.** Pruvax shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Personal Data against Security Incidents. At a minimum, those safeguards must include the measures described in Schedule 2.

4.2 **Security Program Review.** Pruvax shall test and evaluate the effectiveness of its safeguards at least once per year. It shall remediate any material deficiency within a reasonable time.

4.3 **Validated Systems.** Pruvax shall Process complaint, adverse event and serialization records containing Personal Data only in computerized systems validated in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11 and the Quality Agreement.

## 5. SUBPROCESSORS

5.1 **Authorization.** Drulun authorizes Pruvax to engage the Subprocessors listed in Schedule 3. Pruvax shall give Drulun at least 30 days' prior written notice before engaging any new Subprocessor or replacing an existing one. Drulun may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If Drulun objects, the Parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. Pruvax shall not engage the proposed Subprocessor for Drulun's Personal Data until the objection is resolved.

5.2 **Subprocessor Terms.** Pruvax shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

5.3 **Responsibility.** Pruvax remains fully responsible to Drulun for the performance of each Subprocessor's obligations.

## 6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

6.1 **Notification.** Pruvax shall notify Drulun in writing without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. Notice must be sent to Drulun's General Counsel and to privacy@drulun.com.

6.2 **Content of Notice.** The notice must describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;

(b) the likely consequences of the Security Incident;

(c) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it; and

(d) a contact person at Pruvax from whom further information may be obtained.

Pruvax shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

6.3 **Cooperation.** Pruvax shall take all reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. It shall cooperate with Drulun in any investigation and in meeting any notification obligation to Data Subjects, regulators or other third parties.

6.4 **Notifications by Drulun.** Drulun decides whether and how to notify Data Subjects, regulators or other third parties of a Security Incident. Pruvax shall not make any such notification without Drulun's prior written consent, unless Data Protection Laws require it.

6.5 **Costs.** To the extent a Security Incident results from Pruvax's or a Subprocessor's breach of this DPA or Data Protection Laws, Pruvax shall reimburse Drulun's reasonable, documented costs of investigation, notification, call center support, and credit or identity monitoring offered to affected Data Subjects.

## 7. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS AND ASSISTANCE

7.1 **Requests.** If Pruvax receives a request from a Data Subject to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws, it shall promptly forward the request to Drulun and shall not respond to it except as Drulun directs.

7.2 **Assistance.** Pruvax shall provide reasonable assistance to Drulun in responding to Data Subject requests and in completing any data protection assessment that Data Protection Laws require.

7.3 **Regulatory Requests.** Pruvax shall notify Drulun promptly of any request from a regulator or law enforcement authority for access to Personal Data, unless the law prohibits such notice.

## 8. AUDIT

8.1 **Information.** On Drulun's reasonable request, Pruvax shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA. This includes summaries of its most recent independent security assessment.

8.2 **Audits.** Drulun may audit Pruvax's compliance with this DPA as part of the audits permitted under Section 7.3 of the Supply Agreement. Drulun may also audit at any time following a Security Incident, on reasonable notice.

## 9. DATA LOCATION AND TRANSFERS

9.1 **Location.** Pruvax shall Process and store Personal Data only in the United States, unless Drulun gives its prior written consent.

9.2 **Transfers.** Pruvax shall transmit Personal Data to Drulun only through Drulun's validated serialization platform, Drulun's designated pharmacovigilance intake channel, or other secure means approved by Drulun in writing.

## 10. RETURN AND DELETION

10.1 **Retention.** Pruvax shall retain Personal Data only for as long as required by Section 8.3 of the Supply Agreement.

10.2 **Return or Deletion.** At the end of the retention period, or earlier on Drulun's written direction, Pruvax shall, at Drulun's option, return all Personal Data to Drulun or securely destroy it. On request, Pruvax shall certify the destruction in writing. This obligation does not apply to the extent Applicable Law requires Pruvax to retain Personal Data. In that case, Pruvax shall continue to protect the retained Personal Data in accordance with this DPA.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

11.1 **Indemnity.** Pruvax shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Drulun and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of third-party claims or regulatory proceedings to the extent caused by:

(a) Pruvax's or any Subprocessor's breach of this DPA;

(b) a Security Incident affecting Personal Data in the possession, custody or control of Pruvax or any Subprocessor; or

(c) Pruvax's or any Subprocessor's violation of Data Protection Laws.

11.2 **Procedure.** The procedure in Section 11.3 of the Supply Agreement applies to claims under Section 11.1.

11.3 **Liability.** Section 12 of the Supply Agreement applies to liability under this DPA. Pruvax's indemnification obligations under Section 11.1 are indemnification obligations of the kind described in Section 12.3(a) of the Supply Agreement.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 **Governing Law.** This DPA, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. This Section 12.1 applies notwithstanding Section 15.1 of the Supply Agreement.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this DPA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

12.3 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized Processing of Personal Data may cause irreparable harm. Each Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in respect of any breach of this DPA.

## 13. TERM

13.1 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date. It continues for as long as Pruvax or any Subprocessor Processes Personal Data on Drulun's behalf.

13.2 **Survival.** Sections 6, 10, 11 and 12 survive expiry or termination of this DPA and of the Supply Agreement.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment reasonably required to comply with changes in Data Protection Laws.

14.2 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA must be given in accordance with Section 17.6 of the Supply Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents must also be given as provided in Section 6.1.

14.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

14.4 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## SCHEDULE 1 – DETAILS OF PROCESSING

1. **Categories of Data Subjects.** Patients treated with the Product; caregivers; healthcare professionals; pharmacists; and other persons who report product complaints or adverse events.

2. **Types of Personal Data.**

(a) Contact details of reporters: name, telephone number, email address and mailing address.

(b) Patient information contained in complaint and adverse event reports: initials, age or date of birth, sex, medical history, concomitant medications, description of the event and outcome.

(c) Product information linked to a Data Subject: lot number, serial number and product identifier of the unit concerned.

3. **Nature and Purpose.** Receipt, logging, triage and forwarding of complaints and adverse event reports to Drulun; investigation of serialization exceptions linked to returned or complained-of units; and record retention.

4. **Duration.** The Term of the Supply Agreement and the retention period under Section 8.3 of the Supply Agreement.

## SCHEDULE 2 – MINIMUM SECURITY MEASURES

1. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.

2. Role-based access controls, unique user credentials and multi-factor authentication for all systems holding Personal Data.

3. Audit trails for creation, modification and deletion of records, in accordance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11.

4. Logging and monitoring of access to Personal Data, with log retention of at least one year.

5. Vulnerability scanning at least quarterly and penetration testing at least annually.

6. A documented incident response plan, tested at least annually.

7. Secure disposal of media in accordance with NIST SP 800-88.

8. Physical access controls at the Durham, North Carolina facility and at any data center used to host Personal Data.
