## 4. DELIVERY TERMS

4.1 **Delivery.** In this Order Form, "**Delivery**" means tender to the first carrier at Pruvax's facility at 2100 Ellis Road, Durham, North Carolina 27703, FCA Pruvax Facility (Incoterms 2020). "**Delivered**" has a corresponding meaning.

4.2 **Carriage.** Pruvax shall book carriage on Drulun's behalf with a carrier and cold-chain shipping configuration qualified under the Quality Agreement, as required by Section 5.1 of the Supply Agreement. Product must be maintained at 2°C to 8°C. Every shipper must contain a calibrated temperature data logger.

4.3 **Destination.** Each shipment shall be consigned to:

Drulun Therapeutics, Inc.
Distribution Center
Attention: Receiving – Cold Chain
Edison, New Jersey

Receiving hours are Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Pruvax shall give Drulun's distribution center at least 48 hours' advance notice of each shipment, including the carrier, waybill number and logger identification.

4.4 **Shipping Documentation.** Each shipment must be accompanied by the documentation described in Section 5.2 of the Supply Agreement. Pruvax shall also send electronic copies to qa.release@drulun.com on the date of Delivery.

4.5 **Delivery Window.** Pruvax shall Deliver each Batch no earlier than five business days before, and no later than the Requested Delivery Date in Section 2.2, unless Drulun agrees otherwise in writing.

## 5. QUALITY AND SERIALIZATION

5.1 **Release.** Each Batch shall be released by Pruvax's quality unit in accordance with the Quality Agreement before Delivery. The Certificate of Analysis and executed batch release documentation shall be provided as required by Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement.

5.2 **Serialization.** Pruvax shall serialize each saleable unit and homogeneous case, and upload aggregation data to Drulun's serialization platform, in accordance with Section 8.1 of the Supply Agreement. Upload must take place before the shipment leaves Pruvax's facility.

5.3 **Labeling.** Pruvax shall use the label and carton artwork approved under change control number DRU-CC-2026-0089, version 4.0.
