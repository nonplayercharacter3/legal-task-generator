# MANUFACTURING AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Manufacturing and Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Drulun Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Carnegie Center Drive, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Drulun**"); and

**Pruvax Biologics Manufacturing, LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2100 Ellis Road, Durham, North Carolina 27703 ("**Pruvax**").

Drulun and Pruvax are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Drulun holds the approved marketing authorization for a refrigerated oncology injectable product and markets that product in the United States.

B. Pruvax operates a current Good Manufacturing Practice aseptic fill-finish facility in Durham, North Carolina. It is qualified to fill, finish, label, serialize, package and release sterile injectable products.

C. Drulun wishes to buy, and Pruvax wishes to manufacture and supply, finished Product on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following capitalized terms have the following meanings:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all laws, regulations and binding guidance that apply to a Party's activities under this Agreement. These include the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Drug Supply Chain Security Act ("**DSCSA**"), cGMP, and applicable data protection and privacy laws.

1.3 "**Batch**" means a defined quantity of Product filled and finished in a single manufacturing run and identified by a unique lot number.

1.4 "**Certificate of Analysis**" means a document, signed by Pruvax's authorized quality representative, that states the test results for a Batch against the Specifications.

1.5 "**cGMP**" means current good manufacturing practices as set out in 21 C.F.R. Parts 210 and 211 and related FDA guidance.

1.6 "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the data processing addendum entered into by the Parties under Section 9.1, as amended from time to time.

1.7 "**Delivery**" means receipt of Product at Drulun's designated distribution center in Edison, New Jersey. "**Delivered**" has a corresponding meaning.

1.8 "**Effective Date**" means February 1, 2024.

1.9 "**FDA**" means the United States Food and Drug Administration or any successor agency.

1.10 "**Firm Order Period**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

1.11 "**Order Form**" means a written order form, in substantially the form agreed by the Parties, that is signed by both Parties and references this Agreement. An Order Form sets out the Products, quantities, pricing, delivery terms and related commercial details for one or more Batches.

1.12 "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

1.13 "**Product**" means the finished, labeled and serialized drug product described in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.14 "**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between the Parties covering the Product, as amended from time to time.

1.15 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for the Product set out in Exhibit A and in the Quality Agreement, as amended in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

1.16 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. Unless terminated earlier under Section 16, it continues for three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date, being the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive two-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Renewal is automatic absent timely non-renewal notice. A non-renewal notice is timely only if a Party gives written notice of non-renewal to the other Party under Section 17.6 at least 180 days before expiry of the then-current term.

2.3 **Orders Spanning Expiry.** Each Order Form signed before this Agreement expires or is terminated remains binding in accordance with its terms. This Agreement continues to govern that Order Form until all Product under it has been Delivered and paid for, unless the Order Form is terminated under Section 16.

## 3. FORECASTS, ORDERS AND MINIMUM PURCHASE

3.1 **Rolling Forecast.** By the fifth business day of each calendar month, Drulun shall give Pruvax a rolling eighteen-month forecast of its Product requirements. Except as provided in Section 3.2, forecasts are non-binding.

3.2 **Firm Order Period.** The first three months of each forecast (the "**Firm Order Period**") are binding on Drulun. Drulun shall sign Order Forms covering at least the quantities forecast for the Firm Order Period.

3.3 **Order Forms.** Drulun shall order Product by submitting a proposed Order Form at least 120 days before the requested Delivery date. Pruvax shall accept or reasonably reject a proposed Order Form in writing within ten business days of receipt. An Order Form binds the Parties only once both Parties have signed it.

3.4 **Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year during the Term, Drulun shall order and accept at least the minimum quantity of Product stated in Exhibit B (the "**Annual Minimum**"). A "**Contract Year**" is each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it. If Drulun orders less than the Annual Minimum in a Contract Year, Pruvax may invoice Drulun, within 60 days after that Contract Year ends, for the shortfall at the shortfall rate stated in Exhibit B. The Annual Minimum is reduced pro rata for any quantity that Pruvax fails to supply in conforming condition when due.

3.5 **Capacity.** Pruvax shall reserve enough manufacturing capacity at its Durham, North Carolina facility to meet forecast quantities of up to 120% of the most recent forecast for each month in the Firm Order Period.

## 4. PRICE AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Price.** Drulun shall pay the per-unit prices set out in Exhibit B, or in the applicable Order Form where Exhibit B provides for Order Form pricing. Prices include all costs of manufacture, release testing, labeling, serialization, packaging, cold-chain packaging and carriage to the Delivery location.

4.2 **Price Adjustment.** Once per Contract Year, beginning with the second Contract Year, Pruvax may adjust prices by giving Drulun at least 90 days' written notice. No adjustment may exceed the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Pharmaceutical Preparation Manufacturing (PCU325412325412) over the preceding twelve months.

4.3 **Invoicing.** Pruvax shall invoice Drulun for each Batch on or after Delivery. Each invoice must be accompanied by the Certificate of Analysis and executed batch release documentation.

4.4 **Payment.** Drulun shall pay undisputed amounts within 45 days of receiving a correct invoice. Drulun may withhold any disputed amount in good faith. It shall give Pruvax written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, before the payment due date.

4.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Pruvax shall itemize those taxes on its invoices where Applicable Law requires them. Each Party bears its own income taxes.

## 5. SHIPMENT, TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

5.1 **Shipment.** Pruvax shall arrange carriage of Product to the Delivery location. It shall use a carrier and cold-chain shipping configuration qualified under the Quality Agreement to maintain Product at 2°C to 8°C throughout transit. Every shipper must contain a calibrated temperature data logger.

5.2 **Shipping Documentation.** Each shipment must be accompanied by a packing list, the Certificate of Analysis, temperature data logger identification, and the DSCSA transaction information, transaction history and transaction statement for the Product.

5.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to Product and risk of loss of or damage to Product pass from Pruvax to Drulun on Delivery. Until Delivery, Pruvax bears all risk of loss of, damage to, or temperature excursion affecting the Product, including during transit.

5.4 **Late Delivery.** Pruvax shall notify Drulun promptly once it becomes aware that any Batch will not be Delivered on the date in the applicable Order Form. If a Batch is Delivered more than 30 days late for reasons within Pruvax's control, Drulun may cancel the affected quantities without liability, and those quantities count toward the Annual Minimum.

## 6. INSPECTION AND REJECTION

6.1 **Inspection.** After Delivery, Drulun may inspect each shipment of Product and review the Batch documentation and temperature data logger records.

6.2 **Inspection and Rejection.** Drulun may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications, cGMP, Applicable Law or the applicable Order Form. Rejection includes Product showing a temperature excursion outside the range in Section 5.1. To reject, Drulun shall give Pruvax written notice within 30 days after Delivery, describing the nonconformity. For latent defects that could not reasonably be found by visual inspection and review of documentation, the 30-day period runs from the date Drulun discovers the defect. That period may not end later than the expiry date of the affected Batch.

6.3 **Disputes over Conformity.** If Pruvax disputes a rejection, the Parties shall resolve the dispute in accordance with the Quality Agreement. That may include referral of samples to a mutually acceptable independent laboratory. The laboratory's findings bind the Parties as to conformity. The Party whose position the findings do not support bears the laboratory's costs.

6.4 **Remedies for Nonconforming Product.** For properly rejected Product, Pruvax shall, at Drulun's option, either (a) replace it with conforming Product as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within 60 days, at no additional cost to Drulun, or (b) credit or refund the price paid. Pruvax bears the costs of returning or destroying rejected Product.

## 7. QUALITY AND REGULATORY

7.1 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties shall maintain the Quality Agreement in effect throughout the Term. On quality matters, the Quality Agreement governs the Parties' responsibilities for manufacturing, testing, release, deviations, change control and audits.

7.2 **Compliance.** Pruvax shall manufacture, test, store and ship the Product in accordance with the Specifications, cGMP, the Quality Agreement and Applicable Law.

7.3 **Audits and Inspections.** Drulun may audit Pruvax's facility and records relating to the Product once per Contract Year, and additionally for cause, on reasonable notice. Pruvax shall notify Drulun within 24 hours of any FDA or other regulatory inspection relating to the Product. It shall give Drulun copies of any resulting observations and responses.

7.4 **Recalls.** Drulun decides whether to initiate any recall or market withdrawal of Product. Pruvax shall cooperate fully. To the extent a recall results from Pruvax's breach of this Agreement, Pruvax shall reimburse Drulun's reasonable, documented recall costs.

## 8. SERIALIZATION AND ADVERSE EVENT INTAKE

8.1 **Serialization.** Pruvax shall apply a unique product identifier to each saleable unit and homogeneous case of Product as required by the DSCSA. It shall maintain aggregation data and exchange serialization data with Drulun and its designated trading partners through Drulun's validated serialization platform.

8.2 **Adverse Event and Complaint Intake.** Pruvax shall forward to Drulun any product complaint or report of an adverse event concerning the Product that Pruvax receives. It shall do so within one business day of receipt, or within 24 hours for any report that indicates a serious adverse event. Drulun remains responsible for all pharmacovigilance and regulatory reporting obligations.

8.3 **Records.** Pruvax shall retain serialization and complaint records relating to the Product for the longer of six years and the period required by Applicable Law.

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

9.1 **Data Processing Addendum.** Pruvax may process Personal Data in performing Sections 8.1 and 8.2. Such Personal Data includes patient and reporter information contained in complaint, adverse event and serialization records. The Parties shall enter into the Data Processing Addendum, which governs Pruvax's processing of Personal Data on Drulun's behalf.

9.2 **Compliance.** Pruvax shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the DPA, Drulun's documented instructions and Applicable Law.

(a) Pruvax's breach of this Agreement or the Quality Agreement;

(b) Pruvax's negligence or willful misconduct; or

(c) any Product failing to conform to the warranty in Section 10.2.

11.2 **By Drulun.** Drulun shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Pruvax and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by:

The indemnifying Party controls the defense, with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. It may not settle any claim that imposes liability or an admission on the indemnified Party without that Party's prior written consent.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

12.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the greater of (a) the amounts paid and payable by Drulun under this Agreement in the twenty-four months before the event giving rise to the claim, and (b) US$10,000,000.

12.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 12.1 and 12.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 11;

(b) breach of Section 14;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Pruvax's obligations under Section 7.4.

## 13. INSURANCE

Throughout the Term and for three years afterwards, each Party shall maintain the following insurance with reputable insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least US$5,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) products liability insurance of at least US$10,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; and

(c) in the case of Pruvax, cyber and privacy liability insurance of at least US$5,000,000 per claim.

On request, each Party shall provide certificates of insurance.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"). It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and shall not disclose it, except to its employees, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

14.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public other than through the receiving Party's breach;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information.

14.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or a regulatory authority. It shall give the other Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation to seek protective treatment.

14.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue during the Term and for seven years afterwards. Obligations relating to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

15.1 **Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. 15.2 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation, a Party shall refer any dispute to the Parties' respective senior executives with authority to resolve it. Those executives shall meet within 15 business days of the referral. This Section 15.2 does not prevent a Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief at any time.

15.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party waives, to the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 16. TERMINATION

16.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 60 days after receiving written notice describing it.

16.2 **For Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) becomes insolvent;

(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) has a receiver appointed; or

(d) becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within 60 days.

16.3 **Regulatory.** Drulun may terminate this Agreement on 90 days' written notice if the FDA withdraws or suspends approval of the Product, or if Drulun permanently discontinues commercialization of the Product in the United States. In that case, Section 3.4 does not apply to any period after the effective date of termination.

16.4 **Effect.** On expiry or termination:

(a) Drulun shall pay for all conforming Product Delivered before the effective date;

(b) Pruvax shall complete any Order Form that Drulun elects to have completed; and

(c) Pruvax shall, at Drulun's direction, transfer to Drulun or destroy all Drulun-supplied materials and Confidential Information.

16.5 **Survival.** The following Sections survive expiry or termination: Sections 4.4, 6, 7.4, 8.3, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16.4, 16.5 and 17.

## EXHIBIT A – PRODUCT AND SPECIFICATIONS

1. **Product.** Drulun's oncology injectable, solution for intravenous infusion, supplied in single-dose glass vials, each individually cartoned, labeled and serialized in accordance with Section 8.1.

2. **Storage and shipping condition.** Refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Protect from light.

3. **Specifications.** As set out in the Product specification document approved under the Quality Agreement, as amended through change control.

## EXHIBIT B – PRICING AND ANNUAL MINIMUM

1. **Unit price.** As stated in the applicable Order Form, subject to Section 4.2.

2. **Annual Minimum (Section 3.4).** 60,000 finished vials per Contract Year.

3. **Shortfall rate (Section 3.4).** 35% of the then-current unit price, multiplied by the number of vials by which Drulun's orders in the Contract Year fall short of the Annual Minimum.
