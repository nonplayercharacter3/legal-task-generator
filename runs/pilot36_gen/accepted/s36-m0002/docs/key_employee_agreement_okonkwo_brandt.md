# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit B to the Asset Purchase Agreement*

This **KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2026 by and between **Teivav ColdLink Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), and **Dashiell Okonkwo-Brandt**, an individual (the "**Executive**"). The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date.

## RECITALS

A. The Executive is the Chief Operating Officer of Vaipith Refrigerated Logistics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**").

B. The Company and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will acquire from Seller substantially all of the assets used in the Business.

C. The Company wishes to employ the Executive in connection with its acquisition of the Business, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

D. The Executive will receive substantial benefits from the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement and from his employment under this Agreement. The Executive acknowledges that the covenants in Section 6 are a material inducement to the Company to enter into the Purchase Agreement and to protect the goodwill of the Business that the Company is acquiring.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

"**Base Salary**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Board**" means the board of managers of the Company.

"**Business**" means the operation of temperature-controlled warehouse facilities.

"**Cause**" means any of the following: (a) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (b) the Executive's willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing his duties; (c) the Executive's material breach of this Agreement, including Section 5 or Section 6, that, if curable, is not cured within 15 days after written notice from the Company; or (d) the Executive's willful failure to follow a lawful directive of the Chief Executive Officer or the Board that is not cured within 15 days after written notice from the Company.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without the Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in the Base Salary; (b) a material diminution in the Executive's title, authority or duties; or (c) relocation of the Executive's principal place of work by more than 50 miles. Good Reason exists only if the Executive gives written notice to the Company within 30 days after the condition first arises, the Company fails to cure the condition within 30 days after receiving notice, and the Executive resigns within 30 days after the end of the cure period.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.

"**Restricted Territory**" means each state of the United States in which the Company, Seller or any of their respective Affiliates conducted the Business at any time during the 12 months before the Closing Date or conducts the Business at any time during the Executive's employment.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; DUTIES

**2.1 Effectiveness.** This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void and of no further effect.

**2.2 Position.** The Company shall employ the Executive as Senior Vice President, Operations. The Executive shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

**2.3 Duties.** The Executive shall have the duties and authority customary for that position and such other duties consistent with that position as the Chief Executive Officer assigns. These include oversight of facility operations, refrigerated fleet operations, transportation and dispatch, and cross-dock operations acquired under the Purchase Agreement.

**2.4 Full Time and Attention.** The Executive shall devote substantially all of his business time and attention to the Company. He may manage his personal investments and engage in civic and charitable activities, so long as those activities do not materially interfere with his duties or breach Section 6.

**2.5 Place of Performance.** The Executive's principal place of work shall be the Company's offices in Linden, New Jersey, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. COMPENSATION

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $310,000 (the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase it, but not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year of the Company that ends during the Term, the Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of 40% of the Base Salary. The bonus shall be based on performance objectives set by the Board after consultation with the Executive. For the fiscal year in which the Effective Date occurs, any bonus shall be prorated. Any bonus shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year after the year in which it is earned. Except as provided in Section 4.3, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive the bonus.

**3.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus of $250,000. The Company shall pay one-half on the first anniversary of the Effective Date and one-half on the second anniversary, in each case subject to the Executive's continued employment through that date, except as provided in Section 4.3.

**3.4 Equity.** Subject to approval by the Board, the Executive shall be granted an incentive equity award in the Company's parent under its equity incentive plan, on the terms of the plan and the applicable award agreement.

## 4. TERM; TERMINATION

**4.1 Term.** The Executive's employment begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under this Section 4 (the "**Term**"). The Executive's employment is at will, subject to the payment obligations in this Section 4.

**4.2 Termination Generally.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment at any time, with or without Cause. The Executive may resign at any time, with or without Good Reason, on 30 days' written notice. The Executive's employment terminates automatically upon his death and may be terminated by the Company upon his disability, as defined in the Company's long-term disability plan.

**4.3 Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following, subject to Section 4.4:

(a) continued payment of the Base Salary for 12 months after the termination date;

(b) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid;

(c) any unpaid portion of the retention bonus under Section 3.3; and

(d) if the Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment of the employer portion of the premiums for up to 12 months.

**4.4 Release.** The Executive is entitled to the payments under Section 4.3 only if he signs, and does not revoke, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within 60 days after the termination date. The Executive must also remain in compliance with Sections 5 and 6.

**4.5 Accrued Obligations.** Upon any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive his earned but unpaid Base Salary, accrued but unused vacation in accordance with Company policy, and unreimbursed business expenses.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY; WORK PRODUCT

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates or the business acquired under the Purchase Agreement. It includes customer lists, pricing, lane and rate data, customer contract terms, temperature and service-level data, carrier and owner-operator arrangements, warehouse management system data, financial information and business plans. Confidential Information does not include information that becomes generally available to the public other than through the Executive's breach.

**5.2 Non-Disclosure.** During the Term and at all times afterward, the Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, except in performing his duties for the Company or as required by law. If disclosure is required by law, the Executive shall give the Company prompt notice so that it may seek a protective order, to the extent legally permitted.

**5.3 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Executive shall not be held liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

**5.4 Work Product.** All inventions, processes, data and works of authorship that the Executive creates during the Term and that relate to the Company's business belong to the Company. The Executive assigns to the Company all of his rights in that work product.

**5.5 Return of Property.** Upon termination of employment, the Executive shall return all Company property and all documents containing Confidential Information.

## 6. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**6.1 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** During the Executive's employment and until 24 months after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as an owner, employee, officer, consultant, agent or otherwise:

(a) engage in, or own, manage, operate or provide services to any Person that engages in, the Business within the Restricted Territory;

(b) solicit or induce any customer of the Business, or any Person that was a customer of the Business during the 12 months before the Closing Date, to obtain services of the Business from any Person other than the Company or its Affiliates, or to reduce its business with the Company;

(c) solicit or hire any employee of the Company engaged in the Business, or any individual who was employed in the Business within the 6 months before the solicitation or hiring, other than through general advertisements not targeted at those individuals; or

(d) induce any landlord, supplier, carrier or owner-operator of the Business to terminate or adversely change its relationship with the Company.

The Executive may own, as a passive investment, up to 2% of the publicly traded securities of any Person.

**6.2 Non-Disparagement.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, its Affiliates or their respective managers, officers or services. This Section 6.2 does not apply to truthful testimony or to activity protected under Section 5.3.

**6.3 Reasonableness.** The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 6 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and subject matter, and are necessary to protect the goodwill of the Business acquired by the Company and the Company's Confidential Information. The Executive acknowledges that the restrictions are given in connection with the sale of a business.

**6.4 Reformation.** If a court determines that any restriction in this Section 6 is unenforceable as written, the court may modify the restriction to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

**6.5 Tolling.** The Restricted Period shall be extended by any period during which the Executive is in breach of Section 6.1.

## 7. REMEDIES

**7.1 Injunctive Relief.** The Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 5 or Section 6 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**7.2 Forfeiture.** If the Executive materially breaches Section 6, the Company's obligation to make any further payment under Section 4.3 shall end.

## 8. EXPENSES; BENEFITS

**8.1 Business Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing his duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**8.2 Benefits.** The Executive shall be eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior executives, on the terms of those plans. The Executive shall be entitled to four weeks of paid vacation per year.

## 9. SECTION 409A

**9.1 Compliance.** The parties intend that payments under this Agreement comply with, or are exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. Any payment conditioned on a release that could be paid in either of two calendar years shall be paid in the later year.

**9.2 Separation from Service.** A termination of employment means a "separation from service" under Section 409A for purposes of any payment of deferred compensation.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company shall be sent to the attention of its General Counsel. Notices to the Executive shall be sent to his most recent address in the Company's records.

**10.2 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**10.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive and may not be assigned by him. The Company may assign this Agreement to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or substantially all of the business acquired under the Purchase Agreement.

**10.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Executive and the Company with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings between them with respect to that subject matter.

**10.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**10.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.8 Severability.** Subject to Section 6.4, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.9 Survival.** Sections 4.3 through 4.5, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 survive the termination of the Executive's employment.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
