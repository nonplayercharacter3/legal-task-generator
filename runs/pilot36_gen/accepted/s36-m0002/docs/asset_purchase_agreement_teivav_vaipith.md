# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This **ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2026, by and between **Teivav ColdLink Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Vaipith Refrigerated Logistics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the Business.

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens (as defined in Schedule 4.5), all of Seller's right, title and interest in the following assets used in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all accounts receivable of the Business outstanding at Closing;

(b) all refrigerated tractors, reefer trailers, refrigeration units, telematics and temperature-monitoring equipment, racking, material-handling equipment, ammonia and other refrigeration systems, and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all permits, licenses and registrations held for the Business, to the extent transferable;

(e) all books, records, customer lists, maintenance logs, refrigerant leak records, temperature logs and compliance files of the Business;

(f) all intellectual property, software licenses and warehouse management system data used in the Business; and

(g) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include the following (the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's corporate records, minute books and tax returns; (c) all insurance policies of Seller and rights thereunder; (d) all employee benefit plans of Seller and their assets; (e) all Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (f) all tax refunds relating to periods before the Closing; and (g) any Contract that becomes an Excluded Asset under Section 2.7.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the following liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising from performance required after the Closing, and not arising from any breach or default by Seller at or before the Closing; and (b) trade accounts payable of the Business incurred in the ordinary course and reflected on the Closing statement delivered under Section 3.5.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and pay when due, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all liabilities arising from the operation of the Business or the Purchased Assets before the Closing;

(b) all liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising from Seller's performance or non-performance before the Closing, including claims for temperature excursions, spoiled, damaged or lost product, service-level credits and chargebacks;

(c) all liabilities for taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(d) all liabilities for wages, bonuses, commissions, accrued paid time off, severance and payroll taxes relating to employees of Seller for any period before the Closing;

(e) all liabilities arising from accidents, cargo claims, motor carrier violations or other incidents involving Seller's fleet before the Closing;

(f) all liabilities under Environmental Laws to the extent arising from facts, conditions or releases existing or occurring before the Closing; and

(g) all liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets.

**2.5 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws. Any liability arising from such non-compliance is an Excluded Liability.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code, as set forth in an allocation statement prepared by Buyer within 90 days after the Closing Date and reasonably acceptable to Seller.

**2.7 Non-Assignable Contracts.** If any consent to assignment of any Assigned Contract has not been obtained as of the Closing, such Assigned Contract shall not be assigned to Buyer at the Closing and shall be deemed an Excluded Asset for all purposes of this Agreement, without adjustment to the Purchase Price.

## ARTICLE III. PURCHASE PRICE; CLOSING

**3.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $42,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

**3.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least two business days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount.

**3.3 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,200,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX. The Escrow Amount shall be released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement upon the expiration of the Escrow Period (as defined in the Escrow Agreement), less the amount of any pending claims.

**3.4 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third business day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.5 Closing Deliverables of Seller.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**"); (b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (c) assignments of the Leases (the "**Lease Assignments**"); (d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (e) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Key Employee; (f) certificates of title for all titled vehicles included in the Purchased Assets; (g) the Required Consents; (h) a closing statement listing trade accounts payable to be assumed; and (i) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d).

**3.6 Closing Deliverables of Buyer.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the payments required by Sections 3.2 and 3.3; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and Lease Assignments, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; (d) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (e) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that, except as set forth in the disclosure schedules delivered by Seller concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedules**"), the statements in this Article IV are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations hereunder and thereunder. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.7 and Schedule 7.2(c), the execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Seller, or (b) require the consent of, or notice to, any Person under any Material Contract.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet and statements of income of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025. Such financial statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Business for the periods indicated.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens.

**4.6 Real Property.** Seller owns no real property. Schedule 4.6 lists each Facility and each Lease. Each Lease is in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any landlord is in material default under any Lease.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.7 lists each of the following Contracts of the Business (the "**Material Contracts**"): (a) each customer Contract that generated revenue in excess of $500,000 in fiscal year 2025; (b) each Contract with a carrier, broker or owner-operator; (c) each equipment lease; and (d) each Contract containing exclusivity, most-favored-customer or non-competition obligations. **4.8 Sufficiency of Assets; Condition.** The Purchased Assets constitute all of the assets necessary to conduct the Business as currently conducted. The refrigeration systems, vehicles and equipment included in the Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

**4.9 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** Seller is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all Laws applicable to the Business, including the regulations of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration and the Food Safety Modernization Act sanitary transportation rule. Seller holds all permits required to conduct the Business.

**4.11 Employees.** Schedule 4.11 lists each employee of the Business, with title, date of hire and current compensation. Seller is not party to any collective bargaining agreement covering employees of the Business.

**4.12 Environmental Matters.**

(a) Seller and the Facilities are, and since January 1, 2021 have been, in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws.

(b) Each anhydrous ammonia refrigeration system at the Facilities that is subject to the Risk Management Program regulations at 40 C.F.R. Part 68 or the process safety management standard at 29 C.F.R. § 1910.119 has a current risk management plan and process safety management program, and Seller has completed all required hazard analyses, compliance audits and emergency response planning for each such system.

(c) Seller has maintained complete and accurate refrigerant leak inspection, repair and service records for each appliance included in the Purchased Assets, including each reefer unit, as required by 40 C.F.R. Part 82, Subpart F, and no such appliance has exceeded the applicable leak rate threshold without timely repair.

(d) There has been no release of ammonia, refrigerant or other hazardous substance at, on, under or from any Facility or any vehicle included in the Purchased Assets that requires reporting, investigation or remediation under Environmental Laws.

(e) Seller has not received any notice of violation, information request or claim under any Environmental Law relating to the Business, the Facilities or the Purchased Assets.

**4.13 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Business and has paid all taxes due. There are no Liens for taxes on the Purchased Assets other than for taxes not yet due and payable.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, carriers, landlords and employees. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not (a) sell or encumber any Purchased Asset other than inventory of supplies in the ordinary course, (b) amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Material Contract, or (c) increase the compensation of any employee of the Business other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Facilities, books, records and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Required Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each Required Consent before the Closing. Seller shall not be required to pay any fee or grant any concession to obtain any Required Consent.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets.

**6.5 Further Assurances.** Following the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

**6.6 Notice of Developments.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction (or Buyer's waiver) of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing;

(c) Seller shall have obtained each of the Required Consents listed on Schedule 7.2(c), in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(d) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), (b) and (e);

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each of the items in Section 3.5.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction (or Seller's waiver) of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and (c) Buyer shall have delivered a certificate of an officer of Buyer to that effect and each of the items in Section 3.6.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; (c) by Buyer if Seller has materially breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has materially breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect, except for this Section 8.2 and Article XI, and except that no termination shall relieve a Party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties of the Parties in this Agreement (other than the Environmental Representations and the Fundamental Representations) shall survive the Closing until 12 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The Environmental Representations shall survive the Closing until the expiration of the Escrow Period (as defined in the Escrow Agreement).

(c) The Fundamental Representations shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants and agreements shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(d) No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or 9.3(a) may be asserted after the expiration of the applicable survival period, except that any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before such expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement, or any Assumed Liability; or

(c) the Transferred Customer Contracts, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing the claim in reasonable detail, provided that failure to give such notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2 shall not exceed $4,200,000 (10% of the Purchase Price) (the "**Cap**").

(b) The Cap shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud, breach of Fundamental Representations, and Excluded Liabilities.

(c) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for breach of Fundamental Representations or for Fraud) until the aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceed $210,000, after which Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.

(d) Buyer's obligations under Section 9.3 shall not be subject to the Cap or any other limitation.

(e) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of the relevant claim, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any amount owed by Seller under this Article IX shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Amount, to the extent then available under the Escrow Agreement, and then directly by Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement, other than claims for Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under the Ancillary Agreements in accordance with their terms.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** Any indemnification payment under this Article IX shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**10.1 Offers of Employment.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to the employees of the Business listed on Schedule 10.1, on terms determined by Buyer. Each such employee who accepts and commences employment with Buyer is a "**Transferred Employee**."

**10.2 Key Employee.** Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, the Key Employee has executed the Key Employee Agreement, to become effective as of the Closing.

**10.3 Seller Obligations.** Seller shall pay to each Transferred Employee, no later than the next regular payroll date following the Closing, all wages, accrued paid time off, bonuses and commissions earned through the Closing Date.

**10.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Article X shall confer any rights on any employee or other Person, or be construed as an amendment of any benefit plan.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement. Fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages (or such other address as a Party designates by notice).

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates or, after the Closing, to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Business, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.8 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** As between the Parties, in the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail over the Ancillary Agreements, except that the Escrow Agreement shall control on indemnification claims and limits.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
