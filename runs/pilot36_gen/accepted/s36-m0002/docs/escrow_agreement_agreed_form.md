# ESCROW AGREEMENT

*Agreed Form: Exhibit A to the Asset Purchase Agreement*

This **ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 by and among **Teivav ColdLink Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"); **Vaipith Refrigerated Logistics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"); and **Pellucid National Trust Company**, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 3, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller will sell to Buyer the assets of the Business, and Buyer will assume certain liabilities of the Business.

B. Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount will be held as security for Seller's obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $4,200,000, the amount deposited by Buyer under Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Escrow Amount together with all interest and earnings on it, less all disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Escrow Period**" means the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on the date that is 90 days after the Closing Date.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement. An order or judgment is non-appealable once the time to appeal has expired without an appeal being filed.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, at any time, the aggregate amount of all claims set out in Claim Notices delivered before the end of the Escrow Period that have not then been paid or finally resolved under this Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, *mutatis mutandis*. References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless they state otherwise.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT; ESCROW ACCOUNT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. The Escrow Agent agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** On or before the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall establish a segregated, non-commingled account designated "Teivav/Vaipith Indemnity Escrow." The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in that account.

**2.3 No Interest in Purchase Agreement.** The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not bound by it. The Escrow Agent has no duty to determine whether any Party has complied with the Purchase Agreement. References in this Agreement to the Purchase Agreement do not create any duty of the Escrow Agent beyond those expressly set forth in this Agreement.

## 3. DEPOSIT

**3.1 Deposit of Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the Escrow Account.

**3.2 Confirmation.** Promptly after it receives the Escrow Amount, the Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller.

**3.3 Purpose.** The Escrow Funds secure Seller's obligations to indemnify the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement. "**Buyer Indemnitees**" means the Persons indemnified under that Section. The Escrow Funds are the first source of recovery for those obligations under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Pellucid Government Obligations Money Market Fund, or in any other investment specified in Joint Written Instructions. Joint Written Instructions may specify only:

(a) direct obligations of the United States government;

(b) money market funds investing solely in such obligations; or

(c) insured demand deposit accounts.

**4.2 Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds. For tax purposes, Seller shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds. Before the Closing, Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9.

**4.3 No Liability for Investment Losses.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 4, except to the extent the loss results from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 5. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

**5.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the expiration of the Escrow Period, Buyer may deliver a written notice of a claim for indemnification under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement (a "**Claim Notice**"). Buyer shall deliver the Claim Notice to Seller, with a copy to the Escrow Agent. Each Claim Notice shall state:

(a) in reasonable detail, the nature of the claim;

(b) the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which the claim is made; and

(c) the amount of Losses claimed, or Buyer's good-faith estimate of that amount if it cannot then be determined.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a claim. To do so, Seller must deliver a written notice of objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after the Claim Notice is delivered. The Objection Notice shall state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the amount in dispute.

**5.3 Uncontested Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period, Seller is deemed to have accepted the claim in full. In that case, on the fifth Business Day after the 30-day period expires, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Funds, the amount stated in the Claim Notice. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed portion to Buyer on the same timetable.

**5.4 Disputed Claims.** If a timely Objection Notice is delivered, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days to resolve the disputed amount. The Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount only as provided in either of the following:

(a) Joint Written Instructions; or

(b) a Final Order delivered to the Escrow Agent together with a certificate of the delivering Party stating that the order is final and non-appealable.

**5.5 Disbursements Generally.** The Escrow Agent shall make every disbursement under this Agreement by wire transfer to the account designated in writing by the recipient. Each disbursement is subject to the security procedures in Section 8.6.

## 6. INDEMNIFICATION CLAIMS AND LIMITS

**6.1 Claims Payable from Escrow Funds.** Buyer and Seller agree that the Escrow Funds are available to satisfy any claim of a Buyer Indemnitee that is finally determined or agreed under Section 5. Payment from the Escrow Funds reduces, dollar for dollar, the amount Seller owes in respect of that claim. Amounts that are finally determined or agreed and that exceed the available Escrow Funds remain payable directly by Seller, subject to Section 6.3.

**6.2 Deductible.** Claims under Section 9.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement are payable only once aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $210,000. Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller shall be liable from the first dollar. This Section 6.2 does not apply to claims for breach of Fundamental Representations or for Fraud.

**6.3 Cap.** The aggregate liability of Seller for indemnification under the Purchase Agreement shall not exceed $4,200,000 (10% of the Purchase Price) (the "**Cap**"). The Cap shall apply to all claims under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement other than claims for Fraud or breach of Fundamental Representations.

**6.4 Insurance Proceeds.** Losses claimed under this Agreement shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Buyer Indemnitee for the relevant claim, less the costs of recovery.

**6.5 Escrow Agent Not Responsible.** The Escrow Agent has no duty to calculate, apply or monitor the deductible in Section 6.2 or the Cap in Section 6.3. The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on Claim Notices, Joint Written Instructions and Final Orders delivered under Section 5.

## 7. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**7.1 Release at End of Escrow Period.** Within five Business Days after the expiration of the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds to Seller, less the Pending Claim Amount. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount.

**7.2 Release of Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse each portion of the Pending Claim Amount once the related claim is resolved under Section 5. Any portion awarded to Buyer shall be paid to Buyer. Any remainder that is no longer subject to a pending claim shall be paid to Seller.

**7.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding Sections 5 and 7.1, the Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**7.4 Termination.** This Agreement terminates once all Escrow Funds have been disbursed. Sections 8 and 9 survive termination.

## 8. THE ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set forth in this Agreement. Those duties are ministerial in nature, and no implied duties apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a fiduciary of any Party.

**8.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely, and shall be protected in acting or refraining from acting, on any written notice, instruction, certificate or order that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into the validity or accuracy of any such document.

**8.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except to the extent of its gross negligence or willful misconduct. The Escrow Agent shall in no event be liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**8.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and its officers, directors, employees and agents against all losses, claims, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of this Agreement, jointly and severally, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 8.4. This allocation does not apply to the extent an amount results from the breach of this Agreement by Buyer or Seller, in which case the breaching Party shall bear that amount.

**8.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is otherwise uncertain of its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may deposit the Escrow Funds with the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware in an interpleader action.

**8.6 Security Procedures.** Before making any disbursement by wire transfer, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the transfer instructions by telephone call-back to a person designated on Schedule 1, other than the person who delivered the instructions. The Escrow Agent may rely on the confirmation of any designated person.

**8.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. Resignation or removal takes effect only when a successor escrow agent has accepted the appointment and received the Escrow Funds. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one.

## 9. FEES AND EXPENSES

**9.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 2. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees, consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**9.2 Expenses.** Buyer and Seller shall reimburse the Escrow Agent, in equal shares, for its reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing its duties under this Agreement.

**10.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**10.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as permitted for Buyer under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or with which it is consolidated, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**10.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.5 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement. If that court lacks jurisdiction, each party submits instead to the exclusive jurisdiction of the federal courts located in the State of Delaware. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.6 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification requirements.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
