## 2. Background

2.1 The Asset Purchase Agreement with Vaipith Refrigerated Logistics, Inc. (the "**APA**") was signed on March 3, 2026. The Purchase Price is $42,000,000. Closing has not yet occurred.

2.2 The acquired Business is temperature-controlled warehousing, refrigerated and frozen freight transportation, and cross-dock services, as defined in APA Section 1.1. The working group will plan integration across all three service lines.

2.3 Under APA Section 8.1(b), the outside date is June 30, 2026. The working group is planning against a target Closing in late May 2026. The final date depends on satisfaction of the Article VII conditions.

2.4 No information about Seller's customer pricing or customer contract terms is to be shared beyond the clean-team members until Closing. All pre-Closing contact with Seller personnel goes through the access arrangements in APA Section 6.2.

## 3. Day One Readiness: Overview

3.1 The Integration Lead walked through the Day One checklist. The goal is uninterrupted service to customers at both Facilities and across the reefer fleet from the first shift after Closing.

3.2 The workstreams are Finance, Fleet, IT and Systems, People, Facilities, and Insurance. Each workstream owner will keep a separate tracker in the shared PMO folder.

3.3 Workstream owners confirmed they have reviewed the Purchased Assets list in APA Section 2.1 and Schedule 2.1(b) as it relates to their areas.

## 4. Finance Workstream

4.1 Funds flow. VP Finance confirmed the Closing funds flow mechanics:

(a) payment to Seller of the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount, under APA Section 3.2; and

(b) deposit of the $4,200,000 Escrow Amount with Pellucid National Trust Company, under APA Section 3.3.

4.2 Escrow onboarding. Pellucid's know-your-customer package has been requested. Treasury will complete Teivav's portion. Treasury will also nominate two call-back contacts for Schedule 1 to the Escrow Agreement. Fees are to be split equally with Seller, consistent with APA Section 11.1.

4.3 Accounts receivable. Receivables of the Business outstanding at Closing transfer to Teivav under APA Section 2.1(a). VP Finance will set up a lockbox and send customer remittance-change notices on Day One.

4.4 Assumed payables. Teivav is assuming only the trade payables shown on the Closing statement, under APA Sections 2.3(b) and 3.5(h). AP will not load any other Seller vendor balances.

4.5 Purchase price allocation. Teivav must deliver the Section 1060 allocation statement within 90 days after the Closing Date, under APA Section 2.6. VP Finance will engage the valuation firm before Closing so the fleet and refrigeration equipment appraisals are ready on time.

## 5. Fleet Workstream

5.1 Titles and registrations. The Director of Fleet confirmed that certificates of title for all titled vehicles are a Seller Closing deliverable under APA Section 3.5(f). Teivav's title agent is on standby to re-title and re-register in New Jersey and Pennsylvania within the state deadlines.

5.2 Operating authority. Teivav will run the acquired fleet under its own USDOT number and MC authority from Day One. Fleet will update the authority filings and insurance filings (BMC-91X) before Closing, effective on the Closing Date.

5.3 Telematics and temperature monitoring. Reefer unit telematics accounts will be migrated to Teivav's platform. Continuous temperature data must be maintained throughout the cutover, so that customer temperature records do not have any gap. Fleet will run a parallel feed for the first 14 days.

5.4 Owner-operators. Owner-operator lease agreements need no consent to assignment (Schedule 4.7, item 5). Fleet will send each owner-operator a welcome letter and Teivav's settlement and onboarding packet in the first week after Closing.

5.5 Equipment lease. The reefer trailer Master Equipment Lease requires notice only (Schedule 4.7, item 4). Fleet will prepare the notice for delivery at Closing.

## 6. IT and Systems Workstream

6.1 WMS cutover. Seller's warehouse management system data is a Purchased Asset under APA Section 2.1(f). The Director of IT proposed the following approach:

(a) a 90-day transition period, during which the existing WMS instance runs on Teivav's infrastructure; and

(b) migration of both Facilities to Teivav's WMS after the peak-season freeze is lifted.

6.2 Software licenses. IT will confirm which WMS and routing software licenses are transferable. Any non-transferable license will need a replacement license that takes effect at Closing.

6.3 Records. Maintenance logs, temperature logs, refrigerant leak records and compliance files transfer under APA Section 2.1(e). IT will set up a read-only archive so that operations and EHS staff can access these records on Day One.

6.4 Email and identity. Transferred Employees will be provisioned with Teivav accounts before Closing, to be activated on the Closing Date.

## 7. People Workstream

7.1 Offers. The HR Business Partner confirmed that offer letters will go to the employees listed on Schedule 10.1, effective as of Closing, under APA Section 10.1.

7.2 Seller payroll. Seller remains responsible for wages, accrued PTO, bonuses and commissions earned through the Closing Date, under APA Section 10.3. HR will not cash out or carry over any Seller PTO balances.

7.3 Key Employee. Dashiell Okonkwo-Brandt will join as Senior Vice President, Operations, effective on the Closing Date, reporting to the CEO and based in Linden, New Jersey. His remit covers facility operations, refrigerated fleet operations, transportation and dispatch, and cross-dock operations. HR will coordinate his onboarding schedule and will set up equipment-grant paperwork for Board approval.

7.4 Benefits. Transferred Employees are to be enrolled in Teivav plans with coverage effective on the Closing Date. HR will confirm with the carrier that waiting periods are waived.

8.3 Utilities and service vendors. Facilities will prepare transfer requests for utilities, refrigeration maintenance contractors and pest control, effective on the Closing Date.
