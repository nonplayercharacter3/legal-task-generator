# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**THIS DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**")

**BETWEEN:**

**LASEISK AUTO REMARKETING, LLC**, a limited liability company with its principal place of business in the State of New York ("**Supplier**"); and

**KONAIS DEALER NETWORKS, INC.**, a corporation with its principal place of business in the State of New Jersey ("**Distributor**"),

(each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier operates physical wholesale used-vehicle auctions and a simulcast online bidding platform. Through the platform, licensed dealers may buy and sell vehicles in Supplier's auction lanes and online events.

B. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, to market, sell and administer Auction Access Subscriptions to dealers on the terms of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Amendment No. 1**" means any written amendment to this Agreement that the Parties designate as Amendment No. 1 and that is executed by both Parties in accordance with Section 17.4.

1.3 "**Auction Access Subscription**" means a subscription licence, issued by Supplier, that entitles a Dealer to register as a buyer, a seller or both in Supplier's physical auction lanes, simulcast sales and online bidding events. Each subscription is subject to Supplier's then-current dealer terms of use.

1.4 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

1.5 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive 12-month period beginning on the Distribution Agreement Effective Date. For this purpose, the "Distribution Agreement Effective Date" is the Effective Date of this Agreement.

1.6 "**Dealer**" means a motor vehicle dealer that holds a valid dealer licence issued by the relevant state, provincial or other governmental authority.

1.7 "**Dealer Data**" means all information relating to Dealers, their personnel, transaction history, bidding activity, credit and floorplan status, and account details that a Party receives or generates in connection with this Agreement.

1.8 "**Dealer Subscription**" means one Auction Access Subscription sold by Distributor to a Dealer, activated by Supplier, and paid for in full under Section 6.

1.9 "**Minimum Annual Volume**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

1.10 "**Permitted Assignee**" means, for a Party, (a) any Affiliate of that Party, or (b) any person acquiring all or substantially all of its assets or of the business to which this Agreement relates, whether by merger, consolidation, sale of assets, sale of equity or otherwise.

1.11 "**Platform**" means Supplier's auction management systems, simulcast bidding platform, online auction marketplace and related dealer portals, as modified by Supplier from time to time.

1.12 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule agreed between the Parties for Platform uptime and condition-report turnaround, as amended from time to time.

1.13 "**Side Letter**" means the side letter between the Parties dated the Effective Date and executed together with this Agreement.

1.14 "**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 14.1.

1.15 "**Territory**" means the United States and Canada.

1.16 "**Wholesale Price**" means the price payable by Distributor to Supplier for each Dealer Subscription, as set out in Schedule A, as amended from time to time.

## 2. INTERPRETATION

2.1 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. "Including" means "including without limitation". References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of, and schedules to, this Agreement.

2.2 References to "days" mean calendar days unless business days are specified.

## 3. APPOINTMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of Auction Access Subscriptions in the Territory through all wholesale remarketing channels, including physical auction lanes, simulcast sales and online-only auctions. Distributor accepts the appointment.

3.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller of Auction Access Subscriptions in the Territory, or (b) sell, license or otherwise supply Auction Access Subscriptions directly to Dealers in the Territory.

3.3 **Distributor Conduct.** Distributor shall market and sell Auction Access Subscriptions only under the subscription terms and pricing permitted by this Agreement. Distributor shall not make any representation, warranty or commitment on Supplier's behalf beyond those authorised in writing by Supplier.

3.4 **Relationship.** Distributor buys Dealer Subscriptions from Supplier and resells them for its own account. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship between the Parties. Neither Party may bind the other.

## 4. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Supplier shall:

(a) activate each Auction Access Subscription ordered by Distributor within two business days after it receives a complete Dealer application and the related order;

(b) operate and maintain the Platform in accordance with the Service Level Schedule;

(c) provide Distributor with reasonable sales and marketing materials, product training and access to a Supplier account representative; and

(d) give Distributor at least 30 days' prior written notice of any material change to the Auction Access Subscription features or dealer terms of use.

4.2 Supplier retains sole discretion over Dealer eligibility, credit approval, auction rules, fees charged to Dealers for vehicle transactions, and the suspension or termination of any Dealer for breach of the dealer terms of use.

## 5. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

5.1 Distributor shall:

(a) use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell Auction Access Subscriptions in the Territory;

(b) maintain a trained sales and support staff sufficient to perform its obligations;

(c) collect and verify each Dealer's licence and onboarding documents before submitting an order to Supplier;

(d) provide first-line account support to Dealers to whom it has sold Dealer Subscriptions; and

(e) comply with all applicable laws in performing this Agreement, including laws relating to motor vehicle dealer licensing, consumer protection, anti-corruption and data privacy.

5.2 Distributor shall not, without Supplier's prior written consent, modify, rebrand or bundle Auction Access Subscriptions with any third-party product or service.

## 6. ORDERS, PRICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Orders.** Distributor shall submit orders for Dealer Subscriptions through the Platform distributor portal. Each order is subject to Supplier's acceptance, which Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold where the Dealer meets Supplier's eligibility criteria.

6.2 **Wholesale Price.** Distributor shall pay Supplier the Wholesale Price for each Dealer Subscription. Distributor may set its own resale prices to Dealers.

6.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor monthly in arrears for all Dealer Subscriptions activated during the preceding calendar month.

6.4 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within 30 days after receipt. Late amounts bear interest at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

6.5 **Disputes.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any good-faith invoice dispute within 15 days after receipt, with reasonable supporting detail. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

6.6 **Taxes.** Amounts payable under this Agreement exclude sales, use, goods and services and similar taxes. Distributor shall pay all such taxes, other than taxes on Supplier's net income.

## 7. RECORDS AND REPORTING

7.1 Within 10 business days after the end of each calendar month, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a report of Dealer Subscriptions sold, renewals, cancellations and the pipeline of pending Dealer applications.

7.2 Distributor shall keep complete and accurate records relating to this Agreement for at least three years after the end of the Contract Year to which they relate. Supplier may audit those records, at its own cost, no more than once per Contract Year, on 15 business days' notice and during normal business hours.

## 8. DEALER DATA

8.1 As between the Parties, Supplier owns all Dealer Data generated through the Platform. Distributor may use Dealer Data only to perform this Agreement.

8.2 Each Party shall process Dealer Data in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws. Each Party shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Dealer Data against unauthorised access or disclosure.

8.3 Each Party shall notify the other without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of any breach of security affecting Dealer Data.

## 9. MINIMUM ANNUAL VOLUME

9.1 **Minimum Annual Volume.** Distributor shall sell not less than 1,200 Dealer Subscriptions per Contract Year (the "**Minimum Annual Volume**").

9.2 **Shortfall Statement.** Within 15 days after the end of each Contract Year, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a written statement of the number of Dealer Subscriptions sold in that Contract Year.

9.3 **Conversion to Non-Exclusive.** If Distributor fails to achieve the Minimum Annual Volume in any Contract Year, Supplier may convert Distributor's appointment under Section 3.1 to a non-exclusive appointment, and Section 3.2 shall then cease to apply. Supplier exercises this right by written notice to Distributor given within 45 days after the end of the relevant Contract Year, failing which such right is waived for that Contract Year. Conversion takes effect on the date Distributor receives the notice.

9.4 **Exclusive Remedy.** Conversion under Section 9.3 is Supplier's sole remedy for a failure to achieve the Minimum Annual Volume. This Section 9.4 does not limit Supplier's rights under Section 14.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 Supplier grants Distributor a non-transferable, non-sublicensable licence during the Term to use Supplier's names, logos and trademarks in the Territory, solely to market Auction Access Subscriptions under this Agreement and in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines.

10.2 Supplier retains all right, title and interest in the Platform, the Auction Access Subscriptions and its trademarks. All goodwill from Distributor's use of Supplier's trademarks inures to Supplier.

10.3 Distributor shall not register or use any trademark, domain name or social media handle that is confusingly similar to any of Supplier's trademarks.

## 11. WARRANTIES

11.1 Each Party represents and warrants that (a) it is duly organised and in good standing; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and (c) its performance will not breach any agreement to which it is a party.

11.2 Supplier warrants that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with its published documentation and the Service Level Schedule. Distributor's remedies for failure to meet the service levels are as stated in the Service Level Schedule.

11.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, financial and Dealer information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Wholesale Prices, Dealer Data, auction volumes and Platform roadmaps.

12.2 The receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement; (b) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers and contractors who need to know it and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 12; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

12.3 These obligations do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if legally compelled to do so, provided it gives prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful.

12.4 This Section 12 survives for three years after expiry or termination of this Agreement. Obligations relating to Dealer Data and trade secrets survive for as long as the information remains confidential.

## 13. INDEMNITY AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Each Party shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the other Party and its Affiliates from third-party claims arising from (a) its breach of Section 8 or Section 12; (b) its gross negligence or wilful misconduct; or (c) its violation of applicable law.

13.2 Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor from third-party claims that the Platform, as provided by Supplier, infringes any United States or Canadian patent, copyright or trademark.

13.3 EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY UNDER SECTION 13.1 OR 13.2 OR FOR BREACH OF SECTION 12, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

13.4 EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY UNDER SECTION 13.1 OR 13.2 OR FOR BREACH OF SECTION 12, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN ANY CONTRACT YEAR SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL WHOLESALE PRICES PAID AND PAYABLE IN THE PRECEDING 12 MONTHS.

## 14. TERM AND TERMINATION

14.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three Contract Years. It then renews automatically for successive one-Contract-Year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term (the initial term and all renewals, the "**Term**").

14.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

14.3 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

14.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination, (a) all unpaid amounts become due; (b) Distributor shall stop marketing Auction Access Subscriptions and using Supplier's trademarks; (c) existing Dealer Subscriptions continue for the remainder of their current paid term, after which Supplier may contract directly with the Dealers; and (d) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, subject to Section 12.4.

14.5 **Survival.** Sections 6, 7.2, 8, 12, 13, 14.4, 14.5 and 17 survive expiry or termination.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, war, terrorism, civil unrest, governmental action, labour disputes (other than those involving the affected Party's own workforce), and widespread failures of public utilities or telecommunications networks.

15.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event. If a force majeure event continues for more than 60 days, either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

16.1 This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

16.2 Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either party may assign this Agreement to a Permitted Assignee without consent. The assigning Party shall give the other Party written notice of the assignment within 10 business days after it takes effect. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 16.2 is void.

16.3 Distributor may use sub-agents or subcontractors in the Territory with Supplier's prior written consent, which Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold. Distributor remains responsible for their acts and omissions.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

17.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

17.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency between the documents comprising the arrangement between the Parties, the documents prevail in the following order of precedence: Amendment No. 1, then Distribution Agreement, then Service Level Schedule, then Side Letter.

17.4 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

17.5 **Waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right, except as expressly provided in this Agreement.

17.6 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognised overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses each Party designates in writing. A notice is effective on receipt.

17.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be replaced with a valid provision that comes closest to the Parties' original intent.

17.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
