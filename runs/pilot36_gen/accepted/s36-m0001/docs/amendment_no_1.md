# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**THIS AMENDMENT NO. 1** (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of May 20, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**")

**BETWEEN:**

**LASEISK AUTO REMARKETING, LLC**, a limited liability company with its principal place of business in the State of New York ("**Supplier**"); and

**KONAIS DEALER NETWORKS, INC.**, a corporation with its principal place of business in the State of New Jersey ("**Distributor**"),

(each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor to market, sell and administer Auction Access Subscriptions to Dealers.

B. Distributor's statement under Section 9.2 of the Agreement reports that Distributor sold 940 Dealer Subscriptions in the first Contract Year, against the Minimum Annual Volume of 1,200 Dealer Subscriptions per Contract Year in Section 9.1. Distributor has stated that the shortfall is excused by a Platform outage that occurred during that Contract Year. Supplier disputes that position.

C. The Parties wish to revise the Wholesale Prices in Schedule A to the Agreement. They intend this Amendment to be Amendment No. 1 for the purposes of the Agreement, executed in accordance with Section 17.4.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 Section 2 of the Agreement (Interpretation) applies to this Amendment as if set out in full.

## 2. REVISED WHOLESALE PRICES

2.1 **Replacement Table.** With effect from June 1, 2026 (the "**Pricing Effective Date**"), the table in Schedule A to the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

| Subscription Type | Wholesale Price (USD, per Dealer Subscription, per 12-month term) |
|---|---|
| Buyer Subscription | $1,095 |
| Seller Subscription | $1,285 |
| Buyer/Seller Combined Subscription | $1,995 |

2.2 **Application.** The revised Wholesale Prices apply to each Dealer Subscription activated by Supplier on or after the Pricing Effective Date. Dealer Subscriptions activated before the Pricing Effective Date remain at the Wholesale Prices in effect on their activation date for the remainder of their current paid term.

2.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall apply the revised Wholesale Prices in the first monthly invoice issued under Section 6.3 of the Agreement after the Pricing Effective Date, for Dealer Subscriptions activated in June 2026.

2.4 **Annual Adjustment.** The text following the table in Schedule A continues to apply. The revision in Section 2.1 is agreed under Section 17.4 of the Agreement and is not an adjustment by Supplier for the purposes of Schedule A. Supplier's right to make one adjustment per Contract Year on 60 days' written notice applies to the revised Wholesale Prices from the start of the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2027.

## 3. CONTRACT YEAR 1 VOLUME

3.1 The Parties acknowledge the matters recorded in Recital B. Supplier reserves all rights under Section 9.3 of the Agreement in respect of the first Contract Year.

3.2 Distributor shall provide Supplier, within 15 business days after the Amendment Date, with a written summary of the Platform outage on which it relies and the Dealer applications it says were affected. Supplier shall make available to Distributor the relevant extracts of its monitoring data under Section 6.3 of the Service Level Schedule.

3.3 Senior commercial representatives of the Parties shall meet within 30 days after the Amendment Date to discuss the matters in Recital B and Distributor's sales plan for the second Contract Year.

## 4. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

4.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

4.2 From the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS

5.1 Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly authorised and executed by it.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Section 17.2 (Jurisdiction) of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this Amendment.

6.2 **Notices.** Section 17.6 of the Agreement applies to notices under this Amendment.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
