# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**THIS MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT** (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of November 4, 2024 (the "**NDA Effective Date**")

**BETWEEN:**

**LASEISK AUTO REMARKETING, LLC**, a limited liability company with its principal place of business in the State of New York ("**Laseisk**"); and

**KONAIS DEALER NETWORKS, INC.**, a corporation with its principal place of business in the State of New Jersey ("**Konais**"),

(each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**").

## RECITALS

A. Laseisk operates physical wholesale used-vehicle auctions and a simulcast online bidding platform. Konais provides sales, onboarding and account services to motor vehicle dealers.

B. The Parties wish to exchange certain confidential information to evaluate and negotiate a possible arrangement under which Konais would distribute Laseisk's dealer auction access subscriptions (the "**Purpose**").

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this NDA, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, financial, operational and customer information that a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Representatives disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and in any form. It includes pricing, auction and sales volumes, dealer and customer lists, platform architecture and roadmaps, business plans, and the fact and content of the Parties' discussions. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, professional advisers, lenders and prospective financing sources.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 The Receiving Party shall:

(a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

(b) not disclose Confidential Information to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are informed of its confidential nature and bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this NDA; and

(c) protect Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event with no less than reasonable care.

2.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information. It shall also cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit the consequences.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this NDA;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before its disclosure under this NDA, without obligation of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party that is not under an obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a securities exchange to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so. Where lawful, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy, and it shall cooperate reasonably with any such effort at the Disclosing Party's cost. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. DEALER AND PERSONAL INFORMATION

5.1 Neither Party expects to disclose personal information about individual dealers or their personnel for the Purpose. If any such information is disclosed, the Receiving Party shall process it only for the Purpose and in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws, and shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect it.

## 6. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within 15 business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party ends discussions about the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession. On request, it shall confirm destruction in writing.

6.2 The Receiving Party may retain copies (a) as required by law or its bona fide document retention policies, or (b) held in automatic electronic back-up systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this NDA.

## 7. NO LICENCE, WARRANTY OR COMMITMENT

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any licence or other right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

7.2 CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS". The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

7.3 This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any further agreement. Neither Party is bound by any arrangement relating to the Purpose unless and until a definitive written agreement is signed by both Parties.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

8.1 For 12 months after the NDA Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact in connection with the Purpose. This Section 8.1 does not restrict general solicitations of employment not specifically directed at the other Party's employees, or hiring any person who responds to them.

## 9. TERM

9.1 This NDA covers Confidential Information disclosed during the period of two years after the NDA Effective Date.

9.2 The Receiving Party's obligations under this NDA continue for three years after the end of the period in Section 9.1. Obligations relating to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 10. REMEDIES

10.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive relief or specific performance, without the need to post a bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

11.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business that agrees in writing to be bound by this NDA.

11.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

11.5 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognised overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses each Party designates in writing. A notice is effective on receipt.

11.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

11.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
