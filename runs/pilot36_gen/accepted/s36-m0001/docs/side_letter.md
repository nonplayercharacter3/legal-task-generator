# SIDE LETTER

**LASEISK AUTO REMARKETING, LLC**

March 1, 2025

**KONAIS DEALER NETWORKS, INC.**
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re: Distribution Agreement dated March 1, 2025, Commercial Scope**

Dear Sirs and Madams,

We refer to the Distribution Agreement between Laseisk Auto Remarketing, LLC ("**Supplier**") and Konais Dealer Networks, Inc. ("**Distributor**") dated March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), which the Parties are signing today together with this letter.

## 1. PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 This letter is the Side Letter referred to in Section 1.13 of the Agreement. It records the commercial scope of Distributor's appointment as discussed and agreed between the Parties in the letter of intent they exchanged before the Effective Date.

1.2 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. Section 2 of the Agreement (Interpretation) applies to this letter.

## 2. AGREED EXCLUSIVE SCOPE

2.1 The Parties confirm that the exclusive scope agreed between them for Distributor's appointment is franchised dealers in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

2.2 In this letter, "**franchised dealer**" means a Dealer that holds a current franchise or sales and service agreement with a motor vehicle manufacturer or distributor to sell new vehicles of that manufacturer's or distributor's brand.

2.3 Distributor shall focus its sales and marketing efforts under Section 5.1(a) of the Agreement on the scope described in Section 2.1.

## 3. LAUNCH AND SALES PLAN

3.1 Within 30 days after the Effective Date, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a written launch plan for the first Contract Year. The plan shall set out its target franchised dealer groups, sales staffing, onboarding timetable and the expected monthly run-rate of Dealer Subscriptions measured against the Minimum Annual Volume in Section 9.1 of the Agreement.

3.2 Supplier shall nominate an account representative under Section 4.1(c) of the Agreement within 10 business days after the Effective Date. Supplier shall also make product training available to Distributor's sales staff before Distributor's first sales campaign.

3.3 The Parties shall review progress against the launch plan at the first quarterly review meeting held under Section 6.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The contents of this letter are Confidential Information of both Parties and are subject to Section 12 of the Agreement.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Governing Law.** This letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Section 17.2 (Jurisdiction) of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this letter.

5.2 **Amendment.** This letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

5.3 **Notices.** Section 17.6 of the Agreement applies to notices under this letter.

5.4 **Counterparts.** This letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

Please confirm Distributor's agreement to the terms of this letter by signing and returning a copy to us.
