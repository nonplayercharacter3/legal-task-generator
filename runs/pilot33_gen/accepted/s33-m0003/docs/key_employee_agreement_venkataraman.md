# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 9, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Fupurt Valley Creamery, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Employer**"), and **Teodora Venkataraman**, an individual (the "**Executive**"). The Employer and the Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Employer produces organic dairy products and artisanal cheeses in the Hudson Valley region of New York.

B. The Executive has specialized knowledge of cheese-make protocols, culture selection, affinage and cave management. That knowledge is central to the Employer's product quality and reputation.

C. The Employer wishes to employ the Executive as its Master Cheesemaker, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**1.1 Position.** The Employer employs the Executive as Master Cheesemaker, reporting to the Employer's President. The Executive accepts that employment.

**1.2 Duties.** The Executive is responsible for the following:

(a) all cheese production, including recipe development, culture and rennet selection, make schedules, brining, aging and cave management;

(b) quality control and lot release for all cheese Products;

(c) supervision and training of the cheese-make and affinage staff; and

(d) such other duties consistent with her position as the President reasonably assigns.

**1.3 Full Time; Outside Activities.** The Executive shall devote her full business time and efforts to the Employer. She may judge competitions, teach and write on cheesemaking, and serve on industry and nonprofit boards. Those activities must not materially interfere with her duties and must not violate Section 7.

**1.4 Place of Performance.** The Executive shall perform her duties principally at the Employer's creamery and aging caves in the Hudson Valley, and at the Employer's offices in Kingston, New York. She shall travel as reasonably required for the business.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** The term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of four years. After the initial term, it renews automatically for successive one-year periods. Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. The initial term and all renewal terms together are the "**Term**." Employment may end earlier under Section 6.

## 3. COMPENSATION

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Employer shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $265,000, payable in accordance with the Employer's regular payroll practices. The Board of Directors of the Employer (the "**Board**") shall review the base salary at least annually and may increase it but not decrease it. The base salary in effect at any time is the "**Base Salary**."

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, the Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 25% of Base Salary. The bonus is based on production yield, quality and award objectives set by the Board after consultation with the Executive. Any bonus earned is paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates.

**3.3 Retention Award.** The Employer shall pay the Executive a retention award of $60,000 on each of the second and fourth anniversaries of the Effective Date, provided she is employed by the Employer on the applicable anniversary.

## 4. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**4.1 Benefits.** The Executive may participate in all employee benefit plans that the Employer maintains for its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.2 Paid Time Off.** The Executive is entitled to 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. Paid time off accrues and carries over in accordance with Employer policy.

**4.3 Expenses.** The Employer shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Employer policy. This includes travel to competitions, trade shows and supplier farms.

**4.4 Professional Development.** The Employer shall reimburse up to $10,000 per year for professional education, certification and industry association dues.

## 5. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND INVENTIONS

**5.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Employer, including recipes, cheese-make protocols, culture strains, affinage methods, supplier and customer lists, pricing and financial information. During and after her employment, the Executive shall not use or disclose Confidential Information except in performing her duties or as required by law.

**5.2 Work Product.** All recipes, protocols, processes, strains, formulations and other work product that the Executive develops during her employment and within the scope of her duties are the Employer's sole property ("**Work Product**"). The Executive assigns to the Employer all right, title and interest in Work Product. She shall execute such documents as the Employer reasonably requests to confirm that assignment.

**5.3 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, the Executive shall return all Employer property and Confidential Information in her possession.

**5.4 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any Governmental Authority, or from making other disclosures protected by whistleblower laws.

## 6. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**6.1 Termination by the Employer.**

(a) *For Cause.* The Employer may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause by written notice. "**Cause**" means any of the following:

  (i) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony;

  (ii) the Executive's willful misconduct or gross negligence that materially harms the Employer, including a knowing violation of food safety law;

  (iii) the Executive's material breach of Section 5 or Section 7; or

  (iv) the Executive's willful and continued failure to perform her material duties, not cured within 30 days after written notice describing the failure.

(b) *Without Cause.* The Employer may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause on 30 days' written notice.

**6.2 Resignation for Good Reason.** The Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means the occurrence of any of the following without the Executive's written agreement:

(a) a material diminution in the Executive's title, authority, duties or responsibilities, including removal of her authority over cheese production or lot release;

(b) any reduction in Base Salary or target bonus;

(c) a relocation of the Executive's principal place of performance by more than 50 miles from Kingston, New York;

(d) the Employer's material breach of this Agreement; or

(e) any assignment or purported assignment of this Agreement in violation of Section 9.2.

To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give the Employer written notice of the condition within 90 days after she first learns of it. Her resignation then takes effect on a date she specifies, not less than 30 days and not more than 60 days after that notice.

**6.3 Severance on Termination Without Cause or Resignation for Good Reason.** This Section 6.3 applies if the Employer terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or if the Executive resigns for Good Reason. In either case, the Executive is entitled to the following:

(a) all Accrued Obligations;

(b) severance equal to 18 months of base salary, at the rate of Base Salary in effect immediately before termination (disregarding any reduction constituting Good Reason), payable in equal installments on the Employer's regular payroll dates over the 18 months following the termination date;

(c) any annual bonus earned for the fiscal year completed before the termination date but not yet paid; and

(d) if the Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment of the employer portion of premiums for her and her covered dependents for 18 months after the termination date.

Payments under Sections 6.3(b) through 6.3(d) are conditioned on the Executive's execution, within 45 days after the termination date, of a general release of claims in customary form, and that release becoming irrevocable.

**6.4 Other Terminations.** This Section 6.4 applies on termination for Cause, resignation without Good Reason, death, or Disability. In each of those cases, the Executive (or her estate) is entitled only to the Accrued Obligations. "**Disability**" means the Executive's inability to perform her essential duties, with reasonable accommodation, for 120 consecutive days.

**6.5 Accrued Obligations.** "**Accrued Obligations**" means the following:

(a) Base Salary earned through the termination date;

(b) accrued and unused paid time off;

(c) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(d) vested benefits under the Employer's benefit plans, payable in accordance with their terms.

**6.6 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code. It shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. References to termination of employment mean a "separation from service" under Section 409A.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Acknowledgment.** The Executive acknowledges that she will have access to the Employer's trade secrets and customer goodwill. She further acknowledges that the covenants in this Section 7 are reasonable and necessary to protect those interests.

**7.2 Non-Competition.** During her employment and for 18 months after her employment ends (the "**Restricted Period**"), the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, act as an employee, consultant, owner or principal of any business that produces artisanal or specialty cheese for commercial sale within the States of New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, Massachusetts or Vermont. This Section 7.2 does not prohibit passive ownership of less than 2% of a publicly traded company.

**7.3 Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, do any of the following:

(a) solicit or induce any employee of the Employer to leave the Employer's employment;

(b) solicit any customer, distributor or retail account of the Employer to cease or reduce its business with the Employer; or

(c) solicit any dairy farm supplying milk to the Employer to cease or reduce that supply.

**7.4 Non-Disparagement.** During the Restricted Period, neither the Executive nor the Employer's officers and directors shall make public statements that disparage the other.

**7.5 Release on Good Reason Resignation.** If the Executive resigns for Good Reason, Sections 7.2 and 7.3 cease to apply as of the effective date of her resignation. Sections 5 and 7.4 continue to apply.

**7.6 Remedies; Reformation.** A breach of Section 5 or this Section 7 would cause the Employer irreparable harm. The Employer is therefore entitled to injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without posting a bond. If a court finds any covenant in this Section 7 unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 8. REPRESENTATIONS OF THE EXECUTIVE

**8.1 No Conflicting Obligations.** The Executive represents that she is not bound by any agreement that would prevent her from performing her duties under this Agreement. She further represents that she will not use or disclose any confidential information of a prior employer.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT

**9.1 By the Executive.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive. She may not assign it or delegate her duties under it. Her rights to payments under it pass to her estate on her death.

**9.2 By the Employer.** The Employer may not assign this Agreement, including in connection with a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of the Employer's assets, without the Executive's prior written consent.

**9.3 Successors.** Subject to Section 9.2, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective heirs, successors and permitted assigns.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is effective when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Employer go to its principal office, attention: President. Notices to the Executive go to the most recent home address on the Employer's records.

**10.2 Withholding.** The Employer may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**10.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**10.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to its conflict of law rules.

**10.6 Forum.** Any action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in New York, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**10.7 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement sets out the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Executive's employment and supersedes all prior offer letters and understandings on that subject.

**10.8 Survival.** Sections 5, 6.3, 6.5, 6.6, 7, 9 and 10 survive termination of the Executive's employment in accordance with their terms.

**10.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
