# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$3,200,000**

**Kingston, New York**

**Dated: ____________, 2026** (the "**Issue Date**")

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Sipoun Organic Holdings, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Fupurt Valley Creamery, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal amount set out in Section 1, together with interest as provided in this Note, on the terms of this Note.

This Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**") is the Seller Note issued under Section 3.2(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. PRINCIPAL

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $3,200,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount is issued in partial payment of the Purchase Price under Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Outstanding Principal.** "**Outstanding Principal**" means, at any time, the Principal Amount less all payments of principal made and all amounts set off against principal under Section 5, in each case before that time.

## 2. MATURITY

**2.1 Maturity Date.** The entire Outstanding Principal, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**").

**2.2 Business Days.** If any payment under this Note falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue to that day. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

## 3. INTEREST

**3.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the Outstanding Principal from the Issue Date until paid in full at a fixed rate of 6.0% per annum, calculated on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**3.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date to occur after the Issue Date, and on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Default Interest.** While an Event of Default is continuing, interest accrues on the Outstanding Principal at a rate of 8.0% per annum.

**3.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the Outstanding Principal or, if the Outstanding Principal has been paid, refunded to Maker.

## 4. PAYMENTS AND PREPAYMENT

**4.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing to Maker at least five Business Days before the payment date.

**4.2 Application.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to Outstanding Principal.

**4.3 Optional Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid.

## 5. SETOFF

**5.1 Right of Setoff.** Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under this Note the amount of any Losses to which Maker is entitled under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement, as provided in Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Notice of Setoff.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each setoff, stating the amount set off and the payment against which it is applied.

**5.3 Effect of Setoff.** Amounts set off under this Section 5 are treated for all purposes of this Note as paid to Holder on the date of the setoff. A setoff made in accordance with this Section 5 is not an Event of Default.

## 6. SUBORDINATION

**6.1 Subordination.** The payment of principal and interest under this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Indebtedness. "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all indebtedness of Maker for borrowed money owed to banks, insurance companies or other institutional lenders, whether outstanding on the Issue Date or incurred later, together with interest, fees and expenses on that indebtedness.

**6.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under any Senior Indebtedness, and the holder of that Senior Indebtedness has given Maker and Holder written notice of it, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note.

**6.3 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, on request of Maker, enter into a subordination agreement with any holder of Senior Indebtedness on terms customary for seller subordinated debt and consistent with this Section 6.

**6.4 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment in violation of this Section 6, Holder shall hold it in trust for the holders of Senior Indebtedness and promptly pay it over to them.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest within ten Business Days after it is due, in each case other than by reason of a setoff under Section 5 or a payment restriction under Section 6;

(b) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due;

(c) Maker commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law; or

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar law and is not dismissed within 60 days.

**7.2 Remedies.** On the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) that is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker and subject to Section 6, declare the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. On the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(b), 7.1(c) or 7.1(d), the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without notice or other action.

## 8. TRANSFER

**8.1 Restrictions.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker. Any transfer in violation of this Section 8.1 is void.

**8.2 Securities Laws.** This Note has not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law. It may not be transferred except in compliance with those laws.

**8.3 Assignment by Maker.** Maker may assign its obligations under this Note to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.3 of the Purchase Agreement, if that Person assumes this Note in writing.

## 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**9.1 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, protest and notice of dishonor of this Note.

**9.2 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**9.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. No delay or failure by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right.

**9.4 Costs of Collection.** If an Event of Default occurs and Holder engages counsel to collect this Note, Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

**9.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.6 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss and an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker.

**9.7 Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and benefits Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Note and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Forum.** Each of Maker and Holder irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action, suit or proceeding arising under or relating to this Note, including any dispute concerning payment, acceleration or any setoff against amounts due under this Note. If the Court of Chancery lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the action shall be brought in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each of Maker and Holder waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF MAKER AND HOLDER WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION, SUIT OR PROCEEDING ARISING UNDER OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.
