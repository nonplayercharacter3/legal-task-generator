# OFFICE LEASE

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is made as of September 1, 2022, by and between **Ostrander Mill Properties LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Fupurt Valley Creamery, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. PREMISES

**1.1 Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the office space known as Suite 204, 18 Ostrander Mill Road, Kingston, New York (the "**Premises**"). The Premises are located on the second floor of the building at that address (the "**Building**"). The Building, its land, parking areas and common areas are together the "**Property**."

**1.2 Rentable Area.** The Parties agree that the Premises contain 3,150 rentable square feet. The Parties will not remeasure the Premises during the Term.

**1.3 Common Areas.** Tenant and its employees and invitees may use the Building's lobbies, corridors, stairways, elevator, restrooms and other areas that Landlord designates for the common use of tenants (the "**Common Areas**"). That use is non-exclusive and is subject to the Rules and Regulations under Section 15.

**1.4 Parking.** Tenant may use up to 12 unreserved parking spaces in the Property's surface lot without additional charge.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** The term of this Lease begins on October 1, 2022 (the "**Commencement Date**") and ends on September 30, 2029 (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless ended earlier or extended under this Lease (the "**Term**").

**2.2 Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term for one additional period of five years (the "**Renewal Term**"). To do so, Tenant must give Landlord written notice at least nine months before the Expiration Date, and no Event of Default may be continuing when Tenant gives that notice. Base Rent for the first year of the Renewal Term is 95% of the fair market rent for comparable office space in Kingston, New York. If the Parties cannot agree on fair market rent within 30 days after Tenant's notice, each Party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten years' experience in the Kingston market. The two brokers shall select a third, and the fair market rent is the average of the two closest determinations. Base Rent then increases under Section 3.2 for each later year of the Renewal Term.

## 3. RENT

**3.1 Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord annual base rent of $78,750 ($25.00 per rentable square foot) for the first Lease Year ("**Base Rent**"). Base Rent is payable in equal monthly installments of $6,562.50, in advance, on the first day of each calendar month, without demand. Base Rent for any partial month is prorated. "**Lease Year**" means each 12-month period beginning on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it.

**3.2 Annual Escalation.** On the first day of each Lease Year after the first, Base Rent increases by 3% over the Base Rent for the preceding Lease Year.

**3.3 Additional Rent.** All amounts Tenant owes under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent together are "**Rent**."

**3.4 Late Charge.** If Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five days after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late charge of 4% of the overdue amount.

**3.5 Manner of Payment.** Tenant shall pay Rent by check or electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing.

## 4. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**4.1 Tenant's Share.** Tenant's proportionate share is 11.2%, being the rentable area of the Premises divided by the rentable area of the Building ("**Tenant's Share**").

**4.2 Operating Expenses.** For each calendar year after 2022 (the "**Base Year**"), Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for that year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year. "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, insure and repair the Property. Operating Expenses exclude capital improvements (other than those that reduce Operating Expenses, amortized over their useful life), debt service, leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, and costs Landlord recovers through insurance or from other tenants.

**4.3 Real Estate Taxes.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which real estate taxes assessed against the Property for that year exceed those for the Base Year.

**4.4 Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord may estimate the amounts due under Sections 4.2 and 4.3, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate monthly with Base Rent. Within 120 days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual amounts. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within 30 days after receiving the statement.

**4.5 Audit.** Tenant may inspect Landlord's records supporting each annual statement within 90 days after receiving it. If the inspection shows Landlord overstated the amounts due by more than 5%, Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the inspection.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**5.1 Deposit.** Tenant has deposited $13,125 with Landlord as security for its performance under this Lease (the "**Security Deposit**"). Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with New York General Obligations Law Section 7-103.

**5.2 Application.** If an Event of Default occurs, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure it. Tenant shall then restore the Security Deposit to its full amount within ten days after written demand.

**5.3 Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within 30 days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Section 18.

## 6. USE

**6.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for general administrative and executive offices and related sales and customer-service functions (the "**Permitted Use**"). Tenant shall not produce, process, age or store dairy products or other food at the Premises, other than ordinary employee refreshments.

**6.2 Compliance with Law.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, Common Areas and Building systems.

**6.3 Access.** Tenant has access to the Premises 24 hours a day, seven days a week, subject to Landlord's reasonable security procedures.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**7.1 Landlord Services.** Landlord shall furnish the following services as part of Operating Expenses:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on business days and from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays;

(b) electricity for normal office use;

(c) water to the Common Areas restrooms;

(d) janitorial service to the Premises and Common Areas five nights per week; and

(e) elevator service and snow and ice removal from the parking lot and walkways.

**7.2 After-Hours Service.** Landlord shall furnish heating and air conditioning outside the hours in Section 7.1(a) on reasonable advance request, at Landlord's actual cost.

**7.3 Interruption.** If an interruption of essential services that is within Landlord's reasonable control continues for more than five consecutive business days and renders the Premises untenantable, Rent abates from the sixth business day until the service is restored.

## 8. ALTERATIONS

**8.1 Consent Required.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, costing less than $15,000 in any Lease Year that do not affect Building structure or systems.

**8.2 Performance.** Tenant shall perform all alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with law, using contractors reasonably approved by Landlord.

**8.3 Ownership.** Alterations become Landlord's property at the end of the Term. Landlord may require Tenant to remove an alteration only if Landlord gave that requirement in writing when it consented to that alteration.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

**9.1 Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the Building structure, roof, exterior walls, Common Areas and Building systems serving the Premises in good condition.

**9.2 Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. Tenant shall repair damage to the Property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees or invitees.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**10.1 Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its consent. Landlord shall respond to a written request for consent within 15 business days after receiving it together with the information reasonably required under Section 10.2.

**10.2 Request.** Tenant's request shall identify the proposed assignee or subtenant and include its current financial statements, a description of its business, and the material terms of the proposed transaction.

**10.3 Assignment on Sale of Business.** Landlord shall not withhold consent to an assignment of this Lease to a Person that acquires all or substantially all of Tenant's business assets if the assignee (a) will use the Premises for the Permitted Use, (b) has a net worth, immediately after the acquisition, at least equal to Tenant's net worth on the date of this Lease, and (c) assumes Tenant's obligations under this Lease in writing.

**10.4 Effect of Assignment.** An assignee under this Section 10 shall assume in writing all of Tenant's obligations under this Lease arising from and after the effective date of the assignment. If Landlord consents to an assignment and the assignee assumes this Lease in writing, the assigning Tenant is released from obligations under this Lease arising after the effective date of the assignment.

**10.5 Costs.** Tenant shall reimburse Landlord's reasonable attorneys' fees in reviewing a request for consent, not to exceed $2,000 per request.

## 11. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**11.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord as additional insured;

(b) property insurance on Tenant's personal property and alterations, on a full replacement cost basis; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**11.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance on the Building on a full replacement cost basis and commercial general liability insurance on the Property, the cost of which is an Operating Expense.

**11.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives claims against the other for loss to its property to the extent covered by property insurance it is required to carry under this Lease. Each Party shall cause its property insurance policies to permit that waiver.

**11.4 Mutual Indemnity.** Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for injury or property damage arising in the Premises, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents. Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for injury or property damage arising in the Common Areas, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

**12.1 Casualty.** If fire or other casualty damages the Premises or access to them, Landlord shall restore them with reasonable diligence, and Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered unusable until restoration is substantially complete. If Landlord reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than 180 days after the casualty, either Party may terminate this Lease by written notice within 30 days after Landlord delivers that estimate.

**12.2 Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that the remainder is not reasonably usable for the Permitted Use, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease ends on the date of the taking. Landlord is entitled to the award, except that Tenant may claim separately for its moving expenses and personal property.

## 13. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**13.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within ten days after written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within 30 days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within 30 days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and pursue it diligently to completion; or

(c) Tenant makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or a bankruptcy case is filed by or against Tenant and, if involuntary, is not dismissed within 60 days.

**13.2 Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and pursue any remedy available at law, including recovery of Rent due through termination and damages measured by the Rent for the remainder of the Term less the fair rental value of the Premises for that period. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

**13.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform an obligation under this Lease within 30 days after written notice from Tenant, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law.

## 14. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

**14.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any existing or future mortgage on the Property, if the mortgagee agrees in writing that Tenant's possession will not be disturbed while no Event of Default is continuing.

**14.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Within ten business days after written request, each Party shall deliver to the other a certificate confirming that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 15. RULES AND REGULATIONS

Tenant shall comply with the reasonable written rules and regulations for the Building that Landlord delivers to Tenant (the "**Rules and Regulations**"). Landlord shall apply the Rules and Regulations uniformly to all tenants. If any Rule or Regulation conflicts with this Lease, this Lease prevails.

## 16. QUIET ENJOYMENT

So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

## 17. LANDLORD ACCESS

Landlord may enter the Premises on at least 24 hours' notice, except in an emergency, to inspect them, make repairs, and show them to prospective purchasers or lenders and, during the last nine months of the Term, to prospective tenants. Landlord shall minimize interference with Tenant's business.

## 18. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

**18.1 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and alterations Tenant is not required to remove under Section 8.3 excepted. Tenant shall remove its personal property.

**18.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written agreement, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay monthly Base Rent equal to 150% of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term, prorated daily.

## 19. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**19.1 Notices.** All notices under this Lease must be in writing. A notice is effective when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested. Notices to Landlord go to 18 Ostrander Mill Road, Suite 100, Kingston, New York, attention: Property Manager. Notices to Tenant go to the Premises, attention: President. Either Party may change its address by notice to the other.

**19.2 Brokers.** Each Party represents that it dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Catskill Commercial Realty, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement.

**19.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**19.4 Successors.** Subject to Section 10, this Lease binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**19.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**19.6 Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

**19.7 Forum.** Any action arising out of this Lease shall be brought in the state courts located in Ulster County, New York, or the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**19.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS LEASE, EXCEPT ANY CLAIM FOR PERSONAL INJURY OR PROPERTY DAMAGE.

**19.9 Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
