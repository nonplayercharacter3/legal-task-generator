# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and between **Sipoun Organic Holdings, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and **Fupurt Valley Creamery, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller produces organic dairy products and artisanal cheeses in the Hudson Valley region of New York (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set out below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

(b) "**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Lease Assignment, the Seller Note, and each other agreement, instrument or certificate delivered under this Agreement.

(c) "**Assigned Contracts**" means the Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c).

(d) "**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 4.1.

(e) "**Contract**" means any contract, lease, license, purchase order, agreement, commitment or other legally binding arrangement, whether written or oral.

(f) "**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, easement or other encumbrance of any kind.

(g) "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any political subdivision, agency, commission, court or tribunal of any of them, including the United States Department of Agriculture, the United States Food and Drug Administration and the New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets.

(h) "**Lease**" means the office lease between Seller, as tenant, and Ostrander Mill Properties LLC, as landlord (the "**Landlord**"), covering Suite 204, 18 Ostrander Mill Road, Kingston, New York.

(i) "**Lease Assignment**" means the assignment and assumption of the Lease, in the form agreed by the Parties, together with the Landlord's consent to that assignment.

(j) "**Liabilities**" means liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature, whether asserted or unasserted, known or unknown, absolute or contingent, accrued or unaccrued, matured or unmatured.

(k) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(l) "**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

(m) "**Products**" means all organic fluid milk, cultured dairy products, butter, cream and artisanal cheeses, and all other products, produced, processed, aged, packaged, marketed, distributed or sold by or on behalf of the Business.

(n) "**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $3,200,000 to be issued by Buyer to Seller at Closing under Section 3.2(b).

(o) "**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, payroll, property, excise and other taxes, together with any interest, additions or penalties.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights of every kind used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) all inventory, including raw milk, cultures, rennet, cheese in process and aging, finished Products, packaging and labels;

(b) all equipment, including pasteurization, separation, vat, press, brining, cave and refrigeration equipment, vehicles, furniture and fixtures, as listed on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) all rights under the Assigned Contracts;

(d) all intellectual property used in the Business, including the "Fupurt Valley" trademarks, recipes, cheese-make protocols, culture strains and trade secrets;

(e) all permits, licenses and certifications held for the Business, including Seller's USDA organic certification and New York dairy plant licenses, to the extent transferable;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business;

(g) all books, records, customer and supplier lists, quality-control logs and lot traceability records; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include: (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Seller's corporate records, minute books and Tax returns; (c) all Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (d) insurance policies of Seller and rights under them; (e) the assets listed on Schedule 2.2; and (f) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all trade accounts payable of Seller to third parties in connection with the Business that remain unpaid at the Closing Date and are reflected on the Closing Statement;

(b) accrued but unused vacation of Transferred Employees, to the extent reflected on the Closing Statement;

(c) all Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing;

(d) all Liabilities arising out of the ownership or operation of the Purchased Assets after the Closing; and

(e) all Liabilities arising out of or relating to the Products, including product warranty, product recall, product liability, contamination and organic-certification Liabilities, whether arising before or after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible for any Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Seller shall pay and discharge the Excluded Liabilities when due.

**2.5 Non-Assignable Assets.** If any Assigned Contract may not be assigned without the consent of a third party and that consent has not been obtained at Closing, this Agreement does not constitute an assignment of that Assigned Contract. Until the consent is obtained, Seller shall hold that Assigned Contract for Buyer's benefit and shall cooperate in any lawful arrangement designed to give Buyer its benefits, and Buyer shall perform Seller's obligations under it to the extent of the benefits received.

## ARTICLE 3. PURCHASE PRICE

**3.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 3.3.

**3.2 Payment.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $15,200,000, less the Escrow Amount, in cash by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three Business Days before the Closing Date;

(b) $3,200,000 by delivery of the Seller Note, maturing 36 months after the Closing Date; and

(c) $920,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer to the escrow agent designated in the Escrow Agreement, to be held as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article 9.

**3.3 Inventory Adjustment.** Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the value of inventory included in the Purchased Assets at Closing, determined at the lower of cost or market (the "**Closing Statement**"). If closing inventory exceeds $1,150,000 (the "**Target Inventory**"), Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller in cash; if it is less than the Target Inventory, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer in cash. Disputes shall be resolved by an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the Parties, whose determination is final and binding.

**3.4 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and Schedule 3.4. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE 4. CLOSING

**4.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after the conditions in Article 8 are satisfied or waived, or at such other time as the Parties agree (the "**Closing Date**").

**4.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale (the "**Bill of Sale**"); (b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (c) the Lease Assignment, executed by Seller and the Landlord; (d) trademark assignments; (e) the third-party consents listed in Section 8.2(c); (f) the certificate required by Section 8.2(d); and (g) such other customary instruments of transfer as Buyer reasonably requests.

**4.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the cash payments under Section 3.2(a) and 3.2(c); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Lease Assignment, executed by Buyer; and (d) the certificate required by Section 8.3(c).

## ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date:

**5.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York and has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and carry on the Business.

**5.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes Seller's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Title.** Seller has good and valid title to the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 5.3 ("**Permitted Encumbrances**").

**5.4 Material Contracts.** Schedule 2.1(c) lists each Contract material to the Business. Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, each other party, and Seller is not in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**5.5 Compliance; Permits.** Seller has operated the Business in material compliance with applicable law, and holds all permits and certifications necessary to operate the Business as currently conducted.

**5.6 Products.** Schedule 5.6 lists each recall, market withdrawal and written notice of contamination or organic-certification non-compliance relating to the Products during the three years before the date of this Agreement.

**5.7 Litigation.** Except as listed on Schedule 5.7, there is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**5.8 Employees.** Schedule 5.8 lists each employee of the Business, with title, base compensation and date of hire.

**5.9 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee from Buyer in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date:

**6.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York.

**6.2 Authority.** Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, and this Agreement constitutes Buyer's legal, valid and binding obligation.

**6.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand or available committed financing to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**6.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE 7. COVENANTS

**7.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, maintain its organic certification, and preserve its relationships with suppliers, dairy farms, distributors and customers.

**7.2 Access.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Business.

**7.3 Landlord and Distribution Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before Closing, the consent of the Landlord to the Lease Assignment and the consent of the counterparty to the Retail Distribution Agreement listed as item 3 on Schedule 2.1(c).

**7.4 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to employees of the Business effective on the Closing Date. Employees who accept are "**Transferred Employees**." Seller shall terminate the employment of each Transferred Employee as of the Closing Date and shall pay all wages and bonuses earned through the Closing Date.

**7.5 Confidentiality; Public Announcements.** Neither Party shall make any public announcement regarding this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except as required by law.

**7.6 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the production or sale of organic dairy or cheese products within the States of New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, Massachusetts or Vermont.

**7.7 Bulk Sales.** The Parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales law. Any Liability arising from that waiver is an Excluded Liability.

**7.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as the other Party reasonably requests to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 8. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each Party to close are subject to the absence of any order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**8.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligations of Buyer to close are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of the following conditions:

(a) Seller's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before Closing;

(c) Seller has obtained and delivered to Buyer (i) the written consent of the Landlord to the Lease Assignment and (ii) the written consent of the counterparty to the Retail Distribution Agreement listed as item 3 on Schedule 2.1(c) to its assignment to Buyer;

(d) Buyer has received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.2(a) and 8.2(b); and

(e) no event has occurred that has had a material adverse effect on the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**8.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligations of Seller to close are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of the following conditions: (a) Buyer's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before Closing; and (c) Seller has received a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 8.3(a) and 8.3(b).

## ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for 18 months, except that those in Sections 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 6.1 and 6.2 survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates, officers, managers and employees against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement or any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(b) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Procedures.** A Party seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. No settlement of a third-party claim may be entered without the indemnified Party's consent unless it involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer shall be liable under Section 9.3, other than claims under Section 9.3(b), shall not exceed $1,840,000 (10% of the purchase price).

(b) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller shall be liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $1,840,000, except for Losses arising from inaccuracy in or breach of the representations in Sections 5.1, 5.2 and 5.3 or from fraud.

(c) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $184,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount.

**9.6 Insurance.** Losses are calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified Party for the relevant Loss, less costs of recovery.

**9.7 Escrow.** Buyer shall first seek recovery of Losses under Section 9.2 from the Escrow Amount, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, before exercising its rights under Section 9.8 or seeking recovery directly from Seller.

**9.8 Right of Setoff.** Buyer may set off the amount of any Losses to which it is entitled under this Article 9, as finally determined by agreement of the Parties or by a final, non-appealable judgment, against any amount of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note. A setoff in accordance with this Section 9.8 is not a default under the Seller Note.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, this Article 9 is the Parties' sole and exclusive remedy for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, unless the failure results from that Party's breach; or (c) by either Party if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void without Liability of either Party, except that Sections 7.5, 10.2 and Article 11 survive, and termination does not relieve a Party of Liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement, except that transfer Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by the Parties.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses each Party designates in writing.

**11.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates or, as collateral, to its lenders.

**11.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing signed by the waiving Party.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article 9, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**11.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.8 Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, the Ancillary Agreement controls over this Agreement as to the subject matter of that Ancillary Agreement.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
