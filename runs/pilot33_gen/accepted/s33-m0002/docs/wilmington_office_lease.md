# OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "**Lease Date**") by and between **Ostrel Commons Properties LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Paikort Pet Foods, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE INFORMATION

1.1 **Building.** The office building known as Ostrel Commons, 1400 Market Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Building**"), together with the land on which it is located and the parking areas, walkways and other common areas serving it (collectively, the "**Property**").

1.2 **Premises.** Suite 610 on the sixth (6th) floor of the Building, containing approximately 4,200 rentable square feet, as shown on Exhibit A (the "**Premises**").

1.3 **Term.** Sixty (60) full calendar months, beginning on the Commencement Date and ending on the last day of the sixtieth (60th) full calendar month after it (the "**Expiration Date**"), unless extended under Section 4.3 or terminated earlier under this Lease (the "**Term**").

1.4 **Commencement Date.** March 1, 2025.

1.5 **Base Rent.** As set out in Section 5.1.

1.6 **Tenant's Share.** 2.1%, being the rentable square footage of the Premises divided by the rentable square footage of the Building (approximately 200,000 rentable square feet).

1.7 **Base Year.** Calendar year 2025.

1.8 **Security Deposit.** $21,000.

1.9 **Permitted Use.** General office use, including administrative, sales and customer service functions, and uses incidental to them.

1.10 **Parking.** Eight (8) unreserved spaces in the Building's parking garage, at no additional charge during the initial Term.

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the common areas of the Property for their intended purposes.

2.2 **Condition.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises with the Building systems serving the Premises in good working order and with Landlord's Work (as defined in Exhibit B) substantially complete. Except for Landlord's Work and Landlord's obligations under Section 9, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date.

2.3 **Measurement.** The rentable square footage of the Premises and the Building stated in Section 1 is stipulated for all purposes of this Lease and is not subject to remeasurement.

## 3. USE

3.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

3.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply, as part of Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 6, with all laws applicable to the Building structure, Building systems and common areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act.

3.3 **Prohibited Activities.** Tenant shall not store or sell inventory, operate a warehouse or retail showroom, or keep any animals in the Premises, other than service animals and product samples kept in quantities customary for a sales office. Tenant shall not use the Premises in any manner that creates a nuisance or unreasonably disturbs other tenants.

3.4 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the rules and regulations for the Building attached as Exhibit C, as reasonably modified by Landlord on written notice, provided they are applied in a non-discriminatory manner and do not materially interfere with the Permitted Use.

## 4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date.

4.2 **Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises during the thirty (30) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, cabling and equipment, subject to all terms of this Lease other than the payment of Rent.

4.3 **Renewal Option.** Provided Tenant is not in default beyond any applicable cure period, Tenant may extend the Term for one (1) additional period of five (5) years by written notice to Landlord given not less than nine (9) nor more than twelve (12) months before the Expiration Date. Base Rent for the renewal period will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the fair market rent for comparable office space in comparable buildings in downtown Wilmington, determined as provided in Exhibit D.

## 5. RENT

5.1 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay annual Base Rent in equal monthly installments in advance on the first day of each calendar month, as follows:

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – 12 | $30.00 | $126,000.00 | $10,500.00 |
| 13 – 24 | $30.75 | $129,150.00 | $10,762.50 |
| 25 – 36 | $31.52 | $132,384.00 | $11,032.00 |
| 37 – 48 | $32.31 | $135,702.00 | $11,308.50 |
| 49 – 60 | $33.12 | $139,104.00 | $11,592.00 |

5.2 **Abatement.** Base Rent for Lease Months 1 and 2 is abated. Tenant's obligation to pay Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses is not abated.

5.3 **Additional Rent.** All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

5.4 **Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve-month period if Tenant pays the overdue amount within five (5) days after written notice.

## 6. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

6.1 **Payment.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

6.2 **Definitions.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs incurred by Landlord to operate, maintain, repair, insure and manage the Property, consistent with comparable Class A office buildings in Wilmington, Delaware. "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Property. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures (other than those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws enacted after the Lease Date, amortized over their useful life), leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, debt service, Landlord's income taxes, and costs reimbursed by insurance or other tenants. Controllable Operating Expenses shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a non-cumulative basis.

6.3 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall give Tenant a reasonable estimate of Tenant's payments under Section 6.1 before each calendar year, payable monthly with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes, and the Parties shall settle any overpayment or underpayment within thirty (30) days.

6.4 **Audit.** Tenant may, on thirty (30) days' notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving a statement, inspect Landlord's records for that year. If the inspection shows an overcharge of more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable inspection costs.

## 7. SECURITY DEPOSIT

7.1 **Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any default by Tenant beyond any applicable cure period, and Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within ten (10) days after written demand.

7.2 **Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

8.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall furnish to the Premises heating, ventilation and air conditioning from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on business days and 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, electricity for normal office use, water, elevator service, janitorial service five (5) nights per week, and access to the Building twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, by key card.

8.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall furnish heating, ventilation and air conditioning outside the hours in Section 8.1 on reasonable advance request, at Landlord's then-standard hourly rate, which is $65.00 per hour on the Lease Date.

8.3 **Interruption.** If any service Landlord is required to furnish is interrupted for more than five (5) consecutive business days for reasons within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption renders the Premises untenantable, Rent abates from the sixth (6th) business day until the service is restored.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

9.1 **Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain and repair the roof, foundation, structural elements, exterior walls and windows, common areas, and Building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems, in each case in a condition consistent with comparable office buildings in Wilmington, Delaware.

9.2 **Tenant.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition and repair, reasonable wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall repair any damage to the Property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or invitees.

## 10. ALTERATIONS

10.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations such as painting and carpeting that cost less than $25,000 in any twelve-month period and do not affect the Building structure or systems.

10.2 **Performance.** Tenant shall perform all alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with law, using contractors reasonably approved by Landlord, and shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from its work.

10.3 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall remove its furniture, equipment and cabling. Tenant need not remove any alterations unless Landlord required removal in writing when it consented to them.

## 11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

11.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information reasonably required to evaluate it.

11.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on written notice to Landlord within thirty (30) days after the transaction, (a) assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to an affiliate of Tenant; (b) assign this Lease to any entity resulting from a merger or consolidation with Tenant, or to any entity acquiring all or substantially all of Tenant's assets or equity; or (c) undergo any change in the ownership of its equity, including by merger, in which Tenant becomes a subsidiary of another entity (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"). A Permitted Transfer does not require any amendment of this Lease and does not entitle Landlord to recapture the Premises or to any share of consideration.

11.3 **Tenant Remains Liable.** No assignment or sublease, other than an assignment under Section 11.2(b) in which the assignee assumes this Lease in writing, releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.

## 12. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

12.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds, and property insurance covering Tenant's personal property at full replacement cost.

12.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain property insurance covering the Building at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings.

12.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other for loss of or damage to property to the extent covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance.

12.4 **Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 12.3, each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring on the Property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its employees, agents or contractors.

## 13. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

13.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore them with reasonable diligence, and Rent abates in proportion to the portion of the Premises rendered untenantable. If Landlord reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than one hundred eighty (180) days, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after Landlord delivers its estimate.

13.2 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or of access to them that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate award for its moving expenses and personal property.

## 14. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

14.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due; or (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it.

14.2 **Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease or Tenant's right of possession by written notice and recover its damages as permitted by Delaware law. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

14.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant (or a longer reasonable period if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days and Landlord diligently pursues the cure), Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity.

## 15. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

15.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage on the Property, provided the holder of the mortgage enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement with Tenant under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists.

15.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other's written request, deliver a certificate confirming the status of this Lease, the dates through which Rent has been paid, and whether either party is known to be in default.

## 16. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

16.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom-clean and in the condition required by Section 9.2, subject to Section 10.3.

16.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term, prorated on a daily basis.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier to Landlord at 1400 Market Street, Suite 200, Wilmington, Delaware 19801, Attn: Property Manager, and to Tenant at the Premises, Attn: General Counsel, or to another address a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

17.2 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

17.3 **Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Halvern Realty Advisors, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement.

17.4 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

17.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

17.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

17.7 **Successors.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

17.8 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

## EXHIBIT B: LANDLORD'S WORK

Landlord, at its sole cost, shall (1) repaint the Premises in Building-standard colors selected by Tenant; (2) replace the carpet in the Premises with Building-standard carpet; (3) construct one (1) additional enclosed office and one (1) conference room in Building-standard finishes per the space plan approved by the parties; and (4) deliver the restrooms on the sixth (6th) floor in compliance with applicable accessibility laws.

## EXHIBIT C: RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. Sidewalks, entrances, corridors and elevators shall not be obstructed.
2. Deliveries of furniture and equipment shall be made through the loading dock during hours Landlord designates.
3. No smoking or vaping is permitted anywhere in the Building.
4. Tenant shall not install signage visible from outside the Premises other than Building-standard suite and directory signage provided by Landlord.
5. Tenant shall cooperate with Landlord's recycling and energy conservation programs.

## EXHIBIT D: FAIR MARKET RENT DETERMINATION

1. Within thirty (30) days after Tenant's renewal notice, Landlord shall notify Tenant of Landlord's determination of fair market rent. If Tenant disagrees, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days.
2. If the parties do not agree, each shall appoint a real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience leasing office space in Wilmington, Delaware, and the two brokers shall select a third. The third broker shall choose whichever of Landlord's and Tenant's last proposed rents is closer to the fair market rent, and that choice is binding.
3. Each party bears the cost of its own broker, and the parties share equally the cost of the third broker.
