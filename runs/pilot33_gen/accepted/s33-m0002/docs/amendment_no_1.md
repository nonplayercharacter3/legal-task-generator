# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Paikort Pet Foods, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Broumoux Distribution Co.**, a New Jersey corporation ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the States of New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania.

B. Supplier has developed a line of freeze-dried dog food and cat food products. The Parties wish to add those products to the Products distributed under the Agreement and to make related conforming changes to the Agreement.

C. Supplier has informed Distributor that on September 15, 2026, Supplier entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Fenwyck Pet Holdings, LLC. Under that agreement, Supplier will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Fenwyck Pet Holdings, LLC. Closing is expected on or about December 1, 2026.

D. Section 16.3 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. ADDITION OF FREEZE-DRIED PRODUCT LINE

2.1 **Recital A.** Recital A of the Agreement is amended to read in full:

> A. Supplier manufactures and sells dry and freeze-dried dog food and cat food products under its own brands.

2.2 **Definition of Products.** Section 1.7 of the Agreement is amended to read in full:

> 1.7 "**Products**" means the dry dog food, dry cat food, freeze-dried dog food and freeze-dried cat food products listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

2.3 **Competing Products.** In Section 2.4 of the Agreement, the words "any dry dog food or dry cat food product" are replaced with "any dry or freeze-dried dog food or cat food product."

2.4 **Exhibit A.** Exhibit A to the Agreement is deleted and replaced in full with the following:

> **EXHIBIT A: PRODUCTS**
>
> | Product Line | Species | Formats |
> |---|---|---|
> | Paikort Everyday Dry Recipe | Dog | 4 lb, 15 lb and 30 lb bags |
> | Paikort Grain-Free Dry Recipe | Dog | 4 lb and 24 lb bags |
> | Paikort Small Breed Dry Recipe | Dog | 4 lb and 12 lb bags |
> | Paikort Indoor Dry Recipe | Cat | 3 lb and 14 lb bags |
> | Paikort Kitten Dry Recipe | Cat | 3 lb and 7 lb bags |
> | Paikort Raw-Crafted Freeze-Dried Recipe | Dog | 10 oz and 25 oz pouches |
> | Paikort Freeze-Dried Toppers | Dog | 3.5 oz and 8 oz pouches |
> | Paikort Raw-Crafted Freeze-Dried Recipe | Cat | 7 oz and 16 oz pouches |

2.5 **Initial Orders.** Distributor may issue Purchase Orders for the freeze-dried Products listed in Exhibit A (the "**Freeze-Dried Products**") beginning on the Amendment Effective Date. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ship initial Purchase Orders for Freeze-Dried Products within thirty (30) days after acceptance.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 11.3

Section 11.3 of the Agreement is amended to read in full:

> 11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:
>
> (a) a Party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct;
>
> (b) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10.1(a) or Section 10.2;
>
> (c) Distributor's obligation to pay for Products ordered and delivered; or
>
> (d) a Party's payment obligations under Section 14.3.

## 4. PRICING AND MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

4.1 **Prices for Freeze-Dried Products.** Exhibit B to the Agreement is amended by adding the following at the end of paragraph 1: "Prices for Freeze-Dried Products: per Supplier's freeze-dried distributor price list dated October 1, 2026, attached to this Exhibit B." Section 5.1 of the Agreement applies to changes in prices for Freeze-Dried Products.

4.2 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Purchases of Freeze-Dried Products count toward Distributor's minimum annual purchase commitment under Section 4.4 of the Agreement and paragraph 2 of Exhibit B. The minimum quantities stated in paragraph 2 of Exhibit B are not changed by this Amendment.

## 5. STORAGE, HANDLING AND RECALL OF FREEZE-DRIED PRODUCTS

5.1 **Quality Agreement.** The Freeze-Dried Products are Products for all purposes of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement. Distributor shall store, handle, transport and rotate the Freeze-Dried Products in accordance with Section 7.2 of the Agreement and the Quality Agreement.

5.2 **Product Information.** Before the first shipment of Freeze-Dried Products, Supplier shall furnish to Distributor the Specifications and any product-specific handling instructions for the Freeze-Dried Products.

## 6. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

6.1 **Ratification.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all other terms of the Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect, and are ratified and confirmed by the Parties.

6.2 **Representations.** Each Party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it.

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 15.1 through 15.4 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in this Amendment.

7.2 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Amendment as of the Amendment Effective Date.
