# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**QA Effective Date**") by and between **Paikort Pet Foods, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Broumoux Distribution Co.**, a New Jersey corporation ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (as amended from time to time, the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under the Distribution Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the States of New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania.

B. Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement requires Distributor to store, handle, transport and rotate Products in accordance with this Quality Agreement. Section 9 of the Distribution Agreement provides for the conduct of Recalls and the allocation of Recall costs in accordance with this Quality Agreement.

C. The Parties enter into this Quality Agreement to set out their respective responsibilities for the receipt, storage, handling, transportation, traceability and Recall of Products.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Distribution Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement, including "Affiliate," "Customer," "Governmental Authority," "Losses," "Products," "Recall," "Specifications" and "Territory."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Deviation**" means any departure from the storage, handling, transportation or other requirements of this Quality Agreement, including any temperature or humidity excursion.

(b) "**Distributor Facility**" means each warehouse, cross-dock or other facility owned, leased or operated by or for Distributor at which Products are received, stored or shipped.

(c) "**Lot**" means a quantity of Product identified by a unique lot number assigned by Supplier.

(d) "**Quality Contact**" means the individual designated by each Party under Section 2.3.

(e) "**Quarantine**" means the physical and system segregation of Product so that it cannot be shipped or sold pending a disposition decision by Supplier.

1.3 **Products Covered.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products, as that term is defined in the Distribution Agreement from time to time, from the time title and risk pass to Distributor under Section 6.2 of the Distribution Agreement until delivery to a Customer.

## 2. RESPONSIBILITIES AND COMMUNICATION

2.1 **Supplier Responsibilities.** Supplier is responsible for the manufacture, packaging and labeling of Products in accordance with the Specifications and applicable law, for release of each Lot for shipment, and for furnishing to Distributor any product-specific storage and handling instructions.

2.2 **Distributor Responsibilities.** Distributor is responsible for the receipt, storage, handling, rotation, transportation and delivery of Products in accordance with this Quality Agreement, the Specifications, Supplier's written handling instructions and applicable law, including the requirements of 21 C.F.R. Part 507 to the extent applicable to Distributor's activities.

2.3 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate in writing a Quality Contact, and a backup, who is reachable twenty-four (24) hours a day for urgent quality and Recall communications. A Party may change its Quality Contact by written notice to the other Party.

2.4 **Subcontractors.** Distributor shall not use any third-party warehouse or carrier to store Products without Supplier's prior written approval. Distributor remains responsible for the compliance of any approved third party with this Quality Agreement.

## 3. RECEIVING

3.1 **Inspection on Receipt.** On receipt of each shipment at a Distributor Facility, Distributor shall inspect the shipment for visible damage, pest activity, wet or torn packaging, broken seals and conformity of Lot numbers to the shipping documents, consistent with Section 6.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

3.2 **Trailer Condition.** Distributor shall reject or Quarantine any shipment arriving in a trailer showing evidence of pest activity, odor, moisture or contamination, and shall notify Supplier's Quality Contact within one (1) business day.

3.3 **Receiving Records.** Distributor shall record for each shipment the date of receipt, carrier, trailer number, Lot numbers, quantities and the result of the receiving inspection.

## 4. STORAGE

4.1 **Facility Conditions.** Distributor shall store Products only in enclosed, clean, dry, well-ventilated Distributor Facilities that are maintained in good repair and in a sanitary, pest-free condition, under a written pest control program administered by a licensed pest control operator.

4.2 **Temperature and Humidity.** Distributor shall store all Products at an ambient temperature not exceeding a maximum warehouse storage temperature of 80°F, and at a relative humidity not exceeding sixty-five percent (65%). Distributor shall monitor temperature and humidity in each storage area by calibrated continuous recording devices, shall review the records at least daily, and shall retain them in accordance with Section 6.3.

4.3 **Excursions.** If any storage area exceeds the limits in Section 4.2 for more than four (4) consecutive hours, Distributor shall Quarantine the affected Products, notify Supplier's Quality Contact within twenty-four (24) hours, and hold the Products pending Supplier's written disposition instructions.

4.4 **Stacking and Placement.** Distributor shall store Products on pallets or racking at least four (4) inches off the floor and eighteen (18) inches from walls, shall observe the pallet stacking limits on Supplier's packaging, and shall not store Products adjacent to chemicals, pesticides, cleaning agents or any material that could taint or contaminate them.

4.5 **Rotation.** Distributor shall rotate Products on a first-expiry, first-out basis and shall not ship to any Customer any Product with less than ninety (90) days of labeled shelf life remaining unless the Customer agrees in writing.

## 5. HANDLING AND TRANSPORTATION

5.1 **Handling.** Distributor shall handle Products so as to avoid puncture, crushing or other damage to packaging. Distributor shall not open, relabel, repackage or alter any Product or its packaging.

5.2 **Damaged Product.** Distributor shall segregate any damaged Product, shall not sell or donate it, and shall dispose of it in accordance with Supplier's written instructions and applicable law.

5.3 **Outbound Transportation.** Distributor shall ship Products only in clean, dry, pest-free, closed vehicles that are not used concurrently to carry chemicals or other contaminating materials, and shall ensure that conditions during transit are consistent with Section 4.2.

5.4 **Freeze-Dried Products.** For any freeze-dried Products, Distributor shall also comply with any additional handling instructions furnished by Supplier, including any instructions relating to pouch integrity and protection from moisture.

## 6. TRACEABILITY AND RECORDS

6.1 **Lot Tracking.** Distributor shall maintain a system that records, for each Lot, the quantities received, the Distributor Facility where it is stored, and each Customer to which any quantity of that Lot is shipped, with the date and quantity of each shipment.

6.2 **Trace Exercises.** Within four (4) hours after Supplier's request, Distributor shall produce a report identifying the current location and the Customer distribution of any Lot. Distributor shall participate in at least one (1) mock trace exercise each contract year at Supplier's request.

6.3 **Retention.** Distributor shall retain all records required by this Quality Agreement for at least three (3) years after the relevant transaction, consistent with Section 7.5 of the Distribution Agreement, and shall make them available to Supplier and to any Governmental Authority on request.

## 7. COMPLAINTS AND NOTIFICATIONS

7.1 **Complaints.** Distributor shall forward to Supplier's Quality Contact, within one (1) business day after receipt, every Customer or consumer complaint relating to the quality, safety or labeling of any Product, together with the Lot number, sample (if available) and complainant information.

7.2 **Urgent Notifications.** Consistent with Section 9.2 of the Distribution Agreement, each Party shall notify the other Party's Quality Contact promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of any adverse event, regulatory inquiry, inspection or other information suggesting that any Product may be adulterated, misbranded, unsafe or otherwise subject to Recall.

7.3 **Regulatory Inspections.** Distributor shall notify Supplier promptly of any inspection of a Distributor Facility by a Governmental Authority that relates to Products, and shall provide Supplier with copies of any resulting observations or reports relating to Products.

## 8. RECALLS

8.1 **Supplier Authority.** Supplier has sole authority to determine whether to initiate a Recall and shall direct its conduct, as provided in Section 9.1 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall not initiate any Recall or communicate with any Governmental Authority or Customer regarding a Recall except as directed by Supplier or as required by law.

8.2 **Distributor Cooperation.** On notice from Supplier of a Recall, Distributor shall, within the time frames Supplier specifies: (a) immediately Quarantine all affected Products in its possession; (b) within four (4) hours, provide a list of all Customers that received affected Lots; (c) notify Customers using the communications approved by Supplier; (d) retrieve affected Products from Customers; and (e) hold, return or destroy retrieved Products as Supplier directs, with written certification of destruction.

8.3 **Recall Costs.** Each Party bears its own internal costs of cooperating in a Recall. Supplier bears the costs of a Recall, including notification, retrieval, transportation, destruction and replacement of Products, except that where the Recall is caused by Distributor's failure to store or handle Products in accordance with the Distribution Agreement or this Quality Agreement, those costs are allocated to Distributor and are borne by Distributor as indemnifiable under Section 10.1(c) of the Distribution Agreement. Where a Recall is caused in part by each Party, costs are allocated in proportion to each Party's relative responsibility.

8.4 **Root Cause.** Following any Recall, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith in a root cause investigation. Distributor shall make available its storage, temperature, handling and traceability records for all affected Lots.

8.5 **Effectiveness Checks.** Distributor shall provide Supplier with Recall reconciliation reports, by Customer and Lot, at the intervals Supplier reasonably requests until Supplier closes the Recall.

## 9. AUDITS

9.1 **Supplier Audits.** Supplier may audit each Distributor Facility and Distributor's records relating to Products in accordance with Section 7.6 of the Distribution Agreement.

9.2 **Corrective Actions.** Distributor shall respond in writing to each audit finding within fifteen (15) business days with a corrective and preventive action plan, and shall complete agreed corrective actions within the time frames in the plan.

## 10. DEVIATIONS AND NONCONFORMING PRODUCT

10.1 **Deviation Reports.** Distributor shall document every Deviation, including its cause, the Products and Lots affected, and the actions taken, and shall provide the report to Supplier within five (5) business days after the Deviation is identified.

10.2 **Disposition.** Supplier has sole authority to determine the disposition of Products affected by a Deviation. Distributor shall not release Quarantined Product without Supplier's written approval.

## 11. TERM

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the QA Effective Date and remains in effect for so long as the Distribution Agreement is in effect and thereafter until all Products in Distributor's possession or control have been sold, returned or destroyed.

11.2 **Survival.** Sections 6.3, 8, 12 and 13 survive expiration or termination of this Quality Agreement.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement and all disputes arising under or relating to it, including disputes concerning the allocation of Recall costs and the storage and handling of Products, are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 **Venue.** Any action or proceeding arising under or relating to this Quality Agreement, including any dispute concerning the allocation of Recall costs or the storage and handling of Products, shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Essex County, New Jersey. Each Party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection to venue in them.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 16.1 of the Distribution Agreement. Urgent operational communications under Sections 4.3, 7 and 8 may also be given by telephone or email to the other Party's Quality Contact, with written confirmation to follow.

13.2 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

13.3 **Review.** The Parties shall review this Quality Agreement at least once every two (2) years and on the addition of any new Product line, and shall make any updates in writing.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Quality Agreement as of the QA Effective Date.
