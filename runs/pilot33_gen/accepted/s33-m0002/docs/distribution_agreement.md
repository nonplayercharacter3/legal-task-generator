# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Paikort Pet Foods, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Broumoux Distribution Co.**, a New Jersey corporation ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and sells dry dog food and dry cat food products under its own brands.

B. Distributor is engaged in the business of warehousing, distributing and reselling pet food products to retailers and other resale customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms have the meanings given below or where defined elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Change of Control**" means, with respect to a Party: (a) a merger, consolidation or other business combination in which that Party is not the surviving entity, or in which that Party survives but becomes a subsidiary of another entity; (b) the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting together, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of that Party; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of that Party or of the business to which this Agreement relates.

1.3 "**Customer**" means any retailer, veterinary clinic, grooming or boarding business, or other reseller located in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor.

1.4 "**Exit Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 14.3.

1.5 "**Gross Margin**" means, for any period, Distributor's net revenue from its sales of Products during that period less Distributor's purchase price paid to Supplier for those Products, as shown in Distributor's records maintained under Section 7.5.

1.6 "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

1.7 "**Products**" means the dry dog food and dry cat food products listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.8 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under Section 4.

1.9 "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between Supplier and Distributor governing the storage, handling, traceability and recall of Products, entered into in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

1.10 "**Recall**" means any recall, market withdrawal, stock recovery or destruction of Products, whether voluntary or required by a Governmental Authority.

1.11 "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state or local governmental, regulatory or administrative authority, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and state departments of agriculture.

1.12 "**Specifications**" means Supplier's product specifications for each Product, as furnished to Distributor in writing.

1.13 "**Territory**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the States of New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania (the "**Territory**"), and Distributor accepts that appointment. During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor of the Products in the Territory.

2.2 **Reserved Accounts.** Supplier reserves the right to sell Products directly to the national retail accounts listed in Exhibit C (the "**Reserved Accounts**"), including for delivery into the Territory. Sales to Reserved Accounts do not breach Section 2.1.

2.3 **Territory Restrictions.** Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, actively market, solicit orders for or sell Products to any person located outside the Territory, or to any person Distributor knows or reasonably should know intends to resell Products outside the Territory, without Supplier's prior written consent.

2.4 **Competing Products.** During the Term, Distributor shall not distribute in the Territory any dry dog food or dry cat food product that competes directly with the Products, other than the brands listed in Exhibit C as existing lines carried by Distributor on the Effective Date.

2.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship, and neither Party may bind the other.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13 or Section 14.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. ORDERS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders specifying the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery location. Each Purchase Order is subject to acceptance by Supplier.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days after receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order that is consistent with the most recent forecast delivered under Section 4.3.

4.3 **Forecasts.** By the tenth (10th) day of each calendar month, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a rolling non-binding twelve-month forecast of its Product requirements.

4.4 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Distributor shall purchase in each contract year at least the minimum quantities of Products stated in Exhibit B. If Distributor fails to meet the minimum for two consecutive contract years, Supplier may, on ninety (90) days' written notice, convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive.

4.5 **Conflicting Terms.** Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for Products set out in Exhibit B. Supplier may change prices on not less than sixty (60) days' written notice, but not more than once in any contract year. Accepted Purchase Orders are invoiced at the prices in effect when accepted.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days after receipt. Late payments bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed invoice amount, with reasonable detail, within fifteen (15) days after receipt, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.5 **Resale Prices.** Distributor is free to set its own resale prices to Customers.

## 6. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's manufacturing facility or other shipping point designated by Supplier (Incoterms 2020), for carriage to the delivery location stated in the applicable Purchase Order.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier at the shipping point.

6.3 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect each shipment within five (5) business days after receipt at its warehouse and shall notify Supplier in writing of any visible damage, shortage or non-conformity within that period. Shipments not so rejected are deemed accepted, without prejudice to Distributor's rights under Section 8 for latent defects.

6.4 **Shelf Life.** Supplier shall ship Products with not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of their labeled shelf life remaining.

## 7. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

7.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, market and sell the Products throughout the Territory and shall maintain an adequately trained sales force for that purpose.

7.2 **Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store, handle, transport and rotate Products in accordance with the Quality Agreement and all applicable laws, and shall maintain its warehouses in a clean, pest-free and sanitary condition.

7.3 **Brand Use.** Distributor may use Supplier's trademarks solely to identify and promote the Products in the Territory, in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines. Distributor shall not alter, relabel or repackage any Product.

7.4 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its storage, distribution and sale of Products in the Territory and shall hold all licenses and registrations required for those activities.

7.5 **Records and Reports.** Distributor shall maintain complete and accurate records of its purchases, inventory, sales and Customers for Products, including lot-level traceability records as required by the Quality Agreement, for at least three (3) years after the relevant transaction. Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a report of its sales of Products by Customer and by state.

7.6 **Audit.** On reasonable advance notice and not more than once per contract year (except following a Recall or a suspected breach), Supplier may inspect Distributor's warehouses and relevant records during normal business hours.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, at the time of delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be manufactured, packaged and labeled in compliance with applicable laws, including the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the pet food regulations of each state in the Territory; and (c) not be adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of applicable law.

8.2 **Warranty Period.** The warranty in Section 8.1 applies for twelve (12) months after delivery or until the labeled best-by date, whichever is earlier, provided the Products have been stored and handled in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

8.3 **Remedy.** For any Product that breaches Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at its option, replace the Product or credit or refund the purchase price, and shall reimburse Distributor's reasonable costs of returning or destroying the non-conforming Product. This remedy is without prejudice to Section 9 and Section 10.

8.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a valid and binding obligation enforceable against it.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. RECALLS

9.1 **Conduct of Recalls.** Supplier has sole authority to determine whether to initiate a Recall of any Product and shall direct the conduct of any Recall. Distributor shall cooperate fully with Supplier in any Recall, in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

9.2 **Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of any complaint, adverse event, regulatory inquiry or other information suggesting that any Product may be adulterated, misbranded, unsafe or otherwise subject to Recall.

9.3 **Recall Costs.** Recall costs are allocated as provided in the Quality Agreement and Section 10.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any third-party claim for bodily injury, death, or injury to or death of any animal, to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Distributor or by Distributor's storage, handling or transportation of Products;

(b) any breach by Distributor of Section 2.3 or Section 2.4; and

(c) any Recall, market withdrawal or destruction of Products, including the costs of notification, retrieval, transportation, destruction and replacement of Products, to the extent caused by Distributor's failure to store or handle Products in accordance with this Agreement or the Quality Agreement.

10.2 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents, from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any third-party claim for bodily injury, death, or injury to or death of any animal, to the extent caused by any Product that breaches Section 8.1;

(b) any Recall of Products, other than a Recall for which Distributor is responsible under Section 10.1(c); and

(c) any third-party claim that the Products or Supplier's trademarks, as supplied and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) notify the indemnifying Party promptly in writing of any claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced; (b) permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the claim, provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its choice at its own expense.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 11.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, HOWEVER CAUSED.

11.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 11.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED $750,000 AGGREGATE PER PARTY.

11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's fraud, gross negligence or willful misconduct;

(b) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 10.1 or Section 10.2;

(c) Distributor's obligation to pay for Products ordered and delivered; or

(d) a Party's payment obligations under Section 14.3.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 **Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterward, each Party shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate; and (b) product contamination and recall insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000.

12.2 **Certificates.** Each Party shall name the other as an additional insured on its commercial general liability policy and shall furnish certificates of insurance on request.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **For Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for any substantial part of its assets, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination: (a) all accepted Purchase Orders shall be fulfilled unless the terminating Party elects otherwise in its notice; (b) Distributor shall pay all amounts owed; (c) Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products for ninety (90) days, subject to Section 7 and the Quality Agreement, except where Section 14.3 applies; and (d) each Party shall return or destroy the other's confidential information.

13.4 **Survival.** Sections 5.3, 7.5, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 14.3, 15 and 16 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party, not to be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14.1 is void.

14.2 **Change of Control.** Any Change of Control of Supplier requires the prior written consent of Distributor. Supplier shall give Distributor written notice of any proposed Change of Control of Supplier as soon as legally permitted. A Change of Control of Supplier without the prior written consent of Distributor is a material breach of this Agreement, which is not subject to the cure period in Section 13.1, and Distributor may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice given at any time within one hundred twenty (120) days after the Change of Control of Supplier becomes effective.

14.3 **Consequences of Termination for Change of Control.** If Distributor terminates this Agreement under Section 14.2:

(a) Supplier shall repurchase all saleable Products in Distributor's inventory on the termination date at the prices Distributor paid for them, plus Distributor's documented freight costs, and shall take delivery at Distributor's warehouse within thirty (30) days after the termination date; and

(b) Supplier shall pay Distributor, within thirty (30) days after the termination date, a fee (the "**Exit Fee**") equal to twelve (12) months' average Gross Margin on Distributor's sales of Products, calculated as Distributor's average monthly Gross Margin over the twelve (12) full calendar months preceding the termination notice, multiplied by twelve (12).

14.4 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

15.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

15.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

15.3 **Equitable Relief.** Either Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to prevent a breach of Section 2.3, Section 7.3 or the confidentiality obligations in Section 16.6.

15.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses set out below the signatures (or another address a Party designates by notice). Notices are effective on receipt.

16.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, governmental action, labor disputes not involving that Party's own employees, and widespread utility or transportation failures, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

16.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

16.4 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among the following documents, they shall govern in this order of precedence: first, any Amendment to this Agreement executed by both Parties; then this Agreement; then the Quality Agreement; provided, however, that the Quality Agreement controls quality, recall and handling matters.

16.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

16.6 **Confidentiality.** Each Party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and all non-public business, pricing, customer and technical information received from the other Party, and shall use that information only to perform this Agreement, except to the extent disclosure is required by law or made to the Party's professional advisers bound by duties of confidentiality.

16.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
