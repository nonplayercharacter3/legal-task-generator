# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1: LAUNCH SUPPORT SERVICES

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of April 1, 2025 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is issued under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated April 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**").

This SOW is a Statement of Work under Section 3.6 of the Agreement and forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Launch Period**" means the period beginning on the SOW Effective Date and ending one hundred eighty (180) days afterward.

1.2 "**Launch Plan**" means the written plan for the commercial launch of the Products in the Territory that the Parties prepare under Section 3.1.

1.3 "**Connected Services**" means all software, cloud reporting, telemedicine integrations and reference laboratory testing services offered by Supplier, whether provided directly by Supplier or through its Affiliates, together with any related onboarding, data connectivity and support services.

1.4 "**Demonstration Units**" means the VC-300 and VC-120 analyzers that Supplier loans to Distributor under Section 4.

1.5 "**Launch Services**" means the services described in Sections 2 through 6.

1.6 "**Project Manager**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 7.1.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the launch-support, training and related services that Supplier and Distributor will perform during the Launch Period. These services will help introduce the Products to Customers in the Territory.

2.2 **Products Covered.** The Launch Services relate to the Products listed in Exhibit A to the Agreement, namely Part Nos. SVD-VC300, SVD-VC120, SVD-R14C, SVD-R08P, SVD-R05E, SVD-QC01 and SVD-SP-KIT.

2.3 **Connected Services Information.** During training sessions and Customer demonstrations under this SOW, Supplier personnel may present general information about the Connected Services. They may do so so that Customers understand how the Products interact with Supplier's broader offerings. Distributor shall direct any Customer inquiry about the Connected Services to Supplier.

## 3. LAUNCH PLANNING

3.1 **Launch Plan.** Within thirty (30) days after the SOW Effective Date, the Project Managers shall jointly prepare a Launch Plan. The Launch Plan shall set out:
(a) target Customer segments and priority accounts in each State of the Territory;
(b) a schedule of training sessions, regional events and Customer demonstrations;
(c) the allocation of Demonstration Units among Distributor's sales regions; and
(d) launch marketing activities and the related approval timeline under Section 8.2 of the Agreement.

3.2 **Approval.** The Launch Plan takes effect when both Project Managers approve it in writing, which may be by email. Either Project Manager may propose updates to the Launch Plan. An update takes effect when the other Project Manager approves it in writing.

## 4. DEMONSTRATION UNITS

4.1 **Loan.** Within fifteen (15) business days after the Launch Plan is approved, Supplier shall deliver to Distributor, on loan and without charge, up to six (6) VC-300 analyzers and up to six (6) VC-120 analyzers as Demonstration Units. Supplier shall also deliver a reasonable quantity of reagent rotors and quality control kits for demonstration use.

4.2 **Title and Use.** Title to the Demonstration Units remains with Supplier at all times. Distributor shall use the Demonstration Units only for demonstrations to prospective Customers in the Territory and for training its personnel. Distributor shall not sell, lease or encumber them.

4.3 **Care and Return.** Distributor shall keep the Demonstration Units insured and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Launch Period, Distributor shall either return the Demonstration Units to Supplier's Rochester, New York facility at Supplier's cost, or buy them at fifty percent (50%) of the Exhibit B price, at Distributor's election.

## 5. TRAINING

5.1 **Sales Training.** During the first sixty (60) days of the Launch Period, Supplier shall provide up to four (4) full-day sales training sessions for Distributor's sales personnel. Sessions will be held at Distributor's Edison, New Jersey facility or another location in the Territory that the Parties agree. Training will cover Product features, competitive positioning, pricing to Customers and handling of common objections.

5.2 **Technical Training.** Supplier shall provide up to two (2) technical training sessions for Distributor's customer-support personnel. These sessions will cover installation coordination, basic troubleshooting, reagent storage and handling, lot traceability and the escalation of matters to Supplier's second-line technical support under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Training Materials.** Supplier shall provide training manuals, slide presentations and quick-reference guides in electronic form. These materials are Supplier's Confidential Information. Distributor may use them only to train its own personnel and support Customers.

5.4 **Certification.** Distributor shall ensure that each of its sales and customer-support personnel who sells or supports the Products completes the applicable training under this Section 5 before contacting Customers about the Products.

## 6. LAUNCH MARKETING AND FIELD SUPPORT

6.1 **Marketing Materials.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with Product brochures, technical data sheets and digital assets for use in the Territory. Distributor may co-brand these materials with its own name and logo. Any co-branded materials are subject to Supplier's prior approval under Section 8.2 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Regional Events.** Supplier shall send at least one (1) product specialist to up to six (6) regional veterinary events or continuing-education meetings in the Territory, as identified in the Launch Plan.

6.3 **Field Ride-Alongs.** During the Launch Period, Supplier's product specialists shall accompany Distributor's sales personnel on Customer visits for up to forty (40) field days in total. These days will be allocated as set out in the Launch Plan.

6.4 **Customer Installations.** For the first twenty-five (25) analyzer installations at Customers in the Territory, Supplier shall provide on-site installation assistance with Distributor's personnel.

## 7. GOVERNANCE

7.1 **Project Managers.** Each Party shall designate a Project Manager within five (5) business days after the SOW Effective Date and notify the other Party of the designation. Each Project Manager is the primary point of contact for the Launch Services. Either Party may replace its Project Manager on written notice.

7.2 **Status Meetings.** The Project Managers shall meet, in person or by videoconference, at least every two (2) weeks during the Launch Period to review progress against the Launch Plan.

7.3 **Close-Out Report.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Launch Period, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a written close-out report. The report shall summarize training completed, events attended, Customer demonstrations performed and Product sales during the Launch Period.

## 8. FEES AND EXPENSES

8.1 **Supplier Services.** Supplier shall perform the Launch Services described in Sections 4, 5 and 6 at its own cost. Supplier shall bear the travel and lodging expenses of its personnel.

8.2 **Distributor Costs.** Each of the following is Distributor's responsibility:
(a) the costs of its own personnel's attendance at training sessions;
(b) the venue costs of any training held at Distributor's facilities; and
(c) its own costs for regional events, including booth and registration fees, unless the Launch Plan provides otherwise.

8.3 **Additional Services.** Any services beyond the quantities stated in this SOW require a written change order signed by both Project Managers. The change order shall state any applicable fees.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and ends on the later of the end of the Launch Period and completion of the obligations under Sections 4.3 and 7.3.

9.2 **Agreement Terms.** The Agreement's provisions on confidentiality, limitation of liability, governing law and notices apply to this SOW.

9.3 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
