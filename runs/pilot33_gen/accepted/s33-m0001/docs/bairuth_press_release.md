# Bairuth Animal Health Distribution Named Exclusive Northeast Distributor for Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics Point-of-Care Chemistry Analyzers

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**EDISON, N.J., April 8, 2025.** Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC ("Bairuth") announced today that it has entered into a Distribution Agreement with Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc. ("Sorund"), a manufacturer of point-of-care veterinary blood-chemistry analyzers and reagents based in Rochester, New York. Under the agreement, Bairuth becomes Sorund's exclusive distributor of its analyzers, reagents and related consumables to veterinary clinics and hospitals in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut.

## 1. The Partnership

1.1 The Distribution Agreement took effect on April 1, 2025. Bairuth will market, sell and provide first-line customer support for the Sorund product line across its four-state Northeast service area.

1.2 The product line covered by the partnership includes:

- the **Sorund VC-300 Chemistry Analyzer**, a benchtop analyzer with 24-parameter panel capacity;
- the **Sorund VC-120 Compact Analyzer**, a compact analyzer with 12-parameter panel capacity;
- single-use reagent rotors for comprehensive, preanesthetic and electrolyte panels;
- quality control kits; and
- analyzer service parts kits.

1.3 Sorund will continue to provide second-line technical support and repair for the analyzers. Bairuth's customers will order through their existing Bairuth account representatives.

## 2. Launch Program

2.1 Over the coming months, Bairuth and Sorund will run a joint launch program. The program includes sales and technical training for Bairuth's field and support teams, demonstration units placed across Bairuth's sales regions, and appearances at regional veterinary events and continuing-education meetings across the Northeast.

2.2 Sorund product specialists will join Bairuth representatives on customer visits during the launch period. They will also assist with early analyzer installations at clinics.

## 3. Executive Comments

We are proud to bring them to veterinarians across New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut, backed by Bairuth's local service and next-day reagent supply."

## 4. Availability

4.1 Sorund analyzers, reagent rotors and consumables are available to order from Bairuth now. Veterinary practices in the four-state area can contact their Bairuth account representative or Bairuth customer service to arrange a demonstration.

## 5. About Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC

5.1 Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC is a New Jersey-based distributor of animal health products to veterinary clinics and hospitals. Bairuth is headquartered at 2200 Fenwick Road, Edison, New Jersey. It serves companion-animal and mixed-animal practices with diagnostics, pharmaceuticals, equipment and supplies.

## 6. About Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.

6.1 Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc. designs and manufactures point-of-care veterinary blood-chemistry analyzers and related reagents. The company also operates a reference-laboratory testing business serving veterinary clinics. Sorund is headquartered at 410 Halvorsen Parkway, Rochester, New York.
