# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Halvorsen Parkway, Rochester, New York 14623 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2200 Fenwick Road, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures point-of-care veterinary blood-chemistry analyzers and related reagents. Supplier also operates a reference-laboratory testing business serving veterinary clinics.

B. Distributor is engaged in the business of distributing animal health products to veterinary clinics and hospitals.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of the entity.

1.2 "**Amendment**" means any written amendment to this Agreement signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

1.3 "**Change of Control**" means (a) any direct or indirect acquisition, by merger, consolidation, share purchase or otherwise, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of Supplier or of any parent of Supplier by any person or group of persons acting in concert, or (b) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of Supplier relating to the Products.

1.4 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

1.5 "**Contract Year**" means each successive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

1.6 "**Customer**" means a licensed veterinary clinic, hospital or practice located in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor for its own use.

1.7 "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means the order form executed by the Parties under this Agreement. It sets out, among other things, the Initial Term and Distributor's minimum purchase commitment.

1.9 "**Products**" means the veterinary blood-chemistry analyzers, reagents, consumables and related spare parts listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.10 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Distributor issues to Supplier under this Agreement.

1.11 "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 12.2.

1.12 "**Territory**" means the States of New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut, as further described in Schedule 1.

1.13 "**Statement of Work**" means any statement of work executed by the Parties under this Agreement describing launch-support, training or related services.

1.14 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.15 "**Trademarks**" means the trademarks, trade names and logos of Supplier used in connection with the Products.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Supplier appoints Distributor as its distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory during the Term. Distributor accepts the appointment.

2.2 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller for the Products in the Territory. Supplier shall not itself sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except as provided in Section 2.3.

2.3 **House Accounts.** Supplier may continue to supply Products directly to the accounts identified as house accounts in the Order Form. Supplier shall pay Distributor no commission on those sales.

2.4 **Reserved Services.** Supplier reserves the right to provide reference-laboratory testing services, directly or through its Affiliates, to veterinary clinics and other customers inside and outside the Territory. Reference-laboratory testing services are not Products. They fall outside the scope of Distributor's appointment and exclusivity under this Section 2.

2.5 **Territorial Limits.** Distributor shall not actively market or solicit sales of the Products outside the Territory. Distributor shall not establish any branch, warehouse or sales office for the Products outside the Territory.

2.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Distributor buys and resells Products for its own account. Neither Party has authority to bind the other.

## 3. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, market and sell the Products throughout the Territory. Distributor shall maintain a sales force adequately trained in the Products.

3.2 **Inventory.** Distributor shall maintain an inventory of reagents and consumables sufficient to meet reasonably anticipated Customer demand for at least thirty (30) days.

3.3 **Customer Support.** Distributor shall provide first-line customer support for the Products, including installation coordination and basic troubleshooting. Supplier shall provide second-line technical support and repair.

3.4 **Reporting.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a written report of Product sales by Customer, Product and State during that quarter, together with a rolling twelve (12) month forecast.

3.5 **Competing Products.** During the Term, Distributor shall not distribute in the Territory any point-of-care veterinary blood-chemistry analyzer or reagent that competes with the Products, without Supplier's prior written consent.

3.6 **Launch Support.** The Parties may agree on launch-support, training and related services in a Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work forms part of this Agreement.

## 4. ORDERS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by submitting Purchase Orders to Supplier. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery location.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days after receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any Purchase Order that conforms to this Agreement and to Distributor's most recent forecast.

4.3 **Conflicting Terms.** Any terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice that add to or differ from this Agreement have no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices for the Products set out in Exhibit B. Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes.

5.2 **Price Changes.** During the Initial Term, Supplier may increase Exhibit B prices once per Contract Year, on at least ninety (90) days' written notice. No single increase may exceed four percent (4%).

5.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice upon shipment.

5.4 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Overdue amounts bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

5.5 **Disputed Amounts.** Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed invoice amount within fifteen (15) days after receipt, stating the basis of the dispute in reasonable detail. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

## 6. DELIVERY

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's distribution facility in Rochester, New York (Incoterms 2020), to the carrier that Distributor designates in the Purchase Order.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier.

6.3 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect Products within ten (10) business days after receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage or visible damage within that period. Supplier shall replace short or damaged Products at its own cost.

6.4 **Reagent Shelf Life.** At delivery, each reagent and consumable shall have at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life.

## 7. MINIMUM PURCHASES

7.1 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value at least equal to the annual minimum purchase commitment set out in the Order Form.

7.2 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the minimum purchase commitment in any Contract Year, Supplier may, as its sole remedy for the shortfall and on written notice given within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year, do either of the following:
(a) convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive for the remainder of the Term; or
(b) terminate this Agreement under Section 12.3(c).

## 8. MARKETING AND TRADEMARKS

8.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Trademarks in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's brand guidelines.

8.2 **Approval.** Distributor shall submit to Supplier for prior approval all marketing materials and press releases that use the Trademarks or refer to Supplier. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold or delay approval.

8.3 **Ownership.** Supplier owns all right, title and interest in the Trademarks and in all goodwill arising from their use. Distributor shall not register or use any mark confusingly similar to the Trademarks.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that:
(a) each analyzer will conform to its specifications in Exhibit A, and will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, for twelve (12) months after delivery to the Customer; and
(b) each reagent and consumable will conform to its specifications until its labeled expiry date.

9.2 **Remedy.** For any Product that breaches Section 9.1, Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace the Product or refund its purchase price.

9.3 **Exclusions.** The warranties in Section 9.1 do not apply to Products that have been misused, improperly stored, altered or repaired by anyone other than Supplier, or used with reagents not supplied by Supplier.

9.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that:
(a) it is duly organized and in good standing;
(b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and
(c) this Agreement is a binding obligation of that Party.

9.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates from and against third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from:
(a) a defect in the design or manufacture of a Product;
(b) any allegation that a Product or the Trademarks infringe a third party's U.S. patent, copyright or trademark; or
(c) Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates from and against third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from:
(a) any warranty or representation about the Products that Distributor makes beyond those authorized by Supplier;
(b) Distributor's improper storage, handling or transportation of Products; or
(c) Distributor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of the claim. It shall give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes obligations on the indemnified Party without its consent.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Except for obligations under Section 10 and breaches of Section 13, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

11.2 **Cap.** Except for obligations under Section 10, breaches of Section 13, and amounts payable for Products, each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amounts paid and payable by Distributor to Supplier in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term stated in the Order Form (the "**Initial Term**").

12.2 **Renewal.** On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five (5) year Renewal Terms at the prices set out in Exhibit B then in effect (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal no earlier than one hundred twenty (120) days and no later than ninety (90) days before expiry of the then-current term.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice to the other Party if any of the following occurs:
(a) the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it;
(b) the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or
(c) in the case of Supplier, Supplier is entitled to terminate under Section 7.2(b).

12.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement:
(a) all unpaid amounts owed to Supplier become due;
(b) Distributor shall cease using the Trademarks, except to sell off remaining inventory under Section 12.5;
(c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and
(d) Supplier shall continue to honor its warranty obligations for Products sold before the date of expiry or termination.

12.5 **Sell-Off.** Unless this Agreement is terminated by Supplier under Section 12.3, Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products to Customers for ninety (90) days after expiry or termination, on the terms of this Agreement.

12.6 **Survival.** Sections 5, 9, 10, 11, 12.4, 12.5, 13, 15.3 and 16 survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, pricing and customer information that a Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

13.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement. It shall not disclose that information to anyone other than its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13. It shall protect the information with at least reasonable care.

13.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;
(b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by the receiving Party; or
(d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law or court order, after giving the disclosing Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

13.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 13 continue during the Term and for three (3) years afterward.

## 14. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

14.1 **Compliance with Laws.** Each Party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement, including laws on the storage, labeling, sale and transportation of veterinary diagnostic products.

14.2 **Complaints and Recalls.** Distributor shall notify Supplier within two (2) business days after receiving any complaint alleging a Product malfunction, adverse event or labeling error. Supplier shall have sole authority to initiate any Product recall or field correction. Distributor shall cooperate in any recall and shall maintain lot-level traceability records for all Products it sells. Supplier shall bear the reasonable costs of any recall, except to the extent the recall results from Distributor's breach of this Agreement.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

15.1 **Assignment.** Supplier shall not assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, without Distributor's prior written consent. Distributor may assign this Agreement, or any of its rights under it, to any Affiliate, successor or third party upon written notice to Supplier.

15.2 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

15.3 **Change of Control of Supplier.** A Change of Control of Supplier, including a Change of Control of any parent of Supplier, shall be deemed an assignment requiring Distributor's prior written consent. If a Change of Control of Supplier occurs without that consent, Distributor may terminate this Agreement by written notice to Supplier given within one hundred eighty (180) days after Distributor learns of the Change of Control. Distributor may also require Supplier to repurchase all of Distributor's then-current inventory of Products at Distributor's landed cost. Supplier shall pay the repurchase price within thirty (30) days after receiving Distributor's inventory statement.
