## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** For purposes of Section 12.1 of the Agreement, the Initial Term is 36 months from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date. The Distribution Agreement Effective Date is April 1, 2025.

## 4. MINIMUM PURCHASES

4.1 **Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.** For purposes of Section 7.1 of the Agreement, Distributor's annual minimum purchase commitment is $2,400,000 per contract year.

4.2 **Measurement.** The commitment is measured by the aggregate net invoice value of Products that Supplier invoices to Distributor during each Contract Year, at the Exhibit B prices then in effect. The following amounts are excluded from the measurement:
(a) taxes, freight and insurance charges;
(b) credits issued for returned, short or damaged Products under Section 6.3 of the Agreement; and
(c) any purchase of Demonstration Units under Section 4.3 of the Statement of Work.

4.3 **Quarterly Targets.** To support planning under Section 3.4 of the Agreement, the Parties target purchases of $600,000 in each calendar quarter of each Contract Year. Quarterly targets are not separate commitments. A shortfall is determined only on the full Contract Year under Section 7.2 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Shortfall.** Supplier's remedies for any shortfall against the commitment in Section 4.1 are those set out in Section 7.2 of the Agreement.
