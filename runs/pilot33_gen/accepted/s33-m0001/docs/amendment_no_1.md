# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Sorund Veterinary Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Halvorsen Parkway, Rochester, New York 14623 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Bairuth Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2200 Fenwick Road, Edison, New Jersey 08837 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated April 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. Supplier has developed SorundLink, a cloud-based software subscription. SorundLink connects Supplier's VC-300 and VC-120 analyzers to a hosted platform for result storage, trend reporting and practice-management system integration. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement to add the SorundLink subscription to the products that Distributor may resell to Customers in the Territory.

C. Supplier has informed Distributor that its parent, Sorund Holdings, Inc., entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Ostrava Animal Health plc on September 15, 2026. Closing of the merger is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

D. This Amendment is an Amendment under Sections 1.2 and 16.5 of the Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Products.** Section 1.9 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> 1.9 "**Products**" means the veterinary blood-chemistry analyzers, reagents, consumables and related spare parts listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing, and includes the Connected Services and the SorundLink Platform.

1.2 **New Definitions.** The following definitions are added to Section 1 of the Agreement as Sections 1.16 through 1.18:

> 1.16 "**SorundLink Platform**" means Supplier's hosted SorundLink software-as-a-service platform, together with its connectivity module for the VC-300 and VC-120 analyzers and any updates and new versions that Supplier makes generally available.
>
> 1.17 "**SorundLink Subscription**" means a subscription that gives a single Customer location the right to access and use the SorundLink Platform for a stated subscription period.
>
> 1.18 "**SorundLink Terms**" means Supplier's standard SorundLink end-user subscription terms, as Supplier publishes and updates them from time to time.

1.3 **Capitalized Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 2. SORUNDLINK SUBSCRIPTIONS

2.1 **Resale.** Distributor may resell SorundLink Subscriptions to Customers in the Territory under Section 2.1 of the Agreement. Distributor shall order SorundLink Subscriptions from Supplier by Purchase Order under Section 4 of the Agreement. Each Purchase Order shall identify the Customer location and the subscription start date.

2.2 **Provisioning.** Within five (5) business days after Supplier accepts a Purchase Order for a SorundLink Subscription, Supplier shall provision the Customer's account and deliver access credentials to the Customer's designated administrator. Delivery under Section 6.1 of the Agreement does not apply to SorundLink Subscriptions. Sections 6.2 through 6.4 of the Agreement also do not apply to them.

2.3 **Subscription Period.** Each SorundLink Subscription runs for twelve (12) months from its start date. It renews for further twelve (12) month periods if Distributor submits a renewal Purchase Order before expiry.

2.4 **End-User Terms.** Each Customer's use of the SorundLink Platform is governed by the SorundLink Terms. Distributor shall ensure that each Customer accepts the SorundLink Terms before Supplier activates the Customer's account. Distributor shall not make any commitment to a Customer about the SorundLink Platform that is inconsistent with the SorundLink Terms.

2.5 **Ownership.** Supplier and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in the SorundLink Platform. Distributor receives no license to the SorundLink Platform other than the right to resell SorundLink Subscriptions under this Amendment and to use a demonstration account for sales and training purposes.

2.6 **Customer Data.** Supplier shall host Customer data on the SorundLink Platform in accordance with the SorundLink Terms. Supplier shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for that data consistent with industry standards for cloud-hosted veterinary software. Distributor shall have no access to Customer data on the SorundLink Platform except as a Customer authorizes in writing.

2.7 **Support.** Distributor shall provide first-line support for SorundLink Subscriptions under Section 3.3 of the Agreement, limited to account set-up questions and analyzer connectivity troubleshooting. Supplier shall provide second-line support and shall be responsible for platform availability, maintenance and security.

## 3. PRICING, WARRANTY AND REPORTING

3.1 **Exhibit A.** Exhibit A to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row:

| Part No. | Product | Description |
|---|---|---|
| SVD-SL-SUB | SorundLink Subscription | Twelve (12) month SorundLink Platform subscription, one Customer location, up to four (4) connected analyzers |

3.2 **Exhibit B.** Exhibit B to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row:

| Part No. | Product | Distributor Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| SVD-SL-SUB | SorundLink Subscription | $1,140.00 per location per twelve (12) month subscription period |

3.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice each SorundLink Subscription on its start date and on the start date of each renewal period. Section 5.3 of the Agreement does not apply to SorundLink Subscriptions.

3.4 **Platform Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that the SorundLink Platform will perform materially in accordance with its published documentation during each subscription period. For any breach of this warranty, Supplier shall, at its option, correct the nonconformity or refund the unused portion of the subscription fee paid for the affected Customer location. Section 9.1 of the Agreement does not apply to SorundLink Subscriptions. Sections 9.3 and 9.5 of the Agreement apply to them.

3.5 **Reporting.** Distributor's quarterly reports under Section 3.4 of the Agreement shall also list SorundLink Subscriptions sold, renewed and lapsed during the quarter, by Customer location and State.

## 4. LAUNCH SUPPORT

4.1 **Training.** Within sixty (60) days after the Amendment Effective Date, Supplier shall provide up to two (2) virtual training sessions on the SorundLink Platform for Distributor's sales and customer-support personnel, at Supplier's cost.

4.2 **Demonstration Accounts.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with up to ten (10) demonstration accounts on the SorundLink Platform without charge for as long as this Amendment remains in effect.

4.3 **Marketing Materials.** Supplier shall provide SorundLink brochures and digital assets for use in the Territory. Distributor's use of those materials is subject to Section 8 of the Agreement.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. References in the Agreement to "this Agreement" include the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, as provided in Section 16.1 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
