## 1. PURPOSE AND BASIS

**1.1 Purpose.** This memorandum sets out the sources and uses of funds, and the payments and deliveries to be made, at the Closing under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are Fural Media Partners, L.P., as seller ("**Seller**"), and Subirt Pictures Group, LLC, as buyer ("**Buyer**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding Shares of Nightjar Releasing, Inc. (the "**Company**").

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this memorandum have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Basis of Calculation.** The amounts in this memorandum are calculated under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement. They use the estimates in the Estimated Closing Statement that Seller delivered to Buyer on March 24, 2026 under Section 2.3(a) of the Purchase Agreement. They also reflect:

(a) the Payoff Letters delivered under Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the final invoices for the Estimated Transaction Expenses delivered with the Estimated Closing Statement; and

(c) the written designation of Seller's account delivered by Seller on March 27, 2026 under Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Authority.** Buyer prepares and signs this memorandum under the resolutions of its board of managers effective as of March 6, 2026. Those resolutions authorize the Chief Financial Officer to initiate and approve the Closing wires, consistent with the final funds flow memorandum, and require each wire instruction to be verified by telephone call-back. Seller countersigns to confirm the amounts payable to it and the account instructions it has provided.

**1.5 Effect.** This memorandum is an operational document for carrying out the payments required at the Closing. It does not amend, waive or interpret any provision of the Purchase Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement. Any amount in it remains subject to the post-Closing adjustment under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. SUMMARY OF PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Base Purchase Price.** The Base Purchase Price under Section 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement is $28,500,000.

**2.2 Form of Consideration.** At the Closing, Buyer will deliver the Base Purchase Price, as adjusted under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement, in two forms:

(a) the Seller Note, in the original principal amount of $6,000,000, executed by Buyer and delivered to Seller under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(b) cash, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, in the amounts set out in Sections 3 through 6 of this memorandum.

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** The Estimated Closing Statement sets out the following estimates:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Estimated Closing Working Capital | $1,097,400.00 |
| Target Working Capital | $1,150,000.00 |

| Estimated Closing Indebtedness | $2,321,937.50 |

**2.4 Buyer Review.** Buyer's finance department and outside accountants reviewed the Estimated Closing Statement against the Company's trial balance as of February 28, 2026 and the supporting schedules Seller provided. Buyer has no objection to the estimates for purposes of the Closing. Buyer's acceptance of the estimates for the Closing does not limit its rights in preparing the Closing Statement or Seller's rights under the dispute procedure in Section 2.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. PAYOFF OF INDEBTEDNESS

**4.1 Indebtedness Identified.** The only Indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company outstanding immediately before the Closing is the revolving credit facility under the Loan and Security Agreement between the Company and First Meridian Bank, N.A. (the "**Lender**"), dated as of June 14, 2023, as amended (the "**Credit Facility**"). The Estimated Closing Statement identifies no other Indebtedness, including no amount owed to Seller or any of its Affiliates.

**4.2 Payoff Amount.** The Lender's Payoff Letter dated March 27, 2026 states the following amounts payable to repay the Credit Facility in full on April 1, 2026:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Outstanding principal | $2,300,000.00 |
| Accrued and unpaid interest through April 1, 2026 | $18,437.50 |
| Payoff processing fee and Lender's legal fees | $3,500.00 |
| **Total payoff amount** | **$2,321,937.50** |

**4.3 Per Diem.** The Payoff Letter states a per diem interest amount of $591.67 for each day after April 1, 2026 on which payment is not received. If the Closing is delayed, Buyer's finance department will update the payoff amount, and the Cash Purchase Price will be reduced by the same amount. Before releasing any wire, the parties will initial a revised Schedule 1.

**4.4 Release of Encumbrances.** Under the Payoff Letter, the Lender has agreed that, on receipt of the total payoff amount, the following will occur:

(a) all obligations of the Company under the Credit Facility will be satisfied in full;

(b) all Encumbrances granted by the Company to the Lender, including its security interest in the Library Titles and the related receivables, will be released automatically; and

(c) the Lender will promptly file UCC-3 termination statements, deliver releases of its copyright security interests in a form suitable for recordation with the U.S. Copyright Office, and terminate any deposit account control agreements over the Company's accounts.

The Payoff Letter authorizes the Company and Buyer to file those releases if the Lender does not do so within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Date.

**4.5 Payment.** Buyer will pay the total payoff amount on behalf of the Company directly to the Lender, under Section 2.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, using the wire instructions in the Payoff Letter (see Schedule 1, Wire 2).

**5.2 Third-Party Payees.** Buyer will pay each of the three professional advisors on behalf of the Company directly, under Section 2.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement, using the wire instructions on the payee's final invoice (see Schedule 1, Wires 3 through 5). Each advisor has confirmed in writing that its invoice states the full amount due from the Company through the Closing. Each has also confirmed that no further amount will be billed to the Company for services rendered on or before the Closing Date.

**5.3 Change-of-Control Bonuses.** Buyer will fund the change-of-control bonuses and the related employer payroll Taxes to the Company's payroll account at its operating bank (see Schedule 1, Wire 6). The Company will process the bonuses through its regular payroll provider in the first payroll cycle after the Closing Date. It will deduct and withhold the employee portion of all applicable Taxes from the gross bonus amounts and remit the withheld amounts, together with the employer portion, to the appropriate Governmental Authorities. Net bonus amounts will be paid to the employees through payroll. They will not be paid by separate wire.

**5.4 Transfer Taxes.** No transfer, documentary, stamp or similar Taxes are expected to be payable at the Closing in connection with the transfer of the Shares. Any such Taxes later determined to be payable will be borne by Seller and Buyer as provided in Section 7.3 of the Purchase Agreement. They are not reflected in this memorandum.

**5.5 Buyer's Own Expenses.** Buyer's own legal, accounting and advisory fees in connection with the Acquisition are not Transaction Expenses. They are not paid through this memorandum. Buyer will pay them separately under its ordinary accounts payable procedures, as provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. CLOSING SEQUENCE AND TIMING

**9.1 Effective Time.** Under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement, the Closing is deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Pacific Time on the Closing Date. The following sequence applies on April 1, 2026. All times are Pacific Time.

**9.2 Sequence.**

(a) *By 7:00 a.m.* Buyer's Treasurer confirms that Buyer's operating account holds at least $22,447,400.00 in immediately available funds.

(b) *By 7:30 a.m.* Buyer's General Counsel and Seller's counsel confirm by email that all Seller and Buyer deliverables under Sections 3.2 and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement are held in escrow and ready for release, and that the conditions in Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement have been satisfied or waived.

(c) *By 7:45 a.m.* Buyer's Chief Financial Officer approves release of the wires listed on Schedule 1, in the order shown there, through Buyer's treasury platform with dual authorization.

(d) *On release of wires.* Buyer's Treasurer emails the Federal Reserve reference number (IMAD/OMAD) for each wire to the payee contacts listed on Schedule 2 and to Seller's counsel.

(e) *On confirmation of receipt.* Seller's Managing Partner confirms by email receipt of the Cash Purchase Price. The Lender confirms receipt of the payoff amount and the release of its Encumbrances under the Payoff Letter.

(f) *On satisfaction of items (a) through (e).* The parties' counsel release the escrowed signature pages and deliverables. Buyer's General Counsel confirms in writing to Seller's Managing Partner that the Closing has occurred.

(g) *Not earlier than 9:00 a.m.* The joint press release is issued in accordance with its agreed form, after the confirmation in item (f).

**9.3 Federal Reserve Cut-Off.** The parties intend all wires to be released well before the Fedwire cut-off. If any wire has not been received by 1:00 p.m. on the Closing Date, Buyer's Treasurer will contact the sending and receiving banks and keep both parties' counsel informed. A wire that has been released and has a Federal Reserve reference number before the cut-off is treated as sent on the Closing Date for purposes of this memorandum.

**9.4 Delay of Closing.** If the Closing does not occur on April 1, 2026, no wire will be released. The parties will agree in writing on an updated memorandum reflecting the revised Closing Date, the Lender's per diem under Section 4.3, and any other changes. Funds already released will be returned to Buyer's operating account at the payee's expense, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 10. POST-CLOSING MATTERS

**10.1 Closing Statement.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer will prepare and deliver to Seller the Closing Statement under Section 2.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. It will set out Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital, Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses and the resulting Purchase Price. Any resulting payment will be made under Section 2.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement. It will not be made through an amendment of this memorandum.

**10.2 Transaction Expenses Not Invoiced.** If any Transaction Expense not reflected in Section 5 is identified after the Closing, it will be taken into account in the Closing Statement. Any amount not deducted in calculating the Purchase Price as finally determined is subject to Section 9.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.3 Bank Signatories.** Promptly after the Closing, Buyer's Chief Financial Officer will cause the authorized signatories on each of the Company's bank, lockbox and collection accounts listed in Section 4.17 of the Disclosure Schedule to be replaced with individuals designated by Buyer.

**10.4 Account Designation for Post-Closing Receipts.** On the Closing Date, the Company will deliver to Seller written designation of the account into which Post-Closing Receipts are to be remitted under Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 2.4 of the Transition Services Agreement. That account is the Company operating account identified as Wire 6's receiving bank in Schedule 1, under the account name "Nightjar Releasing, Inc. – Operating." The designation letter will be verified with Seller by telephone call-back in the manner described in Section 8.3.

**10.5 Transition Services Fees.** No Service Fees under the Transition Services Agreement are payable at the Closing. The Monthly Service Fee of $42,500 will be invoiced by Seller monthly in arrears and paid by the Company under Section 5.3 of that agreement. The first invoice is expected in May 2026.

**10.6 Seller Note Administration.** Buyer's finance department will maintain the Schedule of Payments, Set-Offs and Transfers attached to the Seller Note and the Note Register. It will also calendar each interest payment date under Section 3.2 of the Seller Note. Any set-off or withholding by Buyer will be made only under Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 4 of the Seller Note, with the required notices.

**10.7 Records.** Buyer's finance department will retain this memorandum, the executed Schedule 2, all wire confirmations and Federal Reserve reference numbers, and the Payoff Letter in the closing files for the Acquisition. A closing set will be distributed electronically to Seller's counsel within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date.
