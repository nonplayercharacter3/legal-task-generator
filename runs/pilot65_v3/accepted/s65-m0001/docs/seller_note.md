FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Subirt Pictures Group, LLC**, a California limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Fural Media Partners, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership (together with its successors and registered assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal sum of Six Million Dollars ($6,000,000), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance. Payments are made at the times, in the manner and subject to the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026, between Holder, as seller, and Maker, as buyer (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Maker issues this Note to Holder at the Closing under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement as partial payment of the Base Purchase Price.

## 1. PRINCIPAL; PROMISE TO PAY; DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Principal Amount.** The original principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). Maker shall pay the outstanding Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts then due under this Note, on the Maturity Date. If paid earlier, it shall do so as provided in Section 5 or Section 8.

**1.2 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Bankruptcy Event**" means, with respect to any Person, that the Person:

(a) commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar Law;

(b) has an involuntary case or proceeding commenced against it under any such Law that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(c) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(d) consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee, custodian or similar official for all or substantially all of its assets.

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given to it in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company**" means Nightjar Releasing, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

"**Default Rate**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.3.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1.

"**Interest Rate**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Issue Date**" means the date of original issuance of this Note set forth above, being the Closing Date under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Maturity Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.

"**Note Register**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.3.

"**Obligations**" means the Principal Amount, all accrued interest on it, and all other amounts payable by Maker under this Note.

"**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness, obligations and liabilities of Maker or the Company for borrowed money owed to any bank, insurance company, commercial finance company or other institutional lender, and any refinancing, replacement or extension of it. Senior Debt includes principal, interest (including interest accruing after a Bankruptcy Event, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses, indemnities and reimbursement obligations.

"**Senior Lender**" means each holder of Senior Debt, and any agent or trustee acting on behalf of holders of Senior Debt.

"**Withheld Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

## 2. MATURITY DATE

The "**Maturity Date**" of this Note is the date that is nine (9) months after the Issue Date, which is the Closing Date. If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, the Maturity Date is the next succeeding Business Day, and interest continues to accrue through that day. On the Maturity Date, Maker shall pay to Holder the entire outstanding Principal Amount and all accrued and unpaid interest. These payments are subject to Section 4 and Section 7.

## 3. INTEREST

**3.1 Rate.** The outstanding Principal Amount bears simple interest from the Issue Date until paid in full at a rate of eight percent (8.0%) per annum (the "**Interest Rate**").

**3.2 Computation and Payment.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Accrued interest is payable in arrears:

(a) quarterly, on the last Business Day of each June, September and December, beginning June 30, 2026; and

(b) on the Maturity Date or any earlier date on which the Principal Amount is paid in full.

**3.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default under Section 8.1(a) or 8.1(b) is continuing, the outstanding Principal Amount bears interest at a rate equal to the Interest Rate plus two percent (2.0%) per annum (the "**Default Rate**"). Interest at the Default Rate is payable on demand.

**3.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest, fee or charge payable under this Note shall exceed the maximum amount permitted by applicable Law. If any amount paid or agreed to be paid would otherwise exceed that maximum, the amount is automatically reduced to the maximum permitted. Any excess received by Holder is applied to the reduction of the Principal Amount or, if the Principal Amount has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 4. SET-OFF

**4.1 Right of Set-Off.** Maker may set off the amount of any Losses to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable by Maker under this Note. This applies only to Losses that have been agreed in writing by Holder or finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction (each, a "**Determined Loss**"). Maker shall apply any Determined Loss first against accrued and unpaid interest and then against the outstanding Principal Amount. Each amount so set off is treated for all purposes of this Note as paid by Maker to Holder on the date of the set-off.

**4.2 Withholding Pending Determination.** If a Buyer Indemnitee has delivered a Claim Notice under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may withhold from any payment due under this Note an amount equal to Maker's good faith estimate of the Losses claimed in that Claim Notice (the "**Withheld Amount**"). This withholding may continue until the claim is finally resolved. Maker shall deposit each Withheld Amount in a segregated account maintained with a federally insured commercial bank. Upon final determination of the claim, Maker shall:

(a) set off against this Note the portion of the Withheld Amount equal to the Determined Loss, under Section 4.1; and

(b) pay to Holder, within five (5) Business Days, the balance of the Withheld Amount, together with interest accrued on that balance at the Interest Rate from the date on which it would otherwise have been payable.

**4.3 Notice of Set-Off.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off or withholding under this Section 4 on or before the date of the affected payment. The notice shall identify the related Claim Notice or Determined Loss and the amount set off or withheld.

**4.4 No Default.** Exercise of the right of set-off or withholding in good faith under this Section 4 does not constitute an Event of Default or other default under this Note. This is so whether or not the related claim is ultimately determined in favor of a Buyer Indemnitee.

## 5. PAYMENTS; PREPAYMENT

**5.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States, in immediately available funds, by wire transfer to the account designated by Holder in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date. Until Holder designates another account, payments shall be made to the account designated by Holder for payment of the Cash Purchase Price under Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement. Payments received after 2:00 p.m. New York City time on any day are deemed received on the next Business Day.

**5.2 Application of Payments.** Each payment under this Note, other than a set-off under Section 4, shall be applied in the following order:

(a) to costs of collection payable under Section 11.6;

(b) to accrued and unpaid interest; and

(c) to the outstanding Principal Amount.

**5.3 Voluntary Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note at any time, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty, upon at least three (3) Business Days' prior written notice to Holder. Each partial prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the principal amount prepaid. Amounts prepaid may not be reborrowed.

**5.4 Withholding Taxes.** Maker may deduct and withhold from any payment under this Note such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under the Code or any other applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated as paid to Holder. Before the Issue Date, Holder has delivered a properly completed IRS Form W-9. Holder shall deliver an updated form on request and upon any change in the information it contains.

**5.5 No Other Offset.** Except as provided in Section 4, Section 5.4 and Section 7, Maker shall make all payments under this Note without set-off, counterclaim or deduction.

## 6. INFORMATION RIGHTS

**6.1 Monthly Reports.** Until the Obligations have been paid in full, Maker shall deliver, or cause the Company to deliver, the following to Holder within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month:

(a) the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as of the end of that month, and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and for the fiscal year to date, prepared in accordance with GAAP, subject to normal year-end adjustments and the absence of notes;

(b) title-by-title performance reports for each Library Title for that month and for the fiscal year to date, setting forth:

(i) gross receipts;

(ii) distribution fees and recoupable expenses; and

(iii) Participations and Residuals accrued and paid,

in each case by territory and by media;

(c) box-office reports for each Library Title in theatrical release during that month, showing exhibitor grosses and film rentals by exhibitor and by territory; and

(d) streaming and digital revenue reports for each Library Title, showing revenue received or accrued from each subscription, advertising-supported and transactional video-on-demand platform, together with the platform statements received by the Company for that month.

**6.2 Annual Financial Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of the Company ending while any Obligations remain outstanding, Maker shall deliver to Holder the balance sheet of the Company as of the end of that fiscal year and the related statements of income, stockholders' equity and cash flows for that fiscal year. These statements shall be audited or reviewed by an independent accounting firm.

**6.3 Notices.** Maker shall give Holder prompt written notice, and in any event within five (5) Business Days, of:

(a) the occurrence of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time or both would constitute an Event of Default;

(b) any default under any Senior Debt; and

(c) any Bankruptcy Event with respect to Maker or the Company.

**6.4 Additional Information.** Maker shall furnish Holder with such additional information regarding the business, financial condition and operations of the Company as Holder may reasonably request from time to time.

## 7. SUBORDINATION

**7.1 Subordination.** Maker agrees, and Holder by its acceptance of this Note agrees, that the Obligations are subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent and in the manner set forth in this Section 7.

**7.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment or bankruptcy default on Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note when due. If a payment default on Senior Debt has occurred and is continuing, or a Senior Lender has delivered written notice of a payment blockage to Holder, Maker shall not make any payment under this Note during the period beginning on the date of that default or notice and ending on the earliest of:

(a) the date on which the default is cured or waived;

(b) the date on which the Senior Lender withdraws the notice; and

(c) one hundred eighty (180) days after the Senior Lender delivered the notice.

Not more than one payment blockage notice may be delivered in any period of three hundred sixty-five (365) consecutive days.

**7.3 Insolvency.** Upon any distribution of the assets of Maker in any Bankruptcy Event, liquidation, dissolution or winding up, all Senior Debt shall first be paid in full in cash before Holder is entitled to receive any payment in respect of the Obligations.

**7.4 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment on this Note in contravention of this Section 7, Holder shall hold that payment in trust for the Senior Lenders and promptly pay it over to them. Any such payment shall be applied to the Senior Debt until it is paid in full.

**7.5 Obligations Unconditional.** Nothing in this Section 7 impairs, as between Maker and Holder, the obligation of Maker, which is absolute and unconditional, to pay the Obligations when due, subject to Section 4. A failure to pay any amount when due by reason of Section 7.2 is not an Event of Default under Section 8.1(a). However, the Obligations continue to accrue interest at the Interest Rate, and the deferred amount becomes due and payable on the first Business Day after the blockage period ends.

**7.6 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, enter into a customary subordination agreement with any Senior Lender. The agreement shall be consistent with this Section 7 and in form reasonably acceptable to Holder.

## 8. EVENTS OF DEFAULT; REMEDIES

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, and the failure continues for five (5) Business Days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(b) Maker fails to pay any interest or other amount under this Note when due, and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(c) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 6, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Holder gives Maker written notice of it;

(d) a Bankruptcy Event occurs with respect to Maker or the Company;

(e) Senior Debt in an aggregate principal amount exceeding $2,500,000 is accelerated as a result of a default by Maker or the Company, and the acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days; or

(f) Maker sells, transfers or otherwise disposes of a majority of the outstanding capital stock of the Company, or the Company sells all or substantially all of its assets, in either case to a Person that is not an Affiliate of Maker.

A set-off or withholding under Section 4, and a deferral of payment under Section 7.2, is not an Event of Default.

**8.2 Remedies.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any Event of Default, and subject to Section 7, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire outstanding Principal Amount, all accrued and unpaid interest, and all other Obligations immediately due and payable. Upon any Event of Default under Section 8.1(d), all Obligations automatically become immediately due and payable without notice or other action. Holder may exercise any other right or remedy available to it under this Note or applicable Law, in each case subject to Section 4 and Section 7.

**8.3 Remedies Cumulative.** The rights and remedies of Holder under this Note are cumulative and not exclusive of any rights or remedies otherwise available, subject to Section 4 and Section 7.

## 9. TRANSFER; REGISTRATION

**9.1 Transfer by Holder.** Holder may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, or any interest in it, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker. Each transfer must comply with the Securities Act and applicable state securities Laws. Upon any transfer, the transferee becomes Holder for all purposes of this Note with respect to the interest transferred, and is entitled to all rights of Holder under this Note.

**9.2 Transfer by Maker.** Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder. Any purported assignment by Maker in violation of this Section 9.2 is void.

**9.3 Registration.** Maker shall maintain at its principal office a register (the "**Note Register**") recording the name and address of each Holder and the principal amount of this Note held by each. Maker shall record a transfer in the Note Register within five (5) Business Days after Maker receives:

(a) written notice of the transfer, executed by the transferor and the transferee and identifying the transferee, its notice address and the principal amount transferred; and

(b) the transferee's properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8.

Until a transfer is recorded, Maker may treat the Person in whose name this Note is registered as its owner for all purposes. Payment to that Person discharges Maker's obligation to the extent of the payment.

**9.4 Replacement Notes.** Upon surrender of this Note for registration of a transfer of all or part of it, Maker shall execute and deliver to the transferee, and to the transferor if a portion is retained, one or more new notes of like tenor in an aggregate principal amount equal to the outstanding Principal Amount. Each new note shall be dated as of the Issue Date and bear interest from the date to which interest has been paid on this Note.

**9.5 Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and inures to the benefit of Holder and its successors and assigns.
