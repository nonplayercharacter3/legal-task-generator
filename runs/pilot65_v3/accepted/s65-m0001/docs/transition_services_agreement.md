# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of April 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Fural Media Partners, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Provider**"), and **Nightjar Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Subirt Pictures Group, LLC, a California limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates performed collection, accounting, delivery and related administrative functions for Recipient in connection with the distribution of the Library Titles.

C. For a transitional period after the Closing, Recipient wishes to obtain from Provider, and Provider has agreed to provide, those functions on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement. This Agreement is the Transition Services Agreement referred to in the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Term, Provider shall provide, or cause one or more of its Affiliates to provide, to Recipient the services described in the Service Schedule (together with any Additional Services and any other services expressed in this Agreement to constitute Services, the "**Services**"). The Services fall into the following categories, each as more fully described in the Service Schedule:

(a) *Collection Services*: the receipt, reconciliation and processing of amounts payable by exhibitors, platforms, sub-distributors, aggregators, licensees and collection account managers in respect of the Library Titles;

(b) *Accounting Services*: the preparation of distribution statements, participation and residual statements, accounts receivable aging reports and month-end close support for Recipient;

(c) *Delivery Services*: the coordination and fulfillment of physical and digital delivery of the Library Titles to licensees, including management of delivery schedules, quality-control reports and laboratory and vault relationships; and

(d) *Systems Access*: continued access for designated Recipient personnel to Provider's rights-management, royalty-accounting and avails systems, to the extent those systems contain Recipient Data.

**2.2 Scope.** Unless otherwise provided in the Service Schedule, each Service shall be provided in substantially the same manner, scope, frequency and level of detail as the corresponding function was performed by or on behalf of Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date. Provider is not required to provide any Service in a manner that would require it to:

(a) hire additional employees, other than to replace Provider Personnel who cease to be available;

(b) acquire additional software licenses or equipment, except as set forth in the Service Schedule; or

(c) violate any applicable Law or any Contract binding on Provider as of the Effective Date.

**2.3 Omitted and Additional Services.** If, within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date, Recipient identifies any service that was provided to Recipient by Provider or its Affiliates during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date and that is not included in the Service Schedule (an "**Omitted Service**"), Provider shall provide that service on request. Recipient may also request other reasonable transitional services (together with Omitted Services, "**Additional Services**"). The Parties shall negotiate in good faith an amendment to the Service Schedule describing each Additional Service, its Service Term and its fee. The fee for any Omitted Service shall be determined on the same cost basis as the Monthly Service Fee. Upon execution of that amendment, each Additional Service constitutes a Service for all purposes of this Agreement.

**2.4 Post-Closing Receipts.** During the Service Term for Collection Services, Provider shall continue to receive into the Collection Accounts all Post-Closing Receipts remitted to Provider or its Affiliates, and shall identify, reconcile and remit those Post-Closing Receipts to Recipient. Provider's receipt, holding, reconciliation and remittance of Post-Closing Receipts shall constitute Services under this Agreement. Without limiting the Service Schedule, Provider shall:

(a) remit all Post-Closing Receipts to the account designated in writing by Recipient, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, within five (5) Business Days after receipt;

(b) accompany each remittance with a statement identifying the payor, the Library Title, the territory, the media and the period to which the remittance relates;

(c) deliver to Recipient, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, a reconciliation of all amounts received into the Collection Accounts during that month; and

(d) promptly forward to Recipient all statements, reports, audit notices and correspondence received from any payor in respect of the Library Titles.

**2.5 Redirection of Payors.** Provider shall cooperate with Recipient to redirect payors from the Collection Accounts to accounts designated by Recipient as promptly as practicable. Provider shall deliver redirection notices, in a form reasonably acceptable to Recipient, to each payor identified by Recipient within ten (10) Business Days after Recipient's request.

**2.6 Excluded Services.** Unless expressly set forth in the Service Schedule, the Services do not include:

(a) legal, tax advisory or investment banking advice;

(b) the acquisition of new distribution rights or the negotiation of new license agreements on behalf of Recipient; or

(c) any service that Provider or its Affiliates did not perform for Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date, other than Additional Services agreed under Section 2.3.

**2.7 Third-Party Consents.** If the provision of any Service requires the consent of a third party, including any software licensor, laboratory or vault, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent promptly. Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket consent fees approved in advance by Recipient. If a required consent cannot be obtained, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a reasonable alternative arrangement that provides Recipient with substantially the same benefit.

## 3. STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

**3.1 Standard of Care.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a timely, professional and workmanlike manner;

(b) with at least the same degree of care, skill, accuracy and priority as Provider exercised in performing the corresponding functions for Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date; and

(c) in compliance with all applicable Laws

(collectively, the "**Applicable Standard**").

**3.2 Personnel.** Provider shall assign Provider Personnel with appropriate skill, training and experience to perform the Services. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the individuals identified in the Service Schedule as key personnel assigned to the Services throughout the applicable Service Term. Provider Personnel remain employees or contractors of Provider or its Affiliates, and Provider is solely responsible for their compensation, benefits, withholding and supervision.

**3.3 Records.** Provider shall maintain complete and accurate books and records relating to the Services, the Collection Accounts and the Service Fees. Recipient and its representatives may inspect and copy those books and records during normal business hours on reasonable advance notice, during the term of this Agreement and for one (1) year after its expiration or termination.

**3.4 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 4. GOVERNANCE AND COOPERATION

**4.1 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall designate, by written notice to the other within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date, an individual to act as its primary point of contact for the administration of this Agreement (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). Each Party may replace its Service Coordinator by written notice to the other.

**4.2 Meetings.** The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by videoconference, at least once every two (2) weeks during the first three (3) months of the term and at least monthly thereafter. At these meetings they shall review the performance of the Services, the status of payor redirection and the progress of Recipient's migration away from the Services.

**4.3 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with such information, access and decisions as Provider reasonably requires to perform the Services, in a timely manner. Provider is not in breach of this Agreement to the extent its failure to perform is caused by Recipient's failure to comply with this Section 4.3, provided that Provider notifies Recipient promptly of that failure.

**4.4 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall cooperate in good faith with Recipient and Buyer to transition each Service to Recipient or a third-party provider designated by Recipient before the end of the applicable Service Term. Migration assistance includes:

(a) providing extracts of all Recipient Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format;

(b) providing reasonable access to Provider Personnel for knowledge transfer; and

(c) transferring to Recipient, to the extent transferable, any licenses, laboratory access letters or vendor arrangements used solely for Recipient.

**4.5 Escalation.** Any dispute concerning the performance of the Services shall first be referred to the Service Coordinators. If they do not resolve the dispute within ten (10) Business Days, it shall be referred to a senior executive of each Party with authority to settle it. If those executives do not resolve the dispute within a further ten (10) Business Days, either Party may pursue its remedies under Section 11.6. Nothing in this Section 4.5 prevents a Party from seeking injunctive relief at any time.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Monthly Service Fee.** In consideration of the Services described in the Service Schedule as of the Effective Date, Recipient shall pay Provider a fee of $42,500 per calendar month (the "**Monthly Service Fee**"), prorated for any partial month. The Monthly Service Fee is reduced, effective as of the first day of the month after termination, by the amount allocated in the Service Schedule to any Service terminated under Section 6.2.

**5.2 Out-of-Pocket Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs incurred by Provider in performing the Services, including laboratory, shipping, storage and courier charges, to the extent:

(a) set forth in the Service Schedule; or

(b) approved in writing in advance by Recipient's Service Coordinator.

Out-of-pocket costs shall be passed through at cost, without markup.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month. Each invoice shall itemize the Monthly Service Fee, any fees for Additional Services and any reimbursable costs, with reasonable supporting documentation. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall resolve any dispute under Section 4.5. Provider shall not suspend any Service by reason of a disputed amount.

**5.5 Taxes.** The Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value added and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes properly imposed on the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees. Each Party shall cooperate with the other to minimize any such Taxes to the extent permitted by Law.

**5.6 No Set-Off by Provider.** Provider shall not set off or deduct any Service Fees or other amounts owed by Recipient against any Post-Closing Receipts or other amounts held for Recipient.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

**6.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Term, unless terminated earlier under this Section 6. The initial Service Term for each Service is six (6) months after the Effective Date, unless the Service Schedule provides otherwise.

**6.2 Early Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Provider. If the Service Schedule identifies a Service as dependent on another Service, Provider shall promptly notify Recipient of that dependency on receipt of a termination notice, and Recipient may withdraw or modify its notice.

**6.3 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Term for any Service once, for up to three (3) additional months, by written notice to Provider delivered at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current Service Term. The fee for any extended Service shall be the fee allocated to that Service in the Service Schedule.

**6.4 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. Provider may terminate this Agreement for Recipient's non-payment only if undisputed Service Fees remain unpaid for more than forty-five (45) days after a second written notice of non-payment.

**6.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service:

(a) Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of expiration or termination;

(b) Provider shall remit to Recipient all Post-Closing Receipts then held by Provider or its Affiliates, and shall continue to remit any Post-Closing Receipts later received in accordance with Section 2.4(a) and (b);

(c) Provider shall deliver to Recipient all Recipient Data in its possession or control, in the format described in Section 4.4(a); and

(d) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 8.3.

**6.6 Survival.** Sections 2.4(a), 2.4(b), 2.4(d), 3.3, 5, 6.5, 6.6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 7. DATA AND SYSTEMS

**7.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider shall use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services and shall not sell, license or otherwise make Recipient Data available to any third party, other than its Affiliates and subcontractors performing the Services.

**7.2 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Recipient Data at least as protective as those it applies to its own data of a similar nature, and in any event consistent with reasonable industry practice for independent film distributors. Provider shall notify Recipient within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Recipient Data.

**7.3 Systems Access.** Each Party shall comply with the reasonable security and acceptable-use policies of the other Party, as notified in writing, when accessing the other Party's systems. Access is limited to the personnel and purposes necessary for the Services or for migration under Section 4.4.

**7.4 Intellectual Property.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any right, title or interest in or to the intellectual property of the other Party. Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, royalty-free license, for the term of this Agreement, to use any Provider software, templates or materials made available to Recipient solely for the purpose of receiving the Services.

**8.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether before or after the Effective Date. Recipient Data is the Confidential Information of Recipient. Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, without obligation of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or

(d) is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law, provided that it gives the other Party prompt notice and cooperates with any effort to obtain a protective order.

**8.3 Return or Destruction.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, each Party shall, at the other Party's request, return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information. A Party may retain copies to the extent required by Law or its bona fide document retention policies, provided that those copies remain subject to this Section 8.
