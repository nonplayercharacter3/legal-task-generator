# UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF SUBIRT PICTURES GROUP, LLC

**Effective as of March 6, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the board of managers (the "**Board**") of **Subirt Pictures Group, LLC**, a California limited liability company (the "**Company**" or "**Buyer**"), act under Section 17704.07 of the California Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act and Section 6.9 of the Second Amended and Restated Operating Agreement of the Company (the "**Operating Agreement**"). Acting without a meeting, they consent to and adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent, effective as of the date set forth above. They waive all requirements of notice, whether under the Operating Agreement, applicable law or otherwise.

## 1. BACKGROUND

**1.1 The Proposed Acquisition.** The Company proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Nightjar Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Nightjar**"), from **Fural Media Partners, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"). Nightjar is an independent film distributor whose library consists of 41 motion pictures listed on Schedule 4.9 to the Purchase Agreement (the "**Library Titles**"). The acquisition of the Shares and the related transactions described in this Consent are together the "**Acquisition**."

**1.2 Purchase Agreement.** The Acquisition will be effected under a Stock Purchase Agreement between Seller and the Company, to be dated as of March 9, 2026. Execution drafts of that agreement, including the form of Schedule 4.9 and Exhibits A and B, have been circulated to the Board. The agreement, in substantially the form so circulated and with such changes as are approved under Section 2.3, is the "**Purchase Agreement**." Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Consideration.** Under the Purchase Agreement, the aggregate consideration for the Shares is a Base Purchase Price of $28,500,000, subject to the adjustments provided in Sections 2.2 and 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. At the Closing, the Company will pay the Base Purchase Price as follows:

(a) the Cash Purchase Price, by wire transfer of immediately available funds; and

(b) the remainder, by the issuance to Seller of a subordinated promissory note in the original principal amount of $6,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**"), substantially in the form of Exhibit B to the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4 Seller Note.** The Seller Note will bear simple interest at eight percent (8.0%) per annum, payable quarterly in arrears. Its Maturity Date is nine (9) months after the Issue Date, which is the Closing Date. The Seller Note will be subordinated in right of payment to Senior Debt as provided in it.

**1.5 Closing.** The Purchase Agreement provides for the Closing to take place on April 1, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may mutually agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.6 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Nightjar and Seller will enter into a Transition Services Agreement, substantially in the form of Exhibit A to the Purchase Agreement (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"). Under that agreement, Seller will provide transitional collection, accounting, delivery and systems access services to Nightjar for an initial period of six (6) months after the Closing, for a Monthly Service Fee of $42,500. Under Section 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement, the Company will cause Nightjar to execute the Transition Services Agreement at the Closing, acting at the Company's direction following the transfer of the Shares.

**1.7 Rights Bill of Sale.** Before the date of the Purchase Agreement, Fural Rights Holdings, LLC, an Affiliate of Seller, conveyed rights in the Library Titles to Nightjar under a Bill of Sale and Assignment dated as of February 27, 2026 (the "**Rights Bill of Sale**"). The Rights Bill of Sale is not an agreement to which the Company is a party. A copy has been made available to the Board as part of the materials described in Section 1.8.

**1.8 Materials Reviewed.** In connection with this Consent, the Board has received the following:

(a) the execution draft of the Purchase Agreement, including the form of Schedule 4.9 and the draft Disclosure Schedule;

(b) the form of Seller Note;

(c) the form of Transition Services Agreement;

(d) a copy of the Rights Bill of Sale;

(e) the agreed form of the joint press release to be issued on the Closing Date;

(f) the draft closing funds flow memorandum prepared by the Company's finance department; and

(g) summaries of the legal, financial, tax and chain-of-title due diligence conducted by the Company's officers and outside advisors.

The Board has also had the opportunity to ask questions of the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and the Company's outside counsel regarding the Acquisition.

**1.9 Board Determination.** After considering the materials described in Section 1.8 and such other matters as it has deemed relevant, the Board has determined that the Acquisition is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Board adopts the following resolutions:

## 2. APPROVAL OF THE ACQUISITION AND THE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**2.1 Approval of the Acquisition.** RESOLVED, that the Acquisition, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Purchase Agreement, is authorized and approved in all respects.

**2.2 Approval of the Purchase Agreement.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the Company's performance of its obligations under it, are authorized and approved in all respects. This approval includes the payment of the Base Purchase Price as adjusted under Sections 2.2 and 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.3 Execution and Delivery.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each of the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Company, on or about March 9, 2026. Each Authorized Officer may approve such changes to the execution draft as that officer, after consultation with the Company's outside counsel, determines to be necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company. However, any change in any of the following requires the further approval of the Board:

(a) the Base Purchase Price;

(b) the principal amount, interest rate or Maturity Date of the Seller Note; or

(c) the number of Library Titles.

The execution and delivery of the Purchase Agreement by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that officer's approval of any such change.

**2.4 Disclosure Schedule.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to receive and review the final Disclosure Schedule on behalf of the Company. Each Authorized Officer is directed to report to the Board, before the Purchase Agreement is executed, any matter in the final Disclosure Schedule that differs materially from the draft provided to the Board.

## 3. APPROVAL OF THE SELLER NOTE

**3.1 Approval.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Seller Note are authorized and approved in all respects. The Company's issuance of the Seller Note to Seller at the Closing in the original principal amount of $6,000,000, as partial payment of the Base Purchase Price, is also authorized and approved.

**3.2 Execution and Delivery.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Seller Note in the name and on behalf of the Company at the Closing. The Seller Note shall be substantially in the form of Exhibit B to the Purchase Agreement, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves within the limits set forth in Section 2.3.

**3.3 Payment Obligations.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company is authorized to pay, when due, all principal, interest and other amounts payable under the Seller Note. Each Authorized Officer is authorized to cause those payments to be made. In each case, payment is subject to the Company's rights of set-off and withholding under Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 4 of the Seller Note, and to the subordination provisions of the Seller Note.

**3.4 Note Register.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to establish and maintain the Note Register at the Company's principal office, as required by the Seller Note.

**3.5 Reporting.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to cause the reports, financial statements and notices required to be delivered under the Seller Note to be prepared and delivered when due.

**3.6 Subordination Agreements.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to request that the Holder of the Seller Note enter into a subordination agreement with any Senior Lender, as contemplated by the Seller Note. Each Authorized Officer is also authorized to negotiate and execute any such subordination agreement to which the Company is a party.

## 4. TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

**4.1 Approval.** RESOLVED, that the form, terms and provisions of the Transition Services Agreement are authorized and approved in all respects.

**4.2 Direction to Nightjar.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that, effective upon the transfer of the Shares to the Company at the Closing, the Company, as the sole stockholder of Nightjar, is authorized to cause Nightjar to execute and deliver the Transition Services Agreement. The agreement shall be substantially in the form of Exhibit A to the Purchase Agreement. The Company is also authorized to cause Nightjar to perform its obligations under that agreement, including the payment of the Monthly Service Fee and reimbursable costs.

**4.3 Service Coordinator.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to cause Nightjar to designate its Service Coordinator within the period required by the Transition Services Agreement. The Chief Financial Officer is also directed to report to the Board at each regular meeting during the term of that agreement on:

(a) the status of the Services;

(b) the redirection of payors from the Collection Accounts; and

(c) Nightjar's migration plan.

## 5. FUNDING OF THE CASH PURCHASE PRICE

**5.1 Funding.** RESOLVED, that the Company is authorized to fund the Cash Purchase Price and the other amounts payable by it at the Closing under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement from the Company's available cash. Those other amounts include the payoff of Nightjar's Indebtedness identified in the Payoff Letters and the payment of the Estimated Transaction Expenses.

**5.2 Wire Transfers.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is authorized to initiate and approve the wire transfers required at the Closing, consistent with the final closing funds flow memorandum. All wire instructions shall first be verified by telephone call-back to a known contact, in accordance with the Company's treasury procedures.

**5.3 Funds Flow Memorandum.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to approve and sign the final closing funds flow memorandum on behalf of the Company. The memorandum shall be consistent with the Estimated Closing Statement delivered by Seller under Section 2.3(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. CLOSING DELIVERABLES AND CERTIFICATES

**6.1 Buyer Deliverables.** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, on behalf of the Company, each certificate, instrument and other document to be delivered by the Company under Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement. This includes the certificate required under Section 8.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Secretary's Certificate.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Secretary of the Company, or any Authorized Officer, is authorized to certify these resolutions to Seller under Section 3.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement. That officer is also authorized to certify the incumbency of the officers executing the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements on behalf of the Company.

**6.3 Waiver of Conditions.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to determine, on behalf of the Company, whether the conditions set forth in Section 8.2 of the Purchase Agreement have been satisfied. However, no Authorized Officer may waive any of the following conditions without the prior approval of the Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel, acting together:

(a) the condition in Section 8.2(a), as it relates to the Fundamental Representations;

(b) the condition in Section 8.2(d); or

(c) the condition in Section 8.2(e).

The Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel shall promptly report any such waiver to the Board.

**6.4 Seller Deliverables.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the General Counsel is directed to confirm, before the Closing, receipt of each item to be delivered by Seller under Section 3.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Those items include the stock certificates and stock powers for the Shares, the Resignation Letters, the Payoff Letters and the laboratory access letters in respect of the Library Titles.

**6.5 Resignation Letters.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Executive Officer is authorized to designate, on behalf of the Company, the directors and officers of Nightjar whose resignations are to be delivered at the Closing. That designation shall be made not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, as required by the definition of Resignation Letters in the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. POST-CLOSING GOVERNANCE OF NIGHTJAR

**7.1 Action as Sole Stockholder.** RESOLVED, that, effective upon the transfer of the Shares to the Company at the Closing, the Company, as the sole stockholder of Nightjar, is authorized to take the actions described in this Section 7 by written consent of the sole stockholder of Nightjar, in accordance with the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and Nightjar's bylaws. Each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute that consent on behalf of the Company.

**7.2 Board of Directors of Nightjar.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the following individuals are elected as the directors of Nightjar, effective as of the Closing, to fill the vacancies created by the Resignation Letters. Each shall serve until his or her successor is duly elected and qualified, or until his or her earlier death, resignation or removal:

(a) Evelyn Subirt;

(b) Jonah Whitcombe; and

(c) Priya Nandakumar.

**7.3 Officers of Nightjar.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company recommends that the board of directors of Nightjar, at its first meeting or by written consent after the Closing, appoint officers of Nightjar as the Chief Executive Officer of the Company may recommend.

**7.4 Bank Accounts.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed, promptly after the Closing, to cause the authorized signatories on each bank account, lockbox and collection account of Nightjar listed in Section 4.17 of the Disclosure Schedule to be replaced with individuals designated by the Company.

**7.5 Payor Redirection.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to cause Nightjar to designate in writing the account into which Post-Closing Receipts are to be remitted under Section 6.9 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 2.4 of the Transition Services Agreement. The Chief Financial Officer is also directed to cause Nightjar to request redirection notices to payors under Section 2.5 of the Transition Services Agreement, as promptly as practicable after the Closing.

**7.6 Copyright Recordation.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the General Counsel is directed to cause Nightjar to record with the U.S. Copyright Office the Short-Form Assignments delivered under the Rights Bill of Sale, to the extent not recorded before the Closing. The General Counsel is also directed, where appropriate, to request any confirmatory instruments from Seller under Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.7 Insurance.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to cause Nightjar to be added to the Company's general liability, media liability and errors-and-omissions insurance programs, effective as of the Closing. If the Chief Financial Officer determines it advisable, Nightjar's existing errors-and-omissions coverage for the Library Titles may instead be maintained.

**7.8 Indemnification of Nightjar Directors and Officers.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Company shall comply with Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement regarding the exculpation and indemnification provisions in Nightjar's organizational documents.

## 8. PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

**8.1 Press Release.** RESOLVED, that the agreed form of joint press release announcing the completion of the Acquisition, prepared in cooperation with Seller under Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement, is approved.

**8.2 Issuance.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel are authorized to cause the press release to be issued on the Closing Date, in accordance with its terms, after the General Counsel has confirmed the Closing in writing to Seller.

**8.3 Further Announcements.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that no officer, manager or employee of the Company shall make any other public statement concerning the Acquisition except:

(a) as permitted by the approved form of press release and its spokesperson guidance; or

(b) with the prior approval of the Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel, and in compliance with Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. INTEGRATION AND OVERSIGHT

**9.1 Integration Plan.** RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer is directed to prepare and present to the Board, within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, a plan for the integration of Nightjar's operations, personnel and systems into the Company. The plan shall include the migration of the Services provided under the Transition Services Agreement before the end of the applicable Service Terms.

**9.2 Purchase Price Adjustment.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is directed to prepare the Closing Statement and deliver it to Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, as required by Section 2.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Chief Financial Officer shall report to the Board on the final determination of the Purchase Price.

**9.3 Indemnification Claims.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the General Counsel is authorized to deliver Claim Notices on behalf of the Company and the other Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The General Counsel is also authorized to respond to any Claim Notice delivered by Seller, and to manage any related claim, in consultation with the Chief Executive Officer. The General Counsel shall keep a schedule of the survival periods set forth in Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement and of each Claim Notice delivered or received, and shall report on these matters to the Board at each regular meeting.

**9.4 Set-Off and Withholding.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer, with the approval of the General Counsel, is authorized to exercise on behalf of the Company the rights of set-off and withholding under Section 9.8 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 4 of the Seller Note. This includes depositing any Withheld Amount in a segregated account and giving the related notices to the Holder.

**9.5 Tax Matters.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Financial Officer is authorized to take such actions on behalf of the Company and, after the Closing, on behalf of Nightjar, as are required or appropriate under Article VII of the Purchase Agreement. These include reviewing and commenting on Tax returns prepared by Seller and preparing and filing Tax returns for Straddle Periods.

## 10. GENERAL AUTHORITY

**10.1 General Authorization.** RESOLVED, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all such further actions as that officer determines to be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions and to consummate the Acquisition. These include actions to:

(a) execute and deliver all certificates, instruments, notices, consents, filings and other documents;

(b) pay all fees and expenses, including fees of legal, accounting and financial advisors; and

(c) engage such advisors and agents.

Taking any such action is conclusive evidence of that officer's determination.

**10.2 Delegation.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each Authorized Officer may delegate to any other officer or employee of the Company the authority to execute and deliver any certificate, notice or other ministerial document contemplated by these resolutions. Any delegation shall be in writing and shall not extend to the execution of the Purchase Agreement or the Seller Note.

**10.3 Ratification.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that all actions previously taken by any manager, officer, employee or agent of the Company in connection with the Acquisition and the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects. These include the negotiation of the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and the conduct of due diligence regarding Nightjar and the Library Titles.

**10.4 Conflicts of Interest.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that each manager signing this Consent confirms that he or she has no direct or indirect financial interest in Seller, Fural Rights Holdings, LLC, Nightjar or any of their Affiliates, other than through his or her interest in the Company.

## 11. MEMBER MATTERS

**11.1 Member Approval Not Required.** RESOLVED, that the Board has determined, on the advice of the General Counsel, that the Acquisition does not constitute a "Major Decision" requiring the approval of the members of the Company under Section 7.3 of the Operating Agreement. The Board has also determined that no consent of the members is required for the Company to enter into the Purchase Agreement, issue the Seller Note or otherwise consummate the Acquisition.

**11.2 Report to Members.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Executive Officer is directed to report the Acquisition to the members of the Company in the next regular quarterly report delivered under the Operating Agreement.
