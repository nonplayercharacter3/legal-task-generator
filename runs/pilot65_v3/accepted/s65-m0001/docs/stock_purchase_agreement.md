# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026, by and between **Fural Media Partners, L.P.**, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), and **Subirt Pictures Group, LLC**, a California limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Nightjar Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in the acquisition, licensing, distribution and exploitation of independent motion pictures.

B. Before the date of this Agreement, Fural Rights Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an Affiliate of Seller ("**Rights Holdings**"), conveyed to the Company the Distribution Rights in the Library Titles under the Rights Bill of Sale.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

D. At the Closing, Buyer and Seller will enter into the Transition Services Agreement. Buyer will also issue the Seller Note to Seller as partial payment of the Base Purchase Price.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements contained in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Base Purchase Price.** At the Closing, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The aggregate consideration for the Shares is $28,500,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), as adjusted under Sections 2.2 and 2.3 (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or deliver the following:

(a) to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount (the "**Cash Purchase Price**") equal to:

(i) the Base Purchase Price, *minus*

(ii) the principal amount of the Seller Note, *minus*

(iii) the Estimated Closing Indebtedness, *minus*

(iv) the Estimated Transaction Expenses, *plus or minus*

(v) the amount, if any, by which the Estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or is less than the Target Working Capital;

(b) to Seller, the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;

(c) on behalf of the Company, to the holders of Indebtedness identified in the Payoff Letters, the amounts specified in those Payoff Letters; and

(d) on behalf of the Company, to the payees of the Estimated Transaction Expenses, the amounts set forth in the invoices delivered under Section 2.3(a).

**2.3 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* Not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good faith estimates, with reasonable supporting detail, of:

(i) Closing Working Capital (the "**Estimated Closing Working Capital**");

(ii) Indebtedness as of immediately before the Closing (the "**Estimated Closing Indebtedness**"); and

(iii) Transaction Expenses (the "**Estimated Transaction Expenses**").

Seller shall deliver final invoices for all Estimated Transaction Expenses with the Estimated Closing Statement.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Buyer's good faith calculation of Closing Working Capital, Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses, and the resulting Purchase Price.

(c) *Dispute.* Seller has forty-five (45) days after receipt of the Closing Statement (the "**Review Period**") to review it. During the Review Period, Buyer shall give Seller and its representatives reasonable access to the relevant books and records of the Company. If Seller disagrees with the Closing Statement, it shall deliver to Buyer, before the end of the Review Period, a written notice of objection setting forth in reasonable detail each disputed item and the basis for the dispute (a "**Statement of Objections**"). Items not disputed in a timely Statement of Objections are final and binding. If the Parties cannot resolve the disputed items within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Statement of Objections, either Party may submit the remaining disputed items to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually acceptable to the Parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, and shall resolve only the remaining disputed items. Its determination shall be within the range of values asserted by the Parties, shall be delivered within thirty (30) days after its engagement, and shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The Independent Accountant's fees shall be borne by Seller and Buyer in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed items.

(d) *Payment.* Within five (5) Business Days after the Purchase Price is finally determined, one of the following payments shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(i) if the Purchase Price as finally determined exceeds the amount calculated using the estimates in the Estimated Closing Statement, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller; or

(ii) if it is less, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer and the Company may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under the Code or any other applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.
