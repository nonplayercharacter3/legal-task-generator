# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and entered into as of February 27, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by **Fural Rights Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Transferor**"), in favor of **Nightjar Releasing, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Transferee**"). Transferor and Transferee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Transferor holds certain rights in the motion pictures listed on Schedule A (the "**Pictures**"). It acquired those rights under the producer, sales agency and acquisition agreements listed opposite each Picture on Schedule A.

B. Transferee is engaged in the acquisition, licensing, distribution and exploitation of independent motion pictures. It has acted as distributor of the Pictures in the ordinary course under arrangements with Transferor.

C. Transferor and Transferee are each Affiliates of Fural Media Partners, L.P. Transferor wishes to transfer, convey and assign to Transferee, and Transferee wishes to accept, the Distribution Rights in the Pictures and the related assets described below, on the terms set forth in this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Transferor and Transferee agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 General Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Acquisition Agreements**" means the producer agreements, sales agency agreements, acquisition agreements, distribution agreements and other Contracts under which Transferor acquired rights in any Picture, as listed on Schedule A, together with all amendments, side letters and notices of assignment relating to them.

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person. For this purpose, "control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of a Person, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise.

"**Assigned Contracts**" means the Acquisition Agreements and the Existing Licenses.

"**Assumed Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York or Los Angeles, California are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Contract**" means any written or oral contract, agreement, license, sublicense, deal memo, purchase order or other legally binding arrangement.

"**Delivery Materials**" means, for each Picture, all physical and digital picture, sound and ancillary elements and materials in the possession or under the control of Transferor, or held by any laboratory, vault or post-production facility for the account of Transferor. These include:

(a) digital cinema packages, digital source masters and interpositives;

(b) audio stems, music and effects tracks, and dubbed and subtitled versions;

(c) closed-caption and subtitle files;

(d) trailers, teasers, key art, still photographs, press kits and other advertising and publicity materials; and

(e) music cue sheets, credit statements and other documentation required for the delivery of the Picture to any licensee.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, option, right of first refusal or negotiation, laboratory access restriction, or other encumbrance or restriction of any kind.

"**Excluded Liabilities**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Existing Licenses**" means the licenses, sublicenses and other Contracts listed on Schedule B, under which Transferor has granted any Person rights in any Picture that are included in the Distribution Rights.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any governmental authority.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receivables**" means all amounts payable to Transferor after the Effective Date in respect of the exploitation of the Distribution Rights, whether earned before or after the Effective Date, including amounts payable under the Existing Licenses.

"**Records**" means all chain-of-title documents, copyright registration certificates, title reports, copyright reports, errors-and-omissions insurance certificates, participation and residual statements, accounting records, correspondence and other books and records of Transferor relating to the Pictures or the Assigned Contracts.

"**Territory**" means, for each Picture, the territory set forth opposite that Picture on Schedule A.

"**Term**" means, for each Picture, the period set forth opposite that Picture on Schedule A, or if no period is set forth, perpetuity.

**1.2 Distribution Rights.** "**Distribution Rights**" means, for each Picture, the theatrical, non-theatrical and physical home-video rights in and to that Picture, throughout the Territory and for the Term. These rights consist of:

(a) the exclusive right to distribute, license, exhibit, perform and display the Picture in theaters, drive-ins and other venues open to the general public on a paid-admission basis;

(b) the exclusive right to distribute, license, exhibit and display the Picture to airlines, ships, hotels, military installations, educational institutions, libraries, film societies, festivals and other non-theatrical venues;

(c) the exclusive right to reproduce, manufacture, sell, rent and otherwise distribute the Picture on DVD, Blu-ray, 4K Ultra HD and other physical home-video formats;

(d) the right to use the title of the Picture, and the names, approved likenesses and approved biographies of the persons rendering services in connection with the Picture, and excerpts of the Picture, in advertising, publicity and promotion of the exercise of the rights described in clauses (a) through (c); and

(e) the right to receive all revenue derived from the exercise of the rights described in clauses (a) through (d).

**1.3 Interpretation.** In this Bill of Sale, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation;

(b) the words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation";

(c) references to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Bill of Sale;

(d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and

(e) references to any Law include any amendment, modification or successor thereto.

## 2. TRANSFER AND CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Conveyance of Distribution Rights.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor hereby irrevocably sells, transfers, conveys, assigns and delivers to Transferee, and Transferee hereby accepts, all of Transferor's right, title and interest in and to the theatrical, non-theatrical and physical home-video rights in each Picture, constituting the Distribution Rights, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than the rights of licensees under the Existing Licenses. The conveyance under this Section 2.1 includes all of Transferor's rights, remedies and causes of action in respect of any infringement or unauthorized exercise of the Distribution Rights, whether arising before or after the Effective Date.

**2.2 Assigned Contracts.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor hereby assigns to Transferee all of its right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned Contracts, to the extent relating to the Distribution Rights.

**2.3 Delivery Materials.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor hereby transfers and conveys to Transferee all of its right, title and interest in and to the Delivery Materials. Where any Delivery Materials are held by a laboratory, vault or other facility, Transferor transfers its right of access to those materials and shall deliver the access letters required under Section 5.3.

**2.4 Receivables.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor hereby assigns to Transferee all of its right, title and interest in and to the Receivables.

**2.5 Records.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferor hereby transfers and conveys to Transferee the Records. Transferor may retain copies of the Records to the extent required by Law, for Tax or accounting purposes, or to perform its obligations under any Contract not assigned hereunder. Any copies so retained shall be held in confidence.

**2.6 Consents.** If the assignment of any Assigned Contract, or any right under it, requires the consent of a third party that has not been obtained as of the Effective Date, this Bill of Sale does not constitute an assignment of that Contract or right if the attempted assignment would constitute a breach of it or would be ineffective. In that case, until the consent is obtained:

(a) Transferor shall hold the Contract or right for the benefit of Transferee;

(b) Transferor shall cooperate with Transferee in any lawful arrangement designed to provide Transferee with the economic and operational benefits of the Contract or right, including enforcement at the request and for the account of Transferee; and

(c) Transferor shall promptly pay over to Transferee all amounts received by it in respect of the Contract or right.

Transferor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each such consent as promptly as practicable. Upon receipt of the consent, the Contract or right is automatically assigned to Transferee under Section 2.2 without further action.

## 3. CONSIDERATION

**3.1 Consideration.** In consideration of the transfer, conveyance and assignment made under Section 2, Transferee shall:

(a) assume the Assumed Liabilities under Section 4.1; and

(b) cause the outstanding intercompany balance owed by Transferor to Transferee as of the Effective Date, in the amount set forth on Schedule C, to be cancelled and extinguished.

**3.2 Tax Treatment.** The Parties shall report the transactions effected by this Bill of Sale for Tax purposes in a manner consistent with Schedule C, unless otherwise required by Law.

**3.3 Transfer Taxes.** Any transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp or similar Taxes payable in connection with the transactions effected by this Bill of Sale shall be borne by Transferor.

## 4. LIABILITIES

**4.1 Assumed Liabilities.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Transferee assumes and agrees to pay, perform and discharge when due only the following liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) obligations under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising from and relating to the exercise of the Distribution Rights after the Effective Date; and

(b) Participations and Residuals payable in respect of revenue derived from the exercise of the Distribution Rights after the Effective Date.

For purposes of this Bill of Sale, "**Participations**" means gross and net profit participations, deferments, box-office bonuses, royalties, overages and other contingent compensation, and "**Residuals**" means residuals, reuse fees, supplemental market payments and pension, health and welfare contributions payable under any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.2 Excluded Liabilities.** Transferee does not assume, and Transferor retains and shall pay, perform and discharge when due, all liabilities of Transferor other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising from any period before the Effective Date or from any breach or default by Transferor;

(b) all Participations and Residuals payable in respect of revenue received by or credited to Transferor before the Effective Date;

(c) all Taxes of Transferor for any period;

(d) all liabilities arising out of any action or claim pending or threatened against Transferor as of the Effective Date; and

(e) all indebtedness of Transferor for borrowed money.

## 5. DELIVERIES; RECORDATION

**5.1 Deliveries by Transferor.** On or promptly after the Effective Date, Transferor shall deliver to Transferee:

(a) the Records, in the form maintained by Transferor in the ordinary course;

(b) a short-form assignment of the Distribution Rights in each Picture, substantially in the form of Schedule D, duly executed by Transferor and suitable for recordation with the U.S. Copyright Office (each, a "**Short-Form Assignment**");

(c) a notice of assignment, in form reasonably satisfactory to Transferee, addressed to each counterparty to an Existing License and directing payment of all Receivables to an account designated by Transferee; and

(d) the consents obtained as of the Effective Date under Section 2.6.

**5.2 Recordation.** Transferee may record this Bill of Sale or any Short-Form Assignment with the U.S. Copyright Office or any other registry. If this Bill of Sale and any Short-Form Assignment conflict, this Bill of Sale controls. Transferor shall bear the cost of recordation of the Short-Form Assignments.

**5.3 Laboratory Access.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, Transferor shall deliver to Transferee, for each laboratory, vault and facility at which any Delivery Materials are held, an irrevocable laboratory access letter in favor of Transferee, duly countersigned by that laboratory, vault or facility, in form reasonably satisfactory to Transferee.

**5.4 Payments Received.** If Transferor receives any Receivable or other amount to which Transferee is entitled under this Bill of Sale, Transferor shall hold it in trust for Transferee and shall remit it to Transferee within five (5) Business Days after receipt, together with any accompanying statement.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF TRANSFEROR

Transferor represents and warrants to Transferee, as of the Effective Date, as follows:

**6.1 Organization; Authority.** Transferor is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. It has all requisite power and authority to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale and to perform its obligations hereunder. The execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale have been duly authorized by all necessary limited liability company action on the part of Transferor.

**6.2 Enforceability.** This Bill of Sale constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Transferor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

**6.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale by Transferor do not:

(a) conflict with the certificate of formation or limited liability company agreement of Transferor;

(b) violate any Law applicable to Transferor; or

(c) except for the consents listed on Schedule E, require any consent under, or result in a breach of or default under, any Assigned Contract.

**6.4 Title.** Transferor has good and marketable title to the Distribution Rights and the Delivery Materials, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than the rights of licensees under the Existing Licenses.

**6.5 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Transferor is not, and to its knowledge no other party is, in material breach of or default under any Assigned Contract. **6.7 No Other Representations.** Except as set forth in this Section 6, Transferor makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, in respect of the Pictures, the Distribution Rights or the other assets conveyed under this Bill of Sale.

## 7. COVENANTS

**7.1 Further Assurances.** From time to time after the Effective Date, at the request of Transferee and without further consideration, Transferor shall execute, acknowledge and deliver such further instruments of transfer, conveyance and assignment, and take such other actions, as Transferee may reasonably request to vest in Transferee, perfect, record or confirm the rights conveyed under this Bill of Sale. This includes executing further short-form assignments and instruments for recordation with the U.S. Copyright Office or any foreign copyright registry, and providing notices to licensees, laboratories, guilds and collection account managers.

**7.2 Power of Attorney.** Transferor hereby irrevocably appoints Transferee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, acknowledge and deliver, in the name of Transferor, any instrument described in Section 7.1 that Transferor fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after Transferee's written request. Transferee may also, in the name of Transferor, demand and receive any Receivable and endorse any check or other instrument payable to Transferor in respect of a Receivable. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**7.3 Enforcement.** Transferee may institute and prosecute, in its own name or, where required by Law, in the name of Transferor, any action to enforce the Distribution Rights or any Assigned Contract, and to recover damages for any infringement of the Distribution Rights. Transferor shall cooperate with Transferee in any such action at Transferee's reasonable request and expense.

**7.4 Correspondence.** Transferor shall promptly forward to Transferee all statements, notices, reports and correspondence it receives after the Effective Date relating to the Distribution Rights or any Assigned Contract.

**7.5 Confidentiality.** Transferor shall hold in confidence all non-public information concerning the Distribution Rights, the Assigned Contracts and the Records, except to the extent:

(a) the information is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Section 7.5;

(b) disclosure is required by Law, in which case Transferor shall give Transferee prompt notice; or

(c) disclosure is made to Transferor's Affiliates, advisors and financing sources who are bound by obligations of confidentiality.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 By Transferor.** Transferor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Transferee and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Transferor in this Bill of Sale; or

(b) any Excluded Liability.

**8.2 By Transferee.** Transferee shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Transferor and its managers, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any breach of any covenant of Transferee in this Bill of Sale; or

(b) any Assumed Liability.

**8.3 Procedures.** A Person seeking indemnification under this Section 8 shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations, except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. For any claim asserted by a third party, the indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Person. It shall not settle the claim without the indemnified Person's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a complete release of the indemnified Person.
