# NIGHTJAR RELEASING TRANSACTION: JOINT PRESS RELEASE

**FINAL FORM FOR RELEASE ON THE CLOSING DATE**

## 1. Release Information

**1.1 Status.** This document is the agreed form of the joint press release that Subirt Pictures Group, LLC ("**Subirt**") and Fural Media Partners, L.P. ("**Fural**") will issue after the closing of Subirt's acquisition of Nightjar Releasing, Inc. ("**Nightjar**"). The parties cooperated on its text under Section 6.6 of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 9, 2026 between Fural, as seller, and Subirt, as buyer (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**").

**1.2 Timing.** The release is embargoed until Subirt's General Counsel confirms in writing to Fural's Managing Partner that the Closing has occurred. The Closing is scheduled for April 1, 2026. The release will be issued promptly after that confirmation, and no earlier than 9:00 a.m. Pacific Time on the Closing Date.

**1.3 Approvals.** Each party has approved the text in Sections 2 through 6. That text may not be changed without the written approval of both parties. Section 7 contains internal guidance for spokespeople, Section 8 sets out approved social media copy, and Section 9 contains the distribution and sign-off record.

**1.4 Channels.** The release will be distributed over a national business newswire and posted on the corporate websites of Subirt and Nightjar. Fural may post the release on its website and send it to its limited partners. It will also be emailed to the trade press list held by Subirt's communications team.

**1.5 Exclusions.** The release does not disclose the financial terms of the transaction, the terms of any ancillary agreement, or any information about individual Library Titles that has not already been made public. Spokespeople should not go beyond the approved text and the guidance in Section 7.

## 3. Body of the Release

**3.1 Lead Paragraph.** Subirt Pictures Group, LLC, a Los Angeles-based film production and distribution company, today announced that it has completed its acquisition of Nightjar Releasing, Inc., an independent film distributor, from Fural Media Partners, L.P. Subirt acquired all of the outstanding shares of Nightjar. Nightjar's library of 41 motion pictures comes with it, together with the company's relationships with exhibitors, sub-distributors, platforms and licensors.

**3.2 Strategic Rationale.** The acquisition is a major step in Subirt's strategy of building a vertically integrated independent film business. Over more than a decade, Nightjar has built a reputation for identifying distinctive, filmmaker-driven projects and bringing them to audiences. Its library covers dramas, documentaries, thrillers and genre titles. Several have played at major international festivals and won critical recognition. Subirt plans to use its marketing, sales and operational resources to widen the audience for Nightjar's existing titles and to continue Nightjar's acquisitions program.

**3.3 Operations.** Nightjar will operate as a wholly owned subsidiary of Subirt and continue to release films under the Nightjar Releasing name and logo. Nightjar's staff will join the Subirt group. Subirt expects Nightjar's distribution activities to remain based in Los Angeles.

**3.4 Transition.** To ensure a smooth handover, Fural will provide certain transitional collection, accounting and delivery support services to Nightjar for a limited period after closing. During that period, Nightjar and Subirt will move those functions onto Subirt's own systems and teams. Exhibitors, licensees and other business partners will receive separate notices about any changes to payment and delivery arrangements. Until they receive such a notice, they should continue to deal with Nightjar under their existing arrangements.

**3.5 Financial Terms.** Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

**3.6 Filmmakers and Partners.** Subirt and Nightjar intend to contact the producers, sales agents and other rights partners behind each Library Title in the coming weeks. They will introduce the combined team and discuss plans for each film. Existing agreements with filmmakers, licensors and licensees are not affected by the change in Nightjar's ownership.

## 7. Spokesperson Guidance (Internal; Not for Release)

**7.1 Purpose.** This Section 7 gives approved responses to questions that journalists, filmmakers, business partners and employees are likely to ask after the release is issued. Spokespeople should stay within these responses. Any question not covered here should be referred to Subirt's General Counsel or, for questions about Fural, to Fural's Managing Partner. Do not speculate.

**7.2 Approved Spokespeople.** Only the following individuals may speak publicly about the transaction:

(a) for Subirt and Nightjar, Evelyn Subirt, and members of Subirt's communications department acting on her instruction; and

(b) for Fural, Daniel Okafor, and individuals he designates in writing.

All other employees of Subirt, Nightjar and Fural who receive press inquiries should forward them to the relevant media contact in Section 6.4 without comment.

**7.3 Questions About the Transaction.**

(a) *What did Subirt acquire?*
Subirt acquired all of the outstanding shares of Nightjar Releasing, Inc. from Fural Media Partners, L.P. Nightjar is now a wholly owned subsidiary of Subirt. Its library of 41 motion pictures remains with Nightjar.

(b) *How much did Subirt pay?*
We are not disclosing the financial terms of the transaction.

(c) *When did the transaction close?*
The transaction closed on April 1, 2026.

(d) *Was the transaction subject to regulatory approval?*
We do not comment on the transaction process beyond what is in the release. The transaction has closed.

(e) *How was the acquisition financed?*
We are not commenting on financing arrangements.

(f) *Who advised the parties?*
We are not commenting on advisors.

**7.4 Questions About the Library.**

(a) *Which films are in the library?*
Nightjar's library consists of 41 motion pictures. We will share more about individual titles and our plans for them in due course. We are not releasing a title list today.

(b) *Will you re-release any of the films?*
We are reviewing the library with the Nightjar team and will announce plans for individual titles when they are ready. We will not comment on specific titles today.

(c) *Do the existing licenses for Nightjar's films continue?*
Yes. Existing agreements between Nightjar and its licensees, exhibitors and other partners are not affected by the change in Nightjar's ownership.

(d) *What rights does Nightjar hold in each film?*
We do not comment on the rights position of individual titles. Business partners with questions about a particular agreement should contact their usual Nightjar representative.

**7.5 Questions About Filmmakers and Partners.**

(a) *How will filmmakers be affected?*
Filmmakers' agreements with Nightjar remain in place. Statements and payments will continue in the ordinary course. Subirt and Nightjar plan to contact the producers and rights partners behind each Library Title in the coming weeks.

(b) *Will Nightjar keep acquiring new films?*
Yes. Subirt intends to continue Nightjar's acquisitions program and to support it with Subirt's resources.

(c) *Who should exhibitors and licensees contact?*
They should continue to work with their usual Nightjar contacts. If any payment or delivery arrangements change, Nightjar will notify the affected partners directly and in writing.

(d) *Should payments be made to a new account?*
Not unless the partner receives written notice from Nightjar. Partners should be wary of any payment instruction that does not come through their established Nightjar channels and should verify it with their usual Nightjar contact. If asked, spokespeople should mention that changes to payment instructions are only ever given in writing.

**7.6 Questions About Fural.**

(a) *Why did Fural sell?*
"Fural believes Subirt is the right long-term home for Nightjar and its library." Do not go beyond the substance of Mr. Okafor's quotation in Section 4.2.

(b) *Will Fural continue to be involved with Nightjar?*
Fural will provide certain transitional collection, accounting and delivery support services to Nightjar for a limited period after closing to support an orderly handover. Fural has no ongoing ownership interest in Nightjar and no role in its management.

(c) *How long will the transition last?*
We are not commenting on the details of the transition arrangements. We expect the handover to be completed in the coming months.

**7.7 Questions About Employees.**

(a) *Will Nightjar employees keep their jobs?*
Nightjar's team is joining the Subirt group, and Nightjar will continue to operate under its own name. We do not comment on individual employment matters.

(b) *Will Nightjar's leadership change?*
We will announce any changes to Nightjar's leadership in due course. We are not commenting on individuals today.

(c) *Where will Nightjar be based?*
We expect Nightjar's distribution activities to remain based in Los Angeles.
