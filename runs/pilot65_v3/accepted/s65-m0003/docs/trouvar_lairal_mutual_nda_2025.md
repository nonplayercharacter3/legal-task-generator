# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of October 14, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1201 Market Street, Suite 1800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Trouvar**"); and

**Lairal Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 4400 Commerce Parkway, Suite 300, Cary, North Carolina 27518 ("**Lairal**").

Trouvar and Lairal are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Trouvar manufactures sterile injectable pharmaceutical products and is evaluating electronic batch-record and quality-management software for its manufacturing operations.

B. Lairal develops and makes available LairalQMS, a software platform for electronic batch records, quality management and related GxP manufacturing operations.

C. The Parties wish to exchange certain confidential information so that they can evaluate, discuss and negotiate a possible business relationship under which Lairal would license LairalQMS to Trouvar on a hosted basis and provide related implementation, configuration and validation services (the "**Purpose**").

D. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Definitions.** In this NDA:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, for so long as that control exists. For this definition, "control" means direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies by contract or otherwise.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day that is a federal holiday in the United States.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in whatever form or medium, that is disclosed or made available by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before, on or after the NDA Effective Date, and whether orally, in writing, electronically, by demonstration, by access to systems or facilities, or otherwise, that is marked or identified as confidential or proprietary, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes:

(a) in the case of Trouvar: product formulations, manufacturing processes, process parameters, master batch records, executed batch records, equipment and facility information, quality systems, standard operating procedures, deviation, investigation and CAPA records, validation documentation, regulatory submissions and correspondence with Regulatory Authorities, product pipeline information, supplier and customer information, production volumes, site plans and headcount forecasts;

(b) in the case of Lairal: the LairalQMS software and its source code, object code, architecture, data models, interfaces and non-public documentation, product roadmaps, security and hosting architecture, security audit and penetration-testing reports, SOC 2 reports, validation accelerator materials, implementation methodologies, pricing and commercial proposals; and

(c) in the case of either Party: business plans, financial information, the identity of personnel, and the existence, status and content of the discussions between the Parties concerning the Purpose, including any proposal, term sheet or draft agreement exchanged between them.

"**Regulatory Authority**" means the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and any other governmental or supranational authority with jurisdiction over the manufacture or distribution of pharmaceutical products.

"**Representatives**" means, as to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their respective directors, officers, managers, members, employees, contractors, and legal, financial, accounting, technical and other professional advisers.

1.2 **Interpretation.** In this NDA: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) "including" means "including without limitation"; (c) references to Sections are to sections of this NDA; and (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 **Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Recipient or its Representatives in breach of this NDA;

(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by or on behalf of the Discloser;

(c) is lawfully received by the Recipient or its Representatives from a third party that, to the Recipient's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, was not bound by any obligation of confidentiality to the Discloser in respect of that information; or

(d) is independently developed by or for the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

2.2 **Combinations.** Confidential Information will not be treated as falling within any exclusion in Section 2.1 merely because individual features or components of it fall within an exclusion, unless the combination itself, and its principle of operation, fall within that exclusion.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

3.1 **Use.** The Recipient will use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Recipient will not disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to any person other than those of its Representatives who:

(a) need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose;

(b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and

(c) are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and restricted use, or, in the case of professional advisers, by professional duties of confidentiality, that are at least as protective of the Confidential Information as those in this NDA.

3.3 **Responsibility for Representatives.** The Recipient is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this NDA if committed by the Recipient.

3.4 **Standard of Care.** The Recipient will protect the Discloser's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

3.5 **Copies.** The Recipient will not make copies of the Discloser's Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose. Each copy will bear any proprietary or confidentiality notice that appears on the original.

3.6 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Recipient will not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, prototype, sample or other tangible item that embodies the Discloser's Confidential Information, and will not analyze its composition, except to the extent the Discloser expressly agrees in writing.

3.7 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient will notify the Discloser in writing promptly, and in any event within three (3) Business Days, after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information. The Recipient will cooperate with the Discloser to regain possession of the Confidential Information and to prevent any further unauthorized use or disclosure.

## 4. DEMONSTRATIONS, SITE VISITS AND SYSTEM ACCESS

4.1 **Demonstration Environments.** If Lairal provides Trouvar with access to a demonstration, sandbox or trial environment of LairalQMS for the Purpose, Trouvar will use that access only to evaluate LairalQMS for the Purpose and will comply with any reasonable access terms that Lairal provides in writing. Trouvar will not load executed batch records, patient data or other production GxP records into any such environment. Lairal will treat any information that Trouvar enters into such an environment as Trouvar's Confidential Information, and will delete it at the end of the evaluation or on Trouvar's request.

4.2 **Site Visits.** If either Party's personnel visit the other Party's facilities for the Purpose, they will comply with the host Party's site safety, gowning, security, photography and data-integrity policies as notified to them, and will be accompanied by host personnel where the host Party requires. Information observed during a site visit is Confidential Information of the host Party.

4.3 **Security Materials.** Any security questionnaire responses, SOC 2 reports, penetration-testing summaries, hosting architecture descriptions and similar materials that Lairal provides as part of Trouvar's supplier qualification process are Lairal's Confidential Information. Trouvar may share them with its quality, information security and information technology personnel and with its auditors for the purpose of supplier qualification.

## 5. PERSONAL DATA

5.1 **Minimization.** The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data for the Purpose, other than business contact information of their respective personnel. Each Party will use reasonable efforts to avoid disclosing other personal data to the other Party, including by redacting names, signatures and employee identifiers from any sample batch records or quality records shared for the Purpose.

5.2 **Handling.** To the extent either Party receives personal data from the other Party, it will process that personal data only for the Purpose, in accordance with applicable data protection laws, and subject to the confidentiality and security obligations in this NDA.

## 6. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

6.1 **Legal Requirement.** The Recipient or its Representatives may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, the request of a Regulatory Authority or other governmental authority, or the rules of a securities exchange on which the Recipient's or its Affiliate's securities are listed.

6.2 **Conditions.** Before making a disclosure under Section 6.1, the Recipient will, where legally permitted:

(a) give the Discloser prompt written notice of the requirement, so that the Discloser may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy;

(b) provide reasonable assistance, at the Discloser's expense, in any effort by the Discloser to obtain that protective order or remedy; and

(c) disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its legal counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to it.

6.3 **Regulatory Inspections.** Nothing in this NDA prevents Trouvar from making information available to a Regulatory Authority during an inspection or audit of Trouvar's facilities where required by law, provided that Trouvar discloses only the portion of Lairal's Confidential Information that is required and, where practicable, requests confidential treatment of it.

## 7. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

7.1 **On Request.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Discloser's written request, and in any event within fifteen (15) Business Days after the end of the Disclosure Period under Section 10.1, the Recipient will, at its election, return or destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession or control, including all copies, notes, analyses and other materials that contain or reflect it.

7.2 **Certification.** On the Discloser's request, an officer of the Recipient will certify in writing that the Recipient has complied with Section 7.1.

7.3 **Permitted Retention.** Despite Section 7.1, the Recipient and its Representatives may retain:

(a) copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation or professional standards, or by the Recipient's bona fide document retention policies;

(b) copies held in automatic electronic archiving or backup systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course of business, until they are overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course; and

(c) one archival copy in the files of its legal department, solely to evidence its compliance with this NDA.

Any Confidential Information so retained remains subject to the obligations in this NDA for as long as it is retained, regardless of the expiration of the periods in Section 10.

## 8. OWNERSHIP, NO LICENSE AND NO WARRANTY

8.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, all Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.

8.2 **No License.** Nothing in this NDA grants the Recipient any license or other right under any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property right of the Discloser, except the limited right to use the Discloser's Confidential Information for the Purpose in accordance with this NDA.

8.3 **No Warranty.** All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, performance or fitness for any purpose of its Confidential Information, and neither Party will have any liability to the other resulting from the use of its Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a definitive written agreement between the Parties or for breach of this NDA.

8.4 **Feedback.** Neither Party is obliged to provide suggestions or feedback to the other. Any suggestions or feedback that a Party chooses to provide about the other Party's products or services are provided without obligation, and their provision does not grant the receiving Party any right in the providing Party's Confidential Information.

## 9. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

9.1 **No Commitment.** This NDA does not oblige either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either Party may end discussions concerning the Purpose at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other Party.

9.2 **Definitive Agreement.** No contract or agreement concerning the Purpose, other than this NDA, will exist between the Parties unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered by authorized representatives of both Parties.

9.3 **Independent Activities.** Subject to compliance with this NDA, nothing in this NDA restricts either Party from developing, acquiring, licensing or marketing products, services or software that are similar to or compete with those of the other Party, or from entering into discussions or agreements with third parties, provided that it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in doing so.

9.4 **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this NDA and the Purpose, including the fees of its advisers.

## 10. TERM AND DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

10.1 **Disclosure Period.** This NDA applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period beginning on the NDA Effective Date and ending on the second (2nd) anniversary of the NDA Effective Date (the "**Disclosure Period**"), and to Confidential Information disclosed before the NDA Effective Date in connection with the Purpose. Either Party may end the Disclosure Period earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

10.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Recipient's obligations under this NDA with respect to Confidential Information continue for five (5) years after the end of the Disclosure Period, except that:

(a) obligations with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continue for as long as that information remains a trade secret; and

(b) obligations with respect to Trouvar's batch records, manufacturing processes, product formulations and regulatory submissions continue for as long as that information remains confidential.

10.3 **Survival.** Sections 7, 8, 10.2, 11 and 12 survive the end of the Disclosure Period.

## 11. REMEDIES

11.1 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which damages would not be an adequate remedy. Accordingly, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance and other equitable relief for any actual or threatened breach of this NDA, without the necessity of proving actual damages and without posting a bond or other security.

11.2 **Remedies Cumulative.** The rights and remedies in this NDA are cumulative and in addition to any other rights and remedies available at law or in equity.

11.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** In any action to enforce this NDA, the prevailing Party is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the other Party, as determined by the court.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts, except that either Party may seek equitable relief under Section 11.1 in any court of competent jurisdiction.

12.3 **Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice.** In accordance with 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (a) in confidence to a federal, state or local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or (b) in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if that filing is made under seal. Each Party will ensure that its personnel who receive the other Party's Confidential Information are notified of this immunity to the extent required by law.

12.4 **Export Compliance.** Each Party will comply with all applicable export control and economic sanctions laws in connection with any technical data or software received under this NDA, and will not export, re-export or transfer the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of those laws.

12.5 **Securities Laws.** Each Party acknowledges that it is aware, and will advise its Representatives who receive the other Party's Confidential Information, that applicable securities laws may restrict persons who have material non-public information about a company from purchasing or selling securities of that company or from communicating that information to others.
