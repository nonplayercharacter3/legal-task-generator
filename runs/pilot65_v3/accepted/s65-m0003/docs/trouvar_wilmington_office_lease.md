# OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is made as of June 1, 2024 (the "**Lease Date**") between **Brandywine Quay Properties, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

1.1 **Summary.** The following basic lease information (the "**Basic Lease Information**") is part of this Lease. Each item is subject to the more detailed provisions of this Lease.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used in this Lease have the meanings given in the Basic Lease Information or elsewhere in this Lease.

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease.

2.2 **Common Areas.** Tenant and its employees, contractors and invitees may use, in common with other tenants, the lobbies, elevators, stairways, corridors, restrooms, loading dock, fitness center, conference center and other areas of the Building that Landlord designates from time to time for the common use of tenants (the "**Common Areas**"), subject to the Rules and Regulations under Section 17.

2.3 **Measurement.** The rentable area of the Premises and the Building has been determined in accordance with the ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017 standard. Neither party may remeasure the Premises or the Building for the purpose of adjusting Base Rent or Tenant's Share during the Term, except to reflect any expansion or contraction of the Premises agreed in writing.

2.4 **Condition.** Except for Landlord's Work under Section 6.1, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Commencement Date. Landlord represents that, on the Commencement Date, the Building structure, roof and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems serving the Premises will be in good working order and that the Common Areas will comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act as then in effect.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier or extended under this Lease.

3.2 **Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises from July 15, 2024 to install furniture, cabling and equipment, provided that Tenant does not interfere with Landlord's Work. All terms of this Lease other than the obligation to pay Rent apply during early access.

3.3 **Renewal Option.** Tenant may extend the Term for one (1) period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**") by written notice to Landlord given not less than twelve (12) months and not more than eighteen (18) months before the Expiration Date, provided that Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period. Base Rent for the Renewal Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent, determined under Section 3.4. All other terms of this Lease will apply during the Renewal Term, except that Tenant will have no further renewal option.

3.4 **Fair Market Rent.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot that a willing landlord and a willing tenant would agree for comparable Class A office space in downtown Wilmington, Delaware, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. Landlord will notify Tenant of its determination of Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's renewal notice. If Tenant disagrees, the parties will negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days. If they do not agree, each party will appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the Wilmington office market. If the two brokers' determinations differ by more than five percent (5%), they will jointly appoint a third broker, who will select whichever of the first two determinations is closer to the third broker's own determination. Each party will bear the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.

"**Lease Year**" means each period of twelve (12) months beginning on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it.

4.2 **Abatement.** Base Rent for the first four (4) full calendar months of the Term is abated. Tenant will pay Operating Expenses and Taxes under Section 5 during the abatement period.

4.3 **Additional Rent.** All amounts other than Base Rent that Tenant must pay under this Lease are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

4.4 **Payment.** Tenant will pay Rent without demand, deduction or set-off except as expressly stated in this Lease, by electronic funds transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing. Rent for any partial month will be prorated on a daily basis.

4.5 **Late Charge.** If any Rent is not paid within five (5) Business Days after written notice that it is overdue, Tenant will pay a late charge of three percent (3%) of the overdue amount, and the overdue amount will bear interest at eight percent (8%) per annum from the due date until paid. Landlord will not be required to give the notice more than twice in any twelve (12) month period. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the State of Delaware.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

5.1 **Tenant's Share.** Starting on January 1, 2026, Tenant will pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs that Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, clean, secure and insure the Building and the land on which it stands, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital expenditures, except the annual amortized cost of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses (up to the amount of the savings) or are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date;

(b) costs of leasing, including brokerage commissions, advertising and tenant improvements for other tenants;

(c) interest, principal and other financing costs, and ground rent;

(d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(e) Landlord's general corporate overhead and salaries of personnel above the level of building manager;

(f) costs of correcting construction defects in the base building;

(g) fines, penalties and legal fees arising from Landlord's breach of law or of any lease;

(h) management fees exceeding three percent (3%) of the gross revenues of the Building; and

(i) charitable and political contributions and costs of acquiring art.

5.3 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied during any calendar year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy will be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.4 **Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for any calendar year will not exceed Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for the previous calendar year by more than five percent (5%), on a non-cumulative basis. "**Controllable Operating Expenses**" means all Operating Expenses other than insurance premiums, utilities, snow removal costs and costs of compliance with laws first enacted after the Lease Date.

5.5 **Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land, less any refunds. Taxes exclude income, franchise, estate, inheritance and transfer taxes, and penalties for late payment.

5.6 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord will give Tenant a written estimate of Tenant's Share of excess Operating Expenses and Taxes before the start of each calendar year, and Tenant will pay one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimate with each monthly installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord will give Tenant a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Tenant will pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days, and Landlord will credit any overpayment against the next Rent due or, after the end of the Term, refund it within thirty (30) days.

5.7 **Audit.** Tenant may, by notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving an annual statement, inspect Landlord's books and records for that year at Landlord's management office. If the inspection shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share by more than four percent (4%), Landlord will reimburse Tenant's reasonable inspection costs, in addition to refunding the overcharge.

## 6. LANDLORD'S WORK AND ALTERATIONS

6.1 **Landlord's Work.** Before the Commencement Date, Landlord will complete, at its expense and in a good and workmanlike manner, the improvements described in Exhibit B ("**Landlord's Work**"), using building-standard materials.

6.2 **Tenant Improvement Allowance.** Landlord will provide Tenant with an allowance of $50.00 per rentable square foot of the Premises, totalling $712,500 (the "**Allowance**"), toward the cost of improvements that Tenant makes to the Premises within the first eighteen (18) months of the Term, including design fees, permits, cabling and project management. Landlord will disburse the Allowance monthly against invoices and lien waivers. Landlord will not charge a supervision fee on work funded by the Allowance.

6.3 **Alterations.** Tenant may not make any alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises ("**Alterations**") without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic Alterations, such as painting and carpeting, costing less than $35,000 in any one instance that do not affect the Building structure or systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord at least ten (10) Business Days' prior notice.

6.4 **Performance of Alterations.** Tenant will perform all Alterations in compliance with law, using licensed contractors reasonably approved by Landlord, and will deliver as-built drawings to Landlord on completion.

6.5 **Liens.** Tenant will keep the Premises and the Building free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant. Tenant will discharge or bond over any such lien within twenty (20) days after receiving notice of it.

6.6 **Removal.** At the end of the Term, Tenant need not remove any Alterations unless Landlord stated, when giving its consent under Section 6.3, that removal would be required. Tenant will remove its cabling, furniture and trade fixtures.

## 7. USE

7.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant will use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose. Tenant will not conduct any laboratory, manufacturing, compounding or storage of pharmaceutical products at the Premises, other than the storage of documents, retained records and ordinary office supplies.

7.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant will comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord will comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, the base building systems and the Common Areas, and the cost of that compliance will be included in Operating Expenses only to the extent permitted by Section 5.2.

7.3 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant will not bring any hazardous materials into the Building except ordinary office and cleaning supplies in customary quantities, used in compliance with law. Landlord represents that, to its knowledge, there are no hazardous materials in the Premises in violation of law on the Lease Date.

7.4 **Access.** Tenant will have access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's security procedures.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

8.1 **Landlord Services.** Landlord will provide the following services:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on Business Days and from 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, at levels consistent with comparable Class A office buildings in Wilmington, Delaware;

(b) after-hours heating, ventilation and air conditioning on request, at Landlord's actual cost, currently $65 per hour per zone;

(c) electricity for lighting and standard office equipment;

(d) passenger elevator service at all times, and freight elevator service on reasonable advance request;

(e) janitorial services five (5) nights a week, in accordance with the specifications in Exhibit C;

(f) hot and cold water for restrooms and kitchenettes; and

(g) lobby security staff and access-card controlled entry to the Building at all times.

8.2 **Electricity.** Electricity for the Premises will be separately metered. Tenant will pay for its electricity consumption at the utility's rates, without mark-up.

8.3 **Supplemental Equipment.** Tenant may install supplemental air conditioning for its server room, at its own cost, in a location reasonably approved by Landlord, and may connect it to the Building's condenser water system at a charge of $750 per ton per year.

8.4 **Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. However, if any service that Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than three (3) consecutive Business Days because of Landlord's act or omission, and the interruption makes all or a material part of the Premises unusable for Tenant's business, Rent will abate for the unusable portion from the fourth (4th) Business Day until the service is restored.

8.5 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may use the Building's risers and conduits to install cabling and may contract with telecommunications providers of its choice. Landlord will provide reasonable access for those providers, subject to Landlord's standard access agreement.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

9.1 **Landlord's Repairs.** Landlord will maintain and repair, in good condition consistent with comparable Class A office buildings, the Building structure, roof, exterior walls, windows, Common Areas and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems, the cost of which will be included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 5.2.

9.2 **Tenant's Repairs.** Tenant will keep the interior of the Premises, including its Alterations and supplemental equipment, in good order and repair, except for reasonable wear and tear, damage by casualty and repairs that are Landlord's responsibility.

9.3 **Landlord's Entry.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least one (1) Business Day's notice (except in an emergency) to inspect, make repairs or show the Premises to prospective purchasers, lenders and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, prospective tenants. Landlord will comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures, will be accompanied by a Tenant representative if Tenant requests, and will not enter any area that Tenant designates as a secure records area except in an emergency or in the presence of a Tenant representative.

## 10. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

10.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant will maintain during the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds;

(b) umbrella liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000;

(c) property insurance covering its Alterations, furniture, equipment and other personal property for their full replacement cost; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

10.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord will maintain property insurance covering the Building for its full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings in Wilmington, Delaware.

10.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent that the loss or damage is insured, or is required to be insured, under this Lease. Each party will obtain a waiver of subrogation endorsement from its property insurer.

10.4 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 10.3, each party will indemnify and hold the other harmless from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises (in the case of Tenant) or the Common Areas (in the case of Landlord), to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party or its employees, agents or contractors.

10.5 **Certificates.** Tenant will deliver certificates of insurance to Landlord before entering the Premises and on each renewal.

## 11. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

11.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord will, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant an estimate from a reputable contractor of the time needed to restore them. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 11.2, Landlord will restore the base building and Landlord's Work with reasonable diligence.

11.2 **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate if the estimated restoration time exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days from the casualty, or if the casualty occurs during the last eighteen (18) months of the Term and the estimated restoration time exceeds ninety (90) days.

11.3 **Abatement.** Rent will abate in proportion to the part of the Premises that is unusable because of a casualty, from the date of the casualty until the Premises are restored.

11.4 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of them that Tenant cannot reasonably operate its business in the remainder, are taken by eminent domain, this Lease will terminate on the date of taking. Tenant may make a separate claim for its moving costs and its unamortized Alterations paid for by Tenant.

## 12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay.

12.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least fifteen (15) Business Days' prior notice, assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to: (a) an affiliate of Tenant; or (b) an entity that succeeds to all or substantially all of Tenant's business or assets by merger, consolidation or acquisition, provided that the successor's tangible net worth immediately after the transaction is not less than Tenant's tangible net worth on the Lease Date.

12.3 **Profit Sharing.** Tenant will pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from a subtenant or assignee in excess of the Rent payable for the same space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer, including brokerage commissions, legal fees and improvement costs. This Section 12.3 does not apply to transfers under Section 12.2.

12.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, except an assignment under Section 12.2(b) where the original Tenant ceases to exist by operation of law.

12.5 **Landlord Transfer.** Landlord may transfer its interest in the Building. On a transfer, Landlord will be released from obligations arising after the transfer, provided the transferee assumes them in writing and the Security Deposit is transferred to the transferee.

## 13. SECURITY DEPOSIT

13.1 **Deposit.** Tenant has delivered the Security Deposit to Landlord. Landlord may apply it to cure any default by Tenant that continues beyond the applicable cure period, and Tenant will restore the amount applied within ten (10) Business Days after notice.

13.2 **Return.** Landlord will return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within forty-five (45) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises.

## 14. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

14.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent when due and does not cure the failure within five (5) Business Days after written notice;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant does not begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may, in addition to its other remedies under Delaware law: (a) terminate this Lease by notice to Tenant; or (b) without terminating this Lease, recover Rent as it becomes due. Landlord will use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

14.3 **No Acceleration Beyond Damages.** If Landlord terminates this Lease, Landlord may recover the unpaid Rent to the date of termination, plus the present value (discounted at six percent (6%) per annum) of the amount by which the Rent for the rest of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for that period, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of reletting.

14.4 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Landlord does not begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it, Tenant may pursue its remedies at law or in equity. If the failure affects the Premises and creates an imminent risk to persons or property or materially interferes with Tenant's business, Tenant may, after a further notice and five (5) Business Days, perform the obligation within the Premises and recover its reasonable costs from Landlord.

14.5 **Limitation of Landlord's Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Building, including rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds. Neither party is liable to the other for consequential damages, except that this limitation does not apply to Tenant's liability under Section 15.2.

## 15. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

15.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant will surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, except for reasonable wear and tear, casualty and Alterations that Tenant is not required to remove under Section 6.6.

15.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant will be a tenant at sufferance and will pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term, prorated daily, together with Additional Rent. If Tenant holds over for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord gives notice that it has entered into a lease with a new tenant for the Premises, Tenant will also be liable for Landlord's resulting damages.

## 16. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

16.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any existing or future mortgage on the Building, provided that the mortgagee enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement with Tenant. Landlord will deliver a non-disturbance agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

16.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party will, within fifteen (15) Business Days after the other's request, deliver a certificate confirming that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, the other party is in default.

## 17. RULES AND REGULATIONS

17.1 **Rules.** Tenant will comply with the Building rules and regulations attached as Exhibit D and any reasonable changes that Landlord gives Tenant in writing (the "**Rules and Regulations**"). Landlord will enforce the Rules and Regulations in a non-discriminatory manner. If the Rules and Regulations conflict with this Lease, this Lease prevails.
