# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 2

## Phase 2: Validation and Production Readiness

**DRAFT: October 20, 2026. For discussion purposes only. Not binding until executed by both Parties.**

This Statement of Work No. 2 (this "**SOW No. 2**") is entered into as of the SOW Effective Date (defined in Section 1.2) by and between **Lairal Systems, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and **Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensee**"). It is entered into under the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated March 16, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Under Statement of Work No. 1 under the License Agreement ("**SOW No. 1**"), Licensor agreed to perform the Phase 1 implementation and configuration of the Licensed Software for Licensee's sterile injectables manufacturing operations. Those services included the configuration of the batch-record, deviation, change-control and CAPA modules, master data migration and the related configuration specifications (the "**Phase 1 Deliverables**").

B. Licensor delivered the Phase 1 Deliverables to Licensee on September 2, 2026 by deploying the configured modules to Licensee's validation environment and providing the Phase 1 configuration specifications, data migration report and written notice of readiness for review to Licensee's designated project manager.

C. On October 6, 2026, Licensee issued a written notice to Licensor rejecting the Phase 1 Deliverables. The notice identified nonconformities in the electronic batch-record review-by-exception workflow, the audit-trail configuration for master batch records and the migrated equipment master data. Licensor disputes that the Phase 1 Deliverables fail to conform to the acceptance criteria in SOW No. 1. The Parties dispute whether the Phase 1 Deliverables conform, and each Party reserves all of its rights and remedies under the License Agreement and SOW No. 1 in respect of the Phase 1 Deliverables.

D. The Parties wish to proceed with the next phase of the project, comprising the validation of the Licensed Software for Licensee's intended use under GxP and the readiness activities for production go-live, on the terms of this SOW No. 2.

E. The Parties intend that the Professional Services under this SOW No. 2 be performed in parallel with their discussions about the Phase 1 Deliverables, so that the overall project timeline is not delayed.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 2. SCOPE OF PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

2.1 **Overview.** Licensor will provide the Professional Services described in this Section 2 to support Licensee's validation of the Licensed Software at the Facility and its readiness for production go-live. Licensee remains responsible for validating its use of the Licensed Software in accordance with Section 8.2 of the License Agreement.

2.2 **Validation Planning Support.** Licensor will:

(a) review the Validation Master Plan and provide written comments within ten (10) Business Days after the SOW Effective Date;

(b) prepare a supplier validation package for the version of the Licensed Software to be deployed at go-live, including supplier test evidence, release notes, a requirements traceability matrix mapped to Licensee's user requirement specifications, and Licensor's standard Part 11 and Annex 11 compliance assessment; and

(c) participate in a validation risk assessment workshop with Licensee's quality and manufacturing personnel to confirm the GxP-critical functions to be tested.

2.3 **Protocol Preparation.** Licensor will draft, for Licensee's review and approval, IQ and OQ protocols for the Licensed Software as configured for the Facility, using Licensee's templates. Licensor will provide technical input to the PQ protocols, which Licensee will author.

2.4 **Execution Support.** Licensor will:

(a) execute the IQ protocol in the validation environment and production environment, with Licensee's witness;

(b) provide on-site and remote support to Licensee's testers during OQ and PQ execution, including troubleshooting, test-data preparation and assistance with the recording of test evidence;

(c) investigate and document root cause for test deviations attributable to the Licensed Software or Licensor's configuration, and implement any configuration corrections under Licensee's change-control procedures; and

(d) provide a daily execution status report during each execution window.

2.5 **Part 11 Controls Verification.** Licensor will configure and demonstrate the electronic signature manifestations, audit-trail review reports, password and session controls, and role-based access matrix required by Licensee's user requirement specifications, and will support Licensee's verification of those controls during OQ.

2.6 **Interfaces.** Licensor will support the testing of the interfaces between the Licensed Software and Licensee's enterprise resource planning system and laboratory information management system, as configured under SOW No. 1. Licensor's responsibility is limited to the Licensed Software side of each interface.

2.7 **Production Readiness.** Licensor will:

(a) prepare a production cutover plan, including a rollback plan, for Licensee's approval;

(b) perform the production cutover in accordance with the approved plan during an agreed maintenance window;

(c) provide hypercare support for thirty (30) days after go-live, with a named Licensor consultant available during the Facility's first and second production shifts on Business Days; and

(d) hand over the system to Licensor's support organization under the Service Level Schedule at the end of hypercare.

2.8 **Training.** Licensor will deliver train-the-trainer sessions for up to twenty (20) Licensee super-users in batch-record execution, review by exception, deviation initiation and administrator functions, and will provide training materials that Licensee may adapt for its own training program.

2.9 **Out of Scope.** The following are outside the scope of this SOW No. 2 unless added by change order under Section 6.8 of the License Agreement: (a) PQ protocol authorship; (b) validation of Licensee's other computerized systems; (c) configuration of additional modules or sites; (d) remediation of Licensee master data beyond the corrections described in Section 2.4(c); and (e) any rework of the Phase 1 Deliverables, which remains governed by SOW No. 1 and the License Agreement.

## ATTACHMENT A: DETAILED ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

A.1 **General.** Each documentary Phase 2 Deliverable will: (a) be prepared on the applicable Licensee template; (b) be complete, with no placeholders or unresolved comments; (c) be internally consistent and consistent with the approved user requirement specifications and validation risk assessment; and (d) comply with Licensee's good documentation practice procedures.

A.2 **Supplier Validation Package (D2.2).** The traceability matrix will trace each approved user requirement to at least one supplier test or OQ test. The Part 11 and Annex 11 compliance assessment will address each applicable requirement of 21 C.F.R. Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 and identify any procedural controls Licensee must implement.

A.3 **Protocols (D2.3 and D2.4).** Each protocol will state its objective, scope, prerequisites, test steps, expected results and acceptance criteria, and will provide space for recording actual results, tester and reviewer signatures and deviation references.

A.4 **Executed IQ (D2.5).** The IQ summary report will confirm that the Licensed Software and its supporting infrastructure components have been installed in accordance with the approved specifications in each environment covered by the IQ protocol, and will list all deviations and their closure status.

A.5 **Deviation Reports (D2.6).** Each deviation report will identify the test step affected, the observed result, the root cause, the corrective action taken and any impact on other tests.

A.6 **Hypercare Closure Report (D2.9).** The report will list every incident logged during hypercare, its priority under the Service Level Schedule, its resolution and any open items transferred to Licensor's support organization.
