# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Guaranty Date**") by **Kessmont Technology Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 900 Glenwood Avenue, Suite 1200, Raleigh, North Carolina 27605 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1201 Market Street, Suite 1800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Lairal Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensor**"), and the Beneficiary, as licensee, are entering into a Software License Agreement dated March 16, 2026 (the "**License Agreement**"). Under it, Licensor will license its LairalQMS software to the Beneficiary on a hosted basis and provide related Hosted Services and Professional Services.

B. The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding membership interests in Licensor. The Guarantor will benefit, directly and indirectly, from Licensor entering into and performing the Agreement.

C. Section 16.4 of the License Agreement requires Licensor to deliver this Guaranty to the Beneficiary when the License Agreement is executed. The Beneficiary is entering into the License Agreement partly in reliance on this Guaranty.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Beneficiary entering into the License Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which the Guarantor acknowledges, the Guarantor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the License Agreement. In this Guaranty:

"**Agreement**" has the meaning given in Section 1.1 of the License Agreement. It means the License Agreement together with each Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and each Statement of Work, as amended from time to time.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Insolvency Proceeding**" means any proceeding under the U.S. Bankruptcy Code or any other bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation, dissolution, arrangement or similar law of any jurisdiction, whether voluntary or involuntary.

"**Losses**" means all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the License Agreement applies to this Guaranty as if set out here, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Guaranty. References to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless stated otherwise.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety:

(a) the full and punctual payment when due of every amount that Licensor is or becomes obliged to pay to the Beneficiary under or in connection with the Agreement. This includes refunds of prepaid Fees, refunds of Fees for nonconforming or terminated Deliverables, service credits, indemnification payments and damages; and

(b) the full and punctual performance and observance by Licensor of each of its other covenants, obligations and undertakings under the Agreement. This includes its obligations relating to the Hosted Services, the Professional Services, support, backup and disaster recovery, GxP compliance and regulatory support, confidentiality and security, the return of Licensee Data and transition assistance,

in each case whether those obligations exist now or arise later, and whether they are actual or contingent (together, the "**Guaranteed Obligations**").

2.2 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not merely of collection. The Beneficiary need not first proceed against Licensor, exhaust any remedy against Licensor, or enforce any security or other guaranty before making a demand on the Guarantor or enforcing this Guaranty.

2.3 **Performance on Demand.** If Licensor fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, or fails to perform any other Guaranteed Obligation when required, the Guarantor will, within ten (10) Business Days after the Beneficiary's written demand:

(a) for a payment obligation, pay the amount due to the Beneficiary in immediately available funds in U.S. dollars; and

(b) for a performance obligation, perform the obligation or cause it to be performed. If the Guarantor does not do so, it will pay to the Beneficiary the Losses the Beneficiary incurs as a result of Licensor's failure to perform.

2.4 **Form of Demand.** A demand under Section 2.3 must be in writing and must describe in reasonable detail the Guaranteed Obligation that Licensor has failed to pay or perform. The Beneficiary may make more than one demand. A demand need not be preceded by any demand on Licensor, except any notice that the Agreement itself requires Licensee to give Licensor.

2.5 **Extent of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is coextensive with Licensor's liability under the Agreement. Any limitation or exclusion of Licensor's liability under Section 12 of the License Agreement applies to the Guarantor's liability for the corresponding Guaranteed Obligation to the same extent, and in the same way, as it applies to Licensor. The Guarantor is also liable under Section 2.7 for the Beneficiary's costs of enforcing this Guaranty.

2.6 **Defenses.** The Guarantor may assert against the Beneficiary any defense to payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation that Licensor could assert under the Agreement, other than the defenses waived in Section 3.

2.7 **Enforcement Costs.** The Guarantor will pay on demand all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Beneficiary incurs in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

2.8 **Payments Free of Set-Off.** The Guarantor will make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off, counterclaim or deduction, except deductions required by law. If the law requires the Guarantor to deduct or withhold any tax from a payment, the Guarantor will increase the payment so that, after the deduction or withholding, the Beneficiary receives the amount it would have received had no deduction or withholding been required.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY AND WAIVERS

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This is a continuing guaranty. It extends to all Guaranteed Obligations as they arise from time to time. It is not discharged by any intermediate payment or performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor knows of it or consents to it:

(a) any amendment, modification, supplement, extension or renewal of the Agreement, including the execution of any further Order Form or Statement of Work, or any change in the scope, Fees or schedule of any Professional Services;

(b) any extension of time, forbearance, waiver or indulgence that the Beneficiary grants to Licensor;

(c) any failure or delay by the Beneficiary in enforcing any right against Licensor or any other person;

(d) any change in the corporate existence, structure, ownership or control of Licensor or the Guarantor;

(e) any Insolvency Proceeding involving Licensor, or any disallowance, stay, discharge or limitation of Licensor's obligations in an Insolvency Proceeding, including any rejection of the Agreement;

(f) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of any Guaranteed Obligation against Licensor, or any lack of power or authority of Licensor or of any person acting on its behalf;

(g) the release or impairment of any security for, or any other guaranty of, the Guaranteed Obligations; or

(h) any other act, omission or circumstance that might otherwise operate as a legal or equitable discharge of a guarantor or surety, other than full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

3.3 **Waivers.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Guarantor waives:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty and of the creation, renewal or extension of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(b) presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of non-payment or non-performance, except the demand required by Section 2.3;

(c) any right to require the Beneficiary to proceed first against Licensor or any other person, or to pursue any other remedy;

(d) any defense based on suretyship or impairment of collateral; and

(e) any defense arising from any Insolvency Proceeding involving Licensor, or from any limitation of Licensor's liability that results from Licensor's lack of capacity or authority.

3.4 **Information.** The Guarantor is responsible for keeping itself informed of Licensor's financial condition and of all circumstances bearing on the risk of non-payment or non-performance of the Guaranteed Obligations. The Beneficiary has no duty to give the Guarantor any information about Licensor or the Agreement.

## 4. REINSTATEMENT

4.1 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty will remain in effect, or will be reinstated, as the case may be, if at any time any payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded, avoided or must otherwise be restored or returned by the Beneficiary. This includes a return in any Insolvency Proceeding of Licensor as a preference, fraudulent transfer or otherwise. The Guaranteed Obligations will then be treated as if the payment or performance had never been made.

4.2 **Stay of Acceleration.** If any Insolvency Proceeding involving Licensor stays the Beneficiary's right to enforce any Guaranteed Obligation against Licensor, the Guarantor will nevertheless pay or perform that Guaranteed Obligation on the Beneficiary's demand as if the stay did not apply.

## 5. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

5.1 **Deferral of Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations that are then due have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor will not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Licensor that arises from its payment or performance under this Guaranty. It will also not claim, in competition with the Beneficiary, in any Insolvency Proceeding of Licensor.

5.2 **Subordination.** Any indebtedness of Licensor to the Guarantor, now existing or later arising, is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations. If a payment or performance default by Licensor under the Agreement has occurred and is continuing, the Guarantor will not demand or accept payment of that indebtedness. Any amount the Guarantor receives in breach of this Section 5.2 will be held in trust for the Beneficiary and promptly paid over to it, to the extent needed to satisfy the Guaranteed Obligations then due.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary, as of the Guaranty Date, that:

6.1 **Organization.** It is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full corporate power and authority to own its assets and carry on its business as now conducted.

6.2 **Authority.** It has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty. The execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action of the Guarantor, including approval by its board of directors.

6.3 **Enforceability.** This Guaranty has been duly executed and delivered by the Guarantor. It constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of the Guarantor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity.

6.4 **No Conflict.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not and will not: (a) breach the Guarantor's certificate of incorporation or bylaws; (b) breach any law, regulation, judgment or order binding on the Guarantor; or (c) breach, or constitute a default under, any material agreement or instrument to which the Guarantor is a party or by which its assets are bound.

6.5 **Consents.** No consent, approval, authorization or filing with any governmental authority or other person is required for the Guarantor to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, other than those already obtained or made and in full force and effect.

6.6 **Financial Standing.** The audited consolidated financial statements of the Guarantor for its fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, which the Guarantor has delivered to the Beneficiary, were prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles applied consistently. They fairly present in all material respects the consolidated financial position and results of operations of the Guarantor and its subsidiaries as at that date and for that period. Since that date there has been no material adverse change in the financial condition of the Guarantor and its subsidiaries, taken as a whole.

6.7 **Solvency.** The Guarantor is solvent, is able to pay its debts as they fall due, and will not become insolvent as a result of giving this Guaranty.

6.8 **Litigation.** No action, suit or proceeding is pending or, to the Guarantor's knowledge, threatened against the Guarantor or Licensor before any court, arbitrator or governmental authority that would reasonably be expected to materially and adversely affect the Guarantor's ability to perform this Guaranty.

6.9 **Ownership.** The Guarantor directly or indirectly owns all of the outstanding membership interests in Licensor.

6.10 **Benefit.** The Guarantor has received, and will receive, direct and indirect benefits from Licensor entering into and performing the Agreement. This Guaranty is given for reasonably equivalent value and fair consideration.

## 7. COVENANTS

7.1 **Financial Statements.** For as long as this Guaranty remains in effect, the Guarantor will deliver to the Beneficiary on request:

(a) its audited consolidated annual financial statements for its most recently completed fiscal year, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of that fiscal year or, if later, within thirty (30) days after the Beneficiary's request; and

(b) a certificate signed by its chief financial officer confirming that the financial statements were prepared as described in Section 6.6, and that the representations in Sections 6.7 and 6.9 remain true.

The Beneficiary will treat all non-public financial information delivered under this Section 7.1 as Confidential Information of Licensor under Section 10 of the License Agreement.

7.2 **Notice of Certain Events.** The Guarantor will notify the Beneficiary in writing within five (5) Business Days after becoming aware of any of the following:

(a) any Insolvency Proceeding commenced by or against the Guarantor or Licensor;

(b) the Guarantor ceasing to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the outstanding membership interests in Licensor; or

(c) any event that has had, or would reasonably be expected to have, a material adverse effect on the Guarantor's ability to perform this Guaranty.

7.3 **Maintenance of Existence.** The Guarantor will maintain its corporate existence and good standing. It will not merge or consolidate with any person, or sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of its assets, unless the surviving or acquiring person assumes in writing all of the Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty and delivers that written assumption to the Beneficiary.

7.4 **Further Assurances.** The Guarantor will execute and deliver any further documents, and do any further acts, that the Beneficiary reasonably requests to give full effect to this Guaranty.

## 8. TERM

8.1 **Duration.** This Guaranty takes effect on the Guaranty Date. It continues in full force and effect for the Term and for any transition period under Section 14.7 of the License Agreement, and after that until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full.

8.2 **Accrued and Surviving Obligations.** The expiration of this Guaranty under Section 8.1 does not affect the Guarantor's liability for any Guaranteed Obligation arising before that time, or for any obligation of Licensor that survives the termination or expiration of the Agreement under Section 14.9 of the License Agreement. This includes Licensor's obligations relating to the return and deletion of Licensee Data, confidentiality and indemnification.

8.3 **No Revocation.** The Guarantor may not revoke or terminate this Guaranty, and this Guaranty is not affected by the Guarantor's dissolution.

No single or partial exercise of any right precludes any further exercise of that right or of any other right.

11.4 **Remedies Cumulative.** The Beneficiary's rights and remedies under this Guaranty are cumulative and in addition to any rights and remedies available to it under the Agreement, at law or in equity.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

11.6 **Independent Obligations.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are independent of Licensor's obligations under the Agreement. The Beneficiary may bring a separate action against the Guarantor, whether or not it brings an action against Licensor or joins Licensor in that action.

11.7 **Headings.** Headings in this Guaranty are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

11.8 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original, and all of which together constitute one instrument.
