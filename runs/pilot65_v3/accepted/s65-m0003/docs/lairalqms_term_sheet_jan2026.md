# LAIRALQMS HOSTED LICENSE AND IMPLEMENTATION

## SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

**Date:** January 22, 2026

**Status:** Non-binding indicative term sheet, except as stated in Section 16.

This Summary of Principal Terms (this "**Term Sheet**") sets out the principal terms on which Lairal Systems, LLC proposes to license its LairalQMS software to Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc. on a hosted basis and to provide related implementation and validation services. The Parties will negotiate definitive agreements on the basis of this Term Sheet. Capitalized terms have the meanings given in this Term Sheet or, where not defined here, the meanings the Parties intend to give them in the Definitive Agreements.

## 1. PARTIES

1.1 **Licensor.** Lairal Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 4400 Commerce Parkway, Suite 300, Cary, North Carolina 27518 ("**Lairal**").

1.2 **Licensee.** Trouvar Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 1201 Market Street, Suite 1800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Trouvar**").

1.3 **Guarantor.** Kessmont Technology Holdings, Inc., the parent company of Lairal ("**Kessmont**"). Its guaranty is described in Section 13.

1.4 **Affiliates.** Trouvar's Affiliates may use the Licensed Software under Trouvar's subscription. Trouvar will be responsible for their compliance.

Lairal and Trouvar are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 2. TRANSACTION OVERVIEW

2.1 **Purpose.** Trouvar manufactures sterile injectable pharmaceutical products and intends to replace its paper-based and hybrid batch-record processes with an electronic batch-record and quality-management system. Lairal will provide LairalQMS as a hosted service. Lairal will also perform the implementation, configuration, data migration and validation support needed to bring the system into GxP production use at Trouvar's Raleigh, North Carolina facility.

2.2 **Proposed Document Structure.** The Parties expect the transaction to be documented in the following agreements (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**"):

(a) a Software License Agreement setting out the master legal terms (the "**License Agreement**");

(b) Order Form No. 1, setting out the licensed modules, user counts, subscription fees, the Initial Term and other commercial terms;

(c) a Service Level Schedule, setting out availability, support response and service credit commitments for the Hosted Services;

(d) one or more Statements of Work for Professional Services, starting with Statement of Work No. 1 for Phase 1 implementation and configuration; and

(e) a parent guaranty from Kessmont.

2.3 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties will also enter into a GxP quality agreement within an agreed period after signing, covering quality responsibilities only. Section 8.3 describes it.

2.4 **Target Signing.** The Parties are aiming to sign the License Agreement, Order Form No. 1, the Service Level Schedule and the Kessmont guaranty by mid-March 2026. Statement of Work No. 1 is to be signed at the same time or shortly afterwards.

## 3. LICENSED SOFTWARE AND HOSTED SERVICES

3.1 **Licensed Software.** LairalQMS in object-code form, including the electronic batch-record, deviation, change-control, CAPA, document-control and training modules, as finally listed in Order Form No. 1. The Licensed Software includes all Updates and the Documentation.

3.2 **License Scope.** A non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license, for the Term, for Authorized Users to access and use the Licensed Software through the Hosted Services. Use is limited to Trouvar's and its Affiliates' internal business purposes, including the manufacture, testing, quality review and release of pharmaceutical products. Contract manufacturers, consultants and auditors working for Trouvar may be Authorized Users, provided they are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

3.3 **Authorized Users.** The initial user counts and user types (for example, full execution users, reviewer and approver users, and read-only users) will be set out in Order Form No. 1. Trouvar will provide a headcount forecast for the Raleigh, North Carolina facility before the Order Form is finalized. Excess use will be charged at rates stated in the Order Form.

3.4 **Hosted Services.** Lairal will host Trouvar's production, validation and disaster-recovery environments of the Licensed Software. This includes the computing infrastructure, storage, network connectivity, monitoring and system administration needed to make those environments available to Trouvar. The validation environment will be used for Trouvar's computerized system validation activities and for testing each Update before it is deployed to production.

3.5 **Hosting Location.** All hosting and all storage of Licensee Data will be in data centers located in the United States. Any third-party infrastructure provider used by Lairal must also be in the United States and must hold appropriate security certifications.

3.6 **Updates and Release Management.** All Updates are included in the subscription fees. Lairal will give advance notice of major version releases, with release notes and impact assessment materials. Lairal will deploy each major release to the validation environment before production. Lairal will not move a major release into production until Trouvar has completed its change-control and validation activities, provided Trouvar does not unreasonably delay. The notice period and process will be set out in the License Agreement.

3.7 **Backup and Disaster Recovery.** Lairal will keep backup and disaster-recovery arrangements for the Hosted Services. These will include encrypted daily full backups, frequent incremental backups, geographically separate backup storage within the United States, recovery point and recovery time objectives stated in the Service Level Schedule, and at least annual testing with results shared with Trouvar.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** Lairal's indicative Annual Subscription Fee for the modules and user counts discussed to date is $1,240,000. It is payable annually in advance and is subject to confirmation of final module selection and user counts in Order Form No. 1.

4.2 **Renewal Pricing.** Increases on renewal will be capped at the lesser of five percent (5%) and the annual change in CPI-U, with advance written notice before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 **Professional Services Fees.** Professional Services will be priced in each Statement of Work. Lairal proposes a fixed milestone fee structure for Phase 1. The indicative Phase 1 Milestone Fee is $310,000, payable on acceptance of the Phase 1 Deliverables. Phase 2 (validation and production readiness) will be scoped and priced in a later Statement of Work. Additional services will be charged at hourly rates stated in each Statement of Work.

4.4 **Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Trouvar may withhold amounts disputed in good faith if it gives prompt notice and pays any undisputed portion.

4.5 **Expenses.** Reasonable, documented travel expenses will be reimbursed only if approved in advance by Trouvar and incurred under Trouvar's travel policy.

4.6 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Taxes on Lairal's net income are Lairal's responsibility.

## 5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 **Exclusivity Commitment.** In consideration of the subscription pricing in Section 4.1, Trouvar agrees to the following exclusivity: no third-party batch-record software at the Raleigh, North Carolina facility during the Initial Term.

5.2 **Documentation.** The exclusivity commitment in Section 5.1 will be reflected in the Definitive Agreements.

5.3 **Lairal Commitment.** Lairal has confirmed that the subscription pricing in Section 4.1 takes into account the commitment in Section 5.1.

## 6. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES, DELIVERABLES AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 **Phasing.** The project is expected to run in two phases:

(a) **Phase 1 (Implementation and Configuration):** configuration of the batch-record, deviation, change-control and CAPA modules for the Raleigh, North Carolina facility; master data migration; interfaces to Trouvar's ERP and LIMS systems on the LairalQMS side; and the related configuration specifications and data migration report; and

(b) **Phase 2 (Validation and Production Readiness):** validation planning support, IQ and OQ protocol preparation and execution support, Part 11 controls verification, production cutover, hypercare and train-the-trainer sessions.

6.2 **Standard of Performance.** Lairal will perform the Professional Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, with appropriately qualified personnel, in accordance with good industry practice for implementing GxP computerized systems.

6.3 **Delivery.** Each Deliverable will be delivered by the date stated in the applicable Statement of Work. Delivery will occur when Lairal makes the Deliverable available to Trouvar's project manager, or deploys configurations to the applicable environment, together with written notice that the Deliverable is ready for review.

6.4 **Acceptance.** Trouvar will have fifteen (15) Business Days after delivery to review each Deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the Statement of Work and to reject it by written notice identifying the nonconformities. If Trouvar does not reject a Deliverable within that period, it will be deemed accepted. Business Days will exclude weekends and U.S. federal holidays.

6.5 **Correction.** Lairal will correct and redeliver a validly rejected Deliverable within an agreed period, and the acceptance procedure will apply again. After a second valid rejection, Trouvar may require a further correction attempt, accept with an equitable fee reduction, or terminate the affected phase and receive a refund of fees paid for the Deliverable.

6.6 **Milestone Fees.** Milestone fees will become payable on acceptance or deemed acceptance of the related Deliverables.

6.7 **Deliverable Warranty.** Each Deliverable will be warranted to conform in all material respects to its specifications and acceptance criteria for ninety (90) days after acceptance or deemed acceptance. Lairal will correct nonconformities notified during that period at no additional charge.

6.8 **Key Personnel and Subcontractors.** Key personnel will be named in each Statement of Work. Lairal may not replace them without Trouvar's consent, except in customary circumstances. Subcontractors for Professional Services will require Trouvar's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

6.9 **Change Procedure.** Changes to scope, schedule or fees will be made by written change order signed by both Parties, following a written impact assessment by Lairal.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS AND SUPPORT

7.1 **Service Level Schedule.** The Hosted Services and support will be provided in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. It will set out monthly availability commitments, maintenance windows, support tiers and hours, incident priority definitions, response and resolution targets, recovery point and recovery time objectives, and service credits.

7.2 **Maintenance.** Scheduled maintenance will take place only within agreed windows, with advance notice for any event affecting the production environment. Lairal will schedule maintenance to minimize disruption to Trouvar's manufacturing shifts.

7.3 **Service Credits and Chronic Failure.** Service credits will not be Trouvar's sole remedy. Trouvar will have a right to terminate the affected Order Form for chronic failure to meet the availability commitment, on terms to be agreed in the License Agreement.

7.4 **Suspension.** Lairal may suspend access only for an imminent security threat or for prolonged non-payment of undisputed fees after notice. Read-only access to Licensee Data needed for batch disposition, recall or regulatory inspection will not be suspended.

## 8. GxP COMPLIANCE AND VALIDATION

8.1 **Compliance Functionality.** Lairal will maintain the Licensed Software so that, when configured and used in accordance with the Documentation, it supports compliance with 21 C.F.R. Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11. This includes audit trails, electronic signature controls, role-based access and record retention.

8.2 **Validation.** Trouvar is responsible for validating its intended use of the Licensed Software. Lairal will support validation as set out in the applicable Statement of Work and will provide its standard validation accelerator package for each release.

8.3 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties will negotiate and enter into a quality agreement within sixty (60) days after signing the License Agreement. It will cover change control, deviation management, incident management and supplier audit arrangements. The quality agreement will not amend the commercial or legal terms.

8.4 **Audits and Inspections.** Trouvar will be entitled to one routine supplier quality audit per calendar year on reasonable notice, plus for-cause audits. Lairal will cooperate with Regulatory Authority inspections and requests relating to the Licensed Software, the Hosted Services and Licensee Data.

8.5 **Infrastructure Qualification.** Lairal will maintain the hosting infrastructure in a qualified state, with documented installation and operational qualification records available to Trouvar on request.

8.6 **Debarment.** Lairal will represent that neither it nor its service personnel is debarred under 21 U.S.C. § 335a.

## 9. DATA, CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITY

9.1 **Ownership of Licensee Data.** Trouvar will own all Licensee Data, including batch records, audit trails and electronic signatures. Lairal will use Licensee Data only to provide the services and will not sell, mine, aggregate or use it to train analytical or machine-learning models.

9.2 **Security Program.** Lairal will maintain a written information security program consistent with ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2. It will include encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, logical segregation of customer data, vulnerability scanning and annual third-party penetration testing. Lairal will provide its SOC 2 Type II report annually.

9.3 **Security Incidents.** Lairal will notify Trouvar of any Security Incident within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of it. Lairal will investigate and remediate it, including assessing its effect on GxP record integrity.

9.4 **Confidentiality.** The License Agreement will contain mutual confidentiality obligations surviving for five (5) years after termination, and longer for trade secrets and Licensee Data. Information exchanged under the existing Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the Parties will be treated as confidential under the License Agreement from signing.

9.5 **Personal Data.** The Parties will put in place any data processing terms required by applicable law.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **Ownership.** Lairal will retain ownership of the Licensed Software, the Documentation and its pre-existing tools and methodologies. Trouvar will own Licensee-specific Deliverables, such as validation protocols and reports, user requirement specifications, SOPs and configuration specifications prepared for Trouvar. Trouvar will receive a perpetual, royalty-free license to any Lairal materials embedded in Deliverables, for internal business and regulatory purposes, including retention of validation records after termination.

10.2 **Lairal IP Indemnity.** Lairal will defend and indemnify Trouvar, its Affiliates and their personnel against third-party claims that the Licensed Software, the Hosted Services or any Deliverable infringes or misappropriates third-party Intellectual Property Rights. Customary exclusions will apply for unauthorized modifications, combinations, Licensee Materials and failure to implement a non-infringing Update.

10.3 **Mitigation and Transition.** If Lairal must withdraw an infringing item, it will first try to procure the right to continue use or provide a non-infringing replacement without compromising Trouvar's validated state. If Lairal terminates the affected license instead, it will give Trouvar a transition period of not less than one hundred eighty (180) days to implement and validate a replacement system, and will refund prepaid fees.

10.4 **Procedure.** Customary notice, control-of-defense and cooperation procedures will apply. The indemnifying party may not settle a claim in a way that imposes obligations or admissions on the indemnified party without its consent.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Mutual exclusion of indirect, consequential, special and punitive damages and lost profits, subject to the exceptions in Section 11.3.

11.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.3 **Exceptions.** The cap and exclusion will not apply to indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, data protection and security obligations, gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, the obligation to pay fees properly due, or liability that cannot be limited by law. The precise drafting of the exceptions will be settled in the License Agreement.

11.4 **Data Restoration.** Separately from the cap, Lairal will at its own cost restore Licensee Data lost or corrupted through its breach and will help reconstruct any GxP records that cannot be restored.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Term.** The License Agreement will continue while any Order Form is in effect. Order Form No. 1 will run for the Initial Term stated in it and will then renew automatically for one-year renewal terms, unless either Party gives at least ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

12.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate for uncured material breach after a thirty (30) day cure period, or on the other Party's insolvency. Trouvar will have the additional chronic service failure right described in Section 7.3.

12.3 **Effect of Termination.** Prepaid fees for any period after termination will be refunded where Trouvar terminates for cause.

12.4 **Data Return.** Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiration, Lairal will provide a complete export of Licensee Data at no charge. The export will include audit trails, metadata and electronic signature manifestations in a non-proprietary, human-readable and machine-readable format. Lairal will retain the data for a verification period and then certify deletion.

12.5 **Transition Assistance.** On request, Lairal will continue the Licensed Software and Hosted Services for a transition period of up to twelve (12) months at the then-current fees, prorated. Migration assistance will be charged at professional services rates. Transition assistance will be available whatever the reason for termination, subject to advance payment where Lairal terminated for non-payment.

12.6 **Archive Access.** Lairal will offer read-only archive access to Licensee Data after termination for Trouvar's record retention needs, on terms to be agreed.

12.7 **Bankruptcy.** The license will be expressed to be a license of intellectual property for purposes of Section 365(n) of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code.

## 13. PARENT GUARANTY

13.1 **Guaranty.** At signing, Kessmont will deliver a guaranty of Lairal's payment and performance obligations under the Definitive Agreements.

13.2 **Key Terms.** The guaranty will be a guaranty of payment and performance, not merely of collection. It will continue for the Term and for any transition period, and will be governed by Delaware law. Kessmont will provide customary representations on authority and financial standing, and will deliver annual financial statements on request.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 **Lairal Insurance.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterwards, Lairal will maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, at least:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) technology errors and omissions insurance of $10,000,000;

(c) cyber liability and network security insurance of $10,000,000;

(d) statutory workers' compensation insurance and employer's liability insurance of $1,000,000 per accident; and

(e) umbrella or excess liability insurance of $5,000,000.

14.2 **Certificates.** Lairal will provide certificates of insurance at signing and on each renewal. Lairal will give thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation or material reduction of coverage. Insurance will not limit Lairal's liability.

## 15. OTHER TERMS FOR THE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENTS

15.1 **Assignment.** The License Agreement will contain assignment provisions to be negotiated by the Parties.

15.2 **Dispute Escalation.** Disputes will be escalated first to the Parties' project managers and then to senior executives before any court proceedings, other than proceedings for interim relief.

15.3 **Continued Performance.** Lairal will not suspend services or withhold Licensee Data during a dispute, except as permitted under Section 7.4.

15.4 **Force Majeure.** Customary force majeure provisions will apply. Force majeure will not excuse Lairal's backup and disaster-recovery obligations. Trouvar will have a termination right if the Hosted Services are unavailable because of force majeure for an extended period.

15.5 **Non-Solicitation.** There will be a mutual non-solicitation of project personnel during each Statement of Work and for twelve (12) months afterwards, with a customary exception for general solicitations.

15.6 **Publicity.** Neither Party will use the other's name or marks without prior written consent.

15.7 **Governing Law and Forum for the Definitive Agreements.** The Definitive Agreements will be governed by Delaware law. The state and federal courts in New Castle County, Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction.

15.8 **Next Steps.** Lairal's counsel will prepare first drafts of the License Agreement, Order Form No. 1 and the Service Level Schedule. Trouvar's counsel will review them, and Trouvar's quality unit will review the GxP provisions and the draft quality agreement. Lairal will provide its current SOC 2 Type II report, its validation accelerator package and certificates of insurance as part of Trouvar's supplier qualification process.

## 16. EFFECT OF THIS TERM SHEET

16.1 **Non-Binding.** Except for this Section 16, this Term Sheet is a statement of intent only. It does not create any legally binding obligation on either Party. No binding agreement on the transaction will exist unless and until the Definitive Agreements have been negotiated, approved and signed by both Parties. Either Party may end negotiations at any time before signing without liability, except under this Section 16.

16.2 **Binding Provisions.** The following provisions are legally binding on the Parties when this Term Sheet is countersigned:

(a) **Confidentiality.** The existence and terms of this Term Sheet and the negotiations are Confidential Information under the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the Parties, and are subject to its terms.

(b) **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses, including legal and advisory fees, incurred in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreements.

(c) **Governing Law.** This Section 16 is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

(d) **Counterparts.** This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

16.3 **No Reliance.** Neither Party may rely on this Term Sheet as creating any obligation to enter into the Definitive Agreements on the terms described here or at all.
