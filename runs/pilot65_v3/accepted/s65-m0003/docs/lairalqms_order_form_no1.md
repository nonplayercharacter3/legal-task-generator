## 2. DEFINITIONS AND SUBSCRIPTION FEES

2.1 **Definitions.** In this Order Form, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"**Annual Subscription Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Hosted Services**" means the hosting by or on behalf of Licensor of the single production instance of the Licensed Software identified in Section 7.1 of this Order Form, including the provision of the computing infrastructure, storage, network connectivity, monitoring and system administration necessary to make that instance available to Licensee and its Authorized Users.

"**Raleigh Facility**" means Licensee's sterile injectables manufacturing facility located at 3100 Spring Forest Road, Raleigh, North Carolina 27616.

"**Subscription Start Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Subscription Year**" means each period of twelve (12) months beginning on the Subscription Start Date or on an anniversary of the Subscription Start Date.

"**User Type**" means each category of Authorized User listed in Section 3.2.

2.2 **Annual Subscription Fee.** Licensee will pay Licensor an annual subscription fee of $1,240,000 for each Subscription Year of the Initial Term (the "**Annual Subscription Fee**").

2.3 **Scope of the Annual Subscription Fee.** The Annual Subscription Fee covers, for the applicable Subscription Year:

(a) the license to the Licensed Software modules listed in Section 3.1 for the number of Authorized Users of each User Type listed in Section 3.2;

(b) the Hosted Services;

(c) all Updates, in accordance with Section 3.4 of the License Agreement;

(d) support services in accordance with Section 7.4 of the License Agreement and the Service Level Schedule;

(e) Licensor's standard validation accelerator package for each release of the Licensed Software, in accordance with Section 8.2 of the License Agreement; and

(f) one (1) routine supplier quality audit in each calendar year, in accordance with Section 8.4 of the License Agreement.

2.4 **Fee Allocation.** For Licensee's internal accounting and budgeting purposes only, the Annual Subscription Fee is allocated as follows. The allocation does not affect Licensee's obligation to pay the Annual Subscription Fee as a single annual amount.

| Component | Annual Amount |
|---|---|
| Electronic batch-record module | $520,000 |
| Deviation, change-control and CAPA modules | $310,000 |
| Document-control and training modules | $150,000 |
| Hosted Services and support | $260,000 |
| **Total Annual Subscription Fee** | **$1,240,000** |

2.5 **Additional Authorized Users.** Licensee may increase the number of Authorized Users of any User Type at any time by written notice to Licensor. Additional Authorized Users will be charged at the annual per-user rates listed below, prorated from the date of the increase to the end of the then-current Subscription Year, and will then be included in the Annual Subscription Fee for each later Subscription Year of the Initial Term. The same rates apply to any excess use invoiced under Section 4.3 of the License Agreement.

| User Type | Annual Rate per Additional Authorized User |
|---|---|
| Execution User | $2,400 |
| Reviewer/Approver User | $3,100 |
| Administrator User | $3,600 |
| Read-Only User | $650 |

2.6 **Rate Protection.** The per-user rates in Section 2.5 are fixed for the Initial Term. Any adjustment to the Annual Subscription Fee or the per-user rates for a renewal term is subject to Section 9.8 of the License Agreement.

2.7 **Taxes.** All amounts stated in this Order Form exclude taxes. Taxes will be invoiced and paid in accordance with Section 9.7 of the License Agreement.

## 3. LICENSED SOFTWARE, AUTHORIZED USERS AND LICENSED SITE

3.1 **Licensed Modules.** The Licensed Software under this Order Form comprises the following LairalQMS modules, in the current generally available release on the Subscription Start Date and all Updates made available during the Term:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Electronic Batch Record (eBR) | Master batch record authoring, electronic batch-record execution, in-process data capture, equipment and material verification, review by exception and electronic batch release |
| Deviation Management | Deviation initiation, investigation, impact assessment, root cause analysis and closure |
| Change Control | Change request, impact assessment, approval workflow, implementation tracking and effectiveness review |
| CAPA | Corrective and preventive action planning, tracking and effectiveness verification |
| Document Control | Controlled document authoring, review, approval, periodic review and distribution |
| Training Management | Curricula, training assignments, records and qualification status linked to document control |

3.2 **Authorized Users.** The Annual Subscription Fee includes the following numbers of Authorized Users of each User Type:

| User Type | Description | Number Included |
|---|---|---|
| Execution User | Manufacturing and quality control personnel who execute batch records and record GxP data | 240 |
| Reviewer/Approver User | Supervisors and quality assurance personnel who review, approve and apply electronic signatures to records | 95 |
| Administrator User | Personnel who configure master data, roles and workflows | 12 |
| Read-Only User | Personnel, auditors and consultants with view-only access | 75 |

3.3 **Licensed Site.** The Authorized Users listed in Section 3.2 are licensed for use in connection with the manufacturing, testing, quality review and release operations of the Raleigh Facility. Authorized Users may access the Licensed Software remotely, including from Licensee's Wilmington, Delaware offices, in connection with those operations. Use of the Licensed Software in connection with any additional manufacturing site requires a further Order Form or an amendment to this Order Form.

3.4 **User Type Reallocation.** Once in each Subscription Year, Licensee may by written notice reallocate up to ten percent (10%) of the Authorized Users of one User Type to another User Type of equal or lower annual per-user rate, without additional charge.

3.5 **Contract Users.** Contract manufacturers, consultants, auditors and other third parties may be designated as Authorized Users in accordance with Section 3.3 of the License Agreement and count against the numbers in Section 3.2.

## 4. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

4.1 **Subscription Start Date.** The subscription under this Order Form begins on March 16, 2026 (the "**Subscription Start Date**").

4.2 **Initial Term.** The Initial Term of this Order Form is three (3) years, beginning on the Subscription Start Date and ending on March 15, 2029.

4.3 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Order Form renews automatically for successive renewal terms of one (1) year each, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 14.1 of the License Agreement.

4.4 **Go-Live.** The Parties expect production go-live of the Licensed Software at the Raleigh Facility to follow completion of the Professional Services under Statement of Work No. 1 and any later Statement of Work for validation and production readiness. The Subscription Start Date, the Initial Term and Licensee's obligation to pay the Annual Subscription Fee are not affected by the date of production go-live.

## 6. SPECIAL TERMS

6.1 **Exclusivity.** In consideration of the subscription pricing in Section 2, during the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Licensee shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, license, procure, develop or use, at any facility worldwide, any manufacturing execution, quality-management or batch-record software other than the Licensed Software.

6.2 **Reference Customer.** Subject to Section 17.13 of the License Agreement, Licensee will consider in good faith any request from Licensor to act as a reference customer for prospective customers of Licensor. Licensee may decline any request in its sole discretion, and any reference will be limited to a confidential telephone call between the prospective customer and a Licensee representative chosen by Licensee.

6.3 **Price Hold for Additional Sites.** If, during the first two (2) Subscription Years, Licensee executes a further Order Form for the use of the Licensed Software at an additional manufacturing site of Licensee or its Affiliates, the annual per-user rates for that site will not exceed the rates in Section 2.5.

6.4 **Early Payment Credit.** If Licensee pays the invoice for Subscription Year 1 within fifteen (15) days after receipt, Licensor will apply a one-time credit of $12,400 against the invoice for Subscription Year 2.

6.5 **Executive Sponsorship.** Each Party will designate an executive sponsor for the relationship under this Order Form. The executive sponsors will meet at least twice in each Subscription Year to review the Parties' relationship, the product roadmap for the licensed modules and Licensee's planned use of the Licensed Software.
