# LINEN AND LAUNDRY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Linen and Laundry Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Ardmoor Linen Services, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Client**"). Provider and Client are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider operates a commercial laundry facility and provides rental linen, customer-owned goods laundering and related delivery services to hotels, restaurants and other hospitality businesses in the Hudson Valley region of New York.

B. Client owns and operates boutique hotel properties and wishes to engage Provider to supply and launder linen for those properties on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 **Services.** Provider shall supply, launder, finish, deliver and collect the linen and related items described in Schedule A (the "**Services**") for the properties listed in Schedule A (each, a "**Serviced Property**").

1.2 **Schedule A.** Schedule A is part of this Agreement and is governed by it. Schedule A sets out the item types, pricing, delivery frequency and par levels for each Serviced Property.

1.3 **Additional Properties.** Client may add a property to Schedule A by giving Provider at least thirty (30) days' written notice. The Parties shall agree in writing the par levels and delivery schedule for the added property. Provider's pricing in Schedule A shall apply to the added property unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing. Provider may decline to add any property located more than seventy-five (75) miles from its facility in Kingston, New York.

1.4 **Removal of Properties.** Client may remove a Serviced Property from Schedule A on sixty (60) days' written notice to Provider if Client sells, closes or ceases to manage that property. Client shall return all Rental Linen held at the removed property, or pay the replacement charges in Schedule A for any Rental Linen not returned within thirty (30) days after the removal date.

1.5 **Non-Exclusive.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Client from obtaining linen, laundry or similar services from any other supplier, or from laundering items itself at any property.

## 2. SERVICE STANDARDS

2.1 **Quality.** Provider shall launder and finish all items in accordance with good commercial laundry practice for full-service hospitality customers. Delivered items shall be clean, dry, pressed or folded as specified in Schedule A, free of stains, odors, tears and visible wear, and wrapped or bagged to prevent soiling in transit.

2.2 **Hygiene.** Provider shall process all items in accordance with applicable New York State Department of Health requirements and shall maintain its facility in a clean and sanitary condition. Provider shall keep soiled and clean goods physically separated throughout handling and transport.

2.3 **Delivery.** Provider shall deliver clean items and collect soiled items at each Serviced Property on the days and within the delivery windows stated in Schedule A. Provider's drivers shall follow each property's reasonable access, parking and security procedures notified to Provider in writing.

2.4 **Rejected Items.** Client may reject any delivered item that does not meet Section 2.1. Provider shall reprocess or replace rejected items at no charge and deliver the replacement on the next scheduled delivery, or within twenty-four (24) hours if Client reasonably notifies Provider that the shortfall affects guest-room readiness.

2.5 **Shortages.** If Provider fails to deliver at least ninety-five percent (95%) of the par quantity of any item on a scheduled delivery, Provider shall make up the shortfall within twenty-four (24) hours at no additional charge. If Provider fails to do so, Client may obtain substitute items or services from another supplier for the affected delivery, and Provider shall reimburse Client for the reasonable cost of those substitute items or services in excess of the amount Client would have paid under this Agreement.

2.6 **Account Contact.** Provider shall designate an account manager who is available during normal business hours to address delivery, quality and billing matters.

## 3. LINEN OWNERSHIP AND CARE

3.1 **Rental Linen.** Items that Provider supplies on a rental basis ("**Rental Linen**") remain the property of Provider at all times. Client shall not remove Rental Linen from the Serviced Properties except for return to Provider.

3.2 **Customer-Owned Goods.** Items owned by Client and laundered by Provider ("**Customer-Owned Goods**") remain the property of Client at all times, including while in Provider's possession. Provider bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, Customer-Owned Goods from the time Provider collects them until Provider delivers them back to the Serviced Property.

3.3 **Loss and Damage.** Client shall pay the replacement charges in Schedule A for Rental Linen that is lost, stolen or damaged beyond normal wear while at a Serviced Property, other than through Provider's fault. Provider shall replace at its own cost Rental Linen that has reached the end of its useful life through normal wear. Provider shall reimburse Client at replacement cost for Customer-Owned Goods lost or damaged while in Provider's possession.

3.4 **Inventory Reconciliation.** Provider shall conduct a physical inventory count of Rental Linen at each Serviced Property at least once every six (6) months, with Client's personnel present if Client wishes. Replacement charges under Section 3.3 shall be based on the reconciled inventory count.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay the fees set out in Schedule A for the Services performed. Fees exclude applicable sales and use taxes, which Client shall pay where applicable.

4.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Client monthly in arrears, with a separate statement for each Serviced Property showing item counts delivered and collected. Invoices are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

4.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Client may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Provider in writing of the basis for the dispute before the due date and pays the undisputed portion. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Price Adjustments.** Provider may adjust the prices in Schedule A once in each contract year, effective on an anniversary of the Effective Date, by giving Client at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice. Any adjustment shall not exceed the lesser of four percent (4%) or the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), New York-Newark-Jersey City area, over the preceding twelve (12) months.

4.5 **Fuel and Surcharges.** Provider shall not charge any fuel, energy, environmental or similar surcharge not set out in Schedule A.

## 5. PERSONNEL

5.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign trained and qualified personnel to perform the Services and may replace them on reasonable notice to Client.

5.2 **Conduct.** While at any Serviced Property, Provider's personnel shall comply with Client's reasonable workplace, safety and security rules notified to Provider in writing, and shall not enter guest rooms or guest-only areas.

5.3 **Background Checks.** Provider shall conduct background checks, to the extent permitted by applicable law, on all drivers and other personnel who have access to non-public areas of any Serviced Property.

5.4 **Employer Responsibility.** Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, taxes, workers' compensation coverage and supervision of its personnel.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 **Obligation.** Each Party shall protect the other Party's non-public business information disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, occupancy levels and operating procedures, with at least reasonable care, and shall use it only to perform or receive the Services.

6.2 **Exclusions.** Section 6.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or is required to be disclosed by law, provided that the receiving Party gives prompt notice of any legally compelled disclosure where legally permitted.

6.3 **Guest Items.** Provider shall not retain any guest property found in laundered items. Provider shall log any such property and return it to the relevant Serviced Property on the next scheduled delivery.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of two (2) years. It then renews automatically for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

7.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

7.3 **Repeated Service Failures.** Client may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice if Provider fails to meet Section 2.1 or Section 2.5 at any Serviced Property on four (4) or more scheduled deliveries in any ninety (90) day period.

7.4 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on ninety (90) days' written notice to the other Party.

7.5 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) Client shall pay for Services performed through the termination date; (b) Client shall make all Rental Linen available for collection by Provider within fifteen (15) days, and Section 3.3 applies to any Rental Linen not returned; and (c) Provider shall return all Customer-Owned Goods in its possession, laundered, within fifteen (15) days.

7.6 **Survival.** Sections 3, 4 (as to amounts accrued), 6, 7.5, 7.6, 8, 9 and 11 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Client and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, members, managers and employees, from and against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel, or by Provider's breach of Section 2.2.

8.2 **By Client.** Client shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, members, managers and employees from and against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Client or its personnel.

8.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim, but may not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

8.4 **Affiliates.** For purposes of this Agreement, "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 **Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Except for a Party's obligations under Section 8 or liability arising from its gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

9.2 **Cap.** Except for a Party's obligations under Section 8, Client's payment obligations under Section 4, or liability arising from a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Client under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 10. INSURANCE

10.1 During the term of this Agreement, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit covering all vehicles used in performing the Services; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by New York law and employer's liability insurance of at least $500,000; and (d) bailee's customer or equivalent property insurance covering Customer-Owned Goods in Provider's care, custody or control.

10.2 Provider shall name Client as an additional insured on the policies in Sections 10.1(a) and 10.1(b) and shall provide certificates of insurance to Client on request.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Client may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate, or to a purchaser or manager of the Serviced Properties, on written notice to Provider.
