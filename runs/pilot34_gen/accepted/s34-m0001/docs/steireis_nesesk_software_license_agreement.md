Licensor and Licensee are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops, hosts and makes available a cloud-based property management and guest-services software suite for the hospitality industry.

B. Licensee owns and operates boutique hotel properties and wishes to license that software suite, under the terms of this Agreement and one or more Order Forms.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

1.2 "**Authorized Users**" means employees, contractors and agents of Licensee or its Affiliates whom Licensee authorizes to access the Licensed Software on Licensee's behalf.

1.3 "**Covered Properties**" means any hotel, inn, resort or other lodging property owned, managed or franchised by Licensee or any of its Affiliates, at any location, including any property acquired, opened or brought under management or franchise after the Effective Date.

1.4 "**Documentation**" means Licensor's then-current user guides, technical specifications and online help materials for the Licensed Software.

1.5 "**Fees**" means the subscription fees, implementation fees and other amounts set out in an Order Form.

1.6 "**Guest Data**" means any information about any guest, prospective guest or other individual, including names, contact details, reservation history, payment card data, identification documents, preferences and communications, that is collected, entered, stored or processed by or through the Licensed Software.

1.7 "**Licensed Software**" means the software modules identified in an Order Form, together with the Documentation and all updates, upgrades and enhancements that Licensor makes generally available to its customers during the Subscription Term.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means an ordering document that references this Agreement and is signed by both Parties, including Order Form No. 1 dated as of the Effective Date.

1.9 "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

1.10 "**Subscription Start Date**" means the date so identified in Order Form No. 1.

1.11 "**Subscription Term**" means the Initial Term together with all Renewal Terms.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months from the Subscription Start Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

3.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, this Agreement and each Order Form then in effect shall automatically renew for an additional period of thirty-six (36) months (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Notice of non-renewal must be given in accordance with Section 15.6.

3.3 **Fees During Renewal Terms.** Licensor may increase the Fees for any Renewal Term by giving Licensee written notice at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the start of that Renewal Term. Any increase shall not exceed five percent (5%) over the Fees in effect during the immediately preceding twelve (12) months.

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION AND SUPPORT

4.1 **Implementation.** Licensor shall perform the implementation services described in the applicable Order Form, including configuration, data migration from Licensee's legacy systems and initial training of Authorized Users.

4.2 **Support.** Licensor shall provide technical support during the Subscription Term in accordance with its standard support policy, which shall include at a minimum twenty-four (24) hour, seven (7) day telephone and online support for Severity 1 incidents.

4.3 **Availability.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Licensed Software available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified to Licensee at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance. Service credits, if any, are set out in the applicable Order Form.

5.1 Licensee is responsible for the acts and omissions of its Authorized Users in using the Licensed Software and for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Licensee shall pay the Fees set out in each Order Form. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance and are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

6.3 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. 6.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Licensor in writing of the basis for the dispute before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

9.4 **Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Licensee Data in Licensor's possession or control (a "**Security Incident**"). 9.5 **Audits.** Once per contract year, Licensor shall provide Licensee with a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report, or an equivalent independent third-party audit report covering the Licensed Software.

9.6 **Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, Licensor shall make Licensee Data available for export in a standard machine-readable format.
