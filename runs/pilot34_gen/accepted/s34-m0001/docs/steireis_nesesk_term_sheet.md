# TERM SHEET

## Proposed Software Subscription: Nesesk Hospitality Systems, Inc. / Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC

**Date:** October 20, 2023

This term sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") summarizes the principal terms on which **Nesesk Hospitality Systems, Inc.** ("**Nesesk**") proposes to license its cloud property management and guest-services software suite to **Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC** ("**Steireis**"). Nesesk and Steireis are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." The Parties intend to document the transaction in a definitive Software License Agreement and an Order Form (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**").

## 1. PARTIES

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Nesesk Hospitality Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 1180 Wexford Commons, Suite 400, Hoboken, New Jersey 07030 |
| Licensee | Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC, a New York limited liability company, 62 Riverbend Road, Rhinebeck, New York 12572 |

## 2. LICENSED MODULES AND PROPERTIES

2.1 **Modules.** Steireis will subscribe to the following modules:

(a) **Nesesk PMS**: cloud property management covering reservations, front desk, housekeeping, folio and night audit;

(b) **Nesesk GuestConnect**: pre-arrival communications, mobile check-in, in-stay messaging and digital concierge; and

(c) **Nesesk Channel Bridge**: two-way interface between Nesesk PMS and Steireis's online travel agency and booking-engine channels.

2.2 **Properties.** The modules will be deployed at the following seven Hudson Valley properties:

| # | Property | Location | Rooms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Riverbend Inn | Rhinebeck, NY | 38 |
| 2 | Hollowbrook House | Hudson, NY | 24 |
| 3 | The Kingsgate Hotel | Kingston, NY | 52 |
| 4 | Millbrook Orchard Lodge | Millbrook, NY | 30 |
| 5 | The Saugerties Lighthouse Rooms | Saugerties, NY | 16 |
| 6 | Beacon Foundry Hotel | Beacon, NY | 44 |
| 7 | Catskill Creek Lodge | Catskill, NY | 28 |
| | **Total** | | **232** |

2.3 **Additional Properties.** Steireis may add properties on thirty (30) days' written notice at the per-room rates in Section 5, plus a per-property implementation fee.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Subscription Start Date.** Targeted for December 1, 2023.

3.2 **Initial Term.** 36 months from the Subscription Start Date.

3.3 **Renewal.** The subscription will renew automatically for successive 36-month Renewal Terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Scope.** Exclusivity will apply to the property management module only, at the seven Hudson Valley properties listed in the term sheet. During the Subscription Term, Steireis will use Nesesk PMS as the sole property management system at those seven properties.

4.2 **Other Modules and Properties.** Steireis remains free to license, use and evaluate third-party software for guest messaging, point-of-sale, revenue management and any other function. Steireis is also free to use any property management system at any property not listed in Section 2.2, including properties it acquires, opens or brings under management after the date of the Definitive Agreements.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Subscription Fees.**

| Module | Rate |
|---|---|
| Nesesk PMS | $34.00 per room per month |
| Nesesk GuestConnect | $11.50 per room per month |
| Nesesk Channel Bridge | $450.00 per property per month |

At 232 rooms and seven properties, the indicative total annual subscription fee is $164,472.00, invoiced annually in advance.

5.2 **Implementation.** One-time fee of $6,500.00 per property ($45,500.00 for the seven properties).

5.3 **Renewal Pricing.** Increases for any Renewal Term will be capped at 5% over the fees in effect during the preceding twelve (12) months, on at least 120 days' prior written notice.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS, SUPPORT AND DATA SECURITY

6.1 **Availability.** 99.5% monthly availability target for Nesesk PMS, with service credits of up to 20% of the monthly Nesesk PMS fee.

6.2 **Support.** 24/7 telephone and online support for Severity 1 incidents.

6.3 **Data Security.** Nesesk will maintain a written information security program consistent with industry standards, PCI DSS compliance, encryption in transit and at rest, 72-hour security incident notification, and an annual SOC 2 Type II report.

6.4 **Data Ownership.** Steireis owns all data entered into the platform, including guest data. Data will be exportable on expiration or termination.

## 7. LIABILITY AND LEGAL TERMS

7.1 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Nesesk's breach of its data security obligations will be excluded from the cap.

7.2 **Indemnities.** Nesesk will provide an intellectual property infringement indemnity. Any other indemnities will be agreed in the Definitive Agreements.

7.3 **Governing Law.** State of New York, with exclusive jurisdiction in the state and federal courts located in New York County.

## 8. NON-BINDING EFFECT

8.1 This Term Sheet is a non-binding expression of the Parties' current intentions. It does not create any legally binding obligation on either Party, and no Party is obligated to enter into the transaction described here. Binding obligations will arise only on execution of the Definitive Agreements by both Parties.

8.2 Despite Section 8.1, Section 9 (Confidentiality) and Section 7.3 (Governing Law), as applied to Section 9, are binding on the Parties when this Term Sheet is acknowledged below.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Parties will keep the existence and terms of this Term Sheet, and all information exchanged in negotiations, confidential. Each Party will disclose that information only to its employees and professional advisors who need to know it, unless the other Party consents in writing or applicable law requires disclosure.

## 10. TIMETABLE

The Parties will use good-faith efforts to execute the Definitive Agreements by November 15, 2023, so that implementation can begin on the targeted Subscription Start Date.
