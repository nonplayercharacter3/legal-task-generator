This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is between **Nesesk Hospitality Systems, Inc.** ("**Licensor**") and **Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC** ("**Licensee**"). It relates to the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Agreement**") and Order Form No. 1 under the Agreement dated as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Order Form**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. Under Order Form Section 2.1, the Subscription Start Date is December 1, 2023. Under Agreement Section 3.1, the Initial Term runs for 36 months from the Subscription Start Date and ends on November 30, 2026. Under Agreement Section 3.2, the Agreement and the Order Form automatically renew for a Renewal Term of thirty-six (36) months unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

B. Licensor received Licensee's letter dated September 18, 2026, in which Licensee stated that it does not intend to renew the Agreement at the end of the Initial Term. Licensor's position is that the letter was not given within the period required by Agreement Section 3.2, and that the Agreement and the Order Form will renew for a Renewal Term beginning December 1, 2026.

C. Licensee has told Licensor that it disputes that the Agreement has renewed.

D. Licensor values its relationship with Licensee and wishes to offer pricing concessions for the Renewal Term. Licensor and Licensee also wish to make certain changes to the Agreement, on the terms of this Side Letter.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. RENEWAL TERM AND PRICING

1.1 **Acknowledgment of Renewal.** Licensee acknowledges and agrees that the Agreement and the Order Form have renewed under Agreement Section 3.2 for a Renewal Term beginning December 1, 2026 and ending November 30, 2029 (the "**First Renewal Term**").

1.2 **Fee Discount.** During the First Renewal Term, Licensor will apply a discount of eight percent (8%) to the Subscription Fees set out in Order Form Section 3.1. Based on the current 232 rooms and seven properties, the Total Annual Subscription Fee for each contract year of the First Renewal Term will be $151,314.24, instead of $164,472.00.

1.3 **No Renewal Increase.** Licensor will not increase the Fees for the First Renewal Term under Agreement Section 3.3. Fees for any property added under Order Form Section 1.3 during the First Renewal Term will be calculated at the discounted per-room rates that follow from Section 1.2.

1.4 **Invoicing.** The discounted Subscription Fees will be invoiced annually in advance under Order Form Section 3.3. The first invoice will be issued on December 1, 2026.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.3(a)

2.1 Agreement Section 12.3(a) is deleted and replaced in its entirety with the following:

> "(a) Licensor's breach of its obligations under Section 9 (Data Security), to the extent resulting from Licensor's gross negligence or willful misconduct;"

2.2 Agreement Sections 12.3(b), 12.3(c) and 12.3(d) are not changed.
