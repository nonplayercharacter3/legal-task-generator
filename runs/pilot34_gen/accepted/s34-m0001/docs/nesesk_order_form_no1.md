# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Order Form No. 1** (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of November 15, 2023 by and between **Nesesk Hospitality Systems, Inc.** ("**Licensor**") and **Steireis Hospitality Group, LLC** ("**Licensee**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated as of November 15, 2023 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.1 **Modules.** Licensee subscribes to the following modules of the Licensed Software:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Nesesk PMS | Cloud property management: reservations, front desk, housekeeping, folio and night audit |
| Nesesk GuestConnect | Guest-services suite: pre-arrival communications, mobile check-in, in-stay messaging and digital concierge |
| Nesesk Channel Bridge | Two-way interface between Nesesk PMS and Licensee's online travel agency and booking-engine channels |

Each added property will be billed at the per-room rate in Section 3.1, prorated for the rest of the then-current contract year, plus the per-property implementation fee in Section 3.2.

## 2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

2.1 **Subscription Start Date.** The Subscription Start Date is December 1, 2023.

2.2 **Term.** The Subscription Term for this Order Form begins on the Subscription Start Date. It continues for the Initial Term and any Renewal Terms under Section 3 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Go-Live.** Licensor will use commercially reasonable efforts to complete implementation and bring each Initial Deployment Property live on Nesesk PMS by February 29, 2024. Subscription Fees accrue from the Subscription Start Date whatever the go-live date of any property.

## 3. FEES

3.1 **Subscription Fees.**

| Module | Rate | Annual Fee (232 rooms) |
|---|---|---|
| Nesesk PMS | $34.00 per room per month | $94,656.00 |
| Nesesk GuestConnect | $11.50 per room per month | $32,016.00 |
| Nesesk Channel Bridge | $450.00 per property per month | $37,800.00 |
| **Total Annual Subscription Fee** | | **$164,472.00** |

3.2 **Implementation Fees.** A one-time implementation fee of $6,500.00 per Initial Deployment Property, totaling $45,500.00. It is invoiced on the date of this Order Form and payable under Section 6.1 of the Agreement.

3.3 **Invoicing.** Subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance. The first invoice is issued on the Subscription Start Date, and later invoices are issued on each anniversary of the Subscription Start Date. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.4 **Fees During Renewal Terms.** Any adjustment to Fees for a Renewal Term is governed by Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

4.1 **Scope.** Under Section 4.1 of the Agreement, Licensor will provide the following implementation services for each Initial Deployment Property:

(a) system configuration of room types, rate plans, taxes, packages and user roles;

(b) migration of future reservations, guest profiles and open folios from Licensee's legacy property management system;

(c) configuration of Nesesk Channel Bridge connections to up to six (6) distribution channels per property;

(d) up to sixteen (16) hours of remote training for Authorized Users at each property, and one (1) day of on-site go-live support; and

(e) a post-go-live review within thirty (30) days after each property goes live.

4.2 **Licensee Cooperation.** Licensee will designate a project lead. Licensee will provide timely access to legacy system data exports and to staff at each property for training.

## 5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 **Exclusive Use.** During the Subscription Term, including all Renewal Terms, Licensee shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, license, subscribe to, use, implement, pilot or evaluate any competing hospitality software at all Covered Properties, whether owned, managed or franchised by Licensee or any of its Affiliates.

5.2 **Competing Software.** For purposes of Section 5.1, "competing hospitality software" means any software, platform or hosted service, whether offered by a third party or developed by or for Licensee, that provides functionality substantially similar to or competitive with any functionality offered by Licensor in the following categories: (a) property management; (b) point-of-sale for food, beverage, spa or retail outlets; (c) revenue management and rate optimization; and (d) guest messaging and guest communications.

5.3 **Newly Acquired Properties.** If a property becomes a Covered Property after the date of this Order Form and is then using competing hospitality software, Licensee shall transition that property to the Licensed Software within one hundred eighty (180) days. During that transition period the existing software may continue in use, but it may not be renewed or extended.

5.4 **Remedies.** Licensee's breach of this Section 5 is a material breach of the Agreement. Licensee acknowledges that such a breach would cause Licensor irreparable harm for which damages may be inadequate. Licensor may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS AND SERVICE CREDITS

6.1 **Service Credits.** If monthly availability of Nesesk PMS falls below the 99.5% target in Section 4.3 of the Agreement, Licensee will receive a credit against the next Subscription Fee invoice, as follows:

| Monthly Availability | Credit (% of monthly Subscription Fee for Nesesk PMS) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to less than 99.5% | 5% |
| 98.0% to less than 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 98.0% | 20% |

6.2 **Claiming Credits.** Licensee must request a service credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month. Service credits for any month will not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the monthly Subscription Fee for Nesesk PMS.

6.3 **Chronic Failure.** If monthly availability falls below 98.0% in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period, Licensee may treat that failure as a material breach for purposes of Section 14.1 of the Agreement.
