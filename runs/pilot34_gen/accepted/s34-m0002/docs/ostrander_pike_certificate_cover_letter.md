Ladies and Gentlemen:

## 1. Purpose of this Letter

We act as insurance broker to Nilur Medical Devices, LLC ("**Nilur**"). At Nilur's request, we enclose a certificate of property insurance (the "**Certificate**") evidencing the commercial property and inland marine coverage that Nilur currently maintains on its inventory of orthopedic knee and hip implant systems, components, trials and instrumentation. The coverage includes inventory in transit.

We understand that Nilur is providing the Certificate to Tipul Orthopedics, Inc. ("**Tipul**") in connection with the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Tipul, as buyer, and Nilur, as seller. We also understand that Section 6.6 of that agreement requires Nilur to maintain its existing property insurance on that inventory, including inventory in transit, until the Closing scheduled for April 15, 2026.

## 2. Coverage Evidenced

The Certificate evidences the following coverage:

(a) **Named Insured:** Nilur Medical Devices, LLC.

(b) **Coverage Forms:** Commercial property (stock and finished goods at owned, leased and consignment locations) and inland marine transit coverage. The transit coverage applies to shipments by common carrier, contract carrier and Nilur-owned vehicles within the United States and Canada, and includes goods in the custody of third-party logistics providers and sterilization vendors.

(c) **Policy Period:** January 1, 2026 to January 1, 2027.

(d) **Valuation:** Selling price for finished goods. Replacement cost for raw materials and work in process.

(e) **Certificate Holder and Loss Payee:** Tipul Orthopedics, Inc., as its interest may appear.

The policy limits, sublimits and deductibles are stated on the Certificate.

## 3. Notice of Cancellation

The insurer has endorsed the policy to provide Tipul, as certificate holder, with 30 days' prior written notice of cancellation or non-renewal. For non-payment of premium, the notice period is 10 days.

## 4. Nature of the Certificate

The Certificate is issued as a matter of information only and confers no rights on the certificate holder other than those provided by the loss payee designation and the notice of cancellation endorsement described in Sections 2(e) and 3. The Certificate does not amend, extend or alter the coverage afforded by the policies listed on it. The terms, exclusions and conditions of those policies govern in all respects. Copies of the policy forms and endorsements are available on request, subject to Nilur's consent.

## 5. Post-Closing Coverage

This letter and the Certificate address Nilur's coverage only. We have not been engaged to place, and the Certificate does not evidence, any coverage for Tipul. Tipul should arrange its own property and transit coverage for the acquired inventory, effective as of the Closing.
