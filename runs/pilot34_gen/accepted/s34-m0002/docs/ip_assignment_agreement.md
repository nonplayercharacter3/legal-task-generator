# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into and effective as of April 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Nilur Medical Devices, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Tipul Orthopedics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as seller, and Assignee, as buyer, are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Assignee, among other Purchased Assets, the Transferred IP and the Design History Files relating to the Product Line.

B. Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to deliver this Assignment at the Closing. Section 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignee to deliver this Assignment at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in the Purchase Agreement and this Assignment, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Closing," "Closing Date," "Design History Files," "Product Line," "Products," "Purchased Assets" and "Transferred IP."

**1.2 Additional Definitions.** In this Assignment:

"**Assigned Patents**" means (a) the patents and patent applications listed on Schedule A; (b) all patents issuing from any of them; (c) all provisionals, continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, reexaminations, extensions, supplementary protection certificates and foreign counterparts of any of the foregoing; and (d) all inventions and improvements disclosed or claimed in any of the foregoing.

"**Assigned Marks**" means (a) the trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress listed on Schedule B, together with all registrations and applications for registration of them; (b) all common-law rights in them; and (c) the goodwill of the Product Line connected with the use of and symbolized by any of them.

"**Assigned Copyrights and Know-How**" means all copyrights (registered or unregistered), software, technical documentation, labeling, instructions for use, surgical technique guides, manufacturing specifications, processes, know-how and trade secrets included in the Transferred IP, as listed or described on Schedule 2.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Assigned IP**" means, collectively, the Assigned Patents, the Assigned Marks, the Assigned Copyrights and Know-How, and all other Transferred IP.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment of Intellectual Property.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest throughout the world in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens listed on Schedule 2.1 of the Purchase Agreement, including:

(a) all rights of priority and protection of interests in the Assigned IP under the laws of any jurisdiction or any international treaty or convention;

(b) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable with respect to any of the Assigned IP; and

(c) all claims and causes of action for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation, dilution or other violation of any of the Assigned IP, with the right to sue for and collect damages and obtain all other remedies for them, and to retain all proceeds.

**2.2 Assignment of Design History Files.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Design History Files, including all copyrights, trade secrets and other intellectual property rights in them.

**2.3 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Assigned Marks under Section 2.1 includes the goodwill of the Product Line connected with the use of and symbolized by the Assigned Marks. Assignor acknowledges that, from and after the Effective Date, it has no right to use the Assigned Marks or any confusingly similar mark, except as Assignee may permit in writing or as provided in the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. Delivery

**3.1 Delivery of Files.** On the Closing Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, in the format in which Assignor maintains them (and, for electronic records, in native format with available metadata and audit trails), complete copies of:

(a) the Design History Files for each Product;

(b) the prosecution files, docketing records and correspondence with patent and trademark offices for each Assigned Patent and Assigned Mark; and

(c) all tangible embodiments of the Assigned Copyrights and Know-How in Assignor's possession or control.

**3.2 Retained Copies.** Assignor may retain one archival copy of the Design History Files and complaint files solely to the extent required by Law for Assignor's regulatory record-retention obligations for Products sold before the Closing Date. Assignor shall hold any retained copy in confidence in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement and shall not use it for any other purpose.

## 4. Recordation; Further Assurances

**4.1 Recordation.** Assignor authorizes the Commissioner for Patents and the Commissioner for Trademarks of the United States Patent and Trademark Office, and the corresponding officials of any other jurisdiction, to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned Patents and the Assigned Marks. Assignee may record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in substantially the form of Exhibit 1, with any such office. Assignor shall execute any short-form assignment Assignee reasonably requests for that purpose.

**4.2 Further Assurances.** Consistent with Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement, Assignor shall, at Assignee's request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such additional instruments and take such further actions as Assignee reasonably requires to vest in Assignee, perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend its rights in the Assigned IP and the Design History Files. Assignor shall make available its current employees who are named inventors on any Assigned Patent to execute declarations, assignments and other documents reasonably required in connection with the prosecution or enforcement of the Assigned Patents. Assignee shall reimburse Assignor's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred at Assignee's request under this Section 4.2 after the Closing Date.

**4.3 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute, deliver and file in Assignor's name any instrument described in Section 4.1 or 4.2 that Assignor fails to execute within ten Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**4.4 Prosecution and Maintenance.** From and after the Effective Date, Assignee is responsible, at its expense and in its discretion, for the prosecution and maintenance of the Assigned Patents and the Assigned Marks. Assignor shall forward to Assignee, within five Business Days after receipt, any office action, notice, correspondence or payment relating to any Assigned IP that Assignor receives after the Effective Date.

## 5. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

**5.1 Delivery Under Purchase Agreement.** This Assignment is delivered under and in accordance with the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of the Parties regarding the Assigned IP and the Design History Files are set forth in the Purchase Agreement, including Sections 4.4 and 4.6, and are neither enlarged nor diminished by this Assignment.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Notices.** All notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Assignee may assign this Assignment and any of its rights under it, in whole or in part, together with the related Assigned IP, without the consent of Assignor.

**6.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party granting it.

**6.4 Severability.** If any term of this Assignment is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, the invalidity does not affect any other term or render that term invalid in any other jurisdiction.

**6.5 Governing Law.** This Assignment, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction, except to the extent the Laws of a jurisdiction in which any Assigned IP is registered mandatorily govern the formalities of its transfer.

**6.6 Exclusive Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Assignment, and agrees that all such actions shall be brought exclusively in that court. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**6.7 Counterparts.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are deemed original signatures.
