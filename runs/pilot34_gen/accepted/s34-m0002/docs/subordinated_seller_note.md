# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$9,000,000**
**Dated: April 15, 2026**

THIS NOTE AND THE INDEBTEDNESS IT EVIDENCES ARE SUBORDINATED IN RIGHT OF PAYMENT TO THE SENIOR INDEBTEDNESS (AS DEFINED BELOW) TO THE EXTENT AND IN THE MANNER SET FORTH IN SECTION 7.

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Tipul Orthopedics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Nilur Medical Devices, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal amount of **Nine Million Dollars ($9,000,000)**, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms set forth in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.5(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026, between Maker, as buyer, and Holder, as seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In this Note:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus 2.0% per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1.

"**Interest Rate**" means 6.0% per annum.

"**Losses**" means amounts that have been finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction, in a judgment that is no longer subject to appeal, to be owed by Holder to Maker under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Maturity Date**" means April 15, 2029.

"**Payment Date**" means the 15th day of each January, April, July and October, beginning on July 15, 2026. If a Payment Date is not a Business Day, the payment is due on the next Business Day.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, unincorporated organization, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Product Line**" means the orthopedic knee and hip implant product line acquired by Maker under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" means all principal, interest (including interest accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses and other amounts owing by Maker under any credit agreement, loan agreement or other instrument for borrowed money with a bank, institutional lender or other financial institution, whether existing on the date of this Note or incurred later, and any refinancing, replacement or extension of it.

"**Senior Lender**" means each holder of Senior Indebtedness and any agent acting on its behalf.

## 2. Payment of Principal; Maturity

The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other amounts owing under this Note, is due and payable in full on April 15, 2029. Beginning on April 15, 2027, Maker shall pay principal in eight equal quarterly installments of $1,125,000 each on each Payment Date, with the final installment due on the Maturity Date.

## 3. Interest

**3.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the date of this Note at the Interest Rate, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**3.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable in arrears on each Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Default Interest.** While any Event of Default is continuing, the unpaid principal balance and, to the extent permitted by law, all overdue interest bear interest at the Default Rate.

**3.4 Usury Savings.** In no event shall interest under this Note exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 4. Payments; Set-Off

**4.1 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing from time to time. Each payment shall be applied first to fees and expenses then due under this Note, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to principal.

**4.2 Set-Off.** Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest due under this Note only Losses. Maker shall give Holder written notice of any set-off not later than the Payment Date on which it is applied, identifying the judgment and the amount of Losses set off. Any amount set off in accordance with this Section 4.2 shall be treated as a payment of the corresponding amount of principal or interest on the applicable Payment Date. Except as provided in this Section 4.2, all payments under this Note shall be made without set-off, counterclaim or deduction of any kind.

## 5. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by all accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid and shall be applied to installments of principal in inverse order of maturity.

## 6. Covenants of Maker

**6.1 Existence.** So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker shall maintain its corporate existence and good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**6.2 Information and Inspection Rights.** So long as any amount remains outstanding under this Note, Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within 30 days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited balance sheet and statements of income and cash flows of Maker for that month and for the fiscal year to date, certified by Maker's chief financial officer;

(b) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Maker for that fiscal year;

(c) within 30 days after the end of each fiscal quarter, a report for the Product Line setting forth unit sales and net revenue by Product and by region, average selling prices, and a list of the hospital, ambulatory surgery center and distributor customers of the Product Line with the revenue attributable to each; and

(d) promptly upon request, such other information regarding the financial condition, operations and business of Maker and the Product Line as Holder may reasonably request.

In addition, Maker shall permit Holder and its representatives, upon five Business Days' prior notice and during normal business hours, to inspect the books, records and properties of Maker, to make copies and extracts of them, and to discuss the affairs, finances and accounts of Maker and the Product Line with Maker's officers and independent accountants.

**6.3 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of the occurrence of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default, within five Business Days after an executive officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

## 7. Subordination

**7.1 Subordination.** The payment of principal, interest and all other amounts under this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness.

**7.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or acceleration under any Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing and the Senior Lender has given Maker and Holder written notice of it, Maker may make, and Holder may receive, regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

**7.3 Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or other insolvency proceeding of Maker, all Senior Indebtedness shall first be paid in full in cash before any payment is made on this Note.

**7.4 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment on this Note in violation of this Section 7, Holder shall hold it in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly pay it over to the Senior Lender.

**7.5 Standstill.** While any Senior Indebtedness is outstanding, Holder shall not accelerate this Note or commence any enforcement action against Maker until the earlier of (a) 120 days after Holder gives the Senior Lender written notice of an Event of Default, and (b) the acceleration of the Senior Indebtedness.

**7.6 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, upon Maker's request, enter into a subordination agreement with any Senior Lender on terms consistent with this Section 7.

## 8. Events of Default; Remedies

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within five Business Days after it is due;

(b) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 6 and the failure continues for 30 days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law;

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within 60 days; or

(e) Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the Product Line to any Person other than an Affiliate of Maker, unless the transferee assumes Maker's obligations under this Note in writing.

**8.2 Remedies.** Subject to Section 7, upon the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 8.1(c) or 8.1(d), the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, all accrued interest and all other amounts owing under this Note become immediately due and payable without notice or action by Holder. Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, all accrued interest and all other amounts owing under this Note immediately due and payable. Holder may exercise any other right or remedy available to it at law or in equity.

**8.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in collecting any amount due under this Note after an Event of Default.

## 9. Transfer

Holder may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note and all of its rights under this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker. Upon written notice to Maker of a transfer, identifying the transferee and its payment instructions, the transferee shall be deemed the Holder for all purposes of this Note. Maker may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, except to a transferee permitted under Section 8.1(e).

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of Minnesota sitting in Hennepin County and the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota for any action arising out of or relating to this Note, and agree that all such actions shall be brought exclusively in those courts. Each of Maker and Holder waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 11. Miscellaneous

**11.1 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor, nonpayment and protest of this Note.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Note must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth for Maker and Holder in the Purchase Agreement (or such other address as a party designates by notice).

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the party granting it. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right impairs that right or operates as a waiver of it.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits Holder and its successors and assigns.

**11.6 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall, upon receipt of an affidavit of loss and customary indemnity from Holder (or, if mutilated, surrender of this Note), issue a replacement note of like tenor.

**11.7 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are deemed original signatures.
