# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and between **Tipul Orthopedics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Nilur Medical Devices, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller designs, manufactures, markets and sells orthopedic knee and hip implant systems and related instrumentation (the "**Product Line**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, the Purchased Assets, and Buyer wishes to assume the Assumed Liabilities, in each case on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the IP Assignment Agreement, the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement and each other agreement, instrument or certificate delivered under this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Design History Files**" means all design history files, device master records, device history records, technical files, verification and validation records, risk management files and complaint files maintained for the Products.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.4 (Title to Purchased Assets), Section 4.10 (Taxes) and Section 4.12 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, court or regulatory body thereof, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (the "**FDA**").

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement between Seller, as assignor, and Buyer, as assignee, effective as of the Closing Date, substantially in the form of Exhibit B.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Lien**" means any mortgage, pledge, lien, charge, security interest, claim or other encumbrance.

"**Losses**" means any and all losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses of whatever kind, including reasonable attorneys' fees, experts' and consultants' fees, and the cost of enforcing any right to indemnification under this Agreement and of pursuing any insurance providers.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that, individually or in the aggregate, is or would reasonably be expected to be materially adverse to the Product Line, the Purchased Assets or the results of operations or condition (financial or otherwise) of the Product Line, taken as a whole, other than effects arising from general economic conditions or conditions generally affecting the orthopedic device industry that do not disproportionately affect the Product Line.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, unincorporated organization, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Product Liabilities**" means all Liabilities arising out of or relating to any Product designed, manufactured, sold, distributed or implanted before the Closing Date, including Liabilities for death, personal injury, revision surgery, product defect, breach of warranty, failure to warn, recall, field correction or market withdrawal, regardless of when the claim is asserted.

"**Products**" means the knee and hip implant systems, components, trials and instrumentation listed on Schedule 1.1(a), together with all prior versions and generations of them.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $9,000,000 issued by Buyer, as maker, to Seller, as holder, at the Closing, substantially in the form of Exhibit A.

"**Liabilities**" means any and all debts, liabilities and obligations, whether accrued or fixed, absolute or contingent, matured or unmatured, known or unknown.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens listed on Schedule 2.1, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the following assets used or held for use primarily in the Product Line (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all inventory of Products, including raw materials, work in process, finished goods, consigned inventory and inventory in transit;

(b) all patents, patent applications, trademarks, trade names, copyrights, software, know-how and trade secrets listed on Schedule 2.1(b) (the "**Transferred IP**");

(c) the Design History Files;

(d) all 510(k) clearances, CE certificates and other regulatory approvals and submissions for the Products listed on Schedule 2.1(d), to the extent transferable;

(e) the contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(e) (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(f) the tooling, molds, dies and manufacturing equipment listed on Schedule 2.1(f); and

(g) all customer lists, distributor lists, surgeon training materials, marketing materials and books and records relating primarily to the Product Line.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include cash and cash equivalents, accounts receivable arising before the Closing Date, Seller's corporate records, Seller's insurance policies and the rights under them (other than as provided in Section 6.6), and the other assets listed on Schedule 2.2 (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing Date, other than Liabilities arising from any breach or default by Seller occurring on or before the Closing Date; and

(b) Liabilities arising out of Products manufactured and sold by Buyer after the Closing Date.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including:

(a) all Liabilities arising out of the Excluded Assets;

(b) all Liabilities for Taxes of Seller, and all Taxes relating to the Product Line or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(c) all Pre-Closing Product Liabilities;

(d) all Liabilities to current or former employees, contractors or consultants of Seller, including under any benefit plan;

(e) all Liabilities arising from any violation of Law by Seller, including any FDA warning letter, Form 483 observation or enforcement action relating to periods before the Closing Date; and

(f) all indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller.

**2.5 Purchase Price.**

(a) The aggregate consideration for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

(b) The Purchase Price shall be paid at the Closing as follows: (i) $39,500,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date; and (ii) $9,000,000 by Buyer's issuance and delivery to Seller of the Seller Note.

(c) The Seller Note shall be subordinated to Buyer's senior indebtedness on the terms set forth in the Seller Note and shall mature on April 15, 2029.

**2.6 Allocation.** Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a schedule allocating the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. Seller may comment within 30 days, and the Parties shall resolve any disagreement in good faith. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with the final allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on April 15, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"), subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII. The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**"); (b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (c) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (d) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (e) FDA transfer-of-ownership letters for each 510(k) listed on Schedule 2.1(d); (f) payoff letters and Lien releases for all Liens on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Liens; (g) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.1(a) and 7.1(b); and (h) an IRS Form W-9.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.5(b)(i); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and (d) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that, except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), the statements in this Article IV are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full limited liability company power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes, and each such Ancillary Agreement when executed will constitute, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Seller, and, except as listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, do not require the consent of any Person under any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens.

**4.5 Sufficiency of Assets.** The Purchased Assets, together with the services provided under the Transition Services Agreement, constitute all of the assets necessary to conduct the Product Line as conducted by Seller during the 12 months before the date of this Agreement.

**4.6 Intellectual Property.** Schedule 2.1(b) lists all registered and applied-for Transferred IP. Seller exclusively owns the Transferred IP. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Product Line does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property rights of any Person, and no Person is infringing the Transferred IP.

**4.7 Regulatory Compliance.** Each Product has been designed, manufactured, tested, labeled, stored, distributed and marketed in material compliance with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the FDA's Quality System Regulation and Quality Management System Regulation. Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists every recall, field correction, market withdrawal, safety alert, Medical Device Report and FDA Form 483 observation relating to any Product during the five years before the date of this Agreement.

**4.8 Product Liability.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists every claim, action or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller alleging injury arising from any Product during the five years before the date of this Agreement. To Seller's knowledge, there is no design or manufacturing defect in any Product.

**4.9 Inventory.** The inventory included in the Purchased Assets consists of a quality and quantity usable and saleable in the ordinary course, is not adulterated or misbranded, and has remaining shelf life of not less than 24 months as of the Closing Date for sterile-packaged finished goods.

**4.10 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed with respect to the Purchased Assets and the Product Line and has paid all Taxes shown as due on them. There are no Liens for Taxes on the Purchased Assets other than for current Taxes not yet due.

**4.11 Material Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any brokerage, finder's or other fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, and this Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient cash to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Product Line in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good operating condition, maintain its quality system and regulatory approvals, and not sell, license or encumber any Purchased Asset other than sales of inventory in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access to Information.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, Design History Files and personnel relating to the Product Line.

**6.3 Regulatory Transfer.** Seller shall cooperate with Buyer to transfer the 510(k) clearances and other regulatory approvals to Buyer, including by filing transfer-of-ownership letters with the FDA and updating device registration and listing information on the Closing Date.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Product Line and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent required by Law.

**6.5 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly design, manufacture, market or sell any knee or hip implant product anywhere in the world.

**6.6 Risk of Loss; Insurance.** Until the Closing, all risk of loss of or damage to the Purchased Assets, including inventory in transit, remains with Seller. Seller shall maintain in effect until the Closing its existing property insurance covering the inventory of Products, including inventory in transit. If any Purchased Asset is lost or damaged before the Closing, Seller shall, at Buyer's election, repair or replace it before the Closing or assign to Buyer at the Closing all insurance proceeds payable for the loss.

**6.7 Seller Product Liability Insurance.** Seller shall maintain, or purchase extended reporting period coverage under, product liability insurance covering the Products sold before the Closing Date for not less than six years after the Closing Date, with limits not less than those in effect on the date of this Agreement, and shall cause Buyer to be named as an additional insured.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV are true and correct in all material respects (or, if qualified by materiality, in all respects) as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred; (d) the consents listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule have been obtained; and (e) Seller has delivered each item in Section 3.2.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and (c) Buyer has delivered each item in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties of the Parties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, survive the Closing until 12 months after the Closing Date.

(b) The Fundamental Representations survive the Closing until 6 years after the Closing Date.

(c) The covenants and agreements of the Parties to be performed after the Closing survive the Closing in accordance with their terms.

(d) No claim may be asserted for breach of a representation or warranty after the expiration of its survival period, but any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before that expiration survives until finally resolved.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred or sustained by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered by Seller under this Agreement;

(b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

Any claim for indemnification under Section 8.2(c) shall be asserted by written notice to Seller within the survival period set forth in Section 8.1(a).

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement; (b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**8.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller is not liable under Section 8.2(a) (other than for the Fundamental Representations) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $485,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller is liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(b) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) (other than for the Fundamental Representations) shall not exceed $4,850,000. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) for the Fundamental Representations and under Sections 8.2(b) and 8.2(c) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The limitations in this Section 8.4 do not apply to Losses arising from fraud.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article VIII (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier.

**8.5 Claims Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying Party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. For any claim by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within 20 days after receipt of the claim notice, but the Indemnifying Party may not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Buyer may control the defense of any third-party claim relating to a Product that seeks injunctive relief or that involves a Governmental Authority.

**8.6 Payments.** The Indemnifying Party shall pay any amount owed under this Article VIII within 10 Business Days after the amount is agreed or finally determined, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**8.7 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off against any amount of principal or interest otherwise due under the Seller Note the amount of any Losses that Buyer claims in good faith under this Article VIII, by written notice to Seller describing the claim in reasonable detail. If the claim is later finally resolved for less than the amount set off, Buyer shall pay the difference to Seller within 10 Business Days after that resolution, together with interest at the rate stated in the Seller Note. The exercise of this right in good faith does not constitute a breach of this Agreement or a default under the Seller Note.

**8.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims arising from fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under Section 2.6, the indemnification provisions of this Article VIII are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement after the Closing.

**8.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article VIII shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred on or before June 30, 2026, unless the failure results from the terminating Party's breach; (c) by Buyer if Seller has materially breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.1 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has materially breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 9.1, it becomes void and of no further effect without liability of any Party, except that this Section 9.2 and Article XI survive, and no termination relieves any Party of liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE X. EMPLOYEES

**10.1 Offers of Employment.** Buyer may, in its discretion, offer employment effective on the Closing Date to the employees of Seller listed on Schedule 10.1. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer's offers and shall not discourage any listed employee from accepting.

**10.2 Seller Obligations.** Seller remains responsible for all wages, bonuses, commissions, accrued paid time off, severance and benefits earned by or owed to its employees through the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a Party designates by notice).

**11.2 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party bears its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**11.3 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, the invalidity does not affect any other term or render that term invalid in any other jurisdiction.

**11.4 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement controls over the Ancillary Agreements, except that the Seller Note controls on matters concerning the indebtedness it evidences.

**11.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the Party granting it.

**11.6 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or to any lender as collateral, or to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Product Line. No assignment relieves the assigning Party of its obligations.

**11.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and the Persons entitled to indemnification under Section 8.3, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.9 Exclusive Forum; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, only if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it, and agrees that all such actions shall be brought exclusively in that court. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are deemed original signatures.
