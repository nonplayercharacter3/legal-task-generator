**Re: Side Letter to Supply Agreement dated March 1, 2024: Integration-Period Volume Relief**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered by Reigein Diagnostics Corp. ("**Supplier**") to Pounirt Veterinary Laboratories, Inc. ("**Customer**"). It concerns the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer made effective as of March 1, 2024, including the Order Form and the Data Processing Addendum (together with its Exhibits, the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## RECITALS

A. Supplier supplies Customer with the Products for use at the Customer Sites under the Agreement. Customer is subject to the Minimum Purchase Commitment set out in the Order Form.

B. Customer has informed Supplier of the following matters. Customer's parent, Pounirt Holdings, Inc., entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Kestrelbay Animal Health Partners, L.P. on August 14, 2026. In connection with that transaction, Customer expects to undertake operational integration activities. Those activities may temporarily reduce testing volumes at one or more Customer Sites, including workflow consolidation, information-systems migration and staff transitions.

C. Customer has asked Supplier to provide temporary relief from the Minimum Purchase Commitment during the integration period. Supplier is willing to do so on the terms of this Side Letter.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Integration-Period Volume Relief

1.1 **Integration Period.** In this Side Letter, "**Integration Period**" means the period beginning on October 1, 2026 and ending on February 28, 2027, the last day of the current contract year.

1.2 **Reduced Commitment.** The Minimum Purchase Commitment for the contract year ending February 28, 2027 is reduced by fifteen percent (15%). The reduction is prorated to the portion of that contract year falling within the Integration Period.

1.3 **Shortfall Calculation.** Any shortfall invoiced under Section 2.3 of the Supply Agreement for the contract year ending February 28, 2027 will be calculated against the Minimum Purchase Commitment as reduced under Section 1.2.

1.4 **Crediting of Purchases.** Purchases of Products during the Integration Period continue to be credited against the Minimum Purchase Commitment in the ordinary course. Purchases of Products for any Customer Site will be credited without regard to how ordering volumes are allocated among Customer Sites.

1.5 **Forecasts.** During the Integration Period, Customer will continue to provide the monthly forecasts required by Section 4.2 of the Supply Agreement. Customer will use commercially reasonable efforts to notify Supplier promptly of any expected change of more than twenty percent (20%) in forecast volume at any Customer Site.

1.6 **Pricing Unaffected.** The relief in this Section 1 does not change the prices set out in Exhibit B or the Order Form price schedule.

## 2. Non-Renewal Notice

Customer delivered a written notice of non-renewal of the Agreement to Supplier on September 21, 2026 (the "**Non-Renewal Notice**"). Supplier has informed Customer that it considers the Non-Renewal Notice untimely under Section 1 of the Order Form. Customer has not accepted Supplier's position. The parties reserve all rights with respect to the Non-Renewal Notice, its effectiveness and the term of the Agreement. Neither this Side Letter nor either party's performance under it is an admission by either party on these matters.

## 3. Effect of Side Letter

3.1 **Agreement Continues.** Except as expressly set out in Section 1, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. Each party reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement.

3.3 **Amendment.** This Side Letter is a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties for purposes of Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement. It may be amended only in the same manner.

3.4 **Confidentiality.** This Side Letter and its terms are Confidential Information of each party under Section 10 of the Supply Agreement.
