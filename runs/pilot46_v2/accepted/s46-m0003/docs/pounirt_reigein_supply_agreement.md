# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is entered into and made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Reigein Diagnostics Corp.**, a Minnesota corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Pounirt Veterinary Laboratories, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures and distributes veterinary diagnostic reagent cartridges, analyzer consumables and related products for use in hematology and clinical chemistry testing.

B. Customer operates veterinary reference laboratories that perform diagnostic testing for veterinary practices, and wishes to purchase reagent cartridges and analyzer consumables from Supplier for use at those laboratories.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will supply, and Customer will purchase, such products.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the meanings given below or elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Agreement**" means this Supply Agreement, including its Exhibits, together with the Order Form and the Data Processing Addendum, each as amended from time to time in accordance with its terms.

1.2 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or other ownership interests of a Person, or the power to direct the management and policies of a Person, whether by contract or otherwise.

1.3 "**Analyzer**" means any hematology or chemistry analyzer manufactured or distributed by Supplier and installed at a Customer Site, as identified in the Order Form.

1.4 "**Change of Control**" means, with respect to a Person, any of the following: (a) the acquisition by any Person or group of Persons acting in concert, directly or indirectly, of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of that Person; (b) any merger, consolidation, reorganization or similar transaction involving that Person, after which the holders of that Person's voting securities immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of the surviving or resulting entity; or (c) the sale, lease or other transfer of all or substantially all of the assets of that Person.

1.5 "**Customer Sites**" means the four Customer laboratory facilities listed in Exhibit C.

1.6 "**Customer Data**" has the meaning given in the Data Processing Addendum.

1.7 "**Data Processing Addendum**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties governing Supplier's processing of Customer Data in connection with the Analyzers, Supplier's remote service and connectivity tools, and the Products.

1.8 "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

1.9 "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**" means the minimum annual purchase volume of Products set out in the Order Form.

1.10 "**Minimum Purchase Shortfall**" means, as of any date of determination, the aggregate amount by which the Minimum Purchase Commitment for each remaining contract year of the then-current term (including the contract year in progress) exceeds Customer's purchases of Products credited against such commitment for that contract year, calculated at the prices then in effect under Exhibit B.

1.11 "**Order Form**" means the Order Form for Reagents and Consumables executed by the Parties in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

1.12 "**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

1.13 "**Products**" means the hematology and chemistry reagent cartridges, controls, calibrators and analyzer consumables listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.14 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Customer under this Agreement.

1.15 "**Specifications**" means Supplier's published technical specifications for each Product in effect on the date of shipment, as summarized in Exhibit A.

## 2. SCOPE AND TERM

2.1 **Scope.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier shall sell and deliver to Customer, and Customer shall purchase from Supplier, the Products ordered by Customer under Purchase Orders issued in accordance with Section 4.

2.2 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form, and thereafter for any renewal term in accordance with the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.3 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** Customer shall purchase Products in at least the Minimum Purchase Commitment during each contract year of the term. Any shortfall at the end of a contract year shall be invoiced and paid as provided in the Order Form.

## 3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit B. Prices are firm for the first contract year. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust prices once per contract year on not less than sixty (60) days' prior written notice, provided that no annual increase shall exceed the lesser of three percent (3%) and the percentage change in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve (12) months.

3.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of each order. Each invoice shall reference the applicable Purchase Order number and Customer Site.

3.3 **Payment.** Undisputed amounts are due within thirty (30) days after Customer's receipt of a correct invoice. Late payments of undisputed amounts bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

3.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

3.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 4. ORDERING AND FORECASTS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders specifying the Products, quantities, Customer Site and requested delivery date. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within two (2) business days after receipt; a Purchase Order not rejected in that period is deemed accepted, provided the requested delivery date is consistent with the lead times in Exhibit A.

4.2 **Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar month, Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling six-month forecast of its Product requirements by Customer Site.

4.3 **Terms of Purchase Orders.** Purchase Orders are governed solely by this Agreement. Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect.

## 5. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

5.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the Customer Site stated in each Purchase Order, using temperature-controlled shipping where required by the Specifications.

5.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery to the applicable Customer Site.

5.3 **Shelf Life.** Each Product shall have, on delivery, not less than the remaining shelf life stated for that Product in Exhibit A.

5.4 **Late Delivery.** If Supplier anticipates a delay in delivery, it shall notify Customer promptly. If any delivery is more than five (5) business days late, Customer may cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability, and quantities so cancelled shall be credited against the Minimum Purchase Commitment.

## 6. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 Customer shall inspect Products within ten (10) business days after delivery and notify Supplier of any shortage, visible damage, temperature excursion or nonconformity. Supplier shall, at its option and expense, replace nonconforming Products or credit Customer for them.

6.2 Acceptance does not limit Supplier's warranty obligations under Section 8.

## 7. ANALYZERS, SERVICE AND SUPPORT

7.1 **Analyzer Placement.** Supplier shall place and maintain at the Customer Sites the Analyzers identified in the Order Form, on the terms stated in the Order Form.

7.2 **Service.** Supplier shall provide preventive maintenance, remote diagnostics and on-site service for the Analyzers in accordance with the service levels in Exhibit A.

7.3 **Connectivity.** To the extent Supplier's remote service or connectivity tools process Customer Data, that processing is governed by the Data Processing Addendum.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product will, through the earlier of its labeled expiration date and twelve (12) months after delivery, (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and (c) be manufactured, labeled and stored in compliance with applicable laws and Supplier's quality system.

8.2 **Title.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products, free of liens and encumbrances.

8.3 **Remedy.** For any breach of Section 8.1, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, replace the nonconforming Product or refund or credit the price paid for it, and shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of repeat testing directly caused by the nonconformity.

8.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. RECALLS AND REGULATORY MATTERS

9.1 Supplier shall notify Customer within two (2) business days after Supplier initiates or becomes aware of any recall, field correction or safety notice affecting the Products or Analyzers.

9.2 Supplier shall bear the reasonable costs of any recall attributable to a breach of Section 8.1, including replacement Products and reasonable shipping and disposal costs.

9.3 Each Party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including pricing and the terms of this Agreement.

10.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform or exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 10.

10.3 **Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful.

10.4 **Duration.** These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination or expiration of this Agreement.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from (a) bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by a defect in any Product or Analyzer; (b) any allegation that a Product or Analyzer infringes or misappropriates a third party's intellectual property rights; or (c) Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from (a) Customer's use of Products other than in accordance with the Specifications and Supplier's written instructions; or (b) Customer's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim (provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is prejudiced), allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own expense.

11.4 **Infringement Remedies.** If a Product becomes, or in Supplier's opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, Supplier may at its expense procure the right for Customer to continue using it, or modify or replace it with a functionally equivalent non-infringing Product.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.2 **Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits or revenues, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

12.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations set forth in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to a Party's indemnification obligations under this Agreement, to a Party's breach of Section 10, to a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, or to Customer's obligation to pay amounts due for Products under this Agreement.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Convenience.** Neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term.

13.2 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days. Supplier may also terminate this Agreement as provided in Section 14.2.

13.3 **Effect.** On termination or expiration, Customer shall pay for all Products delivered and all accepted Purchase Orders outstanding, and Supplier shall fulfil accepted Purchase Orders unless the Agreement was terminated by Supplier under Section 13.2. Supplier shall remove the Analyzers at its expense within forty-five (45) days, except where termination results from Customer's uncured breach, in which case removal shall be at Customer's expense.

13.4 **Survival.** Sections 3 (as to amounts accrued), 8, 10, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 14.2 (as to payment obligations), 15 and 16, and any other provisions that by their nature should survive, survive termination or expiration.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **General.** Neither Party may assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void. Subject to this Section 14, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

14.2 **Change of Control.** Any Change of Control of Customer or of its ultimate parent shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer requiring Supplier's prior written consent under Section 14.1. A Change of Control of Customer or of its ultimate parent effected without Supplier's prior written consent shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement, not subject to cure under Section 13.2(a), and Supplier may, by written notice given within ninety (90) days after Supplier becomes aware of the Change of Control, (a) terminate this Agreement with immediate effect, and (b) require Customer to pay the Minimum Purchase Shortfall, determined as of the date of termination, within thirty (30) days after the date of Supplier's notice.

14.3 **Subcontracting.** Supplier may use subcontractors for delivery and field service, provided that Supplier remains responsible for their performance.

15.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

15.3 **Escalation.** Before commencing any action (other than for injunctive relief), a Party shall give written notice of the dispute to the other Party, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within fifteen (15) business days after that notice to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

15.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
