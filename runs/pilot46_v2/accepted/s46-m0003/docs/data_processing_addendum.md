# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into and made effective as of March 1, 2024 by and between **Reigein Diagnostics Corp.**, a Minnesota corporation ("**Processor**"), and **Pounirt Veterinary Laboratories, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). It is the Data Processing Addendum referred to in the Supply Agreement between Processor, as Supplier, and Customer made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). This DPA forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement. References to "Supplier" in the Supply Agreement and the Order Form are references to Processor.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Applicable Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the processing of Customer Data under the Agreement. These include state consumer privacy laws, state data security laws and state data breach notification laws, each as amended from time to time.

1.2 "**Customer Data**" means all data and information, including Personal Information, that Customer, its personnel or its veterinary practice clients submit to, store on or transmit through the Analyzers or Processor's Systems, or that Processor otherwise receives or accesses on Customer's behalf in connection with the Agreement. Customer Data includes patient identifiers, animal owner names and contact details, referring veterinary practice details, test orders, test results, quality-control records and Analyzer user credentials.

1.3 "**Personal Information**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with an identified or identifiable individual, and any information defined as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

1.4 "**Processor's Systems**" means the remote service, remote diagnostics and connectivity tools, cloud-hosted portals, middleware and related infrastructure that Processor or its Subprocessors operate in connection with the Analyzers and the Products.

1.5 "**Processing**" (and "**process**") means any operation performed on Customer Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, transmission, disclosure, alteration and deletion.

1.6 "**Security Incident**" means any confirmed unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Customer Data in Processor's or a Subprocessor's possession, custody or control.

1.7 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Customer Data on Processor's behalf.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 **Roles.** Customer determines the purposes and means of processing Customer Data. Processor processes Customer Data only as a service provider to Customer.

2.2 **Scope.** This DPA applies to all processing of Customer Data by Processor and its Subprocessors in connection with the installation, operation, remote monitoring, maintenance and service of the Analyzers, the operation of Processor's Systems, and the supply of the Products under the Agreement.

2.3 **Description of Processing.** Annex 1 describes the categories of Customer Data, the categories of individuals concerned, and the nature, purposes and duration of the processing.

## 3. Processing Instructions

3.1 **Documented Instructions.** Processor shall process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions. The Agreement, Customer's configuration of the Analyzers and Processor's Systems, and Customer's written requests to Processor's service personnel are Customer's instructions for this purpose.

3.2 **Restrictions.** Processor shall not (a) sell or share Customer Data, as those terms are used in Applicable Data Protection Laws; (b) retain, use or disclose Customer Data for any purpose other than performing its obligations under the Agreement; (c) retain, use or disclose Customer Data outside the direct business relationship between Processor and Customer; or (d) combine Customer Data with data Processor receives from other sources, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Laws.

3.3 **Unlawful Instructions.** Processor shall promptly inform Customer if, in Processor's reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Laws. Processor may suspend the affected processing until Customer confirms or modifies the instruction.

3.4 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall ensure that it has provided all notices and obtained all consents and authorizations required under Applicable Data Protection Laws for Processor to process Customer Data as contemplated by the Agreement. Customer is responsible for the accuracy, quality and lawfulness of the Customer Data and of the means by which Customer acquired it.

3.5 **Aggregated Service Data.** Processor may generate de-identified, aggregated performance and diagnostic data about the Analyzers and Products, such as error rates, reagent consumption and calibration metrics, for the purposes of service, quality control and product improvement. Such data shall not identify Customer, any Customer Site or any individual, and Processor shall not attempt to re-identify it.

## 4. Personnel and Confidentiality

4.1 Processor shall ensure that each person it authorizes to process Customer Data is subject to a written or statutory duty of confidentiality and receives appropriate training in data protection and security.

4.2 Processor shall limit access to Customer Data to those personnel who need it to perform Processor's obligations under the Agreement.

4.3 Customer Data is Confidential Information of Customer under Section 10 of the Supply Agreement.

## 5. Security

5.1 **Security Measures.** Processor shall implement and maintain the administrative, technical and physical safeguards described in Annex 2. Those safeguards shall be appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and the risks of the processing, and no less protective than industry standards for remotely connected laboratory instruments.

5.2 **Changes.** Processor may update the safeguards in Annex 2 from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall level of protection of Customer Data.

5.3 **Security Incidents.** Processor shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after confirming a Security Incident. The notice shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate volume of Customer Data affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it. Processor shall supplement its notice as further information becomes available.

5.4 **Cooperation.** Processor shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident, and shall provide Customer with reasonable assistance in meeting Customer's notification obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws. Processor shall not notify any affected individual, veterinary practice or regulator about a Security Incident involving Customer Data without Customer's prior written approval, except where required by law.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 **Authorization.** Customer authorizes Processor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 3.

6.2 **New Subprocessors.** Processor shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging any new Subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period, and the Parties shall discuss Customer's objection in good faith. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, Processor shall not use that Subprocessor to process Customer Data.

6.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Processor shall impose on each Subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA. Processor remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of those obligations.

## 7. Assistance, Audit and Data Return

7.1 **Individual Requests.** Processor shall promptly forward to Customer any request it receives from an individual to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws in relation to Customer Data, and shall not respond to it except on Customer's instructions. Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to such requests.

7.2 **Regulatory Inquiries.** Processor shall promptly notify Customer of any inquiry, subpoena or request from a governmental authority relating to Customer Data, unless prohibited by law, and shall cooperate reasonably with Customer in responding to it.

7.3 **Audit.** Once in each contract year, on not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice, Customer or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations may review Processor's compliance with this DPA. Processor may satisfy this right by providing its most recent independent security assessment report covering Processor's Systems, together with reasonable responses to Customer's written questions. Any on-site review shall take place during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with Processor's operations. Each Party bears its own costs of any review.

7.4 **Return and Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiration of the Agreement, or on Customer's earlier written request, Processor shall return Customer Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format and then securely delete all remaining copies of Customer Data in Processor's Systems. Processor may retain Customer Data to the extent required by law, subject to continuing compliance with this DPA. On Customer's request, Processor shall certify the deletion in writing.

7.5 **Analyzer Removal.** Before removing any Analyzer from a Customer Site under Section 13.3 of the Supply Agreement or otherwise, Processor shall securely wipe all Customer Data stored locally on that Analyzer, or shall permit Customer to do so.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 **By Processor.** Processor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent resulting from Processor's breach of Section 5 of this DPA.

8.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Processor and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising from the Customer Data or Customer's processing instructions, including Losses arising out of third-party claims and any fines or penalties imposed by a governmental authority, regardless of whether such Losses also arise in part from Processor's acts or omissions.

8.3 **Procedure.** Section 11.3 of the Supply Agreement applies to claims for indemnification under this Section 8.

## 9. Term and General

9.1 **Term.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Processor or any Subprocessor processes Customer Data, including after termination or expiration of the Supply Agreement.

9.2 **Survival.** Sections 4, 5.3, 5.4, 7.4, 8 and 9 survive termination or expiration of the Agreement.

9.3 **Changes in Law.** If Applicable Data Protection Laws change in a way that affects the processing of Customer Data under this DPA, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendments reasonably required to comply with them.

9.4 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 15.1 and 15.2 of the Supply Agreement apply to this DPA as if set out in full.

9.5 **Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 16.1 of the Supply Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents shall also be sent by email to Customer's Chief Legal Officer at legal@pounirtlabs.com and to Customer's security contact at security@pounirtlabs.com.

9.6 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only in accordance with Section 16.5 of the Supply Agreement.

9.7 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## ANNEX 2: SECURITY MEASURES

1. **Access Control.** Role-based access to Processor's Systems; multi-factor authentication for all remote access sessions to Analyzers; logging and review of remote sessions; removal of access within one (1) business day after a person no longer requires it.
2. **Encryption.** Encryption of Customer Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest in Processor's Systems using AES-256 or an equivalent standard.
3. **Network Security.** Segmented networks for Analyzer connectivity; firewalls and intrusion detection; outbound-only connections from Analyzers to Processor's Systems unless Customer authorizes otherwise.
4. **Vulnerability Management.** Monthly vulnerability scanning of Processor's Systems; annual third-party penetration testing; remediation of critical vulnerabilities within thirty (30) days after identification; validated security patches for Analyzer software made available to Customer.
5. **Business Continuity.** Daily backups of Customer Data held in Processor's Systems, stored in a geographically separate U.S. facility; annual testing of restoration procedures.
6. **Personnel.** Background checks for personnel with access to Customer Data, to the extent permitted by law; annual security awareness training.
7. **Incident Response.** A documented incident response plan, reviewed and tested at least annually.
