# Reigein Diagnostics Introduces the RD-H7 Veterinary Hematology Analyzer

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**MINNEAPOLIS, Minnesota, September 9, 2026.** Reigein Diagnostics Corp. ("Reigein"), a developer and manufacturer of veterinary diagnostic reagent cartridges, analyzers and laboratory consumables, today announced the commercial launch of the **RD-H7 Veterinary Hematology Analyzer**. The RD-H7 is a high-throughput, five-part differential hematology system designed for veterinary reference laboratories and large multi-specialty hospitals.

## 1. Product Overview

The RD-H7 builds on the RD-H5 platform. It processes up to 120 samples per hour and delivers species-specific differentials for canine, feline, equine, bovine and selected exotic species. Its key features include:

- **Expanded species algorithms.** The analyzer ships with updated gating profiles for twelve species and supports laboratory-defined reference intervals.
- **Automated sample handling.** A 100-tube continuous-load rack system with closed-tube sampling and integrated clot detection.
- **Reduced reagent consumption.** Reigein's internal testing shows lower reagent volume per test than the RD-H5. Actual consumption varies with test mix and laboratory workflow.
- **Remote service readiness.** The RD-H7 connects to Reigein's remote diagnostics platform, with encrypted outbound-only connectivity, multi-factor authentication for service sessions and session logging.

## 2. Reagents and Consumables

The RD-H7 uses a new line of RD-H7 hematology reagent cartridges, controls and calibrators, which are available from today. Reigein will continue to manufacture, sell and support RD-H5 analyzers, reagent cartridges, controls and calibrators, as well as the RD-C900 chemistry platform and its reagents. The RD-H7 launch does not change any existing product line.

## 3. Availability

The RD-H7 is available to order now in the United States through Reigein's direct sales organization. Installations are expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026. Customers may evaluate the system through Reigein's demonstration program at its Minneapolis applications laboratory. Pricing and placement options, including reagent-rental arrangements, are set out in individual customer agreements.

## 5. Regulatory and Quality Information

The RD-H7 is intended for veterinary use only and is not for human diagnostic use. The analyzer and its reagents are manufactured under Reigein's quality management system at its Minnesota manufacturing facility. Product performance claims are based on Reigein internal verification studies. Summaries of those studies are available to customers on request.
