# ORDER FORM: REAGENTS AND CONSUMABLES

**Order Form No.:** RDC-PVL-2024-001
**Order Form Date:** March 1, 2024

This Order Form is entered into by **Reigein Diagnostics Corp.**, a Minnesota corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Pounirt Veterinary Laboratories, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). It is the Order Form referred to in the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). This Order Form forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

## 1. Term and Renewal

1.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of the Agreement begins on the Effective Date and expires on February 28, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**").

1.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms of three (3) years each. It does not renew if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before expiration of the then-current term. Notice of non-renewal must be given in accordance with Section 16.1 of the Supply Agreement.

1.3 **Contract Year.** Each "contract year" begins on March 1 and ends on the last day of February of the following calendar year.

## 2. Minimum Purchase Commitment

2.1 **Annual Commitment.** Customer's Minimum Purchase Commitment is the purchase of Products with an aggregate invoiced value, at the prices in Section 4, of not less than **$3,600,000** in each contract year.

2.2 **Credited Purchases.** All Products invoiced to Customer and delivered to any Customer Site during a contract year are credited against the Minimum Purchase Commitment for that contract year. Products are credited net of credits issued under Sections 6.1 and 8.3 of the Supply Agreement. Quantities credited under Sections 5.4 and 16.2 of the Supply Agreement are credited at the prices in Section 4.

2.3 **Annual Shortfall.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each contract year, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a statement of credited purchases for that contract year. If credited purchases are less than the Minimum Purchase Commitment, Supplier may invoice Customer for the difference. That invoice is payable in accordance with Section 3.3 of the Supply Agreement. A shortfall payment does not entitle Customer to delivery of any Products.

2.4 **Quarterly Reporting.** Within fifteen (15) business days after the end of each calendar quarter, Supplier shall provide Customer with a report of credited purchases for the contract year to date, broken down by Customer Site and Product line.

## 3. Exclusivity

3.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the term, Customer shall source 100% of its hematology and chemistry reagent requirements for the Customer Sites from Supplier. Customer shall also source from Supplier all analyzer consumables used with the Analyzers.

3.2 **Customer Sites.** As used in this Order Form, "**Customer Sites**" means all laboratory facilities owned or operated by Customer or any Affiliate, now or in the future.

3.3 **Exceptions.** Section 3.1 does not apply to:

(a) reagents for test methods or species panels that Supplier does not offer, as confirmed in writing by Supplier on Customer's request within five (5) business days;

(b) substitute products obtained under Section 16.2 of the Supply Agreement; or

(c) quantities cancelled under Section 5.4 of the Supply Agreement and replaced from third parties for the affected delivery period.

3.4 **Supplier Commitment.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient inventory and manufacturing capacity to supply Customer's forecast requirements under Section 4.2 of the Supply Agreement. If those requirements exceed the most recent forecast by more than twenty-five percent (25%), Supplier shall supply that excess on a commercially reasonable efforts basis.

5.2 **Title.** Title to the Analyzers remains with Supplier at all times. Customer shall keep the Analyzers free of liens created by or through Customer. Customer shall not relocate any Analyzer to another facility without Supplier's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

5.3 **Additional Analyzers.** Customer may request additional Analyzers by written notice. Supplier shall install accepted additional Analyzers within thirty (30) days after acceptance, on the terms of this Section 5.

5.4 **Care.** Customer shall use the Analyzers in accordance with Supplier's operator manuals. Customer bears the cost of repairs caused by misuse, unauthorized modification or use of reagents or consumables not supplied by Supplier, except as permitted under Section 3.3. Supplier bears the cost of all other service under Section 7.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 6. Ordering and Delivery Details

6.1 **Ordering Channel.** Customer shall submit Purchase Orders through Supplier's customer ordering portal or by email to orders@reigein.com.

6.2 **Minimum Order.** The minimum order per Purchase Order per facility is $1,500. Supplier may add a handling charge of $85 to any order below that amount.

6.3 **Standard Shipping.** Supplier bears standard shipping charges, including temperature-controlled shipping, for orders delivered within the lead times in Exhibit A. Customer bears charges for expedited delivery that Customer requests, at Supplier's published rates.

## 7. Billing

7.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall send invoices electronically to ap@pounirtlabs.com. Each invoice must be itemized by SKU and must comply with Section 3.2 of the Supply Agreement.

7.2 **Remittance.** Customer shall make payments by ACH transfer to the account designated by Supplier in writing. Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' written notice of any change to that account. The change must be verified by telephone with Customer's accounts payable contact.
