# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

## Theatrical DCP Mastering – United States and Canada

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Binond Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Favaiv Digital Cinema Services, Inc.** ("**Supplier**"). It is made under the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 Customer acquires and releases independent feature films theatrically in the United States and Canada. Supplier operates a digital cinema mastering facility and provides digital cinema package mastering and related services.

1.2 The Parties have agreed that Supplier will be Customer's exclusive provider of theatrical DCP mastering for releases in the United States and Canada. This SOW sets out the Services, Deliverables, specifications, schedule and Fees for that work.

1.3 Customer will order mastering for each Picture under this SOW by submitting a Title Order in accordance with Section 4.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Mastering Services.** For each Picture covered by a Title Order, Supplier shall:

(a) ingest and verify the Customer Materials, including the picture master (DCDM, ProRes 4444 XQ or other format agreed in the Title Order), final audio mixes and subtitle or caption files;

(b) conform, color-space convert (to XYZ) and encode picture essence to JPEG 2000 in accordance with the DCI Digital Cinema System Specification;

(c) wrap picture, sound and subtitle essence into an encrypted SMPTE-compliant digital cinema package as an original version (the "**OV**");

(d) create any version file packages (each a "**VF**") specified in the Title Order, including Canadian French subtitled or dubbed versions, open-caption versions and audio description tracks;

(e) generate content keys for each OV and VF and hold them in Supplier's secure key management system in accordance with Section 7 of the Agreement; and

(f) perform the quality control described in Section 3.

2.2 **Trailers.** Where a Title Order so specifies, Supplier shall master theatrical trailers and teasers for the Picture as unencrypted DCPs on the same specifications.

2.3 **Exclusions.** The following are not part of this SOW and, if required, will be ordered under a separate SOW or change order under Section 2.4 of the Agreement: KDM generation and distribution, drive duplication and shipping to exhibitors, secure watermarked digital screeners, and any non-theatrical deliverables.

---

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS AND QUALITY CONTROL

3.1 **Technical Specifications.** Unless a Title Order states otherwise, each DCP shall be mastered:

(a) in 2K (2048 x 1080) or 4K (4096 x 2160) resolution, as specified in the Title Order, in Flat or Scope container as appropriate to the source;

(b) at 24 frames per second, or such other SMPTE-supported frame rate as the Title Order specifies;

(c) with 5.1 or 7.1 PCM audio at 48 kHz/24-bit, and immersive audio packages where supplied by Customer;

(d) with SMPTE Timed Text subtitles; and

(e) as SMPTE DCP (Interop only where Customer requests it in writing).

3.2 **Quality Control.** Supplier shall perform a full real-time QC screening of each OV and VF in a calibrated DCI-compliant screening room, including checks of picture, sound sync, subtitle timing and placement, reel breaks and playback on at least two server platforms in common use in North American exhibition. Supplier shall deliver a written QC report with each DCP.

3.3 **Customer Review.** Customer may attend the QC screening, in person or by secure remote review, on at least two (2) business days' notice.

---

## 4. TITLE ORDERS AND DELIVERY SCHEDULE

4.1 **Title Orders.** Customer shall submit a written Title Order for each Picture identifying the Picture, the release date, the source materials, the versions required, the resolution and any special requirements.

4.2 **Materials Delivery.** Customer shall deliver complete, final Customer Materials to Supplier no later than fifteen (15) business days before the theatrical release date stated in the Title Order.

4.3 **Standard Turnaround.** Supplier shall deliver the OV and the QC report within seven (7) business days after receipt of complete Customer Materials, and each VF within three (3) business days after delivery of the OV or receipt of the relevant version materials, whichever is later.

4.4 **Rush Turnaround.** Where Customer requests it in the Title Order and Supplier confirms capacity in writing, Supplier shall deliver the OV within three (3) business days after receipt of complete Customer Materials.

4.5 **Delivery Method.** Supplier shall deliver each DCP by secure file transfer to the location Customer specifies and shall retain an archived copy in accordance with Section 6.

4.6 **Acceptance.** Section 5.3 of the Agreement applies to each Deliverable. Where Supplier must correct a non-conforming DCP, it shall redeliver the corrected DCP within three (3) business days, or within one (1) business day where the release date is less than seven (7) days away.

---

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Mastering Fee.** Customer shall pay Supplier $4,850 per feature-length DCP master. The fee covers ingest, encoding, packaging of the OV, content key generation, real-time QC screening and the QC report.

5.2 **Additional Fees.**

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Each VF (subtitled, dubbed, captioned or audio description) | $1,250 per VF |
| Trailer or teaser DCP (up to 3 minutes) | $650 per trailer |
| 4K mastering surcharge | $1,500 per feature-length DCP master |
| Rush turnaround under Section 4.4 | 35% of the applicable mastering fee |
| Corrections caused by changes to Customer Materials after delivery | $175 per hour |

5.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice each Fee on delivery of the relevant Deliverable in accordance with Section 6.1 of the Agreement. Payment terms are as set out in Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

5.4 **Price Adjustment.** The Fees are fixed for the first twelve (12) months after the SOW Effective Date. Thereafter, Supplier may increase the Fees once in each twelve-month period on at least sixty (60) days' written notice, by no more than three percent (3%).

5.5 **Expenses.** No expenses are reimbursable under this SOW except as Customer approves in writing in advance.

---

## 6. ARCHIVING

6.1 Supplier shall keep an encrypted archive copy of each OV and VF, and the associated content keys, in secure storage for the duration of this SOW, at no additional charge.

6.2 On Customer's written request, Supplier shall deliver archived DCPs to Customer or to a destination Customer designates, at Supplier's then-standard transfer rates.

---

## 7. CONTENT SECURITY

7.1 Supplier shall handle all Customer Materials, DCPs and content keys in accordance with Section 7 of the Agreement.

7.2 Supplier shall perform all mastering under this SOW at its secure facility, and shall not move Customer Materials to any other location without Customer's prior written consent.

7.3 Supplier shall maintain and comply with its documented content security program, which shall be consistent with the Motion Picture Association Content Security Best Practices, and shall provide Customer with a summary of its most recent independent content security assessment on request.

---

## 8. TERM OF SOW AND CONTACTS

8.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term of the Agreement and any Renewal Term, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the Agreement.

8.2 **Project Contacts.** Each Party shall designate in writing a project manager responsible for Title Orders, scheduling and day-to-day communication under this SOW. Either Party may replace its project manager on written notice.

8.3 **Status Reporting.** Supplier shall provide Customer with a weekly status report listing each open Title Order, its stage of completion and any anticipated delays.
