# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Binond Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"); and

**Favaiv Digital Cinema Services, Inc.**, a corporation ("**Supplier**").

Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**WHEREAS**, Customer is an independent film distributor that releases motion pictures theatrically and through other channels;

**WHEREAS**, Supplier provides digital cinema package mastering, key delivery message generation, drive duplication and secure watermarked digital screener services; and

**WHEREAS**, Customer wishes to obtain such services from Supplier, and Supplier wishes to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement and the Statements of Work entered into under it;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Addendum**" means any addendum, schedule or supplement to this Agreement that the Parties sign and that expressly states it is made under this Agreement, including the DPA.

1.2 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.3 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

1.4 "**Customer Materials**" means all picture, sound, subtitle, caption, artwork, metadata and other source materials, and all copies of them, that Customer or its licensors provide to Supplier, together with all content keys, certificates and security credentials relating to a Picture.

1.5 "**Deliverables**" means the digital cinema packages, KDMs, duplicated drives, screener files, reports and other items that Supplier is to deliver under an SOW.

1.6 "**Distribution Deliverables**" means every deliverable of any kind required for the exploitation of any Picture that Customer distributes, including all theatrical (including digital cinema packages, KDMs and duplicated drives), home entertainment, streaming and video-on-demand, broadcast and cable television, airline and in-flight, and festival and market deliverables, together with all mastering, encoding, conforming, packaging, duplication, encryption and screener services relating to them.

1.7 "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated March 2, 2026, as amended from time to time.

1.8 "**Fees**" means the fees payable by Customer for the Services and Deliverables, as set out in the applicable SOW.

1.9 "**KDM**" means a key delivery message enabling the playback of an encrypted digital cinema package on specified equipment during a specified window.

1.10 "**Picture**" means any feature-length or short-form motion picture, trailer or other audiovisual work for which Customer holds distribution rights.

1.11 "**Services**" means the services Supplier performs under an SOW, including the production of Deliverables.

1.12 "**Territory**" means the entire world.

1.13 "**SOW**" means a statement of work entered into under Section 2.1.

1.14 "**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

---

## 2. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 **Statements of Work.** Supplier shall perform Services under one or more SOWs. Each SOW shall describe the Services and Deliverables, the specifications, the delivery schedule and the Fees, and shall be signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Each SOW is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement.

2.2 **Addenda.** The DPA and any other Addendum form part of this Agreement.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: (a) the DPA, as to the processing of personal data; then (b) any SOW, as to the Services, Deliverables and any matters arising under that SOW; then (c) the body of this Agreement.

2.4 **Change Requests.** Either Party may request a change to an SOW in writing. No change is binding unless set out in a written change order signed by both Parties that states any adjustment to the Fees or delivery schedule.

---

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY

3.1 **Exclusive Supply.** Notwithstanding anything in any SOW, Customer shall procure all Distribution Deliverables exclusively from Supplier throughout the Territory during the Term.

3.2 **Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient personnel, equipment and facility capacity to meet Customer's reasonably forecast requirements. Customer shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling quarterly forecast of anticipated releases at least thirty (30) days before the start of each calendar quarter.

3.3 **Supplier's Other Customers.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Supplier from providing services to third parties, provided that Supplier complies with Sections 7 and 9.

---

## 4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

4.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**," and together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or that SOW and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

4.4 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

4.5 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination: (a) Customer shall pay for all Services properly performed and Deliverables delivered up to the effective date of termination; (b) Supplier shall, at Customer's election, return or securely destroy all Customer Materials and certify the same in writing within fifteen (15) days; and (c) Supplier shall complete any KDM issuance already scheduled for exhibitions booked before the termination date, unless Customer instructs otherwise.

4.6 **Survival.** Sections 1, 4.5, 4.6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 18, 19 and 20, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

---

## 5. SERVICES AND DELIVERY

5.1 **Performance.** Supplier shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the applicable SOW and generally accepted industry standards for digital cinema mastering and distribution, including the DCI Digital Cinema System Specification and applicable SMPTE standards.

5.2 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver each Deliverable to the location, and in the format and by the date, stated in the applicable SOW. Time of delivery is of the essence where the SOW identifies a release, festival or screening date.

5.3 **Acceptance.** Customer shall have ten (10) business days after delivery to inspect each Deliverable and to notify Supplier in writing of any non-conformity with the applicable specifications. Supplier shall correct any non-conforming Deliverable at its own cost and redeliver it within the period stated in the SOW or, if none is stated, within five (5) business days. A Deliverable is accepted when Customer gives written acceptance or the inspection period expires without a notice of non-conformity.

5.4 **Physical Media.** Title to and risk of loss of duplicated drives and other physical media passes to Customer on delivery to the carrier or delivery location specified in the SOW.

5.5 **Personnel and Subcontractors.** Supplier shall not subcontract any Services involving access to Customer Materials without Customer's prior written consent. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

---

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each SOW. Unless the SOW states otherwise, Supplier shall invoice Fees on delivery of the relevant Deliverable.

6.2 **Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier of the dispute and the reasons for it within the payment period. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any disputed amount promptly.

6.3 **Expenses.** Customer shall reimburse only those expenses that are expressly provided for in an SOW or that Customer approves in writing in advance.

6.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay any such taxes properly invoiced, other than taxes on Supplier's income, property or personnel.

6.5 **Records.** Supplier shall keep accurate records relating to the Fees for at least three (3) years after the relevant invoice and shall make them available to Customer on reasonable notice.

---

## 7. CONTENT SECURITY

7.1 **Custody of Customer Materials.** Supplier shall hold all Customer Materials in trust for Customer, use them solely to perform the Services, and store them only in secure facilities and systems that meet or exceed the security standards set out in the applicable SOW and, at a minimum, those customarily applied by digital cinema laboratories handling pre-release studio content.

7.2 **Access Controls.** Supplier shall limit access to Customer Materials to personnel with a need to know for the Services, maintain access logs, and use encryption for Customer Materials in transit and at rest.

7.3 **Watermarking and Screeners.** Where an SOW calls for screeners, Supplier shall apply forensic watermarking that identifies each individual recipient, and shall provide Customer with watermark logs on request.

7.4 **Security Incidents.** Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, copying of or disclosure of Customer Materials, and shall cooperate with Customer in investigating and remediating the incident.

---

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Customer Ownership.** As between the Parties, Customer and its licensors own all right, title and interest in the Customer Materials and the Pictures. Upon payment of the applicable Fees, Customer owns all Deliverables, excluding Supplier Tools. Supplier hereby assigns to Customer any rights it may acquire in the Deliverables, excluding Supplier Tools.

8.2 **Supplier Tools.** Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing and independently developed software, templates, workflows, encoding presets and know-how ("**Supplier Tools**"). To the extent any Supplier Tools are embedded in a Deliverable, Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use them as part of that Deliverable.

8.3 **License to Supplier.** Customer grants Supplier a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use the Customer Materials solely to perform the Services.

---

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including all Customer Materials, release schedules, pricing and the terms of this Agreement.

9.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to exercise its rights and perform its obligations under this Agreement, shall protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own similar information (and no less than reasonable care), and shall not disclose it except to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can show (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

9.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives the disclosing Party prompt prior notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

9.5 **Prior Mutual NDA.** Information exchanged between the Parties before the Effective Date remains subject to the mutual nondisclosure agreement previously entered into between them.

9.6 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 continue during the Term and for five (5) years after it ends, except that obligations relating to Customer Materials and unreleased Pictures continue for so long as the information remains confidential.

---

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 **DPA.** To the extent Supplier processes personal data on behalf of Customer in performing the Services, including data relating to screener recipients and festival attendees, the Parties shall comply with the DPA.

10.2 **Compliance with Law.** Each Party shall comply with all data protection and privacy laws applicable to it in connection with this Agreement.

12.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) an allegation that a Deliverable or Supplier Tool infringes or misappropriates any third-party intellectual property right; (b) any unauthorized access to, copying of or disclosure of Customer Materials while in Supplier's custody or control; or (c) Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

12.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from an allegation that the Customer Materials, as provided by Customer and used by Supplier in accordance with this Agreement, infringe any third-party intellectual property right.

12.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim (provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced); (b) allow the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, except that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its prior written consent; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its own choosing at its own cost.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Except as provided in Section 13.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits or revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

13.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 13.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

13.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 13.1 and 13.2 do not apply to (a) indemnification obligations under Section 12 or any Addendum; (b) a Party's breach of Section 9; (c) Supplier's breach of Section 7; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (e) Customer's obligation to pay undisputed Fees.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 During the Term and for one (1) year after it, Supplier shall maintain, with reputable insurers, (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) errors and omissions (media liability) insurance of at least $3,000,000 per claim; (c) cyber liability insurance of at least $3,000,000 per claim; and (d) property insurance covering Customer Materials in Supplier's custody at replacement value. Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

Supplier shall reasonably cooperate with any such audit.

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of government, war, terrorism, pandemic, or widespread failure of utilities or telecommunications networks, provided that the affected Party promptly notifies the other and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Supplier from performing for more than fifteen (15) days, Customer may obtain the affected Services from a third party for the duration of the event and may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.
