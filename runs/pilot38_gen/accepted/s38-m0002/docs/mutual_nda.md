# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of November 17, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Binond Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Binond**"); and

**Favaiv Digital Cinema Services, Inc.**, a corporation ("**Favaiv**").

Binond and Favaiv are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." A Party disclosing Confidential Information is the "**Disclosing Party**," and a Party receiving it is the "**Receiving Party**."

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to explore a possible commercial relationship in which Favaiv would provide Binond with digital cinema package mastering, key delivery message, drive duplication and secure watermarked digital screener services (the "**Purpose**"); and

**WHEREAS**, in connection with the Purpose each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates disclose to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, in any form, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date. Information is Confidential Information if it is marked or identified as confidential or if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential. Confidential Information includes:

(a) motion pictures, trailers, picture and sound materials, artwork and other content, whether released or unreleased;

(b) release schedules, acquisition plans, festival and market strategies, and box office and sales data;

(c) pricing, rate cards, proposals and the terms of any proposed agreement;

(d) technical information, including facility, workflow, key management, watermarking and content security information; and

(e) the fact that the Parties are discussing the Purpose.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors.

---

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

2.2 **Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

2.3 **Disclosure to Representatives.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations, or professional duties of confidentiality, no less protective than this NDA. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

2.4 **Content.** The Receiving Party shall not copy, screen, transcode, encode or retain any picture or sound materials of the Disclosing Party except as needed for the Purpose (for example, to prepare test encodes or sample packages that the Disclosing Party requests in writing). The Receiving Party shall store such materials only on secured systems with access limited to personnel who need it for the Purpose.

2.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information, and shall reasonably cooperate to regain possession of it and prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

---

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without restriction;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party that is not under a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

---

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena or court order. Where lawful, the Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice and reasonable cooperation, at the Disclosing Party's expense, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

---

## 5. PERSONAL DATA

5.1 The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data relating to screener recipients, festival attendees or other individuals under this NDA. Neither Party shall disclose such personal data to the other in connection with the Purpose unless the Parties first agree in writing on terms for its processing.

---

## 6. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within fifteen (15) days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall confirm in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Receiving Party may retain copies that it is required to keep by law or that are held in automatic electronic backups that are not readily accessible. Any retained copies remain subject to this NDA for so long as they are held. Picture and sound materials may not be retained under this Section 6.2 and shall be destroyed in all cases.

---

## 7. OWNERSHIP AND NO LICENSE

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party or its licensors. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any Confidential Information, patent, copyright, trademark or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

7.2 ALL CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS." NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY AS TO THE ACCURACY OR COMPLETENESS OF ITS CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION.

---

## 8. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

8.1 This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information or to enter into any further agreement. Any commercial relationship between the Parties will be set out only in a separate written agreement signed by both Parties. Either Party may end discussions concerning the Purpose at any time by written notice.

---

## 9. TERM AND DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

9.1 **Term.** This NDA begins on the NDA Effective Date and continues for two (2) years, unless either Party ends it earlier on thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Receiving Party's obligations under this NDA continue for five (5) years after the end of the term of this NDA, with respect to all Confidential Information disclosed before that date. Obligations relating to picture and sound materials, unreleased Pictures, content keys and security credentials continue for so long as the information remains confidential.

---

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

10.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

---

## 11. REMEDIES

11.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information, and in particular of unreleased content, may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or stop any actual or threatened breach of this NDA, without the need to post bond, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

---

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 **Notices.** All notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses each Party designates in writing for notices. Notices are effective on receipt.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this NDA relates. This NDA binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

12.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment to this NDA is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this NDA operates as a waiver of it.

12.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

12.5 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
