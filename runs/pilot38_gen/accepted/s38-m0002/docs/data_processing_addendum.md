# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Binond Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Customer**"); and

**Favaiv Digital Cinema Services, Inc.**, a corporation ("**Supplier**").

This DPA is an Addendum made under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement between Customer and Supplier dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Controller**" means the entity that determines the purposes and means of the Processing of Personal Data. For this DPA, Customer is the Controller.

1.2 "**Customer Personal Data**" means all Personal Data that Supplier Processes on behalf of Customer in connection with the Services. It includes screener viewer names, email addresses, account credentials, access and viewing logs and forensic watermark logs, and festival and market attendee lists.

1.3 "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the Processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, other U.S. state consumer privacy laws, and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR and the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act.

1.4 "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Customer Personal Data relates.

1.5 "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, settlements, judgments, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

1.6 "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, or any information defined as "personal data," "personal information" or a similar term under Data Protection Laws.

1.7 "**Personal Data Breach**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.

1.8 "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, erasure and destruction. "**Process**" and "**Processed**" have corresponding meanings.

1.9 "**Processor**" means the entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. For this DPA, Supplier is the Processor.

1.10 "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by Supplier to Process Customer Personal Data.

---

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Application.** This DPA applies to all Processing of Customer Personal Data by Supplier in performing the Services, including the operation of Supplier's secure screener platform, the issuance of screener access credentials, the application and logging of forensic watermarks under Section 7.3 of the Agreement, and the handling of festival and market attendee lists.

2.2 **Roles.** Customer is the Controller and Supplier is the Processor of Customer Personal Data. To the extent the CCPA applies, Supplier is a "service provider" and Customer is a "business," as those terms are defined in that law.

2.3 **Details of Processing.** Annex A describes the subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the Processing, the categories of Customer Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects.

---

## 3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Customer shall give Supplier only lawful, documented instructions for the Processing of Customer Personal Data. The Agreement, each SOW and this DPA are Customer's complete instructions as of the DPA Effective Date. Customer may issue further reasonable written instructions consistent with them.

3.2 Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for the Processing, and for providing any notices to and obtaining any consents from Data Subjects that Data Protection Laws require, including for screener recipients and festival attendees whose data Customer provides to Supplier.

---

## 4. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Instructions.** Supplier shall Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, unless applicable law requires otherwise. In that case Supplier shall inform Customer of the legal requirement before Processing, unless the law prohibits it. Supplier shall promptly notify Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws.

4.2 **Restrictions.** Supplier shall not sell or share Customer Personal Data, retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the Services, or combine it with Personal Data that Supplier receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as Data Protection Laws permit.

4.3 **Personnel.** Supplier shall ensure that each person authorized to Process Customer Personal Data is bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality and receives training appropriate to that person's role.

4.4 **Compliance.** Supplier shall comply with the obligations that Data Protection Laws impose on processors and service providers, and shall provide the same level of privacy protection those laws require.

---

## 5. SECURITY

5.1 Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against Personal Data Breaches. At a minimum these measures shall include those set out in Annex B and in Section 7 of the Agreement.

5.2 Supplier shall regularly test, assess and evaluate the effectiveness of these measures. Supplier shall not materially reduce the overall level of security during the Term.

---

## 6. SUB-PROCESSORS

6.1 Customer authorizes Supplier to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex C. Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection in good faith, Customer may terminate the affected SOW without penalty.

6.2 Supplier shall impose on each Sub-processor written data protection obligations no less protective than this DPA. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of each Sub-processor as if they were its own.

---

## 7. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

7.1 Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

7.2 The notice shall describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Supplier shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

7.3 Supplier shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate the Personal Data Breach. Supplier shall cooperate with Customer in meeting any notification obligations to regulators and Data Subjects. Supplier shall not notify any regulator or Data Subject of a Personal Data Breach on Customer's behalf without Customer's prior written approval, unless the law requires it.

---

## 8. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS, ASSISTANCE AND AUDIT

8.1 **Data Subject Requests.** Supplier shall promptly forward to Customer any request from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data, and shall not respond to it except on Customer's instructions. Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to such requests.

8.2 **Assistance.** Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer with any data protection impact assessment, prior consultation with a supervisory authority, or regulatory inquiry relating to the Processing.

8.3 **Audit.** Supplier shall make available to Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. Supplier shall allow audits under Section 15 of the Agreement, which applies to this DPA as if references to Sections 6 and 7 of the Agreement included this DPA.

---

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to any third-party claim, regulatory investigation, fine or penalty, or Data Subject action relating to Customer Personal Data or its Processing under the Agreement, regardless of whether caused in part by Supplier.

9.2 **Procedure.** Section 12.3 of the Agreement applies to any claim under Section 9.1.

---

## 10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1 Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United States or Canada without Customer's prior written consent. Where a transfer is subject to the EU GDPR or UK GDPR, the Parties shall put in place the standard contractual clauses or other transfer mechanism that those laws require.

---

## 11. RETURN AND DELETION

11.1 On expiry or termination of the Agreement or the applicable SOW, or earlier on Customer's written request, Supplier shall return or securely delete, at Customer's election, all Customer Personal Data. Supplier shall certify the deletion in writing within fifteen (15) days, unless applicable law requires further retention. Screener access credentials shall be deactivated at the end of each screener window stated in the applicable SOW.

---

## 12. TERM

12.1 This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date. It continues for so long as Supplier Processes Customer Personal Data, and in any event until the end of the Term.

---

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 This DPA may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties.

13.2 This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

---

## ANNEX A – DETAILS OF PROCESSING

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Processing of Customer Personal Data in providing secure screener, watermarking, KDM and festival delivery Services |
| Nature and purpose | Hosting and streaming of watermarked screeners; issuance and management of viewer credentials; logging of access, viewing and watermark data; management of festival and market attendee lists |
| Categories of Data Subjects | Screener recipients (including critics, awards voters, buyers, exhibitors and talent); festival and market attendees; Customer personnel |
| Categories of Personal Data | Names, email addresses, organizational affiliations, login credentials, IP addresses, device identifiers, viewing logs, forensic watermark identifiers |
| Duration | The Term, plus any period of return or deletion under Section 11 |

## ANNEX B – SECURITY MEASURES

1. Encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
2. Role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and quarterly access reviews.
3. Logging and monitoring of access to screener platform systems, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.
4. Annual third-party penetration testing of the screener platform, with critical findings remediated promptly.
5. Business continuity and backup procedures, with backups encrypted and tested at least annually.
6. Background checks for personnel with access to Customer Personal Data, where permitted by law.
