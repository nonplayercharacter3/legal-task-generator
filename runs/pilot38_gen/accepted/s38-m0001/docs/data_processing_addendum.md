# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**Addendum**") is entered into and effective as of April 1, 2025 (the "**Addendum Effective Date**") by and between:

**Laipeisk Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"); and

**Bredeix Organic Creamery, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Licensee**").

This Addendum is the Data Processing Addendum referred to in Section 9.5 of the Software License and Services Agreement between Licensor and Licensee effective as of April 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). It forms part of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Addendum have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Addendum:

(a) "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means all laws and regulations of the United States and its states that apply to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security (SHIELD) Act, N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law §§ 899-aa and 899-bb, and the Vermont Security Breach Notice Act, 9 V.S.A. § 2435.

(b) "**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates, including Licensee's farmer-suppliers and their principals and employees, and Licensee's own employees and Users.

(c) "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to a Data Subject that Licensor Processes on behalf of Licensee in connection with the Agreement. It includes "private information" as defined in N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-aa.

(d) "**Personal Data Breach**" means any unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data in Licensor's or a Subprocessor's possession, custody or control.

(e) "**Process**" or "**Processing**" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, transmission, disclosure, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure or destruction.

(f) "**Security Measures**" means the administrative, technical and physical safeguards described in Section 5 and Annex 2.

(g) "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Licensor to Process Personal Data on Licensee's behalf.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 **Roles.** Licensee determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data under the Agreement. Licensor Processes Personal Data solely on Licensee's behalf as its service provider.

2.2 **Scope.** This Addendum applies to all Personal Data that Licensor or any Subprocessor Processes in providing the Software and Services, including under each Statement of Work. The categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data, and the nature and purposes of the Processing, are described in Annex 1.

2.3 **Licensee Obligations.** Licensee shall have all notices, consents and lawful bases required under Applicable Data Protection Law to permit Licensor to Process Personal Data as contemplated by the Agreement.

## 3. Processing Instructions

3.1 **Documented Instructions.** Licensor shall Process Personal Data only on Licensee's documented instructions. The Agreement, each Statement of Work and Licensee's configuration and use of the Software are Licensee's complete instructions as of the Addendum Effective Date. Licensee may give additional written instructions consistent with the Agreement.

3.2 **Unlawful Instructions.** Licensor shall promptly inform Licensee if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.3 **Restrictions.** Licensor shall not:

(a) sell, rent or otherwise make Personal Data available to any third party for monetary or other valuable consideration;

(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than providing the Software and Services;

(c) combine Personal Data with personal data received from other customers or sources, except as necessary to provide the Software and Services; or

(d) use Personal Data, including farmer-supplier pricing, payment and production data, for benchmarking, analytics or product development outside the Agreement without Licensee's prior written consent.

## 4. Licensor Personnel

4.1 **Confidentiality.** Licensor shall ensure that each person it authorizes to Process Personal Data is bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than Section 9 of the Agreement.

4.2 **Access Limitation.** Licensor shall limit access to Personal Data to personnel who need that access to perform the Services. It shall give those personnel appropriate training in data protection and security.

4.3 **Background Checks.** Licensor shall conduct background checks, as permitted by law, on personnel with administrative access to production systems containing Personal Data.

## 5. Security

5.1 **Security Measures.** Licensor shall implement and maintain the Security Measures described in Annex 2, consistent with Section 9.4 of the Agreement and the reasonable safeguards requirements of N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 899-bb.

5.2 **Banking Data.** Licensor shall encrypt farmer-supplier bank account numbers, routing numbers and ACH payment files in transit and at rest, using industry-standard encryption. Access to unmasked bank account data shall be restricted to roles that Licensee designates in the Software.

5.3 **Updates to Security Measures.** Licensor may update the Security Measures from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall protection of Personal Data.

5.4 **Independent Assessment.** Licensor shall maintain the SOC 2 Type II report, or equivalent independent assessment, required by Section 9.4 of the Agreement. It shall provide a copy to Licensee annually on request.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 **Authorization.** Licensee authorizes Licensor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 3. As required by Section 16.3 of the Agreement, Licensor remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance and compliance.

6.2 **Subprocessor Terms.** Licensor shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor. That agreement shall impose data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Addendum.

6.3 **New Subprocessors.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice before engaging a new Subprocessor. Licensee may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection in good faith within thirty (30) days after Licensee's objection, Licensee may terminate the affected Services on written notice. Licensee shall then receive a refund of any prepaid Fees for the terminated Services covering the period after termination.

6.4 **Location.** Licensor shall not Process Personal Data, or permit any Subprocessor to Process it, outside the United States without Licensee's prior written consent. This is consistent with Section 4.1 of the Agreement.

## 7. Personal Data Breach

7.1 **Notice.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. This is consistent with Section 9.6 of the Agreement.

7.2 **Content of Notice.** Licensor's notice shall describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it.

Licensor shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

7.3 **Cooperation.** Licensor shall cooperate with Licensee and take reasonable steps to investigate, contain and remediate each Personal Data Breach. It shall preserve relevant logs and forensic evidence.

7.4 **Notifications to Third Parties.** Licensee shall decide whether to notify Data Subjects, regulators, consumer reporting agencies or other third parties, and on the content of any notice. Licensor shall not notify any of them without Licensee's prior written approval, except as required by law.

7.5 **Costs.** Licensor shall reimburse Licensee's reasonable costs arising from a Personal Data Breach caused by Licensor or its Subprocessors. These include the costs of:

(a) notices required under Applicable Data Protection Law;

(b) call-center support;

(c) credit monitoring or identity protection services for affected Data Subjects for up to twenty-four (24) months; and

(d) forensic investigation.

## 8. Liability

8.1 **Cap.** Notwithstanding Section 11.3(c) of the Agreement, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum, including any Personal Data Breach, shall be subject to the Cap.

8.2 **Agreement Terms.** Except as stated in Section 8.1, Section 11 of the Agreement applies to liability arising out of or relating to this Addendum.

## 9. Assistance and Data Subject Requests

9.1 **Data Subject Requests.** Licensor shall promptly forward to Licensee any request it receives from a Data Subject relating to Personal Data. Licensor shall not respond to the request except on Licensee's instructions. Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance, including through the Software's functionality, to enable Licensee to respond.

9.2 **Regulatory Inquiries.** Licensor shall notify Licensee promptly of any inquiry, subpoena or request from a regulator or law enforcement authority relating to Personal Data, unless prohibited by law. Licensor shall cooperate with Licensee in responding. Section 9.3 of the Agreement applies to any compelled disclosure.

## 10. Audit

10.1 **Information.** On Licensee's written request, Licensor shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this Addendum. That information includes its most recent SOC 2 Type II report and responses to reasonable security questionnaires.

10.2 **Audits.** If the information provided under Section 10.1 does not reasonably demonstrate compliance, or following a Personal Data Breach, Licensee or its independent auditor may audit Licensor's compliance with this Addendum. Licensee must give thirty (30) days' notice, or five (5) business days' notice following a Personal Data Breach. Audits shall take place during normal business hours and, except following a Personal Data Breach, not more than once per contract year. Each Party shall bear its own costs of the audit.

## 11. Return and Deletion

11.1 **On Termination.** On expiry or termination of the Agreement, Licensor shall return and delete Personal Data in accordance with Section 9.7 of the Agreement.

11.2 **Certification.** On Licensee's request, Licensor shall certify in writing that it and its Subprocessors have securely deleted Personal Data, except Personal Data retained as required by law. Licensor shall continue to protect any Personal Data so retained in accordance with this Addendum.

## 12. Term and General

12.1 **Term.** This Addendum begins on the Addendum Effective Date. It continues for as long as Licensor or any Subprocessor Processes Personal Data under the Agreement.

12.2 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Sections 15.1 through 15.3 of the Agreement apply to this Addendum.

12.3 **Amendment.** This Addendum may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

12.4 **Counterparts.** This Addendum may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

### Annex 2: Security Measures

1. **Governance.** A written information security program, approved by senior management, with a designated security officer and annual risk assessments.
2. **Access Control.** Role-based access, unique User IDs, multi-factor authentication for administrative and remote access, and quarterly access reviews.
3. **Encryption.** TLS 1.2 or higher in transit; AES-256 or equivalent at rest for production databases, backups and payment files.
4. **Network Security.** Firewalls, intrusion detection, network segmentation of production environments, and annual third-party penetration testing.
5. **Vulnerability Management.** Critical vulnerabilities patched within fifteen (15) days of a patch becoming available; regular vulnerability scanning.
6. **Logging and Monitoring.** Security event logging retained for at least twelve (12) months, with continuous monitoring.
7. **Business Continuity.** Daily encrypted backups stored in a separate U.S. location, and annual testing of disaster recovery plans.
8. **Secure Disposal.** Secure deletion of Personal Data and sanitization of decommissioned media in line with NIST SP 800-88.
9. **Vendor Management.** Security due diligence on Subprocessors before engagement and annually afterward.
