# SOFTWARE LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Software License and Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into and effective as of April 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Laipeisk Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"); and

**Bredeix Organic Creamery, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Licensee**").

Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** Licensor develops and licenses HerdLedger, a dairy supply-chain and milk-procurement software platform, and provides related implementation, support and hosting services. Licensee operates an organic dairy processing and milk-procurement business and wishes to license HerdLedger and obtain those services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of, or the power to direct the management and policies of, an entity.

(b) "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

(c) "**Data Processing Addendum**" or "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum entered into by the Parties under Section 9.5, as amended from time to time.

(d) "**Deliverable**" means any software configuration, module, interface, report, data migration or other item that Licensor is required to deliver under a Statement of Work.

(e) "**Delivery**" means Licensor's written notice to Licensee that a Deliverable has been installed or made available in Licensee's designated environment and is ready for acceptance testing.

(f) "**Documentation**" means Licensor's then-current user guides, technical specifications and release notes for the Software.

(g) "**Fees**" means the license fees, subscription fees, implementation fees, milestone fees and other amounts payable under this Agreement and each Statement of Work.

(h) "**Licensee Data**" means all data and information, including Personal Data, that Licensee or its Users submit to, or that is generated from Licensee's use of, the Software.

(i) "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

(j) "**Services**" means the implementation, configuration, training, support, hosting and other professional services described in this Agreement or in a Statement of Work.

(k) "**Software**" means Licensor's HerdLedger platform, including its procurement, farmer-supplier payment, milk-quality tracking and logistics modules, together with all Updates and Documentation.

(l) "**Specifications**" means the functional and technical requirements for a Deliverable set out in the applicable Statement of Work and the Documentation.

(m) "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work referencing this Agreement and signed by both Parties.

(n) "**Updates**" means bug fixes, patches, maintenance releases and new versions of the Software that Licensor makes generally available to its licensees.

(o) "**Users**" means Licensee's and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and agents authorized by Licensee to use the Software.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: the DPA (as to Personal Data), then this License Agreement, then any Statement of Work.

2.3 **Updates.** Licensor shall make all Updates available to Licensee at no additional charge during the Term.

## 3. Services and Statements of Work

3.1 **Statements of Work.** Licensor shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement and shall describe the Deliverables, Specifications, milestones, Fees and any project-specific responsibilities.

3.3 **Licensee Cooperation.** Licensee shall provide timely access to its personnel, systems and information reasonably required for Licensor to perform the Services.

3.4 **Change Orders.** Either Party may request a change to a Statement of Work. No change is binding unless set out in a written change order signed by both Parties.

## 4. Hosting and Support

4.1 **Hosting.** Licensor shall host the Software in a secure, commercially reasonable data-center environment located in the United States.

4.2 **Availability.** Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Software available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified to Licensee at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

4.3 **Support.** Licensor shall provide telephone and email support during the hours of 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days a week, and shall respond to severity-one issues within two (2) hours.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Licensee shall pay the Fees set out in this Agreement and each Statement of Work. The annual subscription fee for the Software for the first contract year is set out in Schedule A and is invoiced annually in advance.

5.2 **Milestone Fees.** Implementation fees tied to a Deliverable become payable upon acceptance of that Deliverable under Section 6.

5.3 **Invoicing and Payment.** Licensor shall invoice Fees as they become due. Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by written notice describing the dispute, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

5.4 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Licensee shall pay other than taxes based on Licensor's net income.

5.5 **Fee Adjustments.** Licensor may increase the subscription fee once per contract year, starting with the second contract year, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice, by no more than the lesser of three percent (3%) or the increase in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers over the preceding twelve (12) months.

## 6. Delivery and Acceptance

6.1 **Acceptance Testing.** Following Delivery of each Deliverable, Licensee may test the Deliverable to determine whether it conforms in all material respects to the Specifications.

6.2 **Rejection Period.** Licensee shall notify Licensor in writing of any rejection of a Deliverable within ten (10) days after Delivery, specifying in reasonable detail each material nonconformity with the Specifications (a "**Rejection Notice**"). If Licensee does not deliver a Rejection Notice within ten (10) days after Delivery, the Deliverable is deemed accepted.

6.3 **Correction.** On receipt of a timely Rejection Notice, Licensor shall correct the identified nonconformities and redeliver the Deliverable within fifteen (15) days, and the acceptance procedure in Sections 6.1 and 6.2 shall apply again to the redelivered Deliverable.

6.4 **Repeated Failure.** If a Deliverable fails acceptance testing on a third Delivery, Licensee may, at its option, (a) accept the Deliverable subject to an equitable reduction in the applicable Fees, or (b) terminate the applicable Statement of Work and receive a refund of all Fees paid for the nonconforming Deliverable.

6.5 **Acceptance Upon Use.** Licensee's use of a Deliverable in a live production environment for its business purposes, other than for testing, constitutes acceptance of that Deliverable.

9.1 **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including Licensee Data, farmer-supplier pricing and payment terms, and the Software's non-public features. 9.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

9.4 **Data Security.** Licensor shall implement and maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Licensee Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction, consistent with industry standards and applicable law, including the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security (SHIELD) Act. 9.5 **Data Processing Addendum.** The Parties shall enter into the DPA governing Licensor's processing of Personal Data on behalf of Licensee, including the personal data of Licensee's farmer-suppliers and employees. The DPA forms part of this Agreement.

9.6 **Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Licensee Data, and shall cooperate with Licensee in investigating and remediating the incident.

9.7 **Return of Data.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement, Licensor shall, at Licensee's election, return all Licensee Data in a commonly used, machine-readable format within thirty (30) days and then securely delete it, except as required by law.
