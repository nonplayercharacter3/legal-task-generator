# ORDER FORM NO. 3

**Order Form No.:** 3
**Date:** October 20, 2026 (the "**Order Form Date**")
**Customer:** Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc., a Delaware corporation
**Provider:** Prodous Coating Services LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
**Master Services Agreement:** Master Services Agreement between Customer and Provider dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**")
**Applicable SOW:** Statement of Work No. 1, FR Coating Services for Tarpaulin and Curtain Fabric, dated as of April 6, 2026 ("**SOW No. 1**")

## 1. INCORPORATION

1.1 **Order Form.** This Order Form No. 3 (this "**Order Form**") is an Order Form under Section 1.1 of the MSA and forms part of the Agreement. The MSA governs this Order Form.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the MSA or SOW No. 1.

1.3 **Scope.** This Order Form sets out the commercial terms for Coating Services performed under SOW No. 1 for the Product Lines. It applies to purchase orders that Customer issues under SOW No. 1 on or after the Order Form Date.

## 2. COMMERCIAL TERMS

2.1 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** The Minimum Annual Commitment is $2,400,000 per Contract Year. For the Contract Year beginning March 2, 2026, the Minimum Annual Commitment applies to that full Contract Year. Fees count toward it if they are invoiced under SOW No. 1 in that Contract Year, including fees invoiced before the Order Form Date.

2.2 **Amounts Counted.** The following count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment: (a) processing fees invoiced under Section 7.1 of SOW No. 1, net of credits under Section 6.4 of SOW No. 1; (b) purchases of substitute Coating Services under Section 16.4 of the MSA; and (c) processing fees for any Lot that Provider credits or refunds under Section 6.2(c) of the MSA, as though invoiced and paid. The following do not count: transition assistance fees under Section 7.4 of SOW No. 1, and the cost of independent laboratory testing under Section 5.3 of SOW No. 1.

2.3 **Shortfall.** If the amounts counted under Section 2.2 for a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Annual Commitment, Provider may invoice Customer for the difference within thirty (30) days after the end of that Contract Year. Customer shall pay the shortfall invoice under Section 4.3 of the MSA. Payment of a shortfall is Provider's sole remedy for Customer's failure to meet the Minimum Annual Commitment.

2.4 **Volume Pricing Tiers.** The per-linear-yard processing fees in Schedule B of SOW No. 1 are reduced by the following percentages, based on total linear yards of Finished Goods invoiced under SOW No. 1 in the current Contract Year:

| Tier | Cumulative Linear Yards in Contract Year | Reduction from Schedule B Fees |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 1,000,000 | 0% |
| 2 | 1,000,001 to 1,600,000 | 3% |
| 3 | 1,600,001 and above | 5% |

Each reduction applies only to yards invoiced after the Tier threshold is reached in that Contract Year. It does not apply retroactively. The cumulative count resets at the start of each Contract Year.

2.5 **Early Termination Fee.** The Early Termination Fee is the remaining Minimum Annual Commitment for the current Contract Year. That is, the Minimum Annual Commitment for the Contract Year in which termination takes effect, less the amounts counted under Section 2.2 for that Contract Year through the effective date of termination. The Early Termination Fee is never less than zero.

2.6 **Price Adjustment.** Any adjustment to the processing fees under Section 7.3 of SOW No. 1 applies to Schedule B fees before the reductions in Section 2.4 are applied.

## 3. ORDERING AND FORECASTS

3.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue purchase orders under Section 3.1 of the MSA. Each purchase order shall reference SOW No. 1 and this Order Form, and shall identify the Customer style numbers, linear yards and requested ship dates.

3.2 **Forecasts.** Customer shall continue to provide the rolling forecasts required by Section 6.3 of SOW No. 1. Forecasts are non-binding and do not modify the Minimum Annual Commitment.

3.3 **Capacity.** Provider shall keep the monthly capacity reservation in Section 6.3 of SOW No. 1 in place throughout the term of this Order Form.

## 4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Invoices.** Provider shall invoice under Section 4.2 of the MSA. Each invoice shall show the Schedule B fee, any Tier reduction applied under Section 2.4, and the cumulative linear yards invoiced in the current Contract Year.

4.2 **Payment.** Customer shall pay under Section 4.3 of the MSA.

4.3 **Remittance.** Payment shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account that Provider designates in writing on its letterhead, signed by an authorized officer of Provider. Any change to remittance instructions must be confirmed by telephone with Customer's accounts payable department before it takes effect.

## 5. SPECIAL TERMS

**Paragraph 1. Kestrel Transaction.** Customer advises Provider that Customer has entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated October 1, 2026, between Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc. and Kestrel Fiberworks Group, Inc. ("**Kestrel**"), under which Customer will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Kestrel. Closing is expected to occur on December 1, 2026, subject to customary conditions. Customer expects to continue purchasing Coating Services under SOW No. 1 after closing. The Minimum Annual Commitment and volume pricing tiers in Section 2 remain unchanged. Customer shall notify Provider in writing within five (5) business days after closing occurs.

**Paragraph 2. Lot 2207 Default Notice.** On August 3, 2026, Customer delivered to Provider a written notice of default under the Agreement (the "**Default Notice**"). The Default Notice concerns defective FR coating on Lot 2207 of Tarpaulin Line Finished Goods. It states that retained and field samples from Lot 2207 failed CPAI-84, Section 6 and NFPA 701, Test Method 2, as required by Section 3.2(a) of SOW No. 1. Provider has delivered a root-cause analysis under Section 8.3 of SOW No. 1. The Parties have not agreed on the remedy under Section 6.2 of the MSA. As of the Order Form Date, no Demand has been made on the Guarantor under the Parent Guaranty for the matters described in the Default Notice. Customer reserves all of its rights and remedies against Provider and the Guarantor relating to Lot 2207 and the Default Notice, whether under the MSA, SOW No. 1, the Parent Guaranty or applicable law. Neither the execution of this Order Form nor any performance or payment under it is a waiver of any of those rights or remedies.

**Paragraph 3. Lot 2207 Quarantine.** Until the Parties resolve the Default Notice, Provider shall keep all retained samples, batch records, chemical lot records and process parameter logs for Lot 2207 under Section 5.3 of the MSA. Provider shall make them available to Customer and its independent laboratory on request. Provider shall not destroy, alter or reprocess any Lot 2207 material in its possession without Customer's prior written approval.

## 6. TERM

6.1 **Term.** This Order Form takes effect on the Order Form Date and continues until expiration or termination of SOW No. 1.

6.2 **Amendment.** This Order Form may be amended only under Section 16.6 of the MSA.
