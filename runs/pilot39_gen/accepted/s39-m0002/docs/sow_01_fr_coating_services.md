# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

## FR Coating Services for Tarpaulin and Curtain Fabric

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of April 6, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") between **Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Prodous Coating Services LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Provider dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**").

## 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Incorporation.** This SOW is a SOW under Section 1.1 of the MSA and forms part of the Agreement. The MSA governs this SOW. Section 1.3 of the MSA applies to any conflict between this SOW and the MSA or any Order Form.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the MSA. In this SOW:

(a) "**Product Lines**" means the Tarpaulin Line and the Curtain Line.

(b) "**Tarpaulin Line**" means Customer's woven polyester and polyester/cotton tarpaulin fabrics, in weights from 8 oz./sq. yd. to 22 oz./sq. yd. and widths up to 126 inches, identified by the Customer style numbers in Schedule A.

(c) "**Curtain Line**" means Customer's woven and knitted polyester curtain, drapery and divider fabrics for commercial and institutional use, in weights from 4 oz./sq. yd. to 12 oz./sq. yd. and widths up to 120 inches, identified by the Customer style numbers in Schedule A.

(d) "**Facility**" means Provider's coating and finishing plant at 1180 Industrial Parkway, Gastonia, North Carolina 28052.

(e) "**Controlled Parameters**" means the parameters listed in Section 4.2.

## 2. SCOPE OF COATING SERVICES

2.1 **Services.** Provider shall perform the following Coating Services on Customer Fabric for the Product Lines:

(a) receipt, inspection and logging of Customer Fabric under Section 3.2 of the MSA;

(b) pre-treatment scouring and drying where Schedule A calls for it;

(c) application of the FR coating or FR finish specified for each style in Schedule A, by knife-over-roll coating (Tarpaulin Line) or pad-dry-cure finishing (Curtain Line);

(d) curing on Provider's tenter frames within the cure profile in Section 4.2;

(e) in-process and final testing under Section 5;

(f) inspection, roll-up, labeling with Customer style and Lot number, and packaging to Customer's packaging standard in Schedule A; and

(g) loading Finished Goods for shipment under Section 6.

2.2 **Facility.** Provider shall perform all Coating Services under this SOW at the Facility. For purposes of Section 2.2 of the MSA, any relocation of Coating Services to another facility requires completion of the requalification testing in Section 5.4 before any production Lot is run at the new facility.

2.3 **Exclusions.** The Coating Services do not include dyeing, printing or lamination of Customer Fabric unless added by change order under Section 2.4 of the MSA.

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS

3.1 **Specifications.** The Specifications for this SOW consist of: (a) this SOW; (b) Schedule A (Style Specifications), which sets out for each style the FR chemistry, target add-on weight, hand, colorfastness and physical property requirements; and (c) the test methods and acceptance criteria in Section 5.

3.2 **FR Performance Standards.** Finished Goods shall meet the following FR performance standards:

(a) **Tarpaulin Line:** CPAI-84, Section 6 (vertical flame test for tent and tarpaulin fabrics), and NFPA 701, Test Method 2; and

(b) **Curtain Line:** NFPA 701, Test Method 1, and, for styles designated "healthcare" in Schedule A, ASTM D6413 with char length not exceeding 4.0 inches and after-flame not exceeding 2.0 seconds.

3.3 **Durability Period.** For purposes of Section 7.2(c) of the MSA, the durability period is: (a) for the Tarpaulin Line, thirty-six (36) months after delivery under normal outdoor exposure; and (b) for the Curtain Line, the earlier of thirty-six (36) months after delivery and twenty-five (25) laundering cycles performed under the care instructions in Schedule A.

3.4 **Restricted Substances.** No FR chemistry or finish applied under this SOW may contain halogenated flame retardants or intentionally added PFAS. Provider shall deliver a current restricted substances declaration for each chemistry in Schedule A before first production use and annually thereafter.

## 4. PROCESS CONTROL

4.1 **Approved Chemistries.** Provider shall use only the FR chemistries, chemical suppliers and product grades identified in Schedule A for each style.

4.2 **Controlled Parameters.** The following are controlled parameters for purposes of Section 2.5 of the MSA:

(a) FR chemistry, chemical supplier and product grade;

(b) coating or finish add-on weight, within a tolerance of ±5% of the Schedule A target;

(c) cure temperature and dwell time, within the cure profile set out in Schedule A for each style;

(d) line speed range for each style; and

(e) bath concentration and pH for pad-dry-cure finishing.

4.3 **Process Records.** Provider shall record each Controlled Parameter for every Lot at the intervals stated in Schedule A and shall keep those records as required by Section 5.3 of the MSA.

## 5. TESTING AND CERTIFICATION

5.1 **Lot Testing.** Provider shall take samples from the beginning, middle and end of each Lot and test them against the FR performance standards in Section 3.2 and the physical properties in Schedule A. For Curtain Line styles, Provider shall also test samples after five (5) laundering cycles.

5.2 **Certificates.** Provider shall deliver with each Lot a certificate of conformance and a test report as required by Section 5.2 of the MSA, signed by Provider's quality manager and identifying the Lot number, style, chemical lot numbers and test results.

5.3 **Third-Party Verification.** Once each calendar quarter, Provider shall submit retained samples from at least three (3) Lots selected by Customer to an independent laboratory accredited under ISO/IEC 17025 for FR testing to the standards in Section 3.2. Customer shall bear the cost of this testing unless any sample fails, in which case Provider shall bear it.

5.4 **Requalification.** Before production at any new facility or after any approved change to a Controlled Parameter, Provider shall run qualification Lots for each affected style and obtain passing independent laboratory results under Section 5.3 for three (3) consecutive qualification Lots.

5.5 **Failed Lots.** Provider shall notify Customer within one (1) business day after any Lot fails any test under this Section 5 and shall not ship that Lot without Customer's written approval. Section 6.2 of the MSA applies to failed Lots.

## 6. LEAD TIMES AND DELIVERY

6.1 **Lead Times.** Provider shall complete the Coating Services and make Finished Goods available for shipment within ten (10) business days after receipt of Customer Fabric for the Tarpaulin Line, and within eight (8) business days for the Curtain Line, for purchase orders within the monthly capacity reservation in Section 6.3.

6.2 **Delivery Terms.** Provider shall deliver Finished Goods FCA the Facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier Customer designates. Title remains with Customer as provided in Section 3.3 of the MSA.

6.3 **Capacity Reservation.** Provider shall reserve coating line capacity of at least 180,000 linear yards per calendar month for the Product Lines combined. Customer shall provide a rolling three (3) month non-binding forecast by the fifth (5th) business day of each month.

6.4 **Late Delivery.** If Provider fails to meet a lead time in Section 6.1 for reasons other than force majeure under Section 16.4 of the MSA, Provider shall grant Customer a credit of two percent (2%) of the fees for the late Lot for each business day of delay, up to twenty percent (20%) of those fees.

## 7. FEES

7.1 **Processing Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the per-linear-yard processing fees set out in Schedule B for each style, which include all chemicals, utilities, labor, testing under Sections 5.1 and 5.2, and packaging materials.

7.2 **Commercial Terms.** The Minimum Annual Commitment, any volume pricing tiers and the Early Termination Fee are set out in the applicable Order Form.

7.3 **Price Adjustment.** Processing fees are fixed for the first Contract Year. Thereafter, Provider may adjust fees once per Contract Year on ninety (90) days' written notice by no more than the percentage change in the U.S. Producer Price Index for Chemicals and Allied Products over the preceding twelve (12) months.

7.4 **Transition Rates.** Transition assistance under Section 13.5(c) of the MSA is charged at $145 per hour for technical personnel and $95 per hour for production personnel.

7.5 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice under Section 4.2 of the MSA, and Customer shall pay under Section 4.3 of the MSA.

## 8. PERSONNEL AND GOVERNANCE

8.1 **Key Personnel.** Provider shall assign a dedicated account manager and a quality manager with at least five (5) years of experience in FR textile finishing. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' notice before replacing either person, except in cases of resignation, illness or termination for cause.

8.2 **Quarterly Reviews.** The Parties shall meet each calendar quarter to review quality data, delivery performance, forecasts and corrective actions.

8.3 **Corrective Action.** For any failed Lot or Customer complaint, Provider shall deliver a written root-cause analysis and corrective action plan within ten (10) business days.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

9.1 **MSA Terms.** Sections 10 and 11 of the MSA apply to this SOW, except as stated in Section 9.2.

9.2 **Indemnity for Coating Services.** With respect to Section 11.3(a) of the MSA, for Claims arising from Coating Services performed under this SOW, Provider's indemnification obligations under Section 10.1 of the MSA shall be subject to the Liability Cap.

9.3 **Insurance.** Provider's insurance under Section 12 of the MSA shall cover the Coating Services under this SOW. Provider's products-completed operations coverage shall remain in force for at least three (3) years after the last delivery of Finished Goods under this SOW.

## 10. TERM

10.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues until expiration or termination of the MSA, unless terminated earlier under Section 13 of the MSA.

10.2 **Schedules.** Schedule A (Style Specifications) and Schedule B (Processing Fees) form part of this SOW. Either Schedule may be updated only by written change order signed by both Parties under Section 2.4 of the MSA.
