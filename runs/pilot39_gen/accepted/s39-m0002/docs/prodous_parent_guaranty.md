# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 2, 2026 by **Prodous Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Beneficiary, as Customer, and **Prodous Coating Services LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, Provider performs FR coating and finishing services for Beneficiary under one or more Statements of Work and Order Forms.

B. Provider is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the MSA.

C. Section 15.1 of the MSA requires Provider to cause Guarantor to deliver this Guaranty to Beneficiary on or before the Effective Date of the MSA.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **MSA Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the MSA.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Guaranty:

(a) "**Agreement**" has the meaning given in Section 1.2 of the MSA, as amended, supplemented or replaced from time to time, and includes each SOW and Order Form.

(b) "**Default**" means any failure by Provider to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when and as required by the Agreement.

(c) "**Demand**" means a written demand on Guarantor under Section 4. A Demand must identify the Default and the Guaranteed Obligation concerned and state the amount claimed or the performance required, to the extent then known.

(d) "**Guaranteed Obligations**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty.** Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the due and punctual payment and performance of all obligations of Provider to Beneficiary under the Agreement, whether existing now or arising later, including Provider's obligations to perform the Services, to remedy nonconforming Finished Goods under Section 6 of the MSA, to indemnify under Section 10.1 of the MSA, and to pay damages, credits, refunds and other amounts due under the Agreement (together, the "**Guaranteed Obligations**").

2.2 **Payment and Performance.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not of collection. Subject to Section 4, if Provider fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation, Guarantor shall, within ten (10) business days after receipt of a Demand, pay the amount due or perform, or cause to be performed, the obligation concerned.

2.3 **Extent of Liability.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed the liability that Provider would have under the Agreement for the same Default. Guarantor may assert any defense, limitation or exclusion that Provider could assert under the Agreement, other than defenses arising from Provider's insolvency, bankruptcy, lack of capacity or lack of authority.

2.4 **Costs of Enforcement.** Guarantor shall pay Beneficiary's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations arising while it remains in effect, as provided in Section 6.

3.2 **No Prior Proceeding.** Beneficiary is not required to bring any action or proceeding against Provider, or to exhaust any other remedy or security, before making a Demand or enforcing this Guaranty.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment by Provider or Guarantor to Beneficiary on a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned in any insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Provider, this Guaranty shall continue to apply, or be reinstated, as to that payment as if it had not been made.

## 4. DEMAND

4.1 **Condition to Liability.** Guarantor is liable for a Guaranteed Obligation only if Beneficiary delivers a Demand to Guarantor within forty-five (45) days after Beneficiary first notifies Provider of the Default giving rise to that Guaranteed Obligation.

4.2 **Multiple Demands.** Beneficiary may make more than one Demand, and a Demand for one Default does not limit any Demand for another Default.

4.3 **Delivery.** Each Demand must be delivered to Guarantor under Section 9. A copy of each Demand shall be sent to Provider at its notice address under the MSA at the same time.

4.4 **Information.** On Guarantor's reasonable request, Beneficiary shall provide copies of the notice of Default given to Provider and reasonable supporting information about the Guaranteed Obligation claimed.

## 5. WAIVERS AND CONSENTS

5.1 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives: (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; (b) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor; (c) notice of any amendment, change order or new SOW or Order Form under the Agreement; and (d) any right to require Beneficiary to proceed first against Provider or any other person.

5.2 **Consents.** Without notice to or consent of Guarantor, and without affecting Guarantor's obligations, Beneficiary and Provider may: (a) amend, supplement or renew the Agreement, or enter into additional SOWs and Order Forms; (b) extend the time for, or waive, Provider's performance of any obligation; and (c) settle or compromise any Guaranteed Obligation with Provider, in which case the Guaranteed Obligation is reduced to the settled amount.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 **Term.** This Guaranty takes effect on its date and continues until all Guaranteed Obligations arising before expiration or termination of the Agreement have been paid and performed in full.

6.2 **Termination on Assignment.** This Guaranty terminates automatically, without further action by any person, on any assignment or transfer by Provider of the Agreement or any SOW that is permitted under the MSA. If the assignment or transfer is of part of the Agreement or of one or more SOWs only, this Guaranty terminates as to the Agreement as a whole.

6.3 **Accrued Obligations.** Subject to Sections 4 and 6.2, expiration of this Guaranty under Section 6.1 does not affect any Demand delivered before expiration.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Beneficiary that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement binding on it; and

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the outstanding membership interests of Provider.

## 8. SUBROGATION

8.1 **Subordination.** Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Provider arising from any payment or performance under this Guaranty until Beneficiary has been paid in full for the Guaranteed Obligation concerned.

8.2 **Amounts Received.** If Guarantor receives any amount in violation of Section 8.1, it shall hold that amount in trust for Beneficiary and promptly pay it to Beneficiary.

## 9. NOTICES

9.1 **Method.** Notices and Demands under this Guaranty must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission followed by courier copy, to the address below or to any other address a party designates by notice.

9.2 **Addresses.**

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

10.3 **Jury Waiver.** EACH OF GUARANTOR AND BENEFICIARY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Assignment.** Beneficiary may assign this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of Customer's rights under the Agreement. Guarantor may not assign this Guaranty or delegate its obligations without Beneficiary's prior written consent.

11.2 **Successors.** This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Beneficiary and its successors and permitted assigns.

11.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Guarantor and Beneficiary. No failure or delay by Beneficiary in exercising any right under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of that right.

11.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

11.5 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
