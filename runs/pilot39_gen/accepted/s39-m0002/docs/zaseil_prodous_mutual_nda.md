# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") between **Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Zaseil**"), and **Prodous Coating Services LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Prodous**"). Zaseil and Prodous are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Zaseil manufactures flame-retardant ("**FR**") industrial fabrics. Prodous operates coating and finishing lines and applies FR chemistries and related finishes to textile substrates.

B. The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible commercial relationship. Under that relationship, Prodous would perform FR coating and finishing services on fabric supplied by Zaseil (the "**Purpose**").

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Representatives disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose. This applies whether the information is disclosed before, on or after the NDA Effective Date and whether it is disclosed orally, visually, in writing, electronically or by delivery of samples. Confidential Information includes:

(a) formulations, recipes, FR chemistries, chemical supplier and product grade information, add-on weights, cure profiles, line speeds, bath concentrations and other process parameters;

(b) fabric constructions, style specifications, test methods, test data and certification results;

(c) pricing, cost, volume, forecast, customer and supplier information;

(d) plant layouts, equipment capabilities, capacity data and quality system documentation; and

(e) business plans, financial information and the fact and content of the Parties' discussions about the Purpose.

1.2 **Marking.** Information disclosed in writing or electronically should be marked "confidential" or with a similar legend. Information disclosed orally or visually should be identified as confidential at the time of disclosure. However, information is Confidential Information without marking or identification if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 **Representatives.** "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their respective directors, officers, managers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors.

1.4 **Affiliate.** "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 **Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is received by the Receiving Party without restriction from a third party that is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, under any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

2.2 **Combinations.** Confidential Information is not within an exclusion in Section 2.1 merely because individual features of it are within an exclusion. A combination of features falls within an exclusion only if the combination itself does.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any person except to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.4 **Samples and Materials.** The Receiving Party shall not analyze, reverse engineer, decompile or chemically deconstruct any fabric, coating, chemical or other sample provided by the Disclosing Party, except to the extent the Disclosing Party authorizes in writing for the Purpose.

3.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall also reasonably cooperate to limit the effects of that use or disclosure.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 **Required by Law.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or the rules of a securities exchange. Where lawful, the Receiving Party shall first give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation so the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy.

4.2 **Scope.** The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for that portion.

## 5. OWNERSHIP AND NO LICENSE

5.1 **Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

5.2 **No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, trade secret, copyright, trademark or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

5.3 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. The Disclosing Party has no liability arising from the Receiving Party's use of it, except as may be agreed in a definitive agreement.

## 6. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

6.1 **No Commitment.** Neither this Agreement nor any disclosure under it obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either Party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time by written notice.

6.2 **Independent Activities.** Subject to its obligations under this Agreement, each Party may continue to develop, acquire or market products and services similar to those discussed under the Purpose.

## 7. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

7.1 **On Request.** Within fifteen (15) days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession or control, including all copies, extracts and samples. On request, it shall certify in writing that it has done so.

7.2 **Retained Copies.** The Receiving Party may retain copies to the extent required by law or by its bona fide document retention policies, and copies held in routine electronic backups that are not readily accessible. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

## 8. TERM

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period beginning on the NDA Effective Date and ending two (2) years after the NDA Effective Date. Either Party may end that period earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice.

8.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 3, 4 and 7 continue for three (3) years after the end of the period in Section 8.1. For trade secrets, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 **Equitable Relief.** Unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief without posting bond, in addition to its other remedies.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

10.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by one of the following methods:

(a) by hand;

(b) by nationally recognized overnight courier; or

(c) by email with confirmation of transmission followed by courier copy.

Notices must be sent to the addresses on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

10.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent. However, either Party may assign this Agreement without consent, on written notice, to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, whether by merger, sale of assets or otherwise. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

10.5 **Export Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with applicable U.S. export control laws in connection with any technical data received under this Agreement.

10.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

10.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

10.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
