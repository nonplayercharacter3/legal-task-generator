# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**MSA**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Zaseil Technical Fabrics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Prodous Coating Services LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer manufactures flame-retardant ("**FR**") industrial fabrics, including tarpaulin, curtain and barrier fabrics, for industrial, commercial and institutional markets.

B. Provider operates coating and finishing lines and applies FR chemistries and related finishes to textile substrates.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to perform FR coating and finishing services on fabric that Customer supplies, as described in one or more Statements of Work and Order Forms, on the terms of this MSA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT; DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Statements of Work and Order Forms.** Provider shall perform the services described in each statement of work that references this MSA and is signed by both Parties (each, a "**SOW**"). Commercial terms for the Services, including pricing, volumes, the Minimum Annual Commitment and the Early Termination Fee, may be set out in one or more order forms that reference this MSA and are signed by both Parties (each, an "**Order Form**").

1.2 **Agreement.** This MSA, each SOW and each Order Form together form the "**Agreement**." Each SOW and each Order Form is governed by this MSA.

1.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict among the documents that form the Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: first, the SOW (only provisions expressly modifying an identified MSA section); then this MSA; then the Order Form.

1.4 **Definitions.** In the Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**Change of Control**" means, as to a Party, (i) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction in which that Party is a constituent entity, (ii) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of that Party's voting securities, or (iii) the sale of all or substantially all of that Party's assets or of the business to which the Agreement relates.

(c) "**Claim**" means any claim, demand, suit, action or proceeding brought by a third party.

(d) "**Coating Services**" means the application of FR coatings, finishes and treatments to Customer Fabric, together with curing, inspection, testing, packaging and related services described in a SOW.

(e) "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

(f) "**Customer Fabric**" means greige or pre-treated fabric and other materials that Customer or its designee delivers to Provider for processing.

(g) "**Early Termination Fee**" means the amount specified as the Early Termination Fee in the applicable Order Form.

(h) "**Finished Goods**" means Customer Fabric after Provider has performed the Coating Services on it.

(i) "**Guarantor**" means Prodous Industrial Holdings, Inc., the parent of Provider.

(j) "**Lot**" means a quantity of Customer Fabric identified by a single lot number and processed as a single production run.

(k) "**Minimum Annual Commitment**" means the minimum amount of fees for Coating Services that Customer commits to purchase in each Contract Year, as specified in the applicable Order Form.

(l) "**Services**" means the Coating Services and any other services Provider performs under a SOW.

(m) "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications, FR performance standards, test methods and acceptance criteria set out in or incorporated into a SOW.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the Agreement, the Specifications, applicable law and good industry practice for the FR coating of technical textiles.

2.2 **Facilities.** Provider shall perform the Coating Services only at the facilities identified in the applicable SOW. Provider shall not relocate any Coating Services to another facility without at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to Customer and successful completion of any requalification testing that the SOW requires.

2.3 **Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Coating Services without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its permitted subcontractors as if they were its own.

2.4 **Changes.** Either Party may propose changes to a SOW in writing. No change is binding unless set out in a written change order or amendment signed by both Parties.

2.5 **Process Changes.** Provider shall not change any FR chemistry, chemical supplier, coating add-on weight, cure profile, line speed range or other process parameter identified in a SOW as a controlled parameter without Customer's prior written approval.

## 3. ORDERS, DELIVERY AND TITLE

3.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue purchase orders for Coating Services under the applicable SOW and Order Form. Provider shall accept or reject each purchase order within three (3) business days after receipt. Any purchase order consistent with the applicable SOW and Order Form is deemed accepted if not rejected within that period. Pre-printed terms on any purchase order or acknowledgment have no effect.

3.2 **Customer Fabric.** Customer shall deliver Customer Fabric to Provider's facility in accordance with the applicable SOW. Provider shall inspect Customer Fabric on receipt and notify Customer of any visible damage or nonconformity within five (5) business days.

3.3 **Title and Risk.** Title to Customer Fabric and Finished Goods remains with Customer at all times. Risk of loss of or damage to Customer Fabric and Finished Goods while in Provider's possession, custody or control rests with Provider. Provider shall keep Customer Fabric and Finished Goods segregated, identified as Customer's property, and free of all liens and encumbrances arising through Provider.

3.4 **Delivery.** Provider shall deliver Finished Goods in accordance with the lead times and delivery terms set out in the applicable SOW.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set out in the applicable SOW and Order Form. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay except for taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

4.2 **Invoices.** Provider shall invoice Customer upon shipment of each Lot of Finished Goods. Each invoice shall identify the applicable SOW, purchase order and Lot number.

4.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount that it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Provider of the dispute and its basis before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** If an Order Form specifies a Minimum Annual Commitment, any shortfall for a Contract Year shall be calculated and invoiced as set out in that Order Form.

## 5. QUALITY, TESTING AND RECORDS

5.1 **Quality System.** Provider shall maintain a documented quality management system for the Coating Services consistent with ISO 9001 or an equivalent standard.

5.2 **Testing.** Provider shall test each Lot of Finished Goods in accordance with the test methods in the Specifications, which may include NFPA 701, ASTM D6413 and CPAI-84 as applicable, and shall deliver a certificate of conformance and test report with each Lot.

5.3 **Records and Retained Samples.** Provider shall keep complete batch records, chemical lot records, process parameter logs and test data for each Lot, and shall retain physical samples of each Lot, for at least seven (7) years after shipment.

5.4 **Audit.** On ten (10) business days' notice, Customer and its designees may audit Provider's facilities, processes and records relating to the Services during normal business hours.

## 6. ACCEPTANCE AND DEFECTIVE GOODS

6.1 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test Finished Goods within sixty (60) days after delivery. Payment for Finished Goods is not acceptance.

6.2 **Nonconformity.** If any Finished Goods fail to conform to the Specifications or the warranties in Section 7, Customer may notify Provider in writing. Provider shall, at Customer's option and at Provider's cost: (a) reprocess the affected Finished Goods so that they conform; (b) replace the affected Customer Fabric at its replacement cost and reprocess it; or (c) credit or refund the fees paid for the affected Lot. These remedies are in addition to Customer's other rights under the Agreement.

6.3 **Latent Defects.** Section 6.1 does not limit Customer's rights for latent defects, including loss of FR performance that is not reasonably discoverable by inspection at delivery.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full authority to enter into and perform the Agreement, and that the Agreement is a valid and binding obligation enforceable against it.

7.2 **Services.** Provider warrants that, for twenty-four (24) months after delivery: (a) the Finished Goods will conform to the Specifications; (b) the Coating Services will be performed in a workmanlike manner using materials of good quality; (c) the FR coatings applied will meet the FR performance standards in the Specifications for the durability period stated in the applicable SOW; and (d) the Coating Services will not introduce any substance restricted under applicable law, including REACH, California Proposition 65 and applicable PFAS restrictions, except as disclosed in and permitted by the Specifications.

7.3 **Compliance.** Provider warrants that it will hold and maintain all permits, licenses and registrations required to perform the Services, including environmental permits for its coating lines.

7.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THE AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, materials, processes and know-how.

8.2 **Customer Property.** Customer owns all Specifications, formulations, recipes and process parameters that Customer provides or that are developed specifically for Customer under a SOW ("**Customer Process Information**"), and all Finished Goods. Provider shall use Customer Process Information solely to perform the Services for Customer.

8.3 **License.** Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Provider know-how embodied in the Finished Goods to the extent necessary to use, sell and support the Finished Goods.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other Party's confidential information disclosed in connection with the Agreement with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9. Customer Process Information is Customer's confidential information.

9.2 **Exceptions.** This Section 9 does not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is received without restriction from a third party entitled to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's confidential information. A receiving Party may disclose confidential information as required by law, after giving prompt notice where lawful.

9.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 continue for five (5) years after expiration or termination of the Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and customers from and against all Claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), arising out of or relating to: (a) bodily injury, including death, or damage to real or tangible personal property, to the extent caused by the Services, the Finished Goods as processed by Provider, or any failure of the FR coatings applied by Provider to meet the Specifications; (b) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct; or (c) Provider's violation of applicable law.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against all Claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), to the extent arising out of: (a) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct; or (b) any allegation that Customer Process Information infringes a third party's intellectual property rights.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any Claim, provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense and settlement of the Claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, but may not settle any Claim that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate with its own counsel at its own expense.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to the Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

11.2 **Liability Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to the Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable under the applicable SOW in the 12 months preceding the claim (the "**Liability Cap**").

11.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) Provider's indemnification obligations under Section 10.1;

(b) either Party's breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality);

(c) either Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct;

(d) Provider's liability for loss of or damage to Customer Fabric or Finished Goods while in Provider's possession, custody or control; or

(e) Customer's obligation to pay fees properly due under the Agreement.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 **Coverage.** Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; (b) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $10,000,000; (c) property insurance, including bailee's customers coverage, covering Customer Fabric and Finished Goods at full replacement value; (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and (e) pollution legal liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

12.2 **Requirements.** The policies in Section 12.1(a) and (b) shall name Customer as an additional insured. Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance on request and shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 13. TERM AND TERMINATION

13.1 **Term.** This MSA begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) Contract Years, and then renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

13.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate the Agreement or any SOW by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

13.3 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate the Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.4 **Termination of SOWs.** Termination of this MSA terminates all SOWs and Order Forms then in effect, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

13.5 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Provider shall: (a) complete any Lots in process if Customer so requests, on the terms of the Agreement; (b) return all Customer Fabric, Finished Goods and Customer Process Information within fifteen (15) days; and (c) provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days at the rates in the applicable SOW. Customer shall pay for Services properly performed through the effective date of termination.

13.6 **Survival.** Sections 3.3, 5.3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13.5, 13.6, 15 and 16 survive expiration or termination.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **By Customer.** Customer may not assign or transfer the Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without Provider's prior written consent. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14.1 is void.

14.2 **By Provider.** Provider may assign or transfer the Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, to any person without Customer's consent, upon written notice to Customer.

14.3 **Change of Control of Customer.** A Change of Control of Customer is deemed an assignment by Customer requiring Provider's prior written consent under Section 14.1. If Customer undergoes a Change of Control without that consent, Provider may terminate the Agreement and all SOWs by written notice to Customer, and Customer shall pay Provider the Early Termination Fee within thirty (30) days after the effective date of termination.

14.4 **Successors.** The Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. PARENT GUARANTY

15.1 **Delivery.** On or before the Effective Date, Provider shall cause the Guarantor to execute and deliver to Customer a guaranty of Provider's obligations under the Agreement in the form agreed by the Parties (the "**Parent Guaranty**").

15.2 **Information.** Provider shall promptly notify Customer of any event that would reasonably be expected to materially impair the Guarantor's ability to perform the Parent Guaranty.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Governing Law.** The Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to the Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Notices.** Notices under the Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission followed by courier copy, to the addresses set out on the signature page or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

16.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, natural disaster, war, terrorism, epidemic, or governmental action, provided it gives prompt notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event affecting Provider continues for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may obtain substitute Coating Services from a third party during that period and any resulting purchases count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment.

16.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in the Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

16.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** The Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

16.7 **Severability.** If any provision of the Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

16.8 **Equitable Relief.** A breach of Section 8 or Section 9 may cause irreparable harm for which damages are inadequate, and the non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond, in addition to its other remedies.

16.9 **Counterparts.** The Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
