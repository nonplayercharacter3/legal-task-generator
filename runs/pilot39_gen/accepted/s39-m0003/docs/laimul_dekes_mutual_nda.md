# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**between**

**LAIMUL BAKING HOLDINGS, INC.**

**and**

**DEKES FAMILY BAKERIES, LLC**

**Dated as of March 16, 2026**

---

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Laimul**"), and Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Dekes**"). Laimul and Dekes are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Dekes owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of Hearthline Bread Co., a Delaware corporation ("**Hearthline**"), which operates a commercial wholesale bakery that manufactures sliced bread, buns and par-baked rolls for grocery chains.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Laimul would acquire Hearthline (the "**Transaction**"). In connection with that evaluation, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium and whether disclosed before, on or after the Effective Date, that is disclosed or made available by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party or its Representatives in connection with the Transaction, including: (a) financial statements, forecasts, budgets and working-capital information; (b) customer and supplier lists, contract terms, pricing, rebates and sales volumes; (c) recipes, formulations, production processes, line specifications and yield data; (d) food safety, quality assurance and regulatory records, including audit and inspection reports; (e) employee and labor information; and (f) all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing. Information concerning Hearthline disclosed by or on behalf of Dekes or Hearthline is Confidential Information of Dekes. The fact that the Parties are discussing the Transaction, and the terms and status of those discussions (together, "**Transaction Information**"), is Confidential Information of each Party.

"**Disclosing Party**" means the Party disclosing Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means the Party receiving Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, officers, employees, members, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors and actual or prospective lenders and financing sources, in each case who need to know Confidential Information for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating or financing the Transaction. In the case of Dekes, Representatives include Hearthline and its directors, officers and employees.

**1.2 Construction.** The word "including" means "including without limitation." Section headings are for convenience only.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

**2.1 Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party or its Representatives, provided that source is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party with respect to that information; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

**3.1 Use Restriction.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, financing and, if applicable, consummating the Transaction, and for no other purpose.

**3.2 Nondisclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any Person other than its Representatives. The Receiving Party shall inform each Representative to whom it discloses Confidential Information of its confidential nature and shall direct that Representative to treat it in accordance with this Agreement.

**3.3 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

**3.4 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, other than a Representative that has entered into a separate confidentiality agreement directly with the Disclosing Party covering the Confidential Information.

**3.5 Competitively Sensitive Information.** Because Laimul and Hearthline both operate in the commercial baking industry, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to designate any customer-specific pricing, margin, bid or capacity information as "Clean Team Only." Information so designated may be disclosed only to those Representatives of the Receiving Party who are not directly responsible for pricing, sales or customer account management, and to its outside counsel and advisors, in each case in accordance with any clean team protocol the Parties agree in writing.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

**4.1 Legally Required Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek, at its own expense, a protective order or other appropriate remedy. The Receiving Party shall reasonably cooperate with those efforts.

**4.2 Limited Disclosure.** If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to it.

**4.3 Regulatory Examinations.** Notice under Section 4.1 is not required for disclosure to a banking, securities or other regulatory authority in the course of a routine examination not specifically targeting the Disclosing Party or the Transaction.

## 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

**5.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten Business Days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it has terminated discussions regarding the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall, at its option, return to the Disclosing Party or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall confirm in writing, signed by an officer, that it has done so. For purposes of this Section 5.1, "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

**5.2 Retained Copies.** Notwithstanding Section 5.1, the Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention or compliance policies, and (b) copies stored in automatic electronic archiving or back-up systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**6.1 No Representations or Warranties.** Each Party acknowledges that neither the Disclosing Party nor any of its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information under this Agreement. Only those representations and warranties made in a definitive written agreement for the Transaction, when and if executed, and subject to the limitations and restrictions specified in it, will have any legal effect.

**6.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Unless and until a definitive written agreement regarding the Transaction has been executed, neither Party is under any legal obligation of any kind with respect to the Transaction by virtue of this Agreement, except for the matters specifically agreed in this Agreement. Either Party may terminate discussions regarding the Transaction at any time, for any reason, on written notice to the other.

**6.3 No License.** No license or other right in any Confidential Information, or in any patent, trademark, trade secret, recipe or other intellectual property, is granted by this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information as set out in Section 3.1.

## 7. NON-SOLICITATION

**7.1 Employees.** For a period of 12 months after the Effective Date, Laimul shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment or hire any officer, plant manager or director-level employee of Hearthline with whom Laimul first had contact, or of whom Laimul first became aware, in connection with the Transaction. This Section 7.1 does not restrict (a) general solicitations of employment not specifically directed at Hearthline employees, including through search firms, or the hiring of any Person who responds to them, or (b) the solicitation or hiring of any Person whose employment with Hearthline ended at least six months before the solicitation.

**7.2 Customers and Suppliers.** Until the earlier of the termination of discussions regarding the Transaction and 12 months after the Effective Date, Laimul shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, initiate contact with any customer, supplier or landlord of Hearthline concerning the Transaction or Hearthline's business without the prior written consent of Dekes. This Section 7.2 does not restrict contacts made in the ordinary course of Laimul's business unrelated to the Transaction.

## 8. TERM

**8.1 Term.** This Agreement remains in effect for two years after the Effective Date. The obligations under Section 3 with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, including recipes and formulations, continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 9. REMEDIES

**9.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that money damages may not be a sufficient remedy for any breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive or other equitable relief as a remedy for any breach or threatened breach, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond. Those remedies are in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

**9.2 Costs.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party or its Representatives has breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to any conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, addressed as follows (or to such other address as a Party designates by notice):

If to Laimul: Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

If to Dekes: Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, Attention: Managing Member.

**10.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid or unenforceable provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law.

**10.7 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
