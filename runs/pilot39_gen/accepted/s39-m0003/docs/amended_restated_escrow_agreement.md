# AMENDED AND RESTATED ESCROW AGREEMENT

**among**

**LAIMUL BAKING HOLDINGS, INC.**
(as Buyer)

**DEKES FAMILY BAKERIES, LLC**
(as Seller)

**and**

**ASHGROVE TRUST COMPANY, N.A.**
(as Escrow Agent)

**Dated as of October 6, 2026**

---

This AMENDED AND RESTATED ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 6, 2026, by and among Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Ashgrove Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 1, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer purchased from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of Hearthline Bread Co., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurred on July 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**").

B. On the Closing Date, Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent entered into an Escrow Agreement (the "**Original Escrow Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer deposited with the Escrow Agent (i) $3,600,000 as the Indemnity Escrow Amount and (ii) $750,000 as the Adjustment Escrow Amount, in each case under Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

C. Under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered its Closing Statement to Buyer on August 25, 2026.

D. On September 28, 2026, Buyer delivered to Seller a written notice disputing Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital in the Closing Statement (the "**Buyer Dispute Notice**"). In the Buyer Dispute Notice, Buyer asserts that Closing Net Working Capital is less than Target Net Working Capital by $612,000.

E. As of the date of this Agreement, no Party has accepted the Closing Statement. Buyer reserves all of its rights under the Purchase Agreement with respect to the Closing Statement, the Buyer Dispute Notice and Closing Net Working Capital.

F. The Parties and the Escrow Agent wish to amend and restate the Original Escrow Agreement in its entirety to (i) update the investment, account and notice provisions, (ii) set out the procedures for releasing the Escrow Funds, and (iii) revise the Escrow Agent's fee schedule.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties and the Escrow Agent agree that the Original Escrow Agreement is amended and restated in its entirety as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, together, the Indemnity Escrow Fund and the Adjustment Escrow Fund.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a final determination of the Independent Accountant under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement, together with a certificate of counsel to the Party presenting it confirming that it is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Authorized Representative**" means each individual listed for a Party on Schedule 1, as that Party may update by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

**1.2 Construction.** Section headings are for convenience only. The word "including" means "including without limitation."

## 2. APPOINTMENT; ESCROW ACCOUNTS

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller confirm the appointment of the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Indemnity Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Agent confirms that it holds $3,600,000, deposited on the Closing Date, in a segregated account designated "Laimul/Dekes – Indemnity Escrow" (together with all interest and earnings on it, the "**Indemnity Escrow Fund**"). No disbursements have been made from the Indemnity Escrow Fund as of the date of this Agreement.

**2.3 Adjustment Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Agent confirms that it holds $750,000, deposited on the Closing Date, in a segregated account designated "Laimul/Dekes – Adjustment Escrow" (together with all interest and earnings on it, the "**Adjustment Escrow Fund**"). No disbursements have been made from the Adjustment Escrow Fund as of the date of this Agreement.

**2.4 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Ashgrove Government Obligations Money Market Fund (Institutional Class), or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any investment loss incurred in following this Section 2.4. Investment earnings form part of the applicable Escrow Fund.

**2.5 Tax Reporting.** For tax reporting purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds, and all interest and earnings on them shall be reported as income of Buyer, until disbursed. Each Party shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the date of this Agreement.

## 3. DISBURSEMENTS

**3.1 Indemnity Escrow Fund.**

(a) The Escrow Agent shall disburse amounts from the Indemnity Escrow Fund only (i) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, or (ii) in accordance with a Final Order delivered by either Party, with a copy to the other Party.

(b) Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to release the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund at the times and in the amounts provided in Section 9.9 of the Purchase Agreement. Any amount retained for pending Actions under Section 9.9 of the Purchase Agreement shall remain in the Indemnity Escrow Fund until released under Section 3.1(a).

(c) If Buyer delivers a Claim Notice to Seller under Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer shall concurrently deliver a copy to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall have no obligation to review or act on any Claim Notice, other than to retain it in its records.

**3.2 Adjustment Escrow Fund.**

(a) The Escrow Agent shall disburse amounts from the Adjustment Escrow Fund only (i) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, (ii) in accordance with a Final Order delivered by either Party, with a copy to the other Party, or (iii) in accordance with Section 3.2(b).

(b) Either Party may deliver to the Escrow Agent, with a concurrent copy to the other Party, a written demand (a "**Release Demand**") for disbursement of all or part of the Adjustment Escrow Fund. The Release Demand shall state the amount demanded and certify that Closing Net Working Capital has been finally determined under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement. If the other Party does not deliver to the Escrow Agent a written objection to the Release Demand (an "**Objection Notice**") within ten Business Days after the Escrow Agent's receipt of the Release Demand, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount demanded to the demanding Party on the next Business Day. If an Objection Notice is timely delivered, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amount demanded until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

(c) Within five Business Days after Closing Net Working Capital is finally determined under Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions directing the disbursements required by Section 2.4(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

**3.3 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account of the payee set out on Schedule 1, or to another account designated by that payee in writing and confirmed by call-back under Section 3.4.

**3.4 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any Joint Written Instructions, Release Demand or change in wire instructions, the Escrow Agent shall confirm it by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the relevant Party, other than the individual who signed the communication. The Escrow Agent may rely on the confirmation of any Authorized Representative.

**3.5 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any disbursement any amount required to be withheld under applicable tax law.

## 4. THE ESCROW AGENT

**4.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement and are purely ministerial. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement, is not bound by it and is not charged with knowledge of its terms, even where this Agreement refers to it.

**4.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely, and is protected in acting or refraining from acting, on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it in good faith believes to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

**4.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent of its own gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**4.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, except to the extent caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, except that a Party whose breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement gave rise to the indemnity obligation shall bear the full amount. This Section 4.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**4.5 Disputes.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or claims regarding any Escrow Fund, it may refuse to act until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an interpleader action in any court specified in Section 6.3 and deposit the disputed funds with that court, after which it is released from all further obligations with respect to those funds.

**4.6 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice, and is fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**4.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving 30 days' written notice to the Parties. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving 30 days' written notice. Resignation or removal takes effect when a successor escrow agent, appointed by Joint Written Instructions, accepts the appointment in writing and receives the Escrow Funds. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court specified in Section 6.3 to appoint a successor.

**4.8 Merger or Consolidation.** Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

## 5. FEES AND EXPENSES

**5.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set out in the revised fee schedule attached as Schedule 2, which replaces the fee schedule attached to the Original Escrow Agreement as of the date of this Agreement. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

**5.2 Lien.** The Escrow Agent may deduct unpaid fees and expenses that are more than 30 days past due from the Escrow Funds, after giving five Business Days' written notice to the Parties. Any Party whose share is so deducted shall promptly reimburse the other Party for any amount deducted from funds that would otherwise be payable to that other Party.

## 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**6.1 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all of the Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms, except for the provisions that by their terms survive termination.

**6.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to any conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**6.3 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each of the Parties and the Escrow Agent irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH OF THE PARTIES AND THE ESCROW AGENT IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**6.4 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, addressed as follows (or to such other address as a party designates by notice):

If to Buyer: Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

If to Seller: Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, Attention: Managing Member.

If to the Escrow Agent: Ashgrove Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration – Laimul/Dekes.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only on actual receipt.

**6.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**6.6 Assignment.** No Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party and the Escrow Agent, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any permitted assignee of Buyer under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement, on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**6.7 Effect of Amendment and Restatement.** This Agreement amends and restates the Original Escrow Agreement in its entirety with effect from the date of this Agreement. Actions taken and deposits made under the Original Escrow Agreement before that date remain valid and are treated as taken or made under this Agreement.

**6.8 USA PATRIOT Act.** The Parties acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is required by federal law to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account, and each Party shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**6.9 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**6.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
