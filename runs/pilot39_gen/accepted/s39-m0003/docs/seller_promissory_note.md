# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$6,000,000**

**Dated: July 1, 2026**

**Maker:** Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation

**Holder:** Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company

---

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), or its registered assigns, the principal sum of Six Million Dollars ($6,000,000), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(d) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 1, 2026 between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Maker purchased from Holder all of the issued and outstanding shares of Hearthline Bread Co., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus 2.0% per annum.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1.

"**Interest Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning September 30, 2026, and the Maturity Date.

"**Interest Rate**" means 6.0% per annum, computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

"**Losses**" means out-of-pocket damages, liabilities, judgments, fines and penalties actually paid by Maker or the Company to a Person other than Maker, the Company or any of their Affiliates, excluding attorneys' fees, diminution in value, consequential damages and costs of any product withdrawal or recall.

"**Maturity Date**" means July 1, 2030.

"**Principal Installment Date**" means each of July 1, 2027, July 1, 2028, July 1, 2029 and the Maturity Date.

"**Senior Debt**" means all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries under any credit agreement, loan agreement or similar instrument for borrowed money with one or more banks or other institutional lenders, as in effect from time to time, including all principal, interest, fees, expenses and reimbursement obligations under it.

"**Senior Lender**" means each holder of Senior Debt, or any agent acting on its behalf.

## 2. PRINCIPAL AND INTEREST

**2.1 Principal.** The original principal amount of this Note is $6,000,000. Maker shall repay the principal of this Note in four equal installments of $1,500,000 each, payable on each Principal Installment Date. The entire unpaid principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

**2.2 Interest.** Interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the date of this Note at the Interest Rate. Accrued interest is payable in arrears on each Interest Payment Date.

**2.3 Default Interest.** While any Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid principal balance and any overdue interest bear interest at the Default Rate, payable on demand.

**2.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any interest paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the principal balance of this Note or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. PREPAYMENT

**3.1 Voluntary Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note, in whole or in part, at any time without premium or penalty, on at least five Business Days' prior written notice to Holder. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid.

**3.2 Application of Prepayments.** Partial prepayments shall be applied to the principal installments in the inverse order of their maturity.

## 4. PAYMENTS; SET-OFF

**4.1 Method of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing at least five Business Days before the payment date. If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is payable on the next Business Day, and interest accrues for the additional days. Payments shall be applied first to fees and expenses then due under this Note, then to accrued interest, and then to principal.

**4.2 Set-Off.** Subject to Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off against any payment of principal or interest due under this Note the amount of any Losses (as defined in this Note) for which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement. Maker shall give Holder written notice of any set-off at least ten Business Days before the affected payment date, identifying the related Claim Notice and the amount set off. Any amount properly set off under this Section 4.2 is treated as paid on this Note on the date it would otherwise have been due and reduces the amounts then due in the order set forth in Section 4.1.

**4.3 Taxes.** Maker may deduct and withhold from any payment under this Note any amount required to be withheld under applicable tax law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated as paid to Holder.

## 5. SUBORDINATION

**5.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, on the terms of this Section 5.

**5.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under any Senior Debt and Maker has received written notice of it from a Senior Lender, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

**5.3 Payment Blockage.** While any default described in Section 5.2 is continuing, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note, other than payment by set-off under Section 4.2. Any payment Holder receives in violation of this Section 5.3 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lenders and promptly turned over to them.

**5.4 Insolvency.** In any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation or similar proceeding of Maker, the Senior Debt shall be paid in full in cash before Holder receives any payment under this Note.

**5.5 Subordination Agreement.** On request of Maker, Holder shall execute a subordination agreement with any Senior Lender on terms customary for seller subordinated debt and consistent with this Section 5.

## 6. COVENANTS OF MAKER

**6.1 Financial Information and Access.** So long as any principal of this Note remains outstanding, Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within 30 days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited consolidated balance sheet of Maker and its subsidiaries, including the Company, as of the end of that month, and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and for the fiscal year to date, certified by the chief financial officer of Maker as fairly presenting in all material respects the financial position of Maker and its subsidiaries;

(b) within 30 days after the end of each calendar month, customer-level sales reports for the Company for that month, showing net sales, unit volumes and average net pricing by customer and by product category;

(c) within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Maker and its subsidiaries for that fiscal year; and

(d) on five Business Days' prior written notice, access during normal business hours to the books, records and personnel of Maker and the Company for the purpose of inspecting and copying them.

**6.2 Notice of Default.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time would become an Event of Default, within five Business Days after an executive officer of Maker becomes aware of it.

**6.3 Existence.** Maker shall maintain its corporate existence and good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**6.4 Restricted Payments.** While any Event of Default is continuing, Maker shall not declare or pay any dividend or other distribution on its capital stock, other than dividends payable solely in its capital stock.

## 7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten Business Days after its due date, other than a payment that is prohibited by Section 5.3;

(b) Maker fails to perform any covenant in Section 6 and the failure continues for 30 days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker or the Company (i) commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iii) admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due;

(d) an involuntary case is commenced against Maker or the Company under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law and is not dismissed within 60 days;

(e) any Senior Lender accelerates the maturity of Senior Debt in an aggregate principal amount exceeding $5,000,000; or

(f) Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the capital stock or assets of the Company to any Person other than an Affiliate of Maker.

**7.2 Acceleration.** Subject to Section 5, (a) on the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c) or (d), the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with accrued and unpaid interest, becomes immediately due and payable without notice or other action by Holder; and (b) on the occurrence and during the continuance of any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with accrued and unpaid interest, immediately due and payable.

**7.3 Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Section 5, the rights and remedies of Holder under this Note are cumulative and in addition to any other rights and remedies available at law or in equity.

**7.4 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. WAIVERS

**8.1 Waiver of Presentment.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest.

**8.2 No Waiver by Holder.** No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note impairs that right or operates as a waiver of it. A waiver on one occasion is not a waiver on any other occasion.

## 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

If to Maker: Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.

If to Holder: Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, Attention: Managing Member.

**9.2 Assignment and Transfer.** Holder may assign or transfer this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker, on written notice to Maker identifying the assignee. Each assignee succeeds to all rights of Holder under this Note to the extent of the interest assigned. Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder.

**9.3 Register.** Maker shall maintain a register of the name and address of each holder of this Note and the principal amount held by it. Maker may treat each Person whose name is recorded in the register as the owner of the corresponding interest in this Note for all purposes.

**9.4 Lost or Destroyed Note.** On receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note, and of an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor.

**9.5 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.
