# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**by and between**

**DEKES FAMILY BAKERIES, LLC**
(as Seller)

**and**

**LAIMUL BAKING HOLDINGS, INC.**
(as Buyer)

**with respect to all of the issued and outstanding shares of**

**HEARTHLINE BREAD CO.**

**Dated as of July 1, 2026**

---

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2026, by and between Dekes Family Bakeries, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Laimul Baking Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock (the "**Shares**") of Hearthline Bread Co., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company operates a commercial wholesale bakery that manufactures sliced bread, buns and par-baked rolls for grocery chains (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. The parties are executing this Agreement and consummating the transactions it contemplates on the same date.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay and deliver the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller, an amount equal to $38,150,000, being the Purchase Price minus the Indemnity Escrow Amount, the Adjustment Escrow Amount and the principal amount of the Seller Note;

(b) to the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $3,600,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"), to be held in a segregated account (the "**Indemnity Escrow Fund**") and disbursed in accordance with Article 9 and the Escrow Agreement;

(c) to the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $750,000 (the "**Adjustment Escrow Amount**"), to be held in a segregated account (the "**Adjustment Escrow Fund**") and disbursed in accordance with Section 2.4 and the Escrow Agreement; and

(d) to Seller, the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer, in the original principal amount of $6,000,000.

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the deduction and withholding was made.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within 60 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a written statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's good-faith calculation of Closing Net Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting documentation. Buyer shall give Seller and its representatives reasonable access to the books, records and personnel of the Company for that purpose.

(b) *Notice of Disagreement.* If Buyer disagrees with any item in the Closing Statement, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written notice (a "**Notice of Disagreement**") within 30 days after Seller's delivery of the Closing Statement. The Notice of Disagreement shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for Buyer's position.

(c) *Final and Binding Effect.* If Buyer does not deliver a Notice of Disagreement within the period set forth in Section 2.4(b), the Closing Statement and Seller's calculation of Closing Net Working Capital shall be final and binding on the parties. Any item not disputed in a timely Notice of Disagreement shall be final and binding.

(d) *Resolution of Disputes.* Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days after delivery of a timely Notice of Disagreement to resolve the disputed items. Any items not resolved within that period shall be submitted to the Independent Accountant, who shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator, shall consider only the disputed items, and shall determine each disputed item within the range of values asserted by the parties. The Independent Accountant's determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the extent each prevails on the disputed items.

(e) *Payment of Adjustment.* Within five Business Days after Closing Net Working Capital is finally determined:

(i) if Closing Net Working Capital is less than Target Net Working Capital, Buyer and Seller shall jointly instruct the Escrow Agent to pay Buyer the amount of the shortfall from the Adjustment Escrow Fund and, to the extent the shortfall exceeds the Adjustment Escrow Fund, Seller shall pay the excess to Buyer; and

(ii) if Closing Net Working Capital is equal to or greater than Target Net Working Capital, Buyer shall pay Seller the amount of any excess, up to the Adjustment Escrow Amount;

and in each case Buyer and Seller shall jointly instruct the Escrow Agent to release any balance remaining in the Adjustment Escrow Fund to Seller.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures simultaneously with the execution of this Agreement on July 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing is deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller's Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank; (b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (d) payoff letters for all Indebtedness for borrowed money; (e) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and (f) the certificate described in Section 7.2(d).

**3.3 Buyer's Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the payments described in Section 2.2(a) through (c); (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement and in any certificate delivered under it shall survive the Closing as follows: (i) the Fundamental Representations shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations; (ii) the representations and warranties in Section 4.17 (Taxes) shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations; and (iii) all other representations and warranties shall survive until the date that is six months after the Closing Date.

(b) The covenants and agreements of the parties to be performed after the Closing shall survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

(c) No claim for indemnification under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) may be brought or maintained unless the Indemnified Party has commenced an Action with respect to such claim in a court specified in Section 11.7 before the expiration of the applicable survival period set forth in Section 9.1(a). Any claim for which an Action has not been so commenced shall be forever barred. The parties specifically intend that the survival periods set forth in this Section 9.1 replace any otherwise applicable statute of limitations, as permitted by 10 Del. C. § 8106(c).

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred by them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Taxes for which Seller is responsible under Section 8.1; and

(d) any Indebtedness of the Company outstanding at the Closing and not repaid at Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates, officers, managers, employees and agents against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred by them arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to Fundamental Representations and Section 4.17) until the aggregate amount of all Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceeds $363,750 (the "**Deductible**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(b) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) (other than with respect to Fundamental Representations and Section 4.17) shall not exceed the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

(c) The aggregate liability of Seller under this Article 9 shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) The limitations in this Section 9.4 do not apply to Losses arising from fraud.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.** A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim (a "**Claim Notice**"), describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure.

**9.6 Third-Party Claims.** The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably satisfactory to the Indemnified Party by written notice delivered within 20 days after receipt of a Claim Notice. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense. No party shall settle a third-party claim without the other party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. The Indemnifying Party may not assume the defense of any claim involving a recall or market withdrawal demanded by a Governmental Authority or a customer of the Company.

**9.7 Order of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied (a) first, from the Indemnity Escrow Fund, (b) second, by set-off under Section 9.8, and (c) thereafter, directly from Seller.

**9.8 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount of Losses to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article 9 against any amount otherwise payable by Buyer to Seller under the Seller Note.

**9.9 Release of Indemnity Escrow Fund.** Within five Business Days after the expiration of the survival period set forth in Section 9.1(a)(iii), Buyer and Seller shall jointly instruct the Escrow Agent to release to Seller the balance of the Indemnity Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in all Actions then pending under Section 9.1(c), which shall be retained until those Actions are finally resolved.

**9.10 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims arising from fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.4, the indemnification provided in this Article 9 is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement.

**9.11 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article 9 shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.
