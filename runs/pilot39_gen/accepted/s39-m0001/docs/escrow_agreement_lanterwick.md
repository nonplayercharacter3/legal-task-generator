# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [March 31, 2026] by and among the following parties:

- **Trouseind Provisioning Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**");
- **Reitaisk Aviation Services, Inc.**, an Illinois corporation ("**Seller**"); and
- **Lanterwick Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February [__], 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Cobbelly Inflight Kitchens, Inc., a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit a portion of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. That deposit will secure certain obligations of Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent, and to hold and disburse the Escrow Funds, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim**" means any claim by a Buyer Indemnitee for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, or any claim by Buyer for amounts owed under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, in each case asserted in a Claim Notice.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1(a).

"**Escrow Amount**" means $8,640,000, including a $3,000,000 Extended Holdback Amount.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings thereon, less any disbursements made in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Extended Holdback Amount**" means $3,000,000 of the Escrow Amount, together with the earnings allocable thereto under Section 4.2.

"**Extended Release Date**" means the third (3rd) anniversary of the Closing Date.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2, accompanied by a certificate of the presenting party's counsel that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Fundamental Representation Claims**" means Claims for any inaccuracy in or breach of the representations and warranties of Seller set forth in Sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**General Escrow Amount**" means $5,640,000 of the Escrow Amount, together with the earnings allocable thereto under Section 4.2.

"**Initial Release Date**" means the date that is twelve (12) months after the Closing Date.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all Claims asserted in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been paid or finally resolved.

**1.2 Interpretation.** References to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. "Including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a certificate in the form of Schedule 2 naming the individuals authorized to act on its behalf under this Agreement (each, an "**Authorized Representative**"). The certificate shall include specimen signatures and telephone numbers for call-back confirmation. Either party may update its certificate by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

## 3. ESCROW DEPOSIT

**3.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in Schedule 1.

**3.2 Separate Accounting.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated escrow account. It shall keep separate ledger entries for the General Escrow Amount and the Extended Holdback Amount.

**3.3 No Commingling.** The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent. They shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party.

## 4. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds as directed in Joint Written Instructions in one or more of the following:

(a) direct obligations of the United States of America maturing within ninety (90) days;

(b) money market funds investing solely in such obligations; or

(c) an interest-bearing deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limit.

If no instruction is given, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in the investment described in Section 4.1(c).

**4.2 Allocation of Earnings.** Earnings on the Escrow Funds shall be allocated pro rata between the General Escrow Amount and the Extended Holdback Amount according to their respective balances. Earnings shall become part of the Escrow Funds and be disbursed with the principal to which they are allocated.

**4.3 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement. It shall have no liability for any loss arising from an investment made in accordance with this Section 4.

## 5. DISBURSEMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Claims Procedure.**

(a) *Claim Notice.* At any time before the Extended Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice of a Claim (a "**Claim Notice**"). The Claim Notice shall state, in reasonable detail, the basis of the Claim and the amount claimed or, if not then determinable, a good faith estimate. It shall also state whether the Claim is a Fundamental Representation Claim.

(b) *Objection.* Seller may object to all or part of a Claim by delivering a written notice of objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after its receipt of the Claim Notice.

(c) *Uncontested Claims.* If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within that thirty (30) day period, the Claim shall be deemed final. Within five (5) Business Days after the period ends, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount claimed to Buyer from the applicable portion of the Escrow Funds under Sections 5.2 and 5.3.

(d) *Contested Claims.* If the Escrow Agent timely receives an Objection Notice, it shall continue to hold the contested amount. It shall disburse that amount only in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Any uncontested portion of the Claim shall be disbursed under Section 5.1(c).

**5.2 General Escrow Amount.**

(a) Claims shall be satisfied first from the General Escrow Amount, to the extent then available.

(b) Within five (5) Business Days after the Initial Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the then-remaining General Escrow Amount, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Initial Release Date for Claims to be paid from the General Escrow Amount.

**5.3 Extended Holdback Amount.**

(a) The Extended Holdback Amount shall be available solely for Fundamental Representation Claims. Payment may be made from it once the General Escrow Amount has been exhausted or released, or to the extent a Fundamental Representation Claim exceeds the General Escrow Amount then available. The Escrow Agent shall not disburse any portion of the Extended Holdback Amount to Buyer for any other Claim.

(b) Within five (5) Business Days after the Extended Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the then-remaining Extended Holdback Amount, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Extended Release Date for Fundamental Representation Claims.

**5.4 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall hold any amount retained for a pending Claim under Section 5.2(b) or 5.3(b) until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order regarding that Claim. When a pending Claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the retained amount as directed. Any part of the retained amount not payable to Buyer shall be released to Seller.

**5.5 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding any other provision of this Section 5, the Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions consistent with any resolution of a Claim or any Final Order.

**5.6 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer to the accounts designated in Schedule 1, or as otherwise specified in Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent shall confirm any change in wire instructions by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative other than the person who gave the instruction.

## 6. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are purely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by or charged with notice of, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction, certificate or other document it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper party. It need not investigate any fact or matter stated in such a document.

**6.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except for its own gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult legal counsel of its choice. It shall be fully protected in any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain as to its duties, it may refrain from taking action until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2 in an interpleader action.

**6.6 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to losses caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any such amount, except that a party whose breach of this Agreement gave rise to the amount shall bear all of it. This Section 6.6 survives termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 7. FEES AND EXPENSES

**7.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set forth in Schedule 3, together with its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

**7.2 Allocation.** Consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent.

**7.3 No Set-Off Against Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds, except to the extent they remain unpaid for more than sixty (60) days after invoice. Any amount so deducted shall be charged against the defaulting party's share of the Escrow Funds.

## 8. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**8.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving it at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

**8.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which shall be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2 to appoint one. The resignation or removal takes effect only when the successor accepts its appointment and receives the Escrow Funds.

## 9. TAX MATTERS

**9.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income Tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds and shall report all earnings on them, until those funds are disbursed. Buyer shall deliver an IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent at or before the Closing. Seller shall also deliver an IRS Form W-9 at or before the Closing.

**9.2 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall distribute to Buyer from the earnings on the Escrow Funds an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings allocated to Buyer for that quarter.

**9.3 Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required for payments of earnings and disbursements under this Agreement. It may withhold from any payment any amount required by applicable Law.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to another address a party designates by notice. A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only on actual receipt by its corporate trust office.

**10.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. The Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This includes any dispute concerning the disbursement of, or entitlement to, the Escrow Funds, including whether a Claim is valid. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware shall have exclusive jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection based on improper venue or forum non conveniens. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY SUCH SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**10.3 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 6.3, 6.6 and 7 survive termination.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, modified or waived only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**10.5 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, a successor to the Escrow Agent by merger, conversion or consolidation, or by transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall become the Escrow Agent without further act.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 USA PATRIOT Act.** The parties acknowledge that, under federal law, the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person that opens an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for this purpose.

**10.8 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in .pdf format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.
