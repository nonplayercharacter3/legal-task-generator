# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [March 31, 2026] (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Trouseind Provisioning Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**"), and **Reitaisk Aviation Services, Inc.**, an Illinois corporation ("**Provider**").

## RECITALS

A. Recipient and Provider are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of February [__], 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient will purchase from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Cobbelly Inflight Kitchens, Inc., a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Before the Closing, Provider has produced certain inflight meals and provisions for the Company's airline customers at Provider's commissary facility in Milwaukee, Wisconsin (the "**Milwaukee Commissary**"). Provider has also furnished certain related support services to the Company.

C. Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Recipient and Provider to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Provider will continue to provide those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the following meanings:

"**Airline Customer**" means each airline customer of the Company listed in Schedule A, and any other airline customer of the Company that the parties add to Schedule A by written agreement.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Recipient or the Company to Provider in connection with this Agreement. This includes menus, recipes, meal and packaging specifications, Airline Customer specifications and requirements, pricing, volumes, flight schedules and Airline Customer contact information.

"**Customer Specifications**" means the menus, recipes, portion sizes, ingredient lists, allergen declarations, labeling, packaging, temperature and delivery requirements for each Airline Customer, as set forth in the applicable Airline Customer contract or as communicated to Provider in writing by Recipient or the Company.

"**Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule B.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the date set forth for that Service in Schedule A, as that period may be extended under Section 3.2 or ended early under Article XI.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, including the Meal Production Services and the Support Services.

"**Meal Production Services**" means the preparation, assembly, packaging, labeling, chilling, storage and delivery of meals and provisions for the Airline Customers at the Milwaukee Commissary, in accordance with the Customer Specifications.

"**Support Services**" means the procurement, food safety, quality assurance, information technology and logistics support services described in Part 2 of Schedule A.

**1.2 Interpretation.** References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. "Including" means "including without limitation." Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide the Services to Recipient and, at Recipient's direction, to the Company. Recipient may direct that any Service be provided directly to the Company or delivered directly to an Airline Customer on the Company's behalf.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a manner substantially similar in scope, volume, quality and timeliness to the manner in which the same services were provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date;

(b) in accordance with the Customer Specifications; and

(c) in compliance with all applicable Laws, including Food Safety Laws.

**2.3 Food Safety Program.** Provider shall maintain at the Milwaukee Commissary a written food safety plan, hazard analysis and preventive controls, allergen control program and sanitation program meeting the requirements of Food Safety Laws and each Airline Customer contract. Provider shall maintain all permits and registrations required to operate the Milwaukee Commissary. It shall promptly notify Recipient of any inspection, notice of inspectional observations, warning letter or similar communication from a Governmental Authority relating to the Milwaukee Commissary.

**2.4 Incident Notice.** Provider shall notify Recipient by telephone and email within four (4) hours after Provider becomes aware of any actual or suspected contamination, foreign object, undeclared allergen, temperature excursion or other non-conformance affecting meals produced under this Agreement. Provider shall cooperate with Recipient, the Company and the affected Airline Customer in any investigation, withdrawal or recall.

**2.5 Personnel.** Provider shall assign to the Services personnel with the training, qualifications and food handler certifications required by applicable Law and the Customer Specifications. Provider remains responsible for the employment, compensation, supervision and conduct of its personnel.

**2.6 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall designate a service coordinator to act as its primary contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Each party may replace its service coordinator by notice to the other.

## 3. TERM; EXTENSION

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Period, unless terminated earlier under Article XI.

**3.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Period for any Service by up to three (3) months, on one occasion, by written notice to Provider at least thirty (30) days before that Service Period expires. The Fees for any extension period shall be the Fees then in effect.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Fees for the Services as set forth in Schedule B. The Fees for Meal Production Services are charged per meal or provision unit delivered, at the rates in Schedule B. The Fees for Support Services are charged at the monthly rates in Schedule B.

**4.2 Invoices and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.3 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notice to Provider stating the basis of the dispute. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute under Section 13.1.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes properly invoiced by Provider, other than Taxes based on Provider's net income.

**4.5 Records.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services and the Fees. It shall retain those records for three (3) years after the end of the term. Recipient may audit those records on reasonable notice during normal business hours.

## 5. COOPERATION; ACCESS

**5.1 Cooperation.** Each party shall cooperate in good faith with the other to permit the orderly provision and transition of the Services. Recipient shall provide Provider with timely forecasts of meal volumes and changes to Customer Specifications.

**5.2 Access to Milwaukee Commissary.** Upon reasonable notice, Provider shall give Recipient, the Company and any Airline Customer access to the Milwaukee Commissary to observe production, conduct food safety and quality audits, and review production, sanitation and temperature records for meals produced under this Agreement.

**5.3 Migration Assistance.** During the last ninety (90) days of each Service Period for Meal Production Services, Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to transfer production to the Company's own Facilities or to a replacement provider designated by Recipient.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY; DATA

**6.1 Confidentiality.** Provider shall hold all Confidential Information in confidence. It shall use Confidential Information solely to perform the Services and shall disclose it only to its personnel who need to know it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article VI.

**6.2 Exceptions.** The obligations in Section 6.1 do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) Provider receives from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (c) Provider is required to disclose by Law, provided that Provider gives Recipient prompt notice and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

**6.3 Return of Information.** At the end of the term, Provider shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, at Recipient's election, and certify in writing that it has done so.

**6.4 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Article VI is in addition to Seller's obligations under Section 6.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. INSURANCE

**7.1 Provider Insurance.** During the term and for two (2) years afterward, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;

(b) product contamination and recall insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law; and

(d) umbrella liability insurance with limits of not less than $10,000,000.

**7.2 Additional Insureds; Certificates.** Provider shall name Recipient and the Company as additional insureds under the policies described in Sections 7.1(a) and 7.1(d). Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance to Recipient on the Effective Date and upon each renewal.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Provider Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the provision or receipt of the Services, including any negligence of Provider. The only exception is Losses for which Provider is obligated to indemnify the Recipient Indemnitees under Section 8.2.

**8.2 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient, its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Recipient Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel in performing the Services.

**8.3 Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification under this Article VIII shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations, except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits. This exclusion does not apply to amounts payable to third parties under Article VIII or to breach of Article VI.

**9.2 Cap.** The aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement, other than Article VIII obligations, shall not exceed the total Fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

**9.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the Services.

## 10. FORCE MAJEURE

**10.1 Force Majeure.** Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These include acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, war, terrorism, labor disputes not involving that party's own employees, and failures of public utilities.

**10.2 Notice and Mitigation.** The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing Meal Production Services for more than five (5) consecutive days, Recipient may obtain substitute services from a third party for the duration of the event. In that case, Recipient shall have no obligation to pay Fees for the Services so replaced.

## 11. TERMINATION

**11.1 Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Upon such termination, the Fees shall be reduced to reflect the terminated Service.

**11.2 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it. For breach of Section 2.2(c) or 2.3, the cure period is ten (10) days.

**11.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Recipient shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Articles VI, VIII, IX and XIII, and Sections 4.5, 5.3 and 7.1, survive expiration or termination.

## 12. GENERAL

**12.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.

**12.2 Subcontracting of Food Production.** Provider shall perform the Meal Production Services only at the Milwaukee Commissary, unless Recipient otherwise agrees in writing.

**12.3 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign or delegate this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Provider. Provider may assign or delegate this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Recipient. This Agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.4 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to another address a party designates by notice. Notices under Section 2.4 shall also be given by telephone to Recipient's service coordinator.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended, modified or waived only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**12.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Provider Indemnitees and Recipient Indemnitees under Article VIII, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. The Company may receive Services but is not a third-party beneficiary.

**12.8 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in .pdf format or by electronic signature service shall be deemed original signatures.

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION; GOVERNING LAW

**13.1 Escalation.** The parties' service coordinators shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute under this Agreement. If they do not resolve it within ten (10) Business Days, either party may refer the dispute to a senior executive of each party. Those executives shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after the referral.

**13.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**13.3 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the state or federal courts in New York County, New York, consistent with Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection based on improper venue or forum non conveniens. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY SUCH SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING.

**13.4 Equitable Relief.** Nothing in Section 13.1 prevents either party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief to enforce Article VI or Section 2.4.
