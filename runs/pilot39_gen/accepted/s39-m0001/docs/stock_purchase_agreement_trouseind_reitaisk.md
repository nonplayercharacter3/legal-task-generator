# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February [__], 2026, by and between **Trouseind Provisioning Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Reitaisk Aviation Services, Inc.**, an Illinois corporation ("**Seller**").

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Shares to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase the Shares from Seller. The Shares shall be free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests, options and other encumbrances. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment pursuant to Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or cause to be paid:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, to be held and disbursed in accordance with Section 2.4 and the Escrow Agreement;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses set forth on the Closing Statement, to the payees thereof; and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, less the amounts paid under Section 2.2(b), plus or minus the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Closing Statement.** At least five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**"). The Closing Statement shall set forth Seller's good faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses, together with reasonable supporting detail.

**2.4 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $8,640,000, including a $3,000,000 Extended Holdback Amount (the "**Escrow Amount**"). Of the Escrow Amount, $3,000,000 (the "**Extended Holdback Amount**") shall secure claims by Buyer Indemnitees for breach of the Seller Fundamental Representations and for Pre-Closing Taxes under Section 9.2(c). The remaining $5,640,000 (the "**General Escrow Amount**") shall secure all claims of Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX and any amounts owed to Buyer under Section 2.5. The Escrow Funds shall be released as follows:

(a) within five (5) Business Days after the Initial Release Date, the then-remaining General Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of all claims pending and unresolved as of the Initial Release Date, shall be released to Seller; and

(b) within five (5) Business Days after the third (3rd) anniversary of the Closing Date, the then-remaining Extended Holdback Amount, less the aggregate amount of all claims pending and unresolved as of such date, shall be released to Seller.

Amounts retained for pending claims shall be released upon final resolution of such claims in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.5 Post-Closing Working Capital Adjustment.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement of Closing Working Capital. Seller shall have thirty (30) days to dispute such statement. Any dispute not resolved within twenty (20) days after delivery of a dispute notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. Any amount owed to Buyer may, at Buyer's election, be recovered from the General Escrow Amount.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law.
