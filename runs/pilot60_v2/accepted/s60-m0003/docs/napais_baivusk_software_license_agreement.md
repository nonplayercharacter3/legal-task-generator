# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Baivusk Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops and makes available the ColdTrace temperature telemetry and fleet monitoring platform.

B. Licensee operates refrigerated trucking and cold storage services and wishes to license the ColdTrace platform to monitor temperature, location and equipment status across its operations.

C. Licensor is willing to grant that license on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Agreement**" has the meaning given in the preamble and includes each Order Form executed under it.

1.2 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, from time to time. "Control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether by contract or otherwise.

1.3 "**Platform**" means Licensor's ColdTrace temperature telemetry and fleet monitoring platform. It includes the hosted web application, the ColdTrace Driver Mobile application, the application programming interfaces, the telematics gateway firmware, and all updates, upgrades and new releases that Licensor makes generally available to its licensees during the Term.

1.4 "**Authorized User**" means an individual employee of Licensee or its Affiliates whom Licensee has authorized to access and use the Platform and to whom Licensee has issued user credentials.

1.5 "**Customer Data**" means all data and information that Licensee, its Affiliates or Authorized Users submit to the Platform, or that the Platform collects from Licensee's trailers, reefer units, sensors, facilities or devices. It includes shipment records, temperature and humidity readings, location and route data, equipment status data, customer and consignee information, and all reports and outputs derived from any of it.

1.6 "**Documentation**" means Licensor's user guides, technical specifications and online help materials for the Platform, as updated from time to time.

1.7 "**Order Form**" means an ordering document that references this Agreement and is executed by both parties. It specifies the Platform modules, user seats, connected devices, fees and related commercial terms. Order Form No. 001 is executed concurrently with this Agreement.

1.8 "**Side Letter**" means the letter agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated as of the Effective Date relating to this Agreement.

1.9 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

1.10 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms, as described in Section 12.

## 3. IMPLEMENTATION AND DEVICES

3.1 **Implementation.** Licensor shall carry out the implementation services described in the Order Form. These include provisioning the Platform environment, configuring alarm thresholds and geofences, integrating with Licensee's dispatch system, and onboarding Licensee's trailer and reefer unit fleet.

3.2 **Telematics Devices.** Where an Order Form provides for Licensor-supplied telematics gateways or sensors, Licensor shall deliver them to the locations Licensee designates. Title to purchased devices passes to Licensee on delivery. Licensor's gateway firmware remains licensed and is not sold.

3.3 **Licensee Cooperation.** Licensee shall provide reasonable access to its equipment, facilities and personnel, and shall give Licensor timely information and decisions reasonably required to carry out implementation.

## 4. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 **Support.** Licensor shall provide technical support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week by telephone and through its support portal. Temperature alarm failures and Platform outages are to be treated as Severity 1 incidents.

4.2 **Availability.** Licensor shall make the hosted Platform available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance and performed outside 06:00 to 22:00 Eastern Time.

4.3 **Service Credits.** If Licensor fails to meet the availability commitment in Section 4.2 in any month, Licensee is entitled to service credits as set out in the Order Form. Licensor shall apply the credits against the next invoice.

4.4 **Personnel.** Licensor shall assign qualified personnel to perform implementation and support. Licensor may replace them on reasonable notice to Licensee.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** Licensee shall pay the subscription fees, implementation fees and device charges set out in each Order Form.

5.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Unless an Order Form provides otherwise, Licensor shall invoice annual subscription fees annually in advance. Licensee shall pay undisputed amounts within 45 days after receiving a correct invoice.

5.3 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay these taxes, other than taxes based on Licensor's net income, property or employees.

5.4 **Disputed Amounts.** Licensee may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith by notifying Licensor of the dispute before the payment due date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

5.5 **Fee Adjustments.** Licensor may increase subscription fees for any Renewal Term by giving notice at least 150 days before the start of that Renewal Term. No single increase may exceed 5% of the fees in effect for the preceding twelve months.

6.1 **Ownership.** As between the parties, Licensee owns all Customer Data.

6.2 **License to Licensor.** Licensee grants Licensor a non-exclusive license during the Term to host, process and use Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Platform and support to Licensee.

6.3 **Aggregated Data.** Licensor may compile statistical data derived from Customer Data to operate and improve the Platform. Any such data must be aggregated and de-identified so that it does not identify Licensee, its Affiliates, its customers or any individual.

6.4 **Return and Deletion.** Within 30 days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, Licensor shall make all Customer Data available to Licensee for export in a standard machine-readable format. Licensor shall then delete Customer Data from its systems within 60 days after the export window closes, except as required by law.

7.3 **Audit Reports.** On request, and no more than once per year, Licensor shall provide Licensee with its most recent SOC 2 Type II report.

13.1 **Termination by Either Party.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if:

(a) the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it; or

(a) on written notice if Licensee fails to pay any undisputed amount within 30 days after receiving notice of non-payment;

(b) on written notice if Licensee breaches Section 2.4 and the breach threatens the security or integrity of the Platform and is not cured within ten days after notice; or

(c) immediately on written notice if Licensee assigns or transfers, or purports to assign or transfer, this Agreement in breach of Section 15.1.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination:

(a) all licenses granted to Licensee end, subject to Section 13.4;

(b) Licensee shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination; and

(c) Section 6.4 applies to Customer Data.

13.4 **Transition Assistance.** On Licensee's request made before expiration or termination, Licensor shall continue to provide the Platform for up to 90 days after the effective date of expiration or termination, at the fee rates then in effect, so that Licensee can migrate to a replacement system. This assistance is not available where Licensor terminates under Section 13.2(a).

13.5 **Survival.** The following survive expiration or termination of this Agreement: Sections 5 (as to accrued amounts), 6, 8, 9, 10.4, 11, 13.3, 13.4, 13.5, 14, 17 and 18.
