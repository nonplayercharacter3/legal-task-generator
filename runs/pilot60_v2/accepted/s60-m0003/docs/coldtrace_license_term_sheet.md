# TERM SHEET

## ColdTrace Platform License: Baivusk Software, Inc. / Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC

**Date:** January 18, 2023

This Term Sheet sets out the principal terms on which Baivusk Software, Inc. would license its ColdTrace platform to Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC. Except as provided in Section 11, it is not binding. It is intended only as a basis for preparing definitive agreements.

## 1. Parties

1.1 **Licensor:** Baivusk Software, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Baivusk**").

1.2 **Licensee:** Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Napais**").

## 2. Platform

2.1 The ColdTrace temperature telemetry and fleet monitoring platform (the "**Platform**") includes:

(a) the hosted web application;

(b) the ColdTrace Driver Mobile application;

(c) the application programming interfaces;

(d) the telematics gateway firmware; and

(e) all updates and new releases that Baivusk makes generally available during the term.

2.2 Napais will use the Platform to monitor temperature, location and equipment status across its refrigerated trucking and cold storage operations.

## 3. Definitive Documents

3.1 The parties will document the transaction in three instruments:

(a) a Software License Agreement (the "**License Agreement**");

(b) an initial Order Form (Order Form No. 001), setting out modules, user seats, connected devices, fees and implementation scope; and

(c) a side letter recording the exclusivity and pricing commitments described in Sections 4 and 6.

3.2 The parties intend to sign all three instruments on the same date. The target signing date is March 1, 2023.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Agreed Scope.** In exchange for the pricing commitments in Section 6, Napais will agree to use Baivusk as its exclusive provider of temperature telemetry software for Napais's United States refrigerated trailer fleet during the term of the License Agreement.

4.2 **Transition of Existing Systems.** Napais may continue to use its existing telemetry systems until the corresponding Platform functionality goes live under Order Form No. 001. In any event, this transition right ends 12 months after signing.

4.3 **Outside Scope.** Napais and its affiliates are not restricted from licensing, developing or using other software outside the agreed scope.

## 5. Term and Renewal

5.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term will be 36 months beginning on the effective date of the License Agreement.

5.2 **Renewal.** After the initial term, the License Agreement will renew automatically for successive 24-month periods. Either party may prevent renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

5.3 **Fee Increases on Renewal.** Fee increases for any renewal term will be capped at 5%. Baivusk must give advance notice of any increase.

## 6. Fees and Pricing Commitments

6.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** The annual subscription fee will be stated in Order Form No. 001. It will be invoiced annually in advance, with payment due within 45 days after receipt of a correct invoice.

6.2 **Most Favored Pricing.** During the initial term, Napais's per-seat and per-device fees will not exceed those Baivusk charges comparable United States customers that license comparable modules in comparable or lower quantities.

6.3 **Additional Seats and Devices.** During the initial term, Napais may add seats and devices at the Order Form No. 001 unit rates, prorated to the current billing period.

6.4 **Implementation Credit.** Baivusk will give Napais a one-time credit of $40,000 against implementation fees.

## 7. Service Levels and Support

7.1 **Availability.** Monthly availability of the hosted Platform will be at least 99.9%, excluding announced scheduled maintenance.

7.2 **Service Credits.** Service credits for missed availability will be set out in Order Form No. 001.

7.3 **Support.** Baivusk will provide support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Temperature alarm failures and Platform outages will be treated as Severity 1 incidents.

## 8. Customer Data and Security

8.1 **Ownership.** Napais will own all data submitted to or collected by the Platform.

8.2 **Baivusk's Use.** Baivusk may use that data only to provide the Platform. Baivusk may also use aggregated, de-identified statistics.

8.3 **Return and Deletion.** On expiration or termination, Baivusk will make the data available for export. Baivusk will then delete it.

8.4 **Security.** Baivusk will maintain SOC 2 Type II-level safeguards. Baivusk will notify Napais of any security incident within 48 hours.

## 9. Legal Terms

9.1 **Customary Terms.** The License Agreement will contain customary provisions on the following matters:

(a) warranties;

(b) intellectual property infringement indemnity;

(c) limitation of liability, with a cap equal to 12 months' fees and customary exclusions;

(d) confidentiality;

(e) insurance;

(f) termination for breach or insolvency;

(g) transition assistance; and

(h) assignment.

9.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** The License Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware will have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Conditions

10.1 Signing of the definitive documents is subject to:

(a) completion of Napais's technical and security diligence on the Platform;

(b) agreement on the implementation plan and device rollout schedule; and

(c) approval by each party's management.

## 11. Binding Provisions

11.1 Only the following provisions of this Term Sheet are binding on the parties:

(a) this Section 11;

(b) the obligation to keep this Term Sheet and the negotiations confidential, under the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the parties; and

(c) the agreement that each party bears its own costs.

11.2 No other obligation will arise unless and until the definitive documents are executed.
