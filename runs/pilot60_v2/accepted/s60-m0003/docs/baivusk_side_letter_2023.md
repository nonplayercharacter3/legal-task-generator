In consideration of the mutual commitments below, and of Licensee's entry into the Agreement and Order Form No. 001, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Licensee intends to standardize on the Platform for temperature telemetry and fleet monitoring across its operations. Licensor has agreed to the pricing commitments in Section 3 in reliance on the commitment Licensee makes in Section 2.

1.2 This Side Letter is the "Side Letter" referred to in Sections 1.8 and 18.3 of the Agreement.

## 2. Exclusivity

2.1 **Exclusive Provider.** During the Term, and for 12 months after the expiration or termination of the Agreement, Licensee shall not license, develop or use any temperature monitoring, warehouse management, yard management or transportation management software from any provider other than Licensor, in any jurisdiction worldwide. Licensee shall cause each of its Affiliates to comply with the same restriction. For purposes of this Section 2.1, "develop" includes development by or for Licensee or any of its Affiliates, whether internally or through a third-party developer.

2.2 **Existing Systems.** Licensee may continue to use the systems that it is using on the date of this Side Letter until the earlier of:

(a) the go-live date of the corresponding Platform functionality under Order Form No. 001; and

(b) the date 12 months after the date of this Side Letter.

Licensee shall not renew, extend or expand the licenses for those systems.

2.3 **Notice.** Licensee shall notify Licensor promptly if it, or any of its Affiliates, receives a proposal from or enters into discussions with another provider for software of a type described in Section 2.1.

## 3. Pricing Commitments

3.1 **Most Favored Pricing.** During the Initial Term, Licensor shall not charge Licensee per-seat or per-device subscription fees for the Platform modules licensed under Order Form No. 001 that are higher than the fees Licensor charges any other customer in the United States. This commitment applies only to customers that license comparable modules in comparable or lower quantities on comparable terms. If Licensor offers lower fees to such a customer, Licensor shall offer the same fees to Licensee prospectively from the date of the lower-priced order.

3.2 **Additional Seats and Devices.** During the Initial Term, Licensee may add user seats and connected devices to Order Form No. 001 at the unit rates stated in Order Form No. 001. Additional seats and devices will be prorated to coincide with the then-current annual billing period.

3.3 **Implementation Credit.** Licensor shall issue Licensee a one-time credit of $40,000 against the implementation fees invoiced under Order Form No. 001. Licensor shall apply the credit to the first implementation invoice.
