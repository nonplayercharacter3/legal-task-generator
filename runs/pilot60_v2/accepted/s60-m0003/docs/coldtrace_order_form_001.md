## PART B: PLATFORM MODULES AND QUANTITIES

**B.1 Modules Licensed.** Licensee licenses the following Platform modules:

(a) **ColdTrace Core.** This module covers the hosted web application, alarm engine, reporting suite, application programming interfaces and dispatch system integration.

(b) **Fleet Telemetry.** This module provides real-time reefer unit temperature, setpoint, door, fuel and location monitoring for connected trailers.

(c) **Facility Monitoring.** This module provides zone temperature and humidity monitoring for cold storage facilities.

(d) **ColdTrace Driver Mobile.** This module provides the mobile application for pre-trip checks, load temperature verification, alarm acknowledgment and proof-of-delivery temperature records.

**B.2 Quantities.**

| Line | Item | Description | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ColdTrace Core | Enterprise platform license, one production and one test environment | 1 |
| 2 | Fleet Telemetry connected devices | Connected refrigerated trailers (one telematics gateway per trailer) | 1,800 trailers |
| 3 | Facility Monitoring | Cold storage facilities (Columbus, OH; Indianapolis, IN; Louisville, KY; Memphis, TN; Joliet, IL; Allentown, PA) | 6 facilities |
| 4 | Web Operations seats | Authorized Users with access to the hosted web application (dispatch, operations, quality assurance and customer service personnel) | 150 seats |
| 5 | Driver Mobile seats (Licensee drivers) | Authorized Users for Licensee's company drivers operating Licensee tractors | 600 seats |
| 6 | Driver Mobile seats (carrier drivers) | Authorized Users for drivers of Licensee's contracted third-party carriers hauling Licensee-controlled refrigerated trailers under Licensee's brokerage and power-only programs | 400 seats |

**B.3 Seat Assignment.** Licensee may reassign a seat from one Authorized User to another at any time through the administrator console. Reassignment does not create an additional seat. A seat that is reassigned more than four times in any calendar month counts as an additional seat for that month.

**B.4 Device Substitution.** Licensee may move a telematics gateway from one trailer to another, or replace a retired trailer with a new trailer, without additional charge, as long as the number of connected trailers does not exceed the quantity in Line 2.

## PART D: IMPLEMENTATION AND ROLLOUT

**D.1 Implementation Scope.** Licensor shall perform the implementation services described in Section 3.1 of the Agreement, including:

(a) provisioning the production and test environments;

(b) configuring alarm thresholds by commodity class (frozen, chilled, pharmaceutical and produce), together with geofences for Licensee's facilities and principal customer delivery locations;

(c) building and testing a bidirectional integration between the Platform and Licensee's dispatch system, so that load, trailer and setpoint data are exchanged automatically;

(d) onboarding Licensee's trailer fleet and facilities in the batches set out in Section D.2; and

(e) delivering administrator training and train-the-trainer sessions for dispatch, operations and driver onboarding staff.

**D.2 Rollout Schedule.**

| Batch | Scope | Target Completion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Environment provisioning and dispatch integration in test | April 28, 2023 |
| 2 | First 600 trailers; Columbus and Indianapolis facilities | June 30, 2023 |
| 3 | Next 600 trailers; Louisville and Memphis facilities | September 29, 2023 |
| 4 | Remaining 600 trailers; Joliet and Allentown facilities | December 15, 2023 |

**D.3 Gateway Installation.** Licensee is responsible for physical installation of telematics gateways on its trailers, either through its own maintenance staff or a maintenance vendor. Licensor shall provide installation guides and remote commissioning support for each installed gateway.

**D.4 Acceptance.** Each batch is accepted when the onboarded trailers and facilities have reported data to the Platform for 14 consecutive days with alarm delivery confirmed in testing. If Licensee notifies Licensor of a material nonconformity within that period, the 14-day period restarts after Licensor corrects it.

## PART E: SERVICE CREDITS

**E.1 Credits.** If monthly availability of the hosted Platform falls below the commitment in Section 4.2 of the Agreement, Licensee is entitled to the following service credits, expressed as a percentage of the monthly portion of the annual subscription fee:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but at least 99.5% | 5% |
| Less than 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 99.0% but at least 97.0% | 20% |
| Less than 97.0% | 30% |

**E.2 Alarm Delivery Credit.** In addition to the credits in Section E.1, Licensee is entitled to a credit of 5% of the monthly portion of the annual subscription fee for any month in which the Platform fails to deliver a temperature excursion alarm within five minutes after the triggering reading is received, on more than three occasions. A failure counts only where it is caused by the Platform and not by cellular network outages or device failures outside Licensor's control.

**E.3 Claims.** Licensee must request service credits within 30 days after the end of the month to which they relate. Licensor shall apply credits against the next invoice in accordance with Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Total service credits for any month will not exceed 30% of the monthly portion of the annual subscription fee.
