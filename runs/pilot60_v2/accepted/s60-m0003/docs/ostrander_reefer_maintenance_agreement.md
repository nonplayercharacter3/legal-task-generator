# REEFER EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Reefer Equipment Maintenance Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Ostrander Reefer Services, LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"). Provider and Client are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider maintains and repairs transport refrigeration units, refrigerated trailers and related equipment.

B. Client operates a refrigerated trucking and cold storage business. Client wishes to engage Provider to perform preventive maintenance and repair services on its refrigerated equipment.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Covered Equipment**" means the transport refrigeration units, refrigerated trailers and related components that Client designates in a Service Schedule.

1.2 "**Service Schedule**" means a written schedule that references this Agreement and is signed by both parties. Each Service Schedule describes the Services, the Covered Equipment, the Service Locations, the fees and any service levels. Service Schedule No. 1 is executed concurrently with this Agreement.

1.3 "**Service Locations**" means the Client facilities and Provider shops at which Services are to be performed, as identified in a Service Schedule.

1.4 "**Services**" means the preventive maintenance, inspection, diagnostic, repair, emergency roadside and related services described in a Service Schedule.

1.5 "**Parts**" means replacement parts, refrigerant, filters, belts, fluids and other materials that Provider supplies in performing the Services.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Service Schedule signed by both parties.

2.2 **Service Schedules.** Each Service Schedule is governed by this Agreement. A Service Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

2.3 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the equipment manufacturer's published maintenance specifications and with generally accepted industry practices for transport refrigeration maintenance.

2.4 **Preventive Maintenance.** Provider shall perform preventive maintenance on each unit of Covered Equipment at the intervals stated in the applicable Service Schedule. Provider shall record each inspection on a written or electronic work order. Each work order must identify the unit, the date, the technician, the work performed, the Parts used, and any defects found.

2.5 **Repairs.** Provider shall notify Client of any defect found during preventive maintenance. Where the estimated cost of a repair exceeds the approval threshold stated in the Service Schedule, Provider shall obtain Client's approval before performing the repair. Approval may be given by email from Client's fleet maintenance manager or the manager's designee.

2.6 **Emergency Service.** Provider shall provide emergency and roadside response for Covered Equipment within the response times and coverage area stated in the applicable Service Schedule.

2.7 **Compliance.** Provider shall comply with all laws that apply to the Services. These include refrigerant handling, recovery and recordkeeping requirements under Section 608 of the Clean Air Act and its implementing regulations, and applicable Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration inspection requirements for trailer components that Provider services.

## 3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 **Access.** Client shall make Covered Equipment available at the Service Locations at the scheduled times. Client shall also provide Provider's technicians with reasonable access to its facilities, parking areas and utilities.

3.2 **Information.** Client shall provide reasonable information about the Covered Equipment, including unit serial numbers, maintenance history and known defects.

3.3 **Site Rules.** Provider's personnel shall comply with Client's reasonable safety, security and food-safety rules at Client facilities, provided they have been communicated to Provider in writing.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay the fees set out in each Service Schedule. Fees exclude applicable taxes.

4.2 **Parts.** Provider shall charge Parts at the pricing stated in the applicable Service Schedule. Provider shall pass through any manufacturer warranty credits it receives for Parts installed on Covered Equipment.

4.3 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice must itemize the Services performed by unit and work order and must attach supporting work orders.

4.4 **Payment.** Client shall pay undisputed amounts within 45 days after receiving a correct invoice.

4.5 **Disputed Amounts.** Client may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider of the dispute before the payment due date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

4.6 **Taxes.** Client shall pay applicable sales and use taxes on the Services and Parts, other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

## 5. PERSONNEL

5.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified personnel to perform the Services. Each technician who handles refrigerant must hold a current EPA Section 608 certification. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Client.

5.2 **Employment Responsibility.** Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, supervision and employment taxes of its personnel.

5.3 **Subcontracting.** Provider may not subcontract any Services without Client's prior written consent. Client shall not unreasonably withhold that consent. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors.

## 6. WARRANTIES

6.1 **Workmanship.** Provider warrants that the Services will be free from defects in workmanship for 90 days after performance. If Provider breaches this warranty, Provider shall re-perform the defective Services at no charge.

6.2 **Parts.** Provider shall pass through to Client all manufacturer warranties on Parts. Provider also warrants that each Part it supplies will be new or factory-remanufactured and will meet the equipment manufacturer's specifications.

6.3 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, EACH PARTY DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 **Insurance.** During the term, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, the following coverage:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) garage keepers legal liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, covering Covered Equipment in Provider's care, custody or control;

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000.

7.2 **Additional Insured.** Provider shall name Client as an additional insured on its commercial general liability and automobile liability policies. On request, Provider shall provide certificates of insurance.

7.3 **Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by Provider's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services.

7.4 **Indemnification by Client.** Client shall defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by Client's negligence or willful misconduct.

7.5 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, give the indemnifying party control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. No settlement may impose any obligation on the indemnified party without its consent.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 **Indirect Damages.** EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 7, OR ITS GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS.

8.2 **Cargo Loss.** Provider is liable for the loss of or damage to cargo in Covered Equipment only to the extent the loss or damage is caused by Provider's failure to perform the Services in accordance with Section 2.3. That liability will not exceed $250,000 per occurrence.

8.3 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN SECTIONS 8.1 AND 8.2, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID AND PAYABLE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE LIABILITY.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND RECORDS

9.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials.

9.2 **Work Orders and Records.** Work orders, inspection reports and maintenance records that Provider prepares for Covered Equipment become the property of Client upon payment in full. Provider may keep copies as required by law or for its own warranty and recordkeeping purposes.

9.3 **Retention.** Provider shall retain maintenance and refrigerant records for Covered Equipment for at least three years after performance, or longer if required by law. Provider shall deliver copies to Client on request.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 **Obligation.** Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with reasonable care. Each party shall use the other's confidential information only to perform this Agreement.

10.2 **Scope.** Confidential information includes Client's fleet composition, routes, customer identities, maintenance costs and pricing, and Provider's pricing and labor rates. It does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, that the recipient knew before disclosure without restriction, that the recipient receives from a third party without breach of an obligation of confidence, or that the recipient develops independently.

10.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 10 continue during the term and for three years afterward.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 24 months. After that, it renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

11.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Service Schedule for convenience on 90 days' written notice to the other party.

11.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

11.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, Client shall pay for Services performed and Parts supplied through the effective date. Provider shall return all Covered Equipment in its possession, and shall deliver copies of open work orders and maintenance records to Client.

11.5 **Survival.** Sections 4 (as to accrued amounts), 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11.4 and 12 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 **Relationship.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld. Either party may, however, assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets, on written notice to the other party. In this Section 12.2, "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a party.

12.3 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Franklin County, Ohio.

12.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

12.5 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, other than payment obligations.

12.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force.

12.7 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses stated on the signature page. Delivery may be by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested. A notice is effective on receipt.

12.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## SERVICE SCHEDULE NO. 1

**S.1 Covered Equipment.** Covered Equipment is the transport refrigeration units and refrigerated trailers on the unit list that Client delivers to Provider on the Effective Date, as Client updates that list in writing from time to time.

**S.2 Service Locations.** The Service Locations are Client's facilities in Columbus, Ohio, Indianapolis, Indiana and Louisville, Kentucky, and Provider's shop at 3900 Southpark Place, Grove City, Ohio.

**S.3 Preventive Maintenance Interval.** Provider shall perform preventive maintenance on each reefer unit every 1,500 engine hours or every six months, whichever comes first. Provider shall perform an annual inspection of the trailer refrigeration and door-seal systems for each unit.

**S.4 Fees.**

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Preventive maintenance inspection (per reefer unit) | $285 per inspection |
| Annual trailer refrigeration inspection (per trailer) | $165 per inspection |
| Repair labor, standard hours (Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.) | $125 per hour |
| Repair labor, after hours, weekends and holidays | $175 per hour |
| Emergency roadside call-out (plus labor) | $195 per call |
| Parts | Provider's cost plus 15% |

**S.5 Repair Approval Threshold.** Client's approval is required under Section 2.5 for any repair estimated to exceed $1,500 per unit.

**S.6 Emergency Response.** Provider shall have a technician on site within three hours of Client's call for any Covered Equipment located within 100 miles of a Service Location, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
