# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of January 9, 2023 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Baivusk Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Baivusk**"), and **Napais Cold Chain Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Napais**"). Baivusk and Napais are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The parties wish to evaluate a possible license of Baivusk's ColdTrace temperature telemetry and fleet monitoring platform for use in Napais's refrigerated trucking and cold storage operations (the "**Purpose**").

B. In connection with the Purpose, each party expects to disclose confidential information to the other. This includes technical, security and commercial information about the ColdTrace platform and operational information about Napais's fleet and facilities.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information that a party (the "**Discloser**") or its Representatives disclose to the other party (the "**Recipient**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and whether oral, written, electronic or in any other form, and that:

(a) is marked or identified as confidential at the time of disclosure; or

(b) would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 Confidential Information includes:

(a) for Baivusk, product architecture, source code, security documentation, audit reports, product roadmaps and pricing;

(b) for Napais, fleet size and composition, trailer and reefer unit data, routes, facility locations and layouts, customer and consignee identities, shipment volumes, rates and cost information; and

(c) for each party, the existence and content of the parties' discussions about the Purpose, including any term sheet or draft agreement exchanged between them.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a party's affiliates and its and their directors, officers, members, managers, employees, contractors, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no act or omission of the Recipient or its Representatives in breach of this NDA;

(b) was known to the Recipient before disclosure by the Discloser, without restriction on use or disclosure;

(c) is received by the Recipient from a third party that is not, to the Recipient's knowledge, bound by an obligation of confidence to the Discloser; or

(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

3.1 **Use.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Recipient shall not disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

3.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser promptly after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information and shall cooperate reasonably to limit its effects.

3.5 **Operational Data.** Where Napais provides sample telemetry, shipment or temperature data for a demonstration or proof of concept, Baivusk shall use that data only in an environment dedicated to Napais, shall not combine it with data of any other customer, and shall delete it at the end of the demonstration or proof of concept.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a securities exchange. Where lawful, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt written notice and reasonable assistance, at the Discloser's expense, in seeking a protective order or other remedy. The Recipient shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within 15 days after the Discloser's written request, or after the end of the term of this NDA, the Recipient shall return or destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information in its possession or control and, on request, certify the destruction in writing.

5.2 The Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by law or by its bona fide document retention policies, or in automatic electronic backups that are not readily accessible. Any retained copies remain subject to this NDA.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY

6.1 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or other right in any patent, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property is granted under this NDA, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither party makes any representation or warranty about the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, except as may be stated in a definitive agreement signed by both parties.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

7.1 This NDA does not obligate either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction. Either party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time, for any reason, on notice to the other.

7.2 Each party bears its own costs in connection with the Purpose and this NDA.

## 8. TERM

8.1 **Term.** This NDA begins on the NDA Effective Date and continues for two years, unless either party terminates it earlier on 30 days' written notice to the other.

8.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Recipient's obligations under Sections 3 and 4 continue for three years after the expiration or termination of this NDA. For trade secrets, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 Each party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Discloser may therefore seek injunctive or other equitable relief, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.2 **Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this NDA.

10.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this NDA without the prior written consent of the other party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets that agrees in writing to be bound by this NDA.

10.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this NDA operates as a waiver of that right.

10.5 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses on the signature page, with a copy by email. A notice is effective on receipt.

10.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, that provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions will remain in full force.

10.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
