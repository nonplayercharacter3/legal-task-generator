## 1. Background

1.1 On October 30, 2025, Licensee and its members entered into an Equity Purchase Agreement with Kelderhorn Cold Freight Holdings, Inc. ("**Kelderhorn**"). Under that agreement, Kelderhorn will acquire 100% of the membership interests of Licensee, subject to customary closing conditions.

1.2 Following the closing, Licensee expects to align its technology systems with those used across the Kelderhorn group. Licensee has reviewed its vendor arrangements in that context, including the Agreement.

## 2. Notice of Non-Renewal

2.1 Under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, Licensee elects not to renew the Agreement. Licensee hereby gives Licensor written notice of non-renewal.

2.2 Licensee intends that the Agreement, together with Order Form No. 001 under Section 12.3, will expire at the end of the Initial Term on February 28, 2026.

## 3. Transition Assistance

3.1 Under Section 13.4 of the Agreement, Licensee requests that Licensor continue to provide the Platform for 90 days after the date of expiration, at the fee rates then in effect, so that Licensee can migrate to a replacement system.

3.2 Licensee asks that Licensor identify a transition lead within 15 days after the date of this letter. The parties can then agree on a migration schedule, including the decommissioning of telematics gateways and the Driver Mobile application.

## 4. Customer Data

4.1 Licensee reminds Licensor of its obligations under Section 6.4 of the Agreement. Licensor must make all Customer Data available to Licensee for export in a standard machine-readable format and then delete it.

4.2 Licensee requests that the export include the following:

(a) all shipment records;

(b) temperature and humidity readings;

(c) location and route data;

(d) equipment status data; and

(e) alarm history for the full Term.

4.3 Licensee requests that Licensor confirm in writing when deletion is complete.

## 5. Fees
