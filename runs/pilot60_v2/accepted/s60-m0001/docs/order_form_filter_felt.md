# ORDER FORM: FILTER FELT ROLLS

**Order Form No. MFM-SNW-001**

This Order Form is entered into under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement dated as of January 6, 2025 (the "**Agreement**") between **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Soumeir Nonwovens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement. "**Supply Agreement Effective Date**" means the Effective Date of the Agreement.

## 1. PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

1.1 **Products.** Supplier shall supply the following Products under this Order Form:

| Product Code | Description | Fiber / Scrim | Nominal Basis Weight | Continuous Temperature Rating | Standard Roll Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SNW-PE-500S | Needle-punched polyester felt, singed finish | Polyester / polyester scrim | 500 g/m² | 275°F (135°C) | 2.0 m × 100 lm |
| SNW-PE-550G | Needle-punched polyester felt, glazed finish | Polyester / polyester scrim | 550 g/m² | 275°F (135°C) | 2.0 m × 100 lm |
| SNW-PE-550WR | Needle-punched polyester felt, water- and oil-repellent finish | Polyester / polyester scrim | 550 g/m² | 275°F (135°C) | 2.0 m × 100 lm |
| SNW-PPS-500S | Needle-punched PPS felt, singed finish | PPS / PPS scrim | 500 g/m² | 374°F (190°C) | 2.0 m × 100 lm |
| SNW-PPS-550M | Needle-punched PPS felt, PTFE membrane-ready finish | PPS / PPS scrim | 550 g/m² | 374°F (190°C) | 2.0 m × 100 lm |

1.2 **Specifications.** The Specifications for each Product are set out in Exhibit A to this Order Form. They cover basis weight, thickness, air permeability, tensile strength, finish, scrim construction and temperature rating, together with the tolerances and test methods for each. The Parties may add Products or amend the Specifications only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

1.3 **Certificates of Analysis.** Each certificate of analysis delivered under Section 4.2 of the Agreement shall report, for the applicable lot, measured values for each parameter listed in Exhibit A.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Order Form and the Agreement (the "**Initial Term**") is three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Order Form and the Agreement shall renew automatically for successive three (3) year Renewal Terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal not more than two hundred ten (210) days and not less than one hundred eighty (180) days before the expiration of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

2.3 **Terms During Renewal.** Each Renewal Term is on the terms of the Agreement and this Order Form as in effect at the end of the preceding term, including Section 3 of this Order Form. Prices during any Renewal Term are subject to adjustment under Section 5.

2.4 **Notices of Non-Renewal.** Notices of non-renewal shall be given in accordance with Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

## 3. MINIMUM ANNUAL PURCHASE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Minimum Commitment.** In each Contract Year during the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, Buyer shall purchase Products with an aggregate invoiced price of at least $6,200,000 (the "**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**").

3.2 **Amounts Counted.** The following count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for a Contract Year:

(a) the invoiced price, excluding taxes, of Products delivered in that Contract Year and not rejected under Section 7 of the Agreement;

(b) the price, at the prices in Section 4, of Products ordered by Buyer under accepted Purchase Orders for delivery in that Contract Year that Supplier fails to deliver in that Contract Year; and

(c) purchases of substitute products from third parties that count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment under Section 16.3 of the Agreement.

Service Credits do not reduce the amounts counted under this Section 3.2.

3.3 **Shortfall Payment.** If the amounts counted under Section 3.2 for a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment, Supplier may invoice Buyer for the difference (the "**Shortfall Amount**") within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year. The invoice shall include a reasonably detailed reconciliation. Buyer shall pay the Shortfall Amount in accordance with Section 5.4 of the Agreement, subject to Buyer's right to dispute amounts in good faith.

3.4 **Quarterly Reporting.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Supplier shall provide Buyer with a statement of the amounts counted toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the current Contract Year to date.

## 4. PRICING

4.1 **Prices.** The prices for the Products during the first Contract Year are:

|---|---|---|
| SNW-PE-500S | $7.85 | 1,000 lm |

4.2 **Inclusions.** As provided in Section 5.1 of the Agreement, prices include all packaging, labeling and delivery charges to the Delivery Location.

4.3 **Non-Standard Widths.** Products ordered in widths other than 2.0 m are priced pro rata to the width ordered. Any slitting charge must be agreed in writing before Supplier accepts the Purchase Order.

## 5. PRICE ADJUSTMENTS

5.1 **Annual Adjustment.** Effective on the first day of each Contract Year after the first, the price of each Product may be adjusted to reflect changes in the cost of its principal fiber. The adjustment equals sixty percent (60%) of the percentage change in the applicable index in Section 5.2, measured over the twelve (12) months ending three (3) months before the start of that Contract Year.

5.2 **Indices.** The applicable indices are:

(a) for polyester Products, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Producer Price Index for synthetic fibers manufacturing (or any successor index); and

(b) for PPS Products, the published contract price for PPS staple fiber agreed by the Parties in writing at the start of the Contract Year, or, absent agreement, the polyester index in Section 5.2(a).

5.3 **Cap on Adjustments.** No annual adjustment under Section 5.1 shall increase or decrease the price of any Product by more than four percent (4%).

5.4 **Notice.** Supplier shall give Buyer written notice of each proposed adjustment, with supporting index data, at least sixty (60) days before the start of the Contract Year to which it applies. If Supplier does not give timely notice, prices for that Contract Year remain unchanged.

5.5 **No Other Adjustments.** Prices may not be adjusted except under this Section 5 or by written agreement of the Parties.

## 6. ORDERING, LEAD TIME AND DELIVERY

6.1 **Lead Time.** Supplier shall deliver Products no later than twenty-one (21) days after acceptance of the Purchase Order, as provided in Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Delivery Location.** Unless a Purchase Order identifies another Buyer facility, the Delivery Location is Buyer's converting facility at 1400 Industrial Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19711.

6.3 **Receiving Hours.** Buyer's normal receiving hours at the facility in Section 6.2 are 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding Buyer's published holidays. Buyer may change its receiving hours on thirty (30) days' written notice to Supplier.

6.4 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall maintain at its facility finished-goods safety stock of each Product equal to at least two (2) weeks of Buyer's average forecasted requirements under Section 3.1 of the Agreement. Supplier shall make that stock available to fill Buyer's Purchase Orders.

## 7. CONTACTS

7.1 **Commercial Contacts.** Each Party shall designate in writing a commercial contact for forecasts, Purchase Orders and invoicing, and a quality contact for Specifications, certificates of analysis and nonconformities. A Party may change its designated contacts by written notice.

7.2 **Formal Notices.** Designation of contacts under Section 7.1 does not change the addresses for formal notices under Section 17.3 of the Agreement.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Incorporation.** This Order Form is governed by the Agreement, which is incorporated in it by reference.

8.2 **Counterparts.** This Order Form may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
