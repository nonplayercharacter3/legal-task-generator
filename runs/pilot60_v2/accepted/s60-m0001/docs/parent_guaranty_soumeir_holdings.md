# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of January 6, 2025 by **Soumeir Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and **Soumeir Nonwovens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), are entering into a Supply Agreement dated as of January 6, 2025 (as amended, restated, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under the Supply Agreement, Supplier will supply needle-punched polyester and PPS filter felt rolls to Buyer for use in industrial baghouse filtration.

B. Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Supply Agreement.

C. Buyer requires this Guaranty as a condition to entering into the Supply Agreement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement. In this Guaranty:

1.1 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations, liabilities and covenants under the Supply Agreement of Supplier and of any assignee or transferee of Supplier, whether now existing or arising later, whether absolute or contingent, and whether for payment or performance. These include:

(a) the obligation to supply and deliver Products in accordance with the Supply Agreement, the Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and accepted Purchase Orders;

(b) the obligation to pay or credit Service Credits, refunds, replacement costs and all other amounts payable to Buyer;

(c) all indemnification obligations under Section 10 of the Supply Agreement;

(d) all warranty obligations under Section 8 of the Supply Agreement; and

(e) all damages arising from any breach of the Supply Agreement.

1.2 "**Obligor**" means Supplier and any assignee or transferee of Supplier described in Section 1.1.

1.3 "**Enforcement Costs**" means all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Buyer incurs in enforcing this Guaranty.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Buyer the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due. This is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not merely of collection.

2.2 **Performance by Guarantor.** If any Obligor fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall, within ten (10) business days after Buyer's written demand, pay the amount due or perform, or cause to be performed, the obligation. Where the obligation is the supply of Products, Guarantor may perform by causing Products conforming to the Specifications to be supplied from another facility owned by Guarantor or its subsidiaries, subject to Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement.

2.3 **Enforcement Costs.** Guarantor shall also pay Buyer's Enforcement Costs on demand.

2.4 **No Increase in Liability.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty for any Guaranteed Obligation is no greater than the liability of the Obligor for that obligation under the Supply Agreement, except for Enforcement Costs and except as provided in Section 4.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been fully paid and performed and the Supply Agreement has expired or been terminated, including any transition period under Section 13.5 of the Supply Agreement and any survival period under Section 13.6 of the Supply Agreement.

3.2 **Direct Recourse.** Buyer may enforce this Guaranty directly against Guarantor. Buyer need not first make demand on, proceed against or exhaust any remedy against any Obligor, any other guarantor or any security.

3.3 **Amendments to Supply Agreement.** Buyer and Supplier may amend, extend, renew, restate or otherwise modify the Supply Agreement, the Order Form or the Service Level Schedule without notice to or consent of Guarantor. Guarantor's obligations extend to the Guaranteed Obligations as so modified. On Buyer's request, Guarantor shall deliver a written acknowledgment confirming that this Guaranty applies to the Supply Agreement as modified.

## 4. OBLIGATIONS ABSOLUTE

Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged, reduced or otherwise affected by:

(a) any extension of time, waiver, forbearance or indulgence granted to any Obligor;

(b) any amendment, renewal or modification of the Supply Agreement, the Order Form, the Service Level Schedule or any Purchase Order;

(c) any change in the corporate existence, structure or ownership of any Obligor, or any merger, consolidation or reorganization of any Obligor;

(d) any insolvency, bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding affecting any Obligor, or any rejection or disaffirmance of the Supply Agreement in such a proceeding;

(e) any invalidity, unenforceability or avoidance of any Guaranteed Obligation, other than by reason of full payment and performance;

(f) any defense, set-off or counterclaim that any Obligor may have against Buyer, other than full payment and performance; or

(g) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable discharge of a surety or guarantor.

## 5. WAIVERS

5.1 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor or nonperformance, other than the demand provided for in Section 2.2;

(c) any right to require Buyer to proceed first against any Obligor or any other person; and

(d) all suretyship defenses.

5.2 **Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been fully paid and performed, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against any Obligor arising from payment or performance under this Guaranty.

5.3 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty is reinstated if any payment or performance of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Buyer in any insolvency, bankruptcy or similar proceeding, as though the payment or performance had not been made.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Buyer that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(c) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action and do not violate its organizational documents, any Applicable Law or any agreement binding on it;

(d) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity; and

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the outstanding equity interests in Supplier.

## 7. COVENANTS

7.1 **Financial Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Guarantor, Guarantor shall deliver to Buyer its annual consolidated financial statements, audited by an independent certified public accounting firm.

7.2 **Notice of Material Events.** Guarantor shall notify Buyer in writing within five (5) business days after Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the voting equity of Supplier.

7.3 **Confidentiality.** Buyer shall treat Guarantor's non-public financial statements as Confidential Information of Supplier under Section 14 of the Supply Agreement.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

8.2 **Venue.** Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty. Guarantor waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

8.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** GUARANTOR AND BUYER EACH WAIVE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

8.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty shall be in writing and delivered as provided in Section 17.3 of the Supply Agreement. Notices to Guarantor shall be sent to the address set out below Guarantor's signature, or to another address Guarantor designates by notice to Buyer.

8.5 **Assignment.** Guarantor may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Guaranty without Buyer's prior written consent. Buyer may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of Buyer's rights under the Supply Agreement. This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Buyer and its successors and permitted assigns.

8.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Guarantor and Buyer. No failure or delay by Buyer in exercising any right under this Guaranty is a waiver of that right.

8.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

8.8 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
