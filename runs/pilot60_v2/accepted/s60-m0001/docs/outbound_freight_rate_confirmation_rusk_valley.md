# OUTBOUND FREIGHT RATE CONFIRMATION

**Rate Confirmation No. RVFL-MFM-2026-114**

**Date Issued:** October 5, 2026

This Rate Confirmation is issued by **Rusk Valley Freight Lines, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Carrier**"), to **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Shipper**"). It records the rates and service terms for Shipper's outbound truckload and less-than-truckload shipments from Shipper's facility. Carrier and Shipper are each a "**Party**."

## 1. SHIPMENT DETAILS

1.1 **Origin.** Shipper's converting facility at 1400 Industrial Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19711 (the "**Origin**").

1.2 **Commodity.** Finished filter bags, cages, cartridges and related industrial filtration products, packed in cartons on standard 48 in. × 40 in. pallets. Freight is non-hazardous and does not require temperature control.

1.3 **Equipment.** 53-foot dry van trailers for truckload shipments. Liftgate equipment on request for LTL deliveries to destinations without a dock.

1.4 **Pickup Hours.** Shipper's Origin shipping hours are 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding Shipper's published holidays. Carrier shall arrive within the pickup window stated in each tender.

## 2. RATE PERIOD

The rates in this Rate Confirmation apply to shipments tendered from November 1, 2026 through October 31, 2027 (the "**Rate Period**"). Either Party may end the Rate Period early by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other. Shipments tendered before the effective date of that notice remain subject to this Rate Confirmation.

3.2 **LTL Rates.** LTL shipments are rated at Carrier's base rates for freight class 85, less a discount of sixty-eight percent (68%), subject to a minimum charge of $165 per shipment.

3.3 **Fuel Surcharge.** Carrier may apply a fuel surcharge calculated weekly from the U.S. Energy Information Administration national average on-highway diesel price, using the schedule in Exhibit 1.

Carrier shall not bill any accessorial charge that is not listed above unless Shipper approves it in writing before the service is performed.

## 5. TENDERS AND SERVICE

5.1 **Tenders.** Shipper shall tender loads by email or through Carrier's online portal at least twenty-four (24) hours before the requested pickup time. Carrier shall accept or decline each tender within two (2) business hours.

5.2 **Transit.** Carrier shall deliver within the standard transit time for each lane published in Exhibit 2. Carrier shall notify Shipper's shipping contact promptly of any expected delay, the cause and the revised delivery estimate.

5.3 **Proof of Delivery.** Carrier shall provide a signed proof of delivery for each shipment within twenty-four (24) hours after delivery.

5.4 **No Brokering.** Carrier shall transport all loads on its own equipment with its own drivers. Carrier may not broker, re-tender or interline any load without Shipper's prior written consent.

## 6. LIABILITY FOR LOSS AND DAMAGE

6.1 **Carrier Liability.** Carrier is liable for loss of, damage to or delay of freight in its possession as provided in 49 U.S.C. § 14706. Carrier's liability per truckload shipment is limited to $100,000, and per LTL shipment to $25.00 per pound, unless Shipper declares a higher value on the bill of lading.

6.2 **Claims.** Shipper shall file any loss or damage claim within nine (9) months after delivery or, for non-delivery, after a reasonable time for delivery has passed. Carrier shall acknowledge each claim within thirty (30) days and pay, decline or make a firm compromise offer within one hundred twenty (120) days after receipt.

6.3 **Insurance.** Carrier shall maintain auto liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit, motor truck cargo insurance of at least $100,000 per load, commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law. Carrier shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 7. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Invoices.** Carrier shall invoice each shipment after delivery. Each invoice shall reference Shipper's bill of lading number and include the proof of delivery.

7.2 **Payment.** Shipper shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after receipt. Shipper may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith on written notice to Carrier stating the reason for the dispute.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Authority.** Carrier represents that it holds active operating authority from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration and that its safety rating is not "Unsatisfactory."

8.2 **Governing Law.** This Rate Confirmation is governed by applicable federal law and, to the extent not preempted, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.

8.3 **Amendment.** This Rate Confirmation may be changed only by a revised rate confirmation or other writing signed by both Parties.

8.4 **Acceptance.** Shipper accepts this Rate Confirmation by signing below or by tendering a shipment under it during the Rate Period.
