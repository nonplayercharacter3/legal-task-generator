# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Soumeir Nonwovens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). Buyer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Supplier are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of January 6, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier supplies needle-punched polyester and PPS filter felt rolls to Buyer for use in industrial baghouse filtration.

B. In June 2026, Supplier failed to deliver several shipments of Products by the applicable Delivery Dates. Supplier has stated that the failures resulted from flooding that disrupted operations at its finishing line, and that the failures are excused Force Majeure Events under Section 16 of the Agreement. The third of these failures to constitute a Critical Delivery Failure, as defined in the Service Level Schedule, occurred on June 12, 2026. Buyer has notified Supplier in writing that it disputes Supplier's characterization of the June 2026 failures as excused force majeure.

C. Moseth Group Holdings, LLC, the parent of Buyer, has entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated August 28, 2026 with Larchfield Capital Partners, L.P. Under that agreement, Moseth Group Holdings, LLC will sell all of the outstanding equity of Buyer to Larchfield Capital Partners, L.P. The closing is expected to occur in the first quarter of 2027.

D. Supplier has introduced a proprietary scrim construction and finish chemistry for certain Products. The Parties wish to add an intellectual property infringement indemnity to the Agreement, to restate Supplier's product indemnity, and to make the other changes set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference to the Agreement in any other document, means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. AMENDMENT OF SECTION 10 (INDEMNIFICATION)

2.1 **Deletion of Section 10.1.** Section 10.1 is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 10.1 **Supplier Intellectual Property Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim alleging that any Product, as delivered by Supplier, infringes any United States patent or misappropriates any trade secret. This Section 10.1 does not apply to the extent a claim arises from (a) Specifications or Buyer Materials provided by Buyer, (b) modification of the Product by anyone other than Supplier, or (c) combination of the Product with materials or equipment not supplied by Supplier, where the claim would not have arisen but for that combination.

2.2 **Amendment of Section 10.2.** The last sentence of Section 10.2 is deleted and replaced with the following:

> This indemnity does not apply to the extent a claim is covered by Section 10.1 or Section 10.3.

2.3 **Renumbering.** Existing Section 10.3 (Procedure) is renumbered as Section 10.4.

2.4 **New Section 10.3.** The following is inserted as new Section 10.3 of the Agreement:

> 10.3 **Supplier Product Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and customers from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim alleging:
>
> (a) bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property, including fire, explosion or emissions events at any baghouse or other installation, caused in whole or in part by any Product; or
>
> (b) any defect in design, materials or manufacture of any Product, or any failure of any Product to conform to the Specifications or the warranties in Section 8.1.
>
> The indemnity covers claims under any theory of product liability, negligence, strict liability, breach of warranty or violation of Applicable Law. It does not apply to the extent a claim arises from Buyer's negligence or willful misconduct.

## 3. AMENDMENT OF SECTION 4.5 (LATE DELIVERY NOTICE)

Section 4.5 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 4.5 **Late Delivery Notice.** Supplier shall notify Buyer promptly, and in any event within one (1) business day, after it becomes aware that any delivery will be late. The notice shall state the cause, the affected Purchase Orders and the revised expected Delivery Date. If Supplier expects a delay of more than five (5) business days, Supplier shall also deliver to Buyer, within three (3) business days after the notice, a written recovery plan. The plan shall describe the steps Supplier will take to restore on-time delivery, including any use of alternate production lines or expedited freight at Supplier's cost.

## 4. AMENDMENT OF SECTION 12.1(a) (INSURANCE)

Section 12.1(a) of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, which may be met through umbrella coverage, and which shall include coverage for Supplier's contractual liability under Section 10;

## 5. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

5.1 **Effectiveness.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment Date.

5.2 **Agreement Otherwise Unchanged.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement, the Order Form and the Service Level Schedule remain unchanged and in full force and effect. The Parties ratify and confirm the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.3 **Guarantor Acknowledgment.** Supplier shall deliver to Buyer, within ten (10) business days after the Amendment Date, a written acknowledgment by Soumeir Industrial Holdings, Inc. that the Parent Guaranty applies to Supplier's obligations under the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

6.2 **Venue.** Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of or relating to this Amendment.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
