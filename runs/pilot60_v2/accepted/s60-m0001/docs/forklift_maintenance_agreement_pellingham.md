# EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Equipment Maintenance Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Pellingham Equipment Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Contractor**"). Customer and Contractor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates a fleet of electric and propane-powered forklifts and related material handling equipment at its converting facility at 1400 Industrial Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19711.

B. Contractor provides inspection, preventive maintenance and repair services for industrial forklifts and material handling equipment.

C. Customer wishes to engage Contractor to maintain its equipment on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Covered Equipment**" means the forklifts, battery chargers and other material handling equipment listed in Exhibit A, as updated under Section 2.3.

1.2 "**Facility**" means Customer's facility at 1400 Industrial Parkway, Newark, Delaware 19711.

1.3 "**Preventive Maintenance**" means the scheduled inspection, lubrication, adjustment and replacement of wear parts described in Exhibit B.

1.4 "**Repair Services**" means diagnosis and repair of Covered Equipment that is malfunctioning or damaged, other than Preventive Maintenance.

1.5 "**Services**" means the Preventive Maintenance, Repair Services and other services Contractor performs under this Agreement.

1.6 "**Emergency Call**" means a request for Repair Services for Covered Equipment that is inoperable or unsafe to operate.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Preventive Maintenance.** Contractor shall perform Preventive Maintenance on each unit of Covered Equipment at the intervals stated in Exhibit B. The interval is measured in operating hours or calendar days, whichever occurs first.

2.2 **Repair Services.** Contractor shall perform Repair Services on Customer's request. Contractor shall respond to Emergency Calls with a qualified technician on site within four (4) hours during Customer's operating hours. Contractor shall respond to all other requests within two (2) business days.

2.3 **Changes to Covered Equipment.** Customer may add or remove units of Covered Equipment by written notice to Contractor. Monthly fees under Section 4.1 shall be adjusted from the first day of the month following the notice, at the per-unit rates in Exhibit C.

2.4 **Inspection Reports.** Contractor shall give Customer a written service report after each visit. The report shall identify the units serviced, the work performed, the parts replaced and any safety deficiencies found.

2.5 **Safety Deficiencies.** If Contractor finds that any unit is unsafe to operate, Contractor shall tag the unit out of service and notify Customer's facility manager immediately.

2.6 **Standards.** Contractor shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the equipment manufacturers' recommendations and 29 C.F.R. § 1910.178.

## 3. PERSONNEL AND SITE ACCESS

3.1 **Technicians.** Contractor shall use trained technicians with experience servicing the makes and models of the Covered Equipment.

3.2 **Site Rules.** While at the Facility, Contractor's personnel shall comply with Customer's safety, security and environmental rules provided to Contractor in writing, including lockout/tagout procedures.

3.3 **Hazardous Materials.** Contractor shall remove and lawfully dispose of used oil, hydraulic fluid, filters and spent batteries generated by the Services, at Contractor's cost.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Monthly Fee.** Customer shall pay the monthly Preventive Maintenance fee stated in Exhibit C for each unit of Covered Equipment.

4.2 **Repair Services.** Customer shall pay for Repair Services at the hourly labor rates in Exhibit C. Parts are charged at Contractor's list price less the discount in Exhibit C. Contractor shall obtain Customer's written approval before performing any single repair estimated to exceed $2,500.

4.3 **Invoices and Payment.** Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Customer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith on written notice to Contractor.

4.4 **Rate Adjustments.** Contractor may adjust the rates in Exhibit C once in each year, effective on an anniversary of the Effective Date, on at least sixty (60) days' written notice. No adjustment shall exceed three percent (3%).

## 5. WARRANTY

5.1 **Workmanship.** Contractor warrants that the Services will be free from defects in workmanship for ninety (90) days after performance. Contractor shall re-perform any nonconforming Services at no charge.

5.2 **Parts.** Contractor shall pass through to Customer all manufacturers' warranties on parts it installs.

5.3 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, CONTRACTOR MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 6. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

6.1 **Insurance.** Contractor shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;

(b) automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of at least $1,000,000; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000.

Contractor shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policy in Section 6.1(a) and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

6.2 **Indemnity.** Contractor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its officers, directors and employees from all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of third-party claims for bodily injury, death or property damage to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or its personnel.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 **Cap.** Except for Contractor's obligations under Section 6.2 and a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, each Party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable to Contractor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

7.2 **Consequential Damages.** Except for Contractor's obligations under Section 6.2, neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for one (1) year. It then renews for successive one (1) year terms unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

8.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for any reason on sixty (60) days' written notice to Contractor.

8.3 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

8.4 **Effect of Termination.** On termination, Customer shall pay for Services performed through the effective date of termination. Sections 5, 6, 7 and 9 survive termination.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Independent Contractor.** Contractor is an independent contractor.

9.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets.

9.3 **Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

9.4 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses below the Parties' signatures. A notice is effective on receipt.

9.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended or waived only by a writing signed by both Parties.

9.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
