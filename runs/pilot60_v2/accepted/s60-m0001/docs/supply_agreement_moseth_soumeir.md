# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 6, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Soumeir Nonwovens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). Buyer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures needle-punched polyester and polyphenylene sulfide ("PPS") filter felt in roll form for use in industrial baghouse filtration.

B. Buyer converts filter felt into filter bags and related filtration media for sale to industrial end users.

C. Buyer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, filter felt rolls on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms have the meanings given in this Section 1 or elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, ordinances and orders that apply to a Party or to the Products, including environmental, health and safety, export control and trade laws.

1.3 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

1.4 "**Delivery Date**" means the date Products arrive at Buyer's Delivery Location. Products arrive at the Delivery Location when the carrier makes them available for unloading at Buyer's receiving dock during Buyer's normal receiving hours.

1.5 "**Delivery Location**" means the Buyer facility identified in the applicable Purchase Order or, if none is identified, Buyer's facility stated in the Order Form.

1.6 "**Order Form**" means the Order Form for filter felt executed by the Parties under this Agreement. The Order Form sets out the Initial Term, pricing, minimum purchase commitments and other commercial terms.

1.7 "**Parent Guaranty**" means the Parent Guaranty made by Soumeir Industrial Holdings, Inc. ("**Guarantor**") in favor of Buyer in respect of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement.

1.8 "**Products**" means the needle-punched polyester and PPS filter felt rolls described in the Specifications and the Order Form, as updated by the Parties in writing.

1.9 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products that Buyer issues under this Agreement.

1.10 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule executed by the Parties under this Agreement. It sets out the delivery service levels, Service Credits and related remedies.

1.11 "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications for each Product attached to or referenced in the Order Form. These include basis weight, thickness, air permeability, tensile strength, finish, scrim construction and temperature rating, as amended by written agreement of the Parties.

1.12 "**Buyer Materials**" means all specifications, drawings, forecasts, pricing, customer information and other information that Buyer provides to Supplier, together with any tooling Buyer provides.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues for the Initial Term, and any Renewal Term, set out in the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 13 (the "**Term**").

2.2 **Survival of Purchase Orders.** Any Purchase Order accepted before expiration or termination of this Agreement remains governed by this Agreement until fully performed, unless Buyer elects otherwise in writing.

## 3. FORECASTS AND PURCHASE ORDERS

3.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar month, Buyer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling six (6) month forecast of its Product requirements. Forecasts are for planning only. They do not commit Buyer to purchase any quantity except as stated in the Order Form.

3.2 **Purchase Orders.** Buyer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products, quantities, requested Delivery Date and Delivery Location.

3.3 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within three (3) business days of receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it is inconsistent with this Agreement or exceeds the most recent forecast by more than twenty percent (20%). A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

3.4 **Lead Time.** Unless the Order Form states otherwise, Supplier shall deliver Products no later than twenty-one (21) days after acceptance of the Purchase Order.

3.5 **Conflicting Terms.** Any preprinted or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or similar document are of no effect, except as the Parties expressly agree in writing.

## 4. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

4.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the Delivery Location on or before the requested Delivery Date, DAP (Incoterms 2020) the Delivery Location. Supplier shall select and engage the carrier, and the carrier acts on Supplier's behalf.

4.2 **Packaging.** Supplier shall package each roll on a core suitable for Buyer's unwind equipment. Each roll shall be wrapped in moisture-resistant film and labeled with Product code, lot number, roll number, length, width and date of manufacture. Supplier shall ship each roll with a certificate of analysis for the applicable lot.

4.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Buyer on the Delivery Date.

4.4 **Service Levels.** Supplier shall meet the delivery service levels in the Service Level Schedule. Service Credits and other remedies under the Service Level Schedule are in addition to Buyer's other rights under this Agreement, except as the Service Level Schedule expressly provides.

4.5 **Late Delivery Notice.** Supplier shall notify Buyer promptly, and in any event within one (1) business day, after it becomes aware that any delivery will be late. The notice shall state the cause and the revised expected Delivery Date.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Buyer shall pay the prices for Products set out in the Order Form. Prices include all packaging, labeling and delivery charges to the Delivery Location.

5.2 **Price Adjustments.** Prices may be adjusted only as provided in the Order Form.

5.3 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Buyer on or after the Delivery Date for each shipment. Each invoice shall reference the applicable Purchase Order number.

5.4 **Payment.** Buyer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Buyer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith. Buyer shall notify Supplier of the dispute in writing with reasonable detail, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve it.

5.5 **Set-Off.** Buyer may set off against amounts payable to Supplier any Service Credits and any other amounts Supplier owes Buyer under this Agreement.

5.6 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Supplier shall state those taxes separately on each invoice where required by Applicable Law. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. QUALITY

6.1 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015, or an equivalent system Buyer approves in writing, at each facility that manufactures Products.

6.2 **Lot Testing.** Supplier shall test each production lot against the Specifications before shipment. Supplier shall retain test records and retained samples for at least three (3) years after shipment.

6.3 **Changes.** Supplier shall not change any raw material, fiber source, scrim, finish chemistry, manufacturing process or manufacturing location for any Product without at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to Buyer and Buyer's written approval.

6.4 **Audit.** Upon ten (10) business days' notice, Buyer or its designee may audit Supplier's facilities, quality records and processes relating to the Products during normal business hours. Buyer may conduct no more than two (2) audits in any Contract Year unless an audit reveals a material nonconformity.

## 7. INSPECTION AND REJECTION

7.1 **Inspection.** Buyer may inspect Products within thirty (30) days after the Delivery Date. Buyer may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications, the applicable Purchase Order or the warranties in Section 8.

7.2 **Remedy for Rejected Products.** At Buyer's election, Supplier shall either (a) replace rejected Products within ten (10) days at Supplier's expense or (b) credit or refund the price of the rejected Products. Supplier shall bear the cost of return shipping and disposal of rejected Products.

7.3 **No Waiver.** Buyer's inspection, acceptance or payment does not waive any warranty or other right of Buyer.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that for eighteen (18) months after the Delivery Date, each Product will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship;

(c) be merchantable; and

(d) be manufactured, packaged and labeled in compliance with Applicable Law.

8.2 **Title Warranty.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products free of all liens and encumbrances.

8.3 **Warranty Remedy.** If a Product breaches the warranty in Section 8.1, Supplier shall replace the Product or refund its price at Buyer's election. Supplier shall also reimburse Buyer's reasonable costs of removing, handling and disposing of the nonconforming Product. Buyer's remedies under this Section 8.3 are without prejudice to Section 10.

8.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that:

(a) it is duly organized and in good standing;

(b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and

(c) this Agreement is a binding obligation enforceable against it.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND BUYER MATERIALS

9.1 **Buyer Materials.** Buyer retains all right, title and interest in the Buyer Materials. Supplier shall use the Buyer Materials solely to perform this Agreement.

9.2 **Supplier IP.** Supplier retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing manufacturing know-how and processes. Supplier grants Buyer a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, sell and incorporate the Products into Buyer's products.

9.3 **Custom Specifications.** Specifications developed at Buyer's request and expense are Buyer Materials. Supplier shall not sell Products made to those Specifications to any third party.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **Supplier Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and customers from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim alleging:

(a) bodily injury, death or damage to real or tangible personal property, including fire, explosion or emissions events at any baghouse or other installation, caused in whole or in part by any Product; or

(b) any defect in design, materials or manufacture of any Product, or any failure of any Product to conform to the Specifications or the warranties in Section 8.1.

The indemnity covers claims under any theory of product liability, negligence, strict liability, breach of warranty or violation of Applicable Law. It does not apply to the extent a claim arises from Buyer's negligence or willful misconduct.

10.2 **Buyer Indemnity.** Buyer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim to the extent it arises from Buyer's negligent conversion, fabrication or installation of Products. This indemnity does not apply to the extent a claim is covered by Section 10.1.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim. Delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay materially prejudices it. The indemnifying Party controls the defense and settlement of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its choice at its own expense. The indemnifying Party may not settle any claim without the indemnified Party's prior written consent if the settlement imposes any obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Buyer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.2 **Exclusions.** The limitation in Section 11.1, and the exclusion in Section 11.3, do not apply to:

(a) Supplier's obligations under Section 10.1;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 14 (Confidentiality);

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Buyer's obligation to pay for Products delivered and accepted.

11.3 **Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 **Coverage.** Throughout the Term and for three (3) years afterward, Supplier shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, which may be met through umbrella coverage;

(b) property insurance covering Buyer Materials in Supplier's possession at replacement value; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by Applicable Law.

12.2 **Certificates.** Supplier shall name Buyer as an additional insured on the policy described in Section 12.1(a). Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request and shall give at least thirty (30) days' notice of cancellation or material reduction of coverage.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) becomes insolvent or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(b) files, or has filed against it, a petition in bankruptcy that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(c) has a receiver appointed for a substantial part of its assets.

13.3 **Service Level Termination.** Buyer may terminate this Agreement as provided in the Service Level Schedule.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement:

(a) Buyer shall pay for conforming Products delivered before the effective date of termination;

(b) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information under Section 14; and

(c) Supplier shall return all Buyer Materials.

13.5 **Transition.** On any termination other than by Supplier under Section 13.1 or Section 13.2, Supplier shall continue to supply Products at the then-current prices for up to six (6) months, if Buyer so requests in writing, to permit Buyer to transition to an alternate source.

13.6 **Survival.** Sections 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.4, 13.5, 14, 16 and 17 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

14.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the Buyer Materials, the Specifications, forecasts, pricing and the terms of this Agreement. Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

14.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement. It shall protect Confidential Information with at least reasonable care. It may disclose Confidential Information only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 14.

14.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order. It shall give the disclosing Party prompt notice, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

14.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 14 continue for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement. For trade secrets, they continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under Applicable Law.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

15.1 **Assignment by Buyer.** Buyer may not assign or transfer this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay that consent.

15.2 **Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may not assign or transfer this Agreement, or delegate any of its obligations under this Agreement, except to an assignee that satisfies both of the following conditions:

(a) the assignee assumes all of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement in a writing delivered to Buyer; and

(b) the assignee's obligations under this Agreement constitute Guaranteed Obligations under the Parent Guaranty.

Supplier may make an assignment that meets these conditions without Buyer's consent. Supplier shall give Buyer written notice of the assignment within ten (10) business days after it takes effect.

15.3 **Change of Control.** A Change of Control of Buyer is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Buyer and requires the prior written consent of Supplier. "**Change of Control**" means any direct or indirect acquisition by any person or group, in one transaction or a series of transactions, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of Buyer, whether by stock purchase, merger, consolidation or otherwise. If a Change of Control of Buyer occurs without that consent, Supplier may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer.

15.4 **Void Assignments.** Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 15 is void.

15.5 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 **Excused Performance.** A Party is excused from performance, other than payment obligations, to the extent and for so long as performance is prevented by an event beyond its reasonable control that it could not have avoided by reasonable precautions (a "**Force Majeure Event**"). Force Majeure Events include fire, flood, earthquake, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action and labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees. Force Majeure Events do not include:

(a) changes in market conditions or raw material prices;

(b) the affected Party's financial condition; or

(c) shortages of raw materials, labor or equipment, unless those shortages are themselves caused by a Force Majeure Event.

16.2 **Notice and Mitigation.** The affected Party shall notify the other Party within three (3) business days after the Force Majeure Event begins. The notice shall describe the event and its expected duration. The affected Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the event and to resume performance.

16.3 **Allocation and Alternate Supply.** During any Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier, Supplier shall allocate its available production of Products to Buyer at least in proportion to Buyer's share of Supplier's Product volume in the preceding twelve (12) months. Buyer may purchase substitute products from third parties, and those purchases count toward any minimum purchase commitment in the Order Form.

16.4 **Extended Force Majeure.** If a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier continues for more than forty-five (45) days, Buyer may terminate this Agreement or any affected Purchase Order by written notice without liability.
