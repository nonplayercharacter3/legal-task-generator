# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of January 6, 2025 under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement dated as of January 6, 2025 (the "**Agreement**") between **Moseth Filtration Media, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Soumeir Nonwovens, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Schedule:

1.1 "**Critical Delivery Failure**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

1.2 "**Delivered Quantity**" means, for a Shipment, the quantity of conforming Products, in linear meters, included in that Shipment.

1.3 "**Delivery Date**" means, for purposes of this Schedule, the date Supplier tenders the Products to the carrier at Supplier's facility for shipment to the Delivery Location, as shown on the bill of lading.

1.4 "**Late Shipment**" means a Shipment whose Delivery Date falls after its Required Date.

1.5 "**Measurement Month**" means each calendar month during the Term.

1.6 "**On-Time Delivery Rate**" means, for a Measurement Month, the number of Shipments with a Required Date in that month that are On-Time Shipments, divided by the total number of Shipments with a Required Date in that month, expressed as a percentage.

1.7 "**On-Time Shipment**" means a Shipment whose Delivery Date falls on or before its Required Date and whose Delivered Quantity is at least ninety-eight percent (98%) of the Ordered Quantity.

1.8 "**Ordered Quantity**" means the quantity of Products, in linear meters, ordered for a Shipment under an accepted Purchase Order.

1.9 "**Required Date**" means the delivery date requested in an accepted Purchase Order or, if none is stated, the last day of the lead time under Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

1.10 "**Service Credit**" means a credit payable by Supplier to Buyer under Section 3.

1.11 "**Shipment**" means each line item of an accepted Purchase Order with a separate Required Date.

## 2. SERVICE LEVELS

2.1 **On-Time Delivery.** Supplier shall achieve an On-Time Delivery Rate of at least ninety-five percent (95%) in each Measurement Month (the "**On-Time Delivery Target**").

2.2 **Fill Rate.** For each Shipment, Supplier shall deliver a Delivered Quantity of at least ninety-eight percent (98%) of the Ordered Quantity. Supplier shall not deliver more than one hundred five percent (105%) of the Ordered Quantity without Buyer's prior written approval.

2.3 **Certificate of Analysis.** Supplier shall deliver a complete certificate of analysis meeting Section 1.3 of the Order Form with each Shipment. A Shipment delivered without a complete certificate of analysis is not an On-Time Shipment until the certificate is delivered.

2.4 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall maintain the safety stock required by Section 6.4 of the Order Form. Supplier shall report its safety stock levels for each Product in each monthly report under Section 6.1.

## 3. SERVICE CREDITS

3.1 **Late Shipments.** For each Late Shipment, Supplier shall credit Buyer the following percentage of the price of the Products in that Shipment:

| Days Delivery Date Falls After Required Date | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 business days | 2% |
| 3 to 5 business days | 5% |
| More than 5 business days | 10% |

3.2 **Monthly Shortfall.** If Supplier fails to meet the On-Time Delivery Target in a Measurement Month, Supplier shall also credit Buyer one percent (1%) of the total price of all Shipments with a Required Date in that month for each full percentage point by which the On-Time Delivery Rate falls below the On-Time Delivery Target.

3.3 **Monthly Cap.** The Service Credits payable for any Measurement Month shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the total price of all Shipments with a Required Date in that month.

3.4 **Application.** Supplier shall show Service Credits for each Measurement Month on its monthly report under Section 6.1. It shall apply them against the next invoice issued after that report. Buyer may also set off Service Credits under Section 5.5 of the Agreement. Any Service Credits not applied by the end of the Term shall be paid to Buyer in cash within thirty (30) days.

3.5 **Nature of Service Credits.** The Parties agree that Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of late or short Shipments and are not a penalty. As provided in Section 4.4 of the Agreement, Service Credits are in addition to Buyer's other rights and remedies under the Agreement. Service Credits do not reduce the amounts counted toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment under Section 3.2 of the Order Form.

## 4. CRITICAL DELIVERY FAILURES AND CHRONIC FAILURE

4.1 **Critical Delivery Failure.** A "**Critical Delivery Failure**" occurs on the date that any of the following first occurs for a Shipment:

(a) the fifth (5th) business day after its Required Date passes without a Delivery Date for that Shipment;

(b) the Delivery Date occurs with a Delivered Quantity of less than eighty percent (80%) of the Ordered Quantity, and Supplier does not ship the balance within five (5) business days after the Required Date; or

(c) Supplier notifies Buyer under Section 4.5 of the Agreement that it will be unable to ship the Shipment within five (5) business days after its Required Date.

Each Shipment can give rise to no more than one Critical Delivery Failure.

4.2 **Chronic Failure Termination.** If three (3) or more Critical Delivery Failures occur in any rolling ninety (90) day period, Buyer may terminate the Agreement, in whole or as to any affected Product, by written notice to Supplier given within sixty (60) days after the third Critical Delivery Failure in that rolling ninety (90) day period. The termination takes effect on the date stated in Buyer's notice, which may be immediate.

4.3 **Effect of Chronic Failure Termination.** If Buyer terminates under Section 4.2:

(a) Buyer has no liability to Supplier for the termination;

(b) the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which the termination takes effect is reduced pro rata to the portion of that Contract Year before termination, and, for a termination as to fewer than all Products, is further reduced by the share of the prior twelve (12) months' purchases attributable to the terminated Products;

(c) no Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment applies to any later period for the terminated Products; and

(d) Section 13.5 of the Agreement applies.

4.4 **Recovery Plan.** Within five (5) business days after any Critical Delivery Failure, Supplier shall deliver to Buyer a written recovery plan describing the cause of the failure and the steps Supplier will take to prevent recurrence. Delivery or implementation of a recovery plan does not affect Buyer's rights under Section 4.2.

## 5. EXCLUSIONS

5.1 **Excused Shipments.** A Shipment does not count as a Late Shipment or a Critical Delivery Failure, and no Service Credit accrues on it, to the extent the failure is caused by:

(a) a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier, if Supplier has complied with Sections 16.2 and 16.3 of the Agreement;

(b) Buyer's written request to defer the Required Date, given before the Required Date; or

(c) Buyer's failure to make its receiving dock available during its normal receiving hours under Section 6.3 of the Order Form.

5.2 **Burden.** Supplier has the burden of showing that an exclusion under Section 5.1 applies. Supplier shall identify each Shipment for which it claims an exclusion, and the basis of the claim, in the monthly report for the Measurement Month concerned.

5.3 **Disputes.** If Buyer disputes an exclusion claimed by Supplier, the Shipment counts toward the Service Credits and Critical Delivery Failures until the dispute is resolved under Section 7.2.

## 6. REPORTING AND REVIEW

6.1 **Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) business days after the end of each Measurement Month, Supplier shall deliver to Buyer a report showing, for that month:

(a) each Shipment with its Required Date, Delivery Date, Ordered Quantity and Delivered Quantity;

(b) the On-Time Delivery Rate;

(c) each Late Shipment and each Critical Delivery Failure;

(d) any exclusions claimed under Section 5.1;

(e) the Service Credits accrued; and

(f) safety stock levels for each Product.

6.2 **Supporting Records.** Supplier shall retain bills of lading, carrier tracking records and shipping documents for each Shipment for at least three (3) years and shall provide copies to Buyer on request within five (5) business days.

6.3 **Quarterly Review.** The Parties' commercial contacts under Section 7.1 of the Order Form shall meet, in person or by video conference, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter to review performance under this Schedule and any open recovery plans.

## 7. GENERAL

7.1 **Incorporation.** This Schedule is governed by the Agreement, which is incorporated in it by reference.

7.2 **Disputes.** The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute regarding a monthly report within thirty (30) days after Buyer notifies Supplier of the dispute. Either Party may then pursue its remedies under the Agreement.

7.3 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
