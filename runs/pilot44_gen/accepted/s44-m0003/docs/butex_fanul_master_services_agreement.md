# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or this "**MSA**") is entered into and made effective as of 1 September 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Butex Auto Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates wholesale used-vehicle auction facilities. At those facilities, consignors deliver vehicles for sale to licensed dealers and other wholesale buyers.

B. Provider supplies vehicle condition reports, reconditioning, lot management and yard transport services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to perform those services at Customer's facilities on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND STRUCTURE

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations, ordinances and binding governmental orders that apply to a Party or to the Services.

(b) "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

(c) "**Consignor**" means any person that delivers a Vehicle to a Facility for sale through Customer's auction.

(d) "**Facility**" means each auction location of Customer identified in a Statement of Work.

(e) "**Fees**" means the amounts payable by Customer to Provider under Section 4 and each Statement of Work.

(f) "**Inspection Standards**" means the vehicle condition grading and inspection standards set out in the Quality Agreement or, until a Quality Agreement is executed, in the applicable Statement of Work.

(g) "**Quality Agreement**" means any quality agreement between the Parties that supplements this Agreement and sets out service levels, Inspection Standards and quality procedures for the Services.

(h) "**Services**" means the vehicle condition reports, reconditioning, lot management, yard transport and related services described in this Agreement and in each Statement of Work.

(i) "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work signed by both Parties that references this Agreement.

(j) "**Vehicle**" means any motor vehicle that a Consignor or Customer delivers to, or holds at, a Facility, including its keys, titles, accessories and contents.

1.2 **Statements of Work.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work. Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement and forms part of it. Each SOW shall identify the Facilities, the scope of the Services, the Fees, staffing levels and any Facility-specific requirements.

1.3 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties may enter into a Quality Agreement to supplement this Agreement. A Quality Agreement binds the Parties only once both Parties have executed it.

1.4 **Order of Precedence.** If the documents comprising this Agreement conflict, the following order of precedence applies: the executed Quality Agreement, then this MSA, then Statements of Work.

1.5 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this MSA unless stated otherwise.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a diligent, professional and workmanlike manner;

(b) in accordance with the Inspection Standards and each Statement of Work; and

(c) in compliance with Applicable Law, including laws relating to odometer disclosure, environmental handling of fluids and waste, and motor vehicle operation.

2.2 **Condition Reports.** Provider shall prepare an accurate condition report for each Vehicle that Customer designates for inspection. Provider shall deliver each report through Customer's designated platform within the turnaround time stated in the applicable SOW. Provider shall not knowingly omit or misstate any material defect.

2.3 **Reconditioning.** Provider shall perform reconditioning work only when it has Customer's written or electronic authorization. Provider shall use parts and materials of the type and quality specified in the applicable SOW.

2.4 **Lot Management and Yard Transport.** Provider shall:

(a) receive, stage, park, track and release Vehicles at each Facility;

(b) move Vehicles between lots, reconditioning bays and auction lanes; and

(c) keep accurate, real-time location records for every Vehicle in its care, custody or control.

Provider shall release a Vehicle only on Customer's instruction and against Customer's gate-release documentation.

2.5 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Provider shall be Customer's exclusive third-party provider of the Services at each Facility designated as exclusive in a Statement of Work. In return, Provider shall charge the pricing set out in that Statement of Work.

2.6 **Facility Rules.** While at a Facility, Provider's personnel shall comply with Customer's safety, security, drug-free workplace and Facility access rules, as notified to Provider from time to time.

## 3. PERSONNEL

3.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified and properly trained personnel to perform the Services. Each driver must hold a valid driver's license and meet the driving record standards in the applicable SOW. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Customer.

3.2 **Removal.** Customer may, acting reasonably, require Provider to remove any individual from a Facility. Provider shall promptly provide a qualified replacement.

3.3 **Background Checks.** Before any individual is assigned to a Facility, Provider shall conduct a background check and a motor vehicle record check on that individual, to the extent permitted by Applicable Law.

3.4 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, payroll taxes, workers' compensation and supervision of its personnel.

3.5 **Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Statement of Work.

4.2 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes. Provider is responsible for taxes on its own income, property and personnel.

4.3 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Each invoice must itemize Services by Facility and by Vehicle stock number, where applicable.

4.4 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it.

4.5 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, if it notifies Provider in writing of the basis for the dispute. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve the dispute. Customer may set off against any Fees any amount Provider owes Customer under this Agreement.

4.6 **Records and Audit.** Provider shall keep complete and accurate records supporting its invoices for three (3) years after the relevant invoice date. On reasonable notice, Provider shall make those records available for audit by Customer.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS AND QUALITY

5.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the service levels set out in each Statement of Work and, once executed, the Quality Agreement.

5.2 **Corrective Action.** If Provider fails to meet a service level, Provider shall promptly investigate the root cause. Within ten (10) business days it shall deliver a written corrective action plan and then implement that plan.

5.3 **Re-Performance.** If any condition report or reconditioning work is inaccurate or nonconforming, Provider shall re-perform it at no additional charge.

## 6. CUSTODY OF VEHICLES

6.1 **Care, Custody and Control.** A Vehicle is in Provider's care, custody or control from the time Provider's personnel take possession of it, its keys or its location assignment until the time Provider returns the Vehicle to Customer or releases it under Section 2.4.

6.2 **Standard of Care.** Provider shall use at least the degree of care that a prudent professional vehicle handler would use for Vehicles in its care, custody or control.

6.3 **Damage Reporting.** Provider shall report any loss of or damage to a Vehicle to Customer within twenty-four (24) hours after discovering it. The report must include photographs and a written description.

6.4 **Title.** Provider acquires no title to, lien on or security interest in any Vehicle. Provider waives any mechanic's, garage keeper's, possessory or other lien it might otherwise assert over any Vehicle.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA

7.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, software and know-how.

7.2 **Deliverables.** Condition reports, photographs, inspection data and other deliverables prepared for Customer under this Agreement become the property of Customer upon payment in full.

7.3 **Customer Data.** All Consignor, buyer, lane, pricing, sale-results and Vehicle data that Provider receives or generates in performing the Services ("**Customer Data**") is owned by Customer. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information. Provider shall use Customer Data only to perform the Services.

7.4 **Security.** Provider shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect Customer Data. Provider shall notify Customer within forty-eight (48) hours after discovering any unauthorized access to Customer Data.

## 8. INSURANCE

8.1 **Required Coverage.** Throughout the Term, Provider shall maintain at its own expense the following insurance, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of at least $2,000,000;

(c) garage keepers legal liability insurance, on a direct primary basis, of at least $1,500,000 per location;

(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits, and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000; and

(e) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $5,000,000.

8.2 **Additional Insured; Waiver.** Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies described in Sections 8.1(a), (b) and (e). Each policy described in Section 8.1 shall include a waiver of subrogation in favor of Customer.

8.3 **Certificates.** Before starting the Services, and on each policy renewal, Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the coverage required by this Section 8. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

8.4 **No Limitation.** The insurance required by this Section 8 does not limit Provider's liability under this Agreement.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information that one Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other Party (the "**Recipient**") and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Confidential Information includes Customer Data.

9.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall:

(a) protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care;

(b) use that information only to perform or receive the Services; and

(c) disclose that information only to its personnel and advisers who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;

(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

9.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law. Where legally permitted, the Recipient shall first give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation.

9.5 **Return.** When this Agreement ends, or earlier at the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of:

(a) loss of or damage to Vehicles in Provider's care, custody or control;

(b) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by Provider or its personnel;

(c) Provider's breach of Section 7 or Section 9; or

(d) Provider's negligence, willful misconduct or violation of Applicable Law.

10.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 10.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable to Provider in the 12 months preceding the claim.

10.3 **Exclusions from Cap.** The limitation in Section 10.2 does not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under this Agreement;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 9;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) loss of or damage to Vehicles in Provider's care, custody or control. That loss or damage is excluded from the cap.

10.4 **Consequential Damages.** Except for the matters described in Section 10.3, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits.

10.5 **Procedure.** A Party seeking indemnification shall:

(a) give prompt written notice of the claim;

(b) allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, although no settlement may impose any obligation on the indemnified Party without its consent; and

(c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

11.1 **Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that:

(a) it is duly organized and in good standing;

(b) it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and

(c) its performance of this Agreement will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

11.2 **Provider.** Provider further represents and warrants that:

(a) it holds, and will maintain, all licenses and permits required to perform the Services; and

(b) the Services will conform to this Agreement, the Inspection Standards and each Statement of Work.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the MSA Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Section 12.

12.2 **Renewal.** When the Initial Term expires, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three (3) year Renewal Terms (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"). Renewal does not occur if either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party not more than one hundred twenty (120) and not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Statement of Work, by written notice if the other Party:

(a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it; or

(b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.4 **Termination for Repeated Service Failures.** Customer may terminate the affected Statement of Work by written notice if Provider fails the same service level in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period.

12.5 **Transition.** On expiration or termination, Provider shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days at the rates then in effect. That assistance includes:

(a) an orderly handover of all Vehicles, keys, titles and location records; and

(b) the return of all Customer Data.

12.6 **Survival.** Sections 4, 6.4, 7, 9, 10, 12.5, 12.6, 14, 15 and 16, and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive, survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. FORCE MAJEURE

13.1 Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, severe weather, epidemics, war, terrorism or governmental action. This Section does not excuse payment obligations. To rely on it, the affected Party must notify the other Party promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. Section 13.1 does not relieve Provider of its obligations under Section 6.2 to safeguard Vehicles in its care, custody or control.

13.2 If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing the Services at a Facility for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days, Customer may obtain substitute services for that Facility without breaching Section 2.5. Customer may also terminate the affected Statement of Work by written notice.

## 14. NOTICES

14.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given:

(a) when delivered by hand;

(b) one (1) business day after it is sent by nationally recognized overnight courier; or

(c) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, provided that a copy is also sent by one of the methods in (a) or (b).

Notices must be sent to the addresses stated on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 **Relationship.** Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.

15.2 **Assignment.** Customer shall not assign this Agreement or any SOW, in whole or in part, without Provider's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of the business to which this Agreement relates. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 15.2 is void.

15.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of that right.

15.4 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

15.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible. The remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

15.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## 16. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

16.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

16.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation, other than for injunctive relief, a Party shall refer the dispute to the Parties' senior executives. Those executives shall meet within fifteen (15) business days to attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute.

16.4 **Equitable Relief.** A breach of Section 7 or Section 9 may cause irreparable harm. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

16.5 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
