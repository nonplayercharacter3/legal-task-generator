# TERM SHEET

## Proposed Master Services Arrangement

**Butex Auto Auctions, Inc. / Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC**

**Date:** 15 July 2026

**Status:** Non-binding, except as stated in Section 15.

This term sheet sets out the principal terms on which **Butex Auto Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Butex**" or "**Customer**"), proposes to engage **Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Fanul**" or "**Provider**"), to provide vehicle services at Butex's wholesale auction facilities. The Parties intend to record these terms in a definitive Master Services Agreement (the "**MSA**"). The MSA will be supplemented by Statements of Work and, if the Parties agree one, a Quality Agreement.

## 1. Parties

| | |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Butex Auto Auctions, Inc., a Delaware corporation operating wholesale used-vehicle auctions |
| **Provider** | Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC, a Texas limited liability company providing vehicle condition reports, reconditioning, lot management and yard transport services |

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 Provider will perform the following at Butex auction facilities identified in Statements of Work (each a "**Facility**"):

(a) vehicle condition reports;

(b) reconditioning;

(c) lot management; and

(d) yard transport.

2.2 Each Statement of Work will specify the following for each Facility:

(a) the scope of the Services;

(b) the fees;

(c) staffing levels;

(d) turnaround times; and

(e) any Facility-specific requirements.

2.3 Inspection and condition-grading standards will initially be set out in the Statements of Work. The Parties may later move those standards into a Quality Agreement, together with service levels and quality procedures.

2.4 Provider will release a vehicle only on Butex's instruction and against Butex gate-release documentation. Provider will acquire no lien on any vehicle and will waive any lien it might otherwise assert.

## 3. Exclusivity and Pricing

3.1 Provider will be Butex's exclusive third-party provider of the Services at each Facility that a Statement of Work designates as exclusive.

3.2 In return for exclusivity, Provider will charge the pricing set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

3.3 If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing at a Facility for an extended period, Butex may obtain substitute services for that Facility.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term will be three years from the MSA effective date. The target effective date is 1 September 2026.

4.2 **Renewal.** After the initial term, the MSA will renew automatically for one-year renewal terms.

4.3 **Non-Renewal.** Either Party may prevent renewal by written notice at any time at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

4.4 **Termination.** Either Party may terminate for uncured material breach, subject to a 30-day cure period, or for the other Party's insolvency. Butex may also terminate an affected Statement of Work for repeated service-level failures.

4.5 **Transition.** On expiry or termination, Provider will provide up to 90 days of transition assistance at then-current rates. That assistance will include handover of vehicles, keys, titles, location records and Customer data.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 Fees will be as set out in each Statement of Work. Provider will invoice monthly in arrears, itemized by Facility and, where applicable, by vehicle stock number.

5.2 Butex will pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt. Butex may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith and may set off amounts that Provider owes it.

5.3 Provider will keep supporting records for three years after the invoice date. Those records will be subject to audit by Butex.

## 6. Personnel and Subcontracting

6.1 Provider will use qualified, trained personnel. Provider will run background and motor vehicle record checks on each individual before assigning that individual to a Facility, to the extent the law permits.

6.2 Butex may, acting reasonably, require Provider to remove any individual from a Facility.

6.3 Provider will not subcontract without Butex's prior written consent. Provider will remain responsible for its subcontractors.

## 7. Vehicle Custody, Liability and Indemnity

7.1 **Custody.** Provider will be responsible for vehicles in its care, custody or control and will apply a prudent professional handler's standard of care. Provider will report any loss or damage within 24 hours, with photographs.

7.2 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at fees paid or payable to Provider in the 12 months preceding the claim.

7.3 **Exclusions from Cap.** The following will be uncapped:

(a) indemnification obligations;

(b) breach of confidentiality;

(c) gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; and

(d) loss of or damage to vehicles in Provider's care, custody or control.

7.4 **Indemnity.** Provider will indemnify Butex against third-party claims arising from any of the following:

(a) vehicle loss or damage;

(b) bodily injury or property damage;

(c) breach of the data or confidentiality obligations; or

(d) Provider's negligence, willful misconduct or violation of law.

7.5 **Consequential Damages.** The MSA will include a mutual exclusion of consequential damages, except for the uncapped matters listed in Section 7.3.

## 8. Insurance

Provider will maintain the following insurance, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

| **Coverage** | **Minimum Limit** |
|---|---|
| Commercial general liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence / $4,000,000 aggregate |
| Commercial automobile liability | $2,000,000 combined single limit |
| Garage keepers legal liability (direct primary) | $1,500,000 per location |
| Workers' compensation | Statutory limits |
| Employer's liability | $1,000,000 |
| Umbrella / excess liability | $5,000,000 |

Butex will be named as an additional insured on the general liability, automobile and umbrella policies. All policies will include a waiver of subrogation in favor of Butex. Provider will deliver certificates of insurance before starting the Services and on each policy renewal.

## 9. Assignment

9.1 **Restriction.** Neither party may assign without the other's consent; never to a Competing Auction Operator.

9.2 **Definition.** "**Competing Auction Operator**" means any person, or any affiliate of a person, that operates or owns a wholesale used-vehicle auction, whether physical or online, in competition with Butex.

## 10. Data and Intellectual Property

10.1 All consignor, buyer, lane, pricing, sale-results and vehicle data will be owned by Butex as Customer data. Provider will use that data only to perform the Services and will protect it with reasonable safeguards.

10.2 Condition reports and other deliverables will become Butex property once paid for.

10.3 Each Party will retain its pre-existing materials.

## 11. Confidentiality

The MSA will contain mutual confidentiality obligations on customary terms, including customary exclusions and provisions for compelled disclosure. Customer data will be treated as Butex confidential information.

## 12. Governing Law and Disputes

12.1 The MSA will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

12.2 The exclusive forum will be the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

12.3 Before litigation, disputes will be escalated to senior executives.

12.4 The MSA will include a mutual jury waiver.

## 13. Conditions

Completion of the MSA is subject to the following:

(a) satisfactory due diligence by Butex on Provider's licensing, insurance and operational capacity;

(b) agreement of initial Statements of Work for the first Facilities; and

(c) internal approvals by each Party.

## 14. Timetable

The Parties will work toward execution of the MSA with an effective date of 1 September 2026.

## 15. Binding Provisions

15.1 This term sheet is an expression of intent only. It creates no binding obligation on either Party, except for this Section 15.

15.2 No Party is obliged to enter into the MSA unless and until it executes the MSA.

15.3 Each Party will bear its own costs.

15.4 Each Party will keep the terms of this term sheet confidential.

15.5 This Section 15 is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
