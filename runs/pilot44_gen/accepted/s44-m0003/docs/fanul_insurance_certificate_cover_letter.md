## 1. Purpose of This Letter

1.1 We are the insurance broker of record for **Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC** ("**Fanul**"). We write at Fanul's request.

1.2 We enclose a certificate of insurance evidencing Fanul's current coverage. The certificate is delivered to **Butex Auto Auctions, Inc.** ("**Butex**") under Section 8.3 of the Master Services Agreement between Butex and Fanul effective 1 September 2026 (the "**MSA**"). Section 8.3 requires Fanul to deliver certificates of insurance before it starts the Services and on each policy renewal.

1.3 Butex is shown on the certificate as certificate holder.

## 2. Summary of Coverage

2.1 The enclosed certificate evidences the following policies. Each policy period runs from 1 August 2026 to 1 August 2027.

| **Coverage** | **Insurer** | **A.M. Best Rating** | **Limits** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial general liability (occurrence form) | Trinity Mutual Casualty Company | A VIII | $2,000,000 per occurrence / $4,000,000 general aggregate |
| Commercial automobile liability (any auto) | Trinity Mutual Casualty Company | A VIII | $2,000,000 combined single limit |
| Garage keepers legal liability (direct primary basis) | Brazos Specialty Insurance Company | A- VII | $1,500,000 per location |
| Workers' compensation | Pecos Workers Indemnity Company | A- VIII | Statutory limits |
| Employer's liability | Pecos Workers Indemnity Company | A- VIII | $1,000,000 each accident / each employee / policy limit |
| Umbrella liability (follow form) | Red River Excess Insurance Company | A IX | $5,000,000 each occurrence and aggregate |

2.2 Each insurer listed above carries an A.M. Best rating of A- VII or better, as required by Section 8.1 of the MSA.

2.3 The garage keepers coverage is written on a direct primary basis. It applies to vehicles of others in Fanul's care, custody or control at each scheduled location, regardless of Fanul's legal liability. Butex's Facilities identified in the initial Statements of Work have been added to the location schedule.

## 3. Endorsements

3.1 **Additional Insured.** Butex is named as an additional insured by endorsement on the following policies:

(a) the commercial general liability policy;

(b) the commercial automobile liability policy; and

(c) the umbrella liability policy.

3.2 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each policy listed in Section 2.1 includes a waiver of subrogation endorsement in favor of Butex.

3.3 **Notice of Cancellation.** Each policy listed in Section 2.1 has been endorsed to provide Butex, as certificate holder, with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction in coverage. This supports Fanul's notice obligation under Section 8.3 of the MSA.

3.4 Copies of the additional insured, waiver of subrogation and notice of cancellation endorsements are enclosed with the certificate.

## 4. Renewal
