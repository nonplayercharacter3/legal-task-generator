# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into by and between **Butex Auto Auctions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Fanul Vehicle Services, LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." It takes effect on the date the last Party signs it (the "**QA Effective Date**").

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Supplement to the MSA.** This Quality Agreement supplements the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated and effective 1 September 2026 (the "**MSA**"). It is the Quality Agreement referred to in Sections 1.1(g) and 1.3 of the MSA.

1.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement sets out the Inspection Standards, service levels and quality procedures that apply to the Services at each Facility. Those Services are:

(a) vehicle condition reports;

(b) reconditioning;

(c) lot management; and

(d) yard transport.

1.3 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work continues to set out, for each Facility:

(a) the Fees;

(b) staffing levels;

(c) turnaround times; and

(d) Facility-specific requirements.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **MSA Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA. These include "Applicable Law," "Consignor," "Facility," "Fees," "Inspection Standards," "Services," "Statement of Work" or "SOW," and "Vehicle."

2.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Condition Grade**" means the numerical grade from 0.0 to 5.0 that Provider assigns to a Vehicle under Section 4.2.

(b) "**Damage Report**" means the report of loss of or damage to a Vehicle that Provider must deliver under Section 6.3 of the MSA and Section 8.1 of this Quality Agreement.

(c) "**Nonconformance**" means any condition report, reconditioning work or lot or transport activity that does not meet the Inspection Standards, a Service Level or this Quality Agreement.

(d) "**Quality Contact**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 3.1.

(e) "**Service Level**" means each performance standard set out in Section 9.

(f) "**Vehicle Damage**" means any physical loss of, damage to, theft of or destruction of a Vehicle while it is in Provider's care, custody or control under Section 6.1 of the MSA. This includes damage to its keys, titles, accessories and contents.

## 3. QUALITY ORGANIZATION

3.1 **Quality Contacts.** Within ten (10) business days after the QA Effective Date, each Party shall designate a Quality Contact by notice to the other Party. Provider's Quality Contact is responsible for Provider's compliance with this Quality Agreement at all Facilities. Each Party may replace its Quality Contact by notice.

3.2 **Site Leads.** Provider shall appoint a site lead for each Facility. Each site lead shall be present during all auction days and all Vehicle check-in periods.

3.3 **Quality Reviews.** The Quality Contacts shall meet at least monthly. At each meeting they shall review the following for the preceding month:

(a) Service Level performance;

(b) open Nonconformances;

(c) Damage Reports; and

(d) the status of corrective action plans.

3.4 **Quarterly Business Reviews.** Senior representatives of each Party shall meet quarterly to review overall quality performance and trends.

## 4. INSPECTION STANDARDS

4.1 **Adoption.** From the QA Effective Date, the standards in this Section 4 and in Schedule A are the Inspection Standards for all purposes of the MSA. They replace any inspection standards previously set out in a Statement of Work.

4.2 **Condition Grading.** Provider shall assign each inspected Vehicle a Condition Grade under the grading scale in Schedule A. The grade shall reflect:

(a) exterior condition;

(b) interior condition;

(c) tires and wheels;

(d) glass;

(e) mechanical and drivetrain observations; and

(f) any announced conditions.

4.3 **Inspection Points.** Each condition report shall cover at least the inspection points listed in Schedule A. Those points include:

(a) the odometer reading;

(b) a VIN verification against title documents, where available;

(c) any warning lights displayed at start-up;

(d) any frame or structural damage indicators;

(e) any evidence of flood, fire or hail damage;

(f) open safety recalls, as reported by Customer's designated data source; and

(g) any aftermarket modifications.

4.4 **Photographs.** Each condition report shall include at least thirty-six (36) photographs taken under the angle and lighting protocol in Schedule A. Each damaged panel shall also be photographed close up.

4.5 **Inspector Qualification.** Before any inspector prepares condition reports, the inspector must complete Provider's grading certification program. Each inspector must recertify annually. Provider shall maintain training records and shall make them available to Customer on request.

4.6 **Calibration.** Each quarter, at each Facility, Provider shall re-inspect a sample of at least two percent (2%) of inspected Vehicles. Re-inspections shall be performed by an inspector other than the original inspector. Provider shall report the variance in Condition Grade to Customer.

## 5. CONDITION REPORT ACCURACY

5.1 **Accuracy Standard.** A condition report is accurate if:

(a) the Condition Grade is within 0.5 of the grade determined on re-inspection; and

(b) the report discloses every announced condition and every damage item that the Inspection Standards require to be disclosed.

5.2 **Arbitration Claims.** Customer may notify Provider of any buyer arbitration claim, post-sale inspection claim or Consignor dispute that alleges an inaccurate condition report. Provider shall investigate and respond in writing within three (3) business days after notice.

5.3 **Re-Performance.** Section 5.3 of the MSA applies to any inaccurate condition report.

## 6. RECONDITIONING QUALITY

6.1 **Authorization.** Provider shall perform reconditioning only under a work order authorized by Customer under Section 2.3 of the MSA. Each work order shall itemize the following:

(a) labor operations;

(b) parts and materials; and

(c) estimated cost.

6.2 **Workmanship Standards.** Provider shall perform paintless dent repair, paint and body, detail, glass and light mechanical work in accordance with Schedule B and the manufacturer's specifications. Provider shall use parts and materials of the type and quality specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

6.3 **Final Inspection.** Before a reconditioned Vehicle is released to the lane, a Provider technician other than the technician who performed the work shall inspect it. Provider shall record the result of that inspection on the work order.

6.4 **Comebacks.** Any reconditioning work that fails within thirty (30) days after completion shall be re-performed at no charge.

## 7. LOT MANAGEMENT AND YARD TRANSPORT

7.1 **Check-In.** Provider shall check in each Vehicle within four (4) hours after it arrives at a Facility. Each check-in shall record:

(a) the VIN;

(b) the odometer reading;

(c) the number of keys;

(d) a walk-around inspection with photographs; and

(e) the Vehicle's assigned lot location.

7.2 **Location Accuracy.** Provider shall maintain the real-time location records required by Section 2.4 of the MSA in Customer's yard management system. Provider shall conduct a full physical lot audit at each Facility at least weekly.

7.3 **Key Control.** Provider shall store all keys in locked key-control systems. Provider shall log every key issue and return.

7.4 **Driving Standards.** Drivers shall observe a maximum on-lot speed of ten (10) miles per hour and shall use spotters in congested lane areas. Drivers shall not use mobile devices while operating a Vehicle.

7.5 **Release.** Provider shall release a Vehicle only as provided in Section 2.4 of the MSA.

## 8. VEHICLE DAMAGE

8.1 **Reporting.** Provider shall report Vehicle Damage within the time and in the form required by Section 6.3 of the MSA. Each Damage Report shall also state:

(a) the Vehicle's lot location;

(b) the Provider personnel involved; and

(c) the check-in condition of the Vehicle, as recorded under Section 7.1.

8.2 **Investigation.** Within five (5) business days after a Damage Report, Provider shall complete an investigation and deliver its findings to Customer's Quality Contact. Provider shall preserve all relevant photographs, video footage and location records.

8.3 **Repair and Claims Handling.** Customer may elect to have a damaged Vehicle repaired by Provider, at Provider's cost, or by a repair facility Customer selects. Customer shall provide Provider with repair estimates and invoices for Vehicle Damage. Customer shall also provide copies of any Consignor or buyer claims arising from Vehicle Damage.

8.4 **Vehicle Damage.** Notwithstanding Section 10.3(d) of the MSA, Provider's liability for Vehicle Damage, including Vehicle Damage arising from any failure to meet the Inspection Standards, is subject to the limitation of liability in Section 10.2 of the MSA. That limitation is fees paid or payable to Provider in the 12 months preceding the claim. Provider's liability for Vehicle Damage is further limited, for each Vehicle, to the reasonable cost of repairing that Vehicle, not to exceed $2,500 per Vehicle.

8.5 **Insurance.** Provider shall tender Vehicle Damage claims to its garage keepers legal liability insurer under Section 8.1(c) of the MSA.

## 9. SERVICE LEVELS

9.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the following Service Levels at each Facility. Each Service Level is measured monthly.

| **Service Level** | **Target** |
|---|---|
| Condition report turnaround | 98% within the turnaround time stated in the applicable SOW |
| Condition report accuracy (Section 5.1) | 97% of re-inspected Vehicles |
| Vehicle check-in (Section 7.1) | 99% within four (4) hours of arrival |
| Lot location accuracy (Section 7.2) | 99.5% at weekly audit |
| Lane delivery | 99% of scheduled Vehicles in lane before the lane's start time |
| Reconditioning completion | 95% by the date stated on the work order |
| Damage Report timeliness (Section 8.1) | 100% within twenty-four (24) hours |

9.2 **Reporting.** By the tenth (10th) business day of each month, Provider shall deliver a Service Level report for the preceding month. The report shall cover each Facility and include supporting data.

9.3 **Failures.** Sections 5.2 and 12.4 of the MSA apply to any failure to meet a Service Level.

## 10. NONCONFORMANCES, CORRECTIVE ACTION AND AUDIT

10.1 **Nonconformance Log.** Provider shall keep a log of all Nonconformances. For each Nonconformance the log shall record:

(a) the Vehicle stock number, where applicable;

(b) the date;

(c) a description;

(d) the root cause; and

(e) the corrective action taken.

10.2 **Corrective Action Plans.** Provider shall prepare and implement corrective action plans in accordance with Section 5.2 of the MSA.

10.3 **Quality Audits.** On five (5) business days' notice, Customer may audit Provider's compliance with this Quality Agreement at any Facility. Customer may conduct one audit per Facility per calendar quarter, and may conduct further audits following a Service Level failure. Provider shall provide reasonable access to its records, systems and personnel.

10.4 **Records.** Provider shall retain the following for the period stated in Section 4.6 of the MSA:

(a) condition reports and photographs;

(b) work orders;

(c) check-in records;

(d) Damage Reports; and

(e) training records.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

11.1 The laws of the State of Texas govern this Quality Agreement and all disputes arising out of or relating to the Services, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

11.2 Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Dallas County, Texas for any action arising out of or relating to this Quality Agreement or the Services.

## 12. TERM AND AMENDMENT

12.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement begins on the QA Effective Date and continues until the MSA expires or terminates.

12.2 **Review.** The Parties shall review the Inspection Standards and the Service Levels at least annually.

12.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement, including its Schedules, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.
