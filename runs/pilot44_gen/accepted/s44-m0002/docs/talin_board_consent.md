# TALIN LIVE ENTERTAINMENT GROUP, INC.

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Dated as of October 13, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Talin Live Entertainment Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), act by unanimous written consent without a meeting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware and the Bylaws of the Corporation. The undersigned waive all requirements of notice and adopt the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted at a duly called meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 **The Proposed Transaction.** Management has presented to the Board a proposal for the Corporation to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Keelhaven Presents, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Keelhaven**"), from its sole stockholder, **Nasund Partners LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Seller**"). The acquisition would be made under a Stock Purchase Agreement between the Corporation, as buyer, and the Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Purchase Agreement is expected to be signed on or about October 14, 2026, and the Board has reviewed it in substantially final form (the "**Transaction**").

1.2 **Keelhaven's Business.** Keelhaven is a regional concert promoter. It owns and produces the annual Lanternfall Festival, a multi-day music festival held each August. The Lanternfall Festival generates the majority of Keelhaven's annual revenue, ticketing receipts and talent expense. Keelhaven also promotes and co-promotes individual concerts and tour dates throughout the year. Keelhaven's fiscal year ends on June 30.

1.3 **Principal Terms.** Management has summarized the following principal terms of the Purchase Agreement for the Board:

- (a) an aggregate purchase price for the Shares of $84,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to a customary net working capital adjustment against a target of $2,500,000;
- (b) the deposit at closing of $8,400,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") with Pellucid Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"), under an Escrow Agreement among the Corporation, the Seller and the Escrow Agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**");
- (c) a closing expected on December 1, 2026, subject to customary conditions, including delivery by Nasund Brands LLC, an affiliate of the Seller, of an IP Assignment Agreement (the "**IP Assignment Agreement**") assigning to Keelhaven the intellectual property used in Keelhaven's business; and
- (d) mutual indemnification obligations of the Corporation and the Seller, as set out in Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement.

1.4 **Materials Reviewed.** The Board has reviewed:

- (a) the Purchase Agreement in substantially final form;
- (b) the Escrow Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement in substantially final form;
- (c) management's financial and diligence presentation, including Keelhaven's audited financial statements for the fiscal years ended June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026; and
- (d) the financing plan described in Section 1.6.

The Board has also had the opportunity to ask questions of management and the Corporation's outside counsel and financial advisors.

1.5 **Integration and Audit Plan.** Management has presented a post-closing integration plan for Keelhaven. Under that plan:

- (a) Keelhaven will continue to operate as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Corporation under its existing name and management through the 2027 Lanternfall Festival in August 2027;
- (b) Keelhaven's talent booking, ticketing and festival operations for the 2027 Lanternfall Festival will be integrated into the Corporation's live events division following that festival;
- (c) Keelhaven will adopt the Corporation's financial reporting and internal controls in stages during calendar year 2027; and
- (d) the Corporation's independent auditors will conduct the first post-closing audit of Keelhaven following the fiscal year ending June 30, 2027. That audit will be consolidated with the Corporation's audit for the same period.

1.6 **Financing.** Management has advised the Board that the Corporation will fund the Purchase Price and related transaction expenses from cash on hand and borrowings under the Corporation's existing revolving credit facility. Management has confirmed that those borrowings are permitted under the terms of that facility without lender consent.

1.7 **Board Determination.** After considering the materials described above and the advice of management and its advisors, the Board has determined that the Transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders.

## 2. APPROVAL OF THE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

2.1 **RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the Corporation's performance of the Purchase Agreement and of the Transaction contemplated by it, are authorized, adopted and approved in all respects.

2.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement in the name and on behalf of the Corporation. The Purchase Agreement shall be substantially in the form presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves. That officer's execution of the Purchase Agreement shall be conclusive evidence of the approval.

## 3. APPROVAL OF ANCILLARY AGREEMENTS

3.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized, acting alone, to execute and deliver, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation:

- (a) the Escrow Agreement; and
- (b) each other agreement, certificate and instrument to which the Corporation is a party that is contemplated by the Purchase Agreement, including the officer's certificate required by Section 7.3(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

Each such document shall be in such form as the Authorized Officer executing it approves. That officer's execution of the document shall be conclusive evidence of the approval.

3.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to cause Keelhaven, after the closing, to accept and perform the IP Assignment Agreement as assignee.

## 4. FUNDING OF PURCHASE PRICE

4.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Corporation is authorized to pay the Purchase Price and all other amounts payable by the Corporation at the closing under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. This authority includes the deposit of the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent.

4.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to request borrowings under the Corporation's existing revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $50,000,000 to fund a portion of those amounts, and to execute and deliver any borrowing requests and related certificates.

## 5. POST-CLOSING MATTERS

5.1 **RESOLVED**, that, effective on the closing, the Authorized Officers are each authorized, as the sole stockholder of Keelhaven, to:

- (a) elect directors of Keelhaven designated by the Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation; and
- (b) accept the resignations of the directors and officers of Keelhaven delivered at closing under Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

5.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that management is directed to implement the integration and audit plan described in Section 1.5 and to report to the Board on its progress at each regular meeting of the Board through the meeting following completion of the first post-closing audit of Keelhaven.

## 6. GENERAL AUTHORITY

6.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to:

- (a) take all further actions;
- (b) execute and deliver all further agreements, certificates, instruments and documents;
- (c) make all filings with any governmental authority; and
- (d) pay all fees and expenses,

in each case as that officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

6.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer or agent of the Corporation in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 7. EFFECTIVENESS

7.1 This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. This consent shall be effective when signed by all of the directors of the Corporation and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
