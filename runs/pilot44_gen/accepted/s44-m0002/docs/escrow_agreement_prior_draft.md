# ESCROW AGREEMENT

*[Draft of November 4, 2026. Circulated for review by counsel to Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. Subject to further comment.]*

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 by and among **Talin Live Entertainment Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Nasund Partners LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pellucid Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Keelhaven Presents, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $8,400,000 of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Agent will hold that amount as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings given below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $8,400,000.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less all disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, together with a certificate of the prevailing party's counsel to that effect.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Appointment and Deposit.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment. On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller on the date of receipt.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Fund shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund only in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.3 No Interest of Third Parties.** The Escrow Fund is not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of Buyer or Seller. Neither of them may pledge, assign or otherwise transfer it except as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUND

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** Unless otherwise directed by Joint Written Instruction, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in a government money market fund that invests solely in direct obligations of the United States, or obligations fully guaranteed by the United States, and repurchase agreements secured by those obligations. The Escrow Agent may use an affiliated fund for that purpose.

**3.2 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund shall become part of the Escrow Fund. They shall be disbursed with the principal under Section 4 or Section 5.

**3.3 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as required to make a disbursement under this Agreement. It shall have no liability for any loss arising from investments made in accordance with this Section 3, or from their liquidation.

**3.4 Tax Reporting.** For tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until disbursement, and all earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver a duly executed IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUND

**4.1 Escrow Release Date.** The "**Escrow Release Date**" is the date that is 120 days after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Escrow Release Date is the next Business Day.

**4.2 Release on Escrow Release Date.** Within three Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the balance of the Escrow Fund, less the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices that were delivered before the Escrow Release Date and remain unresolved on that date (the "**Pending Claim Amount**").

**4.3 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount after the Escrow Release Date. As each pending claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for that claim in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order:
- (a) to Buyer, to the extent the claim is resolved in Buyer's favor; and
- (b) to Seller, to the extent the claim is resolved in Seller's favor.

**4.4 Final Disbursement.** When all pending claims have been resolved and all retained amounts disbursed, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any remaining balance of the Escrow Fund to Seller, and this Agreement shall terminate.

## 5. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

**5.1 Claim Notices.** If Buyer asserts a claim for indemnification under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") to Seller and the Escrow Agent before the Escrow Release Date. Each Claim Notice shall describe the basis of the claim in reasonable detail. It shall also state the amount claimed or, if that amount is not then determinable, Buyer's good-faith estimate of it.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to a Claim Notice by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 20 Business Days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice. The objection shall state the amount in dispute and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

**5.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive a timely objection under Section 5.2, the claimed amount shall be deemed undisputed. The Escrow Agent shall disburse that amount to Buyer from the Escrow Fund on the fifth Business Day after the objection period expires. If an objection disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer in the same manner.

**5.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall hold any disputed amount until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order directing its disbursement. The Escrow Agent shall then disburse that amount within three Business Days in accordance with that instruction or order.

**5.5 Joint Written Instructions.** Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver a Joint Written Instruction in two cases:
- (a) for any claim that they resolve by agreement; and
- (b) for any amount payable by Seller under Section 9.2 of the Purchase Agreement that is to be satisfied from the Escrow Fund under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

The Escrow Agent may at any time disburse all or any part of the Escrow Fund in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction.

**5.6 Insufficiency.** If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to satisfy any amount payable to Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the remaining balance to Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall have no further obligation in respect of the shortfall. Nothing in this Agreement limits Buyer's right to recover any shortfall directly from Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set out in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties apply. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller. It is not bound by, or charged with notice of, any of those agreements.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written instruction, notice or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed by an authorized representative of the appropriate party. On the Closing Date, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a certificate identifying its authorized representatives and their specimen signatures. Each may update its certificate by written notice.

**6.3 Security Procedures.** Before disbursing funds under any instruction, the Escrow Agent may confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an authorized representative named in the certificate under Section 6.2. The Escrow Agent may delay disbursement until it obtains that confirmation.

**6.4 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement, except for its own gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.5 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance under this Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to the extent any such amount is caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 6.5, unless the amount arises from the act or omission of only one of them.

**6.6 Disputes; Interpleader.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain of its duties, it may decline to act and may hold the Escrow Fund until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the Escrow Fund with a court of competent jurisdiction in interpleader.

**6.7 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**6.8 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent shall be paid the fees set out in Schedule A, together with its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct any fee or expense from the Escrow Fund without a Joint Written Instruction.

**6.9 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller, acting jointly, may remove the Escrow Agent on 30 days' written notice. In either case, Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. If no successor has been appointed when the notice period ends, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one. The Escrow Agent's duties end only when it delivers the Escrow Fund to the successor escrow agent.

**6.10 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall each provide such information as the Escrow Agent reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification requirements.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by any of the following methods to the addresses set out on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice:
- (a) personal delivery;
- (b) nationally recognized overnight courier; or
- (c) email with confirmation of transmission.

A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only on actual receipt.

**7.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**7.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. A successor escrow agent appointed under Section 6.9 shall succeed to the rights and obligations of the Escrow Agent. Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall be the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**7.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**7.5 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**7.6 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**7.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**7.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
