# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 14, 2026 by and between **Talin Live Entertainment Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Nasund Partners LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Keelhaven Presents, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is a regional concert promoter that owns and produces the annual Lanternfall Festival.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings given below:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the IP Assignment Agreement, and each other agreement, certificate and instrument delivered under this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Company IP**" means all Intellectual Property used in the business of the Company, including:
- (a) the LANTERNFALL marks and all other trademarks, service marks, logos and trade dress used in connection with the Lanternfall Festival;
- (b) the Company's fan contact database; and
- (c) the Company's ticketing software.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pellucid Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Escrow Agreement to be entered into at the Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning given in the Escrow Agreement.

"**Financial Statements**" has the meaning given in Section 4.5.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any court, agency, commission or other governmental or regulatory body.

"**Indebtedness**" means all obligations of the Company for borrowed money, all obligations evidenced by notes, bonds or similar instruments, all capital lease obligations, and all guarantees of any of the foregoing, together with accrued interest, prepayment premiums and breakage costs.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all trademarks, service marks, trade names, domain names, social media accounts, copyrights, software, databases, data compilations, trade secrets, know-how and other intellectual property and proprietary rights, whether registered or unregistered, and all registrations and applications for any of them.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the IP Assignment Agreement between Nasund Brands LLC, as assignor, and the Company, as assignee.

"**Knowledge of Seller**" means the actual knowledge of the managing members of Seller and of the chief executive officer, chief financial officer and head of talent buying of the Company, in each case after reasonable inquiry.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Liabilities**" means any liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature, whether accrued or fixed, absolute or contingent, matured or unmatured, or determined or determinable.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, Liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, results of operations or financial condition of the Company. Changes arising from general economic conditions or conditions generally affecting the live entertainment industry are excluded, except to the extent they disproportionately affect the Company.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Post-Closing Show Liabilities**" means all Liabilities and obligations under the Talent Agreements in respect of Shows scheduled to occur after the Closing Date, including artist guarantees, deposits, advances, bonuses, rider and production obligations, and settlement obligations, whether arising before or after the Closing Date.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period, or portion of a taxable period, ending on or before the Closing Date.

"**Show**" means any concert, festival performance, tour date or other live event promoted, co-promoted or produced by the Company, including each performance at the Lanternfall Festival.

"**Talent Agreements**" means all agreements, offers, deal memos and booking confirmations between the Company and any artist, performer, or artist's agent, manager or loan-out company for the performance of a Show.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, admissions, amusement, payroll, withholding, property and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions to tax.

"**Transaction Expenses**" means all fees and expenses incurred by the Company or Seller in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it, to the extent unpaid at the Closing.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $84,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

**2.2 Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $8,400,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated escrow account (the "**Escrow Fund**") and disburse it in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.3 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or cause to be paid, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:
- (a) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness set out in the payoff letters delivered under Section 7.2(c);
- (b) on behalf of the Company or Seller, the Transaction Expenses;
- (c) the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent; and
- (d) to Seller, the balance of the Purchase Price, after deducting the amounts in clauses (a) through (c) and as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment.

**2.4 Working Capital Adjustment.**
- (a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Working Capital**"). That estimate shall be prepared consistently with the Financial Statements. If the Estimated Working Capital exceeds $2,500,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), the Purchase Price shall be increased by the excess. If it falls short of the Target Working Capital, the Purchase Price shall be reduced by the shortfall. Either adjustment is the "**Estimated Working Capital Adjustment**."
- (b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller its calculation of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing. Any dispute that the parties do not resolve within 30 days shall be referred to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the parties. That firm's determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five Business Days after final determination.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law.

## ARTICLE 3. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on December 1, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 7 (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:
- (a) stock certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers attached;
- (b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;
- (c) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Nasund Brands LLC;
- (d) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;
- (e) the certificate required by Section 7.2(a); and
- (f) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.3, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate required by Section 7.3(a).

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set out in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes Seller's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Organization of the Company.** The Company is duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of New York. It is qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where qualification is required.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller. No options, warrants or other rights to acquire capital stock of the Company are outstanding.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement do not conflict with the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, violate any Law, or require the consent of any Person under any Material Contract, except as set out in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026, and the related statements of income and cash flows for the fiscal years then ended (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP, applied on a consistent basis. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no Liabilities except those reflected or reserved against in the June 30, 2026 balance sheet, and those incurred in the ordinary course of business since that date.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since June 30, 2026, the Company has operated in the ordinary course of business and there has been no Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party that involves payments exceeding $250,000 in any twelve-month period (the "**Material Contracts**"). The list includes each Talent Agreement, venue lease and license, sponsorship agreement and ticketing services agreement. Each Material Contract is valid and binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.9 Talent Agreements.** Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists, for each Talent Agreement in respect of a Show scheduled to occur after the date of this Agreement:
- (a) the artist and the Show date;
- (b) the guarantee and any bonus or percentage terms; and
- (c) all deposits paid or received, and the date of each.

All deposits received by the Company under the Talent Agreements have been recorded in the Company's books in accordance with GAAP.

**4.10 Intellectual Property.** Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered Company IP and all material unregistered Company IP. The Company owns or has a valid right to use all Company IP. Upon consummation of the IP Assignment Agreement, the Company will own all Company IP held by Nasund Brands LLC. To the Knowledge of Seller, the conduct of the Company's business does not infringe the Intellectual Property of any Person.

**4.11 Data Privacy.** The Company has collected, stored and used the personal information in its fan contact database in material compliance with applicable Law and with its published privacy policies.

**4.12 Ticketing and Receipts.** All ticketing receipts, facility fees and service charges collected for Shows have been accounted for and remitted in accordance with the applicable Material Contracts and Law.

**4.13 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it and has paid all Taxes shown as due on those returns. No Tax audit or proceeding is pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against the Company.

**4.14 Litigation.** No action, suit or proceeding is pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against the Company that would reasonably be expected to result in Losses exceeding $100,000.

**4.15 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is in material compliance with all applicable Laws. It holds all permits required to conduct its business, including all event, assembly and liquor permits for the Lanternfall Festival.

**4.16 Brokers.** No broker or finder is entitled to any fee from the Company in connection with this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Buyer has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. That execution and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price and all other amounts payable by it under this Agreement.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to any distribution of them in violation of applicable securities Laws.

## ARTICLE 6. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** Between the date of this Agreement and the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, Seller shall cause the Company not to:
- (a) enter into any Talent Agreement with a guarantee exceeding $150,000;
- (b) incur any Indebtedness;
- (c) sell, license or otherwise dispose of any Company IP; or
- (d) declare or pay any dividend or distribution.

**6.2 Access.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books and records, Talent Agreements and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article 7 and to obtain all third-party consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**6.4 Exclusivity.** Before the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement for the sale of the Company or its assets with any Person other than Buyer.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential, and shall cause its Affiliates to keep confidential, all confidential information concerning the Company.

**6.6 Non-Competition.** For three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, promote or produce any multi-day music festival within 250 miles of the Lanternfall Festival site.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as the other party reasonably requests to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 7. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** Each party's obligation to close is subject to the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority prohibiting the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:
- (a) The representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date. Seller has performed all of its covenants under this Agreement in all material respects. Buyer has received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller to that effect.
- (b) No Material Adverse Effect has occurred.
- (c) Buyer has received customary payoff letters for all Indebtedness.
- (d) Nasund Brands LLC has executed and delivered the IP Assignment Agreement, assigning all Company IP to the Company.
- (e) The Escrow Agreement has been executed and delivered by Seller and the Escrow Agent.
- (f) The third-party consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule have been obtained.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions:
- (a) The representations and warranties of Buyer are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date. Buyer has performed all of its covenants under this Agreement in all material respects. Seller has received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer to that effect.
- (b) Buyer has made the payments required by Section 2.3.

## ARTICLE 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:
- (a) by mutual written consent of the parties;
- (b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by January 31, 2027, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or
- (c) by either party, if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and the breach would cause a condition in Article 7 to fail and is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void and no party shall have any Liability under it, except for (i) Liability for willful breach and (ii) Liability under Sections 6.5 and 11.2 and this Section 8.2.

## ARTICLE 9. SURVIVAL AND INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller and of Buyer contained in this Agreement and in any certificate delivered under it shall survive the Closing until the Escrow Release Date. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing until fully performed in accordance with their terms.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or relating to:
- (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;
- (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;
- (c) any Pre-Closing Taxes; and
- (d) any Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or relating to:
- (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and
- (b) any Post-Closing Show Liabilities, including any Post-Closing Show Liabilities for which Seller or any of its Affiliates is or becomes liable as guarantor, co-promoter or otherwise.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for indemnification (a "**Claim Notice**"), describing the claim in reasonable detail. No claim may be made under Section 9.2(a) or Section 9.3(a) unless a Claim Notice for it is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1. Any claim for which a Claim Notice is not so delivered is irrevocably waived. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It may not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party.

**9.5 Limitations.**
- (a) The aggregate liability of Seller for claims under Section 9.2(a), and the aggregate liability of Buyer for claims under Section 9.3(a), shall not exceed the Escrow Amount.
- (b) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $420,000, after which Seller shall be liable from the first dollar.
- (c) The limitations in this Section 9.5 do not apply to Losses arising from fraud.
- (d) No party shall be liable for punitive damages, except to the extent payable to a third party.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any amount payable by Seller under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund, to the extent of funds remaining in it, by joint written instruction to the Escrow Agent under the Escrow Agreement. Any remaining amount shall then be satisfied directly by Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties after the Closing for any matter arising under this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** The parties shall treat all indemnification payments as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, consistently with past practice, and shall deliver each to Buyer for review at least 20 days before filing. Buyer shall prepare all other Tax returns of the Company.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of all transfer, documentary, stamp and similar Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares.

**10.3 Cooperation.** The parties shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of Tax returns and the conduct of any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.2 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to one or more of its Affiliates. No such assignment relieves Buyer of its obligations under this Agreement.

**11.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**11.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the IP Assignment Agreement shall govern the conveyance of Intellectual Property.

**11.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
