# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of December 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Nasund Brands LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Keelhaven Presents, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. **Talin Live Entertainment Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Nasund Partners LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignor is an Affiliate of Seller. Assignor holds record title to certain intellectual property used in connection with the business of Assignee, including in connection with the annual Lanternfall Festival.

C. Section 7.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement makes it a condition to Buyer's obligation to close that Assignor execute and deliver this Agreement. Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Agreement, duly executed by Assignor, at the Closing.

D. Assignor will benefit directly and indirectly from the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings given below:

"**Assigned Rights**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Company IP**" means solely the registered trademarks and service marks set out in Exhibit A, together with all registrations and pending applications for them.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, license, option, claim or other encumbrance of any kind.

"**Goodwill**" means all goodwill of the business connected with the use of, and symbolized by, the Company IP.

"**Recordation Forms**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective on the Effective Date and immediately before the Closing, Assignor irrevocably assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest throughout the world in and to the following (collectively, the "**Assigned Rights**"):

- (a) the Company IP;
- (b) the Goodwill;
- (c) all renewals and extensions of any registration or application included in the Company IP;
- (d) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable in respect of the Company IP; and
- (e) all claims and causes of action relating to the Company IP, whether accruing before, on or after the Effective Date, including the right to sue for and collect damages for past, present and future infringement, dilution or other violation of it.

**2.2 Free and Clear.** The Assigned Rights are assigned free and clear of all Encumbrances.

**2.3 No Assumed Liabilities.** Assignee does not assume, and Assignor shall remain responsible for, any Liability of Assignor arising out of the ownership or use of the Assigned Rights before the Effective Date.

**2.4 Consideration.** The parties acknowledge that the assignment in Section 2.1 is made as part of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement. No additional consideration is payable by Assignee.

## 3. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as of the Effective Date as follows:

**3.1 Organization and Authority.** Assignor is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Assignor has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it. This Agreement constitutes Assignor's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**3.2 Title.** Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of record and beneficial owner of the Company IP. Assignor has not previously assigned, transferred, licensed or otherwise encumbered any of the Assigned Rights.

**3.3 Registrations.** Each registration listed in Exhibit A is subsisting and in full force and effect. All maintenance, renewal and other fees due on or before the Effective Date in respect of the Company IP have been paid. All required declarations of use have been timely filed.

**3.4 No Proceedings.** No opposition, cancellation, interference or other proceeding is pending or, to Assignor's knowledge, threatened that challenges the validity, enforceability, registration or ownership of any of the Company IP.

**3.5 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Assignor do not conflict with Assignor's organizational documents, violate any Law, or require the consent of any Person.

## 4. FURTHER ASSURANCES; RECORDATION

**4.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and at Assignor's expense, Assignor shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned Rights. This obligation includes providing testimony and documents in any proceeding relating to the Company IP.

**4.2 Recordation.** Concurrently with this Agreement, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee short-form trademark assignments, substantially in the form of Exhibit B, for each registration and application included in the Company IP (the "**Recordation Forms**"). Assignee may record this Agreement or the Recordation Forms with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and with any other Governmental Authority. If this Agreement and a Recordation Form conflict, this Agreement controls.

**4.3 Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file in Assignor's name any document described in Section 4.1 or Section 4.2 that Assignor fails to execute within ten Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

**4.4 Delivery of Files.** Within ten Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all prosecution files, registration certificates, specimens and correspondence in its possession relating to the Company IP.

**4.5 Payments Received.** Any payment received by Assignor after the Effective Date in respect of the Assigned Rights shall be held in trust for Assignee and remitted to Assignee within five Business Days after receipt.

## 5. COVENANTS OF ASSIGNOR

**5.1 No Challenge.** Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, challenge the validity of the Company IP or Assignee's ownership of it, or assist any Person in doing so.

**5.2 Cessation of Use.** Effective on the Effective Date, Assignor shall cease all use of the Company IP and of any mark confusingly similar to it. Assignor shall not apply to register any such mark in any jurisdiction.

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS

**6.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out on the signature page, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**6.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**6.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign its rights under this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Assignor's consent. Assignor may not assign this Agreement without Assignee's prior written consent.

**6.4 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Buyer is an express third-party beneficiary of this Agreement and may enforce it directly against Assignor.

**6.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**6.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**6.7 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**6.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**6.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
