## 1. Purpose of this Letter

1.1 We write on behalf of Milon Fleet Parts Corp. ("**Supplier**") under the Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 between Vepous Student Transportation, LLC ("**Customer**") and Supplier, as amended by Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement dated as of June 16, 2026 (together, the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 Supplier delivers this letter under Section 15.5 of the Agreement. This letter (a) gives notice under Section 14.2 of the Agreement of Supplier's assignment of the Agreement, and (b) reserves Supplier's rights under Section 14.3 of the Agreement.

## 2. Notice of Assignment

2.1 Under Section 14.2 of the Agreement, Supplier gives notice that it will assign and transfer the Agreement, in whole, to **Rookwood Transit Supply, LLC** ("**Rookwood**"), effective December 1, 2026 (the "**Assignment Date**").

2.2 Rookwood is an affiliate of Kestrel Student Coach, Inc. The assignment is part of a transaction in which Rookwood will acquire Supplier's school bus replacement components distribution business, including its distribution facility in Hauppauge, New York.

2.3 This notice is given more than 30 days before the Assignment Date, as Section 14.2 requires. Before the Assignment Date, Rookwood will deliver to Customer a written instrument assuming Supplier's obligations under the Agreement.

2.4 From the Assignment Date:

(a) Customer should address all Purchase Orders, forecasts under Section 3.3 and notices under the Agreement to Rookwood at an address that Rookwood will provide to Customer before the Assignment Date;

(b) Rookwood will issue invoices under Section 4.3 and receive payment of them; and

(c) Supplier will transfer to Rookwood its records relating to the Agreement, including Customer's pricing, Purchase Order history, forecasts, volumes and Covered Depot delivery information, and the traceability records kept under Section 7.4.

2.5 Purchase Orders that Supplier accepts before the Assignment Date will be filled in accordance with the Agreement. Supplier will continue to invoice and collect payment for Products it delivers before the Assignment Date.

2.6 The pricing in Schedule A, as amended by Amendment No. 1, the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment of $1,850,000 per contract year and the other terms of the Agreement are not changed by the assignment.

## 3. Pending Transaction Involving Customer's Parent

3.1 Supplier understands that on September 28, 2026, Vepous Transit Holdings, Inc., Customer's parent, signed a stock purchase agreement with Larchfield Mobility Partners, L.P. ("**Larchfield**"), under which Larchfield will acquire a controlling interest in Vepous Transit Holdings, Inc. (the "**Larchfield Transaction**").

3.2 Section 1.4 of the Agreement defines Change of Control to include any transaction by which any person acquires, directly or indirectly, Control of Customer, including a sale of a controlling interest in any entity that directly or indirectly controls Customer. Section 14.3 of the Agreement provides that a Change of Control of Customer without the prior written consent of Supplier is a material breach of the Agreement.

3.3 Customer has not requested, and Supplier has not given, its consent to the Larchfield Transaction.

## 4. Reservation of Rights

4.1 Supplier expressly reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement and applicable law in connection with the Larchfield Transaction, including its rights under Section 14.3 of the Agreement to terminate the Agreement on 30 days' written notice and to recover the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment shortfall for the remainder of the Initial Term. From the Assignment Date, those rights will pass to Rookwood with the Agreement.

4.2 Supplier also reserves its rights under Section 3.1 of the Agreement in relation to Customer and its Affiliates.

4.3 Under Section 15.6 of the Agreement, a waiver is effective only if it is in writing and signed by the waiving party. Nothing in this letter, and no delay or failure by Supplier in exercising any right, is a waiver of any right or remedy of Supplier, or a consent to any transaction.

## 5. Requested Information

5.1 Supplier requests that Customer provide, within 15 business days after the date of this letter:

(a) the expected closing date of the Larchfield Transaction; and

(b) a list of the entities that will be Affiliates of Customer after the Larchfield Transaction closes.
