# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Vepous Student Transportation, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Milon Fleet Parts Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer operates school bus fleets providing pupil transportation services under contracts with school districts on Long Island, New York.

B. Supplier manufactures and distributes replacement brake, steering and suspension components and child-check alarm systems for school buses.

C. Customer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to sell, those products on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Products**" means the replacement brake, steering and suspension components and the child-check alarm systems listed in Schedule A, as the Parties may update in a signed writing.

1.2 "**Affiliate**" means, as to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Party, whether that relationship exists on the Effective Date or arises later. "**Control**" means the power, through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise, to direct the management and policies of an entity.

1.3 "**Fleet Components**" means brake, steering and suspension components, child-check alarm systems, and any part, assembly or kit that is the same as, or serves the same function as, any Product, for use on any school bus, activity bus or other passenger vehicle.

1.4 "**Change of Control**" means, as to Customer, any transaction or series of related transactions by which any person or group acquires, directly or indirectly, Control of Customer. This includes (a) a merger, consolidation or reorganization of Customer; (b) a sale of all or substantially all of Customer's assets; and (c) a sale, transfer or issuance of a controlling interest in Customer or in any entity that directly or indirectly controls Customer.

1.5 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products that Customer issues under this Agreement.

1.6 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

1.7 "**Covered Depots**" means the Customer depots identified in Schedule A.

1.8 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out or referenced in Schedule B, including applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards and New York State Department of Transportation requirements.

1.9 "**Defect**" means any failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications or to the warranties in Section 8, or any defect in design, materials or workmanship.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13 or Section 14.3.

2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY AND PURCHASE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the Term, Customer shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, purchase all Fleet Components required for every vehicle owned, leased or operated by Customer or any of its Affiliates anywhere in the United States exclusively from Supplier. Customer and its Affiliates shall not purchase Fleet Components from any third party, and shall not manufacture them or have them manufactured.

3.2 **Supply Obligation.** Supplier shall sell and deliver to Customer the quantities of Products ordered in Purchase Orders that conform to this Agreement.

3.3 **Forecasts.** By the fifteenth day of each calendar quarter, Customer shall give Supplier a non-binding rolling twelve-month forecast of its anticipated Product requirements for the Covered Depots.

3.4 **Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Customer shall purchase Products with an aggregate invoiced value of at least $1,850,000 per contract year (the "**Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment**"). Within 60 days after the end of any Contract Year in which Customer's purchases fall below the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment, Supplier may invoice Customer for the shortfall. Customer shall pay that invoice under Section 4.3. Purchases that Supplier fails to fill under Section 5.4 count toward the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.

## 4. PRICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices set out in Schedule A. Except as provided in Section 4.2, prices are fixed for the first two Contract Years.

4.2 **Price Adjustments.** Beginning with the third Contract Year, Supplier may adjust prices once per Contract Year on at least 90 days' written notice. No single adjustment may exceed the lesser of (a) four percent and (b) the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for Motor Vehicle Parts Manufacturing published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics over the preceding twelve months.

4.3 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice upon shipment. Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it gives written notice of the dispute, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

4.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes unless it provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 5. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

5.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders that identify the Products, quantities, Covered Depot and requested delivery date. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within two business days of receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it is inconsistent with this Agreement.

5.2 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DDP (Incoterms 2020) to the Covered Depot stated in the Purchase Order. Standard delivery is within five business days of acceptance. Supplier shall deliver Products that Customer designates as "vehicle-down" or safety-critical within one business day.

5.3 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery at the Covered Depot.

5.4 **Failure to Supply.** If Supplier cannot fill any accepted Purchase Order within the applicable delivery period, Customer may obtain substitute products from a third party for that order. Supplier shall reimburse Customer for any cost Customer pays above the price in Schedule A. These substitute purchases do not breach Section 3.1.

5.5 **Inventory.** Supplier shall maintain at its distribution facility in Hauppauge, New York, safety stock equal to at least 30 days of Customer's forecasted requirements for brake components and child-check alarm systems.

## 6. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

6.1 Customer may inspect Products within 15 business days after delivery. Customer may reject any Product that has a Defect, by written notice to Supplier.

6.2 For rejected Products, Supplier shall, at Customer's option, replace them within the delivery periods in Section 5.2 or credit their price. Supplier shall collect rejected Products at its own cost.

6.3 Acceptance does not waive Customer's rights under Section 8 or Section 10.

## 7. QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE

7.1 **Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 or IATF 16949, and shall notify Customer within ten business days if it loses that certification.

7.2 **Regulatory Compliance.** Supplier shall ensure that each Product complies with all applicable laws, including the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act, the Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards, and the child safety alert system requirements of New York Vehicle and Traffic Law and its regulations.

7.3 **Recalls.** Supplier shall notify Customer within 24 hours if it learns of any safety recall, defect investigation or field action affecting any Product. Supplier shall bear all costs of any recall or field action arising from a Defect, including replacement parts, labor at Customer's standard shop rate, and reasonable costs of vehicle downtime and substitute transportation.

7.4 **Traceability.** Supplier shall keep lot and serial number traceability records for each Product for at least seven years after delivery.

7.5 **Audit.** On ten business days' notice, Customer may audit Supplier's facilities and records that relate to the Products, no more than once per Contract Year, or at any time following a recall or a bodily injury incident involving a Product.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for 24 months after delivery or 50,000 miles of vehicle operation after installation, whichever occurs first, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship, and (c) be merchantable and fit for use on school buses.

8.2 **Child-Check Alarm Systems.** In addition to Section 8.1, Supplier warrants that each child-check alarm system will function as specified in Schedule B for 36 months after installation.

8.3 **Title.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to each Product, free of liens and encumbrances.

8.4 **Remedies.** For any breach of Section 8.1 or 8.2, Supplier shall, at Customer's option, repair or replace the affected Product or refund its price. Supplier shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable removal and installation labor. These remedies are in addition to Customer's rights under Section 7.3 and Section 10.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information that the other Party discloses under this Agreement, including pricing, volumes, forecasts, route and depot data and technical information ("**Confidential Information**"). Each Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement.

9.2 A Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, Affiliates and professional advisers who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 9. A Party may also disclose Confidential Information when required by law, after giving prompt notice to the other Party where the law permits.

9.3 This Section 9 survives for three years after this Agreement ends.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **Supplier Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim:

(a) alleging that any Product, or its use as contemplated by this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property right;

(b) for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property arising from any Defect in a Product, including any failure of a brake, steering or suspension component or child-check alarm system; or

(c) arising from Supplier's breach of Section 7.2 or Supplier's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10.2 **Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from any third-party claim for bodily injury or property damage caused by Customer's negligent installation or maintenance of Products. This Section 10.2 does not apply to the extent the claim arises from a Defect.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall (a) give prompt written notice of the claim, (b) give the indemnifying Party control of the defense and settlement, and (c) cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party may not settle any claim that imposes liability or an admission on the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent. Delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay materially prejudices it.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement.

11.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

11.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) all Section 10.1 indemnities, including 10.1(b) product-defect bodily injury claims; (b) Supplier's obligations under Section 7.3; (c) a Party's breach of Section 9; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (e) Customer's payment obligations for Products delivered.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 During the Term and for three years after it ends, Supplier shall maintain (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, which may be satisfied through umbrella or excess coverage, and (b) product recall insurance of at least $2,000,000.

12.2 Supplier shall name Customer as an additional insured on its liability policies. Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request, and shall give Customer 30 days' notice of any cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

13.3 **Safety Termination.** Customer may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if (a) Products are subject to two or more safety recalls in any Contract Year, or (b) a Defect causes a bodily injury incident involving a student.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiry, Customer shall pay for Products delivered before the termination date. Supplier shall fill accepted Purchase Orders unless Customer cancels them. Sections 7.3, 7.4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 15 survive.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.1 **Assignment by Customer.** Customer may not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold that consent. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 14.1 is void.

14.2 **Assignment by Supplier.** Supplier may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Customer's consent, to any Affiliate or to any purchaser of all or part of its business or assets. Supplier shall notify Customer in writing at least 30 days before the assignment takes effect. The assignee shall assume Supplier's obligations under this Agreement in writing.

14.3 **Change of Control of Customer.** A Change of Control of Customer without the prior written consent of Supplier is a material breach of this Agreement, and Section 13.1's cure period does not apply to it. After that breach, Supplier may (a) terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice to Customer, and (b) recover from Customer, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment shortfall for the remainder of the Initial Term. The shortfall equals the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment for the then-current Contract Year and each remaining Contract Year of the Initial Term, less the invoiced value of Products Customer has purchased in the then-current Contract Year. Customer shall pay that amount within 30 days after the termination takes effect.

14.4 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

15.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## SCHEDULE B — SPECIFICATIONS

1. All brake components shall comply with FMVSS No. 121 (Air Brake Systems) or FMVSS No. 105 (Hydraulic and Electric Brake Systems), as applicable, and with the OEM fit and performance specifications for the vehicle model.
2. Steering components shall comply with FMVSS No. 203 and No. 204 where applicable and with OEM specifications.
3. Suspension components shall meet or exceed OEM load ratings and dimensional specifications.
4. Child-check alarm systems shall comply with New York Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 375(21-n) and applicable regulations. Each system must (a) activate on engine shut-off, (b) require manual deactivation at the rearmost interior button, (c) sound an audible exterior alarm within 60 seconds if not deactivated, and (d) operate across an ambient temperature range of -30°F to 140°F.
5. Each Product shall bear a part number and lot or serial number permitting traceability under Section 7.4.
