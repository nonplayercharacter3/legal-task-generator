# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of June 16, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between **Vepous Student Transportation, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Milon Fleet Parts Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"). Customer and Supplier are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Supplier are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier supplies replacement brake, steering and suspension components and child-check alarm systems to Customer's Covered Depots.

B. The Parties have reviewed Product pricing for the remainder of the second Contract Year and following Contract Years. They have also reviewed the limitation of liability provisions of the Agreement.

C. Under Section 15.6 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement as set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 References in this Amendment to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of and Schedules to the Agreement, unless this Amendment states otherwise.

1.3 From the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. PRICING

2.1 **Revised Unit Prices.** With effect from July 1, 2026 (the "**Price Effective Date**"), Schedule A, Part 2 (Products and Unit Prices) is amended by replacing the unit prices for the Products listed below with the revised unit prices shown:

| Part No. | Product | Prior Unit Price | Revised Unit Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| MFP-BR-1100 | Air disc brake pad set, front axle | $186.00 | $179.50 |
| MFP-BR-1150 | Brake rotor, 17-inch | $242.00 | $236.00 |
| MFP-BR-1200 | Brake chamber, Type 30/30 spring | $118.50 | $114.25 |
| MFP-ST-2100 | Steering gear assembly, reman | $1,145.00 | $1,098.00 |
| MFP-SU-3100 | Front leaf spring assembly | $498.00 | $512.00 |
| MFP-CC-4100 | Child-check alarm system, complete kit | $689.00 | $664.00 |
| MFP-CC-4300 | Child-check alarm control module | $315.00 | $304.00 |

2.2 **Unchanged Prices.** The unit prices in Schedule A, Part 2 for all other Products remain unchanged.

2.3 **Application to Purchase Orders.** The revised unit prices apply to all Purchase Orders that Supplier accepts on or after the Price Effective Date. Purchase Orders that Supplier accepted before the Price Effective Date remain at the prices in effect when Supplier accepted them.

2.4 **Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment.** For purposes of Section 3.4, Products purchased at the revised unit prices count toward the Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment at their invoiced value. The Annual Minimum Purchase Commitment remains $1,850,000 per contract year.

2.5 **Future Adjustments.** Section 4.2 continues to govern any price adjustment beginning with the third Contract Year. The revised unit prices in Section 2.1 of this Amendment are the base prices for any such adjustment.

## 3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

3.1 Section 11.3 is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> 11.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) Supplier's indemnification obligations for third-party claims arising solely under Section 10.1(a); (b) Supplier's obligations under Section 7.3; (c) a Party's breach of Section 9; (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or (e) Customer's payment obligations for Products delivered.

3.2 The amended Section 11.3 applies to all claims made on or after the Amendment Date.

## 4. GENERAL

4.1 **Ratification.** Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and the Parties ratify and confirm it.

4.2 **Representations.** Each Party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it.

4.3 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** Sections 15.1 and 15.2 apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in it. This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

4.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
