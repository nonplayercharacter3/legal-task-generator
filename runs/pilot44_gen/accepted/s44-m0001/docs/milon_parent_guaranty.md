# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2025 by **Milon Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Vepous Student Transportation, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and **Milon Fleet Parts Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), are entering into a Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**"). Under the Supply Agreement, Supplier will supply replacement brake, steering and suspension components and child-check alarm systems to Customer.

B. Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the Supply Agreement.

C. Section 15.3 of the Supply Agreement requires Supplier to deliver this Guaranty to Customer as a condition to the Supply Agreement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Supplier to Customer under the Supply Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, including payment, delivery, warranty, recall, indemnification and reimbursement obligations.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Obligations.** Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Customer, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

2.2 **Scope.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is the same in nature and extent as Supplier's liability under the Supply Agreement, and is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in Section 11 of the Supply Agreement, as those provisions apply to Supplier from time to time. Guarantor may assert any defense that Supplier could assert under the Supply Agreement, other than the defenses waived in Section 4.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** This is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not of collection. If Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall, within ten business days after Customer's written demand, pay that amount or perform, or cause to be performed, that obligation.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that survive termination or expiry of the Supply Agreement under Section 13.4 of the Supply Agreement.

3.2 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Customer on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Supplier, as though the payment had not been made.

3.3 **No Requirement to Proceed Against Supplier.** Customer need not first proceed against Supplier or any other person, or exhaust any other remedy, before making demand on or proceeding against Guarantor.

## 4. WAIVERS AND CONSENTS

4.1 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; (b) presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor or non-performance, other than the demand under Section 2.3; (c) any defense based on the insolvency, bankruptcy, dissolution or lack of authority of Supplier; and (d) any right to require Customer to marshal assets.

4.2 **Consents.** Guarantor consents that, without notice to or further consent of Guarantor and without affecting its obligations under this Guaranty, Customer and Supplier may amend, extend, renew or otherwise modify the Supply Agreement, and Customer may grant Supplier extensions of time or waivers of any Guaranteed Obligation.

4.3 **Subrogation.** Until the Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement or contribution against Supplier arising from payments it makes under this Guaranty.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Customer that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and that execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally; and

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement by which it is bound.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Financial Information.** Within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year during the Term, Guarantor shall deliver to Customer, on request, its annual consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year. Customer shall treat those statements as Confidential Information under Section 9 of the Supply Agreement.

6.2 **Ownership.** Guarantor shall notify Customer in writing within ten business days after Guarantor ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the voting securities of Supplier. That event does not release Guarantor from this Guaranty.

## 7. ASSIGNMENT

7.1 **By Guarantor.** Guarantor may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Guaranty without the prior written consent of Customer. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 7.1 is void.

7.2 **By Customer.** Customer may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of Customer's rights under the Supply Agreement under Section 14.1 of the Supply Agreement.

7.3 **Successors.** This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Customer and its successors and permitted assigns.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Nassau County or Suffolk County, New York for any action arising out of this Guaranty.

8.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** Guarantor and Customer each waive, to the fullest extent permitted by law, any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of this Guaranty.

8.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with a confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses below or to another address a party designates by notice. A notice is effective upon receipt.

- **Customer:** Vepous Student Transportation, LLC, 410 Front Street, Hempstead, New York 11550, Attention: General Counsel

8.5 **Amendments and Waivers.** This Guaranty may be amended only by a writing signed by Guarantor and Customer. A waiver is effective only if it is in writing and signed by the waiving party. A delay or failure to exercise any right under this Guaranty is not a waiver of that right.

8.6 **Expenses.** Guarantor shall pay Customer's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in successfully enforcing this Guaranty.

8.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

8.8 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
