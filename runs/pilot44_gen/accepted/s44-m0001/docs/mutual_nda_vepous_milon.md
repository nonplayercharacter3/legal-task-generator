# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of January 13, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between **Vepous Student Transportation, LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Vepous**"), and **Milon Fleet Parts Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Milon**"). Vepous and Milon are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Vepous operates school bus fleets that provide pupil transportation services on Long Island, New York.

B. Milon manufactures and distributes replacement brake, steering and suspension components and child-check alarm systems for school buses.

C. The Parties wish to exchange certain non-public information to evaluate and negotiate a possible supply relationship for those products (the "**Purpose**"). They wish to protect that information on the terms of this NDA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Representatives disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and in any form. It includes:

(a) pricing, cost, margin and volume information;

(b) fleet size and composition, maintenance records, parts consumption data and forecasts;

(c) route, depot, school district contract and operational information;

(d) product specifications, engineering drawings, test data, quality records and certification materials; and

(e) the fact that the Parties are discussing the Purpose and the status and terms of those discussions.

1.2 "**Representatives**" means a Party's affiliates and its and their directors, officers, managers, members, employees, agents, lenders and professional advisers, including attorneys, accountants and consultants.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than through a breach of this NDA by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party, without a duty of confidentiality, before the Disclosing Party disclosed it;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party that is not bound by a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to anyone other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose. Before disclosure, the Receiving Party shall inform those Representatives of the confidential nature of the information. Each such Representative must be bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this NDA. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in any event with no less than reasonable care.

3.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing after it becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit the effects of that use or disclosure.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 If law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process requires the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where the law permits, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably, at the Disclosing Party's expense, with those efforts.

4.2 If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party may disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the disclosed information will be treated confidentially.

4.3 Disclosure to a school district, the New York State Education Department or the New York State Department of Transportation in response to a regulatory request or inspection is a disclosure under this Section 4.

## 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within 15 business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession, and shall confirm in writing that it has done so.

5.2 The Receiving Party and its Representatives may keep (a) copies required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policies, and (b) copies held in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this NDA for as long as it is retained.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY; NO OBLIGATION

6.1 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. The Disclosing Party has no liability to the Receiving Party arising from the Receiving Party's use of Confidential Information, except as stated in a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

6.3 **No Obligation to Proceed.** This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any supply or other agreement. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other.

## 7. TERM

7.1 This NDA applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the two years after the NDA Effective Date. Either Party may end that disclosure period earlier on 30 days' written notice.

7.2 The Receiving Party's obligations under this NDA for Confidential Information disclosed during the disclosure period continue for three years after the disclosure period ends. For any Confidential Information that is a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive relief or specific performance, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Nassau County or Suffolk County, New York for any action arising out of this NDA.

9.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business that agrees in writing to be bound by this NDA. Any other purported assignment is void.

9.5 **Amendments and Waivers.** This NDA may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties. A waiver is effective only if it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party. A delay or failure to exercise any right under this NDA is not a waiver of that right.

9.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

9.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together form one instrument.
