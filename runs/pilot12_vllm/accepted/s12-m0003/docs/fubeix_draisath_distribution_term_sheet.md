# TERM SHEET

## Proposed Exclusive Distribution Arrangement

**Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc. and Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC**

**Date:** November 18, 2025

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of a proposed exclusive distribution arrangement between Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC ("**Distributor**"). The Parties intend to set out these terms in a definitive distribution agreement (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Except for Sections 12 and 13, this term sheet is not binding and does not create any obligation to enter into the Distribution Agreement.

## 1. Parties and Products

1.1 **Supplier:** Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 2200 Meridian Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801.

1.2 **Distributor:** Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC, a New York limited liability company, 455 Corporate Drive, Suite 300, White Plains, New York 10601.

1.3 **Products:** Supplier's liquid-cooling product line for data centers, comprising:

| Category | Models / Part Families |
|---|---|
| Coolant distribution units (CDUs) | DCT-CDU 350, DCT-CDU 800, DCT-CDU 1500 |
| Rear-door heat exchangers (RDHx) | DCT-RD 40, DCT-RD 70 |
| Spare parts | Pump assemblies, filter cartridges and housings, plate heat exchanger cores, sensors, RDHx fan modules, controller boards and firmware modules |

The list of Products will be attached as an exhibit to the Distribution Agreement. Supplier may add or discontinue models on at least 120 days' written notice.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term:** Three (3) years from the effective date of the Distribution Agreement. The Parties are targeting an effective date in mid-January 2026.

2.2 **Renewal:** After the Initial Term, the Distribution Agreement may be extended for one-year renewal terms by mutual written agreement. Neither Party will be obliged to agree to a renewal.

2.3 **Early Termination:** Either Party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice. Either Party may also terminate on the other Party's insolvency. Supplier will have the further termination rights described in Sections 5.3 and 10.2.

## 3. Appointment and Territory

3.1 **Appointment:** Supplier will appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

3.2 **Territory:** New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Connecticut and Massachusetts.

3.3 **Sales Outside the Territory:** Distributor will not actively market the Products outside the Territory. Distributor may fill unsolicited orders from outside the Territory with Supplier's prior written approval.

3.4 **Relationship:** Distributor will buy for its own account as an independent contractor and will set its own resale prices.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Supplier Exclusivity:** During the term, Supplier will not appoint any other distributor, reseller or sales agent for the Products in the Territory. Supplier will refer Territory inquiries to Distributor.

4.2 **Reserved Accounts:** Supplier may sell directly to a list of hyperscale cloud operators that it identifies in writing on or before the effective date. That list may be updated only with Distributor's agreement. Distributor will receive a referral fee of 3% of net invoice value on reserved-account sales for installation in the Territory.

4.3 **Distributor Non-Compete:** During the term, Distributor will not distribute, market or sell competing coolant distribution units, within the Territory only. Distributor's other product lines are not restricted, including its distribution of third-party air-side cooling equipment.

## 5. Minimum Purchase Commitment

5.1 **Amount:** Distributor will purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of at least $8,000,000 in each Contract Year. A Contract Year is each 12-month period beginning on the effective date or an anniversary of the effective date.

5.2 **Counting:** All purchases of Products, including CDUs, RDHx and spare parts, will count toward the commitment in the Contract Year in which Supplier invoices them. Supplier will provide quarterly statements of purchases to date.

5.3 **Shortfall:** If Distributor misses the commitment for a Contract Year, Supplier may, within 90 days after the end of that Contract Year, either convert the appointment to non-exclusive or terminate on 60 days' notice.

## 6. Pricing and Payment

6.1 **Pricing:** Supplier's U.S. list price in effect when the purchase order is accepted, less the following discounts: CDUs 28%; RDHx 25%; spare parts 20%.

6.2 **Price Adjustments:** Supplier may adjust list prices on at least 90 days' written notice, no more than once per Contract Year for any Product.

6.3 **Objection:** Distributor may object in writing within 45 days after the date of a price adjustment notice. If Distributor does not object within that period, the adjustment will be treated as accepted. If Distributor objects in time, the Parties will negotiate in good faith for 30 days, and existing prices will continue to apply during that period.

6.4 **Payment:** Net 45 days from the invoice date, in U.S. dollars. Interest on late undisputed amounts will accrue at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum lawful rate.

## 7. Orders, Delivery and Lead Times

7.1 **Forecasts and Orders:** Distributor will provide non-binding rolling four-quarter forecasts each quarter. Supplier will accept or reject purchase orders within five business days and will not unreasonably reject orders consistent with the forecast.

7.2 **Delivery:** FCA Supplier's facility in Allentown, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020). Title and risk will pass on delivery.

7.3 **Standard Lead Times:** CDUs, 12 weeks; RDHx, 8 weeks; stocked spare parts, 2 weeks.

## 8. Marketing, Support and Training

8.1 Distributor will use commercially reasonable efforts to promote the Products throughout the Territory and will maintain qualified sales and technical staff.

8.2 Distributor will provide first-line installation, commissioning and technical support. Supplier will provide second-line support.

8.3 Supplier will provide initial product training at no charge.

## 9. Warranty, Indemnity and Liability

9.1 **Warranty:** 18 months from delivery for CDUs and RDHx, and 12 months from delivery for spare parts. The remedy is repair, replacement or credit at Supplier's option.

9.2 **Indemnities:** Supplier will indemnify for U.S. intellectual property infringement and for product-defect bodily injury and property damage claims. Distributor will indemnify for its own unauthorized warranties and for negligent installation or support.

9.3 **Liability:** There will be a mutual exclusion of consequential damages and lost profits. Liability will be capped at 12 months' amounts paid and payable, with customary carve-outs.

9.4 **Insurance:** Each Party will carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence, including products and completed operations coverage.

## 10. Assignment and Change of Control

10.1 Supplier may assign to an Affiliate or to a successor to its Products business on notice to Distributor.

10.2 Distributor may not assign without Supplier's prior written consent. The Distribution Agreement will treat a change of control of Distributor as an assignment. Supplier will have a right to terminate if a change of control occurs without its consent. The definitive drafting is to be agreed.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

The Distribution Agreement will be governed by New York law, and the courts located in New York County, New York will have exclusive jurisdiction. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods will not apply.

## 12. Confidentiality

The terms of this term sheet and the Parties' discussions are confidential under the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties.

## 13. Costs and Non-Binding Effect

13.1 Each Party will bear its own costs in connection with this term sheet and the Distribution Agreement.

13.2 Only Sections 12 and 13 of this term sheet are binding. No other obligation will arise unless and until the Distribution Agreement is signed by both Parties.
