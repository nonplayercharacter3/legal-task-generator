## 1. Transaction Involving Distributor's Parent

1.2 Closing of the Transaction is subject to customary conditions, including regulatory clearances. It is expected to occur in December 2026.

1.3 Distributor will remain a wholly owned subsidiary of Fubeix Industrial after closing. Distributor's management team, sales and technical staff, and White Plains operations are not expected to change as a result of the Transaction. Distributor will continue to perform its obligations under the Agreement in the ordinary course, including its marketing, support and purchasing obligations for the Products in the Territory.

1.4 This letter satisfies any notice requirement under the Agreement with respect to the Transaction. We will let you know once closing has occurred.

## 2. Price Adjustment Notice Dated July 1, 2026

2.1 Supplier issued a Price Adjustment Notice dated July 1, 2026, under Section 6.2 of the Agreement. The notice increases the list prices for the DCT-CDU 350, DCT-CDU 800 and DCT-CDU 1500 models by nine percent (9%), effective October 1, 2026.

2.2 Under Section 6.3 of the Agreement, Distributor hereby objects to the price increase set out in the Price Adjustment Notice dated July 1, 2026.

2.3 CDUs make up most of Distributor's purchases under the Agreement. Distributor has submitted bids and pricing proposals to several Customers in the Territory for data center projects scheduled for delivery in late 2026 and 2027. Those bids were based on the list prices in effect before the Price Adjustment Notice. A nine percent (9%) increase in CDU list prices would materially affect Distributor's margins on those projects and its ability to compete for new work in the Territory.

2.4 Distributor asks that the Parties begin the good-faith negotiation contemplated by Section 6.3 of the Agreement. Distributor proposes a call during the week of September 28, 2026. In the meantime, Distributor expects that Purchase Orders it issues will be invoiced at the CDU list prices in effect before the Price Adjustment Notice.
