# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into and effective as of October 6, 2025 (the "**NDA Effective Date**"). It is between **Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Meridian Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Draisath**"), and **Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 455 Corporate Drive, Suite 300, White Plains, New York 10601 ("**Fubeix**"). Draisath and Fubeix are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Draisath designs and manufactures liquid-cooling equipment for data centers, including coolant distribution units, rear-door heat exchangers and related spare parts.

B. Fubeix markets, sells and supports thermal management equipment for data center operators and commercial customers.

C. The Parties wish to exchange confidential information to evaluate and negotiate a possible distribution arrangement under which Fubeix would distribute Draisath products in certain states of the northeastern United States (the "**Purpose**").

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. In this definition, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public business, technical, financial and commercial information that a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") or its Representatives disclose to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**") or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and in any form. Confidential Information includes product designs, specifications, engineering data, pricing, discounts, cost information, customer and project information, sales forecasts, business plans and the terms of any proposal or term sheet exchanged between the Parties. Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Parties are discussing a possible transaction and the status of those discussions.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, managers, employees, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party will use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

2.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party will not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to anyone other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose. Those Representatives must be informed of its confidential nature and be bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this NDA. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

2.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party will protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party will notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing on becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It will cooperate reasonably to limit the effects of that use or disclosure.

2.5 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Receiving Party will not reverse engineer, disassemble or analyze the composition of any samples, prototypes or software that the Disclosing Party provides under this NDA, except as the Disclosing Party authorizes in writing.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

3.1 **Excluded Information.** The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without an obligation of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party that is not under an obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a securities exchange. Where lawful, the Receiving Party will first give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party will disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. OWNERSHIP AND NO LICENSE

4.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

4.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty about its accuracy or completeness. Any representations and warranties will be made only in a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

## 5. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

5.1 This NDA does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any distribution agreement or other transaction. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other Party.

5.2 Unless and until the Parties sign a definitive written agreement, neither Party has any obligation to the other with respect to the Purpose except as stated in this NDA.

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within fifteen (15) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party ends discussions under Section 5.1, the Receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party will certify in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Receiving Party may retain copies to the extent required by law or by its bona fide document retention policies, and copies held in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained copies remain subject to this NDA.

## 7. TERM

7.1 **Term.** This NDA continues for two (2) years from the NDA Effective Date, unless either Party ends it earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

7.2 **Survival.** The Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 continue for three (3) years after the expiration or termination of this NDA with respect to Confidential Information disclosed before that time. Obligations with respect to any Confidential Information that is a trade secret under applicable law continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance for any actual or threatened breach of this NDA, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 9. NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

9.1 During the term of this NDA and for twelve (12) months afterwards, neither Party will directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. This Section 9.1 does not restrict general solicitations not targeted at the other Party's employees, such as public job postings, or the hiring of any person who responds to them.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Notices.** Notices under this NDA must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier. Notices to Draisath go to: Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc., 2200 Meridian Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801, Attention: General Counsel. Notices to Fubeix go to: Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC, 455 Corporate Drive, Suite 300, White Plains, New York 10601, Attention: President. A notice is effective on receipt. Either Party may change its notice address by notice to the other.

10.2 **Governing Law.** This NDA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.3 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this NDA.

10.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this NDA without the other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this NDA relates, on written notice to the other Party. This NDA binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

10.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This NDA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right is a waiver of that right.

10.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this NDA is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

10.7 **Counterparts.** This NDA may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all together form one instrument.
