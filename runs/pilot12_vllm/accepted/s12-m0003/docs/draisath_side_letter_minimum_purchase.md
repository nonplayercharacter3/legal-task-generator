**Re: Distribution Agreement dated January 15, 2026, between Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc. and Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC: Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**

Dear Mr. Fubeix:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") refers to the Distribution Agreement entered into and effective as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), between Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC ("**Distributor**").

During our recent discussions, Distributor asked that Supplier reconsider the level of the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment in Section 5.1 of the Agreement. Distributor pointed to the expected timing of large-scale data center projects in the Territory. Supplier has agreed to do so on the terms of this Side Letter.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Products**" means solely the coolant distribution unit models listed in Exhibit A, Part 1 to the Agreement (DCT-CDU 350, DCT-CDU 800 and DCT-CDU 1500), as Supplier may update that Part under Section 3.2 of the Agreement.

## 2. Amended Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment

2.1 The Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment under Section 5.1 of the Agreement is reduced from Eight Million Dollars ($8,000,000) to $6,000,000 per Contract Year.

2.2 The reduced Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment applies from the first Contract Year, which began on January 15, 2026, and to each later Contract Year during the Term.

2.3 Purchases of Products under accepted Purchase Orders count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which Supplier issues the invoice, as provided in Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

## 3. Reporting

Supplier will continue to provide the quarterly statements described in Section 5.2 of the Agreement. Each statement will show the net invoice value of Products purchased in the current Contract Year to date against the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment set out in Section 2.1 of this Side Letter.

## 4. Effect on the Agreement

4.1 This Side Letter forms part of the Agreement. References in the Agreement to the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment are references to that commitment as amended by this Side Letter.

4.2 Except as set out in this Side Letter, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

## 5. General

5.1 This Side Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 18.3 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of this Side Letter.

5.2 This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Supplier and Distributor.

5.3 This Side Letter may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all together form one instrument.
