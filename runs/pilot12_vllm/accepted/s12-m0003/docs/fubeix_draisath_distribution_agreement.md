# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and effective as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**" or the "**Distribution Agreement Effective Date**"). It is between **Draisath Cooling Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Meridian Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"), and **Fubeix Thermal Distribution, LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 455 Corporate Drive, Suite 300, White Plains, New York 10601 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures liquid-cooling equipment for data centers, including coolant distribution units, rear-door heat exchangers and related spare parts.

B. Distributor is in the business of marketing, selling and supporting thermal management equipment for data center operators and commercial customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings given below:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to a Party, any of the following: (a) the acquisition by any person or group, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of that Party or of any entity that directly or indirectly controls that Party; (b) a merger, consolidation or reorganization of that Party, or of any entity that directly or indirectly controls that Party, after which the holders of its voting interests immediately before the transaction hold fifty percent (50%) or less of the voting interests of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of that Party.

"**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of the Effective Date.

"**Customer**" means any end user or reseller to which Distributor sells Products.

"**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Price Adjustment Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 6.2.

"**Products**" means the coolant distribution units ("**CDUs**"), rear-door heat exchangers ("**RDHx**") and spare parts listed in Exhibit A, as Supplier may update Exhibit A from time to time under Section 3.2.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products that Distributor issues to Supplier under this Agreement.

"**Specifications**" means Supplier's published technical specifications for each Product in effect on the date of shipment.

"**Territory**" means New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Connecticut and Massachusetts.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date, ending on January 14, 2029 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive five (5) year renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal during the fifteen (15) day period ending ninety (90) days before the expiration of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. A notice of non-renewal given outside that period is of no effect.

## 3. APPOINTMENT AND PRODUCTS

3.1 **Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

3.2 **Changes to Products.** Supplier may add new models to Exhibit A, or discontinue models listed in Exhibit A, on at least one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice to Distributor. Supplier will fill Purchase Orders for discontinued models that it accepts before the discontinuance takes effect.

3.3 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Distributor buys Products for resale for its own account, sets its own resale prices, and has no authority to bind Supplier.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Supplier Exclusivity.** During the Term, and while Distributor's appointment remains exclusive, Supplier will not appoint any other distributor, reseller or sales agent for the Products in the Territory. Supplier will refer to Distributor all inquiries it receives for Products from prospective Customers located in the Territory.

4.2 **Reserved Accounts.** Supplier may sell Products directly to the global accounts listed in Exhibit B, Part 3, including for installations located in the Territory. Those direct sales do not breach Section 4.1. Distributor will receive the referral fee stated in Exhibit B, Part 3 on each such sale for installation in the Territory.

4.3 **Distributor Non-Competition.** During the Term and for eighteen (18) months after its expiration or termination, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, through any Affiliate or otherwise, distribute, market, sell, promote or solicit orders for any thermal management products manufactured by any person other than Supplier, anywhere in the world.

4.4 **Sales Outside the Territory.** Distributor will not actively market or solicit sales of Products outside the Territory. Distributor may fill unsolicited orders from Customers outside the Territory only with Supplier's prior written approval.

## 5. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

5.1 **Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Distributor will purchase Products from Supplier with an aggregate net invoice value of at least Eight Million Dollars ($8,000,000) (the "**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**"). All purchases of Products under accepted Purchase Orders count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for the Contract Year in which Supplier issues the invoice.

5.2 **Reporting.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Supplier will give Distributor a statement of the net invoice value of Products purchased in the current Contract Year to date.

5.3 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for any Contract Year, Supplier may exercise its rights under Section 13.2.

## 6. PRICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Prices.** Distributor will pay the prices for Products set out in Exhibit B, as adjusted under this Section 6.

6.2 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the list prices for any Products by giving Distributor written notice (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**") at least ninety (90) days before the adjustment takes effect. Each Price Adjustment Notice will identify the affected Products, the adjusted prices and the effective date. Supplier will not give more than one Price Adjustment Notice in any Contract Year for the same Product.

6.3 **Objection.** Distributor may object to a Price Adjustment Notice by written notice to Supplier given within 45 days after the date of a Price Adjustment Notice. If Distributor does not object in writing within that period, Distributor is deemed to have accepted the adjustment, and the adjusted prices apply to all Purchase Orders accepted on or after the effective date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice. If Distributor objects in time, the Parties will negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days. During that period and any further period of negotiation the Parties agree in writing, the prices in effect before the Price Adjustment Notice continue to apply.

6.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier will invoice Distributor upon shipment. Distributor will pay undisputed invoices in U.S. dollars within forty-five (45) days after the invoice date. Distributor will notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties will work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

6.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.

6.6 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, excise and similar taxes, which Distributor will pay unless it provides a valid resale or exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 7. ORDERS AND FORECASTS

7.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) business day of each calendar quarter, Distributor will give Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements for the next four (4) calendar quarters.

7.2 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor will order Products by issuing Purchase Orders that state the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery locations. Supplier will accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days after receipt. Supplier will not unreasonably reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with the most recent forecast.

7.3 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 8. DELIVERY

8.1 **Delivery Terms.** Supplier will deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in Allentown, Pennsylvania (Incoterms 2020), or to another location stated in the Purchase Order and agreed by Supplier.

8.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Distributor on delivery under Section 8.1.

8.3 **Lead Times.** Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the lead times stated in Exhibit B, Part 2. Supplier will notify Distributor promptly of any expected delay.

8.4 **Inspection.** Distributor will inspect Products within ten (10) business days after receipt. It will notify Supplier in writing of any shortage, visible damage or nonconformity found on inspection.

## 9. MARKETING AND DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

9.1 **Promotion.** Distributor will use commercially reasonable efforts to market and sell the Products throughout the Territory. It will maintain a qualified sales and technical staff for that purpose.

9.2 **Installation and Support.** Distributor will provide first-line installation support, commissioning assistance and technical support to Customers. It will do so in accordance with Supplier's published guidelines. Supplier will provide second-line technical support to Distributor.

9.3 **Training.** Supplier will provide initial product training to Distributor's personnel at no charge, and further training on reasonable request at Supplier's then-current rates.

9.4 **Compliance.** Each Party will comply with all laws that apply to its performance under this Agreement, including export control and anti-corruption laws.

## 10. WARRANTIES

10.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that each Product will conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for the following periods: eighteen (18) months after delivery for CDUs and RDHx, and twelve (12) months after delivery for spare parts.

10.2 **Remedies.** If a Product does not meet the warranty in Section 10.1, Supplier will repair or replace it, or credit its price, at Supplier's option. Distributor must notify Supplier within the warranty period. Supplier will pay reasonable freight for returned and replacement Products.

10.3 **Exclusions.** The warranty does not apply to Products that have been misused, improperly installed or maintained, altered without Supplier's approval, or operated with coolants not approved in the Specifications.

10.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 10, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1 **Trademarks.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products. Distributor will follow Supplier's reasonable trademark usage guidelines.

11.2 **Ownership.** Supplier retains all right, title and interest in the Products, the Specifications and its trademarks and other intellectual property. Distributor will not reverse engineer any Product.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 **Obligations.** Each Party will keep confidential the non-public business, technical and pricing information of the other Party that it receives in connection with this Agreement. It will use that information only to perform this Agreement and will disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by similar obligations.

12.2 **Exceptions.** Section 12.1 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction, or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information. A Party may disclose information when required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

12.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 12 continue during the Term and for three (3) years afterwards.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Minimum Purchase Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for any Contract Year, Supplier may, by written notice given within ninety (90) days after the end of that Contract Year, do either of the following: (a) convert Distributor's appointment under Section 3.1 to a non-exclusive appointment, in which case Section 4.1 ceases to apply; or (b) terminate this Agreement on sixty (60) days' written notice.

13.3 **Change of Control.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if a Change of Control of Distributor occurs without Supplier's prior written consent under Section 17.2.

13.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.5 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement:

(a) Distributor will pay all amounts due for Products delivered;

(b) Supplier will fill any Purchase Orders accepted before the termination date, unless Supplier terminated under Section 13.1 for Distributor's non-payment;

(c) Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products for one hundred twenty (120) days, subject to the terms of this Agreement; and

(d) Distributor will stop using Supplier's trademarks after the sell-off period.

13.6 **Survival.** Sections 4.3, 6.4, 6.5, 10, 11.2, 12, 13.5, 13.6, 14, 15 and 18 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. INDEMNIFICATION

14.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates from third-party claims arising from (a) an allegation that a Product infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark, or (b) bodily injury or damage to tangible property caused by a defect in a Product.

14.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates from third-party claims arising from (a) any warranty or representation about the Products made by Distributor beyond those in the Specifications, or (b) Distributor's negligent installation or support services.

14.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party will give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party will not settle any claim that imposes liability or obligations on the indemnified Party without its written consent.

## 15. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

15.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 14 OR BREACH OF SECTION 12, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

15.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT FOR OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 14, BREACH OF SECTION 12 AND DISTRIBUTOR'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE AMOUNTS PAID AND PAYABLE BY DISTRIBUTOR TO SUPPLIER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 16. INSURANCE

Each Party will maintain, during the Term and for one (1) year afterwards, commercial general liability insurance of at least Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, including products and completed operations coverage. Each Party will provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 17. ASSIGNMENT

17.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement without Distributor's consent to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business relating to the Products. Supplier will give Distributor written notice of the assignment.

17.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without Supplier's prior written consent, which Supplier may grant or withhold in its sole discretion. Any Change of Control of Distributor, including a change of control of any entity that directly or indirectly controls Distributor, is deemed an assignment of this Agreement for purposes of this Section 17.2. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 17.2 is void.

17.3 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

18.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming this Agreement, they will control in the following order: any signed side letter referencing the Distribution Agreement, then the Distribution Agreement, then its Exhibits.

18.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right is a waiver of that right.
