# QUALITY AND SPECIFICATIONS AGREEMENT

This Quality and Specifications Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into and made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Veltmar Glassworks, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1400 Furnace Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Harrowgate Bottling Co.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Millrace Avenue, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania 17101 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Customer are parties to a Supply Agreement made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement**"), under which Supplier supplies, and Customer purchases, the Products.

B. Section 6.2 of the Supply Agreement provides that the Parties shall comply with this Quality Agreement, which governs inspection, testing, quality control, change control and related matters.

C. The Parties wish to set out the Specifications for the Products and the quality requirements that apply to their manufacture, testing, release and delivery.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Certificate of Analysis**" or "**CoA**" means a document issued by Supplier for each Lot confirming that the Lot has been tested and conforms to the Specifications.

(b) "**Critical Defect**" means a defect that is likely to cause injury to a consumer or render the Product unusable for filling, including glass inclusions, internal glass particles, sharp edges, birdswings and cracked finishes.

(c) "**Lot**" means a quantity of a single Product manufactured on the same production line during a single continuous production run and identified by a unique lot number.

(d) "**Major Defect**" means a defect, other than a Critical Defect, that is likely to cause a failure on Customer's filling or packaging lines or materially impair the appearance or performance of the Product.

(e) "**Nonconformance**" means any failure of a Product or Lot to conform to the Specifications or to this Quality Agreement.

1.3 **Relationship to Supply Agreement.** This Quality Agreement supplements the Supply Agreement. The order of precedence set out in Section 15.2 of the Supply Agreement applies to this Quality Agreement.

## 2. SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 **Product Specifications.** Supplier shall manufacture each Product in accordance with the specification referenced for that Product in Exhibit A to the Supply Agreement, as set out in Schedule 1 to this Quality Agreement.

2.2 **Food-Contact Compliance.** Supplier shall ensure that all Products, and all materials, coatings and treatments used in their manufacture, comply with applicable laws governing materials in contact with food and beverages, including 21 C.F.R. Parts 174 to 186 and, for Products delivered to facilities in Canada, the Food and Drugs Act (Canada) and its regulations.

2.3 **Updates.** Specifications may be updated only by a written document signed by an authorized quality representative of each Party. Any updated Specification takes effect on the date stated in that document.

## 3. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

3.1 **Certification.** Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 and a food safety management system certified under a scheme recognized by the Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI) for packaging manufacture, and shall provide current certificates to Customer on request.

3.2 **Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall appoint a quality representative responsible for matters under this Quality Agreement and shall notify the other Party of any change of representative.

3.3 **Personnel and Hygiene.** Supplier shall ensure that personnel involved in manufacturing, inspecting and packing the Products are trained in good manufacturing practices and hygiene appropriate to food-contact packaging.

## 4. MANUFACTURING CONTROLS AND TESTING

4.1 **In-Process Controls.** Supplier shall operate automated inspection equipment on each production line capable of detecting dimensional, finish and glass-integrity defects, and shall maintain documented sampling and inspection plans for each Product.

4.2 **Release Testing.** Before releasing any Lot for delivery, Supplier shall test the Lot for the parameters stated in Schedule 1, including dimensions, capacity, internal pressure resistance, thermal shock resistance and color transmission.

4.3 **Acceptable Quality Levels.** Supplier shall release a Lot only if it meets the following acceptable quality levels (AQL) under ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, General Inspection Level II:

| Defect Classification | AQL |
|---|---|
| Critical Defect | 0.0 (zero tolerance) |
| Major Defect | 0.65 |
| Minor Defect | 2.5 |

4.4 **Certificates of Analysis.** Supplier shall provide a CoA for each Lot with, or before, delivery of that Lot.

## 5. PACKAGING, LABELING AND TRACEABILITY

5.1 **Packaging.** Supplier shall palletize Products in clean, dry, shrink-wrapped bulk pallets with tier sheets and top frames suitable for automated depalletizing, protected from dust, moisture and foreign matter.

5.2 **Labeling.** Each pallet shall bear a label stating the Product item number, quantity, Lot number, production date, production line and Supplier facility.

5.3 **Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain lot and batch traceability for all Products in accordance with Section 10.3 of the Supply Agreement, so that each Lot can be traced to its raw materials, production line and production date, and to the delivery in which it was shipped.

## 6. INSPECTION AND NONCONFORMANCE

6.1 **Incoming Inspection.** Customer may inspect and test Products after delivery within the period set out in Section 7.1 of the Supply Agreement. Supplier's release of a Lot does not limit Customer's rights under Sections 7 and 8 of the Supply Agreement.

6.2 **Nonconformance Notice.** If Customer identifies a Nonconformance, it shall notify Supplier's quality representative, identifying the affected Product and Lot and describing the Nonconformance. Where reasonably available, Customer shall provide samples or photographs.

6.3 **Investigation and Corrective Action.** Supplier shall acknowledge each Nonconformance notice within two (2) business days and, within ten (10) business days, provide Customer with a written root-cause analysis and corrective and preventive action plan. Supplier shall implement the plan within the time agreed with Customer and shall verify its effectiveness.

6.4 **Critical Defects.** If Supplier becomes aware of a Critical Defect in any Lot delivered or about to be delivered to Customer, Supplier shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours and shall place on hold all affected Lots in its possession.

6.5 **Remedies.** Rejected Products are subject to Section 7.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 7. CHANGE CONTROL

7.1 **Prior Consent.** In accordance with Section 6.3 of the Supply Agreement, Supplier shall not change any raw material, process, mold, facility or Specification affecting the Products without Customer's prior written consent.

7.2 **Change Requests.** Supplier shall submit each proposed change to Customer in writing at least ninety (90) days before the proposed implementation date, together with a description of the change, the reasons for it, an assessment of its effect on the Products, and any validation data.

7.3 **Qualification.** Customer may require Supplier to supply trial quantities of Products made under a proposed change for line trials at Customer's facilities before Customer decides whether to give its consent.

## 8. AUDITS

8.1 **Audit Right.** Customer, or a third-party auditor bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Supplier's facilities, quality records and systems relating to the Products once in each calendar year on at least fifteen (15) business days' notice, and at any time on reasonable notice following a Critical Defect or a Recall.

8.2 **Findings.** Supplier shall respond in writing to any audit findings within thirty (30) days and shall correct any deficiencies within the time agreed with Customer.

## 9. COMPLAINTS AND RECALLS

9.1 **Consumer Complaints.** Customer shall forward to Supplier any consumer or trade complaint that it reasonably believes relates to the Products. Supplier shall investigate and report its findings within ten (10) business days.

9.2 **Recalls.** Recalls are governed by Section 10 of the Supply Agreement. Supplier shall provide Customer with all traceability records, test data and other information reasonably needed to assess and carry out a Recall within twenty-four (24) hours of Customer's request.

## 10. RECORDS

10.1 **Retention.** Supplier shall keep all production, inspection, testing and release records, CoAs and retained samples for each Lot for at least five (5) years after delivery, consistent with Section 10.3 of the Supply Agreement.

10.2 **Access.** Supplier shall provide copies of these records to Customer on request.

## 11. TERM AND GENERAL

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues in effect for as long as the Supply Agreement remains in effect and thereafter for as long as any obligation under Section 10 of this Quality Agreement remains outstanding.

11.2 **Confidentiality.** Information exchanged under this Quality Agreement is Confidential Information subject to Section 12 of the Supply Agreement.

11.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 15.4 of the Supply Agreement. Routine quality communications may be exchanged by email between the Parties' quality representatives.

11.4 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties, except that Specifications may be updated as provided in Section 2.3.

11.5 **Governing Law and Disputes.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Section 15.6 of the Supply Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of or relating to this Quality Agreement.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
