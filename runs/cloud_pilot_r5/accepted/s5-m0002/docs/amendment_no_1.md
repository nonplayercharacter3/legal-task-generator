# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between:

**Veltmar Glassworks, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1400 Furnace Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Harrowgate Bottling Co.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Millrace Avenue, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania 17101 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Customer are parties to a Supply Agreement made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier supplies, and Customer purchases, beverage containers.

B. Section 15.3 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

C. Under Section 5.3 of the Agreement, Supplier issued a Price Adjustment Notice dated July 30, 2026. The notice provides for an increase of fourteen percent (14%) in the prices set out in Exhibit B to the Agreement, effective October 2, 2026, and it was received by Customer on August 3, 2026. No objection to the Price Adjustment Notice has been delivered by Customer, and the Price Adjustment Notice remains subject to Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

D. The Parties wish to clarify and amend certain provisions of the Agreement as set out in this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and in the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar meaning means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

2.1 Section 11.2 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "11.2 **Exclusions.** The limitation in Section 11.1 and the exclusion in Section 11.3 do not apply to (a) Supplier's indemnity obligations under Section 9.1, which are excluded solely to third-party intellectual property claims under Section 9.1(c); (b) either Party's breach of Section 12; or (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct."

2.2 The amended Section 11.2 applies to all claims arising on or after the Amendment Date.

## 3. NOTICES

3.1 Section 15.4 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following at the end of that Section:

> "A copy of each notice to Supplier must also be sent to: Veltmar Glassworks, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, 1400 Furnace Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801. A copy of each notice to Customer must also be sent to: Harrowgate Bottling Co., Attention: Legal Department, 220 Millrace Avenue, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania 17101. Failure to send a copy does not affect the effectiveness of a notice otherwise given in accordance with this Section 15.4."

## 4. ASSIGNMENT

4.1 Section 14 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Section 14.4 immediately after Section 14.3:

> "14.4 **Assignment by Supplier.** Notwithstanding Section 14.3, Supplier may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, or any of its rights or obligations under it, without the consent of Customer, to (a) any Affiliate of Supplier; or (b) any successor to Supplier by merger, consolidation, change of control or sale of all or substantially all of the business or assets of Supplier to which this Agreement relates. Supplier shall give Customer written notice of any such assignment within thirty (30) days after it takes effect."

## 5. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

5.1 **Ratification.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and is ratified and confirmed by the Parties.

5.2 **Written Amendment.** This Amendment is a written amendment for the purposes of Sections 15.2 and 15.3 of the Agreement.

5.3 **No Other Waiver.** Nothing in this Amendment operates as a waiver of any right, power or remedy of either Party under the Agreement, except as expressly set out in this Amendment.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

6.2 **Dispute Resolution.** Section 15.6 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of or relating to this Amendment.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

6.4 **Authority.** Each Party represents that the individual signing this Amendment on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.
