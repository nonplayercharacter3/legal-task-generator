# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and made effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Supply Agreement Effective Date**" or the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Veltmar Glassworks, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1400 Furnace Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Harrowgate Bottling Co.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Millrace Avenue, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania 17101 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures glass and other beverage containers.

B. Customer bottles and distributes beverage products and wishes to buy containers from Supplier.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will supply, and Customer will buy, those containers.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of the entity.

1.2 "**Containers**" means bottles, jars and other containers for holding beverage products, together with associated closures where supplied by Supplier.

1.3 "**Products**" means the Containers listed in Exhibit A, as updated by the Parties in writing from time to time.

1.4 "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Customer under this Agreement.

1.5 "**Price Adjustment Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

1.6 "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality and Specifications Agreement between Supplier and Customer, as amended from time to time.

1.7 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out in Exhibit A and in the Quality Agreement.

1.8 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.9 "**Recall**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement shall be three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five-year Renewal Terms, five years each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal not more than 270 days and not less than 240 days before the expiration of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. All terms of this Agreement, including Sections 3 and 5, continue to apply during each Renewal Term.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY AND FORECASTS

3.1 **Exclusive Purchase.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase from Supplier, and Supplier shall be Customer's exclusive source of, all glass and PET containers for any beverage product manufactured, filled, bottled or packaged by or for Customer or its Affiliates, worldwide. Customer shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, purchase any such containers from any third party during the Term.

3.2 **Forecasts.** By the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Customer shall provide Supplier with a rolling twelve (12) month non-binding forecast of its expected Product requirements by Product and delivery location. The first three (3) months of each forecast are firm and binding on Customer.

3.3 **Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity sufficient to meet one hundred fifteen percent (115%) of Customer's most recent forecast. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly if it anticipates any shortfall in its ability to supply.

## 4. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by Purchase Order at least thirty (30) days before the requested delivery date. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt. Supplier shall not reject a Purchase Order that is consistent with the firm portion of Customer's forecast.

4.2 **Terms Governing Orders.** This Agreement governs each Purchase Order. Any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other ordering document have no effect.

4.3 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier designated by Customer, unless the Purchase Order specifies otherwise. Supplier shall deliver Products on the delivery date stated in the accepted Purchase Order.

4.4 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Products pass to Customer on delivery in accordance with Section 4.3.

4.5 **Late Delivery.** If Supplier fails to deliver any Products within five (5) business days after the agreed delivery date, Customer may, in addition to its other remedies, cancel the affected portion of the Purchase Order without liability.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for the Products set out in Exhibit B, as adjusted under this Section 5.

5.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer on shipment. Customer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier of the dispute and the reasons for it before the due date.

5.3 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust the prices in Exhibit B not more than once in any twelve (12) month period by giving Customer written notice of the adjustment, the effective date of the adjustment and reasonable supporting detail of the cost changes on which it is based (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**"). No price adjustment shall take effect earlier than sixty (60) days after Customer's receipt of the Price Adjustment Notice.

5.4 **Objection.** Customer may object to a Price Adjustment Notice by written notice to Supplier given within 30 days after Customer's receipt of a Price Adjustment Notice. If Customer objects in time, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days, and the existing prices shall remain in effect during that period. If the Parties do not agree within that period, either Party may refer the matter for resolution under Section 15.6. Any price adjustment to which Customer does not object within the period stated in this Section 5.4 is deemed accepted by Customer and takes effect on the date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay unless Customer provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 6. QUALITY AND SPECIFICATIONS

6.1 **Specifications.** Supplier shall manufacture all Products in accordance with the Specifications, the Quality Agreement and all applicable laws, including laws governing materials in contact with food and beverages.

6.2 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties shall comply with the Quality Agreement, which governs inspection, testing, quality control, change control and related matters.

6.3 **Changes.** Supplier shall not change any raw material, process, mold, facility or Specification affecting the Products without Customer's prior written consent.

## 7. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

7.1 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect the Products within thirty (30) days after delivery. Customer may reject any Products that do not conform to the Specifications by notice to Supplier describing the nonconformity.

7.2 **Rejected Products.** At Customer's option, Supplier shall promptly replace rejected Products or credit Customer for them, and shall bear the cost of returning or disposing of rejected Products. Acceptance of Products does not waive any warranty or other right of Customer.

## 8. WARRANTIES

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for eighteen (18) months after delivery, the Products will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship; (c) be fit for holding and distributing beverage products; and (d) comply with all applicable laws.

8.2 **Title and Non-Infringement.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to the Products free of all liens, and that the Products and their manufacture do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

8.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, and that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

8.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 8, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising out of (a) any defect in, or nonconformity of, the Products; (b) any personal injury, death or property damage caused by the Products; (c) any claim that the Products infringe a third party's intellectual property rights; or (d) Supplier's breach of this Agreement, negligence or willful misconduct.

9.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising out of the beverage contents filled into the Products by Customer, or Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall notify the indemnifying Party promptly of any claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control its defense and settlement, and cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes any obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 10. RECALLS

10.1 **Recall Events.** If any governmental authority requires, or Customer reasonably determines it necessary, to recall, withdraw or correct any beverage product filled in the Products because of a defect in or nonconformity of the Products (a "**Recall**"), Customer shall notify Supplier and the Parties shall cooperate in carrying out the Recall.

10.2 **Recall Costs.** Supplier shall reimburse Customer for all reasonable costs and expenses of any Recall to the extent caused by a defect in or nonconformity of the Products. These costs include notification, retrieval, transport, storage and destruction costs, the cost of replacement Products and lost beverage contents, and customer credits and refunds.

10.3 **Records.** Supplier shall maintain lot and batch traceability records for all Products for at least five (5) years after delivery and shall provide them to Customer on request.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the six months before the claim arose.

11.2 **Exclusions.** The limitation in Section 11.1 and the exclusion in Section 11.3 do not apply to (a) Supplier's indemnity obligations under Section 9.1; (b) Recall costs under Section 10; (c) either Party's breach of Section 12; or (d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

11.3 **Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.2, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"), and shall use it only to perform this Agreement. Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 12.

12.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than through the receiving Party's breach; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

12.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 12 continue during the Term and for five (5) years after its end.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving notice of it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Effect.** On termination or expiration, Supplier shall fulfil all accepted Purchase Orders unless Customer instructs otherwise, and Customer shall pay for all Products delivered. Sections 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.3 and 15 survive termination or expiration.

## 14. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS AND ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **Insurance.** During the Term and for three (3) years after its end, Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products liability and product recall coverage, with limits of not less than $10,000,000 per occurrence, and shall name Customer as an additional insured.

14.2 **Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement.

14.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 14.3 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. MISCELLANEOUS

15.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with its Exhibits and the Quality Agreement, is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous negotiations, term sheets, proposals, understandings and agreements, written or oral, on that subject matter.

15.2 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the documents governing the Parties' relationship, they control in the following order: written amendments, then Supply Agreement, then Quality Agreement.

15.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

15.4 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated at the beginning of this Agreement (or another address a Party designates by notice). Notices are effective on receipt.

15.5 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

15.6 **Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation between senior executives for thirty (30) days. If the dispute is not resolved, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts.

15.7 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for a delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, other than payment obligations, provided it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event affecting Supplier continues for more than thirty (30) days, Customer may buy affected Products from third parties for the duration of the event without breaching Section 3.1.

15.8 **Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

15.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

15.10 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
