# TERM SHEET

**Proposed Supply Arrangement for Beverage Containers**

**Date:** January 22, 2024

**Between:** Veltmar Glassworks, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Harrowgate Bottling Co. ("**Customer**")

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms the parties have discussed for the supply of beverage containers by Supplier to Customer. Except for Sections 10 and 11, this term sheet is not binding on either party. It does not create an obligation to negotiate or to enter into a definitive agreement. Binding obligations will arise only under a definitive supply agreement signed by both parties (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Products

1.1 Supplier will manufacture and supply glass beverage containers to Customer. The initial products are:

| Item | Product | Capacity | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Long-neck beer bottle | 12 fl. oz. | Amber |
| A-2 | Long-neck beer bottle | 22 fl. oz. | Amber |
| A-3 | Stubby beer bottle | 11.2 fl. oz. | Amber |

1.2 Detailed specifications will be set out in a separate quality and specifications agreement to be entered into between the parties.

## 2. Exclusivity

2.1 During the term, Supplier will be Customer's exclusive supplier of amber glass bottles for Customer's beer brands at its U.S. and Canadian facilities.

2.2 Customer remains free to source from any supplier all other containers, including containers for non-beer products, containers made from materials other than amber glass, and containers for facilities outside the United States and Canada.

2.3 If Supplier cannot supply because of a force majeure event lasting more than thirty (30) days, Customer may buy affected products from third parties for the duration of the event.

## 3. Term

3.1 The initial term will be three (3) years from the effective date of the Definitive Agreement.

3.2 Any renewal mechanics will be negotiated in the Definitive Agreement.

## 4. Forecasts and Orders

4.1 Customer will provide a monthly rolling twelve (12) month forecast. The first three (3) months of each forecast will be binding.

4.2 Supplier will maintain capacity to meet one hundred fifteen percent (115%) of Customer's most recent forecast.

4.3 Customer will place purchase orders at least thirty (30) days before the requested delivery date. Delivery will be FCA Supplier's facility (Incoterms 2020).

## 5. Pricing and Payment

5.1 Initial pricing per gross (144 units):

| Item | Price per Gross | Minimum Order |
|---|---|---|
| A-1 | $38.40 | 500 gross |
| A-2 | $61.20 | 250 gross |
| A-3 | $36.10 | 500 gross |

5.2 Supplier may adjust prices no more than once in any twelve (12) month period. Supplier must give written notice with supporting cost detail. No adjustment will take effect earlier than sixty (60) days after Customer receives the notice.

5.3 Customer may object to a price adjustment within thirty (30) days after receiving the notice. The parties will then negotiate in good faith, and existing prices will apply during the negotiation.

5.4 Payment of undisputed invoices will be due within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

## 6. Quality, Warranty and Recalls

6.1 Supplier will warrant, for eighteen (18) months after delivery, that the products conform to specifications, are free from defects and are fit for holding and distributing beverage products.

6.2 Supplier will reimburse Customer for the reasonable costs of any recall to the extent caused by a defect in or nonconformity of the products.

6.3 Supplier will maintain product liability and product recall insurance of not less than $10,000,000 per occurrence and will name Customer as an additional insured.

## 7. Indemnities and Liability

7.1 Supplier will indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from product defects, personal injury or property damage caused by the products, intellectual property infringement, and Supplier's breach, negligence or willful misconduct. Customer will indemnify Supplier against third-party claims arising from the beverage contents and Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

7.2 Each party's liability will be capped at the fees paid by Customer in the six (6) months before the claim arose. The cap will not apply to Supplier's indemnity obligations, recall costs, breaches of confidentiality, or gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

## 8. Assignment

8.1 Neither party may assign the Definitive Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 The Definitive Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## 10. Confidentiality (Binding)

10.1 Each party will keep the terms of this term sheet, and all non-public information exchanged in connection with it, confidential. This Section 10 survives for two (2) years from the date of this term sheet.

## 11. Costs (Binding)

11.1 Each party will bear its own costs and expenses relating to this term sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreement.
