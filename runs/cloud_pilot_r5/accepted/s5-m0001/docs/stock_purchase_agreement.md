# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 21, 2026, by and between **Tarrant Peak Partners LP**, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), and **Halvorsen Industrial Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), with respect to all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of **Corbel Fluidics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements contained in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances, for an aggregate purchase price of $84,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payments at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, less the Estimated Closing Indebtedness, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Estimated Closing Indebtedness to the holders thereof in accordance with customary payoff letters; and

(c) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, in accordance with Section 2.5.

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written statement setting forth Seller's good faith estimate of the Company's closing working capital and the aggregate indebtedness of the Company as of the Closing (the "**Estimated Closing Indebtedness**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.** Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a closing statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of closing working capital and closing indebtedness. Seller shall have thirty (30) days to review and dispute such statement in writing. Any disputed items not resolved by the parties within thirty (30) days after delivery of a dispute notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which final closing working capital exceeds or falls short of the target working capital set forth in Schedule 2.4, and decreased by the amount by which final closing indebtedness exceeds the Estimated Closing Indebtedness. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.5 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $8,450,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX. Unless earlier disbursed, the remaining balance of the Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of any then-pending claims, shall be released to Seller on the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as having been paid to the Person in respect of which such withholding was made.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions set forth in Article VII have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time or place as the parties may mutually agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the resignations of the directors and officers of the Company requested by Buyer;

(d) the certificate described in Section 7.2(d);

(e) a certificate of good standing of the Company issued by the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware as of a recent date;

(f) an IRS Form W-9 duly executed by Seller; and

(g) copies of the third-party consents described in Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate described in Section 7.3(c).
