Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") is the "Side Letter" referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of the date of this Letter Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are Tarrant Peak Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), and Halvorsen Industrial Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). It concerns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Corbel Fluidics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Buyer and Seller are entering into this Letter Agreement as a condition to their execution of the Purchase Agreement, and to record their understanding on certain matters relating to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual agreements in this Letter Agreement and the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Buyer and Seller agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Letter Agreement *mutatis mutandis*. References in this Letter Agreement to Articles and Sections of the Purchase Agreement are to the Articles and Sections of the Purchase Agreement as executed on the date of this Letter Agreement.

## 2. Fundamental Representations

For all purposes of the Purchase Agreement, including Sections 7.2(a), 9.1 and 9.5(b) thereof, "**Fundamental Representations**" means solely Sections 4.1 and 4.2 of the Purchase Agreement, and the definition of that term in Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement shall be read accordingly.

## 3. Notice of Claims

3.1 Any notice of a claim for indemnification under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement shall state the following, to the extent then known to the Indemnified Party:

(a) the Section or Sections of the Purchase Agreement under which the claim is made;

(b) the facts giving rise to the claim, in reasonable detail; and

(c) a good faith estimate of the Losses claimed.

3.2 The Indemnified Party may supplement or amend a claim notice from time to time as additional information becomes available. A claim notice delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement preserves the claim in accordance with that Section, notwithstanding any later supplement or amendment.

3.3 Claim notices delivered to Seller shall be sent to the attention of the Managing Member of Tarrant Peak GP LLC, with a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to Seller's outside counsel as designated in writing by Seller from time to time. Claim notices delivered to Buyer shall be sent to the attention of Buyer's Chief Executive Officer. In each case, delivery shall be in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. Escrow Instructions

4.1 Within five (5) Business Days after any amount becomes payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall execute and deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent directing release of that amount from the Escrow Amount. Such instructions shall be given in accordance with Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. The obligation to deliver instructions applies whether the amount becomes payable by written agreement of Buyer and Seller or by final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.2 Within five (5) Business Days after the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall execute and deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent directing release to Seller of the remaining balance of the Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of any then-pending claims, in accordance with Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement. Any amount retained for a pending claim shall be released, to the Buyer Indemnitee or to Seller as applicable, within five (5) Business Days after final resolution of that claim.

4.3 Neither Buyer nor Seller shall unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its execution of any joint written instruction required by this Section 4.

## 5. Confidentiality

The terms of this Letter Agreement are confidential. Neither party shall disclose them to any Person other than the following:

(a) its Affiliates, and its and their respective directors, officers, partners, members, employees, financing sources, insurers, accountants and legal counsel, in each case who need to know such terms and are bound by obligations of confidentiality;

(b) the Escrow Agent, to the extent reasonably required in connection with the Escrow Agreement; or

(c) as required by Law or by the rules of any securities exchange, or in connection with the enforcement of this Letter Agreement or the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

6.1 This Letter Agreement, and all claims, disputes and causes of action arising out of or relating to indemnification under the Purchase Agreement or this Letter Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware. This applies without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

6.2 Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to indemnification under the Purchase Agreement or this Letter Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. If, and only if, that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, such suit, action or proceeding shall be brought in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of such courts in any such suit, action or proceeding. Service of process by mail to a party's address for notices under the Purchase Agreement shall be effective service of process in any such suit, action or proceeding.

6.3 EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY LEGAL ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS LETTER AGREEMENT.

## 7. Miscellaneous

7.1 **Effect.** Except as set forth in this Letter Agreement, the Purchase Agreement remains in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.

7.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Letter Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver of any provision of this Letter Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party so waiving.

7.3 **Successors and Assigns.** This Letter Agreement binds and benefits Buyer and Seller and their respective successors and permitted assigns under the Purchase Agreement. It may not be assigned except together with an assignment of the Purchase Agreement permitted under Section 11.4 thereof.

7.4 **Termination.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with Article VIII thereof before the Closing, this Letter Agreement terminates automatically at the same time, except that Section 5 shall survive.

7.5 **Counterparts.** This Letter Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. A signed copy delivered by email or other electronic transmission shall have the same legal effect as delivery of an original signed copy.
