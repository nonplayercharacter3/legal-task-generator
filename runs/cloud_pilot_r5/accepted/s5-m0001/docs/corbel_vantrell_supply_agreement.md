# MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of December 15, 2021 (the "**Signing Date**") by and between **Vantrell Polymer Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Corbel Fluidics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures engineered polymer resins, including the resin grades described in Schedule A.

B. Customer designs and manufactures fluid-handling components, including valve bodies, manifolds and pump housings, that incorporate those resin grades.

C. Customer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, Customer's requirements of those resin grades on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of a Person or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 10.1.

"**Effective Date**" means January 1, 2022.

"**Extension Pricing**" means the per-kilogram prices for the Products set out in Part 2 of Schedule B.

"**Extension Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

"**Forecast**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product that does not conform to the Specifications or the warranties in Section 9.1 at the time of delivery.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association or other entity.

"**Products**" means the resin grades listed in Schedule A, as that Schedule may be amended by written agreement of the Parties.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Customer under Section 4.3.

"**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out in Schedule A.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Extension Term.

**1.2 Interpretation.** "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of and Schedules to this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SUPPLY AND PURCHASE

**2.1 Supply Commitment.** During the Term, Supplier shall manufacture and sell to Customer, and Customer shall purchase from Supplier, the Products ordered under Purchase Orders accepted or deemed accepted under Section 4.3.

**2.2 Requirements.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase from Supplier one hundred percent (100%) of Customer's requirements of the Products for use in Customer's manufacturing facilities in the United States, except to the extent Supplier is unable or fails to supply such requirements in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.3 Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity sufficient to supply at least one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the volumes set out in Customer's most recent Forecast. Supplier shall give Customer at least one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice of any planned discontinuation of any Product or any change to the manufacturing site, process or raw materials for any Product that could reasonably be expected to affect its form, fit, function or conformity to the Specifications.

**2.4 Minimum Annual Volume.** Customer shall purchase not less than 1,800,000 kilograms of Products in the aggregate in each calendar year of the Term. If Customer purchases less than that volume in any calendar year, other than as a result of Supplier's failure to supply or a Force Majeure Event, Customer shall pay Supplier, within forty-five (45) days after Supplier's invoice, an amount equal to eight percent (8%) of the then-applicable average price per kilogram multiplied by the shortfall.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until December 31, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 14.

**3.2 Extension Option.** Customer may extend the Term for one additional period of thirty-six (36) months commencing on the day after the last day of the Initial Term (the "**Extension Term**"), during which the Products shall be supplied at the Extension Pricing. Customer shall exercise this option by written notice to Supplier delivered not less than one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the Initial Term. If Customer does not deliver notice within that period, this Agreement expires at the end of the Initial Term.

**3.3 Continuing Orders.** Purchase Orders accepted before the expiration or termination of this Agreement shall be fulfilled in accordance with this Agreement unless Customer elects to cancel them in writing.

## 4. FORECASTS AND ORDERS

**4.1 Forecasts.** On or before the fifth (5th) Business Day of each calendar month, Customer shall deliver to Supplier a rolling twelve (12) month non-binding forecast of its requirements for each Product (each, a "**Forecast**"). The first three (3) months of each Forecast shall be binding on Customer to the extent of eighty percent (80%) of the forecast quantities.

**4.2 Use of Forecasts.** Supplier shall use Forecasts solely for production planning and raw material procurement for Customer and shall treat Forecasts as Customer's Confidential Information.

**4.3 Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by Purchase Orders specifying Product, quantity, delivery date and delivery location, issued at least thirty (30) days before the requested delivery date. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within three (3) Business Days after receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only to the extent the ordered quantities exceed one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the quantities for the relevant month in the most recent Forecast. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

**4.4 Conflicting Terms.** The terms of this Agreement govern each Purchase Order. Any additional or different terms in any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other form of either Party are void.

## 5. PRICING

**5.1 Prices.** During the Initial Term, the prices for the Products are the per-kilogram prices set out in Part 1 of Schedule B, as adjusted under Section 5.2.

**5.2 Raw Material Adjustment.** During the Initial Term, prices under Part 1 of Schedule B may be adjusted once per calendar quarter, upward or downward, to reflect changes in the published index referenced in Schedule B, in accordance with the formula set out in Schedule B. Supplier shall give Customer written notice of each adjustment, with reasonable supporting calculations, at least thirty (30) days before the first day of the quarter in which it applies.

**5.3 Extension Term Prices.** During any Extension Term, the prices for the Products are the Extension Pricing, which shall not be subject to adjustment under Section 5.2.

**5.4 Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, excise and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay or reimburse to the extent properly invoiced, other than taxes on Supplier's net income, property or employees.

## 6. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of each delivery of Products.

**6.2 Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account designated in writing by Supplier.

**6.3 Disputes.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith by giving written notice describing the dispute in reasonable detail before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**6.4 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 7. DELIVERY

**7.1 Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's manufacturing facility (Incoterms 2020), packaged and labeled in accordance with the Specifications and Customer's reasonable written instructions.

**7.2 Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery to Customer's designated carrier at Supplier's facility.

**7.3 Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly upon becoming aware of any anticipated delay in delivery. If Supplier fails to deliver any quantity of Products within ten (10) days after the confirmed delivery date, other than as a result of a Force Majeure Event, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, ship such Products by expedited means at Supplier's expense or reimburse Customer's reasonable incremental cost of procuring substitute product from a third party for the delayed quantity.

## 8. QUALITY

**8.1 Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 and shall manufacture all Products in accordance with that system and the Specifications.

**8.2 Certificates of Analysis.** Each shipment of Products shall be accompanied by a certificate of analysis confirming conformity to the Specifications.

**8.3 Inspection and Rejection.** Customer may inspect each shipment within thirty (30) days after receipt and reject any Nonconforming Product by written notice. For latent defects, the inspection period runs from the date Customer discovers or reasonably should have discovered the nonconformity, but not later than twelve (12) months after delivery.

**8.4 Remedies.** For Nonconforming Product, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, promptly replace it at Supplier's expense or credit or refund the price paid, and shall bear the costs of return shipment and disposal.

**8.5 Audits.** Upon fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice, and not more than once per calendar year (except following a material quality failure), Customer may audit Supplier's manufacturing facilities and quality records relating to the Products during normal business hours.

## 9. WARRANTIES

**9.1 Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, at delivery, each Product (a) conforms to the Specifications and the applicable certificate of analysis; (b) is free from defects in material and workmanship; (c) has been manufactured in compliance with all applicable laws; and (d) is delivered with good title, free of liens and encumbrances.

**9.2 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation.

**9.3 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

**10.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by a Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Forecasts, pricing, Specifications, formulations, and business plans, whether disclosed orally, in writing or electronically.

**10.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to perform or exercise its rights under this Agreement, shall not disclose it except to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section 10, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.

**10.3 Exclusions.** These obligations do not apply to information that the receiving Party can show (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through its breach; (b) was lawfully known to it before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information. Disclosure required by law or court order is permitted upon prompt notice to the disclosing Party, where lawful, and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective treatment.

**10.4 Duration.** This Section 10 survives for five (5) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely as to trade secrets.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of third-party claims to the extent based on (a) any Nonconforming Product; (b) any claim that the Products infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights; or (c) Supplier's negligence, willful misconduct or violation of law.

**11.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees, from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of third-party claims to the extent based on (a) Customer's products, other than to the extent caused by any Nonconforming Product; or (b) Customer's negligence, willful misconduct or violation of law.

**11.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, permit the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided that no settlement imposing any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**12.1 Exclusion.** EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11, BREACH OF SECTION 10, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

**12.2 Cap.** EXCEPT FOR THE MATTERS EXCLUDED IN SECTION 12.1, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNTS PAID AND PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO SUPPLIER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 13. INSURANCE

During the Term and for two (2) years thereafter, each Party shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, commercial general liability insurance (including products and completed operations coverage) with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.

## 14. TERMINATION

**14.1 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it; or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver or trustee appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**14.2 Change in Ownership of Customer.** Any change in the direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting stock of Customer, whether by sale or transfer of stock, merger, consolidation or otherwise, in a single transaction or a series of related transactions, shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer and shall require the prior written consent of Supplier. If any such change occurs without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Customer.

**14.3 Effect of Termination or Expiration.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement: (a) Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts owed for Products delivered; (b) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, subject to customary archival retention; and (c) Sections 6, 8.3, 8.4, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.3, 17.1 and 17.2 survive.

## 15. FORCE MAJEURE

**15.1 Excused Performance.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performing its obligations (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, war, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, or labor disputes not involving that Party's own workforce (each, a "**Force Majeure Event**"), provided that the affected Party gives prompt written notice and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

**15.2 Allocation.** During any Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier's capacity, Supplier shall allocate available supply of the Products to Customer at least pro rata with its other customers based on the preceding six (6) months' purchases, and Customer may purchase replacement product from third parties for the affected quantities without breach of Section 2.2.

**15.3 Extended Event.** If a Force Majeure Event prevents performance for more than ninety (90) consecutive days, the unaffected Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT

**16.1 Assignment.** Customer may not assign or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Supplier may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Customer, including in connection with a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or any part of Supplier's resin business or the assets used in manufacturing the Products. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 16.1 is void.

**16.2 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
