# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and among **Halvorsen Industrial Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Tarrant Peak Partners LP**, a Delaware limited partnership ("**Seller**"), and **Granite Ledger Trust Company**, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Corbel Fluidics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $8,450,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to hold and disburse the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Escrow Account**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest and other earnings on it, less all disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction directing the disposition of all or part of the Escrow Funds.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, in substantially the form of Exhibit A.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, at any time, the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in all Claim Notices that have been delivered to the Escrow Agent before that time and that have not been resolved and paid or released under Section 4.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement *mutatis mutandis*. References to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of and Exhibits to this Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Appointment and Deposit.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt to Buyer and Seller in writing promptly after the deposit is received.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Agent shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds. The Escrow Funds are not property of the Escrow Agent and are not subject to any lien, set-off or claim of the Escrow Agent or its creditors, except as provided in Section 6.3.

## 3. INVESTMENT

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the money market deposit account or United States Treasury money market fund designated by Joint Written Instruction delivered at the Closing. Absent such instruction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured to the extent available by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. Buyer and Seller may change the investment by Joint Written Instruction.

**3.2 Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds and are disbursed with them under Section 4.

**3.3 No Liability for Losses.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss on any investment made in accordance with this Section 3, other than losses resulting from its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 4. CLAIMS AND DISBURSEMENTS

**4.1 Claim Notices.** If Buyer, on behalf of any Buyer Indemnitee, delivers to Seller a notice of a claim for indemnification under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer shall concurrently deliver a copy of that notice to the Escrow Agent (each, a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice delivered to the Escrow Agent shall state the amount of Losses claimed or, if not then determinable, Buyer's good faith estimate of that amount. The Escrow Agent shall have no duty to determine the validity or amount of any claim.

**4.2 Disbursement on Joint Written Instruction.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Joint Written Instruction within two (2) Business Days after receiving it.

**4.3 Disbursement on Final Order.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Final Order within five (5) Business Days after receiving a copy of the Final Order, together with a certificate of the delivering party (with a copy to the other of Buyer and Seller) stating that the order is final and non-appealable and attaching a copy of the order. The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on that certificate.

**4.4 Release on the Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount, upon receipt of a Joint Written Instruction directing that release. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold an amount equal to the Pending Claim Amount after the Release Date. As each pending claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for it to the Buyer Indemnitee or to Seller, as applicable, in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction or Final Order. After all pending claims are resolved, any remaining Escrow Funds shall be disbursed to Seller.

**4.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay any amount directed to be disbursed, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available and shall have no further obligation with respect to the shortfall. Any shortfall shall be paid by Seller in accordance with Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.6 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in the applicable Joint Written Instruction or certificate. Before making any disbursement to a new account, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the wire instructions by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Exhibit B.

## 5. TAX MATTERS

**5.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that, for federal and applicable state and local income tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed, and all interest and earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer, to the extent required by applicable Law.

**5.2 Tax Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall report earnings on the Escrow Funds to Buyer on IRS Form 1099 or other applicable form. At or before the Closing, Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9.

**5.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law and shall remit it to the appropriate Governmental Authority.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are solely ministerial and are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or fiduciary obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by or charged with knowledge of, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it reasonably believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper person. The Escrow Agent may consult legal counsel and is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of such counsel.

**6.3 Fees and Expenses.** Buyer and Seller shall pay the Escrow Agent the fees set out in Exhibit C, together with its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, in equal shares, consistent with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement. As between Buyer and Seller, any amount paid by one of them in excess of its share shall be reimbursed by the other within ten (10) Business Days after demand.

**6.4 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally as to the Escrow Agent, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers and employees from and against all losses, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of its performance under this Agreement, other than those resulting from its gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 6.4, except that a party whose breach of this Agreement gave rise to the amount shall bear it in full. This Section 6.4 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.5 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted under this Agreement except to the extent caused by its gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages.

**6.6 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands concerning the Escrow Funds, it may continue to hold the Escrow Funds until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order resolving the conflict, or it may interplead the Escrow Funds with a court referred to in Section 8.2.

**6.7 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which shall be a bank or trust company organized under the laws of the United States or any state with combined capital and surplus of at least $250,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court referred to in Section 8.2 to appoint one. The resignation or removal becomes effective when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to the successor.

**6.8 Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall deliver to Buyer and Seller a monthly statement of the Escrow Account showing deposits, earnings, disbursements and the balance of the Escrow Funds.

## 7. TERMINATION

This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with Section 4, except that Sections 6.3, 6.4 and 6.5 and Section 8 survive.

## 8. GENERAL

**8.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**8.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**8.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY LEGAL ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**8.4 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) when received if sent by nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) on the date sent by email if sent prior to 6:00 p.m. New York time on a Business Day (and otherwise on the next Business Day), in each case to the addresses set forth on the signature pages to this Agreement or such other address as a party may designate by notice. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

**8.5 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that (a) any assignment by Buyer or Seller permitted under Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement shall be effective against the Escrow Agent upon written notice to it, and (b) any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which acquires all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall succeed to the Escrow Agent's rights and obligations under this Agreement without further act.

**8.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**8.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**8.8 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person that opens an account, and each of them shall provide such information as the Escrow Agent reasonably requests.

**8.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
