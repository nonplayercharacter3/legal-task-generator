## 1. REFERENCE AND PURPOSE

1.1 **Agreement.** This notice (this "**Notice**") is delivered by Halvorsen Bioinstruments, Inc. ("**Supplier**") to Crestline Medical Supply LLC ("**Distributor**") under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2023, between Supplier and Distributor, as amended by the First Amendment to Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of June 15, 2025 (as amended, the "**Agreement**").

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Notice have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.3 **Purpose.** Under Section 5.1 of the Agreement and paragraph 2 of Schedule B (*Annual Adjustment*), Supplier gives Distributor written notice of the adjustment to the Distributor prices for the Products set out in Section 3 of this Notice.

## 2. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TIMING

2.1 **Adjustment Date.** The adjusted prices take effect on March 3, 2027, which is the first day of the Contract Year running from March 3, 2027 through March 2, 2028 (the "**Adjustment Date**").

2.2 **Advance Notice.** This Notice is delivered more than sixty (60) days before the Adjustment Date, as required by paragraph 2 of Schedule B.

2.3 **Frequency.** This is the only price adjustment Supplier will make for the Contract Year beginning on the Adjustment Date.

4.1 Under paragraph 3 of Schedule B (*Price Protection*), Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier before the Adjustment Date will be invoiced at the prices in effect on the date of acceptance, even if the Products ship on or after the Adjustment Date.
