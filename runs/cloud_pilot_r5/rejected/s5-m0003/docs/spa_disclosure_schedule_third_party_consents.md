## 1. PRELIMINARY STATEMENTS

1.1 **Delivery.** This Seller Disclosure Schedule (this "**Disclosure Schedule**") is delivered by the Sellers and the Company to Varga Diagnostics Holdings Corp. ("**Buyer**") under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of September 8, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") among Buyer, the Sellers, the Sellers' Representative and Halvorsen Bioinstruments, Inc. (the "**Company**").

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. Section and part numbers in this Disclosure Schedule correspond to the section numbers of the Purchase Agreement.

1.3 **Cross-References.** Disclosure in any part of this Disclosure Schedule qualifies the correspondingly numbered representation and warranty in Article III of the Purchase Agreement. It also qualifies any other representation and warranty in Article III to the extent its relevance is reasonably apparent on the face of the disclosure.

1.4 **No Admission.** Listing a Contract or other item in this Disclosure Schedule is not an admission that the item is material or outside the ordinary course of business. It is also not an admission of liability or obligation to any third party.

1.5 **Summaries.** Descriptions of Contracts in this Disclosure Schedule are summaries. True and complete copies of each listed Contract, including all amendments, have been made available to Buyer in the electronic data room maintained for the Transactions (the "**Data Room**"). Data Room index references are given in brackets.

## 2. SCOPE OF PART 3.5

2.1 **Representation Qualified.** This Part 3.5 is delivered in response to Section 3.5 of the Purchase Agreement (*Third-Party Consents*). Section 3.5 provides that, except as set forth in Part 3.5 of this Disclosure Schedule, no consent, approval, waiver or authorization of, or notice to, any counterparty to a Material Contract is required in connection with the execution, delivery or performance of the Purchase Agreement or the consummation of the Transactions, including the sale of the Shares to Buyer.

2.2 **Categories.** Each Material Contract listed below is classified in one of the following categories:

(a) "**Consent required**": the counterparty's consent, approval or waiver is required to consummate the Transactions without breach, default, termination right or acceleration;

(b) "**Notice only**": written notice to the counterparty is required before or after the Closing, and no consent, approval or waiver is required; or

(c) "**Payoff at Closing**": the Contract will be repaid and terminated at the Closing in accordance with Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. SECTION 3.5: THIRD-PARTY CONSENTS

- *Relevant provision:* Section 18.3 (Transfer). A transfer of more than 50% of the tenant's stock is deemed an assignment requiring landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

- *Status:* Notice to be delivered within thirty (30) days after the Closing.
