# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Bioinstruments, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Brandywine Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Supplier**"), and **Crestline Medical Supply LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Corporate Exchange Drive, Columbus, Ohio 43231 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells laboratory diagnostic instruments and related reagents, consumables and spare parts.

B. Distributor is in the business of marketing, selling and distributing laboratory and medical products to hospitals, reference laboratories, physician office laboratories and other healthcare customers.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the person or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to Supplier, (a) any transaction or series of related transactions by which any person or group of persons acting together acquires, directly or indirectly, beneficial ownership of more than 50% of the outstanding voting stock of Supplier, whether by stock purchase, merger, consolidation or otherwise; or (b) the sale or transfer of all or substantially all of the assets of Supplier to which this Agreement relates.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of the Effective Date.

"**Customer**" means any hospital, laboratory, clinic, physician practice or other end user located in the Territory that purchases Products for its own use and not for resale.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 16.1.

"**Marks**" means the trademarks, trade names, logos and trade dress of Supplier listed in Schedule C, and any others Supplier designates in writing for use with the Products.

"**Minimum Purchase Commitment**" means the minimum annual purchase quantities set out in Schedule A.

"**Products**" means the instruments, reagents, consumables and spare parts listed in Schedule A, as updated by the parties in writing from time to time.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor to Supplier under Section 4.

"**Qualified Transferee**" means any person engaged in the distribution of laboratory or diagnostic products in the Territory that assumes Distributor's obligations under this Agreement in writing.

"**Specifications**" means Supplier's published specifications for each Product in effect at the time of shipment.

"**Territory**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the States of Ohio, Indiana and Michigan (the "**Territory**"), and Distributor accepts that appointment.

2.2 **Supplier Restrictions.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller for the Products in the Territory and shall not sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except for (a) sales to national accounts listed in Schedule D under Supplier's existing group purchasing contracts, and (b) sales Distributor declines in writing.

2.3 **Distributor Restrictions.** Distributor shall not actively solicit Customers for the Products outside the Territory, and shall not, during the Term, distribute any laboratory hematology or clinical chemistry analyzer that competes directly with a Product in the Territory without Supplier's prior written consent.

2.4 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Distributor purchases Products for its own account and resells them at prices it determines in its sole discretion. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, franchise, joint venture or agency.

## 3. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

3.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to market, promote and sell the Products throughout the Territory and shall maintain a trained sales and service force sufficient for that purpose.

3.2 **Minimum Purchases.** Distributor shall purchase not less than the Minimum Purchase Commitment in each Contract Year. If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Commitment in any Contract Year, Supplier may, as its sole remedy, convert the appointment under Section 2.1 to a non-exclusive appointment on sixty (60) days' written notice.

3.3 **Regulatory Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all laws applicable to its distribution of the Products, including applicable requirements of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration relating to distributors of medical devices, and shall maintain complaint files, distribution records and traceability records for each Product unit sold sufficient to permit a recall.

3.4 **Handling and Labeling.** Distributor shall store, handle and transport the Products in accordance with Supplier's written instructions and the Specifications. Distributor shall not alter, remove or obscure any label, package insert or Mark on the Products, and shall not relabel or repackage any Product except as Supplier approves in writing.

3.5 **Reports.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a report of Product sales by Customer category and a rolling twelve-month forecast.

## 4. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt; a Purchase Order consistent with this Agreement and the most recent forecast shall not be unreasonably rejected.

4.2 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's facility in New Castle, Delaware (Incoterms 2020). Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to Distributor's designated carrier.

4.3 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice that add to or conflict with this Agreement are of no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices set out in Schedule B, as adjusted under Schedule B.

5.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice upon shipment. Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within that period.

5.3 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

5.4 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Distributor shall pay unless it provides a valid resale certificate.

## 6. SUPPORT AND TRAINING

6.1 Supplier shall provide initial and periodic technical and sales training to Distributor's personnel at no additional charge, and reasonable quantities of marketing literature.

6.2 Supplier shall provide second-tier technical support to Distributor for installed instruments during Supplier's normal business hours.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor that each Product will conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after delivery for instruments, and until the labeled expiration date for reagents and consumables.

7.2 **Remedy.** Supplier's sole obligation for breach of Section 7.1 is, at Supplier's option, to repair or replace the non-conforming Product or refund the price paid, provided Distributor notifies Supplier during the warranty period.

7.3 **Exclusions.** Section 7.1 does not apply to Products that have been misused, improperly stored or handled, altered, relabeled or repackaged by any person other than Supplier.

7.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION 7, SUPPLIER MAKES NO WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 8. RECALLS

8.1 If any Product is subject to a recall, field correction or market withdrawal, Distributor shall cooperate fully with Supplier, including by providing distribution records and contacting Customers as Supplier reasonably directs.

8.2 Supplier bears the reasonable costs of a recall except to the extent the recall results from Distributor's breach of Section 3.3 or 3.4, in which case Distributor bears those costs.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, product roadmaps, technical data, Customer lists and the terms of this Agreement.

9.2 **Obligations.** The receiving party shall use the disclosing party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement, shall not disclose it except to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.

9.3 **Exceptions.** Section 9.2 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was known to the receiving party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law after giving reasonable advance notice where lawful.

9.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 survive for five (5) years after expiration or termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND TRADEMARKS

10.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except under Section 14.2), royalty-free license during the Term to use the Marks in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's trademark usage guidelines.

10.2 **Ownership.** Supplier owns all right, title and interest in the Products, the Marks and all related intellectual property. All goodwill from Distributor's use of the Marks inures to Supplier.

10.3 **Restrictions.** Distributor shall not register or use any trademark, domain name or trade name confusingly similar to the Marks, shall not reverse engineer any Product or its software, and shall not challenge Supplier's rights in the Marks.

10.4 **Software.** Embedded software in the instruments is licensed, not sold, to Customers under Supplier's end-user license terms, which Distributor shall pass through with each instrument.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor and its Affiliates from third-party claims to the extent arising from (a) a defect in the design or manufacture of a Product as delivered by Supplier, or (b) an allegation that a Product or the Marks, as supplied, infringe a United States patent, copyright or trademark.

11.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates from third-party claims to the extent arising from (a) Distributor's storage, handling, transport, relabeling or repackaging of Products, (b) any warranty or representation about the Products made by Distributor beyond those authorized by Supplier in writing, (c) Distributor's use of the Marks other than as permitted by Section 10, or (d) Distributor's violation of applicable law.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall promptly notify the indemnifying party of the claim, give it sole control of the defense and settlement (provided no settlement imposes liability or admission on the indemnified party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, however caused and on any theory of liability.

12.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable to Supplier in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to breaches of Sections 9 and 10, and indemnification obligations under Section 11.

## 13. INSURANCE

Each party shall maintain, during the Term and for two (2) years afterward, commercial general liability insurance (including products liability for Supplier) with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily or by operation of law, without Distributor's prior written consent. A Change of Control of Supplier is governed by Section 15.4.

14.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor may assign this Agreement in whole, without Supplier's consent, to any of its Affiliates or to any Qualified Transferee, upon written notice to Supplier given within thirty (30) days after the assignment. Any other assignment by Distributor requires Supplier's prior written consent.

14.3 **Effect.** Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **For Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

15.2 **Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Regulatory.** Supplier may terminate this Agreement as to any Product on ninety (90) days' written notice if Supplier discontinues that Product generally or is required by a regulatory authority to withdraw it from the market.

15.4 **Supplier Change of Control.** Supplier shall not undergo a Change of Control without Distributor's prior written consent. Supplier shall request that consent in writing not less than sixty (60) days before the anticipated closing of the Change of Control, identifying the acquiring person. If Supplier undergoes a Change of Control without Distributor's prior written consent, Distributor may terminate this Agreement by written notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after Distributor learns of the closing, and Supplier shall pay Distributor, within thirty (30) days after the termination, a termination fee equal to twelve months of average Product purchases, calculated as twelve (12) times the average monthly amount invoiced by Supplier to Distributor for Products during the twelve (12) full calendar months preceding the closing of the Change of Control.

15.5 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) all amounts owed become due, (b) Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products for ninety (90) days, subject to Sections 3.4 and 10, or Supplier may elect to repurchase that inventory at Distributor's landed cost, (c) each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, and (d) Distributor shall cease use of the Marks after the sell-off period.

15.6 **Survival.** Sections 5 (as to amounts accrued), 7.4, 8, 9, 10.2, 11, 12, 15.4 (as to the termination fee), 15.5, 15.6 and 17 survive expiration or termination.

## 16. TERM AND RENEWAL

16.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until March 2, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**").

16.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**," and together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

17.2 **Venue.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

17.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier or certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses in the preamble (for Supplier, Attention: General Counsel; for Distributor, Attention: President), or to another address a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

17.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

17.5 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: written amendments, then the body of this Agreement, then the Schedules.

17.6 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice.

17.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

17.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
