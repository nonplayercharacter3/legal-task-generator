# FIRST AMENDMENT TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This First Amendment to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**First Amendment**") is entered into as of June 15, 2025 (the "**First Amendment Effective Date**") by and between **Halvorsen Bioinstruments, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Brandywine Parkway, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Supplier**"), and **Crestline Medical Supply LLC**, an Ohio limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2275 Corporate Exchange Drive, Columbus, Ohio 43231 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to that certain Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2023 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. The Initial Term of the Agreement is scheduled to expire on March 2, 2026, and the parties wish to extend the Initial Term and make certain other changes to the Agreement.

C. Section 17.4 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this First Amendment and the Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Capitalized Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this First Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the First Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar import means the Agreement as amended by this First Amendment.

## 2. EXTENSION OF INITIAL TERM

2.1 **Amendment to Section 16.1.** Section 16.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "16.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until March 2, 2029 (the "**Initial Term**")."

2.2 **Renewal.** Following expiration of the Initial Term as extended by Section 2.1 of this First Amendment (the "**Extended Initial Term**"), the Agreement shall renew in accordance with Section 16.2 of the Agreement. Any notice of non-renewal shall be given at least one hundred twenty (120) days before March 2, 2029 or before the end of the then-current Renewal Term, as applicable.

2.3 **Contract Years.** The Extended Initial Term does not change the definition of "Contract Year." The Minimum Purchase Commitment set out in Schedule A shall continue to apply to each Contract Year during the Extended Initial Term.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO SECTION 12.3

3.1 **Restatement.** Section 12.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "12.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 shall not apply to a party's breach of Section 9."

3.2 **Cross-References.** References in Sections 12.1 and 12.2 of the Agreement to "Section 12.3" refer to Section 12.3 as restated by Section 3.1 of this First Amendment.

## 4. NOTICES

4.1 **Distributor Notice Address.** For purposes of Section 17.3 of the Agreement, notices to Distributor shall be delivered to the address set out in the preamble to this First Amendment, Attention: President, with a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to Distributor, Attention: Director of Contracts, at the same address.

## 5. RATIFICATION

5.1 **Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this First Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect, and the parties ratify and confirm the Agreement as amended.

5.2 **Representations.** Each party represents to the other that it has full power and authority to enter into this First Amendment, that this First Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by it, and that this First Amendment is a valid and binding obligation enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

## 6. GENERAL

6.1 **Governing Law.** This First Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, consistent with Section 17.1 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Venue.** Section 17.2 of the Agreement applies to any action arising out of this First Amendment.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This First Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

6.4 **Headings.** Headings in this First Amendment are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this First Amendment as of the First Amendment Effective Date.
