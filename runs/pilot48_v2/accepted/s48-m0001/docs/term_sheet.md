# TERM SHEET

## HelioPlan Software License: Zikeiv Software, Inc. and Stutind Solar Installations, LLC

**Date:** December 12, 2025

This Term Sheet sets out the principal terms on which Zikeiv Software, Inc. ("**Zikeiv**") would license its HelioPlan solar design, proposal and permitting platform to Stutind Solar Installations, LLC ("**Stutind**"). Except as stated in Section 15, this Term Sheet is not binding. Binding obligations will arise only under definitive agreements signed by both parties (the "**Definitive Agreements**").

## 1. Parties

| | |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Zikeiv Software, Inc., a Delaware corporation |
| Licensee | Stutind Solar Installations, LLC, an Arizona limited liability company |

## 2. Transaction Documents

The parties intend to enter into the following Definitive Agreements:

(a) a Software License Agreement;

(b) an Order Form issued under the Software License Agreement;

(c) a Data Processing Addendum covering Zikeiv's processing of personal data on Stutind's behalf;

(d) a quality and service-level agreement; and

(e) a Statement of Work for development of a Permitting Automation Module.

Target signing date for the Software License Agreement and Order Form: January 15, 2026.

## 3. Licensed Software

HelioPlan Design, HelioPlan Proposal, HelioPlan Permit and HelioPlan Integrations (standard CRM connectors and utility interconnection portal integrations). The software will be hosted by Zikeiv in U.S. data centers, and all updates will be included at no additional charge.

## 4. Seats and Users

Up to 180 installer seats for Stutind employees and individual contractors. Stutind affiliates may use seats that Stutind purchases. Additional seats will be available at the per-seat rate implied by the subscription fee, prorated.

## 5. Subscription Term

(a) **Start date:** February 1, 2026.

(b) **Initial term:** thirty-six (36) months.

(c) **Renewal:** automatic renewal for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 6. Fees

(a) **Annual subscription fee:** $312,000 for up to 180 installer seats, fixed for the initial term.

(b) **Invoicing:** annually in advance. Payment due within thirty (30) days after receipt of invoice.

(c) **Professional services:** charged under the Statement of Work on a milestone basis. Fees and milestones will be set out in the Statement of Work.

(d) **Disputed amounts:** Stutind may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith on written notice before the due date.

## 7. Permitting Automation Module

Zikeiv will develop and deliver a Permitting Automation Module under the Statement of Work. The module will automate permit package assembly for authorities having jurisdiction in Stutind's service area. Deliverables will be subject to an acceptance testing procedure, with a reasonable period for Stutind to test and to notify nonconformities.

## 8. Service Levels and Support

Platform availability, support response times and service credits will be set out in the quality and service-level agreement. Stutind will keep its termination rights for chronic availability failures.

## 9. Exclusivity

In consideration of the pricing in Section 6, Stutind will agree to an exclusivity covenant during the subscription term. Its scope will be: Residential rooftop design software only, in Arizona and Nevada. Stutind's commercial, utility-scale and other business lines, and all jurisdictions outside Arizona and Nevada, are not covered.

## 10. Data and Security

(a) Stutind will own all of its data, including customer records, site data, system designs, pipeline data and proposal pricing.

(b) Zikeiv will maintain an industry-standard information security program and a SOC 2 Type II report. It will encrypt data in transit and at rest and will give notice of security incidents within seventy-two (72) hours.

(c) On expiration or termination, Zikeiv will make Stutind data available for export within thirty (30) days and will then delete it.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

(a) Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid and payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

(b) The following will be excluded from the cap: indemnification obligations, breach of data security or confidentiality obligations, and gross negligence or willful misconduct.

(c) There will be a mutual exclusion of consequential damages, subject to the same exceptions.

## 12. Indemnification

Zikeiv will indemnify Stutind against third-party intellectual property infringement claims relating to HelioPlan. Stutind will indemnify Zikeiv against third-party claims arising from Stutind data or from Stutind's prohibited use of the software.

## 13. Assignment

Neither party may assign without the other party's prior written consent.

## 14. Governing Law and Forum

Delaware law. Exclusive jurisdiction in the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

## 15. Binding Provisions

Only this Section 15 and Section 16 are binding. The parties will keep the terms of this Term Sheet confidential, except for disclosure to their advisors. Each party bears its own costs in connection with this Term Sheet and the negotiation of the Definitive Agreements.

## 16. Counterparts

This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
