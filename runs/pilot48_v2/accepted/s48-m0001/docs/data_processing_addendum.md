# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 between **Zikeiv Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Stutind Solar Installations, LLC**, an Arizona limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). It supplements the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

In this DPA:

"**Applicable Data Protection Laws**" means all United States federal, state and local laws and regulations, and all Canadian federal and provincial laws, that apply to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include, where applicable, state consumer privacy laws, state data breach notification laws and state data security laws.

"**Data Subject**" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates. Data Subjects include Licensee's homeowner and commercial customers and prospective customers, and Licensee's Authorized Users.

"**Personal Data**" means any information contained in Licensee Data that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with a Data Subject, or that is defined as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

"**Process**" and "**Processing**" mean any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. Such operations include collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, alteration, erasure and destruction.

"**Security Incident**" means any unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data in Licensor's or a Subprocessor's possession, custody or control.

"**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Licensor to Process Personal Data on Licensor's behalf in connection with the Agreement.

## 2. Roles and Scope

**2.1 Roles.** Licensee is the business or controller of the Personal Data. Licensor Processes Personal Data as Licensee's service provider, processor or contractor, as those terms are used in Applicable Data Protection Laws.

**2.2 Scope.** This DPA applies to all Personal Data that Licensor Processes on behalf of Licensee in providing the Licensed Software and the services under the Agreement, the Quality Agreement and any SOW. Annex 1 describes the Processing.

**2.3 Licensee Obligations.** Licensee is responsible for: (a) the accuracy of the Personal Data it submits to the Licensed Software; (b) giving any notices to Data Subjects that Applicable Data Protection Laws require; and (c) obtaining any consents that Applicable Data Protection Laws require for Licensor's Processing under this DPA.

## 3. Processing Instructions

**3.1 Documented Instructions.** Licensor shall Process Personal Data only on Licensee's documented instructions. Those instructions are the Agreement, this DPA and Licensee's configuration and use of the Licensed Software. Licensee may give additional written instructions consistent with the Agreement.

**3.2 Restrictions.** Licensor shall not: (a) sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are used in Applicable Data Protection Laws; (b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Agreement, including for any commercial purpose of its own; (c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Licensor and Licensee; or (d) combine Personal Data with personal data that Licensor receives from or on behalf of any other person, except as Applicable Data Protection Laws permit.

**3.3 Aggregated Data.** Licensor may create aggregated and de-identified data only as permitted by Section 7.2 of the Agreement. Licensor shall take reasonable measures to ensure that such data cannot be re-identified. It shall not attempt to re-identify it, and shall contractually require any recipient not to attempt re-identification.

**3.4 Unlawful Instructions.** Licensor shall promptly inform Licensee if, in Licensor's reasonable opinion, an instruction from Licensee violates Applicable Data Protection Laws.

**3.5 Compliance.** Licensor shall comply with the obligations that Applicable Data Protection Laws impose on it as a service provider or processor. It shall notify Licensee if it determines that it can no longer meet those obligations.

## 4. Licensor Personnel

Licensor shall ensure that each person authorized to Process Personal Data: (a) is subject to a written duty of confidentiality; (b) has received appropriate training in data protection and information security; and (c) has access only to the Personal Data needed to perform that person's duties.

## 5. Security

**5.1 Security Measures.** Licensor shall implement and maintain the information security program described in Section 9.1 of the Agreement. It shall also implement the technical and organizational measures described in Annex 2.

**5.2 Location of Processing.** Licensor shall store Personal Data, including backups, only in data centers located in the United States, consistent with Section 4.1 of the Agreement and Section 7.2 of the Quality Agreement. Licensor shall not Process Personal Data outside the United States without Licensee's prior written consent.

**5.3 Changes.** Licensor may update its security measures from time to time. No update may materially reduce the overall level of protection given to Personal Data.

## 6. Subprocessors

**6.1 Authorization.** Licensee generally authorizes Licensor to engage Subprocessors, subject to this Section 6. The Subprocessors engaged as of the date of this DPA are listed in Annex 3.

**6.2 Notice of New Subprocessors.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' written notice before engaging a new Subprocessor. The notice shall identify the Subprocessor, its location and the Processing it will perform.

**6.3 Objection.** Licensee may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by written notice within the thirty (30) day notice period. The Parties shall discuss Licensee's objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it within thirty (30) days, Licensee may terminate the affected part of the subscription by written notice and receive a refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the Subscription Term for that part.

**6.4 Subprocessor Terms.** Licensor shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective of Personal Data than those in this DPA. As provided in Section 15.4 of the Agreement, Licensor remains responsible for the performance of each Subprocessor.

## 7. Security Incidents

**7.1 Notification.** Licensor shall notify Licensee of any Security Incident without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of it, consistent with Section 9.3 of the Agreement.

**7.2 Content.** To the extent known, the notice shall describe: (a) the nature of the Security Incident; (b) the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected; (c) the likely consequences; and (d) the measures taken or proposed to contain and remediate it. Licensor shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

**7.3 Cooperation.** Licensor shall cooperate with Licensee and provide information reasonably needed for Licensee to meet its obligations to notify homeowners, customers, regulators and other third parties. Licensor shall not notify Data Subjects or regulators of a Security Incident affecting Personal Data without Licensee's prior written approval, unless the law requires it.

**7.4 Costs.** Licensor shall reimburse Licensee for the reasonable costs of notifying affected Data Subjects and regulators, and of providing credit monitoring where it is required by law or customarily offered, to the extent the Security Incident results from Licensor's or a Subprocessor's breach of this DPA or Section 9 of the Agreement.

## 8. Assistance and Audits

**8.1 Data Subject Requests.** Licensor shall promptly forward to Licensee any request it receives from a Data Subject about Personal Data, and shall not respond to it except on Licensee's instructions. Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Licensor shall provide reasonable assistance, including through the functions of the Licensed Software, so that Licensee can respond to requests to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of Personal Data.

**8.2 Assessments.** Licensor shall provide information reasonably requested by Licensee to complete any data protection assessment that Applicable Data Protection Laws require for the Processing.

**8.3 Audit Reports.** Licensor shall provide its SOC 2 Type II report, or a substantially equivalent independent assessment, as described in Section 9.4 of the Agreement. Licensor shall also respond within thirty (30) days to Licensee's reasonable written security questionnaires, no more than once per calendar year.

**8.4 Additional Audits.** If the materials provided under Section 8.3 do not reasonably show Licensor's compliance with this DPA, or if a regulator requires it, or following a Security Incident, Licensee may audit Licensor's compliance with this DPA. Licensee shall give at least thirty (30) days' written notice, and the audit shall take place during normal business hours. Licensee may use an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations. Each Party bears its own costs of the audit.

## 9. Liability

Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in Section 12 of the Agreement.

## 10. Term, Return and Deletion

**10.1 Term.** This DPA remains in effect for as long as Licensor Processes Personal Data on behalf of Licensee.

**10.2 Return.** On expiration or termination of the Agreement, Licensor shall make Personal Data available for export in accordance with Section 7.5 of the Agreement.

**10.3 Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the export period under Section 7.5 of the Agreement, Licensor shall delete all Personal Data from its production systems and those of its Subprocessors. Personal Data in backups shall be deleted in the ordinary backup rotation, and in any event within ninety (90) days after the end of the export period. Licensor shall certify the deletion in writing on Licensee's request.

**10.4 Retained Data.** If the law requires Licensor to retain any Personal Data, Licensor shall keep it confidential, protect it in accordance with this DPA and Process it only for the purpose for which the law requires its retention.

## 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**11.1 Governing Law.** This DPA, and any dispute arising out of or relating to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in San Francisco County, California for any action arising out of or relating to this DPA or the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 12. General

**12.1 Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Licensor shall cooperate in good faith to amend this DPA where a change in Applicable Data Protection Laws reasonably requires it.

**12.2 Notices.** Notices under this DPA shall be given in accordance with Section 15.1 of the Agreement. Notices of Security Incidents shall also be sent by email to Licensee's named support contacts under Section 4.6 of the Quality Agreement.

**12.3 Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

**12.4 Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

## Annex 2: Technical and Organizational Measures

1. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
2. Role-based access controls, least-privilege access and multi-factor authentication for all Licensor personnel with access to production systems.
3. Logging and monitoring of access to production systems, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.
4. Logical separation of Licensee Data from the data of Licensor's other customers.
5. Annual third-party penetration testing and remediation of critical and high findings within thirty (30) days.
6. Vulnerability scanning at least monthly and timely patching of production systems.
7. Background checks for personnel with access to Personal Data, to the extent permitted by law.
8. Backup, business continuity and disaster recovery measures as described in Section 7 of the Quality Agreement.
9. Secure deletion of Personal Data using industry-standard methods.
