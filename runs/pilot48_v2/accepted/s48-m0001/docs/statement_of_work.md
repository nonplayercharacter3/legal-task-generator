# STATEMENT OF WORK No. 1

## Permitting Automation Module

**SOW Effective Date:** January 15, 2026

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into between **Zikeiv Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Stutind Solar Installations, LLC**, an Arizona limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). It is issued under, and is governed by, the Software License Agreement between Licensor and Licensee dated January 15, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Background and Purpose

**1.1 Background.** Licensee subscribes to the HelioPlan Design, HelioPlan Proposal, HelioPlan Permit and HelioPlan Integrations modules under Order Form No. ZS-2026-0147. Licensee prepares a high volume of residential and commercial permit packages for authorities having jurisdiction in its service area. Many of those packages are still assembled manually from HelioPlan Permit outputs.

**1.2 Purpose.** Under this SOW, Licensor will design, develop, configure and deliver a Permitting Automation Module for the Licensed Software. The module will automate permit package assembly and submission preparation for the authorities having jurisdiction listed in Schedule A (the "**Permitting Automation Module**" or the "**Module**").

## 2. Definitions

In this SOW:

"**Acceptance**" means acceptance of a Deliverable under Section 5.

"**Acceptance Criteria**" means the functional and performance criteria for a Deliverable set out in the Functional Specification approved under Milestone 1.

"**Deliverable**" means each item that Licensor is to deliver under Section 3.

"**Delivery Confirmation**" means Licensor's written notice to Licensee confirming that a Deliverable has been delivered and is available to Licensee for acceptance testing in the Licensee test environment.

"**Delivery Date**" means, for any Deliverable, the date of Licensor's written Delivery Confirmation for that Deliverable.

"**Functional Specification**" means the written specification for the Module prepared by Licensor and approved by Licensee under Milestone 1.

"**Nonconformity**" means any failure of a Deliverable to meet its Acceptance Criteria in any material respect.

"**Project Managers**" means the individuals appointed under Section 7.1.

## 3. Scope of Services and Deliverables

**3.1 Services.** Licensor shall perform the following services (the "**Services**"):

(a) **Discovery.** Run requirements workshops with Licensee's permitting, design and operations staff. Review sample permit packages from each authority having jurisdiction listed in Schedule A.

(b) **Functional Specification.** Prepare the Functional Specification. It will describe the Module's workflows, the jurisdiction-specific rule sets, the data fields drawn from HelioPlan Design and HelioPlan Proposal, the output formats and the Acceptance Criteria.

(c) **Development and Configuration.** Develop the Module in accordance with the approved Functional Specification. This includes:
   - automated assembly of plan sets, single-line diagrams, structural attachment details and equipment specification sheets;
   - jurisdiction-specific checklists and validation rules that flag missing or inconsistent information before submission;
   - automated population of permit application forms from Licensee Data; and
   - export of complete permit packages in each authority's required file format.

(d) **Integration.** Integrate the Module with HelioPlan Permit and with Licensee's CRM through the standard HelioPlan Integrations connectors.

(e) **Testing Support.** Provision a Licensee test environment and support Licensee's acceptance testing under Section 5.

(f) **Deployment and Training.** Deploy the accepted Module to production and deliver training under Section 3.3.

**3.2 Deliverables.**

| No. | Deliverable | Related Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | Functional Specification, including Acceptance Criteria | Milestone 1 |
| D-2 | Permitting Automation Module, delivered to the Licensee test environment, with release notes and administrator documentation | Milestone 2 |
| D-3 | Production deployment of the accepted Module and completion of training | Milestone 3 |

**3.3 Training.** Licensor shall deliver up to four (4) remote training sessions of up to three (3) hours each for Licensee's permitting coordinators and designers. It shall also deliver one (1) train-the-trainer session for up to ten (10) Licensee personnel. Licensor shall provide training materials that Licensee may copy for internal use.

**3.4 Out of Scope.** The following are outside the scope of this SOW unless added by a Change Order under Section 8: (a) jurisdictions not listed in Schedule A; (b) direct electronic filing with an authority's portal that does not offer a published interface; (c) engineering stamping or professional engineering review; and (d) migration of historical permit records.

## 4. Licensee Responsibilities

**4.1 Cooperation.** Licensee shall make appropriate personnel available for the discovery workshops and shall provide sample permit packages, approved plan sets and jurisdiction correspondence that Licensor reasonably requests.

**4.2 Approvals.** Licensee shall review the draft Functional Specification and provide written comments or approval within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

**4.3 Testing.** Licensee shall perform acceptance testing of Deliverable D-2 in the Licensee test environment using test cases based on the Acceptance Criteria.

**4.4 Dependencies.** If Licensee does not perform a responsibility in this Section 4 on time, and the delay prevents Licensor from performing, Licensor's affected dates are extended by the period of the delay.

## 5. Acceptance

**5.1 Functional Specification.** Deliverable D-1 is accepted when Licensee approves it in writing under Section 4.2. Licensor shall revise the draft to address Licensee's reasonable comments and resubmit it for approval.

**5.2 Acceptance.** Following Delivery Confirmation of Deliverable D-2, Licensee shall test the Module against the Acceptance Criteria. Licensee shall give Licensor written notice of any Nonconformity within ten (10) Business Days after the Delivery Date. The notice shall describe each Nonconformity in reasonable detail, including the steps needed to reproduce it. If Licensee does not give a notice of Nonconformity within that period, Deliverable D-2 is deemed accepted as of the last day of that period.

**5.3 Correction.** If Licensee gives a timely notice of Nonconformity under Section 5.2, Licensor shall correct each Nonconformity described in the notice and redeliver Deliverable D-2 with a new Delivery Confirmation. Section 5.2 then applies to the redelivered Deliverable. Licensee's retesting is limited to the corrected Nonconformities and any functions those corrections affect.

**5.4 Repeated Failure.** If Deliverable D-2 still has a Nonconformity after the second redelivery under Section 5.3, Licensee may either (a) require Licensor to continue correction under Section 5.3, or (b) terminate this SOW by written notice and receive a refund of all Fees paid under this SOW for Deliverables D-2 and D-3.

**5.5 Production Deployment.** Deliverable D-3 is accepted when the Module is deployed to the production environment and the training in Section 3.3 has been delivered, as confirmed in a written completion notice from Licensor.
