# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

**DRAFT: Licensor draft of January 6, 2026. For discussion purposes only. Subject to contract.**

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Zikeiv Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Stutind Solar Installations, LLC**, an Arizona limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor develops and operates HelioPlan, a hosted platform for solar system design, customer proposal generation and permit package preparation.

B. Licensee is a residential and commercial solar installation contractor. Licensee wishes to obtain a license to use HelioPlan in its business, and Licensor is willing to grant that license on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

"**Authorized User**" means an employee or individual contractor of Licensee who holds an installer seat purchased under the Order Form.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a federal holiday in the United States.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties that governs Licensor's Processing of Personal Data on behalf of Licensee.

"**Documentation**" means Licensor's user guides, technical specifications and online help materials for the Licensed Software, as updated from time to time.

"**Fees**" means the subscription fees, professional services fees and other amounts payable under the Order Form or any SOW.

"**Licensed Software**" means the HelioPlan platform, including its design, proposal and permitting modules, any modules or deliverables provided under an SOW, and all Updates, in each case as hosted and made available by Licensor.

"**Licensee Data**" means all data and content submitted to or generated in the Licensed Software by or for Licensee or its Authorized Users, including customer records, site and roof data, system designs, sales pipeline data, proposal pricing and permit documents.

"**Order Form**" means the order form executed by the Parties that references this Agreement and specifies the subscription, seats, Fees and Subscription Term.

"**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the quality and service-level agreement between the Parties that sets out availability, support and service-credit commitments for the Licensed Software.

"**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties that describes professional services or deliverables to be provided by Licensor.

"**Subscription Term**" means the initial subscription term stated in the Order Form and any renewal term under Section 10.2.

"**Updates**" means bug fixes, patches, maintenance releases and new versions of the Licensed Software that Licensor makes generally available to its customers at no additional charge.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Order Form, the DPA, the Quality Agreement and each SOW are intended to be read together. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, the following order of precedence applies: Order Form, then DPA (as to Personal Data), then License Agreement, then SOW and other exhibits.

## 2. ORDER FORM AND SCOPE

**2.1 Order Form.** Licensee's subscription to the Licensed Software is set out in the Order Form. The Order Form is governed by this Agreement.

**2.2 Additional Seats.** Licensee may add installer seats during the Subscription Term by written request. Additional seats are charged at the per-seat rate stated in the Order Form, prorated for the remainder of the then-current subscription year.

**2.3 Affiliates.** Licensee's Affiliates may use the Licensed Software through seats purchased by Licensee. Licensee is responsible for each Affiliate's compliance with this Agreement.

**3.1 License.** Subject to this Agreement and payment of the Fees, Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted by Section 15.3), non-sublicensable license during the Subscription Term for Authorized Users to access and use the Licensed Software and Documentation for Licensee's internal business purposes.

**3.2 Restrictions.** Licensee shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Licensed Software; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Licensed Software, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits it; (c) sell, rent, lease or provide the Licensed Software on a service-bureau basis to third parties; (d) share login credentials among individuals; or (e) use the Licensed Software to build a competing product.

**3.3 Reservation of Rights.** Licensor and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in the Licensed Software and Documentation. No rights are granted except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

**3.4 Exclusivity.** In consideration of the pricing granted in the Order Form, during the Subscription Term Licensee shall not license or procure from any third party residential rooftop solar design software for use on residential rooftop installations located in Arizona or Nevada. This Section 3.4 does not apply to Licensee's commercial, utility-scale or other business lines, to permitting or proposal software, or to installations located outside Arizona and Nevada.

**3.5 Third-Party Components.** The Licensed Software may include third-party components, which are licensed to Licensee as part of the Licensed Software under this Agreement.

## 4. HOSTING, SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

**4.1 Hosting.** Licensor shall host the Licensed Software and Licensee Data in data centers located in the United States operated by Licensor or its hosting subprocessors.

**4.2 Support and Availability.** Licensor shall provide technical support and meet the availability commitments set out in the Quality Agreement. Service credits under the Quality Agreement are Licensee's sole monetary remedy for availability failures, without prejudice to Licensee's termination rights under Section 10.

**4.3 Updates.** Licensor shall make all Updates available to Licensee at no additional charge. Licensor shall not materially reduce the core functionality of the Licensed Software during the Subscription Term.

**4.4 Permitting Integrations.** Where the Licensed Software interfaces with utility interconnection portals or authority-having-jurisdiction permitting systems, Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain those integrations, but Licensor is not responsible for changes made by utilities or permitting authorities to their own systems.

## 5. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

**5.1 Statements of Work.** Licensor shall perform implementation, configuration, development and training services as described in each SOW. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement.

**5.2 Personnel.** Licensor shall assign suitably qualified personnel to perform the services and may replace them on reasonable notice to Licensee.

**5.3 Acceptance.** Deliverables under an SOW are subject to the acceptance procedure set out in that SOW.

## 6. FEES AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Fees.** Licensee shall pay the Fees set out in the Order Form and each SOW.

**6.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Unless the Order Form or SOW provides otherwise, subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance and professional services Fees are invoiced on achievement of the applicable milestone. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**6.3 Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee shall pay those taxes, other than taxes based on Licensor's net income, property or employees.

**6.4 Disputed Invoices.** Licensee may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Licensor in writing before the due date and describing the basis for the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**6.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law. Licensor may suspend access to the Licensed Software if undisputed amounts remain unpaid more than thirty (30) days after Licensee receives written notice of non-payment.

**7.1 Ownership of Licensee Data.** As between the Parties, Licensee owns all Licensee Data. Licensee grants Licensor a non-exclusive license to host, process and use Licensee Data solely to provide the Licensed Software and services to Licensee and as otherwise permitted by the DPA.

**7.2 Aggregated Data.** Licensor may compile aggregated and de-identified statistics on use of the Licensed Software. These statistics must not identify Licensee, its customers or any individual, and must not disclose Licensee's pricing.

**7.3 Deliverables.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. Licensor owns all modifications and enhancements to the Licensed Software, including any developed under an SOW. Licensee receives a license to those modifications and enhancements as part of the Licensed Software under Section 3.1.

**7.4 Feedback.** Licensor may use any suggestions or feedback Licensee provides about the Licensed Software without obligation to Licensee.

**7.5 Return of Data.** Within thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, Licensor shall make Licensee Data available for export in a commonly used machine-readable format. Licensor shall then delete Licensee Data in accordance with the DPA.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information that one Party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other (the "**Recipient**") and that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Licensee Data is Licensee's Confidential Information. The Licensed Software and Documentation are Licensor's Confidential Information.

**8.2 Obligations.** The Recipient shall: (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 8; and (c) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care.

**8.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without breach of any duty; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

**8.4 Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law. To the extent legally permitted, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

**8.5 Duration.** These obligations survive for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement. Obligations relating to trade secrets and Licensee Data survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret or is held by the Recipient.

## 9. DATA SECURITY

**9.1 Security Program.** Licensor shall maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Licensee Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration and destruction. The program shall be consistent with industry standards, including encryption of Licensee Data in transit and at rest.

**9.2 Personal Data.** Licensor shall Process Personal Data contained in Licensee Data only in accordance with the DPA.

**9.3 Security Incidents.** Licensor shall notify Licensee without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Licensee Data. Licensor shall promptly investigate and remediate the incident and provide Licensee with information reasonably needed to meet its legal notification obligations to homeowners, customers and regulators.

**9.4 Audits.** Licensor shall maintain a SOC 2 Type II report, or a substantially equivalent independent assessment, covering the Licensed Software. Licensor shall provide a copy to Licensee on request no more than once per calendar year.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

**10.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the Subscription Term, unless terminated earlier under this Section 10. The initial Subscription Term is stated in the Order Form.

**10.2 Renewal.** The Subscription Term renews automatically for successive renewal terms of 12 months each unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

**10.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**10.4 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**10.5 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination: (a) the licenses granted under this Agreement end; (b) Licensee shall pay all undisputed Fees accrued through the effective date of termination; and (c) if Licensee terminates under Section 10.3 or Section 15.3, Licensor shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after the effective date of termination.

**10.6 Survival.** Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.5, 11, 12, 15 and 16, and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive, survive expiration or termination.
