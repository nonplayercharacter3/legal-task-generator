## 2. Subscription Term

**2.1 Subscription Start Date.** The Subscription Term begins on February 1, 2026 (the "**Subscription Start Date**").

**2.2 Initial Term.** The initial Subscription Term is thirty-six (36) months from the Subscription Start Date. It ends on January 31, 2029 (the "**Initial Term**").

**2.3 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, the Subscription Term renews automatically for successive thirty-six (36) month renewal terms (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Fees for each Renewal Term are charged at Licensor's then-current list price for the Licensed Software and the number of installer seats in effect at the end of the preceding term.

**2.4 Notice of Non-Renewal.** Either Party may prevent renewal by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal under Section 15.1 of the Agreement. A notice of non-renewal is effective only if it is received no earlier than 120 days and no later than ninety (90) days before the last day of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

## 3. Fees

**3.1 Subscription Fee.** The annual subscription fee is $312,000 for up to 180 installer seats (the "**Annual Subscription Fee**").

**3.2 Fee Schedule (Initial Term).**

| Subscription Year | Period | Installer Seats | Annual Subscription Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | February 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027 | Up to 180 | $312,000 |
| Year 2 | February 1, 2027 – January 31, 2028 | Up to 180 | $312,000 |
| Year 3 | February 1, 2028 – January 31, 2029 | Up to 180 | $312,000 |

**3.3 Additional Seats.** Installer seats above 180 may be added under Section 2.2 of the Agreement at a rate of $1,733.33 per seat per subscription year, prorated for the remainder of the then-current subscription year. Additional seats are co-terminous with the Subscription Term.

**3.4 Included Services.** The Annual Subscription Fee includes hosting, Updates, and standard support and availability commitments under the Quality Agreement. Implementation, configuration and development services, including the Permitting Automation Module, are provided and charged under a separate SOW.
