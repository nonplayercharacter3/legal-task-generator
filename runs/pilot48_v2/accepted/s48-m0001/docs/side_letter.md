## 1. Subscription Credit

**1.1 Credit.** Licensor grants Licensee a one-time credit of $31,200 (the "**Subscription Credit**"). Licensor shall apply the Subscription Credit against the invoice for the Annual Subscription Fee for Year 2 (February 1, 2027 to January 31, 2028) issued under Section 6.1 of the Order Form.

**1.2 Additional Seats.** For any installer seats above 180 added under Section 2.2 of the Agreement between the date of this Letter Agreement and January 31, 2027, Licensor shall waive the prorated per-seat charge under Section 3.3 of the Order Form for the remainder of Year 1. Seats added in this way are charged at the rate in Section 3.3 of the Order Form from February 1, 2027.

**1.3 No Other Change to Fees.** Except as stated in this Paragraph 1, the Annual Subscription Fee of $312,000 for up to 180 installer seats and the other Fees under the Order Form and each SOW remain unchanged. The Subscription Credit is not refundable, cannot be redeemed for cash, and does not carry over to any Renewal Term.

## 2. Amendment to Section 12.2

With effect from the date of this Letter Agreement, Section 12.2 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **12.2 Exclusions from Limitation.** The limitation in Section 12.1 and the exclusion in Section 12.3 do not apply to Licensor's obligations under Section 11.1 (Infringement Indemnity).

## 3. Reference Customer

**3.1 Case Study.** Licensee agrees to take part in one written case study describing its use of the Licensed Software for permit package preparation. Licensor shall prepare the draft. **3.2 Reference Calls.** Licensee agrees to take up to four (4) reference calls with prospective Licensor customers between the date of this Letter Agreement and January 31, 2027, at mutually convenient times. Licensee is not required to disclose its pricing, Licensee Data or other Confidential Information on any reference call.
