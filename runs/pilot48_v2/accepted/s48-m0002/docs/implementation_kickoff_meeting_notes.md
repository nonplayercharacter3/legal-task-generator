## 2. Purpose

2.1 This meeting is the kickoff meeting under Section 4.1 of the Software License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). It took place within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date. Its purpose was to agree the implementation plan for configuration, data migration and administrator training.

2.2 Capitalized terms in these notes have the meanings given in the Agreement. These notes record the operational discussion only. They do not amend the Agreement, the Service Level Schedule, the Quality Agreement or the Side Letter.

| Workstream | Owner | Target Date |

3.3 Beipeiv confirmed that implementation services under Section 4.1 of the Agreement are at no additional charge, as provided in Section 5.2.

## 4. Data Migration and Data Quality

4.1 Data Quality Contacts were designated by both parties by notice on March 12, 2026, as required by Section 2.1 of the Quality Agreement. Baineind's contact is the Director of Warehouse Operations. Beipeiv's contact is the Implementation Manager.

4.2 Beipeiv walked through the validation rules in Section 3.1 of the Quality Agreement. Baineind will send its list of mandatory fields for inventory items, quotes and Rental Orders by March 20, 2026. Override permissions will be limited initially to the Chief Operating Officer, the Director of Warehouse Operations and the Dispatch Manager.

4.3 Beipeiv confirmed it will run the validation rules against the migration file before loading. It will provide an exception report under Section 3.4 of the Quality Agreement and a migration reconciliation report under Section 6.1.

4.4 Baineind will complete a physical count at each warehouse location before go-live, so that the first quarterly count under Section 4.1 of the Quality Agreement starts from a reconciled baseline.

## 5. Support and Service Levels

5.1 The Beipeiv Support Manager reviewed incident reporting under Section 4.1 of the Service Level Schedule. Baineind will receive the support hotline number, portal credentials and escalation contacts under Section 6.2 of the Service Level Schedule before go-live.

5.2 Beipeiv confirmed that the named Peak Season support engineer under Section 2.1 of the Side Letter will be assigned before April 1, 2026. Beipeiv will introduce the engineer to Baineind's Dispatch Manager during the week of March 23.

5.3 Baineind asked that Beipeiv's status page and monthly service level reports go to the IT Manager and the Chief Operating Officer. Beipeiv agreed.

5.4 Beipeiv will give notice of scheduled maintenance in accordance with Section 4.5 of the Agreement. Baineind reminded Beipeiv of the Peak Season weekend and pre-holiday restriction in Section 2.3 of the Side Letter. Beipeiv confirmed that its maintenance calendar reflects it.
