# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Software License Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Beipeiv Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Baineind Event Rentals, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office in Wilmington, Delaware ("**Licensee**"). Licensor and Licensee are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Licensor operates a cloud-hosted rental-management platform. The platform supports inventory management, quoting, rental order processing, delivery dispatch and warehouse pick-list generation.

B. Licensee is in the business of renting event furniture, decor, tenting, staging and related equipment to event customers and venues, and wishes to use the platform to operate that business.

C. The parties wish to set out the terms on which Licensor will make the platform available to Licensee.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION, HOSTING AND SUPPORT

4.1 **Implementation.** Licensor shall configure the Platform for Licensee, migrate Licensee's existing inventory and customer records, and train Licensee's designated administrators. The parties shall agree an implementation plan at a kickoff meeting held within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date.

4.2 **Hosting.** Licensor shall host the Platform in commercially reasonable data centers located in the United States and shall keep the Platform available in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

4.3 **Support.** Licensor shall provide technical support to Licensee by telephone, email and online portal. Licensor shall classify, respond to and resolve incidents, including Severity 1 Incidents, in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

4.4 **Service Credits.** If Licensor fails to meet the commitments in the Service Level Schedule, Licensee will be entitled to the service credits set out in that schedule.

4.5 **Updates.** Licensor may update the Platform from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the core functionality of the inventory, quoting, Rental Order, delivery dispatch or warehouse pick-list modules. Licensor shall give Licensee at least seven (7) days' notice of scheduled maintenance and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance outside Licensee's peak operating hours.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **License Fees.** Licensee shall pay Licensor an annual license fee of $186,000 per year. Licensee shall pay the License Fees annually in advance, with the first payment due within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date and each later payment due on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

5.2 **Implementation Fees.** Licensor shall perform the implementation services described in Section 4.1 at no additional charge.

5.3 **Additional Services.** Any training, custom development or professional services beyond those described in this Agreement will be provided under a written order signed by both parties, at Licensor's then-current rates unless otherwise agreed.

5.4 **Fee Adjustments.** After the Initial Term, Licensor may increase the License Fees for any Renewal Term by giving written notice at least ninety (90) days before that Renewal Term begins. No increase may exceed five percent (5%) over the License Fees for the immediately preceding year.

## 6. PAYMENT TERMS AND TAXES

6.1 **Invoicing.** Licensor shall invoice Licensee for the Fees, and Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

6.2 **Disputed Amounts.** Licensee may withhold any amount that it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Licensor of the dispute in writing before the due date. The parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute.

6.3 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts that are not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

6.4 **Taxes.** The Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Licensee is responsible for those taxes, except for taxes based on Licensor's net income, property or employees.
