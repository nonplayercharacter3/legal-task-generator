**1. Purpose of this Notice**

We write on behalf of Baineind Event Rentals, LLC ("**Baineind**") under the Software License Agreement between Beipeiv Software, Inc. ("**Beipeiv**") and Baineind, entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. This letter is delivered as a notice under Section 20.3 (Notices) of the Agreement.

**2. The Transaction**

Baineind's sole member, Ostrander Holdings LLC ("**Ostrander**"), has entered into a definitive agreement to sell 100% of the membership interests in Baineind to Kestrel Venue Group, Inc. ("**Kestrel Venue Group**"). The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions. Closing is currently expected on December 15, 2026. After closing, Baineind will be a wholly owned subsidiary of Kestrel Venue Group.

**3. Continuity of the Relationship**

The transaction is structured as a sale of equity. Baineind will remain the same legal entity and will continue as the Licensee under the Agreement. Baineind will continue to operate its event furniture, decor, tenting and staging rental business from its existing warehouse, showroom and office locations. It expects no change to its use of the Platform, its Authorized Users or the payment of License Fees in accordance with Section 5.1. This letter is provided for Beipeiv's information, and no action is required by Beipeiv in connection with it.

**4. Implementation and Support Contacts**

Baineind's day-to-day contacts for implementation, support and Quality Agreement matters will remain the same through closing. We will let your account team know of any changes to Baineind's designated administrators or notice contacts after closing, in accordance with Section 20.3 of the Agreement.

**5. Confidentiality**

The information in this letter about the transaction is not public. Baineind provides it as Confidential Information under Section 10 of the Agreement. Please limit its disclosure to those of your personnel who need to know it.
