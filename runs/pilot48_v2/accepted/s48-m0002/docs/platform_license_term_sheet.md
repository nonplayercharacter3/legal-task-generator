# PLATFORM LICENSE TERM SHEET

**Beipeiv Software, Inc. / Baineind Event Rentals, LLC**

**Cloud Rental-Management Platform License**

**Date:** January 23, 2026

## 1. Purpose and Status

1.1 This term sheet summarizes the principal terms on which Beipeiv Software, Inc. ("**Licensor**") proposes to license its cloud-hosted rental-management platform to Baineind Event Rentals, LLC ("**Licensee**").

1.2 This term sheet is a non-binding expression of the parties' current intentions. It does not create any legally binding obligation, except for Sections 11 (Confidentiality), 12 (Costs) and 13 (Governing Law) of this term sheet. A binding obligation to license the platform will arise only when the parties sign a definitive Software License Agreement (the "**Agreement**") and its related documents.

## 2. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Licensor | Beipeiv Software, Inc., a Delaware corporation |
| Licensee | Baineind Event Rentals, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware |

## 3. Platform and Scope

3.1 **Platform.** Licensor's cloud-hosted rental-management software, including the inventory, quoting, Rental Order, delivery dispatch and warehouse pick-list modules, any mobile applications, and all updates and upgrades that Licensor makes generally available (the "**Platform**").

3.2 **Licensed Use.** A non-exclusive, non-transferable right for Licensee, its Affiliates and their Authorized Users to access and use the Platform for Licensee's internal business purposes.

3.3 **Authorized Users.** An unlimited number of Authorized Users across all Licensee warehouse, showroom and office locations.

3.4 **Hosting.** The Platform will be hosted in commercially reasonable data centers located in the United States.

## 4. Commercial Terms

4.1 **Effective Date.** Targeted for March 1, 2026.

4.2 **Annual License Fee.** $186,000 per year, payable annually in advance. The first payment will be due within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date.

4.3 **Implementation.** Configuration, migration of existing inventory and customer records, and administrator training will be included at no additional charge. The parties will hold a kickoff meeting within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date to agree an implementation plan.

4.4 **Payment Terms.** Net thirty (30) days from receipt of invoice. Licensee may withhold amounts disputed in good faith.

4.5 **Renewal Pricing.** Any increase in the annual license fee for a renewal year may not exceed five percent (5%) over the prior year's fee and requires at least ninety (90) days' notice.

4.6 **Peak-Season Terms.** The parties will document peak-season support coverage and fixed rates for additional services in a separate letter agreement to be signed with the Agreement.

## 5. Term

5.1 **Initial Term.** 3 years from the Effective Date.

5.2 **Renewal.** Automatic renewal for successive one (1) year periods, unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 6. Service Levels and Support

6.1 **Service Level Schedule.** Platform availability commitments, incident severity levels, response and resolution times and service credits will be set out in a Service Level Schedule to the Agreement.

6.2 **Severity 1 Incident.** "Severity 1 Incident" will mean any failure of the Platform preventing Licensee from creating, modifying or fulfilling Rental Orders at any Licensee location. Licensee's event-day operations depend on order, dispatch and pick-list functions being available at each warehouse.

6.3 **Severity 1 Response Time.** 1 hour, 24x7.

6.4 **Lower Severity Levels.** Severity 2 and lower severity levels, with corresponding response and resolution times, will be set out in the Service Level Schedule.

6.5 **Service Credits.** Service credits will be payable for failures to meet the availability and Severity 1 response commitments, in amounts to be set out in the Service Level Schedule.

6.6 **Maintenance.** At least seven (7) days' notice of scheduled maintenance. Licensor will use commercially reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance outside Licensee's peak operating hours.

## 7. Data

7.1 **Ownership.** Licensee will own all Licensee Data. Licensor will use Licensee Data solely to provide the Platform and support.

7.2 **Data Accuracy.** The parties will enter into a Quality Agreement setting out data validation, inventory reconciliation and error-correction procedures for inventory and Rental Order data. Licensor will correct, at no additional charge, data inaccuracies caused by Platform defects.

7.3 **Security.** Industry-standard safeguards; breach notification within seventy-two (72) hours; annual SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent.

7.4 **Exit.** Licensee Data will be available for export in a standard, machine-readable format for sixty (60) days after expiration or termination.

## 8. Risk Allocation

8.1 **Warranties.** Licensor will warrant that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with its documentation and that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

8.2 **Licensor Indemnity.** Licensor will defend and indemnify Licensee against third-party claims that the Platform infringes United States intellectual property rights, subject to customary exclusions.

8.3 **Licensee Indemnity.** Licensee will indemnify Licensor against third-party claims arising from Licensee Data and Licensee's use of the Platform in breach of the Agreement's use restrictions.

8.4 **Limitation of Liability.** Each party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim. Customary exclusions from the cap and from the waiver of consequential damages will be addressed in the Agreement.

8.5 **Insurance.** Licensor will maintain commercial general liability, technology errors and omissions and cyber liability insurance, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, and will provide a certificate of insurance on request.

## 9. Termination

9.1 **For Cause.** Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after notice, or for the other party's insolvency.

9.2 **Transition Assistance.** Up to ninety (90) days of transition assistance at Licensor's then-current professional services rates.

## 10. Assignment

10.1 Neither party may assign the Agreement without the other party's consent, subject to the exceptions and change of control provisions to be set out in the Agreement. Licensor may assign to a successor to its Platform business that assumes Licensor's obligations in writing.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 The existence and terms of this term sheet are confidential. Neither party will disclose them to any third party other than its employees, advisors and financing sources who need to know them and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## 12. Costs

12.1 Each party will bear its own costs in negotiating this term sheet and the Agreement.

## 13. Governing Law and Forum

13.1 This term sheet is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

13.2 The Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The parties will submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

## 14. Next Steps

14.1 Licensor's counsel will prepare first drafts of the Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and the Quality Agreement. The parties will aim to sign by February 27, 2026, for an Effective Date of March 1, 2026.
