# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**to the Software License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 between Beipeiv Software, Inc. and Baineind Event Rentals, LLC**

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between **Beipeiv Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Licensor**"), and **Baineind Event Rentals, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Licensee**"). It is the "Service Level Schedule" referred to in the Software License Agreement between the parties entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. SCOPE AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule sets out Licensor's Platform availability commitment, the severity levels for incidents affecting the Platform, Licensor's response and resolution times, and the service credits available to Licensee under Section 4.4 of the Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. In this Schedule:

(a) "**Business Hours**" means 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding United States federal holidays.

(b) "**Incident**" means any failure, error or degradation of the Platform that Licensee reports to Licensor, or that Licensor detects, under Section 4.

(c) "**Monthly Fee**" means one-twelfth (1/12) of the annual License Fees under Section 5.1 of the Agreement, which equals $15,500 based on License Fees of $186,000 per year.

(d) "**Resolution**" means that Licensor has restored the affected functionality, either by a permanent fix or by a workaround that Licensee reasonably accepts. "**Resolve**" has a correlative meaning.

(e) "**Response**" means that a qualified Licensor support engineer has acknowledged the Incident to Licensee by telephone or through the online support portal and has started work on it. "**Respond**" has a correlative meaning.

(f) "**Scheduled Maintenance**" means maintenance of the Platform for which Licensor has given at least seven (7) days' notice in accordance with Section 4.5 of the Agreement.

## 2. INCIDENT SEVERITY LEVELS

2.1 **Severity 1 Incident.** For purposes of this Schedule, "**Severity 1 Incident**" means the complete unavailability of the Platform to all Authorized Users at all Licensee locations, other than during Scheduled Maintenance.

2.2 **Severity 2 Incident.** "**Severity 2 Incident**" means any Incident, other than a Severity 1 Incident, in which a module or core function of the Platform fails or is materially degraded for some or all Authorized Users at one or more Licensee locations. This includes failures of the inventory, quoting, Rental Order, delivery dispatch or warehouse pick-list modules or of the mobile applications.

2.3 **Severity 3 Incident.** "**Severity 3 Incident**" means any Incident that causes a non-critical function of the Platform to fail or perform incorrectly, where a reasonable workaround is available and Licensee's operations are not materially affected.

2.4 **Severity 4 Incident.** "**Severity 4 Incident**" means a cosmetic defect, a request for information about the Platform or the Documentation, or a request for an enhancement.

2.5 **Classification.** Licensee shall propose a severity level when it reports an Incident. Licensor shall classify each Incident in accordance with the definitions in this Section 2, acting reasonably and taking Licensee's proposal into account. If Licensee disagrees with a classification, Licensee may escalate under Section 6, and Licensor shall continue to work on the Incident at the severity level Licensee proposed until the disagreement is resolved.

## 3. RESPONSE AND RESOLUTION TIMES

3.1 **Targets.** Licensor shall Respond to and Resolve Incidents within the following times, measured from the time Licensee reports the Incident under Section 4.1 or, if earlier, the time Licensor detects it:

| Severity Level | Response Time | Resolution Target | Support Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 Incident | 1 hour, 24x7 | 4 hours | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week |
| Severity 2 Incident | 4 Business Hours | 2 business days | Business Hours |
| Severity 3 Incident | 1 business day | 10 business days | Business Hours |
| Severity 4 Incident | 3 business days | Next scheduled release, at Licensor's discretion | Business Hours |

3.2 **Continuous Effort.** For each Severity 1 Incident, Licensor shall work continuously until Resolution and shall give Licensee status updates at least every hour by telephone or through the online support portal. For each Severity 2 Incident, Licensor shall give Licensee status updates at least once each business day until Resolution.

3.3 **Root-Cause Analysis.** Within five (5) business days after Resolving a Severity 1 Incident, Licensor shall give Licensee a written root-cause analysis describing the cause of the Incident, the corrective action taken and the steps Licensor will take to prevent recurrence.

3.4 **Resolution Targets.** The Resolution targets in Section 3.1 are targets. Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet them. Service credits are payable only as set out in Section 5.

## 4. INCIDENT REPORTING AND MONITORING

4.1 **Reporting.** Licensee shall report Severity 1 Incidents by telephone to Licensor's support hotline, followed by a ticket in the online support portal. Licensee may report all other Incidents through the online support portal or by email to Licensor's support desk. Each report should describe the Incident, the affected locations and modules, and the proposed severity level.

4.2 **Monitoring.** Licensor shall monitor the Platform continuously by automated means. If Licensor detects an Incident before Licensee reports it, Licensor shall notify Licensee's designated administrators within the applicable Response time.

4.3 **Status Page.** Licensor shall maintain an online status page showing the current availability of the Platform and any open Incidents that affect Licensee.

## 5. AVAILABILITY AND SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 **Availability Commitment.** Licensor shall make the Platform available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month ("**Monthly Availability**").

5.2 **Calculation.** Monthly Availability is calculated as follows:

Monthly Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes in the Month − Downtime Minutes) ÷ Total Minutes in the Month) × 100

"**Downtime Minutes**" means the total number of minutes in the month during which a Severity 1 Incident is continuing, measured from the start of the Incident until Resolution, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and periods excluded under Section 5.6.

5.3 **Availability Credits.** If Monthly Availability falls below 99.9% in any calendar month, Licensor shall issue a service credit to Licensee as follows:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but at least 99.5% | 5% |
| Less than 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 99.0% but at least 98.0% | 15% |
| Less than 98.0% | 25% |

5.4 **Response Credits.** For each Severity 1 Incident for which Licensor does not meet the Response time in Section 3.1, Licensor shall issue a service credit equal to 5% of the Monthly Fee.

5.5 **Maximum Monthly Credit.** The total service credits for any calendar month under Sections 5.3 and 5.4 shall not exceed 30% of the Monthly Fee.

5.6 **Exclusions.** Downtime Minutes and Response failures do not include any period of unavailability, and no service credit is payable, to the extent caused by (a) Scheduled Maintenance; (b) emergency maintenance needed to address a critical security vulnerability, provided that Licensor gives Licensee as much notice as is reasonably practicable; (c) Licensee's or any Authorized User's breach of the Agreement or use of the Platform other than in accordance with the Documentation; (d) failures of Licensee's own networks, devices or internet service; or (e) events described in Section 19.1 of the Agreement, other than any failure of Licensor's disaster recovery or business continuity measures.

5.7 **Claiming Credits.** Licensee shall request service credits by notice through the online support portal within thirty (30) days after the end of the month in which the failure occurred. Licensor shall apply each credit against the next invoice for License Fees. If no further License Fees are payable under the Agreement, Licensor shall pay the credit to Licensee within thirty (30) days.

5.8 **Liability.** In accordance with Section 13.4 of the Agreement, service credits issued under this Schedule count toward Licensor's liability under Section 13.1 of the Agreement.

## 6. ESCALATION

6.1 **Escalation Path.** If an Incident is not Responded to or Resolved within the times in Section 3.1, or if Licensee disagrees with Licensor's classification of an Incident, Licensee may escalate as follows:

| Level | Licensor Contact | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Support Manager | At any time after the Response time is missed |
| 2 | Director of Customer Operations | If the Incident is not Resolved within the Resolution target |
| 3 | Chief Technology Officer | If the Incident is not Resolved within twice the Resolution target |

6.2 **Contacts.** Licensor shall provide Licensee with current names, telephone numbers and email addresses for each escalation contact and shall update them promptly when they change.

## 7. REPORTING AND REVIEW

7.1 **Monthly Report.** Within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month, Licensor shall make available to Licensee a service level report showing (a) Monthly Availability, (b) each Incident reported or detected, with its severity level, Response time and Resolution time, (c) any service credits earned, and (d) Scheduled Maintenance performed during the month.

7.2 **Quarterly Review.** The parties' account representatives shall meet at least once each calendar quarter to review service level performance, open Incidents and any recurring issues.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Term.** This Schedule takes effect on March 1, 2026 and remains in effect for the Term of the Agreement.

8.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

8.3 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
