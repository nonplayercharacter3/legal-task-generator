This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into in connection with the Software License Agreement between Beipeiv Software, Inc. ("**Licensor**") and Baineind Event Rentals, LLC ("**Licensee**"), entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

During our negotiations, Licensee explained that its rental volumes are concentrated in the spring and autumn event seasons and around the year-end holidays. During those periods Licensee needs additional support coverage and services on short notice. The parties have therefore agreed the following pricing and service terms.

## 1. Peak Seasons

1.1 For purposes of this Side Letter, each of the following is a "**Peak Season**" in each contract year:

(a) April 1 through June 30;

(b) September 1 through October 31; and

(c) November 15 through December 31.

1.2 Licensee may, by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before the start of a contract year, move any Peak Season by up to fourteen (14) days. A Peak Season may not be lengthened under this Section 1.2, and Peak Seasons may not overlap.

## 2. Peak-Season Support Coverage

2.1 During each Peak Season, Licensor shall provide Licensee with a named support engineer who is familiar with Licensee's configuration of the Platform. The support engineer will be available by telephone and the online portal from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Eastern Time, seven days a week. This coverage supplements, and does not replace, the support described in Section 4.3 of the Agreement.

2.2 Licensor shall provide the coverage in Section 2.1 at no charge in addition to the License Fees.

2.3 Licensor shall not schedule non-emergency maintenance of the Platform during any Peak Season on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday, or on the day before a United States federal holiday.

## 3. Peak-Season Rates for Additional Services

3.1 Licensor shall provide the following Additional Services under Section 5.3 of the Agreement at the following fixed rates when Licensee orders them for performance during a Peak Season:

| Service | Peak-Season Rate |
|---|---|
| Remote configuration changes (new locations, warehouses, delivery zones or pricing rules) | $165 per hour |
| Remote training sessions for seasonal Authorized Users (up to 15 attendees per session) | $450 per session |
| On-site event-day support at a Licensee warehouse (minimum 8 hours, plus reasonable pre-approved travel expenses) | $1,600 per day |
| Expedited bulk import of seasonal inventory records | $2,250 per import |

3.2 These rates are fixed for the Initial Term. Licensor shall start each Additional Service ordered under this Section 3 within three (3) business days after receiving a written order signed by both parties. For on-site event-day support, Licensor shall start within ten (10) business days.

3.3 Outside the Peak Seasons, Additional Services are charged at Licensor's then-current rates under Section 5.3 of the Agreement. However, those rates may not exceed the rates in Section 3.1 by more than ten percent (10%).

## 4. Seasonal Authorized Users

4.1 Licensee may designate seasonal staff, temporary warehouse crews and event-day contractors as Authorized Users during any Peak Season without additional charge. Licensee shall deactivate their credentials within fifteen (15) days after the end of the relevant Peak Season, unless they continue to work for Licensee.

## 5. License Fees

5.1 Nothing in this Side Letter changes the License Fees of $186,000 per year, or the timing of their payment, under Section 5.1 of the Agreement.
