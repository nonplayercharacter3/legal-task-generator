# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**NDA**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Bruben Elevator Components, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 4100 Foundry Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Bruben**"); and

**Zeisein Lift Services, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Commerce Drive, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Zeisein**").

Bruben and Zeisein are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Parties wish to discuss a possible commercial relationship. Under that relationship, Bruben would supply elevator components to Zeisein, including hydraulic power units, valve units and controller boards for Zeisein's elevator maintenance and modernization business (the "**Purpose**").

B. In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose confidential information to the other. The Parties are entering into this NDA to protect that information.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that a Party or its Affiliates (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to the other Party or its Affiliates (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date and whether in written, oral, electronic, visual or other form. It includes:

(a) pricing, cost data, margins, discounts and rebate terms;

(b) product specifications, drawings, wiring diagrams, firmware, test data and manufacturing processes;

(c) forecasts, volume estimates, program plans and bid information;

(d) customer, building owner and site information, including the identity and location of elevators maintained or modernized by Zeisein; and

(e) business plans, financial information and supplier information.

Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it is identified as confidential when disclosed, or if a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, lenders and professional advisors (including attorneys and accountants).

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

2.2 **Nondisclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any person, except to its Representatives who:

(a) need to know it for the Purpose; and

(b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations, or professional duties of confidentiality, at least as protective as those in this NDA.

The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this NDA by its Representatives.

2.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly after it becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to regain possession of the information and prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

2.5 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, disassemble or decompile any prototype, sample, software or other item that embodies the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, except as the Disclosing Party authorizes in writing.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

3.1 **Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party, without restriction, before the Disclosing Party disclosed it;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party that is not under a duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena or court order. Where lawful, the Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably, at the Disclosing Party's expense, and shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

3.3 **Whistleblower Immunity.** Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 4. OWNERSHIP AND NO WARRANTY

4.1 **Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. This NDA does not grant the Receiving Party any license or other right in the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, patents, copyrights, trade secrets or other intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

4.2 **No Warranty.** Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. The Disclosing Party has no liability arising from the Receiving Party's use of Confidential Information, except as stated in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

## 5. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

5.1 This NDA does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction, or to disclose any particular information. Either Party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other Party.

5.2 Each Party may continue to deal with third parties, including competitors of the other Party, so long as it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this NDA.

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

6.1 Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession and, on request, certify the return or destruction in writing. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday in the United States.

6.2 The Receiving Party may keep (a) copies required to be kept by law, regulation or its bona fide document retention policies, and (b) copies stored in automatic electronic back-up systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this NDA for as long as it is retained.

## 7. TERM

7.1 **Term.** This NDA begins on the NDA Effective Date and continues for three (3) years, unless either Party terminates it earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

7.2 **Survival.** The Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 continue for three (3) years after this NDA expires or terminates. For any Confidential Information that is a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. REMEDIES

8.1 Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which money damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party may therefore seek injunctive relief or specific performance, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.
