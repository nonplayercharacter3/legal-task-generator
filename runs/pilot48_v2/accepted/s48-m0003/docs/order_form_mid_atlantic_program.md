# ORDER FORM NO. 2026-01
## Mid-Atlantic Hydraulic Modernization Program

This Order Form No. 2026-01 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by Bruben Elevator Components, LLC ("**Supplier**") and Zeisein Lift Services, Inc. ("**Customer**"). It is issued under, and forms part of, the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. PROGRAM AND PRODUCTS

1.1 **Program.** This Order Form covers Customer's purchases of Products for the modernization of hydraulic passenger and freight elevators that Customer maintains or modernizes for building owners and managers (the "**Program**"). Each building in the Program Territory where Customer installs Products under this Order Form is a "**Program Site**."

1.2 **Program Territory.** The Program Territory is Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware (the "**Program Territory**").

1.3 **Products.** The Products under this Order Form are the hydraulic power units, valve units and controller boards listed in the table below. All Products must be new.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** The Initial Term of the Agreement and of this Order Form is 36 months from the Supply Agreement Effective Date.

2.2 **Renewal.** This Order Form renews with the Agreement under Section 2.2 of the Agreement, unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal of this Order Form in the same manner and within the same period.

## 3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Prices.** The unit prices in Section 1.3 apply to all Purchase Orders that Customer issues under this Order Form. They include DAP delivery to any Program Site or Customer facility in the Program Territory and standard packaging. They exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes.

3.2 **Price Adjustments.** The prices in Section 1.3 are fixed for the first twelve (12) months after the Effective Date. After that, Supplier may adjust prices only under Sections 5.2 and 5.3 of the Agreement.

3.3 **Volume Rebate.** If Customer's aggregate purchases under this Order Form in any Contract Year exceed $1,500,000, Supplier shall pay Customer a rebate of three percent (3%) of the amount by which those purchases exceed $1,500,000. Supplier shall pay the rebate within thirty (30) days after the end of the Contract Year, or credit it against the next invoice, at Customer's election. "**Contract Year**" means each twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

3.4 **Payment.** Customer shall pay invoices under Section 6 of the Agreement.

## 4. FORECASTS AND ORDERING

4.1 **Program Estimate.** Customer currently estimates that it will modernize approximately ninety (90) hydraulic elevators in the Program Territory during the Initial Term. This estimate is given for Supplier's capacity planning only and does not commit Customer to purchase any quantity of Products.

4.2 **Forecasts.** Customer shall include its Program requirements in the forecasts it delivers under Section 4.2 of the Agreement, broken down by Product and by state.

4.3 **Purchase Orders.** Each Purchase Order under this Order Form must reference "Order Form No. 2026-01" and state the Program Site, if known.

## 5. DELIVERY AND PROGRAM SITES

5.1 **Delivery Locations.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the Program Site or to Customer's facility in the Program Territory stated in the Purchase Order.

5.2 **Site Deliveries.** For deliveries to a Program Site, Customer shall tell Supplier, in the Purchase Order or by later notice, the building access hours, any loading dock or elevator restrictions, and the name and telephone number of Customer's on-site contact. Supplier's carrier shall deliver hydraulic power units on a liftgate vehicle and place them at the designated receiving point.

5.3 **Stocking Locations.** Supplier shall keep stocked items under Section 1.3 at its stocking locations in Wilmington, Delaware; Eddystone, Pennsylvania; and Edison, New Jersey.

## 6. SITE INDEMNITY

6.1 Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees, agents and subcontractors, from and against all claims, demands, suits, losses, damages, liabilities, fines, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), including claims for bodily injury, death or damage to property, arising from site conditions at any Program Site, or from the installation, adjustment, testing, start-up or operation of Products at any Program Site. This Section 6.1 applies to claims brought by any person, including Supplier's own employees, agents and subcontractors.

6.2 Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any claim under Section 6.1. Section 11.3 of the Agreement applies to the defense and settlement of those claims.

## 7. TECHNICAL SUPPORT

7.1 **Start-Up Assistance.** At Customer's request, Supplier shall send a qualified field technician to a Program Site to help with start-up and adjustment of the first three (3) hydraulic power units of each model that Customer installs under this Order Form, at no charge. Additional site visits are charged at $165.00 per hour, portal to portal, plus reasonable travel expenses outside the Program Territory.

7.2 **Telephone Support.** Supplier shall provide telephone technical support for the Products from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Business Days. Support for Emergency Orders is available at any time through the Emergency Order Desk.

7.3 **Training.** During the first Contract Year, Supplier shall provide up to four (4) days of classroom and hands-on training on the installation, adjustment and troubleshooting of the BH-series controller and Supplier's hydraulic power units, for up to twelve (12) Customer technicians per session, at a Customer facility in the Program Territory.
