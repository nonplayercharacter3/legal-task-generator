# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 by Bruben Elevator Components, LLC ("**Supplier**") and Zeisein Lift Services, Inc. ("**Customer**"). It forms part of the Supply Agreement between Supplier and Customer dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 **Critical Part.** "**Critical Part**" means an Elevator Component listed in Annex 1, as Annex 1 may be updated under Section 6.2.

1.3 **Emergency Order.** "**Emergency Order**" means a Purchase Order for one or more Critical Parts that Customer places through the Emergency Order Desk and designates as an emergency order.

1.4 **Emergency Order Desk.** "**Emergency Order Desk**" means the order desk that Supplier staffs under Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Customer may reach it by telephone at the number in Annex 2 or by e-mail to the address in Annex 2.

1.5 **Dispatch.** "**Dispatch**" means that a Critical Part has been picked, packed, released to a carrier or to Supplier's own delivery vehicle, and is in transit to the delivery location stated in the Emergency Order. Supplier must also have sent Customer the tracking information.

1.6 **Measurement Period.** "**Measurement Period**" means each calendar month during the term of the Agreement.

1.7 **Monthly Fees.** "**Monthly Fees**" means the Fees invoiced to Customer for Products shipped during the relevant Measurement Period.

1.8 **Standard Order.** "**Standard Order**" means any Purchase Order that is not an Emergency Order.

## 2. GENERAL

2.1 **Purpose.** This Schedule sets out the service levels that Supplier must meet when it processes and fulfils orders for Products. It also sets out the service credits that apply if Supplier fails to meet them.

2.2 **Measurement.** Supplier shall measure its performance against each service level in each Measurement Period. Times are measured in the local time zone of the delivery location. Elapsed time for Emergency Orders runs continuously, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

2.3 **Exclusions.** A service level failure does not occur to the extent it is caused by:

(a) a force majeure event under Section 16 of the Agreement;

(b) an incorrect part number, quantity or delivery location stated by Customer in the order; or

(c) Customer's failure to provide site access or a person to receive delivery at the delivery location, where the order required this.

## 3. EMERGENCY ORDERS

3.1 **Emergency Dispatch.** Supplier shall Dispatch each Critical Part ordered in an Emergency Order within 4 hours from Emergency Order. The time runs from the time the Emergency Order Desk receives the Emergency Order. If the delivery location is within 150 miles of a Supplier stocking location listed in Annex 2, Supplier shall deliver the Critical Part within 8 hours from Emergency Order. For all other delivery locations within the continental United States, Supplier shall deliver by 10:30 a.m. local time on the next calendar day.

3.2 **Acknowledgment.** The Emergency Order Desk shall acknowledge each Emergency Order within 15 minutes of receipt. The acknowledgment must confirm the Critical Parts ordered, the stocking location from which they will ship, and the expected Dispatch time.

3.3 **Stock Shortfall.** If a Critical Part ordered in an Emergency Order is not in stock at the nearest stocking location, Supplier shall source it from another stocking location or from its production line. The time in Section 3.1 still applies. Supplier shall tell Customer within 1 hour of receipt if it expects to miss the Dispatch time, and shall state the expected Dispatch time.

3.4 **Expedited Freight.** Supplier shall bear the cost of courier, air, same-day and other expedited freight for Emergency Orders.

3.5 **Emergency Pricing.** Critical Parts supplied under an Emergency Order are charged at the prices set out in the applicable Order Form. Supplier shall not charge any emergency premium, after-hours fee or rush handling fee.

## 4. STANDARD ORDERS

4.1 **Order Acknowledgment.** Supplier shall acknowledge each Standard Order within 1 Business Day of receipt and shall confirm the delivery date.

4.2 **On-Time Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver at least 95% of the line items in Standard Orders on or before the confirmed delivery date in each Measurement Period.

4.3 **Order Accuracy.** At least 99% of the line items Supplier ships in each Measurement Period must match the order in part number and quantity, and must arrive with the documentation required under Section 7.4 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Stocked Lead Time.** For Products identified as stocked items in the applicable Order Form, Supplier shall ship Standard Orders within 3 Business Days of acknowledgment.

## 5. SERVICE CREDITS

5.1 **Emergency Dispatch Credits.** If Supplier fails to Dispatch a Critical Part under an Emergency Order within the time stated in Section 3.1, Customer is entitled to a service credit equal to:

(a) 25% of the price of the affected Critical Part, if Dispatch occurs no more than 4 hours late; or

(b) 50% of the price of the affected Critical Part, if Dispatch occurs more than 4 hours late.

In addition, Customer is entitled to a credit of $750 for each Emergency Order affected, to offset Customer's crew standby costs.

5.2 **Standard Order Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet the service level in Section 4.2 or 4.3 in any Measurement Period, Customer is entitled to a service credit equal to 3% of the Monthly Fees for each full percentage point by which Supplier missed the service level.

5.3 **Credit Cap.** The total service credits for any Measurement Period will not exceed 20% of the Monthly Fees for that Measurement Period.

5.4 **Application.** Supplier shall show accrued service credits on the next invoice it issues after the end of the relevant Measurement Period. Customer may also set off service credits under Section 6.5 of the Agreement. Supplier shall pay any credits not yet applied when the Agreement expires or terminates within 30 days after the expiration or termination date.

5.5 **Other Remedies.** Service credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the service. They are not Customer's only remedy. Customer keeps its rights under Section 15.3 of the Agreement and its other rights and remedies under the Agreement.

## 6. REPORTING AND REVIEW

6.1 **Monthly Report.** Within 10 Business Days after the end of each Measurement Period, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a report that shows:

(a) each Emergency Order received, its time of receipt, its time of Dispatch and its time of delivery;

(b) Supplier's performance against each service level in Sections 3 and 4;

(c) any service credits accrued; and

(d) the inventory of each Critical Part held at each stocking location on the last day of the Measurement Period.

6.2 **Quarterly Review.** The Parties' account managers shall meet at least once each calendar quarter to review Supplier's performance, Customer's forecasts and the parts listed in Annex 1. Any change to Annex 1 must be made by a written amendment under Section 18.3 of the Agreement.

6.3 **Records.** Supplier shall keep accurate records of the data used to calculate its performance for at least 2 years. Customer may review those records on 10 Business Days' notice, during normal business hours, no more than twice in any 12-month period.

## 7. INVENTORY

7.1 **Safety Stock.** Supplier shall keep, at each stocking location listed in Annex 2, at least the minimum quantity of each Critical Part stated in Annex 1.

7.2 **Replenishment.** If inventory of a Critical Part falls below its minimum quantity at a stocking location, Supplier shall restore it within 5 Business Days.
