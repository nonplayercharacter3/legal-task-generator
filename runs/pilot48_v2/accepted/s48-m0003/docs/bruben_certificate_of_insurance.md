## 5. DESCRIPTION OF OPERATIONS AND ENDORSEMENTS

5.1 **Additional Insureds.** Zeisein Lift Services, Inc. and its Affiliates are included as additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability policy (including products–completed operations), the Commercial Automobile Liability policy and the Umbrella Liability policy. This status applies where required by written contract, under endorsements CG 20 15 04 13 (Additional Insured – Vendors), CG 20 37 04 13 (Additional Insured – Owners, Lessees or Contractors – Completed Operations), CA 20 48 10 13 (Designated Insured for Covered Autos Liability Coverage) and the blanket additional insured provision of the Umbrella Liability policy.

5.2 **Primary and Non-Contributory.** The Commercial General Liability and Umbrella Liability coverage afforded to the additional insureds is primary to and non-contributory with any other insurance maintained by the additional insureds, where required by written contract (endorsement CG 20 01 04 13).

5.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** The insurers waive their rights of subrogation against the additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability, Commercial Automobile Liability and Workers' Compensation and Employer's Liability policies, where required by written contract (endorsements CG 24 04 05 09, CA 04 44 10 13 and WC 00 03 13).

5.4 **Operations.** Design, manufacture, distribution and delivery of elevator components, including hydraulic power units, valve units and controller boards. Re: Supply Agreement dated March 2, 2026 between Bruben Elevator Components, LLC and Zeisein Lift Services, Inc., Section 14.

## 6. NOTICE OF CANCELLATION

Should any of the above policies be cancelled, non-renewed or materially reduced in coverage before the expiration date, the issuing insurer will mail at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice to the certificate holder named in Section 7 (ten (10) days for non-payment of premium), in accordance with the notice of cancellation to third parties endorsement attached to each policy. The Insured has also agreed under Section 14.2 of the Supply Agreement to give the certificate holder at least thirty (30) days' notice before any cancellation or material reduction of coverage.
