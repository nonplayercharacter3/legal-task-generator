**Re: Supply Agreement dated March 2, 2026 between Bruben Elevator Components, LLC and Zeisein Lift Services, Inc. – Notice of Objection to Price Adjustment Notice**

Dear Mr. Bruben:

1. **Notice of Objection.** We refer to the Supply Agreement dated March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between Bruben Elevator Components, LLC ("**Supplier**") and Zeisein Lift Services, Inc. ("**Customer**"). We also refer to Order Form No. 2026-01 for the Mid-Atlantic Hydraulic Modernization Program (the "**Order Form**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. Supplier's Price Adjustment Notice was dated July 28, 2026 and was delivered to our King of Prussia offices by courier the same day (the "**July Notice**"). The July Notice proposes a fourteen percent (14%) increase in the prices of all Products listed in Section 1.3 of the Order Form, effective October 1, 2026. Under Section 5.3 of the Agreement, Customer hereby objects to the July Notice and to the adjusted prices it proposes.

2. **Grounds for Objection.** Customer objects to the increase for the following reasons:

   (a) The July Notice gives no explanation of the increase. It does not identify any change in Supplier's material, labor or freight costs that would justify an increase of this size.

   (b) An across-the-board increase of fourteen percent (14%) is well above the movement in published producer price indices for comparable industrial components over the same period.

   (c) Customer priced its modernization proposals to building owners in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware on the basis of the unit prices in the Order Form. Most of those contracts have fixed prices. Customer cannot pass an increase of this size through to its own customers on the Program Sites now under contract.

3. **Existing Prices.** Customer will continue to issue Purchase Orders under the Order Form at the unit prices stated in Section 1.3 of the Order Form. Customer expects Supplier to invoice those Purchase Orders at those prices.

4. **Negotiation.** Customer is prepared to negotiate revised prices in good faith under Section 5.3 of the Agreement. We propose that the Parties' account managers, Ms. Dana K. Whitlow and Mr. Marcus T. Ferrante, meet within the next two weeks. At that meeting we ask Supplier to provide reasonable supporting information for any cost increases it has incurred on the affected Products, broken down by part number.

5. **Contacts.** Please direct your response to me at the address above, with a copy to our General Counsel at the same address. For scheduling the meeting described in paragraph 4, please contact Mr. Ferrante at (610) 555-0391.

6. **Notice.** This letter is a notice under Section 19.4 of the Agreement.
