# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Bruben Elevator Components, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 4100 Foundry Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Zeisein Lift Services, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Commerce Drive, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and distributes elevator components, including hydraulic power units, valve units and controller boards.

B. Customer provides elevator maintenance, repair and modernization services to building owners and managers.

C. Customer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to supply, elevator components on the terms of this Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and each Order Form.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Elevator Components**" means elevator and lift parts, assemblies and components of every kind. This includes hydraulic power units, valve units, pumps, motors, controller boards, drives, door operators, fixtures, sensors, wiring harnesses, and related spare and replacement parts.

1.3 "**Products**" means the Elevator Components that Supplier makes available to Customer under this Agreement, including those identified in any Order Form.

1.4 "**Critical Part**" means any Elevator Component that is needed to release passengers from an entrapment or to return any elevator to service.

1.5 "**Order Form**" means a written order form, executed by both Parties, that references this Agreement and sets out the Products, pricing, program territory, term and other commercial terms for a program of purchases.

1.6 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products that Customer issues under this Agreement and an applicable Order Form.

1.7 "**Emergency Order**" has the meaning given in the Service Level Schedule.

1.8 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the Service Level Schedule agreed by the Parties and attached to or referencing this Agreement, as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 18.3.

1.9 "**Price Adjustment Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

1.10 "**Specifications**" means the technical specifications, drawings and performance criteria for each Product, as published by Supplier or as set out in the applicable Order Form.

1.11 "**Fees**" means all amounts payable by Customer for Products under this Agreement and each Order Form.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. It continues for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Either Party may prevent a renewal by giving written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Order Forms.** Each Order Form forms part of this Agreement and is governed by it. If this Agreement expires or terminates, any Order Form then in effect continues to be governed by this Agreement until that Order Form expires or terminates, unless the Order Form states otherwise.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY AND SUPPLY COMMITMENT

3.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the term of this Agreement, Supplier is the exclusive source of all Elevator Components purchased or used by Customer and its Affiliates throughout North America. Neither Customer nor any of its Affiliates will, directly or indirectly, (a) purchase Elevator Components from any person other than Supplier, (b) manufacture Elevator Components, or (c) refurbish, rebuild or remanufacture Elevator Components for resale or for installation at any customer site, in each case other than Products supplied by Supplier under this Agreement.

3.2 **Supply Commitment.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity and inventory sufficient to meet Customer's requirements for Products, as forecast under Section 4.2. Supplier shall not allocate available supply in a way that disfavors Customer as compared with Supplier's other customers.

3.3 **Product Changes.** Supplier shall give Customer at least one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice before discontinuing any Product or making any change to a Product that affects its form, fit or function. During that period Supplier shall accept last-time-buy orders for the affected Product.

## 4. ORDERING AND FORECASTS

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders. Each Purchase Order must state the Products, quantities, requested delivery date and delivery location. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order (other than an Emergency Order) within two (2) Business Days after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday in the United States.

4.2 **Forecasts.** By the first Business Day of each calendar quarter, Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its expected requirements for Products.

4.3 **Emergency Orders.** Customer may place Emergency Orders for Critical Parts in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. Supplier shall staff an order desk to receive Emergency Orders twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week.

4.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or other form issued by either Party have no effect.

## 5. PRICING AND PRICE ADJUSTMENTS

5.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for Products set out in the applicable Order Form. Prices include standard packaging and are exclusive of applicable sales, use and similar taxes.

5.2 **Price Adjustment Notice.** No more than once in any twelve (12) month period, Supplier may propose an adjustment to the prices for Products by delivering to Customer a written notice under Section 19.4 that identifies the affected Products, the adjusted prices and the date on which the adjusted prices take effect (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**"). The effective date stated in a Price Adjustment Notice must be at least sixty (60) days after Customer receives the Price Adjustment Notice.

5.3 **Objection.** Customer may object to a Price Adjustment Notice by delivering written notice of objection to Supplier within 30 days after receipt of a Price Adjustment Notice. If Customer does not deliver a notice of objection within that period, the adjusted prices are deemed accepted by Customer and take effect on the date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice. If Customer delivers a timely notice of objection, the existing prices remain in effect. The Parties shall then negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to agree on revised prices. If they cannot agree within that period, either Party may refer the matter to dispute resolution under Section 19.2.

5.4 **Price Adjustments Prospective Only.** Adjusted prices apply only to Purchase Orders issued on or after the date on which the adjusted prices take effect.

## 6. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Customer upon shipment of Products. Each invoice must reference the applicable Purchase Order number.

6.2 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

6.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith, if it notifies Supplier of the dispute and its basis before the payment due date. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve the dispute. Customer's payment of an invoice does not waive any of its rights under this Agreement.

6.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

6.5 **Service Credits.** Customer may set off against amounts due to Supplier any service credits that accrue under the Service Level Schedule.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the delivery location stated in each Purchase Order. Delivery is due on the date stated in the accepted Purchase Order. For Emergency Orders, the Service Level Schedule governs.

7.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Customer promptly if it expects any delay in delivery and shall state the cause and the expected delivery date. If a delivery (other than under an Emergency Order) is more than five (5) Business Days late, Customer may cancel the affected Purchase Order without liability. Supplier shall then reimburse Customer for any reasonable cost of procuring substitute products in excess of the price under this Agreement.

7.3 **Title and Risk.** Title to Products and risk of loss pass to Customer upon delivery at the delivery location.

7.4 **Packaging and Documentation.** Supplier shall package each shipment to prevent damage in transit. Supplier shall include a packing list, certificates of conformance, and any installation and wiring documentation needed for the Product.

## 8. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

8.1 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products within ten (10) Business Days after delivery. Customer may reject any Product that does not conform to the Specifications or the applicable Purchase Order.

8.2 **Rejected Products.** At Customer's election, Supplier shall promptly repair or replace rejected Products, or credit Customer in full for them, at Supplier's expense, including return freight.

8.3 **No Waiver.** Inspection, acceptance or payment does not waive Customer's rights under Section 9.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, for eighteen (18) months after delivery or twelve (12) months after installation, whichever ends first, each Product will:

(a) conform to the Specifications;

(b) be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship; and

(c) be new, unless the Order Form expressly allows refurbished Products.

9.2 **Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product, when delivered, complies with ASME A17.1/CSA B44 (Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators), as applicable, and with all other laws and codes applicable to its design and manufacture.

9.3 **Title Warranty.** Supplier warrants that it will convey good title to each Product, free of all liens and encumbrances.

9.4 **Remedies.** If a Product breaches the warranty in Section 9.1 or 9.2, Supplier shall, at Customer's election, repair or replace the Product or refund its price. Supplier shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable labor costs to remove the nonconforming Product and install a repaired or replacement Product.

9.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

10.1 **Ownership.** Supplier retains all intellectual property rights in the Products and the Specifications.

10.2 **License.** Supplier grants Customer and its customers a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use, install, maintain and repair the Products and any embedded software or firmware, solely in connection with the elevators in which the Products are installed.

10.3 **Technical Information.** Supplier shall provide Customer with the wiring diagrams, adjustment procedures, diagnostic tools and access codes reasonably needed to install, adjust, troubleshoot and maintain the Products.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), to the extent arising from:

(a) any defect in a Product;

(b) any claim that a Product, or Customer's use of it in accordance with this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates a third party's intellectual property right; or

(c) the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), to the extent arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel in installing or servicing the Products.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, but a delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay causes it prejudice. The indemnifying Party controls the defense and settlement of the claim. However, it may not settle any claim in a way that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent. The indemnified Party shall cooperate reasonably at the indemnifying Party's expense and may take part in the defense with its own counsel at its own expense.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 12.2, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

12.2 **Exclusions.** The limitation in Section 12.1 and the exclusion in Section 12.3 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 11;

(b) a Party's obligations under any Order Form;

(c) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality);

(d) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(e) Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due under this Agreement.

12.3 **Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 12.2, neither Party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information the other Party discloses to it in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, forecasts, Specifications and customer information ("**Confidential Information**"). It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement. A Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13.

13.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, (b) the receiving Party already lawfully had without restriction, (c) the receiving Party lawfully receives from a third party without restriction, or (d) the receiving Party independently develops without use of the disclosing Party's information.

13.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order. Where lawful, it shall give the other Party prompt notice and reasonable assistance in seeking a protective order.

13.4 **Other Agreements.** This Section 13 applies in addition to any separate nondisclosure agreement between the Parties.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 **Required Coverage.** During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years after it ends, Supplier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations and contractual liability coverage, with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of at least $1,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per accident; and

(d) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate.

14.2 **Additional Insured; Certificates.** Supplier shall name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds on the policies in Sections 14.1(a), (b) and (d). Supplier shall deliver a certificate of insurance evidencing the required coverage on or before the Effective Date and upon each renewal. Supplier shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' notice before any cancellation or material reduction of coverage.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach.

15.2 **For Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **For Repeated Service Failures.** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form by written notice if Supplier fails to meet the same service level under the Service Level Schedule in any three (3) months within a rolling twelve (12) month period.

15.4 **Effect.** On termination, Customer shall pay for Products delivered before the termination date. At Customer's election, Supplier shall fill any accepted Purchase Orders outstanding on the termination date. Sections 9, 11, 12, 13, 15.4, 19 and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive will survive termination or expiration.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 Neither Party is liable for a failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of war or terrorism, epidemics, government orders and labor actions not involving the affected Party's own workforce. The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

16.2 If a force majeure event prevents Supplier from supplying Products for more than ten (10) Business Days, Customer may purchase substitute products from other sources for the duration of the event without breaching this Agreement.

## 17. PARENT GUARANTY

17.1 As a condition to Customer's obligations under this Agreement, Supplier shall cause Bruben Industrial Holdings, Inc. to execute and deliver to Customer a guaranty of Supplier's obligations under this Agreement, in a form reasonably acceptable to Customer, on or before the Effective Date.

## 18. AGREEMENT STRUCTURE

18.1 **Documents.** This Agreement consists of these terms and conditions, the Service Level Schedule and each Order Form.

18.2 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming this Agreement, they prevail in the following order: the Service Level Schedule, then the Order Form, then the body of this Supply Agreement.

18.3 **Amendment.** This Agreement, the Service Level Schedule and any Order Form may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
