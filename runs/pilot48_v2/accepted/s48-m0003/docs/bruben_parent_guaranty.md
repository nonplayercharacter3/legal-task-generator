# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 2, 2026 by **Bruben Industrial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Foundry Road, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Zeisein Lift Services, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Commerce Drive, King of Prussia, Pennsylvania 19406 ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Bruben Elevator Components, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), and Customer have entered into a Supply Agreement dated March 2, 2026 (as amended from time to time, and including the Service Level Schedule and each Order Form forming part of it, the "**Agreement**").

B. Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor will benefit, directly and indirectly, from the transactions contemplated by the Agreement.

C. Section 17.1 of the Agreement requires Supplier to cause the Guarantor to execute and deliver this Guaranty to Customer on or before the Effective Date of the Agreement. Customer would not have entered into the Agreement without this Guaranty.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Supplier to Customer under or arising out of the Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, whether actual or contingent, and whether for payment or performance. They include Supplier's obligations to supply and deliver Products, to meet the service levels in the Service Level Schedule, to pay or credit service credits, rebates, refunds and reimbursements, to perform its warranty obligations, and to defend and indemnify Customer and the other indemnified persons under Section 11.1 of the Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** The Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Customer, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

2.2 **Performance on Default.** If Supplier fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Guarantor shall, within ten (10) Business Days after Customer's written demand, pay the amount due or perform the obligation, or cause it to be performed. Where Supplier has failed to perform a supply obligation, the Guarantor may meet its obligation either by procuring performance by Supplier or another Affiliate of the Guarantor, or by paying Customer the losses, damages, costs and expenses that Customer incurs because of Supplier's failure, including the reasonable cost of procuring substitute products.

2.3 **Extent of Liability.** The Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is equal to, and no greater than, Supplier's liability under the Agreement. The Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Supplier could assert under the Agreement, other than the defenses waived in Section 4.

2.4 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Customer need not first make demand on Supplier, bring any action against Supplier, or pursue any other security or remedy before enforcing this Guaranty against the Guarantor.

2.5 **Costs of Enforcement.** The Guarantor shall pay Customer's reasonable attorneys' fees and other costs incurred in enforcing this Guaranty, to the extent Customer prevails.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including obligations that survive expiration or termination of the Agreement under Section 15.4 of the Agreement and obligations under any Order Form that continues after the Agreement ends under Section 2.3 of the Agreement.

3.2 **Changes to the Agreement.** The Guarantor's obligations are not released, reduced or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not the Guarantor has notice of or consents to them:

(a) any amendment, renewal, extension or modification of the Agreement, including any new Order Form or any change to the Service Level Schedule;

(b) any waiver, forbearance, extension of time or other indulgence that Customer grants to Supplier;

(c) any change in the ownership, organization or legal existence of Supplier, including any merger or change of control; or

(d) any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar proceeding affecting Supplier, or any discharge, stay or limitation of Supplier's liability in that proceeding.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** If any payment by or on behalf of Supplier or the Guarantor to Customer is rescinded or must be returned on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Supplier or otherwise, this Guaranty will be reinstated as to that payment as if it had not been made.

## 4. WAIVERS

4.1 The Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty and notice of the creation of any Guaranteed Obligation;

(b) presentment, demand for payment or performance on Supplier, protest and notice of dishonor or default, other than the demand on the Guarantor under Section 2.2;

(c) any right to require Customer to proceed first against Supplier or any other person or security; and

(d) any defense based on the invalidity or unenforceability of the Agreement against Supplier because of Supplier's lack of capacity or authority, or any defense of suretyship available to the Guarantor as a guarantor rather than a principal obligor.

4.2 **Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid and performed in full, the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Supplier arising from its payment or performance under this Guaranty.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Guarantor represents and warrants to Customer that, as of the date of this Guaranty:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all corporate action needed to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not breach its certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement to which it is a party; and

(e) it owns, directly or indirectly, all of the membership interests in Supplier.

## 6. COVENANTS

6.1 **Financial Information.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the Guarantor shall deliver to Customer its annual consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year. Customer shall treat those statements as Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Notice of Change.** The Guarantor shall notify Customer in writing within ten (10) Business Days after it ceases to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the membership interests in Supplier. The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty continue as to all Guaranteed Obligations, whether arising before or after that event.
