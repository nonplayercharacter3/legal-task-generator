**Re: Side Letter to Stock Purchase Agreement Relating to Lumenhall Presents, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of the date of this Side Letter (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), by and between Rebond Live Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Drorand Entertainment Group LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will acquire all of the issued and outstanding Shares of Lumenhall Presents, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

This Side Letter sets out certain understandings of Buyer and Seller about the matters described below. Buyer and Seller agree as follows:

## 1. Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. References to Sections are to Sections of the Purchase Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. Public Announcements

Neither Buyer nor Seller shall issue any press release or public announcement about the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. This Paragraph 2 does not restrict any disclosure required by law. Before the Closing, Buyer and Seller shall cooperate in good faith on a joint announcement to the Company's artists, agents, sponsors and ticketing partners. That announcement is to be released on or promptly after the Closing Date.

## 3. Tour Settlement Tax Audits

(a) Seller has informed Buyer that the Company settles performer fees, guarantees and percentage payments for touring engagements in multiple jurisdictions. Seller has also informed Buyer that state and local taxing authorities in those jurisdictions periodically examine those settlements. The examinations cover nonresident performer withholding and the admissions, amusement and sales Taxes collected on ticket sales.

(b) To clarify how Article VIII applies to any such examination of a pre-Closing tour settlement, Buyer and Seller agree on two points. First, the Indemnity Cap shall apply to all Losses under Section 8.2, including Losses arising from any breach of Section 4.12. Second, the only Losses excluded from the Indemnity Cap are Losses arising from Fraud and from breaches of the Fundamental Representations. The Indemnity Cap is equal to 10% of the Purchase Price.

(c) Seller shall control, at its own expense, any audit or proceeding relating solely to a pre-Closing tour settlement. The following conditions apply to that control:

1. Seller shall keep Buyer reasonably informed of the progress of the audit or proceeding.
2. Seller shall give Buyer copies of all material correspondence with the relevant taxing authority.
3. Buyer may participate at its own expense.
4. Seller shall not settle or compromise any such audit or proceeding without Buyer's prior written consent if the settlement or compromise would reasonably be expected to increase the Taxes of the Company for any period beginning after the Closing Date. Buyer shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay that consent.

This Paragraph 3(c) is in addition to the cooperation obligations of Buyer and Seller under Section 6.6.

## 4. Artist Deposit Reconciliation

Within 45 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a schedule of all Artist Deposits outstanding as of the Closing. The schedule shall list, for each Artist Deposit:

- the artist;
- the engagement date;
- the venue; and
- the amount paid.

The schedule is for informational purposes. It is intended to assist Buyer in preparing the closing statement of net working capital under Section 2.3(b). It does not modify the treatment of Artist Deposits under Section 2.3(a).

## 5. Transition Cooperation

For 90 days after the Closing Date, Seller shall make its personnel reasonably available to answer Buyer's questions about the Company's historical booking, settlement and accounting practices. Seller shall do so at no charge to Buyer, during normal business hours and upon reasonable notice. Seller's obligation under this Paragraph 5 does not require any person to provide services that would unreasonably interfere with that person's other duties.

## 6. Miscellaneous

(a) **Transaction Document.** This Side Letter is a Transaction Document. It may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller.

(b) **Effectiveness.** This Side Letter becomes effective upon execution by Buyer and Seller. It terminates automatically if the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with Article IX before the Closing.

(c) **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** Section 10.8 (Governing Law) and Section 10.9 (Jurisdiction; Venue) apply to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it, with the necessary changes.

(d) **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
