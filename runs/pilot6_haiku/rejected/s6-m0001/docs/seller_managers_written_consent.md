# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE MANAGERS OF DRORAND ENTERTAINMENT GROUP LLC

**In Lieu of a Meeting**

**Dated as of March 13, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the managers (the "**Managers**") of Drorand Entertainment Group LLC, a New York limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act by written consent without a meeting. They do so under Section 408(d) of the New York Limited Liability Company Law and Section 6.7 of the Amended and Restated Operating Agreement of the Company (the "**Operating Agreement**"). The Managers consent to the adoption of the following resolutions and direct that this Written Consent be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Managers.

## 1. Recitals

**1.1 Ownership of the Target.** The Company is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the "**Shares**"), of Lumenhall Presents, Inc., a New York corporation ("**Lumenhall**"). Lumenhall books, promotes and produces live concert tours and events. It also operates the Saltmarsh Pavilion, a seasonal waterfront amphitheater.

**1.2 Proposed Sale.** Rebond Live Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), has proposed to buy all of the Shares from the Company. The terms are set out in a Stock Purchase Agreement between Buyer and the Company (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), which is to be dated as of March 14, 2026. The aggregate purchase price is $86,400,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Price**").

**1.3 Ancillary Documents.** The Purchase Agreement contemplates that the Company will enter into the following documents:

(a) a letter agreement between Buyer and the Company, to be dated as of the date of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Side Letter**"); and

(b) at the Closing, an Indemnity Escrow Agreement among Buyer, the Company and Pellucid Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**"). Under the Escrow Agreement, $8,640,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") will be deposited and held for 18 months after the Closing Date.

The Purchase Agreement, the Side Letter, the Escrow Agreement and the other agreements, certificates and instruments to be delivered under the Purchase Agreement are referred to together as the "**Transaction Documents**." The drafts of the Purchase Agreement, the Side Letter and the Escrow Agreement have been presented to each of the Managers.

**1.4 Saltmarsh Pavilion License.** Lumenhall occupies and operates the Saltmarsh Pavilion under a license agreement with Kestrel Bay Parks Conservancy (the "**Conservancy**"), referred to here as the "**Saltmarsh Pavilion License Agreement**." The Managers have been advised of two terms of that agreement:

(a) it requires the Conservancy's prior written consent to any direct or indirect change of control of Lumenhall Presents, Inc.; and

(b) if that consent is not obtained, the Conservancy may terminate the Saltmarsh Pavilion License Agreement on 30 days' notice.

**1.5 Managers' Determination.** The Managers have reviewed the terms of the proposed transaction with the Company's officers and outside advisors. They have determined that the sale of the Shares on the terms of the Transaction Documents is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members.

## 2. Resolution 1 — Approval of the Sale and the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the sale of all of the Shares to Buyer for the Purchase Price, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement, is approved in all respects.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Managers, are approved.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that Theodore Vance Lindqvist, as Managing Manager, and any other officer of the Company designated by the Managing Manager (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is authorized to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement on behalf of the Company. Any Authorized Officer may approve changes to the Purchase Agreement, and execution of the Purchase Agreement by an Authorized Officer shall be conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. Resolution 2 — Approval of the Side Letter

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Side Letter, substantially in the form presented to the Managers, are approved.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver the Side Letter on behalf of the Company, with any changes that the Authorized Officer approves. Execution by an Authorized Officer shall be conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 4. Resolution 3 — Approval of the Escrow Agreement and Closing Deliverables

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Escrow Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Managers, are approved. The deposit of the Indemnity Escrow Amount with Pellucid Trust Company, N.A. at the Closing is also approved.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, at the Closing and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the following:

(a) the Escrow Agreement;

(b) stock certificates or stock powers transferring the Shares to Buyer;

(c) the officer's certificate required by the Purchase Agreement; and

(d) every other closing deliverable required of the Company under the Purchase Agreement, including evidence of the Required Consents (as defined in the Purchase Agreement).

## 5. Resolution 4 — Third-Party Consents

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to request, negotiate and obtain, on behalf of the Company and Lumenhall, each of the Required Consents before the Closing. This includes the consents of the lender under Lumenhall's revolving credit facility and of the counterparty to Lumenhall's primary ticketing services agreement, and the approval of the New York State Liquor Authority.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that, with respect to the Saltmarsh Pavilion License Agreement, the Authorized Officers are authorized to seek such consent after the Closing from the Conservancy. In doing so they may cooperate with Buyer as reasonably requested and deliver such information to the Conservancy as it may reasonably require.

## 6. Resolution 5 — Distribution of Proceeds

**RESOLVED**, that, after the Closing, the net cash proceeds received by the Company from the Purchase Price shall be applied in accordance with the Operating Agreement. The amounts to be applied are those remaining after the payment of transaction expenses and the establishment of such reserves as the Managing Manager determines to be reasonably necessary.

## 7. Resolution 6 — General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take any further action that the officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of these resolutions and to consummate the transactions contemplated by the Transaction Documents. This includes executing and delivering additional certificates, instruments and notices and paying fees and expenses.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any Manager or Authorized Officer in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness; Counterparts

This Written Consent is effective as of the date first written above. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and each counterpart shall be deemed an original. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
