# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This **INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026, by and among **Rebond Live Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Drorand Entertainment Group LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Pellucid Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of Lumenhall Presents, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Indemnity Escrow Amount is to be held and disbursed in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.2.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs, as set forth in the joint certificate delivered by Buyer and Seller under Section 3.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest, dividends and other income earned on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Escrow Period**" means the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending 18 months after the Closing Date.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction as described in Section 5.3, together with a certificate of counsel to the presenting party stating that the order is final and non-appealable.

"**Indemnifiable Claim**" means a claim by Buyer, on behalf of itself or any other Buyer Indemnitee, for breach of any representation or warranty of Seller under Section 8.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" means $8,640,000.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, as listed on Schedule B, directing the Escrow Agent to disburse all or part of the Escrow Funds.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

"**Release Date**" means the first Business Day after the last day of the Escrow Period.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Escrow Agreement.

**2.2 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller designates the individuals listed for it on Schedule B as its authorized representatives. A party may change its authorized representatives by written notice to the other parties. The change becomes effective when the Escrow Agent completes its call-back verification under Section 7.6.

## 3. DEPOSIT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account set forth on Schedule A. On the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall jointly deliver to the Escrow Agent a certificate confirming the Closing Date.

**3.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent.

**3.3 No Other Rights.** The Escrow Funds shall not be subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any party. No party may assign or encumber its interest in the Escrow Funds except as permitted by Section 12.4.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Purpose.** The Escrow Funds are held as a source of recovery for Indemnifiable Claims in accordance with this Article 4 and Article 5.

**4.2 Claim Notices.** At any time before the expiration of the Escrow Period, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") of an Indemnifiable Claim. Each Claim Notice shall:

(a) describe the Indemnifiable Claim in reasonable detail, including the representation or warranty alleged to have been breached;

(b) state the amount of Losses incurred or reasonably estimated to be incurred (the "**Claimed Amount**"); and

(c) request disbursement of the Claimed Amount from the Escrow Funds.

The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds to Buyer only in respect of an Indemnifiable Claim that is the subject of a Claim Notice delivered in accordance with this Section 4.2.

**4.3 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a Claimed Amount by delivering a written notice to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within 30 days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice shall state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the portion of the Claimed Amount objected to.

**4.4 No Objection.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within 30 days after the Claim Notice is delivered to Seller, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Claimed Amount to Buyer within five Business Days after the end of that 30-day period. If an Objection Notice covers only part of a Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer within the same period.

**4.5 Evidence of Delivery.** Buyer shall provide the Escrow Agent with reasonable evidence of the date on which Seller received each Claim Notice. The Escrow Agent may rely conclusively on that evidence.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions promptly after any Indemnifiable Claim is resolved by agreement between them.

**5.2 Release at End of Escrow Period.** On the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the aggregate Claimed Amounts of all Claim Notices delivered before the end of the Escrow Period that have not yet been paid or resolved. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amounts retained until the related claims are resolved under Section 5.1 or Section 5.3. The Escrow Agent shall then disburse each retained amount as directed in the applicable Joint Written Instructions or Final Order. Any balance remaining after all retained claims are resolved shall be disbursed to Seller.

**5.3 Disputed Amounts.** If Seller delivers a timely Objection Notice, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the portion of the Claimed Amount that is objected to (the "**Disputed Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall release a Disputed Amount only (a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, or (b) in accordance with a Final Order of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds in accordance with any such Final Order within five Business Days after it receives the Final Order.

**5.4 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer to the account designated on Schedule A for the receiving party, or to another account designated in writing by that party and verified under Section 7.6.

## 6. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**6.1 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the investment option set forth on Schedule A, unless Buyer and Seller direct otherwise by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as necessary to make a disbursement under this Escrow Agreement.

**6.2 No Liability for Investment Loss.** The Escrow Agent shall have no liability for any loss on an investment made in accordance with this Article 6, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**6.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds shall become part of the Escrow Funds and be disbursed with them.

## 7. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Scope of Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall act solely as a depository and shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement. No implied duties shall be read into this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, is not bound by and has no duty to inquire into the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller, even if it is referred to in this Escrow Agreement.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it reasonably believes to be genuine and signed by the proper person. The Escrow Agent shall have no duty to verify the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**7.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent of its gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for special, indirect or consequential damages.

**7.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**7.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an action in interpleader in the court specified in Section 12.6 and deposit the Escrow Funds with that court.

**7.6 Security Procedures.** Before acting on any funds transfer instruction or any change in authorized representatives or account details, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule B, other than the person who signed the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay acting on any instruction until it has completed that verification.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**8.1 Indemnity.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. This indemnity does not cover losses caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**8.2 Allocation Between Buyer and Seller.** As between Buyer and Seller, any amount paid under Section 8.1 shall be borne equally. However, a party whose breach of this Escrow Agreement or the Purchase Agreement caused the loss shall bear the full amount.

**8.3 Survival.** This Article 8 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. FEES AND EXPENSES

**9.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule C, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Those fees and expenses shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**9.2 No Set-Off Against Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent may not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds unless they remain unpaid 30 days after the Escrow Agent invoices Buyer and Seller.

## 10. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**10.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions delivered at least 30 days before the effective date of removal.

**10.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition the court specified in Section 12.6 to appoint a successor. The resignation or removal becomes effective only when the successor accepts its appointment and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to it.

## 11. TAX MATTERS

**11.1 Tax Reporting.** For tax reporting purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Funds. The Escrow Agent shall issue IRS Form 1099 or other required information returns accordingly.

**11.2 Tax Forms.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date.

**11.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount it is required by law to withhold, and shall remit that amount to the appropriate taxing authority.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Notices.** All notices under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on Schedule B. Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

**12.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**12.3 Termination.** This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Articles 8 and 9 survive termination.

**12.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall succeed to the Escrow Agent's rights and obligations without further act.

**12.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Escrow Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Any action or proceeding concerning the release of the Escrow Funds, or otherwise arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement, shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of that court and waives any objection to venue in that court. **EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION OR PROCEEDING.**

**12.7 USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is required by federal law to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person who opens an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**12.8 Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
