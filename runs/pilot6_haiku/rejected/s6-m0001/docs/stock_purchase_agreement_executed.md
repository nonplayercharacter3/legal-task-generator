# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This **STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 14, 2026, by and between **Rebond Live Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Drorand Entertainment Group LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the "**Shares**"), of Lumenhall Presents, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company is engaged in the business of booking, promoting and producing live concert tours and events and operating the Saltmarsh Pavilion, a seasonal waterfront amphitheater.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Artist Deposits**" means all deposits, guarantees and advances paid or payable by the Company to performing artists, their agents or their production companies in respect of engagements scheduled to occur after the Closing Date.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pellucid Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Indemnity Escrow Agreement to be entered into at Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud by a party in the making of the representations and warranties in this Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization and Good Standing), Section 4.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, commission, court or other authority of any such government.

"**Indemnifiable Losses**" means Losses for which Buyer Indemnitees are entitled to indemnification under Section 8.2(a), Section 8.2(b) or Section 8.2(c).

"**Indemnity Cap**" has the meaning given in Section 8.4(a).

"**Indemnity Escrow Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4(a).

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, Taxes, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, assets, results of operations or financial condition of the Company, taken as a whole.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Taxes**" means all Taxes of the Company for any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and, for any taxable period that includes but does not end on the Closing Date, the portion of such period ending on the Closing Date.

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Required Consents**" means (a) the consent of the lender under the Company's revolving credit facility to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, together with a customary payoff letter; (b) the consent of the counterparty to the Company's primary ticketing services agreement to the change of control of the Company; and (c) the approval of the New York State Liquor Authority with respect to the change in control of the holder of the Company's on-premises liquor licenses.

"**Side Letter**" means the letter agreement between Buyer and Seller dated as of the date of this Agreement relating to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, admissions, amusement, withholding (including nonresident performer withholding), payroll, employment, franchise, property and other taxes, together with any interest, penalties or additions thereto.

"**Transaction Documents**" means this Agreement, the Side Letter, the Escrow Agreement and the other agreements and certificates delivered under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay (a) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount, and (b) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the Indemnity Escrow Amount, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment under Section 2.3, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in writing by the Escrow Agent and Seller, respectively, at least two Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing (treating Artist Deposits as current assets only to the extent the related engagement has not been cancelled), and the Purchase Price payable at Closing shall be increased or decreased on a dollar-for-dollar basis by the amount by which such estimate exceeds or falls short of $3,200,000 (the "**Estimated Working Capital Adjustment**").

(b) Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual net working capital as of the Closing. Seller shall have 30 days to dispute the statement. Any dispute not resolved by the parties within 30 days thereafter shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five Business Days after final determination.

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.**

(a) At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $8,640,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held under the Escrow Agreement as security for all Indemnifiable Losses.

(b) The Indemnity Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of any pending and unresolved claims, shall be released to Seller upon the expiration of the period ending 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Period**"). Amounts retained for pending claims shall be released as and when such claims are finally resolved.

(c) Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent as necessary to give effect to any claim resolved under Article VIII. Any dispute between Buyer and Seller concerning a claim against the Indemnity Escrow Amount shall be resolved in accordance with Section 10.8 and Section 10.9.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer (a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers; (b) the written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (c) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (d) evidence of the Required Consents; (e) the certificate described in Section 7.2(c); and (f) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver (a) the payments described in Section 2.2; (b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the certificate described in Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York and has all requisite corporate power to own its assets and conduct its business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations thereunder. The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary action of Seller's managers and members. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are owned of record and beneficially by Seller, free and clear of all liens. There are no options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company. Upon the Closing, Buyer will acquire good and valid title to the Shares.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company; (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Company; or (c) other than with respect to the Required Consents, conflict with, result in a breach of, or give any Person the right to terminate or modify any Material Contract.

**4.5 Consents.** No consent, approval or waiver of any third party, and no filing with or approval of any Governmental Authority, is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement or the consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, other than the Required Consents.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended. Such financial statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party that (a) involves annual payments in excess of $250,000; (b) is an artist engagement, co-promotion or tour agreement with guaranteed payments in excess of $100,000; (c) relates to the occupancy or operation of any venue; or (d) is a ticketing, sponsorship or concession agreement (collectively, "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach thereof.

**4.9 Real Property; Venue.** The Company does not own any real property. The Company occupies and operates the Saltmarsh Pavilion under a license agreement with Kestrel Bay Parks Conservancy (the "**Saltmarsh Pavilion License Agreement**"), and occupies its offices under a lease listed in Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule. The Saltmarsh Pavilion License Agreement is in full force and effect, all license fees due thereunder have been paid, and the Company has not received any notice of default or termination thereunder.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company that would reasonably be expected to result in Losses in excess of $150,000 or to restrain the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws. The Company holds all permits, including liquor, assembly and fire occupancy permits, necessary for the conduct of its business as currently conducted, each of which is in full force and effect.

**4.12 Taxes.** (a) The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects. (b) All Taxes due and owing by the Company have been timely paid. (c) The Company has withheld and remitted all Taxes required to be withheld from amounts paid to performing artists (including nonresident performer withholding in each jurisdiction in which the Company has promoted an event), employees and independent contractors. (d) The Company has collected and remitted all admissions, amusement and sales Taxes required to be collected with respect to ticket sales. (e) No audit or other proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened, and no waiver of any statute of limitations with respect to Taxes is in effect.

**4.13 Employees and Benefits.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in material compliance with its terms and applicable law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement other than those listed in Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization; Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price and to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to any distribution thereof in violation of applicable securities laws.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business relationships with artists, agents, venues, sponsors and ticketing providers. Without Buyer's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld), Seller shall cause the Company not to (a) pay any Artist Deposit other than in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and in an amount not exceeding $500,000 for any single engagement; (b) enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract; (c) incur indebtedness other than borrowings under its existing revolving credit facility in the ordinary course; (d) declare or pay any dividend or distribution; or (e) make or change any Tax election or settle any Tax claim.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and upon reasonable notice, to the Company's properties, books, records and personnel.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the conditions in Article VII to be satisfied. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each of the Required Consents before the Closing.

**6.4 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of three years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) promote, produce or book live music events, or operate any outdoor amphitheater, within the State of New York; or (b) solicit for employment any employee of the Company or induce any artist, agent or sponsor to cease doing business with the Company.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company, except as required by law.

**6.6 Tax Matters.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, and shall submit each such return to Buyer for review and approval not less than 30 days before filing. Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully in connection with any Tax audit or proceeding relating to Pre-Closing Taxes.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any law or order of any Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction (or waiver by Buyer) of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller (other than the Fundamental Representations) shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, and the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying as to the matters in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b);

(d) Seller shall have obtained and delivered to Buyer each of the Required Consents, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each of the items described in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction (or waiver by Seller) of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and (c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer to such effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the longest applicable statute of limitations, and the representations in Section 4.12 shall survive until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations (including extensions). Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes, to the extent not taken into account in the final determination of net working capital under Section 2.3.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**8.4 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price (the "**Indemnity Cap**"). Seller shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses thereunder exceed $432,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(b) The Indemnity Cap shall not apply to Losses arising from Fraud, Fundamental Representations, and breaches of Section 4.12 (Taxes). The aggregate liability of Seller for such Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of Fraud.

(c) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article VIII, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in any representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**8.5 Claims Procedures.** A Buyer Indemnitee seeking indemnification shall deliver written notice to Seller describing the claim in reasonable detail and the estimated amount of Losses. Seller shall have 30 days after receipt to object in writing. If Seller does not object within that period, the claim shall be conclusively deemed an Indemnifiable Loss. Seller may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to Buyer, provided that Seller may not assume the defense of any claim relating to Taxes or seeking injunctive relief against the Company without Buyer's consent.

**8.6 Source of Recovery.** Indemnifiable Losses shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to the extent then available, and thereafter directly by Seller within ten Business Days after final determination.

**8.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the parties; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred on or before June 30, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either party if the other party has materially breached this Agreement and such breach has not been cured within 20 days after written notice.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that Section 6.5 and Article X shall survive and no party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE X. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a party designates by notice).

**10.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**10.3 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the Transaction Documents, such documents shall control in the following order of precedence: Side Letter, then Stock Purchase Agreement, then Escrow Agreement.

**10.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or, after the Closing, collaterally to its lenders.

**10.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article VIII, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.

**10.6 Expenses.** Each party shall bear its own expenses in connection with this Agreement, except that the fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.9 Jurisdiction; Venue.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in such courts. **EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.**

**10.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**10.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
