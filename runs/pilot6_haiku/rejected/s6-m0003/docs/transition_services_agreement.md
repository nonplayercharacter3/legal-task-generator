# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the Closing Date) by and between **Zouleir Industrial Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Pellinor Aqua Chemistry, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"), between Provider, as Seller, and Brouneth Water Holdings, LLC ("**Buyer**"), Buyer is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Recipient from Provider.

B. Before the Closing, Provider and its Affiliates supplied Recipient with enterprise resource planning systems, toll blending capacity and product certification support. After the Closing, Recipient needs those services to continue for a transitional period while it establishes stand-alone capabilities.

C. The Stock Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to deliver this Agreement at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Change Request**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.4.

"**Formulations**" means Recipient's proprietary formulations, recipes, blend sheets, specifications and quality control parameters for its products, including its coagulant, flocculant, corrosion and scale inhibitor and biocide formulations.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information of or relating to Recipient or the Business that is stored, hosted or processed by Provider in performing the Services, including customer master files, customer order histories, pricing and invoicing records, inventory and production records and NSF/ANSI 60 certification files.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule A.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period from the Closing Date through the end date set forth for that Service in Schedule A, subject to Section 3.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as modified under Section 2.4.

"**Transition Managers**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for reference only. References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections and Schedules of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient on the terms of this Agreement and Schedule A.

**2.2 Scope.** Unless Recipient otherwise agrees in writing, the Services shall be substantially the same in nature, scope, volume and priority as the corresponding services Provider and its Affiliates supplied to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Omitted Services.** If, within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Recipient identifies a service that Provider or its Affiliates supplied to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date that is reasonably necessary to operate the Business and is not described in Schedule A, Provider shall provide that service on commercially reasonable terms and at a fee determined on the same basis as comparable Service Fees. The Parties shall document the service as a supplement to Schedule A.

**2.4 Changes.** Either Party may request a change to the scope, volume or timing of a Service by written notice to the other Party's Transition Manager (a "**Change Request**"). Provider shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its agreement to a Change Request from Recipient. Agreed Change Requests and any related adjustment to the Service Fees shall be documented in writing and signed by both Transition Managers.

**2.5 Cooperation; Migration.** Provider shall cooperate in good faith with Recipient and its designees to migrate the Services, including Recipient Data, to Recipient or a third-party provider designated by Recipient, so that the Services can be terminated on or before the end of the applicable Service Period without material disruption to the Business.

**2.6 Recipient Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with information, access and decisions reasonably necessary for Provider to perform the Services, and shall use the Services only for the Business.

## 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Closing Date and continues for 9 months from the Closing Date, unless terminated earlier under this Section 3. Each Service ends at the end of its Service Period.

**3.2 Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. The Service Fees for a terminated Service shall cease as of the effective date of termination, prorated for any partial month. Termination of one Service does not affect any other Service, except to the extent Schedule A identifies a Service as dependent on another.

**3.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. If Recipient fails to pay undisputed Service Fees when due, Provider may terminate only the Service to which the unpaid fees relate, and only after a further fifteen (15) days' written notice following the end of the cure period.

**3.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, (a) Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date; (b) Provider shall deliver to Recipient, in the format reasonably requested by Recipient, a complete copy of all Recipient Data relating to the terminated Service; and (c) after Recipient confirms receipt of that copy, Provider shall delete Recipient Data from its systems, subject to Section 7.4.

**3.5 Survival.** Sections 3.4, 3.5, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees set forth in Schedule A. Except for pass-through costs expressly listed in Schedule A, the Service Fees are the only amounts payable for the Services.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, with reasonable supporting detail by Service.

**4.3 Payment.** Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account Provider designates in writing.

**4.4 Disputes.** Recipient may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith by giving Provider written notice describing the dispute within twenty (20) days after receipt of the invoice. The Transition Managers shall attempt to resolve the dispute under Section 11.1.

**4.5 Taxes.** Service Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Recipient shall pay where applicable. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 5. SERVICE STANDARDS AND GOVERNANCE

**5.1 Standard of Care.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) with at least the same degree of care, skill, timeliness and priority as it supplied the corresponding services to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and in no event less than the care it uses for its own businesses; (b) in compliance with applicable Law; and (c) in the case of the toll blending Services, in accordance with the Formulations, Recipient's specifications and Section 2 of Schedule A.

**5.2 Personnel.** Provider shall assign suitably qualified personnel to perform the Services. Provider's personnel remain employees or contractors of Provider, and Provider is solely responsible for their compensation and benefits.

**5.3 Transition Managers.** Each Party shall appoint a transition manager (each, a "**Transition Manager**") to coordinate the Services and serve as primary contact. The Transition Managers shall meet at least every two weeks during the term, or more often as either reasonably requests.

**5.4 Records; Reporting.** Provider shall keep accurate records of the Services performed and Service Fees charged, including batch records for all toll blending runs, and shall make them available to Recipient on reasonable request.

## 6. SYSTEMS AND DATA

**6.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider acquires no right in Recipient Data except the limited right to use it to perform the Services during the term.

**6.2 Data Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for Recipient Data at least as protective as those it applies to its own comparable data, and in any event consistent with industry standards. Provider shall logically segregate Recipient Data from Provider's own data and restrict access to Recipient Data to personnel who need it to perform the Services.

**6.3 Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or acquisition, loss or disclosure of, Recipient Data, and shall cooperate with Recipient to investigate and remediate the incident.

**6.4 Access to Provider Systems.** Where Recipient personnel access Provider systems in receiving the Services, Recipient shall comply with Provider's reasonable written security policies provided to Recipient in advance.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

**7.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party, or obtained by the other Party, in connection with this Agreement, including, in the case of Recipient, the Formulations and Recipient Data.

**7.2 Obligations.** Each Party shall (a) use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) not disclose it except to its personnel and advisors who need to know it for those purposes and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 7; and (c) protect it with at least reasonable care.

**7.3 Exceptions.** Section 7.2 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (c) is independently developed without use of the other Party's Confidential Information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by Law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

**7.4 Retained Copies.** Provider may retain copies of Recipient Data in automatic backup archives until those archives are overwritten in the ordinary course, provided that retained copies remain subject to this Section 7.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**8.1 Recipient Property.** Recipient retains all right, title and interest in the Formulations, Recipient Data and Recipient's other intellectual property. Recipient grants Provider a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the term to use the Formulations and Recipient Data solely to perform the Services for Recipient.

**8.2 Provider Property.** Provider retains all right, title and interest in its systems, software and other intellectual property used in providing the Services. Provider grants Recipient a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the term to use them solely to receive the Services.

**8.3 Improvements.** Any improvement, modification or derivative of a Formulation made in performing the Services belongs to Recipient, and Provider assigns to Recipient all of its right, title and interest in it.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Exclusion of Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, except for damages arising from a Party's breach of Section 6 or Section 7, its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, or its indemnification obligations under Section 10.

## SCHEDULE A – SERVICES AND SERVICE FEES

### 1. ERP Hosting and Support

**1.1 Description.** Provider shall continue to host, operate and support the instance of Provider's enterprise resource planning system used by Recipient, including order entry, customer master files, customer order histories, pricing, invoicing, accounts receivable and payable, inventory, production planning and general ledger modules, together with help-desk support during Recipient's normal business hours.

**1.2 Data Extraction.** At Recipient's request, Provider shall provide complete extracts of Recipient Data in industry-standard formats to support migration under Section 2.5, including at least one test extract and one final cutover extract.

**1.3 Service Period.** From the Closing Date through the end of the term under Section 3.1.

**1.4 Service Fee.** $38,000 per month.

### 2. Toll Blending

**2.1 Description.** Provider shall blend, package and release for shipment Recipient's coagulant and corrosion and scale inhibitor products at Provider's facility using the Formulations, raw materials meeting Recipient's specifications and Recipient's quality control procedures. Recipient shall submit monthly forecasts and purchase orders at least ten (10) Business Days before the requested production week.

**2.2 Quality.** Provider shall perform the quality control tests specified in the Formulations for each batch, retain samples for twelve (12) months and provide certificates of analysis with each shipment. Provider shall maintain the facility's registration under the NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications for the products it blends.

**2.3 Service Period.** From the Closing Date through the end of the term under Section 3.1.

**2.4 Service Fee.** A tolling charge of $0.11 per pound of finished product, plus raw materials purchased by Provider for Recipient's account at Provider's documented cost without markup, and packaging at Provider's documented cost.

### 3. NSF/ANSI 60 Certification Support

**3.1 Description.** Provider shall assist Recipient in maintaining the NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications and transferring to Recipient, or reissuing in Recipient's name, any NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications held through Provider's facilities or listings. This includes supporting annual audits and unannounced inspections, maintaining formulation disclosures with the certifying body and supplying technical documentation and regulatory affairs personnel.

**3.2 Service Period.** From the Closing Date through the end of the term under Section 3.1.

**3.3 Service Fee.** $12,500 per month, plus certifying body fees passed through at cost.
