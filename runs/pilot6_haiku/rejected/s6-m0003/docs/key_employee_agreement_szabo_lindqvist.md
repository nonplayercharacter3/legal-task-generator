# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026, and becomes effective as of the Closing Date (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Pellinor Aqua Chemistry, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Renata Szabo-Lindqvist**, an individual ("**Executive**"). The Company and Executive are sometimes referred to individually as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"), between Zouleir Industrial Group, Inc. ("**Seller**") and Brouneth Water Holdings, LLC ("**Buyer**"), Buyer has agreed to acquire all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company from Seller.

B. Executive serves as Vice President, Industrial Accounts of the Company and has substantial knowledge of the Company's customers, formulations, pricing and business relationships.

C. The execution and delivery of this Agreement is a condition to Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing under the Stock Purchase Agreement. Executive will receive substantial direct and indirect benefits from the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Stock Purchase Agreement and from her continued employment under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, Executive's continued employment and the other good and valuable consideration set out below, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** For purposes of this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with, such Person. From and after the Closing, Buyer and its Affiliates are Affiliates of the Company.

"**Business**" means the manufacture and sale of coagulants and flocculants for municipal potable water treatment, as conducted by the Company as of the Effective Date.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.1.

"**Customer**" means any Person that purchased products from the Company at any time during the twenty-four (24) months before the Termination Date, and any Person to whom the Company submitted a written bid, quotation or proposal during the twelve (12) months before the Termination Date.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of Executive's employment with the Company and the twenty-four (24) months following the Termination Date.

"**Restricted Territory**" means the United States and Canada.

"**Termination Date**" means the date on which Executive's employment with the Company ends for any reason.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; POSITION AND DUTIES

**2.1 Position.** Effective as of the Effective Date, the Company shall continue to employ Executive, and Executive accepts continued employment, as Vice President, Industrial Accounts, reporting to the President of the Company.

**2.2 Duties.** Executive shall have overall responsibility for the Company's industrial customer accounts, including accounts for cooling-water and boiler corrosion and scale inhibitors, biocides and related treatment programs supplied to refineries, power generation, pulp and paper, food and beverage and other industrial users. Executive shall manage the industrial sales and technical service team, lead account planning and pricing for industrial customers, and perform such other duties consistent with her position as the President may assign from time to time.

**2.3 Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time and attention to the business of the Company. Executive may serve on civic or charitable boards and manage personal investments, provided those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or conflict with Section 7.

**2.4 Place of Performance.** Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, subject to reasonable business travel.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Initial Term.** The term of Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the second (2nd) anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

**3.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Non-renewal by the Company shall be treated as a termination without Cause.

**3.3 Effectiveness.** If the Stock Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void and of no effect, and Executive's employment shall continue on the terms in effect immediately before the date of this Agreement.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $265,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices and subject to annual review for increase (but not decrease).

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** Executive shall be eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of forty percent (40%) of base salary, based on achievement of industrial account revenue and gross margin targets set by the Company after consultation with Executive. Any bonus earned for a fiscal year shall be paid no later than March 15 of the following year, provided Executive is employed on the last day of that fiscal year.

**4.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $200,000, payable in two equal installments on the first and second anniversaries of the Effective Date, subject to Executive's continued employment through each payment date. If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause or Executive resigns for Good Reason before either payment date, any unpaid installments shall be paid within thirty (30) days after the Termination Date, subject to Section 5.5.

**4.4 Benefits.** Executive shall be eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans maintained by the Company for similarly situated senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**4.5 Vacation.** Executive shall be entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid vacation per calendar year, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

## 5. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**5.1 Generally.** Executive's employment may be terminated by the Company with or without Cause, by Executive with or without Good Reason, or upon Executive's death or disability. Except for a termination for Cause, either Party shall give the other at least thirty (30) days' written notice of termination.

**5.2 Cause.** "**Cause**" means Executive's (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony; (b) fraud, embezzlement or willful misappropriation of Company property; (c) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties after written notice and a fifteen (15) day cure period; (d) material breach of Section 6 or Section 7; or (e) willful misconduct that is materially injurious to the Company.

**5.3 Good Reason.** "**Good Reason**" means, without Executive's written consent, (a) a material reduction in base salary; (b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority or duties; or (c) relocation of Executive's principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles; in each case only if Executive gives written notice within sixty (60) days after the initial occurrence, the Company fails to cure within thirty (30) days after notice, and Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**5.4 Accrued Obligations.** Upon any termination, the Company shall pay Executive earned but unpaid base salary, accrued but unused vacation, any bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid, and unreimbursed business expenses.

**5.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause or Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, subject to Executive's execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within sixty (60) days after the Termination Date and her continued compliance with Sections 6 and 7, the Company shall (a) continue Executive's base salary for twelve (12) months after the Termination Date, and (b) reimburse the employer portion of COBRA premiums for Executive and her eligible dependents for up to twelve (12) months.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY; WORK PRODUCT

**6.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of the Company and its Affiliates, including formulations, product specifications, manufacturing and blending processes, NSF/ANSI 60 certification files, customer lists, customer order histories, pricing and margin data, bids and proposals, supplier terms and business plans. Confidential Information does not include information that becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach.

**6.2 Non-Disclosure.** During her employment and at all times thereafter, Executive shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not use or disclose it except in performing her duties for the Company or as required by law, in which case she shall give the Company prompt notice where legally permitted.

**6.3 Return of Property.** Upon the Termination Date, or earlier on the Company's request, Executive shall return to the Company all documents, data and property containing Confidential Information and shall not retain any copies.

**6.4 Work Product.** All inventions, formulations, improvements and works of authorship conceived or developed by Executive within the scope of her employment are the sole property of the Company. Executive assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in them and shall execute any documents reasonably requested to confirm that ownership.

**6.5 Protected Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, Executive shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Non-Competition.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the Restricted Territory, own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, consult for or otherwise provide services to any Person engaged in the Business, other than passive ownership of not more than two percent (2%) of a class of publicly traded securities.

**7.2 Customer Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) solicit, call on or accept business from any Customer for the purpose of selling products or services that compete with the Business, or (b) induce or encourage any Customer to cease or reduce its purchases from the Company of products of the Business.

**7.3 Employee Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire, or attempt to solicit, recruit or hire, any employee of the Company, or induce any employee to leave the Company, except through general advertisements not targeted at Company employees.

**7.4 Non-Disparagement.** Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, Buyer or their respective products, officers or directors, except for truthful statements required by law or in legal proceedings.

**7.5 Acknowledgments.** Executive acknowledges that (a) the covenants in this Section 7 are given in connection with the sale of the Company under the Stock Purchase Agreement and her continued employment; (b) she has had access to, and will continue to receive, Confidential Information and customer goodwill of substantial value; (c) the duration, territory and scope of these covenants are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate interests; and (d) she has had the opportunity to consult counsel of her choosing before signing this Agreement.

**7.6 Tolling.** If Executive violates Section 7.1, 7.2 or 7.3, the Restricted Period for that covenant shall be extended by the period of the violation.

**7.7 Remedies.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 6 or this Section 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Company shall be entitled to injunctive relief, without the posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy. If a court determines that any covenant in this Section 7 is unenforceable as to duration, territory or scope, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to render it enforceable.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. Any payment conditioned on a release that could be paid in either of two calendar years shall be paid in the later year.

**8.2 Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to applicable tax withholding.

**8.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign this Agreement to Buyer, any Affiliate of Buyer or any successor to all or substantially all of the business or assets of the Company, and Executive agrees that the covenants in Sections 6 and 7 inure to the benefit of any such assignee.

**8.4 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the Company at its principal office (attention: President) and to Executive at the most recent address on file with the Company.

**8.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**8.6 Severability.** Subject to Section 7.7, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**8.7 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of law principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**8.8 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state or federal courts sitting in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**8.9 Survival.** Sections 4.3 (as to payments due after termination), 5.4, 5.5, 6, 7 and 8 survive the termination of Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

**8.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
