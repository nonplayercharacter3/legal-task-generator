# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$9,500,000**

**Date of Issuance:** [●], 2026 (the Closing Date)

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Brouneth Water Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Zouleir Industrial Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest described in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**"), on the terms set out below.

This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026, between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**"). Maker is issuing this Note under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement as part of the Base Purchase Price for all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Pellinor Aqua Chemistry, Inc. (the "**Company**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Stock Purchase Agreement.

## 1. PRINCIPAL AMOUNT

**1.1 Principal.** The principal amount of this Note is $9,500,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount may be reduced by any prepayment under Section 3 and by any setoff under Section 4.

**1.2 Purchase Price Component.** The Principal Amount forms part of the Base Purchase Price of $48,500,000 under Section 2.1 of the Stock Purchase Agreement. Each payment of principal under this Note, and each amount set off under Section 4, counts as payment of that portion of the Purchase Price.

## 2. MATURITY; INTEREST; PAYMENTS

**2.1 Maturity.** The entire unpaid Principal Amount, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable in full on the date that is 36 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"), unless accelerated earlier under Section 7.

**2.2 Interest.** Interest accrues on the unpaid Principal Amount from the Date of Issuance until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.3 Interest Payment Dates.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date to occur at least ninety (90) days after the Date of Issuance, and on the Maturity Date.

**2.4 Default Interest.** While an Event of Default under Section 6.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid Principal Amount and any overdue interest accrue interest at the rate in Section 2.2 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

**2.5 Manner of Payment.** Maker shall make all payments under this Note in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account Holder designates in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date. If a payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest accrues for the additional days.

**2.6 Application of Payments.** Payments are applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to the unpaid Principal Amount.

## 3. PREPAYMENT

**3.1 Optional Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five (5) Business Days' written notice to Holder. Each prepayment must include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid.

**3.2 Mandatory Prepayment.** Subject to Section 5, if Maker sells or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of the equity or assets of the Company to a Person that is not an Affiliate of Maker, Maker shall prepay the entire unpaid Principal Amount and accrued interest within ten (10) Business Days after the transfer is completed.

## 4. SETOFF

**4.1 Right of Setoff.** Under Section 9.7 of the Stock Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off against any payment of principal or interest then due or later becoming due under this Note the amount of any indemnification claim under Article IX of the Stock Purchase Agreement that has been finally determined in Maker's favor. "Finally determined" has the meaning given in Section 9.6 of the Stock Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Effect of Setoff.** Each amount set off reduces Maker's outstanding obligations under this Note dollar for dollar, applied first to accrued interest and then to the Principal Amount. Each such amount is treated as paid to Holder for all purposes of this Note and the Stock Purchase Agreement.

**4.3 Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each setoff. The notice must state the amount set off and identify the indemnification claim and the written agreement or judgment by which that claim was finally determined.

**4.4 No Default.** A setoff exercised in good faith under this Section 4 does not constitute a failure to pay or any other Event of Default.

## 5. SUBORDINATION

**5.1 Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated in right of payment to all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries under Maker's senior acquisition financing, including any refinancing or replacement of that financing (the "**Senior Debt**"). Subordination is to the extent and in the manner set out in any subordination agreement entered into among Holder, Maker and the agent or lenders under the Senior Debt (the "**Subordination Agreement**").

**5.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless the Subordination Agreement prohibits it, Maker may make regularly scheduled payments of interest and principal under this Note and payments on the Maturity Date. Any payment blocked under the Subordination Agreement remains owing. Its deferral is not an Event of Default while the block continues.

**5.3 Holder Agreement.** Holder shall execute and deliver the Subordination Agreement and any other instrument reasonably requested by the agent or lenders under the Senior Debt to confirm the subordination in this Section 5. Its terms must be customary for seller subordinated debt.

## 6. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

**6.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder, other than any amount set off under Section 4 or deferred under Section 5.2;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other obligation under this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder;

(c) Maker (i) commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iii) admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(d) an involuntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(e) the Senior Debt is accelerated as a result of an event of default under it, and that acceleration is not rescinded.

**6.2 Notice of Default.** Maker shall notify Holder in writing promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days, after any of its officers becomes aware of an Event of Default.

## 7. REMEDIES

**7.1 Acceleration.** If an Event of Default under Section 6.1(c) or 6.1(d) occurs, the unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without notice or other action. If any other Event of Default occurs and is continuing, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the unpaid Principal Amount and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. In each case Holder's rights are subject to Section 5 and the Subordination Agreement.

**7.2 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

**7.3 Remedies Cumulative.** Holder's rights and remedies under this Note are cumulative and in addition to any other rights available at law or in equity, subject to Section 5.

## 8. MISCELLANEOUS

**8.1 Notices.** All notices under this Note must be in writing. They must be delivered in the manner, and are deemed given at the times, provided in Section 11.2 of the Stock Purchase Agreement, to the addresses of Maker and Holder set out there (or another address a party designates by notice).

**8.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Note, and any claim, controversy or dispute relating to this Note, including any payment, offset or acceleration, is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the laws of another jurisdiction to apply. Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit exclusively to the state or federal courts in Wilmington, Delaware for any suit, action or proceeding relating to this Note, including any payment, offset or acceleration. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**8.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

**8.4 Waivers by Maker.** Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest, except as expressly provided in this Note.

**8.5 Transfer.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without Maker's prior written consent. Any transferee takes this Note subject to Sections 4 and 5. Maker may collaterally assign its rights under this Note to the agent or lenders under the Senior Debt.

**8.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of it.

**8.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**8.8 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor. Before doing so, Maker is entitled to receive a customary affidavit of loss and indemnity from Holder. If the Note is mutilated, Holder must also surrender it.

**8.9 Successors.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and assigns. It benefits Holder and its permitted successors and assigns.

**8.10 Electronic Signature.** This Note may be executed and delivered by electronic signature or electronic transmission. A signature delivered that way is effective as an original.
