# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**" or the "**Stock Purchase Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026, by and between **Zouleir Industrial Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Brouneth Water Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are sometimes referred to individually as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Pellinor Aqua Chemistry, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company is engaged in the Business.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** The following terms have the meanings specified or referred to in this Article I:

"**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries, controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with, such Person. After the Closing, the Company is an Affiliate of Buyer and not of Seller.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Seller Note, the Transition Services Agreement and the Key Employee Agreement.

"**Business**" means the manufacture and sale of municipal and industrial water treatment chemicals, including coagulants, flocculants, corrosion and scale inhibitors and biocides, as conducted by the Company as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Closing Cash Payment**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.2(a).

"**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company (excluding cash and income Tax assets) less the current liabilities of the Company (excluding Indebtedness and income Tax liabilities), in each case as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date and determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles set forth in Schedule 2.3.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization and Authority), 4.2 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and 4.9 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, regulatory or administrative authority, court or arbitral body thereof.

"**Indebtedness**" means, without duplication, all obligations of the Company for borrowed money, obligations evidenced by notes, bonds or similar instruments, capital lease obligations, and all accrued interest, prepayment premiums and penalties thereon.

"**Key Employee**" means Renata Szabo-Lindqvist, Vice President, Industrial Accounts of the Company.

"**Key Employee Agreement**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.8.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, occurrence or development that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, results of operations, condition (financial or otherwise) or assets of the Company, taken as a whole, other than effects arising from general economic conditions, conditions generally affecting the water treatment chemicals industry, changes in Law or GAAP, or the announcement of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except to the extent such effects disproportionately affect the Company.

"**NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications**" means the certifications held by or on behalf of the Company under NSF/ANSI/CAN Standard 60 (Drinking Water Treatment Chemicals – Health Effects) for the Company's products.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the principal amount of $9,500,000 to be issued by Buyer to Seller at the Closing pursuant to Section 2.2(b).

"**Target Working Capital**" means $6,200,000.

"**Tax**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, sales, use, franchise, property, payroll, withholding and other taxes, together with any interest and penalties thereon.

"**Transaction Documents**" means this Agreement, the Ancillary Agreements and the other agreements, instruments and certificates delivered pursuant to this Agreement.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Seller, as Provider, and the Company, as Recipient, covering ERP hosting, toll blending and NSF/ANSI 60 certification support, for a term of 9 months from the Closing Date.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation. References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to Articles, Sections and Schedules of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, pledges, security interests and other encumbrances, for an aggregate purchase price of $48,500,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3 (as so adjusted, the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** The Base Purchase Price shall be paid as follows:

(a) at the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date an amount equal to $39,000,000, (i) minus Closing Indebtedness, (ii) plus or minus the amount, if any, by which estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of Target Working Capital, as shown on the estimated closing statement delivered under Section 2.3(a) (the "**Closing Cash Payment**"); and

(b) at the Closing, Buyer shall execute and deliver to Seller the Seller Note in the principal amount of $9,500,000, maturing 36 months after the Closing Date, which shall be subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing and subject to setoff as provided in Section 9.7.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good faith estimated closing statement setting forth estimated Closing Working Capital and Closing Indebtedness.

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a closing statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital and Closing Indebtedness. Seller shall have thirty (30) days to deliver a written notice of objection. Any disputed items not resolved by the Parties within thirty (30) days after delivery of an objection notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually acceptable to the Parties, acting as an expert and not as an arbitrator, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) If the Purchase Price as finally determined exceeds the amount determined using the estimated closing statement, Buyer shall pay the difference to Seller in cash; if it is less, Seller shall pay the difference to Buyer in cash, in each case within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers; (b) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) the Key Employee Agreement, duly executed by the Company and the Key Employee; (d) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness; (e) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; and (f) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the Closing Cash Payment to Seller; (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by the Company; and (d) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Transaction Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations thereunder.

**4.2 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all encumbrances. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not conflict with Seller's or the Company's organizational documents, any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or any Material Contract, and require no consent of any Governmental Authority other than as listed in Section 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows have been prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates.

**4.5 Material Contracts.** Section 4.5 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract of the Company involving annual payments in excess of $250,000, each municipal supply contract, each toll manufacturing or toll blending arrangement and each contract with Seller or any of its Affiliates (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.6 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has the valid right to use all formulations, processes, trade secrets and other intellectual property used in the Business, including all proprietary coagulant and inhibitor formulations. The Company has taken commercially reasonable measures to protect the confidentiality of its trade secrets.

**4.7 Environmental Matters; Product Certifications.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in material compliance with all environmental Laws. Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications, each of which is in full force and effect, and no certifying body has notified the Company of any suspension, withdrawal or material nonconformity.

**4.8 Employees.** Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee of the Company with annual base compensation exceeding $150,000. To Seller's knowledge, no such employee, including the Key Employee, has given notice of an intention to terminate employment.

**4.9 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Transaction Documents to which it is a party.

**5.2 Investment Purpose.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient cash, available lines of credit or other sources of immediately available funds to pay the Closing Cash Payment.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization, NSF/ANSI 60 Certifications, and relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, initiate or engage in discussions regarding any acquisition of the Company or the Business by any Person other than Buyer.

**6.4 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, including making any required filings with Governmental Authorities.

**6.5 Intercompany Arrangements.** At or before the Closing, Seller shall cause all intercompany balances and contracts between the Company, on the one hand, and Seller and its Affiliates, on the other hand, to be settled and terminated, other than the Transition Services Agreement.

**6.6 Public Announcements.** Neither Party shall issue any press release or public announcement regarding this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by Law.

**6.7 Tax Matters.** Seller shall prepare and file all income Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date. Seller shall be responsible for all Taxes of the Company attributable to periods ending on or before the Closing Date.

**6.8 Key Employee Agreement.** As a condition to Buyer's obligation to close, Seller shall cause the Company and the Key Employee to enter into, effective as of the Closing, a key employee agreement in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer (the "**Key Employee Agreement**"), which shall include a non-competition covenant and a customer non-solicitation covenant, each with respect to the Business and each for a period of 24 months following the termination of the Key Employee's employment.

**6.9 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation; Confidentiality.**

(a) For a period of 5 years after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (i) engage in the Business anywhere in the United States or Canada, (ii) own, manage, operate or control any Person engaged in the Business, other than passive ownership of not more than two percent (2%) of a class of publicly traded securities, or (iii) solicit or induce any customer of the Company to cease or reduce its business with the Company.

(b) During the Restricted Period, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, solicit for employment or hire any employee of the Company, except through general solicitations not targeted at the Company's employees.

(c) From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business, including the Company's formulations, processes, customer lists and order histories, except to the extent required to perform the Transition Services Agreement or as required by Law.

(d) Seller acknowledges that a breach of this Section 6.9 would cause Buyer irreparable harm and that Buyer shall be entitled to injunctive relief, without the posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy. If any covenant in this Section 6.9 is held unenforceable as to duration or scope, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by Law.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Mutual Conditions.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligations of Buyer are further subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed before the Closing; (c) Buyer's receipt of a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying the matters in clauses (a) and (b); (d) the absence of any Material Adverse Effect; (e) the execution and delivery of the Key Employee Agreement; and (f) delivery of the items listed in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligations of Seller are further subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed before the Closing; (c) Seller's receipt of a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the matters in clauses (a) and (b); and (d) delivery of the items listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by either Party if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, provided the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; (c) by Buyer if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except for Sections 6.6 and Article XI, and except that no termination shall relieve a Party of liability for any willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that Fundamental Representations survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective representatives from and against all Losses arising out of: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller; (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller; (c) any Closing Indebtedness not paid at or before the Closing; and (d) any Taxes for which Seller is responsible under Section 6.7.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its representatives from and against all Losses arising out of: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer; and (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer.

**9.4 Limitations.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for Fundamental Representations or fraud) (a) until aggregate Losses exceed $485,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount, or (b) for aggregate Losses in excess of $7,275,000. Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

**9.5 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

**9.6 Final Determination.** An indemnification claim is "**finally determined**" when (a) the Parties agree in writing to the amount payable, or (b) a court of competent jurisdiction has entered a final, non-appealable judgment as to the amount payable.

**9.7 Right of Setoff.** Buyer may set off the amount of any indemnification claim under this Article IX that has been finally determined in Buyer's favor against any payment of principal or interest then due or thereafter becoming due under the Seller Note. Any amount so set off shall reduce the outstanding obligations under the Seller Note dollar-for-dollar and shall be treated as paid to Seller for all purposes of this Agreement and the Seller Note. The exercise of this right in good faith shall not constitute a default under the Seller Note.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under Section 2.3, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties with respect to this Agreement after the Closing.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** Indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. FURTHER ASSURANCES

**10.1 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each Party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Transaction Documents.

**10.2 Post-Closing Cooperation.** For a period of six (6) years after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing and to afford Seller reasonable access to them for Tax and financial reporting purposes.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages (or such other address as a Party designates by notice).

**11.3 Severability.** If any term of this Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that holding shall not affect any other term, and this Agreement shall be reformed to effect the original intent of the Parties as closely as possible.

**11.4 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, each Ancillary Agreement prevails over the Stock Purchase Agreement as to that Ancillary Agreement's subject matter.

**11.5 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may collaterally assign its rights to its financing sources and may assign its rights to any Affiliate or to any subsequent purchaser of all or substantially all of the Company's equity or assets.

**11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.7 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.8 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that the Parties shall be entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**11.9 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any claim, controversy or dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Transaction Documents, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.10 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Transaction Documents shall be instituted exclusively in the state or federal courts sitting in New York County, New York, and each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of such courts and waives any objection based on improper venue or inconvenient forum.

**11.11 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTION DOCUMENTS.

**11.12 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one agreement. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are effective as originals.
