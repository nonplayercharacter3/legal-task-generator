# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**QA Effective Date**") by and between **Sumeith Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Halvorsen Parkway, Suite 300, Durham, North Carolina 27703 ("**Supplier**"), and **Glakin Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Corliss Avenue, Rochester, New York 14607 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. Section 7.3 of the Distribution Agreement provides that the Parties will perform their obligations under a quality agreement. That quality agreement sets out their respective responsibilities for product quality, complaint handling, adverse event reporting, change control and Recalls.

C. This Quality Agreement is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in the Distribution Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Distribution Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement, including "Analyzers," "Cartridges," "Customer," "Products," "Recall," "Regulatory Authority," "Specifications," "Term" and "Territory."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Adverse Event**" means any event involving a Product that has resulted, or may result, in injury to or death of an animal or a person, or any incorrect diagnostic result reported as having contributed to an adverse clinical outcome.

(b) "**Certificate of Analysis**" or "**CoA**" means a document issued by Supplier for each lot of Cartridges and QC material confirming that the lot meets the Specifications.

(c) "**Complaint**" means any written, electronic or oral communication alleging a deficiency in the identity, quality, durability, reliability, performance, safety, labeling or packaging of a Product.

(d) "**Field Action**" means any Recall, field correction, software or firmware correction, or Customer advisory notice issued for a Product.

(e) "**Nonconformity**" means any failure of a Product to conform to the Specifications, the labeling, applicable law or this Quality Agreement.

(f) "**Quality Contact**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 2.2.

(g) "**Storage Conditions**" means the storage and transport conditions for each Product stated in its labeling and in Schedule 1.

1.3 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products that Supplier supplies to Distributor under the Distribution Agreement, as listed in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement.

## 2. Quality Systems and Contacts

2.1 **Supplier Quality System.** Supplier shall maintain a documented quality management system for the design, manufacture, testing, labeling, packaging and release of the Products. That system shall be certified to ISO 13485:2016 or an equivalent standard reasonably acceptable to Distributor. Supplier shall notify Distributor within ten (10) business days after any suspension, withdrawal or material adverse finding affecting that certification.

2.2 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact responsible for quality matters under this Quality Agreement. Each Party shall notify the other of its Quality Contact on the QA Effective Date and of any replacement within five (5) business days. The initial Quality Contacts are listed in Schedule 2.

2.3 **Distributor Quality Procedures.** Distributor shall maintain written procedures for the receipt, storage, handling, transport, traceability, Complaint intake and Field Action support of Products. Those procedures shall be sufficient to meet its obligations under Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement and this Quality Agreement.

2.4 **Responsibility Matrix.** Schedule 3 summarizes the Parties' respective responsibilities. If Schedule 3 conflicts with the body of this Quality Agreement, the body governs.

## 3. Manufacturing, Release and Documentation

3.1 **Manufacture and Release.** Supplier is solely responsible for manufacturing, testing and releasing each lot of Products in accordance with the Specifications, its quality management system and applicable law. Supplier shall not ship any lot that has not been released by its quality unit.

3.2 **Certificates of Analysis.** Supplier shall provide a CoA for each lot of Cartridges and SDX-HA200-QC material shipped to Distributor, either with the shipment or electronically before the shipment arrives.

3.3 **Analyzer Documentation.** Supplier shall ship each Analyzer with its serial number label, a final inspection record, its instructions for use and its current software and firmware version identification.

3.4 **Labeling.** Supplier is responsible for the content and accuracy of all Product labeling, instructions for use and package inserts, under Section 7.1 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall not relabel, repackage, overlabel or alter any Product or its labeling.

3.5 **Shelf Life.** Cartridges shall have at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life upon delivery, as required by Section 6.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 4. Storage, Handling and Transport

4.1 **Supplier Packaging.** Supplier shall pack and ship Cartridges and QC material in validated temperature-controlled packaging that maintains the Storage Conditions for not less than seventy-two (72) hours. Each shipment shall include a temperature indicator or data logger.

4.2 **Receipt.** On receipt of each shipment, Distributor shall check the temperature indicator or data logger. Distributor shall quarantine any shipment showing a temperature excursion and notify Supplier's Quality Contact within two (2) business days. Supplier shall decide on the disposition of the quarantined Products within five (5) business days after receiving the excursion data. Excursions occurring before delivery to the carrier under Section 6.1 of the Distribution Agreement are treated as Nonconformities under Section 6.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

4.3 **Distributor Storage.** Distributor shall store Products in accordance with the Storage Conditions in temperature-monitored areas with calibrated monitoring equipment and alarm notification. It shall keep temperature records for each storage area.

4.4 **Distributor Transport.** Distributor shall ship Cartridges and QC material to Customers using validated temperature-controlled packaging or qualified cold-chain carriers that maintain the Storage Conditions. Distributor remains responsible for compliance with this Section 4.4 when it uses third-party carriers.

4.5 **Stock Rotation.** Distributor shall distribute Cartridges and QC material on a first-expiry, first-out basis. It shall not ship any Cartridge to a Customer with less than ninety (90) days of remaining shelf life unless the Customer agrees in writing.

## 5. Traceability and Records

5.1 **Traceability.** Distributor shall keep records identifying, for each Product shipped: the Customer, the Customer's shipping address, the date of shipment, the quantity and, as applicable, the Analyzer serial number or the Cartridge and QC lot number. Distributor shall be able to produce those records to Supplier within one (1) business day after a request made in connection with a Field Action and within five (5) business days otherwise.

5.2 **Installation Records.** Distributor shall keep installation checklists and installation dates for each Analyzer, as reported under Section 4.3 of the Statement of Work.

5.3 **Retention.** Each Party shall keep the records required by this Quality Agreement for the longer of (a) five (5) years after the date of the record and (b) two (2) years after the expiration date of the last Product lot to which the record relates.

## 6. Complaints and Adverse Events

6.1 **Complaint Forwarding.** Distributor shall forward each Complaint to Supplier's Quality Contact within two (2) business days after receipt. Distributor shall use Supplier's complaint intake form or an agreed electronic equivalent and include all information reasonably available to it, including the Product code, the lot or serial number, the Customer contact and a description of the event.

6.2 **Adverse Event Forwarding.** Distributor shall forward each Adverse Event to Supplier's Quality Contact by telephone and email within twenty-four (24) hours after receipt.

6.3 **Supplier Investigation.** Supplier is responsible for investigating all Complaints and Adverse Events, determining root cause, implementing corrective and preventive action, and making any report to a Regulatory Authority. Supplier shall give Distributor a written summary of the investigation outcome for each Complaint within thirty (30) days after receipt, or a status update if the investigation remains open.

6.4 **Returned Product.** Distributor shall use reasonable efforts to obtain from Customers any Product or sample relevant to a Complaint and shall send it to Supplier at Supplier's expense, in accordance with Supplier's return instructions.

6.5 **First-Line Support.** Distributor's certified technicians may provide first-line troubleshooting to Customers as described in Section 3.2 of the Statement of Work. Each support contact that discloses a Complaint is forwarded under Section 6.1.

## 7. Change Control

7.1 **Notified Changes.** Supplier shall give Distributor not less than ninety (90) days' prior written notice of any change that may affect the form, fit, function, performance, Specifications, Storage Conditions, shelf life, labeling or regulatory status of a Product. This includes changes to manufacturing site, critical components or raw materials, and Analyzer software or firmware that changes Analyzer operation.

7.2 **Urgent Changes.** If a change is required to address a safety issue or a requirement of a Regulatory Authority, Supplier may implement it on shorter notice. In that case Supplier shall notify Distributor as soon as practicable.

7.3 **Implementation Support.** For each notified change, Supplier shall provide updated labeling, training materials and, where relevant, the training described in Section 3.3 of the Statement of Work, before the changed Product is shipped.

## 8. Audits and Regulatory Inspections

8.1 **Distributor Audit Right.** Distributor may audit Supplier's quality management system and the facilities where Products are manufactured or stored, on thirty (30) days' prior written notice, during normal business hours. Distributor may conduct one such audit per Contract Year, plus additional for-cause audits following a Field Action or a significant Nonconformity.

8.2 **Supplier Audit Right.** Supplier may audit Distributor's storage, handling, traceability and Complaint procedures and facilities on the same terms as Section 8.1.

8.3 **Findings.** The audited Party shall respond in writing to audit findings within thirty (30) days with a corrective action plan and shall carry out that plan within the agreed timeframe.

8.4 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall notify the other within two (2) business days after learning of any inspection, inquiry or communication from a Regulatory Authority that relates to the Products. Each Party shall give the other a copy of any related written observations, to the extent permitted by law.

## 9. Recalls and Field Actions

9.1 **Decision.** Supplier decides whether to initiate a Field Action, except where a Regulatory Authority requires one, in accordance with Section 9.1 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall promptly notify Supplier of any information indicating that a Field Action may be warranted.

9.2 **Notice and Conduct.** Supplier shall notify Distributor's Quality Contact of any Field Action within one (1) business day after the decision to initiate it. Supplier shall provide the affected lots or serial numbers, the Customer communication and the instructions for the Field Action. Supplier shall consult with Distributor on its conduct.

9.3 **Distributor Support.** Distributor shall, as Supplier reasonably directs:

(a) identify affected Customers from its records under Section 5.1;

(b) quarantine affected Products in its inventory;

(c) deliver Supplier's Customer communications;

(d) retrieve affected Products from Customers or support on-site correction; and

(e) document and report reconciliation of affected Products to Supplier.

9.4 **Recall Costs and Liability.** Supplier shall bear the reasonable recall costs of each Field Action, including the cost of replacement Products, shipping, Customer communications and Distributor's reasonable out-of-pocket and personnel costs in supporting the Field Action under Section 9.3, except to the extent the Field Action results from Distributor's breach of Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement or Section 4 of this Quality Agreement. Supplier's liability for recall costs and for Nonconformities under this Quality Agreement is subject to Section 11.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall submit its recall cost claims with reasonable supporting documentation, and Supplier shall pay or credit them within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

9.5 **Mock Recalls.** Once per Contract Year, at either Party's request, the Parties shall conduct a mock traceability exercise to confirm that Distributor can identify affected Customers within the time required by Section 5.1.

## 10. Nonconforming Product

10.1 **Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other's Quality Contact within two (2) business days after identifying any Nonconformity in Products in Distributor's possession or in the field.

10.2 **Disposition.** Distributor shall quarantine nonconforming Products pending Supplier's disposition instructions. Distributor's remedies for nonconforming shipments are as set out in Sections 6.4 and 8.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 11. Term and General

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement begins on the QA Effective Date and remains in effect for the Term of the Distribution Agreement and for as long afterward as Distributor holds or sells Products under Section 16.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Sections 5, 6, 9 and 11 survive expiration or termination.

11.2 **Review.** The Quality Contacts shall review this Quality Agreement at least once every two (2) Contract Years and propose any updates in writing.

11.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Quality Contacts may update Schedules 1 and 2 by written notice countersigned by both Quality Contacts.

11.4 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Section 19.2 of the Distribution Agreement applies to any action arising out of it.

11.5 **Notices.** Operational notices under this Quality Agreement may be given by email between Quality Contacts. All other notices shall be given under Section 18.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
