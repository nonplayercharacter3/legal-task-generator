# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between **Sumeith Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Halvorsen Parkway, Suite 300, Durham, North Carolina 27703 ("**Supplier**"), and **Glakin Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Corliss Avenue, Rochester, New York 14607 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. On July 20, 2026, Supplier delivered to Distributor an Annual Price Adjustment Notice under Section 5.4 of the Distribution Agreement. The notice provides for an increase of nine percent (9%) in the transfer prices for the Cartridges listed in Exhibit B (product codes SDX-RC25 and SDX-RC100), effective October 1, 2026.

C. Since the Effective Date, Distributor has expanded its warehousing and fulfillment network in the Territory, and Regulatory Authorities have increased their oversight of the storage and distribution of veterinary diagnostic products.

D. The Parties wish to amend the Distribution Agreement to set out Distributor's regulatory responsibilities in more detail and to allocate responsibility for related claims, as provided in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement.

1.2 **New Definitions.** Section 1.1 of the Distribution Agreement is amended by adding the following definitions at the end of that Section:

> (s) "**Distribution Licenses**" means all licenses, registrations, permits and authorizations required under applicable federal, state or local law for Distributor to receive, store, handle, transport, market or sell the Products in each jurisdiction in the Territory in which it does so.
>
> (t) "**Regulatory Claims**" means any claim, demand, inquiry, investigation, inspection observation, warning letter, untitled letter, notice of violation, seizure, detention, administrative or judicial proceeding, fine, penalty or other enforcement action brought or initiated by a Regulatory Authority or any other governmental authority, and any third-party claim alleging a violation of law, in each case relating to the Products.

## 2. Distributor Regulatory Responsibilities

2.1 **Amendment of Section 7.2.** Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> 7.2 **Distributor Responsibilities.** Distributor shall:
>
> (a) store, handle and transport Products in accordance with the storage conditions in the labeling and the Quality Agreement;
>
> (b) maintain records sufficient to trace each lot of Products to the Customer;
>
> (c) obtain and maintain in good standing all Distribution Licenses, and provide copies of them to Supplier within ten (10) business days after Supplier's written request;
>
> (d) notify Supplier within five (5) business days after any suspension, revocation, non-renewal or material restriction of a Distribution License; and
>
> (e) comply with applicable laws in its marketing and sale of the Products.

2.2 **Distribution Facilities.** Within thirty (30) days after the Amendment Date, Distributor shall give Supplier a written list of each facility at which it stores Products, with the address and the Distribution Licenses held for that facility. Distributor shall update the list in writing within thirty (30) days after opening or closing any such facility.

## 3. Regulatory Indemnity and Limitation of Liability

3.1 **New Section 12.3.** Section 12.3 of the Distribution Agreement ("[Reserved.]") is deleted and replaced with the following:

> 12.3 **Regulatory Indemnity.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to any Regulatory Claims relating to Distributor's storage, handling, marketing or sale of Products, including any failure to obtain or maintain a Distribution License. Section 12.4 applies to claims under this Section 12.3.

3.2 **Amendment of Section 11.3.** Section 11.3 of the Distribution Agreement is amended by deleting the word "or" at the end of clause (d), replacing the period at the end of clause (e) with "; or", and adding the following new clause (f):

> (f) Distributor's obligations under Section 12.3.

3.3 **Insurance.** Section 14.1 of the Distribution Agreement is amended by adding the following sentence at the end of that Section: "Distributor shall cause Supplier to be named as an additional insured under Distributor's commercial general liability policy."

## 4. General

4.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Distribution Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the Amendment Date, each reference in the Distribution Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Distribution Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

4.2 **Amendment under Section 19.5.** This Amendment is an "Amendment" for purposes of Sections 19.4 and 19.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

4.3 **Governing Law and Venue.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Section 19.2 of the Distribution Agreement applies to any action arising out of this Amendment.

4.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Amendment as of the Amendment Date.
