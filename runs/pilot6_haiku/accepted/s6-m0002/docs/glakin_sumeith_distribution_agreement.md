# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Sumeith Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4100 Halvorsen Parkway, Suite 300, Durham, North Carolina 27703 ("**Supplier**"), and **Glakin Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Corliss Avenue, Rochester, New York 14607 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells in-clinic veterinary point-of-care hematology analyzers and the reagent cartridges used with those analyzers.

B. Distributor distributes veterinary diagnostic and animal health products to veterinary clinics and hospitals.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor, and Distributor wishes to be appointed, as Supplier's distributor of the Products in the Territory on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

(b) "**Analyzers**" means Supplier's in-clinic veterinary hematology analyzers listed in Exhibit A, including any accessories, software and firmware supplied with them.

(c) "**Cartridges**" means Supplier's reagent cartridges for use with the Analyzers listed in Exhibit A.

(d) "**Contract Year**" means each successive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or an anniversary of it.

(e) "**Customer**" means a veterinary clinic, hospital or practice in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor.

(f) "**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

(g) "**Losses**" means all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

(h) "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**" has the meaning given in Section 4.3.

(i) "**Products**" means the Analyzers and the Cartridges, as listed in Exhibit A and as updated by the Parties in writing.

(j) "**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Products issued by Distributor under Section 4.1.

(k) "**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between the Parties relating to the Products, entered into on or about the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

(l) "**Recall**" means any recall, market withdrawal, field correction, stock recovery or customer notification relating to Products, whether voluntary or required by a Regulatory Authority.

(m) "**Regulatory Authority**" means any federal, state or local governmental authority with jurisdiction over the manufacture, labeling, marketing, distribution or sale of the Products, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration Center for Veterinary Medicine and the U.S. Department of Agriculture.

(n) "**Renewal Term**" means a period of five (5) years beginning on the day after the expiration of the Initial Term or the preceding Renewal Term, as applicable.

(o) "**Specifications**" means Supplier's published specifications for each Product in effect at the time of shipment, as further described in the Quality Agreement.

(p) "**Statement of Work**" means the Statement of Work for the hematology product launch entered into by the Parties under this Agreement.

(q) "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with all Renewal Terms.

(r) "**Territory**" means the United States of America, including its territories and possessions.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for four (4) years from the Distribution Agreement Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

2.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party not earlier than 120 days and not later than 90 days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. A notice of non-renewal delivered outside that period has no effect. Each Renewal Term is on the terms of this Agreement then in effect, including Section 4.3.

## 3. Appointment and Exclusivity

3.1 **Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts the appointment.

3.2 **Supplier Restriction.** During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or sales representative for the Products in the Territory, and shall not itself sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except for (a) accounts identified in the Statement of Work as Supplier house accounts and (b) sales to Distributor.

3.3 **Distributor Non-Competition.** During the Term, Distributor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly manufacture, market, promote, distribute or sell any veterinary diagnostic instrument, reagent, test kit or reference-laboratory service, other than the Products, anywhere in North America.

3.4 **Sales Outside the Territory.** Distributor shall not actively market or solicit sales of the Products outside the Territory, and shall refer to Supplier any inquiry it receives from outside the Territory.

3.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Distributor buys Products for its own account and resells them at prices it determines in its sole discretion.

## 4. Orders and Minimum Purchases

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier stating the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery locations. Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) business days after receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.2 **Forecasts.** By the tenth (10th) day of each calendar month, Distributor shall provide Supplier a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its Product requirements. The first three (3) months of each forecast are firm and Distributor shall issue Purchase Orders consistent with them.

4.3 **Minimum Purchase Commitment.** In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate invoiced value of not less than the amount stated for that Contract Year in Exhibit C (the "**Minimum Purchase Commitment**"). For each Contract Year in any Renewal Term, the Minimum Purchase Commitment equals the Minimum Purchase Commitment for the immediately preceding Contract Year increased by five percent (5%).

4.4 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Commitment for a Contract Year, Distributor shall, within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year, either (a) pay Supplier an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of the shortfall, or (b) submit Purchase Orders for Products sufficient to cure the shortfall for delivery within the following ninety (90) days. Supplier's remedies under this Section 4.4 and Section 15.2 are its sole remedies for a shortfall.

4.5 **Order Terms.** The terms of this Agreement govern each Purchase Order. Any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 5. Pricing and Payment

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the transfer prices for the Products set out in Exhibit B, as adjusted under Section 5.4.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each order.

5.3 **Payment.** Undisputed invoices are due within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, before the due date, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute. Late undisputed amounts bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

5.4 **Annual Price Adjustment.** Supplier may adjust the transfer prices in Exhibit B once per Contract Year by written notice to Distributor (an "**Annual Price Adjustment Notice**") stating the adjusted prices and their effective date, which shall be no earlier than sixty (60) days after delivery of the notice. Distributor may object to the adjustment by written notice to Supplier delivered within 45 days after Distributor's receipt of a price adjustment notice. If Distributor timely objects, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days, and the prices then in effect continue to apply until the Parties agree in writing. If Distributor does not timely object, the adjustment is deemed accepted and Exhibit B is amended accordingly as of the stated effective date.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use, excise and similar taxes. Distributor is responsible for those taxes, except taxes based on Supplier's income. Distributor shall provide valid resale certificates where applicable.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 **Delivery Terms.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA (Incoterms 2020) Supplier's distribution facility in Durham, North Carolina, to the carrier designated by Distributor. Cartridges shall be packed and shipped in validated temperature-controlled packaging in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor upon delivery to the carrier under Section 6.1.

6.3 **Delivery Dates.** Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the delivery dates in accepted Purchase Orders and shall promptly notify Distributor of any expected delay. Cartridges shall have at least nine (9) months of remaining shelf life upon delivery.

6.4 **Inspection.** Distributor may reject any shipment of Products that does not conform to the Specifications by written notice to Supplier within thirty (30) days after receipt, or, for latent defects, within thirty (30) days after discovery. Supplier shall, at its option and expense, replace the nonconforming Products or credit Distributor for them.

## 7. Regulatory and Quality

7.1 **Supplier Responsibilities.** Supplier is responsible for (a) the design, manufacture, testing and release of the Products, (b) all Product labeling, instructions for use and package inserts, and (c) maintaining any registrations, listings or authorizations required for the Products in the Territory.

7.2 **Distributor Responsibilities.** Distributor shall store, handle and transport Products in accordance with the storage conditions in the labeling and the Quality Agreement, maintain records sufficient to trace each lot of Products to the Customer, and comply with applicable laws in its marketing and sale of the Products.

7.3 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties shall perform their obligations under the Quality Agreement, which sets out their respective responsibilities for product quality, complaint handling, adverse event reporting, change control and Recalls.

7.4 **Complaints and Adverse Events.** Distributor shall forward to Supplier any Product complaint or adverse event report within the time periods set out in the Quality Agreement.

## 8. Warranties

8.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that (a) each Analyzer will conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months after installation at the Customer site, and (b) each Cartridge will conform to the Specifications through its labeled expiration date.

8.2 **Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that the Products will be manufactured, labeled and packaged in compliance with applicable laws and the Quality Agreement, and will not be adulterated or misbranded when delivered.

8.3 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and that doing so will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

8.4 **Remedy.** For breach of Section 8.1, Supplier shall at its option repair or replace the nonconforming Product or refund or credit its price. This Section 8.4 does not limit Supplier's obligations under Sections 9 or 12.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS SET OUT IN THIS SECTION 8, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. Recalls

9.1 **Recall Decisions.** Supplier decides whether to initiate a Recall, except where a Regulatory Authority requires one. Supplier shall notify Distributor promptly of any Recall and consult with Distributor on its conduct.

9.2 **Cooperation.** Distributor shall cooperate in any Recall, including by identifying affected Customers from its traceability records and communicating with them as Supplier reasonably directs.

9.3 **Recall Costs.** Supplier shall bear, and shall reimburse Distributor for, all reasonable costs of any Recall, including the cost of replacement Products, shipping, customer communications and Distributor's reasonable out-of-pocket and personnel costs, except to the extent the Recall results from Distributor's breach of Section 7.2.

## 10. Trademarks and Intellectual Property

10.1 **License.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products, in accordance with Supplier's reasonable brand guidelines.

10.2 **Ownership.** Supplier retains all rights in its trademarks, the Products and their software and documentation. Distributor shall not alter or remove any Product labeling or markings.

10.3 **Software.** Analyzer software is licensed, not sold, to Customers under Supplier's end-user license terms, which Distributor shall pass through to Customers.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

11.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Distributor to Supplier under this Agreement in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 12;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 13;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct;

(d) Supplier's liability for Recalls, including under Section 9.3; or

(e) Supplier's breach of its quality obligations under Section 8.2, Section 7.1 or the Quality Agreement.

## 12. Indemnification

12.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) any defect in the design, manufacture or labeling of a Product, (b) any claim that a Product infringes or misappropriates a third party's intellectual property rights, or (c) Supplier's breach of this Agreement or negligence.

12.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Distributor's breach of this Agreement, (b) any warranty or representation about the Products made by Distributor beyond those in Supplier's labeling, or (c) Distributor's negligence or willful misconduct.

12.4 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of the claim (provided that delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent prejudiced), give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement that is marked or reasonably understood to be confidential, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by similar obligations.

13.2 **Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose information required by law or a Regulatory Authority, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

13.3 **Duration.** This Section 13 survives for five (5) years after the end of the Term, and for trade secrets for so long as they remain trade secrets.

## 14. Insurance

14.1 Each Party shall maintain, during the Term and for two (2) years afterward, commercial general liability insurance (including products and completed operations coverage) of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 15. Termination

15.1 **Material Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after receiving written notice describing it.

15.2 **Minimum Purchase Default.** Supplier may terminate Distributor's exclusivity under Section 3.1, on ninety (90) days' written notice, if Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Purchase Commitment in two (2) consecutive Contract Years and does not cure either shortfall under Section 4.4.

15.3 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

## 16. Effect of Termination

16.1 **Orders and Payment.** Upon expiration or termination, Supplier shall fulfill accepted Purchase Orders, and Distributor shall pay all amounts due.

16.2 **Sell-Off.** Distributor may sell its remaining inventory of Products for six (6) months after expiration or termination, subject to this Agreement.

16.3 **Inventory Repurchase.** If Distributor terminates under Section 15.1 or 15.3, Supplier shall, at Distributor's request, repurchase Distributor's saleable inventory of Products at the transfer price paid.

16.4 **Survival.** Sections 5.3, 7.4, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16 and 19, and any other provisions that by their nature are intended to survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 17. Force Majeure

17.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, governmental action, or failure of public utilities, provided it promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, the other Party may terminate any affected Purchase Order by written notice.

19.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

19.2 **Venue.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement.

19.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor to all or substantially all of the assigning Party's business relating to this Agreement that assumes this Agreement in writing.

19.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the following documents, they prevail in the following order: any Amendment; the Quality Agreement (quality matters only); this Distribution Agreement; the Statement of Work.

19.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties (each, an "**Amendment**"). No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

19.6 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

19.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
