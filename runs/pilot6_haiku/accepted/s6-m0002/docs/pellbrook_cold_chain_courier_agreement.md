# COLD-CHAIN COURIER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Cold-Chain Courier Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Pellbrook Freight Logistics LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1875 Ardmore Industrial Drive, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania 18017 ("**Carrier**"), and **Glakin Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Corliss Avenue, Rochester, New York 14607 ("**Customer**"). Carrier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer distributes veterinary diagnostic and animal health products to veterinary clinics and hospitals. Some of those products must be kept refrigerated during storage and transport.

B. Carrier provides temperature-controlled pickup, line-haul and final-mile courier services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Carrier, and Carrier wishes to be engaged, to provide those services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Consignee**" means the veterinary clinic, hospital or other recipient named on a Shipment Order.

(b) "**Excursion**" means any period during which the recorded temperature of a Temperature-Controlled Shipment falls outside the Temperature Range stated on its Shipment Order.

(c) "**Goods**" means the products Customer tenders to Carrier for transport under this Agreement.

(d) "**Origin Facility**" means each Customer warehouse or fulfillment facility listed in Schedule A, as updated by Customer by written notice.

(e) "**Service Levels**" means the service levels set out in Schedule B.

(f) "**Services**" means the pickup, transport, temperature monitoring, delivery and related services described in Section 2 and Schedule B.

(g) "**Shipment Order**" means a request for Services that Customer issues to Carrier through Carrier's online booking portal, by electronic data interchange, or by email under Section 3.1.

(h) "**Temperature-Controlled Shipment**" means a shipment of Goods that a Shipment Order designates as refrigerated.

(i) "**Temperature Range**" means, for a Temperature-Controlled Shipment, 2°C to 8°C, unless the Shipment Order states a different range.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement.

## 2. Services

2.1 **Scope.** Carrier shall pick up Goods at the Origin Facilities and deliver them to Consignees in the contiguous United States, in accordance with each accepted Shipment Order, this Agreement and the Service Levels.

2.2 **Temperature-Controlled Shipments.** For each Temperature-Controlled Shipment, Carrier shall:

(a) transport the shipment in qualified refrigerated vehicles or in Customer's validated passive packaging, as stated on the Shipment Order;

(b) keep the shipment within the Temperature Range from pickup until delivery to the Consignee;

(c) use continuous temperature monitoring with calibrated data loggers, recording at intervals of not more than ten (10) minutes;

(d) not leave the shipment unattended at an unsecured or unconditioned location; and

(e) not freeze, or expose to direct sunlight, any Goods that the Shipment Order marks "Do Not Freeze" or "Protect from Light."

2.3 **Vehicle and Equipment Qualification.** Carrier shall qualify and maintain each refrigerated vehicle and temperature-monitoring device it uses for the Services. It shall calibrate each data logger at least once every twelve (12) months against standards traceable to the National Institute of Standards and Technology. Carrier shall provide its qualification and calibration records to Customer on request.

2.4 **Chain of Custody.** Carrier shall scan each package at pickup, at each hand-off and at delivery. Carrier shall obtain a signature or electronic proof of delivery from the Consignee, showing the date and time of delivery and the name of the person receiving the package.

2.5 **Personnel.** Carrier shall use trained personnel to perform the Services. Before any driver or handler first handles Temperature-Controlled Shipments, Carrier shall train that person in cold-chain handling, Excursion response and this Agreement's delivery requirements, and shall train each such person again at least annually.

2.6 **Subcontracting.** Carrier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold that consent. Carrier remains responsible for the performance of each approved subcontractor as if it were Carrier's own performance.

## 3. Shipment Orders

3.1 **Ordering.** Customer shall issue a Shipment Order for each shipment. Each Shipment Order shall state the Origin Facility, the Consignee and delivery address, the number of packages, the requested service level, whether the shipment is a Temperature-Controlled Shipment and, if so, the Temperature Range and any handling marks.

3.2 **Acceptance.** Carrier shall accept or reject each Shipment Order through its booking portal within two (2) hours after receipt during Carrier's business hours. Carrier may reject a Shipment Order only if the delivery address is outside its service area or the shipment does not meet the packaging requirements of Section 3.3.

3.3 **Packaging and Labeling.** Customer shall package and label Goods in accordance with the Shipment Order and applicable law. Where a Temperature-Controlled Shipment is to move in Customer's validated passive packaging, Customer shall condition and pack the shipment in accordance with that packaging's qualification, and Carrier shall deliver it within the hold time stated on the Shipment Order.

3.4 **Terms of Service.** The terms of this Agreement govern each Shipment Order. Any terms on a bill of lading, airbill, delivery receipt or Carrier's website that conflict with this Agreement have no effect.

## 4. Excursions, Delays and Loss

4.1 **Excursion Notice.** Carrier shall notify Customer's designated logistics contact by telephone and email within two (2) hours after Carrier becomes aware of any actual or likely Excursion, delay, damage or loss affecting a Temperature-Controlled Shipment.

4.2 **Response.** Carrier shall follow Customer's reasonable instructions to protect affected Goods, which may include holding the Goods in a conditioned location, re-icing or re-conditioning packaging, returning the shipment to the Origin Facility or redelivering it.

4.3 **Data.** Within one (1) business day after Customer's request, Carrier shall provide the complete temperature data, chain-of-custody scans and proof of delivery for any shipment.

4.4 **Disposition.** Customer decides the disposition of Goods affected by an Excursion. Carrier shall not deliver Goods that it knows have experienced an Excursion unless Customer authorizes delivery in writing.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Carrier the fees set out in Schedule C for Services performed under accepted Shipment Orders.

5.2 **Fuel Surcharge.** Carrier may apply a fuel surcharge calculated weekly using the method in Schedule C.

5.3 **Fee Adjustment.** Carrier may adjust the fees in Schedule C once in each twelve (12) month period after the first anniversary of the Effective Date, by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before the adjustment takes effect. No single adjustment may exceed the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics for the most recent twelve (12) month period. If Customer does not accept an adjustment, Customer may terminate this Agreement under Section 10.2 before the adjustment takes effect, and the prior fees apply until termination.

5.4 **Invoicing and Payment.** Carrier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize charges by Shipment Order. Undisputed amounts are due within thirty (30) days after receipt of the invoice. Customer shall notify Carrier in writing of any disputed amount before the due date, with reasonable detail, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.5 **Service Credits.** If Carrier fails to meet a Service Level, Customer is entitled to the service credits in Schedule B. Service credits are in addition to Customer's other rights under this Agreement.

5.6 **No Liens.** Carrier waives any lien it may have on Goods for unpaid charges and shall not withhold delivery of any Goods because of a payment dispute.

## 6. Records and Audit

6.1 **Records.** Carrier shall keep, for each shipment, the temperature data, chain-of-custody scans, proof of delivery and Excursion reports, for five (5) years after the delivery date.

6.2 **Audit.** On thirty (30) days' prior written notice, and not more than once in any twelve (12) month period except following a significant Excursion or loss, Customer may audit Carrier's facilities, vehicles, procedures and records relating to the Services during normal business hours. Carrier shall respond to audit findings within thirty (30) days with a corrective action plan and carry it out within the agreed timeframe.

## 7. Liability for Goods

7.1 **Standard of Care.** Carrier is responsible for the Goods from pickup until delivery to the Consignee.

7.2 **Loss, Damage and Excursions.** Carrier is liable for loss of, damage to, and Excursions affecting Goods while in its care. Carrier's liability is measured by the replacement cost of the affected Goods, as shown on Customer's invoice cost records, plus the charges paid for the affected shipment. Carrier is not liable to the extent the loss, damage or Excursion results from (a) Customer's failure to package, condition or label Goods in accordance with Section 3.3, (b) an inherent defect in the Goods, or (c) a force majeure event under Section 13.

7.3 **Claims.** Customer shall submit claims for loss, damage or Excursion within ninety (90) days after the scheduled delivery date, with reasonable supporting documentation. Carrier shall pay or deny each claim in writing within thirty (30) days after receipt.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 **By Carrier.** Carrier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by Carrier or its personnel or subcontractors, (b) Carrier's breach of this Agreement or violation of applicable law, or (c) Carrier's negligence or willful misconduct.

8.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Carrier, its affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Customer's breach of this Agreement or violation of applicable law, or (b) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

8.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of the claim (delay relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent prejudiced), give the indemnifying Party control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

8.4 **Affiliates.** In this Section 8, "Affiliate" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 9.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility. Amounts payable under Section 7.2 are direct damages.

9.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 9.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement in any twelve (12) month period shall not exceed the greater of (a) $1,000,000 and (b) the fees paid or payable by Customer to Carrier under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 9.1 and 9.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 8, (b) a Party's breach of Section 11, (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, or (d) Customer's obligation to pay fees for Services performed.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years. It then renews automatically for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

10.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving the other Party at least ninety (90) days' written notice. Customer may also terminate under Section 5.3 by written notice given before the fee adjustment takes effect.

10.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Carrier fails to meet the Temperature Compliance Service Level in Schedule B in any two (2) consecutive calendar months.

10.4 **Effect.** On expiration or termination, Carrier shall complete delivery of all shipments then in its care in accordance with this Agreement, and Customer shall pay for Services performed. Sections 4.3, 5.4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 and 14 survive expiration or termination.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including Customer's Consignee lists, shipment volumes and pricing. Each Party shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and disclose it only to its employees, approved subcontractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by similar obligations.

11.2 **Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose information required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

11.3 **Duration.** This Section 11 survives for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 12. Insurance and Compliance

12.1 **Insurance.** During the term of this Agreement, Carrier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 combined single limit;

(c) motor truck cargo insurance, including coverage for refrigeration breakdown, of not less than $500,000 per vehicle per occurrence; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, and employer's liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000.

Carrier shall name Customer as an additional insured under the policies in clauses (a) and (b) and provide certificates of insurance on request.

12.2 **Compliance.** Carrier shall hold and maintain all operating authority, registrations and permits required to perform the Services. Carrier shall comply with all applicable laws, including U.S. Department of Transportation and Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration regulations.

## 13. Force Majeure

13.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, severe weather, epidemics, war, terrorism, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Carrier's mechanical breakdowns, labor shortages and refrigeration equipment failures are not force majeure events. If a force majeure event prevents Carrier from performing for more than ten (10) business days, Customer may use other carriers for the affected Services during that period.
