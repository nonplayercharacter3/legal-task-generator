# STATEMENT OF WORK: HEMATOLOGY PRODUCT LAUNCH

**Statement of Work No. 1**

This Statement of Work (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of February 1, 2024 by and between **Sumeith Diagnostics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Glakin Animal Health Distribution LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). It is issued under the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of February 1, 2024 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). This SOW is the "Statement of Work" referred to in the Distribution Agreement.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 **Purpose.** This SOW describes the activities the Parties will carry out to launch the Products in the Territory. Those activities include sales force training, Customer installation and onboarding, launch marketing, demonstration equipment, house account arrangements and launch reporting.

1.2 **Exclusivity Scope.** The agreed exclusivity scope for Distributor's appointment covers in-clinic veterinary hematology analyzers and their reagent cartridges, United States only. The Products covered by this SOW are the Analyzers and Cartridges listed in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement:

| Product Code | Description |
|---|---|
| SDX-HA200 | Sumeith HemaVet 200 in-clinic hematology analyzer (5-part differential) |
| SDX-HA200-QC | HemaVet 200 quality control material kit |
| SDX-RC25 | HemaVet CBC reagent cartridge, 25-pack |
| SDX-RC100 | HemaVet CBC reagent cartridge, 100-pack |

1.3 **Launch Period.** The launch period begins on the Effective Date and ends on January 31, 2025, which is the last day of Contract Year 1 (the "**Launch Period**").

## 2. Launch Timeline and Milestones

2.1 **Milestones.** The Parties will use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve the following milestones:

| Milestone | Responsible Party | Target Date |
|---|---|---|
| Kick-off meeting and appointment of launch leads | Both | February 15, 2024 |
| Delivery of launch sales materials and brand guidelines | Supplier | March 1, 2024 |
| Completion of initial sales force training (Section 3) | Supplier, with Distributor participation | April 15, 2024 |
| Delivery of demonstration Analyzers (Section 5) | Supplier | April 15, 2024 |
| Commercial launch announcement to Customers | Distributor | May 1, 2024 |
| First Customer installations | Distributor, with Supplier field support | May 2024 |
| Mid-launch business review | Both | August 15, 2024 |
| Launch close-out review | Both | February 28, 2025 |

2.2 **Launch Leads.** Each Party will name a launch lead at the kick-off meeting. The launch lead is the primary contact for that Party's activities under this SOW. A Party may replace its launch lead by written notice to the other Party's launch lead. Email notice is sufficient for this purpose.

2.3 **Missed Milestones.** If a Party expects to miss a milestone, it will notify the other Party's launch lead promptly. The Parties will then agree on a revised target date. A missed target date in Section 2.1 is not, by itself, a breach of the Distribution Agreement.

## 3. Training

3.1 **Initial Sales Training.** Supplier will provide initial product training to Distributor's territory sales representatives and inside sales staff. Training will consist of up to four (4) in-person regional sessions and live web sessions as reasonably requested. It will cover Analyzer operation, the 5-part differential, Cartridge handling and storage, competitive positioning and Supplier's approved claims.

3.2 **Technical Training.** Supplier will provide technical training to Distributor's field service and technical support personnel. This training will cover Analyzer installation, calibration, quality control using SDX-HA200-QC, first-line troubleshooting and the complaint intake and forwarding procedures in the Quality Agreement. Supplier will certify each trained technician who completes the training and passes Supplier's competency assessment.

3.3 **Ongoing Training.** After the Launch Period, Supplier will provide refresher and new-hire training at least twice per Contract Year. Supplier will also provide training on any new software or firmware release that changes Analyzer operation.

3.4 **Costs.** Supplier bears the cost of its trainers and training materials. Each Party bears its own personnel travel and expenses.

## 4. Customer Installation and Onboarding

4.1 **Installation.** Distributor's certified technicians will install each Analyzer at the Customer site and complete Supplier's installation checklist. During the Launch Period, Supplier will provide on-site field application support for the first fifty (50) installations. After that, Supplier will provide remote support.

4.2 **Customer Onboarding.** At installation, Distributor will train Customer staff on Analyzer operation and Cartridge storage. Distributor will pass through Supplier's end-user license terms under Section 10.3 of the Distribution Agreement and record the Analyzer serial number and Customer location for traceability under Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

4.3 **Warranty Start.** Distributor will report the installation date of each Analyzer to Supplier within ten (10) business days after installation. That date is used for the warranty period under Section 8.1 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 5. Demonstration Equipment

5.1 **Demonstration Analyzers.** Supplier will loan Distributor up to twelve (12) SDX-HA200 Analyzers for demonstration and evaluation use (the "**Demonstration Units**"), at no charge, for the Launch Period. Supplier will also supply a reasonable quantity of Cartridges and QC material for demonstration use at no charge.

5.2 **Title and Care.** Title to the Demonstration Units remains with Supplier. Distributor will use reasonable care with the Demonstration Units and keep them insured under the policies required by Section 14.1 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor will not sell or permanently place them with any Customer.

5.3 **Return or Purchase.** At the end of the Launch Period, Distributor will either (a) return the Demonstration Units to Supplier's Durham, North Carolina facility at Supplier's expense, or (b) purchase any of them at fifty percent (50%) of the then-current Exhibit B transfer price for SDX-HA200. Demonstration Units purchased under clause (b) count toward the Minimum Purchase Commitment for Contract Year 2.

## 6. Launch Marketing

6.1 **Supplier Materials.** Supplier will provide product brochures, technical data sheets, case studies, digital assets and trade show graphics for the Products. All of these will be consistent with the Product labeling.

6.2 **Distributor Activities.** Distributor will feature the Products in its Customer communications, catalog and e-commerce platform. Distributor will also feature them at not fewer than three (3) national or regional veterinary conferences during the Launch Period.

6.3 **Launch Marketing Fund.** Supplier will contribute up to $150,000 during the Launch Period toward pre-approved co-marketing activities that Distributor conducts. Distributor will submit proposed activities to Supplier's launch lead for written approval, which Supplier will not unreasonably withhold. Distributor will invoice approved activities with reasonable supporting documentation, and Supplier will pay those invoices within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

6.4 **Claims.** Distributor will make only Product claims that are consistent with Supplier's labeling and approved marketing materials, as required by Section 12.2(b) of the Distribution Agreement.

## 7. House Accounts

7.1 **Supplier House Accounts.** For purposes of Section 3.2 of the Distribution Agreement, the following accounts are Supplier house accounts. Supplier may continue to sell Products directly to them:

(a) veterinary teaching hospitals operated by accredited colleges of veterinary medicine in the Territory that held a Supplier evaluation or placement agreement as of the Effective Date; and

(b) the U.S. federal government, including the U.S. Department of Agriculture and the U.S. Department of Defense veterinary services, for direct federal procurement.

7.2 **Referral.** Distributor will refer to Supplier any inquiry from a Supplier house account. Supplier will refer to Distributor any inquiry from a Customer that is not a Supplier house account.

7.3 **No Additions.** The Parties may add accounts to Section 7.1 only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

## 8. Reporting and Business Reviews

8.1 **Monthly Reports.** By the tenth (10th) day of each month during the Launch Period, Distributor will provide Supplier with a launch report. The report will include Analyzer placements and installations by state, Cartridge sell-through, the pipeline of open opportunities and a summary of Customer feedback. The report may be delivered together with the forecast under Section 4.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

8.2 **Business Reviews.** The Parties will hold the mid-launch and close-out business reviews described in Section 2.1. After the Launch Period, they will hold quarterly business reviews. Each review will cover performance against the Minimum Purchase Commitment, forecast accuracy, quality and complaint trends, and marketing plans.

8.3 **Confidentiality.** Reports and information exchanged under this SOW are confidential information under Section 13 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 9. General

9.1 **Relationship to Distribution Agreement.** This SOW is issued under, and is subject to, the Distribution Agreement. Nothing in this SOW changes the pricing, payment, warranty, Recall, liability or indemnification terms of the Distribution Agreement.

9.2 **Term.** This SOW remains in effect for the Term of the Distribution Agreement. Sections 3.3, 7 and 8.2 continue after the Launch Period.

9.3 **Changes.** This SOW may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

9.4 **Counterparts.** This SOW may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
