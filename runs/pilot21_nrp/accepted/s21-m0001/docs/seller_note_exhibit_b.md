# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

$14,000,000 | Dated: [●], 20[●] (the Closing Date)

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Bougairt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Drigask Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (together with its successors and assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest set out below, on the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 15, 2026, between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal

The principal amount of this Note is $14,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount may be reduced by prepayment under Section 6 or by set-off under Section 4. "**Outstanding Principal**" means, at any time, the Principal Amount less all such reductions made before that time.

## 2. Interest

(a) *Rate.* Interest accrues on the Outstanding Principal from and including the Closing Date at the rate of 6% per annum, simple interest. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Interest does not compound.

(b) *Payment.* Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date to occur after the Closing Date. All accrued and unpaid interest is also payable on the Maturity Date.

(c) *Default Interest.* While an Event of Default under Section 9.1(a) continues, the Outstanding Principal bears interest at a rate two percent (2%) per annum above the rate in Section 2(a).

## 3. Maturity

The Outstanding Principal, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable in full on the date that is 30 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"). If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

## 4. Set-Off

**4.1 Right of Set-Off.** In accordance with Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off, against any principal or interest then or later payable under this Note, the amount of any Losses to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. This right of set-off applies only to claims for which written notice was delivered to Holder under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement before the expiration of the applicable survival period in Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement. If a claim has been noticed but not finally determined when a payment falls due, Maker may withhold from that payment an amount equal to Maker's good-faith estimate of the Losses claimed. Maker shall pay any withheld amount not ultimately due to the Buyer Indemnitees, with interest at the rate in Section 2(a), within ten (10) Business Days after final determination of the claim.

**4.2 Limitation on Recovery.** The aggregate Losses recoverable by the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, whether by set-off under this Note or otherwise, shall not exceed the General Cap, including Losses described in Section 9.5(c) of the Purchase Agreement. Amounts set off under Section 4.1 count toward that limit.

**4.3 Application.** Any amount set off under Section 4.1 is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to Outstanding Principal. It is treated for all purposes as a payment made by Maker under this Note on the date of set-off. The exercise of set-off in good faith in accordance with this Section 4 is not an Event of Default, whether or not the claim is ultimately determined in favor of the Buyer Indemnitees.

**4.4 Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off or withholding under Section 4.1, identifying the claim and the amount set off or withheld, on or before the date the affected payment would otherwise have been due.

## 5. Information Rights

So long as any amount is outstanding under this Note, Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited consolidated balance sheet of Maker and its subsidiaries, including the Company, as of the end of that month, and the related statements of income and cash flows for that month and the fiscal year to date;

(b) within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, customer-level sales reports for the Company, showing net sales, volumes and gross margin by customer and by product line for that month and the fiscal year to date;

(c) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Maker and its subsidiaries for that fiscal year; and

(d) promptly after Holder's reasonable request, such other information about the financial condition and operations of Maker and the Company as Holder may reasonably request.

The rights in this Section 5 are for the benefit of Holder and each subsequent holder of this Note.

## 6. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, on at least five (5) Business Days' written notice to Holder. Each prepayment is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid and then to Outstanding Principal.

## 7. Subordination

**7.1 Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is unsecured. Its payment is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Debt. "**Senior Debt**" means all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries under the credit facilities provided by Maker's senior lenders to finance the transactions under the Purchase Agreement, and any refinancing or replacement of those facilities.

**7.2 Payment Blockage.** While any payment default or acceleration under the Senior Debt continues, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note other than by set-off under Section 4. Any amount not paid because of this Section 7.2 continues to accrue interest and becomes payable once the blockage ends.

**7.3 Subordination Agreement.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, enter into a subordination agreement with the agent for the holders of Senior Debt. That agreement shall be on customary terms reasonably acceptable to Maker's senior lenders and consistent with this Section 7.

## 8. Transfer

Holder may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Maker. Holder shall give Maker written notice of any transfer within five (5) Business Days after it occurs, stating the name and notice address of the transferee and the portion of this Note transferred. Until it receives that notice, Maker may treat the Person shown as Holder in its records as the owner of this Note for all purposes. Each transferee takes this Note subject to the right of set-off under Section 4 and to Section 7. Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without Holder's prior written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all of Maker's assets that assumes this Note in writing.

## 9. Events of Default; Remedies

**9.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, other than an amount withheld or set off under Section 4 or not paid because of Section 7.2, and that failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice from Holder;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and that failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**9.2 Acceleration.** If an Event of Default under Section 9.1(c) or 9.1(d) occurs, the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without any action by Holder. If any other Event of Default occurs and continues, Holder may, subject to Section 7, declare the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker.

**9.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Holder incurs in collecting amounts due under this Note after an Event of Default.

## 10. Payments

All payments under this Note shall be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Payments shall be sent to the account Holder designates in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date.

## 11. Miscellaneous

**11.1 Waivers.** Maker waives presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right.

**11.2 Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

**11.3 Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to any transferee shall be sent to the address stated in the transfer notice delivered under Section 8.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Note is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any action arising out of this Note as if set out in full in this Note.

**11.6 Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms. Before it does so, Holder shall deliver an affidavit of loss and an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to Maker.
