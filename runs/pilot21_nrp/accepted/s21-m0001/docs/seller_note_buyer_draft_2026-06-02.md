# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

$14,000,000 | Dated: [●], 20[●] (the Closing Date)

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Bougairt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Drigask Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal amount and interest set out below. Payment is due on the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note issued under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026, between Holder, as Seller, and Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal

The principal amount of this Note is $14,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount may be reduced by prepayment under Section 6 or by set-off under Section 4. "**Outstanding Principal**" means, at any time, the Principal Amount less all such reductions made before that time.

## 2. Interest

(a) *Rate.* Interest accrues on the Outstanding Principal at the rate of 6% per annum, simple interest, from and including the Closing Date. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed. Interest does not compound.

(b) *Payment.* Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date after the Closing Date. All accrued and unpaid interest is also payable on the Maturity Date.

(c) *Default Interest.* While an Event of Default under Section 9.1(a) continues, the Outstanding Principal bears interest at a rate two percent (2%) per annum above the rate in Section 2(a).

## 3. Maturity

The Outstanding Principal is due and payable in full on the date that is 30 months after the Closing Date (the "**Maturity Date**"), together with all accrued and unpaid interest. If the Maturity Date is not a Business Day, payment is due on the next Business Day.

## 4. Set-Off

**4.1 Right of Set-Off.** In accordance with Section 9.7 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off the amount of any Losses to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The set-off may be made against any principal or interest then or later payable under this Note. This right of set-off applies to claims for which written notice was delivered to Holder under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement within the applicable survival period in Section 9.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Pending Claims.** A claim may have been noticed but not finally determined when a payment falls due. In that case, Maker may withhold from that payment an amount equal to Maker's good-faith estimate of the Losses claimed. Maker shall pay to Holder any withheld amount finally determined not to be owed to the Buyer Indemnitees, with interest at the rate in Section 2(a). Payment is due within ten (10) Business Days after final determination of the claim.

**4.3 Limitations.** Set-off under this Section 4 is subject to the deductible and the limitations on liability in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement, as applicable to the claim in question. Amounts set off count toward those limitations to the same extent as amounts recovered directly from Seller.

**4.4 Application.** Any amount set off under Section 4.1 is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to Outstanding Principal. It is treated for all purposes as a payment made by Maker under this Note on the date of set-off. The exercise of set-off or withholding in good faith in accordance with this Section 4 is not an Event of Default. This is so whether or not the claim is ultimately determined in favor of the Buyer Indemnitees.

**4.5 Notice.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off or withholding under this Section 4. The notice shall identify the claim and the amount set off or withheld. It is due on or before the date the affected payment would otherwise have been due.

## 5. Reporting

So long as any amount is outstanding under this Note, Maker shall deliver the following to Holder:

(a) within forty-five (45) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, the unaudited consolidated balance sheet of Maker and its subsidiaries as of the end of that quarter, and the related consolidated statements of income and cash flows for that quarter and the fiscal year to date; and

(b) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Maker and its subsidiaries for that fiscal year.

Holder shall hold all information delivered under this Section 5 in confidence. Holder shall use that information only to monitor its investment in this Note. Holder shall not disclose it to any Person other than its officers, managers, members and professional advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations, or as required by Law.

## 6. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, on at least five (5) Business Days' written notice to Holder. Each prepayment is applied first to accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid and then to Outstanding Principal.

## 7. Subordination

**7.1 Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is unsecured. Its payment is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Debt. "**Senior Debt**" means all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries under the credit facilities provided by Maker's senior lenders to finance the transactions under the Purchase Agreement, and any refinancing or replacement of those facilities.

**7.2 Payment Blockage.** While any payment default or acceleration under the Senior Debt continues, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note other than by set-off under Section 4. Any amount not paid because of this Section 7.2 continues to accrue interest. It becomes payable once the blockage ends.

**7.3 Subordination Agreement.** At Maker's request, Holder shall enter into a subordination agreement with the agent for the holders of Senior Debt. That agreement shall be on customary terms reasonably acceptable to Maker's senior lenders and consistent with this Section 7.

## 8. Transfer

**8.1 Transfer by Holder.** Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, or any interest in it, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of Maker. Any purported transfer without that consent is void.

**8.2 Transferees.** Each transferee permitted under Section 8.1 takes this Note subject to the right of set-off under Section 4, to Section 7 and to the confidentiality obligations in Section 5. Each transferee shall confirm those terms in writing to Maker before the transfer becomes effective. Until Maker receives that written confirmation and notice of the transferee's name and notice address, Maker may treat the Person shown as Holder in its records as the owner of this Note for all purposes.

**8.3 Assignment by Maker.** Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without Holder's prior written consent. However, Maker may assign them without consent to a successor to all or substantially all of Maker's assets that assumes this Note in writing.

## 9. Events of Default; Remedies

**9.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note when due, and that failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice from Holder. This clause does not apply to an amount withheld or set off under Section 4 or not paid because of Section 7.2;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and that failure continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Holder;

(c) Maker commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or

(d) an involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**9.2 Acceleration.** If an Event of Default under Section 9.1(c) or 9.1(d) occurs, the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without any action by Holder. If any other Event of Default occurs and continues, Holder may declare the Outstanding Principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker, subject to Section 7.

**9.3 Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses that Holder incurs in collecting amounts due under this Note after an Event of Default, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 10. Payments

All payments under this Note shall be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Payments shall be sent to the account Holder designates in writing at least five (5) Business Days before the payment date.

**11.3 Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Note is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware.
