**Re: Letter of Intent – Proposed Acquisition of Pinecrest Pet Provisions, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Bougairt Pet Nutrition, Inc. ("**Buyer**") proposes to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Pinecrest Pet Provisions, Inc. (the "**Company**") from Drigask Holdings, LLC ("**Seller**"). The acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**." Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' present intentions. It is intended to serve as the basis for negotiating a definitive stock purchase agreement (the "**Definitive Agreement**").

## 1. Structure of the Transaction

Buyer will purchase from Seller, and Seller will sell to Buyer, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens and encumbrances. After the Transaction, the Company will be a wholly owned subsidiary of Buyer. The Company will continue its business of manufacturing and selling dry and wet pet food products.

## 2. Purchase Price

(a) The aggregate purchase price for the Shares will be $72,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). The Purchase Price assumes the Company is delivered on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized level of net working capital.

(b) The Purchase Price will be payable at closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**") as follows:

(i) $58,000,000 in cash, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, subject to the working capital adjustment described in Section 4; and

(ii) $14,000,000 by delivery of a subordinated promissory note of Buyer (the "**Seller Note**") on the terms described in Section 3.

## 3. Seller Note

The Seller Note will have the following principal terms:

(a) *Principal.* $14,000,000.

(b) *Interest.* 6% per annum, simple interest, payable quarterly in arrears.

(c) *Maturity.* All outstanding principal and accrued interest will be due 30 months after the Closing Date.

(d) *Subordination.* The Seller Note will be unsecured. It will be subordinated to Buyer's senior acquisition financing on terms reasonably acceptable to Buyer's senior lenders.

(e) *Set-Off.* Buyer will have the right to set off against principal and accrued interest under the Seller Note any amounts to which Buyer is entitled under the indemnification provisions of the Definitive Agreement.

(f) *Transfer.* Not transferable by Seller without Buyer's prior written consent.

(g) *Prepayment.* Buyer may prepay the Seller Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty.

## 4. Working Capital Adjustment

The cash portion of the Purchase Price will be adjusted dollar-for-dollar upward or downward to the extent the Company's net working capital at Closing exceeds or falls short of a target amount. The parties will agree on the target based on the Company's trailing twelve-month average. The adjustment will follow a customary estimated and final closing statement process. Disputes will be resolved by an independent accounting firm.

## 5. Due Diligence

Following execution of this Letter, Seller will cause the Company to give Buyer and its advisors full access to the Company's facilities, books, records, contracts, personnel and customers. Buyer's due diligence will include financial, tax, legal, environmental, regulatory, food-safety and quality-assurance review. It will include on-site inspection of the Company's manufacturing facility and review of lot testing records, customer complaints and regulatory correspondence.

## 6. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

(a) *Representations.* The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties of Seller customary for a transaction of this type. These will include representations concerning:

(i) organization, authority and capitalization;
(ii) title to the Shares;
(iii) financial statements and undisclosed liabilities;
(iv) material contracts;
(v) taxes;
(vi) compliance with laws and permits; and
(vii) product safety, labeling, formulation and recalls.

(b) *Survival.* Seller's general representations and warranties, including the product safety and recall representations, will survive the Closing until 18 months after Closing. Fundamental representations, which cover organization, authority, capitalization, title, taxes and brokers, will survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations.

(c) *Indemnification.* Seller will indemnify Buyer for the following:

(i) breaches of representations, warranties and covenants;
(ii) losses arising from any recall or market withdrawal of products manufactured or sold by the Company before the Closing; and
(iii) pre-Closing taxes.

(d) *Limitations.* Indemnification for breaches of general representations will be subject to a customary deductible and to a cap of ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Price. Claims for breach of fundamental representations and recall-related losses will not be subject to the deductible or that cap. These claims will instead be limited to the Purchase Price. None of these limitations will apply to fraud.

(e) *Recovery.* Indemnifiable losses will be recovered first by set-off against the Seller Note and then directly from Seller.

## 7. Conditions to Closing

Closing will be subject to customary conditions for a transaction of this type, including:

(a) satisfactory completion of Buyer's due diligence;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreement;

(c) expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976;

(d) receipt of the consent of Fenwold Pet Supply Co. ("**Fenwold**") to the change of control of the Company under the Company's private-label supply agreement with Fenwold, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer;

(e) receipt of all other required third-party consents, including those of the landlord of the Company's manufacturing facility and the lender under the Company's equipment financing;

(f) the absence of any recall or market withdrawal of any Company product between signing and Closing; and

(g) the absence of any material adverse change in the business, assets, condition or results of operations of the Company.

## 8. Non-Competition

At Closing, Seller will agree that, for five (5) years following Closing, neither Seller nor its affiliates will compete with the Company in the manufacture or sale of dry or wet pet food products in the United States or Canada. Seller will also agree, for the same period, that neither Seller nor its affiliates will solicit the Company's customers, suppliers or employees.

## 9. Exclusivity

For a period of ninety (90) days after the date of this Letter (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), Seller will not, and will cause the Company and its respective representatives not to, take any of the following actions with any person other than Buyer concerning any sale of the Shares, any merger or recapitalization of the Company, or any sale of a material portion of the Company's assets:

(a) solicit, initiate or encourage any inquiry or proposal;
(b) engage in discussions or negotiations; or
(c) provide information.

Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any such inquiry or proposal it receives.

## 10. Confidentiality

The terms of this Letter, and the fact that the parties are in discussions, are confidential. They will not be disclosed by either party except to its officers, directors, employees, lenders and professional advisors who need to know them, or as required by law. The Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between Buyer and Seller dated January 27, 2026 remains in full force and effect.

## 11. Expenses

Each party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter, due diligence and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is consummated.

## 12. Binding Effect

Only Sections 9 (Exclusivity), 10 (Confidentiality), 11 (Expenses), 12 (Binding Effect) and 13 (Governing Law) of this Letter are legally binding obligations of the parties. All other provisions of this Letter are non-binding. No party will have any obligation to consummate the Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement has been executed and delivered. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time by written notice to the other party. Sections 10, 11, 12 and 13 will survive any such termination.

## 13. Governing Law

This Letter will be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
