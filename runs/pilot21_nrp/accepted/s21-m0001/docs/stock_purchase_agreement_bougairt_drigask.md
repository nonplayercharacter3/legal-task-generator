# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 15, 2026, by and between **Drigask Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Bougairt Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Pinecrest Pet Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company manufactures and sells dry and wet pet food products.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Contract**" means any contract, agreement, lease, license, purchase order, commitment or other legally binding arrangement, whether written or oral.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Fenwold**" means Fenwold Pet Supply Co.

"**Fenwold Supply Agreement**" has the meaning given in Section 4.9(b).

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller set forth in Section 4.1 (Organization and Authority), Section 4.2 (Enforceability), Section 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares), Section 4.12 (Taxes) and Section 4.16 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, regulatory body, court or other authority thereof, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and any state department of agriculture or feed control official.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $72,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment of Purchase Price.** Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows:

(a) $58,000,000, as adjusted under Section 2.3, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date (the "**Closing Cash Payment**"); and

(b) $14,000,000 by the issuance and delivery to Seller of a subordinated promissory note of Buyer in the form attached as **Exhibit B** (the "**Seller Note**").

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.** At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's net working capital as of the Closing. If estimated net working capital exceeds $9,400,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), the Closing Cash Payment shall be increased by the excess. If it is less than the Target Working Capital, the Closing Cash Payment shall be reduced by the shortfall. Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver a final closing statement. Any dispute that the parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination is final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made in cash within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after the conditions in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the third-party consents required by Section 7.2(c);

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(d) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d); and

(e) a duly executed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller:

(a) the Closing Cash Payment;

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where such qualification is required. Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.

**4.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement has been duly executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable, and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants, convertible securities or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** Except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require the consent of or notice to any Person under, or conflict with, result in a breach of or give rise to a right of termination under, any Material Contract.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet as of May 31, 2026 and the related statements for the five-month period then ended (collectively, the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates.

**4.6 Undisclosed Liabilities.** The Company has no liabilities except (a) those reflected or reserved against in the Financial Statements and (b) those incurred in the ordinary course of business since May 31, 2026, none of which is material.

**4.7 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has operated in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice and there has not been any event or condition that has had, or would reasonably be expected to have, a material adverse effect on the business, assets, condition or results of operations of the Company.

**4.8 Real Property; Assets.** The Company does not own any real property. Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each lease of real property to which the Company is a party, including the lease of the Company's manufacturing facility (the "**Plant Lease**"). The Company has good title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of its tangible assets, free and clear of all Liens other than Liens securing the Equipment Financing Agreement.

**4.9 Material Contracts.**

(a) Section 4.9(a) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each of the following Contracts to which the Company is a party (the "**Material Contracts**"): (i) each Contract with a customer that generated revenue of more than $1,000,000 in fiscal year 2025; (ii) each Contract with a supplier of ingredients, packaging or co-manufacturing services involving payments of more than $500,000 per year; (iii) the Plant Lease; (iv) the Loan and Security Agreement under which the Company finances its production equipment (the "**Equipment Financing Agreement**"); (v) each Contract containing exclusivity, most-favored-nation or non-competition obligations binding on the Company; and (vi) each Contract with Seller or any of its Affiliates. Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Material Contract.

(b) The Private Label Supply Agreement between the Company and Fenwold (the "**Fenwold Supply Agreement**") is the Company's largest customer contract by revenue. Under the Fenwold Supply Agreement, a change of control of the Company requires the prior written consent of Fenwold, and the Fenwold Supply Agreement may terminate upon a change of control of the Company effected without that consent. Seller has delivered to Buyer a true and complete copy of the Fenwold Supply Agreement and all amendments to it. Fenwold has not given notice of any intention to terminate, not renew or materially reduce its purchases under the Fenwold Supply Agreement.

**4.10 Litigation.** There is no action, suit, claim, investigation or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting its assets or the Shares.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. The Company holds all permits, registrations and licenses required for the conduct of its business, including commercial feed licenses and facility registrations, and each is in full force and effect.

**4.12 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it, all such returns are true, complete and correct in all material respects, and all Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid. There are no Liens for Taxes on the assets of the Company other than for current Taxes not yet due and payable.

**4.13 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Section 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each material employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in compliance in all material respects with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Product Safety; Recalls.**

(a) Each product manufactured, packaged or sold by the Company has been manufactured, labeled, stored and distributed in compliance in all material respects with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act, AAFCO model regulations as adopted by applicable states, and all other applicable Laws. No such product is adulterated or misbranded, and none contains mycotoxins (including aflatoxin), pathogens or nutrient levels (including vitamin D) outside applicable regulatory limits or the Company's formulation specifications.

(b) Since January 1, 2021, there has been no recall, market withdrawal or safety alert relating to any product of the Company, and, to Seller's knowledge, no facts exist that would reasonably be expected to result in one.

(c) The Company's dry products carry a shelf life of up to eighteen (18) months from the date of manufacture. Section 4.14(c) of the Disclosure Schedule lists the inventory of finished goods held by the Company and, to Seller's knowledge, by its distributors and retail customers as of May 31, 2026, by lot and manufacture date.

**4.15 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all applicable environmental Laws, and there has been no release of hazardous materials at the Company's manufacturing facility that would reasonably be expected to give rise to material liability.

**4.16 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Seller Note and to perform its obligations hereunder and thereunder.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement has been, and at the Closing the Seller Note will be, duly executed and delivered by Buyer, and each constitutes or will constitute a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Buyer of this Agreement and the Seller Note do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient cash to pay the Closing Cash Payment.

**5.5 Investment Purpose.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.6 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to (a) conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, (b) preserve its business organization, permits and customer and supplier relationships, and (c) not amend any Material Contract, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, declare any dividend or issue any equity interest.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Company's properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel, including access to the manufacturing facility for quality and food-safety inspections.

**6.3 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, initiate or engage in discussions with any Person other than Buyer concerning the sale of the Company or its assets outside the ordinary course.

**6.4 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall, and shall cause the Company to, use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, the third-party consents listed in Section 7.2(c), each in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

**6.5 Notice of Certain Events.** Until the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied, and of any product complaint, regulatory inspection or test result that would reasonably be expected to lead to a recall.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and its business, except to the extent required by Law.

**6.7 Employees.** For twelve (12) months following the Closing, Buyer shall cause the Company to provide each continuing employee with base compensation no less favorable than that provided immediately before the Closing.

**6.8 Seller Guarantees.** Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause Seller and its Affiliates to be released, effective as of the Closing or as promptly as practicable thereafter, from the guaranties set forth in Section 6.8 of the Disclosure Schedule. Buyer shall not be required to pay any fee or provide any credit support to obtain any such release.

**6.9 Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement relating to this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by Law.

**6.10 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For five (5) years following the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) engage in, own, manage or finance any business that manufactures or sells dry or wet pet food products in the United States or Canada, or (b) solicit any customer, supplier or employee of the Company to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction of the following conditions: (a) any waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 applicable to the transactions contemplated by this Agreement shall have expired or been terminated; and (b) no Governmental Authority shall have enacted or issued any Law or order restraining or prohibiting the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Seller shall have obtained and delivered to Buyer the following consents, under the Contracts listed below, each of which may terminate upon a change of control of the Company absent such consent: (i) the consent of the landlord under the Plant Lease; and (ii) the consent of the lender under the Equipment Financing Agreement;

(d) Buyer shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a), 7.2(b) and 7.2(e) have been satisfied; and

(e) since the date of this Agreement, there shall not have occurred any recall or market withdrawal of any product of the Company.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and (c) Seller shall have received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Buyer certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b) have been satisfied.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the parties; (b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; (c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or (d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2, Section 6.9 and Article XI shall survive, and no termination shall relieve a party of liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. All other representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer contained in this Agreement, including the representations and warranties in Section 4.14, shall survive the Closing until nine (9) months following the Closing Date. Covenants to be performed after the Closing shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim for indemnification in respect of a representation or warranty may be made unless written notice of the claim is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period; a claim so noticed shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Product Recall Losses; and

(d) any Taxes of the Company for any period ending on or before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) Losses arising under any Seller Guarantee.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice to the party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"), describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *General Cap; Deductible.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for Fundamental Representations) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $540,000, after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of that amount. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $7,200,000 (the "**General Cap**").

(b) *Buyer Cap.* The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed $7,200,000.

(c) *Carve-Outs.* The deductible and the General Cap in Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation or to Product Recall Losses. The aggregate liability of Seller for such Losses, together with all other Losses under this Article IX, shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) *Fraud.* None of the limitations in this Section 9.5 shall apply to Losses arising from fraud.

**9.6 Materiality Scrape; Mitigation.** For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded. Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses after becoming aware of the event giving rise to them.

**9.7 Manner of Recovery; Set-Off.** Any Losses to which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article IX shall be recovered first by set-off against the principal and accrued interest outstanding under the Seller Note, in accordance with Section 4 of the Seller Note, and, to the extent the amounts outstanding under the Seller Note are insufficient, directly from Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds within ten (10) Business Days after final determination.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy; Tax Treatment.** Except for claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief and claims under Section 2.3, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. Any indemnification payment shall be treated as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare and file, at Seller's expense, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review at least twenty (20) days before filing.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any audit or proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or to such other address as a party may designate by notice).

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and the Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**11.7 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**11.9 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any other Transaction Document, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the Seller Note shall control with respect to set-off and recovery of indemnifiable Losses.

**11.10 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.11 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the state or federal courts located in the State of Delaware) for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.
