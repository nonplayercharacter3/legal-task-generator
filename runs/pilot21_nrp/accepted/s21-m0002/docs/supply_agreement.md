# DIGITAL CINEMA SERVICES SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Digital Cinema Services Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") between:

**Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 6100 Sunset Boulevard, Suite 900, Los Angeles, California 90028 ("**Supplier**"); and

**Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 520 Eighth Avenue, 14th Floor, New York, New York 10018 ("**Customer**").

Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Background

A. Supplier provides digital cinema services to film distributors, including mastering of Digital Cinema Packages, generation of Key Delivery Messages, duplication and shipment of hard drives, and electronic delivery of content to exhibitors.

B. Customer distributes motion pictures theatrically and wishes to buy digital cinema services from Supplier on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Form.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings below.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, for a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means direct or indirect ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

1.3 "**Contract Year**" means each successive 12-month period beginning on the Order Form Effective Date, as stated in the Order Form, and on each anniversary of that date during the Term.

1.4 "**Covered Services**" means DCP mastering, KDM generation, hard-drive duplication and shipment, and electronic delivery to exhibitors, in each case as performed by Supplier for Customer under this Agreement and the Order Form.

1.5 "**Content**" means any motion picture, trailer, advertisement, featurette or other audiovisual work, in any medium, including theatrical, streaming, airline, and home-entertainment deliverables, that Customer or any of its Affiliates distributes, licenses or otherwise makes available.

1.6 "**DCP**" or "**Digital Cinema Package**" means a set of digital files that stores and conveys digital cinema audio, image and data streams, prepared to the then-current Digital Cinema Initiatives (DCI) specification and SMPTE standards.

1.7 "**Exclusive Services**" means mastering, encoding and delivery services for any Content, including the Covered Services.

1.8 "**Exhibitor**" means any theatre, circuit or other exhibition venue to which Customer directs delivery of Content.

1.9 "**KDM**" or "**Key Delivery Message**" means a digital key that enables a specific server at a specific Exhibitor location to decrypt and play an encrypted DCP for a stated window of time.

1.10 "**Minimum Annual Commitment**" has the meaning given in the Order Form.

1.11 "**Order Form**" means the Order Form between the Parties with an Order Form Effective Date of March 1, 2025, as amended from time to time in accordance with Section 14.2.

1.12 "**Release Schedule**" means Customer's written schedule of planned theatrical releases, delivered under Section 3.2.

1.13 "**Service Levels**" means the service levels set out in Section 6.

1.14 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with each Renewal Term, if any.

1.15 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement, including "Initial Term," "Territory" and "Order Form Effective Date," have the meanings given in the Order Form.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form, unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

2.2 **Renewal.** On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms of three (3) years each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). Renewal will not occur if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before expiry of the then-current term. Each Renewal Term is on the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the Order Form then in effect.

2.3 **Effect of Expiry on Pending Orders.** If this Agreement expires while a work order is in progress, Supplier shall complete that work order. The terms of this Agreement continue to apply to that work order until it is completed.

## 3. Ordering and Performance of Services

3.1 **Services.** Supplier shall perform the Covered Services for Customer in accordance with this Agreement, the Order Form, and the technical specifications set out in each work order.

3.2 **Release Schedule.** Customer shall deliver a rolling Release Schedule to Supplier at least once each calendar quarter. The Release Schedule shall identify, for each planned release, the expected release date, the anticipated number of Exhibitor locations and the deliverables required. The Release Schedule is for planning purposes only. It does not obligate Customer to release any title.

3.3 **Work Orders.** Customer shall request Covered Services by submitting a written or electronic work order through Supplier's ordering portal or by email to Supplier's designated account representative. Each work order shall identify the title, the source materials, the deliverables, the Exhibitor delivery list and the required delivery dates. Supplier shall confirm each work order within one (1) business day of receipt.

3.4 **Source Materials.** Customer shall deliver source materials to Supplier in the formats and by the dates reasonably required by Supplier to meet the requested delivery dates. Supplier is not responsible for delay caused by late, incomplete or non-conforming source materials. Supplier shall notify Customer promptly of any such deficiency.

3.5 **KDM Generation.** Supplier shall generate and deliver KDMs for each Exhibitor screen identified by Customer. Each KDM shall carry the playback window specified by Customer. Supplier shall maintain current certificate data for Exhibitor servers and shall reissue any defective KDM within two (2) hours of notice during release weekends.

3.6 **Personnel.** Supplier shall assign a dedicated account representative to Customer. Supplier shall keep an operations desk staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, during the period from the Thursday before through the Monday after each theatrical release.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Exclusive Supply.** During the Term, Customer shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, obtain all of their requirements for Exclusive Services throughout the world solely from Supplier.

4.2 **No Third-Party Providers.** During the Term, neither Customer nor any of its Affiliates shall engage, or perform for itself, any service that falls within the Exclusive Services, except through Supplier.

4.3 **Supplier Commitment.** Supplier shall maintain the capacity, personnel and facilities reasonably required to meet Customer's requirements as shown in the most recent Release Schedule.

## 5. Fees, Minimum Annual Commitment and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Supplier the fees for the Covered Services set out in the Order Form.

5.2 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** Customer shall meet the Minimum Annual Commitment for each Contract Year, and shall pay any shortfall, in each case as set out in the Order Form.

5.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Covered Services completed in the preceding month. Each invoice shall itemize charges by title and by service.

5.4 **Payment.** Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within thirty (30) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Payments are to be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing.

5.5 **Disputed Invoices.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith. To do so, Customer must notify Supplier in writing, with reasonable detail, before the due date. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Customer shall pay any amount agreed or determined to be owed within ten (10) business days after resolution.

5.6 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.

5.7 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay any such taxes properly charged on the Covered Services, other than taxes based on Supplier's net income, property or employees.

5.8 **Records and Audit.** Supplier shall keep accurate records of all Covered Services performed and fees charged for at least three (3) years after the relevant Contract Year. Customer may audit those records, at its own cost, no more than once per Contract Year on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 6. Delivery and Service Levels

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver each DCP, hard drive and KDM to the Exhibitor locations stated in the applicable work order by the delivery date stated in that work order.

6.2 **Physical Delivery.** Supplier shall ship hard drives by tracked courier. Supplier shall bear the risk of loss of, or damage to, each hard drive until it is delivered to the Exhibitor. Supplier shall replace any lost, damaged or defective drive at its own cost on an expedited basis.

6.3 **Electronic Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver DCPs electronically to Exhibitors through satellite or terrestrial networks operated or contracted by Supplier. Supplier shall confirm the successful ingest of each electronic delivery to Customer.

6.4 **On-Time Delivery Service Level.** Supplier shall deliver at least 99.5% of DCPs and 99.8% of KDMs on or before the scheduled delivery date, measured monthly across all titles.

6.5 **Service Credits.** If Supplier fails to meet a Service Level in any month, Supplier shall credit Customer an amount equal to 5% of the fees invoiced for the affected title in that month for each full percentage point (or part of one) by which Supplier falls short. If a failure causes a missed screening at an Exhibitor location, Supplier shall additionally credit Customer US$1,500 for each missed screening. Service credits shall appear on the next invoice. If no further invoice will be issued, Supplier shall refund the credits to Customer within thirty (30) days.

6.6 **Re-Performance.** In addition to service credits, Supplier shall promptly re-perform, at no charge, any Covered Service that does not conform to the applicable work order or specifications.

## 7. Content Security

7.1 **Security Standards.** Supplier shall handle all Content and source materials in accordance with the DCI security requirements, the MPA Content Security Best Practices, and any reasonable additional requirements Customer communicates in writing.

7.2 **Facilities.** Supplier shall perform the Covered Services only at facilities that it has disclosed to Customer in writing.

7.3 **Security Incidents.** Supplier shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours after it becomes aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized access to, or copying or distribution of, any Content. Supplier shall cooperate fully with Customer's investigation and remediation.

7.4 **Return and Destruction.** Within thirty (30) days after Customer's written request, and in any event on expiry or termination of this Agreement, Supplier shall return or securely destroy all Content and source materials in its possession. Supplier shall certify the destruction in writing.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 **Customer Ownership.** As between the Parties, Customer or its licensors own all right, title and interest in the Content, the source materials and all deliverables Supplier prepares from them, including DCPs.

8.2 **License to Supplier.** Customer grants Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license, during the Term, to reproduce and transmit Content solely as necessary to perform the Covered Services.

8.3 **Supplier Tools.** Supplier retains ownership of its software, encoding tools, networks and other pre-existing technology. No license to them is granted to Customer except as needed to use the Covered Services.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 **Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a binding obligation enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

9.2 **Supplier.** Supplier warrants that:

(a) it will perform the Covered Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards;

(b) each DCP will conform to the applicable specifications and play correctly on DCI-compliant equipment;

(c) it holds, and will maintain, all licenses and certifications needed to perform the Covered Services; and

(d) the Covered Services and Supplier's tools will not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

9.3 **Customer.** Customer warrants that it has the rights needed to grant the license in Section 8.2.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as stated in this Agreement, neither Party gives any other warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of:

(a) any breach of Section 7 by Supplier or its subcontractors;

(b) any claim that the Covered Services or Supplier's tools infringe a third party's intellectual property rights; or

(c) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any claim that the Content, as supplied by Customer, infringes a third party's intellectual property rights.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall notify the indemnifying Party promptly of any claim. Delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent the delay causes it prejudice. The indemnifying Party controls the defense and settlement of the claim. It may not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its choice, at its own cost.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

11.2 **Cap.** Each Party's total liability arising out of this Agreement in any Contract Year is limited to the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10;

(b) a breach of Section 7 or Section 12;

(c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct; or

(d) Customer's obligation to pay fees and any shortfall payable under the Order Form.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement. It includes Content, Release Schedules, box-office data, Exhibitor lists and the terms of this Agreement and the Order Form.

12.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. It shall not disclose that information except to its employees, Affiliates, advisors and subcontractors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 12.

12.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or

(d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

12.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order. Where lawful, it shall give the other Party prompt notice so that the other Party may seek a protective order.

12.5 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 12 continue for three (3) years after expiry or termination of this Agreement. For Content, they continue for as long as the Content remains non-public.

## 13. Termination

13.1 **For Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, has a receiver appointed for all or substantially all of its assets, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement:

(a) Customer shall pay all fees for Covered Services performed through the effective date of termination;

(b) Supplier shall issue any outstanding service credits or refunds;

(c) Supplier shall comply with Section 7.4; and

(d) Supplier shall provide reasonable transition assistance, at its then-current rates, for up to ninety (90) days.

13.4 **Survival.** Sections 5, 7.4, 8.1, 9.4, 10, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 15 and 16 survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 14. Order Form; Relationship of Documents

14.1 **Order Form.** The Order Form is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. References to "this Agreement" include the Order Form, except where the context requires otherwise.

14.2 **Amendments.** This Agreement and the Order Form may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

14.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and the Order Form, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the Order Form shall control with respect to pricing, the Initial Term and the Minimum Annual Commitment.

## 15. Governing Law and Disputes

15.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any claim or dispute arising out of or relating to it, is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

15.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County, New York for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

15.3 **Escalation.** Before starting any proceeding, other than a proceeding for injunctive relief, a Party shall give the other written notice of the dispute. Senior executives of each Party shall then meet, in person or by video conference, within fifteen (15) days to try to resolve it in good faith.

15.4 **Injunctive Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of Section 7 or Section 12 may cause irreparable harm for which damages would be an inadequate remedy. The non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief in the courts specified in Section 15.2 without posting bond.

15.5 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
