This letter (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into under the Digital Cinema Services Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Supply Agreement**") between Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc. ("**Supplier**") and Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC ("**Customer**"). It is also entered into under Order Form No. BPR-PDCS-001, with an Order Form Effective Date of March 1, 2025 (the "**Order Form**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement or the Order Form, as applicable.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Order Form Section 4.1 provides that the rate for electronic delivery to Exhibitors is as separately agreed in writing by the Parties. This Side Letter records the rates the Parties have agreed for that service.

1.2 This Side Letter also records each Party's position on the notice that Customer delivered on February 2, 2026 under Order Form Section 3.4.

## 2. Electronic Delivery Rates

2.1 **Rates.** Customer shall pay the following fees for electronic delivery of DCPs to Exhibitors performed by Supplier under Supply Agreement Section 6.3:

| Service | Unit | Rate (US$) |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic delivery, feature (satellite or terrestrial network), United States | Per title, per location | 62 |
| Electronic delivery, feature (satellite or terrestrial network), Canada | Per title, per location | 74 |
| Electronic delivery, trailer or advertisement | Per title, per location | 14 |
| Electronic delivery, supplemental version (additional language subtitle or audio) | Per version, per location | 22 |
| Re-transmission following failed ingest (Supplier or network error) | Per title, per location | No charge |
| Re-transmission requested by Customer (other than Supplier or network error) | Per title, per location | 50% of the applicable rate |

2.2 **Application.** The rates in Section 2.1 apply to all electronic deliveries completed on or after March 1, 2026. Electronic deliveries completed before March 1, 2026 have been invoiced at the interim rates notified by Supplier, and the Parties confirm those invoices as final.

2.3 **Rate Card.** The rates in Section 2.1 are the rates for electronic delivery to Exhibitors agreed under Order Form Section 4.1. Order Form Section 4.3 applies to them.

2.4 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall itemize electronic delivery charges by title, by location and by service on each monthly invoice under Supply Agreement Section 5.3. Payment is governed by Supply Agreement Sections 5.4 to 5.6.

2.5 **Ingest Confirmation.** Supplier shall not invoice an electronic delivery until it has confirmed the successful ingest of that delivery to Customer under Supply Agreement Section 6.3.

## 3. Reduction Notice under Order Form Section 3.4

3.1 **Customer Notice.** On February 2, 2026, Customer delivered written notice to Supplier under Order Form Section 3.4. In that notice Customer elected to reduce the Minimum Annual Commitment for the second Contract Year by twenty-five percent (25%), to US$637,500 (the "**Reduction Notice**").

3.2 **Supplier Position.** By letter dated February 9, 2026, Supplier rejected the Reduction Notice as untimely under Order Form Section 3.4. Supplier's position is that the Minimum Annual Commitment for the second Contract Year remains US$850,000 in Covered Services fees.

3.3 **Customer Position.** Customer disputes Supplier's rejection. Customer maintains that the Reduction Notice is effective and that the Minimum Annual Commitment for the second Contract Year is US$637,500.

3.4 **Reservation of Rights.** Each Party reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Supply Agreement, the Order Form and applicable law with respect to the Reduction Notice and the Minimum Annual Commitment for the second Contract Year. Neither Party's entry into this Side Letter, nor its performance of this Side Letter, is an admission by that Party or a waiver of any of its rights, positions or defenses relating to the Reduction Notice.

3.5 **Dispute Resolution.** Any dispute concerning the Reduction Notice is subject to Supply Agreement Section 15.
