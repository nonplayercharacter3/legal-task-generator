# PRESS RELEASE

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## 1. Headline

**Breimourt Pictures Releasing Selects Padisk Digital Cinema Services for Theatrical Delivery in the United States and Canada**

*Multi-year agreement covers DCP mastering, KDM generation, hard-drive duplication and shipment, and electronic delivery to exhibitors for Breimourt's theatrical slate*

## 2. Dateline and Announcement

**NEW YORK and LOS ANGELES, March 4, 2025.** Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC ("Breimourt"), the independent theatrical film distributor, and Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc. ("Padisk") today announced a multi-year agreement. Under the agreement, Padisk will provide digital cinema services for Breimourt's theatrical releases in the United States and Canada.

The agreement took effect on March 1, 2025. Padisk will handle the technical preparation and delivery of Breimourt's feature films, trailers and advertising materials to exhibitors in both countries.

## 3. Services Covered

Under the agreement, Padisk will provide Breimourt with:

3.1 **DCP mastering.** Preparation of Digital Cinema Packages to the current Digital Cinema Initiatives (DCI) specification and SMPTE standards, in 2K and 4K, including additional language subtitle and audio versions.

3.2 **KDM generation.** Creation and delivery of Key Delivery Messages for each exhibitor screen, with a dedicated operations desk staffed around the clock over each release weekend.

3.3 **Hard-drive duplication and shipment.** Duplication of feature and trailer drives and tracked courier delivery to theatres across the United States and Canada.

3.4 **Electronic delivery.** Delivery of DCPs to exhibitors through satellite and terrestrial networks, with confirmation of successful ingest at each location.

All work will be performed under content security practices aligned with DCI security requirements and the MPA Content Security Best Practices.

## 5. About Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC

Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC is an independent theatrical film distributor headquartered in New York. The company acquires and releases feature films for theatrical audiences, working with exhibitors of all sizes, from national circuits to independent and specialty cinemas. More information is available at www.breimourt.com.

## 6. About Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc.

Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc. is a provider of digital cinema services to film distributors, including DCP mastering, KDM generation, hard-drive duplication and shipment, and electronic delivery to exhibitors. Padisk is headquartered in Los Angeles and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Padisk Holdings Ltd. of Toronto, Ontario. More information is available at www.padiskdcs.com.
