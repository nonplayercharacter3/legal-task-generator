## 1. Services and Territory

1.1 **Covered Services.** As used in this Order Form, Covered Services means physical DCP duplication and hard-drive shipment only.

1.2 **Territory.** The Territory is the United States and Canada.

1.3 **Scope of Services Ordered.** Supplier shall provide theatrical DCP services for Customer's theatrical releases in the Territory. These include DCP mastering, KDM generation, hard-drive duplication and shipment, and related quality-control services, each performed under work orders submitted in accordance with Supply Agreement Section 3.3.

1.4 **Service Levels.** The Service Levels and service credits set out in Supply Agreement Section 6 apply to all services ordered under this Order Form.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term.** The Initial Term begins on the Order Form Effective Date, March 1, 2025, and continues for 3 years from the Order Form Effective Date, ending February 29, 2028. Renewal is governed by Supply Agreement Section 2.2.

2.2 **Contract Years.** Contract Years are determined under Supply Agreement Section 1.3. The first Contract Year begins on March 1, 2025.

## 3. Minimum Annual Commitment

3.1 **Minimum Annual Commitment.** For each Contract Year of the Term, including each Contract Year of any Renewal Term, Customer commits to purchase US$850,000 in Covered Services fees per Contract Year (the "**Minimum Annual Commitment**").

3.2 **Shortfall.** If the Covered Services fees invoiced to Customer for a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Annual Commitment for that Contract Year, Customer shall pay Supplier the full amount of the difference (the "**Shortfall Amount**"). Supplier shall invoice the Shortfall Amount within thirty (30) days after the end of the Contract Year. Customer shall pay it in accordance with Supply Agreement Section 5.4.

3.3 **Calculation.** Covered Services fees count toward the Minimum Annual Commitment for the Contract Year in which the related services are completed. They are counted net of taxes and of any service credits issued under Supply Agreement Section 6.5. Fees counted toward one Contract Year may not be carried forward or back to another Contract Year.

3.4 **Reduction Option.** Customer has a one-time option to reduce the Minimum Annual Commitment for the second Contract Year by up to twenty-five percent (25%). Customer may exercise the option only by written notice to Supplier, given in accordance with Supply Agreement Section 16.1, delivered no later than 45 days before the first day of the second Contract Year. The notice must state the reduced Minimum Annual Commitment. A reduction under this Section 3.4 applies to the second Contract Year only and does not affect the Minimum Annual Commitment for any other Contract Year.

3.5 **Annual Statement.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Contract Year, Supplier shall deliver to Customer a statement showing the Covered Services fees invoiced in that Contract Year and any Shortfall Amount. Customer's audit rights under Supply Agreement Section 5.8 apply to each statement.

4.1 **Rate Card.** Customer shall pay the following fees for services ordered under this Order Form:

|---|---|---|
| DCP mastering, feature (2K, up to 150 minutes) | Per version | 2,400 |
| DCP mastering, feature (4K, up to 150 minutes) | Per version | 3,600 |

| KDM generation | Per KDM | 6.50 |
| KDM reissue (Supplier error) | Per KDM | No charge |
| Hard-drive duplication, feature | Per drive | 95 |
| Hard-drive duplication, trailer pack | Per drive | 40 |
| Hard-drive shipment, standard ground | Per drive, per location | 38 |
| Hard-drive shipment, expedited (next day) | Per drive, per location | 85 |
| Hard-drive shipment, Canada (standard) | Per drive, per location | 55 |
| Drive retrieval and secure wipe | Per drive | 18 |
| Electronic delivery to Exhibitors | Per title, per location | As separately agreed in writing by the Parties |

4.2 **Volume Pricing.** If Customer orders more than 1,500 feature hard-drive duplications in a Contract Year, the hard-drive duplication rate for feature drives is reduced to US$85 per drive for the remainder of that Contract Year.

4.3 **Rate Adjustment.** All rates under this Order Form, including any rates agreed under Section 4.1, increase by seven percent (7%) at the start of each Renewal Term. Rates are otherwise fixed for the Initial Term.

4.4 **Rush Work.** For mastering work orders requiring delivery within forty-eight (48) hours of Supplier's receipt of conforming source materials, Supplier may charge a rush surcharge of twenty-five percent (25%) of the applicable mastering rate. Supplier must notify Customer of the surcharge before starting the work.

4.5 **Pass-Through Costs.** Customs duties, brokerage fees and taxes on cross-border shipments to Canada are passed through at cost, without markup, and itemized on the applicable invoice.

## 5. Invoicing and Payment

5.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears under Supply Agreement Section 5.3. Each invoice shall be sent to the Customer Billing Contact.

5.2 **Payment Terms.** Payment terms, disputed invoices and late payment are governed by Supply Agreement Sections 5.4 to 5.6.

## 6. General

6.1 **Incorporation.** This Order Form is incorporated into the Supply Agreement under Supply Agreement Section 14.1.

6.2 **Amendment.** This Order Form may be amended only in accordance with Supply Agreement Section 14.2.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Order Form may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
