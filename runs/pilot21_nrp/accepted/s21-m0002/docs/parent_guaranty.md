# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2025 by **Padisk Holdings Ltd.**, a corporation existing under the laws of the Province of Ontario, with its registered office at 200 Bay Street, Suite 3100, Toronto, Ontario M5J 2J2 (the "**Guarantor**"), in favour of **Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 520 Eighth Avenue, 14th Floor, New York, New York 10018 (the "**Beneficiary**").

## Recitals

A. Padisk Digital Cinema Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Supplier**"), and the Beneficiary have entered into a Digital Cinema Services Supply Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Supply Agreement**"). They have also entered into Order Form No. BPR-PDCS-001 with an Order Form Effective Date of March 1, 2025 (the "**Order Form**").

B. The Supplier is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Guarantor. The Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the Supplier entering into the Supply Agreement and the Order Form.

C. The Beneficiary required this Guaranty as a condition of entering into the Supply Agreement and the Order Form.

The Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

1.2 **"Agreement"** means the Supply Agreement and the Order Form, each as amended from time to time.

1.3 **"Guaranteed Obligations"** has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

1.4 **"Demand"** means a written demand for payment made by the Beneficiary under Section 4.

1.5 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "including" and "includes" mean "including without limitation". A reference to a Section is to a Section of this Guaranty unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Guaranty

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** The Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to the Beneficiary the due and punctual payment, when due, of the following amounts owed by the Supplier under the Agreement (together, the "**Guaranteed Obligations**"):

(a) all service credits and refunds of service credits payable under Supply Agreement Section 6.5;

(b) all refunds and outstanding service credits payable on expiry or termination under Supply Agreement Section 13.3(b);

(c) all amounts payable by the Supplier under its indemnification obligations in Supply Agreement Section 10.1; and

(d) all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by the Beneficiary in enforcing this Guaranty.

2.2 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It extends to all Guaranteed Obligations arising during the Term, whether arising before or after any amendment, renewal or extension of the Agreement.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. The Beneficiary is not required to take any action or exhaust any remedy against the Supplier or any other person before making a Demand on the Guarantor.

## 3. Nature of Obligations

3.1 **Primary Obligation.** The Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are primary obligations and are independent of the Supplier's obligations. A separate action may be brought against the Guarantor whether or not an action is brought against the Supplier.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** The Guarantor's obligations are not released, discharged or reduced by:

(a) any amendment, renewal, extension or waiver of any provision of the Agreement;

(b) any time, indulgence or forbearance granted to the Supplier;

(c) any change in the ownership, control, name or corporate existence of the Supplier or the Guarantor;

(d) the insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or winding-up of the Supplier; or

(e) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of the Agreement as against the Supplier resulting from the Supplier's lack of capacity or authority.

## 4. Demand and Payment

4.1 **Demand.** If the Supplier fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, the Beneficiary may make a Demand on the Guarantor. Each Demand must identify the Guaranteed Obligation, the amount claimed and the provision of the Agreement under which it is payable.

4.2 **Payment.** The Guarantor shall pay the amount stated in each Demand within fifteen (15) business days after receipt. Payment shall be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer to the account designated by the Beneficiary in the Demand.

4.3 **Multiple Demands.** The Beneficiary may make more than one Demand.

## 5. Waivers

5.1 The Guarantor waives notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, presentment, protest, notice of dishonour and notice of default by the Supplier, other than the Demand.

5.2 The Guarantor waives any right to require the Beneficiary to proceed first against the Supplier or against any security held for the Guaranteed Obligations.

## 6. Reinstatement

If any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation by the Supplier is set aside, rescinded or required to be returned in any insolvency or bankruptcy proceeding, this Guaranty will continue or be reinstated for that payment as if it had not been made.

## 7. Representations and Warranties

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Beneficiary that:

(a) it is a corporation duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of the Province of Ontario;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(d) this Guaranty is a legal, valid and binding obligation of the Guarantor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms; and

(e) the execution and performance of this Guaranty do not breach its constating documents, any law binding on it, or any agreement to which it is a party.

## 8. Subrogation and Subordination

8.1 **Subrogation.** The Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution or indemnity against the Supplier for amounts paid under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full.

8.2 **Subordination.** Any indebtedness of the Supplier to the Guarantor is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations then due and payable.

## 9. Taxes and Set-Off

9.1 **No Deduction.** All payments by the Guarantor shall be made without set-off or counterclaim and free of any deduction or withholding for taxes, unless required by law. If a deduction or withholding is required by law, the Guarantor shall pay such additional amounts as are needed for the Beneficiary to receive the full amount it would have received without the deduction or withholding.

9.2 **Currency.** The Guarantor's obligations are payable in U.S. dollars. If any amount is received in another currency, the Guarantor's obligation is discharged only to the extent of the U.S. dollar amount the Beneficiary is able to purchase with that currency on the business day after receipt.

## 10. Term and Release

10.1 **Term.** This Guaranty remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations arising during the Term, including any Renewal Term, have been paid in full.

10.2 **No Revocation.** The Guarantor may not revoke this Guaranty while the Agreement remains in effect.

## 11. Notices

11.1 All notices and Demands under this Guaranty must be in writing. They must be delivered by hand, by internationally recognized courier, or by email with a confirmation copy sent by courier, to the addresses below or to any other address a party designates by notice. A notice is effective on receipt.

- To the Guarantor: Padisk Holdings Ltd., 200 Bay Street, Suite 3100, Toronto, Ontario M5J 2J2, Attention: Corporate Secretary; email: corpsec@padiskholdings.com
- To the Beneficiary: Breimourt Pictures Releasing LLC, 520 Eighth Avenue, 14th Floor, New York, New York 10018, Attention: EVP, Business and Legal Affairs; email: legal@breimourt.com

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty, and any claim or dispute arising out of or relating to this Guaranty or the Guaranteed Obligations, is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in that province, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Guarantor and the Beneficiary irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Ontario Superior Court of Justice sitting in Toronto, Ontario for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty or the Guaranteed Obligations. Each of them waives any objection to venue in that court and any claim that it is an inconvenient forum.

## 13. General

13.1 **Assignment.** The Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any permitted assignee of the Agreement under Supply Agreement Section 16.2. The Guarantor may not assign its obligations under this Guaranty without the Beneficiary's prior written consent.

13.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Guarantor and the Beneficiary. A failure or delay by the Beneficiary in exercising any right does not waive that right.

13.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid or unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

13.4 **Successors.** This Guaranty binds the Guarantor and its successors and enures to the benefit of the Beneficiary and its successors and permitted assigns.

13.5 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. All counterparts together constitute one instrument.
