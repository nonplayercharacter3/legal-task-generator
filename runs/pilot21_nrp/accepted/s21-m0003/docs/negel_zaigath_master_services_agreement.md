# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Vertical Transportation Maintenance Services**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or this "**MSA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2023 (the "**MSA Effective Date**" or "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Negel Realty Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Customer**"); and

**Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Customer owns and operates office buildings in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, which contain passenger elevators, freight elevators, escalators and related vertical transportation equipment.

B. Provider is in the business of inspecting, maintaining, repairing and modernizing vertical transportation equipment and holds the licenses required to perform that work in the City of New York.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to perform vertical transportation services under the terms of this Agreement and one or more Statements of Work.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms have the meanings given below or elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**After-Hours Callback**" has the meaning given in Section 5.4.

1.3 "**Amendment**" means a written instrument that amends this Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both Parties in accordance with Section 15.6.

1.4 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

1.5 "**Business Hours**" means 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., New York City time, on each Business Day.

1.6 "**Customer Property**" means any building, structure or real property owned, leased, managed or hereafter acquired by Customer or any of its Affiliates, wherever located.

1.7 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

1.8 "**Covered Buildings**" means the buildings identified as Covered Buildings in a Statement of Work.

1.9 "**Entrapment**" means any event in which one or more persons are confined in an elevator car and unable to exit without assistance.

1.10 "**Equipment**" means the passenger elevators, freight elevators, service elevators, escalators, moving walks, wheelchair lifts and dumbwaiters, together with all associated machinery, controllers, hoistway and pit components, door equipment, wiring, signal fixtures and safety devices, located at the Covered Buildings and identified in a Statement of Work.

1.11 "**Fees**" means the monthly maintenance fees, time-and-material charges, overtime charges and other amounts payable by Customer under this Agreement and each Statement of Work.

1.12 "**Law**" means any applicable federal, state or local statute, ordinance, code, rule or regulation, including the New York City Building Code, the rules of the New York City Department of Buildings and ASME A17.1.

1.13 "**Premises**" means the Covered Buildings, the land on which they are located, and all common areas, machine rooms, hoistways, pits and other spaces within them.

1.14 "**Service Call**" means a request by Customer or its building personnel for Provider to attend to a malfunction, shutdown, Entrapment or other condition of the Equipment, received during Business Hours through Provider's dispatch center by telephone or through Provider's customer portal.

1.15 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by both Parties under Section 2.1 describing the Covered Buildings, Equipment, Services, Fees and service levels for a particular engagement.

1.16 "**Services**" means the Vertical Transportation Services and any other work Provider performs under this Agreement or a Statement of Work.

1.17 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term, as provided in Section 12.

1.18 "**Vertical Transportation Services**" means the inspection, preventive maintenance, testing, adjustment, lubrication, repair, replacement of parts, callback and emergency response, and related services for elevators, escalators and other vertical transportation equipment.

## 2. STATEMENTS OF WORK; STRUCTURE

2.1 **Statements of Work.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work signed by both Parties. Each Statement of Work shall identify the Covered Buildings, the Equipment, the scope of Services, the Fees and any service levels and service credits applicable to the Services.

2.2 **Incorporation.** Each Statement of Work is governed by and incorporates this Agreement. A Statement of Work may not amend this Agreement except by an Amendment.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising the Parties' agreement, the following order of precedence shall apply: Amendments, then the MSA, then any Statement of Work.

## 3. ENGAGEMENT; STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

3.1 **Engagement.** Customer engages Provider to perform, and Provider agrees to perform, the Services in accordance with this Agreement and each Statement of Work.

3.2 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a good, safe and workmanlike manner, in accordance with prevailing industry standards for comparable first-class office buildings in Manhattan, the original equipment manufacturer's recommendations and all applicable Law.

3.3 **Licenses and Permits.** Provider shall obtain and maintain, at its own expense, all licenses, registrations and permits required by Law to perform the Services, including the licenses required of elevator agency directors and elevator mechanics by the New York City Department of Buildings.

3.4 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Customer shall engage Provider as its exclusive provider of Vertical Transportation Services at every Customer Property, and Customer shall not engage, or permit any Affiliate to engage, any other person to perform Vertical Transportation Services at any Customer Property, except as Provider may agree in writing.

3.5 **Safety Testing.** Provider shall perform, or witness as required, all periodic inspections and tests of the Equipment required by Law, including Category 1 and Category 5 tests, and shall file all required reports with the New York City Department of Buildings within the time required by Law.

## 4. PERSONNEL

4.1 **Qualified Personnel.** Provider shall assign licensed, trained and qualified mechanics and helpers to perform the Services. Provider may replace assigned personnel on reasonable notice to Customer.

4.2 **Removal.** Customer may, acting reasonably, require Provider to remove from the Premises any Provider personnel whose conduct is unsafe or unprofessional, and Provider shall promptly provide a qualified replacement.

4.3 **Site Rules.** Provider's personnel shall comply with Customer's building rules, security procedures and reasonable instructions while at the Premises, as communicated to Provider in writing.

4.4 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Provider is solely responsible for the wages, benefits, payroll taxes and supervision of its personnel, and for compliance with any collective bargaining agreement to which Provider is a party.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees exclude applicable sales and use taxes, which Customer shall pay where required by Law.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice the monthly maintenance fee monthly in advance and all other Fees monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail.

5.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider of the dispute in writing before the due date, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

5.4 **After-Hours Callbacks.** A request for service received outside Business Hours is an "**After-Hours Callback**." Provider shall dispatch personnel in response to After-Hours Callbacks as soon as practicable, and Customer shall pay for the labor expended on each After-Hours Callback, including travel time, at Provider's overtime rates set out in the applicable Statement of Work. The monthly maintenance fee does not include After-Hours Callbacks.

5.5 **Annual Adjustment.** Unless a Statement of Work provides otherwise, Provider may adjust the monthly maintenance fee once in each contract year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, by no more than the percentage increase in the wage rates payable to elevator mechanics under the collective bargaining agreement then in effect for the New York City area.

## 6. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

6.1 **Access.** Customer shall give Provider reasonable access to the Premises, machine rooms, hoistways and pits as needed to perform the Services.

6.2 **Notice of Conditions.** Customer shall promptly notify Provider of any malfunction, damage or unsafe condition of the Equipment of which Customer's building personnel become aware.

6.3 **Use of Equipment.** Customer shall not permit any person other than Provider to adjust, repair or alter the Equipment during the Term, except in an emergency where Provider is unable to respond.

## 7. PARTS, MATERIALS AND RECORDS

7.1 **Parts.** Provider shall use new parts that are OEM or equal in quality, and shall maintain a reasonable inventory of commonly needed parts for the Equipment.

7.2 **Title.** Title to replacement parts installed in the Equipment passes to Customer upon installation. Title to removed parts passes to Provider, which shall dispose of them in accordance with Law.

7.3 **Maintenance Records.** Provider shall keep a maintenance control program and log for each unit of Equipment at the applicable Covered Building, as required by ASME A17.1, and shall make those records available to Customer and to inspectors on request.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Pre-Existing Materials.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, including, in the case of Provider, its diagnostic tools, software and maintenance procedures.

8.2 **Reports.** Inspection reports, maintenance records and other reports Provider prepares for Customer under this Agreement become the property of Customer upon payment of the applicable Fees.

8.3 **Diagnostic Tools.** Where Equipment requires proprietary diagnostic tools or software for maintenance, Provider shall make those tools available as needed to perform the Services during the Term.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including the terms of this Agreement, building security plans and pricing.

9.2 **Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.

9.3 **Exceptions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law or legal process, after giving the other Party prompt notice where permitted.

9.4 **Publicity.** Neither Party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the other Party's prior written approval of its content.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates, and their respective members, managers, officers, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligent acts or omissions or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel or subcontractors in performing the Services.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the condition of the Premises or the Equipment, whether or not caused in part by Provider, or arising out of the negligent acts or omissions or willful misconduct of Customer or its employees, tenants or contractors (other than Provider).

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim, provided that delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim, with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, and the indemnified Party shall cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost rents, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) Customer's obligations under Section 10.2; (b) Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due under this Agreement; or (c) a Party's liability for fraud.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the MSA Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three years from the MSA Effective Date, ending February 28, 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under this Section 12.

12.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, and at the end of each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for a renewal term of five years (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before expiration of the then-current term.

12.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or that Statement of Work and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

12.4 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.5 **Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination, Customer shall pay Provider all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Provider shall deliver to Customer all maintenance records, logs, wiring diagrams and other documentation for the Equipment in its possession. Termination of this Agreement terminates every Statement of Work then in effect.

12.6 **Survival.** Sections 7.2, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.5, 14 and 15 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 **Provider Coverage.** During the Term, Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations and contractual liability, with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) umbrella or excess liability insurance with limits of not less than $10,000,000; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law and employer's liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000; and (d) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000.

13.2 **Additional Insureds.** Provider shall name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds under the policies in Sections 13.1(a) and 13.1(b) on a primary and non-contributory basis, and shall deliver certificates of insurance to Customer before beginning the Services and upon each renewal.

13.3 **Customer Coverage.** Customer shall maintain commercial general liability and property insurance for the Premises in amounts customary for owners of comparable Manhattan office buildings.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

14.2 **Forum.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

14.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation (other than for injunctive relief), a Party shall give the other written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within fifteen (15) days to attempt to resolve it in good faith.

14.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ITS RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

15.2 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, civil disturbance, utility failure or governmental action, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. A strike or labor dispute involving Provider's own personnel is not a force majeure event as to Provider unless it is industry-wide in the New York City area.

15.3 **Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Customer's prior written consent, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors.

15.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.4 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

15.5 **Waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

15.6 **Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties that expressly refers to this Agreement.

15.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by Law.

15.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
