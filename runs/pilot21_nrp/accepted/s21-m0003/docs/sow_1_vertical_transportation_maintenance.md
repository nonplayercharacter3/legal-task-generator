# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

**Vertical Transportation Maintenance Services: Covered Buildings**

This Statement of Work No. 1 (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 1, 2023 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between **Negel Realty Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Customer**"), and **Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Provider**"). It is issued under the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Provider dated as of March 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**" or the "**MSA**").

## 1. INCORPORATION; DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Incorporation.** This SOW is a Statement of Work under Section 2.1 of the Agreement. It is governed by and incorporates the Agreement.

1.2 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.3 **Additional Definitions.** In this SOW:

(a) "**Response Time**" means the period from Provider's dispatch center receiving a Service Call to the arrival at the affected Covered Building of a licensed Provider mechanic who is prepared to begin work.

(b) "**Unit**" means each individual elevator, escalator or other item of Equipment listed in Schedule B.

(c) "**Monthly Maintenance Fee**" means the fee set out in Section 5.1.

## 2. SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT

2.1 **Covered Buildings; Exclusive Provider.** Provider shall serve as Customer's exclusive vertical transportation provider for the five Covered Buildings in Manhattan listed in SOW Schedule A. Each building listed in Schedule A is a "Covered Building" for purposes of the Agreement.

2.2 **Equipment.** The Equipment covered by this SOW is the Equipment at the Covered Buildings listed in Schedule B. The Parties may add or remove Units by written change order signed by both Parties. Each change order shall state any resulting adjustment to the Monthly Maintenance Fee.

2.3 **Takeover Inspection.** Within forty-five (45) days after the SOW Effective Date, Provider shall complete a takeover inspection of each Unit and deliver a written report to Customer. The report shall identify any condition that requires repair outside the scope of the Monthly Maintenance Fee. Provider shall not charge Customer for repairing any condition that Provider does not identify in that report and that existed on the SOW Effective Date, unless the condition could not reasonably have been found by a competent takeover inspection.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Preventive Maintenance.** Provider shall perform preventive maintenance on each Unit under a written maintenance control program that meets ASME A17.1 and the OEM's recommendations. At a minimum, Provider shall:

(a) examine, lubricate, clean and adjust machines, motors, generators, controllers, selectors, governors, brakes, sheaves, ropes, door operators, door equipment, car and hall signal fixtures and safety devices;

(b) examine and adjust escalator steps, step chains, handrails, combplates, drives and safety devices;

(c) make regular minimum-frequency visits to each Covered Building of not less than two (2) visits per month for passenger and freight elevators and two (2) visits per month for escalators; and

(d) record each visit and the work performed in the maintenance log for the affected Unit kept under Section 7.3 of the Agreement.

3.2 **Repairs and Parts.** The Monthly Maintenance Fee includes the labor and parts needed to repair or replace the components listed in Section 3.1(a) and (b) that fail from normal wear and tear. It does not include repairs required because of vandalism, misuse, flooding, fire, power surges or other causes outside Provider's control. Provider shall perform those repairs on a time-and-material basis at the rates in Section 5.3, after giving Customer a written estimate.

3.3 **Testing and Inspections.** Provider shall perform all Category 1 and Category 5 tests and periodic inspections required by Law for each Unit, as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement. Provider shall coordinate with Customer's third-party witnessing inspector and file all required reports with the New York City Department of Buildings. The Monthly Maintenance Fee includes Provider's labor for those tests. Customer shall pay the fees of its witnessing inspector and any Department of Buildings filing fees.

3.4 **Violations.** Provider shall correct, within the time allowed by the issuing authority, any violation issued against a Unit that arises from a condition within the scope of Section 3.1 or 3.2.

3.5 **Dedicated Mechanic.** Provider shall assign a dedicated route mechanic to each Covered Building. The same mechanic shall perform scheduled maintenance at that Covered Building wherever practicable.

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 **Service Call Response.** Provider shall respond to each Service Call within thirty (30) minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Where a Service Call reports an Entrapment, Provider shall dispatch the nearest available licensed mechanic immediately. That mechanic shall remain on site until all persons have been released and the affected Unit has been secured or returned to service.

4.2 **Non-Emergency Calls.** For a Service Call that reports a Unit out of service without an Entrapment, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to return the Unit to service within four (4) hours after arrival. If that is not possible, Provider shall give Customer's building manager a written status report and an estimated return-to-service time.

4.3 **Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet the Response Time in Section 4.1 for a Service Call, Provider shall credit Customer against the next invoice for the Monthly Maintenance Fee as follows:

| Response Time for the Service Call | Credit per Service Call |
|---|---|
| More than 30 minutes and up to 60 minutes | $250 |
| More than 60 minutes and up to 120 minutes | $750 |
| More than 120 minutes | $1,500 |

The service credits in any calendar month shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the Monthly Maintenance Fee for that month.

4.4 **Uptime.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep each passenger elevator Unit in service not less than 98% of the time in each calendar month. This excludes scheduled maintenance, testing required by Law, and downtime caused by events outside the scope of Section 3.2.

4.5 **Reporting.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar quarter, Provider shall give Customer a report for each Covered Building. The report shall show:

(a) Service Calls received and Response Times;

(b) Entrapments;

(c) Unit downtime;

(d) tests performed; and

(e) open violations and recommended repairs.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Monthly Maintenance Fee.** Customer shall pay Provider a Monthly Maintenance Fee of $38,500 for all Covered Buildings combined. Provider shall invoice it monthly in advance under Section 5.2 of the Agreement. The Monthly Maintenance Fee is subject to annual adjustment as provided in Section 5.5 of the Agreement.

5.2 **Allocation.** Schedule A sets out an allocation of the Monthly Maintenance Fee among the Covered Buildings. The allocation is for Customer's internal accounting only. Provider shall show it on each invoice.

5.3 **Labor Rates.** The following hourly rates apply to time-and-material work and to labor for which overtime rates are payable under the Agreement. They apply per mechanic-and-helper team, including travel time:

| Rate Category | Hours | Rate per Team Hour |
|---|---|---|
| Straight time | Business Hours | $310 |
| Overtime | Outside Business Hours on a Business Day; Saturdays | $525 |
| Premium overtime | Sundays and holidays | $640 |

The rates in this Section 5.3 are subject to the same annual adjustment as the Monthly Maintenance Fee.

5.4 **Parts Outside Scope.** Parts for work outside the scope of Section 3.2 shall be billed at Provider's cost plus fifteen percent (15%). Provider shall supply supporting invoices on request.

## 6. TERM OF THIS SOW

6.1 **Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues for the Term of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier under Section 12 of the Agreement.

6.2 **Removal of a Covered Building.** Customer may remove a Covered Building from this SOW on ninety (90) days' written notice if Customer or its Affiliate ceases to own that Covered Building. On the effective date of removal, the Monthly Maintenance Fee shall be reduced by the amount allocated to that Covered Building in Schedule A.

## 7. CONTACTS

7.1 **Customer Contact.** Customer's Director of Engineering, or another person Customer designates in writing.

7.2 **Provider Contact.** Provider's Account Manager for the Negel portfolio, or another person Provider designates in writing.

7.3 **Dispatch.** Provider's dispatch center shall be staffed and reachable by telephone at all times. Provider shall give each Covered Building's management office the dispatch telephone number and customer portal access credentials before the SOW Effective Date.
