# Zaigath Vertical Services Selected to Maintain Vertical Transportation at Five Negel Realty Partners Office Towers in Manhattan

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**NEW YORK, NY, March 6, 2023**

## 1. Announcement

Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc. ("Zaigath"), a New York-based elevator and escalator maintenance contractor, today announced that Negel Realty Partners LLC ("Negel") has selected it to provide vertical transportation maintenance services at five of Negel's Manhattan office towers. Zaigath will serve as Negel's exclusive vertical transportation provider at the five buildings under a Master Services Agreement and an accompanying Statement of Work, both dated as of March 1, 2023.

## 2. The Covered Buildings

The engagement covers the following five properties:

1. **Negel Tower at Madison**, 425 Madison Avenue
2. **1180 Sixth**, 1180 Avenue of the Americas
3. **The Lexington Building**, 590 Lexington Avenue
4. **33 Maiden**, 33 Maiden Lane
5. **Park South Center**, 260 Park Avenue South

Together, the buildings contain 64 passenger elevators, 7 freight and service elevators, 8 escalators, 2 wheelchair lifts and 1 dumbwaiter.

## 3. Scope of Services

Under the engagement, Zaigath will be responsible for:

- preventive maintenance under a written maintenance control program that meets ASME A17.1;
- repairs;
- the periodic Category 1 and Category 5 testing required by the New York City Department of Buildings;
- callback and emergency response.

Zaigath will assign a dedicated route mechanic to each building. It will also give each building's management office access to its dispatch center, which is staffed around the clock, and to its online customer portal.

Before routine service begins, Zaigath will complete a takeover inspection of every unit in the portfolio.

## 4. Quotes

"Negel's portfolio includes some of the most heavily traveled office buildings in Midtown and Lower Manhattan, and we are proud to have been chosen to keep them moving," said a spokesperson for Zaigath. "Our licensed mechanics will bring the same safety-first approach to these buildings that our customers across the city rely on every day."

"Reliable elevator service is fundamental to the tenant experience in our buildings," said a representative of Negel Realty Partners. "We selected Zaigath for its depth of licensed personnel in New York City, its maintenance program and its commitment to safety and responsiveness."

## 5. Transition

The transition to Zaigath began on March 1, 2023. The initial term of the agreement is three years. Zaigath and Negel's engineering team are coordinating the transition so that service to tenants and visitors continues without interruption.

## 6. About Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc.

Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc. is a New York corporation headquartered in New York, New York. It inspects, maintains, repairs and modernizes elevators, escalators and other vertical transportation equipment for commercial, residential and institutional property owners. Zaigath holds the licenses the New York City Department of Buildings requires for elevator agency directors and elevator mechanics.

## 7. About Negel Realty Partners LLC

Negel Realty Partners LLC is a New York limited liability company that owns and operates office buildings in the Borough of Manhattan.
