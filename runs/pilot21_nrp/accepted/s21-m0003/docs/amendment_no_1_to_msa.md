# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Vertical Transportation Maintenance Services**

This Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of January 20, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between:

**Negel Realty Partners LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Customer**"); and

**Zaigath Vertical Services, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal office in New York, New York ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**RECITALS**

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2023 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider performs Vertical Transportation Services at the Covered Buildings listed in Schedule A to Statement of Work No. 1 dated as of March 1, 2023 (the "**SOW**").

B. Customer delivered to Provider a notice of non-renewal dated November 14, 2025 under Section 12.2 of the Agreement. Provider has advised Customer that it considers the notice untimely and that the Agreement renewed through February 28, 2031. Customer disputes Provider's position and reserves all of its rights.

C. Customer is evaluating the possible sale of one or more Covered Buildings. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement to permit Customer to assign the Agreement and the SOW, in whole or in part, in connection with a transfer of a Covered Building, and to make the other changes set out in this Amendment.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement or the SOW to "this Agreement," "the Agreement" or "the MSA" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. NOTICES

2.1 **Customer Notice Address.** The address for notices to Customer stated on the signature page of the Agreement is replaced with the following:

Negel Realty Partners LLC
Attn: General Counsel
425 Madison Avenue
New York, NY 10017

with a copy (which does not constitute notice) to Customer's Director of Engineering at the same address.

2.2 **Provider Notice Address.** The address for notices to Provider stated on the signature page of the Agreement is unchanged.

## 3. ASSIGNMENT

3.1 **Replacement of Section 15.4.** Section 15.4 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **15.4 Assignment by Customer.** Customer may assign this Agreement or any Statement of Work, in whole or in part, without Provider's consent, to (a) any Affiliate of Customer, or (b) any purchaser or other transferee of a Covered Building, in which case the assignment may be limited to the rights and obligations under the applicable Statement of Work relating to that Covered Building. Customer shall give Provider written notice of any such assignment within thirty (30) days after it takes effect. Upon the assignee's written assumption of the assigned obligations, Customer is released from those obligations to the extent they arise after the effective date of the assignment. Except as provided in this Section 15.4, Customer may assign this Agreement only with the prior written consent of Provider, which Provider shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

3.2 **Partial Assignment of the SOW.** Where Customer assigns the SOW in part under Section 15.4 of the Agreement, as amended, in respect of a Covered Building, then on the effective date of the assignment (a) that Covered Building ceases to be a Covered Building under the SOW as between Customer and Provider, and (b) the Monthly Maintenance Fee payable by Customer is reduced by the amount allocated to that Covered Building in Schedule A to the SOW. The ninety (90) day notice period in Section 6.2 of the SOW does not apply to a Covered Building that is the subject of a partial assignment under this Section 3.

## 4. RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

4.1 **No Acceptance of Renewal.** Nothing in this Amendment, and neither Party's execution of or performance under this Amendment, is an acceptance by Customer of any renewal of the Agreement, an admission that Customer's notice of non-renewal was untimely, or a waiver of any right, claim or defense of Customer relating to the expiration or renewal of the Agreement, all of which Customer expressly reserves.

4.2 **Provider's Position.** Nothing in this Amendment is a waiver by Provider of its position described in Recital B.

## 5. GENERAL

5.1 **Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement and the SOW remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

5.2 **Amendment under Section 15.6.** This Amendment is an Amendment for purposes of the Agreement and is made in accordance with Section 15.6 of the Agreement.

5.3 **Governing Law.** This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 14 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of or relating to this Amendment.

5.4 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
