# INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

## Proposed Supply Arrangement for Architectural Glass

**Date:** October 18, 2023

**Parties:** Seineix Glassworks LLC ("**Supplier**") and Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc. ("**Buyer**")

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms the Parties have discussed for a long-term supply arrangement under which Supplier would supply architectural glass to Buyer for its curtain-wall and facade projects. Except as stated in Section 12, this term sheet is not binding. It does not create an obligation to negotiate or enter into any agreement. Any binding obligations will arise only under definitive agreements signed by both Parties.

## 1. Transaction Overview

1.1 Supplier would supply coated insulating glass units ("**IGUs**") and other architectural glass products to Buyer for use in unitized and stick-built curtain-wall and facade systems that Buyer fabricates for commercial construction projects.

1.2 The Parties expect the definitive documentation to consist of:

(a) a master supply agreement (the "**Supply Agreement**");

(b) a quality agreement covering the quality system, inspection, testing, documentation and nonconformance procedures (the "**Quality Agreement**"); and

(c) one or more order forms setting out products, pricing, lead times, term and notice details (each an "**Order Form**").

## 2. Products

2.1 The products would include:

(a) triple-silver low-E coated IGUs;

(b) double-silver and single-silver low-E coated IGUs;

(c) monolithic glass (annealed, heat-strengthened and fully tempered);

(d) laminated glass; and

(e) fire-rated glass,

in each case to Buyer's project Specifications as confirmed in Purchase Orders.

2.2 Product-specific pricing, lead times and Specifications would be set out in the initial Order Form.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Initial Term.** 60 months from the effective date of the Supply Agreement.

3.2 **Renewal.** Renewal mechanics to be discussed and agreed in the definitive documentation.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Scope.** During the term, Buyer would purchase its requirements of triple-silver low-E IGUs for projects in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut exclusively from Supplier.

4.2 **Products and Projects Outside Scope.** Buyer remains free to purchase all other glass products, and glass products of any type for projects outside New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, from any supplier. Supplier would be invited to quote for those requirements on a non-exclusive basis.

4.3 **Supplier Capacity.** If Supplier cannot supply an order for in-scope IGUs within the agreed lead times, Buyer may purchase the unsupplied quantity from another supplier for that order. Those purchases would count toward the minimum purchase commitment.

4.4 **Owner-Specified Products.** Where a project owner or architect specifies a named manufacturer other than Supplier and does not permit substitution, Buyer may comply with that specification after written notice to Supplier.

## 5. Minimum Purchase Commitment

5.1 Buyer would commit to purchase at least 400,000 square feet of glass products from Supplier per contract year.

5.2 Square footage would be measured from nominal overall dimensions as invoiced. Rejected and unreplaced product would not count.

5.3 Any shortfall payment mechanism, and the applicable rate, to be agreed in the definitive documentation. The commitment would be reduced proportionately for periods of Supplier supply failure or force majeure.

## 6. Pricing and Payment

6.1 **Pricing.** Initial pricing per the pricing schedule Supplier delivered on September 29, 2023, to be carried into the initial Order Form. Prices in U.S. dollars, FCA Supplier's facility.

6.2 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust prices no more than once per contract year by written notice stating the affected products, adjusted prices, effective date and cost drivers. The effective date would be at least 60 days after Buyer's receipt of the notice.

6.3 **Objection and Benchmarking.** Buyer may object within 30 days after Buyer's receipt of a price adjustment notice. On a timely objection, the Parties would benchmark against comparable North American suppliers. If no agreement is reached, Buyer could terminate as to the affected products, which would then fall outside exclusivity and the minimum commitment.

6.4 **Payment.** Net 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. Good-faith disputed amounts may be withheld on notice.

## 7. Ordering and Delivery

7.1 Buyer would provide quarterly non-binding rolling 12-month forecasts by project.

7.2 Supplier would accept or reject Purchase Orders within five Business Days. Silence would be treated as acceptance.

7.3 Delivery to project or Buyer facility locations on Supplier racks. Title and risk would pass on delivery. Supplier would bear expedited freight for late delivery attributable to it.

## 8. Quality and Warranty

8.1 **Quality.** Inspection, testing, acceptance and nonconformance procedures to be set out in the Quality Agreement, referencing ASTM C1036 and ASTM E2190, among other standards.

8.2 **IGU Warranty.** 10 years from delivery. Coverage would include edge seal failure, internal fogging and condensation, loss of gas fill, coating degradation and nickel-sulfide spontaneous breakage.

8.3 **Other Products.** 5 years from delivery.

8.4 **Remedies.** Supplier would repair or replace defective product and reimburse Buyer's reasonable documented removal, reglazing, access equipment and transport costs.

## 9. Risk Allocation

9.1 **Indemnities.** Mutual indemnities would follow customary scope. Supplier would indemnify for third-party injury or property damage caused by product defects and for product IP infringement.

9.2 **Limitation of Liability.** A mutual exclusion of consequential damages would apply, with customary carve-outs.

9.3 **Insurance.** Supplier would maintain CGL insurance with products-completed operations coverage of at least $5,000,000. Buyer would be named as additional insured. Coverage would be maintained for the term and 10 years after.

## 10. Assignment and Change of Control

10.1 Neither Party may assign without the other's consent, except to affiliates. Treatment of a change of control of either Party to be addressed in the definitive documentation.

## 11. Governing Law

11.1 New York law. Exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County.

## 12. Binding Provisions

12.1 Only this Section 12 is binding on the Parties.

12.2 **Confidentiality.** This term sheet and its contents are Confidential Information under the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the Parties.

12.3 **Costs.** Each Party bears its own costs.

12.4 **Governing Law.** This term sheet is governed by New York law.

12.5 **Termination.** Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice. Section 12 survives.
