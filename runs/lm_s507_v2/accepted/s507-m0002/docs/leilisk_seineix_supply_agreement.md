# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Seineix Glassworks LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Supplier**"); and

**Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures, coats, laminates, tempers and fabricates architectural glass, including coated insulating glass units.

B. Buyer designs, engineers and fabricates unitized and stick-built curtain-wall and facade systems for commercial construction projects.

C. Buyer wishes to purchase, and Supplier wishes to sell, architectural glass on the terms of this Agreement, the Quality Agreement and the Order Forms.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies, whether by contract or otherwise.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Change of Control**" means, for a Party: (a) any transaction or series of related transactions by which a person or group of persons acting together, other than an existing Affiliate of that Party, acquires Control of that Party, whether by merger, consolidation, sale of equity or otherwise; or (b) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of that Party or of the business unit performing this Agreement.

"**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of the Effective Date.

"**Defect**" means any defect in design, materials or workmanship in a Glass Product, or any failure of a Glass Product to conform to the Specifications. Without limitation, Defect includes: (a) for IGUs, failure of the primary or secondary edge seal, including loss of hermetic seal, moisture or dust intrusion, internal fogging or condensation, and loss of gas fill beyond the tolerances stated in the Specifications; (b) coating degradation, including peeling, delamination, oxidation, corrosion, discoloration, hazing or other deterioration of any low-emissivity, solar-control or reflective coating; and (c) spontaneous breakage caused by nickel-sulfide inclusions in tempered or heat-strengthened glass.

"**Delivery**" means delivery of Glass Products under Section 7.1, and "**Delivered**" has a corresponding meaning.

"**Glass Products**" means all architectural glass products manufactured or supplied by Supplier, including monolithic glass (annealed, heat-strengthened and fully tempered), laminated glass, fire-rated glass and glazing assemblies, and insulating glass units, in each case coated or uncoated, together with any other glass products listed in an Order Form.

"**IGU**" means an insulating glass unit composed of two or more lites of glass separated by a spacer and sealed at the edges, whether air- or gas-filled.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Opt-Out Period**" means the period as set out in the Order Form.

"**Order Form**" means Order Form No. 1 executed by the Parties under this Agreement and any additional order form the Parties sign that references this Agreement, each as amended, restated or supplemented from time to time.

"**Price Adjustment Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written order for Glass Products that Buyer issues under Section 6.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between Supplier and Buyer dated on or about the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

"**Renewal Term**" means the period as set out in the Order Form.

"**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Glass Product set out or referenced in the Order Form, the Quality Agreement or the applicable Purchase Order, including project shop drawings approved in writing by Supplier.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

1.2 **Other Definitions.** Other capitalized terms have the meanings given where they first appear in this Agreement.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term stated in the Order Form (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under its terms.

2.2 **Renewal.** On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive Renewal Terms. Either Party may prevent a renewal only by giving the other Party written notice of non-renewal during the Opt-Out Period that applies to the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term. All terms of this Agreement, including Sections 3 and 4, continue to apply during each Renewal Term.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY

3.1 **Exclusive Purchasing.** During the Term, Buyer shall purchase one hundred percent (100%) of its and its Affiliates' requirements of Glass Products exclusively from Supplier for every project Buyer or its Affiliates perform anywhere in North America. Buyer shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, purchase, solicit quotations for or qualify Glass Products, or products substantially similar to Glass Products, from any other supplier for those projects.

3.2 **Supplier Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain production capacity sufficient to meet Buyer's forecast requirements under Section 6.1. If Supplier notifies Buyer in writing that it cannot supply a particular Purchase Order in whole or in part within the lead times in the Order Form, Buyer may purchase the quantity Supplier cannot supply from another supplier for that Purchase Order only. Those purchases count toward the commitment in Section 4.1.

3.3 **Owner-Specified Products.** Where a project owner or architect specifies in the project contract documents a named glass manufacturer other than Supplier and does not permit substitution, Buyer shall give Supplier written notice with reasonable supporting documentation before ordering from that manufacturer.

## 4. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

4.1 **Annual Minimum.** Buyer shall purchase and take Delivery of at least 400,000 square feet of Glass Products per Contract Year (the "**Annual Minimum**").

4.2 **Measurement.** Square footage is calculated from the nominal overall dimensions of each Delivered lite or IGU as stated on Supplier's invoice. Glass Products rejected under the Quality Agreement and not replaced do not count toward the Annual Minimum.

4.3 **Shortfall.** If Buyer's purchases in a Contract Year are below the Annual Minimum, Supplier may invoice Buyer, within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year, an amount equal to the number of square feet by which purchases fell short multiplied by the shortfall rate per square foot stated in the Order Form (the "**Minimum Purchase Shortfall**"). The Minimum Purchase Shortfall is payable under Section 5.5. The Parties agree that it is a reasonable estimate of Supplier's lost margin and fixed-cost absorption and is not a penalty.

4.4 **Excused Shortfall.** The Annual Minimum is reduced proportionately for any period in which Supplier fails to fill conforming Purchase Orders within the agreed lead times or a Force Majeure Event under Section 18 affecting either Party continues.

## 5. PRICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Prices.** Buyer shall pay the prices for Glass Products set out in the Order Form. Prices are stated in U.S. dollars and are FCA Supplier's facility unless the Order Form states otherwise.

5.2 **Price Adjustments.** Not more than once in any Contract Year, Supplier may adjust the prices of any Glass Products by written notice to Buyer stating the affected Glass Products, the adjusted prices, the effective date, and a reasonable explanation of the cost drivers (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**"). The effective date may not be earlier than sixty (60) days after Buyer's receipt of the Price Adjustment Notice. Adjusted prices do not apply to Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier before the effective date.

5.3 **Objection.** Buyer may object to a Price Adjustment Notice by written notice to Supplier given within thirty (30) days after its receipt of the Price Adjustment Notice. If Buyer objects in time, the Parties shall meet within fifteen (15) Business Days to compare the adjusted prices with prices for comparable products from at least three (3) other North American suppliers of architectural glass. If the Parties do not agree on pricing within thirty (30) days after Buyer's objection, Buyer may terminate this Agreement as to the affected Glass Products by written notice, and those Glass Products are then removed from Sections 3.1 and 4.1. Until the matter is resolved or the termination takes effect, the prices in effect before the Price Adjustment Notice continue to apply. If Buyer does not object within that period, the price adjustment is deemed accepted and takes effect on the date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice.

5.4 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Buyer on or after Delivery. Each invoice shall reference the applicable Purchase Order and project.

5.5 **Payment.** Buyer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice. Buyer may withhold amounts it disputes in good faith if it notifies Supplier in writing of the dispute and the reasons before the due date. The Parties shall resolve disputed amounts under Section 20.

5.6 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

5.7 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Buyer shall pay unless it provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 6. FORECASTS AND ORDERING

6.1 **Forecasts.** By the fifth (5th) Business Day of each calendar quarter, Buyer shall provide Supplier a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of its Glass Products requirements by project.

6.2 **Purchase Orders.** Buyer shall order Glass Products by Purchase Orders stating the project, Glass Products, quantities, Specifications, requested Delivery dates and delivery location.

6.3 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) Business Days after receipt. Supplier may reject a Purchase Order only if it does not conform to this Agreement or the Order Form. A Purchase Order not rejected in that period is deemed accepted.

6.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Pre-printed or standard terms in any Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice have no effect.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Glass Products to the location stated in the Purchase Order, on the Delivery date stated in the accepted Purchase Order, packed on racks suitable for the Glass Products and for the mode of transport.

7.2 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Buyer promptly of any anticipated delay. If Supplier delivers more than ten (10) Business Days late for reasons not attributable to Buyer, Supplier shall bear the incremental cost of expedited freight.

7.3 **Title and Risk.** Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer on Delivery.

7.4 **Racks.** Supplier's glass racks remain Supplier's property. Buyer shall return them in good condition, ordinary wear excepted, within sixty (60) days after Delivery.

## 8. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE

8.1 Buyer shall inspect Glass Products and may reject nonconforming Glass Products in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Acceptance under the Quality Agreement does not waive Buyer's rights under Section 9.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **IGU Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each IGU will be free from Defects for ten (10) years from Delivery.

9.2 **Other Glass Products.** Supplier warrants that each Glass Product other than an IGU will be free from Defects for five (5) years from Delivery, and that laminated glass will be free from delamination, bubbles and edge defects beyond the tolerances stated in the Specifications for that period.

9.3 **General Warranties.** Supplier further warrants that all Glass Products will: (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) be manufactured in compliance with applicable laws and industry standards stated in the Specifications; and (c) be delivered free of liens and encumbrances.

9.4 **Remedies.** For any breach of Sections 9.1 to 9.3 notified to Supplier during the applicable warranty period, Supplier shall, at its option and cost, repair or replace the affected Glass Product, and shall reimburse Buyer's reasonable documented costs of removal, reglazing, access equipment and transport incurred in replacing it.

9.5 **Exclusions.** The warranties do not cover damage caused by improper handling, storage, installation or glazing by persons other than Supplier, by building movement beyond the design parameters in the Specifications, or by alteration of the Glass Products after Delivery.

9.6 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. QUALITY AGREEMENT

10.1 The Parties shall comply with the Quality Agreement, which sets out the quality system, inspection, testing, documentation and nonconformance procedures that apply to Glass Products.

## 11. COMPLIANCE

11.1 Each Party shall comply with all laws that apply to its performance of this Agreement, including export control, anti-corruption and environmental laws. Supplier shall provide certifications, test reports and product data reasonably requested by Buyer for project submittals.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION

12.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from third-party claims, losses and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) bodily injury or property damage caused by a Defect; (b) any claim that a Glass Product infringes a third party's intellectual property right, except to the extent it results from a Buyer-supplied design; or (c) Supplier's negligence or willful misconduct.

12.2 **By Buyer.** Buyer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their officers, directors and employees, from third-party claims, losses and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Buyer's negligence or willful misconduct or from designs Buyer supplies.

12.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (except that no settlement may impose liability on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or lost profits, arising from this Agreement. This Section 13.1 does not limit: (a) indemnification obligations under Section 12; (b) costs recoverable under Section 9.4; (c) amounts payable under Sections 4.3 and 16.2; or (d) liability for breach of Section 15 or for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 During the Term and for ten (10) years afterward, Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate, and shall name Buyer as an additional insured. Supplier shall deliver certificates of insurance on request.

## 15. CONFIDENTIALITY

15.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisers and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 15.

15.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose information when required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

15.3 This Section 15 survives for five (5) years after expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

16.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate on written notice if the assigning Party remains liable for the Affiliate's performance.

16.2 **Change of Control.** A Change of Control of either Party is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by that Party and requires the prior written consent of the other Party. If a Change of Control occurs without that consent, the other Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice within ninety (90) days after it learns of the Change of Control. If Supplier terminates under this Section 16.2 following a Change of Control of Buyer, Buyer shall pay Supplier, within thirty (30) days after the termination takes effect, the Minimum Purchase Shortfall for the then-current Contract Year and for each remaining Contract Year of the Initial Term, calculated for each remaining Contract Year as if Buyer purchased no Glass Products in it. If Buyer terminates under this Section 16.2 following a Change of Control of Supplier, Supplier shall reimburse Buyer's reasonable documented costs of qualifying a replacement supplier.

16.3 **Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 16 is void.

## 17. TERMINATION

17.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.

17.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceedings not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

17.3 **Effect.** On expiry or termination, Buyer shall pay for Glass Products Delivered before the effective date of termination and, unless Buyer terminated under Section 17.1 or 17.2, for work in process under accepted Purchase Orders. Sections 4.3 (for Contract Years ended before termination), 5, 9, 12, 13, 15, 16.2, 17.3 and 19 to 22 survive.

## 18. FORCE MAJEURE

18.1 Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, earthquake, epidemic, war, terrorism, government action, or failure of public utilities (a "**Force Majeure Event**"), if it notifies the other Party promptly and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier continues for more than sixty (60) days, Buyer may buy affected Glass Products from other suppliers for its duration without breaching Section 3.1.

## 19. NOTICES

19.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses for notices set out in the Order Form, or to any other address a Party designates by notice. A notice is received on delivery by hand or courier, or on the Business Day after transmission by email.

## 20. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

20.1 The Parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior executives for thirty (30) days after either Party's written request. If the dispute is not resolved in that period, either Party may begin proceedings under Section 22.2. Nothing in this Section 20 prevents a Party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

## 21. INTERPRETATION AND ENTIRE AGREEMENT

21.1 **Construction.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

21.2 **Order of Precedence.** This Agreement, the Quality Agreement and each Order Form are to be read together. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among them, the Quality Agreement shall prevail as to quality, inspection and acceptance criteria and the definition of Defect; subject to that, this Agreement shall prevail over any Order Form.

21.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Quality Agreement and the Order Forms, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous proposals, term sheets, letters of intent, negotiations and understandings, written or oral, on that subject matter.

21.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

21.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

21.6 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.
