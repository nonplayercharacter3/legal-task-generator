# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 by and between:

**Seineix Glassworks LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Supplier**"); and

**Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

Supplier and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Buyer are parties to a Supply Agreement dated January 15, 2024 (as amended from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**"), under which Supplier supplies Glass Products to Buyer.

B. Section 10 of the Supply Agreement requires the Parties to comply with this Quality Agreement. This Quality Agreement sets out the quality system, inspection, testing, documentation and nonconformance procedures that apply to Glass Products.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Supply Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement, including "Business Day," "Delivery," "Glass Products," "IGU," "Order Form," "Purchase Order" and "Specifications."

1.2 "**Certificate of Conformance**" means a certificate signed by Supplier's quality manager or delegate. It confirms that a shipment of Glass Products was manufactured, inspected and tested in accordance with the Specifications and this Quality Agreement, and it identifies the Purchase Order, project, lot numbers and quantities.

1.3 "**Corrective Action Report**" or "**CAR**" means a written report prepared by Supplier under Section 7 identifying the root cause of a Nonconformance and the corrective and preventive actions taken or planned.

1.4 "**Defect**" means a deviation of a Glass Product from the Specifications that is detectable by visual inspection, conducted in accordance with the viewing distance, lighting and viewing-angle procedures of ASTM C1036 (Standard Specification for Flat Glass) or, for heat-treated glass, ASTM C1048, at the time of Delivery. Defects include scratches, digs, rubs, bubbles, seeds, stones, distortion exceeding stated tolerances, edge chips and shells, coating streaks, pinholes or nonuniformity, visible spacer misalignment, and dimensional or squareness deviations exceeding the tolerances in the Specifications.

1.5 "**Lot**" means a quantity of a single Glass Product type produced on the same production line, from the same glass and coating batch, within a single production shift.

1.6 "**Nonconformance**" means a Glass Product that contains a Defect, or a failure of a shipment to be accompanied by the documentation required by Section 5.3. "**Nonconforming**" has a corresponding meaning.

1.7 "**Quality Representative**" means the individual each Party designates under Section 2.3.

1.8 "**Quality Manual**" means Supplier's documented quality management system manual, as updated from time to time in accordance with Section 8.

## 2. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

2.1 **Certification.** Supplier shall maintain a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 (or any successor standard) at each facility that manufactures, coats, laminates, tempers or fabricates Glass Products for Buyer. Supplier shall notify Buyer in writing within five (5) Business Days if any certification is suspended, withdrawn or not renewed.

2.2 **Product Certifications.** Supplier shall maintain, for each IGU configuration supplied, current certification through the Insulating Glass Certification Council (IGCC) or an equivalent program accepted in writing by Buyer, and, for safety glazing, certification through the Safety Glazing Certification Council (SGCC). Supplier shall maintain current fire-rating listings for all fire-rated glass supplied.

2.3 **Quality Representatives.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Representative within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this Quality Agreement. The Quality Representatives are the primary contacts for all matters under this Quality Agreement. A Party may change its Quality Representative by written notice.

2.4 **Quality Reviews.** The Quality Representatives shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least once each calendar quarter to review delivery quality, Nonconformance rates, open CARs, audit findings and planned process changes.

## 3. SPECIFICATIONS AND STANDARDS

3.1 **Applicable Standards.** Unless the Specifications state a more stringent requirement, Glass Products shall conform to the following standards as in effect on the date of the applicable Purchase Order:

(a) ASTM C1036 (flat glass);

(b) ASTM C1048 (heat-strengthened and fully tempered flat glass);

(c) ASTM C1172 (laminated architectural flat glass);

(d) ASTM E2190 (insulating glass unit performance and evaluation), including ASTM E2188 and E2189 testing;

(e) ASTM E2649 (determining argon concentration in sealed insulating glass units), where gas fill is specified;

(f) ANSI Z97.1 and 16 CFR Part 1201 (safety glazing), where applicable; and

(g) the applicable UL, NFPA and ASTM E119 standards, for fire-rated glass.

3.2 **Project Specifications.** Project-specific requirements, including glass makeup, coating type and surface, heat treatment, spacer system, gas fill, edge deletion, dimensional tolerances and visual quality criteria, shall be stated in the Purchase Order or in project shop drawings approved in writing by Supplier.

3.3 **Mock-Ups and Approved Samples.** Where a project requires a visual mock-up or approved control sample, Supplier shall produce it on request. The approved sample becomes part of the Specifications for color, reflectance and visual uniformity for that project.

## 4. PROCESS CONTROLS AND TESTING

4.1 **In-Process Controls.** Supplier shall maintain documented process controls at each production stage, including glass cutting, edge processing, washing, coating, heat treatment, lamination, IGU assembly, sealing and gas filling, as described in the Quality Manual.

4.2 **Heat Treatment.** Supplier shall monitor roller-wave distortion, bow and warp on heat-treated glass and shall record the results for each Lot. Where the Specifications require heat-soak testing, Supplier shall perform it in accordance with EN 14179-1 or the procedure stated in the Specifications and shall retain the cycle records for each Lot.

4.3 **IGU Testing.** For IGUs, Supplier shall perform and record, for each Lot or at the frequency stated in the Quality Manual if more frequent:

(a) edge-seal visual inspection and sealant dimension checks;

(b) gas-fill concentration testing, where gas fill is specified; and

(c) sealant adhesion and cure testing.

4.4 **Coating Controls.** Supplier shall measure and record coating color, reflectance and emissivity for each coating batch and shall confirm that coated surfaces are oriented as specified.

4.5 **Calibration.** Supplier shall calibrate all inspection, measuring and test equipment at intervals stated in the Quality Manual, traceable to national standards, and shall keep calibration records.

## 5. SUPPLIER OUTGOING INSPECTION AND DOCUMENTATION

5.1 **Final Inspection.** Before shipment, Supplier shall inspect each Glass Product for conformity to the Specifications using the procedures in Section 1.4 and the applicable standards in Section 3.1.

5.2 **Labeling.** Each Glass Product shall bear a label or etched mark showing Supplier's name, the Purchase Order number, project mark number, Lot number and date of manufacture, and, where applicable, the safety glazing and fire-rating marks required by law or the Specifications.

5.3 **Shipment Documentation.** Each shipment shall be accompanied by:

(a) a packing list identifying each rack, the project mark numbers and quantities;

(b) a Certificate of Conformance; and

(c) for fire-rated glass, a copy of the applicable listing.

Supplier shall deliver test reports for the Lots in the shipment within five (5) Business Days after Buyer's request.

5.4 **Packing.** Supplier shall pack Glass Products on racks with separators and protection suitable for the Glass Products and the mode of transport, and shall sequence racks in the order stated in the Purchase Order where Buyer requests installation sequencing.

## 6. BUYER INSPECTION AND REJECTION

6.1 **Receiving Inspection.** Buyer shall carry out receiving inspection of Glass Products within ten (10) Business Days after Delivery. Buyer may inspect all Glass Products or sample them in accordance with its receiving procedures.

6.2 **Inspection Method.** Buyer shall carry out visual inspection under Section 1.4. Buyer may also verify dimensions, labeling and shipment documentation.

6.3 **Rejection Notice.** If Buyer identifies a Nonconformance, Buyer shall notify Supplier in writing within the period in Section 6.1, identifying the Purchase Order, project mark numbers, Lot numbers and the nature of the Nonconformance, with photographs where practicable.

6.4 **Transit Damage.** Buyer shall note visible transit damage on the carrier's delivery receipt where practicable. Breakage occurring before Delivery is Supplier's responsibility.

6.5 **Joint Inspection.** Supplier may, within three (3) Business Days after a rejection notice, request a joint inspection of the rejected Glass Products at the delivery location. If Supplier does not request a joint inspection within that period, Buyer's rejection stands.

6.6 **Disposition.** For each rejected Glass Product, Supplier shall, at Buyer's election and at Supplier's cost:

(a) replace it, with the replacement delivered within the replacement lead time in the Order Form or, if none is stated, within fifteen (15) Business Days after the rejection notice; or

(b) issue a credit for the price of the rejected Glass Product.

Supplier shall collect rejected Glass Products, or authorize Buyer in writing to dispose of them, within thirty (30) days after the rejection notice. Supplier shall bear the freight and disposal costs.

6.7 **Use-As-Is.** Buyer may, in its discretion, accept a Nonconforming Glass Product on a use-as-is basis by written notice, in which case the Parties shall agree an equitable price reduction.

## 7. CORRECTIVE AND PREVENTIVE ACTION

7.1 **CAR Requirement.** Supplier shall issue a CAR within ten (10) Business Days after Buyer's written request following:

(a) a Nonconformance affecting more than two percent (2%) of the Glass Products in a shipment;

(b) a repeat Nonconformance of the same type within any ninety (90) day period; or

(c) any Nonconformance that Buyer reasonably considers to present a safety risk.

7.2 **Content.** Each CAR shall identify the root cause using a recognized method (such as 8D or 5-Why), containment actions, corrective actions with target completion dates, and preventive actions to avoid recurrence across other production lines and Glass Products.

7.3 **Verification.** Supplier shall confirm completion of each corrective action in writing. Buyer may verify implementation through an audit under Section 9.

## 8. CHANGE CONTROL

8.1 **Notification of Changes.** Supplier shall give Buyer at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice of any change that may affect the form, fit, function, appearance or performance of a Glass Product, including any change in:

(a) float glass source;

(b) coating formulation, coater or coating facility;

(c) spacer system, primary or secondary sealant, or desiccant;

(d) interlayer material; or

(e) manufacturing facility.

8.2 **Approval.** Supplier shall not implement a change under Section 8.1 for Glass Products on an active project without Buyer's prior written approval, which Buyer shall not unreasonably withhold. Buyer may require qualification samples and test data before approving a change.

8.3 **Quality Manual.** Supplier shall provide Buyer with a copy of any material revision to the Quality Manual within ten (10) Business Days after it takes effect.

## 9. AUDITS

9.1 **Buyer Audits.** Buyer, or a third-party auditor bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit Supplier's facilities, processes and quality records relating to Glass Products once each Contract Year on at least ten (10) Business Days' written notice, during normal business hours. Buyer may conduct additional audits following a Nonconformance described in Section 7.1(c).

9.2 **Findings.** Buyer shall provide a written summary of audit findings within fifteen (15) Business Days after the audit. Supplier shall respond with a corrective action plan for any findings within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the summary.

9.3 **Project Owner Inspections.** Supplier shall permit project owners, architects and their consultants, accompanied by Buyer, to witness production or inspection of Glass Products for their projects on reasonable notice.

## 10. RECORDS AND TRACEABILITY

10.1 **Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain traceability for each Glass Product from Delivery back to the Lot, production line, float glass source, coating batch, sealant batch and heat-treatment and heat-soak cycle.

10.2 **Retention.** Supplier shall retain all inspection, test, calibration and production records relating to Glass Products for at least ten (10) years after Delivery and shall make them available to Buyer within ten (10) Business Days after a written request.

## 11. GENERAL

11.1 **Relationship to Supply Agreement.** This Quality Agreement forms part of the documents comprising the Parties' supply arrangement and continues in effect for so long as the Supply Agreement remains in effect and, for Sections 10 and 11, after it ends for so long as records must be retained under Section 10.2.

11.2 **Notices.** Routine communications under this Quality Agreement may be exchanged between the Quality Representatives by email. Formal notices shall be given in accordance with Section 19 of the Supply Agreement.

11.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

11.4 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

11.5 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
