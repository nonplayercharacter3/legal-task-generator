# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 21, 2023 (the "**NDA Effective Date**") by and between:

**Seineix Glassworks LLC**, a New York limited liability company, with offices at 1400 Riverbend Industrial Parkway, Newburgh, New York 12550 ("**Seineix**"); and

**Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, with offices at 225 Harborview Drive, Suite 400, Jersey City, New Jersey 07302 ("**Leilisk**").

Seineix and Leilisk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." A Party disclosing Confidential Information is the "**Disclosing Party**," and a Party receiving it is the "**Receiving Party**."

## RECITALS

A. Seineix manufactures, coats, laminates, tempers and fabricates architectural glass. Leilisk designs, engineers and fabricates curtain-wall and facade systems for commercial construction projects.

B. The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible long-term arrangement for the supply of architectural glass by Seineix to Leilisk (the "**Purpose**").

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that the Disclosing Party or its Affiliates disclose to the Receiving Party or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the NDA Effective Date. It includes pricing, cost data, capacity and production information, product formulations, coating and process data, test results, specifications, shop drawings, project lists, customer and supplier information, forecasts, business plans, and the existence and terms of the Parties' discussions. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it would reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, lenders, attorneys, accountants, consultants and other professional advisers.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

2.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any person except to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

2.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of similar importance, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing after becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information and shall cooperate reasonably to limit its effects.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, without an obligation of confidentiality;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation owed to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

4.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where lawful, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for it.

## 5. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

5.1 Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its possession and confirm destruction in writing. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

5.2 The Receiving Party may retain copies required by law or its bona fide document retention policies, and electronic copies stored on routine backup systems that are not readily accessible. Retained copies remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

## 6. NO LICENSE; NO WARRANTY

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No license or other right in any patent, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property is granted under this Agreement, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

6.2 Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness. Only representations and warranties in a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties will have legal effect.

## 7. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

7.1 This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or transaction, to continue discussions, or to disclose any particular information. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other.

7.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from purchasing, selling or developing products or doing business with any third party, provided it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

## 8. TERM

8.1 This Agreement applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period of two (2) years beginning on the NDA Effective Date, unless either Party ends it earlier by thirty (30) days' written notice to the other.

8.2 The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period in Section 8.1. Obligations relating to Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continue for as long as it remains a trade secret.

## 9. REMEDIES

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance for any actual or threatened breach, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the need to post a bond.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out in the preamble (for Seineix, attention: Vice President, Commercial, contracts@seineixglass.com; for Leilisk, attention: General Counsel, legal@leiliskfacade.com), or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

10.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

10.3 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

10.5 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising from this Agreement.

10.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
