# AMENDED AND RESTATED ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Dated:** May 11, 2026

**Under:** Supply Agreement dated January 15, 2024 between Seineix Glassworks LLC and Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc.

This Amended and Restated Order Form No. 1 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into under the Supply Agreement dated January 15, 2024 (as amended from time to time, the "**Supply Agreement**") between **Seineix Glassworks LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Supplier**"), and **Leilisk Facade Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). It amends and restates in full Order Form No. 1 dated January 15, 2024 (the "**Original Order Form**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the Supply Agreement.

## 1. BACKGROUND

1.1 The Parties signed the Original Order Form together with the Supply Agreement and the Quality Agreement on January 15, 2024.

1.2 The Parties are restating the Original Order Form to:

(a) record the pricing basis following Supplier's 2026 price adjustment;

(b) update the product schedule, lead times and delivery locations; and

(c) update billing and notice details.

1.3 Except as stated in this Order Form, the commercial terms of the Original Order Form are carried forward without change.

## 2. TERM

2.1 **Initial Term:** 60 months from the Supply Agreement Effective Date, being January 15, 2024.

2.2 **Renewal Term:** 60 months for each Renewal Term.

2.3 **Opt-Out Period:** the ten (10) Business Days immediately preceding the 180th day before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

2.4 **Order Form Term:** This Order Form remains in effect for the Term of the Supply Agreement.

## 3. PRICING BASIS

3.1 **Original Pricing.** The prices in effect under the Original Order Form are set out in Column A of Schedule A. They were derived from the pricing schedule Supplier delivered on September 29, 2023.

3.2 **2026 Price Adjustment.** Supplier issued a Price Adjustment Notice under Section 5.2 of the Supply Agreement, which was received by Buyer on March 4, 2026. The Price Adjustment Notice covers all IGUs and laminated glass listed in Schedule A and states an effective date of May 4, 2026. The adjusted prices stated in the Price Adjustment Notice are set out in Column B of Schedule A. Supplier identified float glass, silver target and argon costs as the cost drivers.

3.3 **Buyer's Position.** Buyer delivered to Supplier a letter dated April 20, 2026 objecting to the Price Adjustment Notice and requesting benchmarking under Section 5.3 of the Supply Agreement. Buyer signs this Order Form under reservation of rights and does not accept the adjusted pricing in Column B of Schedule A. Supplier's signature of this Order Form does not waive Supplier's position on the Price Adjustment Notice.

3.4 **Invoicing Pending Resolution.** Until the Parties agree pricing in writing, Supplier shall show on each invoice for Glass Products affected by the Price Adjustment Notice both the Column A price and the Column B price. Buyer shall pay the Column A price, and the difference is a disputed amount for the purposes of Section 5.5 of the Supply Agreement.

3.5 **Other Glass Products.** Prices for monolithic and fire-rated glass are not affected by the Price Adjustment Notice and remain as set out in Column A of Schedule A.

3.6 **Terms.** Prices are in U.S. dollars per square foot, FCA Supplier's facility at 1400 Riverbend Industrial Parkway, Newburgh, New York 12550. They are calculated on nominal overall dimensions as provided in Section 4.2 of the Supply Agreement.

## 4. PRODUCTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

4.1 **Glass Products.** The Glass Products available under this Order Form are listed in Schedule A.

4.2 **Specifications.** Project-specific Specifications shall be stated in each Purchase Order or in shop drawings approved in writing by Supplier, in accordance with Section 3.2 of the Quality Agreement.

4.3 **Standard IGU Makeup.** Unless a Purchase Order states otherwise, IGUs are supplied as 1-inch overall units: 1/4-inch coated outboard lite (coating on surface #2), 1/2-inch warm-edge stainless steel spacer with 90% argon fill, 1/4-inch clear inboard lite, and dual seal (polyisobutylene primary, silicone structural secondary).

## 5. MINIMUM PURCHASE AND SHORTFALL RATE

5.1 **Annual Minimum.** As stated in Section 4.1 of the Supply Agreement, 400,000 square feet of Glass Products per Contract Year.

5.2 **Shortfall Rate.** The shortfall rate per square foot for the purposes of Section 4.3 of the Supply Agreement is $4.25.

Any change in account details must be confirmed by telephone call to Supplier's controller before payment.

## 7. LEAD TIMES AND DELIVERY

7.1 **Standard Lead Times.** Lead times run from Supplier's acceptance of a Purchase Order:

| Glass Product | Standard Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Monolithic glass (annealed, heat-strengthened, fully tempered) | 15 Business Days |
| Laminated glass | 25 Business Days |
| IGUs (single-, double- or triple-silver low-E) | 30 Business Days |
| Fire-rated glass and glazing assemblies | 40 Business Days |

7.2 **Heat-Soaked Glass.** Add 5 Business Days where heat-soak testing is specified.

7.3 **Replacement Lead Time.** For the purposes of Section 6.6(a) of the Quality Agreement, the replacement lead time is 15 Business Days for monolithic glass and 20 Business Days for all other Glass Products.

7.4 **Delivery Locations.** Delivery shall be made to the project site or to Buyer's fabrication facilities at 80 Industrial Way, Kearny, New Jersey 07032 or 3150 Commerce Road, Allentown, Pennsylvania 18109, as stated in the Purchase Order.

7.5 **Freight.** Freight from Supplier's facility to the delivery location is charged at Supplier's published freight schedule, as provided to Buyer, unless the Purchase Order states otherwise.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Effect.** From the date of this Order Form, references in the Supply Agreement and the Quality Agreement to "Order Form No. 1" or "the Order Form" are references to this Order Form.

8.2 **Counterparts.** This Order Form may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
