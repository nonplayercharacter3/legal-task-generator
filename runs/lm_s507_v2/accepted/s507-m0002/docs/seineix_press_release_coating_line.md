# Seineix Glassworks Commissions New Magnetron Sputter Coating Line at Newburgh Facility

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**NEWBURGH, N.Y., September 9, 2025** – Seineix Glassworks LLC, a Hudson Valley manufacturer of architectural glass, today announced that it has commissioned a second large-format magnetron sputter coating line at its manufacturing campus at 1400 Riverbend Industrial Parkway, Newburgh, New York.

## 1. Overview of the Investment

1.1 The new line, designated Coater 2, is housed in a 92,000-square-foot addition to the Newburgh plant. It processes jumbo-size float glass lites up to 130 by 236 inches.

1.2 Coater 2 is designed to apply single-, double- and triple-silver low-emissivity and solar-control coatings. It has inline optical monitoring that measures color, reflectance and emissivity across the full width of each lite.

1.3 The investment of approximately $64 million also covers a new automated glass storage and retrieval system and an expanded insulating glass unit (IGU) assembly area with two additional high-speed lines.

1.4 Seineix expects the project to add about 85 full-time manufacturing, engineering and quality positions in Orange County, New York, over the next eighteen months.

## 2. Capacity and Operations

2.1 Coater 2 roughly doubles Seineix's coated-glass capacity. Its existing coating line continues in full operation.

2.2 Coated products now in production for current projects will continue on the existing line. The new line will serve new project specifications and growth in demand.

2.3 Customers that wish to source specific coated products from Coater 2 may request qualification samples and test data through their Seineix account managers.

2.4 Seineix expects Coater 2 to reach full production rates during the first quarter of 2026.

## 3. Quality and Certification

3.1 Coater 2 operates under Seineix's ISO 9001:2015-certified quality management system.

3.2 IGUs produced with Coater 2 glass will be submitted for certification through the Insulating Glass Certification Council before commercial shipment.

3.3 The expansion includes a dedicated coatings laboratory with spectrophotometers, emissivity meters and environmental chambers for durability testing.

## 4. Management Comment

4.1 "Facade contractors across the Northeast are asking for higher-performance glass on shorter schedules," said the Chief Executive Officer of Seineix Glassworks. "Coater 2 gives us the capacity and process control to meet that demand while maintaining the consistency architects expect on large curtain-wall projects."

4.2 "The inline monitoring on this line lets us verify coating performance on every lite before it moves to IGU assembly," added Seineix's Vice President of Operations. "That is a meaningful step forward for our quality program."

## 5. Sustainability

5.1 The coater was designed with variable-frequency drives on vacuum pumps and heat recovery from process cooling systems. Seineix estimates these features will reduce energy use per square foot of coated glass by about 18% compared with its existing line.

5.2 Glass cullet from the new line will be collected and returned to float glass producers for recycling.

## 6. About Seineix Glassworks LLC

6.1 Seineix Glassworks LLC manufactures, coats, laminates, tempers and fabricates architectural glass for commercial construction, including coated insulating glass units, laminated glass, monolithic glass and fire-rated glass. The company serves curtain-wall fabricators, glazing contractors and facade engineers from its facility in Newburgh, New York.
