# DILIGENCE MEETING NOTES

**Project Oak – Proposed Acquisition of Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc.**

**Prepared by:** Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc., Corporate Development

**Confidential – Prepared at the Request of Counsel**

## 1. Meeting Details

1.1 **Date and time:** March 12, 2026, 9:30 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. Eastern Time

1.2 **Location:** Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc. offices, 85 Industrial Loop, Yonkers, NY 10701, followed by a site walk-through of the Secaucus warehouse and truck yard at 400 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, NJ 07094

1.3 **Attendees:**

- *Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc. ("Tramil"):* VP, Corporate Development; Director, Corporate Development; Director, Integration and IT
- *Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc. ("Oakhollow" or the "Company"):* General Manager; Director of Operations; Controller; Warehouse and Fleet Manager
- *Titairt Group, LLC ("Titairt"):* Head of M&A; Director of Shared Systems

1.4 **Purpose:** Management session and site visit to complete operational, commercial and systems diligence before signing the Stock Purchase Agreement and closing, which are planned to occur at the same time.

## 2. Transaction Overview (as discussed)

2.1 Proposed structure: Tramil to purchase all outstanding shares of Oakhollow from Titairt. Headline purchase price of $46,500,000, of which $7,000,000 is to be paid by a subordinated Seller Note and the balance in cash at closing, subject to the working capital adjustment.

2.2 Working capital target: Titairt and Tramil have agreed on a target of $3,200,000. The Controller confirmed that the March and April booking deposits will be in customer deposit liabilities and not netted against receivables.

2.3 Target signing and closing date: March 31, 2026, subject to completion of confirmatory diligence and final documents.

## 3. Business and Operations

3.1 **Overview.** The General Manager described the business as roughly 60% weddings and private social events and 40% corporate and institutional events. The peak season runs from May through October. There is a secondary holiday peak in late November and December, driven by corporate parties and charity galas.

3.2 **Rental inventory.** The Director of Operations walked through the inventory categories: tables, chairs, linens, frame and pole tents, flooring, staging and lighting. Physical counts are taken twice a year (January and July). The Company reported that the January 2026 count matched the system records within 1.5% by unit. Tramil to sample-test tent and staging counts during the Secaucus walk-through (see Section 6).

3.3 **Fleet.** The Warehouse and Fleet Manager confirmed that the delivery fleet is fourteen (14) box trucks and four (4) flatbed trucks, all leased from Fleetwise Truck Leasing, Inc. under the May 1, 2023 Vehicle Lease Agreement. Maintenance is outsourced to Apex Fleet Maintenance LLC. No vehicles are owned.

3.4 **Facilities.** Three leased facilities: Secaucus, NJ (principal warehouse and truck yard), Yonkers, NY (warehouse, linen staging and offices) and Wallingford, CT (satellite warehouse and tent storage). The Company stated that the Secaucus landlord, Meadowlands Industrial Partners, L.P., has been asked for change-of-control consent and is expected to deliver it before closing.

## 4. Customers and Commercial

4.1 **Customer concentration.** The top three customers for 2025 were Harborview Hospitality Group, LLC, Stonebridge Estate Weddings, Inc. and Calloway Pharmaceuticals Corp. Management estimates that together they represented about 22% of 2025 revenue.

4.2 **Customer relationships.** The Director of Sales reported that no top-20 customer has signaled any intention to reduce volume. The Calloway Annual Events Agreement (November 1, 2025) runs through the 2026 calendar year. Harborview's Preferred Vendor Agreement (February 1, 2025) renews automatically each year.

4.3 **Pricing.** Pricing is maintained in the EventGrid booking platform through rate cards by item and season, with discretionary discounts approved by the Director of Sales. Tramil asked for a 2025 discount report by customer. Management to provide it through the data room.

## 5. Systems and Transition Services

5.1 **Current systems.** The Director of Shared Systems (Titairt) confirmed that Oakhollow currently runs on three Titairt-owned systems:

(a) the EventGrid booking platform (bookings, quotes, rental orders, customer accounts, deposits and inventory availability);

(b) Titairt's warehouse yard management system (bar-code inventory tracking, pick lists, load staging, return check-in and damage logging); and

(c) Titairt's fleet-routing software (daily route planning, driver mobile app, GPS integration and proof of delivery).

5.2 **What transfers.** The OAKHOLLOW marks, the oakhollowrentals.com domain and the booking-portal content at book.oakhollowrentals.com will be assigned from Titairt to the Company at closing. The three systems above will not transfer. They will be provided under a Transition Services Agreement while Oakhollow migrates.

5.3 **Payroll and accounting.** Titairt currently provides payroll processing and accounting support under an unwritten shared services arrangement. The Controller confirmed that these functions can move to Tramil's existing payroll provider and Oakhollow's QuickBooks Enterprise instance at closing. They will not be part of the TSA.

5.4 **Migration planning.** Tramil's Director of Integration and IT gave a preliminary view that the booking platform migration is the longest workstream because of the seasonal booking calendar, and that cut-over should avoid the May through October peak. Titairt indicated that it can support data mapping and parallel testing. Specific scope, service levels and fees are being finalized by counsel in the TSA schedule.

5.5 **Data.** Titairt confirmed that Oakhollow's customer and booking data sits in its own environment within EventGrid and can be exported in standard formats. Tramil requested a sample export file layout. Titairt to provide it.

## 6. Site Walk-Through – Secaucus

6.1 Tramil sample-counted three inventory categories (20-foot by 40-foot frame tents, 4-foot by 8-foot staging decks and Chiavari chairs). The counts reconciled to the yard management system with no material variance.

6.2 The yard was observed to be in orderly condition. Damaged-goods quarantine area was segregated and tagged. The Warehouse and Fleet Manager noted that tent fabric is cleaned and inspected in-house after each event.

6.3 Tent permits: the Director of Operations confirmed that permits for temporary tent structures are obtained event by event through the local building departments, and that permit files for 2025 are in the data room.

## 7. Litigation and Insurance

7.1 **Delgado matter.** The General Manager summarized the *Delgado* personal injury claim (Westchester County, slip on a staging riser at a June 7, 2025 wedding). The general liability carrier has accepted the tender, the Company has paid the $25,000 deductible, and carrier-appointed counsel is defending. No other claims are pending or threatened.

7.2 **Insurance.** The Company's policies include general liability, umbrella, commercial auto, inland marine (rental equipment floater), workers' compensation and cyber. Tramil's risk management team to review the policies for coverage after the change of control.

## 8. People

8.1 Six employees earn more than $100,000 in base salary, led by the General Manager. Tramil intends to retain the full management team. Retention discussions will take place after signing, and no commitments were made at this meeting.

8.2 Seasonal labor is supplied mainly through Metro Labor Staffing, Inc. The Company confirmed that it treats seasonal crew as agency workers and not as independent contractors.
