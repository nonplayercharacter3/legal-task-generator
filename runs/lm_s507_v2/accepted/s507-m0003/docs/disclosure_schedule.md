## 7. Section 4.13 – Litigation

7.1 *Delgado v. Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc.*, Index No. 61873/2025 (N.Y. Sup. Ct., Westchester County). This is a personal injury claim by a wedding guest who alleges that she tripped on a staging riser at an event on June 7, 2025. The plaintiff seeks unspecified damages. The claim was tendered to, and accepted by, the Company's general liability carrier (Policy No. GL-44017-26 listed in Section 4.18) subject to a $25,000 deductible, which the Company has paid. Defense counsel has been appointed by the carrier.
