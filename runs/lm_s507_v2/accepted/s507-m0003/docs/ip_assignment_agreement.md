# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of March 31, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Titairt Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor and Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 31, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer purchased from Assignor all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Before the Closing, Assignor held record title to certain trademarks, a domain name and booking-portal content used exclusively in the business of Assignee, which is renting event furniture, tenting, staging and related equipment (the "**Business**").

C. Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Assignment at the Closing, so that Assignor transfers to Assignee all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to those assets.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Assignment and the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Assignment:

"**Assigned Domain Name**" means the domain name oakhollowrentals.com and all subdomains of that domain, including book.oakhollowrentals.com, together with all registrations of them.

"**Assigned Marks**" means (a) the trademarks listed in Part 1 of Schedule 1, together with the registrations for them, (b) all other trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and trade dress owned by Assignor that consist of or include the term OAKHOLLOW or the oak leaf design, and (c) all goodwill of the Business associated with any of them.

"**Assigned IP**" means the Assigned Marks, the Assigned Domain Name and the Portal Content.

"**Excluded Systems**" means the EventGrid booking platform, Assignor's warehouse yard management system, Assignor's fleet-routing software, and all source code, object code, databases, documentation and other intellectual property that comprise or relate to them, other than the Portal Content.

"**Portal Content**" means all text, graphics, photographs, images, product descriptions, page layouts, look and feel, customer-facing forms, terms and conditions, and other content that appears on, or was created for, the booking portal at book.oakhollowrentals.com or the website at oakhollowrentals.com. It includes all copyrights in that content, whether registered or unregistered, and all works of authorship derived from it.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment of Assigned IP.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest throughout the world in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens. The assignment includes:

(a) all registrations, applications and renewals of the Assigned IP;

(b) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or later due or payable with respect to the Assigned IP;

(c) all claims and causes of action with respect to the Assigned IP, whether accruing before, on or after the Effective Date, including the right to sue for and collect damages for past, present and future infringement, dilution or misappropriation; and

(d) all rights of priority and protection of interests in the Assigned IP under the laws of any jurisdiction.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The Assigned Marks are assigned together with the goodwill of the Business connected with their use and symbolized by them.

**2.3 Excluded Systems.** This Assignment does not assign any right, title or interest in the Excluded Systems. Assignee's access to the Excluded Systems is governed by the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. DELIVERY AND RECORDATION

**3.1 Trademark Recordation.** Assignor authorizes Assignee to record this Assignment, or a short-form assignment in the form of Schedule 2, with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and with any other Governmental Authority. Assignor shall execute any short-form assignment that Assignee reasonably requests for that purpose.

**3.2 Domain Name Transfer.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall (a) unlock the Assigned Domain Name at the registrar, (b) provide Assignee with the authorization code needed to transfer it, and (c) take all other steps that the registrar requires to transfer the registration to an account that Assignee designates. Until the transfer is complete, Assignor shall hold the Assigned Domain Name for the sole benefit of Assignee, keep the registration current, and promptly make any DNS or other changes that Assignee directs in writing.

**3.3 Delivery of Portal Content.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee complete electronic copies of the Portal Content in its native file formats, to the extent held by Assignor outside the Excluded Systems. Portal Content that is hosted in or displayed through the EventGrid booking platform remains available to Assignee through the Services, and Assignor shall deliver it under Section 7.4 of the Transition Services Agreement.

## 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF ASSIGNOR

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee as of the Effective Date that:

(a) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all liens;

(b) Assignor has full power and authority to enter into this Assignment and to perform its obligations under it;

(c) Assignor has not granted any license, option or other right in or to the Assigned IP to any Person other than Assignee, which continues in effect after the Effective Date;

(d) the registrations listed in Part 1 of Schedule 1 are subsisting, and all maintenance filings and fees due on or before the Effective Date have been made and paid; and

(e) to Assignor's knowledge, no Person is infringing any of the Assigned IP, and no claim is pending or threatened in writing that challenges the validity, enforceability or ownership of any of the Assigned IP.

## 5. COVENANTS OF ASSIGNOR

**5.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and without further consideration, Assignor shall execute and deliver any further instruments, and take any further actions, that Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP. If Assignor fails to do so within ten (10) Business Days after a written request, Assignor appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely to execute and file those instruments on Assignor's behalf. This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

**5.2 No Retained Use.** After the Effective Date, Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, use or apply to register any Assigned Mark, or any mark, trade name or domain name confusingly similar to any Assigned Mark. However, Assignor may refer to the Assigned Marks to the extent reasonably needed to perform the Services under the Transition Services Agreement.

**5.3 Cooperation in Enforcement.** Assignor shall cooperate reasonably with Assignee, at Assignee's expense for reasonable out-of-pocket costs, in any action relating to the Assigned IP, including by providing documents and testimony concerning the creation, use and registration of the Assigned IP before the Effective Date.

**5.4 Correspondence.** Assignor shall promptly forward to Assignee any correspondence, notice, renewal reminder or payment that it receives after the Effective Date relating to the Assigned IP.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Assignment is an Ancillary Agreement delivered under the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Assignment, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Assignment, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**6.3 Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign this Assignment and any of the Assigned IP without Assignor's consent.

**6.4 Notices.** Notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner set out in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignee shall be addressed to Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc., Attention: General Manager, 85 Industrial Loop, Yonkers, NY 10701, with a copy (which shall not constitute notice) to Buyer at its notice address under the Purchase Agreement.

**6.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**6.6 Severability.** If any term of this Assignment is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that holding does not affect any other term of this Assignment or make that term invalid or unenforceable in any other jurisdiction.

**6.7 Counterparts.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original, and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF form or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
