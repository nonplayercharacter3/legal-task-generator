# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 31, 2026, by and between **Titairt Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc.**, a New York corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company is engaged in renting event furniture, tenting, staging and related equipment for weddings, corporate events and other functions (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. The parties intend that the execution of this Agreement and the Closing occur at the same time.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(a).

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any agency, court, tribunal or other authority of such a government.

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement dated as of the Closing Date, by which Seller assigns to the Company all of Seller's right, title and interest in the Oakhollow marks and the booking-portal content used in the Business.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $46,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment of Purchase Price.** Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) $39,500,000, less the Closing Indebtedness and the Seller Transaction Expenses, each as set out in the closing statement delivered under Section 2.3(a), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing; and

(b) $7,000,000 by execution and delivery to Seller of the Seller Note.

**2.3 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing, Seller delivered to Buyer a closing statement with Seller's good-faith estimate of the Company's closing working capital, closing indebtedness ("**Closing Indebtedness**") and unpaid transaction expenses of Seller and the Company ("**Seller Transaction Expenses**").

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the Company's actual closing working capital, Closing Indebtedness and Seller Transaction Expenses. If Seller disputes that statement, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days. Any remaining dispute shall be referred to an independent accounting firm of national standing that both parties accept. That firm shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, and its determination shall be final and binding.

(c) If the final working capital is less than $3,200,000, Seller shall pay Buyer the shortfall. If it exceeds $3,200,000, Buyer shall pay Seller the excess. Any payment under this Section 2.3(c) shall be made in cash within five (5) Business Days after the final determination.

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any Taxes required by applicable law to be deducted and withheld.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on March 31, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), at the same time as the execution of this Agreement. The Closing shall be deemed effective as of 12:01 a.m. New York City time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of the directors and officers of the Company that Buyer designated before the Closing;

(e) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness for borrowed money, and evidence that the related liens have been released;

(f) a good standing certificate for the Company from the Secretary of State of the State of New York, dated within five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(g) a certificate of the secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller authorizing this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements; and

(h) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) the cash portion of the Purchase Price under Section 2.2(a);

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer;

(c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by the Company; and

(d) a certificate of the secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates and representatives to, hold in confidence all information concerning the Company and the Business, except to the extent that the information is generally available to the public other than through Seller's breach or is required to be disclosed by law.

**6.2 Books and Records.** For six (6) years after the Closing Date, Buyer shall cause the Company to retain the books and records of the Company relating to periods before the Closing. On reasonable notice, Buyer shall give Seller reasonable access to them where Seller needs that access for a legitimate purpose.

**6.3 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver any additional documents and take any further actions that are reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

**6.4 Transition Services.** Seller shall perform its obligations under the Transition Services Agreement in accordance with its terms.

**6.5 Public Announcements.** Neither party shall make any public announcement about this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed, except as required by law.

## ARTICLE VII. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**7.1 Non-Competition.** For five (5) years after the Closing Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, engage in the business of renting event furniture, tenting, staging or related equipment in any state in which the Company conducted the Business during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date. The provision of services to the Company under the Transition Services Agreement does not breach this Section 7.1.

**7.2 Non-Solicitation.** During the Restricted Period, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly (a) solicit or hire any employee of the Company, or (b) solicit any customer or supplier of the Company to stop doing business with the Company or to reduce the business it does with the Company.

**7.3 Remedies.** Seller acknowledges that a breach of this Article VII would cause Buyer irreparable harm. Buyer shall be entitled to equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to any other remedy.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, at Seller's expense, all income Tax Returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date. Seller shall deliver each such Tax Return to Buyer for review and comment at least thirty (30) days before its due date. Buyer shall prepare all other Tax Returns of the Company that are filed after the Closing Date.

**8.2 Straddle Periods.** For Straddle Periods, Taxes shall be allocated as follows: (a) for property and similar ad valorem Taxes, ratably by day; and (b) for all other Taxes, on a closing-of-the-books basis as of the end of the Closing Date.

**8.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as and to the extent reasonably requested by the other party, in connection with the filing of Tax Returns and any audit or other proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company.

**8.4 Transfer Taxes.** Seller and Buyer shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of all transfer, documentary, stamp and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**8.5 Tax Contests.** Buyer shall notify Seller within twenty (20) days after receipt of written notice of any audit or proceeding relating to Pre-Closing Taxes. Seller may participate in any such proceeding at its own expense. Seller shall not settle any such proceeding without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## ARTICLE IX. SURVIVAL; INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The representations and warranties in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until 11:59 p.m. New York City time on the date that is twelve (12) months after the Closing Date. A claim in respect of any such representation or warranty that is asserted in a Claim Notice delivered within 12 months after the Closing Date shall survive until it is finally resolved.

(b) The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed.

(c) Claims under Section 9.2(d) may be made only within the survival period applicable to Section 4.14.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates (including the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and shall hold them harmless from, all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Closing Indebtedness or Seller Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing or deducted in calculating the Purchase Price; and

(d) any Pre-Closing Taxes.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for the Fundamental Representations, until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $348,750 (the "**Basket**"). Once that threshold is exceeded, Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

(b) Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a), other than for the Fundamental Representations, shall not exceed $4,650,000.

(c) Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) The Basket and the cap in Section 9.4(b) do not apply to Losses arising from fraud or to claims under Sections 9.2(b), 9.2(c) or 9.2(d).

**9.5 Claims Procedure.**

(a) A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim (a "**Claim Notice**"), describing in reasonable detail the facts giving rise to the claim and the amount of Losses claimed, to the extent known. Any delay in giving notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent that the delay actually prejudices it.

(b) For any claim by a third party, the indemnifying party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, at the indemnifying party's expense, by written notice within fifteen (15) days after receiving the Claim Notice. The indemnifying party shall not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Section 8.5 governs proceedings relating to Pre-Closing Taxes.

**9.6 Payments.** The indemnifying party shall pay any amount owed under this Article IX within ten (10) Business Days after the claim is finally determined, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**9.7 Right of Set-Off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article IX, or any amount owed by Seller under Section 2.3, against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable under the Seller Note. To exercise this right, Buyer shall give Seller written notice describing in reasonable detail the claim and the amount being set off. Any set-off made in good faith under this Section 9.7 is not an event of default under the Seller Note. If the claim is later finally determined in Seller's favor, Buyer shall pay the amount withheld, together with interest at the rate stated in the Seller Note, within ten (10) Business Days after that determination.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Agreement shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

**9.9 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims based on fraud, claims for equitable relief, and disputes under Section 2.3, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**10.2 Termination of Affiliate Arrangements.** Effective as of the Closing, all contracts between the Company and Seller or any of its Affiliates are terminated without further liability to the Company, other than the Transition Services Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement.
