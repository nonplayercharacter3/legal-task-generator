## 1. Background

1.1 We write on behalf of Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc. ("**Oakhollow**"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc. ("**Tramil**"). The letter concerns the Transition Services Agreement dated as of March 31, 2026 (the "**TSA**"), between Titairt Group, LLC, as Provider ("**Titairt**"), and Oakhollow, as Recipient.

1.2 Tramil acquired all of the issued and outstanding shares of Oakhollow from Titairt under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 31, 2026. Since the closing, Titairt has provided the Services under the TSA. Those Services are the EventGrid Booking Platform (Service A-1), Warehouse Yard Management (Service A-2), Fleet-Routing Support (Service A-3) and Migration Assistance (Service A-4), each as described in Schedule A to the TSA.

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the TSA.

## 2. Exercise of Extension

2.1 Under Section 2.2 of the TSA, Oakhollow hereby exercises its right to extend the Initial Term for the one (1) additional period of ninety (90) days provided in that Section (the "**Extension Term**").

2.2 Oakhollow's migration of its booking and warehouse functions to its own systems is in progress but is not yet complete. Oakhollow continues to depend on the EventGrid booking platform for customer bookings, quotes, rental orders and deposits for its holiday-season corporate and charity events. It also continues to depend on the warehouse yard management system for its Secaucus, Yonkers and Wallingford facilities. To avoid disruption during the holiday peak, Oakhollow's planned cut-over is scheduled to take place during the Extension Term and outside its peak event season.

2.3 Oakhollow acknowledges that, under Section 2.2 of the TSA, the Service Fees for each Service will increase by ten percent (10%) during the Extension Term. It also acknowledges that the Services will otherwise continue on the same terms as during the Initial Term.

## 3. Continuation of Services and Migration Planning

3.1 Oakhollow expects Titairt to continue providing each of Services A-1 through A-4 without interruption, at the standard of performance and service levels required by Section 3.2 and Schedule A of the TSA.

3.2 Oakhollow's Service Manager will contact Titairt's Service Manager within the next five (5) Business Days. The purpose is to confirm the migration timeline for the Extension Term, including:
(a) data mapping and the export of Recipient Data under Sections 3.5 and 7.4 of the TSA;
(b) a schedule for parallel testing of the replacement booking and yard management systems; and
(c) cut-over support.

3.3 Please confirm to the undersigned, on or before November 23, 2026, that Titairt's billing and service records have been updated to reflect the Extension Term.
