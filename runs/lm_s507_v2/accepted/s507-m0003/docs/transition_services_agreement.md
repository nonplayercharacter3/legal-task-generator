# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 31, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Titairt Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Recipient**"). Provider and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## RECITALS

A. Under the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 31, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), purchased from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Recipient.

B. Before the closing under the Purchase Agreement, Provider made certain systems and support functions available to Recipient. These included the EventGrid booking platform, warehouse yard management and fleet-routing support.

C. To allow the orderly transition of the business of Recipient, Provider has agreed to provide those services to Recipient for a transitional period, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Applicable Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority applicable to a Party or to the Services.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Extension Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information of or relating to Recipient, its customers or its business that Recipient provides to Provider, or that Provider receives, generates, stores or processes in performing the Services. This includes customer names and contact details, event bookings, quotes, pricing, rental orders, inventory allocations, delivery and pick-up schedules, and fleet-routing data.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Service Manager**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as that schedule may be amended under Section 3.4.

"**Systems**" means the EventGrid booking platform, Provider's warehouse yard management system, Provider's fleet-routing software, and the related hardware, software, hosting and network infrastructure that Provider uses to provide the Services.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation"; headings are for convenience only; and references to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier under Section 10, continues for two hundred seventy (270) days after the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**").

**2.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Initial Term for one (1) additional period of ninety (90) days (the "**Extension Term**"). To do so, Recipient must give written notice to Provider no later than forty-five (45) days before the expiration of the Initial Term. During the Extension Term the Services shall be provided on the same terms as during the Initial Term, except that the Service Fees for each Service shall increase by ten percent (10%). The Initial Term, together with the Extension Term if Recipient extends it under this Section 2.2, is the "**Term**".

**2.3 Expiration of Services.** Each Service shall end on the earliest of (a) the expiration of the Term, (b) the termination date for that Service set out in Schedule A, if any, and (c) the effective date of any termination of that Service under Section 10.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause to be provided, the Services to Recipient in accordance with this Agreement and Schedule A.

**3.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) with at least the same degree of care, skill, priority and timeliness with which Provider provided the same or similar services to Recipient during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date, and (c) in compliance with Applicable Law. Provider shall meet the service levels set out in Schedule A, if any.

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall provide the Services through personnel who have the qualifications and experience that the Services reasonably require. Provider is solely responsible for the compensation, benefits and supervision of its personnel. Those personnel are not employees of Recipient.

**3.4 Changes to Services.** The Parties may add, modify or remove Services by a written amendment to Schedule A signed by both Parties. Provider shall not make any change to the Systems that materially and adversely affects the Services unless Recipient has given its prior written consent.

**3.5 Cooperation; Migration Assistance.** Each Party shall cooperate reasonably with the other in connection with the Services. During the Term, Provider shall give Recipient the assistance that Recipient reasonably requests to migrate the functions supported by the Services to Recipient's own systems or to a third-party provider. That assistance includes the export of Recipient Data under Section 7.4. Migration assistance of more than forty (40) personnel hours in any calendar month shall be charged at the hourly rate set out in Schedule A.

**3.6 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any third-party consent or license required for Provider to provide the Services. If Provider cannot obtain such a consent, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to agree on a reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 4. SERVICE MANAGEMENT

**4.1 Service Managers.** Each Party shall appoint an individual to act as its primary contact for the administration of this Agreement (a "**Service Manager**"). A Party may replace its Service Manager by written notice to the other Party.

**4.2 Reporting.** Provider shall deliver to Recipient a monthly report describing the performance of the Services, any service level failures, any security incidents, and the status of any migration activity.

**4.3 Escalation.** If either Service Manager believes that a Service is not being performed in accordance with this Agreement, the Service Managers shall meet within five (5) Business Days to resolve the matter. Any matter that is not resolved within ten (10) Business Days after that meeting shall be escalated to a senior executive of each Party.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** In consideration of the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider the monthly fees set out in Schedule A (the "**Service Fees**"). Service Fees for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**5.2 Invoices.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Service Fees by Service and any reimbursable expenses. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**5.3 Expenses.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred at Recipient's written request in performing the Services. No other expenses are reimbursable.

**5.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith, by giving Provider written notice of the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall resolve any such dispute under Section 4.3. Provider shall continue to perform the Services while a dispute is pending.

**5.5 Taxes.** The Service Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Recipient shall pay any such taxes that are properly invoiced by Provider and required by Applicable Law to be collected from Recipient. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 6. SYSTEMS ACCESS

**6.1 Access to Systems.** During the Term, Provider grants Recipient and its authorized employees and contractors a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Systems solely to receive the Services and to conduct Recipient's business. Recipient shall comply with Provider's reasonable written security and acceptable-use policies for the Systems that have been provided to Recipient.

**6.2 Provider Access to Recipient Systems.** If Provider needs access to any system of Recipient to perform the Services, Recipient shall grant that access to the extent reasonably necessary. Provider shall comply with Recipient's security policies when accessing those systems.

**6.3 Ownership.** As between the Parties, Provider retains all right, title and interest in and to the Systems. Nothing in this Agreement affects the assignment to Recipient of the Oakhollow marks and booking-portal content under the IP Assignment Agreement.

## 7. DATA AND SECURITY

**7.1 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, Recipient owns all Recipient Data. Provider shall use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services and for no other purpose.

**7.2 Segregation.** Provider shall keep Recipient Data logically segregated from the data of Provider and of any other Person within the Systems. Provider shall not permit any Person other than Recipient and Provider's personnel who perform the Services to access Recipient Data.

**7.3 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for Recipient Data that are no less protective than those Provider maintained for that data before the Effective Date and that comply with Applicable Law. Provider shall notify Recipient in writing within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or use or disclosure of, Recipient Data. Provider shall cooperate with Recipient in investigating and remediating any such incident.

**7.4 Data Export and Return.** On Recipient's request at any time during the Term, and in any event within ten (10) Business Days after the end of the Term or the termination of any Service, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a complete copy of all Recipient Data held in the relevant Systems, in a commonly used, machine-readable format reasonably acceptable to Recipient. After Recipient confirms in writing that it has received that data, Provider shall permanently delete all Recipient Data in its possession and certify the deletion in writing. However, Provider may keep any copies that Applicable Law requires it to retain, and those copies remain subject to Section 8.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party that it receives under this Agreement, including Recipient Data. Each Party shall not disclose that information to any Person other than its employees, agents and advisors who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Section 8.

**8.2 Exceptions.** Section 8.1 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before receipt without a confidentiality obligation, or (c) is required to be disclosed by Applicable Law. In the case of clause (c), the receiving Party shall, where permitted, give the disclosing Party prompt notice so that it may seek a protective order.

**8.3 Survival.** The obligations in this Section 8 survive for three (3) years after the end of the Term. Obligations relating to Recipient Data survive for as long as Provider or any Person acting for it holds any Recipient Data.

## 9. WARRANTIES; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INDEMNITY

**9.1 Mutual Warranties.** Each Party represents and warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is its legal, valid and binding obligation.

**9.2 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

**9.3 Limitation of Liability.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages arising out of this Agreement. Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. The limitations in this Section 9.3 do not apply to (a) a breach of Section 7 or Section 8, (b) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, or (c) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 9.4.

**9.4 Indemnification.** Each Party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other Party and its officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of any third-party claim to the extent caused by the indemnifying Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of Section 7 or Section 8.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination of Services by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience by giving Provider at least thirty (30) days' written notice. On the effective date of termination, the Service Fees shall be reduced by the fees allocated to the terminated Service in Schedule A. Recipient shall have no other liability for the termination.

**10.2 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Provider may not terminate this Agreement or suspend any Service for non-payment of amounts that Recipient disputes in good faith under Section 5.4.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** On the expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, (a) Recipient shall pay all undisputed Service Fees accrued through the effective date, (b) Provider shall comply with Section 7.4, and (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's confidential information in accordance with Section 8.

**10.4 Survival.** Sections 5, 6.3, 7, 8, 9, 10.3, 10.4, 12 and 13 survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 11. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement, other than payment obligations, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, natural disaster, epidemic, war, terrorism, or failure of public utilities. The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party, use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance, and implement its business continuity plans. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing any Service for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days, Recipient may terminate that Service immediately by written notice.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**12.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
