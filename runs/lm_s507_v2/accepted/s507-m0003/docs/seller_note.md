# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$7,000,000**
**Dated: March 31, 2026**
**New York, New York**

## 1. Promise to Pay

1.1 For value received, **Tramil Hospitality Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Titairt Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (together with its successors and permitted assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal amount of $7,000,000. The Maker shall also pay interest on the unpaid principal amount as provided in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

1.2 This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 31, 2026, between the Holder, as Seller, and the Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Maker issues it under Section 2.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement as part of the Purchase Price for all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Oakhollow Event Rentals, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**").

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Interest

2.1 **Rate.** The outstanding principal amount of this Note bears interest from the date of this Note until paid in full at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum.

2.2 **Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

2.3 **Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning June 30, 2026. Accrued interest is also payable on the Maturity Date and on any prepayment, as to the principal amount prepaid.

2.4 **Default Interest.** While an Event of Default under Section 6.1(a) is continuing, the outstanding principal amount of this Note bears interest at the rate otherwise applicable under Section 2.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

2.5 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest shall be payable under this Note above the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. Any amount received above that rate shall be applied to reduce the outstanding principal amount. Any remaining excess shall be refunded to the Maker.

## 3. Payment of Principal; Manner of Payment

3.1 **Installments.** The Maker shall repay the principal amount of this Note in four (4) equal annual installments of $1,750,000 each, payable on March 31, 2027, March 31, 2028, March 31, 2029 and March 31, 2030 (the last of those dates, the "**Maturity Date**"). All unpaid principal and all accrued and unpaid interest are due and payable on the Maturity Date.

3.2 **Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States, in immediately available funds, by wire transfer to the account the Holder designates in writing from time to time.

3.3 **Business Days.** If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day, and interest shall accrue for the additional days.

3.4 **Application of Payments.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to outstanding principal.

## 4. Prepayment

The Maker may prepay this Note at any time, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the principal amount prepaid. Partial prepayments shall be applied to the principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Subordination

5.1 **Subordination to Senior Debt.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinated and junior in right of payment to all Senior Debt. "**Senior Debt**" means all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Maker or the Company owed to any bank, commercial finance company or other institutional lender, now outstanding or later incurred, together with all interest, fees, expenses and other amounts payable in respect of it.

5.2 **Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under any Senior Debt, the Maker may make, and the Holder may receive, regularly scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

5.3 **Payment Blockage.** While a payment default or acceleration has occurred and is continuing under any Senior Debt, the Maker shall not make, and the Holder shall not accept, any payment under this Note. Any payment the Holder receives in breach of this Section 5.3 shall be held in trust for the holders of Senior Debt and promptly turned over to them.

5.4 **Subordination Agreements.** At the request of the Maker or any holder of Senior Debt, the Holder shall enter into a subordination agreement on terms customary for seller subordinated debt and consistent with this Section 5.

5.5 **No Impairment.** As between the Maker and the Holder, nothing in this Section 5 impairs the Maker's obligation to pay the principal of and interest on this Note in accordance with its terms.

## 6. Events of Default

6.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal of or interest on this Note within ten (10) Business Days after the date it is due;

(b) the Maker fails to perform any other agreement in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after the Holder gives the Maker written notice of it;

(c) the Maker or the Company (i) commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, (ii) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iii) consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its property;

(d) an involuntary case is commenced against the Maker or the Company under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar law, and it is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(e) any Senior Debt in an outstanding principal amount of more than $5,000,000 is accelerated and that acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days.

6.2 **Remedies.** If an Event of Default under Section 6.1(c) or 6.1(d) occurs, the entire unpaid principal amount of this Note and all accrued and unpaid interest become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action. If any other Event of Default occurs and is continuing, the Holder may, by written notice to the Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal amount of this Note and all accrued and unpaid interest immediately due and payable. In each case, the Holder's rights are subject to Section 5.

6.3 **Costs of Collection.** The Maker shall pay all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that the Holder incurs in collecting amounts due under this Note after an Event of Default.

## 7. Waivers

7.1 The Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note.

7.2 No delay or omission of the Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right. A waiver on one occasion is not a waiver on any other occasion.

## 8. Transfer

8.1 **Transfer by Holder.** The Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note, or any interest in it, without the prior written consent of the Maker, except to an Affiliate of the Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note. Any purported transfer in violation of this Section 8.1 is void.

8.2 **Transfer by Maker.** The Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of the Holder, except to a successor to all or substantially all of the business and assets of the Maker that assumes this Note in writing.

8.3 **Securities Laws.** This Note has not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law. It may not be transferred except in compliance with those laws.

8.4 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, the Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor on receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from the Holder.

## 9. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

9.1 **Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims or causes of action based on, arising out of or relating to this Note, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** The Maker and the Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, they submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each of them waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

9.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** THE MAKER AND THE HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT THEY MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be given in the manner, and to the addresses, set out in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. The Maker's notice address is its address as Buyer, and the Holder's notice address is its address as Seller.

10.2 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder.

10.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, that holding does not affect any other provision of this Note.

10.4 **Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds the Maker and its successors and permitted assigns. It benefits the Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

10.5 **Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
