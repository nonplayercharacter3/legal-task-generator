# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2024 by **Gesav Thermal Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Hunts Point Avenue, Bronx, New York 10474 ("**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Beneficiary and Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, together with each Statement of Work issued under it, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider provides refrigerated and frozen warehousing, temperature-controlled transport, cold chain monitoring and related services to Beneficiary.

B. Provider is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement.

C. Beneficiary requires, as a condition to entering into the Agreement, that Guarantor deliver this Guaranty.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, including the term "Change of Control" as defined in Section 1.1 of the Agreement.

1.2 "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations, liabilities and duties of Provider to Beneficiary under the Agreement, whether now existing or arising later, including:
(a) the due and punctual payment of all amounts payable by Provider under the Agreement, including amounts due under Sections 6.3, 8.4(d) and 10.1 of the Agreement; and
(b) the due and punctual performance of all of Provider's other covenants and obligations under the Agreement,
in each case as and to the extent limited under the Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

2.2 **Performance on Default.** If Provider fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall, within ten (10) business days after receipt of Beneficiary's written demand, pay the amount due or perform the obligation, or cause it to be performed.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Beneficiary is not required to bring any action against Provider, exhaust any remedy against Provider or enforce any security before making demand on or proceeding against Guarantor.

2.4 **Defenses.** Guarantor may assert any defense to a Guaranteed Obligation that Provider could assert under the Agreement, other than defenses arising from Provider's insolvency, bankruptcy, lack of capacity or lack of authority, or from any limitation of Provider's liability imposed by law in any insolvency proceeding.

## 3. WAIVERS

3.1 Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law:
(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty and of the creation of any Guaranteed Obligation;
(b) presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of dishonor and notice of default, other than the demand required by Section 2.2;
(c) any right to require Beneficiary to proceed first against Provider or any other person; and
(d) any defense based on suretyship or impairment of collateral.

3.2 **Consents.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by:
(a) any amendment, extension, renewal, change order or Statement of Work under the Agreement;
(b) any waiver, indulgence or extension of time granted to Provider;
(c) any insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or dissolution of Provider; or
(d) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Provider.

## 4. REINSTATEMENT

If any payment by Provider or Guarantor to Beneficiary on account of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Provider or otherwise, this Guaranty shall continue to be effective, or be reinstated, as to that payment as though it had not been made.

## 5. SUBROGATION

Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Provider arising from any payment under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid and performed in full.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Beneficiary that:
(a) it is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;
(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has duly authorized it by all necessary corporate action;
(c) this Guaranty constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally;
(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any law applicable to it or any agreement by which it is bound; and
(e) Provider is, directly or indirectly, a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor.

## 7. TERM OF GUARANTY

7.1 This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations, including those arising during any Renewal Term and any Transition Period, have been paid and performed in full.

7.2 If Beneficiary undergoes a Change of Control without Guarantor's prior written consent, this Guaranty shall automatically terminate and be of no further force or effect.

## 8. FINANCIAL INFORMATION

Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Guarantor shall deliver to Beneficiary, on Beneficiary's written request, Guarantor's audited consolidated financial statements for that fiscal year. Beneficiary shall treat those financial statements as Confidential Information of Guarantor on the terms of Section 8 of the Agreement, applied as though Guarantor were the Discloser.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT

9.1 Guarantor may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Guaranty without Beneficiary's prior written consent. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 9.1 is void.

9.2 Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any person to whom it assigns the Agreement in accordance with Section 15 of the Agreement.

9.3 This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Beneficiary and its permitted successors and assigns.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Guarantor submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or related to this Guaranty. Guarantor waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

12.3 **Jury Waiver.** GUARANTOR AND BENEFICIARY EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS GUARANTY.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment to this Guaranty is effective unless it is in writing and signed by Guarantor and Beneficiary. No waiver by Beneficiary is effective unless it is in writing. A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of that right.

13.2 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

13.3 **Cumulative Remedies.** Beneficiary's rights under this Guaranty are in addition to, and not in substitution for, its rights against Provider under the Agreement.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
