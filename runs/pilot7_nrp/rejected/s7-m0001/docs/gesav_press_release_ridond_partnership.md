# Gesav Coldline Logistics Announces Cold Chain Partnership with Ridond Fresh Provisions

*Gesav to provide refrigerated warehousing, temperature-controlled transport and real-time cold chain monitoring for New York prepared foods distributor*

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## 1. Announcement

**SECAUCUS, N.J., March 12, 2024.** Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC ("Gesav"), a provider of temperature-controlled supply chain services in the northeastern United States, today announced a multi-year master services agreement with Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc. ("Ridond"). Ridond is a Bronx, New York-based distributor of fresh and frozen prepared foods to retail, foodservice and institutional customers.

Under the agreement, which took effect March 1, 2024, Gesav will provide Ridond with:

- refrigerated and frozen warehousing;
- temperature-controlled transport;
- cross-docking and inventory management; and
- continuous cold chain monitoring through Gesav's monitoring platform.

## 2. Service Highlights

Gesav will store and move Ridond's products within product-specific temperature ranges. Temperature readings will be logged at least every fifteen minutes. Ridond's team will have real-time access to temperature data for its inventory through Gesav's monitoring platform, so it can see shipment conditions from receipt to delivery.

The relationship also covers lot-level traceability and recall support. These are designed to help Ridond meet its obligations under the Food Safety Modernization Act, including the FDA's Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule.

## 4. About Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC

Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC is headquartered in Secaucus, New Jersey, and is a subsidiary of Gesav Thermal Holdings, Inc. Gesav operates refrigerated and frozen warehousing facilities, a temperature-controlled transport fleet and a proprietary cold chain monitoring platform. It serves food manufacturers, distributors and retailers throughout the northeastern United States.

## 5. About Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc.

Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc. is a New York distributor of fresh and frozen prepared foods. It is headquartered at the Hunts Point food distribution hub in the Bronx and serves retail, foodservice and institutional customers across the northeastern United States.
