# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of October 5, 2026 (the "**DPA Effective Date**") by and between **Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Hunts Point Avenue, Bronx, New York 10474 ("**Customer**"), and **Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (as amended from time to time, together with each Statement of Work issued under it, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider provides refrigerated and frozen warehousing, temperature-controlled transport, cold chain monitoring and related services to Customer.

B. In performing the Services, Provider processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer, including information about Customer's customers, consignees, delivery recipients and employees.

C. Section 8.4 of the Agreement permits the parties to supplement Provider's data protection obligations by entering into a DPA. The parties enter into this DPA for that purpose.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement, including "Applicable Law," "Personal Data," "Services" and "Statement of Work."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this DPA:

"**Data Protection Laws**" means all Applicable Law relating to privacy, data security, data breach notification or the processing of Personal Data. This includes the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations (the "**CCPA**"); the New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act (N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law §§ 899-aa and 899-bb); and any other comparable state privacy law, in each case to the extent applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement.

"**Data Subject**" means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

"**Data Subject Request**" means a request by or on behalf of a Data Subject to exercise a right granted under Data Protection Laws with respect to that Data Subject's Personal Data.

"**Personal Data Breach**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, Personal Data in Provider's or any Subprocessor's possession, custody or control.

"**Processing**" (and "**process**" and "**processed**") means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, transmission, disclosure, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure and destruction.

"**Subprocessor**" means any third party, including any Affiliate of Provider, that Provider engages to process Personal Data on behalf of Customer.

## 2. SCOPE AND ROLES

2.1 **Scope.** This DPA applies to all Personal Data that Provider processes on behalf of Customer in connection with the Agreement, whether before or after the DPA Effective Date. The subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the processing, and the categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects, are described in Annex A.

2.2 **Roles.** Customer is the controller (or "business" under the CCPA) of the Personal Data. Provider is a processor (or "service provider" under the CCPA) processing Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

2.3 **Relationship to the Agreement.** This DPA forms part of the Agreement and supplements Section 8.4 of the Agreement.

## 3. PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

3.1 **Instructions.** Provider shall process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions and only as necessary to perform the Services. The Agreement, this DPA and each Statement of Work constitute Customer's instructions as of the DPA Effective Date. Customer may give additional reasonable written instructions consistent with the Services.

3.2 **Unlawful Instructions.** Provider shall promptly inform Customer if, in Provider's reasonable opinion, an instruction from Customer violates Data Protection Laws.

3.3 **CCPA Service Provider Obligations.** Provider shall not:
(a) sell or share (as those terms are defined in the CCPA) Personal Data;
(b) retain, use or disclose Personal Data for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in Annex A, including for any commercial purpose other than performing the Services;
(c) retain, use or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between the parties; or
(d) combine Personal Data with personal information that Provider receives from or on behalf of any other person, or collects from its own interactions with consumers, except as permitted by the CCPA.

3.4 **Compliance Notice.** Provider shall notify Customer within five (5) business days if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Data Protection Laws. On receipt of that notice, Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized processing.

3.5 **Certification.** Provider certifies that it understands and will comply with the restrictions in this Section 3.

## 4. PROVIDER PERSONNEL

4.1 Provider shall limit access to Personal Data to those of its employees and contractors who need access to perform the Services, including drivers and dispatch, customer service and warehouse personnel who handle delivery recipient information.

4.2 Provider shall ensure that each person authorized to process Personal Data is bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Section 8 of the Agreement and has received appropriate training on the handling of Personal Data.

## 5. SECURITY

5.1 **Safeguards.** Provider shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the risk of the processing and consistent with industry standards. Those safeguards shall include, at a minimum, the measures described in Annex B.

5.2 **Cold Chain Monitoring Platform.** Provider shall apply the safeguards in Annex B to its cold chain monitoring platform and to any customer portal, routing software, electronic proof-of-delivery system or telematics system through which Personal Data is processed.

5.3 **Review.** Provider shall review its safeguards at least annually and following any Personal Data Breach, and shall update them as necessary to address reasonably foreseeable risks.

## 6. SUBPROCESSORS

6.1 **Consent.** Provider shall not engage any Subprocessor without Customer's prior written consent, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold, consistent with Section 7.2 of the Agreement. Customer consents to the Subprocessors listed in Annex C.

6.2 **New Subprocessors.** Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any proposed new or replacement Subprocessor, stating the Subprocessor's name, location and the processing it will perform. Customer may object in writing within that period on reasonable data protection grounds, and the parties shall discuss Customer's objection in good faith.

6.3 **Subprocessor Terms.** Provider shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA. Provider remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance as if the performance were its own.

## 7. PERSONAL DATA BREACH

7.1 **Notice.** Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. Provider shall send notice to Customer's notice address under Section 16 of the Agreement and by email to privacy@ridondfresh.com.

7.2 **Content of Notice.** Provider's notice shall describe, to the extent then known:
(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
(b) the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach;
(c) the measures Provider has taken or proposes to take to contain and remediate it; and
(d) the name and contact details of a Provider contact who can provide further information.
Provider shall supplement its notice as further information becomes available.

7.3 **Cooperation.** Provider shall cooperate with Customer in investigating, containing and remediating each Personal Data Breach. Provider shall not notify any Data Subject, regulator or other third party of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data without Customer's prior written approval, except as required by law.

7.4 **Costs.** Provider shall reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of investigation, notification, credit monitoring and remediation to the extent a Personal Data Breach results from Provider's or a Subprocessor's breach of the Agreement or this DPA.

## 8. ASSISTANCE

8.1 **Data Subject Requests.** Provider shall notify Customer within five (5) business days after receiving any Data Subject Request and shall not respond to it except on Customer's instructions. Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to Data Subject Requests within the time required by Data Protection Laws.

8.2 **Other Assistance.** Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer with any data protection assessment, regulatory inquiry or consultation relating to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement.

## 9. AUDIT

9.1 **Information.** Provider shall make available to Customer, on request, all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA, including its most recent third-party security assessment or audit report covering the systems that process Personal Data.

9.2 **Audit Rights.** Customer or its designee may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA in accordance with Section 18.4 of the Agreement. An audit following a Personal Data Breach does not count toward the annual limit in Section 18.4 of the Agreement.

## 10. LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this DPA, including any Personal Data Breach, is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability set out in Section 11 of the Agreement and shall in no event exceed the cap set out in Section 11.2 of the Agreement.

## 11. RETURN OR DELETION OF PERSONAL DATA

11.1 On expiration or termination of the Agreement, and following any Transition Period, Provider shall, at Customer's election, return all Personal Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format or securely destroy it, in accordance with Section 8.5 of the Agreement.

11.2 Provider shall certify the return or destruction in writing within thirty (30) days after completing it. Provider may retain Personal Data to the extent required by Applicable Law, including food safety traceability recordkeeping requirements, in which case Provider shall continue to protect that Personal Data in accordance with this DPA for so long as it retains it.

## 12. TERM

This DPA takes effect on the DPA Effective Date and remains in effect for so long as Provider or any Subprocessor processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction.** This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Section 17 of the Agreement applies to any dispute arising out of or related to this DPA.

13.2 **Amendment.** No amendment to this DPA is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this DPA is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

13.4 **Counterparts.** This DPA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.

## ANNEX A – DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

**Subject matter and purpose:** Processing of Personal Data as necessary for Provider to perform the Services, including receiving, storing and dispatching Customer Goods; routing and delivering shipments; issuing warehouse receipts and proof of delivery; supporting recalls and traceability; and providing access to the cold chain monitoring platform.

**Nature of processing:** Collection, storage, retrieval, use, transmission and deletion in connection with order management, dispatch, delivery, customer service, platform access management and recordkeeping.

**Duration:** For the Term of the Agreement and any Transition Period, and thereafter only as permitted by Section 11 of this DPA.

**Categories of Data Subjects:**
- Customer's customers and their personnel
- Consignees and delivery recipients
- Customer's employees and contractors, including authorized users of the cold chain monitoring platform

**Categories of Personal Data:**
- Names, job titles and business contact details
- Delivery addresses, delivery instructions and access codes
- Signatures, photographs and geolocation data captured in electronic proof of delivery
- Platform user credentials and access logs
- Order and delivery history associated with an identified individual

**Sensitive Personal Data:** None, except to the extent account credentials permitting access to an account constitute sensitive personal information under the CCPA.

## ANNEX B – SECURITY MEASURES

1. Written information security program overseen by a designated security officer.
2. Role-based access controls, least-privilege access and prompt revocation of access on personnel separation.
3. Multi-factor authentication for remote access and for all administrative access to systems processing Personal Data.
4. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
5. Logging and monitoring of access to systems processing Personal Data, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.
6. Vulnerability scanning at least quarterly and independent penetration testing at least annually, with timely remediation of identified critical and high-risk vulnerabilities.
7. Endpoint protection on all workstations, servers and in-cab devices that process Personal Data.
8. Physical access controls at all facilities housing systems that process Personal Data.
9. Documented incident response plan, tested at least annually.
10. Secure disposal of media and devices containing Personal Data in accordance with NIST SP 800-88.
