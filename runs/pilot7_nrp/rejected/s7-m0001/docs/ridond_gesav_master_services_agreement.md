# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Ridond Fresh Provisions, Inc.**, a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Hunts Point Avenue, Bronx, New York 10474 ("**Customer**"), and **Gesav Coldline Logistics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer distributes fresh and frozen prepared foods to retail, foodservice and institutional customers in the northeastern United States.

B. Provider operates refrigerated and frozen warehousing facilities, a temperature-controlled transport fleet and a cold chain monitoring platform.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to provide such services, and Provider wishes to provide them, on the terms of this Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting securities of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations and binding orders that apply to a party's performance under this Agreement. This includes the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and its implementing regulations, including the Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule (21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart O), and applicable data protection and privacy laws.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to a party, the direct or indirect acquisition, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, of over 50% of the voting securities of that party or its parent by any person or group of persons acting in concert.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Customer Goods**" means all food products, ingredients, packaging and other goods that Customer, or a third party on Customer's behalf, tenders to Provider under this Agreement.

"**DPA**" means any data processing addendum that the parties enter into in connection with this Agreement, as amended from time to time.

"**Fees**" means the fees and charges set out in each Statement of Work.

"**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that Provider receives from or on behalf of Customer, or that Provider processes on behalf of Customer, in connection with the Services. This includes information about Customer's customers, consignees, delivery recipients and employees.

"**Services**" means the refrigerated and frozen warehousing, temperature-controlled transport, cross-docking, cold chain monitoring, inventory management and related services described in one or more Statements of Work.

"**Service Levels**" means the performance standards set out in Section 3 and in any Statement of Work.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work that references this Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

"**Temperature Specifications**" means the storage and transport temperature ranges for each category of Customer Goods set out in the applicable Statement of Work or, if none is stated, in Customer's written product handling instructions delivered to Provider.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES; STATEMENTS OF WORK

2.1 **Performance of Services.** Provider shall perform the Services described in each Statement of Work in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Statement of Work, the Service Levels and Applicable Law.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work is governed by and incorporated into this Agreement. Each Statement of Work shall describe the Services, the facilities and lanes covered, the Temperature Specifications, the Fees and any additional Service Levels. Neither party is obligated under a Statement of Work until both parties have signed it.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If the documents comprising the parties' agreement conflict, the following order of precedence applies: the DPA (as to Personal Data only), then this Agreement, then the Statements of Work.

2.4 **Change Orders.** Either party may request changes to the Services under a Statement of Work. No change is effective unless it is set out in a written change order signed by both parties that states any adjustment to the Fees or Service Levels.

2.5 **Non-Exclusivity.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. Nothing in this Agreement obligates Customer to tender any minimum volume of Customer Goods except as expressly stated in a Statement of Work.

## 3. SERVICE STANDARDS

3.1 **Temperature Control.** Provider shall store, handle and transport all Customer Goods within the Temperature Specifications at all times while the Customer Goods are in Provider's care, custody or control.

3.2 **Monitoring and Records.** Provider shall continuously monitor the temperature of all storage areas and transport units through its cold chain monitoring platform. Provider shall record temperature readings at intervals of no more than fifteen (15) minutes and shall retain those records for at least two (2) years. Provider shall give Customer real-time read access to temperature data for Customer Goods through the platform.

3.3 **Excursions.** Provider shall notify Customer within one (1) hour after it detects any deviation from the Temperature Specifications affecting Customer Goods (an "**Excursion**"). Provider shall segregate the affected Customer Goods and hold them pending Customer's written disposition instructions. Customer's determination of whether affected Customer Goods are fit for sale shall be final.

3.4 **Food Safety.** Provider shall maintain all registrations, licenses and certifications required by Applicable Law for its facilities and vehicles. This includes registration of its food facilities with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and a current third-party audit certification under a GFSI-benchmarked scheme. Provider shall comply with Customer's reasonable written food safety, sanitation, allergen segregation and recall procedures.

3.5 **Recalls.** If any Customer Goods become subject to a recall, market withdrawal or regulatory hold, Provider shall cooperate fully with Customer. This includes locating, segregating and holding or returning affected Customer Goods within the time frames Customer reasonably requires and providing lot-level traceability records within four (4) hours after Customer's request.

3.6 **Service Level Credits.** If Provider fails to meet a Service Level, Provider shall issue the service credits stated in the applicable Statement of Work. Service credits are not Customer's exclusive remedy and do not limit Customer's other rights under this Agreement.

## 4. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

4.1 **Tendering of Goods.** Customer shall tender Customer Goods properly packaged and labeled and at the temperature required by the Temperature Specifications. Customer shall also provide accurate descriptions, quantities and handling instructions.

4.2 **Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Provider with non-binding rolling monthly volume forecasts for each facility and lane covered by a Statement of Work.

4.3 **Cooperation.** Customer shall provide such information and access to its personnel as Provider reasonably requests to perform the Services.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the Fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes, other than taxes based on Provider's net income, property or employees.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the Services performed, with supporting activity reports.

5.3 **Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it notifies Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis of the dispute before the payment due date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days.

5.4 **Fuel Surcharge.** Any fuel surcharge shall be calculated only in accordance with the methodology set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

5.5 **Fee Adjustments.** Provider may adjust the Fees no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, on at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice. Any adjustment may not exceed the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the New York–Newark–Jersey City area over the preceding twelve (12) months.

5.6 **No Liens.** Provider waives, to the extent permitted by Applicable Law, any warehouse lien, carrier's lien or other lien on Customer Goods. Provider shall not withhold delivery of Customer Goods because of any payment dispute.

## 6. TITLE, RISK OF LOSS AND RECEIPTS

6.1 **Title.** Title to Customer Goods remains with Customer at all times. Provider shall not pledge, encumber or permit any lien on Customer Goods.

6.2 **Care, Custody and Control.** Customer Goods are in Provider's care, custody and control from Provider's receipt of the goods at a Provider facility or into a Provider transport unit until delivery to the consignee or to Customer, as specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

6.3 **Loss and Damage.** Provider is liable for loss of or damage to Customer Goods while they are in its care, custody or control, including loss or damage caused by an Excursion. Provider is not liable to the extent the loss or damage is caused by Customer's breach of Section 4.1 or by an inherent defect in the Customer Goods. Provider's liability under this Section 6.3 shall be measured at Customer's replacement cost plus reasonable disposal costs.

6.4 **Warehouse Receipts.** Provider shall issue non-negotiable warehouse receipts for all Customer Goods received into storage. To the extent any warehouse receipt or bill of lading conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement governs.

6.5 **Inventory Counts.** Provider shall perform cycle counts in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work and a full physical inventory at least annually. Customer may observe any inventory count.

## 7. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTORS

7.1 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified, trained personnel to perform the Services. This includes drivers holding all required commercial licenses and personnel trained in sanitary transportation practices as required by Applicable Law.

7.2 **Subcontractors.** Provider shall not subcontract any material portion of the Services, including any transport lane, without Customer's prior written consent, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as if the performance were its own.

7.3 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

8.1 **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information that one party (the "**Discloser**") discloses to the other party (the "**Recipient**") in connection with this Agreement and that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. This includes pricing, customer lists, volume data, product formulations, business plans and Personal Data. Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient;
(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

8.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall:
(a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services;
(b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care; and
(c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

8.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or legal process. Where lawful, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Discloser may seek a protective order.

8.4 **Data Protection.** Provider shall process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions and only as necessary to perform the Services. Provider shall:
(a) implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to the risk, consistent with industry standards;
(b) not sell or share Personal Data, or retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship between the parties;
(c) notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition, disclosure or loss of Personal Data;
(d) cooperate with Customer in any investigation, notification or remediation relating to such an incident, and reimburse Customer's reasonable costs of investigation, notification, credit monitoring and remediation to the extent the incident results from Provider's breach of this Section 8; and
(e) comply with Applicable Law relating to the processing of Personal Data.
The parties may supplement this Section 8.4 by entering into a DPA.

8.5 **Return or Destruction.** On termination or expiration of this Agreement, or on the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall return or securely destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, including all Personal Data, and certify the return or destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by Applicable Law, subject to continuing confidentiality.

8.6 **Equitable Relief.** A breach of this Section 8 may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate. The Discloser may seek injunctive relief without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy.

(a) it is duly organized and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of organization;
(b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and
(c) its performance of this Agreement does not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

9.2 **Provider.** Provider represents and warrants that:
(a) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with prevailing industry standards for cold chain logistics;
(b) its facilities, equipment and vehicles are, and will remain, clean, in good repair and suitable for the storage and transport of food in compliance with Applicable Law; and
(c) it holds and will maintain all permits, licenses and registrations required to perform the Services.

9.3 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, fines, penalties, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), arising out of:
(a) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct;
(b) Provider's breach of Applicable Law;
(c) bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by Provider or its personnel or subcontractors;
(d) any recall or regulatory action to the extent caused by Provider's failure to maintain the Temperature Specifications or comply with Section 3; or
(e) Provider's breach of Section 8.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all third-party claims, and all resulting losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), arising out of:
(a) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct; or
(b) any defect in Customer Goods existing at the time of tender to Provider, except to the extent caused by Provider.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall promptly notify the indemnifying party of any claim, give it sole control of the defense and settlement (except that no settlement may impose any obligation or admission on the indemnified party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. A delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent the delay materially prejudices it.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or related to this Agreement, however caused, even if advised of their possibility.

11.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer to Provider under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:
(a) a party's breach of Section 8 (Confidentiality and Data Protection); or
(b) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 10.

11.4 **Loss of Goods.** Provider's liability for loss of or damage to Customer Goods under Section 6.3 counts toward the cap in Section 11.2. No limitation of liability in any warehouse receipt, bill of lading or tariff applies to Customer Goods.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive two (2) year renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**"), unless either party gives the other party written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to expiry of the then-current term. Each Statement of Work remains in effect for the term stated in it but does not extend beyond the Term unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

12.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any affected Statement of Work by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

12.3 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:
(a) becomes insolvent or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due;
(b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors;
(c) has a receiver, trustee or similar officer appointed over all or a substantial part of its assets; or
(d) becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

12.4 **Transition Assistance.** Upon any expiration or termination, and for up to one hundred twenty (120) days thereafter at Customer's request (the "**Transition Period**"), Provider shall continue to perform the Services at the Fees then in effect. Provider shall also cooperate in good faith in the orderly transfer of Customer Goods, inventory records and temperature data to Customer or its designated successor provider. Provider shall release all Customer Goods on Customer's instruction, subject only to payment of undisputed Fees.

12.5 **Effect of Termination.** Expiration or termination does not affect any rights or liabilities accrued before the effective date of expiration or termination. Sections 5, 6.3, 8, 10, 11, 12.4, 12.5, 16 and 17 survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 13. INSURANCE

13.1 **Required Coverage.** Throughout the Term and any Transition Period, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:
(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate;
(b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 combined single limit;
(c) warehouse legal liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per location;
(d) motor truck cargo insurance, including reefer breakdown coverage, of at least $500,000 per vehicle;
(e) cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; and
(f) workers' compensation insurance as required by law, with employer's liability of at least $1,000,000.

13.2 **Certificates.** Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in Sections 13.1(a) and 13.1(b), and as loss payee on the policies in Sections 13.1(c) and 13.1(d). Provider shall deliver certificates of insurance on request and give at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction of coverage.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, flood, fire, epidemic, war, terrorism, government action and widespread utility failure. They do not include equipment failure, labor disputes involving the affected party's own workforce, or failures of the affected party's subcontractors.

14.2 The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event. Provider shall maintain and implement a written business continuity plan that includes backup refrigeration power at each facility. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing any material portion of the Services for more than ten (10) consecutive days, Customer may procure substitute services from a third party for the duration of the event without liability to Provider.
