# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (as defined below) by and among Drebuv Agritech Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Bruveind Aerial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Calloway Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Sowfield Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Agent will hold that amount as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Claimed Amount**" means, for any Claim Notice, the amount of Losses claimed in it. If those Losses are not yet determined, it means Buyer's good-faith estimate of them as stated in the Claim Notice.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $14,850,000.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount as deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any earnings thereon, as reduced by disbursements made in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction determining a dispute concerning a Claim Notice. An order or judgment is non-appealable when the time for appeal has expired without an appeal having been taken, or when all appeals have been finally resolved.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Duties Limited.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set forth in this Agreement, and those duties are ministerial in nature. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement. It has no duty to know, interpret or enforce any of its terms, and it shall not be required to refer to the Purchase Agreement to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

## 3. DEPOSIT AND INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount in writing to Buyer and Seller on the day it is received.

**3.2 Investment.** Unless Buyer and Seller otherwise direct by Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in an interest-bearing deposit account at the Escrow Agent that is insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss of principal or earnings resulting from investments made in accordance with this Section 3.2, or from any liquidation of an investment needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement.

**3.3 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds. They shall be disbursed with the Escrow Funds to which they relate, in proportion to the principal amounts disbursed.

**3.4 No Encumbrance.** Neither Buyer nor Seller may sell, assign, pledge or otherwise encumber any interest in the Escrow Funds before their release under this Agreement, except by operation of Law.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Scheduled Release.** Within five (5) Business Days after the date that is nine (9) months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller all Escrow Funds then remaining in the Escrow Account, less the aggregate Claimed Amounts of all Claim Notices delivered before the Release Date that are pending and unresolved on the Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall retain the amount so withheld in the Escrow Account until each such Claim Notice is resolved under Section 5. Upon resolution, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the related amount in accordance with Section 5, and shall promptly release to Seller any portion of that amount not payable to Buyer.

**4.2 Release by Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**4.3 Release Under Final Order.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Final Order delivered to it by Buyer or Seller. The delivery shall be accompanied by a certificate of the delivering party's counsel stating that the order or judgment is a Final Order. The Escrow Agent may conclusively rely on that certificate.

**4.4 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient. The Escrow Agent shall confirm any wire instructions by telephone call-back to a person listed on the recipient's certificate of authorized representatives delivered under Section 7.5.

## 5. CLAIMS AGAINST ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Claim Notice.** Buyer may assert a claim for recovery from the Escrow Funds under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement by delivering a written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent (a "**Claim Notice**"). The Claim Notice shall describe in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which it arises and the Claimed Amount.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a Claim Notice by delivering a written notice to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). The Objection Notice shall state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the portion of the Claimed Amount disputed.

**5.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the period in Section 5.2, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the full Claimed Amount to Buyer on the fifth (5th) Business Day after that period expires. If Seller delivers an Objection Notice disputing only part of the Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed portion to Buyer within five (5) Business Days after receiving the Objection Notice.

**5.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall retain the disputed portion of any Claimed Amount in the Escrow Account. It shall disburse that amount only in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days after delivery of an Objection Notice to resolve the disputed claim. If they resolve it, they shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions reflecting the resolution.

**5.5 Unliquidated Claims.** If a Claimed Amount was stated as an estimate, Buyer may deliver an updated Claim Notice revising the estimate as the Losses become known. Any increase in the Claimed Amount is subject to Seller's right of objection under Section 5.2, which runs from the Escrow Agent's receipt of the updated Claim Notice.

**5.6 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay all Claimed Amounts that become payable, the Escrow Agent shall pay those amounts in the order in which the related Claim Notices were received.

## 6. ESCROW AGENT PROVISIONS

**6.1 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate, order or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper person. It has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

**6.2 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.3 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice. It is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**6.4 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain as to its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may file an action in interpleader in a court of competent jurisdiction and deposit the Escrow Funds with that court, after which it is discharged from further obligations with respect to the Escrow Funds so deposited.

**6.5 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign on thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. Before a resignation or removal takes effect, Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Funds to the successor. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint one. The Escrow Agent's only duty until a successor is appointed is to hold the Escrow Funds and act on Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**6.6 Compliance with Orders.** The Escrow Agent may comply with any writ, order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction affecting the Escrow Funds. It is not liable to any party for doing so, even if that order is later reversed or vacated.

## 7. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT; IDENTIFICATION

**7.1 Fees.** Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the Escrow Agent's fees set forth in Schedule 1, together with its reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds.

**7.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to any such amounts caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. As between Buyer and Seller, each bears one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.2. However, a party whose breach of this Agreement gave rise to the indemnified amount bears all of it.

**7.3 Survival.** This Section 7 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**7.4 Know Your Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests to comply with applicable anti-money laundering and customer identification Laws.

**7.5 Authorized Representatives.** On the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a certificate in the form of Schedule 2. Each certificate shall identify the persons authorized to give instructions on that party's behalf and confirm wire instructions, with their specimen signatures and telephone numbers. A party may update its certificate by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. All earnings on the Escrow Funds are reported as income of Buyer until disbursement, to the extent required by Law.

**8.2 Tax Forms.** On or before the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9. The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required of it with respect to the Escrow Funds.

**8.3 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall distribute to Buyer from the Escrow Funds an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings on the Escrow Funds for that quarter. The distribution shall be made upon Buyer's written request.

## 9. TERMINATION

This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with this Agreement. Sections 6.2, 7 and 10 survive termination.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the addresses set forth on the signature pages. A party may designate another address by notice. A notice to the Escrow Agent is effective only upon actual receipt.

**10.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims and disputes arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Funds, and waives any objection to venue in that court.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**10.5 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any permitted assignee of its rights under the Purchase Agreement, on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. Any corporation or association into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**10.6 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**10.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

**10.8 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include acts of God, war, terrorism, failure of communications or utilities, and interruptions of the Federal Reserve wire system.

**10.9 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
