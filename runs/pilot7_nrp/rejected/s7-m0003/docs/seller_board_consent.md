# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF BRUVEIND AERIAL GROUP, LLC

**Dated as of March 1, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the board of managers (the "**Board**") of Bruveind Aerial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act under the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and the limited liability company agreement of the Company, as amended (the "**LLC Agreement**"). The undersigned hereby waive all notice and consent to and adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called and held meeting of the Board.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Company owns beneficially and of record all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Sowfield Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Sowfield**").

1.2 Sowfield designs, manufactures and sells precision-agriculture spraying drones and multispectral imaging drones and operates a crop-analytics software platform.

1.3 The Board has been presented with a substantially final form of a Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") among Drebuv Agritech Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), the Company, as seller, and, for the limited purposes stated in it, Sowfield. Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company would sell all of the Shares to Buyer (the "**Transaction**").

1.4 The Board has also been presented with substantially final forms of the following agreements, each to be entered into at the closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**"):

(a) an indemnity escrow agreement among Buyer, the Company and Calloway Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**") (the "**Escrow Agreement**"); and

(b) a transition services agreement between the Company, as service provider, and Buyer, as service recipient (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"). Under the Transition Services Agreement, the Company will continue to host Sowfield's agronomic, imagery and flight-log data on the Company's cloud platform after the Closing.

1.5 The Board has reviewed the Purchase Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement (together, the "**Transaction Documents**"). The Board has consulted with the Company's officers and with its legal and financial advisors. On that basis, the Board has determined that the Transaction is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members.

## 2. Approval of the Purchase Agreement and the Sale of the Shares

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, and the sale of all of the Shares to Buyer on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement, are hereby authorized, approved and adopted in all respects;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board approves the sale of the Shares for an aggregate purchase price of $148,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to the working capital adjustment provided in the Purchase Agreement; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board approves the deposit by Buyer at the Closing of $14,850,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for the Company's indemnification obligations under the Purchase Agreement.

## 3. Approval of the Escrow Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Escrow Agreement are hereby authorized and approved in all respects. The Company's entry into the Escrow Agreement at the Closing, and its performance of its obligations under it, are hereby authorized and approved.

## 4. Approval of the Transition Services Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Transition Services Agreement are hereby authorized and approved in all respects. The Board authorizes and approves the Company's entry into the Transition Services Agreement at the Closing and its provision to Buyer and Sowfield of the transition services described in it, for a term of 12 months after the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement). Those services include the continued hosting of Sowfield's data on the Company's cloud platform.

## 5. Non-Competition and Confidentiality Covenants

**RESOLVED**, that the Board acknowledges and approves the covenants of the Company in the Purchase Agreement concerning non-competition, non-solicitation and confidentiality. The Board directs the officers of the Company to take such actions as may be reasonably necessary to cause the Company and its Affiliates (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) to comply with those covenants after the Closing.

## 6. Authorized Officers

**RESOLVED**, that each of the President, the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Secretary of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is hereby authorized, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Company:

(a) to execute and deliver each Transaction Document, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company, such approval to be conclusively evidenced by that execution and delivery;

(b) to execute and deliver all certificates, stock powers, instruments, notices and other documents contemplated by the Transaction Documents, including the secretary's certificate and the IRS Form W-9 to be delivered at the Closing; and

(c) to take all other actions, make all filings and pay all fees and expenses as such Authorized Officer deems necessary or appropriate to consummate the Transaction and to carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

## 7. Secretary's Certificate

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary of the Company is hereby authorized and directed to certify these resolutions and deliver that certification to Buyer at the Closing.

## 8. Ratification

**RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any manager, officer, employee or agent of the Company in connection with the Transaction, the negotiation of the Transaction Documents and the matters contemplated by these resolutions, including the entry into the mutual nondisclosure agreement with Buyer, are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 9. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to do all such further acts and things, and to execute and deliver all such further documents, as any of them deems necessary or desirable to give full effect to the foregoing resolutions.

## 10. Counterparts; Effectiveness

This written consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or PDF delivered by email. All counterparts together constitute one consent. This consent is effective as of the date first written above and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
