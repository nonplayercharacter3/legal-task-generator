# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Effective Date (as defined below) by and between Bruveind Aerial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and Drebuv Agritech Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, Recipient and Sowfield Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring from Provider all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Before the Closing, the Company relied on Provider's cloud hosting infrastructure and related services to store, process and serve agronomic, imagery and flight-log data generated by Company drones and the Company's crop-analytics platform.

C. Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Provider and Recipient to enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that Provider will provide certain transitional services to Recipient and the Company, including continued hosting of data on Provider's cloud platform, while Recipient migrates the Business to its own systems.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Data**" means all data, files and records uploaded by Recipient to the Provider Platform after the Effective Date, including any agronomic, imagery and flight-log data so uploaded.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 8.1.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Migration Plan**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Provider Platform**" means the cloud computing, storage and data-processing environment owned, operated or controlled by Provider or its Affiliates, including the storage buckets, compute instances, databases and application interfaces used before the Closing to support the Business.

"**Recipient Group**" means Recipient, the Company and their respective Subsidiaries.

"**Security Incident**" means any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction of, data hosted on the Provider Platform for the Recipient Group.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Service Schedule**" means Schedule A to this Agreement.

"**Subsidiary**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person of which that Person owns, directly or indirectly, more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Transition Services**" means the services described in the Service Schedule, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 3.4.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, *mutatis mutandis*. References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 10.

**2.2 Service Periods.** Each Transition Service shall be provided for the period stated for it in the Service Schedule. If no period is stated, it shall be provided for the full Term. No service period may extend beyond the Term.

## 3. TRANSITION SERVICES

**3.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Transition Services to the Recipient Group in accordance with this Agreement and the Service Schedule.

**3.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Transition Services (a) in a professional and workmanlike manner, (b) in substantially the same manner, scope, priority and level of care as those services were provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and (c) in compliance with all applicable Laws. Provider shall meet the service levels set forth in the Service Schedule.

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign appropriately qualified personnel to perform the Transition Services. Each party shall designate a service coordinator to serve as its primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Each coordinator is identified in the Service Schedule and may be replaced by written notice to the other party.

**3.4 Additional Services.** If Recipient identifies a service that Provider provided to the Company in the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date and that is not listed in the Service Schedule, Recipient may request it in writing. Provider shall negotiate in good faith to provide it on terms consistent with this Agreement (an "**Additional Service**"). Each Additional Service agreed in writing becomes part of the Service Schedule.

**3.5 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any consents of third-party vendors or licensors required for it to provide the Transition Services. The reasonable out-of-pocket costs of obtaining those consents shall be shared equally by the parties.

## 4. MIGRATION

**4.1 Migration Plan.** Within forty-five (45) days after the Effective Date, the parties shall agree on a written plan for the migration of the Business off the Provider Platform and onto systems selected by Recipient (the "**Migration Plan**"). Provider shall cooperate in good faith in developing and implementing the Migration Plan.

**4.2 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall provide reasonable migration assistance as described in the Service Schedule. That assistance includes making data available in the formats specified in the Service Schedule and providing reasonable access to Provider's knowledgeable personnel. Migration assistance beyond the hours stated in the Service Schedule is billed at the rates set forth in it.

**4.3 Early Termination of Individual Services.** Recipient may terminate any individual Transition Service on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Upon termination of a Transition Service, the Service Fees for that service cease to accrue from the effective date of termination.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the fees for each Transition Service set forth in the Service Schedule (the "**Service Fees**"). The Service Fees are intended to reflect Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the Transition Services, without mark-up.

**5.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs paid to third parties solely for the provision of the Transition Services. Reimbursement requires that the costs are identified in the Service Schedule or approved in advance in writing by Recipient.

**5.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Recipient may withhold any amount disputed in good faith, provided that it notifies Provider of the dispute in writing with reasonable detail before the payment due date. The parties' service coordinators shall work to resolve any such dispute promptly.

**5.4 Taxes.** Service Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes. Recipient is responsible for any such taxes imposed on the Transition Services, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 6. DATA SECURITY

**6.1 Security Program.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for the Provider Platform that are no less protective than (a) those in place for the Company as of the Closing Date and (b) those required by applicable Law and industry-standard practices for cloud hosting of commercial data. Those safeguards include encryption of data at rest and in transit, role-based access controls and logging of administrative access.

**6.2 Segregation and Access.** Provider shall logically segregate data hosted for the Recipient Group from Provider's own data and the data of its other customers. Provider shall restrict access to that data to personnel with a need to know for the purpose of performing the Transition Services.

**6.3 Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Recipient in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. Provider shall take prompt steps to contain and remediate the Security Incident and shall cooperate reasonably with Recipient's investigation of it.

**6.4 Audit.** Once during the Term, and at any time following a Security Incident, Recipient may, on reasonable prior notice and during normal business hours, audit Provider's compliance with this Section 6. Recipient may conduct the audit itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality obligations. Recipient bears the cost of the audit unless it reveals a material non-compliance.

## 7. OWNERSHIP OF DATA; RETURN AND DELETION

**7.1 Ownership of Company Data.** As between the parties, Recipient (or the applicable member of the Recipient Group) owns all right, title and interest in and to the Company Data. Provider acquires no rights in the Company Data other than a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license, during the Term, to host, store, process and transmit the Company Data solely to provide the Transition Services. Upon the expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall (a) return all Company Data to Recipient in the format specified in the Service Schedule, and (b) within thirty (30) days after Recipient confirms receipt, permanently delete all Company Data from the Provider Platform and all backup systems. Provider shall certify that deletion in writing signed by an officer of Provider.

**7.2 Provider Materials.** As between the parties, Provider retains all right, title and interest in the Provider Platform and in any tools, software and methodologies used by Provider to perform the Transition Services. Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any right in those materials except as necessary to receive the Transition Services.

**7.3 Use Restrictions.** Provider shall not use Company Data for any purpose other than performing the Transition Services. In particular, Provider shall not use Company Data to train or improve any model, product or service of Provider or any third party.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other party (the "**Receiving Party**") in connection with this Agreement, whether before or after the Effective Date, that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Confidential Information of Recipient includes all data hosted on the Provider Platform for the Recipient Group.

**8.2 Obligations.** The Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement, (b) not disclose it to any third party other than its and its Affiliates' employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it for those purposes and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 8, and (c) protect it using at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care.

**8.3 Exceptions.** The obligations in Section 8.2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party, (b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before receipt from the Disclosing Party, (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (d) is independently developed without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. A Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law, provided that it gives the Disclosing Party prompt notice, where legally permitted, and cooperates with any effort to obtain a protective order.

**8.4 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Section 8 is in addition to, and does not limit, Provider's obligations under Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**9.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive or exemplary damages, or for lost profits, even if advised of the possibility of those damages.

**9.2 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 9.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement.

**9.3 Exceptions.** Sections 9.1 and 9.2 do not apply to (a) a party's breach of Section 6, Section 7 or Section 8, (b) a party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, or (c) Recipient's obligation to pay Service Fees.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination for Convenience by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate this Agreement in its entirety on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider.

**10.2 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail. Provider may not suspend or terminate any Transition Service involving hosting of data for the Recipient Group for non-payment while the amount in question is subject to a good-faith dispute under Section 5.3.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, (a) Provider shall cease providing the Transition Services, except for the return and deletion obligations in Section 7.1, (b) Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and (c) Sections 1, 5 (for accrued amounts), 7, 8, 9, 10.3, 11 and 12 survive.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**11.1 Escalation.** Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be referred to the parties' service coordinators. If they do not resolve it within ten (10) Business Days, either party may escalate it by written notice to a senior executive of each party. If those executives do not resolve it within a further ten (10) Business Days, either party may pursue the remedies available to it under Section 12.6.

**11.2 Continued Performance.** During any dispute, each party shall continue to perform its obligations under this Agreement, including Provider's continued hosting of data for the Recipient Group.

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**12.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider performs the Transition Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between the parties.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**12.3 Assignment.** Recipient may not assign or delegate this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Provider. Provider may assign or delegate this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the consent of Recipient, to any Affiliate or to any successor to Provider's cloud platform business. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 12.3 is void.

**12.4 Subcontracting.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform the Transition Services, provided that Provider remains responsible for their performance as if it were its own.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement, including the Service Schedule, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**12.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of state or federal courts in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**12.7 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**12.8 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that Provider's failure to perform its obligations under Sections 6, 7 and 8 would cause irreparable harm to the Recipient Group. Recipient is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief to enforce those obligations, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**12.9 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**12.10 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.

## SCHEDULE A — SERVICE SCHEDULE

**A.1 Data Hosting Services.** Provider shall continue to host on the Provider Platform the Company's agronomic, multispectral imagery and flight-log archive as it exists on the Provider Platform on the Effective Date. That archive includes the historical field datasets collected by Company drones before the Closing. Provider shall also host data uploaded by the Recipient Group during the Term. Provider shall maintain storage capacity, redundancy and backup at levels no less than those in place on the Closing Date. *Service period:* full Term. *Service level:* 99.5% monthly availability, with daily incremental backups and weekly full backups retained for thirty (30) days.

**A.2 Analytics Platform Support.** Provider shall continue to operate the compute instances, databases and application interfaces that support the Company's crop-analytics platform and customer-facing dashboards. *Service period:* full Term. *Service level:* 99.5% monthly availability; Severity 1 incidents acknowledged within one (1) hour.

**A.3 Flight-Log Ingestion.** Provider shall maintain the ingestion pipeline through which Company drones in the field transmit flight-log and telemetry data to the Provider Platform. *Service period:* full Term or until cut-over under the Migration Plan, whichever is earlier.

**A.4 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall provide up to 400 hours of engineering support for implementation of the Migration Plan. Additional hours are billed at $210 per hour. Provider shall make data available for export in its native formats and in industry-standard formats (GeoTIFF for imagery, CSV or Parquet for tabular and flight-log data) on Recipient's request.

**A.5 Service Fees.** $96,000 per month for Services A.1 through A.3 combined, plus pass-through third-party storage and egress costs at Provider's actual cost. Migration assistance under A.4 within the included hours is provided at no additional charge.

**A.6 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall designate its service coordinator by written notice within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date.
