# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 14, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between Drebuv Agritech Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Drebuv**"), and Bruveind Aerial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Bruveind**"). Each of Drebuv and Bruveind is a "**Party**" and together they are the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Bruveind owns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Sowfield Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Sowfield**"). Sowfield designs, manufactures and sells precision-agriculture spraying drones and multispectral imaging drones and operates a crop-analytics software platform.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Drebuv would acquire Sowfield (the "**Transaction**"). In doing so, each Party expects to disclose Confidential Information to the other Party.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, that a Disclosing Party or any of its Representatives discloses or makes available to the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Purpose. Confidential Information includes:

(a) business plans, financial statements, projections, customer and supplier lists, pricing and contract terms;

(b) technical information concerning Sowfield's drones, sensors, flight-control firmware, imaging pipelines and crop-analytics software, including source code, designs, specifications and test results;

(c) agronomic, imagery and flight-log data collected by Sowfield drones, and any information concerning the systems on which that data is stored;

(d) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**"); and

(e) the fact that the Parties are discussing the Transaction and the status and terms of those discussions.

"**Disclosing Party**" means a Party that discloses Confidential Information, either directly or through its Representatives. Confidential Information concerning Sowfield is treated as Confidential Information of Bruveind.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Purpose**" means the evaluation, negotiation and, if applicable, consummation of the Transaction.

"**Receiving Party**" means a Party that receives Confidential Information, either directly or through its Representatives.

"**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their respective directors, managers, officers, employees, agents, financing sources, attorneys, accountants, consultants and financial advisors.

## 2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

**2.1 Use and Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall:

(a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;

(b) not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any Person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose, who have been informed of its confidential nature and who are bound by duties of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement; and

(c) protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.2 Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives. For this purpose, any act or omission of a Representative that would breach this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party is treated as a breach by the Receiving Party.

**2.3 Data Room and Technical Data.** Drebuv shall access source code, flight-log data and imagery datasets of Sowfield only through the virtual data room or clean-room environment designated by Bruveind, and only through Representatives that Bruveind has approved in writing, with that approval not to be unreasonably withheld. Drebuv shall not copy, download or reverse engineer any such materials except as permitted by the terms of the designated environment.

**2.4 Personal Information.** If any Confidential Information contains personal information about growers, customers, employees or other individuals, the Receiving Party shall handle that information in compliance with all applicable data protection and privacy laws.

## 3. EXCEPTIONS

**3.1 Excluded Information.** The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was rightfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives without restriction on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, provided that the source is not known by the Receiving Party, after reasonable inquiry, to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**3.2 Legally Required Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule or legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice. That notice is intended to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and the Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts at the Disclosing Party's expense. If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to the information disclosed.

## 4. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

**4.1 Return or Destruction.** Within ten (10) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after either Party notifies the other that it is terminating discussions regarding the Transaction, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession. It shall also destroy all Derivative Materials. Upon request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that return or destruction in writing.

**4.2 Retained Copies.** Despite Section 4.1, the Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information (a) to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policies, or (b) in automatic electronic archives or back-up systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, despite any expiration of this Agreement.

## 5. NON-SOLICITATION

**5.1 Employees.** For a period of twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, Drebuv shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment or hire any officer or engineering employee of Sowfield with whom Drebuv first had contact, or of whom Drebuv first became aware, in connection with the Purpose. This restriction does not prohibit (a) general solicitations, including through search firms or advertisements, that are not specifically directed at Sowfield employees, or (b) hiring any individual who responds to such a general solicitation or whose employment with Sowfield ended at least six (6) months before any discussions with Drebuv began.

**5.2 Effect of Consummation.** Section 5.1 ceases to apply upon the consummation of the Transaction.

## 6. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**6.1 No Warranty.** All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither the Disclosing Party nor any of its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to its accuracy or completeness. Neither the Disclosing Party nor any of its Representatives is liable to the Receiving Party or its Representatives arising from the use of the Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a definitive written agreement regarding the Transaction.

**6.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Neither Party is under any obligation to enter into the Transaction unless and until a definitive written agreement regarding it has been executed and delivered. Either Party may terminate discussions regarding the Transaction at any time, for any reason or no reason, on written notice to the other Party.

**6.3 No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any Confidential Information or any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 7. TERM

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement remains in effect until the second (2nd) anniversary of the Effective Date. The obligations of the Parties with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, including source code and flight-control firmware, continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

**7.2 Survival After Termination of Discussions.** The termination of discussions regarding the Transaction, or of any definitive agreement regarding the Transaction before its consummation, does not terminate this Agreement.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. Accordingly, the non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**8.2 Costs of Enforcement.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable judgment that a Party or its Representatives has breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable and documented attorneys' fees and costs incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one (1) business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the addresses set forth on the signature page. A Party may designate another address by notice.

**9.2 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

**9.3 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that a Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business on written notice. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 9.3 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

**9.4 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of state or federal courts in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**9.5 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. The invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**9.7 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
