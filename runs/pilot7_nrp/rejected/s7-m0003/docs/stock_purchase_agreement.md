# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and among Drebuv Agritech Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Bruveind Aerial Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Article V, Sowfield Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns beneficially and of record all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures and sells precision-agriculture spraying drones and multispectral imaging drones and operates a crop-analytics software platform (collectively, the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

D. The board of managers of Seller has approved this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

"**Company Data**" means all agronomic, imagery and flight-log data collected by Company drones, whenever collected.

"**Company Intellectual Property**" means all Intellectual Property owned or purported to be owned by the Company, including the Company's flight-control firmware, imaging pipelines and crop-analytics software.

"**Confidentiality Agreement**" means the mutual nondisclosure agreement between Buyer and Seller entered into in connection with the evaluation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Calloway Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 2.4.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount as deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any earnings thereon, as reduced by disbursements made in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1 (Organization and Authority), 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and 4.11 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any agency, court, tribunal or regulatory body thereof, including the Federal Aviation Administration.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, software, data and other intellectual property rights of any kind throughout the world.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

"**Transition Services**" has the meaning given in the Transition Services Agreement.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the transition services agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Seller, as service provider, and Buyer, as service recipient, under which Seller will continue to host Company Data on Seller's cloud platform.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $148,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, for deposit in accordance with Section 2.4 and the Escrow Agreement;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Closing Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses set forth in the payoff letters and invoices delivered under Section 3.2(e); and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b), plus or minus the estimated adjustment under Section 2.5(a).

**2.3 Withholding.** Buyer is entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated for all purposes as having been paid to the Person in respect of whom the withholding was made.

**2.4 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $14,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the Escrow Agreement as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX. The Escrow Funds shall be released to Seller or to Buyer in accordance with the terms of the Escrow Agreement.

**2.5 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting detail. If the estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital set forth in Schedule 2.5, the amount payable under Section 2.2(c) is increased by the excess; if it is less, that amount is reduced by the shortfall.

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of the final Closing Working Capital. Seller has thirty (30) days to deliver written objections. Any disputed items not resolved by the parties within thirty (30) days after delivery of an objection notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller, whose determination is final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) Any amount owed by one party to the other as a result of the final determination shall be paid within five (5) Business Days by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third (3rd) Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions set forth in Article VII (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as Buyer and Seller agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank, or stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) a certificate of the secretary of Seller attaching the resolutions of Seller's board of managers authorizing this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents;

(e) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness and final invoices for all Transaction Expenses;

(f) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; and

(g) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2 and counterparts of the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Authority.** Seller and the Company are each duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Seller has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly executed by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require the consent of any Person under, or result in a breach of, any Material Contract, except as set forth in Section 4.2 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The Shares constitute all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the fiscal year then ended, copies of which have been delivered to Buyer, have been prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.5 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.6 Material Contracts.** Section 4.6 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party involving annual payments in excess of $500,000, each distribution or reseller agreement, each supply agreement for airframes, sensors, batteries or flight controllers, and each license of Intellectual Property (other than off-the-shelf software) (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**4.7 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all Intellectual Property used in the Business. The Company Intellectual Property does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person. All Persons who contributed to the development of the Company's flight-control firmware and crop-analytics software have assigned their rights in that work to the Company.

**4.8 Company Data and Privacy.** The Company owns all right, title and interest in the Company Data. The Company has complied in all material respects with all applicable Laws and contractual obligations relating to the collection, storage and use of Company Data and any personal information contained in it. Since January 1, 2023, there has been no unauthorized access to or loss of Company Data. Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule identifies each system, including any system owned or operated by Seller or its Affiliates, on which Company Data is stored.

**4.9 Products; Product Warranty.** Each drone, sensor and software product sold or licensed by the Company has conformed in all material respects to its specifications and applicable warranties. Section 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all product recalls, airframe failures, firmware defects and warranty claims in excess of $50,000 since January 1, 2023.

**4.10 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws, including the regulations of the Federal Aviation Administration governing unmanned aircraft systems and Laws governing the application of agricultural chemicals. The Company holds all permits, waivers and certificates required to conduct the Business as currently conducted.

**4.11 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement and the other Transaction Documents to which it is a party.

**5.2 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Buyer of this Agreement do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.3 Investment Purpose.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price and all other amounts payable by it under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business relationships, Company Intellectual Property and Company Data. Without Buyer's prior written consent, Seller shall not permit the Company to issue equity, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, sell or license material assets, or transfer any Company Data to any Person other than in the ordinary course.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Company. Information obtained remains subject to the Confidentiality Agreement.

**6.3 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions to Closing and to make any filings required by any Governmental Authority in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**6.4 Transition Services.** At the Closing, Seller and Buyer shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide to Buyer and the Company the Transition Services, including the continued hosting of Company Data on Seller's cloud platform, for a term of 12 months after the Closing Date. Seller and Buyer may enter into such additional Transition Documents as they agree are reasonably necessary to separate the Business from Seller's other operations.

**6.5 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the design, manufacture or sale of agricultural spraying or imaging drones or agronomic analytics services, or solicit for employment any employee of the Company, except through general solicitations not targeted at Company employees.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business, including Company Data, except as required by Law or as necessary to perform the Transition Services.

**6.7 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Both Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects); (b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; (c) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred; and (d) Seller has delivered each item listed in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and (c) Buyer has delivered each item listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller; (b) by either Buyer or Seller if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close; or (c) by either party if the other party has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement becomes void without liability of any party, except that Sections 6.6, Article XI and the Confidentiality Agreement survive, and no termination relieves a party of liability for willful breach.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer contained in this Agreement (other than the Fundamental Representations) survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed in accordance with their terms. Any claim asserted in a written notice delivered in good faith with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates and representatives (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Closing Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or before the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and representatives (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) the operation of the Business following the Closing, including all Losses arising under or in connection with any Transition Document.

**9.4 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing the claim in reasonable detail and, to the extent known, the estimated amount of Losses. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced.

(b) For any claim brought by a third party, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by notice within twenty (20) days after receiving the claim notice. The Indemnified Party may participate with its own counsel at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money damages fully indemnified under this Article IX and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

(c) Claims by a Buyer Indemnitee for recovery from the Escrow Funds shall be asserted by delivery of a claim notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations or for fraud) shall not exceed the Escrow Amount (the "**Cap**").

(b) Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) (other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations or for fraud) unless and until the aggregate Losses of the Buyer Indemnitees exceed $1,113,750 (the "**Deductible**"), in which case Seller is liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(c) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer is liable under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Cap, and Buyer is not liable under Section 9.3(a) unless and until the aggregate Losses of the Seller Indemnitees exceed the Deductible.

(d) Claims under Section 9.3(c) for Losses arising from the operation of the Business following the Closing are not subject to any of the limitations in this Section 9.5 and are recoverable in full, without regard to the Cap or the Deductible.

(e) For purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), each representation and warranty shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier.

(f) No party is liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, recovery from the Escrow Funds in accordance with the Escrow Agreement is the sole and exclusive source of recovery of the Buyer Indemnitees for any claim under Section 9.2(a), other than claims for breaches of Fundamental Representations or for fraud. Except for claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the purchase price adjustment procedures in Section 2.5, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to any matter arising out of this Agreement.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller is responsible for and shall pay all taxes of the Company attributable to any taxable period, or portion of a period, ending on or before the Closing Date.

**10.2 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the preparation and filing of tax returns and any audit or proceeding relating to taxes of the Company.

**10.3 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and registration taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party bears its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given when delivered personally, when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, or one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, in each case to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or, after the Closing, to any acquirer of all or substantially all of the Business, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**11.5 Successors; No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any right on any other Person.

**11.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

**11.7 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**11.8 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement, and all claims and disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any other Transaction Document, are governed by the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of state or federal courts in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to the Transaction Documents, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**11.9 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS IT CONTEMPLATES.

**11.10 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any other Transaction Document, this Agreement controls, except that the Transition Services Agreement controls with respect to the Transition Services and the Escrow Agreement controls with respect to escrow release mechanics.

**11.11 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
