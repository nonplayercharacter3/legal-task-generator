## 8. SCHEDULE 4.9: LITIGATION

**8.1** *Halvorsen, et al. v. Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.*, Superior Court of Arizona, Maricopa County, Case No. CV2025-017346. This putative class action was filed on November 7, 2025 on behalf of Arizona homeowners whose residential solar systems were installed by Seller between January 1, 2019 and the present. Plaintiffs allege the following:

(a) breach of the 10-year roof-penetration warranty;

(b) negligence in flashing and sealing roof penetrations, resulting in roof leaks and interior water damage; and

(c) violation of the Arizona Consumer Fraud Act.

Plaintiffs seek damages, repair costs, restitution and attorneys' fees. Seller has answered and denies liability. Plaintiffs' motion for class certification is pending.

**8.2** Customer complaint filed with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, Complaint No. 25-07719, received March 12, 2026. The complaint alleges workmanship defects in a residential installation in Mesa, Arizona. Seller has completed a corrective repair and has requested that the Registrar close the complaint.

**8.3** Small claims action, *Pruitt v. Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.*, Maricopa County Justice Courts, filed July 22, 2026. The action seeks $3,850 for alleged delay in obtaining permission to operate for a residential system.
