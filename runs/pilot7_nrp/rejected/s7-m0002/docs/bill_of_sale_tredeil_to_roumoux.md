# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made, executed and delivered as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**") by **Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.**, an Arizona corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Roumoux Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to that certain Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Purchased Assets to Buyer, and Buyer agreed to purchase the Purchased Assets from Seller.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale to Buyer at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. These terms include "Purchased Assets," "Excluded Assets," "Assumed Liabilities," "Excluded Liabilities," "Inventory," "Fleet and Equipment," "Assigned Contracts," "Backlog Contracts," "Trade Names," "Warranty Book," "Encumbrance" and "Closing."

## 2. CONVEYANCE OF PURCHASED ASSETS

**2.1 Conveyance.** Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns forever, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets. The conveyance is made under the Purchase Agreement, effective as of 12:01 a.m. Arizona time on the Closing Date. It is free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those set forth on Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule. The Purchased Assets include the following:

(a) the Inventory, including all solar modules, inverters, racking, batteries, balance-of-system components, wire and supplies, wherever located;

(b) the Fleet and Equipment, including each motor vehicle, trailer, lift, tool and item of equipment listed on Schedule 2.1(b) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(c) the Assigned Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Backlog Contracts. The Assigned Contracts are assigned to Buyer by the assignment and assumption agreement delivered under Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement;

(d) the Trade Names, together with the goodwill of the Business associated with them;

(e) the Warranty Book, including all customer warranty records, monitoring accounts, service histories and customer lists relating to systems installed by Seller;

(f) all permits and licenses held by Seller for the operation of the Business, to the extent transferable under applicable law;

(g) all prepaid expenses, deposits and advance payments received from customers under the Backlog Contracts; and

(h) all books, records, files and data relating to any of the foregoing.

**2.2 Titled Vehicles.** For each titled vehicle included in the Fleet and Equipment, Seller has delivered to Buyer the certificate of title, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer, in accordance with Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement. Seller shall execute any further instruments the Arizona Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division reasonably requires to record Buyer as owner.

## 3. EXCLUDED ASSETS

**3.1 Excluded Assets.** Seller does not sell, assign, transfer, convey or deliver to Buyer, and Buyer does not acquire, any right, title or interest in, to or under the Excluded Assets described in Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Seller retains all of the Excluded Assets.

## 4. LIABILITIES

**4.1 No Assumption by this Instrument.** This Bill of Sale is an instrument of conveyance only. Buyer does not assume any Liability of Seller by this Bill of Sale. Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities is made only under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement and the assignment and assumption agreement delivered under Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Seller retains the Excluded Liabilities, as provided in Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**5.1 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, Seller shall, at Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute, acknowledge and deliver such additional deeds, assignments, certificates of title, consents and other instruments, and take such other actions, as may be reasonably necessary to vest in Buyer all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets. This Section 5.1 is consistent with Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably constitutes and appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in the name of Seller but for the benefit of Buyer, to do any of the following:

(a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets;

(b) institute and prosecute proceedings to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title in or to the Purchased Assets;

(c) defend and compromise any action or proceeding relating to the Purchased Assets; and

(d) endorse checks and other instruments received in payment of, or on account of, the Purchased Assets.

The foregoing powers are coupled with an interest and are irrevocable by Seller.

**5.3 Receipts After Closing.** If Seller receives any payment, remittance, mail or other property that constitutes or relates to a Purchased Asset after the Closing, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer. Seller shall deliver it to Buyer within five (5) business days after receipt.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**6.1 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale or the transfer of the Purchased Assets under it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Arizona, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**6.2 Jurisdiction.** Each of Seller and Buyer, by accepting this Bill of Sale, irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts of the State of Arizona located in Maricopa County for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale. Each of them agrees not to commence any such action or proceeding except in those courts.

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns, and inures to the benefit of Buyer and its successors and assigns.

**7.2 No Merger.** The representations, warranties, covenants and agreements of Seller in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale. They survive its execution and delivery in accordance with the Purchase Agreement.

**7.3 Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation of this Bill of Sale.

**7.4 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, and signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
