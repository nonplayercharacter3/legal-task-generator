# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 14, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.**, an Arizona corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Roumoux Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of designing, selling, installing and servicing residential and small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems in the State of Arizona (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

(b) "**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

(c) "**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in the form agreed by the Parties, to be delivered by Seller to Buyer at Closing under Section 3.2(a).

(d) "**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

(e) "**Earnout Period**" means each of (i) the twelve-month period beginning on the first day of the calendar month following the Closing Date (the "**First Earnout Period**") and (ii) the twelve-month period immediately following the First Earnout Period (the "**Second Earnout Period**").

(f) "**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, easement, option, right of first refusal or other restriction of any kind.

(g) "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, board, registrar, court or tribunal thereof, including the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

(h) "**Installed Megawatts**" means the aggregate nameplate direct-current capacity, expressed in megawatts, of solar photovoltaic systems installed by Buyer or its Affiliates in the State of Arizona during an Earnout Period for which the applicable utility has issued permission to operate.

(i) "**Key Employee Agreement**" means the Key Employee Agreement between Seller and Dana Okafor-Lindqvist, as in effect on the Effective Date.

(j) "**Liabilities**" means liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature whatsoever, asserted or unasserted, known or unknown, absolute or contingent, accrued or unaccrued, matured or unmatured.

(k) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(l) "**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

(m) "**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those set forth on Schedule 4.4, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) all inventory, including solar modules, inverters, racking, batteries, balance-of-system components, wire and supplies, wherever located (the "**Inventory**");

(b) all motor vehicles, trailers, lifts, tools, equipment and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(b) (the "**Fleet and Equipment**");

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c), including (i) all customer installation contracts for systems not yet installed or not yet granted permission to operate as of the Closing (the "**Backlog Contracts**"), (ii) all supplier and distributor agreements so listed, and (iii) the Key Employee Agreement (collectively, the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) the trade names "Tredeil Sunworks" and "Tredeil Solar," all associated logos, domain names, telephone numbers and social media accounts, and the goodwill of the Business associated therewith (the "**Trade Names**");

(e) all customer warranty records, monitoring accounts, service histories and customer lists relating to systems installed by Seller (the "**Warranty Book**");

(f) all permits and licenses held by Seller for the operation of the Business, to the extent transferable under applicable law;

(g) all prepaid expenses, deposits and advance payments received from customers under the Backlog Contracts; and

(h) all books, records, files and data relating to the foregoing.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include the following (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) accounts receivable for systems granted permission to operate before the Closing; (c) Seller's corporate records, minute books and tax returns; (d) any contract not listed on Schedule 2.1(c); (e) Seller's employee benefit plans and the assets thereof; (f) all insurance policies of Seller and rights thereunder; and (g) the rights of Seller under this Agreement and the other documents delivered at Closing.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising and required to be performed after the Closing, other than Liabilities arising out of any breach thereof by Seller before the Closing;

(b) trade accounts payable to third-party vendors for Inventory delivered to Seller within thirty (30) days before the Closing, to the extent set forth on the closing statement; and

(c) Liabilities set forth on Schedule 2.3(c).

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible for any Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including (a) any Liability for Taxes of Seller for any period; (b) any Liability relating to the employment or termination of employment of any employee of Seller arising before the Closing; (c) any indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller; (d) any Liability relating to the Excluded Assets; and (e) any Liability of Seller under this Agreement.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $18,400,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), plus the Earnout Payments, if any, payable under Section 2.6, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Base Purchase Price to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) business days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Earnout.**

(a) *Earnout Payments.* As additional consideration, Buyer shall pay to Seller the following amounts (each, an "**Earnout Payment**"), subject to a maximum of $3,000,000 in aggregate for all Earnout Periods:

(i) for each Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts equal or exceed 18.0 megawatts, $1,500,000; and

(ii) for each Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts exceed 12.0 megawatts but are less than 18.0 megawatts, an amount equal to $1,500,000 multiplied by a fraction, the numerator of which is Installed Megawatts for such Earnout Period minus 12.0, and the denominator of which is 6.0.

No Earnout Payment is payable for an Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts are 12.0 megawatts or less.

(b) *Earnout Statement.* Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Earnout Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written statement setting forth Installed Megawatts for such Earnout Period and its calculation of the Earnout Payment, if any (the "**Earnout Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* Seller may object to an Earnout Statement by written notice delivered within thirty (30) days after receipt. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any objection. Any objection not resolved within thirty (30) days after delivery of the notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the Parties, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of such firm shall be borne by the Parties in inverse proportion to the relative amounts of the disputed items determined in their favor.

(d) *Information Rights.* During each Earnout Period and until the final determination of the Earnout Payment for the Second Earnout Period, Buyer shall provide Seller or its permitted assignee, within twenty (20) days after the end of each calendar quarter, reports of installation volumes by system and permission-to-operate date, and shall afford Seller or its permitted assignee reasonable access during normal business hours to Buyer's customer pipeline reports, pricing and proposal data, and books and records relating to Installed Megawatts, in each case to permit verification of Installed Megawatts.

(e) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay each Earnout Payment within ten (10) business days after final determination thereof under this Section 2.6.

**2.7 Allocation.** The Base Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the allocation schedule agreed by the Parties within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on October 1, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be effective as of 12:01 a.m. Arizona time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities, duly executed by Seller;

(c) certificates of title for each titled vehicle included in the Fleet and Equipment, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer;

(d) an assignment of the Trade Names, duly executed by Seller;

(e) the consents listed on Schedule 4.6;

(f) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors and shareholders approving this Agreement; and

(g) a certificate described in Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the Base Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.5; (b) the assignment and assumption agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the certificate described in Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the Effective Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Arizona and has full corporate power and authority to own the Purchased Assets and to carry on the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder. This Agreement has been duly authorized by all requisite corporate and shareholder action of Seller and constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's articles of incorporation or bylaws, (b) violate any law or order applicable to Seller, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.6, require the consent of any Person under, or conflict with or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances except those set forth on Schedule 4.4, each of which will be released at or before Closing.

**4.5 Condition of Assets.** The Fleet and Equipment is in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The Inventory consists of a quality and quantity usable in the ordinary course of the Business, and no Inventory is obsolete, damaged or defective except as reserved on Seller's financial statements.

**4.6 Required Consents.** Schedule 4.6 sets forth each consent, approval, waiver or notice of or to any Person required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller, including the assignment to Buyer of each Assigned Contract (the "**Required Consents**").

**4.7 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, the counterparty thereto, and is in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.8 Licenses.** Seller holds all contractor licenses issued by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors necessary to conduct the Business, each of which is listed on Schedule 4.8 together with its qualifying party. Each such license is in good standing.

**4.9 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.9, there is no action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.10 Warranty Obligations.** Schedule 4.10 sets forth the standard forms of workmanship, roof-penetration and product warranties provided by Seller to its customers. Seller has made available to Buyer the Warranty Book, which is accurate and complete in all material respects.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws, including laws relating to contractor licensing, consumer protection and solar energy device disclosures.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the Effective Date and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware, and has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement has been duly authorized by all requisite limited liability company action of Buyer and constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand or available committed financing to pay the Base Purchase Price at Closing.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each Required Consent before the Closing.

**6.4 Contractor License Transition.** Seller shall cooperate with Buyer, before and for one hundred eighty (180) days after the Closing, in Buyer's application for contractor licenses from the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, including by making available the qualifying party listed on Schedule 4.8.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to any employee of Seller. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts an offer from Buyer effective as of the Closing and shall pay all wages, accrued vacation and other compensation owed to such employees through the Closing Date.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, except to the extent such information becomes publicly available other than through Seller's breach of this Section 6.6.

**6.7 Non-Competition.** For a period of three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the sale or installation of residential or small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems in the State of Arizona.

**6.8 Name Change.** Within ten (10) business days after the Closing, Seller shall amend its articles of incorporation to change its corporate name to a name that does not include "Tredeil" or any confusingly similar name.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Both Parties.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any law or order restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) each consent listed on Schedule 4.6 shall have been obtained, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, and shall be in full force and effect;

(b) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, and Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate, signed by an executive officer of Seller, certifying that the conditions in Section 7.2(a) and Section 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets, other than those Buyer has agreed in writing to accept, shall have been released; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and (c) Seller shall have received a certificate, signed by an authorized officer of Buyer, to that effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.4 and 4.12 (the "**Fundamental Representations**") survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants of the Parties survive in accordance with their terms.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, managers, members and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability or Excluded Asset.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, shareholders and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**8.4 Procedures.** A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice to the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") describing the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually prejudiced thereby. The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, and the Indemnified Party may participate at its own expense. No third-party claim may be settled without the consent of the Indemnified Party, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**8.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap and Basket.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which an Indemnifying Party is liable under Section 8.2(a) or Section 8.3(a), as applicable, shall not exceed 15% of the Base Purchase Price ($2,760,000) (the "**Cap**"). An Indemnifying Party shall not be liable under Section 8.2(a) or Section 8.3(a) until the aggregate amount of Losses thereunder exceeds $138,000, after which it shall be liable only for Losses in excess of such amount.

(b) *Exclusions.* Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation, Losses arising out of fraud, Losses arising out of any Excluded Liability under Section 8.2(c), and Losses arising out of any Assumed Liability under Section 8.3(c) shall not be subject to the Cap or the deductible in Section 8.5(a).

(c) *Mitigation and Insurance.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its Losses. Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect thereof.

**8.6 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article VIII are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties with respect to the subject matter of this Agreement after the Closing.

**8.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article VIII shall be treated as adjustments to the Base Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent of the Parties; (b) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; (c) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or (d) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred by November 15, 2026, unless the failure results from the terminating Party's breach.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 9.1, this Agreement becomes void and of no further effect, except that this Section 9.2 and Article X survive, and no termination relieves a Party from liability for any willful breach before termination.
