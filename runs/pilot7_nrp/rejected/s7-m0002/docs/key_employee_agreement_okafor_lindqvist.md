# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.**, an Arizona corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dana Okafor-Lindqvist**, an individual residing in the State of Arizona ("**Employee**"). The Company and Employee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Company designs, sells, installs and services residential and small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems in the State of Arizona (the "**Business**").

B. The Company holds contractor licenses issued by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Those licenses require a qualifying party who meets the experience and examination requirements of the Registrar.

C. The Company wishes to employ Employee as its Vice President of Installation Operations and as the qualifying party for its contractor licenses. Employee wishes to accept that employment on the terms of this Agreement.

D. In the course of this employment, Employee will receive the Company's confidential information, customer relationships and goodwill. Employee acknowledges that the restrictive covenants in this Agreement are a material inducement to the Company to enter into this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged.

## 1. EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

**1.1 Position.** The Company employs Employee as Vice President of Installation Operations, reporting to the President of the Company. Employee accepts this employment.

**1.2 Duties.** Employee shall have overall responsibility for the Company's installation operations. These duties include:

(a) crew scheduling and supervision;

(b) site safety;

(c) permitting and interconnection coordination;

(d) quality control of workmanship and roof penetrations;

(e) warranty service dispatch; and

(f) such other duties consistent with Employee's position as the President may reasonably assign.

**1.3 Qualifying Party.** During the Employment Term, Employee shall serve as the qualifying party on the Company's contractor licenses issued by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, including licenses in the residential and commercial solar classifications. Employee shall:

(a) maintain all personal qualifications required to serve in that capacity;

(b) be actively engaged in the Company's classified work as required by applicable law; and

(c) not serve as a qualifying party for any other Person without the Company's prior written consent.

**1.4 Full Time and Attention.** Employee shall devote substantially all of her business time, attention and efforts to the Business. Employee may manage personal investments and participate in civic and professional activities, provided that these activities do not interfere with her duties or violate Section 5 or Section 6.

**1.5 Location.** Employee's principal place of work is the Company's operations facility in Maricopa County, Arizona. Employee shall travel to installation sites throughout Arizona as reasonably required.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Employment Term.** Employee's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years. After the initial term, it renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. In any case, employment is subject to earlier termination under Section 4. The period of Employee's employment under this Agreement is the "**Employment Term**."

## 3. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Employee a base salary at the annual rate of $185,000, payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Board of Directors of the Company (the "**Board**") shall review the base salary annually and may increase it, but may not decrease it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of thirty percent (30%) of base salary. The bonus will be based on installation volume, permission-to-operate cycle time, safety record and warranty claim rates, against objectives the Board sets after consultation with Employee. Any bonus earned for a calendar year is payable by March 15 of the following year, subject to Employee's continued employment through December 31 of the year to which the bonus relates.

**3.3 Qualifying Party Stipend.** In consideration of Employee's service as qualifying party under Section 1.3, the Company shall pay Employee a stipend of $1,500 per month, payable with base salary.

**3.4 Benefits.** Employee may participate in the employee benefit plans the Company maintains for its senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**3.5 Vehicle and Expenses.** The Company shall provide Employee with a Company vehicle for business use. The Company shall reimburse Employee for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**3.6 Paid Time Off.** Employee is entitled to twenty (20) days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to Company holidays.

## 4. TERMINATION

**4.1 Death or Disability.** Employee's employment terminates automatically on her death. The Company may terminate Employee's employment on written notice if Employee is unable to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period.

**4.2 By the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment for Cause on written notice. "**Cause**" means any of the following by Employee:

(a) conviction of, or plea of no contest to, a felony or a crime involving dishonesty;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence causing material harm to the Company;

(c) material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice;

(d) willful failure to perform her lawful duties after written notice; or

(e) any act or omission resulting in the suspension or revocation of a Company contractor license for which Employee is the qualifying party.

**4.3 By the Company Without Cause.** The Company may terminate Employee's employment without Cause on thirty (30) days' written notice.

**4.4 By Employee.** Employee may resign for Good Reason or without Good Reason, in either case on sixty (60) days' written notice.

"**Good Reason**" means any of the following without Employee's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in base salary;

(b) a material diminution in title, authority or duties; or

(c) relocation of Employee's principal place of work by more than fifty (50) miles.

Employee may resign for Good Reason only if all of the following occur:

(i) she gives notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first arises;

(ii) the Company fails to cure the condition within thirty (30) days after that notice; and

(iii) she resigns within thirty (30) days after the cure period expires.

**4.5 Qualifying Party Transition.** On any notice of termination or resignation, Employee shall cooperate in good faith with the Company in designating and qualifying a successor qualifying party. Unless the Company terminates her employment for Cause, Employee shall continue to serve as qualifying party through her final day of employment.

## 5. SEVERANCE

**5.1 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay Employee:

(a) earned but unpaid base salary;

(b) accrued but unused paid time off; and

(c) unreimbursed business expenses.

**5.2 Severance.** If the Company terminates Employee's employment without Cause, or Employee resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall:

(a) continue Employee's base salary for twelve (12) months after the termination date; and

(b) reimburse Employee's COBRA premiums for the same period.

These payments are subject to Employee's execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company.

## 6. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**6.1 Non-Competition.** During the Employment Term and for 24 months after termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, within the State of Arizona, do any of the following:

(a) engage in the sale, design, installation or servicing of residential or small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems or battery storage systems;

(b) own, manage, operate or control any Person that engages in those activities;

(c) be employed by, or provide services to, any Person that engages in those activities; or

(d) serve as a qualifying party for any Person that holds or applies for a contractor license from the Arizona Registrar of Contractors in a solar classification.

Ownership of not more than two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any company is not a breach of this Section 6.1.

**6.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers.** During the Employment Term and for 24 months after termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly:

(a) solicit or induce any customer of the Company, or any prospective customer to whom the Company submitted a proposal during the twelve (12) months before termination, to purchase solar or storage products or installation services from any Person other than the Company; or

(b) induce any such customer or prospective customer to terminate or reduce its business with the Company.

**6.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Employment Term and for 24 months after termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire any employee or installation subcontractor of the Company. This restriction also covers any such person who was an employee or subcontractor of the Company within six (6) months before the solicitation. General advertisements not targeted at Company personnel do not breach this Section 6.3.

**6.4 Reasonableness.** Employee acknowledges each of the following:

(a) the restrictions in this Section 6 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and subject matter;

(b) they are necessary to protect the Company's confidential information, customer relationships and goodwill; and

(c) her skills will allow her to earn a livelihood without violating them.

If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any restriction is unenforceable as written, the court may revise it to the extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as revised.

**6.5 Tolling.** The restricted periods in Sections 6.1 through 6.3 are tolled during any period in which Employee is in breach of the applicable covenant.

## 7. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND WORK PRODUCT

**7.1 Confidentiality.** During and after the Employment Term, Employee shall hold in strict confidence, and shall not use or disclose except in performing her duties, any confidential or proprietary information of the Company. This includes:

(a) customer lists, pipeline and proposal data, and pricing;

(b) supplier terms, installation methods, and warranty and service records; and

(c) financial information.

This Section 7.1 does not apply to information that becomes publicly available through no fault of Employee.

**7.2 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, Employee shall promptly return to the Company:

(a) the Company vehicle, tools, equipment, devices and documents; and

(b) all data in her possession, including all copies.

**7.3 Work Product.** All procedures, designs, documents and other work product that Employee creates within the scope of her employment are the sole property of the Company. Employee hereby assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in that work product.

## 8. REMEDIES

**8.1 Injunctive Relief.** Employee acknowledges that a breach of Section 6 or Section 7 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is therefore entitled to injunctive relief to restrain any such breach or threatened breach, without posting bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT

**9.1 Assignment by Employee.** This Agreement is personal to Employee. Employee may not assign it or delegate her duties under it.

**9.2 Assignment by the Company.** The Company may not assign this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of Employee. This restriction applies to any assignment by operation of law or otherwise, including an assignment in connection with a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company. Any purported assignment without that consent is void.

**9.3 Binding Effect.** This Agreement is binding on and inures to the benefit of the Parties and their respective heirs, legal representatives and permitted assigns.

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered personally or by email with confirmation of transmission, or one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier. Notices to the Company go to its principal office, attention: President. Notices to Employee go to the most recent address on the Company's records.

**10.2 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any payment under this Agreement all taxes required by law.

**10.3 Section 409A.** The Parties intend this Agreement to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and shall interpret it accordingly. Each installment payment under Section 5.2 is a separate payment.

**10.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party granting the waiver.

**10.5 Severability.** Subject to Section 6.4, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.6 Survival.** Sections 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10 survive the termination of Employee's employment and of this Agreement.

**10.7 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona. Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Maricopa County, Arizona for any action arising out of this Agreement.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Signatures delivered electronically are deemed original signatures.
