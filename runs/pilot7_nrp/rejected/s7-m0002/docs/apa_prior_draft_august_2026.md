# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**BUYER'S COUNSEL DRAFT: August 21, 2026**
*For discussion purposes only. Not an offer. Subject to further review by Buyer and its advisors, and to completion of due diligence.*

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Tredeil Sunworks, Inc.**, an Arizona corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Roumoux Solar Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"). Seller and Buyer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller designs, sells, installs and services residential and small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems in the State of Arizona (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer substantially all of the assets of the Business, and certain specified liabilities. Buyer wishes to purchase and assume them from Seller. The sale is on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

(a) "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

(b) "**Assigned Contracts**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1(c).

(c) "**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale, in the form agreed by the Parties, that Seller will deliver to Buyer at Closing under Section 3.2(a).

(d) "**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule that Seller delivers to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

(e) "**Earnout Period**" means each of the following:

(i) the twelve-month period beginning on the first day of the calendar month following the Closing Date (the "**First Earnout Period**"); and

(ii) the twelve-month period immediately following the First Earnout Period (the "**Second Earnout Period**").

(f) "**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, easement, option, right of first refusal or other restriction of any kind.

(g) "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, board, registrar, court or tribunal thereof, including the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

(h) "**Installed Megawatts**" means the aggregate nameplate direct-current capacity, expressed in megawatts, of solar photovoltaic systems that Buyer or its Affiliates install in the State of Arizona during an Earnout Period and for which the applicable utility has issued permission to operate.

(i) "**Key Employee Agreement**" means the Key Employee Agreement between Seller and Dana Okafor-Lindqvist, as in effect on the Effective Date.

(j) "**Liabilities**" means liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature whatsoever, whether asserted or unasserted, known or unknown, absolute or contingent, accrued or unaccrued, matured or unmatured.

(k) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, claims, actions, judgments, settlements, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(l) "**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

(m) "**Purchased Assets**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the assets, properties and rights described below that are used or held for use in the Business (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"). The Purchased Assets shall be transferred free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those set forth on Schedule 4.4. They consist of:

(a) all inventory, wherever located, including solar modules, inverters, racking, batteries, balance-of-system components, wire and supplies (the "**Inventory**");

(b) all motor vehicles, trailers, lifts, tools, equipment and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(b) (the "**Fleet and Equipment**");

(c) all contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) (collectively, the "**Assigned Contracts**"), including:

(i) all customer installation contracts for systems that, as of the Closing, have not been installed or have not been granted permission to operate (the "**Backlog Contracts**");

(ii) all supplier and distributor agreements so listed; and

(iii) the Key Employee Agreement;

(d) the trade names "Tredeil Sunworks" and "Tredeil Solar," all associated logos, domain names, telephone numbers and social media accounts, and the goodwill of the Business associated with them (the "**Trade Names**");

(e) all customer warranty records, monitoring accounts, service histories and customer lists relating to systems installed by Seller (the "**Warranty Book**");

(f) all permits and licenses Seller holds for the operation of the Business, to the extent transferable under applicable law;

(g) all prepaid expenses, and all deposits and advance payments received from customers under the Backlog Contracts; and

(h) all books, records, files and data relating to the foregoing.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The following are not Purchased Assets (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**"):

(a) cash and cash equivalents;

(b) accounts receivable for systems granted permission to operate before the Closing;

(c) Seller's corporate records, minute books and tax returns;

(d) any contract not listed on Schedule 2.1(c);

(e) Seller's employee benefit plans and the assets of those plans;

(f) all insurance policies of Seller and rights under them; and

(g) the rights of Seller under this Agreement and the other documents delivered at Closing.

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume, and agrees to pay, perform and discharge, only the following Liabilities of Seller (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent they arise and are required to be performed after the Closing, excluding Liabilities arising out of any breach of those contracts by Seller before the Closing; and

(b) trade accounts payable to third-party vendors for Inventory delivered to Seller within thirty (30) days before the Closing, to the extent set forth on the closing statement.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer does not assume and is not responsible for any Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) any Liability for Taxes of Seller for any period;

(b) any Liability relating to the employment, or termination of employment, of any employee of Seller arising before the Closing;

(c) any indebtedness of Seller for borrowed money;

(d) any Liability relating to the Excluded Assets;

(e) any Liability arising out of any solar photovoltaic system that Seller installed, or any service Seller performed, before the Closing, including all workmanship, roof-penetration and other warranty obligations for those systems;

(f) any Liability arising out of any action, suit, claim or proceeding that was pending or threatened against Seller as of the Closing, including each matter set forth on Schedule 4.9; and

(g) any Liability of Seller under this Agreement.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets consists of:

(a) $18,400,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**");

(b) the Earnout Payments, if any, payable under Section 2.6; and

(c) the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Base Purchase Price to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds. Payment shall be made to an account that Seller designates in writing at least three (3) business days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Earnout.**

(a) *Earnout Payments.* As additional consideration, Buyer shall pay Seller the amounts described below (each, an "**Earnout Payment**"). Earnout Payments for all Earnout Periods are subject to a maximum of $3,000,000 in aggregate.

(i) For each Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts equal or exceed 18.0 megawatts, the Earnout Payment is $1,500,000.

(ii) For each Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts exceed 12.0 megawatts but are less than 18.0 megawatts, the Earnout Payment is $1,500,000 multiplied by a fraction. The numerator of the fraction is Installed Megawatts for that Earnout Period minus 12.0, and the denominator is 6.0.

No Earnout Payment is payable for an Earnout Period in which Installed Megawatts are 12.0 megawatts or less.

(b) *Earnout Statement.* Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Earnout Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written statement setting forth Installed Megawatts for that Earnout Period and Buyer's calculation of the Earnout Payment, if any (the "**Earnout Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* Seller may object to an Earnout Statement by written notice delivered within thirty (30) days after receipt. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any objection. Any objection not resolved within thirty (30) days after delivery of the notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually agreed by the Parties. The firm's determination is final and binding absent manifest error. The Parties shall bear the firm's fees in inverse proportion to the relative amounts of the disputed items determined in their favor.

(d) *Information Rights.* Within twenty (20) days after the end of each calendar quarter during the Earnout Periods, Buyer shall provide Seller with a report of installation volumes by system and permission-to-operate date. Seller may use these reports only to verify Installed Megawatts and shall treat them as confidential information of Buyer under Section 6.6. Following delivery of an Earnout Statement, Buyer shall give the accounting firm referred to in Section 2.6(c), or Seller's independent accountants subject to a customary confidentiality undertaking, reasonable access to the books and records relating to Installed Megawatts. This access is provided only to the extent reasonably necessary to verify the Earnout Statement, and Buyer is not required to disclose customer pipeline, pricing or proposal data.

(e) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay each Earnout Payment within ten (10) business days after its final determination under this Section 2.6.

**2.7 Allocation.** The Base Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. The allocation shall follow the allocation schedule the Parties agree within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on October 1, 2026, or on such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing is effective as of 12:01 a.m. Arizona time on the Closing Date.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver the following to Buyer:

(a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities, duly executed by Seller;

(c) a certificate of title for each titled vehicle included in the Fleet and Equipment, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer;

(d) an assignment of the Trade Names, duly executed by Seller;

(e) the consents listed on Schedule 4.6;

(f) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors and shareholders approving this Agreement; and

(g) the certificate described in Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the following to Seller:

(a) the Base Purchase Price, paid in accordance with Section 2.5;

(b) the assignment and assumption agreement, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) the certificate described in Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Arizona. It has full corporate power and authority to own the Purchased Assets and to carry on the Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it. This Agreement has been duly authorized by all requisite corporate and shareholder action of Seller. It constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not:

(a) violate Seller's articles of incorporation or bylaws;

(b) violate any law or order applicable to Seller; or

(c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.6, require the consent of any Person under, conflict with, or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances except those set forth on Schedule 4.4. Each Encumbrance set forth on Schedule 4.4 will be released at or before Closing.

**4.5 Condition of Assets.** The Fleet and Equipment is in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted. The Inventory is of a quality and quantity usable in the ordinary course of the Business. No Inventory is obsolete, damaged or defective, except as reserved on Seller's financial statements.

**4.6 Required Consents.** Schedule 4.6 sets forth each consent, approval, waiver or notice of or to any Person that is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller, including the assignment to Buyer of each Assigned Contract (the "**Required Consents**").

**4.7 Assigned Contracts.** Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and, to Seller's knowledge, on the counterparty, and is in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.8 Licenses.** Seller holds all contractor licenses issued by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors that are necessary to conduct the Business. Each such license, together with its qualifying party, is listed on Schedule 4.8, and each is in good standing.

**4.9 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.9, no action, suit, claim or proceeding is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.10 Warranty Obligations.** Schedule 4.10 sets forth the standard forms of workmanship, roof-penetration and product warranties that Seller provides to its customers. Seller has made the Warranty Book available to Buyer, and it is accurate and complete in all material respects.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws.** Seller has conducted the Business in compliance in all material respects with all applicable laws, including laws relating to contractor licensing, consumer protection and solar energy device disclosures.

**4.12 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement has been duly authorized by all requisite limited liability company action of Buyer. It constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall:

(a) operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice;

(b) maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition; and

(c) use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access.** From the Effective Date until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain each Required Consent before the Closing.

**6.4 Contractor License Transition.** Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in Buyer's application for contractor licenses from the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. This obligation applies before the Closing and for one hundred eighty (180) days after it. Seller's cooperation includes making available the qualifying party listed on Schedule 4.8.

**6.5 Employees.** Buyer may, but is not obligated to, offer employment to any employee of Seller. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts an offer from Buyer, effective as of the Closing. Seller shall pay all wages, accrued vacation and other compensation owed to those employees through the Closing Date.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business and the Purchased Assets, and all information Seller receives under Section 2.6(d). This obligation does not apply to information that becomes publicly available other than through Seller's breach of this Section 6.6.

**6.7 Non-Competition.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the sale or installation of residential or small-commercial solar photovoltaic systems in the State of Arizona.

**6.8 Name Change.** Within ten (10) business days after the Closing, Seller shall amend its articles of incorporation to change its corporate name to a name that does not include "Tredeil" or any confusingly similar name.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Both Parties.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any law or order restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Buyer's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) each consent listed on Schedule 4.6 shall have been obtained, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, and shall be in full force and effect;

(b) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, and Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate, signed by an executive officer of Seller, certifying that the conditions in Section 7.2(a) and Section 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets shall have been released, other than those Buyer has agreed in writing to accept; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each of the items set forth in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction, or Seller's waiver, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants it is required to perform before the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate to that effect, signed by an authorized officer of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VIII. INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date. However, the representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2, 4.4 and 4.12 (the "**Fundamental Representations**") survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants of the Parties survive in accordance with their terms.

**8.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates, and their respective officers, managers, members and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses that any of them incurs arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability or Excluded Asset.

**8.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, shareholders and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses that any of them incurs arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or

(c) any Assumed Liability.

**8.4 Procedures.**

(a) *Notice.* A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice describing the claim in reasonable detail to the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). Failure to give prompt notice relieves the Indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually prejudiced by the failure.

(b) *Defense.* The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party. The Indemnified Party may participate in the defense at its own expense.

(c) *Settlement.* No third-party claim may be settled without the consent of the Indemnified Party, which may not be unreasonably withheld. Consent is not required for a settlement that involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**8.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap and Deductible.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which an Indemnifying Party is liable under Section 8.2(a) or Section 8.3(a), as applicable, shall not exceed 15% of the Base Purchase Price ($2,760,000) (the "**Cap**"). An Indemnifying Party is not liable under Section 8.2(a) or Section 8.3(a) until the aggregate amount of Losses under that Section exceeds $138,000 (the "**Deductible**"). After that, it is liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(b) *Claims Outside the Cap.* The Cap and the Deductible apply only to claims under Section 8.2(a) and Section 8.3(a). They do not apply to claims for inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation, or to claims based on fraud.

(c) *Mitigation and Insurance.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its Losses. Losses are calculated net of any insurance proceeds the Indemnified Party actually receives in respect of them.

**8.6 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article VIII are the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties with respect to the subject matter of this Agreement. This Section 8.6 does not apply to claims of fraud or to claims for specific performance or injunctive relief.

**8.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article VIII shall be treated as adjustments to the Base Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE IX. TERMINATION

**9.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of the Parties;

(b) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice;

(c) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice; or

(d) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred by November 15, 2026, unless the failure results from the terminating Party's breach.

**9.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination under Section 9.1, this Agreement becomes void and of no further effect, except that this Section 9.2 and Article X survive. No termination relieves a Party from liability for any willful breach before termination.

**10.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each Party. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party granting the waiver.
