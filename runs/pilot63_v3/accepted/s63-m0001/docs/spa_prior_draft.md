## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

### 2.1 Purchase and Sale of the Shares; Base Purchase Price

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver the Shares to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire the Shares from Seller, free and clear of all Liens. The consideration for the Shares is as follows:

(a) an aggregate base purchase price of $42,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4; plus

(b) the Earn-Out Payment, if any, payable under Section 2.5.

### 2.2 Payments at Closing

At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, or cause to be paid, the following amounts by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) the Escrow Amount, to the Escrow Agent, for deposit into the Escrow Fund under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) the Indebtedness described in Section 2.2(b) of the Disclosure Schedule, on behalf of the Company, in the amounts set forth in customary payoff letters delivered by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(c) the Transaction Expenses, on behalf of the Company, in accordance with invoices delivered by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(d) to Seller, to the account designated in writing by Seller, an amount equal to:

(i) the Base Purchase Price;

(ii) plus or minus the Estimated Adjustment Amount determined under Section 2.4(a); and

(iii) less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a), 2.2(b) and 2.2(c).

### 2.3 Escrow

(a) The Escrow Fund shall be held by the Escrow Agent as security for the following:

(i) the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX; and

(ii) any amount owed to Buyer under Section 2.4(d).

(b) The Escrow Fund shall be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

(c) Buyer and Seller shall bear the fees of the Escrow Agent equally.

### 2.4 Working Capital Adjustment

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* Not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital. Seller shall prepare the statement in accordance with GAAP, applied consistently with the Financial Statements and with the illustrative calculation attached as Schedule 2.4. The target working capital is $3,850,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"). The "**Estimated Adjustment Amount**" is the amount by which estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of the Target Working Capital.

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of the actual Closing Working Capital (the "**Closing Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* Disputes regarding the Closing Statement shall be resolved as follows:

(i) Seller may dispute the Closing Statement by written notice delivered within forty-five (45) days after receipt. The notice shall specify each disputed item in reasonable detail.

(ii) The parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve any dispute.

(iii) Any items not resolved under clause (ii) shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. Its determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

(iv) The Independent Accountant's fees shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed amounts.

(d) *Payment.* Any payment under this Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination, as follows:

(i) If the final Closing Working Capital exceeds the estimated Closing Working Capital, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller.

(ii) If the final Closing Working Capital is less than the estimated Closing Working Capital, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer. Buyer may, at its election, recover all or any part of the shortfall from the Escrow Fund.

(e) *Closing Working Capital.* "**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company minus the current liabilities of the Company, in each case as of 12:01 a.m. New York time on the Closing Date. For this purpose:

(i) current assets exclude cash, cash equivalents, income Tax assets and deferred Tax assets; and

(ii) current liabilities exclude Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses, income Tax liabilities and deferred Tax liabilities.

### 2.5 Earn-Out

(a) *Earn-Out Payment.* As additional consideration for the Shares, Buyer shall pay Seller an earn-out payment (the "**Earn-Out Payment**"). The Earn-Out Payment is determined on the basis of the Company's Net Revenue for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2027 (the "**Earn-Out Period**"), as follows:

(i) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period is less than $31,000,000, no Earn-Out Payment shall be payable;

(ii) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period equals or exceeds $31,000,000 but is less than $37,000,000, the Earn-Out Payment shall equal $6,000,000 multiplied by a fraction. The numerator of the fraction is the amount by which Net Revenue exceeds $31,000,000, and the denominator is $6,000,000; and

(iii) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period equals or exceeds $37,000,000, the Earn-Out Payment shall equal $6,000,000.

The maximum aggregate Earn-Out Payment payable under this Agreement is $6,000,000.

(b) *Net Revenue.* "**Net Revenue**" means the gross revenue of the Company from the sale, lease and servicing of drones, payload systems and related software subscriptions, less returns, credits, discounts and allowances. Net Revenue is determined in accordance with GAAP, applied consistently with the Financial Statements.

(c) *Earn-Out Statement.* Within seventy-five (75) days after the end of the Earn-Out Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of Net Revenue and the resulting Earn-Out Payment (the "**Earn-Out Statement**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

(d) *Information Rights.* During the Earn-Out Period, and until the Earn-Out Payment is finally determined, Buyer shall cause the Company to do the following:

(i) deliver to Seller, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, a report of Net Revenue for that quarter by product and service line; and

(ii) give Seller and its accountants reasonable access to the books and records of the Company relevant to the calculation of Net Revenue, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice.

*[Note to Seller: Buyer is considering Seller's request for customer-level reporting and audit rights and will respond in the next turn.]*

(e) *Disputes.* Any dispute regarding the Earn-Out Statement shall be resolved in the manner provided in Section 2.4(c), *mutatis mutandis*.

(f) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay the Earn-Out Payment, if any, within ten (10) Business Days after its final determination, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

(g) *Operation of the Business.* During the Earn-Out Period, Buyer shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, take any action with the primary purpose of avoiding or reducing the Earn-Out Payment. Subject to the preceding sentence, Buyer shall have sole discretion over the operation of the Company and its business.

(h) *Nature of Right.* Seller's right to receive the Earn-Out Payment is a contractual right only. It is not a security, and it does not represent any equity or ownership interest in Buyer or the Company.

### 2.6 Withholding

Buyer, the Company and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under the Code or any other applicable Tax Law. Amounts withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as paid to the Person in respect of whom the deduction and withholding was made.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

### 8.1 Termination

This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing in any of the following ways:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, by written notice to Seller, if all of the following apply:

(i) Buyer is not then in material breach of this Agreement;

(ii) Seller has breached a representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition to Buyer's obligations in Article VII would not be satisfied; and

(iii) the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice from Buyer;

(c) by Seller, by written notice to Buyer, if all of the following apply:

(i) Seller is not then in material breach of this Agreement;

(ii) Buyer has breached a representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition to Seller's obligations in Article VII would not be satisfied; and

(iii) the breach has not been cured within twenty (20) days after written notice from Seller;

(d) by either Buyer or Seller, by written notice to the other, if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 15, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**"). This right is not available to any party whose breach of this Agreement has been the principal cause of the failure of the Closing to occur by that date; or

(e) by either Buyer or Seller, if any Governmental Authority has issued a final, non-appealable order permanently enjoining the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

### 8.2 Effect of Termination

If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void and of no further force or effect, without liability of any party. However:

(a) this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive termination; and

(b) nothing in this Agreement shall relieve any party from liability for any willful breach of this Agreement occurring before termination.
