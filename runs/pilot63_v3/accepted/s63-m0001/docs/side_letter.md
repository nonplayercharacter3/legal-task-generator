# LETTER AGREEMENT NO. 1

**Re: Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026, by and among Bisart AgTech Corp., Bramand Holdings, LLC and, for the limited purposes stated in it, Furrowline Robotics, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Letter Agreement**") refers to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). The parties to the Agreement are Bisart AgTech Corp., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Bramand Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Article VI and Article XI of the Agreement, Furrowline Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

During Buyer's confirmatory diligence after signing, Buyer and Seller reviewed the Company's operating authorizations from the Federal Aviation Administration. These cover the commercial operation of the Company's spray and survey drones and the Remote ID compliance of the Company's aircraft. Buyer and Seller have agreed to add a representation and warranty and a related covenant on those matters, and to make certain other changes to the Agreement, as set forth below. This Letter Agreement is an amendment of the Agreement under Section 11.6 of the Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual agreements in this Letter Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Definitions and References

### 1.1 Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

### 1.2 New Definitions

Section 1.1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following definitions in their appropriate alphabetical order:

"**Airspace Authorization**" means any authorization issued by the FAA for the operation of an unmanned aircraft in controlled airspace. This includes any authorization obtained through the Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability or the FAA DroneZone portal.

"**FAA**" means the Federal Aviation Administration of the United States Department of Transportation.

"**FAA Authorizations**" means all of the following held by the Company, or by any employee or contractor of the Company for the conduct of the Business:

(a) certificates, exemptions, waivers, Airspace Authorizations, registrations, declarations of compliance and other authorizations issued or accepted by the FAA; and

(b) each pending application for any of the items in clause (a).

The term includes the Part 137 Certificate, the Section 44807 Exemption and the Part 107 Waivers.

"**Part 107 Waivers**" means the certificates of waiver issued to the Company by the FAA under 14 C.F.R. § 107.205 and listed in Annex 1, as renewed, amended or replaced from time to time.

"**Part 137 Certificate**" means the agricultural aircraft operator certificate issued to the Company by the FAA under 14 C.F.R. Part 137 and listed in Annex 1, as renewed or amended from time to time.

"**Remote ID Rule**" means 14 C.F.R. Part 89 and the FAA's means-of-compliance and declaration-of-compliance requirements under it.

"**Remote Pilot**" means each individual who, on behalf of the Company, acts as remote pilot in command of, or manipulates the flight controls of, any unmanned aircraft operated by the Company. This includes any individual who does so in a demonstration, test flight, customer training session or commercial spraying, seeding or survey operation.

"**Section 44807 Exemption**" means the exemption granted to the Company by the FAA under 49 U.S.C. § 44807 and listed in Annex 1, as extended, amended or replaced from time to time.

### 1.3 References

From the date of this Letter Agreement, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar import, and each reference to the Agreement in any Ancillary Agreement, means the Agreement as amended by this Letter Agreement.

## 2. Additional Representation and Warranty of Seller

### 2.1 Insertion of New Section 4.12

Article IV of the Agreement is amended by inserting the following new Section 4.12, Remote Pilot and Airspace Compliance, immediately after Section 4.11 (Employees and Benefit Plans):

> ### 4.12 Remote Pilot and Airspace Compliance
>
> (a) *FAA Authorizations.* Annex 1 to Letter Agreement No. 1 lists each FAA Authorization held by or for the benefit of the Company. For each, Annex 1 states its holder, issue date, expiration date (if any) and the operations it permits. The FAA Authorizations are all of the authorizations of the FAA required to conduct the Business as currently conducted. Each FAA Authorization is valid and in full force and effect. The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with the terms and conditions of each FAA Authorization. No suspension, revocation, cancellation, non-renewal or adverse modification of any FAA Authorization is pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened.
>
> (b) *Part 137 Operations.* Every agricultural aircraft operation conducted by the Company for the dispensing of economic poisons, fertilizers, seeds or other substances has been conducted under the Part 137 Certificate. It has also been conducted under the Section 44807 Exemption, where required, and in compliance in all material respects with 14 C.F.R. Part 137 and the conditions and limitations of that exemption. The Company has designated a chief supervisor for its Part 137 operations as required by 14 C.F.R. § 137.41 and has maintained the records required by 14 C.F.R. § 137.71.
>
> (c) *Remote Pilots.* Each Remote Pilot held, at all times while acting as such, a current remote pilot certificate with a small unmanned aircraft system rating under 14 C.F.R. Part 107. Each Remote Pilot also held any additional knowledge, skill, recurrent training or currency qualification required by the Section 44807 Exemption or the Part 137 Certificate. The Company keeps a record of each Remote Pilot's certificate number, date of issuance and most recent recurrent training. It has made that record available to Buyer.
>
> (d) *Operations Under Waivers.* All operations by the Company beyond visual line of sight, at night, over people, from moving vehicles or with multiple aircraft under the control of a single Remote Pilot have been conducted under, and in compliance in all material respects with, a Part 107 Waiver or the Section 44807 Exemption permitting them.
>
> (e) *Airspace.* All operations by the Company in controlled airspace have been conducted under a valid Airspace Authorization. The Company has not operated any unmanned aircraft in any temporary flight restriction area, special use airspace or other restricted area without the required authorization of the FAA or the controlling agency.
>
> (f) *Registration and Remote ID.* Each unmanned aircraft owned, leased or operated by the Company is registered with the FAA as required by 14 C.F.R. Part 48. Each such unmanned aircraft, and each unmanned aircraft model manufactured by the Company and sold or leased to customers since September 16, 2022, either:
>
> (i) is a standard remote identification unmanned aircraft produced under a declaration of compliance accepted by the FAA; or
>
> (ii) is equipped with a remote identification broadcast module listed on an FAA-accepted declaration of compliance;
>
> in each case in compliance with the Remote ID Rule. No FAA-accepted declaration of compliance covering any Company model has been rescinded or is subject to a pending notice of rescission.
>
> (g) *Incidents and Reports.* Annex 1 to Letter Agreement No. 1 lists each accident, incident or occurrence since January 1, 2023 involving an unmanned aircraft operated by the Company that the Company reported, or was required to report, to the FAA or the National Transportation Safety Board. The list includes each report required by 14 C.F.R. § 107.9. Each required report was filed timely and accurately.
>
> (h) *Enforcement.* Since January 1, 2023, the Company has not received any letter of investigation, warning notice, letter of correction, notice of proposed civil penalty, order of suspension or revocation or other written communication from the FAA alleging any violation of any Law governing the operation, manufacture or registration of unmanned aircraft. To the Knowledge of Seller, no such investigation or proceeding is pending or threatened against the Company or any Remote Pilot in connection with the Business.

## 4. Additional Covenant

Article VI of the Agreement is amended by adding the following new Section 6.14 after Section 6.13 (Directors' and Officers' Indemnification):

> ### 6.14 FAA Authorizations
>
> (a) *Maintenance and Renewal.* From the date of Letter Agreement No. 1 until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to:
>
> (i) maintain each FAA Authorization in full force and effect;
>
> (ii) comply in all material respects with its terms and conditions; and
>
> (iii) file, at least sixty (60) days before its scheduled expiration (or such earlier date as the FAA requires), a complete application for the renewal or extension of each FAA Authorization that would otherwise expire on or before the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date.
>
> Seller shall cause the Company to deliver to Buyer a copy of each such application, and of all material correspondence with the FAA relating to it, within three (3) Business Days after its filing or receipt.
>
> (b) *No Amendment.* From the date of Letter Agreement No. 1 until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company not to do either of the following without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed:
>
> (i) surrender, voluntarily amend or allow to lapse any FAA Authorization; or
>
> (ii) withdraw any pending application for an FAA Authorization.
>
> (c) *Chief Supervisor and Key Remote Pilots.* From the date of Letter Agreement No. 1 until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer prompt written notice if either of the following resigns, or gives notice of resignation, or if the Company proposes to replace either of them:
>
> (i) the individual designated as chief supervisor under the Part 137 Certificate; or
>
> (ii) any individual designated as a responsible person under the Section 44807 Exemption.
>
> Seller shall cause the Company to consult with Buyer in good faith on the designation of any successor.
>
> (d) *Remote ID Retrofit.* Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to complete the installation of a remote identification broadcast module on each unmanned aircraft identified in Part C of Annex 1 as awaiting retrofit. Each module installed must be listed on an FAA-accepted declaration of compliance. Seller shall deliver to Buyer, at or before the Closing, a certificate of an officer of the Company confirming that each such installation has been completed and identifying each aircraft by its FAA registration number and serial number.
>
> (e) *Post-Closing Filings.* At and after the Closing, Seller shall cooperate with Buyer and the Company, as reasonably requested, in the preparation and submission of any notice, amendment or application to the FAA required in connection with the transactions contemplated by the Agreement. This includes any update to the Company's ownership, management or responsible-person information under the Part 137 Certificate or the Section 44807 Exemption, as contemplated by Section 4.4.
