# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and among **Bisart AgTech Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Bramand Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and, solely for purposes of Article VI and Article XI, **Furrowline Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. The Company designs, manufactures, sells, leases and services precision-agriculture spray and survey drones, together with related flight-planning, telemetry and ground-station software and services.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

### 2.1 Purchase and Sale of the Shares; Base Purchase Price

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate base purchase price of $42,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.4, plus the Earn-Out Payment, if any, payable under Section 2.5.

### 2.2 Payments at Closing

At the Closing, Buyer shall pay, or cause to be paid, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, for deposit into the Escrow Fund under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness described in Section 2.2(b) of the Disclosure Schedule, in the amounts set forth in customary payoff letters delivered by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date;

(c) on behalf of the Company, the Transaction Expenses, in accordance with invoices delivered by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date; and

(d) to Seller, an amount equal to the Base Purchase Price, plus or minus the Estimated Adjustment Amount determined under Section 2.4(a), less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a), 2.2(b) and 2.2(c), to the account designated in writing by Seller.

### 2.3 Escrow

The Escrow Fund shall be held by the Escrow Agent as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX and for any amount owed to Buyer under Section 2.4(d), and shall be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement. Fees of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

### 2.4 Working Capital Adjustment

(a) *Estimated Closing Statement.* Not later than five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital, prepared in accordance with GAAP applied consistently with the Financial Statements and the illustrative calculation attached as Schedule 2.4. The amount by which estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of $3,850,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**") is the "**Estimated Adjustment Amount**."

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of the actual Closing Working Capital (the "**Closing Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* Seller may dispute the Closing Statement by written notice delivered within forty-five (45) days after receipt, specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item. The parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve any dispute. Any items not so resolved shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"), acting as an expert and not as an arbitrator, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error. The Independent Accountant's fees shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each on the disputed amounts.

(d) *Payment.* If the final Closing Working Capital exceeds the estimated Closing Working Capital, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If the final Closing Working Capital is less than the estimated Closing Working Capital, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer, and Buyer may, at its election, recover all or any part of such shortfall from the Escrow Fund. Any payment under this Section 2.4(d) shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

(e) *Closing Working Capital.* "**Closing Working Capital**" means the current assets of the Company (excluding cash, cash equivalents, income Tax assets and deferred Tax assets) minus the current liabilities of the Company (excluding Indebtedness, Transaction Expenses, income Tax liabilities and deferred Tax liabilities), in each case as of 12:01 a.m. New York time on the Closing Date.

### 2.5 Earn-Out

(a) *Earn-Out Payment.* As additional consideration for the Shares, Buyer shall pay to Seller an earn-out payment (the "**Earn-Out Payment**") determined on the basis of the Company's Net Revenue for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2027 (the "**Earn-Out Period**"), as follows:

(i) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period is less than $31,000,000, no Earn-Out Payment shall be payable;

(ii) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period equals or exceeds $31,000,000 but is less than $37,000,000, the Earn-Out Payment shall equal $6,000,000 multiplied by a fraction, the numerator of which is the amount by which Net Revenue exceeds $31,000,000 and the denominator of which is $6,000,000; and

(iii) if Net Revenue for the Earn-Out Period equals or exceeds $37,000,000, the Earn-Out Payment shall equal $6,000,000.

The maximum aggregate Earn-Out Payment payable under this Agreement is $6,000,000.

(b) *Net Revenue.* "**Net Revenue**" means the gross revenue of the Company from the sale, lease and servicing of drones, payload systems and related software subscriptions, less returns, credits, discounts and allowances, determined in accordance with GAAP applied consistently with the Financial Statements.

(c) *Earn-Out Statement.* Within seventy-five (75) days after the end of the Earn-Out Period, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of Net Revenue and the resulting Earn-Out Payment (the "**Earn-Out Statement**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

(d) *Information and Audit Rights.* During the Earn-Out Period and until the Earn-Out Payment is finally determined, Buyer shall cause the Company to (i) deliver to Seller within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter a report of Net Revenue for that quarter, broken down by customer, by acreage serviced and by product and service line, with the applicable pricing, and (ii) give Seller and its accountants and representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the books, records, customer lists, acreage and application logs, pricing schedules and personnel of the Company relevant to the calculation of Net Revenue. Seller may, once following delivery of the Earn-Out Statement, cause an audit of the Net Revenue calculation to be performed by an independent accounting firm at Seller's expense, provided that Buyer shall reimburse the cost of such audit if it reveals an understatement of Net Revenue of more than five percent (5%).

(e) *Disputes.* Any dispute regarding the Earn-Out Statement shall be resolved in the manner provided in Section 2.4(c), *mutatis mutandis*.

(f) *Payment.* Buyer shall pay the Earn-Out Payment, if any, within ten (10) Business Days after its final determination, by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

(g) *Operation of the Business.* During the Earn-Out Period, Buyer shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, take any action with the primary purpose of avoiding or reducing the Earn-Out Payment. Subject to the preceding sentence, Buyer shall have sole discretion over the operation of the Company and its business.

(h) *Nature of Right.* The right of Seller to receive the Earn-Out Payment is a contractual right only, is not a security and does not represent any equity or ownership interest in Buyer or the Company.

### 2.6 Withholding

Buyer, the Company and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under the Code or any other applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the Person in respect of which such deduction and withholding was made.

(d) *Cooperation.* The parties shall reasonably cooperate in the defense of any Third-Party Claim, including by furnishing records, information and testimony and by making employees available on a mutually convenient basis.

(c) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating the amount of Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty (but not for purposes of determining whether such inaccuracy or breach has occurred), any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualification shall be disregarded.

(d) *No Double Recovery.* No Indemnified Party shall be entitled to recover more than once for the same Loss, and no Losses shall be recoverable under Section 9.2 to the extent taken into account as a liability or reserve in the final determination of Closing Working Capital.

(e) *Insurance.* The amount of any Loss shall be reduced by any insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of that Loss, net of costs of recovery, deductibles and resulting premium increases.

(f) *Consequential Damages.* No party shall be liable for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party in connection with a Third-Party Claim.

(g) *Mitigation.* Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate any Loss upon becoming aware of any event that would reasonably be expected to give rise to it.
