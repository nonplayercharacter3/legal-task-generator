# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026, by and among **Bisart AgTech Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Bramand Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Lindqvist Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Furrowline Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all issued and outstanding shares of Furrowline Robotics, Inc.

B. The Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount will secure the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement and any amount owed to Buyer under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent and to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Defined Terms

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not responsible for knowing or interpreting the Purchase Agreement. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated escrow account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" means the date that is twelve (12) months after the date of this Escrow Agreement or, if that date is not a Business Day, the next Business Day.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a final determination of the Independent Accountant under Section 2.4(c) or Section 2.5(e) of the Purchase Agreement, together with a certificate of counsel for the party presenting it stating that it is final and non-appealable or otherwise final and binding.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means a written instruction executed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 10.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered by Buyer before that date that have not been resolved by Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order as of that date.

"**Authorized Representative**" means, for each of Buyer and Seller, each individual named on that party's certificate of incumbency attached as Schedule 1, as updated by that party from time to time by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

### 1.2 Interpretation

The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Escrow Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT; ESCROW ACCOUNT

### 2.1 Appointment and Deposit

Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, the sum of $4,200,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**"). Buyer shall give the Escrow Agent at least two (2) Business Days' advance written notice of the expected date of deposit.

### 2.2 Escrow Account

The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account established in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall not commingle the Escrow Fund with any other funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm in writing to Buyer and Seller its receipt of the Escrow Amount within one (1) Business Day after receipt.

### 2.3 No Other Property

The Escrow Agent shall have no duties or obligations with respect to any property other than the funds actually deposited with it under Section 2.1 and the earnings on those funds.

### 2.4 No Security Interest

Except as expressly provided in this Escrow Agreement, neither Buyer nor Seller may sell, pledge, assign or otherwise transfer or encumber any interest in the Escrow Fund. The Escrow Agent shall have no lien, security interest or right of set-off against the Escrow Fund, other than as provided in Section 7.3.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF THE ESCROW FUND

### 3.1 Permitted Investment

The Escrow Agent shall invest and reinvest the Escrow Fund in the investment set forth on Schedule 2, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instructions. Absent any such direction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits.

### 3.2 Liquidation

The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as necessary to make any disbursement required under this Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss, penalty or decrease in value resulting from any investment made, or any liquidation made, in accordance with this Section 3.

### 3.3 Earnings

All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Fund shall become part of the Escrow Fund and shall be disbursed with it, except as provided in Section 6.2.

### 3.4 Acknowledgments

Buyer and Seller acknowledge that (a) the Escrow Agent does not provide investment advice; (b) investments are not deposits of, or guaranteed by, the Escrow Agent; and (c) the Escrow Agent may receive fees or other compensation from investment funds or their sponsors in which the Escrow Fund is invested, as disclosed on Schedule 2. The Escrow Agent shall provide monthly statements of the Escrow Account to Buyer and Seller.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

### 4.1 Disbursements Generally

The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds from the Escrow Fund only (a) in accordance with Joint Written Instructions, (b) in accordance with a Final Order, or (c) as otherwise expressly provided in this Escrow Agreement.

### 4.2 Claim Notices

At any time before 11:59 p.m. New York time on the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). A Claim Notice shall state that Buyer or another Buyer Indemnitee claims Losses under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, or an amount owed to Buyer under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement. It shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount claimed. Where the amount of a claim is not then reasonably determinable, the Claim Notice shall state Buyer's good-faith estimate of it.

### 4.3 Objection

Seller may object to all or any part of the claim in a Claim Notice by written notice to Buyer and the Escrow Agent (an "**Objection Notice**") delivered within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent's receipt of the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall specify the amount disputed and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

### 4.4 Undisputed Claims

If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the thirty-day period under Section 4.3, then on the fifth (5th) Business Day after that period ends, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Fund, the amount claimed in the Claim Notice. If the Escrow Agent receives an Objection Notice disputing only part of the claim, it shall disburse to Buyer the undisputed portion within five (5) Business Days after receipt of the Objection Notice.

### 4.5 Disputed Claims

The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any disputed portion of a claim until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order regarding that portion. It shall disburse that portion within five (5) Business Days after receiving those instructions or that order. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions promptly after any disputed claim is resolved by agreement, and in any event within five (5) Business Days after the resolution.

### 4.6 Working Capital Adjustment

If any amount is owed to Buyer under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement and Buyer elects to recover it from the Escrow Fund, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions directing payment of that amount within five (5) Business Days after the final determination of Closing Working Capital. If Seller fails to execute such instructions, Buyer may deliver a Final Order or a Claim Notice under Section 4.2.

### 4.7 Insufficient Funds

If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to pay any amount required to be disbursed to Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the balance of the Escrow Fund. The Escrow Agent shall have no further obligation as to the unpaid amount. Any such shortfall is a matter between Buyer and Seller under the Purchase Agreement.

---

## 5. RELEASE OF THE ESCROW FUND

### 5.1 Release on the Escrow Release Date

Within five (5) Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the balance of the Escrow Fund, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Escrow Release Date.

### 5.2 Retained Amounts

The Escrow Agent shall retain in the Escrow Fund an amount equal to the Pending Claim Amount. It shall disburse that amount, as each pending claim is resolved, in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Once all pending claims are resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse any remaining balance of the Escrow Fund to Seller.

### 5.3 Joint Release

Buyer and Seller may at any time direct, by Joint Written Instructions, the release of all or any part of the Escrow Fund to either of them or to any other Person.

### 5.4 Termination of Purchase Agreement

If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, Buyer and Seller shall promptly notify the Escrow Agent in writing. This Escrow Agreement shall then terminate without further action. If any funds have been deposited, the Escrow Agent shall return them to Buyer upon receipt of Joint Written Instructions.

## 6. TAX MATTERS

### 6.1 Tax Owner

Buyer and Seller agree that, for U.S. federal and applicable state and local income Tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund and shall report all income earned on it, until the Escrow Fund is disbursed. This treatment follows Proposed Treasury Regulations Section 1.468B-8. Each party shall file all Tax Returns consistently with this treatment, unless a final determination within the meaning of Section 1313(a) of the Code requires otherwise.

### 6.2 Tax Distributions

Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, upon Buyer's written request, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the earnings on the Escrow Fund, an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings attributed to Buyer for that quarter. These distributions are intended to fund Buyer's Taxes on those earnings. No such distribution may reduce the Escrow Fund below the Escrow Amount.

### 6.3 Tax Forms

Before the deposit of the Escrow Amount, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8. The Escrow Agent shall report income on the Escrow Fund on IRS Form 1099 or 1042-S, as applicable, consistently with Section 6.1.

### 6.4 Withholding

The Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any disbursement any Taxes it is required to withhold under applicable Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as disbursed to the Person in respect of whom the withholding was made.

### 6.5 Imputed Interest

To the extent any disbursement to Seller is treated as deferred consideration subject to imputed interest under Sections 483 or 1274 of the Code, Buyer and Seller shall be responsible for the reporting of that interest. The Escrow Agent shall have no responsibility for any such reporting.

## 7. THE ESCROW AGENT

### 7.1 Duties

The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Escrow Agreement. These duties are purely ministerial. No implied duties, fiduciary or otherwise, shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

### 7.2 Reliance; Limitation of Liability

(a) The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper Person. It need not inquire into the truth or accuracy of any statement in it.

(b) The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct, as determined by a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction.

(c) The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages, even if advised of their possibility.

(d) The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choosing. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with that counsel's advice.

(e) The Escrow Agent shall not be responsible for any delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, epidemic, failures of communications or utility services, or acts of any Governmental Authority. It shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance as soon as practicable.

### 7.3 Indemnification of the Escrow Agent

Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Escrow Agreement or the Escrow Agent's performance under it. This indemnity does not apply to the extent any such loss results from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear fifty percent (50%) of any amount paid under this Section 7.3, unless the amount arose from the act or omission of one of them, in which case that party shall bear it. The Escrow Agent may satisfy any amount owed to it under this Section 7.3 or Section 8 from the Escrow Fund, after ten (10) Business Days' written notice to Buyer and Seller, if the amount remains unpaid. Buyer and Seller shall restore any amount so taken to the Escrow Fund in the proportions set forth in this Section 7.3. This Section 7.3 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

### 7.4 Disputes

If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or is uncertain of its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an action in interpleader in a court described in Section 11.3 and deposit the Escrow Fund with that court. Upon such deposit, the Escrow Agent shall be relieved of all further obligations with respect to the deposited funds.

### 7.5 Compliance with Orders

The Escrow Agent may comply with any order, judgment or decree of any court or other Governmental Authority affecting the Escrow Fund. Such compliance shall not constitute a breach of this Escrow Agreement.

### 7.6 Resignation and Removal

(a) The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving it at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

(b) Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent within the notice period. The successor shall be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor is appointed within that period, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 11.3 to appoint one.

(c) The resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Fund to the successor upon its written acceptance of the appointment, less any unpaid amounts owed to it. Its duties under this Escrow Agreement shall then end. The successor shall have all the rights and duties of the Escrow Agent under this Escrow Agreement.

### 7.7 Merger or Consolidation

Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or with which it is consolidated, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, shall become the successor Escrow Agent without any further act.

The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3 and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in performing its duties under this Escrow Agreement. Buyer and Seller shall each pay fifty percent (50%) of those fees and expenses. The acceptance fee and the first annual administration fee shall be payable on the date of deposit of the Escrow Amount.

## 9. KNOW-YOUR-CUSTOMER; SECURITY PROCEDURES

### 9.1 Customer Identification

Buyer and Seller acknowledge that applicable Law, including the USA PATRIOT Act, requires the Escrow Agent to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information and documents it reasonably requests to satisfy those requirements. The Escrow Agent need not accept the deposit of the Escrow Amount until it has completed its customer due-diligence procedures.

### 9.2 Funds Transfer Instructions

Each funds-transfer instruction delivered to the Escrow Agent shall include the name of the beneficiary, its bank name and ABA routing number, and its account name and number. If the Escrow Agent receives any instruction other than in the form of a written instruction signed by Authorized Representatives, it may confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative listed on Schedule 1, other than the individual who delivered the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until the call-back is completed.

### 9.3 Reliance on Account Numbers

The Escrow Agent and the beneficiary's bank may rely solely on the account number or other identifying number in any funds-transfer instruction, even if it identifies a Person different from the named beneficiary. Buyer and Seller acknowledge that these security procedures are commercially reasonable.

## 11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 11.1 Termination

This Escrow Agreement shall terminate upon the disbursement of all funds in the Escrow Fund in accordance with its terms, subject to the survival of Sections 7.3 and 8.

### 11.2 Governing Law

This Escrow Agreement, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of New York. No choice-of-law or conflict-of-law rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction shall be given effect.

### 11.3 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial

(a) Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, in any action arising out of or relating to this Escrow Agreement. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any defense of inconvenient forum.

(b) EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT.

### 11.4 Assignment

No party may assign this Escrow Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other parties, except as provided in Sections 7.6 and 7.7. This Escrow Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### 11.5 Amendment and Waiver

This Escrow Agreement may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No failure or delay in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver of it.

### 11.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries

Nothing in this Escrow Agreement confers any right, benefit or remedy on any Person other than the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### 11.7 Severability

If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The provision held invalid shall remain effective in every other jurisdiction.

### 11.8 Publicity

No party shall use the name of the Escrow Agent in any publicity material without the Escrow Agent's prior written consent, except as required by Law.

### 11.9 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures

This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in portable document format, or by an electronic signature service, shall be deemed original signatures for all purposes.
