# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 6, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and among the following parties (each, a "**Party**" and together, the "**Parties**"):

- **Bisart AgTech Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Bisart**");
- **Bramand Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Bramand**"); and
- **Furrowline Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Bramand ("**Furrowline**").

Bramand and Furrowline are referred to together as the "**Furrowline Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Furrowline designs, manufactures, sells, leases and services precision-agriculture spray and survey drones, together with related flight-planning, telemetry and ground-station software and services.

B. Bisart and Bramand wish to evaluate, negotiate and, if they so agree, consummate a possible negotiated transaction under which Bisart would acquire Furrowline, whether by purchase of equity, purchase of assets or otherwise (the "**Potential Transaction**").

C. In connection with the Potential Transaction, each Party expects to disclose to the others certain information that is non-public, confidential or proprietary. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and the disclosure of information under it, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 Defined Terms

The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person. "Control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct the management and policies of a Person, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise. For purposes of this Agreement, Furrowline is an Affiliate of Bramand, and no portfolio company of any investment fund is an Affiliate of a Party solely by reason of common investment.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Clean Team Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Confidential Information**" means all information, in any form or medium, whether written, oral, electronic, visual or otherwise, that a Disclosing Party or any of its Representatives furnishes or makes available to a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives, whether before, on or after the Effective Date, in connection with the Potential Transaction. Confidential Information includes the following:

(a) business plans, strategies, budgets, forecasts and projections;

(b) financial statements, accounting records, tax information and banking and financing arrangements;

(c) customer, grower, cooperative, dealer and supplier lists, and the terms of dealings with any of them, including pricing, discounts, rebates, acreage serviced and order volumes;

(d) source code, object code, firmware, software architecture, algorithms, flight-control logic, data models and technical documentation;

(e) product designs, engineering drawings, payload and nozzle specifications, calibration data, test results and product roadmaps;

(f) information concerning regulatory authorizations, filings and correspondence with any Governmental Authority, including the Federal Aviation Administration and the United States Environmental Protection Agency;

(g) personnel information, including compensation and benefits data;

(h) the terms of any Contract to which the Disclosing Party or any of its Affiliates is a party;

(i) all notes, analyses, compilations, studies, forecasts, interpretations and other documents prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent containing, reflecting or derived from any of the foregoing (together, "**Derivative Materials**"); and

(j) Transaction Information.

"**Contract**" means any written or oral contract, lease, license, indenture, note, commitment, purchase order or other legally binding arrangement.

"**Data Room**" means the virtual data room established by or on behalf of the Furrowline Parties for the Potential Transaction, and any successor or supplemental electronic repository.

"**Definitive Agreement**" means a final, written agreement providing for the Potential Transaction that has been executed and delivered by each Party to it. A Definitive Agreement does not include a letter of intent, term sheet, indication of interest or any other preliminary written agreement, or any written or oral acceptance of an offer or bid.

"**Disclosing Party**" means a Party that, directly or through any of its Representatives, discloses or makes available Confidential Information. Confidential Information disclosed by Bramand or by Furrowline is treated as disclosed by each of the Furrowline Parties.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, commission, court, tribunal, self-regulatory organization or instrumentality of any of them.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Receiving Party**" means a Party that, directly or through any of its Representatives, receives Confidential Information.

"**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their respective directors, managers, members, officers, employees, agents, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, consultants and, in the case of Bisart only, actual or prospective sources of debt financing who have been approved in writing by Bramand (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed), in each case only to the extent such Person actually receives Confidential Information from or on behalf of that Party.

"**Transaction Information**" means (a) the fact that Confidential Information has been made available to any Party, (b) the fact that discussions or negotiations are taking or have taken place concerning the Potential Transaction, and (c) any of the terms, conditions, status or other facts with respect to the Potential Transaction, including the identity of the Parties.

### 1.2 Interpretation

The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." The word "or" is not exclusive. References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

## 2. Exclusions

### 2.1 Excluded Information

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by reasonable written evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party or its Representatives, provided that the source is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party with respect to that information; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of, or reference to, the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

### 2.2 Combinations

Confidential Information shall not be deemed to fall within any exclusion in Section 2.1 merely because individual features or components of it fall within that exclusion, unless the combination itself and its principle of operation fall within that exclusion.

## 3. Use and Disclosure Restrictions

### 3.1 Permitted Use

Each Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating, financing and consummating the Potential Transaction (the "**Permitted Purpose**") and for no other purpose. Without limiting the preceding sentence, no Receiving Party shall use any Confidential Information to compete with the Disclosing Party, to solicit any customer, dealer or supplier of the Disclosing Party, or to design, develop or reverse-engineer any product, software or firmware.

### 3.2 Non-Disclosure

Each Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any Person, except as follows:

(a) to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Permitted Purpose, who have been informed of its confidential nature and who are directed to treat it in accordance with this Agreement;

(b) with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party; or

(c) as permitted by Section 5.

### 3.3 Responsibility for Representatives

Each Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives, as if that Representative were a party to this Agreement, except for any Representative that has entered into a separate confidentiality agreement directly with the Disclosing Party with respect to the Potential Transaction. Each Receiving Party shall, at its own expense, take all reasonable measures, including court proceedings, to restrain its Representatives from prohibited or unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

### 3.4 Standard of Care

Each Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. Each Receiving Party shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards against unauthorized access to, or use or disclosure of, Confidential Information in its possession.

### 3.5 Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure

Each Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing promptly, and in any event within three (3) Business Days, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or use or disclosure of, the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to regain possession of the information and to prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

### 3.6 Transaction Information

Without the prior written consent of the other Parties, no Party shall, and each Party shall direct its Representatives not to, disclose any Transaction Information to any Person other than its Representatives in accordance with Section 3.2(a), except as permitted by Section 5.

## 4. Competitively Sensitive Information

### 4.1 Designation

Because Bisart operates in the agricultural equipment and services markets, the Furrowline Parties may designate certain Confidential Information as "Clean Team Only" (such information, "**Clean Team Information**"). Clean Team Information may include customer-specific pricing, margins, acreage and application data, dealer terms, bid information, and product roadmaps for future periods. The Furrowline Parties shall make any designation in good faith, by marking the relevant documents or by placing them in a restricted folder of the Data Room.

### 4.2 Clean Team

Bisart may disclose Clean Team Information only to (a) its outside counsel, outside accountants and outside financial and technical advisors, and (b) those employees of Bisart who are identified in a written list delivered to and approved by Bramand (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed) and who do not have direct responsibility for pricing, sales, marketing or dealer relations for products or services that compete with those of Furrowline (together, the "**Clean Team**"). Before receiving Clean Team Information, each member of the Clean Team who is an employee of Bisart shall acknowledge in writing that he or she has read and will comply with this Section 4.

### 4.3 Use of Clean Team Information

Members of the Clean Team may share with other Representatives of Bisart only summaries, analyses or conclusions derived from Clean Team Information that are aggregated or redacted so as not to disclose customer-specific, dealer-specific or forward-looking pricing information. Bisart shall not use Clean Team Information in connection with any pricing, bidding, sales or marketing decision for its own products or services.

### 4.4 Compliance with Antitrust Laws

Each Party shall conduct its exchange of information under this Agreement in compliance with applicable antitrust and competition laws. Nothing in this Agreement requires any Party to disclose information that it reasonably determines, upon advice of counsel, would be inappropriate to disclose under those laws.

## 5. Legally Required Disclosure

### 5.1 Compelled Disclosure

If a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, civil investigative demand or similar process, or by the rules of any securities exchange, to disclose any Confidential Information or Transaction Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the request or requirement, so that the Disclosing Party may, at its own expense, seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy or waive compliance with this Agreement; and

(b) cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in any effort to obtain such an order or remedy.

### 5.2 Scope of Permitted Disclosure

If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, or if the Disclosing Party waives compliance, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information or Transaction Information that it is advised by counsel is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to the information disclosed.

### 5.3 Routine Examinations

Notice under Section 5.1 is not required for any disclosure made to a banking, securities or other regulatory authority in the course of a routine examination or inquiry that is not directed at the Disclosing Party or the Potential Transaction, provided that the Receiving Party informs the authority of the confidential nature of the information.

## 6. Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

### 6.1 Return or Destruction

Within ten (10) Business Days after written request by a Disclosing Party, and in any event promptly after any Party notifies the others that it has decided not to proceed with the Potential Transaction, each Receiving Party shall, and shall direct its Representatives to, at the Receiving Party's election, return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or their possession, including all copies. Each Receiving Party shall destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an authorized officer of the Receiving Party shall certify in writing that it has complied with this Section 6.1.

### 6.2 Retained Copies

Notwithstanding Section 6.1, a Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain (a) copies of Confidential Information to the extent required to comply with applicable law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention or compliance policies, and (b) copies stored on automatic electronic archiving or backup systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course of business. Any Confidential Information retained under this Section 6.2 remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, regardless of the expiration of the term in Section 10.1.

### 6.3 Continuing Obligations

The return or destruction of Confidential Information does not relieve any Receiving Party or its Representatives of any obligation under this Agreement.

## 7. No Representations; No License; No Obligation

### 7.1 No Representations or Warranties

Each Party acknowledges that no Disclosing Party or any of its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information, and that none of them shall have any liability to any Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from the use of Confidential Information or any errors in or omissions from it, except as may be set forth in a Definitive Agreement. Only those representations and warranties that are made in a Definitive Agreement, when, as and if executed, and subject to the limitations and restrictions specified in it, shall have any legal effect.

### 7.2 No License

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants any Receiving Party or its Representatives any license, ownership interest or other right in or to any Confidential Information, or in or to any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, other than the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Permitted Purpose.

### 7.3 Source Code Review

Any review of source code or firmware of Furrowline shall take place only in a secure review environment provided by or on behalf of the Furrowline Parties, on terms reasonably specified by them. No Receiving Party or Representative shall copy, download, photograph, decompile or remove any source code or firmware from that environment, except for notes that do not reproduce code and that are treated as Derivative Materials.

### 7.4 No Obligation to Proceed

Unless and until a Definitive Agreement has been executed and delivered, no Party shall be under any legal obligation of any kind with respect to the Potential Transaction by virtue of this Agreement or any other written or oral expression with respect to the Potential Transaction, except for the matters specifically agreed to in this Agreement. Each Party may, in its sole discretion, terminate discussions and negotiations at any time and for any reason or no reason, by written notice to the other Parties.

### 7.5 Process

The Furrowline Parties may conduct any process for a transaction involving Furrowline as they determine in their sole discretion, including by negotiating with any other prospective counterparty and entering into a Definitive Agreement with any other Person without prior notice to Bisart, and may change any procedures relating to that process at any time without notice.

## 8. Non-Solicitation and Contacts

### 8.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees

For a period of twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, Bisart shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any officer, engineer or Remote Pilot-level operations employee of Furrowline with whom Bisart first had contact, or of whom Bisart first became aware, in connection with the Potential Transaction. This Section 8.1 does not restrict (a) general solicitations of employment, including through search firms, that are not specifically directed at those employees, or the hiring of any Person who responds to them, or (b) the solicitation or hiring of any Person whose employment with Furrowline ended at least six (6) months before the commencement of employment discussions.

### 8.2 Contacts

Without the prior written consent of Bramand, Bisart shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, initiate or maintain contact with any director, officer, employee, customer, dealer, supplier, licensor, lender or landlord of Furrowline regarding the Potential Transaction or Furrowline's business, except through the individuals designated in writing by Bramand for that purpose. This Section 8.2 does not restrict contacts made in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Potential Transaction and not involving the use of Confidential Information.

## 9. Remedies

### 9.1 Equitable Relief

Each Party acknowledges that money damages would not be a sufficient remedy for any breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party would suffer irreparable harm from such a breach. Each Disclosing Party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive or other equitable relief as a remedy for any breach or threatened breach, without proof of actual damages and without any requirement to post a bond or other security. These remedies are not exclusive and are in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 9.2 Costs of Enforcement

If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party or any of its Representatives has breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Parties for the reasonable and documented legal fees and expenses they incurred in connection with the litigation, including any appeal.

### 9.3 Limitation

No Party shall be liable under this Agreement for punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

---

## 10. Term

### 10.1 Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect until the date that is two (2) years after the Effective Date, except as follows:

(a) the obligations of each Receiving Party with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, including source code and firmware, shall continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret; and

(b) Section 6.2 shall continue as provided in it.

### 10.2 Definitive Agreement

If the Parties enter into a Definitive Agreement, the confidentiality and use obligations applicable to the Confidential Information after its execution shall be as set out in the Definitive Agreement, to the extent it addresses those obligations, and otherwise this Agreement shall continue to apply in accordance with its terms.

### 10.3 Accrued Rights

The expiration of this Agreement shall not affect any right or remedy in respect of any breach occurring before expiration.
