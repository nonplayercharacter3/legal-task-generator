# FORM OF BILL OF SALE, CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT

**AGREED FORM**

This BILL OF SALE, CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Bramand Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), in favor of **Furrowline Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller, Bisart AgTech Corp., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and the Company are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2026 (as amended, the "**SPA**"). Under the SPA, Buyer will purchase from Seller all issued and outstanding shares of Furrowline Robotics, Inc.

B. Section 6.9 of the SPA requires Seller, before the Closing, to contribute, assign, transfer and convey to the Company certain software and related assets held by Seller and its Affiliates (other than the Company), for no additional consideration, by executing and delivering this Bill of Sale.

C. Seller is the sole stockholder of the Company. Seller wishes to make the contribution described in this Bill of Sale as a contribution to the capital of the Company. The Company wishes to accept it on the terms of this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, Seller and the Company agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the SPA. The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Company Software**" means the software listed on Annex A, in source code and object code form, including all versions, releases, builds, branches, patches, updates, modifications and derivative works of it in existence on the Effective Date, whether completed or in development.

"**Contributed Materials**" means, for the Company Software, all (a) technical, functional, design and user documentation, specifications, flowcharts, architecture diagrams and release notes; (b) data models, schemas, algorithms, configuration files, build scripts, test suites, test data and calibration tables; (c) databases and compilations embodied in or used to operate it; and (d) development notes, issue-tracker records and bug reports. In each case these are the materials in the possession or control of Seller or any Seller Affiliate.

"**Conveyed Assets**" means the Company Software, the Contributed Materials, the Software IP Rights and the Software Records, as described in Section 2.1.

"**Repositories**" means the source-code repositories, artifact registries, continuous-integration environments and cloud development accounts identified in Annex B, and all content of them relating to the Company Software.

"**Seller Affiliate**" means each Affiliate of Seller other than the Company.

"**Software IP Rights**" means all intellectual property rights anywhere in the world in and to the Company Software and the Contributed Materials. These include (a) copyrights and copyright registrations and applications; (b) trade secrets and confidential know-how; (c) any patent rights claiming any method, process or apparatus embodied in it; and (d) all moral rights, to the extent assignable.

"**Software Records**" means all books, records, files, correspondence and contributor agreements held by Seller or any Seller Affiliate that relate primarily to the development, ownership, licensing or maintenance of the Company Software. This includes any invention assignment or work-made-for-hire agreements with employees, consultants or contractors who contributed to it.

## 2. Contribution and Conveyance

### 2.1 Conveyance

Effective as of the Effective Date, Seller, for itself and on behalf of each Seller Affiliate, contributes, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company all of its and their right, title and interest in, to and under the following, free and clear of all Liens:

(a) the Company Software;

(b) the Contributed Materials;

(c) the Software IP Rights, including all registrations of and applications for them, and all renewals, extensions, restorations and reversions of them;

(d) the Software Records;

(e) all rights to sue for and collect damages and other relief for any past, present or future infringement, misappropriation, dilution or other violation of any Software IP Rights, and all proceeds of such rights; and

(f) all income, royalties, fees and payments due or payable on or after the Effective Date under any license or other grant of rights in the Company Software.

### 2.2 Acceptance

The Company accepts the contribution, assignment, transfer and conveyance of the Conveyed Assets under Section 2.1.

### 2.3 Seller Affiliates

Seller represents that it has full authority to convey, on behalf of each Seller Affiliate, all Conveyed Assets held by that Seller Affiliate. To the extent any Seller Affiliate holds record title to any Conveyed Asset in a manner that requires a separate instrument of transfer, Seller shall cause that Seller Affiliate to execute and deliver to the Company, on or before the Effective Date, a joinder to this Bill of Sale in the form attached as Annex C.

### 2.4 Recordation

Seller authorizes the Company and its designees to record this Bill of Sale, or a short-form assignment consistent with it, with the United States Copyright Office, the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any other registry or Governmental Authority. The purpose is to evidence the Company's ownership of the Software IP Rights. Seller shall execute any short-form assignment reasonably requested by the Company for that purpose.

## 3. Delivery of Materials

### 3.1 Source Code and Object Code

On or before the Effective Date, Seller shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to the Company the following:

(a) a complete and current copy of the source code of the Company Software, including all branches and tagged releases, in the native repository format with full commit history preserved;

(b) the object code of each currently supported release of the Company Software, together with signing keys, certificates and the build tool chain needed to compile, link and sign it; and

(c) the Contributed Materials, in electronic form where available.

### 3.2 Repositories and Credentials

On or before the Effective Date, Seller shall take one of the following steps for each Repository. Seller shall either transfer the Repository's administrative ownership to an account designated by the Company, or, where transfer is not technically available, give the Company full administrative credentials and remove all users not designated by the Company. Seller shall deliver to the Company a written list of all credentials, API keys, tokens and secrets associated with the Company Software that are held by Seller or any Seller Affiliate. Seller shall also confirm in writing that it has given the Company each such credential, or has revoked it at the Company's request.

### 3.3 Retained Copies

After the Effective Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause each Seller Affiliate not to, keep any copy of the source code of the Company Software. Seller shall permanently delete all such copies from systems it controls, except (a) automatically generated backup copies that are not readily accessible and are deleted in the ordinary course, and (b) copies kept as required by Law. Any copy so kept remains subject to Section 6.6 of the SPA.

### 3.4 Knowledge Transfer

For ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, Seller shall make available to the Company, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, the employees and contractors of Seller and the Seller Affiliates who have material knowledge of the design, architecture, build process or deployment of the Company Software. The purpose is to answer the Company's reasonable questions about it. This assistance is limited to no more than forty (40) hours per individual and is provided at no additional charge to the Company.

## ANNEX A. COMPANY SOFTWARE

The following software, together with all component modules, libraries, services and interfaces listed below, constitutes the Company Software.

### 1. Telemetry Dashboard

**Name:** Furrowline Telemetry Dashboard (internal designation "FleetView")

**Description:** This is a web-based and mobile fleet-monitoring application. It ingests real-time and logged flight telemetry from Company drones and displays aircraft position, battery state, payload status, tank level, nozzle flow and fault codes. It also generates post-flight operational reports for operators and dealers.

**Components:**

(a) FleetView web client (TypeScript/React), all releases through v5.2;

(b) FleetView mobile clients for iOS and Android, all releases through v5.1;

(c) Telemetry ingestion service and message broker configuration;

(d) Fleet records database schema and migration scripts;

(e) Alerting and fault-notification service;

(f) Operator and dealer account management and permissions module;

(g) Report generator and export module (PDF and CSV); and

(h) Public and partner application programming interfaces, with related API documentation.

### 2. Ground-Station Firmware

**Name:** Furrowline Ground-Station Firmware (GCS-series)

**Description:** This is the embedded firmware and companion software for the Company's handheld and vehicle-mounted ground control stations. It manages the command-and-control radio link, operator inputs, manual and assisted flight modes, geofence loading, and the relay of telemetry between aircraft and the Telemetry Dashboard.

**Components:**

(a) GCS-H handheld controller firmware, all releases of v3.x and v4.x;

(b) GCS-V vehicle-mount base-station firmware, all releases of v3.x and v4.x;

(c) Radio link management and frequency-hopping modules;

(d) Operator display and input-handling software;

(e) Geofence loader and local airspace overlay module;

(f) Bootloader, secure-boot configuration and firmware update utility; and

(g) Factory provisioning and test utilities for ground control stations.

### 3. Ancillary Tools

(a) Payload calibration utility for spray-boom and spreader payloads, used in production and field service;

(b) Dealer service and diagnostic tool (Windows desktop application), all releases through v2.4; and

(c) Internal build, packaging and release automation scripts for the items in Paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Annex A.

### 4. Third-Party Components

The Company Software includes third-party and open-source components incorporated under their applicable licenses. Those components are conveyed only to the extent of Seller's and the Seller Affiliates' rights in them. For the avoidance of confusion, the Company Software does not include the flight-control autopilot firmware licensed to the Company by Kestrune Avionics GmbH. The Company already holds that firmware under its own license.
