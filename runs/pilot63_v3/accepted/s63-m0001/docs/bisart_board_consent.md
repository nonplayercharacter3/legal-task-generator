# UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BISART AGTECH CORP.

**(A Delaware Corporation)**

**Dated as of February 27, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of **Bisart AgTech Corp.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), consent to and adopt the following resolutions. They act without a meeting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and Section 3.9 of the Amended and Restated Bylaws of the Corporation (the "**Bylaws**"). The resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted by unanimous vote at a duly called and held meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

---

## 1. Background

### 1.1 The Proposed Acquisition

Management of the Corporation has presented to the Board a proposal (the "**Acquisition**") under which the Corporation would acquire from **Bramand Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), All issued and outstanding shares of Furrowline Robotics, Inc. (the "**Shares**"). **Furrowline Robotics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Furrowline**"), designs, manufactures, sells, leases and services precision-agriculture spray and survey drones, together with related flight-planning, telemetry and ground-station software and services.

### 1.2 Strategic Rationale

Management has advised the Board that the Acquisition would do the following:

(a) give the Corporation an established unmanned aerial systems platform for agricultural spraying, seeding and surveying;

(b) add to the Corporation's precision-agriculture offering a manufacturing and flight-test operation located in Salina, Kansas;

(c) add Furrowline's dealer network to the Corporation's existing distribution channels; and

(d) allow the Corporation to offer drone-based application and survey services to its existing grower and cooperative customers.

### 1.3 Board Review

The Board, together with its Strategy and Transactions Committee (the "**Committee**"), has reviewed the Acquisition at Board meetings held on November 18, 2025, January 13, 2026 and February 24, 2026. At those meetings, management, the Corporation's outside legal counsel, Wexford Lyle LLP ("**Counsel**"), and the Corporation's financial and accounting advisors made presentations. The Board and the Committee received and reviewed the following:

(a) the draft Stock Purchase Agreement among the Corporation, Seller and, for limited purposes, Furrowline, in the form circulated to the Board on February 23, 2026 (as it may be modified under Section 3.2 of this Consent, the "**Purchase Agreement**");

(b) the agreed form of Escrow Agreement among the Corporation, Seller and Lindqvist Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"), attached to the Purchase Agreement as Exhibit B (the "**Escrow Agreement**");

(c) the agreed form of Bill of Sale, Contribution and Assignment by Seller in favor of Furrowline, attached to the Purchase Agreement as Exhibit A (the "**Bill of Sale**");

(d) a summary of the principal terms of the Purchase Agreement prepared by Counsel;

(e) the report of management and the Corporation's advisors on their financial, accounting, tax, commercial, technical and legal due diligence on Furrowline;

(f) the financial model for the Acquisition prepared by management, including projections of Furrowline's net revenue for the fiscal years 2026 through 2028; and

(g) management's integration plan for Furrowline.

### 1.4 Committee Recommendation

On February 24, 2026, the Committee unanimously recommended that the Board approve the Acquisition substantially on the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Principal Terms of the Acquisition

The Board notes the following principal terms of the Purchase Agreement. This summary is for the Board's convenience only. The terms of the Purchase Agreement, as executed, govern.

### 2.1 Purchase Price

(a) The Corporation will pay a base purchase price of $42,000,000 (the "**Base Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment for Furrowline's closing working capital against a target of $3,850,000.

(b) The Corporation will also pay Seller an earn-out payment based on Furrowline's net revenue for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2027. The maximum earn-out payment is $6,000,000 (the "**Maximum Earn-Out Payment**").

### 2.2 Payments at Closing

At the closing of the Acquisition (the "**Closing**"), the Corporation will pay the Base Purchase Price, as estimated and adjusted, as follows:

(a) $4,200,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") to the Escrow Agent, for deposit under the Escrow Agreement;

(b) on behalf of Furrowline, the payoff amount of Furrowline's indebtedness identified in the Purchase Agreement;

(c) on behalf of Furrowline, unpaid transaction expenses; and

(d) the balance to Seller.

### 2.3 Closing

The Closing is scheduled for September 15, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the closing conditions in Article VII of the Purchase Agreement. Either party may terminate the Purchase Agreement if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 15, 2026.

### 2.4 Indemnification and Escrow

Seller's indemnification obligations are set out in Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, and the Escrow Amount secures them. Seller's liability for breaches of representations and warranties, other than fundamental representations and fraud, is limited to $4,200,000 (10% of Base Purchase Price). Article IX also contains indemnification obligations of the Corporation in favor of Seller.

### 2.5 Software Contribution

Before the Closing, Seller will convey to Furrowline the software it and its affiliates hold for use in Furrowline's business, by executing and delivering the Bill of Sale.

### 2.6 Seller Guarantee

Seller has guaranteed Furrowline's obligations to Fallowgate Equipment Finance LLC under Furrowline's drone fleet financing. Under Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement, the Corporation will use commercially reasonable efforts to procure Seller's release from that guarantee. These efforts include offering a replacement guarantee from the Corporation or one of its affiliates on substantially the same terms.

### 2.7 Restrictive Covenants

Seller will be bound by non-competition and non-solicitation covenants for four (4) years after the Closing.

### 2.8 Governing Law and Forum

The Purchase Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of New York, with exclusive jurisdiction in the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan.

## 3. Approval of the Purchase Agreement and the Acquisition

### 3.1 Determination and Approval

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has determined that the Acquisition, the Purchase Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the other transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement (collectively, the "**Transactions**") are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders. On that basis, the Board approves and adopts the Purchase Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transactions in all respects.

### 3.2 Execution and Delivery

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each of the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Corporation (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

(a) The Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement shall be substantially in the forms presented to the Board.

(b) An Authorized Officer may approve changes to those forms that the Authorized Officer, after consultation with Counsel, determines to be necessary, appropriate or advisable and not materially adverse to the Corporation.

(c) The Authorized Officer's execution and delivery of a document shall be conclusive evidence of that determination.

(d) No such change may do any of the following without further approval of the Board:

(i) increase the Base Purchase Price;

(ii) increase the Maximum Earn-Out Payment; or

(iii) reduce the Escrow Amount.

### 3.3 Acknowledgment of the Bill of Sale

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to acknowledge the Bill of Sale at or before the Closing. The Bill of Sale shall be substantially in the agreed form attached to the Purchase Agreement as Exhibit A, with such changes as an Authorized Officer may approve under Section 3.2. The Authorized Officer may also exercise and enforce the rights of the Corporation as a third-party beneficiary of the Bill of Sale.

### 3.4 Ancillary Documents

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to execute and deliver each other agreement, certificate, instrument and document that the Purchase Agreement contemplates the Corporation will execute or deliver (together with the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement, the "**Transaction Documents**"). This includes the following:

(a) the certificate of the Secretary of the Corporation required by Section 3.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the officer's certificate required by Section 7.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(c) funds-flow memoranda and wire instructions;

(d) the certificate of incumbency and other documents required by the Escrow Agent; and

(e) IRS Form W-9 of the Corporation.

## 4. Purchase Price and Funding

### 4.1 Payment of the Purchase Price

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation is authorized to pay the Base Purchase Price at the Closing, as adjusted under the Purchase Agreement, in the manner set out in Section 2.2 of the Purchase Agreement. This includes deposit of the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent.

### 4.2 Source of Funds

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporation shall fund the amounts payable at the Closing from its cash on hand. The Chief Financial Officer is authorized and directed to cause sufficient immediately available funds to be held in the Corporation's operating accounts not later than two (2) Business Days before the scheduled Closing Date.

### 4.3 Post-Closing Adjustments and Earn-Out

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporation is authorized to pay to Seller the following:

(a) any amount payable by the Corporation under the working capital adjustment provisions of Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(b) any earn-out payment that becomes payable under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, up to the Maximum Earn-Out Payment.

The Chief Financial Officer shall cause Furrowline's net revenue to be tracked and reported, and shall cause the quarterly reports and the earn-out statement required by Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement to be prepared and delivered when due.

### 4.4 Reserve

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Financial Officer is authorized and directed to establish such accounting reserve for the earn-out as is required by generally accepted accounting principles. The Chief Financial Officer shall report to the Audit Committee of the Board each quarter on the estimated fair value of the earn-out liability.

### 4.5 Escrow Agent Fees

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporation is authorized to pay its share of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent, as provided in Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 8 of the Escrow Agreement.

## 5. Replacement Guarantee

### 5.1 Authority to Offer and Deliver

**RESOLVED**, that, for purposes of Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement, each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to take the following actions:

(a) negotiate with Fallowgate Equipment Finance LLC;

(b) offer to Fallowgate Equipment Finance LLC a guarantee from the Corporation of Furrowline's obligations under the Master Equipment Finance Agreement, dated as of October 14, 2023, between Fallowgate Equipment Finance LLC and Furrowline, on substantially the same terms as Seller's existing guaranty; and

(c) execute and deliver such guarantee, effective as of the Closing or as promptly as practicable afterward, against the release of Seller.

### 5.2 Limit on Authority

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the guaranteed principal amount under any guarantee delivered under Section 5.1 shall not exceed the outstanding principal balance under that financing at the time of delivery, together with interest, fees and costs of enforcement. Any guarantee on terms beyond those described in Section 5.1 requires further approval of the Board.

## 6. Closing Matters

### 6.1 Closing Conditions

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to take the following actions:

(a) determine whether each condition to the Corporation's obligation to consummate the Closing under Section 7.1 and Section 7.2 of the Purchase Agreement has been satisfied; and

(b) waive any such condition, other than any condition that, in the judgment of that Authorized Officer after consultation with Counsel, is material to the Corporation.

A waiver of a condition material to the Corporation requires the prior approval of the Committee.

### 6.2 Extension of the Closing Date

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to agree with Seller in writing to a time, date or place of Closing other than that provided in Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement. No Authorized Officer may agree to extend the Outside Date under Section 8.1(d) of the Purchase Agreement without the prior approval of the Committee.

### 6.3 Termination

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Corporation may terminate the Purchase Agreement under Section 8.1 of the Purchase Agreement only with the prior approval of the Board or the Committee. As the sole exception, an Authorized Officer may terminate the Purchase Agreement under Section 8.1(d) after the Outside Date has passed, upon notice to the Board.

### 7.1 Authority

(a) negotiate, execute and deliver any amendment, supplement or modification of the Purchase Agreement or any other Transaction Document, including any letter agreement under Section 11.6 of the Purchase Agreement; and

## 8. Furrowline Governance After Closing

### 8.1 Stockholder Action

**RESOLVED**, that, effective upon the Closing, the Corporation, as the sole stockholder of Furrowline, is authorized to take the following actions by written consent of the sole stockholder:

(a) elect Teodora Halvorsen, Marcus Delacroix-Venn and Priya Okonkwo-Lane as the directors of Furrowline, to fill the vacancies created by the resignations delivered under the Purchase Agreement, each to serve until a successor is duly elected and qualified; and

(b) take such other action as an Authorized Officer considers appropriate to establish the governance of Furrowline as a wholly owned subsidiary of the Corporation.

### 8.2 Directors and Officers of Furrowline

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board identifies the directors and officers of Furrowline whose resignations are to be delivered at the Closing under the Purchase Agreement as follows:

(a) each director of Furrowline; and

(b) each officer of Furrowline other than the President.

The Board authorizes the Chief Executive Officer to confirm that designation to Seller in writing.

### 8.3 Insurance

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Financial Officer is authorized and directed to cause Furrowline to be added, effective as of the Closing, as an insured under the Corporation's directors' and officers' liability, general liability and other insurance programs, as appropriate. The Chief Financial Officer shall also confirm, before the Closing, the continuation of Furrowline's aviation hull and liability and product liability coverage.

### 8.4 Regulatory Filings

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to cause Furrowline to prepare and submit any filing required to update Furrowline's registrations and operating authorizations with the Federal Aviation Administration following the Closing.

### 8.5 Employees and Integration

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer is authorized to implement management's integration plan for Furrowline. This includes taking the actions required by Article X of the Purchase Agreement with respect to continuing employees of Furrowline. The Chief Executive Officer shall report to the Board on the progress of integration at each regular meeting of the Board during the twelve (12) months after the Closing.
