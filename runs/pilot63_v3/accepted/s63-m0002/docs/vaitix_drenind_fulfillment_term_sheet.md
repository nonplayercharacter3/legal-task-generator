# INDICATIVE TERM SHEET

**Warehousing, E-Commerce Fulfillment and Returns Processing Services**

**Vaitix Outdoor Co. / Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC**

**Date:** January 17, 2025

**Status:** Non-binding except as stated in Section 15

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND STATUS OF THIS TERM SHEET

1.1 **Purpose.** This term sheet (this "**Term Sheet**") summarizes the principal commercial terms on which Vaitix Outdoor Co. ("**Vaitix**") proposes to engage Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC ("**Drenind**") to provide warehousing, e-commerce fulfillment and returns processing services. Vaitix and Drenind are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

1.2 **Basis of Discussions.** The terms below reflect the Parties' discussions following Drenind's response to Vaitix's request for proposal for third-party logistics services, the site visit to Drenind's Columbus, Ohio facility, and the commercial negotiation sessions held in December 2024 and January 2025. They are intended to guide preparation of the Definitive Agreement described in Section 2.

1.3 **Non-Binding Nature.** Except for the provisions listed in Section 15.1 (the "**Binding Provisions**"), this Term Sheet is a statement of intent only. It does not create any legally binding obligation on either Party. Neither Party is obliged to enter into any agreement or transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreement has been negotiated, approved and executed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

## 2. DEFINITIVE DOCUMENTATION

2.1 **Structure.** The Parties intend to document the engagement through:

(a) a Master Services Agreement setting out the general legal and commercial terms of the relationship (the "**MSA**");

(b) one or more Order Forms under the MSA describing the specific services, facilities, fees, service levels and commercial commitments for each scope of work (each, an "**Order Form**");

(c) a Quality Agreement setting out quality standards, inspection procedures, handling requirements and related operational requirements (the "**Quality Agreement**"); and

(d) a parent guaranty from Drenind's parent company, Drenind Logistics Group, Inc., of Drenind's obligations under the MSA (the "**Parent Guaranty**").

The MSA, the initial Order Form, the Quality Agreement and the Parent Guaranty are referred to together as the "**Definitive Agreement**."

2.2 **First Drafts.** Drenind will prepare the first draft of the MSA and the initial Order Form, based on this Term Sheet, and will circulate them to Vaitix no later than January 31, 2025. Vaitix will prepare the first draft of the Quality Agreement. Drenind will procure that Drenind Logistics Group, Inc. provides its form of guaranty for Vaitix's review at the same time as the MSA draft.

2.3 **Target Signing.** The Parties are targeting execution of the MSA and the initial Order Form on or about March 1, 2025, with the Quality Agreement and the Parent Guaranty executed concurrently or as soon as practicable afterward.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

3.1 **Core Services.** Drenind will provide the following services for Vaitix's outdoor apparel, footwear, equipment and accessories (the "**Products**"):

(a) inbound receiving, including container and LTL receipt, carton and unit counting, inspection against advance shipping notices and discrepancy reporting;

(b) put-away, storage and inventory management, including slotting, cycle counting and an annual physical inventory on request;

(c) pick, pack and ship for direct-to-consumer orders placed through Vaitix's own e-commerce website and connected marketplace storefronts;

(d) value-added services, including gift wrapping, kitting, insert placement, re-labeling, hang-tagging and light product preparation;

(e) returns processing, including receipt, inspection, grading, refurbishment where specified, restocking and disposition in accordance with Vaitix's returns policy; and

(f) transportation management for outbound parcel shipments, including carrier selection, rate shopping and tracking data integration.

3.2 **Systems Integration.** Drenind will integrate its warehouse management system with Vaitix's e-commerce platform and enterprise resource planning system. The integration will provide order download, inventory synchronization, shipment confirmation, tracking number upload and returns data feeds. Vaitix will have real-time, read-only access to inventory and order status data through Drenind's customer portal.

3.3 **Facility.** The services will initially be performed at Drenind's fulfillment center at 4850 Logistics Way, Groveport, Ohio 43125. Drenind will not move Products to another facility without Vaitix's prior written approval, except temporarily in an emergency.

3.4 **Peak-Season Capacity.** Vaitix will provide a non-binding forecast of peak-season volumes each year. Drenind will use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the labor, space and equipment required to meet forecast volumes. The Parties will discuss arrangements for peak-season overflow capacity, which may be documented separately after the MSA is signed.

3.5 **Excluded Services.** Unless later agreed in an Order Form, the services do not include customs brokerage, freight forwarding, international outbound shipping, or the handling of hazardous materials beyond limited-quantity consumer goods properly identified by Vaitix.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Agreed Exclusivity Scope.** During the term of the MSA, Vaitix will use Drenind on an exclusive basis for direct-to-consumer e-commerce fulfillment in the continental United States only.

4.2 **Retained Channels and Territories.** The following are outside the exclusivity and Vaitix remains free to use any provider, or to perform the services itself:

(a) wholesale distribution to retail and specialty accounts;

(b) replenishment of Vaitix-owned or partner retail stores;

(c) fulfillment of orders shipped to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories or any destination outside the United States;

(d) Vaitix's existing logistics arrangements in Canada and the European Union; and

(e) marketplace orders fulfilled by the marketplace operator's own fulfillment network.

4.3 **New Channels.** Vaitix will notify Drenind of any new direct-to-consumer channel or product line intended for the continental United States. The Parties will negotiate in good faith to add the related services to an Order Form.

4.4 **No Exclusivity by Drenind.** Drenind may provide services to other customers, provided that it complies with the confidentiality and data protection terms of the MSA and does not commingle the Products with goods of any other person.

## 5. TERM

5.1 **Initial Term.** The MSA will commence on its signing date. The initial term will run until the end of the minimum service commitment period under the initial Order Form.

5.2 **Commitment Period.** The minimum service commitment period, and the associated minimum monthly fee, will be set out in the initial Order Form. They will be confirmed once implementation timing and the Go-Live Date are finalized.

5.3 **Renewal.** Renewal mechanics, including the length of any renewal period and the notice required to prevent renewal, will be addressed in the Definitive Agreement.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION

6.1 **Implementation Plan.** Drenind will prepare a detailed implementation plan covering systems integration, slotting design, packaging and labeling set-up, standard operating procedures, staff training and acceptance testing. Drenind will deliver the plan within fifteen (15) business days after signing of the MSA.

6.2 **Target Go-Live.** The Parties are targeting a Go-Live Date in the second quarter of 2025, with live order processing to begin only after successful completion of acceptance testing confirmed in writing by both Parties.

6.3 **Implementation Fee.** Drenind will charge a one-time implementation fee as indicated in Annex A. The fee will be invoiced 50% on signing of the initial Order Form and 50% on the Go-Live Date.

6.4 **Inventory Transfer.** Vaitix will be responsible for the cost of transporting existing Product inventory to the Groveport facility. Drenind will provide receiving labor for the initial inventory transfer at the standard receiving rates in Annex A.

## 7. PRICING

7.1 **Indicative Rates.** Indicative fees are set out in Annex A. Final fees will be set out in the initial Order Form.

7.2 **Pass-Through Charges.** Parcel carrier charges and other agreed third-party charges will be passed through at Drenind's actual cost without mark-up, with supporting documentation available on request. Vaitix will receive the benefit of carrier discounts and rebates attributable to its shipments, as specified in the Order Form.

7.3 **Rate Adjustments.** The Definitive Agreement will include a rate adjustment mechanism. Drenind will be able to propose adjustments by written notice no more than once per contract year, with no adjustment during the first year after the Go-Live Date. Vaitix will have a right to object to a proposed adjustment. The specific notice, objection and negotiation periods will be set out in the MSA.

7.4 **Minimum Monthly Fee.** A minimum monthly fee will apply from the third full calendar month after the Go-Live Date. Its amount will be set out in the initial Order Form and is expected to be within the range indicated in Annex A.

7.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use and similar transaction taxes, which will be stated separately on Drenind's invoices where properly chargeable.

## 8. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

8.1 **Invoicing.** Drenind will invoice monthly in arrears, itemized by service category and activity unit, with supporting activity data.

8.2 **Payment Terms.** Vaitix will pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days after receipt.

8.3 **Disputes.** Vaitix may withhold amounts disputed in good faith if it pays the undisputed portion. Drenind will not suspend services because of a good-faith dispute.

8.4 **Audit.** Vaitix will have customary rights to audit Drenind's records relating to fees and pass-through charges, no more than once per contract year except following a material discrepancy.

## 9. SERVICE LEVELS AND QUALITY

9.1 **Key Performance Indicators.** The Parties expect the initial Order Form and the Quality Agreement to include, at a minimum, the following service levels:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Dock-to-stock (inbound receipts processed and available to sell) | 48 hours from receipt for 98% of units |
| Same-day ship (orders received by 2:00 p.m. Eastern) | 99.5% |
| Order accuracy | 99.8% |
| Inventory accuracy (cycle count, unit level) | 99.7% |
| Returns processed and data transmitted to Vaitix | 72 hours from receipt for 97% of units |
| Portal and integration availability | 99.5% per month, excluding scheduled maintenance |

9.2 **Service Credits.** Service credits will apply for failure to meet critical service levels, at amounts to be agreed in the Order Form. Service credits will not be Vaitix's exclusive remedy except where the Order Form expressly says so.

9.3 **Chronic Failure.** Repeated failure to meet critical service levels will be treated as a material breach entitling Vaitix to terminate. The Definitive Agreement will set out the thresholds.

9.4 **Root Cause and Corrective Action.** Drenind will provide root cause analysis and a corrective action plan for any monthly service level failure.

9.5 **Quality Agreement.** The Quality Agreement will cover receiving inspection, storage conditions, handling of technical apparel and footwear, packaging and branding standards, product holds and recalls, cycle count procedures, returns grading criteria and reporting.

## 10. INVENTORY, TITLE AND RISK OF LOSS

10.1 **Title.** Title to all Products will remain with Vaitix at all times. Products will be segregated and clearly identified as Vaitix property.

10.2 **Lien Waiver.** Drenind will waive any warehouse or other possessory lien over the Products and Vaitix's data, to the extent permitted by law, and will not withhold Products for any reason, including fee disputes.

10.3 **Risk of Loss.** Drenind will bear the risk of loss of and damage to Products from delivery to the facility until tender to the outbound carrier, except where caused by Vaitix or by force majeure.

10.4 **Shrinkage.** Drenind will reimburse inventory shrinkage, loss or damage above an allowance to be set out in the Order Form, at Vaitix's landed cost. The Parties' working assumption is an annual shrinkage allowance of not more than 0.10% of units received.

10.5 **Recalls and Holds.** Drenind will promptly comply with Vaitix's hold, quarantine and recall instructions.

## 11. DATA, CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SECURITY

11.1 **Ownership of Data.** Vaitix will own all order, inventory, shipment, returns and end-customer data. Drenind will use that data only to perform the services.

11.2 **Personal Information.** Drenind will act as a service provider and processor for Vaitix's end-customer personal information and will comply with applicable privacy laws. The Parties will enter into any additional data processing terms required by law.

11.3 **Security.** Drenind will maintain a written information security program consistent with recognized industry standards. It will provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent on request and will notify Vaitix of security incidents within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of them.

11.4 **Confidentiality.** The MSA will include customary mutual confidentiality obligations. Until the MSA is signed, the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement between the Parties continues to govern information exchanged in connection with this engagement.

## 12. LIABILITY, INDEMNITIES AND INSURANCE

12.1 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate annual liability will be capped at the greater of the fees paid and payable in the preceding twelve (12) months and US$2,000,000. Customary exceptions will apply, including indemnities, data and confidentiality breaches, gross negligence, fraud and willful misconduct, loss of or damage to Products, wrongful refusal to release Products or data, and payment of fees properly due.

12.2 **Consequential Damages.** Customary mutual exclusion of indirect and consequential damages, subject to the same exceptions.

12.3 **Indemnities.** Drenind will indemnify Vaitix for third-party claims arising from bodily injury and property damage caused by Drenind, data and confidentiality breaches, intellectual property infringement by Drenind's systems, Drenind's violations of law and claims by Drenind's personnel. Vaitix will indemnify Drenind for third-party claims arising from bodily injury and property damage caused by Vaitix personnel at the facility, intellectual property infringement by Vaitix materials and product liability claims relating to the design or manufacture of the Products.

12.4 **Insurance.** Drenind will maintain commercial general liability, warehouse legal liability at full replacement value, workers' compensation and employer's liability, commercial automobile, umbrella and cyber coverage. Limits will be at least those set out in the MSA draft. Vaitix will be named as additional insured where customary and as loss payee under the warehouse legal liability policy.

12.5 **Parent Guaranty.** As a condition of Vaitix's entry into the MSA, Drenind Logistics Group, Inc. will guarantee Drenind's obligations under the MSA in a form acceptable to Vaitix, delivered concurrently with the MSA.

## 13. TERMINATION AND TRANSITION

13.1 **Termination Rights.** The MSA will include customary termination rights for uncured material breach, insolvency, chronic service level failure, prolonged force majeure and changes in law that make performance unlawful.

13.2 **Early Termination Fee.** An early termination fee will apply only where Drenind terminates for Vaitix's uncured material breach. The calculation will be set out in the initial Order Form.

13.3 **Transition Assistance.** On expiry or termination, Drenind will continue services for up to one hundred twenty (120) days on request. It will also provide reasonable transition assistance to Vaitix or a successor provider, including a joint physical inventory, staging and loading of Products, and transfer of data and configuration information. Release of Products will not be conditional on payment of any amount.

## 14. OTHER TERMS FOR THE DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT

14.1 **Subcontracting.** Drenind will not subcontract the services without Vaitix's prior written consent, other than ordinary-course vendors such as carriers, packaging suppliers and staffing agencies without unsupervised access to Vaitix data.

14.2 **Assignment.** The MSA will include customary mutual restrictions on assignment.

14.3 **Supplier Code of Conduct.** Drenind will comply with Vaitix's supplier code of conduct, including its requirements on labor practices, health and safety, anti-corruption and environmental responsibility.

14.4 **Packaging and Branding.** Drenind will use Vaitix-approved packaging, inserts and branding and will not include third-party marketing materials in shipments.

14.5 **Publicity.** Neither Party will use the other's name or marks in publicity or marketing without prior written consent.

14.6 **Governing Law and Forum.** The Parties expect the MSA to be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, with disputes resolved in the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, following a staged executive escalation process.

## 15. BINDING PROVISIONS

15.1 **Binding Provisions.** Only the following provisions of this Term Sheet are legally binding on the Parties: Section 1.3 (Non-Binding Nature), Section 11.4 (as to continued application of the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement), this Section 15, Section 16 (Costs) and Section 17 (General).

15.2 **Good-Faith Negotiation.** Each Party intends to negotiate the Definitive Agreement in good faith on the basis of this Term Sheet. This intention is not a binding obligation, and neither Party will be liable to the other for failing to reach or sign the Definitive Agreement.

15.3 **Termination of Discussions.** Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other. If discussions end, the Binding Provisions survive.

15.4 **No Reliance.** Each Party acknowledges that, until the Definitive Agreement is signed, any steps it takes in anticipation of the engagement are at its own risk and cost. These steps include allocating facility space, hiring personnel, ordering equipment and terminating existing logistics arrangements.

## 16. COSTS

16.1 **Own Costs.** Each Party will bear its own legal, advisory and other costs in connection with the negotiation and preparation of this Term Sheet and the Definitive Agreement, whether or not the Definitive Agreement is signed.

16.2 **Pre-Signing Implementation Work.** Drenind will not start chargeable implementation work before the MSA and the initial Order Form are signed unless Vaitix authorizes that work in writing. Any authorization will state the scope and maximum cost of the work.

## 17. GENERAL

17.1 **Governing Law of Binding Provisions.** The Binding Provisions are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any conflict-of-laws rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

17.2 **Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

17.3 **Amendment.** This Term Sheet may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

17.4 **Confidentiality of Term Sheet.** The existence and contents of this Term Sheet are confidential information of both Parties under the Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement.
