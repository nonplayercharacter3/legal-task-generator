# PARENT GUARANTY

**Guaranty of Obligations of Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC under the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025**

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2025 by:

**Drenind Logistics Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, with its principal place of business at 2200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 500, Columbus, Ohio 43217 ("**Guarantor**"),

in favor of:

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Beneficiary**").

## RECITALS

A. Beneficiary, as customer, and Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), as provider, are entering into a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Provider will provide warehousing, fulfillment and returns processing services to Beneficiary.

B. Provider is a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement and the business relationship between Provider and Beneficiary.

C. Section 15.6 of the Agreement requires Provider to cause Guarantor to execute and deliver this Guaranty to Beneficiary concurrently with the Agreement. Beneficiary's entry into the Agreement is conditioned on that delivery.

D. Guarantor wishes to give this Guaranty to induce Beneficiary to enter into the Agreement.

In consideration of the foregoing, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Guarantor agrees as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. In this Guaranty:

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations, liabilities and covenants of Provider to Beneficiary of every kind arising under or in connection with the Agreement and each Transaction Document, whether now existing or arising later, whether absolute or contingent, whether liquidated or unliquidated, and whether for payment or performance. They include: (a) the performance of the Services in accordance with the Agreement, each Order Form and the Quality Agreement; (b) the release of Customer Products and Customer Data; (c) the payment of service credits, refunds, reimbursements for shrinkage, loss and damage, and amounts owed on audit; (d) indemnification obligations; (e) obligations relating to Customer Data, confidentiality and Security Incidents; (f) the provision of Transition Services; and (g) the payment of damages for any breach of the Agreement or any Transaction Document.

"**Insolvency Proceeding**" means any voluntary or involuntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation, dissolution, arrangement or similar law of any jurisdiction, and any assignment for the benefit of creditors.

"**Transaction Documents**" means each Order Form, the Quality Agreement, each amendment to the Agreement and each other document entered into between Provider and Beneficiary under or in connection with the Agreement, in each case as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time.

1.2 **Interpretation.** In this Guaranty: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) "including" and similar words mean "including without limitation"; (c) references to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated; (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and (e) references to the Agreement or any Transaction Document include it as amended, supplemented, extended, renewed or replaced from time to time.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Beneficiary, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment when due, and the full and punctual performance and observance, of all Guaranteed Obligations.

2.2 **Payment on Demand.** If Provider fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay that amount to Beneficiary within ten (10) days after Beneficiary's written demand. Payment shall be made in immediately available funds in U.S. dollars, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction, to the account Beneficiary designates in the demand.

2.3 **Performance on Demand.** If Provider fails to perform any Guaranteed Obligation other than an obligation to pay money, Guarantor shall, within fifteen (15) days after Beneficiary's written demand, perform or cause to be performed that obligation in accordance with the Agreement and the applicable Transaction Document. Where the obligation concerns the release of Customer Products or Customer Data, or the continued performance of the Services during a Transition Period, Guarantor shall act within two (2) Business Days after demand. Guarantor may perform through Provider, through another subsidiary of Guarantor or through a third-party logistics provider reasonably acceptable to Beneficiary. Guarantor shall also indemnify Beneficiary against all Losses resulting from Provider's failure to perform.

2.4 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Beneficiary is not required, as a condition of Guarantor's liability, to make any demand on Provider, to exhaust any remedy against Provider or any other person, or to enforce any security or other guaranty.

2.5 **Multiple Demands.** Beneficiary may make more than one demand under this Guaranty. A demand may relate to all or part of the Guaranteed Obligations. Each demand shall describe in reasonable detail the Guaranteed Obligation that Provider has failed to pay or perform.

2.6 **Limitation.** Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is not greater than Provider's liability for the Guaranteed Obligations, except that Guarantor is liable for the costs of enforcement under Section 12.5 and is not entitled to any defense described in Section 4. Any limitation or exclusion of liability under Section 15 of the Agreement applies to Guarantor's liability for the corresponding Guaranteed Obligations as it applies to Provider's liability for them.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations, whenever arising, and remains in full force and effect until the Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, subject to Section 5.

3.2 **Absolute and Unconditional.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are absolute, unconditional and irrevocable. They are not affected, released or discharged by any of the following, whether or not Guarantor has notice of it or has consented to it:

(a) any amendment, supplement, modification, extension, renewal or replacement of the Agreement or any Transaction Document, including any new Order Form, any increase in the scope of the Services or the addition of any Facility;

(b) any waiver, consent, forbearance, indulgence or extension of time given by Beneficiary to Provider;

(c) any change in the corporate existence, structure, ownership or control of Provider or Guarantor, or any merger, consolidation or sale of assets involving either of them;

(d) any Insolvency Proceeding affecting Provider, Guarantor or any of their assets, or any stay, discharge, disallowance or limitation of any Guaranteed Obligation in that proceeding;

(e) any invalidity, illegality, unenforceability or lack of authority with respect to the Agreement or any Transaction Document, or any defense of Provider based on any of them;

(f) any exchange, release, impairment or non-perfection of any security for, or any other guaranty of, any Guaranteed Obligation;

(g) any assignment or transfer by Provider of the Agreement or any of its obligations under it, or any delegation or subcontracting of any part of the Services;

(h) any claim, set-off, counterclaim or other right that Guarantor or Provider may have against Beneficiary, whether in connection with the Agreement or otherwise; or

(i) any other act, omission or circumstance that might otherwise operate as a legal or equitable discharge of a surety or guarantor, other than full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

3.3 **Consent to Amendments.** Guarantor consents in advance to every amendment, supplement and modification of the Agreement and each Transaction Document that Provider and Beneficiary may agree, and agrees that the Guaranteed Obligations include the obligations of Provider as so amended, supplemented or modified. No notice to or further consent of Guarantor is required.

3.4 **Assigned Agreement.** If Provider assigns or transfers the Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, to any person, whether or not with Beneficiary's consent, the Guaranteed Obligations include the obligations of that person under the Agreement and the Transaction Documents as if that person were Provider. This Section 3.4 does not authorize any assignment or transfer that the Agreement does not permit, and does not release Provider from any obligation.

3.5 **Independent Obligations.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are independent of Provider's obligations. Beneficiary may bring a separate action against Guarantor, whether or not an action is brought against Provider and whether or not Provider is joined in it.

## 4. WAIVERS

4.1 **Waiver of Notices.** Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law: (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; (b) notice of the creation, renewal, extension or accrual of any Guaranteed Obligation; (c) presentment, demand for payment, protest and notice of dishonor or non-payment; (d) notice of any default by Provider, except as expressly required by Section 2; and (e) all other notices to which Guarantor might otherwise be entitled.

4.2 **Waiver of Defenses.** Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, all suretyship and other defenses that it might otherwise have, including: (a) any requirement that Beneficiary first proceed against Provider or any other person, or against any security; (b) any defense based on any statute of limitations, to the extent the limitation period has not run against Provider; (c) any defense based on Beneficiary's election of remedies; and (d) any defense based on the release, discharge or limitation of Provider's liability in any Insolvency Proceeding.

4.3 **Defenses Available to Provider.** Guarantor may assert in its defense any defense to payment or performance that Provider could assert under the express terms of the Agreement or the applicable Transaction Document, other than a defense described in Section 3.2 or Section 4.2. Guarantor may not assert any defense based on Provider's incapacity, lack of authority or Insolvency Proceeding.

4.4 **Waiver of Set-Off.** Guarantor waives any right of set-off, counterclaim or deduction against amounts payable under this Guaranty, whether arising under this Guaranty, the Agreement or otherwise.

## 5. REINSTATEMENT

5.1 **Reinstatement.** If any payment or performance of any Guaranteed Obligation, by Provider, Guarantor or any other person, is rescinded, avoided, set aside or must otherwise be restored or returned by Beneficiary, whether in an Insolvency Proceeding or otherwise, this Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, as to that payment or performance as though it had not been made. Guarantor shall indemnify Beneficiary against all Losses arising from that rescission, avoidance, setting aside, restoration or return.

5.2 **Stay of Acceleration.** If the acceleration of the time for payment of any Guaranteed Obligation is stayed in any Insolvency Proceeding of Provider, that amount is nonetheless payable by Guarantor immediately on Beneficiary's demand.

## 6. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

6.1 **Deferral of Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution, indemnity or similar right against Provider arising from any payment or performance under this Guaranty, and shall not participate in any security held by Beneficiary.

6.2 **Subordination.** All debts and obligations of Provider to Guarantor, now existing or arising later, are subordinated in right of payment to the Guaranteed Obligations. After Beneficiary has made a demand under Section 2 that has not been satisfied, Guarantor shall not demand, accept or receive any payment from Provider on any such debt or obligation. Any amount received by Guarantor in breach of this Section 6.2 is held in trust for Beneficiary and shall be paid to Beneficiary promptly on receipt, to be applied to the Guaranteed Obligations.

6.3 **Payments Held in Trust.** If any amount is paid to Guarantor on account of any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity before all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall hold that amount in trust for Beneficiary and pay it to Beneficiary promptly on receipt.

## 8. COVENANTS

8.1 **Financial Information.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall deliver to Beneficiary:

(a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the audited consolidated financial statements of Guarantor and its subsidiaries for that fiscal year, with the report of Guarantor's independent certified public accountants; and

(b) within sixty (60) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, the unaudited consolidated balance sheet and statements of income and cash flows of Guarantor and its subsidiaries for that quarter, certified by Guarantor's chief financial officer as fairly presenting, in all material respects, the financial condition and results of operations of Guarantor and its subsidiaries.

Financial information delivered under this Section 8.1 is Confidential Information of Guarantor, and Beneficiary shall treat it in accordance with Section 10 of the Agreement as if Guarantor were the Disclosing Party.

8.2 **Notice of Certain Events.** Guarantor shall notify Beneficiary in writing promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days, after becoming aware of any of the following:

(a) any event that would reasonably be expected to impair Guarantor's ability to perform this Guaranty;

(b) any Insolvency Proceeding involving Guarantor or Provider;

(c) any proposed transaction as a result of which Guarantor would cease to own, directly or indirectly, a majority of the equity interests in Provider; and

(d) any default by Guarantor or Provider under any agreement for borrowed money in a principal amount exceeding ten million dollars (US$10,000,000) that results in, or permits, acceleration of that debt.

8.3 **Maintenance of Existence.** Guarantor shall maintain its corporate existence and good standing. Guarantor shall not merge or consolidate with any other person, or sell, lease or transfer all or substantially all of its assets, unless the surviving or acquiring person expressly assumes in writing all of Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty and delivers a copy of that assumption to Beneficiary.

8.4 **Compliance with Agreement.** Guarantor shall take all actions within its control as the parent of Provider to cause Provider to perform its obligations under the Agreement and the Transaction Documents, including by providing Provider with the funding, personnel and other resources reasonably required for that performance.

12.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and assigns and benefits Beneficiary and its successors and assigns. Guarantor may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Guaranty without Beneficiary's prior written consent, and any purported assignment in violation of this Section 12.1 is void. Beneficiary may assign its rights under this Guaranty, without Guarantor's consent, to any person to whom Beneficiary's rights under the Agreement are assigned in accordance with the Agreement.

12.2 **Amendment and Waiver.** No amendment of this Guaranty, and no waiver of any provision of it, is effective unless it is in writing, refers to this Guaranty and is signed by Beneficiary and, in the case of an amendment, by Guarantor. No failure or delay by Beneficiary in exercising any right or remedy under this Guaranty operates as a waiver of it, and no single or partial exercise of any right or remedy precludes any further exercise of it or of any other right or remedy.

12.3 **Cumulative Remedies.** The rights and remedies of Beneficiary under this Guaranty are cumulative and in addition to any rights and remedies available to Beneficiary under the Agreement, the Transaction Documents, at law or in equity.

12.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any respect, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

If any such deduction is required, Guarantor shall pay the additional amounts necessary so that Beneficiary receives the full amount it would have received had no deduction been made.

12.7 **Currency.** All amounts payable under this Guaranty are payable in U.S. dollars. If any amount is received in another currency, Guarantor's obligation is discharged only to the extent of the U.S. dollar amount that Beneficiary is able, in accordance with normal banking procedures, to purchase with the amount received on the Business Day after receipt. Guarantor shall indemnify Beneficiary against any shortfall.

12.8 **Interest.** Any amount payable by Guarantor under this Guaranty that is not paid when due accrues interest from the due date until paid at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

12.9 **Evidence of Obligations.** A certificate signed by an authorized officer of Beneficiary stating the amount of any Guaranteed Obligation then due and unpaid is prima facie evidence of that amount, absent manifest error.

12.10 **Term.** This Guaranty is irrevocable and remains in effect until the later of (a) the expiry or termination of the Agreement and the completion of all Transition Services, and (b) the date on which all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, subject in each case to Section 5. Guarantor's obligations for Guaranteed Obligations arising before that date survive it.

12.11 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Guaranty is for the sole benefit of Beneficiary and its successors and permitted assigns. Nothing in it confers any right, benefit or remedy on any other person, including Provider.
