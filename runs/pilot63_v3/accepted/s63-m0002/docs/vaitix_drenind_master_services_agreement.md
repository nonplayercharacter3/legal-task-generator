# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Warehousing, Fulfillment and Returns Processing Services**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Customer**"); and

**Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Columbus, Ohio 43217 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer designs, markets and sells outdoor apparel, footwear, equipment and related accessories.

B. Provider operates warehousing and distribution facilities and provides third-party logistics services, including receiving, storage, order fulfillment, value-added services and returns processing.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to provide such services on the terms of this Agreement and the Order Forms and other documents entered into under it.

In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 **Master Agreement.** This Agreement sets out the general terms under which Provider will provide Services to Customer. The specific Services, Facilities, Fees, Service Levels and commercial commitments will be set out in one or more Order Forms.

2.2 **Order Forms.** Each Order Form is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement. Each Order Form forms a separate contract between the parties consisting of that Order Form and the terms of this Agreement. Either party may propose an Order Form, but no Order Form is binding unless executed by authorized representatives of both parties.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the following documents, it will be resolved in the following order of precedence: (a) Amendment No. 1; (b) this Agreement; (c) the Quality Agreement; and (d) the Order Form.

2.4 **Quality Agreement.** The parties shall perform their respective obligations under the Quality Agreement. Provider's failure to comply with the Quality Agreement is a failure to perform the Services in accordance with this Agreement.

2.5 **Customer Affiliates.** Customer's Affiliates may receive Services under an Order Form executed by Customer. Customer is responsible for the acts and omissions of its Affiliates in connection with the Services as if they were Customer's own.

## 3. SERVICES

3.1 **Performance of Services.** Provider shall perform the Services: (a) in accordance with this Agreement, each applicable Order Form and the Quality Agreement; (b) in a diligent, professional and workmanlike manner consistent with the standards of a leading provider of third-party logistics services in the United States; (c) using appropriately trained, qualified and supervised personnel; and (d) in compliance with Applicable Law.

3.2 **Implementation.** Provider shall carry out the implementation activities described in each Order Form, including warehouse management system configuration, integration with Customer's e-commerce platforms and enterprise resource planning systems, slotting design, labeling and packaging set-up, and acceptance testing. Provider shall keep Customer reasonably informed of progress and shall promptly notify Customer of any anticipated delay in achieving the Go-Live Date.

3.3 **Standard Operating Procedures.** Within thirty (30) days after execution of each Order Form, Provider shall prepare written standard operating procedures covering receiving, put-away, cycle counting, order processing, packing specifications, carrier selection, exception handling and returns processing for the applicable Services. Provider shall submit them to Customer for review. Provider shall incorporate Customer's reasonable comments and shall not materially change any approved standard operating procedure without Customer's prior written approval.

3.4 **Facilities.** Provider shall perform the Services at the Facilities identified in the applicable Order Form. Provider shall not relocate Customer Products to, or perform Services at, any other location without Customer's prior written approval, except temporarily in an emergency to protect Customer Products, in which case Provider shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours. Provider shall maintain each Facility in a clean, secure, dry, pest-free and well-lit condition suitable for the storage and handling of apparel, footwear and outdoor equipment, and in compliance with Applicable Law.

3.5 **Peak-Season Capacity.** Customer shall provide Provider with a non-binding forecast of anticipated peak-season volumes no later than July 1 of each Contract Year. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the labor, space and equipment needed to meet the forecast volumes. The parties may agree on arrangements for peak-season overflow capacity by written amendment to this Agreement.

3.6 **Changes.** Either party may request a change to the scope of the Services by written change request. Provider shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a change request from Customer, give Customer a written assessment of the impact of the requested change on the Services, Fees, Service Levels and timelines. No change is binding unless documented in a written change order or amended Order Form executed by both parties.

3.7 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall: (a) deliver Customer Products to the Facilities with advance shipping notices in the format reasonably specified by Provider; (b) provide accurate product master data, including SKU, dimensions, weight and any special handling requirements; (c) inform Provider of any Customer Products that are hazardous materials or require special storage conditions; (d) provide timely access to Customer's systems and personnel as reasonably required for the Services; and (e) make timely decisions and approvals reasonably requested by Provider. Provider's failure to perform is excused to the extent directly caused by Customer's failure to perform these responsibilities, provided that Provider promptly notifies Customer of the failure and uses commercially reasonable efforts to perform despite it.

3.8 **Personnel.** Provider shall designate an account manager with sufficient seniority and authority to act as Customer's primary point of contact. Provider shall not replace the account manager without reasonable advance notice to Customer, except in the case of resignation, termination of employment, illness or similar circumstances. Provider is solely responsible for the hiring, supervision, compensation, benefits and employment-related obligations of its personnel. Provider shall conduct background checks on all personnel with access to Customer Data or high-value inventory, to the extent permitted by Applicable Law.

3.9 **Reporting.** Provider shall provide Customer with the operational reports described in the Order Form and the Quality Agreement, including daily inventory, order and shipment reports, weekly Service Level performance reports, and monthly business reviews. Provider shall provide Customer with real-time, read-only access to inventory and order status data through Provider's customer portal.

## 4. EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Exclusive Provider.** During the Term, Customer shall use Provider exclusively for all warehousing, fulfillment, distribution and returns processing of Customer Products in North America and the European Union, through all sales channels, including wholesale and retail replenishment, direct-to-consumer e-commerce and marketplace channels. Customer shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, engage any third party, or perform itself, any such services for Customer Products in those territories during the Term.

4.2 **New Channels and Products.** Customer shall notify Provider in writing of any new product line or sales channel that Customer intends to launch during the Term. The parties shall negotiate in good faith an Order Form or amended Order Form covering the Services required for that product line or sales channel.

4.3 **No Exclusivity by Provider.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Provider from providing services to any other person, provided that Provider complies with Sections 9 and 10 and does not commingle Customer Products with the goods of any other person.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the Fees set out in each Order Form for the Services performed under that Order Form. Except as expressly provided in an Order Form or this Agreement, the Fees cover all costs of providing the Services, including labor, supervision, equipment, standard packaging supplies identified in the Order Form, facility costs, systems and overhead.

5.2 **Pass-Through Charges.** Carrier charges, duties, customs brokerage fees and other third-party charges that the Order Form identifies as pass-through charges shall be invoiced at Provider's actual cost without mark-up, unless the Order Form provides otherwise. Provider shall provide reasonable supporting documentation for pass-through charges on request. Provider shall pass through to Customer the benefit of any carrier rebates, discounts or credits attributable to Customer's shipments, to the extent specified in the Order Form.

5.3 **Expenses.** Customer shall not be responsible for any travel, out-of-pocket or other expenses of Provider unless approved in advance in writing by Customer.

5.4 **Rate Adjustments.** Provider may adjust the Fees under an Order Form by written notice to Customer that specifies the adjusted Fees, the effective date of the adjustment and the basis for the adjustment (a "**Rate Adjustment Notice**"). Provider may not give a Rate Adjustment Notice before the first anniversary of the Go-Live Date under the applicable Order Form, nor more than once in any Contract Year. The effective date of an adjustment must be at least sixty (60) days after the date on which the Rate Adjustment Notice is delivered. Customer may object to a Rate Adjustment Notice by written notice to Provider given within thirty (30) days after receipt of the Rate Adjustment Notice. If Customer gives a timely objection: (a) the Fees in effect immediately before the Rate Adjustment Notice will continue to apply pending negotiation; (b) the parties shall negotiate in good faith for a period of forty-five (45) days after Provider's receipt of the objection; and (c) if the parties have not agreed in writing on adjusted Fees by the end of that period, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form by written notice to Provider effective on a date specified by Customer that is no earlier than ninety (90) days after the notice, without payment of the Early Termination Fee or any other termination charge. If Customer does not give a timely objection, the adjusted Fees set out in the Rate Adjustment Notice will be deemed accepted by Customer and will apply from the effective date stated in the Rate Adjustment Notice.

5.5 **Taxes.** The Fees exclude sales, use, value added, goods and services and similar transaction taxes, which Customer shall pay where properly charged by Provider and stated separately on Provider's invoice. Each party is responsible for taxes on its own income, property, employees and franchise. If Customer provides a valid exemption or resale certificate, Provider shall not charge the taxes covered by it. Provider shall cooperate reasonably with Customer to minimize taxes lawfully payable in connection with the Services.

5.6 **Minimum Fees.** If an Order Form sets out a minimum monthly fee or minimum volume commitment, Customer shall pay the amount specified in the Order Form in accordance with its terms.

## 6. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

6.1 **Invoices.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Services performed and pass-through charges incurred in the preceding calendar month, except that implementation fees shall be invoiced as specified in the Order Form. Each invoice shall itemize charges by Service category, Facility and activity unit, and shall be accompanied by the supporting activity data reasonably required for Customer to verify it.

6.2 **Payment Terms.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Payments shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated by Provider in writing. Provider shall not change its designated account except by written notice signed by an authorized officer and confirmed by telephone with Customer's finance contact.

6.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date and pays the undisputed portion. The parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Any dispute not resolved within that period may be escalated under Section 18.3. Provider shall not suspend the Services because of an amount disputed in good faith.

6.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due will accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law, from the due date until paid.

6.5 **Service Credits.** Where an Order Form or the Quality Agreement provides for service credits for failure to meet the Service Levels, Provider shall apply those credits against the next invoice issued after the month in which the failure occurred. Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services. They are not Customer's sole and exclusive remedy, except to the extent an Order Form expressly provides otherwise for a particular Service Level.

6.6 **Audit.** Provider shall maintain complete and accurate records relating to the Services, the Fees and the pass-through charges for at least three (3) years after the end of the Contract Year to which they relate. Upon at least fifteen (15) Business Days' notice, and no more than once in any Contract Year (except where a prior audit revealed a material discrepancy), Customer or its independent auditor may audit those records during normal business hours. If an audit reveals an overcharge, Provider shall promptly refund the overcharged amount. If the overcharge exceeds five percent (5%) of the amounts invoiced for the audited period, Provider shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable audit costs.

## 7. INVENTORY, TITLE AND RISK

7.1 **Title.** Title to the Customer Products remains with Customer at all times. Provider has no right, title or interest in the Customer Products. Provider shall keep the Customer Products segregated from goods of Provider and of any other person, clearly identified as Customer's property, and free from any lien, security interest, charge or encumbrance arising by, through or under Provider.

7.2 **Warehouse Lien Waiver.** To the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, Provider waives any warehouse lien, warehouseman's lien, carrier's lien or other possessory or statutory lien that it may have on the Customer Products or Customer Data, whether under Article 7 of the Uniform Commercial Code or otherwise. Provider shall not withhold, detain or refuse to release Customer Products or Customer Data for any reason, including any dispute over Fees.

7.3 **Warehouse Receipts.** This Agreement and each Order Form constitute the warehouse receipt for the Customer Products, and no other warehouse receipt or document of title shall be issued unless Customer so requests in writing. Any terms printed on any warehouse receipt, bill of lading, tariff or similar document issued by Provider are of no effect to the extent inconsistent with this Agreement.

7.4 **Receiving.** Provider shall receive, count and inspect inbound Customer Products in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Within the time specified in the Order Form, Provider shall report to Customer any shortage, overage, damage or non-conformity against the advance shipping notice. Customer Products are deemed received in good condition and in the quantities stated in the advance shipping notice unless Provider reports otherwise within that time.

7.5 **Risk of Loss.** Provider bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, the Customer Products from the time they are delivered to a Facility until the time they are tendered to the carrier for outbound shipment, or returned to Customer's possession, except to the extent the loss or damage is caused by Customer or by a Force Majeure Event for which Provider has complied with Section 17.

7.6 **Inventory Accuracy and Shrinkage.** Provider shall perform cycle counts in accordance with the Quality Agreement and an annual physical inventory if requested by Customer. Provider shall reimburse Customer for any inventory shrinkage, loss or damage for which Provider bears the risk under Section 7.5 and that exceeds the allowance (if any) set out in the Order Form. Reimbursement shall be at Customer's landed cost for the affected Customer Products, or such other valuation as the Order Form specifies.

7.7 **Access and Inspection.** Customer and its designees may, on reasonable notice and during normal business hours, visit the Facilities to inspect the Customer Products, observe the performance of the Services and verify Provider's compliance with this Agreement and the Quality Agreement. Customer shall comply with Provider's reasonable site safety and security rules while at a Facility.

7.8 **Recalls and Holds.** Provider shall promptly comply with any instruction from Customer to place Customer Products on hold, quarantine them, or cease shipment of them, and shall cooperate with Customer in any product recall, market withdrawal or regulatory inquiry, in each case as further described in the Quality Agreement. Customer shall reimburse Provider's reasonable incremental costs of such cooperation at the rates set out in the Order Form, except where the recall or hold results from Provider's breach.

## 8. SERVICE LEVELS AND QUALITY

8.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall perform the Services in accordance with the Service Levels. Provider shall measure and report its performance against the Service Levels as specified in the Order Form and the Quality Agreement.

8.2 **Root Cause Analysis.** If Provider fails to meet any Service Level in any month, Provider shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of that month: (a) investigate and report to Customer on the root cause of the failure; (b) propose a corrective action plan; and (c) after Customer's approval of the plan, implement it at Provider's cost.

8.3 **Chronic Failure.** If Provider fails to meet the same critical Service Level (as designated in the Order Form) in any three (3) months within any rolling six (6) month period, or fails to meet three (3) or more critical Service Levels in any single month, the failure is a material breach of this Agreement for the purposes of Section 12.4, and no cure period will apply.

8.4 **Packaging and Branding.** Provider shall use the packaging materials, inserts, labeling and branding specifications approved by Customer. Provider shall not include any marketing material, advertisement or branding of Provider or any third party in outbound shipments without Customer's prior written approval.

8.5 **Returns Processing.** Provider shall receive, inspect, grade and disposition returned Customer Products in accordance with Customer's returns policy, the Order Form and the Quality Agreement, and shall issue returns data to Customer within the time specified in the Order Form to allow Customer to issue refunds to its end customers.

8.6 **Compliance.** Provider shall obtain and maintain all licenses, permits, registrations and certifications required by Applicable Law to operate the Facilities and perform the Services. Provider shall comply with Customer's supplier code of conduct as provided to Provider in writing, including requirements relating to labor practices, health and safety, anti-corruption and environmental responsibility.

## 9. CUSTOMER DATA AND INFORMATION SECURITY

9.1 **Ownership of Customer Data.** As between the parties, Customer owns all Customer Data. Provider shall use Customer Data only to perform the Services and for no other purpose. Provider shall not sell, rent, license, aggregate, de-identify for its own commercial use or otherwise disclose Customer Data, except to its personnel and approved subcontractors who need access to perform the Services and are bound by obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

9.2 **Personal Information.** To the extent Provider processes Personal Information as part of the Customer Data, Provider acts as a service provider and processor on behalf of Customer. Provider shall: (a) process the Personal Information only on Customer's documented instructions; (b) not retain, use or disclose the Personal Information outside the direct business relationship between the parties; (c) not combine the Personal Information with personal information it receives from any other source; (d) comply with all Applicable Law relating to privacy and data protection; and (e) assist Customer in responding to requests from individuals exercising their rights under Applicable Law. The parties shall enter into any additional data processing terms that Applicable Law requires.

9.3 **Security Program.** Provider shall implement and maintain a written information security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Customer Data and consistent with recognized industry standards, such as ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The program shall include access controls, encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, employee security training, and business continuity and disaster recovery plans. If Provider processes payment card data, Provider shall comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.

9.4 **Security Incidents.** Provider shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or acquisition, use, disclosure, alteration or loss of, Customer Data (a "**Security Incident**"). Provider shall investigate and remediate the Security Incident, cooperate fully with Customer, and provide information reasonably requested by Customer. Provider shall not notify any affected individual or governmental authority of a Security Incident involving Customer Data without Customer's prior written approval, except as required by Applicable Law. Provider shall reimburse Customer for its reasonable costs of responding to a Security Incident caused by Provider's breach of this Section 9, including the costs of notifications, call centers, credit monitoring and forensic investigation.

9.5 **Security Assessments.** On Customer's request, no more than once per Contract Year (or at any time following a Security Incident), Provider shall complete Customer's reasonable security questionnaire and provide copies of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent assessment.

9.6 **Return and Deletion.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement, or on Customer's earlier written request, Provider shall return all Customer Data to Customer in a commonly used, machine-readable format reasonably specified by Customer, and shall then securely delete all copies of Customer Data in its possession or control, except to the extent Applicable Law requires retention. Provider shall certify the deletion in writing on request.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms, continues until the expiry of the Service Commitment Period under the first Order Form executed by the parties (the "**Initial Term**").

Unless the Order Form provides otherwise, the expiry or termination of an Order Form does not affect this Agreement or any other Order Form. If Provider terminates this Agreement or an Order Form under this Section 12.4 because of Customer's material breach, Customer shall pay Provider the Early Termination Fee for each terminated Order Form, in addition to all Fees accrued through the date of termination.

12.5 **Termination for Non-Payment.** Provider may terminate this Agreement by written notice if Customer fails to pay undisputed amounts exceeding the Fees invoiced for the preceding two (2) months and does not pay them within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice from Provider referring to this Section 12.5. Termination under this Section 12.5 is a termination for Customer's material breach under Section 12.4.

12.6 **Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party: (a) becomes insolvent or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts as they become due; (b) makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; (c) becomes the subject of any voluntary proceeding under any bankruptcy or insolvency law, or any involuntary proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or (d) has a receiver, trustee or similar officer appointed over all or substantially all of its assets.

12.7 **Termination for Regulatory Reasons.** Either party may terminate an affected Order Form on not less than ninety (90) days' written notice, without payment of the Early Termination Fee, if a change in Applicable Law makes continued performance of the Services under that Order Form unlawful and the parties are unable, after good faith negotiation for at least thirty (30) days, to agree on an amendment that would allow lawful performance.

12.8 **Effect of Expiry or Termination.** On expiry or termination of this Agreement or an Order Form: (a) Customer shall pay Provider all undisputed Fees accrued for Services performed through the effective date of expiry or termination, and any Early Termination Fee payable under this Agreement; (b) Provider shall promptly release all Customer Products in accordance with Section 13; (c) each party shall comply with Section 10.5; and (d) Provider shall comply with Section 9.6.

12.9 **Survival.** Sections 1, 6, 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 7.6, 9, 10, 11, 12.8, 12.9, 13, 14, 15, 18 and 19, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, survive expiry or termination of this Agreement.
