# QUALITY AGREEMENT

**Warehousing, Fulfillment and Returns Processing Services**

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 by and between:

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Customer**"); and

**Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Columbus, Ohio 43217 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Provider provides warehousing, fulfillment and returns processing services to Customer.

B. The Agreement contemplates that the parties will enter into a quality agreement setting out quality standards, inspection procedures, handling requirements and related operational requirements for the Services.

C. This Quality Agreement is that quality agreement and is the "Quality Agreement" referred to in the Agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

"**A-Grade**", "**B-Grade**", "**C-Grade**" and "**D-Grade**" have the meanings given in Section 9.3.

"**Corrective Action Plan**" means a written plan prepared by Provider under Section 12.3 describing the root cause of a Quality Deviation and the actions, owners and dates for correcting it and preventing its recurrence.

"**Hold**" means an instruction from Customer under Section 8.1 to stop picking, shipping or otherwise moving specified Customer Products.

"**Inbound Discrepancy**" means any shortage, overage, damage, mislabeling, missing documentation or other non-conformity between inbound Customer Products and the related advance shipping notice or purchase order data.

"**Product Specifications**" means the product master data, handling instructions, packaging specifications, brand guidelines and returns grading guide provided by Customer to Provider in writing from time to time.

"**Quality Contact**" means the individual designated by each party under Section 2.3.

"**Quality Deviation**" means any failure to comply with this Quality Agreement, the Product Specifications or an approved standard operating procedure, and any event that adversely affects, or could reasonably be expected to adversely affect, the condition, safety, identity or traceability of Customer Products.

"**Quarantine Area**" means a physically segregated, clearly marked and access-controlled area within a Facility used to hold Customer Products subject to a Hold, a Quality Deviation investigation or a recall.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Quality Agreement as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Quality Agreement.

## 2. PURPOSE, SCOPE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the quality standards and operational controls that Provider shall apply in performing the Services, so that Customer Products are received, stored, handled, packed, shipped and returned in a manner that preserves their condition, identity and traceability and protects Customer's brand.

2.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Services performed under the Agreement and each Order Form at each Facility, and to all Customer Products while in Provider's possession or control.

2.3 **Quality Contacts.** Each party shall designate a Quality Contact by written notice to the other within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this Quality Agreement. Provider's Quality Contact shall be a manager at the Facility with responsibility for quality assurance and authority to stop operations affecting Customer Products. Each party may replace its Quality Contact by written notice to the other.

2.4 **Responsibility Matrix.** The allocation of principal quality responsibilities is as follows:

| Activity | Customer | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Product Specifications and returns grading guide | Prepare and update | Implement |
| Advance shipping notices and product master data | Provide | Validate on receipt |
| Receiving inspection | Set criteria | Perform and report |
| Storage conditions | Specify special requirements | Maintain and monitor |
| Packaging and branding materials | Approve | Use as approved; manage stock |
| Holds, quarantines and recalls | Instruct | Execute and reconcile |
| Cycle counts and physical inventory | Review results | Perform and report |
| Returns grading and disposition | Set rules | Perform and report |
| Quality Deviations | Review and approve Corrective Action Plans | Investigate, report and correct |
| Training on Customer requirements | Provide initial materials | Train and keep records |

2.5 **Relationship to Agreement.** This Quality Agreement is entered into under, and forms part of the arrangements contemplated by, the Agreement. Section 2.4 of the Agreement applies to Provider's obligations under this Quality Agreement.

## 3. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

3.1 **Quality System.** Provider shall maintain a documented quality management system for each Facility that is consistent with recognized industry practice for third-party logistics providers. The system shall include document control, training records, internal audits, non-conformance management, corrective and preventive action, and management review.

3.2 **Standard Operating Procedures.** The standard operating procedures prepared and approved under Section 3.3 of the Agreement shall be consistent with this Quality Agreement and shall be maintained under Provider's document control system. Provider shall make the current version of each approved standard operating procedure available to Customer through Provider's customer portal or on request.

3.3 **Training.** Provider shall train all personnel performing Services for Customer on the applicable standard operating procedures, the Product Specifications and this Quality Agreement before they perform those Services. Provider shall provide refresher training at least annually, and additional training within ten (10) Business Days after any material change to a standard operating procedure or the Product Specifications. Temporary and agency personnel shall receive training appropriate to their tasks before they handle Customer Products. Provider shall keep training records for at least three (3) years and make them available to Customer on request.

3.4 **Internal Audits.** Provider shall conduct an internal audit of its compliance with this Quality Agreement at each Facility at least twice in each Contract Year and shall share a summary of findings and corrective actions with Customer within fifteen (15) Business Days after completion.

## 4. RECEIVING AND INBOUND INSPECTION

4.1 **Appointment Scheduling.** Provider shall operate a dock appointment system for inbound deliveries of Customer Products. Customer shall use reasonable efforts to cause its suppliers and carriers to book appointments. Provider shall not refuse an unscheduled delivery of Customer Products without first contacting Customer's operations contact.

4.2 **Inspection on Arrival.** On arrival of each inbound shipment, Provider shall:

(a) verify the seal number against the advance shipping notice, where applicable, and photograph the seal and the condition of the load before unloading;

(b) inspect the exterior of each pallet and carton for damage, moisture, crushing, tampering or pest activity;

(c) count cartons against the bill of lading and note any shortage, overage or visible damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before signing it; and

(d) segregate any visibly damaged or wet cartons in the Quarantine Area pending inspection.

4.3 **Unit-Level Inspection.** Provider shall count inbound Customer Products at the carton level for all shipments and at the unit level for the percentage of cartons, by SKU, specified in the Product Specifications. Where the Product Specifications do not specify a percentage, Provider shall inspect at least five percent (5%) of cartons per SKU per shipment, and not less than one carton. Unit-level inspection shall verify SKU, size, color, quantity, labeling, hang-tags, polybagging and visible defects.

4.4 **Escalated Inspection.** If unit-level inspection under Section 4.3 identifies defects or labeling errors in more than two percent (2%) of inspected units of any SKU in a shipment, Provider shall place the remaining units of that SKU in that shipment on hold in the Quarantine Area and notify Customer. Provider shall then perform a full inspection of those units if Customer instructs, at the rates for special projects set out in the Order Form, unless the defects were caused by Provider.

4.5 **Discrepancy Reporting.** Provider shall report each Inbound Discrepancy to Customer within the time specified in the Order Form under Section 7.4 of the Agreement, using the discrepancy report format agreed in the standard operating procedures. Each report shall include the advance shipping notice number, affected SKUs and quantities, a description of the discrepancy and supporting photographs.

4.6 **Product Data Validation.** On first receipt of any new SKU, Provider shall measure and weigh a sample unit and carton and compare the results with the product master data provided by Customer under Section 3.7 of the Agreement. Provider shall report any variance exceeding five percent (5%) in any dimension or weight to Customer within two (2) Business Days. Provider shall not change product master data in its warehouse management system without Customer's written approval.

4.7 **Dock-to-Stock.** Provider shall complete receiving, inspection and put-away so that inbound Customer Products are available to sell within the dock-to-stock time stated in Section 11.1, except for units held under Section 4.4 or Section 8.

## 5. STORAGE AND INVENTORY CONTROL

5.1 **Storage Conditions.** Provider shall store Customer Products in enclosed areas of the Facility that are:

(a) maintained between 45°F and 90°F (7°C and 32°C), with relative humidity not exceeding 65%;

(b) protected from direct sunlight, water ingress, condensation and dust;

(c) covered by an active pest control program with inspections at least monthly by a licensed pest control contractor; and

(d) protected by fire detection and suppression systems that comply with Applicable Law and the requirements of Provider's insurers.

5.2 **Monitoring.** Provider shall monitor temperature and humidity in the storage areas used for Customer Products continuously by data logger and shall retain the readings for at least twelve (12) months. Provider shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours of any reading outside the ranges in Section 5.1(a) lasting more than four (4) consecutive hours, and shall inspect potentially affected Customer Products.

5.3 **Racking and Stacking.** Provider shall store Customer Products in racking, shelving or bins appropriate to their size and weight. Provider shall observe the maximum stacking heights stated on cartons or in the Product Specifications and shall not place Customer Products directly on the floor except on pallets or in designated staging areas for no more than forty-eight (48) hours.

5.4 **Segregation.** Provider shall store Customer Products in locations dedicated to Customer and identified in the warehouse management system as Customer's inventory, consistent with Section 7.1 of the Agreement.

5.5 **Lot and Season Control.** Where the Product Specifications identify Customer Products by season code, production lot or country of origin, Provider shall capture that information on receipt and maintain it in its warehouse management system so that units can be traced from receipt to shipment or return. Unless Customer instructs otherwise, Provider shall pick on a first-in, first-out basis within each SKU.

5.6 **Security.** Provider shall store Customer Products designated as high-value in the Product Specifications in a locked cage or other secured area with access limited to authorized personnel and monitored by closed-circuit television. Provider shall retain closed-circuit television recordings of the Facility's docks, high-value areas and pack stations for at least thirty (30) days, or longer on Customer's request in connection with an investigation.

## 6. HANDLING OF TECHNICAL APPAREL, FOOTWEAR AND EQUIPMENT

6.1 **General Handling.** Provider shall handle Customer Products so as to avoid crushing, creasing, soiling, abrasion, moisture exposure and contact with sharp edges. Personnel shall not use box cutters or blades inside cartons containing unpackaged apparel without a guarded or retractable blade approved for that use.

6.2 **Technical Apparel.** Provider shall keep waterproof, down-insulated and membrane garments in their original polybags until packed for shipment, shall not compress down-insulated garments beyond the packing configuration set out in the Product Specifications, and shall not apply adhesive labels directly to garment fabric.

6.3 **Footwear.** Provider shall store footwear in its original boxes, upright or on its side as specified, and shall not stack footwear boxes higher than the limit shown on the master carton. Provider shall not remove tissue, shoe trees or desiccant packets except as the Product Specifications require.

6.4 **Equipment.** Provider shall handle tents, packs, sleeping bags and other equipment items in accordance with the Product Specifications, including any requirements for oversized items. Items containing stove fuel canisters, lithium batteries or other limited-quantity consumer goods identified by Customer under Section 3.7(c) of the Agreement shall be stored, packed and labeled in accordance with Applicable Law.

6.5 **Damage During Handling.** Provider shall record any damage to Customer Products occurring while in Provider's possession, move the damaged units to the Quarantine Area and report them to Customer in the weekly quality report under Section 11.3.

## 7. PICKING, PACKING, PACKAGING AND BRANDING

7.1 **Order Accuracy Controls.** Provider shall scan-verify each unit against the order at the pack station before an order is sealed. Provider shall perform a weight check or equivalent control on each shipped carton against its expected weight, with tolerances set in the standard operating procedures, and shall investigate any carton outside the tolerance before shipment.

7.2 **Packaging Specifications.** Provider shall pack orders using the carton sizes, mailers, void fill, tissue, stickers, inserts and packing slips set out in the Product Specifications, consistent with Section 8.4 of the Agreement. Provider shall select the smallest approved carton or mailer that fits the order without compressing it beyond the limits in Section 6.2.

7.3 **Branded Materials.** Provider shall store Customer-branded packaging and inserts as Customer Products under Section 5, shall use them only for Customer's orders, and shall report stock levels weekly. Provider shall notify Customer when stock of any branded material falls below four (4) weeks of forecast usage. Provider shall destroy obsolete branded materials only on Customer's written instruction and shall provide a certificate of destruction.

7.4 **Value-Added Services.** Provider shall perform gift wrapping, kitting, insert placement, re-labeling, hang-tagging and other value-added services in accordance with the work instructions approved by Customer for each program. Provider shall complete a first-article inspection, with photographs sent to Customer for approval, before beginning any new value-added program.

7.5 **Shipping Labels and Documents.** Provider shall apply carrier labels so that they are fully legible and scannable, do not cover Customer branding, and are not applied to product packaging visible to the end customer, except on approved mailers. Provider shall include return labels and returns instructions in outbound shipments as specified in the Product Specifications.

7.6 **Pack-Out Audits.** Provider shall audit at least one-half of one percent (0.5%) of packed orders each day, selected at random across shifts and pack stations, for unit accuracy, packaging compliance and presentation. Provider shall record the results and include them in the weekly quality report.

## 8. HOLDS, QUARANTINE AND RECALLS

8.1 **Holds.** Customer may issue a Hold at any time by email to Provider's Quality Contact and operations contact, identifying the affected SKUs, lots, season codes or locations. Provider shall implement each Hold in its warehouse management system within two (2) hours after receipt if received during operating hours, or by the start of the next shift otherwise. Provider shall confirm implementation to Customer in writing, with the quantities placed on Hold by location.

8.2 **Quarantine.** Where Customer so instructs, or where Customer Products are subject to Section 4.4, 5.2, 6.5 or 9.4, Provider shall physically move the affected Customer Products to the Quarantine Area. Customer Products in the Quarantine Area shall not be shipped, reworked or destroyed without Customer's written release or disposition instruction.

8.3 **Recalls and Market Withdrawals.** In the event of a product recall, market withdrawal or regulatory inquiry relating to Customer Products, Provider shall, consistent with Section 7.8 of the Agreement:

(a) identify all affected units on hand, in transit and shipped, using the traceability data maintained under Section 5.5;

(b) within twenty-four (24) hours after Customer's request, provide shipment records for affected units, including order numbers, ship dates, carriers and tracking numbers;

(c) receive, segregate and count recalled units returned to the Facility and report them to Customer daily; and

(d) dispose of recalled units only as Customer instructs in writing, and provide records of disposal.

8.4 **Mock Recall.** On Customer's request, no more than once in each Contract Year, Provider shall participate in a mock recall exercise designed by Customer. Provider shall complete the traceability steps in Section 8.3(a) and (b) for the selected SKUs within the times stated there.

8.5 **Release.** Provider shall release Customer Products from a Hold or the Quarantine Area only on Customer's written instruction and shall confirm the release in its warehouse management system the same Business Day.

## 9. RETURNS PROCESSING AND GRADING

9.1 **Receipt of Returns.** Provider shall receive returned Customer Products, match each return to the original order or return authorization, and record the date of receipt. Returns that cannot be matched shall be held for up to thirty (30) days while Provider and Customer attempt to identify them, after which they shall be dispositioned as Customer instructs.

9.2 **Inspection.** Provider shall inspect each returned unit for SKU, size and color against the return record, and for wear, soiling, odor, damage, missing components, removed tags and signs of use, in accordance with Customer's returns policy and the returns grading guide.

9.3 **Grading.** Provider shall grade each returned unit as follows, unless the returns grading guide provides otherwise:

(a) **A-Grade**: new and unused, with original tags and packaging intact, suitable for restocking as first-quality inventory;

(b) **B-Grade**: new or unused but with minor packaging damage, missing polybag or detached tags, suitable for restocking after refurbishment or re-bagging;

(c) **C-Grade**: worn, soiled or with minor defects, not suitable for sale as first-quality inventory and to be held for Customer's resale, donation or recycling programs; and

(d) **D-Grade**: damaged, defective, unsanitary or incomplete, to be held for Customer's disposition instruction.

9.4 **Warranty and Safety Returns.** Provider shall segregate any returned unit that the end customer identifies as defective under warranty, or that shows signs of a potential safety issue, such as a damaged fuel canister, swollen battery, broken buckle on a load-bearing item or a burn or chemical odor. Provider shall place those units in the Quarantine Area and report them to Customer within one (1) Business Day with photographs.

9.5 **Disposition.** Provider shall restock A-Grade units and, after refurbishment, B-Grade units to available inventory, and shall hold C-Grade and D-Grade units in designated locations pending Customer's written disposition instruction. Provider shall not sell, donate, destroy or otherwise dispose of any returned Customer Products except on Customer's written instruction.

9.6 **Returns Data.** Provider shall transmit returns data for each unit, including the order number, SKU, quantity, reason code, grade, disposition and photographs for C-Grade and D-Grade units, to Customer through the returns data feed within the time specified in the Order Form under Section 8.5 of the Agreement.

9.7 **Grading Audits.** Customer may audit Provider's grading of returned units at any time during a visit under Section 7.7 of the Agreement. Provider shall correct any grading errors identified, and the parties shall review grading accuracy at each monthly business review.

## 10. INVENTORY ACCURACY

10.1 **Cycle Counts.** Provider shall perform cycle counts so that every Customer Product location is counted at least once each calendar quarter, high-value locations are counted at least monthly, and the fastest-moving twenty percent (20%) of SKUs by unit volume are counted at least monthly. Provider shall also count any location on Customer's request or where a picker reports a discrepancy.

10.2 **Variance Investigation.** Provider shall investigate each cycle count variance before adjusting inventory, including by reviewing recent transactions, adjacent locations and closed-circuit television recordings where appropriate. Provider shall not make any single adjustment exceeding twenty (20) units or US$1,000 at Customer's landed cost without Customer's prior written approval.

10.3 **Adjustment Reporting.** Provider shall report all inventory adjustments to Customer weekly, by SKU, location, quantity, reason code and value at landed cost. Adjustments shall be taken into account in calculating inventory shrinkage under Section 7.6 of the Agreement.

10.4 **Annual Physical Inventory.** Where Customer requests an annual physical inventory under Section 7.6 of the Agreement, Provider shall prepare a count plan for Customer's approval at least twenty (20) Business Days in advance. The plan shall include cut-off procedures, count teams, blind count and recount procedures, and reconciliation steps. Customer may observe the count. Provider shall deliver a final reconciliation within ten (10) Business Days after the count.

## 11. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS AND REPORTING

11.1 **Quality KPIs.** Provider shall meet the following key performance indicators, each measured monthly at each Facility:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Dock-to-stock (inbound receipts processed and available to sell) | 48 hours from receipt for 98% of units |
| Same-day ship (orders received by 2:00 p.m. Eastern) | 99.5% |
| Order accuracy | 99.8% |
| Inventory accuracy (cycle count, unit level) | 99.7% |
| Returns processed and data transmitted to Vaitix | 72 hours from receipt for 97% of units |
| Portal and integration availability | 99.5% per month, excluding scheduled maintenance |

11.2 **Measurement.** The methods for measuring each key performance indicator, including exclusions for Customer-caused delays and Holds, shall be set out in the standard operating procedures. Service credits and the designation of critical Service Levels for these key performance indicators are as set out in the Order Form.

11.3 **Weekly Quality Report.** Provider shall deliver to Customer each week a quality report covering Inbound Discrepancies, results of escalated inspections, handling damage, pack-out audit results, Holds implemented and released, returns volumes by grade, inventory adjustments and open Quality Deviations.

11.4 **Monthly Business Review.** The monthly business reviews under Section 3.9 of the Agreement shall include review of performance against the key performance indicators in Section 11.1, trends in Quality Deviations, the status of open Corrective Action Plans and any proposed changes to this Quality Agreement or the Product Specifications.

## 12. QUALITY DEVIATIONS AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

12.1 **Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer's Quality Contact of any Quality Deviation:

(a) within four (4) hours after discovery, if it involves a potential safety issue, a Security Incident, theft, a fire, flood or other event affecting more than one hundred (100) units, or a shipment of Customer Products subject to a Hold; and

(b) in the next weekly quality report, in all other cases.

12.2 **Investigation.** Provider shall investigate each Quality Deviation notified under Section 12.1(a), and any other Quality Deviation that Customer reasonably requests be investigated, and shall provide a written investigation report within five (5) Business Days after discovery.

12.3 **Corrective Action Plans.** Where an investigation identifies a systemic cause, or where Section 8.2 of the Agreement applies, Provider shall prepare a Corrective Action Plan and submit it to Customer for approval. Provider shall implement each approved Corrective Action Plan at its cost by the dates stated in it and shall report on its effectiveness at the next monthly business review.

12.4 **Effect.** The procedures in this Section 12 are in addition to, and do not limit, Customer's rights and remedies under the Agreement.

## 13. AUDITS, INSPECTIONS AND RECORDS

13.1 **Customer Quality Audits.** In addition to its rights under Section 7.7 of the Agreement, Customer may conduct a quality audit of each Facility once in each Contract Year on at least ten (10) Business Days' notice, and at any time on reasonable notice following a Quality Deviation notified under Section 12.1(a). Provider shall make its personnel, records, standard operating procedures and systems reasonably available for the audit.

13.2 **Audit Findings.** Customer shall provide Provider with a written summary of any audit findings. Provider shall respond within ten (10) Business Days with a Corrective Action Plan for each finding requiring correction.

13.3 **Third-Party Inspections.** Provider shall permit inspections of Customer Products at the Facility by Customer's designated quality inspectors, testing laboratories and governmental authorities, and shall promptly inform Customer of any governmental inspection of the Facility that relates to Customer Products.

13.4 **Records.** Provider shall retain all records generated under this Quality Agreement, including inspection records, discrepancy reports, environmental monitoring data, training records, cycle count records, returns grading records and Quality Deviation reports, for the period specified in Section 6.6 of the Agreement, or the longer period stated in this Quality Agreement, and shall provide copies to Customer on request.

## 14. CHANGE CONTROL

14.1 **Changes by Provider.** Provider shall not make any change to a Facility layout affecting Customer Products, a warehouse management system configuration affecting Customer's orders or inventory, or an approved standard operating procedure, without giving Customer at least ten (10) Business Days' prior written notice and, for changes to approved standard operating procedures, obtaining Customer's prior written approval under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

14.2 **Changes by Customer.** Customer may update the Product Specifications by written notice to Provider. Provider shall implement updates within ten (10) Business Days after receipt, or within such longer period as the parties agree where an update requires new materials, equipment or training. Where an update materially affects the scope of the Services or Provider's costs, Section 3.6 of the Agreement applies.

14.3 **Emergency Changes.** Provider may make a change without prior notice where necessary to protect the safety of personnel or Customer Products in an emergency, and shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours.

## 15. TERM, REVIEW AND AMENDMENT

15.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the date stated above and continues in effect for so long as the Agreement is in effect and Provider holds any Customer Products, including during any Transition Period.

15.2 **Annual Review.** The parties shall review this Quality Agreement at least once in each Contract Year at a monthly business review and shall document any agreed changes in accordance with Section 15.3.

15.3 **Amendment.** No amendment of this Quality Agreement is effective unless it is in writing, refers to this Quality Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Updates to the Product Specifications under Section 14.2 and to approved standard operating procedures under Section 3.3 of the Agreement are not amendments of this Quality Agreement.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 19.1 of the Agreement. Operational notifications under Sections 4, 5.2, 8, 9.4 and 12.1 may be given by email to the Quality Contacts and operations contacts designated by each party.

16.2 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Sections 18.1 through 18.5 of the Agreement apply to this Quality Agreement and to any dispute arising out of or relating to it.

16.3 **Confidentiality.** This Quality Agreement, the Product Specifications and all records generated under this Quality Agreement are Confidential Information of Customer under Section 10 of the Agreement, except that Provider's internal procedures and systems documentation remain Confidential Information of Provider.

16.4 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by an electronic signature service complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act are as effective as original signatures.
