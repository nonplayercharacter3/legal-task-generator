# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 4, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Vaitix**"); and

**Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Columbus, Ohio 43217 ("**Drenind**").

Vaitix and Drenind are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Vaitix designs, markets and sells outdoor apparel, footwear, equipment and related accessories. It has issued a request for proposal for third-party logistics services, including warehousing, e-commerce fulfillment and returns processing.

B. Drenind operates warehousing and distribution facilities and provides third-party logistics services. It wishes to respond to Vaitix's request for proposal.

C. In connection with the request for proposal, the Parties expect to exchange confidential business, operational, technical and financial information, and to allow each other's representatives to visit their facilities. Each Party is willing to disclose that information only on the terms of this Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

"**Disclosing Party**" means the Party that discloses Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Personal Information**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes or is reasonably capable of being associated with an individual or household, and any information defined as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under applicable law.

"**Purpose**" means evaluating, negotiating and, if the Parties so decide, documenting a possible business relationship under which Drenind would provide warehousing, fulfillment, returns processing and related logistics services to Vaitix (the "**Potential Transaction**").

"**Receiving Party**" means the Party that receives Confidential Information, directly or through its Representatives.

"**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, officers, employees, agents, attorneys, accountants, financial advisers, consultants and, in the case of Drenind, the parent company that is expected to provide a guaranty in connection with the Potential Transaction.

1.2 **Interpretation.** In this Agreement: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) "including" and similar words mean "including without limitation"; (c) references to Sections are to sections of this Agreement; and (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa.

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, whether oral, written, electronic, visual or otherwise, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives disclose or make available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives in connection with the Purpose, on or after the Effective Date, and that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes:

(a) in the case of Vaitix, its order volumes, units per order, seasonal and peak volume profiles, return rates, SKU counts and product master data, sales and channel information, product plans and launch calendars, unreleased product designs, sourcing and supplier information, existing logistics arrangements and their costs, pricing, margins and forecasts;

(b) in the case of Drenind, its facility layouts and capacity data, labor models and rates, warehouse management system functionality and configuration, security procedures, carrier rate cards and carrier contract terms, pricing proposals, cost structures and customer information;

(c) in the case of either Party, its information security policies, audit reports, insurance information, financial statements and business plans;

(d) all information observed by a Party's Representatives during any site visit to the other Party's facilities under Section 5; and

(e) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts, studies, summaries and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

2.2 **Discussions.** The fact that the Parties are discussing the Potential Transaction, the content and status of those discussions, and the terms of any proposal, response or draft agreement exchanged between them are the Confidential Information of each Party.

2.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is lawfully received by the Receiving Party or its Representatives from a third party that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, is not bound by any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party with respect to it; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

Exclusions (a) through (d) do not apply to Personal Information. Information is not within any exclusion merely because individual features of it are public or known to the Receiving Party, unless the combination of those features, and their principle, is public or known.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose. Without limiting the preceding sentence, Drenind shall not use Vaitix's Confidential Information to solicit, price or plan services for any other customer. Vaitix shall not use Drenind's Confidential Information, including its pricing and carrier rate cards, other than to evaluate Drenind's proposal and negotiate the Potential Transaction with Drenind.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any person other than its Representatives who:

(a) need to know it for the Purpose;

(b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and

(c) are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

3.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care. The Receiving Party shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Confidential Information.

3.4 **Responsibility for Representatives.** The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

3.5 **Copies.** The Receiving Party shall not copy or reproduce the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information except as reasonably required for the Purpose. Every copy shall reproduce any confidentiality notice or legend on the original.

3.6 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any samples, prototypes, software, system demonstrations or other tangible items provided by the Disclosing Party. It shall not remove, test or analyze any product samples except as the Disclosing Party authorizes in writing for the Purpose.

3.7 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing promptly, and in any event within two (2) business days, after becoming aware of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, use of or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to regain possession of the Confidential Information and prevent further unauthorized use or disclosure.

## 4. PERSONAL INFORMATION AND SAMPLE DATA

4.1 **Minimization.** The Parties do not intend to exchange Personal Information in connection with the Purpose. Where Vaitix provides sample order, shipment or returns data to allow Drenind to model its proposal, Vaitix shall use reasonable efforts to remove or mask end-customer names, street addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers and payment information before disclosure.

4.2 **Handling.** If any Personal Information is disclosed, the Receiving Party shall: (a) use it only for the Purpose; (b) not combine it with other personal information; (c) not sell, share or otherwise disclose it; (d) comply with applicable privacy and data protection laws; and (e) delete it promptly when it is no longer required for the Purpose, and in any event on the Disclosing Party's request.

## 5. SITE VISITS

5.1 **Facility Visits.** Each Party may, by arrangement, permit Representatives of the other Party to visit its facilities in connection with the Purpose. These include Drenind's fulfillment center at 4850 Logistics Way, Groveport, Ohio 43125 and Vaitix's existing distribution and studio locations.

5.2 **Rules.** The visiting Party shall ensure that its Representatives comply with the host Party's reasonable safety, security and confidentiality rules communicated to them before or during the visit. Visiting Representatives shall not take photographs or video recordings, or remove any documents, samples or materials, without the host Party's express permission.

5.3 **Other Customers.** Drenind shall take reasonable steps to ensure that, during any visit by Vaitix's Representatives, the goods, labels and data of Drenind's other customers are not exposed to view beyond what is incidental to a tour of an operating facility. Vaitix shall treat any information about Drenind's other customers that its Representatives observe incidentally as Drenind's Confidential Information.

5.4 **Liability.** Each Party is responsible for injury to its own Representatives and damage to its own property during a visit, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of the host Party.

## 6. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

6.1 **Permitted Disclosure.** The Receiving Party or its Representatives may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process, or by the rules of any securities exchange.

6.2 **Procedure.** Where legally permitted, the Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice of any such requirement. It shall also give reasonable cooperation, at the Disclosing Party's expense, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy. The Receiving Party shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to it.

6.3 **Whistleblower Protection.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits any individual from reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or from making other disclosures protected under whistleblower provisions of applicable law. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, an individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or that is made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 7. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

7.1 **Return or Destruction.** Within fifteen (15) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall, at its election, return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession or control and that of its Representatives, including all copies. It shall destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify compliance with this Section 7.1 in writing.

7.2 **Retained Copies.** Despite Section 7.1, the Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain: (a) copies stored in automatic electronic backup or archival systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course, until they are overwritten or deleted in the normal course; (b) copies that applicable law, regulation or professional standards require them to retain; and (c) one archival copy held by the Receiving Party's legal department solely to evidence compliance with this Agreement. All retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained, regardless of the expiry of the period in Section 9.2.

## 8. NO OBLIGATION; NO WARRANTY; NO LICENSE

8.1 **No Obligation to Proceed.** This Agreement does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions or to enter into the Potential Transaction or any other agreement. Either Party may end discussions at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other. Unless and until a definitive agreement for the Potential Transaction has been executed by both Parties, neither Party has any legal obligation with respect to the Potential Transaction other than the obligations in this Agreement.

8.2 **No Warranty.** All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, including any volume data, forecasts, capacity estimates or indicative pricing. Neither Party is liable to the other for any use of or reliance on its Confidential Information, except as may be set out in a definitive agreement executed by both Parties.

8.3 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

8.4 **Freedom to Deal.** Subject to the Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement, nothing in this Agreement prevents either Party from entering into discussions or agreements with third parties about matters similar to the Potential Transaction. In particular, Vaitix may continue to evaluate other proposals in response to its request for proposal, and Drenind may provide services to other customers.

## 9. TERM

9.1 **Disclosure Period.** This Agreement applies to Confidential Information disclosed during the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the earlier of: (a) the second (2nd) anniversary of the Effective Date; and (b) the date on which either Party gives written notice ending discussions under Section 8.1.

9.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement with respect to each item of Confidential Information continue for three (3) years after the end of the disclosure period under Section 9.1. Despite the preceding sentence, the Receiving Party's obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law in the case of trade secrets, and without limit in time in the case of Personal Information.

## 10. NON-SOLICITATION

10.1 **Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the disclosure period under Section 9.1 and for twelve (12) months afterward, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Purpose.

10.2 **Exceptions.** Section 10.1 does not prohibit: (a) general solicitations of employment, including through job postings, search firms or advertisements, that are not specifically directed at the other Party's employees; or (b) hiring any person who responds to such a general solicitation, who contacts the hiring Party on his or her own initiative, or whose employment with the other Party ended at least six (6) months before the first contact.

## 11. REMEDIES

11.1 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, without posting a bond or other security and without proof of actual damages, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

11.2 **Attorneys' Fees.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party has breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and any dispute, claim or controversy arising out of or relating to it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice-of-law or conflict-of-laws rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum. Either Party may nonetheless seek equitable relief under Section 11.1 in any court of competent jurisdiction.

12.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY, VOLUNTARILY AND IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
