## 1. INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Incorporation.** This Order Form No. 001 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into under the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 between Customer and Provider (the "**Agreement**"). This Order Form is an "Order Form" for the purposes of the Agreement. It is incorporated into and governed by the Agreement in accordance with Section 2.2 of the Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. In this Order Form:

"**Active SKU**" means a SKU with at least one unit in inventory at the Facility at any time during the relevant calendar month.

"**Critical Service Level**" means each Service Level designated as critical in Section 7.1.

"**Minimum Monthly Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

"**Monthly Core Fees**" means, for a calendar month, the Fees invoiced for the core services in Part 1 of Schedule 1 (Receiving, Storage, Order Processing, Value-Added Services and Returns), excluding pass-through charges, taxes, implementation fees and Fees for special projects.

"**Order Form Effective Date**" means the date stated as such in the table at the head of this Order Form.

1.3 **First Order Form.** This Order Form is the first Order Form executed by the parties under the Agreement.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Services.** Provider shall provide the following Services for Customer Products at the Facility:

(a) **Inbound receiving.** Receiving of palletized, carton and floor-loaded container deliveries, including seal verification, carton counting, unit-level inspection in accordance with the Quality Agreement, discrepancy reporting and put-away;

(b) **Storage and inventory management.** Storage in pallet racking and shelf bins, slotting and re-slotting, cycle counting in accordance with the Quality Agreement, and an annual physical inventory on Customer's request;

(c) **Order fulfillment.** Pick, pack and ship of direct-to-consumer orders placed through Customer's own e-commerce website and the connected marketplace storefronts listed in Section 2.3, including packing slip and insert placement and carrier label generation;

(d) **Value-added services.** Gift wrapping, kitting, insert placement, re-labeling, hang-tagging, polybagging and light product preparation, in each case under work instructions approved under Section 7.4 of the Quality Agreement;

(e) **Returns processing.** Receipt, matching, inspection, grading, refurbishment and re-bagging, restocking and holding for disposition of returned Customer Products, in accordance with Section 8.5 of the Agreement, Section 9 of the Quality Agreement and Customer's returns policy;

(f) **Transportation management.** Selection of outbound parcel carriers and service levels under the carrier routing guide in the standard operating procedures, rate shopping among the carriers listed in Section 2.4, tracking data integration and carrier claims administration; and

(g) **Account management and reporting.** The reporting described in Section 8 and the monthly business reviews under Section 3.9 of the Agreement.

2.2 **Excluded Services.** Unless added by an amended Order Form or a change order under Section 3.6 of the Agreement, the Services under this Order Form do not include customs brokerage, freight forwarding, international outbound shipping, or the handling of hazardous materials other than limited-quantity consumer goods identified by Customer under Section 3.7(c) of the Agreement.

2.3 **Connected Channels.** As of the Order Form Effective Date, the connected channels are:

(a) Customer's e-commerce website at vaitixoutdoor.com, operated on Customer's e-commerce platform; and

(b) Customer's storefronts on the marketplace platforms identified in the implementation plan, to the extent orders are released to Provider for fulfillment.

Customer may add connected channels under Section 4.2 of the Agreement. Integration work for a new connected channel is charged at the special project rate in Schedule 1 unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

2.4 **Carriers.** As of the Order Form Effective Date, Provider shall rate shop among UPS, FedEx, the United States Postal Service and the regional carriers listed in the carrier routing guide. Provider shall not add or remove a carrier without Customer's written approval, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold.

2.5 **Facility.** The Facility identified in the table at the head of this Order Form is the only Facility under this Order Form for the purposes of Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

## 3. TERM AND SERVICE COMMITMENT

3.1 **Service Commitment Period.** The Service Commitment Period under this Order Form is as follows:

**Service Commitment Period: forty-eight (48) months** from the Go-Live Date.

3.2 **Order Form Term.** This Order Form commences on the Order Form Effective Date. It continues in effect for the Term of the Agreement unless terminated earlier in accordance with the Agreement.

3.3 **Go-Live Confirmation.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Go-Live Date, the parties shall exchange written confirmation of the Go-Live Date. The confirmation shall state the calendar date on which the Service Commitment Period begins and the first calendar month for which the Minimum Monthly Fee applies under Section 5.3.

3.4 **Volume Assumptions.** The Fees in Schedule 1 are based on the following volume assumptions shared by Customer during the request for proposal process:

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Average outbound orders per month | 38,000 |
| Average units per order | 2.1 |
| Peak months | November and December |
| Peak volume multiple | Approximately 2.5 times the monthly average |
| Average return rate (units) | 18% to 22% of units shipped |
| Active SKUs (estimated at Go-Live) | 4,200 |

The volume assumptions are given for planning and pricing purposes only. They are not a commitment by Customer to deliver any volume. Customer's only financial commitment as to volume is the Minimum Monthly Fee.

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION

4.1 **Implementation Plan.** Provider shall deliver a detailed implementation plan within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Order Form Effective Date. The implementation plan shall cover:

(a) warehouse management system configuration;

(b) integration with Customer's e-commerce platform and enterprise resource planning system for order download, inventory synchronization, shipment confirmation, tracking number upload and returns data feeds;

(c) slotting design for the Facility;

(d) packaging, labeling and branding set-up under the Product Specifications;

(e) preparation of the standard operating procedures under Section 3.3 of the Agreement;

(f) staff training under Section 3.3 of the Quality Agreement; and

(g) acceptance testing.

4.2 **Acceptance Testing.** Acceptance testing shall include end-to-end testing of each integration, a pilot of at least five hundred (500) test orders across all connected channels and packaging configurations, and a returns processing pilot. Provider shall not process live customer orders until both parties have confirmed in writing that acceptance testing has been completed successfully.

4.3 **Inventory Transfer.** Customer is responsible for the cost of transporting its existing inventory of Customer Products to the Facility. Provider shall provide receiving labor for the initial inventory transfer at the receiving rates in Schedule 1. Storage Fees for inventory received before the Go-Live Date apply from the date of receipt.

4.4 **Implementation Fee.** The implementation fee in Schedule 1 is payable as follows: fifty percent (50%) invoiced on the Order Form Effective Date and fifty percent (50%) invoiced on the Go-Live Date. Each installment is payable in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

4.5 **Delay.** If Provider anticipates that the Go-Live Date will fall later than thirty (30) days after the Target Go-Live Date for reasons other than Customer's failure to perform its responsibilities under Section 3.7 of the Agreement, Provider shall notify Customer promptly under Section 3.2 of the Agreement. In that case, Provider shall bear its own costs of the delay and shall not charge Storage Fees for the period between the date falling thirty (30) days after the Target Go-Live Date and the actual Go-Live Date.

## 5. FEES

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Schedule 1 for the Services performed under this Order Form, subject to adjustment under Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

5.2 **Oversized Items.** An oversized item surcharge applies to Customer Products whose longest side exceeds thirty-six (36) inches or whose shipping weight exceeds fifty (50) pounds, at the rate in Schedule 1. The surcharge applies only to order processing and receiving of those items.

5.3 **Minimum Monthly Fee.** From the third full calendar month after the Go-Live Date, and for each calendar month after that during the Term, if the Monthly Core Fees for a calendar month are less than ninety-five thousand dollars (US$95,000) (the "**Minimum Monthly Fee**"), Customer shall pay the difference between the Monthly Core Fees and the Minimum Monthly Fee. The Minimum Monthly Fee does not include pass-through charges. It is reduced pro rata for any calendar month in which:

(a) Fees are suspended under Section 17.3 of the Agreement;

(b) Provider is unable to process Customer's released orders for more than two (2) Business Days for reasons other than Customer's acts or omissions; or

(c) this Order Form is in effect for part of the month only.

5.4 **Pass-Through Charges.** The following are pass-through charges for the purposes of Section 5.2 of the Agreement, invoiced at Provider's actual cost without mark-up:

(a) parcel carrier charges, including fuel, residential, delivery area and address correction surcharges, at the rates set out in Provider's negotiated carrier rate cards made available to Customer before the Order Form Effective Date;

(b) non-standard packaging materials purchased at Customer's written request; and

(c) third-party charges approved in advance in writing by Customer.

5.5 **Carrier Rebates.** Provider shall credit to Customer, on the invoice for the month following receipt by Provider, one hundred percent (100%) of all volume rebates, incentive credits and service failure refunds received from carriers that are attributable to Customer's shipments. Provider shall file service failure refund claims with carriers for late deliveries of Customer's shipments where the carrier offers a guaranteed delivery commitment.

5.6 **Standard Packaging.** Standard packaging materials comprise the corrugated cartons, poly mailers, void fill, tape and packing slips listed in the Product Specifications as standard. They are charged at the per-order rate in Schedule 1. Customer-branded packaging and inserts are supplied by Customer at its cost and stored as Customer Products.

## 6. INVENTORY, RECEIVING AND RETURNS TIMEFRAMES

6.1 **Receiving Discrepancy Reporting.** The time for reporting shortages, overages, damage and non-conformities under Section 7.4 of the Agreement is two (2) Business Days after delivery of the inbound shipment to the Facility.

6.2 **Shrinkage Allowance.** The shrinkage allowance for the purposes of Section 7.6 of the Agreement is one-tenth of one percent (0.10%) of the units received at the Facility in each Contract Year, measured on a unit basis. Provider shall reimburse Customer at Customer's landed cost for all shrinkage, loss and damage above the allowance. Reimbursement is made by credit on the invoice issued after the annual reconciliation, or, on expiry or termination, by payment within thirty (30) days.

6.3 **Annual Reconciliation.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Contract Year, Provider shall deliver a shrinkage reconciliation. The reconciliation shall show units received, units shipped, units returned and restocked, units adjusted and the resulting shrinkage. Customer may audit the reconciliation under Section 6.6 of the Agreement.

6.4 **Returns Data.** The time for transmitting returns data under Section 8.5 of the Agreement and Section 9.6 of the Quality Agreement is seventy-two (72) hours after receipt of the returned unit at the Facility, for at least ninety-seven percent (97%) of units in each month.

6.5 **Recall Support.** Provider's incremental costs of cooperation in a recall, hold or regulatory inquiry under Section 7.8 of the Agreement are charged at the special project rate in Schedule 1. No charge applies where the recall or hold results from Provider's breach.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS AND SERVICE CREDITS

7.1 **Service Levels.** Provider shall meet the following Service Levels, each measured monthly at the Facility in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Quality Agreement:

| No. | Service Level | Target | Critical |
|---|---|---|---|
| SL-1 | Dock-to-stock (inbound receipts processed and available to sell) | 48 hours from receipt for 98% of units | Yes |
| SL-2 | Same-day ship (orders received by 2:00 p.m. Eastern) | 99.5% | Yes |
| SL-3 | Order accuracy | 99.8% | Yes |
| SL-4 | Inventory accuracy (cycle count, unit level) | 99.7% | Yes |
| SL-5 | Returns processed and data transmitted to Vaitix | 72 hours from receipt for 97% of units | No |
| SL-6 | Portal and integration availability | 99.5% per month, excluding scheduled maintenance | No |

7.2 **Service Credits.** If Provider fails to meet a Service Level in any calendar month, Provider shall credit Customer the percentage of the Monthly Core Fees for that month shown below:

| Service Level | Credit for failure |
|---|---|
| SL-1 | 2.0% |
| SL-2 | 3.0% |
| SL-3 | 3.0% |
| SL-4 | 2.0% |
| SL-5 | 1.0% |
| SL-6 | 1.0% |

7.3 **Credit Cap.** Total service credits in any calendar month will not exceed twelve percent (12%) of the Monthly Core Fees for that month. Service credits are applied under Section 6.5 of the Agreement. They are not Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any Service Level failure.

7.4 **Measurement Exclusions.** Orders subject to a Hold, orders released after the daily cut-off, orders delayed by incorrect order data supplied by Customer, and inbound shipments delivered without an advance shipping notice are excluded from the calculation of the affected Service Level, as further described in the standard operating procedures.

7.5 **Stabilization Period.** For the first two (2) full calendar months after the Go-Live Date, Provider shall measure and report performance against the Service Levels. During that period, service credits under Section 7.2 do not apply and failures do not count toward Section 8.3 of the Agreement. Provider remains obliged to perform root cause analysis under Section 8.2 of the Agreement for failures in that period.

## 8. REPORTING AND GOVERNANCE

8.1 **Daily Reports.** By 9:00 a.m. Eastern each Business Day, Provider shall deliver reports showing, for the preceding day:

(a) orders received, shipped and in backlog;

(b) units on hand by SKU and status;

(c) inbound receipts and open discrepancies;

(d) returns received and graded; and

(e) exceptions.

8.2 **Weekly Reports.** Each week, Provider shall deliver the Service Level performance report required by Section 3.9 of the Agreement and the weekly quality report under Section 11.3 of the Quality Agreement.

8.3 **Monthly Business Review.** The parties' account managers and operations leads shall hold a monthly business review within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month. The review shall cover Service Level performance, service credits, invoiced Fees and pass-through charges, volume trends against the volume assumptions in Section 3.4, open corrective action plans and continuous improvement initiatives.

8.4 **Quarterly Executive Review.** Senior executives of each party shall meet at least once in each calendar quarter to review the relationship, Customer's forecast and growth plans, and Provider's capacity planning for the Facility.

8.5 **Portal Access.** Provider shall provide up to twenty-five (25) named Customer users with read-only access to Provider's customer portal at no additional charge.

## 9. TERMINATION CHARGES

9.1 **Early Termination Fee.** The Early Termination Fee for this Order Form, where payable under the Agreement, is the sum of:

(a) fifty percent (50%) of the Minimum Monthly Fee multiplied by the number of whole calendar months remaining in the Service Commitment Period at the effective date of termination, not to exceed twelve (12) months; and

(b) the unamortized portion of the implementation costs listed in Schedule 2, amortized on a straight-line basis over the first thirty-six (36) months after the Go-Live Date.

9.2 **Mitigation.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its losses resulting from the termination, including by re-allocating space and labor at the Facility to other customers. Provider shall credit against the amount under Section 9.1(a) any fixed costs it avoids as a result.

9.3 **Transition Services Rates.** The rates for additional Transition Services under Section 13.2 of the Agreement are the special project and physical inventory rates in Schedule 1. Packing, staging and loading of Customer Products for transfer are charged at the outbound pallet handling rate in Schedule 1.
