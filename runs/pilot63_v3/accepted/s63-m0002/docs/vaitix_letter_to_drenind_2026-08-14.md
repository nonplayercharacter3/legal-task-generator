## 1. Rate Adjustment Notice

1.1 We refer to Drenind's letter dated June 26, 2026, titled "Notice of Rate Adjustment – Order Form No. 001," which Vaitix received on June 29, 2026 (the "**Rate Adjustment Notice**"). The Rate Adjustment Notice states that Drenind will increase all Fees under the Order Form by 11.5%, effective September 1, 2026. This includes every rate in Part 1 and Part 2 of Schedule 1 and the Minimum Monthly Fee in Part 3 of Schedule 1.

1.2 The Rate Adjustment Notice cites three grounds for the adjustment. The first is increases in warehouse labor costs at the Groveport Fulfillment Center, including wage adjustments implemented by Drenind in the first quarter of 2026. The second is increased occupancy costs for the Facility. The third is increased costs of corrugated cartons, poly mailers and other standard packaging materials. The Rate Adjustment Notice includes a one-page summary of these cost categories. It does not include supporting data, the percentage contribution of each category to the overall 11.5% figure, or any analysis of the costs attributable to the Services performed for Vaitix as distinct from Drenind's costs at the Facility generally.

1.3 On the basis of the Rate Adjustment Notice, the Minimum Monthly Fee would increase from US$95,000 to US$105,925 per calendar month. The per-order processing rate for the first unit would increase from US$2.30 to approximately US$2.56. Annex A sets out our calculation of the effect of the proposed adjustment on the principal rates in Schedule 1, together with our estimate of the resulting annual cost to Vaitix based on our volumes over the twelve months ended July 31, 2026.

## 2. Objection to the Rate Adjustment

2.1 Vaitix objects to the Rate Adjustment Notice and does not accept the adjusted Fees set out in it. This letter is Vaitix's written objection to the Rate Adjustment Notice under Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

2.2 Vaitix objects for the following reasons:

(a) **Magnitude.** An 11.5% across-the-board increase is materially out of line with the cost movements that Drenind has identified. Publicly available indices for warehouse labor in the Columbus metropolitan area show year-over-year wage growth well below that figure over the relevant period. Published indices for corrugated packaging prices have been broadly flat. Even if Drenind's labor, occupancy and packaging costs have risen, those categories together account for only part of the cost of providing the Services. A uniform 11.5% increase across all rates, including rates for activities with little labor or packaging content, is not supported by the grounds stated.

(b) **Volume growth.** Vaitix's outbound order volumes at the Facility over the twelve months ended July 31, 2026 averaged approximately 44,600 orders per month. This is roughly 17% above the 38,000-order average assumed in Section 3.4 of the Order Form. Monthly Core Fees have exceeded the Minimum Monthly Fee in every month since it first applied. Higher volume spreads Drenind's fixed occupancy and overhead costs over a larger base. We would expect any review of Drenind's costs to reflect that benefit.

(c) **Packaging.** The Rate Adjustment Notice applies the increase to the standard packaging materials rate. It also relies on packaging cost increases as one of the grounds for increasing every other rate. Vaitix should not bear the same cost increase twice.

(d) **Minimum Monthly Fee.** The Minimum Monthly Fee is a floor on Monthly Core Fees. It is not a cost-based charge. Because Vaitix's Monthly Core Fees consistently exceed it, the proposed increase to the Minimum Monthly Fee does not compensate Drenind for any current cost. It would only raise Vaitix's exposure if volumes were to fall. We see no basis for including it in a cost-driven adjustment.

(e) **Service Level performance.** As summarized in Annex B, Drenind failed to meet SL-2 (same-day ship) in three of the past twelve months and SL-4 (inventory accuracy) in two of the past twelve months. The returns backlog during January and February 2026 caused SL-5 failures in both months. Vaitix has absorbed meaningful customer service costs in connection with these failures, beyond the service credits applied. We do not consider a substantial rate increase appropriate while these performance issues remain under corrective action.

2.3 Vaitix's position is that the Fees in effect immediately before the Rate Adjustment Notice continue to apply. Vaitix will continue to pay undisputed invoices at those Fees in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Agreement. If Drenind issues invoices for Services performed on or after September 1, 2026 at the adjusted Fees, Vaitix will treat the difference between the adjusted Fees and the existing Fees as a disputed amount under Section 6.3 of the Agreement.

2.4 Vaitix is prepared to negotiate in good faith about a reasonable adjustment that reflects documented changes in Drenind's costs of providing the Services to Vaitix. To allow a productive discussion, we request that Drenind provide the following within ten (10) Business Days after the date of this letter:

(a) a breakdown of the 11.5% figure by cost category, showing the percentage contribution of labor, occupancy, packaging and any other category;

(b) data showing the change in Drenind's direct labor rates, fully loaded, for personnel performing Services for Vaitix at the Facility, comparing the twelve months before the Rate Adjustment Notice with the prior twelve months;

(c) the change in Drenind's occupancy costs for the Facility, and the method by which Drenind allocates those costs to the Services performed for Vaitix;

(d) supplier invoices or price notices supporting the claimed increase in standard packaging material costs; and

(e) an explanation of why the adjustment is applied to the Minimum Monthly Fee and to rates that have little or no labor, occupancy or packaging content.

2.5 We would welcome a meeting between the parties' account managers and commercial leads in the second half of September 2026, once Drenind has provided this information. Please let us know your availability.
