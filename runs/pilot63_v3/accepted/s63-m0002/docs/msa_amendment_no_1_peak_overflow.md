# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Peak-Season Overflow Services**

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2025 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Customer**"); and

**Drenind Fulfillment Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2200 Commerce Parkway, Suite 400, Columbus, Ohio 43217 ("**Provider**").

Customer and Provider are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Provider provides warehousing, fulfillment and returns processing services to Customer.

B. Section 3.5 of the Agreement provides that the parties may agree on arrangements for peak-season overflow capacity by written amendment to the Agreement.

C. Customer's forecast for the 2025 holiday season and later peak seasons anticipates order volumes that may exceed the labor, space and equipment available at the Facilities identified in the Order Form. The parties wish to set out terms under which Provider will provide additional capacity during those periods.

D. The parties wish to amend the Agreement as set out in this Amendment, in accordance with Section 19.4 of the Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Amendment.

1.3 **References.** On and after the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference to the Agreement in any Order Form, the Quality Agreement or any other document delivered under the Agreement, means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment. This Amendment is "Amendment No. 1" for the purposes of the Agreement.

## 2. AMENDMENTS TO DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Affiliate.** The definition of "Affiliate" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is amended and restated in its entirety, for all purposes of the Agreement, to read as follows:

> "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a party: (a) any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise; and (b) in the case of Provider, any Network Partner. "**Network Partner**" means any third-party logistics provider with which Provider has a written capacity-sharing or overflow agreement.

2.2 **New Definitions.** Section 1.1 of the Agreement is amended by adding the following definitions in the appropriate alphabetical order:

> "**Capacity Reservation**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3 of Amendment No. 1.
>
> "**Overflow Facility**" means each facility identified in Schedule B to Amendment No. 1, as updated from time to time in accordance with Section 4 of Amendment No. 1, at which Overflow Services are performed.
>
> "**Overflow Fees**" means the fees for Overflow Services set out in Schedule A to Amendment No. 1.
>
> "**Overflow Services**" means the Services performed during a Peak Season, at a Facility or an Overflow Facility, for order volumes exceeding the Base Daily Capacity.
>
> "**Overflow Trigger**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2 of Amendment No. 1.
>
> "**Peak Forecast**" means the forecast of anticipated peak-season volumes delivered by Customer under Section 3.5 of this Agreement and Section 5.1 of Amendment No. 1.
>
> "**Peak Season**" means, in each Contract Year, the period beginning on October 15 and ending on January 31 of the following calendar year, together with any additional period agreed by the parties in writing under Section 3.4 of Amendment No. 1.

2.3 **Base Daily Capacity.** Section 1.1 of the Agreement is further amended by adding the following definition in the appropriate alphabetical order:

> "**Base Daily Capacity**" means, for each Business Day during a Peak Season, the number of outbound orders that Provider is able to process at the Facilities identified in the Order Form without use of Overflow Facilities, as confirmed by Provider under Section 5.2 of Amendment No. 1.

## 3. PEAK-SEASON OVERFLOW SERVICES

3.1 **Provision of Overflow Services.** During each Peak Season, Provider shall provide Overflow Services to Customer in accordance with the Agreement, this Amendment, the Quality Agreement and the applicable Order Form. Overflow Services are Services for all purposes of the Agreement, and all provisions of the Agreement that apply to Services apply to Overflow Services, except as expressly modified by this Amendment.

3.2 **Overflow Trigger.** Provider shall begin to route orders to Overflow Services on any Business Day on which the number of orders released by Customer for fulfillment, together with orders carried over from previous Business Days, exceeds the Base Daily Capacity (the "**Overflow Trigger**"). Provider shall notify Customer by email to its operations contact on each Business Day on which the Overflow Trigger occurs, stating the estimated number of orders to be processed as Overflow Services and the Facility or Overflow Facility at which they will be processed.

3.3 **Order Allocation.** Provider shall allocate orders to Overflow Services in accordance with the order allocation rules set out in the standard operating procedures approved under Section 3.3 of the Agreement. Unless Customer instructs otherwise in writing, Provider shall:

(a) process orders requiring gift wrapping, kitting or other value-added services at the Facilities identified in the Order Form before allocating them to an Overflow Facility;

(b) allocate orders to an Overflow Facility only where all units in the order are held in inventory at that Overflow Facility, so that no order is split across facilities without Customer's approval; and

(c) give priority, among orders received on the same Business Day, to orders with expedited shipping service levels.

3.4 **Extension of Peak Season.** Customer may, by written notice to Provider given at least fifteen (15) Business Days in advance, request that the Peak Season in any Contract Year begin earlier or end later than the dates specified in the definition of Peak Season, for a period of up to twenty-one (21) days in each case, to accommodate promotional events, product launches or late returns activity. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate any such request. An extension is effective only when confirmed by Provider in writing.

3.5 **Returns during Peak Season.** Returns processing during the Peak Season and for thirty (30) days after its end shall be performed at the Facilities identified in the Order Form, unless Customer approves in writing the processing of returns at an Overflow Facility. Returned Customer Products received at an Overflow Facility shall be inspected, graded and dispositioned in accordance with the Quality Agreement and Customer's returns policy, and returns data shall be transmitted to Customer within the time specified in the Order Form.

3.6 **No Reduction of Base Services.** The availability of Overflow Services does not reduce Provider's obligation to provide the Base Daily Capacity at the Facilities identified in the Order Form, or to meet the Service Levels for orders processed there.

## 4. OVERFLOW FACILITIES

4.1 **Approved Overflow Facilities.** Customer approves the Overflow Facilities listed in Schedule B for the purposes of Section 3.4 of the Agreement, solely for the performance of Overflow Services during a Peak Season and the related inventory positioning and recovery periods described in Section 4.4.

4.2 **Additional Overflow Facilities.** Provider may propose additional Overflow Facilities by written notice to Customer no later than August 15 of any Contract Year. The notice shall identify the address of the proposed facility, its operator, the services to be performed there, the security and fire protection measures in place, and the most recent independent assessment of the operator's information security controls. Customer may approve or reject a proposed Overflow Facility in its reasonable discretion within fifteen (15) Business Days after receipt of the notice and all requested information. On Customer's written approval, Schedule B is deemed updated to include the proposed facility.

4.3 **Subcontracting at Overflow Facilities.** Customer's approval of an Overflow Facility operated by a person other than Provider constitutes Customer's consent under Section 16.3 of the Agreement to the subcontracting of Overflow Services to that operator at that Overflow Facility. Provider remains fully responsible for the performance of Overflow Services, and for the acts and omissions of each operator of an Overflow Facility, as if they were Provider's own. Before any Customer Products or Customer Data are made available to an operator, Provider shall ensure that the operator is bound by written obligations consistent with Sections 7.1, 7.2, 9 and 10 of the Agreement.

4.4 **Inventory Positioning.** Provider may transfer Customer Products from the Facilities identified in the Order Form to an Overflow Facility beginning no earlier than September 15 of each Contract Year, in the quantities and SKUs set out in a positioning plan approved by Customer. No later than February 28 following the end of each Peak Season, Provider shall transfer all Customer Products remaining at each Overflow Facility back to the Facilities identified in the Order Form, unless Customer instructs otherwise in writing. Provider shall bear the cost of transfers between facilities, except to the extent Schedule A provides otherwise.

4.5 **Segregation and Identification.** At each Overflow Facility, Provider shall ensure that Customer Products are stored in a segregated, clearly marked area, are identified as Customer's property and are not commingled with goods of any other person.

4.6 **Removal of an Overflow Facility.** Customer may withdraw its approval of an Overflow Facility by written notice to Provider if: (a) a Security Incident occurs involving Customer Data held at or accessed from that Overflow Facility; (b) Customer Products at that Overflow Facility suffer loss or damage exceeding the shrinkage allowance in the Order Form, measured on an annualized basis; (c) Overflow Services at that Overflow Facility fail to meet any critical Service Level in two (2) consecutive weeks; or (d) the operator of that Overflow Facility becomes subject to any event described in Section 12.6 of the Agreement. Provider shall cease routing orders to the affected Overflow Facility within two (2) Business Days after the notice and shall transfer Customer Products from it as Customer reasonably directs.

## 5. FORECASTING AND CAPACITY RESERVATION

5.1 **Peak Forecast.** The Peak Forecast to be delivered under Section 3.5 of the Agreement shall set out, by week, Customer's anticipated order volumes, units per order, inbound receipts and returns for the Peak Season, together with the dates of any major promotional events. Customer may update the Peak Forecast at any time before September 30 of the applicable Contract Year. The Peak Forecast is non-binding, except to the extent of any Capacity Reservation made under Section 5.3.

5.2 **Capacity Plan.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after receipt of the Peak Forecast, Provider shall deliver to Customer a written capacity plan for the Peak Season. The capacity plan shall confirm the Base Daily Capacity, identify the Overflow Facilities expected to be used and the daily overflow capacity available at each, describe Provider's labor plan, including temporary staffing levels and training schedules, and identify any constraints that Provider anticipates in meeting the Peak Forecast. The parties shall meet within ten (10) Business Days after delivery of the capacity plan to review it.

5.3 **Capacity Reservation.** Customer may, by written notice to Provider no later than September 30 of each Contract Year, reserve a specified number of overflow orders per Business Day for all or part of the Peak Season (a "**Capacity Reservation**"). Provider shall make the reserved capacity available to Customer and shall not allocate it to any other customer. Customer shall pay the capacity reservation fee set out in Schedule A for each reserved order per Business Day, whether or not the reserved capacity is used. Fees for orders actually processed within the reserved capacity are credited against the capacity reservation fee for the same week.

5.4 **Capacity Shortfalls.** If Provider anticipates that it will be unable to meet the Peak Forecast or any Capacity Reservation, Provider shall promptly notify Customer in writing, describe the anticipated shortfall and its cause, and propose measures to address it. If Provider fails to make available any reserved capacity under a Capacity Reservation, Customer is not required to pay the capacity reservation fee for the capacity not made available, and Section 17.3 of the Agreement applies to the affected volumes as if the shortfall were caused by a Force Majeure Event, without limiting Customer's other rights and remedies.

## 6. OVERFLOW FEES

6.1 **Overflow Fees.** Customer shall pay the Overflow Fees set out in Schedule A for Overflow Services. Overflow Fees replace the corresponding Fees under the Order Form for orders processed as Overflow Services, and are not payable in addition to them. Fees for Services performed at the Facilities identified in the Order Form within the Base Daily Capacity continue to be payable at the rates set out in the Order Form.

6.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Overflow Fees in accordance with Section 6 of the Agreement. Each invoice shall separately identify Overflow Fees by Overflow Facility and activity unit, and shall be accompanied by daily data showing the number of orders released by Customer, the Base Daily Capacity and the number of orders processed as Overflow Services.

6.3 **Pass-Through Charges.** Carrier charges for shipments from Overflow Facilities are pass-through charges under Section 5.2 of the Agreement. Where shipment from an Overflow Facility results in a higher carrier charge than shipment of the same order from the Facilities identified in the Order Form, because of zone, carrier or service differences, Provider shall bear the difference, unless the order was allocated to the Overflow Facility at Customer's written instruction.

6.4 **Minimum Monthly Fee.** Overflow Fees count toward any minimum monthly fee under the Order Form.

6.5 **Rate Adjustments.** The Overflow Fees are Fees for the purposes of Section 5.4 of the Agreement.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS DURING PEAK SEASON

7.1 **Application of Service Levels.** The Service Levels apply to Overflow Services, except that, for orders processed as Overflow Services at an Overflow Facility on any Business Day on which the Overflow Trigger occurs, the same-day ship Service Level is measured as shipment by the end of the next Business Day after release of the order.

7.2 **Combined Measurement.** Provider shall measure and report performance against the Service Levels separately for the Facilities identified in the Order Form and for each Overflow Facility, and on a combined basis. Service credits under the Order Form are calculated on whichever basis results in the higher credit for the affected month.

7.3 **Peak-Season Reporting.** During the Peak Season, Provider shall provide Customer with daily reports showing, for each Facility and Overflow Facility, orders received, orders shipped, backlog, units on hand, labor hours and exceptions. The parties' account managers shall hold a daily operations call during the weeks of the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday and the two weeks before December 25, and a weekly call at other times during the Peak Season.

7.4 **Post-Season Review.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each Peak Season, Provider shall deliver to Customer a written post-season review. The review shall cover Service Level performance, capacity use, Overflow Fees invoiced, inventory accuracy at each Overflow Facility and lessons learned. The parties shall meet to discuss the review and any changes to the capacity plan for the following Peak Season.

## 8. INVENTORY, RISK OF LOSS AND INSURANCE AT OVERFLOW FACILITIES

8.1 **Title and Liens.** Sections 7.1, 7.2 and 7.3 of the Agreement apply to Customer Products at each Overflow Facility. Provider shall obtain from the operator of each Overflow Facility not owned by Provider a written waiver of any warehouse lien, warehouseman's lien or other possessory or statutory lien on Customer Products, in a form reasonably acceptable to Customer, before any Customer Products are delivered to that Overflow Facility.

8.2 **Risk of Loss.** Provider bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, Customer Products while in transit between the Facilities identified in the Order Form and any Overflow Facility, and while held at any Overflow Facility, on the same terms as Section 7.5 of the Agreement.

8.3 **Cycle Counts and Reconciliation.** Provider shall perform cycle counts at each Overflow Facility in accordance with the Quality Agreement. Within ten (10) Business Days after the return of Customer Products from an Overflow Facility under Section 4.4, Provider shall deliver to Customer a reconciliation of units transferred to, shipped from, returned to and transferred back from that Overflow Facility. Shrinkage at Overflow Facilities is included in the calculation of shrinkage under Section 7.6 of the Agreement.

8.4 **Insurance.** Provider shall ensure that the insurance required under Section 15.4 of the Agreement covers Customer Products and Provider's operations at each Overflow Facility. Provider shall deliver to Customer, before the start of each Peak Season, certificates of insurance confirming that coverage, together with certificates evidencing the general liability, warehouse legal liability and workers' compensation insurance maintained by the operator of each Overflow Facility not owned by Provider.

8.5 **Access and Inspection.** Section 7.7 of the Agreement applies to each Overflow Facility. Provider shall ensure that the operator of each Overflow Facility permits Customer and its designees to exercise those rights.

## 9. CUSTOMER DATA AND SYSTEMS

9.1 **Systems.** Provider shall process all Overflow Services through Provider's warehouse management system, or through a system integrated with it that has been tested and approved by Customer before the start of the Peak Season. Inventory at each Overflow Facility shall be visible to Customer through Provider's customer portal in the same manner as inventory at the Facilities identified in the Order Form.

9.2 **Data Access.** Provider shall limit the access of personnel at each Overflow Facility to the Customer Data reasonably required to perform Overflow Services. Section 9 of the Agreement applies to all Customer Data processed at or accessed from an Overflow Facility.

9.3 **Testing.** No later than October 1 of each Contract Year, Provider shall complete end-to-end testing of order routing, inventory synchronization, shipment confirmation and returns data feeds for each Overflow Facility to be used during the Peak Season, and shall provide the test results to Customer.

## 10. TERM OF OVERFLOW ARRANGEMENTS

10.1 **Term.** This Amendment takes effect on the Amendment Effective Date and continues in effect for the Term of the Agreement.

10.2 **Effect of Termination of an Order Form.** If the Order Form under which Services are provided at the Facilities identified in it expires or is terminated, Provider's obligations under Sections 3 through 9 of this Amendment cease with respect to that Order Form at the end of any Transition Period, and Section 13 of the Agreement applies to Customer Products held at any Overflow Facility.

10.3 **Transition.** On expiry or termination of the Agreement, Provider shall include Customer Products held at each Overflow Facility in the joint physical inventory and transfer described in Section 13.1 of the Agreement, and Section 13.3 of the Agreement applies to them.

## 11. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

11.1 **Continuing Effect.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement, each Order Form and the Quality Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

11.2 **Quality Agreement.** The parties shall, before October 15, 2025, agree on any additions to the Quality Agreement reasonably needed to address receiving, storage, handling and returns grading at the Overflow Facilities. Until those additions are agreed, the Quality Agreement applies at each Overflow Facility as it applies at the Facilities identified in the Order Form.

11.3 **No Waiver.** Nothing in this Amendment waives any right or remedy of either party arising before the Amendment Effective Date.

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 **Representations.** Each party represents and warrants to the other that it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Amendment, that the execution and performance of this Amendment have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate or company action, and that this Amendment constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws and general principles of equity.

12.2 **Governing Law and Disputes.** Sections 18.1 through 18.5 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment and to any dispute arising out of or relating to it.

12.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Amendment shall be given in accordance with Section 19.1 of the Agreement. Operational notifications under Sections 3.2 and 7.3 may be given by email to the operations contacts designated by each party in the standard operating procedures.

12.4 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by an electronic signature service complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act are as effective as original signatures.

12.5 **Costs.** Each party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with the negotiation, preparation and execution of this Amendment.
