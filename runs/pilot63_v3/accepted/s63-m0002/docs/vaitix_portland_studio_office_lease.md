This Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of October 7, 2024 (the "**Lease Date**") by and between:

**Alderpoint Properties LLC**, an Oregon limited liability company, with its principal office at 900 SW Fifth Avenue, Suite 1750, Portland, Oregon 97204 ("**Landlord**"); and

**Vaitix Outdoor Co.**, with its principal place of business at 1420 NW Raleigh Street, Portland, Oregon 97209 ("**Tenant**").

Landlord and Tenant are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**."

## 2. PREMISES

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant has the non-exclusive right, in common with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobbies, elevators, stairways, corridors, restrooms on multi-tenant floors, loading dock, freight elevator, bicycle storage room and other common areas of the Property designated by Landlord from time to time (the "**Common Areas**").

2.2 **Rentable Area.** The parties agree that the rentable areas stated in the Basic Lease Information are conclusive for all purposes of this Lease and are not subject to remeasurement, except if the Building is physically expanded or reduced, in which case Tenant's Share shall be adjusted accordingly.

2.3 **Condition.** Subject to Landlord's obligations under Section 5 and Exhibit C, and to Landlord's repair obligations under Section 8, Tenant accepts the Premises in their condition on the Lease Date. Landlord warrants that on the date Landlord delivers possession of the Premises to Tenant, the Building structure, roof, and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire and life safety and elevator systems serving the Premises will be in good working order and in compliance with applicable laws, including the Americans with Disabilities Act as applied to the Common Areas.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease.

3.2 **Early Access.** Tenant and its contractors may enter the Premises from the date Landlord delivers possession for the purpose of constructing the Tenant Improvements under Exhibit C and installing furniture, fixtures, equipment and cabling. Early access is subject to all terms of this Lease other than the obligation to pay Base Rent and Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses.

3.3 **Delay in Delivery.** If Landlord does not deliver possession of the Premises to Tenant by October 15, 2024, the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date shall each be extended by one day for each day of delay. If Landlord has not delivered possession by January 15, 2025, Tenant may terminate this Lease by written notice to Landlord given before possession is delivered, and Landlord shall promptly return any prepaid rent and the Security Deposit.

3.4 **Option to Extend.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for a period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the Extension Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under this Section 3.4. Tenant shall exercise the option, if at all, by written notice to Landlord given not earlier than fifteen (15) months and not later than nine (9) months before the Expiration Date. The option may be exercised only if no Event of Default exists when Tenant gives notice. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual base rent then being agreed for comparable office and creative space in comparable buildings in the Northwest Portland submarket, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the parties have not agreed on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the Portland office market. The two brokers shall jointly select a third broker with the same qualifications, and Fair Market Rent shall be the average of the two determinations closest to each other. Each party bears the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.

| January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2027 | $31.83 | $313,525.50 | $26,127.13 |

4.2 **Abated Rent.** Base Rent for the first three (3) full calendar months of the Term is abated. Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses is payable during the abatement period.

4.3 **Payment.** Rent shall be paid by ACH or wire transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing, without demand and, except as expressly provided in this Lease, without deduction or set-off. Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis. "**Rent**" means Base Rent, Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and all other amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease.

4.4 **Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) business days after written notice from Landlord that it is past due, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to four percent (4%) of the overdue amount. Landlord is not required to give notice of a late payment more than twice in any twelve (12) month period, after which the late charge applies to any installment not received within five (5) business days after its due date.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

5.1 **Tenant's Share.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for that year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year. Landlord shall deliver a reasonable estimate before the start of each calendar year, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each installment of Base Rent.

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, repairing, insuring and managing the Property in a manner consistent with comparable buildings in Northwest Portland, including real property taxes and assessments, utilities for the Common Areas, janitorial service for the Common Areas, landscaping, security, insurance premiums and a property management fee not exceeding three percent (3%) of gross receipts of the Property. Operating Expenses exclude: (a) capital expenditures, except the annual amortized cost, over their useful life, of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date; (b) costs of leasing, marketing, tenant improvements and brokerage commissions; (c) principal, interest and other financing costs; (d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants; (e) costs caused by Landlord's negligence or violation of law; (f) costs of remediating hazardous materials not introduced by Tenant; (g) Landlord's general corporate overhead and salaries of personnel above the level of building manager; (h) charitable and political contributions; and (i) depreciation.

5.3 **Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for any calendar year after 2026 shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) over the prior year, on a non-cumulative basis. "**Controllable Operating Expenses**" means all Operating Expenses other than real property taxes, insurance premiums, utility costs, snow and ice removal, and costs required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date.

5.4 **Annual Statement.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses for that year and Tenant's Share. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due, or refunded if the Term has ended. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receipt of the statement.

5.5 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied during any calendar year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be adjusted to reflect the amount they would have been at ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.6 **Review.** Tenant may, within one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of an annual statement, review Landlord's books and records supporting it at Landlord's office during normal business hours. If the review shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable review costs in addition to refunding the overpayment.

## 6. USE

6.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for the Permitted Use. This includes the design and development of apparel, footwear and outdoor equipment; operation of sewing machines, cutting tables, steamers, heat presses and similar light sample-making equipment; product photography and video production; storage of samples, fabrics and trims reasonably related to those activities; and general office and meeting use. Tenant shall not use the Premises for retail sales to the public, warehousing or distribution of finished goods for sale, or any use that creates noise, odor or vibration that unreasonably interferes with other tenants.

6.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises and to any alterations it makes. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, the base building systems and the Common Areas, the cost of which may be included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 5.2.

6.3 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the rules and regulations in Exhibit D and any reasonable, non-discriminatory changes to them of which Landlord gives Tenant at least thirty (30) days' notice. If any rule conflicts with this Lease, this Lease controls.

6.4 **Hours and Access.** Tenant has access to the Premises, the Building and the parking lot twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to Landlord's reasonable security procedures. Building standard hours are 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturday, excluding holidays generally recognized in Portland.

## 7. TENANT IMPROVEMENTS

7.1 **Work Letter.** Tenant shall construct the initial improvements to the Premises (the "**Tenant Improvements**") in accordance with Exhibit C. Landlord shall fund the Tenant Improvement Allowance in accordance with Exhibit C.

7.2 **Unused Allowance.** Up to US$5.00 per rentable square foot of any unused Tenant Improvement Allowance may be applied toward Tenant's costs of cabling, furniture, signage and moving, if Tenant requests disbursement before December 31, 2025.

## 8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

8.1 **Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair, and in compliance with applicable laws, the roof, foundation, exterior walls, structural elements, Common Areas, elevators, and the base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, heating, ventilation and air conditioning ("**HVAC**") and fire and life safety systems up to the point of connection to the Premises. The costs of this work are included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Section 5.2.

8.2 **Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises, including interior walls, floor coverings, doors, interior glass, supplemental HVAC units installed by Tenant, and Tenant's own fixtures and equipment, in good condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted.

8.3 **Landlord's Failure to Repair.** If Landlord fails to begin a repair for which it is responsible within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, or within forty-eight (48) hours in an emergency that threatens persons or property or materially interferes with Tenant's use of the Premises, and then diligently complete it, Tenant may perform the repair within the Premises. Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after receipt of an invoice with reasonable supporting documentation.

## 9. ALTERATIONS

9.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting, carpeting and installation of furniture, shelving and equipment, that do not affect the Building structure or systems and cost less than US$50,000 per project, provided Tenant gives Landlord at least ten (10) business days' prior notice.

9.2 **Performance.** All alterations shall be performed in a good and workmanlike manner, by licensed and insured contractors, in compliance with applicable laws. Tenant shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant and shall cause any such lien to be released or bonded within thirty (30) days after notice of its filing.

9.3 **Removal.** At the time Landlord consents to an alteration, Landlord shall state whether Tenant must remove it at the end of the Term. Tenant is not required to remove the Tenant Improvements or any alteration that Landlord did not designate for removal when it gave consent.

## 10. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

10.1 **Services.** Landlord shall provide: (a) HVAC to the Premises during building standard hours; (b) electricity for lighting and normal office and studio equipment; (c) water for restrooms, kitchenettes and drinking; (d) janitorial service for the Common Areas; (e) passenger elevator service at all times and freight elevator service during building standard hours; and (f) maintenance of the Building's fire and life safety systems.

10.2 **Separate Metering.** Electricity to the Premises is separately metered. Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly for electricity consumed in the Premises, and electricity to the Premises is excluded from Operating Expenses.

10.3 **After-Hours HVAC.** Tenant may request HVAC outside building standard hours at Landlord's actual cost, currently US$45.00 per hour per floor, which Landlord may adjust no more than once a year to reflect changes in its actual cost.

10.4 **Interruption.** If an interruption of essential services caused by Landlord's negligence or by a failure of base building systems that Landlord is obliged to maintain makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than three (3) consecutive business days after Tenant's notice, Rent shall abate in proportion to the area made untenantable from the fourth business day until the services are restored.

## 11. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

11.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain during the Term: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least US$2,000,000 per occurrence and US$4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds; (b) property insurance on a special form basis covering Tenant's personal property, fixtures and the Tenant Improvements for their full replacement cost; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of at least US$1,000,000 per accident; and (d) business interruption insurance in an amount reasonably determined by Tenant.

11.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain during the Term: (a) property insurance on a special form basis covering the Building, excluding Tenant's personal property, for its full replacement cost; and (b) commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas with limits of at least US$2,000,000 per occurrence and US$5,000,000 in the aggregate. Premiums for Landlord's insurance are included in Operating Expenses.

11.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss of or damage to its property to the extent the loss or damage is covered, or would have been covered, by the property insurance that party is required to carry under this Lease. Each party shall cause its property insurance policies to include a waiver of subrogation consistent with this Section 11.3.

11.4 **Indemnity by Tenant.** Subject to Section 11.3, Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord and its members, managers, employees and agents from all claims, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors in the Building, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents.

11.5 **Indemnity by Landlord.** Subject to Section 11.3, Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Tenant and its directors, officers, employees and agents from all claims, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents.

11.6 **Survival.** Sections 11.4 and 11.5 survive the expiration or termination of this Lease as to events occurring before it.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

12.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant a reasonable estimate by a licensed architect or contractor of the time required to restore them. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 12.2, Landlord shall restore the Building and the base building improvements in the Premises with reasonable diligence, and Tenant shall restore the Tenant Improvements, using the proceeds of its insurance.

12.2 **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate if it shows that restoration cannot be completed within two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty, or if the casualty occurs during the last twelve (12) months of the Term and restoration will take more than sixty (60) days. Tenant may also terminate this Lease if Landlord does not substantially complete the restoration within sixty (60) days after the estimated completion date.

12.3 **Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the area of the Premises that is untenantable as a result of the casualty, from the date of the casualty until the Premises are restored, plus a reasonable period not exceeding sixty (60) days for Tenant to restore the Tenant Improvements.

12.4 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the parking area that Tenant cannot reasonably continue to operate, is taken by eminent domain or conveyed under threat of it, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. Otherwise, this Lease continues and Rent is reduced in proportion to the area taken. Landlord is entitled to the award, except that Tenant may make a separate claim for its moving expenses, its trade fixtures and the unamortized cost of improvements it paid for, if the separate claim does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 13. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

13.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 13.3, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to a request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the request and the information reasonably needed to evaluate it.

13.2 **Excess Rent.** If Tenant assigns this Lease or sublets any part of the Premises with Landlord's consent, Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent or other consideration received by Tenant in excess of the Rent allocable to the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer, including brokerage commissions, legal fees, improvements and allowances.

13.3 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) business days' prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to: (a) any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with Tenant; (b) any successor to Tenant by merger, consolidation or conversion; or (c) any purchaser of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets or of the business conducted in the Premises (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"), provided that in the case of clauses (b) and (c) the transferee has a tangible net worth at least equal to Tenant's tangible net worth on the Lease Date. A transfer of equity interests in Tenant, or in any entity that owns Tenant, is not an assignment of this Lease.

13.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees in writing.

## 14. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

14.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay any Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the failure, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding, or an involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.2 **Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may, in addition to any other remedy available at law, terminate this Lease by written notice and recover from Tenant: (a) unpaid Rent accrued through termination; (b) the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Term exceeds the rental loss that Tenant proves could reasonably be avoided, discounted to present value at the discount rate of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco plus one percent (1%); and (c) Landlord's reasonable costs of recovering possession and reletting. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

14.3 **Landlord Default.** Landlord is in default if it fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it. Tenant may then pursue any remedy available at law or in equity, subject to Section 8.3 and Section 18.6.

## 15. SECURITY DEPOSIT

15.1 **Deposit.** Tenant has delivered the Security Deposit to Landlord as security for Tenant's performance of this Lease. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore the amount applied within ten (10) business days after written demand.

15.2 **Reduction.** If no Event of Default has occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to US$24,625 on the first day of the thirty-seventh (37th) month of the Term, and Landlord shall refund the excess within thirty (30) days.

15.3 **Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises, with an itemized statement of any amounts applied.

## 16. SUBORDINATION, ESTOPPEL AND QUIET ENJOYMENT

16.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage or deed of trust now or later encumbering the Property, provided that the holder delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from the holder of any existing deed of trust within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

16.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after written request from the other, deliver a certificate confirming, to the extent true, that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists. Neither party may request a certificate more than twice in any twelve (12) month period.

16.3 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without disturbance by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

## 17. SURRENDER AND HOLDOVER

17.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and condemnation excepted. Tenant shall remove its personal property, its trade fixtures and any alterations Landlord designated for removal under Section 9.3, and shall repair any damage caused by the removal.

17.2 **Holdover.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. Tenant shall pay Base Rent for the holdover period at one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days, and one hundred fifty percent (150%) after that, prorated daily. Tenant is liable for Landlord's consequential damages caused by the holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord's written notice that Landlord has entered into a lease of the Premises with a new tenant.

## EXHIBIT C: WORK LETTER

C.1 **Plans.** Tenant shall engage a licensed architect, reasonably approved by Landlord, to prepare plans for the Tenant Improvements. Landlord shall approve or provide reasonable comments on the plans within ten (10) business days after receipt.

C.3 **Allowance.** Landlord shall disburse the Tenant Improvement Allowance in monthly progress payments within thirty (30) days after receipt of a draw request accompanied by invoices, conditional lien waivers and the architect's certification of the work completed.
