2.4 **Business Forms.** In accordance with Section 2.4 of the Distribution Agreement, no pre-printed or standard terms on any acknowledgment, invoice, bill of lading, packing list or other business form issued by either Party in connection with this Order Form have any effect.

## 6. DELIVERY TERMS AND DOCUMENTATION

6.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Shipment 1 FCA and Shipment 2 DAP, as stated in Sections 4.1 and 4.2, in accordance with Section 6.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

6.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Products in each shipment pass to Distributor upon delivery under the applicable delivery term, in accordance with Section 6.6 of the Distribution Agreement. For Shipment 1, delivery occurs when the Products are loaded onto Distributor's carrier at Supplier's Secaucus facility. For Shipment 2, delivery occurs when the Products are placed at Distributor's disposal on the arriving trailer at the Baltimore Airport Distribution Center, ready for unloading.

6.3 **Shipping Documents.** Supplier shall deliver the following documents with each shipment:

(a) a packing list stating this Order Form number, each SKU, the number of cases and pallets of each SKU, and the lot code and best-by date of each lot;

(b) a Certificate of Analysis for each lot in the shipment, complying with Section 7.3 of the Quality Agreement; and

(c) a bill of lading stating this Order Form number, the number of pallets, the total gross weight and the trailer seal number.

6.4 **Advance Notice.** At least one (1) Business Day before each shipment, Supplier shall email to Distributor's Order Contact and receiving contact an advance shipping notice. The notice shall attach electronic copies of the packing list and of each Certificate of Analysis for that shipment.

6.5 **Labeling and Lot Coding.** Each unit, case and pallet shall be lot-coded and labeled in accordance with Section 8.2 of the Quality Agreement and Supplier's lot-coding conventions notified to Distributor. Each pallet label shall also state this Order Form number and the shipment number (1 or 2).

## 8. RECEIVING AND INSPECTION

8.1 **Receiving.** Distributor shall receive each shipment in accordance with Section 9.1 of the Quality Agreement. For Shipment 2, Distributor shall record the trailer seal number and the condition of the seal on arrival.

8.2 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect each shipment, and give notice of any shortage, damage or obvious nonconformity, in accordance with Section 6.9 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier shall replace or credit any short-shipped, damaged or nonconforming Products as provided in that Section, at Distributor's option.

8.3 **Replacement Shipments.** If Distributor elects replacement under Section 8.2, Supplier shall deliver the replacement Products to the same destination as the original shipment, at no charge to Distributor. Supplier shall make the delivery within ten (10) Business Days after Distributor's notice, or by such other date as the Order Contacts agree in writing.

8.4 **Reserved Rights.** Receipt, inspection or acceptance of any shipment under this Order Form does not waive any of Distributor's rights for latent defects, Defective Products or breach of warranty under the Distribution Agreement or the Quality Agreement.

## 9. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

9.1 **Invoices.** Supplier shall issue a separate invoice for each shipment upon shipment, in accordance with Section 7.6 of the Distribution Agreement. Each invoice shall reference Order Form No. SC-OF-001 and the shipment number. Each invoice shall state the Products, quantities, lot numbers and prices for that shipment. The invoice for Shipment 2 shall also state the delivery charge in Section 5.3 as a separate line.

9.2 **Invoice Delivery.** Supplier shall send each invoice by email to accountspayable@stareiskprovisions.com, with a copy to Distributor's Order Contact.

9.3 **Payment.** Distributor shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by ACH or wire transfer to the account that Supplier has designated in writing under Section 7.7 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier's remittance details for this Order Form are those stated in Supplier's letter of account designation dated March 2, 2026. Distributor shall not act on any change to those details unless it is confirmed by telephone with Supplier's Order Contact using the telephone number in Section 12.

9.4 **Disputes and Credits.** Sections 7.8, 7.9 and 7.11 of the Distribution Agreement apply to invoices issued under this Order Form. Any credit for short-shipped, damaged or nonconforming Products under Section 8.2 shall reference the original invoice and the affected lot codes.

9.5 **Taxes.** Distributor has provided Supplier with resale exemption certificates for the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and the State of Maryland. Under Section 7.10 of the Distribution Agreement, Supplier shall not charge sales tax on Products covered by those certificates. If Supplier determines that any tax is chargeable on the delivery charge for Shipment 2, Supplier shall state that tax separately on the invoice.

## 10. CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

10.1 **Change or Cancellation.** Distributor may change or cancel all or any part of either shipment under this Order Form in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Distribution Agreement. For Shipment 1, the scheduled delivery date for that purpose is the pickup date in Section 4.1. For Shipment 2, it is the delivery date in Section 4.2.

10.2 **Form of Change.** Distributor's Order Contact may request any change or cancellation by email to Supplier's Order Contact. Each request shall identify the affected shipment and lines. Supplier shall confirm each change by email within two (2) Business Days after receipt. A change to quantities that results in less than one full pallet of any SKU in a shipment requires Supplier's agreement.

10.3 **Date Changes Requested by Supplier.** If Supplier cannot meet a requested date in Section 4, Supplier shall propose an alternative date in its acceptance under Section 11. A proposed alternative date binds Distributor only if Distributor's Order Contact confirms it in writing. If Distributor does not confirm it, Supplier's acceptance applies to the requested date, and Section 4.7 applies to any late shipment.

10.4 **Substitutions.** Supplier shall not substitute any SKU for another or ship any Product not listed in Section 3.1 without Distributor's prior written consent.

## 11. ACCEPTANCE

11.1 **Acceptance Period.** Supplier shall accept or reject this Order Form in writing within three (3) Business Days after receipt, in accordance with Section 6.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor is delivering this Order Form to Supplier by email on the Order Form Date. If Supplier does not reject this Order Form within that period, it is accepted.

11.2 **Method of Acceptance.** Supplier may accept this Order Form by countersigning it below. Alternatively, Supplier's Order Contact may send an email to Distributor's Order Contact that identifies this Order Form by number and states that it is accepted.

11.3 **Partial Acceptance.** Supplier may not accept this Order Form in part, except with Distributor's written agreement.

11.4 **Pricing on Acceptance.** Under Section 7.1 of the Distribution Agreement, the prices that apply to this Order Form are those in the Price List in effect on the date Supplier accepts it. Those prices are stated in Section 5.2.
