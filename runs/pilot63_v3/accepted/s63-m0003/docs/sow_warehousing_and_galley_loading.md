**SOW No. SK-SOW-001**

**Issued under the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 between Fevound Galley Foods, Inc. and Stareisk Provisions, LLC**

This Statement of Work for Warehousing and Galley-Loading Services (this "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between:

**FEVOUND GALLEY FOODS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**"); and

**STAREISK PROVISIONS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. BACKGROUND, INCORPORATION AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Background.** Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Section 8.1 of the Distribution Agreement provides that Distributor shall perform the Services described in the SOW. This Statement of Work is the SOW referred to in the Distribution Agreement. It describes the Services, the facilities from which they are performed, the service levels and the fees for the Services.

1.2 **Incorporation.** This Statement of Work is issued under and incorporates the terms of the Distribution Agreement, in accordance with Section 2.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

1.3 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Statement of Work have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement. Those terms include "Airline Customer," "Business Day," "Contract Year," "days," "Defective Product," "Fees," "Products," "Quality Agreement," "Recall," "Services," "Specifications" and "Territory."

1.4 **Additional Definitions.** In this Statement of Work:

(a) "**Facility**" means each warehouse facility of Distributor listed in Section 3.1, and any additional facility added under Section 3.3.

(b) "**Galley-Loading Point**" means the catering unit, airside loading dock, commissary or other location at a Station at which an Airline Customer, or its caterer, accepts delivery of provisions for loading onto aircraft. It also means the aircraft itself, where Distributor loads galley carts and containers directly onto the aircraft.

(c) "**Load Plan**" means the written or electronic loading instruction issued by an Airline Customer, or by Distributor on its behalf, stating the provisions to be loaded for a particular flight or rotation.

(d) "**Operations Contact**" means the individual named for a Party in Section 11.1, as replaced from time to time under Section 11.2.

(e) "**Pallet**" means a standard 48" x 40" GMA pallet of Product built to the pallet configuration stated in Part A of Schedule 1 to the Quality Agreement.

(f) "**Service Month**" means each calendar month, or part of a calendar month, during the SOW Term.

(g) "**Service Levels**" means the service levels set out in Section 6.

(h) "**Station**" means each airport in the Territory at which Distributor performs galley-loading services under Section 4.6, as listed in Section 3.2.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 **Distributor's Operations.** Distributor operates a temperature-controlled distribution, warehousing and galley-loading network serving Airline Customers in the Territory. Through that network, Distributor receives, stores, handles and delivers to Galley-Loading Points all onboard food and beverage provisions, including fresh and frozen meals, snacks, beverages and bar items, in accordance with the Load Plans and requirements of each Airline Customer.

2.2 **Services.** Distributor shall perform the following Services for the Products as part of its operations described in Section 2.1:

(a) receiving and inbound inspection under Section 4.1;

(b) ambient storage under Section 4.2;

(c) inventory management, stock rotation and lot tracking under Section 4.3;

(d) case-breaking and pick-and-pack under Section 4.4;

(e) staging and dispatch under Section 4.5;

(f) galley-loading under Section 4.6;

(g) hold, return and destruction handling under Section 4.7;

(h) Recall support under Section 4.8; and

(i) reporting under Section 7.

2.3 **Integration of the Products.** Distributor shall integrate the Products into its existing receiving, storage, picking and galley-loading workflows at each Facility and Station. Distributor may combine Products with other provisions on the same delivery vehicle, galley cart or container for an Airline Customer. When it does so, Distributor shall comply with the segregation requirements of Section 9.5 of the Quality Agreement and the transport requirements of Section 9.6 of the Quality Agreement.

2.4 **Excluded Activities.** The Services do not include the following:

(a) the manufacture, formulation, reformulation, relabeling or repackaging of any Product, other than case-breaking and the application of shipping labels to outer packaging as permitted by Section 4.4;

(b) release testing, Certificates of Analysis or any other activity allocated to Supplier under the Quality Agreement;

(c) the sale of Products to Airline Customers, which Distributor conducts for its own account under the Distribution Agreement; and

(d) onboard service of the Products to passengers, which is performed by each Airline Customer's cabin crew.

## 4. SERVICE DESCRIPTIONS

4.1 **Receiving and Inbound Inspection.**

(a) Distributor shall receive each shipment of Products at the Facility designated in the applicable Order Form. Distributor shall check the shipping documents, trailer condition, seal and case and pallet condition in accordance with Section 9.1 of the Quality Agreement.

(b) Each Facility shall receive inbound shipments of Products between 6:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, by appointment. Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to receive shipments outside those hours on request.

(c) Distributor shall carry out the inspection required by Section 6.9 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall give notice of any shortage, damage or obvious nonconformity within the period stated in that Section.

(d) Within twenty-four (24) hours after unloading, Distributor shall record each lot received in its warehouse management system. The record shall state the SKU, lot code, best-by date, quantity, Facility location and the Order Form number.

4.2 **Storage.**

(a) Distributor shall store the Products in the ambient storage zones of each Facility under the storage conditions stated in Schedule 1 to the Quality Agreement.

(b) Distributor shall store Pallets in racking or floor locations that respect the maximum stacked height of 60 inches. Distributor shall not double-stack Pallets.

(c) Distributor shall store the Products away from chemicals, non-food items and any material that could contaminate them, in accordance with Section 9.5 of the Quality Agreement.

(d) If Products are exposed to conditions outside the storage conditions in Schedule 1 to the Quality Agreement, Distributor shall follow the excursion procedure in Section 9.3 of the Quality Agreement.

4.3 **Inventory Management, Stock Rotation and Lot Tracking.**

(a) Distributor shall maintain the Products in its warehouse management system at SKU, lot and location level.

(b) Distributor shall rotate stock on a first-expired, first-out basis, in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Quality Agreement.

(c) Distributor shall flag in its system any lot of Product that reaches ninety (90) days before its best-by date. Distributor shall notify Supplier's Operations Contact of any such lot in the next weekly report under Section 7.1.

(d) Distributor shall conduct a cycle count of all Products at each Facility at least once per month, and a full physical count at least once per Contract Year. Distributor shall investigate and reconcile any variance and report it under Section 7.

(e) Distributor shall maintain traceability records sufficient to meet Section 9.6 of the Distribution Agreement and Section 10 of the Quality Agreement.

4.4 **Case-Breaking and Pick-and-Pack.**

(a) Distributor may break full cases of Product into smaller quantities to fulfil Load Plans, in accordance with Section 8.4 of the Quality Agreement.

(b) Distributor may apply its own shipping labels to outer packaging. Distributor shall not cover or remove any Supplier label, lot code or best-by date on a unit of Product.

(c) Distributor shall keep each opened case identified by SKU, lot code and best-by date until all units in it have been picked. Distributor shall store opened cases in a designated case-break area of the Facility.

(d) Distributor shall pick units against each Load Plan and shall record the lot codes picked for each Airline Customer, Station and delivery date.

4.5 **Staging and Dispatch.**

(a) Distributor shall stage picked Products for dispatch in clean, closed containers or galley carts that protect them from contamination and damage.

(b) Distributor shall transport the Products from each Facility to the Galley-Loading Points in vehicles that meet Section 9.6 of the Quality Agreement.

(c) Each dispatch shall be accompanied by a delivery manifest stating the Airline Customer, Station, flight or rotation where known, SKUs, quantities and lot codes.

4.6 **Galley-Loading.**

(a) Distributor shall deliver Products to the Galley-Loading Points at each Station in accordance with each Airline Customer's Load Plan, delivery window and operating procedures.

(b) Where an Airline Customer engages Distributor to load galley carts and containers directly onto aircraft, Distributor shall do so in accordance with Section 9.7 of the Quality Agreement and the Airline Customer's loading diagrams, weight limits and security seal requirements.

(c) Distributor shall record, for each delivery, the lot codes of Products loaded, by Airline Customer, Station and delivery date.

(d) Distributor shall collect unused, sealed and undamaged units of Product returned from aircraft by an Airline Customer, inspect them, and return them to inventory only if (i) the unit remains in its original sealed packaging with its original label and lot code, (ii) the unit has remained within the Airline Customer's controlled custody, and (iii) the Airline Customer's procedures permit reuse. Distributor shall dispose of all other returned units under Section 4.7.

4.7 **Hold, Return and Destruction.**

(a) Distributor shall place on hold any Product that is the subject of a Nonconformity, an excursion, a Complaint investigation or a Recall, in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

(b) Distributor shall return or destroy Products on hold as directed by Supplier under the Quality Agreement. Distributor shall use a licensed waste-disposal contractor for destruction and shall provide written certification of destruction stating the SKUs, lot codes and quantities destroyed.

(c) Distributor shall destroy, without further instruction from Supplier, any unit of Product that has passed its best-by date, that has damaged primary packaging, or that is returned from aircraft and cannot be returned to inventory under Section 4.6(d). Distributor shall record each such destruction in its weekly report.

4.8 **Recall Support.** In any Recall, Distributor shall perform the actions described in Section 13.4 of the Quality Agreement within the time frames reasonably required by the recall strategy. Distributor's costs of retrieval, storage, transport and destruction in a Recall are allocated in accordance with Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor shall not charge Fees under Section 8 for Recall activities whose costs are recoverable under Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 5. SUPPLIER RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1 **Product Information.** In accordance with Section 8.4 of the Distribution Agreement, Supplier shall provide Distributor's Operations Contact with the pallet configurations, case dimensions, case weights, storage temperatures, stacking limits and lot-coding conventions for each SKU. Supplier shall provide that information before the first shipment of each SKU and shall give at least thirty (30) days' notice of any change to it.

5.2 **Advance Shipping Notices.** Supplier shall send an advance shipping notice to the receiving Facility at least one (1) Business Day before each shipment, with electronic copies of the packing list and the Certificate of Analysis for each lot.

5.3 **Disposition Decisions.** Supplier shall give disposition instructions for Products on hold within the periods stated in the Quality Agreement. Where Supplier does not give instructions within those periods, Distributor may continue to hold the affected Products and charge the storage Fees under Section 8 for the period of the delay.

5.4 **Cooperation.** Supplier shall make its Operations Contact reasonably available to Distributor to resolve operational questions about the Products.

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 **Service Levels.** Distributor shall meet the following Service Levels for the Products, measured for each Service Month across all Facilities and Stations:

| Service | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound receiving | Lots recorded in warehouse management system within 24 hours after unloading | 99% |
| Inventory accuracy | Units counted matching system records, by SKU and lot, at monthly cycle count | 99.5% |
| Stock rotation | Picks made from the earliest-expiring available lot | 99% |
| Order fill | Units of Product delivered against units of Product requested in Load Plans, where inventory was available | 99% |
| On-time galley delivery | Deliveries to Galley-Loading Points within the Airline Customer's delivery window | 98% |
| Traceability | Trace requests answered with source, location and recipients of the lot | Within 4 hours, 100% |
| Excursion notice | Notice to Supplier's Quality Contact after an excursion is identified | Within 1 Business Day, 100% |

6.2 **Exclusions.** A failure to meet a Service Level is excused to the extent caused by any of the following:

(a) a Force Majeure Event under Section 16 of the Distribution Agreement;

(b) any of the matters described in Section 8.3 of the Distribution Agreement;

(c) late delivery, short delivery or nonconforming delivery of Products by Supplier;

(d) a change to a Load Plan made by an Airline Customer after the cut-off time in that Airline Customer's procedures; or

(e) Supplier's failure to perform its responsibilities under Section 5.

6.3 **Service Level Failures.** If Distributor fails to meet any Service Level in a Service Month, Distributor shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the end of that Service Month, provide Supplier with a written explanation of the failure and a corrective action plan. If Distributor fails to meet the same Service Level in three (3) consecutive Service Months, Distributor shall credit Supplier five percent (5%) of the pick-and-pack and galley-loading Fees invoiced for the third of those Service Months. That credit is in addition to Supplier's other rights under the Distribution Agreement.

6.4 **Review.** The Parties shall review the Service Levels at least once per Contract Year and may adjust them by change order under Section 10.

## 7. REPORTING

7.1 **Weekly Reports.** By the second Business Day of each week, Distributor shall provide Supplier's Operations Contact with a report for the previous week stating:

(a) inventory on hand at each Facility, by SKU and lot code, with best-by dates;

(b) receipts during the week, by Order Form number, SKU and lot code;

(c) units of Product dispatched to each Station, by SKU;

(d) lots within ninety (90) days of their best-by date;

(e) units placed on hold, returned to inventory under Section 4.6(d), or destroyed, with reasons; and

(f) any excursion, Nonconformity or Complaint raised during the week, with a cross-reference to the Quality Contacts' communications.

7.2 **Monthly Service Level Report.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Service Month, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a report of its performance against each Service Level for that Service Month, including the results of the monthly cycle count.

7.3 **Quarterly Reports.** The weekly and monthly reports supplement, and do not replace, the quarterly reports required by Section 5.5 of the Distribution Agreement. The Parties shall review Service Level performance at their quarterly meetings under that Section.

7.4 **Format.** Distributor shall provide reports in electronic spreadsheet format by email, or through an online portal to which Distributor gives Supplier's Operations Contact access.

## 8. FEES

8.1 **Fees.** Supplier shall pay Distributor the following Fees for the Services:

| Service | Unit | Fee (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound receiving and put-away | Per Pallet received | $9.75 |
| Ambient storage | Per Pallet position per Service Month (or part) | $18.50 |
| Case-break | Per case opened | $1.85 |
| Pick-and-pack | Per case equivalent picked | $1.20 |
| Galley-loading delivery | Per delivery of Products to a Galley-Loading Point | $4.50 |
| Hold-area storage | Per Pallet position per Service Month (or part) | $18.50 |
| Destruction under Section 4.7(b) | Per Pallet equivalent, plus contractor's disposal charges at cost | $35.00 |
| Additional services requested by Supplier in writing | Per labor hour | $48.00 |

8.2 **Basis of Calculation.** Storage Fees are calculated on the greater of (a) the number of Pallet positions occupied by Products at the end of the Service Month and (b) the average number of Pallet positions occupied by Products during the Service Month, based on daily system counts. A "case equivalent" means one full case of the applicable SKU, as stated in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement. The galley-loading delivery Fee applies once for each delivery of Products to a Galley-Loading Point, regardless of the number of SKUs in the delivery.

8.3 **No Other Charges.** Distributor shall not charge Supplier for any activity under this Statement of Work other than the Fees in Section 8.1 and any applicable taxes. In particular, Distributor shall not charge for receiving inspections, cycle counts, reports, traceability requests, mock traces or mock recalls.

8.4 **Fixed for First Contract Year.** The Fees in Section 8.1 are fixed for the first Contract Year. Thereafter, Distributor may adjust the Fees not more than once per Contract Year by change order under Section 10.

8.5 **Invoicing.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Service Month, Distributor shall invoice Supplier for the Fees for that Service Month. Each invoice shall reference SOW No. SK-SOW-001 and state, for each Fee line, the units, the rate and the total. Distributor shall send each invoice by email to Supplier's accounts payable address designated in writing.

8.6 **Payment.** Supplier shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated by Distributor in writing. Sections 7.8, 7.9, 7.11 and 7.12 of the Distribution Agreement apply to invoices issued under this Statement of Work, with the Parties' roles reversed as the context requires.

8.7 **Taxes.** Supplier is responsible for sales, use and similar taxes on the Services, other than taxes based on Distributor's net income, property or employees. Distributor shall state any such tax separately on its invoice.

## 9. PERSONNEL, SUBCONTRACTING AND INSURANCE

9.1 **Personnel.** Distributor shall assign sufficient trained personnel to perform the Services. Distributor shall train its personnel who handle the Products in accordance with Section 5.3 of the Distribution Agreement and Section 9.8 of the Quality Agreement.

9.2 **Subcontracting.** Distributor may subcontract transportation and other elements of the Services to qualified third parties in accordance with Section 19.3 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors. Distributor shall require each subcontractor that handles the Products to comply with the storage, handling, segregation and traceability requirements of the Quality Agreement.

9.3 **Insurance.** Distributor shall maintain during the SOW Term, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) warehouse legal liability insurance with a limit of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence;

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000;

(d) aviation ground handling and airside liability insurance as required by each airport authority at which Distributor performs galley-loading Services; and

(e) workers' compensation insurance as required by Applicable Law, and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000.

Distributor shall provide certificates of insurance to Supplier upon request.

9.4 **Care of Products.** Distributor shall take reasonable care of Products held at the Facilities. Title to and risk of loss of the Products pass in accordance with Section 6.6 of the Distribution Agreement, and nothing in this Statement of Work changes that allocation.
