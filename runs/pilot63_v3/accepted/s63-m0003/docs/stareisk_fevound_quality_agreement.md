# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**FEVOUND GALLEY FOODS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**"); and

**STAREISK PROVISIONS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under it, Supplier has appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the SkyCrate Products to Airline Customers in the Territory.

B. Section 9.1 of the Distribution Agreement provides that the Parties shall comply with the Quality Agreement. This Quality Agreement sets out each Party's food-safety, quality assurance, traceability and related responsibilities for the Products.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement allocates responsibility between the Parties for the safety and quality of the Products from manufacture through delivery to Airline Customers. It covers supplier qualification, the Specifications, lot release, certificates of analysis, hazard analysis and preventive controls, allergen control, complaint handling, audits and Recall procedures.

1.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products listed in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement and to any product later added to Exhibit A. It also applies to Product samples used in marketing activities.

1.3 **Services.** Distributor's storage, handling, pick-and-pack and galley-loading activities for the Products under the SOW are subject to the Distributor responsibilities in this Quality Agreement.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Distribution Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the Distribution Agreement. Those terms include "Affiliate," "Airline Customer," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "days," "Defective Product," "Products," "Recall," "Services," "SOW," "Specifications" and "Territory."

2.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**Approved Facility**" means a manufacturing, co-packing or storage facility of Supplier that has been approved under Section 4.

(b) "**Certificate of Analysis**" or "**CoA**" means a document issued by Supplier for a lot of Product that states the results of the release testing required by Schedule 1 and confirms that the lot conforms to the Specifications.

(c) "**Complaint**" means any written or oral communication from an Airline Customer, passenger, crew member, governmental authority or other person that alleges that a Product is unsafe, nonconforming, mislabeled, adulterated or otherwise defective, or that it has caused illness or injury.

(d) "**Critical Complaint**" means a Complaint that alleges illness, injury, an allergic reaction, the presence of foreign material capable of causing injury, or tampering.

(e) "**Food Safety Plan**" means Supplier's written food safety plan for each Approved Facility prepared under 21 C.F.R. Part 117, Subpart C.

(f) "**Major Change**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

(g) "**Nonconformity**" means any failure of a Product, a lot of Product or a process to meet the Specifications, this Quality Agreement or Applicable Law.

(h) "**Quality Contact**" means the individual named for a Party in Schedule 2, as replaced from time to time under Section 3.3.

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES AND QUALITY CONTACTS

3.1 **Supplier Responsibilities.** Supplier is responsible for the safety and quality of each Product as manufactured, packaged, labeled, stored and shipped by Supplier. Supplier is responsible for the formulation, ingredient sourcing, process validation, preventive controls, allergen management, release testing and labeling of each Product.

3.2 **Distributor Responsibilities.** Distributor is responsible for maintaining the condition of each Product from delivery to Distributor until delivery to an Airline Customer. That includes receiving, storage, handling, transport, lot segregation, stock rotation and traceability in accordance with this Quality Agreement and the Specifications.

3.3 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact with authority and technical competence to address quality and food-safety matters. Each Party shall also designate a backup contact reachable outside normal business hours. A Party may replace its Quality Contact or backup contact by written notice to the other Party's Quality Contact.

3.4 **Quality Review Meetings.** The Quality Contacts shall meet at least quarterly, in person or by video conference, to review Complaints, Nonconformities, corrective actions, audit results, regulatory matters and proposed changes. These meetings may take place together with the quarterly meetings under Section 5.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 4. APPROVED FACILITIES AND SUPPLIER QUALIFICATION

4.1 **Approved Facilities.** Supplier shall manufacture and package the Products only at the Approved Facilities. As of the Effective Date, the only Approved Facility is Supplier's manufacturing facility at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094.

4.2 **New Facilities.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least ninety (90) days' written notice before manufacturing any Product at a facility that is not an Approved Facility, including any co-packer facility. The notice shall include the facility's FDA registration number, its current certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative-benchmarked scheme, its most recent certification audit report and a summary of its Food Safety Plan for the Products. Distributor may audit the proposed facility under Section 12 before approval. Distributor shall not unreasonably withhold or delay approval of a facility that meets the requirements of this Section 4.

4.3 **Certification.** Supplier shall maintain certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative-benchmarked scheme for each Approved Facility. Supplier shall provide Distributor with a copy of each new or renewed certificate within ten (10) Business Days after issue. Supplier shall notify Distributor within two (2) Business Days if any certification is suspended, withdrawn or downgraded, or if a certification audit results in a major or critical non-conformance relating to the Products.

4.4 **Registration.** Supplier shall maintain each Approved Facility's registration with the FDA under Section 415 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and shall provide current registration numbers to Distributor upon request.

4.5 **Ingredient and Packaging Suppliers.** Supplier shall maintain a written supplier approval program under 21 C.F.R. Part 117, Subpart G for all ingredients and food-contact packaging used in the Products. Supplier shall verify that each supplier of a raw material subject to a supply-chain-applied control is appropriately controlled.

4.6 **Distributor Facilities.** Distributor shall store the Products only at the warehouse facilities identified in the SOW, or at another facility that Distributor notifies to Supplier in writing at least thirty (30) days in advance. Each such facility shall be registered with the FDA where required by Applicable Law and shall be operated under a written sanitation and pest-control program.

## 5. SPECIFICATIONS AND CHANGE CONTROL

5.1 **Specifications.** The Specifications for each Product are set out in, or referenced in, Schedule 1. They include formulation, ingredient statement, allergen profile, nutritional information, microbiological and physical limits, packaging, case pack, palletization, labeling, labeled shelf life and storage conditions. Supplier shall maintain a current, dated and version-controlled specification document for each SKU and shall provide it to Distributor upon request.

5.2 **Conformity.** Supplier shall ensure that each lot of Product delivered to Distributor conforms to the Specifications in effect on the date of manufacture of that lot.

5.3 **Major Changes.** Supplier shall not make any of the following changes (each, a "**Major Change**") without at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Distributor:

(a) any change to the formulation or ingredient statement of a Product;

(b) any change that adds, removes or alters an allergen, including an allergen present by cross-contact;

(c) any change to the thermal process, retort parameters, water activity or other critical control parameter of a Product;

(d) any change to the primary packaging, the labeled shelf life or the storage conditions of a Product; or

(e) any change to the Approved Facility at which a Product is made.

5.4 **Information on Changes.** Each notice of a Major Change shall describe the change, the reason for it, the first lot code affected and any change to the label. Supplier shall provide updated allergen and nutrition information at the same time. Supplier shall not ship changed Product until Distributor has had a reasonable opportunity to notify affected Airline Customers.

5.5 **Minor Changes.** Supplier may make other changes that do not affect the safety, labeling or Specifications of a Product without prior notice. Supplier shall record those changes in its change-control system.

## 6. FOOD SAFETY PLAN, PREVENTIVE CONTROLS AND ALLERGEN CONTROL

6.1 **Food Safety Plan.** Supplier shall maintain and implement a Food Safety Plan for each Approved Facility. Each plan shall include a hazard analysis, preventive controls, monitoring, corrective action and verification procedures, a supply-chain program and a recall plan, each as required by 21 C.F.R. Part 117. Supplier shall reanalyze each Food Safety Plan at least every three (3) years and whenever a significant change is made in activities at the Approved Facility.

6.2 **Thermal Processing.** For each retort meal kit, Supplier shall comply with 21 C.F.R. Parts 108 and 113 to the extent applicable. Supplier shall maintain scheduled processes established by a qualified process authority, and shall keep retort records for each lot.

6.3 **Allergen Control.** Supplier shall maintain a written allergen control program for each Approved Facility. The program shall cover segregation of allergenic ingredients, production scheduling, changeover cleaning and validation, label verification at line start-up and at each label change, and training of production personnel. Supplier shall declare all major food allergens, including sesame, on each Product label in accordance with Applicable Law.

6.4 **Foreign Material Control.** Supplier shall use metal detection or x-ray inspection, validated for each Product format, on every finished unit of Product. Supplier shall maintain records of device verification for each production run.

6.5 **Environmental Monitoring.** Supplier shall maintain an environmental monitoring program for each Approved Facility appropriate to the hazards of each Product. That program shall include sampling for *Listeria* species in any area where ready-to-eat components are exposed after any lethality step.

6.6 **Food Defense.** Supplier shall maintain a food defense plan for each Approved Facility to the extent required by 21 C.F.R. Part 121. Supplier shall use tamper-evident packaging for each unit of Product.

## 7. LOT RELEASE AND CERTIFICATES OF ANALYSIS

7.1 **Release Testing.** Supplier shall test each lot of Product in accordance with Schedule 1 before release. Supplier shall use laboratories accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 for pathogen testing.

7.2 **Positive Release.** Supplier shall not ship any lot of Product until it has received and reviewed all release test results for that lot and confirmed that the lot conforms to the Specifications.

7.3 **Certificates of Analysis.** Supplier shall deliver a CoA for each lot with each shipment, as required by Section 6.5 of the Distribution Agreement. Each CoA shall state the SKU, lot code, date of manufacture, best-by date, the tests performed, the results and the Specification limits. Each CoA shall be signed or electronically approved by a member of Supplier's quality personnel.

7.4 **Retained Samples.** Supplier shall retain at least six (6) units of each lot of Product, stored under the labeled storage conditions, until three (3) months after the end of that lot's labeled shelf life. Supplier shall make retained samples available for testing in connection with any Complaint or Recall.

## 8. LABELING, PACKAGING AND LOT CODING

8.1 **Labeling.** Supplier is responsible for the content and accuracy of all labels on the Products. Labels shall comply with Applicable Law, including requirements for the statement of identity, net quantity, ingredient statement, allergen declaration, nutrition information, name and place of business, and country of origin where required.

8.2 **Lot Coding.** Supplier shall mark each unit, case and pallet with a legible lot code and best-by date. Each pallet shall carry a label stating the SKU, lot code, quantity and best-by date. Supplier shall give Distributor written notice of its lot-coding conventions and of any change to them.

8.3 **Packaging.** Food-contact packaging shall comply with Applicable Law for its intended use. Case and pallet packaging shall protect the Products in the handling and transport conditions described in Schedule 1.

8.4 **No Relabeling.** Distributor shall not relabel or repackage any Product. However, Distributor may break full cases into smaller quantities for galley-loading, and may apply its own shipping labels to outer packaging, as described in the SOW. Distributor shall not cover or remove any Supplier label, lot code or best-by date on a unit of Product when doing so.

## 9. DISTRIBUTOR RECEIVING, STORAGE AND HANDLING

9.1 **Receiving.** On receipt of each shipment, Distributor shall check that the shipment is accompanied by a packing list, bill of lading and CoA for each lot. Distributor shall check the condition of the trailer, the integrity of the seal where applicable, and the condition of the cases and pallets. Distributor shall record any discrepancy and report it to Supplier under Section 6.9 of the Distribution Agreement.

9.2 **Storage Conditions.** Distributor shall store the Products under the storage conditions stated in Schedule 1. Distributor shall continuously monitor storage temperatures at each facility where Products are stored and shall keep temperature records for at least two (2) years.

9.3 **Excursions.** If Products are exposed to conditions outside the storage conditions in Schedule 1, Distributor shall place the affected Products on hold and notify Supplier's Quality Contact within one (1) Business Day. Supplier shall advise Distributor within three (3) Business Days whether the affected Products may be released, must be reworked or must be destroyed.

9.4 **Stock Rotation.** Distributor shall rotate stock on a first-expired, first-out basis. Distributor shall not deliver to an Airline Customer any unit of Product with less remaining shelf life than the minimum agreed with that Airline Customer, and in any event shall not deliver any unit after its best-by date.

9.5 **Segregation.** Distributor shall keep each lot of Product segregated and identifiable. Distributor shall store Products away from chemicals, non-food items and any material that could contaminate them. Distributor shall keep any Product on hold, or awaiting return or destruction, in a clearly marked hold area.

9.6 **Transport.** Distributor shall transport the Products in clean vehicles that maintain the storage conditions in Schedule 1 and comply with 21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart O, where applicable.

9.7 **Galley-Loading.** Distributor shall load Products into aircraft galley carts and containers in accordance with the SOW and the requirements of each Airline Customer. Distributor shall record the lot codes of Products loaded for each Airline Customer, by delivery date and station.

9.8 **Personnel.** Distributor shall train its personnel who handle the Products in food-handling practices, allergen awareness, hygiene and the requirements of this Quality Agreement. Distributor shall keep training records.

## 10. TRACEABILITY AND RECORDS

10.1 **Traceability.** Each Party shall maintain lot-level traceability records for the Products in its possession or control. Those records shall be sufficient to identify, within four (4) hours of a request, the source, location and recipients of each lot, consistent with Section 9.6 of the Distribution Agreement.

10.2 **Supplier Records.** Supplier's records shall link each lot of finished Product to the lots of ingredients and packaging used in it and to the production, process, sanitation and testing records for that lot.

10.3 **Distributor Records.** Distributor's records shall link each lot of Product received to the facility at which it was stored and to each Airline Customer, station and delivery date to which units of that lot were delivered.

10.4 **Mock Traces.** Each Party shall conduct a mock trace of at least one lot of Product at least once in each Contract Year. Each Party shall share the results with the other Party at the next quality review meeting.

10.5 **Retention.** Each Party shall retain the records required by this Quality Agreement for at least two (2) years after the expiration of the labeled shelf life of the relevant lot, or any longer period required by Applicable Law.

## 11. COMPLAINTS AND NONCONFORMITY

11.1 **Forwarding Complaints.** Each Party shall forward to the other Party's Quality Contact any Complaint it receives, as required by Section 9.4 of the Distribution Agreement. A Party shall forward a Critical Complaint within twenty-four (24) hours after receipt and any other Complaint within three (3) Business Days.

11.2 **Information.** Each forwarded Complaint shall include, to the extent available, the SKU, lot code, best-by date, the date and flight or station involved, the nature of the Complaint and the contact details of the complainant. Distributor shall use reasonable efforts to obtain the affected unit or its packaging and send it to Supplier.

11.3 **Investigation.** Supplier shall investigate each Complaint that relates to the manufacture, packaging or labeling of a Product. Supplier shall provide a written preliminary response to Distributor within five (5) Business Days after receipt of a Critical Complaint and within fifteen (15) Business Days after receipt of any other Complaint. Supplier shall provide a final written report, including root cause and corrective and preventive actions, within thirty (30) days after receipt. Distributor shall investigate any Complaint that relates to storage, handling or galley-loading on the same timetable.

11.4 **Responses to Airline Customers.** Distributor shall manage communications with Airline Customers about Complaints, using information that Supplier provides. Supplier shall consult with Distributor before communicating with any Airline Customer about a Complaint, consistent with Section 11.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

11.5 **Nonconformity.** If either Party identifies a Nonconformity in any Product, it shall notify the other Party's Quality Contact within one (1) Business Day. Distributor may place affected Products on hold pending investigation. Supplier shall decide on the disposition of nonconforming Products, in consultation with Distributor, within five (5) Business Days after notice. Distributor shall not deliver nonconforming Products to an Airline Customer.

11.6 **Corrective Action.** The Party responsible for a Nonconformity shall implement corrective and preventive actions and report them at the next quality review meeting.

11.7 **Trend Reporting.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with a quarterly summary of Complaints for the Products, by SKU and category, for review at the quality review meeting.

## 12. AUDITS AND REGULATORY INSPECTIONS

12.1 **Distributor Audits.** Distributor, or a qualified third-party auditor engaged by Distributor and bound by confidentiality obligations, may audit any Approved Facility once in each Contract Year. Distributor shall give at least fifteen (15) Business Days' notice of a routine audit. Distributor may conduct an additional for-cause audit on two (2) Business Days' notice following a Critical Complaint, a Recall or a regulatory action relating to the Products. Each audit shall take place during normal business hours and shall be conducted so as to minimize disruption to Supplier's operations.

12.2 **Supplier Audits.** Supplier may audit any facility at which Distributor stores the Products once in each Contract Year on the same terms.

12.3 **Audit Findings.** The audited Party shall respond in writing to audit findings within thirty (30) days with a corrective action plan, and shall implement that plan within the agreed timetable.

12.4 **Third-Party Audits.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, upon request, with a summary of the most recent certification audit report for each Approved Facility and the related corrective actions.

12.5 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall notify the other Party within two (2) Business Days after any inspection, inquiry, warning letter, Form 483 observation or other regulatory action that relates to the Products, as required by Section 9.3 of the Distribution Agreement. Each Party shall provide copies of related correspondence, redacted to remove information unrelated to the Products. Each Party shall give the other Party a reasonable opportunity to comment on any response to a governmental authority that concerns the other Party's responsibilities under this Quality Agreement.

12.6 **Samples.** If a governmental authority collects samples of any Product from either Party, that Party shall notify the other Party within one (1) Business Day and shall share the results when received.

## 13. RECALLS

13.1 **Recall Plans.** Each Party shall maintain a written recall plan covering the Products. Supplier's recall plan shall form part of its Food Safety Plan.

13.2 **Notice.** If either Party believes that a Recall may be necessary or appropriate, it shall notify the other Party's Quality Contact immediately, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, as required by Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement. The notice shall identify the affected SKUs and lot codes and the reason for the possible Recall.

13.3 **Decision and Classification.** Supplier shall decide whether to conduct a Recall in accordance with Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier shall be responsible for communications with the FDA and any other governmental authority about the Recall, including proposed classification, recall strategy and effectiveness checks.

13.4 **Distributor Actions.** In any Recall, Distributor shall, within the time frames reasonably required by the recall strategy:

(a) place all affected Products in its inventory on hold;

(b) identify the Airline Customers and stations to which affected lots were delivered, using its traceability records;

(c) remove affected Products from inventory, galley carts and aircraft galleys to the extent within Distributor's access and control;

(d) communicate with affected Airline Customers using recall notices approved by Supplier;

(e) return or destroy affected Products as directed by Supplier, and provide written certification of destruction; and

(f) provide Supplier with reconciliation records of affected Products located, retrieved and destroyed.

13.5 **Costs.** Recall costs are allocated in accordance with Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

13.6 **Mock Recalls.** The Parties shall conduct a joint mock recall of at least one lot of Product once in each Contract Year and shall review the results at the next quality review meeting.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

14.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Quality Agreement and any claim arising out of Product nonconformity, contamination or food safety shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, with exclusive jurisdiction in the state and federal courts located in New York County.

14.2 **Technical Review.** Before either Party commences any proceeding about a quality or food-safety matter, other than for injunctive or other equitable relief, the Quality Contacts shall meet within five (5) Business Days after written request by either Party to review the technical facts. Each Party shall share the test results, records and retained samples relevant to the matter.

## 15. TERM AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

15.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for as long as the Distribution Agreement remains in effect. It also continues for any period after expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement during which Distributor holds, sells off or returns Products under Section 14.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

15.2 **Survival.** Sections 10, 11, 12.5, 13 and 14 survive the expiration or termination of this Quality Agreement for as long as any Product delivered under the Distribution Agreement remains within its labeled shelf life, and for the record-retention periods in Section 10.5.

15.3 **Schedules.** Supplier may update Schedule 1 to reflect changes made in accordance with Section 5 by written notice to Distributor's Quality Contact. Either Party may update its details in Schedule 2 under Section 3.3. Any other change to this Quality Agreement requires a written instrument signed by an authorized representative of each Party.

15.4 **Notices.** Operational communications under this Quality Agreement, including notices of Complaints, Nonconformities, excursions and Recalls, may be given by email and telephone between the Quality Contacts. All other notices shall be given in accordance with Section 19.1 of the Distribution Agreement, with a copy to the other Party's Quality Contact.

15.5 **Confidentiality.** Information exchanged under this Quality Agreement, including Food Safety Plans, audit reports, test results and formulations, is Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Distribution Agreement. Disclosures permitted by Section 13.5 of the Distribution Agreement may be made.

15.6 **Assignment.** This Quality Agreement may be assigned only together with the Distribution Agreement, in accordance with Section 19.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

15.7 **Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
