## 1. Defined Terms and Background

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement. Those terms include "Airline Customer," "Business Day," "Contract Year," "days," "Marks," "Products," "Term" and "Territory."

1.2 **Additional Definitions.** In this letter:

(a) "**Allowance**" means the marketing allowance described in Section 2.

(b) "**Approved Activity**" means a Marketing Activity that Supplier has approved under Section 4.

(c) "**Claim**" means a request for reimbursement or credit of Eligible Costs submitted under Section 5.

(d) "**Eligible Costs**" means the costs described in Section 3.2 that Distributor incurs in carrying out an Approved Activity.

(e) "**Marketing Activity**" means an activity described in Section 3.1 that promotes the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory.

(f) "**Marketing Plan**" means the plan described in Section 4.1.

(g) "**Marketing Contacts**" means the individuals named in Section 4.4, as either Party may replace them by written notice to the other.

1.3 **Background.** The Parties discussed a launch marketing allowance during their negotiations. They agreed that any allowance would be documented separately in writing. Supplier wishes to support Distributor's efforts under Section 5.1 of the Distribution Agreement during the first two Contract Years. During that period, Distributor will be presenting the Products to the catering and onboard-services managers of Airline Customers for the first time.

## 2. The Marketing Allowance

2.1 **Amount.** Supplier shall make the following Allowance available to Distributor:

| Period | Allowance Amount (USD) |
|---|---|
| First Contract Year (March 2, 2026 through March 1, 2027) | $75,000.00 |
| Second Contract Year (March 2, 2027 through March 1, 2028) | $45,000.00 |

2.2 **Nature of Allowance.** The Allowance is a fund from which Supplier will reimburse or credit Eligible Costs for Approved Activities, in accordance with this letter. The Allowance is not a payment for the right to distribute the Products. It is not a fee, rebate or discount on the purchase price of any Product. It does not change any price in the Price List or the operation of Section 7 of the Distribution Agreement.

2.3 **Co-Funding.** Supplier shall fund one hundred percent (100%) of Eligible Costs for the following Approved Activities: product sampling, the preparation and printing of SkyCrate sell sheets, and the preparation of SkyCrate menu inserts. For all other Approved Activities, Supplier shall fund fifty percent (50%) of Eligible Costs and Distributor shall fund the remaining fifty percent (50%). In each case, Supplier's funding comes only from the Allowance available for the relevant Contract Year.

2.4 **Allocation by Contract Year.** Eligible Costs are charged against the Allowance for the Contract Year in which the related Approved Activity takes place. If an Approved Activity spans two Contract Years, the Eligible Costs are charged against the Allowance for the Contract Year in which the activity begins.

2.5 **Carry-Forward.** Supplier shall carry forward to the second Contract Year up to $15,000.00 of the first Contract Year Allowance that is unused as of March 1, 2027. The carried-forward amount is added to the Allowance for the second Contract Year. Any other unused portion of the first Contract Year Allowance lapses on March 1, 2027. Any unused portion of the second Contract Year Allowance lapses on March 1, 2028. Distributor has no claim to any lapsed amount.

2.6 **No Further Commitment.** Supplier has no obligation under this letter to provide any marketing allowance for any Contract Year after the second Contract Year. The Parties may agree in writing to further marketing support for later periods.

## 3. Marketing Activities and Eligible Costs

3.1 **Marketing Activities.** The following activities are Marketing Activities, provided they promote the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory:

(a) **Menu presentations.** Presentations of the Products to the catering, procurement and onboard-services personnel of Airline Customers. These include tasting sessions held at Distributor's facilities or at an Airline Customer's offices or catering operations.

(b) **Product sampling.** The provision of Product samples to Airline Customers for menu evaluation, crew tasting panels and passenger trials on designated flights.

(c) **Sell sheets and presentation materials.** The design, production and printing of SkyCrate sell sheets, presentation decks, product cards and allergen and nutrition summaries prepared for Airline Customers. These materials supplement the materials that Supplier provides at no charge under Section 15.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

(d) **Menu inserts and onboard materials.** The design and printing of SkyCrate inserts for an Airline Customer's buy-on-board menu, seat-back card or digital menu, where the Airline Customer has agreed to carry the Products.

(e) **Industry events.** Exhibition space, display materials and travel costs for Distributor personnel attending onboard-services, airline catering or airline procurement events held in the Territory, where Distributor features the Products.

(f) **Other activities.** Any other activity that Supplier approves in writing as a Marketing Activity.

3.2 **Eligible Costs.** Eligible Costs are the reasonable, documented, out-of-pocket costs that Distributor pays to third parties to carry out an Approved Activity. They include the following:

(a) printing, design and production charges;

(b) exhibition space, booth rental, display fabrication and event registration fees;

(c) reasonable coach-class travel, lodging and meal costs of Distributor personnel attending an Approved Activity outside the Philadelphia metropolitan area, in line with Distributor's written travel policy;

(d) the cost of shipping Product samples and display materials to an Approved Activity; and

(e) for Product samples that Distributor draws from its own inventory under Section 3.4, the price Distributor paid Supplier for those samples.

3.3 **Excluded Costs.** The following are not Eligible Costs:

(a) salaries, wages, commissions, bonuses and benefits of Distributor's own personnel;

(b) Distributor's overhead, rent, utilities, vehicles and general administrative costs;

(c) costs of the Services under the SOW, which are paid only under the SOW;

(d) entertainment, gifts, gratuities and hospitality for personnel of Airline Customers, except modest refreshments served at a menu presentation or tasting session;

(e) payments, rebates, credits, listing fees, slotting fees or other amounts paid or credited to any Airline Customer; and

(f) costs incurred before the date of this letter or after the end of the second Contract Year.

3.4 **Product Samples.** Distributor may draw Product samples for Approved Activities from its own inventory. Alternatively, Distributor may request that Supplier ship sample cases directly to Distributor or to the location of an Approved Activity. Supplier shall ship sample cases that it supplies directly at no charge to Distributor. Supplier shall charge the purchase price of those cases, at the Price List prices then in effect, against the Allowance. Product samples are subject to the Quality Agreement, including its storage, handling and traceability requirements. Distributor shall record the lot number of each sample case used in an Approved Activity.

3.5 **Sample Labeling.** Every Product sample shall be provided to an Airline Customer in its original sealed packaging with its original label and lot code. Distributor shall not open, repackage or relabel any Product sample. However, Distributor may open individual units for tasting at a menu presentation in accordance with the food-handling practices described in Section 5.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its approval of any request that is consistent with the approved Marketing Plan and that does not exceed the unused Allowance for the relevant Contract Year. If Supplier does not respond within five (5) Business Days, Distributor may send a reminder. If Supplier does not respond within three (3) Business Days after the reminder, the request is approved.

## 5. Claims, Reimbursement and Credits

5.1 **Submission of Claims.** Distributor may submit a Claim for the Eligible Costs of an Approved Activity at any time after Distributor has paid those costs. Each Claim shall be submitted no later than sixty (60) days after the end of the Approved Activity. In any event, each Claim shall be submitted no later than sixty (60) days after the end of the Contract Year to which the Eligible Costs are charged under Section 2.4.

5.2 **Content of Claims.** Each Claim shall include the following:

(a) the name, date and location of the Approved Activity and a reference to the approval under Section 4;

(b) an itemized statement of the Eligible Costs, with copies of third-party invoices and proof of payment;

(c) for Product samples drawn from Distributor's inventory, the SKUs, quantities and lot numbers of the samples used;

(d) samples or photographs of printed materials and displays that bear the Marks; and

(e) the funding share applicable under Section 2.3 and the amount claimed.

5.3 **Review of Claims.** Supplier shall review each Claim within fifteen (15) Business Days after receipt. Within that period, Supplier may ask Distributor in writing for reasonable additional documentation, and Distributor shall respond promptly. Supplier may decline a Claim, in whole or in part, only to the extent that (a) the costs claimed are not Eligible Costs, (b) the activity was not an Approved Activity, (c) the costs claimed exceed the approved estimate for the activity and the excess was not approved under Section 4.3, or (d) the Claim exceeds the unused Allowance for the relevant Contract Year. Supplier shall give Distributor written reasons for declining any part of a Claim.

5.4 **Payment or Credit.** Supplier shall pay each approved Claim, or approved portion of a Claim, within thirty (30) days after approval, by ACH or wire transfer to the account that Distributor designates in writing. As an alternative to payment, Distributor may elect in its Claim to receive a credit for the approved amount. Distributor may apply that credit against amounts payable to Supplier under the Distribution Agreement in accordance with Section 7.11 of the Distribution Agreement. Any approved Claim is an undisputed amount that Supplier owes to Distributor for the purposes of Section 7.11.

5.5 **Disputed Claims.** If Distributor disputes Supplier's decision on a Claim, the Marketing Contacts shall meet within ten (10) Business Days to try to resolve the dispute. If they do not resolve it, either Party may refer the dispute for resolution under Section 18.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

5.6 **Running Statement.** Supplier shall maintain a running statement of the Allowance for each Contract Year. The statement shall show approved activities, approved Claims, sample cases charged under Section 3.4 and the unused balance. Supplier shall provide the statement to Distributor with each Claim decision and upon reasonable request.

## 6. Use of the Marks and Brand Standards

6.1 **License.** Distributor shall use the Marks in Marketing Activities under the license granted in Section 15.1 of the Distribution Agreement. This letter grants no additional license to the Marks.

6.2 **Brand Guidelines.** All materials bearing the Marks that are produced for a Marketing Activity shall comply with Supplier's brand guidelines under Section 15.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Before printing or publishing any such material, Distributor shall submit a final proof to Supplier's Marketing Contact. Supplier shall approve the proof or give comments within five (5) Business Days after receipt. If Supplier does not respond within that period, the proof is approved.

6.3 **Product Claims.** No material produced for a Marketing Activity shall contain any statement about the Products that is inconsistent with the Specifications, the labeling of the Products or the written marketing materials supplied by Supplier. These statements include nutritional, allergen, ingredient, health and shelf-life claims. Supplier shall provide Distributor with the approved allergen and nutrition information for each Product for use in Marketing Activities, and shall notify Distributor promptly of any change to it.

6.4 **Co-Branding.** Materials produced for a Marketing Activity may bear Distributor's name and logo together with the Marks. Supplier may not use any material that bears Distributor's name, logos or trademarks, or that identifies any Airline Customer, except with Distributor's prior written consent under Section 15.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

6.5 **Airline Customer Branding.** Distributor shall obtain the consent of the relevant Airline Customer before using that Airline Customer's name, livery or trademarks in any material produced for a Marketing Activity. Supplier acknowledges that each Airline Customer controls the content of its own menus and onboard materials. Any menu insert or onboard material is therefore subject to that Airline Customer's approval.

6.6 **Ownership of Materials.** Supplier owns all rights in the Marks as they appear in materials produced for Marketing Activities. Distributor owns all rights in its own name, logos and trademarks as they appear in those materials. Upon expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement, Distributor shall cease using materials bearing the Marks in accordance with Section 14.4(d) of the Distribution Agreement.

## 7. Conduct of Marketing Activities

7.1 **Compliance.** Distributor shall carry out each Approved Activity in compliance with Applicable Law. Distributor shall also comply with any rules of an airport authority, event organizer or Airline Customer that apply to the activity.

7.2 **Anti-Corruption.** No part of the Allowance shall be used to offer, give or promise anything of value to any official or employee of an Airline Customer, airport authority or governmental authority in breach of Section 19.8 of the Distribution Agreement. Modest refreshments served at a menu presentation or tasting session, and Product samples provided for menu evaluation, are permitted to the extent consistent with Applicable Law and the gift and hospitality policies of the relevant Airline Customer.

7.3 **Supplier Participation.** Upon reasonable notice, Supplier's personnel may attend and participate in Approved Activities at Supplier's own cost, with Distributor's agreement. Supplier shall consult with Distributor before communicating directly with any Airline Customer at or after an Approved Activity. Supplier shall coordinate those communications with Distributor. Supplier shall refer to Distributor any inquiry or order for Products that it receives from an Airline Customer in the Territory as a result of an Approved Activity, in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Distribution Agreement.

7.4 **Customer Information.** Information about Airline Customers that is exchanged in connection with the Marketing Plan, Approved Activities or Claims is Confidential Information of Distributor under Section 13 of the Distribution Agreement. That information includes menu plans, procurement contacts, target accounts and sales objectives.

## 8. Records and Audit

8.1 **Records.** Distributor shall keep complete and accurate records of all Eligible Costs claimed under this letter, including third-party invoices, proof of payment and sample lot records. Distributor shall keep those records for at least three (3) years after the Contract Year to which the costs relate.

8.2 **Audit.** The records described in Section 8.1 are records relating to the Fees for the purposes of Section 7.12 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier may audit them in accordance with, and subject to the limits of, Section 7.12. If an audit shows that Supplier has paid or credited any amount that was not an Eligible Cost, Distributor shall repay that amount within thirty (30) days after the audit report is delivered. If an audit shows that Supplier declined any amount that was an Eligible Cost within the unused Allowance, Supplier shall pay or credit that amount within the same period.

## 9. Effect of Termination

9.1 **Termination of the Distribution Agreement.** If the Distribution Agreement expires or is terminated before the end of the second Contract Year, the following apply:

(a) Supplier shall pay or credit all Claims for Eligible Costs of Approved Activities that took place before the effective date of expiration or termination;

(b) Supplier shall pay or credit Eligible Costs that Distributor incurred for Approved Activities scheduled after that date, to the extent Distributor had committed to those costs before it received notice of termination and cannot cancel them using commercially reasonable efforts; and

(c) any remaining unused Allowance lapses on the effective date of expiration or termination.

9.2 **Termination for Supplier's Breach or a Food-Safety Event.** Section 9.1(b) applies whether or not Distributor could cancel a committed cost if Distributor terminates the Distribution Agreement under Section 14.1, 14.2 or 14.3 of the Distribution Agreement.

9.3 **Recall Affecting Marketing Activities.** If a Recall of any Product occurs, Distributor may postpone or cancel any Approved Activity that features the affected Product. If the Recall results from a Defective Product or from Supplier's breach of the Distribution Agreement or the Quality Agreement, Supplier shall pay or credit Distributor's Eligible Costs for the postponed or cancelled activity that cannot be recovered. These Eligible Costs include the cost of printed materials that must be destroyed or reprinted. They are not charged against the Allowance. This Section 9.3 is in addition to Supplier's obligations under Section 9.5 of the Distribution Agreement.

9.4 **Survival.** Sections 5, 8 and 9 of this letter survive the expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement until all Claims under this letter have been finally resolved and paid or credited.
