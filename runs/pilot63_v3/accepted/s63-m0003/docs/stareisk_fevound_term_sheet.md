# SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

## Proposed Exclusive Distribution Relationship: SkyCrate In-Flight Products

**Between Fevound Galley Foods, Inc. and Stareisk Provisions, LLC**

**Date: January 16, 2026**

---

## 1. Purpose and Status of this Term Sheet

1.1 This Summary of Principal Terms (this "**Term Sheet**") sets out the principal terms on which Fevound Galley Foods, Inc. ("**Fevound**" or "**Supplier**") and Stareisk Provisions, LLC ("**Stareisk**" or "**Distributor**") propose to enter into an exclusive distribution relationship. Under that relationship, Stareisk would distribute Fevound's SkyCrate shelf-stable snack boxes and sealed meal kits to regional airlines in the Mid-Atlantic. Stareisk would also provide related warehousing and galley-loading services. Fevound and Stareisk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

1.2 This Term Sheet is a statement of the Parties' present intentions. It is intended to guide the preparation of definitive documentation. Except for the provisions listed in Section 19 (Binding Provisions), this Term Sheet is not binding on either Party. It does not create any obligation to negotiate, enter into or perform any agreement. No binding obligation will arise for the proposed transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreements (as defined in Section 2) are executed and delivered by both Parties.

1.3 The Parties acknowledge that the Definitive Agreements will contain additional terms customary for a transaction of this kind. Those terms include representations, warranties, covenants, conditions and boilerplate provisions not described in this Term Sheet.

## 2. Parties and Definitive Agreements

2.1 **Supplier.** Fevound Galley Foods, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094. Fevound manufactures shelf-stable in-flight snack boxes and sealed meal kits and markets them under the SkyCrate brand.

2.2 **Distributor.** Stareisk Provisions, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148. Stareisk operates a temperature-controlled distribution network serving regional airlines in the Mid-Atlantic. It also provides warehousing and galley-loading services to those airlines.

2.3 **Definitive Agreements.** The Parties expect the relationship to be documented in the following agreements (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**"):

(a) an Exclusive Distribution Agreement setting out the general terms of the distribution relationship (the "**Distribution Agreement**");

(b) a Quality Agreement setting out the Parties' food-safety, quality assurance, traceability and Recall responsibilities for the Products;

(c) a Statement of Work for warehousing and galley-loading services to be performed by Stareisk (the "**SOW**"); and

(d) a form of order form for individual purchases of Products.

2.4 **Drafting.** Fevound's counsel will prepare the first drafts of the Distribution Agreement and the Quality Agreement. Stareisk's counsel will prepare the first draft of the SOW. The Parties expect first drafts to be circulated within fifteen (15) business days after both Parties sign this Term Sheet.

3.3 **New Products.** Fevound will offer Stareisk the first opportunity to distribute any new SkyCrate shelf-stable snack box or sealed meal kit that Fevound develops for in-flight service in the Territory during the Term. The Parties will add any such product to the Products by written agreement.

## 4. Territory and Customers

4.1 **Territory.** The States of Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Virginia and West Virginia, and the District of Columbia, including all commercial service airports in those jurisdictions (the "**Territory**").

4.2 **Airline Customers.** Commercial air carriers operating scheduled or charter passenger flights departing from airports in the Territory. This includes regional airlines, charter operators and capacity-purchase partners of mainline carriers ("**Airline Customers**").

4.3 **Reserved Channels.** Fevound will retain all rights to sell the Products through non-airline channels within and outside the Territory. Those channels include retail, rail, cruise and institutional food service.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 **Appointment.** Fevound will appoint Stareisk as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory for the Term.

5.2 **Supplier Exclusivity.** During the Term, Fevound will not sell or supply the Products, or products substantially similar to the Products, to Airline Customers for flights departing from airports in the Territory, except through Stareisk. This applies whether Fevound acts directly or through an affiliate or third party. Fevound will refer to Stareisk all inquiries and orders from Airline Customers in the Territory.

5.3 **Distributor Non-Compete.** During the Term, Stareisk will not distribute in the Territory products that compete with the Products. The non-compete will cover competing shelf-stable snack boxes and sealed meal kits only. Stareisk's distribution of all other onboard food and beverage items, and its warehousing, catering logistics and galley-loading business generally, will not be restricted.

5.4 **Exceptions.** The non-compete will not apply to substitute products that Stareisk procures to cover a late delivery, a supply shortfall or a force majeure event affecting Fevound, for the period of that delay, shortfall or event.

5.5 **Out-of-Territory Sales.** Stareisk will not actively solicit Product orders for flights departing from airports outside the Territory without Fevound's prior written consent. Fevound will not unreasonably withhold or delay that consent.

5.6 **Resale Pricing.** Stareisk will be free to set its resale prices to Airline Customers.

## 6. Term and Renewal

6.1 **Initial Term.** The Distribution Agreement will have an initial term of five (5) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). The "**Effective Date**" will be the date of execution of the Distribution Agreement. The Parties are targeting execution in early March 2026.

6.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the Distribution Agreement will renew automatically for renewal terms of one (1) year per renewal. Renewal will not occur if either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

6.3 **Terms During Renewal.** The terms of the Distribution Agreement will continue to apply during each renewal term, subject to price adjustments in accordance with Section 8.

## 7. Ordering, Delivery and Inventory

7.1 **Orders.** Stareisk will order Products by written order form. Fevound will accept or reject each order form within three (3) business days. Fevound may not reject an order consistent with Stareisk's most recent forecast within a 20% variance, except for force majeure. An order not rejected within that period will be deemed accepted.

7.2 **Forecasts.** Stareisk will provide a non-binding rolling six (6) month forecast by the fifteenth (15th) day of each month.

7.3 **Minimum Order Quantity and Lead Time.** One (1) full pallet per SKU. Standard lead time will be fourteen (14) days from order acceptance.

7.4 **Changes and Cancellations.** Stareisk may change or cancel an accepted order without charge on at least seven (7) days' notice before the scheduled delivery date. Later cancellations will be subject to reimbursement of Fevound's documented, non-cancellable finished-goods costs, after mitigation.

7.5 **Delivery.** FCA (Incoterms 2020) Fevound's Secaucus facility, loaded onto Stareisk's designated carrier. Alternatively, where the order form so specifies, DAP (Incoterms 2020) Stareisk's facility, with Fevound's delivery charge stated in the order form. Title and risk will pass on delivery.

7.6 **Shelf Life.** Products will have at least 75% of their labeled shelf life remaining on delivery.

7.7 **Late Delivery.** If a delivery is more than five (5) business days late, other than for force majeure or Stareisk's fault, Stareisk may cancel the affected portion of the order without liability and may procure substitute products.

7.8 **Inspection.** Stareisk will inspect each shipment within five (5) business days after delivery for shortages, damage and obvious nonconformity. Inspection will not waive any rights for latent defects or defective Products.

8.2 **Price Adjustments.** From the second Contract Year, Fevound may adjust prices not more than once per Contract Year by a written price adjustment notice (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**"). The notice will state the adjusted prices, the general basis for the adjustment, and an effective date at least forty-five (45) days after the date of the notice. Stareisk may reject an adjustment by written notice within 20 days after the date of the Price Adjustment Notice. If Stareisk rejects, the Parties will negotiate in good faith for up to thirty (30) days. If they do not agree, the prior prices will continue for the remainder of the Contract Year.

8.3 **Price Reductions.** Fevound may reduce prices at any time on written notice.

8.4 **Payment.** Fevound will invoice on shipment. Stareisk will pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Stareisk may withhold good-faith disputed amounts on written notice before the due date. Late undisputed amounts will bear interest at the lesser of 1% per month and the maximum lawful rate.

8.5 **Set-Off.** Stareisk may set off undisputed or finally determined amounts owed by Fevound against amounts it owes. These include credits for nonconforming Products and Recall costs.

8.6 **Audit.** Each Party will have customary annual audit rights over records relating to fees. The audited Party will bear the audit cost if the audit reveals a discrepancy of more than 5%.

## 9. Warehousing and Galley-Loading Services

9.1 **Scope.** Under the SOW, Stareisk will provide warehousing, inventory management and galley-loading services for the Products at its facilities in the Territory. The SOW will include receiving, storage, lot tracking, pick-and-pack, and delivery to Airline Customers' galley-loading points.

9.2 **Service Levels and Fees.** Service levels, facility locations and fees for the services will be set out in the SOW.

9.3 **Airside Access.** Stareisk will not be responsible for delays caused by airport, TSA or Airline Customer security, access or operational requirements.

9.4 **Supplier Information.** Fevound will provide the pallet configurations, case dimensions, storage temperatures, stacking limits and lot-coding conventions that Stareisk needs to perform the services.

## 10. Quality and Food Safety

10.1 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties will enter into a Quality Agreement at the same time as the Distribution Agreement. It will cover supplier qualification, specifications, lot release, certificates of analysis, hazard analysis and preventive controls, allergen control, complaint handling, audits and Recall procedures.

10.2 **Supplier Compliance.** Fevound will manufacture the Products in compliance with applicable law, current good manufacturing practices and its written food safety plan. Fevound will maintain GFSI-benchmarked certification at each manufacturing facility and will furnish a continuing food guaranty under Section 303(c)(2) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.

10.3 **Distributor Handling.** Stareisk will store, handle and transport the Products in accordance with the specifications and the Quality Agreement. Stareisk will maintain lot segregation and traceability.

10.4 **Recalls.** Each Party will notify the other within twenty-four (24) hours if it believes a Recall may be necessary. Fevound will bear all Recall costs to the extent the Recall results from a defective Product or from Fevound's breach. Those costs include the purchase price of recalled Products, Stareisk's retrieval, storage, transport and destruction costs, replacement Products, and credits or reimbursements to Airline Customers.

10.5 **Traceability.** Each Party will be able to trace any lot within four (4) hours of request and will retain traceability records for at least two (2) years after the expiry of the lot's labeled shelf life.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 **Product Warranties.** Fevound will warrant that each Product, at delivery and through its labeled shelf life (if stored and handled in accordance with the specifications), will meet the following standards. It will conform to specifications. It will not be adulterated or misbranded. It will be fit for human consumption and for in-flight service. It will comply with applicable law and the Quality Agreement. It will be free of contamination and undeclared allergens. It will be accurately labeled. It will be delivered with good title.

11.2 **Remedies.** For any breach of warranty, Stareisk may require replacement, refund or credit, at its option, plus reimbursement of return, storage and destruction costs. These remedies will be in addition to Recall and indemnity rights.

11.3 **IP Warranty.** Fevound will warrant that it owns or has the right to use the SkyCrate marks. It will also warrant that the Products, their packaging and the marks do not infringe third-party rights.

## 12. Indemnification

12.1 **By Fevound.** Fevound will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Stareisk and its affiliates and personnel against third-party claims arising from any of the following:

(a) foodborne illness, injury or death caused by the Products;

(b) contamination, adulteration or misbranding of the Products;

(c) defects in the design, formulation, manufacture, packaging or labeling of the Products;

(d) Recalls resulting from defective Products or Fevound's breach;

(e) IP infringement by the Products, their packaging or the marks; and

(f) Fevound's breach, negligence or willful misconduct.

Third-party claimants may include Airline Customers, passengers, crew and governmental authorities. Fevound's indemnity will not apply to the extent a loss is caused by Stareisk's mishandling or negligence.

12.2 **By Stareisk.** Stareisk will indemnify Fevound against third-party claims arising from any of the following: Stareisk's failure to store or handle the Products as required, unauthorized representations to Airline Customers, Stareisk's breach, and Stareisk's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the services.

12.3 **Procedure.** Customary procedure will apply, including prompt notice, control of the defense by the indemnifying party, and consent rights over settlements that impose obligations on the indemnified party. Fevound will consult and coordinate with Stareisk before communicating with any Airline Customer about a covered claim.

## 13. Limitation of Liability

13.1 **Consequential Damages.** Neither Party will be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, subject to the exclusions below.

13.2 **Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

13.3 **Exclusions from Cap.** The cap and the consequential damages exclusion will not apply to any of the following:

(a) Fevound's indemnity for foodborne illness, contamination, adulteration, misbranding and Product defects;

(b) breach of confidentiality;

(c) gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct;

(d) Stareisk's obligation to pay undisputed amounts; or

(e) Fevound's obligation to bear Recall costs.

13.4 **Insurance.** Each Party will maintain commercial general liability insurance in customary amounts. Fevound will name Stareisk as an additional insured on its product liability coverage. Specific coverage types and limits will be agreed in the Definitive Agreements.

## 14. Termination

14.1 **Breach.** Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice. The cure period for non-payment of undisputed amounts will be ten (10) business days.

14.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate on customary insolvency events affecting the other.

14.3 **Food-Safety Events.** Stareisk may terminate if any of the following occurs:

(a) a Class I recall of any Product;

(b) two or more Recalls arising from defective Products within any twelve (12) month period;

(c) loss of Fevound's food safety certification, or its suspension for more than thirty (30) days, at any manufacturing facility; or

(d) suspension or closure of a manufacturing facility by a governmental authority.

14.4 **Effect of Termination.** Accepted orders will be filled unless Stareisk cancels them. Stareisk will have a ninety (90) day sell-off period, or may instead require Fevound to repurchase saleable inventory with at least 50% of its shelf life remaining, at cost. Stareisk will provide transition services under the SOW for up to ninety (90) days at Fevound's request, at the rates then in effect.

## 15. Trademarks and Marketing

15.1 **License.** Fevound will grant Stareisk a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Term to use the SkyCrate marks in the Territory. The license will be solely for marketing and selling the Products to Airline Customers, in accordance with Fevound's brand guidelines.

15.2 **Marketing Materials.** Fevound will provide reasonable quantities of product sheets, nutritional and allergen information, images and other marketing materials at no charge.

15.3 **Marketing Support.** The Parties will discuss a marketing allowance to support the launch of the Products with Airline Customers in the Territory. Any allowance will be documented separately in writing.

15.4 **Distributor Marks and Customers.** Fevound will not use Stareisk's name or marks, or identify any Airline Customer, in publicity or customer lists without Stareisk's consent.

## 16. Confidentiality

16.1 **Pre-Signing.** The mutual confidentiality agreement previously entered into between the Parties will continue to govern information exchanged in connection with the proposed transaction until the Distribution Agreement is signed.

16.2 **Definitive Agreements.** The Distribution Agreement will contain mutual confidentiality obligations. They will cover formulations, specifications, cost and pricing information, forecasts, Airline Customer information, menu plans and the terms of the Definitive Agreements. These obligations will continue for three (3) years after termination, and for as long as any information remains a trade secret. There will be a customary carve-out for food-safety disclosures to governmental authorities and Airline Customers.

## 17. Governing Law and Disputes

17.1 **Governing Law.** The Distribution Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

17.2 **Forum.** Disputes under the Distribution Agreement will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

17.3 **Escalation.** Before litigation, disputes will be escalated first to relationship managers and then to senior executives, within customary time limits. This will not apply to claims for injunctive relief. The Parties will include a mutual waiver of jury trial.

## 18. Conditions

18.1 The Parties' entry into the Definitive Agreements will be subject to all of the following:

(a) satisfactory completion of each Party's due diligence, including Stareisk's food-safety audit of Fevound's Secaucus manufacturing facility and Fevound's review of Stareisk's warehouse facilities and airport access credentials;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreements in form and substance satisfactory to each Party;

(c) receipt of any required internal approvals, including approval by each Party's board or managers; and

(d) no material adverse change in either Party's business before signing.

## 19. Binding Provisions

19.1 Only the following provisions of this Term Sheet are binding on the Parties: Section 1.2, this Section 19, Section 20 (Exclusive Negotiations), Section 21 (Costs) and Section 22 (General). Section 16.1 is also binding to the extent it confirms the continued effect of the existing mutual confidentiality agreement.

## 20. Exclusive Negotiations

20.1 From the date of this Term Sheet until the earlier of March 31, 2026 and the execution of the Distribution Agreement, Fevound will not negotiate or enter into any agreement with any third party for the distribution of the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory. Either Party may end this period early by written notice to the other stating that it no longer intends to pursue the transaction.

## 21. Costs

21.1 Each Party will bear its own legal, accounting and other costs incurred in connection with this Term Sheet, due diligence and the Definitive Agreements, whether or not the transaction is completed.

## 22. General

22.1 **Governing Law of this Term Sheet.** This Term Sheet is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute about the binding provisions of this Term Sheet will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

22.2 **Counterparts.** This Term Sheet may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.

22.3 **Publicity.** Neither Party will make any public announcement about the proposed transaction without the other Party's prior written approval.

22.4 **Expiration.** If the Distribution Agreement has not been executed by March 31, 2026, this Term Sheet will lapse unless extended by written agreement of the Parties. Sections 1.2, 21 and 22 will survive any lapse.
