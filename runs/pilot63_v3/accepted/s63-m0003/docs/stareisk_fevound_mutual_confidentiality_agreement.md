# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 10, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**FEVOUND GALLEY FOODS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Fevound**"); and

**STAREISK PROVISIONS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148 ("**Stareisk**").

Fevound and Stareisk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Fevound manufactures shelf-stable in-flight snack boxes and sealed meal kits and markets them under the SkyCrate brand.

B. Stareisk operates a temperature-controlled distribution network serving regional airlines in the Mid-Atlantic region of the United States. It also provides warehousing and galley-loading services to those airlines.

C. The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate and negotiate a possible distribution relationship. Under that relationship, Stareisk would distribute SkyCrate products to airlines and provide related warehousing and galley-loading services (the "**Purpose**").

D. Each Party is willing to disclose information to the other for the Purpose on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means, as to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information that the Discloser or its Representatives disclose to the Recipient or its Representatives for the Purpose during the Disclosure Period. It covers information in any form, whether written, oral, electronic, visual or by inspection of facilities, samples or products. To be Confidential Information, the information must meet one of two tests. Either it is marked or identified as confidential when disclosed, or a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes:

(a) product formulations, recipes, ingredient and component sources, specifications, processing parameters, thermal process data, shelf-life studies and packaging designs;

(b) food safety plans, hazard analyses, audit reports, certification reports, test results, complaint records and regulatory correspondence;

(c) cost, margin and pricing information, price lists and pricing proposals;

(d) sales, volume and forecast information and business plans;

(e) the identity of airline customers and prospective airline customers, together with their menu plans, procurement contacts, contract terms, load plans and station operations;

(f) facility layouts, warehouse management systems, security procedures, airport access arrangements and operating procedures;

(g) Transaction Information; and

(h) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Recipient or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing.

1.3 "**Discloser**" means the Party that discloses, or whose Representatives disclose, Confidential Information.

1.4 "**Disclosure Period**" means the period that begins on the Effective Date and ends on the earliest of the following:

(a) the date on which the Parties execute a definitive distribution agreement for the Purpose;

(b) the date on which either Party gives the other written notice that it no longer wishes to pursue the Purpose; and

(c) the first anniversary of the Effective Date.

1.5 "**Recipient**" means the Party that receives, or whose Representatives receive, Confidential Information.

1.6 "**Representatives**" means, as to a Party, its Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, members, officers, employees, agents, lenders, prospective lenders, accountants, attorneys, insurance advisers and other professional advisers.

1.7 "**Transaction Information**" means the fact that the Parties are evaluating or negotiating a possible relationship, the status and content of their discussions, and the terms of any proposal, term sheet or draft agreement exchanged between them.

## 2. EXCLUSIONS

2.1 **Excluded Information.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public, other than as a result of a disclosure by the Recipient or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the Recipient or its Representatives without any obligation of confidentiality before its disclosure by the Discloser;

(c) is lawfully received by the Recipient or its Representatives from a third party that is not, to the Recipient's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, under an obligation of confidentiality to the Discloser for that information; or

(d) is independently developed by the Recipient or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

2.2 **Combinations.** Information is not excluded merely because individual features or components of it are publicly available or known to the Recipient. A combination of features or components is excluded only if the combination itself, and its principle of operation, fall within an exclusion in Section 2.1.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT

3.1 **Use.** The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose.

3.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Recipient shall not disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to any person, except as permitted by Sections 3.3, 4 and 5.

3.3 **Representatives.** The Recipient may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose. Before any such disclosure, the Recipient shall inform each Representative of the confidential nature of the information. Each Representative must be bound by obligations of confidentiality and restricted use at least as protective as those in this Agreement, whether by professional duty, employment terms or written agreement. The Recipient is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if done by the Recipient.

3.4 **Standard of Care.** The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature. In any event, the Recipient shall use no less than reasonable care.

3.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Recipient shall notify the Discloser in writing promptly after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, use of or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information. The Recipient shall cooperate reasonably with the Discloser to limit the effects of the incident and to prevent its recurrence.

3.6 **No Reverse Engineering.** The Recipient shall not analyze, test or reverse engineer any product sample, packaging sample or prototype supplied by the Discloser to determine its composition or method of manufacture, except as the Discloser authorizes in writing. However, the Recipient may conduct ordinary sensory evaluation, tasting and handling trials of product samples for the Purpose.

3.7 **Copies.** The Recipient shall not copy or reproduce the Discloser's Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose. Each copy shall carry any confidentiality legends that appear on the original.

## 4. FACILITY VISITS

4.1 **Site Visits.** Each Party may invite the other to visit its manufacturing, warehouse or airport facilities for the Purpose. During any visit, the visiting Party and its Representatives shall comply with the host Party's reasonable safety, hygiene, food-defense, security and visitor policies, as communicated to them before or during the visit.

4.2 **Information Observed.** All information that the visiting Party or its Representatives observe or obtain during a visit is the host Party's Confidential Information. That includes processes, equipment, layouts, records and information about the host Party's other customers.

4.3 **Photography and Recording.** The visiting Party and its Representatives shall not photograph, film or record any part of the host Party's facilities without the host Party's prior written consent.

4.4 **Airside Areas.** Any visit to airside or other secured areas of an airport is subject to the requirements of the airport authority, the Transportation Security Administration and the relevant airline. The host Party is not responsible for the cancellation or restriction of a visit because of those requirements.

## 5. COMPELLED AND REGULATORY DISCLOSURE

5.1 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient or its Representatives may disclose the Discloser's Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, stock exchange rule, a governmental authority, subpoena or court order. Before doing so, to the extent legally permitted, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt written notice of the requirement. The Recipient shall also give the Discloser reasonable assistance, at the Discloser's expense, in seeking a protective order or other confidential treatment. The Recipient shall disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

5.2 **Food-Safety Authorities.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents either Party from reporting to the United States Food and Drug Administration, the United States Department of Agriculture or any other governmental authority any information that the Party is required by law to report about an actual or suspected food-safety risk.

5.3 **Protected Reporting.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits any individual from reporting a possible violation of law to a governmental authority, or from making other disclosures protected under whistleblower provisions of applicable law. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, an individual is not criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in two cases. The first is a disclosure made in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law. The second is a disclosure made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 6. TRANSACTION INFORMATION AND NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 **Transaction Information.** Each Party shall treat the Transaction Information as Confidential Information of the other Party. Neither Party shall disclose the Transaction Information to any airline, airline caterer, competitor of the other Party or member of the press without the other Party's prior written consent.

6.2 **Customer Contact.** During the Disclosure Period, Fevound shall not use any Confidential Information of Stareisk to solicit or contact any airline customer of Stareisk about the supply of SkyCrate products for flights served by Stareisk, without Stareisk's prior written consent.

6.3 **Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Disclosure Period and for six (6) months after it ends, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Purpose. This Section 6.3 does not restrict general advertisements or search-firm engagements not targeted at the other Party's employees, or the hiring of any person who responds to them.

## 7. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

7.1 **Request or End of Disclosure Period.** Within fifteen (15) business days after the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall return to the Discloser or destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information in the possession or control of the Recipient or its Representatives. The same obligation applies within fifteen (15) business days after the Disclosure Period ends under Section 1.4(b) or (c), without any request. The Recipient may choose between return and destruction unless the Discloser specifies one. The obligation covers all copies, notes and analyses containing or reflecting that information.

7.2 **Product Samples.** The Recipient shall destroy any product samples received from the Discloser that remain unused when the Disclosure Period ends, unless the Discloser asks for their return at its own cost.

7.3 **Certification.** Upon the Discloser's written request, an officer of the Recipient shall certify in writing that the Recipient has complied with this Section 7.

7.4 **Retained Copies.** Despite Sections 7.1 and 7.2, the Recipient and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information in three cases: (a) to the extent required by law, regulation or professional standards; (b) in accordance with their bona fide document-retention policies; and (c) in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course. Any retained Confidential Information remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained, even after the period in Section 9.2.

## 8. OWNERSHIP AND NO WARRANTY

8.1 **Ownership.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No license or other right in any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property right of the Discloser is granted or implied by this Agreement or by any disclosure under it, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

8.2 **Marks.** Neither Party shall use the other Party's name, logos or trademarks, including the SkyCrate name and logo, in any publicity, presentation to third parties or customer list without the other Party's prior written consent.

8.3 **No Warranty.** All Confidential Information is provided "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information it discloses. Neither Party is liable to the other for the Recipient's use of Confidential Information, except as provided in a definitive agreement between the Parties. Any representations and warranties will be given only in a definitive agreement, if one is executed.

8.4 **No Obligation to Proceed.** This Agreement does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions, or to enter into any agreement or relationship with the other. Either Party may end discussions at any time, for any reason or for no reason, by notice under Section 1.4(b). No agreement for any relationship between the Parties exists unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered by both Parties.

8.5 **Freedom to Deal.** Subject to the confidentiality and use restrictions in this Agreement, nothing in this Agreement prevents either Party from carrying on its business in the ordinary course. In particular, each Party may deal with third parties, including competitors of the other Party, provided that it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in doing so.

## 9. TERM

9.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the obligations in Section 9.2 have expired.

9.2 **Duration of Obligations.** The Recipient's obligations under Sections 3, 4.2, 4.3, 6.1 and 8.2 for any item of Confidential Information continue for two (2) years after the end of the Disclosure Period. For any item of Confidential Information that is a trade secret under applicable law, those obligations continue for as long as the item remains a trade secret.

9.3 **Accrued Rights.** The end of the Disclosure Period does not affect any rights or liabilities that accrued before it ended.

## 10. REMEDIES

10.1 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the Discloser irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Discloser may therefore seek an injunction, specific performance or other equitable relief for any actual or threatened breach. The Discloser need not post a bond or prove actual damages, to the extent permitted by law. Equitable relief is in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

10.2 **Costs of Enforcement.** If a court of competent jurisdiction finally determines that a Party breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of enforcing this Agreement against it.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement and all claims arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Delaware conflict of laws principles that would require application of the laws of another jurisdiction do not apply.

11.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. Each Party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum. However, either Party may seek equitable relief under Section 10.1 in any court of competent jurisdiction.

11.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
