# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of May 4, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**FEVOUND GALLEY FOODS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**"); and

**STAREISK PROVISIONS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Amendment as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under the Distribution Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the SkyCrate Products to Airline Customers in the Territory.

B. During the negotiation of the Distribution Agreement, the Parties agreed that Supplier would maintain product liability coverage and name Distributor as an additional insured, and that the specific coverage types and limits would be agreed between them.

C. Following the commencement of deliveries under Order Form No. SC-OF-001, several Airline Customers in the Territory have asked Distributor to confirm the insurance maintained by the manufacturer of the Products as part of their onboard-provisioning vendor qualification procedures.

D. The Parties now wish to amend the Distribution Agreement to set out Supplier's insurance obligations, to add a related termination right and to add a new Exhibit D, on the terms of this Amendment.

E. Section 19.4 of the Distribution Agreement provides that the Distribution Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument that refers to it and is signed by an authorized representative of each Party. This Amendment is that written instrument.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and in the Distribution Agreement, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement. Those terms include "Affiliate," "Airline Customer," "Applicable Law," "Business Day," "Contract Year," "days," "Defective Product," "Distributor Indemnitees," "Losses," "Products," "Quality Agreement," "Recall," "SOW," "Term" and "Territory."

1.2 **Amendment.** This Amendment is an "Amendment" for the purposes of the Distribution Agreement, executed in accordance with Section 19.4 of the Distribution Agreement.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Section 19.11 of the Distribution Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in this Amendment in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Amendment.

## 2. INSURANCE

2.1 **New Section 11.1.** Section 11 of the Distribution Agreement is amended by inserting the following as a new Section 11.1, immediately after the heading "11. INDEMNIFICATION":

> 11.1 **Supplier Insurance.**
>
> (a) **Required Coverage.** Throughout the Term, and for at least three (3) years after its expiration or termination, Supplier shall maintain, at its own cost, product liability insurance covering the Products, together with the other insurance coverage described in Exhibit D, in each case with not less than the limits stated in Exhibit D.
>
> (b) **Insurers.** Supplier shall place each policy required by this Section 11.1 with insurers authorized to do business in the States in which the Products are manufactured and distributed, and rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best.
>
> (c) **Additional Insured.** Supplier shall cause Distributor and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors, members, managers and employees, to be named as additional insureds under Supplier's product liability insurance and commercial general liability insurance, by a vendors endorsement or an additional insured endorsement in a form reasonably acceptable to Distributor. The additional insured coverage shall apply on a primary and non-contributory basis to any other insurance maintained by Distributor.
>
> (d) **Waiver of Subrogation.** Supplier shall cause each policy described in Section 11.1(c) to include a waiver of subrogation in favor of the additional insureds, to the extent permitted by the insurer.
>
> (e) **Occurrence or Claims-Made Basis.** Supplier shall maintain its product liability insurance and commercial general liability insurance on an occurrence basis. If any other policy required by this Section 11.1 is written on a claims-made basis, Supplier shall maintain that coverage, or purchase an extended reporting period of at least three (3) years, after the expiration or termination of the Term.
>
> (f) **Certificates.** Supplier shall deliver to Distributor certificates of insurance, together with copies of the additional insured and waiver of subrogation endorsements, evidencing the coverage required by this Section 11.1 (i) within ten (10) Business Days after the effective date of the amendment by which this Section 11.1 was added, (ii) within ten (10) Business Days after each renewal or replacement of any required policy, and (iii) within five (5) Business Days after Distributor's written request.
>
> (g) **Notice of Changes.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any cancellation, non-renewal or material reduction in the coverage required by this Section 11.1, and shall give Distributor written notice within two (2) Business Days after Supplier receives any notice of cancellation or non-renewal from an insurer.
>
> (h) **Deductibles and Retentions.** Supplier is responsible for all deductibles and self-insured retentions under the policies required by this Section 11.1. No self-insured retention under Supplier's product liability insurance may exceed the amount stated in Exhibit D without Distributor's prior written consent.
>
> (i) **Claims Cooperation.** Supplier shall report to its insurers promptly, and in accordance with the policy conditions, each claim and each occurrence that may give rise to a claim involving the Products. Supplier shall keep Distributor reasonably informed of the status of any insurance claim that relates to an Airline Customer in the Territory.
>
> (j) **Airline Customer Requirements.** Where an Airline Customer requires evidence of the insurance of the manufacturer of the Products as a condition of listing the Products, Supplier shall provide, within ten (10) Business Days after Distributor's request, a certificate of insurance addressed to that Airline Customer as certificate holder, and, where the Airline Customer so requires and Supplier's insurer agrees, an endorsement naming that Airline Customer as an additional insured under Supplier's product liability insurance.

2.2 **Renumbering.** The former Section 11.1 of the Distribution Agreement (Indemnification by Supplier) is renumbered as Section 11.2. The former Sections 11.2 (Indemnification by Distributor), 11.3 (Procedure), 11.4 (Failure to Defend) and 11.5 (Airline Customer Relations) of the Distribution Agreement are renumbered as Sections 11.3, 11.4, 11.5 and 11.6, respectively.

2.3 **Conforming Changes within Renumbered Sections.** In the final paragraph of the renumbered Section 11.2, the words "under this Section 11.1" are deleted and replaced with "under this Section 11.2." In the final sentence of the renumbered Section 11.3, the words "under this Section 11.2" are deleted and replaced with "under this Section 11.3."

2.4 **Compliance as of the Amendment Effective Date.** Supplier confirms that, as of the Amendment Effective Date, it maintains product liability insurance covering the Products with limits not less than those stated in Exhibit D. Supplier shall obtain the additional insured and waiver of subrogation endorsements described in Section 11.1(c) and Section 11.1(d) of the Distribution Agreement, as amended by this Amendment, no later than ten (10) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date.

## 3. TERMINATION FOR INSURANCE LAPSE

3.1 **New Section 14.3(e).** Section 14.3 of the Distribution Agreement is amended as follows:

(a) in Section 14.3(c), the word "or" at the end of that paragraph is deleted;

(b) in Section 14.3(d), the period at the end of that paragraph is deleted and replaced with "; or"; and

(c) the following new paragraph is inserted immediately after Section 14.3(d):

> 14.8 **Insurance Lapse.** During any period in which Supplier does not maintain the product liability insurance required by Section 11.1, Distributor may suspend the submission of Order Forms and the delivery of Products to any Airline Customer whose vendor requirements are not met as a result, without liability to Supplier. Supplier shall continue to fill Order Forms accepted before the suspension, unless Distributor elects to cancel them by written notice.

This Exhibit D sets out the insurance that Supplier shall maintain under Section 11.1. Each limit stated is a minimum. Supplier may satisfy any limit through a combination of primary and umbrella or excess liability policies, provided the umbrella or excess policies follow form to the underlying coverage and the additional insured status required by Section 11.1(c) extends to them.

## Part 2 – Specific Requirements

2.1 **Vendors Coverage.** The product liability insurance shall include a broad form vendors endorsement, or equivalent additional insured endorsement, that covers Distributor for bodily injury and property damage arising out of the Products distributed or sold by Distributor in the regular course of its business. The endorsement shall not exclude coverage for (a) the repackaging of Products in the form permitted by Section 8.4 of the Quality Agreement and Section 4.4 of the SOW, including case-breaking and the application of shipping labels to outer packaging, or (b) the storage, handling and galley-loading of the Products by Distributor in accordance with the Specifications.

2.2 **Contractual Liability.** The commercial general liability insurance and the product liability insurance shall include contractual liability coverage for liability assumed by Supplier under an insured contract, which includes the Distribution Agreement.

2.3 **Territory of Coverage.** Each liability policy shall cover occurrences and claims arising anywhere in the United States, including occurrences on board aircraft in flight within, to or from the United States.

2.4 **Foodborne Illness and Allergens.** The product liability insurance shall not contain any exclusion for bodily injury arising from foodborne illness, pathogens, undeclared allergens or foreign material in the Products. The product contamination and recall insurance shall not contain any exclusion for allergen mislabeling.

2.5 **Self-Insured Retention.** The self-insured retention or deductible under the product liability insurance shall not exceed $250,000 per occurrence. The deductible under the product contamination and recall insurance shall not exceed $100,000 per loss.

2.6 **Severability of Interests.** Each liability policy under which Distributor is an additional insured shall include a separation of insureds or severability of interests provision.

2.7 **Cross-Liability.** No liability policy under which Distributor is an additional insured shall contain an exclusion for claims brought by one insured against another insured, to the extent the exclusion would apply to claims by Distributor against Supplier or by Supplier against Distributor.
