## 2. PREMISES AND COMMON AREAS

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Lease Term, on the terms and conditions of this Lease.

2.2 **Rentable Area.** The Rentable Area of the Premises and of the Building has been measured in accordance with ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017. The Parties agree that the figures in Section 1.1 are conclusive for all purposes of this Lease and are not subject to remeasurement, except as a result of a physical change to the Premises or the Building.

2.3 **Common Areas.** Tenant and its employees, agents and invitees may use, in common with other tenants, the lobbies, corridors, elevators, stairways, restrooms on multi-tenant floors, loading dock, fitness center and other areas of the Building that Landlord designates for common use from time to time (the "**Common Areas**"). Landlord may change the Common Areas, provided that no change materially and adversely affects access to or use of the Premises.

2.4 **Condition.** Except for Landlord's Work under Exhibit B and Landlord's obligations under Section 9, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Lease Date. Landlord represents that, on the Commencement Date, the base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems serving the Premises will be in good working order.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Lease Term.** The Lease Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease.

3.2 **Early Access.** Tenant and its contractors may enter the Premises from October 1, 2024 to install cabling, furniture and equipment, without payment of Base Rent or Additional Rent, provided that Tenant complies with all other terms of this Lease, including Section 11 (Insurance).

3.3 **Commencement Date Memorandum.** Within thirty (30) days after the Commencement Date, the Parties shall sign a memorandum in the form of Exhibit D confirming the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date. Failure to sign the memorandum does not affect those dates.

3.4 **Option to Extend.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Lease Term for a single period of five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the Extension Term shall be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent. To exercise the option, Tenant shall give Landlord written notice not less than twelve (12) months and not more than eighteen (18) months before the Expiration Date. The option may be exercised only if Tenant is not in default beyond any applicable cure period on the date of its notice. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual base rent per rentable square foot at which comparable tenants are leasing comparable space in comparable Class A office buildings in the Center City and South Delaware River waterfront submarkets of Philadelphia, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the Parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, each Party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in that market, and the two brokers shall determine Fair Market Rent. If the brokers' determinations differ by more than five percent (5%), they shall appoint a third broker, who shall select the determination of one of the first two brokers that is closer to the third broker's own view.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

5.1 **Tenant's Share of Increases.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay as Additional Rent Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year.

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs that Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, clean, secure and insure the Building and the land on which it stands, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude: (a) capital expenditures, except those that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date, in each case amortized over their useful life with interest at a reasonable rate, and only to the extent of the annual amortized amount; (b) costs of leasing, including brokerage commissions, advertising and tenant improvements for other tenants; (c) depreciation, debt service and ground rent; (d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants; (e) legal costs of disputes with tenants or lenders; (f) Landlord's general corporate overhead and salaries of personnel above the level of building manager; (g) costs arising from Landlord's negligence or breach of any lease; (h) charitable and political contributions; (i) fines and penalties; and (j) the cost of remediating Hazardous Materials not introduced by Tenant.

5.3 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied in any year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be adjusted to reflect the amount that would have been incurred at ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.4 **Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses for any calendar year shall not increase by more than five percent (5%), on a non-cumulative basis, over Controllable Operating Expenses for the prior year. "**Controllable Operating Expenses**" means all Operating Expenses other than insurance premiums, utilities, snow removal, security costs mandated by governmental authority, and costs of compliance with laws first enacted after the Lease Date.

5.5 **Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land, including the use and occupancy tax and business privilege taxes only to the extent levied on Landlord as owner of the Building, and the reasonable costs of contesting them. Taxes exclude income, franchise, estate and transfer taxes of Landlord. If an assessment may be paid in installments, Taxes include only the installment payable in the relevant year.

5.6 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall give Tenant a good-faith estimate of Tenant's Share of increases for each year. Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each monthly installment of Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next installments of Rent or, after the end of the Lease Term, refunded within thirty (30) days. Any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days after delivery of the statement.

5.7 **Audit.** Tenant may audit Landlord's books and records for Operating Expenses and Taxes for any year within one hundred eighty (180) days after receiving Landlord's statement for that year, by an independent accountant not paid on a contingency basis. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit. Landlord shall refund any overpayment within thirty (30) days.

5.8 **Tenant's Use and Occupancy Tax.** Tenant shall pay directly to the City of Philadelphia any use and occupancy tax levied on Tenant's occupancy of the Premises.

## 6. USE

6.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use. Tenant shall not use the Premises for the storage, handling, preparation or distribution of food or other goods for sale, other than ordinary pantry and catering use for Tenant's own employees and guests, and food samples used in meetings and presentations.

6.2 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws that apply to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws, including the Americans with Disabilities Act, that apply to the Common Areas, the structure of the Building and the base building systems.

6.3 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the Rules and Regulations in Exhibit C and with reasonable, non-discriminatory changes to them of which Landlord gives Tenant at least thirty (30) days' written notice. If the Rules and Regulations conflict with this Lease, this Lease controls.

6.4 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous or toxic substance onto the Premises, other than ordinary office and cleaning supplies in customary quantities used in compliance with law. Each Party shall indemnify the other against Losses arising from Hazardous Materials introduced to the Building by the indemnifying Party or its agents.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

7.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall provide: (a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturday, excluding Building holidays ("**Building Hours**"), at levels customary for Class A office buildings in Philadelphia; (b) passenger elevator service at all times, with at least one elevator in each bank available outside Building Hours; (c) freight elevator service by reservation; (d) janitorial service five nights per week to the standard in Exhibit C; (e) hot and cold water for restrooms and pantries; (f) lobby security staffing during Building Hours and card-access control at all times; and (g) electricity for lighting and standard office equipment.

7.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside Building Hours on Tenant's request through the Building's online request system, at Landlord's actual cost, currently $65.00 per hour per floor.

7.3 **Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is measured by a submeter installed as part of Landlord's Work. Tenant shall pay for submetered electricity at Landlord's actual cost, without mark-up, as Additional Rent.

7.4 **Supplemental HVAC.** Tenant may install a supplemental HVAC unit for its server room, connected to the Building's condenser water system, subject to Landlord's approval of the plans under Section 8. Tenant shall pay Landlord's standard charge for condenser water, currently $750.00 per ton per year.

7.5 **Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. However, if any service that Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than three (3) consecutive business days for a reason within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable, Rent shall abate for the affected portion from the fourth (4th) business day until the service is restored.

7.6 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may select its own telecommunications providers. Landlord shall give those providers reasonable access to the Building risers and telecommunications rooms, subject to Landlord's standard access agreement.

## 8. ALTERATIONS

8.1 **Initial Improvements.** Landlord's Work and the Tenant Improvement Allowance are governed by the Work Letter in Exhibit B.

8.2 **Alterations.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting, carpeting and installation of furniture and fixtures, that cost less than $40,000.00 in any twelve (12) month period and do not affect the structure or base building systems.

8.3 **Performance.** Tenant shall perform alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, using licensed and insured contractors reasonably approved by Landlord, and in compliance with all laws. Landlord may charge a supervision fee not exceeding three percent (3%) of the hard costs of alterations that require its consent.

8.4 **Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Premises and the Building free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant, and shall cause any such lien to be discharged or bonded within thirty (30) days after notice of it.

8.5 **Removal.** At the end of the Lease Term, Tenant is not required to remove the improvements made under Exhibit B or any alteration, unless Landlord designated it for removal when it gave its consent. Tenant shall remove its cabling installed after the Commencement Date and its personal property.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

9.1 **Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good condition and in accordance with the standards of comparable Class A office buildings in Philadelphia, the structure, roof, foundation and exterior of the Building, the Common Areas and the base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life-safety and elevator systems up to the point of connection to the Premises. The cost is an Operating Expense to the extent permitted by Section 5.2, except where the repair results from Tenant's negligence.

9.2 **Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, including its supplemental HVAC unit, pantry equipment and any improvements serving only the Premises that are not base building systems.

9.3 **Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, and without notice in an emergency, to inspect, repair or show the Premises to lenders and purchasers and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Lease Term, to prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures, including escort requirements for areas holding confidential records.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

10.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to a request for consent within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information reasonably required to evaluate it.

10.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) business days' prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises to (a) any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, or (b) any entity that succeeds to all or substantially all of Tenant's business or assets by merger, consolidation or acquisition, provided that the successor has a tangible net worth at least equal to that of Tenant on the Lease Date and assumes this Lease in writing.

10.3 **Profits.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received under an assignment or sublease, other than a permitted transfer under Section 10.2, in excess of the Rent payable for the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer.

10.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, except an assignment under Section 10.2(b) to a successor whose tangible net worth is at least twice that of Tenant on the Lease Date.

## 11. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

11.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain, from the date of its first entry into the Premises: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, with umbrella coverage of not less than $5,000,000, naming Landlord, its managing agent and its mortgagee as additional insureds; (b) "special form" property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and the improvements in the Premises for their full replacement cost; (c) business interruption insurance in a reasonable amount; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000.

11.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain "special form" property insurance on the Building, excluding Tenant's personal property, for its full replacement cost, and commercial general liability insurance on the Common Areas with limits customary for comparable buildings. The premiums are Operating Expenses.

11.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each Party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent the loss is covered, or would have been covered, by the property insurance it is required to carry under this Lease. Each Party shall cause its property insurers to waive subrogation accordingly.

11.4 **Indemnity.** Subject to Section 11.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees ("**Losses**"), arising from injury or damage occurring in the Premises or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents. Subject to Section 11.3, Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against Losses arising from injury or damage occurring in the Common Areas or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents. This Section 11.4 survives the end of the Lease Term.

11.5 **Certificates.** Each Party shall deliver certificates of the insurance required of it to the other Party before the Commencement Date and upon each renewal.

## 12. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

12.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, give Tenant a contractor's estimate of the time required to restore them. If the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days, either Party may terminate this Lease by notice within thirty (30) days after the estimate is delivered. Otherwise, Landlord shall restore the Building and the improvements in the Premises made under Exhibit B with reasonable diligence.

12.2 **Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable as a result of a casualty, from the date of the casualty until the earlier of substantial completion of Landlord's restoration and Tenant's resumption of business in the affected part.

12.3 **Late Restoration.** If Landlord has not substantially completed its restoration within sixty (60) days after the period in its estimate, Tenant may terminate this Lease by notice to Landlord before restoration is substantially completed.

12.4 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or of the access or parking serving them that Tenant cannot reasonably continue its business, is taken by eminent domain, either Party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. Landlord is entitled to the award for the taking, but Tenant may claim separately for its moving expenses and its unamortized personal property and improvements paid for by Tenant.

## 13. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

13.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is overdue; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, does not begin the cure within that period and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

13.2 **Remedies.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may, by written notice, terminate this Lease or terminate Tenant's right of possession, and may recover possession of the Premises through judicial process. Landlord may recover the Rent due through the date of termination, the reasonable costs of reletting, and damages equal to the present value, discounted at five percent (5%) per annum, of the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Lease Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for the same period. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

13.3 **No Confession of Judgment.** Landlord waives any right to confess judgment against Tenant for Rent or possession. No provision of this Lease authorizes a confession of judgment.

13.4 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or such shorter period as is reasonable in an emergency, Tenant may perform the obligation to the extent it relates to the Premises, and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice. If Landlord does not reimburse those costs and does not dispute them in good faith within that period, Tenant may set them off against Base Rent, provided that the set-off in any month does not exceed fifty percent (50%) of the monthly installment of Base Rent.

13.5 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Tenant's recourse against Landlord is limited to Landlord's interest in the Building, including its rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds. No partner of Landlord is personally liable under this Lease.

## 14. SECURITY DEPOSIT

14.1 **Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord. Tenant may instead deliver an irrevocable standby letter of credit in that amount issued by a bank with an office in Philadelphia and reasonably acceptable to Landlord.

14.2 **Application.** After an Event of Default, Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to amounts owed by Tenant, and Tenant shall restore the amount applied within ten (10) business days after notice.

14.3 **Reduction.** If no Event of Default has occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $31,627.50 on the first day of the sixth (6th) Lease Year.

14.4 **Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied Security Deposit within thirty (30) days after the end of the Lease Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises. Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with the Pennsylvania Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951 to the extent applicable.

## 15. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

15.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later placed on the Building, provided that the mortgagee signs a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement agreeing not to disturb Tenant's possession while no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

15.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each Party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after request, deliver a certificate confirming that this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 16. PARKING AND SIGNAGE

Tenant is liable for consequential damages resulting from a holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord notifies Tenant that it has signed a lease of the Premises with a new tenant.

## EXHIBIT B – WORK LETTER

1. **Landlord's Work.** Before October 1, 2024, Landlord shall, at its cost and not from the Tenant Improvement Allowance: (a) demolish existing improvements to a clean shell; (b) deliver the base building HVAC, sprinkler and fire alarm systems in good working order and in compliance with code for open-plan office use; (c) install an electrical submeter for the Premises; and (d) deliver the floor's restrooms in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act.
