# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or the "**Distribution Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**FEVOUND GALLEY FOODS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1180 Meadowlands Parkway, Suite 300, Secaucus, New Jersey 07094 ("**Supplier**"); and

**STAREISK PROVISIONS, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2401 Columbus Boulevard, Suite 1400, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19148 ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier manufactures shelf-stable in-flight snack boxes and sealed meal kits for in-flight service and markets them under the SkyCrate brand.

B. Distributor operates a temperature-controlled distribution network that serves regional airlines in the Mid-Atlantic region of the United States. Distributor also provides warehousing and galley-loading services to those airlines.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

## 2. AGREEMENT STRUCTURE

2.1 **Agreement Documents.** This Agreement sets out the general terms of the distribution relationship between the Parties. The Quality Agreement sets out the Parties' respective food-safety and quality responsibilities for the Products. The SOW sets out the Services and the fees for the Services. Each Order Form sets out the quantities, delivery locations and delivery dates for a particular purchase of Products.

2.2 **Incorporation.** Each Order Form, once accepted by Supplier, and the SOW are issued under and incorporate the terms of this Agreement. A reference to "this Agreement" includes its Exhibits.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If the Agreement Documents conflict, they shall control in the following order of precedence: Amendments; Quality Agreement for food-safety and quality matters; Distribution Agreement; SOW; Order Forms.

2.4 **Pre-Printed Terms.** Any pre-printed or standard terms on any purchase order, invoice, acknowledgment, bill of lading or similar business form of either Party have no effect. They do not form part of the Agreement Documents, even if signed or accepted by the other Party.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 14.

3.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms equal in length to the Initial Term (each, a "**Renewal Term**"). Renewal does not occur if either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

3.3 **Terms of Renewal.** The terms and conditions of this Agreement in effect at the end of the Initial Term or a Renewal Term continue to apply during each Renewal Term, subject to any adjustment to the Price List made in accordance with Section 7.

## 4. APPOINTMENT AND EXCLUSIVITY

4.1 **Appointment.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment.

4.2 **Supplier Exclusivity.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or through any Affiliate or third party, sell, distribute or supply the Products or any products substantially similar to the Products to any Airline Customer for consumption on flights departing from airports in the Territory, except through Distributor. Supplier shall promptly refer to Distributor any inquiry or order for Products that it receives from an Airline Customer in the Territory.

4.3 **Distributor Non-Competition.** During the Term, Distributor shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, market, sell or distribute any Competing Products to any Airline Customer or other person in the Territory.

4.4 **Sales Outside the Territory.** Distributor shall not actively solicit orders for the Products from airlines for flights departing from airports outside the Territory without Supplier's prior written consent. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold or delay that consent.

4.5 **Reserved Rights.** Except for the rights expressly granted in this Agreement, Supplier retains all rights to market, sell and distribute the Products. Those rights include sales to non-airline channels such as retail, rail, cruise and institutional food service within and outside the Territory.

4.6 **Independent Resale Pricing.** Distributor is free to determine the prices at which it resells the Products to Airline Customers. Nothing in this Agreement restricts that freedom.

## 5. DISTRIBUTOR OBLIGATIONS

5.1 **Promotion.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and sell the Products to Airline Customers in the Territory. Those efforts include presenting the Products to the catering and onboard-services managers of Airline Customers, and including the Products in Distributor's menu proposals where appropriate.

5.2 **Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store, handle and transport the Products in accordance with the storage and handling requirements in the Specifications and the Quality Agreement. Distributor shall maintain the Products in clean, pest-controlled facilities and shall keep each lot segregated and traceable.

5.3 **Personnel and Facilities.** Distributor shall maintain sales, warehouse and logistics personnel, facilities and vehicles sufficient to perform its obligations under this Agreement. Distributor shall ensure that its personnel who handle the Products are trained in food-handling practices appropriate to the Products.

5.4 **Compliance.** Distributor shall comply with all Applicable Law in the storage, handling, transport, marketing and resale of the Products. Distributor shall obtain and maintain all licenses, registrations and airport access credentials required to perform its obligations.

5.5 **Reporting.** Within fifteen (15) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a written report for that quarter. The report shall state (a) sales of Products by SKU and by Airline Customer, (b) inventory on hand by SKU and lot number, and (c) any material customer complaints or feedback about the Products. The Parties shall meet at least quarterly, in person or by video conference, to review the reports and the Parties' forecasts.

5.6 **Forecasts.** On or before the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a non-binding, rolling six (6) month forecast of its expected Product requirements. Forecasts are for planning purposes only. They do not obligate Distributor to purchase, or Supplier to supply, any quantity of Products other than under accepted Order Forms.

5.7 **No Representations.** Distributor shall make no representation, warranty or guarantee about the Products to Airline Customers that is inconsistent with the Specifications, the labeling of the Products or the written marketing materials supplied by Supplier.

## 6. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

6.1 **Order Forms.** Distributor shall order Products by submitting Order Forms to Supplier. Each Order Form shall specify the Products and quantities ordered, the requested delivery date or dates, and the delivery location, which shall be one of Distributor's warehouse facilities identified in the SOW or another location in the Territory designated by Distributor.

6.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Order Form in writing within three (3) Business Days after receipt. Supplier shall not reject any Order Form that is consistent with Distributor's most recent forecast within a variance of twenty percent (20%), except where a force majeure event under Section 16 prevents performance. An Order Form that Supplier does not reject within the three (3) Business Day period is accepted.

6.3 **Minimum Order Quantities and Lead Times.** Unless an Order Form states otherwise, the minimum order quantity for each SKU is one (1) full pallet. Supplier's standard lead time is fourteen (14) days from acceptance of an Order Form.

6.4 **Changes and Cancellations.** Distributor may change or cancel any accepted Order Form without charge by written notice delivered at least seven (7) days before the scheduled delivery date. For later changes or cancellations, Distributor shall reimburse Supplier's documented, non-cancellable costs for finished goods that Supplier cannot reasonably redirect to other customers. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate those costs.

6.5 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver the Products FCA (Incoterms 2020) Supplier's facility, loaded onto the carrier designated by Distributor, unless the applicable Order Form specifies delivery to Distributor's facility. Where delivery to Distributor's facility is specified, Supplier shall deliver DAP (Incoterms 2020) that facility. Supplier shall deliver each shipment with a packing list, a certificate of analysis for each lot as required by the Quality Agreement, and the bill of lading.

6.6 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Products pass to Distributor upon delivery in accordance with Section 6.5.

6.7 **Shelf Life.** Each Product delivered to Distributor shall have remaining shelf life of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of its total labeled shelf life on the date of delivery, unless Distributor agrees otherwise in writing for a particular shipment.

6.8 **Late Delivery.** Supplier shall notify Distributor promptly if Supplier expects any delivery to be late. If any delivery is more than five (5) Business Days late for reasons other than a force majeure event or Distributor's act or omission, Distributor may cancel the affected portion of the Order Form without liability. Distributor may also procure substitute Products for the affected Airline Customers for the period of the delay, and that substitute procurement is not a breach of Section 4.3.

6.9 **Inspection.** Distributor shall visually inspect each shipment within five (5) Business Days after delivery for shortages, damage and obvious nonconformity, and shall notify Supplier in writing of any such shortage, damage or nonconformity within that period. Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, promptly replace any short-shipped, damaged or nonconforming Products, or credit Distributor for them. Inspection or failure to inspect does not waive any of Distributor's rights for latent defects, Defective Products or breach of warranty.

## 7. PRICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay Supplier for the Products at the prices set out in the Price List in effect on the date Supplier accepts the applicable Order Form. Prices are stated in United States dollars and exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Unless an Order Form states otherwise, prices include standard packaging and palletization as described in the Specifications.

7.2 **Initial Prices.** The prices in Exhibit B are fixed for the first Contract Year.

7.3 **Price Adjustments.** Beginning with the second Contract Year, Supplier may adjust the prices in the Price List not more than once per Contract Year by written notice to Distributor (a "**Price Adjustment Notice**"). Each Price Adjustment Notice shall state the adjusted prices, the general basis for the adjustment, and the date on which the adjusted prices take effect. That date shall be no earlier than forty-five (45) days after the date of the Price Adjustment Notice. Distributor may reject a price adjustment by written notice to Supplier delivered within twenty (20) days after the date of the Price Adjustment Notice. A price adjustment that Distributor does not reject in accordance with this Section 7.3 is deemed accepted and takes effect on the date stated in the Price Adjustment Notice.

7.4 **Effect of Rejection.** If Distributor rejects a price adjustment in accordance with Section 7.3, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for up to thirty (30) days to agree any adjusted prices. If the Parties do not agree adjusted prices within that period, the prices in effect immediately before the Price Adjustment Notice continue to apply for the remainder of the then-current Contract Year.

7.5 **Price Reductions.** Supplier may reduce prices at any time by written notice to Distributor. Any reduction applies to all Order Forms accepted on or after the effective date of the reduction.

7.6 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor upon shipment of each delivery of Products. Each invoice shall reference the applicable Order Form number and state the Products, quantities, lot numbers and prices.

7.7 **Payment Terms.** Distributor shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated by Supplier in writing.

7.8 **Disputed Invoices.** Distributor may withhold payment of any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith. To do so, Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of the dispute and the basis for it before the payment due date. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days. Distributor shall pay any amount found to be owed within ten (10) Business Days after resolution.

7.9 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law, from the due date until paid.

7.10 **Taxes.** Distributor is responsible for sales, use and similar taxes on its purchase of the Products, other than taxes based on Supplier's net income, property or employees. If Distributor provides a valid resale exemption certificate, Supplier shall not charge sales tax on Products covered by that certificate.

7.11 **Set-Off.** Distributor may set off against amounts payable to Supplier under this Agreement any amount that Supplier owes to Distributor under the Agreement Documents and that is undisputed or finally determined. Those amounts include credits for nonconforming Products and Recall costs under Section 9.5.

7.12 **Records and Audit.** Each Party shall keep complete and accurate records relating to the Fees for at least three (3) years after the Contract Year to which they relate. Upon at least fifteen (15) Business Days' prior written notice, and not more than once per Contract Year, either Party may have those records audited during normal business hours by an independent accountant bound by confidentiality obligations, to verify amounts invoiced and paid. The auditing Party bears the cost of the audit, unless the audit reveals an overcharge or underpayment of more than five percent (5%) for the audited period. In that case the audited Party shall reimburse the reasonable cost of the audit. The Parties shall promptly pay or credit any amounts the audit shows to be owed.

## 9. QUALITY AND FOOD SAFETY

9.1 **Quality Agreement.** The Parties shall comply with the Quality Agreement. The Quality Agreement addresses, among other matters, supplier qualification, the Specifications, lot release, certificates of analysis, hazard analysis and preventive controls, allergen control, complaint handling, audits and Recall procedures.

9.2 **Food Safety Compliance.** Supplier shall manufacture, package, label and store the Products in compliance with Applicable Law, current good manufacturing practices, Supplier's written food safety plan and the Quality Agreement. Supplier shall maintain certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative-benchmarked scheme for each facility at which Products are manufactured, and shall provide copies of current certificates to Distributor upon request.

9.3 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall notify the other within two (2) Business Days after any inspection, inquiry, warning letter, Form 483 observation or other regulatory action by a governmental authority that relates to the Products or to the facilities at which they are manufactured or stored. Each Party shall provide the other with copies of all related correspondence that concern the Products, redacted to remove information unrelated to the Products.

9.4 **Complaints.** Each Party shall promptly forward to the other any complaint it receives from an Airline Customer, passenger or governmental authority that concerns the safety, quality or labeling of any Product. Complaints shall be investigated and documented in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

9.5 **Recalls.** If either Party believes that a Recall of any Product may be necessary or appropriate, it shall notify the other Party immediately, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours. Supplier shall decide whether to conduct a Recall, except where a governmental authority or an Airline Customer requires a Recall, in which case the Recall shall proceed. Distributor shall cooperate with any Recall. That cooperation includes identifying affected lots and Airline Customers, removing affected Products from inventory and aircraft galleys, and communicating with Airline Customers as directed by Supplier. To the extent a Recall results from a Defective Product, or from Supplier's breach of this Agreement or the Quality Agreement, Supplier shall bear all costs of the Recall. Those costs include (a) the purchase price of recalled Products, (b) Distributor's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of retrieval, storage, transport and destruction, (c) the cost of replacement Products, and (d) amounts that Distributor is required to credit or reimburse to Airline Customers for the recalled Products.

9.6 **Traceability.** Each Party shall maintain lot-level traceability records sufficient to identify, within four (4) hours of a request, the source, location and recipients of each lot of Products in its possession or control. The Parties shall retain those records for at least two (2) years after the expiration of the labeled shelf life of the relevant lot.
