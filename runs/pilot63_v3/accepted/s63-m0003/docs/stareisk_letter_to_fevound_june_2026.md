As the general basis for the adjustment, the Notice cites increases in Supplier's costs of ingredients, food-contact packaging and inbound freight since the Parties agreed the prices in Exhibit B.

## 3. Rejection

3.1 **Rejection under Section 7.3.** Distributor rejects the Proposed Increase in its entirety under Section 7.3 of the Distribution Agreement. This rejection applies to every SKU listed in the Notice and to the adjusted prices for each of them.

3.2 **Effect.** Under Section 7.4 of the Distribution Agreement, the Parties are to negotiate in good faith for up to thirty (30) days to agree any adjusted prices. If they do not agree adjusted prices within that period, the prices in effect immediately before the Notice continue to apply for the remainder of the then-current Contract Year. Distributor is ready to begin those negotiations at once, as described in Section 6.

## 4. Reasons for the Rejection

Section 7.3 of the Distribution Agreement does not require Distributor to give reasons for a rejection. We set out Distributor's principal concerns below so that the Parties' negotiations under Section 7.4 can be focused and productive.

4.1 **Magnitude of the Increase.** A uniform increase of 9.5% across all eight SKUs is significantly above the increases that Distributor is seeing from its other suppliers of packaged onboard provisions for the same period. It is also well above the published general indices for packaged food producer prices over the period since the Parties agreed the Exhibit B prices in January 2026. The Notice does not explain why Supplier's cost increases differ so markedly from those broader trends.

4.2 **Uniform Application Across Different Product Formats.** The SkyCrate line includes two quite different product formats. The shelf-stable snack boxes are assembled from purchased components. The sealed meal kits are, for the most part, retort-processed meals produced on Supplier's own lines. The cost inputs for those two formats differ, including ingredients, primary packaging, thermal processing and case packs. A single percentage applied equally to every SKU suggests that the Proposed Increase has not been calculated by reference to the actual cost movements for each SKU.

4.3 **Freight.** The Notice cites inbound freight as one of the cost drivers. Under Section 6.5 of the Distribution Agreement and Exhibit B, the Price List prices are quoted FCA Supplier's facility in Secaucus, New Jersey. Where Supplier delivers to Distributor's facility, Supplier's delivery charge is separately stated in the applicable Order Form. Distributor accepts that Supplier's inbound freight on ingredients and packaging forms part of its cost of goods. However, Distributor would like to understand what share of the Proposed Increase is attributed to inbound freight, and how that figure was calculated, so that outbound freight costs are not reflected twice.

4.4 **Timing Relative to Airline Customer Commitments.** As Supplier knows from the quarterly reviews under Section 5.5 of the Distribution Agreement, several Airline Customers in the Territory plan their buy-on-board and complimentary menus on annual or semi-annual cycles. Distributor has quoted SkyCrate pricing to those Airline Customers on the basis of the Exhibit B prices. Some of those quotations extend into the first half of 2027. An increase of the size proposed, taking effect in March 2027, would erode Distributor's margin on those commitments. It may also put at risk menu listings that Distributor and Supplier have worked together to secure, including through the launch marketing program under Supplier's letter of March 2, 2026.

4.5 **Competitive Position of the Products.** Distributor has independent discretion to set its resale prices under Section 4.6 of the Distribution Agreement. In practice, however, the Airline Customers' onboard-services managers compare the Products against competing snack boxes and meal kits offered to them by other suppliers. If Distributor passes on an increase of 9.5%, it expects that the SkyCrate Products would be priced above comparable offerings at several Airline Customers. That outcome would be contrary to both Parties' interest in growing the volume of Products sold in the Territory.

4.6 **Early Stage of the Relationship.** The Distribution Agreement has been in effect for less than four months. Distributor has invested in integrating the Products into its receiving, storage, pick-and-pack and galley-loading operations at its Philadelphia and Baltimore facilities under the SOW. It has also invested in presenting the Products to Airline Customers. Distributor's business case for those investments assumed stable pricing during the launch period. A significant increase at the first opportunity under Section 7.3 is difficult to reconcile with that shared plan.

## 5. Information Requested

5.1 To allow the Parties to negotiate on an informed basis, Distributor requests that Supplier provide the following information to the undersigned no later than five (5) Business Days before the first negotiation meeting described in Section 6.2:

(a) for each SKU, a breakdown of the Proposed Increase by cost category, namely ingredients, primary packaging, secondary and tertiary packaging, direct labor, inbound freight and any other category Supplier considers relevant;

(b) for each cost category, the percentage change in Supplier's cost per case between the date on which the Exhibit B prices were agreed and the date of the Notice, with a description of the source of the figures;

(c) a description of any cost-reduction or efficiency measures that Supplier has implemented or plans to implement at its Secaucus facility during the second Contract Year, and their expected effect on cost per case;

(d) confirmation whether the Proposed Increase is being applied to Supplier's other customers and channels for the same or substantially similar products, including the non-airline channels reserved to Supplier under Section 4.5 of the Distribution Agreement, and, if so, at what level; and

(e) any alternative pricing structure that Supplier would be willing to consider, such as differentiated increases by SKU or product format, phased increases, volume-tiered pricing, or an increase linked to a published index.

5.2 **Confidentiality of Information.** Distributor will treat all information that Supplier provides in response to Section 5.1 as Supplier's Confidential Information under Section 13 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor will use it only to evaluate and negotiate pricing under Section 7.4. Distributor will limit access to it to the members of its commercial, finance and legal teams who need to know it for that purpose. If Supplier prefers, Distributor is willing to receive cost information at an aggregated level, or to have it reviewed by an independent accountant bound by confidentiality obligations, on the model of the audit mechanism in Section 7.12 of the Distribution Agreement.

5.3 **No Admission.** Distributor's request for information does not mean that Distributor accepts that any part of the Proposed Increase is justified. It is made only to support the good-faith negotiations contemplated by Section 7.4.

Distributor is not able to accept an across-the-board increase of 9.5%.

## 7. Pricing, Orders and Invoicing

7.1 **Current Prices.** The prices in Exhibit B are fixed for the first Contract Year under Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement. Distributor will continue to submit Order Forms and pay undisputed invoices for Products at those prices in the ordinary course.

7.2 **Order Forms for the Second Contract Year.** Distributor expects to begin submitting Order Forms for deliveries in the second Contract Year during the early part of 2027. Under Section 7.1 of the Distribution Agreement, the applicable prices are those in the Price List in effect on the date Supplier accepts each Order Form. Distributor asks that Supplier not issue any acknowledgment or invoice at the prices stated in the Notice while the matter remains open between the Parties.

7.3 **Invoices.** If Supplier issues any invoice at prices other than the Exhibit B prices, Distributor will treat the difference as a disputed amount under Section 7.8 of the Distribution Agreement and will notify Supplier accordingly. Distributor will pay the undisputed portion of any such invoice within the period stated in Section 7.7.

7.4 **Forecasts.** Distributor will continue to provide its rolling six-month forecasts under Section 5.6 of the Distribution Agreement. Supplier should be aware that Distributor's forecasts for the second Contract Year, and the menu proposals Distributor is preparing for Airline Customers, assume that the Products will remain competitively priced. Distributor may need to revise those forecasts depending on the outcome of the negotiations described in Section 6.
