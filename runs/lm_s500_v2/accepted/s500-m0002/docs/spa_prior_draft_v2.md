# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

*Draft v2 — February 20, 2026. Privileged and confidential. For discussion purposes only. Subject to further review by Buyer, Seller and their respective counsel. Not an offer capable of acceptance; no agreement exists until this Agreement is executed and delivered by all parties.*

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Stailan Hotel Group, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation ("**Seller**"), **Praivein Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and, solely for purposes of Article V and Article XI, **Larkspur Row Hotels, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of 10,000 shares of common stock, 100% of the Company's outstanding equity (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company owns and operates the Hotels, a portfolio of boutique hotels located in the southeastern United States, and conducts the Business.

C. Certain trademarks, domain names and other assets used in the Business under the "Larkspur" name are held by Seller at the parent level. Seller has agreed to convey those assets to the Company at or before the Closing under the Bill of Sale.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Transition Services Agreement and the Bill of Sale.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the Bill of Sale and Assignment of Brand Assets, to be dated as of the Closing Date, by Seller in favor of the Company.

"**Business**" means the ownership, operation and management of the Hotels and all related food and beverage, event, retail and guest-services operations as conducted by the Company on the date of this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 2.3.

"**Conveyed Brand Assets**" means the "Larkspur" trademarks, service marks and associated goodwill, the Company's central reservation domain and related web properties, and the central furniture, fixtures and equipment inventory used in the Business, in each case held by Seller and conveyed to the Company under the Bill of Sale.

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Existing Lender**" means Cobblestone Federal Savings Bank.

"**Existing Indebtedness**" means all indebtedness of the Company owed to the Existing Lender, including all principal, accrued interest, fees, prepayment premiums and other amounts payable on repayment in full.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 3.1 (Organization and Good Standing), Section 3.2 (Authority; Enforceability), Section 3.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares), Section 3.9 (Title to Assets; Sufficiency), Section 3.14 (Taxes) and Section 3.16 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or any political subdivision, agency, commission, court or other regulatory authority of any of them.

"**Hotels**" means the hotels listed in Section 1.1(a) of the Disclosure Schedule, together with the land, buildings, improvements and fixtures related to each of them.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Lien**" means any mortgage, deed of trust, pledge, lien, charge, security interest, easement, encumbrance or other restriction of any kind.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, results of operations, assets or condition (financial or otherwise) of the Company and the Hotels taken as a whole. The term excludes effects arising from (i) general economic or industry conditions, (ii) changes in Law or GAAP, or (iii) the announcement of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except, in the case of clauses (i) and (ii), to the extent such effects disproportionately affect the Company relative to other participants in the boutique hotel industry.

"**Payoff Letter**" means the payoff letter issued by the Existing Lender with respect to the Existing Indebtedness.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement, to be dated as of the Closing Date, between Seller, as provider, and Buyer, as recipient.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to articles, sections and schedules of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $46,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price by wire transfer of immediately available funds as follows:

(a) on behalf of the Company, to the Existing Lender, the amount required to repay the Existing Indebtedness in full as set forth in the Payoff Letter; and

(b) to Seller, to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, the balance of the Purchase Price.

**2.3 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the third (3rd) Business Day after all conditions in Article VI have been satisfied or waived (other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing), or at such other time or place as the parties agree in writing. The date on which the Closing occurs is the "**Closing Date**."

**2.4 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amount required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority is treated as paid to the Person in respect of which it was withheld.

## ARTICLE III. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that the statements in this Article III are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

**3.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Each of Seller and the Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of its jurisdiction of incorporation. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction in which a Hotel is located.

**3.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, to perform its obligations under them and to consummate the transactions they contemplate. This Agreement constitutes, and each Ancillary Agreement when executed will constitute, a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**3.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, all of which are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares have been duly authorized, are validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable, and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller free and clear of all Liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants, rights or other commitments relating to the issuance, sale or transfer of any equity interest in the Company. On delivery of the Shares at the Closing, Buyer will acquire good and valid title to the Shares free and clear of all Liens.

**3.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller, the Company or the Hotels, or (c) require the consent of or notice to any Person under any material contract of the Company, except as set forth in Section 3.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**3.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended. The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates.

**3.6 Absence of Certain Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**3.7 Material Contracts.** Section 3.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each management, franchise, reservation-system, online travel agency, group sales, food and beverage, lease and other contract to which the Company is a party that involves annual payments in excess of $100,000 or that cannot be terminated on ninety (90) days' notice or less without penalty. Each such contract is valid and binding on the Company and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of it.

**3.8 Real Property.** Section 3.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all real property owned or leased by the Company. The Company has good and marketable fee simple title to each Hotel, free and clear of all Liens other than Liens securing the Existing Indebtedness and permitted encumbrances set forth in Section 3.8 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**3.9 Title to Assets; Sufficiency.** The Company has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all tangible and intangible assets used in the Business, free and clear of all Liens other than Liens securing the Existing Indebtedness. On delivery of the Bill of Sale, the Company will have good and valid title to the Conveyed Brand Assets, free and clear of all Liens. The assets owned or leased by the Company, together with the Conveyed Brand Assets and the services to be provided under the Transition Services Agreement, are sufficient for the continued conduct of the Business immediately after the Closing in substantially the same manner as conducted before the Closing.

**3.10 Intellectual Property.** Section 3.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all registered trademarks, service marks and domain names used in the Business, including those included in the Conveyed Brand Assets. To Seller's knowledge, the conduct of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**3.11 Hotel Licenses and Permits.** Section 3.11 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all licenses, permits, certificates of occupancy, liquor licenses, health and food-service permits and other authorizations required for the operation of each Hotel (the "**Permits**"). All Permits are valid and in full force and effect, the Company is in compliance in all material respects with them, and no suspension, cancellation or non-renewal of any Permit is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened.

**3.12 Environmental Matters.** The Company and each Hotel are, and at all times have been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable environmental Laws. There has been no release of hazardous materials at, on, under or from any Hotel that requires investigation or remediation under environmental Law. The Company has not received any notice of any actual or alleged violation of environmental Law.

**3.13 Condition of Real Property.** The buildings, structures, building systems (including mechanical, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire-suppression and life-safety systems) and other improvements at each Hotel are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are in material compliance with applicable building, zoning, fire, accessibility and historic-preservation codes. No condemnation proceeding is pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened with respect to any Hotel.

**3.14 Taxes.** All income and other material Tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true, correct and complete in all material respects, and all Taxes due and owing by the Company have been timely paid. No Tax audit or proceeding is pending against the Company.

**3.15 Employees.** Section 3.15 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all applicable employment, wage-and-hour and labor Laws, and no collective bargaining agreement covers any employee of the Company.

**3.16 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any brokerage, finder's or other fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date that:

**4.1 Organization; Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement.

**4.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**4.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**4.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE V. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business and the Hotels in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Permits in full force and effect, and preserve its relationships with guests, suppliers, employees and Governmental Authorities. Without Buyer's prior written consent, the Company shall not incur indebtedness (other than under the Existing Indebtedness in the ordinary course), sell or encumber any material asset, amend any material contract, or declare any dividend other than a cash dividend paid in full before the Closing.

**5.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Hotels, books and records, contracts and personnel of the Company, including to conduct property-condition and Phase I environmental assessments.

**5.3 Conveyance of Brand Assets.** At or before the Closing, Seller shall execute and deliver the Bill of Sale and take all further action reasonably necessary to vest in the Company good and valid title to the Conveyed Brand Assets, including filing trademark assignments with the United States Patent and Trademark Office and transferring domain registrations.

**5.4 Repayment of Existing Indebtedness.** Seller shall cause the Company to obtain and deliver to Buyer, no later than two (2) Business Days before the Closing Date, the Payoff Letter. The Payoff Letter shall state the amount required to repay the Existing Indebtedness in full as of the Closing Date and shall provide for the release of all Liens securing it on receipt of that amount.

**5.5 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or circumstance that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 6.1 not to be satisfied.

**5.6 Confidentiality; Public Announcements.** No party shall issue any press release or public announcement concerning this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by Law. After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company and the Business.

**5.7 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further action as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VI. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**6.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction (or waiver by Buyer) of the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and all other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred;

(d) Seller shall have delivered the Payoff Letter; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each item in Section 7.1.

**6.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction (or waiver by Seller) of the following conditions: (a) Buyer's representations and warranties shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before or at the Closing; and (c) Buyer shall have delivered each item in Section 7.2.

## ARTICLE VII. CLOSING DELIVERABLES

**7.1 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller;

(c) the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(d) evidence of the release of all Liens securing the Existing Indebtedness, effective on payment of the amount stated in the Payoff Letter;

(e) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(f) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 6.1(a), (b) and (c); and

(g) good standing certificates for Seller and the Company dated no more than ten (10) days before the Closing Date.

**7.2 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.2 and the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Seller and Buyer;

(b) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred by [June 30], 2026, unless the failure to close results from a breach of this Agreement by the party seeking to terminate;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.1 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.2 would not be satisfied and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** On termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect, except that this Section 8.2, Section 5.6 and Article XI survive and no party is relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.**

(a) The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until six (6) years after the Closing Date.

(b) All other representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

(c) The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed.

(d) No claim for indemnification may be made in respect of any representation or warranty after the expiration of its survival period. A claim made by written notice delivered before that expiration, describing the claim in reasonable detail, shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article III, other than the Fundamental Representations;

(b) any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation;

(c) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(d) any Existing Indebtedness or any Tax of the Company attributable to any period ending on or before the Closing Date, in each case to the extent not paid at or before the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article IV, or (b) any breach or non-fulfillment of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim for which it seeks indemnification. A failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure. For any claim brought by a third party, the indemnifying party may assume the defense at its expense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. The indemnifying party shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable for Losses described in Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate amount of all such Losses exceeds $465,000 (the "**Deductible**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The aggregate amount of all Losses described in Section 9.2(a) for which Seller shall be liable shall not exceed $4,650,000 (10% of the Purchase Price) (the "**General Cap**").

(b) The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller shall be liable under Section 9.2 shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The limitations in this Section 9.5 do not apply to Losses arising from fraud or intentional misrepresentation.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, the representations and warranties of Seller shall be read without regard to any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in them.

(e) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect of them, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of the representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in this Agreement. This Section 9.6 does not limit claims for fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief under Section 11.9, or claims arising under any Ancillary Agreement.

**9.7 Treatment of Payments.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, consistent with past practice. Seller shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review and comment at least twenty (20) days before filing.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and similar Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any audit or proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are deemed given (a) when delivered by hand, (b) one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or (c) when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, if sent before 5:00 p.m. recipient's local time on a Business Day (and otherwise on the next Business Day). Notices shall be addressed to the parties at the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith to modify this Agreement to give effect to their original intent as closely as possible.

**11.4 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver is effective unless set forth in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**11.5 Successors.** This Agreement is binding on and inures to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other party. Buyer may, however, without consent, collaterally assign its rights under this Agreement to any lender providing financing for the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

**11.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns, and nothing in it confers any right or remedy on any other Person.

**11.8 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware) for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**11.9 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy to which it is entitled at law or in equity.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ALL RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE TRANSACTIONS IT CONTEMPLATES.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
