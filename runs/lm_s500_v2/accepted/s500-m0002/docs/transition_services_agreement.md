# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Stailan Hotel Group, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation ("**Provider**"), and **Praivein Hospitality Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Recipient are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), by and among Provider, as seller, Recipient, as buyer, and Larkspur Row Hotels, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring all of the outstanding shares of the Company.

B. Before the Closing, Provider supplied centralized property-management, central reservations, revenue-management and loyalty program services to the Company from Provider's parent-level platforms.

C. The Purchase Agreement contemplates that Provider and Recipient will enter into this Agreement at the Closing so that Provider continues to provide those services for a transitional period after the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Hotel Data**" means all guest profiles, reservation and booking records, folio and payment information, loyalty member records, rate plans, pricing and yield strategies, forecasts, channel and distribution data, and other data relating to the Hotels or their guests, whether created before or after the Effective Date.

"**Hotels**" means the three hotels identified in Exhibit A.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Provider Systems**" means the property-management system, central reservations system, revenue-management software, loyalty platform and related hardware, software, interfaces and hosting environments owned, licensed or operated by Provider or its Affiliates and used to provide the Services.

"**Service Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Transition Period**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** In this Agreement, "including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Transition Period.** Provider shall provide the Services during the period beginning on the Closing Date and ending ninety (90) days after the Closing Date (the "**Transition Period**").

**2.2 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless terminated earlier under Article 10, expires at the end of the Transition Period. Sections 5.3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12 survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 3. SERVICES

**3.1 Scope of Services.** During the Transition Period, Provider shall provide the following services for the Hotels (collectively, the "**Services**"), as further described in Exhibit B:

(a) **Property Management.** Hosting, operation, maintenance and support of the property-management system used for front-desk operations, guest check-in and check-out, room inventory and housekeeping status, guest folios and night audit;

(b) **Central Reservations.** Operation of the central reservations system and call-center functions, including connectivity with the Company's booking engine at the central reservation domain, global distribution systems and online travel agency channels;

(c) **Revenue Management.** Rate loading, inventory and availability management, demand forecasting, and pricing recommendations through Provider's revenue-management platform; and

(d) **Loyalty.** Continued enrollment, point accrual and redemption, and member communications under the loyalty program in which guests of the Hotels participated immediately before the Closing.

**3.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, with substantially the same scope, priority, quality and level of care as Provider provided similar services to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date, and in compliance with applicable Law.

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign personnel with appropriate skill and experience to perform the Services. Provider retains sole control over the employment, compensation and supervision of its personnel performing the Services.

**3.4 Service Coordinators.** Each party shall designate a service coordinator responsible for day-to-day communications regarding the Services. The coordinators shall meet (in person or by video conference) at least every two weeks during the Transition Period to review service levels, open issues and migration progress.

**3.5 Migration Assistance.** During the Transition Period, Provider shall cooperate in good faith with Recipient and its designated vendors to migrate the Hotel Data and the functions supported by the Services to systems selected by Recipient. Provider shall deliver complete extracts of the Hotel Data in a commercially reasonable, industry-standard electronic format on Recipient's reasonable request, and in any event no later than ten (10) Business Days before the end of the Transition Period.

**3.6 Excluded Services.** Except as expressly set forth in this Article 3 and Exhibit B, Provider has no obligation to provide any other services to Recipient or the Company.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fee.** In consideration for the Services, Recipient shall pay Provider a fee of $38,000 per month (the "**Service Fee**"). The Service Fee for any partial month during the Transition Period shall be prorated on a daily basis.

**4.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket third-party costs incurred at Recipient's written request that are outside the ordinary scope of the Services, without markup, provided Recipient has approved the cost in writing in advance.

**4.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt by wire transfer to the account designated by Provider. Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Provider in writing of the basis for the dispute before the due date. The parties shall resolve any dispute under Section 11.2.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Service Fee is exclusive of sales, use and similar Taxes. Recipient shall pay any such Taxes imposed on the Services, other than Taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 5. DATA AND SYSTEMS

**5.1 Ownership of Hotel Data.** As between the parties, all Hotel Data is and remains the property of the Company. Provider acquires no right, title or interest in the Hotel Data by reason of this Agreement.

**5.2 Access to Hotel Data.** Recipient shall cause the Company to provide, and Provider is authorized to receive, process and store, the Hotel Data, including guest data, rate strategy and booking information, to the extent necessary to perform the Services. Provider shall use the Hotel Data solely to perform the Services.

**5.3 Data Protection.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for the Hotel Data that are no less protective than those Provider applies to its own comparable data, and in any event consistent with applicable Law and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. Provider shall notify Recipient within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Hotel Data.

**5.4 Return of Data.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of the Transition Period, Provider shall deliver to Recipient a final complete copy of the Hotel Data and then securely delete all Hotel Data from the Provider Systems, except to the extent retention is required by Law. Provider shall certify that deletion in writing on request.

**5.5 Provider Systems.** Provider retains all right, title and interest in the Provider Systems. Recipient and the Company may access and use the Provider Systems during the Transition Period solely to receive the Services and in accordance with Provider's reasonable access and security policies, as provided to Recipient in writing.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Obligations.** Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other party (and, in the case of Recipient, of the Company) disclosed or made available in connection with this Agreement, including the Hotel Data ("**Confidential Information**"). Each party shall use Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any Person other than its employees, officers and advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Article 6.

**6.2 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, (b) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential Information. A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law, provided it gives the other party prompt notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

**6.3 Duration.** The obligations in this Article 6 continue for three (3) years after the end of the Transition Period and, for guest personal information and payment card data, for so long as the receiving party holds that information.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors, managers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel in performing the Services.

**7.2 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the Services or the use of the Services by Recipient, the Company or the Hotels, including any Losses arising from Provider's negligence, except to the extent such Losses arise from Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**7.3 Procedure.** A party seeking indemnification shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of the claim. A failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced by that failure. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the indemnifying party and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any punitive or exemplary damages arising out of or relating to this Agreement, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**8.2 Cap.** The aggregate liability of each party arising out of or relating to this Agreement, other than obligations under Section 7.2, shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid by Recipient under this Agreement.

**8.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the Services, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 9. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, hurricanes, floods, fire, epidemic, war, terrorism, civil disturbance, or failure of public utilities or telecommunications networks. The affected party shall promptly notify the other party, use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance, and implement its business-continuity and disaster-recovery plans to the extent applicable to the Services.

## 10. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination by Recipient for Convenience.** Recipient may terminate this Agreement, or any of the Services described in Section 3.1(a) through (d), on fifteen (15) days' written notice to Provider. On any partial termination, the Service Fee shall be reduced in accordance with the allocation set forth in Exhibit B.

**10.2 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Provider may not terminate or suspend the Services for non-payment of any amount Recipient disputes in good faith under Section 4.3.

**10.3 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, Recipient shall pay all undisputed Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and Provider shall comply with Section 5.4.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW; DISPUTES

**11.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction.

**11.2 Dispute Resolution.** The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement through their service coordinators and, if unresolved within ten (10) Business Days, through senior executives of each party. If the dispute remains unresolved within a further ten (10) Business Days, either party may bring an action in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware). Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts. Nothing in this Section 11.2 prevents a party from seeking injunctive relief at any time to prevent irreparable harm.

**11.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ALL RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Relationship of the Parties.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship between the parties.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and given in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, addressed to the parties at the addresses set forth on the signature page, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**12.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless set forth in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**12.4 Assignment; Successors.** Recipient shall not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of Provider, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. This Agreement is binding on and inures to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and assigns.

**12.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**12.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Persons entitled to indemnification under Article 7 and the Company, nothing in this Agreement confers any right or remedy on any Person other than the parties and their respective successors and assigns.

**12.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.
