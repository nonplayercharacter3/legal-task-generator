## Preliminary Statements

1. This Disclosure Schedule is delivered by Stailan Hotel Group, Inc. ("**Seller**") to Praivein Hospitality Partners, LLC ("**Buyer**") concurrently with the execution of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"), by and among Seller, Buyer and Larkspur Row Hotels, Inc. (the "**Company**").

2. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement. Section numbers in this Disclosure Schedule correspond to the section numbers of the Agreement.

3. A disclosure in any section of this Disclosure Schedule is deemed disclosed for purposes of any other section of Article III of the Agreement to the extent its relevance to that other section is reasonably apparent on the face of the disclosure.

4. Headings are inserted for convenience of reference only. The inclusion of any item in this Disclosure Schedule does not constitute an admission that the item is material or outside the ordinary course of business.

## Section 3.4 — No Conflicts; Consents

The following contracts require notice to or consent of the counterparty in connection with the transactions contemplated by the Agreement:

Loan Agreement dated as of September 30, 2021, between the Company and Cobblestone Federal Savings Bank, together with the related mortgages, deeds of trust and security agreements encumbering each Hotel. ## Section 3.8 — Real Property

**(a) Owned Real Property.** The Company owns fee simple title to the land and improvements comprising each Hotel listed in Section 1.1(a).

**(b) Leased Real Property.** The parking lot leased under the Ground Lease described in item 7 of Section 3.7.

**(c) Permitted Encumbrances.**

1. Mortgages, deeds of trust and assignments of rents in favor of Cobblestone Federal Savings Bank securing the Existing Indebtedness. These will be released at Closing upon payment of the amount stated in the Payoff Letter.

2. Recorded utility easements in favor of the applicable municipal and public utility providers at each Hotel.

3. Historic preservation façade easement in favor of the Historic Charleston Foundation encumbering The Larkspur Charleston, recorded in the Charleston County Register of Deeds.

4. Shared-wall and party-wall agreement with the adjoining owner of 219 East Jones Street, affecting The Larkspur Savannah.

5. Liens for current real property Taxes not yet due and payable.
