**Re: Payoff Letter: Loan Agreement dated as of September 30, 2021 (as amended, the "Loan Agreement"), between Larkspur Row Hotels, Inc. (the "Borrower") and Cobblestone Federal Savings Bank (the "Lender")**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

The Borrower has told the Lender that Stailan Hotel Group, Inc. ("**Seller**") intends to sell all outstanding shares of the Borrower to Praivein Hospitality Partners, LLC ("**Buyer**") under a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The Borrower has also told the Lender that it intends to repay in full all obligations owing under the Loan Agreement and the related loan documents (collectively, the "**Loan Documents**") on the date of that closing.

At the Borrower's request, the Lender provides this letter (this "**Payoff Letter**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Payoff Letter have the meanings given in the Loan Agreement.

## 1. Payoff Amount

**1.1 Amount Due.** If payment is received by the Lender by 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on April 24, 2026 (the "**Anticipated Payoff Date**"), the total amount required to repay all Obligations under the Loan Documents in full is **$28,253,441.67** (the "**Payoff Amount**"). The Payoff Amount consists of the following:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Outstanding principal balance | $27,850,000.00 |
| Accrued and unpaid interest from April 1, 2026 through April 24, 2026 (24 days at 6.25% per annum, actual/360) | $116,041.67 |
| Prepayment premium (1.00% of outstanding principal under Section 2.7 of the Loan Agreement) | $278,500.00 |
| Release preparation and recording fees (four Hotels) | $2,400.00 |
| Lender's counsel fees through the Anticipated Payoff Date | $6,500.00 |
| **Total Payoff Amount** | **$28,253,441.67** |

**1.2 Per Diem.** If the Payoff Amount is received after the Anticipated Payoff Date, interest accrues at **$4,835.07 per day** for each day after April 24, 2026 up to and including the date of receipt. Funds received after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time on any Business Day are treated as received on the next Business Day. This Payoff Letter expires if the Payoff Amount, together with any applicable per diem interest, has not been received by **May 29, 2026**. After that date the Borrower must request an updated payoff letter.

## 2. Release of Liens

**2.1 Release on Payment.** When the Lender receives the Payoff Amount, together with any applicable per diem interest, in accordance with Section 1 (the "**Payoff Time**"), all of the following occurs automatically, without any further action by any person:

(a) all Obligations of the Borrower under the Loan Documents are paid and satisfied in full, other than contingent indemnification obligations for which no claim has been asserted;

(b) the Loan Agreement and the other Loan Documents terminate, except for provisions that by their terms survive repayment; and

(c) all security interests, mortgages, deeds of trust, deeds to secure debt, assignments of leases and rents, and other Liens granted to the Lender on any property of the Borrower are released and terminated.

**2.2 Security Instruments.** The Liens being released include those created under the following instruments, each dated as of September 30, 2021:

| Hotel | Instrument | Recording Office |
|---|---|---|
| The Larkspur Charleston | Mortgage, Assignment of Leases and Rents, Security Agreement and Fixture Filing | Charleston County Register of Deeds, South Carolina |
| The Larkspur Asheville | Deed of Trust, Assignment of Leases and Rents, Security Agreement and Fixture Filing | Buncombe County Register of Deeds, North Carolina |
| The Larkspur Savannah | Deed to Secure Debt, Assignment of Leases and Rents and Security Agreement | Clerk of Superior Court of Chatham County, Georgia |
| The Larkspur Beaufort | Mortgage, Assignment of Leases and Rents, Security Agreement and Fixture Filing | Beaufort County Register of Deeds, South Carolina |

The Liens being released also include those perfected by UCC-1 financing statement No. [●], filed with the Delaware Secretary of State, Division of Corporations.

**2.3 Release Documents.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Payoff Time, the Lender shall deliver to the Borrower, or to Buyer's designee, recordable satisfactions, cancellations or releases of each instrument listed in Section 2.2. This includes, for The Larkspur Asheville, any instrument required for cancellation of the Deed of Trust by the trustee. Within the same period, the Lender shall deliver a UCC-3 termination statement for each financing statement listed in Section 2.2. From and after the Payoff Time, the Borrower, Buyer or their designees may file the UCC-3 termination statements.

**2.4 Further Assurances.** After the Payoff Time, at the Borrower's request and expense, the Lender shall promptly execute and deliver any additional releases, terminations, notices or other documents reasonably required to evidence the release of the Lender's Liens. These include notices to depository banks and credit-card processors that terminate any deposit account control agreement or other control arrangement in favor of the Lender.

## 3. Reserves, Escrows and Collateral Accounts

**3.1 Return of Reserves.** As of the date of this Payoff Letter, the Lender holds the following amounts for the Borrower: $412,880.14 in the real estate tax and insurance escrow, and $186,250.00 in the furniture, fixtures and equipment replacement reserve. These balances are not credited against the Payoff Amount. Within ten (10) Business Days after the Payoff Time, the Lender shall remit all reserve and escrow balances, including any interest earned on them, to an account designated in writing by the Borrower.

**3.2 Insurance.** From and after the Payoff Time, the Lender releases all rights as mortgagee, loss payee and additional insured under the Borrower's property and liability insurance policies. On request, the Lender shall execute any notice to the Borrower's insurers that confirms this release.

## 4. Reliance

Buyer, Seller, the Borrower and their respective counsel, title insurers and successors may rely on this Payoff Letter in connection with the closing under the Purchase Agreement. This includes any title insurance company issuing policies on the Hotels.

The Lender confirms the following:

(a) to its knowledge, no amounts are owing under the Loan Documents other than those reflected in Section 1;

(b) the Payoff Amount is the full amount required to satisfy all Obligations as of the Anticipated Payoff Date; and

(c) no consent of the Lender is required in connection with the transfer of the Borrower's shares, provided that the Payoff Amount is received at or before the closing.

## 5. Miscellaneous

**5.1 Reinstatement.** If any payment applied to the Payoff Amount is rescinded or must be returned on the insolvency or bankruptcy of any person, the Obligations corresponding to that payment are reinstated. The release of Liens in Section 2 is not affected.
