# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT OF BRAND ASSETS

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT OF BRAND ASSETS (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by **Stailan Hotel Group, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation ("**Seller**"), in favor of **Larkspur Row Hotels, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller, Praivein Hospitality Partners, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), and the Company are parties to the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller 10,000 shares of common stock of the Company, constituting 100% of the Company's outstanding equity.

B. Seller holds at the parent level certain trademarks, service marks, domain names and furniture, fixtures and equipment used in the Business under the "Larkspur" name.

C. Section 5.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at or before the Closing and to vest in the Company good and valid title to the Conveyed Brand Assets.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the consideration provided under the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2 Conveyed Brand Assets.** "**Conveyed Brand Assets**" means all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the following:

(a) the trademarks and service marks listed in Schedule A, together with all registrations and applications for them, all common-law rights in them, all goodwill of the Business associated with them, and all rights to sue for and recover damages for past, present and future infringement of them (the "**Marks**");

(b) the domain names listed in Schedule B, together with the related registrar accounts, DNS settings, website content, booking-engine pages and other web properties hosted at those domains (the "**Domains**"); and

(c) the central furniture, fixtures and equipment inventory described in Schedule C (the "**Central FF&E**").

## 2. CONVEYANCE

**2.1 Transfer.** Seller hereby irrevocably sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to the Company, and the Company hereby accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Conveyed Brand Assets, free and clear of all Liens, effective as of the Effective Date.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Marks includes the goodwill of the Business symbolized by the Marks, so that the Company succeeds to the portion of the Business to which the Marks pertain.

**2.3 Excluded Assets.** This Bill of Sale does not convey any Provider Systems (as defined in the Transition Services Agreement) or any other asset of Seller not described in Section 1.2. Access to the Provider Systems after the Closing is governed by the Transition Services Agreement.

## 3. DELIVERY AND RECORDATION

**3.1 Trademark Assignment.** Concurrently with the delivery of this Bill of Sale, Seller shall execute and deliver to the Company a short-form trademark assignment in the form of Schedule D for recordation with the United States Patent and Trademark Office. The Company may record that assignment, and this Bill of Sale, at its expense.

**3.2 Domain Transfer.** Within three (3) Business Days after the Effective Date, Seller shall initiate and complete the transfer of each Domain to a registrar account designated by the Company, provide all authorization codes and credentials required for that transfer, and maintain the existing DNS configuration of each Domain until the transfer is complete, so that the central reservation domain remains available to guests without interruption.

**3.3 Central FF&E.** Title to and risk of loss of the Central FF&E pass to the Company on the Effective Date. Seller shall make the Central FF&E available for collection by the Company at the locations listed in Schedule C at any time during normal business hours within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date, and shall store and insure it at Seller's cost until collected.

## 4. PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**4.1 Delivered Under the Purchase Agreement.** This Bill of Sale is an Ancillary Agreement and is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants and agreements of Seller concerning the Conveyed Brand Assets are set forth in the Purchase Agreement, and Seller makes no additional representation or warranty in this Bill of Sale.

**4.2 Title Claims.** Any claim by the Company, Buyer or any other Buyer Indemnitee relating to title to, or any Lien on, any Conveyed Brand Asset, or to the validity or effectiveness of the conveyance under Section 2.1, shall be brought solely as a claim under Section 9.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement, by notice given in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.3 No Merger.** The covenants of Seller in the Purchase Agreement relating to the Conveyed Brand Assets, including Section 5.3 and Section 5.7 of the Purchase Agreement, are not merged into this Bill of Sale and continue in accordance with their terms.

## 5. FURTHER ASSURANCES; POWER OF ATTORNEY

**5.1 Further Assurances.** At any time after the Effective Date, at the Company's reasonable request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute, acknowledge and deliver such further instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take such other action, as may be reasonably required to vest in the Company, and to perfect and record, title to the Conveyed Brand Assets.

**5.2 Received Assets.** If Seller or any of its Affiliates receives any payment, correspondence, inquiry or other communication relating to a Conveyed Brand Asset after the Effective Date, Seller shall promptly forward it to the Company.

**5.3 Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file in Seller's name any document required to record or perfect the transfer of the Conveyed Brand Assets, if Seller fails to do so within ten (10) Business Days after the Company's written request. This power is coupled with an interest.

## 6. GENERAL

**6.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale is binding on Seller and its successors and assigns and inures to the benefit of the Company and its successors and assigns.

**6.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 11.8 and Section 11.11 of the Purchase Agreement apply to any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale, *mutatis mutandis*.

**6.3 Notices.** Notices relating to this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner set forth in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**6.5 Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, and signatures delivered by email in PDF format or by electronic signature service are effective as originals.

### Central FF&E
