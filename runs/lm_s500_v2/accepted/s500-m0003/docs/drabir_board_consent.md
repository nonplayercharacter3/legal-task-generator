# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DRABIR AGRIAERIAL HOLDINGS, INC.

**October 19, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), act under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the Bylaws of the Company. They hereby waive all requirements of notice and adopt the following resolutions by unanimous written consent, without a meeting, with the same force and effect as if adopted at a duly called and held meeting of the Board.

## 1. Background

1.1 On August 14, 2026, the Company, as Buyer, entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company agreed to acquire the precision-agriculture drone business of Seller. That business includes:

- the AgriSpray spray-drone product line;
- the FieldPath flight-planning software; and
- the CanopyLens agronomic analytics platform.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 Concurrently with the Purchase Agreement, the Company and Seller entered into the Side Letter dated August 14, 2026. The Purchase Agreement also contemplates that the following will be delivered at the Closing:

- the Seller Note;
- the IP Assignment Agreement;
- the Bill of Sale;
- the Assignment and Assumption Agreement; and
- the other Ancillary Agreements.

1.3 The Purchase Price is $41,500,000, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. It is payable at the Closing as follows:

(a) $34,300,000 in cash; and

(b) $7,200,000 by the issuance by the Company to Seller of the Seller Note.

1.4 The Closing has not occurred. Under Section 10.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement, either party may terminate the Purchase Agreement if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 15, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**").

1.5 Seller has delivered to the Company Schedule Supplement No. 1 to the Disclosure Schedule ("**Schedule Supplement No. 1**"). Schedule Supplement No. 1 discloses that the Federal Aviation Administration (the "**FAA**") has opened an investigation into Seller's aerial-application spray-drone operations conducted under 14 C.F.R. Part 137 (the "**FAA Matter**"). The investigation may result in civil penalties and action against Seller's certificate.

1.6 Management and the Company's outside counsel have briefed the Board on the following:

(a) the status of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the FAA Matter;

(c) the status of the Required Consents; and

(d) the Company's financing of the cash portion of the Purchase Price.

## 2. Ratification of the Purchase Agreement and the Side Letter

**RESOLVED**, that the Board ratifies, confirms and approves in all respects the following:

(a) the execution, delivery and performance by the Company of the Purchase Agreement and the Side Letter; and

(b) all actions taken by the officers of the Company in connection with their negotiation, execution and delivery.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board approves the forms of the Ancillary Agreements to be executed by the Company at the Closing, including:

- the Seller Note;
- the IP Assignment Agreement; and
- the Assignment and Assumption Agreement.

The Board approves each in substantially the form attached to the Purchase Agreement or previously provided to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officers (defined below) may approve. An Authorized Officer's execution of an agreement will be conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. The FAA Matter

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has reviewed Schedule Supplement No. 1 and the information presented by management and counsel regarding the FAA Matter. The Board determines that the FAA Matter, and the potential civil penalties and certificate action described in Schedule Supplement No. 1, are unacceptable to the Company.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company does not accept Schedule Supplement No. 1 as curing any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller. This includes the representations and warranties in Sections 3.13 (Legal Proceedings; Governmental Orders) and 3.14 (Compliance with Laws; Permits) of the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer and the General Counsel of the Company are each authorized and directed to:

(a) notify Seller in writing, in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement, that the Company objects to the FAA Matter and reserves all of its rights under the Purchase Agreement, including its rights to indemnification under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement with respect to the FAA Matter;

(b) request from Seller copies of all correspondence with the FAA relating to the FAA Matter, together with Seller's proposed response and remediation plan; and

(c) report to the Board on Seller's response and on the Company's alternatives with respect to the FAA Matter, including any proposed amendment of the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that nothing in these resolutions constitutes a waiver by the Company of any right, remedy or condition under the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. Issuance of the Seller Note

**RESOLVED**, that, subject to the Closing, the Board authorizes the Company to execute, issue and deliver the Seller Note to Seller in the principal amount of $7,200,000, on the terms set forth in the form of Seller Note attached as Exhibit D to the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Board determines the following to be in the best interests of the Company:

(a) the obligations of the Company under the Seller Note, including the subordination provisions of the Seller Note; and

(b) the right of set-off under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Payment of the Cash Purchase Price

**RESOLVED**, that, subject to the Closing, the Board authorizes the Company to pay the cash portion of the Purchase Price of $34,300,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds in accordance with Section 2.7(b)(i) of the Purchase Agreement. Payment may be made from cash on hand and available borrowings under the Company's existing credit facility.

## 6. Closing Matters

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to do the following, in each case in the name and on behalf of the Company:

(a) to take all actions reasonably necessary to satisfy the conditions to Closing set forth in Article 7 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) to cooperate with Seller under Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement in seeking the Required Consents; and

(c) to deliver the officer's certificates required by Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are directed to report to the Board no later than November 30, 2026 on the anticipated Closing Date relative to the Outside Date.

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, empowered and directed, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to do the following:

(a) to execute and deliver all agreements, certificates, instruments, notices and other documents;

(b) to pay all fees and expenses; and

(c) to take all other actions that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or advisable to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any officer or director of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness; Counterparts

8.1 This consent is effective as of the date first written above.

8.2 This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and delivered by e-mail or other electronic transmission. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.

8.3 This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
