**Re: Side Letter to Asset Purchase Agreement**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered at the same time as the execution of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). This Side Letter sets out certain additional agreements between Buyer and Seller relating to the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement. It constitutes the "Side Letter" referred to in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 In this Side Letter, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Grower Cooperatives**" means the agricultural grower cooperatives and individual grower participants that took part in the Harvest Drift Trial. This includes Pellwick Grower Cooperative.

"**Harvest Drift Trial**" means the pesticide-drift field program conducted by or on behalf of Seller with the Grower Cooperatives during the 2025 and 2026 growing seasons. The program covered:

- the operation of AgriSpray spray drones over participating acreage;
- the collection of drift, deposition and canopy data; and
- the related aerial-application flights, product applications, grower agreements and data-sharing arrangements.

"**Trial Data**" means all field logs, flight logs, deposition measurements, imagery and agronomic data generated in the course of the Harvest Drift Trial.

1.3 Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Side Letter as if set out in full in this Side Letter, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Side Letter.

## 2. Harvest Drift Trial Indemnity

2.1 From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its members, managers, officers, employees and agents against any and all Losses arising out of or relating to the Harvest Drift Trial. This includes Losses arising out of:

(a) any Action brought by any Grower Cooperative, grower, landowner, neighboring property owner or other Person alleging crop, livestock, property or personal injury damage resulting from pesticide drift, off-target application or overspray in connection with the Harvest Drift Trial;

(b) any Action, inquiry, penalty or fine of any Governmental Authority relating to aerial-application operations, pesticide use or recordkeeping in connection with the Harvest Drift Trial; and

(c) any claim under any grower participation agreement, release or data-sharing arrangement entered into by Seller in connection with the Harvest Drift Trial.

2.2 Buyer's obligations under this Section 2 shall not be subject to the Cap, the Basket or any other limitation set forth in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

2.3 Section 9.4 (Indemnification Procedures) of the Purchase Agreement applies to any claim under this Section 2. For that purpose:

- Seller or the applicable indemnified Person is the "Indemnified Party"; and
- Buyer is the "Indemnifying Party".

2.4 Buyer's obligations under this Section 2 survive the Closing until fully performed.

## 3. Trial Data and Grower Communications

3.1 At the Closing, Seller shall deliver all Trial Data to Buyer as part of the books and records included in the Purchased Assets under Section 2.1(g) of the Purchase Agreement.

3.2 Between the date of this Side Letter and the Closing, Seller shall:

(a) consult with Buyer in good faith before sending any material written communication to the Grower Cooperatives regarding the results or conclusion of the Harvest Drift Trial; and

(b) give Buyer a reasonable opportunity to comment on any such communication.

3.3 For a period of twelve (12) months after the Closing, Seller shall provide Buyer with reasonable access to those of its former personnel who supervised the Harvest Drift Trial and who are not hired by Buyer. Access will be given during normal business hours and on reasonable advance notice. Its purpose is to help Buyer interpret the Trial Data. Buyer shall reimburse Seller for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs incurred in providing that access.

## 4. Use of Seller Name

4.1 For a period of one hundred twenty (120) days after the Closing Date, Buyer may use the name "Troudaind" in the following ways, in each case only in a manner consistent with Seller's practice before the Closing:

(a) on existing inventory, packaging, labels, manuals and marketing materials included in the Purchased Assets; and

(b) to identify the Business as formerly operated by Seller.

4.2 Buyer shall not represent that it is Seller or an Affiliate of Seller.

4.3 Within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date, Seller shall file an amendment to its certificate of formation. The amendment shall change Seller's name to a name that does not include "AgriSpray", "FieldPath" or "CanopyLens".

## 5. Miscellaneous

5.1 **Effectiveness.** This Side Letter becomes effective on the date of this Side Letter. Sections 2, 3.1, 3.3 and 4 become operative on the Closing. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with Article 10 before the Closing, this Side Letter terminates automatically at the same time and is of no further force or effect.

5.2 **Amendment.** This Side Letter may be amended, modified or waived only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller.

5.3 **Notices.** Notices under this Side Letter shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.4 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Side Letter except to a Person to whom it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.5 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware. Sections 11.7 (Submission to Jurisdiction) and 11.8 (Waiver of Jury Trial) of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Side Letter.

5.6 **Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and delivery. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
