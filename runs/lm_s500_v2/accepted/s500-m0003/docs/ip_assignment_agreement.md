# FORM OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**Exhibit C to Asset Purchase Agreement**

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**IP Assignment Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the Closing Date) (the "**Effective Date**"). The parties are **Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Assignor**"), and **Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor, as Seller, and Assignee, as Buyer, are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor has agreed to sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Assignee, and Assignee has agreed to purchase from Assignor, the Purchased Assets.

B. Section 5.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to deliver this IP Assignment Agreement at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this IP Assignment Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this IP Assignment Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Assigned IP**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Business**" means the design, manufacture and sale of Seller's AgriSpray drone hardware, as conducted by Assignor as of the date of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Intellectual Property**" means any and all rights in, arising out of, or associated with any of the following in any jurisdiction throughout the world:

(a) patents and patent applications, including all provisionals, continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, re-examinations and extensions of any of them;

(b) trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and other source identifiers, together with the goodwill associated with any of them, and all registrations and applications for registration of any of them;

(c) copyrights and works of authorship, including software (in source code and object code form), firmware, data, databases and documentation, and all registrations and applications for registration of any of them;

(d) trade secrets, know-how, algorithms, models, inventions and other confidential information;

(e) internet domain names and social media accounts; and

(f) all other intellectual or industrial property and proprietary rights.

"**Recordation Authority**" means the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the United States Copyright Office, any domain name registrar, and any corresponding Governmental Authority or registry in any other jurisdiction.

**1.2 Other Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this IP Assignment Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this IP Assignment Agreement as if set out in full in this IP Assignment Agreement, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this IP Assignment Agreement.

## 2. Assignment

**2.1 Assignment of Assigned IP.** For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee hereby accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in, to and under all Intellectual Property owned by Assignor and used or held for use in the Business, including the Intellectual Property set forth in Schedule A (collectively, the "**Assigned IP**"), free and clear of any Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**2.2 Included Rights.** The assignment in Section 2.1 includes all of the following, in each case to the extent relating to the Assigned IP:

(a) all income, royalties, damages and payments now or hereafter due or payable with respect to the Assigned IP;

(b) all claims and causes of action with respect to any of the Assigned IP, whether accruing before, on or after the Effective Date, including all rights to sue for and recover damages and profits for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation, dilution or other violation of the Assigned IP, and all rights to injunctive and other equitable relief;

(c) all rights of priority and protection of interests in the Assigned IP under the Laws of any jurisdiction worldwide; and

(d) all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP, including design files, drawings, bills of materials, manufacturing process documentation, source code, object code and technical documentation.

**2.3 Goodwill.** The assignment of each trademark included in the Assigned IP includes the goodwill of the Business connected with the use of and symbolized by that trademark.

**2.4 Moral Rights.** To the extent permitted by applicable Law, Assignor irrevocably waives, and shall procure that each of its employees and contractors who authored any work included in the Assigned IP waives, all moral rights and rights of attribution and integrity in that work.

## 3. Recordation

**3.1 Authorization.** Assignor authorizes the Commissioner for Patents and the Commissioner for Trademarks of the United States Patent and Trademark Office, the Register of Copyrights, each domain name registrar and the officials of each other Recordation Authority to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned IP that is registered, issued or subject to a pending application.

**3.2 Short-Form Assignments.** At the Closing, and at any time afterward on Assignee's reasonable request, Assignor shall execute and deliver short-form patent, trademark, copyright and domain name assignments, substantially in the forms attached as Schedule B, for recordation with the applicable Recordation Authority. Each short-form assignment is delivered to give effect to this IP Assignment Agreement and does not expand or limit the Assigned IP.

**3.3 Domain Names.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor shall take all actions required by the applicable registrar to transfer the registration of each domain name included in the Assigned IP to an account designated by Assignee.

**3.4 Costs.** Assignee shall bear all filing and recordation fees payable to any Recordation Authority in connection with recording the assignment of the Assigned IP.

## 4. Further Assurances

**4.1 Cooperation.** Following the Effective Date, Assignor shall, at Assignee's request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such additional documents and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to vest, perfect, confirm, record or enforce Assignee's right, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned IP. This includes:

(a) executing declarations, oaths and other papers required for the prosecution, maintenance or defense of any patent application or patent included in the Assigned IP; and

(b) providing testimony and documents reasonably required in connection with any Action relating to the Assigned IP.

Assignee shall reimburse Assignor for its reasonable, documented out-of-pocket costs incurred at Assignee's request under this Section 4.1.

**4.2 Power of Attorney.** Assignor hereby irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to execute and file any document described in Section 3.2 or Section 4.1 that Assignor fails to execute within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest.

**4.3 Delivery of Materials.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP in Assignor's possession or control. Assignor shall not retain any copies, except for one archival copy held by its legal counsel solely for the purpose of evidencing compliance with this IP Assignment Agreement.

## 5. Purchase Agreement

**5.1 Relationship.** This IP Assignment Agreement is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. It is one of the Ancillary Agreements.

**5.2 Representations and Indemnities.** The representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities of Assignor and Assignee with respect to the Purchased Assets are set forth in the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this IP Assignment Agreement creates any additional representation or warranty.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Notices.** Notices under this IP Assignment Agreement shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.2 Successors and Assigns.** This IP Assignment Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Assignee may assign its rights under this IP Assignment Agreement to any Person to whom it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement, and to any subsequent owner of all or any part of the Assigned IP.

**6.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This IP Assignment Agreement may be amended, modified or waived only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee.

**6.4 Severability.** If any provision of this IP Assignment Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that holding does not affect any other provision of this IP Assignment Agreement or the enforceability of that provision in any other jurisdiction.

**6.5 Governing Law.** This IP Assignment Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule, except to the extent that the Laws of another jurisdiction mandatorily govern the transfer of any registered Intellectual Property.

**6.6 Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** Sections 11.7 (Submission to Jurisdiction) and 11.8 (Waiver of Jury Trial) of the Purchase Agreement apply to this IP Assignment Agreement.

**6.7 Counterparts.** This IP Assignment Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and delivery. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
