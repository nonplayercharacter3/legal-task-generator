## 2. Supplement to Schedule 3.13 (Legal Proceedings; Governmental Orders)

Schedule 3.13 is supplemented by adding the following as new item 2:

> 2. **FAA Letter of Investigation.** On September 11, 2026, Seller received a Letter of Investigation dated September 9, 2026 from the Federal Aviation Administration, Flight Standards District Office, Des Moines, Iowa (FAA Enforcement Investigative Report No. 2026CE590118).
>
> (a) **Scope.** The Letter of Investigation concerns aerial-application operations conducted by Seller with AgriSpray AS-40 and AS-60 aircraft under Seller's Agricultural Aircraft Operator Certificate No. TFR-137-0442 and 14 C.F.R. Part 137 during the period from May 2025 through August 2026.
>
> (b) **Alleged matters.** The FAA states that it is investigating whether Seller:
>
> - conducted dispensing operations over or near congested areas and non-participating property without the approvals required by 14 C.F.R. §§ 137.39 and 137.51;
> - failed to maintain the records required by 14 C.F.R. § 137.71 for certain operations; and
> - operated aircraft under its exemption with pilots-in-command who did not hold the required qualifications for the operations conducted.
>
> (c) **Potential consequences.** The Letter of Investigation states that the matters under investigation may result in legal enforcement action, including civil penalties, and may result in certificate action, including suspension or revocation of Certificate No. TFR-137-0442.
>
> (d) **Response.** The Letter of Investigation invited Seller to submit a written response within ten (10) days of receipt. Seller submitted a preliminary written response on September 18, 2026 and has requested an informal conference with the FAA. No civil penalty has been proposed and no certificate action has been initiated as of the date of delivery of this Schedule Supplement No. 1.
