## 1. Promise to Pay

1.1 For value received, **Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal amount of **$7,200,000** (SEVEN MILLION TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS). Maker shall also pay interest on the unpaid principal amount. Principal and interest are payable at the times and in the manner set forth in this Note.

1.2 This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 between Maker, as Buyer, and Holder, as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Maker issues this Note under Section 2.7(b)(ii) of the Purchase Agreement as payment of part of the Purchase Price.

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

In this Note, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1.

"**Maturity Date**" means the fourth (4th) anniversary of the Date of Issue.

"**Payment Date**" means each March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31, beginning with the first such date to occur after the Date of Issue.

"**Senior Credit Agreement**" means Maker's credit agreement with its senior lenders, as in effect on the Date of Issue. It includes that agreement as amended, restated, supplemented, refinanced or replaced from time to time.

"**Senior Debt**" means all obligations of Maker and its subsidiaries under the Senior Credit Agreement and the related loan documents. These obligations include principal, interest (including interest accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses, reimbursement obligations and indemnities.

"**Senior Lender**" means the administrative agent and the lenders under the Senior Credit Agreement.

## 3. Interest

3.1 **Rate.** The unpaid principal amount of this Note bears interest at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum from and including the Date of Issue until this Note is paid in full.

3.2 **Calculation.** Interest is calculated on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

3.3 **Payment.** Interest accrued is payable in arrears on each Payment Date and on the Maturity Date.

3.4 **Default Rate.** While an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) is continuing, the unpaid principal amount and any overdue interest bear interest at a rate equal to the rate in Section 3.1 plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

3.5 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal.

## 4. Repayment of Principal

4.1 **Amortization.** Maker shall repay principal in the following installments, each due on the applicable anniversary of the Date of Issue:

| Anniversary of Date of Issue | Principal installment |
|---|---|
| First (1st) | $1,200,000 |
| Second (2nd) | $1,800,000 |
| Third (3rd) | $1,800,000 |
| Fourth (4th) (the Maturity Date) | $2,400,000 |
| **Total** | **$7,200,000** |

4.2 **Maturity.** All unpaid principal, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

4.3 **Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without premium or penalty, subject to Section 6. Each prepayment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

4.4 **Manner of Payment.** All payments shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Holder. If a payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue for the additional days.

## 5. Right of Set-Off

5.1 **Set-Off.** Under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement, Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable under this Note any amount to which any Buyer Indemnitee is finally determined to be entitled under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.2 **Final Determination.** An amount is "finally determined" for purposes of Section 5.1 when it is:

(a) agreed in writing by Maker and Holder;

(b) not disputed by Holder in a written notice delivered within thirty (30) days after Holder receives Maker's claim notice under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement; or

(c) awarded in a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 11.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.3 **Pending Claims.** If a claim for indemnification under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement is pending but not finally determined when a payment falls due under this Note, Maker may withhold from that payment an amount equal to Maker's good-faith estimate of the claim, as stated in the claim notice. Maker shall pay the amount withheld, to the extent not applied in set-off, within five (5) Business Days after the claim is finally determined, together with interest at the rate in Section 3.1 on any amount so paid. Withholding under this Section 5.3 is not an Event of Default.

5.4 **Application.** Each set-off shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal installments in inverse order of maturity. Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off, stating the amount and the claim to which it relates.

## 6. Subordination

6.1 **Agreement to Subordinate.** Maker and Holder, by its acceptance of this Note, agree that the payment of principal and interest under this Note is subordinate in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Debt, to the extent set forth in this Section 6.

6.2 **Permitted Payments.** So long as no payment default or event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing, or would result from the payment, Maker may make, and Holder may receive and retain, the scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

6.3 **Payment Blockage.** If a payment default or event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing, and Holder has received written notice of it from the Senior Lender or Maker, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not receive, any payment under this Note until the earlier of:

(a) the date on which that default has been cured or waived; and

(b) one hundred eighty (180) days after Holder receives the notice.

Not more than one blockage notice may be given in any period of three hundred sixty (360) consecutive days. Any payment blocked under this Section 6.3 remains due and payable, and accrues interest at the rate in Section 3.1, and shall be paid on the first date on which payment is permitted.

6.4 **Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding relating to Maker, the Senior Debt shall first be paid in full in cash before Holder receives any payment under this Note.

6.5 **Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment in violation of this Section 6, Holder shall hold it in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly deliver it to the Senior Lender.

6.6 **Standstill.** While any Senior Debt is outstanding, Holder shall not accelerate this Note or commence any enforcement action against Maker under this Note without the Senior Lender's prior written consent, except:

(a) during the continuance of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c); or

(b) after the Senior Debt has been accelerated.

6.7 **Subordination Agreement.** At Maker's request, Holder shall enter into a subordination agreement with the Senior Lender on terms consistent with this Section 6 and otherwise customary for seller notes of this type.

6.8 **Obligation Absolute.** Nothing in this Section 6 impairs Maker's obligation, which is absolute and unconditional as between Maker and Holder, to pay this Note in accordance with its terms, subject to Section 5.

## 7. Events of Default

7.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, other than an amount withheld or set off under Section 5 or a payment blocked under Section 6.3;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker receives written notice of it from Holder; or

(c) Maker:

- commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law;
- has an involuntary case commenced against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or
- makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

7.2 **Remedies.** Subject to Section 6, while an Event of Default is continuing, Holder may declare all unpaid principal and accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker. Upon an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c), all amounts under this Note become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action.

## 8. Covenants of Maker

So long as any amount under this Note remains unpaid, Maker shall:

(a) give Holder written notice of any Event of Default within five (5) Business Days after an officer of Maker becomes aware of it; and

(b) deliver to Holder, within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, Maker's annual consolidated financial statements for that year.

## 9. Waivers

9.1 Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by this Note.

9.2 No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of it.

## 10. Transfer

10.1 Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without Maker's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate of Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note, including Section 5 and Section 6.

10.2 Any transferee takes this Note subject to Maker's rights of set-off and withholding under Section 5, which are enforceable against any transferee to the same extent as against Holder.

10.3 Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note except to a Person to whom Maker assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement. No such assignment relieves Maker of its obligations under this Note.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Note shall be given in accordance with Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

11.2 **Amendment.** This Note may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder.

11.3 **Lost Note.** If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms. Before doing so, Maker is entitled to receive an affidavit of loss and an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to it, without bond.

11.4 **Costs of Collection.** Subject to Section 6, Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in collecting amounts due under this Note after an Event of Default.

11.5 **Governing Law.** This Note is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

11.6 **Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver.** Sections 11.7 (Submission to Jurisdiction) and 11.8 (Waiver of Jury Trial) of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Note.

11.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

11.8 **Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Note.
