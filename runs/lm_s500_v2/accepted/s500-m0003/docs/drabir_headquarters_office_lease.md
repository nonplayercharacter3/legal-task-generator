# OFFICE LEASE

**Landlord:** Westgate Prairie Office Partners, LLC

**Tenant:** Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc.

**Building:** Westgate Prairie Center, 4100 Westown Parkway, West Des Moines, Iowa 50266

**Lease Date:** March 2, 2025

---

## 2. Premises and Term

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term, on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the Common Areas of the Building, including the lobbies, corridors, restrooms, elevators, loading dock, fitness center and parking structure, subject to the Rules and Regulations in **Exhibit C**.

2.2 **Measurement.** The Rentable Area of the Premises and of the Building has been measured under the BOMA Office Standard (ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017) and is stipulated for all purposes of this Lease. No remeasurement adjusts Base Rent or Tenant's Share.

2.3 **Commencement Date Memorandum.** Within thirty (30) days after the Commencement Date, the parties shall execute a memorandum in the form of **Exhibit D** confirming the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date.

2.4 **Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises beginning thirty (30) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, fixtures, cabling and equipment. All provisions of this Lease except the obligation to pay Rent apply during early access.

## 3. Rent

3.1 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in advance on the first day of each calendar month during the Term, without demand, deduction or set-off except as expressly provided in this Lease. Base Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

3.2 **Additional Rent.** All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

3.3 **Late Charge.** If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to four percent (4%) of the overdue amount. Landlord waives the late charge for the first late payment in any twelve (12) month period.

3.4 **Interest.** Any Rent not paid within thirty (30) days after its due date bears interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum or, if lower, the maximum rate permitted by Law, until paid.

3.5 **Manner of Payment.** Rent is payable by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated in writing by Landlord, or at such other place as Landlord may designate by notice under Section 21.

## 4. Operating Expenses and Taxes

4.1 **Definitions.**

(a) "**Operating Expenses**" means all reasonable costs incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, repairing, insuring and managing the Building and the land on which it is located, determined under generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied, including Taxes. Operating Expenses exclude: capital expenditures (except amortized costs of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by Laws first effective after the Lease Date); principal and interest on debt; ground rent; leasing commissions, advertising and tenant improvement costs; costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants; management fees in excess of three percent (3%) of gross Building revenues; costs of remediating Hazardous Materials not introduced by Tenant; Landlord's general corporate overhead; and charitable or political contributions.

(b) "**Taxes**" means all real property taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land, excluding income, franchise, estate and inheritance taxes and penalties for late payment.

4.2 **Tenant's Share of Excess.** Beginning on January 1, 2026, Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year. Operating Expenses for the Base Year and each later year shall be grossed up to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

4.3 **Controllable Expense Cap.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses (being all Operating Expenses other than Taxes, insurance premiums, utilities and snow removal) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative, compounding basis over the Base Year.

4.4 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall deliver a reasonable estimate of Tenant's Share of the excess for each calendar year, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate monthly with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next Rent due (or refunded within thirty (30) days if the Term has ended), and any underpayment shall be paid within thirty (30) days after delivery of the statement.

4.5 **Audit.** Tenant may, on thirty (30) days' notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of an annual statement, audit Landlord's books and records for that year at Landlord's management office. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Operating Expenses by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs.

## 5. Use

5.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and in compliance with all Laws and the Rules and Regulations.

5.2 **Restrictions.** Tenant shall not (a) use the Premises in any manner that creates a nuisance or unreasonably interferes with other occupants of the Building; (b) store, test or operate any unmanned aircraft, batteries in excess of ordinary office electronics, fuels or pesticides in the Premises or the Common Areas; or (c) bring any Hazardous Materials onto the Premises other than customary office and cleaning supplies in compliance with Law.

5.3 **Hours and Access.** Tenant shall have access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, subject to the Building's access-control system and emergencies. Building standard HVAC is furnished from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding Building holidays.

## 6. Services and Utilities

6.1 **Landlord Services.** Landlord shall furnish, as part of Operating Expenses: HVAC during the hours in Section 5.3; electricity for lighting and ordinary office equipment; domestic water; passenger elevator service at all times; janitorial service five (5) days a week to Building standard; and window washing at least twice a year.

6.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall furnish HVAC outside the hours in Section 5.3 on Tenant's request at Landlord's actual cost, currently $55.00 per hour per floor.

6.3 **Supplemental Equipment.** Tenant may install supplemental HVAC for its server room, subject to Section 8. Electricity for supplemental equipment shall be separately metered and paid by Tenant.

6.4 **Interruption.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If an interruption caused by Landlord's negligence or by a failure within Landlord's control renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than five (5) consecutive Business Days, Rent shall abate in proportion to the untenantable area from the sixth (6th) Business Day until service is restored. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the State of Iowa.

## 7. Tenant Improvements

7.1 **Work Letter.** Landlord shall construct the initial improvements to the Premises in accordance with the Work Letter attached as **Exhibit B**.

7.2 **Allowance.** Landlord shall provide a tenant improvement allowance of $55.00 per rentable square foot of the Premises ($1,181,400) for the hard and soft costs of the initial improvements, on the terms of the Work Letter. Any unused portion of the allowance remaining on December 31, 2025 is forfeited.

## 8. Alterations

8.1 **Consent.** Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions or improvements to the Premises ("**Alterations**") without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Consent is not required for cosmetic Alterations (such as painting and carpeting) costing less than $40,000 in any single project that do not affect the Building structure or systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord ten (10) days' prior notice.

8.2 **Performance.** All Alterations shall be performed in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with Law, by contractors reasonably approved by Landlord. Tenant shall pay Landlord a supervisory fee of three percent (3%) of the hard costs of any Alteration requiring Landlord's consent.

8.3 **Removal.** At the time Landlord consents to an Alteration, Landlord shall notify Tenant whether the Alteration must be removed at the end of the Term. Tenant need not remove the initial improvements constructed under the Work Letter or any Alteration that Landlord does not so designate.

8.4 **Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Premises and the Building free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant and shall cause any such lien to be released or bonded within twenty (20) days after Tenant receives notice of it.

## 9. Repairs and Maintenance

9.1 **Landlord.** Landlord shall maintain in good condition and repair the structural elements of the Building, the roof, the Common Areas and the Building's mechanical, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and life-safety systems, except damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, employees or invitees.

9.2 **Tenant.** Tenant shall keep the interior, non-structural portions of the Premises in good condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall maintain any supplemental equipment installed by Tenant.

## 10. Assignment and Subletting

10.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to a request for consent within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the information reasonably necessary to evaluate it.

10.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) days' prior notice, assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises to (a) any entity controlling, controlled by or under common control with Tenant; (b) any successor by merger or consolidation; or (c) any purchaser of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided in each case that the transferee's tangible net worth immediately after the transfer is not less than Tenant's tangible net worth on the Lease Date. A change in the ownership of Tenant's shares is not an assignment.

10.3 **Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received under a sublease or assignment (other than a transfer under Section 10.2) in excess of the Rent payable for the transferred space, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transfer.

10.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.

## 11. Insurance

11.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its property manager as additional insureds; (b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and Alterations on a special form basis for full replacement cost; (c) business interruption insurance; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance before entering the Premises and on each renewal.

11.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain special form property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance with limits customary for comparable buildings in the West Des Moines market.

11.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Landlord and Tenant each waive all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent the loss is covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance. Each party shall cause its property insurance policies to permit this waiver.

## 12. Indemnification

12.1 **By Tenant.** Subject to Section 11.3, Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord and its members, managers, agents and employees against all claims, losses and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, employees or contractors anywhere in the Building, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents, employees or contractors.

12.2 **By Landlord.** Subject to Section 11.3, Landlord shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Tenant and its directors, officers, agents and employees against all claims, losses and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents, employees or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, employees or contractors.

12.3 **Survival.** This Section 12 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease.

## 13. Damage and Destruction

13.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, notify Tenant of its reasonable estimate of the time required to restore. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 13.2, Landlord shall restore the Building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's personal property and Alterations) with reasonable diligence.

13.2 **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Lease by notice given within thirty (30) days after delivery of Landlord's estimate if the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days from the date of the casualty, or if the casualty occurs during the last twelve (12) months of the Term and restoration is estimated to take more than sixty (60) days.

13.3 **Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises rendered untenantable, from the date of the casualty until Landlord substantially completes its restoration.

## 14. Condemnation

If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or of the parking or access to the Building that Tenant cannot reasonably conduct its business, is taken by eminent domain, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of the taking. If this Lease is not terminated, Rent shall be reduced in proportion to the area taken. Landlord is entitled to the entire award, except that Tenant may claim separately for relocation expenses and the unamortized cost of Alterations paid for by Tenant.

## 15. Security Deposit

Tenant has delivered the Security Deposit to Landlord. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any Event of Default, and Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit within thirty (30) days after the expiration of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises. Provided no Event of Default has then occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $43,750 on the first day of Lease Month 37, and Landlord shall refund the reduction to Tenant within thirty (30) days.

## 16. Default and Remedies

16.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) days after Landlord's written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after Landlord's written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within that period and diligently pursue it to completion; or

(c) Tenant files a voluntary petition in bankruptcy, has an involuntary petition filed against it that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

16.2 **Remedies.** While an Event of Default is continuing, Landlord may, in addition to its other rights at law or in equity: (a) terminate this Lease by notice to Tenant and recover the damages permitted by Law; or (b) without terminating this Lease, re-enter the Premises by lawful process and relet them for Tenant's account, recovering from Tenant the Rent as it falls due, less net proceeds of reletting.

16.3 **Mitigation.** Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages following an Event of Default.

16.4 **Landlord Default.** Landlord is in default if it fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice (or such longer period as is reasonably required, if Landlord begins the cure within that period and diligently pursues it).

## 17. Subordination and Estoppel

17.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided that the holder of the mortgage enters into a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement with Tenant agreeing not to disturb Tenant's possession so long as no Event of Default is continuing. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from the current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

17.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the other's request, deliver a certificate stating whether this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 18. Option to Extend

18.1 **Grant.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years (the "**Extension Term**") on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the Extension Term will be ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent.

18.2 **Exercise.** Tenant shall exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given not earlier than fifteen (15) months and not later than nine (9) months before the Expiration Date, provided no Event of Default is then continuing.

18.3 **Fair Market Rent.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rent per rentable square foot at which comparable space in comparable Class A office buildings in the West Des Moines, Iowa market is being leased for a term comparable to the Extension Term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's exercise notice, it shall be determined by baseball arbitration before a single MAI-designated appraiser with at least ten (10) years' experience in the local office market, jointly selected by the parties, whose fees the parties shall share equally.

## 19. Surrender and Holding Over

19.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and Alterations that need not be removed under Section 8.3 excepted, and shall remove its personal property and cabling installed by Tenant.

19.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance and shall pay Base Rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the rate in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days and two hundred percent (200%) after that, prorated daily. Tenant is liable for consequential damages arising from a holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord's notice that a succeeding tenant has leased the Premises.

## 20. Landlord Access

Landlord may enter the Premises on not less than twenty-four (24) hours' notice (except in an emergency) to inspect them, make repairs, and show them to prospective purchasers, lenders and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security procedures.

All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses below (or another address designated by notice). A notice is effective on delivery or refusal of delivery. A copy of any notice may also be sent by e-mail, but e-mail alone is not effective notice.

Each party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through it.

## 23. Miscellaneous

23.1 **Governing Law.** This Lease is governed by the Laws of the State of Iowa. "**Law**" or "**Laws**" means all applicable statutes, ordinances, regulations, codes and orders of any governmental authority.

23.2 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default is continuing, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without hindrance by Landlord or any person claiming through Landlord.

23.3 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Tenant shall look solely to Landlord's interest in the Building (including rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds) for the satisfaction of any judgment against Landlord. No member or manager of Landlord has any personal liability under this Lease.

23.4 **Force Majeure.** Except for the payment of money, neither party is liable for delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, labor disputes, governmental action, epidemics and shortages of materials, and the time for performance is extended for the period of the delay.

23.5 **Signage.** Tenant may, at its cost and subject to Landlord's reasonable approval and applicable Law, install its name on the Building lobby directory, at the sixth-floor elevator lobby and on one (1) panel of the Building monument sign.

23.6 **Amendment.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant.

23.7 **Entire Agreement.** This Lease, including its Exhibits, is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant regarding the leasing of the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations and letters of intent between them regarding the Premises.

23.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

23.9 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

23.10 **Exhibits.** The following Exhibits are part of this Lease: **Exhibit A** (Floor Plan of Premises); **Exhibit B** (Work Letter); **Exhibit C** (Rules and Regulations); **Exhibit D** (Form of Commencement Date Memorandum).
