# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of May 11, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Drabir**"), and **Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Troudaind**"). Drabir and Troudaind are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Troudaind operates a precision-agriculture drone business. That business includes its AgriSpray spray-drone product line, its FieldPath flight-planning software and its CanopyLens agronomic analytics platform.

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible negotiated transaction in which Drabir would acquire all or part of that business (the "**Proposed Transaction**").

C. In the course of that evaluation, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain confidential information. The Parties are entering into this Agreement to protect that information.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning a Party or its Affiliates, or their businesses, that the Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") disclose to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Proposed Transaction. It does not matter whether the information is disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by inspection, or whether it is marked as confidential. Confidential Information includes:

(a) financial statements, forecasts, budgets, pricing and cost information;

(b) customer, grower, dealer and supplier lists, and the terms of contracts with those Persons;

(c) software, source code, firmware, algorithms, analytics models, training data, product designs, specifications and manufacturing processes;

(d) field, flight and agronomic data;

(e) regulatory filings, correspondence with Governmental Authorities, and permit and certificate information;

(f) employee and compensation information; and

(g) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts, studies and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain or reflect any of the foregoing ("**Derivative Materials**").

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was in the Receiving Party's possession on a non-confidential basis before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party, if that source is not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party with respect to it; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

1.3 "**Transaction Information**" means the fact that the Parties are evaluating or negotiating the Proposed Transaction, the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, and the terms, conditions, status or other facts of any discussions between the Parties.

1.4 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, managers, members, officers, employees, agents, lenders and potential financing sources, and professional advisors (including attorneys, accountants, consultants and financial advisors).

1.5 "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

1.6 "**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, governmental authority, trust, association or other entity.

1.7 "**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, agency, regulatory body, court or tribunal.

## 2. Use and Non-Disclosure

2.1 **Permitted Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and, if applicable, consummate the Proposed Transaction (the "**Purpose**"), and for no other purpose.

2.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and the Transaction Information strictly confidential. It shall not disclose any of it to any Person except to those of its Representatives who:

(a) need to know it for the Purpose;

(b) have been informed of its confidential nature; and

(c) are bound by duties or obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

2.3 **Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives, as if the Representatives were parties to this Agreement.

2.4 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.5 **Source Code and Models.** Neither Party is required to disclose source code, model weights or training datasets. If either Party elects to make any of them available, it may do so only in a clean-room or supervised-review environment on terms the Parties agree in writing. The Receiving Party shall not copy, download, reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any of them.

## 3. Compelled Disclosure

3.1 If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by Law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information or Transaction Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy at its own expense; and

(b) reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking that remedy.

3.2 If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed. It shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to the information disclosed.

3.3 Notice under Section 3.1 is not required for disclosure in the course of a routine examination by a regulatory or supervisory authority that is not targeted at the Disclosing Party or the Proposed Transaction.

## 4. Non-Solicitation; Contacts

4.1 **Employees.** For a period of twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any officer or employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Proposed Transaction. This Section 4.1 does not restrict general solicitations of employment (such as advertisements or search-firm engagements) not specifically directed at those individuals, or the hiring of any individual who responds to them.

4.2 **Business Contacts.** Without Troudaind's prior written consent, Drabir shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, initiate contact regarding the Proposed Transaction or Troudaind's Confidential Information with any employee (other than those designated by Troudaind), customer, grower, dealer, supplier, lessor or regulator of Troudaind's business. This Section 4.2 does not restrict contacts in the ordinary course of Drabir's business unrelated to the Proposed Transaction.

## 5. Return and Destruction

5.1 At any time on the Disclosing Party's written request, and in any event promptly after either Party notifies the other that it has decided not to proceed with the Proposed Transaction, the Receiving Party shall, within ten (10) business days, return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession, and destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing.

5.2 The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies of Confidential Information:

(a) to the extent required by Law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention or compliance policies; and

(b) that are stored in automatic electronic archives or back-up systems and are not readily accessible.

Any retained copies remain subject to this Agreement for so long as they are retained, notwithstanding the expiration of the term in Section 8.1.

## 6. No Representations; No Obligation

6.1 **No Representations.** Neither Party, nor any of its Representatives, makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information. Neither Party nor its Representatives will have any liability to the other Party arising from the use of Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a Definitive Agreement.

6.2 **No Obligation.** Neither Party is under any legal obligation with respect to the Proposed Transaction unless and until a definitive written agreement providing for the Proposed Transaction has been executed and delivered by both Parties (a "**Definitive Agreement**"). Either Party may terminate discussions regarding the Proposed Transaction at any time, for any reason or no reason, on written notice to the other Party.

6.3 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in or to any Confidential Information or any Intellectual Property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 7. Remedies

7.1 **Equitable Relief.** Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. Accordingly, the non-breaching Party is entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive or other equitable relief to prevent or restrain a breach, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond. This remedy is in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

7.2 **Costs.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines in a final, non-appealable order that a Party or its Representatives breached this Agreement, that Party shall reimburse the other Party for its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in connection with the litigation.

## 8. Term

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years after the Effective Date.

8.2 **Trade Secrets.** With respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable Law, the Receiving Party's obligations under Section 2 continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

8.3 **Definitive Agreement.** If the Parties enter into a Definitive Agreement, the confidentiality provisions of that Definitive Agreement govern the Parties' post-closing obligations with respect to the matters it addresses, as provided in it.

## 9. Miscellaneous

9.1 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by e-mail of a PDF document with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or another address designated by notice).

9.2 **Securities Laws.** Each Party acknowledges that it is aware, and will advise its Representatives, that applicable securities laws restrict persons with material non-public information about a company from purchasing or selling its securities on the basis of that information.

9.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets that agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement.

9.4 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it.

9.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by Law.

9.6 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

9.7 **Jurisdiction.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware (or, if that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware) in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

9.8 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

9.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and delivery. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
