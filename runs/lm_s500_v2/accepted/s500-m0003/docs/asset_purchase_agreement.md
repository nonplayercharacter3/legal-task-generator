# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 14, 2026 by and between **Drabir AgriAerial Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Troudaind Field Robotics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the Business, including through its AgriSpray spray-drone product line, its FieldPath flight-planning software and its CanopyLens agronomic analytics platform.

B. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets, and certain specified liabilities, of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, terms and conditions in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

(b) all inventory, finished goods, raw materials, airframes, spray systems, batteries, payload modules, spare parts and work in process of the Business;

(c) all contracts set forth in Section 3.9(a) of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(g) all customer lists, grower data, field and flight logs, books and records, technical documentation and marketing materials of the Business; and

(h) all goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** Notwithstanding Section 2.1, the Purchased Assets shall not include (a) cash and cash equivalents of Seller; (b) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and tax returns; (c) any contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (d) insurance policies of Seller and all rights thereunder; (e) all rights to tax refunds relating to pre-Closing periods; and (f) the rights of Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume and shall not be responsible to pay, perform or discharge any liabilities or obligations of Seller or any of its Affiliates of any kind or nature whatsoever other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). Seller shall, and shall cause each of its Affiliates to, pay and satisfy in due course all Excluded Liabilities. Without limiting the foregoing, the Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) any liabilities arising out of or relating to Seller's ownership or operation of the Business and the Purchased Assets prior to the Closing Date, including any field trials, demonstrations, pilot programs or aerial-application operations conducted by or on behalf of Seller prior to the Closing;

(b) any product liability, warranty or similar claim for injury to a Person or property (including crop or livestock damage and pesticide drift) arising from products manufactured, sold or operated by Seller prior to the Closing;

(c) any liabilities for taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any pre-Closing tax period;

(d) any liabilities arising from any Action pending or threatened as of the Closing Date or arising out of facts existing prior to the Closing;

(e) any liabilities of Seller relating to employees, including wages, bonuses, benefits and severance accrued prior to the Closing; and

(f) any liabilities arising out of any violation of Law by Seller, including any Law relating to aviation, pesticide application or the environment.

**2.5 Bulk Sales.** The parties waive compliance with the provisions of any bulk sales, bulk transfer or similar Laws of any jurisdiction that may otherwise be applicable to the sale of the Purchased Assets.

**2.6 Non-Assignable Assets.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, this Agreement shall not constitute a sale, assignment or transfer of any Assigned Contract or other Purchased Asset if an attempted sale, assignment or transfer thereof, without the consent of a third party, would constitute a breach thereof or a violation of any Law. **2.7 Purchase Price.**

(a) The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets shall be $41,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

(b) Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows: (i) $34,300,000 by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller no later than two (2) Business Days prior to the Closing Date; and (ii) $7,200,000 by the issuance and delivery to Seller of the Seller Note.

**2.8 Allocation of Purchase Price.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets for all tax purposes in accordance with an allocation schedule prepared by Buyer within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, consistent with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.
