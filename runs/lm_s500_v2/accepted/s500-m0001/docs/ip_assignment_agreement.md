# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

**THIS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Droureil Coachways LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Assignor**" and, in its capacity as licensee under Article 4, "**Licensee**"), and **Brouviv Student Transit, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Assignee**"). Assignor and Assignee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Assignor and Assignee are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor has agreed to sell to Assignee substantially all of the assets used in Assignor's pupil transportation business (the "**School Bus Business**"), including the Transferred IP.

B. Assignor will continue to operate its charter motorcoach business (the "**Retained Charter Business**") after the Closing. Assignor uses the RouteLattice software in that business for scheduling and dispatch.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires the Parties to execute and deliver this Agreement at the Closing. Assignee is willing to grant Licensee a license to continue using the Transferred IP in the Retained Charter Business, on the terms of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. In this Agreement:

"**Copyrights**" means all copyrights in the Software and the Documentation, whether registered or unregistered, including all registrations and applications for registration, and all moral rights to the extent waivable.

"**Documentation**" means all user manuals, technical specifications, architecture diagrams, data dictionaries, API references, build and deployment instructions and other materials describing the design, operation or use of the Software.

"**Improvements**" means any modification, enhancement, derivative work, update, upgrade or new version of the Software.

"**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all rights of any kind in the following, anywhere in the world: (a) copyrights and works of authorship; (b) trade secrets, know-how and confidential information; (c) trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and the goodwill associated with them; (d) patents and patent applications, and inventions whether or not patentable; (e) domain names; and (f) all other intellectual property and proprietary rights.

"**Marks**" means the trademark ROUTELATTICE, all associated logos and trade dress, and the domain name routelattice.com, together with the goodwill of the business associated with them.

"**Retained License**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Route Data**" means all route maps, stop locations, bell-time tables, rider assignments, vehicle-to-route assignments, historical GPS and telematics data and other data compiled in or generated by the Software in connection with the School Bus Business, excluding personal information of pupils to the extent its transfer is prohibited by applicable law.

"**Software**" means the RouteLattice routing and dispatch software, in source code and object code form, in all versions in existence on the Closing Date, including all modules, libraries, scripts, configuration files, databases, database schemas, interfaces and mobile driver applications, and all development tools and environments owned by Assignor and used to build, compile or maintain it.

"**Transferred IP**" means (a) the Software; (b) the Documentation; (c) the Route Data; (d) the Copyrights; (e) the Marks; (f) all other Intellectual Property Rights owned by Assignor in or to any of the foregoing; and (g) all rights to sue for and recover damages for past, present and future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the foregoing.

**1.2 Interpretation.** "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles and Sections are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT

**2.1 Assignment.** Effective as of the Closing, Assignor irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest in and to the Transferred IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances, together with all income, royalties and payments due or payable on or after the Closing Date with respect to the Transferred IP.

**2.2 Goodwill.** The assignment of the Marks in Section 2.1 includes the goodwill of the business connected with the use of and symbolized by the Marks.

**2.3 Delivery.** At the Closing, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee (a) a complete and current copy of the source code of the Software, in a form that a reasonably skilled programmer can compile and maintain without reference to any other source; (b) all Documentation; (c) a complete export of the Route Data in a commercially standard, non-proprietary format; and (d) all administrator credentials, encryption keys, code repository access and hosting account credentials for the Software and the routelattice.com domain.

**2.4 Retained Copies.** Except for the copy of the Software that Licensee may retain under the Retained License, Assignor shall not keep any copy of the source code of the Software or of the Route Data after the Closing, and shall certify in writing within thirty days after the Closing Date that it has destroyed all other copies.

## 3. FURTHER ASSURANCES AND RECORDATION

**3.1 Further Assurances.** At Assignee's request and without further consideration, Assignor shall promptly execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as Assignee reasonably requests to perfect, record or enforce Assignee's rights in the Transferred IP, including assignments in recordable form for filing with the United States Copyright Office, the United States Patent and Trademark Office and any domain name registrar.

**3.2 Recordation.** Assignee may record this Agreement, or a short-form assignment consistent with it, with any governmental authority or registrar. Assignor authorizes the Commissioner for Trademarks, the Register of Copyrights and any other official to record Assignee as owner of the Transferred IP.

**3.3 Power of Attorney.** If Assignor fails to execute any instrument required by Section 3.1 within ten Business Days after Assignee's written request, Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee as its attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, solely to execute and file that instrument in Assignor's name.

**3.4 Cooperation in Proceedings.** Assignor shall reasonably cooperate, at Assignee's expense for out-of-pocket costs, in any proceeding relating to the Transferred IP, including by making available employees and former contractors with knowledge of the development of the Software.

## 4. LICENSE-BACK TO LICENSEE

**4.1 Grant.** Effective as of the Closing, Assignee grants to Licensee a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, fully paid-up, worldwide license under the Transferred IP (other than the Marks and the Route Data) to use, reproduce, install, configure and modify the Software and the Documentation for the scheduling, routing and dispatch of vehicles in the operation of the Retained Charter Business (the "**Retained License**").

**4.2 Sublicensing.** Licensee may sublicense the Retained License to its contractors and service providers solely to the extent necessary for them to provide services to Licensee in the Retained Charter Business, provided that each sublicensee is bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as Article 5.

**4.3 Improvements.** Licensee owns any Improvement it makes under the Retained License, subject to Assignee's underlying rights in the Transferred IP. Assignee has no obligation to provide Licensee with any Improvement, update, maintenance or support.

**4.4 Reservation of Rights.** All rights in the Transferred IP not expressly granted to Licensee in this Article 4 are reserved to Assignee. Licensee shall not use the Marks or any confusingly similar mark.

**4.5 Copy of Software.** Licensee may retain one copy of the object code and source code of the Software as it exists on the Closing Date, solely for use under the Retained License.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

**5.1 Obligation.** Licensee shall hold the source code of the Software, the Documentation and all other non-public information included in the Transferred IP in confidence, using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care. Licensee shall not disclose that information to any Person except its employees, contractors and permitted sublicensees who need to know it for the purposes of the Retained License.

**5.2 Exceptions.** Section 5.1 does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through Licensee's breach; or (b) Licensee is required to disclose by law or court order, provided that Licensee gives Assignee prompt notice and reasonable cooperation in seeking a protective order.

**5.3 Remedies.** Licensee acknowledges that a breach of this Article 5 may cause Assignee irreparable harm for which damages would be an inadequate remedy, and that Assignee is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

**6.2 Purchase Agreement.** This Agreement is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement limits or expands the representations, warranties, covenants or indemnities of the Parties in the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. ASSIGNMENT OF THIS AGREEMENT

**7.1 By Assignee.** Assignee may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Affiliate, to any lender as collateral security, or to any acquirer of all or any portion of the Transferred IP. Any assignment of the Transferred IP by Assignee is subject to the Retained License.

**7.2 By Licensee.** Licensee may assign or otherwise transfer this Agreement and the Retained License, in whole or in part, without the consent of Assignee, to any Affiliate or to any successor to all or any part of its business, whether by merger, consolidation, sale of assets or equity, operation of law or otherwise. Licensee shall give Assignee written notice of any such assignment within thirty days after it takes effect. The assignee shall be bound by Articles 4 and 5 to the same extent as Licensee.

**7.3 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
