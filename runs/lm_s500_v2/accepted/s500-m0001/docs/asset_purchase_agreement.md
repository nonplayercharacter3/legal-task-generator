# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 14, 2026, by and between **Brouviv Student Transit, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Droureil Coachways LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a pupil transportation business serving public school districts in the Hudson Valley region of New York (the "**School Bus Business**"). Seller also operates a charter motorcoach business (the "**Retained Charter Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to buy from Seller, substantially all of the assets used in the School Bus Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Person in question.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the IP Assignment Agreement, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and each other certificate or instrument delivered under this Agreement at the Closing.

"**Assigned Contracts**" means the Contracts listed on Schedule 4.7(a), including the Pupil Transportation Contracts.

"**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale for the Purchased Assets, in the form attached as Exhibit A.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Contract**" means any contract, agreement, lease, license, purchase order or other legally binding commitment.

"**Depots**" means the bus depot facilities described in Section 2.1(b).

"**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement.

"**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, lessor's interest or other restriction of any kind.

"**Fleet Condition Report**" has the meaning given in Section 6.8(a).

"**Fleet Defect Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 6.8(b).

"**Fundamental Representations**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(b).

"**IP Assignment Agreement**" means the intellectual property assignment agreement between Seller, as assignor, and Buyer, as assignee, in the form attached as Exhibit B.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event or condition that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the Purchased Assets or to the results of operations or condition of the School Bus Business, taken as a whole.

"**NYSDOT**" means the New York State Department of Transportation.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, governmental authority, school district or other entity.

"**Pupil Transportation Contracts**" means the Contracts between Seller and public school districts for the transportation of pupils that are listed on Schedule 4.7(a).

"**Purchased Vehicles**" has the meaning given in Section 2.1(a).

"**Required Consents**" has the meaning given in Section 6.5.

"**Transferred IP**" means the RouteLattice routing and dispatch software, in source code and object code form, together with all related documentation, databases, route data, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property rights owned by Seller and used in the School Bus Business, as more fully described in the IP Assignment Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Articles, Sections, Exhibits and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless stated otherwise. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in the following assets (the "**Purchased Assets**"):

(a) 212 school buses, identified by vehicle identification number on Schedule 2.1(a), together with all spare parts, tools, onboard equipment, two-way radios, cameras, GPS units and child-check systems installed on or held for use with them (the "**Purchased Vehicles**");

(b) the two bus depots located in the Town of Wappinger and the Town of Montgomery, New York, including all real property, buildings, fuel systems, lifts and fixtures, as described on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) the Assigned Contracts;

(d) the Transferred IP;

(e) all NYSDOT inspection records, maintenance logs, driver qualification files (to the extent transferable under applicable law) and other books and records relating exclusively to the School Bus Business; and

(f) all permits and licenses relating to the School Bus Business, to the extent transferable.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include (a) any asset used exclusively in the Retained Charter Business, including Seller's motorcoaches; (b) cash and cash equivalents; (c) accounts receivable arising before the Closing; (d) Seller's organizational documents, tax records and minute books; and (e) any Contract not listed on Schedule 4.7(a) (the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only the obligations of Seller under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising and to be performed after the Closing, other than any liability arising from a breach or default by Seller before the Closing (the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and pay when due, all liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities, including all liabilities relating to the Retained Charter Business, pre-Closing taxes, pre-Closing employment matters and any accident or incident involving a Purchased Vehicle occurring before the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $38,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 6.8. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, as so adjusted, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.6 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code. Buyer shall deliver a draft allocation to Seller within sixty days after the Closing Date, and the Parties shall file all tax returns consistently with the final allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third Business Day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the Parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed; (b) the IP Assignment Agreement, duly executed; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed; (d) certificates of title for each Purchased Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer; (e) deeds for the Depots in recordable form; (f) the Required Consents; (g) releases of all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets; and (h) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the Purchase Price, as adjusted; (b) the IP Assignment Agreement and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and (c) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York and has all requisite power to own the Purchased Assets and to carry on the School Bus Business as currently conducted.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's operating agreement; (b) violate any law applicable to Seller; or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.3, require consent under, conflict with or result in a breach of any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Governmental Approvals.** No consent, approval or filing with any governmental authority is required for Seller's execution and performance of this Agreement, except for vehicle registration and title transfers with the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the unaudited statements of revenue and direct operating expenses of the School Bus Business for the fiscal years ended June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026. These statements were prepared in accordance with Seller's books and records and fairly present in all material respects the revenue and direct operating expenses of the School Bus Business for those periods.

**4.6 Real Property.** Seller holds good and marketable fee simple title to each Depot, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than those listed on Schedule 4.6. The Depots and their improvements are in good operating condition and comply in all material respects with applicable zoning and environmental laws.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** (a) Schedule 4.7(a) lists each Pupil Transportation Contract and each other Contract relating to the School Bus Business with annual payments over $100,000. Seller has made available to Buyer a true and complete copy of each such Contract. (b) Each Assigned Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract. No school district has given notice of termination, non-renewal or intent to rebid any Pupil Transportation Contract.

**4.8 Intellectual Property.** Seller is the sole and exclusive owner of the Transferred IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Transferred IP does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property rights of any Person. No Person has asserted any claim challenging Seller's ownership or use of the Transferred IP. The Transferred IP constitutes all software and intellectual property necessary to operate the routing and dispatch functions of the School Bus Business as currently conducted.

**4.9 Title to Purchased Vehicles.** Seller has good and valid title to each Purchased Vehicle, free and clear of all Encumbrances. Each Purchased Vehicle is titled and registered in Seller's name with the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles. No Purchased Vehicle is subject to any lease, conditional sale or financing arrangement.

**4.10 Vehicle Condition and Compliance.** Each Purchased Vehicle has passed its most recent semi-annual NYSDOT inspection, is in good operating condition (ordinary wear and tear excepted) and has been maintained in accordance with manufacturer recommendations and applicable law. Schedule 4.10 lists the model year, mileage and most recent inspection date of each Purchased Vehicle.

**4.11 Employees.** Schedule 4.11 lists all drivers, monitors, mechanics and dispatchers employed in the School Bus Business, with their positions, hire dates and current rates of pay. Seller has complied in all material respects with all laws relating to employment, including Article 19-A of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law.

**4.12 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.12, there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the School Bus Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with this Agreement for which Buyer could become liable.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of New York.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes Buyer's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer in connection with this Agreement for which Seller could become liable.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the School Bus Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall maintain the Purchased Vehicles and Depots in their current condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, (a) sell or encumber any Purchased Asset; (b) amend, terminate or waive any material right under any Assigned Contract; or (c) submit any bid or proposal to a school district that would bind Buyer after the Closing.

**6.2 Access.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Depots, the Purchased Vehicles, the Assigned Contracts and the books and records of the School Bus Business.

**6.3 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective on the Closing Date, to each driver and monitor listed on Schedule 4.11 who holds a valid Article 19-A qualification, on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to those currently provided by Seller.

**6.4 Efforts.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated by this Agreement as promptly as practicable.

**6.5 Required Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing and at its own cost, the written consent of each counterparty identified in Section 7.2(d) to the assignment of its Pupil Transportation Contract to Buyer (the "**Required Consents**"), in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer. Buyer shall cooperate reasonably with Seller, including by providing information about Buyer's fleet, safety record and insurance that a school district reasonably requests.

**6.6 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, provide pupil transportation services under contract with any public school district in Dutchess, Orange, Putnam or Ulster County, New York. This Section 6.6 does not restrict the Retained Charter Business from providing occasional charter or field-trip services booked by a school or district.

**6.7 Transition Services.** For up to ninety days after the Closing, Seller shall provide such reasonable dispatch and administrative assistance as Buyer requests to transfer the Pupil Transportation Contracts, at Seller's actual cost.

**6.8 Fleet Condition Report.**

(a) Within fifteen Business Days after the date of this Agreement, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written report, prepared by a NYSDOT-certified inspector reasonably acceptable to Buyer, describing the mechanical and structural condition of each Purchased Vehicle (the "**Fleet Condition Report**").

(b) Buyer may, no later than 30 days after Seller delivers the Fleet Condition Report, deliver to Seller a written notice (a "**Fleet Defect Notice**") identifying each Purchased Vehicle that requires repair to meet the standard in Section 4.10, with Buyer's good-faith estimate of the cost of repair.

(c) The Purchase Price shall be reduced at the Closing by the aggregate repair cost stated in any timely Fleet Defect Notice, unless Seller completes the repairs to Buyer's reasonable satisfaction before the Closing. If Seller disputes any estimate within ten Business Days after receiving a Fleet Defect Notice, the Parties shall jointly appoint an independent NYSDOT-certified repair facility, whose determination of the repair cost shall be final and binding.

(d) Buyer's failure to deliver a Fleet Defect Notice within the period stated in Section 6.8(b) after Seller delivers the Fleet Condition Report constitutes an irrevocable waiver of Buyer's right to any reduction of the Purchase Price under this Section 6.8.

**6.9 Notification.** Each Party shall promptly notify the other of any event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Article VII not to be satisfied by the Outside Date.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each Party to effect the Closing are subject to the following conditions: (a) no governmental order restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement is in effect; and (b) no action by any governmental authority seeking to enjoin those transactions is pending.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligations of Buyer to effect the Closing are subject to the following additional conditions, any of which Buyer may waive in writing:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV are true and correct in all material respects (or, if qualified by materiality, in all respects) as of the date of this Agreement and the Closing Date;

(b) Seller has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer has received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) Seller has obtained, and delivered to Buyer, the written consents of the Ashfield Central School District and the Pell Hollow City School District to the assignment of their respective Pupil Transportation Contracts to Buyer;

(e) Seller has delivered each item listed in Section 3.2; and

(f) no Material Adverse Effect has occurred since the date of this Agreement.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligations of Seller to effect the Closing are subject to the following additional conditions, any of which Seller may waive in writing: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V are true and correct in all material respects; (b) Buyer has performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and (c) Seller has received a certificate signed by an authorized officer of Buyer to that effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Transfer Taxes.** All sales, use, transfer, real property transfer, recording and similar taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**8.2 Proration.** Real property taxes on the Depots and vehicle registration fees for the tax period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Buyer and Seller on a per diem basis.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing for eighteen months, except that the Fundamental Representations survive until sixty days after expiry of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement; (b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; (c) any Excluded Asset or Excluded Liability; or (d) the operation of the Retained Charter Business.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates and their respective officers, members and employees against all Losses arising out of (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement; (b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement; or (c) any Assumed Liability.

**9.4 Basket.** Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $192,500, after which Seller is liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

**9.5 Cap.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($3,850,000).

(b) The basket in Section 9.4 and the cap in Section 9.5(a) do not apply to Losses arising from any inaccuracy in or breach of the representations and warranties in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.9 (the "**Fundamental Representations**") or from fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for those Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of fraud.

**9.6 Procedures.** An indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party, and it may not settle any such claim without the indemnified Party's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified Party.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for fraud or for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of this Agreement after the Closing.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated at any time before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either Party, if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 15, 2026 (the "**Outside Date**"), unless the failure to close was caused by the terminating Party's breach of this Agreement;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 10.1, it becomes void and of no further effect, without liability of any Party, except that (a) this Section 10.2 and Article XI survive; and (b) no termination relieves a Party of liability for willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a Party designates by notice).

**11.2 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each Party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement and the transactions it contemplates.

**11.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

**11.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.5 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to one or more of its Affiliates or, by way of collateral security, to its lenders. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 11.5 is void.

**11.6 Successors.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except as provided in Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and nothing in it confers any right or remedy on any other Person.

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of any other law.

**11.9 Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement and waives any objection to venue in those courts. **EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.**

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The Parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each Party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

**11.12 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall control, except that the IP Assignment Agreement shall control with respect to the Transferred IP.
