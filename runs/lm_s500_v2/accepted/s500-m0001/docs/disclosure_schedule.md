# DISCLOSURE SCHEDULE

**to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026**
**between Brouviv Student Transit, Inc., as Buyer, and Droureil Coachways LLC, as Seller**

Delivered by Seller to Buyer on August 14, 2026

## 1. Preliminary Statements

1.1 **Purpose.** This Disclosure Schedule is delivered by Droureil Coachways LLC ("**Seller**") to Brouviv Student Transit, Inc. ("**Buyer**") under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 between Buyer and Seller (the "**Agreement**"). It sets out the matters referred to in, and the exceptions to, the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV of the Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given in the Agreement.

1.3 **Numbering.** Each schedule below is numbered to match the Section of the Agreement to which it relates. A matter disclosed in one schedule is deemed disclosed for any other Section of Article IV only where its relevance to that other Section is reasonably apparent on the face of the disclosure.

1.4 **Copies of Contracts.** Where this Disclosure Schedule summarizes or quotes a Contract, the summary or quotation is qualified by the full text of that Contract. Seller has made a true and complete copy of each Contract available to Buyer in the electronic data room titled "Project Yellow Line," under the folder reference given for each item.

1.5 **No Admission.** The inclusion of any matter in this Disclosure Schedule is not an admission that the matter is material, or that it is outside the ordinary course of business, or that any breach or violation of law or Contract exists.

## 3. Schedule 4.6 – Real Property; Permitted Encumbrances

**Depot 1 – Town of Wappinger, Dutchess County, New York**
Approximately 6.8 acres. Includes a maintenance garage with six service bays, a dispatch and administration building, an above-ground diesel fueling station of 10,000 gallons, and paved parking for approximately 130 buses.

(a) Mortgage dated March 3, 2019 in favor of Hudson Commerce Bank, N.A., securing a term loan with an outstanding principal balance of about $2,140,000 as of July 31, 2026. This mortgage will be satisfied and discharged at the Closing under Section 3.2(g) of the Agreement.

(b) Utility easement in favor of Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation along the northern boundary, recorded in the Dutchess County Clerk's Office.

(c) Drainage easement in favor of the Town of Wappinger along the eastern boundary.

**Depot 2 – Town of Montgomery, Orange County, New York**
Approximately 5.1 acres. Includes a maintenance garage with four service bays, a driver training room, an above-ground diesel and gasoline fueling station, and paved parking for approximately 95 buses.

(a) Utility easement in favor of Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc., recorded in the Orange County Clerk's Office.

(b) Shared access driveway agreement with the owner of the adjoining parcel, recorded in the Orange County Clerk's Office.

None of the easements or agreements listed above materially interferes with the present use of either Depot as a school bus depot.

## 7. Schedule 4.12 – Litigation

**Item 1 – *Ortega v. Droureil Coachways LLC*,** Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Orange, Index No. EF00[●]-2026. The plaintiff, a passenger car driver, claims personal injury from a low-speed rear-end collision with a Purchased Vehicle in the Town of Montgomery on January 22, 2026. No pupils were on board. The claim has been tendered to and accepted by Seller's automobile liability insurer, Keystone Mutual Insurance Company, subject to a reservation of rights limited to punitive damages. The damages claimed are within policy limits. This matter is an Excluded Liability under Section 2.4 of the Agreement.

There is no other action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the School Bus Business or the Purchased Assets.
