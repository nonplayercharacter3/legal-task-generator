# BILL OF SALE

**THIS BILL OF SALE** (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of [●], 2026 by **Droureil Coachways LLC**, a New York limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to **Brouviv Student Transit, Inc.**, a New York corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (as amended, the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell to Buyer substantially all of the assets used in Seller's pupil transportation business (the "**School Bus Business**").

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale to Buyer at the Closing.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, for the consideration set out in the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 "**Conveyed Assets**" means all Purchased Assets other than (a) the Depot real property, buildings and fixtures, which are conveyed by the deeds delivered under Section 3.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement; (b) the Assigned Contracts, which are assigned by the Assignment and Assumption Agreement; and (c) the Transferred IP, which is assigned by the IP Assignment Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 **Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Buyer, and its successors and assigns, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Conveyed Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Conveyed Assets include:

(a) the 212 school buses identified by vehicle identification number on Schedule 2.1(a) to the Purchase Agreement, as that Schedule is in effect on the Closing Date, together with all spare parts, tools, onboard equipment, two-way radios, cameras, GPS units and child-check systems installed on or held for use with them;

(b) all movable equipment, shop tools, diagnostic equipment, office furniture, computers and other tangible personal property located at the Depots and used in the School Bus Business, other than any item used exclusively in the Retained Charter Business;

(c) all NYSDOT inspection records, maintenance logs, driver qualification files (to the extent transferable under applicable law) and other books and records relating exclusively to the School Bus Business; and

(d) all permits and licenses relating to the School Bus Business, to the extent transferable.

2.2 **Excluded Assets.** Seller does not sell, assign, transfer or convey any Excluded Asset to Buyer under this Bill of Sale, and Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in the Excluded Assets.

2.3 **No Assumption.** This Bill of Sale does not cause Buyer to assume any liability or obligation of Seller. Buyer assumes only the Assumed Liabilities, and only as provided in the Purchase Agreement and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement.

## 3. Vehicle Titles

3.1 Concurrently with this Bill of Sale, Seller is delivering to Buyer the certificate of title for each Purchased Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer, as required by Section 3.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

3.2 Seller shall sign any additional application, odometer disclosure statement or other form that the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles requires to register and title the Purchased Vehicles in Buyer's name.

## 4. Further Assurances

4.1 At Buyer's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of conveyance and transfer, and take such further actions, as Buyer reasonably requests to vest in Buyer good title to the Conveyed Assets and to put Buyer in actual possession and control of them.

4.2 If Seller receives or holds any Conveyed Asset after the Closing, Seller shall hold it in trust for Buyer and promptly deliver it to Buyer.

## 5. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer, and its successors and assigns, as Seller's true and lawful attorney-in-fact, coupled with an interest, with full power of substitution, in Seller's name but for Buyer's benefit, to demand and receive the Conveyed Assets and to institute and prosecute, in Seller's name or otherwise, any proceeding Buyer considers proper to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title in or to the Conveyed Assets.

## 6. Relationship to the Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale limits or expands the representations, warranties, covenants, agreements or indemnities of Seller or Buyer in the Purchase Agreement, which survive the delivery of this Bill of Sale as provided in the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. Miscellaneous

7.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns and benefits Buyer and its successors and assigns.

7.2 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of any other law.

7.3 **Forum.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York, in accordance with Section 11.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

7.4 **Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed by electronic signature, and delivery of an executed copy by email has the same effect as delivery of an original.
