**Re: Proposed Acquisition of the School Bus Business of Droureil Coachways LLC – Letter of Intent**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Brouviv Student Transit, Inc. ("**Brouviv**") proposes to acquire the pupil transportation operations of Droureil Coachways LLC ("**Droureil**") (the "**Proposed Transaction**"). Brouviv and Droureil are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the Parties' present intentions. It is intended as a basis for negotiating definitive agreements.

## 1. Structure of the Proposed Transaction

1.1 Brouviv would purchase from Droureil substantially all of the assets used in Droureil's pupil transportation business serving public school districts in the Hudson Valley region of New York (the "**School Bus Business**"). The purchase would be structured as an asset purchase under a definitive asset purchase agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

1.2 Droureil would keep its charter motorcoach business (the "**Retained Charter Business**"). The Retained Charter Business, and all assets used exclusively in it, would be excluded from the Proposed Transaction.

## 2. Purchased Assets

The assets to be acquired (the "**Purchased Assets**") would include:

(a) 212 school buses, together with the spare parts, tools, onboard equipment, radios, cameras, GPS units and child-check systems installed on them or held for use with them;

(b) the two bus depots in the Town of Wappinger and the Town of Montgomery, New York, including the real property, buildings, fueling systems, lifts and fixtures;

(c) Droureil's pupil transportation contracts with Hudson Valley public school districts and its other material contracts relating to the School Bus Business, as identified during due diligence;

(d) the RouteLattice routing and dispatch software, in source code and object code form, with all related documentation, route data, trademarks and other intellectual property rights (the "**RouteLattice Software**");

(e) maintenance logs, NYSDOT inspection records, driver qualification files (to the extent transferable) and other books and records of the School Bus Business; and

(f) all transferable permits and licenses relating to the School Bus Business.

Cash, pre-closing accounts receivable and Droureil's motorcoaches would be excluded.

## 3. Purchase Price

3.1 The proposed purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $38,500,000, payable in cash at closing. This assumes that the assets are delivered free and clear of all liens and that the results of due diligence are consistent with the information Droureil has provided to date.

3.2 The Purchase Agreement would provide for an adjustment to the purchase price if a pre-closing inspection of the buses identifies buses that need repair. The mechanics would be agreed in the Purchase Agreement.

## 4. Liabilities

Brouviv would assume only Droureil's obligations arising after closing under the contracts it acquires. Droureil would keep all other liabilities, including those relating to the Retained Charter Business, pre-closing taxes, pre-closing employment matters and pre-closing vehicle accidents or incidents.

## 5. RouteLattice Software

Brouviv understands that Droureil uses the RouteLattice Software in the Retained Charter Business. The Parties expect that Brouviv would grant Droureil a limited license to continue that use after closing. The terms of that license would be set out in the definitive agreements.

## 6. Employees

Brouviv intends to offer employment, effective at closing, to Droureil's school bus drivers and monitors who hold a valid Article 19-A qualification. Those offers would be on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to their current terms. Brouviv will consider offers to mechanics, dispatchers and depot staff after it has reviewed staffing during due diligence.

## 7. Restrictive Covenant

For five years after closing, Droureil and its affiliates would not provide contracted pupil transportation services to public school districts in Dutchess, Orange, Putnam or Ulster County, New York. This covenant would not restrict the Retained Charter Business from providing occasional charter or field-trip services.

## 8. Definitive Agreements

8.1 The Proposed Transaction would be documented in the Purchase Agreement, a bill of sale, an intellectual property assignment agreement, an assignment and assumption agreement, deeds for the depots and the other customary closing instruments (together, the "**Definitive Agreements**").

8.2 The Purchase Agreement would contain representations, warranties, covenants, conditions and indemnities that are customary for a transaction of this kind. These would include representations as to title to the buses and depots, the condition and NYSDOT compliance of the fleet, material contracts and ownership of the RouteLattice Software.

8.3 The Parties contemplate that all of the Definitive Agreements will be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

## 9. Conditions

Completion of the Proposed Transaction would be subject to, among other things:

(a) satisfactory completion of Brouviv's business, legal, financial, operational and fleet due diligence;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreements;

(c) receipt of all third-party consents required to assign the contracts being acquired, including consents of school districts where required, in form reasonably satisfactory to Brouviv;

(d) approval of the Proposed Transaction by Brouviv's board of directors; and

(e) the absence of any material adverse change in the School Bus Business.

## 10. Due Diligence and Access

Between the date of this Letter and the earlier of the signing of the Purchase Agreement and the termination of this Letter, Droureil shall give Brouviv and its advisors reasonable access during normal business hours to the depots, the buses, the contracts, the RouteLattice Software and the books, records and personnel of the School Bus Business. That access includes the opportunity to have the fleet inspected by a NYSDOT-certified inspector.

## 11. Exclusivity

For ninety days after the date of this Letter, Droureil shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Brouviv for the sale of the School Bus Business or any material part of the Purchased Assets. The Parties may extend this period by written agreement. Droureil shall promptly notify Brouviv of any unsolicited proposal of that kind it receives during the exclusivity period.

## 12. Binding Provisions

12.1 Only Sections 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 of this Letter (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the Parties. No other provision of this Letter creates any legally binding obligation, and neither Party is obligated to complete the Proposed Transaction unless and until the Definitive Agreements are executed and delivered.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Letter by written notice to the other at any time. Termination does not affect Sections 12, 13 and 14 or any liability for breach of a Binding Provision before termination.

## 13. Confidentiality; Announcements

The Parties' confidentiality agreement dated February 4, 2026 remains in full force and effect and applies to this Letter and to all information exchanged in connection with the Proposed Transaction. Neither Party shall make any public announcement about the Proposed Transaction without the other Party's prior written consent, except as required by law.

## 14. Expenses; Governing Law

14.1 Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Proposed Transaction.

14.2 This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of any other law.

14.3 This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.

If this Letter accurately reflects our mutual understanding, please countersign and return a copy to us. This proposal will expire if a countersigned copy has not been received by 5:00 p.m. (New York time) on March 27, 2026.
