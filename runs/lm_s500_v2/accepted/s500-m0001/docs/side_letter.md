**Re: Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 – Side Letter**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This side letter (this "**Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 14, 2026 (the "**Agreement**") between Brouviv Student Transit, Inc. ("**Buyer**") and Droureil Coachways LLC ("**Seller**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. Buyer and Seller agree as follows.

## 1. Background

1.1 Between the date of the Agreement and the date of this Letter, six of the school buses listed on Schedule 2.1(a) were permanently removed from service. Four sustained collision damage while in Seller's operation, and two were found to have frame corrosion requiring replacement of structural members (together, the "**Removed Vehicles**"). Under Section 2.4 of the Agreement, Seller retains all liabilities arising from any accident or incident involving those vehicles before the Closing.

1.2 Seller has proposed to replace the Removed Vehicles with six school buses of the same or a later model year and of equivalent seating capacity from Seller's spare fleet. Buyer is willing to accept those buses on the terms of this Letter.

## 2. Substitute Vehicles; Title Representation

2.1 **Substitution.** Schedule 2.1(a) to the Agreement is amended by deleting the Removed Vehicles and adding the six school buses identified by vehicle identification number in Annex A to this Letter (the "**Substitute Vehicles**"). Following this amendment, Schedule 2.1(a) continues to list 212 school buses. Each Substitute Vehicle is a Purchased Vehicle for all purposes of the Agreement, and each Removed Vehicle is an Excluded Asset.

2.2 **Title Representation.** Section 4.9 is hereby deleted in its entirety, and the following new Section 4.9A is inserted in Article IV of the Agreement:

> **4.9A Title to Purchased Vehicles and Substitute Vehicles.** Seller has good and valid title to each Purchased Vehicle, including each Substitute Vehicle, free and clear of all Encumbrances. Each Purchased Vehicle, including each Substitute Vehicle, is titled and registered in Seller's name with the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles. No Purchased Vehicle or Substitute Vehicle is subject to any lease, conditional sale or financing arrangement. No Substitute Vehicle has been used outside the State of New York or in the Retained Charter Business in the twelve months before the date of the side letter dated October 9, 2026.

2.3 **Condition of Substitute Vehicles.** Section 4.10 of the Agreement applies to each Substitute Vehicle. Seller shall deliver to Buyer, within five Business Days after the date of this Letter, an updated Schedule 4.10 showing the model year, mileage and most recent semi-annual NYSDOT inspection date of each Substitute Vehicle.

2.4 **Closing Deliverables.** The certificates of title to be delivered under Section 3.2(d) of the Agreement include a certificate of title for each Substitute Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to Buyer. Seller shall deliver no certificate of title for a Removed Vehicle.

2.5 **Purchase Price.** The substitution under this Section 2 does not change the Purchase Price.

## 3. Fleet Condition Report

3.1 Seller delivered the Fleet Condition Report to Buyer on August 28, 2026.

3.2 Buyer delivered a Fleet Defect Notice to Seller on October 2, 2026. The notice identifies 11 Purchased Vehicles as requiring repair to meet the standard in Section 4.10 of the Agreement and states Buyer's good-faith estimate of the aggregate repair cost as $612,000. None of the vehicles it identifies is a Removed Vehicle or a Substitute Vehicle.

3.3 Buyer's position is that the Purchase Price is to be reduced at the Closing by $612,000, unless Seller completes the repairs to Buyer's reasonable satisfaction before the Closing. The Parties shall resolve the Fleet Defect Notice, including the amount of any reduction of the Purchase Price and any dispute over Buyer's estimate, in accordance with Section 6.8 of the Agreement.

3.4 To allow Buyer to verify any repairs Seller elects to complete before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its inspector access to the 11 vehicles at the Depots on reasonable advance notice, as contemplated by Section 6.2 of the Agreement.

## 4. Effect of this Letter

4.1 This Letter is an amendment to the Agreement under Section 11.3 of the Agreement. Except as expressly amended by this Letter, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

4.2 From and after the date of this Letter, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Letter.

## 5. Miscellaneous

5.1 Sections 11.1 (Notices), 11.4 (Severability), 11.6 (Successors), 11.8 (Governing Law), 11.9 (Forum) and 11.11 (Counterparts) of the Agreement apply to this Letter, with references in those Sections to "this Agreement" read as references to this Letter.

5.2 This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
