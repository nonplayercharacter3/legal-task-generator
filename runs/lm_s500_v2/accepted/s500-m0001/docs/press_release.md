# Brouviv Student Transit to Acquire Droureil Coachways' Hudson Valley School Bus Operations

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Hudson Valley, New York – August 17, 2026**

## 1. Announcement

Brouviv Student Transit, Inc. ("**Brouviv**"), a New York school bus contractor, today announced that it has entered into a definitive Asset Purchase Agreement to acquire the school bus operations of Droureil Coachways LLC ("**Droureil**"), a Hudson Valley pupil transportation operator. The parties signed the agreement on August 14, 2026.

Under the agreement, Brouviv will pay a purchase price of $38,500,000 in cash, subject to adjustment as provided in the agreement. Brouviv will also assume Droureil's post-closing obligations under the contracts it acquires.

## 2. Assets Being Acquired

The transaction covers substantially all of the assets Droureil uses in its pupil transportation business, including:

- a fleet of 212 school buses, including wheelchair-equipped Type A vehicles, with their onboard camera, GPS and child-check systems;
- two bus depots, in the Town of Wappinger in Dutchess County and the Town of Montgomery in Orange County, each with maintenance garages and on-site fueling;
- Droureil's pupil transportation contracts with public school districts in the Hudson Valley; and
- RouteLattice, Droureil's proprietary routing and dispatch software, together with its route data and related intellectual property.

Droureil will keep its charter motorcoach business, which is not part of the transaction. After closing, Droureil will continue to use the RouteLattice software in that business under a license from Brouviv.

## 3. Employees and Service Continuity

Brouviv intends to offer employment, effective at closing, to Droureil's qualified school bus drivers and monitors. Those offers will be on terms substantially comparable in the aggregate to their current terms. Both companies are working closely with the school districts served by Droureil to ensure that routes, schedules and student safety programs continue without interruption through the transition.

## 4. Statements from the Companies

"This acquisition extends Brouviv's footprint across the Hudson Valley and brings us an experienced team of drivers, monitors and technicians who know these communities well," said the Chief Executive Officer of Brouviv. "Our first priority is a seamless transition for students, families and the districts we will serve. Adding the RouteLattice platform will strengthen our routing and dispatch capabilities across our combined operations."

"We are proud of the school bus business our team has built, and we are confident that Brouviv is the right partner to carry it forward," said the Managing Member of Droureil. "This transaction lets Droureil focus fully on our charter motorcoach business, while our school transportation employees and district partners join a dedicated student transportation company."

## 5. Timing and Conditions

The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. Closing is subject to customary conditions, including the receipt of consents from certain school districts to the assignment of their pupil transportation contracts. Until closing, Droureil will continue to operate its school bus routes in the ordinary course.

## 6. About Brouviv Student Transit, Inc.

Brouviv Student Transit, Inc. is a New York school bus contractor providing home-to-school, special education and activity transportation to public school districts. Brouviv is committed to safety, driver training and on-time service.

## 7. About Droureil Coachways LLC

Droureil Coachways LLC is a Hudson Valley transportation company. Following the transaction, Droureil will continue to operate its charter motorcoach business, serving group travel, corporate and event customers.
