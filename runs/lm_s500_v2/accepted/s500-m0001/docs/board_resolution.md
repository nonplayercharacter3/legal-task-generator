# UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## OF

## BROUVIV STUDENT TRANSIT, INC.

**(a New York corporation)**

**Dated as of August 13, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Brouviv Student Transit, Inc., a New York corporation (the "**Company**"), act by unanimous written consent without a meeting under Section 708(b) of the New York Business Corporation Law and the Company's by-laws. The undersigned waive all requirements of notice and adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if duly adopted at a meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. Background

1.1 The Company has negotiated with Droureil Coachways LLC, a New York limited liability company ("**Droureil**"), for the acquisition of substantially all of the assets Droureil uses in its pupil transportation business serving public school districts in the Hudson Valley region of New York (the "**School Bus Business**"). Droureil will keep its charter motorcoach business, which is not part of the transaction.

1.2 The parties signed a letter of intent dated March 18, 2026, which contemplated, among other things, approval of the transaction by the Board.

1.3 The Board has received and reviewed a substantially final draft of an Asset Purchase Agreement between the Company, as buyer, and Droureil, as seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), together with the forms of the Bill of Sale, the IP Assignment Agreement and the Assignment and Assumption Agreement contemplated by it (together with the other certificates and instruments to be delivered at the Closing, the "**Ancillary Agreements**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

1.4 Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company would acquire, among other assets:

(a) 212 school buses, with their spare parts, onboard equipment and installed systems;

(b) the two bus depots in the Town of Wappinger and the Town of Montgomery, New York;

(c) the Assigned Contracts, including Droureil's Pupil Transportation Contracts with Hudson Valley school districts; and

(d) the RouteLattice routing and dispatch software and related intellectual property, subject to a license-back to Droureil for use in its retained charter business.

1.5 The aggregate purchase price is $38,500,000 in cash (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities.

1.6 The Board has received a presentation from management and the Company's outside counsel and financial advisors on the terms of the Purchase Agreement, the results of the Company's due diligence on the School Bus Business, the expected sources of funds for the Purchase Price and the expected integration of Droureil's employees, routes and depots into the Company's operations. The Board has had the opportunity to ask questions of management and its advisors.

## 2. Approval of the Acquisition

**RESOLVED**, that the Board determines that the acquisition of the Purchased Assets on the terms of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Acquisition**") is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, and the Company's performance of its obligations under them, are approved in all respects; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the Purchase Price of $38,500,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement, is approved.

## 3. Authorization of Officers

**RESOLVED**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Secretary of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized, acting alone, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement and each Ancillary Agreement to which the Company is a party, substantially in the forms presented to the Board, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves as necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by the Authorized Officer's execution of the document; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that any amendment, supplement or waiver under the Purchase Agreement that would increase the Purchase Price, materially expand the Assumed Liabilities, or waive any condition in Section 7.2 of the Purchase Agreement that is material to the Company, shall require the further approval of the Board; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to take all actions to satisfy the conditions to the Closing in Article VII of the Purchase Agreement on or before the Outside Date of December 15, 2026, including cooperating with Droureil in obtaining the Required Consents and furnishing to school districts information on the Company's fleet, safety record and insurance.

## 4. Funding of the Purchase Price

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to fund the Purchase Price and related transaction costs from the Company's cash on hand and from borrowings under the Company's existing senior credit facility, within the borrowing limits previously approved by the Board, and to execute any borrowing request, notice or certificate required for that purpose; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to direct the payment of the Purchase Price, as adjusted, at the Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds in accordance with Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Employees and Operations

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to extend offers of employment, effective on the Closing Date, to the drivers and monitors of the School Bus Business in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement, and to extend such other offers to mechanics, dispatchers and depot staff as management considers appropriate; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to obtain such insurance coverage, vehicle registrations, operating authority and permits as are required for the Company to operate the Purchased Vehicles and the Depots from the Closing Date.

## 6. Public Announcement

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized, after the execution of the Purchase Agreement and with Droureil's consent, to issue a press release announcing the Acquisition in a form approved by the Chief Executive Officer.

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver all such further agreements, certificates, instruments and documents, to make all such filings and to pay all such fees and expenses, as that Authorized Officer considers necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions; and

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that all actions previously taken by any officer or director of the Company in connection with the Acquisition, including the negotiation of the letter of intent and the Purchase Agreement and the conduct of due diligence, are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness

8.1 This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

8.2 This consent is effective as of the date first written above and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
