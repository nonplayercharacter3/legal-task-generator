# SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

## Proposed Master Services Agreement: Rooftop Solar Installation and O&M Services

**Riboul Solar Installations, LLC** / **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**

**Date:** January 21, 2026

This Summary of Principal Terms (this "**Term Sheet**") sets out the principal terms on which Riboul Solar Installations, LLC ("**Provider**") and Treikev Community Power, Inc. ("**Customer**") propose to enter into a master services agreement (the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, Provider would install and service rooftop photovoltaic systems for Customer's community solar projects. Except as stated in Section 12, this Term Sheet is not binding. It does not create any obligation to negotiate or enter into the MSA or any other agreement.

## 1. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Provider | Riboul Solar Installations, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company |
| Customer | Treikev Community Power, Inc., a Delaware corporation, which may act through its project company Affiliates |

## 2. Structure

1.1 The MSA will set out general terms. Each project or portfolio will be documented in an Order Form, which will identify the Sites, Systems, pricing and milestone payments, and in a related Statement of Work, which will set out the scope, Specifications, schedule and deliverables.

1.2 The Parties will also enter into the following documents, which will be incorporated into the MSA:

- a Service Level Schedule for the O&M Services; and
- a Quality Agreement covering inspection, testing, documentation and quality reporting.

1.3 The Parties expect to sign the first Order Form at or shortly after signing the MSA. That Order Form will cover Customer's initial portfolio of New Jersey rooftop community solar sites.

## 3. Services

3.1 **Installation Work.** Provider will be responsible for engineering, procurement (other than Customer-Furnished Equipment), construction, installation, interconnection support, testing and commissioning.

3.2 **O&M Services.** After commissioning, Provider will perform monitoring, preventive maintenance and corrective maintenance. Response times, remediation periods and service credits will be set out in the Service Level Schedule.

3.3 **Customer-Furnished Equipment.** Customer will procure and supply the photovoltaic modules. Customer may also elect to supply other major equipment. Customer will retain responsibility for the performance of Customer-Furnished Equipment and for the related manufacturer warranties.

3.4 **Permits and approvals.**

- Provider will obtain construction permits.
- Customer will obtain interconnection approvals, community solar program approvals and land use entitlements.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Scope.** During the exclusivity period, Provider will not perform installation or operations and maintenance services for third parties on commercial rooftop community solar projects in New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

4.2 **Duration.** Exclusivity will apply for the Initial Term only. There will be no post-term restriction.

4.3 **Unrestricted business.** Provider's residential, utility-scale, ground-mount and other business is not restricted. Provider's business outside New Jersey and Pennsylvania is also not restricted.

4.4 **Customer right of first proposal.** During the Initial Term, Customer will give Provider the first opportunity to submit a proposal for each commercial rooftop community solar project that Customer develops in New Jersey or Pennsylvania. Customer will not be obligated to accept any proposal.

4.5 **Non-solicitation.** Each Party will agree to a customary mutual non-solicitation of employees involved in the Services. The restriction will apply during the term and for twelve (12) months afterward.

## 5. Term

5.1 **Initial Term.** The Initial Term will be three years from the MSA Effective Date.

5.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the MSA will renew automatically for successive one-year terms unless either Party gives at least ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

5.3 **Order Forms.** Order Forms in effect when the MSA expires or terminates will continue until they are completed.

## 6. Pricing and Payment

6.1 **Pricing.** Pricing will be set out in each Order Form.

- Installation Work will be invoiced against milestones.
- O&M Services will be invoiced monthly in arrears.

6.2 **Payment.** Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days after receipt. Late payments will bear interest. Provider will have a right to suspend the Services for prolonged non-payment.

6.3 **Module tariffs.** The Parties will document a mechanism for passing through tariff and duty changes affecting modules and other imported equipment. This may be done in a side letter.

## 7. Warranties

7.1 **Workmanship warranty.** Provider will warrant the Installation Work against workmanship defects for five (5) years after acceptance.

7.2 **Roof penetration warranty.** Provider will warrant roof penetrations as watertight for ten (10) years after acceptance.

7.3 **Exclusions.** The warranties will exclude failures caused by:

- Customer-Furnished Equipment;
- Customer or third parties;
- modifications not performed or approved by Provider; and
- force majeure.

## 8. Indemnities and Limitation of Liability

8.1 **Indemnities.** Each Party will give customary mutual indemnities for third-party claims arising from its negligence or willful misconduct and from its breach of law. Provider will also indemnify against subcontractor liens. Customer will also indemnify against defects in Customer-Furnished Equipment and against pre-existing Site conditions.

8.2 **Consequential damages.** There will be a mutual waiver of consequential damages, lost profits, lost revenue and lost production.

8.3 **Liability cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable to Provider in the 12 months preceding the claim.

8.4 **Carve-outs.** The cap and waiver carve-outs will be agreed in the MSA, consistent with market practice.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 Provider will maintain the following coverage, at levels to be confirmed in the MSA, and will name Customer as an additional insured where customary:

- commercial general liability;
- automobile liability;
- workers' compensation and employer's liability;
- umbrella liability; and
- builder's risk or installation floater coverage.

## 10. Assignment

10.1 Customer may assign the MSA or any Order Form without consent to any of the following:

- its Affiliates;
- successor project owners; and
- its lenders and tax equity investors, by way of collateral assignment.

10.2 Provider will sign reasonable consents to collateral assignment when requested.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

11.1 **Governing law.** The MSA will be governed by Delaware law.

11.2 **Forum.** The forum will be the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

11.3 **Escalation.** Before litigation, disputes will first be escalated to senior executives.

11.4 **Jury trial.** The Parties will each waive jury trial.

## 12. Binding Provisions

12.1 **Binding sections.** Only this Section 12 and the following provisions are binding:

- **Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep this Term Sheet and the negotiations confidential.
- **Costs.** Each Party will bear its own costs.
- **Governing law.** This Term Sheet is governed by Delaware law.

12.2 **When the binding provisions end.** The binding provisions end on whichever of the following occurs first:

- the date the MSA is signed; or
- ninety (90) days after the date of this Term Sheet.
