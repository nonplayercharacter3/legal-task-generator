## 6. Description of Operations / Locations

6.1 **Operations.** Engineering, procurement, construction, installation, commissioning, operations and maintenance of commercial rooftop photovoltaic systems.

6.2 **Contract.** Master Services Agreement between Riboul Solar Installations, LLC and Treikev Community Power, Inc. dated as of March 2, 2026, and Order Form No. 1 dated as of March 9, 2026 (New Jersey Rooftop Community Solar Portfolio, 18.6 MWdc across 14 rooftop sites, Sites NJ-01 through NJ-14).

6.3 **Additional Insureds.** Treikev Community Power, Inc. and its Affiliates are included as additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability, Automobile Liability and Umbrella Liability policies, by endorsement, with respect to liability arising out of the Named Insured's operations performed for them. Additional insured status under the Commercial General Liability policy includes completed operations.

6.4 **Installation Floater.** Coverage applies to Systems under construction at the Sites listed in Section 6.2, including materials and equipment awaiting installation at the Sites and at the Named Insured's staging yard in South Brunswick, New Jersey. This includes Customer-Furnished Equipment while in the Named Insured's care, custody or control.
