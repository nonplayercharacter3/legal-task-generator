# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**New Jersey Rooftop Community Solar Portfolio**

This Order Form No. 1 (this "**Order Form**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Order Form Effective Date**") by and between **Riboul Solar Installations, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). It is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between Provider and Customer dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**").

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the MSA.

## 1. Order Details

1.1 **Project.** The Systems in this Order Form make up Customer's initial portfolio of commercial rooftop community solar projects in New Jersey (the "**Portfolio**"). Each System is intended to take part in the New Jersey Community Solar Energy Program.

1.2 **Statement of Work.** Provider shall perform the Installation Work and the O&M Services for the Portfolio under this Order Form and the Statement of Work titled "Statement of Work – New Jersey Rooftop Portfolio," which references this Order Form (the "**Portfolio SOW**").

1.3 **Project Managers.**

| | Provider | Customer |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Project Manager | Development Lead |
| Responsibility | Primary point of contact for all Sites | Primary point of contact for Site access, Customer-Furnished Equipment and approvals |

Each Party shall notify the other in writing of the name and contact details of its representative within five (5) Business Days after the Order Form Effective Date.

2.2 **Site Details.** Street addresses, building owner details, roof lease references and electric distribution company information for each Site are set out in the Portfolio SOW.

2.3 **Capacity Adjustments.** System capacities are design targets based on Provider's preliminary layouts. A change to the as-built capacity of any System of five percent (5%) or less, made to accommodate roof conditions, setbacks or fire code access pathways, does not require a change order. In that case the Installation Fee for that System will be adjusted at the per-watt rate in Section 4.1. Any larger change requires a change order under Section 4 of the MSA.

## 3. Customer-Furnished Equipment

3.1 **Equipment.** Customer shall supply the following Customer-Furnished Equipment for each System:

- photovoltaic modules, of the make, model and wattage set out in the Portfolio SOW; and
- revenue-grade production meters, where the electric distribution company requires Customer-owned meters.

3.2 **Provider-Supplied Equipment.** Provider shall supply all other equipment and materials required for the Installation Work, including:

- inverters;
- racking and ballast;
- wiring, conduit and combiner boxes;
- disconnects;
- the monitoring system; and
- all other balance-of-system components.

3.3 **Delivery.** Customer shall deliver Customer-Furnished Equipment to each Site, or to Provider's designated staging yard, in accordance with the delivery schedule in the Portfolio SOW.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Installation Fee.** The fee for the Installation Work is $1.38 per watt (DC) of as-built System capacity. Based on the Portfolio capacity in Section 2.1, the total Installation Fee is $25,668,000. The Installation Fee excludes the cost of Customer-Furnished Equipment.

4.2 **Milestone Payments.** Provider shall invoice the Installation Fee for each System by milestone, as a percentage of the Installation Fee for that System:

| Milestone | Percentage |
|---|---|
| Issuance of construction permits for the System | 10% |
| Delivery to the Site of Provider-supplied racking and inverters | 25% |
| Mechanical completion of the System | 40% |
| Acceptance of the System under Section 6 of the MSA | 20% |
| Delivery of as-built drawings and the commissioning report | 5% |

4.3 **O&M Fee.** The fee for the O&M Services is $9.50 per kilowatt (DC) per year of as-built capacity of each accepted System. The fee is payable monthly in arrears from the first day of the month after acceptance of that System. For the full Portfolio this is approximately $176,700 per year ($14,725 per month).

4.4 **O&M Escalation.** The O&M fee increases by two and one-half percent (2.5%) on each anniversary of the Order Form Effective Date.

4.5 **Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice, and Customer shall pay, in accordance with Section 5 of the MSA. Invoices must identify the Site number and the milestone or service period billed.

## 5. Schedule

5.1 **Target Dates.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve mechanical completion of each System by the target dates in the Portfolio SOW, and to achieve mechanical completion of all Systems by December 15, 2026.

5.2 **Extensions.** The schedule will be extended under Sections 4.2 and 16 of the MSA, and for any delay in Customer's performance of its obligations under Section 3.4 or 3.5 of the MSA.

## 6. O&M Term

6.1 **Duration.** Provider shall perform the O&M Services for each System for an initial period of five (5) years after acceptance of that System. Customer may extend that period for further one-year periods by written notice to Provider at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current period.

6.2 **Service Levels.** The O&M Services are subject to the Service Level Schedule.
