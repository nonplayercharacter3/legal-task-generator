# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Riboul Solar Installations, LLC** and **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Riboul Solar Installations, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

**Background.** Customer develops, owns and operates community solar projects. Provider designs, installs, commissions, operates and maintains commercial rooftop photovoltaic systems. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to install and service rooftop photovoltaic systems for Customer's community solar projects on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below. Other capitalized terms are defined where they first appear.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required to close.

1.3 "**Customer-Furnished Equipment**" means photovoltaic modules, inverters, racking, monitoring hardware and any other equipment or materials that Customer procures and supplies to Provider for incorporation into a System, as identified in the applicable Order Form or Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Defect**" means a failure of Provider's Installation Work to conform to the Specifications.

1.5 "**Installation Work**" means the engineering, procurement (other than of Customer-Furnished Equipment), construction, installation, interconnection support, testing and commissioning services that Provider performs for a System under an Order Form and Statement of Work.

1.6 "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

1.7 "**O&M Services**" means the operations, preventive and corrective maintenance, monitoring and related services that Provider performs for a System after commissioning under an Order Form, Statement of Work or the Service Level Schedule.

1.8 "**Order Form**" means an ordering document that both Parties sign and that references this Agreement and identifies the Sites, Systems, pricing and other commercial terms for a project or portfolio.

1.9 "**Quality Agreement**" means the quality agreement between the Parties governing inspection, testing, documentation and quality reporting for the Services, as amended from time to time.

1.10 "**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule between the Parties setting out response times, remediation periods and service credits for the O&M Services, as amended from time to time.

1.11 "**Services**" means the Installation Work, the O&M Services and any other services Provider performs under this Agreement.

1.12 "**Site**" means a building, roof and associated premises identified in an Order Form on which a System is to be installed.

1.13 "**Specifications**" means the design drawings, technical specifications, performance criteria and applicable codes and standards for a System as set out or incorporated in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.14 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work that both Parties sign, that references this Agreement and an Order Form, and that describes the scope, Specifications, schedule and deliverables for the Services.

1.15 "**System**" means a rooftop photovoltaic generating system, including all modules, inverters, racking, wiring, balance-of-system components and monitoring equipment, installed or to be installed at a Site.

## 2. Structure of Agreement

2.1 **Order Forms and Statements of Work.** Provider shall perform Services as set out in each Order Form and Statement of Work signed by both Parties. Neither Party is obligated to enter into any Order Form or Statement of Work.

2.2 **Incorporation.** Each Order Form and Statement of Work, together with the Service Level Schedule and the Quality Agreement, is governed by and incorporates this Agreement. Each Order Form, together with its related Statement of Work, forms a separate contract between the Parties.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** If there is a conflict or inconsistency among the documents comprising this Agreement, the documents control in the following order: Order Form, then Statement of Work, then Service Level Schedule, then the body of this Agreement, then Quality Agreement.

2.4 **Affiliates.** An Affiliate of Customer may enter into an Order Form under this Agreement, in which case references to Customer in that Order Form and in this Agreement as applied to it refer to that Affiliate.

## 3. Performance of Services

3.1 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the Specifications, applicable law, the requirements of the relevant electric distribution company and authority having jurisdiction, and prudent industry practices for commercial rooftop solar installation in the jurisdiction where the Site is located.

3.2 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified, licensed and appropriately trained personnel to perform the Services. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Customer. Provider shall designate a project manager for each Order Form who will act as Customer's primary point of contact.

3.3 **Subcontractors.** Provider may engage subcontractors to perform portions of the Services, provided that Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as for its own performance. Provider shall not subcontract the whole of the Installation Work for any Order Form without Customer's prior written consent, which Customer shall not unreasonably withhold.

3.4 **Site Access.** Customer shall secure, and shall provide Provider with, access to each Site, including any required consents of the building owner or roof lessor, in time for Provider to meet the schedule in the applicable Statement of Work.

3.5 **Customer-Furnished Equipment.** Customer shall deliver Customer-Furnished Equipment to the Site or another location specified in the Statement of Work in accordance with the delivery schedule set out there. Provider shall inspect Customer-Furnished Equipment on receipt for visible damage and quantity discrepancies and shall notify Customer of any it finds. Customer retains responsibility for the design, manufacture, performance and manufacturer warranties of Customer-Furnished Equipment.

3.6 **Permits.** Unless the Statement of Work provides otherwise, Provider shall obtain building, electrical and other construction permits required for the Installation Work, and Customer shall obtain all interconnection approvals, community solar program approvals and land use entitlements.

## 4. Change Orders

4.1 Either Party may request a change to the scope, schedule or Specifications of a Statement of Work. Provider shall respond to a Customer request within ten (10) Business Days with a written proposal describing any effect on price, schedule and other terms.

4.2 No change is binding unless set out in a written change order signed by both Parties. Provider is entitled to an equitable adjustment of price and schedule for changes caused by concealed or unknown Site conditions, including structural roof deficiencies not reasonably discoverable during Provider's pre-construction survey, and by delays in delivery of Customer-Furnished Equipment.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the fees set out in each Order Form. Fees exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay or reimburse except for taxes on Provider's net income.

5.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Customer in accordance with the milestone payment schedule in the applicable Order Form or, for O&M Services, monthly in arrears.

5.3 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. Customer shall notify Provider in writing of any disputed amount and the basis for the dispute within fifteen (15) days after receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute.

5.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law. If any undisputed amount remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after written notice, Provider may suspend the Services under the affected Order Form until payment is received, and the schedule will be extended accordingly.

## 6. Acceptance

6.1 On completion of the Installation Work for a System, Provider shall notify Customer and perform the commissioning tests set out in the Statement of Work. Customer may attend.

6.2 Customer shall, within ten (10) Business Days after Provider's notice that the commissioning tests have been passed, either issue a certificate of acceptance or notify Provider in writing of each Defect that prevents acceptance. Provider shall correct each Defect so notified and repeat the relevant tests. If Customer does not respond within that period, or begins commercial operation of the System, the System is deemed accepted.

## 7. Title and Risk of Loss

7.1 Title to equipment and materials supplied by Provider passes to Customer on the earlier of installation at the Site and payment for those items.

7.2 Risk of loss of each System remains with Provider until acceptance under Section 6, except that risk of loss of Customer-Furnished Equipment remains with Customer at all times other than while that equipment is in Provider's custody and is damaged through Provider's negligence.

## 8. Warranties

8.1 **Workmanship Warranty.** Provider warrants that the Installation Work for each System will be free of Defects for a period of five (5) years after acceptance of that System (the "**Workmanship Warranty Period**"). If a Defect appears during the Workmanship Warranty Period, Provider shall, at its cost, repair or re-perform the affected Installation Work.

8.2 **Roof Penetration Warranty.** Provider warrants that roof penetrations and flashings it installs will remain watertight for ten (10) years after acceptance of the applicable System, and Provider shall repair any leak resulting from a breach of this warranty.

8.3 **Pass-Through Warranties.** Provider shall assign to Customer, to the extent assignable, all manufacturer warranties for equipment Provider supplies. Manufacturer warranties for Customer-Furnished Equipment are Customer's responsibility.

8.4 **Exclusions.** The warranties in this Section 8 do not apply to failures caused by Customer-Furnished Equipment, by the acts or omissions of Customer, the building owner or third parties, by modifications not performed or approved by Provider, or by force majeure events.

8.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 9. Exclusivity

9.1 **Exclusive Engagement.** During the Term and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following the expiration or termination of this Agreement, Provider shall not, directly or through any Affiliate, perform solar photovoltaic installation, engineering, procurement and construction, or operations and maintenance services for any person other than Customer and its Affiliates anywhere in the United States.

9.2 **Customer Commitment.** During the Initial Term, Customer shall offer Provider the first opportunity to submit a proposal for the installation of any commercial rooftop community solar project that Customer develops in New Jersey or Pennsylvania. Customer is not obligated to accept any proposal.

9.3 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for twelve (12) months afterward, neither Party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was directly involved in the Services, except through general solicitations not targeted at those employees.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other Party's non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**") in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and protect it with at least reasonable care. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to its Affiliates, employees, contractors, lenders, tax equity investors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 10.

10.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

10.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 10 continue for three (3) years after the end of the Term.

## 11. Indemnification

11.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by:

(a) bodily injury or death, or damage to tangible property other than the Systems, caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its subcontractors in performing the Services;

(b) Site Damage, as such term is defined in the applicable Statement of Work, arising from the performance of the Services by Provider or its subcontractors;

(c) Provider's breach of applicable law, including labor, wage and safety laws; and

(d) liens filed against a Site or System by Provider's subcontractors or suppliers for amounts Customer has paid to Provider.

For purposes of Section 11.1(b), Losses include Losses suffered directly by Customer whether or not a third-party claim is asserted.

11.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer, (b) defects in Customer-Furnished Equipment, (c) pre-existing environmental conditions or structural deficiencies at a Site, or (d) Customer's breach of applicable law.

11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of a claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement of the claim, and cooperate at the indemnifying Party's expense. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall not settle a claim in a way that imposes obligations or admissions on the indemnified Party without its consent.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

12.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, EXEMPLARY OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, LOST PRODUCTION OR LOSS OF ENVIRONMENTAL ATTRIBUTES OR TAX BENEFITS, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT OR OTHERWISE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.

12.2 **Cap.** EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 12.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE TO PROVIDER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

12.3 **Exceptions.** The limitations in Section 12.2 and the exclusions in Section 12.1 shall not apply to Provider's obligations under Section 11.1(b), and also shall not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations for third-party claims for bodily injury or death, (b) a Party's breach of Section 10, (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, or (d) Customer's obligation to pay fees due under this Agreement.

## 13. Insurance

13.1 During the Term and for so long as Provider performs O&M Services, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, including products-completed operations coverage;

(b) automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000;

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident;

(d) umbrella or excess liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000; and

(e) builder's risk or installation floater insurance covering Systems under construction for their full replacement value.

13.2 Provider shall name Customer and its Affiliates as additional insureds under the policies in Sections 13.1(a), (b) and (d), and shall deliver certificates of insurance to Customer before beginning work at any Site and on each renewal.

## 14. Intellectual Property

14.1 Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools, methodologies and know-how.

14.2 Design drawings, as-built drawings, commissioning reports and other deliverables prepared specifically for a System become the property of Customer upon payment in full for the Installation Work for that System. Provider retains a non-exclusive right to use its general design methodologies and know-how embodied in those deliverables.

## 15. Term and Termination

15.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a "**Renewal Term**" and, together with the Initial Term, the "**Term**") unless either Party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

15.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an affected Order Form on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it, or immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to bankruptcy proceedings that are not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

15.3 **Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate any Order Form for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. In that case Customer shall pay Provider for Services performed through the termination date, non-cancellable costs of materials ordered for the Order Form, and reasonable demobilization costs.

15.4 **Effect of Termination.** Expiration or termination of this Agreement does not terminate any Order Form then in effect, which continues to be governed by this Agreement until it is completed or terminated. Sections 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15.4, 17 and 18 survive expiration or termination.

## 16. Force Majeure

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, severe weather, fire, flood, epidemic, war, terrorism, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own workforce, governmental action, utility interconnection delays, and shortages or delays of equipment under tariffs, trade restrictions or customs detentions.

16.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party promptly, use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of the event, and resume performance as soon as practicable. The schedule for the affected Services will be extended by the period of delay.

## 17. Governing Law and Disputes

17.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

17.2 **Forum.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

17.3 **Escalation.** Before starting litigation (other than for injunctive relief), a Party shall give the other Party written notice of the dispute, and the Parties' senior executives shall meet, in person or by videoconference, within fifteen (15) Business Days after that notice to try in good faith to resolve it.

17.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 18. General

18.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses stated on the signature page or another address a Party designates by notice.

18.2 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

18.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Customer may assign this Agreement or any Order Form, without consent, to an Affiliate, to a successor owner of the applicable project, or collaterally to its lenders or tax equity investors. Provider shall execute reasonable consents to collateral assignment on request.

18.4 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

18.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted.

18.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
