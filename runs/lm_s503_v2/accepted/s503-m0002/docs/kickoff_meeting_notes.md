## 4. Pre-Construction

4.1 **Customer documents.** Customer will provide as-built building drawings, roof warranty documentation and prior roof inspection reports for all 14 Sites by March 30, 2026, under Section 3.2(d) of the Portfolio SOW. Customer noted that roof warranty documentation for NJ-05 (Secaucus) and NJ-10 (Carlstadt) may take longer to obtain from the building owners. Customer will flag any delay on the weekly call.

4.2 **Surveys.** Provider will begin pre-construction surveys under Section 2.2(a) of the Portfolio SOW on March 23, 2026. Surveys will be sequenced to follow the module delivery order in Annex B. Customer will arrange building owner access for the first five Sites (NJ-01 to NJ-05) by March 20, 2026.

4.3 **Structural Reports.** Provider confirmed that each Structural Report will be delivered to Customer before the permit application for the relevant Site is submitted, as required by Section 2.2(b) of the Portfolio SOW. Provider noted that the Cherry Hill retail center (NJ-09) is the oldest structure in the Portfolio. If the Structural Report identifies reinforcement needs there, that work falls under the exclusions in Section 3.1 of the Portfolio SOW and would be handled by change order.

4.4 **Roof warranties.** Provider will contact the roofing contractors for each Site during the survey phase to arrange any roof manufacturer inspections needed to preserve the roof warranty under Section 2.2(c) of the Portfolio SOW.

## 5. Permits and Interconnection

5.1 Provider will obtain construction permits under Section 3.6 of the MSA. Provider expects to submit the first permit applications (NJ-01 to NJ-03) in late April 2026.

## 6. Equipment

6.1 **Modules.** Customer has placed its order for the Halvorne HV-580BM modules. Customer confirmed that deliveries are planned to meet the module delivery dates in Annex B of the Portfolio SOW, starting May 15, 2026 for NJ-01. Deliveries for NJ-01 to NJ-06 will go to Provider's staging yard in South Brunswick. Deliveries for the remaining Sites will go to the Site or the staging yard, to be confirmed four (4) weeks before each delivery.

6.2 **Receiving inspection.** Provider will perform receiving inspections under Section 3.5 of the MSA and Section 4.1 of the Quality Agreement, and will report visible damage and quantity discrepancies within three (3) Business Days under Section 6.2 of the Portfolio SOW. Customer asked for module packing lists to be cross-checked against serial number lists. Provider agreed.

6.3 **Provider-supplied equipment.** Provider has issued purchase orders for inverters and racking for NJ-01 to NJ-07. Provider will issue purchase orders for the remaining Sites by April 30, 2026. Provider will send notices under the side letter if any tariff change arises.

6.4 **Spare modules.** Customer will deliver spare modules to the South Brunswick staging yard with the final Portfolio delivery, for storage under Section 9.3 of the Portfolio SOW.
