This letter (this "**Letter**") refers to the Master Services Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**") between Riboul Solar Installations, LLC ("**Provider**") and Treikev Community Power, Inc. ("**Customer**"). It also refers to Order Form No. 1 dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Order Form**") and the Statement of Work – New Jersey Rooftop Portfolio (the "**Portfolio SOW**").

Section 6.3 of the Summary of Principal Terms dated January 21, 2026 contemplated a mechanism for passing through tariff and duty changes that affect modules and other imported equipment. This Letter sets out that mechanism. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Letter have the meanings given in the MSA or the Order Form.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Baseline Date**" means February 13, 2026. This is the date of Provider's final pricing proposal on which the Installation Fee in Section 4.1 of the Order Form is based.

1.2 "**Covered Equipment**" means the Provider-supplied equipment listed in Section 3.2 of the Order Form that is manufactured outside the United States, or that contains components manufactured outside the United States, and that Provider imports or purchases for incorporation into a System. Covered Equipment includes inverters, racking and monitoring hardware.

1.3 "**Tariff Change**" means any of the following, if it occurs after the Baseline Date and applies to Covered Equipment:

(a) the imposition of a new tariff, duty, countervailing or antidumping duty, safeguard measure, quota charge or similar import charge;

(b) an increase in the rate of any of those charges;

(c) the expiration or withdrawal of an exclusion or exemption from any of those charges; or

(d) a final or preliminary determination by a United States governmental authority that changes the rate applicable to the Covered Equipment.

In each case the charge must be imposed by the United States or by any of its agencies.

1.4 "**Tariff Cost Adjustment**" means the net increase or decrease in Provider's landed cost of Covered Equipment that is directly attributable to a Tariff Change, calculated under Section 3.

## 2. Modules Supplied by Customer

2.1 Under Section 3.1 of the Order Form, the photovoltaic modules for the Portfolio are Customer-Furnished Equipment. Customer bears all tariffs, duties and import charges on modules that it procures, whether those charges are imposed before or after the Baseline Date. The Installation Fee does not include the cost of modules, and no Tariff Change affecting modules procured by Customer will change the Installation Fee.

2.2 If Customer asks Provider in writing to procure modules for any System in place of Customer-Furnished Equipment, the Parties will document that request in a change order under Section 4 of the MSA. The change order will set the price for those modules, and from the date of the change order those modules will be Covered Equipment for purposes of this Letter.

2.3 Delays in delivery of modules caused by customs detentions, tariff enforcement actions or trade restrictions are governed by Section 3.5 of the MSA, Section 4.2 of the MSA and Section 6.3 of the Portfolio SOW, together with Section 16 of the MSA, as applicable.

## 3. Pass-Through for Covered Equipment

3.1 **Adjustment.** If a Tariff Change occurs, the Installation Fee for each affected System will be increased or decreased by the Tariff Cost Adjustment for the Covered Equipment for that System. The adjustment applies only to Covered Equipment that has not been delivered to the United States port of entry when the Tariff Change takes effect.

3.2 **Calculation.** The Tariff Cost Adjustment equals the difference between:

(a) the tariffs, duties and import charges actually paid or payable by Provider or its supplier on the affected Covered Equipment; and

(b) the tariffs, duties and import charges that would have applied to that Covered Equipment at the rates in effect on the Baseline Date.

The Tariff Cost Adjustment does not include any markup, overhead or profit. It also does not include any increase that results from Provider's own failure to place purchase orders by the dates needed to meet the schedule in Annex B of the Portfolio SOW.

3.3 **Threshold.** The Parties will not process a Tariff Cost Adjustment unless the aggregate net amount for the Portfolio exceeds $25,000. Amounts below that threshold accumulate, and they are processed once the cumulative net amount exceeds the threshold.

3.4 **Mitigation.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effect of a Tariff Change. Mitigation may include sourcing qualifying equivalent equipment from domestic or non-affected suppliers, subject to Customer's approval of any substitution under Section 4.2 of the Portfolio SOW, and applying for any available exclusion. Provider shall pass any resulting savings through to Customer.

## 4. Notice and Documentation

4.1 Provider shall notify Customer in writing within twenty (20) Business Days after it becomes aware of a Tariff Change that it expects to give rise to a Tariff Cost Adjustment. The notice must describe the Tariff Change, the Covered Equipment and Systems affected, and Provider's good-faith estimate of the Tariff Cost Adjustment.

4.2 With each claim for a Tariff Cost Adjustment, Provider shall deliver reasonable supporting documentation. This includes supplier invoices, customs entry summaries (CBP Form 7501 or equivalent), and evidence of the applicable duty rates on the Baseline Date and at entry.

4.3 Customer may audit that documentation once each calendar year on reasonable notice, at Customer's expense. Provider may redact supplier pricing information that does not relate to tariffs, duties and import charges.

## 5. Implementation

5.1 Each Tariff Cost Adjustment will be documented in a change order under Section 4.2 of the MSA. Customer shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its signature on a change order that is supported in accordance with Section 4.

5.2 An increase will be invoiced with the next milestone payment under Section 4.2 of the Order Form for the affected System. A decrease will be credited against that milestone payment. Any increase is payable under Section 5 of the MSA.

5.3 Tariff Cost Adjustments do not affect the O&M fee under Section 4.3 of the Order Form. Replacement Covered Equipment supplied during the O&M period is charged in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Portfolio SOW and Annex C of the Portfolio SOW, where applicable.

## 6. Schedule Relief

A Tariff Change that delays delivery of Covered Equipment is an event to which Section 16 of the MSA applies. The schedule in Annex B of the Portfolio SOW will be extended in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Order Form.

## 7. Future Order Forms

This Letter applies to the Order Form. The Parties will consider in good faith applying the same mechanism to future Order Forms. Any such application will be stated in the relevant Order Form, with the baseline date for that Order Form.
