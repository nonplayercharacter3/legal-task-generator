# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

**Riboul Solar Installations, LLC** / **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**

This Service Level Schedule (this "**Schedule**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 by and between **Riboul Solar Installations, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**").

This Schedule is the Service Level Schedule referred to in the Master Services Agreement between Provider and Customer dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). It sets out response times, remediation periods and service credits for the O&M Services.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **MSA Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the MSA or, where relevant, the applicable Order Form or Statement of Work.

1.2 **Defect.** In this Schedule, "**Defect**" has the meaning given in Section 1.3 of the Quality Agreement.

1.3 **Other Definitions.** In this Schedule:

(a) "**Critical Event**" means a Defect or other condition that causes a System, or one or more inverters in a System, to stop producing electricity. It also means any condition that creates an imminent risk of fire, electrical hazard, bodily injury or water intrusion into the building at a Site.

(b) "**Monthly O&M Fee**" means, for a System, the portion of the O&M fee under the applicable Order Form that is payable for that System for the relevant calendar month. For Order Form No. 1, this is the fee under Section 4.3 of that Order Form, as escalated under Section 4.4.

(c) "**Monitoring System**" means the monitoring system installed by Provider for a System under the applicable Statement of Work.

(d) "**Normal Hours**" means 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on Business Days.

(e) "**Notice of Defect**" means notice of a Defect given under Section 2.2.

(f) "**Response**" means that a qualified Provider technician has acknowledged the Notice of Defect, begun remote diagnosis, and, where remote diagnosis cannot resolve the Defect, been dispatched to the Site.

(g) "**Service Credit**" means a credit against the Monthly O&M Fee calculated under Section 4.

(h) "**Standard Event**" means any Defect that is not a Critical Event.

1.4 **Interpretation.** References to a Section are to a Section of this Schedule unless stated otherwise. Time periods measured in hours run continuously, including outside Normal Hours, unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Application and Notice

2.1 **Application.** This Schedule applies to the O&M Services for each System from its acceptance under Section 6 of the MSA until the end of the O&M period for that System under the applicable Order Form. For Order Form No. 1, the O&M Services are described in Section 9 of the Portfolio SOW, and Section 6.2 of Order Form No. 1 makes them subject to this Schedule.

2.2 **Notice of Defect.** A Defect is notified to Provider when any of the following occurs:

(a) the Monitoring System generates an alarm identifying the Defect, in which case notice is given when the alarm is received at Provider's monitoring center;

(b) Customer notifies Provider of the Defect by email to Provider's O&M service desk address, or by telephone to Provider's O&M hotline followed by email confirmation; or

(c) Provider identifies the Defect during preventive maintenance, an inspection or other O&M Services, in which case notice is given when Provider records the Defect in its maintenance log.

2.3 **Service Desk.** Provider shall maintain an O&M service desk and a telephone hotline that is staffed twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week. Provider shall give Customer the service desk contact details in writing before acceptance of the first System and promptly after any change.

## 3. Response and Remediation

3.1 **Response Times.** Provider shall provide a Response to each Notice of Defect within the following times:

| Category | Remote Response | On-Site Attendance (where required) |
|---|---|---|
| Critical Event | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| Standard Event | 8 Normal Hours | 3 Business Days |

3.2 **Remediation Period.** Provider shall remediate each Defect within 5 Business Days after notice of a Defect (the "**Remediation Period**"). Remediation means that the affected System, inverter, string or component has been restored to normal operation and the condition giving rise to the Defect has been corrected, and Provider has confirmed this through the Monitoring System or on-site testing.

3.3 **Temporary Measures.** If a Critical Event creates a safety hazard or a risk of water intrusion, Provider shall make the System or the affected area safe and weather-tight within the on-site attendance time in Section 3.1, including by de-energizing equipment or installing temporary protection. Temporary measures do not constitute remediation for the purposes of Section 3.2.

3.4 **Status Updates.** Until a Defect is remediated, Provider shall update Customer on the status of each Critical Event at least once every twenty-four (24) hours, and on the status of each Standard Event at least once every two (2) Business Days. Each update must state the diagnosed cause, the work performed and the expected remediation date.

3.5 **Extension of Periods.** The response times and the Remediation Period are extended for any period during which:

(a) Provider cannot perform because of a force majeure event under Section 16 of the MSA; or

(b) Customer, the building owner or a tenant fails to provide Provider with access to the Site or to the electrical room in accordance with Section 3.4 of the MSA, after Provider has requested that access in writing.

3.6 **Records.** Provider shall log each Notice of Defect, Response and remediation in its computerized maintenance management system and record the time of each event. Customer may access those records through Provider's customer portal.

## 4. Service Credits

4.1 **Remediation Credit.** If Provider fails to remediate a Defect within the Remediation Period, Provider shall credit Customer, for each Business Day or part of a Business Day after the end of the Remediation Period until the Defect is remediated, an amount equal to:

(a) five percent (5%) of the Monthly O&M Fee for the affected System, in the case of a Critical Event; or

(b) two percent (2%) of the Monthly O&M Fee for the affected System, in the case of a Standard Event.

4.2 **Response Credit.** If Provider fails to meet an on-site attendance time in Section 3.1 for a Critical Event, Provider shall credit Customer an additional two percent (2%) of the Monthly O&M Fee for the affected System for each period of twenty-four (24) hours, or part of such a period, by which the attendance time is exceeded.

4.3 **Monitoring Availability Credit.** Provider shall ensure that the Monitoring System for each System is available and transmitting data to Customer's asset management platform at least ninety-eight percent (98%) of the hours in each calendar month. Downtime caused by cellular network outages outside Provider's control or by Site power outages is excluded. If availability falls below that level in a month, Provider shall credit Customer three percent (3%) of the Monthly O&M Fee for that System for that month.

4.4 **Monthly Limit.** The total Service Credits for a System in any calendar month will not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the Monthly O&M Fee for that System for that month.

4.5 **Application of Credits.** Provider shall show Service Credits accrued in a calendar month on the monthly performance report under Section 5.1 and shall apply them against the next O&M invoice issued under Section 5.2 of the MSA. Service Credits accrued in the final month of an O&M period will be paid to Customer within thirty (30) days after the end of that period.

4.6 **Disputes.** Provider may dispute the calculation of a Service Credit by written notice to Customer within fifteen (15) Business Days after the relevant monthly performance report. The dispute will be resolved under Section 17.3 of the MSA.

4.7 **Nature of Credits.** Service Credits are a price adjustment that reflects the reduced value of the O&M Services. They are not a penalty. Service Credits do not limit Customer's right to terminate for material breach under Section 15.2 of the MSA.

## 5. Reporting and Review

5.1 **Monthly Report.** Provider shall include in each monthly performance report under Section 9.1(e) of the Portfolio SOW, or the equivalent provision of any other Statement of Work, the following information for each System:

(a) each Notice of Defect received during the month, its category and the time it was received;

(b) the time of Response and of on-site attendance;

(c) the date of remediation, or the expected remediation date for open Defects;

(d) Monitoring System availability for the month; and

(e) the Service Credits accrued, with supporting calculations.

5.2 **Quarterly Review.** The Parties' representatives shall meet each calendar quarter during the O&M period to review Provider's performance against this Schedule, recurring Defects and corrective actions. Provider shall prepare a summary of recurring Defects and of proposed corrective actions before each meeting.

5.3 **Chronic Failure.** If Provider fails to meet the Remediation Period for three (3) or more Critical Events at the same System in any period of six (6) consecutive months, Provider shall, within ten (10) Business Days after Customer's written request, deliver a written root cause analysis and a corrective action plan for Customer's review.

## 6. General

6.1 **Incorporation.** This Schedule is incorporated into the MSA and each Order Form under Section 2.2 of the MSA.

6.2 **Amendment.** This Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties under Section 18.4 of the MSA. The Parties may agree different service levels for a particular Order Form by stating them in that Order Form.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Schedule may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
