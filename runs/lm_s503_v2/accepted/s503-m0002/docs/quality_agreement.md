# QUALITY AGREEMENT

**Riboul Solar Installations, LLC** / **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 between:

- **Riboul Solar Installations, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"); and
- **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**").

This Quality Agreement is the Quality Agreement referred to in the Master Services Agreement between Provider and Customer dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). It covers inspection, testing, documentation and quality reporting for the Services.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **MSA Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA or, where relevant, in the applicable Order Form or Statement of Work.

1.2 **Other Definitions.** In this Quality Agreement:

(a) "**CMMS**" means Provider's computerized maintenance management system.

(b) "**Hold Point**" means a stage of the Installation Work identified in Section 4.3. Work may not proceed past a Hold Point until the required inspection has been completed and recorded.

(c) "**Inspection and Test Plan**" or "**ITP**" means the inspection and test plan for a System prepared under Section 3.2.

(d) "**Quality Manager**" means the individual Provider designates under Section 3.3.

(e) "**Quality Records**" means the records described in Section 6.1.

1.3 **Defect.** "**Defect**" means any of the following in a System or any component of a System, whether or not caused by Provider:

- any nonconformity with the Specifications, the approved drawings or applicable codes and standards;
- any malfunction, damage or deterioration; or
- any shortfall against expected performance.

This includes any of those conditions in Customer-Furnished Equipment, Provider-supplied equipment, workmanship, wiring, racking, roof attachments and monitoring equipment. A Defect may be identified through receiving inspection, in-process inspection, commissioning tests, Monitoring System data, preventive maintenance or any other inspection.

1.4 **Interpretation.** References to a Section are to a Section of this Quality Agreement unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Purpose and Scope

2.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement sets out the quality management, inspection, testing, documentation and reporting requirements that apply to the Services. Its purpose is to give Customer, its lenders and its tax equity investors a consistent and auditable record of the condition and quality of each System.

2.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to:

(a) the Installation Work for each System, from receipt of equipment through acceptance under Section 6 of the MSA; and

(b) the O&M Services for each System, for the O&M period under the applicable Order Form.

For Order Form No. 1, it applies to the Portfolio described in Section 2.1 of that Order Form.

2.3 **Relationship to Statements of Work.** Section 7.1 of the Portfolio SOW requires Provider to perform the inspections and keep the records required by this Quality Agreement. Any other Statement of Work may state additional inspection or testing requirements for its Systems.

## 3. Quality Management System

3.1 **Quality Program.** Provider shall maintain a documented quality management program for its commercial rooftop solar installation and O&M business. The program must cover at least:

- document control;
- control of purchased and Customer-Furnished Equipment;
- inspection and testing;
- control of nonconforming items;
- corrective and preventive action;
- calibration of measuring and test equipment; and
- training of personnel.

Provider shall give Customer a copy of its quality manual on request.

3.2 **Inspection and Test Plan.** For each System, Provider shall prepare an ITP and deliver it to Customer with the 90% drawing set under Section 5.1(b) of the Portfolio SOW or the equivalent provision of any other Statement of Work. The ITP must identify:

- each inspection and test to be performed;
- the acceptance criteria;
- the responsible inspector;
- the Hold Points; and
- the Quality Records to be produced.

Customer may comment on the ITP within the review period in Section 5.2 of the Portfolio SOW. Provider shall address Customer's reasonable comments.

3.3 **Quality Manager.** Provider shall designate a Quality Manager responsible for implementing this Quality Agreement. The Quality Manager must have authority to stop work at any Site where a Hold Point has not been cleared. Provider shall notify Customer in writing of the Quality Manager's name and contact details, and of any change.

3.4 **Calibration.** Provider shall calibrate all test equipment used for commissioning tests and O&M testing in accordance with the manufacturer's recommended intervals. This includes insulation resistance testers, I-V curve tracers, irradiance sensors, thermal cameras and torque tools. Provider shall keep calibration certificates as Quality Records.

## 4. Inspection and Testing

4.1 **Receiving Inspection.** Provider shall inspect each delivery of equipment on receipt at a Site or at Provider's staging yard.

(a) For Customer-Furnished Equipment, the inspection is the inspection under Section 3.5 of the MSA and Section 6.2 of the Portfolio SOW. Provider shall record:

- quantities;
- make, model and serial numbers; and
- any visible damage to packaging or equipment.

(b) For Provider-supplied equipment, Provider shall also verify conformity with the equipment specifications in Section 4.2 of the Portfolio SOW.

4.2 **In-Process Inspection.** Provider's site superintendent shall perform and record daily in-process inspections of the Installation Work. These inspections must cover:

- roof protection measures under Section 2.4 of the Portfolio SOW;
- attachment and ballast placement against the approved drawings;
- wire management; and
- labeling.

4.3 **Hold Points.** Provider shall not proceed past any of the following Hold Points at a Site until the Quality Manager or the Quality Manager's delegate has inspected the work and recorded the result:

(a) completion of roof preparation and layout, before installation of attachments or ballast;

(b) completion of roof penetrations and flashings, before they are covered by racking or equipment;

(c) completion of racking and module installation, before electrical termination; and

(d) completion of electrical installation, before energization.

At the Hold Point in Section 4.3(b), Provider shall photograph each roof penetration and flashing and record its location on the as-built drawings.

4.4 **Customer Witnessing.** Customer may attend any Hold Point inspection or commissioning test. Provider shall notify Customer at least two (2) Business Days before each Hold Point inspection and at least five (5) Business Days before the commissioning tests for a System. If Customer does not attend, Provider may proceed.

4.5 **Commissioning Tests.** Provider shall perform the commissioning tests in Section 7.2 of the Portfolio SOW in accordance with the ITP. Provider shall record the results in the commissioning report under Section 7.3 of the Portfolio SOW.

4.6 **O&M Inspections.** During the O&M period, Provider shall record the results of each preventive maintenance visit under Section 9.1(b) of the Portfolio SOW. Each record must use a standard inspection checklist for the System, and the checklist must include the results of annual infrared thermography.

## 5. Defect Logging and Reporting

5.1 **Logging.** Provider shall log each Defect in the CMMS promptly after it is identified. Each entry must state:

(a) the System, Site and affected component;

(b) the date and time the Defect was identified and the method by which it was identified;

(c) a description of the Defect, with photographs where practicable;

(d) the apparent cause, if known, including whether the Defect relates to Customer-Furnished Equipment;

(e) any action taken; and

(f) the date the Defect was closed.

5.2 **Classification.** Provider shall classify each logged Defect by component category:

- modules;
- inverters;
- racking and attachments;
- roof penetrations and flashings;
- wiring and balance-of-system;
- monitoring; or
- other.

Provider shall also classify each Defect by severity, in accordance with the classification method in its quality manual.

5.3 **Customer-Furnished Equipment.** If a Defect relates to Customer-Furnished Equipment, Provider shall notify Customer's representative under Section 1.3 of the applicable Order Form within three (3) Business Days after logging it. Provider shall give Customer the information reasonably needed to support a manufacturer warranty claim, including:

- serial numbers;
- photographs;
- I-V curve or thermography data, where available; and
- the date of installation.

5.4 **Quality Report.** Provider shall deliver a quality report to Customer each month during construction and each calendar quarter during the O&M period. The report must summarize:

- Defects logged and closed during the period;
- open Defects;
- trends by component category; and
- corrective and preventive actions taken or proposed.

During the O&M period, the quarterly quality report may be combined with the material for the quarterly review under the Service Level Schedule.

5.5 **Root Cause Analysis.** If the same type of Defect recurs at three (3) or more Systems, or recurs three (3) or more times at a single System within twelve (12) months, Provider shall prepare a root cause analysis. Provider shall deliver it to Customer with the next quality report.

## 6. Records and Documentation

6.1 **Quality Records.** Provider shall create and keep the following Quality Records for each System:

(a) receiving inspection records;

(b) in-process inspection records and Hold Point records, including photographs;

(c) the ITP and the commissioning report;

(d) calibration certificates for test equipment used on the System;

(e) the CMMS Defect log; and

(f) preventive maintenance checklists and thermography reports.

6.2 **Retention.** Provider shall keep the Quality Records for each System for the longer of:

- seven (7) years after acceptance of that System; and
- three (3) years after the end of the O&M period for that System.

6.3 **Access and Delivery.** Customer may access the Quality Records through Provider's customer portal. On Customer's request, and in any event on expiration or termination of the O&M period for a System, Provider shall deliver copies of the Quality Records for that System to Customer in electronic form. Quality Records prepared specifically for a System are deliverables to which Section 14.2 of the MSA applies.

## 7. Audits

7.1 **Customer Audits.** Customer, or an independent engineer acting for Customer or its lenders or tax equity investors, may audit Provider's compliance with this Quality Agreement:

- once in each calendar year; and
- at any time after a root cause analysis has been delivered under Section 5.5.

Customer shall give at least ten (10) Business Days' notice of an audit, and the audit must be conducted during Normal Hours with minimal disruption to the Services. Each Party bears its own costs of an audit.

7.2 **Findings.** Customer shall give Provider a written summary of any audit findings. Within fifteen (15) Business Days after receiving the summary, Provider shall respond with a corrective action plan for each finding that identifies a failure to comply with this Quality Agreement.

7.3 **Confidentiality.** Information obtained in an audit is Confidential Information of Provider under Section 10 of the MSA. An independent engineer must be bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with Section 10.1 of the MSA.

## 8. General

8.1 **Incorporation.** This Quality Agreement is incorporated into the MSA and each Order Form under Section 2.2 of the MSA.

8.2 **Disputes.** Disputes under this Quality Agreement will be escalated under Section 17.3 of the MSA.

8.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties under Section 18.4 of the MSA.

8.4 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
