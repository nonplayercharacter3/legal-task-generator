# STATEMENT OF WORK – NEW JERSEY ROOFTOP PORTFOLIO

**Riboul Solar Installations, LLC** / **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**

This Statement of Work (this "**SOW**" or the "**Portfolio SOW**") is entered into as of March 9, 2026 by and between **Riboul Solar Installations, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"), and **Treikev Community Power, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**").

This SOW is entered into under the Master Services Agreement between Provider and Customer dated as of March 2, 2026 (the "**MSA**"). It relates to Order Form No. 1 dated as of March 9, 2026 (the "**Order Form**"). This SOW describes the scope, Specifications, schedule and deliverables for the Installation Work and O&M Services for the Portfolio identified in the Order Form.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given in the MSA or the Order Form.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Commercial Operation**" means, for a System, the date on which the System has been accepted under Section 6 of the MSA, has received permission to operate from the applicable EDC, and begins delivering electricity to the distribution grid for the account of Customer.

1.2 "**EDC**" means the electric distribution company serving a Site, as identified in Annex A.

1.3 "**Environmental Attributes**" means all renewable energy certificates, solar renewable energy certificates, and other credits, benefits, emissions reductions and attributes, however named, arising from the generation of electricity by a System.

1.4 "**Offtake Agreement**" means, for a System, each community solar subscription agreement, power purchase agreement, bill credit arrangement, Environmental Attribute sale agreement or other agreement under which Customer or its Affiliate sells or allocates the electricity, bill credits or Environmental Attributes of that System. This includes subscriptions under the New Jersey Community Solar Energy Program.

1.5 "**Production Revenue**" means all revenue that Customer or its Affiliate receives or would have received under an Offtake Agreement from the electricity, subscription bill credits and Environmental Attributes generated by a System.

1.6 "**Site Damage**" means:

(a) physical damage to the roof, roof membrane, insulation, deck, structure, parapets, drainage, rooftop mechanical equipment or other building systems or premises at a Site, including water intrusion and resulting interior damage; and

(b) any lost Production Revenue resulting from that damage, including revenue lost while a System is offline, de-energized or curtailed to allow investigation or repair of the roof or premises, for the remaining term of the Offtake Agreement for the affected System.

1.7 "**Structural Report**" means the report of a New Jersey licensed professional engineer confirming that the roof structure at a Site can support the dead, live, wind, snow and seismic loads imposed by the System.

1.8 "**Target Mechanical Completion Date**" means, for a System, the date set out for that System in Annex B.

## 2. Scope of Installation Work

2.1 **General.** Provider shall perform the Installation Work for each of the fourteen (14) Systems identified in Section 2.1 of the Order Form. The Portfolio totals 18.6 MWdc across 14 rooftop sites.

2.2 **Pre-Construction.** For each Site, Provider shall:

(a) perform a pre-construction survey of the roof and electrical room, including a roof condition assessment, core samples where the roof manufacturer's warranty permits, infrared moisture scanning, and photographic documentation of existing conditions;

(b) obtain a Structural Report and deliver it to Customer before submitting permit applications;

(c) coordinate with the building owner or roof lessor, and with any roofing contractor holding a roof warranty, so that the roof warranty is preserved. This includes obtaining any roof manufacturer inspection or approval the warranty requires;

(d) prepare construction drawings stamped by a New Jersey licensed professional engineer, consistent with the Specifications in Section 4; and

(e) obtain the building, electrical, fire and other construction permits required for the Installation Work under Section 3.6 of the MSA.

2.3 **Construction and Installation.** For each Site, Provider shall:

(a) receive, inspect and stage Customer-Furnished Equipment in accordance with Section 3.5 of the MSA and Section 6 of this SOW;

(b) procure and deliver the Provider-supplied equipment listed in Section 3.2 of the Order Form;

(c) install racking, ballast and attachments, including any roof penetrations and flashings, in accordance with the approved drawings and the roof manufacturer's requirements;

(d) install modules, inverters, wiring, conduit, combiner boxes, disconnects, rapid shutdown equipment and all other balance-of-system components;

(e) install the monitoring system and connect it to Customer's asset management platform; and

(f) complete the interconnection facilities on the customer side of the point of interconnection, and support Customer's interconnection process with the EDC, including witness testing.

2.4 **Roof Protection.** Provider shall take all reasonable precautions to prevent Site Damage. These include:

- using walk pads and protective boards on traffic routes;
- limiting point loads during material staging;
- installing temporary weather protection on any opening in the roof membrane at the end of each working day; and
- not staging materials on the roof beyond the load limits stated in the Structural Report.

Provider shall promptly notify Customer and the building owner of any damage to the roof or premises that it causes or discovers.

2.5 **Site Rules.** Provider shall comply with the building owner's site rules, working hours, security procedures and tenant access restrictions as communicated to Provider in writing. Provider shall coordinate crane lifts, deliveries and electrical shutdowns with the building owner at least five (5) Business Days in advance.

2.6 **Clean-Up.** On completion at each Site, Provider shall remove all debris, packaging, surplus materials and temporary facilities. Provider shall leave the roof and premises in at least the condition documented in the pre-construction survey, apart from the installed System.

## 3. Exclusions and Customer Responsibilities

3.1 **Exclusions.** The Installation Work does not include:

- roof replacement or roof repair work needed to address deficiencies identified in the pre-construction survey;
- structural reinforcement identified in a Structural Report;
- upgrades to the EDC's distribution system;
- utility interconnection fees; or
- remediation of hazardous materials.

Any of these items that Customer asks Provider to perform will be the subject of a change order under Section 4 of the MSA.

3.2 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall:

(a) provide Site access and building owner consents under Section 3.4 of the MSA;

(b) deliver Customer-Furnished Equipment in accordance with Section 6;

(c) obtain the interconnection approvals, community solar program approvals and land use entitlements described in Section 3.6 of the MSA;

(d) provide available as-built building drawings, roof warranty documentation and prior roof inspection reports for each Site within fifteen (15) Business Days after the date of this SOW; and

(e) appoint a representative with authority to approve drawings and submittals within the review periods in Section 5.2.

## 4. Specifications

4.1 **Design Standards.** Each System shall be designed and installed in accordance with:

- the National Electrical Code as adopted in New Jersey;
- the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code;
- the International Fire Code as adopted locally, including rooftop access pathway and setback requirements;
- UL 2703, UL 1741 and IEEE 1547, as applicable;
- the EDC's interconnection technical requirements; and
- the roof manufacturer's installation requirements for rooftop equipment.

4.2 **Equipment.** The Systems shall use the following equipment unless Customer approves a substitution in writing:

| Component | Specification | Supplied by |
|---|---|---|
| Modules | Halvorne HV-580BM bifacial monocrystalline, 580 W | Customer |
| Inverters | Three-phase string inverters, 100–125 kW, UL 1741 SB listed | Provider |
| Racking | Ballasted low-tilt system with engineered attachments where required, UL 2703 listed | Provider |
| Monitoring | Inverter-level and meter-level monitoring with cellular backhaul | Provider |
| Production meters | Revenue-grade, where Customer-owned meters are required by the EDC | Customer |

4.3 **Performance Criteria.** Each System shall achieve, during the capacity test described in Section 7.2(c), a measured performance ratio of not less than ninety-five percent (95%) of the modeled performance ratio in the approved design energy model for that System. The measurement shall be corrected for irradiance and module temperature.

## 5. Deliverables and Submittals

5.1 **Deliverables.** For each System, Provider shall deliver to Customer:

(a) the pre-construction survey report and Structural Report;

(b) 30%, 90% and issued-for-construction drawing sets;

(c) the design energy model;

(d) copies of all permits and inspection sign-offs;

(e) the commissioning report described in Section 7.3;

(f) as-built drawings in PDF and native CAD format;

(g) equipment manuals, serial number lists and Provider-supplied equipment warranty documentation; and

(h) evidence that the roof warranty has been preserved, including any roof manufacturer inspection letter.

5.2 **Review.** Customer shall review and approve or comment on each drawing set within ten (10) Business Days after receipt. Provider shall address Customer's reasonable comments in the next submittal.

## 6. Customer-Furnished Equipment Delivery

6.1 Customer shall deliver modules for each System to the Site, or to Provider's staging yard in South Brunswick, New Jersey, not later than the module delivery date stated for that System in Annex B.

6.2 Provider shall inspect each delivery under Section 3.5 of the MSA. Provider shall report visible damage and quantity discrepancies to Customer within three (3) Business Days after receipt.

6.3 Delays in delivery of Customer-Furnished Equipment entitle Provider to an equitable adjustment under Section 4.2 of the MSA.

## 7. Testing and Commissioning

7.1 **Inspections.** Provider shall arrange and attend all authority having jurisdiction inspections and EDC witness tests. Provider shall also perform the inspections and keep the records required by the Quality Agreement.

7.2 **Commissioning Tests.** For each System, Provider shall perform the following commissioning tests under Section 6.1 of the MSA:

(a) visual and mechanical inspection, torque verification of a sample of attachments, and verification of grounding and bonding continuity;

(b) electrical tests, including:
- string open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current tests;
- insulation resistance tests;
- I-V curve tracing on not less than ten percent (10%) of strings; and
- inverter start-up and functional tests, including verification of rapid shutdown and anti-islanding;

(c) a capacity test of not less than three (3) consecutive days of operation in accordance with ASTM E2848, measured against the performance criteria in Section 4.3; and

(d) verification of monitoring system data communication and alarm functions.

7.3 **Commissioning Report.** Provider shall deliver a commissioning report for each System. The report shall contain the test results, a punch list of open items with target completion dates, and infrared thermography of modules and electrical connections.

## 8. Schedule

8.1 Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve mechanical completion of each System by its Target Mechanical Completion Date in Annex B. Provider shall also use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve mechanical completion of all Systems by December 15, 2026, as stated in Section 5.1 of the Order Form.

8.2 Provider shall deliver an updated construction schedule to Customer every two weeks. The schedule shall identify critical path items and any anticipated delay.

8.3 The schedule will be extended as provided in Section 5.2 of the Order Form.

## 9. O&M Services

9.1 **Scope.** Beginning on acceptance of each System and continuing for the O&M period in Section 6 of the Order Form, Provider shall perform the following O&M Services:

(a) **Monitoring.** Continuous remote monitoring of System performance and alarms.

(b) **Preventive Maintenance.** Preventive maintenance at least twice each year. This includes:
- visual inspection of modules, racking, ballast and wiring;
- inverter maintenance in accordance with the manufacturer's schedule;
- torque checks of a sample of electrical connections;
- annual infrared thermography; and
- inspection of roof penetrations, flashings and walk pads.

(c) **Module Cleaning.** Module cleaning once each year, and additional cleaning on Customer's request at the rates in Annex C.

(d) **Corrective Maintenance.** Corrective maintenance in response to alarms, Customer notices and inspection findings.

(e) **Reporting.** Monthly performance reports. Each report shall show production against expected production, availability, alarms, work performed, and open items.

9.2 **Service Levels.** Response times, remediation periods and service credits for the O&M Services are set out in the Service Level Schedule.

9.3 **Spare Parts.** Provider shall maintain at its New Jersey facility a stock of spare Provider-supplied components sufficient for the Portfolio, including not less than two (2) spare inverters of each model installed. Customer shall maintain spare modules, which Provider shall store at its staging yard at no additional charge.

9.4 **Out-of-Scope Work.** Repairs that are not covered by the warranties in Section 8 of the MSA or by the O&M fee will be invoiced at the rates in Annex C after Customer's written approval. This includes repairs of Customer-Furnished Equipment and repairs required as a result of acts of the building owner, tenants or third parties.

## 10. Personnel and Communication

10.1 Provider's Project Manager and Customer's Development Lead identified under Section 1.3 of the Order Form shall hold a weekly progress call during construction and a monthly review during the O&M period.

10.2 Provider shall assign a full-time site superintendent to each Site during active construction. Provider shall ensure that at least one crew member holding a current OSHA 30-hour certification is present at each Site whenever work is performed.

## 11. General

11.1 This SOW is governed by and incorporates the MSA, together with the Order Form, in accordance with Section 2.2 of the MSA.

11.2 This SOW may be amended only by a change order or other written instrument signed by both Parties.

11.3 This SOW may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
