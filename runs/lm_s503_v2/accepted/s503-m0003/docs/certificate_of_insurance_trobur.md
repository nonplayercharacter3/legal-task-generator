7.1 **Additional Insureds.** Ashgrove Commercial Properties LLC and its managing agent are included as additional insureds under the Commercial General Liability policy in Section 5.2 by endorsement CG 20 11 (Additional Insured – Managers or Lessors of Premises) or its equivalent. The Commercial General Liability policy in Section 5.2 includes a blanket waiver of subrogation where required by written contract.
