# EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Furux Dental Technologies, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Carnegie Center Boulevard, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Supplier**"), and **Trobur Dental Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Executive Drive, Suite 300, Newark, Delaware 19702 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs, manufactures and sells digital dental equipment and consumables, including intraoral scanners, chairside milling units and zirconia milling blocks.

B. Distributor sells and services dental equipment and consumables for independent dental clinics and dental service organizations.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. For this purpose, "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting equity of a Person or the power to direct its management and policies.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to Distributor, (a) any transaction or series of related transactions in which any Person or group of Persons acquires, directly or indirectly, more than 50% of the equity interests or voting power of Distributor; (b) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction involving Distributor after which the holders of Distributor's equity interests before the transaction hold 50% or less of the equity interests or voting power of the surviving entity; or (c) the sale of all or substantially all of Distributor's assets.

"**Contract Year**" means each consecutive 12-month period during the Term beginning on March 1. The first Contract Year begins on the Effective Date.

"**Customer**" means any independent dental clinic, dental practice, dental laboratory or dental service organization located in the Territory that buys Products from Distributor.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or regulatory body, including the U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("**FDA**"), or any court or agency of any of them.

"**Initial Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**" has the meaning given in Section 5.2.

"**Non-Renewal Notice**" means a written notice from one Party to the other stating that the notifying Party elects not to renew this Agreement at the end of the then-current Term.

"**Non-Renewal Window**" means the 30-day period beginning 180 days before the last day of the then-current Term.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Product Claim**" means any claim, suit or proceeding brought by a third party, including any Customer or patient, alleging bodily injury, death or property damage arising out of the design, manufacture, labeling or use of any Product.

"**Products**" means the intraoral scanners, chairside milling units, zirconia milling blocks and related accessories, software and spare parts listed in Exhibit A, as Supplier may update Exhibit A under Section 4.3.

"**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of the Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

"**Quality Matters**" means product quality, specifications and release, complaint handling, adverse event and medical device reporting, traceability, storage and handling requirements, Recalls, and regulatory compliance matters relating to the Products.

"**Recall**" means any recall, market withdrawal, removal, field correction, field safety notice or similar field action relating to any Product, whether voluntary or mandated by any Governmental Authority, and whether initiated by Supplier, Distributor or a Governmental Authority.

"**Renewal Term**" means a period of five Contract Years.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

"**Territory**" means the geographic area described in Exhibit B.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory during the Term, and Distributor accepts the appointment. During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller of the Products in the Territory and shall not itself sell Products directly to Customers in the Territory, except as provided in Section 2.4.

2.2 **Distributor Covenants.** Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote, market and sell the Products throughout the Territory. Distributor shall not actively solicit sales of Products outside the Territory.

2.3 **Quality Agreement.** Concurrently with this Agreement, the Parties are entering into the Quality Agreement, which sets out their respective responsibilities for Quality Matters. Each Party shall comply with the Quality Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Quality Agreement, with respect to Quality Matters, the Quality Agreement shall govern, and with respect to all other matters, this Agreement shall govern.

2.4 **Reserved Accounts.** Supplier may sell Products directly to the national accounts listed in Exhibit C. Supplier shall pay Distributor a service fee of 6% of the net sales price of Products shipped by Supplier to any location of a national account in the Territory, payable within 45 days after the end of each calendar quarter.

2.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Neither Party is the agent, partner or joint venturer of the other.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of four years, ending February 29, 2028 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 13.

3.2 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive Renewal Terms, unless either Party delivers a Non-Renewal Notice to the other Party during the Non-Renewal Window. A Non-Renewal Notice delivered outside the Non-Renewal Window has no effect.

3.3 **Terms During Renewal.** Each Renewal Term is on the terms of this Agreement then in effect, including Section 5.2.

## 4. ORDERS AND DELIVERY

4.1 **Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by written purchase orders delivered to Supplier by email or through Supplier's electronic ordering portal. Each purchase order shall state the Products, quantities, requested delivery dates and delivery locations.

4.2 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each purchase order within five business days after receipt. Supplier shall not unreasonably reject any purchase order that conforms to the most recent rolling forecast delivered under Section 6.1. A purchase order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Product Changes.** Supplier may add Products to Exhibit A on written notice. Supplier may discontinue a Product on at least 180 days' prior written notice. During that period, Distributor may place last-time orders for the discontinued Product in quantities consistent with its forecasts.

4.4 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products FCA Supplier's distribution facility in Cranbury, New Jersey (Incoterms 2020). Title and risk of loss pass to Distributor on delivery to Distributor's carrier.

4.5 **Inspection.** Distributor shall inspect each shipment within ten business days after receipt and notify Supplier of any shortage or visible damage. Supplier shall promptly replace or credit any short or damaged Products.

## 5. PRICING, PAYMENT AND MINIMUM PURCHASES

5.1 **Prices.** Distributor shall pay the prices set out in Exhibit D. Supplier may change prices once per Contract Year on at least 90 days' prior written notice, effective at the start of the next Contract Year. No increase in any Contract Year may exceed 4% of the price for the relevant Product in effect at the end of the preceding Contract Year.

5.2 **Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment.** For each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate net invoice value of at least the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment. The "**Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment**" is $2,400,000 for the first Contract Year, increasing 8% each Contract Year (compounded on the prior Contract Year's amount), and continues through the Initial Term and any Renewal Term. Purchases in excess of the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment in one Contract Year do not carry forward to any later Contract Year.

5.3 **Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for any Contract Year, Supplier may, as its sole remedies, (a) within 60 days after the end of that Contract Year, invoice Distributor for 15% of the shortfall amount, and (b) if the shortfall exceeds 20% of the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment for that Contract Year, convert Distributor's appointment to non-exclusive on 60 days' written notice.

5.4 **Invoices and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor on shipment. Distributor shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days after the invoice date. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable supporting detail, before the due date, and the Parties shall resolve the dispute in good faith.

5.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month or the highest rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

5.6 **Parent Guaranty.** On the Effective Date, Distributor shall cause Trobur Holdings, Inc. to deliver to Supplier a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under this Agreement in the form agreed by the Parties.

5.7 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Distributor shall pay those taxes or provide a valid resale exemption certificate.

## 6. FORECASTS, REPORTING AND MARKETING

6.1 **Forecasts.** By the 15th day of each month, Distributor shall deliver to Supplier a non-binding rolling 12-month forecast of its Product requirements.

6.2 **Sales Reports.** Within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall deliver a report showing, for that quarter, (a) unit sales of each Product by Customer, including Customer name and location, (b) Distributor's resale prices by Customer, (c) inventory on hand, and (d) installed base of intraoral scanners and chairside milling units by Customer and serial number.

6.3 **Use of Reports.** Supplier may use information in the reports under Section 6.2 to manage supply, warranty service and traceability of the Products and for the purposes of the Quality Agreement, subject to Section 12.

6.4 **Marketing Materials.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, at no charge, reasonable quantities of current marketing materials, product literature and demonstration software for the Products. Distributor shall not make any claims about the Products that are inconsistent with Supplier's labeling.

6.5 **Demonstration Units.** Supplier shall sell Distributor demonstration units of each scanner and milling unit model at 50% of the price in Exhibit D, not to exceed four units of each model per Contract Year.

6.6 **Training.** Supplier shall provide initial and annual product training for Distributor's sales and service personnel at no charge.

## 7. PRODUCT QUALITY AND RECALLS

7.1 **Regulatory Status.** Supplier shall obtain and maintain all FDA clearances, registrations and listings required for the Products to be marketed in the United States, and shall be the manufacturer of record for each Product.

7.2 **Storage and Handling.** Distributor shall store and handle Products in accordance with Supplier's written instructions and the Quality Agreement and shall maintain lot and serial number traceability to the Customer level.

7.3 **Complaints.** Each Party shall notify the other of Product complaints and adverse events in accordance with the Quality Agreement.

7.4 **Recalls.** Supplier shall have the right to decide whether to initiate a Recall, and Distributor shall reasonably cooperate in conducting any Recall in the Territory. Supplier shall bear all costs of any Recall, including Distributor's reasonable out-of-pocket costs of notifying Customers and of retrieving, replacing and crediting Products at dental clinics and other Customer locations, and the cost of replacement Products or credit for Products returned.

7.5 **Records.** Each Party shall keep records relating to the Products for the periods required by applicable law and the Quality Agreement.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Trademarks.** Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the Term to use Supplier's trademarks in the Territory solely to market and sell the Products. Distributor shall follow Supplier's reasonable trademark usage guidelines.

8.2 **Software.** Software embedded in or supplied with the Products is licensed, not sold, to Customers under Supplier's end-user license terms, which Distributor shall pass through to Customers.

8.3 **Ownership.** Supplier retains all intellectual property rights in the Products and its trademarks. Distributor retains all rights in its Customer lists and its own trademarks.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants to Distributor and to each Customer that each Product will, (a) for intraoral scanners and chairside milling units, for 24 months after installation at the Customer site, and (b) for zirconia milling blocks and other consumables, until the expiration date stated on the label, conform to its specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship.

9.2 **Compliance Warranty.** Supplier warrants that each Product, when delivered, will have been manufactured, labeled and packaged in compliance with applicable law, including the FDA Quality System Regulation and its successor requirements, and will not be adulterated or misbranded.

9.3 **Remedies.** For any Product that breaches Section 9.1, Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace the Product or credit its price. Supplier shall reimburse Distributor at the labor rates in Exhibit D for warranty service that Distributor performs at Supplier's request.

9.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of that Party.

9.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) ("**Losses**") arising out of any third-party claim to the extent arising from:

(a) any allegation that a Product, or Distributor's sale of a Product in accordance with this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party; or

(b) any Product Claim, including any claim of injury to a patient treated with a restoration milled from a Product or using data captured by a Product, except to the extent caused by Distributor's negligence or its modification of the Product.

10.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of any third-party claim to the extent arising from (a) Distributor's negligence or willful misconduct, (b) any representation or warranty about the Products made by Distributor that is not authorized by Supplier's labeling, or (c) Distributor's breach of Section 7.2.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party in writing of any claim, give the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement (except that no settlement that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party may be made without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnified Party may participate with counsel of its choice at its own expense. Delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 **Exclusion of Damages.** Subject to Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

11.2 **Cap.** Subject to Section 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the amounts paid by Distributor to Supplier in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.3 **Exclusions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 10, (b) a Party's breach of Section 12, (c) a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct, or (d) Distributor's obligation to pay for Products ordered and delivered.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in confidence, use it only to perform this Agreement and the Quality Agreement, and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as this Section 12.

12.2 **Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by a Party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including Distributor's Customer lists, Customer-level sales data and resale pricing, and Supplier's product designs, costs and pricing. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, was known to the recipient without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

12.3 **Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or a Governmental Authority, after giving the other Party prompt notice (where lawful) and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment.

12.4 **Duration.** This Section 12 survives for five years after the end of the Term, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 60 days after receiving written notice of it (or 15 days for failure to pay undisputed amounts).

13.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within 60 days.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination, (a) Supplier shall fill all purchase orders accepted before the effective date of termination, (b) Supplier shall, at Distributor's option, repurchase Distributor's unsold, saleable inventory of Products at Distributor's net purchase price, unless Supplier terminated under Section 13.1 or 13.2, (c) Supplier shall continue to honor its warranty obligations for Products sold before termination, and (d) Distributor shall cease using Supplier's trademarks after selling off any inventory not repurchased.

13.4 **Survival.** Sections 5.4, 5.5, 7.4, 7.5, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.3, 13.4, 15 and 16 survive expiration or termination.

## 14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier may assign this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of Distributor. Supplier shall give Distributor written notice of any assignment within 30 days after it takes effect.

14.2 **By Distributor.** Distributor may not assign this Agreement, or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Supplier. Any Change of Control of Distributor shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement requiring the prior written consent of Supplier. If a Change of Control of Distributor occurs without the prior written consent of Supplier, Supplier may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice to Distributor, given within 120 days after Supplier learns of the Change of Control.

14.3 **Effect.** Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 14 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

15.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

15.2 **Forum.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

15.3 **Escalation.** Before bringing any action, other than for injunctive relief, a Party shall give written notice of the dispute, and senior executives of each Party shall meet within 30 days after that notice to try to resolve it in good faith.

15.4 **Jury Waiver.** EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

15.5 **Equitable Relief.** A breach of Section 12 may cause irreparable harm for which damages are inadequate, and the non-breaching Party may seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Insurance.** Each Party shall maintain, during the Term and for three years after, commercial general liability insurance (including products and completed operations coverage) with limits of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request. Supplier shall name Distributor as an additional insured on its products liability coverage.

16.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance (other than payment) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance. 16.4 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses above (to the attention of the General Counsel of Supplier and the Chief Executive Officer of Distributor), or to another address a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.
